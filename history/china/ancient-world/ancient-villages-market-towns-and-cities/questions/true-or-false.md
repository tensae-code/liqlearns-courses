# Ancient villages market towns and cities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From farming village to Chang'an's million — walls, markets and wards organise pre-modern life
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ancient villages market towns and cities" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
