# China household registration and grassroots governance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Ming stores its Yellow Registers of every household on five guarded islands in Nanjing's Xuanwu Lake, safe from fire and thieves — the empire counted in paper
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China household registration and grassroots governance" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
