# China song economic revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 9: Song economic development"?
**Answer:** Early rice and the yangma transplanting horse push rice to first place — 'when Su and Hu ripen, the world is fed' — cotton spreads north, and southern silk, porcelain and shipbuilding outstrip the north

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 11: Liao-Song-Xia-Jin-Yuan economy and society"?
**Answer:** Paper money, sea-going junks, coal and iron at medieval heights, and cities whose night markets outshine Europe's
