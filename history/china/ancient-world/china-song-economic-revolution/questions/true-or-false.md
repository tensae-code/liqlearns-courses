# China song economic revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Early rice and the yangma transplanting horse push rice to first place — 'when Su and Hu ripen, the world is fed' — cotton spreads north, and southern silk, porcelain and shipbuilding outstrip the north
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China song economic revolution" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
