# China tang zhenguan kaiyuan and wu zetian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 2: From Zhenguan to Kaiyuan"?
**Answer:** Taizong learns from Sui's fall, keeps Wei Zheng as his 'mirror' through 200 remonstrances; Wu Zetian deposes her own sons to become China's only woman emperor and still grows the realm; Xuanzong's Kaiyuan reign makes Tang the strongest state on earth

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 3: Flourishing Tang"?
**Answer:** Curved ploughs and tube wheels, Shu silk, Tang sancai pottery and Chang'an in flower season — a century of farm, craft and city prosperity and warm ethnic relations
