# China tang zhenguan kaiyuan and wu zetian — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Taizong learns from Sui's fall, keeps Wei Zheng as his 'mirror' through 200 remonstrances; Wu Zetian deposes her own sons to become China's only woman emperor and still grows the realm; Xuanzong's Kaiyuan reign makes Tang the strongest state on earth
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China tang zhenguan kaiyuan and wu zetian" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
