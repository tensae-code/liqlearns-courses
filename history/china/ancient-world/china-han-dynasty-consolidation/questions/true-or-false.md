# China han dynasty consolidation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Han Wudi centralises, strikes the Xiongnu and opens the west, and the restored Eastern Han carries the unified state to its first great height — and its slow decay
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China han dynasty consolidation" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
