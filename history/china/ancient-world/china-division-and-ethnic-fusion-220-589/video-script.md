This lesson tells one story: China division and ethnic fusion 220 589.

Three centuries of rival courts become a melting pot — northern peoples settle, learn Chinese farming and dress, and the peoples fuse into a wider China

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
