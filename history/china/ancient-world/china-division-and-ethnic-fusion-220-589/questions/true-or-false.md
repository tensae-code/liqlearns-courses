# China division and ethnic fusion 220 589 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three centuries of rival courts become a melting pot — northern peoples settle, learn Chinese farming and dress, and the peoples fuse into a wider China
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China division and ethnic fusion 220 589" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
