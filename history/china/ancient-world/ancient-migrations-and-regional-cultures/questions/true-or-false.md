# Ancient migrations and regional cultures — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Peoples pour out of steppe and river valleys — Indo-Europeans, Huns, Turks — and regional cultures crystallise where they settle
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ancient migrations and regional cultures" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
