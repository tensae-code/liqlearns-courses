# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Germanic kingdoms on Rome's ruins build feudalism — fiefs, manors and serfs — while towns and royal power slowly grow back in the later Middle Ages
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Medieval world the dark age and feudalism" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
