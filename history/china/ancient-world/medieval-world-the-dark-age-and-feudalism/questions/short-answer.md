# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 3: Medieval Europe"?
**Answer:** Germanic kingdoms on Rome's ruins build feudalism — fiefs, manors and serfs — while towns and royal power slowly grow back in the later Middle Ages

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 3 guidance: Medieval Europe (west)"?
**Answer:** Guide names the hard point plainly — feudalism is both the lesson's focus and its difficulty: fief-granting, the manor and serfdom, then cities and kings rising against it
