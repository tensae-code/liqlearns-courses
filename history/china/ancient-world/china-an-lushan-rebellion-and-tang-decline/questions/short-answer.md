# China an lushan rebellion and tang decline — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 5: An Lushan rebellion and Tang's fall"?
**Answer:** Eight years of rebellion leave the north 'empty for a thousand li' in Du Fu's poem; warlords carve the empire into fanzhen, Huang Chao's rebels take Chang'an, and Zhu Wen ends Tang in 907

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 6: Tang falls apart"?
**Answer:** After 755 the court's writ shrinks to the capital's shadow while warlords keep the taxes and the armies
