# China an lushan rebellion and tang decline — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Eight years of rebellion leave the north 'empty for a thousand li' in Du Fu's poem; warlords carve the empire into fanzhen, Huang Chao's rebels take Chang'an, and Zhu Wen ends Tang in 907
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China an lushan rebellion and tang decline" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
