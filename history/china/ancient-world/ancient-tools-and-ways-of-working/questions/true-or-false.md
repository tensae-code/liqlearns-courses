# Ancient tools and ways of working — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Stone to bronze to iron, the plough and the waterwheel — each tool rewrites who works, how long, and who eats
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ancient tools and ways of working" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
