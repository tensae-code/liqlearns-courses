# Ancient civilisations the great wall and chinas gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Shen Kuo's Dream Pool Essays record Bi Sheng's movable type — slow for two copies, miraculously fast for ten thousand — as compass and gunpowder spread and sea trade booms
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ancient civilisations the great wall and chinas gifts" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
