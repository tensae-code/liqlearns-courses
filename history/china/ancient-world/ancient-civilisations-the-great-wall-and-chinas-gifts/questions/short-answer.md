# Ancient civilisations the great wall and chinas gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 13: Song-Yuan science and foreign traffic"?
**Answer:** Shen Kuo's Dream Pool Essays record Bi Sheng's movable type — slow for two copies, miraculously fast for ten thousand — as compass and gunpowder spread and sea trade booms

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 2: Chinese culture's significance for the world"?
**Answer:** Paper, printing, gunpowder and the compass travel west; rice, tea and Confucian statecraft shape East Asia — China's gifts, given their own world-history lesson
