# China sui unification grand canal and keju — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yang Jian reunifies China in 589 after centuries of division; the Grand Canal links five river systems over 2,700 km and the keju examinations open office to talent, but Yangdi's palaces, dragon boats and three Goguryeo campaigns drive people to cut off their own hands to escape service, and he is killed in 618
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China sui unification grand canal and keju" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
