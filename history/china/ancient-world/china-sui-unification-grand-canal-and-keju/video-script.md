# Video script — China sui unification grand canal and keju

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: China sui unification grand canal and keju.

Yang Jian reunifies China in 589 after centuries of division; the Grand Canal links five river systems over 2,700 km and the keju examinations open office to talent, but Yangdi's palaces, dragon boats and three Goguryeo campaigns drive people to cut off their own hands to escape service, and he is killed in 618

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
