# The rise and expansion of islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Islam unites Arabia and the Arab empire becomes the bridge carrying goods and learning between East and West
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The rise and expansion of islam" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
