# China northern song centralisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 6: Northern Song politics"?
**Answer:** Fearing another warlord age, Song Taizu invites his generals to wine and persuades them to trade their armies for estates — 'a cup of wine releases military power' — then splits command so generals never own their troops

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 9: Song politics and military"?
**Answer:** Song's founders trade military glory for civil rule — strong trunk, weak branches — gaining order and losing armies that can win
