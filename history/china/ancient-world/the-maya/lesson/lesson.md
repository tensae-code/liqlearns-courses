## Lesson 5: Ancient Americas — Maya

The Maya raise stepped temple-pyramids like Kukulkan's, a feathered-serpent temple whose base runs 55 metres to a side, and keep their festivals by an exact calendar

## Lesson 5 guidance: Ancient Americas

Guide corrects the textbook myth directly — Maya pyramids are temple platforms, not pharaohs' tombs; at Kukulkan's temple the feathered serpent is the sun god, and crowds still gather at the equinox rites

## Lesson 5: Cultures of South Asia, East Asia and the Americas (2)

Maya calendars, Aztec lakeside cities and Inca roads — whole civilisations raised in isolation from the old world
