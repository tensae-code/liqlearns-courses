# The maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 5: Ancient Americas — Maya"?
**Answer:** The Maya raise stepped temple-pyramids like Kukulkan's, a feathered-serpent temple whose base runs 55 metres to a side, and keep their festivals by an exact calendar

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 5 guidance: Ancient Americas"?
**Answer:** Guide corrects the textbook myth directly — Maya pyramids are temple platforms, not pharaohs' tombs; at Kukulkan's temple the feathered serpent is the sun god, and crowds still gather at the equinox rites
