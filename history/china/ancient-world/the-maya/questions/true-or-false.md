# The maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Maya raise stepped temple-pyramids like Kukulkan's, a feathered-serpent temple whose base runs 55 metres to a side, and keep their festivals by an exact calendar
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The maya" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
