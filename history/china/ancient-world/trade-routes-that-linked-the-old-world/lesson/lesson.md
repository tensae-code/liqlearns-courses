As empires stabilise the routes, goods, religions and technologies begin their long walk between East and West

## Lesson 4: Zhang Qian opens the Western Regions

Captured by the Xiongnu for ten years, Zhang Qian still completes his mission, and the Silk Road carries silk west and glass, music and Buddhism east

## Lesson 11: The Silk Road as statecraft

Zhang Qian's mission is diplomacy before it is trade; the road binds Han China to Central Asia's peoples

## Lesson 7: Ancient commerce and trade

## Lesson 9: Ancient trade routes, trade and cultural exchange

The Silk Road carries Buddhism in and glassware back; the sea routes bind Canton to Baghdad — goods are culture's couriers
