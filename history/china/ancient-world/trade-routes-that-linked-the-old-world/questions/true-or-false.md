# Trade routes that linked the old world — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The blind monk Jianzhen fails five times and loses 36 companions before his sixth voyage reaches Japan in 754, carrying Buddhism, medicine, calligraphy and architecture; Silla students like Choe Chiwon pass Tang examinations and carry Tang learning home
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Trade routes that linked the old world" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
