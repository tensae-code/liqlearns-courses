# China culture from three kingdoms to sui tang — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Buddhism localises, Daoism organises, Wang Xizhi's calligraphy and the Tang poets make a cosmopolitan high culture on the Silk Road's edge
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China culture from three kingdoms to sui tang" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
