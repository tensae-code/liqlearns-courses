# China warring states reforms and hundred schools — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 2: Feudal lords contend, reform movements rise"?
**Answer:** Shang Yang leaves Wei for Qin in 356 BCE preaching 'Tang and Wu did not follow the ancients and became kings'; his reforms reward farming and war and make Qin rich and feared — and Qin executes him anyway, but keeps his laws

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 2: The Hundred Schools"?
**Answer:** Amid the chaos Confucius, Laozi, Mozi, Han Fei and the rest argue rival answers to one question — how shall the world be ordered again?
