# China warring states reforms and hundred schools — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Shang Yang leaves Wei for Qin in 356 BCE preaching 'Tang and Wu did not follow the ancients and became kings'; his reforms reward farming and war and make Qin rich and feared — and Qin executes him anyway, but keeps his laws
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China warring states reforms and hundred schools" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
