# Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cyrus's Persia binds three continents with royal roads and a post system, the first great land empire of the ancient world
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
