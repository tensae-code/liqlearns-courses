# China traditional culture essence and character — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every spring the Western Zhou king ploughs and prays at the Altar of Land and Grain — sheji — until the altar's name becomes the word for the state itself; agriculture-first, family and harmony are read out of such rites
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China traditional culture essence and character" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
