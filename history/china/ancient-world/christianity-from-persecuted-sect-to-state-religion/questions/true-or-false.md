# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Christianity, born in West Asia, becomes Europe's binding faith and carries its culture through the medieval centuries
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
