# Ancient civilisations china — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 8 Lesson 2: Tracing the origins of Chinese civilisation"?
**Answer:** Banpo pottery bears the first carved signs, Longshan eggshell-black pottery turns 'thin as paper', Liangzhu tombs yield jade cong for a lord who held army and religion together, and walled towns of 5,000 years ago grow drains, quarters and class

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 1: Early states"?
**Answer:** Neolithic villages multiply into a 'sky full of stars' of cultures, then Xia, Shang and Western Zhou build the first states with bronze, oracle bones and enfeoffment
