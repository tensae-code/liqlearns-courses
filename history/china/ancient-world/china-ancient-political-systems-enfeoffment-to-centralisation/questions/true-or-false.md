# China ancient political systems enfeoffment to centralisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At a birthday banquet in 213 BCE a guest praises the First Emperor for replacing lords with commanderies; another begs him to restore the old enfeoffments — Li Si wins the argument, and centralised government is fixed for two thousand years
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China ancient political systems enfeoffment to centralisation" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
