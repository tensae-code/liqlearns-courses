# China korea joint resistance to hideyoshi 1592 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Toyotomi Hideyoshi sends 200,000 men into Korea; Korea begs Ming aid, Ming general Deng Zilong and Korean admiral Yi Sun-sin die fighting side by side, and after seven years China and Korea together throw the invasion back
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China korea joint resistance to hideyoshi 1592" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
