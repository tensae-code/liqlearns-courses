# China mongol rise and yuan founding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Temujin unites the steppe in 1206 as Genghis Khan, dies at Liupan Mountain leaving orders to borrow Song's road against Jin; Mongke falls at Hezhou, yet in 1234 Jin falls and a 40-year war with Southern Song begins
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China mongol rise and yuan founding" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
