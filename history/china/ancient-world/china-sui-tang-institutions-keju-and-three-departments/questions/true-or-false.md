# China sui tang institutions keju and three departments — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The three departments check one another's drafts, keju examinations let a poor student rise by his brush, and the two-tax reform rewrites the state's bargain with the peasant
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China sui tang institutions keju and three departments" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
