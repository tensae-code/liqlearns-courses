# China qin unification and the first emperor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 3: Qin builds the unified state"?
**Answer:** Li Si's memorial stops the expulsion of foreign advisers, Ying Zheng conquers the six states by 221 BCE, and the First Emperor standardises script, coinage, weights and axle-widths and rules through commanderies

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 3: Burning books, burying scholars, and Qin's fall"?
**Answer:** On Li Si's advice the First Emperor burns the histories of the other states and buries scholars alive; within four years of his death peasant rebels and Xiang Yu and Liu Bang tear the empire down
