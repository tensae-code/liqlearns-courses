# China qin unification and the first emperor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Li Si's memorial stops the expulsion of foreign advisers, Ying Zheng conquers the six states by 221 BCE, and the First Emperor standardises script, coinage, weights and axle-widths and rules through commanderies
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China qin unification and the first emperor" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
