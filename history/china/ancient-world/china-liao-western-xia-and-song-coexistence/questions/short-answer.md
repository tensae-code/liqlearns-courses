# China liao western xia and song coexistence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 7: Liao, Western Xia and Northern Song side by side"?
**Answer:** Khitan nomads build Liao, the Tangut Yuanhao proclaims Western Xia in 1038 with its own script, coinage and law, and three states share the map with Song

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 10: Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (1)"?
