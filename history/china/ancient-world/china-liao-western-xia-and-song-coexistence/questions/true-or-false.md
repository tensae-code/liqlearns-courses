# China liao western xia and song coexistence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Khitan nomads build Liao, the Tangut Yuanhao proclaims Western Xia in 1038 with its own script, coinage and law, and three states share the map with Song
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China liao western xia and song coexistence" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
