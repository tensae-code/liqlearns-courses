# Peace development and a community of shared future — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book closes by arguing humanity's shared problems — war, poverty, climate — admit only shared answers
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Peace development and a community of shared future" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
