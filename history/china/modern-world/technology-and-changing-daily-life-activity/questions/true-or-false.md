# Technology and changing daily life activity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils document one technology that changed their own family's daily life and present it as social history
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Technology and changing daily life activity" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
