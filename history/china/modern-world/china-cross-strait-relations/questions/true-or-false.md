# China cross strait relations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Separated families in 1987 finally cross to visit kin after 38 years, and trade, flights and talks slowly re-stitch the two shores
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China cross strait relations" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
