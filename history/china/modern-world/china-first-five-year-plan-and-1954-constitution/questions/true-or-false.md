# China first five year plan and 1954 constitution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The First Five-Year Plan raises Anshan steel and the first Liberation trucks, and in 1954 the first National People's Congress adopts the new state's first constitution
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China first five year plan and 1954 constitution" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
