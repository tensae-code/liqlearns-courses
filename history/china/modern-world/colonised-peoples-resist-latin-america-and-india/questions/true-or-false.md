# Colonised peoples resist latin america and india — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** As the powers carve up the world, colonised peoples rise — Latin America wins independence and Asia and Africa resist
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Colonised peoples resist latin america and india" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
