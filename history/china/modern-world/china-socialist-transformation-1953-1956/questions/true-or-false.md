# China socialist transformation 1953 1956 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Farming, handicrafts and capitalist industry and commerce are guided step by step into public and collective ownership, completing the socialist transformation by 1956
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China socialist transformation 1953 1956" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
