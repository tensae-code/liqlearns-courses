# Workers movement and the birth of marxism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Factory workers organise and strike for their rights, and Marx and Engels give their struggle a theory and a programme
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Workers movement and the birth of marxism" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
