# Workers movement and the birth of marxism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 7 Lesson 2: Workers' movements and Marxism is born"?
**Answer:** Factory workers organise and strike for their rights, and Marx and Engels give their struggle a theory and a programme

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 11: Marxism is born and spreads"?
**Answer:** Marx and Engels publish the Communist Manifesto in 1848, the First International and the Paris Commune carry the workers' cause forward
