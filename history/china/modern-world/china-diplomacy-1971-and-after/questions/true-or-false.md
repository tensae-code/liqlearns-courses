# China diplomacy 1971 and after — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1971 Qiao Guanhua laughs as China's UN seat is restored, Nixon lands in Beijing in 1972, and China steps onto the world's main stage
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China diplomacy 1971 and after" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
