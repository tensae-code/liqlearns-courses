# China national defence modernisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The PLA grows from 'millet plus rifles' to missiles, carriers and peacekeeping blue helmets
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China national defence modernisation" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
