# The renaissance what it was — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dante, 'last poet of the Middle Ages and first poet of the new age', walks Hell, Purgatory and Heaven in the Divine Comedy and puts a corrupt pope head-down in a rock hole
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The renaissance what it was" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
