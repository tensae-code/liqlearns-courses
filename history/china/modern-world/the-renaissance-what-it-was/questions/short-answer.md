# The renaissance what it was — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 6 Lesson 1: Out of the medieval twilight"?
**Answer:** Dante, 'last poet of the Middle Ages and first poet of the new age', walks Hell, Purgatory and Heaven in the Divine Comedy and puts a corrupt pope head-down in a rock hole

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 8: Europe's liberation of thought — Renaissance"?
**Answer:** Italian cities recover Greece and Rome and put man, not God, at the centre of art and learning
