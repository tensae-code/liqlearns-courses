# China xinhai revolution 1911 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Qing sells the railways, Sichuan rises to protect them, an accidental bomb in Wuchang starts the revolution on 10 October 1911, 14 provinces secede in two months, and Sun Yat-sen takes office on New Year's Day 1912 — but the fruits fall to Yuan Shikai
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China xinhai revolution 1911" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
