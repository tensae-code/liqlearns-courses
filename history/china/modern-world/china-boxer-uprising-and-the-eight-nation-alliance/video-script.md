This lesson tells one story: China boxer uprising and the eight nation alliance.

The Boxers' spirit-boxing meets the Eight-Nation Alliance's guns in Beijing, and the 1901 protocol's indemnity mortgages China's customs for a generation

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
