# China boxer uprising and the eight nation alliance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Boxers' spirit-boxing meets the Eight-Nation Alliance's guns in Beijing, and the 1901 protocol's indemnity mortgages China's customs for a generation
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China boxer uprising and the eight nation alliance" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
