# China early qing literature and art — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dream of the Red Chamber traces Baoyu and Daiyu through the rise and fall of four great houses — banned by rulers, unstoppable among readers, 700 named characters strong
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China early qing literature and art" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
