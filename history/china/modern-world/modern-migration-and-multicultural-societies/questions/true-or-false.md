# Modern migration and multicultural societies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ellis Island to today's refugee routes; receiving countries argue over the multicultural bargain their new citizens are offered
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Modern migration and multicultural societies" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
