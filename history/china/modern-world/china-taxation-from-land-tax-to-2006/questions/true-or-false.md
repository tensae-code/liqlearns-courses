# China taxation from land tax to 2006 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 2006 China abolishes the agricultural tax outright — ending a levy older than the empire itself — while income tax, born in Britain in 1799, arrives only in 1980
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China taxation from land tax to 2006" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
