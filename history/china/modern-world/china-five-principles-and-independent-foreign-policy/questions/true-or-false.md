# China five principles and independent foreign policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zhou Enlai carries the Five Principles of Peaceful Coexistence to Bandung in 1955, seeking common ground while reserving differences
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China five principles and independent foreign policy" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
