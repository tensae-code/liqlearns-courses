# China socialism with chinese characteristics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Deng's 1992 Southern Tour talks relaunch reform, and successive leaderships carry the theory from Deng Xiaoping Theory through to the new era
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China socialism with chinese characteristics" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
