# China modern political systems republic to peoples congress — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1912 republic, warlord constitutions, then 1954's People's Congress system — three answers in forty years to who rules China
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China modern political systems republic to peoples congress" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
