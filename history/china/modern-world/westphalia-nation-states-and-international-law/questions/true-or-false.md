# Westphalia nation states and international law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Westphalia 1648 invents sovereign equals, Grotius writes the law of war and peace, and the nation-state becomes the world's default container
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Westphalia nation states and international law" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
