# China land reform 1950 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Land reform teams carry the 1950 Land Reform Law into every village, and some 300 million peasants receive the fields their families had rented for generations
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China land reform 1950" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
