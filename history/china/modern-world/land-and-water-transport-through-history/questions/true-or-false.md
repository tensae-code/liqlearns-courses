# Land and water transport through history — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Courier roads, the Grand Canal's grain boats, steamships and railways — each network shrinks its world
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Land and water transport through history" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
