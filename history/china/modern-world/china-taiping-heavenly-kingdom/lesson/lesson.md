## Lesson 17: Seeking a way out (1) — Taiping

Failed examination candidate Hong Xiuquan preaches a heavenly kingdom, takes Nanjing and holds half of China for 14 years before the gentry armies crush him

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of China taiping heavenly kingdom as China's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
