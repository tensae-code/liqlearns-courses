# China taiping heavenly kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Failed examination candidate Hong Xiuquan preaches a heavenly kingdom, takes Nanjing and holds half of China for 14 years before the gentry armies crush him
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China taiping heavenly kingdom" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
