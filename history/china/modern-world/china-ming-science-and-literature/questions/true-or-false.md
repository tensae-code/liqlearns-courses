# China ming science and literature — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Li Shizhen's Compendium of Materia Medica records 1,800 drugs and 11,000 prescriptions and travels the world in translation, while Song Yingxing's Tiangong Kaiwu pictures 18 trades at work
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China ming science and literature" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
