# China ming science and literature — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 16: Ming science, building and literature"?
**Answer:** Li Shizhen's Compendium of Materia Medica records 1,800 drugs and 11,000 prescriptions and travels the world in translation, while Song Yingxing's Tiangong Kaiwu pictures 18 trades at work

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 5 Lesson 2: Ming-Qing culture develops"?
**Answer:** The Ming-Qing cultural flowering — science, novels and art of a society rich in goods but ruled ever more tightly
