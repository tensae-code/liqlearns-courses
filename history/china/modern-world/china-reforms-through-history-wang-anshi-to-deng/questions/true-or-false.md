# China reforms through history wang anshi to deng — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Wang Anshi's failed New Policies, Zhang Juzheng's Single Whip, the 1898 hundred days and 1978's reform and opening — the same drama in four costumes
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China reforms through history wang anshi to deng" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
