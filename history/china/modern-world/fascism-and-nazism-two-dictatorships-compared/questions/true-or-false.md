# Fascism and nazism two dictatorships compared — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hitler's Germany and Mussolini's Italy arm, annex and invade while the democracies appease
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Fascism and nazism two dictatorships compared" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
