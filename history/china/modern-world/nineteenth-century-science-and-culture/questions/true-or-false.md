# Nineteenth century science and culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Darwin's evolution, the great physicists and chemists, and the novelists and artists who remade how people saw nature and society
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Nineteenth century science and culture" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
