# China early qing economy and craft workshops — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Maize and sweet potato conquer the hills until 'when Huguang ripens, the world is fed'; Jiangning weavers own 500 looms each and Foshan's cotton workshops employ 50,000
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China early qing economy and craft workshops" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
