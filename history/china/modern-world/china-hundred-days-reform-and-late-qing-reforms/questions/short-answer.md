# China hundred days reform and late qing reforms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 8 Lesson 3: Late Qing attempts to reform and become strong"?
**Answer:** Reformers around the young Guangxu emperor try in 1898 to remake schools, offices and the army in a hundred days, and the court later abolishes the thousand-year keju examinations themselves

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 18: The struggle to save the nation (1) — 1898"?
**Answer:** Kang Youwei and the Guangxu emperor issue a hundred days of reform edicts until Cixi's coup locks the emperor on an island and executes six reformers
