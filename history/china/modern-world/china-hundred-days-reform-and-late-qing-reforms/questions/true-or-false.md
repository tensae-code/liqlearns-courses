# China hundred days reform and late qing reforms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Reformers around the young Guangxu emperor try in 1898 to remake schools, offices and the army in a hundred days, and the court later abolishes the thousand-year keju examinations themselves
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China hundred days reform and late qing reforms" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
