# Modern technology and human society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Electricity, the computer and the internet each dissolve one world and assemble another within a single lifetime
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Modern technology and human society" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
