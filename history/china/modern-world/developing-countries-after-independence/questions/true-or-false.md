# Developing countries after independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Newly independent nations build industries and regional blocs and demand a fairer world economic order
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Developing countries after independence" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
