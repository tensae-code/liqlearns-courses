# Media and how culture travels — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Travellers leave poems carved on Mount Tai, Song-Yuan silk and porcelain sail from Quanzhou port while spices and jewels sail in, and culture rides trade, migration and schooling down to today's media
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Media and how culture travels" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
