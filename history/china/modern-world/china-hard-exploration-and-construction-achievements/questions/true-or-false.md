# China hard exploration and construction achievements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The young republic stumbles through the Great Leap and commune years yet still builds Daqing oilfield, its first atomic and hydrogen bombs and a satellite, carried by model workers and scientists
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China hard exploration and construction achievements" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
