## Lesson 6: Hard exploration and construction achievements

The young republic stumbles through the Great Leap and commune years yet still builds Daqing oilfield, its first atomic and hydrogen bombs and a satellite, carried by model workers and scientists

## Lesson 27: Socialist construction twists and turns

The Eighth Congress's good start, then the Great Leap, communes and the Cultural Revolution's decade — against Daqing, the bombs and the satellite
