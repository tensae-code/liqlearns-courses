# Socialism development and setbacks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Socialist states reform and stall, and the Eastern European changes and Soviet collapse of 1989–91 end the socialist camp in Europe
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Socialism development and setbacks" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
