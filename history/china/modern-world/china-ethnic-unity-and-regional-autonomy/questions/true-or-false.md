# China ethnic unity and regional autonomy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** 56 peoples under regional autonomy — Tibet's serfs emancipated, border regions schooled and doctored — presented as one family
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China ethnic unity and regional autonomy" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
