# China qing autocracy junji chu and literary inquisition — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Grand Council's ministers may only copy the emperor's words, never add their own; under Kangxi, Yongzheng and Qianlong the literary inquisition kills 70 people over a history book's dating and a poet over one ambiguous line
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China qing autocracy junji chu and literary inquisition" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
