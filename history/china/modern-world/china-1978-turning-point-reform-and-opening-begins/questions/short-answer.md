# China 1978 turning point reform and opening begins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 7: The great historical turning point"?
**Answer:** A May 1978 newspaper article, 'Practice is the sole criterion of truth', breaks the Gang of Four's mental shackles; Deng Xiaoping backs it, and in December the Third Plenary Session ends 'class struggle as the key link' and turns the whole country toward modernisation

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 8: Economic system reform"?
**Answer:** Xiaogang's farmers secretly divide the collective land by household fingerprint contract, harvests leap, and the household responsibility system sweeps rural China before reform enters the cities
