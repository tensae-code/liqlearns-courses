# China 1978 turning point reform and opening begins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A May 1978 newspaper article, 'Practice is the sole criterion of truth', breaks the Gang of Four's mental shackles; Deng Xiaoping backs it, and in December the Third Plenary Session ends 'class struggle as the key link' and turns the whole country toward modernisation
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China 1978 turning point reform and opening begins" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
