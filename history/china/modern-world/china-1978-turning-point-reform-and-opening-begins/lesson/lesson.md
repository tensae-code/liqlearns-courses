# China 1978 turning point reform and opening begins

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "China 1978 turning point reform and opening begins" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Lesson 7: The great historical turning point

A May 1978 newspaper article, 'Practice is the sole criterion of truth', breaks the Gang of Four's mental shackles; Deng Xiaoping backs it, and in December the Third Plenary Session ends 'class struggle as the key link' and turns the whole country toward modernisation

## Lesson 8: Economic system reform

Xiaogang's farmers secretly divide the collective land by household fingerprint contract, harvests leap, and the household responsibility system sweeps rural China before reform enters the cities

## Lesson 9: Opening to the outside

Shenzhen grows from a border fishing town into a skyscraper special economic zone, and opening widens from four zones to the whole coast and interior

## Lesson 28: The road of socialism with Chinese characteristics opens

1978's turn, the special zones, the market's rehabilitation and Deng's Southern Tour relaunch the whole project
