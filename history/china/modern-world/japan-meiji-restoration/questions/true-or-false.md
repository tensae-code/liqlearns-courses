# Japan meiji restoration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Lower samurai, ruined by gold outflows and Tokugawa failure, overthrow the shogunate in 1868, restore the boy emperor, abolish the domains, conscript an army and chase industry under 'enrich the country, strengthen the army'
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Japan meiji restoration" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
