# China founding of the prc 1949 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 1: The PRC is founded"?
**Answer:** In September 1949 the CPPCC elects Mao Zedong chairman, renames Beiping Beijing, adopts the March of the Volunteers as anthem and the five-star flag, and on 1 October the new state is proclaimed at Tiananmen

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 26: The PRC founded"?
**Answer:** The CPPCC, the Common Programme and 1 October 1949 open the new state, and land reform and the Korean War secure it
