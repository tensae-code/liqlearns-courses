# China founding of the prc 1949 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In September 1949 the CPPCC elects Mao Zedong chairman, renames Beiping Beijing, adopts the March of the Volunteers as anthem and the five-star flag, and on 1 October the new state is proclaimed at Tiananmen
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China founding of the prc 1949" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
