# The first world war versailles and the results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The victors at Versailles punish Germany and divide spoils, and at Washington the Nine-Power Treaty reopens China to all the powers at once
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The first world war versailles and the results" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
