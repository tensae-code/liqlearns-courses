# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Copernicus to Newton remakes the heavens, and the philosophes arm the coming revolutions with reason, rights and the separation of powers
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Scientific revolution and enlightenment" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
