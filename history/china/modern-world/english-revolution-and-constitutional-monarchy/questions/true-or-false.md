# English revolution and constitutional monarchy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Charles I storms Parliament to arrest its leaders and starts a civil war; Cromwell wins it, Charles is executed in 1649, and after the bloodless Glorious Revolution of 1688 the Bill of Rights makes Parliament supreme — the world's first constitutional monarchy
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "English revolution and constitutional monarchy" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
