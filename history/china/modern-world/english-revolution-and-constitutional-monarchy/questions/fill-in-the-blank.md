# English revolution and constitutional monarchy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** This lesson is called "____".
**Answer:** English revolution and constitutional monarchy

**2.** In "Unit 6 Lesson 3: Bourgeois revolution — England", the story turns on: ____.
**Answer:** Charles I storms Parliament to arrest its leaders and starts a civil war; Cromwell wins it, Charles is executed in 1649, and after the bloodless Glorious Revolution of 1688 the Bill of Rights makes Parliament supreme — the world's first constitutional monarchy
