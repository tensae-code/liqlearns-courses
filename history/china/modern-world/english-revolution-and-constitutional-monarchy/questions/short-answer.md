# English revolution and constitutional monarchy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 6 Lesson 3: Bourgeois revolution — England"?
**Answer:** Charles I storms Parliament to arrest its leaders and starts a civil war; Cromwell wins it, Charles is executed in 1649, and after the bloodless Glorious Revolution of 1688 the Bill of Rights makes Parliament supreme — the world's first constitutional monarchy

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 9: Bourgeois revolutions — England"?
**Answer:** Parliament executes a king, survives Cromwell and a restoration, and in 1688–89 settles power in Parliament for good
