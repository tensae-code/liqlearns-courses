# China science and culture achievements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yuan Longping's hybrid rice feeds millions more, Tu Youyou's artemisinin saves malaria patients worldwide, and Shenzhou spacecraft carry Chinese astronauts into orbit
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China science and culture achievements" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
