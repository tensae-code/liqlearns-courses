# China nanjing government and the long march — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Chiang rules from Nanjing while the Communists, encircled, break out on the Long March — 25,000 li to Yan'an — and invent rural revolution
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China nanjing government and the long march" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
