# China first to open eyes to the world — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils meet Lin Zexu and Wei Yuan, who first translated the world's geography and urged learning the foreigners' strengths to master the foreigners
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China first to open eyes to the world" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
