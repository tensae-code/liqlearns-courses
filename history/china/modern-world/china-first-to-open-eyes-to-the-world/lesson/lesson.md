# China first to open eyes to the world

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "China first to open eyes to the world" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Unit 8 Inquiry: The first modern Chinese to 'open their eyes to the world'

Pupils meet Lin Zexu and Wei Yuan, who first translated the world's geography and urged learning the foreigners' strengths to master the foreigners

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of China first to open eyes to the world as China's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's story in your own words.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the story happens in real life. Note it.
