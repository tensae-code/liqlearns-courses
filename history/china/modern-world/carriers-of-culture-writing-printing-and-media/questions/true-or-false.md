# Carriers of culture writing printing and media — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From bamboo slips and movable type to the museum and the archive — what survives depends on what can carry it
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Carriers of culture writing printing and media" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
