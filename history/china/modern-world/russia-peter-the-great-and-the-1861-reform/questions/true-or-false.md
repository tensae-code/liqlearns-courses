# Russia peter the great and the 1861 reform — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Peter the Great works incognito in foreign shipyards, builds a new army and a new capital on the Baltic he wins from Sweden, then a later tsar frees the serfs in 1861 to push Russia toward industry
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Russia peter the great and the 1861 reform" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
