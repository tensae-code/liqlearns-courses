# The industrial revolution its two stages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Electricity, steel and oil open industry's second age, heavier and faster than steam
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The industrial revolution its two stages" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
