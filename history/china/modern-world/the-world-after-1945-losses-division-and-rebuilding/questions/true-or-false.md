# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yalta's victors draw the new map, found the United Nations and split into two camps
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
