# China changing everyday life 1950s to today — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the 1950s–70s families buy grain, cloth and bicycles only with ration coupons; the 'three big items' change from sewing machine, bicycle and watch to TV, fridge and washer, then to house, car and insurance
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China changing everyday life 1950s to today" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
