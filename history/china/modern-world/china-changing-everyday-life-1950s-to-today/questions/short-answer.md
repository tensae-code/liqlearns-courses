# China changing everyday life 1950s to today — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 7 Lesson 3: Stories of life"?
**Answer:** In the 1950s–70s families buy grain, cloth and bicycles only with ration coupons; the 'three big items' change from sewing machine, bicycle and watch to TV, fridge and washer, then to house, car and insurance

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 19: Social life changes"?
**Answer:** Wardrobes turn from blue-grey uniformity to fashion, ration coupons become museum pieces, and trains give way to high-speed rail and mobile payment
