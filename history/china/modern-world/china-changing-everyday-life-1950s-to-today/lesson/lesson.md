# China changing everyday life 1950s to today

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "China changing everyday life 1950s to today" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Unit 7 Lesson 3: Stories of life

In the 1950s–70s families buy grain, cloth and bicycles only with ration coupons; the 'three big items' change from sewing machine, bicycle and watch to TV, fridge and washer, then to house, car and insurance

## Lesson 19: Social life changes

Wardrobes turn from blue-grey uniformity to fashion, ration coupons become museum pieces, and trains give way to high-speed rail and mobile payment

## Say it to your companion

Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
