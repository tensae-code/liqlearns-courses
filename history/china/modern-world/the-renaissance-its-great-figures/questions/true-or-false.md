# The renaissance its great figures — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Leonardo paints the Mona Lisa's inner life with gesture and pose, breaking the medieval church's grip on the human person
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The renaissance its great figures" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
