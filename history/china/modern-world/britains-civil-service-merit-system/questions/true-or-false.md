# Britains civil service merit system — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain, stung by Crimean chaos and impressed by China's examinations, builds a neutral merit civil service that America copies
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Britains civil service merit system" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
