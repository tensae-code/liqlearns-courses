# China liberation war 1946 1949 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Outnumbered four to one in 1946, the PLA wins the three great campaigns of 1948–49, crosses the Yangtze and takes Nanjing
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China liberation war 1946 1949" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
