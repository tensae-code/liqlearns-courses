# Information revolution and cultural sharing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils present how digital networks let a village craft or a local song reach the world in a day
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Information revolution and cultural sharing" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
