# World wars and cultural collision — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two world wars mix peoples on battlefields and in exile, and anti-colonial movements forge new national cultures from the collision
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "World wars and cultural collision" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
