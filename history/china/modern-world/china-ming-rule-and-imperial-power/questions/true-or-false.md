# China ming rule and imperial power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zhu Yuanzhang, blaming Yuan's fall on weak centre, abolishes the chancellorship forever, splits provinces into three offices that cannot command each other, and makes the six ministries answer to the emperor alone
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China ming rule and imperial power" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
