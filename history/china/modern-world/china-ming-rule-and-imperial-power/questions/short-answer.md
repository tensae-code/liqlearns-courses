# China ming rule and imperial power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 14: Ming rule"?
**Answer:** Zhu Yuanzhang, blaming Yuan's fall on weak centre, abolishes the chancellorship forever, splits provinces into three offices that cannot command each other, and makes the six ministries answer to the emperor alone

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 5 Lesson 1: The great unified state rises"?
**Answer:** Zhu Yuanzhang abolishes the chancellorship, creates the palace guard and secret-police factories and moves the capital to Beijing under Zhu Di, while Zheng He's voyages open the sea road to Asia and Africa
