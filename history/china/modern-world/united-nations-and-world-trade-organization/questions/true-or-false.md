# United nations and world trade organization — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The United Nations and the world trading system give states a table instead of a battlefield
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "United nations and world trade organization" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
