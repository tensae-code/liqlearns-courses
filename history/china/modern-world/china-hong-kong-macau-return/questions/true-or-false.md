# China hong kong macau return — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Deng's 'one country, two systems' opens the road; at 23:59 on 30 June 1997 the British flag lowers in silence, and at 0:00 the five-star flag and bauhinia flag rise to the anthem as cameras catch the tears — Macau follows in 1999
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China hong kong macau return" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
