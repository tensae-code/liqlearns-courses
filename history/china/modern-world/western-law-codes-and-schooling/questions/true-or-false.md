# Western law codes and schooling — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Napoleon's code, jury trials and compulsory schooling spread a citizen-making package from Europe to the world
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Western law codes and schooling" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
