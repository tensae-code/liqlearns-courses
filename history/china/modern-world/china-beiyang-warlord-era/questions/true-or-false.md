# China beiyang warlord era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yuan's monarchy farce, his death, and a decade of cliques at war while national industry enjoys its brief golden age and new culture stirs
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China beiyang warlord era" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
