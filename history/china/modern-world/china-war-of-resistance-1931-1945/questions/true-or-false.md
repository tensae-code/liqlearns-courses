# China war of resistance 1931 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mukden 1931, Shanghai 1932, Lugou Bridge 1937 — piecemeal resistance hardens into a united national front
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China war of resistance 1931 1945" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
