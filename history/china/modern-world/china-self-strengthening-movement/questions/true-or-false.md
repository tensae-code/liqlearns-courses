# China self strengthening movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Beaten officials learn Western arms and machines, founding arsenals, shipyards and China's first modern national industries
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China self strengthening movement" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
