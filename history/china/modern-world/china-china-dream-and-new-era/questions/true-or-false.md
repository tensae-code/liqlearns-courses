# China china dream and new era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The newest generation inherits the story — national rejuvenation as a relay from 1840's humiliation to today's new era
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China china dream and new era" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
