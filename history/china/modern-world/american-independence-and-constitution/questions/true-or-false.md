# American independence and constitution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** England's parliament, America's 1787 constitution and France's republics turn subjects into citizens on paper
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "American independence and constitution" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
