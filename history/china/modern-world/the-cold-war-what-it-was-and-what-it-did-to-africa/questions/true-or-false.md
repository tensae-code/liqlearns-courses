# The cold war what it was and what it did to africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The wartime allies split into two armed camps, Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan and NATO against the Warsaw Pact
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The cold war what it was and what it did to africa" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
