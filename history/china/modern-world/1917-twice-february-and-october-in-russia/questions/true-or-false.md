# 1917 twice february and october in russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Lenin leads the Bolsheviks through 1917's two revolutions to found the first socialist state
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "1917 twice february and october in russia" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
