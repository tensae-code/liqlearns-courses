# China korean war resist america aid korea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mao argues 'if we let the enemy press to the Yalu, not fighting harms us more'; on the night of 19 October 1950 the Volunteers cross secretly, win their first battle on 25 October, and in five campaigns drive MacArthur — who promised his men home by Christmas — back to the 38th parallel
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China korean war resist america aid korea" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
