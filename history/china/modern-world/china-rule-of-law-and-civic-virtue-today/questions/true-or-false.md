# China rule of law and civic virtue today — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From the 1954 constitution through the civil code of 2020, law and civic-virtue campaigns are built as one system
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China rule of law and civic virtue today" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
