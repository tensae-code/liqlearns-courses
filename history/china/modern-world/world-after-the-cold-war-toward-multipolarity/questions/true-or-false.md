# World after the cold war toward multipolarity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** One superpower remains, but Europe, Japan, China and others pull the world toward many poles amid regional wars
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "World after the cold war toward multipolarity" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
