# China qing consolidation of a multi ethnic state — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zheng Chenggong besieges the Dutch for eight months and in 1662 recovers Taiwan after 38 years, then settles soldiers to farm it and teaches highland peoples new farming — Qing binds Taiwan, Tibet and the northwest into one state
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China qing consolidation of a multi ethnic state" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
