# Money and the world monetary system — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain legislates the gold standard in the 1820s, the world follows, two wars break it, and Bretton Woods crowns the dollar in 1944
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Money and the world monetary system" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
