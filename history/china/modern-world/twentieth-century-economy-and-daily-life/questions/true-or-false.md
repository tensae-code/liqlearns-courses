# Twentieth century economy and daily life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Welfare states, planned economies, consumer societies and China's reform-era kitchens — one century, several rival answers to the good life
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Twentieth century economy and daily life" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
