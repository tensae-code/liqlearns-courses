# China may fourth movement and ccp founding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Versailles gives Shandong to Japan; on 4 May 1919 students march, workers strike, and within two years a dozen communists found a party in Shanghai and on a boat at South Lake
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China may fourth movement and ccp founding" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
