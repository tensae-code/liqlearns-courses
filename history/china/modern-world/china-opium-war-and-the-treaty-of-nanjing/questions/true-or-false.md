# China opium war and the treaty of nanjing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Lin Zexu vows 'if opium is not ended for one day, I do not return for one day'; Britain attacks in 1840, Guan Tianpei dies at his guns, Sanyuanli villagers surround the British in the rain with hoes and knives, and the Treaty of Nanjing cedes Hong Kong, 21 million silver dollars and five treaty ports — China's first unequal treaty
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China opium war and the treaty of nanjing" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
