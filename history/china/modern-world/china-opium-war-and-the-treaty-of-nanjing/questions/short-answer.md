# China opium war and the treaty of nanjing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 8 Lesson 1: National crisis and the people's brave resistance"?
**Answer:** Lin Zexu vows 'if opium is not ended for one day, I do not return for one day'; Britain attacks in 1840, Guan Tianpei dies at his guns, Sanyuanli villagers surround the British in the rain with hoes and knives, and the Treaty of Nanjing cedes Hong Kong, 21 million silver dollars and five treaty ports — China's first unequal treaty

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 16: The two Opium Wars"?
**Answer:** A British writer admits the opium seller 'corrupts, debases and destroys' his victims; 40 warships come in 1840 anyway, the Arrow incident is manufactured in 1856, and the treaties of Nanjing and Beijing open, cede and indemnify China — the start of modern Chinese history
