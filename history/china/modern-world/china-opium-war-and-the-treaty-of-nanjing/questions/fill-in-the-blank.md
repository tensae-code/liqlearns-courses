# China opium war and the treaty of nanjing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** This lesson is called "____".
**Answer:** China opium war and the treaty of nanjing

**2.** In "Unit 8 Lesson 1: National crisis and the people's brave resistance", the story turns on: ____.
**Answer:** Lin Zexu vows 'if opium is not ended for one day, I do not return for one day'; Britain attacks in 1840, Guan Tianpei dies at his guns, Sanyuanli villagers surround the British in the rain with hoes and knives, and the Treaty of Nanjing cedes Hong Kong, 21 million silver dollars and five treaty ports — China's first unequal treaty
