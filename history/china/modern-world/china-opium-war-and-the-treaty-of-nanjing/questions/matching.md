Match each part with its story.

Meanings:
a) Lin Zexu vows 'if opium is not ended for one day, I do not return for one day'; Britain attacks in 1840, Guan Tianpei dies at his guns, Sanyuanli villagers surround the British in the rain with hoes and knives, and the Treaty of Nanjing cedes Hong Kong, 21 million silver dollars and five treaty ports — China's first unequal treaty
