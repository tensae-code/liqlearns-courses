# Video script — China opium war and the treaty of nanjing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: China opium war and the treaty of nanjing.

Lin Zexu vows 'if opium is not ended for one day, I do not return for one day'; Britain attacks in 1840, Guan Tianpei dies at his guns, Sanyuanli villagers surround the British in the rain with hoes and knives, and the Treaty of Nanjing cedes Hong Kong, 21 million silver dollars and five treaty ports — China's first unequal treaty

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
