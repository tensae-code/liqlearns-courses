# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France's revolution storms the old order and proclaims the rights of man across Europe
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The french revolution breaks out 1789" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
