# China fall of ming li zicheng — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Shepherd boy turned soldier Li Zicheng promises 'equal land, no grain tax', keeps strict discipline, feeds the poor, and marches a million men into Xi'an to found Dashun in 1644 as drought and the three surtaxes empty the villages
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China fall of ming li zicheng" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
