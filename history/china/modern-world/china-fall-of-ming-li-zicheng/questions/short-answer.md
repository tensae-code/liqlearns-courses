# China fall of ming li zicheng — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 17: The fall of Ming"?
**Answer:** Shepherd boy turned soldier Li Zicheng promises 'equal land, no grain tax', keeps strict discipline, feeds the poor, and marches a million men into Xi'an to found Dashun in 1644 as drought and the three surtaxes empty the villages

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 5 Lesson 1: Ming falls"?
**Answer:** Eunuchs and land-grabbing lords hollow out late Ming until drought-starved Shaanxi rises; the folk song runs 'kill the cattle and sheep, open the gates, welcome King Chuang — when King Chuang comes, no grain tax'
