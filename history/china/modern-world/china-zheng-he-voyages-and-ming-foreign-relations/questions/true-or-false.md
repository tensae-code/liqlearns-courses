# China zheng he voyages and ming foreign relations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From 1405 to 1433 Zheng He's fleets — 200 ships, 27,000 men, treasure ships carrying a thousand — visit 30 countries to East Africa and the Red Sea, signalling by flags by day and lanterns by night, trading silk and porcelain and bringing 16 nations' envoys home
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China zheng he voyages and ming foreign relations" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
