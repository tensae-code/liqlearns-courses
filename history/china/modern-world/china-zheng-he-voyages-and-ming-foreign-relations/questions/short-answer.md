# China zheng he voyages and ming foreign relations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 15: Ming foreign relations"?
**Answer:** From 1405 to 1433 Zheng He's fleets — 200 ships, 27,000 men, treasure ships carrying a thousand — visit 30 countries to East Africa and the Red Sea, signalling by flags by day and lanterns by night, trading silk and porcelain and bringing 16 nations' envoys home

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 6 Inquiry: Zheng He and Columbus compared"?
**Answer:** Pupils set Zheng He's great treasure fleets of 1405 beside Columbus's three small ships of 1492 and ask why the two voyages led to such different worlds
