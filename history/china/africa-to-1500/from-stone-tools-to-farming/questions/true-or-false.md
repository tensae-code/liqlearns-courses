# From stone tools to farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Hemudu, 7,000-year-old rice grains and stalks show Chinese farmers among the world's first; everywhere, farming trades the forager's freedom for the village, the surplus and civilisation
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "From stone tools to farming" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
