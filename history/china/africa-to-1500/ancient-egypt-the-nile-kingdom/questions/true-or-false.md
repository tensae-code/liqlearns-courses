# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 6,600 km Nile, flowing south to north into the Mediterranean, gives birth to brilliant ancient Egypt
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ancient egypt the nile kingdom" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
