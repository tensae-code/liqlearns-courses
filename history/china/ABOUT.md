# History — China course — ABOUT

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — China's own History books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How the course is organised

One course for China, organised by levels, never grades (see CURRICULUM.md). Lessons sit under title folders named for the placements' own units (`first-steps/`, `ancient-world/`, `modern-world/`, `africa-to-1500/`, `africa-after-1500/`). Every placement in the placements file stocks into exactly one lesson, with exactly one provenance row in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`. Placements at `liqlearns-courses-work/expansion-history-2026-11-15/china-placements.md` (261 placements — the count of lines containing `story beats:`; the lines govern).

Records as filed: 12 records / 12 distinct books / read via grab.py (tree route), cover-verified; the blob-route books are image-only scans, read by page renders and OCR of contents and lesson pages; History parts only placed, other parts of combined books named in headers.

## The way of the books

China's own way, as filed book by book: History taught inside society in the younger combined books — how rules, media and family life changed, then civilisation traced from fossils and potsherds, every unit ending in an inquiry (Book 1); each lesson of the younger Chinese History book opening with a poem or scene, the dynasty told as a story of rulers and turning points, with related-facts and source-reading boxes (Book 2); Chinese and world history woven in one book — Ming-Qing China first, then Renaissance-to-industrial Europe, ending with China's own crisis after 1840 (Book 3); world history as one rising line — resistance, reform, industrialisation, world wars, Cold War, and a peaceful-development future — with a person-scan portrait in nearly every lesson (Book 4); the PRC's story as the nation's own family album, down to the 1997 handover told minute by minute (Book 5); at senior high the whole world story compressed into 23 outline lessons ending in inquiry-and-extension with real scholarly voices pupils must judge (Book 6, with its PARTIAL teacher guide, Book 7, teaching teachers to teach by comparison); one spine for five thousand years of Chinese history, the unified multi-ethnic state as the thread from Qin to Qing, then invasion, revolution and the Party's road (Book 8; Book 9 is its parallel lower-volume companion's second scan — see Honesty notes); and the three selective-compulsory books teaching history as systems (Book 10), from the kitchen and the workshop (Book 11), and as culture itself, receiver and giver across five thousand years (Book 12).
