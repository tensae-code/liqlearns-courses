# The atlantic slave trade scale coasts and the gun cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The new sea routes become a colonial system — American silver and plantations, African bodies in the triangular trade, Asia drawn into Europe's market
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The atlantic slave trade scale coasts and the gun cycle" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
