# Colonisation the partition and fashoda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Africa is partitioned in a generation, Asia is carved into colonies and spheres, and Latin America passes under the United States' shadow
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Colonisation the partition and fashoda" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
