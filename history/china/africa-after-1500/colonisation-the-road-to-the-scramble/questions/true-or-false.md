# Colonisation the road to the scramble — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Spain, Portugal, the Netherlands, France and Britain succeed one another as sea powers while Latin America's colonial order hardens
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Colonisation the road to the scramble" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
