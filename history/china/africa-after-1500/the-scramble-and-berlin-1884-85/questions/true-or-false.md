# The scramble and berlin 1884 85 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** By 1914 the powers hold the whole map; Hobsbawm's 'age of empire' passage and Marx on Britain's double mission in India are set for pupils to judge
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The scramble and berlin 1884 85" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
