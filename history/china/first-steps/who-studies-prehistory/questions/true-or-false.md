# Who studies prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils reconstruct a day in Peking Man's life from fossils, ash heaps and stone tools, and a rebuilt head from a skull
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Who studies prehistory" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
