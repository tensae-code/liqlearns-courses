# Dating the past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils work a year-ruler — Confucius (551–479 BCE), Western Han (202 BCE–9 CE), Han Wudi's era names, the ganzhi cycle that names the 1894 war, centuries and decades
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Dating the past" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
