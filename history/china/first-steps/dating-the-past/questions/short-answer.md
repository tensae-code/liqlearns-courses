# Dating the past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Unit 8 Inquiry: How the past is recorded"?
**Answer:** Pupils work a year-ruler — Confucius (551–479 BCE), Western Han (202 BCE–9 CE), Han Wudi's era names, the ganzhi cycle that names the 1894 war, centuries and decades

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Appendix: Chronology of ancient Chinese history (lower volume)"?
**Answer:** A dated spine from Sui to Qing that pupils use to place every lesson's events in order
