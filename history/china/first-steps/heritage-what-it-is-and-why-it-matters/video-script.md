This lesson tells one story: Heritage what it is and why it matters.

Dragon boats at Duanwu and New Year greetings in Song paintings show festivals gathering farming, ritual, food and astronomy into customs still lived today

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
