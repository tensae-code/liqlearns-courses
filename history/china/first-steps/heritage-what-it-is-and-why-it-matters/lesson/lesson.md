# Heritage what it is and why it matters

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Heritage what it is and why it matters" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Lesson 22 (activity): Origins of China's traditional festivals

Dragon boats at Duanwu and New Year greetings in Song paintings show festivals gathering farming, ritual, food and astronomy into customs still lived today

## Activity lesson: Family-country feeling and the evolution of the unified multi-ethnic state

Pupils trace their own region's place in the long unification story and present it as patriotic heritage work

## Activity lesson: Governing the unified great state in Chinese history

Pupils present how different dynasties held one vast country together, as heritage of statecraft

## Lesson 15: Cultural heritage — the common wealth of all humanity

Dunhuang's caves, looted and guarded, become the argument for world heritage as everyone's inheritance
