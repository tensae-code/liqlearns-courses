# Heritage what it is and why it matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dragon boats at Duanwu and New Year greetings in Song paintings show festivals gathering farming, ritual, food and astronomy into customs still lived today
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Heritage what it is and why it matters" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
