Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "liberation movements and the neocolonial warning" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Africa freed itself through organised anticolonial movements, above all the liberation struggles against Portugal in Angola, Mozambique, Guinea and Cape Verde — but the book warns political sovereignty arrived chained to economic and cultural dependence, "a new modality of colonialism"

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "liberation movements and the neocolonial warning" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
