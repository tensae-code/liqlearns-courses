# From stone tools to farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 22: de la piedra al fuego y la agricultura" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** a 2.5-million-year timeline runs first humans, stone tools, fire (400,000), cave painting (20,000), then agriculture and herding (10,000) — cooking, warmth and light from fire change the species' life

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 22: aportes prehistóricos (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** fire, farming, herding, tools and houses are listed as prehistory's still-used inheritance
