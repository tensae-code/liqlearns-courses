# From stone tools to farming — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 21: de nómadas a sedentarios" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** bands of hunters, fishers and gatherers who know fire, bury their dead and paint caves become, with the discovery of agriculture (maize, beans, potato, yuca) and domesticated dog, turkey, alpaca and llama, settled farming tribes

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 21: sedentarios (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the class hunts evidence of first settlers around its own community and describes what it finds
