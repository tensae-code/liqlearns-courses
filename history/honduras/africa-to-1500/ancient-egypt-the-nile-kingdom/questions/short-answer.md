# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 23: Egipto" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Nile's floods are turned into artificial irrigation; mummification advances anatomy, and hieroglyphs, a 365-day solar calendar, navigation and mathematics follow

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 23: Egipto (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Nile irrigation, mummification and the 365-day calendar are Egypt's charted contributions
