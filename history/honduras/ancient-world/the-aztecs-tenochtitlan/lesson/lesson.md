# The aztecs tenochtitlan

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The aztecs tenochtitlan" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 23: la azteca

a military theocracy whose emperor is also high priest; warrior conquerors of neighbours, pictographic-hieroglyphic writing, a vigesimal system without zero, frequent human sacrifice, schools, and Tenochtitlan as the built proof

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 23: cultura azteca (guide)

the Aztec military theocracy and Tenochtitlan are charted beside Maya and Inca
