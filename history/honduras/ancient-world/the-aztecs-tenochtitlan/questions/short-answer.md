# The aztecs tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 23: la azteca" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a military theocracy whose emperor is also high priest; warrior conquerors of neighbours, pictographic-hieroglyphic writing, a vigesimal system without zero, frequent human sacrifice, schools, and Tenochtitlan as the built proof

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 23: cultura azteca (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Aztec military theocracy and Tenochtitlan are charted beside Maya and Inca
