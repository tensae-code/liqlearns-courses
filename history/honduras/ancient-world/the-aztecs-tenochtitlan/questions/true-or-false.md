# The aztecs tenochtitlan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 23: la azteca", a military theocracy whose emperor is also high priest; warrior conquerors of neighbours, pictographic-hieroglyphic writing, a vigesimal system without zero, frequent human sacrifice, schools, and Tenochtitlan as the built proof (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 23: cultura azteca (guide)", the Aztec military theocracy and Tenochtitlan are charted beside Maya and Inca (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
