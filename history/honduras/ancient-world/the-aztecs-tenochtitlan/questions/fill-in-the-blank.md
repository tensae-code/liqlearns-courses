# The aztecs tenochtitlan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 23: la azteca" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a military theocracy whose emperor is also high priest; warrior conquerors of neighbours, pictographic-hieroglyphic writing, a vigesimal system without zero, frequent human sacrifice, schools, and Tenochtitlan as the built proof

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 23: cultura azteca (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Aztec military theocracy and Tenochtitlan are charted beside Maya and Inca
