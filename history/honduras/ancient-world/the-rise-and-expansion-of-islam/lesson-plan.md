# Lesson plan — The rise and expansion of islam

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** The rise and expansion of islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The world survey: two rival periodisations, prehistory and the river civilisations, Greece and Rome, feudalism and Arab culture, Renaissance, Enlightenment, the French Revolution and both world wars — closing back home with Honduras's civic dates and customs (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The rise and expansion of islam as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — The world survey: two rival periodisations, prehistory and the river civilisations, Greece and Rome, feudalism and Arab culture, Renaissance, Enlightenment, the French Revolution and both world wars — closing back home with Honduras's civic dates and customs — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
