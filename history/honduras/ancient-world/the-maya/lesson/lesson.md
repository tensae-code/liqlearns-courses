# The maya

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The maya" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 25 Pueblos originarios: los primeros pobladores y los mayas

small hunter bands cross into the continent chasing herds and settle at La Esperanza; thousands of years later the Maya flourish at Copán, extend over the west, bring maize farming and advances in astronomy, mathematics and art, and play Pok-ta-pok

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 25 Pueblos originarios (guide)

first hunters at La Esperanza, then Copán's Maya flowering with maize, astronomy and the ball game

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 21 Raíces prehispánicas: Copán

Copán, UNESCO World Heritage, was a Maya city of plazas, stelae, altars and tombs — Acropolis, Ball Court, Hieroglyphic Stairway, Rosalila temple — across the preclassic, classic and postclassic periods

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 21 Raíces prehispánicas: Copán (guide)

Copán's plazas, stelae and Hieroglyphic Stairway are the evidence lesson for Maya development across three periods

## Sub lesson 5 — Lección 22 Población prehispánica de Centroamérica: los mayas

the Maya, oldest and most outstanding, build Tikal, Palenque, Chichén Itzá and Copán as ceremonial cities under king, nobles, priests and warriors; they leave hieroglyphic writing (three surviving codices), the zero, vigesimal numbers and exact calendars, then decline — land exhaustion, pests and climate are the theories offered

## Sub lesson 6 — Lección 22: los mayas (guide)

Maya social pyramid, cities, glyphs, zero and calendars are completed as exercises, ending on the rulers of Copán

## Sub lesson 7 — Lección 23 Principales culturas: la maya

city-states under a hereditary Halach Uinic and a theocratic priesthood, nobles, people and slaves, maize-bean-chile farming, cacao for the elite, 31 Maya languages still a patrimony of humanity

## Sub lesson 8 — Lección 23: cultura maya (guide)

the comparison chart fixes Maya government, economy and the zero against its peers
