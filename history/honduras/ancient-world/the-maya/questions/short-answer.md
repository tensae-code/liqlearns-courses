# The maya — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 22 Población prehispánica de Centroamérica: los mayas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** the Maya, oldest and most outstanding, build Tikal, Palenque, Chichén Itzá and Copán as ceremonial cities under king, nobles, priests and warriors; they leave hieroglyphic writing (three surviving codices), the zero, vigesimal numbers and exact calendars, then decline — land exhaustion, pests and climate are the theories offered

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 22: los mayas (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Maya social pyramid, cities, glyphs, zero and calendars are completed as exercises, ending on the rulers of Copán
