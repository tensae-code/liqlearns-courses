# The maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 25 Pueblos originarios: los primeros pobladores y los mayas"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) small hunter bands cross into the continent chasing herds and settle at La Esperanza; thousands of years later the Maya flourish at Copán, extend over the west, bring maize farming and advances in astronomy, mathematics and art, and play Pok-ta-pok  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
