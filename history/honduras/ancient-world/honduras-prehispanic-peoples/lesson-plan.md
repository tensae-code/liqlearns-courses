# Lesson plan — Honduras prehispanic peoples

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Honduras prehispanic peoples
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Honduras whole in three periods — pre-Hispanic, colonial, independent: Copán and the pre-Hispanic peoples, discovery and conquest, independence, Morazán and the Federation, the Liberal Reform and its enclaves, and democracy to the 2009 crisis (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Honduras prehispanic peoples as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 21: los pueblos antes de 1502** — gatherers, hunters and fishers discover farming and maize; the numerous Lenca practise the Guancasco, Tolupán live in the Yoro mountains, Pech along the Aguán–Coco, Chorotega round the Gulf of Fonseca, and the Maya invent zero and watch the stars
2. **§2 Lección 21: pueblos prehispánicos (guide)** — Maya, Lenca, Tolupán, Pech and Chorotega are placed on the map with their own knowledge, maize farming and rituals such as the Guancasco
3. **§3 first evidence and cultural areas** — human footprints in lava deposits (~10,000 BCE), rock art at Ayasta and Oropolí, and the ancient sites of Playa de los Muertos, Los Naranjos and Yarumela show Honduras peopled long before the Maya, between Mesoamerica and the Intermediate Area
4. **§4 first evidence and cultural areas** — the guide's key: lava footprints, Ayasta rock art, Playa de los Muertos, Los Naranjos and Yarumela prove occupation long before the Maya, between Mesoamerica and the Intermediate Area
5. **§5 Honduras between two areas** — Honduras's pre-Hispanic peoples shared traits with both Mesoamerica and the Intermediate Area, living as chiefdoms and tribes on farming, hunting and fishing, each with its own language
6. **§6 Honduras between two areas** — the guide's key places Honduras's chiefdoms and tribes between Mesoamerica and the Intermediate Area
