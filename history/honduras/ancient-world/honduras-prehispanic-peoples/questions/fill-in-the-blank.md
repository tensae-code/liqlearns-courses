# Honduras prehispanic peoples — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 21: los pueblos antes de 1502" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** gatherers, hunters and fishers discover farming and maize; the numerous Lenca practise the Guancasco, Tolupán live in the Yoro mountains, Pech along the Aguán–Coco, Chorotega round the Gulf of Fonseca, and the Maya invent zero and watch the stars

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 21: pueblos prehispánicos (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Maya, Lenca, Tolupán, Pech and Chorotega are placed on the map with their own knowledge, maize farming and rituals such as the Guancasco
