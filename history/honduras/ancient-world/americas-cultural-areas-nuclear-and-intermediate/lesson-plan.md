**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Americas cultural areas nuclear and intermediate

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 cultural areas of pre-Hispanic America** — the continent is mapped into cultural areas — North American, Caribbean, Mesoamerican, Intermediate (Chibcha, Chorotega), Amazonian, Andean and South-Andean — with two nuclear areas, Mesoamerica and the Andes, whose "mother cultures", Olmec and Chavín, modelled urban civilisations for the rest
2. **§2 cultural areas of pre-Hispanic America** — the guide teaches the cultural-area map with the Mesoamerican and Andean nuclear areas and their Olmec and Chavín mother cultures

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
