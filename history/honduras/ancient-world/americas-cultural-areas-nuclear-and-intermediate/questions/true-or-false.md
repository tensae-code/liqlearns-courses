# Americas cultural areas nuclear and intermediate — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "cultural areas of pre-Hispanic America", the continent is mapped into cultural areas — North American, Caribbean, Mesoamerican, Intermediate (Chibcha, Chorotega), Amazonian, Andean and South-Andean — with two nuclear areas, Mesoamerica and the Andes, whose "mother cultures", Olmec and Chavín, modelled urban civilisations for the rest (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "cultural areas of pre-Hispanic America", the guide teaches the cultural-area map with the Mesoamerican and Andean nuclear areas and their Olmec and Chavín mother cultures (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
