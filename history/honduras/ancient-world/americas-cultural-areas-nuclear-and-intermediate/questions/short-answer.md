# Americas cultural areas nuclear and intermediate — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "cultural areas of pre-Hispanic America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the continent is mapped into cultural areas — North American, Caribbean, Mesoamerican, Intermediate (Chibcha, Chorotega), Amazonian, Andean and South-Andean — with two nuclear areas, Mesoamerica and the Andes, whose "mother cultures", Olmec and Chavín, modelled urban civilisations for the rest

**2.** Tell the story of "cultural areas of pre-Hispanic America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide teaches the cultural-area map with the Mesoamerican and Andean nuclear areas and their Olmec and Chavín mother cultures
