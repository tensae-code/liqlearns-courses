# Americas other prehispanic cultures

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Americas other prehispanic cultures" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 24 Otras culturas: Norteamérica

Atapascans, Algonquins, Californians, Arctic Inuit eating raw meat, matriarchal Iroquois in their confederation leagues, Dakota/Sioux west of the Mississippi — varied peoples the book says never reached the great civilisations' development

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 24: Centroamérica, Antillas y Suramérica

Chorotega middlemen between Maya-Aztec and Andean worlds, gentle Taíno and Siboney first islanders of Cuba, warlike Caribs, Chibcha, Lenca, Pipil, Jicaque, Paya, Miskito — and Amazonian peoples whose rock art records their daily life

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 24 Otras culturas: norte (guide)

Inuit, Iroquois, Sioux and Algonquin are placed on the regional map by colour-coded region

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 24: centro y sur (guide)

Chorotega, Taíno, Carib and Chibcha are characterised in one scheme, then a Honduran group is researched in pairs

## Say it to your companion

Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
