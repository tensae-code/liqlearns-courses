# Americas other prehispanic cultures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 24 Otras culturas: Norteamérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Atapascans, Algonquins, Californians, Arctic Inuit eating raw meat, matriarchal Iroquois in their confederation leagues, Dakota/Sioux west of the Mississippi — varied peoples the book says never reached the great civilisations' development

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 24: Centroamérica, Antillas y Suramérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chorotega middlemen between Maya-Aztec and Andean worlds, gentle Taíno and Siboney first islanders of Cuba, warlike Caribs, Chibcha, Lenca, Pipil, Jicaque, Paya, Miskito — and Amazonian peoples whose rock art records their daily life
