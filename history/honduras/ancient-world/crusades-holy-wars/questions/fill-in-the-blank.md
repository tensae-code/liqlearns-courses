# Crusades holy wars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "two centuries of crusading" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pope Urban II launched the campaigns to recover the Holy Land, but over 200 years crusades were also waged against pagan Slavs, Jews, Orthodox Christians, Mongols, Cathars, Hussites, Waldensians, Prussians and the popes' political enemies, their soldiers earning indulgences

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "two centuries of crusading" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide teaches Urban II's call and the crusades' widening targets over 200 years
