# Honduras first peoples caves and origins — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 37 Origen de la población" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** bone tools and stone artefacts in the Cueva del Gigante (Marcala) date Honduras's origins to millennia before Christ; Yarumela and the Talgua Caves, where ancestors buried bones at the entrance to another world, complete the evidence

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 37 Origen de la población (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** children dramatise the first groups' division of labour, then ask parents and grandparents how the first families reached their own community
