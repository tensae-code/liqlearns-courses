# Honduras first peoples caves and origins

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras first peoples caves and origins" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 37 Origen de la población

bone tools and stone artefacts in the Cueva del Gigante (Marcala) date Honduras's origins to millennia before Christ; Yarumela and the Talgua Caves, where ancestors buried bones at the entrance to another world, complete the evidence

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 37 Origen de la población (guide)

children dramatise the first groups' division of labour, then ask parents and grandparents how the first families reached their own community

## Sub lesson 3 — the peopling theories debated

three rival theories for America's first peoples — Ameghino's autochthonous origin, the Polynesian Pacific crossing (judged unlikely), and the Bering Strait route — are weighed with their evidence

## Sub lesson 4 — the peopling theories debated

the guide weighs Ameghino's autochthonous theory, the Polynesian route and the Bering Strait route for the first Americans
