# Americas first peoples peopling theories

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Americas first peoples peopling theories" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 21 Primeros pobladores de América

three explanations compete — Ameghino's autochthonous Americans (rejected for lacking scientific base), Hrdlicka's single Bering crossing on the ice-age land bridge, Rivet's multiple origins adding an Oceanic sea route — for people who arrived some 50,000 years ago

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 21 Primeros pobladores (guide)

Ameghino, Hrdlicka and Rivet are matched to their theories in the workbook scheme

## Sub lesson 3 — the named theorists compared

Ameghino's autochthonous claim from pampas bones faces Hrdlicka's single Asian origin via Bering (~12,000 BCE) and Paul Rivet's multiple origin — four routes including Antarctica (Méndez Correa) and the transoceanic Polynesian-Melanesian crossing

## Sub lesson 4 — the named theorists compared

the guide's key compares Ameghino, Hrdlicka's Bering route and Rivet's four-route multiple origin
