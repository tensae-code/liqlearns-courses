Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**Answer:** three explanations compete — Ameghino's autochthonous Americans (rejected for lacking scientific base), Hrdlicka's single Bering crossing on the ice-age land bridge, Rivet's multiple origins adding an Oceanic sea route — for people who arrived some 50,000 years ago
