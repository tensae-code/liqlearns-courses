# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "High and Low Middle Ages" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** the 2022 guide's key covers the feudal lord–serf order and the Church's grip

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 25 La Edad Media" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from Rome's fall (476) to Constantinople's (1453), a rural world of feudal lords, villains and unfree serfs bound by vassalage, with the Church holding education in its monasteries and raising Gothic cathedrals — Notre Dame, Milan, Cologne — and Dante and Boccaccio writing
