# Ancient civilisations athens and democracy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 24 Grecia y Roma: la democracia griega" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece, no empire but independent city-states, invents democracy as government of the people with participation — the pillar, the book says, under most societies today

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 24: democracia (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** demonstrations of democracy are marked with an X — participation is the test
