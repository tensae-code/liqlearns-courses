# Americas prehispanic cultural stages — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 22 América prehispánica: las etapas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the long climb is staged — megafauna hunters to 7000 BC, incipient farmers, the formative (1500 BC–AD 300: sedentism, pottery, chiefdoms, first writing), the classic (AD 300–1000: science and cities at their height)
