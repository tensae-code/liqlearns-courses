# Americas prehispanic cultural stages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 22 América prehispánica: las etapas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the long climb is staged — megafauna hunters to 7000 BC, incipient farmers, the formative (1500 BC–AD 300: sedentism, pottery, chiefdoms, first writing), the classic (AD 300–1000: science and cities at their height)

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 22 América prehispánica (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** one cultural stage is chosen, illustrated and characterised by each team
