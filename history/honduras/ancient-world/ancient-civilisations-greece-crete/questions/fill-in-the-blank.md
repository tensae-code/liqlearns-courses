# Ancient civilisations greece crete — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 24: Grecia (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** beauty as a civilisation's wealth — Parthenon, sculpture periods, the three philosophers

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the Greek legacy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** city-states, Doric art centred on the human body, sculptors Myron, Polyclitus and Phidias, Homer fathering epic and tragedy with the Iliad and Odyssey, and Socrates, Plato and Aristotle founding philosophy
