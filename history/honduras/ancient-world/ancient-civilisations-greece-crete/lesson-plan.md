## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 24: la cultura griega** — lovers of beauty with the human as its measure — theatre, sport, the Parthenon in white marble, sculpture in archaic, classical and Hellenistic periods, and Socrates, Plato and Aristotle remaking thought
2. **§2 Lección 24: Grecia (guide)** — beauty as a civilisation's wealth — Parthenon, sculpture periods, the three philosophers
3. **§3 the Greek legacy** — city-states, Doric art centred on the human body, sculptors Myron, Polyclitus and Phidias, Homer fathering epic and tragedy with the Iliad and Odyssey, and Socrates, Plato and Aristotle founding philosophy
4. **§4 the Greek legacy** — the guide's key lists the city-states, Doric art, Homer's epics and the Socrates–Plato–Aristotle line
5. **§5 the Greek legacy** — the 2022 guide's key lists city-states, Doric art, Homer and the three philosophers
