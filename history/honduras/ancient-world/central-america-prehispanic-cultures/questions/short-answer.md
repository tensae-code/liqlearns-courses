# Central america prehispanic cultures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 22: los pueblos y la leyenda de Sac-Nicté" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from the 5th century BC maize-farming peoples fill the isthmus — Quiché, Cakchiquel, Pipil, Chorotega, Matagalpa, Ulwa and Macro-Chibcha groups sharing cacao money and communal land; the Sac-Nicté legend tells how Canek of Chichén Itzá steals the promised princess and his Itzaes abandon their city to the pursuers

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 22: pueblos de Centroamérica (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Sac-Nicté legend is read as the Maya world's own story-source before the peoples of the isthmus are listed
