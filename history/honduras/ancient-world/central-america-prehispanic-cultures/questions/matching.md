# Central america prehispanic cultures — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lección 22: los pueblos y la leyenda de Sac-Nicté — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Lección 22: pueblos de Centroamérica (guide) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) from the 5th century BC maize-farming peoples fill the isthmus — Quiché, Cakchiquel, Pipil, Chorotega, Matagalpa, Ulwa and Macro-Chibcha groups sharing cacao money and communal land; the Sac-Nicté legend tells how Canek of Chichén Itzá steals the promised princess and his Itzaes abandon their city to the pursuers
b) the Sac-Nicté legend is read as the Maya world's own story-source before the peoples of the isthmus are listed

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
