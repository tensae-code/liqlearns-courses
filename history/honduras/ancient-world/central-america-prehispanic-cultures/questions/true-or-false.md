# Central america prehispanic cultures — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 22: los pueblos y la leyenda de Sac-Nicté", from the 5th century BC maize-farming peoples fill the isthmus — Quiché, Cakchiquel, Pipil, Chorotega, Matagalpa, Ulwa and Macro-Chibcha groups sharing cacao money and communal land; the Sac-Nicté legend tells how Canek of Chichén Itzá steals the promised princess and his Itzaes abandon their city to the pursuers (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 22: pueblos de Centroamérica (guide)", the Sac-Nicté legend is read as the Maya world's own story-source before the peoples of the isthmus are listed (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
