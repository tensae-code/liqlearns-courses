# Central america prehispanic cultures

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Central america prehispanic cultures" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 22: los pueblos y la leyenda de Sac-Nicté

from the 5th century BC maize-farming peoples fill the isthmus — Quiché, Cakchiquel, Pipil, Chorotega, Matagalpa, Ulwa and Macro-Chibcha groups sharing cacao money and communal land; the Sac-Nicté legend tells how Canek of Chichén Itzá steals the promised princess and his Itzaes abandon their city to the pursuers

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 22: pueblos de Centroamérica (guide)

the Sac-Nicté legend is read as the Maya world's own story-source before the peoples of the isthmus are listed
