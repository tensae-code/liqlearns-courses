# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Sumerian firsts", Mesopotamia is "the cradle of history": the Sumerians invented writing (pictograms pressed into clay, then cuneiform), the wheel and the plough, followed by Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians until the Persian conquest (Sub lesson 3)
