Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 23: Mesopotamia (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Sumerian firsts" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Mesopotamia is "the cradle of history": the Sumerians invented writing (pictograms pressed into clay, then cuneiform), the wheel and the plough, followed by Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians until the Persian conquest
