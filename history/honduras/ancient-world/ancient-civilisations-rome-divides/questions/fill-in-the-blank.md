# Ancient civilisations rome divides — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 24: el imperio se divide y cae" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from Augustus (29 BC) the empire reaches three continents and must split into East and West under absolute emperors, until Romulus Augustulus closes the West in the 5th century

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 24: imperio (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Augustus to Romulus Augustulus, East against West, closes the ancient world
