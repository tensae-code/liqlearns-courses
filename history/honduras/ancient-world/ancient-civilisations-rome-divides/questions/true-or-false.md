# Ancient civilisations rome divides — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 24: el imperio se divide y cae", from Augustus (29 BC) the empire reaches three continents and must split into East and West under absolute emperors, until Romulus Augustulus closes the West in the 5th century (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 24: imperio (guide)", Augustus to Romulus Augustulus, East against West, closes the ancient world (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
