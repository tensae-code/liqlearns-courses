# Ancient civilisations rome divides — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lección 24: el imperio se divide y cae — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Lección 24: imperio (guide) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) from Augustus (29 BC) the empire reaches three continents and must split into East and West under absolute emperors, until Romulus Augustulus closes the West in the 5th century
b) Augustus to Romulus Augustulus, East against West, closes the ancient world

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
