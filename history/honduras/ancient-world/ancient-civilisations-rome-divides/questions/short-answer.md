# Ancient civilisations rome divides — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 24: el imperio se divide y cae" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from Augustus (29 BC) the empire reaches three continents and must split into East and West under absolute emperors, until Romulus Augustulus closes the West in the 5th century

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 24: imperio (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Augustus to Romulus Augustulus, East against West, closes the ancient world
