# Dating the past — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 21 Lo que pasó y lo que está pasando (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** duration and sequence are taught through recurring feasts, ending on Morazán's portrait and the question of which event his month celebrates

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 22: árbol genealógico (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the teacher has each child build the family tree and relate their own events to the events of each relative
