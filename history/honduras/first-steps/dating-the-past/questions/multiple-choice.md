# Dating the past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 20 Ayer, hoy y mañana"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) a fallen leaf was on the branch yesterday, dreams today and is blown away tomorrow; time is measured in hours, days and years, and Comayagua, founded 8 December 1537, turns 482 in 2019  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Lección 21 Lo que pasó y lo que está pasando"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) birthdays and civic feasts return on the same date each year with a duration and a sequence; September becomes the Mes de la Patria because it holds the acts commemorating independence  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
