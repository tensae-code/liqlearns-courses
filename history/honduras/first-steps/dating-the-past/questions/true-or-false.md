# Dating the past — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 22: El árbol genealógico", grandparents, parents, brothers and sisters are placed on a drawn family tree so the child can see where their own story joins the family's (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 20 Ayer, hoy y mañana (guide)", past, present and future are taught through clocks and calendars and the founding date of the children's own community, then and now (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
