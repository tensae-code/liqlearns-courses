# What history is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 20 Construyendo nuestra memoria histórica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Herodotus, "father of history", makes history an inquiry (historéo, to find out); the science studies human deeds that changed a social process, so a people learns from past lessons and does not repeat its errors

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 29 Valorando los procesos históricos centroamericanos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** history is not isolated facts but processes — pre-Hispanic, colonial, independent — whose stages (Federation, Liberal Reform, dictatorships, civil wars, peace) explain the present; the class builds a timeline of Central America's important events
