# What history is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 22 Nosotros y nuestra familia: historia personal y familiar"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) grandparents tell events from before the children were born; one person's important life-events make a personal history, and all the family's stories together make the family history  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Lección 22 Nosotros y nuestra familia (guide)"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) a child (Mateo, in the workbook story) narrates his personal history aloud, and the class learns that family history is all its members' stories joined  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
