# What history is — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 20 El valor de la historia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** History is a social science studying past human deeds to know the past, understand the present and build a better future; every fact happens in a place and a time and has causes and consequences

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 20 El valor de la historia (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** History's value is taught through causes and consequences — knowing them lets a people understand its present and choose a better future
