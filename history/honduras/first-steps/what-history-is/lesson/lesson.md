# What history is

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "What history is" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 22 Nosotros y nuestra familia: historia personal y familiar

grandparents tell events from before the children were born; one person's important life-events make a personal history, and all the family's stories together make the family history

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 22 Nosotros y nuestra familia (guide)

a child (Mateo, in the workbook story) narrates his personal history aloud, and the class learns that family history is all its members' stories joined

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 20 La Historia: nuestro presente y futuro

History studies people's past through time, with causes and consequences; every historical fact needs a person, a place and a date, and knowing origins tells a society who it is and where it wants to go

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 20 La Historia: nuestro presente y futuro (guide)

the school's own name, first classrooms and recent changes become the class's first historical inquiry

## Sub lesson 5 — Lección 20 El valor de la historia

History is a social science studying past human deeds to know the past, understand the present and build a better future; every fact happens in a place and a time and has causes and consequences

## Sub lesson 6 — Lección 20 El valor de la historia (guide)

History's value is taught through causes and consequences — knowing them lets a people understand its present and choose a better future

## Sub lesson 7 — Lección 20 Construyendo nuestra memoria histórica

Herodotus, "father of history", makes history an inquiry (historéo, to find out); the science studies human deeds that changed a social process, so a people learns from past lessons and does not repeat its errors

## Sub lesson 8 — Lección 29 Valorando los procesos históricos centroamericanos

history is not isolated facts but processes — pre-Hispanic, colonial, independent — whose stages (Federation, Liberal Reform, dictatorships, civil wars, peace) explain the present; the class builds a timeline of Central America's important events

## Sub lesson 9 — Lección 20 Construyendo nuestra memoria histórica (guide)

the school's own history, researched from oral and written sources, is the guide's entry to Herodotus and the definition of history

## Sub lesson 10 — Lección 29 Valorando los procesos (guide)

four important Central American events are identified by each team as processes, not isolated facts

## Sub lesson 11 — Lección 20 Las Ciencias Sociales al servicio de la humanidad

History stands in the family portrait of the social sciences (with anthropology, archaeology, economics, demography, sociology) as the discipline that lets a society understand its changes through time

## Sub lesson 12 — Lección 20 Las Ciencias Sociales (guide)

History is placed among the social sciences by its object — people in society through time
