# Honduras historians and history writing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 20: historiadores de Honduras" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** six named Honduran historians — Medardo Mejía, Rafael Heliodoro Valle, Longino Becerra, Mario Argueta, Rolando Sierra, Jorge Amaya — are set as research subjects, a national historiography with faces

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 20: historiadores (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** each team researches the life and work of one of the six pictured Honduran historians
