# Is history a science — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 20 La historia como ciencia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** history earns the name science by method — a systematised knowledge of the human past, interpreted in time and space with causes and consequences, so societies can set future goals and not repeat errors

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 20 La historia como ciencia (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide frames history's scientific claim — systematic knowledge, method, objectivity demanded of the historian
