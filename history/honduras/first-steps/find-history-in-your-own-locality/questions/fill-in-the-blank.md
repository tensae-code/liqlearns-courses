# Find history in your own locality — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 23 Esta es nuestra comunidad: historia de La Ceiba" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** La Ceiba begins as a hamlet of 20 Pech families sheltering under a great sacred ceiba tree, is then settled by Garífuna and others, and grows into the city of the Carnival of Friendship

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 25 El pasado de nuestra comunidad" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Morazán's statue in Tegucigalpa and José Cecilio del Valle's house in Choluteca open a hunt for each town's own monuments, historic places and important people
