# Find history in your own locality — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 22 El municipio a través del tiempo" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** old photographs (Comayagüela 1928, the old Presidential House) show buildings changed by weather and neglect; the class dramatises three municipal stories — independence, Morazán's birth, the capital's move from Comayagua to Tegucigalpa

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 22 El municipio a través del tiempo (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the municipality's illustrious people — painters, poets, merchants, singers — are named, and its relevant events dramatised
