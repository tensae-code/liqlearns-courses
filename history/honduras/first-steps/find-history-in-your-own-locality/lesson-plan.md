# Lesson plan — Find history in your own locality

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** First steps · **Lesson:** Find history in your own locality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my own time, my family and my community — and the nation through its symbols, its anthem and Morazán (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Find history in your own locality as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my own time, my family and my community — and the nation through its symbols, its anthem and Morazán — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 23 Esta es nuestra comunidad: historia de La Ceiba** — La Ceiba begins as a hamlet of 20 Pech families sheltering under a great sacred ceiba tree, is then settled by Garífuna and others, and grows into the city of the Carnival of Friendship
2. **§2 Lección 25 El pasado de nuestra comunidad** — Morazán's statue in Tegucigalpa and José Cecilio del Valle's house in Choluteca open a hunt for each town's own monuments, historic places and important people
3. **§3 Lección 23 Esta es nuestra comunidad (guide)** — La Ceiba's Pech-and-ceiba-tree origin story is the model; every town's song, name and patronal fair is treated as its history
4. **§4 Lección 25 El pasado de nuestra comunidad (guide)** — children list their community's historic places and people, then build one monument from recycled material
5. **§5 Lección 22 El municipio a través del tiempo** — old photographs (Comayagüela 1928, the old Presidential House) show buildings changed by weather and neglect; the class dramatises three municipal stories — independence, Morazán's birth, the capital's move from Comayagua to Tegucigalpa
6. **§6 Lección 22 El municipio a través del tiempo (guide)** — the municipality's illustrious people — painters, poets, merchants, singers — are named, and its relevant events dramatised
