# Who studies prehistory

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Who studies prehistory" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 22 La prehistoria, origen de la humanidad

two origin theories are weighed — creation by God, held by faith, against Darwin's evolution through hominisation from primates; with no writing, prehistory is known only from material remains studied archaeologically and anthropologically

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 22 La prehistoria (guide)

creationist and evolutionist theories are compared, and the writing threshold names prehistory's end
