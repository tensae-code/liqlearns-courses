# Who studies prehistory — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 22 La prehistoria, origen de la humanidad" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two origin theories are weighed — creation by God, held by faith, against Darwin's evolution through hominisation from primates; with no writing, prehistory is known only from material remains studied archaeologically and anthropologically

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 22 La prehistoria (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** creationist and evolutionist theories are compared, and the writing threshold names prehistory's end
