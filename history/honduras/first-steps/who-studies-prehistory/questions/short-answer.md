# Who studies prehistory — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 22 La prehistoria, origen de la humanidad" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two origin theories are weighed — creation by God, held by faith, against Darwin's evolution through hominisation from primates; with no writing, prehistory is known only from material remains studied archaeologically and anthropologically

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 22 La prehistoria (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** creationist and evolutionist theories are compared, and the writing threshold names prehistory's end
