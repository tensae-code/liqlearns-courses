# Heritage what it is and why it matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 25: Formas en que el pasado se manifiesta"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the past survives in the present as people read about heroes, practise traditions, visit Copán and museums, and guard places like Fuerte San Cristóbal at Gracias, where President Juan Lindo lies buried  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Lección 29 Costumbres y tradiciones"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Comayagua's sawdust carpets at Holy Week, sopa de caracol, colaciones and folk songs are what the society is; the song Conozca a Honduras asks where the Ruins of Copán and Morazán's cradle are  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
