# Heritage what it is and why it matters — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 8 Nuestras costumbres y tradiciones" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** traditions pass from generation to generation — patronal fairs, the Baile de los Diablitos, typical foods — and oral transmission is named living history to be guarded jealously

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 22: los escudos cuentan historia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** coats of arms, old or new, are full of history; Honduras's shield, conceived in 1840, speaks of natural wealth, liberty and sovereignty, and children design a shield for their own family or town
