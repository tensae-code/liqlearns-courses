# Heritage what it is and why it matters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 23 Raíces culturales" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** the fortresses of San Cristóbal (Gracias) and San Fernando (Omoa) were raised for defence against an enemy; around them live legends, fairs and traditions that are the legacy of the past to conserve

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 27 El aporte cultural" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** crafts handed down for centuries, the Baile de los Diablitos danced in Comayagua for over 370 years, and the Guancasco pact-feast between Ilama, Gualala and Chinda are intangible heritage carried by oral tradition
