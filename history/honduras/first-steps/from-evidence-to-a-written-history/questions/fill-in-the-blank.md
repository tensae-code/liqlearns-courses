# From evidence to a written history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 20: fuentes y ciencias auxiliares" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** the past is rebuilt indirectly from sources — primary against secondary, direct against indirect, a Maya stela against a book about it — with geography, archaeology, anthropology and other auxiliary sciences checking the story

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 21 Las huellas de la historia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Tikal, Khafre's pyramid, Paestum and the Great Wall open a typology of material, documentary, oral and audiovisual sources; Ciudad Blanca in the Mosquitia is Honduras's own lost-city evidence, and "when an elder dies, a library burns"
