# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 21: visita a las huellas (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** teacher, children and a family adult walk to the plaza to identify and draw the oldest house or building

**Answer:** museums are presented as society's memory store; specialised museums (art, religion, Indigenous peoples, gold-work) each hold one kind of evidence
