# From evidence to a written history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 21 Buscando huellas de nuestro pasado: las fuentes"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) without sources there is no past to know; sources are sorted into archaeological (buildings, monuments, tools), written (letters, laws, chronicles, censuses) and oral tradition (elders' testimonies)  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Lección 21: huellas materiales"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) a Maya stela at Copán Ruinas and a colonial church at Gracias are vestiges that let the class reconstruct past societies, then children hunt the oldest buildings in their own plaza  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
