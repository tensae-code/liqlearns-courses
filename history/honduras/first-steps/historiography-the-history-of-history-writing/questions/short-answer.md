# Historiography the history of history writing — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "how the writing of history changed" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** history begins in Greece as moralising chronicle of kings and wars, turns ecclesiastical in the Middle Ages, becomes a social science in the 18th century, and the Annales school finally widens it to everyday life, culture and feelings — with photo-history and film as new sources

**2.** Tell the story of "how the writing of history changed" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the guide teaches the same arc — Greek moralising chronicle, medieval ecclesiastical history, 18th-century social science, the Annales school's everyday-life history and photo/film sources
