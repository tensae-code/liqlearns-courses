# Historiography the history of history writing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 20: periodización" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** time itself is divided to be understood — prehistory (before writing) against history, then Ancient, Medieval, Modern and Contemporary ages, and special histories (of art, law, literature) branch off

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 20: periodización (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** prehistory/history and the four ages are taught with the sayings "history repeats itself" and "a people that does not know its history is condemned to repeat it"
