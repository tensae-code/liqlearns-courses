Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 21 La historia como ciencia social: dos periodizaciones", two rival maps of the past are set side by side — the traditional (prehistory/history, ages) and the historical-materialist (primitive community, slavery, feudalism, capitalism) which periodises by ways of organising production, not by dates (Sub lesson 3)
