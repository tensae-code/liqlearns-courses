# Two schools of periodization traditional and historical materialism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "two ways to cut up the past" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the traditional school divides by political events (Ancient, Medieval 476–1453, Modern from 1453 or 1492, Contemporary) while Marx's historical materialism divides by modes of production — primitive society, slavery, feudalism, capitalism, socialism — and the book teaches both without choosing

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "two ways to cut up the past" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide teaches the traditional ages against Marx's modes of production, with timelines for both
