# Video script — Two schools of periodization traditional and historical materialism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Two schools of periodization traditional and historical materialism.

the traditional school divides by political events (Ancient, Medieval 476–1453, Modern from 1453 or 1492, Contemporary) while Marx's historical materialism divides by modes of production — primitive society, slavery, feudalism, capitalism, socialism — and the book teaches both without choosing

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
