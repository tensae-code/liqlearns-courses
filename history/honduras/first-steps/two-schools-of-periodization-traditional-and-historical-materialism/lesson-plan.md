## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 two ways to cut up the past** — the traditional school divides by political events (Ancient, Medieval 476–1453, Modern from 1453 or 1492, Contemporary) while Marx's historical materialism divides by modes of production — primitive society, slavery, feudalism, capitalism, socialism — and the book teaches both without choosing
2. **§2 two ways to cut up the past** — the guide teaches the traditional ages against Marx's modes of production, with timelines for both
3. **§3 two ways to cut up the past** — the 2022 guide teaches the same two periodisations, opening from the 2008 crisis article as a living hecho histórico
