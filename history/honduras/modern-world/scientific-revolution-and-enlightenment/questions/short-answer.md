# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 26: absolutismo e Ilustración" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** absolute kings ruling by divine right meet the Century of Lights exalting reason and equality before the law; Copernicus's heliocentrism is condemned as heresy, and 1776, 1789 and the Spanish-American wars close the age

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 26: Ilustración (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** divine-right absolutism is contrasted with reason and equality before the law
