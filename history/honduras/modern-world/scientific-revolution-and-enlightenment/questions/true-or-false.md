# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 26: absolutismo e Ilustración", absolute kings ruling by divine right meet the Century of Lights exalting reason and equality before the law; Copernicus's heliocentrism is condemned as heresy, and 1776, 1789 and the Spanish-American wars close the age (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 26: Ilustración (guide)", divine-right absolutism is contrasted with reason and equality before the law (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
