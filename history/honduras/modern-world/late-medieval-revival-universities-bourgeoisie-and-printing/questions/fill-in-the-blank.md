# Late medieval revival universities bourgeoisie and printing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the late medieval turn" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** kings consolidated and distanced themselves from the Church, European universities were founded (an experience the Muslim world already had), trades and commerce flowered, a free bourgeoisie of merchants and artisans organised itself, and Gutenberg's press spread the new learning

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the late medieval turn" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide's key covers consolidating kings, the first universities, the rising bourgeoisie and Gutenberg's press
