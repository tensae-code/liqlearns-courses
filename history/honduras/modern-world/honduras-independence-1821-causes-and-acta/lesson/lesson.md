# Honduras independence 1821 causes and acta

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras independence 1821 causes and acta" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 25 Honduras durante la independencia: causas y proclamación

US independence, the French Revolution, Napoleon in Spain and South American wars press from outside; criollo–peninsular rivalry and high taxes press from inside; on 15 September 1821 the five provinces proclaim independence, Valle drafts the Acta, and the Pliegos reach Comayagua on 28 September

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 25: rebeliones previas y legado

before 1821 come risings — Delgado in El Salvador, León and Granada in Nicaragua, Tegucigalpa's Plaza Mayor in 1812; the criollo-led process frees Indigenous, criollo, mestizo and Black people from three centuries of subjection

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 25: causas y Acta (guide)

external sparks and internal grievances are listed, then the Acta and the Pliegos' arrival at Comayagua close the story

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 25: rebeliones y legado (guide)

the 1812 Tegucigalpa rising and its neighbours show independence as a process, celebrated yearly in school marches

## Sub lesson 5 — the two actas

Honduras has TWO independence acts: 15 September 1821, a transfer of power among Spanish-descended groups "to prevent the people proclaiming it themselves", and the stronger 1 July 1823 act breaking with Spain AND Mexico after Iturbide's fall

## Sub lesson 6 — the two actas

the guide teaches both actas — 1821's cautious transfer of power and the definitive 1 July 1823 declaration after Iturbide's fall
