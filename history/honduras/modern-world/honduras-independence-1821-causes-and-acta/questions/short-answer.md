# Honduras independence 1821 causes and acta — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "the two actas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Honduras has TWO independence acts: 15 September 1821, a transfer of power among Spanish-descended groups "to prevent the people proclaiming it themselves", and the stronger 1 July 1823 act breaking with Spain AND Mexico after Iturbide's fall

**2.** Tell the story of "the two actas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the guide teaches both actas — 1821's cautious transfer of power and the definitive 1 July 1823 declaration after Iturbide's fall
