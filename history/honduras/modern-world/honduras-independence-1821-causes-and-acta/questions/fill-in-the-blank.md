# Honduras independence 1821 causes and acta — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 25 Honduras durante la independencia: causas y proclamación" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** US independence, the French Revolution, Napoleon in Spain and South American wars press from outside; criollo–peninsular rivalry and high taxes press from inside; on 15 September 1821 the five provinces proclaim independence, Valle drafts the Acta, and the Pliegos reach Comayagua on 28 September

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 25: rebeliones previas y legado" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** before 1821 come risings — Delgado in El Salvador, León and Granada in Nicaragua, Tegucigalpa's Plaza Mayor in 1812; the criollo-led process frees Indigenous, criollo, mestizo and Black people from three centuries of subjection
