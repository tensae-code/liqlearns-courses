# Honduras independence 1821 causes and acta — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 25: causas y Acta (guide)", external sparks and internal grievances are listed, then the Acta and the Pliegos' arrival at Comayagua close the story (Sub lesson 3)
