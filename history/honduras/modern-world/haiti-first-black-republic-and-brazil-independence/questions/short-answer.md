# Haiti first black republic and brazil independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 27: Haití y Brasil" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Toussaint Louverture is captured but Jean Dessalines expels the French in 1804 — Haiti, first independent Latin American republic and first Black republic in world history; Brazil's court flees Napoleon to Rio, and Prince Pedro's Cry of Ipiranga (7 September 1822) makes a peaceful empire

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 27: Haití y Brasil (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Louverture and Dessalines, then the transferred court and Ipiranga — two unlike roads out of empire
