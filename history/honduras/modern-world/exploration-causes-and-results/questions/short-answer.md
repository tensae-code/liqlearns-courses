# Exploration causes and results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 25 Descubrimiento y conquista de América: las causas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** compass, astrolabe and sextant, the spice trade, and the Ottoman taking of Constantinople (1453) push Portugal and Spain to new routes; Cabot reaches Labrador (1497), Raleigh plants Virginia (1585) and Jamestown follows (1607)

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 25 Descubrimiento (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Spanish, Portuguese and English expeditions are compared in one scheme of characteristics
