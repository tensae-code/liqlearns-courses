# Exploration causes and results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 25 Descubrimiento y conquista de América: las causas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** compass, astrolabe and sextant, the spice trade, and the Ottoman taking of Constantinople (1453) push Portugal and Spain to new routes; Cabot reaches Labrador (1497), Raleigh plants Virginia (1585) and Jamestown follows (1607)

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 25 Descubrimiento (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Spanish, Portuguese and English expeditions are compared in one scheme of characteristics
