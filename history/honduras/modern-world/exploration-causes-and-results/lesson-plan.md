# Lesson plan — Exploration causes and results

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Exploration causes and results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Americas whole: the peopling debate, pre-Hispanic stages, the three great civilisations and all the others, three colonial empires side by side, the independence wave, the continental Liberal Reform, the Cold War and neoliberal Honduras (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Exploration causes and results as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Americas whole: the peopling debate, pre-Hispanic stages, the three great civilisations and all the others, three colonial empires side by side, the independence wave, the continental Liberal Reform, the Cold War and neoliberal Honduras — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
