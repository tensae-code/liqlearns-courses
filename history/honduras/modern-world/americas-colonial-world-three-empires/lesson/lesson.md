# Americas colonial world three empires

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Americas colonial world three empires" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 26 Periodo colonial: las colonias españolas

some 700 cities founded (1494–1599), four viceroyalties on the map, trade policed by Seville's Casa de Contratación, encomienda labour, and haciendas spreading over temperate lands

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 26: colonias portuguesas, inglesas y holandesas

Brazil's nine captaincies work sugar and cacao with Indigenous and African slaves; English religious refugees found Virginia (1607) and the east-coast colonies; the Dutch hold Curaçao, Aruba, Bonaire and the Guianas on farming

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 26: colonias españolas (guide)

viceroyalties are identified on the map and colonial buildings of the pupils' own department listed

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 26: otras colonias (guide)

the social pyramids of Spanish, Portuguese, English and French colonies are organised side by side

## Sub lesson 5 — dividing and conquering America

the Treaty of Tordesillas (1494) split the new world between Spain and Portugal 370 leagues west of Cape Verde; England colonised differently, settling families without large-scale mestizaje; Tenochtitlan fell after the Templo Mayor massacre, the Noche Triste and Cuauhtémoc's surrender

## Sub lesson 6 — dividing and conquering America

the guide teaches Tordesillas (1494), the contrasting English settlement model, and Tenochtitlan's fall through the Noche Triste to Cuauhtémoc
