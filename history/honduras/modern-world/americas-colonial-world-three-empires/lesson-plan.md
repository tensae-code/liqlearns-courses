# Lesson plan — Americas colonial world three empires

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Americas colonial world three empires
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Americas whole: the peopling debate, pre-Hispanic stages, the three great civilisations and all the others, three colonial empires side by side, the independence wave, the continental Liberal Reform, the Cold War and neoliberal Honduras (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Americas colonial world three empires as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Americas whole: the peopling debate, pre-Hispanic stages, the three great civilisations and all the others, three colonial empires side by side, the independence wave, the continental Liberal Reform, the Cold War and neoliberal Honduras — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 26 Periodo colonial: las colonias españolas** — some 700 cities founded (1494–1599), four viceroyalties on the map, trade policed by Seville's Casa de Contratación, encomienda labour, and haciendas spreading over temperate lands
2. **§2 Lección 26: colonias portuguesas, inglesas y holandesas** — Brazil's nine captaincies work sugar and cacao with Indigenous and African slaves; English religious refugees found Virginia (1607) and the east-coast colonies; the Dutch hold Curaçao, Aruba, Bonaire and the Guianas on farming
3. **§3 Lección 26: colonias españolas (guide)** — viceroyalties are identified on the map and colonial buildings of the pupils' own department listed
4. **§4 Lección 26: otras colonias (guide)** — the social pyramids of Spanish, Portuguese, English and French colonies are organised side by side
5. **§5 dividing and conquering America** — the Treaty of Tordesillas (1494) split the new world between Spain and Portugal 370 leagues west of Cape Verde; England colonised differently, settling families without large-scale mestizaje; Tenochtitlan fell after the Templo Mayor massacre, the Noche Triste and Cuauhtémoc's surrender
6. **§6 dividing and conquering America** — the guide teaches Tordesillas (1494), the contrasting English settlement model, and Tenochtitlan's fall through the Noche Triste to Cuauhtémoc
