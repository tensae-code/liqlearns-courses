# Americas colonial world three empires — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26 Periodo colonial: las colonias españolas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** some 700 cities founded (1494–1599), four viceroyalties on the map, trade policed by Seville's Casa de Contratación, encomienda labour, and haciendas spreading over temperate lands

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26: colonias portuguesas, inglesas y holandesas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Brazil's nine captaincies work sugar and cacao with Indigenous and African slaves; English religious refugees found Virginia (1607) and the east-coast colonies; the Dutch hold Curaçao, Aruba, Bonaire and the Guianas on farming
