# Americas colonial world three empires — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "dividing and conquering America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** the Treaty of Tordesillas (1494) split the new world between Spain and Portugal 370 leagues west of Cape Verde; England colonised differently, settling families without large-scale mestizaje; Tenochtitlan fell after the Templo Mayor massacre, the Noche Triste and Cuauhtémoc's surrender

**2.** Tell the story of "dividing and conquering America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the guide teaches Tordesillas (1494), the contrasting English settlement model, and Tenochtitlan's fall through the Noche Triste to Cuauhtémoc
