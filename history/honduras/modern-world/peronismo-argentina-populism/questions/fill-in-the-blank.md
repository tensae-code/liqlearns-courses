# Peronismo argentina populism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Peronism's rise" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Argentina's cereal-and-beef oligarchy lost power in the early 1900s to bourgeoisie and proletariat; nationalist economics and labour laws followed, and in 1946 Perón reached the presidency
