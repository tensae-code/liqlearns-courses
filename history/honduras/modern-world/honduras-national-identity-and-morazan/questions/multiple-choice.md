# Honduras national identity and morazan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 8 Identidad nacional"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) a child learns that being born in Honduras makes them Honduran, that heroes and próceres did great deeds for everyone, and meets Francisco Morazán, named the country's greatest prócer  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Lección 8 Identidad nacional (guide)"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the teacher leads the class from nationality as a child's right to Morazán as prócer máximo, with children naming outstanding people of their own community  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
