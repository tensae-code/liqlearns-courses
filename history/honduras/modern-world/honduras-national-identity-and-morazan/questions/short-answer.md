# Honduras national identity and morazan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 9: el legado de los próceres" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Valle drafted the Act of Independence, Herrera decreed the first constitution in 1825 as first Head of State, Morazán presided over the Federation and left the unionist ideal, and Lempira gave his life defending the land

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 9: legado de los próceres (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** each prócer is fixed by his deed — Valle the Acta, Herrera the 1825 constitution, Morazán the unionist ideal, Cabañas and Reyes to be researched by the pupils
