# Honduras national identity and morazan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 9: los próceres y el héroe", Morazán, Valle, Herrera, Cabañas and Reyes are named founders of the Patria, men of flesh and blood with virtues and defects, beside national hero Cacique Lempira and a painting of the Battle of La Trinidad (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 9: próceres y héroe (guide)", prócer is distinguished from héroe — civic virtue and founding deeds against brave life-risking acts — with Lempira as hero and the five próceres as founders (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
