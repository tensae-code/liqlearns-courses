# Video script — Central america independence federation and liberal reform

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Central america independence federation and liberal reform.

Napoleon in Spain, Cádiz 1812, the French Revolution and US independence meet criollo–peninsular rivalry and pre-independence movements; independence (15 September 1821) is signed at Guatemala by Gainza, Delgado, Cañas, Aycinena and others as an elite settlement, then comes annexation to Iturbide's Mexico and absolute independence on 1 July 1823 (Chiapas stays with Mexico)

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
