# Central america independence federation and liberal reform — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "annexation, elections and the federation's advances" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** the criollo elite annexed Central America to Iturbide's Mexico (5 January 1822) over the fierce resistance of Molina, Barrundia and Córdova; freed 1 July 1823, the federation held 1824 elections (liberal Arce vs the conservatives' Valle), planned an interoceanic canal, abolished slavery, consecrated asylum and limited the death penalty — before dissolving in 1839; Morazán's 1842 testament is printed at length

**2.** Tell the story of "annexation, elections and the federation's advances" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the guide teaches the 1822 annexation and its opponents, the 1824 Arce–Valle elections, the canal project, abolition of slavery and asylum — and Morazán's testament
