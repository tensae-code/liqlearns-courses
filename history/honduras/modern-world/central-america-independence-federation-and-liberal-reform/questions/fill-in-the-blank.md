# Central america independence federation and liberal reform — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26 Independencia de Centroamérica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Napoleon in Spain, Cádiz 1812, the French Revolution and US independence meet criollo–peninsular rivalry and pre-independence movements; independence (15 September 1821) is signed at Guatemala by Gainza, Delgado, Cañas, Aycinena and others as an elite settlement, then comes annexation to Iturbide's Mexico and absolute independence on 1 July 1823 (Chiapas stays with Mexico)

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26: la Federación y la Reforma Liberal centroamericana" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Federation's 1824 constitution abolishes slavery; Arce presides, Valle dies travelling to take the presidency he won, and Morazán (1835–1839) separates church and State and frees education; a generation later Barrios in Guatemala and Soto and Rosa in Honduras carry the Liberal Reform — codes, the 1880 constitution, secularised cemeteries and church goods, the southern road
