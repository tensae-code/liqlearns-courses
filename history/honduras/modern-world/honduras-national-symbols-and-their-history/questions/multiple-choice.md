# Honduras national symbols and their history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 9 Símbolos patrios de Honduras"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) flag, shield, anthem, pine, orchid, macaw and deer are introduced one by one, each standing for a piece of the country, and the class asks what is celebrated on 15 September  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the symbols themselves tell the story — the National Anthem narrates what happened from the arrival of the Spanish to independence, and the class is sent to find where the anthem speaks of Lempira  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
