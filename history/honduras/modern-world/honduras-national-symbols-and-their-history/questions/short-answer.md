# Honduras national symbols and their history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres: los símbolos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** the flag's five stars are the five Central American countries, the anthem (words by Augusto C. Coello, music by Carlos Hartling) narrates historical events, and the shield has changed several times through time

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the anthem is declaimed and explained stanza by stanza as a narration of the country's historical events
