# Honduras national symbols and their history

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras national symbols and their history" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 9 Símbolos patrios de Honduras

flag, shield, anthem, pine, orchid, macaw and deer are introduced one by one, each standing for a piece of the country, and the class asks what is celebrated on 15 September

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia

the symbols themselves tell the story — the National Anthem narrates what happened from the arrival of the Spanish to independence, and the class is sent to find where the anthem speaks of Lempira

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 9 Símbolos patrios (guide)

each symbol is taught by what it represents, Central America's flags are compared, and children research who authored the map of Honduras

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia (guide)

legends and tales are named as representations of oral tradition, and the anthem and shield are read as a telling of the national past

## Sub lesson 5 — Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres: los símbolos

the flag's five stars are the five Central American countries, the anthem (words by Augusto C. Coello, music by Carlos Hartling) narrates historical events, and the shield has changed several times through time

## Sub lesson 6 — Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres (guide)

the anthem is declaimed and explained stanza by stanza as a narration of the country's historical events

## Sub lesson 7 — Lección 9 Identidad Nacional: los símbolos

greater symbols (flag, shield, anthem) and lesser symbols (orchid, scarlet macaw, white-tailed deer, pine, official map) are classified, each representing the country's history, culture and natural wealth

## Sub lesson 8 — Lección 9 Identidad Nacional (guide)

greater and lesser symbols are taught as representations of the nation's history, culture and natural wealth

## Sub lesson 9 — Lección 30 El patriotismo de América Central

the seven countries' shields, flags and flowers are set side by side, and 15 September is kept by five of them as the shared independence feast

## Sub lesson 10 — Lección 30 Patriotismo centroamericano (guide)

the seven countries' symbols are described and matched to their countries

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Honduras national symbols and their history as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
