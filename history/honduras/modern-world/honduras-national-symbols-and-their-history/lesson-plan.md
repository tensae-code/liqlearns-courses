# Lesson plan — Honduras national symbols and their history

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Honduras national symbols and their history
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my own time, my family and my community — and the nation through its symbols, its anthem and Morazán (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Honduras national symbols and their history as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my own time, my family and my community — and the nation through its symbols, its anthem and Morazán — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 9 Símbolos patrios de Honduras** — flag, shield, anthem, pine, orchid, macaw and deer are introduced one by one, each standing for a piece of the country, and the class asks what is celebrated on 15 September
2. **§2 Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia** — the symbols themselves tell the story — the National Anthem narrates what happened from the arrival of the Spanish to independence, and the class is sent to find where the anthem speaks of Lempira
3. **§3 Lección 9 Símbolos patrios (guide)** — each symbol is taught by what it represents, Central America's flags are compared, and children research who authored the map of Honduras
4. **§4 Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia (guide)** — legends and tales are named as representations of oral tradition, and the anthem and shield are read as a telling of the national past
5. **§5 Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres: los símbolos** — the flag's five stars are the five Central American countries, the anthem (words by Augusto C. Coello, music by Carlos Hartling) narrates historical events, and the shield has changed several times through time
6. **§6 Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres (guide)** — the anthem is declaimed and explained stanza by stanza as a narration of the country's historical events
7. **§7 Lección 9 Identidad Nacional: los símbolos** — greater symbols (flag, shield, anthem) and lesser symbols (orchid, scarlet macaw, white-tailed deer, pine, official map) are classified, each representing the country's history, culture and natural wealth
8. **§8 Lección 9 Identidad Nacional (guide)** — greater and lesser symbols are taught as representations of the nation's history, culture and natural wealth
9. **§9 Lección 30 El patriotismo de América Central** — the seven countries' shields, flags and flowers are set side by side, and 15 September is kept by five of them as the shared independence feast
10. **§10 Lección 30 Patriotismo centroamericano (guide)** — the seven countries' symbols are described and matched to their countries
