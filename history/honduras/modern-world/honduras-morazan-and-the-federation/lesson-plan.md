# Lesson plan — Honduras morazan and the federation

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Honduras morazan and the federation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Honduras whole in three periods — pre-Hispanic, colonial, independent: Copán and the pre-Hispanic peoples, discovery and conquest, independence, Morazán and the Federation, the Liberal Reform and its enclaves, and democracy to the 2009 crisis (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Honduras morazan and the federation as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Honduras whole in three periods — pre-Hispanic, colonial, independent: Copán and the pre-Hispanic peoples, discovery and conquest, independence, Morazán and the Federation, the Liberal Reform and its enclaves, and democracy to the 2009 crisis — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 26 Nuestro país después de la independencia: Morazán** — Morazán, born in Tegucigalpa in 1792, wins the five-hour Battle of La Trinidad (11 November 1827) against Justo Milla, retakes the country, presides over the Federation, abolishes the tithe and sets civil marriage — then Rafael Carrera brings him down and he is shot in San José on 15 September 1842
2. **§2 Lección 26: anexión, Federación y Estado** — fearing invasion, the provinces annex to Iturbide's Mexico; on 1 July 1823 they declare absolute independence and form the Central American Federation under Arce, liberals against conservatives; in 1838 the Federation dissolves and Honduras becomes a State with its first constitution
3. **§3 Lección 26: Morazán (guide)** — La Trinidad is narrated hour by hour — Morazán marches from Choluteca, beats Milla in five hours, retakes Tegucigalpa and Comayagua
4. **§4 Lección 26: Federación y Estado (guide)** — annexation, absolute independence in 1823, Federation, dissolution in 1838 — the State's elements (population, territory, government) are named
5. **§5 the Federal Republic 1825–1839** — the five provinces united with free press and curbed church power, but fiscal deficit, regionalism, church boycott and Guatemala's rivalry killed it; Morazán, proclaimed liberator of Costa Rica, was shot in San José on 15 September 1842, and thirty years of conservative reaction and caudillismo followed
6. **§6 the Federal Republic 1825–1839** — the guide teaches the federation's reforms, its fiscal/regional/church causes of failure, Morazán's 1842 execution and the conservative reaction
