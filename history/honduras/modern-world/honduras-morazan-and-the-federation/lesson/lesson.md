# Honduras morazan and the federation

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras morazan and the federation" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 26 Nuestro país después de la independencia: Morazán

Morazán, born in Tegucigalpa in 1792, wins the five-hour Battle of La Trinidad (11 November 1827) against Justo Milla, retakes the country, presides over the Federation, abolishes the tithe and sets civil marriage — then Rafael Carrera brings him down and he is shot in San José on 15 September 1842

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 26: anexión, Federación y Estado

fearing invasion, the provinces annex to Iturbide's Mexico; on 1 July 1823 they declare absolute independence and form the Central American Federation under Arce, liberals against conservatives; in 1838 the Federation dissolves and Honduras becomes a State with its first constitution

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 26: Morazán (guide)

La Trinidad is narrated hour by hour — Morazán marches from Choluteca, beats Milla in five hours, retakes Tegucigalpa and Comayagua

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 26: Federación y Estado (guide)

annexation, absolute independence in 1823, Federation, dissolution in 1838 — the State's elements (population, territory, government) are named

## Sub lesson 5 — the Federal Republic 1825–1839

the five provinces united with free press and curbed church power, but fiscal deficit, regionalism, church boycott and Guatemala's rivalry killed it; Morazán, proclaimed liberator of Costa Rica, was shot in San José on 15 September 1842, and thirty years of conservative reaction and caudillismo followed

## Sub lesson 6 — the Federal Republic 1825–1839

the guide teaches the federation's reforms, its fiscal/regional/church causes of failure, Morazán's 1842 execution and the conservative reaction
