# Honduras morazan and the federation — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 26: Morazán (guide)", La Trinidad is narrated hour by hour — Morazán marches from Choluteca, beats Milla in five hours, retakes Tegucigalpa and Comayagua (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 26: Federación y Estado (guide)", annexation, absolute independence in 1823, Federation, dissolution in 1838 — the State's elements (population, territory, government) are named (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
