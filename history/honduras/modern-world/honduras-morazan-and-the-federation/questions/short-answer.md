# Honduras morazan and the federation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "the Federal Republic 1825–1839" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** the five provinces united with free press and curbed church power, but fiscal deficit, regionalism, church boycott and Guatemala's rivalry killed it; Morazán, proclaimed liberator of Costa Rica, was shot in San José on 15 September 1842, and thirty years of conservative reaction and caudillismo followed

**2.** Tell the story of "the Federal Republic 1825–1839" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the guide teaches the federation's reforms, its fiscal/regional/church causes of failure, Morazán's 1842 execution and the conservative reaction
