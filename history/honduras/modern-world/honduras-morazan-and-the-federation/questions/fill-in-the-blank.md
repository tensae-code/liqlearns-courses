# Honduras morazan and the federation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26 Nuestro país después de la independencia: Morazán" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Morazán, born in Tegucigalpa in 1792, wins the five-hour Battle of La Trinidad (11 November 1827) against Justo Milla, retakes the country, presides over the Federation, abolishes the tithe and sets civil marriage — then Rafael Carrera brings him down and he is shot in San José on 15 September 1842

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26: anexión, Federación y Estado" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** fearing invasion, the provinces annex to Iturbide's Mexico; on 1 July 1823 they declare absolute independence and form the Central American Federation under Arce, liberals against conservatives; in 1838 the Federation dissolves and Honduras becomes a State with its first constitution
