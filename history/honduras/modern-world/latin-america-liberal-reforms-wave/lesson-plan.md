## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 28 Reforma Liberal en América** — one continental wave — Morazán's reforms (1830–1839) named the first great Liberal Reform, Juárez in Mexico (1854–1857), López and Mosquera in Colombia, Venezuela's Federal War, Barrios in Guatemala (1871), Zelaya in Nicaragua (1893), Alfaro in Ecuador — against church property and caudillos, for land markets and world trade
2. **§2 Lección 28 Reforma Liberal (guide)** — the reform's ideology file is completed country by country from Morazán to Alfaro
3. **§3 the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall** — Mexico's reform built a federal, secular republic; Central America's liberal period ran 1870–1950, but in Honduras the reform stayed unfinished — no consolidated class to drive it, excessive foreign capital, a weak export economy — until Gálvez (1949–1954) completed pieces of it: roads, the Central Bank, the development bank
4. **§4 the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall** — the guide teaches Mexico's secular federal reform, the 1870–1950 Central American liberal period, Honduras's unfinished reform and Gálvez's 1949–1954 modernisation
