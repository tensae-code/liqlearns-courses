# Latin america liberal reforms wave — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Mexico's reform built a federal, secular republic; Central America's liberal period ran 1870–1950, but in Honduras the reform stayed unfinished — no consolidated class to drive it, excessive foreign capital, a weak export economy — until Gálvez (1949–1954) completed pieces of it: roads, the Central Bank, the development bank
