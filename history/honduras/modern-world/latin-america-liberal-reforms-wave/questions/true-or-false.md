# Latin america liberal reforms wave — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall", Mexico's reform built a federal, secular republic; Central America's liberal period ran 1870–1950, but in Honduras the reform stayed unfinished — no consolidated class to drive it, excessive foreign capital, a weak export economy — until Gálvez (1949–1954) completed pieces of it: roads, the Central Bank, the development bank (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall", the guide teaches Mexico's secular federal reform, the 1870–1950 Central American liberal period, Honduras's unfinished reform and Gálvez's 1949–1954 modernisation (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
