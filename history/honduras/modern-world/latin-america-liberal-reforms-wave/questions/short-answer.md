# Latin america liberal reforms wave — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 28 Reforma Liberal en América" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** one continental wave — Morazán's reforms (1830–1839) named the first great Liberal Reform, Juárez in Mexico (1854–1857), López and Mosquera in Colombia, Venezuela's Federal War, Barrios in Guatemala (1871), Zelaya in Nicaragua (1893), Alfaro in Ecuador — against church property and caudillos, for land markets and world trade

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 28 Reforma Liberal (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the reform's ideology file is completed country by country from Morazán to Alfaro
