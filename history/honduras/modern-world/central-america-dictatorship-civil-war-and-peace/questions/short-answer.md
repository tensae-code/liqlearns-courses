# Central america dictatorship civil war and peace — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 27 Democratización y autoritarismo: las dictaduras" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the 1929 crash breaks the banana-and-coffee economies and dictators rise — Ubico (1931–1944) in Guatemala, Hernández Martínez in El Salvador, whose answer to a rising is La Matanza of 10,000–30,000 including Farabundo Martí, Carías in Honduras, Somoza in Nicaragua — all keeping power by armies, rewritten laws and US companies' support

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 27: los acuerdos de paz" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Costa Rica, army-free since 1949, leads the way out — Esquipulas (1987), Chapultepec for El Salvador (1992), Guatemala's final peace (1996); guerrillas (FSLN, FMLN, URNG) disarm and become legal parties that later win power
