# Video script — Central america dictatorship civil war and peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Central america dictatorship civil war and peace.

the 1929 crash breaks the banana-and-coffee economies and dictators rise — Ubico (1931–1944) in Guatemala, Hernández Martínez in El Salvador, whose answer to a rising is La Matanza of 10,000–30,000 including Farabundo Martí, Carías in Honduras, Somoza in Nicaragua — all keeping power by armies, rewritten laws and US companies' support

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
