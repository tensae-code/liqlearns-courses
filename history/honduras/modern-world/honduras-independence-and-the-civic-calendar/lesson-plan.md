# Lesson plan — Honduras independence and the civic calendar

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Honduras independence and the civic calendar
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my own time, my family and my community — and the nation through its symbols, its anthem and Morazán (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Honduras independence and the civic calendar as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my own time, my family and my community — and the nation through its symbols, its anthem and Morazán — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 26 Nos identificamos con todos: fechas cívicas** — the civic calendar gathers the births of Morazán and Valle, Independence Day and Labour Day, and a picture of the signing of the Act of Independence asks what event it shows
2. **§2 Lección 27 Nos identificamos y así celebramos** — 1 September Flag Day, 15 September independence of Central America, 28 September arrival of the Pliegos de la Independencia, 20 July Lempira Day and 12 October arrival of the Spanish are celebrated as feasts that changed laws and authorities
3. **§3 Lección 26 Nos identificamos con todos (guide)** — civic dates are taught as shared memory — próceres' births, independence, and 14 April Day of the Americas for the history the American peoples share
4. **§4 Lección 27 Nos identificamos y así celebramos (guide)** — the year's civic feasts are walked through in order, from Flag Day to the Pliegos and Lempira Day, each tied to the event it commemorates
5. **§5 Lección 30 Elementos de identidad nacional: fechas cívicas** — a dated civic year runs Honduran Women's Day (25 January), Suyapa (3 February), Afro-Honduran Day (12 April), Day of the Americas (14 April), Spanish Language Day (23 April) and the September independence feasts
6. **§6 Lección 30 Identidad nacional (guide)** — the civic calendar is memorised as the nation's dated self-portrait
