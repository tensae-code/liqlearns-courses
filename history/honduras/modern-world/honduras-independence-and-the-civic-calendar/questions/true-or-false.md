# Honduras independence and the civic calendar — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 26 Nos identificamos con todos (guide)", civic dates are taught as shared memory — próceres' births, independence, and 14 April Day of the Americas for the history the American peoples share (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 27 Nos identificamos y así celebramos (guide)", the year's civic feasts are walked through in order, from Flag Day to the Pliegos and Lempira Day, each tied to the event it commemorates (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
