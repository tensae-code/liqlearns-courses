# Honduras independence and the civic calendar — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26 Nos identificamos con todos: fechas cívicas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the civic calendar gathers the births of Morazán and Valle, Independence Day and Labour Day, and a picture of the signing of the Act of Independence asks what event it shows

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 27 Nos identificamos y así celebramos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1 September Flag Day, 15 September independence of Central America, 28 September arrival of the Pliegos de la Independencia, 20 July Lempira Day and 12 October arrival of the Spanish are celebrated as feasts that changed laws and authorities
