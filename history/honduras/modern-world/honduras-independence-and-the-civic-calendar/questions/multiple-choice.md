# Honduras independence and the civic calendar — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 26 Nos identificamos con todos: fechas cívicas"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the civic calendar gathers the births of Morazán and Valle, Independence Day and Labour Day, and a picture of the signing of the Act of Independence asks what event it shows  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Lección 27 Nos identificamos y así celebramos"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) 1 September Flag Day, 15 September independence of Central America, 28 September arrival of the Pliegos de la Independencia, 20 July Lempira Day and 12 October arrival of the Spanish are celebrated as feasts that changed laws and authorities  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
