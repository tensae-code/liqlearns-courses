# Mexican revolution 1910 1920 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Madero against Díaz" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** foreign-capital growth under Porfirio Díaz neglected urban workers and mestizo and Indigenous peasants until Madero's rising opened the Mexican Revolution — one of the century's three great popular revolutions named here, with Peronism and Cuba
