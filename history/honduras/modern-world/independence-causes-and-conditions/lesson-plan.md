# Lesson plan — Independence causes and conditions

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Independence causes and conditions
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Honduras in depth: Honduran historians and their rival periodisations, the prolonged conquest, the two independence actas, the Federal Republic, the Liberal Reform's enclaves, and the long twentieth century to the 1982 democratic return (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Independence causes and conditions as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — Honduras in depth: Honduran historians and their rival periodisations, the prolonged conquest, the two independence actas, the Federal Republic, the Liberal Reform's enclaves, and the long twentieth century to the 1982 democratic return — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video.
