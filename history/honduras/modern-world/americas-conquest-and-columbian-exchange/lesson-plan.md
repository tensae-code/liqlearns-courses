# Lesson plan — Americas conquest and columbian exchange

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Americas conquest and columbian exchange
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Americas whole: the peopling debate, pre-Hispanic stages, the three great civilisations and all the others, three colonial empires side by side, the independence wave, the continental Liberal Reform, the Cold War and neoliberal Honduras (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Americas conquest and columbian exchange as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Americas whole: the peopling debate, pre-Hispanic stages, the three great civilisations and all the others, three colonial empires side by side, the independence wave, the continental Liberal Reform, the Cold War and neoliberal Honduras — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 25: conquista e intercambio** — Ojeda, Vespucio, Cortés and Ponce de León open Tierra Firme; the conquest's ledger is forced labour, epidemic population collapse, mestizaje and imposed faith — and a two-way exchange of maize, potato, cacao and tomato against wheat, horse, cow and chicken that changes both worlds' tables
2. **§2 Lección 25: intercambio (guide)** — products carried to Europe and brought to America are listed from the exchange diagram
3. **§3 empires fall by alliance** — Cortés took Tenochtitlan with Tlaxcalan allies and Pizarro exploited the Huáscar–Atahualpa civil war; the surviving Aztec sages' 1524 lament to the Franciscans is quoted as the conquered world's own verdict
4. **§4 empires fall by alliance** — the guide teaches the Tlaxcalan alliance against the Aztecs, the Inca civil war exploited by Pizarro, and the Aztec sages' 1524 lament
