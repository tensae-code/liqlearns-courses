# Americas conquest and columbian exchange

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Americas conquest and columbian exchange" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 25: conquista e intercambio

Ojeda, Vespucio, Cortés and Ponce de León open Tierra Firme; the conquest's ledger is forced labour, epidemic population collapse, mestizaje and imposed faith — and a two-way exchange of maize, potato, cacao and tomato against wheat, horse, cow and chicken that changes both worlds' tables

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 25: intercambio (guide)

products carried to Europe and brought to America are listed from the exchange diagram

## Sub lesson 3 — empires fall by alliance

Cortés took Tenochtitlan with Tlaxcalan allies and Pizarro exploited the Huáscar–Atahualpa civil war; the surviving Aztec sages' 1524 lament to the Franciscans is quoted as the conquered world's own verdict

## Sub lesson 4 — empires fall by alliance

the guide teaches the Tlaxcalan alliance against the Aztecs, the Inca civil war exploited by Pizarro, and the Aztec sages' 1524 lament
