# Americas conquest and columbian exchange — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "empires fall by alliance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Cortés took Tenochtitlan with Tlaxcalan allies and Pizarro exploited the Huáscar–Atahualpa civil war; the surviving Aztec sages' 1524 lament to the Franciscans is quoted as the conquered world's own verdict

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "empires fall by alliance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 4)
