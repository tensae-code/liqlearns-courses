## Sub lesson 1 — the first live-televised war

Iraq, indebted from its Iran war and accusing Kuwait of stealing Rumaila oil, invaded on 2 August 1990; a UN coalition of 34 countries — Honduras among them — answered with Desert Shield and Desert Storm (January 1991), Saddam Hussein's "mother of all battles" ended in Kuwait's liberation, US unipolar military hegemony, and a coda: Baghdad's fall in 2003
