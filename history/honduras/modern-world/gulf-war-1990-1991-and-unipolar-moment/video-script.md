# Video script — Gulf war 1990 1991 and unipolar moment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Gulf war 1990 1991 and unipolar moment.

Iraq, indebted from its Iran war and accusing Kuwait of stealing Rumaila oil, invaded on 2 August 1990; a UN coalition of 34 countries — Honduras among them — answered with Desert Shield and Desert Storm (January 1991), Saddam Hussein's "mother of all battles" ended in Kuwait's liberation, US unipolar military hegemony, and a coda: Baghdad's fall in 2003

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
