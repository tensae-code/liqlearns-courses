# Gulf war 1990 1991 and unipolar moment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the first live-televised war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Iraq, indebted from its Iran war and accusing Kuwait of stealing Rumaila oil, invaded on 2 August 1990; a UN coalition of 34 countries — Honduras among them — answered with Desert Shield and Desert Storm (January 1991), Saddam Hussein's "mother of all battles" ended in Kuwait's liberation, US unipolar military hegemony, and a coda: Baghdad's fall in 2003

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the first live-televised war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide teaches the Rumaila oil dispute, the 34-country coalition including Honduras, Desert Storm and the unipolar outcome
