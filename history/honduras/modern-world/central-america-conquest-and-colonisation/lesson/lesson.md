# Central america conquest and colonisation

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Central america conquest and colonisation" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 24 Conquista de Centroamérica

unlike Mexico, the isthmus has no single power whose fall delivers the rest — many tribes in jungle and mountains make the conquest long and hard against firearms, war dogs, horses and disease; it proceeds in three kinds (military, economic, spiritual) and two thrusts, from Mexico and from Panama (1524–1540)

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 25 Colonización de Centroamérica

the Captaincy General of Guatemala rules five provinces through governors, alcaldías mayores, cabildos and bishoprics; encomienda assigns natives to an encomendero to work, be protected and be Christianised, repartimiento drafts their labour, and society stratifies under peninsulares and criollos

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 24 Conquista (guide)

conqueror is matched to country (Balboa, the Alvarados, Hernández de Córdoba, Olid) and the three conquest types distinguished

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 25 Colonización (guide)

encomienda and repartimiento are defined from the pupils' own questions, with the Captaincy's political ladder drawn out

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Central america conquest and colonisation as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
