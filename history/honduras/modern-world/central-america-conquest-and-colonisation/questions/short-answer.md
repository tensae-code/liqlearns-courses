# Central america conquest and colonisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 24 Conquista de Centroamérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** unlike Mexico, the isthmus has no single power whose fall delivers the rest — many tribes in jungle and mountains make the conquest long and hard against firearms, war dogs, horses and disease; it proceeds in three kinds (military, economic, spiritual) and two thrusts, from Mexico and from Panama (1524–1540)

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 25 Colonización de Centroamérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Captaincy General of Guatemala rules five provinces through governors, alcaldías mayores, cabildos and bishoprics; encomienda assigns natives to an encomendero to work, be protected and be Christianised, repartimiento drafts their labour, and society stratifies under peninsulares and criollos
