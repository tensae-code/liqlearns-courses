# Central america conquest and colonisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 24 Conquista (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** conqueror is matched to country (Balboa, the Alvarados, Hernández de Córdoba, Olid) and the three conquest types distinguished

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 25 Colonización (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** encomienda and repartimiento are defined from the pupils' own questions, with the Captaincy's political ladder drawn out
