# Honduras peoples indigenous afro and mestizo — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 36 Diversidad cultural", each ethnic people's food, dance, music, language, sayings, legends and games enrich the national patrimony, and Lenca women of Marcala who pushed coffee exports to Europe show peoples still making history (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 4 Una misma nación (guide)", the teacher presents the ten peoples and eight languages as one nation's diversity and has children compare physical and cultural traits with respect (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
