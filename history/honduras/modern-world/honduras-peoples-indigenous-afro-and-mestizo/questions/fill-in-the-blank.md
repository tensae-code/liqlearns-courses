# Honduras peoples indigenous afro and mestizo — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 33 Un país multicultural" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** mestizos speak Spanish while Miskito, Garífuna, Tol, Chortí and Tawahka tongues survive; punta, tzolkin ceremonies and chilateadas, coconut foods against maize foods — on 3 August 1994 President Reina formally recognises Honduras as multicultural and multiethnic

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 33 Un país multicultural (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** languages, dances and foods are mapped people by people, closing on the 1994 official recognition of a multicultural, multiethnic Honduras
