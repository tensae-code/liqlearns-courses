# Central america indigenous resistance leaders — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 24: los líderes de la resistencia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tecún Umán in Guatemala, Atlacatl in El Salvador, Lempira, Copán Galel, Entepica, Toreba and Cicumba in Honduras, Nicarao in Nicaragua, Garabito in Costa Rica and Urracá in Panama resist to the death; the Crown answers with the Audiencia de los Confines (1542), seated for a time at Gracias

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 24: resistencia (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the named resistance leaders country by country, and 500 years of continuing Indigenous resistance, are the lesson's close
