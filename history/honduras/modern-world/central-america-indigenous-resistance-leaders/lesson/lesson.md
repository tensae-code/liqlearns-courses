# Central america indigenous resistance leaders

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Central america indigenous resistance leaders" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 24: los líderes de la resistencia

Tecún Umán in Guatemala, Atlacatl in El Salvador, Lempira, Copán Galel, Entepica, Toreba and Cicumba in Honduras, Nicarao in Nicaragua, Garabito in Costa Rica and Urracá in Panama resist to the death; the Crown answers with the Audiencia de los Confines (1542), seated for a time at Gracias

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 24: resistencia (guide)

the named resistance leaders country by country, and 500 years of continuing Indigenous resistance, are the lesson's close

## Sub lesson 3 — Lempira and the Honduran resistance

Lempira stands out with Copán Galel and Cicumba among the leaders who organised resistance; Honduras's peoples, hardened by their own wars, made the conquest long and cruel

## Sub lesson 4 — Lempira and the Honduran resistance

the guide names Lempira, Copán Galel and Cicumba as the organisers of Honduran resistance
