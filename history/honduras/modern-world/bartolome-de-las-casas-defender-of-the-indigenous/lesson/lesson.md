## Sub lesson 1 — the Dominican who accused the conquest

Fray Bartolomé de las Casas, who saw the encomienda as slavery, told the Council of the Indies in 1562–63 of "two species of tyranny" — the conquest itself and the repartimientos/encomiendas that stripped America's natural lords of liberty and life
