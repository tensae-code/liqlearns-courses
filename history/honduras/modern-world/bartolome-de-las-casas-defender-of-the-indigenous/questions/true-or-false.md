# Bartolome de las casas defender of the indigenous — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "the Dominican who accused the conquest", Fray Bartolomé de las Casas, who saw the encomienda as slavery, told the Council of the Indies in 1562–63 of "two species of tyranny" — the conquest itself and the repartimientos/encomiendas that stripped America's natural lords of liberty and life (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "the Dominican who accused the conquest", the guide teaches Las Casas's 1562–63 memorial and its "two species of tyranny" charge against conquest and encomienda (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
