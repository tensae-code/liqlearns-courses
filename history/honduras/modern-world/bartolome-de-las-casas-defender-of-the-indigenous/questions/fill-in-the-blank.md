# Bartolome de las casas defender of the indigenous — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the Dominican who accused the conquest" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fray Bartolomé de las Casas, who saw the encomienda as slavery, told the Council of the Indies in 1562–63 of "two species of tyranny" — the conquest itself and the repartimientos/encomiendas that stripped America's natural lords of liberty and life

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the Dominican who accused the conquest" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide teaches Las Casas's 1562–63 memorial and its "two species of tyranny" charge against conquest and encomienda
