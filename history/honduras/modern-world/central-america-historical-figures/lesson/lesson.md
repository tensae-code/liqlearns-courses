# Central america historical figures

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Central america historical figures" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 28 Personajes destacados: letras y derechos

Asturias wins the 1967 Nobel for a literature built on Indigenous heritage and invents magical realism against dictatorship in El Señor Presidente; Monseñor Romero marks El Salvador; Honduras's Clementina Suárez, feminist poet against Carías, Roberto Sosa and Sor María Rosa stand beside Nicaragua's Chamorro, martyr of public liberties against the Somozas

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 28: Carrasco y los Nobel de la Paz

Francisca Carrasco, a peasant who taught herself to read, marches with Costa Rica's troops against William Walker in 1856, fights at Rivas, nurses cholera victims on the walk home and is buried with a general's honours; Óscar Arias (1987) and Rigoberta Menchú (1992) take Nobel Peace Prizes

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 28 Personajes (guide)

one figure per country and field — Asturias, Romero, Padilla, Chamorro — with Honduras's poets and Sor María Rosa

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 28: Carrasco y Nobel (guide)

Carrasco's 1856 march against Walker and the two Nobel Peace laureates close the figures lesson
