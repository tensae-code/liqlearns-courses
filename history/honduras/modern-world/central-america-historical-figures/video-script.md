# Video script — Central america historical figures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Central america historical figures.

Asturias wins the 1967 Nobel for a literature built on Indigenous heritage and invents magical realism against dictatorship in El Señor Presidente; Monseñor Romero marks El Salvador; Honduras's Clementina Suárez, feminist poet against Carías, Roberto Sosa and Sor María Rosa stand beside Nicaragua's Chamorro, martyr of public liberties against the Somozas

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
