# Central america historical figures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 28 Personajes destacados: letras y derechos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Asturias wins the 1967 Nobel for a literature built on Indigenous heritage and invents magical realism against dictatorship in El Señor Presidente; Monseñor Romero marks El Salvador; Honduras's Clementina Suárez, feminist poet against Carías, Roberto Sosa and Sor María Rosa stand beside Nicaragua's Chamorro, martyr of public liberties against the Somozas

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 28: Carrasco y los Nobel de la Paz" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Francisca Carrasco, a peasant who taught herself to read, marches with Costa Rica's troops against William Walker in 1856, fights at Rivas, nurses cholera victims on the walk home and is buried with a general's honours; Óscar Arias (1987) and Rigoberta Menchú (1992) take Nobel Peace Prizes
