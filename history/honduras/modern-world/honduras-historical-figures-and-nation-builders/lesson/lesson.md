# Honduras historical figures and nation builders

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras historical figures and nation builders" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 28 Personajes de la historia de Honduras

the próceres are joined by nation-builders in every field — Reyes founding the university, scientist Salvador Moncada, painter José Antonio Velásquez, musician Guillermo Anderson, women's-rights defender Visitación Padilla, novelist Lucila Gamero de Medina, rector Julieta Castellanos

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 28 Personajes (guide)

statues, busts, school and street names are the memory map; women (Padilla, Gamero, Castellanos) stand beside the próceres

## Sub lesson 3 — biographies of outstanding Hondurans

writers Rafael Heliodoro Valle, Clementina Suárez ("la mujer nueva") and Alfonso Guillén Zelaya, painters, singer Guillermo Anderson and activist Berta Cáceres are paraded as proof Honduras produces talent in every field

## Sub lesson 4 — biographies of outstanding Hondurans

the guide teaches the same gallery — Valle, Clementina Suárez, Guillén Zelaya, Guillermo Anderson, Berta Cáceres — as identity-building national biography
