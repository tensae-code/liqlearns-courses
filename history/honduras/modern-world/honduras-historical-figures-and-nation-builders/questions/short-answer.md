# Honduras historical figures and nation builders — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 28 Personajes de la historia de Honduras" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the próceres are joined by nation-builders in every field — Reyes founding the university, scientist Salvador Moncada, painter José Antonio Velásquez, musician Guillermo Anderson, women's-rights defender Visitación Padilla, novelist Lucila Gamero de Medina, rector Julieta Castellanos

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 28 Personajes (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** statues, busts, school and street names are the memory map; women (Padilla, Gamero, Castellanos) stand beside the próceres
