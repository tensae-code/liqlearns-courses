# Honduras historical figures and nation builders — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "biographies of outstanding Hondurans", writers Rafael Heliodoro Valle, Clementina Suárez ("la mujer nueva") and Alfonso Guillén Zelaya, painters, singer Guillermo Anderson and activist Berta Cáceres are paraded as proof Honduras produces talent in every field (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "biographies of outstanding Hondurans", the guide teaches the same gallery — Valle, Clementina Suárez, Guillén Zelaya, Guillermo Anderson, Berta Cáceres — as identity-building national biography (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
