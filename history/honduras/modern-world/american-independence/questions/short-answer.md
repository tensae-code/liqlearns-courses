# American independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 27 Período independiente: Estados Unidos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** taxes and trade limits radicalise the thirteen colonies; Samuel Adams calls the Philadelphia congress, and on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration, with Washington, Franklin and John Adams, opens the first American independence
