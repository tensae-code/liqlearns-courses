# American independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 27 Período independiente: Estados Unidos", taxes and trade limits radicalise the thirteen colonies; Samuel Adams calls the Philadelphia congress, and on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration, with Washington, Franklin and John Adams, opens the first American independence (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 27: Estados Unidos (guide)", date, participants and cause of US independence are the guide's set questions (Sub lesson 2)
