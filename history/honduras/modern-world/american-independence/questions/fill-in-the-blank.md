# American independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 27 Período independiente: Estados Unidos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** taxes and trade limits radicalise the thirteen colonies; Samuel Adams calls the Philadelphia congress, and on 4 July 1776 Jefferson's Declaration, with Washington, Franklin and John Adams, opens the first American independence

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 27: Estados Unidos (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** date, participants and cause of US independence are the guide's set questions
