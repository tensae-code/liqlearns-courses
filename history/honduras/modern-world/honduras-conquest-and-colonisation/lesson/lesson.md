# Honduras conquest and colonisation

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras conquest and colonisation" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 23 Conquista y colonización: la conquista

Gil González Dávila comes for gold, Cortés sends Cristóbal de Olid, who founds Triunfo de la Cruz, captures Dávila, betrays Cortés and is condemned to die for treason; Cortés restores order, stays a year and leaves Hernando de Saavedra as governor

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 23: la colonización y sus consecuencias

mining cities rise (Trujillo, Comayagua, Tegucigalpa, Yuscarán) inside the Captaincy of Guatemala under New Spain; Indigenous people lose land, are forced to labour, resist and rebel, while Catholicism, Spanish, new classes, mestizaje and African slavery remake the land

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 23: la conquista (guide)

Dávila, Olid's betrayal and death sentence, Cortés's year of order and Saavedra's governorship are the guide's conquest narrative

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 23: la colonización (guide)

pupils write the biographies of Cortés and Olid and trace how founded cities, forced labour and rebellion followed

## Sub lesson 5 — the prolonged conquest of Honduras

unlike the Aztec and Inca empires that fell with their chiefs, Central America's many dispersed peoples resisted from 1502 to 1573; in Honduras the zones of Trujillo-Aguán, the east, the west-centre and the Ulúa-Sula valley held out, and whole populations were decimated by mita and encomienda

## Sub lesson 6 — colonisation: cities, encomienda and mestizaje

grid-plan cities rose around the plaza mayor; encomienda, repartimiento, mita and slavery powered mining and farming, and the meeting of Europeans, Indigenous peoples and Africans made Honduras a crucible — today 90% mestizo

## Sub lesson 7 — the prolonged conquest of Honduras

the guide's key stresses the 1502–1573 conquest, the resistance zones (Trujillo-Aguán, east, west-centre, Ulúa-Sula) and the demographic catastrophe of mita and encomienda

## Sub lesson 8 — colonisation: cities, encomienda and mestizaje

the guide teaches grid cities, encomienda/repartimiento/mita/slavery and the three-root mestizaje that makes Honduras 90% mestizo
