# Honduras conquest and colonisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the prolonged conquest of Honduras" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** the guide's key stresses the 1502–1573 conquest, the resistance zones (Trujillo-Aguán, east, west-centre, Ulúa-Sula) and the demographic catastrophe of mita and encomienda

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "colonisation: cities, encomienda and mestizaje" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** the guide teaches grid cities, encomienda/repartimiento/mita/slavery and the three-root mestizaje that makes Honduras 90% mestizo
