# Honduras conquest and colonisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "the prolonged conquest of Honduras" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** unlike the Aztec and Inca empires that fell with their chiefs, Central America's many dispersed peoples resisted from 1502 to 1573; in Honduras the zones of Trujillo-Aguán, the east, the west-centre and the Ulúa-Sula valley held out, and whole populations were decimated by mita and encomienda

**2.** Tell the story of "colonisation: cities, encomienda and mestizaje" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** grid-plan cities rose around the plaza mayor; encomienda, repartimiento, mita and slavery powered mining and farming, and the meeting of Europeans, Indigenous peoples and Africans made Honduras a crucible — today 90% mestizo
