Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Lección 23 Conquista y colonización: la conquista"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Gil González Dávila comes for gold, Cortés sends Cristóbal de Olid, who founds Triunfo de la Cruz, captures Dávila, betrays Cortés and is condemned to die for treason; Cortés restores order, stays a year and leaves Hernando de Saavedra as governor  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
