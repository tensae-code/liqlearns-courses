# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 26 El departamento a través del tiempo: Colón en Trujillo

Columbus lands at Trujillo on 14 August 1502 and names it Punta Caxinas (today Puerto Castilla) — the department of Colón still tells the event as its own founding story

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 26 (guide)

Columbus's 1502 landing at Trujillo is the entry event for the department-by-department historical record

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 22 El descubrimiento de nuestro territorio

backed by Ferdinand and Isabella, Columbus sails for the Indies in 1492 with paper, compass and caravels; only on the fourth voyage (1502) does he reach Guanaja, the Island of Pines, and Punta Caxinas, where the first mass on the mainland is celebrated

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 22 El descubrimiento (guide)

the four voyages are mapped from Iberia; the fourth reaches Guanaja and Punta Caxinas in 1502

## Sub lesson 5 — Lección 23 La llegada de los españoles

a Spain unified under Castile and Aragon, Granada taken and Constantinople fallen (1453), backs Columbus; the four voyages are told ship by ship — Rodrigo de Triana's "¡Tierra!" at Guanahaní, 17 ships and 1,500 men on the second — and the Treaty of Tordesillas splits the world between Spain and Portugal

## Sub lesson 6 — Lección 23 La llegada de los españoles (guide)

15th-century Spain is summarised, one main idea extracted per voyage, and Tordesillas closes the lesson

## Sub lesson 7 — Lección 26: la era de los descubrimientos

compass, caravel, astrolabe and printing open the age of Columbus, Vasco da Gama and Magellan; the world is mapped, the first world economy forms, and colonialism's bases are laid

## Sub lesson 8 — Lección 26: descubrimientos (guide)

pupils identify which invention made America's discovery possible
