# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 22 El descubrimiento de nuestro territorio", backed by Ferdinand and Isabella, Columbus sails for the Indies in 1492 with paper, compass and caravels; only on the fourth voyage (1502) does he reach Guanaja, the Island of Pines, and Punta Caxinas, where the first mass on the mainland is celebrated (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 22 El descubrimiento (guide)", the four voyages are mapped from Iberia; the fourth reaches Guanaja and Punta Caxinas in 1502 (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
