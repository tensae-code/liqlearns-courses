# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 23 La llegada de los españoles" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** a Spain unified under Castile and Aragon, Granada taken and Constantinople fallen (1453), backs Columbus; the four voyages are told ship by ship — Rodrigo de Triana's "¡Tierra!" at Guanahaní, 17 ships and 1,500 men on the second — and the Treaty of Tordesillas splits the world between Spain and Portugal

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 23 La llegada de los españoles (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** 15th-century Spain is summarised, one main idea extracted per voyage, and Tordesillas closes the lesson
