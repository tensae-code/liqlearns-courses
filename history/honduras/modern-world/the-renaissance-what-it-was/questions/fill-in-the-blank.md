# The renaissance what it was — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26 La Edad Moderna: el Renacimiento" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Italy puts the human back at the centre — humanism trusts observation and experience, printing spreads knowledge, and Michelangelo and Leonardo da Vinci remake art

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26: Renacimiento (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** humanism and the printing press are the guide's twin engines of the age
