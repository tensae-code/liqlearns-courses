# The second world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 27: la Segunda Guerra Mundial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a Germany aggrieved by Versailles and broken by the 1929 crash follows Hitler; Axis (Berlin, Rome, Tokyo) fights Allies (Britain, France, Russia, USA) until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in August 1945

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 27: Segunda Guerra (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Versailles, the crash and Hitler are chained as cause to war to atomic end
