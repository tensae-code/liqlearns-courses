# The second world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 27: la Segunda Guerra Mundial" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a Germany aggrieved by Versailles and broken by the 1929 crash follows Hitler; Axis (Berlin, Rome, Tokyo) fights Allies (Britain, France, Russia, USA) until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in August 1945

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 27: Segunda Guerra (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Versailles, the crash and Hitler are chained as cause to war to atomic end
