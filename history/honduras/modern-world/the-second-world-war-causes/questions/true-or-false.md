# The second world war causes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 27: la Segunda Guerra Mundial", a Germany aggrieved by Versailles and broken by the 1929 crash follows Hitler; Axis (Berlin, Rome, Tokyo) fights Allies (Britain, France, Russia, USA) until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in August 1945 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 27: Segunda Guerra (guide)", Versailles, the crash and Hitler are chained as cause to war to atomic end (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
