# The second world war causes

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The second world war causes" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 27: la Segunda Guerra Mundial

a Germany aggrieved by Versailles and broken by the 1929 crash follows Hitler; Axis (Berlin, Rome, Tokyo) fights Allies (Britain, France, Russia, USA) until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima and Nagasaki in August 1945

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 27: Segunda Guerra (guide)

Versailles, the crash and Hitler are chained as cause to war to atomic end
