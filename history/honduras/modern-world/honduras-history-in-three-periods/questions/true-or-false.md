# Honduras history in three periods — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 20: los tres períodos y Comayagua", Honduras's past is divided into pre-Hispanic, colonial (1502–1821) and independent (from 15 September 1821); Comayagua, founded by Alonso de Cáceres in 1537 as Santa María de la Nueva Valladolid, is capital from 1540 until the capital moves to Tegucigalpa in 1880 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 20: tres períodos (guide)", Comayagua's story (Lenca name, Cáceres 1537, capital 1540–1880) anchors the pre-Hispanic / colonial / independent division of the national past (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
