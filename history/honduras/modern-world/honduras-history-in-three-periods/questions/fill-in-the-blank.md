# Honduras history in three periods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 20: los tres períodos y Comayagua" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Honduras's past is divided into pre-Hispanic, colonial (1502–1821) and independent (from 15 September 1821); Comayagua, founded by Alonso de Cáceres in 1537 as Santa María de la Nueva Valladolid, is capital from 1540 until the capital moves to Tegucigalpa in 1880

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 20: tres períodos (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Comayagua's story (Lenca name, Cáceres 1537, capital 1540–1880) anchors the pre-Hispanic / colonial / independent division of the national past
