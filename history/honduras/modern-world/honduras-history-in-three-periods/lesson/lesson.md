# Honduras history in three periods

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras history in three periods" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 20: los tres períodos y Comayagua

Honduras's past is divided into pre-Hispanic, colonial (1502–1821) and independent (from 15 September 1821); Comayagua, founded by Alonso de Cáceres in 1537 as Santa María de la Nueva Valladolid, is capital from 1540 until the capital moves to Tegucigalpa in 1880

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 20: tres períodos (guide)

Comayagua's story (Lenca name, Cáceres 1537, capital 1540–1880) anchors the pre-Hispanic / colonial / independent division of the national past
