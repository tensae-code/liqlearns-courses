# Honduras democracy since 1982 and the 2009 crisis — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "the long 20th century", Carías ruled 16 years, Gálvez continued his roads and coffee support, the 1949–1963 reformismo modernised, and after military hegemony Suazo Córdova's 1981 election victory opened the democratic era in 1982 (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "the long 20th century", the guide teaches Carías's 16 years, the 1949–1963 reformismo and the 1982 democratic return under Suazo Córdova (Sub lesson 4)
