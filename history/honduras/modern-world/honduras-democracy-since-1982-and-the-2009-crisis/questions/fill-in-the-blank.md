# Honduras democracy since 1982 and the 2009 crisis — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the long 20th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Carías ruled 16 years, Gálvez continued his roads and coffee support, the 1949–1963 reformismo modernised, and after military hegemony Suazo Córdova's 1981 election victory opened the democratic era in 1982
