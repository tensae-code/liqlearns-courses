# Honduras democracy since 1982 and the 2009 crisis — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 29 Democracia electoral en Honduras" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** after Gálvez, Villeda Morales, military rulers and a Junta, the 1980 Constituent Assembly writes the still-current constitution and continuous democracy begins in 1982; in 2009 President Zelaya is deposed and Micheletti installed, and new parties (PAC, Libre, FAPER, APH) are born of the crisis

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 29 Democracia electoral (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the presidents' gallery from Suazo Córdova to Hernández, the 2009 deposition of Zelaya, and the parties born after it
