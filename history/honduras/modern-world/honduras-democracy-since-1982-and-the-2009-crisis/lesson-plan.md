# Lesson plan — Honduras democracy since 1982 and the 2009 crisis

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Honduras democracy since 1982 and the 2009 crisis
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Honduras whole in three periods — pre-Hispanic, colonial, independent: Copán and the pre-Hispanic peoples, discovery and conquest, independence, Morazán and the Federation, the Liberal Reform and its enclaves, and democracy to the 2009 crisis (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Honduras democracy since 1982 and the 2009 crisis as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Honduras whole in three periods — pre-Hispanic, colonial, independent: Copán and the pre-Hispanic peoples, discovery and conquest, independence, Morazán and the Federation, the Liberal Reform and its enclaves, and democracy to the 2009 crisis — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 29 Democracia electoral en Honduras** — after Gálvez, Villeda Morales, military rulers and a Junta, the 1980 Constituent Assembly writes the still-current constitution and continuous democracy begins in 1982; in 2009 President Zelaya is deposed and Micheletti installed, and new parties (PAC, Libre, FAPER, APH) are born of the crisis
2. **§2 Lección 29 Democracia electoral (guide)** — the presidents' gallery from Suazo Córdova to Hernández, the 2009 deposition of Zelaya, and the parties born after it
3. **§3 the long 20th century** — Carías ruled 16 years, Gálvez continued his roads and coffee support, the 1949–1963 reformismo modernised, and after military hegemony Suazo Córdova's 1981 election victory opened the democratic era in 1982
4. **§4 the long 20th century** — the guide teaches Carías's 16 years, the 1949–1963 reformismo and the 1982 democratic return under Suazo Córdova

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
