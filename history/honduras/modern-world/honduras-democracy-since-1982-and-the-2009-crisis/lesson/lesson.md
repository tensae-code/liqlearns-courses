# Honduras democracy since 1982 and the 2009 crisis

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras democracy since 1982 and the 2009 crisis" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 29 Democracia electoral en Honduras

after Gálvez, Villeda Morales, military rulers and a Junta, the 1980 Constituent Assembly writes the still-current constitution and continuous democracy begins in 1982; in 2009 President Zelaya is deposed and Micheletti installed, and new parties (PAC, Libre, FAPER, APH) are born of the crisis

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 29 Democracia electoral (guide)

the presidents' gallery from Suazo Córdova to Hernández, the 2009 deposition of Zelaya, and the parties born after it

## Sub lesson 3 — the long 20th century

Carías ruled 16 years, Gálvez continued his roads and coffee support, the 1949–1963 reformismo modernised, and after military hegemony Suazo Córdova's 1981 election victory opened the democratic era in 1982

## Sub lesson 4 — the long 20th century

the guide teaches Carías's 16 years, the 1949–1963 reformismo and the 1982 democratic return under Suazo Córdova
