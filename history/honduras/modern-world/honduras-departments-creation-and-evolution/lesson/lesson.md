# Honduras departments creation and evolution

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras departments creation and evolution" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 24 Mi departamento: su origen y evolución

departments are born after independence from Spain (1821): Herrera creates the first seven, the Bay Islands belong to England until 1860, and Gracias a Dios, the last, is founded in 1957 to make 18

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 26: hechos históricos de los departamentos

Ocotepeque is destroyed by the Marchala flood in 1934 and refounded as Nueva Ocotepeque in 1935; Amapala is named capital in 1876 for Soto's provisional government; Santa Bárbara (1825) is divided to create Cortés in 1893

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 33 Creación de mi departamento

the first departmental division is set on 28 June 1825 with seven departments; Congress creates the rest one by one until there are 18 departments, 298 municipalities and thousands of villages

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 24 Mi departamento (guide)

the 1825 map of seven departments is read against today's 18, and each child relates their own life to their department's evolution

## Sub lesson 5 — Lección 26: hechos departamentales (guide)

a flood that moves a city, a port made capital, a department divided — department history is taught as dated turning points

## Sub lesson 6 — Lección 33 Creación de mi departamento (guide)

the 1825 seven-department division is the founding act; departments are created by Congress and subdivided into municipalities
