# Honduras departments creation and evolution — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 26: hechos departamentales (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** a flood that moves a city, a port made capital, a department divided — department history is taught as dated turning points

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 33 Creación de mi departamento (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the 1825 seven-department division is the founding act; departments are created by Congress and subdivided into municipalities
