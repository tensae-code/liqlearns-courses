# Honduras departments creation and evolution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 24 Mi departamento: su origen y evolución" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** departments are born after independence from Spain (1821): Herrera creates the first seven, the Bay Islands belong to England until 1860, and Gracias a Dios, the last, is founded in 1957 to make 18

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 26: hechos históricos de los departamentos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ocotepeque is destroyed by the Marchala flood in 1934 and refounded as Nueva Ocotepeque in 1935; Amapala is named capital in 1876 for Soto's provisional government; Santa Bárbara (1825) is divided to create Cortés in 1893
