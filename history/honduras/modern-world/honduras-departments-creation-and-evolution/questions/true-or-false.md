# Honduras departments creation and evolution — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 33 Creación de mi departamento", the first departmental division is set on 28 June 1825 with seven departments; Congress creates the rest one by one until there are 18 departments, 298 municipalities and thousands of villages (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lección 24 Mi departamento (guide)", the 1825 map of seven departments is read against today's 18, and each child relates their own life to their department's evolution (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
