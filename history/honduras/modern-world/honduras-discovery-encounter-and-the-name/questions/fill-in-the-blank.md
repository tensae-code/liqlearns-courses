# Honduras discovery encounter and the name — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 22: encuentro de dos culturas y el nombre Honduras" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two unlike worlds meet — armour, horses, gunpowder and monarchy against maize, nature-linked spirituality and hunting weapons; storm-tossed Spaniards thank God they escaped "estas Honduras", Guaymuras and Higueras give way to the name Honduras

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 22: encuentro y nombre (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the two cultures are compared item by item, and the storm story explains how Honduras got its name
