# Honduras discovery encounter and the name

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Honduras discovery encounter and the name" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 22: encuentro de dos culturas y el nombre Honduras

two unlike worlds meet — armour, horses, gunpowder and monarchy against maize, nature-linked spirituality and hunting weapons; storm-tossed Spaniards thank God they escaped "estas Honduras", Guaymuras and Higueras give way to the name Honduras

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 22: encuentro y nombre (guide)

the two cultures are compared item by item, and the storm story explains how Honduras got its name

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 23: consecuencias del descubrimiento

Europe gains gold, silver, tobacco and empires and the Atlantic becomes the highway; America gains colonists, mestizaje, slave sales, mine extraction, an imposed culture, language and religion, unknown diseases and a falling population

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 23: consecuencias (guide)

advantages and disadvantages of the arrival are debated for both worlds, tied to the national anthem's second stanza

## Sub lesson 5 — the "encounter of cultures" debate

1492 was chance, not plan: Columbus miscalculated, and the book insists a populated continent cannot be "discovered" — two worlds met, with Galeano's "Las venas abiertas" assigned as the radiography of what followed

## Sub lesson 6 — the "encounter of cultures" debate

the guide teaches 1492 as a chance encounter, not a discovery of a populated continent, with Galeano's "Las venas abiertas" as the set reading
