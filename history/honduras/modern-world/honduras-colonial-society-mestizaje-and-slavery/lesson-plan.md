## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 24 Vida social y cultural en la colonia: las clases** — three centuries of colony sort people by skin — peninsulares on top, criollos without political power, Indigenous as the labour base, mestizos born of the mixing, Black Africans brought for the heaviest work — a new world built from the three peoples
2. **§2 Lección 24: mujeres, Iglesia y enfermedad** — the Church teaches reading, Spanish and submission; Indigenous and African women are subjected and the mestizo people is born; epidemics the natives cannot resist (smallpox, measles, plague) shrink the population, so from 1545 Black slaves are imported for the mines
3. **§3 Lección 24: clases coloniales (guide)** — the social pyramid — peninsulares, criollos, Indigenous, mestizos, Black Africans — is built as the colony's organising fact
4. **§4 Lección 24: enfermedad y esclavitud (guide)** — epidemics and forced work empty the land, and from 1545 enslaved Africans are brought to the mines
5. **§5 Lección 25: el impacto demográfico** — conquest and colonisation cost some 90% of the Indigenous population (smallpox, measles, flu, tuberculosis, harsh treatment, slave traffic, mestizaje); the 1778 census counts 811,675 in the Kingdom of Guatemala, and lost labour feeds the African slave traffic
6. **§6 Lección 25: demografía colonial (guide)** — the 90% collapse and the 1778 census figures are the numbers lesson of the colony
7. **§7 colonial government of Honduras** — the Audiencia de los Confines (1542, first seated at Gracias) governed Honduras, moving to Guatemala in 1566; the Alcaldía Mayor de Tegucigalpa (1578) was created to control mining, and from 1609 Honduras joined the Captaincy General of Guatemala
8. **§8 colonial government of Honduras** — the guide's key: Audiencia de los Confines (1542, Gracias), its move to Guatemala (1566), the Alcaldía Mayor de Tegucigalpa (1578) and the Captaincy General from 1609
