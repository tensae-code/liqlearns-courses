# Honduras colonial society mestizaje and slavery — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lección 25: el impacto demográfico" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** conquest and colonisation cost some 90% of the Indigenous population (smallpox, measles, flu, tuberculosis, harsh treatment, slave traffic, mestizaje); the 1778 census counts 811,675 in the Kingdom of Guatemala, and lost labour feeds the African slave traffic

**2.** Tell the story of "Lección 25: demografía colonial (guide)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** the 90% collapse and the 1778 census figures are the numbers lesson of the colony
