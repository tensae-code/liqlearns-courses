# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lección 27: Revolución Francesa (guide)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Bastille and the rights of man open the contemporary age

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "1789 and the rights of man" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** a bourgeoisie educated by the Enlightenment rose against feudal inequality and absolutist terror; the guillotine took monarchs and revolutionaries alike, and the revolution's direct fruit was the modern constitutional state of three powers
