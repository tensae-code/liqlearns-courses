# Lesson plan — Latin america border settlements and 20th century wars

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Latin america border settlements and 20th century wars

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video.
