# Latin america border settlements and 20th century wars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "the last border changes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Panama split from Colombia in 1903; Arica went to Chile and Tacna back to Peru (1929); Leticia to Colombia (1934); Bolivia and Paraguay bled in the Chaco War (1932–1935); Ecuador–Peru fought in 1941 and settled at Rio in 1942
