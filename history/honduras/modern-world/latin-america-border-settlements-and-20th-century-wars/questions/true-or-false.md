# Latin america border settlements and 20th century wars — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "the last border changes", Panama split from Colombia in 1903; Arica went to Chile and Tacna back to Peru (1929); Leticia to Colombia (1934); Bolivia and Paraguay bled in the Chaco War (1932–1935); Ecuador–Peru fought in 1941 and settled at Rio in 1942 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "the last border changes", the guide teaches Panama 1903, Arica/Tacna 1929, Leticia 1934, the Chaco War 1932–1935 and Ecuador–Peru 1941–1942 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
