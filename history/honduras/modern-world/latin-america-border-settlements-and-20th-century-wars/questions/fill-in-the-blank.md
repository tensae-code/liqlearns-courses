# Latin america border settlements and 20th century wars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the last border changes" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Panama split from Colombia in 1903; Arica went to Chile and Tacna back to Peru (1929); Leticia to Colombia (1934); Bolivia and Paraguay bled in the Chaco War (1932–1935); Ecuador–Peru fought in 1941 and settled at Rio in 1942

**Answer:** the guide teaches Panama 1903, Arica/Tacna 1929, Leticia 1934, the Chaco War 1932–1935 and Ecuador–Peru 1941–1942
