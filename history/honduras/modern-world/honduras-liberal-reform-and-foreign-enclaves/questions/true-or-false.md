# Honduras liberal reform and foreign enclaves — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Lección 27: la Reforma (guide)", Soto and Rosa's programme is taught as a young nation's wish-list — free thought, secular schooling, the vote, coffee, bananas and mines (Sub lesson 3)
