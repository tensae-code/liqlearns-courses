# Video script — The first world war course of the war

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The first world war course of the war.

Balkan, Franco-German and Anglo-German rivalries primed the powder; Sarajevo lit it; the Lusitania sinking brought the US in; trenches infested with rats and disease were the soldiers' hell; Russia's October Revolution and Germany's November 1918 socialist revolution forced the Compiègne armistice, and Versailles (28 June 1919) closed a war of over 9 million dead

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
