# The first world war course of the war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "the trench war", the 2022 guide's model answers name the Sarajevo assassination as detonator, the belligerents (Germany, Italy, Austria-Hungary, Russia, Britain, France) and the consequences — mass chemical warfare, expanded powers, demographic imbalance, human losses — with the six-part historieta activity (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "the trench war", Balkan, Franco-German and Anglo-German rivalries primed the powder; Sarajevo lit it; the Lusitania sinking brought the US in; trenches infested with rats and disease were the soldiers' hell; Russia's October Revolution and Germany's November 1918 socialist revolution forced the Compiègne armistice, and Versailles (28 June 1919) closed a war of over 9 million dead (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
