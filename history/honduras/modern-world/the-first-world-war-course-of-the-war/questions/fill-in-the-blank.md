# The first world war course of the war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the trench war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Balkan, Franco-German and Anglo-German rivalries primed the powder; Sarajevo lit it; the Lusitania sinking brought the US in; trenches infested with rats and disease were the soldiers' hell; Russia's October Revolution and Germany's November 1918 socialist revolution forced the Compiègne armistice, and Versailles (28 June 1919) closed a war of over 9 million dead

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "the trench war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide's key lists the pre-war rivalries, Sarajevo, the Lusitania, trench warfare, the two 1917–18 revolutions and Versailles's 9 million dead
