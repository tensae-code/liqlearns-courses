# Video script — The second world war course in europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The second world war course in europe.

Versailles' un-revised humiliations, fascism's rise and the 1929 crisis built the Axis; fronts ran through Europe, Africa (Rommel, the Desert Fox, beaten by Montgomery) and the Pacific; Hitler's Holocaust against the Jews, his suicide on 30 April and Germany's capitulation on 8 May 1945 led to Yalta and Potsdam's four occupation zones — some 60 million dead, 2% of humanity

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
