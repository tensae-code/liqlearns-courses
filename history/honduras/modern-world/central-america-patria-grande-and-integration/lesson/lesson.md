# Central america patria grande and integration

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Central america patria grande and integration" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 29: el sueño de Morazán y la integración

every integration attempt tries to revive Morazán's Federation dream and has failed as union, yet Esquipulas (Cerezo, Azcona) births SICA and the Central American Parliament as regional institutions

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 14 América Central, mi patria grande

Centroamérica is a historical term — the five Federation countries — while América Central is the geographic isthmus of seven, Panama once part of Colombia; the distinction itself is a history lesson

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 32 La integración de América Central

SICA summits of heads of state pursue a region of peace, liberty, democracy and development — integration as a present-day political project with a Federation past

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 29: integración (guide)

Cerezo and Azcona at Esquipulas, SICA and PARLACEN — integration as Morazán's dream pursued by institutions

## Sub lesson 5 — Lección 14 La patria grande (guide)

historical Centroamérica against geographic América Central is the guide's framing distinction for the whole block

## Sub lesson 6 — Lección 32 Integración (guide)

SICA's summitry is presented as the current form of the old unionist project

## Sub lesson 7 — Lección 31 Bloques de integración en América

NAFTA, SICA, CARICOM, MERCOSUR and CAN map a continent organising into blocs for trade, technology and bargaining power

## Sub lesson 8 — Lección 31 Bloques (guide)

each bloc is assigned to its region and Honduras's memberships named

## Sub lesson 9 — the integration blocs

the 20th century answered fragmentation with blocs — the Central American Common Market (planned since 1951), the Andean Community (1969) and Mercosur (1991) — while US modernisation arrived as the Alliance for Progress

## Sub lesson 10 — the integration blocs

the guide's key lists the Central American Common Market, the Andean Community (1969), Mercosur (1991) and the Alliance for Progress
