# Lesson plan — Central america patria grande and integration

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Central america patria grande and integration
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Central America, the patria grande: method first, then the isthmus's peoples, a hard conquest, colonisation, independence and Federation, dictatorships, civil war and the Esquipulas peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Central america patria grande and integration as Honduras's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Central America, the patria grande: method first, then the isthmus's peoples, a hard conquest, colonisation, independence and Federation, dictatorships, civil war and the Esquipulas peace — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 29: el sueño de Morazán y la integración** — every integration attempt tries to revive Morazán's Federation dream and has failed as union, yet Esquipulas (Cerezo, Azcona) births SICA and the Central American Parliament as regional institutions
2. **§2 Lección 14 América Central, mi patria grande** — Centroamérica is a historical term — the five Federation countries — while América Central is the geographic isthmus of seven, Panama once part of Colombia; the distinction itself is a history lesson
3. **§3 Lección 32 La integración de América Central** — SICA summits of heads of state pursue a region of peace, liberty, democracy and development — integration as a present-day political project with a Federation past
4. **§4 Lección 29: integración (guide)** — Cerezo and Azcona at Esquipulas, SICA and PARLACEN — integration as Morazán's dream pursued by institutions
5. **§5 Lección 14 La patria grande (guide)** — historical Centroamérica against geographic América Central is the guide's framing distinction for the whole block
6. **§6 Lección 32 Integración (guide)** — SICA's summitry is presented as the current form of the old unionist project
7. **§7 Lección 31 Bloques de integración en América** — NAFTA, SICA, CARICOM, MERCOSUR and CAN map a continent organising into blocs for trade, technology and bargaining power
8. **§8 Lección 31 Bloques (guide)** — each bloc is assigned to its region and Honduras's memberships named
9. **§9 the integration blocs** — the 20th century answered fragmentation with blocs — the Central American Common Market (planned since 1951), the Andean Community (1969) and Mercosur (1991) — while US modernisation arrived as the Alliance for Progress
10. **§10 the integration blocs** — the guide's key lists the Central American Common Market, the Andean Community (1969), Mercosur (1991) and the Alliance for Progress
