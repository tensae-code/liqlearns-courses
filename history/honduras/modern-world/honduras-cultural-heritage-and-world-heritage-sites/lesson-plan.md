# Lesson plan — Honduras cultural heritage and world heritage sites

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Honduras cultural heritage and world heritage sites
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Honduras in depth: Honduran historians and their rival periodisations, the prolonged conquest, the two independence actas, the Federal Republic, the Liberal Reform's enclaves, and the long twentieth century to the 1982 democratic return (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 protecting the national patrimony** — the 1984 Heritage Law (Decree 81-84) protects colonial monuments and movable goods, and three Honduran sites hold UNESCO World Heritage status: Copán Ruins (1980), the Río Plátano Biosphere (1982) and San Marcos de Colón (2017)
2. **§2 protecting the national patrimony** — the guide's key: Heritage Law Decree 81-84 and the UNESCO sites Copán (1980), Río Plátano (1982) and San Marcos de Colón (2017)
