# Honduras cultural heritage and world heritage sites — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "protecting the national patrimony", the 1984 Heritage Law (Decree 81-84) protects colonial monuments and movable goods, and three Honduran sites hold UNESCO World Heritage status: Copán Ruins (1980), the Río Plátano Biosphere (1982) and San Marcos de Colón (2017) (Sub lesson 1)
