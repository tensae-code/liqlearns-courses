# Honduras cultural heritage and world heritage sites — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "protecting the national patrimony" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the 1984 Heritage Law (Decree 81-84) protects colonial monuments and movable goods, and three Honduran sites hold UNESCO World Heritage status: Copán Ruins (1980), the Río Plátano Biosphere (1982) and San Marcos de Colón (2017)

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "protecting the national patrimony" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide's key: Heritage Law Decree 81-84 and the UNESCO sites Copán (1980), Río Plátano (1982) and San Marcos de Colón (2017)
