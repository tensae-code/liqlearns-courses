# Honduras cultural heritage and world heritage sites — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "protecting the national patrimony" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the 1984 Heritage Law (Decree 81-84) protects colonial monuments and movable goods, and three Honduran sites hold UNESCO World Heritage status: Copán Ruins (1980), the Río Plátano Biosphere (1982) and San Marcos de Colón (2017)
