## Sub lesson 1 — Engels names the revolution

Friedrich Engels coined "Industrial Revolution" (c. 1845) for the substitution of skilled hands by machines; in England peasants were displaced from enclosed land turned to sheep pasture for the wool mills, and Watt's steam engine, the locomotive, telegraph and steamship remade production and trade
