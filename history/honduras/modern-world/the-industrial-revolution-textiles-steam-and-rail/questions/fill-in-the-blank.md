# The industrial revolution textiles steam and rail — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Engels names the revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Friedrich Engels coined "Industrial Revolution" (c. 1845) for the substitution of skilled hands by machines; in England peasants were displaced from enclosed land turned to sheep pasture for the wool mills, and Watt's steam engine, the locomotive, telegraph and steamship remade production and trade
