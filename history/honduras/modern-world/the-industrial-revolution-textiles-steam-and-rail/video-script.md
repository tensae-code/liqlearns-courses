# Video script — The industrial revolution textiles steam and rail

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The industrial revolution textiles steam and rail.

Friedrich Engels coined "Industrial Revolution" (c. 1845) for the substitution of skilled hands by machines; in England peasants were displaced from enclosed land turned to sheep pasture for the wool mills, and Watt's steam engine, the locomotive, telegraph and steamship remade production and trade

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
