# 1917 twice february and october in russia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "land, bread and peace" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Nicholas II's incapable tsarism, famine and the WWI defeat brought Kerensky's Menshevik government first — which changed nothing — then the Bolsheviks under the simple slogans land, bread and peace; their conquests: universal compulsory education and abolition of private ownership of the means of production

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "land, bread and peace" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide teaches Kerensky's failed first government, the Bolshevik slogans and the revolution's educational and property conquests
