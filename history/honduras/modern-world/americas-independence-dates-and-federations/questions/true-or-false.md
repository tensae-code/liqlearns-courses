# Americas independence dates and federations — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "where the movements centred", the great independence movements centred on Mexico, Caracas and Buenos Aires — rich, populous, trading captaincies and viceroyalties — while Central America's independence, unlike South America's junta-led wars, was NOT won by armed struggle, its 1811 risings (Delgado, Argüello) having failed (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "where the movements centred", the guide's key centres the movements on Mexico, Caracas and Buenos Aires and notes Central America's unarmed independence after failed 1811 risings (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
