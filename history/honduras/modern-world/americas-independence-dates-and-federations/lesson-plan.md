# Lesson plan — Americas independence dates and federations

**Course:** History — Honduras · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Americas independence dates and federations
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Americas whole: the peopling debate, pre-Hispanic stages, the three great civilisations and all the others, three colonial empires side by side, the independence wave, the continental Liberal Reform, the Cold War and neoliberal Honduras (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Lección 27: el mapa de las independencias** — a dated table runs 1776 (USA) to 1982 (Belize); Bolívar defends federal union as the best form, most new states choose federation, and Central America's federation even adds a sixth state, Los Altos, in 1838
2. **§2 Lección 27: fechas (guide)** — every country's independence date is placed on the map of the Americas
3. **§3 where the movements centred** — the great independence movements centred on Mexico, Caracas and Buenos Aires — rich, populous, trading captaincies and viceroyalties — while Central America's independence, unlike South America's junta-led wars, was NOT won by armed struggle, its 1811 risings (Delgado, Argüello) having failed
4. **§4 where the movements centred** — the guide's key centres the movements on Mexico, Caracas and Buenos Aires and notes Central America's unarmed independence after failed 1811 risings

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
