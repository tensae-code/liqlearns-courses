# Honduras periodization of history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "how Honduran historians divide the national past" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two world schemes (traditional ages vs materialist modes of production) frame Muñoz Lara's Honduras: Prehispanic (20,000 BCE–1492), Colonial (1502–1821), Contemporary (1821–today), with rival schemes by Mejía, Quiñónez, Becerra, Euraque and Argueta compared

**2.** Tell the story of "how Honduran historians divide the national past" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide teaches Muñoz Lara's three periods (Prehispanic, Colonial, Contemporary) against the traditional and materialist world schemes, with Mejía, Quiñónez, Becerra, Euraque and Argueta as rival national periodisers
