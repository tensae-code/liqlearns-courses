# Latin america state formation and caudillismo — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "building nation-states after independence" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** after emancipation came anarchy, civil wars and caudillismo; liberal and conservative blueprints fought over the new states, the great independence-era units fragmented into smaller countries (Brazil the exception), and consolidation only came as ideological conflicts settled and state authority grew

**2.** Tell the story of "building nation-states after independence" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide's key covers post-independence anarchy, caudillismo, fragmentation (Brazil excepted) and gradual consolidation
