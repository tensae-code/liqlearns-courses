# What history is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Introduction: why and how to study history"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the book opens by asking what history is, why the past matters today, and how a pupil's own family story joins the nation's  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Thematic planning and introductory lessons (Lessons 1–2)"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the teacher opens the year by introducing the textbook and the ancient world itself — what this history is and how the class will travel it  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
