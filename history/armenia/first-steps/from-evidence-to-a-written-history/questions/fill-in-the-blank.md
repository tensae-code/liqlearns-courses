# From evidence to a written history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "How to work with this textbook" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** pupils learn to interrogate the book's source boxes, maps and timelines — evidence first, then the written history built from it

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The guide's method: participatory lessons built on sources" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the teacher is shown how each chapter's Xenophon, Strabo or Khorenatsi extract becomes class evidence from which pupils build their own account
