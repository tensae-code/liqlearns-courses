# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "How to work with this textbook" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** pupils learn to interrogate the book's source boxes, maps and timelines — evidence first, then the written history built from it

**2.** Tell the story of "The guide's method: participatory lessons built on sources" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the teacher is shown how each chapter's Xenophon, Strabo or Khorenatsi extract becomes class evidence from which pupils build their own account
