# Fact or opinion — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§4.3–4.4 Carrying out a study project; studying material with media-literacy skills" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** pupils learn to test a claim against its source before believing or repeating it

**2.** Tell the story of "Preface: how history is formed — why distinguish facts from interpretations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the same news is told differently on different channels, so pupils learn to split the hard fact (a battle's date, a treaty) from the teller's interpretation before the course begins
