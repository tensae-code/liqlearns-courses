# Dating the past — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1.2 The evolution of society–nature relations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** pupils sort all of human time by its tools — Stone Age, Bronze Age, then pre-industrial, industrial and post-industrial eras split by the Industrial Revolution

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lesson plan for §1.2, the evolution of society–nature relations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the teacher walks the class through society's stages by their tools, from pre-industrial to post-industrial
