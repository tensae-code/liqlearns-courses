## 5.1 The struggle for the homeland's independence

Persia's king Hazkert II tries to force Zoroastrianism on Christian Armenia, and Vardan Mamikonian's army falls at Avarayr on 26 May 451 defending the faith — told through Ghazar Parpetsi.
