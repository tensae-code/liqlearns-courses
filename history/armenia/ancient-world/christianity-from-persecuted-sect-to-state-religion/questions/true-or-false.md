# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§30 Christianity in the empire (part)", a persecuted faith spreads through Roman cities until emperors first tolerate, then adopt it (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 3 §6: the spread of Christianity in Rome; society's change of religion", a persecuted sect from Judea wins the empire's heart until emperors first tolerate, then adopt, the new faith (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
