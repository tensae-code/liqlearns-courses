# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§30 Christianity in the empire (part)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a persecuted faith spreads through Roman cities until emperors first tolerate, then adopt it

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 3 §6: the spread of Christianity in Rome; society's change of religion" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a persecuted sect from Judea wins the empire's heart until emperors first tolerate, then adopt, the new faith
