# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§30 Christianity in the empire (part)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a persecuted faith spreads through Roman cities until emperors first tolerate, then adopt it

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 3 §6: the spread of Christianity in Rome; society's change of religion" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a persecuted sect from Judea wins the empire's heart until emperors first tolerate, then adopt, the new faith
