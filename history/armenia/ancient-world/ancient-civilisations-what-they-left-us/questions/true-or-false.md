# Ancient civilisations what they left us — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Theme 1 §3: the influence of the ancient civilisations on the modern world", writing, law, calendars and building pass from the first civilisations into today's world, and pupils trace what still survives (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Thematic plan, Theme 1: History and I", the year's opening theme makes pupils place themselves inside history and trace what the first civilisations still give the modern world (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
