# Ancient civilisations what they left us — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 1 §3: the influence of the ancient civilisations on the modern world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** writing, law, calendars and building pass from the first civilisations into today's world, and pupils trace what still survives

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Thematic plan, Theme 1: History and I" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the year's opening theme makes pupils place themselves inside history and trace what the first civilisations still give the modern world
