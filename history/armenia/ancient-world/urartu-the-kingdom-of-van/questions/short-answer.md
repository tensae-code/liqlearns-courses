# Urartu the kingdom of van — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "1.4 The founding of the Van (Urartu) kingdom and its flourishing" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sarduri I raises Tushpa-Van, Ishpuini and Menua organise the kingdom, and Argishti I plants Erebuni in 782 BCE

**2.** Tell the story of "1.5 The fall of the Van kingdom and independence" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sarduri II clashes with Tiglath-Pileser III, Rusa I fights Assyria and Cimmerians, and by the early 6th century BCE the kingdom is gone — but its lands pass to Armenian rulers
