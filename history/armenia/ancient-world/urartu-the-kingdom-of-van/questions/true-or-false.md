# Urartu the kingdom of van — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "1.4 The founding of the Van (Urartu) kingdom and its flourishing", Sarduri I raises Tushpa-Van, Ishpuini and Menua organise the kingdom, and Argishti I plants Erebuni in 782 BCE (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "1.5 The fall of the Van kingdom and independence", Sarduri II clashes with Tiglath-Pileser III, Rusa I fights Assyria and Cimmerians, and by the early 6th century BCE the kingdom is gone — but its lands pass to Armenian rulers (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
