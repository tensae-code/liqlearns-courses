# Urartu the kingdom of van — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** 1.4 The founding of the Van (Urartu) kingdom and its flourishing — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** 1.5 The fall of the Van kingdom and independence — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Sarduri I raises Tushpa-Van, Ishpuini and Menua organise the kingdom, and Argishti I plants Erebuni in 782 BCE
b) Sarduri II clashes with Tiglath-Pileser III, Rusa I fights Assyria and Cimmerians, and by the early 6th century BCE the kingdom is gone — but its lands pass to Armenian rulers

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
