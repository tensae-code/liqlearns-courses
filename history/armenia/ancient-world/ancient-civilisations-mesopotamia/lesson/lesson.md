## Sub lesson 1 — §5 The rise of the Mesopotamian civilisation

Sumerian cities grow between the Tigris and Euphrates, Sargon of Akkad welds them into the first empire, and king Ur-Namma issues early laws.

## Sub lesson 2 — §6 The Old Babylonian kingdom

Babylon rises on the Euphrates and Hammurabi (1792–1750 BCE) carves his law code on stone so rich and poor alike can read the king's justice.

## Sub lesson 3 — §7 Assyria and the Neo-Babylonian kingdom

Assyria's iron army under Tiglath-Pileser III and Sargon II swallows the Near East, then Nebuchadnezzar II rebuilds Babylon in splendour before both empires fall.

## Sub lesson 4 — Theme 2 §2: ancient Mesopotamia and its civilisation achievements

between the Tigris and Euphrates the Sumerians invent the city, the wheel and cuneiform, and Babylon later gives law a written code.
