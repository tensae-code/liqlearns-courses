# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§5 The rise of the Mesopotamian civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sumerian cities grow between the Tigris and Euphrates, Sargon of Akkad welds them into the first empire, and king Ur-Namma issues early laws

**2.** Tell the story of "§6 The Old Babylonian kingdom" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Babylon rises on the Euphrates and Hammurabi (1792–1750 BCE) carves his law code on stone so rich and poor alike can read the king's justice
