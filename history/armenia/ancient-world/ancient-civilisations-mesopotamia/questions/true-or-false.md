# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§5 The rise of the Mesopotamian civilisation", Sumerian cities grow between the Tigris and Euphrates, Sargon of Akkad welds them into the first empire, and king Ur-Namma issues early laws (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§6 The Old Babylonian kingdom", Babylon rises on the Euphrates and Hammurabi (1792–1750 BCE) carves his law code on stone so rich and poor alike can read the king's justice (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
