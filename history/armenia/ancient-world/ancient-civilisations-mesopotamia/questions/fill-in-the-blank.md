# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5 The rise of the Mesopotamian civilisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sumerian cities grow between the Tigris and Euphrates, Sargon of Akkad welds them into the first empire, and king Ur-Namma issues early laws

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§6 The Old Babylonian kingdom" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Babylon rises on the Euphrates and Hammurabi (1792–1750 BCE) carves his law code on stone so rich and poor alike can read the king's justice
