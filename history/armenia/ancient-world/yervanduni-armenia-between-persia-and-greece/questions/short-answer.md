# Yervanduni armenia between persia and greece — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "2.1 The formation of Yervanduni Armenia and Achaemenid Persia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** after Van falls, Yervanduni kings rule a satrapy of Persia, Armenia joins the great revolt of 522–521 BCE recorded at Behistun, and Tigran Yervanduni hosts Xenophon's Greeks

**2.** Tell the story of "2.2 Yervanduni Armenia, Alexander's empire and the Seleucids" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alexander's conquest reshuffles the East, and Yervanduni kings keep Armenia together between Macedonian successors
