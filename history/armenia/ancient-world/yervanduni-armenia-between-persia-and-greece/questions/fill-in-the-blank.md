# Yervanduni armenia between persia and greece — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "2.1 The formation of Yervanduni Armenia and Achaemenid Persia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** after Van falls, Yervanduni kings rule a satrapy of Persia, Armenia joins the great revolt of 522–521 BCE recorded at Behistun, and Tigran Yervanduni hosts Xenophon's Greeks

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "2.2 Yervanduni Armenia, Alexander's empire and the Seleucids" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alexander's conquest reshuffles the East, and Yervanduni kings keep Armenia together between Macedonian successors
