# Yervanduni armenia between persia and greece — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "2.1 The formation of Yervanduni Armenia and Achaemenid Persia", after Van falls, Yervanduni kings rule a satrapy of Persia, Armenia joins the great revolt of 522–521 BCE recorded at Behistun, and Tigran Yervanduni hosts Xenophon's Greeks (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "2.2 Yervanduni Armenia, Alexander's empire and the Seleucids", Alexander's conquest reshuffles the East, and Yervanduni kings keep Armenia together between Macedonian successors (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
