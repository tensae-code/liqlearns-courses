# Video script — Yervanduni armenia between persia and greece

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Yervanduni armenia between persia and greece.

after Van falls, Yervanduni kings rule a satrapy of Persia, Armenia joins the great revolt of 522–521 BCE recorded at Behistun, and Tigran Yervanduni hosts Xenophon's Greeks.

Alexander's conquest reshuffles the East, and Yervanduni kings keep Armenia together between Macedonian successors.

trade routes cross the Highland, cities grow, and Greek and Iranian cultures meet in Armenian temples and coinage.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
