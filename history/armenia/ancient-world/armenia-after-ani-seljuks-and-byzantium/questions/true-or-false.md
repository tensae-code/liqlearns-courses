# Armenia after ani seljuks and byzantium — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§1.1 The region after the fall of Ani's Bagratuni kingdom", Byzantium annexes Armenia, disbands its armies and exiles its catholicos - and the Seljuks walk through the opened gate
