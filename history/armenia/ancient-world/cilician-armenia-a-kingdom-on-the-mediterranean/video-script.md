# Video script — Cilician armenia a kingdom on the mediterranean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Cilician armenia a kingdom on the mediterranean.

refugee princes carve a state from Byzantium's edge, and in 1198 Levon is crowned king with a crown sent by pope and emperor.

ports like Ayas grow rich on the Europe–Asia trade while crusader alliances slowly fail to save the kingdom.

the guide stages Cilicia as Armenia's civilisational answer — a trading kingdom and a culture that flowered without one.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
