# Cilician armenia a kingdom on the mediterranean — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§2.1–2.2 Armenian statehood arises in Cilicia and the kingdom is proclaimed" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** refugee princes carve a state from Byzantium's edge, and in 1198 Levon is crowned king with a crown sent by pope and emperor

**2.** Tell the story of "§§2.3–2.4 Cilician Armenia in the 1200s–1300s: state order and socio-economic life" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** ports like Ayas grow rich on the Europe–Asia trade while crusader alliances slowly fail to save the kingdom
