# Cilician armenia a kingdom on the mediterranean — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§2.1–2.2 Armenian statehood arises in Cilicia and the kingdom is proclaimed" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** refugee princes carve a state from Byzantium's edge, and in 1198 Levon is crowned king with a crown sent by pope and emperor

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§2.3–2.4 Cilician Armenia in the 1200s–1300s: state order and socio-economic life" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** ports like Ayas grow rich on the Europe–Asia trade while crusader alliances slowly fail to save the kingdom
