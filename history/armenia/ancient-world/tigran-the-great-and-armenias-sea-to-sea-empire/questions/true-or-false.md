# Tigran the great and armenias sea to sea empire — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "3.2 The Armenian world power: Tigran II the Great", Tigran (95–55 BCE) builds an empire from sea to sea, founds Tigranakert, and makes Armenia Rome's great eastern rival (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "3.3 The Armenian–Roman wars and the decline of Tigran's empire", Lucullus and Pompey invade, Tigran keeps his crown but loses the empire, and Armenia becomes the prize Rome and Parthia fight over (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
