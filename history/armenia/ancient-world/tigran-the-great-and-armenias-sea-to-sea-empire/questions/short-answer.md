# Tigran the great and armenias sea to sea empire — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "3.2 The Armenian world power: Tigran II the Great" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tigran (95–55 BCE) builds an empire from sea to sea, founds Tigranakert, and makes Armenia Rome's great eastern rival

**2.** Tell the story of "3.3 The Armenian–Roman wars and the decline of Tigran's empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lucullus and Pompey invade, Tigran keeps his crown but loses the empire, and Armenia becomes the prize Rome and Parthia fight over
