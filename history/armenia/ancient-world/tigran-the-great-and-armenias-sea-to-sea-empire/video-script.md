# Video script — Tigran the great and armenias sea to sea empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Tigran the great and armenias sea to sea empire.

Tigran (95–55 BCE) builds an empire from sea to sea, founds Tigranakert, and makes Armenia Rome's great eastern rival.

Lucullus and Pompey invade, Tigran keeps his crown but loses the empire, and Armenia becomes the prize Rome and Parthia fight over.

teachers stage the debate over Tigran's sea-to-sea empire — how it rose, and what its fall cost.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
