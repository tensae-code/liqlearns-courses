# Artashes i and the artashesian kingdom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "3.1 The formation of Artashesian Armenia: Artashes I" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Artashes (189–160 BCE) reunites Armenian lands, founds Artashat on Hannibal's advice in the story Plutarch tells, and reforms land and army

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "3.4 Artavasdes II and the last Artashesians" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Artavasdes II plays Rome against Parthia, is seized by Mark Antony, and the dynasty fades as foreign kings are imposed
