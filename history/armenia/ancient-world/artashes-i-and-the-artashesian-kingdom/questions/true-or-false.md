# Artashes i and the artashesian kingdom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "3.1 The formation of Artashesian Armenia: Artashes I", Artashes (189–160 BCE) reunites Armenian lands, founds Artashat on Hannibal's advice in the story Plutarch tells, and reforms land and army (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "3.4 Artavasdes II and the last Artashesians", Artavasdes II plays Rome against Parthia, is seized by Mark Antony, and the dynasty fades as foreign kings are imposed (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
