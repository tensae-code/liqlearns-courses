# Artashes i and the artashesian kingdom — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "3.1 The formation of Artashesian Armenia: Artashes I" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Artashes (189–160 BCE) reunites Armenian lands, founds Artashat on Hannibal's advice in the story Plutarch tells, and reforms land and army

**2.** Tell the story of "3.4 Artavasdes II and the last Artashesians" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Artavasdes II plays Rome against Parthia, is seized by Mark Antony, and the dynasty fades as foreign kings are imposed
