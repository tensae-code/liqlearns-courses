# Video script — Artashes i and the artashesian kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Artashes i and the artashesian kingdom.

Artashes (189–160 BCE) reunites Armenian lands, founds Artashat on Hannibal's advice in the story Plutarch tells, and reforms land and army.

Artavasdes II plays Rome against Parthia, is seized by Mark Antony, and the dynasty fades as foreign kings are imposed.

Hellenistic cities, theatres and trade flourish, and Artavasdes II himself writes Greek tragedies.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
