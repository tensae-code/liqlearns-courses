# The golden age of armenian culture mashtots and the alphabet — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "6.1 The Golden Age of Armenian culture (5th–9th centuries)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mesrop Mashtots creates the Armenian alphabet in 405, the Bible is translated, and Khorenatsi and other fathers write the nation's first histories

**2.** Tell the story of "6.2 Natural sciences and art in the 5th–9th centuries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Anania Shirakatsi maps the cosmos, physicians and architects raise a learning culture that outlives the kingdom
