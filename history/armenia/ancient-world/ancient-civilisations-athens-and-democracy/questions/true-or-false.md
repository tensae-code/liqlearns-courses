# Ancient civilisations athens and democracy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§16 The Athenian state", Draco's harsh laws give way to Solon's debt-canceling reforms of 594 BCE, Pisistratus rules as tyrant, and Cleisthenes' ten tribes and ostracism open the road to democracy (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§17 Pericles' Athens (closing part)", Pericles (444–429 BCE) makes Athens a paid, participating democracy at its height — then rivalry with Sparta ignites the Peloponnesian War in 431 BCE (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
