# Ancient civilisations athens and democracy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§16 The Athenian state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Draco's harsh laws give way to Solon's debt-canceling reforms of 594 BCE, Pisistratus rules as tyrant, and Cleisthenes' ten tribes and ostracism open the road to democracy

**2.** Tell the story of "§17 Pericles' Athens (closing part)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pericles (444–429 BCE) makes Athens a paid, participating democracy at its height — then rivalry with Sparta ignites the Peloponnesian War in 431 BCE
