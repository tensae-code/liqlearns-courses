## Sub lesson 1 — §16 The Athenian state

Draco's harsh laws give way to Solon's debt-canceling reforms of 594 BCE, Pisistratus rules as tyrant, and Cleisthenes' ten tribes and ostracism open the road to democracy.

## Sub lesson 2 — §17 Pericles' Athens (closing part)

Pericles (444–429 BCE) makes Athens a paid, participating democracy at its height — then rivalry with Sparta ignites the Peloponnesian War in 431 BCE.

## Sub lesson 3 — Theme 3 §2: the development of the Greek polis

the polis grows from village to citizen state, and in Athens ordinary men win the vote and the jury courts.
