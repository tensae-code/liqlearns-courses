# Video script — Ancient civilisations athens and democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Ancient civilisations athens and democracy.

Draco's harsh laws give way to Solon's debt-canceling reforms of 594 BCE, Pisistratus rules as tyrant, and Cleisthenes' ten tribes and ostracism open the road to democracy.

Pericles (444–429 BCE) makes Athens a paid, participating democracy at its height — then rivalry with Sparta ignites the Peloponnesian War in 431 BCE.

the polis grows from village to citizen state, and in Athens ordinary men win the vote and the jury courts.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
