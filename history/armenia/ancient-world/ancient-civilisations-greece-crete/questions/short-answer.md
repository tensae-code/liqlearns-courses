# Ancient civilisations greece crete — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§13 The beginning of the Greek civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cretan palaces at Knossos and the Phaistos disc open the story, Mycenae's Lion Gate follows, then Dorian newcomers plunge Greece into a dark age Homer sings about

**2.** Tell the story of "§14 The Greek world in the archaic period" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the polis is born, Greek colonists ring the Mediterranean and Black Sea coasts in the Great Colonisation, tyrants seize cities, and the Olympic Games begin in 776 BCE
