# Ancient civilisations greece crete — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§13 The beginning of the Greek civilisation", Cretan palaces at Knossos and the Phaistos disc open the story, Mycenae's Lion Gate follows, then Dorian newcomers plunge Greece into a dark age Homer sings about (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§14 The Greek world in the archaic period", the polis is born, Greek colonists ring the Mediterranean and Black Sea coasts in the Great Colonisation, tyrants seize cities, and the Olympic Games begin in 776 BCE (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
