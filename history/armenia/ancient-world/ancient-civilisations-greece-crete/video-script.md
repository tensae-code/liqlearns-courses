# Video script — Ancient civilisations greece crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Ancient civilisations greece crete.

Cretan palaces at Knossos and the Phaistos disc open the story, Mycenae's Lion Gate follows, then Dorian newcomers plunge Greece into a dark age Homer sings about.

the polis is born, Greek colonists ring the Mediterranean and Black Sea coasts in the Great Colonisation, tyrants seize cities, and the Olympic Games begin in 776 BCE.

Aegean islanders and Mycenaean kings give way to the Greek city-states scattered round the sea like frogs round a pond.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
