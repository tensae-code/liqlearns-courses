# Alexander the great and his empire — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§20 The empire of Alexander the Great" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Philip II unites Greece at Chaeronea, his son Alexander smashes Persia at Issus and Gaugamela, founds Alexandria, marches to India and dies at Babylon in 323 BCE
