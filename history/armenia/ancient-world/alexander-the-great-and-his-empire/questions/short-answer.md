# Alexander the great and his empire — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§20 The empire of Alexander the Great" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Philip II unites Greece at Chaeronea, his son Alexander smashes Persia at Issus and Gaugamela, founds Alexandria, marches to India and dies at Babylon in 323 BCE
