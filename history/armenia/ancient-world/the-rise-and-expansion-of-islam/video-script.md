# Video script — The rise and expansion of islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The rise and expansion of islam.

Muhammad unites Arabia under Islam, Abu Bakr and Omar launch conquests that reach Spain and Persia, and Umayyad then Abbasid caliphs rule from Damascus and Baghdad (founded 762) until the Mongols take Baghdad in 1258.

from Mecca's caravan city a new faith unites Arabia and within a century rules from Spain to Central Asia, building its own civilisation.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
