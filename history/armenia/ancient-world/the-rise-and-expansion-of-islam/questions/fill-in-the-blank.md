# The rise and expansion of islam — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§36 The Arab Caliphate" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Muhammad unites Arabia under Islam, Abu Bakr and Omar launch conquests that reach Spain and Persia, and Umayyad then Abbasid caliphs rule from Damascus and Baghdad (founded 762) until the Mongols take Baghdad in 1258

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 5 §1: Arab-Muslim civilisation takes shape" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** from Mecca's caravan city a new faith unites Arabia and within a century rules from Spain to Central Asia, building its own civilisation
