# The rise and expansion of islam — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§36 The Arab Caliphate", Muhammad unites Arabia under Islam, Abu Bakr and Omar launch conquests that reach Spain and Persia, and Umayyad then Abbasid caliphs rule from Damascus and Baghdad (founded 762) until the Mongols take Baghdad in 1258 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 5 §1: Arab-Muslim civilisation takes shape", from Mecca's caravan city a new faith unites Arabia and within a century rules from Spain to Central Asia, building its own civilisation (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
