# The rise and expansion of islam

## Sub lesson 1 — §36 The Arab Caliphate

Muhammad unites Arabia under Islam, Abu Bakr and Omar launch conquests that reach Spain and Persia, and Umayyad then Abbasid caliphs rule from Damascus and Baghdad (founded 762) until the Mongols take Baghdad in 1258.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 5 §1: Arab-Muslim civilisation takes shape

from Mecca's caravan city a new faith unites Arabia and within a century rules from Spain to Central Asia, building its own civilisation.
