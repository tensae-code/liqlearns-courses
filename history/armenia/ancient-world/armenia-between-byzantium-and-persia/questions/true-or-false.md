Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "5.2 Armenian–Byzantine and Persian–Byzantine rivalry in the 6th century", two empires carve Armenia between wars and treaties, and Armenians serve, resist and survive inside both (Sub lesson 1)
