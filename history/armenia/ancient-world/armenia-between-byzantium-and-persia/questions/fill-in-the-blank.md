# Armenia between byzantium and persia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "5.2 Armenian–Byzantine and Persian–Byzantine rivalry in the 6th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two empires carve Armenia between wars and treaties, and Armenians serve, resist and survive inside both

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "5.3 The Arab Caliphate's domination in Armenia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Arab armies take the country in the 7th century, and Armenia lives under caliphs through tribute, revolts and uneasy treaties
