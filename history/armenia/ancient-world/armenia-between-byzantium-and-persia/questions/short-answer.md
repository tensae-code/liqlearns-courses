# Armenia between byzantium and persia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "5.2 Armenian–Byzantine and Persian–Byzantine rivalry in the 6th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two empires carve Armenia between wars and treaties, and Armenians serve, resist and survive inside both

**2.** Tell the story of "5.3 The Arab Caliphate's domination in Armenia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Arab armies take the country in the 7th century, and Armenia lives under caliphs through tribute, revolts and uneasy treaties
