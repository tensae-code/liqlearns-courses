# Video script — Bagratuni armenia the kingdom restored

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Bagratuni armenia the kingdom restored.

Ashot Bagratuni wins recognition as king in 885, and a crowned Armenian kingdom returns after four and a half centuries.

Ani, the city of a thousand and one churches, crowns the kingdom's golden century before Byzantium annexes it in 1045.

trade, crafts and church building enrich the kingdom, and its culture seeds the Armenian revival.

the guide closes the year by following Armenia's survival between empires to its restored crown at Ani.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
