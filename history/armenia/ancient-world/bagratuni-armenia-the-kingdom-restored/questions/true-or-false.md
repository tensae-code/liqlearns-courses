# Bagratuni armenia the kingdom restored — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "7.1 The establishment of the Bagratunis in Armenia", Ashot Bagratuni wins recognition as king in 885, and a crowned Armenian kingdom returns after four and a half centuries (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "7.2 Bagratuni Armenia's flourishing and fall", Ani, the city of a thousand and one churches, crowns the kingdom's golden century before Byzantium annexes it in 1045 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
