# Bagratuni armenia the kingdom restored — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "7.1 The establishment of the Bagratunis in Armenia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ashot Bagratuni wins recognition as king in 885, and a crowned Armenian kingdom returns after four and a half centuries

**2.** Tell the story of "7.2 Bagratuni Armenia's flourishing and fall" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ani, the city of a thousand and one churches, crowns the kingdom's golden century before Byzantium annexes it in 1045
