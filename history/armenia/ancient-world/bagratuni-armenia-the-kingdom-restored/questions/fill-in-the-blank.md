# Bagratuni armenia the kingdom restored — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "7.1 The establishment of the Bagratunis in Armenia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ashot Bagratuni wins recognition as king in 885, and a crowned Armenian kingdom returns after four and a half centuries

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "7.2 Bagratuni Armenia's flourishing and fall" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ani, the city of a thousand and one churches, crowns the kingdom's golden century before Byzantium annexes it in 1045
