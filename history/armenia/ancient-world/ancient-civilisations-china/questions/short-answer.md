# Ancient civilisations china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§11 Ancient China (second part of a combined lesson)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** farmers on the Huang He and Yangtze build a bronze-age civilisation of warring states until Qin Shi Huang unites China in 221 BCE

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 2 §6: China — civilisation achievements and their foundations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** along the Yellow River China unites under emperors, and Confucius's teaching, silk and paper shape East Asia for two millennia
