# Ancient civilisations china — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§11 Ancient China (second part of a combined lesson)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** farmers on the Huang He and Yangtze build a bronze-age civilisation of warring states until Qin Shi Huang unites China in 221 BCE

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 2 §6: China — civilisation achievements and their foundations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** along the Yellow River China unites under emperors, and Confucius's teaching, silk and paper shape East Asia for two millennia
