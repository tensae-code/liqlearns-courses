# The maya

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The maya" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — §47 America's "undiscovered" civilisations: the Maya

Maya cities of Central America build pyramids, a calendar and hieroglyphic writing long before Europeans know the continent exists.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 5 §5: the achievements of America's oldest societies

cut off from the Old World, the Maya, Aztecs and Inca raise pyramids, calendars and empires of their own.
