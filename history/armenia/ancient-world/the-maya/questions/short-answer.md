# The maya — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§47 America's "undiscovered" civilisations: the Maya" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Maya cities of Central America build pyramids, a calendar and hieroglyphic writing long before Europeans know the continent exists

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 5 §5: the achievements of America's oldest societies" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** cut off from the Old World, the Maya, Aztecs and Inca raise pyramids, calendars and empires of their own
