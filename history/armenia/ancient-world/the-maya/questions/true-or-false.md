# The maya — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§47 America's "undiscovered" civilisations: the Maya", Maya cities of Central America build pyramids, a calendar and hieroglyphic writing long before Europeans know the continent exists (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 5 §5: the achievements of America's oldest societies", cut off from the Old World, the Maya, Aztecs and Inca raise pyramids, calendars and empires of their own (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
