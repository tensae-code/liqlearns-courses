# Ancient civilisations india — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§11 Ancient India (first part of a combined lesson)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Indus cities Harappa and Mohenjo-daro flourish, Aryan newcomers sort society into varnas, and Ashoka's Mauryan empire spreads a message of peace

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 2 §5: India — civilisation achievements and their foundations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Indus cities, then Vedic society, caste, and the teachings of Buddha grow from the Ganges plain into a lasting civilisation
