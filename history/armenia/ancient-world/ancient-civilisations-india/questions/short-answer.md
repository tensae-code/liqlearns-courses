# Ancient civilisations india — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§11 Ancient India (first part of a combined lesson)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Indus cities Harappa and Mohenjo-daro flourish, Aryan newcomers sort society into varnas, and Ashoka's Mauryan empire spreads a message of peace

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 2 §5: India — civilisation achievements and their foundations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Indus cities, then Vedic society, caste, and the teachings of Buddha grow from the Ganges plain into a lasting civilisation
