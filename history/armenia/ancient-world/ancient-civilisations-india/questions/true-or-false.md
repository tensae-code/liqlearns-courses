# Ancient civilisations india — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§11 Ancient India (first part of a combined lesson)", Indus cities Harappa and Mohenjo-daro flourish, Aryan newcomers sort society into varnas, and Ashoka's Mauryan empire spreads a message of peace (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 2 §5: India — civilisation achievements and their foundations", the Indus cities, then Vedic society, caste, and the teachings of Buddha grow from the Ganges plain into a lasting civilisation (Sub lesson 2)
