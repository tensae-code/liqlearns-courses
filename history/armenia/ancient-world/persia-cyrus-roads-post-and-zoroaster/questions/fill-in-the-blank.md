# Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10 The Achaemenid Persian Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cyrus II frees peoples and builds an empire, Cambyses takes Egypt, and Darius I organises satrapies, roads and taxes before Alexander brings the empire down in 330 BCE

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 2 §3: ancient Iran and its civilisation achievements" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cyrus builds an empire that rules many peoples with tolerance, roads and post, under the teaching of Zoroaster
