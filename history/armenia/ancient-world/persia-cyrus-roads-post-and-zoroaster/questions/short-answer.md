# Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§10 The Achaemenid Persian Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cyrus II frees peoples and builds an empire, Cambyses takes Egypt, and Darius I organises satrapies, roads and taxes before Alexander brings the empire down in 330 BCE

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 2 §3: ancient Iran and its civilisation achievements" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cyrus builds an empire that rules many peoples with tolerance, roads and post, under the teaching of Zoroaster
