# Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§10 The Achaemenid Persian Empire", Cyrus II frees peoples and builds an empire, Cambyses takes Egypt, and Darius I organises satrapies, roads and taxes before Alexander brings the empire down in 330 BCE (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 2 §3: ancient Iran and its civilisation achievements", Cyrus builds an empire that rules many peoples with tolerance, roads and post, under the teaching of Zoroaster (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
