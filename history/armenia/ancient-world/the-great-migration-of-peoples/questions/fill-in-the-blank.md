# The great migration of peoples — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§33 "The Great Migration of peoples": new states arise in Europe" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** the Huns' arrival in 375 sets peoples moving, Alaric's Goths sack Rome in 410, Vandals follow in 455, and Germanic kingdoms — Franks, Ostrogoths, Anglo-Saxons, Lombards — replace the West by 568
