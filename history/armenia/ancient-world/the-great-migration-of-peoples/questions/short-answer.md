# The great migration of peoples — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§33 "The Great Migration of peoples": new states arise in Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** the Huns' arrival in 375 sets peoples moving, Alaric's Goths sack Rome in 410, Vandals follow in 455, and Germanic kingdoms — Franks, Ostrogoths, Anglo-Saxons, Lombards — replace the West by 568
