# Arshakuni armenia kings between rome and persia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "4.1 The establishment of the Arshakunis in Armenia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rome and Parthia settle Armenia's crown on Tiridates I in the treaty of Rhandeia (62–63), founding a dynasty that lasts four centuries

**2.** Tell the story of "4.2 The Arshakuni kingdom at its height (same chapter, political part)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** strong Arshakuni kings hold the buffer kingdom together while Rome and Persia watch each other across it
