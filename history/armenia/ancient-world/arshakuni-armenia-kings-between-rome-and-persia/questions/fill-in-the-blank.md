# Arshakuni armenia kings between rome and persia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "4.1 The establishment of the Arshakunis in Armenia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rome and Parthia settle Armenia's crown on Tiridates I in the treaty of Rhandeia (62–63), founding a dynasty that lasts four centuries

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "4.2 The Arshakuni kingdom at its height (same chapter, political part)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** strong Arshakuni kings hold the buffer kingdom together while Rome and Persia watch each other across it
