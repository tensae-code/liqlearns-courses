# Arshakuni armenia kings between rome and persia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "4.1 The establishment of the Arshakunis in Armenia", Rome and Parthia settle Armenia's crown on Tiridates I in the treaty of Rhandeia (62–63), founding a dynasty that lasts four centuries (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "4.2 The Arshakuni kingdom at its height (same chapter, political part)", strong Arshakuni kings hold the buffer kingdom together while Rome and Persia watch each other across it (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
