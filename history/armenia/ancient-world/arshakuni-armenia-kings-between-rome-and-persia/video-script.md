# Video script — Arshakuni armenia kings between rome and persia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Arshakuni armenia kings between rome and persia.

Rome and Parthia settle Armenia's crown on Tiridates I in the treaty of Rhandeia (62–63), founding a dynasty that lasts four centuries.

strong Arshakuni kings hold the buffer kingdom together while Rome and Persia watch each other across it.

Rome and Persia partition Armenia in 387, the last Arshakuni king is deposed in 428, and the kingdom ends — but not the nation.

nakharar houses, cities and church schools structure a feudal Christian kingdom.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
