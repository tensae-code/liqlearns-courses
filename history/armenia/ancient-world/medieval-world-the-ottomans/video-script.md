# Video script — Medieval world the ottomans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Medieval world the ottomans.

Osman founds a small beylik in 1299, Murad I builds the Janissaries, Mehmed II takes Constantinople on 29 May 1453, and Suleiman carries Ottoman power across three continents.

a frontier beylik becomes the empire of Suleiman the Magnificent, ruling three continents from Constantinople.

the Islamic civilisation, the Ottoman centuries, Mughal India, the Discoveries and the Reformation close the medieval world.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
