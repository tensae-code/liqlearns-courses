## Sub lesson 1 — §42 The Ottoman Empire

Osman founds a small beylik in 1299, Murad I builds the Janissaries, Mehmed II takes Constantinople on 29 May 1453, and Suleiman carries Ottoman power across three continents.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 5 §3: the Ottoman state and society in the 14th–16th centuries

a frontier beylik becomes the empire of Suleiman the Magnificent, ruling three continents from Constantinople.

## Sub lesson 3 — Thematic plan, Theme 5: the medieval and early-modern world

the Islamic civilisation, the Ottoman centuries, Mughal India, the Discoveries and the Reformation close the medieval world.
