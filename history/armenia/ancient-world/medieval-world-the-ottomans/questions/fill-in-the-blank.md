# Medieval world the ottomans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§42 The Ottoman Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Osman founds a small beylik in 1299, Murad I builds the Janissaries, Mehmed II takes Constantinople on 29 May 1453, and Suleiman carries Ottoman power across three continents

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 5 §3: the Ottoman state and society in the 14th–16th centuries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a frontier beylik becomes the empire of Suleiman the Magnificent, ruling three continents from Constantinople
