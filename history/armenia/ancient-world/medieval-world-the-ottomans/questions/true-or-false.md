# Medieval world the ottomans — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§42 The Ottoman Empire", Osman founds a small beylik in 1299, Murad I builds the Janissaries, Mehmed II takes Constantinople on 29 May 1453, and Suleiman carries Ottoman power across three continents (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 5 §3: the Ottoman state and society in the 14th–16th centuries", a frontier beylik becomes the empire of Suleiman the Magnificent, ruling three continents from Constantinople (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
