# Medieval world the ottomans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§42 The Ottoman Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Osman founds a small beylik in 1299, Murad I builds the Janissaries, Mehmed II takes Constantinople on 29 May 1453, and Suleiman carries Ottoman power across three continents

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 5 §3: the Ottoman state and society in the 14th–16th centuries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a frontier beylik becomes the empire of Suleiman the Magnificent, ruling three continents from Constantinople
