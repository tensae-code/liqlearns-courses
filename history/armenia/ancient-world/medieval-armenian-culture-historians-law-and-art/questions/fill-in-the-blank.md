# Medieval armenian culture historians law and art — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§3.1–3.2 Armenian culture of the 1000s–1300s: historiography, philosophy, law and natural science" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** even without a highland kingdom, scholars write histories, Mkhitar Gosh writes a law code, and monasteries run schools

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3.3 Armenian literature and art, 1000s–1300s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** poets like Frik voice the people's suffering while khachkars and miniature painting reach their peak
