# Ancient civilisations sparta and athens against sparta — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§15 The Spartan state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sparta masters the helots, Lycurgus' laws freeze society in place, two kings and the elders rule, and boys are raised in the agoge to fill the phalanx

**2.** Tell the story of "§17 The Greco-Persian wars (500–449 BCE)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Darius' invasion breaks at Marathon in 490 BCE, Xerxes' host is stopped at Thermopylae and beaten at Salamis in 480 BCE, and Athens leads the Greek victory
