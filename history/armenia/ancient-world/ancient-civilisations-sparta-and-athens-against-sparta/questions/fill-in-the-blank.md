# Ancient civilisations sparta and athens against sparta — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§15 The Spartan state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sparta masters the helots, Lycurgus' laws freeze society in place, two kings and the elders rule, and boys are raised in the agoge to fill the phalanx

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§17 The Greco-Persian wars (500–449 BCE)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Darius' invasion breaks at Marathon in 490 BCE, Xerxes' host is stopped at Thermopylae and beaten at Salamis in 480 BCE, and Athens leads the Greek victory
