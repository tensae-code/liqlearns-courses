# Video script — Medieval world the dark age and feudalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Medieval world the dark age and feudalism.

Clovis founds the Frankish kingdom and takes Christianity, Charles Martel and Pepin build Carolingian power, and Charlemagne's empire revives a Roman crown in the West.

kings grant land down a ladder of vassals to knights, peasants work the lord's fields with the three-field system, and castles and a money-less natural economy hold the order together.

the Black Death kills a third of Europe, peasants revolt from France to England, towns and guilds shift the balance, and kings begin gathering feudal Europe into centralised states.

feudal ranks and the slow swelling of town populations show how the medieval world was organised.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
