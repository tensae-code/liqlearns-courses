# Lesson plan — Medieval world the dark age and feudalism

**Course:** History — Armenia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Medieval world the dark age and feudalism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The first books: how to study history, the ancient world whole, and Armenia from the Highland's first peoples to the Bagratuni kingdom (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Medieval world the dark age and feudalism as Armenia's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The first books: how to study history, the ancient world whole, and Armenia from the Highland's first peoples to the Bagratuni kingdom — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§34 The Carolingian empire** — Clovis founds the Frankish kingdom and takes Christianity, Charles Martel and Pepin build Carolingian power, and Charlemagne's empire revives a Roman crown in the West
2. **§38 European feudal society** — kings grant land down a ladder of vassals to knights, peasants work the lord's fields with the three-field system, and castles and a money-less natural economy hold the order together
3. **§49 Changes in the feudal society of Europe** — the Black Death kills a third of Europe, peasants revolt from France to England, towns and guilds shift the balance, and kings begin gathering feudal Europe into centralised states
4. **Lesson plan for §2.1, feudal society and the historical growth of cities** — feudal ranks and the slow swelling of town populations show how the medieval world was organised
