# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§34 The Carolingian empire", Clovis founds the Frankish kingdom and takes Christianity, Charles Martel and Pepin build Carolingian power, and Charlemagne's empire revives a Roman crown in the West (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§38 European feudal society", kings grant land down a ladder of vassals to knights, peasants work the lord's fields with the three-field system, and castles and a money-less natural economy hold the order together (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
