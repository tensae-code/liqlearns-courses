# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§34 The Carolingian empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Clovis founds the Frankish kingdom and takes Christianity, Charles Martel and Pepin build Carolingian power, and Charlemagne's empire revives a Roman crown in the West

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§38 European feudal society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** kings grant land down a ladder of vassals to knights, peasants work the lord's fields with the three-field system, and castles and a money-less natural economy hold the order together
