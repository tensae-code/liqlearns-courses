# Ancient civilisations rome — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§23 The beginning of the Roman civilisation", Etruscans, Latins and Samnites share Italy, Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, kings rule until the last is expelled and the republic is proclaimed in 510 BCE (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§24 The Roman republic's social and political order", patricians and plebeians fight a two-century duel, plebeians win tribunes and the Twelve Tables, and the Senate, consuls and citizen legions run the republic (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
