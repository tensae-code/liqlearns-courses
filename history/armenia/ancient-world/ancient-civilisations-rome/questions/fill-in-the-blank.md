# Ancient civilisations rome — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23 The beginning of the Roman civilisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Etruscans, Latins and Samnites share Italy, Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, kings rule until the last is expelled and the republic is proclaimed in 510 BCE

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24 The Roman republic's social and political order" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** patricians and plebeians fight a two-century duel, plebeians win tribunes and the Twelve Tables, and the Senate, consuls and citizen legions run the republic
