# Ancient civilisations rome — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§23 The beginning of the Roman civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Etruscans, Latins and Samnites share Italy, Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE, kings rule until the last is expelled and the republic is proclaimed in 510 BCE

**2.** Tell the story of "§24 The Roman republic's social and political order" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** patricians and plebeians fight a two-century duel, plebeians win tribunes and the Twelve Tables, and the Senate, consuls and citizen legions run the republic
