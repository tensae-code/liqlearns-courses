# Lesson plan — Ancient egypt the nile kingdom

**Course:** History — Armenia · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient egypt the nile kingdom
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The first books: how to study history, the ancient world whole, and Armenia from the Highland's first peoples to the Bagratuni kingdom (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Ancient egypt the nile kingdom as Armenia's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The first books: how to study history, the ancient world whole, and Armenia from the Highland's first peoples to the Bagratuni kingdom — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§2 The rise of the Egyptian civilisation** — the Nile's floods feed one valley, Menes unites Upper and Lower Egypt about 3100 BCE, pharaohs raise pyramids, and the Hyksos invasion breaks the kingdom
2. **§3 Egypt's economy and society** — New Kingdom pharaohs Thutmose III and Ramesses II push Egypt's borders out, peasants lift Nile water with the shaduf, and Persia finally conquers the weakened land in 525 BCE
3. **Theme 2 §1: ancient Egyptian society and the civilisation's achievements** — the Nile's flood makes a kingdom; pharaohs raise pyramids, priests keep the calendar and scribes write in hieroglyphs for three thousand years
4. **Thematic plan, Theme 2: the civilisations of the Ancient East** — Egypt, Mesopotamia, Iran, India and China are taught in sequence as the first great civilisation stories of the year
