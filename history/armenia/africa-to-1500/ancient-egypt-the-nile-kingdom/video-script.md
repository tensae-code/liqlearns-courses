# Video script — Ancient egypt the nile kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Ancient egypt the nile kingdom.

the Nile's floods feed one valley, Menes unites Upper and Lower Egypt about 3100 BCE, pharaohs raise pyramids, and the Hyksos invasion breaks the kingdom.

New Kingdom pharaohs Thutmose III and Ramesses II push Egypt's borders out, peasants lift Nile water with the shaduf, and Persia finally conquers the weakened land in 525 BCE.

the Nile's flood makes a kingdom; pharaohs raise pyramids, priests keep the calendar and scribes write in hieroglyphs for three thousand years.

Egypt, Mesopotamia, Iran, India and China are taught in sequence as the first great civilisation stories of the year.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
