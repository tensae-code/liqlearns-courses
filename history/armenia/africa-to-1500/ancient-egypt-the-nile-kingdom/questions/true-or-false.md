# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§2 The rise of the Egyptian civilisation", the Nile's floods feed one valley, Menes unites Upper and Lower Egypt about 3100 BCE, pharaohs raise pyramids, and the Hyksos invasion breaks the kingdom (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§3 Egypt's economy and society", New Kingdom pharaohs Thutmose III and Ramesses II push Egypt's borders out, peasants lift Nile water with the shaduf, and Persia finally conquers the weakened land in 525 BCE (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
