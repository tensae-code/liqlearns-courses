# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§2 The rise of the Egyptian civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Nile's floods feed one valley, Menes unites Upper and Lower Egypt about 3100 BCE, pharaohs raise pyramids, and the Hyksos invasion breaks the kingdom

**2.** Tell the story of "§3 Egypt's economy and society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** New Kingdom pharaohs Thutmose III and Ramesses II push Egypt's borders out, peasants lift Nile water with the shaduf, and Persia finally conquers the weakened land in 525 BCE
