## Sub lesson 1 — §1 The beginning of civilisation

farming peoples settle, clans join into tribes, bronze then iron tools change work, and surplus food lets the first civilisations rise.

## Sub lesson 2 — 1.2 The Neolithic revolution and the beginning of the Armenian people's formation

Gordon Childe's "Neolithic revolution" reaches the Highland about 12,000 years ago — farming, pottery and metal — and tribes slowly fuse into a people.
