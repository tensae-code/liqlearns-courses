# From stone tools to farming — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§1 The beginning of civilisation", farming peoples settle, clans join into tribes, bronze then iron tools change work, and surplus food lets the first civilisations rise (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "1.2 The Neolithic revolution and the beginning of the Armenian people's formation", Gordon Childe's "Neolithic revolution" reaches the Highland about 12,000 years ago — farming, pottery and metal — and tribes slowly fuse into a people (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
