# From stone tools to farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1 The beginning of civilisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** farming peoples settle, clans join into tribes, bronze then iron tools change work, and surplus food lets the first civilisations rise

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "1.2 The Neolithic revolution and the beginning of the Armenian people's formation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gordon Childe's "Neolithic revolution" reaches the Highland about 12,000 years ago — farming, pottery and metal — and tribes slowly fuse into a people
