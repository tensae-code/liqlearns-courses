# From stone tools to farming — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§1 The beginning of civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** farming peoples settle, clans join into tribes, bronze then iron tools change work, and surplus food lets the first civilisations rise

**2.** Tell the story of "1.2 The Neolithic revolution and the beginning of the Armenian people's formation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gordon Childe's "Neolithic revolution" reaches the Highland about 12,000 years ago — farming, pottery and metal — and tribes slowly fuse into a people
