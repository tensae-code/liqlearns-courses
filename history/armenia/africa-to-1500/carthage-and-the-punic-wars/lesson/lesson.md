## Sub lesson 1 — §25 The Punic Wars (part)

Carthage and Rome collide over Sicily, Hannibal crosses the Alps with elephants in 218 BCE, and Scipio's victory at Zama ends Carthage's great-power story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 4 §2: Carthage and the formation of its society

Tyre's daughter city rules the western sea trade until its duel with Rome ends in the Punic Wars.
