# Carthage and the punic wars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§25 The Punic Wars (part)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Carthage and Rome collide over Sicily, Hannibal crosses the Alps with elephants in 218 BCE, and Scipio's victory at Zama ends Carthage's great-power story

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 4 §2: Carthage and the formation of its society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tyre's daughter city rules the western sea trade until its duel with Rome ends in the Punic Wars
