# Carthage and the punic wars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 The Punic Wars (part)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Carthage and Rome collide over Sicily, Hannibal crosses the Alps with elephants in 218 BCE, and Scipio's victory at Zama ends Carthage's great-power story

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 4 §2: Carthage and the formation of its society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tyre's daughter city rules the western sea trade until its duel with Rome ends in the Punic Wars
