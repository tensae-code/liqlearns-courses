# The first world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1 International relations, 1800s–early 1900s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Vienna order decays into two armed camps, Entente and Triple Alliance, competing for colonies and arms

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 1, Lessons 2–4: international relations, 19th century to early 20th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the great powers bind themselves into rival alliances and empires, and every local quarrel becomes a danger to the whole continent
