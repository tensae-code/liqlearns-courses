# The first world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§1 International relations, 1800s–early 1900s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Vienna order decays into two armed camps, Entente and Triple Alliance, competing for colonies and arms

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 1, Lessons 2–4: international relations, 19th century to early 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the great powers bind themselves into rival alliances and empires, and every local quarrel becomes a danger to the whole continent
