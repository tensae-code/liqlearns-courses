# Post industrial society — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Theme 5 §§19–20: post-industrial society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the scientific-technical revolution moves work from factory to screen, and creative, educated labour becomes society's main wealth

**2.** Tell the story of "Programme Theme 5: The world at the modern stage" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the formation of the post-industrial (information) society, economic and unification prospects, and media literacy as a named outcome
