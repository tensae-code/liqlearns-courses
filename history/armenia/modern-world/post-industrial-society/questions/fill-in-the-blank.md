# Post industrial society — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 5 §§19–20: post-industrial society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the scientific-technical revolution moves work from factory to screen, and creative, educated labour becomes society's main wealth

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Programme Theme 5: The world at the modern stage" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the formation of the post-industrial (information) society, economic and unification prospects, and media literacy as a named outcome
