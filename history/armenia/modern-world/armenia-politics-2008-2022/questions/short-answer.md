# Armenia politics 2008 2022 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "6.3 Armenia's public-political life, 2008–2022" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Serzh Sargsyan's decade ends in the 2018 Velvet Revolution that carries Nikol Pashinyan to power, then the 2020 war defeat shakes the new government

**2.** Tell the story of "Appendix theme guide 6: the Republic of Armenia 1992–2022" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** three decades of presidents, disputed elections, the Velvet Revolution and the 2020 defeat are set out as one continuous post-independence story
