# The long road to human rights — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§6.3 The history of human rights and freedoms", a timeline runs from Cyrus's clay cylinder (539 BC) through Magna Carta (1215) to America's 1776 and France's 1789 declarations and the UN's 1948 charter - with Armenia's own Shahamir Shahamirian printing his «Vorogayt Parats» constitution draft in Madras in 1773 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lesson plan for §6.3, the history of human rights", teachers are advised to present rights as a long struggle won step by step, not a gift handed down (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
