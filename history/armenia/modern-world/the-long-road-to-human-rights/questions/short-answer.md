# The long road to human rights — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§6.3 The history of human rights and freedoms" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a timeline runs from Cyrus's clay cylinder (539 BC) through Magna Carta (1215) to America's 1776 and France's 1789 declarations and the UN's 1948 charter — with Armenia's own Shahamir Shahamirian printing his «Vorogayt Parats» constitution draft in Madras in 1773

**2.** Tell the story of "Lesson plan for §6.3, the history of human rights" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** teachers are advised to present rights as a long struggle won step by step, not a gift handed down
