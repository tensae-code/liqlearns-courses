# Video script — The long road to human rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The long road to human rights.

a timeline runs from Cyrus's clay cylinder (539 BC) through Magna Carta (1215) to America's 1776 and France's 1789 declarations and the UN's 1948 charter — with Armenia's own Shahamir Shahamirian printing his «Vorogayt Parats» constitution draft in Madras in 1773.

teachers are advised to present rights as a long struggle won step by step, not a gift handed down.

rights arrive in generations — civil and political rights won in the age of revolutions, social rights in the industrial age, collective rights after 1945.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
