# The age of world wars

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The age of world wars" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Theme 3 §12: the world wars

twice in thirty years the great powers drag every continent into total war, and the second ends with atomic bombs.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of The age of world wars as Armenia's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
