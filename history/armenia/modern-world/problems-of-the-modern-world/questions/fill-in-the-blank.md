# Problems of the modern world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 4, Lessons 60–61: the problems of the modern world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** terrorism, trafficking, cybercrime and ecological danger cross every border, and no state can answer them alone

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 4 §18: environmental, war and other problems" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the greenhouse effect, the Green movement, the Balkan wars, Iraq, the Arab-Israeli conflict and a demographic map being redrawn — the problems theme closes in the pupils' own present
