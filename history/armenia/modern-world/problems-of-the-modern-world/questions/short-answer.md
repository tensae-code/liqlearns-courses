# Problems of the modern world — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Theme 4, Lessons 60–61: the problems of the modern world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** terrorism, trafficking, cybercrime and ecological danger cross every border, and no state can answer them alone

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 4 §18: environmental, war and other problems" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the greenhouse effect, the Green movement, the Balkan wars, Iraq, the Arab-Israeli conflict and a demographic map being redrawn — the problems theme closes in the pupils' own present
