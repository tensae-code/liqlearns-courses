# International relations after the cold war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Theme 4, Lessons 58–59: changes in international relations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** one superpower remains, alliances search for new purposes, and regional wars replace the old East–West standoff
