# International relations after the cold war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 4, Lessons 58–59: changes in international relations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** one superpower remains, alliances search for new purposes, and regional wars replace the old East–West standoff

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 5 §22: prospects of unification and international structures" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** after the Cold War the world argues between one centre of power and many, while integration projects multiply
