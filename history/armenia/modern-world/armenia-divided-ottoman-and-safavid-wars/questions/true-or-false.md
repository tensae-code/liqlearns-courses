Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§4.2 Armenia and the Ottoman–Safavid wars, 1500s–early 1600s", two empires fight over Armenia for a century; Shah Abbas deports Julfa's Armenians to Persia, and the land is split east–west (Sub lesson 1)
