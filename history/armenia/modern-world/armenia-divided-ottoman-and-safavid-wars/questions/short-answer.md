# Armenia divided ottoman and safavid wars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§4.2 Armenia and the Ottoman–Safavid wars, 1500s–early 1600s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two empires fight over Armenia for a century; Shah Abbas deports Julfa's Armenians to Persia, and the land is split east–west

**2.** Tell the story of "§5.1 Armenia's internal and external situation, late 1600s–early 1700s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** melik principalities in Karabakh and Syunik keep a half-free Armenian life alive between the empires
