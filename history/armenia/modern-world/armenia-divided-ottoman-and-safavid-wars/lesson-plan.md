# Lesson plan — Armenia divided ottoman and safavid wars

**Course:** History — Armenia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Armenia divided ottoman and safavid wars
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — New Times and Armenia's stateless centuries: the modern world begins, and Armenia answers conquest, division and liberation plans (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Armenia divided ottoman and safavid wars as Armenia's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — New Times and Armenia's stateless centuries: the modern world begins, and Armenia answers conquest, division and liberation plans — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§4.2 Armenia and the Ottoman–Safavid wars, 1500s–early 1600s** — two empires fight over Armenia for a century; Shah Abbas deports Julfa's Armenians to Persia, and the land is split east–west
2. **§5.1 Armenia's internal and external situation, late 1600s–early 1700s** — melik principalities in Karabakh and Syunik keep a half-free Armenian life alive between the empires
