# The first world war course of the war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§3 The First World War", the Schlieffen plan fails, trenches freeze the front, and a war of peoples and machines consumes a generation (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 1, Lessons 8–10: the First World War", the alliance machine fires, armies dig into trenches from the Channel to the Alps, and a short war of movement becomes four years of attrition (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
