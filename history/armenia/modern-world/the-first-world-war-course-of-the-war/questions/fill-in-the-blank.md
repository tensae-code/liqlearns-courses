# The first world war course of the war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3 The First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Schlieffen plan fails, trenches freeze the front, and a war of peoples and machines consumes a generation

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 1, Lessons 8–10: the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the alliance machine fires, armies dig into trenches from the Channel to the Alps, and a short war of movement becomes four years of attrition
