# Video script — Assessment early modern and modern world history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Assessment early modern and modern world history.

a designed review gathers discoveries, revolutions and industrialisation into one arc.

the closing review ties the century's transformations to the beliefs that drove them.

every section is planned with its key concepts and the textbook pages that carry them, from the Discoveries to Japan's Asian path.

the year's second half is planned as one arc — Napoleon's transformations to the ideologies that promised better worlds — with its summary assessments.

a designed review binds the alliance system, the national problem, the war and 1917 into one crisis story.

the review asks why an order built to end war produced a worse one in twenty years.

the review closes the bipolar age, from total war to the two-camp peace.

the review closes the century with the bipolar order gone and a new one unformed.

a final review and one long dated timeline close the three-year world-history course.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
