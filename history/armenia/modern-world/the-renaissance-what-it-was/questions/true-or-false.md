# The renaissance what it was — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§52 Changes in society and cultural life", Gutenberg's press (1445) puts books in many hands, universities and new academies spread, and a humanist curiosity turns Europe's eyes from heaven to man (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§2 The Renaissance", Italian cities grow rich, and their artists and thinkers turn back to Greece and Rome to invent a new image of man (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
