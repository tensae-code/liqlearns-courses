# The renaissance what it was — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§52 Changes in society and cultural life" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Gutenberg's press (1445) puts books in many hands, universities and new academies spread, and a humanist curiosity turns Europe's eyes from heaven to man

**2.** Tell the story of "§2 The Renaissance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Italian cities grow rich, and their artists and thinkers turn back to Greece and Rome to invent a new image of man
