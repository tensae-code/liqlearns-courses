# Armenia first republic state and symbols — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "2.3 The state system and symbols of the Republic of Armenia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** on 28 May 1918 the Armenian National Council proclaims the republic; Aram Manukyan's government builds a parliament, ministries and the tricolour from nothing

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Appendix theme guide 2: Armenia on the road to restored independence; the First Republic" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** teachers present 1917's collapse, Sardarabad and the 1918 republic's state-building as the nation's recovered statehood
