# Armenia first republic state and symbols — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "2.3 The state system and symbols of the Republic of Armenia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** on 28 May 1918 the Armenian National Council proclaims the republic; Aram Manukyan's government builds a parliament, ministries and the tricolour from nothing

**2.** Tell the story of "Appendix theme guide 2: Armenia on the road to restored independence; the First Republic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** teachers present 1917's collapse, Sardarabad and the 1918 republic's state-building as the nation's recovered statehood
