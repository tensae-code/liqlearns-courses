# World religions and how they spread — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§21 World religions" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Buddhism, Christianity and Islam are compared as world-shaping faiths — how each arose, spread and organised its believers

**2.** Tell the story of "Teaching guidance for Theme 4, world religions and ideologies" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide has teachers compare the great faiths and the great "isms" as rival answers to the same question — how should people live together?
