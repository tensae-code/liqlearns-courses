# World religions and how they spread — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§21 World religions" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Buddhism, Christianity and Islam are compared as world-shaping faiths — how each arose, spread and organised its believers

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Teaching guidance for Theme 4, world religions and ideologies" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide has teachers compare the great faiths and the great "isms" as rival answers to the same question — how should people live together?
