# 1917 twice february and october in russia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4 Russia's revolutions of 1917" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** February topples the tsar; October brings the Bolsheviks, who promise peace, land and bread

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 1, Lessons 11–13: the Russian revolutions of 1917" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** bread queues and a collapsing front bring down the Tsar in February, then Lenin's Bolsheviks seize power in October and take Russia out of the war
