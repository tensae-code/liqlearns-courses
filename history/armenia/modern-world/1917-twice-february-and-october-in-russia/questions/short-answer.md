# 1917 twice february and october in russia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§4 Russia's revolutions of 1917" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** February topples the tsar; October brings the Bolsheviks, who promise peace, land and bread

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 1, Lessons 11–13: the Russian revolutions of 1917" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** bread queues and a collapsing front bring down the Tsar in February, then Lenin's Bolsheviks seize power in October and take Russia out of the war
