# 1917 twice february and october in russia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§4 Russia's revolutions of 1917", February topples the tsar; October brings the Bolsheviks, who promise peace, land and bread (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 1, Lessons 11–13: the Russian revolutions of 1917", bread queues and a collapsing front bring down the Tsar in February, then Lenin's Bolsheviks seize power in October and take Russia out of the war (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
