# War in the modern era — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 3 §10: the modern era changes the nature of war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** gunpowder, conscription and industry turn war from a duel of armies into a struggle of whole nations

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Programme Theme 3: War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the changing meaning of "war" across the ages, the question of justice in war, and researching war's traces in one's own community
