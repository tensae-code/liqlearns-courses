# Armenia diaspora formation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "7.1 The formation of the Armenian diaspora" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from medieval merchant colonies to the genocide survivors' settlements in Lebanon, France and America, dispersion becomes a second Armenian homeland

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Appendix theme guide 7: the Armenian diaspora, from formation to our days" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide treats the diaspora as half the nation — formed by trade and genocide, bound to the homeland after 1991
