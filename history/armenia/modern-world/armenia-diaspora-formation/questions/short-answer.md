# Armenia diaspora formation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "7.1 The formation of the Armenian diaspora" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from medieval merchant colonies to the genocide survivors' settlements in Lebanon, France and America, dispersion becomes a second Armenian homeland

**2.** Tell the story of "Appendix theme guide 7: the Armenian diaspora, from formation to our days" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide treats the diaspora as half the nation — formed by trade and genocide, bound to the homeland after 1991
