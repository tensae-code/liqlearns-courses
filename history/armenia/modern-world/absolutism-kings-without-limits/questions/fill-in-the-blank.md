# Absolutism kings without limits — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§55 Absolutism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** kings claim power from God alone — Richelieu breaks France's rivals for Louis XIII, Louis XIV reigns as the Sun King, and Tudor England and Habsburg Spain show other faces of the same system

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3 Political changes in Europe: absolute monarchies" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** kings gather every thread of power into one hand and rule as the state itself
