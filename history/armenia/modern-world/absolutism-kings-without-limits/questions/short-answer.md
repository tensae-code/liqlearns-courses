# Absolutism kings without limits — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§55 Absolutism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** kings claim power from God alone — Richelieu breaks France's rivals for Louis XIII, Louis XIV reigns as the Sun King, and Tudor England and Habsburg Spain show other faces of the same system

**2.** Tell the story of "§3 Political changes in Europe: absolute monarchies" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** kings gather every thread of power into one hand and rule as the state itself
