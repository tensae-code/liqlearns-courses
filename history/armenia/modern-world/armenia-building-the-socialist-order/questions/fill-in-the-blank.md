# Armenia building the socialist order — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "3.5 Soviet Armenia on the road to establishing the "socialist order"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** NEP gives way to collectivisation and crash industrialisation; peasants lose their land as factories and canals rise across the small republic

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Appendix theme guide 3: Soviet Armenia 1920–1991" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide frames seventy Soviet years as both modernisation and subordination — federation, borders fixed against Armenia, repression, war service and everyday life
