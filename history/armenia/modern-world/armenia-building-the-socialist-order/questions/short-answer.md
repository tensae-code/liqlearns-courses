# Armenia building the socialist order — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "3.5 Soviet Armenia on the road to establishing the "socialist order"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** NEP gives way to collectivisation and crash industrialisation; peasants lose their land as factories and canals rise across the small republic

**2.** Tell the story of "Appendix theme guide 3: Soviet Armenia 1920–1991" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide frames seventy Soviet years as both modernisation and subordination — federation, borders fixed against Armenia, repression, war service and everyday life
