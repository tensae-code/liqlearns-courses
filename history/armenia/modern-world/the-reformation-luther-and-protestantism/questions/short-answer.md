# The reformation luther and protestantism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§54 The Reformation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** anger at indulgences boils over when Luther nails his 95 theses in 1517, and his protest splits Western Christianity

**2.** Tell the story of "§3 Political changes in Europe: the Reformation's Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Luther's protest splits the western church, and princes and kings rebuild their states in the quarrel's shadow
