# The reformation luther and protestantism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§54 The Reformation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** anger at indulgences boils over when Luther nails his 95 theses in 1517, and his protest splits Western Christianity

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3 Political changes in Europe: the Reformation's Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Luther's protest splits the western church, and princes and kings rebuild their states in the quarrel's shadow
