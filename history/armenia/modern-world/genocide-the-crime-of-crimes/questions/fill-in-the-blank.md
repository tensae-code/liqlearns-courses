# Genocide the crime of crimes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 4 §15: genocide" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Young Turks' destruction of the Armenians in 1915 — the Mets Yeghern — becomes the case from which Raphael Lemkin builds the very word "genocide" and the 1948 UN Convention, yet Rwanda and Darfur show prevention still failing

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Programme Theme 4: Problems of all mankind" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** genocide heads the theme's content list, ahead of terrorism, trafficking, cybercrime and environmental problems
