# The great depression 1929

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The great depression 1929" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — §7 The Great Crisis and the ways out of it

Wall Street crashes, millions lose work, and states answer with the New Deal, Keynesian spending — or dictatorship.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2, Lessons 21–22: the Great Economic Crisis

Wall Street crashes in 1929, banks and factories close across the industrial world and millions lose work and faith in democracy.
