# The west after the cold war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16 Western countries in the post-Cold-War period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the victors of 1991 integrate Europe, globalise markets and meet terrorism and financial crisis as the new century's tests

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 4, Lessons 51–52: the countries of the West in the post-Cold War period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** with the Soviet rival gone, the Western states face unification in Europe, new wars at its edges and the question of what their victory means
