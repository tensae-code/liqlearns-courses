# The west after the cold war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§16 Western countries in the post-Cold-War period" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the victors of 1991 integrate Europe, globalise markets and meet terrorism and financial crisis as the new century's tests

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 4, Lessons 51–52: the countries of the West in the post-Cold War period" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** with the Soviet rival gone, the Western states face unification in Europe, new wars at its edges and the question of what their victory means
