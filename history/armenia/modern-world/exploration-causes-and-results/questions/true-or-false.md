# Exploration causes and results — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§1 The Great Geographical Discoveries", Europe's hunger for spices and gold pushes ships past the known world's edge, and the map of mankind is redrawn (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Theme 5 §9: the Great Geographical Discoveries and their consequences", Columbus, da Gama and Magellan join the world's halves; silver and slaves, empires and epidemics follow (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
