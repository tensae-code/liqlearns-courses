Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "§1 The Great Geographical Discoveries"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Europe's hunger for spices and gold pushes ships past the known world's edge, and the map of mankind is redrawn  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
