# Exploration causes and results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§1 The Great Geographical Discoveries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Europe's hunger for spices and gold pushes ships past the known world's edge, and the map of mankind is redrawn

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 5 §9: the Great Geographical Discoveries and their consequences" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Columbus, da Gama and Magellan join the world's halves; silver and slaves, empires and epidemics follow
