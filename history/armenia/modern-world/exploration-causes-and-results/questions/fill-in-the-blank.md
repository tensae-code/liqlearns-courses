# Exploration causes and results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1 The Great Geographical Discoveries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Europe's hunger for spices and gold pushes ships past the known world's edge, and the map of mankind is redrawn

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 5 §9: the Great Geographical Discoveries and their consequences" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Columbus, da Gama and Magellan join the world's halves; silver and slaves, empires and epidemics follow
