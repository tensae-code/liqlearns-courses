# The armenian question goes international — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§6.2 Western Armenia and the internationalisation of the Armenian Question" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** reform promises for Ottoman Armenians become a European diplomatic question at Berlin in 1878 — and remain promises
