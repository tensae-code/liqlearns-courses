# The second world war course in europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§11 The Second World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** blitzkrieg overruns Europe, the USSR and USA are pulled in, and Stalingrad and Normandy turn the tide to Berlin

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 3, Lessons 35–37: the Second World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hitler's blitzkrieg overruns Europe, the turn comes at Stalingrad and El Alamein, and the Allies fight back to Berlin and the Pacific surrender
