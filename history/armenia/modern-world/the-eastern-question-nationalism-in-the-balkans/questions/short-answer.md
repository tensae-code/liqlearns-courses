# The eastern question nationalism in the balkans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§2 The national problem in the multinational powers" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** nations inside the Ottoman, Habsburg and Russian empires demand states of their own, and the empires crack along the seams

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 1, Lessons 5–7: the national question in the multinational powers" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
