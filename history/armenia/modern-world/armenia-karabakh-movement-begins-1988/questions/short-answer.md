# Armenia karabakh movement begins 1988 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "4.1 The beginning of the Karabakh movement; Nagorno-Karabakh within Azerbaijan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** on 20 February 1988 Karabakh's soviet votes to join Armenia, Yerevan fills with a million marchers, and the Sumgait pogrom a week later turns a petition into a national movement

**2.** Tell the story of "Appendix theme guide 4: the Karabakh movement; winning independence" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the movement of 1988, the earthquake and the 1991 referendum are taught as the hinge between Soviet Armenia and the third republic
