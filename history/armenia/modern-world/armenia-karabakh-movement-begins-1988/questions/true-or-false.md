# Armenia karabakh movement begins 1988 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "4.1 The beginning of the Karabakh movement; Nagorno-Karabakh within Azerbaijan", on 20 February 1988 Karabakh's soviet votes to join Armenia, Yerevan fills with a million marchers, and the Sumgait pogrom a week later turns a petition into a national movement (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Appendix theme guide 4: the Karabakh movement; winning independence", the movement of 1988, the earthquake and the 1991 referendum are taught as the hinge between Soviet Armenia and the third republic (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
