# International cooperation un eu au — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Theme 1 §1: international and regional political cooperation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** after 1945 states build the UN, then the EU, the African Union and the OAS; Armenia joins the UN and pupils debate whether such bodies have a future

**2.** Tell the story of "Programme Theme 1: Cooperation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide's outcome list requires pupils to explain cooperation in every sphere of society and find it at work in their own community
