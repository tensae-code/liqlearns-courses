# The first world war versailles and the results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5 The Versailles–Washington system" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the victors redraw maps at Paris and limit navies at Washington, building an order Germany, Russia and the colonies never accepted

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 2, Lessons 16–18: forming the new world order, the Versailles–Washington system" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the victors redraw the map at Versailles and in Washington, Germany is humbled and new states appear, but the settlement plants the next war
