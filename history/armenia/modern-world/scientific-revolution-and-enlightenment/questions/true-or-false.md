# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§57 Cultural changes in Europe at the end of the Middle Ages", printing and universities multiply readers, Giordano Bruno is burned in 1600 for a sun-centred universe, and the Thirty Years' War (1618–1648) closes the age at Westphalia (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§4 The first Scientific Revolution", telescopes and mathematics dethrone the old cosmos — the Earth moves, and authority must now prove itself (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
