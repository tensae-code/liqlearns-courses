# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§57 Cultural changes in Europe at the end of the Middle Ages" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** printing and universities multiply readers, Giordano Bruno is burned in 1600 for a sun-centred universe, and the Thirty Years' War (1618–1648) closes the age at Westphalia

**2.** Tell the story of "§4 The first Scientific Revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** telescopes and mathematics dethrone the old cosmos — the Earth moves, and authority must now prove itself
