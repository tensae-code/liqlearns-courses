# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§57 Cultural changes in Europe at the end of the Middle Ages" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** printing and universities multiply readers, Giordano Bruno is burned in 1600 for a sun-centred universe, and the Thirty Years' War (1618–1648) closes the age at Westphalia

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4 The first Scientific Revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** telescopes and mathematics dethrone the old cosmos — the Earth moves, and authority must now prove itself
