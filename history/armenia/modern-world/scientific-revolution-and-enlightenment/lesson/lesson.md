# Scientific revolution and enlightenment

## Sub lesson 1 — §57 Cultural changes in Europe at the end of the Middle Ages

printing and universities multiply readers, Giordano Bruno is burned in 1600 for a sun-centred universe, and the Thirty Years' War (1618–1648) closes the age at Westphalia.

## Sub lesson 2 — §4 The first Scientific Revolution

telescopes and mathematics dethrone the old cosmos — the Earth moves, and authority must now prove itself.

## Sub lesson 3 — §6 The age of Enlightenment

Voltaire, Montesquieu and Rousseau argue that reason, not birth, should order society.

## Sub lesson 4 — Theme 6 §4: scientific innovations as the driving force of society's development

the compass, gunpowder, printing and the new astronomy show knowledge itself becoming an engine of change.
