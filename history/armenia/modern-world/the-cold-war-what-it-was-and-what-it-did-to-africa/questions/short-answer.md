# The cold war what it was and what it did to africa — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§12 The Cold War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two armed camps face off with atom bombs, fighting by proxy from Korea to Cuba while avoiding the final war

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 3, Lessons 38–39: the Cold War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the wartime allies split into two armed camps, and for forty years the world lives under the shadow of nuclear war without a direct superpower battle
