# The cold war what it was and what it did to africa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 The Cold War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two armed camps face off with atom bombs, fighting by proxy from Korea to Cuba while avoiding the final war

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 3, Lessons 38–39: the Cold War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the wartime allies split into two armed camps, and for forty years the world lives under the shadow of nuclear war without a direct superpower battle
