# Armenia the genocide 1915 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "1.3 The First World War and the Armenian Genocide" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** under cover of war the Young Turk government arrests Constantinople's Armenian leaders on 24 April 1915, then deports and kills the Ottoman Armenians, while Van and Musa Dagh resist

**2.** Tell the story of "Appendix theme guide 1: the Armenian people at the start of the 20th century; the Armenian Genocide (lessons 1–18 of the thematic plan, keyed to student chapters 1.1–1.4)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide walks teachers through the Hamidian massacres, the wartime genocide and its condemnation as the course's opening national catastrophe
