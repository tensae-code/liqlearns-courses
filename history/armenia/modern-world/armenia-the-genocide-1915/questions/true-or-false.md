# Armenia the genocide 1915 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "1.3 The First World War and the Armenian Genocide", under cover of war the Young Turk government arrests Constantinople's Armenian leaders on 24 April 1915, then deports and kills the Ottoman Armenians, while Van and Musa Dagh resist (Sub lesson 1)
