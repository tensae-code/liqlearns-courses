# Armenia the genocide 1915 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "1.3 The First World War and the Armenian Genocide" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** under cover of war the Young Turk government arrests Constantinople's Armenian leaders on 24 April 1915, then deports and kills the Ottoman Armenians, while Van and Musa Dagh resist

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Appendix theme guide 1: the Armenian people at the start of the 20th century; the Armenian Genocide (lessons 1–18 of the thematic plan, keyed to student chapters 1.1–1.4)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the guide walks teachers through the Hamidian massacres, the wartime genocide and its condemnation as the course's opening national catastrophe
