# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Niue, each district traditionally owned its stretch of coast and shared it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Niue's gardens hold nothing from the ancestors, because its crops were never learned or handed down. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: False — Niue's gardens grow the main food crops its ancestors learned to grow on a raised coral atoll — taro, sweet potato, yams and more; each garden is a living archive of that handed-down knowledge.**
