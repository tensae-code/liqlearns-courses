# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How are whales told of across the Pacific, in the people's stories? (Sub lesson 1)
a) as guardians  b) as enemies of the islands  c) as cargo for ships  d) as weather signs only
**Answer: a) as guardians** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does Niue's name break down into? (Sub lesson 5)
a) Niu (island) + e (sea)  b) Niu (coconut) + e (here) — "here is the coconut"  c) Niu (fish) + e (reef)  d) it cannot be broken down
**Answer: b) Niu (coconut) + e (here) — "here is the coconut"** — That is what this lesson teaches.
