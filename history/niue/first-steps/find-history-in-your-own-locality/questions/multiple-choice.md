# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who does the guide send pupils to talk to?
a) a visiting sailor  b) an elder  c) a shopkeeper in another country  d) nobody — pupils only read
**Answer: b) an elder** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the class list, from what the elder remembers?
a) the prices of imported food  b) the names of foreign ships  c) traditional fishing practices and the fish that were caught  d) the school timetable
**Answer: c) traditional fishing practices and the fish that were caught** — That is what this lesson teaches.
