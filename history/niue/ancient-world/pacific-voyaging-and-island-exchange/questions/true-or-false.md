# Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The islands in this lesson were cut off from each other, each living entirely alone.
**Answer: False — they were part of one large exchange community, joined by regular voyaging.**

**2.** The reader's title for this story is "The Ocean in our blood."
**Answer: True**
