# Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three of the islands that belonged to the one large exchange community.
**Answer:** Any three of: Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea.

**2.** In this lesson, was the sea a wall or a road? Say why.
**Answer:** A road — peoples voyaged regularly across it between the islands, circulating wealth, skills and goods, so one sea joined many homes.
