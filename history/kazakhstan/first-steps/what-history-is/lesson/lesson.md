# What history is

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "What history is" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Introduction: what history is

Cicero calls history life's teacher; pupils meet historical personalities who changed their country and the world.

## Sub lesson 2 — §1 What the subject "History" studies

Herodotus, the first historian, opens the question of where we come from and who we are.

## Sub lesson 3 — Introduction

Pupils are told why the primitive commune and the first civilisations still shape their own lives.

## Sub lesson 4 — §1 Overview of Kazakhstan's ancient history

Pupils survey the whole ancient road from first tools to tribal states before walking it.

## Sub lesson 5 — §1 What the medieval history of Kazakhstan studies

Pupils enter a thousand-year steppe story and learn its periods and sources.

## Sub lesson 6 — §1 General overview of medieval Kazakhstan's history (lesson plan)

The teacher opens the medieval course by mapping a thousand years of steppe states.

## Sub lesson 7 — Introduction

The course opens the medieval millennium from khaganates to khanate as one story.

## Sub lesson 8 — Introduction

Pupils cross from the ancient world into a thousand years called the Middle Ages.

## Sub lesson 9 — Introduction: the medieval course and its periodisation

Pupils place the Middle Ages on the world timeline from Rome's fall to the 17th century.

## Sub lesson 10 — Introduction

The medieval millennium is introduced as world history's middle act.

## Sub lesson 11 — Introductory lesson: what do we learn from medieval history?

The medieval course opens by asking what the middle centuries explain about today's Kazakhstan.

## Sub lesson 12 — Introduction

The new-time course opens by framing the 18th–19th centuries as one age of revolutions.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of What history is as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
