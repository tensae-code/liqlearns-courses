# Lesson plan — What history is

**Course:** History — Kazakhstan · **Title:** First steps · **Lesson:** What history is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The ancient world and ancient Kazakhstan — first humans, river civilisations, Greece and Rome, and the steppe tribes from the Stone Age to the Huns (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of What history is as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The ancient world and ancient Kazakhstan — first humans, river civilisations, Greece and Rome, and the steppe tribes from the Stone Age to the Huns — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Introduction: what history is** — Cicero calls history life's teacher; pupils meet historical personalities who changed their country and the world
2. **§1 What the subject "History" studies** — Herodotus, the first historian, opens the question of where we come from and who we are
3. **Introduction** — pupils are told why the primitive commune and the first civilisations still shape their own lives
4. **§1 Overview of Kazakhstan's ancient history** — pupils survey the whole ancient road from first tools to tribal states before walking it
5. **§1 What the medieval history of Kazakhstan studies** — pupils enter a thousand-year steppe story and learn its periods and sources
6. **§1 General overview of medieval Kazakhstan's history (lesson plan)** — the teacher opens the medieval course by mapping a thousand years of steppe states
7. **Introduction** — the course opens the medieval millennium from khaganates to khanate as one story
8. **Introduction** — pupils cross from the ancient world into a thousand years called the Middle Ages
9. **Introduction: the medieval course and its periodisation** — pupils place the Middle Ages on the world timeline from Rome's fall to the 17th century
10. **Introduction** — the medieval millennium is introduced as world history's middle act
11. **Introductory lesson: what do we learn from medieval history?** — the medieval course opens by asking what the middle centuries explain about today's Kazakhstan
12. **Introduction** — the new-time course opens by framing the 18th–19th centuries as one age of revolutions

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
