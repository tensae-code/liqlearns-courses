# What history is — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Introduction: what history is" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cicero calls history life's teacher; pupils meet historical personalities who changed their country and the world.

**2.** Tell the story of "§1 What the subject "History" studies" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Herodotus, the first historian, opens the question of where we come from and who we are.
