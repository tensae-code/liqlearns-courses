# Colonisation the partition and fashoda — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§2 The territorial division of the world and the political order of states by the early 20th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The great powers finish carving up the globe into colonies while monarchies and republics face rising workers', women's and peace movements.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23 The completion of the colonial division of the world; the forming of military-political blocs" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The last free territories are seized and Europe sorts itself into the armed camps of 1914.
