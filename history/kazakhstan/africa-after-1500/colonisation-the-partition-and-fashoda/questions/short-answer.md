# Colonisation the partition and fashoda — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§2 The territorial division of the world and the political order of states by the early 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The great powers finish carving up the globe into colonies while monarchies and republics face rising workers', women's and peace movements.

**2.** Tell the story of "§23 The completion of the colonial division of the world; the forming of military-political blocs" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The last free territories are seized and Europe sorts itself into the armed camps of 1914.
