# Kazakhstan sarmatians — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§41–42 Sarmatians in written and archaeological sources" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Roman-era writers and rich graves reconstruct the Sarmatian world.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§43–44 Sarmatian social order and economy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
