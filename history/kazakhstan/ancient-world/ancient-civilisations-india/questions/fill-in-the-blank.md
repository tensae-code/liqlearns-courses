# Ancient civilisations india — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§14 How the ancient city of Mohenjo-Daro vanished" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A planned Indus city of drains and baths empties and is buried, its script still unread.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§14 Creation of the ancient Indian state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Indus cities fall, Aryan kingdoms rise, and Magadha unites northern India.
