# Ancient civilisations india — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§14 How the ancient city of Mohenjo-Daro vanished" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A planned Indus city of drains and baths empties and is buried, its script still unread.

**2.** Tell the story of "§14 Creation of the ancient Indian state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Indus cities fall, Aryan kingdoms rise, and Magadha unites northern India.
