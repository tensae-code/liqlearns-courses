# Greek schools and academies — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§27 What gymnasia, lyceums and academies taught" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greek boys train body and mind in schools whose names our own schools still carry.

**2.** Tell the story of "§26 Education and science in ancient Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Greek schools, Euclid and Archimedes lay the tracks of European science.
