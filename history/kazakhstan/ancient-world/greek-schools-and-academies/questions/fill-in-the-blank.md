# Greek schools and academies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§27 What gymnasia, lyceums and academies taught" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greek boys train body and mind in schools whose names our own schools still carry.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 Education and science in ancient Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Greek schools, Euclid and Archimedes lay the tracks of European science.
