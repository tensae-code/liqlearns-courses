# Ancient civilisations athens and democracy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§22 Why Greece is called democracy's birthplace" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athenian citizens gather, speak and vote, inventing rule by the people.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§22 Ancient Greek city-states" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The polis is born — small citizen states that govern themselves.
