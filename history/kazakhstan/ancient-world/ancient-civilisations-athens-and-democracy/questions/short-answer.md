# Ancient civilisations athens and democracy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§22 Why Greece is called democracy's birthplace" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athenian citizens gather, speak and vote, inventing rule by the people.

**2.** Tell the story of "§22 Ancient Greek city-states" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The polis is born — small citizen states that govern themselves.
