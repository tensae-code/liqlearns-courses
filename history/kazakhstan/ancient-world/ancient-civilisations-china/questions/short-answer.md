# Ancient civilisations china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§18 What China's ancient thinkers taught" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Confucius and his rivals argue over how rulers and families should live, and their schools shape China.

**2.** Tell the story of "§19 What the Terracotta Army tells about ancient China" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The First Emperor buries an army of clay soldiers to guard him in death, revealing his power.
