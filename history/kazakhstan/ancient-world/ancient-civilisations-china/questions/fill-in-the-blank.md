# Ancient civilisations china — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§18 What China's ancient thinkers taught" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Confucius and his rivals argue over how rulers and families should live, and their schools shape China.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§19 What the Terracotta Army tells about ancient China" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The First Emperor buries an army of clay soldiers to guard him in death, revealing his power.
