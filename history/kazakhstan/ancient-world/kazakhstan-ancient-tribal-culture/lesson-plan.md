# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan ancient tribal culture

**Course:** History — Kazakhstan · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan ancient tribal culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The ancient world and ancient Kazakhstan — first humans, river civilisations, Greece and Rome, and the steppe tribes from the Stone Age to the Huns (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Kazakhstan ancient tribal culture as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The ancient world and ancient Kazakhstan — first humans, river civilisations, Greece and Rome, and the steppe tribes from the Stone Age to the Huns — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§50–51 Culture of the ancient tribes** — animal-style art, epics and monuments carry the steppe tribes' memory
2. **§53 Brief conclusion: a journey through ancient Kazakhstan** — pupils retrace the whole road from stone tools to the Huns as one national story
3. **1.7 Ancient pictures carved on Kazakhstan's rocks** — petroglyphs of hunts, suns and warriors are the steppe's first picture-stories
4. **1.8 A journey into ancient people's life** — pupils travel the Stone and Bronze Age chapters as one review quest
5. **7.1 Peoples of ancient Kazakhstan (review)** — Sakas, Usuns, Kangly, Huns and Sarmatians are gathered as ancestors of the Kazakh people
6. **7.2 A journey to ancient Kazakhstan** — the course closes with a travel-quest back through every age studied
7. **§16 Religious ideas and worldview of primitive people** — totems, ancestors and nature spirits — the first people explain their world
8. **§17 Rock-painting art and the first people's worldview** — petroglyph galleries turn cliff faces into the steppe's first picture books
9. **§18 Ancient everyday life** — food, clothing and dwellings reconstruct one ordinary ancient day
10. **§23 Art of the ancient tribes** — animal-style gold and carved bone carry the tribes' myths
11. **§61 Bronze Age inhabitants' appearance and anthropology** — skulls and reconstructions give the bronze tribes back their faces
12. **§62 Iron Age inhabitants' appearance and racial features** — the iron-age steppe mixes Europoid and Mongoloid features into new peoples
13. **§63 Let's travel to the ancient world** — pupils pack for an imaginary journey through every age of ancient Kazakhstan
14. **§64 Culture of the early tribes** — the course closes by gathering the ancient tribes' art, faith and craft
15. **1.7 Ancient rock drawings of Kazakhstan (lesson plans)** — petroglyph images become sources pupils read like texts
16. **1.8 A journey into ancient people's life (lesson plans)** — a review journey lesson gathers the whole first chapter
17. **7.1 Anthropological appearance of ancient Kazakhstan's people (lesson plans)** — reconstructions give ancient faces back in the review lessons
18. **7.2 A journey to ancient Kazakhstan (lesson plans)** — the final travel-quest lesson reviews the whole course

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
