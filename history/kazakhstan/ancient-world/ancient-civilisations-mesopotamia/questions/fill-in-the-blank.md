# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4 Why ancient civilisations grew in river valleys" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Great rivers flood, soften the soil and reward organised irrigation with surplus and cities.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§11 How Hammurabi made Babylon a mighty empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hammurabi unites Mesopotamia and carves his laws on stone so one rule holds the empire.
