# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§4 Why ancient civilisations grew in river valleys" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Great rivers flood, soften the soil and reward organised irrigation with surplus and cities.

**2.** Tell the story of "§11 How Hammurabi made Babylon a mighty empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hammurabi unites Mesopotamia and carves his laws on stone so one rule holds the empire.
