# Greek art and theatre — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 What Greek art was like" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sculptors and builders seek perfect proportion and theatres turn myth into public drama.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§27 Culture of ancient Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Temples, statues and tragedies make Greek culture the ancient world's model.
