# Greek art and theatre — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§26 What Greek art was like" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sculptors and builders seek perfect proportion and theatres turn myth into public drama.

**2.** Tell the story of "§27 Culture of ancient Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Temples, statues and tragedies make Greek culture the ancient world's model.
