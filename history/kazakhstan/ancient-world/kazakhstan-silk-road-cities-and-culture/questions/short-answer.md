# Kazakhstan silk road cities and culture — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§23 The Silk Road's influence on medieval Kazakh cities' culture" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Otrar, Taraz and Syganak flower as trade makes urban culture.

**2.** Tell the story of "§24 Stone-working, fine, pottery and applied art of the ancient Turks" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Turkic craftsmen carve, paint and fire the goods the caravans carry.
