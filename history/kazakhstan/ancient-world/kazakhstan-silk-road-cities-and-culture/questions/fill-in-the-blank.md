# Kazakhstan silk road cities and culture — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23 The Silk Road's influence on medieval Kazakh cities' culture" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Otrar, Taraz and Syganak flower as trade makes urban culture.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24 Stone-working, fine, pottery and applied art of the ancient Turks" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Turkic craftsmen carve, paint and fire the goods the caravans carry.
