# The crusades causes and course — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§10 Causes of the Crusades" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pope, knights and pilgrims converge on a holy war for Jerusalem.

**2.** Tell the story of "§11 Europe and the Muslim world after the Crusades" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two centuries of war end in trade, translation and wary coexistence.
