# The crusades causes and course — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10 Causes of the Crusades" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pope, knights and pilgrims converge on a holy war for Jerusalem.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§11 Europe and the Muslim world after the Crusades" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two centuries of war end in trade, translation and wary coexistence.
