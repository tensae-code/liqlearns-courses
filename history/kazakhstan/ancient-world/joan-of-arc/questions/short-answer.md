# Joan of arc — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§17 Joan of Arc" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A peasant girl raises the siege of Orléans and turns France's war around.

**2.** Tell the story of "§16 Joan of Arc; France's victory in the Hundred Years War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Joan's banner turns the war and France emerges united.
