# Joan of arc — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§17 Joan of Arc" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A peasant girl raises the siege of Orléans and turns France's war around.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16 Joan of Arc; France's victory in the Hundred Years War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Joan's banner turns the war and France emerges united.
