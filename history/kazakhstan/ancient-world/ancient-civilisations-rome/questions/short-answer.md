# Ancient civilisations rome — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§28 How Rome became the ancient world's mightiest empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A Tiber village conquers Italy, then Carthage, then the Mediterranean world.

**2.** Tell the story of "§29 How culture reflects Roman society's development" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Roman law, roads, baths and letters mirror an empire growing rich and ordered.
