# Ancient civilisations rome — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§28 How Rome became the ancient world's mightiest empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A Tiber village conquers Italy, then Carthage, then the Mediterranean world.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§29 How culture reflects Roman society's development" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
