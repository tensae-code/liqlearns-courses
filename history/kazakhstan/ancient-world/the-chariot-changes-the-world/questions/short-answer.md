# The chariot changes the world — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§10 How the chariot changed the world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The light war chariot arrives and kings who master it conquer faster than any infantry.

**2.** Tell the story of "§11 Military art of the ancient epoch" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chariots, infantry and siegecraft decide which ancient state survives.
