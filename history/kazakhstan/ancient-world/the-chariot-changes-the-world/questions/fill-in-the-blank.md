# The chariot changes the world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10 How the chariot changed the world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The light war chariot arrives and kings who master it conquer faster than any infantry.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§11 Military art of the ancient epoch" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chariots, infantry and siegecraft decide which ancient state survives.
