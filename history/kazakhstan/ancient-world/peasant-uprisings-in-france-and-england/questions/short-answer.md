# Peasant uprisings in france and england — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§14 Peasant uprisings in France and England" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Jacquerie and Wat Tyler's rebels shake two kingdoms.

**2.** Tell the story of "§16 Peasant uprisings in France and England" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Jacquerie and the English rising of 1381 demand an end to serfdom.
