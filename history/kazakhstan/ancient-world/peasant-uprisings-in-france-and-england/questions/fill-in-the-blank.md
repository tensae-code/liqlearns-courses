# Peasant uprisings in france and england — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§14 Peasant uprisings in France and England" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Jacquerie and Wat Tyler's rebels shake two kingdoms.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16 Peasant uprisings in France and England" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Jacquerie and the English rising of 1381 demand an end to serfdom.
