# Ancient civilisations indias order and gifts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§15 Why Hinduism prevailed over Buddhism in ancient India" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Buddha's teaching spreads abroad while at home Hindu practice absorbs and outlasts it.

**2.** Tell the story of "§16 India's role as an ancient trade centre" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Indian ports and passes link East and West and grow rich on the exchange.
