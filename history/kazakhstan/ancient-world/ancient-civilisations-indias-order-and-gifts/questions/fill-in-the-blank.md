# Ancient civilisations indias order and gifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§15 Why Hinduism prevailed over Buddhism in ancient India" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Buddha's teaching spreads abroad while at home Hindu practice absorbs and outlasts it.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16 India's role as an ancient trade centre" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
