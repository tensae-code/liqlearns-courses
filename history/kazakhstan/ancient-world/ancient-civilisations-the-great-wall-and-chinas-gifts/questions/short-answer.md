# Ancient civilisations the great wall and chinas gifts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§20 China's ancient discoveries that reached our days" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Paper, printing, gunpowder and the compass travel from Chinese workshops into world use.

**2.** Tell the story of "§20 Culture of ancient China" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Characters, Confucian schools and inventions give China its lasting gifts.
