# Ancient civilisations the great wall and chinas gifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§20 China's ancient discoveries that reached our days" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Paper, printing, gunpowder and the compass travel from Chinese workshops into world use.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§20 Culture of ancient China" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Characters, Confucian schools and inventions give China its lasting gifts.
