# Centralised states rise in europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§16 Centralised states form in Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kings tame their barons and France, England and Spain become single states.

**2.** Tell the story of "§17 Centralised states form in Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kings gather taxes, armies and courts into single states.
