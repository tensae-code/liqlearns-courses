# Centralised states rise in europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16 Centralised states form in Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kings tame their barons and France, England and Spain become single states.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§17 Centralised states form in Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kings gather taxes, armies and courts into single states.
