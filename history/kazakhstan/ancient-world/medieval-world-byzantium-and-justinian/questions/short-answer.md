# Medieval world byzantium and justinian — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§2–3 The Eastern Roman Empire; a Byzantine ambassador visits Turkic Khagan Istemi" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Byzantium survives in the East and its envoys reach the Turkic khagan's court on the steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of "§3 The Byzantine Empire in the 5th–10th centuries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Constantinople's emperors hold Rome's eastern half for a thousand years.
