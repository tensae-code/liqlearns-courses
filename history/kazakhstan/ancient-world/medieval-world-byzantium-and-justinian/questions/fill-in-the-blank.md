# Medieval world byzantium and justinian — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§2–3 The Eastern Roman Empire; a Byzantine ambassador visits Turkic Khagan Istemi" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Byzantium survives in the East and its envoys reach the Turkic khagan's court on the steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3 The Byzantine Empire in the 5th–10th centuries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Constantinople's emperors hold Rome's eastern half for a thousand years.
