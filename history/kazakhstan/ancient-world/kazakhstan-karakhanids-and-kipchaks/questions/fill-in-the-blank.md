# Kazakhstan karakhanids and kipchaks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16 The political map of the Great Steppe changes, 10th–early 13th c." turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Old khaganates fall and new powers — Karakhanids, Kipchaks, Khwarezm — divide the steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§17 The Karakhanid state (942–1212)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Karakhanids unite the south-east and take Islam as a state faith.
