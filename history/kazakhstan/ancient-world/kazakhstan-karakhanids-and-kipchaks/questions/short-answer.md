# Kazakhstan karakhanids and kipchaks — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§16 The political map of the Great Steppe changes, 10th–early 13th c." — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Old khaganates fall and new powers — Karakhanids, Kipchaks, Khwarezm — divide the steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of "§17 The Karakhanid state (942–1212)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Karakhanids unite the south-east and take Islam as a state faith.
