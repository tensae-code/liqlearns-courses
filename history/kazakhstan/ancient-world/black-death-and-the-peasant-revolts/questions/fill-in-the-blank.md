# Black death and the peasant revolts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§14 The Black Death; peasant revolts in France and England" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Plague empties villages, then the Jacquerie and Wat Tyler's rebels rise.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "6.1 Causes of the Black Death's rapid spread" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rats, fleas and trade roads carry plague across a continent in five years.
