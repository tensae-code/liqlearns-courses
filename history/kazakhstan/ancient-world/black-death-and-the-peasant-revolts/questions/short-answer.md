# Black death and the peasant revolts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§14 The Black Death; peasant revolts in France and England" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Plague empties villages, then the Jacquerie and Wat Tyler's rebels rise.

**2.** Tell the story of "6.1 Causes of the Black Death's rapid spread" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rats, fleas and trade roads carry plague across a continent in five years.
