# Kazakhstan mongol age and golden horde — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§30 Chinggis Khan and the creation of the Mongol state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Temujin unites the Mongols in 1206 and turns them outward.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§31–32 Turko-Mongol campaigns into Central Asia and Kazakhstan; the defence of Otrar" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Otrar's governor resists, the city is destroyed, and the steppe is conquered.
