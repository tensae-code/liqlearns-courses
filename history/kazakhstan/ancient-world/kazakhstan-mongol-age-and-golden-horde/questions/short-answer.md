# Kazakhstan mongol age and golden horde — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§30 Chinggis Khan and the creation of the Mongol state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Temujin unites the Mongols in 1206 and turns them outward.

**2.** Tell the story of "§31–32 Turko-Mongol campaigns into Central Asia and Kazakhstan; the defence of Otrar" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Otrar's governor resists, the city is destroyed, and the steppe is conquered.
