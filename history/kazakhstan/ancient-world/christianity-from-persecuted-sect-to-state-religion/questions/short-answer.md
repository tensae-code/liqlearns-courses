# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§31 The rise of Christianity" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A preacher in Judea and his followers begin a faith that will remake the empire.

**2.** Tell the story of "§32 The first Christians and the Roman Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Persecuted communities survive the arena and win the empire from below.
