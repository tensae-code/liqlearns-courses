# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§31 The rise of Christianity" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A preacher in Judea and his followers begin a faith that will remake the empire.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§32 The first Christians and the Roman Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Persecuted communities survive the arena and win the empire from below.
