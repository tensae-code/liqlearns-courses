# Ancient civilisations rome divides — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§31 The Roman Empire in the 1st–3rd centuries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Emperors stretch the empire to its widest, then soldiers and crisis tear at it.

**2.** Tell the story of "§32 The Roman Empire in the 4th–5th centuries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The empire splits East and West, takes Christianity, and the West falls to invaders.
