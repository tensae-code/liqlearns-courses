# Ancient civilisations rome divides — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§31 The Roman Empire in the 1st–3rd centuries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Emperors stretch the empire to its widest, then soldiers and crisis tear at it.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§32 The Roman Empire in the 4th–5th centuries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The empire splits East and West, takes Christianity, and the West falls to invaders.
