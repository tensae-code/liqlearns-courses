# Kazakhstan bronze age tribes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§14–15 The Bronze Age (2nd–1st millennia BC)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bronze replaces stone and great tribal cultures spread across the Kazakh steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16–17 Economy of the early-metal-age tribes" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Herders and metalworkers divide the work and the steppe economy takes shape.
