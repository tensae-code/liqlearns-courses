# Kazakhstan bronze age tribes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§14–15 The Bronze Age (2nd–1st millennia BC)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bronze replaces stone and great tribal cultures spread across the Kazakh steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of "§16–17 Economy of the early-metal-age tribes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Herders and metalworkers divide the work and the steppe economy takes shape.
