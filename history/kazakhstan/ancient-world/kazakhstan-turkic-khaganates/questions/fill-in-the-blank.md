# Kazakhstan turkic khaganates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§2 The Turkic Khaganate (552–603)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bumin and Istemi found the first great Turkic empire across the steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3–4 The Western and Eastern Turkic Khaganates" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The khaganate splits into rival West and East, each ruling its half of Eurasia.
