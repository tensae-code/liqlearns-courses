# Kazakhstan turkic khaganates — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§2 The Turkic Khaganate (552–603)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bumin and Istemi found the first great Turkic empire across the steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of "§3–4 The Western and Eastern Turkic Khaganates" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The khaganate splits into rival West and East, each ruling its half of Eurasia.
