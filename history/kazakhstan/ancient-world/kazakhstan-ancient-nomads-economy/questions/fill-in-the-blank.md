# Kazakhstan ancient nomads economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§31 The formation of cattle-breeding in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Steppe tribes commit fully to nomadic herding, the economy that defines them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§32 Economy of the steppe tribes" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Herds on seasonal routes feed, clothe and carry whole peoples.
