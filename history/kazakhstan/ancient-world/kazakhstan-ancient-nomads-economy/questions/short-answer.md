# Kazakhstan ancient nomads economy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§31 The formation of cattle-breeding in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Steppe tribes commit fully to nomadic herding, the economy that defines them.

**2.** Tell the story of "§32 Economy of the steppe tribes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Herds on seasonal routes feed, clothe and carry whole peoples.
