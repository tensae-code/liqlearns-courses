# Kazakhstan the huns and the great migration — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§45–46 The Hunnu in written sources" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chinese chronicles record the Hunnu empire pressing on China's wall.

**2.** Tell the story of "§47 Hunnu economy, social and state order" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The shanyu rules a confederation of herder-warriors from a mobile court.
