# Kazakhstan the huns and the great migration — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§45–46 The Hunnu in written sources" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chinese chronicles record the Hunnu empire pressing on China's wall.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§47 Hunnu economy, social and state order" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The shanyu rules a confederation of herder-warriors from a mobile court.
