# Ancient civilisations greece crete — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§21 Where Greek civilisation begins" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Crete and Mycenae raise palaces and legends that mainland Greece inherits.

**2.** Tell the story of "§21 First centres of Greek civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Crete's palaces and Mycenae's warriors open the Greek story.
