# Ancient civilisations greece crete — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§21 Where Greek civilisation begins" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Crete and Mycenae raise palaces and legends that mainland Greece inherits.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§21 First centres of Greek civilisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Crete's palaces and Mycenae's warriors open the Greek story.
