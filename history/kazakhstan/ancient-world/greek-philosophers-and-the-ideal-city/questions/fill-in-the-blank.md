# Greek philosophers and the ideal city — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 How Greek philosophers imagined the ideal society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Plato and others draw perfect cities on paper to judge the flawed ones they live in.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 History and philosophy of ancient Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Herodotus writes the first history while philosophers ask how to live and rule.
