# Greek philosophers and the ideal city — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§25 How Greek philosophers imagined the ideal society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Plato and others draw perfect cities on paper to judge the flawed ones they live in.

**2.** Tell the story of "§25 History and philosophy of ancient Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Herodotus writes the first history while philosophers ask how to live and rule.
