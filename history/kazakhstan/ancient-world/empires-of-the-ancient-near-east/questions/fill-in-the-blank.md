# Empires of the ancient near east — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 Why the ancient empires of the Near and Middle East were mighty" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Assyrian and Persian kings bind conquered peoples with armies, roads and tribute.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 Empires of South-West Asia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Assyria and Persia swallow the Near East into the first great empires.
