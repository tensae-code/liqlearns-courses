# Empires of the ancient near east — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§12 Why the ancient empires of the Near and Middle East were mighty" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Assyrian and Persian kings bind conquered peoples with armies, roads and tribute.

**2.** Tell the story of "§12 Empires of South-West Asia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Assyria and Persia swallow the Near East into the first great empires.
