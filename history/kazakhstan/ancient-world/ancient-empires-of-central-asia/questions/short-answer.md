# Ancient empires of central asia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§13 How mighty were Central Asia's ancient empires" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** On Kazakhstan's own steppe frontier, Central Asian powers rise on horses and trade routes.

**2.** Tell the story of "§13 Ancient states of Central Asia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bactria, Sogdiana and Khwarezm flourish on Kazakhstan's southern doorstep.
