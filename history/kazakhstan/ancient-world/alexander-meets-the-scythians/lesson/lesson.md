# Alexander meets the scythians

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Alexander meets the scythians" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — §24 Why Alexander could not conquer the Scythians

Alexander chases steppe horsemen who refuse pitched battle and slip away unconquered.

## Sub lesson 2 — 3.8 The Sakas' struggle against Alexander the Great's army

Saka horsemen harry Alexander on the Jaxartes and he cannot hold the steppe.

## Sub lesson 3 — §42 Alexander the Great's campaign against the Sakas

Alexander wins a river battle yet cannot conquer the free steppe tribes.

## Sub lesson 4 — 3.8 The Sakas' struggle against Alexander's army (lesson plans)

The Jaxartes fighting is taught from both conqueror's and defenders' sides.

## Sub lesson 5 — §24 Why Alexander could not conquer the Scythians (lesson plan)

The steppe campaign is taught as the limit of Alexander's power.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Alexander meets the scythians as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
