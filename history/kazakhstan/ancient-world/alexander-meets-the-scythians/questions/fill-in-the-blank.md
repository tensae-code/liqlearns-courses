# Alexander meets the scythians — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24 Why Alexander could not conquer the Scythians" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alexander chases steppe horsemen who refuse pitched battle and slip away unconquered.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "3.8 The Sakas' struggle against Alexander the Great's army" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Saka horsemen harry Alexander on the Jaxartes and he cannot hold the steppe.
