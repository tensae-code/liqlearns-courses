# Alexander meets the scythians — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§24 Why Alexander could not conquer the Scythians" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alexander chases steppe horsemen who refuse pitched battle and slip away unconquered.

**2.** Tell the story of "3.8 The Sakas' struggle against Alexander the Great's army" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Saka horsemen harry Alexander on the Jaxartes and he cannot hold the steppe.
