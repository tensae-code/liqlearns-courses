Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§23 How Sparta differed from Athens" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Two sister cities raise children in opposite ways — barracks discipline against debate and trade.

**2.** Tell the story of "§23 Socio-political order and economy of ancient Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Athens votes and trades while Sparta drills — two orders in one Greece.
