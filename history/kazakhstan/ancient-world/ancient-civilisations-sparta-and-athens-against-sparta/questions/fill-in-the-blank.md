# Ancient civilisations sparta and athens against sparta — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23 How Sparta differed from Athens" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Two sister cities raise children in opposite ways — barracks discipline against debate and trade.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23 Socio-political order and economy of ancient Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Athens votes and trades while Sparta drills — two orders in one Greece.
