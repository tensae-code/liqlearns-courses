# Trade routes that linked the old world — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§52 The ancient Silk Road route" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Caravans cross Kazakhstan linking China to the West with silk, gold and ideas.

**2.** Tell the story of "§22 The Great Silk Road's role in international relations, economy and culture" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Caravan cities grow rich and diplomacy follows the silk caravans.
