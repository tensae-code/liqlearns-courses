# Trade routes that linked the old world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§52 The ancient Silk Road route" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Caravans cross Kazakhstan linking China to the West with silk, gold and ideas.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§22 The Great Silk Road's role in international relations, economy and culture" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Caravan cities grow rich and diplomacy follows the silk caravans.
