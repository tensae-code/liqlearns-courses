# Kazakhstan usun and kangly — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§36–37 History of the Usuns and their economy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Usuns build a state in the Ili valley and grow rich between China and the steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of "§38 Material culture of the Usuns" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Usun pottery, tools and dwellings show a settled-herder civilisation.
