# Kazakhstan usun and kangly — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§36–37 History of the Usuns and their economy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Usuns build a state in the Ili valley and grow rich between China and the steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§38 Material culture of the Usuns" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Usun pottery, tools and dwellings show a settled-herder civilisation.
