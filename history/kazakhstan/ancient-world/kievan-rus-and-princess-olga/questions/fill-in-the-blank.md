# Kievan rus and princess olga — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4 Kievan Rus' forms; Princess Olga visits Constantinople" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Rus' princes build a state and Olga's embassy baptises its diplomacy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4 The formation of Kievan Rus'" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Varangian princes and Slav towns grow into the Rus' state.
