# Kievan rus and princess olga — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§4 Kievan Rus' forms; Princess Olga visits Constantinople" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Rus' princes build a state and Olga's embassy baptises its diplomacy.

**2.** Tell the story of "§4 The formation of Kievan Rus'" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Varangian princes and Slav towns grow into the Rus' state.
