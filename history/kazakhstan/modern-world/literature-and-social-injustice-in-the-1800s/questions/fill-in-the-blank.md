# Literature and social injustice in the 1800s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§28 The idea of social injustice in 19th-century literature" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Novelists put factory children, debtors and the poor at the centre of the story and indict their societies.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§11.1 How did the movements of art and the literature of the 19th century depict social injustice?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Painters and novelists make the poor visible and put their societies on trial.
