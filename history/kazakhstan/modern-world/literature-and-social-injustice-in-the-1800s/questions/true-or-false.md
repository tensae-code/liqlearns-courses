# Literature and social injustice in the 1800s — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§28 The idea of social injustice in 19th-century literature", novelists put factory children, debtors and the poor at the centre of the story and indict their societies (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§11.1 How did the movements of art and the literature of the 19th century depict social injustice?", painters and novelists make the poor visible and put their societies on trial (Sub lesson 2)
