# China the boxer uprising — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§16 China at the end of the 19th century; the Yihetuan (Boxer) uprising" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Boxers rise against foreign domination and eight foreign armies march on Beijing to crush them.
