# China guomindang civil war and the united front — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§13 The work of the Guomindang; the Civil War; Chiang Kai-shek's government policy; the problem of the united front" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chiang Kai-shek's Guomindang turns on the Communists, civil war follows, and Japanese attack forces the rivals back into a united front.

**2.** Tell the story of "§13 The Guomindang's work; the Civil War; Chiang Kai-shek's policy; the united-front problem" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chiang turns on the Communists; Japan's attack forces a second united front.
