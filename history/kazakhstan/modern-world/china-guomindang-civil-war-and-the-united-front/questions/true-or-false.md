# China guomindang civil war and the united front — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§13 The work of the Guomindang; the Civil War; Chiang Kai-shek's government policy; the problem of the united front", chiang Kai-shek's Guomindang turns on the Communists, civil war follows, and Japanese attack forces the rivals back into a united front (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§13 The Guomindang's work; the Civil War; Chiang Kai-shek's policy; the united-front problem", chiang turns on the Communists; Japan's attack forces a second united front (Sub lesson 2)
