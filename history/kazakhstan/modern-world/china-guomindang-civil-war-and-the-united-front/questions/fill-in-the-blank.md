# China guomindang civil war and the united front — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§13 The work of the Guomindang; the Civil War; Chiang Kai-shek's government policy; the problem of the united front" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chiang Kai-shek's Guomindang turns on the Communists, civil war follows, and Japanese attack forces the rivals back into a united front.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§13 The Guomindang's work; the Civil War; Chiang Kai-shek's policy; the united-front problem" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chiang turns on the Communists; Japan's attack forces a second united front.
