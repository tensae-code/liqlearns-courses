# Kazakhstan syrym datuly uprising — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§19–20 The national-liberation movement of 1783–1797 led by Syrym Datuly" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Syrym Datuly leads the Junior Zhuz against both the tsar's officials and a puppet khan, and Igelstrom's reform meant to abolish khan power collapses.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§21–22 The national-liberation movement led by Syrym Datov" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Syrym Datuly's 14-year war in the Junior Zhuz fights tsar and puppet khan alike.
