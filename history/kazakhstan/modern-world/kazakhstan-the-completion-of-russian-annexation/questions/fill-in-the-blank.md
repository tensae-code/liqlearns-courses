# Kazakhstan the completion of russian annexation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§29–30 The completion of Kazakhstan's joining to the Russian Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Russian columns take Turkestan, Shymkent and Tashkent's approaches, joining the Orenburg and Siberian lines and ending the annexation.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§31–32 The joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cherniaev's columns take Shymkent and Aulie-Ata and the south is annexed.
