# Kazakhstan the completion of russian annexation — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§§29–30 The completion of Kazakhstan's joining to the Russian Empire", russian columns take Turkestan, Shymkent and Tashkent's approaches, joining the Orenburg and Siberian lines and ending the annexation (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§§31–32 The joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire", cherniaev's columns take Shymkent and Aulie-Ata and the south is annexed (Sub lesson 2)
