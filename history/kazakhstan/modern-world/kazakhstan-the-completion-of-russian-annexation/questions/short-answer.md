# Kazakhstan the completion of russian annexation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§29–30 The completion of Kazakhstan's joining to the Russian Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Russian columns take Turkestan, Shymkent and Tashkent's approaches, joining the Orenburg and Siberian lines and ending the annexation.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§31–32 The joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cherniaev's columns take Shymkent and Aulie-Ata and the south is annexed.
