# The reformation luther and protestantism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§27–28 Causes of the Reformation; Luther and Müntzer" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Luther's theses split Western Christianity and Müntzer radicalises the peasants.
