# The reformation luther and protestantism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§27–28 Causes of the Reformation; Luther and Müntzer" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Luther's theses split Western Christianity and Müntzer radicalises the peasants.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§28 The Reformation's causes and its spread in Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Indulgences and corruption push Luther's protest across Germany and beyond.
