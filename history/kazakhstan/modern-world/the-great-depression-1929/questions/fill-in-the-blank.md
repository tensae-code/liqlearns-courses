# The great depression 1929 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§17 US economic and political development in the Prosperity period; the world economic crisis and its consequences" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** America's roaring prosperity ends in the 1929 crash and the slump spreads across the capitalist world.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§19 Ways out of the world economic crisis in Germany, France and Japan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Three countries escape the slump by three roads — rearmament in Germany and Japan, political turmoil in France.
