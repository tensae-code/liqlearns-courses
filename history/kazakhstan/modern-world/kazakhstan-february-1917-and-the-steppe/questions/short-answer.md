# Kazakhstan february 1917 and the steppe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§4–5 The February bourgeois-democratic revolution of 1917 in Russia and its influence on Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Tsar falls, and in the steppe Kazakh congresses rush to claim the freedom Petrograd has proclaimed.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§10–11 Kazakhstan in the period of the 1917 revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two revolutions in one year reach the steppe and every camp races to organise.
