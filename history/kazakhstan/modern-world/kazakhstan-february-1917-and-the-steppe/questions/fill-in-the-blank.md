# Kazakhstan february 1917 and the steppe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§4–5 The February bourgeois-democratic revolution of 1917 in Russia and its influence on Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Tsar falls, and in the steppe Kazakh congresses rush to claim the freedom Petrograd has proclaimed.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§10–11 Kazakhstan in the period of the 1917 revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two revolutions in one year reach the steppe and every camp races to organise.
