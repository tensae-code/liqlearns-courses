# Kazakh khanate founding and first khans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§48–49 Creation of the unified Kazakh state: Khans Kerey and Janibek" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kerey and Janibek lead their people away from Abulkhair and found the Kazakh Khanate.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§50 Kasym Khan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kasym unites a million Kazakhs and his 'bright road' of laws orders the khanate.
