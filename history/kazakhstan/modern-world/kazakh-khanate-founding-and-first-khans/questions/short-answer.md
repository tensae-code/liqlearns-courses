# Kazakh khanate founding and first khans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§48–49 Creation of the unified Kazakh state: Khans Kerey and Janibek" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kerey and Janibek lead their people away from Abulkhair and found the Kazakh Khanate.

**2.** Tell the story of "§50 Kasym Khan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kasym unites a million Kazakhs and his 'bright road' of laws orders the khanate.
