# Kazakhstan the reforms of 1867 1868 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§§31–32 Administrative-territorial reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century", the 1867–1868 statutes carve the steppe into oblasts and uezds under governor-generals, finishing the colonial administration (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§§33–34 The tsarist reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century", the 1867–1868 statutes impose oblasts, uezds and the kibitka tax (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
