# Kazakhstan the reforms of 1867 1868 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§31–32 Administrative-territorial reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 1867–1868 statutes carve the steppe into oblasts and uezds under governor-generals, finishing the colonial administration.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§33–34 The tsarist reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 1867–1868 statutes impose oblasts, uezds and the kibitka tax.
