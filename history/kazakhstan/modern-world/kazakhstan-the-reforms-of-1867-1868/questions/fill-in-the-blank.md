# Kazakhstan the reforms of 1867 1868 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§31–32 Administrative-territorial reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 1867–1868 statutes carve the steppe into oblasts and uezds under governor-generals, finishing the colonial administration.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§33–34 The tsarist reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 1867–1868 statutes impose oblasts, uezds and the kibitka tax.
