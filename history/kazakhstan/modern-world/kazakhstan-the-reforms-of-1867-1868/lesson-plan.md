## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§31–32 Administrative-territorial reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century** — the 1867–1868 statutes carve the steppe into oblasts and uezds under governor-generals, finishing the colonial administration
2. **§§33–34 The tsarist reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century** — the 1867–1868 statutes impose oblasts, uezds and the kibitka tax
3. **§42 Administrative-territorial reforms of the 1880s–90s** — the Steppe and Turkestan statutes finish the colonial map
4. **§5.1 Was the tsarist government's "divide and rule" principle fully realised?** — the 1867–1868 map is tested against its own slogan
5. **§5.4 Why was the "New Regulation" of 1886–1891 adopted?** — the Steppe and Turkestan statutes tighten the colonial grip a generation on
6. **Lesson plans §§42–44: the administrative-territorial reforms of the 1880s–90s; trade; social-political thought** — the late-century statutes and ideas are taught as the empire's second tightening
