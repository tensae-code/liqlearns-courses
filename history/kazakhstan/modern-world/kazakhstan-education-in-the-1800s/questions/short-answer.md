# Kazakhstan education in the 1800s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§45–46 Education and enlightenment in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mektebs and medreses, then Russian-Kazakh schools, spread literacy — and parents fear Russification of their children.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§53–54 The development of enlightenment and education in Kazakhstan in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From mekteb to Russian-Kazakh schools, schooling spreads slowly.
