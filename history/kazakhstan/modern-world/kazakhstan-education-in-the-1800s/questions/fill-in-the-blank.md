# Kazakhstan education in the 1800s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§45–46 Education and enlightenment in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mektebs and medreses, then Russian-Kazakh schools, spread literacy — and parents fear Russification of their children.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§53–54 The development of enlightenment and education in Kazakhstan in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From mekteb to Russian-Kazakh schools, schooling spreads slowly.
