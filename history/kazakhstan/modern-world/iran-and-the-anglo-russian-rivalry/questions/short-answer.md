# Iran and the anglo russian rivalry — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§12 Iran's position in the 19th century; Anglo-Russian rivalry in Iran" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A weakening Qajar Iran becomes the chessboard of the British-Russian "Great Game".

**2.** Tell the story of "§5.1 How similar were the interests of Russia and Great Britain in Persia (Iran)?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two empires' ambassadors and officers compete over the same Persian chessboard.
