# Iran and the anglo russian rivalry — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 Iran's position in the 19th century; Anglo-Russian rivalry in Iran" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A weakening Qajar Iran becomes the chessboard of the British-Russian "Great Game".

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5.1 How similar were the interests of Russia and Great Britain in Persia (Iran)?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two empires' ambassadors and officers compete over the same Persian chessboard.
