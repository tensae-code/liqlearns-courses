# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan dzungar wars and great calamity

**Course:** History — Kazakhstan · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan dzungar wars and great calamity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The medieval world as inquiry, and the colonial centuries open — verdicts on medieval Kazakhstan, and Kazakhstan in the 18th–19th centuries (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Kazakhstan dzungar wars and great calamity as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The medieval world as inquiry, and the colonial centuries open — verdicts on medieval Kazakhstan, and Kazakhstan in the 18th–19th centuries — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 The Kazakh Khanate's internal and external position in the early 18th century** — a weakened khanate faces Dzungars, Russians and khanates at once
2. **§2 Organising the all-people's resistance to the Dzungar invasion** — the three zhuzes unite their militias against the invader
3. **§3 The years of the Great Calamity — 'Aktaban Shubyryndy'** — the barefoot flight of 1723 leaves the steppe starving and scattered
4. **§4 Organising the united people's militia** — Abylai's generation rebuilds a single Kazakh army
5. **§5 The Battle of Anrakai and its historical significance** — the 1729/30 victory at Anrakai turns the war against the Dzungars
6. **§6 Famous khans, biys and batyrs in the struggle against the Dzungar invasion** — Bogenbai, Kabanbai and Raiymbek become the war's remembered heroes
7. **§§1–2 The Patriotic War against the Dzungar invaders begins** — the khanate rallies as Dzungar armies strike in 1723
8. **§§3–4 Organising the all-people's resistance to the Dzungar invasion** — zhuz militias unite under common command
9. **§§5–6 Kazakh-Dzungar relations and the war's course** — victories at Bulanty and Anrakai break the invasion

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
