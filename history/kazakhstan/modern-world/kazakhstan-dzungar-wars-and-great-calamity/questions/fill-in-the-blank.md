# Kazakhstan dzungar wars and great calamity — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1 The Kazakh Khanate's internal and external position in the early 18th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A weakened khanate faces Dzungars, Russians and khanates at once.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§2 Organising the all-people's resistance to the Dzungar invasion" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The three zhuzes unite their militias against the invader.
