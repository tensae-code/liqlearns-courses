# Kazakhstan demographic change since 1991 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§45–46. Socio-demographic processes in Kazakhstan in the 1990s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Emigration, return of oralmans, a falling then recovering population.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§21 Socio-demographic processes in the first years of independence" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Emigration and oralman return reshape the population.
