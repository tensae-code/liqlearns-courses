# Kazakhstan demographic change since 1991 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§45–46. Socio-demographic processes in Kazakhstan in the 1990s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Emigration, return of oralmans, a falling then recovering population.

**2.** Tell the story of "§21 Socio-demographic processes in the first years of independence" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Emigration and oralman return reshape the population.
