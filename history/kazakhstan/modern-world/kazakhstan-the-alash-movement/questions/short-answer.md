# Kazakhstan the alash movement — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§6–7 The Alash movement and the Kazakh national idea" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bukeikhanov, Baitursynuly and Dulatov build a party and a programme — Alash — to give the Kazakhs their own state.

**2.** Tell the story of "§12 The creation of the "Alash" party" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The July 1917 congress founds a national party with a published programme.
