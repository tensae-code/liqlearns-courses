# Kazakhstan the alash movement — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§6–7 The Alash movement and the Kazakh national idea" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bukeikhanov, Baitursynuly and Dulatov build a party and a programme — Alash — to give the Kazakhs their own state.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 The creation of the "Alash" party" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The July 1917 congress founds a national party with a published programme.
