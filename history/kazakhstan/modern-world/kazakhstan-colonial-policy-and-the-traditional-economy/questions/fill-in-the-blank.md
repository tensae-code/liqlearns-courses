# Kazakhstan colonial policy and the traditional economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lesson plan §37: the colonial policy's effect on the Kazakhs' traditional economy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
