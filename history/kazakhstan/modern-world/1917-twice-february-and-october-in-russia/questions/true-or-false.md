# 1917 twice february and october in russia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§8 The revolutionary events of 1917 in the Russian Empire; Civil War and foreign military intervention", the Tsar falls in February, the Bolsheviks seize power in October, and Russia tears itself apart in civil war as foreign armies intervene (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§8 The revolutionary events of 1917 in the Russian Empire; Civil War and foreign intervention", tsar falls, Bolsheviks rise, civil war and intervention tear Russia apart (Sub lesson 2)
