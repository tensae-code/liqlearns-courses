# 1917 twice february and october in russia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§8 The revolutionary events of 1917 in the Russian Empire; Civil War and foreign military intervention" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Tsar falls in February, the Bolsheviks seize power in October, and Russia tears itself apart in civil war as foreign armies intervene.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§8 The revolutionary events of 1917 in the Russian Empire; Civil War and foreign intervention" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tsar falls, Bolsheviks rise, civil war and intervention tear Russia apart.
