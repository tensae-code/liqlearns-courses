# 1917 twice february and october in russia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§8 The revolutionary events of 1917 in the Russian Empire; Civil War and foreign military intervention" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Tsar falls in February, the Bolsheviks seize power in October, and Russia tears itself apart in civil war as foreign armies intervene.

**2.** Tell the story of "§8 The revolutionary events of 1917 in the Russian Empire; Civil War and foreign intervention" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tsar falls, Bolsheviks rise, civil war and intervention tear Russia apart.
