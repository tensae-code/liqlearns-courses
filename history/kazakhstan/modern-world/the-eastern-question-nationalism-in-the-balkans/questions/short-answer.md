# The eastern question nationalism in the balkans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§14 The Crimean War: causes and consequences" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Russia's drive south collides with Britain and France at Sevastopol, exposing the empire's backwardness.

**2.** Tell the story of "§5.3 Why is the memory of the Crimean War so important for Russia, which lost it?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A lost war becomes a national lesson that forces the Great Reforms.
