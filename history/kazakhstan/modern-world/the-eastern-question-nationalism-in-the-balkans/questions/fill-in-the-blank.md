# The eastern question nationalism in the balkans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§14 The Crimean War: causes and consequences" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Russia's drive south collides with Britain and France at Sevastopol, exposing the empire's backwardness.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5.3 Why is the memory of the Crimean War so important for Russia, which lost it?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
