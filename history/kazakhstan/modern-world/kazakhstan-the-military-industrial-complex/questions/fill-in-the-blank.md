# Kazakhstan the military industrial complex — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4. The building of the military-industrial complex in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Secret plants and test sites make the republic part of the Soviet war machine.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§14. The military-industrial complex in Kazakhstan; the Baikonur cosmodrome" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rockets rise from the steppe — Gagarin's launch site is Kazakh soil leased to a secret programme.
