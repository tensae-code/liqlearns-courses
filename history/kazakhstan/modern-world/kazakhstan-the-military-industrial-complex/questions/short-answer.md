# Kazakhstan the military industrial complex — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§4. The building of the military-industrial complex in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Secret plants and test sites make the republic part of the Soviet war machine.

**2.** Tell the story of "§14. The military-industrial complex in Kazakhstan; the Baikonur cosmodrome" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rockets rise from the steppe — Gagarin's launch site is Kazakh soil leased to a secret programme.
