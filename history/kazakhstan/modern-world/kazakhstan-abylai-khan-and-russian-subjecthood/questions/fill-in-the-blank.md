# Kazakhstan abylai khan and russian subjecthood — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§7–8 Kazakh-Russian relations in the 18th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Embassies and oaths draw the khanate toward the empire.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9 Causes and consequences of Abulkhair's acceptance of Russian subjecthood" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Junior Zhuz khan's 1730 oath opens the door to colonisation.
