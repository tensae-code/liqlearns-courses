# Kazakhstan abylai khan and russian subjecthood — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§7–8 Kazakh-Russian relations in the 18th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Embassies and oaths draw the khanate toward the empire.
