# Japans aggression in china and southeast asia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§15 Japan's aggression in China and Southeast Asia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Japanese armies seize Manchuria, then invade China proper and push into Southeast Asia.

**2.** Tell the story of "§15 Japan's aggression in China and Southeast Asia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
