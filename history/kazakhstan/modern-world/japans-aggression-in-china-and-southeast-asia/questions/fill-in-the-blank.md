# Japans aggression in china and southeast asia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§15 Japan's aggression in China and Southeast Asia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Japanese armies seize Manchuria, then invade China proper and push into Southeast Asia.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§15 Japan's aggression in China and Southeast Asia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
