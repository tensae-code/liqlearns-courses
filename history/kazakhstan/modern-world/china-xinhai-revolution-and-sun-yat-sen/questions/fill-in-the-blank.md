# China xinhai revolution and sun yat sen — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 China's position after the First World War; the Xinhai Revolution; Sun Yat-sen, provisional president of China" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Xinhai Revolution topples the Qing emperor, and Sun Yat-sen becomes provisional president of a republic that warlords soon fragment.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 China's position after the First World War; the Xinhai Revolution; Sun Yat-sen" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Qing fall and Sun's republic fragments among warlords.
