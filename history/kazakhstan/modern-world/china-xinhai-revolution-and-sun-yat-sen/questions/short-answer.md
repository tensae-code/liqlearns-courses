# China xinhai revolution and sun yat sen — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§12 China's position after the First World War; the Xinhai Revolution; Sun Yat-sen, provisional president of China" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Xinhai Revolution topples the Qing emperor, and Sun Yat-sen becomes provisional president of a republic that warlords soon fragment.

**2.** Tell the story of "§12 China's position after the First World War; the Xinhai Revolution; Sun Yat-sen" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Qing fall and Sun's republic fragments among warlords.
