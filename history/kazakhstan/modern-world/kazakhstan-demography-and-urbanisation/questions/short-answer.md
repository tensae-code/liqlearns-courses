# Kazakhstan demography and urbanisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§23. Demographic changes and urbanisation in the "stagnation" years" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cities swell, villages age, Kazakhs become a minority in their own republic.

**2.** Tell the story of "§9 Demographic processes in the stagnation years" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cities swell; Kazakhs are a minority in their own republic.
