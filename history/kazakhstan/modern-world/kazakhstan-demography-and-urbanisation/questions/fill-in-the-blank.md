# Kazakhstan demography and urbanisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23. Demographic changes and urbanisation in the "stagnation" years" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cities swell, villages age, Kazakhs become a minority in their own republic.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9 Demographic processes in the stagnation years" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
