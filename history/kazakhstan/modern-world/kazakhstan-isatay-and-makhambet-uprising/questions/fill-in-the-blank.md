# Kazakhstan isatay and makhambet uprising — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§23–24 The Kazakh uprising of 1836–1838 in the Bukey Horde" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Isatay Taimanov and the poet Makhambet Utemisov rise against Khan Zhangir's land seizures and are crushed at Akbulak.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§25–26 The uprising of the Kazakhs of the Bukey Horde in 1836–1838" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Isatay and Makhambet rise against Zhangir Khan's land grants to his favourites.
