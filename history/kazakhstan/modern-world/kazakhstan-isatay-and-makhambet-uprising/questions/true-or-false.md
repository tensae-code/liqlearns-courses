# Kazakhstan isatay and makhambet uprising — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§§23–24 The Kazakh uprising of 1836–1838 in the Bukey Horde", isatay Taimanov and the poet Makhambet Utemisov rise against Khan Zhangir's land seizures and are crushed at Akbulak (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§§25–26 The uprising of the Kazakhs of the Bukey Horde in 1836–1838", isatay and Makhambet rise against Zhangir Khan's land grants to his favourites (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
