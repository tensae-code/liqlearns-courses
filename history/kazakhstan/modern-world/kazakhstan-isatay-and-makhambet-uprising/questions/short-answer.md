# Kazakhstan isatay and makhambet uprising — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§23–24 The Kazakh uprising of 1836–1838 in the Bukey Horde" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Isatay Taimanov and the poet Makhambet Utemisov rise against Khan Zhangir's land seizures and are crushed at Akbulak.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§25–26 The uprising of the Kazakhs of the Bukey Horde in 1836–1838" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Isatay and Makhambet rise against Zhangir Khan's land grants to his favourites.
