# Kazakhstan uprisings of the 1860s 1870s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§33–34 The liberation struggle of the Kazakhs in the 1860s–1870s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mangystau and other regions rise against the new taxes and land rules and are put down by punitive columns.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§35–36 The liberation struggle in 1860–1870" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mangystau and the steppe districts rise against the new order.
