# Kazakhstan uprisings of the 1860s 1870s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§33–34 The liberation struggle of the Kazakhs in the 1860s–1870s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mangystau and other regions rise against the new taxes and land rules and are put down by punitive columns.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§35–36 The liberation struggle in 1860–1870" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mangystau and the steppe districts rise against the new order.
