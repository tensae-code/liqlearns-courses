# The renaissance its great figures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§25 Art develops in the Renaissance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael remake art around the human figure.

**2.** Tell the story of "9.1 How and why did European art change, 1300–1500?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Perspective and the human body remake painting and sculpture.
