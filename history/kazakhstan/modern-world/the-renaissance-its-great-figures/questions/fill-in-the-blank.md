# The renaissance its great figures — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 Art develops in the Renaissance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael remake art around the human figure.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "9.1 How and why did European art change, 1300–1500?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Perspective and the human body remake painting and sculpture.
