# Kazakhstan abylai khan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§11–12 The domestic and foreign policy of Abylai Khan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Abylai holds the khanate together by balancing Russia and China, freeing captives and refusing to be either empire's pawn.

**2.** Tell the story of "§12 Abylai Khan's rise as a strong political figure" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The captive who charmed the Dzhungar court returns to become the khanate's central figure.
