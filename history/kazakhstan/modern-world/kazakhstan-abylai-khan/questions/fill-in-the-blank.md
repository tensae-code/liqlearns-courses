# Kazakhstan abylai khan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§11–12 The domestic and foreign policy of Abylai Khan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Abylai holds the khanate together by balancing Russia and China, freeing captives and refusing to be either empire's pawn.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 Abylai Khan's rise as a strong political figure" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The captive who charmed the Dzhungar court returns to become the khanate's central figure.
