# Kazakhstan oral art of the 1700s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§13–14 Kazakh oral folk art in the 18th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Zhyrau poets like Bukhar and Umbetai sing counsel to khans and keep the nation's memory in verse.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§15 Historical events reflected in the works of the akyn-zhyraus of the 18th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The zhyrau's verses to the khans are read as chronicles of the Dzhungar wars.
