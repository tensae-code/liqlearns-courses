# Kazakhstan oral art of the 1700s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§13–14 Kazakh oral folk art in the 18th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Zhyrau poets like Bukhar and Umbetai sing counsel to khans and keep the nation's memory in verse.

**2.** Tell the story of "§15 Historical events reflected in the works of the akyn-zhyraus of the 18th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The zhyrau's verses to the khans are read as chronicles of the Dzhungar wars.
