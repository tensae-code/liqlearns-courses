# Kazakhstan the kazakh intelligentsia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§8–9 Outstanding representatives of the Kazakh intelligentsia of the early 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A small generation of teachers, poets and lawyers invents modern Kazakh public life.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§5–6 The Kazakh national intelligentsia and its influence on socio-political processes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A thin layer of graduates becomes the nation's voice.
