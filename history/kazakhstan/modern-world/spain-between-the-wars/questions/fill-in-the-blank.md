# Spain between the wars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24 Spain in the interwar period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Spain swings from monarchy to republic and then into a civil war that becomes Europe's rehearsal for 1939.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24 Spain in the interwar period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Monarchy, republic, civil war — Spain becomes Europe's rehearsal for 1939.
