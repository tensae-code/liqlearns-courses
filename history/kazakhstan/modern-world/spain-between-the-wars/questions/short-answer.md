# Spain between the wars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§24 Spain in the interwar period" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Spain swings from monarchy to republic and then into a civil war that becomes Europe's rehearsal for 1939.

**2.** Tell the story of "§24 Spain in the interwar period" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Monarchy, republic, civil war — Spain becomes Europe's rehearsal for 1939.
