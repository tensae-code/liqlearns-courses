# Kazakhstan battles against the dzhungars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§5–6 The Kazakh-Dzhungar battles" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** United zhuz armies strike back at Bulanty and Anyrakai, and batyrs like Bogenbai and Kabanbai become the people's heroes.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5 The Anyrakai battle and its historical significance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The united Kazakh army crushes the Dzhungars at Anyrakai in 1730 and turns the war.
