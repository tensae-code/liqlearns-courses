# Kazakhstan battles against the dzhungars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§5–6 The Kazakh-Dzhungar battles" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** United zhuz armies strike back at Bulanty and Anyrakai, and batyrs like Bogenbai and Kabanbai become the people's heroes.

**2.** Tell the story of "§5 The Anyrakai battle and its historical significance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The united Kazakh army crushes the Dzhungars at Anyrakai in 1730 and turns the war.
