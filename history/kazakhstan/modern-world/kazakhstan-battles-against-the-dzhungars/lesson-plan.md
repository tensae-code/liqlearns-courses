# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan battles against the dzhungars

**Course:** History — Kazakhstan · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan battles against the dzhungars
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Kazakhstan from the Dzhungar wars to the Great Patriotic War — the 18th–19th-century national books and the 1900–1945 national books of Grade 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Kazakhstan battles against the dzhungars as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Kazakhstan from the Dzhungar wars to the Great Patriotic War — the 18th–19th-century national books and the 1900–1945 national books of Grade 8 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§5–6 The Kazakh-Dzhungar battles** — united zhuz armies strike back at Bulanty and Anyrakai, and batyrs like Bogenbai and Kabanbai become the people's heroes
2. **§5 The Anyrakai battle and its historical significance** — the united Kazakh army crushes the Dzhungars at Anyrakai in 1730 and turns the war
3. **§6 Famous khans, biys and batyrs who distinguished themselves against the Dzhungars** — Abylai the young warrior, Bogenbai, Kabanbai and the biy judges become the war's remembered names
4. **§1.2 Why did Ordabasy become a symbol of national unity?** — at the Ordabasy gathering the three zhuzes bury their quarrels and unite for the war
5. **§1.3 Why is the Anyrakai battle considered the turning point of the Kazakh-Dzhungar war?** — pupils weigh the 1730 victory's claim to be the war's hinge
6. **Lesson plans §§3–6: organising the resistance; the people's militia; Anyrakai; the heroes** — Karakum and Ordabasy gatherings, Bulanty/"Kalmak Kyrylgan" and Anyrakai are sequenced into one story of unity winning

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
