# The industrial revolution what changed and why england first — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§3 The industrial revolution and the social structure of industrial society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Steam and factories pull workers into cities and split society into bourgeoisie and proletariat.

**2.** Tell the story of "§1.1 How revolutionary was the industrial breakthrough?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils judge for themselves whether steam, factories and railways deserve the name "revolution".
