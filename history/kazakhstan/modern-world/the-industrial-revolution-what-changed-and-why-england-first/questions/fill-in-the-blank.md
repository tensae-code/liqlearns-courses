# The industrial revolution what changed and why england first — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3 The industrial revolution and the social structure of industrial society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Steam and factories pull workers into cities and split society into bourgeoisie and proletariat.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1.1 How revolutionary was the industrial breakthrough?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils judge for themselves whether steam, factories and railways deserve the name "revolution".
