# Kazakhstan shokan ualikhanov — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§47–48 Shokan Ualikhanov — the great Kazakh scholar and enlightener" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The officer-scholar maps Kashgaria, records the Manas epic and dies at 29, leaving a nation's science begun.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§56 Shokan Ualikhanov — great Kazakh scholar" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Shokan's Kashgar journey and folklore collecting found Kazakh science.
