# Kazakhstan shokan ualikhanov — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§47–48 Shokan Ualikhanov — the great Kazakh scholar and enlightener" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The officer-scholar maps Kashgaria, records the Manas epic and dies at 29, leaving a nation's science begun.

**2.** Tell the story of "§56 Shokan Ualikhanov — great Kazakh scholar" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Shokan's Kashgar journey and folklore collecting found Kazakh science.
