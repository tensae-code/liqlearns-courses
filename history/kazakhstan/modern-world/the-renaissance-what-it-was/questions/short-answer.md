# The renaissance what it was — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§25 Culture of the Renaissance epoch" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Italian cities revive antiquity and invent a new art and learning.

**2.** Tell the story of "§26 Humanist ideas' influence on medieval culture" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Humanists put man at the centre and criticise the old order.
