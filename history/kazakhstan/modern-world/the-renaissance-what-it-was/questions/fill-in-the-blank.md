# The renaissance what it was — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 Culture of the Renaissance epoch" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Italian cities revive antiquity and invent a new art and learning.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 Humanist ideas' influence on medieval culture" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Humanists put man at the centre and criticise the old order.
