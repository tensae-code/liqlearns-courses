# The american civil war union against slavery — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§25 The Civil War of 1861–1865 and the Reconstruction period in the USA" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Union survives a war over slavery and then struggles to rebuild the South.

**2.** Tell the story of "§10.2 How was slavery abolished in the USA?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lincoln's emancipation turns a war for union into a war that ends slavery.
