# The american civil war union against slavery — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 The Civil War of 1861–1865 and the Reconstruction period in the USA" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Union survives a war over slavery and then struggles to rebuild the South.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10.2 How was slavery abolished in the USA?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lincoln's emancipation turns a war for union into a war that ends slavery.
