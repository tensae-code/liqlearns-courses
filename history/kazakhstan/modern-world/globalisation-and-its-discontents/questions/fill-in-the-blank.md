# Globalisation and its discontents — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 The essence of globalisation; the threat of terrorism and extremism in the modern world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** One world market — and the backlash of terror against it.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 The problem of preserving national identity under globalisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
