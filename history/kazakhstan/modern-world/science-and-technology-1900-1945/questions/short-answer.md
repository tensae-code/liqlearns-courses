# Science and technology 1900 1945 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§30 The most important discoveries and achievements in natural science, the social sciences, technology and production in the first half of the 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Relativity, quanta, aviation, antibiotics and mass production change what humans know and can do.

**2.** Tell the story of "§30 The most important discoveries and achievements of science, technology and production in the first half of the 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
