# Kazakh khanate economy and culture — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§58 Spiritual culture of the Kazakhs, 16th–17th c." turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Zhyrau poets and biys' eloquence carry the khanate's memory.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§59 Traditional material culture and applied art" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Yurt furnishings, jewellery and weapons show a mature nomad craft.
