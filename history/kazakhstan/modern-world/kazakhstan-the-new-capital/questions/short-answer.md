# Kazakhstan the new capital — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§54. Our country's capital — a symbol of new Kazakhstan's prosperity" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The capital moves to Akmola/Astana in 1997 — a city rises on the steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of "§26 Astana (Nur-Sultan) — the capital of independent Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The capital moves north in 1997 and a new city rises.
