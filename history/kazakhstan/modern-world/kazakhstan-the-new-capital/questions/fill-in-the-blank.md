# Kazakhstan the new capital — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§54. Our country's capital — a symbol of new Kazakhstan's prosperity" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The capital moves to Akmola/Astana in 1997 — a city rises on the steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 Astana (Nur-Sultan) — the capital of independent Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The capital moves north in 1997 and a new city rises.
