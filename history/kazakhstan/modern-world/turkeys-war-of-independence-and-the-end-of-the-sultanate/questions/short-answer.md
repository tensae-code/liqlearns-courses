# Turkeys war of independence and the end of the sultanate — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§10 The fall of the sultan's regime and the struggle against foreign intervention in the former Ottoman Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The defeated Ottoman Empire is occupied and partitioned, and Turkish patriots take up arms rather than accept the carve-up.

**2.** Tell the story of "§10 The fall of the sultan's regime and the struggle against foreign intervention in the former Ottoman Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Occupied and partitioned, Turkish patriots refuse the carve-up and take up arms.
