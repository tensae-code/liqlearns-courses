Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§10 The fall of the sultan's regime and the struggle against foreign intervention in the former Ottoman Empire", the defeated Ottoman Empire is occupied and partitioned, and Turkish patriots take up arms rather than accept the carve-up (Sub lesson 1)
