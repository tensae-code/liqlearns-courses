# Turkeys war of independence and the end of the sultanate — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10 The fall of the sultan's regime and the struggle against foreign intervention in the former Ottoman Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The defeated Ottoman Empire is occupied and partitioned, and Turkish patriots take up arms rather than accept the carve-up.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10 The fall of the sultan's regime and the struggle against foreign intervention in the former Ottoman Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Occupied and partitioned, Turkish patriots refuse the carve-up and take up arms.
