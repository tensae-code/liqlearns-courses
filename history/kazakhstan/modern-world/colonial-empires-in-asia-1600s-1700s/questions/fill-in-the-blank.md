# Colonial empires in asia 1600s 1700s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5 The colonial policy of the European powers and the countries of the East" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Trading companies turn footholds in Asia into territorial power over older eastern states.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§2.1 Why did the British turn from traders into rulers of India?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A trading company wins Plassey, collects Bengal's taxes and ends up governing India.
