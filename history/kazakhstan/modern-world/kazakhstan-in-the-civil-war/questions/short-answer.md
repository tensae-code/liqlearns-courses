# Kazakhstan in the civil war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§10–11 Kazakhstan in the years of civil confrontation, 1917–1920s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Reds, Whites, Alash Orda troops and peasant revolts fight over the steppe as fronts roll through its towns.

**2.** Tell the story of "§15 The process of establishing Soviet power in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Soviets seize the towns while the steppe waits and watches.
