# Kazakhstan in the civil war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§10–11 Kazakhstan in the years of civil confrontation, 1917–1920s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Reds, Whites, Alash Orda troops and peasant revolts fight over the steppe as fronts roll through its towns.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§15 The process of establishing Soviet power in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Soviets seize the towns while the steppe waits and watches.
