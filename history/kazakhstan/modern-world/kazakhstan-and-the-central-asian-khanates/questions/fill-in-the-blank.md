# Kazakhstan and the central asian khanates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§27–28 Kazakh relations with the Central Asian states in the 1840s–1860s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kokand and Khiva tax and raid the southern Kazakhs while Russia advances from the north — a people caught between two colonisers.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§29–30 Kazakh uprisings against the invasions of the Central Asian khans" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The south resists Kokand's and Khiva's tax collectors and fortress-builders too.
