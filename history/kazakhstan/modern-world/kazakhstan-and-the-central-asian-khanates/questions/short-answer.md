# Kazakhstan and the central asian khanates — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§27–28 Kazakh relations with the Central Asian states in the 1840s–1860s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kokand and Khiva tax and raid the southern Kazakhs while Russia advances from the north — a people caught between two colonisers.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§29–30 Kazakh uprisings against the invasions of the Central Asian khans" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The south resists Kokand's and Khiva's tax collectors and fortress-builders too.
