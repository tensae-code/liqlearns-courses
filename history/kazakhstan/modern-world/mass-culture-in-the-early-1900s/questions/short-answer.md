# Mass culture in the early 1900s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§29 Features of the development of culture; mass culture; the changing role of religion in society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cinema, radio and the press create a mass culture while churches lose their old grip on public life.

**2.** Tell the story of "§29 Features of culture's development; mass culture; religion's changing role" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
