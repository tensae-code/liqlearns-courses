# Mass culture in the early 1900s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§29 Features of the development of culture; mass culture; the changing role of religion in society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cinema, radio and the press create a mass culture while churches lose their old grip on public life.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§29 Features of culture's development; mass culture; religion's changing role" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
