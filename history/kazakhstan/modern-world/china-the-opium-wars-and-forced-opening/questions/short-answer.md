# China the opium wars and forced opening — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§15 The isolation policy of the Qing Empire; the "Opium Wars"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Britain forces China's doors open with opium and gunboats, and the unequal treaties begin.

**2.** Tell the story of "§6.1 What can we learn about imperialism from the correspondence of the English and Chinese governments?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The two empires' letters are read against each other — politeness on one side, ultimatums on the other.
