# China the opium wars and forced opening — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§15 The isolation policy of the Qing Empire; the "Opium Wars"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Britain forces China's doors open with opium and gunboats, and the unequal treaties begin.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§6.1 What can we learn about imperialism from the correspondence of the English and Chinese governments?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The two empires' letters are read against each other — politeness on one side, ultimatums on the other.
