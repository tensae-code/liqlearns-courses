# American peoples and first colonial empires — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24 American peoples in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Aztec and Inca worlds meet conquistadors and colonial empire begins.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 Peoples of America in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Aztec and Inca civilisations fall to small bands of conquistadors.
