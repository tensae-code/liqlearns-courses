# American peoples and first colonial empires — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§24 American peoples in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Aztec and Inca worlds meet conquistadors and colonial empire begins.

**2.** Tell the story of "§26 Peoples of America in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Aztec and Inca civilisations fall to small bands of conquistadors.
