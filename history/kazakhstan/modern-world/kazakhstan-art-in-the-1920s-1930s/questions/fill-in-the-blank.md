# Kazakhstan art in the 1920s 1930s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§27–28 The development of Kazakh art in the 1920s–30s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kasteev's canvases, the first Kazakh theatre and opera give the republic a professional art of its own.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§49 The art of Soviet Kazakhstan in the 1920s–30s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kasteev paints the steppe's new life; theatre and cinema are born.
