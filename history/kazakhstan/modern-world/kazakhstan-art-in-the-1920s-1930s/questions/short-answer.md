# Kazakhstan art in the 1920s 1930s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§27–28 The development of Kazakh art in the 1920s–30s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kasteev's canvases, the first Kazakh theatre and opera give the republic a professional art of its own.

**2.** Tell the story of "§49 The art of Soviet Kazakhstan in the 1920s–30s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kasteev paints the steppe's new life; theatre and cinema are born.
