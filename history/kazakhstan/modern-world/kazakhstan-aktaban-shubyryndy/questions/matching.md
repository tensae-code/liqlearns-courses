# Kazakhstan aktaban shubyryndy — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** §§3–4 Organising a united people's militia; the "Years of Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy" — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §3 The years of "Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy" — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) in 1723 the Dzhungar onslaught drives whole peoples fleeing barefoot across the steppe — the Aktaban Shubyryndy, remembered in song as the nation's great calamity
b) the 1723 invasion drives the Kazakh clans fleeing west in hunger and disorder

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
