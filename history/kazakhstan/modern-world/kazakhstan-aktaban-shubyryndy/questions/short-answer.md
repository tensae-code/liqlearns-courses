# Kazakhstan aktaban shubyryndy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§3–4 Organising a united people's militia; the "Years of Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 1723 the Dzhungar onslaught drives whole peoples fleeing barefoot across the steppe — the Aktaban Shubyryndy, remembered in song as the nation's great calamity.

**2.** Tell the story of "§3 The years of "Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 1723 invasion drives the Kazakh clans fleeing west in hunger and disorder.
