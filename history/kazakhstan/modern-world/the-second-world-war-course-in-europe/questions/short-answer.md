# The second world war course in europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§26 The start of the Second World War; Germany's occupation of the countries of Western Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Poland is crushed, then blitzkrieg overruns Denmark, Norway, the Low Countries and France.

**2.** Tell the story of "§27 The entry of the USA into the war; the problem of opening the second front" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pearl Harbor brings America in, and the Allies argue for two years over when and where to open the second front in Europe.
