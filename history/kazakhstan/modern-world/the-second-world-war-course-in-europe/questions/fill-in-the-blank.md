# The second world war course in europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 The start of the Second World War; Germany's occupation of the countries of Western Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Poland is crushed, then blitzkrieg overruns Denmark, Norway, the Low Countries and France.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§27 The entry of the USA into the war; the problem of opening the second front" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pearl Harbor brings America in, and the Allies argue for two years over when and where to open the second front in Europe.
