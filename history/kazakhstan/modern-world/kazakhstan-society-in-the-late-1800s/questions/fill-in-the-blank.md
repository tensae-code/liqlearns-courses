# Kazakhstan society in the late 1800s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§41–42 Kazakh society in the second half of the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sultans lose their privileges, biys judge under Russian oversight, and new classes of workers and traders appear.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§39 Changes in traditional Kazakh society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Clan and zhuz ties loosen as money, towns and Russian law reshape society.
