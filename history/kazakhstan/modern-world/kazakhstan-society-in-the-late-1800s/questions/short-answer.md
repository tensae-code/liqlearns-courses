# Kazakhstan society in the late 1800s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§41–42 Kazakh society in the second half of the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sultans lose their privileges, biys judge under Russian oversight, and new classes of workers and traders appear.

**2.** Tell the story of "§39 Changes in traditional Kazakh society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Clan and zhuz ties loosen as money, towns and Russian law reshape society.
