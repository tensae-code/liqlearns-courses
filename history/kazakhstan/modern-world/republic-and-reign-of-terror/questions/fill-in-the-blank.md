# Republic and reign of terror — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9 The main stages of the French Revolution of the 18th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From constitutional monarchy the revolution radicalises into republic, war and the Terror.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4.1 Why did David and Baudry judge Marat differently?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two painters look at the murdered revolutionary Marat and produce opposite verdicts — art used as evidence.
