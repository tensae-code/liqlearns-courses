# Republic and reign of terror — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§9 The main stages of the French Revolution of the 18th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From constitutional monarchy the revolution radicalises into republic, war and the Terror.

**2.** Tell the story of "§4.1 Why did David and Baudry judge Marat differently?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two painters look at the murdered revolutionary Marat and produce opposite verdicts — art used as evidence.
