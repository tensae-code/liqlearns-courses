# India the sepoy uprising of 1857 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§21 The Sepoy uprising in India" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greased-cartridge rumours ignite a vast revolt of Indian soldiers and princes against the East India Company.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9.1 How are the events in India of 1857–1859 told about?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** British and Indian tellings of the same uprising are compared as rival stories.
