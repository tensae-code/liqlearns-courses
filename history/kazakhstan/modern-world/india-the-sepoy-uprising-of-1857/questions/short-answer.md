# India the sepoy uprising of 1857 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§21 The Sepoy uprising in India" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greased-cartridge rumours ignite a vast revolt of Indian soldiers and princes against the East India Company.

**2.** Tell the story of "§9.1 How are the events in India of 1857–1859 told about?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** British and Indian tellings of the same uprising are compared as rival stories.
