# Nep and the founding of the ussr — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9 From war communism to the New Economic Policy (NEP); the formation of the USSR" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lenin retreats from war communism to the NEP's mixed economy, and the Soviet republics are bound into one USSR in 1922.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9 From war communism to the NEP; the formation of the USSR" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lenin's retreat to a mixed economy, then the republics are bound into the USSR.
