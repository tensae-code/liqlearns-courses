# Nep and the founding of the ussr — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§9 From war communism to the New Economic Policy (NEP); the formation of the USSR" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lenin retreats from war communism to the NEP's mixed economy, and the Soviet republics are bound into one USSR in 1922.

**2.** Tell the story of "§9 From war communism to the NEP; the formation of the USSR" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lenin's retreat to a mixed economy, then the republics are bound into the USSR.
