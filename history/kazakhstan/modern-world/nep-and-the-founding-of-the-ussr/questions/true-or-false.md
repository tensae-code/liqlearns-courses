Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§9 From war communism to the New Economic Policy (NEP); the formation of the USSR", lenin retreats from war communism to the NEP's mixed economy, and the Soviet republics are bound into one USSR in 1922 (Sub lesson 1)
