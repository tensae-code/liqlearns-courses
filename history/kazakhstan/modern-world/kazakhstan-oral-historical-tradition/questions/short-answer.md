# Kazakhstan oral historical tradition — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§53–54 The development of the Kazakhs' oral historical tradition at the end of the 19th and start of the 20th centuries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Shezhire genealogies and epic singers hand the past down by memory as print begins to take over.

**2.** Tell the story of "§48 Oral folk art in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Epic singers carry Koblandy and Er Targyn to new audiences.
