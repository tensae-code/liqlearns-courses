# Kazakhstan oral historical tradition — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§53–54 The development of the Kazakhs' oral historical tradition at the end of the 19th and start of the 20th centuries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Shezhire genealogies and epic singers hand the past down by memory as print begins to take over.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§48 Oral folk art in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Epic singers carry Koblandy and Er Targyn to new audiences.
