# Kazakhstan shakarim and the historian poets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "§60 The historical value of the works of Shakarim Kudaiberdiev, Mashkhur Zhusip Kopeev and Kurbangali Khalidi"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) three scholar-poets write the Kazakh past down — genealogy, chronicle and history — before it can be forgotten  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
