# Kazakhstan shakarim and the historian poets — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§60 The historical value of the works of Shakarim Kudaiberdiev, Mashkhur Zhusip Kopeev and Kurbangali Khalidi" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Three scholar-poets write the Kazakh past down — genealogy, chronicle and history — before it can be forgotten.

**2.** Tell the story of "§6.7 What is the historical value of the works of Sh. Kudaiberduly, M. Zh. Kopeiuly and K. Khalid?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Three shezhire-writers are assessed as historians of their own people.
