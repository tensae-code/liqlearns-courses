# Kazakhstan shakarim and the historian poets — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** §60 The historical value of the works of Shakarim Kudaiberdiev, Mashkhur Zhusip Kopeev and Kurbangali Khalidi — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §6.7 What is the historical value of the works of Sh. Kudaiberduly, M. Zh. Kopeiuly and K. Khalid? — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) three scholar-poets write the Kazakh past down — genealogy, chronicle and history — before it can be forgotten
b) three shezhire-writers are assessed as historians of their own people

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
