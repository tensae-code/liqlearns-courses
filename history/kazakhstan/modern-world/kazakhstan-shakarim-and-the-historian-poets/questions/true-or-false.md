# Kazakhstan shakarim and the historian poets — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§60 The historical value of the works of Shakarim Kudaiberdiev, Mashkhur Zhusip Kopeev and Kurbangali Khalidi", three scholar-poets write the Kazakh past down — genealogy, chronicle and history — before it can be forgotten (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§6.7 What is the historical value of the works of Sh. Kudaiberduly, M. Zh. Kopeiuly and K. Khalid?", three shezhire-writers are assessed as historians of their own people (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
