# Italy unifies cavour and the patriots — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§18 The unification of Italy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cavour's diplomacy and Garibaldi's redshirts stitch the Italian kingdoms into one state.

**2.** Tell the story of "§7.2 How similar were the paths of unification of Italy and Germany? (Italy)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cavour's diplomacy and Garibaldi's volunteers are set beside Bismarck's blood and iron.
