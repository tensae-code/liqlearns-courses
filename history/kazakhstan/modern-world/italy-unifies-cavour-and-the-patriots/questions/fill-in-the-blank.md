# Italy unifies cavour and the patriots — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§18 The unification of Italy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cavour's diplomacy and Garibaldi's redshirts stitch the Italian kingdoms into one state.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§7.2 How similar were the paths of unification of Italy and Germany? (Italy)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cavour's diplomacy and Garibaldi's volunteers are set beside Bismarck's blood and iron.
