# Kazakhstan the december 1986 events — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§31. The December 1986 events in Kazakhstan: causes, course, consequences" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kunaev is replaced by an outsider; students fill the square; troops clear it — the first crack in the Union.

**2.** Tell the story of "§32. Punitive measures against participants in the December 1986 events" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trials, expulsions and ruined careers follow the square.
