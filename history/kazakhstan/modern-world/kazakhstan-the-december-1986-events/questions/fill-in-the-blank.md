# Kazakhstan the december 1986 events — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§31. The December 1986 events in Kazakhstan: causes, course, consequences" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kunaev is replaced by an outsider; students fill the square; troops clear it — the first crack in the Union.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§32. Punitive measures against participants in the December 1986 events" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trials, expulsions and ruined careers follow the square.
