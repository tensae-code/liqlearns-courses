# The first world war versailles and the results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§7 The world after the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Victors redraw the map at Versailles while defeated empires collapse and new states appear on their ruins.

**2.** Tell the story of "§7 The world after the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Versailles redraws the map over the ruins of defeated empires.
