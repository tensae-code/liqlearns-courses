# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan the 1916 uprising

**Course:** History — Kazakhstan · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan the 1916 uprising
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Kazakhstan from the Dzhungar wars to the Great Patriotic War — the 18th–19th-century national books and the 1900–1945 national books of Grade 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Kazakhstan the 1916 uprising as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Kazakhstan from the Dzhungar wars to the Great Patriotic War — the 18th–19th-century national books and the 1900–1945 national books of Grade 8 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
