# Kazakhstan the 1916 uprising — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§3 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 in Kazakhstan", the Tsar's decree conscripting Kazakhs for rear labour sparks a steppe-wide revolt under Amangeldy Imanov, drowned in blood and flight to China (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§§8–9 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 in Kazakhstan", the labour-conscription decree ignites the steppe; Amangeldy's army and the Torgai front hold out longest (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
