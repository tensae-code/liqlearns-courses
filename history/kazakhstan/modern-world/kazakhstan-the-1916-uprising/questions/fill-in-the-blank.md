# Kazakhstan the 1916 uprising — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Tsar's decree conscripting Kazakhs for rear labour sparks a steppe-wide revolt under Amangeldy Imanov, drowned in blood and flight to China.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§8–9 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The labour-conscription decree ignites the steppe; Amangeldy's army and the Torgai front hold out longest.
