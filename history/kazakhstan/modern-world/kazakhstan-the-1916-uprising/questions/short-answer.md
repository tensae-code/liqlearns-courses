# Kazakhstan the 1916 uprising — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§3 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Tsar's decree conscripting Kazakhs for rear labour sparks a steppe-wide revolt under Amangeldy Imanov, drowned in blood and flight to China.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§8–9 The national-liberation uprising of 1916 in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The labour-conscription decree ignites the steppe; Amangeldy's army and the Torgai front hold out longest.
