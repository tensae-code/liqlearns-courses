# Kazakhstan kenesary kasymuly uprising — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§25–26 The national-liberation movement of 1837–1847 led by Kenesary Kasymuly" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kenesary, elected khan, fights a ten-year all-nation war to restore the khanate before falling in the Kyrgyz mountains.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§27–28 The national-liberation movement led by Kenesary Kasymov" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kenesary restores the khan's court and taxes, and fights Russia for a decade.
