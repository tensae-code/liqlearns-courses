# Kazakhstan kenesary kasymuly uprising — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§25–26 The national-liberation movement of 1837–1847 led by Kenesary Kasymuly" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kenesary, elected khan, fights a ten-year all-nation war to restore the khanate before falling in the Kyrgyz mountains.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§27–28 The national-liberation movement led by Kenesary Kasymov" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kenesary restores the khan's court and taxes, and fights Russia for a decade.
