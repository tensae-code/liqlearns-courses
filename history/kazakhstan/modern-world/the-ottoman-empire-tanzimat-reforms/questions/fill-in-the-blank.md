# The ottoman empire tanzimat reforms — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§13 The deepening crisis of the Ottoman Empire and the Tanzimat reforms" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sultans try to save a shrinking empire by importing European law, armies and schools.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5.2 Did the Tanzimat policy help to strengthen the Ottoman Empire?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ottoman reform edicts are weighed against the empire's continued shrinking.
