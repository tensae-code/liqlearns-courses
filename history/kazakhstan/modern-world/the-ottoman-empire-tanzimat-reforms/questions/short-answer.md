# The ottoman empire tanzimat reforms — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§13 The deepening crisis of the Ottoman Empire and the Tanzimat reforms" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sultans try to save a shrinking empire by importing European law, armies and schools.

**2.** Tell the story of "§5.2 Did the Tanzimat policy help to strengthen the Ottoman Empire?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ottoman reform edicts are weighed against the empire's continued shrinking.
