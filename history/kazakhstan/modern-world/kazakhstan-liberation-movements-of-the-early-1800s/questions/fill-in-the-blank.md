# Kazakhstan liberation movements of the early 1800s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24 The Kazakh people's national-liberation movement in the first half of the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Revolts flare across the steppe — Zhankozha, Eset and others — as colonisation tightens.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4.6 What was the role of the batyrs Eset and Zhankozha in the national-liberation struggle?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two regional heroes hold the Syr Darya and the Aral steppe against Khiva and Russia.
