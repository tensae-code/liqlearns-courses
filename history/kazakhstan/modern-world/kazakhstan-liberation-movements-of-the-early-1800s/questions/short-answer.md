# Kazakhstan liberation movements of the early 1800s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§24 The Kazakh people's national-liberation movement in the first half of the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Revolts flare across the steppe — Zhankozha, Eset and others — as colonisation tightens.

**2.** Tell the story of "§4.6 What was the role of the batyrs Eset and Zhankozha in the national-liberation struggle?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two regional heroes hold the Syr Darya and the Aral steppe against Khiva and Russia.
