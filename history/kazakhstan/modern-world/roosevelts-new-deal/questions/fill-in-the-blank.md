# Roosevelts new deal — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§18 F. D. Roosevelt's "New Deal"; US foreign policy in the interwar period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
