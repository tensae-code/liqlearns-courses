# Kazakhstan ibrai altynsarin — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§49–50 Ibrai Altynsarin — outstanding enlightener and innovator-teacher" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Altynsarin opens schools for Kazakh children, writes their first textbooks and trains their first teachers.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§57 The innovation of Ibrai Altynsarin" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Altynsarin builds schools, textbooks and a Kazakh alphabet for print.
