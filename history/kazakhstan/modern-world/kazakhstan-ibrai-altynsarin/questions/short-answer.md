# Kazakhstan ibrai altynsarin — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§49–50 Ibrai Altynsarin — outstanding enlightener and innovator-teacher" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Altynsarin opens schools for Kazakh children, writes their first textbooks and trains their first teachers.

**2.** Tell the story of "§57 The innovation of Ibrai Altynsarin" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Altynsarin builds schools, textbooks and a Kazakh alphabet for print.
