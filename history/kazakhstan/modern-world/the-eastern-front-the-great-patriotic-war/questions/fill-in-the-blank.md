# The eastern front the great patriotic war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§28 The Great Patriotic War of the Soviet people against fascist Germany; the results and consequences of the Second World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The USSR bears the main land war from invasion to Berlin, and the book closes the war with its human and political reckoning.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§28 The Great Patriotic War of the Soviet people against fascist Germany; the results and consequences of the Second World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The USSR bears the main land war to Berlin; the reckoning follows.
