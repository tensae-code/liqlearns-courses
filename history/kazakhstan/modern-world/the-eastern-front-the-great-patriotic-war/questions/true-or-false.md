# The eastern front the great patriotic war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§28 The Great Patriotic War of the Soviet people against fascist Germany; the results and consequences of the Second World War", the USSR bears the main land war from invasion to Berlin, and the book closes the war with its human and political reckoning (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§28 The Great Patriotic War of the Soviet people against fascist Germany; the results and consequences of the Second World War", the USSR bears the main land war to Berlin; the reckoning follows (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
