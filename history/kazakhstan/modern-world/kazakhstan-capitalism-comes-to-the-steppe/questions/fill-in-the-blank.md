# Kazakhstan capitalism comes to the steppe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§37–38 The development of capitalist relations in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mines, railways, hired labour and markets pull the steppe into the empire's economy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§40–41 Socio-economic development of Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century; the penetration of capitalist relations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Railways, mines and wage labour reach the steppe.
