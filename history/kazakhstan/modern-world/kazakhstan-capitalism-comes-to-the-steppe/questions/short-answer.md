# Kazakhstan capitalism comes to the steppe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§37–38 The development of capitalist relations in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mines, railways, hired labour and markets pull the steppe into the empire's economy.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§40–41 Socio-economic development of Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century; the penetration of capitalist relations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Railways, mines and wage labour reach the steppe.
