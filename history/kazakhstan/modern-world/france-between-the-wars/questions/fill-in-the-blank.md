# France between the wars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§20 The development of France after the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Victorious but bled white, France rebuilds, hosts governments that rise and fall, and faces the Popular Front.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§20 France's development after the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Victorious but bled white, France rebuilds through unstable governments to the Popular Front.
