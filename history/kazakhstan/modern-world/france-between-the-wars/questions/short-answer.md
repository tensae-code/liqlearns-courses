# France between the wars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§20 The development of France after the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Victorious but bled white, France rebuilds, hosts governments that rise and fall, and faces the Popular Front.

**2.** Tell the story of "§20 France's development after the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Victorious but bled white, France rebuilds through unstable governments to the Popular Front.
