# Kazakhstan industry in the khrushchev era — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§12–13. Industrial development in Kazakhstan in the Khrushchev years" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sovnarkhozes and giant combines — Karaganda steel and Temirtau grow.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§15–16. The state of Kazakhstan's industry in 1965–1985" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Raw-materials appendage of the Union — extraction grows, processing lags.
