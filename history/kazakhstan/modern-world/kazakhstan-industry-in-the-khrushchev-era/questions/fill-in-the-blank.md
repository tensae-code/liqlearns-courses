# Kazakhstan industry in the khrushchev era — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§12–13. Industrial development in Kazakhstan in the Khrushchev years" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sovnarkhozes and giant combines — Karaganda steel and Temirtau grow.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§15–16. The state of Kazakhstan's industry in 1965–1985" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Raw-materials appendage of the Union — extraction grows, processing lags.
