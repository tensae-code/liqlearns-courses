# Kazakhstan inside the russian empire — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§33–34 Tsarism's reforms in Kazakhstan in the second half of the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 1867–68 statute remakes steppe administration.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§35–36 The liberation struggle of 1860–1870" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ural and Turgai Kazakhs resist the new order by arms.
