# Kazakhstan the start of russian annexation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§9–10 The beginning of the Kazakh Khanate's joining to the Russian Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Abulkhair's oath of 1731, taken under Dzhungar pressure, opens the long road from alliance to annexation.

**2.** Tell the story of "§9 The causes and consequences of Abulkhair's acceptance of Russian subjecthood" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Abulkhair swears the oath of 1731 seeking Russian guns against the Dzhungars — and opens the door to empire.
