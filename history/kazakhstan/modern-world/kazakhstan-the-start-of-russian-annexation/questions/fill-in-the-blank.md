# Kazakhstan the start of russian annexation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§9–10 The beginning of the Kazakh Khanate's joining to the Russian Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Abulkhair's oath of 1731, taken under Dzhungar pressure, opens the long road from alliance to annexation.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9 The causes and consequences of Abulkhair's acceptance of Russian subjecthood" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Abulkhair swears the oath of 1731 seeking Russian guns against the Dzhungars — and opens the door to empire.
