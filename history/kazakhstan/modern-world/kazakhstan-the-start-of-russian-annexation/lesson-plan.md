# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan the start of russian annexation

**Course:** History — Kazakhstan · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan the start of russian annexation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Kazakhstan from the Dzhungar wars to the Great Patriotic War — the 18th–19th-century national books and the 1900–1945 national books of Grade 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Kazakhstan the start of russian annexation as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Kazakhstan from the Dzhungar wars to the Great Patriotic War — the 18th–19th-century national books and the 1900–1945 national books of Grade 8 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§9–10 The beginning of the Kazakh Khanate's joining to the Russian Empire** — Abulkhair's oath of 1731, taken under Dzhungar pressure, opens the long road from alliance to annexation
2. **§9 The causes and consequences of Abulkhair's acceptance of Russian subjecthood** — Abulkhair swears the oath of 1731 seeking Russian guns against the Dzhungars — and opens the door to empire
3. **§10 The Russian Empire's policy in Kazakhstan in the 1730s–40s** — Orenburg is founded and the empire turns a defensive alliance into administration
4. **§2.2 What were the reasons for Abulkhair Khan's letter to Empress Anna Ioannovna?** — one letter of 1730 is cross-examined — plea for help, or surrender of sovereignty?
5. **§2.3 How did the Middle Zhuz join Russia?** — the Middle Zhuz's oaths and embassies follow a different road to the same empire
6. **Lesson plans §§9–14: Abulkhair's oath; Russia's policy in the 1730s–50s; Abylai's rise and policy** — the oath is analysed for causes and aims, then Abylai is built up lesson by lesson as the khanate's restorer

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
