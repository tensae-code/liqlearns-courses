# Kazakhstan deportations to kazakhstan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§35–36 The deportation of the peoples of the USSR to Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Koreans, Germans, Chechens, Ingush and others are dumped from cattle cars onto the steppe, and Kazakh families share their bread.

**2.** Tell the story of "§60 Kazakhstan — a land of the deported peoples of the USSR" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Whole peoples arrive in freight cars and are taken in by Kazakh auls.
