# Kazakhstan deportations to kazakhstan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§35–36 The deportation of the peoples of the USSR to Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Koreans, Germans, Chechens, Ingush and others are dumped from cattle cars onto the steppe, and Kazakh families share their bread.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§60 Kazakhstan — a land of the deported peoples of the USSR" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Whole peoples arrive in freight cars and are taken in by Kazakh auls.
