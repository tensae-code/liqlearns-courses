# Kazakhstan deportations to kazakhstan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§§35–36 The deportation of the peoples of the USSR to Kazakhstan", koreans, Germans, Chechens, Ingush and others are dumped from cattle cars onto the steppe, and Kazakh families share their bread (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§60 Kazakhstan — a land of the deported peoples of the USSR", whole peoples arrive in freight cars and are taken in by Kazakh auls (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
