## Sub lesson 1 — §§35–36 The deportation of the peoples of the USSR to Kazakhstan

Koreans, Germans, Chechens, Ingush and others are dumped from cattle cars onto the steppe, and Kazakh families share their bread.

## Sub lesson 2 — §60 Kazakhstan — a land of the deported peoples of the USSR
