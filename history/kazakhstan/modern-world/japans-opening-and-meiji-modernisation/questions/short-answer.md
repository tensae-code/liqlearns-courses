# Japans opening and meiji modernisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§26 The development of Japan; the Far Eastern policy of the European countries and the "opening" of Japan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** American black ships force Japan open, and the Meiji leaders answer by modernising at speed.

**2.** Tell the story of "§10.3 Why did the USA send warships to Japan?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Perry's black ships anchor off Japan to force trade, and a closed country is opened at cannon-mouth.
