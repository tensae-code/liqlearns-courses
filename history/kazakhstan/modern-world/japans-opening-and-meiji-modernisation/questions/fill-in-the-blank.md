# Japans opening and meiji modernisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 The development of Japan; the Far Eastern policy of the European countries and the "opening" of Japan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** American black ships force Japan open, and the Meiji leaders answer by modernising at speed.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10.3 Why did the USA send warships to Japan?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Perry's black ships anchor off Japan to force trade, and a closed country is opened at cannon-mouth.
