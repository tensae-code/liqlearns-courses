# Russias revolutionary movements — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§20 The rise of new socio-political movements and organisations in Russia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Populists, then Marxist social democrats, organise in secret against the Tsar.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§8.1 Why did Tsar Alexander II become a victim of "Narodnaya Volya"?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The tsar who freed the serfs is hunted and killed by the revolutionary terrorists of the People's Will.
