# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§10 France's wars of conquest in the era of Napoleon Bonaparte" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Napoleon's armies carry the revolution's codes across Europe and meet their end from Spain to Russia to Waterloo.

**2.** Tell the story of "§4.2 To what extent did Napoleon I betray or defend the revolution?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The general who crowned himself emperor is tried by the class on the revolution's own charges.
