# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10 France's wars of conquest in the era of Napoleon Bonaparte" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Napoleon's armies carry the revolution's codes across Europe and meet their end from Spain to Russia to Waterloo.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4.2 To what extent did Napoleon I betray or defend the revolution?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The general who crowned himself emperor is tried by the class on the revolution's own charges.
