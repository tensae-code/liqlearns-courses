# Kazakhstan literature in the 1920s 1930s — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§26 The development of Kazakh literature in the 1920s–30s", seifullin, Zhansugurov and Mailin write a new literature — and two of the three are shot in 1938 (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§§47–48 Kazakhstan's literature in the 1920s–30s; socialist realism", writers are ordered to write socialist realism — and the best of them perish in 1937–38 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
