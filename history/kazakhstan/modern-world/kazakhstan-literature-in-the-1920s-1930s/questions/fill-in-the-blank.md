# Kazakhstan literature in the 1920s 1930s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 The development of Kazakh literature in the 1920s–30s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Seifullin, Zhansugurov and Mailin write a new literature — and two of the three are shot in 1938.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§47–48 Kazakhstan's literature in the 1920s–30s; socialist realism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Writers are ordered to write socialist realism — and the best of them perish in 1937–38.
