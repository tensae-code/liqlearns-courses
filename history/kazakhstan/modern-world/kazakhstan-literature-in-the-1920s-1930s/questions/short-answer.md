# Kazakhstan literature in the 1920s 1930s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§26 The development of Kazakh literature in the 1920s–30s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Seifullin, Zhansugurov and Mailin write a new literature — and two of the three are shot in 1938.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§47–48 Kazakhstan's literature in the 1920s–30s; socialist realism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Writers are ordered to write socialist realism — and the best of them perish in 1937–38.
