# Kazakhstan nazarbayev and the first presidency — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§37–38. The role of Elbasy N. A. Nazarbayev in forming the independent state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The first president builds the state — symbols, borders, a new capital to come.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§17 N. A. Nazarbayev — First President of the Republic of Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The first president builds the state — symbols, borders, currency.
