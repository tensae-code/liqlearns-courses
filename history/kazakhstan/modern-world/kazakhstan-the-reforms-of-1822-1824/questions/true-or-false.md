# Kazakhstan the reforms of 1822 1824 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§§21–22 The tsarist reforms of 1822–1824", speransky's and the Orenburg statutes abolish khan rule in the Middle and Junior zhuzes and replace it with Russian districts and taxes (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§23 The reforms of 1822–1824 and Kazakh society", the statutes abolish the khanates and Kazakh society is re-sorted into Russian districts (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
