# Kazakhstan the reforms of 1822 1824 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§21–22 The tsarist reforms of 1822–1824" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Speransky's and the Orenburg statutes abolish khan rule in the Middle and Junior zhuzes and replace it with Russian districts and taxes.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23 The reforms of 1822–1824 and Kazakh society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The statutes abolish the khanates and Kazakh society is re-sorted into Russian districts.
