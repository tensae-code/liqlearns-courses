# Kazakhstan joins the world — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§42–43. Kazakhstan's entry into the world community" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The UN flag rises in 1992; embassies open both ways.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§51–52. Kazakhstan's links with regional and international organisations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** CIS, SCO, OSCE chairmanship — the multi-vector web is woven.
