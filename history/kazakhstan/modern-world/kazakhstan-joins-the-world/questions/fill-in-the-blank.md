# Kazakhstan joins the world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§42–43. Kazakhstan's entry into the world community" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The UN flag rises in 1992; embassies open both ways.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§51–52. Kazakhstan's links with regional and international organisations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** CIS, SCO, OSCE chairmanship — the multi-vector web is woven.
