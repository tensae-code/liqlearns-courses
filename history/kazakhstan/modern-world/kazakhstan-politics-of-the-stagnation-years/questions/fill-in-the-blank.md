# Kazakhstan politics of the stagnation years — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§19–20. Features of socio-political life in Kazakhstan in 1965–1985" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Stability at the price of stagnation — Kunaev's long rule.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§22. "Personalities": the work of the Kazakh SSR's leaders Zh. Shayakhmetov, D. Kunaev, Zh. Tashenev" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Three leaders, three fates — the republic's room to manoeuvre inside the Union.
