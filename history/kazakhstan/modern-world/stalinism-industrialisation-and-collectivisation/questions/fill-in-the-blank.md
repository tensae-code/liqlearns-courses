# Stalinism industrialisation and collectivisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§21 I. V. Stalin and totalitarianism in the USSR; industrialisation; collectivisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Stalin's five-year plans force factories up and peasants into collective farms at a terrible human cost.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§21 Stalin and totalitarianism in the USSR; industrialisation; collectivisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Five-year plans and forced collectivisation at a terrible human cost.
