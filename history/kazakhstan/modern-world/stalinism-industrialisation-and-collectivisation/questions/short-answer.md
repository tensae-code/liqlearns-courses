# Stalinism industrialisation and collectivisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§21 I. V. Stalin and totalitarianism in the USSR; industrialisation; collectivisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Stalin's five-year plans force factories up and peasants into collective farms at a terrible human cost.

**2.** Tell the story of "§21 Stalin and totalitarianism in the USSR; industrialisation; collectivisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Five-year plans and forced collectivisation at a terrible human cost.
