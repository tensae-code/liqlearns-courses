# Kazakhstan the dzhungar invasion begins — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§1–2 The start of the patriotic war against Dzhungar aggression" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Dzhungar state arms for conquest while a divided Kazakh Khanate faces invasion from the east and Russian forts from the north.

**2.** Tell the story of "§1 The domestic and foreign political position of the Kazakh Khanate at the start of the 18th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tauke's unified khanate weakens after his death just as Dzhungaria arms and Russia builds forts.
