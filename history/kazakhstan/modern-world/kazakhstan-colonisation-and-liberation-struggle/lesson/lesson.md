# Kazakhstan colonisation and liberation struggle

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Kazakhstan colonisation and liberation struggle" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — §§19–20 The Russian Empire's colonial policy: goals and methods

Land seizure and administrative control are named as colonial aims.

## Sub lesson 2 — §§21–22 The national-liberation movement led by Syrym Datov

The Junior Zhuz rises under Syrym against khan and empire alike.

## Sub lesson 3 — §23 The reforms of 1822–1824 and Kazakh society

Speransky's and the Orenburg charters abolish khan rule in two zhuzes.

## Sub lesson 4 — §24 The Kazakh people's national-liberation movement in the first half of the 19th century

Zholaman and Isatai keep armed resistance alive.

## Sub lesson 5 — §§25–26 The uprising of the Bukey Horde Kazakhs, 1836–1838

Isatai and Makhambet lead the Inner Horde's revolt.

## Sub lesson 6 — §§27–28 The national-liberation movement led by Kenesary Kasymov

Kenesary restores the khanate and wars on two empires for a decade.

## Sub lesson 7 — §§29–30 The Kazakhs' uprising against the Central Asian khanates' invasions

Steppe towns resist Kokand's and Khiva's tax collectors.

## Sub lesson 8 — §§31–32 The joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire

Fort by fort, the south is annexed by the 1860s.

## Sub lesson 9 — §§17–18 Kazakhstan's colonisation begins

Administrative control replaces alliance as empire advances.

## Sub lesson 10 — §§19–20 Syrym Datuly's national-liberation uprising (1783–1797)

The Junior Zhuz wars on puppet khans and border forts.

## Sub lesson 11 — §§21–22 The tsarist reforms of 1822–1824

Charters dissolve khan power in the Middle and Junior zhuzes.

## Sub lesson 12 — §§23–24 The uprising in the Bukey Horde (1836–1838)

Isatai Taimanuly and Makhambet rise against Jangir Khan.

## Sub lesson 13 — §§25–26 Kenesary Kasymuly's national-liberation uprising (1837–1847)

The last khan restores the state and falls fighting in the Kyrgyz mountains.

## Sub lesson 14 — §§27–28 The Kazakhs in the 1840s–60s; relations with the Central Asian khanates

Kokand's forts tax the southern auls until revolts spread.

## Sub lesson 15 — §§29–30 The completion of Kazakhstan's joining of the Russian Empire

The Great Horde and the south are absorbed by 1864.
