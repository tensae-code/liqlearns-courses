# Science in the 1800s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§29 The scientific discoveries of Western scholars and thinkers ("Development of scientific thought" unit)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Darwin, Pasteur, Faraday and their peers rebuild the picture of nature and life.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12.1 Which scientific discovery of the 19th century had the greatest influence on people's lives?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Nobel's dynamite fortune becomes peace prizes, Daguerre fixes the image, Darwin rewrites life's story — pupils argue for the most life-changing discovery.
