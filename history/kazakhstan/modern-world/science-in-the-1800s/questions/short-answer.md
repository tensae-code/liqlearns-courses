# Science in the 1800s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§29 The scientific discoveries of Western scholars and thinkers ("Development of scientific thought" unit)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Darwin, Pasteur, Faraday and their peers rebuild the picture of nature and life.

**2.** Tell the story of "§12.1 Which scientific discovery of the 19th century had the greatest influence on people's lives?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Nobel's dynamite fortune becomes peace prizes, Daguerre fixes the image, Darwin rewrites life's story — pupils argue for the most life-changing discovery.
