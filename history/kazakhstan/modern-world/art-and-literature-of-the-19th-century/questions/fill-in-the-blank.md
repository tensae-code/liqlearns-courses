# Art and literature of the 19th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "11.1 How did the century's art and literature movements reflect social injustice?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Realists and romantics put the poor and oppressed on the page and canvas.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "11.2 How does Émile Zola's article 'J'accuse' show us the power of the word?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** One open letter shakes the French Republic in the Dreyfus affair.
