# Art and literature of the 19th century — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "11.1 How did the century's art and literature movements reflect social injustice?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Realists and romantics put the poor and oppressed on the page and canvas.

**2.** Tell the story of "11.2 How does Émile Zola's article 'J'accuse' show us the power of the word?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** One open letter shakes the French Republic in the Dreyfus affair.
