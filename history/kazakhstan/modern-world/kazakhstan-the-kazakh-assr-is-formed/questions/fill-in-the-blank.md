# Kazakhstan the kazakh assr is formed — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§13 The formation of the Kirghiz (Kazakh) Autonomous Soviet Republic" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 1920 the Kazakhs get an autonomy inside Soviet Russia — with borders and a capital argued over district by district.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§22–23 The process of forming the Kirghiz (Kazakh) Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Decrees in Moscow, a founding congress in Orenburg — an autonomy is proclaimed.
