# Kazakhstan the kazakh assr is formed — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§13 The formation of the Kirghiz (Kazakh) Autonomous Soviet Republic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 1920 the Kazakhs get an autonomy inside Soviet Russia — with borders and a capital argued over district by district.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§22–23 The process of forming the Kirghiz (Kazakh) Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Decrees in Moscow, a founding congress in Orenburg — an autonomy is proclaimed.
