## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§13 The formation of the Kirghiz (Kazakh) Autonomous Soviet Republic** — in 1920 the Kazakhs get an autonomy inside Soviet Russia — with borders and a capital argued over district by district
2. **§§22–23 The process of forming the Kirghiz (Kazakh) Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic** — decrees in Moscow, a founding congress in Orenburg — an autonomy is proclaimed
3. **§24 The forming of the Kazakh ASSR's territory; the Kazakh intelligentsia's role in restoring the integrity of the Kazakh lands** — Alash leaders inside Soviet commissions argue whole districts back into Kazakhstan
4. **§13 The formation of the Kirghiz (Kazakh) Autonomous Soviet Republic** — in 1920 the Kazakhs gain an autonomy inside Soviet Russia
5. **§2.5 The formation of the Kazakh ASSR** — autonomy inside Soviet Russia is argued into being in 1920
