# India gandhi jinnah and the road to independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§16 India after the First World War; the views of M. Gandhi and M. Jinnah on how to win India's independence" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Gandhi leads mass non-violent resistance to British rule while Jinnah argues the Muslims need a state of their own.

**2.** Tell the story of "§16 India after the First World War; Gandhi's and Jinnah's views on winning independence" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gandhi's non-violence against the Raj; Jinnah's separate-state argument.
