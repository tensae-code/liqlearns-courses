# Absolutism in crisis charles i and osman ii — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§21 Charles I and Osman II" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** An English king loses his head and an Ottoman sultan is killed by his own janissaries — absolutism's limits.

**2.** Tell the story of "7.3 Why could Charles I and Osman II not become absolute monarchs?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Parliament and janissaries each kill a would-be absolute ruler.
