# Absolutism in crisis charles i and osman ii — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§21 Charles I and Osman II" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** An English king loses his head and an Ottoman sultan is killed by his own janissaries — absolutism's limits.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "7.3 Why could Charles I and Osman II not become absolute monarchs?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
