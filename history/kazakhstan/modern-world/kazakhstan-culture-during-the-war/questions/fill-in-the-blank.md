# Kazakhstan culture during the war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§33–34 The culture of the Kazakh SSR in the Great Patriotic War period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Evacuated film studios and theatres in Alma-Ata make the city a wartime cultural capital.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§59 The development of science, literature and art in Kazakhstan in the war years" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Evacuated Lenfilm crews and Kazakh writers keep culture working for victory.
