# Kazakhstan culture during the war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§33–34 The culture of the Kazakh SSR in the Great Patriotic War period" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Evacuated film studios and theatres in Alma-Ata make the city a wartime cultural capital.

**2.** Tell the story of "§59 The development of science, literature and art in Kazakhstan in the war years" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Evacuated Lenfilm crews and Kazakh writers keep culture working for victory.
