# China and the west in the opium era — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "6.1 What can we learn about imperialism from the correspondence of the English and Chinese governments?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lin Zexu's letter to Queen Victoria meets Palmerston's gunboat answer.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "6.2 Were economic problems the main cause of China's weakening by 1900?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Opium deficits, rebellions and foreign pressure are weighed together.
