# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§29 Scientific knowledge develops; Copernicus's theory" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Copernicus moves the Earth and science begins its revolution.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§30 Founders of the modern scientific worldview and their followers" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Copernicus, Galileo and Newton rebuild the universe.
