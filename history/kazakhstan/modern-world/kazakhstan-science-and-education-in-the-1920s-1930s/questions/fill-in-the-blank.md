# Kazakhstan science and education in the 1920s 1930s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§23–24 Science and the education system in the 1920s–30s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Likbez literacy campaigns, new universities and institutes grow even as ideology tightens its grip.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§41–42 Public education" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Likbez points, schools and teacher courses fight illiteracy across the steppe.
