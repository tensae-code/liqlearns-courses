# Kazakhstan education 1945 1985 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§24–25. Development of the education system in Kazakhstan in 1945–1985" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Universal schooling arrives — in Russian more than in Kazakh.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10 The development of the education system in the second half of the 20th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
