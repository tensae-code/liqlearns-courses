# Kazakhstan education 1945 1985 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§24–25. Development of the education system in Kazakhstan in 1945–1985" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Universal schooling arrives — in Russian more than in Kazakh.

**2.** Tell the story of "§10 The development of the education system in the second half of the 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
