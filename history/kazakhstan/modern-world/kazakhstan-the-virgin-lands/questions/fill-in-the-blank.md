# Kazakhstan the virgin lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§9–10. The development of the virgin and fallow lands in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Millions rush to plough the steppe; the grain gamble remakes the republic's people and soil.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§11. Economic, ecological and demographic consequences of the virgin-lands development" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Dust, erosion and a shifted ethnic balance — the bill for the ploughed steppe arrives.
