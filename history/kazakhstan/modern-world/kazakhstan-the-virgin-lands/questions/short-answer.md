# Kazakhstan the virgin lands — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§9–10. The development of the virgin and fallow lands in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Millions rush to plough the steppe; the grain gamble remakes the republic's people and soil.

**2.** Tell the story of "§11. Economic, ecological and demographic consequences of the virgin-lands development" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Dust, erosion and a shifted ethnic balance — the bill for the ploughed steppe arrives.
