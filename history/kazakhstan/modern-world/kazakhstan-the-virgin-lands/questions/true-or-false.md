# Kazakhstan the virgin lands — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§§9–10. The development of the virgin and fallow lands in Kazakhstan", millions rush to plough the steppe; the grain gamble remakes the republic's people and soil (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§11. Economic, ecological and demographic consequences of the virgin-lands development", dust, erosion and a shifted ethnic balance — the bill for the ploughed steppe arrives (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
