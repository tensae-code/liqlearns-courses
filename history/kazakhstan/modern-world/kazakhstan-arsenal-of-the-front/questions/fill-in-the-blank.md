# Kazakhstan arsenal of the front — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§31–32 Kazakhstan — arsenal of the front" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Evacuated factories, Karaganda coal and steppe farms feed and arm the front while women and children do the work.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§57–58 The shock work of Kazakhstanis in the war years" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Miners, collective farmers and children out-produce the plan for the front.
