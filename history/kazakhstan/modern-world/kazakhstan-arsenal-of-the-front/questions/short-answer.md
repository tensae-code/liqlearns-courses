# Kazakhstan arsenal of the front — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§31–32 Kazakhstan — arsenal of the front" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Evacuated factories, Karaganda coal and steppe farms feed and arm the front while women and children do the work.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§57–58 The shock work of Kazakhstanis in the war years" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Miners, collective farmers and children out-produce the plan for the front.
