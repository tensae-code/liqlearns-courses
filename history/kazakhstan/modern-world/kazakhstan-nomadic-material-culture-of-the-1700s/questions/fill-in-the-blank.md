# Kazakhstan nomadic material culture of the 1700s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§15–16 Kazakh material culture in the 18th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The yurt, dress, food, crafts and weapons of a nomadic world are described piece by piece.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§17 Kazakh national applied art" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Felt, silver, wood and bone are worked into the ornaments of nomadic life.
