# Kazakhstan nomadic material culture of the 1700s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§15–16 Kazakh material culture in the 18th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The yurt, dress, food, crafts and weapons of a nomadic world are described piece by piece.

**2.** Tell the story of "§17 Kazakh national applied art" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Felt, silver, wood and bone are worked into the ornaments of nomadic life.
