# Kazakhstan music of the 1800s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§50 The musical art of Kazakhstan in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The kuishi composers turn the dombra into the steppe's orchestra.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§51 Song art of Kazakhstan in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Birzhan-sal and Akan-seri make the singing poet a public figure.
