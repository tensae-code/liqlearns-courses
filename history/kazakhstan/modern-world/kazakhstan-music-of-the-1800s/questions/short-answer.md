# Kazakhstan music of the 1800s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§50 The musical art of Kazakhstan in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The kuishi composers turn the dombra into the steppe's orchestra.

**2.** Tell the story of "§51 Song art of Kazakhstan in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Birzhan-sal and Akan-seri make the singing poet a public figure.
