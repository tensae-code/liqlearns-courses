# Kazakhstan akhmet baitursynuly — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§22 A. Baitursynuly — "teacher of the nation"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The linguist who reformed the Kazakh alphabet and edited its first newspaper is honoured in life and destroyed by the regime he outlived.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§39–40 A. Baitursynov's contribution to the development of Kazakh linguistics" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The alphabet reformer gives Kazakh a scientific grammar — and is shot as an "enemy of the people".
