# Kazakhstan akhmet baitursynuly — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** §22 A. Baitursynuly — "teacher of the nation" — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** §§39–40 A. Baitursynov's contribution to the development of Kazakh linguistics — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the linguist who reformed the Kazakh alphabet and edited its first newspaper is honoured in life and destroyed by the regime he outlived
b) the alphabet reformer gives Kazakh a scientific grammar — and is shot as an "enemy of the people"

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
