# Kazakhstan akhmet baitursynuly — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§22 A. Baitursynuly — "teacher of the nation"", the linguist who reformed the Kazakh alphabet and edited its first newspaper is honoured in life and destroyed by the regime he outlived (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§§39–40 A. Baitursynov's contribution to the development of Kazakh linguistics", the alphabet reformer gives Kazakh a scientific grammar — and is shot as an "enemy of the people" (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
