# Kazakhstan akhmet baitursynuly — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§22 A. Baitursynuly — "teacher of the nation"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The linguist who reformed the Kazakh alphabet and edited its first newspaper is honoured in life and destroyed by the regime he outlived.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§39–40 A. Baitursynov's contribution to the development of Kazakh linguistics" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The alphabet reformer gives Kazakh a scientific grammar — and is shot as an "enemy of the people".
