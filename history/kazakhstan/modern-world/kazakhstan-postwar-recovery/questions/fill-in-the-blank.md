# Kazakhstan postwar recovery — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§1–2. The Kazakh SSR's post-war socio-economic development; mineral exploration and industrial growth" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A ruined republic rebuilds — geologists open new deposits and factories rise on the steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1 The Kazakh SSR's socio-economic development in the post-war years" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A ruined republic rebuilds its farms, mines and factories.
