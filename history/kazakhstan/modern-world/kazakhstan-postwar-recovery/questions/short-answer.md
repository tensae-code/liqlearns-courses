# Kazakhstan postwar recovery — short answer

**1.** Tell the story of "§§1–2. The Kazakh SSR's post-war socio-economic development; mineral exploration and industrial growth" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A ruined republic rebuilds — geologists open new deposits and factories rise on the steppe.
