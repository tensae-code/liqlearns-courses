# Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§18 The Franco-Prussian War; the unification of Germany" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bismarck's blood-and-iron wars humble France and proclaim a German Empire at Versailles.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§7.2 How similar were the paths of unification of Italy and Germany? (Germany)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
