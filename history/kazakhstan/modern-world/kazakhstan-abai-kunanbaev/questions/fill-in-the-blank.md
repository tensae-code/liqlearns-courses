# Kazakhstan abai kunanbaev — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§51–52 Abai Kunanbaev — great poet and thinker" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Abai's poems and Words of Edification call his people to learning, work and honesty.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§58–59 The greatness of Abai Kunanbaev" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Abai's poetry and prose judge and teach his own people.
