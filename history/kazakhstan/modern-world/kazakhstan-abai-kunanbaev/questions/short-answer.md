# Kazakhstan abai kunanbaev — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§51–52 Abai Kunanbaev — great poet and thinker" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Abai's poems and Words of Edification call his people to learning, work and honesty.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§58–59 The greatness of Abai Kunanbaev" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Abai's poetry and prose judge and teach his own people.
