# Kazakhstan industrialisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§16–17 Industrialisation in Kazakhstan in the 1920s–1930s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Turksib railway, Karaganda coal and Balkhash copper are built at breakneck speed to make Kazakhstan the USSR's raw-material base.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§29–30 Socialist industrialisation in Kazakhstan (its essence and features)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The republic is assigned the raw-material role — mines and Turksib before factories.
