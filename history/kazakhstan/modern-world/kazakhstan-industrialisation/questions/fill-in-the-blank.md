# Kazakhstan industrialisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§16–17 Industrialisation in Kazakhstan in the 1920s–1930s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Turksib railway, Karaganda coal and Balkhash copper are built at breakneck speed to make Kazakhstan the USSR's raw-material base.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§29–30 Socialist industrialisation in Kazakhstan (its essence and features)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The republic is assigned the raw-material role — mines and Turksib before factories.
