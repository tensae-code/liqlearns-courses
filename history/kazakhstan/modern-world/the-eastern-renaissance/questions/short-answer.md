# The eastern renaissance — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§26 The Eastern Renaissance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Timurid Samarkand and Herat flower with Ulugh Beg's astronomy and Navoi's poetry.

**2.** Tell the story of "§27 The Eastern Renaissance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Samarkand's Ulugh Beg and Herat's artists flower in the East's own renaissance.
