# The eastern renaissance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§26 The Eastern Renaissance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Timurid Samarkand and Herat flower with Ulugh Beg's astronomy and Navoi's poetry.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§27 The Eastern Renaissance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Samarkand's Ulugh Beg and Herat's artists flower in the East's own renaissance.
