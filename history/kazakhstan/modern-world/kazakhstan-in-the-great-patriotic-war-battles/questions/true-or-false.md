# Kazakhstan in the great patriotic war battles — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§§29–30 Kazakhstanis' part in the battles of the Great Patriotic War", the Panfilov division at Moscow, Bauyrzhan Momyshuly, and snipers and pilots like Aliya Moldagulova carry Kazakhstan's name through the war (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§50 The start of the Great Patriotic War; the mobilisation of military formations in Kazakhstan", divisions form in Alma-Ata and Aktyubinsk and entrain for the front (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
