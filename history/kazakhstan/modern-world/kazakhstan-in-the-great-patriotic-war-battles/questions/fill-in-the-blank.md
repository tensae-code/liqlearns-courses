# Kazakhstan in the great patriotic war battles — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§29–30 Kazakhstanis' part in the battles of the Great Patriotic War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Panfilov division at Moscow, Bauyrzhan Momyshuly, and snipers and pilots like Aliya Moldagulova carry Kazakhstan's name through the war.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§50 The start of the Great Patriotic War; the mobilisation of military formations in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Divisions form in Alma-Ata and Aktyubinsk and entrain for the front.
