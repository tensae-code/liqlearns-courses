# Kazakhstan in the great patriotic war battles — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§29–30 Kazakhstanis' part in the battles of the Great Patriotic War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Panfilov division at Moscow, Bauyrzhan Momyshuly, and snipers and pilots like Aliya Moldagulova carry Kazakhstan's name through the war.

**2.** Tell the story of "§50 The start of the Great Patriotic War; the mobilisation of military formations in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Divisions form in Alma-Ata and Aktyubinsk and entrain for the front.
