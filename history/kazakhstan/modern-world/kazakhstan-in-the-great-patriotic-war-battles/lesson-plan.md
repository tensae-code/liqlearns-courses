# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan in the great patriotic war battles

**Course:** History — Kazakhstan · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan in the great patriotic war battles
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Kazakhstan from the Dzhungar wars to the Great Patriotic War — the 18th–19th-century national books and the 1900–1945 national books of Grade 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Kazakhstan in the great patriotic war battles as Kazakhstan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Kazakhstan from the Dzhungar wars to the Great Patriotic War — the 18th–19th-century national books and the 1900–1945 national books of Grade 8 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§29–30 Kazakhstanis' part in the battles of the Great Patriotic War** — the Panfilov division at Moscow, Bauyrzhan Momyshuly, and snipers and pilots like Aliya Moldagulova carry Kazakhstan's name through the war
2. **§50 The start of the Great Patriotic War; the mobilisation of military formations in Kazakhstan** — divisions form in Alma-Ata and Aktyubinsk and entrain for the front
3. **§51 Kazakhstanis' part in the battles of the war's initial period; the defence of Leningrad** — Kazakh soldiers hold at Moscow's approaches and on the Leningrad front
4. **§§52–53 The heroism and fate of Kazakhstanis in the war years** — letters, wounds and missing-in-action notices — the war told through soldiers' fates
5. **§54 Kazakhstanis — Heroes of the Soviet Union; B. Momyshuly, a legendary personality** — Momyshuly's battalion at Volokolamsk becomes the stuff of Bek's novel and national legend
6. **§55 Kazakhstanis' part in the offensive operations and in the partisan movement** — from Stalingrad to Berlin, and Kazakh partisans behind German lines
7. **§§29–30 Kazakhstanis' part in the battles of the Great Patriotic War** — the Panfilov division at Moscow, Momyshuly, Moldagulova
8. **§6.2 How did Kazakhstanis show heroism in the battles of the Great Patriotic War?** — the Panfilovtsy at Moscow and Moldagulova's sniper war
9. **§6.10 Kazakhstan's contribution to the victory** — soldiers, steel, grain and refuge — the republic's war balance is drawn
