# Kazakhstan steppe fairs and trade — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§39–40 The development of fair trade" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Great fairs like Koyandy and Karkaraly link nomad producers to Russian and Central Asian merchants.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§43 The development of trade relations in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Caravan routes and great fairs bind the steppe to Russian and Asian markets.
