# Kazakhstan steppe fairs and trade — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§39–40 The development of fair trade" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Great fairs like Koyandy and Karkaraly link nomad producers to Russian and Central Asian merchants.

**2.** Tell the story of "§43 The development of trade relations in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Caravan routes and great fairs bind the steppe to Russian and Asian markets.
