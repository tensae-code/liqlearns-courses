# Exploration causes and results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "8.3 What changes did the Great Discoveries bring to the world?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** New continents, crops and silver rewire the world economy.

**2.** Tell the story of "§25 The Great Discoveries of the late 15th–early 16th c. and their consequences" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** America's discovery joins the hemispheres and shifts trade to the oceans.
