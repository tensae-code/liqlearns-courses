# Exploration causes and results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "8.3 What changes did the Great Discoveries bring to the world?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** New continents, crops and silver rewire the world economy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 The Great Discoveries of the late 15th–early 16th c. and their consequences" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** America's discovery joins the hemispheres and shifts trade to the oceans.
