# Cold war crises 1948 1962 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§6 The formation of the world socialist camp; key Cold War events of 1948–1962" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Berlin blockade, Korea, Cuba — the superpowers repeatedly step to the brink.

**2.** Tell the story of "§13 Key Cold War events of 1979–1985 and its completion" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Afghanistan restarts the confrontation; then Gorbachev and Reagan wind it down.
