# Cold war crises 1948 1962 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§6 The formation of the world socialist camp; key Cold War events of 1948–1962" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Berlin blockade, Korea, Cuba — the superpowers repeatedly step to the brink.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§13 Key Cold War events of 1979–1985 and its completion" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Afghanistan restarts the confrontation; then Gorbachev and Reagan wind it down.
