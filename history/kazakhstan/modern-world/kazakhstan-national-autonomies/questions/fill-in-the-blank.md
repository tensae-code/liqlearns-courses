# Kazakhstan national autonomies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 National autonomies in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alash Orda and the Turkestan (Kokand) autonomy try two different Kazakh statehoods and both are extinguished by the Bolsheviks.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§19 The activity of the national autonomies in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alash Orda governs from Semey while Soviet power grows around it.
