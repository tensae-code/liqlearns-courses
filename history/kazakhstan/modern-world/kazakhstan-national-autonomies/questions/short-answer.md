# Kazakhstan national autonomies — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§12 National autonomies in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alash Orda and the Turkestan (Kokand) autonomy try two different Kazakh statehoods and both are extinguished by the Bolsheviks.

**2.** Tell the story of "§19 The activity of the national autonomies in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alash Orda governs from Semey while Soviet power grows around it.
