# Europes 1848 revolutions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§17 European countries in the 19th century; the bourgeois revolutions of 1848 and the forming of new political ideas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Revolution sweeps capital after capital in 1848 as liberalism, nationalism and socialism take organised shape.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§7.1 Which political ideas influenced the revolutions in Europe in 1848?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Liberal, national and socialist ideas each claim the barricades of 1848.
