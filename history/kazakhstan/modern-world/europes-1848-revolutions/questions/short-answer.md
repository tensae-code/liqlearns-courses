# Europes 1848 revolutions — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§17 European countries in the 19th century; the bourgeois revolutions of 1848 and the forming of new political ideas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Revolution sweeps capital after capital in 1848 as liberalism, nationalism and socialism take organised shape.

**2.** Tell the story of "§7.1 Which political ideas influenced the revolutions in Europe in 1848?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Liberal, national and socialist ideas each claim the barricades of 1848.
