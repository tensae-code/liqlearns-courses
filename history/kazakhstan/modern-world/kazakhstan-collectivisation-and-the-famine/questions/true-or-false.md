# Kazakhstan collectivisation and the famine — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§§18–19 Collectivisation in Kazakhstan", forced sedentarisation and grain and livestock seizures bring the Asharshylyk famine of 1931–1933, which kills a vast share of the Kazakh people and scatters the rest (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§§32–33 The policy of forced collectivisation in agriculture and its economic consequences", livestock is seized, nomads are settled by force, and the herds that feed the nation vanish (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
