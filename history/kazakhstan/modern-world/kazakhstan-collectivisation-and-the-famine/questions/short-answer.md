# Kazakhstan collectivisation and the famine — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§18–19 Collectivisation in Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Forced sedentarisation and grain and livestock seizures bring the Asharshylyk famine of 1931–1933, which kills a vast share of the Kazakh people and scatters the rest.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§32–33 The policy of forced collectivisation in agriculture and its economic consequences" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Livestock is seized, nomads are settled by force, and the herds that feed the nation vanish.
