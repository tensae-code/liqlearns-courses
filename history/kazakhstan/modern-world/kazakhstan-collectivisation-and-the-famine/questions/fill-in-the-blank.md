# Kazakhstan collectivisation and the famine — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§18–19 Collectivisation in Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Forced sedentarisation and grain and livestock seizures bring the Asharshylyk famine of 1931–1933, which kills a vast share of the Kazakh people and scatters the rest.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§32–33 The policy of forced collectivisation in agriculture and its economic consequences" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Livestock is seized, nomads are settled by force, and the herds that feed the nation vanish.
