# The first world war course of the war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§6 The main military operations of 1914–1918 and the consequences of the war" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Schlieffen gamble fails, trenches freeze the front, and four years of slaughter exhaust every empire that started it.

**2.** Tell the story of "§6 The main military operations of 1914–1918 and the war's consequences" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trenches freeze the front and four years of slaughter exhaust the empires.
