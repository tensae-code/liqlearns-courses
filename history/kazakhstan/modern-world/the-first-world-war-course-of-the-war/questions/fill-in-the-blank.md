# The first world war course of the war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§6 The main military operations of 1914–1918 and the consequences of the war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Schlieffen gamble fails, trenches freeze the front, and four years of slaughter exhaust every empire that started it.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§6 The main military operations of 1914–1918 and the war's consequences" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
