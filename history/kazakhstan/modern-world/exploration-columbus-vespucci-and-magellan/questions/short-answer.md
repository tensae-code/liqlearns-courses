# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§24 The Great Geographical Discoveries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan redraw the world map in one generation.

**2.** Tell the story of "§23 The Great Geographical Discoveries" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ocean voyages join the continents into one world.
