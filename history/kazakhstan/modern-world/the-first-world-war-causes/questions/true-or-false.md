# The first world war causes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§5 Contradictions between the leading capitalist countries; causes of the First World War", rival empires collide over colonies and markets, alliances harden, and one assassination lights the fuse (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§5 Contradictions between the leading capitalist countries; causes of the First World War", rival empires and alliances harden until one assassination lights the fuse (Sub lesson 2)
