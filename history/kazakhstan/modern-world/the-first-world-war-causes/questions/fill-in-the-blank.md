# The first world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5 Contradictions between the leading capitalist countries; causes of the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rival empires collide over colonies and markets, alliances harden, and one assassination lights the fuse.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§5 Contradictions between the leading capitalist countries; causes of the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rival empires and alliances harden until one assassination lights the fuse.
