# The first world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§5 Contradictions between the leading capitalist countries; causes of the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rival empires collide over colonies and markets, alliances harden, and one assassination lights the fuse.

**2.** Tell the story of "§5 Contradictions between the leading capitalist countries; causes of the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rival empires and alliances harden until one assassination lights the fuse.
