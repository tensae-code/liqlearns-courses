# Kazakhstan from war communism to nep — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§14–15 The transition from the policy of "war communism" to the NEP" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Requisitioning gives way to the tax in kind and revived markets, and the steppe breathes again.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§25–26 The NEP in Kazakhstan (1921–1925)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The tax in kind replaces seizure and bazaars reopen.
