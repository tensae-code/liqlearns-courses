# Kazakhstan spiritual culture and family traditions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§46 Features of spiritual culture and family traditions" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Weddings, funerals and hospitality carry the nation's values.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§47 National games" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Baiga races, kokpar and altybakan — the games that train riders and bind the aul.
