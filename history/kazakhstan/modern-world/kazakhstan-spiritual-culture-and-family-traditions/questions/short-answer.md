# Kazakhstan spiritual culture and family traditions — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§46 Features of spiritual culture and family traditions" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Weddings, funerals and hospitality carry the nation's values.

**2.** Tell the story of "§47 National games" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Baiga races, kokpar and altybakan — the games that train riders and bind the aul.
