# Kazakhstan political repressions — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§20–21 Political repressions of the 1920s–30s" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Alash generation is arrested and shot in the Great Terror, and camps like Karlag and ALZHIR fill the steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of "§36 Socio-political processes in Kazakhstan in the 1920s–30s; the Stalinist repressions" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Case after case is fabricated against the Alash generation and ordinary people alike.
