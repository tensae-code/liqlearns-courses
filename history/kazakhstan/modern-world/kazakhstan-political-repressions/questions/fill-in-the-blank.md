# Kazakhstan political repressions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§20–21 Political repressions of the 1920s–30s" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Alash generation is arrested and shot in the Great Terror, and camps like Karlag and ALZHIR fill the steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§36 Socio-political processes in Kazakhstan in the 1920s–30s; the Stalinist repressions" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Case after case is fabricated against the Alash generation and ordinary people alike.
