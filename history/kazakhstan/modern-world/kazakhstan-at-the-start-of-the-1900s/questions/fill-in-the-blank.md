# Kazakhstan at the start of the 1900s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§1–2 The socio-economic situation in Kazakhstan at the start of the 20th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A colonial steppe of nomads, settlers, mines and bazaars enters the new century with land hunger growing.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§1–2 Socio-political processes in Kazakhstan at the start of the 20th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Parties, petitions and the first workers' strikes stir a colonial steppe.
