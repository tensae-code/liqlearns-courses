# Kazakhstan at the start of the 1900s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§1–2 The socio-economic situation in Kazakhstan at the start of the 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A colonial steppe of nomads, settlers, mines and bazaars enters the new century with land hunger growing.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§1–2 Socio-political processes in Kazakhstan at the start of the 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Parties, petitions and the first workers' strikes stir a colonial steppe.
