# Kazakhstan cossack military colonisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§17–18 The military-Cossack colonisation of Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lines of Cossack forts and stanitsas fence off Kazakh pasture and turn the border into a land grab.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§19–20 The colonisation of Kazakhstan by the Russian Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Fortress lines, Cossack stanitsas and land seizures turn alliance into colonisation.
