# Kazakhstan cossack military colonisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§17–18 The military-Cossack colonisation of Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lines of Cossack forts and stanitsas fence off Kazakh pasture and turn the border into a land grab.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§19–20 The colonisation of Kazakhstan by the Russian Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Fortress lines, Cossack stanitsas and land seizures turn alliance into colonisation.
