# Fascism and nazism two dictatorships compared — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§22 Italy after the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Post-war chaos and fear of socialism carry Mussolini's fascists into power in Rome.

**2.** Tell the story of "§23 Germany: the fascist party and the establishment of the totalitarian regime" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hitler's party exploits crisis and propaganda to turn the Weimar Republic into a one-party dictatorship.
