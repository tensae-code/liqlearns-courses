# Fascism and nazism two dictatorships compared — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§22 Italy after the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Post-war chaos and fear of socialism carry Mussolini's fascists into power in Rome.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§23 Germany: the fascist party and the establishment of the totalitarian regime" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hitler's party exploits crisis and propaganda to turn the Weimar Republic into a one-party dictatorship.
