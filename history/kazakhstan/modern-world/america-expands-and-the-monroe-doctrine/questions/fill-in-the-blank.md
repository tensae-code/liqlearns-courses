# America expands and the monroe doctrine — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§24 The socio-economic development of the USA in the first half of the 19th century; the "Monroe Doctrine"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A young republic grows westward and warns Europe to keep out of the Americas.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10.1 How did westward expansion shape the forming of American national self-awareness?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pioneers, trails and annexations forge a continental identity.
