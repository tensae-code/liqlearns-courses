# Kazakhstan kanysh satpaev — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 Kanysh Satpaev — outstanding scholar and public figure" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The geologist who proved Zhezkazgan's copper wealth builds Kazakhstan's Academy of Sciences.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§43–44 The appearance of higher educational institutions; the creation of the Kazakh branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences and K. Satpaev's role in developing Kazakhstan's science" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The republic's first universities and its Academy grow around Satpaev's geology.
