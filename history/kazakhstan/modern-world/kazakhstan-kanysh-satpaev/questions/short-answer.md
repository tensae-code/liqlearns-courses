# Kazakhstan kanysh satpaev — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§25 Kanysh Satpaev — outstanding scholar and public figure" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The geologist who proved Zhezkazgan's copper wealth builds Kazakhstan's Academy of Sciences.

**2.** Tell the story of "§§43–44 The appearance of higher educational institutions; the creation of the Kazakh branch of the USSR Academy of Sciences and K. Satpaev's role in developing Kazakhstan's science" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The republic's first universities and its Academy grow around Satpaev's geology.
