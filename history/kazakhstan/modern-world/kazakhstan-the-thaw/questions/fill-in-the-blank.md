# Kazakhstan the thaw — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§7–8. Kazakhstan in the "Thaw" (1954–1964)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Stalin, camps empty and voices soften — but the system endures.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4 Socio-political development of Kazakhstan in the "Khrushchev thaw" period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
