# Kazakhstan the thaw — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§7–8. Kazakhstan in the "Thaw" (1954–1964)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Stalin, camps empty and voices soften — but the system endures.

**2.** Tell the story of "§4 Socio-political development of Kazakhstan in the "Khrushchev thaw" period" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Stalin dies; camps empty; voices soften.
