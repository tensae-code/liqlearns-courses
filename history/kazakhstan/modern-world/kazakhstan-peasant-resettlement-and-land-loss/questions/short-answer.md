# Kazakhstan peasant resettlement and land loss — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§§35–36 The mass peasant colonisation of Kazakhstan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Settler villages take the best watered land and nomads are squeezed onto poorer pastures.

**2.** Tell the story of "§38 The Russian Empire's resettlement policy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Peasant settlers are planted on the best steppe land by state plan.
