# Kazakhstan peasant resettlement and land loss — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§§35–36 The mass peasant colonisation of Kazakhstan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Settler villages take the best watered land and nomads are squeezed onto poorer pastures.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§38 The Russian Empire's resettlement policy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Peasant settlers are planted on the best steppe land by state plan.
