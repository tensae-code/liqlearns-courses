# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§6 Why Egypt was called the Black Land" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Nile's black silt feeds a kingdom that calls its own soil Black Land and the desert Red Land.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§7 How Egypt's pyramids were built" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Thousands haul and stack stone so a pharaoh's tomb rises to outlast him.
