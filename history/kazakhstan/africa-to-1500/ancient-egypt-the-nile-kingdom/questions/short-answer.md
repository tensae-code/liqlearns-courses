# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§6 Why Egypt was called the Black Land" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Nile's black silt feeds a kingdom that calls its own soil Black Land and the desert Red Land.

**2.** Tell the story of "§7 How Egypt's pyramids were built" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Thousands haul and stack stone so a pharaoh's tomb rises to outlast him.
