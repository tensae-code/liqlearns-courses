# Ancient egypt what egypt gave — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§9 What Tutankhamun's tomb keeps about Egyptian life" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A boy-king's sealed tomb survives untouched and spills gold, food and furniture as evidence of daily life.

**2.** Tell the story of "§10 Egyptians' religious beliefs and scientific knowledge" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gods, mummies, calendars and medicine — Egypt thinks about eternity and measures time.
