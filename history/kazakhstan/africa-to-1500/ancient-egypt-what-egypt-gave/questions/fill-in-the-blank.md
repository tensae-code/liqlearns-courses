# Ancient egypt what egypt gave — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§9 What Tutankhamun's tomb keeps about Egyptian life" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A boy-king's sealed tomb survives untouched and spills gold, food and furniture as evidence of daily life.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§10 Egyptians' religious beliefs and scientific knowledge" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gods, mummies, calendars and medicine — Egypt thinks about eternity and measures time.
