# From stone tools to farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§2 Why Lascaux cave walls were painted" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Hunter bands paint bulls and hunts deep in the Lascaux cave, leaving the first art as a record of their life.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3 Why people began farming and herding" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gathering fails to feed growing bands, so people plant grain and tame animals and settle down.
