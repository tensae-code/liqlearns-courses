# From stone tools to farming — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§2 Why Lascaux cave walls were painted" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Hunter bands paint bulls and hunts deep in the Lascaux cave, leaving the first art as a record of their life.

**2.** Tell the story of "§3 Why people began farming and herding" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gathering fails to feed growing bands, so people plant grain and tame animals and settle down.
