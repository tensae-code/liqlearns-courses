# Hands-on — The Second World War: Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a chain of three links: a feeling, a rivalry, a war. Label them from this lesson. The chain from nationalism to world war is the background every later chapter stands on.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: European nationalism hardens into imperial rivalry and ignites the First World War (1914–1918) and then the Second World War (1939–1945) — the background against which war comes to Malaya. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
