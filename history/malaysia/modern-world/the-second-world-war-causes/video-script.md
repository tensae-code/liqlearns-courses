# Video script — The Second World War: Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
European nationalism hardens into imperial rivalry and ignites the First World War (1914–1918) and then the Second World War (1939–1945) — the background against which war comes to Malaya.

The background of Malaya's war is Europe's. European nationalism — the same feeling the last lesson tracked, here in its hard form — hardens into imperial rivalry: empires competing for colonies, markets and pride. The rivalry ignites the First World War (1914–1918), and a generation later the Second (1939–1945). Malaya's occupation story begins in this European fire.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
