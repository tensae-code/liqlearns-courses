# The Second World War: Causes

## Do it first

Draw a chain of three links: a feeling, a rivalry, a war. Label them from this lesson. The chain from nationalism to world war is the background every later chapter stands on.

The background of Malaya's war is Europe's. European nationalism — the same feeling the last lesson tracked, here in its hard form — hardens into imperial rivalry: empires competing for colonies, markets and pride. The rivalry ignites the First World War (1914–1918), and a generation later the Second (1939–1945). Malaya's occupation story begins in this European fire.
