# Hands-on — Malay Rulers Answer the West: Bangkok 1909 and Johor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two answers to the same pressure on two slips: 'reject it outright' and 'modernise so well there is no excuse to intervene'. Two states in this lesson choose one each — read for which, and why each might work.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Bangkok Treaty of 1909 splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain — Terengganu's sultan, ulama and chiefs reject it outright — while Johor answers differently: Sultan Abu Bakar's modernisation of administration, finance and law leaves Britain no opening to intervene. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
