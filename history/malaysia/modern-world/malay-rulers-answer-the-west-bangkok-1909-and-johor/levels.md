# Malay Rulers Answer the West: Bangkok 1909 and Johor — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Bangkok Treaty of 1909 splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain — Terengganu's sultan, ulama and chiefs reject it outright — while Johor answers differently: Sultan Abu Bakar's modernisation of administration, finance and law leaves Britain no opening to intervene. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
