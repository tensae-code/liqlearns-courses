# Malay Rulers Answer the West: Bangkok 1909 and Johor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Terengganu's sultan, ulama and chiefs reject the 1909 treaty outright.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Johor falls under a Resident before all other states.
**Answer: False** — Johor's modernisation is designed to leave Britain no opening to intervene.
