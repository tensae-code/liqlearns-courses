# Malay Rulers Answer the West: Bangkok 1909 and Johor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the Bangkok Treaty of 1909 do?
a) splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain  b) founds Singapore  c) ends the Emergency  d) creates the Straits Settlements
**Answer: a) splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is Johor's strategy under Sultan Abu Bakar?
a) reject all treaties and trade  b) invite a Resident early  c) move the capital inland  d) modernise administration, finance and law so Britain has no opening
**Answer: d) modernise administration, finance and law so Britain has no opening** — That is what this lesson teaches.
