# Lesson plan — Malay Rulers Answer the West: Bangkok 1909 and Johor

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Malay Rulers Answer the West: Bangkok 1909 and Johor
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Bangkok Treaty of 1909 splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain — Terengganu's sultan, ulama and chiefs reject it outright — while Johor answers differently: Sultan Abu Bakar's modernisation of administration, finance and law leaves Britain no opening to intervene.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (recommended, never forced).
