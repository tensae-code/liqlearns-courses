# Hands-on — Malayan Resistance to the British

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pin nine names on a map in your mind, one per state. Resistance in this lesson is not one rising but many, each with its own leader and ground — read it as a map, not a single battle.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Stripped of governing, legal and financial power, local leaders rise state by state — Maharaja Lela in Perak, Tok Janggut in Kelantan, Mat Salleh in Sabah, Rentap in Sarawak and many more — winning early successes before Britain crushes the risings one by one. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
