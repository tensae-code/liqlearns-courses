# Video script — Malayan Resistance to the British

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Stripped of governing, legal and financial power, local leaders rise state by state — Maharaja Lela in Perak, Tok Janggut in Kelantan, Mat Salleh in Sabah, Rentap in Sarawak and many more — winning early successes before Britain crushes the risings one by one.

Stripped of governing, legal and financial power, local leaders rise — state by state. Dato' Maharaja Lela in Perak; Yamtuan Antah in Negeri Sembilan; Dol Said in Naning; Dato' Bahaman in Pahang; Tok Janggut in Kelantan; Haji Abdul Rahman Limbong in Terengganu; Rentap and Sharif Masahor in Sarawak; Mat Salleh and Mat Sator in Sabah. The risings win early successes — before Britain crushes them, one by one. The lesson names them all, because the remembering is part of the resistance's result.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
