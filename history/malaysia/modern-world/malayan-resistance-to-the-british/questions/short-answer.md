# Malayan Resistance to the British — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the resistance leaders in Sarawak and Sabah.
**Answer:** Rentap and Sharif Masahor in Sarawak; Mat Salleh and Mat Sator in Sabah.

**2.** How does Britain finally end the risings?
**Answer:** It crushes them one by one.
