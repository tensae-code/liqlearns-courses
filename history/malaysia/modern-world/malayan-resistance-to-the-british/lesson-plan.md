# Lesson plan — Malayan Resistance to the British

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Malayan Resistance to the British
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Stripped of governing, legal and financial power, local leaders rise state by state — Maharaja Lela in Perak, Tok Janggut in Kelantan, Mat Salleh in Sabah, Rentap in Sarawak and many more — winning early successes before Britain crushes the risings one by one.
