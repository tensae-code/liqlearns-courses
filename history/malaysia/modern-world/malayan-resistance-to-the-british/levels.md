# Malayan Resistance to the British — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Stripped of governing, legal and financial power, local leaders rise state by state — Maharaja Lela in Perak, Tok Janggut in Kelantan, Mat Salleh in Sabah, Rentap in Sarawak and many more — winning early successes before Britain crushes the risings one by one. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
