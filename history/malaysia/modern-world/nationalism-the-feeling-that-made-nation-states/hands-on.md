# Hands-on — Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one sentence beginning 'I love my country because…' and one beginning '…so it should govern itself by…'. Joining those two sentences is nationalism, the feeling this lesson tracks across Asia.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Nationalism — deep love of nation and race, driving for political and economic freedom against colonisation — spreads from the West through Asia, liberates the Philippines, Burma, Vietnam and Indonesia, and awakens in Malaya through the ulama-led newspaper Al-Imam (23 July 1906), intellectuals and a flood of writing. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
