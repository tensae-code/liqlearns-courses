# Video script — Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Nationalism — deep love of nation and race, driving for political and economic freedom against colonisation — spreads from the West through Asia, liberates the Philippines, Burma, Vietnam and Indonesia, and awakens in Malaya through the ulama-led newspaper Al-Imam (23 July 1906), intellectuals and a flood of writing.

Nationalism is defined as deep love of nation and race — the drive for political and economic freedom against colonisation. It spreads from the West through Asia: in Southeast Asia it liberates the Philippines, Burma, Vietnam and Indonesia, while Thailand's nationalism defends sovereignty by reform. In Malaya it awakens through print — the ulama-led newspaper Al-Imam (23 July 1906), intellectuals, and a flood of writing. A feeling becomes a movement when it is published.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
