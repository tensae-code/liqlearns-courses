# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Define nationalism as this lesson does.
**Answer:** Deep love of nation and race, driving for political and economic freedom against colonisation.

**2.** Through what does nationalism awaken in Malaya?
