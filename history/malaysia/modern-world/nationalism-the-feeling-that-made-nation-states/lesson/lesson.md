# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States

## Do it first

Write one sentence beginning 'I love my country because…' and one beginning '…so it should govern itself by…'. Joining those two sentences is nationalism, the feeling this lesson tracks across Asia.

Nationalism is defined as deep love of nation and race — the drive for political and economic freedom against colonisation. It spreads from the West through Asia: in Southeast Asia it liberates the Philippines, Burma, Vietnam and Indonesia, while Thailand's nationalism defends sovereignty by reform. In Malaya it awakens through print — the ulama-led newspaper Al-Imam (23 July 1906), intellectuals, and a flood of writing. A feeling becomes a movement when it is published.
