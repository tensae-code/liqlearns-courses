# Lesson plan — Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — War, Occupation and the Road to Merdeka (CURRICULUM.md).
