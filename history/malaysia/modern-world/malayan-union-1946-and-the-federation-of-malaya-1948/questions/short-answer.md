# Malayan Union 1946 and the Federation of Malaya 1948 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the Perlembagaan Rakyat?
**Answer:** The People's Constitution — the alternative constitution proposed by another camp.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What do Sarawak's anti-cession youths found at Sibu?
**Answer:** The secret Rukun 13 (20 August 1948).
