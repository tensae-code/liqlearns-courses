# Malayan Union 1946 and the Federation of Malaya 1948 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Federation is drafted by a Working Committee into the Federation Agreement.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Malayan Union is welcomed by mass rallies of support.
**Answer: False** — Mass protest rallies oppose it — 10,000 at Kota Bharu alone.
