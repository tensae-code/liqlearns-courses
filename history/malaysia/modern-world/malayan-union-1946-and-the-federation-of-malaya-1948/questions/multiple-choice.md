# Malayan Union 1946 and the Federation of Malaya 1948 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who is sent to extract the sultans' signatures for the Malayan Union?
a) Sir Harold MacMichael  b) Raffles  c) James Brooke  d) Sir Donald MacGillivray
**Answer: a) Sir Harold MacMichael** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What replaces the Malayan Union in 1948?
a) the Straits Settlements  b) the Japanese administration  c) the Republic of Singapore  d) the Federation of Malaya
**Answer: d) the Federation of Malaya** — That is what this lesson teaches.
