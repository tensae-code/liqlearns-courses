# Video script — Malayan Union 1946 and the Federation of Malaya 1948

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Britain's Malayan Union of 1946 — signatures extracted from the sultans by Sir Harold MacMichael — is rejected by mass Malay protest, and replaced in 1948 by the Federation of Malaya, drafted into the Federation Agreement, while another camp proposes its own Perlembagaan Rakyat (People's Constitution).

After the British Military Administration, Sir Harold MacMichael is sent to extract the sultans' signatures for the Malayan Union — H.C. Willan scouting ahead of him. The Malays rise in protest: 10,000 rally at Kota Bharu on 15 December 1945 as he arrives, and thousands more in Kedah on 19 January 1946. The same years, Sarawak and Sabah are ceded to the British Crown — and Sarawak's anti-cession youths found the secret Rukun 13 at Sibu (20 August 1948) under Awang Rambli, Morshidi Sidek and Bujang Suntong.
The rejected Malayan Union of 1946 is replaced by the Federation of Malaya, drafted by a Working Committee into the Federation Agreement of 1948. The argument is not one-sided: another camp proposes its own alternative, the Perlembagaan Rakyat — the People's Constitution. Malaya's postwar order is thus argued into being, not simply handed down.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
