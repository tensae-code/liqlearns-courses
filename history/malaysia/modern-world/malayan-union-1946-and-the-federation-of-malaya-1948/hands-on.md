# Hands-on — Malayan Union 1946 and the Federation of Malaya 1948

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a signature being handed over under pressure, then a crowd of ten thousand refusing the paper. The crowd wins in this lesson: a constitution imposed is replaced by one negotiated.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Britain's Malayan Union of 1946 — signatures extracted from the sultans by Sir Harold MacMichael — is rejected by mass Malay protest, and replaced in 1948 by the Federation of Malaya, drafted into the Federation Agreement, while another camp proposes its own Perlembagaan Rakyat (People's Constitution). Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
