# Lesson plan — Malayan Union 1946 and the Federation of Malaya 1948

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Malayan Union 1946 and the Federation of Malaya 1948
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — War, Occupation and the Road to Merdeka (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain's Malayan Union of 1946 — signatures extracted from the sultans by Sir Harold MacMichael — is rejected by mass Malay protest, and replaced in 1948 by the Federation of Malaya, drafted into the Federation Agreement, while another camp proposes its own Perlembagaan Rakyat (People's Constitution).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — War, Occupation and the Road to Merdeka (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab 4: the Malayan Union fight and the cessions** — After the British Military Administration, Sir Harold MacMichael is sent to extract the sultans' signatures for the Malayan Union — H.C.
2. **Bab 5: the Federation of Malaya 1948** — The rejected Malayan Union of 1946 is replaced by the Federation of Malaya, drafted by a Working Committee into the Federation Agreement of 1948.
