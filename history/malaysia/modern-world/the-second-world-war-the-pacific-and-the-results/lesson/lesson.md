# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

## Do it first

Turn your map east. Europe's war becomes a Pacific war in this lesson: mark Japan, the Pacific, mainland Asia and the Southeast Asian islands, and say in what years the fire spreads.

The war comes east. Japan emerges as a military, imperial power in the Asia-Pacific, and the war spreads across the Pacific, mainland Asia and the Southeast Asian islands (1941–1945). For Malaya this is the doorway chapter: the world war is no longer a European background — it is arriving, by sea and air, at the peninsula itself.
