# Hands-on — The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Turn your map east. Europe's war becomes a Pacific war in this lesson: mark Japan, the Pacific, mainland Asia and the Southeast Asian islands, and say in what years the fire spreads.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Japan emerges as a military, imperial power in the Asia-Pacific, and the war spreads across the Pacific, mainland Asia and the Southeast Asian islands (1941–1945) — bringing the war to Malaya's own shores. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
