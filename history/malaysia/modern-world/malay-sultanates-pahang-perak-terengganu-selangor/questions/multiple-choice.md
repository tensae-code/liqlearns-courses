# Malay Sultanates: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers which laws?
a) Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99  b) the Madina Charter  c) the Pangkor Treaty  d) the Undang-Undang Laut only
**Answer: a) Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does Selangor's Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah build?
a) a fleet of warships  b) the Colosseum  c) canals to Siam  d) roads and railways across Kuala Lumpur and Klang
**Answer: d) roads and railways across Kuala Lumpur and Klang** — That is what this lesson teaches.
