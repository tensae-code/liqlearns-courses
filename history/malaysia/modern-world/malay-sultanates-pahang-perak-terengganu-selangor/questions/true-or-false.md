# Malay Sultanates: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Perak's law is called the Undang-Undang 99.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Melaka's law has no descendants in the other sultanates.
**Answer: False** — Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers the laws of Pahang and Perak.
