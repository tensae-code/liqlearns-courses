# Lesson plan — Malay Sultanates: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Malay Sultanates: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Four more sultanates carry Melaka's inheritance — its law above all: Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99 — while Perak's Iskandar Dzulkarnain tightens administration and Selangor's Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah builds roads and railways and is honoured with a Golden Jubilee.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (recommended, never forced).
