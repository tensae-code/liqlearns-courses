# Hands-on — Malay Sultanates: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Copy one family rule onto a fresh page in your own book, changed a little for your house. That copying-with-change is how Melaka's law lives on in Pahang and Perak in this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Four more sultanates carry Melaka's inheritance — its law above all: Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99 — while Perak's Iskandar Dzulkarnain tightens administration and Selangor's Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah builds roads and railways and is honoured with a Golden Jubilee. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
