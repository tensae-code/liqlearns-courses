# Malay Sultanates: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor

## Do it first

Copy one family rule onto a fresh page in your own book, changed a little for your house. That copying-with-change is how Melaka's law lives on in Pahang and Perak in this lesson.

Four more sultanates carry Melaka's inheritance — its law above all. Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99. The states also govern in their own persons: Perak's 15th sultan, Iskandar Dzulkarnain (1752–1765), tightens the state's administration; and Selangor's Sultan Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah (1898–1938) builds roads and railways across Kuala Lumpur and Klang and is honoured with a Golden Jubilee at Klang.
