# Video script — Malay Sultanates: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Four more sultanates carry Melaka's inheritance — its law above all: Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99 — while Perak's Iskandar Dzulkarnain tightens administration and Selangor's Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah builds roads and railways and is honoured with a Golden Jubilee.

Four more sultanates carry Melaka's inheritance — its law above all. Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99. The states also govern in their own persons: Perak's 15th sultan, Iskandar Dzulkarnain (1752–1765), tightens the state's administration; and Selangor's Sultan Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah (1898–1938) builds roads and railways across Kuala Lumpur and Klang and is honoured with a Golden Jubilee at Klang.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
