# Malay Sultanates: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Four more sultanates carry Melaka's inheritance — its law above all: Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99 — while Perak's Iskandar Dzulkarnain tightens administration and Selangor's Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah builds roads and railways and is honoured with a Golden Jubilee. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
