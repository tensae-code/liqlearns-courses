# Sarawak and Sabah: Local Governments Before Brooke and North Borneo — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Before Brooke and the Company, Borneo is already governed: Brunei's sultanate draws Sarawak's trade, the Sulu Sultanate lifts north Sabah's trade in bird's nests for China, the wakil raja system orders Brunei's lands, free chiefs like Syarif Osman rule in Sabah, and the bumiputera peoples order their lives by the rivers. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
