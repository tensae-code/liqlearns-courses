# Sarawak and Sabah: Local Governments Before Brooke and North Borneo — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The wakil raja system is used by Brunei to govern.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Sarawak and Sabah have no rulers or chiefs before Brooke.
**Answer: False** — Brunei, Sulu and free chiefs all govern parts of Borneo before Brooke.
