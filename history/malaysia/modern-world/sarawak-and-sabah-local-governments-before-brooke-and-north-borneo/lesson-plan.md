# Lesson plan — Sarawak and Sabah: Local Governments Before Brooke and North Borneo

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Sarawak and Sabah: Local Governments Before Brooke and North Borneo
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before Brooke and the Company, Borneo is already governed: Brunei's sultanate draws Sarawak's trade, the Sulu Sultanate lifts north Sabah's trade in bird's nests for China, the wakil raja system orders Brunei's lands, free chiefs like Syarif Osman rule in Sabah, and the bumiputera peoples order their lives by the rivers.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (recommended, never forced).
