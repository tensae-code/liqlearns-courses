# Video script — Sarawak and Sabah: Local Governments Before Brooke and North Borneo

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Before Brooke and the Company, Borneo is already governed: Brunei's sultanate draws Sarawak's trade, the Sulu Sultanate lifts north Sabah's trade in bird's nests for China, the wakil raja system orders Brunei's lands, free chiefs like Syarif Osman rule in Sabah, and the bumiputera peoples order their lives by the rivers.

Borneo before the Europeans is a governed land. Brunei's sultanate rises and draws Sarawak's trade to itself, while the Sulu Sultanate (15th century) lifts north Sabah's trade in sea and forest produce — bird's nests for China above all. Government runs through the wakil raja system, inherited from Majapahit and Sambas and used by Brunei; in Sabah it runs through free chiefs, ketua bebas, like Syarif Osman at Marudu and Datu Kurunding at Tungku. And the bumiputera peoples — Orang Ulu (Kayan, Kenyah, Kelabit, Penan, Punan), Bidayuh, Melanau, Kadazandusun, Bajau, Suluk and the Murut, 'hill people' in Brunei's naming — order their lives by the rivers.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
