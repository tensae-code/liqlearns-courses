# Hands-on — Sarawak and Sabah: Local Governments Before Brooke and North Borneo

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a river and mark who decides things at its mouth, its middle and its headwaters. Government in Borneo, in this lesson, already runs along the rivers before any European arrives.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Before Brooke and the Company, Borneo is already governed: Brunei's sultanate draws Sarawak's trade, the Sulu Sultanate lifts north Sabah's trade in bird's nests for China, the wakil raja system orders Brunei's lands, free chiefs like Syarif Osman rule in Sabah, and the bumiputera peoples order their lives by the rivers. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
