# Video script — The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories, survive Acheh, Jambi and internal Bendahara–Laksamana struggles by moving capitals, building forts and allying with the Dutch, and flower again as a trade centre and a centre of Malay letters guarded by the Orang Laut.

Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau, with Melaka's government and territories. It is tested from every side: the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle — Tun Habib Abdul Majid against Tun Abdul Jamil, who withdraws to Terengganu — and Raja Kechil's threat. It survives by moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch. Then it flowers again: a trade centre reviving commerce with Arabia, India and China, a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
