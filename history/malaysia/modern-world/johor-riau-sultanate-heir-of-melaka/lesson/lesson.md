# The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka

## Do it first

Take one family heirloom — a recipe, a song, a tool — and say how it proves the family continues in a new house. Johor Riau is Melaka's heir in exactly that way in this lesson: new capital, same government and law.

Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau, with Melaka's government and territories. It is tested from every side: the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle — Tun Habib Abdul Majid against Tun Abdul Jamil, who withdraws to Terengganu — and Raja Kechil's threat. It survives by moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch. Then it flowers again: a trade centre reviving commerce with Arabia, India and China, a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut.
