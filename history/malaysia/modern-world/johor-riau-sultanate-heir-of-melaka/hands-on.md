# Hands-on — The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one family heirloom — a recipe, a song, a tool — and say how it proves the family continues in a new house. Johor Riau is Melaka's heir in exactly that way in this lesson: new capital, same government and law.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories, survive Acheh, Jambi and internal Bendahara–Laksamana struggles by moving capitals, building forts and allying with the Dutch, and flower again as a trade centre and a centre of Malay letters guarded by the Orang Laut. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
