# The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories, survive Acheh, Jambi and internal Bendahara–Laksamana struggles by moving capitals, building forts and allying with the Dutch, and flower again as a trade centre and a centre of Malay letters guarded by the Orang Laut. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
