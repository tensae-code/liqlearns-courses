# Lesson plan — The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories, survive Acheh, Jambi and internal Bendahara–Laksamana struggles by moving capitals, building forts and allying with the Dutch, and flower again as a trade centre and a centre of Malay letters guarded by the Orang Laut.
