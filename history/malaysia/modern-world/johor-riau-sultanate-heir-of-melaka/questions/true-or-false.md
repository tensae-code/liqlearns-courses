# The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Johor Riau flowers again as a centre of Malay letters.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Johor Riau is destroyed in its first war and never recovers.
**Answer: False** — It survives its wars and flowers again as a trade centre.
