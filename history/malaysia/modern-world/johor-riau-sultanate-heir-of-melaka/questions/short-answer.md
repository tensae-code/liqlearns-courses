# The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two dangers Johor Riau faces.
**Answer:** Any two of: the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle, Raja Kechil's threat.

**2.** Who guards Johor Riau at sea?
**Answer:** The Orang Laut.
