# The Johor Riau Sultanate: Heir of Melaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Johor Riau carries forward what from Melaka?
a) Melaka's government and territories  b) nothing at all  c) only its port taxes  d) the Portuguese garrison
**Answer: a) Melaka's government and territories** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does Johor Riau survive its many rivals?
a) by hiding inland forever  b) by giving up trade  c) by disbanding its fleet  d) moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch
**Answer: d) moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch** — That is what this lesson teaches.
