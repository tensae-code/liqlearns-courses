# Japanese Occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** English and Chinese schools are closed under the occupation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Japan surrenders before the atomic bombs of August 1945.
**Answer: False** — The bombs of 6 and 9 August 1945 end the war; Japan surrenders on 15 August.
