# Japanese Occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945

## Do it first

Write two columns: 'what the occupier imposes' and 'how the people answer'. Fill both from this lesson — occupation is only half its story; resistance is the other half.

Japan occupies the country and rules by Nippon Seishin — the Japanese spirit imposed on daily life: English and Chinese schools closed, the Japanese language imposed, the Emperor revered. The people fight back. Force 136 is raised in 1943 under South East Asia Command at Colombo, lands in Ulu Perak, and raises the Askar Melayu Setia with Kapten Mohd Salleh Hj. Sulaiman and the Gerik District Officer. Individuals resist too — Gurchan Singh, the 'Lion of Malaya', leafleting against the occupation, and Sybil Kathigasu. The atomic bombs of 6 and 9 August 1945 end it; Japan surrenders on 15 August.
