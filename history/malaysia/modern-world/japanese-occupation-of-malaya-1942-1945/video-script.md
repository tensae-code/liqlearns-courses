# Video script — Japanese Occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Japan occupies the country and rules by Nippon Seishin — English and Chinese schools closed, Japanese imposed, the Emperor revered — while the people fight back through Force 136 and heroes like Gurchan Singh and Sybil Kathigasu, until the atomic bombs of 6 and 9 August 1945 end the occupation.

Japan occupies the country and rules by Nippon Seishin — the Japanese spirit imposed on daily life: English and Chinese schools closed, the Japanese language imposed, the Emperor revered. The people fight back. Force 136 is raised in 1943 under South East Asia Command at Colombo, lands in Ulu Perak, and raises the Askar Melayu Setia with Kapten Mohd Salleh Hj. Sulaiman and the Gerik District Officer. Individuals resist too — Gurchan Singh, the 'Lion of Malaya', leafleting against the occupation, and Sybil Kathigasu. The atomic bombs of 6 and 9 August 1945 end it; Japan surrenders on 15 August.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
