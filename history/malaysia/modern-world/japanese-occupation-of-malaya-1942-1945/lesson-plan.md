# Lesson plan — Japanese Occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Japanese Occupation of Malaya, 1942–1945
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — War, Occupation and the Road to Merdeka (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Japan occupies the country and rules by Nippon Seishin — English and Chinese schools closed, Japanese imposed, the Emperor revered — while the people fight back through Force 136 and heroes like Gurchan Singh and Sybil Kathigasu, until the atomic bombs of 6 and 9 August 1945 end the occupation.
