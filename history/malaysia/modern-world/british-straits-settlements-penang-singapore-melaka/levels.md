# British Straits Settlements: Penang, Singapore and Melaka — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore in January 1819 and sign a preliminary treaty with Temenggung Abdul Rahman — who insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification — and the London Treaty of 1824 splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres, joining Penang, Singapore and Melaka as the Straits Settlements. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
