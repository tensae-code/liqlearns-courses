# Lesson plan — British Straits Settlements: Penang, Singapore and Melaka

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** British Straits Settlements: Penang, Singapore and Melaka
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore in January 1819 and sign a preliminary treaty with Temenggung Abdul Rahman — who insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification — and the London Treaty of 1824 splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres, joining Penang, Singapore and Melaka as the Straits Settlements.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (recommended, never forced).
