# Video script — British Straits Settlements: Penang, Singapore and Melaka

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore in January 1819 and sign a preliminary treaty with Temenggung Abdul Rahman — who insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification — and the London Treaty of 1824 splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres, joining Penang, Singapore and Melaka as the Straits Settlements.

In January 1819 Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore and sign a preliminary treaty with Temenggung Abdul Rahman — who insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification. The wider rivalry is settled in Europe: the London Treaty of 1824 splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres. Penang, Singapore and Melaka are then governed together as the Straits Settlements — Britain's three ports on the strait.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
