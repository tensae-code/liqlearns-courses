# British Straits Settlements: Penang, Singapore and Melaka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the 1819 treaty count as only preliminary?
**Answer:** Because Temenggung Abdul Rahman insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification.

**2.** Name the three Straits Settlements.
**Answer:** Penang, Singapore and Melaka.
