# British Straits Settlements: Penang, Singapore and Melaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who lands in Singapore in January 1819?
a) Raffles and Farquhar  b) Brooke and Hashim  c) Raffles and Brooke  d) Mahmud Shah and Farquhar
**Answer: a) Raffles and Farquhar** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the London Treaty of 1824 do?
a) founds the Federation  b) ends the Emergency  c) creates Malaysia  d) splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres
**Answer: d) splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres** — That is what this lesson teaches.
