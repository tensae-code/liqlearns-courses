# British Straits Settlements: Penang, Singapore and Melaka — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Penang, Singapore and Melaka are governed together as the Straits Settlements.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Temenggung Abdul Rahman signs a final treaty needing nobody else's agreement.
**Answer: False** — He signs a preliminary treaty and insists on the sultan's ratification.
