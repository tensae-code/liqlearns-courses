# British Straits Settlements: Penang, Singapore and Melaka

## Do it first

Draw a line down a map of the Malay world. Label one side British, one Dutch, the year 1824 on the line. One treaty line remakes a whole region in this lesson — draw it before you read.

In January 1819 Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore and sign a preliminary treaty with Temenggung Abdul Rahman — who insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification. The wider rivalry is settled in Europe: the London Treaty of 1824 splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres. Penang, Singapore and Melaka are then governed together as the Straits Settlements — Britain's three ports on the strait.
