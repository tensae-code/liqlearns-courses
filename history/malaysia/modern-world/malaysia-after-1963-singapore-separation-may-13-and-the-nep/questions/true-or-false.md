# Malaysia After 1963: Singapore Separation, May 13 and the NEP — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) The Rukun Negara is proclaimed as the nation's unity charter.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The New Economic Policy runs from 1963 to 1965.
**Answer: False** — The New Economic Policy (Dasar Ekonomi Baru) runs 1970–1990.
