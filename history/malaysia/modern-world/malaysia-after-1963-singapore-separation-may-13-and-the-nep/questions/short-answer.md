# Malaysia After 1963: Singapore Separation, May 13 and the NEP — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What two aims does the New Economic Policy set out?
**Answer:** To eradicate poverty and to restructure society's economic imbalances.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who heads MAGERAN, and how long does it govern?
**Answer:** Tun Abdul Razak Hussein; from 16 May 1969 to 23 February 1971.
