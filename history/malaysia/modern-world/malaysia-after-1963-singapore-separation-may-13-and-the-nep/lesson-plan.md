# Lesson plan — Malaysia After 1963: Singapore Separation, May 13 and the NEP

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Malaysia After 1963: Singapore Separation, May 13 and the NEP
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Malaysia Formed and Rebuilt (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Malaysia is stress-tested after 1963 — Singapore separates in August 1965, the communist insurgency renews in Sarawak and the peninsula, and the race riots of 13 May 1969 bring MAGERAN's emergency rule — then rebuilt through the Rukun Negara, education re-knit around Bahasa Melayu, and the New Economic Policy (1970–1990) against poverty and imbalance.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Malaysia Formed and Rebuilt (recommended, never forced).
