# The OIC Founded After the 1969 al-Aqsa Arson — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded on 25 September 1969 at the first Islamic summit in Rabat, Morocco, in direct response to the attack on al-Aqsa Mosque — and Malaysia takes its seat among what becomes 57 member states. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
