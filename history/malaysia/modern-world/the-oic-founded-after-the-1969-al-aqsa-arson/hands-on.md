# Hands-on — The OIC Founded After the 1969 al-Aqsa Arson

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Read the date and place aloud three times: 25 September 1969, Rabat. A world organisation in this lesson is born from one attack and one summit — Malaysia's school story ends looking outward at the world.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded on 25 September 1969 at the first Islamic summit in Rabat, Morocco, in direct response to the attack on al-Aqsa Mosque — and Malaysia takes its seat among what becomes 57 member states. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
