# The OIC Founded After the 1969 al-Aqsa Arson

## Do it first

Read the date and place aloud three times: 25 September 1969, Rabat. A world organisation in this lesson is born from one attack and one summit — Malaysia's school story ends looking outward at the world.

Malaysia's school story ends on the world stage. The Organisation of Islamic Cooperation is founded on 25 September 1969, at the first Islamic summit in Rabat, Morocco — in direct response to the Zionist attack on al-Aqsa Mosque. Malaysia takes its seat among what becomes 57 member states: a nation built at home in the earlier chapters now acts abroad, in a body born from a shared shock.
