# The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The MCP turns to armed struggle after the Calcutta conferences of February–March 1948, and the state answers with Emergency laws, national registration, the Briggs Plan's resettlement, amnesty offers, curfews, psychological warfare and the Baling Talks — and raises the Senoi Praaq, Orang Asli trackers, to hunt in the deep jungle. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
