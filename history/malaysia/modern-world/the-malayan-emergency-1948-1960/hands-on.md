# Hands-on — The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List the state's answers in two kinds: force (laws, curfews, jungle trackers) and persuasion (amnesty, talks, psychological warfare). The Emergency in this lesson is fought with both at once.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The MCP turns to armed struggle after the Calcutta conferences of February–March 1948, and the state answers with Emergency laws, national registration, the Briggs Plan's resettlement, amnesty offers, curfews, psychological warfare and the Baling Talks — and raises the Senoi Praaq, Orang Asli trackers, to hunt in the deep jungle. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
