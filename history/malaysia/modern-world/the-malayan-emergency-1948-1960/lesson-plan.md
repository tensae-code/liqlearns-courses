# Lesson plan — The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — War, Occupation and the Road to Merdeka (CURRICULUM.md).
