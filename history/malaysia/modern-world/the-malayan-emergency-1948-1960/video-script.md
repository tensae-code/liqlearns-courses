# Video script — The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The MCP turns to armed struggle after the Calcutta conferences of February–March 1948, and the state answers with Emergency laws, national registration, the Briggs Plan's resettlement, amnesty offers, curfews, psychological warfare and the Baling Talks — and raises the Senoi Praaq, Orang Asli trackers, to hunt in the deep jungle.

The MCP's delegates attend the Calcutta conferences of February–March 1948, and the party turns to armed struggle. The state answers on many fronts at once: Emergency laws, national registration, the Briggs Plan's resettlement of squatters into new villages, amnesty offers, curfews and psychological warfare, and the Baling Talks. For the deep jungle it raises the Senoi Praaq (1956) — Orang Asli Senoi, Semai and Temuan trackers, the 'Silent Killers'. Twelve years of emergency are fought with paper, wire, words and trackers together.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
