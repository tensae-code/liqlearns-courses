# The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four of the state's answers to the Emergency.
**Answer:** Any four of: Emergency laws, national registration, the Briggs Plan's resettlement, amnesty offers, curfews, psychological warfare, the Baling Talks, the Senoi Praaq.

**2.** What are the Senoi Praaq nicknamed, and why?
**Answer:** The 'Silent Killers' — Orang Asli trackers who hunt silently in the deep jungle.
