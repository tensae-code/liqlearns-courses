# The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Senoi Praaq are Orang Asli trackers raised in 1956.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The state offers no amnesty during the Emergency.
**Answer: False** — Amnesty offers are among the state's answers.
