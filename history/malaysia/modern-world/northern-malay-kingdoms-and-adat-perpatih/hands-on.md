# Hands-on — Northern Malay Kingdoms and Adat Perpatih

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw your family line two ways: down through fathers, then down through mothers. Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih in this lesson follows the mother's line — say one thing that changes when inheritance follows mothers.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The northern kingdoms — Kedah, Kelantan, Perlis — leave a living heritage of poems, hikayat, wau kites, batik and wayang kulit, while Negeri Sembilan's Minangkabau settlers bring Adat Perpatih, the matrilineal custom where descent and inheritance run through the mother and four Undang elect the Yamtuan Besar. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
