# Northern Malay Kingdoms and Adat Perpatih — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Wayang kulit is raised to national heritage.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Adat Perpatih is a patrilineal custom from Java.
**Answer: False** — Adat Perpatih is the matrilineal custom brought by Minangkabau migrants.
