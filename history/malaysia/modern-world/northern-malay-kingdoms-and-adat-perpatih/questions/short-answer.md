# Northern Malay Kingdoms and Adat Perpatih — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name two items of the northern kingdoms' living heritage.
**Answer:** Any two of: the Syair Sultan Maulana, the Hikayat Seri Kelantan, wau kites, batik, wayang kulit.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Where do the Minangkabau migrants settle in Negeri Sembilan?
**Answer:** Rembau, Sungai Ujong, Johol and Naning.
