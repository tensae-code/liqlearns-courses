# Northern Malay Kingdoms and Adat Perpatih — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Under Adat Perpatih, descent and inheritance run through whom?
a) the mother  b) the father  c) the eldest son only  d) the district officer
**Answer: a) the mother** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who elect the Yamtuan Besar?
a) the people by general election  b) the British Resident  c) the Bendahara of Johor  d) the four Undang
**Answer: d) the four Undang** — That is what this lesson teaches.
