# Lesson plan — Northern Malay Kingdoms and Adat Perpatih

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Northern Malay Kingdoms and Adat Perpatih
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The northern kingdoms — Kedah, Kelantan, Perlis — leave a living heritage of poems, hikayat, wau kites, batik and wayang kulit, while Negeri Sembilan's Minangkabau settlers bring Adat Perpatih, the matrilineal custom where descent and inheritance run through the mother and four Undang elect the Yamtuan Besar.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab 8–9.1: Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage** — The northern kingdoms deal with their Malay neighbours and leave a living heritage.
2. **Bab 9.2: Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih** — Minangkabau migrants settle Rembau, Sungai Ujong, Johol and Naning, bringing Adat Perpatih — the matrilineal custom.
