# Video script — Colonialism and Its End

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Western administration transforms the land: a modern economy of large-scale tin mining and estate agriculture brings immigrant labour, new towns and a money economy — and a widening gap between the modern sector and the village world.

What does Western rule actually change? The book's answer is the economy and the society itself. The West introduces a modern economy — large-scale tin mining and estate agriculture — and with it a transformed society: immigrant labour, new towns, a money economy, and widening gaps between the modern sector and the village world. Administration is the surface; transformation is the depth.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
