# Lesson plan — Colonialism and Its End

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Colonialism and Its End
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Western administration transforms the land: a modern economy of large-scale tin mining and estate agriculture brings immigrant labour, new towns and a money economy — and a widening gap between the modern sector and the village world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (recommended, never forced).
