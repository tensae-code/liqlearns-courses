# Colonialism and Its End — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What two industries anchor the new modern economy?
a) large-scale tin mining and estate agriculture  b) fishing and pottery  c) shipbuilding and silk  d) tourism and banking
**Answer: a) large-scale tin mining and estate agriculture** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What gap widens under the new economy?
a) between two rivers  b) between north and south coasts  c) between two dynasties  d) between the modern sector and the village world
**Answer: d) between the modern sector and the village world** — That is what this lesson teaches.
