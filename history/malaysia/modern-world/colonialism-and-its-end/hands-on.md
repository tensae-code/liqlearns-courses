# Hands-on — Colonialism and Its End

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw two circles: a mine-and-estate town with wages and shops, and a village with its own fields. Draw the gap between them and label what crosses it and what does not. That gap is this lesson's warning.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Western administration transforms the land: a modern economy of large-scale tin mining and estate agriculture brings immigrant labour, new towns and a money economy — and a widening gap between the modern sector and the village world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
