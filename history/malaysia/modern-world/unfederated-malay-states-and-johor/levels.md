# Unfederated Malay States and Johor — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Perlis, Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu live first under Siam's shadow before British influence reaches them, and Johor follows its own path — kept outside the federation, these five sultanates retain more of their rulers' hand in administration. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
