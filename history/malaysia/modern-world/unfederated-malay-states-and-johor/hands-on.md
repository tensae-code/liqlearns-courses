# Hands-on — Unfederated Malay States and Johor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Put two lists side by side: states inside a federation, states outside it. This lesson is the outside list — five sultanates that keep more of their rulers' hand. Notice what 'outside' protects.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Perlis, Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu live first under Siam's shadow before British influence reaches them, and Johor follows its own path — kept outside the federation, these five sultanates retain more of their rulers' hand in administration. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
