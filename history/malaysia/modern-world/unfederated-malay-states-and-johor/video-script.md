# Video script — Unfederated Malay States and Johor

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Perlis, Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu live first under Siam's shadow before British influence reaches them, and Johor follows its own path — kept outside the federation, these five sultanates retain more of their rulers' hand in administration.

Not every state is federated. Perlis, Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu live first under Siam's shadow, before British influence reaches them; and Johor follows its own path in the south. Kept outside the federation, these five sultanates retain more of their rulers' hand in administration — the same British century, a different measure of control.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
