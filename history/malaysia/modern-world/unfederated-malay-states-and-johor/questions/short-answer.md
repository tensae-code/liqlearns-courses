# Unfederated Malay States and Johor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the five Unfederated Malay States.
**Answer:** Perlis, Kedah, Kelantan, Terengganu and Johor.

**2.** What do the unfederated states retain more of?
**Answer:** Their rulers' hand in administration.
