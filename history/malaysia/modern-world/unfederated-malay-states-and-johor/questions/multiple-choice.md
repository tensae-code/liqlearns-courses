# Unfederated Malay States and Johor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is an Unfederated Malay State?
a) Kelantan  b) Perak  c) Selangor  d) Pahang
**Answer: a) Kelantan** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Under whose shadow do the four northern states live first?
a) Holland's  b) Portugal's  c) Japan's  d) Siam's
**Answer: d) Siam's** — That is what this lesson teaches.
