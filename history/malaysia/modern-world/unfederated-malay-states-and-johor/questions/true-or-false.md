# Unfederated Malay States and Johor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Johor follows its own path, outside the federation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The unfederated states keep less of their rulers' hand than the federated ones.
**Answer: False** — Kept outside the federation, they retain more of their rulers' hand in administration.
