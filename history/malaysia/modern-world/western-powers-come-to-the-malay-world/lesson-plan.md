# Lesson plan — Western Powers Come to the Malay World

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Western Powers Come to the Malay World
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The West comes to a prosperous country — strategic ports trading minerals, fertile soil and sea produce, with a flourishing intellectual life — because industrial revolutions demand markets and raw materials, the Suez Canal and steamship shorten the road east, and the Western powers race one another with trade, treaty and force.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Western Powers and Colonial Malaya (recommended, never forced).
