# Western Powers Come to the Malay World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why do the industrial powers need the Malay world?
a) for markets and raw materials  b) for winter harbours  c) for soldiers only  d) for universities
**Answer: a) for markets and raw materials** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What two inventions shorten the road east?
a) the aeroplane and radio  b) the railway and telegraph only  c) the compass and astrolabe  d) the Suez Canal and the steamship
**Answer: d) the Suez Canal and the steamship** — That is what this lesson teaches.
