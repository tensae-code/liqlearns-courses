# Western Powers Come to the Malay World — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The West comes to a prosperous country — strategic ports trading minerals, fertile soil and sea produce, with a flourishing intellectual life — because industrial revolutions demand markets and raw materials, the Suez Canal and steamship shorten the road east, and the Western powers race one another with trade, treaty and force. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
