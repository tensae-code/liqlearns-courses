# Video script — Western Powers Come to the Malay World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The West comes to a prosperous country — strategic ports trading minerals, fertile soil and sea produce, with a flourishing intellectual life — because industrial revolutions demand markets and raw materials, the Suez Canal and steamship shorten the road east, and the Western powers race one another with trade, treaty and force.

The book first shows the country as it is: prosperous — strategic ports trading its minerals, fertile soil and sea produce to the world, with a flourishing intellectual life. Then the pressure builds from outside. Britain's Industrial Revolution — and by the 1870s Germany's and France's — demands markets and raw materials. The Suez Canal and the steamship shorten the road east. And the Western powers race one another, each with its own strategy of trade, treaty and force. The arrival is explained before it is narrated.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
