# Lesson plan — Melaka Falls to the Portuguese, 1511

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Melaka Falls to the Portuguese, 1511
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In 1511 the Portuguese take Melaka; Sultan Mahmud Shah retreats through his dependencies to Bentan, attacks three times between 1515 and 1519 to win it back, and withdraws to Kampar in 1526 — the sultanate ends in Melaka but not in exile.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (recommended, never forced).
