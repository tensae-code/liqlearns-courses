# Video script — Melaka Falls to the Portuguese, 1511

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
In 1511 the Portuguese take Melaka; Sultan Mahmud Shah retreats through his dependencies to Bentan, attacks three times between 1515 and 1519 to win it back, and withdraws to Kampar in 1526 — the sultanate ends in Melaka but not in exile.

In 1511 the Portuguese take Melaka. Sultan Mahmud Shah does not surrender his claim: he retreats through his dependencies to Bentan, and between 1515 and 1519 attacks three times to win Melaka back. In 1526 the Portuguese destroy Bentan, and he withdraws to Kampar. The sultanate ends in Melaka — but not in exile: the court's line, law and claim travel with the sultan.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
