# Melaka Falls to the Portuguese, 1511 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens to Bentan in 1526?
**Answer:** The Portuguese destroy it, and the sultan withdraws to Kampar.

**2.** In what sense does the sultanate not end in 1511?
**Answer:** It ends in Melaka but continues in exile — the sultan's line, law and claim travel with him.
