# Melaka Falls to the Portuguese, 1511 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mahmud Shah attacks three times to win Melaka back.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Mahmud Shah never tries to retake Melaka.
**Answer: False** — He attacks three times between 1515 and 1519.
