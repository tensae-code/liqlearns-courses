# Melaka Falls to the Portuguese, 1511

## Do it first

Draw Melaka, then draw an arrow retreating from it to Bentan, and three arrows striking back. The arrows that fail are still part of the story — this lesson is about a fall that does not end a line of kings.

In 1511 the Portuguese take Melaka. Sultan Mahmud Shah does not surrender his claim: he retreats through his dependencies to Bentan, and between 1515 and 1519 attacks three times to win Melaka back. In 1526 the Portuguese destroy Bentan, and he withdraws to Kampar. The sultanate ends in Melaka — but not in exile: the court's line, law and claim travel with the sultan.
