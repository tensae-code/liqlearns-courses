# Video script — Formation of Malaysia, 1963

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Tunku Abdul Rahman announces the Malaysia idea on 27 May 1961; Singapore votes to join in its 1962 referendum, the Inter-Governmental Committee writes Sabah and Sarawak's safeguards into the constitution, U Thant's UN mission verifies the Borneo peoples' wishes, and Malaysia is proclaimed in 1963 — the constitution bending again in 1965 when Singapore leaves.

The Federal Constitution — grown from the Malay states' own laws — is amended in 1963 to admit Singapore, Sabah and Sarawak into a wider federation. And it is amended again in 1965 when Singapore leaves. The constitution is the federation's frame, and the frame bends as the federation changes shape.
Tunku Abdul Rahman announces the Malaysia idea on 27 May 1961. Singapore votes to join in its referendum of early September 1962. The Inter-Governmental Committee (August 1962) writes the safeguards for Sarawak and Sabah into the constitutional frame. Indonesia protests at the United Nations — and U Thant's UN Malaysia Mission under Laurence Michelmore (12 August 1963) verifies the Borneo peoples' wishes. The July 1963 Agreement is signed and Malaysia is proclaimed. Then Indonesia's Konfrontasi is fought off diplomatically, the counter-campaign run by the Foreign Ministry under Muhammad Ghazali Shafie.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
