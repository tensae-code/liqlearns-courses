# Formation of Malaysia, 1963 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Singapore votes to join in a referendum in September 1962.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sabah and Sarawak receive no safeguards in the new federation.
**Answer: False** — The Inter-Governmental Committee writes their safeguards into the constitutional frame.
