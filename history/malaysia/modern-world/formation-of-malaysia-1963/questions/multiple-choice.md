# Formation of Malaysia, 1963 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When does Tunku Abdul Rahman announce the Malaysia idea?
a) 27 May 1961  b) 31 August 1957  c) 9 August 1965  d) 27 May 1971
**Answer: a) 27 May 1961** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the UN Malaysia Mission verify?
a) the tin output  b) the election results of 1955  c) Singapore's port trade  d) the Borneo peoples' wishes
**Answer: d) the Borneo peoples' wishes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
