# Hands-on — Formation of Malaysia, 1963

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Assemble a puzzle of four pieces — Malaya, Singapore, Sabah, Sarawak — then remove one piece two years later. Malaysia in this lesson is assembled by votes, safeguards and verification, then changed by a departure.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Tunku Abdul Rahman announces the Malaysia idea on 27 May 1961; Singapore votes to join in its 1962 referendum, the Inter-Governmental Committee writes Sabah and Sarawak's safeguards into the constitution, U Thant's UN mission verifies the Borneo peoples' wishes, and Malaysia is proclaimed in 1963 — the constitution bending again in 1965 when Singapore leaves. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
