# Lesson plan — Formation of Malaysia, 1963

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Formation of Malaysia, 1963
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Malaysia Formed and Rebuilt (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Tunku Abdul Rahman announces the Malaysia idea on 27 May 1961; Singapore votes to join in its 1962 referendum, the Inter-Governmental Committee writes Sabah and Sarawak's safeguards into the constitution, U Thant's UN mission verifies the Borneo peoples' wishes, and Malaysia is proclaimed in 1963 — the constitution bending again in 1965 when Singapore leaves.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Malaysia Formed and Rebuilt (recommended, never forced).
