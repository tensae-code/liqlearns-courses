# Federated Malay States and the Resident System

## Do it first

Weigh something small and grey in your hand — a spoon will do: it is mostly tin's cousin in the kitchen. The metal in the Larut mines pulls an empire into four states in this lesson.

Tin makes the west-coast states rich: Larut's mines from the 1840s, worked by Chinese miners, raise Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan into world tin leaders. Britain binds the rulers by treaty — beginning with the Pangkor Treaty of 1874 in Perak — and installs Residents whose 'advice' must be followed in government. Then it federates four states: Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang. Wealth draws the treaties; the treaties bring the Residents; the Residents bring the federation.
