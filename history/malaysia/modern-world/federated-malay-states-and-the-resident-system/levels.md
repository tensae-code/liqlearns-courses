# Federated Malay States and the Resident System — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Tin makes the west-coast states rich — Larut's mines and Chinese miners raise Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan into world tin leaders — and Britain binds the rulers by treaty from Pangkor in 1874, installs Residents whose 'advice' must be followed, and federates Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
