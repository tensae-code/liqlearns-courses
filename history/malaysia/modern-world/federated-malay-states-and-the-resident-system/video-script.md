# Video script — Federated Malay States and the Resident System

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Tin makes the west-coast states rich — Larut's mines and Chinese miners raise Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan into world tin leaders — and Britain binds the rulers by treaty from Pangkor in 1874, installs Residents whose 'advice' must be followed, and federates Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang.

Tin makes the west-coast states rich: Larut's mines from the 1840s, worked by Chinese miners, raise Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan into world tin leaders. Britain binds the rulers by treaty — beginning with the Pangkor Treaty of 1874 in Perak — and installs Residents whose 'advice' must be followed in government. Then it federates four states: Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang. Wealth draws the treaties; the treaties bring the Residents; the Residents bring the federation.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
