# Federated Malay States and the Resident System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What mineral makes the west-coast states rich?
a) tin  b) gold only  c) oil  d) rubber
**Answer: a) tin** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What begins the treaty-binding of the rulers?
a) the London Treaty of 1824  b) the Bangkok Treaty of 1909  c) the Federation Agreement of 1948  d) the Pangkor Treaty of 1874
**Answer: d) the Pangkor Treaty of 1874** — That is what this lesson teaches.
