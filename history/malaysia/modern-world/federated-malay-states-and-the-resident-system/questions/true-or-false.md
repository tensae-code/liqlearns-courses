# Federated Malay States and the Resident System — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A Resident's advice must be followed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Federated Malay States are Kedah, Kelantan, Perlis and Johor.
**Answer: False** — The federation joins Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang.
