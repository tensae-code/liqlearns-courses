# Hands-on — Federated Malay States and the Resident System

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Weigh something small and grey in your hand — a spoon will do: it is mostly tin's cousin in the kitchen. The metal in the Larut mines pulls an empire into four states in this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Tin makes the west-coast states rich — Larut's mines and Chinese miners raise Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan into world tin leaders — and Britain binds the rulers by treaty from Pangkor in 1874, installs Residents whose 'advice' must be followed, and federates Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
