# Lesson plan — Malaya's Road to Merdeka: Elections, Reid and 1957

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Malaya's Road to Merdeka: Elections, Reid and 1957
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — War, Occupation and the Road to Merdeka (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Malaya builds independence step by step — the Communities Liaison Committee, the Member System apprenticing local leaders, multiplying parties — then elections climb from municipal to federal, the 1955 election makes Tunku Abdul Rahman Chief Minister, the Reid Commission drafts the constitution from 131 memoranda, and Merdeka is proclaimed in the rain at Stadium Merdeka on 31 August 1957.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — War, Occupation and the Road to Merdeka (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab 7: the machinery of independence** — Independence is machined before it is declared.
2. **Bab 8: the first general election** — Elections climb from municipal to state to federal level.
3. **Bab 9–10: Reid, the Agreement, and Merdeka** — At Lancaster House, London (18 January–6 February 1956), the Rulers' representatives and the Alliance win agreement on self-government.
