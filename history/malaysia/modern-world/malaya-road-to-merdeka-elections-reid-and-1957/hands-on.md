# Hands-on — Malaya's Road to Merdeka: Elections, Reid and 1957

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Climb stairs with your finger on paper: municipal, state, federal elections; then a commission writing; then a stadium in the rain. Independence in this lesson is climbed, not granted in one step.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Malaya builds independence step by step — the Communities Liaison Committee, the Member System apprenticing local leaders, multiplying parties — then elections climb from municipal to federal, the 1955 election makes Tunku Abdul Rahman Chief Minister, the Reid Commission drafts the constitution from 131 memoranda, and Merdeka is proclaimed in the rain at Stadium Merdeka on 31 August 1957. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
