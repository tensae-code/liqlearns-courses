# Malaya's Road to Merdeka: Elections, Reid and 1957 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Merdeka is proclaimed on 31 August 1957 at Stadium Merdeka.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The 1955 election is the last step before independence with nothing after it.
**Answer: False** — After it come the Lancaster House talks and the Reid Commission's constitution.
