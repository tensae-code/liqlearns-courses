# Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — By the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and his dynasty expands for a century, while in Sabah the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter with conditions it must obey. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
