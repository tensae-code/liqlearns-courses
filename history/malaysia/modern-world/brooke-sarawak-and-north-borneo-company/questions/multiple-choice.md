# Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** By what does James Brooke come to rule Sarawak?
a) treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim  b) the Pangkor Treaty  c) a general election  d) the London Treaty
**Answer: a) treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What governs Sabah under Western rule?
a) the Brooke dynasty  b) the Dutch East India Company  c) the Straits Settlements  d) the British North Borneo Company
**Answer: d) the British North Borneo Company** — That is what this lesson teaches.
