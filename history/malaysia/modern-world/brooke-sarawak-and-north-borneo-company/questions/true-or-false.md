# Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter with conditions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Brooke pledges to abolish Malay law and custom.
**Answer: False** — Brooke pledges to respect Malay law and custom.
