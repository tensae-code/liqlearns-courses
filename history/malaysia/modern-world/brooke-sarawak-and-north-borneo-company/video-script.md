# Video script — Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
By the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and his dynasty expands for a century, while in Sabah the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter with conditions it must obey.

Sarawak gets a family, Sabah a company. By the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and the Brunei sultan cedes the territory; the Brooke dynasty then expands for a century. In Sabah, the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter, with conditions it must obey. Two Western rules, neither of them a colony office: a dynasty and a chartered company.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
