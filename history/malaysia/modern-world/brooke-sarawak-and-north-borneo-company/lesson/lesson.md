# Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company

## Do it first

Write two column heads: 'a family rules' and 'a company rules'. Borneo gets one of each in this lesson — Brooke's dynasty in Sarawak, a chartered company in Sabah. Keep the two apart as you read.

Sarawak gets a family, Sabah a company. By the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and the Brunei sultan cedes the territory; the Brooke dynasty then expands for a century. In Sabah, the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter, with conditions it must obey. Two Western rules, neither of them a colony office: a dynasty and a chartered company.
