# Hands-on — Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two column heads: 'a family rules' and 'a company rules'. Borneo gets one of each in this lesson — Brooke's dynasty in Sarawak, a chartered company in Sabah. Keep the two apart as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: By the treaties of 1841 and 1842 with Pengiran Raja Muda Hashim, James Brooke rules Sarawak — pledged to respect Malay law and custom — and his dynasty expands for a century, while in Sabah the British North Borneo Company governs under a Royal Charter with conditions it must obey. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
