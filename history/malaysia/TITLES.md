# History — Malaysia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Malaysia's
own — built from Malaysia's Sejarah books for Tahun 4 and
Tingkatan 1–5 (Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka, for the Ministry of
Education Malaysia, in Malay). Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below
sit inside Levels 1–6, in path order — what the learner
proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live
only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': history begins at home —
family, school, hometown — then widens to the Ice Age and the
world's civilisations, returns to the Malay kingdoms and
Melaka, follows the Western arrival state by state and the
leaders who resisted or outmanoeuvred it, and climbs through
occupation, the Emergency and the elections to Merdeka and
to Malaysia itself. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
