# History — Malaysia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Malaysia inside the History folder, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved
the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade
each book comes from appears only in each lesson's sources
(provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Malaysia's books in hand are six
student books — *Sejarah Tahun 4 SK* (2019), then *Sejarah
Tingkatan 1–5* (2016–2020) — and the placements file's book
order is the teaching order, earliest books first. Inside that
order, the ladder follows the eras as Malaysia's own books
present them: history begins at home and learns its tools
(Level 1); the deep past and the world-civilisations tour
(Level 2); the Malay kingdoms and Melaka (Level 3); the Western
arrival and colonial Malaya, state by state (Level 4); war,
occupation and the road to Merdeka (Level 5); and Malaysia
formed in 1963 and rebuilt after it (Level 6). The ladder is a
recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Sejarah books for Tahun 4 and Tingkatan 1–5 (Dewan Bahasa
dan Pustaka, for the Ministry of Education Malaysia) — except
the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **First Steps: History Begins at Home** — the historian's first steps as Malaysia's own books open them — history begins at home, with the child's own biodata, family salasilah, school and hometown, and the tools of the trade: what history is, its sources and methods, and chronology with cause and effect, told at Tahun 4 and deepened at Tingkatan 1 4 lessons.

2. **The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations** — the deep past and the world's civilisations — the Ice Age and Malaysia's caves, then the world-civilisations tour of the Tingkatan 1 book: Mesopotamia, Egypt, India, China, Greece, Athens, Rome, the dynasties, Arabia before Islam and the rise of Islam 11 lessons.

3. **Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate** — the Malay world's own kingdoms — the early kingdoms from Funan to Majapahit, the Melaka Sultanate whole (society, Parameswara, Tun Perak, Hang Tuah, its glory and its recall as the first nation-state), its fall in 1511, and its heirs: Johor Riau, Pahang, Perak, Terengganu, Selangor, the northern kingdoms with Adat Perpatih, and Borneo's local governments before Brooke 7 lessons.

4. **Western Powers and Colonial Malaya** — the Western arrival taught state by state — why the West came, the Straits Settlements, the Federated Malay States and the Resident system, the Unfederated States and Johor, Brooke's Sarawak and the North Borneo Company, what colonial administration changed, the named resistance leaders, and the rulers' own answers at Bangkok in 1909 and in Johor 8 lessons.

5. **War, Occupation and the Road to Merdeka** — the nation-building years — nationalism's awakening in print, the world wars' background and Pacific spread, the Japanese occupation and its resistance, the rejected Malayan Union and the 1948 Federation, the Emergency, and the climbed road of elections, the Reid Commission and Merdeka in 1957 7 lessons.

6. **Malaysia Formed and Rebuilt** — Malaysia itself — the 1963 federation assembled step by step (referendum, safeguards, UN mission), stress-tested by Konfrontasi, Singapore's exit, the second insurgency and 13 May 1969, rebuilt through the Rukun Negara and the New Economic Policy, and seated in the OIC founded in 1969 3 lessons.

**Totals: 40 lessons** (Level 1: 4 · Level 2: 11 · Level 3: 7 · Level 4: 8 · Level 5: 7 · Level 6: 3 — 65 placements, all from six student books; 13 lessons carry sub lessons).
