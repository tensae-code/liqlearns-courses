# Early Malay Kingdoms: Funan, Srivijaya and Majapahit

## Do it first

On a page, draw a river running to the sea. Dot five towns along the coasts and straits and give each a kingdom's name from this lesson. Trade boats between them: that network is the early Malay world.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 7 Kerajaan Melayu Awal: the early Malay kingdoms

From the first centuries, kingdoms rise across the Malay world. Funan comes first; then Champa, Kedah Tua in the Bujang Valley, Chih-Tu, Gangga Nagara, Beruas, Srivijaya in the 7th century, Mataram in the 9th, Santubong, and Majapahit in the 13th. They leave Hindu-Buddhist monuments — Borobudur, Prambanan, Muara Jambi — trade in spices, tin and gold, and exchange envoys with China and India.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 1: the famous kingdoms of Alam Melayu

The Form 2 book opens the heritage year with the famous kingdoms of Alam Melayu. Funan is founded by the Brahmin Kaundinya at Vyadhapura. Srivijaya is founded by Dapunta Hyang Sri Jayanasa at Palembang in 683, takes Bangka and Taruma, and under Sangramadhananjaya in the 8th century conquers Sumatra, southern Myanmar, the Malay Peninsula and the Kra Isthmus. Angkor builds its capital at Angkor Thom.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bab 1: Majapahit, Kedah Tua and Gangga Nagara

Majapahit is founded in 1294 by Raden Vijaya with Mongol help, rules from Trowulan, and is steadied by Patih Gajah Mada under Queen Tribhuwana Tunggadewi. In the peninsula, Kedah Tua's candi dot the Bujang Valley and Gangga Nagara works as a port from the 6th century. All of them are contemporaries of Rome and the other outside powers — trading, and exchanging envoys.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bab 2–4: how the Alam Melayu kingdoms lived

Beneath the many crowns lies one civilisation in many kingdoms: the Malay language and its writing, a literature of hikayat and syair, candi architecture, a ranked social structure, and tin-gold-spice economies run from river-mouth ports. Its religions travel too — from Hindu-Buddhist faiths to Islam — leaving a unique shared heritage across the whole Malay world.
