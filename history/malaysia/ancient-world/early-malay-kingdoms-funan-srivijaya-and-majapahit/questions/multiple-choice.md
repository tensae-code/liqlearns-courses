# Early Malay Kingdoms: Funan, Srivijaya and Majapahit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who founds Srivijaya at Palembang in 683?
a) Dapunta Hyang Sri Jayanasa  b) Raden Vijaya  c) Kaundinya  d) Parameswara
**Answer: a) Dapunta Hyang Sri Jayanasa** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What steadies Majapahit under Queen Tribhuwana Tunggadewi?
a) a Portuguese fleet  b) the Bendahara of Melaka  c) a Mongol garrison forever  d) Patih Gajah Mada
**Answer: d) Patih Gajah Mada** — That is what this lesson teaches.
