# Early Malay Kingdoms: Funan, Srivijaya and Majapahit — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Kedah Tua's candi dot the Bujang Valley.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The Alam Melayu kingdoms had no contact with China or India.
**Answer: False** — The kingdoms trade and exchange envoys with China and India.
