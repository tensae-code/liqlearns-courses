# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Greece tries five systems of rule, and its lasting gift is democracy — government chosen by the people's vote — inherited by every generation since.

How should a people be ruled? Greece tries five systems of rule in turn — and its lasting gift is one of them: democracy, government chosen by the people's vote. The experiment matters as much as the answer: a people that tests ways of ruling, and keeps the one where the people themselves choose, hands that choice down to every generation since.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
