# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many systems of rule does Greece try in this lesson?
a) five  b) two  c) ten  d) one
**Answer: a) five** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is democracy, as this lesson defines it?
a) rule by one family's oldest son  b) rule by generals only  c) having no government at all  d) government chosen by the people's vote
**Answer: d) government chosen by the people's vote** — That is what this lesson teaches.
