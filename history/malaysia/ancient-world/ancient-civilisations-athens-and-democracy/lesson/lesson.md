# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

## Do it first

Hold a class vote on one small real choice — which game to play — and count the hands honestly. That choosing by vote is democracy, the gift this lesson follows from Greece.

How should a people be ruled? Greece tries five systems of rule in turn — and its lasting gift is one of them: democracy, government chosen by the people's vote. The experiment matters as much as the answer: a people that tests ways of ruling, and keeps the one where the people themselves choose, hands that choice down to every generation since.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Greece tries five systems of rule, and its lasting gift is democracy — government chosen by the people's vote — inherited by every generation since. Give one real example: of the five systems Greece tries, democracy is the one the world keeps — government chosen by the people's vote, and name the lesson's key words: Athens, democracy, five systems, the people's vote, government. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
