# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Greece tries five systems of rule, and its lasting gift is democracy — government chosen by the people's vote — inherited by every generation since. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
