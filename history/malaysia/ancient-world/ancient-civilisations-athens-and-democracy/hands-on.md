# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Hold a class vote on one small real choice — which game to play — and count the hands honestly. That choosing by vote is democracy, the gift this lesson follows from Greece.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Greece tries five systems of rule, and its lasting gift is democracy — government chosen by the people's vote — inherited by every generation since. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
