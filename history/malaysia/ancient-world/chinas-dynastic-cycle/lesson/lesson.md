# China's Dynastic Cycle

## Do it first

Draw a wheel with two spokes labelled with two ruling houses. Turn the page slowly: houses change, the wheel — China — rolls on. That turning wheel is the dynastic story in this lesson.

China's story is told by its dynasties — its ruling houses. Under the Qin and Han dynasties, China advances across the board: in government and administration, in economy, in technology, and above all in education and intellect. The houses give the civilisation its frame; the learning gives it its height.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Under the Qin and Han dynasties, China advances in government and administration, economy, technology and above all education and intellect — ruling houses rise and carry the civilisation forward. Give one real example: the Qin and Han are the dynasties named for China's great advance in this lesson, and name the lesson's key words: Qin, Han, dynasty, education, administration. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
