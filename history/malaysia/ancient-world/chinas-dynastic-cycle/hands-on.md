# Hands-on — China's Dynastic Cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a wheel with two spokes labelled with two ruling houses. Turn the page slowly: houses change, the wheel — China — rolls on. That turning wheel is the dynastic story in this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Under the Qin and Han dynasties, China advances in government and administration, economy, technology and above all education and intellect — ruling houses rise and carry the civilisation forward. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
