# Video script — China's Dynastic Cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Under the Qin and Han dynasties, China advances in government and administration, economy, technology and above all education and intellect — ruling houses rise and carry the civilisation forward.

China's story is told by its dynasties — its ruling houses. Under the Qin and Han dynasties, China advances across the board: in government and administration, in economy, in technology, and above all in education and intellect. The houses give the civilisation its frame; the learning gives it its height.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
