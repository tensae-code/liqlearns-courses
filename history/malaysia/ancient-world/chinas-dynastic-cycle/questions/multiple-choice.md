# China's Dynastic Cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which two dynasties carry China's advancement in this lesson?
a) Qin and Han  b) Ming and Qing only  c) Srivijaya and Majapahit  d) Old and New Kingdoms
**Answer: a) Qin and Han** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Above all, in what does China advance under these dynasties?
a) sea trade only  b) cave painting  c) pyramid building  d) education and intellect
**Answer: d) education and intellect** — That is what this lesson teaches.
