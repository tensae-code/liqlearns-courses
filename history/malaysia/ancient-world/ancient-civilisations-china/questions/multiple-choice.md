# Ancient Civilisations: China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the Huang He also called?
a) the Yellow River  b) the Long River  c) the Mother Sea  d) the Indus
**Answer: a) the Yellow River** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is the Huang He's mud precious to farmers?
a) it is good for building walls only  b) it dyes cloth yellow  c) it keeps floods away forever  d) it is fertile silt that enriches the fields
**Answer: d) it is fertile silt that enriches the fields** — That is what this lesson teaches.
