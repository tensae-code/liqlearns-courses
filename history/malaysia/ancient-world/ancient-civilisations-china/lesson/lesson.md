# Ancient Civilisations: China

## Do it first

Take a handful of soil or sand and let it slip through your fingers into a pot, the way a river drops its silt. Say what that dropped silt does for farmers. That gift of the river is China's start in this lesson.

China's first civilisation is a river's gift too. On the silt-yellow Huang He — the Yellow River — and on the Yangtze, China's first farmers work the fertile mud of the north. The river carries the yellow silt down and lays it on the fields; the fields feed the people; and a civilisation grows where the mud is richest.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: China's first civilisation farms the fertile silt of the Huang He — the Yellow River — and the Yangtze in the north, where the yellow mud makes the farming land rich. Give one real example: the Huang He is called the Yellow River for the silt-yellow mud it carries and drops on its farming plains, and name the lesson's key words: Huang He, Yellow River, Yangtze, silt, fertile mud. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
