# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: China

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: China
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** China's first civilisation farms the fertile silt of the Huang He — the Yellow River — and the Yangtze in the north, where the yellow mud makes the farming land rich.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: China's first civilisation farms the fertile silt of the Huang He — the Yellow River — and the Yangtze in the north, where the yellow mud makes the farming land rich.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: the Huang He is called the Yellow River for the silt-yellow mud it carries and drops on its farming plains.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
