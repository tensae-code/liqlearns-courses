# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: China

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take a handful of soil or sand and let it slip through your fingers into a pot, the way a river drops its silt. Say what that dropped silt does for farmers. That gift of the river is China's start in this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: China's first civilisation farms the fertile silt of the Huang He — the Yellow River — and the Yangtze in the north, where the yellow mud makes the farming land rich. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
