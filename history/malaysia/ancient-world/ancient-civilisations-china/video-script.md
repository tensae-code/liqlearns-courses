# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: China

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
China's first civilisation farms the fertile silt of the Huang He — the Yellow River — and the Yangtze in the north, where the yellow mud makes the farming land rich.

China's first civilisation is a river's gift too. On the silt-yellow Huang He — the Yellow River — and on the Yangtze, China's first farmers work the fertile mud of the north. The river carries the yellow silt down and lays it on the fields; the fields feed the people; and a civilisation grows where the mud is richest.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
