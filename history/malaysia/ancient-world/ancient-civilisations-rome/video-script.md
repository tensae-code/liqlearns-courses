# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Rome inherits Greek free thought, defeats its rivals to become the Mediterranean's strongest power, and builds on a scale — the Colosseum above all — that still defines classical Europe.

Rome begins by learning: it inherits Greek free thought. Then it fights — defeating its rivals until it stands as the Mediterranean's strongest power. And it builds on a scale that outlasts its armies: the Colosseum above all, the great arena that still defines classical Europe. Thought inherited, rivals defeated, stone raised — that is Rome's three-part story.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
