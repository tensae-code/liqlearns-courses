# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Rome inherits Greek free thought, defeats its rivals to become the Mediterranean's strongest power, and builds on a scale — the Colosseum above all — that still defines classical Europe. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
