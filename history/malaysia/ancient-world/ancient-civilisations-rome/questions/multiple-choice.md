# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does Rome inherit from Greece?
a) Greek free thought  b) the Indus cities  c) the Huang He's silt  d) Egypt's pyramids
**Answer: a) Greek free thought** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which building above all marks Rome's building scale?
a) the Great Wall  b) Borobudur  c) the Nabji pillar  d) the Colosseum
**Answer: d) the Colosseum** — That is what this lesson teaches.
