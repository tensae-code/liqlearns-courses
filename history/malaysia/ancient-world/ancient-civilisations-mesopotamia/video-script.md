# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
A civilisation is a society's highest achievement — farming and trade, government, cities, specialised work, writing and records, religion and art — and the first of all grows in Mesopotamia, the land between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers.

First, what is a civilisation? The book defines it as a society's highest achievement, known by its marks: farming and trade, government, cities, specialised work, writing and records, religion and art. Then the first of all is named: Mesopotamia, growing between the Tigris and the Euphrates — its very name means the land between two rivers. Every civilisation in the lessons after this one is measured against these marks.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
