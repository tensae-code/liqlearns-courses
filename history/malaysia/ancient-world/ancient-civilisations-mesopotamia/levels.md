# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A civilisation is a society's highest achievement — farming and trade, government, cities, specialised work, writing and records, religion and art — and the first of all grows in Mesopotamia, the land between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
