# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List seven things a big town needs that a single farm family does not — leaders, builders, writers of records, and more. You have just listed what makes a civilisation, as this lesson teaches.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A civilisation is a society's highest achievement — farming and trade, government, cities, specialised work, writing and records, religion and art — and the first of all grows in Mesopotamia, the land between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
