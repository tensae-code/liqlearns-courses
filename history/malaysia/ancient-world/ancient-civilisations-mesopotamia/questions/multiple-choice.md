# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Mesopotamia grew between which two rivers?
a) the Tigris and the Euphrates  b) the Nile and the Jordan  c) the Huang He and the Yangtze  d) the Indus and the Ganges
**Answer: a) the Tigris and the Euphrates** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is a mark of civilisation in this lesson?
a) having no cities  b) everyone doing the same work  c) living without government  d) writing and records
**Answer: d) writing and records** — That is what this lesson teaches.
