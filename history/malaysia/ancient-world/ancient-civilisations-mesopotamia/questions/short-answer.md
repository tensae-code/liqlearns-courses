# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four marks of a civilisation from this lesson.
**Answer:** Any four of: farming and trade, government, cities, specialised work, writing and records, religion and art.

**2.** Why is Mesopotamia called the land between two rivers?
**Answer:** Because it grew between the Tigris and the Euphrates.
