# Video script — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Islam rises with the Prophet Muhammad — born to the Quraysh in the Year of the Elephant (570) — who leads as head of state, society-builder, economic organiser and commander, drafts the Madina Charter for a plural city, and founds a civilisation balancing material and spiritual progress, signed in its mosque architecture.

Muhammad is born to the Quraysh on 12 Rabiulawal, the Year of the Elephant — 20 April 570. As leader he does four works at once: he drafts the Madina Charter, a constitution for a plural city; and he leads as head of state, society-builder, economic organiser and commander. Islam's rise is thus a city's order as much as a faith's preaching.
The civilisation that grows from this rise balances material and spiritual progress, and contributes in politics, economy and society. Its signature in stone is the mosque — architecture shaped for worship, in its spaces, its light and its feeling. A civilisation can be recognised, the lesson shows, by what it builds to pray in.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
