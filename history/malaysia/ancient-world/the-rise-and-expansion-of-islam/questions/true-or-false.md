# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Muhammad leads as head of state, society-builder, economic organiser and commander.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Islamic civilisation ignores material progress entirely.
**Answer: False** — Islamic civilisation balances material and spiritual progress.
