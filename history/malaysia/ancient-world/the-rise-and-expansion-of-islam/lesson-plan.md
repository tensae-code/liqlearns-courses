# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Islam rises with the Prophet Muhammad — born to the Quraysh in the Year of the Elephant (570) — who leads as head of state, society-builder, economic organiser and commander, drafts the Madina Charter for a plural city, and founds a civilisation balancing material and spiritual progress, signed in its mosque architecture.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab 8.2–8.3: the rise of Islamic civilisation and the Prophet as leader** — Muhammad is born to the Quraysh on 12 Rabiulawal, the Year of the Elephant — 20 April 570.
2. **Bab 8.4–8.5: Islamic civilisation's gifts to the world** — The civilisation that grows from this rise balances material and spiritual progress, and contributes in politics, economy and society.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
