# Hands-on — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write four hats on four slips of paper: ruler, builder of society, organiser of livelihoods, commander. Read the lesson and match each hat to one thing the Prophet does. One leader, four works — that is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Islam rises with the Prophet Muhammad — born to the Quraysh in the Year of the Elephant (570) — who leads as head of state, society-builder, economic organiser and commander, drafts the Madina Charter for a plural city, and founds a civilisation balancing material and spiritual progress, signed in its mosque architecture. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
