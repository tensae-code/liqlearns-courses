# Lesson plan — The Melaka Sultanate: Parameswara to Hang Tuah

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Melaka Sultanate: Parameswara to Hang Tuah
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Melaka is founded by the Palembang prince Parameswara at the Bertam river — where a white mousedeer kicks his hunting dog into the water — and grows into the strait's great entrepot ruled through the Pembesar Berempat, served by Bendahara Tun Perak and Laksamana Hang Tuah, and remembered later as Malaysia's first nation-state.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Malay Kingdoms and the Melaka Sultanate (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 8: society and great figures of the Melaka Sultanate** — Melaka's society stacks in order: the rulers — sultan, then pembesar — above the ruled, the rakyat, then the hamba.
2. **Unit 9: Parameswara founds Melaka** — A Palembang prince flees Majapahit's attack, kills Temagi at Temasik, and retreats before Siam through Muar and Sening Ujong.
3. **Unit 10: Tun Perak, the great bendahara** — Tun Perak serves four sultans as bendahara.
4. **Unit 11: Hang Tuah, the great laksamana** — Hang Tuah, a Sungai Duyung boy trained in silat, rises under Sultan Mansur Shah.
5. **Bab 5.2: the glory of the Melaka Sultanate** — At its glory, Melaka's government rests on the Pembesar Berempat — Bendahara, Penghulu Bendahari, Temenggung and Laksamana.
6. **Bab 1 Warisan Negara Bangsa: Melaka as the first nation-state** — The oldest book in the ladder looks back: the Melaka Sultanate is presented as the inherited model of a negara bangsa, a nation-state — an ordered administration, excellent law, and defined roles for ruler and people.
