# The Melaka Sultanate: Parameswara to Hang Tuah — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What sign marks the lucky spot where Melaka is founded?
a) a white mousedeer kicking the hunting dog into the water  b) an eagle over the harbour  c) a burning tree  d) a whale in the strait
**Answer: a) a white mousedeer kicking the hunting dog into the water** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is Melaka's great bendahara, serving four sultans?
a) Hang Tuah  b) Temenggung Daeng Ibrahim  c) Gajah Mada  d) Tun Perak
**Answer: d) Tun Perak** — That is what this lesson teaches.
