# The Melaka Sultanate: Parameswara to Hang Tuah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 5) Name the four officers of the Pembesar Berempat.
**Answer:** Bendahara, Penghulu Bendahari, Temenggung and Laksamana.

**2.** (Sub lesson 6) Why is Melaka's strait position so valuable?
**Answer:** It is monsoon-sheltered and sits between the eastern and western sea roads, so trade gathers there.
