# The Melaka Sultanate: Parameswara to Hang Tuah — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Hang Tuah is Melaka's great laksamana who guards its waters.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) Tun Perak takes revenge when his son is killed.
**Answer: False** — Tun Perak forbids revenge and commands loyalty to the sultan.
