# Video script — Arabia Before Islam: Trade and Tribes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
On the eve of Islam, the desert-ringed Arabian Peninsula holds Arabs living two ways — settled and nomadic — with their own governments, economies and social orders.

Before Islam, the Arabian Peninsula — a land ringed by desert — is already a full society. Arabs live two ways: settled lives in towns and oases, and nomadic lives moving with their herds. Both ways have their own governments, economies and social orders. This is the world on the eve of Islam: not an emptiness waiting, but peoples with orders of their own.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
