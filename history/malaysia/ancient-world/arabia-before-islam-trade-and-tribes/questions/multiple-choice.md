# Arabia Before Islam: Trade and Tribes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What two ways do Arabs live before Islam?
a) settled and nomadic  b) farming and fishing only  c) city and cave  d) rich and poor only
**Answer: a) settled and nomadic** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What rings the Arabian Peninsula?
a) ice  b) jungle  c) a great lake  d) desert
**Answer: d) desert** — That is what this lesson teaches.
