# Hands-on — Arabia Before Islam: Trade and Tribes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a desert with an oasis town on one side and a moving camp with camels on the other. Label the settled life and the nomadic life. Arabia before Islam, in this lesson, holds both at once.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On the eve of Islam, the desert-ringed Arabian Peninsula holds Arabs living two ways — settled and nomadic — with their own governments, economies and social orders. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
