# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: India

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: India
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** India's civilisation begins about 2500 BC in the Indus valley — a planned civilisation built by settled farmers and traders — and climbs again in later ages in government, economy, culture, technology and religion to stand among the world's foremost.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab 5.1: the Indus civilisation** — From about 2500 BC, nomads of the north-west settle down in the Indus valley — the land between the Makran coast, Baluchistan, Punjab and Gujarat — and build a planned civilisation.
2. **Bab 7.1: India's advancement** — The story does not stop at the first cities.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
