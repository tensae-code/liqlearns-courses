# Ancient Civilisations: India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Indus civilisation was a planned civilisation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) India's civilisation never advanced after the Indus age.
**Answer: False** — India's civilisation climbs again to stand among the world's foremost.
