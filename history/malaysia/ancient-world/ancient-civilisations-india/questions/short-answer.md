# Ancient Civilisations: India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where exactly did the Indus civilisation grow?
**Answer:** In the Indus valley, between the Makran coast, Baluchistan, Punjab and Gujarat.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who settled the valley to build it?
**Answer:** Nomads of the north-west who settled down.
