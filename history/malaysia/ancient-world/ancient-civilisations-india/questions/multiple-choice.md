# Ancient Civilisations: India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) From about when does the Indus civilisation date?
a) about 2500 BC  b) about 250 BC  c) about 250 AD  d) about 1500 BC
**Answer: a) about 2500 BC** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In its later advancement, India climbs in which fields?
a) only warfare  b) only shipbuilding  c) only poetry  d) government, economy, culture, technology and religion
**Answer: d) government, economy, culture, technology and religion** — That is what this lesson teaches.
