# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: India

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Sketch a planned town: straight streets, houses in rows, a place for storing grain. Under it write the valley and the river it stands beside. That planned town is India's first civilisation in this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: India's civilisation begins about 2500 BC in the Indus valley — a planned civilisation built by settled farmers and traders — and climbs again in later ages in government, economy, culture, technology and religion to stand among the world's foremost. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
