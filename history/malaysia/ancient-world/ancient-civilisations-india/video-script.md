# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: India

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
India's civilisation begins about 2500 BC in the Indus valley — a planned civilisation built by settled farmers and traders — and climbs again in later ages in government, economy, culture, technology and religion to stand among the world's foremost.

From about 2500 BC, nomads of the north-west settle down in the Indus valley — the land between the Makran coast, Baluchistan, Punjab and Gujarat — and build a planned civilisation. Settling is the turn: fields are farmed, towns are laid out, and a river valley becomes one of the world's first civilisations.
The story does not stop at the first cities. India's civilisation climbs again — in government, expanding power, economy, culture, technology and religion — until it stands among the world's foremost. The lesson's shape is rise, then rise again: advancement piled on the old foundations.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
