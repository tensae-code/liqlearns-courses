# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Between 1000 and 800 BC, Greece sparks Europe's development and bequeaths democracy, science and a model of the examined life to every generation after it.

Between 1000 and 800 BC, Greece sparks Europe's development. Its bequest is named plainly: democracy, science, and a model of the examined life — a life that asks questions about itself. Small cities around a sea thus set a pattern the whole of Europe, and then the world, inherits.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
