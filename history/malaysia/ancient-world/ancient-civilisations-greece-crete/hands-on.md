# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask one question about your own life today — why do I do this, is it right — and write it down. Asking that kind of question is the examined life Greece leaves behind in this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Between 1000 and 800 BC, Greece sparks Europe's development and bequeaths democracy, science and a model of the examined life to every generation after it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
