# Video script — Find History in Your Own Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
History starts at home: a family's salasilah (family tree), a school's timeline, and a hometown's founding story — like Johor Bahru beginning as Tanjung Puteri in 1855 — are real history, found in your own locality.

The first history a Malaysian child studies is their own. The child builds a family salasilah — a family tree — from a grandfather's telling. The school has a history too: the book reads the timeline of SK Permatang Tok Mahat, founded in 1890. And the hometown has a founding story: Johor Bahru begins as Tanjung Puteri, founded by Temenggong Daeng Ibrahim in 1855 and renamed by Sultan Abu Bakar in 1866, while Ranau in Sabah was once a fighters' stronghold. Family, school, town — the past begins where you stand.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
