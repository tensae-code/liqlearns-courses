# Find History in Your Own Locality — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — History starts at home: a family's salasilah (family tree), a school's timeline, and a hometown's founding story — like Johor Bahru beginning as Tanjung Puteri in 1855 — are real history, found in your own locality. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
