# Hands-on — Find History in Your Own Locality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask a grandparent or elder to name their parents and grandparents, and draw your family tree. Then ask when your school or your street began. You have just done this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: History starts at home: a family's salasilah (family tree), a school's timeline, and a hometown's founding story — like Johor Bahru beginning as Tanjung Puteri in 1855 — are real history, found in your own locality. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
