# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a salasilah?
a) a family tree  b) a kind of boat  c) a royal title  d) a cave painting
**Answer: a) a family tree** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Johor Bahru was founded as Tanjung Puteri by whom?
a) Sultan Abu Bakar  b) Parameswara  c) James Brooke  d) Temenggong Daeng Ibrahim
**Answer: d) Temenggong Daeng Ibrahim** — That is what this lesson teaches.
