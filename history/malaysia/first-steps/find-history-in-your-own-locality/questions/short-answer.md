# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In what year was Tanjung Puteri founded, and who renamed it Johor Bahru?
**Answer:** Founded in 1855; renamed Johor Bahru by Sultan Abu Bakar in 1866.

**2.** Where does this lesson say a child's history begins?
**Answer:** At home — with their family, their school and their hometown.
