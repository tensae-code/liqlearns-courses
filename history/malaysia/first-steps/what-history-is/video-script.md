# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
History is the study of events that truly happened in the past — told by Herodotus as the story of human actions and their causes, by Ibn Khaldun as the story of civilisation and change, and framed at secondary level as the study of a society's and a nation's past.

Malaysia's youngest history learners start with a plain claim: history is events that truly happened in the past. The book lines up the people who defined it. Herodotus told history as the storytelling of human actions and their causes. Ibn Khaldun saw it as the story of civilisation and how civilisations change. And Malaysia's own historian Khoo Kay Kim defined it simply: events that have already happened. Three voices, one subject — the real past.
At secondary level the definers return, deeper. Herodotus is met through his Histories of the Persian War; E.H. Carr joins the line, with Ibn Khaldun again and Malaysia's Muhd Yusof Ibrahim. History is now framed as the study of a society's and a nation's past — not one person's story, but a whole people's. The lesson the learner carries forward: every later chapter is this study at work.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
