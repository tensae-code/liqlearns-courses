# What History Is

## Do it first

Think of one thing that happened in your family before you were born. Ask an older person about it, then write down what happened and one cause of it. That asking and telling is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1 Mengenali Sejarah: what history is

Malaysia's youngest history learners start with a plain claim: history is events that truly happened in the past. The book lines up the people who defined it. Herodotus told history as the storytelling of human actions and their causes. Ibn Khaldun saw it as the story of civilisation and how civilisations change. And Malaysia's own historian Khoo Kay Kim defined it simply: events that have already happened. Three voices, one subject — the real past.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bab 1 Mengenali Sejarah: what history is

At secondary level the definers return, deeper. Herodotus is met through his Histories of the Persian War; E.H. Carr joins the line, with Ibn Khaldun again and Malaysia's Muhd Yusof Ibrahim. History is now framed as the study of a society's and a nation's past — not one person's story, but a whole people's. The lesson the learner carries forward: every later chapter is this study at work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: History is the study of events that truly happened in the past — told by Herodotus as the story of human actions and their causes, by Ibn Khaldun as the story of civilisation and change, and framed at secondary level as the study of a society's and a nation's past. Give one real example: a grandfather explaining that the family moved to a new town because work opened there — the event plus its cause is history, not just a date, and name the lesson's key words: history, Herodotus, Ibn Khaldun, Khoo Kay Kim, E.H. Carr, the past. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
