# Lesson plan — What History Is

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What History Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: History Begins at Home (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History is the study of events that truly happened in the past — told by Herodotus as the story of human actions and their causes, by Ibn Khaldun as the story of civilisation and change, and framed at secondary level as the study of a society's and a nation's past.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: History Begins at Home (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History is the study of events that truly happened in the past — told by Herodotus as the story of human actions and their causes, by Ibn Khaldun as the story of civilisation and change, and framed at secondary level as the study of a society's and a nation's past.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: a grandfather explaining that the family moved to a new town because work opened there — the event plus its cause is history, not just a date.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Malaysia, as the books themselves work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks History is only a list of dates to memorise → Dates are the frame: this lesson's history always asks what happened and why — the causes are part of the story.
- Go back to the one thing: History is the study of events that truly happened in the past — told by Herodotus as the story of human actions and their causes, by Ibn Khaldun as the story of civilisation and change, and framed at secondary level as the study of a society's and a nation's past. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
