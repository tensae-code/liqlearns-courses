# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ibn Khaldun saw history as the story of civilisation and change.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Khoo Kay Kim defined history as events that have not happened yet.
**Answer: False** — Khoo Kay Kim defined history as events that have already happened.
