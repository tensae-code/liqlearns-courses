# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who told history as the storytelling of human actions and their causes?
a) Herodotus  b) Ibn Khaldun  c) Khoo Kay Kim  d) E.H. Carr
**Answer: a) Herodotus** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is history framed at secondary level in this lesson?
a) as a list of kings' birthdays  b) as stories nobody can check  c) as a way to predict the future  d) as the study of a society's and a nation's past
**Answer: d) as the study of a society's and a nation's past** — That is what this lesson teaches.
