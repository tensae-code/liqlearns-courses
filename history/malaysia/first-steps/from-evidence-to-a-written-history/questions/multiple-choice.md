# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a primary source as this lesson teaches?
a) a fossil in its original form  b) a modern textbook  c) a newspaper article about the past  d) a history website
**Answer: a) a fossil in its original form** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can the same event carry several honest interpretations?
a) because evidence never matters  b) because historians invent freely  c) because sources are always wrong  d) because historians interpret the evidence they research
**Answer: d) because historians interpret the evidence they research** — That is what this lesson teaches.
