# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The oral method means interviewing the orang sumber who saw the event.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Archaeology means reading newspapers about the past.
**Answer: False** — Archaeology means digging up the remains of the past.
