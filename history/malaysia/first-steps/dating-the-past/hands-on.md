# Hands-on — Dating the Past

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write three events from your own life in the order they happened, youngest last. Now add one cause and one effect beside the middle event. That line, with its causes, is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Historians order the past on one straight line called chronology — decades, centuries and millennia, split by Before Masihi and Masihi — and every event on that line has a cause and an effect. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
