# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What two great parts is the chronology line split into?
**Answer:** Before Masihi (Sebelum Masihi) and Masihi.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do historians order events by chronology?
**Answer:** So the past can be measured and ordered, and each event placed before and after the others.
