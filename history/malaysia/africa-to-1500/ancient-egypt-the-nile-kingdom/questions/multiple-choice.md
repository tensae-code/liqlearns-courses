# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How long is the Nile, as this lesson teaches?
a) 6,650 km  b) 650 km  c) 66,500 km  d) 1,650 km
**Answer: a) 6,650 km** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Egypt's story is told through which three kingdoms?
a) Upper, Lower and Sea  b) First, Second and Last Pharaoh  c) River, Desert and Delta  d) Old, Middle and New
**Answer: d) Old, Middle and New** — That is what this lesson teaches.
