# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw one long river down a page. Mark three stretches along it and label them Old, Middle and New. You have drawn Egypt's story as this lesson tells it — one river, three kingdoms.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On the Nile — at 6,650 km the world's longest river — Ancient Egypt raises the pyramids and the pharaohs through three ages: the Old, Middle and New Kingdoms. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
