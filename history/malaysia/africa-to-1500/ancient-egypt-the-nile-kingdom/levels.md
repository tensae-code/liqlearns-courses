# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — On the Nile — at 6,650 km the world's longest river — Ancient Egypt raises the pyramids and the pharaohs through three ages: the Old, Middle and New Kingdoms. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
