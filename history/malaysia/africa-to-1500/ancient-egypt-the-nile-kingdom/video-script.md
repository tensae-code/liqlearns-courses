# Video script — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
On the Nile — at 6,650 km the world's longest river — Ancient Egypt raises the pyramids and the pharaohs through three ages: the Old, Middle and New Kingdoms.

Egypt's civilisation is a river's gift. On the 6,650 km Nile, the world's longest river, rise the pyramids and the pharaohs. The story is told in three ages — the Old Kingdom, the Middle Kingdom and the New Kingdom — one kingdom growing, changing and enduring along a single river through the desert.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Malaysia.
