# Lesson plan — From Stone Tools to Farming

**Course:** History — Malaysia · **Title:** The Deep Past · **Lesson:** From Stone Tools to Farming
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Life climbs from stone tools to farming in steps: the Ice Ages freeze and thaw the world, seas rise over the Sunda Shelf, and in Malaysia the Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic and Metal Ages are proven by its own caves — Gua Niah, Lenggong, Gua Cha and Bukit Tengkorak.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the World's Civilisations (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 5 Zaman Air Batu: the Ice Age** — The earth has frozen five times.
2. **Unit 6 Zaman Prasejarah: prehistory in Malaysia** — Before writing, life in Malaysia climbs four steps: Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic, Metal Age.
3. **Bab 2 Zaman Air Batu: the Ice Age** — The secondary book retells the last Ice Age — its features and its changes — and follows its Southeast Asian sequel: rising seas remaking the region's coasts and driving the movement of people and animals across the drowned lands.
4. **Bab 3 Zaman Prasejarah: world prehistory** — World prehistory is traced in the same steps: the Stone Age's three parts and then the Metal Age, followed through world sites and the changing life of cave, shore and farm.
5. **Bab 3.5: prehistory in Malaysia** — Malaysia's own chapter is dated: humans have lived in Malaysia for 40,000 years, since the Palaeolithic.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
