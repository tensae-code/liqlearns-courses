# Hands-on — From Stone Tools to Farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find a stone outside and a metal object at home. Hold one in each hand and say which age each belongs to, and what its maker could do that the other could not. That climb is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Life climbs from stone tools to farming in steps: the Ice Ages freeze and thaw the world, seas rise over the Sunda Shelf, and in Malaysia the Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic and Metal Ages are proven by its own caves — Gua Niah, Lenggong, Gua Cha and Bukit Tengkorak. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
