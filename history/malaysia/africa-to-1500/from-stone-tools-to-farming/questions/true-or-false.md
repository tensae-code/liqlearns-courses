# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Gua Niah's artefacts prove occupation through every prehistoric age.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The Mesolithic of Southeast Asia is called the Metal Age.
**Answer: False** — The Mesolithic of Southeast Asia is called the Hoabinhian.
