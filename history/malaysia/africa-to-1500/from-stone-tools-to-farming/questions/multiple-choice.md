# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How long have humans lived in Malaysia, as this lesson teaches?
a) 40,000 years  b) 400 years  c) 4,000 years  d) since 1957
**Answer: a) 40,000 years** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happened when the last Ice Age warmed?
a) the seas froze solid forever  b) farming stopped everywhere  c) the mammoth spread across Asia  d) seas rose over the Sunda Shelf and people and animals moved across Southeast Asia
**Answer: d) seas rose over the Sunda Shelf and people and animals moved across Southeast Asia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
