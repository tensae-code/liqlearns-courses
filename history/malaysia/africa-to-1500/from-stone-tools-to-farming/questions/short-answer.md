# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 5) Name the four steps of prehistory in Malaysia, in order.
**Answer:** Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic, Metal Age.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name two Malaysian prehistoric sites besides Gua Niah.
**Answer:** Any two of: Lenggong in Perak, Gua Cha in Kelantan, Bukit Tengkorak in Sabah.
