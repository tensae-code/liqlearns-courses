# Find History in Your Own Locality — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — pupils learn what an artefact is and work the Plain of Jars as the model — then go and record the old things in their own district. — in their own words, retell the story (who, where, what turned, what came of it), and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain story as told in the lesson, then tellings from the later, deeper books stocked in this lesson, then the story applied in a new place — the learner's own locality and people, as Laos's own books themselves close each year by asking.
