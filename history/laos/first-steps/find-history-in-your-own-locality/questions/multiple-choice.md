# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Pupils learn what an artefact is and work the Plain of Jars as the model — then go and record the old things in their own district.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) that the past explains nothing about the present
