# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils learn what an artefact is and work the Plain of Jars as the model — then go and record the old things in their own district.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Find History in Your Own Locality" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
