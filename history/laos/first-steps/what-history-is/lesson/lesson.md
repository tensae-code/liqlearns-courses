# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "What History Is" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.


## First knowledge about the subject of history

History is the story of human society itself; stories, inscriptions and artefacts are the traces it leaves, and learning it explains the present.
