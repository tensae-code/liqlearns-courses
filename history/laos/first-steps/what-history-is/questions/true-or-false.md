# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History is the story of human society itself; stories, inscriptions and artefacts are the traces it leaves, and learning it explains the present.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "What History Is" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
