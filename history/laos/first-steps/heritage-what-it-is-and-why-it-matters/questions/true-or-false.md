# Heritage — What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Plain of Jars, Luang Prabang and Wat Phou are taught as the nation's heritage — places whose stories visitors cross the world to hear.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Heritage — What It Is and Why It Matters" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
