# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils convert between Buddhist Era and Christian Era years (BE 2553 = CE 2010, 543 years apart) and date a Lao independence day both ways.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Dating the Past" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
