# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Dating the Past" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.


## Calculating time in history

Pupils convert between Buddhist Era and Christian Era years (BE 2553 = CE 2010, 543 years apart) and date a Lao independence day both ways.

## Calculating time in history

Buddhist Era and Christian Era years are converted back and forth (543 years apart) so Lao and world dates can meet.

Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Dating, the, Past. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
