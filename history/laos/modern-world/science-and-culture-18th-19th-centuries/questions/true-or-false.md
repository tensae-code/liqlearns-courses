# Science and Culture, 18th–19th Centuries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Engines, Darwin, novels and paintings — a civilisation remakes its tools and its imagination together.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Science and Culture, 18th–19th Centuries" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
