# The First Coalition, 1957 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** America steps into France's place; the Pathet Lao wins a coalition seat in 1957 and is then pushed out.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The First Coalition, 1957" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
