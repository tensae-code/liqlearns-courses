# The Third International and Revolutionary Movements — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — after 1917 revolution is attempted across Europe and America, and the Comintern is founded to lead it. — in their own words, retell the story (who, where, what turned, what came of it), and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain story as told in the lesson, then tellings from the later, deeper books stocked in this lesson, then the story applied in a new place — the learner's own locality and people, as Laos's own books themselves close each year by asking.
