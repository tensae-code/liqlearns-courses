# The Third International and Revolutionary Movements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After 1917 revolution is attempted across Europe and America, and the Comintern is founded to lead it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Third International and Revolutionary Movements" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
