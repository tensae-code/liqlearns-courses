# America's Escalating Special War in Laos — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The secret war — Hmong armies, massive bombing — and the Lao people's struggle deepens in answer.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "America's Escalating Special War in Laos" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
