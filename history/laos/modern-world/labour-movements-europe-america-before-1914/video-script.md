# Video script — Labour Movements in Europe and America Before 1914

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Labour Movements in Europe and America Before 1914.

Unions and socialist parties grow across Europe and America as industry swallows the old world.

Unions and parties grow on both sides of the Atlantic.

Mass parties and unions force the vote, the eight-hour day and social laws onto the agenda.

Mass parties and unions force the vote and the eight-hour day.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
