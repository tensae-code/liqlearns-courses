# The Second World War — Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Versailles grievances, depression and appeasement let fascism arm for war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Second World War — Causes" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
