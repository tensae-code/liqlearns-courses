# Imperialism and Capitalism in Europe and America — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Capitalism outgrows its home markets and turns imperial — monopolies reach for the whole world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Imperialism and Capitalism in Europe and America" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
