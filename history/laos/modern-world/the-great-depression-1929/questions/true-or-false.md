# The Great Depression, 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1929 crash throws millions out of work and shakes faith in the capitalist order.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Great Depression, 1929" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
