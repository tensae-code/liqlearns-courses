# Laos, 1945–1954 — the Second French War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France returns with troops; the free government retreats to the countryside and guerrilla war begins.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Laos, 1945–1954 — the Second French War" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
