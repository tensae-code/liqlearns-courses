# China, Vietnam and the World Since 1991 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** China and Vietnam renovate socialism and rise, while the post-1991 world finds new tensions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China, Vietnam and the World Since 1991" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
