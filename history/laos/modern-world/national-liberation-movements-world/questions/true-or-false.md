# National Liberation Movements of the World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Colonised peoples everywhere begin to organise, rise and demand their countries back.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "National Liberation Movements of the World" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
