# The Russian Revolution of 1905 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** 1905 — workers, peasants and mutinous sailors shake the Tsar; the dress rehearsal for 1917.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Russian Revolution of 1905" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
