# The Paris Commune, 1871 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Paris workers hold their city for two months in 1871 — the first workers' government, drowned in blood.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Paris Commune, 1871" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
