# The English Revolution and Civil War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** England's bourgeoisie rises against the king, and the old order is tried and executed with him.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The English Revolution and Civil War" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
