# Video script — The English Revolution and Civil War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The English Revolution and Civil War.

England's bourgeoisie rises against the king, and the old order is tried and executed with him.

King against parliament; a king is executed and a new order begins.

King and parliament fight for England's future; the bourgeoisie wins and the road to modern Britain opens.

King and parliament fight for England's future; the bourgeoisie wins.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
