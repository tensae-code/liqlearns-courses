# The World After 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war ends and the world splits — Eastern Europe goes one way, the West another, and every country must pick its road.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The World After 1945" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
