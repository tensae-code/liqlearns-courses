# Hands-on — The World After 1945

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The war ends and the world splits Say each part aloud as you do it, and hold the story's turn in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's story: draw the map of where "The World After 1945" happened, place the people in it, and act the turn with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story in real life — at home, at school, in the street: a place, a name, an old thing, a story an elder tells. Each time you meet it, note it: what it was, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
