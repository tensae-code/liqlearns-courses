# The World After 1945

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The World After 1945" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.


## The world after the Second World War

The war ends and the world splits — Eastern Europe goes one way, the West another, and every country must pick its road.

## The world after WWII

Two superpowers, a divided Europe and a wave of new nations open the postwar age.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the war ends and the world splits — Eastern Europe goes one way, the West another, and every country must pick its road. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The, World, After, 1945. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's story in your own words: the war ends and the world splits — Eastern Europe goes one way, the West another, and every country must pick its road.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the story happens in real life. Note it.
