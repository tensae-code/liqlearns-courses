# Laos, 1962–1973 — Second Coalition and War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A second coalition is agreed, broken, and the war widens across the whole country.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Laos, 1962–1973 — Second Coalition and War" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
