# The Lao Struggle Against France, 1893–1930 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From Ong Keo's Bolaven rising to the first modern movements, the resistance never fully goes out.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Lao Struggle Against France, 1893–1930" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
