# The Geneva Accords, 1954 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dien Bien Phu breaks French power and Geneva recognises Laos — the first great victory.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Geneva Accords, 1954" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
