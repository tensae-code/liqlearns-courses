# France Conquers Laos, 1893 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Laos, already weakened under Siamese rule, is invaded and cut up by France in 1893.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "France Conquers Laos, 1893" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
