# France Conquers Laos, 1893 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** The French colonialists seize Laos — a)

**2.** France intervenes and invades Laos — b)


Meanings:
a) Laos, already weakened under Siamese rule, is invaded and cut up by France in 1893.
b) Gunboats on the Mekong; in 1893 Siam yields Laos to France.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
