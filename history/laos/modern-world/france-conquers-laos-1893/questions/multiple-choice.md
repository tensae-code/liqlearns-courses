# France Conquers Laos, 1893 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Laos, already weakened under Siamese rule, is invaded and cut up by France in 1893.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) that the past explains nothing about the present
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's story?
a) Laos, already weakened under Siamese rule, is invaded and cut up by France in 1893.  b) a voyage to the moon  c) the invention of the telephone  d) a football final
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
