# France Conquers Laos, 1893

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "France Conquers Laos, 1893" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.


## The French colonialists seize Laos

Laos, already weakened under Siamese rule, is invaded and cut up by France in 1893.

## France intervenes and invades Laos

Gunboats on the Mekong; in 1893 Siam yields Laos to France.
