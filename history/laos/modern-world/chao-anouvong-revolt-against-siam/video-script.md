# Video script — Chao Anouvong's Revolt Against Siam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Chao Anouvong's Revolt Against Siam.

Chao Anouvong rebuilds Vientiane's Lan Xang, then rises against Siam and loses everything in the attempt.

Anouvong rebuilds Vientiane's Lan Xang, rises against Siam, and loses everything in the attempt.

Anouvong rebuilds Vientiane, marches on Bangkok to free his people, and is crushed — Vientiane is razed.

Anouvong marches on Bangkok to free his people and is crushed.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
