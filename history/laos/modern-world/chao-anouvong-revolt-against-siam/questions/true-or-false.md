# Chao Anouvong's Revolt Against Siam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Chao Anouvong rebuilds Vientiane's Lan Xang, then rises against Siam and loses everything in the attempt.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Chao Anouvong's Revolt Against Siam" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
