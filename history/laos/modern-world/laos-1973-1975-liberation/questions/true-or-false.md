# Laos, 1973–1975 — Liberation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The third coalition opens the road, the old regime falls, and the Lao PDR is proclaimed in 1975.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Laos, 1973–1975 — Liberation" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
