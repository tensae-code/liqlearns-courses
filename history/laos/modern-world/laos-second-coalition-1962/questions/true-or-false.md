# The Second Coalition, 1962 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Kong Le's coup and the Plain of Jars fighting, a second coalition and Geneva neutrality are agreed in 1962.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Second Coalition, 1962" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
