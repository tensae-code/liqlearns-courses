# Lao Resistance — Central and Southern Laos — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ong Keo and Ong Kommadam raise the Bolaven and the centre against the French and hold out for years.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Lao Resistance — Central and Southern Laos" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
