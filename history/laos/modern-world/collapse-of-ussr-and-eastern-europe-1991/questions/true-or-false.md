# The Collapse of the USSR and Eastern Europe, 1991 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Reform turns to dissolution; the socialist bloc ends and a unipolar moment begins.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Collapse of the USSR and Eastern Europe, 1991" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
