# The Birth of Capitalism

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The Birth of Capitalism" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.


## The birth of capitalism

Trade, towns and money crack the feudal order open and a new class society begins to grow inside it.

## The birth of capitalism

Inside the feudal order, merchants, workshops and wage labour grow the seed of a new system.

## The decline of feudalism and the birth of capitalism

Towns, trade and money dissolve feudal bonds and the capitalist order is born.

## Feudalism decays; capitalism is born

Towns, trade and money eat the feudal order from inside.
