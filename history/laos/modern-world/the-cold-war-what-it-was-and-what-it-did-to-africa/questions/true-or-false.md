# The Cold War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two blocs face off for forty years; the fighting is pushed into Asia and Africa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Cold War" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
