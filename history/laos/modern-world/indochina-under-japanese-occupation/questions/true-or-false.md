# Indochina Under Japanese Occupation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Japan seizes Indochina, rules through the French, and starves the people — the old order cracks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Indochina Under Japanese Occupation" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
