# The USA, Japan and Western Europe After 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** America leads the West, ruined Japan rebuilds into an economic power, and Western Europe unites and prospers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The USA, Japan and Western Europe After 1945" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
