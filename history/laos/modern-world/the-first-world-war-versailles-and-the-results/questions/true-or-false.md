# Versailles and the Results of the First World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The victors redraw the world at Versailles and plant the grievances of the next war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Versailles and the Results of the First World War" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
