# Laos Under Siam, 1829–1893 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sixty years of tribute, corvée and deportations under Bangkok — the low point of the Lao kingdoms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Laos Under Siam, 1829–1893" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
