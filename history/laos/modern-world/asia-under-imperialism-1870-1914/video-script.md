# Video script — Asia Under Imperialism, 1870–1914

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Asia Under Imperialism, 1870–1914.

Japan modernises and turns empire-builder while China and India are carved up and held down by the Western powers.

Japan modernises to resist, China is carved up, India is held — Asia answers empire three ways.

The empires partition Asia — India, China, Indochina and the rest are seized or subordinated one by one.

The empires partition Asia one country at a time.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
