# Asia Under Imperialism, 1870–1914 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Japan modernises and turns empire-builder while China and India are carved up and held down by the Western powers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Asia Under Imperialism, 1870–1914" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
