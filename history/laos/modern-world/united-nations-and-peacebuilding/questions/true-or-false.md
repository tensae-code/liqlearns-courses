# The United Nations and Peacebuilding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The UN is built to keep the peace the League could not — its promise and its limits.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The United Nations and Peacebuilding" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
