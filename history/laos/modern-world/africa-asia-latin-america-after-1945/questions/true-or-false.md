# Africa, Asia and Latin America After 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Colony after colony wins independence and the three continents fight to develop on their own terms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Africa, Asia and Latin America After 1945" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
