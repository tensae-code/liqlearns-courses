# The Lao PDR, 1975–1985 — Building Socialism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The new republic defends itself and lays the first foundations of a socialist economy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Lao PDR, 1975–1985 — Building Socialism" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
