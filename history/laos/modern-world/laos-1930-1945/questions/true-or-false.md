# Laos, 1930–1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1930 a communist party is founded for all Indochina, and Laos's modern resistance gains its organiser.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Laos, 1930–1945" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
