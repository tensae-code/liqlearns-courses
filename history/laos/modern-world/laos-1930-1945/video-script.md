# Video script — Laos, 1930–1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Laos, 1930–1945.

In 1930 a communist party is founded for all Indochina, and Laos's modern resistance gains its organiser.

A communist party for all Indochina gives Laos's resistance its organiser.

Depression, the Indochinese Communist Party's first cells, and Japan's wartime seizure set the stage for 1945.

Depression, the Party's first cells, and Japan's approach set the stage for 1945.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
