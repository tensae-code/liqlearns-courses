# Siam Invades Lan Xang, 1778–1804 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Siam conquers the Lao kingdoms, carries off the Emerald Buddha and the people, and the Lao resist.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Siam Invades Lan Xang, 1778–1804" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
