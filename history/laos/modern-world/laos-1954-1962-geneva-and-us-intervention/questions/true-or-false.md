# Laos, 1954–1962 — Geneva and US Intervention — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Geneva the Lao people's government forms, and American intervention begins to pull the country back into war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Laos, 1954–1962 — Geneva and US Intervention" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
