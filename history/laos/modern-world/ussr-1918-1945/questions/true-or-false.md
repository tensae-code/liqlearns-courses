# The USSR, 1918–1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Soviet Union survives civil war, industrialises in a rush, and breaks Nazi Germany at terrible cost.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The USSR, 1918–1945" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
