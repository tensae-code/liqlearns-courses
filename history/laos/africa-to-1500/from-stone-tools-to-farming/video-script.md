# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: From Stone Tools to Farming.

Humans appear in East Africa 3–4 million years ago, chip stone tools, master fire, and end by farming — the long first act of everyone's history.

Humans appear in East Africa millions of years ago, chip stone, master fire, and end by farming.

Human kinds spread across the world, tool-making improves age by age, and farming ends the wandering life.

From first tools to settled farming — the long first chapter of everyone.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
