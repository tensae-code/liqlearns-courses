# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Humans appear in East Africa 3–4 million years ago, chip stone tools, master fire, and end by farming — the long first act of everyone's history.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "From Stone Tools to Farming" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
