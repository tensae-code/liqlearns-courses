# Slave Society — the First Class States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Private property and the division of labour split people into masters and slaves, and the first class states (Egypt the worked example) are born.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Slave Society — the First Class States" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
