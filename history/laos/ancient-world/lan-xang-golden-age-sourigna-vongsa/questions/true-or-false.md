# Lan Xang's Golden Age — Sourigna Vongsa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sourigna Vongsa reforms the administration, grows the economy and opens relations abroad in Lan Xang's second flourishing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Lan Xang's Golden Age — Sourigna Vongsa" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
