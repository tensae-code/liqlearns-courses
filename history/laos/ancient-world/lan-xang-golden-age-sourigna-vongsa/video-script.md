The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

This lesson tells one story: Lan Xang's Golden Age — Sourigna Vongsa.

Sourigna Vongsa reforms the administration, grows the economy and opens relations abroad in Lan Xang's second flourishing.

Sourigna Vongsa reforms administration, grows the economy and opens foreign relations.

Sourigna Vongsa's long reign is the golden age — ordered laws, rich trade, embassies from Europe.

Sourigna Vongsa's long peace — law, trade and embassies.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.
