# Lan Xang — Building and Defence, 1480–1600 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kings build temples and laws at home while Setthathirath moves the capital to Vientiane and faces Burma.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Lan Xang — Building and Defence, 1480–1600" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
