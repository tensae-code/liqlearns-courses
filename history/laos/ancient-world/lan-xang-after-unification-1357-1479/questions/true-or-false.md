# Lan Xang After Unification, 1357–1479 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fa Ngum's heirs hold the kingdom together and repel invasion from Dai Viet.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Lan Xang After Unification, 1357–1479" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
