# Video script — Lan Xang Divides — Three Kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Lan Xang Divides — Three Kingdoms.

Princes quarrel over the succession, the kingdom splits into rival Lao states, and Siam moves in to rule them.

Princes quarrel over the throne, the kingdom splits, and Siam moves in.

Sourigna Vongsa dies, the succession is disputed, and Lan Xang splits into Vientiane, Luang Prabang and Champasak.

The succession is disputed and Lan Xang splits into three kingdoms.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
