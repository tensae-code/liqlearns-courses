# Lan Xang Flourishing, 1353–1560 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fa Ngum's successors order the kingdom and deal with Dai Viet and Ayutthaya as an equal power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Lan Xang Flourishing, 1353–1560" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
