# Laos's Archaeology and Ancient Towns — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Stone-age and metal-age traces lead to the first Lao mueang — Sikhottabong, Vientiane and the other old towns.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Laos's Archaeology and Ancient Towns" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
