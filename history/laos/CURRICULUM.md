# History — Laos course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Laos inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grade the book comes from appears only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Laos's own History books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

**How the ladder is derived:** Laos's books tell one long story twice over — once in the lower-secondary years and again, deeper, in the upper-secondary years. The ladder follows the books' own progression in their own order: each level is one year-arc of the country's telling, earliest first — first steps and the ancient world, then feudalism, revolutions and colonisation, the interwar years, the world after 1945, and then the same national story told again at greater depth, ending with Laos's thirty-year struggle, the Lao PDR and renovation. A lesson stands at the level of the earliest arc that teaches it; its later, deeper tellings are stocked into the same lesson. The ladder is a recommended path, never a forced gate. History is taught as stories: who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — First steps, the ancient world, and Lan Xang founded** — 16 lessons: What History Is; Dating the Past; From Stone Tools to Farming; Slave Society — the First Class States; Ancient Civilisations — Mesopotamia; Ancient Egypt — the Nile Kingdom; Ancient Civilisations — India; Ancient Civilisations — China; Laos's Archaeology and Ancient Towns; Fa Ngum Founds Lan Xang; Lan Xang Flourishing, 1353–1560; Lan Xang Resists Burma; Lan Xang's Golden Age — Sourigna Vongsa; Lan Xang Divides — Three Kingdoms; Chao Anouvong's Revolt Against Siam; Find History in Your Own Locality.

2. **Level 2 — Feudalism, revolutions, empire — and Laos colonised** — 18 lessons: The Feudal Order; The Birth of Capitalism; The English Revolution and Civil War; American Independence; The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789; Republic and Reign of Terror; The Industrial Revolution — Textiles, Steam and Rail; Marxism and the Workers' Movement; Labour Movements in Europe and America Before 1914; Asia Under Imperialism, 1870–1914; The First World War — Causes; The First World War — Course of the War; 1917 — February and October in Russia; France Conquers Laos, 1893; French Colonial Rule in Laos, 1893–1945; Lao Resistance — Central and Southern Laos; Lao Resistance — Northern Laos; Napoleon and the Revolution's Legacy.

3. **Level 3 — Between the wars, and Laos 1930–1954** — 12 lessons: Versailles and the Results of the First World War; The USSR, 1918–1945; The Third International and Revolutionary Movements; The World Revolutionary Movement, 1918–1939; The Great Depression, 1929; Fascism and Nazism; The Second World War — Course in Europe; Laos, 1930–1945; Indochina Under Japanese Occupation; Lao Issara — the First Independence, 1945; Laos, 1945–1954 — the Second French War; The Pathet Lao Founded, 1950.

4. **Level 4 — The world after 1945, and Laos to the Lao PDR** — 11 lessons: The World After 1945; The USA, Japan and Western Europe After 1945; Africa, Asia and Latin America After 1945; The New World Order After 1945; The Scientific-Technical Revolution After 1945; Laos, 1954–1962 — Geneva and US Intervention; Laos, 1962–1973 — Second Coalition and War; Laos, 1973–1975 — Liberation; The Lao PDR, 1975–1985 — Building Socialism; The Lao PDR — Renovation From 1986; Heritage — What It Is and Why It Matters.

5. **Level 5 — The ancient world in depth — and Laos before Lan Xang** — 7 lessons: Origins of Social Organisation; Ancient Civilisations — Greece and Crete; Ancient Civilisations — Rome; Feudal States of Asia; The Ancient Lao Mueang; Lan Xang After Unification, 1357–1479; Lan Xang — Building and Defence, 1480–1600.

6. **Level 6 — Revolutions and empire in depth — and Laos 1695–1945** — 10 lessons: Imperialism and Capitalism in Europe and America; The Paris Commune, 1871; Science and Culture, 18th–19th Centuries; Colonisation of Africa — the Berlin Conference; Imperialism in Latin America; National Liberation Movements of the World; The Russian Revolution of 1905; Siam Invades Lan Xang, 1778–1804; Laos Under Siam, 1829–1893; The Lao Struggle Against France, 1893–1930.

7. **Level 7 — World wars, Cold War, and Laos's thirty-year struggle** — 11 lessons: The Interwar World and Building Socialism; The Second World War — Causes; The USSR and Eastern Europe After 1945; The United Nations and Peacebuilding; The Cold War; The Collapse of the USSR and Eastern Europe, 1991; China, Vietnam and the World Since 1991; The Geneva Accords, 1954; The First Coalition, 1957; The Second Coalition, 1962; America's Escalating Special War in Laos.

**Totals: 85 lessons, 244 placements stocked** (Levels 1–7 as above; every lesson stands on a student book except one that stands on teacher-guide evidence only — see ABOUT.md).
