# Hands-on — Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A king's subject obeys because the king commands. A nation's citizen belongs because the country is, in some way, theirs. Say out loud: "A feeling built these states." This lesson tells how subjects became citizens.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Kings first centralised power into single states; then the idea of the nation turned subjects into citizens of national states. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
