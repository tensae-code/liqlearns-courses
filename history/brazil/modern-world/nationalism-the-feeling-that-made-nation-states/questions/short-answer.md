# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the idea of the nation, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** That a people sharing a land, a language and a story form one whole, and the state should be their state.

**2.** Why is a king's state weaker than it looks?
**Answer:** Because it belongs to the king alone: its people are subjects who obey but have no claim on the country as theirs.
