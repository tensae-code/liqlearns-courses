# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A state held together only by a king belongs to the king, not to the people in it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Nationalism is the name of a tax kings collected.
**Answer: False** — Nationalism is the feeling and idea of the nation — that a people form one whole and the state should be their state.
