# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did kings do to power in the early modern centuries?
a) centralised it — gathering armies, taxes and law into one centre  b) gave it all back to the feudal lords  c) abolished the state  d) shared it equally with every village
**Answer: a) centralised it — gathering armies, taxes and law into one centre** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the idea of the nation turn subjects into?
a) citizens of a national state  b) soldiers only  c) tenants of the king  d) strangers in their own land
**Answer: a) citizens of a national state** — That is what this lesson teaches.
