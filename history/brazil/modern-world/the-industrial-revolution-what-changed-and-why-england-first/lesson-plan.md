## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Inside industrialisation, factory workers answered the new discipline with machine-breaking, unions and strikes — the labour movement was born in the factory itself.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a strike or union they have heard of — a teachers', drivers' or health workers' stoppage — and what the workers demanded together that none could win alone.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
