# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What made the factory's discipline new?
**Answer:** Work was set by the clock and the bell, by the speed of the machine and the eye of the foreman, with long hours, low wages and dangerous machines.

**2.** Why could factory workers answer together when earlier workers could not as easily?
**Answer:** Because the factory gathered them in one place, all day, under one discipline — together, they could organise unions and strike.
