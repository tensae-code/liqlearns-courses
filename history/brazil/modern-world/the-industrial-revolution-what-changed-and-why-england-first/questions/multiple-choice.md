# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the workers' first, most direct answer to the new factory discipline?
a) machine-breaking  b) writing letters home  c) buying the factories  d) leaving the country
**Answer: a) machine-breaking** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What lasting organisation did workers build?
a) unions — and the strike, the collective refusal to work  b) a new church  c) a football league  d) nothing lasting
**Answer: a) unions — and the strike, the collective refusal to work** — That is what this lesson teaches.
