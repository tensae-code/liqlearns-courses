# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The labour movement was born inside industrialisation itself.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Factory work kept the old rhythms of farm and craft, with no new discipline.
**Answer: False** — The factory set work by the clock, the bell, the machine's speed and the foreman's eye — a new discipline.
