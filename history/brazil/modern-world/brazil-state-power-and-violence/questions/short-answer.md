# Brazil: State Power and Violence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What good does the monopoly of force promise?
**Answer:** That neighbours cannot settle quarrels by arms and the strong cannot simply take from the weak — force is bound by law, limited and answerable.

**2.** What line must a citizen look for when judging state force?
**Answer:** Whether the force stayed bound by law, or broke its limits and turned against the state's own people.
