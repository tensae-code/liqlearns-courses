# Brazil: State Power and Violence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the state claim about force?
a) a monopoly — it alone may use force lawfully  b) that force is never needed  c) that every citizen may use force freely  d) that force belongs to the richest
**Answer: a) a monopoly — it alone may use force lawfully** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When does state force become violence, in the chapter's question?
a) when it breaks its own legal limits and turns against the state's own people  b) never — state force is always law  c) whenever it is used at all  d) only when foreigners do it
**Answer: a) when it breaks its own legal limits and turns against the state's own people** — That is what this lesson teaches.
