# Brazil: State Power and Violence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under the state's claim, anyone else who uses force is a criminal.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The chapter hands pupils a ready-made answer instead of a question.
**Answer: False** — It hands them the question — when is state force law, and when is it violence — and the line to look for.
