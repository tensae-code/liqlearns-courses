# Hands-on — Brazil: Paraná Urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Every big story in this course so far happened somewhere else first. This one happens at home. Say out loud: "Our own state is in the world story." This lesson walks Paraná's towns — and stands on the dock at Paranaguá.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The world story lands at home: Paraná's own towns industrialised, and its port of Paranaguá shipped the state's produce into the capitalist world market. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
