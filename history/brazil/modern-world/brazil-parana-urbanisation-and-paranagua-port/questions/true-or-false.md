# Brazil: Paraná Urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Port of Paranaguá connected Paraná's produce to buyers in other lands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The world market had no effect on a farming state like Paraná.
**Answer: False** — Paraná's produce was sold inside the world market, at prices set by it — the port is where the state's fields met that market.
