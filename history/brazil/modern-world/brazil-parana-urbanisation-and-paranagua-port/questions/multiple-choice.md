# Brazil: Paraná Urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What role did the Port of Paranaguá play for Paraná?
a) it shipped the state's produce into the capitalist world market  b) it was only a fishing harbour  c) it blocked all trade  d) it minted Paraná's own money
**Answer: a) it shipped the state's produce into the capitalist world market** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened in Paraná's towns in this chapter?
a) they industrialised — workshops and factories rose and towns grew  b) they were abandoned  c) they became one single city  d) nothing changed
**Answer: a) they industrialised — workshops and factories rose and towns grew** — That is what this lesson teaches.
