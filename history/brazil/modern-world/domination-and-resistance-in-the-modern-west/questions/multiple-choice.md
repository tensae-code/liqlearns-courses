# Domination and Resistance in the Modern West — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which three peoples does this lesson tell the modern story through?
a) colonised peoples, enslaved Africans and the new working classes  b) kings, emperors and factory owners  c) priests, poets and merchants  d) only Europeans
**Answer: a) colonised peoples, enslaved Africans and the new working classes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did enslaved Africans resist, in the lesson's telling?
a) by revolt, by escape, and by holding onto forbidden languages, faiths and families  b) they did not resist  c) only by asking owners for freedom  d) by leaving the Americas at once
**Answer: a) by revolt, by escape, and by holding onto forbidden languages, faiths and families** — That is what this lesson teaches.
