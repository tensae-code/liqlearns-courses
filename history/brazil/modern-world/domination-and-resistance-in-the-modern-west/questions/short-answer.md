# Domination and Resistance in the Modern West — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is the story of the earlier chapters only half told without this lesson?
**Answer:** Because it was told from the top — states, empires, factories; the peoples it was done to, and their resistance, are the other half.

**2.** What one pattern joins the lesson's three peoples?
**Answer:** Each faced the modern West's domination, and each answered it with resistance.
