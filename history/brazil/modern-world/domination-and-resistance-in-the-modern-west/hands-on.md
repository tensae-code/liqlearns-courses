# Hands-on — Domination and Resistance in the Modern West

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You have heard this course's modern story from the top: states, empires, factories. Say out loud: "Now from underneath." This lesson retells it through the people it was done to — and what they did back.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Colonised peoples, enslaved Africans and the new working classes resisted the modern West's domination — the story of the earlier chapters told from underneath. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
