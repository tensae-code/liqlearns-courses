# Video script — Domination and Resistance in the Modern West

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Colonised peoples, enslaved Africans and the new working classes resisted the modern West's domination — the story of the earlier chapters told from underneath.

This chapter gathers the whole modern story of the course and turns it over. The national states, the empires, the factories of the earlier units were built — and this lesson asks on whom they were built, and how those people answered.

First, the **colonised peoples**: the peoples of the lands the empires carved up, ruled from far capitals, their lands and labour taken into other nations' wealth. Colonisation was never simply accepted; colonised peoples resisted it in every form their circumstances allowed.

Then the **enslaved Africans**: carried across the Atlantic into the slave societies of the Americas — Brazil among them, as this course's own slavery lesson told — and resisting from the ships onward: by revolt, by escape, by holding onto forbidden languages, faiths and families under a system built to erase them.

And the **new working classes** of the industrial world: the wage workers of Unit I, who answered factory domination with organisation of their own — the subject the next lesson takes up in full. Colonised, enslaved, waged: three peoples, one pattern. The modern West's domination was answered, everywhere, by **resistance** — and the story of the earlier chapters is only half told until it is told from underneath.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Brazil.
