# Brazil: From Slavery to Free Labour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did the road out of slavery differ between Brazil and the United States?
**Answer:** Brazil ended slavery by gradual laws over decades, to abolition in 1888; the United States ended it through civil war.

**2.** Why does this chapter belong in a unit about work?
**Answer:** Because the freed, given no land or tools, had to sell their labour — abolition runs straight into the story of wage labour.
