# Brazil: From Slavery to Free Labour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did slavery end in Brazil?
a) by gradual laws, step by step, to abolition in 1888  b) by civil war  c) it never ended  d) by a slave revolt that won in one year
**Answer: a) by gradual laws, step by step, to abolition in 1888** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the freed people receive in both countries at abolition?
a) no land and no rights — they had to compete as wage workers  b) a farm each  c) full voting rights at once  d) a share of their former owners' wealth
**Answer: a) no land and no rights — they had to compete as wage workers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
