# Hands-on — Brazil: From Slavery to Free Labour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two countries, both built on enslaved labour, both ending slavery in the same century. Say out loud: "The road out matters." This lesson follows the two different roads — and what waited for the freed at the end of each.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Brazil and the United States ended slavery by different roads — Brazil by gradual laws to abolition in 1888, the United States through civil war — and both left the freed people with no land and no rights, to compete as wage workers. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
