# Brazil: From Slavery to Free Labour — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Brazil and the United States ended slavery by different roads — Brazil by gradual laws to abolition in 1888, the United States through civil war — and both left the freed people with no land and no rights, to compete as wage workers. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
