# Brazil: Urbanisation and Industrialisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Brazil's cities grew faster than homes, water and work could follow.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A city can feed itself entirely from its own streets, with no countryside.
**Answer: False** — From the first cities on, the countryside feeds the city; the city gives back crafts, trade, government and ideas.
