# Brazil: Urbanisation and Industrialisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What bargain does a city strike with the countryside, from the first cities on?
**Answer:** The countryside feeds the city; the city gives back crafts, trade, government and ideas.

**2.** Why was Brazil's urban growth so hard to live through?
**Answer:** Because it was explosive — millions arrived in a few decades, faster than homes, water and work could be provided.
