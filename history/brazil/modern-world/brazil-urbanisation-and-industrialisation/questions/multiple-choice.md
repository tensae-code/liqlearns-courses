# Brazil: Urbanisation and Industrialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What pulled country people into Brazil's cities in the 20th century?
a) industry — factories needing hands, offering wages  b) a law ordering them to move  c) the closing of all farms  d) nothing; the cities emptied
**Answer: a) industry — factories needing hands, offering wages** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the fast-growing cities fail to keep up with?
a) homes, water and work for the newcomers  b) rainfall  c) football stadiums  d) churches
**Answer: a) homes, water and work for the newcomers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
