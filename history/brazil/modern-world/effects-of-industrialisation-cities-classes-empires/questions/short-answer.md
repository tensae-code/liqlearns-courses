# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes and Empires — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did the industrial city remake class?
**Answer:** It divided the city into two clearly divided peoples — the owners of the factories and the workers who filled them — often living in different districts.

**2.** Why is the 19th-century industrial city not just a museum piece?
**Answer:** Because its problems — crowding, pollution and segregation — are still the problems of today's cities.
