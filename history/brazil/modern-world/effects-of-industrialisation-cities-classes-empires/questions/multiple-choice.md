# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes and Empires — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three problems of the industrial city does the lesson trace into today's cities?
a) crowding, pollution and segregation  b) floods, earthquakes and storms  c) noise, traffic lights and parking  d) none — its problems all ended
**Answer: a) crowding, pollution and segregation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the industrial city change the family?
a) work left the home for the factory, and family life bent around machine hours and shifts  b) families stopped working altogether  c) children ran the factories  d) families moved back to farms
**Answer: a) work left the home for the factory, and family life bent around machine hours and shifts** — That is what this lesson teaches.
