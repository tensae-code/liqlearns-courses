# Effects of Industrialisation: Cities, Classes and Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the industrial city, factory owners and workers often lived in different districts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Crowding, pollution and segregation were solved completely in the 1800s.
**Answer: False** — The lesson traces all three into today's cities.
