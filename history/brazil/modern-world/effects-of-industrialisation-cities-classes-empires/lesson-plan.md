## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The industrial city of the 1800s remade class, family and daily life — and its problems of crowding, pollution and segregation are still the problems of today's cities.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: their own city or town: one crowded place, one polluted place, one way neighbourhoods are divided — and which of the three problems each shows.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
