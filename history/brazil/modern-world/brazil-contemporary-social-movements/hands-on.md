# Hands-on — Brazil: Contemporary Social Movements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The last lesson of the book is not in the past. Look out of the window: marches, strikes, occupations, banners. Say out loud: "This unit ends in our own streets." This lesson meets the movements of the pupils' own lifetime.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From 1968's slogan "is it forbidden to forbid?" to Brazil's own movements, students, workers, women and Black and landless movements contest power in the pupils' own lifetime. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
