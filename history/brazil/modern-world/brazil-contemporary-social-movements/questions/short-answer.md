# Brazil: Contemporary Social Movements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does every movement in this chapter have in common?
**Answer:** Each contests power — each says some part of how society is run is not natural and not final, and can be changed by people acting together.

**2.** Why does the book end with this chapter?
**Answer:** Because it lands the whole book — work, cities, the state, domination and resistance — in the pupils' own lifetime and streets.
