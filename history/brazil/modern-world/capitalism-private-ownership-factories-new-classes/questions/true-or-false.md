# Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories and New Classes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Labour is one natural, unchanging thing, the same in every society. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: work is organised differently in slave, feudal and capitalist societies — labour itself has a history.

**2.** Much work in Brazil today is precarious or informal — short contracts, or no contract at all. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True**
