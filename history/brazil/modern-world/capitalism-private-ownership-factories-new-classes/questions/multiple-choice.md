# Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories and New Classes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the book's comparison, what does an enslaved worker own? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing — the enslaved person is owned by another, and everything they make belongs to the owner  b) the land they farm  c) the tools of a trade  d) a wage paid each week
**Answer: a) nothing — the enslaved person is owned by another, and everything they make belongs to the owner** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What must a worker sell when they own neither land nor tools? (Sub lesson 2)
a) their labour, for a wage  b) their house  c) their vote  d) nothing — they need not work
**Answer: a) their labour, for a wage** — That is what this lesson teaches.
