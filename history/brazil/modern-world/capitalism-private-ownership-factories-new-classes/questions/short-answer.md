# Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories and New Classes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three societies the book compares, and say in one phrase how work is organised in each. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Slave society — the worker is owned; feudal society — the worker is tied to the land and the lord; capitalist society — the free worker sells their labour for a wage.

**2.** Why does the book call the wage worker both free and unfree? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The wage worker is free to leave one employer — owned by no one — but owns no land or tools, so they must find an employer or go without; they are free to starve if no one hires them.
