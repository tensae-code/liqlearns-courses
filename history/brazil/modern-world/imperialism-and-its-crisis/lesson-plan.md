# Lesson plan — Imperialism and Its Crisis

**Course:** History — Brazil · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Imperialism and Its Crisis
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The State and Power (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Capitalist states carved the world into empires — and the rivalry between those empires brought the imperial order to crisis and world war.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The State and Power — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Capitalist states carved the world into empires — and the rivalry between those empires brought the imperial order to crisis and world war.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: two big shops or teams in their town whose rivalry grows because there is no room left for both to expand — and what the lesson would predict.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
