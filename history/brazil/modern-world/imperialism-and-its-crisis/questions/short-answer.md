# Imperialism and Its Crisis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did a fully carved-up world make war more likely, not less?
**Answer:** Because every land was claimed, the empires could only grow at each other's expense — rivalry, alliances and arms races had nowhere else to go.

**2.** What two halves make up this lesson's story?
**Answer:** The carving of the world into empires, and the crisis of that order as imperial rivalry broke into world war.
