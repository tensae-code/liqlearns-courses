# Imperialism and Its Crisis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the capitalist states do to the world in the age of imperialism?
a) carved it up into empires ruled from far-away capitals  b) gave every land its independence  c) divided it equally among its own peoples  d) left it untouched
**Answer: a) carved it up into empires ruled from far-away capitals** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What brought the imperial order to crisis?
a) rivalry between the empires, breaking open as world war  b) a shortage of flags  c) the empires all becoming friends  d) nothing — it never faced crisis
**Answer: a) rivalry between the empires, breaking open as world war** — That is what this lesson teaches.
