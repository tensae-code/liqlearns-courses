# Imperialism and Its Crisis — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Imperialism grew out of capitalist states needing raw materials and markets beyond their own lands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Once the world was carved up, the empires had plenty of empty space left to grow into peacefully.
**Answer: False** — With no empty space left, each empire's growth was another's loss — rivalry sharpened to world war.
