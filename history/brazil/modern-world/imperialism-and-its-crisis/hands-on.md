# Hands-on — Imperialism and Its Crisis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a map of the world in your head, then watch other people's flags planted across most of it. Say out loud: "The carving-up could not hold." This lesson tells how the empires were made — and how they turned on each other.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Capitalist states carved the world into empires — and the rivalry between those empires brought the imperial order to crisis and world war. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
