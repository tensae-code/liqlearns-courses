# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three forms of the state this lesson meets, in order.
**Answer:** The Greek city-state; the Roman empire; feudal lordship.

**2.** Why does a state appear when societies grow large?
**Answer:** Someone must organise the whole — keep order, settle disputes, raise armies and collect what the rulers need; that organising power standing over society is the state.
