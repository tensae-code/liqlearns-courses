# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When is the state born, in the book's telling?
a) with the first civilisations, as societies grew large  b) in the year 1888  c) with the first factories  d) it has always existed
**Answer: a) with the first civilisations, as societies grew large** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What form did the state take in the Roman world?
a) an empire — one power ruling many peoples and lands  b) a single village  c) a lordship of many small lords only  d) no state at all
**Answer: a) an empire — one power ruling many peoples and lands** — That is what this lesson teaches.
