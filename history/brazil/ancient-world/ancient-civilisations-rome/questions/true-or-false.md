# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the Greek world, the state took the form of the city-state.
**Answer: True**

**2.** After Rome fell in the west, one single empire ruled all Europe at once.
**Answer: False** — Power broke into feudal lordship — many lords, each ruling his own land and people.
