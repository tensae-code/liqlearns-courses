# Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Heresy — belief differing from the Church's teaching — was itself a form of resistance.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Peasants in feudal Europe were free to leave the land whenever they wished.
**Answer: False** — Peasants were tied to the land, working the lord's fields beside their own.
