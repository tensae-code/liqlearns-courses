# Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which two powers ruled feudal Europe's lowest together?
a) lordship and the Church  b) the kings and the merchants  c) the towns and the seas  d) the peasants themselves
**Answer: a) lordship and the Church** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the medieval lowest answer their domination?
a) with revolts against lords and forbidden beliefs — heresy  b) by leaving Europe  c) they never answered  d) by buying the castles
**Answer: a) with revolts against lords and forbidden beliefs — heresy** — That is what this lesson teaches.
