# Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the groups this lesson stands with at the bottom of feudal society.
**Answer:** Peasants, artisans, women, heretics and the sick — the ill and the different pushed to society's edge.

**2.** Why is heresy counted as resistance, when it is about belief?
**Answer:** Because the Church claimed command of belief itself; to believe differently was to refuse that command — resistance in the realm of the soul.
