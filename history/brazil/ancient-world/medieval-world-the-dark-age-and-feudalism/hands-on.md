# Hands-on — Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A peasant, a woman accused of forbidden belief, a sick person turned away — the bottom of the feudal world. Say out loud: "Even the lowest answered back." This lesson hears their answers.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Feudal Europe's lowest — peasants, heretics, women, the ill — endured lordship and the Church, and answered with revolts and forbidden beliefs. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
