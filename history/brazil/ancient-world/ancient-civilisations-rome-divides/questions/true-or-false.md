# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Slave revolts shook the ancient world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Women in Greek and Roman societies held full rights of their own.
**Answer: False** — Women lived without rights of their own, under the authority of fathers and husbands, shut out of public life.
