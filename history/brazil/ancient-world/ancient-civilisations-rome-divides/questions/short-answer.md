# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What claim does the unit open with, and how does this lesson prove it?
**Answer:** That resistance begins with the first states — proved by women shut out of rights, plebeians fighting for power and slaves revolting in Greece and Rome themselves.

**2.** How is this lesson's camera angle different from the earlier Rome lesson?
**Answer:** It tells the Greek and Roman worlds from underneath — from the people the glory stood on, not from the rulers.
