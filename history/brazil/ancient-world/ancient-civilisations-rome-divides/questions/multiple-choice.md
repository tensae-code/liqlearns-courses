# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who are the three groups this lesson stands underneath Greek and Roman glory?
a) women without rights, plebeians, and slaves  b) kings, generals and priests  c) merchants, sailors and poets  d) the emperors only
**Answer: a) women without rights, plebeians, and slaves** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Rome's plebeians do about being shut out of power?
a) fought for a share of power, winning ground step by step  b) left Rome forever  c) asked the patricians politely once  d) nothing
**Answer: a) fought for a share of power, winning ground step by step** — That is what this lesson teaches.
