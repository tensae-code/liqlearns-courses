# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Every glory has people standing underneath it. Say out loud: "Ask who is underneath." This lesson looks under the glory of Greece and Rome — and finds people pushing back from the very start.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Under Greek and Roman glory stand women without rights, plebeians fighting for a share of power, and slaves who revolt — resistance begins with the first states. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
