# The Marinids and Morocco's Civilisational Creativity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How is Marinid creativity shown?
**Answer:** Through madrasa architecture — colleges in Fez built richly in carved plaster, tile and cedar.

**2.** What two kinds of legacy do the Marinids leave?
**Answer:** Buildings — the madrasas of Fez — and a living tradition, the Mawlid celebration kept to today.
