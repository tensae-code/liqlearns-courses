# Hands-on — Prehistoric Morocco: The First Moroccans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Go outside and pick up one stone. Now imagine you have no knife, no pot, no shop and no house — only stones like this one, animals to hunt, and a cave or a shelter to find before dark. Everything you would need to live, you would have to make or find. Hold that thought: it is the whole of this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: bones and tools in Moroccan soil show the first Moroccans hunting, sheltering and surviving long before any state, any city or any writing. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
