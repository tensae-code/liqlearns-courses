# Prehistoric Morocco: The First Moroccans — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — bones and tools in Moroccan soil show the first Moroccans hunting, sheltering and surviving long before any state, any city or any writing — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
