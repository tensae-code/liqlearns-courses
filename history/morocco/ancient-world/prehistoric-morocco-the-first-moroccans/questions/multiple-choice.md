# Prehistoric Morocco: The First Moroccans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How do we know about the first Moroccans?
a) from their remains — bones and tools left in the soil  b) from books they wrote  c) from photographs  d) we know nothing about them
**Answer: a) from their remains — bones and tools left in the soil** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the first Moroccans get their food?
a) they hunted  b) they bought it in markets  c) they farmed with machines  d) they were fed by a king
**Answer: a) they hunted** — That is what this lesson teaches.
