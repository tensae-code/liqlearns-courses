# Prehistoric Morocco: The First Moroccans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two things found in the soil that tell us about the first Moroccans.
**Answer:** Their remains (bones) and their tools.

**2.** Why does Morocco's story begin with these people?
**Answer:** Because they are the true beginning of the line — every later story stands on ground they already walked.
