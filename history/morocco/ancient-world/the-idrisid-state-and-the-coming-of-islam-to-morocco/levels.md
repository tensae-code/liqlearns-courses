# The Idrisid State and the Coming of Islam to Morocco — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — a refugee descendant of the Prophet founds a state, and his successors' city builds the enduring university-mosque — the Idrisid state, the spread of Islam in Morocco, Fez, and the Qarawiyyin — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
