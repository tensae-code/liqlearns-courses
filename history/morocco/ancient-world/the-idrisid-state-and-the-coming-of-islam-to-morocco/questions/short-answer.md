# The Idrisid State and the Coming of Islam to Morocco — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two things does the lesson follow in the Idrisid state?
**Answer:** Its founding and its extent — and the city of Fez in Idrisid times.

**2.** Why does the book stop at the Qarawiyyin for this dynasty?
**Answer:** As the dynasty's monument study: a mosque-university that endures through all the dynasties that follow.
