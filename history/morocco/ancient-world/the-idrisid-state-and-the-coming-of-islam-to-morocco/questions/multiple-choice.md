# The Idrisid State and the Coming of Islam to Morocco — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What rises around Idris, the descendant of the Prophet who reached Morocco?
a) the Idrisid state  b) the Roman empire  c) the city of Lixus  d) a trading company
**Answer: a) the Idrisid state** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the Qarawiyyin in Fez?
a) a mosque-university — a place of prayer and of learning  b) a palace of marble  c) a Roman fort  d) a harbour
**Answer: a) a mosque-university — a place of prayer and of learning** — That is what this lesson teaches.
