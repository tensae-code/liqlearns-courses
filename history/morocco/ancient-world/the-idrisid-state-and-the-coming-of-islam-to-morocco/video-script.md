# Video script — The Idrisid State and the Coming of Islam to Morocco

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
A refugee descendant of the Prophet founds a state, and his successors' city builds the enduring university-mosque — the Idrisid state, the spread of Islam in Morocco, Fez, and the Qarawiyyin.

A descendant of the Prophet Muhammad, fleeing danger in the east, reaches Morocco — and here the land receives him. Around **Idris** a state rises: the **Idrisid state**, the first of the great dynasties in this course's line, and with it Islam spreads through Morocco — the coming of a faith that will shape the country's whole story from here on.

The lesson follows the state's **founding and extent**: where Idrisid rule ran, and the city that became its heart — **Fez** in Idrisid times, growing from the new state's capital ground into one of the great cities of the Islamic world.

Then the book stops at one building, the way it will stop at one monument for every dynasty: the **Qarawiyyin Mosque-University** in Fez — a mosque that is also a university, a place of prayer that is also a place of learning, enduring through all the dynasties that follow.

That is the Idrisid gift to Morocco's line: a state, a faith spread across the land, a city — and a house of learning still standing when the dynasty itself is long gone.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
