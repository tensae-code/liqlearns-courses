# Hands-on — The Idrisid State and the Coming of Islam to Morocco

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine arriving in a new land with nothing but your name and your lineage — no army, no treasure — and being received as a leader. What would you build first: a wall, a market, or a place of prayer and learning? The founder of this lesson's state faced exactly that choice.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a refugee descendant of the Prophet founds a state, and his successors' city builds the enduring university-mosque — the Idrisid state, the spread of Islam in Morocco, Fez, and the Qarawiyyin. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
