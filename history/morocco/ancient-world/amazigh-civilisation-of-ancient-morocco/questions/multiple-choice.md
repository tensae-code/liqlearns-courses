# Amazigh Civilisation of Ancient Morocco — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Before any foreigner landed, the Amazigh…
a) governed themselves, built, dressed, ate and wrote in their own letters  b) had no leaders and no homes  c) lived only in caves  d) could not write at all
**Answer: a) governed themselves, built, dressed, ate and wrote in their own letters** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the book count among the heritage on the Amazigh table?
a) Morocco's Jewish popular dishes  b) only Roman food  c) food from across the ocean  d) nothing — food is not heritage
**Answer: a) Morocco's Jewish popular dishes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
