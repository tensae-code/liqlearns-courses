# Hands-on — Amazigh Civilisation of Ancient Morocco

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write your name. Now imagine your people's letters are not the letters used in the shops and schools around you, but letters of your own, older than the town itself. The Amazigh of ancient Morocco had exactly that. This lesson meets them properly: not as people waiting for history to arrive, but as a civilisation already complete.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: before any foreigner landed, the Amazigh governed themselves, built, dressed, ate and wrote in their own letters — a whole civilisation of their own. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
