# Roman Morocco and Amazigh Resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Rome do with Morocco, in this lesson?
a) exploited it — drawing off its produce and wealth for the empire  b) traded fairly and left at once  c) built it only schools  d) nothing — Rome never came
**Answer: a) exploited it — drawing off its produce and wealth for the empire** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who raised the model revolt against Rome?
a) Aedemon, an Amazigh freedman  b) Hanno of Carthage  c) a Roman emperor  d) the founder of Fez
**Answer: a) Aedemon, an Amazigh freedman** — That is what this lesson teaches.
