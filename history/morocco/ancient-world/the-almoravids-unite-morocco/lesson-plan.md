# Lesson plan — The Almoravids Unite Morocco

**Course:** History — Morocco · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Almoravids Unite Morocco
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Dynasties of Morocco (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** veiled Saharan reformers ride north, take Morocco, found Marrakech and push into Spain — the Almoravids unify the country and extend their state, and the Almoravid Dome stands as their monument.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Dynasties of Morocco — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **The Idrisid State and the Coming of Islam to Morocco** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: veiled Saharan reformers ride north, take Morocco, found Marrakech and push into Spain — the Almoravids unify the country and extend their state, and the Almoravid Dome stands as their monument.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the Morocco they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Morocco and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.

- Thinks the Almoravids only conquered → the book's own order is unifying the country first, then the extension of the state.
- Go back to the one thing: veiled Saharan reformers ride north, take Morocco, found Marrakech and push into Spain — the Almoravids unify the country and extend their state, and the Almoravid Dome stands as their monument. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
