# Hands-on — The Almoravids Unite Morocco

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture riders coming out of the Sahara, their faces veiled against the sand, carrying a strict idea of how people should live and pray. They are few, but they move as one. What happens when such a movement reaches a land of divided cities and rival rulers? Morocco is about to be made one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: veiled Saharan reformers ride north, take Morocco, found Marrakech and push into Spain — the Almoravids unify the country and extend their state, and the Almoravid Dome stands as their monument. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
