# The Almoravids Unite Morocco — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What are the two moves of the Almoravid story, in the book's order?
**Answer:** First unifying the country — making Morocco one state; then the extension of the state, founding Marrakech and pushing into Spain.

**2.** What is the Almoravids' monument study?
**Answer:** The Almoravid Dome — Qubbat al-Barudiyyin — in Marrakech.
