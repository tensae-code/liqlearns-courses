# The Almoravids Unite Morocco — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Almoravids unified Morocco and extended their state into Spain.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Almoravid Dome (Qubbat al-Barudiyyin) stands in Fez.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
