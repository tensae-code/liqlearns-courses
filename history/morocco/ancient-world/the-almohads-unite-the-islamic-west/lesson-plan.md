# Lesson plan — The Almohads Unite the Islamic West

**Course:** History — Morocco · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Almohads Unite the Islamic West
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Dynasties of Morocco (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a mountain preacher's movement swallows the Almoravids' empire and rules from Tripoli to Seville under one creed — the Almohads unite the whole Islamic West, and their architecture carries the mark of the empire.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Dynasties of Morocco — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: a mountain preacher's movement swallows the Almoravids' empire and rules from Tripoli to Seville under one creed — the Almohads unite the whole Islamic West, and their architecture carries the mark of the empire.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the Morocco they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Morocco and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
