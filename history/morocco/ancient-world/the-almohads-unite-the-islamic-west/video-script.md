# Video script — The Almohads Unite the Islamic West

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
A mountain preacher's movement swallows the Almoravids' empire and rules from Tripoli to Seville under one creed — the Almohads unite the whole Islamic West, and their architecture carries the mark of the empire.

In the mountains, a preacher's movement gathers around one creed: the **Almohads**. The lesson follows the state's **founding and its stages** — how a mountain movement becomes a power, step by step, until it does what no Moroccan power had done before.

For the Almohads do not merely replace the Almoravids in Morocco. They form an **empire across the Maghreb** — the whole Islamic West under one rule, from **Tripoli** in the east to **Seville** in Spain. Morocco, for this age, is not the edge of an empire. It is its centre.

An empire that size builds, and the book closes the lesson with the **characteristics of Almohad architecture** — the great gates, towers and mosques in the Almohad manner, the building style of a state that ruled the West and wanted its power seen in stone.

From Tripoli to Seville under one creed: that is the widest reach of any dynasty in this course's line, and the standard every dynasty after them is measured against.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
