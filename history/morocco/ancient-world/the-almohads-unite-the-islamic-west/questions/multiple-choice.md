# The Almohads Unite the Islamic West — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How far did the Almohad empire run?
a) from Tripoli to Seville — the whole Islamic West  b) only one city  c) only the Sahara  d) from Fez to Marrakech only
**Answer: a) from Tripoli to Seville — the whole Islamic West** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did the Almohad movement begin?
a) in the mountains, around a preacher's creed  b) in Rome  c) on the sea  d) in Spain
**Answer: a) in the mountains, around a preacher's creed** — That is what this lesson teaches.
