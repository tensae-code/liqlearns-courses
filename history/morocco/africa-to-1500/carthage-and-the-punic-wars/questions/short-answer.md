# Carthage and the Punic Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does 'mutual civilisational influence' mean here?
**Answer:** Influence running both ways: the newcomers and the Amazigh each brought their goods and ways, and both sides left the meeting changed.

**2.** How is the Phoenician trading centre in Morocco studied?
**Answer:** From a picture and a plan — where the ships landed, where goods were stored, how the place was laid out.
