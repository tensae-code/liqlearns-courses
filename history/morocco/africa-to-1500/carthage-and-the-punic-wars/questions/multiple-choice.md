# Carthage and the Punic Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was Lixus, in this lesson?
a) the first city beyond the Pillars of Hercules, founded by the Carthaginians  b) a Roman fortress  c) a Saharan oasis  d) a Phoenician ship
**Answer: a) the first city beyond the Pillars of Hercules, founded by the Carthaginians** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who sailed down Africa's west coast?
a) King Hanno the Carthaginian  b) a Roman general  c) an Amazigh king of Fez  d) nobody — the coast was never sailed
**Answer: a) King Hanno the Carthaginian** — That is what this lesson teaches.
