# Hands-on — Carthage and the Punic Wars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a ship from a rich city across the sea anchoring off your coast. The sailors want to trade, not to fight — at first. What would your people give them, and what would you take from them? And what would each side learn from the other? That meeting is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: sailors from Tyre and Carthage meet Amazigh Morocco, and both sides leave changed — a Phoenician trading centre on Moroccan ground, Hanno's voyage down Africa's west coast, and Lixus, the first city beyond the Pillars of Hercules. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
