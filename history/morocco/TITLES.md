# History — Morocco course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Morocco's
own — built from Morocco's Grade 5 *Al-Moufid in Social
Studies* teacher guide, in Arabic. Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below
sit inside Levels 1–4, in path order — what the learner
proves, step by step. The grade the book comes from lives
only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: the historian's craft
first (what history is, the witnesses of the past, dating
the past), then ancient Morocco (the first Moroccans, the
Amazigh civilisation, the Carthaginian meeting, Rome and
Aedemon), then the dynasty sequence the guide walks —
Idrisid → Almoravid → Almohad → Marinid — each through one
standing monument, and last the sharifian close: the
Saadians' economic golden age and the Alaouites unifying
and building the state. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — first steps: the historian's craft** (Al-Moufid
Social Studies Teacher Guide, Grade 5, History Lessons 1–2):

- `first-steps/` — Level 1, the opening title. What History
  Is (history as a craft practised by people with tools, not
  a list of kings) · From Evidence to a Written History (the
  witnesses of the past, شواهد الماضي, found and questioned
  before anything is written) · Dating the Past (the line
  where writing begins, separating prehistory from the
  historical eras; development, simultaneity, succession).

**Level 2 — ancient Morocco** (History Lessons 3–6):

- `ancient-world/` — Levels 2–3, Morocco's ancient age and
  its dynasties in one title folder, in path order.
  Prehistoric Morocco: The First Moroccans (remains, hunting,
  shelter, survival long before any state) · Amazigh
  Civilisation of Ancient Morocco (its own system of rule,
  housing, clothing, food — Morocco's Jewish popular dishes
  counted as heritage — and its own script) · Roman Morocco
  and Amazigh Resistance (the occupation map, Rome's
  exploitation, Aedemon's revolt).
- `africa-to-1500/` — Level 2, the sea meeting. Carthage
  and the Punic Wars (a Phoenician trading centre studied
  from a picture and a plan; mutual civilisational
  influence; Hanno's voyage down Africa's west coast; Lixus,
  the first city beyond the Pillars of Hercules).

**Level 3 — the dynasties of Morocco** (History Lessons
7–10), in `ancient-world/`, in path order:

- The Idrisid State and the Coming of Islam to Morocco
  (the state's founding and extent, Fez, the Qarawiyyin
  Mosque-University) · The Almoravids Unite Morocco
  (unifying the country, the extension of the state,
  Marrakech, the Almoravid Dome) · The Almohads Unite the
  Islamic West (the founding and stages, the empire across
  the Maghreb from Tripoli to Seville, Almohad architecture)
  · The Marinids and Morocco's Civilisational Creativity
  (the founding and stages, creativity in the madrasas of
  Fez, the Mawlid tradition still alive).

**Level 4 — the sharifian dynasties** (History Lessons
11–12):

- `modern-world/` — Level 4, the guide's close. The
  Saadians: Morocco's Economic Golden Age (sharifs from the
  south; economic prosperity; the sugar industry and the
  Chichaoua works; El Badi Palace) · The Alaouites Unify
  and Build the Moroccan State (from Tafilalt; Moulay
  Rashid unifying the country, followed on a map; Moulay
  Ismail building the state, the kasbahs as the model).
