# The Alaouites Unify and Build the Moroccan State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Which ruler does which job, in this lesson?
**Answer:** Moulay Rashid unites the country; Moulay Ismail builds the state.

**2.** How do the kasbahs hold the state together?
**Answer:** As fortress-towns planted across the land — walls holding the state together in stone, as its laws hold it together on paper.
