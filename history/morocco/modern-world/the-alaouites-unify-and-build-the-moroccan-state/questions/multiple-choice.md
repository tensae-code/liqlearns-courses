# The Alaouites Unify and Build the Moroccan State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the Alaouite state's two jobs, in the book's title?
a) unifying the country and building the state  b) trading and sailing  c) digging and farming  d) writing and printing
**Answer: a) unifying the country and building the state** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the model of military architecture under Moulay Ismail?
a) the kasbahs — fortress-towns across the land  b) the madrasas  c) the sugar works  d) the trading centre
**Answer: a) the kasbahs — fortress-towns across the land** — That is what this lesson teaches.
