# Hands-on — The Alaouites Unify and Build the Moroccan State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
End the course where it began: with a map. Morocco lies divided — region against region, no single rule holding it. Somebody has to travel that map, town by town, and make it one country again. Then, once it is one, somebody has to build the state that holds it. Two rulers, two jobs: that is the last lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: another sharifian family from Tafilalt stitches a broken Morocco back together and walls it with fortress-towns — Moulay Rashid unites the country, and Moulay Ismail builds the state, with the kasbahs as its model in stone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
