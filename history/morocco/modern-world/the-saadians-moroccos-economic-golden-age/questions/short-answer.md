# The Saadians: Morocco's Economic Golden Age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Where did the Saadian wealth come from, in this lesson?
**Answer:** From the age's economic prosperity — above all the sugar industry (the Chichaoua works), alongside the gold of the age.

**2.** What does El Badi Palace show?
**Answer:** How rich the Saadian age had become — the age's wealth spent on a palace of marble and onyx.
