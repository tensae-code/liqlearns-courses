# The Saadians: Morocco's Economic Golden Age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What stands at the centre of the Saadian lesson?
a) an ambitious economic project — the sugar industry, with the Chichaoua works  b) a sea voyage  c) a Roman war  d) a mountain revolt
**Answer: a) an ambitious economic project — the sugar industry, with the Chichaoua works** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the Saadians' monument study?
a) El Badi Palace  b) the Almoravid Dome  c) the Qarawiyyin  d) a Marinid madrasa
**Answer: a) El Badi Palace** — That is what this lesson teaches.
