# Lesson plan — The Saadians: Morocco's Economic Golden Age

**Course:** History — Morocco · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Saadians: Morocco's Economic Golden Age
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Sharifian Dynasties (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sharifs from the south reunite Morocco, grow rich on sugar and gold, and spend it on a palace of marble and onyx — the Saadian age is the economic one: the sugar works of Chichaoua, and El Badi Palace as its monument.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Sharifian Dynasties — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
