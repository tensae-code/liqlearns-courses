# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What divides prehistory from the historical eras on the timeline?
a) the beginning of writing  b) the building of the first palace  c) the first sea voyage  d) nothing — they are the same
**Answer: a) the beginning of writing** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Two things happening at the same time, in different places, is called…
a) simultaneity  b) succession  c) development  d) prehistory
**Answer: a) simultaneity** — That is what this lesson teaches.
