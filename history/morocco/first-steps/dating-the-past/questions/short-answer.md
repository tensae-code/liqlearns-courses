# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How do we know about the prehistoric eras, if nobody wrote in them?
**Answer:** From what those people left in the ground — bones, stones and tools.

**2.** What does development mean on the timeline?
**Answer:** Things change as they go along the line — a tool, a city or a state grows and transforms.
