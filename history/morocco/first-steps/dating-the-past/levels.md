# Dating the Past — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — a line drawn on the page separates the ages before writing from the ages with it — prehistory from history — and everything Moroccan hangs on that line in order, using development, simultaneity and succession — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
