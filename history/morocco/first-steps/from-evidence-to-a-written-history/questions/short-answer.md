# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two witnesses of the past a historian might find in Morocco.
**Answer:** Any two of: a standing wall, a coin in the soil, a letter, a song, a grave, a worn tool.

**2.** What two jobs does the historian do before writing?
**Answer:** Find the evidence, and question it — what it is, who made it, when, and whether it tells the truth.
