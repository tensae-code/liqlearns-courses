# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the witnesses of the past (شواهد الماضي)?
a) the evidence the past left behind — walls, coins, letters, songs, tools  b) people alive today  c) kings only  d) dreams about the past
**Answer: a) the evidence the past left behind — walls, coins, letters, songs, tools** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the historian's first job?
a) to find the evidence and question it  b) to write the book at once  c) to memorise dates  d) to invent what is missing
**Answer: a) to find the evidence and question it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
