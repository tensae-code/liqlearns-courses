# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is history, in this lesson?
a) a craft practised by people with tools  b) a list of kings to memorise  c) stories invented for fun  d) the study of the future
**Answer: a) a craft practised by people with tools** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does a historian use to do the work?
a) tools, and questions about what the past left behind  b) only a crown and a throne  c) a crystal ball  d) nothing — historians guess
**Answer: a) tools, and questions about what the past left behind** — That is what this lesson teaches.
