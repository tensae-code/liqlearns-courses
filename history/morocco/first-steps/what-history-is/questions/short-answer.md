# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is a historian?
**Answer:** A person who practises the craft of history — finding out what really happened in the past, using tools and questions, and telling it truly.

**2.** Why does this course teach the historian's craft before any Moroccan story?
**Answer:** So that every story that follows can be asked the historian's question — how do we know this happened?
