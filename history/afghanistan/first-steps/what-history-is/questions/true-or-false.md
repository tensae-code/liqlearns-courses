# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History is defined as past events fixed in time and place, which is what separates it from legend; it is called the mirror of the past and a lesson for the present.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "What History Is" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
