# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Dating the Past" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Lesson 4: Converting the dates of history

Afghanistan lives by three calendars, Hijri Solar and Hijri Lunar (both counted from the Prophet's migration) and the Gregorian (from the birth of Jesus), and the pupil learns to convert between them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Dating the Past as Afghanistan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
