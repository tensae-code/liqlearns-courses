# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Afghanistan lives by three calendars, Hijri Solar and Hijri Lunar (both counted from the Prophet's migration) and the Gregorian (from the birth of Jesus), and the pupil learns to convert between them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Dating the Past" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
