# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The historian works from two kinds of sources, written and manuscript documents and surviving monuments and remains, the written ones ranked most trustworthy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "From Evidence to a Written History" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
