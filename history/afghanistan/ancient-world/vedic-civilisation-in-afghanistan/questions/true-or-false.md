# Vedic civilisation in afghanistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first Aryan civilisation in the land, named from the Vedic books, begins around 1800 BCE among the settlements of old Balkh.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Vedic civilisation in afghanistan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
