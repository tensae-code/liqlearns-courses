# Afghanistan under the umayyads — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 2: Afghanistan in the Umayyad era"?
**Answer:** The Umayyads, a house of Quraysh, hold the caliphate from 41 AH/661 CE for ninety years and Islam spreads widely under them, Afghanistan included.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 3: Afghanistan in the Umayyad era"?
