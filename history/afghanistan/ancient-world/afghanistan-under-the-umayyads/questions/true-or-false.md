# Afghanistan under the umayyads — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Umayyads, a house of Quraysh, hold the caliphate from 41 AH/661 CE for ninety years and Islam spreads widely under them, Afghanistan included.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan under the umayyads" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
