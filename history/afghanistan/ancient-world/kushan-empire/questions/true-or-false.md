# Kushan empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Rabatak inscription recites the Kushan royal line — Kujula Kadphises, Vima Takto, Vima Kadphises, Kanishka — and names the empire's furthest cities. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Kushan empire" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
