# Kushan empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 13: Do you know the Kushan dynasty?" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** The Rabatak inscription recites the Kushan royal line — Kujula Kadphises, Vima Takto, Vima Kadphises, Kanishka — and names the empire's furthest cities.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 14: Begram, political centre of the Kushan empire" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** The Kushans rule their vast empire from two political centres, Peshawar and Begram, and Begram's hoard shows the trade of three worlds meeting.
