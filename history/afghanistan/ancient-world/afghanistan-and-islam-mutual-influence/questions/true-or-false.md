# Afghanistan and islam mutual influence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Islam gives the country a new identity and a single name of faith that unites its peoples, and Afghans in return render Islam great services.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan and islam mutual influence" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
