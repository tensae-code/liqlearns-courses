# Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The westward-moving Aryans build Persia; Cyrus's tomb at Pasargadae stands for an empire raised by the same stock that settled Ariana.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
