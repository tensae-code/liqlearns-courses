# Ghurids and khwarazmshahs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Ghurids, one of the land's oldest houses from Ghor, trace themselves to Malik Shansab of Ali's caliphate days; the Khwarazmshahs close the line of local independent Islamic states.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ghurids and khwarazmshahs" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
