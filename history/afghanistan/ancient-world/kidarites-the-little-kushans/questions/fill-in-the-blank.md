# Kidarites the little kushans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** This lesson is called "____".
**Answer:** Kidarites the little kushans

**2.** In "Lesson 18: The Kidarites, or Little Kushans", the story turns on: ____.
