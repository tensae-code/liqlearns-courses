# Kidarites the little kushans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Vasudeva's death the great empire's centre dissolves into local rulers, and the Kidarites hold Balkh as Kushans in miniature.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Kidarites the little kushans" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
