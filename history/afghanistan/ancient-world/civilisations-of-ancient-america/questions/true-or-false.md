# Civilisations of ancient america — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Against the old belief that America's civilisations were young, research shows human life there thousands of years deep, with its own political, social, economic and cultural orders.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Civilisations of ancient america" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
