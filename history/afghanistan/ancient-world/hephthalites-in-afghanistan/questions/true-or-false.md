# Hephthalites in afghanistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** As Kushan power fades and the Sasanians rise in the west, the Hephthalites become the strong state over ancient Afghanistan, leaving their coins and the Surya statue of Kabul's Khair Khana.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Hephthalites in afghanistan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
