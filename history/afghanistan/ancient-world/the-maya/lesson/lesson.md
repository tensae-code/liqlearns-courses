# The Maya

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The Maya" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Chapter 7 (History of America), Lesson 23: How did the Maya civilisation come to be?

The Maya advance in farming and open early trade with North America, until drought in Central America drives their tribes toward new lands.
