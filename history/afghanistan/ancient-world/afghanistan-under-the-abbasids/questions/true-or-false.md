# Afghanistan under the abbasids — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Abbasids reach the caliphate with the support of Afghanistan's own people, and the map of lands loosening from Baghdad begins at the eastern edge.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan under the abbasids" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
