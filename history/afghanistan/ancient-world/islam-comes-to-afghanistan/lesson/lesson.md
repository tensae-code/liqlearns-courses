# Islam comes to afghanistan

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Islam comes to afghanistan" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 1 (Afghanistan and Islam), Lesson 1: The entry of Islam into Afghanistan

A land with no strong central state, its provinces divided, proves ready for the new faith, and the book asks why its people accepted Islam.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 20: The state of Afghanistan at the advent of Islam

In the late 6th and early 7th centuries the land is run by local amirs and Kushan and Hephthalite remnants; Balkh's Nine-Dome mosque is counted its first.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lesson 1: The general state of Afghanistan at the advent of Islam

On Islam's eve the country lacks political, economic and social stability, ruled in pieces since the later 6th century, and the lesson asks what inclined its people toward Islam.

## Sub lesson 4 — Lesson 2: The entry and spread of Islam in Afghanistan

The new faith enters and spreads through the land province by province.
