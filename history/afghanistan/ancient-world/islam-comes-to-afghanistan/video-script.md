# Video script — Islam comes to afghanistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Islam comes to afghanistan.

A land with no strong central state, its provinces divided, proves ready for the new faith, and the book asks why its people accepted Islam.

In the late 6th and early 7th centuries the land is run by local amirs and Kushan and Hephthalite remnants; Balkh's Nine-Dome mosque is counted its first.

On Islam's eve the country lacks political, economic and social stability, ruled in pieces since the later 6th century, and the lesson asks what inclined its people toward Islam.

The new faith enters and spreads through the land province by province.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
