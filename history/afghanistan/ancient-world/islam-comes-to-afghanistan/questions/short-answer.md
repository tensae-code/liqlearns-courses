# Islam comes to afghanistan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Chapter 1 (Afghanistan and Islam), Lesson 1: The entry of Islam into Afghanistan" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** A land with no strong central state, its provinces divided, proves ready for the new faith, and the book asks why its people accepted Islam.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 20: The state of Afghanistan at the advent of Islam" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** In the late 6th and early 7th centuries the land is run by local amirs and Kushan and Hephthalite remnants; Balkh's Nine-Dome mosque is counted its first.
