# Abbasid caliphate rise and fall — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 34: Do you know how power passed from the Umayyads to the Abbasids?" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** Abu Muslim Khorasani, backed by the people of Khorasan, gathers an army of Taloqan, Balkh and Herat men at Merv and overthrows the Umayyads for the Abbasids.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 35: How did the Abbasid caliphate fall?" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Abu Muslim's murder turns the eastern amirs against Baghdad; revolts multiply, the caliphate fragments among local rulers in Herat, Sistan and Balkh, and the Abbasid order collapses.
