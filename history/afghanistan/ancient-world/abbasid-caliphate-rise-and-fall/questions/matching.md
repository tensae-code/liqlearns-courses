# Abbasid caliphate rise and fall — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lesson 34: Do you know how power passed from the Umayyads to the Abbasids? — a)
**2.** Lesson 35: How did the Abbasid caliphate fall? — b)

Meanings:
a) Abu Muslim Khorasani, backed by the people of Khorasan, gathers an army of Taloqan, Balkh and Herat men at Merv and overthrows the Umayyads for the Abbasids.
b) Abu Muslim's murder turns the eastern amirs against Baghdad; revolts multiply, the caliphate fragments among local rulers in Herat, Sistan and Balkh, and the Abbasid order collapses.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
