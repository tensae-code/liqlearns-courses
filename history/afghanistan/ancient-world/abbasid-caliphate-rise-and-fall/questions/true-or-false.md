# Abbasid caliphate rise and fall — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Abu Muslim Khorasani, backed by the people of Khorasan, gathers an army of Taloqan, Balkh and Herat men at Merv and overthrows the Umayyads for the Abbasids. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Abbasid caliphate rise and fall" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
