# Video script — Abbasid caliphate rise and fall

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Abbasid caliphate rise and fall.

Abu Muslim Khorasani, backed by the people of Khorasan, gathers an army of Taloqan, Balkh and Herat men at Merv and overthrows the Umayyads for the Abbasids.

Abu Muslim's murder turns the eastern amirs against Baghdad; revolts multiply, the caliphate fragments among local rulers in Herat, Sistan and Balkh, and the Abbasid order collapses.

Abu Muslim Khorasani's revolution seats the Abbasids at Baghdad.

The Abbasid state is examined whole — its politics, offices, culture and economy in the age that ends in fragmentation.

Abu Muslim's Khorasan revolution moves the caliphate from Damascus to Baghdad.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
