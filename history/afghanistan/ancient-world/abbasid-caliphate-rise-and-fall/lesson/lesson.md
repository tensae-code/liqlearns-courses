# Abbasid caliphate rise and fall

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Abbasid caliphate rise and fall" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 34: Do you know how power passed from the Umayyads to the Abbasids?

Abu Muslim Khorasani, backed by the people of Khorasan, gathers an army of Taloqan, Balkh and Herat men at Merv and overthrows the Umayyads for the Abbasids.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 35: How did the Abbasid caliphate fall?

Abu Muslim's murder turns the eastern amirs against Baghdad; revolts multiply, the caliphate fragments among local rulers in Herat, Sistan and Balkh, and the Abbasid order collapses.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lesson 45: The Abbasids

Abu Muslim Khorasani's revolution seats the Abbasids at Baghdad.

## Sub lesson 4 — Lesson 46: The political, administrative, cultural and economic state of the Abbasid period

The Abbasid state is examined whole — its politics, offices, culture and economy in the age that ends in fragmentation.

## Sub lesson 5 — Lesson 4: The transfer of power from the Umayyads to the Abbasids

Abu Muslim's Khorasan revolution moves the caliphate from Damascus to Baghdad.
