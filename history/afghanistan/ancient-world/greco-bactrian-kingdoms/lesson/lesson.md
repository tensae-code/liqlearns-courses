# Greco bactrian kingdoms

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greco bactrian kingdoms" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 11: The independent Greco-Bactrian states

Alexander's empire is divided among his generals, the Seleucids appoint Diodotus governor of Bactria, and Bactria breaks away as a Greek kingdom on Afghan soil.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 12: The artistic-cultural character of the Greco-Bactrian period

Greek artists, scholars and craftsmen who came with the armies fuse their arts with the East's, and the period's coins carry the blend.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 2 (Early Civilisations in Afghanistan), Lesson 10: The independent Greco-Bactrian states

Bactria breaks from the Seleucids and stands as a Greek kingdom on Afghan soil.

## Sub lesson 4 — Lesson 11: The character of Greco-Bactrian civilisation

Greek and Eastern arts fuse in the kingdom's cities, crafts and coins.

## Sub lesson 5 — Lesson 12: Ai Khanum, famous city of the Greco-Bactrian period

A fortified Greek city founded 2,300 years ago where the Kokcha meets the Amu in Takhar, excavated by French archaeologists from 1946.
