# Greco bactrian kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 11: The independent Greco-Bactrian states" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** Alexander's empire is divided among his generals, the Seleucids appoint Diodotus governor of Bactria, and Bactria breaks away as a Greek kingdom on Afghan soil.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 12: The artistic-cultural character of the Greco-Bactrian period" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Greek artists, scholars and craftsmen who came with the armies fuse their arts with the East's, and the period's coins carry the blend.
