# Aryans in ancient afghanistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Around 2500 BCE the Aryans enter the land that will bear their name (Ariana), calling themselves noble and of good conduct, their earlier homeland unknown.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Aryans in ancient afghanistan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
