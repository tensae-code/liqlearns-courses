# Aryans in ancient afghanistan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 6: Do you know the Aryans?"?
**Answer:** Around 2500 BCE the Aryans enter the land that will bear their name (Ariana), calling themselves noble and of good conduct, their earlier homeland unknown.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Chapter 1 (The Aryans), Lesson 1: The civilisation of Ariana"?
**Answer:** The Aryan settlement gives the land its first name and civilisation, the frame for everything the course will tell.
