# Afghanistans first islamic dynasties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Chapter 2 (Independent Islamic Governments of Afghanistan), Lesson 5: Tahirids, Saffarids and Samanids" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** Tahir ibn Husayn of Pushang by Herat plays kingmaker between the Abbasid brothers Amin and Ma'mun and founds the first of the land's own Islamic dynasties.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 7: Tahirids and Saffarids" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Tahir of Fushanj makes the first post-Islam bid for the country's independence with his semi-independent Tahirid state, and the Saffarids carry the work against the Abbasid caliphate further.
