# Afghanistans first islamic dynasties — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tahir ibn Husayn of Pushang by Herat plays kingmaker between the Abbasid brothers Amin and Ma'mun and founds the first of the land's own Islamic dynasties. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistans first islamic dynasties" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
