# Video script — Afghanistans first islamic dynasties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Afghanistans first islamic dynasties.

Tahir ibn Husayn of Pushang by Herat plays kingmaker between the Abbasid brothers Amin and Ma'mun and founds the first of the land's own Islamic dynasties.

Tahir of Fushanj makes the first post-Islam bid for the country's independence with his semi-independent Tahirid state, and the Saffarids carry the work against the Abbasid caliphate further.

The Samanids raise the land's first great Persianate Islamic state from Bukhara and Herat.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
