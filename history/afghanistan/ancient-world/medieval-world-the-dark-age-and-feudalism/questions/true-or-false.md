# Medieval World — the Dark Age and Feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Europe's middle age lasts ten centuries and is named the age of feudalism, the economic-political order that lay over most of the continent.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Medieval World — the Dark Age and Feudalism" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
