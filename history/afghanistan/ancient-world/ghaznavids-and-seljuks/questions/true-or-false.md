# Ghaznavids and seljuks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Alptigin, a Turkish commander of the Samanids, plants the Ghaznavid seed and his son-in-law Sebuktigin makes it a kingdom at Ghazni; the Seljuks follow as the second great Afghan-based power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ghaznavids and seljuks" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
