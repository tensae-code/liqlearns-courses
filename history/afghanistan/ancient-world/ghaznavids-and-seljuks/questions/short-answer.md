# Ghaznavids and seljuks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 6: Ghaznavids and Seljuks"?
**Answer:** Alptigin, a Turkish commander of the Samanids, plants the Ghaznavid seed and his son-in-law Sebuktigin makes it a kingdom at Ghazni; the Seljuks follow as the second great Afghan-based power.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 9: Ghaznavids and Seljuks"?
**Answer:** Ghazni's Turkish sultans and then the Seljuks hold the land in two successive great powers.
