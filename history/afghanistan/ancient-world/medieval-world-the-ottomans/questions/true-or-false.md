# Medieval World — the Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** While Europe builds its new civilisation to master the world, the lesson turns to the greatest Muslim state of the modern ages and asks how it met colonialism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Medieval World — the Ottomans" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
