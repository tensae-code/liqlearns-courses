# Ancient civilisations india — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Historians begin India's civilisation at Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, where around 3000 BCE a people of the north-west founded cities, grew wheat and worked metals.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ancient civilisations india" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
