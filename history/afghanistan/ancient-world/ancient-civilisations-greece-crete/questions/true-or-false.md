# Ancient civilisations greece crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Thucydides argues the ruins of older towns are the work of peoples before his own; Greece's civilisation grows out of those half-remembered predecessors.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ancient civilisations greece crete" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
