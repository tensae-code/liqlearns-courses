# Afghanistan five thousand years origins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The claim of 5,000 years is tested against the country's prehistoric remains and earliest settlements, the evidence on which the national timeline stands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan five thousand years origins" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
