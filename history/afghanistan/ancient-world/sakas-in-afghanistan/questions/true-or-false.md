# Sakas in afghanistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Heliocles, last Greco-Bactrian emperor, local rulers fragment the land until about 100 BCE, when Saka rule rises over the south — Arachosia (Kandahar) and Sakastan (Sistan).
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Sakas in afghanistan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
