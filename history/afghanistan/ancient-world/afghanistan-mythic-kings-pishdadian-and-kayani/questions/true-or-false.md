# Afghanistan mythic kings pishdadian and kayani — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three legendary houses fill the age before records — Hushang of the Pishdadians wars with the demons, and Rostam the champion strides out of the Shahnameh tradition. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan mythic kings pishdadian and kayani" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
