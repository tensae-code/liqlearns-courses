# Video script — Afghanistan mythic kings pishdadian and kayani

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Afghanistan mythic kings pishdadian and kayani.

Three legendary houses fill the age before records — Hushang of the Pishdadians wars with the demons, and Rostam the champion strides out of the Shahnameh tradition.

The legendary first house, Hushang's line, opens the age before records.

The Kayani kings succeed the Pishdadians in the epic memory of the land.

The mythic sequence is completed with the Aspa line of heroes.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
