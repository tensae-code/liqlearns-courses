# Muhammad childhood youth and first revelation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 37: The childhood of Muhammad" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** The orphaned boy is raised in Mecca and the desert, growing up outside the idolatry around him.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 38: The youth of Muhammad" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** The young Muhammad trades honestly for Khadija and earns the name al-Amin, the trustworthy.
