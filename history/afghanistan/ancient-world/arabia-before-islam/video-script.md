# Video script — Arabia before islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Arabia before islam.

Desert tribes obey their sheikhs and live by camels and trade while Mecca grows rich on the Yemen–Syria road, and women and daughters are held as property.

Abraham raises the Kaaba and the sanctuary at Mecca becomes the Arabs' pilgrimage heart long before Islam.

Every tribe keeps its own idol, superstition and polytheism hold the peninsula, and the strong devour the weak.

A world of superstition and tribal idols awaits the new faith.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
