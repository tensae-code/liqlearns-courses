# Arabia before islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Desert tribes obey their sheikhs and live by camels and trade while Mecca grows rich on the Yemen–Syria road, and women and daughters are held as property. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Arabia before islam" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
