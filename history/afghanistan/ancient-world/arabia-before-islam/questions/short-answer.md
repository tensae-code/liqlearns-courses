# Arabia before islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Chapter 5 (History of Islam), Lesson 35: The economic and social state of Arabia before Islam" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** Desert tribes obey their sheikhs and live by camels and trade while Mecca grows rich on the Yemen–Syria road, and women and daughters are held as property.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 36: The Kaaba" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Abraham raises the Kaaba and the sanctuary at Mecca becomes the Arabs' pilgrimage heart long before Islam.
