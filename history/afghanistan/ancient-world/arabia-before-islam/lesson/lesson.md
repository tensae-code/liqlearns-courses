# Arabia before islam

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Arabia before islam" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 5 (History of Islam), Lesson 35: The economic and social state of Arabia before Islam

Desert tribes obey their sheikhs and live by camels and trade while Mecca grows rich on the Yemen–Syria road, and women and daughters are held as property.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 36: The Kaaba

Abraham raises the Kaaba and the sanctuary at Mecca becomes the Arabs' pilgrimage heart long before Islam.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 4 (History of Islam), Lesson 25: Do you know how Arabia was before Islam?

Every tribe keeps its own idol, superstition and polytheism hold the peninsula, and the strong devour the weak.

## Sub lesson 4 — Chapter 4 (History of Islam), Lesson 32: The state of the world and the Arabian Peninsula at the advent of Islam

A world of superstition and tribal idols awaits the new faith.
