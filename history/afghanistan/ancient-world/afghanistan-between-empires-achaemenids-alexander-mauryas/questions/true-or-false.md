# Afghanistan between empires achaemenids alexander mauryas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** With no central state after the 6th century BCE, the land is dealt among Darius and Xerxes' Persia, Alexander of Macedon and Chandragupta's Mauryas. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan between empires achaemenids alexander mauryas" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
