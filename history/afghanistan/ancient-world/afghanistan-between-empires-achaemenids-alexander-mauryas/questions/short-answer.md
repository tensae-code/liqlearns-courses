# Afghanistan between empires achaemenids alexander mauryas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 10: Ancient Afghanistan and its relations with foreigners (Achaemenids, Greeks and Mauryans)" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** With no central state after the 6th century BCE, the land is dealt among Darius and Xerxes' Persia, Alexander of Macedon and Chandragupta's Mauryas.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 7: Ancient Afghanistan and the Achaemenids" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** The land enters the Persian empire of Darius and Xerxes as its eastern satrapies.
