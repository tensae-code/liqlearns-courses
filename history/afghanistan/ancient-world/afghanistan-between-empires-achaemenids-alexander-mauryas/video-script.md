# Video script — Afghanistan between empires achaemenids alexander mauryas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Afghanistan between empires achaemenids alexander mauryas.

With no central state after the 6th century BCE, the land is dealt among Darius and Xerxes' Persia, Alexander of Macedon and Chandragupta's Mauryas.

The land enters the Persian empire of Darius and Xerxes as its eastern satrapies.

Alexander's conquest and the Greek presence remake the land's cities and coinage.

Chandragupta's Mauryan empire takes the eastern provinces, the third great foreign power of the age.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
