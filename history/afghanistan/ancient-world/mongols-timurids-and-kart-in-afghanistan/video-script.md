# Video script — Mongols timurids and kart in afghanistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Mongols timurids and kart in afghanistan.

The Mongols drown the country in blood and soil, the Kartids still manage a state in Herat under their shadow, and the Timurids rule Afghanistan in their turn.

The Mongol deluge, and the Kartids of Herat keeping a state alive inside it.

Timur rebuilds an empire over the ruins and his successors hold it from Herat and Samarkand.

The same centuries are read a second time for their economies and their brilliant Timurid culture.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
