# Mongols timurids and kart in afghanistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Mongols drown the country in blood and soil, the Kartids still manage a state in Herat under their shadow, and the Timurids rule Afghanistan in their turn. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Mongols timurids and kart in afghanistan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
