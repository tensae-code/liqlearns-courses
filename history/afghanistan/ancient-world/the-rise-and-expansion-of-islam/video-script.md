# Video script — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The Rise and Expansion of Islam.

The Prophet invites Mecca openly and meets mockery, pressure and persecution from the idol-worshippers.

Persecuted believers cross the sea to the just Christian king of Abyssinia, who refuses to hand them back.

Yathrib's tribes pledge at Aqaba and the Muslims leave Mecca to build a new community in Medina.

In Medina the Prophet orders a new society — brotherhood, mosque and covenant — and the Medinans rejoice at his coming.

Mecca opens its gates almost without bloodshed, and soon after, the Prophet dies with Arabia united.

Idolatry gives way to one God and a single community; the peninsula's tribes become one people under Islam.

Muhammad, son of Abdullah and grandson of Abd al-Muttalib, is born in 571 CE, fatherless before birth, nursed by Halima, and grows into the leader of mankind.

As the call spreads through Mecca and Medina, the polytheists strain to crush it, and the first ghazawat and conquests carry the new faith outward.

Born in 571, orphaned, raised to the cave of Hira and the first revelation.

Yathrib's tribes pledge themselves to the Prophet at Aqaba, opening the road to Medina.

The Prophet's battles defend the young community against Mecca's hostility.

Halted at Hudaybiyya, the Prophet takes the pledge under the tree and turns a blocked pilgrimage into a truce that opens Arabia.

Mecca is taken almost without bloodshed and the Kaaba cleansed.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
