# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 40: The call of Muhammad" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** The Prophet invites Mecca openly and meets mockery, pressure and persecution from the idol-worshippers.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 41: The Muslims' migration to Abyssinia" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Persecuted believers cross the sea to the just Christian king of Abyssinia, who refuses to hand them back.
