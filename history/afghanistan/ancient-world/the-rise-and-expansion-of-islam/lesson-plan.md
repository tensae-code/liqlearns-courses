# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Afghanistan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First tellings: schooling, the ancient cities, recent memory and the first Islamic story (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The Rise and Expansion of Islam as Afghanistan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First tellings: schooling, the ancient cities, recent memory and the first Islamic story — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own place and people.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's story in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the scene at the centre of "The Rise and Expansion of Islam" — the place and the people of the story, as an animated map would show them — the student names who is in it, where it is, and what is turning.
