# The Rashidun Caliphs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Saqifa the companions choose Abu Bakr; he holds the community together through the zakat refusals and gathers the Qur'an's scattered pages. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Rashidun Caliphs" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
