# The Rashidun Caliphs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Chapter 6, Lesson 46: The caliphate of Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (11–13 AH)" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** At Saqifa the companions choose Abu Bakr; he holds the community together through the zakat refusals and gathers the Qur'an's scattered pages.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 47: The caliphate of Umar (13–24 AH)" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Umar, the second caliph, builds the administration and justice of a fast-growing Islamic state.
