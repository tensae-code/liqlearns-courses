# Umayyad caliphate rise and fall — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 32: Do you know the beginning of the Umayyad caliphate?" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** After Ali's martyrdom Muawiya, son of Abu Sufyan, takes the caliphate in 41 AH and makes it a dynasty.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 33: Do you know how the Umayyad caliphate fell?" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Though Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz rules justly, too many Umayyads neglect their subjects, and uprisings across the far provinces bring the dynasty down.
