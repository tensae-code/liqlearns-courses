# Umayyad caliphate rise and fall — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Ali's martyrdom Muawiya, son of Abu Sufyan, takes the caliphate in 41 AH and makes it a dynasty. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Umayyad caliphate rise and fall" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
