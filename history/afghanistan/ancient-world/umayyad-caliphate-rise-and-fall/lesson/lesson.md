# Umayyad caliphate rise and fall

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Umayyad caliphate rise and fall" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 32: Do you know the beginning of the Umayyad caliphate?

After Ali's martyrdom Muawiya, son of Abu Sufyan, takes the caliphate in 41 AH and makes it a dynasty.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 33: Do you know how the Umayyad caliphate fell?

Though Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz rules justly, too many Umayyads neglect their subjects, and uprisings across the far provinces bring the dynasty down.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lesson 43: The Umayyads

Muawiya founds the dynastic caliphate at Damascus in 41 AH.

## Sub lesson 4 — Lesson 44: The economic, administrative, cultural and military state in Umayyad times

The Umayyad order is examined as a working state — coinage, administration, armies and culture.
