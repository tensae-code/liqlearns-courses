# Video script — Umayyad caliphate rise and fall

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Umayyad caliphate rise and fall.

After Ali's martyrdom Muawiya, son of Abu Sufyan, takes the caliphate in 41 AH and makes it a dynasty.

Though Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz rules justly, too many Umayyads neglect their subjects, and uprisings across the far provinces bring the dynasty down.

Muawiya founds the dynastic caliphate at Damascus in 41 AH.

The Umayyad order is examined as a working state — coinage, administration, armies and culture.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
