# Avestan civilisation and zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The second civilisation takes its name from the Avesta, Zoroaster's holy book of law and foundation, and runs from about 1200 to 700 BCE preaching good thoughts, good words, good deeds.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Avestan civilisation and zoroaster" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
