# Kalakani interlude and nadir shah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 25: How did Habibullah Kalakani come to power?" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** Revolt against Amanullah's reforms lifts the Tajik bandit-king Kalakani to Kabul's throne for nine months.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 26: Do you know how Muhammad Nadir Shah reached the throne?" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Nadir Khan, the independence war's general, marches from the south, ends the Kalakani interlude and founds a new royal line.
