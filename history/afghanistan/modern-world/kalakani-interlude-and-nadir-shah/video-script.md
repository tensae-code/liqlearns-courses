# Video script — Kalakani interlude and nadir shah

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Kalakani interlude and nadir shah.

Revolt against Amanullah's reforms lifts the Tajik bandit-king Kalakani to Kabul's throne for nine months.

Nadir Khan, the independence war's general, marches from the south, ends the Kalakani interlude and founds a new royal line.

Revolts gather against the pace of change and the reforming king's throne shakes.

Nadir Khan ends the Kalakani crisis and consolidates a new royal order.

The new order writes its fundamental principles into a constitution.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
