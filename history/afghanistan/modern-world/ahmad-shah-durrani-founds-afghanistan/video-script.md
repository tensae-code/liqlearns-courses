# Video script — Ahmad shah durrani founds afghanistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Ahmad shah durrani founds afghanistan.

Ahmad Shah gathers the divided country into one state again, and pupils debate the role of Sabir Shah Kabuli beside the founder's own.

The founder orders the new state's administration and leads its armies across the region.

The founder's character is summed up as the model of the national hero.

The founder gathers the tribes into one central state in 1747.

The founder's armies carry the new state to its greatest extent, India included.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
