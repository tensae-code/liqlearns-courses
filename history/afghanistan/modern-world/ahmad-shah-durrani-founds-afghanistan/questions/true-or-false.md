# Ahmad shah durrani founds afghanistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ahmad Shah gathers the divided country into one state again, and pupils debate the role of Sabir Shah Kabuli beside the founder's own. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ahmad shah durrani founds afghanistan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
