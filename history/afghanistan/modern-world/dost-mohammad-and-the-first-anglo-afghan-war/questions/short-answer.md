# Dost mohammad and the first anglo afghan war — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 12: Do you know Dost Mohammad Khan's first emirate?" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** The Barakzai chief Dost Mohammad founds a new emirate at Kabul as the Durrani empire dissolves.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 13: Do you know the first British invasion of Afghanistan?" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** In February 1839 the British under Sir John Keane, with Macnaghten and Shah Shuja, march through Balochistan and the Bolan Pass on Kandahar; the Kandahar brothers, refused by Russia, flee to Iran and Shuja enters the city.
