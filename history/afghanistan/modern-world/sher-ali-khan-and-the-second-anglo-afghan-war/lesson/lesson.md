# Sher ali khan and the second anglo afghan war

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Sher ali khan and the second anglo afghan war" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 16: Do you know Amir Sher Ali Khan's first reign?

Dost Mohammad's son succeeds amid his brothers' claims and begins a reforming reign.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 17: Do you know the person of Amir Muhammad Afzal Khan?

Afzal Khan, the elder brother, holds Kabul briefly in the succession struggle.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lesson 18: Do you know the person of Amir Muhammad Azam Khan?

Azam Khan follows his brother Afzal on Kabul's throne as the civil war of the princes runs on.

## Sub lesson 4 — Lesson 19: Do you know Amir Sher Ali Khan's second reign?

Sher Ali returns and resumes reform until British demands drive the country toward a second invasion.

## Sub lesson 5 — Lesson 20: Do you know the second British invasion of Afghanistan?

The Treaty of Gandamak is signed amid anarchy by an Amir unrecognised in his own capital; it cuts Khyber, Kurram, Pishin and Sibi away to British India and brings a British envoy, Sir Louis Cavagnari, to Kabul.

## Sub lesson 6 — Lessons 10 (unnumbered reign sections): The first reign of Amir Sher Ali Khan (1863–1866), the reign of Amir Muhammad Afzal Khan (1866–1867) and the reign of Amir Muhammad Azam Khan (1867–1868)

Three brothers hold Kabul in turn through the succession war.

## Sub lesson 7 — Lesson 11: Reforms and reforming (1868–1879)

Sher Ali's restored reign reforms army, administration and schools until the British ultimatum.

## Sub lesson 8 — Lesson 12: The reign of Amir Muhammad Yaqub Khan (March–October 1879)

Yaqub signs Gandamak, loses the border districts and his throne within a single year.
