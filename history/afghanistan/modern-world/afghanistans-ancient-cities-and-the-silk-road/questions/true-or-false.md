# Afghanistans ancient cities and the silk road — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Balkh, "Mother of Cities", Herat, Bamiyan, Kabul, Kapisa, Bust, Ghor and Ghazni are named as the homeland's ancient cities, and the Silk Road through them once tied China and India to Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistans ancient cities and the silk road" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
