# History of education in afghanistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Children once learned only in mosques and homes; Amir Sher Ali Khan founds the first formal schools (one military, one civil), Habibullah opens Habibiya and Harbiya in Kabul, Amanullah adds modern schools and the Masturat girls' school, Kalakani closes them and Nadir Khan reopens them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "History of education in afghanistan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
