# History of education in afghanistan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** This lesson is called "____".
**Answer:** History of education in afghanistan

**2.** In "Lesson 9: History of education in Afghanistan", the story turns on: ____.
**Answer:** Children once learned only in mosques and homes; Amir Sher Ali Khan founds the first formal schools (one military, one civil), Habibullah opens Habibiya and Harbiya in Kabul, Amanullah adds modern schools and the Masturat girls' school, Kalakani closes them and Nadir Khan reopens them.
