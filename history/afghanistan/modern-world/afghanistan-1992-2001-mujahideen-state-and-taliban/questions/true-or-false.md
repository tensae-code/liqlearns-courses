# Afghanistan 1992 2001 mujahideen state and taliban — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Mojaddedi's interim term Rabbani heads the Islamic State, factional war ruins Kabul, and the decade runs on to the Taliban period.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan 1992 2001 mujahideen state and taliban" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
