# Hotak empire in iran and its fall — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Shah Mahmud Hotak is crowned in Iran itself as the Abdalis raise their own state in Herat, and the Afghan Hotak state in Iran ends when Nader Shah rises. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Hotak empire in iran and its fall" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
