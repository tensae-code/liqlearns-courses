# Safavid iran rival of uzbeks and mughals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From Sheikh Safiuddin of Ardabil's Sufi order, Shah Ismail founds Safavid Iran, the strong rival of both the Uzbeks and the Baburids with Afghanistan between them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Safavid iran rival of uzbeks and mughals" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
