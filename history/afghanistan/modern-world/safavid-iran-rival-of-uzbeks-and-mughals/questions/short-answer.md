# Safavid iran rival of uzbeks and mughals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 11: The Safavids of Iran"?
**Answer:** From Sheikh Safiuddin of Ardabil's Sufi order, Shah Ismail founds Safavid Iran, the strong rival of both the Uzbeks and the Baburids with Afghanistan between them.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 16: The Safavids and Afghanistan"?
