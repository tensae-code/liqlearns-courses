# Afghanistan three decades of war what they cost — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The ledger is counted in martyred teachers and scholars, destroyed schools and a fallen standard of education.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan three decades of war what they cost" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
