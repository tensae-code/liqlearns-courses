# Video script — Abdur rahman the iron amir

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Abdur rahman the iron amir.

The Iron Amir binds every official to his personal command, crushes every collective and individual resistance without mercy, and builds a regular army of more than ninety thousand.

Abdur Rahman binds the whole country to Kabul's command and builds the regular army.

The Amir's treaties with Britain and Russia fix the borders the country still lives with.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
