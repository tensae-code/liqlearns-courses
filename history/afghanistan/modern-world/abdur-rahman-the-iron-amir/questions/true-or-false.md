# Abdur rahman the iron amir — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Iron Amir binds every official to his personal command, crushes every collective and individual resistance without mercy, and builds a regular army of more than ninety thousand. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Abdur rahman the iron amir" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
