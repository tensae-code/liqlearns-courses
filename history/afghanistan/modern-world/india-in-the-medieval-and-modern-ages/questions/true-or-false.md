# India in the medieval and modern ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The one land of India (today's India, Pakistan and Bangladesh) receives Islam largely through Afghan effort, and Afghans rule it for long periods. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "India in the medieval and modern ages" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
