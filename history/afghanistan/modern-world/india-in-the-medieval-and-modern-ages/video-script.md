# Video script — India in the medieval and modern ages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: India in the medieval and modern ages.

The one land of India (today's India, Pakistan and Bangladesh) receives Islam largely through Afghan effort, and Afghans rule it for long periods.

Medieval India is surveyed with Afghans inside its story.

Modern India passes under new masters as Europe arrives.

Mughal India's science, culture and art — the world the Taj Mahal crowns.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
