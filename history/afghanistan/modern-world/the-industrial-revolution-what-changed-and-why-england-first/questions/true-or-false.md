# The Industrial Revolution — What Changed and Why England First — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The steam engine's England leads the industrial revolution, the change that puts machines to work in the advanced West.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Industrial Revolution — What Changed and Why England First" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
