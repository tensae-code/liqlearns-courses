# The crusades — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The cross, the sign of Jesus's martyrdom for Christians, gives its name to the wars Europe launches eastward after Christianity's spread.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The crusades" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
