# The crusades — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 19: The Crusades"?
**Answer:** The cross, the sign of Jesus's martyrdom for Christians, gives its name to the wars Europe launches eastward after Christianity's spread.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 31: The Crusades"?
**Answer:** Cross-marked Europe wars eastward for two centuries.
