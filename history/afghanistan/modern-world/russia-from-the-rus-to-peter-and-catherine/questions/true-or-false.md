# Russia from the rus to peter and catherine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Norsemen from Sweden fall on the primitive Slav tribes in the 9th century, and the long road ends with Peter the Great and Catherine the Great.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Russia from the rus to peter and catherine" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
