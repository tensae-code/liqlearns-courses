# Afghanistan bonn settlement and reconstruction 2001 2004 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After 2001 the Afghan factions meet at Bonn and agree a road back to an elected government under international protection. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan bonn settlement and reconstruction 2001 2004" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
