# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The voyager who reaches South America's north sees it is no India but a new continent, later named America after him, and the great states turn toward it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
