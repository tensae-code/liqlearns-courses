# Iran in the medieval and modern ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Much of Iran's history is shared with Afghanistan's, the same kings and dynasties ruling both, until modern Iran draws Europe's attention. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Iran in the medieval and modern ages" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
