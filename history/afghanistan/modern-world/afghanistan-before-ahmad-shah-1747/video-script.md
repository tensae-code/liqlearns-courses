# Video script — Afghanistan before ahmad shah 1747

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Afghanistan before ahmad shah 1747.

The map shows a land parcelled out — the Hotak state at Kandahar (1709–1737), the Uzbek Junaydi state in the north, Mughals in the east, Safavids in the west.

Under the invading Shaybanids, Safavids and Baburids, trade and agriculture stagnate and Herat's art school can no longer shine.

The economy of a partitioned land is weighed as the third factor preparing the country's reunification.

The partitioned land of Hotaks, Uzbeks, Mughals and Safavids is analysed in three factors before its refounder appears.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
