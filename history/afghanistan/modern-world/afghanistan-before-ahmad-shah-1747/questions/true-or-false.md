# Afghanistan before ahmad shah 1747 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The map shows a land parcelled out — the Hotak state at Kandahar (1709–1737), the Uzbek Junaydi state in the north, Mughals in the east, Safavids in the west. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan before ahmad shah 1747" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
