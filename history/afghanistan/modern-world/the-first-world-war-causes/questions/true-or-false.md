# The First World War — Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The West opens the century ruling India, South-East Asia and most of Africa by military and economic power, and its rivalries ignite the Great War.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The First World War — Causes" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
