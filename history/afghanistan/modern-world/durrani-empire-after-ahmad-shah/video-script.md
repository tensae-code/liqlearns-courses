# Video script — Durrani empire after ahmad shah

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Durrani empire after ahmad shah.

The founder's son holds the empire for two decades and moves the seat of rule toward Kabul.

Shah Zaman plans to hold the empire together as its provinces begin to pull away.

The Durrani princes' rivalries open the throne to Mahmud's first rise.

Shuja's first reign plays out amid the princes' contest for the Durrani throne.

Mahmud returns to a throne the empire can no longer hold together.

The empire passes to Timur Shah for twenty years, then to Zaman and the first Mahmud as the princes' rivalries begin.

Shuja and Mahmud trade the throne while the empire breaks.

Durrani and Barakzai contenders multiply and the state's centre cannot hold.

Power shifts among the Barakzai brothers as Persia and Britain begin their interventions.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
