# Durrani empire after ahmad shah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 7: Did you know Timur Shah reigned twenty years?" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** The founder's son holds the empire for two decades and moves the seat of rule toward Kabul.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 8: Do you know Shah Zaman's reign programmes?" (Sub lesson 2)?
**Answer:** Shah Zaman plans to hold the empire together as its provinces begin to pull away.
