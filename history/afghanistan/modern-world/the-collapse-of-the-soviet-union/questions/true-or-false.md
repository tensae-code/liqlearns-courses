# The collapse of the soviet union — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The northern superpower that invaded Afghanistan dissolves itself in 1991.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The collapse of the soviet union" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
