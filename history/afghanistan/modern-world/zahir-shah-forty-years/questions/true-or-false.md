# Zahir shah forty years — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The last king's long reign runs from 1933 to 1973, through his uncles' premierships, Shah Mahmud's "father of democracy" government and Afghanistan's entry into the United Nations. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Zahir shah forty years" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
