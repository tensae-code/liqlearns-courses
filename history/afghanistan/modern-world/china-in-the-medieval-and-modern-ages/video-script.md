# Video script — China in the medieval and modern ages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: China in the medieval and modern ages.

China keeps a brilliant civilisation through the middle ages under a succession of dynasties, its Great Wall standing over it, until Europe presses in during the modern age.

Medieval China's dynasties keep a brilliant civilisation behind the Great Wall.

Modern China faces Europe's pressure.

China's order is read through its economy, culture and art.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
