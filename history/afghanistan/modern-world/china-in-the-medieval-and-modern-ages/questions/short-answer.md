# China in the medieval and modern ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 17: China in the medieval and modern ages" (Sub lesson 1)?
**Answer:** China keeps a brilliant civilisation through the middle ages under a succession of dynasties, its Great Wall standing over it, until Europe presses in during the modern age.
