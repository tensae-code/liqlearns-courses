# China in the medieval and modern ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** China keeps a brilliant civilisation through the middle ages under a succession of dynasties, its Great Wall standing over it, until Europe presses in during the modern age. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "China in the medieval and modern ages" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
