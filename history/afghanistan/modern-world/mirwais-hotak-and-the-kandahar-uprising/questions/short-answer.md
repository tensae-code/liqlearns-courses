# Mirwais hotak and the kandahar uprising — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Chapter 4 (The Kandahar Rising), Lesson 12: The Kandahar uprising"?
**Answer:** On the rising's eve the homeland is divided among Mughal India, Safavid Iran and the Uzbeks; the people of Kandahar under Haji Mirwais Khan Hotak free their land from Safavid rule.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 17: The Kandahar uprising led by Haji Mirwais Nikah"?
**Answer:** Mirwais Hotak frees Kandahar from Safavid rule in 1709.
