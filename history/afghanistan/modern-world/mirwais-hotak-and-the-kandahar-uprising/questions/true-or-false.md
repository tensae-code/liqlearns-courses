# Mirwais hotak and the kandahar uprising — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On the rising's eve the homeland is divided among Mughal India, Safavid Iran and the Uzbeks; the people of Kandahar under Haji Mirwais Khan Hotak free their land from Safavid rule.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Mirwais hotak and the kandahar uprising" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
