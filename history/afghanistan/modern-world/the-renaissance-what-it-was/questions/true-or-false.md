# The Renaissance — What It Was — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** "rebirth" — from the 14th to the 16th century the culture and science of ancient Greece and Rome are revived and Europe's face slowly changes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The Renaissance — What It Was" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
