# Afghanistan 1973 from monarchy to republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 27: The factors in the change of regime from monarchy to republic"?
**Answer:** Drought, corruption, party strife and a sidelined king prepare the monarchy's fall.

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 28: The coup of 26 Saratan (1352 SH / 1973)"?
**Answer:** Daoud Khan's bloodless coup ends two and a half centuries of Durrani monarchy and proclaims the republic.
