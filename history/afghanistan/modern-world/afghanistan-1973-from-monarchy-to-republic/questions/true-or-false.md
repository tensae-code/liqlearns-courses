# Afghanistan 1973 from monarchy to republic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Drought, corruption, party strife and a sidelined king prepare the monarchy's fall.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan 1973 from monarchy to republic" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
