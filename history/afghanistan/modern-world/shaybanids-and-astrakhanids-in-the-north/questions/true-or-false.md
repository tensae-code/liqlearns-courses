# Shaybanids and astrakhanids in the north — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Muhammad Shaybani, claiming descent from Jochi son of Genghis, founds an Uzbek power that makes northern Afghanistan its target.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Shaybanids and astrakhanids in the north" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
