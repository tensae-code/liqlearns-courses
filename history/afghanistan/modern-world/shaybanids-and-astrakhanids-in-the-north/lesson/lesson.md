## Chapter 3 (Afghanistan and the Neighbours before the Kandahar Rising), Lesson 9: The Shaybanids and Astrakhanids of Transoxiana

Muhammad Shaybani, claiming descent from Jochi son of Genghis, founds an Uzbek power that makes northern Afghanistan its target.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Shaybanids and astrakhanids in the north as Afghanistan's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
