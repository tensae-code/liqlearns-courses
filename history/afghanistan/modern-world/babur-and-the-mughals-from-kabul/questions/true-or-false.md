# Babur and the mughals from kabul — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zahiruddin Muhammad Babur, of Timur's line, builds from Kabul the dynasty that will rule India and leave the Taj Mahal.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Babur and the mughals from kabul" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
