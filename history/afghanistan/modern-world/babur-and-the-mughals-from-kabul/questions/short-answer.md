# Babur and the mughals from kabul — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 10: The Baburids of India"?

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Lesson 15: The Baburids and Afghanistan"?
