# Lodis and suris afghan sultans of delhi — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bahlul Lodi, son of Punjab's governor Malik Kala Khan, takes Delhi's sultanate (his line given as 858–868 AH) as the Khizr Khanid state decays, and the Afghan Suris follow him.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Lodis and suris afghan sultans of delhi" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
