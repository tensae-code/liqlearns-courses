# Afghanistan 1973 1992 coups republic and war — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zahir Shah, the last king, falls to Daoud's 1973 coup and a republic; the 1978 Saur coup brings Taraki's revolutionary regime and the country slides toward war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Afghanistan 1973 1992 coups republic and war" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
