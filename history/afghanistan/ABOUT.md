## The way of the books

Afghanistan's own way, as filed book by book: the primary Social Studies books widen the child's circles and let the past in through schooling's own history and the ancient Silk Road cities (Grade 4), drop the past entirely for the working present (Grade 5), then teach the recent past 1973–2004 as national memory and early Islamic history as the deep past (Grade 6). Secondary History opens with the craft itself, gives the homeland five thousand years first and longest (Grade 7), tells the Islamic centuries from Khorasan's side (Grade 8), walks the national epic reign by reign from 1747 (Grade 9), and re-teaches the whole ancient and Islamic arc at greater depth (Grades 10–11) before the final modern epic in full (Grade 12).
