# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

## Do it first

Measure your classroom wall. A kingdom lasting three thousand years would need a timeline longer than the school building — pace it out in the yard: one step per hundred years.

The Nile's floods let pharaohs raise pyramids and a kingdom that lasts three thousand years. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Nile's floods let pharaohs raise pyramids and a kingdom that lasted three thousand years. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The Nile's floods let pharaohs raise pyramids and a kingdom that lasted three thousand years.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
