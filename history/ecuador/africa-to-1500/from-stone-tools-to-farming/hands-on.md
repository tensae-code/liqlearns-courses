# Hands-on — From Stone Tools to Farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Sort six drawings — a hunter, a seed basket, a goat, a village house, a potter, a market stall — into 'before farming' and 'after farming'. Defend each choice.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Neolithic revolution replaced hunting and gathering with farming, animal domestication, settled villages, specialised work and trade. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
