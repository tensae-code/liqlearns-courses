# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The Neolithic revolution replaced hunting and gathering with farming, animal domestication, settled villages, specialised work and trade.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because the Neolithic revolution replaced hunting and gathering with farming, animal domestication, settled villages, specialised work and trade. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
