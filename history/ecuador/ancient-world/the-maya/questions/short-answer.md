# The Maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because the Maya raised pyramid cities in Mexico and Guatemala and mastered calendars and mathematics. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
