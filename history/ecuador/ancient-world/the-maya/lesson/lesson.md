# The Maya

## Do it first

Count the days until your next birthday. Now imagine a people who could track cycles of centuries in stone. Say why a farming people needs a calendar at all.

The Maya raise pyramid cities in Mexico and Guatemala and master calendars and mathematics. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Maya raised pyramid cities in Mexico and Guatemala and mastered calendars and mathematics. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: The Maya. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
