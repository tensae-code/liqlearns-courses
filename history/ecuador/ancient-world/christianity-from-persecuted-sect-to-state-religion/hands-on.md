# Hands-on — Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two short scenes: a believer hiding to pray, and the same faith's bishop blessing an emperor. What had to change between the two scenes?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A persecuted sect became the state religion of Rome and the organising faith of medieval Europe. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
