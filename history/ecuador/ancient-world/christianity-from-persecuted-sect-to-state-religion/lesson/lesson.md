# Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

## Do it first

Write two short scenes: a believer hiding to pray, and the same faith's bishop blessing an emperor. What had to change between the two scenes?

A persecuted sect becomes the state religion of Rome and the organising faith of medieval Europe. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A persecuted sect became the state religion of Rome and the organising faith of medieval Europe. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
