# Medieval World: Byzantium and Justinian

## Do it first

Find Rome and Constantinople on a map. One empire's western half fell; the eastern half stood for a thousand more years. List two things a walled city on a sea strait has that an inland city does not.

Constantinople keeps Rome alive in the East for a thousand years in Greek and Orthodox splendour. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Constantinople kept Rome alive in the East for a thousand years, in Greek and Orthodox splendour, after the West had fallen. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Medieval World: Byzantium and Justinian. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
