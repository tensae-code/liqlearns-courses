# Inca Expansion into Ecuador and Atahualpa

## Do it first

Two brothers both claim one house after their father dies. While they fight over it, a stranger knocks. Retell Ecuador's turn in those terms, then name the real people.

The Incas push north into Ecuador, Atahualpa wins the civil war against his brother Huáscar, and the Tahuantinsuyo he inherits is already split when the Spaniards land. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
