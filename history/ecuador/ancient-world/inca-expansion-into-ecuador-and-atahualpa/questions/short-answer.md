# Inca Expansion into Ecuador and Atahualpa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The Incas pushed north into Ecuador; Atahualpa won the civil war against his brother Huáscar — and the Tahuantinsuyo he inherited was already split when the Spaniards landed.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because the Incas pushed north into Ecuador; Atahualpa won the civil war against his brother Huáscar — and the Tahuantinsuyo he inherited was already split when the Spaniards landed. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
