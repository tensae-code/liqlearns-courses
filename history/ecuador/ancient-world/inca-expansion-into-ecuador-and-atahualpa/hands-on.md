# Hands-on — Inca Expansion into Ecuador and Atahualpa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two brothers both claim one house after their father dies. While they fight over it, a stranger knocks. Retell Ecuador's turn in those terms, then name the real people.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Incas pushed north into Ecuador; Atahualpa won the civil war against his brother Huáscar — and the Tahuantinsuyo he inherited was already split when the Spaniards landed. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
