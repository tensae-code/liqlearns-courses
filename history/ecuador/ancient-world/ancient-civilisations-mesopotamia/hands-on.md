# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pour a cup of water slowly onto dry soil in a tray. Watch where it runs and settles. Say why people would build their first cities beside water that comes back every year.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Between the Tigris and Euphrates, river floods let the first cities rise, with writing and laws — civilisation begins between two rivers. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
