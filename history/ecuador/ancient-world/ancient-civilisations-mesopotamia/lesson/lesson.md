# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

## Do it first

Pour a cup of water slowly onto dry soil in a tray. Watch where it runs and settles. Say why people would build their first cities beside water that comes back every year.

Between the Tigris and Euphrates the first cities, writing and laws rise on river floods. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Between the Tigris and Euphrates, river floods let the first cities rise, with writing and laws — civilisation begins between two rivers. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
