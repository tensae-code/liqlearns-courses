# The Rise and Expansion of Islam

## Do it first

On a blank map, shade Arabia. Then draw arrows: east, west along North Africa, and north into Spain. Under each arrow write one thing that travels with a faith — language, learning, trade.

Islam spreads from Arabia across the Middle East, North Africa and Spain, meeting Christianity and Judaism. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Islam spread from Arabia across the Middle East, North Africa and Spain, meeting Christianity and Judaism along the way. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: The Rise and Expansion of Islam. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
