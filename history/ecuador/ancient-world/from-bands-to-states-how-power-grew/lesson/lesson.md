# From Bands to States: How Power Grew

## Do it first

Take a class of thirty people. If everyone must grow their own food, how many can be builders or rulers? Now imagine the farmers grow extra. Redo the count.

As societies grow, work divides, surplus appears and power concentrates, turning bands and villages into ranked states. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: As societies grow, work divides, a surplus appears and power concentrates — and bands and villages turn into ranked states. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: From Bands to States: How Power Grew. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
