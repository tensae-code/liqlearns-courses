# Ecuador's First Peoples and Chiefdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Long before any empire, hunter-gatherers settled Ecuador's coast, sierra and Amazonia, farmed, and raised chiefdoms — señoríos — that traded and allied with each other.

**Answer:** Because long before any empire, hunter-gatherers settled Ecuador's coast, sierra and Amazonia, farmed, and raised chiefdoms — señoríos — that traded and allied with each other. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
