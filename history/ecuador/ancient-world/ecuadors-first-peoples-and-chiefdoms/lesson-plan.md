# Lesson plan — Ecuador's First Peoples and Chiefdoms

**Course:** History — Ecuador · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ecuador's First Peoples and Chiefdoms
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Long before any empire, hunter-gatherers settled Ecuador's coast, sierra and Amazonia, farmed, and raised chiefdoms — señoríos — that traded and allied with each other.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the Ancient World (recommended, never forced).
