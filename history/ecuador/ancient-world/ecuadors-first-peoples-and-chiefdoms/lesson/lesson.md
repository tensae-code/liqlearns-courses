# Ecuador's First Peoples and Chiefdoms

## Do it first

Draw Ecuador's three regions — coast, sierra, Amazonia — from memory. Put one thing each region could trade to the others.

Hunter-gatherers settle, farm and raise chiefdoms (señoríos) across coast, sierra and Amazonia, trading and allying long before any empire arrives. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Long before any empire, hunter-gatherers settled Ecuador's coast, sierra and Amazonia, farmed, and raised chiefdoms — señoríos — that traded and allied with each other. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Ecuador's First Peoples and Chiefdoms. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
