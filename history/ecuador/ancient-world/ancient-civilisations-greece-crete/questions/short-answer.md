# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Antigua Grecia; Las Amazonas (Mitología griega).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because greek city-states invented democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean — and Greek myth tells of the Amazons, a women-only warrior people ruled by a queen, whose wars with Heracles, Theseus and Achilles the Greeks never stopped retelling. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
