# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Greek city-states invent democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A women-only warrior people ruled by a queen worship Artemis and are credited with founding Ephesus and its great temple; Heracles seizes Queen Hippolyta's belt and kills her, Theseus carries off Antiope and Athens defeats the Amazons' revenge war, and Queen Penthesilea dies aiding Troy against Achilles.
**Answer: True**
