# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Greek city-states invented democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean — and Greek myth tells of the Amazons, a women-only warrior people ruled by a queen, whose wars with Heracles, Theseus and Achilles the Greeks never stopped retelling.

Greek city-states invent democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean.

A women-only warrior people ruled by a queen worship Artemis and are credited with founding Ephesus and its great temple; Heracles seizes Queen Hippolyta's belt and kills her, Theseus carries off Antiope and Athens defeats the Amazons' revenge war, and Queen Penthesilea dies aiding Troy against Achilles.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
