# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

## Do it first

Hold a class vote on a real small question. Then ask: who was allowed to speak, who counted the votes, who was left out? That gap is where the Greek story starts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Antigua Grecia

Greek city-states invent democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this part of the story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Las Amazonas (Mitología griega)

A women-only warrior people ruled by a queen worship Artemis and are credited with founding Ephesus and its great temple; Heracles seizes Queen Hippolyta's belt and kills her, Theseus carries off Antiope and Athens defeats the Amazons' revenge war, and Queen Penthesilea dies aiding Troy against Achilles. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this part of the story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Greek city-states invented democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean — and Greek myth tells of the Amazons, a women-only warrior people ruled by a queen, whose wars with Heracles, Theseus and Achilles the Greeks never stopped retelling. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
