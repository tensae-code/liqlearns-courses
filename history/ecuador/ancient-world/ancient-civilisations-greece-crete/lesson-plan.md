# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

**Course:** History — Ecuador · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Greek city-states invented democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean — and Greek myth tells of the Amazons, a women-only warrior people ruled by a queen, whose wars with Heracles, Theseus and Achilles the Greeks never stopped retelling.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Deep Past and the Ancient World (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Antigua Grecia** — Greek city-states invent democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean.
2. **Las Amazonas (Mitología griega)** — A women-only warrior people ruled by a queen worship Artemis and are credited with founding Ephesus and its great temple; Heracles seizes Queen Hippolyta's belt and kills her, Theseus carries off Antiope and Athens defeats the Amazons' revenge war, and Queen Penthesilea dies aiding Troy against Achilles.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks democracy means everyone voting from the start → the city-states invented the practice and argued about who counted; the Amazon myths show the same Greeks imagining a world ruled by women.
- Go back to the one thing: Greek city-states invented democracy, philosophy and theatre and spread them around the Mediterranean — and Greek myth tells of the Amazons, a women-only warrior people ruled by a queen, whose wars with Heracles, Theseus and Achilles the Greeks never stopped retelling.
