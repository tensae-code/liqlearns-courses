# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one rule in your school that exists in writing. Now imagine it still being used, in changed words, two thousand years from now, in countries Rome never reached. That is what happened to Rome's law.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Rome conquered an empire, then outlived it: civil law across the modern world still descends from Roman law. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
