# Ancient Civilisations: Rome

## Do it first

Name one rule in your school that exists in writing. Now imagine it still being used, in changed words, two thousand years from now, in countries Rome never reached. That is what happened to Rome's law.

Rome conquers an empire, then outlives it — civil law across the modern world still descends from Roman law. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Rome conquered an empire, then outlived it: civil law across the modern world still descends from Roman law. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Ancient Civilisations: Rome. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
