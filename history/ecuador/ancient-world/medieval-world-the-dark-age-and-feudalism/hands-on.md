# Hands-on — Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a ladder. Put a king at the top and a serf family at the bottom. For each rung, write what that person gives the rung above and what they get back.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After Rome, Europe's lords, vassals and serfs locked society into a feudal ladder that lasted a thousand years. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
