# Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism

## Do it first

Draw a ladder. Put a king at the top and a serf family at the bottom. For each rung, write what that person gives the rung above and what they get back.

After Rome, Europe's lords, vassals and serfs lock society into a feudal ladder that lasts a thousand years. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
