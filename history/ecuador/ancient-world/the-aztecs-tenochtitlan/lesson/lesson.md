# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan

## Do it first

Float a small tray of soil on water in a basin. Plant a seed in it. Say why a city on a lake would farm this way — and what problem it solves.

The Aztecs build Tenochtitlan on a lake and farm it with floating chinampa gardens. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Aztecs built Tenochtitlan on a lake and farmed it with floating chinampa gardens. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
