# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The Aztecs built Tenochtitlan on a lake and farmed it with floating chinampa gardens.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because the Aztecs built Tenochtitlan on a lake and farmed it with floating chinampa gardens. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
