# The Incas and the Qhapaq Ñan

## Do it first

Walk your school's corridors and count the rooms one road connects. The Incas connected whole mountain valleys this way — without writing or money. List what runners, roads and storehouses replace.

The Incas bind the Andes with the Qhapaq Ñan road network, terraced farms and a tightly ranked empire run without writing or money. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: The Incas and the Qhapaq Ñan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
