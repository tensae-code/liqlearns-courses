# The Incas and the Qhapaq Ñan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The Incas bound the Andes with the Qhapaq Ñan road network, terraced farms and a tightly ranked empire run without writing or money.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because the Incas bound the Andes with the Qhapaq Ñan road network, terraced farms and a tightly ranked empire run without writing or money. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
