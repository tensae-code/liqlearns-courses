# Ancient Civilisations: China

## Do it first

Write your name three ways: as a picture, as letters, as a symbol you invent. Say which one a whole empire could use to govern far-apart provinces.

On the Yellow River Chinese civilisation grows its own writing, dynasties and inventions. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: On the Yellow River, Chinese civilisation grew its own writing, dynasties and inventions — a river civilisation with its own road. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Ancient Civilisations: China. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: On the Yellow River, Chinese civilisation grew its own writing, dynasties and inventions — a river civilisation with its own road.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
