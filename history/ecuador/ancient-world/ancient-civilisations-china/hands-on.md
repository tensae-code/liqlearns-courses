# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: China

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write your name three ways: as a picture, as letters, as a symbol you invent. Say which one a whole empire could use to govern far-apart provinces.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On the Yellow River, Chinese civilisation grew its own writing, dynasties and inventions — a river civilisation with its own road. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
