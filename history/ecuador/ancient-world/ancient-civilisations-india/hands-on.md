# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: India

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw the streets of your town from above. Now draw a city where every street runs straight and every house connects to a drain. Say what planning like that tells about the people in charge.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Indus valley cities planned their streets and drains, and river-valley India then built its own orders and faiths. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
