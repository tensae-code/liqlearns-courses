# Ancient Civilisations: India

## Do it first

Draw the streets of your town from above. Now draw a city where every street runs straight and every house connects to a drain. Say what planning like that tells about the people in charge.

Indus valley cities plan streets and drains, then river-valley India builds its orders and faiths. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
