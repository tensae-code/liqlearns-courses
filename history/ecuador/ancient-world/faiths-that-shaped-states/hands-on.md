# Hands-on — Faiths That Shaped States

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw one hill town. Mark three places of worship on it. Write one way the three communities can share the town well, and one way they can fall into conflict.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Three monotheisms share one holy land in the Middle East, alternating between coexistence, debate and conflict across the centuries. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
