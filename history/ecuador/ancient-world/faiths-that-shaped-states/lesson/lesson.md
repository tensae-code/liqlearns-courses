# Faiths That Shaped States

## Do it first

Draw one hill town. Mark three places of worship on it. Write one way the three communities can share the town well, and one way they can fall into conflict.

Three monotheisms share one holy land and alternate between coexistence, debate and conflict. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Three monotheisms share one holy land in the Middle East, alternating between coexistence, debate and conflict across the centuries. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Faiths That Shaped States. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
