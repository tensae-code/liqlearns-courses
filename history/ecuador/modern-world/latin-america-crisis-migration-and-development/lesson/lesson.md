# Latin America: Crisis, Migration and Development

## Do it first

A parent leaves to work abroad and sends money home. List what the home gains, what it loses, and what the new country gains. Migration remakes both ends.

Debt crises and stalled development push millions of Latin Americans to migrate, remaking both the homes left and the countries reached. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
