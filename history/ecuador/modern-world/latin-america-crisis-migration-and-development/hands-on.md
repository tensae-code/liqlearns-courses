# Hands-on — Latin America: Crisis, Migration and Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A parent leaves to work abroad and sends money home. List what the home gains, what it loses, and what the new country gains. Migration remakes both ends.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Debt crises and stalled development push millions of Latin Americans to migrate, remaking both the homes left behind and the countries reached. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
