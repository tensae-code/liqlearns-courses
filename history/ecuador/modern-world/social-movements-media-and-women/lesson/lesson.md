# Social Movements, Media and Women

## Do it first

Pick one change you want in your school. Now plan it three ways: a movement of people, a message in media, an organisation that lasts. Which engine moves it furthest?

Movements, media and women's organising become engines that push societies to change. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Movements, media and women's organising became engines that push societies to change. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Social Movements, Media and Women. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
