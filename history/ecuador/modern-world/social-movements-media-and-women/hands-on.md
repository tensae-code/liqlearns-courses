# Hands-on — Social Movements, Media and Women

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pick one change you want in your school. Now plan it three ways: a movement of people, a message in media, an organisation that lasts. Which engine moves it furthest?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Movements, media and women's organising became engines that push societies to change. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
