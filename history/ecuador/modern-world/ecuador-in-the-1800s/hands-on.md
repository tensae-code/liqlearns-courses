# Hands-on — Ecuador in the 1800s

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two strong men both claim to know what the young republic needs. One builds with an iron hand; a rising in the sierra answers him. Say what 'modernising' can cost when it is imposed.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Flores and Rocafuerte contended, the 1845 Marcista revolution rose, García Moreno modernised with an iron hand, and Daquilema's Indigenous rising shook the sierra. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
