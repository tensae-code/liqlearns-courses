# Ecuador in the 1800s

## Do it first

Two strong men both claim to know what the young republic needs. One builds with an iron hand; a rising in the sierra answers him. Say what 'modernising' can cost when it is imposed.

Flores and Rocafuerte contend, the 1845 Marcista revolution rises, García Moreno modernises with an iron hand, and Daquilema's Indigenous rising shakes the sierra. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
