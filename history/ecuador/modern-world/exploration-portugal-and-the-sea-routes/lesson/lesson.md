# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes

## Do it first

Trace a route on a map from Lisbon down Africa's coast, and another across the Atlantic to Brazil. Say what a small country gains by holding sea routes instead of land.

Portugal takes Brazil, and the Portuguese sea routes tie America into a world trading system. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
