# Hands-on — The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A king rules by birth. A crowd answers: rights belong to every man. Write the two claims as two posters, then say why the second one could not be un-said once spoken.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The French Revolution opened waves of revolution, toppling monarchy for republic and proclaiming the rights of man. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
