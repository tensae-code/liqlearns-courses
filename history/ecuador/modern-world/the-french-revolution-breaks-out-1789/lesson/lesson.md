# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789

## Do it first

A king rules by birth. A crowd answers: rights belong to every man. Write the two claims as two posters, then say why the second one could not be un-said once spoken.

The French Revolution opens waves of revolution, toppling monarchy for republic and proclaiming the rights of man. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The French Revolution opened waves of revolution, toppling monarchy for republic and proclaiming the rights of man. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
