# Women Through History

## Do it first

Name one woman in your own family's story who changed something — a schooling, a piece of land, a right. The book's women did the same at the scale of nations.

## Sub lesson 1 — Evolución femenina a través de las eras

Women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña, and Rigoberta Menchú fight across eras for education, land and equality. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this part of the story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tránsito Amaguaña

Born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school as an Indigenous child, Tránsito Amaguaña rebels, founds Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Dolores Cacuango, and fights for agrarian reform and communal lands into old age. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this part of the story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
