# Women Through History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Evolución femenina a través de las eras; Tránsito Amaguaña.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña — born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school, who rebelled, founded Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Cacuango and fought for agrarian reform into old age — and Rigoberta Menchú fought across eras for education, land and equality. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
