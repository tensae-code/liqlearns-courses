# Women Through History — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Evolución femenina a través de las eras — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Tránsito Amaguaña — b)


Meanings:
a) Women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña, and Rigoberta Menchú fight across eras for education, land and equality.
b) Born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school as an Indigenous child, Tránsito Amaguaña rebels, founds Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Dolores Cacuango, and fights for agrarian reform and communal lands into old age.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
