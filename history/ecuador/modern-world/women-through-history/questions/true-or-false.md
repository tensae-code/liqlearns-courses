# Women Through History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña, and Rigoberta Menchú fight across eras for education, land and equality.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school as an Indigenous child, Tránsito Amaguaña rebels, founds Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Dolores Cacuango, and fights for agrarian reform and communal lands into old age.
**Answer: True**
