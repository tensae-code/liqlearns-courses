## Sub lesson 2 — Tránsito Amaguaña
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school as an Indigenous child, Tránsito Amaguaña rebels, founds Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Dolores Cacuango, and fights for agrarian reform and communal lands into old age — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
