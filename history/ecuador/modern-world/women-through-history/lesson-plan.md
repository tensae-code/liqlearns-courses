# Lesson plan — Women Through History

**Course:** History — Ecuador · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Women Through History
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Ecuador and the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña — born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school, who rebelled, founded Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Cacuango and fought for agrarian reform into old age — and Rigoberta Menchú fought across eras for education, land and equality.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Ecuador and the Modern World (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Evolución femenina a través de las eras** — Women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña, and Rigoberta Menchú fight across eras for education, land and equality.
2. **Tránsito Amaguaña** — Born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school as an Indigenous child, Tránsito Amaguaña rebels, founds Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Dolores Cacuango, and fights for agrarian reform and communal lands into old age.
