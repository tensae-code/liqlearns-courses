# Video script — Women Through History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña — born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school, who rebelled, founded Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Cacuango and fought for agrarian reform into old age — and Rigoberta Menchú fought across eras for education, land and equality.

Women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña, and Rigoberta Menchú fight across eras for education, land and equality.

Born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school as an Indigenous child, Tránsito Amaguaña rebels, founds Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Dolores Cacuango, and fights for agrarian reform and communal lands into old age.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
