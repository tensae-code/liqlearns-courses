# Lesson plan — Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes

**Course:** History — Ecuador · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Ecuador and the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
