# Hands-on — Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A lord owns land and serfs work it. A factory owner owns machines and workers sell their labour. List what is owned, and by whom, in each world. The change between the lists is capitalism arriving.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Feudal society gave way to capitalism — private ownership and new classes — and colonial America's mines and plantations fed Europe's shift: modernity begins in that double movement. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
