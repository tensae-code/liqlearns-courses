# Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes

## Do it first

A lord owns land and serfs work it. A factory owner owns machines and workers sell their labour. List what is owned, and by whom, in each world. The change between the lists is capitalism arriving.

## Sub lesson 1 — El paso al capitalismo

Feudal society gives way to capitalism, private ownership and new classes, and modernity begins. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this part of the story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Sub lesson 2 — La economía colonial — del feudalismo al capitalismo

Colonial America's mines and plantations feed Europe's shift from feudalism to capitalism. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this part of the story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
