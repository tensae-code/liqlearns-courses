Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Feudal society gives way to capitalism, private ownership and new classes, and modernity begins.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Colonial America's mines and plantations feed Europe's shift from feudalism to capitalism.
