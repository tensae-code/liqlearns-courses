# Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** El paso al capitalismo; La economía colonial — del feudalismo al capitalismo.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because feudal society gave way to capitalism — private ownership and new classes — and colonial America's mines and plantations fed Europe's shift: modernity begins in that double movement. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
