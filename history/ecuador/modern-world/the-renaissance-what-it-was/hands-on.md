# Hands-on — The Renaissance: What It Was

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at two drawings of the same person: one flat and symbolic, one with shadow and depth. Say what changed in how the artist saw the world — that change is the Renaissance in one picture.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Europe rediscovered classical learning and art and began to remake its picture of the world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
