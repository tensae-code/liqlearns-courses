# The Renaissance: What It Was

## Do it first

Look at two drawings of the same person: one flat and symbolic, one with shadow and depth. Say what changed in how the artist saw the world — that change is the Renaissance in one picture.

Europe rediscovers classical learning and art and begins to remake its picture of the world. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Europe rediscovered classical learning and art and began to remake its picture of the world. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: The Renaissance: What It Was. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
