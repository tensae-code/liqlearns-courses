# Hands-on — American Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List what a colony must do to become a republic: win battles, yes — but also govern, tax, judge and be recognised. Which of those is hardest after the fighting ends?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Spain's American colonies rose, fought long wars of independence and freed themselves as new republics. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
