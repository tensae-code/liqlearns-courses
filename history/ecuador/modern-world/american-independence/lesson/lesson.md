# American Independence

## Do it first

List what a colony must do to become a republic: win battles, yes — but also govern, tax, judge and be recognised. Which of those is hardest after the fighting ends?

Spain's American colonies rise, fight long wars of independence and free themselves as new republics. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Spain's American colonies rose, fought long wars of independence and freed themselves as new republics. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: American Independence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
