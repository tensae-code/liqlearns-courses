# Ecuador: Oil Boom and Return to Democracy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The 1964 agrarian reform banned the huasipungo, oil money boomed in the 1970s, democracy returned in 1979 — then debt crisis, mass migration and Indigenous movements challenged the neoliberal republic. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
