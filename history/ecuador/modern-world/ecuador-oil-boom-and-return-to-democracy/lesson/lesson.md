# Ecuador: Oil Boom and Return to Democracy

## Do it first

Oil is found under the jungle. Money pours in; then the debt comes due and people emigrate. Draw the boom and the bust as two halves of one circle.

The 1964 agrarian reform bans the huasipungo, oil money booms in the 1970s, democracy returns in 1979, then debt crisis, mass migration and Indigenous movements challenge the neoliberal republic. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
