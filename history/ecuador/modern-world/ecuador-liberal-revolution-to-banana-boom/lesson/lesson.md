# Ecuador: Liberal Revolution to Banana Boom

## Do it first

A revolutionary wins, transforms the country — and is dragged to his death by a crowd. Then bananas, not battles, remake the coast. Say what this teaches about how change really comes.

Eloy Alfaro's Liberal Revolution wins and Alfaro is dragged to his death in 1912, plutocracy and the 1925 Juliana revolution follow, Velasco Ibarra returns again and again, war with Peru strikes in 1941, and the banana boom remakes the coast. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
