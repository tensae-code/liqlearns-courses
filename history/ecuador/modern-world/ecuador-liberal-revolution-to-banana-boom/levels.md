# Ecuador: Liberal Revolution to Banana Boom — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Eloy Alfaro's Liberal Revolution won and Alfaro was dragged to his death in 1912; plutocracy and the 1925 Juliana revolution followed, Velasco Ibarra returned again and again, war with Peru struck in 1941, and the banana boom remade the coast. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
