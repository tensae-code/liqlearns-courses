# Conquest and Colony in Ecuador

## Do it first

A room filled with treasure is offered for one man's life — and he is killed anyway. Say what that moment taught the Andes about the newcomers, and why resistance never stopped after it.

Pizarro's 160 men seize Atahualpa at Cajamarca in 1533 and he is killed despite a ransom room of treasure; colony, mestizaje and Afro-Ecuadorian roots follow, and Indigenous and mestizo uprisings keep resisting cultural domination. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
