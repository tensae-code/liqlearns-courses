# Conquest and Colony in Ecuador — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pizarro's 160 men seize Atahualpa at Cajamarca in 1533 and he is killed despite a ransom room of treasure; colony, mestizaje and Afro-Ecuadorian roots follow, and Indigenous and mestizo uprisings keep resisting cultural domination.
