# Conquest and Colony in Ecuador — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Pizarro's 160 men seized Atahualpa at Cajamarca in 1533 and killed him despite a ransom room of treasure; colony, mestizaje and Afro-Ecuadorian roots followed — and Indigenous and mestizo uprisings kept resisting cultural domination.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because pizarro's 160 men seized Atahualpa at Cajamarca in 1533 and killed him despite a ransom room of treasure; colony, mestizaje and Afro-Ecuadorian roots followed — and Indigenous and mestizo uprisings kept resisting cultural domination. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
