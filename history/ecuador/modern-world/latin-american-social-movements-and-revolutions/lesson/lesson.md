# Latin American Social Movements and Revolutions

## Do it first

Make a line of risings: a colonial uprising, an independence war, a modern equality march. What is the same in all three? What is different?

From Indigenous resistance to modern equality struggles, Latin Americans repeatedly rise against the orders imposed on them. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: From Indigenous resistance to modern equality struggles, Latin Americans have repeatedly risen against the orders imposed on them. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Latin American Social Movements and Revolutions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
