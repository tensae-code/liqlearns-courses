# Hands-on — Latin American Social Movements and Revolutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Make a line of risings: a colonial uprising, an independence war, a modern equality march. What is the same in all three? What is different?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From Indigenous resistance to modern equality struggles, Latin Americans have repeatedly risen against the orders imposed on them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
