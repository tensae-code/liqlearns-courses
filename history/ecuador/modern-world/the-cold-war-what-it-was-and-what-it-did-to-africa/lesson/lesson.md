# The Cold War: What It Was

## Do it first

Two giants refuse to fight each other directly, so they arm smaller countries to fight instead. Draw that triangle. Why is it called 'cold' — and for whom was it hot?

Capitalism and communism face off in a Cold War of proxies, arms races and divided countries. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Capitalism and communism faced off in a Cold War of proxies, arms races and divided countries through the second half of the twentieth century. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: The Cold War: What It Was. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
