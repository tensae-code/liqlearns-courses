# Hands-on — The Cold War: What It Was

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two giants refuse to fight each other directly, so they arm smaller countries to fight instead. Draw that triangle. Why is it called 'cold' — and for whom was it hot?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Capitalism and communism faced off in a Cold War of proxies, arms races and divided countries through the second half of the twentieth century. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
