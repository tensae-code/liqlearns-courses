# Ecuador Founded, 1830

## Do it first

Draw Ecuador split down the Andes: coast on one side, sierra on the other. List what each side sells, eats and wants from a government. That split is the new republic's first problem.

Gran Colombia breaks apart in 1830, Juan José Flores becomes first president of a poor, disunited republic split between coast and sierra oligarchies. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
