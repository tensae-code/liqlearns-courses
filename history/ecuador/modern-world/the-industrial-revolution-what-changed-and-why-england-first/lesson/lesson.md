# The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First

## Do it first

Why England first? List what a country needs to start factories: coal, workers, money, markets. Then ask which of those empires then seized abroad.

Factories and steam start in England, capitalism industrialises the world, and empires seize colonies for markets and raw materials. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
