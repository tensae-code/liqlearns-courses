# Hands-on — The Industrial Revolution: What Changed and Why England First

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Why England first? List what a country needs to start factories: coal, workers, money, markets. Then ask which of those empires then seized abroad.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Factories and steam started in England, capitalism industrialised the world, and empires seized colonies for markets and raw materials. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
