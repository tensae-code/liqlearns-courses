# Ecuador in the 21st Century

## Do it first

Your country's money collapses, so it adopts another country's dollar. List one thing that gets easier and one thing the country can never do again. That bargain is dollarisation.

The 2000 dollarisation follows a banking collapse and mass emigration, and a transformed Ecuador argues over oil, rights and its new constitution. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
