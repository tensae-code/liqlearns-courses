# Conquest and Colony in Latin America

## Do it first

Name one word, one food and one festival in Latin America today. For each, guess: Indigenous, Spanish, or both mixed? Colonial centuries are still visible in daily life.

Small Spanish bands topple the Aztec and Inca empires, and three centuries of colonial rule remake America's peoples, languages and faiths. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
