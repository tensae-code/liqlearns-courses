# Conquest and Colony in Latin America — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Small Spanish bands toppled the Aztec and Inca empires, and three centuries of colonial rule remade America's peoples, languages and faiths. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
