# Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States

## Do it first

Sing or say the words of a song your people sing about themselves. What does it claim 'we' are? That claim, spreading, is nationalism at work.

Revolutionary and national ideas cross the Atlantic and teach American peoples to imagine themselves as nations. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Revolutionary and national ideas crossed the Atlantic and taught American peoples to imagine themselves as nations. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Nationalism: The Feeling That Made Nation-States. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
