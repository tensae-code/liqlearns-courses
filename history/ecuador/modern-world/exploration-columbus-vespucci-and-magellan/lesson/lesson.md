# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

## Do it first

Draw two islands that have never touched. Now draw the first ship between them. List three things that will cross on that ship — and three that will cross back.

Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan's voyages join two worlds that had evolved apart. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The voyages of Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan joined two worlds that had evolved apart for thousands of years. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The voyages of Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan joined two worlds that had evolved apart for thousands of years.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
