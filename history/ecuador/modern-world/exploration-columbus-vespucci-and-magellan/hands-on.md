# Hands-on — Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw two islands that have never touched. Now draw the first ship between them. List three things that will cross on that ship — and three that will cross back.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The voyages of Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan joined two worlds that had evolved apart for thousands of years. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
