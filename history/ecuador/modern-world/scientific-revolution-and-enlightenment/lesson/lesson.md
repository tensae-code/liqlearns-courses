# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment

## Do it first

Ask one 'why' question about something you were told never to question. Write how a scientist would test it and how a king would prefer you not to.

New science and Enlightenment reason challenge church and king and prepare revolutions. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: New science and Enlightenment reason challenged church and king and prepared the revolutions to come. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: New science and Enlightenment reason challenged church and king and prepared the revolutions to come.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
