# Hands-on — Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask one 'why' question about something you were told never to question. Write how a scientist would test it and how a king would prefer you not to.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: New science and Enlightenment reason challenged church and king and prepared the revolutions to come. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
