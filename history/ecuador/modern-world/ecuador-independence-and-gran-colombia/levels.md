# Ecuador: Independence and Gran Colombia — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Quito's 1809 cry, Guayaquil's 1820 independence and Sucre's victory at Pichincha in 1822 freed the land; Bolívar and San Martín met at Guayaquil, and Ecuador joined Bolívar's Gran Colombia union. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
