# Ecuador: Independence and Gran Colombia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Quito's 1809 cry, Guayaquil's 1820 independence and Sucre's victory at Pichincha in 1822 freed the land; Bolívar and San Martín met at Guayaquil, and Ecuador joined Bolívar's Gran Colombia union.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because quito's 1809 cry, Guayaquil's 1820 independence and Sucre's victory at Pichincha in 1822 freed the land; Bolívar and San Martín met at Guayaquil, and Ecuador joined Bolívar's Gran Colombia union. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
