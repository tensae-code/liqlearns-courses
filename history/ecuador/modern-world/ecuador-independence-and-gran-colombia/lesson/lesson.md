# Ecuador: Independence and Gran Colombia

## Do it first

Put three dates on a line: 1809, 1820, 1822. For each, say in one sentence what changed. A country's freedom can have more than one birthday.

Quito's 1809 cry, Guayaquil's 1820 independence and Sucre's victory at Pichincha in 1822 free the land, Bolívar and San Martín meet at Guayaquil, and Ecuador joins Bolívar's Gran Colombia union. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
