# History of Economic Thought — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Mercantilists, liberals, Marxists and Keynesians argued in turn over what wealth is and who should control it.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because mercantilists, liberals, Marxists and Keynesians argued in turn over what wealth is and who should control it. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
