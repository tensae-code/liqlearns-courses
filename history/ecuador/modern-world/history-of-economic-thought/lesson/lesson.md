# History of Economic Thought

## Do it first

Four thinkers walk into a market. One counts gold, one frees trade, one sides with workers, one calls on the state. Give each one sentence — you have just summarised four schools.

Mercantilists, liberals, Marxists and Keynesians argue in turn over what wealth is and who should control it. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Mercantilists, liberals, Marxists and Keynesians argued in turn over what wealth is and who should control it. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: History of Economic Thought. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
