# Colonial Art of Spanish America — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Indigenous and mestizo artists filled colonial churches with a baroque art that mixes European forms with American hands and symbols.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because indigenous and mestizo artists filled colonial churches with a baroque art that mixes European forms with American hands and symbols. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
