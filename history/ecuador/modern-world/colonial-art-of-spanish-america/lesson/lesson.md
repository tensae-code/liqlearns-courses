# Colonial Art of Spanish America

## Do it first

Look at any decorated building near you. Find one detail that comes from far away and one that could only be made here. Colonial art works exactly that way.

Indigenous and mestizo artists fill colonial churches with a baroque art that mixes European forms with American hands and symbols. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Indigenous and mestizo artists filled colonial churches with a baroque art that mixes European forms with American hands and symbols. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Colonial Art of Spanish America. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
