# Hands-on — Colonial Art of Spanish America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at any decorated building near you. Find one detail that comes from far away and one that could only be made here. Colonial art works exactly that way.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Indigenous and mestizo artists filled colonial churches with a baroque art that mixes European forms with American hands and symbols. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
