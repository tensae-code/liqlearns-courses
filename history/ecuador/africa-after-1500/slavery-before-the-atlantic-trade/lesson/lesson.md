# Slavery Before the Atlantic Trade

## Do it first

List three jobs in the ancient world. For each, ask: could the empire have run without forced labour? Then ask the book's question about your own century.

Ancient empires build their wealth on enslaved people, and pupils judge whether abolition in the 1800s really ended slavery or only changed its forms. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ancient empires built their wealth on enslaved people — and the book asks pupils to judge whether abolition in the 1800s really ended slavery or only changed its forms. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Slavery Before the Atlantic Trade. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
