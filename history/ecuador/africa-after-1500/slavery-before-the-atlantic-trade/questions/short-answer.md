# Slavery Before the Atlantic Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Ancient empires built their wealth on enslaved people — and the book asks pupils to judge whether abolition in the 1800s really ended slavery or only changed its forms.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because ancient empires built their wealth on enslaved people — and the book asks pupils to judge whether abolition in the 1800s really ended slavery or only changed its forms. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
