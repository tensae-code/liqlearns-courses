# Maroon Resistance and Queen Nanny

## Do it first

Stand very still against a wall or a tree until a partner walks past without noticing you. That stillness, in the mountains, was a weapon. Say what else Nanny needed besides camouflage.

Nanny, an escaped slave of African royal descent, frees others, founds mountain-top Nanny Town in British Jamaica, camouflages her Maroons as trees to ambush soldiers, and holds out until hunger — not British guns — tests her people. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.
