# Maroon Resistance and Queen Nanny — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Nanny, an escaped slave of African royal descent, freed others, founded mountain-top Nanny Town in British Jamaica, camouflaged her Maroons as trees to ambush soldiers, and held out until hunger — not British guns — tested her people.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because nanny, an escaped slave of African royal descent, freed others, founded mountain-top Nanny Town in British Jamaica, camouflaged her Maroons as trees to ambush soldiers, and held out until hunger — not British guns — tested her people. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
