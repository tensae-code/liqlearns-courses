# Maroon Resistance and Queen Nanny — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Nanny, an escaped slave of African royal descent, freed others, founded mountain-top Nanny Town in British Jamaica, camouflaged her Maroons as trees to ambush soldiers, and held out until hunger — not British guns — tested her people. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
