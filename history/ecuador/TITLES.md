# History — Ecuador course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Ecuador's own — built from Ecuador's Estudios Sociales and Historia books (Ministerio de Educación del Ecuador, Spanish): the primary Estudios Sociales books, the Grade 8 thematic book, the Grade 11 Bachillerato Historia, and the two reading-plan booklets. Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside the levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grade each book comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': history begins as the child's own past and heritage, widens to the deep past and the ancient world, turns at conquest, colony and independence, and lands on Ecuador's own modern story inside Latin America's. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
