# Historiography: The History of History Writing

## Do it first

Take any story you have heard about the past. Write two questions you would ask before believing it: who told it, and how could they know?

Jesuit Juan de Velasco writes Ecuador's first history in exile, later critics argue parts read as legend, and pupils must weigh an "official history" against its critics. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ecuador's first history was written in exile by the Jesuit Juan de Velasco — and later critics argue parts of it read as legend, so an 'official history' must always be weighed against its critics. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: Historiography: The History of History Writing. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
