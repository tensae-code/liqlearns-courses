# Video script — Find History in Your Own Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every family, parish and canton has its own history: interview the elders, sort events into past, present and future, and investigate your own place's founding origins and the people it honours.

A child interviews older relatives about ancestors, sorts family events into past, present and future, and learns every family has a history that makes it unique.

Pupils use varied sources to investigate the founding origins and most significant features of their own parish and canton, and learn whose name each one honours.

Every parish and canton has its own history and its own representative figures — Jefferson Pérez, Matilde Hidalgo de Prócel, Eduardo Kingman among them — who leave footprints in their birthplace and bind its people together.

Ecuadorians share one territory, one history and many traditions across mestizo, Indigenous and Afro-Ecuadorian peoples, and each child researches their own family's origin and tells its story to the class.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
