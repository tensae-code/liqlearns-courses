# Find History in Your Own Locality — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — La historia de mi familia
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A child interviews older relatives about ancestors, sorts family events into past, present and future, and learns every family has a history that makes it unique — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mi parroquia y mi cantón (skill: founding origins)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Pupils use varied sources to investigate the founding origins and most significant features of their own parish and canton, and learn whose name each one honours — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Acontecimientos y personajes de la parroquia y del cantón
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Every parish and canton has its own history and its own representative figures — Jefferson Pérez, Matilde Hidalgo de Prócel, Eduardo Kingman among them — who leave footprints in their birthplace and bind its people together — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — La gente ecuatoriana
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Ecuadorians share one territory, one history and many traditions across mestizo, Indigenous and Afro-Ecuadorian peoples, and each child researches their own family's origin and tells its story to the class — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
