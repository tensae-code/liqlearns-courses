# Find History in Your Own Locality — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) La historia de mi familia — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Mi parroquia y mi cantón (skill: founding origins) — b)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Acontecimientos y personajes de la parroquia y del cantón — c)

**4.** (Sub lesson 4) La gente ecuatoriana — d)


Meanings:
a) A child interviews older relatives about ancestors, sorts family events into past, present and future, and learns every family has a history that makes it unique.
b) Pupils use varied sources to investigate the founding origins and most significant features of their own parish and canton, and learn whose name each one honours.
c) Every parish and canton has its own history and its own representative figures — Jefferson Pérez, Matilde Hidalgo de Prócel, Eduardo Kingman among them — who leave footprints in their birthplace and bind its people together.
d) Ecuadorians share one territory, one history and many traditions across mestizo, Indigenous and Afro-Ecuadorian peoples, and each child researches their own family's origin and tells its story to the class.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c; 4. d
