# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La historia de mi familia; Mi parroquia y mi cantón (skill: founding origins); Acontecimientos y personajes de la parroquia y del cantón; La gente ecuatoriana.

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because every family, parish and canton has its own history: interview the elders, sort events into past, present and future, and investigate your own place's founding origins and the people it honours. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
