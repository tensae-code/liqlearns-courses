# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “La historia de mi familia” is right?
a) A child interviews older relatives about ancestors, sorts family events into past, present and future, and learns every family has a history that makes it unique.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Ecuador's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which statement about “Mi parroquia y mi cantón (skill: founding origins)” is right?
a) it has nothing to do with this lesson  b) Pupils use varied sources to investigate the founding origins and most significant features of their own parish and canton, and learn whose name each one honours.  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Ecuador's books
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
