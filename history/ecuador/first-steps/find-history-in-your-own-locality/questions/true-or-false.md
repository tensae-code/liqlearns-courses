# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A child interviews older relatives about ancestors, sorts family events into past, present and future, and learns every family has a history that makes it unique.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Pupils use varied sources to investigate the founding origins and most significant features of their own parish and canton, and learn whose name each one honours.
**Answer: True**
