# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** El papel de la "historia y trabajo" en la evolución de los pueblos americanos; La Historia.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because history is built from traces of the past — time, culture and work define it, and in America work itself remade whole peoples, with women carrying half of that hidden story. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
