# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) American peoples are remade by work, from the first farmers to today's workers, and women carry half that hidden story.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Pupils learn what history is, how time, culture and work define it, and how historians build the past from traces.
**Answer: True**
