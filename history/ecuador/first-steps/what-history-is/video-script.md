# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is built from traces of the past — time, culture and work define it, and in America work itself remade whole peoples, with women carrying half of that hidden story.

American peoples are remade by work, from the first farmers to today's workers, and women carry half that hidden story.

Pupils learn what history is, how time, culture and work define it, and how historians build the past from traces.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
