# What History Is

## Do it first

Name three traces of the past in your own home — an old tool, a photograph, a song someone taught you. For each one, say what it can tell about the people who came before you.

## Sub lesson 1 — El papel de la "historia y trabajo" en la evolución de los pueblos americanos

American peoples are remade by work, from the first farmers to today's workers, and women carry half that hidden story. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this part of the story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Sub lesson 2 — La Historia

Pupils learn what history is, how time, culture and work define it, and how historians build the past from traces. Hold that turn in mind: who is in this part of the story, where it happens, what changes because of it, and what comes of it — that is how this course tells every story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: History is built from traces of the past — time, culture and work define it, and in America work itself remade whole peoples, with women carrying half of that hidden story. Give one real example from your own life, and name the lesson's key words: What History Is. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
