# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** La minga as inherited tradition; Patrimonios culturales del Ecuador; Leyenda La Dama Tapada (old stories); Diversidad humana, cultural y étnica del Ecuador; Diversidad cultural y étnica del Ecuador; Acciones de cooperación y el trabajo solidario; Actividades culturales de la parroquia y del cantón; Patrimonio natural y cultural; Susana Reyes, la danza como signo de identidad; Álbum de las festividades cívicas culturales del Ecuador; Leyenda de las pirámides de Cochasquí (María Eugenia Paz y Miño, plot summary only); La Princesa Súa (tradición oral, from Arte oral del Ecuador, Ministerio de Cultura 2009; plot summary only).

**2.** (Sub lesson 12) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Ecuador?
**Answer:** Because heritage is what a people hands down and must protect: the minga's communal work, randi-randi reciprocity, the festivals, dances and dishes of each town, Quito and Cuenca, Galápagos and Sangay, the toquilla hat and marimba music — treasures of the whole world — and the old legends, like La Dama Tapada, La Princesa Súa and the pyramids of Cochasquí, still retold today. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that story.
