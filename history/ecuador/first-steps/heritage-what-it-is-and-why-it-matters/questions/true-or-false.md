# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Indigenous ancestors hand down the minga, communal work that weaves the village together, and their voices still guide the community from the past.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Quito, declared World Heritage in 1978 for its historic value, Cuenca, Galápagos, Sangay, the toquilla hat and marimba music are treasures belonging to the whole world that children must protect.
**Answer: True**
