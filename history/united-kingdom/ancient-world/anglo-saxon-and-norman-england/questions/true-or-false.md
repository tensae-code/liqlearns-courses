# Anglo-Saxon and Norman England — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     In one generation, conquest remade England: a disputed succession brought invasion in 1066, and William rebuilt land, law, church and government around Norman power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In one generation, conquest remade England: a disputed succession brought invasion in 1066, and William rebuilt land, law, church and government around Norman power.
