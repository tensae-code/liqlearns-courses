# Anglo-Saxon and Norman England — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** In one generation, conquest remade England: a disputed succession brought invasion in 1066, and William rebuilt land, law, church and government around Norman power.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** The Domesday survey of 1085-86 recorded every landholder's wealth, so the new king knew exactly what his conquest had given him.
