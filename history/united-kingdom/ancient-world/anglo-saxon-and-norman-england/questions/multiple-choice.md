# Anglo-Saxon and Norman England — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) In one generation, conquest remade England: a disputed succession brought invasion in 1066, and William rebuilt land, law, church and government around Norman power.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) In one generation, conquest remade England: a disputed succession brought invasion in 1066, and William rebuilt land, law, church and government around Norman power.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) The Domesday survey of 1085-86 recorded every landholder's wealth, so the new king knew exactly what his conquest had given him.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) The Domesday survey of 1085-86 recorded every landholder's wealth, so the new king knew exactly what his conquest had given him.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
