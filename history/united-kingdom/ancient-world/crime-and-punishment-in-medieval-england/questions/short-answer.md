# Crime and Punishment in Medieval England — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Medieval justice was shared between local communities, the king and the Church, and royal power slowly pulled more of it to the centre.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Henry II sent royal justices on circuit, yet manor courts and local punishments carried on underneath the new royal system.
