# Hands-on — Crime and Punishment in Medieval England

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the United Kingdom that connects to this lesson's story: Medieval justice was shared between local communities, the king and the Church, and royal power slowly pulled more of it to the centre. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Medieval justice was shared between local communities, the king and the Church, and royal power slowly pulled more of it to the centre. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
