# History — United Kingdom course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is the United
Kingdom's own — built from its Edexcel GCSE (9-1) History
student books (Pearson Education Limited and Hodder Education,
English, 2016). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside Levels
1–4, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step.
The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the historian's craft
first, stocked from all four books' method spreads; then
England's own story — conquest, and a thousand years of crime
and punishment ending in Whitechapel; then the two depth
studies of other countries' revolutions and dictatorships as
British learners study them — Russia 1917–1941 and Germany
1918–39. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — First Steps: The Historian's Craft**:

- `first-steps/` — What History Is · Dating the Past · From Evidence to a Written History · Historiography: The History of History Writing.

**Level 2 — England's Story: Conquest, Crime and Punishment**:

- `ancient-world/` — Anglo-Saxon and Norman England · Crime and Punishment in Medieval England.
- `modern-world/` — Crime and Punishment in Early Modern England · Crime and Punishment, 1700 to 1900 · Crime and Punishment Since 1900 · Whitechapel: Policing and the Ripper Murders.

**Level 3 — Russia and the Soviet Union: Revolution to Terror**:

- `modern-world/` — 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia · The Bolsheviks in Power, 1917 to 1924 · Stalin Seizes Power and Rules by Terror · Collectivisation and the Five-Year Plans · Life in Stalin's Soviet Union.

**Level 4 — Germany: Weimar, Hitler and the Nazi State**:

- `modern-world/` — Weimar Germany: Origins and Early Challenges · The First World War: Versailles and the Results · Weimar Recovery and the Golden Years · Hitler's Rise to Power, 1919 to 1933 · The Great Depression, 1929 · Nazi Dictatorship, 1933 to 1934 · Nazi Control, Propaganda and Opposition · Life in Nazi Germany, 1933 to 1939 · Nazi Persecution of Minorities.
