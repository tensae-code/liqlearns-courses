# Crime and Punishment, 1700 to 1900 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** As trade and cities grew, so did organised crime, and Britain answered first with a harsher death penalty, then with police, prisons and reform.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Robert Peel founded the Metropolitan Police in 1829, and forces modelled on it spread across the country.
