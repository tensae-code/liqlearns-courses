# Crime and Punishment, 1700 to 1900 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     As trade and cities grew, so did organised crime, and Britain answered first with a harsher death penalty, then with police, prisons and reform.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: As trade and cities grew, so did organised crime, and Britain answered first with a harsher death penalty, then with police, prisons and reform.
