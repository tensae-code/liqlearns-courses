# Crime and Punishment, 1700 to 1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) As trade and cities grew, so did organised crime, and Britain answered first with a harsher death penalty, then with police, prisons and reform.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) As trade and cities grew, so did organised crime, and Britain answered first with a harsher death penalty, then with police, prisons and reform.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Robert Peel founded the Metropolitan Police in 1829, and forces modelled on it spread across the country.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Robert Peel founded the Metropolitan Police in 1829, and forces modelled on it spread across the country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
