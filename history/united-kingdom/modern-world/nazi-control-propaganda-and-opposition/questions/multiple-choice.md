Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The regime watched Germany through a police state, licensed its courts, churches, arts and sport, and filled every home with one message, yet small oppositions still refused.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The regime watched Germany through a police state, licensed its courts, churches, arts and sport, and filled every home with one message, yet small oppositions still refused.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?

**Answer: b) Goebbels' ministry licensed every journalist, actor and musician, and cheap radios carried the one message into every home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
