# Nazi Control, Propaganda and Opposition — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The regime watched Germany through a police state, licensed its courts, churches, arts and sport, and filled every home with one message, yet small oppositions still refused.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The regime watched Germany through a police state, licensed its courts, churches, arts and sport, and filled every home with one message, yet small oppositions still refused.
