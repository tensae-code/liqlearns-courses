# Crime and Punishment Since 1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Since 1900, changing attitudes and new technology have kept redefining crime itself, while punishment moved from execution toward rehabilitation.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Since 1900, changing attitudes and new technology have kept redefining crime itself, while punishment moved from execution toward rehabilitation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Conscription in 1916 made refusing to fight a crime for the first time, creating the conscientious objectors as a new kind of offender.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Conscription in 1916 made refusing to fight a crime for the first time, creating the conscientious objectors as a new kind of offender.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
