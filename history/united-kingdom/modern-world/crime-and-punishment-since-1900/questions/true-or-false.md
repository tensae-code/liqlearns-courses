# Crime and Punishment Since 1900 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Since 1900, changing attitudes and new technology have kept redefining crime itself, while punishment moved from execution toward rehabilitation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Since 1900, changing attitudes and new technology have kept redefining crime itself, while punishment moved from execution toward rehabilitation.
