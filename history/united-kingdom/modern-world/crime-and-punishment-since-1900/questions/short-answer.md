# Crime and Punishment Since 1900 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Since 1900, changing attitudes and new technology have kept redefining crime itself, while punishment moved from execution toward rehabilitation.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Conscription in 1916 made refusing to fight a crime for the first time, creating the conscientious objectors as a new kind of offender.
