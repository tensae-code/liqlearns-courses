# Hands-on — Weimar Recovery and the Golden Years

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the United Kingdom that connects to this lesson's story: From 1924 Stresemann steadied Germany at home and abroad, and cities enjoyed golden years, but the recovery rested on borrowed American money and left many Germans out. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From 1924 Stresemann steadied Germany at home and abroad, and cities enjoyed golden years, but the recovery rested on borrowed American money and left many Germans out. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
