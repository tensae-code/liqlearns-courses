# Weimar Recovery and the Golden Years — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** From 1924 Stresemann steadied Germany at home and abroad, and cities enjoyed golden years, but the recovery rested on borrowed American money and left many Germans out.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Stresemann died in October 1929, weeks before the Crash removed the loans his recovery depended on.
