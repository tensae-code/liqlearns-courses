# Weimar Recovery and the Golden Years — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     From 1924 Stresemann steadied Germany at home and abroad, and cities enjoyed golden years, but the recovery rested on borrowed American money and left many Germans out.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: From 1924 Stresemann steadied Germany at home and abroad, and cities enjoyed golden years, but the recovery rested on borrowed American money and left many Germans out.
