# Weimar Recovery and the Golden Years — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) From 1924 Stresemann steadied Germany at home and abroad, and cities enjoyed golden years, but the recovery rested on borrowed American money and left many Germans out.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) From 1924 Stresemann steadied Germany at home and abroad, and cities enjoyed golden years, but the recovery rested on borrowed American money and left many Germans out.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Stresemann died in October 1929, weeks before the Crash removed the loans his recovery depended on.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Stresemann died in October 1929, weeks before the Crash removed the loans his recovery depended on.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
