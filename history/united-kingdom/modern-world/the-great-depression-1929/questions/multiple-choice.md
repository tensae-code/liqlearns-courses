# The Great Depression, 1929 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) The Wall Street Crash pulled American loans out of Germany, factories closed, and mass unemployment drove desperate voters toward the extremes.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) The Wall Street Crash pulled American loans out of Germany, factories closed, and mass unemployment drove desperate voters toward the extremes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.**   Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) By 1932 about six million Germans were unemployed, and soup kitchens and despair recruited for the extremists.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) By 1932 about six million Germans were unemployed, and soup kitchens and despair recruited for the extremists.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
