# Video script — The Great Depression, 1929

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The Wall Street Crash pulled American loans out of Germany, factories closed, and mass unemployment drove desperate voters toward the extremes.

The Wall Street Crash of 1929 reached Germany through its loans. American money was called home, factories closed in waves, and unemployment climbed to about six million by 1932. Soup kitchens fed the jobless, families lost homes and hope together, and despair did the extremists' recruiting for them: every queue outside a labour exchange was an audience for anyone promising that the system itself was the enemy.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the United Kingdom.
