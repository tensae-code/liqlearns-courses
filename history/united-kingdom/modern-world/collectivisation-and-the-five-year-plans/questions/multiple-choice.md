# Collectivisation and the Five-Year Plans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Stalin forced farming into collective farms and drove industry with Five-Year Plans, industrialising a backward country at brutal human cost.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Stalin forced farming into collective farms and drove industry with Five-Year Plans, industrialising a backward country at brutal human cost.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) While grain was seized and exported, Ukraine and other regions starved under collectivisation.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) While grain was seized and exported, Ukraine and other regions starved under collectivisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
