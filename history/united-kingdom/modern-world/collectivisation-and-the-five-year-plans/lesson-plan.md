# Lesson plan — Collectivisation and the Five-Year Plans

**Course:** History — United Kingdom · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Collectivisation and the Five-Year Plans
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Russia and the Soviet Union: Revolution to Terror (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Stalin forced farming into collective farms and drove industry with Five-Year Plans, industrialising a backward country at brutal human cost.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **4.1 Agriculture and collectivisation** — peasants are forced into collective farms, kulaks liquidated and grain seized, while regions starve and the state exports food.
2. **4.2 Changes in industry** — the Plans set heroic targets for coal, steel and tractors, and a backward country industrialises at breakneck, brutal speed.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Stalin forced farming into collective farms and drove industry with Five-Year Plans, industrialising a backward country at brutal human cost.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
