# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Russia had two revolutions in 1917: hunger and war toppled the Tsar in February, and a weak provisional government let the Bolsheviks seize power in October.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Russia had two revolutions in 1917: hunger and war toppled the Tsar in February, and a weak provisional government let the Bolsheviks seize power in October.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Petrograd's soldiers refused to fire on the crowds, and a dynasty of 300 years fell with nobody having planned its fall.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Petrograd's soldiers refused to fire on the crowds, and a dynasty of 300 years fell with nobody having planned its fall.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
