# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Russia had two revolutions in 1917: hunger and war toppled the Tsar in February, and a weak provisional government let the Bolsheviks seize power in October.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Petrograd's soldiers refused to fire on the crowds, and a dynasty of 300 years fell with nobody having planned its fall.
