# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Russia had two revolutions in 1917: hunger and war toppled the Tsar in February, and a weak provisional government let the Bolsheviks seize power in October.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Russia had two revolutions in 1917: hunger and war toppled the Tsar in February, and a weak provisional government let the Bolsheviks seize power in October.
