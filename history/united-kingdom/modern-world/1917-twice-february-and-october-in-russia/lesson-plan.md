# Lesson plan — 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia

**Course:** History — United Kingdom · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Russia and the Soviet Union: Revolution to Terror (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Russia had two revolutions in 1917: hunger and war toppled the Tsar in February, and a weak provisional government let the Bolsheviks seize power in October.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Russia and the Soviet Union: Revolution to Terror (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1.1 Russia in early 1917** — a peasant empire broken by war: wages double, food prices quadruple, and towns go hungry.
2. **1.2 The February Revolution** — Petrograd crowds, soldiers who will not fire and the Duma bring down Nicholas II, unplanned.
3. **1.3 The Provisional Government** — Kerensky's government shares power with the soviets, stays in the war and delays land reform.
4. **1.4 The Bolshevik Revolution** — Lenin's leadership, Trotsky's organisation and 'Bread, Peace, Land' meet a government nobody will defend.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Russia had two revolutions in 1917: hunger and war toppled the Tsar in February, and a weak provisional government let the Bolsheviks seize power in October.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: Petrograd's soldiers refused to fire on the crowds, and a dynasty of 300 years fell with nobody having planned its fall.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

- Go back to the one thing: Russia had two revolutions in 1917: hunger and war toppled the Tsar in February, and a weak provisional government let the Bolsheviks seize power in October. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
