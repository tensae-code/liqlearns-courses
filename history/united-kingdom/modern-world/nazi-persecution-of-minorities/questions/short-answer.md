# Nazi Persecution of Minorities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Nazi racial doctrine ranked peoples on a ladder and drove Jews and other minorities out of German life step by legal step, from boycott to Nuremberg Laws to Kristallnacht.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** The Nuremberg Laws of 1935 stripped Jews of citizenship, turning prejudice into the law of the state.
