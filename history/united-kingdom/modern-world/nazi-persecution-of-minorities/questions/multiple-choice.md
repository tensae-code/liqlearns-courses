# Nazi Persecution of Minorities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Nazi racial doctrine ranked peoples on a ladder and drove Jews and other minorities out of German life step by legal step, from boycott to Nuremberg Laws to Kristallnacht.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Nazi racial doctrine ranked peoples on a ladder and drove Jews and other minorities out of German life step by legal step, from boycott to Nuremberg Laws to Kristallnacht.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) The Nuremberg Laws of 1935 stripped Jews of citizenship, turning prejudice into the law of the state.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) The Nuremberg Laws of 1935 stripped Jews of citizenship, turning prejudice into the law of the state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
