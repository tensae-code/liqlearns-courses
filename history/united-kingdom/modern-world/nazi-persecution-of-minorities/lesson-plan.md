# Lesson plan — Nazi Persecution of Minorities

**Course:** History — United Kingdom · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Nazi Persecution of Minorities
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Germany: Weimar, Hitler and the Nazi State (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Nazi racial doctrine ranked peoples on a ladder and drove Jews and other minorities out of German life step by legal step, from boycott to Nuremberg Laws to Kristallnacht.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Germany: Weimar, Hitler and the Nazi State (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **13.1 Nazi racial beliefs** — a ladder of races with 'Aryans' on top and Jews at the bottom turns prejudice into state doctrine.
2. **13.2 The persecution of the Jews** — boycott in 1933, the Nuremberg Laws in 1935 and Kristallnacht in 1938 drive Jews from German life step by legal step.
3. **13.3 The treatment of minorities** — Roma, gay men, disabled people and the 'asocial' are registered, sterilised, camped or killed as burdens on the community.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Nazi racial doctrine ranked peoples on a ladder and drove Jews and other minorities out of German life step by legal step, from boycott to Nuremberg Laws to Kristallnacht.
2. Give one real example of it from the lesson: The Nuremberg Laws of 1935 stripped Jews of citizenship, turning prejudice into the law of the state.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
