# Lesson plan — Weimar Germany: Origins and Early Challenges

**Course:** History — United Kingdom · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Weimar Germany: Origins and Early Challenges
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Germany: Weimar, Hitler and the Nazi State (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Weimar Republic was born from defeat in 1918, gave Germany a highly democratic constitution, and was attacked from left and right from its first years.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Germany: Weimar, Hitler and the Nazi State (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1.1 The legacy of the First World War** — the Kaiser abdicates, revolution spreads from the Kiel mutiny, and the republic is born from defeat.
2. **1.2 The setting up of the Weimar Republic** — the 1919 constitution gives women the vote and a democratic frame, yet plants instability in PR and Article 48.
3. **2.2 Challenges to the Republic from the left and right** — Spartacists are crushed by the Freikorps; the Kapp Putsch from the right is stopped by a general strike.
4. **2.3 The challenges of 1923** — France occupies the Ruhr, passive resistance shuts the mines, and hyperinflation wipes out savings until Stresemann acts.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
