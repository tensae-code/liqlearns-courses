# Life in Nazi Germany, 1933 to 1939 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The regime remade daily life: women pressed toward home and children, schools and youth Nazified, and full employment bought with rearmament, frozen wages and longer hours.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The regime remade daily life: women pressed toward home and children, schools and youth Nazified, and full employment bought with rearmament, frozen wages and longer hours.
