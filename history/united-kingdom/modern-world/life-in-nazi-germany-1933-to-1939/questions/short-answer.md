# Life in Nazi Germany, 1933 to 1939 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The regime remade daily life: women pressed toward home and children, schools and youth Nazified, and full employment bought with rearmament, frozen wages and longer hours.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Seven million boys drilled in the Hitler Youth for soldiering while girls of the League of German Maidens trained for motherhood.
