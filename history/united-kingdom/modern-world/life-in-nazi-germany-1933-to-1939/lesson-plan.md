## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **10.1 The Nazi view of the role of women** — Kinder, Küche, Kirche: the ideal woman marries early, bears many children and leaves work and politics to men.
2. **10.2 The changing role of women** — marriage loans and the Mother's Cross push the birth rate up, yet labour shortage draws women back into work.
3. **11.1 Control of the young through education** — schools are Nazified: race science, loaded maths problems, elite Napolas and Adolf Hitler Schools, teachers sworn to the regime.
4. **11.2 The Hitler Youth** — rival youth groups are banned, membership becomes compulsory, and seven million boys and girls are trained for soldiering and motherhood.
5. **12.1 Policies to reduce unemployment** — the Labour Service, autobahns and rearmament erase the six million unemployed, with Jews and women struck off the count.
6. **12.2 Changes in the standard of living** — Strength through Joy holidays and the promised Volkswagen sweeten workers' lives while wages are frozen and hours lengthen.
