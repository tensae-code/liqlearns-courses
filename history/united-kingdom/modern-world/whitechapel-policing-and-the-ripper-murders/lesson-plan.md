## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **5.1 Context: policing the nation** — by the 1870s many separate county and borough forces police Britain; only London answers to the Home Secretary.
2. **5.2 The local context of Whitechapel** — 30,000 people live with overcrowding, workhouses and gang-ruled streets; poverty breeds the district's crime.
3. **5.3 Tensions in Whitechapel** — Irish and Jewish immigrants crowd into the East End; friction and fear of anarchism strain the district.
4. **5.4 Police organisation in Whitechapel** — H Division's roughly 500 constables and 15 detectives patrol set beats under sergeants and inspectors.
5. **5.5 Investigative policing in Whitechapel** — five women are murdered in 1888; press pressure, police rivalry and primitive methods fail to catch the Ripper.
