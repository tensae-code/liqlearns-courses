# Whitechapel: Policing and the Ripper Murders — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Whitechapel in 1888 shows policing at its limit: a poor, crowded and tense district, thinly patrolled, where an uncaught murderer exposed how investigation worked.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Whitechapel in 1888 shows policing at its limit: a poor, crowded and tense district, thinly patrolled, where an uncaught murderer exposed how investigation worked.
