Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Whitechapel in 1888 shows policing at its limit: a poor, crowded and tense district, thinly patrolled, where an uncaught murderer exposed how investigation worked.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
