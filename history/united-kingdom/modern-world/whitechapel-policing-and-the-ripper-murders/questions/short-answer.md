# Whitechapel: Policing and the Ripper Murders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Whitechapel in 1888 shows policing at its limit: a poor, crowded and tense district, thinly patrolled, where an uncaught murderer exposed how investigation worked.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** H Division fielded roughly 500 beat constables and only about 15 detectives for the whole district.
