# The First World War: Versailles and the Results

## Do it first

Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the United Kingdom that connects to this lesson's story: The Versailles settlement punished Germany with lost land, colonies and army and heavy reparations, and many Germans blamed the young republic for a peace it had not chosen. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

The results of the war were written at Versailles: Germany lost land, all its colonies and most of its army, and was fixed with reparations. The settlement's deepest damage was political. Germans branded the politicians who signed as the 'November Criminals', and the young republic was hated for a peace it did not choose and could not have refused. That hatred is the ground the rest of the Germany story grows in.
