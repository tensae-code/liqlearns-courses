# Video script — The First World War: Versailles and the Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The Versailles settlement punished Germany with lost land, colonies and army and heavy reparations, and many Germans blamed the young republic for a peace it had not chosen.

The results of the war were written at Versailles: Germany lost land, all its colonies and most of its army, and was fixed with reparations. The settlement's deepest damage was political. Germans branded the politicians who signed as the 'November Criminals', and the young republic was hated for a peace it did not choose and could not have refused. That hatred is the ground the rest of the Germany story grows in.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the United Kingdom.
