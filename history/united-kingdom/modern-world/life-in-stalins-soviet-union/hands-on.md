# Hands-on — Life in Stalin's Soviet Union

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the United Kingdom that connects to this lesson's story: Everyday Soviet life in the 1930s mixed real gains in schooling, health and work with queues, crowded flats and the fear of a knock at night. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Everyday Soviet life in the 1930s mixed real gains in schooling, health and work with queues, crowded flats and the fear of a knock at night. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
