Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Everyday Soviet life in the 1930s mixed real gains in schooling, health and work with queues, crowded flats and the fear of a knock at night.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
