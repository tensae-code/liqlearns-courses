# Nazi Dictatorship, 1933 to 1934 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Within eighteen months Hitler turned a chancellorship into a complete dictatorship: emergency decree, Enabling Act, opposition removed, the SA purged, and the army's oath to him personally.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** With communist deputies arrested and the SA lining the chamber, the Reichstag voted itself out of power in March 1933.
