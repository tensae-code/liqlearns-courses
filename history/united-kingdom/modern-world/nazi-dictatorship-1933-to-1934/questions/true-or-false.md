# Nazi Dictatorship, 1933 to 1934 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Within eighteen months Hitler turned a chancellorship into a complete dictatorship: emergency decree, Enabling Act, opposition removed, the SA purged, and the army's oath to him personally.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Within eighteen months Hitler turned a chancellorship into a complete dictatorship: emergency decree, Enabling Act, opposition removed, the SA purged, and the army's oath to him personally.
