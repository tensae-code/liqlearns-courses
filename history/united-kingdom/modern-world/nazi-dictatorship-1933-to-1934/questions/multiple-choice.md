# Nazi Dictatorship, 1933 to 1934 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Within eighteen months Hitler turned a chancellorship into a complete dictatorship: emergency decree, Enabling Act, opposition removed, the SA purged, and the army's oath to him personally.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Within eighteen months Hitler turned a chancellorship into a complete dictatorship: emergency decree, Enabling Act, opposition removed, the SA purged, and the army's oath to him personally.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
