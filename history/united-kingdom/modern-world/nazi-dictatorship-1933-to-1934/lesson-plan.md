# Lesson plan — Nazi Dictatorship, 1933 to 1934

**Course:** History — United Kingdom · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Nazi Dictatorship, 1933 to 1934

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **7.1 The importance of the Reichstag fire** — the Reichstag burns on 27 February 1933; an emergency decree suspends civil rights and thousands are arrested.
2. **7.2 The importance of the Enabling Act** — with deputies arrested and the SA lining the chamber, the Reichstag votes itself out of power in March 1933.
3. **7.3 The removal of opposition** — Gleichschaltung brings states, unions and parties into line; by July 1933 the Nazi Party is the only organisation left standing.
4. **7.4 The importance of the Night of the Long Knives** — on 30 June 1934 Hitler purges Röhm and the SA leadership to buy the army's loyalty.
5. **7.5 The support of the army** — Hindenburg dies in August 1934; the army swears its oath to Hitler personally, and he becomes Führer.
