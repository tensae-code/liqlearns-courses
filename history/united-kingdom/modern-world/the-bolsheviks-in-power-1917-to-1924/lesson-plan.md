## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **2.1 Early consolidation of power, 1917–18** — Brest-Litovsk signs away land for peace; the Assembly is dispersed and the Romanovs murdered.
2. **2.2 The Civil War, 1918–21** — Reds defeat Whites, Greens and foreign armies; war communism and famine follow, and unity and railways win through.
3. **2.3 Moves towards totalitarianism** — rival newspapers are banned within weeks and the Cheka created; terror makes a one-party dictatorship.
4. **2.4 Economic and social change, 1918–24** — war communism collapses into the NEP retreat, while society is remade: women equal, schooling free, abortion legal, Church land taken.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
