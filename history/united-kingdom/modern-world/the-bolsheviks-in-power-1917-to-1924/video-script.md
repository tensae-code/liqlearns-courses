# Video script — The Bolsheviks in Power, 1917 to 1924

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The Bolsheviks kept power through peace at a price, civil war and terror, then retreated on the economy while remaking society.

To keep the promise of peace, Trotsky signed the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk, giving away great stretches of land to Germany. At home, the newly elected Constituent Assembly met for one day and was dispersed, and the Romanov family was murdered in a cellar. Within months, the revolution that promised all power to the soviets was ruling by decree and by force.
From 1918 to 1921 the Reds fought the Whites, peasant Green armies and foreign intervention forces on every frontier. War communism requisitioned grain at gunpoint, and famine followed. The Reds won because they were united under one command, held the centre of Russia and its railway hub, and faced enemies who never combined against them at the same time.
Within weeks of October, the new rulers banned rival newspapers and created the Cheka, the secret police. Show trials and terror followed as instruments of government, not emergencies. Step by step, a revolutionary government that had promised the freest state in history turned itself into a one-party dictatorship, and the machinery of that turn outlived the emergency that justified it.
War communism collapsed the economy, and Lenin retreated: the NEP let peasants sell surplus produce and small traders trade again. Society, though, kept being remade at full speed: women were declared equal in 1917, education became free and compulsory, abortion was legalised in 1920, and the Church's land was nationalised. Retreat in economics, revolution in everyday life.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in the United Kingdom.
