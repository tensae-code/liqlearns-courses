# Crime and Punishment in Early Modern England — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Between 1500 and 1700, religious division and growing towns created new crimes and new ways of policing and punishing them.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Give one real example of it from the lesson.
**Answer:** Matthew Hopkins hunted witches in the 1640s, yet within two generations witchcraft had stopped being prosecuted as a serious crime.
