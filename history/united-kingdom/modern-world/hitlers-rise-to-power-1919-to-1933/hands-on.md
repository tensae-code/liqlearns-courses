# Hands-on — Hitler's Rise to Power, 1919 to 1933

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one place, person or event from your own life in the United Kingdom that connects to this lesson's story: Hitler built a fringe party into a national movement, failed in a putsch, waited through lean years, and was handed power in January 1933 after depression and backstairs intrigue. Write what the connection is, and how you could check it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Hitler built a fringe party into a national movement, failed in a putsch, waited through lean years, and was handed power in January 1933 after depression and backstairs intrigue. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
