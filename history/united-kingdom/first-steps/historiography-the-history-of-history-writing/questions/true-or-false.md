# Historiography: The History of History Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Historians can study the same events and reach different interpretations, because they select different evidence and write from different positions and times.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Historians can study the same events and reach different interpretations, because they select different evidence and write from different positions and times.
