# Historiography: The History of History Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) Historians can study the same events and reach different interpretations, because they select different evidence and write from different positions and times.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) Historians can study the same events and reach different interpretations, because they select different evidence and write from different positions and times.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
