# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) History studies change over time: what changed, what stayed the same, and what turned a society in a new direction.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) History studies change over time: what changed, what stayed the same, and what turned a society in a new direction.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) Britain's first mobile phone call in 1985 shows the pattern: new technology and new attitudes together can turn everyday life.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) Britain's first mobile phone call in 1985 shows the pattern: new technology and new attitudes together can turn everyday life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
