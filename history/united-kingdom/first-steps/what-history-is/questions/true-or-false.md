# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     History studies change over time: what changed, what stayed the same, and what turned a society in a new direction.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     This lesson teaches that its story changed nothing and matters to no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: History studies change over time: what changed, what stayed the same, and what turned a society in a new direction.
