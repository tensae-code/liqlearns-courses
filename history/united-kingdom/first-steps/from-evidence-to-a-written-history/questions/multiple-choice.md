# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Which statement best tells this lesson's story?
a) A written history is built from evidence: you weigh sources and interpretations, then shape them into a focused answer.  b) The past is only a list of dates to memorise, with nothing to learn from it  c) This story happened somewhere else and has nothing to do with the lesson  d) Nothing in this lesson ever changed anything
**Answer: a) A written history is built from evidence: you weigh sources and interpretations, then shape them into a focused answer.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     Which of these is a real example from this lesson?
a) A story invented for this question, found nowhere in the lesson  b) An annotated sample answer at two levels shows a learner exactly how the same evidence becomes a stronger piece of writing.  c) An event from another country that the lesson never mentions  d) A date with no story attached to it
**Answer: b) An annotated sample answer at two levels shows a learner exactly how the same evidence becomes a stronger piece of writing.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
