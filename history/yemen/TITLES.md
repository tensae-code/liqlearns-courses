# History — Yemen course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Yemen's history belongs to Yemen: this course is Yemen's own — built from Yemen's Social Studies and History student books (Arabic). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the family and lived time first, the celebrated days, then the prophets and ancient Yemen, Islam and Yemen's Islamic states, the deepened ancient story, the modern world and foreign rule, the ancient world whole, and the Arab Islamic civilisation with the modern world to today. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
