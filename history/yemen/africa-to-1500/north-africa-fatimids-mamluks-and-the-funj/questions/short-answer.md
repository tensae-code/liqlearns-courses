# North Africa: Fatimids, Mamluks and the Funj — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “In the Fatimid state era”.
**Answer:** a caliphate claiming descent from Fatima rises in the Maghreb in 297 AH, builds Mahdia, takes Egypt, founds Cairo and al-Azhar, raises its own Dar al-Hikma and a shipyard that builds some 600 vessels, and counts Yemen's Sulayhid state among the wilayas loyal to it — and ends in 567 AH at Salah al-Din's hand
