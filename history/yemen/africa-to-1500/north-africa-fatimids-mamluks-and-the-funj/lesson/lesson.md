## In the Fatimid state era

a caliphate claiming descent from Fatima rises in the Maghreb in 297 AH, builds Mahdia, takes Egypt, founds Cairo and al-Azhar, raises its own Dar al-Hikma and a shipyard that builds some 600 vessels, and counts Yemen's Sulayhid state among the wilayas loyal to it — and ends in 567 AH at Salah al-Din's hand
