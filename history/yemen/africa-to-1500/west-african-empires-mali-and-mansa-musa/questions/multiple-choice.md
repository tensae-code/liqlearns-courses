# West African Empires: Mali and Mansa Musa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Islamic civilisation in Central and West Africa: the empire of Mali (Unit 5 Lesson 5)”?
a) When a chief embraces Islam his tribe follows and carries it to new lands  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) When a chief embraces Islam his tribe follows and carries it to new lands** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Islamic civilisation in Central and West Africa: the empire of Mali (Unit 5 Lesson 5)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) When a chief embraces Islam his tribe follows and carries it to new lands  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) When a chief embraces Islam his tribe follows and carries it to new lands** — That is what this lesson teaches.
