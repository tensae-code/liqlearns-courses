# Mamluk Egypt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “In the Mamluk era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, second half)”?
a) The slave-soldiers the Ayyubids bought kill the last Ayyubid sultan and rule themselves from 648 to 922 AH, Bahri then Burji  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The slave-soldiers the Ayyubids bought kill the last Ayyubid sultan and rule themselves from 648 to 922 AH, Bahri then Burji** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “In the Mamluk era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, second half)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The slave-soldiers the Ayyubids bought kill the last Ayyubid sultan and rule themselves from 648 to 922 AH, Bahri then Burji  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The slave-soldiers the Ayyubids bought kill the last Ayyubid sultan and rule themselves from 648 to 922 AH, Bahri then Burji** — That is what this lesson teaches.
