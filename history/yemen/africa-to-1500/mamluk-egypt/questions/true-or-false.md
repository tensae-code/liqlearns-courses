# Mamluk Egypt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The slave-soldiers the Ayyubids bought kill the last Ayyubid sultan and rule themselves from 648 to 922 AH, Bahri then Burji.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “In the Mamluk era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, second half)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The slave-soldiers the Ayyubids bought kill the last Ayyubid sultan and rule themselves from 648 to 922 AH, Bahri then Burji.
