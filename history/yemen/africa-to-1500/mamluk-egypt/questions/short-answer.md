# Mamluk Egypt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “In the Mamluk era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, second half)”.
**Answer:** the slave-soldiers the Ayyubids bought kill the last Ayyubid sultan and rule themselves from 648 to 922 AH, Bahri then Burji; they stop the Tatars at Ayn Jalut, revive the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo with its power cut down to blessing sultans, appoint four chief judges, host the scholar-migration that lets Ibn al-Nafis find the lesser circulation of the blood, and exchange fine antiquities even with Yemen's Rasulid kings — until the Ottoman Selim I ends their state
