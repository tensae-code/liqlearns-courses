# Mamluk Egypt

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: In the Mamluk era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, second half). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## In the Mamluk era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, second half)

the slave-soldiers the Ayyubids bought kill the last Ayyubid sultan and rule themselves from 648 to 922 AH, Bahri then Burji; they stop the Tatars at Ayn Jalut, revive the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo with its power cut down to blessing sultans, appoint four chief judges, host the scholar-migration that lets Ibn al-Nafis find the lesser circulation of the blood, and exchange fine antiquities even with Yemen's Rasulid kings — until the Ottoman Selim I ends their state
