# Carthage and the Punic Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Phoenicians from Tyre found Carthage in 814 BCE on a peninsula near today's Tunis.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Maghreb (Carthage)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Phoenicians from Tyre found Carthage in 814 BCE on a peninsula near today's Tunis.
