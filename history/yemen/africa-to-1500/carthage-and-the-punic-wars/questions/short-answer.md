# Carthage and the Punic Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Maghreb (Carthage)”.
**Answer:** Phoenicians from Tyre found Carthage in 814 BCE on a peninsula near today's Tunis; power slides from the king to a Council of Elders of over three hundred and two elected judges, trade becomes the state's backbone — grain, olive oil and salted meat carried by a fleet no rival matches — Ba'l Hammon is worshipped, and Hannibal rises as the city's most famous commander against Rome
