# Carthage and the Punic Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Maghreb (Carthage)”?
a) Phoenicians from Tyre found Carthage in 814 BCE on a peninsula near today's Tunis  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Phoenicians from Tyre found Carthage in 814 BCE on a peninsula near today's Tunis** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Maghreb (Carthage)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Phoenicians from Tyre found Carthage in 814 BCE on a peninsula near today's Tunis  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Phoenicians from Tyre found Carthage in 814 BCE on a peninsula near today's Tunis** — That is what this lesson teaches.
