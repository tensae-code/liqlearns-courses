## Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Maghreb (Carthage)

Phoenicians from Tyre found Carthage in 814 BCE on a peninsula near today's Tunis; power slides from the king to a Council of Elders of over three hundred and two elected judges, trade becomes the state's backbone — grain, olive oil and salted meat carried by a fleet no rival matches — Ba'l Hammon is worshipped, and Hannibal rises as the city's most famous commander against Rome
