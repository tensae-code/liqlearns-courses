# Hands-on — Idrisid Morocco: Idris ibn Abdallah

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: The Islamic state in the age of the Idrisids in the Maghreb (173-375 AH). Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: after the Abbasid massacre of the Prophet's family at Fakhkh in 169 AH, Idris bin Abd Allah escapes hidden in Mecca, crosses Egypt with his servant Rashid and reaches….

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: after the Abbasid massacre of the Prophet's family at Fakhkh in 169 AH, Idris bin Abd Allah escapes hidden in Mecca, crosses Egypt with his servant Rashid and reaches…. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
