Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Islamic state in the age of the Idrisids in the Maghreb (173-375 AH)”?
a) After the Abbasid massacre of the Prophet's family at Fakhkh in 169 AH, Idris bin Abd Allah escapes hidden in Mecca, crosses Egypt with his servant…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
