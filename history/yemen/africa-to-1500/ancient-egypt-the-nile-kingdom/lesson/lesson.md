## Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Nile Valley

Pharaoh holds absolute religious and worldly power through his vizier and chief scribe, over five classes from court to slaves; the army, rebuilt with iron, horses and chariots after the Hyksos, guards a land whose farmers grow cotton, flax and papyrus, whose doctors specialise in the House of Life, whose astronomers fix the 365¼-day year and predict the flood, and whose builders raise Karnak, Abu Simbel and the three pyramids of Giza — while Akhenaten's lone call to one god, Aton, breaks briefly through a crowd of local gods
