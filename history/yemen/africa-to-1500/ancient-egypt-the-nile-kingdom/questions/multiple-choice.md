# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Nile Valley”?
a) Pharaoh holds absolute religious and worldly power through his vizier and chief scribe, over five classes from court to slaves  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Pharaoh holds absolute religious and worldly power through his vizier and chief scribe, over five classes from court to slaves** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Nile Valley”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Pharaoh holds absolute religious and worldly power through his vizier and chief scribe, over five classes from court to slaves  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Pharaoh holds absolute religious and worldly power through his vizier and chief scribe, over five classes from court to slaves** — That is what this lesson teaches.
