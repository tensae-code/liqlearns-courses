# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pharaoh holds absolute religious and worldly power through his vizier and chief scribe, over five classes from court to slaves.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Manifestations of flourishing in Africa: the Nile Valley” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Pharaoh holds absolute religious and worldly power through his vizier and chief scribe, over five classes from court to slaves.
