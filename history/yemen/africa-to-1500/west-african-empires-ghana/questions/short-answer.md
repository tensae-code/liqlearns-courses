# West African Empires: Ghana — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Islamic civilisation in Central and West Africa: the empire of Ghana (Unit 5 Lesson 5)”.
**Answer:** Muslim merchants, scholars and callers cross the Sahara peacefully by five roads — from the Maghreb, from Egypt by Wadi Dar'a, from Algeria, from Tripoli to Lake Chad, and up the Nile to Darfur — and the pagan Ghana empire between the Senegal and upper Niger falls to the Almoravids in 469 AH
