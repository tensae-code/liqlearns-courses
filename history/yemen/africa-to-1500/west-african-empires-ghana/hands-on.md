# Hands-on — West African Empires: Ghana

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Islamic civilisation in Central and West Africa: the empire of Ghana (Unit 5 Lesson 5). Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: muslim merchants, scholars and callers cross the Sahara peacefully by five roads.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: muslim merchants, scholars and callers cross the Sahara peacefully by five roads. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
