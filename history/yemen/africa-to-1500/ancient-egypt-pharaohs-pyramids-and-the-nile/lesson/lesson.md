## Civilisation of the Nile Valley (3500-350 BCE)

on the Nile's banks Egypt builds through three ages — the Old Kingdom of the pyramid builders at Memphis, the Middle Kingdom's farming prosperity at Thebes after the Hyksos are expelled, and the Empire age of Thutmose III, Tutankhamun and Ramesses II, the Qur'an's Pharaoh as this book identifies him — while Kush in the Sudan raises more pyramids than Egypt, only smaller, before corruption, injustice and foreign invasion bring the valley down
