# Ancient Egypt: Pharaohs, Pyramids and the Nile — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Civilisation of the Nile Valley (3500-350 BCE)”?
a) On the Nile's banks Egypt builds through three ages  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) On the Nile's banks Egypt builds through three ages** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Civilisation of the Nile Valley (3500-350 BCE)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) On the Nile's banks Egypt builds through three ages  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) On the Nile's banks Egypt builds through three ages** — That is what this lesson teaches.
