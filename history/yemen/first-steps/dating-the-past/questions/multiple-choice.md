# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Time”?
a) Time is built up from 24 hours in a day and 7 days in a week to 12 months in a year, and the two year-counts are anchored to two journeys  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Time is built up from 24 hours in a day and 7 days in a week to 12 months in a year, and the two year-counts are anchored to two journeys** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Importance of studying history (terms and timeline)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The lesson arms the student with its clocks  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The lesson arms the student with its clocks** — That is what this lesson teaches.
