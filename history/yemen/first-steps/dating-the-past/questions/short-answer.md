# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Time”.
**Answer:** time is built up from 24 hours in a day and 7 days in a week to 12 months in a year, and the two year-counts are anchored to two journeys — the Hijri year to Muhammad's migration from Mecca to Medina, the Gregorian year to the birth of Jesus

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Importance of studying history (terms and timeline)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** the lesson arms the student with its clocks — prehistory known only by word of mouth, BCE and CE counted from the birth of Jesus, "before the Mission" and the Hijri calendar from the Prophet's migration — and ends with the student drawing a timeline from Jesus' birth through the Mission and the Hijra to the present year
