## Sub lesson 1 — How do you read history? (misreadings)

the lesson names the wrong ways to read the past — blind dazzlement by glittering appearances like today's West, attaching oneself to misguided people, and repeating old mistakes, as when the Israelites' deviation is repeated by those who fight America and Israel without learning from it

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Manifestations of the scientific renaissance: the science of history (Unit 6 Lesson 2, history part)

history grows out of the Prophet's sira, the maghazi, the storytellers and genealogy, through al-Tabari, al-Baladhuri and al-Ya'qubi, until Ibn Khaldun's Muqaddima founds the philosophy of history — the book insists he precedes Spencer's organic reading, Marx's economic reading and Comte's social reading, and that Europe, which then knew only chroniclers, finds in him a Descartes and a Montesquieu three centuries early

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
