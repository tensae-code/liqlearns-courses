# Historiography: The History of History-Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “How do you read history? (misreadings)”?
a) The lesson names the wrong ways to read the past  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The lesson names the wrong ways to read the past** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Manifestations of the scientific renaissance: the science of history (Unit 6 Lesson 2, history part)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) History grows out of the Prophet's sira, the maghazi, the storytellers and genealogy, through al-Tabari, al-Baladhuri and al-Ya'qubi, until Ibn…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) History grows out of the Prophet's sira, the maghazi, the storytellers and genealogy, through al-Tabari, al-Baladhuri and al-Ya'qubi, until Ibn…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
