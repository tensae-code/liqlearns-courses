# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

## Do it first

Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Closing thinking card: protecting the heritage (end matter); Closing heritage card (end matter). ## Sub lesson 1 — Closing thinking card: protecting the heritage (end matter)
