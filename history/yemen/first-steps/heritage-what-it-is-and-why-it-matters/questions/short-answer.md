# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Closing thinking card: protecting the heritage (end matter)”.
**Answer:** the book closes by turning heritage into a duty for every pupil: the Arab Islamic civilisation is a source of inspiration for human civilisation, its sanctities are beacons uniting the umma, its historic cities are priceless treasures and its manuscripts a wealth not to be slighted, there is no development without tourism and no tourism without antiquities, and protecting sanctities, historic cities, manuscripts and antiquities is everyone's duty

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Closing heritage card (end matter)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** the book closes with the same charge as Part 1: the Arab Islamic civilisation is a source of inspiration for human civilisation, its historic cities are priceless treasures and its manuscripts a wealth not to be slighted, there is no tourism without antiquities, and protecting sanctities, historic cities, manuscripts and antiquities is everyone's duty
