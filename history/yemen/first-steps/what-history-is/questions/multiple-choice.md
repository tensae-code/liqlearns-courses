# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “My family and my relatives”?
a) A child counts and names the people who make his family  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A child counts and names the people who make his family** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Importance of studying history”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) History is defined as the science of past events studied for their lessons, heritage is everything the ancients left in buildings, language…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) History is defined as the science of past events studied for their lessons, heritage is everything the ancients left in buildings, language…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
