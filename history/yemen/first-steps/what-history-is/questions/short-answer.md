# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “My family and my relatives”.
**Answer:** a child counts and names the people who make his family — grandfather, grandmother, father, mother, brothers and sisters — and learns uncles and aunts are the wider kin who hold it together

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Importance of studying history”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** history is defined as the science of past events studied for their lessons, heritage is everything the ancients left in buildings, language, knowledge and custom, and the student learns why nations that turn from justice fall, however strong they were
