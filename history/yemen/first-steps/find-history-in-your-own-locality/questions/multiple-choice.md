# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “My family and my relatives (relatives section)”?
a) The child writes the names of his paternal and maternal uncles, aunts and grandfather, and sees the family on Eid gathered with relatives he does not…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The child writes the names of his paternal and maternal uncles, aunts and grandfather, and sees the family on Eid gathered with relatives he does not…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
