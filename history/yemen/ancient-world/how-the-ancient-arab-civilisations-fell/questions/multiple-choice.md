# How the Ancient Arab Civilisations Fell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Causes of the collapse of the ancient Arab civilisations”?
a) One by one the sisters fall the same way  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) One by one the sisters fall the same way** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Causes of the collapse of the ancient Arab civilisations”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) One by one the sisters fall the same way  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) One by one the sisters fall the same way** — That is what this lesson teaches.
