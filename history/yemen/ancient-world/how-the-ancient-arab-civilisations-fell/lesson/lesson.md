# How the Ancient Arab Civilisations Fell

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Causes of the collapse of the ancient Arab civilisations. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Causes of the collapse of the ancient Arab civilisations

one by one the sisters fall the same way — Yemen's farmers turn Bedouin, five kingdoms fight at once, the Ptolemies steal the monsoon secrets and break the incense monopoly, Aelius Gallus is beaten at Marib's walls in 24 BCE but Abyssinia finishes the work in 525 CE after Dhu Nuwas burns Najran's Christians; Babylon falls to Cyrus in 539 BCE, the divided Levant passes from Assyria to Babylon to Persia to Alexander, Egypt is passed hand to hand until Alexander takes it in 332 BCE, and Rome, sworn that Carthage must be destroyed, starves it out in 146 BCE
