# Why Civilisations Rose in the Arab Homeland

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Factors in the rise of the ancient Arab civilisations. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Factors in the rise of the ancient Arab civilisations

one homeland mid-old-world, commanding land and sea trade routes, with varied climate, rivers, rains and fertile soil, lifts six sister civilisations at once: Yemen on its dams, terraces and incense; Mesopotamia between the two rivers; the Levant as bridge of nations; Egypt, the gift of the Nile; the Maghreb facing Europe across the sea; and the Gulf, where Dilmun gathers pearls and trade, Failaka re-exports to Iraq and India, and Magan ships copper and builds boats
