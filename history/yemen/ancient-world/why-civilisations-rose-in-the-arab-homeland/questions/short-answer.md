# Why Civilisations Rose in the Arab Homeland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Factors in the rise of the ancient Arab civilisations”.
**Answer:** one homeland mid-old-world, commanding land and sea trade routes, with varied climate, rivers, rains and fertile soil, lifts six sister civilisations at once: Yemen on its dams, terraces and incense; Mesopotamia between the two rivers; the Levant as bridge of nations; Egypt, the gift of the Nile; the Maghreb facing Europe across the sea; and the Gulf, where Dilmun gathers pearls and trade, Failaka re-exports to Iraq and India, and Magan ships copper and builds boats
