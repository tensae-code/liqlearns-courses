# Why Civilisations Rose in the Arab Homeland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Factors in the rise of the ancient Arab civilisations”?
a) One homeland mid-old-world, commanding land and sea trade routes, with varied climate, rivers, rains and fertile soil, lifts six sister civilisations…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) One homeland mid-old-world, commanding land and sea trade routes, with varied climate, rivers, rains and fertile soil, lifts six sister civilisations…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Factors in the rise of the ancient Arab civilisations”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) One homeland mid-old-world, commanding land and sea trade routes, with varied climate, rivers, rains and fertile soil, lifts six sister civilisations…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) One homeland mid-old-world, commanding land and sea trade routes, with varied climate, rivers, rains and fertile soil, lifts six sister civilisations…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
