# Tahirid Yemen and the Navigators Ibn Majid and al-Mahri — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The brothers ____ and Ali ibn Tahir take Aden from the Rasulids they had served and found Yemen's last medieval state (858 AH) from al-Miqrana.
**Answer:** Amir

**2.** The brothers Amir and ____ ibn Tahir take Aden from the Rasulids they had served and found Yemen's last medieval state (858 AH) from al-Miqrana.
**Answer:** Ali
