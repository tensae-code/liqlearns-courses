# Tahirid Yemen and the Navigators Ibn Majid and al-Mahri — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Yemeni states that contributed: the Tahirid state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)”.
**Answer:** the brothers Amir and Ali ibn Tahir take Aden from the Rasulids they had served and found Yemen's last medieval state (858 AH) from al-Miqrana; their age's glory is the sea — Ahmad ibn Majid writes some forty works on geography and navigation and Sulayman al-Mahri's al-'Umda teaches generations of sailors
