# Roots of Yemeni Civilisation before Writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Roots of Yemeni civilisation before recorded history”?
a) After 'Ad perish, survivors flee drought and creeping desert to the wadis of springs  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) After 'Ad perish, survivors flee drought and creeping desert to the wadis of springs** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Roots of Yemeni civilisation before recorded history”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) After 'Ad perish, survivors flee drought and creeping desert to the wadis of springs  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) After 'Ad perish, survivors flee drought and creeping desert to the wadis of springs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
