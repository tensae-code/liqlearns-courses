# Islam in Sind and India: Traders to Mughals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Islamic civilisation in Sind and India (Unit 5 Lesson 2)”.
**Answer:** Yemeni merchants land first on the Malabar coast and preach by honesty, Muhammad ibn al-Qasim opens Sind in 92 AH, Ghaznavids, Ghurids and Khaljis follow, and under Babur's Mughals the civilisation peaks in stone — Qutb Minar, the Red Fort and Shah Jahan's Taj Mahal — while Islam's equality draws the oppressed out of the caste order
