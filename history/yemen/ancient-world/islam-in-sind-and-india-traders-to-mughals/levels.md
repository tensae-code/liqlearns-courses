# Islam in Sind and India: Traders to Mughals — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — yemeni merchants land first on the Malabar coast and preach by honesty, Muhammad ibn al-Qasim opens Sind in 92 AH, Ghaznavids, Ghurids and Khaljis follow, and under… — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
