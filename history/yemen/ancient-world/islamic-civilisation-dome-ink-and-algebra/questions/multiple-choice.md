# Islamic Civilisation: Dome, Ink and Algebra — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Foundations of Islamic civilisation”?
a) A civilisation is born at Medina on three foundations  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A civilisation is born at Medina on three foundations** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Social and cultural manifestations”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The new society rests on piety, equality and brotherhood and lifts women from their pre-Islamic low place to rights of learning, opinion and…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The new society rests on piety, equality and brotherhood and lifts women from their pre-Islamic low place to rights of learning, opinion and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
