# Islamic Civilisation: Dome, Ink and Algebra — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Foundations of Islamic civilisation”.
**Answer:** a civilisation is born at Medina on three foundations — the creed of Qur'an and Sunna, one Islamic order covering politics, economy and society, and the Arabic language, which carries the Qur'an to the world and absorbs Greek and Indian learning — built by a drive for knowledge, schools and mosques, translation and public libraries

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Social and cultural manifestations”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** the new society rests on piety, equality and brotherhood and lifts women from their pre-Islamic low place to rights of learning, opinion and property, while a Qur'anic culture grows sciences of its own — Arabic grammar, the science of history born from the need to know the Prophet's life, and geography for trade routes, the Hajj and the direction of prayer
