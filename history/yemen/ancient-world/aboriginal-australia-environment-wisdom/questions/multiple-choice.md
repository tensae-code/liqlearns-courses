# Aboriginal Australia Environment Wisdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Ancient civilisations in Australia”?
a) Australia's first peoples, arrived by sea perhaps from South-East Asia by 1600 BCE (as the book dates it), master rock engraving and painting…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
