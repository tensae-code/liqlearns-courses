## Causes of the decline of the scientific renaissance (Unit 6 Lesson 4)

the state weakens and splits — three caliphates at once in Baghdad, Cairo and Cordoba, Turks, Buyids and Seljuks holding Baghdad's caliph hostage before the Mongols take the city in 656 AH; shura stays a moral value and never becomes a binding system, so caliphs stop encouraging scholars; 'asabiyya and sectarian fanaticism set society against itself; ijtihad fades into imitation; and foreign powers harvest the weakness — yet the renaissance does not die outright, because its Prophetic-era foundation was strong
