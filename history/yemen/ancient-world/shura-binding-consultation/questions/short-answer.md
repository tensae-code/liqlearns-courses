# Shura: Binding Consultation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation”.
**Answer:** shura is set against mere consultation: advice a ruler may ignore is istishara, but shura binds; it stands only on its conditions — freedom, bindingness, solidarity, morality, consensus, persuasion, correctness and strength — and is staffed by qualified ahl al-hall wa-l-'aqd representing all society; the book argues binding shura simply is democracy, older than the name by fourteen centuries, and asks pupils to name the councils that embody shura and democracy in the Yemeni Republic and how Yemen can develop its experience of both
