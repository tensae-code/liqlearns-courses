# Ancient Yemen Musnad Writing and Sciences — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Intellectual and scientific manifestations of ancient Yemen”.
**Answer:** the old Yemenis write in the Musnad and Zabur scripts on stone, bronze and wood, keep a library in the Awwam temple at Marib, practise medicine from teeth and eyes to fractures and embalming, read the stars for farming seasons and sailing, count for trade and state, and refine copper and bronze into coins, jewellery, weapons and statues of kings
