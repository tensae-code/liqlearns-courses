# Ijtihad and the Freedoms of Thought and Science — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation”?
a) Ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text** — That is what this lesson teaches.
