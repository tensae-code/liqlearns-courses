# Ijtihad and the Freedoms of Thought and Science — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation”.
**Answer:** ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text — the Prophet approving it in his dialogue with Mu'adh, whom he was sending to Yemen — powers five centuries of scientific renaissance and the experimental method Europe later borrows; the striving mujtahid earns two rewards if right and one if wrong, but when ijtihad weakens and its gate closes in the late Mamluk and Ottoman ages, superstitious thought spreads, Arab Muslim society closes in on itself, and the civilisation's torch dies
