# Asad Al Kamil Tubba Who Clothed the Kaaba — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Abu Karib As'ad, Himyarite king of Saba, Dhu Raydan, Hadramawt and Yamnat.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Abu Karib As'ad, Himyarite king of Saba, Dhu Raydan, Hadramawt and Yamnat.
