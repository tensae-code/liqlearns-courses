# Asad Al Kamil Tubba Who Clothed the Kaaba — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood”?
a) Abu Karib As'ad, Himyarite king of Saba, Dhu Raydan, Hadramawt and Yamnat  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Abu Karib As'ad, Himyarite king of Saba, Dhu Raydan, Hadramawt and Yamnat** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Abu Karib As'ad, Himyarite king of Saba, Dhu Raydan, Hadramawt and Yamnat  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Abu Karib As'ad, Himyarite king of Saba, Dhu Raydan, Hadramawt and Yamnat** — That is what this lesson teaches.
