# Asad Al Kamil Tubba Who Clothed the Kaaba — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood”.
**Answer:** Abu Karib As'ad, Himyarite king of Saba, Dhu Raydan, Hadramawt and Yamnat — adding "and their Bedouin in the highlands and Tihama" to the royal title — rules about 370-440 CE from Zafar, first clothes the Kaaba in a full luxurious covering with a door and a key, unifies all Arabia under one authority, secures the trade roads by campaign, reaches across the Red Sea, and, knowing from the old books that a prophet named Muhammad is coming, believes in him before his birth — "do not curse Tubba', for he was a Muslim" — and settles the Yemeni tribes Aws and Khazraj in Yathrib so that, generations later, they will be there to receive him; he dies on campaign in 440 CE and is buried at Ghayman in Sanaa
