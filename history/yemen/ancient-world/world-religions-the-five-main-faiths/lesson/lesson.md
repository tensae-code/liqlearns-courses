# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Philosophical and religious concepts. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Philosophical and religious concepts

the book teaches that God's religion is one through the ages, brought by every prophet, and that the Torah given to Musa and the Injil given to Isa were later distorted by their keepers — the crucifixion invented, Isa raised to God — while the Qur'an restores the religion of Ibrahim; against it stand the pagan religions: Hinduism with karma and reincarnation, Confucius reforming China's ancestor-and-heaven beliefs, Buddha preaching love and peace yet made a god by followers, and Zarathustra's teaching in Persia
