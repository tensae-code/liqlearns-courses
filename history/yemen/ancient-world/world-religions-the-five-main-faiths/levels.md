# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the book teaches that God's religion is one through the ages, brought by every prophet, and that the Torah given to Musa and the Injil given to Isa were later distorted… — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
