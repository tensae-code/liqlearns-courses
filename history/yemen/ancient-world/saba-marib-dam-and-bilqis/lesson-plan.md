**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Prophets and Ancient Yemen — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The state of Saba** — Saba, first at Sirwah then at Marib, unifies Yemen under Karib'il Watar, builds the Marib Dam and trades between north and south Arabia, and its queen Bilqis meets…
2. **States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Saba)** — Saba, the longest and oldest of Yemen's kingdoms, rules first from Sirwah then Marib, reaches the al-Ghala oasis in the north of the Hijaz, and its most famous hour is…
3. **Queen Bilqis: a civilisation and faith** — Bilqis, a king's daughter ruling Saba at its height

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: saba, first at Sirwah then at Marib, unifies Yemen under Karib'il Watar, builds the Marib Dam and trades between north and south Arabia, and its queen Bilqis meets….
2. Give one real example of it from the story as taught — a person, a place and the turn in their fate.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Yemen, Arabia and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
