# Saba, the Marib Dam and Bilqis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The state of Saba”.
**Answer:** Saba, first at Sirwah then at Marib, unifies Yemen under Karib'il Watar, builds the Marib Dam and trades between north and south Arabia, and its queen Bilqis meets Solomon and, in the book's telling, brings the Yemenis into his faith

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Saba)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** Saba, the longest and oldest of Yemen's kingdoms, rules first from Sirwah then Marib, reaches the al-Ghala oasis in the north of the Hijaz, and its most famous hour is Queen Bilqis's dealings with Solomon, which the Qur'an itself records
