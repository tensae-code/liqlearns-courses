# Arab Civilisations Flourish in Asia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Manifestations of flourishing of the ancient Arab civilisations in Asia”.
**Answer:** Yemen's kings rule by shura with a general council and market laws inscribed on a pillar at Tamna', mint coins, work tens of mines, and write in 29-letter Musnad while their doctors embalm and operate; the Gulf kings of Dilmun and Magan grow rich on pearls and re-export; Mesopotamia gives Hammurabi's 282-article code, cuneiform, Ashurbanipal's classified library, the Gilgamesh epic and the ziggurats; and the Levant's city-kingdoms turn cuneiform into the 22-letter alphabet at Ugarit and pass it through Phoenicia to Greece, Rome and the alphabets of today
