# The Medina Document, the Bay'a and the Saqifa: Democracy's Roots — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — four firsts are lined up as the seeds of modern institutions: the Medina Document as the first written constitution in history, the bay'a as the first election — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
