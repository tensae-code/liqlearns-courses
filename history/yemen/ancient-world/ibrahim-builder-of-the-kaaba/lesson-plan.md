4. **Ibrahim in the Levant and Mecca** — Ibrahim migrates with Sarah and his nephew Lot to the blessed land, settles in Palestine where al-Khalil still bears his name, sends Lot to Sodom, and is given Ismail by…

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: ibrahim, born in Iraq in the Babylonian age, smashes his people's idols and faces the tyrant Nimrod, is thrown into the fire and saved, then raises the Kaaba in Mecca….

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Yemen, Arabia and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
