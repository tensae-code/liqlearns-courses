# Ibrahim Builder of the Kaaba — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Our master Ibrahim”?
a) Ibrahim, born in Iraq in the Babylonian age, smashes his people's idols and faces the tyrant Nimrod, is thrown into the fire and saved, then raises…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Ibrahim, born in Iraq in the Babylonian age, smashes his people's idols and faces the tyrant Nimrod, is thrown into the fire and saved, then raises…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Ibrahim in Mesopotamia”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Ibrahim is born in southern Iraq under the first Babylonian state, where a tyrant authority claims divinity, bans the mention of God on pain of…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Ibrahim is born in southern Iraq under the first Babylonian state, where a tyrant authority claims divinity, bans the mention of God on pain of…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
