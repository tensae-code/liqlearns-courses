# Ibrahim Builder of the Kaaba — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Our master Ibrahim”.
**Answer:** Ibrahim, born in Iraq in the Babylonian age, smashes his people's idols and faces the tyrant Nimrod, is thrown into the fire and saved, then raises the Kaaba in Mecca with his son Ismail and founds the Hajj rites people still perform

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Ibrahim in Mesopotamia”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** Ibrahim is born in southern Iraq under the first Babylonian state, where a tyrant authority claims divinity, bans the mention of God on pain of burning and makes idolatry the state religion, and the boy — son of Azar the idol-maker — grows up untouched by it, reasons his way past star, moon and sun that set, and begins his call with his own father before facing Nimrod and the whole society
