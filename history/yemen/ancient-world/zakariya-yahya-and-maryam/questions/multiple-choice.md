# Zakariya Yahya and Maryam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran”?
a) In a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
