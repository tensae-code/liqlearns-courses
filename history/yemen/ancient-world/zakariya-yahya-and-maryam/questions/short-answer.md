# Zakariya Yahya and Maryam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran”.
**Answer:** in a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl, Maryam, who is raised in seclusion in the sanctuary under her uncle Zakariya's care, fed with out-of-season fruit; seeing her devotion, the aged Zakariya prays in that very place for a child and is given Yahya, who takes the Book firmly from childhood — the first reformer of his age
