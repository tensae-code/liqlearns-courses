# Israel in Sinai Calf Cow and Forty Years Wandering — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Children of Israel in the Sinai Peninsula”.
**Answer:** saved through the sea, Israel at once asks Musa for a visible god, demands to see God and is struck down and revived, worships al-Samiri's lowing calf while Musa is on the mountain, argues over the cow they are ordered to slaughter until the murdered man, struck with a piece of it, sits up and names his killer — then refuses to enter the holy land, "go, you and your Lord, and fight", and is sentenced to forty years of wandering in Sinai, fed on manna and quails and water from the rock, until Harun and Musa themselves die and a new generation under Yusha bin Nun goes in
