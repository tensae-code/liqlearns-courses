# Israel in Sinai Calf Cow and Forty Years Wandering — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Children of Israel in the Sinai Peninsula”?
a) Saved through the sea, Israel at once asks Musa for a visible god, demands to see God and is struck down and revived, worships al-Samiri's lowing…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
