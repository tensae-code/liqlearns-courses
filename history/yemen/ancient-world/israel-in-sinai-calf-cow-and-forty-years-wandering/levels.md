# Israel in Sinai Calf Cow and Forty Years Wandering — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — saved through the sea, Israel at once asks Musa for a visible god, demands to see God and is struck down and revived, worships al-Samiri's lowing calf while Musa is on… — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
