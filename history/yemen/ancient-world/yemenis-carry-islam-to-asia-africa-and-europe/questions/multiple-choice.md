# Yemenis Carry Islam to Asia Africa and Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The role of the Yemenis in spreading Islam, creed and civilisation (Unit 4 Lesson 1)”?
a) Yemeni soldiers, merchants and callers carry Islam on every road  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yemeni soldiers, merchants and callers carry Islam on every road** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The role of the Yemenis in spreading Islam, creed and civilisation (Unit 4 Lesson 1)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Yemeni soldiers, merchants and callers carry Islam on every road  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Yemeni soldiers, merchants and callers carry Islam on every road** — That is what this lesson teaches.
