## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ma'in and Hadramawt** — Ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows rich on dams…
2. **States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Ma'in, Qataban, Hadramawt, Awsan)** — Ma'in at Qarnaw in al-Jawf minds trade above all and plants stations up the road to north Arabia, Qataban at Timna organises the land caravans, Hadramawt at Shabwa holds…

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows rich on dams….

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Yemen, Arabia and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
