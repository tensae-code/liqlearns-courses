# Ma'in and Hadramawt: Frankincense Kingdoms

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Ma'in and Hadramawt; States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Ma'in, Qataban, Hadramawt, Awsan). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ma'in and Hadramawt

Ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows rich on dams, irrigation, incense and myrrh caravans, ruled by a king (mukarrib) advised by a council

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Ma'in, Qataban, Hadramawt, Awsan)

Ma'in at Qarnaw in al-Jawf minds trade above all and plants stations up the road to north Arabia, Qataban at Timna organises the land caravans, Hadramawt at Shabwa holds the frankincense groves themselves and reaches Socotra and Zafar in Oman, and Awsan in Wadi Markha breaks from Qataban before being folded into Himyar

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
