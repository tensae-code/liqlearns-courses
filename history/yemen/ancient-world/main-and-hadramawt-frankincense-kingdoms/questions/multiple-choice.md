# Ma'in and Hadramawt: Frankincense Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Ma'in and Hadramawt”?
a) Ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Ma'in, Qataban, Hadramawt, Awsan)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Ma'in at Qarnaw in al-Jawf minds trade above all and plants stations up the road to north Arabia, Qataban at Timna organises the land caravans…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Ma'in at Qarnaw in al-Jawf minds trade above all and plants stations up the road to north Arabia, Qataban at Timna organises the land caravans…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
