# Ma'in and Hadramawt: Frankincense Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows rich on dams….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Ma'in, Qataban, Hadramawt, Awsan)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Ma'in at Qarnaw in al-Jawf minds trade above all and plants stations up the road to north Arabia, Qataban at Timna organises the land caravans….
