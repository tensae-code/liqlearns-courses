# Ma'in and Hadramawt: Frankincense Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Ma'in and Hadramawt”.
**Answer:** Ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows rich on dams, irrigation, incense and myrrh caravans, ruled by a king (mukarrib) advised by a council

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Ma'in, Qataban, Hadramawt, Awsan)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** Ma'in at Qarnaw in al-Jawf minds trade above all and plants stations up the road to north Arabia, Qataban at Timna organises the land caravans, Hadramawt at Shabwa holds the frankincense groves themselves and reaches Socotra and Zafar in Oman, and Awsan in Wadi Markha breaks from Qataban before being folded into Himyar
