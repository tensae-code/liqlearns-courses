# Rasulid Yemen: Taiz, Sea Power and Scholar-Kings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Yemeni states that contributed: the Rasulid state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)”.
**Answer:** Nur al-Din Umar ibn Ali ibn Rasul, appointed over Yemen by the Ayyubids, declares himself king, builds a fleet at Aden and fortifies the coast to Dhofar; his scholar-kings endow schools like al-Mu'tabiyya at Zabid, introduce rice to Yemen, send Baybars 500 knights against the Tatars, strike gold and silver coins, and themselves write books on farming and guidance
