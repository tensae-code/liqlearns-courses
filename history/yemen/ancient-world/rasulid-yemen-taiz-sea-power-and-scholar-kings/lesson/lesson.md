# Rasulid Yemen: Taiz, Sea Power and Scholar-Kings

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Yemeni states that contributed: the Rasulid state (Unit 4 Lesson 2). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Yemeni states that contributed: the Rasulid state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)

Nur al-Din Umar ibn Ali ibn Rasul, appointed over Yemen by the Ayyubids, declares himself king, builds a fleet at Aden and fortifies the coast to Dhofar; his scholar-kings endow schools like al-Mu'tabiyya at Zabid, introduce rice to Yemen, send Baybars 500 knights against the Tatars, strike gold and silver coins, and themselves write books on farming and guidance
