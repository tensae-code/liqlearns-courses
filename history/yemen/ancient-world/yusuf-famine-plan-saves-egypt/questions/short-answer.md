# Yusuf Famine Plan Saves Egypt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “God establishes His prophet Yusuf”.
**Answer:** Yusuf reads the king's dream — seven fat cows, seven lean — and lays down a whole economic plan: plant the fat years to the limit, store the wheat in its ears, ration the lean years; cleared of the Aziz's wife's charge by her own confession, he becomes Egypt's minister, the famine drives his own brothers down from Canaan, and after the test of Benjamin the family is reunited, Yaqub's sight returns at the scent of Yusuf's shirt, and the dream of the stars is fulfilled on the throne of Egypt
