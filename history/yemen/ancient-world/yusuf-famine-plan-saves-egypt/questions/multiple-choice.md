# Yusuf Famine Plan Saves Egypt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “God establishes His prophet Yusuf”?
a) Yusuf reads the king's dream  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yusuf reads the king's dream** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “God establishes His prophet Yusuf”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Yusuf reads the king's dream  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Yusuf reads the king's dream** — That is what this lesson teaches.
