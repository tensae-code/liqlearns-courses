# Yusuf Famine Plan Saves Egypt

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: God establishes His prophet Yusuf. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## God establishes His prophet Yusuf

Yusuf reads the king's dream — seven fat cows, seven lean — and lays down a whole economic plan: plant the fat years to the limit, store the wheat in its ears, ration the lean years; cleared of the Aziz's wife's charge by her own confession, he becomes Egypt's minister, the famine drives his own brothers down from Canaan, and after the test of Benjamin the family is reunited, Yaqub's sight returns at the scent of Yusuf's shirt, and the dream of the stars is fulfilled on the throne of Egypt

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: yusuf reads the king's dream. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Yusuf Famine Plan Saves Egypt. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: yusuf reads the king's dream.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson's story echoes in real life. Note it.
