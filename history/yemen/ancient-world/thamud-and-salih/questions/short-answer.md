# Thamud and Salih — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The civilisation of Thamud”.
**Answer:** Thamud in the Hijaz carve palaces and houses into the mountains and prosper, their prophet Salih brings a she-camel out of the rock as a sign, the leaders hamstring her anyway, and an earthquake and a mighty shout destroy the people while Salih and the believers survive

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “The civilisation of Thamud”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** Thamud rise in the Hijaz after 'Ad, carving palaces in the plains and safe houses inside the mountains at al-Hijr, whose ruins still stand, Salih — known among them for wisdom — is accused of sorcery and challenged to bring a sign, a she-camel comes out of the rock and shares their water day about, the leaders plot against Salih and hamstring her under Qudar bin Salif, and three days later earthquake and shout end Thamud while Salih is saved
