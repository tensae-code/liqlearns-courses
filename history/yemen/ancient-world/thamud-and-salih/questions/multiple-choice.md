# Thamud and Salih — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “The civilisation of Thamud”?
a) Thamud in the Hijaz carve palaces and houses into the mountains and prosper, their prophet Salih brings a she-camel out of the rock as a sign, the…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Thamud in the Hijaz carve palaces and houses into the mountains and prosper, their prophet Salih brings a she-camel out of the rock as a sign, the…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “The civilisation of Thamud”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Thamud rise in the Hijaz after 'Ad, carving palaces in the plains and safe houses inside the mountains at al-Hijr, whose ruins still stand, Salih  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Thamud rise in the Hijaz after 'Ad, carving palaces in the plains and safe houses inside the mountains at al-Hijr, whose ruins still stand, Salih** — That is what this lesson teaches.
