# Thamud and Salih — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Thamud in the Hijaz carve palaces and houses into the mountains and prosper, their prophet Salih brings a she-camel out of the rock as a sign, the leaders hamstring her….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “The civilisation of Thamud” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Thamud rise in the Hijaz after 'Ad, carving palaces in the plains and safe houses inside the mountains at al-Hijr, whose ruins still stand, Salih.
