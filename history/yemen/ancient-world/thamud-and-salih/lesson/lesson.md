# Thamud and Salih

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The civilisation of Thamud; The civilisation of Thamud. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The civilisation of Thamud

Thamud in the Hijaz carve palaces and houses into the mountains and prosper, their prophet Salih brings a she-camel out of the rock as a sign, the leaders hamstring her anyway, and an earthquake and a mighty shout destroy the people while Salih and the believers survive

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The civilisation of Thamud

Thamud rise in the Hijaz after 'Ad, carving palaces in the plains and safe houses inside the mountains at al-Hijr, whose ruins still stand, Salih — known among them for wisdom — is accused of sorcery and challenged to bring a sign, a she-camel comes out of the rock and shares their water day about, the leaders plot against Salih and hamstring her under Qudar bin Salif, and three days later earthquake and shout end Thamud while Salih is saved

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: thamud in the Hijaz carve palaces and houses into the mountains and prosper, their prophet Salih brings a she-camel out of the rock as a sign, the leaders hamstring her…. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Thamud and Salih. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
