# Foundations of Arab Islamic Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation”?
a) Three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
