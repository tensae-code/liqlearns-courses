# Foundations of Arab Islamic Civilisation

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation

three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing the door of ijtihad; the Arab umma, honoured to bear the message, whose Prophet is an Arab from the land God chose as Islam's cradle; and the Arabic language, perfected among the old Arab civilisations, renewed and unified by the Qur'an's descent and carried by Islam into every land it reached
