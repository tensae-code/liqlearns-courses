# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Greek civilisation”.
**Answer:** Greek-speaking tribes migrate in waves — Achaeans, then Ionians and Dorians — into a land of islands and sea roads, borrowing first from Phoenicians, Babylonians and Egyptians, then outstripping them by free thought and fierce civic competition; the city-state passes from kings to aristocrats to tyrants to democracy, Draco's and Solon's laws in Athens replace clan custom with individual responsibility and become a root of Roman and European law, and Socrates, Plato and Aristotle, with Herodotus the father of history, make the 5th–4th centuries BCE a flowering of philosophy, theatre, sculpture and science
