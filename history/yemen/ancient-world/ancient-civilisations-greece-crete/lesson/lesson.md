## Do it first

Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Greek civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Greek civilisation

Greek-speaking tribes migrate in waves — Achaeans, then Ionians and Dorians — into a land of islands and sea roads, borrowing first from Phoenicians, Babylonians and Egyptians, then outstripping them by free thought and fierce civic competition; the city-state passes from kings to aristocrats to tyrants to democracy, Draco's and Solon's laws in Athens replace clan custom with individual responsibility and become a root of Roman and European law, and Socrates, Plato and Aristotle, with Herodotus the father of history, make the 5th–4th centuries BCE a flowering of philosophy, theatre, sculpture and science
