# The Alawite State of Tabaristan: Nasir al-Utrush — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Islamic state in the age of the Alawites in Tabaristan (Imam al-Nasir al-Utrush, 230-304 AH)”.
**Answer:** al-Hasan bin Ali al-Utrush, born in Medina in 230 AH and schooled there and in Iraq, carries the call to Tabaristan south of the Caspian, takes it in 301 AH and enters Amul, its capital, at the head of his army, and rules Tabaristan, Gilan and Daylam on the same principles as the Zaydi states of Yemen
