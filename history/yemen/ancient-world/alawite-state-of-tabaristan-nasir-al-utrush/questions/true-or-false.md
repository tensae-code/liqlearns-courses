# The Alawite State of Tabaristan: Nasir al-Utrush — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Al-Hasan bin Ali al-Utrush, born in Medina in 230 AH and schooled there and in Iraq, carries the call to Tabaristan south of the Caspian, takes it in 301 AH and enters….
**Answer: True**

**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Al-Hasan bin Ali al-Utrush, born in Medina in 230 AH and schooled there and in Iraq, carries the call to Tabaristan south of the Caspian, takes it in….
