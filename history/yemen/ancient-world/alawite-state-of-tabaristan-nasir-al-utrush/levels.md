# The Alawite State of Tabaristan: Nasir al-Utrush — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — al-Hasan bin Ali al-Utrush, born in Medina in 230 AH and schooled there and in Iraq, carries the call to Tabaristan south of the Caspian, takes it in 301 AH and enters… — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
