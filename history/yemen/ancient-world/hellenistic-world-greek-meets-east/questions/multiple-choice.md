# Hellenistic World Greek Meets East — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Hellenistic civilisation”?
a) Alexander dies in 323 BCE and his empire splits three ways  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Alexander dies in 323 BCE and his empire splits three ways** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Hellenistic civilisation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Alexander dies in 323 BCE and his empire splits three ways  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Alexander dies in 323 BCE and his empire splits three ways** — That is what this lesson teaches.
