## The Hellenistic civilisation

Alexander dies in 323 BCE and his empire splits three ways — Ptolemies in Egypt, Seleucids from the Mediterranean to the Indus, Antigonids in Europe — and Greek and eastern civilisations blend: kings sanctify themselves like Pharaohs, rule Persian-style provinces, yet keep Greek councils and restrict citizenship to Greek-speakers, so easterners learn Greek to belong; Alexandria becomes the world's classroom, with Euclid in geometry, Archimedes in mechanics, Herophilus correcting Aristotle, Menander's new theatre and the Stoic school of Zeno the Phoenician — the civilisation Islam's armies later find ruling the Levant and Egypt
