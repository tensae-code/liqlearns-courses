# Sulayman Kingdom Wind Jinn and Copper — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Prophet of God Sulayman”.
**Answer:** Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have — the wind runs at his command like a transport fleet, a spring of molten copper feeds his industries, jinn dive for pearls and raise buildings and cauldrons too heavy to move, and he understands the speech of birds and ants; he dies leaning on his staff and the jinn labour on, not knowing, until a termite eats the staff through — and after him his kingdom splits into Judah and Israel
