# Sulayman Kingdom Wind Jinn and Copper — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Prophet of God Sulayman” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have.
