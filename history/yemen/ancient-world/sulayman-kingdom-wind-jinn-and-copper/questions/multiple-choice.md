# Sulayman Kingdom Wind Jinn and Copper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Prophet of God Sulayman”?
a) Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Prophet of God Sulayman”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have** — That is what this lesson teaches.
