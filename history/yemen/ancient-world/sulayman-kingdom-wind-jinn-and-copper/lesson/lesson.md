# Sulayman Kingdom Wind Jinn and Copper

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Prophet of God Sulayman. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Prophet of God Sulayman

Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have — the wind runs at his command like a transport fleet, a spring of molten copper feeds his industries, jinn dive for pearls and raise buildings and cauldrons too heavy to move, and he understands the speech of birds and ants; he dies leaning on his staff and the jinn labour on, not knowing, until a termite eats the staff through — and after him his kingdom splits into Judah and Israel

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Sulayman Kingdom Wind Jinn and Copper. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
