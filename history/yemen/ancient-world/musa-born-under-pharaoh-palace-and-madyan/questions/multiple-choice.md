# Musa Born Under Pharaoh Palace and Madyan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Prophet Musa in the heart of Egyptian civilisation”?
a) The Israelites Yusuf brought to Egypt sink over the centuries into a crushed labouring class under a Pharaoh who calls himself the highest lord and…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Israelites Yusuf brought to Egypt sink over the centuries into a crushed labouring class under a Pharaoh who calls himself the highest lord and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Prophet Musa in the heart of Egyptian civilisation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The Israelites Yusuf brought to Egypt sink over the centuries into a crushed labouring class under a Pharaoh who calls himself the highest lord and…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The Israelites Yusuf brought to Egypt sink over the centuries into a crushed labouring class under a Pharaoh who calls himself the highest lord and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
