# Musa Born Under Pharaoh Palace and Madyan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Prophet Musa in the heart of Egyptian civilisation”.
**Answer:** the Israelites Yusuf brought to Egypt sink over the centuries into a crushed labouring class under a Pharaoh who calls himself the highest lord and slaughters their sons; the doomed baby Musa is floated down the Nile in a chest, picked up at the palace itself, loved by Pharaoh's wife Asiya, and returned by God's design to his own mother's breast — raised in the palace in horsemanship and rule, he kills an Egyptian overseer by accident while defending an Israelite, flees to Madyan, waters the flocks of two shepherdesses, and marries into the house of Shu'ayb for eight to ten years of herding
