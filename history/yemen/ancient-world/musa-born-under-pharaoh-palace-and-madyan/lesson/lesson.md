# Musa Born Under Pharaoh Palace and Madyan

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Prophet Musa in the heart of Egyptian civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Prophet Musa in the heart of Egyptian civilisation

the Israelites Yusuf brought to Egypt sink over the centuries into a crushed labouring class under a Pharaoh who calls himself the highest lord and slaughters their sons; the doomed baby Musa is floated down the Nile in a chest, picked up at the palace itself, loved by Pharaoh's wife Asiya, and returned by God's design to his own mother's breast — raised in the palace in horsemanship and rule, he kills an Egyptian overseer by accident while defending an Israelite, flees to Madyan, waters the flocks of two shepherdesses, and marries into the house of Shu'ayb for eight to ten years of herding
