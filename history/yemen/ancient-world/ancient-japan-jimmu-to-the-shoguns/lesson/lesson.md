## The Japanese civilisation

on four storm-beaten islands the emperor Jimmu, said to descend from the sun goddess, founds the imperial state in 660 BCE; Shinto worship of ancestors and nature spirits absorbs Buddhism and Confucianism arriving through China in the 6th century CE, capitals move from Nara to Kyoto, and as imperial authority dissolves the samurai order rises — warriors who serve their lord without fearing death, holding suicide above capture — until Yoritomo takes the title Shogun in the Kamakura age; the book notes the first Japanese Hajj pilgrim, the trader Ahmad Arija, in 1909
