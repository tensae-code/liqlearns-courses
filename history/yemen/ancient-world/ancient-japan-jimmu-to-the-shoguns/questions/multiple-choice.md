# Ancient Japan Jimmu to the Shoguns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Japanese civilisation”?
a) On four storm-beaten islands the emperor Jimmu, said to descend from the sun goddess, founds the imperial state in 660 BCE  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) On four storm-beaten islands the emperor Jimmu, said to descend from the sun goddess, founds the imperial state in 660 BCE** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Japanese civilisation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) On four storm-beaten islands the emperor Jimmu, said to descend from the sun goddess, founds the imperial state in 660 BCE  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) On four storm-beaten islands the emperor Jimmu, said to descend from the sun goddess, founds the imperial state in 660 BCE** — That is what this lesson teaches.
