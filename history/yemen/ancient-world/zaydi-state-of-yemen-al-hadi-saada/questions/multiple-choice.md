# The Zaydi State of Yemen: al-Hadi at Saada — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH)”?
a) Yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Yemeni states that contributed to the Islamic civilisation: the Zaydi state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A delegation of Yemeni tribal leaders invites Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Rassi from Medina  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A delegation of Yemeni tribal leaders invites Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Rassi from Medina** — That is what this lesson teaches.
