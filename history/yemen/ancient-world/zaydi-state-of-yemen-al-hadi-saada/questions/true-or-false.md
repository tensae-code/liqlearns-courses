# The Zaydi State of Yemen: al-Hadi at Saada — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Yemeni states that contributed to the Islamic civilisation: the Zaydi state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A delegation of Yemeni tribal leaders invites Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Rassi from Medina.
