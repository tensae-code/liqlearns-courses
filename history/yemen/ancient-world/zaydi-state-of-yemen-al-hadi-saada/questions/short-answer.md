# The Zaydi State of Yemen: al-Hadi at Saada — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH)”.
**Answer:** Yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina; he reaches Yemen in 280 AH, settles disputes, founds a state on the Qur'an and the Sunna with its capital at Saada, unifies Yemen from Najran to Aden, and dies in 298 AH beside his mosque in Saada — and his imams, named for Zayd bin Ali, rule Yemen in stretches from then until the revolution of 1962

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Yemeni states that contributed to the Islamic civilisation: the Zaydi state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** a delegation of Yemeni tribal leaders invites Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Rassi from Medina; he takes the title al-Hadi ila al-Haqq, founds the Zaydi state at Saada (284 AH), and its schools and "hijar al-'ilm" make Saada a meeting point of scholars while al-Hadi himself writes works of tafsir, hadith and law
