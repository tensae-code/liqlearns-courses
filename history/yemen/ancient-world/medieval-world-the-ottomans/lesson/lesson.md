## Islamic civilisation in Anatolia (Unit 5 Lesson 3)

at Manzikert in 462 AH the Seljuks capture the Byzantine emperor and open Anatolia; the Seljuk state of Rum spreads Islam, then Ertugrul's migrant tribe founds the Ottoman emirate in the northwest, absorbs the beyliks by marriage, purchase and war, orders society into soldiers, scholars, taxpayers and protected dhimmis under the millet system, and Muhammad al-Fatih grades the schools and stocks a library of 12,000 volumes
