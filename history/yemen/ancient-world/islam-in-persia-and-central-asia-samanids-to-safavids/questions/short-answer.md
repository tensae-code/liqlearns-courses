# Islam in Persia and Central Asia: Samanids to Safavids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Islamic civilisation in Persia and Central Asia (Unit 5 Lesson 1)”.
**Answer:** Khosrow tears the Prophet's letter and the Prophet foretells his kingdom torn; Sa'd enters al-Mada'in, Yazdegerd III is killed in 31 AH and the Sassanian state ends, then Tahirids, Saffarids, Samanids and Seljuks raise Bukhara and Samarkand into centres where Persian is written in Arabic letters and Nizam al-Mulk founds the Nizamiyya schools
