# Islam in Persia and Central Asia: Samanids to Safavids

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Islamic civilisation in Persia and Central Asia (Unit 5 Lesson 1). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Islamic civilisation in Persia and Central Asia (Unit 5 Lesson 1)

Khosrow tears the Prophet's letter and the Prophet foretells his kingdom torn; Sa'd enters al-Mada'in, Yazdegerd III is killed in 31 AH and the Sassanian state ends, then Tahirids, Saffarids, Samanids and Seljuks raise Bukhara and Samarkand into centres where Persian is written in Arabic letters and Nizam al-Mulk founds the Nizamiyya schools
