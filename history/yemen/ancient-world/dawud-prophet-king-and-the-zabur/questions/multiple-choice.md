# Dawud Prophet King and the Zabur — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Civilisation of the prophet Dawud”?
a) Dawud unites the tribes under one rule, founds the kingdom and takes Jerusalem as its capital, receives the Zabur, and rules as prophet, king and…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Dawud unites the tribes under one rule, founds the kingdom and takes Jerusalem as its capital, receives the Zabur, and rules as prophet, king and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Civilisation of the prophet Dawud”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Dawud unites the tribes under one rule, founds the kingdom and takes Jerusalem as its capital, receives the Zabur, and rules as prophet, king and…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Dawud unites the tribes under one rule, founds the kingdom and takes Jerusalem as its capital, receives the Zabur, and rules as prophet, king and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
