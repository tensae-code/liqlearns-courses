# Dawud Prophet King and the Zabur — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Civilisation of the prophet Dawud”.
**Answer:** Dawud unites the tribes under one rule, founds the kingdom and takes Jerusalem as its capital, receives the Zabur, and rules as prophet, king and judge — startled by two litigants over the wall (angels, with their parable of the ninety-nine ewes and the one), he judges too fast, knows it was a test and repents, while in the case of the sheep in the crop his young son Sulayman's ruling improves on his own; mountains and birds join his praise, and iron softens in his hands into coats of mail
