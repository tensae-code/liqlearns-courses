# Why Science Flourished in the Islamic Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Factors in the rise of the scientific renaissance (Unit 6 Lesson 1)”.
**Answer:** six factors compound: a religion whose first revealed word commands reading and makes scholars heirs of the prophets; the Arabic language, spread by Uthman's gathering of the Qur'an and Abd al-Malik's Arabising of the diwans; stable rule and competing caliphs; a vast, varied geography whose culture centres — Mecca, Alexandria, Baghdad, Sanaa, Cordoba, Jundishapur — fall inside one abode; merchant wealth and the waqf endowing schools; and inherited civilisations, Greek translation, paper and observatories
