# Isa Birth Miracles and the Plot — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Prophet of God Isa”.
**Answer:** the angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle; Jibril comes in human form, she conceives without a husband, gives birth alone under a palm tree wishing she had died, and is told to shake the trunk and keep a fast of silence — the newborn speaks for her: I am God's servant, He has given me the Book and made me a prophet; he heals the blind and the leper, raises the dead and fashions a bird from clay, the disciples believe and declare their submission, the rest plot his death, and God raises him and casts his likeness on the traitor, who is crucified in his place
