# Isa Birth Miracles and the Plot — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Prophet of God Isa”?
a) The angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Prophet of God Isa”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle** — That is what this lesson teaches.
