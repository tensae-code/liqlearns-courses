# Isa Birth Miracles and the Plot — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Prophet of God Isa” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle.
