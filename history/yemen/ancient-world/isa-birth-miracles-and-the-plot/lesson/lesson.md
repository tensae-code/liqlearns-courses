# Isa Birth Miracles and the Plot

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Prophet of God Isa. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Prophet of God Isa

the angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle; Jibril comes in human form, she conceives without a husband, gives birth alone under a palm tree wishing she had died, and is told to shake the trunk and keep a fast of silence — the newborn speaks for her: I am God's servant, He has given me the Book and made me a prophet; he heals the blind and the leper, raises the dead and fashions a bird from clay, the disciples believe and declare their submission, the rest plot his death, and God raises him and casts his likeness on the traitor, who is crucified in his place

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the angels announce to Maryam a word from God, al-Masih Isa, who will speak in the cradle. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Isa Birth Miracles and the Plot. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
