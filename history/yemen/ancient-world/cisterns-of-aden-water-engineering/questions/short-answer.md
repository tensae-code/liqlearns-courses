# Cisterns of Aden Water Engineering — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Cisterns of Aden”.
**Answer:** Aden, the port bridging east-west trade, solves its thirst with giant cisterns cut into Wadi al-Tawila on the slopes of Jabal Shamsan under the Himyarite kings — a cascading chain of tanks some 229 metres long that cleans the water as it falls, holding some 14 million litres and shielding the crater city from the floods; of 55 cisterns, 18 still stand in good repair after the British occupation destroyed many
