# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Muhammad, Seal of the Prophets”?
a) Muhammad, orphaned and raised by his grandfather and his uncle Abu Talib, known to Quraysh as the Truthful and Trustworthy, receives revelation in…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Muhammad, orphaned and raised by his grandfather and his uncle Abu Talib, known to Quraysh as the Truthful and Trustworthy, receives revelation in…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “The Islamic call in Mecca”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Muhammad calls Mecca first in secret for three years, then openly for ten, Quraysh torture the weak believers like Ammar ibn Yasir and Bilal, offer…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Muhammad calls Mecca first in secret for three years, then openly for ten, Quraysh torture the weak believers like Ammar ibn Yasir and Bilal, offer…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
