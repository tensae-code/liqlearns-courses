# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Muhammad, Seal of the Prophets”.
**Answer:** Muhammad, orphaned and raised by his grandfather and his uncle Abu Talib, known to Quraysh as the Truthful and Trustworthy, receives revelation in Cave Hira at forty, endures thirteen years of persecution in Mecca, then migrates to Medina where the Aws and Khazraj welcome him and the first Islamic state is founded

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “The Islamic call in Mecca”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** Muhammad calls Mecca first in secret for three years, then openly for ten, Quraysh torture the weak believers like Ammar ibn Yasir and Bilal, offer his uncle wealth and kingship and are refused, boycott Banu Hashim for three years until termites eat the boycott sheet, and the hunted believers find shelter with the Negus in Abyssinia while the call reaches the tribes at Hajj
