# Hands-on — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Muhammad, Seal of the Prophets; The Islamic call in Mecca; The Islamic state in Medina; The Prophet's military campaigns; Spread of Islam outside the Arabian Peninsula; Islamic conquests in the age of the Caliphs and Imams; In the era of Prophethood (Unit 2 Lesson 1, first half). Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: muhammad, orphaned and raised by his grandfather and his uncle Abu Talib, known to Quraysh as the Truthful and Trustworthy, receives revelation in Cave Hira at forty….

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: muhammad, orphaned and raised by his grandfather and his uncle Abu Talib, known to Quraysh as the Truthful and Trustworthy, receives revelation in Cave Hira at forty…. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
