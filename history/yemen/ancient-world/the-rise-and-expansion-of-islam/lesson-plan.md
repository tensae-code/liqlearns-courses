## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Muhammad, Seal of the Prophets** — Muhammad, orphaned and raised by his grandfather and his uncle Abu Talib, known to Quraysh as the Truthful and Trustworthy, receives revelation in Cave Hira at forty…
2. **The Islamic call in Mecca** — Muhammad calls Mecca first in secret for three years, then openly for ten, Quraysh torture the weak believers like Ammar ibn Yasir and Bilal, offer his uncle wealth and…
3. **The Islamic state in Medina** — Men of Yathrib pledge at Aqaba in the 11th, 12th and 13th years, Mus'ab is sent to teach them, then the Prophet slips out of Mecca with Ali sleeping in his bed and Abu…
4. **The Prophet's military campaigns** — The young state faces Quraysh, the Jewish tribes, Bedouin, Persians and Romans across 27 expeditions and 73 detachments
5. **Spread of Islam outside the Arabian Peninsula** — After Hudaybiyya in 6 AH the Prophet writes to the kings
6. **Islamic conquests in the age of the Caliphs and Imams** — After the apostasy wars Abu Bakr's letter summons Mecca, Taif, Yemen and all Arabia, and the armies roll out
7. **In the era of Prophethood (Unit 2 Lesson 1, first half)** — In one generation the Prophet builds a state in Medina

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Yemen, Arabia and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
