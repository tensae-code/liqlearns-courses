# The Rise and Expansion of Islam

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Muhammad, Seal of the Prophets; The Islamic call in Mecca; The Islamic state in Medina; The Prophet's military campaigns; Spread of Islam outside the Arabian Peninsula; Islamic conquests in the age of the Caliphs and Imams; In the era of Prophethood (Unit 2 Lesson 1, first half). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Muhammad, Seal of the Prophets

Muhammad, orphaned and raised by his grandfather and his uncle Abu Talib, known to Quraysh as the Truthful and Trustworthy, receives revelation in Cave Hira at forty, endures thirteen years of persecution in Mecca, then migrates to Medina where the Aws and Khazraj welcome him and the first Islamic state is founded

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Islamic call in Mecca

Muhammad calls Mecca first in secret for three years, then openly for ten, Quraysh torture the weak believers like Ammar ibn Yasir and Bilal, offer his uncle wealth and kingship and are refused, boycott Banu Hashim for three years until termites eat the boycott sheet, and the hunted believers find shelter with the Negus in Abyssinia while the call reaches the tribes at Hajj

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Islamic state in Medina

men of Yathrib pledge at Aqaba in the 11th, 12th and 13th years, Mus'ab is sent to teach them, then the Prophet slips out of Mecca with Ali sleeping in his bed and Abu Bakr at his side, reaches Quba and renames Yathrib Medina, first capital of Islam, and builds the mosque and the brotherhood of Emigrants and Helpers

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Prophet's military campaigns

the young state faces Quraysh, the Jewish tribes, Bedouin, Persians and Romans across 27 expeditions and 73 detachments — Badr in 2 AH where 314 defeat near a thousand, the Trench in 5 AH, Mecca opened in 8 AH after Quraysh break the Hudaybiyya truce, and Tabuk in 9 AH, the last expedition, against the Romans

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 5 — Spread of Islam outside the Arabian Peninsula

after Hudaybiyya in 6 AH the Prophet writes to the kings — the Negus and Heraclius answer kindly, the Muqawqis sends gifts, Khosrow tears the letter up — then in 10 AH comes the Farewell Pilgrimage and, in this book's telling, the halt at Ghadir Khumm, and the Prophet dies on Monday 12 Rabi I, 11 AH

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 6 — Islamic conquests in the age of the Caliphs and Imams

after the apostasy wars Abu Bakr's letter summons Mecca, Taif, Yemen and all Arabia, and the armies roll out — Ajnadayn and Yarmouk, Damascus and Jerusalem, Babylon and Alexandria in 21 AH, Iraq from 12 AH to the fall of Ctesiphon in 16 AH and the rest of Persia by 21 AH — with Yemeni tribes, Hamdan, Madhhij, Kinda, Hadramawt and the Azd, fighting at Qadisiyya, Yarmouk and Babylon

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 7 — In the era of Prophethood (Unit 2 Lesson 1, first half)

in one generation the Prophet builds a state in Medina — the Sahifa constitution of 47 articles ordering Muslims, Jews and tribes in peace and war, scribes and diwans in embryo, zakat, jizya and land-tax finance, the trench dug before the Ahzab battle, a hospital tent in the mosque, the first quarantine order against plague lands, and mosques raised on Yemen's soil at Sanaa and al-Janad — while Yemeni delegations bring Yemen into Islam willingly, without conquest

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
