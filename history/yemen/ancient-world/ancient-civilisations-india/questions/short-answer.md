# Ancient Civilisations: India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Indian civilisation”.
**Answer:** cities rise at Mohenjo-Daro by 2400 BCE, the Aryans bring the Vedic religion from which Hinduism branches, and society hardens into closed classes — Brahmin priests, warriors, farmers and merchants, servants, and untouchables who may not mix; Buddha's teaching becomes a state faith under the Mauryas, Chandragupta liberates India after Alexander's invasion and his grandson Ashoka carries its trade to its peak, Sanskrit records the Mahabharata and the 24,000-verse Ramayana, and Yemeni frankincense is among the first goods to bind India to the Arabs
