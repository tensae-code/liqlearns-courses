# Ancient Civilisations: India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cities rise at Mohenjo-Daro by 2400 BCE, the Aryans bring the Vedic religion from which Hinduism branches, and society hardens into closed classes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Indian civilisation” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Cities rise at Mohenjo-Daro by 2400 BCE, the Aryans bring the Vedic religion from which Hinduism branches, and society hardens into closed classes.
