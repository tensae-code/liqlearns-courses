# Ancient Civilisations: India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Indian civilisation”?
a) Cities rise at Mohenjo-Daro by 2400 BCE, the Aryans bring the Vedic religion from which Hinduism branches, and society hardens into closed classes  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Cities rise at Mohenjo-Daro by 2400 BCE, the Aryans bring the Vedic religion from which Hinduism branches, and society hardens into closed classes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Indian civilisation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Cities rise at Mohenjo-Daro by 2400 BCE, the Aryans bring the Vedic religion from which Hinduism branches, and society hardens into closed classes  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Cities rise at Mohenjo-Daro by 2400 BCE, the Aryans bring the Vedic religion from which Hinduism branches, and society hardens into closed classes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
