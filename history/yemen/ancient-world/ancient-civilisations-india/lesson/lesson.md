# Ancient Civilisations: India

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Indian civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Indian civilisation

cities rise at Mohenjo-Daro by 2400 BCE, the Aryans bring the Vedic religion from which Hinduism branches, and society hardens into closed classes — Brahmin priests, warriors, farmers and merchants, servants, and untouchables who may not mix; Buddha's teaching becomes a state faith under the Mauryas, Chandragupta liberates India after Alexander's invasion and his grandson Ashoka carries its trade to its peak, Sanskrit records the Mahabharata and the 24,000-verse Ramayana, and Yemeni frankincense is among the first goods to bind India to the Arabs
