## Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Aztecs

Nahua tribes settle the Mexican plateau and raise an empire ruled absolutely yet advised by a council of tribal representatives, fed by farmers growing maize, cacao and tobacco, and enforced by spear-and-sling armies; law is severe — bribery, theft and misuse of office punished by death, with compensation to the victim prized over revenge — boys are schooled at home then in temple schools, and sacrifices are offered to the gods of war, sun and rain above great pyramid temples
