# Manadhira of Al Hira and Kinda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At al-Hira on the Euphrates, 'Amr ibn Adi founds the Lakhmid emirate that serves Persia yet becomes a court of Arab poetry.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Manadhira and Kinda civilisations” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: At al-Hira on the Euphrates, 'Amr ibn Adi founds the Lakhmid emirate that serves Persia yet becomes a court of Arab poetry.
