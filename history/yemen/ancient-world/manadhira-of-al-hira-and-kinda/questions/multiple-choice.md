# Manadhira of Al Hira and Kinda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Manadhira and Kinda civilisations”?
a) At al-Hira on the Euphrates, 'Amr ibn Adi founds the Lakhmid emirate that serves Persia yet becomes a court of Arab poetry  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) At al-Hira on the Euphrates, 'Amr ibn Adi founds the Lakhmid emirate that serves Persia yet becomes a court of Arab poetry** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Manadhira and Kinda civilisations”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) At al-Hira on the Euphrates, 'Amr ibn Adi founds the Lakhmid emirate that serves Persia yet becomes a court of Arab poetry  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) At al-Hira on the Euphrates, 'Amr ibn Adi founds the Lakhmid emirate that serves Persia yet becomes a court of Arab poetry** — That is what this lesson teaches.
