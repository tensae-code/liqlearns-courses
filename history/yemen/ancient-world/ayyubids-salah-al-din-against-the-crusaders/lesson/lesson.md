# The Ayyubids: Salah al-Din Against the Crusaders

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: In the Ayyubid era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, first half). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## In the Ayyubid era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, first half)

Salah al-Din, sent to Egypt and made the Fatimid caliph's wazir, ends the Fatimid caliphate and founds a state ruling Egypt, the Levant, Hijaz and Yemen; facing seven Crusader campaigns he walls Egypt's four capitals into one, builds his citadel on the Muqattam, wins Hattin and recovers Jerusalem in 583 AH, fills the land with schools and bimaristans and a 68,000-volume library — and builds no new city except Taiz in Yemen
