# The Ayyubids: Salah al-Din Against the Crusaders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “In the Ayyubid era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, first half)”.
**Answer:** Salah al-Din, sent to Egypt and made the Fatimid caliph's wazir, ends the Fatimid caliphate and founds a state ruling Egypt, the Levant, Hijaz and Yemen; facing seven Crusader campaigns he walls Egypt's four capitals into one, builds his citadel on the Muqattam, wins Hattin and recovers Jerusalem in 583 AH, fills the land with schools and bimaristans and a 68,000-volume library — and builds no new city except Taiz in Yemen
