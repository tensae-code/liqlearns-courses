# The Ayyubids: Salah al-Din Against the Crusaders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “In the Ayyubid era (Unit 2 Lesson 5, first half)”?
a) Salah al-Din, sent to Egypt and made the Fatimid caliph's wazir, ends the Fatimid caliphate and founds a state ruling Egypt, the Levant, Hijaz and…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Salah al-Din, sent to Egypt and made the Fatimid caliph's wazir, ends the Fatimid caliphate and founds a state ruling Egypt, the Levant, Hijaz and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
