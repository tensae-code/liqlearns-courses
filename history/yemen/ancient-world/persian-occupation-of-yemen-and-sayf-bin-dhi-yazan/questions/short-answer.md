# Persian Occupation of Yemen and Sayf Bin Dhi Yazan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Persian occupation of Yemen”.
**Answer:** Sayf bin Dhi Yazan begs Khosrow of Persia for an army, Wahrez leads it and drives the Abyssinians out, but Sayf rules only by paying tribute and is assassinated, Wahrez takes Yemen for Persia, and Persian rule lasts until the governor Badhan's time, when Yemenis answer Muhammad's call and Yemen joins the Islamic state

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Yemen's condition before Islam: Persian influence and the entry of Islam”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** Sayf's victory turns to tutelage — Abyssinian servants assassinate him in his palace, Khosrow sends Wahriz back in vengeance, and Persian rule begins, shrinking under Yemeni revolts until it holds only Sanaa, Aden and their surroundings under the governor Badhan; then Islam arrives, Badhan's Persians enter it, and Yemen passes freely into the new Arab-Islamic state, Islam uniting the Yemenis under one banner and carrying their civilisation to every corner of the world
