# Persian Occupation of Yemen and Sayf Bin Dhi Yazan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “The Persian occupation of Yemen”?
a) Sayf bin Dhi Yazan begs Khosrow of Persia for an army, Wahrez leads it and drives the Abyssinians out, but Sayf rules only by paying tribute and is…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Sayf bin Dhi Yazan begs Khosrow of Persia for an army, Wahrez leads it and drives the Abyssinians out, but Sayf rules only by paying tribute and is…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Yemen's condition before Islam: Persian influence and the entry of Islam”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Sayf's victory turns to tutelage  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Sayf's victory turns to tutelage** — That is what this lesson teaches.
