# Persian Occupation of Yemen and Sayf Bin Dhi Yazan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sayf bin Dhi Yazan begs Khosrow of Persia for an army, Wahrez leads it and drives the Abyssinians out, but Sayf rules only by paying tribute and is assassinated, Wahrez….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Yemen's condition before Islam: Persian influence and the entry of Islam” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Sayf's victory turns to tutelage.
