# Yemen Answers the Prophets Call

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Yemen in the Prophetic age. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Yemen in the Prophetic age

Yemen answers the call faster than anywhere — the family of Yasir are among Islam's first martyrs, Yemeni delegations crowd to Medina and are praised, Ali is sent to Yemen in 8 AH and Hamdan embraces Islam in a single day, Mu'adh ibn Jabal follows as teacher, and the Prophet's word stands over the lesson: faith is Yemeni and wisdom is Yemeni

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: yemen answers the call faster than anywhere. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Yemen Answers the Prophets Call. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: yemen answers the call faster than anywhere.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson's story echoes in real life. Note it.
