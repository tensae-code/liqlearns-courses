# Yemen Answers the Prophets Call — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Yemen in the Prophetic age”?
a) Yemen answers the call faster than anywhere  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yemen answers the call faster than anywhere** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Yemen in the Prophetic age”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Yemen answers the call faster than anywhere  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Yemen answers the call faster than anywhere** — That is what this lesson teaches.
