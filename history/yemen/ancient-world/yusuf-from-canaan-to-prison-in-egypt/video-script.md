# Video script — Yusuf from Canaan to Prison in Egypt

Yusuf, eleventh of Yaqub's twelve sons in Canaan, dreams of eleven stars and the sun and moon bowing to him, his jealous brothers throw him into a well, a trade caravan fishes him out and sells him in Egypt to the Aziz, whose wife he refuses at the price of prison

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
