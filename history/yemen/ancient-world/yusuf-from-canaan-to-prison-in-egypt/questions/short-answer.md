# Yusuf from Canaan to Prison in Egypt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Prophet of God Yusuf”.
**Answer:** Yusuf, eleventh of Yaqub's twelve sons in Canaan, dreams of eleven stars and the sun and moon bowing to him, his jealous brothers throw him into a well, a trade caravan fishes him out and sells him in Egypt to the Aziz, whose wife he refuses at the price of prison — and in prison the servant of God becomes a teacher, reading the dreams of the cupbearer and the baker
