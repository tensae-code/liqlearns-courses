# Yemeni Scholars in Islamic Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Role of Yemenis in Islamic civilisation”?
a) Yemen becomes a scholarly centre of the Islamic world  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yemen becomes a scholarly centre of the Islamic world** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Yemeni figures in the Arab Islamic sciences (Unit 4 Lesson 4)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Sanaa's early stars  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Sanaa's early stars** — That is what this lesson teaches.
