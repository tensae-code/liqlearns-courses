## Civilisation of Mesopotamia (3500-539 BCE)

between Tigris and Euphrates, fed by migrations out of south Arabia, states succeed one another — Sumer with its cuneiform, Akkad under Sargon, Babylon under Hammurabi and his written laws, Assyria with Ashurbanipal's library, and Babylon again under Nebuchadnezzar of the Hanging Gardens, who takes Jerusalem — until the last Babylonian state falls to the Achaemenid Persians in 539 BCE, each fall laid, in the book's reading, to injustice and turning from God's path
