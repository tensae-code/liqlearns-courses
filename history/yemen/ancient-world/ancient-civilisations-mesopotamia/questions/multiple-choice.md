# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Civilisation of Mesopotamia (3500-539 BCE)”?
a) Between Tigris and Euphrates, fed by migrations out of south Arabia, states succeed one another  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Between Tigris and Euphrates, fed by migrations out of south Arabia, states succeed one another** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Civilisation of Mesopotamia (3500-539 BCE)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Between Tigris and Euphrates, fed by migrations out of south Arabia, states succeed one another  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Between Tigris and Euphrates, fed by migrations out of south Arabia, states succeed one another** — That is what this lesson teaches.
