# Ancient Yemen Cities Palaces and Dams — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Architectural manifestations of ancient Yemen”?
a) Alabaster, marble and granite raise the cities  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Alabaster, marble and granite raise the cities** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Architectural manifestations of ancient Yemen”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Alabaster, marble and granite raise the cities  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Alabaster, marble and granite raise the cities** — That is what this lesson teaches.
