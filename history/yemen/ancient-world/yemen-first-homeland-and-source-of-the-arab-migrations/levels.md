# Yemen First Homeland and Source of the Arab Migrations — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — yemen is the southern gateway of the Arab civilisations, watching Bab al-Mandab and the roads to East Africa and India, and the Arabs' first homeland, where the line of… — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
