# Levant City Kingdoms: Canaanites, Phoenicians, Arameans — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the Levant, bridge of three continents and caravan road between Yemen, Mesopotamia and Egypt, fills with city kingdoms built largely by migrants from south Arabia — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
