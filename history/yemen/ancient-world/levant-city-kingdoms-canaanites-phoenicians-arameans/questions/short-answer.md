# Levant City Kingdoms: Canaanites, Phoenicians, Arameans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Ancient civilisations in the Levant (3000-323 BCE)”.
**Answer:** the Levant, bridge of three continents and caravan road between Yemen, Mesopotamia and Egypt, fills with city kingdoms built largely by migrants from south Arabia — Canaanites founding Jaffa, Gaza, Jericho and Jerusalem, Amorites, Phoenicians who master the sea trade and give the world a 22-letter alphabet from which Greek, Roman and European letters descend, and Arameans at Damascus — until great-power greed from Assyria, Babylon and Persia, and, in the book's main verdict, their turning from the prophets, bring them down
