# Adam's Two Sons: The First Murder

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Story of Adam's two sons. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Story of Adam's two sons

in the first family on earth the two brothers offer sacrifices, Habil the shepherd his best animal and Qabil the farmer his crops, fire takes Habil's offering and not Qabil's — not for the gift but for the giver's lack of God-consciousness — envy turns to a threat, Habil refuses to raise his hand, Qabil kills his brother in his sleep, and a crow scratching the earth teaches the first murderer how to bury a body

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: in the first family on earth the two brothers offer sacrifices, Habil the shepherd his best animal and Qabil the farmer his crops, fire takes Habil's offering and not…. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Adam's Two Sons: The First Murder. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
