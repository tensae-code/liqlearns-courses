# Adam's Two Sons: The First Murder — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Story of Adam's two sons”?
a) In the first family on earth the two brothers offer sacrifices, Habil the shepherd his best animal and Qabil the farmer his crops, fire takes Habil's…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In the first family on earth the two brothers offer sacrifices, Habil the shepherd his best animal and Qabil the farmer his crops, fire takes Habil's…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Story of Adam's two sons”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In the first family on earth the two brothers offer sacrifices, Habil the shepherd his best animal and Qabil the farmer his crops, fire takes Habil's…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In the first family on earth the two brothers offer sacrifices, Habil the shepherd his best animal and Qabil the farmer his crops, fire takes Habil's…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
