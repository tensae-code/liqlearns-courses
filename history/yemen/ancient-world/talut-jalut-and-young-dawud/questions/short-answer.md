# Talut Jalut and Young Dawud — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Talut and Jalut”.
**Answer:** humbled in the Levant and robbed of the Ark, the Israelites beg their prophet for a king, then recoil when fighting is actually decreed and again when the chosen king, Talut, is a nobody without wealth; his sign is the Ark returning on angels' wings, his test is the river — drink more than a handful and fall away — and the little band that is left faces Jalut's giant army, prays, and watches the youth Dawud kill Jalut and turn the day
