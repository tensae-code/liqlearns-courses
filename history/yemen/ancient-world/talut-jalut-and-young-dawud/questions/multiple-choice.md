# Talut Jalut and Young Dawud — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Talut and Jalut”?
a) Humbled in the Levant and robbed of the Ark, the Israelites beg their prophet for a king, then recoil when fighting is actually decreed and again…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Humbled in the Levant and robbed of the Ark, the Israelites beg their prophet for a king, then recoil when fighting is actually decreed and again…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Talut and Jalut”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Humbled in the Levant and robbed of the Ark, the Israelites beg their prophet for a king, then recoil when fighting is actually decreed and again…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Humbled in the Levant and robbed of the Ark, the Israelites beg their prophet for a king, then recoil when fighting is actually decreed and again…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
