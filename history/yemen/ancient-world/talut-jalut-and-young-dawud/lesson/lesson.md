# Talut Jalut and Young Dawud

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Talut and Jalut. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Talut and Jalut

humbled in the Levant and robbed of the Ark, the Israelites beg their prophet for a king, then recoil when fighting is actually decreed and again when the chosen king, Talut, is a nobody without wealth; his sign is the Ark returning on angels' wings, his test is the river — drink more than a handful and fall away — and the little band that is left faces Jalut's giant army, prays, and watches the youth Dawud kill Jalut and turn the day

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: humbled in the Levant and robbed of the Ark, the Israelites beg their prophet for a king, then recoil when fighting is actually decreed and again when the chosen king…. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Talut Jalut and Young Dawud. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
