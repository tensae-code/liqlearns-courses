# Arabia before Islam States and Tribes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Political conditions of the Arabs before Islam”?
a) Arabia before Islam is a patchwork  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Arabia before Islam is a patchwork** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “States of the north of the Peninsula between the Birth and the Mission”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) While Rome and Persia fight their long war over trade and resources with the Levant and Iraq as the arena, Arab buffer states line the frontier  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) While Rome and Persia fight their long war over trade and resources with the Levant and Iraq as the arena, Arab buffer states line the frontier** — That is what this lesson teaches.
