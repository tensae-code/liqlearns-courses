# Arabia before Islam States and Tribes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Political conditions of the Arabs before Islam”.
**Answer:** Arabia before Islam is a patchwork — Kinda in Najd and the Hijaz, Quraysh in Mecca and the Yemeni tribes Aws and Khazraj in Yathrib, the Lakhmids, Nabataeans and Ghassanids in the north — squeezed between two great neighbours, Persia at al-Mada'in and Byzantium at Constantinople

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “States of the north of the Peninsula between the Birth and the Mission”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** while Rome and Persia fight their long war over trade and resources with the Levant and Iraq as the arena, Arab buffer states line the frontier — the Nabataeans at rock-carved Petra, Yemeni migrants who even fight the Jews settled among them; Palmyra, whose Queen Zenobia seizes Rome's weakness, declares independence and overruns the Levant, Egypt and part of Turkey; Yemeni Kinda at al-Faw guarding central Arabia until internal quarrels fell it; the Ghassanids, Rome's Christian Arab tool in the south of the Levant; and the Lakhmids at al-Hira, Persia's Arab tool on the Euphrates
