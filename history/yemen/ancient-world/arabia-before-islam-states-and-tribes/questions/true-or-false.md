# Arabia before Islam States and Tribes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Arabia before Islam is a patchwork.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “States of the north of the Peninsula between the Birth and the Mission” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: While Rome and Persia fight their long war over trade and resources with the Levant and Iraq as the arena, Arab buffer states line the frontier.
