# Arabia before Islam States and Tribes

## Do it first

Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Political conditions of the Arabs before Islam; States of the north of the Peninsula between the Birth and the Mission. ## Sub lesson 1 — Political conditions of the Arabs before Islam

Arabia before Islam is a patchwork — Kinda in Najd and the Hijaz, Quraysh in Mecca and the Yemeni tribes Aws and Khazraj in Yathrib, the Lakhmids, Nabataeans and Ghassanids in the north — squeezed between two great neighbours, Persia at al-Mada'in and Byzantium at Constantinople

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — States of the north of the Peninsula between the Birth and the Mission

while Rome and Persia fight their long war over trade and resources with the Levant and Iraq as the arena, Arab buffer states line the frontier — the Nabataeans at rock-carved Petra, Yemeni migrants who even fight the Jews settled among them; Palmyra, whose Queen Zenobia seizes Rome's weakness, declares independence and overruns the Levant, Egypt and part of Turkey; Yemeni Kinda at al-Faw guarding central Arabia until internal quarrels fell it; the Ghassanids, Rome's Christian Arab tool in the south of the Levant; and the Lakhmids at al-Hira, Persia's Arab tool on the Euphrates

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
