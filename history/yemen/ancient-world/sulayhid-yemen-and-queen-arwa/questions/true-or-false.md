# Sulayhid Yemen and Queen Arwa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ali bin Muhammad al-Sulayhi, seasoned guarding the Hajj road and preaching the Fatimid call with Cairo's leave, declares himself from Haraz, defeats the Zaydis of Saada….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “The development of women's roles: the ruling queen (Unit 3 Lesson 3, fifth part)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Among the models of women's rising roles.
