# Sulayhid Yemen and Queen Arwa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “The Sulayhid state (439-532 AH)”?
a) Ali bin Muhammad al-Sulayhi, seasoned guarding the Hajj road and preaching the Fatimid call with Cairo's leave, declares himself from Haraz, defeats…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Ali bin Muhammad al-Sulayhi, seasoned guarding the Hajj road and preaching the Fatimid call with Cairo's leave, declares himself from Haraz, defeats…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “The development of women's roles: the ruling queen (Unit 3 Lesson 3, fifth part)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Among the models of women's rising roles  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Among the models of women's rising roles** — That is what this lesson teaches.
