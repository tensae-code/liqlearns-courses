# Musa and Pharaoh Magicians and the Sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh”?
a) Called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands** — That is what this lesson teaches.
