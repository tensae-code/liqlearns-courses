# Musa and Pharaoh Magicians and the Sea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands.
