# Musa and Pharaoh Magicians and the Sea

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh

called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands — worship God alone, and let the Israelites go — Pharaoh stakes everything on a festival-day contest, Musa's staff swallows the magicians' ropes, the magicians believe on the spot and face crucifixion unmoved, plagues of drought, flood, locusts, lice, frogs and blood are promised away and the promises broken, until the night exodus ends at the sea, which splits like a great mountain for Israel and closes over Pharaoh, whose body is preserved as a sign

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Musa and Pharaoh Magicians and the Sea. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson's story echoes in real life. Note it.
