# Musa and Pharaoh Magicians and the Sea — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
