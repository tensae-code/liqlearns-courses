# Banu Najah Zabid

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH)

Najah, an Abyssinian freedman of the Ziyadid chamberlain Marjan, outlives his master, takes Zabid with the Abbasid caliph's blessing and founds a state; his sons Sa'id al-Ahwal and Jayyash trade Zabid back and forth with the Sulayhids — Jayyash even slips away to the Hajj and returns to retake the city — until the last ruler, Fatiq, sunk in pleasure, is killed by the Zaydi Imam Ahmad bin Sulayman
