# Banu Najah Zabid — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH)”.
**Answer:** Najah, an Abyssinian freedman of the Ziyadid chamberlain Marjan, outlives his master, takes Zabid with the Abbasid caliph's blessing and founds a state; his sons Sa'id al-Ahwal and Jayyash trade Zabid back and forth with the Sulayhids — Jayyash even slips away to the Hajj and returns to retake the city — until the last ruler, Fatiq, sunk in pleasure, is killed by the Zaydi Imam Ahmad bin Sulayman

**2.** Who is in the story of “The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
