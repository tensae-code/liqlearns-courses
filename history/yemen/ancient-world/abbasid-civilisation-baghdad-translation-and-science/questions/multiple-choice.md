# Abbasid Civilisation Baghdad Translation and Science — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “In the Abbasid state era”?
a) From Baghdad's founding in 145 AH the civilisation peaks: the wazirate in two kinds, thirteen wilayas, the chief judge, Dar al-Hikma's translators…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) From Baghdad's founding in 145 AH the civilisation peaks: the wazirate in two kinds, thirteen wilayas, the chief judge, Dar al-Hikma's translators…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “In the Abbasid state era”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) From Baghdad's founding in 145 AH the civilisation peaks: the wazirate in two kinds, thirteen wilayas, the chief judge, Dar al-Hikma's translators…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) From Baghdad's founding in 145 AH the civilisation peaks: the wazirate in two kinds, thirteen wilayas, the chief judge, Dar al-Hikma's translators…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
