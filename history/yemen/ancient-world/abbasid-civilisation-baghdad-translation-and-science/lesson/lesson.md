# Abbasid Civilisation Baghdad Translation and Science

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## In the Abbasid state era

from Baghdad's founding in 145 AH the civilisation peaks: the wazirate in two kinds, thirteen wilayas, the chief judge, Dar al-Hikma's translators meeting weekly, hundreds of licensed physicians in Baghdad alone, Jabir's chemistry, al-Khwarizmi's algebra, Ibn al-Haytham's optics, observatories and the round city built on fixed rules of site and plan — then after 232 AH the caliph shrinks to a religious figurehead while Turks, Buyids and Seljuks hold the power, until the Tatars kill al-Musta'sim in 656 AH
