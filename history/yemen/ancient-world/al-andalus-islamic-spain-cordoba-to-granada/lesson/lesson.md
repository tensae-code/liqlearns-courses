## Islamic civilisation in al-Andalus (Unit 5 Lesson 4)

Spain receives the conquerors as liberators from Gothic injustice; Umayyad emirs and caliphs rival Baghdad and Cairo, dhimmis adopt Arabic, and a splendid civilisation rises — Cordoba's mosque and bridge, Madinat al-Zahra, Granada's Alhambra, new crops and paper and silk workshops — taught through scholars from Ibn Hazm and Ibn Tufayl to Ibn Rushd, al-Zahrawi and Ziryab, until Granada falls in 897 AH
