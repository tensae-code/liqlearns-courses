# Social Justice in Islamic Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Social justice in the Arab Islamic civilisation”?
a) Justice is equality in rights and duties regardless of colour, race, creed or lineage: Islam lifts women out of a Jahiliyya that buried daughters…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Justice is equality in rights and duties regardless of colour, race, creed or lineage: Islam lifts women out of a Jahiliyya that buried daughters…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Social justice in the Arab Islamic civilisation”?

**Answer: b) Justice is equality in rights and duties regardless of colour, race, creed or lineage: Islam lifts women out of a Jahiliyya that buried daughters…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
