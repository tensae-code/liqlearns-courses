# Nabataeans and Palmyra Caravan Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Nabataean and Palmyrene civilisations”?
a) Two Arab caravan kingdoms grow rich as middlemen of the desert  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Two Arab caravan kingdoms grow rich as middlemen of the desert** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Nabataean and Palmyrene civilisations”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Two Arab caravan kingdoms grow rich as middlemen of the desert  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Two Arab caravan kingdoms grow rich as middlemen of the desert** — That is what this lesson teaches.
