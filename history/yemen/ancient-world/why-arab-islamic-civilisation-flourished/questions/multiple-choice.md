# Why Arab Islamic Civilisation Flourished — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Causes of the flourishing of the Arab Islamic civilisation”?
a) The civilisation flowers because it refuses to close in on itself: it takes trade, dams, irrigation and poetry from the old Arab civilisations…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The civilisation flowers because it refuses to close in on itself: it takes trade, dams, irrigation and poetry from the old Arab civilisations…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Causes of the flourishing of the Arab Islamic civilisation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The civilisation flowers because it refuses to close in on itself: it takes trade, dams, irrigation and poetry from the old Arab civilisations…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The civilisation flowers because it refuses to close in on itself: it takes trade, dams, irrigation and poetry from the old Arab civilisations…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
