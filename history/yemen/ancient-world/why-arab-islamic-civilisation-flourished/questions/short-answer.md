# Why Arab Islamic Civilisation Flourished — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Causes of the flourishing of the Arab Islamic civilisation”.
**Answer:** the civilisation flowers because it refuses to close in on itself: it takes trade, dams, irrigation and poetry from the old Arab civilisations, administration and architecture from Persia, mathematics and medicine from India, philosophy from Greece, law and road-building from Rome, absorbs the conquered lands' sciences, criticises them and adds its own creations — and lets Muslim, Christian and Jew live and create side by side in tolerance and coexistence
