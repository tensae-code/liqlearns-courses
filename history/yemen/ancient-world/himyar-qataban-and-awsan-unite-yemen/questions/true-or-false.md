# Himyar Qataban and Awsan Unite Yemen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Awsan in Wadi Markha and Qataban in Wadi Bayhan give way to Himyar at Zafar, which unites all Yemen under Shammar Yuhar'ish, king of Saba and Dhu Raydan and Hadramawt….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (the Himyarite age)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In the second Sabaean age, the Himyarite kings of Saba and Dhu Raydan unite Yemen from their capital Zafar.
