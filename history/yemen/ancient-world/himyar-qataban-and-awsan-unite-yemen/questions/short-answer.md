# Himyar Qataban and Awsan Unite Yemen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Himyar, Qataban and Awsan”.
**Answer:** Awsan in Wadi Markha and Qataban in Wadi Bayhan give way to Himyar at Zafar, which unites all Yemen under Shammar Yuhar'ish, king of Saba and Dhu Raydan and Hadramawt and Yamnat, down to Abu Karib As'ad and the last king, Sayf bin Dhi Yazan

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (the Himyarite age)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** in the second Sabaean age, the Himyarite kings of Saba and Dhu Raydan unite Yemen from their capital Zafar — Dhamar Ali Watar annexes Raydan, Shammar Yuhar'ish adds Hadramawt and most of Yemen, Abu Karib As'ad al-Kamil stretches rule the length and breadth of Arabia — until the last king, Yusuf As'ar Yath'ar, called Dhu Nuwas, turns to Judaism, massacres the people of Najran, and brings the Abyssinians down on Yemen with Byzantine ships
