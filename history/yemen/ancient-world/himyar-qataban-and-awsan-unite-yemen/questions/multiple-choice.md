# Himyar Qataban and Awsan Unite Yemen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Himyar, Qataban and Awsan”?
a) Awsan in Wadi Markha and Qataban in Wadi Bayhan give way to Himyar at Zafar, which unites all Yemen under Shammar Yuhar'ish, king of Saba and Dhu…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Awsan in Wadi Markha and Qataban in Wadi Bayhan give way to Himyar at Zafar, which unites all Yemen under Shammar Yuhar'ish, king of Saba and Dhu…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (the Himyarite age)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In the second Sabaean age, the Himyarite kings of Saba and Dhu Raydan unite Yemen from their capital Zafar  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In the second Sabaean age, the Himyarite kings of Saba and Dhu Raydan unite Yemen from their capital Zafar** — That is what this lesson teaches.
