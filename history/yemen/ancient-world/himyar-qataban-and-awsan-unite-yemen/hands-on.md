# Hands-on — Himyar Qataban and Awsan Unite Yemen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Himyar, Qataban and Awsan; States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (the Himyarite age). Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: awsan in Wadi Markha and Qataban in Wadi Bayhan give way to Himyar at Zafar, which unites all Yemen under Shammar Yuhar'ish, king of Saba and Dhu Raydan and Hadramawt….

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: awsan in Wadi Markha and Qataban in Wadi Bayhan give way to Himyar at Zafar, which unites all Yemen under Shammar Yuhar'ish, king of Saba and Dhu Raydan and Hadramawt…. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
