The Roman invasion of Yemen.

The emperor Augustus orders Yemen invaded in 24 BCE with 130 ships and 10,000 soldiers to seize the trade routes and Yemen's wealth, the Romans wreck towns on the road to Marib and besiege it, but Yemeni resistance, control of water and disease in the Roman ranks break the campaign

The Roman civilisation.

Romulus founds Rome in 753 BCE on the Tiber, and the city passes from kings to a republic of consuls, Senate and popular bodies, then to empire under Augustus
