**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “The Roman invasion of Yemen”?
a) The emperor Augustus orders Yemen invaded in 24 BCE with 130 ships and 10,000 soldiers to seize the trade routes and Yemen's wealth, the Romans wreck…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
