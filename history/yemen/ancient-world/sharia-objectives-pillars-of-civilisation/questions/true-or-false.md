# The Sharia's Objectives: Pillars of Civilisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Sharia's universal objectives fix civilisation's pillars at four levels: for the individual, guarding life, mind, religion, honour and property.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Pillars of civilisation in the objectives of the Islamic Sharia” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Sharia's universal objectives fix civilisation's pillars at four levels: for the individual, guarding life, mind, religion, honour and property.
