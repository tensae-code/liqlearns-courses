# The Sharia's Objectives: Pillars of Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Pillars of civilisation in the objectives of the Islamic Sharia”?
a) The Sharia's universal objectives fix civilisation's pillars at four levels: for the individual, guarding life, mind, religion, honour and property  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Sharia's universal objectives fix civilisation's pillars at four levels: for the individual, guarding life, mind, religion, honour and property** — That is what this lesson teaches.
