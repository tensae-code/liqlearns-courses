# North Americas First Peoples Bering to the Tribes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Ancient civilisations in North America”?
a) Mongoloid peoples cross the Bering Strait in prehistory and become the continent's first nations  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Mongoloid peoples cross the Bering Strait in prehistory and become the continent's first nations** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Mongoloid peoples cross the Bering Strait in prehistory and become the continent's first nations  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Mongoloid peoples cross the Bering Strait in prehistory and become the continent's first nations** — That is what this lesson teaches.
