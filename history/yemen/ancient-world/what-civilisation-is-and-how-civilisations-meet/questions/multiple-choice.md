# What Civilisation Is, and How Civilisations Meet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Civilisational and cultural concepts”?
a) Civilisation is a people's sum of religious, intellectual and material achievements marked by creativity, continuity and influence, culture the…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Civilisation is a people's sum of religious, intellectual and material achievements marked by creativity, continuity and influence, culture the…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Civilisational and cultural concepts”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Civilisation is a people's sum of religious, intellectual and material achievements marked by creativity, continuity and influence, culture the…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
