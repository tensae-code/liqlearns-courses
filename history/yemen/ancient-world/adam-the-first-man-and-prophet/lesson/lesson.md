# Adam the First Man and Prophet

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God; Abu al-Bashar Adam. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God

God creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all peoples spread across the earth

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Abu al-Bashar Adam

God announces a deputy on earth, teaches Adam the names of all things while the angels confess they know only what they were taught, Iblis alone refuses to bow and is expelled, Adam and Eve live a while in a temporary garden, eat of the one forbidden tree at Satan's false oath, repent and are forgiven, and come down to earth with Satan as man's permanent enemy

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Adam the First Man and Prophet. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
