# Adam the First Man and Prophet — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God”.
**Answer:** God creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all peoples spread across the earth

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Abu al-Bashar Adam”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** God announces a deputy on earth, teaches Adam the names of all things while the angels confess they know only what they were taught, Iblis alone refuses to bow and is expelled, Adam and Eve live a while in a temporary garden, eat of the one forbidden tree at Satan's false oath, repent and are forgiven, and come down to earth with Satan as man's permanent enemy
