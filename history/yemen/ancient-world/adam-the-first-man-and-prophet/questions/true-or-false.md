# Adam the First Man and Prophet — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) God creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all peoples spread across the….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Abu al-Bashar Adam” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: God announces a deputy on earth, teaches Adam the names of all things while the angels confess they know only what they were taught, Iblis alone….
