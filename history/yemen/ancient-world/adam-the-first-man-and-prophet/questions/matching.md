# Adam the First Man and Prophet — matching

Match each name with its meaning.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Abu al-Bashar Adam — b)


Meanings:
a) God creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all…
b) God announces a deputy on earth, teaches Adam the names of all things while the angels confess they know only what they were taught, Iblis alone…

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
