# Adam the First Man and Prophet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God”?
a) God creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) God creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Abu al-Bashar Adam”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) God announces a deputy on earth, teaches Adam the names of all things while the angels confess they know only what they were taught, Iblis alone…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) God announces a deputy on earth, teaches Adam the names of all things while the angels confess they know only what they were taught, Iblis alone…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
