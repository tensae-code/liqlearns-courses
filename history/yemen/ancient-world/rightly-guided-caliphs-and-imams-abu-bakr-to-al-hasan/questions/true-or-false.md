# The Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams: Abu Bakr to al-Hasan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The four caliphs are chosen four different ways.
