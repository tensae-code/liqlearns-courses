# The Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams: Abu Bakr to al-Hasan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams”?
a) Abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The four caliphs are chosen four different ways  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The four caliphs are chosen four different ways** — That is what this lesson teaches.
