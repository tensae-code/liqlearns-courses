# The Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams: Abu Bakr to al-Hasan

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams; In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams

Abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH; Umar, named by him, founds Kufa and Fustat and the register of stipends before Abu Lu'lu'a kills him in 23 AH; Uthman, chosen by a council of six, builds the first fleet and is killed in his besieged house in 35 AH; Ali fights at the Camel, Siffin and Nahrawan and falls to Ibn Muljam in Ramadan 40 AH; and Hasan makes peace in 41 AH, is poisoned, in the book's telling, at Muawiya's instigation, clearing the way for Husayn's stand against Yazid

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half)

the four caliphs are chosen four different ways — Saqifa bay'a, designation, a council of six, emergency choice — and between 11 and 40 AH they gather the Qur'an into one mushaf, found Basra, Kufa and Fustat with Yemenis sharing in the planning, create the diwans of army, land-tax and stipends, the night police and the first fleet that beats Byzantium at Dhat al-Sawari, and dig the canal joining the Nile to the Red Sea

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
