# Noah and the Flood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Prophet of God Noah”?
a) Noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “People of the prophet Noah”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
