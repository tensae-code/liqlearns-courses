# Noah and the Flood — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Prophet of God Noah”.
**Answer:** Noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns even his unbelieving son, and the ark settles on Mount al-Judi where a new humanity begins from Noah's offspring

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “People of the prophet Noah”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah preaches 950 years against chiefs who lead the rejection, drought and barrenness strike first, then he builds the ark to heaven's own measurements — a leap in shipbuilding — loads pairs of every animal, watches his own son choose a mountain and drown, and lands on al-Judi to start mankind again
