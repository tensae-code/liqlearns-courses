# Noah and the Flood — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “People of the prophet Noah” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah….
