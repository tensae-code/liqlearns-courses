# Video script — Noah and the Flood

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

Prophet of God Noah.

Noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns even his unbelieving son, and the ark settles on Mount al-Judi where a new humanity begins from Noah's offspring

People of the prophet Noah.

Generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah preaches 950 years against chiefs who lead the rejection, drought and barrenness strike first, then he builds the ark to heaven's own measurements

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
