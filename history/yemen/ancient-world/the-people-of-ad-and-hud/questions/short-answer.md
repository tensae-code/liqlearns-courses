# The People of 'Ad and Hud — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The people of 'Ad”.
**Answer:** after the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and injustice, they refuse, and after drought a barren wind blows seven nights and eight days and leaves them like hollow palm trunks while Hud and the believers are saved

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “The civilisation of 'Ad”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** 'Ad, the first strong nation after the flood, rooted in al-Ahqaf in the south of Arabia, are blessed with mighty bodies, workers, herds, farms, the city of Iram and a feared army, their own prophet Hud warns them and the chiefs mock him, drought comes first and then the barren wind for seven nights and eight days, and Hud and the believers walk away to build again
