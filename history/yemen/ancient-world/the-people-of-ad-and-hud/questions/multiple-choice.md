# The People of 'Ad and Hud — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “The people of 'Ad”?
a) After the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) After the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “The civilisation of 'Ad”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) 'Ad, the first strong nation after the flood, rooted in al-Ahqaf in the south of Arabia, are blessed with mighty bodies, workers, herds, farms, the…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) 'Ad, the first strong nation after the flood, rooted in al-Ahqaf in the south of Arabia, are blessed with mighty bodies, workers, herds, farms, the…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
