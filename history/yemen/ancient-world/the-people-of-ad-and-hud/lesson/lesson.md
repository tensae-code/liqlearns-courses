# The People of 'Ad and Hud

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The people of 'Ad; The civilisation of 'Ad. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The people of 'Ad

after the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and injustice, they refuse, and after drought a barren wind blows seven nights and eight days and leaves them like hollow palm trunks while Hud and the believers are saved

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The civilisation of 'Ad

'Ad, the first strong nation after the flood, rooted in al-Ahqaf in the south of Arabia, are blessed with mighty bodies, workers, herds, farms, the city of Iram and a feared army, their own prophet Hud warns them and the chiefs mock him, drought comes first and then the barren wind for seven nights and eight days, and Hud and the believers walk away to build again

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
