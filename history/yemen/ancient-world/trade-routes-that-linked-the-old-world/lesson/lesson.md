## Sub lesson 1 — Causes of foreign ambitions in Yemen

Yemen sits in the south of Arabia between the Red Sea, the Arabian Sea and the Indian Ocean astride the trade route between Asia, Africa and Europe, and its frankincense, myrrh, gold and silver make empire after empire covet it
