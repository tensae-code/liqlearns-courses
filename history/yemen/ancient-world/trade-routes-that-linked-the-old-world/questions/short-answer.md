# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Causes of foreign ambitions in Yemen”.
**Answer:** Yemen sits in the south of Arabia between the Red Sea, the Arabian Sea and the Indian Ocean astride the trade route between Asia, Africa and Europe, and its frankincense, myrrh, gold and silver make empire after empire covet it

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Trade and industry in the Islamic state”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** one vast secure state joins the world's land and sea trade roads, frees merchants from customs between its provinces, and its crafts flourish on the science behind them — swords, glass, pottery, medicines, jewellery, leather, carpets and ships
