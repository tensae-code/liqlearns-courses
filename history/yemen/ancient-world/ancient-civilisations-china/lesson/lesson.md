# Ancient Civilisations: China

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Chinese civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Chinese civilisation

along the Yellow River and Yangtze, farmers dig swamps, plant rice and tea for the first time in history, and breed the silkworm whose silk names a road across the world; Confucius (551–479 BCE) teaches honour of ancestors and learning for all, Laozi points the Tao, Buddhism arrives from India to become a state religion, and China's inventors give the world ink, printing, the compass, gunpowder, the earthquake machine and a 12-month calendar — while Qin unifies the land in 221 BCE and the Great Wall grows some 3,000 km against the north
