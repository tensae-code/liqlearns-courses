# Ancient Civilisations: China — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Chinese civilisation”.
**Answer:** along the Yellow River and Yangtze, farmers dig swamps, plant rice and tea for the first time in history, and breed the silkworm whose silk names a road across the world; Confucius (551–479 BCE) teaches honour of ancestors and learning for all, Laozi points the Tao, Buddhism arrives from India to become a state religion, and China's inventors give the world ink, printing, the compass, gunpowder, the earthquake machine and a 12-month calendar — while Qin unifies the land in 221 BCE and the Great Wall grows some 3,000 km against the north
