# Ancient Civilisations: China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Chinese civilisation”?
a) Along the Yellow River and Yangtze, farmers dig swamps, plant rice and tea for the first time in history, and breed the silkworm whose silk names a…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Along the Yellow River and Yangtze, farmers dig swamps, plant rice and tea for the first time in history, and breed the silkworm whose silk names a…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
