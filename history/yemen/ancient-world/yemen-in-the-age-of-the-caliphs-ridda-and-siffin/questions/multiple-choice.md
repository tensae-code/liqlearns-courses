# Yemen in the Age of the Caliphs: Ridda and Siffin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Yemen in the age of the Caliphs and Imams”?
a) The false prophet al-Aswad al-Ansi gathers Madhhij and other tribes in Yemen, Abu Bakr sends three armies and al-Aswad is killed, Yemen is reunited…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The false prophet al-Aswad al-Ansi gathers Madhhij and other tribes in Yemen, Abu Bakr sends three armies and al-Aswad is killed, Yemen is reunited…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Yemen in the age of the Caliphs and Imams”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The false prophet al-Aswad al-Ansi gathers Madhhij and other tribes in Yemen, Abu Bakr sends three armies and al-Aswad is killed, Yemen is reunited…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The false prophet al-Aswad al-Ansi gathers Madhhij and other tribes in Yemen, Abu Bakr sends three armies and al-Aswad is killed, Yemen is reunited…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
