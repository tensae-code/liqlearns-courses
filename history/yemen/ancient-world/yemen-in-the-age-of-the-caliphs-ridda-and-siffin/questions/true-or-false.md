# Yemen in the Age of the Caliphs: Ridda and Siffin — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The false prophet al-Aswad al-Ansi gathers Madhhij and other tribes in Yemen, Abu Bakr sends three armies and al-Aswad is killed, Yemen is reunited and Yemeni leaders….
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Yemen in the age of the Caliphs and Imams” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The false prophet al-Aswad al-Ansi gathers Madhhij and other tribes in Yemen, Abu Bakr sends three armies and al-Aswad is killed, Yemen is reunited….
