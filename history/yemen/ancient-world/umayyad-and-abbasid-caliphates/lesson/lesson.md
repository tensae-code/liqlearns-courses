# The Umayyad and Abbasid Caliphates

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Umayyad age. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Umayyad age

Muawiya, governor of Syria under Umar, turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship after Hasan's peace, names his son Yazid, and the line runs through Abd al-Malik, al-Walid, the just Umar bin Abd al-Aziz and Hisham, in whose reign Imam Zayd bin Ali rises and is killed; the state stretches from Sind and the Caspian to the Atlantic, al-Andalus and southern France, then falls in 132 AH, undone, in the book's telling, by injustice that led to Husayn's revolution at Karbala in 61 AH
