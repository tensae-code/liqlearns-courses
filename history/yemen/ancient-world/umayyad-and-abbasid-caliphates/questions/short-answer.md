# The Umayyad and Abbasid Caliphates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Umayyad age”.
**Answer:** Muawiya, governor of Syria under Umar, turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship after Hasan's peace, names his son Yazid, and the line runs through Abd al-Malik, al-Walid, the just Umar bin Abd al-Aziz and Hisham, in whose reign Imam Zayd bin Ali rises and is killed; the state stretches from Sind and the Caspian to the Atlantic, al-Andalus and southern France, then falls in 132 AH, undone, in the book's telling, by injustice that led to Husayn's revolution at Karbala in 61 AH
