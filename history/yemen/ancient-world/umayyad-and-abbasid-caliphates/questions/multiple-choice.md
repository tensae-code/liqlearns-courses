# The Umayyad and Abbasid Caliphates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Umayyad age”?
a) Muawiya, governor of Syria under Umar, turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship after Hasan's peace, names his son Yazid, and the line runs…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
