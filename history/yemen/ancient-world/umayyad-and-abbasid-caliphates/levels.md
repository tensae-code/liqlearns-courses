# The Umayyad and Abbasid Caliphates — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — muawiya, governor of Syria under Umar, turns the caliphate into a hereditary kingship after Hasan's peace, names his son Yazid, and the line runs through Abd al-Malik… — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
