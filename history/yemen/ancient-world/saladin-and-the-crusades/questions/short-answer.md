# Saladin and the Crusades — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Ayyubid state (564-648 AH)”.
**Answer:** Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria after Nur al-Din's death in 569 AH, crushes the Crusaders at Hattin in 583 AH and frees Jerusalem that Rajab after some ninety years — while his brothers Turan Shah and Tughtakin carry Ayyubid rule into Yemen, where Imam Abd Allah bin Hamza resists them for the 58 years their rule lasts there
