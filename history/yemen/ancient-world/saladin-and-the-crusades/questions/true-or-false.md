# Saladin and the Crusades — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria after Nur al-Din's….
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Ayyubid state (564-648 AH)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria….
