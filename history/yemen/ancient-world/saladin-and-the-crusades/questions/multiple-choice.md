# Saladin and the Crusades — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Ayyubid state (564-648 AH)”?
a) Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Ayyubid state (564-648 AH)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
