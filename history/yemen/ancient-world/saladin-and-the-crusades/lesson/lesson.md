# Saladin and the Crusades

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Ayyubid state (564-648 AH). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Ayyubid state (564-648 AH)

Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria after Nur al-Din's death in 569 AH, crushes the Crusaders at Hattin in 583 AH and frees Jerusalem that Rajab after some ninety years — while his brothers Turan Shah and Tughtakin carry Ayyubid rule into Yemen, where Imam Abd Allah bin Hamza resists them for the 58 years their rule lasts there
