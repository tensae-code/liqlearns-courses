The Ayyubid state (564-648 AH).

Salah al-Din Yusuf bin Ayyub, a Kurd born at Tikrit in 532 AH, goes to Egypt as Nur al-Din's man, ends the Fatimid caliphate, unites Egypt and Syria after Nur al-Din's death in 569 AH, crushes the Crusaders at Hattin in 583 AH and frees Jerusalem that Rajab after some ninety years

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
