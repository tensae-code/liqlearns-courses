# Umayyad Civilisation Hereditary Caliphate and Arabisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “In the Umayyad state era”.
**Answer:** Mu'awiya turns shura-rule into hereditary kingship and moves the capital to Damascus; in 41–132 AH the state Arabises its coinage and its diwans, builds the Dome of the Rock, al-Aqsa and the Umayyad Mosque, raises the first Damascus bimaristan with wards, pharmacy and music for the mentally ill, conscripts a five-part army and a great Levantine fleet, and opens chemistry with Khalid ibn Yazid and history-writing with Yemen's Ubayd ibn Sharya and Wahb ibn Munabbih
