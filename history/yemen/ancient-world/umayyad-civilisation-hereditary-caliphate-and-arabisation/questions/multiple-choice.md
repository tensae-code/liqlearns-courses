# Umayyad Civilisation Hereditary Caliphate and Arabisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “In the Umayyad state era”?
a) Mu'awiya turns shura-rule into hereditary kingship and moves the capital to Damascus  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Mu'awiya turns shura-rule into hereditary kingship and moves the capital to Damascus** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “In the Umayyad state era”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Mu'awiya turns shura-rule into hereditary kingship and moves the capital to Damascus  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Mu'awiya turns shura-rule into hereditary kingship and moves the capital to Damascus** — That is what this lesson teaches.
