# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Persian civilisation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Persian civilisation

Aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE; Darius organises it into satrapies watched by royal inspectors and a three-part army, Marathon (490 BCE) and Salamis (480 BCE) check it, and Alexander ends it in 330 BCE before the Parthians free it again; Zarathustra's faith sets good and evil in permanent struggle and leaves man to choose his path, later joined by Mani's and Mazdak's teachings, until Persia embraces Islam
