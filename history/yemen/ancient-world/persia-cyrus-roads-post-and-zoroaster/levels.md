# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
