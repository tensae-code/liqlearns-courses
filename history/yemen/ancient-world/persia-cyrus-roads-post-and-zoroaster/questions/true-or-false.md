# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Persian civilisation” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE.
