# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Persian civilisation”.
**Answer:** Aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE; Darius organises it into satrapies watched by royal inspectors and a three-part army, Marathon (490 BCE) and Salamis (480 BCE) check it, and Alexander ends it in 330 BCE before the Parthians free it again; Zarathustra's faith sets good and evil in permanent struggle and leaves man to choose his path, later joined by Mani's and Mazdak's teachings, until Persia embraces Islam
