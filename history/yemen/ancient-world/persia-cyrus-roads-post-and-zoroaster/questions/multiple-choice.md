# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Persian civilisation”?
a) Aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Persian civilisation”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Aryan tribes settle the Iranian plateau, Media rises at Ecbatana, then Cyrus founds the Persian empire, taking Lydia in 546 BCE and Babylon in 539 BCE** — That is what this lesson teaches.
