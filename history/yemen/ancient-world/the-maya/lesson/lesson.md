# The Maya

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Maya. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Maya

the Maya never build one strong central state — their cities stay independent under hereditary kings and only sometimes unite, then fall apart again — yet their priests keep hundreds of books of history, astronomy and mathematics in a picture-writing like Egypt's hieroglyphs, predict eclipses, and run a calendar of eighteen 20-day months plus five days, 365 in all; their builders raise stepped pyramids crowned with temples from forest timber and mountain stone, earning the peoples of the two Americas the historians' name "Pharaohs of America"
