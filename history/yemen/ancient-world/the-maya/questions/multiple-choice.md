# The Maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Maya”?
a) The Maya never build one strong central state  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Maya never build one strong central state** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Maya”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The Maya never build one strong central state  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The Maya never build one strong central state** — That is what this lesson teaches.
