# The Maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Maya”.
**Answer:** the Maya never build one strong central state — their cities stay independent under hereditary kings and only sometimes unite, then fall apart again — yet their priests keep hundreds of books of history, astronomy and mathematics in a picture-writing like Egypt's hieroglyphs, predict eclipses, and run a calendar of eighteen 20-day months plus five days, 365 in all; their builders raise stepped pyramids crowned with temples from forest timber and mountain stone, earning the peoples of the two Americas the historians' name "Pharaohs of America"
