# Yemen Under Umayyad and Abbasid Governors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Yemen in the Umayyad and Abbasid ages”?
a) Yemen gets mostly harsh governors from both dynasties and answers with revolts  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yemen gets mostly harsh governors from both dynasties and answers with revolts** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Yemen in the Umayyad and Abbasid ages”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Yemen gets mostly harsh governors from both dynasties and answers with revolts  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Yemen gets mostly harsh governors from both dynasties and answers with revolts** — That is what this lesson teaches.
