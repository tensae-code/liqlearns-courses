# Yemen Under Umayyad and Abbasid Governors — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Yemen in the Umayyad and Abbasid ages”.
**Answer:** Yemen gets mostly harsh governors from both dynasties and answers with revolts — the Tawwabun rising for Husayn after Karbala among them — while Yemeni scholars shine inside and outside the country, Malik bin Anas al-Asbahi, Abd al-Razzaq al-San'ani and Ma'mar bin Rashid, and al-Shafi'i himself studies in Yemen
