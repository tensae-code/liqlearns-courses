## Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Inca

in the Andes the Inca state holds all land, minerals and herds, moves people from crowded valleys to empty ones, feeds the hungry from public stores in famine years, and divides the fields three ways — the state's (the largest), the temples', and each family's plot, re-measured yearly by family size; farmers terrace the mountainsides and fertilise with guano, smiths mix copper and tin into bronze and beat gold foil over silver, and Inca engineers fit huge stones so exactly, without mortar, that their temples and fortresses still stand through earthquakes on roads served by travellers' resthouses
