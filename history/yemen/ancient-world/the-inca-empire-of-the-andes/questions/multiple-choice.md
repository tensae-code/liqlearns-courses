# The Inca Empire of the Andes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Inca”?
a) In the Andes the Inca state holds all land, minerals and herds, moves people from crowded valleys to empty ones, feeds the hungry from public stores…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In the Andes the Inca state holds all land, minerals and herds, moves people from crowded valleys to empty ones, feeds the hungry from public stores…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Ancient civilisations in Central and South America: the Inca”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In the Andes the Inca state holds all land, minerals and herds, moves people from crowded valleys to empty ones, feeds the hungry from public stores…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In the Andes the Inca state holds all land, minerals and herds, moves people from crowded valleys to empty ones, feeds the hungry from public stores…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
