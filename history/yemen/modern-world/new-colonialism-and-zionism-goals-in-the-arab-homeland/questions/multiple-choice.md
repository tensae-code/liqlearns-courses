# New Colonialism and Zionism Goals in the Arab Homeland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Goals of the new colonialism and Zionism in the Arab homeland (Unit 6 Lesson 2)”?
a) Direct rule gives way to rule by loans, the World Bank and the IMF, multinational companies and media  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Direct rule gives way to rule by loans, the World Bank and the IMF, multinational companies and media** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Goals of the new colonialism and Zionism in the Arab homeland (Unit 6 Lesson 2)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Direct rule gives way to rule by loans, the World Bank and the IMF, multinational companies and media  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Direct rule gives way to rule by loans, the World Bank and the IMF, multinational companies and media** — That is what this lesson teaches.
