# The Second World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Second World War: causes (Unit 4 Lesson 2)”?
a) The humiliating peace of 1919 plants the next war  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The humiliating peace of 1919 plants the next war** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Second World War: causes (Unit 4 Lesson 2)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The humiliating peace of 1919 plants the next war  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The humiliating peace of 1919 plants the next war** — That is what this lesson teaches.
