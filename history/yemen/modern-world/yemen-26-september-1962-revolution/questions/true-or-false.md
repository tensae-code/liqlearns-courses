# Yemen: The 26 September 1962 Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961.
