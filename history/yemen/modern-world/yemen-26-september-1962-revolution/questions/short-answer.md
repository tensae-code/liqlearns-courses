# Yemen: The 26 September 1962 Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Revolution of 26 September”.
**Answer:** Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes the Yemen Arab Republic with goals of justice, a national army and a democratic cooperative society

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** the Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961; on 26 September 1962 the free officers, backed by the people's masses, strike the Imamate down forever and raise the republic on its six goals
