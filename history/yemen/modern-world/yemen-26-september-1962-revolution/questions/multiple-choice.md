# Yemen: The 26 September 1962 Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Revolution of 26 September”?
a) Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961** — That is what this lesson teaches.
