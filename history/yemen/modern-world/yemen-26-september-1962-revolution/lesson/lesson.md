# Yemen: The 26 September 1962 Revolution

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Revolution of 26 September; The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Revolution of 26 September

Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes the Yemen Arab Republic with goals of justice, a national army and a democratic cooperative society

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4)

the Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961; on 26 September 1962 the free officers, backed by the people's masses, strike the Imamate down forever and raise the republic on its six goals

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
