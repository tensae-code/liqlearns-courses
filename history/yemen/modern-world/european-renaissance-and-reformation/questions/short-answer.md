# European Renaissance and Reformation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The modern European Renaissance”.
**Answer:** Italy, living next door to the flourishing Arab civilisation of Spain, Sicily and the Levant, wakes first: Constantinople's scholars flee west in 1453 with their manuscripts, rich trading cities pay for art and translation from Arabic, paper and printing spread the new learning, and over three centuries Europe climbs out of its middle ages — Luther attacks the sale of indulgences and the clergy's monopoly on the Gospel, kings gather power into national monarchies, and the humanities, Greek science and the Muslim method of doubt, observation and experiment remake the mind of a continent

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Who is in the story of “Manifestations of the modern European renaissance (Unit 1 Lesson 1)”, where does it happen, and what turns?
**Answer:** Europe climbs out of its medieval darkness over three centuries, drawing on the Arab Islamic civilisation through al-Andalus, Sicily and Iraq; printing breaks the clergy's grip on knowledge, feudalism dissolves as cities and banks rise, national kingdoms form, Luther splits the Church, and gunpowder armies replace the feudal levy
