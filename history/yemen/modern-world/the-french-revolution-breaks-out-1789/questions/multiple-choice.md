# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The French Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 1)”?
a) Louis XVI's absolute monarchy taxes the Third Estate and starves the treasury  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Louis XVI's absolute monarchy taxes the Third Estate and starves the treasury** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The French Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 1)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Louis XVI's absolute monarchy taxes the Third Estate and starves the treasury  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Louis XVI's absolute monarchy taxes the Third Estate and starves the treasury** — That is what this lesson teaches.
