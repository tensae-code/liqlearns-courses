# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The French Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 1)”.
**Answer:** Louis XVI's absolute monarchy taxes the Third Estate and starves the treasury; Montesquieu, Voltaire and Rousseau arm the minds, Paris storms the Bastille on 14 July 1789 and proclaims the Rights of Man; the republic guillotines the king, Robespierre's terror frightens the bourgeoisie into Napoleon's 1799 coup — and the revolution's principles ride to the world on his bayonets
