# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The French Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 1). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The French Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 1)

Louis XVI's absolute monarchy taxes the Third Estate and starves the treasury; Montesquieu, Voltaire and Rousseau arm the minds, Paris storms the Bastille on 14 July 1789 and proclaims the Rights of Man; the republic guillotines the king, Robespierre's terror frightens the bourgeoisie into Napoleon's 1799 coup — and the revolution's principles ride to the world on his bayonets
