# European Geographical Discoveries: Henry to Magellan

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The European geographical discoveries. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The European geographical discoveries

armed with Arab navigation science, the compass, the astrolabe and Islamic maps, and hungry to reach India and China without Muslim middlemen and their prices, Portugal and Spain sail out — Henry the Navigator feeling down Africa's coast, Dias rounding the Cape of Storms, da Gama reaching Calicut, Columbus crossing to a world he dies believing is India, Vespucci's name given to the Americas, Magellan's men completing the circle of the earth the Muslims had already proved on paper — followed by Cabot, Cook and the French and Dutch, until discovery hardens into colonisation, the slave trade, and the wiping out of whole peoples
