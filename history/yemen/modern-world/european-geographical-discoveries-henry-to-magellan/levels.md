# European Geographical Discoveries: Henry to Magellan — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — armed with Arab navigation science, the compass, the astrolabe and Islamic maps, and hungry to reach India and China without Muslim middlemen and their prices, Portugal… — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
