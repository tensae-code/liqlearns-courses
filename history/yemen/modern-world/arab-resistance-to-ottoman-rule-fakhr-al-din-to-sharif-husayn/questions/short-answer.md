# Arab Resistance to Ottoman Rule: Fakhr al-Din to Sharif Husayn — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Arab resistance to Ottoman rule”.
**Answer:** four centuries of Ottoman government — walis rotated yearly and milking their provinces, craftsmen deported to Istanbul, taxes doubled, thought frozen — breed a chain of Arab risings the book tabulates: Fakhr al-Din al-Ma'ni in Lebanon, executed at Istanbul in 1635; Zahir al-Umar in Palestine, destroyed at Acre in 1775; Ali Bey al-Kabir declaring Egypt independent in 1769 and undone by his own son-in-law's betrayal; the Saudi-Wahhabi movement in Najd from 1744, crushed at Diriyah after seventy-four years by Muhammad Ali's armies; and the rising national feeling, sharpened by Turkification, that ends in Sharif Husayn's Arab Revolt of 1916
