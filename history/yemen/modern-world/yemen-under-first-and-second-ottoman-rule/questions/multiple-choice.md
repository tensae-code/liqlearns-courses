# Yemen Under First and Second Ottoman Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Stages of Ottoman rule in Yemen”?
a) Yemen matters to Istanbul for its grip on the trade routes, the keys of the southern seas and Socotra, its coffee, and its place as the forward…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yemen matters to Istanbul for its grip on the trade routes, the keys of the southern seas and Socotra, its coffee, and its place as the forward…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Stages of Ottoman rule in Yemen”?

**Answer: b) Yemen matters to Istanbul for its grip on the trade routes, the keys of the southern seas and Socotra, its coffee, and its place as the forward…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
