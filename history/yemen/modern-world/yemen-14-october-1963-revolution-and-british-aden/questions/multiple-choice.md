# Yemen: The 14 October 1963 Revolution and British Aden — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Revolution of 14 October”?
a) Britain seizes Aden on 19 January 1839 and splits the south into sultanates and protectorates to rule it, until the first spark flies from the Radfan…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Britain seizes Aden on 19 January 1839 and splits the south into sultanates and protectorates to rule it, until the first spark flies from the Radfan…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “British ambitions in Yemen”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Britain, holding India  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Britain, holding India** — That is what this lesson teaches.
