# The Second World War: Course in Europe

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Second World War: course and results (Unit 4 Lesson 2). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Second World War: course and results (Unit 4 Lesson 2)

Poland, Denmark, Norway and France fall; Japan's blow at Pearl Harbor makes the war truly world-wide; El Alamein, Stalingrad and Normandy turn the tide, Germany surrenders on 7 May 1945, and America ends the war with atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki — some 40 million dead, the world split into two camps, and the Arabs emerge from foreign occupation only to fall under the new colonialism
