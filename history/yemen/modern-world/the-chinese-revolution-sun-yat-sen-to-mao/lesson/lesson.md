# The Chinese Revolution Sun Yat Sen to Mao

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Chinese Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 3). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Chinese Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 3)

Sun Yat-sen's republic collapses into civil war; the communists, crushed in the south, survive the Long March under Mao; Japan's invasion exhausts the Nationalists while the communists win the farmers with land; in 1949 Mao proclaims the People's Republic, Chiang flees to Taiwan, and the Great Leap and the Cultural Revolution follow before the 1970 turn to reform
