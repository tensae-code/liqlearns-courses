# The Chinese Revolution Sun Yat Sen to Mao — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Chinese Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 3)”?
a) Sun Yat-sen's republic collapses into civil war  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Sun Yat-sen's republic collapses into civil war** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Chinese Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 3)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Sun Yat-sen's republic collapses into civil war  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Sun Yat-sen's republic collapses into civil war** — That is what this lesson teaches.
