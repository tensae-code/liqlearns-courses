# The Chinese Revolution Sun Yat Sen to Mao — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Chinese Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 3)”.
**Answer:** Sun Yat-sen's republic collapses into civil war; the communists, crushed in the south, survive the Long March under Mao; Japan's invasion exhausts the Nationalists while the communists win the farmers with land; in 1949 Mao proclaims the People's Republic, Chiang flees to Taiwan, and the Great Leap and the Cultural Revolution follow before the 1970 turn to reform
