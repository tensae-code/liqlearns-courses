# The European Union: Coal and Steel to Twenty-Seven — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The European Union (Unit 3 Lesson 3)”.
**Answer:** Churchill's 1946 call for a United States of Europe becomes the Coal and Steel Community of 1951 and the Rome Treaty of 1957; enlargement wave follows wave — Britain in 1973, Greece, Spain and Portugal, a reunified Germany, ten states in 2004, Bulgaria and Romania in 2007 — until the EU stands as the world's biggest economic force of 27 members
