# The European Union: Coal and Steel to Twenty-Seven — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Churchill's 1946 call for a United States of Europe becomes the Coal and Steel Community of 1951 and the Rome Treaty of 1957.
