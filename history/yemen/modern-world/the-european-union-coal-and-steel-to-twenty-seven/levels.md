# The European Union: Coal and Steel to Twenty-Seven — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — churchill's 1946 call for a United States of Europe becomes the Coal and Steel Community of 1951 and the Rome Treaty of 1957 — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
