# The Cold War: What It Was and What It Did to Africa — short answer

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Cold War (Unit 4 Lesson 3)”.
**Answer:** the wartime allies split into a Soviet communist camp and an American capitalist camp that fight without fighting — by proxy in Korea and Vietnam, by the Cuban missile crisis, by an arms race to the atomic bomb — while the Arab world is pulled between the two cultures and the Non-Aligned Movement tries to stand aside, until the Soviet camp collapses in the early 1990s and one pole remains
