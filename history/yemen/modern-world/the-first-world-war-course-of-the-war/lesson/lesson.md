# The First World War: Course of the War

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The First World War: course and results (Unit 4 Lesson 1). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The First World War: course and results (Unit 4 Lesson 1)

Germany storms Belgium and stalls in three years of trenches; Russia collapses into revolution and signs away its front in 1917, America lands in France, and on 11 November 1918 the armistice ends a war that buries four empires — while in the Arab homeland its secret harvest is Sykes-Picot, the Balfour Declaration and the San Remo mandates
