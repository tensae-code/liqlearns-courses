# The First World War: Course of the War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The First World War: course and results (Unit 4 Lesson 1)”?
a) Germany storms Belgium and stalls in three years of trenches  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Germany storms Belgium and stalls in three years of trenches** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The First World War: course and results (Unit 4 Lesson 1)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Germany storms Belgium and stalls in three years of trenches  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Germany storms Belgium and stalls in three years of trenches** — That is what this lesson teaches.
