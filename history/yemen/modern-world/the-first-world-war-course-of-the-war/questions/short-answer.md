# The First World War: Course of the War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The First World War: course and results (Unit 4 Lesson 1)”.
**Answer:** Germany storms Belgium and stalls in three years of trenches; Russia collapses into revolution and signs away its front in 1917, America lands in France, and on 11 November 1918 the armistice ends a war that buries four empires — while in the Arab homeland its secret harvest is Sykes-Picot, the Balfour Declaration and the San Remo mandates
