# Foreign Powers Compete for Yemen Coffee and Ports — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Competition of foreign powers over Yemen”?
a) For four centuries Yemen's position makes it a prize fought over in turn  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) For four centuries Yemen's position makes it a prize fought over in turn** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Competition of foreign powers over Yemen”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) For four centuries Yemen's position makes it a prize fought over in turn  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) For four centuries Yemen's position makes it a prize fought over in turn** — That is what this lesson teaches.
