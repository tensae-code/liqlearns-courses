# Yemen: The 21 September 2014 Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Revolution of 21 September 2014”?
a) As taught in this book, crowds backed by scholars, tribal men, soldiers and students rise on 21 September 2014 to correct the path of the earlier…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) As taught in this book, crowds backed by scholars, tribal men, soldiers and students rise on 21 September 2014 to correct the path of the earlier…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Revolution of 21 September 2014”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) As taught in this book, crowds backed by scholars, tribal men, soldiers and students rise on 21 September 2014 to correct the path of the earlier…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) As taught in this book, crowds backed by scholars, tribal men, soldiers and students rise on 21 September 2014 to correct the path of the earlier…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
