# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The American Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 2)”?
a) Britain's thirteen colonies, taxed to pay for the Seven Years' War and forbidden to trade except with Britain, meet at Philadelphia, declare in July…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Britain's thirteen colonies, taxed to pay for the Seven Years' War and forbidden to trade except with Britain, meet at Philadelphia, declare in July…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The American Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 2)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Britain's thirteen colonies, taxed to pay for the Seven Years' War and forbidden to trade except with Britain, meet at Philadelphia, declare in July…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Britain's thirteen colonies, taxed to pay for the Seven Years' War and forbidden to trade except with Britain, meet at Philadelphia, declare in July…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
