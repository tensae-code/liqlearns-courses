# American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The American Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 2)”.
**Answer:** Britain's thirteen colonies, taxed to pay for the Seven Years' War and forbidden to trade except with Britain, meet at Philadelphia, declare in July 1776 that all men are created equal, win recognition at Versailles in 1783, and in 1787 write a federal constitution that makes Washington first president in 1789
