# American Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The American Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 2). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The American Revolution (Unit 2 Lesson 2)

Britain's thirteen colonies, taxed to pay for the Seven Years' War and forbidden to trade except with Britain, meet at Philadelphia, declare in July 1776 that all men are created equal, win recognition at Versailles in 1783, and in 1787 write a federal constitution that makes Washington first president in 1789

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: American Independence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
