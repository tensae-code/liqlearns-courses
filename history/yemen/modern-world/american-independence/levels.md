# American Independence — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — britain's thirteen colonies, taxed to pay for the Seven Years' War and forbidden to trade except with Britain, meet at Philadelphia, declare in July 1776 that all men… — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
