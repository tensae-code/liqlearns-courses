# Ottoman State Rise Osman to Vienna

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Rise of the Ottoman state. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Rise of the Ottoman state

a Turkish tribe fleeing the Mongols becomes, under Osman, a frontier emirate propped up by the Seljuks in 1299, grows on Byzantine weakness and its own fierce soldiering, takes Constantinople in 1453 where the early Arab attempts had stopped, renames it Istanbul, carries Islam into Hungary, the Balkans, Serbia, Bosnia and Albania, halts the Russian danger on the Black Sea, and is only turned back at the walls of Vienna in 1529 — then, with the Arab world exhausted by Mongols, Crusaders and Portuguese sea-power, turns south to inherit the Mamluks' place
