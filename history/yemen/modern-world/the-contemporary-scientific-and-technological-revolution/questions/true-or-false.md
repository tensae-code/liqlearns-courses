# The Contemporary Scientific and Technological Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Human work passes from hand to machine to the age of space, biology and information networks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The contemporary scientific and technological revolution (Unit 6 Lesson 3)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
