# The Contemporary Scientific and Technological Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The contemporary scientific and technological revolution (Unit 6 Lesson 3)”?
a) Human work passes from hand to machine to the age of space, biology and information networks  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Human work passes from hand to machine to the age of space, biology and information networks** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The contemporary scientific and technological revolution (Unit 6 Lesson 3)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Human work passes from hand to machine to the age of space, biology and information networks  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Human work passes from hand to machine to the age of space, biology and information networks** — That is what this lesson teaches.
