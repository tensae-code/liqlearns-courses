# The Contemporary Scientific and Technological Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The contemporary scientific and technological revolution (Unit 6 Lesson 3)”.
**Answer:** human work passes from hand to machine to the age of space, biology and information networks; the revolution is fast, cumulative and double-edged — new energy, cloning and remote sensing beside widening gaps between advanced and developing worlds — and the book closes by asking what the Arabs must build to root research and invention in their own homeland
