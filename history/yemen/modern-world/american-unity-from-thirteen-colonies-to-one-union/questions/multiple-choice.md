# American Unity from Thirteen Colonies to One Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “American unity (Unit 3 Lesson 2)”?
a) Thirteen self-governing colonies learn union by steps  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Thirteen self-governing colonies learn union by steps** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “American unity (Unit 3 Lesson 2)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Thirteen self-governing colonies learn union by steps  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Thirteen self-governing colonies learn union by steps** — That is what this lesson teaches.
