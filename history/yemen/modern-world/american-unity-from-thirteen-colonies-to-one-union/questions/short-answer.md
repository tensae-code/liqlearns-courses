# American Unity from Thirteen Colonies to One Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “American unity (Unit 3 Lesson 2)”.
**Answer:** thirteen self-governing colonies learn union by steps — the first Philadelphia Congress of 1774 protests and boycotts, the second creates one army under Washington, independence follows in 1776, the 1787 constitution binds them into one republic, and the union is finally sealed in blood when the North wins the Civil War of 1861–1865 and slavery is abolished
