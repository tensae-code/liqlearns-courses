# American Unity from Thirteen Colonies to One Union

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: American unity (Unit 3 Lesson 2). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## American unity (Unit 3 Lesson 2)

thirteen self-governing colonies learn union by steps — the first Philadelphia Congress of 1774 protests and boycotts, the second creates one army under Washington, independence follows in 1776, the 1787 constitution binds them into one republic, and the union is finally sealed in blood when the North wins the Civil War of 1861–1865 and slavery is abolished

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: American Unity from Thirteen Colonies to One Union. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
