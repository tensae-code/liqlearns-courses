# The First World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The First World War: causes and goals (Unit 4 Lesson 1)”?
a) The colonial powers scramble for colonies, markets and raw materials until Germany's rise sharpens every contradiction  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The colonial powers scramble for colonies, markets and raw materials until Germany's rise sharpens every contradiction** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The First World War: causes and goals (Unit 4 Lesson 1)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The colonial powers scramble for colonies, markets and raw materials until Germany's rise sharpens every contradiction  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The colonial powers scramble for colonies, markets and raw materials until Germany's rise sharpens every contradiction** — That is what this lesson teaches.
