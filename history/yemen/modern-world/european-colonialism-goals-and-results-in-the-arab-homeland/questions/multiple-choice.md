Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Goals of European colonialism and its results (Unit 1 Lesson 3)”?
a) Colonialism is defined as a strong state draining weaker lands, and its goals are listed  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
