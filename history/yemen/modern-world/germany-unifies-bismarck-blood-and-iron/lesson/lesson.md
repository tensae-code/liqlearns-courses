# Germany Unifies Bismarck Blood and Iron

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: German unity (Unit 3 Lesson 1). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## German unity (Unit 3 Lesson 1)

more than forty German states share one language and one customs union, but it takes Bismarck's three wars — Denmark in 1864, Austria crushed at Sadowa in 1866, France beaten at Sedan in 1870 — before Wilhelm I is crowned emperor of a united Germany at Versailles in 1871
