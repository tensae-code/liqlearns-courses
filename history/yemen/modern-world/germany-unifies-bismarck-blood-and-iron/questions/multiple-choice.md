# Germany Unifies Bismarck Blood and Iron — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “German unity (Unit 3 Lesson 1)”?
a) More than forty German states share one language and one customs union, but it takes Bismarck's three wars  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) More than forty German states share one language and one customs union, but it takes Bismarck's three wars** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “German unity (Unit 3 Lesson 1)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) More than forty German states share one language and one customs union, but it takes Bismarck's three wars  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) More than forty German states share one language and one customs union, but it takes Bismarck's three wars** — That is what this lesson teaches.
