# Germany Unifies Bismarck Blood and Iron — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “German unity (Unit 3 Lesson 1)”.
**Answer:** more than forty German states share one language and one customs union, but it takes Bismarck's three wars — Denmark in 1864, Austria crushed at Sadowa in 1866, France beaten at Sedan in 1870 — before Wilhelm I is crowned emperor of a united Germany at Versailles in 1871
