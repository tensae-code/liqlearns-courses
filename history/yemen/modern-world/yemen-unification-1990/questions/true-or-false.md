# Yemeni Unification 1990 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A people the book calls one land and one nation, repeatedly targeted by colonisers trying to split north from south, finally proclaim the Republic of Yemen together on….
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: One land and one people, divided only by Britain's occupation of the south, walk back together summit by summit.
