# Yemeni Unification 1990 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Yemeni unity”?
a) A people the book calls one land and one nation, repeatedly targeted by colonisers trying to split north from south, finally proclaim the Republic of…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A people the book calls one land and one nation, repeatedly targeted by colonisers trying to split north from south, finally proclaim the Republic of…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these matches the story of “Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) One land and one people, divided only by Britain's occupation of the south, walk back together summit by summit  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) One land and one people, divided only by Britain's occupation of the south, walk back together summit by summit** — That is what this lesson teaches.
