# Yemeni Unification 1990

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Yemeni unity; Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Yemeni unity

a people the book calls one land and one nation, repeatedly targeted by colonisers trying to split north from south, finally proclaim the Republic of Yemen together on Thursday 22 May 1990, the national day Yemenis celebrate each year

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5)

one land and one people, divided only by Britain's occupation of the south, walk back together summit by summit — Cairo 1972, Tripoli 1972, Kuwait 1979, Aden 1980 and the crowning Aden agreement of 30 November 1989 — until the Republic of Yemen is proclaimed on 22 May 1990; when secession is declared in 1994 the people themselves defend the unity and the war stops on 7 July 1994

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
