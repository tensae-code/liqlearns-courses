A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Yemen, Arabia and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
