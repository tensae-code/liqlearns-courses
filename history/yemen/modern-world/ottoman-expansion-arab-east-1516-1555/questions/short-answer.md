# Ottoman Expansion Arab East 1516 1555 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Ottoman expansion in the Arab East”.
**Answer:** with three Islamic powers — Ottoman, Safavid and Mamluk — contesting the leadership of the Muslim world and the Portuguese loose in the Indian Ocean, Selim strikes: Marj Dabiq in 1516 gives him Syria, the Ridaniya in 1517 ends the Mamluk state in Egypt, Iraq is wrestled from the Safavids by 1538, the Sharif of the Hijaz sends him the keys of the Kaaba, Yemen's Mamluks hand over Aden in 1538 and Sanaa in 1547, and Qatif and al-Ahsa plant an Ottoman spearhead against the Portuguese in the Gulf
