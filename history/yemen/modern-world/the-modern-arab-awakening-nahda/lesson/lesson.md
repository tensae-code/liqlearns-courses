# The Modern Arab Awakening (Nahda)

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Attempts of the modern Arab awakening (Unit 1 Lesson 4). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Attempts of the modern Arab awakening (Unit 1 Lesson 4)

stuck between Ottoman stagnation and European colonialism, the Arabs wake first in print — Urabi's revolt, modern schools, newspapers and secret societies — then in four directions: the return-to-Islam call of the Wahhabis, Sanusiyya and Mahdiyya, al-Kawakibi's Arab caliphate in Mecca, the nationalists who rouse the Arabs by their past, and Mustafa Kamil's Egypt
