# The Modern Arab Awakening (Nahda) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Attempts of the modern Arab awakening (Unit 1 Lesson 4)”?
a) Stuck between Ottoman stagnation and European colonialism, the Arabs wake first in print  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Stuck between Ottoman stagnation and European colonialism, the Arabs wake first in print** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Attempts of the modern Arab awakening (Unit 1 Lesson 4)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Stuck between Ottoman stagnation and European colonialism, the Arabs wake first in print  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Stuck between Ottoman stagnation and European colonialism, the Arabs wake first in print** — That is what this lesson teaches.
