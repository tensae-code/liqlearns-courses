# Arab Navigators and the Cost of the Discoveries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in the part called “Effects of the geographical discoveries on the Arab homeland”?
a) The Arabs were there first  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Arabs were there first** — That is what this lesson teaches.
