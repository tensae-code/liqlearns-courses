# The maya — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Three indigenous civilisations and their books — a)
**2.** People of the maize in Mexico and Central America — b)

Meanings:
a) Maya, Aztec and Inca build splendid palaces; the Maya invent writing and a number system, and their books are of stone, rolled and folded
b) Maya and their neighbours, the people of maize, build cities, calendars and writing in Mexico and Central America

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
