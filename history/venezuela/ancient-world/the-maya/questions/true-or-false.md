# The maya — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Three indigenous civilisations and their books", maya, Aztec and Inca build splendid palaces; the Maya invent writing and a number system, and their books are of stone, rolled and folded
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "People of the maize in Mexico and Central America", maya and their neighbours, the people of maize, build cities, calendars and writing in Mexico and Central America
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
