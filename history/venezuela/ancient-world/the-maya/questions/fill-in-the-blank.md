# The maya — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Three indigenous civilisations and their books" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Maya, Aztec and Inca build splendid palaces; the Maya invent writing and a number system, and their books are of stone, rolled and folded

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "People of the maize in Mexico and Central America" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Maya and their neighbours, the people of maize, build cities, calendars and writing in Mexico and Central America
