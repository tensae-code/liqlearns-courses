# The maya — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Three indigenous civilisations and their books" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Maya, Aztec and Inca build splendid palaces; the Maya invent writing and a number system, and their books are of stone, rolled and folded

**2.** Tell the story of "People of the maize in Mexico and Central America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Maya and their neighbours, the people of maize, build cities, calendars and writing in Mexico and Central America
