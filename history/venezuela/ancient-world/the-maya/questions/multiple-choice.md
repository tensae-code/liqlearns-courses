# The maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Three indigenous civilisations and their books"?
a) Maya, Aztec and Inca build splendid palaces; the Maya invent writing and a number system, and their books are of stone, rolled and folded  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "People of the maize in Mexico and Central America"?
a) Maya and their neighbours, the people of maize, build cities, calendars and writing in Mexico and Central America  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
