# The aztecs tenochtitlan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "As Spain seized America it became an empire" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Aztec chief is imprisoned and his people react; Tenochtitlán and then the splendid Inca state fall to Spain, while Chile's indigenous resistance holds out

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Indigenous cultures in the highlands of Our America" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The highland civilisations of the Andes and Mexico raise cities and empires before any European arrives
