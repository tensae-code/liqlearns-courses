# The aztecs tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "As Spain seized America it became an empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Aztec chief is imprisoned and his people react; Tenochtitlán and then the splendid Inca state fall to Spain, while Chile's indigenous resistance holds out

**2.** Tell the story of "Indigenous cultures in the highlands of Our America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The highland civilisations of the Andes and Mexico raise cities and empires before any European arrives
