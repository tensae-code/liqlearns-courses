# The aztecs tenochtitlan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "As Spain seized America it became an empire", the Aztec chief is imprisoned and his people react; Tenochtitlán and then the splendid Inca state fall to Spain, while Chile's indigenous resistance holds out
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Indigenous cultures in the highlands of Our America", the highland civilisations of the Andes and Mexico raise cities and empires before any European arrives
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
