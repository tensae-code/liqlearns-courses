# Venezuelas first peoples farming and space — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Native communities in harmony with nature", indigenous work creates Venezuela's first lived spaces; the Timotocuica people's farming is the most advanced, and their stone-and-bahareque building orders the land before any Spaniard
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Indigenous peoples before the invaders of 1498", before 1498 indigenous peoples live by collective work in balance with nature; the European arrival breaks that dynamic and introduces new ways of exploiting the land
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
