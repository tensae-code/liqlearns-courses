# Venezuelas first peoples farming and space — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Native communities in harmony with nature — a)
**2.** Indigenous peoples before the invaders of 1498 — b)

Meanings:
a) Indigenous work creates Venezuela's first lived spaces; the Timotocuica people's farming is the most advanced, and their stone-and-bahareque building orders the land before any Spaniard
b) Before 1498 indigenous peoples live by collective work in balance with nature; the European arrival breaks that dynamic and introduces new ways of exploiting the land

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
