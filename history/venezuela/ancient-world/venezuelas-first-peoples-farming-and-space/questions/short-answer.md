# Venezuelas first peoples farming and space — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Native communities in harmony with nature" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Indigenous work creates Venezuela's first lived spaces; the Timotocuica people's farming is the most advanced, and their stone-and-bahareque building orders the land before any Spaniard

**2.** Tell the story of "Indigenous peoples before the invaders of 1498" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Before 1498 indigenous peoples live by collective work in balance with nature; the European arrival breaks that dynamic and introduces new ways of exploiting the land
