# Video script — Venezuelas first peoples farming and space

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuelas first peoples farming and space.

Indigenous work creates Venezuela's first lived spaces; the Timotocuica people's farming is the most advanced, and their stone-and-bahareque building orders the land before any Spaniard

Before 1498 indigenous peoples live by collective work in balance with nature; the European arrival breaks that dynamic and introduces new ways of exploiting the land

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
