# Venezuelas indigenous peoples and numbers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Indigenous peoples and numbers: letters or fingers?" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The books say the first peoples counted with their own systems, and children test fingers against written numerals to feel the invention

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Process of changes in indigenous life; cultures of our indigenous past" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Hunting-gathering gives way to farming and settled cultures in what will become Venezuela, each culture leaving its own traces
