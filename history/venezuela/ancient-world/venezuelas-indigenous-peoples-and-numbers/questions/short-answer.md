# Venezuelas indigenous peoples and numbers — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Indigenous peoples and numbers: letters or fingers?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The books say the first peoples counted with their own systems, and children test fingers against written numerals to feel the invention

**2.** Tell the story of "Process of changes in indigenous life; cultures of our indigenous past" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Hunting-gathering gives way to farming and settled cultures in what will become Venezuela, each culture leaving its own traces
