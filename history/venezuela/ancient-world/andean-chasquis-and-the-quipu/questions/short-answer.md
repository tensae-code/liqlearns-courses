# Andean chasquis and the quipu — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "From air signals to water and land roads: chasquis and quipu" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Relay runners, the chasquis, carry messages along land roads, remembering them with the knotted quipu — an information system without writing
