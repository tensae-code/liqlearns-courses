# Andean chasquis and the quipu — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "From air signals to water and land roads: chasquis and quipu" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Relay runners, the chasquis, carry messages along land roads, remembering them with the knotted quipu — an information system without writing
