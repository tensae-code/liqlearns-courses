# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The barbarians occupy Europe; the Frankish kingdom; Charlemagne; bases of feudalism" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Rome falls, Franks build a kingdom, Charlemagne crowns an empire, and feudalism's bases settle over Europe

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Work, fight and pray: seigneurial rights, serfs of the glebe, medieval society; Crusades; towns revive" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Those who work, fight and pray divide medieval society; crusades march east while artisans, merchants and towns revive
