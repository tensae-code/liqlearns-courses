# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** The barbarians occupy Europe; the Frankish kingdom; Charlemagne; bases of feudalism — a)

Meanings:
a) Rome falls, Franks build a kingdom, Charlemagne crowns an empire, and feudalism's bases settle over Europe
b) Those who work, fight and pray divide medieval society; crusades march east while artisans, merchants and towns revive
