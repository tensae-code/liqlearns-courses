# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "The barbarians occupy Europe; the Frankish kingdom; Charlemagne; bases of feudalism", rome falls, Franks build a kingdom, Charlemagne crowns an empire, and feudalism's bases settle over Europe
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
