# Venezuela simon rodriguez teacher of bolivar — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Simón Rodríguez knew how to educate for freedom" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Bolívar's teacher forms his heart "for liberty, for justice, for what is great and beautiful", and the pupil's letter thanks the master who made him

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Builders of the dream of the Simons" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Simón Rodríguez, who taught Bolívar to learn, and Bolívar himself set the dream of an educated, free homeland that students are called to finish building
