# Venezuela simon rodriguez teacher of bolivar — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Simón Rodríguez knew how to educate for freedom", bolívar's teacher forms his heart "for liberty, for justice, for what is great and beautiful", and the pupil's letter thanks the master who made him
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Builders of the dream of the Simons", simón Rodríguez, who taught Bolívar to learn, and Bolívar himself set the dream of an educated, free homeland that students are called to finish building
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
