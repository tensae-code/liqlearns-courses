# Venezuela simon rodriguez teacher of bolivar — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Simón Rodríguez knew how to educate for freedom" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Bolívar's teacher forms his heart "for liberty, for justice, for what is great and beautiful", and the pupil's letter thanks the master who made him

**2.** Tell the story of "Builders of the dream of the Simons" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Simón Rodríguez, who taught Bolívar to learn, and Bolívar himself set the dream of an educated, free homeland that students are called to finish building
