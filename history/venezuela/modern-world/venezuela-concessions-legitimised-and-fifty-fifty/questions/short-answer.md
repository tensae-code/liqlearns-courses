# Venezuela concessions legitimised and fifty fifty — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The landlord state legitimises the concessions, 1920–1957" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A famous gusher announces Venezuela's wealth to the world, the 1943 Hydrocarbons Law orders the giveaway, and the 1948 fifty-fifty split is exposed as a white elephant

**2.** Tell the story of "When our black gold gained commercial importance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Under Gómez the concessions expand, the state lives off oil rent, and Venezuela becomes an oil country in fact
