# Venezuela concessions legitimised and fifty fifty — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The landlord state legitimises the concessions, 1920–1957" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A famous gusher announces Venezuela's wealth to the world, the 1943 Hydrocarbons Law orders the giveaway, and the 1948 fifty-fifty split is exposed as a white elephant

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "When our black gold gained commercial importance" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Under Gómez the concessions expand, the state lives off oil rent, and Venezuela becomes an oil country in fact
