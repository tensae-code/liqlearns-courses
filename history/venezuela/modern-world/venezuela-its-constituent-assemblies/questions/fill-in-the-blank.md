# Venezuela its constituent assemblies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Constitution and laws to live well: other constituent assemblies in Venezuela" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Venezuela has refounded itself in assembly after assembly; when "participation" came into fashion the constituent process became the people's tool

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Political-legal bases of the republic arisen in 1830" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The 1830 constitution fixes the territory by uti possidetis juris from the 1777 Captaincy General and sets a censitary republic of propertied male voters
