# Venezuela its constituent assemblies — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Constitution and laws to live well: other constituent assemblies in Venezuela" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Venezuela has refounded itself in assembly after assembly; when "participation" came into fashion the constituent process became the people's tool

**2.** Tell the story of "Political-legal bases of the republic arisen in 1830" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The 1830 constitution fixes the territory by uti possidetis juris from the 1777 Captaincy General and sets a censitary republic of propertied male voters
