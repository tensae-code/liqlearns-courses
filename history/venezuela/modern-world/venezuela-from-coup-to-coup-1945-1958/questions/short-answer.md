# Venezuela from coup to coup 1945 1958 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "From one coup to another, 1945–1958" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The 1945 coup opens a democratic triennium, a 1948 coup ends it, and juntas carry the country toward dictatorship

**2.** Tell the story of "Civic-military Junta and government of Gallegos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The 1945 junta brings universal suffrage, writer-president Rómulo Gallegos wins the first free election — and a 1948 coup cuts him down
