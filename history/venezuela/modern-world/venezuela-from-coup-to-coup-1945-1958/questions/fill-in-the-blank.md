# Venezuela from coup to coup 1945 1958 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "From one coup to another, 1945–1958" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The 1945 coup opens a democratic triennium, a 1948 coup ends it, and juntas carry the country toward dictatorship

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Civic-military Junta and government of Gallegos" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The 1945 junta brings universal suffrage, writer-president Rómulo Gallegos wins the first free election — and a 1948 coup cuts him down
