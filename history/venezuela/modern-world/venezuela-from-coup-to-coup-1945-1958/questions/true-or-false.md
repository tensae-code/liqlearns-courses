# Venezuela from coup to coup 1945 1958 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "From one coup to another, 1945–1958", the 1945 coup opens a democratic triennium, a 1948 coup ends it, and juntas carry the country toward dictatorship
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Civic-military Junta and government of Gallegos", the 1945 junta brings universal suffrage, writer-president Rómulo Gallegos wins the first free election — and a 1948 coup cuts him down
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
