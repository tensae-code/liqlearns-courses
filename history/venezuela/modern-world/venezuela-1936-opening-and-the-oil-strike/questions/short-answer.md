# Venezuela 1936 opening and the oil strike — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "1936–1941: "calm and cordure", sowing the oil and the 1936 oil strike" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** After Gómez dies, López Contreras promises calm, Alberto Adriani urges "sembrar el petróleo", the Ley Lara represses, and oil workers strike in 1936 before the 1938 Triennial Plan

**2.** Tell the story of "Children of the strike (complementary reading)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The oil strike is retold through the children who lived it, waiting on fathers who had downed tools
