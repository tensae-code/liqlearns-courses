# Venezuela 1936 opening and the oil strike — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "1936–1941: "calm and cordure", sowing the oil and the 1936 oil strike", after Gómez dies, López Contreras promises calm, Alberto Adriani urges "sembrar el petróleo", the Ley Lara represses, and oil workers strike in 1936 before the 1938 Triennial Plan
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Children of the strike (complementary reading)", the oil strike is retold through the children who lived it, waiting on fathers who had downed tools
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
