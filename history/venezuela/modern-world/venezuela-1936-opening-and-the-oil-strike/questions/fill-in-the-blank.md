# Venezuela 1936 opening and the oil strike — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "1936–1941: "calm and cordure", sowing the oil and the 1936 oil strike" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** After Gómez dies, López Contreras promises calm, Alberto Adriani urges "sembrar el petróleo", the Ley Lara represses, and oil workers strike in 1936 before the 1938 Triennial Plan

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Children of the strike (complementary reading)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The oil strike is retold through the children who lived it, waiting on fathers who had downed tools
