# Video script — Venezuela 1936 opening and the oil strike

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela 1936 opening and the oil strike.

After Gómez dies, López Contreras promises calm, Alberto Adriani urges "sembrar el petróleo", the Ley Lara represses, and oil workers strike in 1936 before the 1938 Triennial Plan

The oil strike is retold through the children who lived it, waiting on fathers who had downed tools

After Gómez dies, López Contreras acts as a bridge, parties and unions appear, and democracy advances shyly to 1945

After Gómez, López Contreras opens a cautious transition — February Programme, parties tolerated, oil strike faced

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
