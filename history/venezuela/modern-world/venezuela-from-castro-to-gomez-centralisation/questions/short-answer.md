# Venezuela from castro to gomez centralisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "1900–1936: from Castro to Gómez, milestones of centralisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Gómez turns Castro's restoration into a long centralised rule, unifying the National Treasury from 1918 and remaking the army and the state

**2.** Tell the story of "Gomecismo" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Gómez rules 1908–1935, centralises the state, pays the foreign debt and hands out the oil concessions that change the country
