# Venezuela from castro to gomez centralisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "1900–1936: from Castro to Gómez, milestones of centralisation" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Gómez turns Castro's restoration into a long centralised rule, unifying the National Treasury from 1918 and remaking the army and the state

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Gomecismo" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Gómez rules 1908–1935, centralises the state, pays the foreign debt and hands out the oil concessions that change the country
