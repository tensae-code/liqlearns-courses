# Exploration causes and results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "An unexpected encounter changed the world's image: causes of an adventure"?
a) In the Renaissance the compass is perfected and instruments invented; many sailors seek new routes and one admiral's error redraws the world  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
