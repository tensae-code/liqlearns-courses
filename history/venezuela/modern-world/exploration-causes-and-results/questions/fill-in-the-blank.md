# Exploration causes and results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "An unexpected encounter changed the world's image: causes of an adventure" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** In the Renaissance the compass is perfected and instruments invented; many sailors seek new routes and one admiral's error redraws the world

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Abya Yala and Europe on the eve of an invasion" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Two worlds face each other in 1492 — a populated continent with owners, and an Europe ready to expand
