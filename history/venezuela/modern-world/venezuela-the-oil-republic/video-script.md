# Video script — Venezuela the oil republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela the oil republic.

Agricultural Venezuela gives way to the República Petrolera, and every later chapter of the book is read under oil's shadow

Foreign companies get concessions from 1909, camps cut into a rural country, and the 1922 Barroso 2 gusher announces commercial oil

Oil redraws the country's face — camps, highways, cities and a rentier state replace the agrarian republic

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
