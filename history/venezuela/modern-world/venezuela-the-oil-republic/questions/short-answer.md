# Venezuela the oil republic — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Oil Republic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Agricultural Venezuela gives way to the República Petrolera, and every later chapter of the book is read under oil's shadow

**2.** Tell the story of "Oil activity in its beginnings: concessions from 1909 and the Barroso 2 blowout" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Foreign companies get concessions from 1909, camps cut into a rural country, and the 1922 Barroso 2 gusher announces commercial oil
