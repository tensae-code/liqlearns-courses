# Venezuela the oil republic — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Oil Republic" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Agricultural Venezuela gives way to the República Petrolera, and every later chapter of the book is read under oil's shadow

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Oil activity in its beginnings: concessions from 1909 and the Barroso 2 blowout" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Foreign companies get concessions from 1909, camps cut into a rural country, and the 1922 Barroso 2 gusher announces commercial oil
