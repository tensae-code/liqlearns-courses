# Venezuela guzmancismo and the silver bolivar — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Guzmancismo, the coffee economy's structure and the silver bolívar" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Guzmán Blanco builds the contemporary state on coffee money, and the fight over minting a silver bolívar shows imperial demands pressing on the new coin

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "An authoritarian, modernising president" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** One strong president drags the country toward modernity by decree — progress and authoritarianism arrive in the same person (Guzmán Blanco)
