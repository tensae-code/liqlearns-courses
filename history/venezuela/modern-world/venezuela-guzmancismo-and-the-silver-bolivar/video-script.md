# Video script — Venezuela guzmancismo and the silver bolivar

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela guzmancismo and the silver bolivar.

Guzmán Blanco builds the contemporary state on coffee money, and the fight over minting a silver bolívar shows imperial demands pressing on the new coin

One strong president drags the country toward modernity by decree — progress and authoritarianism arrive in the same person (Guzmán Blanco)

Guzmán Blanco's Liberalismo Amarillo modernises by decree — order, works and a cult of the chief — for three decades

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
