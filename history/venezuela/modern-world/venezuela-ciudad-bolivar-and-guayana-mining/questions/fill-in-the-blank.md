# Venezuela ciudad bolivar and guayana mining — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ciudad Bolívar, its Apure-Barinas hinterland and Guayana mining" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Ciudad Bolívar fights its last battle as river port while gold and iron make Guayana a mining pole the oil state absorbs

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Guayana: wealth, beauty, refuge of liberty" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Ciudad Bolívar, founded in the colonial period on the Orinoco's bank, anchors a Guayana sold to children as riches and a refuge of freedom
