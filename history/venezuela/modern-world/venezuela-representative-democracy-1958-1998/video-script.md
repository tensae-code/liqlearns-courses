# Video script — Venezuela representative democracy 1958 1998

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela representative democracy 1958 1998.

The Punto Fijo pact trades dictatorship for party democracy, the 1960 agrarian reform promises land, and "shoot first, ask later" betrays the model's violence

The Punto Fijo pact refounds democracy by agreement among parties, setting the rules of the next forty years

Punto Fijo democracy opens under guerrilla challenge, founds OPEC diplomacy and hands power peacefully to the opposition

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
