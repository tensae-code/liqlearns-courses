# Venezuela representative democracy 1958 1998 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Forty years of representative democracy: a dependent model" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Punto Fijo pact trades dictatorship for party democracy, the 1960 agrarian reform promises land, and "shoot first, ask later" betrays the model's violence

**2.** Tell the story of "Representative democracy returns with a political pact" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Punto Fijo pact refounds democracy by agreement among parties, setting the rules of the next forty years
