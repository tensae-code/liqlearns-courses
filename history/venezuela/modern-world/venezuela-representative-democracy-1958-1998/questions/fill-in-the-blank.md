# Venezuela representative democracy 1958 1998 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Forty years of representative democracy: a dependent model" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Punto Fijo pact trades dictatorship for party democracy, the 1960 agrarian reform promises land, and "shoot first, ask later" betrays the model's violence

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Representative democracy returns with a political pact" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Punto Fijo pact refounds democracy by agreement among parties, setting the rules of the next forty years
