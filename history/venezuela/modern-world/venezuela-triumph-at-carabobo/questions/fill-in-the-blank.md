# Venezuela triumph at carabobo — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Triumph at Carabobo!" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The heroes of Carabobo win the battle that seals Venezuela's independence on the field

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Battles that sealed the independence of the Bolivarian countries" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Carabobo and the sister battles seal independence country by country across the Bolivarian north
