# Venezuela founding of caracas and guaicaipuro — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Stories of the founding of Caracas and the death of Guaicaipuro", tocanai falls and Paramaconi retreats; weakened resistance and land watered with indigenous warriors' blood precede the city, and cacique Guaicaipuro is killed resisting
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "The Capital of the Republic", even a Caracas district, Catia, carries cacique Catia's name — the capital's map is an indigenous-and-colonial palimpsest
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
