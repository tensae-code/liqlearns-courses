# Venezuela founding of caracas and guaicaipuro — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Stories of the founding of Caracas and the death of Guaicaipuro" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Tocanai falls and Paramaconi retreats; weakened resistance and land watered with indigenous warriors' blood precede the city, and cacique Guaicaipuro is killed resisting

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Capital of the Republic" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Even a Caracas district, Catia, carries cacique Catia's name — the capital's map is an indigenous-and-colonial palimpsest
