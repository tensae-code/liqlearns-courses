# Venezuela founding of caracas and guaicaipuro — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Stories of the founding of Caracas and the death of Guaicaipuro" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Tocanai falls and Paramaconi retreats; weakened resistance and land watered with indigenous warriors' blood precede the city, and cacique Guaicaipuro is killed resisting

**2.** Tell the story of "The Capital of the Republic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Even a Caracas district, Catia, carries cacique Catia's name — the capital's map is an indigenous-and-colonial palimpsest
