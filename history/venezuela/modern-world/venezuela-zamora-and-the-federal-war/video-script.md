# Video script — Venezuela zamora and the federal war

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela zamora and the federal war.

Ezequiel Zamora's federal struggle fills the countryside with war, and peace, when it comes, must be argued for at a neighbour's table

The Federal War is taught not as generals' quarrels but as the people themselves rising — an insurrection from below

Zamora's federal insurrection turns the countryside into a five-year war for land and equality, ended by the Treaty of Coche

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
