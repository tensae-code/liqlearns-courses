# Venezuela zamora and the federal war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "War and peace in Zamora's times" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Ezequiel Zamora's federal struggle fills the countryside with war, and peace, when it comes, must be argued for at a neighbour's table

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Federal War was a popular insurrection" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The Federal War is taught not as generals' quarrels but as the people themselves rising — an insurrection from below
