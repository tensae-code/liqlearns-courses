# Venezuela zamora and the federal war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "War and peace in Zamora's times" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Ezequiel Zamora's federal struggle fills the countryside with war, and peace, when it comes, must be argued for at a neighbour's table

**2.** Tell the story of "The Federal War was a popular insurrection" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Federal War is taught not as generals' quarrels but as the people themselves rising — an insurrection from below
