# Venezuela indigenous women in the resistance

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Venezuela indigenous women in the resistance" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Indigenous women also fought

At Caraballeda and elsewhere the women of the resistance take their own place in the fighting, not as bystanders

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Venezuela indigenous women in the resistance as Venezuela's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
