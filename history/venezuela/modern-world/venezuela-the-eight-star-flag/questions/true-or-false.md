# Venezuela the eight star flag — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "The flag of the eight stars", children meet the national flag star by star and sing how Caracas set the example the provinces followed

**2.** True or false: In "The national flag has a history full of Homeland", the flag's own biography, from Miranda's banner to the eight stars, is told as a story of the homeland itself
