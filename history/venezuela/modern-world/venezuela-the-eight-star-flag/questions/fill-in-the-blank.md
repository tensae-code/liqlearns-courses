# Venezuela the eight star flag — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The flag of the eight stars" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Children meet the national flag star by star and sing how Caracas set the example the provinces followed

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The national flag has a history full of Homeland" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The flag's own biography, from Miranda's banner to the eight stars, is told as a story of the homeland itself
