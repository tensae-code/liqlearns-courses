# Venezuela the eight star flag — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The flag of the eight stars" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Children meet the national flag star by star and sing how Caracas set the example the provinces followed

**2.** Tell the story of "The national flag has a history full of Homeland" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The flag's own biography, from Miranda's banner to the eight stars, is told as a story of the homeland itself
