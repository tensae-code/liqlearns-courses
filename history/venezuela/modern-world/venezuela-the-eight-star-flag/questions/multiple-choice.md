# Venezuela the eight star flag — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "The flag of the eight stars"?
a) Children meet the national flag star by star and sing how Caracas set the example the provinces followed  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "The national flag has a history full of Homeland"?
a) The flag's own biography, from Miranda's banner to the eight stars, is told as a story of the homeland itself  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
