# Venezuela caracazo and the 1992 rebellions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Carlos Andrés Pérez and the Caracazo" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Austerity sparks the Caracazo explosion, and the economic wreckage behind the 1992 military rebellions is laid out as the old model's end

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "A conflictive period: Pérez's second Presidency" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Austerity, the Caracazo and two 1992 military rebellions break the old system's calm in Pérez's second term
