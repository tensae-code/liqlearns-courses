# Video script — Venezuela indigenous resistance missions and german governors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela indigenous resistance missions and german governors.

Missions subdue less peacefully than was said, Margarita has a girl governor, German governors out of Coro hunt gold, and guarura shells sound in the Caracas valley

Sword and cross together take the territory against indigenous resistance, town by town

Mission towns gather indigenous people under the friars — settlement by conversion, softer in name than the sword and just as binding

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
