# Venezuela indigenous resistance missions and german governors — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "A conflict of great proportions: times of indigenous resistance" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Missions subdue less peacefully than was said, Margarita has a girl governor, German governors out of Coro hunt gold, and guarura shells sound in the Caracas valley

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Conquest-colonisation with sword and cross; conquest of our present territory" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Sword and cross together take the territory against indigenous resistance, town by town
