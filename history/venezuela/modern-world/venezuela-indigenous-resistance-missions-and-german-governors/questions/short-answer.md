# Venezuela indigenous resistance missions and german governors — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "A conflict of great proportions: times of indigenous resistance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Missions subdue less peacefully than was said, Margarita has a girl governor, German governors out of Coro hunt gold, and guarura shells sound in the Caracas valley

**2.** Tell the story of "Conquest-colonisation with sword and cross; conquest of our present territory" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Sword and cross together take the territory against indigenous resistance, town by town
