# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Those who arrived by sea to Our America"?
a) At dawn on 12 October 1492 the first invaders arrive by sea, and the book names them invaders, not discoverers, from the first line  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "What do we commemorate on 12 October? Columbus reaches this land, 1498"?
a) On 2 August 1498 Columbus's fleet touches Venezuela; after his route come the explorers, and America "enters universal history" on Europe's terms  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
