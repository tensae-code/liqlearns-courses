# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Those who arrived by sea to Our America" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** At dawn on 12 October 1492 the first invaders arrive by sea, and the book names them invaders, not discoverers, from the first line

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "What do we commemorate on 12 October? Columbus reaches this land, 1498" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** On 2 August 1498 Columbus's fleet touches Venezuela; after his route come the explorers, and America "enters universal history" on Europe's terms
