# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Those who arrived by sea to Our America — a)
**2.** What do we commemorate on 12 October? Columbus reaches this land, 1498 — b)

Meanings:
a) At dawn on 12 October 1492 the first invaders arrive by sea, and the book names them invaders, not discoverers, from the first line
b) On 2 August 1498 Columbus's fleet touches Venezuela; after his route come the explorers, and America "enters universal history" on Europe's terms

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
