# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Those who arrived by sea to Our America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** At dawn on 12 October 1492 the first invaders arrive by sea, and the book names them invaders, not discoverers, from the first line

**2.** Tell the story of "What do we commemorate on 12 October? Columbus reaches this land, 1498" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** On 2 August 1498 Columbus's fleet touches Venezuela; after his route come the explorers, and America "enters universal history" on Europe's terms
