# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Those who arrived by sea to Our America", at dawn on 12 October 1492 the first invaders arrive by sea, and the book names them invaders, not discoverers, from the first line
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "What do we commemorate on 12 October? Columbus reaches this land, 1498", on 2 August 1498 Columbus's fleet touches Venezuela; after his route come the explorers, and America "enters universal history" on Europe's terms
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
