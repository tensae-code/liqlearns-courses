# Video script — Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan.

At dawn on 12 October 1492 the first invaders arrive by sea, and the book names them invaders, not discoverers, from the first line

On 2 August 1498 Columbus's fleet touches Venezuela; after his route come the explorers, and America "enters universal history" on Europe's terms

Columbus's four voyages open the invasion, and a line of explorers follows him onto Venezuela's coasts

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
