## Olympe de Gouges and the Rights of Woman

Out of the French Revolution, Olympe de Gouges writes the Declaration of the Rights of Woman and the Female Citizen and students judge whether its treatment is fair

## Physiocrats and Encyclopedists; the Estates General; the Constituent Assembly; Rights of the Citizen

New ideas arm a revolution; the Estates General become a Constituent Assembly and proclaim the rights of man and citizen
