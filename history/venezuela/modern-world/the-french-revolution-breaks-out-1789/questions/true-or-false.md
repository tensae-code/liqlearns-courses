# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Olympe de Gouges and the Rights of Woman", out of the French Revolution, Olympe de Gouges writes the Declaration of the Rights of Woman and the Female Citizen and students judge whether its treatment is fair
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Physiocrats and Encyclopedists; the Estates General; the Constituent Assembly; Rights of the Citizen", new ideas arm a revolution; the Estates General become a Constituent Assembly and proclaim the rights of man and citizen
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
