# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Olympe de Gouges and the Rights of Woman" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Out of the French Revolution, Olympe de Gouges writes the Declaration of the Rights of Woman and the Female Citizen and students judge whether its treatment is fair

**2.** Tell the story of "Physiocrats and Encyclopedists; the Estates General; the Constituent Assembly; Rights of the Citizen" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** New ideas arm a revolution; the Estates General become a Constituent Assembly and proclaim the rights of man and citizen
