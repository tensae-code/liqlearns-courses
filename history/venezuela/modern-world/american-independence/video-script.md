# Video script — American independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: American independence.

Set inside the colonial-government unit, the thirteen English colonies' origins are told as the northern mirror of Venezuela's provinces

Mexico, Central and South America each win independence by their own road, a continental emancipation in many keys

England taxes its colonies into rebellion; they declare independence and write a federal constitution

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
