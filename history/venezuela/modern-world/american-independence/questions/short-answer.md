# American independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "How and why the thirteen colonies arose in North America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Set inside the colonial-government unit, the thirteen English colonies' origins are told as the northern mirror of Venezuela's provinces

**2.** Tell the story of "Independence of other sister peoples of Our America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Mexico, Central and South America each win independence by their own road, a continental emancipation in many keys
