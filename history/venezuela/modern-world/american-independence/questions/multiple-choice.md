# American independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "How and why the thirteen colonies arose in North America"?
a) Set inside the colonial-government unit, the thirteen English colonies' origins are told as the northern mirror of Venezuela's provinces  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Independence of other sister peoples of Our America"?
a) Mexico, Central and South America each win independence by their own road, a continental emancipation in many keys  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
