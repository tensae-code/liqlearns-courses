# American independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "How and why the thirteen colonies arose in North America" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Set inside the colonial-government unit, the thirteen English colonies' origins are told as the northern mirror of Venezuela's provinces

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Independence of other sister peoples of Our America" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Mexico, Central and South America each win independence by their own road, a continental emancipation in many keys
