# Venezuela miranda the precursor — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Grandfather Sebastián talks of Miranda" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Miranda crosses the world seeking support, organises his expedition in Haiti and sails for America to free it before anyone else dares

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Miranda's expeditions that announced the storm" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Miranda's 1806 expeditions fail on the coast but announce the tempest of independence
