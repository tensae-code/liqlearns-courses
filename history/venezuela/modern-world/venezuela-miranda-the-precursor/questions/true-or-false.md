# Venezuela miranda the precursor — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Grandfather Sebastián talks of Miranda", miranda crosses the world seeking support, organises his expedition in Haiti and sails for America to free it before anyone else dares
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Miranda's expeditions that announced the storm", miranda's 1806 expeditions fail on the coast but announce the tempest of independence
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
