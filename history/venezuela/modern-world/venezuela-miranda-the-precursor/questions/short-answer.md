# Venezuela miranda the precursor — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Grandfather Sebastián talks of Miranda" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Miranda crosses the world seeking support, organises his expedition in Haiti and sails for America to free it before anyone else dares

**2.** Tell the story of "Miranda's expeditions that announced the storm" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Miranda's 1806 expeditions fail on the coast but announce the tempest of independence
