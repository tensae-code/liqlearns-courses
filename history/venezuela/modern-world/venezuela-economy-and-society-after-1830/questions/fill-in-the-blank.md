# Venezuela economy and society after 1830 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Economy and society after 1830" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The new separate republic lives off its farms and herds, and its society keeps the old orders under new flags

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Venezuela at the start of our republican life" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** In 1830 the new republic is exhausted by war, farms and herds ruined, population fallen, and foreign commercial houses move in to control its markets
