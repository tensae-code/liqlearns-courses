# Venezuela economy and society after 1830 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Economy and society after 1830" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The new separate republic lives off its farms and herds, and its society keeps the old orders under new flags

**2.** Tell the story of "Venezuela at the start of our republican life" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** In 1830 the new republic is exhausted by war, farms and herds ruined, population fallen, and foreign commercial houses move in to control its markets
