# Venezuela borders from the captaincy general — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Limits: for the Homeland they are sovereignty" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The king of Spain drew the Captaincy General of Venezuela's limits, and today's land and sea borders descend from that colonial line

**2.** Tell the story of "Border controversies and international conflicts" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The young republic argues its inherited colonial borders with neighbours and empires, losing and defending territory on paper and in the field
