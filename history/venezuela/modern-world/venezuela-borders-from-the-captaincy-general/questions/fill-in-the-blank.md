# Venezuela borders from the captaincy general — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Limits: for the Homeland they are sovereignty" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The king of Spain drew the Captaincy General of Venezuela's limits, and today's land and sea borders descend from that colonial line

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Border controversies and international conflicts" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The young republic argues its inherited colonial borders with neighbours and empires, losing and defending territory on paper and in the field
