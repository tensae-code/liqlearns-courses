# Venezuela periods of economic history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Reviewing the periods of our economic history" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** By the fifteenth century the story opens, and Venezuela's economy is divided into named periods children must learn to tell apart

**2.** Tell the story of "Cacao, coffee, oil and population" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Three export products in a row remake where Venezuelans live and how many they are — each crop, then oil, redraws the population map
