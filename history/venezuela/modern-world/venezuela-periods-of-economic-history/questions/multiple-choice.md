# Venezuela periods of economic history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Reviewing the periods of our economic history"?
a) By the fifteenth century the story opens, and Venezuela's economy is divided into named periods children must learn to tell apart  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Cacao, coffee, oil and population"?
a) Three export products in a row remake where Venezuelans live and how many they are — each crop, then oil, redraws the population map  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
