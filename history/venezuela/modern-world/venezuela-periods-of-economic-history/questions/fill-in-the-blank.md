# Venezuela periods of economic history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Reviewing the periods of our economic history" turns on this: __________.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Cacao, coffee, oil and population" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Three export products in a row remake where Venezuelans live and how many they are — each crop, then oil, redraws the population map
