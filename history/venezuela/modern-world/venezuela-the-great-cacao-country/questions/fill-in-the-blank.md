# Venezuela the great cacao country — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "When Venezuela was a country of great cacaos" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Cacao plantation owners are rich in land and power, and their crop makes colonial Venezuela's fortune and its ruling class

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The colonial economy" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Cacao, and the colony's other products, organise work, trade and wealth for Spain's benefit
