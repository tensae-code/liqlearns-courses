# Venezuela the great cacao country — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "When Venezuela was a country of great cacaos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Cacao plantation owners are rich in land and power, and their crop makes colonial Venezuela's fortune and its ruling class

**2.** Tell the story of "The colonial economy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Cacao, and the colony's other products, organise work, trade and wealth for Spain's benefit
