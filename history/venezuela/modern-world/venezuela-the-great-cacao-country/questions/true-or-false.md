# Venezuela the great cacao country — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "When Venezuela was a country of great cacaos", cacao plantation owners are rich in land and power, and their crop makes colonial Venezuela's fortune and its ruling class
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "The colonial economy", cacao, and the colony's other products, organise work, trade and wealth for Spain's benefit
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
