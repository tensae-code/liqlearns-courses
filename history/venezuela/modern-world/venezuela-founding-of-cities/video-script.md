# Video script — Venezuela founding of cities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela founding of cities.

Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world

Founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die

Coro is founded in a curious bargain with a cacique, and El Tocuyo becomes the mother city from which other towns are seeded

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
