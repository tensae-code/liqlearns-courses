# Venezuela founding of cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "With the founding of cities a different country is born"?
a) Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Cities are born, grow and can also die"?
a) Founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
