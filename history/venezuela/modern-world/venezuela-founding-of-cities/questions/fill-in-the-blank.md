# Venezuela founding of cities — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "With the founding of cities a different country is born" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Cities are born, grow and can also die" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die
