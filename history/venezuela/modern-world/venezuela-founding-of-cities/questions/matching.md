# Venezuela founding of cities — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** With the founding of cities a different country is born — a)
**2.** Cities are born, grow and can also die — b)

Meanings:
a) Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world
b) Founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
