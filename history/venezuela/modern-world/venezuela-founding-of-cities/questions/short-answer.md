# Venezuela founding of cities — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "With the founding of cities a different country is born" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world

**2.** Tell the story of "Cities are born, grow and can also die" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die
