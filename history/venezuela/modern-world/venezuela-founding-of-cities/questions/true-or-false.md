# Venezuela founding of cities — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "With the founding of cities a different country is born", spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Cities are born, grow and can also die", founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
