# Venezuela bolivar president — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bolívar President" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The liberator of armies becomes a president who must govern, and the story follows the sword turned into an office

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "A president on liberating campaigns" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Bolívar governs from the saddle, campaigning and presiding at once, so the republic is born already shaped by war
