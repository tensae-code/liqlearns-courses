# Venezuela oil culture and the two venezuelas — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Oil culture, country-to-city migration and the two Venezuelas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Oil money remakes values and empties the countryside into the cities, leaving two deeply different Venezuelas inside one state-capitalist country

**2.** Tell the story of "When the oil economy changed customs and even the spatial structure" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Oil does not only pay bills; it changes what people eat, want and build, and rearranges the country's very space
