# Venezuela oil culture and the two venezuelas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Oil culture, country-to-city migration and the two Venezuelas" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Oil money remakes values and empties the countryside into the cities, leaving two deeply different Venezuelas inside one state-capitalist country

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "When the oil economy changed customs and even the spatial structure" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Oil does not only pay bills; it changes what people eat, want and build, and rearranges the country's very space
