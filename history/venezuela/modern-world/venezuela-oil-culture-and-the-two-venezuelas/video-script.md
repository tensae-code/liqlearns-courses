# Video script — Venezuela oil culture and the two venezuelas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela oil culture and the two venezuelas.

Oil money remakes values and empties the countryside into the cities, leaving two deeply different Venezuelas inside one state-capitalist country

Oil does not only pay bills; it changes what people eat, want and build, and rearranges the country's very space

Oil pulls country people to the camps and cities, new social classes appear, and customs change with the new work

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
