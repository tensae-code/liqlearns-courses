# Venezuela first fights against the spanish yoke — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "First fights against the Spanish yoke: Chirino and Gual-España", zambo rebel José Leonardo Chirino rises at Coro and the rebels take the city; the Gual and España conspiracy follows, and both are crushed
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Conspiracy of Gual and España (1797)", gual and España conspire in La Guaira for a republic of equals and are betrayed and executed
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
