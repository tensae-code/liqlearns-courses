# Venezuela first fights against the spanish yoke — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "First fights against the Spanish yoke: Chirino and Gual-España" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Zambo rebel José Leonardo Chirino rises at Coro and the rebels take the city; the Gual and España conspiracy follows, and both are crushed

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Conspiracy of Gual and España (1797)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Gual and España conspire in La Guaira for a republic of equals and are betrayed and executed
