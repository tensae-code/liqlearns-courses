# Venezuela first fights against the spanish yoke — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "First fights against the Spanish yoke: Chirino and Gual-España" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Zambo rebel José Leonardo Chirino rises at Coro and the rebels take the city; the Gual and España conspiracy follows, and both are crushed

**2.** Tell the story of "Conspiracy of Gual and España (1797)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Gual and España conspire in La Guaira for a republic of equals and are betrayed and executed
