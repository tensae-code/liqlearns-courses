# Venezuela collective lands enslavement and pirates — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Foreign owners appeared for collective lands" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Communal indigenous lands gain foreign owners, enslaved people rebel, colonists bring cattle, Spain imposes mercantilism and pirates raid the coasts

**2.** Tell the story of "When private property appeared in Our America; the encomienda" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Communal land becomes private property and the encomienda turns indigenous labour into a grant — a new way to exploit the worker
