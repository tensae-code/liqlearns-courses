# Venezuela collective lands enslavement and pirates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Foreign owners appeared for collective lands" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Communal indigenous lands gain foreign owners, enslaved people rebel, colonists bring cattle, Spain imposes mercantilism and pirates raid the coasts

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "When private property appeared in Our America; the encomienda" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Communal land becomes private property and the encomienda turns indigenous labour into a grant — a new way to exploit the worker
