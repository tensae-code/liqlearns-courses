# Venezuela collective lands enslavement and pirates — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Foreign owners appeared for collective lands", communal indigenous lands gain foreign owners, enslaved people rebel, colonists bring cattle, Spain imposes mercantilism and pirates raid the coasts
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "When private property appeared in Our America; the encomienda", communal land becomes private property and the encomienda turns indigenous labour into a grant — a new way to exploit the worker
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
