# Venezuela bolivars house and birth — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Bolívar's house, his birth-day and an interrupted childhood" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Rain falls in Caracas the day Simón is born; the patio house is happy until death breaks the childhood, and the enslaved Hipólita and Matea raise the child

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "A Liberator is born" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The birth of the child who will liberate is told as a door opening — birds leave the cage as the liberator enters the world
