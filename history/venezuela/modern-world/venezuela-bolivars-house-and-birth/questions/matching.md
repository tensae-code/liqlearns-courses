# Venezuela bolivars house and birth — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Bolívar's house, his birth-day and an interrupted childhood — a)
**2.** A Liberator is born — b)

Meanings:
a) Rain falls in Caracas the day Simón is born; the patio house is happy until death breaks the childhood, and the enslaved Hipólita and Matea raise the child
b) The birth of the child who will liberate is told as a door opening — birds leave the cage as the liberator enters the world

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
