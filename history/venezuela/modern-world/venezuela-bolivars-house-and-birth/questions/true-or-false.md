# Venezuela bolivars house and birth — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Bolívar's house, his birth-day and an interrupted childhood", rain falls in Caracas the day Simón is born; the patio house is happy until death breaks the childhood, and the enslaved Hipólita and Matea raise the child
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "A Liberator is born", the birth of the child who will liberate is told as a door opening — birds leave the cage as the liberator enters the world
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
