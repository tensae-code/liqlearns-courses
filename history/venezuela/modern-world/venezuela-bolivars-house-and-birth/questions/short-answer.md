# Venezuela bolivars house and birth — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Bolívar's house, his birth-day and an interrupted childhood" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Rain falls in Caracas the day Simón is born; the patio house is happy until death breaks the childhood, and the enslaved Hipólita and Matea raise the child

**2.** Tell the story of "A Liberator is born" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The birth of the child who will liberate is told as a door opening — birds leave the cage as the liberator enters the world
