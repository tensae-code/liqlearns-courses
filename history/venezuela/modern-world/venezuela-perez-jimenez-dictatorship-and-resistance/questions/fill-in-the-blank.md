# Venezuela perez jimenez dictatorship and resistance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ten years of struggle against the Pérez Jiménez dictatorship" turns on this: __________.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Decade of dictatorship (1948–1958)" turns on this: __________.
