# Venezuela slavery and afro venezuelans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The enslaved and Afro-descendants" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Africans brought in chains and their descendants build the country while "gossip" fragments show how slavery lived inside everyday speech

**2.** Tell the story of "Maroon settlements and cumbes of the enslaved" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The enslaved flee and found cimarroneras and cumbes — free African towns inside the colony that enslaved them
