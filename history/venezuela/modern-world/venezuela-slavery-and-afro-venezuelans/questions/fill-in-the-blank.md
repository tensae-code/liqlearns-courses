# Venezuela slavery and afro venezuelans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The enslaved and Afro-descendants" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Africans brought in chains and their descendants build the country while "gossip" fragments show how slavery lived inside everyday speech

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Maroon settlements and cumbes of the enslaved" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The enslaved flee and found cimarroneras and cumbes — free African towns inside the colony that enslaved them
