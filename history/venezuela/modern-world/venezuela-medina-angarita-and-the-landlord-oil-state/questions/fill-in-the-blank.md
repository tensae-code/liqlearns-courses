# Venezuela medina angarita and the landlord oil state — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Medina Angarita and the landlord oil state" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Medina's government is judged through Picón Salas's and Uslar Pietri's eyes as oil income grows and the state settles into renting its subsoil to the companies

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Government of Medina Angarita" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Medina legalises the parties, passes the 1943 Hydrocarbons Law and is overthrown in 1945 just as democracy widens
