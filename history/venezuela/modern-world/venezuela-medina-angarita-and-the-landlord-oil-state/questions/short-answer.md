# Venezuela medina angarita and the landlord oil state — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Medina Angarita and the landlord oil state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Medina's government is judged through Picón Salas's and Uslar Pietri's eyes as oil income grows and the state settles into renting its subsoil to the companies

**2.** Tell the story of "Government of Medina Angarita" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Medina legalises the parties, passes the 1943 Hydrocarbons Law and is overthrown in 1945 just as democracy widens
