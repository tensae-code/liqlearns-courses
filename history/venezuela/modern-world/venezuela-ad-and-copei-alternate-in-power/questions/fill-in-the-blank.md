# Venezuela ad and copei alternate in power — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "AD and COPEI take turns in the Presidency" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Two parties alternate in power for decades while oil income rises and falls and the pact system slowly wears out

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "First governments of Pérez and Caldera (1969–1979)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Caldera pacifies the guerrillas, Pérez nationalises oil and iron amid the boom — the two-party alternation at its height
