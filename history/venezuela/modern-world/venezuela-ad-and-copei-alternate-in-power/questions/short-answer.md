# Venezuela ad and copei alternate in power — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "AD and COPEI take turns in the Presidency" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Two parties alternate in power for decades while oil income rises and falls and the pact system slowly wears out

**2.** Tell the story of "First governments of Pérez and Caldera (1969–1979)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Caldera pacifies the guerrillas, Pérez nationalises oil and iron amid the boom — the two-party alternation at its height
