# Video script — Venezuela chavez 1998 and the constituent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela chavez 1998 and the constituent.

Amid economic collapse Hugo Chávez wins in 1998, takes office in February 1999 and a Constituent Assembly refounds the republic in the headlines of that year

Chávez wins in 1998 promising refoundation, and a constituent process opens a participatory model

A consultative referendum, election of the Constituent Assembly and a final referendum approve the 1999 Constitution — the people as constitution-maker

Chávez refounds the republic in 1999, survives coup and oil strike, and governs through missions and a new oil policy to 2012

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
