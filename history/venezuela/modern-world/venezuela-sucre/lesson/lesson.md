# Venezuela sucre

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Venezuela sucre" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Two children think about Sucre

A state bears a great man's name, so two children reconstruct Antonio José de Sucre's life to learn why

## Sucre, a state with a hero's name; the valour and goodness of Sucre

Cumaná's state is named for Antonio José de Sucre, whose childhood listening and refusal to shoot prisoners show goodness matching his valour

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Venezuela sucre as Venezuela's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
