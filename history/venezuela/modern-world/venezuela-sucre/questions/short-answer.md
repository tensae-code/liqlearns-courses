# Venezuela sucre — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Two children think about Sucre" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A state bears a great man's name, so two children reconstruct Antonio José de Sucre's life to learn why

**2.** Tell the story of "Sucre, a state with a hero's name; the valour and goodness of Sucre" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Cumaná's state is named for Antonio José de Sucre, whose childhood listening and refusal to shoot prisoners show goodness matching his valour
