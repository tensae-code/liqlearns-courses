# Venezuela sucre — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Two children think about Sucre" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A state bears a great man's name, so two children reconstruct Antonio José de Sucre's life to learn why

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Sucre, a state with a hero's name; the valour and goodness of Sucre" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Cumaná's state is named for Antonio José de Sucre, whose childhood listening and refusal to shoot prisoners show goodness matching his valour
