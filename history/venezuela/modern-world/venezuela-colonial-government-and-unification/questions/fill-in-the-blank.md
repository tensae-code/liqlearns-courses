# Venezuela colonial government and unification — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Power in America when a king ruled" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Provinces hang from the Real Audiencia until the 1786 Real Audiencia de Caracas unifies them — one territory is assembled by royal paperwork

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Political-territorial organisation of Our America" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Captaincies, audiencias and provinces draw the colonial map whose lines the republics will inherit
