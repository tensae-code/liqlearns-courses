# Venezuela colonial government and unification — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Power in America when a king ruled" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Provinces hang from the Real Audiencia until the 1786 Real Audiencia de Caracas unifies them — one territory is assembled by royal paperwork

**2.** Tell the story of "Political-territorial organisation of Our America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Captaincies, audiencias and provinces draw the colonial map whose lines the republics will inherit
