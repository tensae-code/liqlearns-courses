# Venezuela castro and the restoration 1899 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "From the Restoration to the fall of Medina: Castro takes Caracas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Cipriano Castro marches from Colombia in 1899 at the head of the Liberal Restoration, seizes Caracas and opens the twentieth century while the General Asphalt conspires

**2.** Tell the story of "The twentieth century opens with a nationalist government under the sign of oil" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The century begins with Castro's nationalist government, then Gómez takes power and oil moves to the centre of national life
