Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "From the Restoration to the fall of Medina: Castro takes Caracas", cipriano Castro marches from Colombia in 1899 at the head of the Liberal Restoration, seizes Caracas and opens the twentieth century while the General Asphalt conspires
