# Venezuela castro and the restoration 1899 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "From the Restoration to the fall of Medina: Castro takes Caracas" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Cipriano Castro marches from Colombia in 1899 at the head of the Liberal Restoration, seizes Caracas and opens the twentieth century while the General Asphalt conspires

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The twentieth century opens with a nationalist government under the sign of oil" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The century begins with Castro's nationalist government, then Gómez takes power and oil moves to the centre of national life
