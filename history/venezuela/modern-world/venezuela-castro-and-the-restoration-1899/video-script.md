# Video script — Venezuela castro and the restoration 1899

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela castro and the restoration 1899.

Cipriano Castro marches from Colombia in 1899 at the head of the Liberal Restoration, seizes Caracas and opens the twentieth century while the General Asphalt conspires

The century begins with Castro's nationalist government, then Gómez takes power and oil moves to the centre of national life

Castro's Liberal Restoration marches from the Andes to Caracas and governs defying the foreign houses and fleets

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
