# Video script — Venezuela patria grande our america

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela patria grande our america.

The small homeland opens into Bolívar's Patria Grande, with Neruda's Chilean voice singing the continent as one home

Bolívar's own sentence opens the republic unit — the homeland to be built is a continent, not a province

Bolívar summons America's new republics to Panama in 1826 to unite — the Patria Grande attempted in diplomacy

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
