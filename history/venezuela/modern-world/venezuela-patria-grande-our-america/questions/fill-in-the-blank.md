# Venezuela patria grande our america — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Great Homeland is Our America" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The small homeland opens into Bolívar's Patria Grande, with Neruda's Chilean voice singing the continent as one home

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of ""For us the Homeland is America"" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Bolívar's own sentence opens the republic unit — the homeland to be built is a continent, not a province
