# Venezuela patria grande our america — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "The Great Homeland is Our America", the small homeland opens into Bolívar's Patria Grande, with Neruda's Chilean voice singing the continent as one home
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In ""For us the Homeland is America"", bolívar's own sentence opens the republic unit — the homeland to be built is a continent, not a province
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
