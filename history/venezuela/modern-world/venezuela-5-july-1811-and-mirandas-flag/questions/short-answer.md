# Venezuela 5 july 1811 and mirandas flag — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "5 July 1811: the Republic is born, and the flag Miranda brought" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Congress declares Independence and the national flag is introduced as the daughter of the one Miranda brought

**2.** Tell the story of "The Congress of 1811; a short-lived First Republic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Congress declares independence in 1811, and the First Republic falls within a year to war and earthquake
