# Venezuela 5 july 1811 and mirandas flag — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "5 July 1811: the Republic is born, and the flag Miranda brought" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Congress declares Independence and the national flag is introduced as the daughter of the one Miranda brought

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Congress of 1811; a short-lived First Republic" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Congress declares independence in 1811, and the First Republic falls within a year to war and earthquake
