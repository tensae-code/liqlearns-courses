# Venezuela three growth poles — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Three poles of growth since the late nineteenth century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Caracas, the oil west and Guayana grow as three poles; ports are rebuilt for oil, Judibana rises as a garden-city camp, and a journey crosses the oil basins east to west

**2.** Tell the story of "Caracas in oil times: "Caracas, there it is..."" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The capital grows explosively on oil rent into a metropolis, the visible pole of the oil country's new geography
