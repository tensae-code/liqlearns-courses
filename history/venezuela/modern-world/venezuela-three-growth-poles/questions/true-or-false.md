# Venezuela three growth poles — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Three poles of growth since the late nineteenth century", caracas, the oil west and Guayana grow as three poles; ports are rebuilt for oil, Judibana rises as a garden-city camp, and a journey crosses the oil basins east to west
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Caracas in oil times: "Caracas, there it is..."", the capital grows explosively on oil rent into a metropolis, the visible pole of the oil country's new geography
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
