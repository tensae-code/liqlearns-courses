# Venezuela three growth poles — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Three poles of growth since the late nineteenth century" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Caracas, the oil west and Guayana grow as three poles; ports are rebuilt for oil, Judibana rises as a garden-city camp, and a journey crosses the oil basins east to west

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Caracas in oil times: "Caracas, there it is..."" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The capital grows explosively on oil rent into a metropolis, the visible pole of the oil country's new geography
