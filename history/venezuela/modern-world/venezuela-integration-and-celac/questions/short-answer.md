# Venezuela integration and celac — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The union of the peoples of Our America and CELAC" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Against the interference story the book sets Bolívar's old call to union, carried forward into CELAC as its modern form

**2.** Tell the story of "Latin America and globalisation: ALBA, UNASUR and CELAC against ALCA" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Against the US-proposed ALCA, Chávez proposes ALBA in 2001 and regional blocs UNASUR and CELAC follow
