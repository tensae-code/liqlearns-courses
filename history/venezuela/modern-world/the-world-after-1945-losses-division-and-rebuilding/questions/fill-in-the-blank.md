# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "A new world economic order: Bretton Woods 1944 and the Cold War" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Bretton Woods rebuilds the world economy under US leadership and the Cold War (1945–1990) frames every Latin American choice Venezuela makes

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "A second socialist state; Gandhi and India's decolonisation; Africa extends its independence" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** China turns socialist, Gandhi's non-violence frees India, and Africa's independence spreads country by country
