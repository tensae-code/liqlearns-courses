# Venezuela gomez opposition and the generation of 1928 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "When even the fight against Gómez changed" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Opposition to the long dictatorship changes form — students, workers and exiles find new ways to resist Gómez

**2.** Tell the story of "When youth took the reins of anti-Gomecismo: the Generation of 1928" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Caracas students rise in Carnival week 1928 against Gómez; jailed and exiled, they become the founders of the modern parties
