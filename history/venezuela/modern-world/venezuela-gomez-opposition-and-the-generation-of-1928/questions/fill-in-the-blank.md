# Venezuela gomez opposition and the generation of 1928 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "When even the fight against Gómez changed" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Opposition to the long dictatorship changes form — students, workers and exiles find new ways to resist Gómez

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "When youth took the reins of anti-Gomecismo: the Generation of 1928" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Caracas students rise in Carnival week 1928 against Gómez; jailed and exiled, they become the founders of the modern parties
