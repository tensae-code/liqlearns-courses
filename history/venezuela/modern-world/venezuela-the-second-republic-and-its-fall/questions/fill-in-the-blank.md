# Venezuela the second republic and its fall — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Second victory and second republican failure" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The patriots win a second republic and lose it again — victory and collapse teach that proclaiming is not the same as holding

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Second Republic; the war continues for the Republic's restoration" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Bolívar's Admirable Campaign wins a second republic, Boves's llanero war destroys it, and the fight goes on from exile and the east
