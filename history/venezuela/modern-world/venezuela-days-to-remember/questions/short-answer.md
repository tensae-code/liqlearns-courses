# Venezuela days to remember — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Days to remember" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The school year is pinned to shared dates, among them 12 October, Day of Indigenous Resistance, when the country remembers first peoples instead of celebrating conquest

**2.** Tell the story of "October at school: the Day of Indigenous Resistance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Soon after classes begin the school remembers October's resistance day, turning the calendar itself into a history lesson
