# Venezuela days to remember — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Days to remember", the school year is pinned to shared dates, among them 12 October, Day of Indigenous Resistance, when the country remembers first peoples instead of celebrating conquest
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "October at school: the Day of Indigenous Resistance", soon after classes begin the school remembers October's resistance day, turning the calendar itself into a history lesson
