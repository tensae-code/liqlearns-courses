# Venezuela days to remember — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Days to remember"?
a) The school year is pinned to shared dates, among them 12 October, Day of Indigenous Resistance, when the country remembers first peoples instead of celebrating conquest  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "October at school: the Day of Indigenous Resistance"?
a) Soon after classes begin the school remembers October's resistance day, turning the calendar itself into a history lesson  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
