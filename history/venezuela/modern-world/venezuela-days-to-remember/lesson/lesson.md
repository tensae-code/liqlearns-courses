# Venezuela days to remember

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Venezuela days to remember" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Days to remember

The school year is pinned to shared dates, among them 12 October, Day of Indigenous Resistance, when the country remembers first peoples instead of celebrating conquest

## October at school: the Day of Indigenous Resistance

Soon after classes begin the school remembers October's resistance day, turning the calendar itself into a history lesson

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Venezuela days to remember as Venezuela's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
