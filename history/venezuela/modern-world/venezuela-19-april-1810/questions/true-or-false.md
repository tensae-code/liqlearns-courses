# Venezuela 19 april 1810 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "19 April 1810", caracas's city council day becomes a classroom play children write, direct and stage themselves — the first step from colony to republic
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "When the people said: we do not want him!", on 19 April 1810 Caracas's crowd rejects the captain general — the people, not only the council, open emancipation
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
