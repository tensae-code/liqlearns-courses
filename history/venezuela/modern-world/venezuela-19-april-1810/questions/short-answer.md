# Venezuela 19 april 1810 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "19 April 1810" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Caracas's city council day becomes a classroom play children write, direct and stage themselves — the first step from colony to republic

**2.** Tell the story of "When the people said: we do not want him!" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** On 19 April 1810 Caracas's crowd rejects the captain general — the people, not only the council, open emancipation
