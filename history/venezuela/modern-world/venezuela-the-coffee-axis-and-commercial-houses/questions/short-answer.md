# Venezuela the coffee axis and commercial houses — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The coffee axis and the foreign commercial houses" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Coffee ties Venezuela to the world market, but English, German, Italian and US houses finance, ship and profit from the crop while the country stays a mono-producer

**2.** Tell the story of "When coffee lit up our history" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Coffee becomes the light by which the nineteenth-century republic is seen — its money, its ports and its politics all run on the bean
