# Venezuela the coffee axis and commercial houses — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "The coffee axis and the foreign commercial houses", coffee ties Venezuela to the world market, but English, German, Italian and US houses finance, ship and profit from the crop while the country stays a mono-producer
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "When coffee lit up our history", coffee becomes the light by which the nineteenth-century republic is seen — its money, its ports and its politics all run on the bean
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
