# Venezuela the 1902 blockade and drago doctrine — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "The blockade of Venezuelan ports, 1902–1903", european fleets blockade the ports to collect debts by force; the Drago Doctrine answers that public debt can never justify armed intervention, settled at Washington in 1903
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "A December of cannon fire: 1902", in December 1902 foreign warships blockade Venezuela violently over its debts, and a child of the time hears the guns
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
