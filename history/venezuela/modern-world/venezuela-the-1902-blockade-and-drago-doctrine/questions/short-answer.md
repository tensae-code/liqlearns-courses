# Venezuela the 1902 blockade and drago doctrine — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The blockade of Venezuelan ports, 1902–1903" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** European fleets blockade the ports to collect debts by force; the Drago Doctrine answers that public debt can never justify armed intervention, settled at Washington in 1903

**2.** Tell the story of "A December of cannon fire: 1902" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** In December 1902 foreign warships blockade Venezuela violently over its debts, and a child of the time hears the guns
