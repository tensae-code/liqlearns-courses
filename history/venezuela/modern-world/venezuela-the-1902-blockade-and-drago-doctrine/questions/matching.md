# Venezuela the 1902 blockade and drago doctrine — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** The blockade of Venezuelan ports, 1902–1903 — a)
**2.** A December of cannon fire: 1902 — b)

Meanings:
a) European fleets blockade the ports to collect debts by force; the Drago Doctrine answers that public debt can never justify armed intervention, settled at Washington in 1903
