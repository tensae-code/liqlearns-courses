# Venezuela the 1902 blockade and drago doctrine — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The blockade of Venezuelan ports, 1902–1903" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** European fleets blockade the ports to collect debts by force; the Drago Doctrine answers that public debt can never justify armed intervention, settled at Washington in 1903

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "A December of cannon fire: 1902" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** In December 1902 foreign warships blockade Venezuela violently over its debts, and a child of the time hears the guns
