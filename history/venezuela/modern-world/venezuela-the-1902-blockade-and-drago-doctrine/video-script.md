# Video script — Venezuela the 1902 blockade and drago doctrine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Venezuela the 1902 blockade and drago doctrine.

European fleets blockade the ports to collect debts by force; the Drago Doctrine answers that public debt can never justify armed intervention, settled at Washington in 1903

In December 1902 foreign warships blockade Venezuela violently over its debts, and a child of the time hears the guns

British, German and Italian warships blockade the coasts to collect debts by force, the crisis that produces the Drago answer

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
