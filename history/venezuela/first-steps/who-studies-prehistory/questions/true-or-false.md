# Who studies prehistory — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Our most remote ancestors: a look at the past", before any written Venezuela, remote ancestors leave the traces from which the country's deepest past must be read
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "A settlement hard to find out; peopling a country that had no name yet", how America was first peopled is a detective problem of routes and evidence, long before the land had the name Venezuela
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
