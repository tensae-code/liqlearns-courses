# Video script — Who studies prehistory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Who studies prehistory.

Before any written Venezuela, remote ancestors leave the traces from which the country's deepest past must be read

How America was first peopled is a detective problem of routes and evidence, long before the land had the name Venezuela

The first Americans cross into a new continent and reach the land that will be Venezuela, the deepest root of its people

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
