# Video script — Find history in your own locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Find history in your own locality.

An old song-and-dance at the school party is traced back to indigenous peoples and to Africans the Spanish brought by force long ago

Children are sent out to ask their own community what it was before, making the neighbourhood the first archive

A barrio writes its own charter from its founding, its first settlers and their shared struggles — the settlement's past becomes its identity document

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
