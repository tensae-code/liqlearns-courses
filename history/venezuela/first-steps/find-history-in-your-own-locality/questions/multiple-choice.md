# Find history in your own locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "The school invites the community: Los Chimichimitos"?
a) An old song-and-dance at the school party is traced back to indigenous peoples and to Africans the Spanish brought by force long ago  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
