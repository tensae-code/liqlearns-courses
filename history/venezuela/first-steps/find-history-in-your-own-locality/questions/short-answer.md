# Find history in your own locality — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The school invites the community: Los Chimichimitos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** An old song-and-dance at the school party is traced back to indigenous peoples and to Africans the Spanish brought by force long ago

**2.** Tell the story of "The teacher invites us to know the community's present and past" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Children are sent out to ask their own community what it was before, making the neighbourhood the first archive
