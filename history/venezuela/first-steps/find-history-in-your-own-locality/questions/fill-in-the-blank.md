# Find history in your own locality — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The school invites the community: Los Chimichimitos" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** An old song-and-dance at the school party is traced back to indigenous peoples and to Africans the Spanish brought by force long ago

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The teacher invites us to know the community's present and past" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Children are sent out to ask their own community what it was before, making the neighbourhood the first archive
