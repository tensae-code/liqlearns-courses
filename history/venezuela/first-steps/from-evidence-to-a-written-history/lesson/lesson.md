A grandmother's childhood memory — her mother citing the law to president López Contreras, who boasted of obeying it — is used as living evidence

## Peoples have history and people do too

## What times those were: Ana Graciela's personal history of Maracaibo

A woman born in 1924 rebuilds her city's past from family photos, parish archives and elders' talk, and her lifetime measures how oil remade Maracaibo
