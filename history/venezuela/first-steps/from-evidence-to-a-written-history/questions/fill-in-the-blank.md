# From evidence to a written history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "When I was little: a mother's memory of López Contreras" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A grandmother's childhood memory — her mother citing the law to president López Contreras, who boasted of obeying it — is used as living evidence

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Peoples have history and people do too" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Between the papers a family keeps at home, a child's own third-grade computer memory becomes a dated source beside the nation's papers
