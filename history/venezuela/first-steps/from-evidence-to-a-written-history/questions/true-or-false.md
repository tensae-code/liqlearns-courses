# From evidence to a written history — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "When I was little: a mother's memory of López Contreras", a grandmother's childhood memory — her mother citing the law to president López Contreras, who boasted of obeying it — is used as living evidence
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Peoples have history and people do too", between the papers a family keeps at home, a child's own third-grade computer memory becomes a dated source beside the nation's papers
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
