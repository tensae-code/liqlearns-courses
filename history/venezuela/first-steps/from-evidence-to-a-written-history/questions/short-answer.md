# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "When I was little: a mother's memory of López Contreras" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A grandmother's childhood memory — her mother citing the law to president López Contreras, who boasted of obeying it — is used as living evidence

**2.** Tell the story of "Peoples have history and people do too" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Between the papers a family keeps at home, a child's own third-grade computer memory becomes a dated source beside the nation's papers
