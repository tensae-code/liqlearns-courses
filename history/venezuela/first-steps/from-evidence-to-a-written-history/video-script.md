# Video script — From evidence to a written history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: From evidence to a written history.

A grandmother's childhood memory — her mother citing the law to president López Contreras, who boasted of obeying it — is used as living evidence

Between the papers a family keeps at home, a child's own third-grade computer memory becomes a dated source beside the nation's papers

A woman born in 1924 rebuilds her city's past from family photos, parish archives and elders' talk, and her lifetime measures how oil remade Maracaibo

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
