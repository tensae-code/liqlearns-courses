# Dating the past — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Day, night, week and yesterday/today/tomorrow", the Sun rises and sets, children count the seven steps up to Sunday again and learn that yesterday is already the past
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "The train of life: my own past in stages", every person rides the same train — born, grows, studies, works, ages — so a child's own life is told as an ordered timeline with a last wagon
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
