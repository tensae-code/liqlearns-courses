# Dating the past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Day, night, week and yesterday/today/tomorrow"?
a) The Sun rises and sets, children count the seven steps up to Sunday again and learn that yesterday is already the past  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "The train of life: my own past in stages"?
a) Every person rides the same train — born, grows, studies, works, ages — so a child's own life is told as an ordered timeline with a last wagon  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
