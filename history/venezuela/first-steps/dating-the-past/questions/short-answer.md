# Dating the past — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Day, night, week and yesterday/today/tomorrow" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The Sun rises and sets, children count the seven steps up to Sunday again and learn that yesterday is already the past

**2.** Tell the story of "The train of life: my own past in stages" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Every person rides the same train — born, grows, studies, works, ages — so a child's own life is told as an ordered timeline with a last wagon
