# What history is — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Changes that have happened (before/now)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Families once lived mostly in the countryside with no television or computer; now most live in cities with infocentres and Canaima laptops in school

**2.** Tell the story of ""…And our Venezuela said: why have they changed me again and again?"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Venezuela speaks in first person — a cacique's blood watered her soil, and later "thanks to oil they centralised me" — the country narrating its own changes
