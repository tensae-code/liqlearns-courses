# Video script — What history is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: What history is.

Families once lived mostly in the countryside with no television or computer; now most live in cities with infocentres and Canaima laptops in school

Venezuela speaks in first person — a cacique's blood watered her soil, and later "thanks to oil they centralised me" — the country narrating its own changes

Peoples are not stuck with the history they inherited; as things change, a people rewrites its own story

Neruda's line frames the lesson — the present is a succession of pasts with traces, and the future is decided by the people in the present

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
