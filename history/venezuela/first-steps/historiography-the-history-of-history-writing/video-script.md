# Video script — Historiography the history of history writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Historiography the history of history writing.

The author tells students history need not run in a straight line; constitutions, a political story and three axes are different doors into the same dependent country

A founding legend about Coro is tested against what the Spanish official Juan de Ampíes, based in La Española, actually did

A settlement myth is weighed by the invisible anthropologist Gliptocoro against evidence for how America was really peopled

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
