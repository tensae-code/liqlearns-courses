# Historiography the history of history writing — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "How this book writes history: dependence, axes and recurrent time" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The author tells students history need not run in a straight line; constitutions, a political story and three axes are different doors into the same dependent country

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "From legend to historical truth: Juan de Ampíes" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A founding legend about Coro is tested against what the Spanish official Juan de Ampíes, based in La Española, actually did
