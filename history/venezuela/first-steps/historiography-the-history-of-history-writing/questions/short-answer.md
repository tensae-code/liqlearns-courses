# Historiography the history of history writing — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "How this book writes history: dependence, axes and recurrent time" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** The author tells students history need not run in a straight line; constitutions, a political story and three axes are different doors into the same dependent country

**2.** Tell the story of "From legend to historical truth: Juan de Ampíes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A founding legend about Coro is tested against what the Spanish official Juan de Ampíes, based in La Española, actually did
