# Historiography the history of history writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "How this book writes history: dependence, axes and recurrent time"?
a) The author tells students history need not run in a straight line; constitutions, a political story and three axes are different doors into the same dependent country  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
