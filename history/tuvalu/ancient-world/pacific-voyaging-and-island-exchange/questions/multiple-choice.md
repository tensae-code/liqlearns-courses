# Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What joined Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea into one community?
a) one king ruling all the islands  b) regular voyaging between the islands across one sea  c) a bridge built between the islands  d) nothing — the islands were never joined
**Answer: b) regular voyaging between the islands across one sea** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the voyaging circulate between the islands?
a) only fish  b) only stories  c) wealth, skills and goods  d) nothing — the canoes travelled empty
**Answer: c) wealth, skills and goods** — That is what this lesson teaches.
