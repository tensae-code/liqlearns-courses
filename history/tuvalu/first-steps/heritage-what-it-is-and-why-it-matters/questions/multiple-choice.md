# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How are whales told of across the Pacific, in the people's stories? (Sub lesson 1)
a) as guardians and ancestors  b) as enemies of the islands  c) as cargo for ships  d) as weather signs only
**Answer: a) as guardians and ancestors** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did Tuvalu's pulaka come from, in the case study's story? (Sub lesson 4)
a) it was bought from a shop overseas  b) travellers from Kiribati brought the giant swamp taro to Tuvalu  c) it grew wild in the lagoon  d) whaling fleets left it behind
**Answer: b) travellers from Kiribati brought the giant swamp taro to Tuvalu** — That is what this lesson teaches.
