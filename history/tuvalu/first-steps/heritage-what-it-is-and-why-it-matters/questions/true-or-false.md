# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On Nukufetau, women and children gathered shellfish, and men dived for pearl shells to make fish hooks. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In the teacher guide's Tuvalu section, a lagoon such as Namo is closed to all use only after it has been emptied for good. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer: False — the ancestors' way is to close the lagoon to all use until it is healthy again, so it recovers.**
