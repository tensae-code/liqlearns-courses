# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How was the Pacific people's understanding of the environment carried through time? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** It was passed down through the generations — in stories, poems, legends and songs, and by elders showing the young: knowing when to plant, fish and spare the land was an inheritance carried in memory from grandparents to children.

**2.** What five things does the coconut palm give, in the case study? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Food, drink, oil, fibre and shelter — and in Tuvalu its sap is also tapped for traditional red toddy.
