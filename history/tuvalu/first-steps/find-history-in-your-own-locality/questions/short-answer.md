# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the elder asked about, in the guide's Book 1 method?
**Answer:** What they know of sacred and traditional practices — and the class lists traditional fishing practices and the fish that were caught, and finds a legend from their own culture about the turtle, whale or shark.

**2.** Why must what the elder remembers be written down?
**Answer:** Because it lives only in the elder's memory — the class writes it down before it is lost, so it can be handed on.
