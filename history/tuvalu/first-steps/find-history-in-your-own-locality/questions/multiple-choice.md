# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the teacher guide's method for Book 1, who are pupils sent to talk to?
a) a shopkeeper  b) an elder  c) a visiting sailor  d) nobody — they only read the reader again
**Answer: b) an elder** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What story-task does the guide give the class, alongside the elder talk?
a) write a song about the sea  b) draw a map of Tonga  c) find a legend from their own culture about the turtle, whale or shark  d) memorise the reader's last page
**Answer: c) find a legend from their own culture about the turtle, whale or shark** — That is what this lesson teaches.
