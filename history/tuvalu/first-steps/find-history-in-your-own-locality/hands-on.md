# Hands-on — Find History in Your Own Locality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say your chosen elder's name and your two questions again, as the opening act taught. Hold them in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and make the class list of this lesson yourself: head the page "Traditional practices remembered in my place." Under it, write two columns — the practice, and who remembers it. Fill in two rows from people you actually know, or mark them "to ask" if you must ask first. Say each row aloud as you write it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for unwritten knowledge in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time an older person knows something by memory that nobody has written down — a practice, a story, how a thing was done — note it: what it is, who holds it, and what the lesson says to do about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
