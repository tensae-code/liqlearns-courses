# European Whaling in the Pacific — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the Pacific people's own way with whales, before the Europeans came?
a) they hunted whales at sea every season  b) they used only stranded whales, taking teeth for chiefs' necklaces  c) they kept whales in lagoons  d) they never used any part of a whale
**Answer: b) they used only stranded whales, taking teeth for chiefs' necklaces** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who reached Tonga in the 1890s and changed everything for the whales?
a) travellers from Kiribati  b) a single trading canoe  c) North American whaling fleets  d) the fleets of Niue's districts
**Answer: c) North American whaling fleets** — That is what this lesson teaches.
