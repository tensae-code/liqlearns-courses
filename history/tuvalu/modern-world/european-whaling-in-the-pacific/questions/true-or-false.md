# European Whaling in the Pacific — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The whaling fleets spared mothers and calves, hunting only old whales.
**Answer: False — the fleets hunted mothers and calves too, which is part of why the whales fell so fast.**

**2.** Under legal protection, the whales slowly recover.
**Answer: True**
