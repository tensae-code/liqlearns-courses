# European Whaling in the Pacific — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How fast did the whales fall, and after how long of safety?
**Answer:** After centuries of living safely beside the islands, they were driven near extinction in one century of commercial hunting.

**2.** What did Pacific people take from stranded whales, and for whom?
**Answer:** Their teeth, for chiefs' necklaces.
