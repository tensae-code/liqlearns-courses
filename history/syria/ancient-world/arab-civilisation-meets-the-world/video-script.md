# Video script — Arab Civilisation Meets the World


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The medieval book closes with an opening. Arab-Islamic civilisation does not grow in a sealed garden: it opens itself to Greek, Persian and Indian cultures — some old-rooted in its lands, some newly arrived by book and caravan — takes what they offer, and transforms it. Then the movement reverses: its own creations go out to the world. From creativity to the universal, من الإبداع إلى العالمية — that two-way road is the book's final word on the age.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
