# Arab Civilisation Meets the World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson 4: من الإبداع إلى العالمية (From creativity to the universal)”?
**Answer:** The medieval book closes with an opening. Arab-Islamic civilisation does not grow in a sealed garden: it opens itself to Greek, Persian and Indian cultures — some old-rooted in its lands, some newly arrived by book and caravan — takes what they offer, and transforms it. Then the movement reverses: its own creations go out to the world. From creativity to the universal, من الإبداع إلى العالمية — that two-way road is the book's final word on the age.
