# Arab Civilisation Meets the World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Arab-Islamic civilisation opens to Greek, Persian and Indian cultures, old-rooted and newly arrived, and its creations go out to the world.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 4, Lesson 4: من الإبداع إلى العالمية (From creativity to the universal)” is mainly about this: The Constantine massacre of 8 May 1945, in which 45,000 Algerians were martyred, taught the national movement that only arms would free the country; the Great Algerian Revolution opened on 1 November 1954 from the Aurès, a provisional government formed in Cairo in 1958, De Gaulle failed to crush it, and the Evian accords brought independence on 5 July 1962 after a struggle that cost over a million martyrs.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Arab-Islamic civilisation opens to Greek, Persian and Indian cultures, old-rooted and newly arrived, and its creations go out to the world.
