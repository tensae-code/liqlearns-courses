# Crafts and Industry in Islamic Syria — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Arab craftsmen turn local and imported raw materials into masterworks admired by the great cities of the world.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 3, Lesson 2: من الحاجة إلى الإبداع (From need to creativity)” is mainly about this: Revolt followed revolt — Jaffa 1921, al-Buraq 1929 — until the Great Palestinian Revolt of 1936: Izz al-Din al-Qassam fell near Jenin, the strike and the fighting spread with Arab volunteers including Syrians, Britain answered with the Peel partition plan of 1937, which the Palestinians rejected, and the White Paper of 1939, which both sides rejected.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Arab craftsmen turn local and imported raw materials into masterworks admired by the great cities of the world.
