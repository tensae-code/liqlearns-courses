# Crafts and Industry in Islamic Syria — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson 2: من الحاجة إلى الإبداع (From need to creativity)”?
**Answer:** The lesson's title is an arc: from need to creativity. Arab craftsmen begin with what the land gives — local raw materials, and imported ones arriving on the trade routes — and end with masterworks: textiles, glass, metalwork and carved wood admired by the great cities of the world. Need starts the work, but creativity finishes it, and Islamic Syria's workshops become a byword for excellence far beyond its borders.
