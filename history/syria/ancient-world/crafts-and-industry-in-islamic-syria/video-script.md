# Video script — Crafts and Industry in Islamic Syria


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The lesson's title is an arc: from need to creativity. Arab craftsmen begin with what the land gives — local raw materials, and imported ones arriving on the trade routes — and end with masterworks: textiles, glass, metalwork and carved wood admired by the great cities of the world. Need starts the work, but creativity finishes it, and Islamic Syria's workshops become a byword for excellence far beyond its borders.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
