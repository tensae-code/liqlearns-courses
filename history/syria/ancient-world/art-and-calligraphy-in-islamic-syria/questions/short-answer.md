# Art and Calligraphy in Islamic Syria — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson 3: ثقافة الفن (The culture of art)”?
**Answer:** Art in the Arab-Islamic world is not one frozen style. Painting, sculpture and calligraphy renew themselves in every place and every age, each generation reshaping the inherited forms to carry its own hopes. Calligraphy above all — the art of the written word — rises until the letter itself becomes the civilisation's signature ornament. The culture of art, the lesson says, is how a people dreams in public.
