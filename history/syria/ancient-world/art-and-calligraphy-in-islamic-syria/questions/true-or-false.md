# Art and Calligraphy in Islamic Syria — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Painting, sculpture and calligraphy renew themselves in every place and age to carry a people's hopes.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 4, Lesson 3: ثقافة الفن (The culture of art)” is mainly about this: The Portuguese seized the East African coast in 1505–1506, but their rule never settled: the Ya'rubi state of Oman sent the fleet that expelled them in 1698 and extended Arab influence down the coast, and Zanzibar stayed under the Busaid sultans from 1839.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Painting, sculpture and calligraphy renew themselves in every place and age to carry a people's hopes.
