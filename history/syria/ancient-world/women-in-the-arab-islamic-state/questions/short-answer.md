# Women in the Arab-Islamic State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson 2: شريكة في إعمار الحياة (A partner in building life)”?
**Answer:** The Grade 8 book gives women of the Arab state a title that is also a verdict: شريكة في إعمار الحياة — a partner in building life. The lesson follows their creating everywhere it happened: in the home, in the sciences, in letters, in the crafts, in the markets, and even in politics and administration. As with the ancient lesson, the medieval story is told whole — the civilisation was built by all its people.
