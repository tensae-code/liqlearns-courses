# Women in the Arab-Islamic State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Women of the Arab state create in the home, the sciences, letters, crafts, markets, politics and administration.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 2, Lesson 2: شريكة في إعمار الحياة (A partner in building life)” is mainly about this: Under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals; after independence from French colonialism in 1946 the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew past 23 million by 2010.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Women of the Arab state create in the home, the sciences, letters, crafts, markets, politics and administration.
