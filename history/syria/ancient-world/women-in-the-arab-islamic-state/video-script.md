# Video script — Women in the Arab-Islamic State


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The Grade 8 book gives women of the Arab state a title that is also a verdict: شريكة في إعمار الحياة — a partner in building life. The lesson follows their creating everywhere it happened: in the home, in the sciences, in letters, in the crafts, in the markets, and even in politics and administration. As with the ancient lesson, the medieval story is told whole — the civilisation was built by all its people.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
