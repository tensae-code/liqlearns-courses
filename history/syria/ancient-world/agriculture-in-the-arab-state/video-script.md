# Video script — Agriculture in the Arab State


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Once the Arab state settles and its roads are safe, farming expands on every side: more land brought under the plough, more water carried to dry fields, peasants better off than before. And farming becomes a science — علوم الفلاحة — studied, written down and improved, as scholars of the state turn their minds to soils, seasons and irrigation. The lesson roots the state's wealth where the book believes it belongs: in the cultivated earth.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
