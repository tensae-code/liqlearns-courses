# Agriculture in the Arab State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson 1: علوم الفلاحة (The sciences of farming)”?
**Answer:** Once the Arab state settles and its roads are safe, farming expands on every side: more land brought under the plough, more water carried to dry fields, peasants better off than before. And farming becomes a science — علوم الفلاحة — studied, written down and improved, as scholars of the state turn their minds to soils, seasons and irrigation. The lesson roots the state's wealth where the book believes it belongs: in the cultivated earth.
