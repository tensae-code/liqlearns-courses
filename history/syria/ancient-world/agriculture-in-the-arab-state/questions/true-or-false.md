# Agriculture in the Arab State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Once the Arab state settles, farming expands — more land, more water, better-off peasants — and agricultural science flourishes.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 3, Lesson 1: علوم الفلاحة (The sciences of farming)” is mainly about this: Hafez al-Assad's coup of 16 November 1970 began some thirty years of despotism — the massacres of Jisr al-Shughur, Aleppo and Hama in 1982, and tens of thousands disappeared in the prisons; the rule was inherited by his son Bashar in 2000; when the children of Daraa were arrested and tortured for writing freedom slogans, the revolution of March 2011 rose, and the story ends with the liberation of all Syria's land and the fall of Bashar al-Assad's regime on 8 December 2024.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Once the Arab state settles, farming expands — more land, more water, better-off peasants — and agricultural science flourishes.
