# Agriculture in the Arab State

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Agriculture in the Arab State** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Once the Arab state settles and its roads are safe, farming expands on every side: more land brought under the plough, more water carried to dry fields, peasants better off than before. And farming becomes a science — علوم الفلاحة — studied, written down and improved, as scholars of the state turn their minds to soils, seasons and irrigation. The lesson roots the state's wealth where the book believes it belongs: in the cultivated earth.
