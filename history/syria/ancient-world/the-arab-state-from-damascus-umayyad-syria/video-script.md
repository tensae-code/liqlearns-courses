# Video script — The Arab State from Damascus: Umayyad Syria


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

After liberation comes building. The Arabs raise their world state with Damascus at its heart — the capital of an empire — and give that state its own character: its own administration, its own coinage and post, its own style in building and ruling. States and civilisation, the lesson's title joins them: the state is the frame, and the civilisation is what grows inside it, with Syria's capital at the centre of both.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
