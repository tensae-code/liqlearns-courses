# The Arab State from Damascus: Umayyad Syria

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Arab State from Damascus: Umayyad Syria** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

After liberation comes building. The Arabs raise their world state with Damascus at its heart — the capital of an empire — and give that state its own character: its own administration, its own coinage and post, its own style in building and ruling. States and civilisation, the lesson's title joins them: the state is the frame, and the civilisation is what grows inside it, with Syria's capital at the centre of both.
