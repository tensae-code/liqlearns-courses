# The Arab State from Damascus: Umayyad Syria — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson 2: دول وحضارة (States and civilisation)”?
**Answer:** After liberation comes building. The Arabs raise their world state with Damascus at its heart — the capital of an empire — and give that state its own character: its own administration, its own coinage and post, its own style in building and ruling. States and civilisation, the lesson's title joins them: the state is the frame, and the civilisation is what grows inside it, with Syria's capital at the centre of both.
