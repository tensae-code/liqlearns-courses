# The Arab State from Damascus: Umayyad Syria — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   After liberation the Arabs raise their world state with Damascus at its heart and give it its own character.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson 2: دول وحضارة (States and civilisation)” is mainly about this: A divided Arab state is invaded — Europeans come as Crusaders and others from the east — and a civilisation of thousands of years is put to the sword.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After liberation the Arabs raise their world state with Damascus at its heart and give it its own character.
