# Syria Between Crusaders and Mongols — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   A divided Arab state is invaded — Europeans come as Crusaders and others from the east — and a civilisation of thousands of years is put to the sword.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson 4: أطماع وحروب (Greed and wars)” is mainly about this: France occupied Fez in 1911 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Fez in 1912; Abd al-Krim al-Khattabi's Rif revolt destroyed the Spanish army at Annual in 1921 and proclaimed the Republic of the Rif before the joint Franco-Spanish assault exiled him in 1926; France exiled Sultan Muhammad V to Madagascar in 1953, and the armed struggle forced his return in 1955 and independence in 1956.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A divided Arab state is invaded — Europeans come as Crusaders and others from the east — and a civilisation of thousands of years is put to the sword.
