# Syria Between Crusaders and Mongols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson 4: أطماع وحروب (Greed and wars)”?
**Answer:** The Arab state's weakness invites invasion. A divided state — its unity broken — is attacked from two directions at once: Europeans come from the west as Crusaders, and others sweep in from the east, the Mongol storm. The lesson's title names the motive without flinching: أطماع وحروب, greed and wars. A civilisation of thousands of years, the book mourns, is put to the sword — and Syria stands in the path of both invasions.
