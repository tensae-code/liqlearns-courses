# Video script — Learning and Translation in the Arab State


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Writing, in all its stages, is what saves history from loss — that conviction drives the lesson. A hunger to record knowledge seizes the Arab state: books are copied, libraries filled, and the learning of older peoples gathered and translated into Arabic. Out of that hunger comes a creative age, إبداعات ثقافية, cultural creations in every field — because a civilisation that writes everything down can build on everything it knows.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
