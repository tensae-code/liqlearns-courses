# Lesson plan — Syria's Historic Cities: Palmyra, Bosra and Apamea

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Syria's Historic Cities: Palmyra, Bosra and Apamea
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Syria and the First Civilisations (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Palmyra in the middle of the Badia with its temples, tombs and colonnaded street ending at the triumphal arch, Bosra's great amphitheatre in Hawran, Apamea above the Ghab plain, and Rusafa, the desert city the Umayyad caliph Hisham took as his retreat.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Syria and the First Civilisations (recommended, never forced).
