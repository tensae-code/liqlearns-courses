# The Society of Medieval Syria: Roots and Mixture

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Society of Medieval Syria: Roots and Mixture** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Who are the Syrians of the medieval centuries? The book answers: a historical mixture. Syria's society was shaped by its land, by its crossroads position — every road between continents passes through it — and by the peoples who came and settled across the ages. Roots and extension: deep roots in the soil, and branches reaching out to every neighbour. Society itself, the lesson teaches, is a product of history.
