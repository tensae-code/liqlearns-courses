# The Society of Medieval Syria: Roots and Mixture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson 1: جذور وامتداد (Roots and extension)”?
**Answer:** Who are the Syrians of the medieval centuries? The book answers: a historical mixture. Syria's society was shaped by its land, by its crossroads position — every road between continents passes through it — and by the peoples who came and settled across the ages. Roots and extension: deep roots in the soil, and branches reaching out to every neighbour. Society itself, the lesson teaches, is a product of history.
