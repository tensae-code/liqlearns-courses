# The Society of Medieval Syria: Roots and Mixture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Syria's society is a historical mixture shaped by its land, its crossroads position and the peoples who settled it.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 2, Lesson 1: جذور وامتداد (Roots and extension)” is mainly about this: The 1928 constitution and the 1932 parliament elected Muhammad Ali al-Abid first president; the sixty-day strike forced the 1936 treaty project; the nationalists won the 1943 elections under Shukri al-Quwatli; France bombarded the parliament on 29 May 1945, and the French forces evacuated on 17 April 1946.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Syria's society is a historical mixture shaped by its land, its crossroads position and the peoples who settled it.
