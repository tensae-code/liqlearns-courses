# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Cave drawings served the first hunters, but a settled, trading, law-making world needs to say far more than a picture can carry. So writing is invented — and with writing, knowledge begins to travel: a message can cross a desert, a law can outlive its king, a story can reach grandchildren a hundred generations away. The spread of knowledge, the book teaches, is the moment civilisation learns to remember everything.

How far did these peoples climb? The lesson measures them by their finest work: sculpture that still commands a museum room, music organised into art, and science that mapped the heavens — preserved for us in works like the royal treasures of Mari, dug from Syria's own soil. Artistic masterpieces and scientific achievements are not decoration on the story; they are the record of how high a civilisation rose.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
