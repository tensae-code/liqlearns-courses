# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Cave drawings cannot carry all that people need to say, so writing is invented and knowledge begins to travel.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 4, Lesson 4: تحف فنية ومنجزات علمية (Artistic masterpieces and scientific achievements)” is mainly about this: Writing calls schools and libraries into being, the Arab region's gift to all later learning.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Sculpture, music and science record how far these peoples climbed, preserved in works like the royal treasures of Mari.
