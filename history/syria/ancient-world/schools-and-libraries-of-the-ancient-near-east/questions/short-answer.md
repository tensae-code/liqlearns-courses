# Schools and Libraries of the Ancient Near East — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson 2: مدارس ومكتبات (Schools and libraries)”?
**Answer:** Writing creates a problem: someone must be taught to write. So writing calls schools into being, where the wedge and the reed pen pass to a new generation — and libraries, where the tablets are gathered, ordered and kept. The book claims this proudly as the Arab region's gift to all later learning: every school and library in the world today descends from these first classrooms and tablet-rooms of the ancient Near East.
