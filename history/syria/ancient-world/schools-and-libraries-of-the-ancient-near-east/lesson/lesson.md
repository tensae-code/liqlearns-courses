# Schools and Libraries of the Ancient Near East

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Schools and Libraries of the Ancient Near East** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Writing creates a problem: someone must be taught to write. So writing calls schools into being, where the wedge and the reed pen pass to a new generation — and libraries, where the tablets are gathered, ordered and kept. The book claims this proudly as the Arab region's gift to all later learning: every school and library in the world today descends from these first classrooms and tablet-rooms of the ancient Near East.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
