# Video script — Schools and Libraries of the Ancient Near East


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Writing creates a problem: someone must be taught to write. So writing calls schools into being, where the wedge and the reed pen pass to a new generation — and libraries, where the tablets are gathered, ordered and kept. The book claims this proudly as the Arab region's gift to all later learning: every school and library in the world today descends from these first classrooms and tablet-rooms of the ancient Near East.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
