# Communication: From Signal Fires to the Umayyad Post — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   People once signalled with loud sounds, fire lit on mountain tops and castles by night and smoke by day, carrier pigeons and lighthouse signals for coastal cities; in the Umayyad age Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan founded the Diwan al-Barid, a state bureau in Damascus to carry the post and the news across the state.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 2, Lesson وسائل الاتصال (Means of communication)” is mainly about this: Fathers show the tools and machines of their trades then and now, and children match each old tool to the faster machine that replaced it.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: People once signalled with loud sounds, fire lit on mountain tops and castles by night and smoke by day, carrier pigeons and lighthouse signals for coastal cities; in the Umayyad age Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan founded the Diwan al-Barid, a state bureau in Damascus to carry the post and the news across the state.
