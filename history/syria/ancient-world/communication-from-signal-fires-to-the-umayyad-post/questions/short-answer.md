# Communication: From Signal Fires to the Umayyad Post — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson وسائل الاتصال (Means of communication)”?
**Answer:** Before wires and waves, how did news travel? The lesson climbs the ladder of signals: loud sounds that carry across a valley; fire lit on mountain tops and castle towers by night, smoke by day; carrier pigeons beating home faster than any horse; lighthouse signals flashing to the coastal cities. Then the state organises it all: in the Umayyad age, Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan founds the Diwan al-Barid, a state bureau in Damascus to carry the post and the news across the whole state — the signal fire become a system.
