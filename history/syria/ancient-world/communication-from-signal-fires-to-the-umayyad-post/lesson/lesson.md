# Communication: From Signal Fires to the Umayyad Post

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Communication: From Signal Fires to the Umayyad Post** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Before wires and waves, how did news travel? The lesson climbs the ladder of signals: loud sounds that carry across a valley; fire lit on mountain tops and castle towers by night, smoke by day; carrier pigeons beating home faster than any horse; lighthouse signals flashing to the coastal cities. Then the state organises it all: in the Umayyad age, Muawiya ibn Abi Sufyan founds the Diwan al-Barid, a state bureau in Damascus to carry the post and the news across the whole state — the signal fire become a system.
