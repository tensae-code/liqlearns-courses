# Video script — Feasts and Celebrations in Islamic Syria


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

A society shows its bonds on its feast days. The lesson gathers them all: religious feasts, official celebrations, the seasonal feasts of the farming year, and the folk feasts of town and village. Rulers watch over them and share in them, and for those days the whole society — high and low, city and country — is bound together in one calendar of joy. Feasts, the book says, are the threads that stitch a people into one cloth.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
