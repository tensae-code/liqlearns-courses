# Feasts and Celebrations in Islamic Syria

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Feasts and Celebrations in Islamic Syria** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

A society shows its bonds on its feast days. The lesson gathers them all: religious feasts, official celebrations, the seasonal feasts of the farming year, and the folk feasts of town and village. Rulers watch over them and share in them, and for those days the whole society — high and low, city and country — is bound together in one calendar of joy. Feasts, the book says, are the threads that stitch a people into one cloth.
