# Video script — Islamic Resistance Figures: Nur al-Din and Omar al-Mukhtar


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Two resisters, eight centuries apart, are held up in one lesson as one example. Nur al-Din Zengi unites the Islamic lands against the Crusaders, gathering a divided world into a front that his successor Salah al-Din will lead to Hattin. Centuries later, in Libya, Omar al-Mukhtar — an old teacher turned guerrilla — fights the Italian coloniser from the desert. Different ages, different invaders; the book's point is the sameness: when occupation comes, resistance answers.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
