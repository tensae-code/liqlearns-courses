# Islamic Resistance Figures: Nur al-Din and Omar al-Mukhtar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson 4: شخصيات إسلامية (Islamic personalities)”?
**Answer:** Two resisters, eight centuries apart, are held up in one lesson as one example. Nur al-Din Zengi unites the Islamic lands against the Crusaders, gathering a divided world into a front that his successor Salah al-Din will lead to Hattin. Centuries later, in Libya, Omar al-Mukhtar — an old teacher turned guerrilla — fights the Italian coloniser from the desert. Different ages, different invaders; the book's point is the sameness: when occupation comes, resistance answers.
