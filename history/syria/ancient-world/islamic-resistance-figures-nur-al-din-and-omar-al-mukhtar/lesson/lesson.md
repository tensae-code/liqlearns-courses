# Islamic Resistance Figures: Nur al-Din and Omar al-Mukhtar

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Islamic Resistance Figures: Nur al-Din and Omar al-Mukhtar** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Two resisters, eight centuries apart, are held up in one lesson as one example. Nur al-Din Zengi unites the Islamic lands against the Crusaders, gathering a divided world into a front that his successor Salah al-Din will lead to Hattin. Centuries later, in Libya, Omar al-Mukhtar — an old teacher turned guerrilla — fights the Italian coloniser from the desert. Different ages, different invaders; the book's point is the sameness: when occupation comes, resistance answers.
