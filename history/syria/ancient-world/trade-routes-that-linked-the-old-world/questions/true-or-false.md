# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Lapis, carnelian and rock crystal travel far routes to reach Syria by 3200 BCE, and villages begin to need each other's goods.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 2, Lesson 4: وسائل التجارة (The means of trade)” is mainly about this: Kings hold their states together with officials, laws, institutions and treaties.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Barter gives way to coined money, taxes and ships as trade grows too big for swapping goods.
