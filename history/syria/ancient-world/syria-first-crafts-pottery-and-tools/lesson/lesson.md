# Syria's First Crafts: Pottery and Tools

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Syria's First Crafts: Pottery and Tools** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Once people settled, their hands grew clever. The lesson — 'the art of using materials' — watches stone, bone, wood and clay pass through human fingers and come out changed: stone becomes a blade, bone a needle, wood a handle, clay a pot that can carry water and store grain. Raw nature becomes craft, and craft becomes the foundation on which every later industry stands. Syria's first makers are the ancestors of all its famous craftsmen.
