# Syria's First Crafts: Pottery and Tools — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson 2: فن استخدام المادة (The art of using materials)”?
**Answer:** Once people settled, their hands grew clever. The lesson — 'the art of using materials' — watches stone, bone, wood and clay pass through human fingers and come out changed: stone becomes a blade, bone a needle, wood a handle, clay a pot that can carry water and store grain. Raw nature becomes craft, and craft becomes the foundation on which every later industry stands. Syria's first makers are the ancestors of all its famous craftsmen.
