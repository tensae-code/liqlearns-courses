# Video script — Syria's First Crafts: Pottery and Tools


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Once people settled, their hands grew clever. The lesson — 'the art of using materials' — watches stone, bone, wood and clay pass through human fingers and come out changed: stone becomes a blade, bone a needle, wood a handle, clay a pot that can carry water and store grain. Raw nature becomes craft, and craft becomes the foundation on which every later industry stands. Syria's first makers are the ancestors of all its famous craftsmen.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
