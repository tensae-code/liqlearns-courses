# Women in Ancient Syria and the Near East

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Women in Ancient Syria and the Near East** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

When farming begins and the first crafts are born, the Grade 7 book refuses to tell the story with only half its people. Women work beside men in the field, in the home and in the workshop — planting, weaving, shaping, storing — and in that shared work settled society is founded. The lesson restores them to the centre of the story: the first villages of Syria and the Near East were built by women's hands as much as by men's.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: When farming and the first crafts begin, women work beside men in field, home and workshop and help found settled society. Give one real example: When farming and the first crafts begin, women work beside men in field, home and workshop and help found settled society. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
