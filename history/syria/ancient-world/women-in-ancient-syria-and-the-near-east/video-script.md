# Video script — Women in Ancient Syria and the Near East


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

When farming begins and the first crafts are born, the Grade 7 book refuses to tell the story with only half its people. Women work beside men in the field, in the home and in the workshop — planting, weaving, shaping, storing — and in that shared work settled society is founded. The lesson restores them to the centre of the story: the first villages of Syria and the Near East were built by women's hands as much as by men's.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
