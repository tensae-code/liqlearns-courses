# Women in Ancient Syria and the Near East — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson 2: المرأة ودورها في بناء المجتمع (Women and their role in building society)”?
**Answer:** When farming begins and the first crafts are born, the Grade 7 book refuses to tell the story with only half its people. Women work beside men in the field, in the home and in the workshop — planting, weaving, shaping, storing — and in that shared work settled society is founded. The lesson restores them to the centre of the story: the first villages of Syria and the Near East were built by women's hands as much as by men's.
