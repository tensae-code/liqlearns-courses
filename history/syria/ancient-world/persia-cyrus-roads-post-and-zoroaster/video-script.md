# Video script — Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Once empires exist, they compete — and the Arab lands, rich and perfectly placed between continents, become the prize. The book calls this lesson 'colonial rivalry': foreign empires race to seize the region for its position and its riches. First among the racers stand the Achaemenid Persians, whose empire (559–330 BCE) reaches over Syria and far beyond, setting the pattern every later empire would follow.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
