# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Once empires exist, they compete — and the Arab lands, rich and perfectly placed between continents, become the prize. The book calls this lesson 'colonial rivalry': foreign empires race to seize the region for its position and its riches. First among the racers stand the Achaemenid Persians, whose empire (559–330 BCE) reaches over Syria and far beyond, setting the pattern every later empire would follow.
