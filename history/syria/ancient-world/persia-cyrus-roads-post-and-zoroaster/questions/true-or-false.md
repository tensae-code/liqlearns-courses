# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Foreign empires race to seize the Arab lands for their position and riches, the Achaemenid Persians first among them (559–330 BCE).
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 3, Lesson 4: تنافس استعماري (Colonial rivalry)” is mainly about this: The year is cut into flood, sowing and harvest; the shaduf, canals, astronomy and engineering turn the river into a civilisation.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Foreign empires race to seize the Arab lands for their position and riches, the Achaemenid Persians first among them (559–330 BCE).
