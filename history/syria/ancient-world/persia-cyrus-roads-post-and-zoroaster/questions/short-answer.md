# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson 4: تنافس استعماري (Colonial rivalry)”?
**Answer:** Once empires exist, they compete — and the Arab lands, rich and perfectly placed between continents, become the prize. The book calls this lesson 'colonial rivalry': foreign empires race to seize the region for its position and its riches. First among the racers stand the Achaemenid Persians, whose empire (559–330 BCE) reaches over Syria and far beyond, setting the pattern every later empire would follow.
