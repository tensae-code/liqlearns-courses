# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Sargon of Akkad's kingdom swallows its rivals and stretches into the first empire, and empire becomes the prize of war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 4, Lesson 3: تمدن وحضارة (Urban life and civilisation)” is mainly about this: The widened state's roads and adventures link furthest east and west in trade that carries culture with the goods.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Planned cities and the stepped ziggurat show a society organising building, worship and knowledge in stone.
