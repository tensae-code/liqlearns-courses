# Ancient Syria: Religion and Gods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Settled farmers invent gods for rain and crops and warriors gods for war, and belief grows out of daily work.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson 3: الحياة الدينية (Religious life)” is mainly about this: Stone, bone, wood and clay are shaped into the first tools and pots, and raw nature becomes craft.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Settled farmers invent gods for rain and crops and warriors gods for war, and belief grows out of daily work.
