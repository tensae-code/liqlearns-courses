# Ancient Syria: Religion and Gods — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson 3: الحياة الدينية (Religious life)”?
**Answer:** Why did the first settled people invent gods? The book answers from the furrow and the battlefield: farmers, whose lives hang on rain and harvest, raise gods for the rain and the crops; warriors, whose lives hang on victory, honour gods of war. Belief grows out of daily work — each community's gods mirror what that community fears and needs most. Religion, in Syria's ancient story, begins as the settled people's way of facing a world they could not control.
