# Syria's City-States: Ebla, Mari and Ugarit — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Syria's cities grow into city-states, then kingdoms — Ebla, Mari and Ugarit each rule their own land.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 2, Lesson سورية مهد الحضارة (Syria, cradle of civilisation)” is mainly about this: Syria's cities grow into city-states, then kingdoms — Ebla, Mari and Ugarit each rule their own land.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Ebla (Tell Mardikh) near Aleppo kept a huge library of thousands of clay tablets, Mari on the Euphrates had its royal palace, temples and statues, and Ugarit (Ras Shamra) invented the alphabetic writing it gave to all peoples.
