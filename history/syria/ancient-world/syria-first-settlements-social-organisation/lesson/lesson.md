# Syria's First Settlements: How People Organised

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Syria's First Settlements: How People Organised** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The story of settled Syria begins in caves. In the Yabroud caves, high in the mountains, the first humans of this land sheltered and hunted; but the turning point comes down in the valleys, at places like Abu Hureyra, where people stop wandering and build the first villages. With houses side by side, work must be shared, food stored, and kin organised — society itself is invented. Syria's history, the book insists, starts not with kings but with neighbours learning to live together.
