# The Rise and Expansion of Islam

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Rise and Expansion of Islam** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1, Lesson 1: حروب التحرير (The wars of liberation)

The medieval centuries in Syria open with armies on the move. The Grade 8 book calls them the wars of liberation: Arab armies free the Levant from the empires that held it, and the state they build stretches in time from India to al-Andalus — one state across three continents. With this lesson the book's whole medieval story begins: Syria is no longer a province fought over by others, but part of a world its own people rule.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 5, Lesson 4: الأندلس قبيل الفتح العربي الإسلامي وبعده (Al-Andalus before and after the Arab-Islamic conquest)

The expansion reaches its furthest shore in Europe. The Grade 6 book tells how the Islamic state steps beyond Arabia and lands in al-Andalus — the first landfall in Europe, won by Arab arms and then organised by Arab administration, so that conquest becomes government. Before and after: the lesson's own frame shows a land transformed by the arrival of the new state and its civilisation.
