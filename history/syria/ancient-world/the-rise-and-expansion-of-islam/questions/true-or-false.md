# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Arab armies free the Levant and build one state from India to al-Andalus, opening the medieval centuries in Syria.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 5, Lesson 4: الأندلس قبيل الفتح العربي الإسلامي وبعده (Al-Andalus before and after the Arab-Islamic conquest)” is mainly about this: Castles built for war in places enemies could hardly reach — Salah al-Din's castle, Aleppo's citadel, the Krak and al-Marqab — the Umayyad Mosque in Damascus built by the caliph al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik, the church of St Simeon near Aleppo, and the palaces: al-Azem in Damascus, Harun al-Rashid's in Raqqa, al-Hayr East and West near Palmyra.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Islamic state steps beyond Arabia into Europe; al-Andalus is the first landfall, won by Arab arms and organised by Arab administration.
