# Video script — Portuguese Forts on the East African Coast


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The Portuguese seize the East African coast in 1505–1506 — but their rule never settles. The coast resists, and rescue comes from Arabia: the Ya'rubi state of Oman sends the fleet that expels the Portuguese in 1698 and extends Arab influence down the coast, while Zanzibar stays under the Busaid sultans from 1839. The forts change hands; the coast, the lesson shows, was never simply conquered.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
