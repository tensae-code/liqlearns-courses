# Portuguese Forts on the East African Coast — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The Portuguese seized the East African coast in 1505–1506, but their rule never settled: the Ya'rubi state of Oman sent the fleet that expelled them in 1698 and extended Arab influence down the coast, and Zanzibar stayed under the Busaid sultans from 1839.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 4, Lesson القرن الإفريقي وجزر القمر منذ الاستعمار إلى الاستقلال (The Horn of Africa and the Comoros from colonialism to independence)” is mainly about this: The Umayyad state shoulders the liberation of Ifriqiya and the Maghreb, from Kairouan to Tangier, and settles it as one province.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Portuguese seized the East African coast in 1505–1506, but their rule never settled: the Ya'rubi state of Oman sent the fleet that expelled them in 1698 and extended Arab influence down the coast, and Zanzibar stayed under the Busaid sultans from 1839.
