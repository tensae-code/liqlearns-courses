# Portuguese Forts on the East African Coast — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson القرن الإفريقي وجزر القمر منذ الاستعمار إلى الاستقلال (The Horn of Africa and the Comoros from colonialism to independence)”?
**Answer:** The Portuguese seize the East African coast in 1505–1506 — but their rule never settles. The coast resists, and rescue comes from Arabia: the Ya'rubi state of Oman sends the fleet that expels the Portuguese in 1698 and extends Arab influence down the coast, while Zanzibar stays under the Busaid sultans from 1839. The forts change hands; the coast, the lesson shows, was never simply conquered.
