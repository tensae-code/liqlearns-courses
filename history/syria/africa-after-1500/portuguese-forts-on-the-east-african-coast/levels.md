# Portuguese Forts on the East African Coast — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Portuguese seized the East African coast in 1505–1506, but their rule never settled: the Ya'rubi state of Oman sent the fleet that expelled them in 1698 and extended Arab influence down the coast, and Zanzibar stayed under the Busaid sultans from 1839. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
