# Video script — Africa Resists the Scramble: Algeria, Ethiopia, Sudan


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

France uses the fly-whisk incident as its excuse to blockade and take Algiers in 1830 — and meets a resistance that outlives generations. Emir Abd al-Qadir builds a state and fights for fifteen years, winning the Treaty of Tafna in 1837 before Bugeaud's scorched-earth war forces his surrender in 1847; he dies in exile in Damascus in 1883, a guest of Syria. The resistance goes on without him: al-Muqrani in 1871, Bouamama in the Sahara, and Lalla Fatma N'Soumer, who beats the French in 1854 and dies in their prison in 1863.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
