# Lesson plan — Africa Resists the Scramble: Algeria, Ethiopia, Sudan

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Africa Resists the Scramble: Algeria, Ethiopia, Sudan
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** France used the fly-whisk incident to blockade and take Algiers in 1830; Emir Abd al-Qadir built a state and fought for fifteen years, winning the Treaty of Tafna in 1837 before Bugeaud's scorched-earth war forced his surrender in 1847 — he died in exile in Damascus in 1883 — and the resistance went on: al-Muqrani in 1871, Bouamama in the Sahara, and Lalla Fatma N'Soumer, who beat the French in 1854 and died in their prison in 1863.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (recommended, never forced).
