# Africa Resists the Scramble: Algeria, Ethiopia, Sudan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 5, Lesson الجزائر في مواجهة الاحتلال الفرنسي (Algeria confronting the French occupation)”?
**Answer:** France uses the fly-whisk incident as its excuse to blockade and take Algiers in 1830 — and meets a resistance that outlives generations. Emir Abd al-Qadir builds a state and fights for fifteen years, winning the Treaty of Tafna in 1837 before Bugeaud's scorched-earth war forces his surrender in 1847; he dies in exile in Damascus in 1883, a guest of Syria. The resistance goes on without him: al-Muqrani in 1871, Bouamama in the Sahara, and Lalla Fatma N'Soumer, who beats the French in 1854 and dies in their prison in 1863.
