# Africa Resists the Scramble: Algeria, Ethiopia, Sudan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   France used the fly-whisk incident to blockade and take Algiers in 1830; Emir Abd al-Qadir built a state and fought for fifteen years, winning the Treaty of Tafna in 1837 before Bugeaud's scorched-earth war forced his surrender in 1847 — he died in exile in Damascus in 1883 — and the resistance went on: al-Muqrani in 1871, Bouamama in the Sahara, and Lalla Fatma N'Soumer, who beat the French in 1854 and died in their prison in 1863.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 5, Lesson الجزائر في مواجهة الاحتلال الفرنسي (Algeria confronting the French occupation)” is mainly about this: Writing in all its stages saves history from loss, and the hunger to record knowledge drives a creative age.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: France used the fly-whisk incident to blockade and take Algiers in 1830; Emir Abd al-Qadir built a state and fought for fifteen years, winning the Treaty of Tafna in 1837 before Bugeaud's scorched-earth war forced his surrender in 1847 — he died in exile in Damascus in 1883 — and the resistance went on: al-Muqrani in 1871, Bouamama in the Sahara, and Lalla Fatma N'Soumer, who beat the French in 1854 and died in their prison in 1863.
