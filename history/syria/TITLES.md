# History — Syria course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Syria's own — built from Syria's Ministry of Education student books, all in Arabic (combined Social Studies books for the youngest grades, History books for Grades 7–9). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside Levels 1–4, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': begin at home, with your own day, your family tree and your homeland's treasures; walk ancient Syria from its first villages to its city-states, empires and trade roads; enter the Arab state ruled from Damascus and the civilisation it built; and end with the modern Arab world — occupation, resistance and independence, told from Damascus outward. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
