# Saudi Arabia: Three States to the Kingdom, 1932 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson المملكة العربية السعودية منذ التأسيس حتى الوقت الحاضر (The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia from its founding to the present)”?
**Answer:** Three states rise on the same ground. The first Saudi state rises at Diriyah (1745–1818) and falls to Ibrahim Pasha's campaign; the second, under Turki ibn Abdullah, falls to the Al Rashid in 1891. Then Abd al-Aziz ibn Saud retakes Riyadh in 1902 and, step by step — al-Ahsa, Hail, Mecca — unites the land, proclaiming the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1932. Oil becomes its chief resource, and in the October 1973 war King Faisal turns that resource into a weapon, cutting oil to America and Europe.
