# Saudi Arabia: Three States to the Kingdom, 1932 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The first Saudi state rose at Diriyah (1745–1818) and fell to Ibrahim Pasha's campaign; the second state fell to the Al Rashid in 1891; Abd al-Aziz ibn Saud retook Riyadh in 1902, then al-Ahsa, Hail and Mecca, and proclaimed the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1932; King Faisal cut oil to America and Europe in the October 1973 war. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
