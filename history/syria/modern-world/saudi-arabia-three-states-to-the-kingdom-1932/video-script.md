# Video script — Saudi Arabia: Three States to the Kingdom, 1932


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Three states rise on the same ground. The first Saudi state rises at Diriyah (1745–1818) and falls to Ibrahim Pasha's campaign; the second, under Turki ibn Abdullah, falls to the Al Rashid in 1891. Then Abd al-Aziz ibn Saud retakes Riyadh in 1902 and, step by step — al-Ahsa, Hail, Mecca — unites the land, proclaiming the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1932. Oil becomes its chief resource, and in the October 1973 war King Faisal turns that resource into a weapon, cutting oil to America and Europe.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
