# Lesson plan — Egypt: From the 1919 Revolution to 2011

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Egypt: From the 1919 Revolution to 2011
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fired the revolution of 1919; the coup of 23 July 1952 made Egypt a republic under Naguib then Nasser — the Tripartite Aggression of 1956, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ended Mubarak's rule in 2011.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (recommended, never forced).
