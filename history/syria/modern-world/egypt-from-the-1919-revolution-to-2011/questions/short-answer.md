# Egypt: From the 1919 Revolution to 2011 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson مصر منذ الحماية البريطانية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Egypt from the British protectorate to the present)”?
**Answer:** The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fire the revolution of 1919; Britain's declaration of 28 February 1922 ends the protectorate — with reservations — and the 1936 treaty follows. Then the coup of 23 July 1952 makes Egypt a republic, under Naguib then Nasser: evacuation, nationalisations, the High Dam, the Tripartite Aggression of 1956 survived, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ends Mubarak's rule in 2011, in the pupils' own lifetime.
