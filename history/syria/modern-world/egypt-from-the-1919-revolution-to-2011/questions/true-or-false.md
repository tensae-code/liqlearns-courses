# Egypt: From the 1919 Revolution to 2011 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fired the revolution of 1919; the coup of 23 July 1952 made Egypt a republic under Naguib then Nasser — the Tripartite Aggression of 1956, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ended Mubarak's rule in 2011.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 4, Lesson مصر منذ الحماية البريطانية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Egypt from the British protectorate to the present)” is mainly about this: Arab armies free the Levant and build one state from India to al-Andalus, opening the medieval centuries in Syria.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fired the revolution of 1919; the coup of 23 July 1952 made Egypt a republic under Naguib then Nasser — the Tripartite Aggression of 1956, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ended Mubarak's rule in 2011.
