# Egypt: From the 1919 Revolution to 2011 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fired the revolution of 1919; the coup of 23 July 1952 made Egypt a republic under Naguib then Nasser — the Tripartite Aggression of 1956, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ended Mubarak's rule in 2011. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
