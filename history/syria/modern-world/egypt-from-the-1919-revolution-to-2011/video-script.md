# Video script — Egypt: From the 1919 Revolution to 2011


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fire the revolution of 1919; Britain's declaration of 28 February 1922 ends the protectorate — with reservations — and the 1936 treaty follows. Then the coup of 23 July 1952 makes Egypt a republic, under Naguib then Nasser: evacuation, nationalisations, the High Dam, the Tripartite Aggression of 1956 survived, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ends Mubarak's rule in 2011, in the pupils' own lifetime.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
