# Syria's Independence 1946 and the Growing Nation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals; after independence from French colonialism in 1946 the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew past 23 million by 2010.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 3, Lesson السكان في بلدي سورية (Population in my country Syria)” is mainly about this: Feasts, tales, crafts and customs pass hand to hand down the generations and become a people's living heritage.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals; after independence from French colonialism in 1946 the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew past 23 million by 2010.
