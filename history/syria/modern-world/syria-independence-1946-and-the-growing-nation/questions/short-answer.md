# Syria's Independence 1946 and the Growing Nation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson السكان في بلدي سورية (Population in my country Syria)”?
**Answer:** Hassan asks his teacher a simple question: why does Syria's population keep growing? The answer is a history lesson. Under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals, and many of its youths were martyred defending the homeland. After independence from French colonialism in 1946, the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew — past 23 million by 2010. A population graph, the book shows, is a nation's biography in numbers.
