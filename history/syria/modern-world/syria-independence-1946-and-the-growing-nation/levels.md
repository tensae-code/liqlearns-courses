# Syria's Independence 1946 and the Growing Nation — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals; after independence from French colonialism in 1946 the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew past 23 million by 2010. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
