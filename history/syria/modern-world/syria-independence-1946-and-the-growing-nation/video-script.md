# Video script — Syria's Independence 1946 and the Growing Nation


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Hassan asks his teacher a simple question: why does Syria's population keep growing? The answer is a history lesson. Under French colonialism Syria's people were no more than three million, suffering poverty, disease and few hospitals, and many of its youths were martyred defending the homeland. After independence from French colonialism in 1946, the state spread schools and hospitals, deaths fell, and the people grew — past 23 million by 2010. A population graph, the book shows, is a nation's biography in numbers.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
