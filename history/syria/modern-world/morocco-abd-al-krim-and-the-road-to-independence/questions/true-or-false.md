# Morocco: Abd al-Krim and the Road to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   France occupied Fez in 1911 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Fez in 1912; Abd al-Krim al-Khattabi's Rif revolt destroyed the Spanish army at Annual in 1921 and proclaimed the Republic of the Rif before the joint Franco-Spanish assault exiled him in 1926; France exiled Sultan Muhammad V to Madagascar in 1953, and the armed struggle forced his return in 1955 and independence in 1956.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 5, Lesson المغرب (مراكش) منذ التنافس الاستعماري حتى الوقت الحاضر (Morocco from colonial rivalry to the present)” is mainly about this: The Syrian Congress rejected the Faisal–Clemenceau agreement and San Remo put Syria under French mandate; General Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demanded the army's dissolution, and though Faisal accepted, France marched; War Minister Yusuf al-Azma led the volunteers to Maysalun and was martyred there on 24 July 1920, and the French entered Damascus and ended the Arab government.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: France occupied Fez in 1911 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Fez in 1912; Abd al-Krim al-Khattabi's Rif revolt destroyed the Spanish army at Annual in 1921 and proclaimed the Republic of the Rif before the joint Franco-Spanish assault exiled him in 1926; France exiled Sultan Muhammad V to Madagascar in 1953, and the armed struggle forced his return in 1955 and independence in 1956.
