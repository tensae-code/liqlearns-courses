# Morocco: Abd al-Krim and the Road to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 5, Lesson المغرب (مراكش) منذ التنافس الاستعماري حتى الوقت الحاضر (Morocco from colonial rivalry to the present)”?
**Answer:** The European powers settle their rivalry over Morocco at Algeciras in 1906; France occupies Fez in 1911 and imposes the protectorate by the Treaty of Fez in 1912. In the Rif mountains, Abd al-Krim al-Khattabi's revolt destroys the Spanish army at Annual in 1921 and proclaims the Republic of the Rif, until the joint Franco-Spanish assault exiles him in 1926. The Berber Dahir of 1930 wakes the national movement; the Istiqlal Party demands independence in 1943; France exiles Sultan Muhammad V to Madagascar in 1953 — and the armed struggle forces his return in 1955 and independence in 1956.
