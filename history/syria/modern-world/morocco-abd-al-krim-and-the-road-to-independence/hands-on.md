# Hands-on — Morocco: Abd al-Krim and the Road to Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Morocco: Abd al-Krim and the Road to Independence — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: France occupied Fez in 1911 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Fez in 1912; Abd al-Krim al-Khattabi's Rif revolt destroyed the Spanish army at Annual in 1921 and proclaimed the Republic of the Rif before the joint Franco-Spanish assault exiled him in 1926; France exiled Sultan Muhammad V to Madagascar in 1953, and the armed struggle forced his return in 1955 and independence in 1956. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
