# Morocco: Abd al-Krim and the Road to Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Morocco: Abd al-Krim and the Road to Independence** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The European powers settle their rivalry over Morocco at Algeciras in 1906; France occupies Fez in 1911 and imposes the protectorate by the Treaty of Fez in 1912. In the Rif mountains, Abd al-Krim al-Khattabi's revolt destroys the Spanish army at Annual in 1921 and proclaims the Republic of the Rif, until the joint Franco-Spanish assault exiles him in 1926. The Berber Dahir of 1930 wakes the national movement; the Istiqlal Party demands independence in 1943; France exiles Sultan Muhammad V to Madagascar in 1953 — and the armed struggle forces his return in 1955 and independence in 1956.
