# Lesson plan — Morocco: Abd al-Krim and the Road to Independence

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Morocco: Abd al-Krim and the Road to Independence
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** France occupied Fez in 1911 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Fez in 1912; Abd al-Krim al-Khattabi's Rif revolt destroyed the Spanish army at Annual in 1921 and proclaimed the Republic of the Rif before the joint Franco-Spanish assault exiled him in 1926; France exiled Sultan Muhammad V to Madagascar in 1953, and the armed struggle forced his return in 1955 and independence in 1956.
