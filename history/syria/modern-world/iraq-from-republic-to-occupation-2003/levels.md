# Iraq: From Republic to Occupation 2003 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's 1941 government was crushed by a second British occupation; then the revolution of 14 July 1958 made Iraq a republic; after al-Bakr and Saddam Hussein came the war with Iran (1980–1988), the invasion of Kuwait in 1990, the siege, and the American occupation of 2003, ending with the withdrawal of late 2011 — the same lesson tells of Arab Ahwaz (Arabistan), annexed by Reza Shah in 1925. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
