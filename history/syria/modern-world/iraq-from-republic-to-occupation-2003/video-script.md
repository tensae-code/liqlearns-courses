# Video script — Iraq: From Republic to Occupation 2003


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's 1941 government is crushed by a second British occupation; the Baghdad Pact of 1955 and the Hashemite Union follow — then, on 14 July 1958, revolution makes Iraq a republic. After al-Bakr and Saddam Hussein come the war with Iran (1980–1988), the invasion of Kuwait in 1990, the siege years, and the American occupation of 2003, launched on a pretext the lesson says proved false, ending with the withdrawal of late 2011. The same lesson tells of Arab Ahwaz (Arabistan), annexed by Reza Shah in 1925 and Persianised — an Arab land the book refuses to forget.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
