# Lesson plan — Iraq: From Republic to Occupation 2003

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Iraq: From Republic to Occupation 2003
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's 1941 government was crushed by a second British occupation; then the revolution of 14 July 1958 made Iraq a republic; after al-Bakr and Saddam Hussein came the war with Iran (1980–1988), the invasion of Kuwait in 1990, the siege, and the American occupation of 2003, ending with the withdrawal of late 2011 — the same lesson tells of Arab Ahwaz (Arabistan), annexed by Reza Shah in 1925.
