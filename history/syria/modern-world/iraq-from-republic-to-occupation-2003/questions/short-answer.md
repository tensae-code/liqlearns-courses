# Iraq: From Republic to Occupation 2003 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson العراق منذ الحرب العالمية الثانية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Iraq from the Second World War to the present)”?
**Answer:** Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's 1941 government is crushed by a second British occupation; the Baghdad Pact of 1955 and the Hashemite Union follow — then, on 14 July 1958, revolution makes Iraq a republic. After al-Bakr and Saddam Hussein come the war with Iran (1980–1988), the invasion of Kuwait in 1990, the siege years, and the American occupation of 2003, launched on a pretext the lesson says proved false, ending with the withdrawal of late 2011. The same lesson tells of Arab Ahwaz (Arabistan), annexed by Reza Shah in 1925 and Persianised — an Arab land the book refuses to forget.
