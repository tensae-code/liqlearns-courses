# Iraq: From Republic to Occupation 2003 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's 1941 government was crushed by a second British occupation; then the revolution of 14 July 1958 made Iraq a republic; after al-Bakr and Saddam Hussein came the war with Iran (1980–1988), the invasion of Kuwait in 1990, the siege, and the American occupation of 2003, ending with the withdrawal of late 2011 — the same lesson tells of Arab Ahwaz (Arabistan), annexed by Reza Shah in 1925.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 3, Lesson العراق منذ الحرب العالمية الثانية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Iraq from the Second World War to the present)” is mainly about this: In old times people exchanged what they produced for goods, a barter (مقايضة) in which the oil-maker gave part of his oil and received eggs and wheat; as life developed, peoples began to mint money and a person could sell his product or his work for coins.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's 1941 government was crushed by a second British occupation; then the revolution of 14 July 1958 made Iraq a republic; after al-Bakr and Saddam Hussein came the war with Iran (1980–1988), the invasion of Kuwait in 1990, the siege, and the American occupation of 2003, ending with the withdrawal of late 2011 — the same lesson tells of Arab Ahwaz (Arabistan), annexed by Reza Shah in 1925.
