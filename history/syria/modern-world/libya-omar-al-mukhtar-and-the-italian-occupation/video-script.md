# Video script — Libya: Omar al-Mukhtar and the Italian Occupation


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Italy invades in 1911 and the Ottoman state cedes Libya at Ouchy in 1912 — but the Libyans do not cede it. They form the Tripoli government under Sulayman al-Baruni; Ahmad al-Sharif al-Senussi beats the Italians at Derna in 1913; Italy recognises the Tripolitanian Republic in 1918. From 1921 Omar al-Mukhtar leads the guerrilla war from the Jabal al-Akhdar, until Italy walls the Egyptian border, captures him and executes him in 1931. The United Nations grants independence in 1949, Idris becomes king in 1951, Gaddafi's coup of 1969 brings a tyranny of prisons and the Abu Salim massacre — and the popular revolution of February 2011 ends his rule and his life.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
