# Libya: Omar al-Mukhtar and the Italian Occupation — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Italy invaded in 1911; from 1921 Omar al-Mukhtar led the guerrilla war from the Jabal al-Akhdar until Italy captured him and executed him in 1931; the United Nations granted independence in 1949, Idris became king in 1951, Gaddafi's coup of 1969 brought a tyranny of prisons and the Abu Salim massacre, and the popular revolution of February 2011 ended his rule and his life. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
