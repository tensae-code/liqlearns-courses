# Libya: Omar al-Mukhtar and the Italian Occupation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Italy invaded in 1911; from 1921 Omar al-Mukhtar led the guerrilla war from the Jabal al-Akhdar until Italy captured him and executed him in 1931; the United Nations granted independence in 1949, Idris became king in 1951, Gaddafi's coup of 1969 brought a tyranny of prisons and the Abu Salim massacre, and the popular revolution of February 2011 ended his rule and his life.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 5, Lesson ليبيا منذ الاحتلال الإيطالي حتى الوقت الحاضر (Libya from the Italian occupation to the present)” is mainly about this: While Sharif Hussein negotiated with Britain and joined the Allies in 1916, the Allies were secretly dividing the Arab lands in the Sykes–Picot agreement of 1916 and Britain promised the Zionists a national home in Palestine in the Balfour Declaration of 1917; the Paris peace conference of 1919 invented the mandate system, applied at San Remo in 1920.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Italy invaded in 1911; from 1921 Omar al-Mukhtar led the guerrilla war from the Jabal al-Akhdar until Italy captured him and executed him in 1931; the United Nations granted independence in 1949, Idris became king in 1951, Gaddafi's coup of 1969 brought a tyranny of prisons and the Abu Salim massacre, and the popular revolution of February 2011 ended his rule and his life.
