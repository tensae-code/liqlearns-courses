# Libya: Omar al-Mukhtar and the Italian Occupation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 5, Lesson ليبيا منذ الاحتلال الإيطالي حتى الوقت الحاضر (Libya from the Italian occupation to the present)”?
**Answer:** Italy invades in 1911 and the Ottoman state cedes Libya at Ouchy in 1912 — but the Libyans do not cede it. They form the Tripoli government under Sulayman al-Baruni; Ahmad al-Sharif al-Senussi beats the Italians at Derna in 1913; Italy recognises the Tripolitanian Republic in 1918. From 1921 Omar al-Mukhtar leads the guerrilla war from the Jabal al-Akhdar, until Italy walls the Egyptian border, captures him and executes him in 1931. The United Nations grants independence in 1949, Idris becomes king in 1951, Gaddafi's coup of 1969 brings a tyranny of prisons and the Abu Salim massacre — and the popular revolution of February 2011 ends his rule and his life.
