# Lesson plan — Libya: Omar al-Mukhtar and the Italian Occupation

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Libya: Omar al-Mukhtar and the Italian Occupation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Italy invaded in 1911; from 1921 Omar al-Mukhtar led the guerrilla war from the Jabal al-Akhdar until Italy captured him and executed him in 1931; the United Nations granted independence in 1949, Idris became king in 1951, Gaddafi's coup of 1969 brought a tyranny of prisons and the Abu Salim massacre, and the popular revolution of February 2011 ended his rule and his life.
