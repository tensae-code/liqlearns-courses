# The Horn of Africa Divided by Empires — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The empires carved up the Somali lands — Djibouti to France, the north to Britain, the south and Eritrea to Italy; Sayyid Muhammad ibn Abd Allah Hassan's revolt held out against Britain from 1899 to 1920, the Somali Youth League carried the struggle from 1943, and north and south united as the Somali Republic in 1960; Eritrea won independence in 1993, Djibouti in 1977, and the Comoros declared independence in 1975. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
