# The Horn of Africa Divided by Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The empires carved up the Somali lands — Djibouti to France, the north to Britain, the south and Eritrea to Italy; Sayyid Muhammad ibn Abd Allah Hassan's revolt held out against Britain from 1899 to 1920, the Somali Youth League carried the struggle from 1943, and north and south united as the Somali Republic in 1960; Eritrea won independence in 1993, Djibouti in 1977, and the Comoros declared independence in 1975.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 4, Lesson القرن الإفريقي وجزر القمر (The Horn of Africa and the Comoros)” is mainly about this: Religious, official, seasonal and folk feasts bind the whole society together, watched over and shared by its rulers.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The empires carved up the Somali lands — Djibouti to France, the north to Britain, the south and Eritrea to Italy; Sayyid Muhammad ibn Abd Allah Hassan's revolt held out against Britain from 1899 to 1920, the Somali Youth League carried the struggle from 1943, and north and south united as the Somali Republic in 1960; Eritrea won independence in 1993, Djibouti in 1977, and the Comoros declared independence in 1975.
