# The Horn of Africa Divided by Empires

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Horn of Africa Divided by Empires** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The empires carve up the Somali lands between them — Djibouti to France, the north to Britain, the south and Eritrea to Italy — ban Arabic and plunder the resources. Sayyid Muhammad ibn Abd Allah Hassan's revolt holds out against Britain from 1899 to 1920; the Somali Youth League carries the struggle from 1943, and in 1960 north and south unite as the Somali Republic. Eritrea's armed struggle from 1961 wins independence in 1993, Djibouti follows in 1977, and the Comoros — French since 1892 — declare independence in 1975. A divided land, reassembled piece by piece.
