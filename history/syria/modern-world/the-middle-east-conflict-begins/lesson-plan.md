# Lesson plan — The Middle East Conflict Begins

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Middle East Conflict Begins
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the June 1967 aggression the Zionist enemy occupied wide Arab lands — Jerusalem, the West Bank, Gaza, Sinai and the Syrian Golan; Syria fought again beside Egypt in the October 1973 war.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 1, Lesson سورية منذ الاستقلال وحتى الوقت الحاضر (Syria from independence to the present)** — In the June 1967 aggression the Zionist enemy occupied wide Arab lands — Jerusalem, the West Bank, Gaza, Sinai and the Syrian Golan; Syria fought again beside Egypt in the October 1973 war.
2. **Unit 2, Lesson فلسطين تواجه الانتداب البريطاني والمشروع الصهيوني (Palestine faces the British mandate and the Zionist project)** — The Zionist project began in the nineteenth century — Herzl's book 'The Jewish State' and the Basel congress of 1897; Sultan Abd al-Hamid II refused Herzl's 1901 offer; Britain issued the Balfour Declaration of 2 November 1917, and the mandate government opened immigration, recognised the Jewish Agency and armed the Haganah.
3. **Unit 2, Lesson فلسطين منذ الحرب العالمية الثانية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Palestine from the Second World War to the present)** — The United Nations' partition project of 1947, Britain's withdrawal on 14 May 1948 and the proclamation of the Zionist state, the Deir Yassin massacre and the Nakba; then the PLO founded in 1964, the June 1967 occupation, the First Intifada of 1987, the Oslo agreement of 1993, the Second Intifada of 2000, the aggression on Gaza in 2008, and Palestine's full UNESCO membership in 2011.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
