# The Middle East Conflict Begins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     In the June 1967 aggression the Zionist enemy occupied wide Arab lands — Jerusalem, the West Bank, Gaza, Sinai and the Syrian Golan; Syria fought again beside Egypt in the October 1973 war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 2, Lesson فلسطين تواجه الانتداب البريطاني والمشروع الصهيوني (Palestine faces the British mandate and the Zionist project)” is mainly about this: The ancestors left many antiquities as a witness to their achievements — Bosra's amphitheatre, Apamea's colonnade, the Krak des Chevaliers and the kingdom of Eimar on the Euphrates.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Zionist project began in the nineteenth century — Herzl's book 'The Jewish State' and the Basel congress of 1897; Sultan Abd al-Hamid II refused Herzl's 1901 offer; Britain issued the Balfour Declaration of 2 November 1917, and the mandate government opened immigration, recognised the Jewish Agency and armed the Haganah.
