# The Middle East Conflict Begins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson سورية منذ الاستقلال وحتى الوقت الحاضر (Syria from independence to the present)”?
**Answer:** In the June 1967 aggression, the book tells Syria's pupils, the Zionist enemy occupied wide Arab lands — Jerusalem, the West Bank, Gaza, Sinai, and Syria's own Golan, whose fall the Defence Minister Hafez al-Assad announced before the enemy had even reached Quneitra. Six years later Syria fought again, beside Egypt, in the October 1973 war. The conflict that begins in these years runs through everything that follows in the region's story.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3)     How does the story in “Unit 2, Lesson فلسطين منذ الحرب العالمية الثانية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Palestine from the Second World War to the present)” end?
**Answer:** The United Nations' partition project of 1947, Britain's withdrawal on 14 May 1948 and the proclamation of the Zionist state; the Deir Yassin massacre and the Nakba that drives the Palestinians from most of their land. Then the long resistance: the PLO founded in 1964, the June 1967 occupation, the First Intifada of the children of stones in 1987, the Oslo agreement of 1993, the Second Intifada of 2000, the aggression on Gaza in 2008 — and Palestine's full UNESCO membership in 2011, the story carried to the pupils' own present.
