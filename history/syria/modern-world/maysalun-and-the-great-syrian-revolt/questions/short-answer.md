# Maysalun and the Great Syrian Revolt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson فرنسا تحتل سورية وتنهي الحكم العربي فيها (France occupies Syria and ends Arab rule)”?
**Answer:** The Syrian Congress rejects the Faisal–Clemenceau agreement, and San Remo hands Syria to France. General Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demands the Arab army's dissolution; Faisal accepts — and France marches anyway. At the pass of Maysalun, War Minister Yusuf al-Azma leads the volunteers out to meet a modern army, knowing what it means, and is martyred there on 24 July 1920. The French enter Damascus, and the Arab government — Syria's two years of self-rule — ends.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Unit 1, Lesson الشعب السوري يواجه الاحتلال الفرنسي بالثورات (The Syrian people confront the French occupation with revolts)” end?
**Answer:** France splits Syria into small statelets and loots it — and the revolts answer, region by region: Saleh al-Ali on the coast, Ibrahim Hananu in the north, Hawran in 1920, an ambush of Gouraud's own convoy in the Golan in 1921. Then they join into one fire: the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925–1927 under Sultan al-Atrash — al-Kafr, the siege of Suwayda, al-Mazraa where the French lost thousands, then the Ghouta and Damascus itself, which France bombards and burns, the al-Hariqa quarter going up in flames. The revolt is crushed, but Syria has announced it will never be quietly ruled.
