# Maysalun and the Great Syrian Revolt — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1, Lesson فرنسا تحتل سورية وتنهي الحكم العربي فيها (France occupies Syria and ends Arab rule)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Syrian Congress rejected the Faisal–Clemenceau agreement and San Remo put Syria under French mandate; General Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demanded the army's dissolution, and though Faisal accepted, France marched; War Minister Yusuf al-Azma led the volunteers to Maysalun and was martyred there on 24 July 1920, and the French entered Damascus and ended the Arab government. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 1, Lesson الشعب السوري يواجه الاحتلال الفرنسي بالثورات (The Syrian people confront the French occupation with revolts)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: France split Syria into small statelets and looted it, and the revolts answered — Saleh al-Ali on the coast, Ibrahim Hananu in the north, Hawran in 1920, the Golan ambush of Gouraud's convoy in 1921 — until the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925–1927 under Sultan al-Atrash: al-Kafr, the siege of Suwayda, al-Mazraa, then the Ghouta and Damascus itself, which France bombarded and burned (the al-Hariqa quarter). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
