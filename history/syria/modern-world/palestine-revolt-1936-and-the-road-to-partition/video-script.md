# Video script — Palestine: The Revolt of 1936 and the Road to Partition


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Revolt follows revolt — Jaffa in 1921, al-Buraq in 1929 — until everything ignites at once: the Great Palestinian Revolt of 1936. Izz al-Din al-Qassam has already fallen near Jenin; now the strike and the fighting spread across the country, with Arab volunteers, Syrians among them, crossing to help. Britain answers with the Peel partition plan of 1937 — rejected by the Palestinians — and the White Paper of 1939, rejected by both sides. The road from revolt runs toward partition, and the book walks it step by step.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
