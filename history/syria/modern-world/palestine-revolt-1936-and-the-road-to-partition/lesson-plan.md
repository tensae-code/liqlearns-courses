# Lesson plan — Palestine: The Revolt of 1936 and the Road to Partition

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Palestine: The Revolt of 1936 and the Road to Partition
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Revolt followed revolt — Jaffa 1921, al-Buraq 1929 — until the Great Palestinian Revolt of 1936: Izz al-Din al-Qassam fell near Jenin, the strike and the fighting spread with Arab volunteers including Syrians, Britain answered with the Peel partition plan of 1937, which the Palestinians rejected, and the White Paper of 1939, which both sides rejected.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (recommended, never forced).
