# Palestine: The Revolt of 1936 and the Road to Partition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson تصاعد النضال الفلسطيني بين الحربين العالميتين (The Palestinian struggle escalates between the two world wars)”?
**Answer:** Revolt follows revolt — Jaffa in 1921, al-Buraq in 1929 — until everything ignites at once: the Great Palestinian Revolt of 1936. Izz al-Din al-Qassam has already fallen near Jenin; now the strike and the fighting spread across the country, with Arab volunteers, Syrians among them, crossing to help. Britain answers with the Peel partition plan of 1937 — rejected by the Palestinians — and the White Paper of 1939, rejected by both sides. The road from revolt runs toward partition, and the book walks it step by step.
