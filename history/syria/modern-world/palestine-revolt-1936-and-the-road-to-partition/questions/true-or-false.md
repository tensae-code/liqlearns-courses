# Palestine: The Revolt of 1936 and the Road to Partition — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Revolt followed revolt — Jaffa 1921, al-Buraq 1929 — until the Great Palestinian Revolt of 1936: Izz al-Din al-Qassam fell near Jenin, the strike and the fighting spread with Arab volunteers including Syrians, Britain answered with the Peel partition plan of 1937, which the Palestinians rejected, and the White Paper of 1939, which both sides rejected.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 2, Lesson تصاعد النضال الفلسطيني بين الحربين العالميتين (The Palestinian struggle escalates between the two world wars)” is mainly about this: Ebla (Tell Mardikh) near Aleppo kept a huge library of thousands of clay tablets, Mari on the Euphrates had its royal palace, temples and statues, and Ugarit (Ras Shamra) invented the alphabetic writing it gave to all peoples.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Revolt followed revolt — Jaffa 1921, al-Buraq 1929 — until the Great Palestinian Revolt of 1936: Izz al-Din al-Qassam fell near Jenin, the strike and the fighting spread with Arab volunteers including Syrians, Britain answered with the Peel partition plan of 1937, which the Palestinians rejected, and the White Paper of 1939, which both sides rejected.
