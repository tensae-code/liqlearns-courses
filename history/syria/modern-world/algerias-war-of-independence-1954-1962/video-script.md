# Video script — Algeria's War of Independence, 1954–1962


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The Constantine massacre of 8 May 1945, in which 45,000 Algerians were martyred, teaches the national movement its hard lesson: only arms will free the country. The Great Algerian Revolution opens on 1 November 1954 from the Aurès mountains — Jamila Bouhired among its fighters — a provisional government forms in Cairo in 1958, and De Gaulle himself fails to crush it. The Evian accords bring independence on 5 July 1962, after a struggle that cost over a million martyrs.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
