# Algeria's War of Independence, 1954–1962

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Algeria's War of Independence, 1954–1962** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Constantine massacre of 8 May 1945, in which 45,000 Algerians were martyred, teaches the national movement its hard lesson: only arms will free the country. The Great Algerian Revolution opens on 1 November 1954 from the Aurès mountains — Jamila Bouhired among its fighters — a provisional government forms in Cairo in 1958, and De Gaulle himself fails to crush it. The Evian accords bring independence on 5 July 1962, after a struggle that cost over a million martyrs.
