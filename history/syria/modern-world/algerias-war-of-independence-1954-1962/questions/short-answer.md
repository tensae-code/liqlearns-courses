# Algeria's War of Independence, 1954–1962 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 5, Lesson الجزائر منذ النضال السياسي حتى نيل الاستقلال (Algeria from the political struggle to independence)”?
**Answer:** The Constantine massacre of 8 May 1945, in which 45,000 Algerians were martyred, teaches the national movement its hard lesson: only arms will free the country. The Great Algerian Revolution opens on 1 November 1954 from the Aurès mountains — Jamila Bouhired among its fighters — a provisional government forms in Cairo in 1958, and De Gaulle himself fails to crush it. The Evian accords bring independence on 5 July 1962, after a struggle that cost over a million martyrs.
