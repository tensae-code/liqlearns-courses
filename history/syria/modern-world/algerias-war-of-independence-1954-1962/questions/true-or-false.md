# Algeria's War of Independence, 1954–1962 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The Constantine massacre of 8 May 1945, in which 45,000 Algerians were martyred, taught the national movement that only arms would free the country; the Great Algerian Revolution opened on 1 November 1954 from the Aurès, a provisional government formed in Cairo in 1958, De Gaulle failed to crush it, and the Evian accords brought independence on 5 July 1962 after a struggle that cost over a million martyrs.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 5, Lesson الجزائر منذ النضال السياسي حتى نيل الاستقلال (Algeria from the political struggle to independence)” is mainly about this: Painting, sculpture and calligraphy renew themselves in every place and age to carry a people's hopes.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Constantine massacre of 8 May 1945, in which 45,000 Algerians were martyred, taught the national movement that only arms would free the country; the Great Algerian Revolution opened on 1 November 1954 from the Aurès, a provisional government formed in Cairo in 1958, De Gaulle failed to crush it, and the Evian accords brought independence on 5 July 1962 after a struggle that cost over a million martyrs.
