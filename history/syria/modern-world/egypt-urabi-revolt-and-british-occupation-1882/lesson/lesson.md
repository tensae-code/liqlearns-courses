# Egypt: The Urabi Revolt and British Occupation, 1882

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Egypt: The Urabi Revolt and British Occupation, 1882** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Egypt's location is its fate: everyone wants the crossroads. Napoleon Bonaparte's campaign of 1798 is forced out by 1801 by popular resistance — but after the Suez Canal opens, foreign interference grows until Ahmad Urabi's revolt of 1881 puts him at the War Ministry carrying the nation's demands. Britain manufactures the Alexandria riot, bombards the city in 1882 killing more than 250, defeats Urabi at Tell el-Kebir and occupies Cairo, exiling him to Ceylon until 1901. The occupation that follows lasts generations.
