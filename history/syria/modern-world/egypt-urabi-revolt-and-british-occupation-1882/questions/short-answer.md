# Egypt: The Urabi Revolt and British Occupation, 1882 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson أهمية موقع مصر عرضها للتنافس الاستعماري (The importance of Egypt's location)”?
**Answer:** Egypt's location is its fate: everyone wants the crossroads. Napoleon Bonaparte's campaign of 1798 is forced out by 1801 by popular resistance — but after the Suez Canal opens, foreign interference grows until Ahmad Urabi's revolt of 1881 puts him at the War Ministry carrying the nation's demands. Britain manufactures the Alexandria riot, bombards the city in 1882 killing more than 250, defeats Urabi at Tell el-Kebir and occupies Cairo, exiling him to Ceylon until 1901. The occupation that follows lasts generations.
