# Egypt: The Urabi Revolt and British Occupation, 1882 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Napoleon Bonaparte's campaign of 1798 was forced out by 1801 by popular resistance; after the Suez Canal opened, foreign interference grew until Ahmad Urabi's revolt of 1881; Britain bombarded Alexandria in 1882, defeated Urabi at Tell el-Kebir and occupied Cairo, exiling him to Ceylon until 1901.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 4, Lesson أهمية موقع مصر عرضها للتنافس الاستعماري (The importance of Egypt's location)” is mainly about this: Princess Elissa sails from the Phoenician coast of the Levant and founds Carthage, whose ships and markets rule the western Mediterranean.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Napoleon Bonaparte's campaign of 1798 was forced out by 1801 by popular resistance; after the Suez Canal opened, foreign interference grew until Ahmad Urabi's revolt of 1881; Britain bombarded Alexandria in 1882, defeated Urabi at Tell el-Kebir and occupied Cairo, exiling him to Ceylon until 1901.
