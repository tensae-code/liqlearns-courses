# Mauritania: From Trading Posts to Independence, 1960 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The gum-arabic trade drew the Portuguese in the fifteenth century, then Dutch and French trading posts; France moved in stages — the protectorate of 1905, direct control 1909–1920 — and fought the Arabic language; Sheikh Ma al-Aynayn led the resistance from the Adrar plateau; the parties united in 1958 under Mokhtar Ould Daddah, and the United Nations referendum of 1960 brought full independence with Ould Daddah as first president.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 5, Lesson موريتانيا منذ فرض الحماية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Mauritania from the imposition of the protectorate to the present)” is mainly about this: After the coups of 1949 and the Cold War pull of the two camps, Syria united with Egypt: on 22 February 1958 President Quwatli stepped aside for Jamal Abd al-Nasser and the United Arab Republic was born, until the secession of 28 September 1961.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The gum-arabic trade drew the Portuguese in the fifteenth century, then Dutch and French trading posts; France moved in stages — the protectorate of 1905, direct control 1909–1920 — and fought the Arabic language; Sheikh Ma al-Aynayn led the resistance from the Adrar plateau; the parties united in 1958 under Mokhtar Ould Daddah, and the United Nations referendum of 1960 brought full independence with Ould Daddah as first president.
