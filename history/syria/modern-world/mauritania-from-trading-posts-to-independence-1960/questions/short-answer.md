# Mauritania: From Trading Posts to Independence, 1960 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 5, Lesson موريتانيا منذ فرض الحماية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Mauritania from the imposition of the protectorate to the present)”?
**Answer:** The gum-arabic trade draws the Portuguese in the fifteenth century, then Dutch and French trading posts along the coast. France moves inland in stages — exploration 1858–1901, the protectorate of 1905, direct control 1909–1920 — renames the land, splits its people and fights the Arabic language itself. Sheikh Ma al-Aynayn leads the resistance from the Adrar plateau, and his son al-Hiba continues it. The parties unite in 1958 under Mokhtar Ould Daddah, and the United Nations referendum of 1960 brings full independence, with Ould Daddah as first president — the last page of the Grade 9 book, and of the Arab world's long liberation.
