# Video script — Lebanon: From Greater Lebanon to Taif


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Lebanon begins as a French cut: France severs it from Syria and Gouraud proclaims Greater Lebanon in 1920. A constitution follows in 1926; in 1943 the national government of Bishara al-Khoury and Riad al-Solh demands real independence — France arrests the president and ministers, the people rise, and Lebanon wins its freedom, completed on 31 December 1946. The lesson then follows the civil wars of 1958 and 1975 — the Syrian army's entry, the Israeli invasions, the Sabra and Shatila massacre — down to the Taif agreement of 1989 that ends the long war.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
