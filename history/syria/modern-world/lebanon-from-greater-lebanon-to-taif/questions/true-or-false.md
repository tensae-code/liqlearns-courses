# Lebanon: From Greater Lebanon to Taif — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   France cut Lebanon from Syria and Gouraud proclaimed Greater Lebanon in 1920; after the 1926 constitution and the national government of 1943, France arrested the president and ministers, the people rose, and Lebanon won independence, completed on 31 December 1946; the lesson follows the civil wars of 1958 and 1975 to the Taif agreement of 1989.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 2, Lesson لبنان من التأسيس حتى الوقت الحاضر (Lebanon from its founding to the present)” is mainly about this: After ages of wandering for food, people on Syria's Euphrates valley plant and reap, and the farming village is born.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: France cut Lebanon from Syria and Gouraud proclaimed Greater Lebanon in 1920; after the 1926 constitution and the national government of 1943, France arrested the president and ministers, the people rose, and Lebanon won independence, completed on 31 December 1946; the lesson follows the civil wars of 1958 and 1975 to the Taif agreement of 1989.
