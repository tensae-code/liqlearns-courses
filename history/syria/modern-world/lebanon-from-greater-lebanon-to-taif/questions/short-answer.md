# Lebanon: From Greater Lebanon to Taif — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson لبنان من التأسيس حتى الوقت الحاضر (Lebanon from its founding to the present)”?
**Answer:** Lebanon begins as a French cut: France severs it from Syria and Gouraud proclaims Greater Lebanon in 1920. A constitution follows in 1926; in 1943 the national government of Bishara al-Khoury and Riad al-Solh demands real independence — France arrests the president and ministers, the people rise, and Lebanon wins its freedom, completed on 31 December 1946. The lesson then follows the civil wars of 1958 and 1975 — the Syrian army's entry, the Israeli invasions, the Sabra and Shatila massacre — down to the Taif agreement of 1989 that ends the long war.
