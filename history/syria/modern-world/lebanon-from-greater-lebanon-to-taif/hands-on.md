# Hands-on — Lebanon: From Greater Lebanon to Taif

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Lebanon: From Greater Lebanon to Taif — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: France cut Lebanon from Syria and Gouraud proclaimed Greater Lebanon in 1920; after the 1926 constitution and the national government of 1943, France arrested the president and ministers, the people rose, and Lebanon won independence, completed on 31 December 1946; the lesson follows the civil wars of 1958 and 1975 to the Taif agreement of 1989. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
