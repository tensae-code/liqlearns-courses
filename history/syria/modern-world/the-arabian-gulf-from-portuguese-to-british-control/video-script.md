# Video script — The Arabian Gulf: From Portuguese to British Control


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

The Portuguese come round the Cape and seize Muscat and Bahrain — and the Arabs of Oman free Muscat in 1650 and drive them out. Britain follows, binding the Gulf rulers by treaties — 1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899 — splitting the coast into separate emirates; after oil is found, American rivalry joins the game. Then the tide turns: Kuwait independent in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council founded in 1981 — the Gulf's states standing together at last.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
