# The Arabian Gulf: From Portuguese to British Control — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The Portuguese seized Muscat and Bahrain, and the Arabs of Oman freed Muscat in 1650 and drove them out; Britain then bound the Gulf rulers by treaties (1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899), and after oil was found faced American rivalry; Kuwait won independence in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council was founded in 1981.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 3, Lesson الخليج العربي محور لتنافس القوى الدولية (The Arab Gulf, an axis of international rivalry)” is mainly about this: Caliphs, governors and diwans run a strong state whose offices keep an empire of many peoples working.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Portuguese seized Muscat and Bahrain, and the Arabs of Oman freed Muscat in 1650 and drove them out; Britain then bound the Gulf rulers by treaties (1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899), and after oil was found faced American rivalry; Kuwait won independence in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council was founded in 1981.
