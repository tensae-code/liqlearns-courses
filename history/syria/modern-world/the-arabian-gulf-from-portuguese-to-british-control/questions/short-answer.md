# The Arabian Gulf: From Portuguese to British Control — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson الخليج العربي محور لتنافس القوى الدولية (The Arab Gulf, an axis of international rivalry)”?
**Answer:** The Portuguese come round the Cape and seize Muscat and Bahrain — and the Arabs of Oman free Muscat in 1650 and drive them out. Britain follows, binding the Gulf rulers by treaties — 1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899 — splitting the coast into separate emirates; after oil is found, American rivalry joins the game. Then the tide turns: Kuwait independent in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council founded in 1981 — the Gulf's states standing together at last.
