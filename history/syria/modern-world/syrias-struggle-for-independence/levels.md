## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1, Lesson التآمر الأجنبي على الحكم العربي في بلاد الشام (The foreign conspiracy against Arab rule in Bilad al-Sham)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Faisal's Arab government in Damascus (1918–1920) spread schools, founded the Arab Academy and made Arabic the language of administration; the Syrian General Congress declared Syria's complete independence including Palestine and Lebanon, and the King–Crane commission recommended independence for Syria and Iraq and no mandate — and its report was shelved until 1922. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
