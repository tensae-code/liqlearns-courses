# Syria's Struggle for Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Faisal's Arab government in Damascus (1918–1920) spread schools, founded the Arab Academy and made Arabic the language of administration; the Syrian General Congress declared Syria's complete independence including Palestine and Lebanon, and the King–Crane commission recommended independence for Syria and Iraq and no mandate — and its report was shelved until 1922.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 1, Lesson الكفاح السياسي في سورية حتى الاستقلال (The political struggle in Syria until independence)” is mainly about this: A father shows the same place in an old photo and a new one, and the child hunts for what changed and what stayed.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The 1928 constitution and the 1932 parliament elected Muhammad Ali al-Abid first president; the sixty-day strike forced the 1936 treaty project; the nationalists won the 1943 elections under Shukri al-Quwatli; France bombarded the parliament on 29 May 1945, and the French forces evacuated on 17 April 1946.
