## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 1, Lesson التآمر الأجنبي على الحكم العربي في بلاد الشام (The foreign conspiracy against Arab rule in Bilad al-Sham)** — Faisal's Arab government in Damascus (1918–1920) spread schools, founded the Arab Academy and made Arabic the language of administration; the Syrian General Congress declared Syria's complete independence including Palestine and Lebanon, and the King–Crane commission recommended independence for Syria and Iraq and no mandate — and its report was shelved until 1922.
2. **Unit 1, Lesson الكفاح السياسي في سورية حتى الاستقلال (The political struggle in Syria until independence)** — The 1928 constitution and the 1932 parliament elected Muhammad Ali al-Abid first president; the sixty-day strike forced the 1936 treaty project; the nationalists won the 1943 elections under Shukri al-Quwatli; France bombarded the parliament on 29 May 1945, and the French forces evacuated on 17 April 1946.
