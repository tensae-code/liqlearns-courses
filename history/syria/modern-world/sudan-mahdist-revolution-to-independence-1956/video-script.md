# Video script — Sudan: From the Mahdist Revolution to Independence, 1956


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Muhammad Ali's campaigns make the Sudan an Egyptian province — and its misrule raises the answer: the Mahdist revolution of 1881 under Muhammad Ahmad al-Mahdi. Britain returns with Kitchener's campaign, and the Condominium agreement of 1899 makes Britain the real ruler, sealing the south off by the closed-districts law. Resistance runs on — from 1903 to the Military College students of 1924 and the parties — until the 1953 self-government agreement and independence in 1956. The lesson closes with the coups, the civil wars and the South's secession in 2011.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
