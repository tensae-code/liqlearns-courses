# Sudan: From the Mahdist Revolution to Independence, 1956 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Muhammad Ali's campaigns made the Sudan an Egyptian province, and its misrule raised the Mahdist revolution of 1881 under Muhammad Ahmad al-Mahdi; Britain returned with Kitchener's campaign and the Condominium agreement of 1899 made Britain the real ruler; resistance ran until the 1953 self-government agreement and independence in 1956 — and the lesson closes with the coups, the civil wars and the South's secession in 2011. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
