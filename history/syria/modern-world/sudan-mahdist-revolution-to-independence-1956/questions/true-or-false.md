# Sudan: From the Mahdist Revolution to Independence, 1956 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Muhammad Ali's campaigns made the Sudan an Egyptian province, and its misrule raised the Mahdist revolution of 1881 under Muhammad Ahmad al-Mahdi; Britain returned with Kitchener's campaign and the Condominium agreement of 1899 made Britain the real ruler; resistance ran until the 1953 self-government agreement and independence in 1956 — and the lesson closes with the coups, the civil wars and the South's secession in 2011.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 4, Lesson السودان منذ عهد محمد علي حتى الوقت الحاضر (The Sudan from Muhammad Ali's era to the present)” is mainly about this: Taxed and squeezed by Roman officials, Egyptians turn against Byzantine rule and the Arab liberation makes Egypt the base from which Islam reaches Nubia.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Muhammad Ali's campaigns made the Sudan an Egyptian province, and its misrule raised the Mahdist revolution of 1881 under Muhammad Ahmad al-Mahdi; Britain returned with Kitchener's campaign and the Condominium agreement of 1899 made Britain the real ruler; resistance ran until the 1953 self-government agreement and independence in 1956 — and the lesson closes with the coups, the civil wars and the South's secession in 2011.
