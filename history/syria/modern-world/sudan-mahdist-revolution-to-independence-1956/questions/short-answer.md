# Sudan: From the Mahdist Revolution to Independence, 1956 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson السودان منذ عهد محمد علي حتى الوقت الحاضر (The Sudan from Muhammad Ali's era to the present)”?
**Answer:** Muhammad Ali's campaigns make the Sudan an Egyptian province — and its misrule raises the answer: the Mahdist revolution of 1881 under Muhammad Ahmad al-Mahdi. Britain returns with Kitchener's campaign, and the Condominium agreement of 1899 makes Britain the real ruler, sealing the south off by the closed-districts law. Resistance runs on — from 1903 to the Military College students of 1924 and the parties — until the 1953 self-government agreement and independence in 1956. The lesson closes with the coups, the civil wars and the South's secession in 2011.
