# Jordan: From Emirate to Kingdom — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Abdullah ibn Hussein reached Ma'an in 1920 and agreed with Churchill in 1921 on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision; the 1946 treaty ended the mandate and Abdullah became king; Jordan fought in 1948, joined the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom, lost them in June 1967, and signed the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
