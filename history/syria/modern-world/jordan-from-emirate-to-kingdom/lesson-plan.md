# Lesson plan — Jordan: From Emirate to Kingdom

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Jordan: From Emirate to Kingdom
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Abdullah ibn Hussein reached Ma'an in 1920 and agreed with Churchill in 1921 on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision; the 1946 treaty ended the mandate and Abdullah became king; Jordan fought in 1948, joined the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom, lost them in June 1967, and signed the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (recommended, never forced).
