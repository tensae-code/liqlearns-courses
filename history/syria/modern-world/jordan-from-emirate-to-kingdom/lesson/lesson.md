# Jordan: From Emirate to Kingdom

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Jordan: From Emirate to Kingdom** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Abdullah ibn Hussein reaches Ma'an in 1920 and, in 1921, agrees with Churchill on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision. The national movement rejects the 1928 treaty and Balfour's promise alike; in 1946 a new treaty ends the mandate and Abdullah becomes king. Jordan fights in 1948 and joins the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom — loses them in June 1967 — and signs the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994. Emirate to kingdom: the whole arc in one lesson.
