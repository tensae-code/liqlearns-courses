# Video script — Jordan: From Emirate to Kingdom


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Abdullah ibn Hussein reaches Ma'an in 1920 and, in 1921, agrees with Churchill on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision. The national movement rejects the 1928 treaty and Balfour's promise alike; in 1946 a new treaty ends the mandate and Abdullah becomes king. Jordan fights in 1948 and joins the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom — loses them in June 1967 — and signs the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994. Emirate to kingdom: the whole arc in one lesson.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
