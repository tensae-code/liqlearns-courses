# Jordan: From Emirate to Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Abdullah ibn Hussein reached Ma'an in 1920 and agreed with Churchill in 1921 on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision; the 1946 treaty ended the mandate and Abdullah became king; Jordan fought in 1948, joined the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom, lost them in June 1967, and signed the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 2, Lesson الأردن منذ تأسيس الإمارة حتى الوقت الحاضر (Jordan from the emirate's founding to the present)” is mainly about this: When farming and the first crafts begin, women work beside men in field, home and workshop and help found settled society.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Abdullah ibn Hussein reached Ma'an in 1920 and agreed with Churchill in 1921 on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision; the 1946 treaty ended the mandate and Abdullah became king; Jordan fought in 1948, joined the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom, lost them in June 1967, and signed the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994.
