# Jordan: From Emirate to Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson الأردن منذ تأسيس الإمارة حتى الوقت الحاضر (Jordan from the emirate's founding to the present)”?
**Answer:** Abdullah ibn Hussein reaches Ma'an in 1920 and, in 1921, agrees with Churchill on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision. The national movement rejects the 1928 treaty and Balfour's promise alike; in 1946 a new treaty ends the mandate and Abdullah becomes king. Jordan fights in 1948 and joins the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom — loses them in June 1967 — and signs the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994. Emirate to kingdom: the whole arc in one lesson.
