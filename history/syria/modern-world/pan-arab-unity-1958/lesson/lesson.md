# Pan-Arab Unity 1958: The United Arab Republic

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Pan-Arab Unity 1958: The United Arab Republic** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Independence brings coups — 1949 opens a decade of them — and the Cold War pulls Syria between the two camps. Then comes the boldest answer to division: unity. On 22 February 1958 Syria unites with Egypt; President Quwatli steps aside for Jamal Abd al-Nasser, and the United Arab Republic is born — one Arab state where two had been. It lasts until the secession of 28 September 1961, but the book treats those three years as proof that the Arab world's unity was attempted in earnest, from Damascus.
