# Pan-Arab Unity 1958: The United Arab Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson سورية منذ الاستقلال وحتى الوقت الحاضر (Syria from independence to the present)”?
**Answer:** Independence brings coups — 1949 opens a decade of them — and the Cold War pulls Syria between the two camps. Then comes the boldest answer to division: unity. On 22 February 1958 Syria unites with Egypt; President Quwatli steps aside for Jamal Abd al-Nasser, and the United Arab Republic is born — one Arab state where two had been. It lasts until the secession of 28 September 1961, but the book treats those three years as proof that the Arab world's unity was attempted in earnest, from Damascus.
