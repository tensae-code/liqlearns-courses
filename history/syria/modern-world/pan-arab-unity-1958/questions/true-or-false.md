# Pan-Arab Unity 1958: The United Arab Republic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   After the coups of 1949 and the Cold War pull of the two camps, Syria united with Egypt: on 22 February 1958 President Quwatli stepped aside for Jamal Abd al-Nasser and the United Arab Republic was born, until the secession of 28 September 1961.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson سورية منذ الاستقلال وحتى الوقت الحاضر (Syria from independence to the present)” is mainly about this: The school is named after Jul Jamal, the Syrian naval officer from Latakia who gave his life defending Egypt in 1956 — a national hero whose 'imprint' the children research and record.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After the coups of 1949 and the Cold War pull of the two camps, Syria united with Egypt: on 22 February 1958 President Quwatli stepped aside for Jamal Abd al-Nasser and the United Arab Republic was born, until the secession of 28 September 1961.
