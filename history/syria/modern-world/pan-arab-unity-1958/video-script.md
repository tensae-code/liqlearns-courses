# Video script — Pan-Arab Unity 1958: The United Arab Republic


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Independence brings coups — 1949 opens a decade of them — and the Cold War pulls Syria between the two camps. Then comes the boldest answer to division: unity. On 22 February 1958 Syria unites with Egypt; President Quwatli steps aside for Jamal Abd al-Nasser, and the United Arab Republic is born — one Arab state where two had been. It lasts until the secession of 28 September 1961, but the book treats those three years as proof that the Arab world's unity was attempted in earnest, from Damascus.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
