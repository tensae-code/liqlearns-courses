# Hands-on — Pan-Arab Unity 1958: The United Arab Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Pan-Arab Unity 1958: The United Arab Republic — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After the coups of 1949 and the Cold War pull of the two camps, Syria united with Egypt: on 22 February 1958 President Quwatli stepped aside for Jamal Abd al-Nasser and the United Arab Republic was born, until the secession of 28 September 1961. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
