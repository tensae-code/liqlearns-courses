# Muhammad Ali's Modernising Egypt Project — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After the French campaign left Egypt, the Ottoman state appointed Muhammad Ali in 1805; his reforms developed agriculture, industry and trade, strengthened the army and fleet, and his project expanded into Arabia and the Sudan and then into Bilad al-Sham, until the European states forced him out of Syria by the London treaty of 1841. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
