# Muhammad Ali's Modernising Egypt Project — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   After the French campaign left Egypt, the Ottoman state appointed Muhammad Ali in 1805; his reforms developed agriculture, industry and trade, strengthened the army and fleet, and his project expanded into Arabia and the Sudan and then into Bilad al-Sham, until the European states forced him out of Syria by the London treaty of 1841.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson العرب في العصر الحديث (The Arabs in the modern age)” is mainly about this: Grandfather Salim narrates fifty years of events and the children order them on a timeline from 1900 to 2000, oldest to newest.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After the French campaign left Egypt, the Ottoman state appointed Muhammad Ali in 1805; his reforms developed agriculture, industry and trade, strengthened the army and fleet, and his project expanded into Arabia and the Sudan and then into Bilad al-Sham, until the European states forced him out of Syria by the London treaty of 1841.
