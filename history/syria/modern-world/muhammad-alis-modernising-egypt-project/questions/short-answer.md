# Muhammad Ali's Modernising Egypt Project — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson العرب في العصر الحديث (The Arabs in the modern age)”?
**Answer:** After the French campaign leaves Egypt, the Ottoman state appoints a new governor in 1805: Muhammad Ali. His project is total — reforms that develop agriculture, industry and trade, and an army and fleet strengthened until Egypt is a power in its own right. The project expands outward, into Arabia and the Sudan, and then into Bilad al-Sham itself, until the European states, alarmed, force him out of Syria by the London treaty of 1841. The modern Arab age, the Grade 9 book says, begins with this one man's state-building drive.
