# Video script — Muhammad Ali's Modernising Egypt Project


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

After the French campaign leaves Egypt, the Ottoman state appoints a new governor in 1805: Muhammad Ali. His project is total — reforms that develop agriculture, industry and trade, and an army and fleet strengthened until Egypt is a power in its own right. The project expands outward, into Arabia and the Sudan, and then into Bilad al-Sham itself, until the European states, alarmed, force him out of Syria by the London treaty of 1841. The modern Arab age, the Grade 9 book says, begins with this one man's state-building drive.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
