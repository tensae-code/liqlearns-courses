# Hands-on — Muhammad Ali's Modernising Egypt Project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Muhammad Ali's Modernising Egypt Project — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After the French campaign left Egypt, the Ottoman state appointed Muhammad Ali in 1805; his reforms developed agriculture, industry and trade, strengthened the army and fleet, and his project expanded into Arabia and the Sudan and then into Bilad al-Sham, until the European states forced him out of Syria by the London treaty of 1841. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
