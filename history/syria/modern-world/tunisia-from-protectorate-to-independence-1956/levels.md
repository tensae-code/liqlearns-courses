# Tunisia: From Protectorate to Independence, 1956 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — France invaded in 1881 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Bardo, and the Kairouan revolt answered; parties carried the struggle until the armed rising of 1954, fired by Algeria's revolution, won independence in 1956; Bourguiba's republic of 1957, Ben Ali, and the 2011 revolution close the lesson. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
