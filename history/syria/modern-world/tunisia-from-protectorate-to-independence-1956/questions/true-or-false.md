# Tunisia: From Protectorate to Independence, 1956 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   France invaded in 1881 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Bardo, and the Kairouan revolt answered; parties carried the struggle until the armed rising of 1954, fired by Algeria's revolution, won independence in 1956; Bourguiba's republic of 1957, Ben Ali, and the 2011 revolution close the lesson.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 5, Lesson تونس منذ الحماية الفرنسية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Tunisia from the French protectorate to the present)” is mainly about this: Nur al-Din Zengi unites the Islamic lands against the Crusaders, and centuries later Omar al-Mukhtar fights the Italian coloniser in Libya — two resisters held up as one lesson.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: France invaded in 1881 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Bardo, and the Kairouan revolt answered; parties carried the struggle until the armed rising of 1954, fired by Algeria's revolution, won independence in 1956; Bourguiba's republic of 1957, Ben Ali, and the 2011 revolution close the lesson.
