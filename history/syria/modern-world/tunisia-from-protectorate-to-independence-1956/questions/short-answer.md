# Tunisia: From Protectorate to Independence, 1956 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 5, Lesson تونس منذ الحماية الفرنسية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Tunisia from the French protectorate to the present)”?
**Answer:** Khayr al-Din's reforms and the constitution of 1861, Ali ibn Ghadhahum's revolt of 1864 and the European debt commission of 1869 prepare the ground — and France invades in 1881, imposing the protectorate by the Treaty of Bardo; the Kairouan revolt answers at once. Parties carry the struggle for decades, until the armed rising of 1954, fired by Algeria's revolution, wins independence in 1956 and the evacuation of the Bizerte base. Bourguiba's republic of 1957, Ben Ali, and the 2011 revolution close the lesson in the pupils' own century.
