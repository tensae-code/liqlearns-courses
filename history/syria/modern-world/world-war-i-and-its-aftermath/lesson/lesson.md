# World War I and Its Aftermath

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **World War I and Its Aftermath** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

While Sharif Hussein negotiated with Britain and joined the Allies in 1916, the Allies were secretly cutting the Arab lands into shares — the Sykes–Picot agreement of 1916 — and in 1917 Britain promised the Zionists a national home in Palestine in the Balfour Declaration. Then the Paris peace conference of 1919 invented the mandate system, a new name for rule by others, and at San Remo in 1920 it was applied to Syria and its neighbours. The war's aftermath, the book teaches, was decided in rooms the Arabs were not in.
