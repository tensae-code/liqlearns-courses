# World War I and Its Aftermath — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — While Sharif Hussein negotiated with Britain and joined the Allies in 1916, the Allies were secretly dividing the Arab lands in the Sykes–Picot agreement of 1916 and Britain promised the Zionists a national home in Palestine in the Balfour Declaration of 1917; the Paris peace conference of 1919 invented the mandate system, applied at San Remo in 1920. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
