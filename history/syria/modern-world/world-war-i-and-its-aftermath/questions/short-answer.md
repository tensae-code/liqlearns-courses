# World War I and Its Aftermath — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson العرب في العصر الحديث بين الضعف ومحاولات النهوض والتحرر (The Arabs in the modern age)”?
**Answer:** While Sharif Hussein negotiated with Britain and joined the Allies in 1916, the Allies were secretly cutting the Arab lands into shares — the Sykes–Picot agreement of 1916 — and in 1917 Britain promised the Zionists a national home in Palestine in the Balfour Declaration. Then the Paris peace conference of 1919 invented the mandate system, a new name for rule by others, and at San Remo in 1920 it was applied to Syria and its neighbours. The war's aftermath, the book teaches, was decided in rooms the Arabs were not in.
