# World War I and Its Aftermath — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   While Sharif Hussein negotiated with Britain and joined the Allies in 1916, the Allies were secretly dividing the Arab lands in the Sykes–Picot agreement of 1916 and Britain promised the Zionists a national home in Palestine in the Balfour Declaration of 1917; the Paris peace conference of 1919 invented the mandate system, applied at San Remo in 1920.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson العرب في العصر الحديث بين الضعف ومحاولات النهوض والتحرر (The Arabs in the modern age)” is mainly about this: A printed timeline (خط زمني) runs from walking on foot through pack animals, wheeled carts, sailing ships, steam and oil, to trains and aeroplanes — transport developing stage by stage.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: While Sharif Hussein negotiated with Britain and joined the Allies in 1916, the Allies were secretly dividing the Arab lands in the Sykes–Picot agreement of 1916 and Britain promised the Zionists a national home in Palestine in the Balfour Declaration of 1917; the Paris peace conference of 1919 invented the mandate system, applied at San Remo in 1920.
