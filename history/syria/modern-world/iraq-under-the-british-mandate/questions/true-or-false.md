# Iraq Under the British Mandate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Britain, eyeing Iraq's wealth and oil, occupied it from al-Faw in 1914 and took the mandate at San Remo; its plunder and forced labour raised the Revolution of 1920, so Britain set Faisal on the throne in 1921, kept Mosul in Iraq by a League decision tied to a 25-year mandate, and by the 1930 treaty recognised independence while keeping two air bases for 25 years.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 3, Lesson الأطماع البريطانية في العراق (British greed in Iraq)” is mainly about this: Cave drawings cannot carry all that people need to say, so writing is invented and knowledge begins to travel.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Britain, eyeing Iraq's wealth and oil, occupied it from al-Faw in 1914 and took the mandate at San Remo; its plunder and forced labour raised the Revolution of 1920, so Britain set Faisal on the throne in 1921, kept Mosul in Iraq by a League decision tied to a 25-year mandate, and by the 1930 treaty recognised independence while keeping two air bases for 25 years.
