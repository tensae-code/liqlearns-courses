# Iraq Under the British Mandate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson الأطماع البريطانية في العراق (British greed in Iraq)”?
**Answer:** Britain eyes Iraq's wealth and its oil, occupies the country from al-Faw in 1914, and takes the mandate at San Remo. Plunder and forced labour raise the Revolution of 1920 — so Britain changes tactics: Faisal is set on the throne in 1921, Mosul is kept in Iraq by a League decision tied to a 25-year mandate, and by the 1930 treaty Britain recognises independence while keeping two air bases for 25 years. Independence with strings: the lesson teaches its pupils to read the strings.
