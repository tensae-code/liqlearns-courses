# Video script — Iraq Under the British Mandate


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Britain eyes Iraq's wealth and its oil, occupies the country from al-Faw in 1914, and takes the mandate at San Remo. Plunder and forced labour raise the Revolution of 1920 — so Britain changes tactics: Faisal is set on the throne in 1921, Mosul is kept in Iraq by a League decision tied to a 25-year mandate, and by the 1930 treaty Britain recognises independence while keeping two air bases for 25 years. Independence with strings: the lesson teaches its pupils to read the strings.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
