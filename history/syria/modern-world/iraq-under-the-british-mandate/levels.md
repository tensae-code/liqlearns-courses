# Iraq Under the British Mandate — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Britain, eyeing Iraq's wealth and oil, occupied it from al-Faw in 1914 and took the mandate at San Remo; its plunder and forced labour raised the Revolution of 1920, so Britain set Faisal on the throne in 1921, kept Mosul in Iraq by a League decision tied to a 25-year mandate, and by the 1930 treaty recognised independence while keeping two air bases for 25 years. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
