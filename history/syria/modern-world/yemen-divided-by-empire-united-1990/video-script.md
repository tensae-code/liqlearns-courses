# Video script — Yemen: Divided by Empire, United 1990


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Britain seizes Aden in 1839 as a station on the India route, and a border line drawn in 1904 splits Yemen in two — the wound the whole lesson works to close. In the north, the Mutawakkilite kingdom falls to the revolution of 1962 and a republic scarred by civil war; in the south, the revolt of 1963 wins independence in 1967. Two states, one people: in 1990 the two Yemens unite under Ali Abdullah Saleh — who steps down after the popular revolution of 2011, where the book leaves the story.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
