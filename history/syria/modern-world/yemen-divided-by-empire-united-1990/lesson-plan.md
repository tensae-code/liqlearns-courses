# Lesson plan — Yemen: Divided by Empire, United 1990

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Yemen: Divided by Empire, United 1990
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Modern Arab World: Occupation, Resistance and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain seized Aden in 1839 and a 1904 border line split Yemen in two; the Mutawakkilite kingdom in the north fell to the revolution of 1962, while the south won independence in 1967 after the revolt of 1963; the two Yemens united in 1990 under Ali Abdullah Saleh, who stepped down after the popular revolution of 2011.
