# Yemen: Divided by Empire, United 1990 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Britain seized Aden in 1839 and a 1904 border line split Yemen in two; the Mutawakkilite kingdom in the north fell to the revolution of 1962, while the south won independence in 1967 after the revolt of 1963; the two Yemens united in 1990 under Ali Abdullah Saleh, who stepped down after the popular revolution of 2011.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 3, Lesson اليمن منذ مقاومة الاحتلال الأجنبي والعزلة إلى الوحدة (Yemen from resisting foreign occupation and isolation to unity)” is mainly about this: The Syrians have been known since ancient times for their skill in buying and selling, and since olden days the trade caravans crossed Syria coming from India to Europe.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Britain seized Aden in 1839 and a 1904 border line split Yemen in two; the Mutawakkilite kingdom in the north fell to the revolution of 1962, while the south won independence in 1967 after the revolt of 1963; the two Yemens united in 1990 under Ali Abdullah Saleh, who stepped down after the popular revolution of 2011.
