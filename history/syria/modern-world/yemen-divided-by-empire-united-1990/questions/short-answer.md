# Yemen: Divided by Empire, United 1990 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 3, Lesson اليمن منذ مقاومة الاحتلال الأجنبي والعزلة إلى الوحدة (Yemen from resisting foreign occupation and isolation to unity)”?
**Answer:** Britain seizes Aden in 1839 as a station on the India route, and a border line drawn in 1904 splits Yemen in two — the wound the whole lesson works to close. In the north, the Mutawakkilite kingdom falls to the revolution of 1962 and a republic scarred by civil war; in the south, the revolt of 1963 wins independence in 1967. Two states, one people: in 1990 the two Yemens unite under Ali Abdullah Saleh — who steps down after the popular revolution of 2011, where the book leaves the story.
