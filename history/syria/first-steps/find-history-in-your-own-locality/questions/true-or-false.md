# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     A father shows the same place in an old photo and a new one, and the child hunts for what changed and what stayed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Skills unit: التاريخ والموقع (History and location)” is mainly about this: After matching photos to events (Damascus, Arab Capital of Culture 2008), each child writes in their notebook an important event from their own family's or homeland's memory.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Every event is pinned to its place on the map, and place explains the event as much as time does.
