# Find History in Your Own Locality — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 6, Lesson 3: صور من وطني (Pictures from my homeland)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A father shows the same place in an old photo and a new one, and the child hunts for what changed and what stayed. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Skills unit: التاريخ والموقع (History and location)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Every event is pinned to its place on the map, and place explains the event as much as time does. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit أنا, Lesson ذاكرة وطن (Memory of a homeland)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: After matching photos to events (Damascus, Arab Capital of Culture 2008), each child writes in their notebook an important event from their own family's or homeland's memory. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit مجتمعي, Lesson العمران في وطني (Building in my homeland)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Photos of an old quarter — narrow alleys, houses joined wall to wall, the Arab courtyard house — are set against a modern quarter of wide streets and spaced towers, and the child reads the past of building in their own streets. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 3, Lesson الحياة في المدينة (Life in the city)
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Most Syrian cities go back to ancient historical periods with successive civilisations; their old quarters of narrow lanes and covered souks are still there to be read, in photographs of Damascus a hundred years ago and of al-Hamidiyah souk in 1886. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
