## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 6, Lesson 3: صور من وطني (Pictures from my homeland)** — A father shows the same place in an old photo and a new one, and the child hunts for what changed and what stayed.
2. **Skills unit: التاريخ والموقع (History and location)** — Every event is pinned to its place on the map, and place explains the event as much as time does.
3. **Unit أنا, Lesson ذاكرة وطن (Memory of a homeland)** — After matching photos to events (Damascus, Arab Capital of Culture 2008), each child writes in their notebook an important event from their own family's or homeland's memory.
4. **Unit مجتمعي, Lesson العمران في وطني (Building in my homeland)** — Photos of an old quarter — narrow alleys, houses joined wall to wall, the Arab courtyard house — are set against a modern quarter of wide streets and spaced towers, and the child reads the past of building in their own streets.
5. **Unit 3, Lesson الحياة في المدينة (Life in the city)** — Most Syrian cities go back to ancient historical periods with successive civilisations; their old quarters of narrow lanes and covered souks are still there to be read, in photographs of Damascus a hundred years ago and of al-Hamidiyah souk in 1886.
