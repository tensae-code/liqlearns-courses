# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **What History Is** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2, Lesson 1: صغار كبار (Small ones, grown ones)

A class of six-year-olds looks at photographs of themselves as babies and at the young adults around them, and a simple discovery lands: the things a small child can do are not the things a grown person can do. A single life is not one fixed picture — it changes as the years pass. That change across time, in one person's own life, is where the book says history begins: the past is the story of how things became what they are.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit أنا, Lesson شجرة أسرتي (My family tree)

The child sits down and draws a tree — but this tree's branches carry people: grandparents at the top, then parents, then the children. Before there was me, the book says, there were the people who came before me, and their lives are the first history anyone ever learns. The family tree turns 'long ago' into faces with names.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit أنا وأنت, Lesson صغار كبار (Small ones, grown ones)

Classmates compare birthdays and find no two lives run at the same speed: each was born in a different month, and people sort naturally into child, young adult and elder. One single life, watched closely, is seen changing across its stages — small, grown, old. The lesson makes the youngest pupils historians of the one story they know best: a human life unfolding in time.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Children compare what they could do at six with what young adults do, and see that a person's own life changes across time — history begins with change in one life. Give one real example: The child draws the family tree — grandparents, parents, siblings — and learns that history begins with the people who came before me. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
