# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Children compare what they could do at six with what young adults do, and see that a person's own life changes across time — history begins with change in one life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit أنا, Lesson شجرة أسرتي (My family tree)” is mainly about this: Calendars grow from watching sun and moon, dating starts from the Nativity and the Hijra, and Syria's eras are cut at writing (3200 BCE), the Umayyads (662) and Hattin (1187).
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The child draws the family tree — grandparents, parents, siblings — and learns that history begins with the people who came before me.
