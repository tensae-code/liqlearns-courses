## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2, Lesson 1: صغار كبار (Small ones, grown ones)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Children compare what they could do at six with what young adults do, and see that a person's own life changes across time — history begins with change in one life. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The child draws the family tree — grandparents, parents, siblings — and learns that history begins with the people who came before me. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
