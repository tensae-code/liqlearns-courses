# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     The antiquities printed on Syria's banknotes — the Umayyad Mosque, Bosra's amphitheatre, Aleppo's citadel, Ugarit's alphabet — are matched to the governorates where they stand.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 1, Lesson 4: التراث الشعبي (Folk heritage)” is mainly about this: The antiquities printed on Syria's banknotes — the Umayyad Mosque, Bosra's amphitheatre, Aleppo's citadel, Ugarit's alphabet — are matched to the governorates where they stand.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Feasts, tales, crafts and customs pass hand to hand down the generations and become a people's living heritage.
