# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Kings hold their states together with officials, laws, institutions and treaties.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 1, Lesson 3: التنظيم الإداري (Administrative organisation)” is mainly about this: Damascus is the oldest inhabited capital in the world, split into an old city inside its wall — the Umayyad Mosque, the citadel and the old souks — and a new city, and Aleppo likewise joins an old part, with its famous citadel, to a new one.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Caliphs, governors and diwans run a strong state whose offices keep an empire of many peoples working.
