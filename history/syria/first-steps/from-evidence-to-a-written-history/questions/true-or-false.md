# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Standing remains, manuscripts, coins, tools and miniatures are weighed as proof, with the al-Hayr palaces and a Fatimid ewer as worked examples.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit وطني, Lesson بين الماضي والحاضر (Between past and present)” is mainly about this: Feasts, tales, crafts and customs pass hand to hand down the generations and become a people's living heritage.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Pupils swap the wedge signs of the Ugaritic alphabet for Arabic letters to decode where the excavated pieces are kept — old writing is evidence to be read.
