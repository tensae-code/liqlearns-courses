# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     A six-year-old orders the day's activities 1–6 on a timeline and learns that putting events in order is the first move of history.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 6, Lesson 4: لنعمل معًا (Let's work together)” is mainly about this: The village tailor sews by hand so slowly the whole village queues until the sewing machine arrives; baker, carpenter and weaver are each shown then and now — trades change through time.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Fathers show the tools and machines of their trades then and now, and children match each old tool to the faster machine that replaced it.
