# Lesson plan — Dating the Past

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Dating the Past
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: History Begins at Home (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A six-year-old orders the day's activities 1–6 on a timeline and learns that putting events in order is the first move of history.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 1, Lesson 4: كيف أمضي يومي؟ (How do I spend my day?)** — A six-year-old orders the day's activities 1–6 on a timeline and learns that putting events in order is the first move of history.
2. **Unit 6, Lesson 4: لنعمل معًا (Let's work together)** — Fathers show the tools and machines of their trades then and now, and children match each old tool to the faster machine that replaced it.
3. **Skills unit: معرفة المراحل الزمنية (Knowing the stages of time)** — Pupils learn eras, millennia, centuries and decades and place Sennacherib and Julia Domna on one timeline.
4. **Skills unit: التقويم والتأريخ وتقسيم العصور (Calendars, dating and dividing the eras)** — Calendars grow from watching sun and moon, dating starts from the Nativity and the Hijra, and Syria's eras are cut at writing (3200 BCE), the Umayyads (662) and Hattin (1187).
5. **Unit أنا, Lesson ذاكرة وطن (Memory of a homeland)** — Grandfather Salim narrates fifty years of events and the children order them on a timeline from 1900 to 2000, oldest to newest.
6. **Unit أنا وأنت, Lesson صغار كبار (Small ones, grown ones)** — Sami's family — grandfather, father, mother, Sami, his sister — is ordered on a timeline (الخط الزمني) by the months they were born in.
7. **Unit وطني, Lesson بين الماضي والحاضر (Between past and present)** — The village tailor sews by hand so slowly the whole village queues until the sewing machine arrives; baker, carpenter and weaver are each shown then and now — trades change through time.
8. **Unit 2 سورية والتاريخ, Lesson وسائل النقل في سورية (Means of transport in Syria)** — A printed timeline (خط زمني) runs from walking on foot through pack animals, wheeled carts, sailing ships, steam and oil, to trains and aeroplanes — transport developing stage by stage.
