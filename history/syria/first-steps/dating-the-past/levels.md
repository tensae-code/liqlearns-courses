# Dating the Past — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1, Lesson 4: كيف أمضي يومي؟ (How do I spend my day?)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A six-year-old orders the day's activities 1–6 on a timeline and learns that putting events in order is the first move of history. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 6, Lesson 4: لنعمل معًا (Let's work together)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Fathers show the tools and machines of their trades then and now, and children match each old tool to the faster machine that replaced it. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Skills unit: معرفة المراحل الزمنية (Knowing the stages of time)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Pupils learn eras, millennia, centuries and decades and place Sennacherib and Julia Domna on one timeline. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Skills unit: التقويم والتأريخ وتقسيم العصور (Calendars, dating and dividing the eras)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Calendars grow from watching sun and moon, dating starts from the Nativity and the Hijra, and Syria's eras are cut at writing (3200 BCE), the Umayyads (662) and Hattin (1187). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit أنا, Lesson ذاكرة وطن (Memory of a homeland)
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Grandfather Salim narrates fifty years of events and the children order them on a timeline from 1900 to 2000, oldest to newest. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit أنا وأنت, Lesson صغار كبار (Small ones, grown ones)
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sami's family — grandfather, father, mother, Sami, his sister — is ordered on a timeline (الخط الزمني) by the months they were born in. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — Unit وطني, Lesson بين الماضي والحاضر (Between past and present)
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The village tailor sews by hand so slowly the whole village queues until the sewing machine arrives; baker, carpenter and weaver are each shown then and now — trades change through time. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 8 — Unit 2 سورية والتاريخ, Lesson وسائل النقل في سورية (Means of transport in Syria)
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A printed timeline (خط زمني) runs from walking on foot through pack animals, wheeled carts, sailing ships, steam and oil, to trains and aeroplanes — transport developing stage by stage. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
