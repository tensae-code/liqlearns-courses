# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Herodotus names Egypt the Nile's gift; facing the flood together, the valley's settlements unite and organise irrigation under one central rule.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson 2: مصر هبة النيل (Egypt, gift of the Nile)” is mainly about this: After liberation the Arabs raise their world state with Damascus at its heart and give it its own character.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Herodotus names Egypt the Nile's gift; facing the flood together, the valley's settlements unite and organise irrigation under one central rule.
