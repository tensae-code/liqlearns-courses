# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson 2: مصر هبة النيل (Egypt, gift of the Nile)”?
**Answer:** Herodotus gave Egypt its most famous name: the gift of the Nile. The Grade 6 book tells why the name fits: every year the river floods, and no village can face the flood alone. Facing it together, the valley's settlements unite, organise their irrigation under one central rule, and a kingdom is born out of water management. Egypt's civilisation begins, in this telling, as a cooperation forced by a river.
