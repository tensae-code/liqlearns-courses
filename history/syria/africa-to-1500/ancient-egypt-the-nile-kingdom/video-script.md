# Video script — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Herodotus gave Egypt its most famous name: the gift of the Nile. The Grade 6 book tells why the name fits: every year the river floods, and no village can face the flood alone. Facing it together, the valley's settlements unite, organise their irrigation under one central rule, and a kingdom is born out of water management. Egypt's civilisation begins, in this telling, as a cooperation forced by a river.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
