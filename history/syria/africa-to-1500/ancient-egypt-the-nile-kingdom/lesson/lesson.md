# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Herodotus gave Egypt its most famous name: the gift of the Nile. The Grade 6 book tells why the name fits: every year the river floods, and no village can face the flood alone. Facing it together, the valley's settlements unite, organise their irrigation under one central rule, and a kingdom is born out of water management. Egypt's civilisation begins, in this telling, as a cooperation forced by a river.
