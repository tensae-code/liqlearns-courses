# Carthage and the Punic Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Princess Elissa sails from the Phoenician coast of the Levant and founds Carthage, whose ships and markets rule the western Mediterranean.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson 4: حضارة قرطاجة (The civilisation of Carthage)” is mainly about this: Women of the Arab state create in the home, the sciences, letters, crafts, markets, politics and administration.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Princess Elissa sails from the Phoenician coast of the Levant and founds Carthage, whose ships and markets rule the western Mediterranean.
