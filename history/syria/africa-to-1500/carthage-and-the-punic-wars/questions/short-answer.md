# Carthage and the Punic Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson 4: حضارة قرطاجة (The civilisation of Carthage)”?
**Answer:** Carthage begins with a voyage from Syria's own coast. Princess Elissa sails from the Phoenician cities of the Levant, crosses the Mediterranean, and founds a new city on the North African shore. Her city grows until its ships and markets rule the western Mediterranean — a Levantine seed become a maritime empire. For Syrian pupils the story carries a point of pride: Carthage's founders set out from beaches they know.
