# Video script — Carthage and the Punic Wars


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Carthage begins with a voyage from Syria's own coast. Princess Elissa sails from the Phoenician cities of the Levant, crosses the Mediterranean, and founds a new city on the North African shore. Her city grows until its ships and markets rule the western Mediterranean — a Levantine seed become a maritime empire. For Syrian pupils the story carries a point of pride: Carthage's founders set out from beaches they know.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
