# Carthage and the Punic Wars

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Carthage and the Punic Wars** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Carthage begins with a voyage from Syria's own coast. Princess Elissa sails from the Phoenician cities of the Levant, crosses the Mediterranean, and founds a new city on the North African shore. Her city grows until its ships and markets rule the western Mediterranean — a Levantine seed become a maritime empire. For Syrian pupils the story carries a point of pride: Carthage's founders set out from beaches they know.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Princess Elissa sails from the Phoenician coast of the Levant and founds Carthage, whose ships and markets rule the western Mediterranean. Give one real example: Princess Elissa sails from the Phoenician coast of the Levant and founds Carthage, whose ships and markets rule the western Mediterranean. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
