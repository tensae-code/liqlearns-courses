# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 1, Lesson 3: حضارة مصر القديمة (The civilisation of ancient Egypt)”?
**Answer:** What did Egypt make of its river? A civilisation measured in seasons: the year cut into flood, sowing and harvest, so that time itself follows the Nile. The shaduf lifts water to the fields, canals carry it where it is needed, and astronomy and engineering turn observation into building. The lesson's claim is simple — Egypt took a river and turned it into a civilisation, and its gifts flowed outward to everyone after.
