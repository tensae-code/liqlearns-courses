# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The year is cut into flood, sowing and harvest; the shaduf, canals, astronomy and engineering turn the river into a civilisation.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 1, Lesson 3: حضارة مصر القديمة (The civilisation of ancient Egypt)” is mainly about this: From its old Phoenician ports — Barqa, Tripoli, Sabratha, Leptis — Libya is liberated after Egypt and an Arab-Islamic civilisation rises there.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The year is cut into flood, sowing and harvest; the shaduf, canals, astronomy and engineering turn the river into a civilisation.
