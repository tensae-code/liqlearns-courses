# Video script — Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

What did Egypt make of its river? A civilisation measured in seasons: the year cut into flood, sowing and harvest, so that time itself follows the Nile. The shaduf lifts water to the fields, canals carry it where it is needed, and astronomy and engineering turn observation into building. The lesson's claim is simple — Egypt took a river and turned it into a civilisation, and its gifts flowed outward to everyone after.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
