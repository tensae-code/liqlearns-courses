# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

What did Egypt make of its river? A civilisation measured in seasons: the year cut into flood, sowing and harvest, so that time itself follows the Nile. The shaduf lifts water to the fields, canals carry it where it is needed, and astronomy and engineering turn observation into building. The lesson's claim is simple — Egypt took a river and turned it into a civilisation, and its gifts flowed outward to everyone after.
