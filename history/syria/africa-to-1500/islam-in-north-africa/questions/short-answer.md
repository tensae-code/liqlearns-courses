# Islam in North Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson 3: تحرير مصر يفتح أبواب وادي النيل (The liberation of Egypt opens the Nile Valley)”?
**Answer:** Egypt under Byzantine rule was taxed and squeezed by Roman officials, until its people turned against their rulers. Into that discontent comes the Arab liberation — and Egypt, freed, becomes the base from which Islam reaches down the Nile into Nubia. The lesson's title carries its logic: liberate Egypt, and the doors of the whole Nile Valley swing open.

**2.** (Sub lesson 4)     How does the story in “Unit 5, Lesson 3: المغرب قبيل التحرير العربي الإسلامي وبعده (The Maghreb before and after the Arab-Islamic liberation)” end?
**Answer:** The last and hardest stage of the westward story is the Maghreb. The Umayyad state shoulders the liberation of Ifriqiya and the Maghreb, city by city from Kairouan to Tangier, and settles the whole region as one province of the state. With Tangier reached, the Arab state stands at the Atlantic — the expansion that began in Arabia now spans a continent's northern shore.
