# Islam in North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     Taxed and squeezed by Roman officials, Egyptians turn against Byzantine rule and the Arab liberation makes Egypt the base from which Islam reaches Nubia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 2, Lesson 4: إنجازات العرب المسلمين الحضارية تؤدي إلى التآلف (The Muslim Arabs' civilisational achievements bring harmony)” is mainly about this: After the French campaign left Egypt, the Ottoman state appointed Muhammad Ali in 1805; his reforms developed agriculture, industry and trade, strengthened the army and fleet, and his project expanded into Arabia and the Sudan and then into Bilad al-Sham, until the European states forced him out of Syria by the London treaty of 1841.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Egypt becomes a province of the caliphate with Fustat as capital; governors reform administration and courts, and a new shared civilisation takes shape.
