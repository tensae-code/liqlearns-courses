# Lesson plan — Islam in North Africa

**Course:** History — Syria · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Islam in North Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Arab State from Damascus: Medieval Syria (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Taxed and squeezed by Roman officials, Egyptians turn against Byzantine rule and the Arab liberation makes Egypt the base from which Islam reaches Nubia.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Arab State from Damascus: Medieval Syria (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 2, Lesson 3: تحرير مصر يفتح أبواب وادي النيل (The liberation of Egypt opens the Nile Valley)** — Taxed and squeezed by Roman officials, Egyptians turn against Byzantine rule and the Arab liberation makes Egypt the base from which Islam reaches Nubia.
2. **Unit 2, Lesson 4: إنجازات العرب المسلمين الحضارية تؤدي إلى التآلف (The Muslim Arabs' civilisational achievements bring harmony)** — Egypt becomes a province of the caliphate with Fustat as capital; governors reform administration and courts, and a new shared civilisation takes shape.
3. **Unit 4, Lesson 3: ليبيا قبيل التحرير العربي الإسلامي وبعده (Libya before and after the Arab-Islamic liberation)** — From its old Phoenician ports — Barqa, Tripoli, Sabratha, Leptis — Libya is liberated after Egypt and an Arab-Islamic civilisation rises there.
4. **Unit 5, Lesson 3: المغرب قبيل التحرير العربي الإسلامي وبعده (The Maghreb before and after the Arab-Islamic liberation)** — The Umayyad state shoulders the liberation of Ifriqiya and the Maghreb, from Kairouan to Tangier, and settles it as one province.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
