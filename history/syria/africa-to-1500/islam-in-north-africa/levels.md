# Islam in North Africa — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2, Lesson 3: تحرير مصر يفتح أبواب وادي النيل (The liberation of Egypt opens the Nile Valley)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Taxed and squeezed by Roman officials, Egyptians turn against Byzantine rule and the Arab liberation makes Egypt the base from which Islam reaches Nubia. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2, Lesson 4: إنجازات العرب المسلمين الحضارية تؤدي إلى التآلف (The Muslim Arabs' civilisational achievements bring harmony)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Egypt becomes a province of the caliphate with Fustat as capital; governors reform administration and courts, and a new shared civilisation takes shape. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 4, Lesson 3: ليبيا قبيل التحرير العربي الإسلامي وبعده (Libya before and after the Arab-Islamic liberation)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: From its old Phoenician ports — Barqa, Tripoli, Sabratha, Leptis — Libya is liberated after Egypt and an Arab-Islamic civilisation rises there. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
