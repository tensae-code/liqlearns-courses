# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     What happens in “Unit 2, Lesson 1: أساس الاستقرار البشري (Farming, the basis of human settlement)”?
**Answer:** For ages beyond counting, people wandered after food — hunting, gathering, never staying long in one place. Then, on Syria's own Euphrates valley, the great turn happens: people plant seeds and reap harvests, and everything changes. A farmer must stay by his field, so the farming village is born, and with it settled life, stored food and the road to civilisation. The book calls farming nothing less than the basis of human settlement.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     How does the story in “Unit 2 سورية والتاريخ, Lesson سورية مهد الحضارة (Syria, cradle of civilisation)” end?
**Answer:** On Syria's land, the Grade 4 book tells its youngest readers, humans lived for thousands of years. The long road runs from hunting and gathering — taking what nature offered — to settling down, farming the plains and raising cities and centres of civilisation. That is why the book gives Syria its proud title: مهد الحضارة, the cradle of civilisation, a land where settled life itself was cradled.
