# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)     After ages of wandering for food, people on Syria's Euphrates valley plant and reap, and the farming village is born.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)     The part “Unit 2 سورية والتاريخ, Lesson سورية مهد الحضارة (Syria, cradle of civilisation)” is mainly about this: The folk dress of Latakia, Raqqa, al-Hasaka, Aleppo, Homs and Suwayda is pictured region by region, and the games children played in old times and the folk songs are named as what the homeland hands down.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: On Syria's land humans lived for thousands of years and moved from hunting and gathering to settling, farming the plains and building cities and centres of civilisation.
