# Video script — First Cities and Trade in the Fertile Crescent


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

In old times, the Grade 4 book explains, people traded by barter — مقايضة: the oil-maker gave part of his oil and received eggs and wheat in return, each side swapping what it had for what it needed. But swapping is slow and awkward, so as life developed, peoples began to mint money, and a person could sell his product, or even his work, for coins. In that small change — from oil-for-eggs to coin-in-hand — the whole world of trade is born.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Syria.
