# First Cities and Trade in the Fertile Crescent — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In old times people exchanged what they produced for goods, a barter (مقايضة) in which the oil-maker gave part of his oil and received eggs and wheat; as life developed, peoples began to mint money and a person could sell his product or his work for coins. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
