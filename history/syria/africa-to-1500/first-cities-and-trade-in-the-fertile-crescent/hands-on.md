# Hands-on — First Cities and Trade in the Fertile Crescent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — First Cities and Trade in the Fertile Crescent — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In old times people exchanged what they produced for goods, a barter (مقايضة) in which the oil-maker gave part of his oil and received eggs and wheat; as life developed, peoples began to mint money and a person could sell his product or his work for coins. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
