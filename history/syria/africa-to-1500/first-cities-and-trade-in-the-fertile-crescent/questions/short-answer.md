# First Cities and Trade in the Fertile Crescent — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Unit 4, Lesson العمل في التجارة في سورية (Work in trade in Syria)”?
**Answer:** In old times, the Grade 4 book explains, people traded by barter — مقايضة: the oil-maker gave part of his oil and received eggs and wheat in return, each side swapping what it had for what it needed. But swapping is slow and awkward, so as life developed, peoples began to mint money, and a person could sell his product, or even his work, for coins. In that small change — from oil-for-eggs to coin-in-hand — the whole world of trade is born.
