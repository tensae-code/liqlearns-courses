# First Cities and Trade in the Fertile Crescent — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   In old times people exchanged what they produced for goods, a barter (مقايضة) in which the oil-maker gave part of his oil and received eggs and wheat; as life developed, peoples began to mint money and a person could sell his product or his work for coins.
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Unit 4, Lesson العمل في التجارة في سورية (Work in trade in Syria)” is mainly about this: Syria was a passage for the trade routes since ancient times, among them the Silk Road, its merchants carrying goods and learning other peoples' cultures; the Phoenicians on its coast were among the first to ride the sea.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: In old times people exchanged what they produced for goods, a barter (مقايضة) in which the oil-maker gave part of his oil and received eggs and wheat; as life developed, peoples began to mint money and a person could sell his product or his work for coins.
