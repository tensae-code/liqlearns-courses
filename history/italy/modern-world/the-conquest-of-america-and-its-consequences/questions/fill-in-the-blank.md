# The conquest of america and its consequences — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §5 The consequences of the conquest in America" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Disease, encomienda and the mines empty the Indies; Las Casas' Brevissima (in the Documents) accuses Spain before Europe, and the colonial system takes shape.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La conquista del Nuovo Mondo; Personaggi: Montezuma; L'impero coloniale spagnolo; L'impero coloniale portoghese; La politica delle conversioni" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cortés meets Montezuma and an empire falls to steel, horses and smallpox; Spain and Portugal build colonial empires and argue over the souls and rights of the conquered.
