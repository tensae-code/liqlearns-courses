# The conquest of america and its consequences — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §5 The consequences of the conquest in America", disease, encomienda and the mines empty the Indies; Las Casas' Brevissima (in the Documents) accuses Spain before Europe, and the colonial system takes shape (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La conquista del Nuovo Mondo; Personaggi: Montezuma; L'impero coloniale spagnolo; L'impero coloniale portoghese; La politica delle conversioni", Cortés meets Montezuma and an empire falls to steel, horses and smallpox; Spain and Portugal build colonial empires and argue over the souls and rights of the conquered (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
