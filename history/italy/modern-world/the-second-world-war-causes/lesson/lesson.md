# The second world war causes

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The second world war causes" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 12 §5 German expansionism and the responses of the other powers

Austria, Munich, Prague and the Pact of Steel (Document) mark the road; Conquest's Storiografia weighs the Molotov-Ribbentrop pact's consequences — appeasement ends where expansion was always going.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 5 §10 Verso la guerra

Rearmament, the Rhineland, Austria, Munich — the democracies' retreats and Hitler's timetable converge on September 1939.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 8 §1 Le origini e le responsabilità

Gribaudi on soldiers and civilians in total war and Liddell Hart on the blitzkrieg (Fare storia) frame the question — a war willed in Berlin, prepared by a decade of retreats.
