# Lesson plan — The second world war causes

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** The second world war causes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The second world war causes as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 3, Cap. 12 §5 German expansionism and the responses of the other powers** — Austria, Munich, Prague and the Pact of Steel (Document) mark the road; Conquest's Storiografia weighs the Molotov-Ribbentrop pact's consequences — appeasement ends where expansion was always going
2. **§2 Modulo 5 §10 Verso la guerra** — rearmament, the Rhineland, Austria, Munich — the democracies' retreats and Hitler's timetable converge on September 1939
3. **§3 Modulo 8 §1 Le origini e le responsabilità** — Gribaudi on soldiers and civilians in total war and Liddell Hart on the blitzkrieg (Fare storia) frame the question — a war willed in Berlin, prepared by a decade of retreats
