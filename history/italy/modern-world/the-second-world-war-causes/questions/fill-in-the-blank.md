# The second world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 12 §5 German expansionism and the responses of the other powers" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Austria, Munich, Prague and the Pact of Steel (Document) mark the road; Conquest's Storiografia weighs the Molotov-Ribbentrop pact's consequences — appeasement ends where expansion was always going.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 5 §10 Verso la guerra" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rearmament, the Rhineland, Austria, Munich — the democracies' retreats and Hitler's timetable converge on September 1939.
