# The second world war causes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Modulo 8 §1 Le origini e le responsabilità", Gribaudi on soldiers and civilians in total war and Liddell Hart on the blitzkrieg (Fare storia) frame the question — a war willed in Berlin, prepared by a decade of retreats (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 12 §5 German expansionism and the responses of the other powers", Austria, Munich, Prague and the Pact of Steel (Document) mark the road; Conquest's Storiografia weighs the Molotov-Ribbentrop pact's consequences — appeasement ends where expansion was always going (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
