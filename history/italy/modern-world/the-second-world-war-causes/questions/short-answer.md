# The second world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 5 §10 Verso la guerra" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rearmament, the Rhineland, Austria, Munich — the democracies' retreats and Hitler's timetable converge on September 1939.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 8 §1 Le origini e le responsabilità" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Gribaudi on soldiers and civilians in total war and Liddell Hart on the blitzkrieg (Fare storia) frame the question — a war willed in Berlin, prepared by a decade of retreats.
