# Mussolinis dictatorship — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §1 The affirmation of the dictatorship and the repression of dissent", after Matteotti, the exceptional laws dissolve opposition and free press; Gramsci's Document reads fascism's associative crimes as the regime's true constitution, and the Storia culturale pauses on the very word "totalitarianism" (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 6 §§1–2 Lo Stato fascista; Il totalitarismo italiano e i suoi limiti", De Felice and Gentile (Fare storia) argue over fascism's nature — myth and organisation — while the narrative weighs how totalitarian Italy really was against Germany's and Russia's models (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
