# Mussolinis dictatorship — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §1 The affirmation of the dictatorship and the repression of dissent" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Matteotti, the exceptional laws dissolve opposition and free press; Gramsci's Document reads fascism's associative crimes as the regime's true constitution, and the Storia culturale pauses on the very word "totalitarianism"

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6 §§1–2 Lo Stato fascista; Il totalitarismo italiano e i suoi limiti" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** De Felice and Gentile (Fare storia) argue over fascism's nature — myth and organisation — while the narrative weighs how totalitarian Italy really was against Germany's and Russia's models.
