# Mussolinis dictatorship — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §1 The affirmation of the dictatorship and the repression of dissent" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Matteotti, the exceptional laws dissolve opposition and free press; Gramsci's Document reads fascism's associative crimes as the regime's true constitution, and the Storia culturale pauses on the very word "totalitarianism"

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 6 §§1–2 Lo Stato fascista; Il totalitarismo italiano e i suoi limiti" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** De Felice and Gentile (Fare storia) argue over fascism's nature — myth and organisation — while the narrative weighs how totalitarian Italy really was against Germany's and Russia's models.
