# The postwar boom the golden age — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 16 §1 The "golden age" of the world economy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Thirty years of growth, full employment and mass consumption — Packard's Document unmasks the consumer's hypnotists while Bouissou's explains Japan's social conditions for growth — the boom and its discontents in one chapter.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 11 §§1–3 La crescita demografica; Il boom economico; Consumi e Welfare" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Baby boom, washing machines and the welfare state — Van der Wee on the demand imperative, Packard's hidden persuaders and McLuhan's medium (Fare storia pieces) describe affluence and its salesmen in one dossier.
