# The postwar boom the golden age — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 16 §1 The "golden age" of the world economy — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 11 §§1–3 La crescita demografica; Il boom economico; Consumi e Welfare — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) thirty years of growth, full employment and mass consumption — Packard's Document unmasks the consumer's hypnotists while Bouissou's explains Japan's social conditions for growth — the boom and its discontents in one chapter
b) baby boom, washing machines and the welfare state — Van der Wee on the demand imperative, Packard's hidden persuaders and McLuhan's medium (Fare storia pieces) describe affluence and its salesmen in one dossier

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
