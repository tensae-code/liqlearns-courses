# The postwar boom the golden age — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 16 §1 The "golden age" of the world economy", thirty years of growth, full employment and mass consumption — Packard's Document unmasks the consumer's hypnotists while Bouissou's explains Japan's social conditions for growth — the boom and its discontents in one chapter (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 11 §§1–3 La crescita demografica; Il boom economico; Consumi e Welfare", baby boom, washing machines and the welfare state — Van der Wee on the demand imperative, Packard's hidden persuaders and McLuhan's medium (Fare storia pieces) describe affluence and its salesmen in one dossier (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
