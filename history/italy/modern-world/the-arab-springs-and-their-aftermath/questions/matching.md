# The arab springs and their aftermath — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 6, Cap. 22 §3 Hopes and dramas of the Arab world — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 19 §§5–6 Le "primavere arabe"; Le divisioni del mondo islamico — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) squares fill with demands for democracy (the chapter's videomap) and meet repression, civil war and Daesh's territories (the maps of 2015–18) — hope and catastrophe in one decade
b) Tahrir Square (Eventi) raises democracy's banner; the springs divide into transitions, restorations and civil wars, and Islam's internal divisions (Kepel's Fare storia piece on the war for Islam's control) decide their fates

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
