# The arab springs and their aftermath — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 6, Cap. 22 §3 Hopes and dramas of the Arab world", squares fill with demands for democracy (the chapter's videomap) and meet repression, civil war and Daesh's territories (the maps of 2015–18) — hope and catastrophe in one decade (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 19 §§5–6 Le "primavere arabe"; Le divisioni del mondo islamico", Tahrir Square (Eventi) raises democracy's banner; the springs divide into transitions, restorations and civil wars, and Islam's internal divisions (Kepel's Fare storia piece on the war for Islam's control) decide their fates (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
