# The arab springs and their aftermath — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 22 §3 Hopes and dramas of the Arab world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Squares fill with demands for democracy (the chapter's videomap) and meet repression, civil war and Daesh's territories (the maps of 2015–18) — hope and catastrophe in one decade.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 19 §§5–6 Le "primavere arabe"; Le divisioni del mondo islamico" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tahrir Square (Eventi) raises democracy's banner; the springs divide into transitions, restorations and civil wars, and Islam's internal divisions (Kepel's Fare storia piece on the war for Islam's control) decide their fates.
