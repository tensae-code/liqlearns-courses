# Europe in 1700 population and the old regime — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–3 The demographic leap; Agriculture, manufactures and trade; The society of the Ancien Régime", plague and famine retreat, Europe's population climbs past 145 million by 1750, yet society stays frozen in orders — a world growing inside an old shell (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 The society and the economy of the '700 (demography, social mobility and hierarchies, family, feudalism and the peasants, the agricultural revolution, rural industry)", late marriage, enclosures (Fare storia: Moore on the enclosures) and the 'industrious revolution' inside households — the old order changing from below before it falls from above (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
