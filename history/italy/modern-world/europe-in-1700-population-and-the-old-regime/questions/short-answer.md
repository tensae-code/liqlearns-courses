# Europe in 1700 population and the old regime — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–3 The demographic leap; Agriculture, manufactures and trade; The society of the Ancien Régime" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Plague and famine retreat, Europe's population climbs past 145 million by 1750, yet society stays frozen in orders — a world growing inside an old shell.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 The society and the economy of the '700 (demography, social mobility and hierarchies, family, feudalism and the peasants, the agricultural revolution, rural industry)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Late marriage, enclosures (Fare storia: Moore on the enclosures) and the 'industrious revolution' inside households — the old order changing from below before it falls from above.
