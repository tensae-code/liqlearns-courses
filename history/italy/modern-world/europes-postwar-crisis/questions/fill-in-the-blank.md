# Europes postwar crisis — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §1 The legacies and aftershocks of the war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Keynes' Economic Consequences of the Peace (the unit's book study) warns that a vindictive settlement will poison Europe; inflation, borders and broken societies prove him right in advance.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§1–3 Le conseguenze economiche della guerra; I mutamenti nella vita sociale; Stati nazionali e minoranze" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Inflation (the module's keyword), broken finances and new nation-states whose minorities Mazower's Fare storia piece counts — the peace creates as many problems as it settles.
