# Lesson plan — Europes postwar crisis

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Europes postwar crisis
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Europes postwar crisis as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 2, Cap. 6 §1 The legacies and aftershocks of the war** — Keynes' Economic Consequences of the Peace (the unit's book study) warns that a vindictive settlement will poison Europe; inflation, borders and broken societies prove him right in advance
2. **§2 Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§1–3 Le conseguenze economiche della guerra; I mutamenti nella vita sociale; Stati nazionali e minoranze** — inflation (the module's keyword), broken finances and new nation-states whose minorities Mazower's Fare storia piece counts — the peace creates as many problems as it settles

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
