# Nineteen sixty eight — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 16 §4 From the United States to Western Europe: the Sixty-Eight" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Students and workers shake Paris, Berlin, Rome and Berkeley in one season; Friedan's American housewife (Document) and Robert Kennedy's critique of GDP give the revolt its domestic and economic manifestos.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 11 §7 Critica del consumismo e contestazione giovanile" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kurlansky's "1968: the epicentre of a turning" and the occupied faculties' own document (Fare storia) — the affluent society's children indict it in its own universities.
