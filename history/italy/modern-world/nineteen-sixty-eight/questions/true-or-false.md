# Nineteen sixty eight — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 16 §4 From the United States to Western Europe: the Sixty-Eight", students and workers shake Paris, Berlin, Rome and Berkeley in one season; Friedan's American housewife (Document) and Robert Kennedy's critique of GDP give the revolt its domestic and economic manifestos (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 11 §7 Critica del consumismo e contestazione giovanile", Kurlansky's "1968: the epicentre of a turning" and the occupied faculties' own document (Fare storia) — the affluent society's children indict it in its own universities (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
