# Nineteen sixty eight

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Nineteen sixty eight" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 16 §4 From the United States to Western Europe: the Sixty-Eight

Students and workers shake Paris, Berlin, Rome and Berkeley in one season; Friedan's American housewife (Document) and Robert Kennedy's critique of GDP give the revolt its domestic and economic manifestos.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 11 §7 Critica del consumismo e contestazione giovanile

Kurlansky's "1968: the epicentre of a turning" and the occupied faculties' own document (Fare storia) — the affluent society's children indict it in its own universities.
