# Elizabethan england — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §2 The England of Elizabeth", Elizabeth holds a divided kingdom together for 45 years, defeats the Armada (1588) and presides over Shakespeare's stage and the buccaneer sea-dogs (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Elisabetta I regina d'Inghilterra; La sconfitta dell'Invincibile Armata", Elizabeth (Personaggi) holds England Protestant and beats the Armada; female monarchy itself is put on trial by the age (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
