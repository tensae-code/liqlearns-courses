# Elizabethan england — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §2 The England of Elizabeth" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Elizabeth holds a divided kingdom together for 45 years, defeats the Armada (1588) and presides over Shakespeare's stage and the buccaneer sea-dogs.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Elisabetta I regina d'Inghilterra; La sconfitta dell'Invincibile Armata" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Elizabeth (Personaggi) holds England Protestant and beats the Armada; female monarchy itself is put on trial by the age.
