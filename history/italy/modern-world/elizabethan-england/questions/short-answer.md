# Elizabethan england — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §2 The England of Elizabeth" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Elizabeth holds a divided kingdom together for 45 years, defeats the Armada (1588) and presides over Shakespeare's stage and the buccaneer sea-dogs.

**2.** Tell the story of "Elisabetta I regina d'Inghilterra; La sconfitta dell'Invincibile Armata" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Elizabeth (Personaggi) holds England Protestant and beats the Armada; female monarchy itself is put on trial by the age.
