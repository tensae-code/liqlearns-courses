# Elizabethan england — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 5, Cap. 17 §2 The England of Elizabeth — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Elisabetta I regina d'Inghilterra; La sconfitta dell'Invincibile Armata — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Elizabeth holds a divided kingdom together for 45 years, defeats the Armada (1588) and presides over Shakespeare's stage and the buccaneer sea-dogs
b) Elizabeth (Personaggi) holds England Protestant and beats the Armada; female monarchy itself is put on trial by the age

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
