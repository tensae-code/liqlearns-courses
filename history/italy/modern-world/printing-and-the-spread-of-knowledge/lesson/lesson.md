# Printing and the spread of knowledge

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Printing and the spread of knowledge" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 10 §§3–4 The progress of knowledge; The progress of technique

Printing spreads across Europe in decades, cheap books carry humanism and later the Reformation, while gunpowder weapons draw Ariosto's curse.

## Sub lesson 2 — Una rivoluzione per lo studio: la stampa; L'invenzione della stampa (Eventi)

Gutenberg's press multiplies books a hundredfold and breaks the Church's and the universities' monopoly on texts.
