# Printing and the spread of knowledge — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 10 §§3–4 The progress of knowledge; The progress of technique — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Una rivoluzione per lo studio: la stampa; L'invenzione della stampa (Eventi) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) printing spreads across Europe in decades, cheap books carry humanism and later the Reformation, while gunpowder weapons draw Ariosto's curse
b) Gutenberg's press multiplies books a hundredfold and breaks the Church's and the universities' monopoly on texts

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
