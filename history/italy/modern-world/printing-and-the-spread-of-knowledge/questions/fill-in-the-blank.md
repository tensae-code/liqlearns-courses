# Printing and the spread of knowledge — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §§3–4 The progress of knowledge; The progress of technique" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Printing spreads across Europe in decades, cheap books carry humanism and later the Reformation, while gunpowder weapons draw Ariosto's curse.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Una rivoluzione per lo studio: la stampa; L'invenzione della stampa (Eventi)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gutenberg's press multiplies books a hundredfold and breaks the Church's and the universities' monopoly on texts.
