# Printing and the spread of knowledge — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §§3–4 The progress of knowledge; The progress of technique", printing spreads across Europe in decades, cheap books carry humanism and later the Reformation, while gunpowder weapons draw Ariosto's curse (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Una rivoluzione per lo studio: la stampa; L'invenzione della stampa (Eventi)", Gutenberg's press multiplies books a hundredfold and breaks the Church's and the universities' monopoly on texts (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
