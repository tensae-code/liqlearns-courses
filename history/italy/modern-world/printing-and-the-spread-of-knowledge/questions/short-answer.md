# Printing and the spread of knowledge — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §§3–4 The progress of knowledge; The progress of technique" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Printing spreads across Europe in decades, cheap books carry humanism and later the Reformation, while gunpowder weapons draw Ariosto's curse.

**2.** Tell the story of "Una rivoluzione per lo studio: la stampa; L'invenzione della stampa (Eventi)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gutenberg's press multiplies books a hundredfold and breaks the Church's and the universities' monopoly on texts.
