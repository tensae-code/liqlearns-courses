# Calvin anabaptists and the church of england — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 15 §4 The birth of new Protestant churches" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Calvin builds a disciplined Geneva, Anabaptists pay for Münster with their lives, and Henry VIII's Act of Supremacy makes a national church for a king's divorce.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ginevra e Calvino; L'Europa riformata; Personaggi: Enrico VIII" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Calvin's Geneva disciplines a city into a model church, Anabaptists radicalise the message, and Henry VIII nationalises it — three different Reformation futures.
