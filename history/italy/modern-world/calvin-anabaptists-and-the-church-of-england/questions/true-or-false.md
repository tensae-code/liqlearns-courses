# Calvin anabaptists and the church of england — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 15 §4 The birth of new Protestant churches", Calvin builds a disciplined Geneva, Anabaptists pay for Münster with their lives, and Henry VIII's Act of Supremacy makes a national church for a king's divorce (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Ginevra e Calvino; L'Europa riformata; Personaggi: Enrico VIII", Calvin's Geneva disciplines a city into a model church, Anabaptists radicalise the message, and Henry VIII nationalises it — three different Reformation futures (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
