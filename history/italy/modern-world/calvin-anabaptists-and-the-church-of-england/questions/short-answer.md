# Calvin anabaptists and the church of england — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 15 §4 The birth of new Protestant churches" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Calvin builds a disciplined Geneva, Anabaptists pay for Münster with their lives, and Henry VIII's Act of Supremacy makes a national church for a king's divorce.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ginevra e Calvino; L'Europa riformata; Personaggi: Enrico VIII" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Calvin's Geneva disciplines a city into a model church, Anabaptists radicalise the message, and Henry VIII nationalises it — three different Reformation futures.
