# Militarism in japan the army above the state — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 3, Cap. 12 §2 Japanese imperialism and the civil war in China — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 7 §§5–7 La guerra civile in Cina; L'imperialismo giapponese; L'Oriente in guerra — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Japan's army drives expansion into Manchuria and China (the Long March on the chapter's maps); Pu Yi, the puppet emperor "inside and beyond the borders" (Storie di frontiera), personifies the fiction of Manchukuo
b) China's warlords, Nationalists and Communists fight their civil war inside Japan's expanding shadow; Beasley's Fare storia piece traces Japanese expansionism to the Pacific war it becomes

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
