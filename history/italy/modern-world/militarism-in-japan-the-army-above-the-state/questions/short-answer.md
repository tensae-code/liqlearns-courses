# Militarism in japan the army above the state — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 12 §2 Japanese imperialism and the civil war in China" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Japan's army drives expansion into Manchuria and China (the Long March on the chapter's maps); Pu Yi, the puppet emperor "inside and beyond the borders" (Storie di frontiera), personifies the fiction of Manchukuo.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 7 §§5–7 La guerra civile in Cina; L'imperialismo giapponese; L'Oriente in guerra" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** China's warlords, Nationalists and Communists fight their civil war inside Japan's expanding shadow; Beasley's Fare storia piece traces Japanese expansionism to the Pacific war it becomes.
