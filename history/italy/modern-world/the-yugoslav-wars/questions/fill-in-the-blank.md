# The yugoslav wars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §2 The wars in the former Yugoslavia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Yugoslavia dissolves into Europe's first wars since 1945 — the "war on civilians" in Bosnia (La storia allo specchio), Dayton's map, Srebrenica (Pirjevec's Document on the UN's responsibilities) and Kosovo's roots (Malcolm).

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 14 §5 La crisi jugoslava" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ethnic cleansing (the module's keyword) returns to Europe; Pirjevec's Fare storia piece on Srebrenica and Romano's on the Europe of nationalisms frame the federation's bloody dissolution.
