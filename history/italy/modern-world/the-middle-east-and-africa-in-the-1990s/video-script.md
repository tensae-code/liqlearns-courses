This lesson tells one story: The middle east and africa in the 1990s.

Oslo's accords (the chapter's document) raise a peace that disappoints; the Arab-Israeli conflict's figures 1947–2023 (La storia in cifre) and Africa's wars fill the unipolar decade's other front.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
