# German rearmament and the collapse of versailles — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 12 §3 The rearmament of Germany and the new European equilibria" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Conscription, the Rhineland and open rearmament dismantle Versailles piece by piece while the democracies answer with words — the equilibria of the twenties dissolve.
