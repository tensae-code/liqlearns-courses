# Britain and france after the great war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §3 The postwar period in Great Britain and France", two victors discover victory's price — Britain's empire strains (Guazzaloca's Storiografia) and France rebuilds in fear of Germany — the winners' peace is already a crisis (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §6 Il dopoguerra dei vincitori", the victors' own postwar — debts, strikes and empires to police on credit — winning proves almost as destabilising as losing (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
