# Britain and france after the great war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §3 The postwar period in Great Britain and France" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Two victors discover victory's price — Britain's empire strains (Guazzaloca's Storiografia) and France rebuilds in fear of Germany — the winners' peace is already a crisis.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §6 Il dopoguerra dei vincitori" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The victors' own postwar — debts, strikes and empires to police on credit — winning proves almost as destabilising as losing.
