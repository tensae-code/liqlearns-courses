# Britain and france after the great war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §3 The postwar period in Great Britain and France" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Two victors discover victory's price — Britain's empire strains (Guazzaloca's Storiografia) and France rebuilds in fear of Germany — the winners' peace is already a crisis.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §6 Il dopoguerra dei vincitori" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The victors' own postwar — debts, strikes and empires to police on credit — winning proves almost as destabilising as losing.
