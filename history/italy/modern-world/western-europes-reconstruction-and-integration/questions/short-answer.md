# Western europes reconstruction and integration — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §3 Reconstruction and integration in Western Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Marshall money rebuilds the West while Schuman's declaration (Document) pools coal and steel so war between France and Germany becomes materially impossible — integration begins as a peace device.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 9 §3 Ricostruzione e riforme nell'Europa occidentale" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Marshall aid, welfare states and the first integration steps rebuild the West as a system; Del Pero's Fare storia piece traces the confrontation's premises inside the recovery.
