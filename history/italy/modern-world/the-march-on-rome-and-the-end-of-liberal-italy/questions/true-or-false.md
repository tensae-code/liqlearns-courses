# The march on rome and the end of liberal italy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§6–7 Fascism in power; From the liberal State to the fascist State", the March on Rome (image study: Mussolini and the quadrumvirs) is a bluff the king ratifies; Matteotti's Document denounces the electoral fraud, Gramsci's the socialists' paralysis — and Mussolini's own speech claims responsibility for it all (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 3 §§5–7 Verso il regime; La dittatura a viso aperto; Il contagio autoritario", the "leggi fascistissime" (Fare storia Document) dismantle the liberal state from inside its own laws, and the authoritarian contagion spreads across Europe — the Anteprima insists it was no fatal outcome: opponents' errors made it (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
