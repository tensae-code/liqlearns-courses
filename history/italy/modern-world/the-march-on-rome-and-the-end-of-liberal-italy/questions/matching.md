# The march on rome and the end of liberal italy — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§6–7 Fascism in power; From the liberal State to the fascist State — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 3 §§5–7 Verso il regime; La dittatura a viso aperto; Il contagio autoritario — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the March on Rome (image study: Mussolini and the quadrumvirs) is a bluff the king ratifies; Matteotti's Document denounces the electoral fraud, Gramsci's the socialists' paralysis — and Mussolini's own speech claims responsibility for it all
b) the "leggi fascistissime" (Fare storia Document) dismantle the liberal state from inside its own laws, and the authoritarian contagion spreads across Europe — the Anteprima insists it was no fatal outcome: opponents' errors made it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
