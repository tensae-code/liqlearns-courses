# The march on rome and the end of liberal italy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§6–7 Fascism in power; From the liberal State to the fascist State" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The March on Rome (image study: Mussolini and the quadrumvirs) is a bluff the king ratifies; Matteotti's Document denounces the electoral fraud, Gramsci's the socialists' paralysis — and Mussolini's own speech claims responsibility for it all.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 3 §§5–7 Verso il regime; La dittatura a viso aperto; Il contagio autoritario" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The "leggi fascistissime" (Fare storia Document) dismantle the liberal state from inside its own laws, and the authoritarian contagion spreads across Europe — the Anteprima insists it was no fatal outcome: opponents' errors made it.
