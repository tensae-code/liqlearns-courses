# The industrial revolution textiles steam and rail — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 3 §2 The technological turn" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Spinning machines multiply threads, Watt's steam engine (image study) multiplies power — invention becomes a system, not an accident.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§2–3 The transport revolution; Industrialisation in Europe and the United States" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Steamships, the Thames tunnel and railway mania carry the British model to the continent and America — industry becomes a race no state can sit out.
