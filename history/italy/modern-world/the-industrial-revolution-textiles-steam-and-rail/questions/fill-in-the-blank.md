# The industrial revolution textiles steam and rail — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 3 §2 The technological turn" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Spinning machines multiply threads, Watt's steam engine (image study) multiplies power — invention becomes a system, not an accident.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§2–3 The transport revolution; Industrialisation in Europe and the United States" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Steamships, the Thames tunnel and railway mania carry the British model to the continent and America — industry becomes a race no state can sit out.
