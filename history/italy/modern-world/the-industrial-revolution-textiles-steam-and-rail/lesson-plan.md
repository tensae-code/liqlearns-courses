# Lesson plan — The industrial revolution textiles steam and rail

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** The industrial revolution textiles steam and rail
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The 1700s and 1800s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 2): Enlightenment and enlightened reform, industry, the American and French revolutions, Napoleon, Restoration, 1848, the Risorgimento and liberal Italy to 1900 (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The 1700s and 1800s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 2): Enlightenment and enlightened reform, industry, the American and French revolutions, Napoleon, Restoration, 1848, the Risorgimento and liberal Italy to 1900 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 2, Cap. 3 §2 The technological turn** — spinning machines multiply threads, Watt's steam engine (image study) multiplies power — invention becomes a system, not an accident
2. **§2 Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§2–3 The transport revolution; Industrialisation in Europe and the United States** — steamships, the Thames tunnel and railway mania carry the British model to the continent and America — industry becomes a race no state can sit out
