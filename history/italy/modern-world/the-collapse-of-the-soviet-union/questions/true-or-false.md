# The collapse of the soviet union — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 18 §7 The implosion of the USSR", Gorbachev's "common European home" (Document) meets republics walking out; Darnton's Document stands at the fallen Berlin Wall — the USSR dissolves without the war everyone feared (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 14 §§1–4 Un impero in crisi; Il crollo del Muro di Berlino e la riunificazione tedesca; La fine delle "democrazie popolari"; La dissoluzione dell'Urss", 9 November 1989 (Eventi) opens the Wall; Bongiovanni, Darnton at the Wall and Maier on reunification (Fare storia pieces) argue the causes — an empire ends by implosion, not invasion (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
