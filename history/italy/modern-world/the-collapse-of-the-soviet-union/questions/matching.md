# The collapse of the soviet union — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 18 §7 The implosion of the USSR — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 14 §§1–4 Un impero in crisi; Il crollo del Muro di Berlino e la riunificazione tedesca; La fine delle "democrazie popolari"; La dissoluzione dell'Urss — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Gorbachev's "common European home" (Document) meets republics walking out; Darnton's Document stands at the fallen Berlin Wall — the USSR dissolves without the war everyone feared
b) 9 November 1989 (Eventi) opens the Wall; Bongiovanni, Darnton at the Wall and Maier on reunification (Fare storia pieces) argue the causes — an empire ends by implosion, not invasion

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
