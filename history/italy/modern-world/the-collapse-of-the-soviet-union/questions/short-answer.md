# The collapse of the soviet union — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 18 §7 The implosion of the USSR" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Gorbachev's "common European home" (Document) meets republics walking out; Darnton's Document stands at the fallen Berlin Wall — the USSR dissolves without the war everyone feared.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 14 §§1–4 Un impero in crisi; Il crollo del Muro di Berlino e la riunificazione tedesca; La fine delle "democrazie popolari"; La dissoluzione dell'Urss" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 9 November 1989 (Eventi) opens the Wall; Bongiovanni, Darnton at the Wall and Maier on reunification (Fare storia pieces) argue the causes — an empire ends by implosion, not invasion.
