# The memory of the great war

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The memory of the great war" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §13 Il mito e la memoria

Monuments, myths and Kubrick's Paths of Glory (the module's Cinema e Storia) — the war goes on being fought in memory, and every regime of the century will claim its dead.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of The memory of the great war as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
