# September 11 and the crisis of american hegemony — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 22 §1 The new millennium and the crisis of US hegemony" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 11 September 2001 (the unit's video) brings war to New York; Bush's "axis of evil" speech (Document) answers with two wars, and the 2008 subprime crash (Stiglitz's Storiografia) breaks the hegemon's economic myth — Politkovskaya's Russian Diary watches from the other side.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 17 §§4–5 La crescita del fondamentalismo; L'attacco all'Occidente" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
