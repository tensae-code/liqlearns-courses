# Italy after unification the historical right — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§5–6 The first years of united Italy: the political, economic and social situation; The annexation of Veneto and Rome as capital" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Historical Right balances the budget and fights brigantaggio in the South (Storici a confronto: was it a civil war?); Venice (1866) and Rome (1870) complete the map, and the Southern question opens.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 14 The first years of united Italy (demography, economy and society; Governing united Italy; The revolts against unity and brigantaggio; The economy and fiscal policy; The conquest of Veneto and the taking of Rome)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A poor, divided kingdom taxes flour and fights brigands (Fare storia: Lupo on southern brigandage) until Porta Pia (Eventi, 1870) makes Rome the capital.
