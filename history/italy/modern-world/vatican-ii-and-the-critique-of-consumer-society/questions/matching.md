# Vatican ii and the critique of consumer society — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The demand for renewal in the Church; The critique of consumer society: the contestation — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 11 §9 La Chiesa e il Concilio — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Vatican II opens the Church's windows while youth counterculture (Storia culturale), Angela Davis' autobiography (book study) and the Black Panthers' programme (Document) contest the affluent society from inside
b) Vatican II and secularisation (Menozzi), Don Milani's duty not to obey, Populorum Progressio and Boff's liberation theology (Fare storia pieces) — the Church argues with modernity from inside it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
