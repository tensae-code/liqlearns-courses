# Vatican ii and the critique of consumer society — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The demand for renewal in the Church; The critique of consumer society: the contestation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Vatican II opens the Church's windows while youth counterculture (Storia culturale), Angela Davis' autobiography (book study) and the Black Panthers' programme (Document) contest the affluent society from inside.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 11 §9 La Chiesa e il Concilio" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Vatican II and secularisation (Menozzi), Don Milani's duty not to obey, Populorum Progressio and Boff's liberation theology (Fare storia pieces) — the Church argues with modernity from inside it.
