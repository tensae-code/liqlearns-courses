# The reformation luther and protestantism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 15 §§1–2 The crisis of the Church; Martin Luther and the Protestant Reformation", indulgence-selling sparks Luther's 95 theses (1517, in the Documents); justification by faith alone and the universal priesthood split Western Christianity (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Lutero contro la corruzione della Chiesa (with DOC: Luther's theses and justification by faith)", Luther's protest against indulgences becomes, in his own documents, a doctrine — man is saved by faith alone — that splits the Church (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
