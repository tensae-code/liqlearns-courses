# The reformation luther and protestantism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 15 §§1–2 The crisis of the Church; Martin Luther and the Protestant Reformation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Indulgence-selling sparks Luther's 95 theses (1517, in the Documents); justification by faith alone and the universal priesthood split Western Christianity.

**2.** Tell the story of "Lutero contro la corruzione della Chiesa (with DOC: Luther's theses and justification by faith)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Luther's protest against indulgences becomes, in his own documents, a doctrine — man is saved by faith alone — that splits the Church.
