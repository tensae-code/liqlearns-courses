# The reformation luther and protestantism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 15 §§1–2 The crisis of the Church; Martin Luther and the Protestant Reformation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Indulgence-selling sparks Luther's 95 theses (1517, in the Documents); justification by faith alone and the universal priesthood split Western Christianity.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Lutero contro la corruzione della Chiesa (with DOC: Luther's theses and justification by faith)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Luther's protest against indulgences becomes, in his own documents, a doctrine — man is saved by faith alone — that splits the Church.
