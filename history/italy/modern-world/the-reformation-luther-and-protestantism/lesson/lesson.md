## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 5, Cap. 15 §§1–2 The crisis of the Church; Martin Luther and the Protestant Reformation

Indulgence-selling sparks Luther's 95 theses (1517, in the Documents); justification by faith alone and the universal priesthood split Western Christianity.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lutero contro la corruzione della Chiesa (with DOC: Luther's theses and justification by faith)

Luther's protest against indulgences becomes, in his own documents, a doctrine — man is saved by faith alone — that splits the Church.
