# Enlightened despotism and the reforming monarchs — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§4–5 The season of enlightened despotism; The Enlightenment and the reforms in Italy — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§8–9 Enlightened absolutism; Reformism in Italy — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Frederick II, Maria Theresa, Joseph II and Catherine rule by reason from above; in Italy Beccaria's On Crimes and Punishments (1764) rewrites Europe's idea of justice
b) monarchs borrow the philosophes to strengthen the state; Beccaria's utility of punishments (Fare storia) shows Italy exporting reform ideas to all Europe

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
