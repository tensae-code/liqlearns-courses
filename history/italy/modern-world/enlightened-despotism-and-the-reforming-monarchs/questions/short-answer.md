# Enlightened despotism and the reforming monarchs — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§4–5 The season of enlightened despotism; The Enlightenment and the reforms in Italy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Frederick II, Maria Theresa, Joseph II and Catherine rule by reason from above; in Italy Beccaria's On Crimes and Punishments (1764) rewrites Europe's idea of justice.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§8–9 Enlightened absolutism; Reformism in Italy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Monarchs borrow the philosophes to strengthen the state; Beccaria's utility of punishments (Fare storia) shows Italy exporting reform ideas to all Europe.
