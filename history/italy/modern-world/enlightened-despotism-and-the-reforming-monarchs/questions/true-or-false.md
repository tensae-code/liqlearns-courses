# Enlightened despotism and the reforming monarchs — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§4–5 The season of enlightened despotism; The Enlightenment and the reforms in Italy", Frederick II, Maria Theresa, Joseph II and Catherine rule by reason from above; in Italy Beccaria's On Crimes and Punishments (1764) rewrites Europe's idea of justice (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§8–9 Enlightened absolutism; Reformism in Italy", monarchs borrow the philosophes to strengthen the state; Beccaria's utility of punishments (Fare storia) shows Italy exporting reform ideas to all Europe (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
