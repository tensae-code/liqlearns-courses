# Enlightened despotism and the reforming monarchs

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§4–5 The season of enlightened despotism; The Enlightenment and the reforms in Italy

Frederick II, Maria Theresa, Joseph II and Catherine rule by reason from above; in Italy Beccaria's On Crimes and Punishments (1764) rewrites Europe's idea of justice.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§8–9 Enlightened absolutism; Reformism in Italy

Monarchs borrow the philosophes to strengthen the state; Beccaria's utility of punishments (Fare storia) shows Italy exporting reform ideas to all Europe.
