# The congress of vienna and the restoration — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–2 Europe after the storm: back to order?; Restoration and reaction: an impossible project", Talleyrand and Metternich redraw Europe at Vienna (1815) on legitimacy and balance, but the ideas of 1789 cannot be un-invented (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 4, Cap. 11 §§1–3 The Restoration and the new European order; The return to order; Aristocracy and bourgeoisie in restored Europe", Metternich's political testament and de Maistre's throne-and-altar doctrine (Fare storia) state the Restoration's case in its own words (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
