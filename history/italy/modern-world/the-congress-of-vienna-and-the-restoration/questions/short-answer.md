# The congress of vienna and the restoration — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–2 Europe after the storm: back to order?; Restoration and reaction: an impossible project" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Talleyrand and Metternich redraw Europe at Vienna (1815) on legitimacy and balance, but the ideas of 1789 cannot be un-invented.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 11 §§1–3 The Restoration and the new European order; The return to order; Aristocracy and bourgeoisie in restored Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Metternich's political testament and de Maistre's throne-and-altar doctrine (Fare storia) state the Restoration's case in its own words.
