# The congress of vienna and the restoration

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The congress of vienna and the restoration" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–2 Europe after the storm: back to order?; Restoration and reaction: an impossible project

Talleyrand and Metternich redraw Europe at Vienna (1815) on legitimacy and balance, but the ideas of 1789 cannot be un-invented.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 4, Cap. 11 §§1–3 The Restoration and the new European order; The return to order; Aristocracy and bourgeoisie in restored Europe

Metternich's political testament and de Maistre's throne-and-altar doctrine (Fare storia) state the Restoration's case in its own words.
