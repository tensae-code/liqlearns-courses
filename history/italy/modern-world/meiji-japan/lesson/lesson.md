# Meiji japan

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Meiji japan" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 13 §3 Japan at a crucial turning point

The Meiji restoration ends the sakoku, imports Western institutions and remakes Japan in a generation — tradition and modernity in one frame (image study).

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 6, Cap. 18 §4 The Japanese road to modernity

Reischauer's 'modernisation from above' and Morishima's Confucian capitalism (Fare storia) debate how Japan industrialised without colonisation.
