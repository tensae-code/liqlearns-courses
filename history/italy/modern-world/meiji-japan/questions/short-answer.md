# Meiji japan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 13 §3 Japan at a crucial turning point" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Meiji restoration ends the sakoku, imports Western institutions and remakes Japan in a generation — tradition and modernity in one frame (image study).

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 18 §4 The Japanese road to modernity" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Reischauer's 'modernisation from above' and Morishima's Confucian capitalism (Fare storia) debate how Japan industrialised without colonisation.
