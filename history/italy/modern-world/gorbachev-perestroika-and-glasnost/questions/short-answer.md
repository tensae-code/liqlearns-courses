# Gorbachev perestroika and glasnost — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 18 §5 The perestroika of Mikhail Gorbachev" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Glasnost's posters (image study) promise open speech and perestroika a rebuilt socialism; the reform meant to save the USSR becomes the solvent of its bloc.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 12 §3 L'Unione Sovietica: da Breznev a Gorbačëv" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Stagnation, Afghanistan and dissent in the East (Focus) hollow the bloc; Gorbachev and Reagan (the joint Personaggi study) become the pair who end it.
