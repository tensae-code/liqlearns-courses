## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 6, Cap. 20 §3 The France of Louis XIV

The Sun King gathers all power at Versailles, revokes toleration and makes France Europe's model and menace — historians debate absolutism's origins (Storiografia).

## Sub lesson 2 — Richelieu e Mazzarino; La Francia delle monarchie assolute; Il declino della Spagna; Le Province Unite

Two cardinals build the absolute monarchy Richelieu theorised, Spain declines, and the Dutch republic shows a state can grow rich without a king's glory.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§1–2 Absolutism in France; The limits of French hegemony

Louis XIV's court state (Fare storia: Elias on the king's awakening, Burke on the king's image) reaches its ceiling as coalitions form against it.
