# Louis xiv and french absolutism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Richelieu e Mazzarino; La Francia delle monarchie assolute; Il declino della Spagna; Le Province Unite" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two cardinals build the absolute monarchy Richelieu theorised, Spain declines, and the Dutch republic shows a state can grow rich without a king's glory.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§1–2 Absolutism in France; The limits of French hegemony" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Louis XIV's court state (Fare storia: Elias on the king's awakening, Burke on the king's image) reaches its ceiling as coalitions form against it.
