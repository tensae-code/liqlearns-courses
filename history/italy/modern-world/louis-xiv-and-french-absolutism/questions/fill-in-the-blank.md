# Louis xiv and french absolutism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 20 §3 The France of Louis XIV" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Sun King gathers all power at Versailles, revokes toleration and makes France Europe's model and menace — historians debate absolutism's origins (Storiografia).

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Richelieu e Mazzarino; La Francia delle monarchie assolute; Il declino della Spagna; Le Province Unite" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two cardinals build the absolute monarchy Richelieu theorised, Spain declines, and the Dutch republic shows a state can grow rich without a king's glory.
