# The american civil war union against slavery — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 13 §1 The United States before and after the Civil War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Slavery's expansion west splits the Union; Lincoln's Gettysburg Address (Document) redefines the republic, and the Trail of Tears pages (Storie di frontiera) remember the frontier's other victims.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 18 §§1–3 The United States at mid-'800; The American Civil War; The formation of a world power" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gettysburg (Eventi); Douglass' slave narrative and Moore's analysis (Fare storia) argue the war's causes while the Union emerges a continental power.
