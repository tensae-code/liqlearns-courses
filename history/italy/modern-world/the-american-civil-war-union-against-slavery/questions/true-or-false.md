Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 13 §1 The United States before and after the Civil War", slavery's expansion west splits the Union; Lincoln's Gettysburg Address (Document) redefines the republic, and the Trail of Tears pages (Storie di frontiera) remember the frontier's other victims (Sub lesson 1)
