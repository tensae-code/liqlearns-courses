# The american civil war union against slavery — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 13 §1 The United States before and after the Civil War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Slavery's expansion west splits the Union; Lincoln's Gettysburg Address (Document) redefines the republic, and the Trail of Tears pages (Storie di frontiera) remember the frontier's other victims.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 18 §§1–3 The United States at mid-'800; The American Civil War; The formation of a world power" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gettysburg (Eventi); Douglass' slave narrative and Moore's analysis (Fare storia) argue the war's causes while the Union emerges a continental power.
