# The new feminism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 11 §8 Il nuovo femminismo" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Mitchell on American feminism, Grasso on Italy's movements and Ribero on the self-consciousness groups (Fare storia pieces) — the personal becomes political, in documents written by the movement itself.
