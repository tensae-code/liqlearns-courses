# Italy 1946 48 republic and the cold war vote — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 13 §§1–3 L'Italia nel 1945; La Repubblica e la Costituente; La Costituzione e il trattato di pace" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Republic's Constitution is itself a Fare storia Document; Colarizi on the mass parties and Zamagni on the economic choices frame a country founded by referendum, assembly and compromise — the foibe and the Cassa per il Mezzogiorno are the module's Focus shadows.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 13 §§4–5 Il tempo delle scelte; De Gasperi e il centrismo" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** 18 April 1948 (Eventi) is the founding election — Galli and Sartori (Fare storia) debate "imperfect bipartism or polarised pluralism"; De Gasperi (Personaggi) governs the choice for a decade.
