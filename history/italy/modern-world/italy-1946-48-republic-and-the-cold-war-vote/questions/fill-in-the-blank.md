# Italy 1946 48 republic and the cold war vote — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 19 §§1–2 A difficult postwar; The turning point of 1948 and the years of centrism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Divided Gorizia (Storie di frontiera) mirrors a country choosing Republic and Constituent Assembly amid hunger; De Gasperi ratifies the Atlantic Pact (Document), Togliatti appeals for concord, Spinelli dreams Europe from Ventotene.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 13 §§1–3 L'Italia nel 1945; La Repubblica e la Costituente; La Costituzione e il trattato di pace" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Republic's Constitution is itself a Fare storia Document; Colarizi on the mass parties and Zamagni on the economic choices frame a country founded by referendum, assembly and compromise — the foibe and the Cassa per il Mezzogiorno are the module's Focus shadows.
