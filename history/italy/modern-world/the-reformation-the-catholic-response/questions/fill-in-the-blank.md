# The reformation the catholic response — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §1 The Council of Trent" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After eighteen years of sessions (1545–63) Trent fixes Catholic doctrine and discipline — seminaries, catechism, no compromise with Protestant teaching.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La reazione della Chiesa; Il concilio di Trento (DOC); I nuovi ordini religiosi; Ignazio di Loyola (DOC)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trent defines the Catholic answer, Loyola's Exercises train its soldiers, and new orders carry the reconquest of souls.
