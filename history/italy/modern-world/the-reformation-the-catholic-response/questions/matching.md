# The reformation the catholic response — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 5, Cap. 16 §1 The Council of Trent — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** La reazione della Chiesa; Il concilio di Trento (DOC); I nuovi ordini religiosi; Ignazio di Loyola (DOC) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) after eighteen years of sessions (1545–63) Trent fixes Catholic doctrine and discipline — seminaries, catechism, no compromise with Protestant teaching
b) Trent defines the Catholic answer, Loyola's Exercises train its soldiers, and new orders carry the reconquest of souls

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
