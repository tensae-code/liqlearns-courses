# The reformation the catholic response — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §1 The Council of Trent" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After eighteen years of sessions (1545–63) Trent fixes Catholic doctrine and discipline — seminaries, catechism, no compromise with Protestant teaching.

**2.** Tell the story of "La reazione della Chiesa; Il concilio di Trento (DOC); I nuovi ordini religiosi; Ignazio di Loyola (DOC)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trent defines the Catholic answer, Loyola's Exercises train its soldiers, and new orders carry the reconquest of souls.
