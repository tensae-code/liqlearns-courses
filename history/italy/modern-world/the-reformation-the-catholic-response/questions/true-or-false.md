# The reformation the catholic response — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §1 The Council of Trent", after eighteen years of sessions (1545–63) Trent fixes Catholic doctrine and discipline — seminaries, catechism, no compromise with Protestant teaching (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La reazione della Chiesa; Il concilio di Trento (DOC); I nuovi ordini religiosi; Ignazio di Loyola (DOC)", Trent defines the Catholic answer, Loyola's Exercises train its soldiers, and new orders carry the reconquest of souls (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
