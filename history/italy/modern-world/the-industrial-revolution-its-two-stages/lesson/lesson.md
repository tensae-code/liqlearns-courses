# The industrial revolution its two stages

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The industrial revolution its two stages" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§1–2 The 'Great Depression', between crisis and growth; The transformations of industry and finance

Prices fall for two decades yet steel, chemicals and electricity power a second industrial revolution; trusts answer crisis with monopoly (Document: the Sherman Antitrust Act).

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 5, Cap. 16 §§1–3 Crisis and protectionism; The second industrial revolution; Mass consumption and productive rationalisation

Steel, electricity, Taylor's scientific management and Ford's line (Fare storia documents) remake production — Mokyr and Landes argue over its engines.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–2 An age of growth and transformations; An age of progress, but not only

Steel, electricity, the Otto engine and the assembly line power a second industrial revolution while Pasteur and Koch's Franco-Prussian rivalry (Storia culturale) shows science itself becoming a national contest — growth with shadows already inside it.
