# Lesson plan — The industrial revolution its two stages

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** The industrial revolution its two stages
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The 1700s and 1800s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 2): Enlightenment and enlightened reform, industry, the American and French revolutions, Napoleon, Restoration, 1848, the Risorgimento and liberal Italy to 1900 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The industrial revolution its two stages as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The 1700s and 1800s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 2): Enlightenment and enlightened reform, industry, the American and French revolutions, Napoleon, Restoration, 1848, the Risorgimento and liberal Italy to 1900 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§1–2 The 'Great Depression', between crisis and growth; The transformations of industry and finance** — prices fall for two decades yet steel, chemicals and electricity power a second industrial revolution; trusts answer crisis with monopoly (Document: the Sherman Antitrust Act)
2. **§2 Modulo 5, Cap. 16 §§1–3 Crisis and protectionism; The second industrial revolution; Mass consumption and productive rationalisation** — steel, electricity, Taylor's scientific management and Ford's line (Fare storia documents) remake production — Mokyr and Landes argue over its engines
3. **§3 Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–2 An age of growth and transformations; An age of progress, but not only** — steel, electricity, the Otto engine and the assembly line power a second industrial revolution while Pasteur and Koch's Franco-Prussian rivalry (Storia culturale) shows science itself becoming a national contest — growth with shadows already inside it
