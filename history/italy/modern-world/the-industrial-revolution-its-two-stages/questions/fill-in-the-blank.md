# The industrial revolution its two stages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§1–2 The 'Great Depression', between crisis and growth; The transformations of industry and finance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Prices fall for two decades yet steel, chemicals and electricity power a second industrial revolution; trusts answer crisis with monopoly (Document: the Sherman Antitrust Act).

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 5, Cap. 16 §§1–3 Crisis and protectionism; The second industrial revolution; Mass consumption and productive rationalisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Steel, electricity, Taylor's scientific management and Ford's line (Fare storia documents) remake production — Mokyr and Landes argue over its engines.
