# The industrial revolution its two stages — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–2 An age of growth and transformations; An age of progress, but not only", steel, electricity, the Otto engine and the assembly line power a second industrial revolution while Pasteur and Koch's Franco-Prussian rivalry (Storia culturale) shows science itself becoming a national contest — growth with shadows already inside it (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§1–2 The 'Great Depression', between crisis and growth; The transformations of industry and finance", prices fall for two decades yet steel, chemicals and electricity power a second industrial revolution; trusts answer crisis with monopoly (Document: the Sherman Antitrust Act) (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
