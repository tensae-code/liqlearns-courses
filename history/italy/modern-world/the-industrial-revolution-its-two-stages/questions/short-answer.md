# The industrial revolution its two stages — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 5, Cap. 16 §§1–3 Crisis and protectionism; The second industrial revolution; Mass consumption and productive rationalisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Steel, electricity, Taylor's scientific management and Ford's line (Fare storia documents) remake production — Mokyr and Landes argue over its engines.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–2 An age of growth and transformations; An age of progress, but not only" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Steel, electricity, the Otto engine and the assembly line power a second industrial revolution while Pasteur and Koch's Franco-Prussian rivalry (Storia culturale) shows science itself becoming a national contest — growth with shadows already inside it.
