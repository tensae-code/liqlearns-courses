# Lesson plan — Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The 1700s and 1800s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 2): Enlightenment and enlightened reform, industry, the American and French revolutions, Napoleon, Restoration, 1848, the Risorgimento and liberal Italy to 1900 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The 1700s and 1800s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 2): Enlightenment and enlightened reform, industry, the American and French revolutions, Napoleon, Restoration, 1848, the Risorgimento and liberal Italy to 1900 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 4, Cap. 12 §6 The rise of Prussia and the unification of Germany** — Bismarck unifies Germany by three wars — Denmark, Austria, France — and proclaims the Reich at Versailles (1871)
2. **§2 Modulo 6, Cap. 17 §§1–4 The continental powers; The defeat of France and German unity; The Paris Commune; The German Empire and Bismarck's policy** — Sedan and Versailles complete unification; the Commune's Paris shows the social war inside the national one; Bismarck (Personaggi) then freezes the map he made

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
