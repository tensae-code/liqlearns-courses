# Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §6 The rise of Prussia and the unification of Germany" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bismarck unifies Germany by three wars — Denmark, Austria, France — and proclaims the Reich at Versailles (1871).

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 17 §§1–4 The continental powers; The defeat of France and German unity; The Paris Commune; The German Empire and Bismarck's policy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sedan and Versailles complete unification; the Commune's Paris shows the social war inside the national one; Bismarck (Personaggi) then freezes the map he made.
