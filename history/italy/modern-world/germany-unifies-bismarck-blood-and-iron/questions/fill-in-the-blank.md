# Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §6 The rise of Prussia and the unification of Germany" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bismarck unifies Germany by three wars — Denmark, Austria, France — and proclaims the Reich at Versailles (1871).

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 17 §§1–4 The continental powers; The defeat of France and German unity; The Paris Commune; The German Empire and Bismarck's policy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sedan and Versailles complete unification; the Commune's Paris shows the social war inside the national one; Bismarck (Personaggi) then freezes the map he made.
