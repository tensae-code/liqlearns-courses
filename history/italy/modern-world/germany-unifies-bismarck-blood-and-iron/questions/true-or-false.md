# Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §6 The rise of Prussia and the unification of Germany", Bismarck unifies Germany by three wars — Denmark, Austria, France — and proclaims the Reich at Versailles (1871) (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 6, Cap. 17 §§1–4 The continental powers; The defeat of France and German unity; The Paris Commune; The German Empire and Bismarck's policy", Sedan and Versailles complete unification; the Commune's Paris shows the social war inside the national one; Bismarck (Personaggi) then freezes the map he made (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
