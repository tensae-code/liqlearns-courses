# Exploration portugal and the sea routes

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Exploration portugal and the sea routes" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 12 §§1–2 In search of spices and gold; The Portuguese explorations in Africa and Asia

Caravels feel their way down Africa for a sea road to India's spices, rounding the Cape until da Gama reaches Calicut (1498).

## Sub lesson 2 — I portoghesi nell'Atlantico

Portugal creeps down Africa, takes the Atlantic islands and learns the winds that will carry Europe around the world.
