# Exploration portugal and the sea routes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §§1–2 In search of spices and gold; The Portuguese explorations in Africa and Asia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Caravels feel their way down Africa for a sea road to India's spices, rounding the Cape until da Gama reaches Calicut (1498).

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "I portoghesi nell'Atlantico" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Portugal creeps down Africa, takes the Atlantic islands and learns the winds that will carry Europe around the world.
