# Exploration portugal and the sea routes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §§1–2 In search of spices and gold; The Portuguese explorations in Africa and Asia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Caravels feel their way down Africa for a sea road to India's spices, rounding the Cape until da Gama reaches Calicut (1498).

**2.** Tell the story of "I portoghesi nell'Atlantico" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Portugal creeps down Africa, takes the Atlantic islands and learns the winds that will carry Europe around the world.
