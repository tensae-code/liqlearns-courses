# Exploration portugal and the sea routes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §§1–2 In search of spices and gold; The Portuguese explorations in Africa and Asia", caravels feel their way down Africa for a sea road to India's spices, rounding the Cape until da Gama reaches Calicut (1498) (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "I portoghesi nell'Atlantico", Portugal creeps down Africa, takes the Atlantic islands and learns the winds that will carry Europe around the world (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
