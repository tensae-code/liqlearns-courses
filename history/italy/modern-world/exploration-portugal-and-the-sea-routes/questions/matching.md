# Exploration portugal and the sea routes — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 12 §§1–2 In search of spices and gold; The Portuguese explorations in Africa and Asia — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** I portoghesi nell'Atlantico — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) caravels feel their way down Africa for a sea road to India's spices, rounding the Cape until da Gama reaches Calicut (1498)
b) Portugal creeps down Africa, takes the Atlantic islands and learns the winds that will carry Europe around the world

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
