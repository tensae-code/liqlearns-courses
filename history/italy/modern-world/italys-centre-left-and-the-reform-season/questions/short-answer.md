# Italys centre left and the reform season — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 19 §4 The centre-left governments and the reforms" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Socialists enter government, electricity is nationalised and the middle school unified; Merlin's inquiry on the Vajont (unit Document) shows the miracle's other face — progress with a body count.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 13 §7 Il centro-sinistra" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Socialists enter government and the reform season opens — divorce and the Vajont (Focus) mark its social advances and its disasters.
