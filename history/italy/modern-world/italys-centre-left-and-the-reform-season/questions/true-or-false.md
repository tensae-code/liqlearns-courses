# Italys centre left and the reform season — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 19 §4 The centre-left governments and the reforms", Socialists enter government, electricity is nationalised and the middle school unified; Merlin's inquiry on the Vajont (unit Document) shows the miracle's other face — progress with a body count (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 13 §7 Il centro-sinistra", the Socialists enter government and the reform season opens — divorce and the Vajont (Focus) mark its social advances and its disasters (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
