# Italys centre left and the reform season — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 5, Cap. 19 §4 The centre-left governments and the reforms — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 13 §7 Il centro-sinistra — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Socialists enter government, electricity is nationalised and the middle school unified; Merlin's inquiry on the Vajont (unit Document) shows the miracle's other face — progress with a body count
