# The price revolution and the first world economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 13 §§4–5 The beginnings of a world-economy; Prices rise, the poor grow" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** American silver floods Seville and prices climb for a century while wages lag; historians debate whether one can already speak of a world economy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "L'Europa nel '500: la crescita demografica; Il costo della vita e la produzione agricola; Salari e profitti; Le miniere e le industrie; L'economia-mondo" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** More mouths, dearer bread, Potosí's silver and falling real wages — the price revolution teaches Europe it now lives in one economy.
