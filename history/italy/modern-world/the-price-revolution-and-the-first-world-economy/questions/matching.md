# The price revolution and the first world economy — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 13 §§4–5 The beginnings of a world-economy; Prices rise, the poor grow — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** L'Europa nel '500: la crescita demografica; Il costo della vita e la produzione agricola; Salari e profitti; Le miniere e le industrie; L'economia-mondo — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) American silver floods Seville and prices climb for a century while wages lag; historians debate whether one can already speak of a world economy
b) more mouths, dearer bread, Potosí's silver and falling real wages — the price revolution teaches Europe it now lives in one economy

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
