# The price revolution and the first world economy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 13 §§4–5 The beginnings of a world-economy; Prices rise, the poor grow", American silver floods Seville and prices climb for a century while wages lag; historians debate whether one can already speak of a world economy (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "L'Europa nel '500: la crescita demografica; Il costo della vita e la produzione agricola; Salari e profitti; Le miniere e le industrie; L'economia-mondo", more mouths, dearer bread, Potosí's silver and falling real wages — the price revolution teaches Europe it now lives in one economy (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
