# The price revolution and the first world economy

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The price revolution and the first world economy" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 13 §§4–5 The beginnings of a world-economy; Prices rise, the poor grow

American silver floods Seville and prices climb for a century while wages lag; historians debate whether one can already speak of a world economy.

## Sub lesson 2 — L'Europa nel '500: la crescita demografica; Il costo della vita e la produzione agricola; Salari e profitti; Le miniere e le industrie; L'economia-mondo

More mouths, dearer bread, Potosí's silver and falling real wages — the price revolution teaches Europe it now lives in one economy.
