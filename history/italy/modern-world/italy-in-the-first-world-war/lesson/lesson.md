# Italy in the first world war

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Italy in the first world war" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§4–5 Italy enters the war (1915); A bloody two years of stalemate (1915–1916)

Benedict XV begs the powers to stop the "horrendous carnage" (Document) while Italy bleeds on the Isonzo and the Carso; Gilbert's Storiografia follows Germany's unrestricted submarine war widening the conflict.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §4 L'Italia dalla neutralità all'intervento

A neutral country argues itself into war — interventionists against Giolitti's "quite a lot", the secret London Pact (in the Fare storia documents) buys Italy with other peoples' lands.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Italy in the first world war as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
