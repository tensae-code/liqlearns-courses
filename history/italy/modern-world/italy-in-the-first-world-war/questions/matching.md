# Italy in the first world war — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§4–5 Italy enters the war (1915); A bloody two years of stalemate (1915–1916) — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §4 L'Italia dalla neutralità all'intervento — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Benedict XV begs the powers to stop the "horrendous carnage" (Document) while Italy bleeds on the Isonzo and the Carso; Gilbert's Storiografia follows Germany's unrestricted submarine war widening the conflict
b) a neutral country argues itself into war — interventionists against Giolitti's "quite a lot", the secret London Pact (in the Fare storia documents) buys Italy with other peoples' lands

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
