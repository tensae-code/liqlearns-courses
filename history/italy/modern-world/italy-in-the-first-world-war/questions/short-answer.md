# Italy in the first world war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§4–5 Italy enters the war (1915); A bloody two years of stalemate (1915–1916)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Benedict XV begs the powers to stop the "horrendous carnage" (Document) while Italy bleeds on the Isonzo and the Carso; Gilbert's Storiografia follows Germany's unrestricted submarine war widening the conflict.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §4 L'Italia dalla neutralità all'intervento" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A neutral country argues itself into war — interventionists against Giolitti's "quite a lot", the secret London Pact (in the Fare storia documents) buys Italy with other peoples' lands.
