# Italy in the first world war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§4–5 Italy enters the war (1915); A bloody two years of stalemate (1915–1916)", Benedict XV begs the powers to stop the "horrendous carnage" (Document) while Italy bleeds on the Isonzo and the Carso; Gilbert's Storiografia follows Germany's unrestricted submarine war widening the conflict (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §4 L'Italia dalla neutralità all'intervento", a neutral country argues itself into war — interventionists against Giolitti's "quite a lot", the secret London Pact (in the Fare storia documents) buys Italy with other peoples' lands (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
