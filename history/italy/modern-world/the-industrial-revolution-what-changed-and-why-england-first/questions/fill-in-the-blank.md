# The industrial revolution what changed and why england first — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 3 §1 The origins of an epochal transformation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Coal, colonies, capital and Adam Smith's invisible hand (Document) converge in Britain — the course asks why the industrial revolution happened there first.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 3, Cap. 8 §§1–3 Characters of the industrial revolution; Why in Britain?; Cotton and iron" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Allen's and Landes' Fare storia essays argue the British case — high wages, coal and markets — over Watt's (Personaggi) engine.
