# Europes 17th century crisis — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 6, Cap. 18 §§1–2 An age of great changes; Production and markets: the Europe that declines and the one that grows", population dips, harvests shrink in a colder century and Mediterranean Europe stagnates while the North Sea economies quietly grow (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "L'Italia dopo Cateau-Cambrésis; Industria e commercio nel '600; Il settore agricolo; Le rivolte", Spanish Italy stagnates, revolts shake Naples and the provinces, and the peninsula's primacy passes north (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
