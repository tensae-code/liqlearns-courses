# Europes 17th century crisis — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 6, Cap. 18 §§1–2 An age of great changes; Production and markets: the Europe that declines and the one that grows" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Population dips, harvests shrink in a colder century and Mediterranean Europe stagnates while the North Sea economies quietly grow.

**2.** Tell the story of "L'Italia dopo Cateau-Cambrésis; Industria e commercio nel '600; Il settore agricolo; Le rivolte" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Spanish Italy stagnates, revolts shake Naples and the provinces, and the peninsula's primacy passes north.
