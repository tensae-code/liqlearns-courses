# The birth of socialist thought — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §5 Socialist thought" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From Owen's cooperatives to Proudhon's 'property is theft' (Document), the factory system's critics build the century's second great ideology.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 10 §§6–8 Socialism; Marx and Engels; The workers' question" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Saint-Simon, Proudhon and Marx (Fare storia: the classless society) answer the factory age; unions (Focus) organise the answer on the ground.
