# The birth of socialist thought — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §5 Socialist thought", from Owen's cooperatives to Proudhon's 'property is theft' (Document), the factory system's critics build the century's second great ideology (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 4, Cap. 10 §§6–8 Socialism; Marx and Engels; The workers' question", Saint-Simon, Proudhon and Marx (Fare storia: the classless society) answer the factory age; unions (Focus) organise the answer on the ground (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
