# The birth of socialist thought — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §5 Socialist thought" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From Owen's cooperatives to Proudhon's 'property is theft' (Document), the factory system's critics build the century's second great ideology.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 10 §§6–8 Socialism; Marx and Engels; The workers' question" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Saint-Simon, Proudhon and Marx (Fare storia: the classless society) answer the factory age; unions (Focus) organise the answer on the ground.
