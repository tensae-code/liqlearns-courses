# The birth of socialist thought

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The birth of socialist thought" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 9 §5 Socialist thought

From Owen's cooperatives to Proudhon's 'property is theft' (Document), the factory system's critics build the century's second great ideology.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 4, Cap. 10 §§6–8 Socialism; Marx and Engels; The workers' question

Saint-Simon, Proudhon and Marx (Fare storia: the classless society) answer the factory age; unions (Focus) organise the answer on the ground.
