# Fascist economic policy and corporativism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §3 Economic policy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The battle for grain, reclamation and the corporate state — corporativism's long genealogy is the Storia culturale's theme — promise an economy beyond classes while wages and freedoms shrink.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6 §4 Economia e ideologia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Autarky and the corporate state promise a third way; the economy's real masters and the ideology's real function are the section's question.
