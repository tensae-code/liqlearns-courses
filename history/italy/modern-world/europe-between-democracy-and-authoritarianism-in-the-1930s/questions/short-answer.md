# Europe between democracy and authoritarianism in the 1930s — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 12 §1 Europe between authoritarianisms and democracies in crisis" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The chapter's map counts the democracies left standing; Otto Bauer's Document anatomises Austria's authoritarian regime — a continent sliding from parliament to dictatorship.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 5 §1 Democrazie in crisi e fascismi" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The module's Anteprima frames the decade — democracy's eclipse, authoritarianism against totalitarianism, Stalinism as modernisation, the ideological war and the planned extermination to come.
