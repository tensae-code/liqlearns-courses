## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 3, Cap. 13 §§1–2 The outbreak of the war; The attack on France and England** — Poland is partitioned, the blitzkrieg breaks France, and Churchill's "blood, toil, tears and sweat" (Document) answers from a Britain sheltering in the Underground (Storie d'arte)
2. **§2 Unità 3, Cap. 13 §3 Italy's parallel war and the invasion of the Soviet Union** — Mussolini announces war on 10 June 1940 (the unit's video) for a cheap seat at the victors' table; the parallel war fails in Greece and Africa, and Barbarossa (the chapter's maps) opens the war's true centre in the East
3. **§3 Unità 3, Cap. 13 §5 The turning point in the war** — the Atlantic Charter (the chapter's document) states the Allies' war aims before they are winning; El Alamein, Stalingrad and the Pacific turn the tide the maps then follow
4. **§4 Modulo 8 §§2–3 La guerra-lampo; La sconfitta della Francia e la resistenza della Gran Bretagna** — Bloch's Strange Defeat (Fare storia; also the module's book study) asks why France fell in six weeks; Churchill (Personaggi) holds Britain's resistance together alone
5. **§5 Modulo 8 §§5–6 1941: l'entrata in guerra di Urss e Stati Uniti; L'ordine dei dominatori: Resistenza e collaborazionismo** — Barbarossa (Bartov's Fare storia on the eastern front's brutalisation) and Pearl Harbor (Eventi) make the war world-wide; under the dominators' order Europe splits between resistance and collaboration
6. **§6 Modulo 8 §§8–9 Le battaglie decisive; Dallo sbarco in Sicilia allo sbarco in Normandia** — Stalingrad, El Alamein and Midway turn the war; Overy's Fare storia piece weighs the Allied bombing that accompanies the long road back to Normandy
