# The second world war course in europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §§1–2 The outbreak of the war; The attack on France and England" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Poland is partitioned, the blitzkrieg breaks France, and Churchill's "blood, toil, tears and sweat" (Document) answers from a Britain sheltering in the Underground (Storie d'arte).

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §3 Italy's parallel war and the invasion of the Soviet Union" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mussolini announces war on 10 June 1940 (the unit's video) for a cheap seat at the victors' table; the parallel war fails in Greece and Africa, and Barbarossa (the chapter's maps) opens the war's true centre in the East.
