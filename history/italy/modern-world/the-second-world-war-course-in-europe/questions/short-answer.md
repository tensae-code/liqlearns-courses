# The second world war course in europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 8 §§5–6 1941: l'entrata in guerra di Urss e Stati Uniti; L'ordine dei dominatori: Resistenza e collaborazionismo" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Barbarossa (Bartov's Fare storia on the eastern front's brutalisation) and Pearl Harbor (Eventi) make the war world-wide; under the dominators' order Europe splits between resistance and collaboration.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 8 §§8–9 Le battaglie decisive; Dallo sbarco in Sicilia allo sbarco in Normandia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Stalingrad, El Alamein and Midway turn the war; Overy's Fare storia piece weighs the Allied bombing that accompanies the long road back to Normandy.
