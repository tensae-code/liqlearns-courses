# The second world war course in europe

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The second world war course in europe" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 13 §§1–2 The outbreak of the war; The attack on France and England

Poland is partitioned, the blitzkrieg breaks France, and Churchill's "blood, toil, tears and sweat" (Document) answers from a Britain sheltering in the Underground (Storie d'arte).

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 3, Cap. 13 §3 Italy's parallel war and the invasion of the Soviet Union

Mussolini announces war on 10 June 1940 (the unit's video) for a cheap seat at the victors' table; the parallel war fails in Greece and Africa, and Barbarossa (the chapter's maps) opens the war's true centre in the East.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unità 3, Cap. 13 §5 The turning point in the war

The Atlantic Charter (the chapter's document) states the Allies' war aims before they are winning; El Alamein, Stalingrad and the Pacific turn the tide the maps then follow.

## Sub lesson 4 — Modulo 8 §§2–3 La guerra-lampo; La sconfitta della Francia e la resistenza della Gran Bretagna

Bloch's Strange Defeat (Fare storia; also the module's book study) asks why France fell in six weeks; Churchill (Personaggi) holds Britain's resistance together alone.

## Sub lesson 5 — Modulo 8 §§5–6 1941: l'entrata in guerra di Urss e Stati Uniti; L'ordine dei dominatori: Resistenza e collaborazionismo

Barbarossa (Bartov's Fare storia on the eastern front's brutalisation) and Pearl Harbor (Eventi) make the war world-wide; under the dominators' order Europe splits between resistance and collaboration.

## Sub lesson 6 — Modulo 8 §§8–9 Le battaglie decisive; Dallo sbarco in Sicilia allo sbarco in Normandia

Stalingrad, El Alamein and Midway turn the war; Overy's Fare storia piece weighs the Allied bombing that accompanies the long road back to Normandy.
