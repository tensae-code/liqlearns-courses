# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The hinges of the Enlightenment; Culture in the age of the Lumi", reason, toleration and the Encyclopédie — Kant, Voltaire, Diderot and Rousseau arm a generation with the idea that society can be remade by thought (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §3 The Enlightenment, a movement open to the world", scientific voyages, salons and freemasonry carry the Lumi beyond Europe's courts, and the course asks whether the Enlightenment's science also fathered modern racism (Storici a confronto) (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
