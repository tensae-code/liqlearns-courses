# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§1–3 A cultural revolution: the new science; Galileo's trial (Eventi); A new political thought" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Science separates its plane from religion's (Document: Galileo), and political thought follows — the century learns to argue from evidence in both worlds.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§4–7 The Enlightenment: its hinges and diffusion; The French illuminists; The new sciences and the Enlightenment in Europe; The Italian illuminists" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Voltaire (Personaggi), the philosophes and Italy's own reformers carry reason into salons, lodges and academies across the continent.
