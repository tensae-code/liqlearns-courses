# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 18 §4 The Scientific Revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Galileo's telescope and his clash with Cardinal Bellarmino (Storiografia) and Newton's Principia (in the Documents) remake the picture of the universe against the Church's resistance.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 18 §5 A new political thought" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Locke argues power rests on consent and toleration (Document: Letter on toleration), giving the coming revolutions their language.
