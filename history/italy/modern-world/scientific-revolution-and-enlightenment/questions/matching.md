# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The hinges of the Enlightenment; Culture in the age of the Lumi — a) (Sub lesson 3)
**2.** Unità 1, Cap. 2 §3 The Enlightenment, a movement open to the world — b) (Sub lesson 4)

a) reason, toleration and the Encyclopédie — Kant, Voltaire, Diderot and Rousseau arm a generation with the idea that society can be remade by thought
