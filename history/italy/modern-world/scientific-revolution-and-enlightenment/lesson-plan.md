# Lesson plan — Scientific revolution and enlightenment

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Scientific revolution and enlightenment
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Europe's revival after the year 1000 to the 1600s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 1): communes and crusades, crisis and plague, signorie, Renaissance, the voyages, Reformation and the century of crisis (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Scientific revolution and enlightenment as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Europe's revival after the year 1000 to the 1600s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 1): communes and crusades, crisis and plague, signorie, Renaissance, the voyages, Reformation and the century of crisis — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 6, Cap. 18 §4 The Scientific Revolution** — Galileo's telescope and his clash with Cardinal Bellarmino (Storiografia) and Newton's Principia (in the Documents) remake the picture of the universe against the Church's resistance
2. **§2 Unità 6, Cap. 18 §5 A new political thought** — Locke argues power rests on consent and toleration (Document: Letter on toleration), giving the coming revolutions their language
3. **§3 Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The hinges of the Enlightenment; Culture in the age of the Lumi** — reason, toleration and the Encyclopédie — Kant, Voltaire, Diderot and Rousseau arm a generation with the idea that society can be remade by thought
4. **§4 Unità 1, Cap. 2 §3 The Enlightenment, a movement open to the world** — scientific voyages, salons and freemasonry carry the Lumi beyond Europe's courts, and the course asks whether the Enlightenment's science also fathered modern racism (Storici a confronto)
5. **§5 Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§1–3 A cultural revolution: the new science; Galileo's trial (Eventi); A new political thought** — science separates its plane from religion's (Document: Galileo), and political thought follows — the century learns to argue from evidence in both worlds
6. **§6 Modulo 1, Cap. 3 §§4–7 The Enlightenment: its hinges and diffusion; The French illuminists; The new sciences and the Enlightenment in Europe; The Italian illuminists** — Voltaire (Personaggi), the philosophes and Italy's own reformers carry reason into salons, lodges and academies across the continent
