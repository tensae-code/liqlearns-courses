# Collectivisation famine and the great purges — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§3–4 Collectivisation of the countryside and the nationality question; Soviet society and the great purges" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A testimony on the great famine (Document) speaks for collectivisation's victims on the chapter's industrialisation maps; the purges then devour the party itself — Bukharin's trial is in the unit's Documents.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 5 §§6–7 L'Urss: collettivizzazione e industrializzazione; L'Urss: le "grandi purghe" e i processi" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Ukrainian famine (Focus) prices collectivisation; the show trials and Zaslavsky's piece on Stalinist terror, Solzhenitsyn's Article 58 (Fare storia) and the Gulag–Auschwitz debate (Nolte–Kocka) close the account — Stalin is the module's Personaggi.
