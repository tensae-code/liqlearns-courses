# Collectivisation famine and the great purges — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§3–4 Collectivisation of the countryside and the nationality question; Soviet society and the great purges — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 5 §§6–7 L'Urss: collettivizzazione e industrializzazione; L'Urss: le "grandi purghe" e i processi — b) (Sub lesson 2)
