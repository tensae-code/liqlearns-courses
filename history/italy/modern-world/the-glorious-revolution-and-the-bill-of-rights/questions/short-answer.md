# The glorious revolution and the bill of rights — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 6, Cap. 20 §2 The 'glorious revolution'" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 1688 Parliament invites William of Orange, James II flees, and the Bill of Rights (Document) makes the king rule by Parliament's leave.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§3–4 The revolution of 1688–89 in England; Toward parliamentary government in Great Britain" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** England's second revolution settles the crown under Parliament (Document: the English constitutional monarchy) while France keeps the opposite model — the module's 'two models of government' dossier.
