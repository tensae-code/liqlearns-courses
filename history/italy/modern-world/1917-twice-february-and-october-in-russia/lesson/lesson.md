## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–2 The collapse of the Tsarist empire; The October revolution

Bread queues and a collapsing front fell the tsar in February; Lenin's April Theses (Document) arm October, and women's participation (Storia culturale) marks a revolution fought by more than soldiers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §9 La rivoluzione d'ottobre (con Eventi: L'assalto al Palazzo d'Inverno)

The Winter Palace falls almost without a fight; Lenin's April Theses and Werth's account (Fare storia) argue how the Bolsheviks took power, Pipes' piece names the Red Terror that followed.
