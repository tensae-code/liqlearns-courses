# 1917 twice february and october in russia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–2 The collapse of the Tsarist empire; The October revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bread queues and a collapsing front fell the tsar in February; Lenin's April Theses (Document) arm October, and women's participation (Storia culturale) marks a revolution fought by more than soldiers.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §9 La rivoluzione d'ottobre (con Eventi: L'assalto al Palazzo d'Inverno)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Winter Palace falls almost without a fight; Lenin's April Theses and Werth's account (Fare storia) argue how the Bolsheviks took power, Pipes' piece names the Red Terror that followed.
