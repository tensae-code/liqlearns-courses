# 1917 twice february and october in russia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–2 The collapse of the Tsarist empire; The October revolution", bread queues and a collapsing front fell the tsar in February; Lenin's April Theses (Document) arm October, and women's participation (Storia culturale) marks a revolution fought by more than soldiers (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §9 La rivoluzione d'ottobre (con Eventi: L'assalto al Palazzo d'Inverno)", the Winter Palace falls almost without a fight; Lenin's April Theses and Werth's account (Fare storia) argue how the Bolsheviks took power, Pipes' piece names the Red Terror that followed (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
