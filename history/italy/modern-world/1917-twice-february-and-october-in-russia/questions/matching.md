# 1917 twice february and october in russia — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–2 The collapse of the Tsarist empire; The October revolution — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §9 La rivoluzione d'ottobre (con Eventi: L'assalto al Palazzo d'Inverno) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) bread queues and a collapsing front fell the tsar in February; Lenin's April Theses (Document) arm October, and women's participation (Storia culturale) marks a revolution fought by more than soldiers
b) the Winter Palace falls almost without a fight; Lenin's April Theses and Werth's account (Fare storia) argue how the Bolsheviks took power, Pipes' piece names the Red Terror that followed

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
