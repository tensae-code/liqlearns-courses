# 1917 twice february and october in russia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–2 The collapse of the Tsarist empire; The October revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bread queues and a collapsing front fell the tsar in February; Lenin's April Theses (Document) arm October, and women's participation (Storia culturale) marks a revolution fought by more than soldiers.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §9 La rivoluzione d'ottobre (con Eventi: L'assalto al Palazzo d'Inverno)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Winter Palace falls almost without a fight; Lenin's April Theses and Werth's account (Fare storia) argue how the Bolsheviks took power, Pipes' piece names the Red Terror that followed.
