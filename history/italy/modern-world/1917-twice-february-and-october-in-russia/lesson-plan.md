# Lesson plan — 1917 twice february and october in russia

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** 1917 twice february and october in russia
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of 1917 twice february and october in russia as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–2 The collapse of the Tsarist empire; The October revolution** — bread queues and a collapsing front fell the tsar in February; Lenin's April Theses (Document) arm October, and women's participation (Storia culturale) marks a revolution fought by more than soldiers
2. **§2 Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §9 La rivoluzione d'ottobre (con Eventi: L'assalto al Palazzo d'Inverno)** — the Winter Palace falls almost without a fight; Lenin's April Theses and Werth's account (Fare storia) argue how the Bolsheviks took power, Pipes' piece names the Red Terror that followed

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
