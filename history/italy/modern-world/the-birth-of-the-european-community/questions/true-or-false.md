# The birth of the european community — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 15 §4 The Europe of the Economic Community and the Franco-German axis", Rome's treaties (1957) turn the coal-and-steel pool into a common market, and Paris-Bonn reconciliation becomes its engine — the integration Schuman imagined takes institutional flesh (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 9 §8 Le democrazie europee e l'avvio dell'integrazione economica", europeismo (Focus) becomes institutions — the Atlantic Pact (Fare storia Document) guards the West while coal, steel and then the common market bind it (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
