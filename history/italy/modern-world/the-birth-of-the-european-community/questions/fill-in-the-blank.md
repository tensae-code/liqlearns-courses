# The birth of the european community — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 15 §4 The Europe of the Economic Community and the Franco-German axis" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rome's treaties (1957) turn the coal-and-steel pool into a common market, and Paris-Bonn reconciliation becomes its engine — the integration Schuman imagined takes institutional flesh.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 9 §8 Le democrazie europee e l'avvio dell'integrazione economica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Europeismo (Focus) becomes institutions — the Atlantic Pact (Fare storia Document) guards the West while coal, steel and then the common market bind it.
