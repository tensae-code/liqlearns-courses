## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 15 §4 The Europe of the Economic Community and the Franco-German axis

Rome's treaties (1957) turn the coal-and-steel pool into a common market, and Paris-Bonn reconciliation becomes its engine — the integration Schuman imagined takes institutional flesh.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 9 §8 Le democrazie europee e l'avvio dell'integrazione economica

Europeismo (Focus) becomes institutions — the Atlantic Pact (Fare storia Document) guards the West while coal, steel and then the common market bind it.
