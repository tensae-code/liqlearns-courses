# The peoples democracies stalinism exported — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §4 Stalinism in Eastern Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Coalition governments are eaten one by one and the Soviet model — party, plan, police — is installed from Warsaw to Sofia; the division of the world (the chapter's videomap) hardens into two systems.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 9 §4 L'Urss e l'Europa orientale" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Warsaw Pact (Fare storia Document) formalises an empire of people's democracies; the Soviet model is exported whole — party, plan and police.
