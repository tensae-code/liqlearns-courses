# The peoples democracies stalinism exported — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §4 Stalinism in Eastern Europe", coalition governments are eaten one by one and the Soviet model — party, plan, police — is installed from Warsaw to Sofia; the division of the world (the chapter's videomap) hardens into two systems (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 9 §4 L'Urss e l'Europa orientale", the Warsaw Pact (Fare storia Document) formalises an empire of people's democracies; the Soviet model is exported whole — party, plan and police (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
