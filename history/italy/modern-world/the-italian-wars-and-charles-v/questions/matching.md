# The italian wars and charles v — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 5, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The first phase of the Italian Wars; Charles V and the ideal of a universal Empire; An Empire to defend — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** La guerra in Italia; Il Sacco di Roma (Eventi); La rinuncia di Carlo V — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Charles VIII's descent (1494) turns Italy into Europe's battlefield; Charles V inherits half the world, fights France, the Ottomans and the Lutherans, sacks Rome (1527) and leaves Italy to Spain at Cateau-Cambrésis
b) Italy pays for Charles V's empire with the Sack of Rome (1527); in 1555–56 the exhausted emperor divides his world and retires

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
