# The italian wars and charles v — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The first phase of the Italian Wars; Charles V and the ideal of a universal Empire; An Empire to defend", Charles VIII's descent (1494) turns Italy into Europe's battlefield; Charles V inherits half the world, fights France, the Ottomans and the Lutherans, sacks Rome (1527) and leaves Italy to Spain at Cateau-Cambrésis (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La guerra in Italia; Il Sacco di Roma (Eventi); La rinuncia di Carlo V", Italy pays for Charles V's empire with the Sack of Rome (1527); in 1555–56 the exhausted emperor divides his world and retires (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
