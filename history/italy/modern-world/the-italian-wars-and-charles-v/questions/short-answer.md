# The italian wars and charles v — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The first phase of the Italian Wars; Charles V and the ideal of a universal Empire; An Empire to defend" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Charles VIII's descent (1494) turns Italy into Europe's battlefield; Charles V inherits half the world, fights France, the Ottomans and the Lutherans, sacks Rome (1527) and leaves Italy to Spain at Cateau-Cambrésis.

**2.** Tell the story of "La guerra in Italia; Il Sacco di Roma (Eventi); La rinuncia di Carlo V" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Italy pays for Charles V's empire with the Sack of Rome (1527); in 1555–56 the exhausted emperor divides his world and retires.
