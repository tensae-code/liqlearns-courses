## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 4 §1 Europe on the eve of the war

Alliances, arms races and colonial friction load the continent (the chapter's map of critical areas and rearmament); the Storici a confronto at the unit's end — Fischer against Ferguson — argue whether Germany willed the war.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§1–2 Venti di guerra; Una reazione a catena

An unstable balance of rival empires and rigid blocs meets the Sarajevo assassination (Eventi); the Anteprima's essay refuses Versailles' simple verdict of guilt and reads Germany's move as a calculated risk the other powers shared.
