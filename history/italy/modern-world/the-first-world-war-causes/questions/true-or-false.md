# The first world war causes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §1 Europe on the eve of the war", alliances, arms races and colonial friction load the continent (the chapter's map of critical areas and rearmament); the Storici a confronto at the unit's end — Fischer against Ferguson — argue whether Germany willed the war (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
