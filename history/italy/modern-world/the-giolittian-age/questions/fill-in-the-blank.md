# The giolittian age — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 21 §§8–10 Economic development and the problems of the South; The Giolittian age; Nationalism, the Libyan war and the end of Giolittism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Giolitti (Personaggi) opens to unions and suffrage (1912), industry booms in the northern triangle, emigration drains the South (Fare storia: Volpe), and the Libyan war (1911) arms the nationalism that will end his system.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §3 Giolitti's domestic policy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Giolitti's pragmatism (the chapter's document) opens the state to unions and strikes, widens the suffrage (1912) and tries to govern the mass age instead of repressing it — Marrocu's Storiografia weighs the electoral reform.
