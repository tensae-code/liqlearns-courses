# The giolittian age — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §4 The take-off of industry and the Southern question", steel (the ILVA story from Elba's iron), electricity and the railway network industrialise the North while Salvemini's Document indicts the South's abandonment, and Pascoli's Italy sings the emigrants leaving it (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 6, Cap. 21 §§8–10 Economic development and the problems of the South; The Giolittian age; Nationalism, the Libyan war and the end of Giolittism", Giolitti (Personaggi) opens to unions and suffrage (1912), industry booms in the northern triangle, emigration drains the South (Fare storia: Volpe), and the Libyan war (1911) arms the nationalism that will end his system (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
