# The giolittian age — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §3 Giolitti's domestic policy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Giolitti's pragmatism (the chapter's document) opens the state to unions and strikes, widens the suffrage (1912) and tries to govern the mass age instead of repressing it — Marrocu's Storiografia weighs the electoral reform.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §4 The take-off of industry and the Southern question" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Steel (the ILVA story from Elba's iron), electricity and the railway network industrialise the North while Salvemini's Document indicts the South's abandonment, and Pascoli's Italy sings the emigrants leaving it.
