# The great depression 1929 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§1–2 The collapse of the New York Stock Exchange; From America to the world: a crisis of deglobalisation", the 1929 crash crosses the Atlantic through loans and gold; the FSA photographers (image study) and the crisis's own figures (La storia in cifre) document a world economy unravelling — De Bernardi's Storiografia weighs its macroeconomic factors (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 4 §§1–3 Dalla ripresa alla crisi; Gli Stati Uniti e il crollo del '29; La crisi diventa mondiale", Wall Street's crashes (Eventi) end the twenties' recovery; Cameron–Neal and Brendon (Fare storia) separate the crash's causes from the Depression's political contagion (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
