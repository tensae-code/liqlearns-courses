# The great depression 1929

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The great depression 1929" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§1–2 The collapse of the New York Stock Exchange; From America to the world: a crisis of deglobalisation

The 1929 crash crosses the Atlantic through loans and gold; the FSA photographers (image study) and the crisis's own figures (La storia in cifre) document a world economy unravelling — De Bernardi's Storiografia weighs its macroeconomic factors.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 4 §§1–3 Dalla ripresa alla crisi; Gli Stati Uniti e il crollo del '29; La crisi diventa mondiale

Wall Street's crashes (Eventi) end the twenties' recovery; Cameron–Neal and Brendon (Fare storia) separate the crash's causes from the Depression's political contagion.
