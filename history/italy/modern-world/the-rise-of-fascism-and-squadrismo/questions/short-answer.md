# The rise of fascism and squadrismo — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§4–5 The rise of fascism; Agrarian fascism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mussolini's San Sepolcro programme (Document) mutates into the landowners' squads; urban and agrarian squadrismo (the chapter's document) burn the unions' chambers while the state looks away — Corner's Storiografia maps the movement's fragments.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 3 §§3–4 Lo squadrismo fascista; Mussolini alla conquista del potere" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The squads (the module's keyword) burn the leagues' chambers province by province — Tasca's Fare storia anatomy of squadrismo — until the March on Rome (Eventi) turns violence into government; Mussolini is the module's Personaggi study.
