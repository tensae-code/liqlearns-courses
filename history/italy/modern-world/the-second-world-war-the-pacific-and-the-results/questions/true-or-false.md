Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §7 The victory of the Allies", the Allied counteroffensive frees Europe and Italy (the chapter's maps), a kamikaze's farewell letter (Document) and Truman's own account of the atomic bomb close the Pacific war — the conflict ends in a new weapon's shadow (Sub lesson 1)
