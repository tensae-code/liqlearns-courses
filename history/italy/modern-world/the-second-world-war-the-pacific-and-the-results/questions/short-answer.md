# The second world war the pacific and the results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §7 The victory of the Allies" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Allied counteroffensive frees Europe and Italy (the chapter's maps), a kamikaze's farewell letter (Document) and Truman's own account of the atomic bomb close the Pacific war — the conflict ends in a new weapon's shadow.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 8 §12 La fine della guerra e la bomba atomica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hara's letter from Hiroshima (Fare storia Document) closes the war where the atomic age opens it — victory and the new weapon in the same pages.
