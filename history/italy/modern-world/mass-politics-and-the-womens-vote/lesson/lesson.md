## Unità 1, Cap. 1 §4 The widening of political participation, education and the women's question

Mass parties and near-universal male suffrage meet their limit in the excluded half — ten schoolteachers from the Marche win a court sentence on women's voting (the unit's document-right), and the suffragettes' courageous struggle runs on video beside it.
