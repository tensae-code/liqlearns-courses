# Mass politics and the womens vote — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §4 The widening of political participation, education and the women's question" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Mass parties and near-universal male suffrage meet their limit in the excluded half — ten schoolteachers from the Marche win a court sentence on women's voting (the unit's document-right), and the suffragettes' courageous struggle runs on video beside it.
