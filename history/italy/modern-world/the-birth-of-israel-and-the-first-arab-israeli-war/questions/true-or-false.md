# The birth of israel and the first arab israeli war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §7 The birth of the State of Israel", Zionism (Storia culturale), the Exodus ship's odyssey (Storie di frontiera) and the 1948 war found a state and a refugee question in the same act — Nasser's Document states the Arabs' denied rights, Vercelli's Storiografia the state's birth (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 10 §4 Il mondo arabo e la nascita di Israele", the UN resolution on Palestine, Codovini on the first Arab-Israeli war and Morris on the Palestinian exodus (Fare storia pieces) tell 1948 from its three sides (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
