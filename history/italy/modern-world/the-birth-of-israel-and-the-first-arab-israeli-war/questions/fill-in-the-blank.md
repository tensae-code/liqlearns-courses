# The birth of israel and the first arab israeli war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §7 The birth of the State of Israel" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Zionism (Storia culturale), the Exodus ship's odyssey (Storie di frontiera) and the 1948 war found a state and a refugee question in the same act — Nasser's Document states the Arabs' denied rights, Vercelli's Storiografia the state's birth.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 10 §4 Il mondo arabo e la nascita di Israele" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The UN resolution on Palestine, Codovini on the first Arab-Israeli war and Morris on the Palestinian exodus (Fare storia pieces) tell 1948 from its three sides.
