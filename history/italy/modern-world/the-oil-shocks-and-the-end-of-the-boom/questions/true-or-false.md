# The oil shocks and the end of the boom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 17 §3 The economic and technological turn", the 1973 oil crisis (Petrini's Storiografia on its causes) ends the golden age; Toyotism (Storia culturale) and Deng's opening (Vogel's Storiografia) announce the productive world that replaces Fordism (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 11 §§10–11 La fine dell'«età dell'oro»: la crisi petrolifera; L'emergere del problema ambientale", the 1973 oil shock ends the golden age as Chernobyl (Eventi) and the Limits to Growth (Fare storia Document) announce the ecological bill — ecology is the module's keyword (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
