# The oil shocks and the end of the boom — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 17 §3 The economic and technological turn — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 11 §§10–11 La fine dell'«età dell'oro»: la crisi petrolifera; L'emergere del problema ambientale — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the 1973 oil crisis (Petrini's Storiografia on its causes) ends the golden age; Toyotism (Storia culturale) and Deng's opening (Vogel's Storiografia) announce the productive world that replaces Fordism
b) the 1973 oil shock ends the golden age as Chernobyl (Eventi) and the Limits to Growth (Fare storia Document) announce the ecological bill — ecology is the module's keyword

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
