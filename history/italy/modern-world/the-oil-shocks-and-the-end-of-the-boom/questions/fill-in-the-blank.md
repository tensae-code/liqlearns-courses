# The oil shocks and the end of the boom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 17 §3 The economic and technological turn" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 1973 oil crisis (Petrini's Storiografia on its causes) ends the golden age; Toyotism (Storia culturale) and Deng's opening (Vogel's Storiografia) announce the productive world that replaces Fordism.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 11 §§10–11 La fine dell'«età dell'oro»: la crisi petrolifera; L'emergere del problema ambientale" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 1973 oil shock ends the golden age as Chernobyl (Eventi) and the Limits to Growth (Fare storia Document) announce the ecological bill — ecology is the module's keyword.
