# Italy 1943 45 armistice resistance and civil war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §6 The war in Italy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The armistice splits Italy in two; a testimony of 16 October 1943 (Document) records Rome's Jews taken, Pintor's last letter (in the unit's Documents) the partisan choice — Resistance, civil war and liberation in one national story.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 8 §§10–11 L'Italia: la caduta del fascismo e l'armistizio; L'Italia: Resistenza e guerra civile" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 25 July and 8 September split Italy three ways; Pavone's contested definition of "civil war", Fenoglio's partisan episode, the foibe violence on the eastern border (Fare storia pieces) and Galli della Loggia's "death of the fatherland" argue the nation's hardest twenty months.
