# Italy 1943 45 armistice resistance and civil war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §6 The war in Italy", the armistice splits Italy in two; a testimony of 16 October 1943 (Document) records Rome's Jews taken, Pintor's last letter (in the unit's Documents) the partisan choice — Resistance, civil war and liberation in one national story (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 8 §§10–11 L'Italia: la caduta del fascismo e l'armistizio; L'Italia: Resistenza e guerra civile", 25 July and 8 September split Italy three ways; Pavone's contested definition of "civil war", Fenoglio's partisan episode, the foibe violence on the eastern border (Fare storia pieces) and Galli della Loggia's "death of the fatherland" argue the nation's hardest twenty months (Sub lesson 2)
