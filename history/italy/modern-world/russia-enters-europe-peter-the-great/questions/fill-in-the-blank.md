# Russia enters europe peter the great — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 20 §4 A new presence on the horizon: Russia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From Ivan the Terrible (Laboratorio) to Peter the Great's reforms, Russia forces its way into Europe's balance as a great power.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Il Regno di Russia (in the '600 crisis module)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
