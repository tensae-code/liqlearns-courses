# Stalinism as a system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§5–6 The characters of Stalinism; Soviet foreign policy" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The personality cult (Benvenuti's Storiografia, the propaganda image study), the Gulag system (Di Sante) and the 1936 Constitution (Document) that proclaims rights the terror denies; Trotsky's Document calls it the revolution betrayed, Pons traces the 1930s foreign-policy turn.
