# Racism social darwinism and antisemitism before 1914 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §6 The darkest shadows of the belle époque" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Haeckel's social Darwinism (Storia culturale) and modern antisemitism darken the age of progress, and the Church faces the new century's challenges — the belle époque's own evidence against itself.
