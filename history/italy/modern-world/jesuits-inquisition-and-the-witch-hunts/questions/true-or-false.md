# Jesuits inquisition and the witch hunts — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The Catholic strategy for the reconquest of souls; Counter-Reformation and the repression of heresies", Ignatius' Jesuits carry schools and missions from Europe to South America while the Roman Inquisition, the Index and the witch-hunts police belief at home (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Inquisizione e censura", the Roman Inquisition, the Index of forbidden books and censorship police the printed word the press had set free (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
