# Jesuits inquisition and the witch hunts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The Catholic strategy for the reconquest of souls; Counter-Reformation and the repression of heresies" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ignatius' Jesuits carry schools and missions from Europe to South America while the Roman Inquisition, the Index and the witch-hunts police belief at home.

**2.** Tell the story of "Inquisizione e censura" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Roman Inquisition, the Index of forbidden books and censorship police the printed word the press had set free.
