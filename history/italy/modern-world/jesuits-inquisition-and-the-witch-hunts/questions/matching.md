# Jesuits inquisition and the witch hunts — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The Catholic strategy for the reconquest of souls; Counter-Reformation and the repression of heresies — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Inquisizione e censura — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Ignatius' Jesuits carry schools and missions from Europe to South America while the Roman Inquisition, the Index and the witch-hunts police belief at home
b) the Roman Inquisition, the Index of forbidden books and censorship police the printed word the press had set free

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
