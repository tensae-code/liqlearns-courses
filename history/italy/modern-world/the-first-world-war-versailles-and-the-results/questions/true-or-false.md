Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §7 The peace treaties (1918–1923)", Wilson's Fourteen Points (Document) meet the victors' accounts; the Ottoman empire is dismembered (the chapter's map), Germany is charged with responsibility — Fischer's Storiografia reopens that charge — and the numbers of the war (La storia in cifre) close the account (Sub lesson 1)
