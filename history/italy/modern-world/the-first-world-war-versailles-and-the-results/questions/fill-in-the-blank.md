# The first world war versailles and the results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §7 The peace treaties (1918–1923)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Wilson's Fourteen Points (Document) meet the victors' accounts; the Ottoman empire is dismembered (the chapter's map), Germany is charged with responsibility — Fischer's Storiografia reopens that charge — and the numbers of the war (La storia in cifre) close the account.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§11–12 1918: la sconfitta degli Imperi centrali; Vincitori e vinti" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Central Empires collapse, four empires vanish from the map, and Versailles' contradictions — Wilson's 14 Points against Keynes' economic warning (both in Fare storia) — plant the next crisis inside the peace.
