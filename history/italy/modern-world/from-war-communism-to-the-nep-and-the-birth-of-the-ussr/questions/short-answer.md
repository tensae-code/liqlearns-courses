# From war communism to the nep and the birth of the ussr — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§5–6 Economic policy from war communism to the NEP; The birth of the Soviet Union and the death of Lenin" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Famine and revolt force the NEP retreat (Graziosi's Storiografia calls it the only alternative to a new civil war); the USSR is founded as a federation — Stalin's Document argues its limits — and Lenin's death opens the succession.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§7–8 La Russia comunista; L'Urss da Lenin a Stalin" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Comintern's 21 Points (Fare storia) export the model while the USSR consolidates at home; Lenin (Personaggi) dies and Stalin inherits — the succession that decides the century's other totalitarianism.
