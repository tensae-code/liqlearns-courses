# From war communism to the nep and the birth of the ussr — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§5–6 Economic policy from war communism to the NEP; The birth of the Soviet Union and the death of Lenin", famine and revolt force the NEP retreat (Graziosi's Storiografia calls it the only alternative to a new civil war); the USSR is founded as a federation — Stalin's Document argues its limits — and Lenin's death opens the succession (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §§7–8 La Russia comunista; L'Urss da Lenin a Stalin", the Comintern's 21 Points (Fare storia) export the model while the USSR consolidates at home; Lenin (Personaggi) dies and Stalin inherits — the succession that decides the century's other totalitarianism (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
