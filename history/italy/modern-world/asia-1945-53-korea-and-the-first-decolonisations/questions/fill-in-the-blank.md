# Asia 1945 53 korea and the first decolonisations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §5 Asia between Cold War and decolonisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** China goes communist, Korea is fought to a stalemate (the chapter's maps), and Asia's decolonisation proceeds inside the superpowers' contest — two revolutions at once.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 9 §5 Rivoluzione in Cina, guerra in Corea" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mao (Personaggi) wins China in 1949 and Korea then freezes the revolution's frontier at the 38th parallel — Asia's Cold War is hot from the start.
