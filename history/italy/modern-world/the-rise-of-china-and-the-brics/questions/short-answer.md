# The rise of china and the brics — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 16 §§1–2 Le nuove gerarchie della ricchezza; Il boom della Cina" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Wealth's map is redrawn — Stiglitz on the Washington Consensus in East Asia and Rampini's "Chinese century" (Fare storia pieces) argue how China rose inside globalisation's rules.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 19 §3 Russia e Cina" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** An authoritarian Russia and a state-capitalist China offer rival models to the liberal order — the present's scenarios are drawn as a contest of systems.
