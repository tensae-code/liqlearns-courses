# The rise of china and the brics — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 22 §2 A new multipolar set-up: China, the BRICS and the Gulf countries" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The New Silk Road (the chapter's maps) draws China's reach; growth shifts the world's centre while the West debates whether its liberal order is ending.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 16 §§1–2 Le nuove gerarchie della ricchezza; Il boom della Cina" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Wealth's map is redrawn — Stiglitz on the Washington Consensus in East Asia and Rampini's "Chinese century" (Fare storia pieces) argue how China rose inside globalisation's rules.
