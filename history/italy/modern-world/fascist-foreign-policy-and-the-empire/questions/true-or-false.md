# Fascist foreign policy and the empire — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §4 Foreign policy", the "mare nostrum" document states the aim; the Ethiopian war builds the empire whose Libyan memory (La storia allo specchio) and Graziani's record (Del Boca's Storiografia) the course refuses to soften (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 6 §5 La politica estera e l'Impero", Mussolini's proclamation of empire (Fare storia Document) crowns the Ethiopian war; the empire is the regime's proudest claim and its road into Hitler's war (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
