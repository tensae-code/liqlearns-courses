# Fascist foreign policy and the empire — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §4 Foreign policy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The "mare nostrum" document states the aim; the Ethiopian war builds the empire whose Libyan memory (La storia allo specchio) and Graziani's record (Del Boca's Storiografia) the course refuses to soften.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 6 §5 La politica estera e l'Impero" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mussolini's proclamation of empire (Fare storia Document) crowns the Ethiopian war; the empire is the regime's proudest claim and its road into Hitler's war.
