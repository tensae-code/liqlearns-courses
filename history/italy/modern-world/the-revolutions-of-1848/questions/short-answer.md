# The revolutions of 1848 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 10 §4 1848, a turning year for socialist thought" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In the year of the barricades Marx and Engels publish the Communist Manifesto (book study) — the workers' movement gets its programme.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 11 §§8–10 The revolutions of 1848–49; The '48 in France: from the Second Republic to the Second Empire; The '48 in central Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Flaubert's revolutionary Paris (Fare storia) and the liberal-vs-democratic constitutions dossier show 1848 arguing with itself before the empires return.
