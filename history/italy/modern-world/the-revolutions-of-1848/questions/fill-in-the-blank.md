# The revolutions of 1848 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 10 §§1–3 1848, the year of wonders; 1848 in France: from the 'bourgeois monarchy' to the Second Republic; 1848 in the countries of central Europe: the national questions" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Paris, Vienna, Berlin, Budapest and Milan rise in one spring; peoples demand constitutions and nations at once, and the empires survive by dividing them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 10 §4 1848, a turning year for socialist thought" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** In the year of the barricades Marx and Engels publish the Communist Manifesto (book study) — the workers' movement gets its programme.
