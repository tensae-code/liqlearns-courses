# The revolutions of 1848

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The revolutions of 1848" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 10 §§1–3 1848, the year of wonders; 1848 in France: from the 'bourgeois monarchy' to the Second Republic; 1848 in the countries of central Europe: the national questions

Paris, Vienna, Berlin, Budapest and Milan rise in one spring; peoples demand constitutions and nations at once, and the empires survive by dividing them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 3, Cap. 10 §4 1848, a turning year for socialist thought

In the year of the barricades Marx and Engels publish the Communist Manifesto (book study) — the workers' movement gets its programme.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 4, Cap. 11 §§8–10 The revolutions of 1848–49; The '48 in France: from the Second Republic to the Second Empire; The '48 in central Europe

Flaubert's revolutionary Paris (Fare storia) and the liberal-vs-democratic constitutions dossier show 1848 arguing with itself before the empires return.
