# The holocaust

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The holocaust" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 13 §4 The genocide of the Jews

From ghettos to the "final solution" (the unit's Document), Husson's Storiografia traces the road to extermination; Anne Frank's Diary (book study) keeps one hidden girl's voice at the centre of the machinery.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 5 §2 Dall'igiene razziale alle politiche di sterminio

The road runs from racial hygiene and eugenics to extermination policy — Mommsen's Fare storia piece asks how the step to genocide was taken, Hitler's Mein Kampf manifesto states the premise.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 8 §7 La Shoah

The extermination lands are the module's geography; Levi's arrival at the camp and Höss' machine of extermination (Fare storia Documents), Goldhagen against Browning on the killers — testimony and debate in one dossier.

## Say it to your companion

Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
