# The holocaust — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 5 §2 Dall'igiene razziale alle politiche di sterminio" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The road runs from racial hygiene and eugenics to extermination policy — Mommsen's Fare storia piece asks how the step to genocide was taken, Hitler's Mein Kampf manifesto states the premise.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 8 §7 La Shoah" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The extermination lands are the module's geography; Levi's arrival at the camp and Höss' machine of extermination (Fare storia Documents), Goldhagen against Browning on the killers — testimony and debate in one dossier.
