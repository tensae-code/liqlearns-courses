# The holocaust — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §4 The genocide of the Jews" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From ghettos to the "final solution" (the unit's Document), Husson's Storiografia traces the road to extermination; Anne Frank's Diary (book study) keeps one hidden girl's voice at the centre of the machinery.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 5 §2 Dall'igiene razziale alle politiche di sterminio" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The road runs from racial hygiene and eugenics to extermination policy — Mommsen's Fare storia piece asks how the step to genocide was taken, Hitler's Mein Kampf manifesto states the premise.
