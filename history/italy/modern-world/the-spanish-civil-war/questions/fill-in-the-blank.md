# The spanish civil war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 12 §4 The Spanish Civil War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A military rising becomes Europe's rehearsal war — Capa's falling soldier (image study), the women of the war (Storia culturale), Rosselli's Document for Italian antifascism in Spain, and the Caudillo's dictatorship (Pallottini) as its long result.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 5 §9 La guerra civile in Spagna" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Spain becomes Europe's ideological battlefield in arms — the rehearsal whose outcome (a forty-year dictatorship) the democracies' non-intervention helps decide.
