# The spanish civil war — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 3, Cap. 12 §4 The Spanish Civil War — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 5 §9 La guerra civile in Spagna — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) a military rising becomes Europe's rehearsal war — Capa's falling soldier (image study), the women of the war (Storia culturale), Rosselli's Document for Italian antifascism in Spain, and the Caudillo's dictatorship (Pallottini) as its long result
b) Spain becomes Europe's ideological battlefield in arms — the rehearsal whose outcome (a forty-year dictatorship) the democracies' non-intervention helps decide

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
