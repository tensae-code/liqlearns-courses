# Fascism and nazism two dictatorships compared

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 10 §§2–3 The construction of the Third Reich; Nazi totalitarianism

Gleichschaltung, the Nuremberg Laws (Document) and the Führer myth (Kershaw's Storiografia) build a racial totalitarianism; Neumann's Document anatomises the totalitarian state while the Churches' attitude (Rothfels) is weighed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 5 §§3–5 L'ascesa del nazismo; Il consolidamento del potere di Hitler; Il Terzo Reich

Hitler (Personaggi), the Night of the Long Knives (Eventi) and the SS state (Frei's Fare storia piece) build the racial dictatorship; Arendt and Friedrich–Brzezinski (Fare storia) define the totalitarian form it shares and contests with Stalin's.
