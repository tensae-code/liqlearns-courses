# Fascism and nazism two dictatorships compared — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 10 §§2–3 The construction of the Third Reich; Nazi totalitarianism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Gleichschaltung, the Nuremberg Laws (Document) and the Führer myth (Kershaw's Storiografia) build a racial totalitarianism; Neumann's Document anatomises the totalitarian state while the Churches' attitude (Rothfels) is weighed.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 5 §§3–5 L'ascesa del nazismo; Il consolidamento del potere di Hitler; Il Terzo Reich" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hitler (Personaggi), the Night of the Long Knives (Eventi) and the SS state (Frei's Fare storia piece) build the racial dictatorship; Arendt and Friedrich–Brzezinski (Fare storia) define the totalitarian form it shares and contests with Stalin's.
