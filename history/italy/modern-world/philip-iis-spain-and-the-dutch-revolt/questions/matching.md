# Philip iis spain and the dutch revolt — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** L'Impero di Filippo II; La battaglia di Lepanto (Eventi); La guerra nei Paesi Bassi — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) from the Escorial Philip rules a world empire, fights the Dutch revolt and the Ottomans at Lepanto, and launches the Armada against England
b) Philip's Spain wins Lepanto (1571) against the Ottomans and loses the northern Netherlands to revolt — one empire, two seas, opposite fates

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
