# Philip iis spain and the dutch revolt — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From the Escorial Philip rules a world empire, fights the Dutch revolt and the Ottomans at Lepanto, and launches the Armada against England.

**2.** Tell the story of "L'Impero di Filippo II; La battaglia di Lepanto (Eventi); La guerra nei Paesi Bassi" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Philip's Spain wins Lepanto (1571) against the Ottomans and loses the northern Netherlands to revolt — one empire, two seas, opposite fates.
