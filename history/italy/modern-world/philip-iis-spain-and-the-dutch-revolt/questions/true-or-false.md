# Philip iis spain and the dutch revolt — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II", from the Escorial Philip rules a world empire, fights the Dutch revolt and the Ottomans at Lepanto, and launches the Armada against England (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "L'Impero di Filippo II; La battaglia di Lepanto (Eventi); La guerra nei Paesi Bassi", Philip's Spain wins Lepanto (1571) against the Ottomans and loses the northern Netherlands to revolt — one empire, two seas, opposite fates (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
