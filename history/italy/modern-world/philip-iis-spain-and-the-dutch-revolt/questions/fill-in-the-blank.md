# Philip iis spain and the dutch revolt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Spain of Philip II" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From the Escorial Philip rules a world empire, fights the Dutch revolt and the Ottomans at Lepanto, and launches the Armada against England.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "L'Impero di Filippo II; La battaglia di Lepanto (Eventi); La guerra nei Paesi Bassi" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Philip's Spain wins Lepanto (1571) against the Ottomans and loses the northern Netherlands to revolt — one empire, two seas, opposite fates.
