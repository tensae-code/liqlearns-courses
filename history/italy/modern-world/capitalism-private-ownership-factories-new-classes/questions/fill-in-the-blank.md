# Capitalism private ownership factories new classes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 13 §3 The birth of a modern financial system" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Jacob Fugger, banker to emperors (his 1523 reminder to Charles V is the Document), and the Antwerp exchange show capital becoming a power of its own.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 3 §3 The organisational turn: the coming of the factory system; The consequences of the factory system's birth" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Workers leave home for the mill's clock and discipline; child labour (Document: John Aikin) and the Luddite question — did the factory improve or worsen people's lives?
