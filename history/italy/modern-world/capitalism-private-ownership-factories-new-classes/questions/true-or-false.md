# Capitalism private ownership factories new classes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Modulo 3, Cap. 8 §§4–5 The birth of the factory; The expansion of industry in continental Europe", Thompson's making of the English working class (Fare storia) and Engels' report on its condition — the factory creates its own gravedigger class (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 13 §3 The birth of a modern financial system", Jacob Fugger, banker to emperors (his 1523 reminder to Charles V is the Document), and the Antwerp exchange show capital becoming a power of its own (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
