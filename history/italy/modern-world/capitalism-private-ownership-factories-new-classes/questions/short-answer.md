# Capitalism private ownership factories new classes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 3 §3 The organisational turn: the coming of the factory system; The consequences of the factory system's birth" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Workers leave home for the mill's clock and discipline; child labour (Document: John Aikin) and the Luddite question — did the factory improve or worsen people's lives?

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 3, Cap. 8 §§4–5 The birth of the factory; The expansion of industry in continental Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Thompson's making of the English working class (Fare storia) and Engels' report on its condition — the factory creates its own gravedigger class.
