## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 13 §3 The birth of a modern financial system

Jacob Fugger, banker to emperors (his 1523 reminder to Charles V is the Document), and the Antwerp exchange show capital becoming a power of its own.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 2, Cap. 3 §3 The organisational turn: the coming of the factory system; The consequences of the factory system's birth

Workers leave home for the mill's clock and discipline; child labour (Document: John Aikin) and the Luddite question — did the factory improve or worsen people's lives?

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 3, Cap. 8 §§4–5 The birth of the factory; The expansion of industry in continental Europe

Thompson's making of the English working class (Fare storia) and Engels' report on its condition — the factory creates its own gravedigger class.
