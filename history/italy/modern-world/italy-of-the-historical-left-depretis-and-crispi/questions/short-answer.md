# Italy of the historical left depretis and crispi — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 18 §§1–2 The moderate Left of Depretis in power; The authoritarian Left of Crispi" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Depretis' trasformismo absorbs opponents into government; Crispi turns to protectionism, triple alliance and African adventure — Dogali's dead (image study) announce the colonial price.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 21 §§1–7 Liberal Italy; From the Right to the Left; Protectionist economic policy; Foreign policy and colonialism; Socialists and Catholics; Crispi; The end-of-century crisis and the new liberal politics" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trasformismo, the tariff of 1887, Adua (Eventi, 1896) and the Milan cannonades — Franchetti on Sicily's mafia and Turati's founding of the Socialist Party (Fare storia) frame the liberal state's crisis.
