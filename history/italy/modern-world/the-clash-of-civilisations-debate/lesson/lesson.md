# The clash of civilisations debate

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The clash of civilisations debate" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Modulo 17 §1 Uno scontro di civiltà?

Huntington's thesis (a Fare storia piece) is put as a question, not a doctrine — Kepel, Guolo, Buruma–Margalit and Sen argue back across the dossier; the module teaches the debate, not the slogan.

Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
