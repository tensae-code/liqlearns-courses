# Lesson plan — Bandung and the third world

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Bandung and the third world
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Bandung and the third world as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 4, Cap. 15 §5 The political protagonism of the "Third World"** — at Bandung (the chapter's Document and map) Asia and Africa refuse both blocs; Mao's Document answers from Beijing's rival claim to lead the world's countryside — non-alignment becomes a third force
2. **§2 Modulo 10 §10 Il Terzo Mondo: non allineamento e sottosviluppo** — Bandung's manifesto (Fare storia Document) claims a third camp; Fanon's anticolonial violence (Document) argues its price — non-alignment between hope and underdevelopment (the module's keyword is neocolonialism)
