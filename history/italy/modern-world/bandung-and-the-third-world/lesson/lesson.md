## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 15 §5 The political protagonism of the "Third World"

At Bandung (the chapter's Document and map) Asia and Africa refuse both blocs; Mao's Document answers from Beijing's rival claim to lead the world's countryside — non-alignment becomes a third force.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 10 §10 Il Terzo Mondo: non allineamento e sottosviluppo

Bandung's manifesto (Fare storia Document) claims a third camp; Fanon's anticolonial violence (Document) argues its price — non-alignment between hope and underdevelopment (the module's keyword is neocolonialism).
