Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 15 §5 The political protagonism of the "Third World"", at Bandung (the chapter's Document and map) Asia and Africa refuse both blocs; Mao's Document answers from Beijing's rival claim to lead the world's countryside — non-alignment becomes a third force (Sub lesson 1)
