# Bandung and the third world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 15 §5 The political protagonism of the "Third World"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** At Bandung (the chapter's Document and map) Asia and Africa refuse both blocs; Mao's Document answers from Beijing's rival claim to lead the world's countryside — non-alignment becomes a third force.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 10 §10 Il Terzo Mondo: non allineamento e sottosviluppo" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bandung's manifesto (Fare storia Document) claims a third camp; Fanon's anticolonial violence (Document) argues its price — non-alignment between hope and underdevelopment (the module's keyword is neocolonialism).
