# Europe of the great powers 1870 1900 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 15 §§2–4 The France of the Third Republic; The Britain of Gladstone and Disraeli; Austria-Hungary and Russia between nationalism and reaction" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A republic born of defeat survives Boulanger; Britain widens the vote; the eastern empires answer nationalism with reaction — the alliance system's pieces settle into place.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 17 §§5–7 The Republic in France; Liberalism in Great Britain; Russian autocracy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Third Republic survives its birth in defeat, Victoria's Britain (Personaggi: the Queen) reforms by instalments, and Russia's autocrats refuse every instalment.
