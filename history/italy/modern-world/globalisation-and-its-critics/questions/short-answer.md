# Globalisation and its critics — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §§5–7 Economic globalisation; New protagonists on the world economic scene; Latin America in transformation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Supply chains planet-wide lift Asia's new protagonists and transform Latin America, while Stiglitz's book (the chapter's study) and Beck's Document indict globalism's errors — Mandela's inauguration speech (unit Document) opens the same decades from the South.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 15 §§3–5 Economia globale e finanza internazionale; Il governo dell'economia mondiale e la tutela dell'ambiente; I nuovi lavori: flessibilità e delocalizzazione" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Seattle's battle (Eventi) names the opposition; multinationals (keyword), post-Fordism (Focus) and delocalised work remake the economy while its government lags.
