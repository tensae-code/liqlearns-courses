# Lesson plan — Globalisation and its critics

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Globalisation and its critics
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Globalisation and its critics as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 6, Cap. 21 §§5–7 Economic globalisation; New protagonists on the world economic scene; Latin America in transformation** — supply chains planet-wide lift Asia's new protagonists and transform Latin America, while Stiglitz's book (the chapter's study) and Beck's Document indict globalism's errors — Mandela's inauguration speech (unit Document) opens the same decades from the South
2. **§2 Modulo 15 §§3–5 Economia globale e finanza internazionale; Il governo dell'economia mondiale e la tutela dell'ambiente; I nuovi lavori: flessibilità e delocalizzazione** — Seattle's battle (Eventi) names the opposition; multinationals (keyword), post-Fordism (Focus) and delocalised work remake the economy while its government lags
