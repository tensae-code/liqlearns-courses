# The depression in europe and the worlds peripheries — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§4–5 The economic depression in Europe; The crisis in the peripheries of the world system" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Europe answers with deflation, protectionism and dictatorship; in the peripheries the crash breeds Latin American populisms (Zanatta's Storiografia) and Gandhi's salt march (Document) turns India's answer into a march.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 4 §4 Le conseguenze in Europa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Deflation, unemployment and collapsing democracies — the crisis crosses the Atlantic and becomes the dictatorships' recruiting sergeant.
