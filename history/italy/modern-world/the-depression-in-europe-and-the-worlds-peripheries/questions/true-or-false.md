# The depression in europe and the worlds peripheries — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§4–5 The economic depression in Europe; The crisis in the peripheries of the world system", Europe answers with deflation, protectionism and dictatorship; in the peripheries the crash breeds Latin American populisms (Zanatta's Storiografia) and Gandhi's salt march (Document) turns India's answer into a march (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 4 §4 Le conseguenze in Europa", deflation, unemployment and collapsing democracies — the crisis crosses the Atlantic and becomes the dictatorships' recruiting sergeant (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
