# The depression in europe and the worlds peripheries — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§4–5 The economic depression in Europe; The crisis in the peripheries of the world system" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Europe answers with deflation, protectionism and dictatorship; in the peripheries the crash breeds Latin American populisms (Zanatta's Storiografia) and Gandhi's salt march (Document) turns India's answer into a march.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 4 §4 Le conseguenze in Europa" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Deflation, unemployment and collapsing democracies — the crisis crosses the Atlantic and becomes the dictatorships' recruiting sergeant.
