# The depression in europe and the worlds peripheries — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§4–5 The economic depression in Europe; The crisis in the peripheries of the world system — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 4 §4 Le conseguenze in Europa — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Europe answers with deflation, protectionism and dictatorship; in the peripheries the crash breeds Latin American populisms (Zanatta's Storiografia) and Gandhi's salt march (Document) turns India's answer into a march
b) deflation, unemployment and collapsing democracies — the crisis crosses the Atlantic and becomes the dictatorships' recruiting sergeant
