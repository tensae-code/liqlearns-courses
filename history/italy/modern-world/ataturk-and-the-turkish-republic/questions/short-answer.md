# Ataturk and the turkish republic — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 7 §2 La rivoluzione kemalista in Turchia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** From the Ottoman wreck Mustafa Kemal builds a secular republic by decree — alphabet, dress, law and capital all moved at once; the one successful national revolution of the postwar East.
