# The iranian revolution and the iran iraq war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 18 §2 The Middle Eastern powder keg" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Khomeini's revolution (Abrahamian's Storiografia) invents the Islamic republic; Saddam's war on Iran and on the Kurds (Froia's Storiografia, the chapter's maps) burns the decade in the Gulf.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 12 §9 La rivoluzione khomeinista in Iran" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Shah falls to a revolution in God's name; the Islamic republic becomes the Middle East's new pole and the Gulf's eight-year war its first test.
