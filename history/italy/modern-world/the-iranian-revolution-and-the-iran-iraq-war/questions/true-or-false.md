# The iranian revolution and the iran iraq war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 18 §2 The Middle Eastern powder keg", Khomeini's revolution (Abrahamian's Storiografia) invents the Islamic republic; Saddam's war on Iran and on the Kurds (Froia's Storiografia, the chapter's maps) burns the decade in the Gulf (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 12 §9 La rivoluzione khomeinista in Iran", the Shah falls to a revolution in God's name; the Islamic republic becomes the Middle East's new pole and the Gulf's eight-year war its first test (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
