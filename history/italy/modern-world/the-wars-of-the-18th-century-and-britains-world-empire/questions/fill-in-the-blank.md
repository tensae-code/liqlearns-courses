# The wars of the 18th century and britains world empire — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §4 The struggle for hegemony in Europe and the world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Succession wars from Spain to Austria end at Utrecht and Aachen; the Seven Years' War makes Britain mistress of Canada and India and Prussia a great power.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§5–8 The reasons for the wars; The rise of Prussia; Russia from Peter the Great to Catherine II; The results of a hundred years of wars" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Rossbach (Eventi) announces Prussia, Russia becomes a European power, and a century of dynastic war quietly builds the modern state system.
