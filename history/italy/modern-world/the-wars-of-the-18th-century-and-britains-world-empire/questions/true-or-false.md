# The wars of the 18th century and britains world empire — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §4 The struggle for hegemony in Europe and the world", succession wars from Spain to Austria end at Utrecht and Aachen; the Seven Years' War makes Britain mistress of Canada and India and Prussia a great power (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§5–8 The reasons for the wars; The rise of Prussia; Russia from Peter the Great to Catherine II; The results of a hundred years of wars", Rossbach (Eventi) announces Prussia, Russia becomes a European power, and a century of dynastic war quietly builds the modern state system (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
