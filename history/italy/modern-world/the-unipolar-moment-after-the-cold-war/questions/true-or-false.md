# The unipolar moment after the cold war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §1 The unipolar world and the search for a new order", one superpower remains and the Gulf war (the chapter's maps) announces its policing; the search for an order to replace bipolarity begins in victory and hubris (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 14 §7 Gli Stati Uniti: la difficile gestione di una vittoria", the victor finds victory unmanageable — interventions, hubris and the first doubts about the unipolar moment (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
