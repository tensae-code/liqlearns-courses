# The unipolar moment after the cold war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §1 The unipolar world and the search for a new order" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** One superpower remains and the Gulf war (the chapter's maps) announces its policing; the search for an order to replace bipolarity begins in victory and hubris.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 14 §7 Gli Stati Uniti: la difficile gestione di una vittoria" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The victor finds victory unmanageable — interventions, hubris and the first doubts about the unipolar moment.
