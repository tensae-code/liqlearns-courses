# The unipolar moment after the cold war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §1 The unipolar world and the search for a new order" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** One superpower remains and the Gulf war (the chapter's maps) announces its policing; the search for an order to replace bipolarity begins in victory and hubris.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 14 §7 Gli Stati Uniti: la difficile gestione di una vittoria" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The victor finds victory unmanageable — interventions, hubris and the first doubts about the unipolar moment.
