# The eastern question nationalism in the balkans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §4 The difficulties of the Habsburg and Russian empires" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Two multinational empires strain against nationalism; Russia abolishes serfdom (1861) and its peasants' disappointed reaction (Document) shows reform from above's limits.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §7 Crises and conflicts between Europe and the Mediterranean" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Morocco, Bosnia and the Balkan wars strip the Ottoman empire in Europe; Trotsky's Document demands "the Balkans to the Balkan peoples" while the great powers draw the borders anyway.
