# The eastern question nationalism in the balkans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §4 The difficulties of the Habsburg and Russian empires" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Two multinational empires strain against nationalism; Russia abolishes serfdom (1861) and its peasants' disappointed reaction (Document) shows reform from above's limits.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §7 Crises and conflicts between Europe and the Mediterranean" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Morocco, Bosnia and the Balkan wars strip the Ottoman empire in Europe; Trotsky's Document demands "the Balkans to the Balkan peoples" while the great powers draw the borders anyway.
