# The weimar republic

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The weimar republic" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 6 §4 The difficult beginnings of the Weimar Republic

Rosa Luxemburg's Spartacus programme (Document) asks what the League wants; the republic is born between revolution and Freikorps, and Wirsching's Storiografia traces its democratic constitution's origins.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §5 La Germania di Weimar

The Anteprima defends Weimar against hindsight — an advanced party democracy that survived its birth traumas and fell, above all, to the world crisis's political fallout; Schulze's Fare storia piece weighs its difficulties.
