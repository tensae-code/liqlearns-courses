# The weimar republic — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 2, Cap. 6 §4 The difficult beginnings of the Weimar Republic — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §5 La Germania di Weimar — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Rosa Luxemburg's Spartacus programme (Document) asks what the League wants; the republic is born between revolution and Freikorps, and Wirsching's Storiografia traces its democratic constitution's origins
b) the Anteprima defends Weimar against hindsight — an advanced party democracy that survived its birth traumas and fell, above all, to the world crisis's political fallout; Schulze's Fare storia piece weighs its difficulties

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
