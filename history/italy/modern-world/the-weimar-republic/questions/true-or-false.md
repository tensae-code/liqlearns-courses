# The weimar republic — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §4 The difficult beginnings of the Weimar Republic", Rosa Luxemburg's Spartacus programme (Document) asks what the League wants; the republic is born between revolution and Freikorps, and Wirsching's Storiografia traces its democratic constitution's origins (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §5 La Germania di Weimar", the Anteprima defends Weimar against hindsight — an advanced party democracy that survived its birth traumas and fell, above all, to the world crisis's political fallout; Schulze's Fare storia piece weighs its difficulties (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
