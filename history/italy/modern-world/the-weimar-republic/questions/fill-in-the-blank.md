# The weimar republic — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §4 The difficult beginnings of the Weimar Republic" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rosa Luxemburg's Spartacus programme (Document) asks what the League wants; the republic is born between revolution and Freikorps, and Wirsching's Storiografia traces its democratic constitution's origins.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 2 §5 La Germania di Weimar" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Anteprima defends Weimar against hindsight — an advanced party democracy that survived its birth traumas and fell, above all, to the world crisis's political fallout; Schulze's Fare storia piece weighs its difficulties.
