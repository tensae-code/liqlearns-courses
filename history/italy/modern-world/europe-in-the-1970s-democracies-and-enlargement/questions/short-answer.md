# Europe in the 1970s democracies and enlargement — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 17 §4 Europe between détente, political crises and the enlargement of the EEC" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece, Spain and Portugal exit dictatorship — Panagulis' fight for Greek democracy is the chapter's document — and knock on the Community's door; enlargement becomes democratisation's reward.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 12 §§4–6 I negoziati sul disarmo; Mutamenti politici in Europa occidentale; Integrazione europea e nuove democrazie" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Disarmament talks run beside democratisation — Greece, Spain and Portugal join the West's club and the Community enlarges to receive them.
