# Europe in the 1970s democracies and enlargement — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 17 §4 Europe between détente, political crises and the enlargement of the EEC", Greece, Spain and Portugal exit dictatorship — Panagulis' fight for Greek democracy is the chapter's document — and knock on the Community's door; enlargement becomes democratisation's reward (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 12 §§4–6 I negoziati sul disarmo; Mutamenti politici in Europa occidentale; Integrazione europea e nuove democrazie", disarmament talks run beside democratisation — Greece, Spain and Portugal join the West's club and the Community enlarges to receive them (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
