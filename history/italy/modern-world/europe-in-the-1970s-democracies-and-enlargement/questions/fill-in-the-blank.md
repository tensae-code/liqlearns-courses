# Europe in the 1970s democracies and enlargement — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 17 §4 Europe between détente, political crises and the enlargement of the EEC" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece, Spain and Portugal exit dictatorship — Panagulis' fight for Greek democracy is the chapter's document — and knock on the Community's door; enlargement becomes democratisation's reward.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 12 §§4–6 I negoziati sul disarmo; Mutamenti politici in Europa occidentale; Integrazione europea e nuove democrazie" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Disarmament talks run beside democratisation — Greece, Spain and Portugal join the West's club and the Community enlarges to receive them.
