# Europe in the 1970s democracies and enlargement — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 17 §4 Europe between détente, political crises and the enlargement of the EEC — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 12 §§4–6 I negoziati sul disarmo; Mutamenti politici in Europa occidentale; Integrazione europea e nuove democrazie — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Greece, Spain and Portugal exit dictatorship — Panagulis' fight for Greek democracy is the chapter's document — and knock on the Community's door; enlargement becomes democratisation's reward
b) disarmament talks run beside democratisation — Greece, Spain and Portugal join the West's club and the Community enlarges to receive them

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
