# Republic and reign of terror — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§5–7 The popular and democratic revolution; The Jacobin dictatorship; Revolutionary continuity and defensive stabilisation — a) (Sub lesson 3)
**2.** Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§4–5 1792, a year of turning; The Republic put to the test — b) (Sub lesson 1)

Meanings:
a) Robespierre (Personaggi) joins virtue to terror (Fare storia: his own words; Furet on the Jacobin model) until the revolution devours its children and seeks order
b) war, the fall of the monarchy and the September massacres push the revolution toward the Republic; the guillotine (cultural history) becomes its emblem

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
