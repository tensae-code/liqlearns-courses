# Republic and reign of terror — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§4–5 1792, a year of turning; The Republic put to the test" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** War, the fall of the monarchy and the September massacres push the revolution toward the Republic; the guillotine (cultural history) becomes its emblem.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§6–7 The Jacobin dictatorship and the Terror; The second republican phase" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Robespierre's Committee of Public Safety governs by terror until Thermidor; Olympe de Gouges' lost revolution for women (cultural history) marks the revolution's limits.
