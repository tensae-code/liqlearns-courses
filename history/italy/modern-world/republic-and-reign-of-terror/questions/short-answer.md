# Republic and reign of terror — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§6–7 The Jacobin dictatorship and the Terror; The second republican phase" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Robespierre's Committee of Public Safety governs by terror until Thermidor; Olympe de Gouges' lost revolution for women (cultural history) marks the revolution's limits.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§5–7 The popular and democratic revolution; The Jacobin dictatorship; Revolutionary continuity and defensive stabilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Robespierre (Personaggi) joins virtue to terror (Fare storia: his own words; Furet on the Jacobin model) until the revolution devours its children and seeks order.
