# Republic and reign of terror — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§5–7 The popular and democratic revolution; The Jacobin dictatorship; Revolutionary continuity and defensive stabilisation", Robespierre (Personaggi) joins virtue to terror (Fare storia: his own words; Furet on the Jacobin model) until the revolution devours its children and seeks order (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§4–5 1792, a year of turning; The Republic put to the test", war, the fall of the monarchy and the September massacres push the revolution toward the Republic; the guillotine (cultural history) becomes its emblem (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
