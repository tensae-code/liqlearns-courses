# Republic and reign of terror

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Republic and reign of terror" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§4–5 1792, a year of turning; The Republic put to the test

War, the fall of the monarchy and the September massacres push the revolution toward the Republic; the guillotine (cultural history) becomes its emblem.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§6–7 The Jacobin dictatorship and the Terror; The second republican phase

Robespierre's Committee of Public Safety governs by terror until Thermidor; Olympe de Gouges' lost revolution for women (cultural history) marks the revolution's limits.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§5–7 The popular and democratic revolution; The Jacobin dictatorship; Revolutionary continuity and defensive stabilisation

Robespierre (Personaggi) joins virtue to terror (Fare storia: his own words; Furet on the Jacobin model) until the revolution devours its children and seeks order.
