# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §3 The Napoleonic empire between triumphs and defeats" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Austerlitz to the Spanish ulcer (Goya's Third of May as image study) and Russia's snows; the Haitian revolution (La storia allo specchio) shows the empire's other face across the Atlantic.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§8–14 The revolutionary expansion; The conquest of Italy and the Jacobin republics; The coup and Bonaparte's authoritarian turn; From Consulate to Empire; Napoleonic France and Europe; The collapse of the Empire; Revolution and Empire: a double modernity" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Italy gets its first modern republics from French bayonets (Fare storia: Cuoco's 'passive revolution'); Austerlitz (Eventi) crowns the empire that Russia and Leipzig will bury.
