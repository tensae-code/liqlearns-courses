# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §§1–2 The rapid rise of General Bonaparte; From the Consulate to the Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Italy's sister republics and the Egyptian expedition make Bonaparte; the 18 Brumaire coup, the Consulate and the Civil Code (Document: the family articles) turn the general into a state-builder.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §3 The Napoleonic empire between triumphs and defeats" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Austerlitz to the Spanish ulcer (Goya's Third of May as image study) and Russia's snows; the Haitian revolution (La storia allo specchio) shows the empire's other face across the Atlantic.
