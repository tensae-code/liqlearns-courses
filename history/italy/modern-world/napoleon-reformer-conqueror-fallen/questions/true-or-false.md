# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§8–14 The revolutionary expansion; The conquest of Italy and the Jacobin republics; The coup and Bonaparte's authoritarian turn; From Consulate to Empire; Napoleonic France and Europe; The collapse of the Empire; Revolution and Empire: a double modernity", Italy gets its first modern republics from French bayonets (Fare storia: Cuoco's 'passive revolution'); Austerlitz (Eventi) crowns the empire that Russia and Leipzig will bury (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §§1–2 The rapid rise of General Bonaparte; From the Consulate to the Empire", Italy's sister republics and the Egyptian expedition make Bonaparte; the 18 Brumaire coup, the Consulate and the Civil Code (Document: the family articles) turn the general into a state-builder (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
