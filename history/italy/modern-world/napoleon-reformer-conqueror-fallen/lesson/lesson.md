# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 6 §§1–2 The rapid rise of General Bonaparte; From the Consulate to the Empire

Italy's sister republics and the Egyptian expedition make Bonaparte; the 18 Brumaire coup, the Consulate and the Civil Code (Document: the family articles) turn the general into a state-builder.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 2, Cap. 6 §3 The Napoleonic empire between triumphs and defeats

Austerlitz to the Spanish ulcer (Goya's Third of May as image study) and Russia's snows; the Haitian revolution (La storia allo specchio) shows the empire's other face across the Atlantic.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§8–14 The revolutionary expansion; The conquest of Italy and the Jacobin republics; The coup and Bonaparte's authoritarian turn; From Consulate to Empire; Napoleonic France and Europe; The collapse of the Empire; Revolution and Empire: a double modernity

Italy gets its first modern republics from French bayonets (Fare storia: Cuoco's 'passive revolution'); Austerlitz (Eventi) crowns the empire that Russia and Leipzig will bury.
