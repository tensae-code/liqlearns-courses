# The bolshevik regime and the civil war — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§3–4 The new Bolshevik regime; The civil war and the centrifugal pushes in the Soviet state — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §10 Guerra civile e dittatura — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the first revolutionary decrees and the literacy posters ("knowledge makes you free") build a new order while civil war consumes the old empire — Ekaterinburg, where the tsar's family is shot, is its last frontier
b) the Anteprima's essay follows the experiment's turn — a movement announcing liberty and equality breaks with civil society over the collectivist project and hardens into a pitilessly authoritarian regime that still fascinates revolutionaries worldwide

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
