# The bolshevik regime and the civil war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§3–4 The new Bolshevik regime; The civil war and the centrifugal pushes in the Soviet state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The first revolutionary decrees and the literacy posters ("knowledge makes you free") build a new order while civil war consumes the old empire — Ekaterinburg, where the tsar's family is shot, is its last frontier.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §10 Guerra civile e dittatura" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Anteprima's essay follows the experiment's turn — a movement announcing liberty and equality breaks with civil society over the collectivist project and hardens into a pitilessly authoritarian regime that still fascinates revolutionaries worldwide.
