# The bolshevik regime and the civil war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§3–4 The new Bolshevik regime; The civil war and the centrifugal pushes in the Soviet state", the first revolutionary decrees and the literacy posters ("knowledge makes you free") build a new order while civil war consumes the old empire — Ekaterinburg, where the tsar's family is shot, is its last frontier (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §10 Guerra civile e dittatura", the Anteprima's essay follows the experiment's turn — a movement announcing liberty and equality breaks with civil society over the collectivist project and hardens into a pitilessly authoritarian regime that still fascinates revolutionaries worldwide (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
