# Mass society and its new politics — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 5, Cap. 16 §§4–8 Hygiene, medicine and demography; Mass society and the new middle classes; The nationalisation of the masses: school, army and universal suffrage; Parties and unions; Reformists and revolutionaries: the Second International" turns on this: __________.
