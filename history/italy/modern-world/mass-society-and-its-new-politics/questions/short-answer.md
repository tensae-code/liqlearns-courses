# Mass society and its new politics — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 5, Cap. 16 §§4–8 Hygiene, medicine and demography; Mass society and the new middle classes; The nationalisation of the masses: school, army and universal suffrage; Parties and unions; Reformists and revolutionaries: the Second International" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Le Bon's crowd psychology and Mosse's new political style (Fare storia) describe a politics of masses that parties, unions and states all learn to organise.
