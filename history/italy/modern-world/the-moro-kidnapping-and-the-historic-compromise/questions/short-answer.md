# The moro kidnapping and the historic compromise — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 20 §3 "Red" terrorism, the historic compromise and the Moro kidnapping" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Berlinguer's "democratic alternative" (Document) seeks a Communist road into government; the Red Brigades answer by kidnapping and killing Aldo Moro (1978) — the compromise dies with him.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 13 §§8–9 Violenza politica e crisi economica; Terrorismo e «solidarietà nazionale»" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Berlinguer's historic compromise (Fare storia Document) seeks safety in unity; Della Porta and Rossi anatomise the Italian terrorisms that answer it, and the Moro case (Focus) is their summit.
