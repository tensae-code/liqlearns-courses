# The moro kidnapping and the historic compromise — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 20 §3 "Red" terrorism, the historic compromise and the Moro kidnapping", Berlinguer's "democratic alternative" (Document) seeks a Communist road into government; the Red Brigades answer by kidnapping and killing Aldo Moro (1978) — the compromise dies with him (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
