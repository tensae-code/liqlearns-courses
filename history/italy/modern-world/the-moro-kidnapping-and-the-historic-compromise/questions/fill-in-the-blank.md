# The moro kidnapping and the historic compromise — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 20 §3 "Red" terrorism, the historic compromise and the Moro kidnapping" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Berlinguer's "democratic alternative" (Document) seeks a Communist road into government; the Red Brigades answer by kidnapping and killing Aldo Moro (1978) — the compromise dies with him.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 13 §§8–9 Violenza politica e crisi economica; Terrorismo e «solidarietà nazionale»" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Berlinguer's historic compromise (Fare storia Document) seeks safety in unity; Della Porta and Rossi anatomise the Italian terrorisms that answer it, and the Moro case (Focus) is their summit.
