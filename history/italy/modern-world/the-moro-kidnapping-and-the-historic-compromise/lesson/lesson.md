## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 5, Cap. 20 §3 "Red" terrorism, the historic compromise and the Moro kidnapping

Berlinguer's "democratic alternative" (Document) seeks a Communist road into government; the Red Brigades answer by kidnapping and killing Aldo Moro (1978) — the compromise dies with him.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 13 §§8–9 Violenza politica e crisi economica; Terrorismo e «solidarietà nazionale»

Berlinguer's historic compromise (Fare storia Document) seeks safety in unity; Della Porta and Rossi anatomise the Italian terrorisms that answer it, and the Moro case (Focus) is their summit.
