# The european union in crisis — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 6, Cap. 22 §4 The difficult road of the European Union", debt crisis, migration and Brexit (the volume's CLIL closing) test the Union built in the nineties; the chapter asks whether integration can survive its own success (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 19 §4 L'Europa e la crisi del debito", the debt crisis pits North against South inside the euro; the Union built to end crises becomes one (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
