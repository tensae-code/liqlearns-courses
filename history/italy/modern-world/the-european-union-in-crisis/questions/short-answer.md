# The european union in crisis — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 6, Cap. 22 §4 The difficult road of the European Union" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Debt crisis, migration and Brexit (the volume's CLIL closing) test the Union built in the nineties; the chapter asks whether integration can survive its own success.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 19 §4 L'Europa e la crisi del debito" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The debt crisis pits North against South inside the euro; the Union built to end crises becomes one.
