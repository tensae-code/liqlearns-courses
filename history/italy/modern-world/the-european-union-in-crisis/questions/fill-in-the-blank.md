# The european union in crisis — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 22 §4 The difficult road of the European Union" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Debt crisis, migration and Brexit (the volume's CLIL closing) test the Union built in the nineties; the chapter asks whether integration can survive its own success.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 19 §4 L'Europa e la crisi del debito" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The debt crisis pits North against South inside the euro; the Union built to end crises becomes one.
