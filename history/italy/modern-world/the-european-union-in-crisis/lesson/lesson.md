Debt crisis, migration and Brexit (the volume's CLIL closing) test the Union built in the nineties; the chapter asks whether integration can survive its own success.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 19 §4 L'Europa e la crisi del debito

The debt crisis pits North against South inside the euro; the Union built to end crises becomes one.
