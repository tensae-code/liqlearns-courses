# The new deal — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §3 The United States toward the exit from the crisis: the laboratory of the New Deal" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Roosevelt's fireside chats (Storia culturale) and his New Deal speech (Document) sell a programme of banks, works and the Tennessee Valley; Steinbeck's Furore (book study) tells the Dust Bowl's answer.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 4 §§5–6 Roosevelt e il New Deal; L'intervento dello Stato in economia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Roosevelt (Personaggi) answers with banks, works and the Tennessee Valley (Focus); Steinbeck's migrant itinerary and Berle on the New Deal's social economy (Fare storia) give the experiment its voices — the state enters the economy to stay.
