# The new deal — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §3 The United States toward the exit from the crisis: the laboratory of the New Deal", Roosevelt's fireside chats (Storia culturale) and his New Deal speech (Document) sell a programme of banks, works and the Tennessee Valley; Steinbeck's Furore (book study) tells the Dust Bowl's answer (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 4 §§5–6 Roosevelt e il New Deal; L'intervento dello Stato in economia", Roosevelt (Personaggi) answers with banks, works and the Tennessee Valley (Focus); Steinbeck's migrant itinerary and Berle on the New Deal's social economy (Fare storia) give the experiment its voices — the state enters the economy to stay (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
