# The new deal

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The new deal" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 9 §3 The United States toward the exit from the crisis: the laboratory of the New Deal

Roosevelt's fireside chats (Storia culturale) and his New Deal speech (Document) sell a programme of banks, works and the Tennessee Valley; Steinbeck's Furore (book study) tells the Dust Bowl's answer.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 4 §§5–6 Roosevelt e il New Deal; L'intervento dello Stato in economia

Roosevelt (Personaggi) answers with banks, works and the Tennessee Valley (Focus); Steinbeck's migrant itinerary and Berle on the New Deal's social economy (Fare storia) give the experiment its voices — the state enters the economy to stay.
