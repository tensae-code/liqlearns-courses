# Imperialism and the partition of the world — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §1 The age of the imperialisms", in one generation the European empires swallow most of the planet (the chapter's map of the colonial empires), and the course asks what drove the partition — markets, strategy, prestige or racism (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Europeans conquer the world", in one generation Europe seizes most of the planet; Hobson and the course ask what economic engine drove imperialism (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
