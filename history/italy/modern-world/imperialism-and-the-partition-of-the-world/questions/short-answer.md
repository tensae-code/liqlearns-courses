# Imperialism and the partition of the world — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 19 The European imperialism (the new colonialism; The conquest of Africa; The Boer Wars; The conquest of Asia; The Europeans in China; Colonial domination)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Betts and Reinhard (Fare storia) price colonialism's causes and exploitation; Kipling's White Man's Burden and Twain's Congo answer Conrad across the dossier.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §1 The age of the imperialisms" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** In one generation the European empires swallow most of the planet (the chapter's map of the colonial empires), and the course asks what drove the partition — markets, strategy, prestige or racism.
