# Imperialism and the partition of the world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §1 The Europeans conquer the world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In one generation Europe seizes most of the planet; Hobson and the course ask what economic engine drove imperialism.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 6, Cap. 19 The European imperialism (the new colonialism; The conquest of Africa; The Boer Wars; The conquest of Asia; The Europeans in China; Colonial domination)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Betts and Reinhard (Fare storia) price colonialism's causes and exploitation; Kipling's White Man's Burden and Twain's Congo answer Conrad across the dossier.
