# Effects of industrialisation cities classes empires — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §3 The social transformations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cities swell, a mass working class and a new middle class form, and childhood itself is re-divided by school and factory (image study).

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §3 Toward mass society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Cities, department stores (Zola's novel as the book study), Fordism and the crowd — "sovereign of the modern age" in the opening document — remake everyday life between the 19th and 20th centuries.
