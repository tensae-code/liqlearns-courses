# Effects of industrialisation cities classes empires — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§1, 4 Britain between industrialisation and the workers' question; Cities and countryside change their face" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chartist petitions (Documents) demand the vote for the factory class; railways and the Irish famine redraw town and country alike.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 16 §3 The social transformations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cities swell, a mass working class and a new middle class form, and childhood itself is re-divided by school and factory (image study).
