# The italian economic miracle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 19 §3 The economic miracle" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fridges (Paris' Storiografia makes one the boom's symbol), televisions (Alfieri) and internal migrations from South to North remake Italy in a decade; Barbagallo's Cassa per il Mezzogiorno (unit Storiografia) measures the South's half-share.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 13 §6 Il «miracolo economico»" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Motorways, mass media and the robotic factory (Focus boxes) remake Italy; the miracle's geography — North industrialising, South emigrating — is its permanent question.
