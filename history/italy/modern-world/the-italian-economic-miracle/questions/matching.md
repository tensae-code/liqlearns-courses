# The italian economic miracle — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 5, Cap. 19 §3 The economic miracle — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 13 §6 Il «miracolo economico» — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) fridges (Paris' Storiografia makes one the boom's symbol), televisions (Alfieri) and internal migrations from South to North remake Italy in a decade; Barbagallo's Cassa per il Mezzogiorno (unit Storiografia) measures the South's half-share
b) motorways, mass media and the robotic factory (Focus boxes) remake Italy; the miracle's geography — North industrialising, South emigrating — is its permanent question

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
