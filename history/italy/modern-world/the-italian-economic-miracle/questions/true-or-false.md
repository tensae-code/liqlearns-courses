# The italian economic miracle — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 19 §3 The economic miracle", fridges (Paris' Storiografia makes one the boom's symbol), televisions (Alfieri) and internal migrations from South to North remake Italy in a decade; Barbagallo's Cassa per il Mezzogiorno (unit Storiografia) measures the South's half-share (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 13 §6 Il «miracolo economico»", motorways, mass media and the robotic factory (Focus boxes) remake Italy; the miracle's geography — North industrialising, South emigrating — is its permanent question (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
