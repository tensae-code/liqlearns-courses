# Nationalism the feeling that made nation states — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Storici a confronto and Laboratorio storia strands: 'How is the idea of a nation born?' (Gellner vs Smith) and 'The libertadores'" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Historians argue whether nations are invented or rooted while students retrace Bolívar's generation — nationalism studied as a question, not a slogan.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 10 §§2–3 Romanticism; Nation and nationalisms" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Byron (Personaggi) dies for Greece; Fichte and Mazzini (Fare storia documents) give the nation its Romantic scriptures.
