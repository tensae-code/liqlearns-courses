# Nationalism the feeling that made nation states — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Storici a confronto and Laboratorio storia strands: 'How is the idea of a nation born?' (Gellner vs Smith) and 'The libertadores'" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Historians argue whether nations are invented or rooted while students retrace Bolívar's generation — nationalism studied as a question, not a slogan.

**Answer:** Byron (Personaggi) dies for Greece; Fichte and Mazzini (Fare storia documents) give the nation its Romantic scriptures.
