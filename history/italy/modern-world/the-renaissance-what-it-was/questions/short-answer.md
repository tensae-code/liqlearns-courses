# The renaissance what it was — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §1 Humanism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Petrarch's heirs hunt lost Latin manuscripts — 'I found Quintilian, still safe and sound' — and put man, not only God, at the centre of study.

**2.** Tell the story of "Cultura classica e Umanesimo; La nuova pedagogia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Humanists recover the classics and reinvent education itself — the child, not only the scholar, is reimagined.
