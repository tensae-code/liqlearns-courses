# The renaissance what it was — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §1 Humanism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Petrarch's heirs hunt lost Latin manuscripts — 'I found Quintilian, still safe and sound' — and put man, not only God, at the centre of study.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Cultura classica e Umanesimo; La nuova pedagogia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Humanists recover the classics and reinvent education itself — the child, not only the scholar, is reimagined.
