# The renaissance what it was — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §1 Humanism", Petrarch's heirs hunt lost Latin manuscripts — 'I found Quintilian, still safe and sound' — and put man, not only God, at the centre of study (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Cultura classica e Umanesimo; La nuova pedagogia", humanists recover the classics and reinvent education itself — the child, not only the scholar, is reimagined (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
