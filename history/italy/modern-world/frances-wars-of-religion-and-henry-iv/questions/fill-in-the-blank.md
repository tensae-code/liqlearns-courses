# Frances wars of religion and henry iv — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §3 The wars of religion and the consolidation of the monarchy in France" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Catholics and Huguenots massacre each other from St Bartholomew's Day (1572) until Henry IV converts, issues the Edict of Nantes (1598, Document) and rebuilds the crown.
