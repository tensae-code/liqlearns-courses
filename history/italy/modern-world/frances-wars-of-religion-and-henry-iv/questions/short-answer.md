# Frances wars of religion and henry iv — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §3 The wars of religion and the consolidation of the monarchy in France" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Catholics and Huguenots massacre each other from St Bartholomew's Day (1572) until Henry IV converts, issues the Edict of Nantes (1598, Document) and rebuilds the crown.
