Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 17 §3 The wars of religion and the consolidation of the monarchy in France", Catholics and Huguenots massacre each other from St Bartholomew's Day (1572) until Henry IV converts, issues the Edict of Nantes (1598, Document) and rebuilds the crown
