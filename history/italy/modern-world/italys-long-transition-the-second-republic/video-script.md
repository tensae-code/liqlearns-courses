This lesson tells one story: Italys long transition the second republic.

After Tangentopoli Italy rewrites its party system without settling it; the chapter's document is an augury for the new millennium's Italy — a transition that becomes a condition.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
