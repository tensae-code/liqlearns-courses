# Italys long transition the second republic — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §8 The long Italian transition" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Tangentopoli Italy rewrites its party system without settling it; the chapter's document is an augury for the new millennium's Italy — a transition that becomes a condition.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 18 §§1–2 Una difficile transizione; La "rivoluzione maggioritaria"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tangentopoli (Focus) clears the old parties; Berlusconi's "discesa in campo" (Fare storia Document), Pasquino on the majoritarian revolution and Orsina defining "berlusconismo" argue what replaced them.
