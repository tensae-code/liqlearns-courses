# Italys long transition the second republic — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §8 The long Italian transition", after Tangentopoli Italy rewrites its party system without settling it; the chapter's document is an augury for the new millennium's Italy — a transition that becomes a condition (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 18 §§1–2 Una difficile transizione; La "rivoluzione maggioritaria"", Tangentopoli (Focus) clears the old parties; Berlusconi's "discesa in campo" (Fare storia Document), Pasquino on the majoritarian revolution and Orsina defining "berlusconismo" argue what replaced them (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
