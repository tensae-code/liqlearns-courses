# The thirty years war and westphalia — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 6, Cap. 19 §1 The Thirty Years' War — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** La crisi del '600: la guerra dei Trent'anni; Ferdinando II; L'intervento della Francia; La popolazione civile e la guerra — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) a Bohemian revolt (1618) becomes Germany's catastrophe — Grimmelshausen's testimony opens the chapter — until Westphalia (1648) redraws Europe on the sovereignty of states
b) Bohemia's revolt becomes Europe's thirty-year catastrophe; civilians starve between armies until France's intervention tips the war toward Westphalia

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
