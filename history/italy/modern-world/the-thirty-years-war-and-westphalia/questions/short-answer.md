# The thirty years war and westphalia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 6, Cap. 19 §1 The Thirty Years' War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A Bohemian revolt (1618) becomes Germany's catastrophe — Grimmelshausen's testimony opens the chapter — until Westphalia (1648) redraws Europe on the sovereignty of states.

**2.** Tell the story of "La crisi del '600: la guerra dei Trent'anni; Ferdinando II; L'intervento della Francia; La popolazione civile e la guerra" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bohemia's revolt becomes Europe's thirty-year catastrophe; civilians starve between armies until France's intervention tips the war toward Westphalia.
