# The thirty years war and westphalia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 19 §1 The Thirty Years' War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A Bohemian revolt (1618) becomes Germany's catastrophe — Grimmelshausen's testimony opens the chapter — until Westphalia (1648) redraws Europe on the sovereignty of states.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La crisi del '600: la guerra dei Trent'anni; Ferdinando II; L'intervento della Francia; La popolazione civile e la guerra" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bohemia's revolt becomes Europe's thirty-year catastrophe; civilians starve between armies until France's intervention tips the war toward Westphalia.
