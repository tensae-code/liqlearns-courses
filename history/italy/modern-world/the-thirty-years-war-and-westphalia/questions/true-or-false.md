# The thirty years war and westphalia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 6, Cap. 19 §1 The Thirty Years' War", a Bohemian revolt (1618) becomes Germany's catastrophe — Grimmelshausen's testimony opens the chapter — until Westphalia (1648) redraws Europe on the sovereignty of states (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La crisi del '600: la guerra dei Trent'anni; Ferdinando II; L'intervento della Francia; La popolazione civile e la guerra", Bohemia's revolt becomes Europe's thirty-year catastrophe; civilians starve between armies until France's intervention tips the war toward Westphalia (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
