## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 6, Cap. 19 §1 The Thirty Years' War

A Bohemian revolt (1618) becomes Germany's catastrophe — Grimmelshausen's testimony opens the chapter — until Westphalia (1648) redraws Europe on the sovereignty of states.

## Sub lesson 2 — La crisi del '600: la guerra dei Trent'anni; Ferdinando II; L'intervento della Francia; La popolazione civile e la guerra

Bohemia's revolt becomes Europe's thirty-year catastrophe; civilians starve between armies until France's intervention tips the war toward Westphalia.
