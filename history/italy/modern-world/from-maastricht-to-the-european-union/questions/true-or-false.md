Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §4 Europe after the Cold War: still more integration", Maastricht turns the Community into a Union with a single currency project (the chapter's maps of members and candidates); Tulli's Storiografia (unit) weighs the Euro-Atlantic relations that frame it (Sub lesson 1)
