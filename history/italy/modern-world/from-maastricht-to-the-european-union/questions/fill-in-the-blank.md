# From maastricht to the european union — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §4 Europe after the Cold War: still more integration" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Maastricht turns the Community into a Union with a single currency project (the chapter's maps of members and candidates); Tulli's Storiografia (unit) weighs the Euro-Atlantic relations that frame it.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 14 §§8–9 Dalla Cee all'Unione europea; Il difficile cammino dell'Unione allargata" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Maastricht builds the Union and enlargement carries it east; European citizenship (the module's Civica box) is the promise the difficult road must keep.
