# From maastricht to the european union — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 6, Cap. 21 §4 Europe after the Cold War: still more integration" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Maastricht turns the Community into a Union with a single currency project (the chapter's maps of members and candidates); Tulli's Storiografia (unit) weighs the Euro-Atlantic relations that frame it.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 14 §§8–9 Dalla Cee all'Unione europea; Il difficile cammino dell'Unione allargata" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Maastricht builds the Union and enlargement carries it east; European citizenship (the module's Civica box) is the promise the difficult road must keep.
