## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 12 §3 The discovery of America

Columbus sails west for Castile in 1492 and meets a continent Europe did not know; the papal bull Inter Caetera and the Spain–Portugal contest divide the new world between them.

## Sub lesson 2 — I viaggi di Cristoforo Colombo; La scoperta dell'America (Eventi); L'età delle scoperte geografiche

Columbus' four voyages and the generation after him turn a landfall into a mapped new world.
