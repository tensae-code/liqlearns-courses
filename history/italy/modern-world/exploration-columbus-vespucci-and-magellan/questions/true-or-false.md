# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §3 The discovery of America", Columbus sails west for Castile in 1492 and meets a continent Europe did not know; the papal bull Inter Caetera and the Spain–Portugal contest divide the new world between them (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "I viaggi di Cristoforo Colombo; La scoperta dell'America (Eventi); L'età delle scoperte geografiche", Columbus' four voyages and the generation after him turn a landfall into a mapped new world (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
