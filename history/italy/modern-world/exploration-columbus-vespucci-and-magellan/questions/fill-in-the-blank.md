# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §3 The discovery of America" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Columbus sails west for Castile in 1492 and meets a continent Europe did not know; the papal bull Inter Caetera and the Spain–Portugal contest divide the new world between them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "I viaggi di Cristoforo Colombo; La scoperta dell'America (Eventi); L'età delle scoperte geografiche" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Columbus' four voyages and the generation after him turn a landfall into a mapped new world.
