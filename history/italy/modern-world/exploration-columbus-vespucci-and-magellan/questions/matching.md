# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 4, Cap. 12 §3 The discovery of America — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** I viaggi di Cristoforo Colombo; La scoperta dell'America (Eventi); L'età delle scoperte geografiche — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Columbus sails west for Castile in 1492 and meets a continent Europe did not know; the papal bull Inter Caetera and the Spain–Portugal contest divide the new world between them
b) Columbus' four voyages and the generation after him turn a landfall into a mapped new world

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
