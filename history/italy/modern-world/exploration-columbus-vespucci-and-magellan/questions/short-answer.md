# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §3 The discovery of America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Columbus sails west for Castile in 1492 and meets a continent Europe did not know; the papal bull Inter Caetera and the Spain–Portugal contest divide the new world between them.

**2.** Tell the story of "I viaggi di Cristoforo Colombo; La scoperta dell'America (Eventi); L'età delle scoperte geografiche" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Columbus' four voyages and the generation after him turn a landfall into a mapped new world.
