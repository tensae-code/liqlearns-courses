# The great ideologies of the 19th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 10 §§1, 4–5 The completion of the modern state; The great ideologies of the '800: liberalism and democracy; Liberal Catholicism and social Catholicism" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Constant's two liberties and Mill's praise of freedom (Fare storia) against the Church's counter-proposals — the century argues over what a citizen is.
