# Italy after the great war the biennio rosso — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§1–2 The postwar crisis; The "red two years"", inflation, unemployment and land hunger (Cordova's Storiografia on Calabria's occupations) push workers and peasants to strikes and factory occupations — the liberal state watches its authority drain away (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 3 §§1–2 Le tensioni del dopoguerra; La crisi politica e il "biennio rosso"", the liberal ruling class's crisis (Sabbatucci's Fare storia piece) meets factory occupations and land leagues; Tasca's piece records the socialists' defeat inside their own strong season (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
