# Italy after the great war the biennio rosso — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§1–2 The postwar crisis; The "red two years"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Inflation, unemployment and land hunger (Cordova's Storiografia on Calabria's occupations) push workers and peasants to strikes and factory occupations — the liberal state watches its authority drain away.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 3 §§1–2 Le tensioni del dopoguerra; La crisi politica e il "biennio rosso"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The liberal ruling class's crisis (Sabbatucci's Fare storia piece) meets factory occupations and land leagues; Tasca's piece records the socialists' defeat inside their own strong season.
