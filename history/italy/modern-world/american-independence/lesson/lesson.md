# American independence

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "American independence" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§1–2 The American colonies and their relations with the mother country; The war of American independence

Taxed without representation, the thirteen colonies rebel; Paine's pamphlet (Document) argues independence is both convenient and indispensable.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 2, Cap. 4 §3 The birth of the United States of America

A federal constitution balances centre and states (Document: Hamilton), and the Bill of Rights writes the revolution's promises into law.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 3, Cap. 6 The birth of the United States (the British colonies in North America; A revolution for independence; The Constitution and American democracy)

Jefferson (Personaggi), the Declaration and the first ten amendments (Fare storia documents) build a republic whose equality idea Wood's essay interrogates.

Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
