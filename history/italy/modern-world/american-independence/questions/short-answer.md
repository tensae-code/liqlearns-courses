# American independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §3 The birth of the United States of America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A federal constitution balances centre and states (Document: Hamilton), and the Bill of Rights writes the revolution's promises into law.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 3, Cap. 6 The birth of the United States (the British colonies in North America; A revolution for independence; The Constitution and American democracy)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Jefferson (Personaggi), the Declaration and the first ten amendments (Fare storia documents) build a republic whose equality idea Wood's essay interrogates.
