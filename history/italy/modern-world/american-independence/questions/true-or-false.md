# American independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Modulo 3, Cap. 6 The birth of the United States (the British colonies in North America; A revolution for independence; The Constitution and American democracy)", Jefferson (Personaggi), the Declaration and the first ten amendments (Fare storia documents) build a republic whose equality idea Wood's essay interrogates (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§1–2 The American colonies and their relations with the mother country; The war of American independence", taxed without representation, the thirteen colonies rebel; Paine's pamphlet (Document) argues independence is both convenient and indispensable (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
