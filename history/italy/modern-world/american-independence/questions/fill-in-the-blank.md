# American independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§1–2 The American colonies and their relations with the mother country; The war of American independence" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Taxed without representation, the thirteen colonies rebel; Paine's pamphlet (Document) argues independence is both convenient and indispensable.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §3 The birth of the United States of America" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A federal constitution balances centre and states (Document: Hamilton), and the Bill of Rights writes the revolution's promises into law.
