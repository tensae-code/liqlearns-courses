# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–3 The crisis of the Ancien Régime in France; The events of 1789: the overturning of the Ancien Régime; The transition to constitutional monarchy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A bankrupt crown summons the Estates-General; the Third State (Sieyès' pamphlet), the Bastille and the Declaration of the Rights of Man remake France in one summer.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§1–4 The financial crisis and the Estates-General; The start of the Revolution and the end of the ancien régime; The taking of the Bastille (Eventi); The liberal revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cahiers de doléances (Fare storia) voice a kingdom's grievances; in months France moves from fiscal collapse to the Rights of Man.
