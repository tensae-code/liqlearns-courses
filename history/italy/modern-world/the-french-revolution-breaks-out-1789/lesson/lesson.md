# The french revolution breaks out 1789

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The french revolution breaks out 1789" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–3 The crisis of the Ancien Régime in France; The events of 1789: the overturning of the Ancien Régime; The transition to constitutional monarchy

A bankrupt crown summons the Estates-General; the Third State (Sieyès' pamphlet), the Bastille and the Declaration of the Rights of Man remake France in one summer.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 3, Cap. 7 §§1–4 The financial crisis and the Estates-General; The start of the Revolution and the end of the ancien régime; The taking of the Bastille (Eventi); The liberal revolution

Cahiers de doléances (Fare storia) voice a kingdom's grievances; in months France moves from fiscal collapse to the Rights of Man.
