# The 1970s turn crisis of ideologies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 12 §1 Crisi delle ideologie, riflusso, terrorismo" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The seventies open with ideologies retreating, private life advancing (the riflusso) and terrorism striking the democracies — a decade that begins by disbelieving its predecessors.
