# Tangentopoli and the end of the first republic — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 20 §5 The end of the "First Republic"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Falcone's book on Cosa Nostra (the unit's book study) and the mafia's war on the state meet Mani Pulite; Craxi's own Document admits illicit party financing as the system built in 1948 dissolves in 1992–93.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 13 §§10–11 Gli anni '80; La crisi del sistema politico" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Qualunquismo and mafia are the module's keywords; the eighties' shine ends in the political system's crisis — the First Republic dies of the corruption its parties normalised.
