# Tangentopoli and the end of the first republic — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 20 §5 The end of the "First Republic"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Falcone's book on Cosa Nostra (the unit's book study) and the mafia's war on the state meet Mani Pulite; Craxi's own Document admits illicit party financing as the system built in 1948 dissolves in 1992–93.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 13 §§10–11 Gli anni '80; La crisi del sistema politico" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Qualunquismo and mafia are the module's keywords; the eighties' shine ends in the political system's crisis — the First Republic dies of the corruption its parties normalised.
