# The italian sixty eight and the hot autumn — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 19 §5 The Italian Sixty-Eight" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Students occupy, then workers' Hot Autumn wins the Statute of Workers; Flores' Storiografia links the season to secularisation — Italy's 1968 lasts a decade, not a spring.
