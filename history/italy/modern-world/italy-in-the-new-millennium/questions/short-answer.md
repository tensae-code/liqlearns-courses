# Italy in the new millennium — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 18 §§3–5 Il centro-sinistra e la scelta europea; Le trasformazioni sociali; La stagione del centro-destra" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The euro choice, a society transformed and the long centre-right season; Amato and Graziosi's Fare storia verdict — "the last illusion: the choice not to choose and its costs"

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 19 §§7–8 L'Italia: crisi economica e nuovi equilibri politici; L'Italia: verso la Terza Repubblica?" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Diamanti on the League's roots and Caracciolo on the political system's crisis (Fare storia pieces) close the volume where it began — Italy's republic still being remade, its name (Second? Third?) an open question.
