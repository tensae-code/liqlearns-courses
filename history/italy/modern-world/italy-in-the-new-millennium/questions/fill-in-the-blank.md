# Italy in the new millennium — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 22 §6 Italy of the present time" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The volume closes at home — Obama's invitation to collaboration and Bhutto on fundamentalism's roots (unit Documents), Carrère d'Encausse on Russia and Zamagni on the 2008 crisis, and a Laboratorio on Russia's war in Ukraine — the present as history's open file.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 18 §§3–5 Il centro-sinistra e la scelta europea; Le trasformazioni sociali; La stagione del centro-destra" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The euro choice, a society transformed and the long centre-right season; Amato and Graziosi's Fare storia verdict — "the last illusion: the choice not to choose and its costs"
