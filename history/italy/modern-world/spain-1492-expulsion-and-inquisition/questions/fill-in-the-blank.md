# Spain 1492 expulsion and inquisition — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 11 §3 The clash between Christians and non-Christians in Spain" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** In 1492 Granada falls and the Catholic Monarchs expel Spain's Jews; the Inquisition and the marrani hunts police the faith of the new unified kingdom.
