# Spain 1492 expulsion and inquisition — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 11 §3 The clash between Christians and non-Christians in Spain" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
