# Fascisms racial laws 1938 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §5 The racial laws" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** On 18 September 1938 Mussolini announces the racial laws at Trieste (the unit's video); Sarfatti's and De Felice's Storiografie argue over Italian racial policy's roots — persecution written into law by the state itself.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 6 §§6–7 La stretta totalitaria e le leggi razziali; L'opposizione al fascismo" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The racial laws (Fare storia Document) turn persecution into statute; against the squeeze stand the fuorusciti — Garosci's and Rosselli's Fare storia pieces, "liberty as means and end" — and Togliatti's rival reading of fascism as class dictatorship.
