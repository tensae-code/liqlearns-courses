# Fascisms racial laws 1938 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §5 The racial laws", on 18 September 1938 Mussolini announces the racial laws at Trieste (the unit's video); Sarfatti's and De Felice's Storiografie argue over Italian racial policy's roots — persecution written into law by the state itself (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 6 §§6–7 La stretta totalitaria e le leggi razziali; L'opposizione al fascismo", the racial laws (Fare storia Document) turn persecution into statute; against the squeeze stand the fuorusciti — Garosci's and Rosselli's Fare storia pieces, "liberty as means and end" — and Togliatti's rival reading of fascism as class dictatorship (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
