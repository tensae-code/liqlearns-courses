# Fascisms racial laws 1938 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 3, Cap. 8 §5 The racial laws — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 6 §§6–7 La stretta totalitaria e le leggi razziali; L'opposizione al fascismo — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) on 18 September 1938 Mussolini announces the racial laws at Trieste (the unit's video); Sarfatti's and De Felice's Storiografie argue over Italian racial policy's roots — persecution written into law by the state itself
b) the racial laws (Fare storia Document) turn persecution into statute; against the squeeze stand the fuorusciti — Garosci's and Rosselli's Fare storia pieces, "liberty as means and end" — and Togliatti's rival reading of fascism as class dictatorship

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
