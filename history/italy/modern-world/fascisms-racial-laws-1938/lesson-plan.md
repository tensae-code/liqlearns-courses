## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 3, Cap. 8 §5 The racial laws** — on 18 September 1938 Mussolini announces the racial laws at Trieste (the unit's video); Sarfatti's and De Felice's Storiografie argue over Italian racial policy's roots — persecution written into law by the state itself
2. **§2 Modulo 6 §§6–7 La stretta totalitaria e le leggi razziali; L'opposizione al fascismo** — the racial laws (Fare storia Document) turn persecution into statute; against the squeeze stand the fuorusciti — Garosci's and Rosselli's Fare storia pieces, "liberty as means and end" — and Togliatti's rival reading of fascism as class dictatorship
