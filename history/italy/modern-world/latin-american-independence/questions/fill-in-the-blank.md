# Latin american independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §2 The insurrections of Latin America" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bolívar and San Martín's armies cross the Andes and a continent leaves the Spanish and Portuguese empires — the course asks (Storiografia) whether these were liberal revolutions or elite self-preservation.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 3, Cap. 9 §§1, 3 The struggles for independence in Latin America; US expansion west and south" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bolívar (Personaggi) frees a continent while the northern republic expands over Native and Mexican land — two Americas born in the same decades, opposite in direction.
