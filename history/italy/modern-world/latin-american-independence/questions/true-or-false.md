# Latin american independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §2 The insurrections of Latin America", Bolívar and San Martín's armies cross the Andes and a continent leaves the Spanish and Portuguese empires — the course asks (Storiografia) whether these were liberal revolutions or elite self-preservation (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 3, Cap. 9 §§1, 3 The struggles for independence in Latin America; US expansion west and south", Bolívar (Personaggi) frees a continent while the northern republic expands over Native and Mexican land — two Americas born in the same decades, opposite in direction (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
