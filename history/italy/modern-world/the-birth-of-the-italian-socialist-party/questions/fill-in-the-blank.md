# The birth of the italian socialist party — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 5, Cap. 18 §3 Social unrest and the birth of the Socialist Party" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Sicilian Fasci, strikes and bread riots shake liberal Italy; in 1892 the workers found their own party at Genoa.
