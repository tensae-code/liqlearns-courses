# The birth of the italian socialist party — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 18 §3 Social unrest and the birth of the Socialist Party" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Sicilian Fasci, strikes and bread riots shake liberal Italy; in 1892 the workers found their own party at Genoa.
