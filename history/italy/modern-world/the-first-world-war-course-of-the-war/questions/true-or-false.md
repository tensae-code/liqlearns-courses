# The first world war course of the war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §3 1914–15: dalla guerra di movimento alla guerra di posizione", the Schlieffen sweep dies in the Marne's mud and the war becomes trenches — submarine war, chemical weapons and industrial mobilisation are the chapter's Focus boxes (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§5–7 1915–16: lo stallo; La vita in guerra; Il "fronte interno"", the Isonzo stalemate, soldiers' lived war (Leed's and Fussell's Fare storia pieces, Lussu's assault, Jünger's shadow of death) and the militarised home front — a total war fought by civilians too (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
