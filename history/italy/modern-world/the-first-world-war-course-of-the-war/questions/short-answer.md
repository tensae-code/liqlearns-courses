# The first world war course of the war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §8 La svolta del 1917 (con Eventi: La disfatta di Caporetto)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Russia exits, America enters, and Caporetto breaks the Italian front — 1917 turns the war's military and political tables at once.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§2–3 Europe at war; A new kind of conflict" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The war of movement dies in the trenches; gas attacks (the chapter's document), cousin-kings at war (Storia culturale) and Senegalese riflemen in French uniform (La storia allo specchio) show a total, global war.
