# The first world war course of the war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §6 The turning of the conflict and the defeat of the Central Empires (1917–1918)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Caporetto, America's entry and the home front's protest (Rugafiori's Storiografia) turn the war; in 1918 the Central Empires collapse under the weight they could no longer carry.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §3 1914–15: dalla guerra di movimento alla guerra di posizione" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The Schlieffen sweep dies in the Marne's mud and the war becomes trenches — submarine war, chemical weapons and industrial mobilisation are the chapter's Focus boxes.
