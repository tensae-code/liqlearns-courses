# The first world war course of the war

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The first world war course of the war" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§2–3 Europe at war; A new kind of conflict

The war of movement dies in the trenches; gas attacks (the chapter's document), cousin-kings at war (Storia culturale) and Senegalese riflemen in French uniform (La storia allo specchio) show a total, global war.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 2, Cap. 4 §6 The turning of the conflict and the defeat of the Central Empires (1917–1918)

Caporetto, America's entry and the home front's protest (Rugafiori's Storiografia) turn the war; in 1918 the Central Empires collapse under the weight they could no longer carry.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §3 1914–15: dalla guerra di movimento alla guerra di posizione

The Schlieffen sweep dies in the Marne's mud and the war becomes trenches — submarine war, chemical weapons and industrial mobilisation are the chapter's Focus boxes.

## Sub lesson 4 — Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§5–7 1915–16: lo stallo; La vita in guerra; Il "fronte interno"

The Isonzo stalemate, soldiers' lived war (Leed's and Fussell's Fare storia pieces, Lussu's assault, Jünger's shadow of death) and the militarised home front — a total war fought by civilians too.

## Sub lesson 5 — Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §8 La svolta del 1917 (con Eventi: La disfatta di Caporetto)

Russia exits, America enters, and Caporetto breaks the Italian front — 1917 turns the war's military and political tables at once.
