# Lesson plan — The first world war course of the war

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** The first world war course of the war
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The first world war course of the war as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§2–3 Europe at war; A new kind of conflict** — the war of movement dies in the trenches; gas attacks (the chapter's document), cousin-kings at war (Storia culturale) and Senegalese riflemen in French uniform (La storia allo specchio) show a total, global war
2. **§2 Unità 2, Cap. 4 §6 The turning of the conflict and the defeat of the Central Empires (1917–1918)** — Caporetto, America's entry and the home front's protest (Rugafiori's Storiografia) turn the war; in 1918 the Central Empires collapse under the weight they could no longer carry
3. **§3 Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §3 1914–15: dalla guerra di movimento alla guerra di posizione** — the Schlieffen sweep dies in the Marne's mud and the war becomes trenches — submarine war, chemical weapons and industrial mobilisation are the chapter's Focus boxes
4. **§4 Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §§5–7 1915–16: lo stallo; La vita in guerra; Il "fronte interno"** — the Isonzo stalemate, soldiers' lived war (Leed's and Fussell's Fare storia pieces, Lussu's assault, Jünger's shadow of death) and the militarised home front — a total war fought by civilians too
5. **§5 Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §8 La svolta del 1917 (con Eventi: La disfatta di Caporetto)** — Russia exits, America enters, and Caporetto breaks the Italian front — 1917 turns the war's military and political tables at once
