## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 6, Cap. 20 §1 The English Revolution

Parliament and Charles I go to war, the king is executed (1649, the chapter's image) and Cromwell's republic tests whether a state can rest on godly rule.

## Sub lesson 2 — Il Parlamento inglese; Il processo a Carlo I (Eventi/DOC); La guerra civile; Cromwell (Personaggi); I levellers e i dibattiti di Putney (Fare storia)

Parliament tries and executes its king; at Putney common soldiers debate who should vote — the revolution arguing with itself in its own documents.
