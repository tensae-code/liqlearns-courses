# The english revolution and cromwell — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 6, Cap. 20 §1 The English Revolution", Parliament and Charles I go to war, the king is executed (1649, the chapter's image) and Cromwell's republic tests whether a state can rest on godly rule (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Il Parlamento inglese; Il processo a Carlo I (Eventi/DOC); La guerra civile; Cromwell (Personaggi); I levellers e i dibattiti di Putney (Fare storia)", Parliament tries and executes its king; at Putney common soldiers debate who should vote — the revolution arguing with itself in its own documents (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
