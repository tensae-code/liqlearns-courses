# The english revolution and cromwell — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 6, Cap. 20 §1 The English Revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Parliament and Charles I go to war, the king is executed (1649, the chapter's image) and Cromwell's republic tests whether a state can rest on godly rule.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Il Parlamento inglese; Il processo a Carlo I (Eventi/DOC); La guerra civile; Cromwell (Personaggi); I levellers e i dibattiti di Putney (Fare storia)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Parliament tries and executes its king; at Putney common soldiers debate who should vote — the revolution arguing with itself in its own documents.
