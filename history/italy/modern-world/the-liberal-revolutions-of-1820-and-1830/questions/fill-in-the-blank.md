# The liberal revolutions of 1820 and 1830 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1, 3 The liberal oppositions and the revolutionary movements of the 1820s; The European revolutions of 1830–1831" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Secret societies and Carbonari rise in Spain, Naples and Piedmont; Greece wins independence, Paris raises barricades again in 1830 and Belgium and Poland follow.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 11 §§4–7 The revolutionary movements of 1820–21; Greek independence; The revolutionary movements of 1830–31; Liberalism and authoritarianism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Carbonari, Greek freedom and the July days — Billington's secret societies (Fare storia) wire the conspiracies together.
