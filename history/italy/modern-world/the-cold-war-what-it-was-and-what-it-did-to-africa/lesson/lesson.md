## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§1–2 The division of Europe; The first years of the Cold War

Kennan's Long Telegram (the chapter's document) names containment before Truman proclaims it (unit Document); Harper's Storiografia asks who started it as the two blocs form on the chapter's maps.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 4, Cap. 15 §7 The years of Khrushchev and Kennedy between crises and dialogue

The Berlin Wall (image study: daily life beneath it), the space race (Storia culturale) and Cuba's missiles bring the superpowers to the edge; Castro's and Che's Documents (in the chapter) give the revolution's own reasons, Kennedy answers at the Wall.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unità 4, Cap. 18 §3 The USSR and the USA in the years of the "new" Cold War

Reagan names the "evil empire" (Document) and rearms through the five-ringed Cold War (Storia culturale: the Olympic boycotts); Afghanistan becomes the Soviet Vietnam.

## Sub lesson 4 — Modulo 9 §7 Guerra fredda e coesistenza pacifica

The nuclear keyword rules the module — blockade, Korea and Khrushchev's report (Fare storia Document) move the confrontation from war scare to coexistence without ending it.

## Sub lesson 5 — Modulo 9 §9 Distensione e confronto: gli anni di Kennedy e Kruscëv

The missile crisis (Eventi) brings the world to the edge; Kennedy's mission speech and Martin Luther King (Personaggi) mark the superpower's other front — the one at home.
