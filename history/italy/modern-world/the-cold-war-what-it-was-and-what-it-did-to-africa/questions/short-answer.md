# The cold war what it was and what it did to africa — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 9 §9 Distensione e confronto: gli anni di Kennedy e Kruscëv" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The missile crisis (Eventi) brings the world to the edge; Kennedy's mission speech and Martin Luther King (Personaggi) mark the superpower's other front — the one at home.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§1–2 The division of Europe; The first years of the Cold War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kennan's Long Telegram (the chapter's document) names containment before Truman proclaims it (unit Document); Harper's Storiografia asks who started it as the two blocs form on the chapter's maps.
