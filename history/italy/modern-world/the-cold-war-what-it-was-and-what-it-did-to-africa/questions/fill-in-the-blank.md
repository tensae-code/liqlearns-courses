# The cold war what it was and what it did to africa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 15 §7 The years of Khrushchev and Kennedy between crises and dialogue" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Berlin Wall (image study: daily life beneath it), the space race (Storia culturale) and Cuba's missiles bring the superpowers to the edge; Castro's and Che's Documents (in the chapter) give the revolution's own reasons, Kennedy answers at the Wall.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 18 §3 The USSR and the USA in the years of the "new" Cold War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Reagan names the "evil empire" (Document) and rearms through the five-ringed Cold War (Storia culturale: the Olympic boycotts); Afghanistan becomes the Soviet Vietnam.
