# Lesson plan — The cold war what it was and what it did to africa

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** The cold war what it was and what it did to africa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The cold war what it was and what it did to africa as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The 1900s and the present day — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 3): the belle époque's shadows, two world wars, fascism and Nazism, Stalinism, the Republic, the Cold War, 1968, Tangentopoli and globalisation to the war in Ukraine — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§1–2 The division of Europe; The first years of the Cold War** — Kennan's Long Telegram (the chapter's document) names containment before Truman proclaims it (unit Document); Harper's Storiografia asks who started it as the two blocs form on the chapter's maps
2. **§2 Unità 4, Cap. 15 §7 The years of Khrushchev and Kennedy between crises and dialogue** — the Berlin Wall (image study: daily life beneath it), the space race (Storia culturale) and Cuba's missiles bring the superpowers to the edge; Castro's and Che's Documents (in the chapter) give the revolution's own reasons, Kennedy answers at the Wall
3. **§3 Unità 4, Cap. 18 §3 The USSR and the USA in the years of the "new" Cold War** — Reagan names the "evil empire" (Document) and rearms through the five-ringed Cold War (Storia culturale: the Olympic boycotts); Afghanistan becomes the Soviet Vietnam
4. **§4 Modulo 9 §7 Guerra fredda e coesistenza pacifica** — the nuclear keyword rules the module — blockade, Korea and Khrushchev's report (Fare storia Document) move the confrontation from war scare to coexistence without ending it
5. **§5 Modulo 9 §9 Distensione e confronto: gli anni di Kennedy e Kruscëv** — the missile crisis (Eventi) brings the world to the edge; Kennedy's mission speech and Martin Luther King (Personaggi) mark the superpower's other front — the one at home

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
