# Lesson plan — Italy unifies cavour and the patriots

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Italy unifies cavour and the patriots
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The 1700s and 1800s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 2): Enlightenment and enlightened reform, industry, the American and French revolutions, Napoleon, Restoration, 1848, the Risorgimento and liberal Italy to 1900 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Italy unifies cavour and the patriots as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The 1700s and 1800s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 2): Enlightenment and enlightened reform, industry, the American and French revolutions, Napoleon, Restoration, 1848, the Risorgimento and liberal Italy to 1900 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§1–3 The rise of a political feeling; The 1830s: Mazzini's thought; The 1840s: new political proposals for the Italian nation** — Mazzini's Young Italy (Document: its programme), Gioberti and Balbo's Speranze d'Italia offer rival roads — republic, federation or Piedmontese monarchy
2. **§2 Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§4–5 The two years of reforms and the Italian 1848; The First War of Independence and the season of the democratic republics** — the Statuto Albertino (Document), Charles Albert's war on Austria, defeat at Custoza and Novara, and the Roman Republic of 1849 close the Risorgimento's first act
3. **§3 Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The Risorgimento in the Kingdom of Sardinia; Another idea of Risorgimento: Mazzini and Garibaldi; The Second War of Independence** — Cavour's reforms and the Plombières bargain bring France in against Austria; Magenta and Solferino (1859) free Lombardy, and Cavour's alarm (Document) at Garibaldi's myth grows
4. **§4 Unità 4, Cap. 14 §4 The expedition of the Thousand and the proclamation of the Kingdom of Italy** — Garibaldi's redshirts take Sicily and Naples with a thousand volunteers; in 1861 the Kingdom of Italy is proclaimed — made, but not yet finished
5. **§5 Modulo 4, Cap. 12 The Italian Risorgimento (Italy and the national question; The first revolutionary movements; The Mazzinian project; Moderates, Catholics and federalists; Pius IX and the reform movement; The Italian '48: war against Austria; The defeat of the Italian democrats)** — Mazzini's insurrection doctrine against Gioberti's pope-led federation and Cattaneo's federal republic (all in Fare storia documents)
6. **§6 Modulo 4, Cap. 13 The process of unification (Cavour's liberal Piedmont; The defeat of the republicans; The alliance with France and the Second War of Independence; The Thousand and the conquest of the South; Unity: its characters and limits)** — Cavour (Personaggi) plays Europe's chessboard, Garibaldi (Personaggi; Fare storia: Abba's epic) wins the South with volunteers, and the plebiscites ratify a unity whose limits Gramsci will judge
