# Italy unifies cavour and the patriots

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Italy unifies cavour and the patriots" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§1–3 The rise of a political feeling; The 1830s: Mazzini's thought; The 1840s: new political proposals for the Italian nation

Mazzini's Young Italy (Document: its programme), Gioberti and Balbo's Speranze d'Italia offer rival roads — republic, federation or Piedmontese monarchy.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§4–5 The two years of reforms and the Italian 1848; The First War of Independence and the season of the democratic republics

The Statuto Albertino (Document), Charles Albert's war on Austria, defeat at Custoza and Novara, and the Roman Republic of 1849 close the Risorgimento's first act.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The Risorgimento in the Kingdom of Sardinia; Another idea of Risorgimento: Mazzini and Garibaldi; The Second War of Independence

Cavour's reforms and the Plombières bargain bring France in against Austria; Magenta and Solferino (1859) free Lombardy, and Cavour's alarm (Document) at Garibaldi's myth grows.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unità 4, Cap. 14 §4 The expedition of the Thousand and the proclamation of the Kingdom of Italy

Garibaldi's redshirts take Sicily and Naples with a thousand volunteers; in 1861 the Kingdom of Italy is proclaimed — made, but not yet finished.

## Sub lesson 5 — Modulo 4, Cap. 12 The Italian Risorgimento (Italy and the national question; The first revolutionary movements; The Mazzinian project; Moderates, Catholics and federalists; Pius IX and the reform movement; The Italian '48: war against Austria; The defeat of the Italian democrats)

Mazzini's insurrection doctrine against Gioberti's pope-led federation and Cattaneo's federal republic (all in Fare storia documents).

## Sub lesson 6 — Modulo 4, Cap. 13 The process of unification (Cavour's liberal Piedmont; The defeat of the republicans; The alliance with France and the Second War of Independence; The Thousand and the conquest of the South; Unity: its characters and limits)

Cavour (Personaggi) plays Europe's chessboard, Garibaldi (Personaggi; Fare storia: Abba's epic) wins the South with volunteers, and the plebiscites ratify a unity whose limits Gramsci will judge.
