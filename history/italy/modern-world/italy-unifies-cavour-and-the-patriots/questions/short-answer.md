# Italy unifies cavour and the patriots — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 12 The Italian Risorgimento (Italy and the national question; The first revolutionary movements; The Mazzinian project; Moderates, Catholics and federalists; Pius IX and the reform movement; The Italian '48: war against Austria; The defeat of the Italian democrats)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Mazzini's insurrection doctrine against Gioberti's pope-led federation and Cattaneo's federal republic (all in Fare storia documents).

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 4, Cap. 13 The process of unification (Cavour's liberal Piedmont; The defeat of the republicans; The alliance with France and the Second War of Independence; The Thousand and the conquest of the South; Unity: its characters and limits)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Cavour (Personaggi) plays Europe's chessboard, Garibaldi (Personaggi; Fare storia: Abba's epic) wins the South with volunteers, and the plebiscites ratify a unity whose limits Gramsci will judge.
