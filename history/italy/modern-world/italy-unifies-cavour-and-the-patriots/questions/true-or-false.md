# Italy unifies cavour and the patriots — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The Risorgimento in the Kingdom of Sardinia; Another idea of Risorgimento: Mazzini and Garibaldi; The Second War of Independence", Cavour's reforms and the Plombières bargain bring France in against Austria; Magenta and Solferino (1859) free Lombardy, and Cavour's alarm (Document) at Garibaldi's myth grows (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §4 The expedition of the Thousand and the proclamation of the Kingdom of Italy", Garibaldi's redshirts take Sicily and Naples with a thousand volunteers; in 1861 the Kingdom of Italy is proclaimed — made, but not yet finished (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
