# Italy unifies cavour and the patriots — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§1–3 The rise of a political feeling; The 1830s: Mazzini's thought; The 1840s: new political proposals for the Italian nation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mazzini's Young Italy (Document: its programme), Gioberti and Balbo's Speranze d'Italia offer rival roads — republic, federation or Piedmontese monarchy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 11 §§4–5 The two years of reforms and the Italian 1848; The First War of Independence and the season of the democratic republics" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Statuto Albertino (Document), Charles Albert's war on Austria, defeat at Custoza and Novara, and the Roman Republic of 1849 close the Risorgimento's first act.
