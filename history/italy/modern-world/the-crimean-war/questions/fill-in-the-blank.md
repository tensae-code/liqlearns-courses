# The crimean war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §3 The Crimean War (1853–1856) and its international consequences" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Britain, France and Piedmont fight Russia in Crimea; the first war reported by journalists and photographers breaks the Vienna system and opens Italy's road to the great-power table.
