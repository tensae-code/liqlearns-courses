# Detente from nixon to carter — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 17 §1 The years of détente from Nixon to Carter" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Nixon goes to Beijing and Moscow (the joint communiqué is a unit Document), SALT caps the missiles, and Helsinki promises rights the East signs and shelves — détente as management, not friendship.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 12 §2 Gli Stati Uniti: da Nixon a Reagan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Watergate to Reagan — Testi's Fare storia piece reads Reagan as "return to the future"; monetarism (the module's keyword) replaces Keynes in the hegemon's house.
