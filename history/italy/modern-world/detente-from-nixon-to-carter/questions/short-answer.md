# Detente from nixon to carter — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 17 §1 The years of détente from Nixon to Carter" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Nixon goes to Beijing and Moscow (the joint communiqué is a unit Document), SALT caps the missiles, and Helsinki promises rights the East signs and shelves — détente as management, not friendship.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 12 §2 Gli Stati Uniti: da Nixon a Reagan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Watergate to Reagan — Testi's Fare storia piece reads Reagan as "return to the future"; monetarism (the module's keyword) replaces Keynes in the hegemon's house.
