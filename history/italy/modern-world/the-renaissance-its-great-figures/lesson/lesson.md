## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 10 §2 The Renaissance

In Florence and the courts, Brunelleschi, Leonardo — the Gioconda as its myth — and Michelangelo remake art and the artist's status itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Il Rinascimento; Personaggi: Leonardo da Vinci; Nuove visioni del mondo

Leonardo's many trades embody the age; art, anatomy and astronomy together redraw man's place in the world.
