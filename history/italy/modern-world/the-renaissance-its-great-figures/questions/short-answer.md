# The renaissance its great figures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §2 The Renaissance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In Florence and the courts, Brunelleschi, Leonardo — the Gioconda as its myth — and Michelangelo remake art and the artist's status itself.

**2.** Tell the story of "Il Rinascimento; Personaggi: Leonardo da Vinci; Nuove visioni del mondo" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Leonardo's many trades embody the age; art, anatomy and astronomy together redraw man's place in the world.
