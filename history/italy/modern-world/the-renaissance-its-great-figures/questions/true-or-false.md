# The renaissance its great figures — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §2 The Renaissance", in Florence and the courts, Brunelleschi, Leonardo — the Gioconda as its myth — and Michelangelo remake art and the artist's status itself (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Il Rinascimento; Personaggi: Leonardo da Vinci; Nuove visioni del mondo", Leonardo's many trades embody the age; art, anatomy and astronomy together redraw man's place in the world (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
