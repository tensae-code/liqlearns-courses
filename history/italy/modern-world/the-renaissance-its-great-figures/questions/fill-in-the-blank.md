# The renaissance its great figures — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 10 §2 The Renaissance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In Florence and the courts, Brunelleschi, Leonardo — the Gioconda as its myth — and Michelangelo remake art and the artist's status itself.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Il Rinascimento; Personaggi: Leonardo da Vinci; Nuove visioni del mondo" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Leonardo's many trades embody the age; art, anatomy and astronomy together redraw man's place in the world.
