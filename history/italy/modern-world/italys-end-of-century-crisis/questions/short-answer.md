# Italys end of century crisis — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 5, Cap. 18 §§4–5 From Giolitti's first government to Crispi's second; The end-of-century crisis" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Banca Romana scandal fells Giolitti, Adwa (1896) fells Crispi, and Bava Beccaris' cannon fire on Milan's crowds (1898) marks the liberal state's darkest hour.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§1–2 The end-of-century crisis and the start of a new political course; Socialists and Catholics, new protagonists of Italian political life" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** After Adwa and the Milan cannonades the liberal state changes course; Turati's socialists and the Catholic movement enter politics as mass forces the old trasformismo cannot digest.
