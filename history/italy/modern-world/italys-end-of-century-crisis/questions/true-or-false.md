# Italys end of century crisis — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 5, Cap. 18 §§4–5 From Giolitti's first government to Crispi's second; The end-of-century crisis", the Banca Romana scandal fells Giolitti, Adwa (1896) fells Crispi, and Bava Beccaris' cannon fire on Milan's crowds (1898) marks the liberal state's darkest hour (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§1–2 The end-of-century crisis and the start of a new political course; Socialists and Catholics, new protagonists of Italian political life", after Adwa and the Milan cannonades the liberal state changes course; Turati's socialists and the Catholic movement enter politics as mass forces the old trasformismo cannot digest (Sub lesson 2)
