# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 3, Cap. 13 §8 Toward a new world order — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Modulo 9 §§1–2 La nascita dell'Onu; I nuovi equilibri mondiali — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Yalta and Potsdam draw the postwar map while the war's figures (La storia in cifre) count its cost; Rodogno on Italy's occupation of Yugoslavia and Bruck's "life after the camp" (unit Documents) refuse a clean ending
b) the UN Charter (Fare storia Document) and Churchill's zones-of-influence paper open the postwar together — universal organisation and spheres of power, the era's twin births

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
