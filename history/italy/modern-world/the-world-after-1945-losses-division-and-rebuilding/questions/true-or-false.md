# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §8 Toward a new world order", Yalta and Potsdam draw the postwar map while the war's figures (La storia in cifre) count its cost; Rodogno on Italy's occupation of Yugoslavia and Bruck's "life after the camp" (unit Documents) refuse a clean ending (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 9 §§1–2 La nascita dell'Onu; I nuovi equilibri mondiali", the UN Charter (Fare storia Document) and Churchill's zones-of-influence paper open the postwar together — universal organisation and spheres of power, the era's twin births (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
