# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §8 Toward a new world order" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Yalta and Potsdam draw the postwar map while the war's figures (La storia in cifre) count its cost; Rodogno on Italy's occupation of Yugoslavia and Bruck's "life after the camp" (unit Documents) refuse a clean ending.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 9 §§1–2 La nascita dell'Onu; I nuovi equilibri mondiali" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The UN Charter (Fare storia Document) and Churchill's zones-of-influence paper open the postwar together — universal organisation and spheres of power, the era's twin births.
