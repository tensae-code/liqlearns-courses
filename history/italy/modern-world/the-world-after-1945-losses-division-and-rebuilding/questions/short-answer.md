# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 13 §8 Toward a new world order" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Yalta and Potsdam draw the postwar map while the war's figures (La storia in cifre) count its cost; Rodogno on Italy's occupation of Yugoslavia and Bruck's "life after the camp" (unit Documents) refuse a clean ending.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 9 §§1–2 La nascita dell'Onu; I nuovi equilibri mondiali" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The UN Charter (Fare storia Document) and Churchill's zones-of-influence paper open the postwar together — universal organisation and spheres of power, the era's twin births.
