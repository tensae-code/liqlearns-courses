# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 13 §8 Toward a new world order

Yalta and Potsdam draw the postwar map while the war's figures (La storia in cifre) count its cost; Rodogno on Italy's occupation of Yugoslavia and Bruck's "life after the camp" (unit Documents) refuse a clean ending.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 9 §§1–2 La nascita dell'Onu; I nuovi equilibri mondiali

The UN Charter (Fare storia Document) and Churchill's zones-of-influence paper open the postwar together — universal organisation and spheres of power, the era's twin births.
