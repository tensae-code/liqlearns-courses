# The italian communes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§1–2 The medieval cities; The development of the Communes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Italian city-dwellers swear collective oaths and govern themselves through consuls and podestà, raising tower-houses and writing their own statutes while bishops and emperors look on.

**2.** Tell the story of "L'Europa dei comuni; Lo scontro tra l'imperatore e i comuni italiani; Il giuramento di Pontida" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The communes swear their oath at Pontida (1167) and beat Barbarossa's empire at Legnano; canon law grows in the same cities as their other constitution.
