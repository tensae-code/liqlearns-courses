# The italian communes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§1–2 The medieval cities; The development of the Communes", Italian city-dwellers swear collective oaths and govern themselves through consuls and podestà, raising tower-houses and writing their own statutes while bishops and emperors look on (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "L'Europa dei comuni; Lo scontro tra l'imperatore e i comuni italiani; Il giuramento di Pontida", the communes swear their oath at Pontida (1167) and beat Barbarossa's empire at Legnano; canon law grows in the same cities as their other constitution (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
