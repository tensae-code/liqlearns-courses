# The italian communes — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§1–2 The medieval cities; The development of the Communes — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** L'Europa dei comuni; Lo scontro tra l'imperatore e i comuni italiani; Il giuramento di Pontida — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Italian city-dwellers swear collective oaths and govern themselves through consuls and podestà, raising tower-houses and writing their own statutes while bishops and emperors look on
