# Milan florence and venice the great regional states — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §2 The great regional states: Milan, Florence and Venice" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Milan's dukes, Florence's Medici-behind-the-scenes republic and Venice's terraferma empire absorb their neighbours until five powers share the peninsula.
