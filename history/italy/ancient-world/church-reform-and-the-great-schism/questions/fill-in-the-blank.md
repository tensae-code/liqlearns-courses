# Church reform and the great schism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§3–4 The reform of the Church in the 11th century; The reform of the Papacy and the Schism of the East" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Cluniac monks and reforming popes fight simony and clerical marriage; in 1054 Rome and Constantinople split, dividing Western and Eastern Christianity for good.
