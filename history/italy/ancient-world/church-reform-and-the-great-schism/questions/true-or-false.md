# Church reform and the great schism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§3–4 The reform of the Church in the 11th century; The reform of the Papacy and the Schism of the East", Cluniac monks and reforming popes fight simony and clerical marriage; in 1054 Rome and Constantinople split, dividing Western and Eastern Christianity for good
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
