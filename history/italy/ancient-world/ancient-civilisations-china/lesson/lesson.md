Song and Yuan China is shown as the world's greatest power of the age — richer, more populous and more inventive than the Europe reading about it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modulo 2, Cap. 4 §3 China from the Ming to the Qing

Matteo Ricci (Personaggi; Fare storia: the Jesuits in China) opens a dialogue of civilisations that Leibniz thought the world's greatest.
