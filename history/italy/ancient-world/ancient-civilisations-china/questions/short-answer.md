# Ancient civilisations china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La Cina, la più grande potenza mondiale" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Song and Yuan China is shown as the world's greatest power of the age — richer, more populous and more inventive than the Europe reading about it.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 2, Cap. 4 §3 China from the Ming to the Qing" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Matteo Ricci (Personaggi; Fare storia: the Jesuits in China) opens a dialogue of civilisations that Leibniz thought the world's greatest.
