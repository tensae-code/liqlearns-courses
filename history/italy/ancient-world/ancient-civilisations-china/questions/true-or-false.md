# Ancient civilisations china — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "La Cina, la più grande potenza mondiale", Song and Yuan China is shown as the world's greatest power of the age — richer, more populous and more inventive than the Europe reading about it (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Modulo 2, Cap. 4 §3 China from the Ming to the Qing", Matteo Ricci (Personaggi; Fare storia: the Jesuits in China) opens a dialogue of civilisations that Leibniz thought the world's greatest (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
