# Ancient civilisations china — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Cina, la più grande potenza mondiale" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Song and Yuan China is shown as the world's greatest power of the age — richer, more populous and more inventive than the Europe reading about it.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 2, Cap. 4 §3 China from the Ming to the Qing" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Matteo Ricci (Personaggi; Fare storia: the Jesuits in China) opens a dialogue of civilisations that Leibniz thought the world's greatest.
