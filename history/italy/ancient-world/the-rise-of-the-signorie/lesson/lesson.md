# The rise of the signorie

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The rise of the signorie" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 9 §1 The birth of the city Signorie

Exhausted by faction fights, the communes hand power to one lord — Visconti, Este, Gonzaga — trading liberty for order and opening Italy's age of courts.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dal comune alla signoria; Firenze e Venezia; I Regni di Napoli e di Sicilia; Lo Stato della Chiesa; L'Italia delle Regioni

Signori replace communes, Florence and Venice become regional states, and Italy settles into five powers — the map the French invasions will shatter.
