# The rise of the signorie — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 3, Cap. 9 §1 The birth of the city Signorie — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Dal comune alla signoria; Firenze e Venezia; I Regni di Napoli e di Sicilia; Lo Stato della Chiesa; L'Italia delle Regioni — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) exhausted by faction fights, the communes hand power to one lord — Visconti, Este, Gonzaga — trading liberty for order and opening Italy's age of courts
b) signori replace communes, Florence and Venice become regional states, and Italy settles into five powers — the map the French invasions will shatter

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
