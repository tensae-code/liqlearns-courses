# The rise of the signorie — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §1 The birth of the city Signorie", exhausted by faction fights, the communes hand power to one lord — Visconti, Este, Gonzaga — trading liberty for order and opening Italy's age of courts (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Dal comune alla signoria; Firenze e Venezia; I Regni di Napoli e di Sicilia; Lo Stato della Chiesa; L'Italia delle Regioni", signori replace communes, Florence and Venice become regional states, and Italy settles into five powers — the map the French invasions will shatter (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
