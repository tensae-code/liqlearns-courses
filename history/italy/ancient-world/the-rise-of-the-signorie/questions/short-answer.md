# The rise of the signorie — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §1 The birth of the city Signorie" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Exhausted by faction fights, the communes hand power to one lord — Visconti, Este, Gonzaga — trading liberty for order and opening Italy's age of courts.

**2.** Tell the story of "Dal comune alla signoria; Firenze e Venezia; I Regni di Napoli e di Sicilia; Lo Stato della Chiesa; L'Italia delle Regioni" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Signori replace communes, Florence and Venice become regional states, and Italy settles into five powers — the map the French invasions will shatter.
