# Medieval world the ottomans — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Il declino dell'Impero bizantino (in the Mediterranean chapters)", Byzantium shrinks between crusaders and Turks while Christian–Muslim trade and war share one sea (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Il Mediterraneo nel '400; I poteri nell'Impero ottomano; Il dominio turco nel Mediterraneo", after 1453 the Ottoman system — sultan, divan, timars — rules the eastern Mediterranean and presses on Italy's own coasts (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
