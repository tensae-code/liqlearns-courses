# Medieval world the ottomans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 11 §1 The fall of the Byzantine empire and the rise of Ottoman power" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mehmed II's cannon breach Constantinople's walls in 1453; the Venetian Senate's report (Document) records Christian Europe's shock as an empire of a thousand years ends.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 11 §2 The age of Suleiman the Magnificent" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Suleiman takes Belgrade, Rhodes and Hungary and besieges Vienna; the Ottoman state becomes the Mediterranean's other superpower.
