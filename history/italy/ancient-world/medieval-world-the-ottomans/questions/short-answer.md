# Medieval world the ottomans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "L'espansione turca; DOC dossiers on the fall of Constantinople (Tedaldi; a Turkish chronicle; Piccolomini)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** 1453 is told three ways at once — a Christian witness, a Turkish chronicle and an Italian humanist — the course's method in one event.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 2, Cap. 4 §1 The decline of the Ottoman Empire and the Safavid parabola; The siege of Vienna (Eventi)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** After 1683 the Ottoman tide ebbs and Persia's Safavid arc closes; Busbecq's Letters from Turkey (Fare storia) let the empire describe itself.
