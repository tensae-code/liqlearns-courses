# Italys balance of power and the peace of lodi — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§3–4 The kingdoms: the State of the Church and southern Italy; The politics of equilibrium" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Popes rule central Italy as princes and Aragonese kings hold the South; after 1454 the Peace of Lodi and Lorenzo de' Medici's balancing keep Italy's five states from war for a generation.
