# Italys balance of power and the peace of lodi — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 9 §§3–4 The kingdoms: the State of the Church and southern Italy; The politics of equilibrium" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Popes rule central Italy as princes and Aragonese kings hold the South; after 1454 the Peace of Lodi and Lorenzo de' Medici's balancing keep Italy's five states from war for a generation.
