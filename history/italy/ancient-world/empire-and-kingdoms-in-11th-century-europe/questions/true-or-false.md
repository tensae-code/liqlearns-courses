# Empire and kingdoms in 11th century europe — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms", the Ottonian emperors rebuild imperial power in Germany while the weak Capetians hold a small French royal domain and Norman adventurers carve kingdoms from England to southern Italy (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia", Ottonian emperors invest bishop-counts to hold Germany and Italy (the body text follows Otto I to Otto III's Roman dream), weak kings share Europe with new regna, and Normans take the South (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
