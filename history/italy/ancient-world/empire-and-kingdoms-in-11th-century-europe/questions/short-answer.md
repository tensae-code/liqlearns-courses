# Empire and kingdoms in 11th century europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Ottonian emperors rebuild imperial power in Germany while the weak Capetians hold a small French royal domain and Norman adventurers carve kingdoms from England to southern Italy.

**2.** Tell the story of "Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ottonian emperors invest bishop-counts to hold Germany and Italy (the body text follows Otto I to Otto III's Roman dream), weak kings share Europe with new regna, and Normans take the South.
