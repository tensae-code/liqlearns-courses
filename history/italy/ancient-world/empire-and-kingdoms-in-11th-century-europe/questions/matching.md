# Empire and kingdoms in 11th century europe — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the Ottonian emperors rebuild imperial power in Germany while the weak Capetians hold a small French royal domain and Norman adventurers carve kingdoms from England to southern Italy
b) Ottonian emperors invest bishop-counts to hold Germany and Italy (the body text follows Otto I to Otto III's Roman dream), weak kings share Europe with new regna, and Normans take the South

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
