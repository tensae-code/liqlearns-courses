# Empire and kingdoms in 11th century europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Ottonian emperors rebuild imperial power in Germany while the weak Capetians hold a small French royal domain and Norman adventurers carve kingdoms from England to southern Italy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ottonian emperors invest bishop-counts to hold Germany and Italy (the body text follows Otto I to Otto III's Roman dream), weak kings share Europe with new regna, and Normans take the South.
