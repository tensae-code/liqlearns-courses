# Lesson plan — Empire and kingdoms in 11th century europe

**Course:** History — Italy · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Empire and kingdoms in 11th century europe
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Europe's revival after the year 1000 to the 1600s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 1): communes and crusades, crisis and plague, signorie, Renaissance, the voyages, Reformation and the century of crisis (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Empire and kingdoms in 11th century europe as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Europe's revival after the year 1000 to the 1600s — the first telling (Barbero, Vol. 1): communes and crusades, crisis and plague, signorie, Renaissance, the voyages, Reformation and the century of crisis — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms** — the Ottonian emperors rebuild imperial power in Germany while the weak Capetians hold a small French royal domain and Norman adventurers carve kingdoms from England to southern Italy
2. **§2 Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia** — Ottonian emperors invest bishop-counts to hold Germany and Italy (the body text follows Otto I to Otto III's Roman dream), weak kings share Europe with new regna, and Normans take the South

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
