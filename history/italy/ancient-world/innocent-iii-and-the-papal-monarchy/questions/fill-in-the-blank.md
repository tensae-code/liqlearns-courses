# Innocent iii and the papal monarchy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §1 The age of Innocent III: theocratic politics and the fight against heresy" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Innocent III claims the papacy stands above kings, launches the Fourth Lateran Council and the Albigensian Crusade — the papal monarchy at its height.
