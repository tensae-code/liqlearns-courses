# Europe after the plague recovery and revolt

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Europe after the plague recovery and revolt" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§4–6 The economic transformations; New social tensions in countryside and cities; Cultural life during the crisis

Survivors inherit land and demand wages; Florentine wool-workers rise in the Ciompi revolt (1378), banks crash, and a new image of death haunts art and faith.

## Sub lesson 2 — Le rivolte (with Eventi: La rivolta dei Ciompi)

Florence's wool-workers seize the city in 1378; across Europe the poor, taxed for wars, turn revolt into the crisis century's second language.
