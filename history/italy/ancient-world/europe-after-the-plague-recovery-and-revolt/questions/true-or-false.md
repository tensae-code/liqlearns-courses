# Europe after the plague recovery and revolt — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§4–6 The economic transformations; New social tensions in countryside and cities; Cultural life during the crisis", survivors inherit land and demand wages; Florentine wool-workers rise in the Ciompi revolt (1378), banks crash, and a new image of death haunts art and faith (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Le rivolte (with Eventi: La rivolta dei Ciompi)", Florence's wool-workers seize the city in 1378; across Europe the poor, taxed for wars, turn revolt into the crisis century's second language (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
