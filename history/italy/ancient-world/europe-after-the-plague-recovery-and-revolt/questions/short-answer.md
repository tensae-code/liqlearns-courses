# Europe after the plague recovery and revolt — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§4–6 The economic transformations; New social tensions in countryside and cities; Cultural life during the crisis" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Survivors inherit land and demand wages; Florentine wool-workers rise in the Ciompi revolt (1378), banks crash, and a new image of death haunts art and faith.

**2.** Tell the story of "Le rivolte (with Eventi: La rivolta dei Ciompi)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Florence's wool-workers seize the city in 1378; across Europe the poor, taxed for wars, turn revolt into the crisis century's second language.
