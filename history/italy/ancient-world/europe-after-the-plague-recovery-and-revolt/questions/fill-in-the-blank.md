# Europe after the plague recovery and revolt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§4–6 The economic transformations; New social tensions in countryside and cities; Cultural life during the crisis" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Survivors inherit land and demand wages; Florentine wool-workers rise in the Ciompi revolt (1378), banks crash, and a new image of death haunts art and faith.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Le rivolte (with Eventi: La rivolta dei Ciompi)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Florence's wool-workers seize the city in 1378; across Europe the poor, taxed for wars, turn revolt into the crisis century's second language.
