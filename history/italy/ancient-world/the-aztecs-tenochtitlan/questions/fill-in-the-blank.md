# The aztecs tenochtitlan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 12 §4 The pre-Columbian civilisations and the clash with the Europeans" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Maya cities, the Aztec Tenochtitlan and the Inca empire — civilisations of millions — meet a few hundred armed strangers, and the Europeans are in turn seen through indigenous eyes.
