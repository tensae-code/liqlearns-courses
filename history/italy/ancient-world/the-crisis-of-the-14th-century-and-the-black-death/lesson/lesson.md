# The crisis of the 14th century and the black death

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The crisis of the 14th century and the black death" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–3 Economy and society: the factors of the crisis; The effects of the crisis and the spread of war; The Great Plague

Harvests fail, famine and war thin the population, then in 1347 the plague Boccaccio describes kills a third of Europe and changes the price of everything, above all labour.

## Sub lesson 2 — La crisi del '300 (module: causes, plague, epidemics and social control)

Famine, plague and war strike together; cities answer with health boards and quarantines — an early machinery of social control is born of the epidemic.
