# The crisis of the 14th century and the black death — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–3 Economy and society: the factors of the crisis; The effects of the crisis and the spread of war; The Great Plague", harvests fail, famine and war thin the population, then in 1347 the plague Boccaccio describes kills a third of Europe and changes the price of everything, above all labour (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La crisi del '300 (module: causes, plague, epidemics and social control)", famine, plague and war strike together; cities answer with health boards and quarantines — an early machinery of social control is born of the epidemic (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
