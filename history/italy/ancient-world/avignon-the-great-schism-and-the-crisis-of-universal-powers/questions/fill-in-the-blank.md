# Avignon the great schism and the crisis of universal powers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1–3 The crisis of the Empire; The Papacy of Avignon and the Great Schism; New spiritual stirrings and conciliarism" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Boniface VIII is seized at Anagni, the popes move to Avignon for seventy years, then two and three rival popes split Christendom until councils claim authority over popes.
