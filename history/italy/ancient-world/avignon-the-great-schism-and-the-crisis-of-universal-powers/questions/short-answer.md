# Avignon the great schism and the crisis of universal powers — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1–3 The crisis of the Empire; The Papacy of Avignon and the Great Schism; New spiritual stirrings and conciliarism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Boniface VIII is seized at Anagni, the popes move to Avignon for seventy years, then two and three rival popes split Christendom until councils claim authority over popes.
