# Avignon the great schism and the crisis of universal powers — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1–3 The crisis of the Empire; The Papacy of Avignon and the Great Schism; New spiritual stirrings and conciliarism", Boniface VIII is seized at Anagni, the popes move to Avignon for seventy years, then two and three rival popes split Christendom until councils claim authority over popes
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1–3 The crisis of the Empire; The Papacy of Avignon and the Great Schism; New spiritual stirrings and conciliarism", Boniface VIII is seized at Anagni, the popes move to Avignon for seventy years, then two and three rival popes split Christendom until councils claim authority over popes
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
