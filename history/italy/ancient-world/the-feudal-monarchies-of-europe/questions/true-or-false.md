# The feudal monarchies of europe — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–5 The formation of the feudal monarchies; Capetian France; Plantagenet England; The kingdoms of the Iberian Peninsula; Eastern Europe and Scandinavia", kings slowly turn feudal overlordship into states — Philip Augustus takes Normandy, Henry II's heirs sign Magna Carta (in the Documents), Iberian and northern kingdoms consolidate (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La monarchia francese: dall'ascesa allo scontro con il papato; La monarchia inglese; I regni nella Penisola iberica", Philip IV humiliates Boniface VIII at Anagni, England's barons extract Magna Carta (DOC), and Iberia's kingdoms grow on the Reconquista frontier (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
