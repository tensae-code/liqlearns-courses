# The feudal monarchies of europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–5 The formation of the feudal monarchies; Capetian France; Plantagenet England; The kingdoms of the Iberian Peninsula; Eastern Europe and Scandinavia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kings slowly turn feudal overlordship into states — Philip Augustus takes Normandy, Henry II's heirs sign Magna Carta (in the Documents), Iberian and northern kingdoms consolidate.

**2.** Tell the story of "La monarchia francese: dall'ascesa allo scontro con il papato; La monarchia inglese; I regni nella Penisola iberica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Philip IV humiliates Boniface VIII at Anagni, England's barons extract Magna Carta (DOC), and Iberia's kingdoms grow on the Reconquista frontier.
