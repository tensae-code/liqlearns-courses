# The feudal monarchies of europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§1–5 The formation of the feudal monarchies; Capetian France; Plantagenet England; The kingdoms of the Iberian Peninsula; Eastern Europe and Scandinavia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kings slowly turn feudal overlordship into states — Philip Augustus takes Normandy, Henry II's heirs sign Magna Carta (in the Documents), Iberian and northern kingdoms consolidate.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La monarchia francese: dall'ascesa allo scontro con il papato; La monarchia inglese; I regni nella Penisola iberica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Philip IV humiliates Boniface VIII at Anagni, England's barons extract Magna Carta (DOC), and Iberia's kingdoms grow on the Reconquista frontier.
