# The crusades east and west — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§1–3 A Europe in movement; The Crusades in the East; The Crusades in the West", pilgrimage roads fill, Urban II's call sends armies to take Jerusalem (1099) and found Latin states, while in Spain the Reconquista turns the same holy-war idea against al-Andalus
