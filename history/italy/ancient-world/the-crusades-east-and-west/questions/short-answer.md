# The crusades east and west — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§1–3 A Europe in movement; The Crusades in the East; The Crusades in the West" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
