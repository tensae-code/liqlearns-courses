# The hundred years war and the making of france and spain — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 3, Cap. 8 §4 The Hundred Years' War between France and England, and the unification of Spain — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** La guerra dei Cent'anni; Esercito e fisco; L'emergere delle nazioni; Ferdinando e Isabella — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) English kings claim the French crown and win at Crécy and Agincourt until Joan of Arc turns the war; across the Pyrenees Ferdinand and Isabella's marriage joins Castile and Aragon into Spain
b) a hundred years of war build standing armies and taxes, forge French and English nations, and end with Spain united under Ferdinand and Isabella

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
