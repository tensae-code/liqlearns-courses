# The hundred years war and the making of france and spain — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §4 The Hundred Years' War between France and England, and the unification of Spain" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** English kings claim the French crown and win at Crécy and Agincourt until Joan of Arc turns the war; across the Pyrenees Ferdinand and Isabella's marriage joins Castile and Aragon into Spain.

**2.** Tell the story of "La guerra dei Cent'anni; Esercito e fisco; L'emergere delle nazioni; Ferdinando e Isabella" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A hundred years of war build standing armies and taxes, forge French and English nations, and end with Spain united under Ferdinand and Isabella.
