# The hundred years war and the making of france and spain — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §4 The Hundred Years' War between France and England, and the unification of Spain", English kings claim the French crown and win at Crécy and Agincourt until Joan of Arc turns the war; across the Pyrenees Ferdinand and Isabella's marriage joins Castile and Aragon into Spain (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La guerra dei Cent'anni; Esercito e fisco; L'emergere delle nazioni; Ferdinando e Isabella", a hundred years of war build standing armies and taxes, forge French and English nations, and end with Spain united under Ferdinand and Isabella (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
