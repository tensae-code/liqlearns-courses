# Tamerlane and ming china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §6 The Asian scene: the parabola of Tamerlane and Ming China" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Timur rebuilds a Mongol-style empire from Samarkand and crushes the Ottomans at Ankara (1402), while the Ming expel the Mongols and refound a Chinese empire.
