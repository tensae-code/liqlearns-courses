# Tamerlane and ming china — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 3, Cap. 8 §6 The Asian scene: the parabola of Tamerlane and Ming China" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Timur rebuilds a Mongol-style empire from Samarkand and crushes the Ottomans at Ankara (1402), while the Ming expel the Mongols and refound a Chinese empire.
