## Unità 3, Cap. 8 §6 The Asian scene: the parabola of Tamerlane and Ming China

Timur rebuilds a Mongol-style empire from Samarkand and crushes the Ottomans at Ankara (1402), while the Ming expel the Mongols and refound a Chinese empire.
