# The investiture contest — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §5 The clash over investitures between pope and emperor", Gregory VII and Henry IV fight over who appoints bishops; the emperor stands barefoot in the snow at Canossa before Matilda's castle, then marches on Rome — the contest ends in compromise at Worms
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
