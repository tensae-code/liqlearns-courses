# The investiture contest — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 2 §5 The clash over investitures between pope and emperor" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Gregory VII and Henry IV fight over who appoints bishops; the emperor stands barefoot in the snow at Canossa before Matilda's castle, then marches on Rome — the contest ends in compromise at Worms.
