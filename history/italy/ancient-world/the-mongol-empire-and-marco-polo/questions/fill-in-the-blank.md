# The mongol empire and marco polo — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §7 The empire of the Mongols" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Genghis Khan's horsemen build the largest land empire in history, the Pax Mongolica reopens the roads to China and the Venetian Marco Polo rides home with its wonders.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "L'epoca dei mongoli; La via della seta e Marco Polo; Il Giappone" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Mongol peace joins Europe to China; Marco Polo walks it home, and Japan's separate feudal path closes the Asian survey.
