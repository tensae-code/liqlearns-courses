# The mongol empire and marco polo — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §7 The empire of the Mongols", Genghis Khan's horsemen build the largest land empire in history, the Pax Mongolica reopens the roads to China and the Venetian Marco Polo rides home with its wonders (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "L'epoca dei mongoli; La via della seta e Marco Polo; Il Giappone", the Mongol peace joins Europe to China; Marco Polo walks it home, and Japan's separate feudal path closes the Asian survey (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
