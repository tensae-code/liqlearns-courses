# The mongol empire and marco polo — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 5 §7 The empire of the Mongols" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Genghis Khan's horsemen build the largest land empire in history, the Pax Mongolica reopens the roads to China and the Venetian Marco Polo rides home with its wonders.

**2.** Tell the story of "L'epoca dei mongoli; La via della seta e Marco Polo; Il Giappone" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Mongol peace joins Europe to China; Marco Polo walks it home, and Japan's separate feudal path closes the Asian survey.
