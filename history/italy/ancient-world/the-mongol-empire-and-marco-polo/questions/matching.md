# The mongol empire and marco polo — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 2, Cap. 5 §7 The empire of the Mongols — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** L'epoca dei mongoli; La via della seta e Marco Polo; Il Giappone — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Genghis Khan's horsemen build the largest land empire in history, the Pax Mongolica reopens the roads to China and the Venetian Marco Polo rides home with its wonders
b) the Mongol peace joins Europe to China; Marco Polo walks it home, and Japan's separate feudal path closes the Asian survey

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
