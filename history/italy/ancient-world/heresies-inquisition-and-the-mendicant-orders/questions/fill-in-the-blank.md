# Heresies inquisition and the mendicant orders — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§4–5 Heresies and the Inquisition; The mendicant orders: Dominicans and Franciscans" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Waldensians and Cathars preach poverty against a rich Church and meet the Inquisition; Francis and Dominic answer the same hunger from inside, with orders of begging friars.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Gli ordini mendicanti (with the heresy and inquisitor dossiers)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Francis' rule (DOC) and Dominic's preachers answer the cities' new poverty and heresy; the Church replies with both embrace and inquisition.
