# Heresies inquisition and the mendicant orders — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§4–5 Heresies and the Inquisition; The mendicant orders: Dominicans and Franciscans", Waldensians and Cathars preach poverty against a rich Church and meet the Inquisition; Francis and Dominic answer the same hunger from inside, with orders of begging friars (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Gli ordini mendicanti (with the heresy and inquisitor dossiers)", Francis' rule (DOC) and Dominic's preachers answer the cities' new poverty and heresy; the Church replies with both embrace and inquisition (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
