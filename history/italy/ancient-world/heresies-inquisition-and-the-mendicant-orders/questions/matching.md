# Heresies inquisition and the mendicant orders — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§4–5 Heresies and the Inquisition; The mendicant orders: Dominicans and Franciscans — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Gli ordini mendicanti (with the heresy and inquisitor dossiers) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Waldensians and Cathars preach poverty against a rich Church and meet the Inquisition; Francis and Dominic answer the same hunger from inside, with orders of begging friars
b) Francis' rule (DOC) and Dominic's preachers answer the cities' new poverty and heresy; the Church replies with both embrace and inquisition

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
