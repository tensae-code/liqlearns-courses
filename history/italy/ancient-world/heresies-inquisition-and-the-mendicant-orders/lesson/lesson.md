# Heresies inquisition and the mendicant orders

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Heresies inquisition and the mendicant orders" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§4–5 Heresies and the Inquisition; The mendicant orders: Dominicans and Franciscans

Waldensians and Cathars preach poverty against a rich Church and meet the Inquisition; Francis and Dominic answer the same hunger from inside, with orders of begging friars.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gli ordini mendicanti (with the heresy and inquisitor dossiers)

Francis' rule (DOC) and Dominic's preachers answer the cities' new poverty and heresy; the Church replies with both embrace and inquisition.
