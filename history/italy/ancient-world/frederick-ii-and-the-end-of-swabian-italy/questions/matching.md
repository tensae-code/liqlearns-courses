# Frederick ii and the end of swabian italy — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 2, Cap. 6 §§2–4 The empire of Frederick II; Frederick II and the Italian Communes; The end of Swabian rule in Italy — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** L'impero e il Mezzogiorno d'Italia dopo la morte di Federico II; Il Vespro siciliano (Eventi) — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the 'wonder of the world' rules a modern state from Palermo and fights popes and communes; after his death his heirs fall at Benevento and Tagliacozzo and Angevin Frenchmen take the South
b) Frederick's Liber Augustalis (DOC) orders the South; a generation later the Sicilian Vespers (1282) throw out the Angevins and split the island from Naples

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
