# Frederick ii and the end of swabian italy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §§2–4 The empire of Frederick II; Frederick II and the Italian Communes; The end of Swabian rule in Italy", the 'wonder of the world' rules a modern state from Palermo and fights popes and communes; after his death his heirs fall at Benevento and Tagliacozzo and Angevin Frenchmen take the South (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "L'impero e il Mezzogiorno d'Italia dopo la morte di Federico II; Il Vespro siciliano (Eventi)", Frederick's Liber Augustalis (DOC) orders the South; a generation later the Sicilian Vespers (1282) throw out the Angevins and split the island from Naples (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
