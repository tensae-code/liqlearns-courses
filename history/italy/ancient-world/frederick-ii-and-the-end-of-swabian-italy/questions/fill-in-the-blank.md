# Frederick ii and the end of swabian italy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 6 §§2–4 The empire of Frederick II; Frederick II and the Italian Communes; The end of Swabian rule in Italy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 'wonder of the world' rules a modern state from Palermo and fights popes and communes; after his death his heirs fall at Benevento and Tagliacozzo and Angevin Frenchmen take the South.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "L'impero e il Mezzogiorno d'Italia dopo la morte di Federico II; Il Vespro siciliano (Eventi)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Frederick's Liber Augustalis (DOC) orders the South; a generation later the Sicilian Vespers (1282) throw out the Angevins and split the island from Naples.
