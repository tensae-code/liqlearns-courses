# Communes against the empire barbarossa and legnano — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §3 The clash between the Communes and the Empire" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Frederick Barbarossa descends to crush the communes' liberties; the Lombard League's carroccio infantry stops his knights at Legnano (1176) and the Peace of Constance recognises the cities.
