# Communes against the empire barbarossa and legnano — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 2, Cap. 4 §3 The clash between the Communes and the Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Frederick Barbarossa descends to crush the communes' liberties; the Lombard League's carroccio infantry stops his knights at Legnano (1176) and the Peace of Constance recognises the cities.
