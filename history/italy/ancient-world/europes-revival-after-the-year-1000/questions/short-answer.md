# Europes revival after the year 1000 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–3 The climate improves and the population grows; The progress of agriculture; A society in movement: the countryside" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** After the year 1000 a warmer climate, new ploughs and cleared land feed a growing population, and the fear that the world would end in the Mille quietly gives way to expansion.
