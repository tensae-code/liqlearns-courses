# Europes revival after the year 1000 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–3 The climate improves and the population grows; The progress of agriculture; A society in movement: the countryside" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** After the year 1000 a warmer climate, new ploughs and cleared land feed a growing population, and the fear that the world would end in the Mille quietly gives way to expansion.
