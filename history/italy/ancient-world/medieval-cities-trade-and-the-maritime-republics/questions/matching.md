# Medieval cities trade and the maritime republics — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§4–6 The cities revive with new protagonists; The expansion of trade; The maritime republics — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Uomini e spazi; Innovazioni tecniche; Le grandi macchine; La viabilità; Splendori e miserie delle città; Il nuovo mondo dei mercanti; Le città marinare italiane — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) towns wake up, merchants and artisans become new powers, trade routes reopen across medieval Europe and Venice, Genoa, Pisa and Amalfi take to the sea
b) mills, roads and ships remake the landscape; merchants invent a new culture of credit and companies while Venice and Genoa rule the Mediterranean routes

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
