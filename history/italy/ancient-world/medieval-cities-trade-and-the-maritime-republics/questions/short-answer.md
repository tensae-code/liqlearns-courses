# Medieval cities trade and the maritime republics — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§4–6 The cities revive with new protagonists; The expansion of trade; The maritime republics" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Towns wake up, merchants and artisans become new powers, trade routes reopen across medieval Europe and Venice, Genoa, Pisa and Amalfi take to the sea.

**2.** Tell the story of "Uomini e spazi; Innovazioni tecniche; Le grandi macchine; La viabilità; Splendori e miserie delle città; Il nuovo mondo dei mercanti; Le città marinare italiane" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mills, roads and ships remake the landscape; merchants invent a new culture of credit and companies while Venice and Genoa rule the Mediterranean routes.
