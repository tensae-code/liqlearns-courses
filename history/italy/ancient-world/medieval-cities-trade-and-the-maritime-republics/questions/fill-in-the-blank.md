# Medieval cities trade and the maritime republics — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§4–6 The cities revive with new protagonists; The expansion of trade; The maritime republics" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Towns wake up, merchants and artisans become new powers, trade routes reopen across medieval Europe and Venice, Genoa, Pisa and Amalfi take to the sea.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Uomini e spazi; Innovazioni tecniche; Le grandi macchine; La viabilità; Splendori e miserie delle città; Il nuovo mondo dei mercanti; Le città marinare italiane" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mills, roads and ships remake the landscape; merchants invent a new culture of credit and companies while Venice and Genoa rule the Mediterranean routes.
