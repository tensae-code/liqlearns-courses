# Medieval cities trade and the maritime republics — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§4–6 The cities revive with new protagonists; The expansion of trade; The maritime republics", towns wake up, merchants and artisans become new powers, trade routes reopen across medieval Europe and Venice, Genoa, Pisa and Amalfi take to the sea (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Uomini e spazi; Innovazioni tecniche; Le grandi macchine; La viabilità; Splendori e miserie delle città; Il nuovo mondo dei mercanti; Le città marinare italiane", mills, roads and ships remake the landscape; merchants invent a new culture of credit and companies while Venice and Genoa rule the Mediterranean routes (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
