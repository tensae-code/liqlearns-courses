Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Modulo 2, Cap. 5 §§1–2 The two directions of European colonial expansion; The slave trade and the Atlantic triangular trade" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Gustavus Vassa's own narrative (Fare storia) and the slavers' ships' accounts price the triangle in human lives; Polanyi's Dahomey study ties the trade to African states.
