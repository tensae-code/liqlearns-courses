# Decolonisation north africa

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Unità 4, Cap. 14 §6 The Middle East and North Africa toward decolonisation

Mandates end and national movements rise from Morocco to Egypt; Hopwood's Storiografia follows Bourguiba's Tunisia bargaining its "internal autonomy" toward independence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unità 4, Cap. 15 §3 The awakening of the Arab world: the cases of Egypt and Algeria

Nasser's Egypt defies the old empires at Suez while the FLN's proclamation to the Algerian people (Document) opens a war France cannot win politically — North Africa's decolonisation turns armed and pan-Arab.

## Sub lesson 3 — Modulo 10 §6 L'indipendenza del Maghreb

Morocco and Tunisia negotiate, Algeria fights an eight-year war — the Maghreb's two roads out of empire.
