# Decolonisation north africa — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unità 4, Cap. 15 §3 The awakening of the Arab world: the cases of Egypt and Algeria" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Nasser's Egypt defies the old empires at Suez while the FLN's proclamation to the Algerian people (Document) opens a war France cannot win politically — North Africa's decolonisation turns armed and pan-Arab.

**2.** Tell the story of "Modulo 10 §6 L'indipendenza del Maghreb" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Morocco and Tunisia negotiate, Algeria fights an eight-year war — the Maghreb's two roads out of empire.
