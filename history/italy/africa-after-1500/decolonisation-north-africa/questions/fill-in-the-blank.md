# Decolonisation north africa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 14 §6 The Middle East and North Africa toward decolonisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mandates end and national movements rise from Morocco to Egypt; Hopwood's Storiografia follows Bourguiba's Tunisia bargaining its "internal autonomy" toward independence.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unità 4, Cap. 15 §3 The awakening of the Arab world: the cases of Egypt and Algeria" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Nasser's Egypt defies the old empires at Suez while the FLN's proclamation to the Algerian people (Document) opens a war France cannot win politically — North Africa's decolonisation turns armed and pan-Arab.
