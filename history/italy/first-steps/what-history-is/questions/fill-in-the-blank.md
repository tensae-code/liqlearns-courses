# What history is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Anteprima: Il concetto di Medioevo; Periodizzare; Medioevo e feudalesimo; Oltre l'eurocentrismo" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The course opens by questioning its own frame — 'Middle Ages' is a label invented later, periodising is a choice, and the story must not be told from Europe alone.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Anteprima: Il '700; La periodizzazione; La modernità politica europea" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The volume opens by defending its own frame — a 'long' 1700s from 1660 to the revolutions — teaching periodisation itself as a historian's choice, not a fact of nature.
