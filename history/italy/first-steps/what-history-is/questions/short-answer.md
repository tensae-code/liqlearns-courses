# What history is — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Anteprima: Il '700; La periodizzazione; La modernità politica europea" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The volume opens by defending its own frame — a 'long' 1700s from 1660 to the revolutions — teaching periodisation itself as a historian's choice, not a fact of nature.

**2.** Tell the story of "Anteprima: La guerra come evento periodizzante: il secolo breve" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The volume opens by defending its frame — Hobsbawm's "short twentieth century" (1914–1991) against the "long nineteenth" — teaching periodisation itself as a historian's choice before a single event is told.
