# What history is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Anteprima: Il concetto di Medioevo; Periodizzare; Medioevo e feudalesimo; Oltre l'eurocentrismo"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the course opens by questioning its own frame — 'Middle Ages' is a label invented later, periodising is a choice, and the story must not be told from Europe alone  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
