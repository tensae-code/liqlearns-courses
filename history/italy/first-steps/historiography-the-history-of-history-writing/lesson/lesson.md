# Historiography the history of history writing

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Historiography the history of history writing" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Storici a confronto and Storiografia strands in every unit

Paired historians argue the same question — was there an agricultural revolution? when do national states begin? is the Renaissance a myth? — so learners watch history being made from evidence.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Historiography the history of history writing as Italy's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
