# From evidence to a written history — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Documenti and 'Un documento, un diritto' strands in every unit", the Declaration of Independence, the Rights of Man, Olympe de Gouges, the Gettysburg Address and the Berlin Act are set as the student's own evidence, each tied to a right still in force (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "The Fare storia dossiers throughout (178 numbered pieces)", from Galileo's two planes of science to Gramsci's missed agrarian revolution, every module closes its argument in documents the student must weigh — the Laterza method in its purest form (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
