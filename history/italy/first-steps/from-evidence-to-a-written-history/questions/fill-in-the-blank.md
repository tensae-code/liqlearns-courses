# From evidence to a written history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Documenti and Laboratorio storia strands in every unit" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Each unit closes its chapters with primary documents — an agrarian contract, Magna Carta, Las Casas, the Bill of Rights — to be read, questioned and turned into written history.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Fare storia dossiers and Guida allo studio / Sviluppare le competenze strands throughout" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Over 180 numbered documents — from Magna Carta to the Putney debates — are set as the student's own evidence, each with study guides teaching how to read, compare and write from them.
