# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Documenti, Storiografia, Laboratorio storia and Storici a confronto strands in every unit" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Fischer against Ferguson on Germany's war guilt, Corner against Ben-Ghiat on fascist modernity, Dian against Parsi on the post-Western order — the student watches historians argue the century from its evidence, 1900 to Ukraine.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Fare storia dossiers throughout (207 numbered pieces in four dossier modules)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** From Clark on whether 1914 could have gone otherwise and the London Pact, through Arendt on totalitarianism, Levi and Höss on the Shoah, the Bandung manifesto and King's America, to Darnton at the Berlin Wall and Piketty on inequality — the Laterza method in its fullest form.
