# From evidence to a written history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Documenti and Laboratorio storia strands in every unit"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) each unit closes its chapters with primary documents — an agrarian contract, Magna Carta, Las Casas, the Bill of Rights — to be read, questioned and turned into written history  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "The Fare storia dossiers and Guida allo studio / Sviluppare le competenze strands throughout"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) over 180 numbered documents — from Magna Carta to the Putney debates — are set as the student's own evidence, each with study guides teaching how to read, compare and write from them  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
