# History — Argentina course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Argentina's
own — built from Argentina's *Cuadernos para el aula:
Ciencias Sociales 6* teacher guide (Spanish). Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below sit inside Level 1, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grade the book comes from
lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: one big case, the
building of the Argentine state 1860–1916, told in three
turns — the state built first, then the great immigration it
engineered, then the conquest of indigenous territories and
the book's question of who progress left out. That ladder IS
the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — building the Argentine state, 1860–1916**
(Cuadernos para el aula: Ciencias Sociales 6, teacher guide):

- `modern-world/` — Level 1, the course's one title.
  Argentina Building the State, 1860–1916 (one national
  state built over the loose provinces — army, laws,
  schools, railways — a country changing fast in one
  generation, and foreign capital building the railways and
  meat-packing plants of the export boom); Argentina: The
  Great Immigration (crisis and crowding pushing millions
  out of Western Europe, and the state pulling them in under
  the 1876 Immigration and Colonisation Law, until nearly a
  third of the country was foreign-born); and The Conquest
  of Indigenous Territories (the army sent against the
  indigenous peoples of the south and the Chaco, their
  hunting and fishing lands taken, and immigrant families
  crowded into conventillos — who progress left out).
