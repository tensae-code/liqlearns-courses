# Hands-on — Argentina Building the State, 1860–1916

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the four things the national state built out loud, one by one: army, laws, schools, railways. Now say what each one joined together. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw a simple map: separate boxes for the provinces, Buenos Aires marked on the coast, and railway lines drawn in joining the boxes to the port. Beside it, draw one meat-packing plant and one ship. Say each part aloud as you draw it: what it is, and what it did for the new country.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see one new thing — a road, a building, a service — changing how people live every day, note it: what changed, who it joined together, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
