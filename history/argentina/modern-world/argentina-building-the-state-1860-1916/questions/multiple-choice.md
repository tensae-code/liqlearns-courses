# Argentina Building the State, 1860–1916 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Argentina's leaders build between 1860 and 1916? (Sub lesson 1)
a) one national state over the loose provinces  b) a wall around Buenos Aires  c) a fleet of warships only  d) nothing — the provinces joined by themselves
**Answer: a) one national state over the loose provinces** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does the book show the country changing? (Sub lesson 2)
a) only through laws  b) through stories, photographs and accounts of Buenos Aires and the pampas  c) through songs only  d) it does not show the change
**Answer: b) through stories, photographs and accounts of Buenos Aires and the pampas** — That is what this lesson teaches.
