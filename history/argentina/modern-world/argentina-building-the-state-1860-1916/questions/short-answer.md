# Argentina Building the State, 1860–1916 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four things the national state built to hold the country together. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** An army, laws, schools and railways.

**2.** What tied Argentina's new wealth to markets across the ocean? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Foreign capital built the railways and meat-packing plants of the export boom, so Argentina's wealth depended on selling its produce to buyers across the ocean.
