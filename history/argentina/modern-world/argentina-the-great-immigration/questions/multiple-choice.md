# Argentina: The Great Immigration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What pushed millions of people to leave Western Europe? (Sub lesson 1)
a) crisis and crowding at home  b) a law forbidding ships  c) too much land for too few people  d) cold weather only
**Answer: a) crisis and crowding at home** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Argentine state do under the 1876 Immigration and Colonisation Law? (Sub lesson 2)
a) closed the ports  b) opened offices in Europe and paid passages  c) taxed every newcomer  d) sent its own people to Europe
**Answer: b) opened offices in Europe and paid passages** — That is what this lesson teaches.
