# Argentina: The Great Immigration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What were the push and the pull of the great immigration? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The push was crisis and crowding in Western Europe; the pull was Argentina's promise of land and work across the Atlantic.

**2.** What did Alberdi's words "to govern is to populate" mean for Argentina? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** That a wide country with few people must bring people in to grow — the idea the state turned into the 1876 Immigration and Colonisation Law.
