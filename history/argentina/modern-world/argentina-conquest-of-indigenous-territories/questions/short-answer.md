# The Conquest of Indigenous Territories — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What was taken from the indigenous peoples of the south and the Chaco?
**Answer:** Their hunting and fishing lands, taken by the state's army in the conquest of indigenous territories.

**2.** How did many welcomed immigrant families end up living in the cities?
**Answer:** Crowded into conventillos — tenements where family after family rented single rooms around a shared courtyard.
