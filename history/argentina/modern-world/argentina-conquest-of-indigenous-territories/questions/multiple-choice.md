# The Conquest of Indigenous Territories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Argentine state send against the indigenous peoples of the south and the Chaco?
a) its army  b) teachers only  c) ships of food  d) nothing — it left them alone
**Answer: a) its army** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is a conventillo?
a) a railway station  b) a crowded tenement where immigrant families rented single rooms around a shared courtyard  c) a meat-packing plant  d) a law of 1876
**Answer: b) a crowded tenement where immigrant families rented single rooms around a shared courtyard** — That is what this lesson teaches.
