# The Conquest of Indigenous Territories — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The lands of the south and the Chaco were empty before the state took them.
**Answer: False** — indigenous peoples lived on them, on their hunting and fishing lands.

**2.** The book teaches that progress reached everyone in the new Argentina.
**Answer: False** — the book asks who progress left out: the indigenous peoples who lost their lands and the immigrant families crowded into conventillos.
