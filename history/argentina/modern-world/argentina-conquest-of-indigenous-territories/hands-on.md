# Hands-on — The Conquest of Indigenous Territories

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask the book's question out loud: who did progress leave out? Name the two answers from the lesson: the indigenous peoples of the south and the Chaco, whose lands were taken, and the immigrant families crowded into the conventillos. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw two pictures side by side: on one side, a simple map of Argentina with the south and the Chaco shaded and marked as the indigenous peoples' hunting and fishing lands; on the other, a conventillo courtyard with many doors around it, one for each family. Say each part aloud as you draw it: whose land it was, and how the families lived.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see a change or a celebration that helps some people and leaves others out, note it: who gained, who was left out, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
