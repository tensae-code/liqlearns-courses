# Video script — The Conquest of Indigenous Territories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The new state that welcomed shiploads of newcomers took land from the people who had always lived on it — and progress did not reach everyone.

Look at the map. The story so far lit up Buenos Aires, the railways, the ports. Now look south — to the wide lands of the south — and north-east, to the Chaco. These lands were not empty. Indigenous peoples lived there, hunting and fishing as their ancestors had.

Now watch what the new state does. It sends its army. Campaign after campaign, the indigenous peoples are defeated, and their hunting and fishing lands are taken into the growing country.

Then come back to the city, and look closely at one building: a conventillo. Room by room around one courtyard, family after family of the welcomed immigrants, crowded into single rooms. This is where the great journey ended for many of them.

Remember it this way: the state asked who would come — but the book asks a harder question: who did progress leave out?

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
