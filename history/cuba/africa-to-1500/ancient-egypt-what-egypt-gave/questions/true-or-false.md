# Ancient egypt what egypt gave — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pharaoh, priests and officials organise village communities to build and irrigate — a state standing on peasants' backs
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Ancient egypt what egypt gave" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
