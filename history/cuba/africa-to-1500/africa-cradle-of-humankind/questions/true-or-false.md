# Africa cradle of humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ape-like ancestors become tool-making humans; work and language turn a band into a society
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Africa cradle of humankind" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
