# From stone tools to farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gatherers become farmers and herders; the clan shares food, tools and the harvest because no one yet owns the means to live
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "From stone tools to farming" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
