# Peopling of the americas

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Peopling of the americas" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## 1.1 The peopling of America

Hunters cross the Bering land bridge into an empty continent; millennia later their descendants fill two Americas

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Peopling of the americas as Cuba's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
