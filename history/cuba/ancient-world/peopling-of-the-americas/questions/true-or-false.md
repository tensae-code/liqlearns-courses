# Peopling of the americas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hunters cross the Bering land bridge into an empty continent; millennia later their descendants fill two Americas
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Peopling of the americas" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
