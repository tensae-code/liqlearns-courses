# Feudalism at its height 11th to 13th centuries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Clearances, mills and growing towns mark feudalism's most productive centuries
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Feudalism at its height 11th to 13th centuries" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
