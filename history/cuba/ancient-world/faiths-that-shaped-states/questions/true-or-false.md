# Faiths that shaped states — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Church owns land, crowns kings and judges heretics — a state above states in medieval Europe
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Faiths that shaped states" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
