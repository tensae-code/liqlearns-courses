# The aztecs tenochtitlan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tenochtitlan, a lake city bigger than London, rules by tribute and war until 1519 stands just beyond the page
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The aztecs tenochtitlan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
