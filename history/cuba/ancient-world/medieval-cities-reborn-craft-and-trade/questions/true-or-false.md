# Medieval cities reborn craft and trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Merchants and artisans win charters from lords; inside feudal walls a burgher class that does not fit the order is born
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Medieval cities reborn craft and trade" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
