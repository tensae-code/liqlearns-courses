# Introduction to america before 1492 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Americas hold everything from bands to empires at once; pupils must explain why development was so uneven
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Introduction to america before 1492" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
