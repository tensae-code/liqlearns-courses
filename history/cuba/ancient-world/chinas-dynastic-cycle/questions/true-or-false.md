# Chinas dynastic cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** China's dynasties, India's kingdoms and steppe empires develop on their own rhythm, ahead of Europe in much of the period
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Chinas dynastic cycle" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
