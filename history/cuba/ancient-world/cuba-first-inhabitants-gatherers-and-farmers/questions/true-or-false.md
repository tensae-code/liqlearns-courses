# Cuba first inhabitants gatherers and farmers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Canoe migrants from South America cross the Antilles; gatherer-hunter-fisher groups live in caves while farmer-potter groups plant conucos, make casabe from yuca, and are led by a cacique and a behíque
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba first inhabitants gatherers and farmers" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
