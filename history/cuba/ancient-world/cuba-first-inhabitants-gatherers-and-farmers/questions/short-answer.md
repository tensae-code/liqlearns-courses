# Cuba first inhabitants gatherers and farmers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "1.1 First settlers, occupations and ways of life"?
**Answer:** Canoe migrants from South America cross the Antilles; gatherer-hunter-fisher groups live in caves while farmer-potter groups plant conucos, make casabe from yuca, and are led by a cacique and a behíque

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Ch.1 activities: comparing gatherer and farmer-potter groups, casabe-making, investigating aborigines in your own locality"?
**Answer:** Two ways of aboriginal life are set side by side in a table; the pupil then hunts for the aborigines who once lived in their own town
