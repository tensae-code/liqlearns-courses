# Greece decline and hellenistic transition — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The poleis exhaust each other in war; Macedon conquers them and Alexander spreads Greek culture east
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Greece decline and hellenistic transition" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
