# Roman empire rise splendour crisis decline — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Augustus founds an empire that rules the Mediterranean world, then crisis, division and invasion bring it down
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Roman empire rise splendour crisis decline" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
