# Community society traits and its breakdown — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Surplus appears, some families hold more than others, and equality quietly breaks into chiefs, priests and the first classes
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Community society traits and its breakdown" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
