# Crisis of feudalism and birth of capitalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Plague, revolts and money eat the feudal order from inside; in its cracks merchants and workshops grow a new society
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Crisis of feudalism and birth of capitalism" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
