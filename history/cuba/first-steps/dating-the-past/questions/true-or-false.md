# Dating the past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A fortress built with hand tools means something different from the same stones raised by cranes today; knowing before/after and where makes the fact understandable
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Dating the past" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
