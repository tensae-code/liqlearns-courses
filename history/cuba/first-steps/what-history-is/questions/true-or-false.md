# What history is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A child asks what their country's past is; the book answers that national history is the people's own life, heroes and customs from their origins
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "What history is" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
