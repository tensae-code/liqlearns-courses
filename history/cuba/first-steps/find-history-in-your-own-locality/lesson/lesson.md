# Find history in your own locality

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Find history in your own locality" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Local history: monuments, plaques, street names in your town

The statues in the park and names on the streets are neighbours' ancestors; every corner of Cuba has a place in the Patria's history

## How to teach local history (la historia local)

The teacher sends pupils to their own municipality's monuments, museums and elders so the national story is found standing in their own street

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Find history in your own locality as Cuba's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
