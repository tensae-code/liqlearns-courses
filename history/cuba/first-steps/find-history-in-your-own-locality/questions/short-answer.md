# Find history in your own locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Local history: monuments, plaques, street names in your town"?
**Answer:** The statues in the park and names on the streets are neighbours' ancestors; every corner of Cuba has a place in the Patria's history

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "How to teach local history (la historia local)"?
**Answer:** The teacher sends pupils to their own municipality's monuments, museums and elders so the national story is found standing in their own street
