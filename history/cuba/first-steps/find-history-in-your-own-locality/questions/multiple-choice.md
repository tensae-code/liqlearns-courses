# Find history in your own locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Local history: monuments, plaques, street names in your town"?
a) The statues in the park and names on the streets are neighbours' ancestors; every corner of Cuba has a place in the Patria's history  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "How to teach local history (la historia local)"?
a) The teacher sends pupils to their own municipality's monuments, museums and elders so the national story is found standing in their own street  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
