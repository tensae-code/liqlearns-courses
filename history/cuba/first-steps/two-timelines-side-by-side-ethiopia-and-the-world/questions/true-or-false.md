# Two timelines side by side ethiopia and the world — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The whole human past is cut into periods (community, ancient, medieval…) so a Cuban classroom can hold world time in one scheme
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Two timelines side by side ethiopia and the world" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
