# From evidence to a written history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Sources of historical knowledge: museum objects, documents, buildings, coins"?
a) A hero's weapon in a museum seems to speak; objects left by aborigines and fighters tell how they lived and thought  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
