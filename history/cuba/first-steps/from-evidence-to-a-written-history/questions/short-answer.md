# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "Sources of historical knowledge: museum objects, documents, buildings, coins"?
**Answer:** A hero's weapon in a museum seems to speak; objects left by aborigines and fighters tell how they lived and thought

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Introductory activities: sorting true/false historical sources"?
**Answer:** Heroes' objects, old buildings and coins count as sources; a brand-new building and a modern map of Africa do not
