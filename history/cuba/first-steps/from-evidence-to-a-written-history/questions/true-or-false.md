# From evidence to a written history — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A hero's weapon in a museum seems to speak; objects left by aborigines and fighters tell how they lived and thought
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "From evidence to a written history" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
