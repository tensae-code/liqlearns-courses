# Historiography the history of history writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils learn to ask why a fact happened, where, and what came before and after — the historian's three questions made into school skills
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Historiography the history of history writing" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
