# Cuba weyler reconcentration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** General Weyler empties the countryside into reconcentration camps where civilians starve by the thousand, meaning to drain the sea the mambí fish swim in
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba weyler reconcentration" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
