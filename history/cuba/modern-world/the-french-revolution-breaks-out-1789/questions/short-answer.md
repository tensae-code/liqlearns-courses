# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "1.2 The French Bourgeois Revolution: causes, absolutism, Louis XVI, Enlightenment preparation, Bastille"?
**Answer:** A bankrupt king summons the Estates-General; Paris storms the Bastille on 14 July 1789 and the old regime cracks
