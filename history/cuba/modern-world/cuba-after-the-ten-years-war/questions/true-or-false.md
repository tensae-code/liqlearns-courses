# Cuba after the ten years war — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cuba emerges ruined, slavery dying but not dead, parties forming under a colony that promised reforms it did not keep
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba after the ten years war" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
