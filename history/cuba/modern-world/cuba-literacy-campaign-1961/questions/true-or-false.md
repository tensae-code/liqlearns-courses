# Cuba literacy campaign 1961 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1961 an army of young literacy teachers goes to the countryside and Cuba declares itself free of illiteracy in a year
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba literacy campaign 1961" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
