# Cuba socialist revolution character — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book closes by declaring the socialist character of the Revolution and Cuba's solidarity with other peoples as its defining turn
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba socialist revolution character" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
