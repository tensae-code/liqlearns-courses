# Cuba granma landing 1956 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "5.1 Start of the new stage of struggle (1956)"?
**Answer:** 82 expeditionaries land from the Granma at Los Cayuelos, are scattered at Alegría de Pío, and a handful regroup in the Sierra Maestra

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "8.4–8.5 Preparations for the war; Granma expedition and support for the landing"?
**Answer:** Mexico training, the yacht's storm crossing, Alegría de Pío and the Frank País uprising that covers the survivors
