# Cuba granma landing 1956 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** 82 expeditionaries land from the Granma at Los Cayuelos, are scattered at Alegría de Pío, and a handful regroup in the Sierra Maestra
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba granma landing 1956" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
