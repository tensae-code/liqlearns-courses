# Cuba bayamo first free city — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mambises take Bayamo and Perucho Figueredo's hymn is sung there; rather than surrender it, Bayamo's people burn their own city in January 1869
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba bayamo first free city" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
