# Cuba bayamo first free city — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "5.2 Bayamo, first free city of Cuba"?
**Answer:** Mambises take Bayamo and Perucho Figueredo's hymn is sung there; rather than surrender it, Bayamo's people burn their own city in January 1869

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "3.3 Main events at the war's start"?
**Answer:** Bayamo taken and burned, the hymn composed, the war's first martyrs — the rising becomes a nation in arms
