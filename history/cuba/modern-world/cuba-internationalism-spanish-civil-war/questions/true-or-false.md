# Cuba internationalism spanish civil war — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cuban volunteers, among them Pablo de la Torriente Brau, cross the Atlantic to fight fascism in Spain and fall there for another people's freedom
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba internationalism spanish civil war" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
