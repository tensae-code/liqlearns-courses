# English in north america colonisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Joint-stock companies and religious exiles plant a different colony — settler, slaveholding and self-governing — on the northern coast
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "English in north america colonisation" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
