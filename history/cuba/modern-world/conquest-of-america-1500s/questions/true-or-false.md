# Conquest of america 1500s — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Small bands of conquerors, helped by disease and division, topple the Aztec and Inca empires and enslave a continent
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Conquest of america 1500s" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
