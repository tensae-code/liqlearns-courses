# Conquest of america 1500s — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "6.6 Invasion and conquest of America by European powers in the 16th c."?
**Answer:** Small bands of conquerors, helped by disease and division, topple the Aztec and Inca empires and enslave a continent

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "2.2 Iberian conquest and colonisation of America"?
**Answer:** Spain and Portugal divide a continent at Tordesillas and build colonial societies on indigenous and African labour
