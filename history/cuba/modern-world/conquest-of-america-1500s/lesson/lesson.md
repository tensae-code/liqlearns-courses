## 6.6 Invasion and conquest of America by European powers in the 16th c.

Small bands of conquerors, helped by disease and division, topple the Aztec and Inca empires and enslave a continent

## 2.2 Iberian conquest and colonisation of America

Spain and Portugal divide a continent at Tordesillas and build colonial societies on indigenous and African labour
