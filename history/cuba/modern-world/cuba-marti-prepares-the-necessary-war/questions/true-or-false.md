# Cuba marti prepares the necessary war — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From exile Martí founds the Cuban Revolutionary Party (1892) and knits emigrants, tobacco workers' cents and veteran chiefs into one war plan
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba marti prepares the necessary war" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
