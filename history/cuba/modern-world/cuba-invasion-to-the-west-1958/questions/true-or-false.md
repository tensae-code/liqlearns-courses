# Cuba invasion to the west 1958 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Che Guevara and Camilo Cienfuegos repeat Maceo's invasion on foot to Las Villas while Fidel advances in Oriente; Santa Clara falls
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba invasion to the west 1958" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
