# Cuba spanish trade monopoly and contraband — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Spain funnels all trade through its fleet system and Seville; Cuban producers, shut out, turn to contraband with foreign traders as the island's lifeline
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba spanish trade monopoly and contraband" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
