# Cuba workers organise unions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tobacco workers, railwaymen and sugar hands build unions and a national labour confederation out of mutual-aid societies, striking against mill owners
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba workers organise unions" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
