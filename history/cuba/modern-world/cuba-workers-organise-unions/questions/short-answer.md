# Cuba workers organise unions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "2.3 Cuban workers organise"?
**Answer:** Tobacco workers, railwaymen and sugar hands build unions and a national labour confederation out of mutual-aid societies, striking against mill owners

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "6.5–6.7 Forms of confronting the Republic's situation in its first quarter-century; struggle against corruption and for institutional reform; toward a higher stage of the Cuban revolutionary movement"?
**Answer:** University reform, Julio Antonio Mella, the first Communist Party (1925) and the CNOC lift protest into organisation
