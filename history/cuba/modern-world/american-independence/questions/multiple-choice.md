# American independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "3.2 The anticolonial revolution in the Thirteen British Colonies"?
a) Colonists who taxed themselves refuse London's taxes, declare independence in 1776 and win it with French help  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "3.2 Anticolonial revolution in North America and birth of the USA"?
