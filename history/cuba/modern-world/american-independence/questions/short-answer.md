# American independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "3.2 The anticolonial revolution in the Thirteen British Colonies"?
**Answer:** Colonists who taxed themselves refuse London's taxes, declare independence in 1776 and win it with French help

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "3.2 Anticolonial revolution in North America and birth of the USA"?
**Answer:** The Thirteen Colonies' revolution is told as America's first independence — and the seed of a new empire
