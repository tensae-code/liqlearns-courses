# Fall of ussr and unipolar world — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1989–91 the socialist camp collapses without a war; one superpower remains and the contemporary world pupils live in begins
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Fall of ussr and unipolar world" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
