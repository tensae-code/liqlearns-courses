# The mexican revolution 1910 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Peasants under Zapata demand land and liberty; Villa raids and Pershing pursues; Mexico writes the first social constitution of the century
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The mexican revolution 1910" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
