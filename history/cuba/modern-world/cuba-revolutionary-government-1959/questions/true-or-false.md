# Cuba revolutionary government 1959 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A new government of the people takes office, tries war criminals, and begins transforming a dependent republic into a sovereign state
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba revolutionary government 1959" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
