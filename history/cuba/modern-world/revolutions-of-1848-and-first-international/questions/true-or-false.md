# Revolutions of 1848 and first international — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Revolution sweeps Europe's capitals in 1848, the bourgeoisie betrays it, and workers answer by founding their own International in 1864
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Revolutions of 1848 and first international" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
