# Cuba population 16th to 18th centuries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Indians vanish, Africans arrive in chains, Spaniards and criollos multiply; a mixed, layered society forms that will later call itself Cuban
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba population 16th to 18th centuries" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
