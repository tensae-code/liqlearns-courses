# Cuba moncada 26 july 1953 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "4.3 The actions of 26 July 1953"?
**Answer:** Young attackers assault the Moncada and Bayamo barracks at carnival dawn; the attack fails militarily, prisoners are murdered, but a programme and a movement are born

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "8.1–8.3 Preparation of the political vanguard; assault on the Moncada and Carlos Manuel de Céspedes barracks; trial of the 26 July survivors"?
**Answer:** The Ortodoxo youth become a vanguard, attack at dawn, and turn the courtroom into a tribune
