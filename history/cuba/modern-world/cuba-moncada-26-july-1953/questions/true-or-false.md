# Cuba moncada 26 july 1953 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Young attackers assault the Moncada and Bayamo barracks at carnival dawn; the attack fails militarily, prisoners are murdered, but a programme and a movement are born
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba moncada 26 july 1953" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
