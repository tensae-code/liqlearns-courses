# Cuba moncada 26 july 1953 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** 4.3 The actions of 26 July 1953 — a)
**2.** 8.1–8.3 Preparation of the political vanguard; assault on the Moncada and Carlos Manuel de Céspedes barracks; trial of the 26 July survivors — b)

Meanings:
a) Young attackers assault the Moncada and Bayamo barracks at carnival dawn; the attack fails militarily, prisoners are murdered, but a programme and a movement are born
b) The Ortodoxo youth become a vanguard, attack at dawn, and turn the courtroom into a tribune

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
