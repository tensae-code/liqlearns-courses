# The paris commune 1871 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Paris workers hold their city for 72 days as the first workers' government; the Commune drowns in blood in the Bloody Week, with Louise Michel among its heroines
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The paris commune 1871" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
