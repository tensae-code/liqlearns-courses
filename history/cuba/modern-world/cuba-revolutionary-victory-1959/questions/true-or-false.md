# Cuba revolutionary victory 1959 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Batista flees on 1 January 1959; a general strike defeats the last junta and the Caravan of Freedom carries the rebels to Havana
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba revolutionary victory 1959" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
