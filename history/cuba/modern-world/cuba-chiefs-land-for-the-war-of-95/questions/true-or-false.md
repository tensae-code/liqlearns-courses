# Cuba chiefs land for the war of 95 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gómez and Maceo land from exile expeditions and take command; the veteran army of 1868 marches again under new orders
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba chiefs land for the war of 95" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
