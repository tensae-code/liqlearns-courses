# Cuba criollo positions reform annexation independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Wealthy criollos split three ways — reformists asking Madrid for rights, annexationists looking to the United States, and a few beginning to dream of independence
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba criollo positions reform annexation independence" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
