# The american civil war union against slavery — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The republic expands west over indigenous lands and Mexico's north, industrialises, and splits over slavery
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The american civil war union against slavery" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
