This lesson tells one story: Cuba mambi offensive and maceos death.

Mambí columns keep attacking in east and west even after Maceo falls at Punta Brava (Dec 1896); Spain cannot finish a war it cannot win

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
