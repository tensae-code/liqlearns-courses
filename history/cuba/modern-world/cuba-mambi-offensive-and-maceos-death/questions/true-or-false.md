# Cuba mambi offensive and maceos death — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mambí columns keep attacking in east and west even after Maceo falls at Punta Brava (Dec 1896); Spain cannot finish a war it cannot win
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba mambi offensive and maceos death" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
