# Cuba culture science education late colonial — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Finlay's mosquito theory, war literature and the Liceos: a culture maturing while its country is at war
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba culture science education late colonial" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
