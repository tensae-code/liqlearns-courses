# Cuba jose marti life 1853 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A poor Havana boy who at sixteen is sent to the quarries for a letter, is exiled, and grows into the writer who will unite Cuba
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba jose marti life 1853" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
