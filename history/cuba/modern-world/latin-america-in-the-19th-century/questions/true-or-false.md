# Latin america in the 19th century — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Caudillos, export oligarchies and civil wars: independence's social promises deferred for a hundred years
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Latin america in the 19th century" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
