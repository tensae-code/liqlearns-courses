# The world at the turn of the 21st century — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Globalisation, unipolar power and its challengers: the book hands pupils the present as history's latest chapter
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The world at the turn of the 21st century" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
