# Anticolonial resistance in asia and africa interwar — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On three continents the colonised organise between the wars; the liberation movements of the 1950s are being prepared
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Anticolonial resistance in asia and africa interwar" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
