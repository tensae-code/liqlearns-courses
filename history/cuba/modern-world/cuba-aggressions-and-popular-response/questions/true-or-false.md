# Cuba aggressions and popular response — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sabotage, air raids and exile attacks are answered by mass mobilisation, militias and the Committees for the Defence of the Revolution
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba aggressions and popular response" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
