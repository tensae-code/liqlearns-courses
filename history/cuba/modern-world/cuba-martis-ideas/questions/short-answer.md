# Cuba martis ideas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "6.3 The ideas of José Martí"?
**Answer:** Martí teaches a republic "with all and for the good of all", warns against the northern giant he has lived inside, and makes dignity the Patria's law

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Ch.6 activities: Martí and preparation of the necessary war"?
**Answer:** Short Martí texts are read and their essential idea underlined, turning the Apostle's prose into the lesson itself
