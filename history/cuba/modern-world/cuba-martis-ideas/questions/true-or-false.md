# Cuba martis ideas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Martí teaches a republic "with all and for the good of all", warns against the northern giant he has lived inside, and makes dignity the Patria's law
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba martis ideas" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
