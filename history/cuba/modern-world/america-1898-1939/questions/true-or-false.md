# America 1898 1939 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** 1898 opens the imperial century: Cuba, Puerto Rico, Panama, interventions and the first anti-imperialist answers
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "America 1898 1939" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
