# Cuba war of 1895 begins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "7.1 The war Martí called necessary"?
**Answer:** On 24 Feb 1895 risings across Cuba restart the war Martí organised; he lands to fight and falls at Dos Ríos on 19 May

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Ch.7 activities + closing list of outstanding dates: War of 95, incl. Weyler's reconcentration order and the Manifiesto de Montecristi fragment"?
**Answer:** Pupils read the enemy's order and Martí–Gómez's manifesto side by side and pick out each side's purpose
