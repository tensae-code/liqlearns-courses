# Cuba war of 1895 begins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On 24 Feb 1895 risings across Cuba restart the war Martí organised; he lands to fight and falls at Dos Ríos on 19 May
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba war of 1895 begins" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
