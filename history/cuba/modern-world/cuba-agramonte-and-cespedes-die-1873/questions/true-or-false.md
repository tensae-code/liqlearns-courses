# Cuba agramonte and cespedes die 1873 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ignacio Agramonte falls at Jimaguayú in 1873 and Céspedes, deposed by the Chamber, dies alone at San Lorenzo in 1874; the war loses its two great chiefs
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba agramonte and cespedes die 1873" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
