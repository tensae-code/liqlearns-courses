# Cuba history will absolve me — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Defending himself at trial, Fidel turns his defence speech into the Moncada programme — land, industry, education — and a promise that history will judge
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba history will absolve me" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
