# Cuba sierra maestra guerrilla war — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Rebel Army wins its first fights, holds liberated territory, runs a radio station and agrarian reform while Batista's offensive fails in the mountains
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba sierra maestra guerrilla war" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
