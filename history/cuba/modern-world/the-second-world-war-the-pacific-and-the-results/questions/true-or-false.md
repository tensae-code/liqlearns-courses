# The second world war the pacific and the results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** 50 million dead, fascism defeated chiefly by the USSR's sacrifice, two superpowers left standing — the lessons are drawn for pupils
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The second world war the pacific and the results" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
