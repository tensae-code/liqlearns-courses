# The second world war course in europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Stalingrad turns the war; from El Alamein and Normandy the Axis is ground down to Berlin
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The second world war course in europe" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
