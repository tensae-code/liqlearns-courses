# Cuba struggle against neocolonial evils — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Students, workers and veterans protest corruption, the Platt Amendment and surrendered sovereignty; repression answers each rising
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba struggle against neocolonial evils" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
