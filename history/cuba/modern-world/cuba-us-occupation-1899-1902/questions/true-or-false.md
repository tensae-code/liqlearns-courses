# Cuba us occupation 1899 1902 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The US occupation government dissolves the Liberation Army and the Revolutionary Party, pays off the mambises, and rules through interventors until a republic it can control is ready
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba us occupation 1899 1902" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
