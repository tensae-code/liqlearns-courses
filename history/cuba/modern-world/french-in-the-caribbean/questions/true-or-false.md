# French in the caribbean — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France takes sugar islands — Saint-Domingue above all — and builds the richest slave colonies of the 18th century, ripe for revolution
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "French in the caribbean" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
