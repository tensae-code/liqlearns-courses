# Cuba zanjon pact and why the war failed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "5.9 End of the Ten Years' War; causes of its failure"?
**Answer:** Exhausted and divided, most chiefs sign the Pact of Zanjón (1878) without independence or abolition; Maceo's Protest of Baraguá refuses it

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "3.7 End of the Ten Years' War"?
**Answer:** Zanjón ends the war without its aims; regionalism, caudillismo and exhaustion are named as the internal causes
