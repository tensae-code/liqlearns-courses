# Cuba zanjon pact and why the war failed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Exhausted and divided, most chiefs sign the Pact of Zanjón (1878) without independence or abolition; Maceo's Protest of Baraguá refuses it
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba zanjon pact and why the war failed" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
