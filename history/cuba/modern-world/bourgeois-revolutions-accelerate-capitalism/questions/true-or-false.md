# Bourgeois revolutions accelerate capitalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils compare the revolutions and name their common turn: feudal barriers fall, capital advances
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Bourgeois revolutions accelerate capitalism" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
