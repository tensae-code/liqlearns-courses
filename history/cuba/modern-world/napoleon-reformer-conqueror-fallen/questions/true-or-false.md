# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Napoleon carries the Revolution's codes across Europe at swordpoint, spreading capitalism while crowning himself emperor
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
