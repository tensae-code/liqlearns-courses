# From utopian socialism to scientific communism marx engels — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two young Germans turn workers' anger into a theory and a programme: the Manifesto calls workers of all countries to unite
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "From utopian socialism to scientific communism marx engels" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
