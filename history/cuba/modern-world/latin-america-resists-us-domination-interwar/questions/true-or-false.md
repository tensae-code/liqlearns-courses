# Latin america resists us domination interwar — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From Sandino's little army in Nicaragua to student and worker movements, the continent answers the hegemon between the wars
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Latin america resists us domination interwar" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
