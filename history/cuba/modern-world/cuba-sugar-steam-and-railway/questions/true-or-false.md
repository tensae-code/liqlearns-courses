# Cuba sugar steam and railway — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Steam replaces oxen in the ingenios from 1817; in 1837 the Havana–Bejucal railway makes Cuba the seventh country in the world with a railway, ahead of Spain itself
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba sugar steam and railway" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
