# Spanish american independence second stage ayacucho — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two liberating armies cross the Andes from north and south, meet at Guayaquil, and end Spanish rule at Ayacucho in 1824
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Spanish american independence second stage ayacucho" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
