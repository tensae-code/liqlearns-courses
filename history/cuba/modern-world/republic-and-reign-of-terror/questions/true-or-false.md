# Republic and reign of terror — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rights are proclaimed for all men, then the Republic devours its enemies and its own children under Robespierre until Thermidor
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Republic and reign of terror" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
