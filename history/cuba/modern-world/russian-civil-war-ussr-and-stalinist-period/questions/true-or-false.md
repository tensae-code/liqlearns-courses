# Russian civil war ussr and stalinist period — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fourteen armies invade the new state and fail; the USSR is founded, industrialises at forced pace, and women enter public life en masse
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Russian civil war ussr and stalinist period" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
