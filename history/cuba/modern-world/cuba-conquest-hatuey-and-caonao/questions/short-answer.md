# Cuba conquest hatuey and caonao — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "2.2 Conquest of Cuba"?
**Answer:** Ocampo sails round the island (bojeo); Velázquez lands in 1510; cacique Hatuey, fled from La Española, warns Cuba and is burned alive refusing heaven with Spaniards in it; at Caonao a feast turns into a massacre

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Ch.2 activities: arrival of the Spaniards and occupation exercises"?
**Answer:** Pupils retrace the conquest chapter's route — Ocampo, Velázquez, Hatuey — through map, ordering and writing tasks
