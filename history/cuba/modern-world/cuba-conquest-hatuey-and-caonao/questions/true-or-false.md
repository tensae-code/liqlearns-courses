# Cuba conquest hatuey and caonao — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ocampo sails round the island (bojeo); Velázquez lands in 1510; cacique Hatuey, fled from La Española, warns Cuba and is burned alive refusing heaven with Spaniards in it; at Caonao a feast turns into a massacre
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba conquest hatuey and caonao" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
