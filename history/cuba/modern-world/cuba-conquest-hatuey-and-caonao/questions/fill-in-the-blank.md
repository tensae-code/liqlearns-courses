# Cuba conquest hatuey and caonao — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** This lesson is called "____".
**Answer:** Cuba conquest hatuey and caonao

**2.** In "2.2 Conquest of Cuba", the story turns on: ____.
**Answer:** Ocampo sails round the island (bojeo); Velázquez lands in 1510; cacique Hatuey, fled from La Española, warns Cuba and is burned alive refusing heaven with Spaniards in it; at Caonao a feast turns into a massacre
