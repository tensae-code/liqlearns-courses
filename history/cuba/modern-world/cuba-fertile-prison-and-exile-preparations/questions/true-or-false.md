# Cuba fertile prison and exile preparations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On the Isle of Pines the prisoners study and organise until amnesty frees them; in Mexico the exiles train and buy a yacht called Granma
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba fertile prison and exile preparations" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
