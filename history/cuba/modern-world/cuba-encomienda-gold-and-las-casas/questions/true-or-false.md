# Cuba encomienda gold and las casas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Indians wash river gold from dawn to dusk under the whip in encomiendas; Las Casas gives up his own encomienda and becomes Protector of the Indians, yet by 1553 only some 3,000 of 300,000 remain; African slavery is authorised from 1512
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba encomienda gold and las casas" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
