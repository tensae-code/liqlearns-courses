# Cuba women in revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Federation of Cuban Women opens study, work and defence roles to women who had been shut out of public life
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba women in revolution" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
