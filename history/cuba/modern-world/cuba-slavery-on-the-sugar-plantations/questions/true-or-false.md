# Cuba slavery on the sugar plantations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** As sugar booms, enslaved Africans cut cane under drivers and live locked in barracones; the plantation's wealth is built on their exhaustion
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba slavery on the sugar plantations" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
