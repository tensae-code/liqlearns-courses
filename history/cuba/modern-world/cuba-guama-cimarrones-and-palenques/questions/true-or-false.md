# Cuba guama cimarrones and palenques — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cacique Guamá holds the Baracoa mountains 1522–1533 and burns the town; runaway cimarrones build fenced palenques in the wilds and choose a hard free life over slavery
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba guama cimarrones and palenques" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
