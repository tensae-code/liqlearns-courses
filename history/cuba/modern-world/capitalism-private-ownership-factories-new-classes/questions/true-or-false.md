# Capitalism private ownership factories new classes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Trusts and banks fuse into monopolies; free-competition capitalism turns into imperialism before pupils' eyes
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Capitalism private ownership factories new classes" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
