# Cuba guerrilla war expands 1958 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** New fronts open under Raúl Castro and Juan Almeida; the rebels defeat the summer offensive and the war leaves the Sierra for the whole island
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba guerrilla war expands 1958" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
