# Cuba reform petitions fail 1867 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Criollo delegates carry reform petitions to Madrid and return empty-handed and taxed more; the failure teaches Cuba that Spain will concede nothing
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba reform petitions fail 1867" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
