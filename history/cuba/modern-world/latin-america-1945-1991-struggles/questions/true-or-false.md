# Latin america 1945 1991 struggles — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cuba breaks the domination system in 1959; a generation fights on — Che falls in Bolivia, Allende dies in La Moneda, Sandinistas triumph, dictatorships and invasions answer
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Latin america 1945 1991 struggles" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
