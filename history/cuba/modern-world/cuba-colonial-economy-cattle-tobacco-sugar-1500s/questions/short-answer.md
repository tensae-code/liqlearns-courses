# Cuba colonial economy cattle tobacco sugar 1500s — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "3.1 Progress of some economic activities"?
**Answer:** Gold runs out and Spaniards leave for Mexico and Peru; those who stay turn to cattle hatos and corrales, tobacco and early sugar, worked by Indians, slaves and free men

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "Ch.3–4 activities: colonial exploitation 16th–18th c. and Cuba to 1867, incl. a document fragment on US designs on Cuba"?
**Answer:** Exercises move from hatos and tobacco monopoly to steam, railways and the question of who else wanted the island
