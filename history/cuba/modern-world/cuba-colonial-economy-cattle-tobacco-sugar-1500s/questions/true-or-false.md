# Cuba colonial economy cattle tobacco sugar 1500s — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gold runs out and Spaniards leave for Mexico and Peru; those who stay turn to cattle hatos and corrales, tobacco and early sugar, worked by Indians, slaves and free men
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba colonial economy cattle tobacco sugar 1500s" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
