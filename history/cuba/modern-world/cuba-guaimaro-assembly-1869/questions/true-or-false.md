# Cuba guaimaro assembly 1869 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Quarrelling regions meet at Guáimaro and agree one Republic in Arms, a constitution and a single flag — unity is proclaimed even as rivalries remain
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba guaimaro assembly 1869" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
