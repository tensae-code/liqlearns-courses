# Cuba guaimaro assembly 1869 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "5.4 Assembly of Guáimaro, 10 April 1869"?
**Answer:** Quarrelling regions meet at Guáimaro and agree one Republic in Arms, a constitution and a single flag — unity is proclaimed even as rivalries remain

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "3.4 The Assembly of Guáimaro"?
**Answer:** Delegates argue civil vs military power and leave with a constitution and a republic that exists first on paper
