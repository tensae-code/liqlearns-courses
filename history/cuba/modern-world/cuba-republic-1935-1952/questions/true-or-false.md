# Cuba republic 1935 1952 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1940 Constitution promises rights the authentic and liberal governments betray amid gangsterismo and corruption, ending in Batista's 1952 coup
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba republic 1935 1952" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
