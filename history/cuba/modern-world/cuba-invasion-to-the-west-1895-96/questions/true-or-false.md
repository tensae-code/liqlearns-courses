# Cuba invasion to the west 1895 96 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gómez and Maceo carry the war the length of the island from Mangos de Baraguá to Mantua in 90 days, burning cane and breaking Spain's trocha lines
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba invasion to the west 1895 96" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
