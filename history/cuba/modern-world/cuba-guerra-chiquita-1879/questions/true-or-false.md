# Cuba guerra chiquita 1879 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Calixto García's little war of 1879–80 fails for lack of unity and timing, teaching the next generation what to fix
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba guerra chiquita 1879" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
