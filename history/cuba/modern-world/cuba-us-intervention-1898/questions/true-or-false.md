# Cuba us intervention 1898 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Maine explodes in Havana harbour; the United States enters a war Cuba was winning and takes the victory, the peace table and Puerto Rico for itself
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba us intervention 1898" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
