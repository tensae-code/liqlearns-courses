# Cuba peace without independence 1898 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Paris, Spain and the United States settle Cuba's fate with no Cuban in the room; occupation replaces colony and independence is postponed
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba peace without independence 1898" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
