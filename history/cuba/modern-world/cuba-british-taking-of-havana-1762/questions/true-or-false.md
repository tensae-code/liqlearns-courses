# Cuba british taking of havana 1762 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A British fleet besieges Havana in 1762; the city falls and is opened to free trade for eleven months before Spain recovers it in exchange for Florida
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba british taking of havana 1762" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
