# Cuba british taking of havana 1762 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "3.4 Taking of Havana by the English"?
**Answer:** A British fleet besieges Havana in 1762; the city falls and is opened to free trade for eleven months before Spain recovers it in exchange for Florida

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "2.4 Colonial evolution in the 18th c. up to the English occupation"?
**Answer:** Bourbon reforms tighten the monopoly until Britain takes Havana and briefly cracks it open
