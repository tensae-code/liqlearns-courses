# Cuba machado tyranny — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Machado extends his term, rules by terror and murder of opponents, and is brought down in 1933 by a general strike and popular rising
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba machado tyranny" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
