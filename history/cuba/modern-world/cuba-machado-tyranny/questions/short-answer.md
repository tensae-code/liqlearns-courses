# Cuba machado tyranny — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "2.4 The tyranny of Gerardo Machado"?
**Answer:** Machado extends his term, rules by terror and murder of opponents, and is brought down in 1933 by a general strike and popular rising

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "6.8–6.10 Government of Gerardo Machado; repercussion of the world economic crisis; rise of the revolutionary movement against Machado"?
**Answer:** The 1929 crash empties the sugar economy; students, workers and the ABC fill the streets until Machado falls in 1933
