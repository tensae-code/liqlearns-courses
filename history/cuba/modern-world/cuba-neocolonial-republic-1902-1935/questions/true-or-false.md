# Cuba neocolonial republic 1902 1935 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Republic is born in 1902 with the Platt Amendment in its constitution; presidents come and go under Washington's eye while sugar wealth flows north
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba neocolonial republic 1902 1935" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
