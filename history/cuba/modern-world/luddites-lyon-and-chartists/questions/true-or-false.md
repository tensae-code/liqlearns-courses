# Luddites lyon and chartists — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Workers smash machines, Lyon's weavers rise twice, and Chartists carry a people's charter to a Parliament that refuses it
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Luddites lyon and chartists" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
