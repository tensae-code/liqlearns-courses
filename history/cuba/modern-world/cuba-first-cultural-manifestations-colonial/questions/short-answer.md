# Cuba first cultural manifestations colonial — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "3.6 First cultural manifestations"?
**Answer:** In a colony built for extraction, the first churches, schools, printing and local writers appear and a Cuban culture begins to speak in its own voice

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "5.8 Cultural development in Cuba during the colonial epoch"?
**Answer:** Four centuries of colony still produce a Cuban culture — Varela, Heredia, Villaverde — that argues for a nation
