# Cuba first cultural manifestations colonial — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In a colony built for extraction, the first churches, schools, printing and local writers appear and a Cuban culture begins to speak in its own voice
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba first cultural manifestations colonial" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
