# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Columbus, rejected in Portugal, wins Spanish backing, sails west for the Indies and lands at Guanahaní, then reaches Cuba (Bariay, 28 Oct 1492) calling it the most beautiful land human eyes had seen
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
