# World socialist system and us hegemony 1945 1991 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A socialist camp rises in eastern Europe while the United States builds bases, coups and alliances to contain it, above all in its "backyard"
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "World socialist system and us hegemony 1945 1991" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
