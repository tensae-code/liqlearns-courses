# Cuba revolution fulfils martis dreams — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Moncada programme becomes law — land to the tillers, schools, health — presented as Martí's republic finally built
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba revolution fulfils martis dreams" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
