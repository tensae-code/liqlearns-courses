# Latinamericanism vs panamericanism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bolívar's and Martí's unity-from-below is set against Washington's unity-from-above; two projects for one continent fight through the century
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Latinamericanism vs panamericanism" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
