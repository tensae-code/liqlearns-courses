# Cuba risings in camaguey and las villas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war spreads west as Camagüey and Las Villas rise, turning an eastern revolt into an island-wide struggle
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba risings in camaguey and las villas" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
