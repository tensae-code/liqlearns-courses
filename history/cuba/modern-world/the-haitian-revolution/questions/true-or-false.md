# The haitian revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The enslaved of France's richest colony rise, defeat three empires under Louverture and his successors, and found Haiti — the only victorious slave revolution
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The haitian revolution" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
