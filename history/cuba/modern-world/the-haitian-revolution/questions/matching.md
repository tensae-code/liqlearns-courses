# The haitian revolution — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** 3.3 The anticolonial revolution of Saint-Domingue (Haiti); Toussaint Louverture — a)
**2.** 3.3 The revolution for Latin America's first independence: Haiti — b)

Meanings:
a) The enslaved of France's richest colony rise, defeat three empires under Louverture and his successors, and found Haiti — the only victorious slave revolution
b) Haiti is placed before all Spanish America: the enslaved liberate themselves first, in 1804, and are punished with isolation for it

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
