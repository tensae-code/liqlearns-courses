# The haitian revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "3.3 The anticolonial revolution of Saint-Domingue (Haiti); Toussaint Louverture"?
**Answer:** The enslaved of France's richest colony rise, defeat three empires under Louverture and his successors, and found Haiti — the only victorious slave revolution

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "3.3 The revolution for Latin America's first independence: Haiti"?
**Answer:** Haiti is placed before all Spanish America: the enslaved liberate themselves first, in 1804, and are punished with isolation for it
