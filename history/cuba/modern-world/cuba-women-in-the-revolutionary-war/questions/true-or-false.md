# Cuba women in the revolutionary war — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Women carry messages and arms, nurse, teach in liberated zones and fight in the Mariana Grajales platoon
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba women in the revolutionary war" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
