# Cuba first villas and cabildos — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "2.3 First villas founded by the conquerors"?
**Answer:** Seven villas rise 1511–1515 (Baracoa to Santiago); church, plaza and cabildo at the centre, the cabildo setting prices, judging neighbours and handing out land and Indians

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "2.2 Establishment of Spanish colonial rule in Cuba"?
**Answer:** Villas, cabildos, encomiendas and the Church lock the island into an empire's administration
