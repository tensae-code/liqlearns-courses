# Cuba first villas and cabildos — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Seven villas rise 1511–1515 (Baracoa to Santiago); church, plaza and cabildo at the centre, the cabildo setting prices, judging neighbours and handing out land and Indians
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba first villas and cabildos" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
