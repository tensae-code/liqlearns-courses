# Brazils peculiar independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Brazil becomes independent without a war of liberation — a Portuguese prince proclaims it and keeps the throne and slavery
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Brazils peculiar independence" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
