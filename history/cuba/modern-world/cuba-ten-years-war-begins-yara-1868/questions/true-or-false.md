# Cuba ten years war begins yara 1868 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Carlos Manuel de Céspedes frees his slaves at La Demajagua on 10 Oct 1868 and calls them to fight for a free Cuba; the Grito de Yara opens the independence war
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba ten years war begins yara 1868" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
