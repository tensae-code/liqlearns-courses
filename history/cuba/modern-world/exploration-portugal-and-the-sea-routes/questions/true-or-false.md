# Exploration portugal and the sea routes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The search for spices and gold sends caravels round Africa and across the Atlantic; plunder becomes capital
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Exploration portugal and the sea routes" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
