# Exploration portugal and the sea routes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in "6.4 European voyages of exploration and the rise of capitalism"?

**2.** In your own words, what happens in "2.1 Europe on the eve of the voyages of "discovery" and conquest"?
