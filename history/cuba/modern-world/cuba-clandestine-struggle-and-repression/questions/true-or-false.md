# Cuba clandestine struggle and repression — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the cities the underground sabotages, strikes and hides fighters while Batista's police torture and murder young revolutionaries
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba clandestine struggle and repression" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
