# Cuba playa giron 1961 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A US-backed exile brigade lands at Playa Girón in April 1961 and is defeated in under 72 hours by militias, workers and the Rebel Army
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Cuba playa giron 1961" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
