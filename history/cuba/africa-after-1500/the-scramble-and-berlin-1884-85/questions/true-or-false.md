# The scramble and berlin 1884 85 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Berlin the powers draw Africa's borders with rulers; China is carved into spheres while its people begin to resist
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "The scramble and berlin 1884 85" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
