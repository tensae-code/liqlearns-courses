# Colonisation the road to the scramble — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Explorers map the interior and European flags follow; Africa's partition is prepared on paper before it is imposed on the ground
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of "Colonisation the road to the scramble" has no turn — nothing changes in it and nothing comes of it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: every story in it turns, and what came of the turn is the point.
