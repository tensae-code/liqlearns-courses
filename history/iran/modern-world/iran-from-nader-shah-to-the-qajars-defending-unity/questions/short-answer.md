**2.**   How does the story in “Chapter 6, Lesson 11 The effort to preserve Iran's independence and political unity” end?
**Answer:** Nader Shah's empire flashes and vanishes, Karim Khan Zand holds the south, and the Qajars inherit the task of keeping Iran one country between empires Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
