# Iran: The Safavid Empire — State and Economy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Shah Ismail's Qizilbash found a Shi'a state in 1501; Shah Abbas centralises, builds an army and a silk economy, and makes Iran a great power again
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 5, Lesson 9 A united and integrated Iran” is mainly about this: Out of catastrophe, a golden age — miniature painting, astronomy and Timurid architecture make Herat and Samarkand the wonder of the age
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After centuries of division, the Safavids weld Iran into one state with one faith — unity itself is the lesson's hero
