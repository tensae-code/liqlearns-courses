# Iran: The Safavid Empire — State and Economy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 13 Political and economic developments of Iran in the Safavid period”?
**Answer:** Shah Ismail's Qizilbash found a Shi'a state in 1501; Shah Abbas centralises, builds an army and a silk economy, and makes Iran a great power again Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 5, Lesson 9 A united and integrated Iran” end?
**Answer:** After centuries of division, the Safavids weld Iran into one state with one faith — unity itself is the lesson's hero Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
