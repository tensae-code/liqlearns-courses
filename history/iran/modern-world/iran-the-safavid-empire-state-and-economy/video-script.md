# Video script — Iran: The Safavid Empire — State and Economy


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Shah Ismail's Qizilbash found a Shi'a state in 1501; Shah Abbas centralises, builds an army and a silk economy, and makes Iran a great power again Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
After centuries of division, the Safavids weld Iran into one state with one faith — unity itself is the lesson's hero Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.


Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Iran.
