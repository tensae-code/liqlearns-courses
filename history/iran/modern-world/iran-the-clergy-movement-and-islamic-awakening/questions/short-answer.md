**2.**   How does the story in “Lesson 17 The emergence of the clergy movement and the heightening of Islamic awakening” end?
**Answer:** A new protagonist steps forward — Khomeini and the clergy networks begin organised opposition to the shah's reforms Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
