# Iran in the First World War

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Iran in the First World War** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Iran declares neutrality and is occupied anyway — Russian, British and Ottoman armies fight across a country that famine then devastates Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
