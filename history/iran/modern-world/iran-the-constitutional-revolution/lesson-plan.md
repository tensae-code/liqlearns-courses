## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 3 The grounds of the Constitutional movement** — Despotism at home, colonial hands abroad, awakening intellectuals and clergy — the ingredients of constitutionalism assemble one by one
2. **Lesson 4 The beginning of the people's movement against internal despotism and foreign colonialism** — The people move — protests and sanctuary-taking force Muzaffar al-Din Shah to grant a constitution and a national assembly in 1906
3. **Lesson 5 The Constitutional movement of Iran** — The whole arc in one lesson — grievance, sanctuary, constitution, civil war — Iran's first attempt to bind its kings by law
4. **Chapter 7, Lesson 13 The Constitutional movement** — The survey version — a nation demands law over kingship and wins a parliament
