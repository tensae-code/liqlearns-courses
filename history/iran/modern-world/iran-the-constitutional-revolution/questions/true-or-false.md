# Iran: The Constitutional Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Despotism at home, colonial hands abroad, awakening intellectuals and clergy — the ingredients of constitutionalism assemble one by one
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Lesson 4 The beginning of the people's movement against internal despotism and foreign colonialism” is mainly about this: Defeats by Russia force the question — Abbas Mirza's reforms, Amir Kabir and the Dar al-Fonun try to modernise before foreigners do it for them
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The people move — protests and sanctuary-taking force Muzaffar al-Din Shah to grant a constitution and a national assembly in 1906
