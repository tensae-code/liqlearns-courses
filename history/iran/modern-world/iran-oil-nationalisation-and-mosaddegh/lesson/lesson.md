# Iran: Oil Nationalisation and Mosaddegh

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Iran: Oil Nationalisation and Mosaddegh** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 12 The movement to nationalise the oil industry

Iran votes to take its own oil back from the British company; Mosaddegh becomes the movement's face and Britain blockades Abadan Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 9 The movement to nationalise Iran's oil industry

The Humanities version deepens the same story — nationalisation as law, movement and international crisis ending in coup Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
