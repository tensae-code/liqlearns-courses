# Iran: Oil Nationalisation and Mosaddegh — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Lesson 12 The movement to nationalise the oil industry”?
**Answer:** Iran votes to take its own oil back from the British company; Mosaddegh becomes the movement's face and Britain blockades Abadan Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Lesson 9 The movement to nationalise Iran's oil industry” end?
**Answer:** The Humanities version deepens the same story — nationalisation as law, movement and international crisis ending in coup Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
