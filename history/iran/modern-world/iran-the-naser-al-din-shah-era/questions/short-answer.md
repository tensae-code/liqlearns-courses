# Iran: The Naser al-Din Shah Era — short answer

**1.**   What happens in “Lesson 2 The era of Naser al-Din Shah”?
**Answer:** A fifty-year reign of reform attempts, concessions sold to foreigners, Amir Kabir's rise and murder, and the Tobacco Protest rehearsing a nation for revolution Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Lesson 2 The era of Naser al-Din Shah” end?
**Answer:** A fifty-year reign of reform attempts, concessions sold to foreigners, Amir Kabir's rise and murder, and the Tobacco Protest rehearsing a nation for revolution Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
