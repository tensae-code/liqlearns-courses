# Iran: Qajar Founding, from Agha Mohammad Khan to Mohammad Shah — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Agha Mohammad Khan reunites a broken Iran by the sword and founds the Qajar dynasty, and his successors immediately face Britain, Russia and France competing over the country
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Lesson 3 Politics and government in the Qajar era (to the end of Naser al-Din Shah's reign)” is mainly about this: Nader Shah's empire flashes and vanishes, Karim Khan Zand holds the south, and the Qajars inherit the task of keeping Iran one country between empires
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Qajar state is anatomised — court, ministers and foreign legations — across its first century of rule
