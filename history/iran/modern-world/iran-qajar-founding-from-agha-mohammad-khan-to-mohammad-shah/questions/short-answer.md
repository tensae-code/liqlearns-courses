# Iran: Qajar Founding, from Agha Mohammad Khan to Mohammad Shah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Lesson 1 Qajar rule from Agha Mohammad Khan to Mohammad Shah”?
**Answer:** Agha Mohammad Khan reunites a broken Iran by the sword and founds the Qajar dynasty, and his successors immediately face Britain, Russia and France competing over the country Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Lesson 3 Politics and government in the Qajar era (to the end of Naser al-Din Shah's reign)” end?
**Answer:** The Qajar state is anatomised — court, ministers and foreign legations — across its first century of rule Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
