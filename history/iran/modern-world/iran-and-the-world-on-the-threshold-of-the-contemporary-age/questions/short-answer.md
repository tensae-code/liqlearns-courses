# Iran and the World on the Threshold of the Contemporary Age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Lesson 2 Iran and the world on the threshold of the contemporary era”?
**Answer:** Two worlds at 1800 — an industrialising, expanding Europe and a Qajar Iran unaware how far the balance has already tipped Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Lesson 2 Iran and the world on the threshold of the contemporary era” end?
**Answer:** Two worlds at 1800 — an industrialising, expanding Europe and a Qajar Iran unaware how far the balance has already tipped Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
