# Iran and the World on the Threshold of the Contemporary Age — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Two worlds at 1800 — an industrialising, expanding Europe and a Qajar Iran unaware how far the balance has already tipped — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
