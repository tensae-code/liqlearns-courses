# Iran: Establishing and Consolidating the Islamic Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Lesson 11 The establishment and consolidation of the Islamic Republic system”?
**Answer:** Referendum, constitution, hostage crisis and purges — how a revolution hardens into a system Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 8, Lesson 16 Iran in the period after the victory of the Islamic Revolution” end?
**Answer:** New system, imposed war and reconstruction — the republic's first decades as the course's closing chapter Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
