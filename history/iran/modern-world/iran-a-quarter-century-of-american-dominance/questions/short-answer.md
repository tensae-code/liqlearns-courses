# Iran: A Quarter Century of American Dominance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Lesson 15 A quarter century of America's dominance over Iran”?
**Answer:** After 1953 the patron changes — American advisers, arms and intelligence reshape the shah's state for twenty-five years Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Lesson 15 A quarter century of America's dominance over Iran” end?
**Answer:** After 1953 the patron changes — American advisers, arms and intelligence reshape the shah's state for twenty-five years Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
