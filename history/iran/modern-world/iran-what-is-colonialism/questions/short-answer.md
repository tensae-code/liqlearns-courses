# Iran: What Is Colonialism? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Chapter 11, Lesson 20 What is colonialism (استعمار)?”?
**Answer:** A new word for an old theft — stronger countries seizing weaker ones' land and wealth is named, defined and recognised Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Chapter 11, Lesson 20 What is colonialism (استعمار)?” end?
**Answer:** A new word for an old theft — stronger countries seizing weaker ones' land and wealth is named, defined and recognised Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
