# Iran: The 15 Khordad Uprising, 1963 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Lesson 18 The uprising of 15 Khordad (June 1963)”?
**Answer:** Khomeini is arrested after denouncing the shah; cities rise, the rising is shot down, and 15 Khordad becomes the revolution's founding date Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Lesson 18 The uprising of 15 Khordad (June 1963)” end?
**Answer:** Khomeini is arrested after denouncing the shah; cities rise, the rising is shot down, and 15 Khordad becomes the revolution's founding date Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
