## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 9 The features of Reza Shah's government

Railways, an army, unveiled women and crushed tribes — forced modernisation from above, judged in the lesson as dictatorship dressed as progress Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 7 Iran in the period of Reza Shah's rule

The Humanities telling weighs the same reign — state-building and modernisation against dictatorship and foreign dependence Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
