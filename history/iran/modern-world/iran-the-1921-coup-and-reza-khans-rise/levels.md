# Iran: The 1921 Coup and Reza Khan's Rise — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A Cossack colonel marches on Tehran in a British-backed coup, and Iran's politics turn on one ambitious soldier, Reza Khan — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
