## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 21 The victory of the Islamic Revolution of Iran

The shah leaves, Khomeini returns to millions, the army declares neutrality, and on 22 Bahman (11 February 1979) the Pahlavi state collapses Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
