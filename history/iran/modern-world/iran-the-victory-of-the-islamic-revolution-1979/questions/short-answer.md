# Iran: The Victory of the Islamic Revolution, 1979 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Lesson 21 The victory of the Islamic Revolution of Iran”?
**Answer:** The shah leaves, Khomeini returns to millions, the army declares neutrality, and on 22 Bahman (11 February 1979) the Pahlavi state collapses Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Lesson 10 The Islamic Revolution” end?
**Answer:** Causes, coalition and collapse — the revolution analysed as a whole, not just chronicled Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
