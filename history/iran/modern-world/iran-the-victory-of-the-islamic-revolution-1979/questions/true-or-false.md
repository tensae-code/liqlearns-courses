# Iran: The Victory of the Islamic Revolution, 1979 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   The shah leaves, Khomeini returns to millions, the army declares neutrality, and on 22 Bahman (11 February 1979) the Pahlavi state collapses
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Lesson 10 The Islamic Revolution” is mainly about this: 1978 — strikes, mourning-cycle protests and millions in the street; each funeral makes the next demonstration bigger
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Causes, coalition and collapse — the revolution analysed as a whole, not just chronicled
