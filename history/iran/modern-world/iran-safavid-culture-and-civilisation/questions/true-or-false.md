# Iran: Safavid Culture and Civilisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Isfahan becomes half the world — mosques, squares, carpets and a court culture that brands Iran's identity to this day
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 5, Lesson 10 Social, economic, scientific and cultural conditions of Iran in the Safavid era” is mainly about this: Shah Ismail's Qizilbash found a Shi'a state in 1501; Shah Abbas centralises, builds an army and a silk economy, and makes Iran a great power again
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The united state at work — bazaars, silk, scholars and Isfahan's building boom
