# Iran: Safavid Culture and Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 14 Culture and civilisation in the Safavid era”?
**Answer:** Isfahan becomes half the world — mosques, squares, carpets and a court culture that brands Iran's identity to this day Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 5, Lesson 10 Social, economic, scientific and cultural conditions of Iran in the Safavid era” end?
**Answer:** The united state at work — bazaars, silk, scholars and Isfahan's building boom Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
