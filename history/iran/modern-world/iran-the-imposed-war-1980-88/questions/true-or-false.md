# Iran: The Imposed War, 1980–88 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Saddam invades in 1980 expecting a short war; eight years of trenches, missiles and chemical attacks follow before the ceasefire
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Lesson 12 The imposed war and the Sacred Defence” is mainly about this: Referendum, constitution, hostage crisis and purges — how a revolution hardens into a system
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The war retold as defence of the revolution itself — fronts, cities under missiles, and the accepted ceasefire
