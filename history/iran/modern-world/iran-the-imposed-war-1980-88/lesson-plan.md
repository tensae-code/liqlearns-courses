## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 24 The imposed war of the Ba'athist regime ruling Iraq against Iran** — Saddam invades in 1980 expecting a short war; eight years of trenches, missiles and chemical attacks follow before the ceasefire
2. **Lesson 12 The imposed war and the Sacred Defence** — The war retold as defence of the revolution itself — fronts, cities under missiles, and the accepted ceasefire
3. **Chapter 12, Lesson 22 Khorramshahr in the enemy's claws** — The port city falls to Iraq's invasion and endures occupation — the war made personal through one city's suffering
4. **Chapter 12, Lesson 23 Khorramshahr in the embrace of the homeland** — In 1982 Iranian forces liberate Khorramshahr, and the city's return becomes the war's most beloved story

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
