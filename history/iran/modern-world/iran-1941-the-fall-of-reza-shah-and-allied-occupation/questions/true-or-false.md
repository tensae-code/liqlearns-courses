# Iran 1941: The Fall of Reza Shah and Allied Occupation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   The Allies demand the Germans leave; Reza Shah refuses, Britain and the USSR invade in 1941, and the shah is shipped into exile
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Lesson 11 The occupation of Iran by the Allies and its effects” is mainly about this: Both Pahlavi shahs in one lesson — Reza Shah's forced modernisation and Mohammad Reza Shah's oil-rich, America-backed monarchy
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Occupied Iran becomes the Persian Corridor to the USSR while famine, inflation and foreign armies press on ordinary people
