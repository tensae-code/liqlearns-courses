# Iran 1941: The Fall of Reza Shah and Allied Occupation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Lesson 10 The fall of Reza Shah”?
**Answer:** The Allies demand the Germans leave; Reza Shah refuses, Britain and the USSR invade in 1941, and the shah is shipped into exile Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Lesson 11 The occupation of Iran by the Allies and its effects” end?
**Answer:** Occupied Iran becomes the Persian Corridor to the USSR while famine, inflation and foreign armies press on ordinary people Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
