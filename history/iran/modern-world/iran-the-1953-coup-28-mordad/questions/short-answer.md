# Iran: The 1953 Coup — 28 Mordad — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Lesson 13 The grounds of the 28 Mordad coup”?
**Answer:** Court, foreign embassies and a split national movement pull in opposite directions, and the ground is prepared coup by coup Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Lesson 14 The coup of 28 Mordad (19 August 1953)” end?
**Answer:** A first coup fails and the shah flees; a second, foreign-backed attempt succeeds, Mosaddegh falls, and the shah returns on American terms Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
