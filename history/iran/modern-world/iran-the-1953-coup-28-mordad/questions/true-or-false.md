# Iran: The 1953 Coup — 28 Mordad — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Court, foreign embassies and a split national movement pull in opposite directions, and the ground is prepared coup by coup
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Lesson 14 The coup of 28 Mordad (19 August 1953)” is mainly about this: Iran votes to take its own oil back from the British company; Mosaddegh becomes the movement's face and Britain blockades Abadan
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A first coup fails and the shah flees; a second, foreign-backed attempt succeeds, Mosaddegh falls, and the shah returns on American terms
