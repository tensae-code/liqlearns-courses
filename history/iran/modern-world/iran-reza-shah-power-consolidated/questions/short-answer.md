# Iran: Reza Shah — Power Consolidated — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Lesson 8 Reza Khan; the consolidation of power”?
**Answer:** Minister, then prime minister, then shah — Reza Khan dismantles rivals step by step and founds the Pahlavi dynasty in 1925 Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Lesson 8 Reza Khan; the consolidation of power” end?
**Answer:** Minister, then prime minister, then shah — Reza Khan dismantles rivals step by step and founds the Pahlavi dynasty in 1925 Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
