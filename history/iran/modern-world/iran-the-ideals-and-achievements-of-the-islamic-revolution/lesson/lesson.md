# Iran: The Ideals and Achievements of the Islamic Revolution

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Iran: The Ideals and Achievements of the Islamic Revolution** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The book pauses the narrative to audit the revolution against its own promises — independence, freedom and Islamic government as the stated ideals Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
