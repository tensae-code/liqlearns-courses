# History — Iran course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Iran's own — built from Iran's Ministry of Education History books (Humanities branch) and combined Social Studies books (Persian, all COMPLETE as filed). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the spiral Iran's own books use — the historian's craft, the ancient world and ancient Iran, Islam and the Iranian-Islamic civilisation, Mongols, Timurids and Safavids, Qajars, constitution and Pahlavis, and oil, revolution, republic and war. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
