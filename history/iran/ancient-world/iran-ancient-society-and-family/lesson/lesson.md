## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 3, Lesson 12 Society and family

Beneath the throne, classes, households and women and children live the empire — the lesson descends from palace to home Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 11, Lesson 21 The social conditions of ancient Iran

Classes and households of the ancient world — who worked, who ruled, who prayed Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
