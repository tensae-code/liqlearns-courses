# Iran: Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Iran: Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 3, Lesson 10 Iran in the Ghaznavid, Seljuk and Khwarazmshah era

Turkish soldier-dynasties take the plateau in turn — Mahmud's raids, Tughril's Seljuk sultanate shielding the caliph, and the Khwarazmshahs who rule on the eve of the Mongol storm Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 7, Lesson 13 Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs

Three Turkish dynasties in sequence — slave-soldiers become sultans, the Seljuks unite the east, and the Khwarazmshahs inherit the storm's eve Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
