# Iran: Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Turkish soldier-dynasties take the plateau in turn — Mahmud's raids, Tughril's Seljuk sultanate shielding the caliph, and the Khwarazmshahs who rule on the eve of the Mongol storm
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 7, Lesson 13 Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs” is mainly about this: The vizier becomes the hero — great ministers like the Seljuk Nizam al-Mulk build schools, roads and thriving cities
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Three Turkish dynasties in sequence — slave-soldiers become sultans, the Seljuks unite the east, and the Khwarazmshahs inherit the storm's eve
