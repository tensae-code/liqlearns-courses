# Iran: Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 10 Iran in the Ghaznavid, Seljuk and Khwarazmshah era”?
**Answer:** Turkish soldier-dynasties take the plateau in turn — Mahmud's raids, Tughril's Seljuk sultanate shielding the caliph, and the Khwarazmshahs who rule on the eve of the Mongol storm Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 7, Lesson 13 Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs” end?
**Answer:** Three Turkish dynasties in sequence — slave-soldiers become sultans, the Seljuks unite the east, and the Khwarazmshahs inherit the storm's eve Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
