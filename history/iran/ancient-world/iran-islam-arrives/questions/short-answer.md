# Iran: Islam Arrives — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 8 Islam in Iran; the grounds for the rise of Iranian-Islamic civilisation”?
**Answer:** Arab armies end Sasanian rule, but Iran converts gradually and on its own terms, preparing a civilisation that will be both Islamic and Iranian Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 6, Lesson 11 The entry of Islam into Iran” end?
**Answer:** Sasanian Iran, exhausted and divided, falls to Arab armies battle by battle, and a new era opens Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
