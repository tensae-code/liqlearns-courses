# Iran: Islam Arrives — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Arab armies end Sasanian rule, but Iran converts gradually and on its own terms, preparing a civilisation that will be both Islamic and Iranian
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 6, Lesson 11 The entry of Islam into Iran” is mainly about this: Baghdad rises as a planned capital of learning and trade while real power slowly leaks to regional dynasties
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Sasanian Iran, exhausted and divided, falls to Arab armies battle by battle, and a new era opens
