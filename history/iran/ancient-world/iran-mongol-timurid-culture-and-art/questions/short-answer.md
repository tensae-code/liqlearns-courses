# Iran: Mongol–Timurid Culture and Art — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 12 Culture and art in the Mongol–Timurid era”?
**Answer:** Out of catastrophe, a golden age — miniature painting, astronomy and Timurid architecture make Herat and Samarkand the wonder of the age Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 8, Lesson 16 Victory of culture over the sword” end?
**Answer:** The chapter's title is the verdict — Persian culture, language and art absorb the conquerors until the conquerors' courts speak Persian Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
