# Iran: Mongol–Timurid Culture and Art — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Out of catastrophe, a golden age — miniature painting, astronomy and Timurid architecture make Herat and Samarkand the wonder of the age
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 8, Lesson 16 Victory of culture over the sword” is mainly about this: Conquest empties cities and qanats, then Ilkhanid and Timurid rulers rebuild an administration and economy on the ruins
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The chapter's title is the verdict — Persian culture, language and art absorb the conquerors until the conquerors' courts speak Persian
