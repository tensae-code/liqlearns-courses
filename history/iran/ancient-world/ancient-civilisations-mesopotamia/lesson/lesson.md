# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Between two rivers, farming villages swell into cities, kings and writing — civilisation's first chapter is Mesopotamian Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
