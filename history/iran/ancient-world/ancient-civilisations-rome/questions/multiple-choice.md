# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.**   What is the heart of the story in “Chapter 2, Lesson 6 Greece and Rome”?
a) Rome grows from city to republic to empire, ruling by law and legion until the western empire falls  b) Small Greek city-states argue, vote and fight their way toward democracy while inventing new ways to…  c) Long before Persia, Elam and the plateau's early cities farm, build and write — Iranian civilisation has…  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Rome grows from city to republic to empire, ruling by law and legion until the western empire falls** — That is what this lesson teaches.
