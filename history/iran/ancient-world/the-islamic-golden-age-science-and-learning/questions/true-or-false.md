# The Islamic Golden Age: Science and Learning — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   While kings fight, scholars measure the earth, heal the sick and invent algebra — the Islamic centuries told through their scientists
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 5, Lesson 10 What factors caused the spread of sciences and techniques in the Islamic period?” is mainly about this: Madrasas, caravanserais, Omar Khayyam and the Nizamiyya — the Seljuk peace is measured by what it built and wrote
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The child asks why — translation, paper, patronage and a shared language turn scattered learning into a civilisation-wide bloom
