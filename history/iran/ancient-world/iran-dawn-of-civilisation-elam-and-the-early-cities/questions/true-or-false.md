# Iran: Dawn of Civilisation — Elam and the Early Cities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Long before Persia, Elam and the plateau's early cities farm, build and write — Iranian civilisation has a dawn far older than its empires
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 9, Lesson 18 The oldest settlements of Iran” is mainly about this: Rome grows from city to republic to empire, ruling by law and legion until the western empire falls
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The first Iranian farmers settle, build and bury their dead in the plateau's oldest sites, thousands of years before any king
