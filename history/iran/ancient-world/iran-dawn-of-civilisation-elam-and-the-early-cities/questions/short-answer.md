# Iran: Dawn of Civilisation — Elam and the Early Cities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 8 Dawn of Iranian civilisation”?
**Answer:** Long before Persia, Elam and the plateau's early cities farm, build and write — Iranian civilisation has a dawn far older than its empires Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 9, Lesson 18 The oldest settlements of Iran” end?
**Answer:** The first Iranian farmers settle, build and bury their dead in the plateau's oldest sites, thousands of years before any king Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
