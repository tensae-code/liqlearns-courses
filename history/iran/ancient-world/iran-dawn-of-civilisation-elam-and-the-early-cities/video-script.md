# Video script — Iran: Dawn of Civilisation — Elam and the Early Cities


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Long before Persia, Elam and the plateau's early cities farm, build and write — Iranian civilisation has a dawn far older than its empires Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
The first Iranian farmers settle, build and bury their dead in the plateau's oldest sites, thousands of years before any king Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
