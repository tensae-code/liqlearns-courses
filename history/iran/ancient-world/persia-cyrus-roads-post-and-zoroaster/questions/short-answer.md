# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 9 From the arrival of the Aryans to the end of the Achaemenids — Cyrus”?
**Answer:** Cyrus founds an empire that rules by tolerance and organisation — roads, post and a proclaimed charter — and Babylon falls without a sack Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Chapter 3, Lesson 9 From the arrival of the Aryans to the end of the Achaemenids — Cyrus” end?
**Answer:** Cyrus founds an empire that rules by tolerance and organisation — roads, post and a proclaimed charter — and Babylon falls without a sack Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
