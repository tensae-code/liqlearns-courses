# Iran: Ancient Economy and Livelihood — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Qanats carry water, caravans carry goods, taxes carry the state — how ancient Iranians made a living is told as one connected system
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 11, Lesson 22 The economic conditions of ancient Iran” is mainly about this: Beneath the throne, classes, households and women and children live the empire — the lesson descends from palace to home
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Farming, herding, craft and trade — how the ancient Iranian world fed and enriched itself
