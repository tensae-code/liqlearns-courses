# Iran: Ancient Economy and Livelihood — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 13 Economy and livelihood”?
**Answer:** Qanats carry water, caravans carry goods, taxes carry the state — how ancient Iranians made a living is told as one connected system Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 11, Lesson 22 The economic conditions of ancient Iran” end?
**Answer:** Farming, herding, craft and trade — how the ancient Iranian world fed and enriched itself Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
