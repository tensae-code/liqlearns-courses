# Islam: From the Prophet's Death to Karbala — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Succession is disputed from the first day; within fifty years Husayn is killed at Karbala, and the martyrdom becomes the axis of Shi'a memory
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 4, Lessons 16–17 Journey to Karbala (1) and (2)” is mainly about this: After the Prophet's death the community nearly splits, then explodes outward — the first caliphs hold Arabia together and carry Islam into Persia and Byzantium's lands
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A pilgrimage trip frames the Karbala story itself — Husayn's stand and martyrdom retold for ten-year-olds at the shrine's door
