# Islam: From the Prophet's Death to Karbala — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 5, Lesson 10 From the Prophet's death to the uprising of Karbala (Nineveh)”?
**Answer:** Succession is disputed from the first day; within fifty years Husayn is killed at Karbala, and the martyrdom becomes the axis of Shi'a memory Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 4, Lessons 16–17 Journey to Karbala (1) and (2)” end?
**Answer:** A pilgrimage trip frames the Karbala story itself — Husayn's stand and martyrdom retold for ten-year-olds at the shrine's door Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
