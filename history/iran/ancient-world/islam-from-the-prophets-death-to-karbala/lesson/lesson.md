# Islam: From the Prophet's Death to Karbala

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Islam: From the Prophet's Death to Karbala** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 5, Lesson 10 From the Prophet's death to the uprising of Karbala (Nineveh)

Succession is disputed from the first day; within fifty years Husayn is killed at Karbala, and the martyrdom becomes the axis of Shi'a memory Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 4, Lessons 16–17 Journey to Karbala (1) and (2)

A pilgrimage trip frames the Karbala story itself — Husayn's stand and martyrdom retold for ten-year-olds at the shrine's door Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
