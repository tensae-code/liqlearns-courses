# The Umayyad Caliphate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The caliphate becomes a dynasty — Damascus rules an empire from Spain to Central Asia, and Karbala's wound opens a permanent divide
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Chapter 2, Lesson 6 The Umayyads on the seat of power” is mainly about this: Succession is disputed from the first day; within fifty years Husayn is killed at Karbala, and the martyrdom becomes the axis of Shi'a memory
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The caliphate becomes a dynasty — Damascus rules an empire from Spain to Central Asia, and Karbala's wound opens a permanent divide
