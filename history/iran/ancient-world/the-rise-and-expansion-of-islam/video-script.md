# Video script — The Rise and Expansion of Islam


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

A merchant city mocks its prophet; Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and the small community's survival is in doubt Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
The Hijra turns refugees into a state — in Medina the ummah gains a charter, a mosque-centre and a government Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
In a peninsula of tribes and caravan cities, a new faith is proclaimed and a community forms that will change two empires' maps Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Iran.
