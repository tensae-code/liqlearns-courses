# The Rise and Expansion of Islam

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Rise and Expansion of Islam** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 2, Lesson 3 Islam in Mecca

A merchant city mocks its prophet; Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and the small community's survival is in doubt Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
