# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 2, Lesson 3 Islam in Mecca”?
**Answer:** A merchant city mocks its prophet; Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and the small community's survival is in doubt Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 2, Lesson 4 The Prophetic ummah and government in Medina” end?
**Answer:** The Hijra turns refugees into a state — in Medina the ummah gains a charter, a mosque-centre and a government Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
