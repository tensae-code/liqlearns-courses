# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Iran · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Islam and the Iranian-Islamic Civilisation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A merchant city mocks its prophet; Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and the small community's survival is in doubt
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Islam and the Iranian-Islamic Civilisation (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 2, Lesson 3 Islam in Mecca** — A merchant city mocks its prophet; Muhammad preaches one God in Mecca, is persecuted, and the small community's survival is in doubt
2. **Chapter 2, Lesson 4 The Prophetic ummah and government in Medina** — The Hijra turns refugees into a state — in Medina the ummah gains a charter, a mosque-centre and a government
3. **Chapter 5, Lesson 9 The rise of Islam in the Arabian Peninsula** — In a peninsula of tribes and caravan cities, a new faith is proclaimed and a community forms that will change two empires' maps
4. **Chapter 4, Lesson 15 Medina, the city of the Prophet** — Medina is walked street by street as the city where the first Muslim community and government were built

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
