# Iran: Capable Viziers and Prosperous Cities — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The vizier becomes the hero — great ministers like the Seljuk Nizam al-Mulk build schools, roads and thriving cities — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
