# Iran: Capable Viziers and Prosperous Cities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   The vizier becomes the hero — great ministers like the Seljuk Nizam al-Mulk build schools, roads and thriving cities
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Chapter 5, Lesson 19 Capable ministers, prosperous cities” is mainly about this: Tahirids, Saffarids, Samanids, Buyids and the Alavids of Tabaristan rule Iran in the caliph's name or against him, and Persian language and learning flower again under their courts
