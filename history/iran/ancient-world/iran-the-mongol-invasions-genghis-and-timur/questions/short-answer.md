# Iran: The Mongol Invasions — Genghis and Timur — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 8, Lesson 15 The attack of Genghis and Timur on Iran”?
**Answer:** Twice in two centuries Iran is devastated by steppe conquerors — Genghis Khan's campaign annihilates the Khwarazmshah state, Timur repeats the terror Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 5, Lesson 20 Ruthless conquerors” end?
**Answer:** The Mongol invasion told without softening — cities destroyed and people massacred by conquerors the title itself calls merciless Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
