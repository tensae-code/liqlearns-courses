## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 3, Lesson 16 Art and architecture

Persepolis's columns and Sasanian arches are read as arguments in stone — what a civilisation builds is what it believes about itself Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
