# Iran: Parthians and Sasanians — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.**   Surena's horse-archers destroy Crassus at Carrhae; Ardashir overthrows the Parthians in 224 and founds the Sasanians; Shapur captures the Roman emperor Valerian — then, after Khosrow Parviz's fall and court chaos, the empire dies at Nahavand and Yazdgerd III is killed in 651
**Answer: True**

**2.**   The part “Chapter 3, Lesson 10 Parthians and Sasanians” is mainly about this: Aryan tribes enter the plateau, the Medes unite them and help destroy Assyria at Nineveh, and Darius then rebuilds the Achaemenid Empire into provinces, roads and law after civil war
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Surena's horse-archers destroy Crassus at Carrhae; Ardashir overthrows the Parthians in 224 and founds the Sasanians; Shapur captures the Roman emperor Valerian — then, after Khosrow Parviz's fall and court chaos, the empire dies at Nahavand and Yazdgerd III is killed in 651
