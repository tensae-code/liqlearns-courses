# Iran: Parthians and Sasanians — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Surena's horse-archers destroy Crassus at Carrhae; Ardashir overthrows the Parthians in 224 and founds the Sasanians; Shapur captures the Roman emperor Valerian — then, after Khosrow Parviz's fall and court chaos, the empire dies at Nahavand and Yazdgerd III is killed in 651 — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
