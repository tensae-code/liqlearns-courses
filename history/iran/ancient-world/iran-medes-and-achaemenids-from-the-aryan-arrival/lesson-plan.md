## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 3, Lesson 9 — Medes to Darius** — Aryan tribes enter the plateau, the Medes unite them and help destroy Assyria at Nineveh, and Darius then rebuilds the Achaemenid Empire into provinces, roads and law after civil war
2. **Chapter 10, Lesson 19 The Aryans and the formation of powerful governments in Iran** — Aryan tribes arrive, Medes and Persians build the first powerful Iranian states, and empire becomes possible
3. **Chapter 4, Lesson 11 A trip to the ancient city of Hamadan** — The class visits Ecbatana, the Medes' golden capital, and walks where the first Iranian kingdom ruled
4. **Chapter 4, Lesson 14 A trip to the ancient city of Kermanshah** — At Bisotun, Darius's carved cliff inscription tells how he seized and held the empire — the king's own words in stone

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
