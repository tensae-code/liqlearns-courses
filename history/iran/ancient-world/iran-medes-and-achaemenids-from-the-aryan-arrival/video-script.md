# Video script — Iran: Medes and Achaemenids, from the Aryan Arrival


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.


---


This lesson tells one story.

Aryan tribes enter the plateau, the Medes unite them and help destroy Assyria at Nineveh, and Darius then rebuilds the Achaemenid Empire into provinces, roads and law after civil war Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
Aryan tribes arrive, Medes and Persians build the first powerful Iranian states, and empire becomes possible Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
