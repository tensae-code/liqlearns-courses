# Iran: Medes and Achaemenids, from the Aryan Arrival — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 3, Lesson 9 — Medes to Darius”?
**Answer:** Aryan tribes enter the plateau, the Medes unite them and help destroy Assyria at Nineveh, and Darius then rebuilds the Achaemenid Empire into provinces, roads and law after civil war Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 10, Lesson 19 The Aryans and the formation of powerful governments in Iran” end?
**Answer:** Aryan tribes arrive, Medes and Persians build the first powerful Iranian states, and empire becomes possible Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
