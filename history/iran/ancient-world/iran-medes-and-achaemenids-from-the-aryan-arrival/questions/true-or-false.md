# Iran: Medes and Achaemenids, from the Aryan Arrival — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Aryan tribes enter the plateau, the Medes unite them and help destroy Assyria at Nineveh, and Darius then rebuilds the Achaemenid Empire into provinces, roads and law after civil war
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 10, Lesson 19 The Aryans and the formation of powerful governments in Iran” is mainly about this: Cyrus founds an empire that rules by tolerance and organisation — roads, post and a proclaimed charter — and Babylon falls without a sack
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Aryan tribes arrive, Medes and Persians build the first powerful Iranian states, and empire becomes possible
