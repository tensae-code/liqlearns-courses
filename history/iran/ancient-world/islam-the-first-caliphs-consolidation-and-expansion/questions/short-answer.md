**1.**   What happens in “Chapter 2, Lesson 5 Consolidation and expansion of Islam in the era of the first caliphs”?
**Answer:** After the Prophet's death the community nearly splits, then explodes outward — the first caliphs hold Arabia together and carry Islam into Persia and Byzantium's lands Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Chapter 2, Lesson 5 Consolidation and expansion of Islam in the era of the first caliphs” end?
**Answer:** After the Prophet's death the community nearly splits, then explodes outward — the first caliphs hold Arabia together and carry Islam into Persia and Byzantium's lands Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
