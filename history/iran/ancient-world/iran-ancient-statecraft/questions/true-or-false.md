# Iran: Ancient Statecraft — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   How did kings hold so vast a land? Satraps, inspectors, roads and the king's justice — the machinery of ruling is the lesson, not the battles
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 10, Lesson 20 How did the empires of ancient Iran administer the country?” is mainly about this: Surena's horse-archers destroy Crassus at Carrhae; Ardashir overthrows the Parthians in 224 and founds the Sasanians; Shapur captures the Roman emperor Valerian — then, after Khosrow Parviz's fall and court chaos, the empire dies at Nahavand and Yazdgerd III is killed in 651
