# The Abbasid Caliphate and the Islamic World

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Abbasid Caliphate and the Islamic World** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Baghdad rises as a planned capital of learning and trade while real power slowly leaks to regional dynasties Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
