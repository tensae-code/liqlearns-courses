# Iran: Local Dynasties and the Iranian-Islamic Renaissance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Tahirids, Saffarids, Samanids, Buyids and the Alavids of Tabaristan rule Iran in the caliph's name or against him, and Persian language and learning flower again under their courts
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 6, Lesson 12 The golden age of Iranian-Islamic culture and civilisation” is mainly about this: Arab armies end Sasanian rule, but Iran converts gradually and on its own terms, preparing a civilisation that will be both Islamic and Iranian
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Conquered Iran conquers back with culture — Persian administrators, scientists and poets make the Islamic golden age substantially Iranian
