**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 6, Lesson 12 The golden age of Iranian-Islamic culture and civilisation” end?
**Answer:** Conquered Iran conquers back with culture — Persian administrators, scientists and poets make the Islamic golden age substantially Iranian Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
