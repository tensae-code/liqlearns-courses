# Historiography: The History of History Writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   History-writing itself has a history — each age rewrites the past with its own questions, so the book teaches suspicion of any single telling
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Lesson 1 Historiography and the sources of the contemporary period” is mainly about this: Before any kings, the student asks what history even is and who writes it — the past does not speak until a historian questions it
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The contemporary past has different witnesses — newspapers, memoirs, photographs, state archives — and the final-year student learns each source's bias before the story begins
