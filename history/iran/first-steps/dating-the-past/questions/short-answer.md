# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 1, Lesson 2 History; time and place”?
**Answer:** An event without a when and a where is a rumour — the lesson builds the historian's two coordinates, chronology and geography, before the story starts Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 6, Lesson 21 The calendar” end?
**Answer:** A calendar is a people's way of counting its own time — solar Hijri years, months named for Zoroastrian spirits, and the dates a nation keeps Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
