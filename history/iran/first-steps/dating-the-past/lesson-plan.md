## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 1, Lesson 2 History; time and place** — An event without a when and a where is a rumour — the lesson builds the historian's two coordinates, chronology and geography, before the story starts
2. **Chapter 6, Lesson 21 The calendar** — A calendar is a people's way of counting its own time — solar Hijri years, months named for Zoroastrian spirits, and the dates a nation keeps
3. **Chapter 1, Lessons 1–2, 6 I was born; I have grown up; Change in the family** — An eight-year-old puts her own life in order — baby photos before school photos — and learns that families, like countries, have a past that changes
