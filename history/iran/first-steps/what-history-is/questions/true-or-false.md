# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   Before any kings, the student asks what history even is and who writes it — the past does not speak until a historian questions it
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   The part “Chapter 9, Lesson 17 Cultural heritage and history” is mainly about this: A 12-year-old meets "history" first as heritage — the inherited things and places that prove a past existed and make it worth studying
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The same lesson names the discipline itself — history as the study of past human life, distinct from the heritage it studies
