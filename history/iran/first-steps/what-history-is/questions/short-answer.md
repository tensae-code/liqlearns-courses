# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1)   What happens in “Chapter 1, Lesson 1 History and historiography”?
**Answer:** Before any kings, the student asks what history even is and who writes it — the past does not speak until a historian questions it Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2)   How does the story in “Chapter 9, Lesson 17 Cultural heritage and history” end?
**Answer:** The same lesson names the discipline itself — history as the study of past human life, distinct from the heritage it studies Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
