# Video script — From Evidence to a Written History


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

This lesson tells one story.

Iran's own deep past must be pieced from shards, inscriptions and foreign reports — the lesson shows the detective work behind the narrative that follows Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
A new period needs new sources — chronicles, coins, buildings and documents of the Islamic centuries are introduced as the evidence base Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
