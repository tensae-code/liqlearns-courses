## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 3, Lesson 7 Study and exploration of the distant pasts** — Iran's own deep past must be pieced from shards, inscriptions and foreign reports — the lesson shows the detective work behind the narrative that follows
2. **Chapter 1, Lesson 1 Sources of research in the history of Islam and Islamic Iran** — A new period needs new sources — chronicles, coins, buildings and documents of the Islamic centuries are introduced as the evidence base
3. **Chapter 3, Lesson 10 How do historians study the past?** — The method lesson for nine-year-olds — historians question objects, buildings and writings until the silent past answers
