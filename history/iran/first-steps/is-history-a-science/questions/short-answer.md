Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.**   What happens in “Chapter 1, Lesson 2 Research method in history; examining and testing the validity of evidence”?
**Answer:** Not every old report is true — the student learns to cross-examine a source's author, date and motive before believing a word Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.**   How does the story in “Chapter 1, Lesson 2 Research method in history; examining and testing the validity of evidence” end?
**Answer:** Not every old report is true — the student learns to cross-examine a source's author, date and motive before believing a word Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
