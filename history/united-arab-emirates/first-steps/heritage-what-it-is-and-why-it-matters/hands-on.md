# Hands-on — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say Sheikh Zayed's three-part teaching out loud: be proud of our heritage, preserve it, develop it. Now name one old thing each part could be done to — a family treasure, an old fort, a museum. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw a museum building and, inside it, three old objects kept safely; beside it draw an old stone fort. Then find one real old thing in your home that your family keeps, and say aloud where it came from and why your family keeps it. Say each part aloud: the museum, the fort, and your own family's kept thing.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see an old thing being kept with care, or hear about a museum or a fort, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
