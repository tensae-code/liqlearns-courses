# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is heritage?
**Answer:** Heritage is what our ancestors left us — their old things and their folk ways — kept in museums and old forts for the people who come after.

**2.** Why must the folk heritage be preserved and developed, in Sheikh Zayed's teaching?
**Answer:** So that it stays a store for this homeland and for coming generations — held with pride, kept safe, and let to grow and live on.
