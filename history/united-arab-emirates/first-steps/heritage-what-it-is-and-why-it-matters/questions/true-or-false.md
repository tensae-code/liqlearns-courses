# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The old forts are named among the country's landmarks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Heritage means only new things that nobody has used yet.
**Answer: False** — heritage is what our ancestors left us: their old things and their folk ways, kept in museums and old forts.
