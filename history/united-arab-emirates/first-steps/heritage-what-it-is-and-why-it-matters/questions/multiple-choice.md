# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a museum, as this lesson teaches it?
a) a shop for new things  b) the place where the old antiquities are preserved  c) a school for teachers  d) a fort for soldiers
**Answer: b) the place where the old antiquities are preserved** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Sheikh Zayed teach about the folk heritage our ancestors left us?
a) throw it away and forget it  b) hide it where no child can see it  c) be proud of it, preserve it and develop it  d) sell it to other countries
**Answer: c) be proud of it, preserve it and develop it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
