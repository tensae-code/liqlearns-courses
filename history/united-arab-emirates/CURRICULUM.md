# History — United Arab Emirates course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for the United Arab Emirates inside the History
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grade the book comes from appears only
in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation.

**How the levels derive:** the United Arab Emirates' book in
hand is one book — the Grade 1 Social Studies and National
Education student book, Term 3 volume — where history at the
very start of schooling is national identity. Its own story
divides by era, and the ladder follows that division: first
the first idea a young child needs — what heritage is, a
thing you can visit in a museum and a founder's instruction
you memorise; then the one great named person the book
reaches back to, Ibn Battuta, the traveller of the old
Islamic world; then the modern nation itself — the union of
the seven emirates, Sheikh Zayed who united it, and how the
young country's own schooling grew from the Mutawa'a's
circle to schools for every child. One book, one level —
the ladder stops where the book in hand stops, not because
the Emirates teach nothing more.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Social Studies and National Education book — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the combined-book contents
map, and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **First Steps → Ancient World → Modern World** — heritage
   first (what heritage is and why it matters, in one
   lesson); then the traveller of the Islamic world (Ibn
   Battuta, in one lesson); then the modern nation (the
   union and Sheikh Zayed, and how schooling began in the
   Emirates, in one lesson with two parts that are taught
   in file order). 3 lessons, in three titles:
   `first-steps/` (1 lesson), `ancient-world/` (1 lesson),
   then `modern-world/` (1 lesson).

**Totals: 3 lessons** (Level 1: 3 — all from one student book).
