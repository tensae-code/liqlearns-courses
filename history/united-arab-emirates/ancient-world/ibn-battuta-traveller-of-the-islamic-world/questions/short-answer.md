# Ibn Battuta, Traveller of the Islamic World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the places on Ibn Battuta's road that this lesson names, in order.
**Answer:** He set out from Morocco, passed through Algeria, Egypt and Bahrain, and travelled on to India — and to more than forty countries in all.

**2.** What title is Ibn Battuta remembered by, and what did he say about China?
**Answer:** He is remembered as the Prince of the Muslim Travellers, and he described China as one of the safest countries to travel in.
