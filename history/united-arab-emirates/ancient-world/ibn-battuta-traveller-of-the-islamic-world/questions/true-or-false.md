# Ibn Battuta, Traveller of the Islamic World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ibn Battuta travelled for twenty-seven years and visited more than forty countries.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ibn Battuta never wrote anything down about the peoples he saw.
**Answer: False** — he wrote down the customs and traditions of the peoples he saw, and the book of his travels is how the medieval world can still be walked in its pages.
