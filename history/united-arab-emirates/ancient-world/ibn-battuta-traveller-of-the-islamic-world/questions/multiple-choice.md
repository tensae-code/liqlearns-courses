# Ibn Battuta, Traveller of the Islamic World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where was Ibn Battuta born, and how did his journeys begin?
a) in India, as a sailor  b) in Morocco, setting out for the Hajj  c) in Egypt, as a teacher  d) in China, as a merchant
**Answer: b) in Morocco, setting out for the Hajj** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did Ibn Battuta travel?
a) by car and plane  b) only on foot  c) on camels and horses and in sailing ships  d) he never left Morocco
**Answer: c) on camels and horses and in sailing ships** — That is what this lesson teaches.
