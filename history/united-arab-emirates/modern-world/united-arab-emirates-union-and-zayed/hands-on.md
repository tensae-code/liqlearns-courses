# Hands-on — The United Arab Emirates: Union and Zayed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw seven shapes on paper, side by side. Now draw one big shape around them all, and say out loud: "Seven emirates — one country." Under it, write the founder's name: Sheikh Zayed. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the map of the seven emirates joined as one country, then draw two landmarks — one old (a fort) and one new (a tall tower) — and a small circle of children around one teacher for the Mutawa'a's school long ago. Say each part aloud as you draw it: the union, the old and the new, and where learning began.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see a landmark of the country, hear a saying of Sheikh Zayed, or sit in your own classroom, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
