# The United Arab Emirates: Union and Zayed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the family visit at the start of this story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the Etihad Museum in Dubai, the house of the union  b) a school in Sharjah  c) a desert camp  d) a sailing ship
**Answer: a) the Etihad Museum in Dubai, the house of the union** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was the first regular school in the country? (Sub lesson 2)
a) a school inside the Etihad Museum  b) Al Qasimiyah School in Sharjah  c) the Mutawa'a's house  d) a school that began in 1953
**Answer: b) Al Qasimiyah School in Sharjah** — That is what this lesson teaches.
