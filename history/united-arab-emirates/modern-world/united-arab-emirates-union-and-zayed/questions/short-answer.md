# The United Arab Emirates: Union and Zayed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two landmarks of the country named in this lesson, one old and one new. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any old-and-new pair from the lesson: Al Maqta Fort in Umm Al Quwain or the Ajman Museum (old), and the Louvre Abu Dhabi or the Burj Khalifa (new).

**2.** What did the Mutawa'a teach the children? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Mutawa'a taught the children the Qur'an, reading, writing and arithmetic — one teacher, before there were school buildings.
