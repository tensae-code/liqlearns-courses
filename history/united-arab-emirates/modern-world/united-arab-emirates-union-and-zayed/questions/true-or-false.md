# The United Arab Emirates: Union and Zayed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan was the founding president who united the emirates, and his sayings run through the book under the heading "Zayed taught me". (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Long ago in the Emirates, every child learned in a big school building with many teachers. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — teaching rested on the Mutawa'a, one teacher who taught the children Qur'an, reading, writing and arithmetic; the first regular school came later, at Al Qasimiyah in Sharjah.
