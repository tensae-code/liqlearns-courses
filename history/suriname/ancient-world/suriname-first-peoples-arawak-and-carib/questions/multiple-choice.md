# Suriname's First Peoples: Arawak and Carib — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Hoofdstuk 1: De eerste bewoners van ons land”?
a) 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here.  b) Children learn the two-verse anthem (Dutch and Sranan) and honour the flag at the school flag parade as the symbols that bind everyone who came to…  c) Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to…  d) Columbus reaches a "new world" believing it is India, names its friendly gift-giving people Indians, and European greed for gold and silver is…
**Answer: a) 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
