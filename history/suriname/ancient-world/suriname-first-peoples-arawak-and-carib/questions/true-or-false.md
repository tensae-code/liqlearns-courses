# Suriname's First Peoples: Arawak and Carib — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Teacher guidance for the first-Indigenous-peoples lesson, same content as the student book.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The part “Hoofdstuk 1: De eerste bewoners van ons land” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here.
