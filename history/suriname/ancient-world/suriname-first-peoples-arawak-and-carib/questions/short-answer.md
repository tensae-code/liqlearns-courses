# Suriname's First Peoples: Arawak and Carib — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Thema 1 Les 3: Eerste Inheemsen in ons land”?
**Answer:** The first Indigenous people reach Suriname 7,000–10,000 years ago via the Sipaliwini savanna; Arawak ancestors come from the Amazon/Orinoco 3,000+ years ago, and around 1100 the Caribs reach the Guiana coast by sea. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the story in “Thema 1 Les 3: Eerste Inheemsen in ons land (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the first-Indigenous-peoples lesson, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
