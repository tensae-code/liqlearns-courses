# Video script — Suriname's First Peoples: Arawak and Carib

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

The first Indigenous people reach Suriname 7,000–10,000 years ago via the Sipaliwini savanna; Arawak ancestors come from the Amazon/Orinoco 3,000+ years ago, and around 1100 the Caribs reach the Guiana coast by sea. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
