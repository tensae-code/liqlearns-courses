# The Maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In parts of the Americas farming begins early and great civilisations rise, the Maya among them, with cities where not everyone had to farm.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Thema 1 Les 2: Grote beschavingen (teacher guidance, Maya part)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance for the great-civilisations lesson, same content as the student book.
