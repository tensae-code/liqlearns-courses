# The Maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Thema 1 Les 2: Grote beschavingen in ons werelddeel (Maya part)”?
a) In parts of the Americas farming begins early and great civilisations rise, the Maya among them, with cities where not everyone had to farm.  b) Gauri, born in India in 1863, is recruited, sails three months to Suriname, works six years on a Commewijne sugar plantation, then saves, buys land…  c) Columbus reaches a "new world" believing it is India, names its friendly gift-giving people Indians, and European greed for gold and silver is…  d) In the 1929 crisis the unemployed organise in the SAWO; the governor declares their leader Louis Doedel insane and locks him in an asylum, and Anton…
**Answer: a) In parts of the Americas farming begins early and great civilisations rise, the Maya among them, with cities where not everyone had to farm.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Thema 1 Les 2: Grote beschavingen (teacher guidance, Maya part)”?
a) Teacher guidance for the great-civilisations lesson, same content as the student book.  b) Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.  c) The book explains how traders obtained captives in Africa and shipped them across the Atlantic to be sold for plantation labour in Suriname.  d) How we know about the first people in the Americas.
**Answer: a) Teacher guidance for the great-civilisations lesson, same content as the student book.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
