# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Teotihuacán in Mexico was one of the world's largest cities 1,500 years ago, and the Inca of highland Peru farmed mountain terraces and built Machu Picchu.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Thema 1 Les 2: Grote beschavingen (teacher guidance, Teotihuacán/Inca part)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance for the great-civilisations lesson, same content as the student book.
