# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Thema 1 Les 2: Grote beschavingen in ons werelddeel (Teotihuacán and Inca part)”?
**Answer:** Teotihuacán in Mexico was one of the world's largest cities 1,500 years ago, and the Inca of highland Peru farmed mountain terraces and built Machu Picchu. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Thema 1 Les 2: Grote beschavingen (teacher guidance, Teotihuacán/Inca part)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the great-civilisations lesson, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
