# History — Suriname course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Suriname's own — built from Suriname's *Wij en ons verleden* books (student books and teacher guides, Dutch). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the youngest readers' story book first, then the chronological survey, then the story rebuilt theme by theme, then modern national life. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
