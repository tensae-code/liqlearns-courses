# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 5) What happens in “Thema 5 Les 2: Eenmaal, andermaal … verkocht! (teacher guidance)”?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance, same content as the student book. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the story in “Hoofdstuk 3: Slavenhandel” end?
**Answer:** The book explains how traders obtained captives in Africa and shipped them across the Atlantic to be sold for plantation labour in Suriname. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
