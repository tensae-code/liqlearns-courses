# Video script — The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The book explains how traders obtained captives in Africa and shipped them across the Atlantic to be sold for plantation labour in Suriname. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Demand for enslaved labour in the Americas drives the trade from African coastal forts such as Elmina in Ghana, which the Dutch fought to capture. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
