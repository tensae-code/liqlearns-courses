# Suriname: The Maroons and Ja-Ja Dandé — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ja-Ja Dandé, priestess daughter of Maroon leader Ayoko, warns her forest people of soldiers, heals with herbs and persuades the Maroons to make peace, even letting the whites hold church in Santea.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 5: Ja-Ja Dandé (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teachers frame Ja-Ja Dandé as respect for elders' knowledge and for people who laid foundations the young now build on.
