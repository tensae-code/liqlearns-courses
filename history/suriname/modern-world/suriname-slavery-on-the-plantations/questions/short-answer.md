# Suriname: Slavery on the Plantations (Sampie's Story) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Les 4: Toch gered”?
**Answer:** Sampie, enslaved house servant of the strict sugar-planter Kosteron who beats him for a dropped cup, swims after his drowning master when the corial capsizes and saves him. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Les 4: Toch gered (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** The guide names the aim as denouncing slavery as one person making another serve him, taught through a corial model and dramatising Sampie's rescue. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
