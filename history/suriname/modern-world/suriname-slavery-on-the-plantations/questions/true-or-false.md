# Suriname: Slavery on the Plantations (Sampie's Story) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sampie, enslaved house servant of the strict sugar-planter Kosteron who beats him for a dropped cup, swims after his drowning master when the corial capsizes and saves him.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 4: Toch gered (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The guide names the aim as denouncing slavery as one person making another serve him, taught through a corial model and dramatising Sampie's rescue.
