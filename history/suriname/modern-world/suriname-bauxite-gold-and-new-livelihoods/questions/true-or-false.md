# Suriname: Bauxite, Gold and New Livelihoods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The part “Hoofdstuk 5: De opkomst van nieuwe middelen van bestaan” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: As plantations decline, freed and immigrant small farmers, balata bleeders, timber workers and gold seekers make new livings; bauxite mining (from the late 1910s) and the Brokopondo dam project later transform the economy.
