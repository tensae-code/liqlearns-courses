# Suriname: Bauxite, Gold and New Livelihoods

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Suriname: Bauxite, Gold and New Livelihoods** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hoofdstuk 5: De opkomst van nieuwe middelen van bestaan

As plantations decline, freed and immigrant small farmers, balata bleeders, timber workers and gold seekers make new livings; bauxite mining (from the late 1910s) and the Brokopondo dam project later transform the economy. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thema 7 Les 3: Werken om te leven

After supervision ends the freed choose work. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
