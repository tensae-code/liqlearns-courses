# Suriname: Escape to Freedom and Abolition, 1863

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Suriname: Escape to Freedom and Abolition, 1863** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Les 6: Varen naar de vrijheid

In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana; Suriname's own abolition comes only thirty years later, 1 July 1863. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Les 6: Varen naar de vrijheid (teacher guidance)

The guide frames Philip's 1833 escape as empathy with a historical event and freedom as a fundamental human right, located on the timeline. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
