# Suriname: Escape to Freedom and Abolition, 1863 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Les 6: Varen naar de vrijheid”?
**Answer:** In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana; Suriname's own abolition comes only thirty years later, 1 July 1863. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Les 6: Varen naar de vrijheid (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** The guide frames Philip's 1833 escape as empathy with a historical event and freedom as a fundamental human right, located on the timeline. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
