# Suriname: Escape to Freedom and Abolition, 1863 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana; Suriname's own abolition comes only thirty years later, 1 July 1863.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 6: Varen naar de vrijheid (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The guide frames Philip's 1833 escape as empathy with a historical event and freedom as a fundamental human right, located on the timeline.
