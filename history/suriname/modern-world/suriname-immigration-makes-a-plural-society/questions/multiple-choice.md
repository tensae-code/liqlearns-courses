# Suriname: Immigration Makes a Plural Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Thema 8 Les 1: Onze samenleving verandert”?
a) From a plantation colony in 1651 the population is remade group by group.  b) Ja-Ja Dandé, priestess daughter of Maroon leader Ayoko, warns her forest people of soldiers, heals with herbs and persuades the Maroons to make…  c) Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.  d) The book explains how traders obtained captives in Africa and shipped them across the Atlantic to be sold for plantation labour in Suriname.
**Answer: a) From a plantation colony in 1651 the population is remade group by group.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Thema 8 Les 1: Onze samenleving verandert (teacher guidance)”?
a) Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.  b) Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to…  c) 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here.  d) Indigenous fighters (Kaikoesie), enslaved rebels and Maroons resist in turn; the Cottica Maroons under Baron, Boni and Joli Coeur hold swamp Fort…
**Answer: a) Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
