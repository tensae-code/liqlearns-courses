# Suriname: Immigration Makes a Plural Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Taking its title from the anthem, the lesson recounts how each population group came to Suriname, building one multicultural society.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The part “Thema 3 Les 3: Ons land, een smeltkroes van culturen” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Suriname as melting pot: cultural exchange, respect and national unity across the groups, voiced by poet Robin Ravales (Dobru).
