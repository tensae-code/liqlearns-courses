# Suriname: Mata Gauri and Indian Immigration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Les 9: Mata Gauri”?
**Answer:** Gauri, born in India in 1863, is recruited, sails three months to Suriname, works six years on a Commewijne sugar plantation, then saves, buys land at Kwattaweg, funds a school in her Indian village and at 100 gives land for the Mata Gauri cultural building. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Les 9: Mata Gauri (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the Mata Gauri lesson, same story as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
