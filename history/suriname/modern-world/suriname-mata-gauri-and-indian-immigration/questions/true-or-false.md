# Suriname: Mata Gauri and Indian Immigration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gauri, born in India in 1863, is recruited, sails three months to Suriname, works six years on a Commewijne sugar plantation, then saves, buys land at Kwattaweg, funds a school in her Indian village and at 100 gives land for the Mata Gauri cultural building.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 9: Mata Gauri (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance for the Mata Gauri lesson, same story as the student book.
