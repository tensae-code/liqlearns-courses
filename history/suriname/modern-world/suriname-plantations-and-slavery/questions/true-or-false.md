# Suriname: Plantations and Slavery — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) One plantation studied whole: Meerzorg in Commewijne; by 1712 there are some 200 plantations, Cassard's French attack exposes weak defences, and Fort Nieuw Amsterdam is built in answer.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The part “Thema 4 Les 3: Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A plantation is a whole organisation under its owner and director; enslaved field gangs do the planting, tending and harvesting under the basya's watch while others look on at punishments.
