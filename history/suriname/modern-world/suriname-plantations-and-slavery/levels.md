# Suriname: Plantations and Slavery — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hoofdstuk 3: Plantages en slaven
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Plantations are laid out along the rivers; Indigenous people are first forced to work, then enslaved Africans are shipped in, sold in Paramaribo and driven by overseers while planters grow rich. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thema 4 Les 1: De aanleg van plantages
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Plantations are drained and laid out along the lower rivers and creeks with sluices and polders; today's place names (Beekhuizen, Berlijn, Totness) still remember them. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Thema 4 Les 2: Plantage Meerzorg
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: One plantation studied whole: Meerzorg in Commewijne; by 1712 there are some 200 plantations, Cassard's French attack exposes weak defences, and Fort Nieuw Amsterdam is built in answer. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Thema 4 Les 3: Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A plantation is a whole organisation under its owner and director; enslaved field gangs do the planting, tending and harvesting under the basya's watch while others look on at punishments. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
