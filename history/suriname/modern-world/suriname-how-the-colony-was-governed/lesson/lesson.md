# Suriname: How the Colony Was Governed

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Suriname: How the Colony Was Governed** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Thema 6 Les 1: De gouverneur en de Politieke Raad

Europeans replace Indigenous self-rule with foreign governors; in 1683 the Chartered Society of Suriname takes the colony's government, and governor and Political Council often clash. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thema 6 Les 2: Invoering van kiesrecht

After 1863 and state supervision the country is divided into districts under district commissioners, and the long road to voting rights and representation begins. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
