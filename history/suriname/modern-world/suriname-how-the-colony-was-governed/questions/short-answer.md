# Suriname: How the Colony Was Governed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Thema 6 Les 1: De gouverneur en de Politieke Raad”?
**Answer:** Europeans replace Indigenous self-rule with foreign governors; in 1683 the Chartered Society of Suriname takes the colony's government, and governor and Political Council often clash. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Thema 6 Les 2: Invoering van kiesrecht” end?
**Answer:** After 1863 and state supervision the country is divided into districts under district commissioners, and the long road to voting rights and representation begins. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
