# Suriname: Emancipation and Contract Labour — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Owners and traders resist abolition; the freed live under state supervision (staatstoezicht) with newspaper notices of release, and the Maroons' freedom is recognised too.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The part “Thema 8 Les 2: De komst van Hindostaanse en Javaanse contractarbeiders” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Following British Guiana's example, the Dutch open a Calcutta emigration office and recruit Indian contract workers, then Javanese.
