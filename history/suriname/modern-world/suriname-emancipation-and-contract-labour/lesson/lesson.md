## Sub lesson 1 — Hoofdstuk 4: Emancipatie, Staatstoezicht en contractarbeid

After protests and the 1804 English ban era, slavery ends 1 July 1863 but the freed face ten years of state supervision; planters import contract labourers instead. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
