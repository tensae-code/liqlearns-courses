## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Hoofdstuk 4: Emancipatie, Staatstoezicht en contractarbeid** — After protests and the 1804 English ban era, slavery ends 1 July 1863 but the freed face ten years of state supervision; planters import contract labourers instead.
2. **Thema 7 Les 1: Afschaffing van de slavernij in ons land** — Ketikoti, 1 July 1863: the book traces abolition of the trans-Atlantic trade, the effect of "Uncle Tom's Cabin" (1852) and the slave registers, to emancipation day itself.
3. **Thema 7 Les 2: Na de afschaffing van de slavernij** — Owners and traders resist abolition; the freed live under state supervision (staatstoezicht) with newspaper notices of release, and the Maroons' freedom is recognised too.
4. **Thema 8 Les 2: De komst van Hindostaanse en Javaanse contractarbeiders** — Following British Guiana's example, the Dutch open a Calcutta emigration office and recruit Indian contract workers, then Javanese.
5. **Thema 7 Les 1: Afschaffing van de slavernij in ons land (teacher guidance)** — Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
6. **Thema 7 Les 2: Na de afschaffing van de slavernij (teacher guidance)** — Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
7. **Thema 8 Les 2: De komst van Hindostaanse en Javaanse contractarbeiders (teacher guidance)** — Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
