# Suriname: Autonomy — Boss in Our Own House — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Thema 6 Les 3: Baas in eigen huis!”?
**Answer:** Elected Staten members represent the Surinamese people while the Dutch-appointed governor guards Dutch interests; their conflicts drive the demand to be boss in one's own house. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Thema 6 Les 3: Baas in eigen huis! (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
