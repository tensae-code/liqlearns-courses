# Suriname: Autonomy — Boss in Our Own House — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Elected Staten members represent the Surinamese people while the Dutch-appointed governor guards Dutch interests; their conflicts drive the demand to be boss in one's own house.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Thema 6 Les 3: Baas in eigen huis! (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
