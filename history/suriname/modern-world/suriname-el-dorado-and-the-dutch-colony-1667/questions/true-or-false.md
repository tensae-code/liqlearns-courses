# Suriname: El Dorado and the Dutch Colony, 1667 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) In the first half of the 1600s English settlers lay out some 60 plantations shipping tobacco and sugar to England, making Suriname an English plantation colony.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The part “Thema 3 Les 3: Ons land wordt een Nederlandse kolonie” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: In the Second Anglo-Dutch War Crijnssen takes Suriname (1667); the West India Company rules it, and in 1683 Cornelis van Aerssen van Sommelsdijck becomes governor.
