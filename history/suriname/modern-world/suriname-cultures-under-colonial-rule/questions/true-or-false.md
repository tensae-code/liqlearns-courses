# Suriname: Cultures Under Colonial Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Under colonial rule Europeans rated their culture above Indigenous, African and Asian cultures and imposed Dutch culture through school; the other cultures survived and were lived despite it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Thema 3 Les 2: Het beleven van cultuur (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance, same content as the student book, with poet Johanna Schouten-Elsenhout among its theme notes.
