# Suriname: Cultures Under Colonial Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Thema 3 Les 2: Het beleven van cultuur”?
a) Under colonial rule Europeans rated their culture above Indigenous, African and Asian cultures and imposed Dutch culture through school; the other…  b) Elected Staten members represent the Surinamese people while the Dutch-appointed governor guards Dutch interests; their conflicts drive the demand to…  c) In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana;…  d) The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: a) Under colonial rule Europeans rated their culture above Indigenous, African and Asian cultures and imposed Dutch culture through school; the other…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
