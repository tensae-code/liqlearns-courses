# Suriname: Cultures Under Colonial Rule

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Suriname: Cultures Under Colonial Rule** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Thema 3 Les 2: Het beleven van cultuur

Under colonial rule Europeans rated their culture above Indigenous, African and Asian cultures and imposed Dutch culture through school; the other cultures survived and were lived despite it. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
