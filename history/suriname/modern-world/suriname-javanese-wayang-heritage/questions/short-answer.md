# Suriname: Javanese Wayang Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Les 12: Pak Saleh laat de poppen bewegen”?
**Answer:** Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Les 12: Pak Saleh laat de poppen bewegen (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the wayang lesson, same story as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
