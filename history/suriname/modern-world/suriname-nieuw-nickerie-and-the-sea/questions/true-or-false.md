# Suriname: Nieuw-Nickerie and the Sea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river in 1879 to found Nieuw-Nickerie behind a sea dike.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 10: Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance for the drowned-town lesson, same story as the student book.
