# Suriname: Nieuw-Nickerie and the Sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Les 10: Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad”?
**Answer:** The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river in 1879 to found Nieuw-Nickerie behind a sea dike. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Les 10: Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the drowned-town lesson, same story as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
