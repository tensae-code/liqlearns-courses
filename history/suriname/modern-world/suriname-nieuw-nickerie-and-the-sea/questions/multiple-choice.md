# Suriname: Nieuw-Nickerie and the Sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Les 10: Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad”?
a) The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river…  b) Under the 1955 Ten-Year Plan the government and Alcoa agree in 1958 to dam the Suriname river at Brokopondo for hydroelectricity, flooding the homes…  c) Sampie, enslaved house servant of the strict sugar-planter Kosteron who beats him for a dropped cup, swims after his drowning master when the corial…  d) Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: a) The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
