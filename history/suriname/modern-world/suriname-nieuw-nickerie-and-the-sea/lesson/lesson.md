# Suriname: Nieuw-Nickerie and the Sea

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Suriname: Nieuw-Nickerie and the Sea** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Les 10: Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad

The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river in 1879 to found Nieuw-Nickerie behind a sea dike. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Les 10: Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad (teacher guidance)

Teacher guidance for the drowned-town lesson, same story as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
