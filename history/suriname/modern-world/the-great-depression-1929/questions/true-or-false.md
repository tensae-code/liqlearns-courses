# The Great Depression, 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The 1929 world crisis closes factories and plantations, raw-material prices fall, and Surinamese workers lose jobs and income.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Thema 1 Les 1: Een tijd van crisis en werkloosheid (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
