# The Great Depression, 1929 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Thema 1 Les 1: Een tijd van crisis en werkloosheid”?
**Answer:** The 1929 world crisis closes factories and plantations, raw-material prices fall, and Surinamese workers lose jobs and income. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Thema 1 Les 1: Een tijd van crisis en werkloosheid (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
