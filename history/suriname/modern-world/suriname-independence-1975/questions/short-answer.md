# Suriname: Independence, 1975 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Thema 7 Les 1: De onafhankelijkheid van ons land”?
**Answer:** After long talks with the Netherlands, on 25 November 1975 the Dutch flag comes down, the Surinamese flag and anthem (with its Sranan verse) rise, and Suriname becomes a republic. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the story in “Thema 7 Les 1: De onafhankelijkheid van ons land (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
