# Suriname: Independence, 1975 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The part “Hoofdstuk 7: Onafhankelijkheid” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After the war Surinamers demand to be "boss in their own house"; on 25 November 1975 Suriname becomes independent with its own flag and anthem and Johan Ferrier as first president.
