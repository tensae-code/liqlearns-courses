# Suriname: Independence, 1975

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Suriname: Independence, 1975** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hoofdstuk 7: Onafhankelijkheid

After the war Surinamers demand to be "boss in their own house"; on 25 November 1975 Suriname becomes independent with its own flag and anthem and Johan Ferrier as first president. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thema 7 Les 1: De onafhankelijkheid van ons land

After long talks with the Netherlands, on 25 November 1975 the Dutch flag comes down, the Surinamese flag and anthem (with its Sranan verse) rise, and Suriname becomes a republic. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
