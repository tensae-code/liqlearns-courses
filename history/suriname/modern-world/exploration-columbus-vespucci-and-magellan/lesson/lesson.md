# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hoofdstuk 2: Europeanen komen naar ons werelddeel (Columbus part)

Columbus reaches a "new world" believing it is India, names its friendly gift-giving people Indians, and European greed for gold and silver is awakened. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thema 2 Les 2: Een zeeweg naar Indië gevonden?

Columbus sails west for Spain on 3 August 1492 with the Niña, Pinta and Santa Maria; the 1507 world map first shows the Americas he had actually reached. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
