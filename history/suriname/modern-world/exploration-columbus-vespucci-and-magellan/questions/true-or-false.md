# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Teacher guidance for the Columbus lesson, same content as the student book.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The part “Hoofdstuk 2: Europeanen komen naar ons werelddeel (Columbus part)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Columbus reaches a "new world" believing it is India, names its friendly gift-giving people Indians, and European greed for gold and silver is awakened.
