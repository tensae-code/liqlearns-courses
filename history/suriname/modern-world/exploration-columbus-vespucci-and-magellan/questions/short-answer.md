# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Thema 2 Les 2: Een zeeweg naar Indië gevonden?”?
**Answer:** Columbus sails west for Spain on 3 August 1492 with the Niña, Pinta and Santa Maria; the 1507 world map first shows the Americas he had actually reached. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the story in “Thema 2 Les 2: Een zeeweg naar Indië gevonden? (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the Columbus lesson, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
