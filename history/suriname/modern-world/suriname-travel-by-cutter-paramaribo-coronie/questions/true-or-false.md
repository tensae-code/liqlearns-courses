# Suriname: Travel by Cutter, Paramaribo to Coronie — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) With no road to Coronie, the three-sailed cutter Alfons carries coconuts, pigs and passengers; ten-year-old Simon's prize trip to Paramaribo takes two days under sail.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 11: Met de kotter naar Paramaribo (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance for the cutter-voyage lesson, same story as the student book.
