# Suriname: Travel by Cutter, Paramaribo to Coronie — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Les 11: Met de kotter naar Paramaribo”?
**Answer:** With no road to Coronie, the three-sailed cutter Alfons carries coconuts, pigs and passengers; ten-year-old Simon's prize trip to Paramaribo takes two days under sail. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Les 11: Met de kotter naar Paramaribo (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the cutter-voyage lesson, same story as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
