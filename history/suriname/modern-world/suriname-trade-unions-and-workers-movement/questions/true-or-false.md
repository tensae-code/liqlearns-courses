# Suriname: Trade Unions and the Workers' Movement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) With no legal right to organise and leaders jailed or exiled (Doedel in an asylum for life, De Kom banished), workers still build unions and union federations to win better hours and conditions, striking when needed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Thema 1 Les 3: Vakbonden en vakcentrales (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
