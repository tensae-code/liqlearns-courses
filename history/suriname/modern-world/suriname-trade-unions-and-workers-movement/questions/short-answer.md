# Suriname: Trade Unions and the Workers' Movement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Thema 1 Les 3: Vakbonden en vakcentrales”?
**Answer:** With no legal right to organise and leaders jailed or exiled (Doedel in an asylum for life, De Kom banished), workers still build unions and union federations to win better hours and conditions, striking when needed. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Thema 1 Les 3: Vakbonden en vakcentrales (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
