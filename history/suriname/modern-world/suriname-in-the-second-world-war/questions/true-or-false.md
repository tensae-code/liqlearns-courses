# Suriname in the Second World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Governor Kielstra orders blackout from 7pm to 7am, dark curtains and air-raid shelters against a German attack that never comes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The part “Thema 4 Les 3: De bauxietindustrie in ons land” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: War cuts imports and queues lengthen in Paramaribo, yet scarcity sparks local production.
