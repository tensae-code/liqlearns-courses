# Video script — Suriname in the Second World War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

As a Dutch colony Suriname is at war; Germans are interned, the German ship Goslar is scuttled in the Suriname river, defences and a militia are organised, imports fail, and bauxite earnings leave the colony able to pay its own way. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

The book defines world war, sets out 1939–45, and brings the war home: as a Dutch colony Suriname is at war with Germany. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn.
