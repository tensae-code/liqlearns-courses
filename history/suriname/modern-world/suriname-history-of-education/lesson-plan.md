## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Thema 2 Les 1: Hoe was het vroeger?** — Before schools, Indigenous children learned hut-building, tools, hunting and healing from adults and the piai; enslaved children were barred from school and learned of slavery's injustice from their parents.
2. **Thema 2 Les 2: Het onderwijs verandert** — Fearing revenge at abolition, the government lets Christian churches from 1844 prepare the enslaved for freedom through religion and reading lessons; schooling slowly opens and changes.
3. **Thema 2 Les 3: Hoe belangrijk is goed onderwijs?** — A country needs nurses, teachers, police and builders who cannot be trained at home.
