## Sub lesson 1 — Thema 2 Les 1: Hoe was het vroeger?

Before schools, Indigenous children learned hut-building, tools, hunting and healing from adults and the piai; enslaved children were barred from school and learned of slavery's injustice from their parents. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thema 2 Les 2: Het onderwijs verandert

Fearing revenge at abolition, the government lets Christian churches from 1844 prepare the enslaved for freedom through religion and reading lessons; schooling slowly opens and changes. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
