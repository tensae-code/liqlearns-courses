# Suriname: The History of Education — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) A country needs nurses, teachers, police and builders who cannot be trained at home.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The part “Thema 2 Les 2: Hoe was het vroeger? (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
