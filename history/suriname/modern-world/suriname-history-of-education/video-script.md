# Video script — Suriname: The History of Education

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Before schools, Indigenous children learned hut-building, tools, hunting and healing from adults and the piai; enslaved children were barred from school and learned of slavery's injustice from their parents. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Fearing revenge at abolition, the government lets Christian churches from 1844 prepare the enslaved for freedom through religion and reading lessons; schooling slowly opens and changes. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
