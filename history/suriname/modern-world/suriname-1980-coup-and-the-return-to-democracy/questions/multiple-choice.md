# Suriname: The 1980 Coup and the Return to Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Thema 7 Les 2: Een staatsgreep in ons land”?
a) Independence brings a Surinamese army in place of the Dutch TRIS; a military coup then puts soldiers in power over the young republic.  b) Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to…  c) 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here.  d) Indigenous fighters (Kaikoesie), enslaved rebels and Maroons resist in turn; the Cottica Maroons under Baron, Boni and Joli Coeur hold swamp Fort…
**Answer: a) Independence brings a Surinamese army in place of the Dutch TRIS; a military coup then puts soldiers in power over the young republic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
