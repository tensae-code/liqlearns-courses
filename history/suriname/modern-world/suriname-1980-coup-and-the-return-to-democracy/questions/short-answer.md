# Suriname: The 1980 Coup and the Return to Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Thema 7 Les 2: Een staatsgreep in ons land”?
**Answer:** Independence brings a Surinamese army in place of the Dutch TRIS; a military coup then puts soldiers in power over the young republic. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Thema 7 Les 3: Veranderingen in het bestuur” end?
**Answer:** From 1985 talks seek free elections, but in July 1986 the interior war breaks out between the Jungle Commando and the National Army, devastating Albina, Pokigron and Moiwana's region in the east. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
