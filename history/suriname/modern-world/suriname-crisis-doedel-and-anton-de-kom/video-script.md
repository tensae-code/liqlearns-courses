# Video script — Suriname: Crisis, Doedel and Anton de Kom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

In the 1929 crisis the unemployed organise in the SAWO; the governor declares their leader Louis Doedel insane and locks him in an asylum, and Anton de Kom, returned in 1933 running a complaints office for workers, is arrested and deported after a fatal protest march. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Doedel organises the unemployed and De Kom writes a programme against the abuses he finds; the government strikes hard at both, and a Comité van Actie presses the workers' demands. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

Teacher guidance, same content as the student book. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Suriname.
