# Suriname: Crisis, Doedel and Anton de Kom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The part “Hoofdstuk 7: Crisis en oorlog (crisis part)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: In the 1929 crisis the unemployed organise in the SAWO; the governor declares their leader Louis Doedel insane and locks him in an asylum, and Anton de Kom, returned in 1933 running a complaints office for workers, is arrested and deported after a fatal protest march.
