# Suriname: Crisis, Doedel and Anton de Kom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens in “Thema 1 Les 2: Louis Doedel en Anton de Kom”?
**Answer:** Doedel organises the unemployed and De Kom writes a programme against the abuses he finds; the government strikes hard at both, and a Comité van Actie presses the workers' demands. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the story in “Thema 1 Les 2: Louis Doedel en Anton de Kom (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
