# Suriname: Jan Ernst Matzeliger, Inventor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Plantation-born Jan Ernst, who hated school but loved screws and wire, works in his father's workshop, sails to North America and invents the shoe-lasting machine that changes shoe factories worldwide.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 7: Een gewone jongen wordt een uitvinder (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teachers open with hand work later taken over by machines (handwriting to computer) to build appreciation for Matzeliger's pioneering achievement.
