# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Thema 2 Les 1: Op zoek naar een zeeweg naar Indië”?
a) As spice prices climb, 15th-century Portuguese sailors.  b) Children learn the two-verse anthem (Dutch and Sranan) and honour the flag at the school flag parade as the symbols that bind everyone who came to…  c) Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to…  d) 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here.
**Answer: a) As spice prices climb, 15th-century Portuguese sailors.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
