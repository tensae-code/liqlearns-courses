# Suriname: Resistance — Maroons and Uprisings — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hoofdstuk 6: Onderdrukking en verzet
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Indigenous fighters (Kaikoesie), enslaved rebels and Maroons resist in turn; the Cottica Maroons under Baron, Boni and Joli Coeur hold swamp Fort Buku until betrayal reveals its underwater path, and Boni fights on until 1792; later contract workers rise at Zoelen, Alliance and Mariënburg under leaders like Ramdjanee. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thema 6 Les 1: Het verzet van slaafgemaakten
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Resistance runs from stories and songs through sabotage of work to open joint revolt on the plantation and flight to camps in the forest. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Thema 6 Les 2: Strijden voor de vrijheid
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Maroon villages deep inland organise well-planned attacks on plantations to free people and seize supplies, while forest patrols hunt the escapees. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Thema 6 Les 3: De strijd gaat door
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Peace with the Ndyuka (10 October 1760) and two more Maroon groups follows, but the Boni wars go on around Fort Boekoe and the Cordonpad under Governor Nepveu's counter-measures. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
