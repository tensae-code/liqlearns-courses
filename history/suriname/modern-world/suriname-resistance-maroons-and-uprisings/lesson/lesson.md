## Sub lesson 1 — Hoofdstuk 6: Onderdrukking en verzet

Indigenous fighters (Kaikoesie), enslaved rebels and Maroons resist in turn; the Cottica Maroons under Baron, Boni and Joli Coeur hold swamp Fort Buku until betrayal reveals its underwater path, and Boni fights on until 1792; later contract workers rise at Zoelen, Alliance and Mariënburg under leaders like Ramdjanee. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Thema 6 Les 1: Het verzet van slaafgemaakten

Resistance runs from stories and songs through sabotage of work to open joint revolt on the plantation and flight to camps in the forest.
