Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Hoofdstuk 6: Onderdrukking en verzet”?

**Answer: a) Indigenous fighters (Kaikoesie), enslaved rebels and Maroons resist in turn; the Cottica Maroons under Baron, Boni and Joli Coeur hold swamp Fort…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
