# Suriname: Resistance — Maroons and Uprisings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Maroon villages deep inland organise well-planned attacks on plantations to free people and seize supplies, while forest patrols hunt the escapees.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) The part “Thema 6 Les 3: De strijd gaat door” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Peace with the Ndyuka (10 October 1760) and two more Maroon groups follows, but the Boni wars go on around Fort Boekoe and the Cordonpad under Governor Nepveu's counter-measures.
