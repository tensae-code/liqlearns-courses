## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Hoofdstuk 6: Onderdrukking en verzet** — Indigenous fighters (Kaikoesie), enslaved rebels and Maroons resist in turn; the Cottica Maroons under Baron, Boni and Joli Coeur hold swamp Fort Buku until betrayal reveals its underwater path, and Boni fights on until 1792; later contract workers rise at Zoelen, Alliance and Mariënburg under leaders like Ramdjanee.
2. **Thema 6 Les 1: Het verzet van slaafgemaakten** — Resistance runs from stories and songs through sabotage of work to open joint revolt on the plantation and flight to camps in the forest.
3. **Thema 6 Les 2: Strijden voor de vrijheid** — Maroon villages deep inland organise well-planned attacks on plantations to free people and seize supplies, while forest patrols hunt the escapees.
4. **Thema 6 Les 3: De strijd gaat door** — Peace with the Ndyuka (10 October 1760) and two more Maroon groups follows, but the Boni wars go on around Fort Boekoe and the Cordonpad under Governor Nepveu's counter-measures.
5. **Thema 8 Les 3: Het verzet van de contractarbeiders** — Cheated of the free housing and fair treatment their contracts promised, contract workers resist; in 1902 the great Mariënburg uprising sees a plantation director killed.
