# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Thema 2 Les 3: Vreemdelingen in de Amerika's”?
a) Europeans after Columbus fight and settle in the Americas; world trade is born and the continent's population changes forever.  b) The 1929 world crisis closes factories and plantations, raw-material prices fall, and Surinamese workers lose jobs and income.  c) Elected Staten members represent the Surinamese people while the Dutch-appointed governor guards Dutch interests; their conflicts drive the demand to…  d) In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana;…
**Answer: a) Europeans after Columbus fight and settle in the Americas; world trade is born and the continent's population changes forever.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
