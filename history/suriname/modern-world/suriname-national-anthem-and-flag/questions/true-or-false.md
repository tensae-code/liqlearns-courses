# Suriname: The National Anthem and Flag — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Children learn the two-verse anthem (Dutch and Sranan) and honour the flag at the school flag parade as the symbols that bind everyone who came to Suriname's soil.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 1: Ons volkslied (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teachers build correct attitude to flag and anthem as citizenship; the guide explains the flag's symbolism.
