# Suriname: The National Anthem and Flag — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Les 1: Ons volkslied”?
a) Children learn the two-verse anthem (Dutch and Sranan) and honour the flag at the school flag parade as the symbols that bind everyone who came to…  b) Under colonial rule Europeans rated their culture above Indigenous, African and Asian cultures and imposed Dutch culture through school; the other…  c) Digging the school garden, Susan turns up a shining flat stone; her teacher identifies a stone axe made by Indigenous people 500+ years ago, before…  d) The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river…
**Answer: a) Children learn the two-verse anthem (Dutch and Sranan) and honour the flag at the school flag parade as the symbols that bind everyone who came to…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Les 1: Ons volkslied (teacher guidance)”?
a) Teachers build correct attitude to flag and anthem as citizenship; the guide explains the flag's symbolism.  b) Europeans replace Indigenous self-rule with foreign governors; in 1683 the Chartered Society of Suriname takes the colony's government, and governor…  c) In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana;…  d) The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: a) Teachers build correct attitude to flag and anthem as citizenship; the guide explains the flag's symbolism.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
