# Video script — Suriname: The National Anthem and Flag

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Children learn the two-verse anthem (Dutch and Sranan) and honour the flag at the school flag parade as the symbols that bind everyone who came to Suriname's soil. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Teachers build correct attitude to flag and anthem as citizenship; the guide explains the flag's symbolism. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Suriname.
