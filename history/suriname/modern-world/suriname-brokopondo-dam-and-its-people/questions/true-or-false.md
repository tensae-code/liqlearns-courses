# Suriname: The Brokopondo Dam and Its People — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Under the 1955 Ten-Year Plan the government and Alcoa agree in 1958 to dam the Suriname river at Brokopondo for hydroelectricity, flooding the homes of the people and animals of the area.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Thema 5 Les 2: De stuwdam in de Surinamerivier (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance, same content as the student book.
