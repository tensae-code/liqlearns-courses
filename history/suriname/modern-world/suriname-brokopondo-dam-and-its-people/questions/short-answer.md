# Suriname: The Brokopondo Dam and Its People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Thema 5 Les 2: De stuwdam in de Surinamerivier”?
**Answer:** Under the 1955 Ten-Year Plan the government and Alcoa agree in 1958 to dam the Suriname river at Brokopondo for hydroelectricity, flooding the homes of the people and animals of the area. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Thema 5 Les 2: De stuwdam in de Surinamerivier (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance, same content as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
