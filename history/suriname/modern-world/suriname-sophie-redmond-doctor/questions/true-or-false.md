# Suriname: Sophie Redmond, Doctor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to home visits until her death in 1955.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 8: Een mevrouw met een gouden hart (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance for the Sophie Redmond lesson, same story as the student book.
