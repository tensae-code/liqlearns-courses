# Suriname: Sophie Redmond, Doctor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Les 8: Een mevrouw met een gouden hart”?
a) Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to…  b) The 1929 world crisis closes factories and plantations, raw-material prices fall, and Surinamese workers lose jobs and income.  c) Elected Staten members represent the Surinamese people while the Dutch-appointed governor guards Dutch interests; their conflicts drive the demand to…  d) In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana;…
**Answer: a) Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Teacher guidance for the Sophie Redmond lesson, same story as the student book.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
