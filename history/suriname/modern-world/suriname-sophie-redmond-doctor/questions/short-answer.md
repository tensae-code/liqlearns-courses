# Suriname: Sophie Redmond, Doctor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Les 8: Een mevrouw met een gouden hart”?
**Answer:** Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to home visits until her death in 1955. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Les 8: Een mevrouw met een gouden hart (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the Sophie Redmond lesson, same story as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
