# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Digging the school garden, Susan turns up a shining flat stone; her teacher identifies a stone axe made by Indigenous people 500+ years ago, before iron tools.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 3: Susan vindt iets (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teachers use found shards and old bottles in dug soil to confront pupils directly with remains of past communities, then place the stone axe on the timeline.
