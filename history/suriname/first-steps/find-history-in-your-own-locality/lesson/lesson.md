# Find History in Your Own Locality

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Find History in Your Own Locality** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Les 13: Was het vroeger saai?

The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Les 13: Was het vroeger saai? (teacher guidance)

Teacher guidance for the Oma Didi oral-history lesson, same story as the student book. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
