# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 13: Was het vroeger saai? (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance for the Oma Didi oral-history lesson, same story as the student book.
