**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Les 13: Was het vroeger saai?”?
a) The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.  b) Under colonial rule Europeans rated their culture above Indigenous, African and Asian cultures and imposed Dutch culture through school; the other…  c) History is introduced as everything that already happened, 8 or 200 years ago, still visible in old houses and furniture and askable from…  d) Gauri, born in India in 1863, is recruited, sails three months to Suriname, works six years on a Commewijne sugar plantation, then saves, buys land…
**Answer: a) The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
