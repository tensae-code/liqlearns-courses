# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **What History Is** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Les 2: Aan alle leerlingen

History is introduced as everything that already happened, 8 or 200 years ago, still visible in old houses and furniture and askable from grandparents. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Les 2: Aan alle leerlingen (teacher guidance)

Teachers introduce the time structure past/now with a board timeline and a child's own baby-to-nine-years photos as the first historical sequence. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
