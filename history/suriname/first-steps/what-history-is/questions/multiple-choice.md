# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Les 2: Aan alle leerlingen”?
a) History is introduced as everything that already happened, 8 or 200 years ago, still visible in old houses and furniture and askable from…  b) Spaniards chase the golden king El Dorado by Lake Parima and leave the "Wild Coast" disappointed; around 1650 Willoughby's English plant tobacco and…  c) As a Dutch colony Suriname is at war; Germans are interned, the German ship Goslar is scuttled in the Suriname river, defences and a militia are…  d) From a plantation colony in 1651 the population is remade group by group.
**Answer: a) History is introduced as everything that already happened, 8 or 200 years ago, still visible in old houses and furniture and askable from…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
