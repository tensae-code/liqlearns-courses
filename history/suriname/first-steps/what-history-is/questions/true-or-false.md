# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) History is introduced as everything that already happened, 8 or 200 years ago, still visible in old houses and furniture and askable from grandparents.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 2: Aan alle leerlingen (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: The class invites nearly-eighty-year-old Oma Didi to tell how children played before radio and TV.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teachers introduce the time structure past/now with a board timeline and a child's own baby-to-nine-years photos as the first historical sequence.
