# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A class visits the Surinamese Museum's Indigenous exhibition, learns objects show how people lived, and hears that a village without a museum can collect its old things and found one.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Les 14: Naar het museum (teacher guidance)” is mainly about this: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees.
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Teacher guidance for the museum lesson, same story as the student book, with the closing explanatory glossary (printed pp. 25–28) as teacher background.
