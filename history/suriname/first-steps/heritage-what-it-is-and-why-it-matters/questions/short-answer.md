# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Les 14: Naar het museum”?
**Answer:** A class visits the Surinamese Museum's Indigenous exhibition, learns objects show how people lived, and hears that a village without a museum can collect its old things and found one. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Les 14: Naar het museum (teacher guidance)” end?
**Answer:** Teacher guidance for the museum lesson, same story as the student book, with the closing explanatory glossary (printed pp. 25–28) as teacher background. Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
