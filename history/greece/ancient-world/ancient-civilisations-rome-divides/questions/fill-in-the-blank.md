# Ancient civilisations rome divides — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 5 Great changes in the empire's administration" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Diocletian and Constantine remake the empire's government, army and coinage to survive its crises.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 9 The Empire is divided into Eastern and Western" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Theodosius's two sons split the empire for good; the West will fall, the Greek East will become Byzantium.
