# Ancient civilisations rome divides — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 5 Great changes in the empire's administration" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Diocletian and Constantine remake the empire's government, army and coinage to survive its crises.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 9 The Empire is divided into Eastern and Western" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Theodosius's two sons split the empire for good; the West will fall, the Greek East will become Byzantium.
