# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 8 Christianity becomes the state's official religion" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Within one lifetime Christianity moves from persecuted sect to Theodosius's official faith, and the old temples close.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 8 Christianity official exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils test whether Theodosius's and Julian's acts fit a Christian or a pagan emperor.
