# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 8 Christianity becomes the state's official religion" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Within one lifetime Christianity moves from persecuted sect to Theodosius's official faith, and the old temples close.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 8 Christianity official exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils test whether Theodosius's and Julian's acts fit a Christian or a pagan emperor.
