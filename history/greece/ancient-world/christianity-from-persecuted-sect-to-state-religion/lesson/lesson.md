# Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Christianity from persecuted sect to state religion" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 8 Christianity becomes the state's official religion

Within one lifetime Christianity moves from persecuted sect to Theodosius's official faith, and the old temples close.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 8 Christianity official exercises

Pupils test whether Theodosius's and Julian's acts fit a Christian or a pagan emperor.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ch. J3 The spread of Christianity

Paul and the apostles carry the new faith along Roman roads through the Greek cities despite persecutions.

## Sub lesson 4 — Late antiquity: the Roman state transformed (4th–5th c.)

Diocletian reorganises the empire, Constantine turns it Christian and eastern, peoples migrate in, and the West falls while the East Hellenises.

## Sub lesson 5 — The opening from Palestine to the Greco-Roman world

A Jewish movement in Jerusalem becomes, within a generation, Greek-speaking communities across the Roman cities.

## Sub lesson 6 — Persecutions, recognition and acceptance of Christianity

Emperors persecute the Church in waves; Constantine legalises it, and within a century it is the empire's own religion.
