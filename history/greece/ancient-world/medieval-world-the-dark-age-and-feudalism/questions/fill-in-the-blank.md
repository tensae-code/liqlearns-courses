# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 6.I.3 Feudalism in Western Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Lords, vassals and serfs bind a land without states into a ladder of oaths and fiefs.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Economy and society in Western Europe: the feudal system" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
