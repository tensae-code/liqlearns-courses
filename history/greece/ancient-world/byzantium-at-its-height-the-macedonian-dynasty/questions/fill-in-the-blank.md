# Byzantium at its height the macedonian dynasty — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 23 Legislation and administration are modernised" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Leo VI and the Macedonian emperors reissue the laws in Greek and tighten the themes' government.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 25 Byzantium reaches the peak of its flourishing" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Basil II grinds Bulgaria down to "the Bulgar-slayer", and the empire again stretches from Italy to the Euphrates.
