# Byzantium at its height the macedonian dynasty — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 23 Legislation and administration are modernised" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Leo VI and the Macedonian emperors reissue the laws in Greek and tighten the themes' government.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 25 Byzantium reaches the peak of its flourishing" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Basil II grinds Bulgaria down to "the Bulgar-slayer", and the empire again stretches from Italy to the Euphrates.
