# Lesson plan — The fall of constantinople 1453

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** The fall of constantinople 1453
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — A thousand years of Byzantium: Constantinople founded, walled, besieged, lost and regained, to 1453 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of The fall of constantinople 1453 as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — A thousand years of Byzantium: Constantinople founded, walled, besieged, lost and regained, to 1453 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unit 34 Constantine Palaiologos tries to save the capital** — the last emperor begs the West for help, accepts a hated church union, and prepares a city of a few thousand defenders
2. **§2 Unit 35 The Turks besiege Constantinople** — Mehmed II builds the throat-cutter castle, drags ships overland into the Golden Horn and points the great cannon at the Theodosian walls
3. **§3 Unit 36 The capture of Constantinople by the Turks** — on 29 May 1453 the walls are breached, Constantine dies fighting at the gate, and the thousand-year empire ends
4. **§4 Unit 34 Constantine Palaiologos exercises** — exercises weigh the last emperor's appeals to the West
5. **§5 Unit 35 Siege exercises** — pupils follow Mehmed's cannon and the ships hauled overland
6. **§6 Unit 36 Fall exercises** — exercises close the Byzantine story on 29 May 1453
7. **§7 Ch. 4.III.2 The Fall of the City** — Mehmed's cannon, the chain across the Horn, Giustiniani's wound and the last liturgy in Hagia Sophia
8. **§8 The Fall of Constantinople** — Mehmed II's great cannon breach the walls on 29 May 1453; the last emperor dies on them
