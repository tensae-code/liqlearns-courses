# The fall of constantinople 1453 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 34 Constantine Palaiologos tries to save the capital" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The last emperor begs the West for help, accepts a hated church union, and prepares a city of a few thousand defenders.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 35 The Turks besiege Constantinople" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mehmed II builds the throat-cutter castle, drags ships overland into the Golden Horn and points the great cannon at the Theodosian walls.
