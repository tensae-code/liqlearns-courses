# The fall of constantinople 1453 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 34 Constantine Palaiologos tries to save the capital" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The last emperor begs the West for help, accepts a hated church union, and prepares a city of a few thousand defenders.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 35 The Turks besiege Constantinople" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mehmed II builds the throat-cutter castle, drags ships overland into the Golden Horn and points the great cannon at the Theodosian walls.
