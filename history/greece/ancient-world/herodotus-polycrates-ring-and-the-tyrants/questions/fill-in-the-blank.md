# Herodotus polycrates ring and the tyrants — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Book III, Unit 7 The ring of Polycrates" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Amasis warns the too-lucky tyrant of Samos; Polycrates throws his ring into the sea, a fish returns it, and his fortune turns to a Persian cross.
