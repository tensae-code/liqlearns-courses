# Herodotus polycrates ring and the tyrants — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Book III, Unit 7 The ring of Polycrates" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Amasis warns the too-lucky tyrant of Samos; Polycrates throws his ring into the sea, a fish returns it, and his fortune turns to a Persian cross.
