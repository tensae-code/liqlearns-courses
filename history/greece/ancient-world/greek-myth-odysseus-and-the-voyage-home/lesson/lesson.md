# Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes

Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 6, ch. 2 With Aeolus, the Laestrygonians and on Circe's island

Aeolus's bag of winds is opened within sight of home, giants destroy the fleet, and Circe turns the scouts into swine until Odysseus resists her.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 6, ch. 3 In Hades, at the Sirens, at Scylla and Charybdis

Odysseus questions the dead in Hades, hears the Sirens bound to the mast, and threads the strait losing six men to Scylla.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 6, ch. 4 The Sun's island, Calypso's island and the Phaeacians

The crew slaughter the Sun's cattle and drown for it; Odysseus alone survives, spends years with Calypso, and the Phaeacians carry him home asleep.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 6, ch. 5 Odysseus in Ithaca

Disguised as a beggar, Odysseus is sheltered by the loyal swineherd Eumaeus and tests who in his house stayed faithful.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit 6, ch. 6 Odysseus kills the suitors

Penelope's weaving trick revealed, Odysseus alone strings the great bow in the contest and turns it on the suitors who consumed his house.

## Sub lesson 7 — Unit 6, ch. 1 Cicones, Lotus-eaters, Cyclopes exercises

Exercises follow Odysseus's first three landfalls and the blinding of Polyphemus.

## Sub lesson 8 — Unit 6, ch. 2 Aeolus, Laestrygonians, Circe exercises

Pupils work out why the pictured ship is named as it is while retelling the bag of winds and Circe's swine.

## Sub lesson 9 — Unit 6, ch. 3 Hades, Sirens, Scylla and Charybdis exercises

Exercises thread Odysseus between the monsters and through the land of the dead.

## Sub lesson 10 — Unit 6, ch. 4 Sun's island, Calypso, Phaeacians exercises

Pupils judge the crew's fatal meal of the Sun's cattle and Odysseus's lone survival.

## Sub lesson 11 — Unit 6, ch. 5 Odysseus in Ithaca exercises

Exercises find signs of the disguised king in today's lesson pictures.

## Sub lesson 12 — Unit 6, ch. 6 Killing of the suitors exercises

Pupils judge Odysseus's revenge on the suitors.
