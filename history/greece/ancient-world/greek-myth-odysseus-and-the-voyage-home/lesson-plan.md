# Lesson plan — Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myth first, then the first farmers: the gods and heroes, prehistory, and the Aegean civilisations, as Greece's youngest readers meet the past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Myth first, then the first farmers: the gods and heroes, prehistory, and the Aegean civilisations, as Greece's youngest readers meet the past — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes** — Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep
2. **§2 Unit 6, ch. 2 With Aeolus, the Laestrygonians and on Circe's island** — Aeolus's bag of winds is opened within sight of home, giants destroy the fleet, and Circe turns the scouts into swine until Odysseus resists her
3. **§3 Unit 6, ch. 3 In Hades, at the Sirens, at Scylla and Charybdis** — Odysseus questions the dead in Hades, hears the Sirens bound to the mast, and threads the strait losing six men to Scylla
4. **§4 Unit 6, ch. 4 The Sun's island, Calypso's island and the Phaeacians** — the crew slaughter the Sun's cattle and drown for it; Odysseus alone survives, spends years with Calypso, and the Phaeacians carry him home asleep
5. **§5 Unit 6, ch. 5 Odysseus in Ithaca** — disguised as a beggar, Odysseus is sheltered by the loyal swineherd Eumaeus and tests who in his house stayed faithful
6. **§6 Unit 6, ch. 6 Odysseus kills the suitors** — Penelope's weaving trick revealed, Odysseus alone strings the great bow in the contest and turns it on the suitors who consumed his house
7. **§7 Unit 6, ch. 1 Cicones, Lotus-eaters, Cyclopes exercises** — exercises follow Odysseus's first three landfalls and the blinding of Polyphemus
8. **§8 Unit 6, ch. 2 Aeolus, Laestrygonians, Circe exercises** — pupils work out why the pictured ship is named as it is while retelling the bag of winds and Circe's swine
9. **§9 Unit 6, ch. 3 Hades, Sirens, Scylla and Charybdis exercises** — exercises thread Odysseus between the monsters and through the land of the dead
10. **§10 Unit 6, ch. 4 Sun's island, Calypso, Phaeacians exercises** — pupils judge the crew's fatal meal of the Sun's cattle and Odysseus's lone survival
11. **§11 Unit 6, ch. 5 Odysseus in Ithaca exercises** — exercises find signs of the disguised king in today's lesson pictures
12. **§12 Unit 6, ch. 6 Killing of the suitors exercises** — pupils judge Odysseus's revenge on the suitors
