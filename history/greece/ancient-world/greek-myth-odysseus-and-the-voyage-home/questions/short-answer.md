# Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 6, ch. 2 With Aeolus, the Laestrygonians and on Circe's island" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Aeolus's bag of winds is opened within sight of home, giants destroy the fleet, and Circe turns the scouts into swine until Odysseus resists her.
