# Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 6, ch. 2 With Aeolus, the Laestrygonians and on Circe's island" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Aeolus's bag of winds is opened within sight of home, giants destroy the fleet, and Circe turns the scouts into swine until Odysseus resists her.
