# Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Unit 6, ch. 2 With Aeolus, the Laestrygonians and on Circe's island — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep
b) Aeolus's bag of winds is opened within sight of home, giants destroy the fleet, and Circe turns the scouts into swine until Odysseus resists her
