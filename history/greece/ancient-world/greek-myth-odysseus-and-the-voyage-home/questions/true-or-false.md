# Greek myth odysseus and the voyage home — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes", Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit 6, ch. 2 With Aeolus, the Laestrygonians and on Circe's island", Aeolus's bag of winds is opened within sight of home, giants destroy the fleet, and Circe turns the scouts into swine until Odysseus resists her (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
