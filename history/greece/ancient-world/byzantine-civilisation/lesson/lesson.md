# Byzantine civilisation

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Byzantine civilisation" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Byzantine civilisation: letters, sciences, technology, art

A thousand years of scholarship, law, icons and domes preserve and extend the ancient inheritance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 7: Byzantine art

The dome, the icon and the gold mosaic turn the church into an image of heaven for a thousand years.

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Byzantine civilisation as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
