# Byzantine civilisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Byzantine civilisation: letters, sciences, technology, art" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A thousand years of scholarship, law, icons and domes preserve and extend the ancient inheritance.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chapter 7: Byzantine art" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The dome, the icon and the gold mosaic turn the church into an image of heaven for a thousand years.
