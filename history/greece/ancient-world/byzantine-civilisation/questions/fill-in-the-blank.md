# Byzantine civilisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Byzantine civilisation: letters, sciences, technology, art" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A thousand years of scholarship, law, icons and domes preserve and extend the ancient inheritance.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chapter 7: Byzantine art" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The dome, the icon and the gold mosaic turn the church into an image of heaven for a thousand years.
