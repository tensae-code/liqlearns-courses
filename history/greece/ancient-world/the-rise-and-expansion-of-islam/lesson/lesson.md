# The rise and expansion of islam

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The rise and expansion of islam" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 19 The Byzantines and the Arabs

The Arab armies take Syria, Egypt and the sea lanes, besiege Constantinople twice, and Greek fire saves the city.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 19 Arabs exercises

Arrows on the map trace the Arab conquests and the sieges Greek fire broke.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ch. 2.II.1 The expansion of the Arabs

Within a generation the Arabs carry Islam from Arabia to Spain and the Indus, besieging Constantinople itself.

## Sub lesson 4 — The appearance of Islam

In Arabia a new faith unites the tribes under one God and one prophet.

## Sub lesson 5 — The Arab conquests and their consequences

Within a century Arab armies hold Syria, Egypt, Persia and Spain, and the Mediterranean stops being a Roman lake.

## Sub lesson 6 — Chapter 3.2: Islam

Forbidden images turn decoration toward geometry, calligraphy and arabesque across tiles, wood and metal.
