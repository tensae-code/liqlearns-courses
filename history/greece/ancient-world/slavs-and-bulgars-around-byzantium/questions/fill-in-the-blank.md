# Slavs and bulgars around byzantium — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 2.I.1 The Slavs and their relations with Byzantium" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Slav tribes settle the Balkans to the Peloponnese and slowly become the empire's Christian neighbours.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 2.I.2 The Bulgarians and their relations with Byzantium" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A Turkic Bulgar state on the Danube becomes Byzantium's most dangerous northern rival.
