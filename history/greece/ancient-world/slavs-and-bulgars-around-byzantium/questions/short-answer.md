# Slavs and bulgars around byzantium — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 2.I.1 The Slavs and their relations with Byzantium" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Slav tribes settle the Balkans to the Peloponnese and slowly become the empire's Christian neighbours.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 2.I.2 The Bulgarians and their relations with Byzantium" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A Turkic Bulgar state on the Danube becomes Byzantium's most dangerous northern rival.
