# Greek myth creation and the olympian gods — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit 1, ch. 1 The Titanomachy", Chaos gives birth to Earth and Sky, the Titans rule, and Zeus leads the younger gods to overthrow them (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit 1, ch. 2 The creation of the world and the gods / The gods of Olympus", the twelve immortal gods settle on Olympus drinking nectar, each with a realm and symbols children learn to read in pictures (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
