# Greek myth creation and the olympian gods — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 1, ch. 1 The Titanomachy" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chaos gives birth to Earth and Sky, the Titans rule, and Zeus leads the younger gods to overthrow them.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 1, ch. 2 The creation of the world and the gods / The gods of Olympus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The twelve immortal gods settle on Olympus drinking nectar, each with a realm and symbols children learn to read in pictures.
