# Greek myth creation and the olympian gods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 1, ch. 1 The Titanomachy" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chaos gives birth to Earth and Sky, the Titans rule, and Zeus leads the younger gods to overthrow them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 1, ch. 2 The creation of the world and the gods / The gods of Olympus" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The twelve immortal gods settle on Olympus drinking nectar, each with a realm and symbols children learn to read in pictures.
