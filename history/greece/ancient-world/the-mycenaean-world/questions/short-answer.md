# The mycenaean world — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Mycenaean civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Warrior kings in citadels at Mycenae and Pylos keep palace records in Linear B, then the palaces burn and writing is lost.
