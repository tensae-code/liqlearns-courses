# Daily life in byzantium — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 11 Daily life and festivals in Byzantium" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Markets, baths, weddings and church feasts fill the Byzantine year in city and home.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 27 Daily life in the countryside in the years of the Isaurians and Macedonians" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Village farmers plough, marry, quarrel over boundaries and pay the tax-collector under the Farmer's Law.
