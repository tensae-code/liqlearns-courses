# Daily life in byzantium — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 11 Daily life and festivals in Byzantium" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Markets, baths, weddings and church feasts fill the Byzantine year in city and home.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 27 Daily life in the countryside in the years of the Isaurians and Macedonians" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Village farmers plough, marry, quarrel over boundaries and pay the tax-collector under the Farmer's Law.
