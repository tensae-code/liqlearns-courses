# Medieval western civilisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Medieval western civilisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Monasteries, cathedrals and the first universities shape a Latin Christian culture.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chapter 8: Christian art in the Middle Ages — Romanesque and Gothic" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Heavy Romanesque pilgrims' churches give way to Gothic cathedrals of light and glass.
