# Medieval western civilisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Medieval western civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Monasteries, cathedrals and the first universities shape a Latin Christian culture.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chapter 8: Christian art in the Middle Ages — Romanesque and Gothic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Heavy Romanesque pilgrims' churches give way to Gothic cathedrals of light and glass.
