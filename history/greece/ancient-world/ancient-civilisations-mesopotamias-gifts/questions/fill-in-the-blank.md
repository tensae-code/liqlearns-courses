# Ancient civilisations mesopotamias gifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Mesopotamian history and civilisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sumerians, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians rise and fall in turn, each leaving laws, astronomy and building behind.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chapter 1: Mesopotamia — dress of the peoples of Mesopotamia and their fabrics" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sumerian, Assyrian and Babylonian dress in fringed wool and linen shows rank at a glance in the first cities.
