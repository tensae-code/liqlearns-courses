# Ancient civilisations mesopotamias gifts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Mesopotamian history and civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sumerians, Akkadians, Babylonians and Assyrians rise and fall in turn, each leaving laws, astronomy and building behind.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chapter 1: Mesopotamia — dress of the peoples of Mesopotamia and their fabrics" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sumerian, Assyrian and Babylonian dress in fringed wool and linen shows rank at a glance in the first cities.
