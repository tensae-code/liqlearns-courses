# The peloponnesian war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 25 Causes and occasions of the war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sparta's alliance votes for war against the Athenian empire, and Greek begins killing Greek in a war Thucydides calls the greatest ever.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 26 The main phases of the war" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Plague, civil strife and the Sicilian disaster bleed Athens until Lysander's fleet starves her into surrender on harsh Spartan terms.
