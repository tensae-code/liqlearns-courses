# The peloponnesian war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 25 Causes and occasions of the war" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sparta's alliance votes for war against the Athenian empire, and Greek begins killing Greek in a war Thucydides calls the greatest ever.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 26 The main phases of the war" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Plague, civil strife and the Sicilian disaster bleed Athens until Lysander's fleet starves her into surrender on harsh Spartan terms.
