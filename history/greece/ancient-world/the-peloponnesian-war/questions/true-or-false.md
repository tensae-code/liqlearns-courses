# The peloponnesian war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Ch. 25 Causes and occasions of the war", Sparta's alliance votes for war against the Athenian empire, and Greek begins killing Greek in a war Thucydides calls the greatest ever (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Ch. 26 The main phases of the war", plague, civil strife and the Sicilian disaster bleed Athens until Lysander's fleet starves her into surrender on harsh Spartan terms (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
