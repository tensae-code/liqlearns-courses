# Ancient civilisations sparta and athens against sparta — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 10 Sparta: society and constitution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sparta lives off conquered land worked by helots, ruled by two kings, elders and an assembly geared for war.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 11 Sparta: life in the city" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Spartan boys are taken into barracks, eat the black broth in common messes, and grow into the ancient world's most feared soldiers.
