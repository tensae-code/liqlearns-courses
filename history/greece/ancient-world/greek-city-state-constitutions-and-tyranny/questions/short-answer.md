# Greek city state constitutions and tyranny — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 6 Constitutions in Archaic Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kings give way to aristocrats, angry farmers revolt, and tyrants like Periander of Corinth seize power promising order.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 6 Constitutions exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils define the tyrant against king and aristocrat and sort who ruled how.
