# Greek city state constitutions and tyranny — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 6 Constitutions in Archaic Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kings give way to aristocrats, angry farmers revolt, and tyrants like Periander of Corinth seize power promising order.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 6 Constitutions exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils define the tyrant against king and aristocrat and sort who ruled how.
