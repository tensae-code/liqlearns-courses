# Ancient civilisations china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "China" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** River farmers become an empire under one script and one bureaucracy, walled against the steppe.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chapter 3.1: Chinese civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lacquer, porcelain, silk and the scholar's simple furniture set a standard Europe will chase for centuries.
