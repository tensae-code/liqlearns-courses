# Ancient civilisations china — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "China" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** River farmers become an empire under one script and one bureaucracy, walled against the steppe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chapter 3.1: Chinese civilisation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lacquer, porcelain, silk and the scholar's simple furniture set a standard Europe will chase for centuries.
