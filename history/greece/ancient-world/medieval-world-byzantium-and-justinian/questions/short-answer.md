# Medieval world byzantium and justinian — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 13 Justinian reforms administration and legislation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Justinian and Tribonian gather Roman law into one code that will govern Europe for a thousand years.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 14 The Demes upset the capital with the Nika riot" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Blues and Greens unite in the cry "Nika!", burn half the city, and Theodora's refusal to flee saves Justinian's throne.
