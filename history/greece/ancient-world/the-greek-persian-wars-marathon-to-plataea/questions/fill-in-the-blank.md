# The greek persian wars marathon to plataea — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 16 The Persian danger (with the Battle of Marathon)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Darius's heralds demand earth and water; at Marathon the outnumbered Athenians charge at a run and drive the Persians to their ships.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 17 The Battle of Thermopylae" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Leonidas and his 300 hold the narrow pass against Xerxes's host until a traitor's path lets the Persians surround them, and they fight to the last with hands and teeth.
