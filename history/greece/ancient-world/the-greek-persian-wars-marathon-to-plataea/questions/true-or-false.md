# The greek persian wars marathon to plataea — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Ch. 16 The Persian danger (with the Battle of Marathon)", Darius's heralds demand earth and water; at Marathon the outnumbered Athenians charge at a run and drive the Persians to their ships (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Ch. 17 The Battle of Thermopylae", Leonidas and his 300 hold the narrow pass against Xerxes's host until a traitor's path lets the Persians surround them, and they fight to the last with hands and teeth (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
