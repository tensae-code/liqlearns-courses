# The greek persian wars marathon to plataea — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 16 The Persian danger (with the Battle of Marathon)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Darius's heralds demand earth and water; at Marathon the outnumbered Athenians charge at a run and drive the Persians to their ships.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 17 The Battle of Thermopylae" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Leonidas and his 300 hold the narrow pass against Xerxes's host until a traitor's path lets the Persians surround them, and they fight to the last with hands and teeth.
