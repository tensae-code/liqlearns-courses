# Constantinople the new rome — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 6 A new capital, Constantinople" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Constantine founds a New Rome on the Bosporus, where Europe meets Asia and the sea lanes cross.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 7 Constantinople is fortified and adorned with works of art" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Theodosian walls ring the city while forums, columns and churches dress the new capital as an empress.
