# Constantinople the new rome — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 6 A new capital, Constantinople" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Constantine founds a New Rome on the Bosporus, where Europe meets Asia and the sea lanes cross.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 7 Constantinople is fortified and adorned with works of art" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Theodosian walls ring the city while forums, columns and churches dress the new capital as an empress.
