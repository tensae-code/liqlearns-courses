# Byzantium restored michael palaiologos — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 31 The recovery of the City by Michael VIII Palaiologos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From the Nicaean exile state, Michael's general slips into Constantinople by night in 1261 and the empire is restored.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 31 Recovery of the City exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises retell the night entry of 1261 that restored the empire.
