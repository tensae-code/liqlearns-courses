# Greece geometric age dorians and early colonies — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 1 The descent of the Dorians" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After the Trojan War unrest drives the Dorians south, Mycenaean wealth is lost, and Greece sinks into the hard Geometric centuries.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 2 The Greeks create colonies" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Crowded, hungry city-states send out settlers who found new Greek cities round the Aegean while shared sanctuaries and festivals keep all Greeks feeling one people.
