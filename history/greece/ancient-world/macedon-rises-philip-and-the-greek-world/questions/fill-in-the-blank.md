# Macedon rises philip and the greek world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 30 Macedon, a new Greek power" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Philip II builds the Macedonian phalanx, defeats the southern Greeks, treats them leniently and summons the Corinth congress to unite Greece against Persia.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 30 Macedon exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises trace Philip's new phalanx and his road to mastery of Greece.
