# Macedon rises philip and the greek world — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 30 Macedon, a new Greek power" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Philip II builds the Macedonian phalanx, defeats the southern Greeks, treats them leniently and summons the Corinth congress to unite Greece against Persia.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 30 Macedon exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises trace Philip's new phalanx and his road to mastery of Greece.
