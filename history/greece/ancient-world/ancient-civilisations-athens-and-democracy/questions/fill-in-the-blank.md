# Ancient civilisations athens and democracy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 12 The old constitution of Athens" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athens slides from kings to aristocrats while poor Attic farmers fall into debt-slavery on barren soil.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 13 The laws of Solon; Peisistratus becomes tyrant" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Solon cancels debts and frees the debt-slaves but refuses to redivide the land, and Peisistratus later seizes the Acropolis as tyrant.
