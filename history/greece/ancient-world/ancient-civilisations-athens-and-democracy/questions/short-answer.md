# Ancient civilisations athens and democracy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 12 The old constitution of Athens" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athens slides from kings to aristocrats while poor Attic farmers fall into debt-slavery on barren soil.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 13 The laws of Solon; Peisistratus becomes tyrant" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Solon cancels debts and frees the debt-slaves but refuses to redivide the land, and Peisistratus later seizes the Acropolis as tyrant.
