# Trade routes that linked the old world — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 2.II.2 The trade and civilisation of Islam" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Baghdad and Cordoba link the world's trade routes and guard Greek science in Arabic dress.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Eastern peoples and the Greeks" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Eastern peoples meet the Greeks through trade, colonies and war, and each side changes the other.
