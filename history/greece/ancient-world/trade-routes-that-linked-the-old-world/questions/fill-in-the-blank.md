# Trade routes that linked the old world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 2.II.2 The trade and civilisation of Islam" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Baghdad and Cordoba link the world's trade routes and guard Greek science in Arabic dress.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Eastern peoples and the Greeks" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Eastern peoples meet the Greeks through trade, colonies and war, and each side changes the other.
