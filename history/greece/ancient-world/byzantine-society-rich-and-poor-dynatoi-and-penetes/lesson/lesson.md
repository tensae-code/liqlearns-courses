# Byzantine society rich and poor dynatoi and penetes

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Byzantine society rich and poor dynatoi and penetes" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 3.II.1 Developments in economy and society

Great estates swallow free villages while towns and trade revive.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 3.II.2 The legislation of the Macedonian dynasty and its clash with the "dynatoi"

Emperors legislate again and again to stop the powerful buying up the poor soldiers' farms.

## Sub lesson 3 — Axis 1: Rich and poor in Byzantium

Aristocratic households and labouring poor are set side by side in city and countryside through sources on dress, food, houses and work.

## Sub lesson 4 — Axis 2: The penetes, the dynatoi, the State

Emperors legislate to protect poor soldiers' farms from the powerful, lose, legislate again.
