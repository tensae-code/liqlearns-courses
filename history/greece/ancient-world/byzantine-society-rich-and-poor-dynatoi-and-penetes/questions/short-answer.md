# Byzantine society rich and poor dynatoi and penetes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 3.II.1 Developments in economy and society" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Great estates swallow free villages while towns and trade revive.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 3.II.2 The legislation of the Macedonian dynasty and its clash with the "dynatoi"" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Emperors legislate again and again to stop the powerful buying up the poor soldiers' farms.
