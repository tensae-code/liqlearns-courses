# Byzantine society rich and poor dynatoi and penetes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 3.II.1 Developments in economy and society" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Great estates swallow free villages while towns and trade revive.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 3.II.2 The legislation of the Macedonian dynasty and its clash with the "dynatoi"" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Emperors legislate again and again to stop the powerful buying up the poor soldiers' farms.
