# Lesson plan — Byzantine society rich and poor dynatoi and penetes

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Byzantine society rich and poor dynatoi and penetes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Byzantium the spine: medieval and modern history, with the empire's society of rich and poor investigated (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Byzantine society rich and poor dynatoi and penetes as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Byzantium the spine: medieval and modern history, with the empire's society of rich and poor investigated — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Ch. 3.II.1 Developments in economy and society** — great estates swallow free villages while towns and trade revive
2. **§2 Ch. 3.II.2 The legislation of the Macedonian dynasty and its clash with the "dynatoi"** — emperors legislate again and again to stop the powerful buying up the poor soldiers' farms
3. **§3 Axis 1: Rich and poor in Byzantium** — aristocratic households and labouring poor are set side by side in city and countryside through sources on dress, food, houses and work
4. **§4 Axis 2: The penetes, the dynatoi, the State** — emperors legislate to protect poor soldiers' farms from the powerful, lose, legislate again

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
