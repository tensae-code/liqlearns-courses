# The fourth crusade and 1204 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 30 The capture of Constantinople by the Franks" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Fourth Crusade, diverted by Venice and debt, storms the Christian capital in 1204 and parcels the empire among Latins.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 30 Fourth Crusade and the Frankish capture exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ordering the Crusade's diversion ends at the storming of a Christian capital.
