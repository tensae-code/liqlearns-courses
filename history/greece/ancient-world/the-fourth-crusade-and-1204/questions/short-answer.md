# The fourth crusade and 1204 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 30 The capture of Constantinople by the Franks" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Fourth Crusade, diverted by Venice and debt, storms the Christian capital in 1204 and parcels the empire among Latins.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 30 Fourth Crusade and the Frankish capture exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ordering the Crusade's diversion ends at the storming of a Christian capital.
