# The hellenistic kingdoms after alexander — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 34 Alexander's empire is divided" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Successors carve the empire into kingdoms.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 35 Pyrrhus, king of Epirus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pyrrhus wins battles against Rome at such cost that "Pyrrhic victory" enters the language, and his dream dies with his third defeat.
