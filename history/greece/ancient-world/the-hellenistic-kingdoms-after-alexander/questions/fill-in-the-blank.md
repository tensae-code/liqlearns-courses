# The hellenistic kingdoms after alexander — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 34 Alexander's empire is divided" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Successors carve the empire into kingdoms.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 35 Pyrrhus, king of Epirus" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pyrrhus wins battles against Rome at such cost that "Pyrrhic victory" enters the language, and his dream dies with his third defeat.
