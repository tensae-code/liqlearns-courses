# Akrotiri the buried city of thera

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Akrotiri the buried city of thera" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## The story

A Bronze Age town preserved by volcanic ash like a Greek Pompeii is excavated street by street under a protective shelter.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Akrotiri the buried city of thera as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's story in your own words.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the story happens in real life. Note it.
