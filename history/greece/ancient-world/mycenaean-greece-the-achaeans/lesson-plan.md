# Lesson plan — Mycenaean greece the achaeans

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Mycenaean greece the achaeans
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myth first, then the first farmers: the gods and heroes, prehistory, and the Aegean civilisations, as Greece's youngest readers meet the past (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unit 10, ch. 1 Achaeans, the first Greeks** — the Achaeans settle Greece and build warrior kingdoms; their king at Mycenae leads the very expedition to Troy the myths described
2. **§2 Unit 10, ch. 2–3 Trade with Cyprus and the acropolis of Mycenae** — Mycenaean merchants sail to Cyprus for its copper, while at home the citadel of Mycenae crowns its hill with palace and massive walls
3. **§3 Unit 10, ch. 4 The daily life of the Mycenaeans** — Mycenaean ships trade across the Mediterranean while warriors, farmers and craftsmen live under the palace's scribes
4. **§4 Unit 10, ch. 5 The religion and writing of the Mycenaeans** — Mycenaeans worship the Great Goddess and the later Olympians, write Greek in Linear B, and bury kings in tholos tombs with gold masks like "Agamemnon's"
5. **§5 Unit 10, ch. 6 The art of the Mycenaeans** — taught by Minoan art, Mycenaean craftsmen work gold, crystal and ivory into vases and weapons for their warrior kings
6. **§6 Unit 10, ch. 1 Achaeans exercises** — exercises open the Mycenaean unit with the arrival of the first Greeks
7. **§7 Unit 10, ch. 3 Acropolis of Mycenae exercises** — pupils read the citadel's walls and gate as a fortress built for war
8. **§8 Unit 10, ch. 4 Daily life exercises** — a trade table of what Mycenaeans bought and sold maps their sea business
9. **§9 Unit 10, ch. 5 Religion and writing exercises** — pupils imagine a Mycenaean explaining Linear B
10. **§10 Unit 10, ch. 6 Art of the Mycenaeans exercises** — listing objects by material shows the range of Mycenaean craft in gold, bronze and crystal
11. **§11 Ch. B5 The Mycenaean world** — warrior kings in citadels from Mycenae to Pylos trade and raid across the Mediterranean and keep palace accounts in Linear B
12. **§12 Ch. B6 Mycenaean religion and art** — gold masks, tholos tombs and frescoed palaces bury and celebrate the Mycenaean kings
