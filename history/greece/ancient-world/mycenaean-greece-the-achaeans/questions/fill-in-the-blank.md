# Mycenaean greece the achaeans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 10, ch. 1 Achaeans, the first Greeks" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Achaeans settle Greece and build warrior kingdoms; their king at Mycenae leads the very expedition to Troy the myths described.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 10, ch. 2–3 Trade with Cyprus and the acropolis of Mycenae" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mycenaean merchants sail to Cyprus for its copper, while at home the citadel of Mycenae crowns its hill with palace and massive walls.
