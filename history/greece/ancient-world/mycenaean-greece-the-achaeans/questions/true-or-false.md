# Mycenaean greece the achaeans — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit 10, ch. 1 Achaeans, the first Greeks", the Achaeans settle Greece and build warrior kingdoms; their king at Mycenae leads the very expedition to Troy the myths described (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit 10, ch. 2–3 Trade with Cyprus and the acropolis of Mycenae", Mycenaean merchants sail to Cyprus for its copper, while at home the citadel of Mycenae crowns its hill with palace and massive walls (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
