# Mycenaean greece the achaeans — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 10, ch. 1 Achaeans, the first Greeks" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Achaeans settle Greece and build warrior kingdoms; their king at Mycenae leads the very expedition to Troy the myths described.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 10, ch. 2–3 Trade with Cyprus and the acropolis of Mycenae" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mycenaean merchants sail to Cyprus for its copper, while at home the citadel of Mycenae crowns its hill with palace and massive walls.
