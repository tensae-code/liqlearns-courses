# Iconoclasm byzantium divided over icons — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 24 The Iconoclasm crisis divides the Byzantines" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Emperors ban and smash the icons, monks and people resist, and for over a century Byzantium argues with itself over a picture.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 24 Iconoclasm exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils sort Leo III's acts between war, reform and the war on icons.
