# Iconoclasm byzantium divided over icons — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 24 The Iconoclasm crisis divides the Byzantines" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Emperors ban and smash the icons, monks and people resist, and for over a century Byzantium argues with itself over a picture.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 24 Iconoclasm exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils sort Leo III's acts between war, reform and the war on icons.
