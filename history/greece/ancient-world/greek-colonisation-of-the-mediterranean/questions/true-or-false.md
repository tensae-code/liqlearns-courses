# Greek colonisation of the mediterranean — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Ch. 5 New colonies of the Greeks", weeping settlers take the sacred fire from home and found the rich cities of Magna Graecia, which soon mint their own silver coins (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Ch. D1 Colonial expansion", land hunger and trade send apoikiai from the Black Sea to Spain, planting Greek cities round two seas (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
