# Greek colonisation of the mediterranean — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 5 New colonies of the Greeks" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Weeping settlers take the sacred fire from home and found the rich cities of Magna Graecia, which soon mint their own silver coins.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. D1 Colonial expansion" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Land hunger and trade send apoikiai from the Black Sea to Spain, planting Greek cities round two seas.
