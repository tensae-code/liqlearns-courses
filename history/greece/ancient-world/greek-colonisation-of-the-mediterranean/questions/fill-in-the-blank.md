# Greek colonisation of the mediterranean — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 5 New colonies of the Greeks" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Weeping settlers take the sacred fire from home and found the rich cities of Magna Graecia, which soon mint their own silver coins.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. D1 Colonial expansion" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Land hunger and trade send apoikiai from the Black Sea to Spain, planting Greek cities round two seas.
