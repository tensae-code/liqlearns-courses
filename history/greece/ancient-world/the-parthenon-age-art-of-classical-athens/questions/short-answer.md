# The parthenon age art of classical athens — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 23 The "golden age" of art" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Phidias and the great sculptors raise the Parthenon and its gold-and-ivory Athena over a city spending League silver on beauty.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 23 Golden age of art exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises match the Parthenon-age sculptors to their works.
