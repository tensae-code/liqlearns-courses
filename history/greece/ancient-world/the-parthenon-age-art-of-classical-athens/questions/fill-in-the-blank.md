# The parthenon age art of classical athens — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 23 The "golden age" of art" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Phidias and the great sculptors raise the Parthenon and its gold-and-ivory Athena over a city spending League silver on beauty.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 23 Golden age of art exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises match the Parthenon-age sculptors to their works.
