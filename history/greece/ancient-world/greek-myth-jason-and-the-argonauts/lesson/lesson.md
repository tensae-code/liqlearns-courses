# Greek myth jason and the argonauts

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greek myth jason and the argonauts" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos, Helle and the golden-fleeced ram

The flying ram rescues Phrixos and Helle, Helle falls into the strait named Hellespont after her, and the ram's golden fleece ends up guarded in Colchis.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 4, ch. 2 Pelias and Jason

Pelias, who stole Iolcus from Jason's father, is warned of a one-sandalled man; Jason arrives with one sandal and is sent to fetch the fleece to win back the throne.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 4, ch. 3 The Argonaut expedition

The Argo's crew of heroes squeezes through the clashing Symplegades rocks, Jason yokes fire-breathing bulls and wins the fleece with Medea's help.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 4, ch. 4 Talos, the first robot?

On the wandering voyage home the Argonauts meet Talos, the bronze giant guarding Crete, whose single vein of ichor is his undoing.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos and Helle exercises

Pupils connect the Hellespont's name to Helle's fall.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit 4, ch. 2 Pelias and Jason exercises

Pupils build their own Argo in paper craft, entering the expedition through its ship.

## Sub lesson 7 — Unit 4, ch. 3 Argonaut expedition exercises

Reading work follows the Argo through the clashing rocks to Colchis and back.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Greek myth jason and the argonauts as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
