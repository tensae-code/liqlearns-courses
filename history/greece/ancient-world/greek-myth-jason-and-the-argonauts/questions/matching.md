# Greek myth jason and the argonauts — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos, Helle and the golden-fleeced ram — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Unit 4, ch. 2 Pelias and Jason — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) the flying ram rescues Phrixos and Helle, Helle falls into the strait named Hellespont after her, and the ram's golden fleece ends up guarded in Colchis
b) Pelias, who stole Iolcus from Jason's father, is warned of a one-sandalled man; Jason arrives with one sandal and is sent to fetch the fleece to win back the throne

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
