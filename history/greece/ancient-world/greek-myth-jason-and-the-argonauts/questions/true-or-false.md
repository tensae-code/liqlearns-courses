# Greek myth jason and the argonauts — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos, Helle and the golden-fleeced ram", the flying ram rescues Phrixos and Helle, Helle falls into the strait named Hellespont after her, and the ram's golden fleece ends up guarded in Colchis (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit 4, ch. 2 Pelias and Jason", Pelias, who stole Iolcus from Jason's father, is warned of a one-sandalled man; Jason arrives with one sandal and is sent to fetch the fleece to win back the throne (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
