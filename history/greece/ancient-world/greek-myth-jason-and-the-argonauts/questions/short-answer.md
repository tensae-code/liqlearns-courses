# Greek myth jason and the argonauts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos, Helle and the golden-fleeced ram" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The flying ram rescues Phrixos and Helle, Helle falls into the strait named Hellespont after her, and the ram's golden fleece ends up guarded in Colchis.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 4, ch. 2 Pelias and Jason" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pelias, who stole Iolcus from Jason's father, is warned of a one-sandalled man; Jason arrives with one sandal and is sent to fetch the fleece to win back the throne.
