# Lesson plan — Athens golden age daily life and education

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Athens golden age daily life and education
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The ancient world in one sweep: Dorians to Alexander, Athens and Sparta, the Persian Wars, and the road to Rome (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Athens golden age daily life and education as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The ancient world in one sweep: Dorians to Alexander, Athens and Sparta, the Persian Wars, and the road to Rome — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Ch. 22 The daily life and education of the Athenians** — Athenian children learn letters, music and wrestling, women run the household, and games fill the courtyards of the golden-age city
2. **§2 Ch. 22 Daily life and education exercises** — vase scenes are mined for how Athenian children were schooled and played
3. **§3 Ch. E4 The composition of society — daily life** — citizens, metics, slaves and women live four different Athens inside one city
4. **§4 Ch. E5 The process of education — the Athenian and work — Athens celebrates** — school, palaestra and festivals shape the Athenian from boyhood to the Panathenaea
