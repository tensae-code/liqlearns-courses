# Athens golden age daily life and education — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 22 The daily life and education of the Athenians" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athenian children learn letters, music and wrestling, women run the household, and games fill the courtyards of the golden-age city.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 22 Daily life and education exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Vase scenes are mined for how Athenian children were schooled and played.
