# Athens golden age daily life and education — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 22 The daily life and education of the Athenians" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athenian children learn letters, music and wrestling, women run the household, and games fill the courtyards of the golden-age city.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 22 Daily life and education exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Vase scenes are mined for how Athenian children were schooled and played.
