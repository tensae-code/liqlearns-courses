# Byzantium declines internal crisis and new enemies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 28 The state faces great internal problems" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Basil II, weak emperors, court factions and a debased coinage hollow out the empire from inside.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 29 New enemies appear and tear away imperial lands" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Seljuk Turks take Anatolia after Manzikert while Normans seize the Italian provinces.
