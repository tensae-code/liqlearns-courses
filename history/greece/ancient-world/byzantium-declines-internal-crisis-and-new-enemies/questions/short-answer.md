# Byzantium declines internal crisis and new enemies — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 28 The state faces great internal problems" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Basil II, weak emperors, court factions and a debased coinage hollow out the empire from inside.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 29 New enemies appear and tear away imperial lands" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Seljuk Turks take Anatolia after Manzikert while Normans seize the Italian provinces.
