# Alexander the great conquers the persian empire

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Alexander the great conquers the persian empire" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 31 Alexander takes Asia Minor and Egypt

Young Alexander crosses into Asia, frees the Greek cities, defeats Darius's armies and is welcomed in Egypt, founding cities as he goes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 32 The march to India; the end of the campaign

At the Indus Alexander meets his great disappointment.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ch. 33 The death of Alexander

Alexander dies at Babylon leaving sixteen cities named Alexandria and an empire glittering with new buildings and no heir.

## Sub lesson 4 — Ch. 31 Alexander takes Asia Minor and Egypt exercises

Map work follows Alexander's first great victories and liberations.

## Sub lesson 5 — Ch. 32 March to India exercises

Exercises end the campaign where the soldiers ended it.

## Sub lesson 6 — Ch. 33 Death of Alexander exercises

True/false on Alexander's death and the cities he left behind.

## Sub lesson 7 — Revision lesson, Classical years

A crossword gathers Persian Wars, golden age, Peloponnesian War and Alexander into one Classical whole.

## Sub lesson 8 — Ch. G3 Alexander: the conquest of the East

Granicus, Issus and Gaugamela in seven years unmake the Persian Empire, and Egypt crowns its liberator pharaoh.

## Sub lesson 9 — Ch. G4 The work of Alexander

Cities, trade roads and a common Greek tongue bind East and West into one world after the conqueror's early death.

## Say it to your companion

Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
