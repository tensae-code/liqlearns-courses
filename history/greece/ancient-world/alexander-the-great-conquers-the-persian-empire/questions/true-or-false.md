# Alexander the great conquers the persian empire — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Ch. 31 Alexander takes Asia Minor and Egypt", young Alexander crosses into Asia, frees the Greek cities, defeats Darius's armies and is welcomed in Egypt, founding cities as he goes (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Ch. 32 The march to India; the end of the campaign", at the Indus Alexander meets his great disappointment (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
