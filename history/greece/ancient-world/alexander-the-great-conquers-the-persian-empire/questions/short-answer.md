# Alexander the great conquers the persian empire — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 31 Alexander takes Asia Minor and Egypt" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Young Alexander crosses into Asia, frees the Greek cities, defeats Darius's armies and is welcomed in Egypt, founding cities as he goes.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 32 The march to India; the end of the campaign" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** At the Indus Alexander meets his great disappointment.
