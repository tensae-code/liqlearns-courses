# Alexander the great conquers the persian empire — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 31 Alexander takes Asia Minor and Egypt" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Young Alexander crosses into Asia, frees the Greek cities, defeats Darius's armies and is welcomed in Egypt, founding cities as he goes.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 32 The march to India; the end of the campaign" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** At the Indus Alexander meets his great disappointment.
