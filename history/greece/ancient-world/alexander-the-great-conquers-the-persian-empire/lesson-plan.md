# Lesson plan — Alexander the great conquers the persian empire

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Alexander the great conquers the persian empire
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The ancient world in one sweep: Dorians to Alexander, Athens and Sparta, the Persian Wars, and the road to Rome (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Alexander the great conquers the persian empire as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The ancient world in one sweep: Dorians to Alexander, Athens and Sparta, the Persian Wars, and the road to Rome — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Ch. 31 Alexander takes Asia Minor and Egypt** — young Alexander crosses into Asia, frees the Greek cities, defeats Darius's armies and is welcomed in Egypt, founding cities as he goes
2. **§2 Ch. 32 The march to India; the end of the campaign** — at the Indus Alexander meets his great disappointment
3. **§3 Ch. 33 The death of Alexander** — Alexander dies at Babylon leaving sixteen cities named Alexandria and an empire glittering with new buildings and no heir
4. **§4 Ch. 31 Alexander takes Asia Minor and Egypt exercises** — map work follows Alexander's first great victories and liberations
5. **§5 Ch. 32 March to India exercises** — exercises end the campaign where the soldiers ended it
6. **§6 Ch. 33 Death of Alexander exercises** — true/false on Alexander's death and the cities he left behind
7. **§7 Revision lesson, Classical years** — a crossword gathers Persian Wars, golden age, Peloponnesian War and Alexander into one Classical whole
8. **§8 Ch. G3 Alexander: the conquest of the East** — Granicus, Issus and Gaugamela in seven years unmake the Persian Empire, and Egypt crowns its liberator pharaoh
9. **§9 Ch. G4 The work of Alexander** — cities, trade roads and a common Greek tongue bind East and West into one world after the conqueror's early death

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
