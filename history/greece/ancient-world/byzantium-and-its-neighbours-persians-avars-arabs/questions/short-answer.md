# Byzantium and its neighbours persians avars arabs — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 17 The neighbours of the Byzantines" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Persians, Avars, Slavs, Arabs, Bulgars and Rus ring the empire.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 18 The Persians and Avars ally against Byzantium" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** While Heraclius campaigns in Persia, Avars and Persians meet under Constantinople's walls in 626.
