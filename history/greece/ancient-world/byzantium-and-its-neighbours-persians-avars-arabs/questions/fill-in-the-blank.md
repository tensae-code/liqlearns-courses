# Byzantium and its neighbours persians avars arabs — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 17 The neighbours of the Byzantines" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Persians, Avars, Slavs, Arabs, Bulgars and Rus ring the empire.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 18 The Persians and Avars ally against Byzantium" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** While Heraclius campaigns in Persia, Avars and Persians meet under Constantinople's walls in 626.
