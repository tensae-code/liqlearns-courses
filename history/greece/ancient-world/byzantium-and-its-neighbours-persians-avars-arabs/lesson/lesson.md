# Byzantium and its neighbours persians avars arabs

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Byzantium and its neighbours persians avars arabs" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 17 The neighbours of the Byzantines

Persians, Avars, Slavs, Arabs, Bulgars and Rus ring the empire.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 18 The Persians and Avars ally against Byzantium

While Heraclius campaigns in Persia, Avars and Persians meet under Constantinople's walls in 626.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 17 Neighbours exercises

Pupils locate every people ringing Byzantium on the student book's map.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 18 Persians and Avars exercises

Exercises retell the 626 siege, when two enemies met under the walls.

## Sub lesson 5 — Revision of Units 17–22 (with the reception of ambassadors)

Pupils imagine themselves foreign envoys received at the palace.
