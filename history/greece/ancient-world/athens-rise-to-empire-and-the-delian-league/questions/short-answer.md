# Athens rise to empire and the delian league — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 20 Athens becomes the strongest city" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athens leads the Delian League against Persia, its treasury and fleet grow, and general Pausanias falls accused of Medism and treason.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 20 Athens strongest city exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises follow the Delian League's treasury and fleet as Athens turns leader into ruler.
