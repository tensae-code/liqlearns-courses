# Athens rise to empire and the delian league — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 20 Athens becomes the strongest city" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athens leads the Delian League against Persia, its treasury and fleet grow, and general Pausanias falls accused of Medism and treason.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 20 Athens strongest city exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises follow the Delian League's treasury and fleet as Athens turns leader into ruler.
