# Herodotus croesus solon and the fall of lydia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Book I, Unit 2 Croesus and Solon; the novella of Adrastus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Solon warns the richest king to call no man happy before his end; Croesus, deaf to it, loses his son Atys to Adrastus's spear.
