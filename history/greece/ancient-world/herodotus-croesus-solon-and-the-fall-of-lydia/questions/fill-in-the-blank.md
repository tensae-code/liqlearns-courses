# Herodotus croesus solon and the fall of lydia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Book I, Unit 2 Croesus and Solon; the novella of Adrastus" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Solon warns the richest king to call no man happy before his end; Croesus, deaf to it, loses his son Atys to Adrastus's spear.
