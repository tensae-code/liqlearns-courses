## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 9, ch. 1 Minoan Crete

King Minos's Crete grows rich as the oldest sea-power of the Aegean, trading from unwalled palace towns.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 9, ch. 2 The palace of Knossos

The labyrinthine palace of Knossos rises with throne room, storerooms and plumbing.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 9, ch. 3 The daily life of the Minoans

Cheerful, nature-loving Minoans farm, harvest grapes, dress brightly and enjoy games and festivals.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 9, ch. 4 The religion and writing of the Minoans

Minoans worship the Great Goddess in cave shrines and spring festivals, leap over bulls, and keep records in scripts we still cannot fully read.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 9, ch. 5 The art of the Minoans

Minoan potters and painters fill palaces with dolphins, lilies and sea life in frescoes and fine vases.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit 9, ch. 6 The eruption of the Thera volcano

In one night the Thera volcano explodes, shaking sleepers from their beds and burying the island town of Akrotiri.
