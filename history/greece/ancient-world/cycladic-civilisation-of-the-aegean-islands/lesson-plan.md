# Lesson plan — Cycladic civilisation of the aegean islands

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Cycladic civilisation of the aegean islands
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myth first, then the first farmers: the gods and heroes, prehistory, and the Aegean civilisations, as Greece's youngest readers meet the past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Cycladic civilisation of the aegean islands as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Myth first, then the first farmers: the gods and heroes, prehistory, and the Aegean civilisations, as Greece's youngest readers meet the past — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unit 8, ch. 1 The life of the Cycladians** — five thousand years ago islanders of the Cyclades sail, fish and trade obsidian across the Aegean from small coastal settlements
2. **§2 Unit 8, ch. 2 The art of the Cycladians** — Cycladic craftsmen carve the famous marble figurines and paint frescoes like the Thera fishermen and boxing boys
3. **§3 Unit 8, ch. 1 Life of the Cycladians exercises** — exercises open the Bronze Age part of the book with the islanders' sea life
4. **§4 Unit 8, ch. 2 Art of the Cycladians exercises** — close picture-reading of Cycladic figurines and frescoes as evidence of island life
5. **§5 Ch. B2 The Cycladic Civilisation** — Bronze Age islanders carve marble figures and sail obsidian across the Aegean centuries before the palaces

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
