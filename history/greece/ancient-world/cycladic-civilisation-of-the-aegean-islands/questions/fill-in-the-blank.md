# Cycladic civilisation of the aegean islands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 8, ch. 1 The life of the Cycladians" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Five thousand years ago islanders of the Cyclades sail, fish and trade obsidian across the Aegean from small coastal settlements.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 8, ch. 2 The art of the Cycladians" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cycladic craftsmen carve the famous marble figurines and paint frescoes like the Thera fishermen and boxing boys.
