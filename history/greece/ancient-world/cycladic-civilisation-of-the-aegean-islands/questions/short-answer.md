# Cycladic civilisation of the aegean islands — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 8, ch. 1 The life of the Cycladians" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Five thousand years ago islanders of the Cyclades sail, fish and trade obsidian across the Aegean from small coastal settlements.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 8, ch. 2 The art of the Cycladians" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Cycladic craftsmen carve the famous marble figurines and paint frescoes like the Thera fishermen and boxing boys.
