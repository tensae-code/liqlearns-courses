# Lesson plan — Ancient civilisations rome

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Ancient civilisations rome
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The ancient world in one sweep: Dorians to Alexander, Athens and Sparta, the Persian Wars, and the road to Rome (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Ancient civilisations rome as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The ancient world in one sweep: Dorians to Alexander, Athens and Sparta, the Persian Wars, and the road to Rome — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Ch. 38 The Roman state** — Rome grows from city to Italian power while its myths tell of heroes who founded her
2. **§2 Ch. 40 The subjugation of the Greek world** — Macedon falls at Pydna in 168 BC and becomes a Roman province; southern Greece follows, and the free Greek world ends
3. **§3 Ch. 38 Roman state exercises** — exercises build Rome's rise as Greece's western neighbour turns great power
4. **§4 Ch. 40 Subjugation of the Greek world exercises** — events put in order end the story
5. **§5 Revision lesson, Hellenistic years** — true/false revision closes the ancient story with Rome master of the Greek world
6. **§6 Unit 1 The Romans rule the Greeks** — Rome governs conquered Greece, and the subject Greeks keep their language and cities under Roman order
7. **§7 Unit 3 The Roman Empire, a superpower of the ancient world** — one empire rings the Mediterranean with roads, armies and law, too big for one city to rule
8. **§8 Unit 1 Romans rule Greeks exercises** — matching Roman acts to their results rebuilds the conquest of Greece
9. **§9 Unit 3 Roman Empire superpower exercises** — map and source work measures the empire that ringed the Mediterranean
10. **§10 Ch. I3 The Roman state gains great power** — Rome unifies Italy and beats Carthage, turning east just as the Greek leagues quarrel
11. **§11 Ch. I4 The subjugation of the Greek world** — Cynoscephalae, Pydna and the sack of Corinth in 146 BC end Greek independence
12. **§12 Ch. J1 The Roman Peace** — Augustus's Pax Romana gives the Greek East two centuries of roads, cities and quiet
13. **§13 The Greeks of the West** — Greek colonists in Sicily and Italy build rich cities; Syracuse becomes the great power of the western Greeks
14. **§14 The peoples of Italy and the forming of the Roman state** — Etruscans, Latins and others share Italy; Rome is founded, throws out its kings and builds the Republic
15. **§15 The Empire at its peak: Augustus and his successors (27 BC–AD 193)** — Augustus ends the civil wars and refounds Rome as an empire; two centuries of peace let Greek and Roman culture fuse
16. **§16 Chapter 5: Rome — general character, women's and men's dress, fabrics and accessories** — the toga makes the citizen visible; its folds are a constitution worn on the body, while silk arrives from the far East
17. **§17 Chapter 1.3: Ancient Rome — the Roman private house, the minor arts and the Fayum portraits** — the domus with its atrium and wall painting shows Rome living privately, while Fayum's painted faces look out from Egypt's graves
18. **§18 Chapter 6: Roman art** — concrete vaults, triumphal arches and truthful portraits put art at the empire's service
