# Ancient civilisations rome — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 38 The Roman state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rome grows from city to Italian power while its myths tell of heroes who founded her.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 40 The subjugation of the Greek world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Macedon falls at Pydna in 168 BC and becomes a Roman province; southern Greece follows, and the free Greek world ends.
