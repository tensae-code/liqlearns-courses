# Ancient civilisations rome — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 38 The Roman state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Rome grows from city to Italian power while its myths tell of heroes who founded her.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 40 The subjugation of the Greek world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Macedon falls at Pydna in 168 BC and becomes a Roman province; southern Greece follows, and the free Greek world ends.
