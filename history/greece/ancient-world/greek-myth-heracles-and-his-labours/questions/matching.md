# Greek myth heracles and his labours — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unit 2, ch. 1 The birth of Heracles — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Unit 2, ch. 2 The Nemean lion — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) baby Heracles, son of Alcmene, strangles the two snakes Hera sends to his cradle, and the frightened household sees the hero he will become
b) sent to serve Eurystheus, Heracles wrestles the unpierceable lion bare-handed and comes back wearing its skin, terrifying the king into a jar

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
