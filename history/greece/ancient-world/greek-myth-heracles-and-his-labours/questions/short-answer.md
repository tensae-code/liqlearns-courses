# Greek myth heracles and his labours — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, ch. 1 The birth of Heracles" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Baby Heracles, son of Alcmene, strangles the two snakes Hera sends to his cradle, and the frightened household sees the hero he will become.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 2, ch. 2 The Nemean lion" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sent to serve Eurystheus, Heracles wrestles the unpierceable lion bare-handed and comes back wearing its skin, terrifying the king into a jar.
