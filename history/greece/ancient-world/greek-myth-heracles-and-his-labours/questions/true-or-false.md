# Greek myth heracles and his labours — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit 2, ch. 1 The birth of Heracles", baby Heracles, son of Alcmene, strangles the two snakes Hera sends to his cradle, and the frightened household sees the hero he will become (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit 2, ch. 2 The Nemean lion", sent to serve Eurystheus, Heracles wrestles the unpierceable lion bare-handed and comes back wearing its skin, terrifying the king into a jar (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
