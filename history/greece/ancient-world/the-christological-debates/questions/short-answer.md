# The christological debates — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The significance of Christ's person in a rapidly changing world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Arius asks who Christ really is; Nicaea and the councils answer, and the arguments split churches that still exist.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Christological discussions continue amid unexpected developments" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ephesus and Chalcedon refine the answer as empires shake; some eastern churches go their own way for good.
