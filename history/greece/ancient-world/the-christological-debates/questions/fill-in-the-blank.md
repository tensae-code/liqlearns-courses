# The christological debates — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The significance of Christ's person in a rapidly changing world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Arius asks who Christ really is; Nicaea and the councils answer, and the arguments split churches that still exist.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Christological discussions continue amid unexpected developments" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ephesus and Chalcedon refine the answer as empires shake; some eastern churches go their own way for good.
