# Greek hegemonies sparta thebes and the road to macedon — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 27 The hegemony of Sparta" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Victorious Sparta rules Greece harshly yet Agesilaus shows mercy to the conquered, and the cities soon combine against her.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 28 Thebes and the Boeotian League" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Thebes builds a Boeotian federation with its own coinage and rises to challenge Sparta's hold on Greece.
