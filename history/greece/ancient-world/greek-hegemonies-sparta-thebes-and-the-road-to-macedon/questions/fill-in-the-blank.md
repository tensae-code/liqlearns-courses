# Greek hegemonies sparta thebes and the road to macedon — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 27 The hegemony of Sparta" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Victorious Sparta rules Greece harshly yet Agesilaus shows mercy to the conquered, and the cities soon combine against her.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 28 Thebes and the Boeotian League" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Thebes builds a Boeotian federation with its own coinage and rises to challenge Sparta's hold on Greece.
