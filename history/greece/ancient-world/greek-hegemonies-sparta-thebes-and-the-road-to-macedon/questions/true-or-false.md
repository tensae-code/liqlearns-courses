# Greek hegemonies sparta thebes and the road to macedon — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Ch. 27 The hegemony of Sparta", victorious Sparta rules Greece harshly yet Agesilaus shows mercy to the conquered, and the cities soon combine against her (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Ch. 28 Thebes and the Boeotian League", Thebes builds a Boeotian federation with its own coinage and rises to challenge Sparta's hold on Greece (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
