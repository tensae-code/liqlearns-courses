# Lesson plan — Greek hegemonies sparta thebes and the road to macedon

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Greek hegemonies sparta thebes and the road to macedon
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The ancient world in one sweep: Dorians to Alexander, Athens and Sparta, the Persian Wars, and the road to Rome (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Greek hegemonies sparta thebes and the road to macedon as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Ch. 27 The hegemony of Sparta** — victorious Sparta rules Greece harshly yet Agesilaus shows mercy to the conquered, and the cities soon combine against her
2. **§2 Ch. 28 Thebes and the Boeotian League** — Thebes builds a Boeotian federation with its own coinage and rises to challenge Sparta's hold on Greece
3. **§3 Ch. 29 The hegemony of Thebes** — Epaminondas's slanting phalanx and the Sacred Band smash Sparta at Leuctra and free Messenia, making Thebes briefly first in Greece
4. **§4 Ch. 27 Hegemony of Sparta exercises** — names sorted into columns place victors and victims of Sparta's harsh decade
5. **§5 Ch. 28 Thebes and the Boeotian League exercises** — exercises build Thebes's federation as Sparta's challenger
6. **§6 Ch. 29 Hegemony of Thebes exercises** — marking tasks follow Epaminondas's Leuctra revolution in tactics
7. **§7 Ch. F3 The hegemony of Sparta: a contested dominion** — Sparta's harmosts and garrisons make her empire hated within a decade of winning it
8. **§8 Ch. F4 The dominion of Thebes in Greece** — Pelopidas and Epaminondas free Thebes, shatter Sparta at Leuctra and Mantinea, and die leaving no successor system
