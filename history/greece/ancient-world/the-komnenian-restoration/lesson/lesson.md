# The komnenian restoration

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The komnenian restoration" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Komnenoi: internal policy (1081–1185)

Alexios I saves a collapsing empire and his dynasty rules through family, ceremony and diplomacy.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Komnenoi's eastern policy: Islam spreads, Asia Minor is lost

Emperors campaign east but the Turkish emirates keep the Anatolian heartland.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Komnenoi's Italian and Balkan policy

Venice buys trade privileges with its fleet, Normans invade, and Balkan peoples test the empire.
