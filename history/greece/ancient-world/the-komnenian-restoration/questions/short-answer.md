# The komnenian restoration — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Komnenoi: internal policy (1081–1185)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alexios I saves a collapsing empire and his dynasty rules through family, ceremony and diplomacy.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Komnenoi's eastern policy: Islam spreads, Asia Minor is lost" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Emperors campaign east but the Turkish emirates keep the Anatolian heartland.
