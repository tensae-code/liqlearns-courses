# The komnenian restoration — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Komnenoi: internal policy (1081–1185)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alexios I saves a collapsing empire and his dynasty rules through family, ceremony and diplomacy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Komnenoi's eastern policy: Islam spreads, Asia Minor is lost" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Emperors campaign east but the Turkish emirates keep the Anatolian heartland.
