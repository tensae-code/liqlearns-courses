# Byzantine letters and the greek classics — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 26 The growth of letters and the study of the Greek classics" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Photius reads everything, copyists rescue Homer and Plato in new minuscule script, and Constantinople keeps Greek literature alive.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 26 Letters and classics exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
