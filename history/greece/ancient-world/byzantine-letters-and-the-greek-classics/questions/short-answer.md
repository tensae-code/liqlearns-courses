# Byzantine letters and the greek classics — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 26 The growth of letters and the study of the Greek classics" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Photius reads everything, copyists rescue Homer and Plato in new minuscule script, and Constantinople keeps Greek literature alive.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 26 Letters and classics exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Matching copyists and scholars to the ancient books they saved.
