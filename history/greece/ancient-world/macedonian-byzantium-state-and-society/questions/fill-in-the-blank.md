# Macedonian byzantium state and society — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Prelude to the Byzantine state's peak (843–867)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Iconoclasm ends, the Macedonian dynasty takes over, and the empire turns from defence to expansion.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Economy, society, administration and legislation at the peak" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trade and silk enrich Constantinople while emperors legislate to protect small farmers from the powerful.
