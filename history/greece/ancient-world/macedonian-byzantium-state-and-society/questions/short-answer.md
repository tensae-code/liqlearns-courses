# Macedonian byzantium state and society — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Prelude to the Byzantine state's peak (843–867)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Iconoclasm ends, the Macedonian dynasty takes over, and the empire turns from defence to expansion.

**2.** Tell the story of "Economy, society, administration and legislation at the peak" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trade and silk enrich Constantinople while emperors legislate to protect small farmers from the powerful.
