# Greek poetry religion and the alphabet — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Ch. 3 The poetry and religion of the Greeks", Homer's epics and the Olympian gods give scattered Greeks one language of story and worship, with hospitality to strangers as a sacred duty (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Ch. 4 Art and writing", Greeks borrow the Phoenician alphabet, add vowels, and paint geometric vases whose shapes serve every use of daily life (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
