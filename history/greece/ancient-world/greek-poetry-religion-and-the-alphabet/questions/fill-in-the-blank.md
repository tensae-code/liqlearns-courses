# Greek poetry religion and the alphabet — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 3 The poetry and religion of the Greeks" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Homer's epics and the Olympian gods give scattered Greeks one language of story and worship, with hospitality to strangers as a sacred duty.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 4 Art and writing" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Greeks borrow the Phoenician alphabet, add vowels, and paint geometric vases whose shapes serve every use of daily life.
