# Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 15 The Persian state and the Greeks of Asia Minor" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Darius rules a vast Persian empire whose Greek subjects in Asia Minor chafe.

**2.** Tell the story of "Book I, Unit 3 Croesus and Cyrus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Croesus misreads the oracle, crosses the Halys, and is saved on his own pyre by Cyrus, who then takes Sardis and founds Persian greatness.
