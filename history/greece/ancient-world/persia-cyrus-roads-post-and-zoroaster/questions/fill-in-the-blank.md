# Persia cyrus roads post and zoroaster — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 15 The Persian state and the Greeks of Asia Minor" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Darius rules a vast Persian empire whose Greek subjects in Asia Minor chafe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Book I, Unit 3 Croesus and Cyrus" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Croesus misreads the oracle, crosses the Halys, and is saved on his own pyre by Cyrus, who then takes Sardis and founds Persian greatness.
