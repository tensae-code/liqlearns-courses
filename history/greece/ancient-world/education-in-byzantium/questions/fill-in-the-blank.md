# Education in byzantium — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 12 Education in Byzantium" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 41 Education in Byzantium (topics)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
