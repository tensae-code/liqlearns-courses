# Education in byzantium — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 12 Education in Byzantium" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Byzantine children learn letters from Homer and the Psalms, and the best go on to rhetoric and law.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 41 Education in Byzantium (topics)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Schools, the university of Constantinople and monastic copyists keep learning alive through the empire's darkest centuries.
