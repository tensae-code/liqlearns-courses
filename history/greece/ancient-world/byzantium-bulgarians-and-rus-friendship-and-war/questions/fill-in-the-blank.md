# Byzantium bulgarians and rus friendship and war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 22 Friendly relations and conflicts with the Bulgarians and the Rus" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bulgarian khans besiege and marry into the empire by turns, while Rus fleets attack and then trade and convert.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 22 Bulgarians and Rus exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises sort wars, treaties and baptisms in Byzantium's northern relations.
