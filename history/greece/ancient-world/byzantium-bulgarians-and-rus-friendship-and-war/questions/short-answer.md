# Byzantium bulgarians and rus friendship and war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 22 Friendly relations and conflicts with the Bulgarians and the Rus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bulgarian khans besiege and marry into the empire by turns, while Rus fleets attack and then trade and convert.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 22 Bulgarians and Rus exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Exercises sort wars, treaties and baptisms in Byzantium's northern relations.
