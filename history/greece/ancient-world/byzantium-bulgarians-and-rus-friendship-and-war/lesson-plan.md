# Lesson plan — Byzantium bulgarians and rus friendship and war

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Byzantium bulgarians and rus friendship and war
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — A thousand years of Byzantium: Constantinople founded, walled, besieged, lost and regained, to 1453 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Byzantium bulgarians and rus friendship and war as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — A thousand years of Byzantium: Constantinople founded, walled, besieged, lost and regained, to 1453 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unit 22 Friendly relations and conflicts with the Bulgarians and the Rus** — Bulgarian khans besiege and marry into the empire by turns, while Rus fleets attack and then trade and convert
2. **§2 Unit 22 Bulgarians and Rus exercises** — exercises sort wars, treaties and baptisms in Byzantium's northern relations
3. **§3 Ch. 3.I.6 The founding, development and Christianisation of the Russian state** — Varangian Rus found Kiev, raid Constantinople, then take baptism from it under Vladimir

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
