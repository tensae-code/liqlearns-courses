# Greek myth the trojan war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 5, ch. 1 The apple of Discord" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Eris throws the apple "for the fairest", Paris judges for Aphrodite, and his prize.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 5, ch. 2 The sacrifice of Iphigenia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Achaean fleet is wind-bound at Aulis, the seer Calchas demands Agamemnon's daughter Iphigenia, and the fleet finally sails for Troy.
