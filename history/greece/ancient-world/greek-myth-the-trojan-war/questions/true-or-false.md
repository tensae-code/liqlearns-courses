# Greek myth the trojan war — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit 5, ch. 1 The apple of Discord", Eris throws the apple "for the fairest", Paris judges for Aphrodite, and his prize (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit 5, ch. 2 The sacrifice of Iphigenia", the Achaean fleet is wind-bound at Aulis, the seer Calchas demands Agamemnon's daughter Iphigenia, and the fleet finally sails for Troy (Sub lesson 2)
