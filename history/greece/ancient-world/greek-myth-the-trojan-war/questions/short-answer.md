# Greek myth the trojan war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 5, ch. 1 The apple of Discord" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Eris throws the apple "for the fairest", Paris judges for Aphrodite, and his prize.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 5, ch. 2 The sacrifice of Iphigenia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Achaean fleet is wind-bound at Aulis, the seer Calchas demands Agamemnon's daughter Iphigenia, and the fleet finally sails for Troy.
