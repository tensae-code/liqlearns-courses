# Greek myth the trojan war

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greek myth the trojan war" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 5, ch. 1 The apple of Discord

Eris throws the apple "for the fairest", Paris judges for Aphrodite, and his prize.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 5, ch. 2 The sacrifice of Iphigenia

The Achaean fleet is wind-bound at Aulis, the seer Calchas demands Agamemnon's daughter Iphigenia, and the fleet finally sails for Troy.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 5, ch. 3 The Achaeans reach Troy

The kings and heroes of all Greece land before Priam's walled city and settle into a siege that will last ten years.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 5, ch. 4 The wrath of Achilles

Dishonoured by Agamemnon, Achilles withdraws from battle with his Myrmidons, and the Achaeans begin to lose.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 5, ch. 5 The death of Patroclus

Patroclus borrows Achilles's armour to save the ships, chases the Trojans too far, and is killed by Hector at the walls.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit 5, ch. 6 Achilles kills Hector

Grief turns Achilles's rage on Hector; he kills him, drags the body round Patroclus's tomb, and old Priam comes by night to ransom his son.

## Sub lesson 7 — Unit 5, ch. 7 The end of Achilles

Achilles falls to Paris's arrow in his one vulnerable heel.

## Sub lesson 8 — Unit 5, ch. 8 The Wooden Horse and the destruction of Troy

Odysseus's wooden horse smuggles warriors inside the celebrating city, Laocoon's warning is silenced by serpents, and Troy burns in one night.

## Sub lesson 9 — Unit 5, ch. 1 Apple of Discord exercises

Matching work rebuilds the chain from Eris's apple to Paris's judgement.

## Sub lesson 10 — Unit 5, ch. 2 Sacrifice of Iphigenia exercises

Pupils table the Achaean kings and their cities gathering at Aulis.

## Sub lesson 11 — Unit 5, ch. 3 Achaeans reach Troy exercises

Map completion traces each contingent's route from home port to Troy.

## Sub lesson 12 — Unit 5, ch. 4 Wrath of Achilles exercises

Exercises probe why Achilles's anger, not Trojan strength, nearly loses the war.

## Sub lesson 13 — Unit 5, ch. 5 Death of Patroclus exercises

Pupils trace who begged Achilles to return and how Patroclus went out in his armour instead.

## Sub lesson 14 — Unit 5, ch. 6 Achilles kills Hector exercises

Reading work on the duel and Priam's night visit to ransom Hector.

## Sub lesson 15 — Unit 5, ch. 7 End of Achilles exercises

Pupils unpack the living phrase "Achilles heel" from the hero's death.

## Sub lesson 16 — Unit 5, ch. 8 Wooden Horse exercises

Exercises retell the horse trick and the one night that ended ten years of siege.

## Sub lesson 17 — Revision lesson for Units 5, 6: From myth to reality

Gap-fill on Priam's Troy and its high walls turns the war story back toward the real city behind it.
