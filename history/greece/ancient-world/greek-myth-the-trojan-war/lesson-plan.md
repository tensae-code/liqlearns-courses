# Lesson plan — Greek myth the trojan war

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Ancient world · **Lesson:** Greek myth the trojan war
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myth first, then the first farmers: the gods and heroes, prehistory, and the Aegean civilisations, as Greece's youngest readers meet the past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Greek myth the trojan war as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Myth first, then the first farmers: the gods and heroes, prehistory, and the Aegean civilisations, as Greece's youngest readers meet the past — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unit 5, ch. 1 The apple of Discord** — Eris throws the apple "for the fairest", Paris judges for Aphrodite, and his prize
2. **§2 Unit 5, ch. 2 The sacrifice of Iphigenia** — the Achaean fleet is wind-bound at Aulis, the seer Calchas demands Agamemnon's daughter Iphigenia, and the fleet finally sails for Troy
3. **§3 Unit 5, ch. 3 The Achaeans reach Troy** — the kings and heroes of all Greece land before Priam's walled city and settle into a siege that will last ten years
4. **§4 Unit 5, ch. 4 The wrath of Achilles** — dishonoured by Agamemnon, Achilles withdraws from battle with his Myrmidons, and the Achaeans begin to lose
5. **§5 Unit 5, ch. 5 The death of Patroclus** — Patroclus borrows Achilles's armour to save the ships, chases the Trojans too far, and is killed by Hector at the walls
6. **§6 Unit 5, ch. 6 Achilles kills Hector** — grief turns Achilles's rage on Hector; he kills him, drags the body round Patroclus's tomb, and old Priam comes by night to ransom his son
7. **§7 Unit 5, ch. 7 The end of Achilles** — Achilles falls to Paris's arrow in his one vulnerable heel
8. **§8 Unit 5, ch. 8 The Wooden Horse and the destruction of Troy** — Odysseus's wooden horse smuggles warriors inside the celebrating city, Laocoon's warning is silenced by serpents, and Troy burns in one night
9. **§9 Unit 5, ch. 1 Apple of Discord exercises** — matching work rebuilds the chain from Eris's apple to Paris's judgement
10. **§10 Unit 5, ch. 2 Sacrifice of Iphigenia exercises** — pupils table the Achaean kings and their cities gathering at Aulis
11. **§11 Unit 5, ch. 3 Achaeans reach Troy exercises** — map completion traces each contingent's route from home port to Troy
12. **§12 Unit 5, ch. 4 Wrath of Achilles exercises** — exercises probe why Achilles's anger, not Trojan strength, nearly loses the war
13. **§13 Unit 5, ch. 5 Death of Patroclus exercises** — pupils trace who begged Achilles to return and how Patroclus went out in his armour instead
14. **§14 Unit 5, ch. 6 Achilles kills Hector exercises** — reading work on the duel and Priam's night visit to ransom Hector
15. **§15 Unit 5, ch. 7 End of Achilles exercises** — pupils unpack the living phrase "Achilles heel" from the hero's death
16. **§16 Unit 5, ch. 8 Wooden Horse exercises** — exercises retell the horse trick and the one night that ended ten years of siege
17. **§17 Revision lesson for Units 5, 6: From myth to reality** — gap-fill on Priam's Troy and its high walls turns the war story back toward the real city behind it
