# Iconoclasm the battle over images

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Iconoclasm the battle over images" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The battle for the icon between Gospel and political power

Emperors ban the icons, monks and empresses defend them; a century of struggle ends with the Triumph of Orthodoxy.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iconoclasm

Emperors smash the icons blaming them for defeats; monks resist; the empire argues about images for over a century.
