# Iconoclasm the battle over images — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The battle for the icon between Gospel and political power" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Emperors ban the icons, monks and empresses defend them; a century of struggle ends with the Triumph of Orthodoxy.

**2.** Tell the story of "Iconoclasm" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Emperors smash the icons blaming them for defeats; monks resist; the empire argues about images for over a century.
