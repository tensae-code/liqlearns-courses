# Iconoclasm the battle over images — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The battle for the icon between Gospel and political power" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Emperors ban the icons, monks and empresses defend them; a century of struggle ends with the Triumph of Orthodoxy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Iconoclasm" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Emperors smash the icons blaming them for defeats; monks resist; the empire argues about images for over a century.
