# Iconoclasm the battle over images — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "The battle for the icon between Gospel and political power", emperors ban the icons, monks and empresses defend them; a century of struggle ends with the Triumph of Orthodoxy (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Iconoclasm", emperors smash the icons blaming them for defeats; monks resist; the empire argues about images for over a century (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
