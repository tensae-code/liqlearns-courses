# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. B1 The peoples and civilisations of the Near East" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** River civilisations of Mesopotamia and Egypt and their neighbours invent writing, cities and empires around the Aegean's eastern shore.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Mesopotamia: the land between the Tigris and Euphrates" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** River farmers between two rivers build the first cities; kings and gods rule them; writing and science grow out of daily work.
