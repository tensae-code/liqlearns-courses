# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. B1 The peoples and civilisations of the Near East" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** River civilisations of Mesopotamia and Egypt and their neighbours invent writing, cities and empires around the Aegean's eastern shore.

**2.** Tell the story of "Mesopotamia: the land between the Tigris and Euphrates" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** River farmers between two rivers build the first cities; kings and gods rule them; writing and science grow out of daily work.
