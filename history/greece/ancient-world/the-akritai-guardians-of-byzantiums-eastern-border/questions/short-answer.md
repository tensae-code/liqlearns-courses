# The akritai guardians of byzantiums eastern border — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 20 Guarding the eastern borders and the Akritai" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Frontier soldier-farmers, the Akritai, hold the Taurus passes for generations and become the heroes of epic song like Digenes.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 20 Akritai exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Border-guard songs and maps place the Akritai on the empire's eastern edge.
