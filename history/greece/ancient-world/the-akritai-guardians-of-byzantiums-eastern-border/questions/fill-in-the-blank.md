# The akritai guardians of byzantiums eastern border — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 20 Guarding the eastern borders and the Akritai" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Frontier soldier-farmers, the Akritai, hold the Taurus passes for generations and become the heroes of epic song like Digenes.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 20 Akritai exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Border-guard songs and maps place the Akritai on the empire's eastern edge.
