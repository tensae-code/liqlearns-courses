# Greek myth theseus and the minotaur — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus, the prince of Troezen", young Theseus lifts the rock, takes his father Aegeus's sword and sandals, and chooses the dangerous land road to Athens over the safe sea (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit 3, ch. 2 The exploits of Theseus / Theseus kills the Minotaur", Theseus clears the road of robbers, then sails to Crete, enters the Labyrinth with Ariadne's thread and kills the Minotaur, freeing Athens from its tribute of youths (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
