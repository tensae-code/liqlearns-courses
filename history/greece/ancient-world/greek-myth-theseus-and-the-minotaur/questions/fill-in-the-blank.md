# Greek myth theseus and the minotaur — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus, the prince of Troezen" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Young Theseus lifts the rock, takes his father Aegeus's sword and sandals, and chooses the dangerous land road to Athens over the safe sea.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 3, ch. 2 The exploits of Theseus / Theseus kills the Minotaur" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Theseus clears the road of robbers, then sails to Crete, enters the Labyrinth with Ariadne's thread and kills the Minotaur, freeing Athens from its tribute of youths.
