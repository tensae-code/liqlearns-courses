# Greek myth theseus and the minotaur — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus, the prince of Troezen" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Young Theseus lifts the rock, takes his father Aegeus's sword and sandals, and chooses the dangerous land road to Athens over the safe sea.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 3, ch. 2 The exploits of Theseus / Theseus kills the Minotaur" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Theseus clears the road of robbers, then sails to Crete, enters the Labyrinth with Ariadne's thread and kills the Minotaur, freeing Athens from its tribute of youths.
