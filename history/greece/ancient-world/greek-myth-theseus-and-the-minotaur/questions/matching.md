# Greek myth theseus and the minotaur — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus, the prince of Troezen — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Unit 3, ch. 2 The exploits of Theseus / Theseus kills the Minotaur — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) young Theseus lifts the rock, takes his father Aegeus's sword and sandals, and chooses the dangerous land road to Athens over the safe sea
b) Theseus clears the road of robbers, then sails to Crete, enters the Labyrinth with Ariadne's thread and kills the Minotaur, freeing Athens from its tribute of youths
