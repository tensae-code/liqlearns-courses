# The scramble and berlin 1884 85 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The peak of European colonial expansion" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In one generation Europe partitions nearly all Africa and dominates Asia's coasts.

**2.** Tell the story of "The peak of European colonialism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The scramble partitions Africa and Asia's coasts; empire becomes the measure of a great power.
