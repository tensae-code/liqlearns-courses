# Find history in your own locality — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 43 Topics of local history: the history of my place" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The child interviews grandparents, follows a family festival letter and tracks one local monument (the Fortezza) from yesterday to today.

**2.** Tell the story of "Ch. 43 History of my place exercises" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils research their own surname from the elders and write their place's history.
