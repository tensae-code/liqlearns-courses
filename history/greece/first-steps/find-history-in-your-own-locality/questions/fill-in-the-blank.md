# Find history in your own locality — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 43 Topics of local history: the history of my place" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The child interviews grandparents, follows a family festival letter and tracks one local monument (the Fortezza) from yesterday to today.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 43 History of my place exercises" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils research their own surname from the elders and write their place's history.
