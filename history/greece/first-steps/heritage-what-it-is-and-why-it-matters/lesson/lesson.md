# Heritage what it is and why it matters

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Heritage what it is and why it matters" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Worksheet 5: Heraklion through time — respect for memory and history?

The class walks its own city asking which Venetian and Ottoman remains were kept, which erased, and who decides.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 5 E: The cultural heritage of the refugees

Language, dances, music and memory associations keep Pontic identity alive a century after the uprooting.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 1: Basic concepts of cultural heritage

What counts as a cultural good, why it must be saved, the monument's authenticity, historicity, quality and symbolism, and the world bodies (UNESCO, ICOMOS, ICCROM) that guard it.

## Sub lesson 4 — Chapter 3: Classification of cultural goods and their protection measures

Goods are sorted by age, place, universal significance and kind, then guarded by preventive conservation, first aid and systematic care.

## Sub lesson 5 — Chapter 7.4: The 'style' of traditional settlements and its protection

A village's look is itself heritage.

Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
