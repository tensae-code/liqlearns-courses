# Heritage what it is and why it matters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Worksheet 5: Heraklion through time — respect for memory and history?" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The class walks its own city asking which Venetian and Ottoman remains were kept, which erased, and who decides.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 5 E: The cultural heritage of the refugees" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Language, dances, music and memory associations keep Pontic identity alive a century after the uprooting.
