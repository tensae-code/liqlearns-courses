# Dating the past — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chronological table of the ancient world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The book closes by laying every people's story on one timeline so learners can see who lived when.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chronological tables (appendix)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The book's closing tables lay Greek and world events side by side on one timeline from 1815 to today.
