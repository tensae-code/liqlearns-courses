# Dating the past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Chronological table of the ancient world"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the book closes by laying every people's story on one timeline so learners can see who lived when  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Chronological tables (appendix)"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the book's closing tables lay Greek and world events side by side on one timeline from 1815 to today  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
