# Ancient egypt what egypt gave — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Book II, Unit 5 "Wonders" and "customs" of the Egyptians" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Egyptian customs invert Greek ones at every turn.

**2.** Tell the story of "Egyptian history and civilisation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pyramid builders and empire makers rise, are invaded, rise again, and leave writing, medicine and monuments.
