## Sub lesson 1 — Book II, Unit 5 "Wonders" and "customs" of the Egyptians

Egyptian customs invert Greek ones at every turn.

## Sub lesson 2 — Egyptian history and civilisation

Pyramid builders and empire makers rise, are invaded, rise again, and leave writing, medicine and monuments.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 5.3: The relocation of the Abu Simbel temples

The Aswan Dam's waters rise, and UNESCO cuts Ramses' temples into blocks and rebuilds them on higher ground.

## Sub lesson 4 — Chapter 5.4: The relocation of the Philae monuments

The Isis temple island is dismantled stone by stone and re-erected on Agilkia before the Nile takes it.

## Sub lesson 5 — Chapter 2: Egypt — general character, women's and men's dress, fabrics and accessories

White linen, pleats and broad collars fit a hot land and an eternal order; jewellery carries gods and rank.

## Sub lesson 6 — Chapter 1.1: Ancient Egypt — houses and palaces, furniture, dress, glasswork, goldwork and metalwork

The Egyptian house and its carved furniture freeze a whole civilisation's daily life in tomb paintings.

## Sub lesson 7 — Chapter 2 (second part): The art of Egypt

Pyramids, temples and tomb painting obey an eternal canon meant to defeat death.
