# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Book II, Unit 4 Egyptian logos: the land and the Nile" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Herodotus calls Egypt the gift of the river and measures its floods, soil and antiquity.

**2.** Tell the story of "Book II, Unit 6 Monuments of Egypt" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pyramids, labyrinth and temples outsize everything Greek, and Herodotus counts their cost in radishes and kings.
