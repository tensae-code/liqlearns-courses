# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Book II, Unit 4 Egyptian logos: the land and the Nile" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Herodotus calls Egypt the gift of the river and measures its floods, soil and antiquity.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Book II, Unit 6 Monuments of Egypt" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pyramids, labyrinth and temples outsize everything Greek, and Herodotus counts their cost in radishes and kings.
