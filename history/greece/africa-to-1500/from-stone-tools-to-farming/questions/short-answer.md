# From stone tools to farming — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 7, ch. 1 Palaeolithic Age: when humans were food-gatherers and hunters" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The first people live by gathering and hunting with stone tools, shelter in caves and leave rock paintings of their hunts.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit 7, ch. 2 Neolithic Age: when humans became farmers and herders" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** People learn to sow grain and tame animals, settle in villages, and grind, weave and make pots instead of wandering.
