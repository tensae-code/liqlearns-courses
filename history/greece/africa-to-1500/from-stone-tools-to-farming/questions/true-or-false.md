# From stone tools to farming — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit 7, ch. 1 Palaeolithic Age: when humans were food-gatherers and hunters", the first people live by gathering and hunting with stone tools, shelter in caves and leave rock paintings of their hunts (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit 7, ch. 2 Neolithic Age: when humans became farmers and herders", people learn to sow grain and tame animals, settle in villages, and grind, weave and make pots instead of wandering (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
