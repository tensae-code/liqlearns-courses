# From stone tools to farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 7, ch. 1 Palaeolithic Age: when humans were food-gatherers and hunters" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The first people live by gathering and hunting with stone tools, shelter in caves and leave rock paintings of their hunts.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit 7, ch. 2 Neolithic Age: when humans became farmers and herders" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** People learn to sow grain and tame animals, settle in villages, and grind, weave and make pots instead of wandering.
