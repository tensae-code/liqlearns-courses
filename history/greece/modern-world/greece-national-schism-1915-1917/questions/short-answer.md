# Greece national schism 1915 1917 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Greece in the First World War — the National Schism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** King and prime minister split the country in two.

**2.** Tell the story of "The National Schism in Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Venizelos and King Constantine pull Greece apart over joining the war.
