# Greece national schism 1915 1917 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greece in the First World War — the National Schism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** King and prime minister split the country in two.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The National Schism in Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Venizelos and King Constantine pull Greece apart over joining the war.
