# Greece occupation and resistance 1941 1944 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Occupation, Resistance and Liberation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Famine winters, a mass resistance movement in mountains and cities, and liberation in 1944 shadowed by coming civil conflict.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Occupation and National Resistance" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Famine, collaboration, and a resistance that liberates much of the countryside before Athens.
