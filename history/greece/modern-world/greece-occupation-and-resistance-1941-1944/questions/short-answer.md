# Greece occupation and resistance 1941 1944 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Occupation, Resistance and Liberation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Famine winters, a mass resistance movement in mountains and cities, and liberation in 1944 shadowed by coming civil conflict.

**2.** Tell the story of "Occupation and National Resistance" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Famine, collaboration, and a resistance that liberates much of the countryside before Athens.
