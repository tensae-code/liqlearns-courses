# Greece building the kingdom 1830 1881 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Greek state and its evolution (1830–1881)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Otto's Bavarian monarchy, the 1843 constitution, George I, the Great Idea and Thessaly's annexation in 1881 close the kingdom's first half-century.
