# Greece 1940 the greco italian war — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greece's part in the Second World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece says Ochi to Italy, beats its army in Albania, then faces Germany too and is overrun in spring 1941.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greece in the war: the Greco-Italian war and the German invasion" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ochi and the Albanian front win Greece glory; the Wehrmacht's spring invasion wins it occupation.
