# Greece 1940 the greco italian war — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Greece's part in the Second World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece says Ochi to Italy, beats its army in Albania, then faces Germany too and is overrun in spring 1941.

**2.** Tell the story of "Greece in the war: the Greco-Italian war and the German invasion" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ochi and the Albanian front win Greece glory; the Wehrmacht's spring invasion wins it occupation.
