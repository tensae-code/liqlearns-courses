# Latin rule in greece and cyprus — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Middle and late Latin rule in Greece (1460–1650) and Cyprus" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Venice holds Crete, the islands and Cyprus against the Ottomans until one by one they fall.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The end of Latin rule" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Napoleon hands Venice's Ionian islands away in 1797 and the last Latin chapter in Greece closes.
