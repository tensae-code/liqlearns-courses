# Latin rule in greece and cyprus — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Middle and late Latin rule in Greece (1460–1650) and Cyprus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Venice holds Crete, the islands and Cyprus against the Ottomans until one by one they fall.

**2.** Tell the story of "The end of Latin rule" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Napoleon hands Venice's Ionian islands away in 1797 and the last Latin chapter in Greece closes.
