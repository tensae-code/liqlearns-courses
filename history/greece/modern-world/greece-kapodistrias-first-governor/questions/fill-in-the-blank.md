# Greece kapodistrias first governor — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Kapodistrias as governor of Greece (1828–1831); the revolution completed (1829)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A Corfiot diplomat returns to govern a ruined, tiny state, builds schools and an army.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Kapodistrias governs the state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The first governor builds an administration from nothing and is murdered by the feuds he tried to end.
