# Greece kapodistrias first governor — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Kapodistrias as governor of Greece (1828–1831); the revolution completed (1829)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A Corfiot diplomat returns to govern a ruined, tiny state, builds schools and an army.

**2.** Tell the story of "Kapodistrias governs the state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The first governor builds an administration from nothing and is murdered by the feuds he tried to end.
