# Greece joins europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 12 Greece and its European course" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From association to full EEC membership in 1981 and the euro, Greece anchors itself in the Europe its diaspora once fled to.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§57 Greece in the 1980s (1981–1989)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** PASOK's "Change", EEC membership lived, and a decade that ends in scandal and stalemate.
