# Greece joins europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 12 Greece and its European course" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** From association to full EEC membership in 1981 and the euro, Greece anchors itself in the Europe its diaspora once fled to.

**2.** Tell the story of "§57 Greece in the 1980s (1981–1989)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** PASOK's "Change", EEC membership lived, and a decade that ends in scandal and stalemate.
