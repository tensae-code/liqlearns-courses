# Greece in the second world war 1940 44 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 7 The Albanian Epic" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece says "Ochi" to Mussolini's ultimatum on 28 October 1940 and throws the Italian invasion back into Albania.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 8 The German attack and the Second World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Germany crushes what Italy could not; Crete falls after its famous battle, and occupation, famine and resistance begin.
