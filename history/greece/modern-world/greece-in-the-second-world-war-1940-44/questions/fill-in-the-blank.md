# Greece in the second world war 1940 44 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 7 The Albanian Epic" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece says "Ochi" to Mussolini's ultimatum on 28 October 1940 and throws the Italian invasion back into Albania.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 8 The German attack and the Second World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Germany crushes what Italy could not; Crete falls after its famous battle, and occupation, famine and resistance begin.
