# Science and ideas in the 19th century

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Science and ideas in the 19th century" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Science and thought in the 19th-century world

Darwin rewrites man's place in nature, Marx rewrites society's, and laboratories change daily life.

## Sub lesson 2 — The 19th-century world scene: the triumph of science

Steam gives way to electricity, germs are found, evolution is argued.

## Sub lesson 3 — The movement of ideas: science and thought of the 19th century

Positivism, Darwin, Marx and the new social sciences remake how the century explains nature and society.
