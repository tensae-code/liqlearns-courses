# Lesson plan — Science and ideas in the 19th century

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Science and ideas in the 19th century
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Two centuries as problems: modern and contemporary history woven with Europe's, the Church's travelling story, and Heraklion investigated (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Science and ideas in the 19th century as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — Two centuries as problems: modern and contemporary history woven with Europe's, the Church's travelling story, and Heraklion investigated — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Science and thought in the 19th-century world** — Darwin rewrites man's place in nature, Marx rewrites society's, and laboratories change daily life
2. **§2 The 19th-century world scene: the triumph of science** — steam gives way to electricity, germs are found, evolution is argued
3. **§3 The movement of ideas: science and thought of the 19th century** — positivism, Darwin, Marx and the new social sciences remake how the century explains nature and society

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
