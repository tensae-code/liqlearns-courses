# Science and ideas in the 19th century — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Science and thought in the 19th-century world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Darwin rewrites man's place in nature, Marx rewrites society's, and laboratories change daily life.

**2.** Tell the story of "The 19th-century world scene: the triumph of science" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Steam gives way to electricity, germs are found, evolution is argued.
