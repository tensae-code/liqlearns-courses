# Science and ideas in the 19th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Science and thought in the 19th-century world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Darwin rewrites man's place in nature, Marx rewrites society's, and laboratories change daily life.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The 19th-century world scene: the triumph of science" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Steam gives way to electricity, germs are found, evolution is argued.
