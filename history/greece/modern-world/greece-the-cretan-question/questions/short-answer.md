# Greece the cretan question — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Cretan question (1821–1905)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Crete rises again and again against Ottoman rule, wins autonomy under Prince George, and waits for union with Greece.

**2.** Tell the story of "Worksheet 4: Toward the end of Ottoman rule (19th century)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tanzimat reforms and the Halepa charter of 1878 loosen the old order; Christian townspeople rise as the Muslim landowning class declines.
