# Greece the cretan question — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Cretan question (1821–1905)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Crete rises again and again against Ottoman rule, wins autonomy under Prince George, and waits for union with Greece.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Worksheet 4: Toward the end of Ottoman rule (19th century)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Tanzimat reforms and the Halepa charter of 1878 loosen the old order; Christian townspeople rise as the Muslim landowning class declines.
