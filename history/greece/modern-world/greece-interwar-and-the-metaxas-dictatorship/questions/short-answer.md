# Greece interwar and the metaxas dictatorship — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Interwar Greece; the dictatorship of 4 August 1936" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Republic, coups and restoration follow each other until Metaxas, with the king's consent, imposes dictatorship.

**2.** Tell the story of "Greece in the interwar period: the political situation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Republic and monarchy alternate amid coups until the 1936 dictatorship.
