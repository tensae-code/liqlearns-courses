# Greece interwar and the metaxas dictatorship — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Interwar Greece; the dictatorship of 4 August 1936" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Republic, coups and restoration follow each other until Metaxas, with the king's consent, imposes dictatorship.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greece in the interwar period: the political situation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Republic and monarchy alternate amid coups until the 1936 dictatorship.
