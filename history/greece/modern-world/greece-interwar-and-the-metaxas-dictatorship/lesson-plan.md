# Lesson plan — Greece interwar and the metaxas dictatorship

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Greece interwar and the metaxas dictatorship
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Two centuries as problems: modern and contemporary history woven with Europe's, the Church's travelling story, and Heraklion investigated (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Greece interwar and the metaxas dictatorship as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — Two centuries as problems: modern and contemporary history woven with Europe's, the Church's travelling story, and Heraklion investigated — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Interwar Greece; the dictatorship of 4 August 1936** — republic, coups and restoration follow each other until Metaxas, with the king's consent, imposes dictatorship
2. **§2 Greece in the interwar period: the political situation** — republic and monarchy alternate amid coups until the 1936 dictatorship
3. **§3 Greece in the critical decade 1930–1940** — default, the failed 1935 Venizelist coup, the restored king and Metaxas's 4th of August dictatorship
4. **§4 Theme 2 E: Modernisation and interventions (1923–1936)** — after the Asia Minor disaster the parties govern a republic through coups and restorations until Metaxas ends party rule in 1936

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
