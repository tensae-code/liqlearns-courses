# The greek revolution 1821 1830 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit C, ch. 1 The Filiki Etaireia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Three merchants in Odessa found a secret society in 1814, swear members by oath, and spin a web of initiates across the diaspora.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit C, ch. 2 The uprising in Moldavia-Wallachia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alexandros Ypsilantis crosses the Prut calling the Balkans to rise; the Sacred Band dies at Dragatsani and the northern rising is crushed.
