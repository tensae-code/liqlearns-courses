# The greek revolution 1821 1830 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit C, ch. 1 The Filiki Etaireia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Three merchants in Odessa found a secret society in 1814, swear members by oath, and spin a web of initiates across the diaspora.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit C, ch. 2 The uprising in Moldavia-Wallachia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alexandros Ypsilantis crosses the Prut calling the Balkans to rise; the Sacred Band dies at Dragatsani and the northern rising is crushed.
