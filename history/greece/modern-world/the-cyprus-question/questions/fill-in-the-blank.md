# The cyprus question — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 11 The Cyprus question" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cyprus's Greeks struggle for enosis through EOKA's campaign, win a fragile independence in 1960, and face invasion and division in 1974.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§54b The Cyprus struggle (1955–1960)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** EOKA's campaign against Britain ends in Zurich-London independence.
