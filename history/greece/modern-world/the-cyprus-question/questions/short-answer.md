# The cyprus question — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 11 The Cyprus question" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cyprus's Greeks struggle for enosis through EOKA's campaign, win a fragile independence in 1960, and face invasion and division in 1974.

**2.** Tell the story of "§54b The Cyprus struggle (1955–1960)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** EOKA's campaign against Britain ends in Zurich-London independence.
