# The renaissance its great figures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Humanism and the Renaissance in letters" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Petrarch to Erasmus, scholars recover the classical world and invent a new kind of learning.

**2.** Tell the story of "Renaissance and Humanism" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Scholars and artists put man at the centre and antiquity on the desk.
