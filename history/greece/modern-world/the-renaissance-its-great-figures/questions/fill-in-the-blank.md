# The renaissance its great figures — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Humanism and the Renaissance in letters" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Petrarch to Erasmus, scholars recover the classical world and invent a new kind of learning.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Renaissance and Humanism" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Scholars and artists put man at the centre and antiquity on the desk.
