# Greece letters and arts in the 20th century — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Letters in 20th-century Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Seferis and Elytis win Nobels while Cavafy's Alexandria poems conquer the world after his death.

**2.** Tell the story of "Arts in 20th-century Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Painters, sculptors and filmmakers search for a modern Greek style between folk tradition and Europe.
