# Greece letters and arts in the 20th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Letters in 20th-century Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Seferis and Elytis win Nobels while Cavafy's Alexandria poems conquer the world after his death.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Arts in 20th-century Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Painters, sculptors and filmmakers search for a modern Greek style between folk tradition and Europe.
