# Lesson plan — Greece letters and arts in the 20th century

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Greece letters and arts in the 20th century
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Two centuries as problems: modern and contemporary history woven with Europe's, the Church's travelling story, and Heraklion investigated (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Greece letters and arts in the 20th century as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — Two centuries as problems: modern and contemporary history woven with Europe's, the Church's travelling story, and Heraklion investigated — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Letters in 20th-century Greece** — Seferis and Elytis win Nobels while Cavafy's Alexandria poems conquer the world after his death
2. **§2 Arts in 20th-century Greece** — painters, sculptors and filmmakers search for a modern Greek style between folk tradition and Europe
3. **§3 Greek culture in the 20th century** — from the Generation of the '30s to Nobel poets, Greece argues with its past in modern forms
4. **§4 Artistic and intellectual life in 20th-century Greece** — Kontoglou to Tsarouchis in painting, Theodorakis in music, Cavafy's afterlife in poetry

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
