# The bauhaus school

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The bauhaus school" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapters 18–19: Schools of decorative and applied arts — the Bauhaus

Gropius founds the Bauhaus to reunite art and industry; Dessau, Meyer and Mies carry it until the Nazis close it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Units 17–18: The Bauhaus school; Bauhaus, graphic design and industrial design

Bauhaus typography throws away ornament and capitals alike and designs the modern page.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of The bauhaus school as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
