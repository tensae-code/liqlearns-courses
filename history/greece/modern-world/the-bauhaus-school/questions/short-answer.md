# The bauhaus school — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Chapters 18–19: Schools of decorative and applied arts — the Bauhaus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Gropius founds the Bauhaus to reunite art and industry; Dessau, Meyer and Mies carry it until the Nazis close it.

**2.** Tell the story of "Units 17–18: The Bauhaus school; Bauhaus, graphic design and industrial design" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bauhaus typography throws away ornament and capitals alike and designs the modern page.
