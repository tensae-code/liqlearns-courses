# The bauhaus school — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chapters 18–19: Schools of decorative and applied arts — the Bauhaus" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Gropius founds the Bauhaus to reunite art and industry; Dessau, Meyer and Mies carry it until the Nazis close it.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Units 17–18: The Bauhaus school; Bauhaus, graphic design and industrial design" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bauhaus typography throws away ornament and capitals alike and designs the modern page.
