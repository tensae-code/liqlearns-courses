# The bauhaus school — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Chapters 18–19: Schools of decorative and applied arts — the Bauhaus", Gropius founds the Bauhaus to reunite art and industry; Dessau, Meyer and Mies carry it until the Nazis close it (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Units 17–18: The Bauhaus school; Bauhaus, graphic design and industrial design", Bauhaus typography throws away ornament and capitals alike and designs the modern page (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
