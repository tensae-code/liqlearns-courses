# Greek traditional furniture and crafts

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greek traditional furniture and crafts" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## The story

Carved chests, pottery, weaving, the folk costume, metalwork and jewellery furnish the Greek house from its own hands.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Greek traditional furniture and crafts as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
