# Greece first assemblies and constitutions — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The revolutionaries' first attempts to build a state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** While fighting, the Greeks hold national assemblies and vote constitutions among the most liberal in Europe.

**2.** Tell the story of "Rivalries over the exercise of power" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Captains, islanders and politicians fall into civil wars even while the enemy is at the gates.
