# Greece first assemblies and constitutions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The revolutionaries' first attempts to build a state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** While fighting, the Greeks hold national assemblies and vote constitutions among the most liberal in Europe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Rivalries over the exercise of power" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Captains, islanders and politicians fall into civil wars even while the enemy is at the gates.
