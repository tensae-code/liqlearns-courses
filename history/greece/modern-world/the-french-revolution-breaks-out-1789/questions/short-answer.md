# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§3 The outbreak and course of the French Revolution (1789–1794)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Bastille, the Declaration, the king's flight and execution, and the Revolution devouring its children in the Terror.

**2.** Tell the story of "The French revolution breaks out and develops (1789–1794)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Bastille falls, the king falls, the republic declares the rights of man and turns on its own.
