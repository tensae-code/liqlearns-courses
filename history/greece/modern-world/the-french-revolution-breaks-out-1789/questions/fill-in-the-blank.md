# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§3 The outbreak and course of the French Revolution (1789–1794)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Bastille, the Declaration, the king's flight and execution, and the Revolution devouring its children in the Terror.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The French revolution breaks out and develops (1789–1794)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Bastille falls, the king falls, the republic declares the rights of man and turns on its own.
