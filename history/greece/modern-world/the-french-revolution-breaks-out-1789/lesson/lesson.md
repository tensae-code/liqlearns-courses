The Bastille, the Declaration, the king's flight and execution, and the Revolution devouring its children in the Terror.

The Bastille falls, the king falls, the republic declares the rights of man and turns on its own.

## Sub lesson 3 — The French Revolution: outbreak, measures, Terror and Directory

Hunger and ideas storm the Bastille; the republic guillotines its enemies and tires itself out.

## Sub lesson 4 — The French Revolution (1789–1815, first part)

The Estates-General becomes a National Assembly and a revolution remakes France.
