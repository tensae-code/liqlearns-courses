# Greece independence and europe navarino — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Greek revolution and Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Governments stay cold, philhellenes volunteer and give money, and in 1827 the allied fleets sink the Ottoman-Egyptian fleet at Navarino.

**2.** Tell the story of "The diplomatic side of the Struggle" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Philhellenes, loans and finally the allied fleets at Navarino turn a local rising into a recognised state.
