# Greece independence and europe navarino — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Greek revolution and Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Governments stay cold, philhellenes volunteer and give money, and in 1827 the allied fleets sink the Ottoman-Egyptian fleet at Navarino.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The diplomatic side of the Struggle" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Philhellenes, loans and finally the allied fleets at Navarino turn a local rising into a recognised state.
