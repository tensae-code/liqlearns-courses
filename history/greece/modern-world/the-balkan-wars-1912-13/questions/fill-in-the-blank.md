# The balkan wars 1912 13 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 3 The Balkan Wars" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece, Serbia, Bulgaria and Montenegro strip Turkey of Europe; the Greek army and navy take Thessaloniki and the Aegean islands, then beat Bulgaria.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§29 The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Elassona, Giannitsa, Thessaloniki taken a day before the Bulgarians, Bizani, the Aegean fleet.
