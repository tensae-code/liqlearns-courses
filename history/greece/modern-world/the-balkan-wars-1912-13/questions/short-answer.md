# The balkan wars 1912 13 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 3 The Balkan Wars" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece, Serbia, Bulgaria and Montenegro strip Turkey of Europe; the Greek army and navy take Thessaloniki and the Aegean islands, then beat Bulgaria.

**2.** Tell the story of "§29 The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Elassona, Giannitsa, Thessaloniki taken a day before the Bulgarians, Bizani, the Aegean fleet.
