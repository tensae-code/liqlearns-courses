# The church under ottoman rule — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "After Byzantium: Christian tradition and modernity" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Patriarchate survives under the sultans as the flock's government, keeping faith and Greek learning alive until the modern age tests both.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The East under Ottoman domination" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Patriarchate heads a millet; Greeks live, trade and keep their faith under the sultans.
