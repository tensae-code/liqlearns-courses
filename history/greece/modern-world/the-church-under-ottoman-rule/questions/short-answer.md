# The church under ottoman rule — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "After Byzantium: Christian tradition and modernity" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Patriarchate survives under the sultans as the flock's government, keeping faith and Greek learning alive until the modern age tests both.

**2.** Tell the story of "The East under Ottoman domination" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Patriarchate heads a millet; Greeks live, trade and keep their faith under the sultans.
