# Arts and letters in the 19th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§25 Arts and letters in the 19th-century world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Romanticism to realism.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Arts and letters in the 19th-century world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Romanticism, realism and impressionism follow the century's hopes and disappointments onto canvas and page.
