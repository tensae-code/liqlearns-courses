# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 7.I.5 Developments in letters, sciences and arts in the 17th–18th c." turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Telescopes, Newton and the philosophes turn the Scientific Revolution into the Enlightenment.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§1 The age of the Enlightenment" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Reason is turned on throne, altar and tradition, and the philosophes draft the blueprint of the modern world.
