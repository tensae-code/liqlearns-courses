# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 7.I.5 Developments in letters, sciences and arts in the 17th–18th c." — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Telescopes, Newton and the philosophes turn the Scientific Revolution into the Enlightenment.

**2.** Tell the story of "§1 The age of the Enlightenment" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Reason is turned on throne, altar and tradition, and the philosophes draft the blueprint of the modern world.
