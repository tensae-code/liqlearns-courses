# Greece refugees of the revolution 1821 1831 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Theme 3 A–B: The refugee question during the Revolution and under Kapodistrias (1821–1831)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greeks flee massacres in Asia Minor, Constantinople, Cyprus, Chios and Crete into the free state, and Kapodistrias's government and the Fourth National Assembly argue over their care.

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 3 C: Refugee rehabilitation under Otto's monarchy (1833–1862)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Bavarian state settles war refugees while 'autochthones' and 'heterochthones' fight over jobs and rights.
