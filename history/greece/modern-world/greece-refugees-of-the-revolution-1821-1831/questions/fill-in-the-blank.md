# Greece refugees of the revolution 1821 1831 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 3 A–B: The refugee question during the Revolution and under Kapodistrias (1821–1831)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greeks flee massacres in Asia Minor, Constantinople, Cyprus, Chios and Crete into the free state, and Kapodistrias's government and the Fourth National Assembly argue over their care.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 3 C: Refugee rehabilitation under Otto's monarchy (1833–1862)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Bavarian state settles war refugees while 'autochthones' and 'heterochthones' fight over jobs and rights.
