# Greece the persecutions of 1914 and the refugee streams

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greece the persecutions of 1914 and the refugee streams" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## The story

The 1914 persecution empties the Aegean coast of Asia Minor, war drives more Greeks from their homes, and a partial repatriation follows in 1919.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Greece the persecutions of 1914 and the refugee streams as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
