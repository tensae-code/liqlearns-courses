# 1917 twice february and october in russia — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§33 The Russian Revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** War and hunger fell the Tsar in February; Lenin's Bolsheviks take October and exit the war.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Russian revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** War and hunger fell the Tsar in February; in October the Bolsheviks seize power and promise peace, land and bread.
