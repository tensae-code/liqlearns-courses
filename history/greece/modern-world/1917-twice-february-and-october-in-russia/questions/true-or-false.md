# 1917 twice february and october in russia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§33 The Russian Revolution", war and hunger fell the Tsar in February; Lenin's Bolsheviks take October and exit the war (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** True or false: In "The Russian revolution", war and hunger fell the Tsar in February; in October the Bolsheviks seize power and promise peace, land and bread (Sub lesson 2)
