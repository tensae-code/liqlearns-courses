# 1917 twice february and october in russia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§33 The Russian Revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** War and hunger fell the Tsar in February; Lenin's Bolsheviks take October and exit the war.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Russian revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** War and hunger fell the Tsar in February; in October the Bolsheviks seize power and promise peace, land and bread.
