# Greece protecting antiquities since 1834 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Chapter 4.1: Historical review of Greek heritage legislation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The new kingdom's 1834 antiquities law is among Europe's first; a century and a half of laws follows the country's changing idea of its past.

**2.** Tell the story of "Chapter 4.2–4.4: The bodies, the scientists and today's framework" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Ministry of Culture, the Archaeological Society (1837) and generations of conservators form the machine that runs Greek protection today.
