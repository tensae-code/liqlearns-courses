# Greece protecting antiquities since 1834 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chapter 4.1: Historical review of Greek heritage legislation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The new kingdom's 1834 antiquities law is among Europe's first; a century and a half of laws follows the country's changing idea of its past.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chapter 4.2–4.4: The bodies, the scientists and today's framework" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Ministry of Culture, the Archaeological Society (1837) and generations of conservators form the machine that runs Greek protection today.
