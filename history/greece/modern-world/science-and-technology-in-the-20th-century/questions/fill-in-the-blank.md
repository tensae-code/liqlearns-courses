# Science and technology in the 20th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Scientific and technological developments in the 20th-century world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Atoms, antibiotics, computers and rockets compress a century of change into one lifetime.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The sciences in the 20th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Relativity, the atom, DNA and computers.
