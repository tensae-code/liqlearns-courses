# Science and technology in the 20th century — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Scientific and technological developments in the 20th-century world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Atoms, antibiotics, computers and rockets compress a century of change into one lifetime.

**2.** Tell the story of "The sciences in the 20th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Relativity, the atom, DNA and computers.
