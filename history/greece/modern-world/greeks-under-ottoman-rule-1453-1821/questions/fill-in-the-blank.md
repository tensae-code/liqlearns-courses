# Greeks under ottoman rule 1453 1821 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit B, ch. 1 The conquest of the Greek peninsula" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After 1453 the Ottomans complete the conquest of the Greek lands, ending the long Byzantine decline in Turkish and Latin lordship.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit B, ch. 2 The living conditions of the enslaved" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Great Byzantine families flee west while the rayah peasants pay the head tax and carry the conqueror's burdens.
