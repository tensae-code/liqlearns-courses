# Greeks under ottoman rule 1453 1821 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit B, ch. 1 The conquest of the Greek peninsula" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After 1453 the Ottomans complete the conquest of the Greek lands, ending the long Byzantine decline in Turkish and Latin lordship.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit B, ch. 2 The living conditions of the enslaved" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Great Byzantine families flee west while the rayah peasants pay the head tax and carry the conqueror's burdens.
