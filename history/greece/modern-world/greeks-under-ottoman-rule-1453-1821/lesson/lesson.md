# Greeks under ottoman rule 1453 1821

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greeks under ottoman rule 1453 1821" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit B, ch. 1 The conquest of the Greek peninsula

After 1453 the Ottomans complete the conquest of the Greek lands, ending the long Byzantine decline in Turkish and Latin lordship.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit B, ch. 2 The living conditions of the enslaved

Great Byzantine families flee west while the rayah peasants pay the head tax and carry the conqueror's burdens.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit B, ch. 3 The religious and political organisation of the Greeks

The Ottomans abolish Byzantine rule but tolerate the Church; the Patriarch becomes the nation's keeper under the millet order.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit B, ch. 4 The Klephts and the Armatoloi

Mountain klephts rob and resist the Turks while armatoloi police the passes.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit B, ch. 5 Economic life

An agrarian empire of wheat and barley slowly fills with Greek merchants, shipowners and guilds trading from Smyrna to Vienna.

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit B, ch. 6 The Greeks of the diaspora communities and the Danubian Principalities

Emigrant Greeks build churches, schools and printing houses abroad, and Phanariots like Alexandros Mavrokordatos rise to rule Moldavia and Wallachia.

## Sub lesson 7 — Unit B, ch. 7 The Teachers of the Nation

Cleric-teachers, many schooled in the West, multiply schools.

## Sub lesson 8 — Unit B, ch. 9 The main revolutionary movements

Maniots, Cretans and islanders rise again and again through three centuries, keeping alive the hope of liberation the sources record.

## Sub lesson 9 — Unit B, ch. 10 The struggles of the Souliots

The shepherd-warriors of Souli defy Ali Pasha for years, write him defiant letters, and end in exile and the dance of Zalongo's shadow.

## Sub lesson 10 — Unit B exercise dossier (Greeks under Ottoman and Latin rule)

Term-matching, map place-names from Moschopolis to Smyrna, a research task on Kosmas the Aetolian and a timeline from Küçük Kaynarca to the Revolution.

## Sub lesson 11 — Ch. 7.II Hellenism under Venetian and Ottoman rule

Crete blooms under Venice while the mainland lives under the Porte.

## Sub lesson 12 — §5 Hellenism from the mid-18th to the early 19th century

Merchants, schools, the diaspora and Rigas's and Korais's Enlightenment prepare a nation inside an empire.
