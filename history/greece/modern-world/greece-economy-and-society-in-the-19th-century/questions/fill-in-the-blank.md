# Greece economy and society in the 19th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greek economy and society in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Currants, shipping and remittances carry a poor farming country; Athens grows while villages empty.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greek political, social and economic life in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A poor agrarian kingdom lives on currants, shipping and the dream of the Great Idea.
