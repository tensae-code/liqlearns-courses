# Greece economy and society in the 19th century — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Greek economy and society in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Currants, shipping and remittances carry a poor farming country; Athens grows while villages empty.

**2.** Tell the story of "Greek political, social and economic life in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A poor agrarian kingdom lives on currants, shipping and the dream of the Great Idea.
