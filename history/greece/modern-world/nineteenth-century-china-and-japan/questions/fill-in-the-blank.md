# Nineteenth century china and japan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "China and Japan in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** China is forced open and humiliated by foreign powers while Japan, opened too, chooses to modernise itself instead.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Far East" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** China is carved into spheres of influence while Japan's Meiji modernisation turns it into the region's rising power.
