# Nineteenth century china and japan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "China and Japan in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** China is forced open and humiliated by foreign powers while Japan, opened too, chooses to modernise itself instead.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Far East" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** China is carved into spheres of influence while Japan's Meiji modernisation turns it into the region's rising power.
