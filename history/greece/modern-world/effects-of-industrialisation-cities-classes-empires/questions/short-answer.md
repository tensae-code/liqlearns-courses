# Effects of industrialisation cities classes empires — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§13 Social and political dimensions of the Industrial Revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Factory cities breed a working class, unions, socialism and the demand for the vote.

**2.** Tell the story of "Social and political dimensions of the industrial revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Factory cities swell, a working class organises, and unions and socialist parties force politics to change.
