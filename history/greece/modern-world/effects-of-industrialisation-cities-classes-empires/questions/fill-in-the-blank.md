# Effects of industrialisation cities classes empires — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§13 Social and political dimensions of the Industrial Revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Factory cities breed a working class, unions, socialism and the demand for the vote.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Social and political dimensions of the industrial revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Factory cities swell, a working class organises, and unions and socialist parties force politics to change.
