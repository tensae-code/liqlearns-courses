# The second world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§45 The omens and causes of the Second World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Appeasement, remilitarisation and the Nazi-Soviet pact clear Hitler's road to Poland.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Portents and causes of the Second World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hitler rearms and expands step by step while the democracies appease, until Poland makes retreat impossible.
