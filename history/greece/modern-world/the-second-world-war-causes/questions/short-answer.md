# The second world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§45 The omens and causes of the Second World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Appeasement, remilitarisation and the Nazi-Soviet pact clear Hitler's road to Poland.

**2.** Tell the story of "Portents and causes of the Second World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hitler rearms and expands step by step while the democracies appease, until Poland makes retreat impossible.
