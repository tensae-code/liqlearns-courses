# The first world war causes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "§31 The causes, outbreak and fronts of the First World War", alliances, arms races and one Sarajevo assassination chain the great powers into a war of trenches (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Causes, outbreak and fronts of the First World War", alliances, arms races and an assassination in Sarajevo pull Europe's empires into a war none can win quickly (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
