# The first world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§31 The causes, outbreak and fronts of the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alliances, arms races and one Sarajevo assassination chain the great powers into a war of trenches.

**2.** Tell the story of "Causes, outbreak and fronts of the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alliances, arms races and an assassination in Sarajevo pull Europe's empires into a war none can win quickly.
