# The first world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§31 The causes, outbreak and fronts of the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Alliances, arms races and one Sarajevo assassination chain the great powers into a war of trenches.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Causes, outbreak and fronts of the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Alliances, arms races and an assassination in Sarajevo pull Europe's empires into a war none can win quickly.
