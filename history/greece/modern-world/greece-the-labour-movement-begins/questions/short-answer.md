# Greece the labour movement begins — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Theme 1 C: The first steps of the labour movement" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Miners, tobacco workers and seamen organise the first unions and strikes and demand a labour law.

**2.** Tell the story of "Theme 2 D: The left and socialist parties" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Wartime hardship births the Socialist Labour Party (SEKE) in 1918 and a left enters Greek politics for good.
