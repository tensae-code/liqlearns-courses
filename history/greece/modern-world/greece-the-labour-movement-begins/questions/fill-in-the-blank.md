# Greece the labour movement begins — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 1 C: The first steps of the labour movement" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Miners, tobacco workers and seamen organise the first unions and strikes and demand a labour law.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Theme 2 D: The left and socialist parties" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Wartime hardship births the Socialist Labour Party (SEKE) in 1918 and a left enters Greek politics for good.
