# Greece the labour movement begins

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greece the labour movement begins" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 1 C: The first steps of the labour movement

Miners, tobacco workers and seamen organise the first unions and strikes and demand a labour law.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2 D: The left and socialist parties

Wartime hardship births the Socialist Labour Party (SEKE) in 1918 and a left enters Greek politics for good.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Greece the labour movement begins as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
