# Latin america wins independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Developments on the American continent" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Spain's American empire breaks into republics while the United States grows westward.

**2.** Tell the story of "The rise of the countries of the American continent: Latin America" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Spain's and Portugal's colonies break away into republics that spend the century searching for stability.
