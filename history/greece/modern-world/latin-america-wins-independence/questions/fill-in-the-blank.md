# Latin america wins independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Developments on the American continent" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Spain's American empire breaks into republics while the United States grows westward.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The rise of the countries of the American continent: Latin America" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Spain's and Portugal's colonies break away into republics that spend the century searching for stability.
