# Nationalism the feeling that made nation states

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Nationalism the feeling that made nation states" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The revolutionary movements of 1820–1821 in Europe

Secret societies and army officers rise in Spain, Italy and Greece against the restored kings.

## Sub lesson 2 — The revolutionary waves of 1830 and 1848 in Europe

Paris rises twice in a generation; in 1848 peoples across Europe demand constitutions and nations, and are mostly beaten back.

## Sub lesson 3 — The movements of the 1820s–1830s

Liberals and nationalists rise from Naples to Warsaw; Greece alone wins through.

## Sub lesson 4 — The 'Spring of the Peoples' (1848)

In one spring, thrones shake from Paris to Budapest before the old order recovers.

## Sub lesson 5 — The national and liberal movements in Europe

Carbonari, Decembrists and the risings of 1820–1848 carry the twin ideas of nation and constitution against the restored kings.

## Sub lesson 6 — The crisis of the Habsburg Empire

The multinational empire cracks in 1848; Hungary's national movement wins a dual monarchy in 1867 but no solution for the other peoples.
