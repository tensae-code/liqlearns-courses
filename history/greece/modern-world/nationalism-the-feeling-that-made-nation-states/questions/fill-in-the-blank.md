# Nationalism the feeling that made nation states — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The revolutionary movements of 1820–1821 in Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Secret societies and army officers rise in Spain, Italy and Greece against the restored kings.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The revolutionary waves of 1830 and 1848 in Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Paris rises twice in a generation; in 1848 peoples across Europe demand constitutions and nations, and are mostly beaten back.
