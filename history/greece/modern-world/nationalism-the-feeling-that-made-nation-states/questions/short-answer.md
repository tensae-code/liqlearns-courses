# Nationalism the feeling that made nation states — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The revolutionary movements of 1820–1821 in Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Secret societies and army officers rise in Spain, Italy and Greece against the restored kings.

**2.** Tell the story of "The revolutionary waves of 1830 and 1848 in Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Paris rises twice in a generation; in 1848 peoples across Europe demand constitutions and nations, and are mostly beaten back.
