# Greece democratic greece 1974 1989 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Greece from the Metapolitefsi to the early 1980s (1974–1981)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Karamanlis restores democracy, a referendum ends the monarchy, and Greece joins the European Community.

**2.** Tell the story of "Greece in the 1980s (1981–1989)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** PASOK's 'Change' brings social reform, European money and new scandals in equal measure.
