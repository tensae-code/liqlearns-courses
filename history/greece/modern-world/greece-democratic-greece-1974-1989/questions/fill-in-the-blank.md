# Greece democratic greece 1974 1989 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greece from the Metapolitefsi to the early 1980s (1974–1981)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Karamanlis restores democracy, a referendum ends the monarchy, and Greece joins the European Community.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greece in the 1980s (1981–1989)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** PASOK's 'Change' brings social reform, European money and new scandals in equal measure.
