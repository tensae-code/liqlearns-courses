# Greece king otto era 1833 1862 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "From Otto's arrival (1833) to the 3rd of September 1843" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A Bavarian teenage king, foreign regents and loans; Athenians and the army force a constitution in 1843.

**2.** Tell the story of "From the 3rd of September 1843 to Otto's expulsion (1862)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Party politics tied to foreign embassies, the Crimean humiliation, and a king finally sent home.
