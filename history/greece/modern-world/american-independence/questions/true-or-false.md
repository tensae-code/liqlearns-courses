# American independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit A, ch. 3 The American and French Revolutions", the colonists rise against Britain declaring that men are born equal (1776), and France follows with its own revolution (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "§2 The American Revolution", thirteen colonies reject a king across an ocean and found a republic on the rights of man (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
