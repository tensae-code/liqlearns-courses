# American independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit A, ch. 3 The American and French Revolutions" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The colonists rise against Britain declaring that men are born equal (1776), and France follows with its own revolution.

**2.** Tell the story of "§2 The American Revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Thirteen colonies reject a king across an ocean and found a republic on the rights of man.
