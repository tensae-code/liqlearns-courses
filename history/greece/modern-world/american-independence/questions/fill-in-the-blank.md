# American independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit A, ch. 3 The American and French Revolutions" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The colonists rise against Britain declaring that men are born equal (1776), and France follows with its own revolution.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§2 The American Revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Thirteen colonies reject a king across an ocean and found a republic on the rights of man.
