# Greece war of independence 1821 1827 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The course of the Greek revolution (1821–1827)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Island fleets and mountain fighters beat Ottoman armies, fall into civil war, face Ibrahim's Egyptians.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The struggle for independence: outline of the Revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1821's rising spreads from the Peloponnese and the islands; eight years of war end in a small free state.
