# Greece war of independence 1821 1827 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The course of the Greek revolution (1821–1827)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Island fleets and mountain fighters beat Ottoman armies, fall into civil war, face Ibrahim's Egyptians.

**2.** Tell the story of "The struggle for independence: outline of the Revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1821's rising spreads from the Peloponnese and the islands; eight years of war end in a small free state.
