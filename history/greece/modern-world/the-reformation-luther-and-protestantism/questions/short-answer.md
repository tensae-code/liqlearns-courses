# The reformation luther and protestantism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch. 7.I.3 The Religious Reformation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Luther's theses split the western Church and redraw Europe's confessional map in blood.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Reformation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Luther's protest against indulgences becomes a break with Rome and remakes the map of European Christianity.
