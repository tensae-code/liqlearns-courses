# The reformation luther and protestantism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Ch. 7.I.3 The Religious Reformation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Luther's theses split the western Church and redraw Europe's confessional map in blood.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Reformation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Luther's protest against indulgences becomes a break with Rome and remakes the map of European Christianity.
