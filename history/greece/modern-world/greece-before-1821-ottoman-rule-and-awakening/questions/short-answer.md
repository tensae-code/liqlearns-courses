# Greece before 1821 ottoman rule and awakening — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Hellenism from the mid-18th to the early 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greeks under Ottoman rule grow rich on trade and shipping, schools and printing spread the Enlightenment, and a nation wakes up.

**2.** Tell the story of "Cultural relations between Europe and the Greeks under foreign rule" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Greek merchants and scholars carry the Enlightenment home in books, schools and ships.
