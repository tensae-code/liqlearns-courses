# Greece before 1821 ottoman rule and awakening — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Hellenism from the mid-18th to the early 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greeks under Ottoman rule grow rich on trade and shipping, schools and printing spread the Enlightenment, and a nation wakes up.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Cultural relations between Europe and the Greeks under foreign rule" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Greek merchants and scholars carry the Enlightenment home in books, schools and ships.
