# The asia minor campaign and catastrophe 1922 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 5 The Asia Minor Campaign and the Catastrophe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Greek army lands at Smyrna in 1919, drives deep into Anatolia, and is destroyed in 1922; Smyrna burns and a million and a half refugees cross to Greece.
