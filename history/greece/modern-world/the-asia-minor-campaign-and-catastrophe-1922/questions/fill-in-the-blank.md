# The asia minor campaign and catastrophe 1922 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 5 The Asia Minor Campaign and the Catastrophe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Greek army lands at Smyrna in 1919, drives deep into Anatolia, and is destroyed in 1922; Smyrna burns and a million and a half refugees cross to Greece.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E exercise dossier (Greece in the 20th century)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Maps marked with the Treaty of Sèvres gains and the Smyrna zone, timelines of 1940–49, the army's advance in Northern Epirus mapped, and EOKA research links for Cyprus.
