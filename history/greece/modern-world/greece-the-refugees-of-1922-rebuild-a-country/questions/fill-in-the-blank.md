# Greece the refugees of 1922 rebuild a country — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The refugee question in interwar Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Economy and society; the refugee problem" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
