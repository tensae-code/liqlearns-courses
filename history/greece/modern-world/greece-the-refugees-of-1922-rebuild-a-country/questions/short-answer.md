# Greece the refugees of 1922 rebuild a country — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The refugee question in interwar Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** One and a half million refugees are housed, fed and settled.

**2.** Tell the story of "Economy and society; the refugee problem" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A state of five million absorbs over a million refugees and is transformed by them.
