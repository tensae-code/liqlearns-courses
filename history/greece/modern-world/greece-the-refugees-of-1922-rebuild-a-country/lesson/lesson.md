# Greece the refugees of 1922 rebuild a country

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greece the refugees of 1922 rebuild a country" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The refugee question in interwar Greece

One and a half million refugees are housed, fed and settled.

## Sub lesson 2 — Economy and society; the refugee problem

A state of five million absorbs over a million refugees and is transformed by them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Internal developments in Greece (1923–1930)

A republic is proclaimed, a million and a half refugees are settled in Macedonia and the cities, and Venizelos's 1928 return brings a last reforming burst.

## Sub lesson 4 — Theme 3 B: The Lausanne Convention and the exchange of populations

The 1923 convention makes the flight compulsory and mutual.

## Sub lesson 5 — Theme 3 C: The rehabilitation of the refugees

The League of Nations' Refugee Settlement Commission houses the newcomers in Macedonian villages and new urban quarters.

## Sub lesson 6 — Theme 3 E: The integration of the refugees into Greece

Within a generation the refugees transform Greece's farming, industry, cities and culture.

## Sub lesson 7 — Theme 5 E: The presence of Black Sea Hellenism today — the refugee problem from 1922

The survivors reach Greece as refugees and rebuild Pontic villages in Macedonia, carrying the loss with them.
