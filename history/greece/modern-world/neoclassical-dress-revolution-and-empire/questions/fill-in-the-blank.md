# Neoclassical dress revolution and empire — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Chapter 4: Classicism and Neoclassicism in dress" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Revolutionaries drop the wig and knee-breeches for trousers and a white muslin 'Greek' dress; Napoleon re-gilds the silhouette as Empire style.
