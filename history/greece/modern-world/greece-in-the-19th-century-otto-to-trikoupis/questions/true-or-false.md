# Greece in the 19th century otto to trikoupis — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Unit D, ch. 1 The reign of Otto — Ioannis Kolettis", Bavarian teenager Otto arrives as king, Athens becomes capital, and Kolettis's Great Idea points the little kingdom at all the unredeemed Greeks (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Unit D, ch. 2 The reign of George I — Charilaos Trikoupis", a Danish prince takes the throne, the Ionian Islands are gained, and Trikoupis modernises with railways, the Corinth Canal and chronic debt (Sub lesson 2)
