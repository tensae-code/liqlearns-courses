# Greece in the 19th century otto to trikoupis — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit D, ch. 1 The reign of Otto — Ioannis Kolettis" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bavarian teenager Otto arrives as king, Athens becomes capital, and Kolettis's Great Idea points the little kingdom at all the unredeemed Greeks.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit D, ch. 2 The reign of George I — Charilaos Trikoupis" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A Danish prince takes the throne, the Ionian Islands are gained, and Trikoupis modernises with railways, the Corinth Canal and chronic debt.
