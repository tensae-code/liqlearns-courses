# Greece in the 19th century otto to trikoupis — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit D, ch. 1 The reign of Otto — Ioannis Kolettis" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bavarian teenager Otto arrives as king, Athens becomes capital, and Kolettis's Great Idea points the little kingdom at all the unredeemed Greeks.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit D, ch. 2 The reign of George I — Charilaos Trikoupis" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A Danish prince takes the throne, the Ionian Islands are gained, and Trikoupis modernises with railways, the Corinth Canal and chronic debt.
