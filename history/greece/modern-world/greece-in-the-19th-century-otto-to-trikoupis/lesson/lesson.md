# Greece in the 19th century otto to trikoupis

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greece in the 19th century otto to trikoupis" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit D, ch. 1 The reign of Otto — Ioannis Kolettis

Bavarian teenager Otto arrives as king, Athens becomes capital, and Kolettis's Great Idea points the little kingdom at all the unredeemed Greeks.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit D, ch. 2 The reign of George I — Charilaos Trikoupis

A Danish prince takes the throne, the Ionian Islands are gained, and Trikoupis modernises with railways, the Corinth Canal and chronic debt.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit D, ch. 3 Revolutionary movements in Macedonia and Crete

Cretans rise again and again for union, and Macedonia's Greeks arm as the Balkan nations circle the dying empire.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit D, ch. 4 Greece at the end of the 19th century

Thessaly gained, then bankruptcy in 1893.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit D exercise dossier (Greece in the 19th century)

Events sorted between the 1844 and 1864 constitutions and the Ionian union, cities matched to their cultural institutions.

## Sub lesson 6 — §18 From Otto's arrival (1833) to 3 September 1843

A Bavarian court rules without a constitution until soldiers and people in the square demand one.

## Sub lesson 7 — §19 From 3 September 1843 to Otto's expulsion (1862)

Constitutional monarchy, the Crimean humiliation at Piraeus, and a dynasty that never learns Greek politics.

## Sub lesson 8 — §20 From Otto's expulsion (1862) to the Goudi movement (1909)

George I, the Ionian and Thessalian gains, Trikoupis's modernisation, bankruptcy and the 1897 disaster.

## Sub lesson 9 — §23 Greek economy and society in the 19th century

Currant booms and busts, emigration to America, and a peasant country slowly building towns and schools.
