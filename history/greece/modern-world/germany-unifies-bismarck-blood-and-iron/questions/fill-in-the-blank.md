# Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The unification of Germany" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bismarck unites Germany by three calculated wars and proclaims an empire at Versailles.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The national unification of Germany" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Prussia's wars make an empire and unbalance Europe for seventy years.
