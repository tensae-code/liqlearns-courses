# Germany unifies bismarck blood and iron — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The unification of Germany" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bismarck unites Germany by three calculated wars and proclaims an empire at Versailles.

**2.** Tell the story of "The national unification of Germany" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Prussia's wars make an empire and unbalance Europe for seventy years.
