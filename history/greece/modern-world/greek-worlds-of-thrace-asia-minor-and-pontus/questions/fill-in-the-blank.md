# Greek worlds of thrace asia minor and pontus — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit D, ch. 5 Thrace, Asia Minor and Pontus, flourishing Greek centres" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Outside the kingdom, Smyrna, Constantinople and the Pontic towns hold richer Greek worlds of banks, schools and newspapers.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§36 The Hellenism of western Asia Minor and Pontus" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Smyrna's cosmopolitan Greeks and the Pontic towns.
