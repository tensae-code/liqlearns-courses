# Greek worlds of thrace asia minor and pontus — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit D, ch. 5 Thrace, Asia Minor and Pontus, flourishing Greek centres" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Outside the kingdom, Smyrna, Constantinople and the Pontic towns hold richer Greek worlds of banks, schools and newspapers.

**2.** Tell the story of "§36 The Hellenism of western Asia Minor and Pontus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Smyrna's cosmopolitan Greeks and the Pontic towns.
