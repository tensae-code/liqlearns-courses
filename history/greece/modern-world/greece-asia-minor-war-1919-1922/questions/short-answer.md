# Greece asia minor war 1919 1922 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Entente and Greek claims on the Ottoman Empire" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The victors carve up the Ottoman lands; Greece lands at Smyrna in 1919 with Allied blessing.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Greeks of western Asia Minor and Pontus" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ancient Greek communities of the coast and Pontus, prosperous under late Ottoman rule, are caught between two nationalisms.
