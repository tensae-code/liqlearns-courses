# Greece asia minor war 1919 1922 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Entente and Greek claims on the Ottoman Empire" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The victors carve up the Ottoman lands; Greece lands at Smyrna in 1919 with Allied blessing.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Greeks of western Asia Minor and Pontus" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ancient Greek communities of the coast and Pontus, prosperous under late Ottoman rule, are caught between two nationalisms.
