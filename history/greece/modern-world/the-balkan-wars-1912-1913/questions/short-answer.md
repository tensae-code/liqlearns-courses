# The balkan wars 1912 1913 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Balkan allies crush the Ottomans, then fight each other over Macedonia; Greece nearly doubles its territory.

**2.** Tell the story of "Greece and the Balkans right after the Balkan Wars" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** New borders, refugees already moving, and every capital arming for the next round.
