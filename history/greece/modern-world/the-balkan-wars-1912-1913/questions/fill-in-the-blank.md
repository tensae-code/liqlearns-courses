# The balkan wars 1912 1913 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Balkan allies crush the Ottomans, then fight each other over Macedonia; Greece nearly doubles its territory.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greece and the Balkans right after the Balkan Wars" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** New borders, refugees already moving, and every capital arming for the next round.
