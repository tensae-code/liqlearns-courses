# The balkan wars 1912 1913

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)

The Balkan allies crush the Ottomans, then fight each other over Macedonia; Greece nearly doubles its territory.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greece and the Balkans right after the Balkan Wars

New borders, refugees already moving, and every capital arming for the next round.

## Sub lesson 3 — Balkan relations at the start of the 20th century and the Balkan Wars

Allies against the Ottomans in 1912 become enemies in 1913; the map is redrawn in blood.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)

The Balkan League frees Macedonia and Thrace, then Bulgaria attacks its allies and loses; Greece takes Thessaloniki, Ioannina and the islands.
