# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§4 The final phase of the French Revolution (1794–1799) and the Napoleonic era (1799–1815)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Thermidor ends the Terror, Bonaparte's coup turns revolution into empire, and Waterloo ends it.

**2.** Tell the story of "The last phase of the revolution and the Napoleonic age (1794–1815)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chaos opens the door to a young general who crowns himself emperor, remakes Europe by war and code, and falls at Waterloo.
