# Napoleon reformer conqueror fallen — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§4 The final phase of the French Revolution (1794–1799) and the Napoleonic era (1799–1815)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Thermidor ends the Terror, Bonaparte's coup turns revolution into empire, and Waterloo ends it.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The last phase of the revolution and the Napoleonic age (1794–1815)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chaos opens the door to a young general who crowns himself emperor, remakes Europe by war and code, and falls at Waterloo.
