# Greece civil war 1946 1949 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Civil War and the main problems of post-civil-war Greece (1944–1963)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Resistance comrades become enemies; three years of civil war end in 1949 and leave a divided, policed democracy.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Civil War (1946–1949)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The wartime split hardens into three years of war in the mountains.
