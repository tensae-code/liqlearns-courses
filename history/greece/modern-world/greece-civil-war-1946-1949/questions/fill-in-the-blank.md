# Greece civil war 1946 1949 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Civil War and the main problems of post-civil-war Greece (1944–1963)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Resistance comrades become enemies; three years of civil war end in 1949 and leave a divided, policed democracy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Civil War (1946–1949)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The wartime split hardens into three years of war in the mountains.
