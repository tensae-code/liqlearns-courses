# The great depression 1929 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§40 The first postwar years, the 1929 world economic crisis and the Great Depression" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Wall Street's crash crosses every ocean; banks, factories and governments fall in sequence.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§41 Social dimensions of the 1929 crisis" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Breadlines, Hoovervilles and hunger marches.
