# The great depression 1929 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§40 The first postwar years, the 1929 world economic crisis and the Great Depression" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Wall Street's crash crosses every ocean; banks, factories and governments fall in sequence.

**2.** Tell the story of "§41 Social dimensions of the 1929 crisis" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Breadlines, Hoovervilles and hunger marches.
