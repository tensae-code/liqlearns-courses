# Greece the junta 1967 1974 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Deepening political crisis and the dictatorship of 21 April 1967 (1963–1974)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Unstable governments, a murdered deputy, then colonels' tanks at dawn; seven years of junta end with the Cyprus catastrophe.

**2.** Tell the story of "Postwar Greece: hopes, disappointment and the military dictatorship (1945–1974)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Reconstruction and growth under a guarded democracy, then the colonels' coup of 1967.
