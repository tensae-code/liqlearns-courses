# Postwar greece reconstruction junta and 1974 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 10 The postwar reconstruction of Greece (1950–1974)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Marshall aid, emigration and growth rebuild Greece until the colonels' junta of 1967–74 ends in the Cyprus tragedy and democracy's return.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§55 Sharpening of the political crisis and the dictatorship of 21 April 1967 (1963–1974)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lambrakis's murder, the apostasia, the colonels' tanks, the Polytechnic uprising and the Cyprus collapse of the junta.
