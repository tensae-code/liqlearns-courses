# Postwar greece reconstruction junta and 1974 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 10 The postwar reconstruction of Greece (1950–1974)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Marshall aid, emigration and growth rebuild Greece until the colonels' junta of 1967–74 ends in the Cyprus tragedy and democracy's return.

**2.** Tell the story of "§55 Sharpening of the political crisis and the dictatorship of 21 April 1967 (1963–1974)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lambrakis's murder, the apostasia, the colonels' tanks, the Polytechnic uprising and the Cyprus collapse of the junta.
