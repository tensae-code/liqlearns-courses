# The american civil war union against slavery — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§16 Developments in the American continent, China and Japan" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The United States survives civil war and abolishes slavery, China is forced open, and Japan answers with the Meiji transformation.

**2.** Tell the story of "Developments in the Americas in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The United States tears itself apart over slavery, reunites, and grows into an industrial giant.
