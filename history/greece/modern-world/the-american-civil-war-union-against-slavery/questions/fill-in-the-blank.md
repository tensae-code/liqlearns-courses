# The american civil war union against slavery — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§16 Developments in the American continent, China and Japan" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The United States survives civil war and abolishes slavery, China is forced open, and Japan answers with the Meiji transformation.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Developments in the Americas in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The United States tears itself apart over slavery, reunites, and grows into an industrial giant.
