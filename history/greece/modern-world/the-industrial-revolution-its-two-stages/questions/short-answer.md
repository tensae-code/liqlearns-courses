# The industrial revolution its two stages — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§12 The maturation of the Industrial Revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Steam, steel, railways and electricity carry industry from British workshops to the whole continent.

**2.** Tell the story of "The maturing of the industrial revolution" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Steam, steel, railways and electricity remake production in a second, heavier wave.
