# The industrial revolution its two stages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§12 The maturation of the Industrial Revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Steam, steel, railways and electricity carry industry from British workshops to the whole continent.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The maturing of the industrial revolution" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Steam, steel, railways and electricity remake production in a second, heavier wave.
