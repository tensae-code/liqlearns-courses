# Greece between the wars — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 6 The Interwar period" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Republic, refugee towns, tobacco strikes and coups fill the fragile decades that end in Metaxas's dictatorship of 1936.

**2.** Tell the story of "§43 Interwar Greece; the dictatorship of 4 August 1936" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Republic, refugee integration, the 1932 bankruptcy and Metaxas's regime close the fragile interwar decades.
