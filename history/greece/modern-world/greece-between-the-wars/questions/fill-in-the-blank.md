# Greece between the wars — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 6 The Interwar period" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Republic, refugee towns, tobacco strikes and coups fill the fragile decades that end in Metaxas's dictatorship of 1936.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§43 Interwar Greece; the dictatorship of 4 August 1936" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Republic, refugee integration, the 1932 bankruptcy and Metaxas's regime close the fragile interwar decades.
