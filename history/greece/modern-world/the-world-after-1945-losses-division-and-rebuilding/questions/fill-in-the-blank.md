# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§49 The results of the Second World War and the founding of the United Nations" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fifty million dead, two superpowers, and the UN founded on the promise of never again.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§50 The political division of postwar Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An iron curtain from Stettin to Trieste splits the continent the war had united.
