# The world after 1945 losses division and rebuilding — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§49 The results of the Second World War and the founding of the United Nations" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fifty million dead, two superpowers, and the UN founded on the promise of never again.

**2.** Tell the story of "§50 The political division of postwar Europe" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An iron curtain from Stettin to Trieste splits the continent the war had united.
