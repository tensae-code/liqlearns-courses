# The renaissance what it was — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit A, ch. 1 The Renaissance and the Religious Reformation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Art and learning are reborn in northern Italy and spread over Europe in two centuries, while the Reformation splits western Christianity.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit A exercise dossier (Europe in modern times)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils sort scholars, scientists and artists into Renaissance, Reformation and Enlightenment, and measure the Ottoman Empire on the student book's map.
