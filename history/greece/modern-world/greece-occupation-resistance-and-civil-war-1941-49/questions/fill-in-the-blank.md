# Greece occupation resistance and civil war 1941 49 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 9 A decade of struggles and sacrifices for freedom (1941–1949)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Resistance movements free mountains and sabotage the occupier, liberation slides into civil war, and the decade closes only in 1949.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§48 Occupation, Resistance and Liberation" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Famine winters, EAM's mountain state, Gorgopotamos, and a liberation already shadowed by December 1944.
