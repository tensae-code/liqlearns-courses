# Greece occupation resistance and civil war 1941 49 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 9 A decade of struggles and sacrifices for freedom (1941–1949)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Resistance movements free mountains and sabotage the occupier, liberation slides into civil war, and the decade closes only in 1949.

**2.** Tell the story of "§48 Occupation, Resistance and Liberation" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Famine winters, EAM's mountain state, Gorgopotamos, and a liberation already shadowed by December 1944.
