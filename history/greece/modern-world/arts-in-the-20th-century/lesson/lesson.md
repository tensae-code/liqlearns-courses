# Arts in the 20th century

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Arts in the 20th century" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arts in the 20th-century world

Painting breaks with realism, cinema is born and becomes the century's popular art.

## Sub lesson 2 — Poetry and prose in the 20th-century world

Two wars and broken empires drive writers to new forms for a fractured world.

## Sub lesson 3 — The arts in the 20th century

Art questions itself, cinema and the new arts democratise it, postwar art goes global.

## Sub lesson 4 — Arts of the 20th century: movements, cinema and mass culture

Fauvism, cubism, Dada, surrealism and pop art follow cinema, jazz and rock into a mass-culture century.

## Sub lesson 5 — Chapter 16: The decades 1900–1930 (part one)

Expressionism, Fauvism, the Blue Rider, Cubism and Futurism break the figure and the canvas in twenty years.

## Sub lesson 6 — Chapter 17: The decades 1900–1930 (part two)

De Stijl, Suprematism, Constructivism, Dada and Surrealism, the Bauhaus and the great masters of architecture remake art between the wars.

## Sub lesson 7 — Chapter 18: Postwar art in America and Europe — the New York School and Abstract Expressionism

Pollock's drips move art's capital across the Atlantic.

## Sub lesson 8 — Chapter 19: The 1960s to the 1980s–90s

Pop, Op and Kinetic art, Minimalism, Conceptual art and Photorealism argue over what art even is.
