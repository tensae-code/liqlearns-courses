# Arts in the 20th century — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Arts in the 20th-century world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Painting breaks with realism, cinema is born and becomes the century's popular art.

**2.** Tell the story of "Poetry and prose in the 20th-century world" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two wars and broken empires drive writers to new forms for a fractured world.
