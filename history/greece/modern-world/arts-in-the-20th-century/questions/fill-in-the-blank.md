# Arts in the 20th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Arts in the 20th-century world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Painting breaks with realism, cinema is born and becomes the century's popular art.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Poetry and prose in the 20th-century world" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Two wars and broken empires drive writers to new forms for a fractured world.
