# Greece 1862 1909 parliamentary life and trikoupis — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "From Otto's expulsion (1862) to the Goudi movement (1909)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A new Danish king, Trikoupis modernises and bankrupts the state, the 1897 war is lost, and the century ends in frustration.

**2.** Tell the story of "Attempts to modernise Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trikoupis builds railways, a canal and an administration, bankrupts the state, and leaves the modernisation half-done.
