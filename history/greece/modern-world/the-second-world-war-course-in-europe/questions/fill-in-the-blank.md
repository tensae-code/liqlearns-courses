# The second world war course in europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§46 The Second World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Blitzkrieg, Barbarossa, Stalingrad and Normandy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Second World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Blitzkrieg takes Europe, Britain holds, Germany invades the USSR and the United States joins.
