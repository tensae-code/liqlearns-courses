# The second world war course in europe — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "§46 The Second World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Blitzkrieg, Barbarossa, Stalingrad and Normandy.

**2.** Tell the story of "The Second World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Blitzkrieg takes Europe, Britain holds, Germany invades the USSR and the United States joins.
