# Europes revolutions 1820 1848 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§6 The revolutionary movements of 1820–1821 in Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Carbonari in Naples, liberals in Spain and juntas in Latin America rise as the Congress system tries to freeze 1815 forever.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "§11 The revolutionary waves of 1830 and 1848 in Europe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Paris barricades in 1830 give Belgium and a citizen-king; 1848 shakes every throne and fails almost everywhere.
