# The refugees of 1922 remake greece

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The refugees of 1922 remake greece" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## The story

A quarter of the population arrives with nothing; refugee quarters from Kokkinia to Nea Ionia remake Greek cities, music and politics.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of The refugees of 1922 remake greece as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
