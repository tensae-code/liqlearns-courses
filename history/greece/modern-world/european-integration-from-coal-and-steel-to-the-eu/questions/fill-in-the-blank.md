# European integration from coal and steel to the eu — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Milestones in Europe's unification efforts (1951–2001)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Coal and steel first, then a common market, a single market and a single currency.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Greece and Europe's unification; Greece as an EU member state" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Greece joins in 1981, struggles to keep up, adopts the euro and argues over what membership costs and gives.
