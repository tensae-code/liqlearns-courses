# European integration from coal and steel to the eu — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Milestones in Europe's unification efforts (1951–2001)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Coal and steel first, then a common market, a single market and a single currency.

**2.** Tell the story of "Greece and Europe's unification; Greece as an EU member state" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Greece joins in 1981, struggles to keep up, adopts the euro and argues over what membership costs and gives.
