# Venizelos and the remaking of greece — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 2 The Goudi movement and the Venizelos government" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Officers revolt at Goudi in 1909 and call the Cretan Venizelos, who rewrites the constitution and rebuilds army and state.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Unit E, ch. 4 Greece in the First World War" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Venizelos and King Constantine split the country in the National Schism.
