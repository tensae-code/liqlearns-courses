# Venizelos and the remaking of greece — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 2 The Goudi movement and the Venizelos government" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Officers revolt at Goudi in 1909 and call the Cretan Venizelos, who rewrites the constitution and rebuilds army and state.

**2.** Tell the story of "Unit E, ch. 4 Greece in the First World War" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Venizelos and King Constantine split the country in the National Schism.
