# Lesson plan — Venizelos and the remaking of greece

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Venizelos and the remaking of greece
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The modern national epic: a short European runway, the Ottoman centuries, the Revolution of 1821, and the building of modern Greece (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Venizelos and the remaking of greece as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The modern national epic: a short European runway, the Ottoman centuries, the Revolution of 1821, and the building of modern Greece — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Unit E, ch. 2 The Goudi movement and the Venizelos government** — officers revolt at Goudi in 1909 and call the Cretan Venizelos, who rewrites the constitution and rebuilds army and state
2. **§2 Unit E, ch. 4 Greece in the First World War** — Venizelos and King Constantine split the country in the National Schism
3. **§3 Unit E exercise dossier, portrait task: Eleftherios Venizelos** — a source portrait of Venizelos anchors the 20th-century unit
4. **§4 §27 The Goudi movement (1909)** — the Military League's pronunciamento forces reform and summons Venizelos from Crete
5. **§5 §28 Eleftherios Venizelos as prime minister: Venizelist policy 1910–1912** — a revised constitution, a rebuilt army and navy, and alliances prepared in two feverish years
6. **§6 §32 Greece in the First World War — the National Schism** — king and prime minister split Greece into two states, Thessaloniki against Athens, until exile settles it

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
