# Greece from goudi to venizelos — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "The Goudi movement (1909)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Officers camp at Goudi demanding reform, refuse power for themselves, and summon a Cretan politician, Venizelos.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Venizelos as prime minister: the politics of 1910–1912" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A revisionary assembly, a new constitution and an army and navy rebuilt just in time for war.
