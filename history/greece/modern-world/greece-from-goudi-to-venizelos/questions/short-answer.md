# Greece from goudi to venizelos — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Goudi movement (1909)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Officers camp at Goudi demanding reform, refuse power for themselves, and summon a Cretan politician, Venizelos.

**2.** Tell the story of "Venizelos as prime minister: the politics of 1910–1912" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A revisionary assembly, a new constitution and an army and navy rebuilt just in time for war.
