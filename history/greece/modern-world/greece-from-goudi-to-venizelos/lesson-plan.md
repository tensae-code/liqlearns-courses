# Lesson plan — Greece from goudi to venizelos

**Course:** History — Greece · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Greece from goudi to venizelos
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — Two centuries as problems: modern and contemporary history woven with Europe's, the Church's travelling story, and Heraklion investigated (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Greece from goudi to venizelos as Greece's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — Two centuries as problems: modern and contemporary history woven with Europe's, the Church's travelling story, and Heraklion investigated — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 The Goudi movement (1909)** — officers camp at Goudi demanding reform, refuse power for themselves, and summon a Cretan politician, Venizelos
2. **§2 Venizelos as prime minister: the politics of 1910–1912** — a revisionary assembly, a new constitution and an army and navy rebuilt just in time for war
3. **§3 Theme 2 C: From the bankruptcy to the military movement at Goudi (1893–1909)** — defeat in 1897 and financial control discredit the old parties until the officers' 1909 movement forces the door open
4. **§4 Theme 2 D: The Liberal Party of Venizelos** — Venizelos builds Greece's first modern programme party and carries the reforms of 1910–1912

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
