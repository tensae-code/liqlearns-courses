# Greece from goudi to venizelos

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Greece from goudi to venizelos" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Goudi movement (1909)

Officers camp at Goudi demanding reform, refuse power for themselves, and summon a Cretan politician, Venizelos.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venizelos as prime minister: the politics of 1910–1912

A revisionary assembly, a new constitution and an army and navy rebuilt just in time for war.

## Sub lesson 3 — Theme 2 C: From the bankruptcy to the military movement at Goudi (1893–1909)

Defeat in 1897 and financial control discredit the old parties until the officers' 1909 movement forces the door open.

## Sub lesson 4 — Theme 2 D: The Liberal Party of Venizelos

Venizelos builds Greece's first modern programme party and carries the reforms of 1910–1912.
