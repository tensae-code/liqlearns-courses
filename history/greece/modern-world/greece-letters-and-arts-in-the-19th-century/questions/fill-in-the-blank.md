# Greece letters and arts in the 19th century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Intellectual life, letters and arts in 19th-century Greece" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A new state argues over its language, writes its novels and poems, and paints its history.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Intellectual and artistic Greece in the 19th century" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Athens builds a neoclassical capital and argues in poetry and prose over the nation's language.
