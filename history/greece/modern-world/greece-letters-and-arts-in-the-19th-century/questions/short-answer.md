# Greece letters and arts in the 19th century — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Intellectual life, letters and arts in 19th-century Greece" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A new state argues over its language, writes its novels and poems, and paints its history.

**2.** Tell the story of "Intellectual and artistic Greece in the 19th century" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Athens builds a neoclassical capital and argues in poetry and prose over the nation's language.
