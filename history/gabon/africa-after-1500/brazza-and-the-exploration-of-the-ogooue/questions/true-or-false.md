# Brazza and the Exploration of the Ogooué — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** One explorer's name stands over this age: Brazza.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Brazza ascends the Ogooué river and signs the treaties that open Gabon and Congo to France — exploration turning into claim.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
