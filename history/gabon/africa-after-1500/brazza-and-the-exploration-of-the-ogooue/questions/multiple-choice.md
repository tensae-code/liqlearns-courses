# Brazza and the Exploration of the Ogooué — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 24 De Brazza et l'exploration de l'Ogooué"?
a) One explorer's name stands over this age: Brazza.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Brazza ascends the Ogooué river and signs the treaties that open Gabon and Congo to France — exploration turning into claim.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
