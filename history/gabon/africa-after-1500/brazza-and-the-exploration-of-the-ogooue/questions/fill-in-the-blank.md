# Brazza and the Exploration of the Ogooué — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: Gabon's great river, the road Brazza travelled into the interior.
**Answer:** Ogooué

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: the explorer whose river journeys and treaties opened Gabon and Congo to France.
