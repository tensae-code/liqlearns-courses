# Brazza and the Exploration of the Ogooué — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 24 De Brazza et l'exploration de l'Ogooué": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** One explorer's name stands over this age: Brazza. He takes the great road into Gabon's interior — the Ogooué river — and ascends it, meeting the peoples of the river and signing treaties with their rulers as he goes. The journey is famous as exploration; the lesson teaches its second half too. The treaties Brazza carries home open Gabon — and Congo beyond it — to France. The explorer's paddle and the treaty paper together prepare the colony that follows.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Brazza ascends the Ogooué river and signs the treaties that open Gabon and Congo to France — exploration turning into claim.
