# Video script — Brazza and the Exploration of the Ogooué

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Brazza ascends the Ogooué river and signs the treaties that open Gabon and Congo to France — exploration turning into claim.

One explorer's name stands over this age: Brazza. He takes the great road into Gabon's interior — the Ogooué river — and ascends it, meeting the peoples of the river and signing treaties with their rulers as he goes. The journey is famous as exploration; the lesson teaches its second half too. The treaties Brazza carries home open Gabon — and Congo beyond it — to France. The explorer's paddle and the treaty paper together prepare the colony that follows.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
