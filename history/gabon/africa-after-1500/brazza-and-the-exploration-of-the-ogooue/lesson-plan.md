# Lesson plan — Brazza and the Exploration of the Ogooué

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Brazza and the Exploration of the Ogooué

**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Brazza ascends the Ogooué river and signs the treaties that open Gabon and Congo to France — exploration turning into claim.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
