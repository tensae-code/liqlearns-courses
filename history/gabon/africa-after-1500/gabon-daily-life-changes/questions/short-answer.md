# Gabon Daily Life Changes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 39 L'évolution des modes de vie (1): l'habitat": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** History also lives in houses. The traditional Gabonese house — planks and beaten earth, built from what the land gives — is the starting point. Then the lesson walks into town: there the house changes into villas and apartment blocks, built of new materials, stacked and planned. Neither house is just shelter; each one shows how its people live, work and gather. When the habitat changes, daily life changes with it.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gabonese daily life keeps changing shape: the traditional house of planks and beaten earth gives way in town to villas and apartment blocks, barter between villages gives way to money and markets, and canoes, feet and bush paths give way to roads and vehicles.
