# Gabon Daily Life Changes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 39 L'évolution des modes de vie (1): l'habitat"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) History also lives in houses.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Gabonese daily life keeps changing shape: the traditional house of planks and beaten earth gives way in town to villas and apartment blocks, barter between villages gives way to money and markets, and canoes, feet and bush paths give way to roads and vehicles.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
