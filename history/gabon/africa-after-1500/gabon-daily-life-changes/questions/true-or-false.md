# Gabon Daily Life Changes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History also lives in houses. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Gabonese daily life keeps changing shape: the traditional house of planks and beaten earth gives way in town to villas and apartment blocks, barter between villages gives way to money and markets, and canoes, feet and bush paths give way to roads and vehicles. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
