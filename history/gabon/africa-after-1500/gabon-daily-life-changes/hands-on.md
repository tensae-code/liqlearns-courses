# Hands-on — Gabon Daily Life Changes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Mime three journeys: paddling a canoe, walking a path, riding in a car. Then mime paying twice: handing over goods, then handing over coins. This lesson is about how everyday life itself changed in Gabon.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Gabonese daily life keeps changing shape: the traditional house of planks and beaten earth gives way in town to villas and apartment blocks, barter between villages gives way to money and markets, and canoes, feet and bush paths give way to roads and vehicles. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
