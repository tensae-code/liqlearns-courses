# Lesson plan — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** explorers like Du Chaillu push into a forest Europeans barely know, photographing gorillas and carrying the interior's image back to the world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the explorers found an empty land → the forest was known and lived in by Gabon's peoples; the explorers mapped it for Europe, not for the people who lived there.
- Go back to the one thing: explorers like Du Chaillu push into a forest Europeans barely know, photographing gorillas and carrying the interior's image back to the world. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
