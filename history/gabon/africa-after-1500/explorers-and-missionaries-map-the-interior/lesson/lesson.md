# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

## Do it first

Push imaginary branches aside and step into a dark green forest, then lift an imaginary camera. This lesson follows the men who first carried Gabon's interior to outside eyes.

The coast was traded on for centuries while the interior stayed, to Europeans, a green blank. Then come the explorers of Gabon. Du Chaillu travels into the forest and does something new: he photographs gorillas, fixing the forest's most famous animal in an image the whole world can see. Expedition by expedition, the interior is mapped and described — for European readers. The lesson holds both truths: the explorers were brave and curious, and the land they 'discovered' had been home, known path by path, to Gabon's peoples all along.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: explorers like Du Chaillu push into a forest Europeans barely know, photographing gorillas and carrying the interior's image back to the world. Give one real example from your own home, school or town, and name the lesson's key words: explorer, interior. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
