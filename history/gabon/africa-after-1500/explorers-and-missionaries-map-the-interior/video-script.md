# Video script — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Explorers like Du Chaillu push into a forest Europeans barely know, photographing gorillas and carrying the interior's image back to the world.

The coast was traded on for centuries while the interior stayed, to Europeans, a green blank. Then come the explorers of Gabon. Du Chaillu travels into the forest and does something new: he photographs gorillas, fixing the forest's most famous animal in an image the whole world can see. Expedition by expedition, the interior is mapped and described — for European readers. The lesson holds both truths: the explorers were brave and curious, and the land they 'discovered' had been home, known path by path, to Gabon's peoples all along.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
