# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 21 Les explorateurs du Gabon": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The coast was traded on for centuries while the interior stayed, to Europeans, a green blank. Then come the explorers of Gabon. Du Chaillu travels into the forest and does something new: he photographs gorillas, fixing the forest's most famous animal in an image the whole world can see. Expedition by expedition, the interior is mapped and described — for European readers. The lesson holds both truths: the explorers were brave and curious, and the land they 'discovered' had been home, known path by path, to Gabon's peoples all along.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Explorers like Du Chaillu push into a forest Europeans barely know, photographing gorillas and carrying the interior's image back to the world.
