# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 21 Les explorateurs du Gabon"?
a) The coast was traded on for centuries while the interior stayed, to Europeans, a green blank.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Explorers like Du Chaillu push into a forest Europeans barely know, photographing gorillas and carrying the interior's image back to the world.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
