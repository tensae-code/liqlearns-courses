# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The coast was traded on for centuries while the interior stayed, to Europeans, a green blank.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: explorers like Du Chaillu push into a forest Europeans barely know, photographing gorillas and carrying the interior's image back to the world.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
