# Hands-on — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Push imaginary branches aside and step into a dark green forest, then lift an imaginary camera. This lesson follows the men who first carried Gabon's interior to outside eyes.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: explorers like Du Chaillu push into a forest Europeans barely know, photographing gorillas and carrying the interior's image back to the world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
