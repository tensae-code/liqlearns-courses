# Gabon in French Congo and the AEF — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 36 Le Gabon dans le Congo français": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Under colonisation, Gabon stops being governed as Gabon. The lesson shows it folded into a larger colony, the Congo français — French Congo — and ruled from outside its own borders. Decisions for Gabonese land are taken in a capital that is not Gabon's, inside an administration where Gabon is one district of someone else's design. The country's name survives on the map; its separate rule does not.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders, then the colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa — the AÉF — with Gabon one territory among four.
