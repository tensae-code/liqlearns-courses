# Gabon in French Congo and the AEF — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under colonisation, Gabon stops being governed as Gabon. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders, then the colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa — the AÉF — with Gabon one territory among four. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
