# Lesson plan — Gabon in French Congo and the AEF

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Gabon in French Congo and the AEF
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders, then the colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa — the AÉF — with Gabon one territory among four.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
