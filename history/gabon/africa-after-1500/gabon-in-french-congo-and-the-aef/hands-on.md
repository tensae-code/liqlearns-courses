# Hands-on — Gabon in French Congo and the AEF

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take four small pieces of paper and lay them inside one big sheet. Label the small ones; the big sheet rules them all. This lesson is about Gabon becoming one of those small pieces.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders, then the colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa — the AÉF — with Gabon one territory among four. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
