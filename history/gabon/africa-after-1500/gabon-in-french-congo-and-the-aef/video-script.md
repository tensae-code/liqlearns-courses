# Video script — Gabon in French Congo and the AEF

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders, then the colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa — the AÉF — with Gabon one territory among four.

Under colonisation, Gabon stops being governed as Gabon. The lesson shows it folded into a larger colony, the Congo français — French Congo — and ruled from outside its own borders. Decisions for Gabonese land are taken in a capital that is not Gabon's, inside an administration where Gabon is one district of someone else's design. The country's name survives on the map; its separate rule does not.

Then the design is redrawn bigger still. The French colonies of the equator are regrouped into one federation: l'Afrique équatoriale française, the AÉF — French Equatorial Africa. Gabon becomes one territory among four, administered together under a single colonial government. The lesson follows the change on the map: Congo français to AÉF, and Gabon smaller on the page each time — a territory inside a federation inside an empire.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
