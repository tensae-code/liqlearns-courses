# Video script — Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Colonial rule is organised to extract Africa's wealth for Europe's benefit — the exploitation coloniale under which Gabon and the continent labour.

What was colonial rule FOR? The lesson answers without softening: exploitation. Colonial rule in Africa — and in Gabon, named in the lesson's own title — is organised to extract the continent's wealth for Europe's benefit. The land's timber, rubber and minerals flow out through the ports; the labour is African; the profit is not. Roads and posts serve the extraction first. To understand the colony, the book says, follow the wealth — and see which way it sails.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
