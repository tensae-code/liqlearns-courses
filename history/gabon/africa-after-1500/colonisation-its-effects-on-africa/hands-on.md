# Hands-on — Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pretend to load a ship: timber, rubber, wealth of the land, box after box — all sailing away, nothing coming back. This lesson asks who colonial rule was organised FOR.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: colonial rule is organised to extract Africa's wealth for Europe's benefit — the exploitation coloniale under which Gabon and the continent labour. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
