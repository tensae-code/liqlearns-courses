# Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 29 L'exploitation coloniale en Afrique et au Gabon": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** What was colonial rule FOR? The lesson answers without softening: exploitation. Colonial rule in Africa — and in Gabon, named in the lesson's own title — is organised to extract the continent's wealth for Europe's benefit. The land's timber, rubber and minerals flow out through the ports; the labour is African; the profit is not. Roads and posts serve the extraction first. To understand the colony, the book says, follow the wealth — and see which way it sails.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Colonial rule is organised to extract Africa's wealth for Europe's benefit — the exploitation coloniale under which Gabon and the continent labour.
