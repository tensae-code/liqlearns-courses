# Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What was colonial rule FOR? The lesson answers without softening: exploitation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: colonial rule is organised to extract Africa's wealth for Europe's benefit — the exploitation coloniale under which Gabon and the continent labour.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
