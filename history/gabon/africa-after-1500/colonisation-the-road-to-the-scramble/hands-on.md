# Hands-on — Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Sign an imaginary paper, then watch it turn into a chain laid across a map. This lesson is about how agreements became rule.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: European powers, needing raw materials and markets, move from coast to conquest: in Gabon, treaties between Europeans and Gabonese peoples slide into direct foreign rule. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
