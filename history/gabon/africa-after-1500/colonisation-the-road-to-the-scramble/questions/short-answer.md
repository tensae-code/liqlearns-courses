# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 29 La colonisation": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The lesson gives the turn its name: la colonisation. Treaties between Europeans and Gabonese peoples — papers signed for trade and friendship — slide, step by step, into direct foreign rule. What was agreed between rulers becomes command over subjects. Gabon passes under a foreign administration, and decisions about Gabonese land and life begin to be made in the coloniser's interest. The slide, not a single blow, is how the colony comes.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** European powers, needing raw materials and markets, move from coast to conquest: in Gabon, treaties between Europeans and Gabonese peoples slide into direct foreign rule.
