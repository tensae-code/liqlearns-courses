# Hands-on — Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk in place, faster and faster, while flags on an imaginary wall fall one by one. This lesson is the march — the whole continent moving the way Gabon moved.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: after 1945 the demands for autonomy grow across the continent until the flags fall — Africa's march to independence, the road Gabon walks to 1960. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
