# Video script — Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
After 1945 the demands for autonomy grow across the continent until the flags fall — Africa's march to independence, the road Gabon walks to 1960.

The last history lesson of the primary books widens to the whole continent's march. After 1945, the demands for autonomy grow across Africa — colony by colony, the pressure builds, the powers yield ground, and until the flags of the empires fall and the flags of free African states rise in their place. Ghana's, Kenya's and the southern countries' roads are the famous paths of this march; Gabon's own road, told in this course's independence lesson, is walked inside the same movement. The continent does not receive independence; it marches to it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
