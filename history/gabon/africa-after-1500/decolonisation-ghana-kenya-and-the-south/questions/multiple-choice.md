# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 46 La marche de l'Afrique vers l'indépendance"?
a) The last history lesson of the primary books widens to the whole continent's march.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) After 1945 the demands for autonomy grow across the continent until the flags fall — Africa's march to independence, the road Gabon walks to 1960.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
