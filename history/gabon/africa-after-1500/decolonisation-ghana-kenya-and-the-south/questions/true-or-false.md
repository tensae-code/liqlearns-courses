# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The last history lesson of the primary books widens to the whole continent's march.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: after 1945 the demands for autonomy grow across the continent until the flags fall — Africa's march to independence, the road Gabon walks to 1960.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
