# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 46 La marche de l'Afrique vers l'indépendance": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The last history lesson of the primary books widens to the whole continent's march. After 1945, the demands for autonomy grow across Africa — colony by colony, the pressure builds, the powers yield ground, and until the flags of the empires fall and the flags of free African states rise in their place. Ghana's, Kenya's and the southern countries' roads are the famous paths of this march; Gabon's own road, told in this course's independence lesson, is walked inside the same movement. The continent does not receive independence; it marches to it.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** After 1945 the demands for autonomy grow across the continent until the flags fall — Africa's march to independence, the road Gabon walks to 1960.
