# Gabon Independence 1960 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The youngest children's book ends Gabon's long story under French rule with its turning point: independence. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: after years inside French rule the colony unwinds step by step — Aubame is elected, Léon Mba becomes prime minister, and at 23:58 on 16 August 1960 independence is proclaimed and Gabon raises its own flag. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
