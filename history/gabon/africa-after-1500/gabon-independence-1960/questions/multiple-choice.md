# Gabon Independence 1960 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 32 L'indépendance du Gabon"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The youngest children's book ends Gabon's long story under French rule with its turning point: independence.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) After years inside French rule the colony unwinds step by step — Aubame is elected, Léon Mba becomes prime minister, and at 23:58 on 16 August 1960 independence is proclaimed and Gabon raises its own flag.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
