# Gabon Independence 1960 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 32 L'indépendance du Gabon": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The youngest children's book ends Gabon's long story under French rule with its turning point: independence. After years inside French rule, Gabon raises its own flag in 1960 and rules itself. The lesson keeps it simple and proud — a country that was governed from outside becomes a country governed by its own people, and the flag is the sign of the change.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** After years inside French rule the colony unwinds step by step — Aubame is elected, Léon Mba becomes prime minister, and at 23:58 on 16 August 1960 independence is proclaimed and Gabon raises its own flag.
