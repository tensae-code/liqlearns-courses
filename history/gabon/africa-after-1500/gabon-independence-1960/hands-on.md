# Hands-on — Gabon Independence 1960

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Raise an imaginary flag slowly while you count: colony, steps, flag. This lesson walks the steps Gabon took to its own flag — and the exact night it rose.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: after years inside French rule the colony unwinds step by step — Aubame is elected, Léon Mba becomes prime minister, and at 23:58 on 16 August 1960 independence is proclaimed and Gabon raises its own flag. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
