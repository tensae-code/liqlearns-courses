# France in Gabon and the Founding of Libreville — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 29 Les débuts de l'installation française au Gabon": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** First come the ships: French vessels on the estuary, trading and treating with the peoples of the shore. Then come the papers: treaties signed between French officers and Gabonese rulers. And then the turn — what began as visits becomes an installation, a permanent French settlement planted on the estuary. The lesson marks this as the moment Gabon's story bends: a foreign power is no longer passing by; it has moved in.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** French ships and treaties turn into a permanent settlement on the estuary, and a settlement of freed slaves becomes Libreville — the free town that grows into Gabon's capital.
