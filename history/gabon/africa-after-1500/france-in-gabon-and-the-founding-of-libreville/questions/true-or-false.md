# France in Gabon and the Founding of Libreville — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** First come the ships: French vessels on the estuary, trading and treating with the peoples of the shore. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: French ships and treaties turn into a permanent settlement on the estuary, and a settlement of freed slaves becomes Libreville — the free town that grows into Gabon's capital. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
