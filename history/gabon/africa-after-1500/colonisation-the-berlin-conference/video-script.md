# Video script — Colonisation: The Berlin Conference

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
In Berlin, far from Africa, the European powers agree the rules for dividing Africa between them — a continent partitioned on paper by people who did not live there.

In Berlin, the capital of a European power, the powers sit down together — and agree the rules for dividing Africa between them. Who may claim what coast, how a claim is made good, how the continent is to be partitioned: all of it settled at a table in Europe, on a map, by men who did not live inside the lines they drew. No African ruler sits at that table. The lesson is short and cold: much of the map Gabon and its neighbours still live in was drawn in that room.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
