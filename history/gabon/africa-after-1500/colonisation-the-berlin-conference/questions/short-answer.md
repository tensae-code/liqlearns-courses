# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 34 La conférence de Berlin": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** In Berlin, the capital of a European power, the powers sit down together — and agree the rules for dividing Africa between them. Who may claim what coast, how a claim is made good, how the continent is to be partitioned: all of it settled at a table in Europe, on a map, by men who did not live inside the lines they drew. No African ruler sits at that table. The lesson is short and cold: much of the map Gabon and its neighbours still live in was drawn in that room.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** In Berlin, far from Africa, the European powers agree the rules for dividing Africa between them — a continent partitioned on paper by people who did not live there.
