# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 34 La conférence de Berlin"?
a) In Berlin, the capital of a European power, the powers sit down together — and agree the rules for dividing Africa between them.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) In Berlin, far from Africa, the European powers agree the rules for dividing Africa between them — a continent partitioned on paper by people who did not live there.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
