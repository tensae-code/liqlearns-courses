# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Berlin, the capital of a European power, the powers sit down together — and agree the rules for dividing Africa between them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: in Berlin, far from Africa, the European powers agree the rules for dividing Africa between them — a continent partitioned on paper by people who did not live there.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
