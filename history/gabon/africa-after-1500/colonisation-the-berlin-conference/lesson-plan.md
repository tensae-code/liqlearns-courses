# Lesson plan — Colonisation: The Berlin Conference

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Colonisation: The Berlin Conference
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Wider World and the Road to Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in Berlin, far from Africa, the European powers agree the rules for dividing Africa between them — a continent partitioned on paper by people who did not live there.
