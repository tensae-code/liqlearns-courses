# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 34 La traite négrière (2)": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The second slave-trade lesson draws the map. The trade runs in a triangle across the Atlantic: from Europe, trade goods sail to Africa's coasts; from Africa, captives are shipped across the ocean — the crossing that kills so many; from the Americas, the produce of plantations worked by the enslaved sails back to Europe. Three continents are chained into one system, and every side of the triangle feeds the others. The map explains the machine; the next book counts what the machine did to people.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The triangular trade is mapped — goods, captives, plantations — across three continents, and the lesson counts the human cost: people who are not free, bought and shipped.
