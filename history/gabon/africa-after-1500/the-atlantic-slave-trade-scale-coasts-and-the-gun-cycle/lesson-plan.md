# Lesson plan — The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the triangular trade is mapped — goods, captives, plantations — across three continents, and the lesson counts the human cost: people who are not free, bought and shipped.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
