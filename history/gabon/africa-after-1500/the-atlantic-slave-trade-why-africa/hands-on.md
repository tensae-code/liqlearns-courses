# Hands-on — The Atlantic Slave Trade: Why Africa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand still and ask the lesson's own question out loud: 'Why Africa?' Do not answer yet. This lesson defines the slave trade first, then faces the question honestly.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the lesson defines the slave trade — the traite négrière — and asks the hard question directly: why did Africa's coasts become its hunting ground? Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
