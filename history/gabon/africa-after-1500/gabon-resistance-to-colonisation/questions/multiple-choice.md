# Gabon Resistance to Colonisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 41 La résistance à la colonisation"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The colony was never simply accepted.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) From the first treaties, Gabonese people refuse French domination — around King Denis's court and beyond, and in named risings: Emane Ntole, Nyonda Makita (Mavurulu), Mbombè and the Binzima movement.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
