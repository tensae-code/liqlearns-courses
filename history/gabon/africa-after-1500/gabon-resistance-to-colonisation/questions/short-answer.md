# Gabon Resistance to Colonisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 41 La résistance à la colonisation": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The colony was never simply accepted. From the first treaties, people around King Denis's court — the great Gabonese ruler of the estuary — and beyond refuse French domination. Refusal takes many forms: papers not honoured, orders not obeyed, authority defended against the newcomers' claims. The lesson plants resistance at the very start of the colonial story, not at its end: as long as there is colonisation in Gabon, there is Gabonese resistance beside it.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From the first treaties, Gabonese people refuse French domination — around King Denis's court and beyond, and in named risings: Emane Ntole, Nyonda Makita (Mavurulu), Mbombè and the Binzima movement.
