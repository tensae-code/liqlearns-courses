# Gabon Resistance to Colonisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The colony was never simply accepted. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: from the first treaties, Gabonese people refuse French domination — around King Denis's court and beyond, and in named risings: Emane Ntole, Nyonda Makita (Mavurulu), Mbombè and the Binzima movement. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
