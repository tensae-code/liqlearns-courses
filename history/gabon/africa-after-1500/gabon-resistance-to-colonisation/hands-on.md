# Hands-on — Gabon Resistance to Colonisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Raise a hand and say 'no' — then say the names in this lesson one by one. Resistance here is not an idea; it has names.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: from the first treaties, Gabonese people refuse French domination — around King Denis's court and beyond, and in named risings: Emane Ntole, Nyonda Makita (Mavurulu), Mbombè and the Binzima movement. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
