# Lesson plan — African Resistance to Colonisation

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** African Resistance to Colonisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Wider World and the Road to Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** across the whole continent, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders — Gabon's resistance is one front of an African refusal.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Wider World and the Road to Independence — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: across the whole continent, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders — Gabon's resistance is one front of an African refusal.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Gabon and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.

- Thinks resistance was one country's affair → the lesson is continental: peoples across Africa resist, with arms and with arguments.
- Go back to the one thing: across the whole continent, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders — Gabon's resistance is one front of an African refusal. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
