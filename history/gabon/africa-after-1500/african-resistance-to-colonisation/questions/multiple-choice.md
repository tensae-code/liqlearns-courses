# African Resistance to Colonisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 27 Les résistances à la colonisation en Afrique"?
a) Gabon's resistance, met in the lesson before, was not alone.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Across the whole continent, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders — Gabon's resistance is one front of an African refusal.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
