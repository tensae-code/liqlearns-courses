# African Resistance to Colonisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 27 Les résistances à la colonisation en Afrique": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Gabon's resistance, met in the lesson before, was not alone. This lesson widens to the whole continent: across Africa, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders. Kings refuse, armies fight, communities argue their rights in the colonisers' own languages and courts. The forms differ from place to place; the refusal is the same. Colonisation, the book teaches, was contested everywhere it went — Africa resisted as a continent, not only village by village.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Across the whole continent, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders — Gabon's resistance is one front of an African refusal.
