# African Resistance to Colonisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gabon's resistance, met in the lesson before, was not alone.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: across the whole continent, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders — Gabon's resistance is one front of an African refusal.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
