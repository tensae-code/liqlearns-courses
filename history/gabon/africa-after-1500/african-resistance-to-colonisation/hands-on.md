# Hands-on — African Resistance to Colonisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the names of three places far apart on an imaginary map, and for each one mime a raised shield. This lesson widens Gabon's 'no' to a continent's.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: across the whole continent, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders — Gabon's resistance is one front of an African refusal. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
