# Video script — African Resistance to Colonisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Across the whole continent, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders — Gabon's resistance is one front of an African refusal.

Gabon's resistance, met in the lesson before, was not alone. This lesson widens to the whole continent: across Africa, peoples take up arms and arguments against the invaders. Kings refuse, armies fight, communities argue their rights in the colonisers' own languages and courts. The forms differ from place to place; the refusal is the same. Colonisation, the book teaches, was contested everywhere it went — Africa resisted as a continent, not only village by village.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
