# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first contacts happen on the beach. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Gabon's coastal communities meet European ships and trade begins on the beach; the small coastal posts and forts planted for economic reasons harden into permanent footholds — the beginning of a new, unequal relationship. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
