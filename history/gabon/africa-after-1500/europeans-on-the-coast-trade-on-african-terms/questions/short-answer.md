# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 19 Les premiers contacts avec les Européens": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The first contacts happen on the beach. Gabon's coastal communities — peoples who know the sea and trade already — meet the European ships that anchor off their shore. Goods are laid out, terms are struck, and trade begins. At this stage the meeting is between traders: the ships need what the coast has, and the coast sets much of the terms. It is the opening scene of a relationship that will not stay equal.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Gabon's coastal communities meet European ships and trade begins on the beach; the small coastal posts and forts planted for economic reasons harden into permanent footholds — the beginning of a new, unequal relationship.
