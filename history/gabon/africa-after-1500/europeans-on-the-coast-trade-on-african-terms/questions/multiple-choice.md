# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 19 Les premiers contacts avec les Européens"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The first contacts happen on the beach.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Gabon's coastal communities meet European ships and trade begins on the beach; the small coastal posts and forts planted for economic reasons harden into permanent footholds — the beginning of a new, unequal relationship.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
