# Gabon's First Peoples and Migrations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gabon was not empty waiting to be discovered. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years: its first peoples — the autochthones, called pygmées in the books — hold the forest first, then other populations arrive, settle and mix, and over centuries the mixtures make the Gabonese people; even the country's name comes from this meeting of worlds, from the Portuguese gabão. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
