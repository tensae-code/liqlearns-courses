# Gabon's First Peoples and Migrations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: the first peoples of a land, there before all later arrivals. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** autochthones

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: the movement of a people who come to settle in a new land. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** migration
