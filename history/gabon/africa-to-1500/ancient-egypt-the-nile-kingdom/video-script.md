# Video script — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
On the Nile a great kingdom rises in Africa's north-east — pharaohs, pyramids and scribes — one of the oldest civilisations on earth, and an African one.

The final primary year widens out from Gabon to the great old worlds — and it starts in Africa. On the Nile, in the continent's north-east, a kingdom rises that will last thousands of years: Egypt. Its kings are the pharaohs; its monuments are the pyramids, still standing; its memory is kept by scribes, the trained writers whose signs cover its walls. The lesson gives Egypt first place in the wider story on purpose: before Greece, before Rome, Africa's Nile kingdom is already great.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
