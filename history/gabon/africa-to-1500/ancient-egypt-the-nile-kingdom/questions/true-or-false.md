# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The final primary year widens out from Gabon to the great old worlds — and it starts in Africa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: on the Nile a great kingdom rises in Africa's north-east — pharaohs, pyramids and scribes — one of the oldest civilisations on earth, and an African one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
