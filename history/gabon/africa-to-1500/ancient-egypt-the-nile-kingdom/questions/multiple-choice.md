# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 1 L'Égypte antique (1)"?
a) The final primary year widens out from Gabon to the great old worlds — and it starts in Africa.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) On the Nile a great kingdom rises in Africa's north-east — pharaohs, pyramids and scribes — one of the oldest civilisations on earth, and an African one.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
