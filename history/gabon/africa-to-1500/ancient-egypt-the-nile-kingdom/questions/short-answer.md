# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 1 L'Égypte antique (1)": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The final primary year widens out from Gabon to the great old worlds — and it starts in Africa. On the Nile, in the continent's north-east, a kingdom rises that will last thousands of years: Egypt. Its kings are the pharaohs; its monuments are the pyramids, still standing; its memory is kept by scribes, the trained writers whose signs cover its walls. The lesson gives Egypt first place in the wider story on purpose: before Greece, before Rome, Africa's Nile kingdom is already great.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** On the Nile a great kingdom rises in Africa's north-east — pharaohs, pyramids and scribes — one of the oldest civilisations on earth, and an African one.
