# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Cup your hands like a river valley, then stack three blocks into a pyramid on the table. This lesson travels to the Nile, where Africa built one of the world's first great kingdoms.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: on the Nile a great kingdom rises in Africa's north-east — pharaohs, pyramids and scribes — one of the oldest civilisations on earth, and an African one. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
