# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 10 Les premiers hommes": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Deep in the earth, archaeologists brush the soil from ancient bones. The bones are not of kings or farmers — they are of the very first people, humankind's first ancestors, dated millions of years back. And the ground that gives them up is African ground. The lesson plants the flag of the whole human story here: before there were countries, languages or towns, the first people walked in Africa. Every later story in this course — and every people's story on earth — grows out of this beginning.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Humankind begins in Africa: archaeologists brush ancient bones from the earth and date the first ancestors millions of years back, and those early people move, spreading across the continent.
