# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 10 Les premiers hommes"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Deep in the earth, archaeologists brush the soil from ancient bones.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Humankind begins in Africa: archaeologists brush ancient bones from the earth and date the first ancestors millions of years back, and those early people move, spreading across the continent.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
