# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Deep in the earth, archaeologists brush the soil from ancient bones. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: humankind begins in Africa: archaeologists brush ancient bones from the earth and date the first ancestors millions of years back, and those early people move, spreading across the continent. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
