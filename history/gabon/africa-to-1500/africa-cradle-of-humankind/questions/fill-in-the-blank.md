# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In this lesson, ____ means: a person who digs up and studies what the earliest people left behind. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** archaeologist

**2.** In this lesson, ____ means: one of the first people, from whom later people descend. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** ancestor
