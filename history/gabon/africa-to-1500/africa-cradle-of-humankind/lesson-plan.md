# Lesson plan — Africa, Cradle of Humankind

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Africa, Cradle of Humankind
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and Gabon's First Peoples (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** humankind begins in Africa: archaeologists brush ancient bones from the earth and date the first ancestors millions of years back, and those early people move, spreading across the continent.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and Gabon's First Peoples — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
