# The Bantu Migrations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Into Gabon come the Bantous — Bantu-speaking newcomers moving into the forest country. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Bantu-speaking peoples spread across central Africa carrying farming, iron and languages, and in Gabon they meet the first peoples — and the two worlds negotiate, trade and intermarry. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
