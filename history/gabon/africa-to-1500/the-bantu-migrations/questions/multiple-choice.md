# The Bantu Migrations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 22 Les relations entre les premiers peuples du Gabon et les Bantous"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Into Gabon come the Bantous — Bantu-speaking newcomers moving into the forest country.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Bantu-speaking peoples spread across central Africa carrying farming, iron and languages, and in Gabon they meet the first peoples — and the two worlds negotiate, trade and intermarry.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
