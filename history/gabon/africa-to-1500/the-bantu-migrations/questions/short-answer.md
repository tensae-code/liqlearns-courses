# The Bantu Migrations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 22 Les relations entre les premiers peuples du Gabon et les Bantous": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Into Gabon come the Bantous — Bantu-speaking newcomers moving into the forest country. They meet the first peoples who are already there. What happens next is not one thing: the two worlds negotiate where each will live, they trade what each has and the other wants, and they intermarry, so that families themselves become the meeting place. Gabon is changed by the encounter — new languages, new ways in the forest — and the newcomers are changed by the land and its first people too.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Bantu-speaking peoples spread across central Africa carrying farming, iron and languages, and in Gabon they meet the first peoples — and the two worlds negotiate, trade and intermarry.
