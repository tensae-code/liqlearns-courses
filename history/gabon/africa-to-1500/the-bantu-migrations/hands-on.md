# Hands-on — The Bantu Migrations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk slowly across the room carrying three imaginary things: a bundle of seed, an iron tool, and a word in a new language. Set them down in a 'new land'. This lesson is about the peoples who carried exactly those three things across central Africa.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Bantu-speaking peoples spread across central Africa carrying farming, iron and languages, and in Gabon they meet the first peoples — and the two worlds negotiate, trade and intermarry. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
