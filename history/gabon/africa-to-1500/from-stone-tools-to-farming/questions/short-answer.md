# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 13 Le mode de vie des premiers hommes": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** How did the first people live, day by day? They shelter where they can — a cave mouth, a screen of branches. They hunt and gather what the land gives. And they make tools: a stone struck against a stone becomes a blade, and the blade changes everything — meat is cut, skins are scraped, wood is worked. The lesson's point is the chain: each invention changes daily life, and a people with better tools lives differently from the people before them.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Each invention changes daily life: the first people shelter, hunt and make tools, tame fire, and at last stop wandering — they plant, herd, and the first villages appear.
