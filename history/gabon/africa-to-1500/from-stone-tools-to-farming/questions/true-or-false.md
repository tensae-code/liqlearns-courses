# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** How did the first people live, day by day? They shelter where they can — a cave mouth, a screen of branches. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: each invention changes daily life: the first people shelter, hunt and make tools, tame fire, and at last stop wandering — they plant, herd, and the first villages appear. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
