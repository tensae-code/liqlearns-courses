# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 13 Le mode de vie des premiers hommes"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) How did the first people live, day by day? They shelter where they can — a cave mouth, a screen of branches.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Each invention changes daily life: the first people shelter, hunt and make tools, tame fire, and at last stop wandering — they plant, herd, and the first villages appear.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
