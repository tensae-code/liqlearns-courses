# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 4 L'Égypte antique (2): la religion et les sciences"?
a) What did Egypt give the world? The second Egypt lesson answers with its priests and scholars.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Egyptian priests and scholars map the stars, count the flood and prepare for the afterlife — a civilisation of religion and sciences whose knowledge served the kingdom and outlived it.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
