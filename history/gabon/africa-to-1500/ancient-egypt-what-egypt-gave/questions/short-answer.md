# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 4 L'Égypte antique (2): la religion et les sciences": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** What did Egypt give the world? The second Egypt lesson answers with its priests and scholars. They map the stars and order the calendar by them. They count the Nile's flood, year by year, so the kingdom can farm by its rhythm. And their religion — the gods, the temples, the careful preparation for the afterlife — drives the learning: to serve eternity, Egypt studies the sky and the river. Religion and sciences grow together in this civilisation, and its knowledge outlives its kings.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Egyptian priests and scholars map the stars, count the flood and prepare for the afterlife — a civilisation of religion and sciences whose knowledge served the kingdom and outlived it.
