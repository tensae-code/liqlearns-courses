# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What did Egypt give the world? The second Egypt lesson answers with its priests and scholars.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Egyptian priests and scholars map the stars, count the flood and prepare for the afterlife — a civilisation of religion and sciences whose knowledge served the kingdom and outlived it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
