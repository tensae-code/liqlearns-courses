# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

## Do it first

Look up and point to three 'stars', then pretend to measure a rising river with a stick. This lesson is about the Egyptians who first did those things carefully — and wrote them down.

What did Egypt give the world? The second Egypt lesson answers with its priests and scholars. They map the stars and order the calendar by them. They count the Nile's flood, year by year, so the kingdom can farm by its rhythm. And their religion — the gods, the temples, the careful preparation for the afterlife — drives the learning: to serve eternity, Egypt studies the sky and the river. Religion and sciences grow together in this civilisation, and its knowledge outlives its kings.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Egyptian priests and scholars map the stars, count the flood and prepare for the afterlife — a civilisation of religion and sciences whose knowledge served the kingdom and outlived it. Give one real example from your own home, school or town, and name the lesson's key words: afterlife, flood. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
