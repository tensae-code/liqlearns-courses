# Video script — Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Egyptian priests and scholars map the stars, count the flood and prepare for the afterlife — a civilisation of religion and sciences whose knowledge served the kingdom and outlived it.

What did Egypt give the world? The second Egypt lesson answers with its priests and scholars. They map the stars and order the calendar by them. They count the Nile's flood, year by year, so the kingdom can farm by its rhythm. And their religion — the gods, the temples, the careful preparation for the afterlife — drives the learning: to serve eternity, Egypt studies the sky and the river. Religion and sciences grow together in this civilisation, and its knowledge outlives its kings.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
