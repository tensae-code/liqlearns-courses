# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look up and point to three 'stars', then pretend to measure a rising river with a stick. This lesson is about the Egyptians who first did those things carefully — and wrote them down.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Egyptian priests and scholars map the stars, count the flood and prepare for the afterlife — a civilisation of religion and sciences whose knowledge served the kingdom and outlived it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
