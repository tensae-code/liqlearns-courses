# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rome begins as one city among many.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Rome grows from one city into an empire ringing the whole Mediterranean — the largest single power the ancient world in this course has seen.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
