# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 9 La Rome antique"?
a) Rome begins as one city among many.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Rome grows from one city into an empire ringing the whole Mediterranean — the largest single power the ancient world in this course has seen.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
