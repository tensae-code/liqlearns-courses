# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 9 La Rome antique": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Rome begins as one city among many. Then, century by century, it grows — conquering, absorbing, organising — until the whole Mediterranean is ringed by a single empire and the sea itself is a Roman lake. Roads run out from the one city to every province; one law and one army hold lands that had been dozens of states. The lesson closes the ancient trio — Egypt, Greece, Rome — with the ancient world's largest lesson in power: how a city becomes an empire, and what an empire does to everyone inside it.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Rome grows from one city into an empire ringing the whole Mediterranean — the largest single power the ancient world in this course has seen.
