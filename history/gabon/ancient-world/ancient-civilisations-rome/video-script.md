# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Rome grows from one city into an empire ringing the whole Mediterranean — the largest single power the ancient world in this course has seen.

Rome begins as one city among many. Then, century by century, it grows — conquering, absorbing, organising — until the whole Mediterranean is ringed by a single empire and the sea itself is a Roman lake. Roads run out from the one city to every province; one law and one army hold lands that had been dozens of states. The lesson closes the ancient trio — Egypt, Greece, Rome — with the ancient world's largest lesson in power: how a city becomes an empire, and what an empire does to everyone inside it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
