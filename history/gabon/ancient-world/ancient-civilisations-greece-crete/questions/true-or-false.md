# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Across the Mediterranean from Egypt, another world takes shape: Greece.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Greek city-states, gathered around the Mediterranean, invent new ways of ruling and thinking that the world still uses.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
