# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 7 La Grèce antique": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** Across the Mediterranean from Egypt, another world takes shape: Greece. It is not one kingdom but many city-states, each a city ruling itself, competing and trading around the sea. And the Greeks invent something new under the sun: ways of ruling and thinking that are argued about openly — citizens debating how a city should be governed, thinkers asking how the world works and refusing the first answer. The lesson places Greece after Egypt deliberately: younger than the Nile kingdom, and the inventor of questions Europe still lives by.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Greek city-states, gathered around the Mediterranean, invent new ways of ruling and thinking that the world still uses.
