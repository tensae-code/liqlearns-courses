# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Greek city-states, gathered around the Mediterranean, invent new ways of ruling and thinking that the world still uses.

Across the Mediterranean from Egypt, another world takes shape: Greece. It is not one kingdom but many city-states, each a city ruling itself, competing and trading around the sea. And the Greeks invent something new under the sun: ways of ruling and thinking that are argued about openly — citizens debating how a city should be governed, thinkers asking how the world works and refusing the first answer. The lesson places Greece after Egypt deliberately: younger than the Nile kingdom, and the inventor of questions Europe still lives by.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
