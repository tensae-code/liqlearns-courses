# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Hold up fingers one by one and name them as separate small cities, each with its own rulers — then make them argue and vote. This lesson is about the Greeks, who turned ruling itself into an argument.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Greek city-states, gathered around the Mediterranean, invent new ways of ruling and thinking that the world still uses. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
