# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Wider World and the Road to Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Greek city-states, gathered around the Mediterranean, invent new ways of ruling and thinking that the world still uses.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Wider World and the Road to Independence — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. 1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Greek city-states, gathered around the Mediterranean, invent new ways of ruling and thinking that the world still uses.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the Gabon they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Gabon and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.

- Go back to the one thing: Greek city-states, gathered around the Mediterranean, invent new ways of ruling and thinking that the world still uses. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
