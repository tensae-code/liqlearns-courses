# Hands-on — The First World War: Versailles and the Results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Carry an imaginary heavy load on your shoulders a long way, then set it down in an empty village. This lesson counts what the war cost — and who paid.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the war ends with Germany losing its colonies and prices crashing — and Africa's soldiers and porters pay heavily for a war that was not theirs. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
