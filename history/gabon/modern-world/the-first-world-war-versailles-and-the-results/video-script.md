# Video script — The First World War: Versailles and the Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The war ends with Germany losing its colonies and prices crashing — and Africa's soldiers and porters pay heavily for a war that was not theirs.

The war ends, and the settlement redraws the world. Germany, defeated, loses its colonies — they are handed to the victors, their peoples consulted no more than before. Prices crash in the disorder that follows. And the book insists on the African count: Africa's soldiers fought in the empires' armies, and Africa's porters carried the war on their backs, and they pay heavily — in lives, in labour, in lands traded over their heads. The consequences of the First World War are African consequences too.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
