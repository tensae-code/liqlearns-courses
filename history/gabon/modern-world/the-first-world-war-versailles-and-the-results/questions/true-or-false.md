# The First World War: Versailles and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war ends, and the settlement redraws the world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: the war ends with Germany losing its colonies and prices crashing — and Africa's soldiers and porters pay heavily for a war that was not theirs.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
