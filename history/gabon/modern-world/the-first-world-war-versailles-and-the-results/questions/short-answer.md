# The First World War: Versailles and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 39 Les conséquences de la Première Guerre mondiale": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The war ends, and the settlement redraws the world. Germany, defeated, loses its colonies — they are handed to the victors, their peoples consulted no more than before. Prices crash in the disorder that follows. And the book insists on the African count: Africa's soldiers fought in the empires' armies, and Africa's porters carried the war on their backs, and they pay heavily — in lives, in labour, in lands traded over their heads. The consequences of the First World War are African consequences too.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The war ends with Germany losing its colonies and prices crashing — and Africa's soldiers and porters pay heavily for a war that was not theirs.
