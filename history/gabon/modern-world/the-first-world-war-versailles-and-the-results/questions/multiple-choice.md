# The First World War: Versailles and the Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 39 Les conséquences de la Première Guerre mondiale"?
a) The war ends, and the settlement redraws the world.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) The war ends with Germany losing its colonies and prices crashing — and Africa's soldiers and porters pay heavily for a war that was not theirs.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
