# Video script — Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
In the 15th and 16th centuries European sailors push down the African coast and around the world, in search of routes and riches — the voyages that first bring European ships to Gabon's shore.

In the 15th and 16th centuries, Europe's sailors take to the oceans in earnest. Portuguese ships push down the African coast cape by cape — it is on this push that Gabon's river mouth gets its gabão name — and other voyages reach around the whole world. The motive is named plainly: routes and riches. Whoever finds a sea route to the goods of the East, and to Africa's own wealth, holds the trade of the age. These voyages open the door through which everything else in this level — trade, posts, colonies — will walk.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
