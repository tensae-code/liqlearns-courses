# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 17 Les grands voyages maritimes des 15e et 16e siècles"?
a) In the 15th and 16th centuries, Europe's sailors take to the oceans in earnest.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) In the 15th and 16th centuries European sailors push down the African coast and around the world, in search of routes and riches — the voyages that first bring European ships to Gabon's shore.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
