# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the 15th and 16th centuries, Europe's sailors take to the oceans in earnest.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: in the 15th and 16th centuries European sailors push down the African coast and around the world, in search of routes and riches — the voyages that first bring European ships to Gabon's shore.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
