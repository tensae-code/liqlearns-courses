# Hands-on — Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Trace a line down an imaginary coastline with your finger, then around a whole round world. This lesson follows the sailors who first drew that line.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: in the 15th and 16th centuries European sailors push down the African coast and around the world, in search of routes and riches — the voyages that first bring European ships to Gabon's shore. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
