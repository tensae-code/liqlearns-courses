# Lesson plan — Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes

**Course:** History — Gabon · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in the 15th and 16th centuries European sailors push down the African coast and around the world, in search of routes and riches — the voyages that first bring European ships to Gabon's shore.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ships, Trade, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
