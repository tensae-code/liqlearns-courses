# Hands-on — The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Mime rubble being cleared, then a new building rising, then a crowd raising its hands and voices. This lesson is what the world did with the war's end.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the war ends, the United Nations is born, and colonial peoples demand their freedom — the war's end becomes the colonies' beginning. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
