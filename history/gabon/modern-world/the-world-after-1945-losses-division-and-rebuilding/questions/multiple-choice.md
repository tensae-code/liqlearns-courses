# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 43 Les conséquences de la Deuxième Guerre mondiale"?
a) The second war ends in ruin — and in two beginnings.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) The war ends, the United Nations is born, and colonial peoples demand their freedom — the war's end becomes the colonies' beginning.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
