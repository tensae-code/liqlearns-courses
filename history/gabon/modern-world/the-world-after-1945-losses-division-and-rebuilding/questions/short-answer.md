# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 43 Les conséquences de la Deuxième Guerre mondiale": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The second war ends in ruin — and in two beginnings. The nations, determined that the world needs a common house, found the United Nations. And across the colonial world, peoples who carried the empires' wars draw the conclusion the empires did not intend: if freedom was worth fighting for in Europe, it is owed in Africa and Asia too. The demands for freedom rise everywhere at once. The road to Gabon's 1960 — and to the whole continent's independences — starts in this lesson, in 1945.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The war ends, the United Nations is born, and colonial peoples demand their freedom — the war's end becomes the colonies' beginning.
