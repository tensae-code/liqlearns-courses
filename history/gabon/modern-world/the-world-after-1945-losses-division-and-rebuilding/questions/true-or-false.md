# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The second war ends in ruin — and in two beginnings.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: the war ends, the United Nations is born, and colonial peoples demand their freedom — the war's end becomes the colonies' beginning.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
