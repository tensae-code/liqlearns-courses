# Video script — The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The war ends, the United Nations is born, and colonial peoples demand their freedom — the war's end becomes the colonies' beginning.

The second war ends in ruin — and in two beginnings. The nations, determined that the world needs a common house, found the United Nations. And across the colonial world, peoples who carried the empires' wars draw the conclusion the empires did not intend: if freedom was worth fighting for in Europe, it is owed in Africa and Asia too. The demands for freedom rise everywhere at once. The road to Gabon's 1960 — and to the whole continent's independences — starts in this lesson, in 1945.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
