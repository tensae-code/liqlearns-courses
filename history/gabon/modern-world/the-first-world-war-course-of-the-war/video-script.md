# Video script — The First World War: Course of the War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Europe's empires go to war in 1914 and the fighting fixes into trenches — a war of the empires that reaches into their colonies.

In 1914 the empires of Europe go to war with each other. The armies march expecting a short war; instead the fighting fixes into trenches — lines of dug earth facing each other across ruined ground, where men live, fight and die for years over small strips of land. And because the fighters are empires, the war does not stay in Europe: it reaches into the colonies, drawing in African soldiers and African land. Gabon's book teaches the war as the empires' war — with the colonies inside it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
