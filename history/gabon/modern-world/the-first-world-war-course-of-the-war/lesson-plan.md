1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Europe's empires go to war in 1914 and the fighting fixes into trenches — a war of the empires that reaches into their colonies.

- Go back to the one thing: Europe's empires go to war in 1914 and the fighting fixes into trenches — a war of the empires that reaches into their colonies. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
