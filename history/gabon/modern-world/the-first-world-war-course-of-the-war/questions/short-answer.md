# The First World War: Course of the War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 37 La Première Guerre mondiale": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** In 1914 the empires of Europe go to war with each other. The armies march expecting a short war; instead the fighting fixes into trenches — lines of dug earth facing each other across ruined ground, where men live, fight and die for years over small strips of land. And because the fighters are empires, the war does not stay in Europe: it reaches into the colonies, drawing in African soldiers and African land. Gabon's book teaches the war as the empires' war — with the colonies inside it.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Europe's empires go to war in 1914 and the fighting fixes into trenches — a war of the empires that reaches into their colonies.
