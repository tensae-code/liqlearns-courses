# The First World War: Course of the War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1914 the empires of Europe go to war with each other.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: Europe's empires go to war in 1914 and the fighting fixes into trenches — a war of the empires that reaches into their colonies.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
