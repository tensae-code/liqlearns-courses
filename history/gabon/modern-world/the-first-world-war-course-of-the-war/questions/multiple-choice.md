# The First World War: Course of the War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 37 La Première Guerre mondiale"?
a) In 1914 the empires of Europe go to war with each other.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Europe's empires go to war in 1914 and the fighting fixes into trenches — a war of the empires that reaches into their colonies.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
