# Hands-on — The First World War: Course of the War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Dig an imaginary trench with a spade, then crouch in it and wait. On the other side of an imaginary field, another trench waits. This lesson is how a war became those two waiting lines.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Europe's empires go to war in 1914 and the fighting fixes into trenches — a war of the empires that reaches into their colonies. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
