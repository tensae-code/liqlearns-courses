# Hands-on — The Second World War: Course in Europe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw one line of trenches, rub it out, then draw a whole map darkening country by country. This lesson is the second time in one lifetime that Europe's war covers the world.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a second world war engulfs Europe a generation later — and reaches into the colonies again. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
