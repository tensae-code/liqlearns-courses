# The Second World War: Course in Europe

## Do it first

Draw one line of trenches, rub it out, then draw a whole map darkening country by country. This lesson is the second time in one lifetime that Europe's war covers the world.

A generation after the first, a second world war engulfs Europe. Country after country is drawn in; the fighting spreads across the continent and beyond it. And again — the lesson marks the repetition — the war reaches into the colonies: the empires draw once more on their colonised peoples for soldiers, labour and materials. For Gabon, inside French Equatorial Africa, Europe's second war is also an African experience, arriving through the empire that rules it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a second world war engulfs Europe a generation later — and reaches into the colonies again. Give one real example from your own home, school or town, and name the lesson's key words: Second World War, engulf. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
