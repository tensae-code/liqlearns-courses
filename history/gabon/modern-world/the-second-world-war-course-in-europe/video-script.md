# Video script — The Second World War: Course in Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
A second world war engulfs Europe a generation later — and reaches into the colonies again.

A generation after the first, a second world war engulfs Europe. Country after country is drawn in; the fighting spreads across the continent and beyond it. And again — the lesson marks the repetition — the war reaches into the colonies: the empires draw once more on their colonised peoples for soldiers, labour and materials. For Gabon, inside French Equatorial Africa, Europe's second war is also an African experience, arriving through the empire that rules it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
