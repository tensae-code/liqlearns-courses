# The Second World War: Course in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A generation after the first, a second world war engulfs Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: a second world war engulfs Europe a generation later — and reaches into the colonies again.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
