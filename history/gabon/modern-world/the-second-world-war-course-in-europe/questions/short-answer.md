# The Second World War: Course in Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 40 La Deuxième Guerre mondiale": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** A generation after the first, a second world war engulfs Europe. Country after country is drawn in; the fighting spreads across the continent and beyond it. And again — the lesson marks the repetition — the war reaches into the colonies: the empires draw once more on their colonised peoples for soldiers, labour and materials. For Gabon, inside French Equatorial Africa, Europe's second war is also an African experience, arriving through the empire that rules it.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A second world war engulfs Europe a generation later — and reaches into the colonies again.
