# The Second World War: Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 40 La Deuxième Guerre mondiale"?
a) A generation after the first, a second world war engulfs Europe.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) A second world war engulfs Europe a generation later — and reaches into the colonies again.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
