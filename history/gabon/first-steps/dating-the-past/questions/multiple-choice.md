# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 7 Le siècle, le millénaire et l'époque"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) How long is the past? The lesson teaches children to count it.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The past is counted from a fixed starting point: years gather into centuries and millennia, writing divides prehistory from history, and the great periods stand in order on a timeline.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
