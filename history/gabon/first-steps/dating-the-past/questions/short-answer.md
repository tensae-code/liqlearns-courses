# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 7 Le siècle, le millénaire et l'époque": who, where, and what turned? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** How long is the past? The lesson teaches children to count it. Years are gathered into bigger measures: a century is a hundred years, a millennium is a thousand. Counting starts from a fixed starting point and runs forward and back, so that an event can be given its place — this many centuries ago, in that millennium. With these measures, the huge stretch of the past stops being a blur and becomes something a child can hold: a line with places on it.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The past is counted from a fixed starting point: years gather into centuries and millennia, writing divides prehistory from history, and the great periods stand in order on a timeline.
