# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** How long is the past? The lesson teaches children to count it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: the past is counted from a fixed starting point: years gather into centuries and millennia, writing divides prehistory from history, and the great periods stand in order on a timeline. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
