# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 1 Définir l'Histoire"?
a) The lesson opens with a photograph: the first Moon landing, people standing where no one had stood before.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) History begins with a question about an event: something happened, and we keep the past because what happened still matters — a photograph of the first Moon landing opens the lesson and asks what an event is and why the past is kept.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
