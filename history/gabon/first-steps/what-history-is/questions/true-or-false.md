# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The lesson opens with a photograph: the first Moon landing, people standing where no one had stood before.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: history begins with a question about an event: something happened, and we keep the past because what happened still matters — a photograph of the first Moon landing opens the lesson and asks what an event is and why the past is kept.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
