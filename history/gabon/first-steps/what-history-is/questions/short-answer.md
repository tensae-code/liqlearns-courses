# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of the part "Leçon 1 Définir l'Histoire": who, where, and what turned?
**Answer:** The lesson opens with a photograph: the first Moon landing, people standing where no one had stood before. Before any dates or names, the class asks the founding question — what is an event? An event is something that happened, at a place, at a time, to people. And why do we keep it? Because the past explains the present: who we are comes from what happened before us. History, the lesson says, is the past kept and questioned — not forgotten, and not just believed, but asked about until it can be told truly.

**2.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** History begins with a question about an event: something happened, and we keep the past because what happened still matters — a photograph of the first Moon landing opens the lesson and asks what an event is and why the past is kept.
