# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
History begins with a question about an event: something happened, and we keep the past because what happened still matters — a photograph of the first Moon landing opens the lesson and asks what an event is and why the past is kept.

The lesson opens with a photograph: the first Moon landing, people standing where no one had stood before. Before any dates or names, the class asks the founding question — what is an event? An event is something that happened, at a place, at a time, to people. And why do we keep it? Because the past explains the present: who we are comes from what happened before us. History, the lesson says, is the past kept and questioned — not forgotten, and not just believed, but asked about until it can be told truly.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in your town.
