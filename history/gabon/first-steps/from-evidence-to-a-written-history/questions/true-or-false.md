# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nobody was there to write down most of the past, so how can a history be written at all? The lesson answers: the past left witnesses behind.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: before any story is told, the past is questioned: objects and traces left behind are treated like witnesses, asked what they are, who made them, and what they can prove.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that one thing.
