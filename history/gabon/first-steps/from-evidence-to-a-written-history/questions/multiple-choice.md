# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part "Leçon 4 Les sources de l'Histoire"?
a) Nobody was there to write down most of the past, so how can a history be written at all? The lesson answers: the past left witnesses behind.  b) Nothing at all happens, and the past is forgotten  c) The story is set in a place this lesson never visits  d) The lesson refuses to name any person or place
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Before any story is told, the past is questioned: objects and traces left behind are treated like witnesses, asked what they are, who made them, and what they can prove.  b) That the past cannot be known at all  c) That only rulers matter in history  d) That Gabon's story begins with its colonisers
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
