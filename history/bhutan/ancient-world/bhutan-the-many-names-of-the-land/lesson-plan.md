# Lesson plan — Bhutan: The Many Names of the Land

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Bhutan: The Many Names of the Land
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** One land, many names, each one a chapter: Lhomon, the southern land of darkness before Buddhism; the four-gates land; Menjong, land of medicinal herbs; the land of cypresses; until Druk Yul, the Thunder Dragon land, and Bhutan, the high land, give it its lasting names.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 5.2 Different names given to our country** — The Class IV book introduces the land through its names, and each name is a small history.
2. **Chapter Two, Ancient Names** — The Class VII book turns the same list into a scholar's exercise — toponymy, the study of place-names.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: One land, many names, each one a chapter: Lhomon, the southern land of darkness before Buddhism; the four-gates land; Menjong, land of medicinal herbs; the land of cypresses; until Druk Yul, the Thunder Dragon land, and Bhutan, the high land, give it its lasting names.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: your own town's older name, still used by grandparents — a name is a fossil of how people once saw the place.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
