# Bhutan: The Many Names of the Land — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Lhomon was the land's name before Buddhism, spoken of as a land in darkness.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Toponymy is the study of temple architecture.
**Answer: False** — Toponymy is the study of place-names.
