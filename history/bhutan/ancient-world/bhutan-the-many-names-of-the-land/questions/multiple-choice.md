# Bhutan: The Many Names of the Land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does Druk Yul mean?
a) the Thunder Dragon land  b) the high land  c) the land of medicinal herbs  d) the four-gates land
**Answer: a) the Thunder Dragon land** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Who fixed 'Bhutan' as a name distinct from Tibet in the late 1700s?
a) Songtsen Gampo  b) George Bogle  c) Ashley Eden  d) Captain John Jones
**Answer: b) George Bogle** — That is what this lesson teaches.
