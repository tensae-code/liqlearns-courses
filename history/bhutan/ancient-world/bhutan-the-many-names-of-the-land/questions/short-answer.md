# Bhutan: The Many Names of the Land — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two of Bhutan's old names and what they mean.
**Answer:** Any two: Lhomon — the southern land in darkness before Buddhism; Menjong — land of medicinal herbs; Lho Mon Kha Zhi — the southern Mon land of the four gateways; Druk Yul — the Thunder Dragon land.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do the books treat names as evidence?
**Answer:** Because each name records who was looking at the land and what they saw in it — names are a record of the country's changing story.
