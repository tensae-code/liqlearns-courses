# Oral Tradition and the Earliest Stories — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does a spoken tale become fixed in a landscape?
**Answer:** Its events attach to places, and the place-names — like Changra and Bubja — keep retelling the story.

**2.** Who was Drimed Kunden?
**Answer:** An exiled prince whose extreme generosity on the road through Trongsa — giving his eye and his children — became Bhutan's famous oral tale.
