# Oral Tradition and the Earliest Stories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Prince Drimed Kunden give away on the road through Trongsa?
a) his crown and palace  b) his eye to a blind man, and his children to strangers  c) his horse and sword only  d) nothing — he refused all beggars
**Answer: b) his eye to a blind man, and his children to strangers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the monk Tonpa's 5th-century journey hint?
a) that Buddhism may have reached Bhutan before Tibet  b) that writing began in Bhutan  c) that Trongsa was founded then  d) that the tale of Drimed Kunden is false
**Answer: a) that Buddhism may have reached Bhutan before Tibet** — That is what this lesson teaches.
