# Oral Tradition and the Earliest Stories — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The place-names Changra and Bubja still carry the Drimed Kunden tale.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Oral tradition counts for nothing in Bhutan's history books.
**Answer: False** — The books treat oral culture as history's first archive — evidence fixed in tales and place-names.
