# Oral Tradition and the Earliest Stories — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Before writing, Bhutan's past travelled mouth to ear: the tale of exiled Prince Drimed Kunden, who gave his eye and his children away on the road through Trongsa, still lives in the place-names Changra and Bubja — and the monk Tonpa's 5th-century journey hints Buddhism reached Bhutan before Tibet. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
