# Lesson plan — Oral Tradition and the Earliest Stories

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Oral Tradition and the Earliest Stories
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before writing, Bhutan's past travelled mouth to ear: the tale of exiled Prince Drimed Kunden, who gave his eye and his children away on the road through Trongsa, still lives in the place-names Changra and Bubja — and the monk Tonpa's 5th-century journey hints Buddhism reached Bhutan before Tibet.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (recommended, never forced).
