# Zorig Chusum: The Thirteen Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many traditional arts make up Zorig Chusum?
a) seven  b) thirteen  c) nine  d) twenty-one
**Answer: b) thirteen** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Thangtong Gyalpo forge from Bhutanese ore?
a) eight iron-chain bridges  b) a royal crown  c) temple bells only  d) iron ploughs for every valley
**Answer: a) eight iron-chain bridges** — That is what this lesson teaches.
