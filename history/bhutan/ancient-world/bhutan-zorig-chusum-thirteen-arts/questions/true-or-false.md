# Zorig Chusum: The Thirteen Arts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Khandro Sonam Peldon was a master weaver of the 13th century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The thirteen arts grew out of factory industry.
**Answer: False** — They grew out of Buddhist and pre-Buddhist practice, carried by master craftsmen.
