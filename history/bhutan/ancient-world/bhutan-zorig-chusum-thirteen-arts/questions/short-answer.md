# Zorig Chusum: The Thirteen Arts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does 'carried by master craftsmen' mean for the thirteen arts?
**Answer:** The skills pass hand to hand, master to learner, through practice — not from books or factories.

**2.** Name the two masters the books name for the thirteen arts.
**Answer:** Khandro Sonam Peldon, the weaver, and Thangtong Gyalpo, builder of the iron-chain bridges.
