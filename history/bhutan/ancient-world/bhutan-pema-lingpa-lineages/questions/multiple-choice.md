# Pema Lingpa's Lineages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many treasures did Pema Lingpa reveal, as the Class IX book counts?
a) thirty-two  b) five  c) thirteen  d) one hundred
**Answer: a) thirty-two** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Whom do the Peling lineages supply, in the books' telling?
a) only temple painters  b) Bhutan's later rulers — down to Jigme Namgyal and the Wangchuck kings  c) foreign merchants  d) no one of importance
**Answer: b) Bhutan's later rulers — down to Jigme Namgyal and the Wangchuck kings** — That is what this lesson teaches.
