# Pema Lingpa's Lineages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What are the Peling lineages?
**Answer:** Pema Lingpa's descendant families, whose line supplies Bhutan's later rulers.

**2.** How does Pema Lingpa connect to the Wangchuck kings?
**Answer:** By descent: his Peling lineage reaches Jigme Namgyal, ancestor of the Wangchuck dynasty.
