# Pema Lingpa's Lineages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Dungkar Choeje family is one of the Peling lineages.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pema Lingpa's lineages died out within two generations.
**Answer: False** — They run from Sumthrang Choeje to Dungkar Choeje and supply rulers centuries later.
