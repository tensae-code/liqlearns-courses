# Pema Lingpa's Lineages — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The tertön who revealed thirty-two treasures leaves more than texts: his Peling lineages — from Sumthrang Choeje to the Dungkar Choeje family — supply Bhutan's later rulers, linking the 15th-century treasure revealer directly to Jigme Namgyal and the Wangchuck kings. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
