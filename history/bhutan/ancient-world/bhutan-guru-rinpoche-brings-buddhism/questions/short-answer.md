# Guru Rinpoche Brings Buddhism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why did Guru Rinpoche hide treasures in Bhutan?
**Answer:** So that discoverers of later ages would find the teachings when their time came.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name two of Guru Rinpoche's sacred places (ney) from the lesson.
**Answer:** Taktshang above Paro and Singye Dzong (also Kurjey and Nabji).
