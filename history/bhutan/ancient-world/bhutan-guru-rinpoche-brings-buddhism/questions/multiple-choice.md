# Guru Rinpoche Brings Buddhism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Whom did Guru Rinpoche heal in Bumthang?
a) the king of Tibet  b) the sick king, by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo  c) a wounded general  d) he healed no one
**Answer: b) the sick king, by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a beyul, as the books use the word?
a) a hidden sanctuary land blessed and seeded with Guru Rinpoche's treasures  b) a fortress tax  c) a type of temple bell  d) a border market
**Answer: a) a hidden sanctuary land blessed and seeded with Guru Rinpoche's treasures** — That is what this lesson teaches.
