# Guru Rinpoche Brings Buddhism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Guru Rinpoche left his body-print at Kurjey in Bumthang.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Guru Rinpoche was born in Bhutan and never travelled.
**Answer: False** — He was the lotus-born master invited from outside — called by Bumthang's ailing king around 750 in the Class VIII telling.
