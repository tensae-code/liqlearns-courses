# Hands-on — Guru Rinpoche Brings Buddhism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A king is sick, and no local cure works — so the court sends far away for a master said to be born from a lotus. Picture the messenger's road. This lesson follows the master who answers the call.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The lotus-born Padmasambhava — Guru Rinpoche — enters 8th-century Bhutan, heals the sick king of Bumthang by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo, leaves his body-print at Kurjey, hides treasures for later discoverers, and leaves Bhutan itself a beyul, a hidden sanctuary land. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
