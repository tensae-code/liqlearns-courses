# Lesson plan — Guru Rinpoche Brings Buddhism

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Guru Rinpoche Brings Buddhism
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The lotus-born Padmasambhava — Guru Rinpoche — enters 8th-century Bhutan, heals the sick king of Bumthang by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo, leaves his body-print at Kurjey, hides treasures for later discoverers, and leaves Bhutan itself a beyul, a hidden sanctuary land.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (recommended, never forced).
