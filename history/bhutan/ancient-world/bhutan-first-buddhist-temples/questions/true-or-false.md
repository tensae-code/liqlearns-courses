# The First Buddhist Temples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The books take Songtsen Gampo's temple-building as the start of Bhutanese recorded history.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Kyichu Lhakhang stands in Bumthang.
**Answer: False** — Kyichu Lhakhang stands in Paro; Jampa Lhakhang stands in Bumthang.
