# The First Buddhist Temples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Who built Jampa Lhakhang and Kyichu Lhakhang?
a) Songtsen Gampo, king of Tibet  b) Guru Rinpoche  c) the Zhabdrung  d) Pema Lingpa
**Answer: a) Songtsen Gampo, king of Tibet** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Class VIII telling, where does Jampa Lhakhang sit on the demoness?
a) on her heart  b) on her left knee  c) on her right hand  d) on her forehead
**Answer: b) on her left knee** — That is what this lesson teaches.
