# The First Buddhist Temples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the Early Diffusion?
**Answer:** The first spreading of Buddhism in Bhutan, beginning with Songtsen Gampo's 7th-century temples.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which later temples were added around the first two?
**Answer:** The thadul and yangdul temples, added by later kings around Songtsen Gampo's network.
