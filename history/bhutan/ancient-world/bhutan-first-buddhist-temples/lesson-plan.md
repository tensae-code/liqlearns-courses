# Lesson plan — The First Buddhist Temples

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The First Buddhist Temples
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo (c.605–650) builds Jampa Lhakhang in Bumthang and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro — and Bhutan's books take that 7th-century temple-building as the start of recorded history, the Early Diffusion of Buddhism.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (recommended, never forced).
