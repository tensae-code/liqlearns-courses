# The Dzong System Begins — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The first fortified monastic dzong rises at Chelkha in Paro in 1194 under Nyo Gyalwa Lhanangpa of the Lhapa Kagyu — but the experiment fades when Phajo Drukgom Zhigpo brings the Drukpa Kagyu in 1222, and dzongs wait four centuries for their revival. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
