# The Dzong System Begins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where and when did the first dzong rise?
a) at Chelkha in Paro, in 1194  b) at Punakha, in 1637  c) at Trongsa, in 1543  d) at Thimphu, in 1216
**Answer: a) at Chelkha in Paro, in 1194** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who brought the Drukpa Kagyu to Bhutan in 1222?
a) Nyo Gyalwa Lhanangpa  b) Phajo Drukgom Zhigpo  c) Songtsen Gampo  d) Pema Lingpa
**Answer: b) Phajo Drukgom Zhigpo** — That is what this lesson teaches.
