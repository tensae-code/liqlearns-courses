# The Dzong System Begins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who built the first dzong, and of which tradition?
**Answer:** Nyo Gyalwa Lhanangpa of the Lhapa Kagyu, at Chelkha in Paro in 1194.

**2.** Why did the dzong idea fade after 1194?
**Answer:** The Lhapa moment passed when Phajo Drukgom Zhigpo brought the Drukpa Kagyu in 1222, and dzong-building slept for four centuries.
