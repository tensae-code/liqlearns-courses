# The Dzong System Begins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A dzong joins two jobs in one building: fortress and monastery.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Dzongs were built continuously from 1194 onward without a break.
**Answer: False** — After Chelkha the experiment faded, and dzongs waited four centuries for revival.
