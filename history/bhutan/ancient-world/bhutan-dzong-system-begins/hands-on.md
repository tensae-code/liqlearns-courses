# Hands-on — The Dzong System Begins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture a building that is half fortress, half monastery, on a ridge above Paro — in the year 1194, centuries before the famous dzongs. Who builds the first one, and why does the idea then sleep for four hundred years? This lesson tells.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The first fortified monastic dzong rises at Chelkha in Paro in 1194 under Nyo Gyalwa Lhanangpa of the Lhapa Kagyu — but the experiment fades when Phajo Drukgom Zhigpo brings the Drukpa Kagyu in 1222, and dzongs wait four centuries for their revival. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
