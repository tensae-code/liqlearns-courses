# Pema Lingpa: Terma and Legacy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In 1476 a ragged monk in a dream hands Pema Lingpa a scroll, and he becomes one of the five King Tertöns — revealing earth treasures and mind treasures, including the Bardo Thoedrol — while his painting, architecture and ironwork shape Bhutan's identity. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
