# Hands-on — Pema Lingpa: Terma and Legacy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A dream hands you a scroll, and you wake knowing where something precious lies hidden. In 1476 that dream comes to Pema Lingpa. This lesson follows what he digs up — and what he builds.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In 1476 a ragged monk in a dream hands Pema Lingpa a scroll, and he becomes one of the five King Tertöns — revealing earth treasures and mind treasures, including the Bardo Thoedrol — while his painting, architecture and ironwork shape Bhutan's identity. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
