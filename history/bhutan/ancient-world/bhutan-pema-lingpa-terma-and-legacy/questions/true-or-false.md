# Pema Lingpa: Terma and Legacy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Bardo Thoedrol is among Pema Lingpa's revealed treasures.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pema Lingpa revealed treasures but made nothing himself.
**Answer: False** — He was also a painter, architect and ironworker whose work shaped Bhutan's identity.
