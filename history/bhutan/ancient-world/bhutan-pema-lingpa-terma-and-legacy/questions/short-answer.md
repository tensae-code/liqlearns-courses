# Pema Lingpa: Terma and Legacy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What rank does Pema Lingpa hold among treasure revealers?
**Answer:** One of the five King Tertöns — the supreme treasure revealers.

**2.** Name the two kinds of terma and one famous revealed text.
**Answer:** Sater (earth treasures) and gongter (mind treasures); the Bardo Thoedrol.
