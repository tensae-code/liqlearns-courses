# Pema Lingpa: Terma and Legacy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened in 1476?
a) Pema Lingpa was born  b) a ragged monk in a dream handed Pema Lingpa a scroll, opening his treasure-revealing work  c) he built his first bridge  d) he left Bhutan for Tibet
**Answer: b) a ragged monk in a dream handed Pema Lingpa a scroll, opening his treasure-revealing work** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What are sater and gongter?
a) earth treasures and mind treasures — the two kinds of terma  b) two kinds of iron  c) two festivals  d) two valleys in Bumthang
**Answer: a) earth treasures and mind treasures — the two kinds of terma** — That is what this lesson teaches.
