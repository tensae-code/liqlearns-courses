# Video script — Pema Lingpa: Terma and Legacy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
In 1476 a ragged monk in a dream hands Pema Lingpa a scroll, and he becomes one of the five King Tertöns — revealing earth treasures and mind treasures, including the Bardo Thoedrol — while his painting, architecture and ironwork shape Bhutan's identity.

The visions become discoveries. In 1476 a ragged monk appears in a dream and hands Pema Lingpa a scroll — the key to his life's work. He becomes one of the five King Tertöns, the supreme treasure revealers, drawing out terma of both kinds: sater, earth treasures hidden in rock and lake, and gongter, mind treasures revealed in vision. Among his revelations stands the Bardo Thoedrol, the famous guide for the dead. And the revealer is also a maker — his painting, his architecture and his ironwork shape Bhutan's identity to this day. The blacksmith's grandson does not just find Bhutan's treasures; he adds to them.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
