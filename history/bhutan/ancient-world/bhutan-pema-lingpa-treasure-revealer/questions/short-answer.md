# Pema Lingpa: The Treasure Revealer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What family did Pema Lingpa come from?
**Answer:** A blacksmith's family — he was a blacksmith's grandson from Chel in Bumthang.

**2.** What is a tertön?
**Answer:** A treasure revealer — one who finds the teachings Guru Rinpoche hid for later ages.
