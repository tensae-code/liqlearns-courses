# Pema Lingpa: The Treasure Revealer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where and when was Pema Lingpa born?
a) at Chel in Bumthang, in 1450  b) at Paro, in 1350  c) at Punakha, in 1550  d) at Trongsa, in 1450
**Answer: a) at Chel in Bumthang, in 1450** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which skills did Pema Lingpa teach himself?
a) painting, dance and song  b) Tibetan, smithery and carpentry  c) law, taxes and war  d) medicine and astrology only
**Answer: b) Tibetan, smithery and carpentry** — That is what this lesson teaches.
