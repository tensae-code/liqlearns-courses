# Pema Lingpa: The Treasure Revealer — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pema Lingpa was prophesied as the rebirth of Princess Pema Sel.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pema Lingpa's visions began at Kurjey temple.
**Answer: False** — His visions began at Mani Gonpa.
