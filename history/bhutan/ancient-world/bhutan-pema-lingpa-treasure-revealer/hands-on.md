# Hands-on — Pema Lingpa: The Treasure Revealer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A blacksmith's grandson in a Bumthang village teaches himself to read, to forge and to build — with no school and no master. Then the visions begin. This lesson tells how Pema Lingpa starts.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Prophesied by Guru Padmasambhava as the rebirth of Princess Pema Sel, a blacksmith's grandson born at Chel in Bumthang in 1450 teaches himself Tibetan, smithery and carpentry — and begins to have visions at Mani Gonpa. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
