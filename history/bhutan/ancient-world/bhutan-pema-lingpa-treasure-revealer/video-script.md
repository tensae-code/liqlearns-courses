# Video script — Pema Lingpa: The Treasure Revealer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Prophesied by Guru Padmasambhava as the rebirth of Princess Pema Sel, a blacksmith's grandson born at Chel in Bumthang in 1450 teaches himself Tibetan, smithery and carpentry — and begins to have visions at Mani Gonpa.

In 1450, at Chel in Bumthang, a boy is born into a blacksmith's family — and, the books say, into a prophecy: Guru Padmasambhava himself had foretold him as the rebirth of Princess Pema Sel. Nothing about his start is grand. Pema Lingpa teaches himself — Tibetan letters, the smith's craft, carpentry — skill by skill, with his own hands. Then, at Mani Gonpa, the visions begin: the first stirrings of the gift that will make him a tertön, a revealer of hidden treasures. Bhutan's greatest religious figure of the age begins as a village craftsman who would not stop learning.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
