# Video script — World Religions: The Five Main Faiths

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Bhutan's youngest learners meet the Buddha inside the world's great faiths: Prince Siddhartha of Lumbini, who saw old age, sickness and death beyond his palace walls, left home to seek an end to suffering — and found that desire causes it, the truth behind the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path.

Inside its survey of the world's main faiths, Bhutan's Class IV book tells Buddhism's founding story as a story. Prince Siddhartha is born at Lumbini and raised sheltered inside palace walls — until the day he goes out and sees what the palace hid: old age, sickness and death. The shock becomes a question that will not let him go: why is there suffering, and can it end? He leaves the palace to seek the answer, and under the tree he grasps it — desire is what causes suffering. From that insight flow Buddhism's core teachings, the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path, the road the Buddha taught for ending suffering. This is the faith that, in the next lessons, comes over the mountains into Bhutan itself.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
