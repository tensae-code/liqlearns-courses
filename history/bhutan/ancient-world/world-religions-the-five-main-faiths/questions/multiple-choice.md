# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What three sights outside the palace shocked Prince Siddhartha?
a) gold, armies and ships  b) old age, sickness and death  c) floods, fire and famine  d) temples, monks and festivals
**Answer: b) old age, sickness and death** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Buddha grasp under the tree as the cause of suffering?
a) desire  b) poverty  c) old age itself  d) bad weather
**Answer: a) desire** — That is what this lesson teaches.
