# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did Siddhartha leave the palace?
**Answer:** Seeing old age, sickness and death, he went to seek an end to suffering.

**2.** Name the two core teachings that follow from his insight.
**Answer:** The Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path.
