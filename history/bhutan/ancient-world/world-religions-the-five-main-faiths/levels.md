# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Bhutan's youngest learners meet the Buddha inside the world's great faiths: Prince Siddhartha of Lumbini, who saw old age, sickness and death beyond his palace walls, left home to seek an end to suffering — and found that desire causes it, the truth behind the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
