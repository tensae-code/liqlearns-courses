# Hands-on — World Religions: The Five Main Faiths

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine being kept inside a beautiful palace where nothing sad is ever shown to you — then one day seeing sickness and old age for the first time. What would you do? A prince once faced exactly that. This lesson tells what he did.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Bhutan's youngest learners meet the Buddha inside the world's great faiths: Prince Siddhartha of Lumbini, who saw old age, sickness and death beyond his palace walls, left home to seek an end to suffering — and found that desire causes it, the truth behind the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
