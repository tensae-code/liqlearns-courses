# World Religions: The Five Main Faiths

## Do it first

Imagine being kept inside a beautiful palace where nothing sad is ever shown to you — then one day seeing sickness and old age for the first time. What would you do? A prince once faced exactly that. This lesson tells what he did.

Inside its survey of the world's main faiths, Bhutan's Class IV book tells Buddhism's founding story as a story. Prince Siddhartha is born at Lumbini and raised sheltered inside palace walls — until the day he goes out and sees what the palace hid: old age, sickness and death. The shock becomes a question that will not let him go: why is there suffering, and can it end? He leaves the palace to seek the answer, and under the tree he grasps it — desire is what causes suffering. From that insight flow Buddhism's core teachings, the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path, the road the Buddha taught for ending suffering. This is the faith that, in the next lessons, comes over the mountains into Bhutan itself.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Bhutan's youngest learners meet the Buddha inside the world's great faiths: Prince Siddhartha of Lumbini, who saw old age, sickness and death beyond his palace walls, left home to seek an end to suffering — and found that desire causes it, the truth behind the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path. Give one real example: a person who leaves a comfortable life to answer a hard question about suffering — the prince's road in ordinary clothes, and name the lesson's key words: Siddhartha, Lumbini, Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, suffering. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
