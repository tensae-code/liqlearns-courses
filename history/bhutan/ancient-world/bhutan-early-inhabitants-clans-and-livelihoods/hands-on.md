# Hands-on — Early Inhabitants: Clans and Livelihoods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at a map of Bhutan's valleys in your mind: each one walled off by ridges. Imagine living where the next valley is a hard day's climb away. How would your people organise themselves? This lesson answers with clans.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Cut off valley by valley, early Bhutanese lived in clan settlements under chieftains — herding yaks to winter pasture, learning farming and tseri shifting cultivation, then crafts and barter, and finally trade with Tibet and India. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
