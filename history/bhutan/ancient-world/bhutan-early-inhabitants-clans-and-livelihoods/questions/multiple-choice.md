# Early Inhabitants: Clans and Livelihoods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How were early Bhutanese organised?
a) in clan settlements under chieftains, valley by valley  b) in one united kingdom  c) in large cities  d) under a single emperor
**Answer: a) in clan settlements under chieftains, valley by valley** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is tseri?
a) a yak breed  b) shifting cultivation — clearing, cropping and resting land in turn  c) a kind of barter coin  d) a clan ceremony
**Answer: b) shifting cultivation — clearing, cropping and resting land in turn** — That is what this lesson teaches.
