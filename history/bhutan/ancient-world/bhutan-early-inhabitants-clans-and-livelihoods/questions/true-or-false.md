# Early Inhabitants: Clans and Livelihoods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Early Bhutanese herded yaks down to winter pasture with the seasons.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Trade with Tibet and India came before farming in the books' order.
**Answer: False** — The order is herding, then farming and tseri, then crafts and barter, and finally trade with Tibet and India.
