# Early Inhabitants: Clans and Livelihoods — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did separate clan settlements form in early Bhutan?
**Answer:** Because the valleys are cut off from each other by the mountains, so each valley's people organised under their own chieftains.

**2.** List the livelihood steps in the books' order.
**Answer:** Yak herding, farming and tseri shifting cultivation, crafts and barter, then trade with Tibet and India.
