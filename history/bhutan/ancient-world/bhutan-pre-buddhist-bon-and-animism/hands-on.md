# Hands-on — Before Buddhism: Bon and Animism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand where you can see a mountain, a forest or a river. People in Bhutan once treated such places as powers to be honoured, not just scenery. Hold that thought — this lesson is about the faith that came before Buddhism.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Before Buddhism reached the Himalayas, people took refuge in mountains, forests and lakes, and animist and shamanist practice hardened into Bon — Black Bon with animal sacrifice, White Bon without — much of it surviving later as folk rite inside Buddhist Bhutan. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
