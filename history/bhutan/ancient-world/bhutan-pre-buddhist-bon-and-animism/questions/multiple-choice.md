# Before Buddhism: Bon and Animism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Himalayan people take refuge in before Buddhism?
a) mountains, forests and lakes  b) printed scriptures  c) stone fortresses  d) foreign kings
**Answer: a) mountains, forests and lakes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the difference between Black Bon and White Bon in the books?
a) Black Bon was older by a thousand years  b) Black Bon kept animal sacrifice; White Bon did without it  c) White Bon was practised only abroad  d) there is no difference
**Answer: b) Black Bon kept animal sacrifice; White Bon did without it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
