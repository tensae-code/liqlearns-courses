# Before Buddhism: Bon and Animism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the faith Bhutan's people held before Buddhism.
**Answer:** Bon, grown out of animist and shamanist practice that honoured spirits in nature.

**2.** Where can Bon's survival still be seen?
**Answer:** In folk rites inside Buddhist Bhutan — older offerings and practices carried on within the newer faith.
