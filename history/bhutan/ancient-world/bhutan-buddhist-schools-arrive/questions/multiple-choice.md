# The Buddhist Schools Arrive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why could so many Buddhist schools settle freely in Bhutan?
a) the king invited all of them at once  b) there was no central authority to stop them  c) they all arrived in one year  d) the valleys were empty
**Answer: b) there was no central authority to stop them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which tradition became Bhutan's state religion?
a) Drukpa Kagyu  b) Sakya  c) Geluk  d) Bon
**Answer: a) Drukpa Kagyu** — That is what this lesson teaches.
