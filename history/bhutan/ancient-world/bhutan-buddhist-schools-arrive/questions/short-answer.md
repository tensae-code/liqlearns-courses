# The Buddhist Schools Arrive — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the traditions that settled in Bhutan in this age.
**Answer:** Nyingma, Kagyu (including Drukpa Kagyu), Sakya and Geluk offshoots.

**2.** How did the contest between schools end?
**Answer:** Nyingma and Drukpa Kagyu absorbed or outlasted the rest, and Drukpa Kagyu became the state religion.
