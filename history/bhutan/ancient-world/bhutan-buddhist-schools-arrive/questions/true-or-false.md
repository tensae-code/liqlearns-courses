# The Buddhist Schools Arrive — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Buddhist schools arrived over roughly six centuries, from the 11th to the 17th.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Nyingma disappeared entirely when the other schools arrived.
**Answer: False** — Nyingma endures — it and Drukpa Kagyu absorb or outlast the rest.
