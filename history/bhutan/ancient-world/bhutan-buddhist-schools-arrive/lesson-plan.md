# Lesson plan — The Buddhist Schools Arrive

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Buddhist Schools Arrive
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Bhutan: Faith, Temples and Makers (CURRICULUM.md).
