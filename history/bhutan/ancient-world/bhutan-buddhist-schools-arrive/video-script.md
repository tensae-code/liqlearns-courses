# Video script — The Buddhist Schools Arrive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
From the 11th to the 17th century, with no central authority to stop them, Tibetan masters of the Nyingma, Kagyu, Sakya and Geluk traditions settle valley by valley — until Nyingma and Drukpa Kagyu absorb or outlast the rest, and Drukpa Kagyu becomes the state religion.

Between the first temples and the unifier lies a long, crowded age. From the 11th to the 17th century, Tibetan masters keep crossing into Bhutan — Nyingma, the old school; Kagyu in its many branches; Sakya; Geluk offshoots — and because no central authority rules the valleys, nobody can stop them: each settles where it finds welcome, valley by valley. Slowly the field narrows. Nyingma endures from the earliest days, and one Kagyu branch, the Drukpa Kagyu, absorbs or outlasts its rivals until it stands alone at the top — the tradition that becomes Bhutan's state religion, and the school of the Zhabdrung who is about to arrive.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
