# Lesson plan — Who Studies Prehistory

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Who Studies Prehistory
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: What History Is and What We Inherit (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Where no writing exists, Bhutan's first people are read from things: namchag stone adzes, household fossils and megaliths like the Nabji oath pillar, sorted into Stone, Bronze and Iron Age periods.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Where no writing exists, Bhutan's first people are read from things: namchag stone adzes, household fossils and megaliths like the Nabji oath pillar, sorted into Stone, Bronze and Iron Age periods.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a worn axe-head found in a field near home — nobody wrote about its maker, yet its shape and stone still tell a true story.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Bhutan, as the books themselves work.
