# Who Studies Prehistory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How is Bhutan's past before the 7th century known?
a) from things — stone tools, fossils and megaliths — because there are no written records  b) from royal chronicles  c) from newspapers  d) it cannot be known at all
**Answer: a) from things — stone tools, fossils and megaliths — because there are no written records** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What are namchag?
a) iron ploughs  b) polished stone adzes dated to about 2000–1500 BC  c) bronze coins  d) written scrolls
**Answer: b) polished stone adzes dated to about 2000–1500 BC** — That is what this lesson teaches.
