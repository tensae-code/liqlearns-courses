# Hands-on — What History Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down one thing that happened in your family long before you were born. Now write four questions beside it: what happened? when? why? how do you know? Those four questions are this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: History is the tool that explains what happened, when, why and how — the living past, rebuilt from sources — so the present can be understood and the future planned. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
