# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does history explain, as this lesson teaches?
a) only the names of kings  b) what happened, when, why and how  c) only future events  d) only stories nobody can check
**Answer: b) what happened, when, why and how** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can the same past have more than one honest interpretation?
a) because history is rebuilt from sources that must be weighed and interpreted  b) because historians may invent freely  c) because sources never matter  d) because dates change every year
**Answer: a) because history is rebuilt from sources that must be weighed and interpreted** — That is what this lesson teaches.
