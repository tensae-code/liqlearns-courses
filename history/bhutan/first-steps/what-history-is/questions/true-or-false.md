# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) History is only a list of dates to memorise.
**Answer: False** — The lesson is plain: history explains what happened, when, why and how, so the present can be understood.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) History is described in the books as the living past, rebuilt from sources.
**Answer: True**
