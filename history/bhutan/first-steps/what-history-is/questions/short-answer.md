# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What four questions does history answer about an event?
**Answer:** What happened, when it happened, why it happened, and how it happened.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is historiography?
**Answer:** The study of how histories are written — how people reconstruct and interpret the past from sources.
