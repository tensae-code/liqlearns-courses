# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one tangible and one intangible example of Bhutanese heritage.
**Answer:** Tangible: a dzong, a relic or a sacred site (nye). Intangible: a ritual, a song, an oral tradition, or driglam etiquette.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Why must each generation 'consciously continue' Bhutan's heritage?
**Answer:** Because the identities the Zhabdrung created live only while people keep practising them — heritage that is not carried on stops existing.
