# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which pair correctly splits heritage as the books teach it?
a) tangible — songs; intangible — dzongs  b) tangible — dzongs, relics, sacred sites; intangible — beliefs, rituals, songs, conduct  c) tangible — only gold objects; intangible — only dances  d) heritage cannot be split
**Answer: b) tangible — dzongs, relics, sacred sites; intangible — beliefs, rituals, songs, conduct** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Class IX teaching, what is tha-damtshig?
a) loyalty and trust, part of the inherited Bhutanese ethos  b) a kind of tax  c) a fortress design  d) a harvest festival
**Answer: a) loyalty and trust, part of the inherited Bhutanese ethos** — That is what this lesson teaches.
