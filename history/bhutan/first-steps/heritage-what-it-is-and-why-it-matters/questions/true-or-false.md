# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sacred sites like Aja and Gomphu-kora carry Guru Rinpoche's meditation story in their rocks and footprints.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Heritage in Bhutan's books is only about museum pieces, never daily conduct.
**Answer: False** — Driglam Choesum teaches heritage as daily conduct — cause and effect, loyalty and trust, and etiquette.
