# History — Bhutan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Bhutan's
own — built from Bhutan's Social Studies books for Classes
IV–VI and its *Bhutan History, Civics & Citizenship
Education* books for Classes VII–X (Royal Government of
Bhutan, English). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside Levels
1–6, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step.
The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the books': one national story in
chronological order — first what history is and what is
inherited, then the ancient land of temples, saints and
treasure revealers, then the Zhabdrung's founding, the
Trongsa climb and the British encounter, the reforming
kings who open the kingdom, and the happiness,
decentralisation and democracy of the living settlement.
That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced —
a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).
