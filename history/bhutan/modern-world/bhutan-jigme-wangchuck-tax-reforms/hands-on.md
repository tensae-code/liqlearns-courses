# Hands-on — Jigme Wangchuck's Tax Reforms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A tax so heavy that families abandon their homes to escape it is not revenue — it is ruin. The second king reads the tax rolls and starts abolishing. This lesson lists what he tears down.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Finding taxes so heavy that families flee beyond the taxman's reach, the second King dismantles whole layers of drungkhags and abolishes the Tsatong vacant-house tax and the Sarim Woola labour relay that forced villagers to carry loads day and night. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
