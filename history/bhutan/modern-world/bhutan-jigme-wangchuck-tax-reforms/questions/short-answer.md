# Jigme Wangchuck's Tax Reforms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the two burdens the King abolished.
**Answer:** The Tsatong vacant-house tax and the Sarim Woola labour relay.

**2.** Why is abolishing a tax on vacant houses just, in the story's logic?
**Answer:** Because the houses stood vacant precisely because families had fled the heavy taxes — Tsatong punished people twice for the same burden.
