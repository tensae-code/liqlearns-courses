# Jigme Wangchuck's Tax Reforms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Families fled beyond the taxman's reach because taxes were so heavy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The second King added new layers of drungkhags to raise more tax.
**Answer: False** — He dismantled whole layers of drungkhags and abolished burdens instead.
