# Jigme Wangchuck's Tax Reforms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the Tsatong?
a) the vacant-house tax, abolished by the second King  b) a land tax he raised  c) a new trade duty  d) a temple offering
**Answer: a) the vacant-house tax, abolished by the second King** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Sarim Woola force villagers to do?
a) serve in the army  b) carry loads day and night in a labour relay  c) build dzongs without pay for life  d) pay double taxes
**Answer: b) carry loads day and night in a labour relay** — That is what this lesson teaches.
