**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Nehru's Visit, 1958, and the Opening

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: In September 1958 Jawaharlal Nehru rides over the Himalayan passes with Indira Gandhi and tells the crowd at Paro — in a speech the King himself translates — that India wishes Bhutan to remain independent and choose its own path; self-imposed isolation begins to end.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a respected visitor who tells a small neighbour 'your choices are your own' — the Paro promise in everyday terms.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
