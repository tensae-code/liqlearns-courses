# Nehru's Visit, 1958, and the Opening — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who accompanied Nehru on the 1958 visit?
a) Charles Bell  b) Indira Gandhi  c) John Claude White  d) the Dalai Lama
**Answer: b) Indira Gandhi** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Nehru promise at Paro?
a) that India wished Bhutan to remain independent and choose its own path  b) that India would rule Bhutan's foreign affairs forever  c) that Bhutan must join India  d) nothing — he gave no speech
**Answer: a) that India wished Bhutan to remain independent and choose its own path** — That is what this lesson teaches.
