# Nehru's Visit, 1958, and the Opening — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The King himself translated Nehru's Paro speech.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Bhutan's isolation deepened after 1958.
**Answer: False** — The visit begins the end of Bhutan's self-imposed isolation.
