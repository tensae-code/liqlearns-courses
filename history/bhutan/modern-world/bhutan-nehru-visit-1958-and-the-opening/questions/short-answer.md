# Nehru's Visit, 1958, and the Opening — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** When did Nehru visit, and where did he speak?
**Answer:** In September 1958, at Paro.

**2.** Why does the King's translating matter in the story?
**Answer:** Bhutan hears the promise of independence in its own king's voice — the opening happens on Bhutan's terms, not as a foreign announcement.
