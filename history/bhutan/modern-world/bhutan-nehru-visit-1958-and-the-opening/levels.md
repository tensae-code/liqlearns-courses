# Nehru's Visit, 1958, and the Opening — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In September 1958 Jawaharlal Nehru rides over the Himalayan passes with Indira Gandhi and tells the crowd at Paro — in a speech the King himself translates — that India wishes Bhutan to remain independent and choose its own path; self-imposed isolation begins to end. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
