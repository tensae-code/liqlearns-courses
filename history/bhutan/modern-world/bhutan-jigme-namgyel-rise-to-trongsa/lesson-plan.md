# Lesson plan — Jigme Namgyel: The Rise to Trongsa

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Jigme Namgyel: The Rise to Trongsa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A Pema Lingpa descendant born in 1825 walks to Trongsa at fifteen and rises from garpa retainer to zimpon by saving his Ponlop from assassins at Punakha — wearing the raven-headed helmet that becomes the prototype of the royal crown — until in 1853 he is made Trongsa Ponlop himself.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (recommended, never forced).
