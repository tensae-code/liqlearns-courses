# Jigme Namgyel: The Rise to Trongsa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Jigme Namgyel became Trongsa Ponlop in 1853.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Jigme Namgyel was a crowned prince who inherited Trongsa.
**Answer: False** — He walked in at fifteen and rose from garpa retainer through service and courage.
