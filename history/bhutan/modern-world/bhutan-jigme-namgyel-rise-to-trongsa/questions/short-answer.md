# Jigme Namgyel: The Rise to Trongsa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) How did Jigme Namgyel rise from retainer to chamberlain?
**Answer:** By saving his Ponlop from assassins at Punakha — trusted service in a crisis.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What did he do in the east in 1850, in the Class VIII telling?
**Answer:** He marched east and reunified the rebellious Sharchog Khorlo Tsibgye districts under Trongsa for the last time.
