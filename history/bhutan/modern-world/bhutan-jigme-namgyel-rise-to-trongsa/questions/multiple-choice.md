Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How old was Jigme Namgyel when he walked to Trongsa?
a) fifteen  b) ten  c) twenty-five  d) thirty
**Answer: a) fifteen** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What did Lama Jangchub Tsondru's raven-headed helmet become?
a) the model for dzong roofs  b) the prototype of the royal Raven Crown  c) a museum piece with no legacy  d) the Desi's seal
**Answer: b) the prototype of the royal Raven Crown** — That is what this lesson teaches.
