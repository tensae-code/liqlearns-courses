# Cooch Behar and the First British Contact — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Desi Zhidar fled to Tibet after the Cooch Behar disaster.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Ten Points Treaty was signed in 1772, before the war.
**Answer: False** — It was signed on 25 April 1774, after the clash — turning enemies into trading friends.
