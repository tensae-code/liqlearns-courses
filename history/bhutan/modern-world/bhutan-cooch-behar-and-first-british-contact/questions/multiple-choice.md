# Cooch Behar and the First British Contact — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What event of 1772 brought the British against Bhutan?
a) a Bhutanese invasion of Bengal  b) Bhutan's capture of Cooch Behar's raja  c) the seizure of a British ship  d) a border tax dispute
**Answer: b) Bhutan's capture of Cooch Behar's raja** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What did the 1774 treaty do for Bhutanese traders in Rangpur?
a) abolished the duties on them and opened exclusive trade  b) raised their duties  c) banned them from Rangpur  d) nothing — trade was not mentioned
**Answer: a) abolished the duties on them and opened exclusive trade** — That is what this lesson teaches.
