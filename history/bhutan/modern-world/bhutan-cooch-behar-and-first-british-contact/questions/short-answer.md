# Cooch Behar and the First British Contact — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Who commanded the British troops sent against Bhutan?
**Answer:** Captain John Jones.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is the 1774 treaty called a turning point?
**Answer:** It turned the first enemies into trading friends and opened Bhutan's modern foreign relations — with duties abolished and exclusive trade opened.
