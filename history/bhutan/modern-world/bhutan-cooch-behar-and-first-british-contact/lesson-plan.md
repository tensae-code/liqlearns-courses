# Lesson plan — Cooch Behar and the First British Contact

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Cooch Behar and the First British Contact
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bhutan's capture of Cooch Behar's raja in 1772 brings Captain John Jones's British troops south against it — Bhutan loses the foothill lands, Desi Zhidar flees to Tibet, and the Ten Points Treaty of 25 April 1774 turns enemies into trading friends, opening exclusive trade and ending duties on Bhutanese traders in Rangpur.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (recommended, never forced).
