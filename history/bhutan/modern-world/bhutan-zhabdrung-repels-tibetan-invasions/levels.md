# The Zhabdrung Repels the Tibetan Invasions — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Tibet invades the new Bhutan five times — the third invasion in 1639 — each time with local allies inside Bhutan, and the young state survives its maker, whose death is concealed while the invasions and intrigues continue. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
