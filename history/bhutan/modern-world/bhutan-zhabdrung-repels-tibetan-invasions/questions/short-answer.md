# The Zhabdrung Repels the Tibetan Invasions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was the Zhabdrung's death concealed?
**Answer:** So the young state could survive — enemies and allies still had to reckon with him while the invasions and intrigues continued.

**2.** What does the state's survival prove about the dual system?
**Answer:** That it could outlive its builder — the order held even under invasion and after the Zhabdrung's hidden death.
