# The Zhabdrung Repels the Tibetan Invasions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The third Tibetan invasion came in 1639.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Zhabdrung's death was announced at once and the invasions stopped.
**Answer: False** — His death was concealed while invasions and intrigues continued — and the state survived its maker.
