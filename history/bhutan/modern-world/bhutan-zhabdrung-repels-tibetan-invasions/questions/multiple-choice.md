# The Zhabdrung Repels the Tibetan Invasions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many times did Tibet invade during the Zhabdrung's era?
a) five times  b) twice  c) three times  d) nine times
**Answer: a) five times** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What made the invasions harder for Bhutan to resist?
a) Bhutan had no dzongs  b) each time the invaders had local allies inside Bhutan  c) the Desi joined Tibet openly  d) the invasions came by sea
**Answer: b) each time the invaders had local allies inside Bhutan** — That is what this lesson teaches.
