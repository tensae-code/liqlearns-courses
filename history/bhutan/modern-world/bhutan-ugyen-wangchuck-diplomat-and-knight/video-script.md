# Video script — Ugyen Wangchuck: Diplomat and Knight

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The Trongsa Ponlop who mediates between the Younghusband expedition and Tibet wins the treaty signed at the Potala on 7 September 1904 and a British knighthood (KCIE) the next year — and uses John Claude White's visit to open talk of modern development for Bhutan.

In 1904 the British Younghusband expedition forces its way toward Lhasa, and Tibet and Britain need a man both can trust. They find Ugyen Wangchuck. The Trongsa Ponlop mediates between the expedition and Tibet, and the work bears fruit on 7 September 1904: a treaty signed at the Potala itself. Britain's thanks come the next year as a knighthood — KCIE, Sir Ugyen Wangchuck. And the diplomat is already looking past ceremony: when the British officer John Claude White visits Bhutan, Ugyen Wangchuck uses the visit to open talk of modern development — schools, roads, a future. The crown of 1907 is being earned in chancelleries before it is sealed in the dzong.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
