# Ugyen Wangchuck: Diplomat and Knight — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Trongsa Ponlop who mediates between the Younghusband expedition and Tibet wins the treaty signed at the Potala on 7 September 1904 and a British knighthood (KCIE) the next year — and uses John Claude White's visit to open talk of modern development for Bhutan. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
