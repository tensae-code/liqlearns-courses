# Ugyen Wangchuck: Diplomat and Knight — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does KCIE mark in Ugyen Wangchuck's story?
**Answer:** His British knighthood of 1905 — Sir Ugyen Wangchuck — earned by his mediation between Younghusband and Tibet.

**2.** What did he discuss with John Claude White?
**Answer:** Modern development for Bhutan — the schools-and-roads future he would open as king.
