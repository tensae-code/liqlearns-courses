# Ugyen Wangchuck: Diplomat and Knight — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1904 treaty was signed at the Potala on 7 September.
**Answer: True**

**2.** John Claude White's visit was used to discuss war plans only.
**Answer: False** — Ugyen Wangchuck used it to open talk of modern development for Bhutan.
