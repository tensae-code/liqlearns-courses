# Ugyen Wangchuck: Diplomat and Knight — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Between whom did Ugyen Wangchuck mediate in 1904?
a) the Younghusband expedition and Tibet  b) Bhutan and Cooch Behar  c) India and China  d) the Desi and the Je Khenpo
**Answer: a) the Younghusband expedition and Tibet** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What honour did Britain give him the year after the Potala treaty?
a) the Duars back  b) a knighthood — KCIE  c) a naval command  d) a seat in Parliament
**Answer: b) a knighthood — KCIE** — That is what this lesson teaches.
