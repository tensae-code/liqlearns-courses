# Hands-on — Ugyen Wangchuck: Diplomat and Knight

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Between two empires' armies stands one Bhutanese mediator, trusted by both sides. Watch what trust earns: a treaty, a knighthood, and the first talk of schools and roads. This lesson collects the earnings.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Trongsa Ponlop who mediates between the Younghusband expedition and Tibet wins the treaty signed at the Potala on 7 September 1904 and a British knighthood (KCIE) the next year — and uses John Claude White's visit to open talk of modern development for Bhutan. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
