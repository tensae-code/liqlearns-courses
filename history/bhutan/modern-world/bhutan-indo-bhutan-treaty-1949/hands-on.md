# Hands-on — The Indo-Bhutan Treaty, 1949

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The empire at your border vanishes and a new republic takes its seat. Every old treaty is suddenly a question. Bhutan's answer comes in 1949. This lesson watches the renegotiation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: With India independent on 15 August 1947 and the 1910 treaty's future in doubt, Bhutan attends the Asian Relations Conference and signs the 1949 treaty with India that anchors Indo-Bhutan relations until its 2007 revision. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
