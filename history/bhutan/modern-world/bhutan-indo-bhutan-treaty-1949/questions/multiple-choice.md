# The Indo-Bhutan Treaty, 1949 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why was a new treaty needed in 1949?
a) Bhutan had lost a war  b) India had become independent in 1947, putting the 1910 treaty's future in doubt  c) the subsidy had doubled  d) a new king demanded it
**Answer: b) India had become independent in 1947, putting the 1910 treaty's future in doubt** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Until when did the 1949 treaty anchor relations?
a) until its 2007 revision  b) until 1958  c) until 1971  d) it was never revised
**Answer: a) until its 2007 revision** — That is what this lesson teaches.
