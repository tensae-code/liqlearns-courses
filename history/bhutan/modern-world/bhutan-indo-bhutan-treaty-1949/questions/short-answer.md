# The Indo-Bhutan Treaty, 1949 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Which treaty did 1949 replace in practice?
**Answer:** The 1910 Treaty of Punakha with British India.

**2.** What does it mean that the 1949 treaty 'anchored' relations?
**Answer:** It fixed the framework of Indo-Bhutan friendship and dealings that held until the 2007 revision.
