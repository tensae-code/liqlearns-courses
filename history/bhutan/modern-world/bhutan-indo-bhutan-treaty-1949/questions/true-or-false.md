# The Indo-Bhutan Treaty, 1949 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bhutan attended the Asian Relations Conference as the new Asia formed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** India became independent on 15 August 1949.
**Answer: False** — India became independent on 15 August 1947; the treaty followed in 1949.
