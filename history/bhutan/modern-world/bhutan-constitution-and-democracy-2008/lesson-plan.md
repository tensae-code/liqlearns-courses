# Lesson plan — The Constitution and Democracy, 2008

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Constitution and Democracy, 2008
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Happiness, Decentralisation and Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A royal Kasho of 4 September 2001 orders a written constitution; the drafting committee delivers its first draft on 9 December 2002; the King tours the country discussing it, abdicates in 2006; National Council elections follow in December 2007, and the Constitution is signed on 18 July 2008.
