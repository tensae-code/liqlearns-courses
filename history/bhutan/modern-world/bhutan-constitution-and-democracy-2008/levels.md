# The Constitution and Democracy, 2008 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A royal Kasho of 4 September 2001 orders a written constitution; the drafting committee delivers its first draft on 9 December 2002; the King tours the country discussing it, abdicates in 2006; National Council elections follow in December 2007, and the Constitution is signed on 18 July 2008. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
