# Hands-on — The Constitution and Democracy, 2008

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A king orders a constitution that will limit kings — then tours his whole country explaining it village by village, then steps down so his son can reign under it. Walk the dates: 2001, 2002, 2006, 2007, 2008. This lesson walks them with you.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A royal Kasho of 4 September 2001 orders a written constitution; the drafting committee delivers its first draft on 9 December 2002; the King tours the country discussing it, abdicates in 2006; National Council elections follow in December 2007, and the Constitution is signed on 18 July 2008. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
