# The Constitution and Democracy, 2008 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Kasho of 4 September 2001 order?
a) a written constitution for Bhutan  b) a new capital  c) the end of the monarchy  d) a war with the militants
**Answer: a) a written constitution for Bhutan** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When was the Constitution signed?
a) 17 December 2007  b) 18 July 2008  c) 4 September 2001  d) 6 November 2008
**Answer: b) 18 July 2008** — That is what this lesson teaches.
