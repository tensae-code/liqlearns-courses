# The Constitution and Democracy, 2008 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give the constitution's road in dates.
**Answer:** Kasho ordering it: 4 September 2001; first draft: 9 December 2002; the King's tour and abdication: 2006; National Council elections: December 2007; signing: 18 July 2008.

**2.** Why did the King abdicate in 2006, in the books' telling?
**Answer:** So that his son's reign and the new constitutional order would begin together.
