# The Constitution and Democracy, 2008 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The King toured the country discussing the draft constitution with his people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The fourth King was forced to abdicate by an election defeat.
**Answer: False** — He abdicated voluntarily in 2006 so the new reign and the new constitutional order would begin together.
