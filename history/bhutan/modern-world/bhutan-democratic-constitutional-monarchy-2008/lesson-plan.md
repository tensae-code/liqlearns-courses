# Lesson plan — A Democratic Constitutional Monarchy, 2008

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** A Democratic Constitutional Monarchy, 2008
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Happiness, Decentralisation and Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From 2008 Bhutan is a Democratic Constitutional Monarchy — parties, an elected Assembly and the King as head of state — completed on 6–7 November 2008 when Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck, the People's King, is formally crowned.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Happiness, Decentralisation and Democracy (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 5.1 The Government** — The Class VI book, written for Bhutan's youngest citizens, states the result in one clean sentence: a kingdom ruled as a monarchy until 2006 votes itself a new order, and from 2008 Bhutan is a Democratic Constitutional Monarchy — political parties, an elected Assembly, and the King as head of state.
2. **Chapter Four, Coronation of the Fifth Druk Gyalpo** — The Class X book completes the year with its crowning.
