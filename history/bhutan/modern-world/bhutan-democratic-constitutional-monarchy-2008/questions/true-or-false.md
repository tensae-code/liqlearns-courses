# A Democratic Constitutional Monarchy, 2008 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the 2008 order the King remains head of state.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The coronation of the fifth King came before the Constitution was signed.
**Answer: False** — The Constitution was signed on 18 July 2008; the coronation followed on 6–7 November 2008.
