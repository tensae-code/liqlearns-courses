# A Democratic Constitutional Monarchy, 2008 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What three features name the 2008 order in the Class VI telling?
**Answer:** Political parties, an elected Assembly, and the King as head of state — a Democratic Constitutional Monarchy.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do the constitution and the coronation belong together?
**Answer:** Because the crown is placed on a head the Constitution has already defined — the King reigns under the written supreme law.
