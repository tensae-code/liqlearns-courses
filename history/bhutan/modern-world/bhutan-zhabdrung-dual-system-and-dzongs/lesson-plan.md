# Lesson plan — The Zhabdrung: The Dual System and the Dzongs

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Zhabdrung: The Dual System and the Dzongs
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Zhabdrung Unifies Bhutan (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The unifier gives Bhutan its lasting shape: a distinct Drukpa Kagyu order, national dress and driglam namzha, laws, taxes and the great dzongs — and in 1651 the Chhoe-sid dual system, Je Khenpo for religion and Desi for temporal rule, under the Kathrim code whose spiritual law is 'like a silken knot' and secular law 'like a golden yoke'.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Zhabdrung Unifies Bhutan (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 5.2 Zhabdrung's contributions** — The Class V book lists what the unifier builds, and it is a whole civilisation in one list: a distinct Bhutanese Drukpa Kagyu order; national dress and driglam namzha, the etiquette that still governs how Bhutanese dress and behave; laws and taxes for the new state; and the great dzongs rising over the valleys.
2. **Chapter Two, Home away from Home / Chapter Three, dzongs under the Zhabdrung** — The Class VIII book watches the building happen.
3. **Chapter One, The Dual System of Governance: Chhoe-sid Nyiden** — The Class X book gives the system's law its poetry and its precision.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
