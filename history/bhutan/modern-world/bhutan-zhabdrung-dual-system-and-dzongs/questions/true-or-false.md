# The Zhabdrung: The Dual System and the Dzongs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The dual system was set up in 1651, the year the Zhabdrung died.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The spiritual law is described as 'a golden yoke' and the secular law as 'a silken knot'.
**Answer: False** — It is the other way: spiritual law like a silken knot, secular law like a golden yoke.
