# The Zhabdrung: The Dual System and the Dzongs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Name three things the Zhabdrung codified besides the dual system.
**Answer:** Any three: the Drukpa Kagyu order as Bhutan's own, national dress, driglam namzha etiquette, laws, taxes, the great dzongs.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) What does a dzong show about the dual system?
**Answer:** It houses the monk body and the administration together — religion and government under one roof, the dual system in stone.
