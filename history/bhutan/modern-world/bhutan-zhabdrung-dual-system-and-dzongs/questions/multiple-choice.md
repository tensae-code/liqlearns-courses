# The Zhabdrung: The Dual System and the Dzongs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the dual system, who heads religion and who heads temporal rule?
a) the Je Khenpo heads religion; the Desi heads temporal rule  b) the Desi heads both  c) the Ponlops head both  d) the Je Khenpo heads temporal rule; the Desi heads religion
**Answer: a) the Je Khenpo heads religion; the Desi heads temporal rule** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the Kathrim?
a) a tax on tea  b) the Zhabdrung's legal code, published in 1651  c) a style of dzong roof  d) a treaty with Tibet
**Answer: b) the Zhabdrung's legal code, published in 1651** — That is what this lesson teaches.
