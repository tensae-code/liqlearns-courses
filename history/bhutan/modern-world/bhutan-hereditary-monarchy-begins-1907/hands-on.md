# Hands-on — The Hereditary Monarchy Begins, 1907

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A system that has run for 250 years is failing, and a council must choose what replaces it. Their answer is an oath, a seal and a date: 17 December 1907. This lesson sits in that council.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On 17 December 1907, with the theocratic dual system plainly failing and the last Druk Desi stepped down in 1906, the state council accepts Ugyen Dorji's proposal and seals the genja oath enthroning Gongsar Ugyen Wangchuck as the first hereditary Druk Gyalpo — the date Bhutan keeps as National Day. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
