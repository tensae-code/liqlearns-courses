# The Hereditary Monarchy Begins, 1907 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On what date was Ugyen Wangchuck enthroned as first Druk Gyalpo?
a) 11 November 1865  b) 17 December 1907  c) 7 September 1904  d) 18 July 2008
**Answer: b) 17 December 1907** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the genja?
a) the sealed oath-document of the 1907 enthronement  b) a royal tax  c) a fortress design  d) a coronation robe
**Answer: a) the sealed oath-document of the 1907 enthronement** — That is what this lesson teaches.
