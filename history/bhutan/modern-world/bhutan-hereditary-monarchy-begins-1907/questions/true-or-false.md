# The Hereditary Monarchy Begins, 1907 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) 17 December is Bhutan's National Day because of the 1907 enthronement.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The last Druk Desi was still ruling strongly in 1907.
**Answer: False** — He had stepped down in 1906 and the theocratic system was plainly failing.
