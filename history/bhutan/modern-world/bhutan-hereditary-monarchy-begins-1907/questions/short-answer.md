# The Hereditary Monarchy Begins, 1907 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Whose proposal did the state council accept in 1907?
**Answer:** Ugyen Dorji's proposal to enthrone Ugyen Wangchuck as hereditary king.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What two things did the new king do soon after, in the Class VIII telling?
**Answer:** He won the 1910 Punakha treaty doubling his subsidy, and opened Bhutan's first modern schools.
