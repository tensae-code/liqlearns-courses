# Lesson plan — The Hereditary Monarchy Begins, 1907

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Hereditary Monarchy Begins, 1907
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On 17 December 1907, with the theocratic dual system plainly failing and the last Druk Desi stepped down in 1906, the state council accepts Ugyen Dorji's proposal and seals the genja oath enthroning Gongsar Ugyen Wangchuck as the first hereditary Druk Gyalpo — the date Bhutan keeps as National Day.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (recommended, never forced).
