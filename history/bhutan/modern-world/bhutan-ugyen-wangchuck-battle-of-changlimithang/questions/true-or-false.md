# Ugyen Wangchuck and the Battle of Changlimithang — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ugyen Wangchuck fought in person at Changlimithang as his line wavered.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Changlimithang began a new century of civil wars.
**Answer: False** — It ended them — by 1886 armed internal strife was over for good.
