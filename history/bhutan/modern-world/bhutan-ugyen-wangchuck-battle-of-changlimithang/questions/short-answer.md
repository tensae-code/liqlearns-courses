# Ugyen Wangchuck and the Battle of Changlimithang — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Which places did Ugyen Wangchuck take before Changlimithang?
**Answer:** Punakha and Semtokha.

**2.** Why is Changlimithang a turning point?
**Answer:** It is the last civil battle: Ugyen Wangchuck's personal stand holds his line, and by 1886 internal strife ends for good.
