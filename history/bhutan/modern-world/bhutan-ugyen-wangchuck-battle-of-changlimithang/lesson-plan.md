# Lesson plan — Ugyen Wangchuck and the Battle of Changlimithang

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Ugyen Wangchuck and the Battle of Changlimithang
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** When his two oldest friends, the Thimphu and Punakha dzongpons, plot his assassination in 1885, Ugyen Wangchuck takes Punakha and Semtokha, fights in person at Changlimithang as his line wavers, and by 1886 ends armed internal strife in Bhutan for good.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (recommended, never forced).
