# Video script — Ugyen Wangchuck and the Battle of Changlimithang

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
When his two oldest friends, the Thimphu and Punakha dzongpons, plot his assassination in 1885, Ugyen Wangchuck takes Punakha and Semtokha, fights in person at Changlimithang as his line wavers, and by 1886 ends armed internal strife in Bhutan for good.

The road to the throne runs through betrayal. In 1885 the Thimphu and Punakha dzongpons — Ugyen Wangchuck's two oldest friends — plot his assassination, the dual system's endless rivalries reaching for their final victim. He answers as his father would: he takes Punakha and Semtokha first, then meets the plotters at Changlimithang, below Thimphu. There, as his own line wavers, Ugyen Wangchuck fights in person at the front — and the line holds. By 1886 the victory is complete, and the books give it its full weight: armed internal strife in Bhutan ends, for good. The man who ends the civil wars is the man the country will crown.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
