# Ugyen Wangchuck and the Battle of Changlimithang — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — When his two oldest friends, the Thimphu and Punakha dzongpons, plot his assassination in 1885, Ugyen Wangchuck takes Punakha and Semtokha, fights in person at Changlimithang as his line wavers, and by 1886 ends armed internal strife in Bhutan for good. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
