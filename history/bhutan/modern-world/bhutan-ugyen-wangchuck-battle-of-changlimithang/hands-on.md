# Hands-on — Ugyen Wangchuck and the Battle of Changlimithang

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The men plotting to kill you are your two oldest friends. That is the trap Ugyen Wangchuck walks into in 1885. This lesson follows him out of it — sword in hand.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: When his two oldest friends, the Thimphu and Punakha dzongpons, plot his assassination in 1885, Ugyen Wangchuck takes Punakha and Semtokha, fights in person at Changlimithang as his line wavers, and by 1886 ends armed internal strife in Bhutan for good. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
