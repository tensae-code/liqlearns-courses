# Hands-on — The First Five-Year Plan, 1961

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A country with almost no roads writes its first plan. First item: roads. Count what follows — over a thousand kilometres of them. This lesson reads the first plan.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Road-building from Phuentsholing to Paro starts in 1960, and in 1961 Bhutan launches its first Five-Year Plan — fully supported by India at 1,747 lakh rupees, with over 1,000 km of roads following. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
