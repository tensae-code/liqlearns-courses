# The First Five-Year Plan, 1961 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Road-building from Phuentsholing to Paro started in 1960, before the plan.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The first plan built about 100 km of roads.
**Answer: False** — Over 1,000 km of roads followed the first plan.
