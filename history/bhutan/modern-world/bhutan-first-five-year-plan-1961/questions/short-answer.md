# The First Five-Year Plan, 1961 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why do roads come first in Bhutan's development story?
**Answer:** Because the valleys were sealed off from each other — roads stitch the country into one reachable whole, and every other service rides on them.

**2.** What is a Five-Year Plan, as Bhutan uses it?
**Answer:** A written, dated, funded programme for the country's development over five years — the first launched in 1961.
