# The First Five-Year Plan, 1961 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When did Bhutan launch its first Five-Year Plan?
a) 1961  b) 1953  c) 1958  d) 1971
**Answer: a) 1961** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How was the first plan supported?
a) by Britain  b) fully by India, at 1,747 lakh rupees  c) by the United Nations  d) it had no funding
**Answer: b) fully by India, at 1,747 lakh rupees** — That is what this lesson teaches.
