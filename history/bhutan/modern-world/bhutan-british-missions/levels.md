# The British Missions — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Hunting a trade road to Tibet, Britain sends mission after mission into Bhutan — Bogle in 1774, who plants potatoes at Tashichhodzong; Hamilton; Turner in 1783, whose sketches show Bhutan to the world; Kishan Kant Bose; Pemberton in 1838, rejected; and Ashley Eden in 1864, whose humiliation lights the fuse of war. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
