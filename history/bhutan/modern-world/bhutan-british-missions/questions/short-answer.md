# The British Missions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the missions in order.
**Answer:** Bogle (1774), Hamilton, Turner (1783), Kishan Kant Bose, Pemberton (1838), Ashley Eden (1864).

**2.** What happened to Pemberton's 1838 mission?
**Answer:** It was rejected.
