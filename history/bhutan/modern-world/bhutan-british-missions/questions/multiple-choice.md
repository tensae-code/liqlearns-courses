# The British Missions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was Britain hunting for through Bhutan?
a) a trade road to Tibet  b) gold mines  c) a naval base  d) tea gardens only
**Answer: a) a trade road to Tibet** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which mission's humiliation lights the fuse of the Duar War?
a) Bogle's, in 1774  b) Ashley Eden's, in 1864  c) Turner's, in 1783  d) Pemberton's, in 1838
**Answer: b) Ashley Eden's, in 1864** — That is what this lesson teaches.
