# The Duar War, 1864–65 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many Duars did Britain take in the war?
a) seven  b) eighteen  c) eleven  d) twenty-four
**Answer: b) eighteen** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Jigme Namgyal capture at Dewathang?
a) two British howitzers  b) a British general  c) a steamship  d) the Eden mission's papers
**Answer: a) two British howitzers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
