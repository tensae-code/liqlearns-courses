# The Duar War, 1864–65 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Jigme Namgyal fight at Dewathang?
**Answer:** He cut the fort's water supply and captured two howitzers.

**2.** Why did Bhutan lose despite the Dewathang stand?
**Answer:** Superior British rifles retook the forts one by one — courage could not outlast industrial firepower.
