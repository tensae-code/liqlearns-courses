# The Duar War, 1864–65 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain attacked in four columns in November 1864.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Bhutan's forces recaptured all the Duars by the war's end.
**Answer: False** — British rifles retook the forts and forced a settlement; the Duars stayed lost.
