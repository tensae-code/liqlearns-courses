# Jigme Namgyel Becomes Druk Desi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which office did Jigme Namgyal reach at the top of his climb?
a) 51st Druk Desi, temporal head of the state  b) Je Khenpo  c) Trongsa Ponlop for a second term  d) chief justice
**Answer: a) 51st Druk Desi, temporal head of the state** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Whose inheritance does his Desi-ship prepare?
a) the Tsangpa ruler's  b) his son Ugyen Wangchuck's  c) the British Resident's  d) no one's — the line ends
**Answer: b) his son Ugyen Wangchuck's** — That is what this lesson teaches.
