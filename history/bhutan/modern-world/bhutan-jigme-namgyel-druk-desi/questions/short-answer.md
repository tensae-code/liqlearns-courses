# Jigme Namgyel Becomes Druk Desi — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is the 51st Desi-ship a hinge of history?
**Answer:** Because the state Jigme Namgyal heads is the one his son Ugyen Wangchuck will inherit and transform into a hereditary monarchy.

**2.** Trace the climb in three steps.
**Answer:** Garpa retainer at Trongsa, zimpon (chamberlain) and Trongsa Ponlop, then 51st Druk Desi.
