# Jigme Namgyel Becomes Druk Desi

## Do it first

The boy who walked to Trongsa at fifteen with nothing — picture him now at the very top of the system he walked into. This short lesson marks the summit of the climb.

Every climb in these lessons ends somewhere, and Jigme Namgyal's ends at the summit. The Class VIII book closes his chapter with the final promotion: the soldier-statesman becomes the 51st Druk Desi — temporal head of Bhutan, master of the political half of the Zhabdrung's dual system. The garpa who walked to Trongsa at fifteen now holds the office the whole country answers to. And the books let you feel the hinge of history in it: the state he heads is the state his son, Ugyen Wangchuck, will inherit — and transform into a hereditary monarchy.

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The soldier-statesman's climb ends at the top of the dual system itself: Jigme Namgyal becomes the 51st Druk Desi, temporal head of the state his son will inherit. Give one real example: a career that reaches the top of the very ladder it started at the bottom of — the climb completed, and name the lesson's key words: Druk Desi, 51st, dual system, temporal head, climb. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
