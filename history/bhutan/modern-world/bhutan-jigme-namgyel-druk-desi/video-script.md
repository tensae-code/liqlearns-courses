# Video script — Jigme Namgyel Becomes Druk Desi

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The soldier-statesman's climb ends at the top of the dual system itself: Jigme Namgyal becomes the 51st Druk Desi, temporal head of the state his son will inherit.

Every climb in these lessons ends somewhere, and Jigme Namgyal's ends at the summit. The Class VIII book closes his chapter with the final promotion: the soldier-statesman becomes the 51st Druk Desi — temporal head of Bhutan, master of the political half of the Zhabdrung's dual system. The garpa who walked to Trongsa at fifteen now holds the office the whole country answers to. And the books let you feel the hinge of history in it: the state he heads is the state his son, Ugyen Wangchuck, will inherit — and transform into a hereditary monarchy.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
