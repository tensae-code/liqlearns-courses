# Hands-on — Jigme Namgyel Becomes Druk Desi

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The boy who walked to Trongsa at fifteen with nothing — picture him now at the very top of the system he walked into. This short lesson marks the summit of the climb.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The soldier-statesman's climb ends at the top of the dual system itself: Jigme Namgyal becomes the 51st Druk Desi, temporal head of the state his son will inherit. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
