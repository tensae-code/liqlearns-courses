# The Bhutan–India Friendship Treaty, 2007 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the fifth King's first visit matter as a message?
**Answer:** Going first to India honours the friendship — while the treaty he signs there ends the era of guidance.

**2.** Which earlier treaties does 2007 complete the story of?
**Answer:** The 1910 Punakha treaty's guidance bargain, carried into the 1949 treaty — both now superseded between equals.
