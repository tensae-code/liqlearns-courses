# The Bhutan–India Friendship Treaty, 2007 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the 2007 treaty replace?
a) the entire friendship with India  b) the 1949 treaty's guidance clause  c) the 1865 subsidy  d) the monarchy itself
**Answer: b) the 1949 treaty's guidance clause** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What replaced guidance in the 2007 treaty?
a) mutual cooperation between equals  b) Indian administration  c) a military alliance against Tibet  d) nothing — the clause was left blank
**Answer: a) mutual cooperation between equals** — That is what this lesson teaches.
