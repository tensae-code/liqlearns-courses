# The Bhutan–India Friendship Treaty, 2007 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In February 2007 the new fifth King makes India his first foreign visit and signs the revised friendship treaty — replacing the 1949 treaty's guidance clause with mutual cooperation between equals. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
