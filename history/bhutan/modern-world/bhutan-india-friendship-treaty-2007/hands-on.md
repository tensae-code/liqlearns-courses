# Hands-on — The Bhutan–India Friendship Treaty, 2007

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A new king's first foreign visit is a message before a word is spoken. In February 2007 Bhutan's fifth King goes first to India — and signs away the word 'guidance'. This lesson reads the new treaty.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In February 2007 the new fifth King makes India his first foreign visit and signs the revised friendship treaty — replacing the 1949 treaty's guidance clause with mutual cooperation between equals. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
