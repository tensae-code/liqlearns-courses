# Hands-on — The Treaty of Punakha, 1910

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A new king, three years crowned, signs a treaty that pays more and promises guidance. What does Bhutan gain, and what does it give? This lesson reads the bargain of 1910.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After Sinchula reopens relations and Desi Jigme Namgyel steadies them, Charles Bell's mission produces the Treaty of Punakha in 1910 — raising Bhutan's subsidy and binding its external relations to British guidance, the arrangement that lasts until 1949. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
