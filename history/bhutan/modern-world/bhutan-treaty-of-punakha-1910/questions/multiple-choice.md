# The Treaty of Punakha, 1910 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who led the British mission that produced the 1910 treaty?
a) Charles Bell  b) Ashley Eden  c) John Claude White  d) Captain John Jones
**Answer: a) Charles Bell** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Bhutan bind to British guidance at Punakha?
a) its internal laws  b) its external relations  c) its religion  d) its school system
**Answer: b) its external relations** — That is what this lesson teaches.
