# The Treaty of Punakha, 1910 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two terms define the Punakha settlement?
**Answer:** A raised annual subsidy for Bhutan, and Bhutan's external relations bound to British guidance.

**2.** How long did the Punakha arrangement's shape last?
**Answer:** Until 1949, when independent India replaced Britain at the table.
