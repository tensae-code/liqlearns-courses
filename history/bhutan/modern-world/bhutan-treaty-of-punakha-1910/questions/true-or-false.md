# The Treaty of Punakha, 1910 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Treaty of Punakha raised Bhutan's annual subsidy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The 1910 treaty ended the monarchy and made Bhutan a colony.
**Answer: False** — The king still ruled inside Bhutan; the treaty bound external relations to British guidance until 1949 renegotiated it.
