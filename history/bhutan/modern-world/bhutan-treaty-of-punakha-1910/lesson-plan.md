# Lesson plan — The Treaty of Punakha, 1910

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Treaty of Punakha, 1910
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After Sinchula reopens relations and Desi Jigme Namgyel steadies them, Charles Bell's mission produces the Treaty of Punakha in 1910 — raising Bhutan's subsidy and binding its external relations to British guidance, the arrangement that lasts until 1949.
