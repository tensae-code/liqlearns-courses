# Video script — The Treaty of Punakha, 1910

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
After Sinchula reopens relations and Desi Jigme Namgyel steadies them, Charles Bell's mission produces the Treaty of Punakha in 1910 — raising Bhutan's subsidy and binding its external relations to British guidance, the arrangement that lasts until 1949.

The Class X book closes its British India chapter at Punakha. The road there runs through the earlier lessons: the Duar War and Sinchula reopen relations; Desi Jigme Namgyel steadies them; Ugyen Wangchuck, as mediator, meets the Younghusband expedition at Chumbi in 1904. In 1910 Charles Bell's mission sits down with the new king, and the Treaty of Punakha is the result: Bhutan's annual subsidy is raised, and in return Bhutan binds its external relations to British guidance. It is the settlement the monarchy is born under — protected, paid, and guided from outside — and it holds, unchanged in shape, until independent India replaces Britain at the table in 1949.
