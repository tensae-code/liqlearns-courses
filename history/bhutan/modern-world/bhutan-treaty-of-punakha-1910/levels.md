# The Treaty of Punakha, 1910 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After Sinchula reopens relations and Desi Jigme Namgyel steadies them, Charles Bell's mission produces the Treaty of Punakha in 1910 — raising Bhutan's subsidy and binding its external relations to British guidance, the arrangement that lasts until 1949. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
