# Jigme Wangchuck the Consolidator — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When was Jigme Wangchuck crowned?
a) 17 December 1907, at Punakha  b) 14 March 1927, at Punakha  c) 14 March 1917, at Thimphu  d) 1 January 1930, at Trongsa
**Answer: b) 14 March 1927, at Punakha** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What were the fifteen men sent to the Gurkha Rifles?
a) the seed of a modern army  b) a trade delegation  c) the first parliament  d) a football team
**Answer: a) the seed of a modern army** — That is what this lesson teaches.
