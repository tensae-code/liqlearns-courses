# Jigme Wangchuck the Consolidator — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the second King's three steadying works.
**Answer:** Upgrading the Bumthang and Haa schools, bringing the first doctor by 1932, and sending fifteen men to train with the Gurkha Rifles.

**2.** Why is he called the consolidator?
**Answer:** Because he steadied the twenty-year-old monarchy — making its schools, medicine and army real and reliable.
