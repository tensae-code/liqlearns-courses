# Hands-on — Jigme Wangchuck the Consolidator

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A crown only twenty years old, a kingdom still held together by habit. The second king's job is not glory — it is steadiness: schools, a doctor, an army's first fifteen men. This lesson honours the steady work.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Crowned at Punakha on 14 March 1927, the second King steadies the young monarchy — upgrading the Bumthang and Haa schools, bringing the first doctor by 1932, and sending fifteen men to train with the Gurkha Rifles as the seed of a modern army. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
