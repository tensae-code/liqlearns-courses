# Jigme Wangchuck the Consolidator — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Crowned at Punakha on 14 March 1927, the second King steadies the young monarchy — upgrading the Bumthang and Haa schools, bringing the first doctor by 1932, and sending fifteen men to train with the Gurkha Rifles as the seed of a modern army. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
