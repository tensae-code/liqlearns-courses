# Video script — Jigme Wangchuck the Consolidator

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Crowned at Punakha on 14 March 1927, the second King steadies the young monarchy — upgrading the Bumthang and Haa schools, bringing the first doctor by 1932, and sending fifteen men to train with the Gurkha Rifles as the seed of a modern army.

The monarchy's second act is consolidation. Jigme Wangchuck is crowned at Punakha on 14 March 1927, inheriting a crown his father won only twenty years before — and the Class IX book judges him by what he steadies. He upgrades the schools at Bumthang and Haa, the monarchy's first classrooms growing up. By 1932 he has brought Bhutan its first doctor. And he sends fifteen men to train with the Gurkha Rifles — a handful of soldiers who are, in the books' phrase, the seed of a modern army. Nothing here is dramatic, and that is the point: a dynasty survives its second king by becoming ordinary, reliable and served.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
