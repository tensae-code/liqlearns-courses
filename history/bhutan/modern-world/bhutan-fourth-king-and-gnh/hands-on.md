# Hands-on — The Fourth King and Gross National Happiness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Every country measures its wealth. One king stands up in 1979 and says his country will measure happiness instead. This lesson follows that sentence around the world.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Crowned in 1974, the fourth King declares in 1979 that Gross National Happiness is more important than Gross National Product, builds it on four pillars, writes it into Article 9 of the Constitution, and takes it to the United Nations. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
