# The Fourth King and Gross National Happiness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the core claim of Gross National Happiness?
**Answer:** That a country's true measure is its people's happiness, which matters more than the size of its product or economy.

**2.** How far did the King take the GNH idea?
**Answer:** Into Article 9 of the Constitution, and to the United Nations — offering Bhutan's measure to the world.
