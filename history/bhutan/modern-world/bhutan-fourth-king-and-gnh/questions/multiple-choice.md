# The Fourth King and Gross National Happiness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the fourth King declare more important than Gross National Product?
a) Gross National Happiness  b) Gross National Product itself  c) military strength  d) tourism income
**Answer: a) Gross National Happiness** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where is GNH written into Bhutan's Constitution?
a) in the preamble only  b) in Article 9  c) it is not in the Constitution  d) in Article 1
**Answer: b) in Article 9** — That is what this lesson teaches.
