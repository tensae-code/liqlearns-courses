# The Fourth King and Gross National Happiness

## Do it first

Every country measures its wealth. One king stands up in 1979 and says his country will measure happiness instead. This lesson follows that sentence around the world.

The fourth Druk Gyalpo, Jigme Singye Wangchuck, is crowned in 1974 before the dignitaries of the world — and then does something no dignitary expects. In 1979 he declares that Gross National Happiness is more important than Gross National Product: a country's true wealth is the happiness of its people, not only the size of its economy. The Class X book builds the idea properly: GNH stands on four pillars, it is written into the Constitution itself, in Article 9, as the state's duty — and the King takes it to the United Nations, offering Bhutan's measure to the world. A phrase from a Himalayan throne becomes a global argument about what development is for.
