# Video script — The Fourth King and Gross National Happiness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Crowned in 1974, the fourth King declares in 1979 that Gross National Happiness is more important than Gross National Product, builds it on four pillars, writes it into Article 9 of the Constitution, and takes it to the United Nations.

The fourth Druk Gyalpo, Jigme Singye Wangchuck, is crowned in 1974 before the dignitaries of the world — and then does something no dignitary expects. In 1979 he declares that Gross National Happiness is more important than Gross National Product: a country's true wealth is the happiness of its people, not only the size of its economy. The Class X book builds the idea properly: GNH stands on four pillars, it is written into the Constitution itself, in Article 9, as the state's duty — and the King takes it to the United Nations, offering Bhutan's measure to the world. A phrase from a Himalayan throne becomes a global argument about what development is for.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
