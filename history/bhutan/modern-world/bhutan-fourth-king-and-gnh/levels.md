# The Fourth King and Gross National Happiness — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Crowned in 1974, the fourth King declares in 1979 that Gross National Happiness is more important than Gross National Product, builds it on four pillars, writes it into Article 9 of the Constitution, and takes it to the United Nations. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
