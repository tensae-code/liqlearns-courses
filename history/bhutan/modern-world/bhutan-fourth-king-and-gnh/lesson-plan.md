# Lesson plan — The Fourth King and Gross National Happiness

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Fourth King and Gross National Happiness
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Happiness, Decentralisation and Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Crowned in 1974, the fourth King declares in 1979 that Gross National Happiness is more important than Gross National Product, builds it on four pillars, writes it into Article 9 of the Constitution, and takes it to the United Nations.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Happiness, Decentralisation and Democracy (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Crowned in 1974, the fourth King declares in 1979 that Gross National Happiness is more important than Gross National Product, builds it on four pillars, writes it into Article 9 of the Constitution, and takes it to the United Nations.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: judging your own week by whether it was good, not only by what it produced — GNH's question at personal scale.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life in Bhutan, as the books themselves work.
