# Lesson plan — The Treaty of Sinchula, 1865

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Treaty of Sinchula, 1865
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On 11 November 1865 Bhutan permanently cedes all the Duars — a fifth of its territory — for peace, free trade and an annual British subsidy rising to Rs 50,000; and the Class X book re-reads the settlement through Jigme Namgyel, the man who fought the war and then rebuilt the relationship.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (recommended, never forced).
