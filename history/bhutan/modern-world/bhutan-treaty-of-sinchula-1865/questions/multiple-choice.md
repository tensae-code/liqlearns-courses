# The Treaty of Sinchula, 1865 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What did Bhutan permanently cede at Sinchula?
a) all the Duars — a fifth of its territory  b) Trongsa Dzong  c) its northern passes  d) nothing — the land was returned
**Answer: a) all the Duars — a fifth of its territory** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What annual subsidy did the treaty promise, at its height?
a) Rs 5,000  b) Rs 50,000  c) Rs 500,000  d) no subsidy was promised
**Answer: b) Rs 50,000** — That is what this lesson teaches.
