# The Treaty of Sinchula, 1865 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Treaty of Sinchula was signed on 11 November 1865.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) After Sinchula, Bhutan's southern trade stayed closed.
**Answer: False** — Jigme Namgyel rebuilt the relationship and southern trade reopened after 1865.
