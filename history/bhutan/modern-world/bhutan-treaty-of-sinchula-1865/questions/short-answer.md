# The Treaty of Sinchula, 1865 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What three things did Bhutan receive under Sinchula?
**Answer:** Peace, free trade, and an annual British subsidy rising to Rs 50,000.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the Class X book present Jigme Namgyel's role after the war?
**Answer:** As the restorer of stability — the man who fought the war and then rebuilt relations with British India.
