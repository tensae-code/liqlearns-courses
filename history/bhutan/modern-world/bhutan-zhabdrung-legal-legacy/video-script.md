# Video script — The Zhabdrung's Legal Legacy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The Kathrim tradition is traced forward through the Thrimzhung Chenmo to the 2008 Constitution — Bhutan's modern supreme law grows, in the books' telling, out of the Zhabdrung's 1651 code.

The Class X book ends its Zhabdrung chapter by looking forward three and a half centuries. The Kathrim, his 1651 legal code, does not die with the dual system: its tradition is traced forward into the Thrimzhung Chenmo, the supreme law of the monarchy years, and from there into the Constitution of 2008, the modern supreme law promulgated under the fifth King. Bhutan's law, in this telling, is one long conversation — the silken knot and the golden yoke of 1651 still audible in the constitution under which Bhutanese vote today.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
