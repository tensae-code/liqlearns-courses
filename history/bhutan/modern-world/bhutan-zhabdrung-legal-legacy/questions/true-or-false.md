# The Zhabdrung's Legal Legacy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Thrimzhung Chenmo stands between the Kathrim and the 2008 Constitution in the legal line.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The books present the 2008 Constitution as having no roots in earlier Bhutanese law.
**Answer: False** — They trace its line back through the Thrimzhung Chenmo to the Zhabdrung's Kathrim.
