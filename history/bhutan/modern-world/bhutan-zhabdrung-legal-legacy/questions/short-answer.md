# The Zhabdrung's Legal Legacy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the Kathrim tradition's legacy?
**Answer:** It runs forward through the Thrimzhung Chenmo to the 2008 Constitution — Bhutan's modern supreme law grows out of the Zhabdrung's code.

**2.** When was the Kathrim published?
**Answer:** In 1651, under the Zhabdrung.
