# The Zhabdrung's Legal Legacy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the order of Bhutan's great laws in the books' telling?
a) Constitution, then Kathrim, then Thrimzhung Chenmo  b) Kathrim, then Thrimzhung Chenmo, then the 2008 Constitution  c) Thrimzhung Chenmo, then Kathrim, then the Constitution  d) only the Constitution is named
**Answer: b) Kathrim, then Thrimzhung Chenmo, then the 2008 Constitution** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Under which king was the 2008 Constitution promulgated?
a) the fifth King  b) the first King  c) the third King  d) the Zhabdrung himself
**Answer: a) the fifth King** — That is what this lesson teaches.
