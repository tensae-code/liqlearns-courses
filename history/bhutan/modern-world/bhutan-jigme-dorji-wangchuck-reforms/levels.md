# Jigme Dorji Wangchuck's Reforms — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Father of Modern Bhutan builds the modern state in fifteen years: the National Assembly (Tshogdu) in 1953, serfdom abolished in 1956, the Thrimzhung Chenmo supreme law in 1959, and the court ladder completed with the High Court and the Bank of Bhutan in 1968. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
