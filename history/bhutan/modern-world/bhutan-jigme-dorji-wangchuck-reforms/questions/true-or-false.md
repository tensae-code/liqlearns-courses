# Jigme Dorji Wangchuck's Reforms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Thrimzhung Chenmo, the supreme law, dates to 1959.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The High Court and the Bank of Bhutan were founded under the second King.
**Answer: False** — Both come in 1968, under the third King, Jigme Dorji Wangchuck.
