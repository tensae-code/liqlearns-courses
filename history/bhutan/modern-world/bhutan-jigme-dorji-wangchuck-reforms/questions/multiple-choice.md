# Jigme Dorji Wangchuck's Reforms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the third King establish in 1953?
a) the National Assembly (Tshogdu)  b) the Bank of Bhutan  c) the High Court  d) the first Five-Year Plan
**Answer: a) the National Assembly (Tshogdu)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was abolished in 1956?
a) the monarchy  b) serfdom  c) the Tsatong tax  d) the dual system
**Answer: b) serfdom** — That is what this lesson teaches.
