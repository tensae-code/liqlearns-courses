# Jigme Dorji Wangchuck's Reforms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is Jigme Dorji Wangchuck called the Father of Modern Bhutan?
**Answer:** Because his reign built the modern state's skeleton: Assembly (1953), abolition of serfdom (1956), supreme law (1959), High Court and Bank of Bhutan (1968).

**2.** What is the Tshogdu?
**Answer:** The National Assembly, established in 1953 — Bhutan's first national assembly.
