# Hands-on — Jigme Dorji Wangchuck's Reforms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Four dates, four foundations: 1953, 1956, 1959, 1968. An assembly, a freedom, a supreme law, a high court and a bank. This lesson lays the four cornerstones in order.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Father of Modern Bhutan builds the modern state in fifteen years: the National Assembly (Tshogdu) in 1953, serfdom abolished in 1956, the Thrimzhung Chenmo supreme law in 1959, and the court ladder completed with the High Court and the Bank of Bhutan in 1968. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
