# Lesson plan — Jigme Dorji Wangchuck's Reforms

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Jigme Dorji Wangchuck's Reforms
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Reforming Kings and the Opening (CURRICULUM.md).
