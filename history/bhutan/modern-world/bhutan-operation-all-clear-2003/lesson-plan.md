# Lesson plan — Operation All Clear, 2003

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Operation All Clear, 2003
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Happiness, Decentralisation and Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
