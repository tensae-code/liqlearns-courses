# Video script — Operation All Clear, 2003

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
After talks with the ULFA, NDFB and KLO militants camped in southern Bhutan fail in late 2003, the fourth King personally leads the Royal Bhutan Army in flushing them out — remembered as his selfless, self-sacrificing act for national security.

Bhutan's hardest modern crisis grows quietly in its southern jungles. Three Indian militant groups — ULFA, the NDFB and the KLO — camp inside Bhutan's forests, using the kingdom as a base beyond India's reach. Bhutan talks first, for years: the camps must go. In late 2003 the talks fail, and the fourth King makes the decision the Class X book remembers above all his others: he personally leads the Royal Bhutan Army into the south and flushes the militants out. The books call it his selfless, self-sacrificing act for national security — a king who would give away his crown within three years first risks his life for the country's safety.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
