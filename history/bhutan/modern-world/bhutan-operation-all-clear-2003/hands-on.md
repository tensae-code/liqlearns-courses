# Hands-on — Operation All Clear, 2003

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Foreign armed groups have camped in your country's southern jungles for years, and talking has failed. The king does not send his army — he leads it. This lesson tells Bhutan's 2003 crisis.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After talks with the ULFA, NDFB and KLO militants camped in southern Bhutan fail in late 2003, the fourth King personally leads the Royal Bhutan Army in flushing them out — remembered as his selfless, self-sacrificing act for national security. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
