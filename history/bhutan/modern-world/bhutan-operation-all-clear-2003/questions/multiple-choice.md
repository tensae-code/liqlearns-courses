# Operation All Clear, 2003 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which groups were camped in southern Bhutan?
a) ULFA, NDFB and KLO  b) the Gurkha Rifles  c) Tibetan armies  d) the Eden mission
**Answer: a) ULFA, NDFB and KLO** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the King do when talks failed in late 2003?
a) asked Britain for troops  b) personally led the Royal Bhutan Army to flush the militants out  c) signed the camps a lease  d) left the country
**Answer: b) personally led the Royal Bhutan Army to flush the militants out** — That is what this lesson teaches.
