# Operation All Clear, 2003 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why were the camps a crisis for Bhutan?
**Answer:** Foreign militant groups were using Bhutan's southern jungles as a base — a threat to Bhutan's security and its relations with India.

**2.** How do the books remember the King's 2003 act?
**Answer:** As his selfless, self-sacrificing act for national security — leading the army in person after talks failed.
