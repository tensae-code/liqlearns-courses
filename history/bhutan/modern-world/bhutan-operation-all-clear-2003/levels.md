# Operation All Clear, 2003 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After talks with the ULFA, NDFB and KLO militants camped in southern Bhutan fail in late 2003, the fourth King personally leads the Royal Bhutan Army in flushing them out — remembered as his selfless, self-sacrificing act for national security. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
