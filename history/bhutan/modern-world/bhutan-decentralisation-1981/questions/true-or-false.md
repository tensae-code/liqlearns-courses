# Decentralisation, 1981 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Dzongkhag Yargay Tshogdu begins the decentralisation story.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Decentralisation began only after the 2008 elections.
**Answer: False** — It was formalised in 1981 — the training ground that prepared democracy.
