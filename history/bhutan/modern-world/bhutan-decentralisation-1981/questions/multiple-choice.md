# Decentralisation, 1981 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year was decentralisation formalised?
a) 1974  b) 1981  c) 2001  d) 2008
**Answer: b) 1981** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** To which levels was planning pushed down?
a) to the dzongkhags (districts) and the gewogs (village blocks)  b) only to the capital's ministries  c) to foreign advisers  d) to the army
**Answer: a) to the dzongkhags (districts) and the gewogs (village blocks)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
