# Decentralisation, 1981 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is a dzongdag?
**Answer:** A district head, appointed under decentralisation to carry real responsibility for a dzongkhag.

**2.** Why do the books call decentralisation a training ground?
**Answer:** Because deciding locally — in districts and gewogs — practised the people in self-government before national democracy arrived.
