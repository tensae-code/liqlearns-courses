# Hands-on — Decentralisation, 1981

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Power that sits only in the capital cannot hear a village. In 1981 Bhutan's king starts pushing power downhill — to districts, then to village blocks. This lesson follows the power down.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Beginning with the Dzongkhag Yargay Tshogdu, the fourth King formalises decentralisation in 1981 — appointing dzongdags and pushing planning down to districts and gewogs as the training ground for democracy. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
