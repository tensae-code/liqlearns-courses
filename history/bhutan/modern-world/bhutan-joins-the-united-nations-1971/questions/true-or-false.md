# Bhutan Joins the United Nations, 1971 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** India sponsored Bhutan's entry to the United Nations.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Bhutan and India exchanged resident representatives in 1969.
**Answer: False** — The exchange came in 1971, the same year as the UN seat.
