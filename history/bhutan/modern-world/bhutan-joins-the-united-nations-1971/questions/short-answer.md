# Bhutan Joins the United Nations, 1971 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the order — post union 1969, UN 1971 — matter?
**Answer:** It shows the opening as deliberate steps: Bhutan joins the world's systems one by one, on a dated path, not in a rush.

**2.** What diplomatic step accompanied the UN seat in 1971?
**Answer:** Bhutan and India exchanged resident representatives.
