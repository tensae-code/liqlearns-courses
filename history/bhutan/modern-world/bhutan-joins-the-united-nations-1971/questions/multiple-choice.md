# Bhutan Joins the United Nations, 1971 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** On what date did Bhutan take its UN seat?
a) 15 August 1947  b) 21 September 1971  c) 26 May 1966  d) 18 July 2008
**Answer: b) 21 September 1971** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which body did Bhutan join in 1969, before the UN?
a) the Universal Postal Union  b) the World Bank  c) the Commonwealth  d) ASEAN
**Answer: a) the Universal Postal Union** — That is what this lesson teaches.
