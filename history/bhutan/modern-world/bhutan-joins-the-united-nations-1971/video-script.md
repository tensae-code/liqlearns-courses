# Video script — Bhutan Joins the United Nations, 1971

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
After joining the Universal Postal Union in 1969, Bhutan — sponsored by India — takes its seat at the United Nations on 21 September 1971, and exchanges resident representatives with India the same year.

The opening reaches the world stage in steps, and the Class IX book dates each one. In 1969 Bhutan joins the Universal Postal Union — its stamps and letters enter the world's system. Then, sponsored by India, Bhutan takes its seat at the United Nations on 21 September 1971: the Himalayan kingdom, self-isolated within living memory, now sits as a member among the nations of the earth. The same year, Bhutan and India exchange resident representatives, diplomacy made permanent and mutual. The sequence is the third King's statecraft in miniature — step by dated step, Bhutan joins the world without ceasing to be itself.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
