# Bhutan Joins the United Nations, 1971

## Do it first

A flag rises outside a great building in New York that has never flown there before. The country behind it has fewer people than many cities. This lesson raises Bhutan's flag at the UN.

The opening reaches the world stage in steps, and the Class IX book dates each one. In 1969 Bhutan joins the Universal Postal Union — its stamps and letters enter the world's system. Then, sponsored by India, Bhutan takes its seat at the United Nations on 21 September 1971: the Himalayan kingdom, self-isolated within living memory, now sits as a member among the nations of the earth. The same year, Bhutan and India exchange resident representatives, diplomacy made permanent and mutual. The sequence is the third King's statecraft in miniature — step by dated step, Bhutan joins the world without ceasing to be itself.
