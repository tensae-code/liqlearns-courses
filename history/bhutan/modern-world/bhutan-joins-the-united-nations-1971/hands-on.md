# Hands-on — Bhutan Joins the United Nations, 1971

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A flag rises outside a great building in New York that has never flown there before. The country behind it has fewer people than many cities. This lesson raises Bhutan's flag at the UN.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: After joining the Universal Postal Union in 1969, Bhutan — sponsored by India — takes its seat at the United Nations on 21 September 1971, and exchanges resident representatives with India the same year. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
