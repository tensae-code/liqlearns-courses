# Lesson plan — Bhutan Joins the United Nations, 1971

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bhutan Joins the United Nations, 1971
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Reforming Kings and the Opening (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After joining the Universal Postal Union in 1969, Bhutan — sponsored by India — takes its seat at the United Nations on 21 September 1971, and exchanges resident representatives with India the same year.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — The Reforming Kings and the Opening (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: After joining the Universal Postal Union in 1969, Bhutan — sponsored by India — takes its seat at the United Nations on 21 September 1971, and exchanges resident representatives with India the same year.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: joining any worldwide body — a sports federation, a student network — as a full member in your own name: 1971 in miniature.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
