# Video script — Jigme Namgyel the Unifier

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
From Trongsa, Jigme Namgyel mediates and fights his way through the Desi civil wars, keeps the east's taxes out of Punakha's hands, and over 38 years lays the stable foundation on which the Wangchuck dynasty will stand.

The second half of the Class VII telling is about holding. From Trongsa, Jigme Namgyel works through the Desi civil wars — the struggles of the dual system's last, fractious decades — sometimes mediating, sometimes fighting, always keeping his seat at the country's centre. His grip on the east is the key: he keeps the eastern taxes out of Punakha's hands, starving the chaos of its money. Thirty-eight years of this holding is the point of the story. When he is done, Bhutan has something it has lacked for generations — a stable foundation — and on that foundation his own family, the Wangchucks, will build the monarchy.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Bhutan.
