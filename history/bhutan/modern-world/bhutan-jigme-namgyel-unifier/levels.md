# Jigme Namgyel the Unifier — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — From Trongsa, Jigme Namgyel mediates and fights his way through the Desi civil wars, keeps the east's taxes out of Punakha's hands, and over 38 years lays the stable foundation on which the Wangchuck dynasty will stand. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
