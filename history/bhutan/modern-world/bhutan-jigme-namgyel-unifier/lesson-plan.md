# Lesson plan — Jigme Namgyel the Unifier

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Jigme Namgyel the Unifier
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Trongsa, the British Encounter and the Road to Kingship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From Trongsa, Jigme Namgyel mediates and fights his way through the Desi civil wars, keeps the east's taxes out of Punakha's hands, and over 38 years lays the stable foundation on which the Wangchuck dynasty will stand.
