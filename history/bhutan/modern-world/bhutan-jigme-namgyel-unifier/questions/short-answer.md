# Jigme Namgyel the Unifier — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did keeping the east's taxes matter?
**Answer:** It starved the civil-war chaos of money and kept the east loyal to Trongsa's steady rule.

**2.** What is Jigme Namgyel's place in the dynasty's story?
**Answer:** He laid, over 38 years, the stable foundation on which the Wangchuck dynasty stands.
