# Jigme Namgyel the Unifier — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Jigme Namgyel both mediated and fought through the Desi civil wars.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Wangchuck dynasty stands on a foundation laid by a foreign power.
**Answer: False** — It stands on the stable foundation Jigme Namgyel laid over 38 years from Trongsa.
