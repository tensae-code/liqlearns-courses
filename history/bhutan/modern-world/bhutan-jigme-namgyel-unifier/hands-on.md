# Hands-on — Jigme Namgyel the Unifier

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
One strong seat, a country tearing itself apart in civil wars around it. Your job: hold the centre for thirty-eight years. This lesson watches the holder at work.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: From Trongsa, Jigme Namgyel mediates and fights his way through the Desi civil wars, keeps the east's taxes out of Punakha's hands, and over 38 years lays the stable foundation on which the Wangchuck dynasty will stand. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
