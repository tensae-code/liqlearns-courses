# Lesson plan — The Zhabdrung Arrives and Unifies Bhutan

**Course:** History — Bhutan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Zhabdrung Arrives and Unifies Bhutan
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Zhabdrung Unifies Bhutan (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ngawang Namgyal, the Zhabdrung — born at Ralung in Tibet in 1594, driven south by a dispute over his incarnation and the relic he would not surrender — enters a Bhutan of warring local rulers through Gasa in 1616, and dzong by dzong brings the valleys under one rule.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Zhabdrung Unifies Bhutan (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

3. **Chapter One, Arrival to the Land of Lhomon / Western Unification** — The Class IX book picks him up on Bhutanese soil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks The Zhabdrung came to Bhutan as a conqueror from the start → The books tell of an exile: enthroned at Ralung, challenged by a rival claimant, he refuses to surrender the Rangjung Kharsapani relic and flees south — unification comes after arrival.
- Go back to the one thing: Ngawang Namgyal, the Zhabdrung — born at Ralung in Tibet in 1594, driven south by a dispute over his incarnation and the relic he would not surrender — enters a Bhutan of warring local rulers through Gasa in 1616, and dzong by dzong brings the valleys under one rule.
