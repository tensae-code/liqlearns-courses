# The Zhabdrung Arrives and Unifies Bhutan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A dream of a raven leading him south is part of the books' story of the Zhabdrung's flight.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Bhutan was already one united kingdom when the Zhabdrung arrived.
**Answer: False** — He entered a land of warring local rulers and brought the valleys under one rule himself.
