# The Zhabdrung Arrives and Unifies Bhutan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Through where did the Zhabdrung enter Bhutan in 1616?
a) through Phuentsholing  b) through Gasa  c) through Paro by ship  d) through Bumthang from India
**Answer: b) through Gasa** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) What did the Zhabdrung refuse to surrender to his rival?
a) the Rangjung Kharsapani relic  b) his monastery at Ralung  c) his title only  d) a herd of yaks
**Answer: a) the Rangjung Kharsapani relic** — That is what this lesson teaches.
