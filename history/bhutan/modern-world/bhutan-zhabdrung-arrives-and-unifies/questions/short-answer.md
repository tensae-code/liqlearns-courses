# The Zhabdrung Arrives and Unifies Bhutan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why was the Zhabdrung's incarnation disputed?
**Answer:** A rival claimant, backed by the Tsangpa ruler, challenged his enthronement at Ralung as Pema Karpo's incarnation.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How did he unify western Bhutan, in the Class IX telling?
**Answer:** Dzong by dzong — each fortress a joint seat of his government and monk body — bringing the western valleys under one rule.
