# Dzongs: Fortresses of Unification — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What three materials build a dzong?
**Answer:** Stone, wood and mud — fitted without nails.

**2.** Why are dzongs called a physical record of unification?
**Answer:** Because their sequence across three periods marks, region by region, the stages of Bhutan coming under one order.
