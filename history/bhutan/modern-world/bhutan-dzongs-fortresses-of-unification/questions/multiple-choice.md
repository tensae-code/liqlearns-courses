# Dzongs: Fortresses of Unification — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What stands at the centre of a dzong?
a) a market square  b) the utse, the towering central tower  c) a royal palace only  d) a granary
**Answer: b) the utse, the towering central tower** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How are dzongs sorted in the Class VIII book?
a) into early medieval, medieval and modern periods  b) by size only  c) by colour of roof  d) they are not sorted
**Answer: a) into early medieval, medieval and modern periods** — That is what this lesson teaches.
