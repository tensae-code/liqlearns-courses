# Dzongs: Fortresses of Unification — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dzongs are built of stone, wood and mud without nails.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Dzongs record nothing about unification; they are purely decorative.
**Answer: False** — The books read them as the physical record of Bhutan being unified, region by region.
