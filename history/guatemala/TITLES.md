# History — Guatemala course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Guatemala's
own — built from Guatemala's Ciencias Sociales y Formación
Ciudadana books (Ministerio de Educación de Guatemala,
Spanish): the Grade 4 student book, which tells the national
story as one continuous timeline, and four teacher guides —
the second-cycle primary guide and the three básico guides
(Juntos construimos la paz) — whose history parts deepen the
same story. Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside the
levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grade each book comes from lives only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': find history where you
live first, then walk the timeline the Grade 4 book walks —
first peoples and the Maya, conquest and colony,
independence, reform, revolution, war and peace — with the
guides' own teachings (the four peoples' ways of knowing,
the nawales, Maya justice, racism's roots) stocked where
the story reaches them. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
