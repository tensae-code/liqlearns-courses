# Guatemala's Living Traditions: Cofradías, Dances and Festivals — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Guatemala's living traditions are its history performed: colonial cofradías — brotherhoods — still keep the saints' feasts alive, and each people turns its own past into dance, costume and the sung corrido ballads of its festivals, from carnival and jaripeo to the mengalas of Amatitlán.
