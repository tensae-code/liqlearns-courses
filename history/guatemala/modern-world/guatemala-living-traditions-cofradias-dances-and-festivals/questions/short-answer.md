# Guatemala's Living Traditions: Cofradías, Dances and Festivals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Cofradías, dances and festivities.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because guatemala's living traditions are its history performed: colonial cofradías — brotherhoods — still keep the saints' feasts alive, and each people turns its own past into dance, costume and the sung corrido ballads of its festivals, from carnival and jaripeo to the mengalas of Amatitlán. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
