# Guatemala's Living Traditions: Cofradías, Dances and Festivals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Cofradías, dances and festivities” is right?
a) Colonial brotherhoods keep saints' feasts alive; each people turns its history into dance, costume and sung corrido ballads.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Guatemala's living traditions are its history performed: colonial cofradías — brotherhoods — still keep the saints' feasts alive, and each people turns its own past into dance, costume and the sung corrido ballads of its festivals, from carnival and jaripeo to the mengalas of Amatitlán.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Guatemala has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
