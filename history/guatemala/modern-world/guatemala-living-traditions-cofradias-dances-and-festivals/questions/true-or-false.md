# Guatemala's Living Traditions: Cofradías, Dances and Festivals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Colonial brotherhoods keep saints' feasts alive; each people turns its history into dance, costume and sung corrido ballads.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Guatemala's living traditions are its history performed: colonial cofradías — brotherhoods — still keep the saints' feasts alive, and each people turns its own past into dance, costume and the sung corrido ballads of its festivals, from carnival and jaripeo to the mengalas of Amatitlán.
