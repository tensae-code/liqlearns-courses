# Guatemala: Racism and Its Historical Roots — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A Maya K'iche' anthropologist in gala traditional dress is turned away from a Guatemala City restaurant while ladina companions are let in; her case becomes a landmark in the national fight against racism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Racism is defined as a relation of domination built on telling peoples apart to strip their goods, its prejudices formed across history; in Guatemala the Maya peoples meet it daily — in schools, hospitals, buses and government — in everyday phrases the guide lists and names as racist.
**Answer: True**
