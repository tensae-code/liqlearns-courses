# Guatemala: Racism and Its Historical Roots — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The 2002 restaurant exclusion of Dr Irma Alicia Velásquez Nimatuj; The content sheet "El racismo escolar".

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because racism in Guatemala is a relation of domination built across history — peoples told apart so their goods could be stripped — and it is still lived daily by the Maya peoples: in 2002 the anthropologist Irma Alicia Velásquez Nimatuj, in gala traditional dress, was turned away from a Guatemala City restaurant while her ladina companions were let in, and her case became a landmark. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
