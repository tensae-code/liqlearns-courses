# Video script — Guatemala: Racism and Its Historical Roots

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Racism in Guatemala is a relation of domination built across history — peoples told apart so their goods could be stripped — and it is still lived daily by the Maya peoples: in 2002 the anthropologist Irma Alicia Velásquez Nimatuj, in gala traditional dress, was turned away from a Guatemala City restaurant while her ladina companions were let in, and her case became a landmark.

In 2002, Dr Irma Alicia Velásquez Nimatuj — a Maya K'iche' anthropologist — arrived at a Guatemala City restaurant in gala traditional dress, with ladina companions.

The guide's content sheet on school racism defines its subject with care.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
