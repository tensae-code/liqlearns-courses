# Guatemala's Four Peoples: Maya, Garífuna, Xinka, Ladino — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Four peoples share one country — Maya rooted before the conquest, Garífuna descended from Africans brought as slaves, Xinka traders of the south coast, Ladino born of colonial mixing — and the Peace Accords recognise them all.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Four peoples, four ways of knowing — Xinka learning centred on the Moon and community, Maya gratitude and the value of work (mitijul) handed down from the first four couples, Garífuna spirituality of sea and ancestors, Ladino identity still being built.
**Answer: True**
