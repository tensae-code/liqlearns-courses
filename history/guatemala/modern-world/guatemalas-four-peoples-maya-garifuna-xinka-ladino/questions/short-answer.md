# Guatemala's Four Peoples: Maya, Garífuna, Xinka, Ladino — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The four peoples, their cosmovisions and the linguistic map; Four ways of knowing (second cycle of primary); A society of many peoples (Grade 5 activities); Four ways of knowing (primero básico); Four ways of knowing (segundo básico); Four ways of knowing (tercero básico).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because one country, four peoples: the Maya, rooted before the conquest; the Garífuna, descended from Africans brought as slaves; the Xinka, traders of the south coast; and the Ladino, born of colonial mixing — each with its own way of knowing, and all recognised together by the Peace Accords. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
