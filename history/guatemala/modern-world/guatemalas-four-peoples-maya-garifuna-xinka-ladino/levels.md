# Guatemala's Four Peoples: Maya, Garífuna, Xinka, Ladino — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The four peoples, their cosmovisions and the linguistic map
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Four peoples share one country — Maya rooted before the conquest, Garífuna descended from Africans brought as slaves, Xinka traders of the south coast, Ladino born of colonial mixing — and the Peace Accords recognise them all. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Four peoples, four ways of knowing — Xinka learning centred on the Moon and community, Maya gratitude and the value of work (mitijul) handed down from the first four couples, Garífuna spirituality of sea and ancestors, Ladino identity still being built. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
