# Video script — Guatemala's Four Peoples: Maya, Garífuna, Xinka, Ladino

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
One country, four peoples: the Maya, rooted before the conquest; the Garífuna, descended from Africans brought as slaves; the Xinka, traders of the south coast; and the Ladino, born of colonial mixing — each with its own way of knowing, and all recognised together by the Peace Accords.

Guatemala is not one people but four, and the Grade 4 book names them plainly.

The primary teacher guide opens with how each people learns.

For Grade 5, the guide makes the four peoples into games a child can hold.

The primero básico guide restates the peoples' ways of learning for older pupils.

The segundo básico guide carries the same opening section as the rest of its series: the ancestral foundations, restated for older pupils.

The tercero básico guide opens as the whole series opens: Xinka learning under the Moon and in community, Maya gratitude and mitijul, the value of diligent work, Garífuna spirituality of sea and ancestors.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
