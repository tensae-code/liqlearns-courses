# Guatemala's Colonial Era — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The four foundings of Santiago de Guatemala
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Founded at Iximche' in 1524, the capital flees Kaqchikel revolt to Almolonga, is drowned by Volcán de Agua mud in 1541, rebuilt at Panchoy for 230 years, then the Santa Marta earthquakes of 1773 drive it to the Ermita valley as Nueva Guatemala. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colonial society: the pyramid and the labour drafts
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Captured indigenous people are enslaved and wash gold; the 1542 New Laws abolish slavery but tribute and the weekly repartimiento labour draft take its place under peninsulares, criollos, castas, indigenous and Black slaves. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Colonial economy, money and learning
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Cacao, cotton and indigo pay the colony's way, cacao beans still pass as coins until the 1733 mint; friars teach reading to teach the faith, the first press arrives in 1660 and San Carlos University in 1676 — women may only attend primary school. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
