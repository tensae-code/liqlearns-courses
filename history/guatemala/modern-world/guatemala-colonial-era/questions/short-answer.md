# Guatemala's Colonial Era — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The four foundings of Santiago de Guatemala; Colonial society: the pyramid and the labour drafts; Colonial economy, money and learning.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because colonial Guatemala was built four times over: a capital founded at Iximche' in 1524 that fled revolt, drowning and earthquakes to its present valley; a society stacked in a pyramid held down by slavery, encomienda and the weekly repartimiento labour draft; and an economy of cacao, cotton and indigo whose cacao beans passed as coins until the mint of 1733. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
