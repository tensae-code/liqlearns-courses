# Guatemala's Colonial Era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Founded at Iximche' in 1524, the capital flees Kaqchikel revolt to Almolonga, is drowned by Volcán de Agua mud in 1541, rebuilt at Panchoy for 230 years, then the Santa Marta earthquakes of 1773 drive it to the Ermita valley as Nueva Guatemala.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Captured indigenous people are enslaved and wash gold; the 1542 New Laws abolish slavery but tribute and the weekly repartimiento labour draft take its place under peninsulares, criollos, castas, indigenous and Black slaves.
**Answer: True**
