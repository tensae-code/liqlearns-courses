# Video script — Guatemala's Colonial Era

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Colonial Guatemala was built four times over: a capital founded at Iximche' in 1524 that fled revolt, drowning and earthquakes to its present valley; a society stacked in a pyramid held down by slavery, encomienda and the weekly repartimiento labour draft; and an economy of cacao, cotton and indigo whose cacao beans passed as coins until the mint of 1733.

Guatemala's capital is a city that would not stay still.

Colonial society was a pyramid, and the book draws it plainly.

What paid for the colony? Cacao, cotton and deep-blue indigo, grown for export.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
