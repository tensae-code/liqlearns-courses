# Video script — Guatemala: Annexation, Federation and Morazán

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Free of Spain, Central America was annexed to Iturbide's Mexico in 1822, declared absolute independence when he fell, abolished slavery in 1824 — and then built a Federation whose liberal reforms under Morazán and Gálvez broke against cholera and Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion in 1838.

Independence lasted months before the new provinces were annexed, in 1822, to Iturbide's Mexican empire.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
