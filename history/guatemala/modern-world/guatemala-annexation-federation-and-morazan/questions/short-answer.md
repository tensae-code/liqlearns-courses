# Guatemala: Annexation, Federation and Morazán — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Annexation to Mexico and the Federal Republic of Central America.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because free of Spain, Central America was annexed to Iturbide's Mexico in 1822, declared absolute independence when he fell, abolished slavery in 1824 — and then built a Federation whose liberal reforms under Morazán and Gálvez broke against cholera and Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion in 1838. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
