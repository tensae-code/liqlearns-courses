# Guatemala: Annexation, Federation and Morazán — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Annexed to Iturbide's Mexico in 1822, Central America declares absolute independence when he falls, loses Chiapas and Soconusco, abolishes slavery in 1824; Morazán and Gálvez push liberal reforms until cholera and Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion break the Federation in 1838.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Free of Spain, Central America was annexed to Iturbide's Mexico in 1822, declared absolute independence when he fell, abolished slavery in 1824 — and then built a Federation whose liberal reforms under Morazán and Gálvez broke against cholera and Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion in 1838.
