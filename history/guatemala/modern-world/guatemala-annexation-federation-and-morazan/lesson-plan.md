# Lesson plan — Guatemala: Annexation, Federation and Morazán

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala: Annexation, Federation and Morazán
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Free of Spain, Central America was annexed to Iturbide's Mexico in 1822, declared absolute independence when he fell, abolished slavery in 1824 — and then built a Federation whose liberal reforms under Morazán and Gálvez broke against cholera and Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion in 1838.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (recommended, never forced).
