# Guatemala: Annexation, Federation and Morazán — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Free of Spain, Central America was annexed to Iturbide's Mexico in 1822, declared absolute independence when he fell, abolished slavery in 1824 — and then built a Federation whose liberal reforms under Morazán and Gálvez broke against cholera and Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion in 1838.
