# Lesson plan — Exploration: Causes and Results

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Causes and Results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Europe went looking for a new road east because its world had turned: Copernicus and Galileo moved the Sun to the centre, Gutenberg's press spread the new maps, and the Turks' capture of Constantinople blocked the old spice road — another way had to be found.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (recommended, never forced).
