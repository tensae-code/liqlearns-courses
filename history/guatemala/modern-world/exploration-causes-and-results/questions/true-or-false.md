# Exploration: Causes and Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Copernicus and Galileo move the Sun to the centre, Gutenberg's press spreads the new maps, the Turks take Constantinople and block the spice road — Europe must find another way east.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Europe went looking for a new road east because its world had turned: Copernicus and Galileo moved the Sun to the centre, Gutenberg's press spread the new maps, and the Turks' capture of Constantinople blocked the old spice road — another way had to be found.
