# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Europe in the fifteenth century” is right?
a) Copernicus and Galileo move the Sun to the centre, Gutenberg's press spreads the new maps, the Turks take Constantinople and block the spice road — Europe must find another way east.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Europe went looking for a new road east because its world had turned: Copernicus and Galileo moved the Sun to the centre, Gutenberg's press spread the new maps, and the Turks' capture of Constantinople blocked the old spice road — another way had to be found.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Guatemala has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
