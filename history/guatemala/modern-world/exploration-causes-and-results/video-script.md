# Video script — Exploration: Causes and Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Europe went looking for a new road east because its world had turned: Copernicus and Galileo moved the Sun to the centre, Gutenberg's press spread the new maps, and the Turks' capture of Constantinople blocked the old spice road — another way had to be found.

Why did Europe's ships suddenly cross unknown oceans? The book answers with fifteenth-century Europe itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
