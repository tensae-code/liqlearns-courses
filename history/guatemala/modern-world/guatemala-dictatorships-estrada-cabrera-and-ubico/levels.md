# Guatemala's Dictatorships: Estrada Cabrera and Ubico — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Two dictatorships bracket Guatemala's early twentieth century: Estrada Cabrera (1898–1920), self-styled protector of studious youth, ruling through eruption, earthquakes and flu until the Assembly declared him insane; and Ubico (1931–1944), who built palaces and roads on forced labour until the street protests of June 1944 brought him down.
