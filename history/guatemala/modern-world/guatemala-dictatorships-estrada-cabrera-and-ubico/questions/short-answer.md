# Guatemala's Dictatorships: Estrada Cabrera and Ubico — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The governments of Estrada Cabrera and Ubico.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because two dictatorships bracket Guatemala's early twentieth century: Estrada Cabrera (1898–1920), self-styled protector of studious youth, ruling through eruption, earthquakes and flu until the Assembly declared him insane; and Ubico (1931–1944), who built palaces and roads on forced labour until the street protests of June 1944 brought him down. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
