# Guatemala's Dictatorships: Estrada Cabrera and Ubico — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Estrada Cabrera rules as self-styled protector of studious youth amid eruption, earthquakes and flu, until the Assembly declares him insane; Ubico builds palaces and roads on forced labour until June 1944 street protests bring him down.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Two dictatorships bracket Guatemala's early twentieth century: Estrada Cabrera (1898–1920), self-styled protector of studious youth, ruling through eruption, earthquakes and flu until the Assembly declared him insane; and Ubico (1931–1944), who built palaces and roads on forced labour until the street protests of June 1944 brought him down.
