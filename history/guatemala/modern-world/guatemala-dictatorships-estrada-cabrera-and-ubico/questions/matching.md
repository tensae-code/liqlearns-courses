# Guatemala's Dictatorships: Estrada Cabrera and Ubico — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Guatemala's Dictatorships: Estrada Cabrera and Ubico — a)

**2.** Ubico — b)


Meanings:
a) Two dictatorships bracket Guatemala's early twentieth century: Estrada Cabrera (1898–1920), self-styled protector of studious youth, ruling through eruption, earthquakes and flu until the Assembly declared him insane; and Ubico (1931–1944), who built palaces and roads on forced labour until the street protests of June 1944 brought him down.
b) A key word of this lesson: Guatemala's Dictatorships: Estrada Cabrera and Ubico.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
