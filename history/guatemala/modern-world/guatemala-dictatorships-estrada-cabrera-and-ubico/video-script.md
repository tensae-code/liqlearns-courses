# Video script — Guatemala's Dictatorships: Estrada Cabrera and Ubico

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Two dictatorships bracket Guatemala's early twentieth century: Estrada Cabrera (1898–1920), self-styled protector of studious youth, ruling through eruption, earthquakes and flu until the Assembly declared him insane; and Ubico (1931–1944), who built palaces and roads on forced labour until the street protests of June 1944 brought him down.

For twenty-two years Manuel Estrada Cabrera ruled Guatemala, styling himself the protector of studious youth while his police watched everyone else.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
