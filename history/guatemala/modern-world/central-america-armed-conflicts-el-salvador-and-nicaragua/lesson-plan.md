# Lesson plan — Central America's Armed Conflicts: El Salvador and Nicaragua

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Central America's Armed Conflicts: El Salvador and Nicaragua
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Guatemala's war was not alone: El Salvador's FMLN war ended at Chapultepec in 1992, and Nicaragua's Sandinistas toppled the Somoza dynasty in 1979, fought the Contras, and lost the 1990 election to Violeta Chamorro — three neighbouring wars, three different endings.

- Thinks all of Central America's wars ended the same way → Nicaragua's ended at the ballot box in 1990, El Salvador's at a negotiating table in 1992, Guatemala's not until 1996 — the book teaches the endings separately on purpose.
- Go back to the one thing: Guatemala's war was not alone: El Salvador's FMLN war ended at Chapultepec in 1992, and Nicaragua's Sandinistas toppled the Somoza dynasty in 1979, fought the Contras, and lost the 1990 election to Violeta Chamorro — three neighbouring wars, three different endings.
