# Central America's Armed Conflicts: El Salvador and Nicaragua — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Armed conflicts in Central America.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because guatemala's war was not alone: El Salvador's FMLN war ended at Chapultepec in 1992, and Nicaragua's Sandinistas toppled the Somoza dynasty in 1979, fought the Contras, and lost the 1990 election to Violeta Chamorro — three neighbouring wars, three different endings. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
