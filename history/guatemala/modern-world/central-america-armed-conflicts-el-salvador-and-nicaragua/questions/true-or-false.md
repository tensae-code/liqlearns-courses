# Central America's Armed Conflicts: El Salvador and Nicaragua — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** El Salvador's FMLN war ends at Chapultepec in 1992; Nicaragua's Sandinistas topple the Somoza dynasty in 1979, fight the Contras, and lose the 1990 election to Violeta Chamorro.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Guatemala's war was not alone: El Salvador's FMLN war ended at Chapultepec in 1992, and Nicaragua's Sandinistas toppled the Somoza dynasty in 1979, fought the Contras, and lost the 1990 election to Violeta Chamorro — three neighbouring wars, three different endings.
