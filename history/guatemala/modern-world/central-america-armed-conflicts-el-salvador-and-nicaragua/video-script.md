# Video script — Central America's Armed Conflicts: El Salvador and Nicaragua

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Guatemala's war was not alone: El Salvador's FMLN war ended at Chapultepec in 1992, and Nicaragua's Sandinistas toppled the Somoza dynasty in 1979, fought the Contras, and lost the 1990 election to Violeta Chamorro — three neighbouring wars, three different endings.

Guatemala's war burned inside a burning region.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
