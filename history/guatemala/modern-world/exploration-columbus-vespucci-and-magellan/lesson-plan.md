# Lesson plan — Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Columbus, refused by Portugal, sold his westward plan to Isabel and Fernando and reached a land he died calling Asia; Balboa then reached the Pacific, Vespucci argued this was a new continent and lent it his name — America — and the capitulación system turned conquest into a signed business of money, swords, land and titles.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The discovery of America: Columbus's four voyages** — Refused by Portugal, Columbus sells his westward plan to Isabel and Fernando; Rodrigo de Triana sights land on 12 October 1492; four voyages follow, the last tracing Honduras to Panama, and Columbus dies still calling it Asia.
2. **Other discoveries and the Capitulación** — Balboa reaches the Pacific, Vespucci argues this is a new continent and lends it his name; would-be conquerors sign a capitulación with the king — their money and swords for land, titles and a share of riches.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
