# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The discovery of America: Columbus's four voyages — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Other discoveries and the Capitulación — b)


Meanings:
a) Refused by Portugal, Columbus sells his westward plan to Isabel and Fernando; Rodrigo de Triana sights land on 12 October 1492; four voyages follow, the last tracing Honduras to Panama, and Columbus dies still calling it Asia.
b) Balboa reaches the Pacific, Vespucci argues this is a new continent and lends it his name; would-be conquerors sign a capitulación with the king — their money and swords for land, titles and a share of riches.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
