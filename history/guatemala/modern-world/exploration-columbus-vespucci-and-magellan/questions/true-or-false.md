# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Refused by Portugal, Columbus sells his westward plan to Isabel and Fernando; Rodrigo de Triana sights land on 12 October 1492; four voyages follow, the last tracing Honduras to Panama, and Columbus dies still calling it Asia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Balboa reaches the Pacific, Vespucci argues this is a new continent and lends it his name; would-be conquerors sign a capitulación with the king — their money and swords for land, titles and a share of riches.
**Answer: True**
