# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The discovery of America: Columbus's four voyages; Other discoveries and the Capitulación.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because columbus, refused by Portugal, sold his westward plan to Isabel and Fernando and reached a land he died calling Asia; Balboa then reached the Pacific, Vespucci argued this was a new continent and lent it his name — America — and the capitulación system turned conquest into a signed business of money, swords, land and titles. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
