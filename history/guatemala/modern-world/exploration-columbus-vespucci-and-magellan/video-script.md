# Video script — Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Columbus, refused by Portugal, sold his westward plan to Isabel and Fernando and reached a land he died calling Asia; Balboa then reached the Pacific, Vespucci argued this was a new continent and lent it his name — America — and the capitulación system turned conquest into a signed business of money, swords, land and titles.

Columbus had a plan: sail west across the ocean and arrive in Asia from the other side.

Others saw clearer.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
