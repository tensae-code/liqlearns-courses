# Guatemala's Revolution of 1944 and the Spring — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Revolution of 1944 and the governments of Arévalo and Arbenz (1945–1954) — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The governments of Castillo Armas and Ydígoras Fuentes — b)


Meanings:
a) Students, teachers and young officers overthrow Ubico's successor on 20 October 1944; Arévalo builds schools, a labour code and social security; Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touches United Fruit land — and in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrows him.
b) Castillo Armas annuls the 1945 constitution, returns the expropriated land and bans unions before his 1957 assassination; under Ydígoras the closed political space breeds the MR-13 rebels of 1962, seed of the guerrilla.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
