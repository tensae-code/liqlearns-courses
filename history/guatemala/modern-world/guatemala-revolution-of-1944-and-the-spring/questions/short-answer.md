# Guatemala's Revolution of 1944 and the Spring — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Revolution of 1944 and the governments of Arévalo and Arbenz (1945–1954); The governments of Castillo Armas and Ydígoras Fuentes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because on 20 October 1944 students, teachers and young officers overthrew Ubico's successor and opened Guatemala's Spring: Arévalo built schools, a labour code and social security, and Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touched United Fruit's land — until in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrew him, and Castillo Armas and Ydígoras Fuentes closed the political space until it bred rebellion. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
