# Guatemala's Revolution of 1944 and the Spring — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Students, teachers and young officers overthrow Ubico's successor on 20 October 1944; Arévalo builds schools, a labour code and social security; Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touches United Fruit land — and in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrows him.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Castillo Armas annuls the 1945 constitution, returns the expropriated land and bans unions before his 1957 assassination; under Ydígoras the closed political space breeds the MR-13 rebels of 1962, seed of the guerrilla.
**Answer: True**
