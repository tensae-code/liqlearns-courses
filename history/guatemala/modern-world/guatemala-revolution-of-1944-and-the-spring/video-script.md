# Video script — Guatemala's Revolution of 1944 and the Spring

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
On 20 October 1944 students, teachers and young officers overthrew Ubico's successor and opened Guatemala's Spring: Arévalo built schools, a labour code and social security, and Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touched United Fruit's land — until in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrew him, and Castillo Armas and Ydígoras Fuentes closed the political space until it bred rebellion.

The protests that felled Ubico did not stop with him.

The man the coup installed, Carlos Castillo Armas, undid the Spring piece by piece: the 1945 constitution annulled, the expropriated land returned, the unions banned.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
