## Sub lesson 1 — The Revolution of 1944 and the governments of Arévalo and Arbenz (1945–1954)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Students, teachers and young officers overthrow Ubico's successor on 20 October 1944; Arévalo builds schools, a labour code and social security; Arbenz's Decree 900 land reform touches United Fruit land — and in 1954 the army, backed by the United States, overthrows him. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
