# Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invade from Mexico and win on 30 June 1871; Barrios strips the Church, orders free secular schooling, brings railway, telegraph and coffee — and dies at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — The Liberal Reform of 1871, won by García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invading from Mexico, rebuilt Guatemala in a generation: the Church stripped, free secular schooling ordered, railway, telegraph and coffee planted across the country — until Barrios died at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force.
