# Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “The Liberal Reform” is right?
a) García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invade from Mexico and win on 30 June 1871; Barrios strips the Church, orders free secular schooling, brings railway, telegraph and coffee — and dies at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The Liberal Reform of 1871, won by García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invading from Mexico, rebuilt Guatemala in a generation: the Church stripped, free secular schooling ordered, railway, telegraph and coffee planted across the country — until Barrios died at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Guatemala has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
