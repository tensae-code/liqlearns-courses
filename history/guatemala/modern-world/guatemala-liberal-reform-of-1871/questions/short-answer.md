# Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Liberal Reform.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because the Liberal Reform of 1871, won by García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invading from Mexico, rebuilt Guatemala in a generation: the Church stripped, free secular schooling ordered, railway, telegraph and coffee planted across the country — until Barrios died at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
