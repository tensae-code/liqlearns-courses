# Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871 — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871 — a)

**2.** Justo Rufino Barrios — b)

b) A key word of this lesson: Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871.
