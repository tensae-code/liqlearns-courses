# Lesson plan — Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Liberal Reform of 1871, won by García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invading from Mexico, rebuilt Guatemala in a generation: the Church stripped, free secular schooling ordered, railway, telegraph and coffee planted across the country — until Barrios died at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. 3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
