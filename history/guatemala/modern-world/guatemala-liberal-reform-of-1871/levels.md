# Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The Liberal Reform of 1871, won by García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invading from Mexico, rebuilt Guatemala in a generation: the Church stripped, free secular schooling ordered, railway, telegraph and coffee planted across the country — until Barrios died at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force.
