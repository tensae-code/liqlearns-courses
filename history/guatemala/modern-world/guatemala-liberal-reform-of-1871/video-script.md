# Video script — Guatemala's Liberal Reform of 1871

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Liberal Reform of 1871, won by García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invading from Mexico, rebuilt Guatemala in a generation: the Church stripped, free secular schooling ordered, railway, telegraph and coffee planted across the country — until Barrios died at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force.

In 1871 the liberals came back — with an army.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
