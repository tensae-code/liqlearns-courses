# Guatemala's Conservative Regime and Carrera — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The conservative regime of the thirty years.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because rafael Carrera, the caudillo from the Montaña, ruled Guatemala for thirty conservative years: he founded the Republic of Guatemala in 1847, crushed the Los Altos separatists, and sent his troops to expel William Walker's filibusters from Nicaragua — a Guatemalan strongman defending all Central America. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
