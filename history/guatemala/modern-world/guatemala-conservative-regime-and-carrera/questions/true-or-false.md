# Guatemala's Conservative Regime and Carrera — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Carrera, the mountain caudillo, founds the Republic of Guatemala in 1847, crushes the Los Altos separatists, and sends troops to expel William Walker's filibusters from Nicaragua.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Rafael Carrera, the caudillo from the Montaña, ruled Guatemala for thirty conservative years: he founded the Republic of Guatemala in 1847, crushed the Los Altos separatists, and sent his troops to expel William Walker's filibusters from Nicaragua — a Guatemalan strongman defending all Central America.
