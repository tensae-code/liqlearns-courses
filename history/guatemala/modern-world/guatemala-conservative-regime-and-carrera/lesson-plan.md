# Lesson plan — Guatemala's Conservative Regime and Carrera

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala's Conservative Regime and Carrera
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Rafael Carrera, the caudillo from the Montaña, ruled Guatemala for thirty conservative years: he founded the Republic of Guatemala in 1847, crushed the Los Altos separatists, and sent his troops to expel William Walker's filibusters from Nicaragua — a Guatemalan strongman defending all Central America.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Rafael Carrera, the caudillo from the Montaña, ruled Guatemala for thirty conservative years: he founded the Republic of Guatemala in 1847, crushed the Los Altos separatists, and sent his troops to expel William Walker's filibusters from Nicaragua — a Guatemalan strongman defending all Central America.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — Los Altos and its capital Quetzaltenango — the separatist state Carrera crushed is still Guatemala's second city region today.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
