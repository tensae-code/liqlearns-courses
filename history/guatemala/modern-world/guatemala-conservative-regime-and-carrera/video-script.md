# Video script — Guatemala's Conservative Regime and Carrera

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rafael Carrera, the caudillo from the Montaña, ruled Guatemala for thirty conservative years: he founded the Republic of Guatemala in 1847, crushed the Los Altos separatists, and sent his troops to expel William Walker's filibusters from Nicaragua — a Guatemalan strongman defending all Central America.

Out of the Federation's ruins rose one man: Rafael Carrera, a caudillo from the Montaña whose rebellion had broken the liberals and who now ruled in their place.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
