# Guatemala from Peace to the Present — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Guatemala from 1996 to the present.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because since the peace, Arzú signed the accords and privatised state firms, and each successor — Portillo, Berger, Colom, Pérez Molina — has been measured against the hurricanes Mitch, Stan and Agatha and against the still-unfinished promises of 1996. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
