# Guatemala from Peace to the Present — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Arzú signs the peace and privatises state firms; each successor is measured against hurricanes Mitch, Stan and Agatha and the still-unfinished promises of 1996.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Since the peace, Arzú signed the accords and privatised state firms, and each successor — Portillo, Berger, Colom, Pérez Molina — has been measured against the hurricanes Mitch, Stan and Agatha and against the still-unfinished promises of 1996.
