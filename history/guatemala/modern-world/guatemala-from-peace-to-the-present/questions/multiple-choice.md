# Guatemala from Peace to the Present — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Guatemala from 1996 to the present” is right?
a) Arzú signs the peace and privatises state firms; each successor is measured against hurricanes Mitch, Stan and Agatha and the still-unfinished promises of 1996.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Since the peace, Arzú signed the accords and privatised state firms, and each successor — Portillo, Berger, Colom, Pérez Molina — has been measured against the hurricanes Mitch, Stan and Agatha and against the still-unfinished promises of 1996.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Guatemala has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
