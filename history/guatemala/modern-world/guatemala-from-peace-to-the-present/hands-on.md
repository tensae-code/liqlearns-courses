# Hands-on — Guatemala from Peace to the Present

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the names of the presidents since the peace in order. Beside the list, write the two measures the book judges them all by: the hurricanes, and the unfinished promises of 1996. Judge the next president you hear of by the same two measures.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Since the peace, Arzú signed the accords and privatised state firms, and each successor — Portillo, Berger, Colom, Pérez Molina — has been measured against the hurricanes Mitch, Stan and Agatha and against the still-unfinished promises of 1996. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
