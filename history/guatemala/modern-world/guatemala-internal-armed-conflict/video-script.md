# Video script — Guatemala's Internal Armed Conflict

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Guatemala's internal armed conflict was born of injustice, racism and exclusion and ran in two phases — from the FAR guerrillas in the east to the four groups united as the URNG in 1982 — while Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wiped out whole villages; some 13,000 died in the first phase alone.

The book names the war's roots before its battles: injustice, racism and exclusion — a country where land, rights and respect were fenced off from the majority.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
