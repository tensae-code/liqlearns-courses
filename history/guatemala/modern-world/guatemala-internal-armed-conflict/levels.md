# Guatemala's Internal Armed Conflict — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Guatemala's internal armed conflict was born of injustice, racism and exclusion and ran in two phases — from the FAR guerrillas in the east to the four groups united as the URNG in 1982 — while Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wiped out whole villages; some 13,000 died in the first phase alone.
