# Guatemala's Internal Armed Conflict — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Born of injustice, racism and exclusion, the war runs from the FAR in the east to the four groups united as URNG in 1982; Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wipes out whole villages; some 13,000 die in the first phase alone.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Guatemala's internal armed conflict was born of injustice, racism and exclusion and ran in two phases — from the FAR guerrillas in the east to the four groups united as the URNG in 1982 — while Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wiped out whole villages; some 13,000 died in the first phase alone.
