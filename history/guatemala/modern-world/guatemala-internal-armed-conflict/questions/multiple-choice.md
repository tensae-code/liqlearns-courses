Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “The internal armed conflict in Guatemala, both phases” is right?
a) Born of injustice, racism and exclusion, the war runs from the FAR in the east to the four groups united as URNG in 1982; Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wipes out whole villages; some 13,000 die in the first phase alone.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
