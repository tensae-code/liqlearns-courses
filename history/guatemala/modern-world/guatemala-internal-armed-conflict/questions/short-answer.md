# Guatemala's Internal Armed Conflict — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The internal armed conflict in Guatemala, both phases.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because guatemala's internal armed conflict was born of injustice, racism and exclusion and ran in two phases — from the FAR guerrillas in the east to the four groups united as the URNG in 1982 — while Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wiped out whole villages; some 13,000 died in the first phase alone. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
