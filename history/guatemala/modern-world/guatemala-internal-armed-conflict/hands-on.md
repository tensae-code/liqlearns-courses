# Hands-on — Guatemala's Internal Armed Conflict

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down the three roots the book names for the war: injustice, racism, exclusion. Beside each, write one thing a peace must repair if the war is not to return — the Peace Accords lesson will test your list.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Guatemala's internal armed conflict was born of injustice, racism and exclusion and ran in two phases — from the FAR guerrillas in the east to the four groups united as the URNG in 1982 — while Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wiped out whole villages; some 13,000 died in the first phase alone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
