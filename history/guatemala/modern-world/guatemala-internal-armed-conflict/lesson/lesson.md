# Guatemala's Internal Armed Conflict

## Do it first

Write down the three roots the book names for the war: injustice, racism, exclusion. Beside each, write one thing a peace must repair if the war is not to return — the Peace Accords lesson will test your list.

The book names the war's roots before its battles: injustice, racism and exclusion — a country where land, rights and respect were fenced off from the majority. In the war's first phase, guerrillas of the FAR fought in the east, and the state's answer fell on whole regions; some 13,000 people died in that phase alone. In 1982 four guerrilla groups united as the URNG. The same years brought the war's darkest chapter: under Ríos Montt, the army's scorched-earth offensive treated whole highland villages as the enemy's base and wiped them out. The conflict would last thirty-six years. Its end — and the question of what could repair its roots — belongs to the peace lesson.
