# Lesson plan — Guatemala's Internal Armed Conflict

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala's Internal Armed Conflict
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Guatemala's internal armed conflict was born of injustice, racism and exclusion and ran in two phases — from the FAR guerrillas in the east to the four groups united as the URNG in 1982 — while Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wiped out whole villages; some 13,000 died in the first phase alone.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Guatemala's internal armed conflict was born of injustice, racism and exclusion and ran in two phases — from the FAR guerrillas in the east to the four groups united as the URNG in 1982 — while Ríos Montt's scorched-earth offensive wiped out whole villages; some 13,000 died in the first phase alone.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the villages themselves — whole communities wiped out under scorched earth are remembered by name in Guatemala to this day.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
