# Lesson plan — Guatemala's Ancestral Authorities and Maya Justice

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala's Ancestral Authorities and Maya Justice
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Where the state court punishes, Guatemala's ancestral authorities gather the community to repair the harm and restore balance — Maya justice is restorative, not punitive, and the guide insists that violence or lynching is not Maya justice at all.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (recommended, never forced).

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Where the state court punishes, Guatemala's ancestral authorities gather the community to repair the harm and restore balance — Maya justice is restorative, not punitive, and the guide insists that violence or lynching is not Maya justice at all.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
