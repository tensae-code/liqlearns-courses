# Video script — Guatemala's Ancestral Authorities and Maya Justice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Where the state court punishes, Guatemala's ancestral authorities gather the community to repair the harm and restore balance — Maya justice is restorative, not punitive, and the guide insists that violence or lynching is not Maya justice at all.

The primary guide's Grade 6 learning experience sets two justices side by side.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
