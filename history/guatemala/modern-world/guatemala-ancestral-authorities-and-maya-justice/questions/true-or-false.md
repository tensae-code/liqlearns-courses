# Guatemala's Ancestral Authorities and Maya Justice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Where the state court punishes, the ancestral authorities gather the community to repair the harm and restore balance — violence or lynching, the guide insists, is not Maya justice.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Where the state court punishes, Guatemala's ancestral authorities gather the community to repair the harm and restore balance — Maya justice is restorative, not punitive, and the guide insists that violence or lynching is not Maya justice at all.
