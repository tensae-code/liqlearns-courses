# Guatemala's Ancestral Authorities and Maya Justice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Maya people and their ancestral ways of resolving conflicts (Grade 6).

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because where the state court punishes, Guatemala's ancestral authorities gather the community to repair the harm and restore balance — Maya justice is restorative, not punitive, and the guide insists that violence or lynching is not Maya justice at all. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
