# Guatemala's Peace Process and Accords

## Do it first

Write the date 29 December 1996. Then list what had to be signed before the final peace could be: human rights, resettlement, indigenous rights. A peace built in pieces is this lesson's whole teaching.

Peace did not arrive in a day; it was built in public, piece by piece. President Cerezo opened the road with the Esquipulas process, Central America's presidents seeking the region's peace together. In 1993 President Serrano tried to seize absolute power in an autogolpe — a self-coup — and civil society's pressure collapsed it within days; the constitution held. Under De León Carpio the real building began: accord after accord, on human rights, on resettling the uprooted, on the identity and rights of the indigenous peoples. And on 29 December 1996 came the last signature: the Agreement on a Firm and Lasting Peace. The war of thirty-six years was over. What the accords promised — that work remained.
