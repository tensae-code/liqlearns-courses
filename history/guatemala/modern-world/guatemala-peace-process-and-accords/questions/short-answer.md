# Guatemala's Peace Process and Accords — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The peace process.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because the peace was built step by step: Cerezo opened the Esquipulas road, Serrano's 1993 autogolpe collapsed under civil pressure, De León Carpio signed the human-rights, resettlement and indigenous-rights accords — and on 29 December 1996 the Firm and Lasting Peace ended thirty-six years of war. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
