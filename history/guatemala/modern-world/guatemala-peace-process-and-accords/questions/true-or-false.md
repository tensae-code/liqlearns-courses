# Guatemala's Peace Process and Accords — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cerezo opens Esquipulas, Serrano's 1993 autogolpe collapses under civil pressure, De León Carpio signs the human-rights, resettlement and indigenous-rights accords; on 29 December 1996 the Firm and Lasting Peace ends the war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — The peace was built step by step: Cerezo opened the Esquipulas road, Serrano's 1993 autogolpe collapsed under civil pressure, De León Carpio signed the human-rights, resettlement and indigenous-rights accords — and on 29 December 1996 the Firm and Lasting Peace ended thirty-six years of war.
