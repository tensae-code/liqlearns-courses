# Guatemala's Peace Process and Accords — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “The peace process” is right?
a) Cerezo opens Esquipulas, Serrano's 1993 autogolpe collapses under civil pressure, De León Carpio signs the human-rights, resettlement and indigenous-rights accords; on 29 December 1996 the Firm and Lasting Peace ends the war.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) The peace was built step by step: Cerezo opened the Esquipulas road, Serrano's 1993 autogolpe collapsed under civil pressure, De León Carpio signed the human-rights, resettlement and indigenous-rights accords — and on 29 December 1996 the Firm and Lasting Peace ended thirty-six years of war.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Guatemala has no way of its own
