# Hands-on — Guatemala's Peace Process and Accords

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the date 29 December 1996. Then list what had to be signed before the final peace could be: human rights, resettlement, indigenous rights. A peace built in pieces is this lesson's whole teaching.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The peace was built step by step: Cerezo opened the Esquipulas road, Serrano's 1993 autogolpe collapsed under civil pressure, De León Carpio signed the human-rights, resettlement and indigenous-rights accords — and on 29 December 1996 the Firm and Lasting Peace ended thirty-six years of war. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
