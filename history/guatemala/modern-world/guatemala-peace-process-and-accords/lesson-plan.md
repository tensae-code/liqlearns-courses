# Lesson plan — Guatemala's Peace Process and Accords

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala's Peace Process and Accords
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The peace was built step by step: Cerezo opened the Esquipulas road, Serrano's 1993 autogolpe collapsed under civil pressure, De León Carpio signed the human-rights, resettlement and indigenous-rights accords — and on 29 December 1996 the Firm and Lasting Peace ended thirty-six years of war.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Reform, Revolution, War and Peace (recommended, never forced).
