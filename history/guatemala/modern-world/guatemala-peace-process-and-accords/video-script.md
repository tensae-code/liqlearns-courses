# Video script — Guatemala's Peace Process and Accords

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The peace was built step by step: Cerezo opened the Esquipulas road, Serrano's 1993 autogolpe collapsed under civil pressure, De León Carpio signed the human-rights, resettlement and indigenous-rights accords — and on 29 December 1996 the Firm and Lasting Peace ended thirty-six years of war.

Peace did not arrive in a day; it was built in public, piece by piece.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
