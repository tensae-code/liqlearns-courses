# Belize and Guatemala's Territorial Claim — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** English logwood cutters spread over Belize while Guatemala claims the territory; Belize wins independence from Britain in 1981 without settling the claim, and in 2008 both accept taking the dispute to a court.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Belize began with English logwood cutters spreading over a coast Guatemala claims as its own; Belize won independence from Britain in 1981 without settling the claim, and in 2008 both countries accepted taking the dispute to a court — a quarrel of centuries handed to judges, not armies.
