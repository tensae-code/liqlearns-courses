# Belize and Guatemala's Territorial Claim — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement about “Belize” is right?
a) English logwood cutters spread over Belize while Guatemala claims the territory; Belize wins independence from Britain in 1981 without settling the claim, and in 2008 both accept taking the dispute to a court.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary people  b) Belize began with English logwood cutters spreading over a coast Guatemala claims as its own; Belize won independence from Britain in 1981 without settling the claim, and in 2008 both countries accepted taking the dispute to a court — a quarrel of centuries handed to judges, not armies.  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Guatemala has no way of its own
**Answer: b)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
