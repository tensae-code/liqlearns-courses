# Video script — Belize and Guatemala's Territorial Claim

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Belize began with English logwood cutters spreading over a coast Guatemala claims as its own; Belize won independence from Britain in 1981 without settling the claim, and in 2008 both countries accepted taking the dispute to a court — a quarrel of centuries handed to judges, not armies.

On Guatemala's Caribbean coast stood forests of logwood, a tree whose heartwood made precious dyes.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
