# Video script — Central America: Foreign Intervention and the Banana Companies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Foreign intervention came to Central America by contract: cash-poor republics paid a United States railway builder in land, the Soto-Keith contract became the model, and the United Fruit Company ended up holding whole regions — Izabal and Tiquisate — inside Guatemala.

Central America's republics wanted railways and had no money.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
