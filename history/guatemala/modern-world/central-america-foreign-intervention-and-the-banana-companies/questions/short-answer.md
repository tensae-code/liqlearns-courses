# Central America: Foreign Intervention and the Banana Companies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Foreign intervention in Central America: the banana.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because foreign intervention came to Central America by contract: cash-poor republics paid a United States railway builder in land, the Soto-Keith contract became the model, and the United Fruit Company ended up holding whole regions — Izabal and Tiquisate — inside Guatemala. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
