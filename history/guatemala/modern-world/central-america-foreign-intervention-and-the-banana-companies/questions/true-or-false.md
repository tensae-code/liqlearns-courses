# Central America: Foreign Intervention and the Banana Companies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cash-poor republics pay a US railway builder in land; the Soto-Keith contract becomes the model, and the United Fruit Company ends up holding Izabal and Tiquisate.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Foreign intervention came to Central America by contract: cash-poor republics paid a United States railway builder in land, the Soto-Keith contract became the model, and the United Fruit Company ended up holding whole regions — Izabal and Tiquisate — inside Guatemala.
