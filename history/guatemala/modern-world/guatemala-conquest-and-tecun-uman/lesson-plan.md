# Lesson plan — Guatemala's Conquest and Tecún Umán

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala's Conquest and Tecún Umán
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Guatemala's conquest came in two stages: Alvarado's army and smallpox advancing together against highland lords who failed to unite — Tecún Umán falling at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burning, Santiago founded at Iximche' — then the betrayed Kaqchikel rising, and Jorge de Alvarado's second campaign, painted on cloth by his Quauhquechollan allies in the Lienzo de Quauhquechollan.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Conquest, Colony and Independence (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The conquest of Guatemala, first stage** — Warned by Moctezuma's envoys, the highland lords still fail to unite; Pedro de Alvarado's army and smallpox advance together; Tecún Umán falls at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burns, and Santiago is founded at Iximche'.
2. **The second stage of the conquest** — The Kaqchikel, betrayed as allies, rise; Jorge de Alvarado retraces his brother's route with Quauhquechollan allies, whose weavers paint the campaign on cloth.
