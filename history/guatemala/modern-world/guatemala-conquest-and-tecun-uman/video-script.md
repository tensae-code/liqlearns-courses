# Video script — Guatemala's Conquest and Tecún Umán

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Guatemala's conquest came in two stages: Alvarado's army and smallpox advancing together against highland lords who failed to unite — Tecún Umán falling at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burning, Santiago founded at Iximche' — then the betrayed Kaqchikel rising, and Jorge de Alvarado's second campaign, painted on cloth by his Quauhquechollan allies in the Lienzo de Quauhquechollan.

The highland lords were warned.

The conquest's second stage began in betrayal.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
