# Guatemala's Conquest and Tecún Umán — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Warned by Moctezuma's envoys, the highland lords still fail to unite; Pedro de Alvarado's army and smallpox advance together; Tecún Umán falls at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burns, and Santiago is founded at Iximche'.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Kaqchikel, betrayed as allies, rise; Jorge de Alvarado retraces his brother's route with Quauhquechollan allies, whose weavers paint the campaign on cloth.
**Answer: True**
