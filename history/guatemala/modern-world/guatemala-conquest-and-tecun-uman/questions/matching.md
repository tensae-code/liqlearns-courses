# Guatemala's Conquest and Tecún Umán — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The conquest of Guatemala, first stage — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The second stage of the conquest — b)


Meanings:
a) Warned by Moctezuma's envoys, the highland lords still fail to unite; Pedro de Alvarado's army and smallpox advance together; Tecún Umán falls at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burns, and Santiago is founded at Iximche'.
b) The Kaqchikel, betrayed as allies, rise; Jorge de Alvarado retraces his brother's route with Quauhquechollan allies, whose weavers paint the campaign on cloth.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
