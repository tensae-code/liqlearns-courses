# Guatemala's Conquest and Tecún Umán — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The conquest of Guatemala, first stage; The second stage of the conquest.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because guatemala's conquest came in two stages: Alvarado's army and smallpox advancing together against highland lords who failed to unite — Tecún Umán falling at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burning, Santiago founded at Iximche' — then the betrayed Kaqchikel rising, and Jorge de Alvarado's second campaign, painted on cloth by his Quauhquechollan allies in the Lienzo de Quauhquechollan. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
