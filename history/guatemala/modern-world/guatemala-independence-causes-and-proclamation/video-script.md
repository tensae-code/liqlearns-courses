# Video script — Guatemala's Independence: Causes and Proclamation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Independence came from pressure outside and rot inside: the United States' independence, the French Revolution's liberty-equality-fraternity and Napoleon's seizure of Spain shook the empire, while criollo exclusion, trade monopoly and war taxes rotted it — until, after a decade of risings and conspiracies, José Cecilio del Valle drafted the Act signed on 15 September 1821.

Empires fall from two directions at once, and the book separates them.

The decade before the Act was not quiet.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
