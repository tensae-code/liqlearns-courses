# Guatemala's Independence: Causes and Proclamation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The causes of independence; The independence of Central America.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because independence came from pressure outside and rot inside: the United States' independence, the French Revolution's liberty-equality-fraternity and Napoleon's seizure of Spain shook the empire, while criollo exclusion, trade monopoly and war taxes rotted it — until, after a decade of risings and conspiracies, José Cecilio del Valle drafted the Act signed on 15 September 1821. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
