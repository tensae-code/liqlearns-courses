# Guatemala's Independence: Causes and Proclamation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) US independence, the French Revolution's liberty-equality-fraternity and Napoleon's seizure of Spain shake the empire from outside; criollo exclusion, trade monopoly and war taxes rot it from inside.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Delgado raises San Salvador in 1811, the Belén conspiracy is uncovered in 1813, Atanasio Tzul rebels against tribute in 1820; liberals and conservatives argue in rival newspapers until José Cecilio del Valle drafts the Act signed on 15 September 1821.
**Answer: True**
