# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Tecún Umán: mythical figure or historical person?.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because a written history is built by weighing evidence: Tecún Umán is the test case — Alvarado's own letter never names him, an indigenous title does, and the book concludes the captain existed while his famous single duel stays an open question. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
