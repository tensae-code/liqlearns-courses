# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Alvarado's letter never names Tecum, an indigenous Título de K'oyoi does; the book weighs both sources and concludes the captain existed, while his single duel with Alvarado stays an open question.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — A written history is built by weighing evidence: Tecún Umán is the test case — Alvarado's own letter never names him, an indigenous title does, and the book concludes the captain existed while his famous single duel stays an open question.
