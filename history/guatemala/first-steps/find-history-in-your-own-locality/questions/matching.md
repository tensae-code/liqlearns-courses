# Find History in Your Own Locality — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tikal then and now: how has my community changed? — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Interviewing the grandparents — b)


Meanings:
a) Two photographs of the same temple — one swallowed by jungle, one restored — open the book, and the child asks how their own community changed in fifty years.
b) Pupils interview grandparents and elders about childhood, nature, legends, feasts and ancestors, then build the class poster "La Cultura que heredamos de nuestros ancestros" — the elders' memories, cross-checked across interviews, become the community's written history.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
