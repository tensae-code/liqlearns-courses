**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “Tikal then and now: how has my community changed?” is right?
a) Two photographs of the same temple — one swallowed by jungle, one restored — open the book, and the child asks how their own community changed in fifty years.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book
