# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Two photographs of the same temple — one swallowed by jungle, one restored — open the book, and the child asks how their own community changed in fifty years.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Pupils interview grandparents and elders about childhood, nature, legends, feasts and ancestors, then build the class poster "La Cultura que heredamos de nuestros ancestros" — the elders' memories, cross-checked across interviews, become the community's written history.
**Answer: True**
