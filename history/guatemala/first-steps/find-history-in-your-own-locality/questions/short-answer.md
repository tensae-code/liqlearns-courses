# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Tikal then and now: how has my community changed?; Interviewing the grandparents.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because history begins where you live: a temple at Tikal, swallowed by jungle and restored, opens the question every child then asks of their own community — how has this place changed in fifty years — and the elders who lived it are the first historians. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
