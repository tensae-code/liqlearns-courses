# Video script — Find History in Your Own Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History begins where you live: a temple at Tikal, swallowed by jungle and restored, opens the question every child then asks of their own community — how has this place changed in fifty years — and the elders who lived it are the first historians.

The book opens with two photographs of one place: Tikal's Great Jaguar temple.

The teacher guide sends the class to its first historians: the grandparents.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
