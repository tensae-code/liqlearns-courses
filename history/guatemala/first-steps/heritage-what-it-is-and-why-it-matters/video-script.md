# Video script — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Heritage is what the grandparents hand down — each people's art, feasts and music carry its past: Maya ceramics and jícaras, Garífuna drum-dance, Xinka crafts, and the marimba, national instrument since 1978, carrying the shared story of all four peoples.

Heritage is not only stone.

The teacher guide reads the class a text on the Maya cosmovision — "our way of understanding the world." Its teaching is simple and deep: culture is the sacred inheritance of grandmothers and grandfathers.

In the primero básico guide, the values of three peoples are set side by side, each in its own words.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
