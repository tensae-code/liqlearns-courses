# Lesson plan — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: History Where You Live (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Heritage is what the grandparents hand down — each people's art, feasts and music carry its past: Maya ceramics and jícaras, Garífuna drum-dance, Xinka crafts, and the marimba, national instrument since 1978, carrying the shared story of all four peoples.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: History Where You Live (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The art of the four peoples and the marimba** — Each people's art tells its past — Maya ceramics and jícaras, Garífuna drum-dance, Xinka crafts; the marimba, national instrument since 1978, carries the shared story.
2. **The Maya cosmovision: our way of understanding the world** — Culture is the sacred inheritance of grandmothers and grandfathers; around the family fire, stories received over millennia teach the young to live in balance with Mother Earth.
3. **The values of the Maya, Xinka and Garífuna cultures** — The elders of three peoples teach the same lesson in their own words — protect everything that has life, respect the creative force Tata Tiwix, love and listen (isiehabuni, aganbadi) — because violence breaks the harmony between humans and nature.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. 3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
