# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The art of the four peoples and the marimba; The Maya cosmovision: our way of understanding the world; The values of the Maya, Xinka and Garífuna cultures.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because heritage is what the grandparents hand down — each people's art, feasts and music carry its past: Maya ceramics and jícaras, Garífuna drum-dance, Xinka crafts, and the marimba, national instrument since 1978, carrying the shared story of all four peoples. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
