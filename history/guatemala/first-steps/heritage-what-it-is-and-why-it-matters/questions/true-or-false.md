# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Each people's art tells its past — Maya ceramics and jícaras, Garífuna drum-dance, Xinka crafts; the marimba, national instrument since 1978, carries the shared story.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Culture is the sacred inheritance of grandmothers and grandfathers; around the family fire, stories received over millennia teach the young to live in balance with Mother Earth.
**Answer: True**
