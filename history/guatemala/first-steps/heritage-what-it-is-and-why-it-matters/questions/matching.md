# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — matching

Match each part with its meaning. Every item is marked with its sub lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The art of the four peoples and the marimba — a)

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Maya cosmovision: our way of understanding the world — b)

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The values of the Maya, Xinka and Garífuna cultures — c)


Meanings:
a) Each people's art tells its past — Maya ceramics and jícaras, Garífuna drum-dance, Xinka crafts; the marimba, national instrument since 1978, carries the shared story.
b) Culture is the sacred inheritance of grandmothers and grandfathers; around the family fire, stories received over millennia teach the young to live in balance with Mother Earth.
c) The elders of three peoples teach the same lesson in their own words — protect everything that has life, respect the creative force Tata Tiwix, love and listen (isiehabuni, aganbadi) — because violence breaks the harmony between humans and nature.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
