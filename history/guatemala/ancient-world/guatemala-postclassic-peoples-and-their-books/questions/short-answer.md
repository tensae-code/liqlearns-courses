# Guatemala's Postclassic Peoples and Their Books — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Postclassic period and its cultures.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because after the Classic collapse, the highlands filled with kingdoms — K'iche' at Q'umarkaj, Kaqchikel at Iximche', Mam at Saqulew and their neighbours — fighting and allying until the Kaqchikel broke from the K'iche' confederation; and these peoples wrote their own account of creation and kings in the Popol Wuj, the Anales de los Kaqchikeles and the Rabinal Achí. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
