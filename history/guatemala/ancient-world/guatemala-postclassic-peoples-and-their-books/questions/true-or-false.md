# Guatemala's Postclassic Peoples and Their Books — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the collapse, highland kingdoms Q'umarkaj, Iximche' and Saqulew fight and ally; the Kaqchikel break from the K'iche' confederation; their books — Popol Wuj, Anales de los Kaqchikeles, Rabinal Achí — carry their own account of creation and kings.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — After the Classic collapse, the highlands filled with kingdoms — K'iche' at Q'umarkaj, Kaqchikel at Iximche', Mam at Saqulew and their neighbours — fighting and allying until the Kaqchikel broke from the K'iche' confederation; and these peoples wrote their own account of creation and kings in the Popol Wuj, the Anales de los Kaqchikeles and the Rabinal Achí.
