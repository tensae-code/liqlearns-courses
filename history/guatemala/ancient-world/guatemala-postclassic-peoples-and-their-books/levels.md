# Guatemala's Postclassic Peoples and Their Books — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: After the Classic collapse, the highlands filled with kingdoms — K'iche' at Q'umarkaj, Kaqchikel at Iximche', Mam at Saqulew and their neighbours — fighting and allying until the Kaqchikel broke from the K'iche' confederation; and these peoples wrote their own account of creation and kings in the Popol Wuj, the Anales de los Kaqchikeles and the Rabinal Achí.
