# Lesson plan — Guatemala's Postclassic Peoples and Their Books

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Guatemala's Postclassic Peoples and Their Books
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and the Maya (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After the Classic collapse, the highlands filled with kingdoms — K'iche' at Q'umarkaj, Kaqchikel at Iximche', Mam at Saqulew and their neighbours — fighting and allying until the Kaqchikel broke from the K'iche' confederation; and these peoples wrote their own account of creation and kings in the Popol Wuj, the Anales de los Kaqchikeles and the Rabinal Achí.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and the Maya (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: After the Classic collapse, the highlands filled with kingdoms — K'iche' at Q'umarkaj, Kaqchikel at Iximche', Mam at Saqulew and their neighbours — fighting and allying until the Kaqchikel broke from the K'iche' confederation; and these peoples wrote their own account of creation and kings in the Popol Wuj, the Anales de los Kaqchikeles and the Rabinal Achí.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — the Popol Wuj — the K'iche' book of creation and kings, still read and honoured in Guatemala today.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
