# The Maya — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Preclassic: Olmecs and early Maya
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Olmec "mother culture" carves colossal stone heads while the first Maya settle, farm maize, start calendars and glyph writing, and trade jade and obsidian from Kaminal Juyú and Tak'alik A'baj. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Classic period (250–900)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Maya city-states Tikal, Uaxactún and Quiriguá rise under ajau kings, count time with two interlocking calendars and the zero, carve their deeds on stelae — then the great cities fall. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
