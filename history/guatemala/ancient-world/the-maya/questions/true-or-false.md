# The Maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Olmec "mother culture" carves colossal stone heads while the first Maya settle, farm maize, start calendars and glyph writing, and trade jade and obsidian from Kaminal Juyú and Tak'alik A'baj.
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Maya city-states Tikal, Uaxactún and Quiriguá rise under ajau kings, count time with two interlocking calendars and the zero, carve their deeds on stelae — then the great cities fall.
**Answer: True**
