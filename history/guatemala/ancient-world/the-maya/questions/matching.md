b) Maya city-states Tikal, Uaxactún and Quiriguá rise under ajau kings, count time with two interlocking calendars and the zero, carve their deeds on stelae — then the great cities fall.
c) Every person is born under a nawal, an energy of nature that shapes their character and gifts; the Ajq'ijab' (Maya priests) still read the days so people live in balance with the world.
