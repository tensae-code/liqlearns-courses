Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about “The Preclassic: Olmecs and early Maya” is right?
a) The Olmec "mother culture" carves colossal stone heads while the first Maya settle, farm maize, start calendars and glyph writing, and trade jade and obsidian from Kaminal Juyú and Tak'alik A'baj.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Guatemala's book
