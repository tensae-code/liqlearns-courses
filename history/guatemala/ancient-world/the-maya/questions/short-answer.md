# The Maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Preclassic: Olmecs and early Maya; The Classic period (250–900); The Nawales (primero básico annex); The Nawales (segundo básico annex); The Nawales (tercero básico annex).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because the Maya story rises in three ages: Olmec neighbours and the first Maya farmers of the Preclassic, the great city-states of the Classic — Tikal, Uaxactún and Quiriguá under their ajau kings, counting time with two calendars and the zero — and, living on today, the twenty nawales of the sacred calendar that still shape how Maya people read their days. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
