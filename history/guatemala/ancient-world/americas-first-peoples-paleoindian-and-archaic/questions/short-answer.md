# America's First Peoples: Paleoindian and Archaic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Prehistoric societies: the Paleoindian and Archaic periods.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because the first Americans were hunters who crossed the ice-covered Bering Strait chasing mammoth and mastodon; in the Archaic period that follows, their descendants settle, plant maize, beans and chile, build villages and begin to trade. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
