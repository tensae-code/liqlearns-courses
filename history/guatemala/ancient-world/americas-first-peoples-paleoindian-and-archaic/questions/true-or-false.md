# America's First Peoples: Paleoindian and Archaic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hunters cross the ice-covered Bering Strait chasing mammoth and mastodon, then settle, plant maize, beans and chile, build villages and trade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — The first Americans were hunters who crossed the ice-covered Bering Strait chasing mammoth and mastodon; in the Archaic period that follows, their descendants settle, plant maize, beans and chile, build villages and begin to trade.
