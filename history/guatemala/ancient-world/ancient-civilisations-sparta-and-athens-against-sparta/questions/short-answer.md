# Ancient Civilisations: Sparta and Athens Against Sparta — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** How the ancient world treated disability.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because how the ancient world treated disability, as one history sheet in Guatemala's segundo básico guide tells it: in Sparta the council of elders judged each newborn, and babies who did not meet expectations were thrown from Mount Taygetus; the Catholic Church later banned infanticide, but treated disability as supernatural, sometimes with exorcisms. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
