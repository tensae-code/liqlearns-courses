# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Sparta and Athens Against Sparta

**Course:** History — Guatemala · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Sparta and Athens Against Sparta
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and the Maya (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** How the ancient world treated disability, as one history sheet in Guatemala's segundo básico guide tells it: in Sparta the council of elders judged each newborn, and babies who did not meet expectations were thrown from Mount Taygetus; the Catholic Church later banned infanticide, but treated disability as supernatural, sometimes with exorcisms.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World: First Peoples and the Maya (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1.
