# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Sparta and Athens Against Sparta

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told animated-map style — who, where, what turned — in plain words.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
How the ancient world treated disability, as one history sheet in Guatemala's segundo básico guide tells it: in Sparta the council of elders judged each newborn, and babies who did not meet expectations were thrown from Mount Taygetus; the Catholic Church later banned infanticide, but treated disability as supernatural, sometimes with exorcisms.

Inside its study of violence against people with disabilities, the segundo básico guide sets a history sheet: how did the ancient world treat a child born different? Its answer begins in Sparta, the soldier-city of Greece.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
