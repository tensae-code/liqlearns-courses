# Mesoamerica: Maize and the Shared Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Maize in Mesoamerica.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a learner in Guatemala?
**Answer:** Because mesoamerica is the shared world built on maize: a tiny wild cob became a people's staple once lime-cooking — nixtamal — unlocked its food value, and the peoples from Mexico to Costa Rica came to share pyramids, the ball game, calendars, glyphs and jade. — and a learner who understands it can take their real place in that shared story.
