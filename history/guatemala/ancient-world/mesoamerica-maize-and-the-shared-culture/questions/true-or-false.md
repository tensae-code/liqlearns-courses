# Mesoamerica: Maize and the Shared Culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A tiny wild maize cob becomes a people's staple once lime-cooking (nixtamal) unlocks its food value; Mexico-to-Costa-Rica peoples share pyramids, ball game, calendars, glyphs and jade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of ordinary people in Guatemala.
**Answer: False** — Mesoamerica is the shared world built on maize: a tiny wild cob became a people's staple once lime-cooking — nixtamal — unlocked its food value, and the peoples from Mexico to Costa Rica came to share pyramids, the ball game, calendars, glyphs and jade.
