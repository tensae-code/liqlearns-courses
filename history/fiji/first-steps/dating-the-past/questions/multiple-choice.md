# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Timeline: History of Fiji, pre-contact to contact period" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) A dated line runs from settlement around 1500 BC through Levuka's founding, Tahitian and Methodist missionaries, Ma'afu's arrival, Cakobau's rise and conversion, to the 1871 Kingdom of Fiji
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
**Answer: b) A dated line runs from settlement around 1500 BC through Levuka's founding, Tahitian and Methodist missionaries, Ma'afu's arrival, Cakobau's rise and conversion, to the 1871 Kingdom of Fiji** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Timeline: History of Fiji, pre-contact to contact period"?
a) A dated line runs from settlement around 1500 BC through Levuka's founding, Tahitian and Methodist missionaries, Ma'afu's arrival, Cakobau's rise and conversion, to the 1871 Kingdom of Fiji  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) A dated line runs from settlement around 1500 BC through Levuka's founding, Tahitian and Methodist missionaries, Ma'afu's arrival, Cakobau's rise and conversion, to the 1871 Kingdom of Fiji** — That is what this lesson teaches.
