# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A dated line runs from settlement around 1500 BC through Levuka's founding, Tahitian and Methodist missionaries, Ma'afu's arrival, Cakobau's rise and conversion, to the 1871 Kingdom of Fiji.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Timeline: History of Fiji, pre-contact to contact period" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
