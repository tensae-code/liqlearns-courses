# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Strand 2, 2.1-2.4 Continuity, Change, Progress and Regress" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Time passes, some things stay (eating, planting, family) and some change (internet, buses, electricity)
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Time passes, some things stay (eating, planting, family) and some change (internet, buses, electricity)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "2.6 Studying the Past"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) A class studies an old photograph of policemen directing traffic, spots what has changed  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) A class studies an old photograph of policemen directing traffic, spots what has changed** — That is what this lesson teaches.
