# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Strand 2, 2.1-2.4 Continuity, Change, Progress and Regress" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Time passes, some things stay (eating, planting, family) and some change (internet, buses, electricity); good change is called progress, harmful change regress.

**2.** What came of "2.6 Studying the Past" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A class studies an old photograph of policemen directing traffic, spots what has changed, and is told plainly: answering such questions about the past is history.
