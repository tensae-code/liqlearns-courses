# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Time passes, some things stay (eating, planting, family) and some change (internet, buses, electricity); a class studies an old photograph of policemen directing traffic, spots what has changed; history is defined five ways; people who study the past are historians.

Now the part called "Strand 2, 2.1-2.4 Continuity, Change, Progress and Regress". Time passes, some things stay (eating, planting, family) and some change (internet, buses, electricity); good change is called progress, harmful change regress.

Now the part called "2.6 Studying the Past". A class studies an old photograph of policemen directing traffic, spots what has changed, and is told plainly: answering such questions about the past is history.

Now the part called "2.1 What is History? Why is history important?". History is defined five ways — everything that happened, a bridge from past to future, the record historians prepare, understanding one's identity, the record of changing lives — and a cartoon is mined for evidence of history.

Now the part called "2.1 Understanding the Past: historians". People who study the past are historians; they tell us the important events that happened, starting here with the origins of the world's peoples and how they colonised and settled their lands.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
