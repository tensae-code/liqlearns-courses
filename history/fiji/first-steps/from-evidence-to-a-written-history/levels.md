# From Evidence to a Written History — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Activity 3 My Cultural Heritage research questionnaire
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: a child questions parents and elders about their heritage and records the answers, turning family memory into evidence — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Independence photo sequence, National Archives of Fiji
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: children arrange captioned archive photographs of the 1970 independence ceremony in order and turn the evidence into a fifty-word written account — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Kon-Tiki Expedition
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: in 1947 Thor Heyerdahl sails a raft from South America for 101 days to the Tuamotu islands to test his claim that Polynesia could have been settled from Peru — a theory tested by re-enacting the voyage — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Photograph evidence: Levuka in the late 19th century
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: a Levuka photograph shows only Fijians, yet Chinese, African Americans, Hawaiians, Germans and Malaysans also lived there — pupils learn a photograph can hide as much as it shows — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 7 The Individual Research Topic
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: each pupil researches and reports Fiji's relationship with the USA, or prepares a 150-word speech on UN military duty, choosing and defending a topic with the teacher — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
