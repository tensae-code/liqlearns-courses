# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Activity 3 My Cultural Heritage research questionnaire" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) A child questions parents and elders about their heritage and records the answers, turning family memory into evidence
c) it is a story with no people and no places in it
d) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
**Answer: b) A child questions parents and elders about their heritage and records the answers, turning family memory into evidence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Independence photo sequence, National Archives of Fiji"? (Sub lesson 2)

**Answer: a) Children arrange captioned archive photographs of the 1970 independence ceremony in order and turn the evidence into a fifty-word written account** — That is what this lesson teaches.
