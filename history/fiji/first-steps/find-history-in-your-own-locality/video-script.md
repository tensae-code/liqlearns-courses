# Video script — Find History in Your Own Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. A child starts with the oldest family member and builds a tree showing how everyone is related, learning ancestry from living memory; a child interviews people who have lived in the community ten years to reconstruct how it changed, making local oral history.

Now the part called "Lesson 2 Family Relationships: family tree diagram". A child starts with the oldest family member and builds a tree showing how everyone is related, learning ancestry from living memory.

Now the part called "2.7 My village/settlement/community history". A child interviews people who have lived in the community ten years to reconstruct how it changed, making local oral history.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
