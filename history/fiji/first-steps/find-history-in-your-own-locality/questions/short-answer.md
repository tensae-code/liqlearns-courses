# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Lesson 2 Family Relationships: family tree diagram" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A child starts with the oldest family member and builds a tree showing how everyone is related, learning ancestry from living memory.

**2.** What came of "2.7 My village/settlement/community history" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A child interviews people who have lived in the community ten years to reconstruct how it changed, making local oral history.
