# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Lesson 2 Family Relationships: family tree diagram" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) A child starts with the oldest family member and builds a tree showing how everyone is related, learning ancestry from living memory
c) it is a story with no people and no places in it
d) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
**Answer: b) A child starts with the oldest family member and builds a tree showing how everyone is related, learning ancestry from living memory** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "2.7 My village/settlement/community history"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) A child interviews people who have lived in the community ten years to reconstruct how it changed, making local oral history  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) A child interviews people who have lived in the community ten years to reconstruct how it changed, making local oral history** — That is what this lesson teaches.
