# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A child starts with the oldest family member and builds a tree showing how everyone is related, learning ancestry from living memory. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "2.7 My village/settlement/community history" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
