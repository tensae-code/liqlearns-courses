# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 4 Cultural Backgrounds
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: children of different Fijian, Indian and other cultures name their language, worship, celebrations and special things, discovering culture differs and is shared — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cultural Values: artefacts and totems
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: fans, necklaces, puja lota and water vessels are shown as made things that carry a culture's values from the past into the present — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — 1.8-1.10 Cultural events and religious festivals in our communities
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: iTaukei, Hindu and Muslim neighbours celebrate weddings, Diwali, Christmas, Eid, Mundan and hair-cutting and death ceremonies differently, and children learn each rite carries a community's past — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Sharks, turtles and whales in Pacific stories
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: across the Pacific sharks are revered, turtles hold a special place, and whales are told of as guardians and even ancestors — animal stories that carry a people's identity — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Traditional uses of the reef and ocean; traditional fishing in Tuvalu and Niue
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: whole communities herded fish with coconut-leaf uloa/pola, Niue districts shared their coast equally, men wove kupega nets from nettle fibre — knowledge now contrasted with taking more than is needed today — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Preserving Our Heritage Sites
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Fiji's unique past is kept in the Fiji Museum and at heritage sites children are taught to preserve, linking the arrival story to places they can visit — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — 3.21 National Heritage
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: heritage is defined as artefacts and intangible attributes inherited from past generations; Fiji's examples — Levuka (world heritage), Koroniyatu and Bouma National Heritage Park — are researched by pupils site by site — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
