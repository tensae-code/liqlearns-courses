# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Lesson 4 Cultural Backgrounds" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Children of different Fijian, Indian and other cultures name their language, worship, celebrations and special things, discovering culture differs and is shared
**Answer: d) Children of different Fijian, Indian and other cultures name their language, worship, celebrations and special things, discovering culture differs and is shared** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Cultural Values: artefacts and totems"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Fans, necklaces, puja lota and water vessels are shown as made things that carry a culture's values from the past into the present  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Fans, necklaces, puja lota and water vessels are shown as made things that carry a culture's values from the past into the present** — That is what this lesson teaches.
