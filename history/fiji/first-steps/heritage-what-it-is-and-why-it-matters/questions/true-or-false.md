# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Children of different Fijian, Indian and other cultures name their language, worship, celebrations and special things, discovering culture differs and is shared. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Cultural Values: artefacts and totems" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
