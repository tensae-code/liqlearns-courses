# History — Fiji course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Fiji's own —
built from Fiji's Ministry of Education Social Studies,
Social Science and History student books for Years 3–12 and
the USP/UNESCO ESD reader *People and the Sea in the Pacific*
carried in Fiji's record (all English). Inside the course
the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below sit inside the levels, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grade the books come from
lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Fiji's books': history starts at
home (family, elders, heritage, evidence, the timeline),
widens to the ancient Pacific and the first settlement,
meets the Europeans, the trades and the missionaries, passes
through Cession and the colonial century, reaches
independence and its coups and constitutions, and ends
facing the world through diplomacy. That ladder IS the path
order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).
