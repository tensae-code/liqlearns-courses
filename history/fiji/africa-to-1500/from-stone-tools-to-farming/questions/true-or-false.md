# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Societies evolve as people learn to gather and hunt, herd animals in tribes, grow tubers with a digging stick, then plough with domesticated animals about 7,000 years ago.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Chapter 1: types of society — hunting and gathering, pastoral, horticultural, agricultural" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
