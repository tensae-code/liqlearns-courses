# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Chapter 1: types of society — hunting and gathering, pastoral, horticultural, agricultural" tell?
a) Societies evolve as people learn to gather and hunt, herd animals in tribes, grow tubers with a digging stick, then plough with domesticated animals about 7,000 years ago
b) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) it is a story with no people and no places in it
**Answer: a) Societies evolve as people learn to gather and hunt, herd animals in tribes, grow tubers with a digging stick, then plough with domesticated animals about 7,000 years ago** — That is what this lesson teaches.
