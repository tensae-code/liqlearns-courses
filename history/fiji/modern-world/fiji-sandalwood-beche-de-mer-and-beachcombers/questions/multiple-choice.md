# Fiji: Sandalwood, Beche-de-Mer and Beachcombers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Early European contact: sandalwood trade, Charles Savage, beche-de-mer" tell?
a) Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools
b) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
c) it is a story with no people and no places in it
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: a) Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Early European contact: sandalwood trade, Charles Savage, beche-de-mer"?
a) Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools** — That is what this lesson teaches.
