# Fiji: Sandalwood, Beche-de-Mer and Beachcombers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Early European contact: sandalwood trade, Charles Savage, beche-de-mer" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools; shipwrecked beachcomber Charles Savage reaches Bau in 1808 with muskets from the wreck and shifts the balance of war; when sandalwood fades, the beche-de-mer trade takes villages over.
