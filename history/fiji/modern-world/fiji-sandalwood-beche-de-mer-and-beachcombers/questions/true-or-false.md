# Fiji: Sandalwood, Beche-de-Mer and Beachcombers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Early European contact: sandalwood trade, Charles Savage, beche-de-mer" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
