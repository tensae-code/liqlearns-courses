# Video script — Fiji: Sandalwood, Beche-de-Mer and Beachcombers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools.

Now the part called "Early European contact: sandalwood trade, Charles Savage, beche-de-mer". Europeans rush in for sandalwood, Fijians trade it eagerly for tabua, iron and tools; shipwrecked beachcomber Charles Savage reaches Bau in 1808 with muskets from the wreck and shifts the balance of war; when sandalwood fades, the beche-de-mer trade takes villages over.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
