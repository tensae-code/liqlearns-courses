# Fiji: Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tongan prince Ma'afu arrives in 1847 and builds a power base in Lau; Cakobau rises at Bau, planters Wilkinson and Thurston push a joint Fijian-European government, rival annexation schemes are weighed and rejected, and the 1871 Kingdom of Bau constitution makes Cakobau king of Fiji outside the northern and eastern confederacy.

**2.** What came of "Traditional government in Fiji: chiefdoms, Ma'afu and Cakobau" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Early Fijian society governs itself through chiefdoms; Ma'afu extends his influence even to Beqa in opposition to Cakobau, yet the Bauan kingdom leads Fiji only because of Tongan intervention, and Cakobau's Tui Viti claim outruns his real power.
