# Fiji: Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tongan prince Ma'afu arrives in 1847 and builds a power base in Lau. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Traditional government in Fiji: chiefdoms, Ma'afu and Cakobau" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
