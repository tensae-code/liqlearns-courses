# Fiji: Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) Tongan prince Ma'afu arrives in 1847 and builds a power base in Lau
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) it is a story with no people and no places in it
**Answer: b) Tongan prince Ma'afu arrives in 1847 and builds a power base in Lau** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Traditional government in Fiji: chiefdoms, Ma'afu and Cakobau"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Early Fijian society governs itself through chiefdoms  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Early Fijian society governs itself through chiefdoms** — That is what this lesson teaches.
