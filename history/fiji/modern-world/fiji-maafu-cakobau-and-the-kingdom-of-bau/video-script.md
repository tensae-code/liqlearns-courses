# Video script — Fiji: Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Tongan prince Ma'afu arrives in 1847 and builds a power base in Lau; early Fijian society governs itself through chiefdoms.

Now the part called "Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau". Tongan prince Ma'afu arrives in 1847 and builds a power base in Lau; Cakobau rises at Bau, planters Wilkinson and Thurston push a joint Fijian-European government, rival annexation schemes are weighed and rejected, and the 1871 Kingdom of Bau constitution makes Cakobau king of Fiji outside the northern and eastern confederacy.

Now the part called "Traditional government in Fiji: chiefdoms, Ma'afu and Cakobau". Early Fijian society governs itself through chiefdoms; Ma'afu extends his influence even to Beqa in opposition to Cakobau, yet the Bauan kingdom leads Fiji only because of Tongan intervention, and Cakobau's Tui Viti claim outruns his real power.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
