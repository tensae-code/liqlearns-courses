# Video script — Fiji Before Christianity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Before missionaries, Fijian religion is animist; before Christianity, Fiji's ancient religions centre on gods such as Ravuyalo and Rakola.

Now the part called "Before Christianity". Before missionaries, Fijian religion is animist; the bete priest in the burekalou foretells the future and directs worship of ancestral gods, and war and cannibalism end only when converted chiefs lead their people to Christianity.

Now the part called "4.2 Ancient religion in Fiji (with R.A. Derrick, A History of Fiji, 1946, extract)". Before Christianity, Fiji's ancient religions centre on gods such as Ravuyalo and Rakola; the bete priest serves them in the bure kalou, and pupils interrogate Derrick's 1946 history as a source.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
