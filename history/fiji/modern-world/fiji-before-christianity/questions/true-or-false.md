# Fiji Before Christianity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Before missionaries, Fijian religion is animist. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "4.2 Ancient religion in Fiji (with R.A. Derrick, A History of Fiji, 1946, extract)" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
