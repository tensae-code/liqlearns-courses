# Fiji Before Christianity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Before Christianity" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Before missionaries, Fijian religion is animist; the bete priest in the burekalou foretells the future and directs worship of ancestral gods, and war and cannibalism end only when converted chiefs lead their people to Christianity.

**2.** What came of "4.2 Ancient religion in Fiji (with R.A. Derrick, A History of Fiji, 1946, extract)" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Before Christianity, Fiji's ancient religions centre on gods such as Ravuyalo and Rakola; the bete priest serves them in the bure kalou, and pupils interrogate Derrick's 1946 history as a source.
