# Fiji Before Christianity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Before Christianity" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) Before missionaries, Fijian religion is animist
**Answer: d) Before missionaries, Fijian religion is animist** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "4.2 Ancient religion in Fiji (with R.A. Derrick, A History of Fiji, 1946, extract)"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Before Christianity, Fiji's ancient religions centre on gods such as Ravuyalo and Rakola  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Before Christianity, Fiji's ancient religions centre on gods such as Ravuyalo and Rakola** — That is what this lesson teaches.
