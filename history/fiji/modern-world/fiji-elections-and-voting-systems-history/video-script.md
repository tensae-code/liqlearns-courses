# Video script — Fiji: Elections and Voting Systems as History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Party politics begins in the 1960s before independence.

Now the part called "Fiji's elections as history: 1972, 1977 and 2006". Party politics begins in the 1960s before independence; the Alliance wins the 1972 election (52 seats) and the split, multi-party 1977 elections, and pupils research how the 2006 Alternative Voting system worked and what changed after it.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
