# Fiji: Elections and Voting Systems as History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Fiji's elections as history: 1972, 1977 and 2006" tell?
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) Party politics begins in the 1960s before independence
**Answer: d) Party politics begins in the 1960s before independence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Fiji's elections as history: 1972, 1977 and 2006"?
a) Party politics begins in the 1960s before independence  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Party politics begins in the 1960s before independence** — That is what this lesson teaches.
