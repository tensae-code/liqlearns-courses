# Fiji: Elections and Voting Systems as History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Party politics begins in the 1960s before independence.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Fiji's elections as history: 1972, 1977 and 2006" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
