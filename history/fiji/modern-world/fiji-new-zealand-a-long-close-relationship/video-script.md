# Video script — Fiji and New Zealand: A Long, Close Relationship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Bank of New Zealand opens in Levuka in 1877; a Bauan learns rugby in New Zealand and returns to teach it.

Now the part called "Unit 2 Fiji and New Zealand: war and airfields". The Bank of New Zealand opens in Levuka in 1877; in WWI Fijians travel to join the Māori Battalion and Sukuna's schooling links the two peoples; the RNZAF builds at Laucala Bay, and in November 1941 the US agrees to finance two 7,000-foot runways at Nadi, built by a New Zealand firm — the origin of Nadi Airport.

Now the part called "Unit 2 Topic E: Fijian rugby's New Zealand origins". A Bauan learns rugby in New Zealand and returns to teach it; a Fiji Times report has the Naililili side singing "We licked them on their own ground", and Robert Dewey's research is set against the myth of Fiji rugby's origins.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
