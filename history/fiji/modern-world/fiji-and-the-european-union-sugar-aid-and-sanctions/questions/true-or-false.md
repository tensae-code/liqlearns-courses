# Fiji and the European Union: Sugar, Aid and Sanctions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The EU, formed by treaty on the Rewa Bridge's plaque as in Suva, becomes Fiji's sugar patron.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 6 Fiji and the European Union" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
