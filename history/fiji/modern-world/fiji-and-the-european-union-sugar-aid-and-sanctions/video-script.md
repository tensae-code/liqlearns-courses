# Video script — Fiji and the European Union: Sugar, Aid and Sanctions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The EU, formed by treaty on the Rewa Bridge's plaque as in Suva, becomes Fiji's sugar patron.

Now the part called "Unit 6 Fiji and the European Union". The EU, formed by treaty on the Rewa Bridge's plaque as in Suva, becomes Fiji's sugar patron — then, after the December 2006 military coup, the European Commission axes $43 million of sugar support and opens Article 96 consultations.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
