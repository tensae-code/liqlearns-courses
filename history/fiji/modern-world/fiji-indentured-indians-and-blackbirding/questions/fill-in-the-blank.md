# Fiji: Indentured Indians and Blackbirding — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Governor Arthur Gordon, needing plantation labour iTaukei would not supply, has Solomon Islanders blackbirded and, from the Leonidas in ____ to 1916, Indian girmit labourers brought from Calcutta. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 1879

**2.** Girmit life is marked by a broken 40-women-per-100-men quota, jealousy and fighting — the name at the heart of this part is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Fiji
