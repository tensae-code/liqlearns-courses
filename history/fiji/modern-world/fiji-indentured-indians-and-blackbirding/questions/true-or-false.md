# Fiji: Indentured Indians and Blackbirding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Governor Arthur Gordon, needing plantation labour iTaukei would not supply, has Solomon Islanders blackbirded and, from the Leonidas in 1879 to 1916, Indian girmit labourers brought from Calcutta. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "The Early Sugar Industry: indentured labourers in Fiji" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
