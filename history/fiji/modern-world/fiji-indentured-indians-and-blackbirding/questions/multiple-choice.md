Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Continuity and Change: arrival of other ethnic groups" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Governor Arthur Gordon, needing plantation labour iTaukei would not supply, has Solomon Islanders blackbirded and, from the Leonidas in 1879 to 1916, Indian girmit labourers brought from Calcutta
b) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) it is a story with no people and no places in it
**Answer: a) Governor Arthur Gordon, needing plantation labour iTaukei would not supply, has Solomon Islanders blackbirded and, from the Leonidas in 1879 to 1916, Indian girmit labourers brought from Calcutta** — That is what this lesson teaches.
