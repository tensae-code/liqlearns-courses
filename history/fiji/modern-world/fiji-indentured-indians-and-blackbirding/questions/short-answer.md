# Fiji: Indentured Indians and Blackbirding — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Continuity and Change: arrival of other ethnic groups" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Governor Arthur Gordon, needing plantation labour iTaukei would not supply, has Solomon Islanders blackbirded and, from the Leonidas in 1879 to 1916, Indian girmit labourers brought from Calcutta — most stay after indenture ends and become farmers and businessmen.

**2.** What came of "The Early Sugar Industry: indentured labourers in Fiji" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Girmit life is marked by a broken 40-women-per-100-men quota, jealousy and fighting, and no schooling for children, until C.F. Andrews and W.W. Pearson investigate in 1915 and their report brings the system down.
