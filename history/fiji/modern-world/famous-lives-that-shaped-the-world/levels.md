# Famous Lives That Shaped the World — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: children pick one life — Lincoln, Gandhi, Mandela, Lenin, Churchill, Columbus, da Vinci, Plato, plus Fiji's own Mara, Sukuna, Bavadra, Chaudhry and Luveni — and explain why that person is famous and what their life changed — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
