# Famous Lives That Shaped the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "2.1A Famous People (list of 60, 19th-21st centuries)" tell?
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
c) Children pick one life
d) it is a story with no people and no places in it
**Answer: c) Children pick one life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "2.1A Famous People (list of 60, 19th-21st centuries)"?
a) Children pick one life  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Children pick one life** — That is what this lesson teaches.
