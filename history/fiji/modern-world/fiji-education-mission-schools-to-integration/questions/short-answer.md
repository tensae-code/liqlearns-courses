# Fiji: Education — Mission Schools to Integration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Early mission education and colonial education, 1800s-1900s" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Missionaries open Fiji's first schools; from 1879 European settlers' children go to Levuka Public School, Fiji's first public school, while chiefs ask the colonial government to school their sons, and Hannah Dudley founds the Methodist Mission School for Indian children.

**2.** What came of "Integrated education and case studies: St Felix College / Marist Brothers, Suva Grammar School (1960- ), and the 2010 school-names ruling" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** "multi-racial schools" grow through case-study schools — Brother Lambert's 1937 report, Suva Grammar turning co-educational — until in 2010 the Minister orders school names changed so schools themselves preach integration.
