# Video script — Fiji: Education — Mission Schools to Integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Missionaries open Fiji's first schools; "multi-racial schools" grow through case-study schools.

Now the part called "Early mission education and colonial education, 1800s-1900s". Missionaries open Fiji's first schools; from 1879 European settlers' children go to Levuka Public School, Fiji's first public school, while chiefs ask the colonial government to school their sons, and Hannah Dudley founds the Methodist Mission School for Indian children.

Now the part called "Integrated education and case studies: St Felix College / Marist Brothers, Suva Grammar School (1960- ), and the 2010 school-names ruling". "multi-racial schools" grow through case-study schools — Brother Lambert's 1937 report, Suva Grammar turning co-educational — until in 2010 the Minister orders school names changed so schools themselves preach integration.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
