# Lesson plan — Exploration: Causes and Results

**Course:** History — Fiji · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Causes and Results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Contact, Trade, Mission and Kingdom (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first; a table of explorers.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Contact, Trade, Mission and Kingdom — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Arrival of Europeans** — Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first.
2. **2.2 European Discovery of Fiji** — A table of explorers — Tasman (1643, Heemskerck), Cook, Bligh (1789 and 1792 in Providence, charting new islands) and Dumont d'Urville (1834, Astrolabe) — turns Fiji from sightings into mapped islands.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first; a table of explorers. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
