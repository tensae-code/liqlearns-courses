# Video script — Exploration: Causes and Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first; a table of explorers.

Now the part called "The Arrival of Europeans". Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first.

Now the part called "2.2 European Discovery of Fiji". A table of explorers — Tasman (1643, Heemskerck), Cook, Bligh (1789 and 1792 in Providence, charting new islands) and Dumont d'Urville (1834, Astrolabe) — turns Fiji from sightings into mapped islands.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
