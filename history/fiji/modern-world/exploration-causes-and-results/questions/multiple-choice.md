# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "The Arrival of Europeans" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first
b) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
c) it is a story with no people and no places in it
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: a) Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "2.2 European Discovery of Fiji"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) A table of explorers  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) A table of explorers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
