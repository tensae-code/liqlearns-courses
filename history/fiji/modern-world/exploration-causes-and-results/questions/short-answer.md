# Exploration: Causes and Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Arrival of Europeans" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first.

**2.** What came of "2.2 European Discovery of Fiji" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A table of explorers — Tasman (1643, Heemskerck), Cook, Bligh (1789 and 1792 in Providence, charting new islands) and Dumont d'Urville (1834, Astrolabe) — turns Fiji from sightings into mapped islands.
