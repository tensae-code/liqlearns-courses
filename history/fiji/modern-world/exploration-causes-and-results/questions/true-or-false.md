# Exploration: Causes and Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tasman's accidental 1643 sighting, Cook in 1774 and Bligh sailing through in 1789 after the Bounty mutiny open Fiji to shipwrecked sailors, runaway convicts, sandalwood traders and then missionaries who convert chiefs first. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "2.2 European Discovery of Fiji" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
