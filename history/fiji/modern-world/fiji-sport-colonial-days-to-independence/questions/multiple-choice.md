# Fiji: Sport — Colonial Days to Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Sports in Fiji: colonial days — history of athletics, rugby and cricket; Royal Suva Yacht Club" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules
**Answer: d) Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Sport and recreation from independence: unifying a nation"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) From 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) From 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities** — That is what this lesson teaches.
