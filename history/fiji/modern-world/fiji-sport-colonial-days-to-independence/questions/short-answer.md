# Fiji: Sport — Colonial Days to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Sports in Fiji: colonial days — history of athletics, rugby and cricket; Royal Suva Yacht Club" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules; the sail-only Royal Suva Yacht Club holds its first race on 22 October 1932 for a two-shilling entry.

**2.** What came of "Sport and recreation from independence: unifying a nation" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities — Fiji stages world amateur golf in 1979, and Waisale Serevi dedicates the 2005 Rugby World Cup Sevens win to a nation cheering as one.
