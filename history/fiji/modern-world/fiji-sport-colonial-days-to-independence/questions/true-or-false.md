# Fiji: Sport — Colonial Days to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Sport and recreation from independence: unifying a nation" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
