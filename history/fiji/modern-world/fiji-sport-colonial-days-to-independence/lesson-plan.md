## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Sports in Fiji: colonial days — history of athletics, rugby and cricket; Royal Suva Yacht Club** — Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules; the sail-only Royal Suva Yacht Club holds its first race on 22 October 1932 for a two-shilling entry.
2. **Sport and recreation from independence: unifying a nation** — From 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities — Fiji stages world amateur golf in 1979, and Waisale Serevi dedicates the 2005 Rugby World Cup Sevens win to a nation cheering as one.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules; from 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
