# Video script — Fiji: Sport — Colonial Days to Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules; from 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities.

Now the part called "Sports in Fiji: colonial days — history of athletics, rugby and cricket; Royal Suva Yacht Club". Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules; the sail-only Royal Suva Yacht Club holds its first race on 22 October 1932 for a two-shilling entry.

Now the part called "Sport and recreation from independence: unifying a nation". From 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities — Fiji stages world amateur golf in 1979, and Waisale Serevi dedicates the 2005 Rugby World Cup Sevens win to a nation cheering as one.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
