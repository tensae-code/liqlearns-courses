# Fiji: Coups and Constitutions, 1987 to 2014 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Mara's Alliance wins in ____, Bavadra's coalition wins in 1987 and Rabuka's two coups make Fiji a republic, Chaudhry becomes first Indo-Fijian PM in 1999 and is toppled by Speight in 2000, Bainimarama coups in 2006, the constitution is suspended in 2009 and rewritten in 2013 before the 2014 election. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 1972

**2.** Chaudhry's government, barely a year old, is seized with most MPs in the ____ coup. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 2000
