# Fiji: Coups and Constitutions, 1987 to 2014 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mara's Alliance wins in 1972, Bavadra's coalition wins in 1987 and Rabuka's two coups make Fiji a republic, Chaudhry becomes first Indo-Fijian PM in 1999 and is toppled by Speight in 2000, Bainimarama coups in 2006, the constitution is suspended in 2009 and rewritten in 2013 before the 2014 election. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Political conditions: the coup of 2000 and the 2006 election" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
