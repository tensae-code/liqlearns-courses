# Fiji: Coups and Constitutions, 1987 to 2014 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Timeline 1970 to present / Leadership in Fiji" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mara's Alliance wins in 1972, Bavadra's coalition wins in 1987 and Rabuka's two coups make Fiji a republic, Chaudhry becomes first Indo-Fijian PM in 1999 and is toppled by Speight in 2000, Bainimarama coups in 2006, the constitution is suspended in 2009 and rewritten in 2013 before the 2014 election.

**2.** What came of "Political conditions: the coup of 2000 and the 2006 election" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Chaudhry's government, barely a year old, is seized with most MPs in the 2000 coup; the story is carried forward to the closely fought Fiji election of 2006.
