# Video script — Fiji: Coups and Constitutions, 1987 to 2014

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Mara's Alliance wins in 1972, Bavadra's coalition wins in 1987 and Rabuka's two coups make Fiji a republic, Chaudhry becomes first Indo-Fijian PM in 1999 and is toppled by Speight in 2000, Bainimarama coups in 2006, the constitution is suspended in 2009 and rewritten in 2013 before the 2014 election; Chaudhry's government, barely a year old, is seized with most MPs in the 2000 coup; after the 1987 turn a new category of leadership emerges; readings carry the story from the disputed aftermath of the 1977 election to Qarase's interim civilian government of 2000, formed after a coup; one month after the 1987 election, on 14 May, Major Sitiveni Rabuka leads a coup; the interim government's 1989 committee produces the 1990 constitution amid brain-drain.

Now the part called "Timeline 1970 to present / Leadership in Fiji". Mara's Alliance wins in 1972, Bavadra's coalition wins in 1987 and Rabuka's two coups make Fiji a republic, Chaudhry becomes first Indo-Fijian PM in 1999 and is toppled by Speight in 2000, Bainimarama coups in 2006, the constitution is suspended in 2009 and rewritten in 2013 before the 2014 election.

Now the part called "Political conditions: the coup of 2000 and the 2006 election". Chaudhry's government, barely a year old, is seized with most MPs in the 2000 coup; the story is carried forward to the closely fought Fiji election of 2006.

Now the part called "Challenges 1980-1987 and new leadership 1987-2006". After the 1987 turn a new category of leadership emerges — interim ministers such as Senilagakali and Nailatikau serve governments born of coups, while non-political leaders champion integration from outside politics.

Now the part called "After the 1977 election; Fiji's interim civilian government 2000". Readings carry the story from the disputed aftermath of the 1977 election to Qarase's interim civilian government of 2000, formed after a coup.

Now the part called "Coup d'état 1987 and the impact of the second coup". One month after the 1987 election, on 14 May, Major Sitiveni Rabuka leads a coup; his second coup destroys the chances of a negotiated settlement between the communities.

Now the part called "The 1990 Constitution, the review commission and the 1997 Constitution". The interim government's 1989 committee produces the 1990 constitution amid brain-drain; a three-person review commission follows, and its work issues in the 1997 Constitution Amendment Act.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
