## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Timeline 1970 to present / Leadership in Fiji** — Mara's Alliance wins in 1972, Bavadra's coalition wins in 1987 and Rabuka's two coups make Fiji a republic, Chaudhry becomes first Indo-Fijian PM in 1999 and is toppled by Speight in 2000, Bainimarama coups in 2006, the constitution is suspended in 2009 and rewritten in 2013 before the 2014 election.
2. **Political conditions: the coup of 2000 and the 2006 election** — Chaudhry's government, barely a year old, is seized with most MPs in the 2000 coup; the story is carried forward to the closely fought Fiji election of 2006.
3. **Challenges 1980-1987 and new leadership 1987-2006** — After the 1987 turn a new category of leadership emerges — interim ministers such as Senilagakali and Nailatikau serve governments born of coups, while non-political leaders champion integration from outside politics.
4. **After the 1977 election; Fiji's interim civilian government 2000** — Readings carry the story from the disputed aftermath of the 1977 election to Qarase's interim civilian government of 2000, formed after a coup.
5. **Coup d'état 1987 and the impact of the second coup** — One month after the 1987 election, on 14 May, Major Sitiveni Rabuka leads a coup; his second coup destroys the chances of a negotiated settlement between the communities.
6. **The 1990 Constitution, the review commission and the 1997 Constitution** — The interim government's 1989 committee produces the 1990 constitution amid brain-drain; a three-person review commission follows, and its work issues in the 1997 Constitution Amendment Act.
