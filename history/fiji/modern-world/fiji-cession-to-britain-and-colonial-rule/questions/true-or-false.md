# Fiji: Cession to Britain and Colonial Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Barter gives way to money, mission schools spread, Cakobau the Tui Viti cedes Fiji to Great Britain. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "2.1.2-2.1.3 Fiji during and reasons for the Deed of Cession" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
