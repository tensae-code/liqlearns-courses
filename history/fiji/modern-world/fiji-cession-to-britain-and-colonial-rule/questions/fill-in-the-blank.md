# Fiji: Cession to Britain and Colonial Rule — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Barter gives way to money, mission schools spread, ____ the Tui Viti cedes Fiji to Great Britain. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cakobau

**2.** US Consul Williams's looted stores and a US warship's demand leave Cakobau owing debts he cannot pay (US$38,531) — the name at the heart of this part is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Fiji
