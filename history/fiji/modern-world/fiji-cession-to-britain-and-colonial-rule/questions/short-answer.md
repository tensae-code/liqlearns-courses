# Fiji: Cession to Britain and Colonial Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Early iTaukei and Europeans / Fiji Today" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Barter gives way to money, mission schools spread, Cakobau the Tui Viti cedes Fiji to Great Britain, and a colonial government at Levuka brings schools, hospitals, police and law.

**2.** What came of "2.1.2-2.1.3 Fiji during and reasons for the Deed of Cession" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** US Consul Williams's looted stores and a US warship's demand leave Cakobau owing debts he cannot pay (US$38,531); Thurston carries his offer to London and on 10 October 1874 Cakobau, Ma'afu and senior chiefs sign Fiji over to Britain.
