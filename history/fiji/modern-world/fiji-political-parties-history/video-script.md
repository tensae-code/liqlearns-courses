# Video script — Fiji: The History of Political Parties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Parties form in the 1960s to contest the road to independence; a seats table runs across Fiji's elections to the 6-13 May 2006 result (SDL/United Fiji 36 seats), taking in the election that made Mahendra Chaudhry, of the Fiji Labour Party, Fiji's first Indian prime minister.

Now the part called "History of political parties in Fiji; the 1966 election". Parties form in the 1960s to contest the road to independence; Brij Lal's Broken Waves cartoon and text anchor a study of the 1966 election, and leader portraits (Jai Ram Reddy; Vanuatu's Father Walter Lini as a Pacific comparison) show who built them.

Now the part called "Past elections table and the 2006 election". A seats table runs across Fiji's elections to the 6-13 May 2006 result (SDL/United Fiji 36 seats), taking in the election that made Mahendra Chaudhry, of the Fiji Labour Party, Fiji's first Indian prime minister.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
