# Pacific Colonisation: New Zealand, Australia and the Islands — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony; Aboriginal Australia is taken with Cook's 1770 landing and the 1788 convict fleet to Botany Bay; Fiji and Kiribati are ruled by Britain, Samoa by Britain and New Zealand — children weigh colonisation's positive and negative effects country by country — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
