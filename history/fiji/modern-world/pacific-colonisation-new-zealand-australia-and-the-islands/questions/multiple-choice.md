# Pacific Colonisation: New Zealand, Australia and the Islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Colonisation in the Pacific" tell?
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony
c) it is a story with no people and no places in it
d) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
**Answer: b) Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Colonisation in the Pacific"?
a) Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony** — That is what this lesson teaches.
