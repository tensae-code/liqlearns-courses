# Pacific Colonisation: New Zealand, Australia and the Islands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Colonisation in the Pacific" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
