# Video script — Pacific Colonisation: New Zealand, Australia and the Islands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony.

Now the part called "Colonisation in the Pacific". Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony; Aboriginal Australia is taken with Cook's 1770 landing and the 1788 convict fleet to Botany Bay; Fiji and Kiribati are ruled by Britain, Samoa by Britain and New Zealand — children weigh colonisation's positive and negative effects country by country.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
