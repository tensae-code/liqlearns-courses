# Video script — Fiji and India: The Agreement for Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Independent India (1947) at first does not press Britain over Fiji.

Now the part called "Unit 5 Fiji and India". Independent India (1947) at first does not press Britain over Fiji; Ratu Mara woos India, his private talks with A.D. Patel open confidential Suva talks, and the agreement for an independence constitution follows; Indira Gandhi visits in 1981 and the Girmit Centre rises at Lautoka.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
