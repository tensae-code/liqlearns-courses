# Fiji: Missionaries, Chiefs and Conversion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cross and Cargill are received at Lakeba in 1835, Hunt and Lythe follow, four Wesleyan stations stand by 1840. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "4.3 Emergence of religious groups in Fiji" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
