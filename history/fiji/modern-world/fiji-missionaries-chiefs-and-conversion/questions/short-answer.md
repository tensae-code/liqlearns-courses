# Fiji: Missionaries, Chiefs and Conversion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Missionaries and their influence" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cross and Cargill are received at Lakeba in 1835, Hunt and Lythe follow, four Wesleyan stations stand by 1840, and conversion works through chiefs; explorer Wilkes meanwhile wins Tanoa of Bau's promise to protect foreign ships and crews.

**2.** What came of "4.3 Emergence of religious groups in Fiji" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** After the Battle of Kaba in 1854 Cakobau's conversion secures Methodism's advance, cession follows in 1874, Catholic missionaries arrive, and Hinduism and Islam come with the girmitiya — Hindus and Muslims cooperating at festivals despite prejudice.
