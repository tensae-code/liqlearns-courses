# Fiji: Missionaries, Chiefs and Conversion — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Cross and Cargill are received at Lakeba in ____, Hunt and Lythe follow, four Wesleyan stations stand by 1840. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 1835

**2.** After the Battle of Kaba in ____ Cakobau's conversion secures Methodism's advance, cession follows in 1874, Catholic missionaries arrive. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1854
