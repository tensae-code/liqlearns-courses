## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Missionaries and their influence** — Cross and Cargill are received at Lakeba in 1835, Hunt and Lythe follow, four Wesleyan stations stand by 1840, and conversion works through chiefs; explorer Wilkes meanwhile wins Tanoa of Bau's promise to protect foreign ships and crews.
2. **4.3 Emergence of religious groups in Fiji** — After the Battle of Kaba in 1854 Cakobau's conversion secures Methodism's advance, cession follows in 1874, Catholic missionaries arrive, and Hinduism and Islam come with the girmitiya — Hindus and Muslims cooperating at festivals despite prejudice.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Cross and Cargill are received at Lakeba in 1835, Hunt and Lythe follow, four Wesleyan stations stand by 1840; after the Battle of Kaba in 1854 Cakobau's conversion secures Methodism's advance, cession follows in 1874, Catholic missionaries arrive. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
