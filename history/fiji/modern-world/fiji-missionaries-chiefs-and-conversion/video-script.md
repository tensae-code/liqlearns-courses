# Video script — Fiji: Missionaries, Chiefs and Conversion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

Now the part called "Missionaries and their influence". Cross and Cargill are received at Lakeba in 1835, Hunt and Lythe follow, four Wesleyan stations stand by 1840, and conversion works through chiefs; explorer Wilkes meanwhile wins Tanoa of Bau's promise to protect foreign ships and crews.

Now the part called "4.3 Emergence of religious groups in Fiji". After the Battle of Kaba in 1854 Cakobau's conversion secures Methodism's advance, cession follows in 1874, Catholic missionaries arrive, and Hinduism and Islam come with the girmitiya — Hindus and Muslims cooperating at festivals despite prejudice.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
