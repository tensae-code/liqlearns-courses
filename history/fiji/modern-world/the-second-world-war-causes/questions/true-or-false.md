# The Second World War: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Japan, short of land and resources, invades Manchuria (1931) and China (1937).
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "2.2 Significant World Events: World War II and its causes" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
