# The Second World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "2.2 Significant World Events: World War II and its causes" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
c) Japan, short of land and resources, invades Manchuria (1931) and China (1937)
d) it is a story with no people and no places in it
**Answer: c) Japan, short of land and resources, invades Manchuria (1931) and China (1937)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Japan, short of land and resources, invades Manchuria (1931) and China (1937)  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Japan, short of land and resources, invades Manchuria (1931) and China (1937)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
