# Video script — The Second World War: Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Japan, short of land and resources, invades Manchuria (1931) and China (1937).

Now the part called "2.2 Significant World Events: World War II and its causes". Japan, short of land and resources, invades Manchuria (1931) and China (1937); fascist dictators rise from WWI's turmoil seeking empires; the Great Depression destabilises governments — together they bring the 1939-1945 total war, the Holocaust and nuclear weapons.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
