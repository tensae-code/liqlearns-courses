# Fiji: Presidents and Prime Ministers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "2.5 Leaders in our communities" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Children research Fiji's Presidents (Ganilau, Mara, Iloilo, Nailatikau) and Prime Ministers (Mara, Bavadra, Rabuka, Chaudhry, Qarase) — where each came from, his traditional title, and his years in office.

**2.** What came of "2.2 Continuity and Change: leaders of the Pacific / Fiji's prime ministers list" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A dated table of Fiji's leaders runs through Qarase (2001-2006), Senilagakali and Bainimarama, teaching leadership change as continuity-and-change.
