# Fiji: Presidents and Prime Ministers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Children research ____'s Presidents (Ganilau, Mara, Iloilo, Nailatikau) and Prime Ministers (Mara, Bavadra, Rabuka, Chaudhry, Qarase). (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fiji

**2.** A dated table of Fiji's leaders runs through Qarase (____-2006), Senilagakali and Bainimarama, teaching leadership change as continuity-and-change. (Sub lesson 2)
