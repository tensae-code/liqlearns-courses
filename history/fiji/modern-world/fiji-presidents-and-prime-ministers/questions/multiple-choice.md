# Fiji: Presidents and Prime Ministers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "2.5 Leaders in our communities" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) Children research Fiji's Presidents (Ganilau, Mara, Iloilo, Nailatikau) and Prime Ministers (Mara, Bavadra, Rabuka, Chaudhry, Qarase)
c) it is a story with no people and no places in it
d) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
**Answer: b) Children research Fiji's Presidents (Ganilau, Mara, Iloilo, Nailatikau) and Prime Ministers (Mara, Bavadra, Rabuka, Chaudhry, Qarase)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
