# Fiji: Presidents and Prime Ministers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Children research Fiji's Presidents (Ganilau, Mara, Iloilo, Nailatikau) and Prime Ministers (Mara, Bavadra, Rabuka, Chaudhry, Qarase). (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "2.2 Continuity and Change: leaders of the Pacific / Fiji's prime ministers list" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
