# Video script — Fiji: Presidents and Prime Ministers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Children research Fiji's Presidents (Ganilau, Mara, Iloilo, Nailatikau) and Prime Ministers (Mara, Bavadra, Rabuka, Chaudhry, Qarase); a dated table of Fiji's leaders runs through Qarase (2001-2006), Senilagakali and Bainimarama, teaching leadership change as continuity-and-change.

Now the part called "2.5 Leaders in our communities". Children research Fiji's Presidents (Ganilau, Mara, Iloilo, Nailatikau) and Prime Ministers (Mara, Bavadra, Rabuka, Chaudhry, Qarase) — where each came from, his traditional title, and his years in office.

Now the part called "2.2 Continuity and Change: leaders of the Pacific / Fiji's prime ministers list". A dated table of Fiji's leaders runs through Qarase (2001-2006), Senilagakali and Bainimarama, teaching leadership change as continuity-and-change.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
