# Fiji's Legal System: From Chiefs to Courts — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the earliest iTaukei rely on their chiefs' decision-making; once the British colony begins, courts replace the chief's word, and the unit traces that handover as the root of Fiji's modern legal order — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
