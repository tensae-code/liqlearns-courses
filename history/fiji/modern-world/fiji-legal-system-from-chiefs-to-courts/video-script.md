# Video script — Fiji's Legal System: From Chiefs to Courts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. The earliest iTaukei rely on their chiefs' decision-making.

Now the part called "Introduction: from chiefly decision-making to colonial courts". The earliest iTaukei rely on their chiefs' decision-making; once the British colony begins, courts replace the chief's word, and the unit traces that handover as the root of Fiji's modern legal order.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.
