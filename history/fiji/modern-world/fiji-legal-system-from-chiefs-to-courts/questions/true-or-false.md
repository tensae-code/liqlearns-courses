# Fiji's Legal System: From Chiefs to Courts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The earliest iTaukei rely on their chiefs' decision-making.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Introduction: from chiefly decision-making to colonial courts" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
