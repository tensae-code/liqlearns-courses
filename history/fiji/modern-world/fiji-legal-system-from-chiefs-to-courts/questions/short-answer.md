# Fiji's Legal System: From Chiefs to Courts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Introduction: from chiefly decision-making to colonial courts" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** The earliest iTaukei rely on their chiefs' decision-making; once the British colony begins, courts replace the chief's word, and the unit traces that handover as the root of Fiji's modern legal order.
