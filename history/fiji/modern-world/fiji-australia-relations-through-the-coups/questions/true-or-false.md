# Fiji and Australia: Relations Through the Coups — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Australia recognises Fiji at independence in 1970.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Unit 4 Fiji and Australia, taught from cartoons" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
