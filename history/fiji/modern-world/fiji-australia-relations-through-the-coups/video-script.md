# Video script — Fiji and Australia: Relations Through the Coups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Australia recognises Fiji at independence in 1970.

Now the part called "Unit 4 Fiji and Australia, taught from cartoons". Australia recognises Fiji at independence in 1970; the two 1987 coups take Canberra by surprise; Australian newspaper cartoons of the 2000 coup and hostage crisis, the Great Council of Chiefs and the tourism collapse are interrogated as sources for Australia's opinion of Fiji.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
