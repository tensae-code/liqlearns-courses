# Fiji: Gordon's Native Policy — Land and Chiefs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Sir Arthur Gordon: land policies, taxation and native administration" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Gordon arrives in 1875 to a measles epidemic that killed about a quarter of Fiji and an uprising in the Sigatoka valley; the "champion of native rights" freezes land sales, proves only 164,000 ha fairly bought, locks the rest as inalienable native land, and under the 1876 Native Affairs Ordinance rules iTaukei through their own ranks — Governor, Council of Chiefs, Roko, Buli, Turaga-ni-Koro — a system lasting to the 1970s.

**2.** What came of "The development of the Native Administration since 1876" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From 10 October 1874 Fiji is home to peoples of different cultures; Gordon builds a separate Fijian Administration from 1876, province by province, expressly to preserve iTaukei society — pupils do a personality study of its makers.
