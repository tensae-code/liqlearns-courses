# Fiji: Gordon's Native Policy — Land and Chiefs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Sir Arthur Gordon: land policies, taxation and native administration" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) Gordon arrives in 1875 to a measles epidemic that killed about a quarter of Fiji and an uprising in the Sigatoka valley
d) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
**Answer: c) Gordon arrives in 1875 to a measles epidemic that killed about a quarter of Fiji and an uprising in the Sigatoka valley** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "The development of the Native Administration since 1876"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) From 10 October 1874 Fiji is home to peoples of different cultures  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) From 10 October 1874 Fiji is home to peoples of different cultures** — That is what this lesson teaches.
