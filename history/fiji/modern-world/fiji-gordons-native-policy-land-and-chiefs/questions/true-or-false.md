# Fiji: Gordon's Native Policy — Land and Chiefs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gordon arrives in 1875 to a measles epidemic that killed about a quarter of Fiji and an uprising in the Sigatoka valley. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "The development of the Native Administration since 1876" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
