# Video script — United Nations Agencies and Their Founders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Polish bacteriologist Ludwik Rajchman founds UNICEF and chairs it 1946-1950 until it becomes a permanent UN body in 1953, alongside the founding stories of WHO, UNESCO and FAO; the UN is built after WWII to keep peace, with veto-bearing permanent members.

Now the part called "Unit 1 Origins and roles of world organisations". Polish bacteriologist Ludwik Rajchman founds UNICEF and chairs it 1946-1950 until it becomes a permanent UN body in 1953, alongside the founding stories of WHO, UNESCO and FAO.

Now the part called "Chapter 1, 3-4 The United Nations Organisation and UNICEF". The UN is built after WWII to keep peace, with veto-bearing permanent members; UNICEF is founded in 1946 for children left homeless by the Second World War.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
