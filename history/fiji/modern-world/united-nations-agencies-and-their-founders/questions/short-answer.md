# United Nations Agencies and Their Founders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Unit 1 Origins and roles of world organisations" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Polish bacteriologist Ludwik Rajchman founds UNICEF and chairs it 1946-1950 until it becomes a permanent UN body in 1953, alongside the founding stories of WHO, UNESCO and FAO.

**2.** What came of "Chapter 1, 3-4 The United Nations Organisation and UNICEF" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The UN is built after WWII to keep peace, with veto-bearing permanent members; UNICEF is founded in 1946 for children left homeless by the Second World War.
