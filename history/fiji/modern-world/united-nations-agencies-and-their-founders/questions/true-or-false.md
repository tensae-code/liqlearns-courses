# United Nations Agencies and Their Founders — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Polish bacteriologist Ludwik Rajchman founds UNICEF and chairs it 1946-1950 until it becomes a permanent UN body in 1953, alongside the founding stories of WHO, UNESCO and FAO. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Chapter 1, 3-4 The United Nations Organisation and UNICEF" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
