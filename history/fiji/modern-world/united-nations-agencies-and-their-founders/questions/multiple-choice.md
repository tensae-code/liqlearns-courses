# United Nations Agencies and Their Founders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Unit 1 Origins and roles of world organisations" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) Polish bacteriologist Ludwik Rajchman founds UNICEF and chairs it 1946-1950 until it becomes a permanent UN body in 1953, alongside the founding stories of WHO, UNESCO and FAO
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) it is a story with no people and no places in it
**Answer: b) Polish bacteriologist Ludwik Rajchman founds UNICEF and chairs it 1946-1950 until it becomes a permanent UN body in 1953, alongside the founding stories of WHO, UNESCO and FAO** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Chapter 1, 3-4 The United Nations Organisation and UNICEF"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The UN is built after WWII to keep peace, with veto-bearing permanent members  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) The UN is built after WWII to keep peace, with veto-bearing permanent members** — That is what this lesson teaches.
