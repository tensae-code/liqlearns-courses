## Sub lesson 1 — Fiji's Independence and the 1874-1970 timeline
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead, and on 10 October 1970 Fiji ends 96 years of British rule — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Moving Towards Independence: constitutional changes 1904-1970
