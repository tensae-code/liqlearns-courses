# Video script — Fiji: Independence 1970

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead; step by step the Legislative Council gains Indian members (1916), the membership system and the common-versus-communal-roll fight shape the 1963 Assembly and 1965 constitution, Alliance and Federation parties spar after 1966; Fiji enters independence in 1970 as a multi-racial society carrying the colonial legacy; as in 1965, the London conference turns on representation in the House.

Now the part called "Fiji's Independence and the 1874-1970 timeline". Measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead, and on 10 October 1970 Fiji ends 96 years of British rule.

Now the part called "Moving Towards Independence: constitutional changes 1904-1970". Step by step the Legislative Council gains Indian members (1916), the membership system and the common-versus-communal-roll fight shape the 1963 Assembly and 1965 constitution, Alliance and Federation parties spar after 1966, and the April 1970 London conference agrees a fully sovereign independent Fiji.

Now the part called "Background to 1970 and leadership in the first ten years, 1970-1980". Fiji enters independence in 1970 as a multi-racial society carrying the colonial legacy; Mara's Alliance leads, the Governor-General's role is questioned in the 1977 crisis, and pupils match leaders to the independence settlement.

Now the part called "The 1970 Constitution: road to independence". As in 1965, the London conference turns on representation in the House; the resulting 1970 constitution's main features and cross-voting seats carry Fiji into independence, and its first governments are surveyed.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
