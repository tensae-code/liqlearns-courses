# Fiji: Independence 1970 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Fiji's Independence and the 1874-1970 timeline" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) Measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead
c) it is a story with no people and no places in it
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Moving Towards Independence: constitutional changes 1904-1970"? (Sub lesson 2)

**Answer: a) Step by step the Legislative Council gains Indian members (1916), the membership system and the common-versus-communal-roll fight shape the 1963 Assembly and 1965 constitution, Alliance and Federation parties spar after 1966** — That is what this lesson teaches.
