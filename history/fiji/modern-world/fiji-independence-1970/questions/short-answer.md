# Fiji: Independence 1970 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Fiji's Independence and the 1874-1970 timeline" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead, and on 10 October 1970 Fiji ends 96 years of British rule.

**2.** What came of "Moving Towards Independence: constitutional changes 1904-1970" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Step by step the Legislative Council gains Indian members (1916), the membership system and the common-versus-communal-roll fight shape the 1963 Assembly and 1965 constitution, Alliance and Federation parties spar after 1966, and the April 1970 London conference agrees a fully sovereign independent Fiji.
