# Fiji: Independence 1970 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Measles kills a third of Fijians in ____, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 1875

**2.** Step by step the Legislative Council gains Indian members (____), the membership system and the common-versus-communal-roll fight shape the 1963 Assembly and 1965 constitution, Alliance and Federation parties spar after 1966. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1916
