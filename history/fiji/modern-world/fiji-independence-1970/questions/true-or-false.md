# Fiji: Independence 1970 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Measles kills a third of Fijians in 1875, the Great Council of Chiefs and sugar mills follow, the capital moves Levuka to Suva, Ratu Sukuna and then Ratu Mara lead. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Moving Towards Independence: constitutional changes 1904-1970" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
