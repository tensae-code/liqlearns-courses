# Video script — Fiji: Multi-Ethnic Policy After Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Colonial policy kept "races" apart.

Now the part called "Ethnic and cultural policy development, 1970-2010". Colonial policy kept "races" apart; after 1970 governments proclaim a special role in managing ethnic affairs, the language shifts from multiracial to multi-ethnic, and by 2010 councils for multi-ethnic affairs and national reconciliation sit under the Prime Minister.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
