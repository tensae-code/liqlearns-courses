# Fiji: Multi-Ethnic Policy After Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ethnic and cultural policy development, 1970-2010" tell?
a) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
b) Colonial policy kept "races" apart
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) it is a story with no people and no places in it
**Answer: b) Colonial policy kept "races" apart** — That is what this lesson teaches.
