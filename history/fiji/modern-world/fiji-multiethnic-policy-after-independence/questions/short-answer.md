# Fiji: Multi-Ethnic Policy After Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ethnic and cultural policy development, 1970-2010" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Colonial policy kept "races" apart; after 1970 governments proclaim a special role in managing ethnic affairs, the language shifts from multiracial to multi-ethnic, and by 2010 councils for multi-ethnic affairs and national reconciliation sit under the Prime Minister.
