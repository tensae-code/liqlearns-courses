# Rotuma: Cession 1881 and War Service — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** To end the unrest Rotumans cede their island to Britain in 1881, Governor Gordon sending Lt Commander Bower to receive it (the cession instrument and Motusa memorial survive).
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Cession of Rotuma; Rotumans in the Second World War" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
