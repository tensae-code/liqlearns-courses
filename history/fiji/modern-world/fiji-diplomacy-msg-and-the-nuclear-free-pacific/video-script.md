# Video script — Fiji Diplomacy: The MSG and the Nuclear-Free Pacific

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. A small nation works the Melanesian Spearhead Group, SPREP and SOPAC, balances Rim nations against Europe, India and the Middle East.

Now the part called "Unit 1 Diplomacy and Fiji: Fiji in the international arena". A small nation works the Melanesian Spearhead Group, SPREP and SOPAC, balances Rim nations against Europe, India and the Middle East, and joins the Nuclear Free and Independent Pacific movement (8th conference report, 1999), while Japan courts Fiji's vote on whaling.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
