# Fiji: The Early Chinese Settlers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "The Early Chinese to Fiji" tell?
a) Moy Ba Ling, also called Houng Lee, sails from Australia in the 1850s, settles at Levuka, fetches relatives
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: a) Moy Ba Ling, also called Houng Lee, sails from Australia in the 1850s, settles at Levuka, fetches relatives** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "The Early Chinese to Fiji"?
a) Moy Ba Ling, also called Houng Lee, sails from Australia in the 1850s, settles at Levuka, fetches relatives  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Moy Ba Ling, also called Houng Lee, sails from Australia in the 1850s, settles at Levuka, fetches relatives** — That is what this lesson teaches.
