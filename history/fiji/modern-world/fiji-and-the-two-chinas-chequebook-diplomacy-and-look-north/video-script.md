# Video script — Fiji and the Two Chinas: Chequebook Diplomacy and Look North

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Fiji must choose between the PRC (founded as the communist state) and the ROC.

Now the part called "Unit 3 Fiji and China". Fiji must choose between the PRC (founded as the communist state) and the ROC; the first Chinese arrivals are recalled, trade and aid grow, the two Chinas compete for recognition with "cheque book diplomacy", and after 2006 Fiji adopts the Look North policy, sealed by the Prime Minister's 2013 visit to China.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
