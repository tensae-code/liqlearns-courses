# Rotuma: Settlement, Contact and Mission War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Legend and early settlers; discovery/contact; missionaries and religious war" tell?
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) Rotuma's strongest cultural invasions come from Samoa and Tonga in the Polynesian "backwash"
d) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
**Answer: c) Rotuma's strongest cultural invasions come from Samoa and Tonga in the Polynesian "backwash"** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Legend and early settlers; discovery/contact; missionaries and religious war"?
a) Rotuma's strongest cultural invasions come from Samoa and Tonga in the Polynesian "backwash"  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Rotuma's strongest cultural invasions come from Samoa and Tonga in the Polynesian "backwash"** — That is what this lesson teaches.
