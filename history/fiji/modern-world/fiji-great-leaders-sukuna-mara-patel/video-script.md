# Video script — Fiji's Great Leaders: Sukuna, Mara, Patel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Ratu Sukuna, first Fijian university graduate and Oxford lawyer, fights with the French Foreign Legion, founds the Native Land Trust Board and mentors independence leaders.

Now the part called "2.6 Local Leaders". Ratu Sukuna, first Fijian university graduate and Oxford lawyer, fights with the French Foreign Legion, founds the Native Land Trust Board and mentors independence leaders; Ratu Mara, founding father, is chief minister then first PM; A.D. Patel, farmers' barrister, founds the NFP preaching a fully integrated independent Fiji.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
