# Fiji's Great Leaders: Sukuna, Mara, Patel — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ratu Sukuna, first Fijian university graduate and Oxford lawyer, fights with the French Foreign Legion, founds the Native Land Trust Board and mentors independence leaders.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "2.6 Local Leaders" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
