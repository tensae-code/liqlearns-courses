# Games That Unite Nations: Olympics, Commonwealth, Pacific — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The modern Olympics revive Olympia's ancient games through Coubertin's IOC (1894).
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Olympic, Commonwealth and Pacific Games" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
