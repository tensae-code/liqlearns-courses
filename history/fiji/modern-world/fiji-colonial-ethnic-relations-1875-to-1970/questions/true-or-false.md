# Fiji: Colonial Ethnic Relations, 1875 to 1970 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** British resettlement policy and a European-dominated economy shape how Fijians, Indians and Europeans meet between 1875 and 1970.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Cultural interaction prior to independence: economy, settlement and the kailoma" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
