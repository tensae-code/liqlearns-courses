# Video script — Fiji: Colonial Ethnic Relations, 1875 to 1970

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. British resettlement policy and a European-dominated economy shape how Fijians, Indians and Europeans meet between 1875 and 1970.

Now the part called "Cultural interaction prior to independence: economy, settlement and the kailoma". British resettlement policy and a European-dominated economy shape how Fijians, Indians and Europeans meet between 1875 and 1970; between them sit the 7,800 kailoma (part-Europeans) counted in 1961, belonging fully to neither side.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.
