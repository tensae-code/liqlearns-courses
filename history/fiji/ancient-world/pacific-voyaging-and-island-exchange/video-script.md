# Video script — Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea once formed one exchange community, people regularly voyaging between islands circulating wealth, skills and goods; Austronesian speakers reach Vanuatu 4,000-6,000 years ago, Solomons settlers come from New Guinea, Polynesians settle Tuvalu about 1,000 years ago spreading from Southeast Asia via Taiwan and Melanesia; Tongan wanderers settle from Samoa to Efate.

Now the part called "The Ocean in our blood / the old exchange community". Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea once formed one exchange community, people regularly voyaging between islands circulating wealth, skills and goods.

Now the part called "Settlement histories of the Pacific islands (Fiji, Vanuatu, Solomon Islands, Samoa, Tonga, Tuvalu, Cook Islands)". Austronesian speakers reach Vanuatu 4,000-6,000 years ago, Solomons settlers come from New Guinea, Polynesians settle Tuvalu about 1,000 years ago spreading from Southeast Asia via Taiwan and Melanesia, and Fiji's Melanesian seafarers intermarry with Polynesians to become the iTaukei.

Now the part called "Movement and migration in the Pacific / the First Fijians". Tongan wanderers settle from Samoa to Efate; the first Fijians arrive after a brave, difficult expedition probably from the Solomon Islands, and later migrants follow from neighbouring islands including Rotuma.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
