# Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Ocean in our blood / the old exchange community
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea once formed one exchange community, people regularly voyaging between islands circulating wealth, skills and goods — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Settlement histories of the Pacific islands (Fiji, Vanuatu, Solomon Islands, Samoa, Tonga, Tuvalu, Cook Islands)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Austronesian speakers reach Vanuatu 4,000-6,000 years ago, Solomons settlers come from New Guinea, Polynesians settle Tuvalu about 1,000 years ago spreading from Southeast Asia via Taiwan and Melanesia, and Fiji's Melanesian seafarers intermarry with Polynesians to become the iTaukei — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Movement and migration in the Pacific / the First Fijians
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tongan wanderers settle from Samoa to Efate; the first Fijians arrive after a brave, difficult expedition probably from the Solomon Islands, and later migrants follow from neighbouring islands including Rotuma — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
