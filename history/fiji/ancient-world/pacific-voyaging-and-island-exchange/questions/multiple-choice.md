# Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "The Ocean in our blood / the old exchange community" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
b) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
c) Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea once formed one exchange community, people regularly voyaging between islands circulating wealth, skills and goods
d) it is a story with no people and no places in it
**Answer: c) Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea once formed one exchange community, people regularly voyaging between islands circulating wealth, skills and goods** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Settlement histories of the Pacific islands (Fiji, Vanuatu, Solomon Islands, Samoa, Tonga, Tuvalu, Cook Islands)"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Austronesian speakers reach Vanuatu 4,000-6,000 years ago, Solomons settlers come from New Guinea, Polynesians settle Tuvalu about 1,000 years ago spreading from Southeast Asia via Taiwan and Melanesia  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Austronesian speakers reach Vanuatu 4,000-6,000 years ago, Solomons settlers come from New Guinea, Polynesians settle Tuvalu about 1,000 years ago spreading from Southeast Asia via Taiwan and Melanesia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
