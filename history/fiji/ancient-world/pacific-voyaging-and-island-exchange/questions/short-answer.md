# Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "The Ocean in our blood / the old exchange community" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea once formed one exchange community, people regularly voyaging between islands circulating wealth, skills and goods.

**2.** What came of "Settlement histories of the Pacific islands (Fiji, Vanuatu, Solomon Islands, Samoa, Tonga, Tuvalu, Cook Islands)" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Austronesian speakers reach Vanuatu 4,000-6,000 years ago, Solomons settlers come from New Guinea, Polynesians settle Tuvalu about 1,000 years ago spreading from Southeast Asia via Taiwan and Melanesia, and Fiji's Melanesian seafarers intermarry with Polynesians to become the iTaukei.
