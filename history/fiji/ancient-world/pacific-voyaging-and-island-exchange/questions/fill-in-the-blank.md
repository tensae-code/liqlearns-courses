# Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea once formed one exchange community, people regularly voyaging between islands circulating wealth, skills and goods. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fiji

**2.** ____ speakers reach Vanuatu 4,000-6,000 years ago, Solomons settlers come from New Guinea, Polynesians settle Tuvalu about 1,000 years ago spreading from Southeast Asia via Taiwan and Melanesia. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Austronesian
