# Pacific Voyaging and Island Exchange — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea once formed one exchange community, people regularly voyaging between islands circulating wealth, skills and goods. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Settlement histories of the Pacific islands (Fiji, Vanuatu, Solomon Islands, Samoa, Tonga, Tuvalu, Cook Islands)" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
