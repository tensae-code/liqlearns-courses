# Fiji's First Settlement: iTaukei and the Drua — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** More than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "5.2.1 The Early Fijians — iTaukei" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
