# Fiji's First Settlement: iTaukei and the Drua — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "5.2.1 The Early Fijians — iTaukei" tell?
a) More than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes
b) it is a story with no people and no places in it
c) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
d) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
**Answer: a) More than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "5.2.1 The Early Fijians — iTaukei"?
a) More than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) More than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
