# Hands-on — Fiji's First Settlement: iTaukei and the Drua

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Do the opening act of lesson/lesson.md again, as it taught — and say the lesson's one thing aloud as you finish it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw this lesson's story as a line of pictures, one for each part in order: 5.2.1 The Early Fijians — iTaukei. Under each picture write one line: who is there, where it is, and what turned. Say each line aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's pattern in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see people, places or customs carrying a story from the past, note it: what you saw, and what this lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
