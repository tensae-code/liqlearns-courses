# Video script — Fiji's First Settlement: iTaukei and the Drua

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. More than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes.

Now the part called "5.2.1 The Early Fijians — iTaukei". More than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes; chief Lutunasobasoba landed at Vuda in the Kaunitoni, Degei's party settled by Nakauvadra, and people lived communally in bures planting food and making stone and wood tools.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
