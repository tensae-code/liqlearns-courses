# Lapita People and Pacific Settlement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Thousands of years ago Lapita voyagers from the Bismarck Archipelago carried a named pottery, farming and seafaring culture to New Caledonia, Fiji, Tonga and Samoa (sherds from about 1500 BC), ancestors of today's Melanesian, Micronesian and Polynesian peoples. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Chapter 2 Unit 1 History of Fiji: Pre-Contact Period / What was early Fiji like?" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Fiji's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
