# Lapita People and Pacific Settlement — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Thousands of years ago Lapita voyagers from the Bismarck Archipelago carried a named pottery, farming and seafaring culture to New Caledonia, Fiji, Tonga and Samoa (sherds from about ____ BC), ancestors of today's Melanesian, Micronesian and Polynesian peoples. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 1500

**2.** ____ pottery found at Lakeba, Beqa, Natunuku and Naigani shows the first Fijians shared a culture with the western Pacific. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lapita
