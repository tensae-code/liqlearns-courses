# Lapita People and Pacific Settlement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "1.1 Lapita Pottery and the three Pacific peoples" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Thousands of years ago Lapita voyagers from the Bismarck Archipelago carried a named pottery, farming and seafaring culture to New Caledonia, Fiji, Tonga and Samoa (sherds from about 1500 BC), ancestors of today's Melanesian, Micronesian and Polynesian peoples.

**2.** What came of "Chapter 2 Unit 1 History of Fiji: Pre-Contact Period / What was early Fiji like?" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Lapita pottery found at Lakeba, Beqa, Natunuku and Naigani shows the first Fijians shared a culture with the western Pacific; chiefs rule villages and islands, and daily life mixes farming, fishing, ceremony and the constant threat of war.
