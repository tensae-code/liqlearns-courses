# History — South Sudan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is South Sudan's own — built from South Sudan's Ministry of General Education and Instruction, Republic of South Sudan books (English). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': first steps at home, Africa's peoples and South Sudan's beginnings, kingdoms and the journey to independence, civilisations and colonisation, South Sudan in depth, revolutions and the world wars, and the world after 1945. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
