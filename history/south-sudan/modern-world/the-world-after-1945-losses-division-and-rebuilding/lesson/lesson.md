# The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

America passes through the inter-war years and emerges as a super power with present political and economic superiority. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
