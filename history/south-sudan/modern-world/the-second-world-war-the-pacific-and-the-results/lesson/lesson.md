# The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Second World War: The Pacific and the Results** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Europe counts political, economic and social losses, Africa counts its own impacts from both world wars, and pupils ask how destructive wars can be avoided. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Give one real example: Europe counts political, economic and social losses, Africa counts its own impacts from both world wars, and pupils ask how destructive wars can be avoided.
