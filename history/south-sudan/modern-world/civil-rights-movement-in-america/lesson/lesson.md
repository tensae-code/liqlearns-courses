# Civil Rights Movement in America

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Civil Rights Movement in America** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Civil Rights Movement, human rights and democracy are studied as America's contribution to global social development. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
