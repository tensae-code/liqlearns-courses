# The Great Depression, 1929

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Great Depression, 1929** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

America's economy collapses in the inter-war years and President Roosevelt rebuilds it through the New Deal; learners analyse the factors that led to recovery. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: America's economy collapses in the inter-war years and President Roosevelt rebuilds it through the New Deal; learners analyse the factors that led to recovery. Give one real example: America's economy collapses in the inter-war years and President Roosevelt rebuilds it through the New Deal; learners analyse the factors that led to recovery. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
