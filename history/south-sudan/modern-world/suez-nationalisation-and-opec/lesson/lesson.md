# Suez Nationalisation and OPEC

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Suez Nationalisation and OPEC** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Western powers clash with Egypt over the nationalised Suez Canal, and oil states organise in OPEC to control their own resource. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Western powers clash with Egypt over the nationalised Suez Canal, and oil states organise in OPEC to control their own resource. Give one real example: Western powers clash with Egypt over the nationalised Suez Canal, and oil states organise in OPEC to control their own resource. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Western powers clash with Egypt over the nationalised Suez Canal, and oil states organise in OPEC to control their own resource.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
