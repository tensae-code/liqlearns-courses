# Chinese Civil War and Communist Revolution

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Chinese Civil War and Communist Revolution** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Nationalists and Communists fight a generation-long civil war; the Communists win, revolution remakes China, and China rises as a world power. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Nationalists and Communists fight a generation-long civil war; the Communists win, revolution remakes China, and China rises as a world power. Give one real example: Nationalists and Communists fight a generation-long civil war; the Communists win, revolution remakes China, and China rises as a world power. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: compare your first map and line with the story you now know, and fix what was wrong.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Nationalists and Communists fight a generation-long civil war; the Communists win, revolution remakes China, and China rises as a world power.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
