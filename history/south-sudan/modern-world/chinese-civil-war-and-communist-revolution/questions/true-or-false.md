# Chinese Civil War and Communist Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Chinese Civil War (1927-1950), Communist Revolution and China as a world power”.
**Answer: False** — Nationalists and Communists fight a generation-long civil war; the Communists win, revolution remakes China, and China rises as a world power.

**2. ** Nationalists and Communists fight a generation-long civil war; the Communists win, revolution remakes China, and China rises as a world power.
**Answer: True**
