# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The French Revolution: causes and course”?
a) The three estates and a clergy grown lazy and worldly on rents and taxes feed the grievances that break into revolution in 1789.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
