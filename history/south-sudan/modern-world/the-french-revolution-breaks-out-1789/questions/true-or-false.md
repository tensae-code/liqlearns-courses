# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The French Revolution: causes and course”.
**Answer: False** — The three estates and a clergy grown lazy and worldly on rents and taxes feed the grievances that break into revolution in 1789.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The revolution's ideas and wars reshape Europe beyond France.
**Answer: True**
