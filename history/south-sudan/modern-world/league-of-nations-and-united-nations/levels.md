# League of Nations and United Nations — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The League of Nations
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: After the First World War the world builds the League of Nations to keep peace, and pupils judge its record. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The United Nations Organisation (UNO)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: After the Second World War the League's successor, the United Nations, takes up the promotion of peace. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — UN peacekeeping case studies
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Learners compare the League and the UN in aims and structure, then case-study UN peacekeeping in the Congo crisis, the Korean War and the Gulf War, and the UN's role in South Sudan or Africa. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
