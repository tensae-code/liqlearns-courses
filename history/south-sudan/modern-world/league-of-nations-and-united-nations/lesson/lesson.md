# League of Nations and United Nations

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **League of Nations and United Nations** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — The League of Nations

After the First World War the world builds the League of Nations to keep peace, and pupils judge its record. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — The United Nations Organisation (UNO)

After the Second World War the League's successor, the United Nations, takes up the promotion of peace. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 3 — UN peacekeeping case studies

Learners compare the League and the UN in aims and structure, then case-study UN peacekeeping in the Congo crisis, the Korean War and the Gulf War, and the UN's role in South Sudan or Africa. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
