# League of Nations and United Nations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The League of Nations”?
a) After the First World War the world builds the League of Nations to keep peace, and pupils judge its record.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) After the First World War the world builds the League of Nations to keep peace, and pupils judge its record.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “The United Nations Organisation (UNO)”?
a) After the Second World War the League's successor, the United Nations, takes up the promotion of peace.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) After the Second World War the League's successor, the United Nations, takes up the promotion of peace.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
