# League of Nations and United Nations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The United Nations Organisation (UNO)”?
