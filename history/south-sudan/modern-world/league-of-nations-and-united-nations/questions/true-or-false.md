# League of Nations and United Nations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The League of Nations”.
**Answer: False** — After the First World War the world builds the League of Nations to keep peace, and pupils judge its record.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** After the Second World War the League's successor, the United Nations, takes up the promotion of peace.
**Answer: True**
