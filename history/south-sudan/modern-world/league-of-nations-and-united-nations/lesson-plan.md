## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The League of Nations** — After the First World War the world builds the League of Nations to keep peace, and pupils judge its record.
2. **The United Nations Organisation (UNO)** — After the Second World War the League's successor, the United Nations, takes up the promotion of peace.
3. **UN peacekeeping case studies** — Learners compare the League and the UN in aims and structure, then case-study UN peacekeeping in the Congo crisis, the Korean War and the Gulf War, and the UN's role in South Sudan or Africa.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
