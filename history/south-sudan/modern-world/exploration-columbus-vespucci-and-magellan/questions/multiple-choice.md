Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Colonialism in America”?
a) Columbus sails west for the Far East in 1492 and lands in a New World; Spain takes the largest share, France and Portugal follow, Britain's United States wins independence in 1776, and plantation slavery reshapes both continents.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Columbus sails west for the Far East in 1492 and lands in a New World; Spain takes the largest share, France and Portugal follow, Britain's United States wins independence in 1776, and plantation slavery reshapes both continents.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
