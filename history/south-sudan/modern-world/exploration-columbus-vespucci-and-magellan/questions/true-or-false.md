# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Colonialism in America”.
**Answer: False** — Columbus sails west for the Far East in 1492 and lands in a New World; Spain takes the largest share, France and Portugal follow, Britain's United States wins independence in 1776, and plantation slavery reshapes both continents.

**2. ** Columbus sails west for the Far East in 1492 and lands in a New World; Spain takes the largest share, France and Portugal follow, Britain's United States wins independence in 1776, and plantation slavery reshapes both continents.
