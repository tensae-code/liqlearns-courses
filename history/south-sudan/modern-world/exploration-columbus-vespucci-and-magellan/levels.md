# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Columbus sails west for the Far East in 1492 and lands in a New World; Spain takes the largest share, France and Portugal follow, Britain's United States wins independence in 1776, and plantation slavery reshapes both continents. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
