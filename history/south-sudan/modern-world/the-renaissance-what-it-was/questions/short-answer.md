# The Renaissance: What It Was — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “The printing press”?
**Answer:** A machine to print books and newspapers, invented by Johannes Gutenberg (printed "Gutinburn") in 1440, puts written words into many hands.
