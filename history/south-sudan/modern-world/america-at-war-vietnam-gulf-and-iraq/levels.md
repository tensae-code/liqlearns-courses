# America at War: Vietnam, Gulf and Iraq — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — After the Second World War the USA rises as global super-power, is compared and contrasted with the USSR, and its roles in the Vietnam, Gulf and Iraq wars are investigated. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
