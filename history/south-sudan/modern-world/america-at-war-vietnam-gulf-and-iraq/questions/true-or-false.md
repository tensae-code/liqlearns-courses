# America at War: Vietnam, Gulf and Iraq — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Rise of USA as super power and its wars”.
**Answer: False** — After the Second World War the USA rises as global super-power, is compared and contrasted with the USSR, and its roles in the Vietnam, Gulf and Iraq wars are investigated.

**2. ** After the Second World War the USA rises as global super-power, is compared and contrasted with the USSR, and its roles in the Vietnam, Gulf and Iraq wars are investigated.
**Answer: True**
