# Lesson plan — Exploration: Causes and Results

**Course:** History — South Sudan · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Exploration: Causes and Results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Civilisations and Colonisation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Migrants move for labour, land, religious freedom and better lives; in the 18th and 19th centuries many were taken to the Americas as slaves for plantations, farms and homes.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Civilisations and Colonisation (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Migration in South and North America** — Migrants move for labour, land, religious freedom and better lives; in the 18th and 19th centuries many were taken to the Americas as slaves for plantations, farms and homes.
2. **Colonisation of South and Central America by the Spanish and Portuguese** — Spain and Portugal colonise South and Central America, planting the rule that independence movements later rise against.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
