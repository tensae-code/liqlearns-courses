# Exploration: Causes and Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Migration in South and North America”.
**Answer: False** — Migrants move for labour, land, religious freedom and better lives; in the 18th and 19th centuries many were taken to the Americas as slaves for plantations, farms and homes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Spain and Portugal colonise South and Central America, planting the rule that independence movements later rise against.
**Answer: True**
