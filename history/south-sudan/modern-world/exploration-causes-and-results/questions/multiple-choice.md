# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Migration in South and North America”?
a) Migrants move for labour, land, religious freedom and better lives; in the 18th and 19th centuries many were taken to the Americas as slaves for plantations, farms and homes.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Migrants move for labour, land, religious freedom and better lives; in the 18th and 19th centuries many were taken to the Americas as slaves for plantations, farms and homes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Colonisation of South and Central America by the Spanish and Portuguese”?
a) Spain and Portugal colonise South and Central America, planting the rule that independence movements later rise against.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Spain and Portugal colonise South and Central America, planting the rule that independence movements later rise against.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
