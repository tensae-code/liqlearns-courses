# Exploration: Causes and Results

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Exploration: Causes and Results** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Migration in South and North America

Migrants move for labour, land, religious freedom and better lives; in the 18th and 19th centuries many were taken to the Americas as slaves for plantations, farms and homes. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colonisation of South and Central America by the Spanish and Portuguese

Spain and Portugal colonise South and Central America, planting the rule that independence movements later rise against.
