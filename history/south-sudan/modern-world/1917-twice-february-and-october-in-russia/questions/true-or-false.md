# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “History of Russia before the Revolution and causes of the Russian Revolution”.
**Answer: False** — Old Russia's order produces the grievances that explode in 1917.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** February and October turn Russia over; Lenin leads the revolution that remakes the state.
**Answer: True**
