# Middle East: Great Powers and Arab Nationalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Great Powers and the Middle East after World War 1, and in World War 2”.
**Answer: False** — The Great Powers carve up the post-Ottoman Middle East after 1918, fight over it again in the Second World War, and European imperialism's end leaves the Middle East conflict.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Arab nationalism rises against foreign tutelage and becomes the region's driving political force.
**Answer: True**
