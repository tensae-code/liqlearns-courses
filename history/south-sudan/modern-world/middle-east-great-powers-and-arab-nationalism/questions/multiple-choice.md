Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Great Powers and the Middle East after World War 1, and in World War 2”?
a) The Great Powers carve up the post-Ottoman Middle East after 1918, fight over it again in the Second World War, and European imperialism's end leaves the Middle East conflict.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) The Great Powers carve up the post-Ottoman Middle East after 1918, fight over it again in the Second World War, and European imperialism's end leaves the Middle East conflict.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
