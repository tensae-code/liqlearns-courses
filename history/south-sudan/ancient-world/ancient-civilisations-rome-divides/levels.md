# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Barbarians turn Roman tactics learned in Roman service against Rome, senate and emperor feud, famine and plague strike, morals decline, cheap slave labour leaves plebs unemployed, and the empire grows too fast to defend. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
