a) Barbarians turn Roman tactics learned in Roman service against Rome, senate and emperor feud, famine and plague strike, morals decline, cheap slave labour leaves plebs unemployed, and the empire grows too fast to defend.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Barbarians turn Roman tactics learned in Roman service against Rome, senate and emperor feud, famine and plague strike, morals decline, cheap slave labour leaves plebs unemployed, and the empire grows too fast to defend.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
