# Ancient Civilisations: Rome

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Civilisations: Rome** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Rome under autocratic emperors becomes the world's most powerful economic, cultural, political and military force, a network of self-ruling towns in provinces under governors, held by the best army of its time. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
