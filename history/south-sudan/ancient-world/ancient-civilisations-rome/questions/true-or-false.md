# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Roman Empire”.
**Answer: False** — Rome under autocratic emperors becomes the world's most powerful economic, cultural, political and military force, a network of self-ruling towns in provinces under governors, held by the best army of its time.

**2. ** Rome under autocratic emperors becomes the world's most powerful economic, cultural, political and military force, a network of self-ruling towns in provinces under governors, held by the best army of its time.
