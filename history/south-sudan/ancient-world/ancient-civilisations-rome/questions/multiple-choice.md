**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Roman Empire”?
a) Rome under autocratic emperors becomes the world's most powerful economic, cultural, political and military force, a network of self-ruling towns in provinces under governors, held by the best army of its time.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Rome under autocratic emperors becomes the world's most powerful economic, cultural, political and military force, a network of self-ruling towns in provinces under governors, held by the best army of its time.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
