# The Maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Maya civilisation”?
**Answer:** The Maya of Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, Honduras and El Salvador build temple cities with the Americas' only fully developed writing, a mathematics calendar and astronomy, then decline amid deforestation, drought, famine and wars over resources as printed.
