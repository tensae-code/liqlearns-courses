# The Maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Maya civilisation”?
a) The Maya of Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, Honduras and El Salvador build temple cities with the Americas' only fully developed writing, a mathematics calendar and astronomy, then decline amid deforestation, drought, famine and wars over resources as printed.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) The Maya of Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, Honduras and El Salvador build temple cities with the Americas' only fully developed writing, a mathematics calendar and astronomy, then decline amid deforestation, drought, famine and wars over resources as printed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
