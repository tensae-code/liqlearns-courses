# The Maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Maya civilisation”.
**Answer: False** — The Maya of Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, Honduras and El Salvador build temple cities with the Americas' only fully developed writing, a mathematics calendar and astronomy, then decline amid deforestation, drought, famine and wars over resources as printed.

**2. ** The Maya of Mexico, Guatemala, Belize, Honduras and El Salvador build temple cities with the Americas' only fully developed writing, a mathematics calendar and astronomy, then decline amid deforestation, drought, famine and wars over resources as printed.
**Answer: True**
