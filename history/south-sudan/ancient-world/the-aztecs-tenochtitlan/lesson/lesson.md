# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

The Meshika, northern hunter-gatherers turned Tenochca of Tenochtitlan, fall when smallpox, Cortes's tactics with native allies, human sacrifice and the belief that Spaniards were gods combine against them. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
