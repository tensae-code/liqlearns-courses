# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “Aztec civilisation”?
a) The Meshika, northern hunter-gatherers turned Tenochca of Tenochtitlan, fall when smallpox, Cortes's tactics with native allies, human sacrifice and the belief that Spaniards were gods combine against them.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) The Meshika, northern hunter-gatherers turned Tenochca of Tenochtitlan, fall when smallpox, Cortes's tactics with native allies, human sacrifice and the belief that Spaniards were gods combine against them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
