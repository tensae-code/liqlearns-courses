# Why Civilisations Rise and Fall — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Reasons for the decline of civilisation”.
**Answer: False** — Civilisations fall from outside (war, natural disaster) and inside (drought, disease, economic disruption, political struggle, overpopulation), and pupils test Greece, Rome and Egypt against the list.

**2. ** Civilisations fall from outside (war, natural disaster) and inside (drought, disease, economic disruption, political struggle, overpopulation), and pupils test Greece, Rome and Egypt against the list.
