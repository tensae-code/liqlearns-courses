# What the Slave Trade Cost Africa — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Slavery persists in the 21st century — Ayak Piol Mabior's testimony (plot summary: abducted as a child in a government-backed raid, enslaved in Darfur, she escapes and is repatriated in February 2014) is compared with 17th-century slavery for motive, victims and payment. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
