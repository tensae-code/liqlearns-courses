**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Collapse of Turko-Egyptian rule and Sudanese response — b)

Meanings:
a) Muhammad Ali invades in 1820 with cannon to build a slave army and seize slaves, gold, ivory and timber; about 30,000 Sudanese are conscripted in 1820-21 and many die on the road to Aswan; Khartoum is founded, Sudan is taxed through provinces, and the south supplies the slaves.
