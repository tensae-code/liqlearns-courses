# South Sudan: Turkiyya and Mahdiyya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Turko-Egyptian rule in Sudan; Collapse of Turko-Egyptian rule and Sudanese response; Mahdist Revolution (1881-1889); Mahdist administration and decline.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Collapse of Turko-Egyptian rule and Sudanese response”?
**Answer:** Weak successors, debt, anti-slavery campaigns that anger merchant traders, ivory violence and heavy taxes hollow out the Turkiyya, while South Sudanese resist forts and garrisons until disease and defection force their abandonment.
