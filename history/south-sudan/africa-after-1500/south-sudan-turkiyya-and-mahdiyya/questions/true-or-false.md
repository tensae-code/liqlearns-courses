# South Sudan: Turkiyya and Mahdiyya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Turko-Egyptian rule in Sudan”.
**Answer: False** — Muhammad Ali invades in 1820 with cannon to build a slave army and seize slaves, gold, ivory and timber; about 30,000 Sudanese are conscripted in 1820-21 and many die on the road to Aswan; Khartoum is founded, Sudan is taxed through provinces, and the south supplies the slaves.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Weak successors, debt, anti-slavery campaigns that anger merchant traders, ivory violence and heavy taxes hollow out the Turkiyya, while South Sudanese resist forts and garrisons until disease and defection force their abandonment.
**Answer: True**
