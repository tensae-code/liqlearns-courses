## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Origin of national symbols** — The anthem praises martyrs whose blood cemented the nation, and the flag's white stripes mark the 1972 Addis Ababa and 2005 Naivasha peace, red the blood, blue the Nile, yellow the guiding star, green the land.
2. **Importance of national symbols on the daily lives of South Sudan people** — Symbols promote unity and peace, and only in peace do roads and trade develop and people work together.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
