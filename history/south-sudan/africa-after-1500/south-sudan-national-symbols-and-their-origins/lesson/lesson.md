## Sub lesson 1 — Origin of national symbols

The anthem praises martyrs whose blood cemented the nation, and the flag's white stripes mark the 1972 Addis Ababa and 2005 Naivasha peace, red the blood, blue the Nile, yellow the guiding star, green the land. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns.
