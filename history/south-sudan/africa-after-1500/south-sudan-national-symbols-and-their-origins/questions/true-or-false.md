# South Sudan's National Symbols and Their Origins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Origin of national symbols”.
**Answer: False** — The anthem praises martyrs whose blood cemented the nation, and the flag's white stripes mark the 1972 Addis Ababa and 2005 Naivasha peace, red the blood, blue the Nile, yellow the guiding star, green the land.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Symbols promote unity and peace, and only in peace do roads and trade develop and people work together.
**Answer: True**
