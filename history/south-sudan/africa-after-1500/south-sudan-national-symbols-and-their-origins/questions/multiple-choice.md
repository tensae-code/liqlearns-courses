**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Origin of national symbols”?
a) The anthem praises martyrs whose blood cemented the nation, and the flag's white stripes mark the 1972 Addis Ababa and 2005 Naivasha peace, red the blood, blue the Nile, yellow the guiding star, green the land.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
