# Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Benin City's straight streets and splendid palace sit inside the Great Wall of Benin — over 16,000 km of walls and ditches built by the Edo between 800 and 1400 CE, partly destroyed by the British army in 1897. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
