# Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “The Benin Empire”?
**Answer:** Benin City's straight streets and splendid palace sit inside the Great Wall of Benin — over 16,000 km of walls and ditches built by the Edo between 800 and 1400 CE, partly destroyed by the British army in 1897.
