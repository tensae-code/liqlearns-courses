# Video script — Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Benin City's straight streets and splendid palace sit inside the Great Wall of Benin — over 16,000 km of walls and ditches built by the Edo between 800 and 1400 CE, partly destroyed by the British army in 1897. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in South Sudan.
