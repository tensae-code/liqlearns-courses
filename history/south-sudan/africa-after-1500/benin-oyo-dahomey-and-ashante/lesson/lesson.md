# Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Benin City's straight streets and splendid palace sit inside the Great Wall of Benin — over 16,000 km of walls and ditches built by the Edo between 800 and 1400 CE, partly destroyed by the British army in 1897. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
