# Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Traders and their companies”?
**Answer:** European traders exchange glassware, wine, guns and silk for ivory, spices, slaves and rhino horns, then form companies that administer and tax — William Mackinnon's Imperial British East Africa Company and Carl Peters's German East Africa Company.
