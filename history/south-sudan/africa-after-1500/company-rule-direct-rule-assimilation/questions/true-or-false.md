# Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Traders and their companies”.
**Answer: False** — European traders exchange glassware, wine, guns and silk for ivory, spices, slaves and rhino horns, then form companies that administer and tax — William Mackinnon's Imperial British East Africa Company and Carl Peters's German East Africa Company.

**2. ** European traders exchange glassware, wine, guns and silk for ivory, spices, slaves and rhino horns, then form companies that administer and tax — William Mackinnon's Imperial British East Africa Company and Carl Peters's German East Africa Company.
**Answer: True**
