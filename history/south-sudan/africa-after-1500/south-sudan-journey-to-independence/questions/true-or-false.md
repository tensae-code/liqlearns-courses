# South Sudan's Journey to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Change in our country”.
**Answer: False** — Marginalised and exploited by the north, the south needs change, and the biggest change is secession — South Sudan breaks from Sudan and becomes independent on 9 July 2011.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** 1947 Juba Conference demands separation and is rejected; 1955 Torit Mutiny opens the Anya-nya 1 war under Joseph Lagu; 1972 Addis Ababa gives semi-autonomy; 1983 Nimeiri tears it up and John Garang's SPLM/A fights to the 2005 Naivasha peace; a 99% referendum vote leads to independence in 2011 with Salva Kiir as first president.
**Answer: True**
