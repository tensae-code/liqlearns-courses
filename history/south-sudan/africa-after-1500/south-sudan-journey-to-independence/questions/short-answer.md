# South Sudan's Journey to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Change in our country; Journey to independence for South Sudan; Comparison between change in South Sudan and change in South Africa; South Sudan in the 20th century: separation policy; The Juba Conference of 1947; Anya-Nya Movement (1955-1972); Addis-Ababa Agreement; The SPLM/A struggle for independence (1983-2005); Development in Sudan since 1965 and in South Sudan since 2011; Relating the Juba Conference of 1947 to the 2005 CPA.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Journey to independence for South Sudan”?
**Answer:** 1947 Juba Conference demands separation and is rejected; 1955 Torit Mutiny opens the Anya-nya 1 war under Joseph Lagu; 1972 Addis Ababa gives semi-autonomy; 1983 Nimeiri tears it up and John Garang's SPLM/A fights to the 2005 Naivasha peace; a 99% referendum vote leads to independence in 2011 with Salva Kiir as first president.
