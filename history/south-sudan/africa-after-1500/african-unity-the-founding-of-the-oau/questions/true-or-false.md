# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Organisation of African Unity (OAU)”.
**Answer: False** — African states found the OAU, sign its charter and set objectives of unity, sovereignty and liberation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Africa's states found the OAU to unite the continent and complete its liberation.
**Answer: True**
