# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Organisation of African Unity (OAU)”?
a) African states found the OAU, sign its charter and set objectives of unity, sovereignty and liberation.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) African states found the OAU, sign its charter and set objectives of unity, sovereignty and liberation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Organisation of African Unity”?
a) Africa's states found the OAU to unite the continent and complete its liberation.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Africa's states found the OAU to unite the continent and complete its liberation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
