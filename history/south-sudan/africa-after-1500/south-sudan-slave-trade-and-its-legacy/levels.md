# South Sudan: Slave Trade and Its Legacy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — From the 652 AD Baqt, under which Christian Nubia traded 360 slaves a year, through Funj slave armies and Muhammad Ali's raids described by Samuel Baker, to Simon Deng, abducted at nine, carried up the Nile, who escapes, regains his Shilluk scars and becomes an abolitionist. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
