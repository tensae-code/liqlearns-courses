# South Sudan: Slave Trade and Its Legacy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Slave trade in South Sudan”.
**Answer: False** — From the 652 AD Baqt, under which Christian Nubia traded 360 slaves a year, through Funj slave armies and Muhammad Ali's raids described by Samuel Baker, to Simon Deng, abducted at nine, carried up the Nile, who escapes, regains his Shilluk scars and becomes an abolitionist.

**2. ** From the 652 AD Baqt, under which Christian Nubia traded 360 slaves a year, through Funj slave armies and Muhammad Ali's raids described by Samuel Baker, to Simon Deng, abducted at nine, carried up the Nile, who escapes, regains his Shilluk scars and becomes an abolitionist.
