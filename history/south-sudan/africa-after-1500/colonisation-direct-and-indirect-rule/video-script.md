# Video script — Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Lewanika, who won Lozi independence from the Kololo in 1864, collaborates with the British from fear of Germans, Portuguese and Ndebele and hope of guns and mission education — yet Barotseland is still alienated and his people made landless. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Colonial systems of administration are compared, and pupils explain why a few African countries were never colonised. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in South Sudan.
