## Sub lesson 1 — Collaboration: Lewanika of the Lozi

Lewanika, who won Lozi independence from the Kololo in 1864, collaborates with the British from fear of Germans, Portuguese and Ndebele and hope of guns and mission education — yet Barotseland is still alienated and his people made landless. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
