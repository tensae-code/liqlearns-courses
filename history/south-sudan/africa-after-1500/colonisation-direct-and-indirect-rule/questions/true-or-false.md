# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Collaboration: Lewanika of the Lozi”.
**Answer: False** — Lewanika, who won Lozi independence from the Kololo in 1864, collaborates with the British from fear of Germans, Portuguese and Ndebele and hope of guns and mission education — yet Barotseland is still alienated and his people made landless.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Colonial systems of administration are compared, and pupils explain why a few African countries were never colonised.
**Answer: True**
