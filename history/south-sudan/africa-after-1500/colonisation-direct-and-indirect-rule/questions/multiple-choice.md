Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Collaboration: Lewanika of the Lozi”?
a) Lewanika, who won Lozi independence from the Kololo in 1864, collaborates with the British from fear of Germans, Portuguese and Ndebele and hope of guns and mission education — yet Barotseland is still alienated and his people made landless.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Lewanika, who won Lozi independence from the Kololo in 1864, collaborates with the British from fear of Germans, Portuguese and Ndebele and hope of guns and mission education — yet Barotseland is still alienated and his people made landless.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
