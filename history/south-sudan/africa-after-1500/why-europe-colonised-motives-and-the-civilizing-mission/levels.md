# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilizing Mission — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Social (end the slave trade, spread civilisation and Christianity, settle excess population, bring medicine), political (France compensating for Alsace-Lorraine, status and prestige), economic (raw materials, markets, capital) and strategic (Suez Canal, the Nile, the Indian Ocean). — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
