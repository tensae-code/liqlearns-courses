# After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “African Union, IGAD, ECOWAS, EAC and COMESA”.
**Answer: False** — The OAU becomes the African Union, and regional bodies — IGAD, ECOWAS, the new East African Community and COMESA — carry integration forward, the blocs South Sudan itself would join.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** African countries develop after the flag while conflicts recur; pupils trace the causes of Africa's conflicts.
**Answer: True**
