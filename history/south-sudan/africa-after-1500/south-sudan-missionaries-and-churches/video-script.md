# Video script — South Sudan: Missionaries and Churches

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Comboni Missionaries founded by Daniel Comboni at Verona, the Anglican Church Missionary Society and others enter South Sudan to convert, teach and heal, face disease and resistance, and leave schools and churches whose problems and positive impact pupils weigh. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in South Sudan.
