# South Sudan: Missionaries and Churches — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Comboni Missionaries founded by Daniel Comboni at Verona, the Anglican Church Missionary Society and others enter South Sudan to convert, teach and heal, face disease and resistance, and leave schools and churches whose problems and positive impact pupils weigh. — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
