# Lesson plan — Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa

**Course:** History — South Sudan · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Civilisations and Colonisation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Africans lose fertile land to settlers and are forced into reserves with pass cards, new boundaries split the Anyuak, Ma'di, Acholi and Kakwa across countries, chiefs become petty officials, cottage industries decline, and money, elites and towns like Juba and Nairobi emerge.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Civilisations and Colonisation (recommended, never forced).
