# Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Impact of the coming of Europeans”.
**Answer: False** — Africans lose fertile land to settlers and are forced into reserves with pass cards, new boundaries split the Anyuak, Ma'di, Acholi and Kakwa across countries, chiefs become petty officials, cottage industries decline, and money, elites and towns like Juba and Nairobi emerge.
