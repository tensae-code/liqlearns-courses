# Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Impact of the coming of Europeans”?
**Answer:** Africans lose fertile land to settlers and are forced into reserves with pass cards, new boundaries split the Anyuak, Ma'di, Acholi and Kakwa across countries, chiefs become petty officials, cottage industries decline, and money, elites and towns like Juba and Nairobi emerge.
