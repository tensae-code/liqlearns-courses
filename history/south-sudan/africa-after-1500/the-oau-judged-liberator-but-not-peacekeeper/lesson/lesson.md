# The OAU Judged: Liberator but Not Peacekeeper

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The OAU Judged: Liberator but Not Peacekeeper** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Groups study key contributors to the OAU, present its contributions to Africa's social, political and economic development, and outline its achievements and failures. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
