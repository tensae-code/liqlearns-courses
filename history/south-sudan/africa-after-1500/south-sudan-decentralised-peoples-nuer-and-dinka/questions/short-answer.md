# South Sudan's Decentralised Peoples: Nuer and Dinka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “The Dinka”?
**Answer:** South Sudan's largest people, the Jieng, are decentralised pastoralists led by ritual chiefs, masters of the fishing spear; boys are scarified into warrior age-sets, name themselves after their cattle's colours, wrestle, and trade cattle, grain and ivory with the Nuer.
