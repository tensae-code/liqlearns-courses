# South Sudan's Decentralised Peoples: Nuer and Dinka — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “The Nuer”.
**Answer: False** — A decentralised people of tribes and lineages whose leopard-skin religious chiefs settle disputes; cattle are wealth and prestige, raiding the Dinka is a duty blessed by women's rituals, and Kwoth is honoured through spirits, ghost marriages and marriages completed only at the third child.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** South Sudan's largest people, the Jieng, are decentralised pastoralists led by ritual chiefs, masters of the fishing spear; boys are scarified into warrior age-sets, name themselves after their cattle's colours, wrestle, and trade cattle, grain and ivory with the Nuer.
**Answer: True**
