# South Sudan's Decentralised Peoples: Nuer and Dinka — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The Nuer — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Dinka — b)

Meanings:
a) A decentralised people of tribes and lineages whose leopard-skin religious chiefs settle disputes; cattle are wealth and prestige, raiding the Dinka is a duty blessed by women's rituals, and Kwoth is honoured through spirits, ghost marriages and marriages completed only at the third child.
b) South Sudan's largest people, the Jieng, are decentralised pastoralists led by ritual chiefs, masters of the fishing spear; boys are scarified into warrior age-sets, name themselves after their cattle's colours, wrestle, and trade cattle, grain and ivory with the Nuer.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
