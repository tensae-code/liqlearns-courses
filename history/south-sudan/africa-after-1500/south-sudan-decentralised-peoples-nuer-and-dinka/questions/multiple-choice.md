# South Sudan's Decentralised Peoples: Nuer and Dinka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “The Nuer”?
a) A decentralised people of tribes and lineages whose leopard-skin religious chiefs settle disputes; cattle are wealth and prestige, raiding the Dinka is a duty blessed by women's rituals, and Kwoth is honoured through spirits, ghost marriages and marriages completed only at the third child.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) A decentralised people of tribes and lineages whose leopard-skin religious chiefs settle disputes; cattle are wealth and prestige, raiding the Dinka is a duty blessed by women's rituals, and Kwoth is honoured through spirits, ghost marriages and marriages completed only at the third child.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
