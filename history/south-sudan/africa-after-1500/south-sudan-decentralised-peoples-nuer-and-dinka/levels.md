## Sub lesson 2 — The Dinka
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: South Sudan's largest people, the Jieng, are decentralised pastoralists led by ritual chiefs, masters of the fishing spear; boys are scarified into warrior age-sets, name themselves after their cattle's colours, wrestle, and trade cattle, grain and ivory with the Nuer. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
