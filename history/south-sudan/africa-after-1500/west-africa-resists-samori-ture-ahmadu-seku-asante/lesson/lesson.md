## Sub lesson 1 — Resistance: Samori Touré of the Mandika

Trader-turned-soldier Samori unites the Mandika, fights France from 1891 with a standing army, modern weapons, a local gun factory and scorched earth, and holds out seven years before capture in 1898 and exile in Gabon, where he dies in 1900. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
