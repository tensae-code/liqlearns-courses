Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Resistance: Samori Touré of the Mandika — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** African response to colonial rule — b)

Meanings:
a) Trader-turned-soldier Samori unites the Mandika, fights France from 1891 with a standing army, modern weapons, a local gun factory and scorched earth, and holds out seven years before capture in 1898 and exile in Gabon, where he dies in 1900.
b) Africans answer colonisation two ways — collaboration and resistance — and pupils weigh the reasons leaders chose each path.
