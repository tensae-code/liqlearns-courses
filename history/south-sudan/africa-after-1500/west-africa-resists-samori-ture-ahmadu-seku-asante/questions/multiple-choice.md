Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Resistance: Samori Touré of the Mandika”?
a) Trader-turned-soldier Samori unites the Mandika, fights France from 1891 with a standing army, modern weapons, a local gun factory and scorched earth, and holds out seven years before capture in 1898 and exile in Gabon, where he dies in 1900.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Trader-turned-soldier Samori unites the Mandika, fights France from 1891 with a standing army, modern weapons, a local gun factory and scorched earth, and holds out seven years before capture in 1898 and exile in Gabon, where he dies in 1900.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
