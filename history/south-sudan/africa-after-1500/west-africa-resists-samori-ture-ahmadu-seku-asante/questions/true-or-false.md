# West Africa Resists: Samori Touré, Ahmadu Seku, Asante — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Resistance: Samori Touré of the Mandika”.
**Answer: False** — Trader-turned-soldier Samori unites the Mandika, fights France from 1891 with a standing army, modern weapons, a local gun factory and scorched earth, and holds out seven years before capture in 1898 and exile in Gabon, where he dies in 1900.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Africans answer colonisation two ways — collaboration and resistance — and pupils weigh the reasons leaders chose each path.
**Answer: True**
