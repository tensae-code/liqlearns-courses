# Lesson plan — West Africa Resists: Samori Touré, Ahmadu Seku, Asante

**Course:** History — South Sudan · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** West Africa Resists: Samori Touré, Ahmadu Seku, Asante
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Civilisations and Colonisation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Trader-turned-soldier Samori unites the Mandika, fights France from 1891 with a standing army, modern weapons, a local gun factory and scorched earth, and holds out seven years before capture in 1898 and exile in Gabon, where he dies in 1900.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Civilisations and Colonisation (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Resistance: Samori Touré of the Mandika** — Trader-turned-soldier Samori unites the Mandika, fights France from 1891 with a standing army, modern weapons, a local gun factory and scorched earth, and holds out seven years before capture in 1898 and exile in Gabon, where he dies in 1900.
2. **African response to colonial rule** — Africans answer colonisation two ways — collaboration and resistance — and pupils weigh the reasons leaders chose each path.
