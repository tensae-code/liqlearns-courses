# South Sudan Community Origins and Traditions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Origin of a community”.
**Answer: False** — The Dinka sing that they came from the black mud of the Nile and their god Nhialic, and every child asks parents how their own community started.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Rituals that cleanse a person or society, ceremonies and festivals are passed generation to generation as the community's living tradition.
**Answer: True**
