# South Sudan Community Origins and Traditions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Origin of a community”?
a) The Dinka sing that they came from the black mud of the Nile and their god Nhialic, and every child asks parents how their own community started.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) The Dinka sing that they came from the black mud of the Nile and their god Nhialic, and every child asks parents how their own community started.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
