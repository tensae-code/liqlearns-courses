# South Sudan Community Origins and Traditions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Origin of a community; Community rituals, ceremonies and festivals; Common practices of African communities in the past; Relationship between ceremonies today and those from the past; Stories of origin; How stories of origin have changed our lives today.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Community rituals, ceremonies and festivals”?
**Answer:** Rituals that cleanse a person or society, ceremonies and festivals are passed generation to generation as the community's living tradition.
