## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Origin of a community** — The Dinka sing that they came from the black mud of the Nile and their god Nhialic, and every child asks parents how their own community started.
2. **Community rituals, ceremonies and festivals** — Rituals that cleanse a person or society, ceremonies and festivals are passed generation to generation as the community's living tradition.
3. **Common practices of African communities in the past** — Children study pictures of traditional practices, name their community's name for God and the sacrifices offered, and list practices they still know.
4. **Relationship between ceremonies today and those from the past** — Today's ceremonies are matched against past ones so children see which traditions carried through and which changed.
5. **Stories of origin** — The teacher reads where Garang and Abuk came from, classes discuss Bible and Koran creation stories, and other peoples say they came from the moon, a superior being, or the mountains.
6. **How stories of origin have changed our lives today** — Children ask parents about other peoples in their state and their origin stories, and judge how those beginnings still shape life today.
