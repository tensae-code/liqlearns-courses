## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Jobs done by people in the past** — Hakim of the Dinka tells how his grandfather never went to school but herded cattle, tilled land and traded, and those jobs fed the whole community.
2. **How economic activities are related to the way people lived** — Farming, fishing and trade pictures are used to explain why people settled where they did and how work shaped daily life.
3. **The plough** — The plough arrives as a technology that changes how land is prepared, and children ask parents how land was prepared in the past.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
