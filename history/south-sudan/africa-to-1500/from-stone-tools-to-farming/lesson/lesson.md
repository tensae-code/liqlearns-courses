# From Stone Tools to Farming

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **From Stone Tools to Farming** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Jobs done by people in the past

Hakim of the Dinka tells how his grandfather never went to school but herded cattle, tilled land and traded, and those jobs fed the whole community. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
