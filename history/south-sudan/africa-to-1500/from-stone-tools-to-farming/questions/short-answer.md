# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Jobs done by people in the past; How economic activities are related to the way people lived; The plough.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “How economic activities are related to the way people lived”?
**Answer:** Farming, fishing and trade pictures are used to explain why people settled where they did and how work shaped daily life.
