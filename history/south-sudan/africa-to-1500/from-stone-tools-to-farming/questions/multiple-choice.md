# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Jobs done by people in the past”?
a) Hakim of the Dinka tells how his grandfather never went to school but herded cattle, tilled land and traded, and those jobs fed the whole community.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Hakim of the Dinka tells how his grandfather never went to school but herded cattle, tilled land and traded, and those jobs fed the whole community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
