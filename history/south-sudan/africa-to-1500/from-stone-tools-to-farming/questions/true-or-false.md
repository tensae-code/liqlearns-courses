# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Jobs done by people in the past”.
**Answer: False** — Hakim of the Dinka tells how his grandfather never went to school but herded cattle, tilled land and traded, and those jobs fed the whole community.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Farming, fishing and trade pictures are used to explain why people settled where they did and how work shaped daily life.
**Answer: True**
