## Sub lesson 1 — Factors that influenced the early settlement and migration to specific places in South Sudan

Water, land and drought push and pull people across South Sudan, and children judge where they would settle and why. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — People of South Sudan

The peoples came from different places — Plain Nilotes from the Ethiopian Highlands, River-Lake Nilotes from the Kush kingdom in northern Sudan, Bantu and Sudanic groups from West Africa — and children name Dinka, Nuer, Shilluk, Acholi, Azande, Bari and many more.
