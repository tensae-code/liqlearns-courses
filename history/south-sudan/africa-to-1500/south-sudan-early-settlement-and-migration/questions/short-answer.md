# South Sudan: Early Settlement and Migration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Factors that influenced the early settlement and migration to specific places in South Sudan; People of South Sudan.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “People of South Sudan”?
**Answer:** The peoples came from different places — Plain Nilotes from the Ethiopian Highlands, River-Lake Nilotes from the Kush kingdom in northern Sudan, Bantu and Sudanic groups from West Africa — and children name Dinka, Nuer, Shilluk, Acholi, Azande, Bari and many more.
