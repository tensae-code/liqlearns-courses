# South Sudan: Early Settlement and Migration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Factors that influenced the early settlement and migration to specific places in South Sudan”.
**Answer: False** — Water, land and drought push and pull people across South Sudan, and children judge where they would settle and why.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The peoples came from different places — Plain Nilotes from the Ethiopian Highlands, River-Lake Nilotes from the Kush kingdom in northern Sudan, Bantu and Sudanic groups from West Africa — and children name Dinka, Nuer, Shilluk, Acholi, Azande, Bari and many more.
**Answer: True**
