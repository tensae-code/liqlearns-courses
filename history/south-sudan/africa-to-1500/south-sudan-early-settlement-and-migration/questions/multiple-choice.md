# South Sudan: Early Settlement and Migration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Factors that influenced the early settlement and migration to specific places in South Sudan”?
a) Water, land and drought push and pull people across South Sudan, and children judge where they would settle and why.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
