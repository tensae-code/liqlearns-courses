# Video script — South Sudan: Early Settlement and Migration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Water, land and drought push and pull people across South Sudan, and children judge where they would settle and why. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

The peoples came from different places — Plain Nilotes from the Ethiopian Highlands, River-Lake Nilotes from the Kush kingdom in northern Sudan, Bantu and Sudanic groups from West Africa — and children name Dinka, Nuer, Shilluk, Acholi, Azande, Bari and many more. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in South Sudan.
