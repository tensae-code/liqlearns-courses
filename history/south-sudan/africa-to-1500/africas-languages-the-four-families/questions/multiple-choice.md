# Africa's Languages: The Four Families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the heart of the story in “People in our community”?
a) Africa's peoples are introduced as Nilotic, Nilo-Hamitic, Bantu, Kushite and Sudanic groups, and children sort what their community used in the past and still uses today.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Africa's peoples are introduced as Nilotic, Nilo-Hamitic, Bantu, Kushite and Sudanic groups, and children sort what their community used in the past and still uses today.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
