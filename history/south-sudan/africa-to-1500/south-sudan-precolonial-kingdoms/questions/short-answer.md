# South Sudan's Pre-Colonial Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Pre-Colonial Kingdoms; Features of kingdoms in South Sudan; The Kingdoms of South Sudan: Shilluk; The Shilluk Kingdom (1490-1865); The Azande Kingdom; The Anyuak (Anywaa) Kingdom.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Features of kingdoms in South Sudan”?
**Answer:** A king heads the kingdom, warriors defend it, a council of elders advises, subjects obey, and each kingdom keeps its own artwork and economic life.
