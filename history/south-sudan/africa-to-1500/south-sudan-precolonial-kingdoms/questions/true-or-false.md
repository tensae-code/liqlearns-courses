# South Sudan's Pre-Colonial Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Pre-Colonial Kingdoms”.
**Answer: False** — Before Europeans, South Sudan's main kingdoms were Azande, Otuho, Chollo (Shilluk) and Anyuak, while Moru-Madi, Luo and Atker had no kings but chiefs, clan heads and family heads.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** A king heads the kingdom, warriors defend it, a council of elders advises, subjects obey, and each kingdom keeps its own artwork and economic life.
**Answer: True**
