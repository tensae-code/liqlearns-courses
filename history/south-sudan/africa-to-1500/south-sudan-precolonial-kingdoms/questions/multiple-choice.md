# South Sudan's Pre-Colonial Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Pre-Colonial Kingdoms”?
a) Before Europeans, South Sudan's main kingdoms were Azande, Otuho, Chollo (Shilluk) and Anyuak, while Moru-Madi, Luo and Atker had no kings but chiefs, clan heads and family heads.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Before Europeans, South Sudan's main kingdoms were Azande, Otuho, Chollo (Shilluk) and Anyuak, while Moru-Madi, Luo and Atker had no kings but chiefs, clan heads and family heads.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Features of kingdoms in South Sudan”?
a) A king heads the kingdom, warriors defend it, a council of elders advises, subjects obey, and each kingdom keeps its own artwork and economic life.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) A king heads the kingdom, warriors defend it, a council of elders advises, subjects obey, and each kingdom keeps its own artwork and economic life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
