## Sub lesson 1 — Pre-Colonial Kingdoms

Before Europeans, South Sudan's main kingdoms were Azande, Otuho, Chollo (Shilluk) and Anyuak, while Moru-Madi, Luo and Atker had no kings but chiefs, clan heads and family heads. ## Sub lesson 4 — The Shilluk Kingdom (1490-1865)

## Sub lesson 5 — The Azande Kingdom

The Vungara dynasty's fierce warriors conquer much land; dead kings' sons fight for the seat and losers found new kingdoms east and north; the Zande work iron, farm millet and sorghum, and order justice through belief in mangu, witchcraft in the belly, as Evans-Pritchard recorded in 1937. ## Sub lesson 6 — The Anyuak (Anywaa) Kingdom

A federation of river villages along the Sobat, Baro and Akobo, each with its Nyiya king, feuds over the Ocwok royal throne and bead until the British make Agada Akway king of kings; his line reaches the 23rd king, Adongo Agada, and the people farm, fish, smith iron and pan gold for demuy beads.
