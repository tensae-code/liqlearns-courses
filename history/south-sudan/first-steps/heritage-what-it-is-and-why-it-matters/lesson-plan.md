## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Objects found at home** — Children sort traditional and modern objects at home and at school and learn the past survives in things people keep and share.
2. **Objects that were used long time ago and the ones used today** — Keji, Adek and Pita sort spears, skin dresses, pots and hoes of long ago against today's objects, and find some old things are still used.
3. **Events that were practiced long time ago and today** — Old events and ceremonies are compared with today's, so children see traditions surviving, changing or disappearing.
4. **Cultural features of the Kingdoms of South Sudan** — Azande kings' weapons, Anyuak royal regalia, the Asante royal stool and cloth, Meroe kings and a Kush stone incense burner are drawn and modelled as heritage children can still see.
5. **What past kingdoms left in present-day South Sudan** — Learners reflect on which characteristics of the old kingdoms are reflected in South Sudan now, and present them through drama and role play.
6. **Benefits of civilisation to modern society** — Civilisation leaves modern society architecture, political structures beyond tribal law, cultural exchange, technology from shared ideas, and safety in numbers.
