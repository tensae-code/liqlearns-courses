# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Objects found at home”.
**Answer: False** — Children sort traditional and modern objects at home and at school and learn the past survives in things people keep and share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Keji, Adek and Pita sort spears, skin dresses, pots and hoes of long ago against today's objects, and find some old things are still used.
**Answer: True**
