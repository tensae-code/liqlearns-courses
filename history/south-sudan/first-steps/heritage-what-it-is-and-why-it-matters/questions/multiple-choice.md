# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Objects found at home”?
a) Children sort traditional and modern objects at home and at school and learn the past survives in things people keep and share.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Children sort traditional and modern objects at home and at school and learn the past survives in things people keep and share.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the heart of the story in “Objects that were used long time ago and the ones used today”?
a) Keji, Adek and Pita sort spears, skin dresses, pots and hoes of long ago against today's objects, and find some old things are still used.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Keji, Adek and Pita sort spears, skin dresses, pots and hoes of long ago against today's objects, and find some old things are still used.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
