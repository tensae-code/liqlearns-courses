# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** How has your family changed over time?; Timeline of objects; Changes that have happened in the place where we live; Placing objects and events in chronological order; Life before and after technology; Placing inventors and events in correct periods of time; Building a timeline of the journey to independence; Periods of history; Characteristics of different periods of civilisation; Building a chronological framework.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Timeline of objects”?
**Answer:** Children investigate how objects at home and school changed over time and build a timeline, learning order by lining up classmates youngest to oldest.
