Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “How has your family changed over time?”?
a) Okot meets grandparents Cucu and Ladu, cousins, aunts and uncles, and asks parents how the family changed.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Okot meets grandparents Cucu and Ladu, cousins, aunts and uncles, and asks parents how the family changed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
