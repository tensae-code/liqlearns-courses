## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Members of our family** — Okot names his mother Alea, father Andiek and sister Lisa, and the class learns who makes a family.
2. **Causes of change of a place** — Drought kills livestock and hunger empties a settlement, and children ask parents what caused change in their own community.
3. **How communities are shaped by the past** — Pupils analyse how ethnic groups in their community reflect South Sudan's history and why today's ceremonies were already present in the past.
4. **Meaning and features of civilisation** — Civilisation is society's most advanced stage — job specialisation, social classes, cities, public works, writing, arts and organised government — the base on which Egypt and Rome flourished.
5. **Introduction to History** — History is the study of past leaders, political structures, armies, ceremonies and livelihoods, divided into political, social and economic history.
