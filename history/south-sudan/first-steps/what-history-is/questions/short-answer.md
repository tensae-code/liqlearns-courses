# What History Is — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Members of our family; Causes of change of a place; How communities are shaped by the past; Meaning and features of civilisation; Introduction to History.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Causes of change of a place”?
**Answer:** Drought kills livestock and hunger empties a settlement, and children ask parents what caused change in their own community.
