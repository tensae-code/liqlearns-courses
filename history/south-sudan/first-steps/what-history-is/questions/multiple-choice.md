**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Members of our family”?
a) Okot names his mother Alea, father Andiek and sister Lisa, and the class learns who makes a family.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Okot names his mother Alea, father Andiek and sister Lisa, and the class learns who makes a family.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
