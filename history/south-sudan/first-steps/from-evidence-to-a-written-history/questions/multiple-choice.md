# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Investigating family history”?
a) Children design questions, talk to family members about ancestors and where the family came from, then turn the answers into a family tree.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
