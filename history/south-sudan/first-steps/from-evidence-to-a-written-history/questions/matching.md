# From Evidence to a Written History — matching

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Investigating family history — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Investigating change from sources — b)

Meanings:
a) Children design questions, talk to family members about ancestors and where the family came from, then turn the answers into a family tree.
