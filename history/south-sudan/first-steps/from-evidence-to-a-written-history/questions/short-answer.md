# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Investigating family history; Investigating change from sources; Investigating the history of South Sudan from a range of resources; How do we know what happened long time ago?; Exploring evidence of change; Sources of information on civilisation; Importance of maps in understanding the development of civilisation; Using maps to explain settlement; Sources of information on history; Researching slave trade as a case study.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Investigating change from sources”?
**Answer:** Children use maps and photographs as second-hand resources, listen to stories from older people, and categorise causes of change — weather, conflict, building, population, technology.
