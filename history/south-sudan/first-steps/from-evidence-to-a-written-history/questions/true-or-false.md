# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Investigating family history”.
**Answer: False** — Children design questions, talk to family members about ancestors and where the family came from, then turn the answers into a family tree.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Children use maps and photographs as second-hand resources, listen to stories from older people, and categorise causes of change — weather, conflict, building, population, technology.
**Answer: True**
