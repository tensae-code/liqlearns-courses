# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** What happened elsewhere in Africa?; Political, cultural and economic organisation of the kingdoms; Comparing and evaluating kingdom governance; Comparing civilisations in detail; Centralised and decentralised societies in South Sudan; Comparing colonial administration with pre-colonial administration.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Political, cultural and economic organisation of the kingdoms”?
**Answer:** Centralised kings command armies yet a council of elders can remove a king; kingdoms share gods of rain and harvest, communal cultivation, iron smelting for spears and shields, and trade of farm produce for textiles.
