# Find History in Your Own Locality — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Family history
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Children compare old and new family pictures, build a family tree, and ask parents about ancestors who died before they were born. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gathering origin stories from people
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Learners talk to a range of people about where they came from, family journeys, jobs, festivals and changes in law, and write to older children in neighbouring schools to learn about other peoples of their state. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
