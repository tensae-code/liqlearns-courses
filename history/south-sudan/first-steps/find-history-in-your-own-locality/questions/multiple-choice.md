# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Family history”?
a) Children compare old and new family pictures, build a family tree, and ask parents about ancestors who died before they were born.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
**Answer: a) Children compare old and new family pictures, build a family tree, and ask parents about ancestors who died before they were born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
