# Find History in Your Own Locality — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Family history — a)

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Gathering origin stories from people — b)

Meanings:
a) Children compare old and new family pictures, build a family tree, and ask parents about ancestors who died before they were born.
b) Learners talk to a range of people about where they came from, family journeys, jobs, festivals and changes in law, and write to older children in neighbouring schools to learn about other peoples of their state.
