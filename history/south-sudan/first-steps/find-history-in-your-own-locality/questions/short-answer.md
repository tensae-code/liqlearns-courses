# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Family history; Gathering origin stories from people.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Gathering origin stories from people”?
**Answer:** Learners talk to a range of people about where they came from, family journeys, jobs, festivals and changes in law, and write to older children in neighbouring schools to learn about other peoples of their state.
