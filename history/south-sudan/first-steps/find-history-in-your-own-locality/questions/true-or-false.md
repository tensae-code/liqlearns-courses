# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Family history”.
**Answer: False** — Children compare old and new family pictures, build a family tree, and ask parents about ancestors who died before they were born.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Learners talk to a range of people about where they came from, family journeys, jobs, festivals and changes in law, and write to older children in neighbouring schools to learn about other peoples of their state.
**Answer: True**
