# Video script — Fact or Opinion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Children compare changes, judge their impact on daily life, and build arguments to persuade others which changes helped and which they would reverse. Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Groups examine and debate why Long Distance, Coastal and Trans-Saharan trade grew and why it collapsed by the end of the 19th century, presenting conclusions with their evidence. Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in South Sudan.
