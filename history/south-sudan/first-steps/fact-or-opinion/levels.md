# Fact or Opinion — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arguing for and against change
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Children compare changes, judge their impact on daily life, and build arguments to persuade others which changes helped and which they would reverse. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Debating the growth and collapse of pre-colonial trade
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Groups examine and debate why Long Distance, Coastal and Trans-Saharan trade grew and why it collapsed by the end of the 19th century, presenting conclusions with their evidence. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Debating agreements and assessing the wars
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Learners debate the outcomes and terms of the Addis-Ababa Agreement and assess the contribution of the Anya-Nya and SPLM/A wars to South Sudan's independence, giving reasons and evidence. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
