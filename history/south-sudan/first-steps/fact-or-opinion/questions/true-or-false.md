# Fact or Opinion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the story of “Arguing for and against change”.
**Answer: False** — Children compare changes, judge their impact on daily life, and build arguments to persuade others which changes helped and which they would reverse.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Groups examine and debate why Long Distance, Coastal and Trans-Saharan trade grew and why it collapsed by the end of the 19th century, presenting conclusions with their evidence.
**Answer: True**
