**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the heart of the story in “Arguing for and against change”?
a) Children compare changes, judge their impact on daily life, and build arguments to persuade others which changes helped and which they would reverse.  b) A story that is not taught in this lesson.  c) The opposite of what this lesson teaches.  d) A place name with no part in this story
