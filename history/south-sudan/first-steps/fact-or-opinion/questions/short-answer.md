**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Arguing for and against change; Debating the growth and collapse of pre-colonial trade; Debating agreements and assessing the wars.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What happens in “Debating the growth and collapse of pre-colonial trade”?
**Answer:** Groups examine and debate why Long Distance, Coastal and Trans-Saharan trade grew and why it collapsed by the end of the 19th century, presenting conclusions with their evidence.
