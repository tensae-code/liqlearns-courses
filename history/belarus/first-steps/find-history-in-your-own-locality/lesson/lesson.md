# Find History in Your Own Locality

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Find History in Your Own Locality. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — "Our Region" lesson after §16

Pupils interview and survey their own locality to name its heroes and achievements of 1950s–1980s.

## Sub lesson 2 — "Our Region" lesson after §§26–27

Pupils build their region's brand from its historical experience at the turn of the centuries.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Pupils interview and survey their own locality to name its heroes and achievements of 1950s–1980s. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: state the lesson's one thing aloud, in your own words.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the people, the place, the turn, and what came of it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
