# Video script — Perestroika and the Road to 1991

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Moscow orders "acceleration" of a stalling economy, but speed without change of system only deepens the crisis.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Moscow orders "acceleration" of a stalling economy, but speed without change of system only deepens the crisis. Gorbachev's perestroika brings contested elections, abolition of the party's constitutional monopoly and the first multiparty stirrings in Belarus.

Remember it this way: Moscow orders "acceleration" of a stalling economy, but speed without change of system only deepens the crisis.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
