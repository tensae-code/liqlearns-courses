# The Partitions of the Commonwealth: Belarus to Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "The partitions of the Commonwealth and the inclusion of the Belarusian lands in the Russian Empire"?
**Answer:** Poniatowski's reforms and the Constitution of 3 May 1791 come too late; Russia, Prussia and Austria partition the state in 1772, 1793 and 1795 and Belarus passes to Russia.
