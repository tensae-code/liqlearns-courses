# Culture, Education and Science in the Postwar BSSR — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "§4 Revival and development of education and science in the postwar BSSR"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Schools, universities and the Academy of Sciences are rebuilt from ruins and a new educated generation rises.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "§5 Literature and art"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A writers' union shrunk to 47 members by war regrows around Melezh, Shamyakin and Bryl, but a 1947 party decree orders literature back into line.
