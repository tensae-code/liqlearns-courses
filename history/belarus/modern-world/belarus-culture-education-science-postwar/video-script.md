# Video script — Culture, Education and Science in the Postwar BSSR

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Schools, universities and the Academy of Sciences are rebuilt from ruins and a new educated generation rises.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Schools, universities and the Academy of Sciences are rebuilt from ruins and a new educated generation rises. A writers' union shrunk to 47 members by war regrows around Melezh, Shamyakin and Bryl, but a 1947 party decree orders literature back into line.

Remember it this way: Schools, universities and the Academy of Sciences are rebuilt from ruins and a new educated generation rises.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
