# Video script — Belarus in the First World War and February 1917

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Pupils draw the front line of late 1915 and its 1916 stabilisation, the Sventsyany breakthrough, the Naroch and Baranovichi operations, and the towns where the first Soviets of workers' and soldiers' deputies formed.
