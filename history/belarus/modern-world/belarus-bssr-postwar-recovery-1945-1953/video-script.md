# Video script — The BSSR's Postwar Recovery, 1945–1953

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Victory in 1945 leaves every third Belarusian dead and the land in ruins, yet the republic co-founds the UN and throws itself into the Fourth Five-Year Plan of rebuilding.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Victory in 1945 leaves every third Belarusian dead and the land in ruins, yet the republic co-founds the UN and throws itself into the Fourth Five-Year Plan of rebuilding. Women and teenagers sow the first liberated fields while workers raise factories, railways and power stations past pre-war levels by 1950. Pupils weigh the recovery's ledger — industry restored, land question settled by force in the west, society exhausted but industrialising.

Remember it this way: Victory in 1945 leaves every third Belarusian dead and the land in ruins, yet the republic co-founds the UN and throws itself into the Fourth Five-Year Plan of rebuilding.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
