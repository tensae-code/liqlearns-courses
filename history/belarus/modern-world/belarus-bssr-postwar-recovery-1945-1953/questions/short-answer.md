# The BSSR's Postwar Recovery, 1945–1953 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Part 1 §1 The BSSR in the first postwar decade"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Victory in 1945 leaves every third Belarusian dead and the land in ruins, yet the republic co-founds the UN and throws itself into the Fourth Five-Year Plan of rebuilding.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "§2 Overcoming the war's consequences in the economy; reaching a new level"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Women and teenagers sow the first liberated fields while workers raise factories, railways and power stations past pre-war levels by 1950.
