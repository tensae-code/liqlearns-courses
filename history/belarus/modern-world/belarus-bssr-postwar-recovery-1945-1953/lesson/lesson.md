# The BSSR's Postwar Recovery, 1945–1953

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — The BSSR's Postwar Recovery, 1945–1953. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Part 1 §1 The BSSR in the first postwar decade

Victory in 1945 leaves every third Belarusian dead and the land in ruins, yet the republic co-founds the UN and throws itself into the Fourth Five-Year Plan of rebuilding.

## Sub lesson 2 — §2 Overcoming the war's consequences in the economy; reaching a new level

Women and teenagers sow the first liberated fields while workers raise factories, railways and power stations past pre-war levels by 1950.

## Sub lesson 3 — §6 Results of the recovery period (summary lesson, document work on the 1946 western-regions report)

Pupils weigh the recovery's ledger — industry restored, land question settled by force in the west, society exhausted but industrialising.
