## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Part 1 §1 The BSSR in the first postwar decade** — Victory in 1945 leaves every third Belarusian dead and the land in ruins, yet the republic co-founds the UN and throws itself into the Fourth Five-Year Plan of rebuilding.
2. **§2 Overcoming the war's consequences in the economy; reaching a new level** — Women and teenagers sow the first liberated fields while workers raise factories, railways and power stations past pre-war levels by 1950.
3. **§6 Results of the recovery period (summary lesson, document work on the 1946 western-regions report)** — Pupils weigh the recovery's ledger — industry restored, land question settled by force in the west, society exhausted but industrialising.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: Victory in 1945 leaves every third Belarusian dead and the land in ruins, yet the republic co-founds the UN and throws itself into the Fourth Five-Year Plan of rebuilding. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
