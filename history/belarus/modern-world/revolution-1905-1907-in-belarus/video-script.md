# Video script — The Revolution of 1905–1907 in Belarus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Pupils flag the October and December all-Russian political strikes, the towns where demonstrators were shot, the illegal teachers' congress and the towns hit by pogroms.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Pupils flag the October and December all-Russian political strikes, the towns where demonstrators were shot, the illegal teachers' congress and the towns hit by pogroms.

Remember it this way: Pupils flag the October and December all-Russian political strikes, the towns where demonstrators were shot, the illegal teachers' congress and the towns hit by pogroms.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
