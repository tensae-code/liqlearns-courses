# Culture, Education and Science, 1950s–1980s — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "§14 Development of education and science, late 1950s — 1980s"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Schooling, universities and research institutes expand across the republic and Belarusian cosmonauts carry its name into orbit.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "§15 Belarusian literature, late 1950s — 1980s"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Thaw rehabilitates repressed writers, and Vasil Bykau's war prose turns the village and the front into questions of conscience.
