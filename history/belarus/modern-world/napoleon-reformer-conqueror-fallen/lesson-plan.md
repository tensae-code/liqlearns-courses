# Lesson plan — Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen

**Course:** History — Belarus · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — In the Russian Empire: The Long Nineteenth Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pupils draw the Grande Armée's road east across Belarus and its frozen road back, marking the battles won by each side.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — In the Russian Empire: The Long Nineteenth Century (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Outline map: Belarus during the war of 1812 — Napoleon's invasion of Russia (June–October 1812) and the expulsion of his army (October–December 1812) (exercise)** — Pupils draw the Grande Armée's road east across Belarus and its frozen road back, marking the battles won by each side.
2. **Outline map: Events of the war of 1812 on the Belarusian lands (exercise)** — Pupils mark the GDL Provisional Government Commission in Vilnius, the French control zone and the districts where GDL regiments formed.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: Pupils draw the Grande Armée's road east across Belarus and its frozen road back, marking the battles won by each side. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
