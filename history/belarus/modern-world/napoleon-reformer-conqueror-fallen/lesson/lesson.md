# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Outline map: Belarus during the war of 1812 — Napoleon's invasion of Russia (June–October 1812) and the expulsion of his army (October–December 1812) (exercise)

Pupils draw the Grande Armée's road east across Belarus and its frozen road back, marking the battles won by each side.

## Sub lesson 2 — Outline map: Events of the war of 1812 on the Belarusian lands (exercise)

Pupils mark the GDL Provisional Government Commission in Vilnius, the French control zone and the districts where GDL regiments formed.
