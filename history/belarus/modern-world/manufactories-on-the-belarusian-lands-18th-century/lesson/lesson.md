# Manufactories on the Belarusian Lands in the 18th Century

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Manufactories on the Belarusian Lands in the 18th Century. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

The Radziwiłłs' glassworks and the Slutsk sash manufactory, then Tyzenhauz's Grodno works, bring factory production — but serf labour gives the magnate manufactories a short life.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Radziwiłłs' glassworks and the Slutsk sash manufactory, then Tyzenhauz's Grodno works, bring factory production — but serf labour gives the magnate manufactories a short life. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the people, the place, the turn, and what came of it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
