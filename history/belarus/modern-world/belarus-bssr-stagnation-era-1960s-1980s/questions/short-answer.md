# The BSSR in the Stagnation Era, 1960s–1980s — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "§10 The social and political situation, late 1960s — first half of the 1980s"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Khrushchev is removed in 1964, reform cools into stability, and Masherov's long rule keeps Belarus orderly while freedoms stay frozen.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "§11 The republic's social and economic situation, late 1960s — first half of the 1980s"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The 1965 economic reform fades by the early 1970s, extensive growth slows and the first signs of a braking mechanism and stagnation appear.
