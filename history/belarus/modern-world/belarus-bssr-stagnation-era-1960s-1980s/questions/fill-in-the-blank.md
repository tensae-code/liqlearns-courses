# The BSSR in the Stagnation Era, 1960s–1980s — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ is removed in 1964, reform cools into stability, and Masherov's long rule keeps Belarus orderly while freedoms stay frozen. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Khrushchev

**2.** The 1965 economic reform fades by the early 1970s, extensive growth slows and the first signs of a braking mechanism and stagnation appear — the land at the centre of this story is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Belarus
