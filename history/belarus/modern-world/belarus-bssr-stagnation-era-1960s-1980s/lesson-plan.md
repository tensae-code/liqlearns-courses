## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§10 The social and political situation, late 1960s — first half of the 1980s** — Khrushchev is removed in 1964, reform cools into stability, and Masherov's long rule keeps Belarus orderly while freedoms stay frozen.
2. **§11 The republic's social and economic situation, late 1960s — first half of the 1980s** — The 1965 economic reform fades by the early 1970s, extensive growth slows and the first signs of a braking mechanism and stagnation appear.
3. **§17 Results of the BSSR's development on the path to an industrial society (summary lesson)** — The audit closes — a peasant republic became an industrial one, at the price of a system that could no longer reform itself.
