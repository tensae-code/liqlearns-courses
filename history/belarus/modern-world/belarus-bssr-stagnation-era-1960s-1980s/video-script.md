# Video script — The BSSR in the Stagnation Era, 1960s–1980s

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Khrushchev is removed in 1964, reform cools into stability, and Masherov's long rule keeps Belarus orderly while freedoms stay frozen.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Khrushchev is removed in 1964, reform cools into stability, and Masherov's long rule keeps Belarus orderly while freedoms stay frozen. The 1965 economic reform fades by the early 1970s, extensive growth slows and the first signs of a braking mechanism and stagnation appear. The audit closes — a peasant republic became an industrial one, at the price of a system that could no longer reform itself.

Remember it this way: Khrushchev is removed in 1964, reform cools into stability, and Masherov's long rule keeps Belarus orderly while freedoms stay frozen.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
