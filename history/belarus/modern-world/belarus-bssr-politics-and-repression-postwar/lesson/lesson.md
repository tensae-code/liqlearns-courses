# BSSR Politics and Repression After the War

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — BSSR Politics and Repression After the War. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

The one-party Soviet system tightens again after the war, collectivisation is forced on the western regions and returnees from occupation and captivity are met with distrust.
