# Belarus's Economy in the First Half of the 19th Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Outline map: Economic development of Belarus in the first half of the 19th century (exercise)"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pupils mark the first factories and sugar works, the great fairs, and Kiselyov's reform map of state-peasant allotments.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "Outline map: Communications routes of Belarus in the first half of the 19th century (exercise)"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils number the shipping canals (Dnieper-Bug, Ogiński, Augustów, Berezina system) and find the river on which Belarus's first steamship appeared.
