# Belarus's Economy in the First Half of the 19th Century — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**2.** Pupils number the shipping canals (____, Ogiński, Augustów, Berezina system) and find the river on which Belarus's first steamship appeared. (Sub lesson 2)
