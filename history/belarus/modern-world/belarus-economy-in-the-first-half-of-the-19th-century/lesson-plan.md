## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Outline map: Economic development of Belarus in the first half of the 19th century (exercise)** — Pupils mark the first factories and sugar works, the great fairs, and Kiselyov's reform map of state-peasant allotments.
2. **Outline map: Communications routes of Belarus in the first half of the 19th century (exercise)** — Pupils number the shipping canals (Dnieper-Bug, Ogiński, Augustów, Berezina system) and find the river on which Belarus's first steamship appeared.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
