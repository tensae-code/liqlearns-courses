# The Livonian War and the GDL, 1558–1583

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — The Livonian War and the GDL, 1558–1583. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

Ivan the Terrible's armies seize Polotsk, Stefan Batory wins it back at Pskov's walls, and the exhausted sides freeze the border almost where it began.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ivan the Terrible's armies seize Polotsk, Stefan Batory wins it back at Pskov's walls, and the exhausted sides freeze the border almost where it began. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
