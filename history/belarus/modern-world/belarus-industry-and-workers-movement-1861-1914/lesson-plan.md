## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Outline map: Development of industry and the workers' movement in Belarus (1861–1900) (exercise)** — Pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes.
2. **Outline map: Industry and towns of Belarus at the beginning of the 20th century; communications routes in the second half of the 19th — beginning of the 20th century (exercise)** — Pupils date the railway lines decade by decade and circle the towns over 100,000 people and the great paper factories.
