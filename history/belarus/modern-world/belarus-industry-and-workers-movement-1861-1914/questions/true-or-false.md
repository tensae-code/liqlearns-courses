# Belarus's Industry and Workers' Movement, 1861–1914 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes. (Sub lesson 1)
