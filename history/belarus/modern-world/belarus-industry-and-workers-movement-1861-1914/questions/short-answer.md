# Belarus's Industry and Workers' Movement, 1861–1914 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Outline map: Development of industry and the workers' movement in Belarus (1861–1900) (exercise)"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "Outline map: Industry and towns of Belarus at the beginning of the 20th century; communications routes in the second half of the 19th — beginning of the 20th century (exercise)"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pupils date the railway lines decade by decade and circle the towns over 100,000 people and the great paper factories.
