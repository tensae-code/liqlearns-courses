# Video script — Belarus's Industry and Workers' Movement, 1861–1914

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes. Pupils date the railway lines decade by decade and circle the towns over 100,000 people and the great paper factories.

Remember it this way: Pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
