# Belarus's Industry and Workers' Movement, 1861–1914

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Belarus's Industry and Workers' Movement, 1861–1914. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Outline map: Development of industry and the workers' movement in Belarus (1861–1900) (exercise)

Pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Outline map: Industry and towns of Belarus at the beginning of the 20th century; communications routes in the second half of the 19th — beginning of the 20th century (exercise)

Pupils date the railway lines decade by decade and circle the towns over 100,000 people and the great paper factories.
