# GDL Trade in the Second Half of the 18th Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Trade of the GDL in the second half of the 18th century"?
**Answer:** Magnates monopolise the grain, timber and flax trade, and new shipping canals — the Ogiński (from 1775) and the Royal — tie the land to the Baltic and Black Sea ports.
