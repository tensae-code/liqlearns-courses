# The Khmelnytsky Uprising Reaches Belarus, 1648–1651

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — The Khmelnytsky Uprising Reaches Belarus, 1648–1651. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

Bohdan Khmelnytsky, denied his rights and his church's, raises Ukraine, and Cossack-peasant detachments carry the war into southern Belarus.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Bohdan Khmelnytsky, denied his rights and his church's, raises Ukraine, and Cossack-peasant detachments carry the war into southern Belarus. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: state the lesson's one thing aloud, in your own words.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the people, the place, the turn, and what came of it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
