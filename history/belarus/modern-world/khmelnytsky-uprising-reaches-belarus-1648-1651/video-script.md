# Video script — The Khmelnytsky Uprising Reaches Belarus, 1648–1651

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bohdan Khmelnytsky, denied his rights and his church's, raises Ukraine, and Cossack-peasant detachments carry the war into southern Belarus.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Bohdan Khmelnytsky, denied his rights and his church's, raises Ukraine, and Cossack-peasant detachments carry the war into southern Belarus.

Remember it this way: Bohdan Khmelnytsky, denied his rights and his church's, raises Ukraine, and Cossack-peasant detachments carry the war into southern Belarus.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
