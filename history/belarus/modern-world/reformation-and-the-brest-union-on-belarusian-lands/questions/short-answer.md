# The Reformation and the Brest Union on Belarusian Lands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Religion and the church on the Belarusian lands in the second half of the 16th century"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Calvinism sweeps the magnates, Jesuits arrive to fight it, and the 1596 Brest Union splits the Orthodox world by tying part of it to Rome.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "Religion and the church on the Belarusian lands in the 17th century"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Counter-Reformation, backed by kings and Sejm, drives to uproot Orthodoxy and Protestantism while the Uniate church becomes the land's largest confession.
