# The Reformation and the Brest Union on Belarusian Lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ sweeps the magnates, Jesuits arrive to fight it, and the 1596 Brest Union splits the Orthodox world by tying part of it to Rome. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Calvinism

**2.** The ____, backed by kings and Sejm, drives to uproot Orthodoxy and Protestantism while the Uniate church becomes the land's largest…. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Counter-Reformation
