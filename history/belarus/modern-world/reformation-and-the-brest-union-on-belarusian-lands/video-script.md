# Video script — The Reformation and the Brest Union on Belarusian Lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Calvinism sweeps the magnates, Jesuits arrive to fight it, and the 1596 Brest Union splits the Orthodox world by tying part of it to Rome.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Calvinism sweeps the magnates, Jesuits arrive to fight it, and the 1596 Brest Union splits the Orthodox world by tying part of it to Rome. The Counter-Reformation, backed by kings and Sejm, drives to uproot Orthodoxy and Protestantism while the Uniate church becomes the land's largest confession.

Remember it this way: Calvinism sweeps the magnates, Jesuits arrive to fight it, and the 1596 Brest Union splits the Orthodox world by tying part of it to Rome.
