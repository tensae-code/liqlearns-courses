# Lesson plan — The Reformation and the Brest Union on Belarusian Lands

**Course:** History — Belarus · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Reformation and the Brest Union on Belarusian Lands
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Commonwealth Centuries (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Commonwealth Centuries (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Religion and the church on the Belarusian lands in the second half of the 16th century** — Calvinism sweeps the magnates, Jesuits arrive to fight it, and the 1596 Brest Union splits the Orthodox world by tying part of it to Rome.
2. **Religion and the church on the Belarusian lands in the 17th century** — The Counter-Reformation, backed by kings and Sejm, drives to uproot Orthodoxy and Protestantism while the Uniate church becomes the land's largest confession.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
