# The Reformation and the Brest Union on Belarusian Lands

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — The Reformation and the Brest Union on Belarusian Lands. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Religion and the church on the Belarusian lands in the second half of the 16th century

Calvinism sweeps the magnates, Jesuits arrive to fight it, and the 1596 Brest Union splits the Orthodox world by tying part of it to Rome.

## Sub lesson 2 — Religion and the church on the Belarusian lands in the 17th century

The Counter-Reformation, backed by kings and Sejm, drives to uproot Orthodoxy and Protestantism while the Uniate church becomes the land's largest confession.
