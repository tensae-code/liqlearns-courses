# The BSSR in Thaw and Reform, 1950s–1960s — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Stalin's death the "Thaw" opens under Mazurov; MAZ and BelAZ trucks, MTZ tractors, Soligorsk potash and Minsk electronics turn the republic industrial. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The lesson says the part "§9 Social and economic development, late 1950s — first half of the 1960s" never happened and nothing changed in it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — the lesson tells that part's story: The scientific-technical revolution reaches Belarus as sovnarkhoz managers and new chemical, radio and machine plants reshape work
