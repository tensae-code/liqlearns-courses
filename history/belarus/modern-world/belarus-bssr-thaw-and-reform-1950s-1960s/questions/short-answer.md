# The BSSR in Thaw and Reform, 1950s–1960s — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Part 2 §7 Belarus under reform of social, political and economic life"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After Stalin's death the "Thaw" opens under Mazurov; MAZ and BelAZ trucks, MTZ tractors, Soligorsk potash and Minsk electronics turn the republic industrial.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "§8 Social and political life, late 1950s — first half of the 1960s"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Khrushchev's Thaw loosens fear and denounces the cult of personality, but the party's monopoly survives untouched.
