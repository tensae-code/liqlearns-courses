# Lesson plan — The BSSR in Thaw and Reform, 1950s–1960s

**Course:** History — Belarus · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The BSSR in Thaw and Reform, 1950s–1960s
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Soviet and Sovereign Belarus (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After Stalin's death the "Thaw" opens under Mazurov; MAZ and BelAZ trucks, MTZ tractors, Soligorsk potash and Minsk electronics turn the republic industrial.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Soviet and Sovereign Belarus (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Part 2 §7 Belarus under reform of social, political and economic life** — After Stalin's death the "Thaw" opens under Mazurov; MAZ and BelAZ trucks, MTZ tractors, Soligorsk potash and Minsk electronics turn the republic industrial.
2. **§8 Social and political life, late 1950s — first half of the 1960s** — Khrushchev's Thaw loosens fear and denounces the cult of personality, but the party's monopoly survives untouched.
3. **§9 Social and economic development, late 1950s — first half of the 1960s** — The scientific-technical revolution reaches Belarus as sovnarkhoz managers and new chemical, radio and machine plants reshape work.
