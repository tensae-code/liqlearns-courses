# Sovereignty and Independence, 1990–1994 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Part 3 §18 Formation and development of the Republic of Belarus"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 1990 sovereignty declaration, the USSR's end in December 1991, a new name and symbols, the 1994 Constitution and the first presidential election remake the state.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "§19 Establishing state sovereignty"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sovereignty is spelled out in symbols, citizenship and law as Belarus decides what an independent state must hold.
