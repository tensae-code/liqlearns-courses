# Fragmentation: The Belarusian Principalities in the 12th Century — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Belarusian lands in the 12th — first half of the 13th century: fragmentation on the Belarusian lands"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Polotsk and Turov splinter into appanage principalities-volosts as princes settle sons on ever smaller towns.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "The fragmentation of Rus"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Brother-princes make war on each other, Kiev's unity dissolves, and Polovtsian and crusader pressure grows on the fragments.
