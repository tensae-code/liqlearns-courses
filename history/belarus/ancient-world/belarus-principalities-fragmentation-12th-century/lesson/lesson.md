## Sub lesson 1 — Belarusian lands in the 12th — first half of the 13th century: fragmentation on the Belarusian lands

Polotsk and Turov splinter into appanage principalities-volosts as princes settle sons on ever smaller towns.

## Sub lesson 2 — The fragmentation of Rus

Brother-princes make war on each other, Kiev's unity dissolves, and Polovtsian and crusader pressure grows on the fragments.
