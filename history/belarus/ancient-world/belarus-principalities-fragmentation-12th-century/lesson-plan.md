## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Belarusian lands in the 12th — first half of the 13th century: fragmentation on the Belarusian lands** — Polotsk and Turov splinter into appanage principalities-volosts as princes settle sons on ever smaller towns.
2. **The fragmentation of Rus** — Brother-princes make war on each other, Kiev's unity dissolves, and Polovtsian and crusader pressure grows on the fragments.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
