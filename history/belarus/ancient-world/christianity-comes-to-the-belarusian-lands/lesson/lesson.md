# Christianity Comes to the Belarusian Lands

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Christianity Comes to the Belarusian Lands. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Christian church and culture of the Belarusian lands, 11th — first half of the 13th century: Christianity on the territory of Belarus

Bishoprics at Polotsk and Turov, St Sophia's cathedral and Euphrosyne of Polotsk's cross carry the new faith and book culture into the towns.

## Sub lesson 2 — Christianity in Eastern Europe

The faith spreads slowly against pagan resistance and splits in 1054 into Orthodox East and Catholic West, with Belarus on the seam.
