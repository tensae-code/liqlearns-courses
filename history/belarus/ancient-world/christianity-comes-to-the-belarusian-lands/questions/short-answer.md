# Christianity Comes to the Belarusian Lands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "The Christian church and culture of the Belarusian lands, 11th — first half of the 13th century: Christianity on the territory of Belarus"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bishoprics at Polotsk and Turov, St Sophia's cathedral and Euphrosyne of Polotsk's cross carry the new faith and book culture into the towns.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "Christianity in Eastern Europe"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The faith spreads slowly against pagan resistance and splits in 1054 into Orthodox East and Catholic West, with Belarus on the seam.
