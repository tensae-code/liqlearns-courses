# Christianity Comes to the Belarusian Lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Bishoprics at ____ and Turov, St Sophia's cathedral and Euphrosyne of Polotsk's cross carry the new faith and book culture into the towns. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Polotsk

**2.** The faith spreads slowly against pagan resistance and splits in 1054 into ____ East and Catholic West, with Belarus on the seam. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Orthodox
