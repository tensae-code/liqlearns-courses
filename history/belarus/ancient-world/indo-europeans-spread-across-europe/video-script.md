# Video script — The Indo-Europeans Spread Across Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Steppe horsemen between the Danube and the Volga spread their languages from India to the Atlantic, becoming Balts, Slavs, Germans and others.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Steppe horsemen between the Danube and the Volga spread their languages from India to the Atlantic, becoming Balts, Slavs, Germans and others.

Remember it this way: Steppe horsemen between the Danube and the Volga spread their languages from India to the Atlantic, becoming Balts, Slavs, Germans and others.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
