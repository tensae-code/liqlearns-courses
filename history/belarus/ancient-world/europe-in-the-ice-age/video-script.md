# Video script — Europe in the Ice Age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
An ice sheet kilometres thick buries northern Europe, and life crowds into the ice-free south until the warming begins.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. An ice sheet kilometres thick buries northern Europe, and life crowds into the ice-free south until the warming begins.

Remember it this way: An ice sheet kilometres thick buries northern Europe, and life crowds into the ice-free south until the warming begins.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
