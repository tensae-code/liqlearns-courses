# Video script — The Slavs Settle Eastern Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the Great Migration the Slavs pour between the Danube, Elbe and Dnieper and divide into western, southern and eastern unions.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. In the Great Migration the Slavs pour between the Danube, Elbe and Dnieper and divide into western, southern and eastern unions. The East Slavs trade and fight with Balts, Finns, Khazars and Varangians along the amber and "from the Varangians to the Greeks" routes.

Remember it this way: In the Great Migration the Slavs pour between the Danube, Elbe and Dnieper and divide into western, southern and eastern unions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
