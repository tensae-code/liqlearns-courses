# The Slavs Settle Eastern Europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** In the ____ Migration the Slavs pour between the Danube, Elbe and Dnieper and divide into western, southern and eastern unions. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Great

**2.** The ____ Slavs trade and fight with Balts, Finns, Khazars and Varangians along the amber and "from the Varangians to the Greeks" routes. (Sub lesson 2)
