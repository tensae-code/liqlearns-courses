# The Slavs Settle Eastern Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the Great Migration the Slavs pour between the Danube, Elbe and Dnieper and divide into western, southern and eastern unions. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The lesson says the part "East Slavic tribal unions and their neighbours" never happened and nothing changed in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson tells that part's story: The East Slavs trade and fight with Balts, Finns, Khazars and Varangians along the amber and "from the Varangians to the Greeks"…
