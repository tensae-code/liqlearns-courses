# The Slavs Settle Eastern Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "The settlement of the Slavs in Europe"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In the Great Migration the Slavs pour between the Danube, Elbe and Dnieper and divide into western, southern and eastern unions.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "East Slavic tribal unions and their neighbours"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The East Slavs trade and fight with Balts, Finns, Khazars and Varangians along the amber and "from the Varangians to the Greeks" routes.
