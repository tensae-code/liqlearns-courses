# The Balts on Belarusian Lands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "The pre-Slavic population of the territory of Belarus: the Balts"? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "The settlement of the ancient Balts in Europe (with "How iron was obtained")"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Baltic peoples hold the south-eastern Baltic shore while bog iron is smelted in forest furnaces.
