# The Balts on Belarusian Lands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The lesson says the part "The settlement of the ancient Balts in Europe (with "How iron was obtained")" never happened and nothing changed in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson tells that part's story: Baltic peoples hold the south-eastern Baltic shore while bog iron is smelted in forest furnaces
