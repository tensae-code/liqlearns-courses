# The Balts on Belarusian Lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Baltic

**2.** ____ peoples hold the south-eastern Baltic shore while bog iron is smelted in forest furnaces. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Baltic
