# Video script — The Balts on Belarusian Lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day. Baltic peoples hold the south-eastern Baltic shore while bog iron is smelted in forest furnaces.

Remember it this way: Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
