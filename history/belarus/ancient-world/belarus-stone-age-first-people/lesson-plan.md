# Lesson plan — The Stone Age: The First People on Belarusian Lands

**Course:** History — Belarus · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Stone Age: The First People on Belarusian Lands
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Belarusian Lands (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Belarusian Lands (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Stone Age (Ice Age period): the first people on the territory of Belarus; map "The oldest Stone Age people"** — Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi.
2. **Stone Age (post-glacial period): settlement of people on the Belarusian lands; map "Life of people in the post-glacial period in Belarus"** — As the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Belarus's books themselves work, telling the past as stories, maps and tasks.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
