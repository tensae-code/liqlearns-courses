# Video script — The Stone Age: The First People on Belarusian Lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi. As the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land.

Remember it this way: Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
