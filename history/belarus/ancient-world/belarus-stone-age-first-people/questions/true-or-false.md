# The Stone Age: The First People on Belarusian Lands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The lesson says the part "Stone Age (post-glacial period): settlement of people on the Belarusian lands; map "Life of people in the post-glacial period in Belarus"" never happened and nothing changed in it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the lesson tells that part's story: As the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land
