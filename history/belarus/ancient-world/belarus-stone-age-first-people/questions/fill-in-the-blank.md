# The Stone Age: The First People on Belarusian Lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Neanderthals

**2.** As the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land — the land at the centre of this story is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Belarus
