# The Stone Age: The First People on Belarusian Lands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happened in "Stone Age (Ice Age period): the first people on the territory of Belarus; map "The oldest Stone Age people""? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi.

**2.** In your own words, what happened in "Stone Age (post-glacial period): settlement of people on the Belarusian lands; map "Life of people in the post-glacial period in Belarus""? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** As the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land.
