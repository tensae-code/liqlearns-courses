# History — Belarus course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Belarus's own —
built from Belarus's History student books (the National
Institute of Education senior book and the Belkartografia
atlases and contour maps). Inside the course the organisation
is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside
their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step
by step. The grade each book comes from lives only in
ABOUT.md and in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Belarus's books': the tools of
history first, then the national story in its own order —
ancient lands, Commonwealth centuries, the long nineteenth
century as map work, and the Soviet and sovereign republic.
That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints
point down this ladder).
