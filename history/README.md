# History — a folder of courses, one per country

History is not the same story in every country: each country
teaches its own past — its own people, places, turns and
celebrations — in its own books, in its own way. So this
top-level entry is a **folder**, not one world course: inside
it there is **one course per country**, found by searching the
country.

Inside each country course, everything is organised by
**levels** — what the learner proves, step by step — never by
school grade. The school grades the books come from appear
only in each lesson's sources, as provenance. History is
taught as stories: who, where, what turned, and what came of
it.

**Ethiopia is the founding member of this folder.** Its
history lessons live in its founding Social Studies build,
and its Civics course anchors the folder pattern in the
sister folder, Civics and Citizenship — the pattern every
course in this folder follows: one course per country,
levels not grades, and provenance kept in each lesson's
sources and the course's ABOUT.md.
