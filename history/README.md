# History — a folder of courses, one per country

History is not the same story in every country: each country
teaches its own past — its own people, places, turns and
celebrations — in its own books, in its own way. So this
top-level entry is a **folder**, not one world course: inside
it there is **one course per country**, found by searching the
country.

Inside each country course, everything is organised by
**levels** — what the learner proves, step by step — never by
school grade. The school grades the books come from appear
only in each lesson's sources, as provenance. History is
taught as stories: who, where, what turned, and what came of
it.

**Ethiopia is the founding member of this folder.** Its
history lessons live in its founding Social Studies build,
and its Civics course anchors the folder pattern in the
sister folder, Civics and Citizenship — the pattern every
course in this folder follows: one course per country,
levels not grades, and provenance kept in each lesson's
sources and the course's ABOUT.md.

## The courses in this folder

- **Guyana** — [`guyana/`](guyana/) · 1 lesson: Guyana's nation story as its first school book tells it — independence on 26 May 1966, the national symbols, anthem and pledge, and the celebrations of Remembrance Day and Mashramani, from Guyana's *Social Studies For Our Children, Book 1* (Ministry of Education, Georgetown, English, 2004 revised second edition, COMPLETE — a combined Social Studies book; its civics, health and geography parts are named, not placed, in the course's contents map). See `guyana/ABOUT.md` for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.
- **Canada** — [`canada/`](canada/) · 3 lessons: the Inuit whaling story of Nunavut in three turns — the traditional hunt (one whale feeding and supplying a whole community; elders' knowledge, open boats, hand harpoons, the catch shared), the foreign commercial whalers from across the sea (oil and baleen, trade and depletion), and whaling today (whales protected and counted, the community hunt continuing small under shared rules), from Canada's *History of Whaling* (Uqalimaariuqsaniq Level 13 leveled reader, Inuktitut in syllabics, Nunavut, COMPLETE — year and ISBN not printed, as filed). See `canada/ABOUT.md` for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.
- **United Arab Emirates** — [`united-arab-emirates/`](united-arab-emirates/) · 3 lessons: the Emirates' history as national identity at the very start of schooling — heritage as a museum you visit and a founder's instruction you memorise, Ibn Battuta the great traveller of the shared Arab past, and the young nation itself (the union of the seven emirates, Sheikh Zayed's sayings, and how schooling grew from the Mutawa'a's circle to schools for every child), from the United Arab Emirates' *Social Studies and National Education, Student Book, Grade 1, Volume 3* (Ministry of Education, United Arab Emirates, Arabic, 1447هـ / 2025–2026, COMPLETE — a combined Social Studies book; its civics, science and economics parts are named, not placed, in the course's contents map; Terms 1–2 are not in this record set). See `united-arab-emirates/ABOUT.md` for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.
