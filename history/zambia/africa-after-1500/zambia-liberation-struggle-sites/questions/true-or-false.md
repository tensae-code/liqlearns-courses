# Zambia: Liberation Struggle Sites — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Chilenje House No. 394 was Kenneth Kaunda's home from 1960 to 1962.
**Answer: True**

**2.** All the liberation struggle sites are palaces and government buildings.
**Answer: False — the book's map is a rock, a tree and ordinary township houses, made history by the meetings held in them.**
