# Zambia: Liberation Struggle Sites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which two places at Kabwe does the book name first in the liberation map?
a) Freedom House and the Freedom Statue  b) the Mulungushi Rock of Authority and the Mukuyu Tree  c) Chilenje House and Matero House  d) two Copperbelt mines
**Answer: b) the Mulungushi Rock of Authority and the Mukuyu Tree** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened at Bwacha House No. E1376 on 8 March 1958?
a) the Federation was signed  b) the first Copperbelt strike began  c) Kaunda was elected president of ZANC  d) independence was declared
**Answer: c) Kaunda was elected president of ZANC** — That is what this lesson teaches.
