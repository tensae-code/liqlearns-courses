# Zambia: Liberation Struggle Sites — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the book teach the struggle through places?
**Answer:** Because each place holds a meeting or moment that moved Zambia toward 1964 — a rock, a tree and ordinary houses become the map of a nation's freedom.

**2.** Name three of the Lusaka places in the lesson.
**Answer:** Any three of: Chilenje House No. 394; Freedom House; Matero House No. 3144B; Bwacha House No. E1376.
