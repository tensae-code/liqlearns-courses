# Video script — Zambia: Liberation Struggle Sites

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Zambia's freedom was planned in places you can still point to — and most of them look perfectly ordinary.

Start the map at Kabwe. Here is the Mulungushi Rock of Authority, where the movement gathered and decided. And here is the Mukuyu Tree, a tree that served as a meeting place — a parliament in the shade.

Now walk into Lusaka. Chilenje House, number 394: Kenneth Kaunda's home from 1960 to 1962. Freedom House, the movement's headquarters. Matero House, number 3144B. And Bwacha House, number E1376 — where, on 8 March 1958, Kaunda was elected president of ZANC.

End the walk at the Freedom Statue, the struggle cast in bronze, and at Isa ya Mwaamba in Choma, remembered in the south.

Remember it this way: a rock, a tree and ordinary township houses — that is the map of a nation's freedom.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
