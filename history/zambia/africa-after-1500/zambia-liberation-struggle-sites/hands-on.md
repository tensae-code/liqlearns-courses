# Hands-on — Zambia: Liberation Struggle Sites

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the walk out loud again: a rock, a tree, and ordinary houses — each one holding a meeting. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw the map of the struggle: Kabwe at the top with the Mulungushi Rock and the Mukuyu Tree; Lusaka below with four houses marked by their numbers — 394, Freedom House, 3144B, E1376 — and Choma with Isa ya Mwaamba. Beside Bwacha House write the date 8 March 1958. Say each place aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for remembered places in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you pass a place people point out for what happened there, note it: the place, what happened, and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
