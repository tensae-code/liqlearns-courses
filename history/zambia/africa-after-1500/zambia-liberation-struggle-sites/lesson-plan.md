# Lesson plan — Zambia: Liberation Struggle Sites

**Course:** History — Zambia · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Zambia: Liberation Struggle Sites
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Slave Trade, Resistance and the Road to Independence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Zambia's freedom struggle can be walked as places: the Mulungushi Rock of Authority and the Mukuyu Tree at Kabwe, Chilenje House No. 394 in Lusaka (Kenneth Kaunda's home 1960–62), Freedom House and Matero House No. 3144B in Lusaka, Bwacha House No. E1376 (where Kaunda was elected ZANC president on 8 March 1958), the Freedom Statue, and Isa ya Mwaamba at Choma — a rock, a tree and ordinary township houses, each holding a meeting that moved Zambia toward 1964.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Slave Trade, Resistance and the Road to Independence — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Zambia's liberation struggle is remembered in named places — a rock, a tree and ordinary township houses — each holding a meeting that moved the country toward 1964.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — a place in your own town where something important for the community happened, and what is remembered there.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks great history needs great buildings → the book's point is the opposite: a rock, a tree and ordinary township houses became the map of a nation's freedom, because of the meetings held in them.
- Mixes up the Lusaka houses → anchor them by their men and moments: Chilenje House No. 394 was Kaunda's home 1960–62; at Bwacha House No. E1376 Kaunda was elected ZANC president on 8 March 1958.
- Go back to the one thing: Zambia's freedom struggle can be walked as places: the Mulungushi Rock of Authority and the Mukuyu Tree at Kabwe, Chilenje House No. 394 in Lusaka (Kenneth Kaunda's home 1960–62), Freedom House and Matero House No. 3144B in Lusaka, Bwacha House No. E1376 (where Kaunda was elected ZANC president on 8 March 1958), the Freedom Statue, and Isa ya Mwaamba at Choma — a rock, a tree and ordinary township houses, each holding a meeting that moved Zambia toward 1964. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
