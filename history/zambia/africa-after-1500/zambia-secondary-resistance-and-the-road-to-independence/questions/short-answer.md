# Zambia: Secondary Resistance and the Road to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two stops on the road of secondary resistance, in order.
**Answer:** Any two in order, e.g. welfare associations from the 1920s, then the Copperbelt strikes of 1935 and 1940; or the Congress of 1948 becoming the ANC in 1951, then the fight against the 1953 Federation.

**2.** What was UNIP's Cha Cha Cha campaign of 1961?
**Answer:** A wave of organised defiance — named for a dance — that made the country ungovernable by the old rules and pressed the colonial government toward conceding independence.
