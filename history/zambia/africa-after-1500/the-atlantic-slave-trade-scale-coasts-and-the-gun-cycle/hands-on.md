# Hands-on — The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask the opening question out loud again — "Can a human being own another?" — and give your answer and its reason. Hold that answer in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw the trade as the book teaches it: a home coast on one side, a far shore on the other, and a ship between them. Under the ship write the book's three hard words — taken, sold, worked without pay — and above the drawing write the opening question. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see a person treated fairly, as a person, or unfairly, as a thing, note it: what happened, and what the lesson's opening question would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
