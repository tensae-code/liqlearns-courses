# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does slavery mean in this lesson?
**Answer:** A person is held as property — taken from home, sold, and made to work without pay for the one who claims to own them.

**2.** Why does the book open with the question "Can a human being own another?"
**Answer:** So learners judge the whole trade against that question and feel with the enslaved people themselves — each one a person with a home, a name and a family.
