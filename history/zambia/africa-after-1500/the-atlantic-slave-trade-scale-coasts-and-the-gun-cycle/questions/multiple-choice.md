# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What question does the book use to open this lesson?
a) "Can a human being own another?"  b) "Who owns the fastest ship?"  c) "Where is the richest coast?"  d) "How much does a worker cost?"
**Answer: a) "Can a human being own another?"** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened to Africans taken in the transatlantic slave trade?
a) they were paid well and sent home  b) they were taken, sold and worked without pay  c) they sailed as passengers by choice  d) they were hired for one season only
**Answer: b) they were taken, sold and worked without pay** — That is what this lesson teaches.
