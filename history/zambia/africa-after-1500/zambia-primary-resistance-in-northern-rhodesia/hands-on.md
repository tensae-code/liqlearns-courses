# Hands-on — Zambia: Primary Resistance in Northern Rhodesia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the chiefs' refusal out loud again: "No — not the tax, not the labour draft, not our land." Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw a simple map of Northern Rhodesia. Mark Mwinilunga, the Gwembe valley, the Luapula Valley and Ngoni country, and beside each mark write who refused there and what they refused — Kasanza's Lunda and the tax, 1907; the Gwembe Tonga and the tax, 1909; Mpezeni I's Ngoni in arms, 1897; Mwata Kazembe in defence of his kingdom. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see people refuse something together, note it: who refused, what they refused, and what the lesson would call it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
