# Zambia: Primary Resistance in Northern Rhodesia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two shapes the refusal took in primary resistance.
**Answer:** Any two of: tax refusal; refusal of forced labour; armed resistance; defence of land and of chiefs' authority; defence of chitemene farming; building independent churches.

**2.** What were the armed fighters defending, according to the book?
**Answer:** Their land, the authority of their chiefs, and their own way of farming — chitemene.
