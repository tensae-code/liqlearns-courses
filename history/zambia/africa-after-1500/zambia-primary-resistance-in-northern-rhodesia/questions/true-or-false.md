# Zambia: Primary Resistance in Northern Rhodesia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Ngoni under Mpezeni I fought armed resistance in 1897.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Primary resistance was organised by national political parties with newspapers.
**Answer: False — it was local and chief-led, village by village, before parties existed.**
