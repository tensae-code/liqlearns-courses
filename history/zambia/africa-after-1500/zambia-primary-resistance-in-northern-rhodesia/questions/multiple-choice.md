# Zambia: Primary Resistance in Northern Rhodesia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does Zambia's book call the first, local, chief-led stage of resistance?
a) primary resistance  b) secondary resistance  c) the Cha Cha Cha campaign  d) the Federation
**Answer: a) primary resistance** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which people refused the tax at Mwinilunga in 1907, under Chief Kasanza?
a) the Ngoni  b) the Bisa  c) the Lunda  d) the Gwembe Tonga
**Answer: c) the Lunda** — That is what this lesson teaches.
