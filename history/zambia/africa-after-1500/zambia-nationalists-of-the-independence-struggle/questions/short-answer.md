# Zambia: Nationalists of the Independence Struggle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three of the leading nationalists and say what Kenneth Kaunda became in 1964. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any three of: Dauti Yamba, Godwin Mbikusita Lewanika, Harry Mwaanga Nkumbula, Kenneth Kaunda, Simon Mwansa Kapwepwe, Julia Mulenga Nsofwa (Mama Chikamoneka), Chibesa Kankasa (Mama Kankasa), Mainza Chona. Kaunda became the first president of independent Zambia in 1964.

**2.** Who were the Mposa Mabwe, and why does the book honour them? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The stone throwers of Kitwe — boys who threw stones at colonial power; the book honours their courage as part of what sped the British toward the exit.
