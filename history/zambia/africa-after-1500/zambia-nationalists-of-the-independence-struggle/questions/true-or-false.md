# Zambia: Nationalists of the Independence Struggle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Julia Mulenga Nsofwa, Mama Chikamoneka, and Chibesa Kankasa, Mama Kankasa, were women organisers in the front company of the struggle. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The book teaches that only the leaders mattered; the other fighters are not named. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the book names the other freedom fighters too, down to the Mposa Mabwe stone throwers of Kitwe.**
