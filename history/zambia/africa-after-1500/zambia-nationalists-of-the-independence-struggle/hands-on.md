# Hands-on — Zambia: Nationalists of the Independence Struggle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the demand out loud again — "Majority rule" — and then say one leader's name and one fighter's name. Hold both companies in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and draw the two companies as two rows. In the front row write the leading nationalists, with their honoured names for the two women organisers; in the row behind, write the other freedom fighters, and draw the Freedom Statue's broken chain above Mutembo's name. Say each name aloud as you write it — the book teaches these names to be spoken.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time a well-known name rests on the work of people nobody names, note it: the famous name, the unnamed workers, and what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
