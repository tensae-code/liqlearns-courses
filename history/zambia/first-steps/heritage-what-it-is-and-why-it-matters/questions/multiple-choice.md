# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one-minute Salute Wall?
a) a wall where learners write exam answers  b) a wall of the heroes' names and faces, honoured by the class for one minute  c) a wall for posting school rules  d) a minute of running along a wall
**Answer: b) a wall of the heroes' names and faces, honoured by the class for one minute** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does honouring the nationalists build, according to the book?
a) unity, identity and responsibility  b) mines, roads and railways  c) taxes and passes  d) nothing — it is only ceremony
**Answer: a) unity, identity and responsibility** — That is what this lesson teaches.
