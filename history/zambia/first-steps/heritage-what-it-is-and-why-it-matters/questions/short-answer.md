# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why must each new generation choose to remember?
**Answer:** Because heroes cannot keep their own names alive — memory is a choice each generation makes again, or the names and sacrifices are lost.

**2.** Name the book's two ways of demonstrating respect in this lesson.
**Answer:** Role play — standing in the nationalists' place and retelling the struggle — and the one-minute Salute Wall, where the class honours the heroes' names and faces together for one minute.
