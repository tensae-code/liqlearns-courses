# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A country's heroes stay alive by themselves, whether or not anyone remembers them.
**Answer: False — the book teaches they stay alive only if each new generation chooses to remember their names and sacrifices.**

**2.** Role play is one of the book's ways of demonstrating respect to nationalists and freedom fighters.
**Answer: True**
