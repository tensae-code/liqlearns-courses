# Video script — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Heroes cannot keep their own names alive. Only the next generation can — and Zambia's book teaches its children how.

Look at the classroom. On the wall, the names and faces go up: the nationalists and freedom fighters, the whole company of them. The class rises. For one minute — the Salute Wall minute — the room is still, and remembering is something the children are doing, not just hearing about.

Now watch the other way the book teaches: role play. A child stands as Kaunda. Another organises as Mama Chikamoneka. The struggle is learned from inside the people who carried it.

Why does the book bother? It tells you: honouring them builds unity, identity and responsibility. A country that remembers together stands together.

Remember it this way: heroes stay alive only where a new generation chooses to remember. The choosing is the lesson.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
