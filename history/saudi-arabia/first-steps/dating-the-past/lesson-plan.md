# Lesson plan — Dating the Past

**Course:** History — Saudi Arabia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Dating the Past
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** events belong in their true order, and a timeline is the drawing of that order — shown on Saudi history itself: the unification of the Kingdom (1351هـ), the death of King Abdulaziz (1373هـ), and King Salman's accession (1436هـ).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past — it stands in the book's own order in this course (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. - Go back to the one thing: events belong in their true order, and a timeline is the drawing of that order — shown on Saudi history itself: the unification of the Kingdom (1351هـ), the death of King Abdulaziz (1373هـ), and King Salman's accession (1436هـ).
