# Video script — Dating the Past

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Events belong in their true order, and a timeline is the drawing of that order — shown on saudi history itself: the unification of the kingdom (1351هـ), the death of king abdulaziz (1373هـ), and king salman's accession (1436هـ).

A pile of events is not yet a story. The events must stand in their true order — chronological order — and the drawing of that order is a timeline: a line along which each event sits at its own date, so a child can walk along it from the earliest to the latest.

The book builds its timeline from Saudi history itself, with three dates a child can hold.

First, 1351هـ: the unification of the Kingdom — the lands of the peninsula brought together as one Kingdom of Saudi Arabia under King Abdulaziz.

Then, 1373هـ: the death of King Abdulaziz, the founder who had unified the country — the timeline moves past the founder's own lifetime.

Then, 1436هـ: the accession of King Salman — the line reaches the present king.

Three dates on one line, and the pile becomes a path: unification first, the founder's death second, the present king third. Any new event the child meets can be placed on the line — before, between, or after — and the story grows without ever losing its order.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
