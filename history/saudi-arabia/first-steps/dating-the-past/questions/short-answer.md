# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Put the lesson's three dates in order and say what each one is.
**Answer:** 1351هـ — the unification of the Kingdom; 1373هـ — the death of King Abdulaziz; 1436هـ — King Salman's accession.

**2.** Why does putting events in chronological order help?
**Answer:** It turns a pile of events into a story you can walk along, and any new event can be placed before, between or after the ones already on the line.
