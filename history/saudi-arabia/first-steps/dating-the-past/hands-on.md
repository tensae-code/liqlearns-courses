# Hands-on — Dating the Past

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take three things that happened in your own life — starting school, a family journey, today. Put them in the order they really happened, oldest first. Say out loud: "Order turns happenings into a story." This lesson builds that order for a whole kingdom. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the lesson's key things — timeline, chronological order, 1351هـ, 1373هـ, 1436هـ, unification, King Abdulaziz, King Salman — and say each one aloud as you place it, in the order the lesson gives them.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you meet an example of it, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
