# From Evidence to a Written History — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: before history can be written, it must be proved — from primary sources (antiquities and monuments, accounts by people who saw the event, books written at the time, photographs, letters and documents) rather than secondary sources, and false news must be refused — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
