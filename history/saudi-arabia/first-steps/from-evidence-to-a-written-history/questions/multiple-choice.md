# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a primary source?
a) a letter written by a person at the time of the event  b) a story written a hundred years later  c) a rumour with no source  d) a guess about the past
**Answer: a) a letter written by a person at the time of the event** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What must be done with false news about the past?
a) it must be refused — a claim with no source is not history  b) it must be repeated until people believe it  c) it must be written down first  d) it does not matter
**Answer: a) it must be refused — a claim with no source is not history** — That is what this lesson teaches.
