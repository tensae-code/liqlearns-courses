# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three primary sources named in this lesson.
**Answer:** Antiquities and monuments, accounts by people who saw the event, books written at the time, photographs, letters and documents — any three.

**2.** Why does a historian check a secondary source against primary sources?
**Answer:** Because a secondary source was written later, one step away from the event; the primary traces are the proof it must agree with.
