# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four sites of this lesson with their places.
**Answer:** The Rajajil columns at Sakaka; al-Hijr at al-Ula; al-Ukhdud at Najran; Juwatha Mosque at al-Ahsa.

**2.** How are the Kingdom's artefacts guarded, according to this lesson?
**Answer:** The National Museum in Riyadh keeps them, and exhibitions carry them to the people.
