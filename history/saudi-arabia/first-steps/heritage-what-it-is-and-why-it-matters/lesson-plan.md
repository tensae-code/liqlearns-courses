# Lesson plan — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

**Course:** History — Saudi Arabia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Kingdom's antiquities — the Rajajil columns at Sakaka, al-Hijr at al-Ula, al-Ukhdud at Najran and Juwatha Mosque at al-Ahsa — are national treasure a child must be able to find on the map, guarded by the National Museum in Riyadh and its exhibitions.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past — it stands in the book's own order in this course (recommended, never forced).
