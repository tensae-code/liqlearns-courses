# Cause and Effect in History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In history, a cause is…
a) what led to an event and pushed it into being  b) anything that happened on the same day  c) the name of the place  d) a story with no proof
**Answer: a) what led to an event and pushed it into being** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** A result of an event is…
a) what followed from it and changed because it happened  b) what happened long before it  c) the historian's question  d) a primary source
**Answer: a) what followed from it and changed because it happened** — That is what this lesson teaches.
