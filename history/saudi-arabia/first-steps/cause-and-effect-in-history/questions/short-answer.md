# Cause and Effect in History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How do you tell a cause from a result?
**Answer:** A cause came before the event and pushed it into being; a result came after the event and followed from it.

**2.** Why does sorting causes and results make a story easier to understand?
**Answer:** Because the events become a chain — each one pushing the next — instead of a pile of happenings with no links between them.
