# Video script — Cause and Effect in History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every historical event has causes that led to it and results that followed it — nothing in the past just happened.

Nothing in the past just happened. Every historical event stands between two sets of things: the causes that led to it, and the results that followed from it.

A cause is what pushed the event into being — the reasons, pressures and happenings before it without which it would not have taken place, or not in that way. A result is what the event pushed in turn — what changed because it happened, for the people in it and for those who came after.

The book practises this the way historians do: take an event, and sort. What came before it and led to it? What came after it and followed from it? Children choose examples and sort them, cause on one side, result on the other, until the sorting becomes a habit of mind.

With that habit, a story stops being a list of happenings. It becomes a chain — each link pushing the next — and a chain can be understood, questioned and remembered in a way a pile of happenings never can.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
