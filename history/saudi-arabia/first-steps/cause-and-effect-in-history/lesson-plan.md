# Lesson plan — Cause and Effect in History

**Course:** History — Saudi Arabia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Cause and Effect in History
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every historical event has causes that led to it and results that followed it — nothing in the past just happened.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past — it stands in the book's own order in this course (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls everything a cause → ask the order question: did it come before the event and push it, or after it because of it? Before and pushing = cause; after and pushed = result. A middle event can be both.
- Go back to the one thing: every historical event has causes that led to it and results that followed it — nothing in the past just happened. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
