# Hands-on — Cause and Effect in History

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Push one domino and watch the next ones fall. The first fall caused the second, and the second became the cause of the third. Say out loud: "In history too, things push other things." This lesson teaches you to find the pushes. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the lesson's key things — cause, effect, result, chain of events — and say each one aloud as you place it, in the order the lesson gives them.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you meet an example of it, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
