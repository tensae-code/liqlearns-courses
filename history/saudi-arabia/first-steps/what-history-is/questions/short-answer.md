# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three of the historian's questions.
**Answer:** When did it happen, what happened, how, who took part, why, and what came of it — any three of these.

**2.** Why does the book teach children to be proud of Saudi history?
**Answer:** Because Saudi Arabia's past shaped the country they live in now; studying it shows how the present was made and how it reaches into the future.
