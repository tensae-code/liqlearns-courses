# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is history, as this lesson teaches it?
a) the study of past events and their effect on present and future  b) a book of made-up tales  c) only the names of kings  d) only dates to memorise
**Answer: a) the study of past events and their effect on present and future** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is one of the historian's questions?
a) why did it happen?  b) what is for lunch?  c) how tall is the school?  d) who won the game today?
**Answer: a) why did it happen?** — That is what this lesson teaches.
