# Hands-on — Faiths That Shaped States

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Some stories are set in faraway lands you will never see. The stories in this lesson are set, in part, on your own ground — a valley near Tabuk, two hills in Makkah. Say out loud: "The oldest stories and my own map can be the same map." Walk into them. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the lesson's key things — Adam, Noah, Messengers of Strong Resolve, Ibrahim, Hajar, Safa, Marwa, Musa, Madyan, Tabuk, Isa — and say each one aloud as you place it, in the order the lesson gives them.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you meet an example of it, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
