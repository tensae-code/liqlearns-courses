# Faiths That Shaped States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did Ibrahim do to his people's idols, and what did it show?
**Answer:** He smashed them, showing their powerlessness — the idols could not protect even themselves.

**2.** Which two places in the child's own country does this lesson pin the prophets' stories to?
**Answer:** Madyan near Tabuk, where Musa fled from Pharaoh; and Safa and Marwa in Makkah, where Hajar searched for water.
