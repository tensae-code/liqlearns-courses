# Lesson plan — Faiths That Shaped States

**Course:** History — Saudi Arabia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Faiths That Shaped States
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the prophets' stories cross the child's own map — Adam and Noah, and the Messengers of Strong Resolve: Ibrahim who smashed his people's idols, Musa who fled Pharaoh to Madyan near Tabuk, and Isa and his miracles — with Hajar's search for water between Safa and Marwa re-enacted at every Hajj.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past — it stands in the book's own order in this course (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
