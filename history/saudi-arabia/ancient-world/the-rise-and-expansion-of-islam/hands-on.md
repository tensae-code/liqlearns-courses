# Hands-on — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A compass always points somewhere. For a whole world faith, the pointing is toward one city in this lesson's own country. Say out loud: "Two cities can become the centre of a world." This lesson tells how. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the lesson's key things — Makkah, Kaaba, qibla, Madinah, Prophet Muhammad, migration, Prophet's Mosque, Masjid al-Qiblatain — and say each one aloud as you place it, in the order the lesson gives them.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you meet an example of it, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
