# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Saudi Arabia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Makkah and Madinah — the Kaaba's city, and the city to which the Prophet Muhammad migrated and from which Islam spread — became the compass points of a world faith, with Masjid al-Qiblatain marking where the direction of prayer turned to the Kaaba.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past — it stands in the book's own order in this course (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the two cities up → Makkah holds the Kaaba and is the qibla, the direction of prayer. Madinah is where the Prophet migrated, spread Islam, and where his Mosque and tomb are. Direction: Makkah. Migration: Madinah.
- Go back to the one thing: Makkah and Madinah — the Kaaba's city, and the city to which the Prophet Muhammad migrated and from which Islam spread — became the compass points of a world faith, with Masjid al-Qiblatain marking where the direction of prayer turned to the Kaaba. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
