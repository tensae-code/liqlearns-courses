# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happened at Masjid al-Qiblatain?
**Answer:** There the direction of prayer — the qibla — turned to the Kaaba in Makkah.

**2.** Why do Makkah and Madinah matter together in this lesson?
**Answer:** Makkah holds the Kaaba, the qibla of the Muslims; Madinah received the Prophet's migration, became the city from which he spread Islam, and holds his Mosque and tomb — together they are the compass points of the faith.
