# The Arabian Peninsula before Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Before Islam, the peninsula was…
a) a tribal land with no single state  b) one united kingdom  c) ruled from Egypt  d) empty of towns
**Answer: a) a tribal land with no single state** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Makkah's place in the economy was…
a) the meeting point of the caravan routes  b) a farming village only  c) a port for ships from China  d) the capital of a single state
**Answer: a) the meeting point of the caravan routes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
