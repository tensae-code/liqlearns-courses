# The Arabian Peninsula before Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three praised values of the land before Islam.
**Answer:** Courage, generosity, and protecting the neighbour.

**2.** What economic life does the lesson describe besides caravans?
**Answer:** Farming in Yathrib, Taif and Yamama, and herding — alongside the caravan trade through Makkah, the winter and summer journeys, and Silk Road goods from China passing toward Europe.
