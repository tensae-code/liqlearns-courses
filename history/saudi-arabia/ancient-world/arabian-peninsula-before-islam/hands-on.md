# Hands-on — The Arabian Peninsula before Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a land of many towns and tribes, each guarding its own ground, with rich caravans crossing between them — and no single ruler over it all. Say out loud: "Rich and brave, but not yet one." That is the land in this lesson. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the lesson's key things — Tasm, Jadis, tribes, courage, generosity, protecting the neighbour, Makkah, caravans, Khadija bint Khuwaylid, Yathrib, Taif, Yamama — and say each one aloud as you place it, in the order the lesson gives them.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you meet an example of it, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
