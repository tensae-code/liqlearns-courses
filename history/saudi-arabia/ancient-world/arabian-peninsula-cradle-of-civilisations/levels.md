# The Arabian Peninsula, Cradle of Civilisations — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: long before Islam, the Arabian Peninsula was already the Arabs' homeland and a cradle of civilisations — 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans and Kinda — a bridge between Egypt and Iraq, the birthplace of the Arabic language, its peoples writing their history in rock inscriptions — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
