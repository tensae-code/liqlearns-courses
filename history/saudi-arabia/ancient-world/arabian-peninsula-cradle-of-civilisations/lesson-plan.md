# Lesson plan — The Arabian Peninsula, Cradle of Civilisations

**Course:** History — Saudi Arabia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Arabian Peninsula, Cradle of Civilisations
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** long before Islam, the Arabian Peninsula was already the Arabs' homeland and a cradle of civilisations — 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans and Kinda — a bridge between Egypt and Iraq, the birthplace of the Arabic language, its peoples writing their history in rock inscriptions.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past — it stands in the book's own order in this course (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. - Go back to the one thing: long before Islam, the Arabian Peninsula was already the Arabs' homeland and a cradle of civilisations — 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans and Kinda — a bridge between Egypt and Iraq, the birthplace of the Arabic language, its peoples writing their history in rock inscriptions.
