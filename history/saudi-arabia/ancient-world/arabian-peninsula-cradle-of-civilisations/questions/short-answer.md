# The Arabian Peninsula, Cradle of Civilisations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three of the ancient peoples or kingdoms of the peninsula in this lesson.
**Answer:** 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans and Kinda — any three.

**2.** Why was the peninsula a bridge, and between what?
**Answer:** Its place made it a crossing between Egypt on one side and Iraq on the other, so goods, ideas and travellers passed through it.
