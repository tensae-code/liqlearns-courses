# The Arabian Peninsula, Cradle of Civilisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The book calls the Arabian Peninsula a cradle of…
a) civilisations  b) deserts only  c) sea trade only  d) modern cities
**Answer: a) civilisations** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did the peninsula's ancient peoples write their history?
a) in rock inscriptions carved into stone  b) in printed books  c) on paper letters  d) they never wrote it
**Answer: a) in rock inscriptions carved into stone** — That is what this lesson teaches.
