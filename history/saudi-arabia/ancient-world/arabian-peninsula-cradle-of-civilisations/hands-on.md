# Hands-on — The Arabian Peninsula, Cradle of Civilisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Scratch words into a rock, and the rock may still be telling them thousands of years later. The peoples of this lesson left few books — but they left rocks. Say out loud: "A land can be old long before its famous days." This lesson walks into that old land. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the lesson's key things — Arabian Peninsula, cradle of civilisations, 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, Nabataeans, Kinda, rock inscriptions, Arabic language — and say each one aloud as you place it, in the order the lesson gives them.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you meet an example of it, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
