# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia's Unification under Abdulaziz

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine you must carry a message that a whole kingdom depends on, along a road where enemies are watching for exactly such a message. Hold your nerve. Say out loud: "Uniting a country is one task; holding it together is another." This story shows both. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the lesson's key things — King Abdulaziz, unification, secret letter, brave courier, Stories I Belong To — and say each one aloud as you place it, in the order the lesson gives them.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you meet an example of it, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
