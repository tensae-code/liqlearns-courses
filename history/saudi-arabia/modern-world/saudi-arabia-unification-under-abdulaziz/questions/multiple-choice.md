# Saudi Arabia's Unification under Abdulaziz — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the story, what must King Abdulaziz get to his men in another city?
a) a secret letter  b) a new flag  c) a load of dates  d) a map of Egypt
**Answer: a) a secret letter** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is the road dangerous for the courier?
a) enemies watch the road between the cities  b) the road is flooded  c) there is no road at all  d) the letter is too heavy
**Answer: a) enemies watch the road between the cities** — That is what this lesson teaches.
