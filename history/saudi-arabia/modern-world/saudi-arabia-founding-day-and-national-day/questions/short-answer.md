# Saudi Arabia's Founding Day and National Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the difference between Founding Day and National Day?
**Answer:** Founding Day marks Imam Muhammad bin Saud founding the Saudi state in 1727 (1139هـ); National Day marks King Abdulaziz unifying the country in 1932 (1351هـ).

**2.** What does Flag Day mark?
**Answer:** The day King Abdulaziz approved the form of the flag that the unified country would fly.
