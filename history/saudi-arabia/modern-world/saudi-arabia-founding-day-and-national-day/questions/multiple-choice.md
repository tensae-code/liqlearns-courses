# Saudi Arabia's Founding Day and National Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Founding Day marks the founding of the Saudi state in which year?
a) 1727  b) 1932  c) 1970  d) 1436
**Answer: a) 1727** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who unified the country in 1932, the event National Day marks?
a) King Abdulaziz  b) Imam Muhammad bin Saud  c) King Salman  d) a brave courier
**Answer: a) King Abdulaziz** — That is what this lesson teaches.
