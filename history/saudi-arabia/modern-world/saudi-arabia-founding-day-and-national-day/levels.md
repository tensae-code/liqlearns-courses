# Saudi Arabia's Founding Day and National Day — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Saudi Arabia keeps two founding dates: Founding Day, when Imam Muhammad bin Saud founded the Saudi state in 1727 (1139هـ), and National Day, when King Abdulaziz unified the country in 1932 (1351هـ) — with Flag Day marking when Abdulaziz approved the flag's form — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
