# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia's Founding Day and National Day

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your family may keep two special days: the day something began, and the day it became complete. A country can keep two such days too. Say out loud: "One day for the beginning, one day for the uniting." This lesson gives you both days. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw or lay out the lesson's key things — Founding Day, 1727, 1139هـ, Imam Muhammad bin Saud, National Day, 1932, 1351هـ, King Abdulaziz, Flag Day — and say each one aloud as you place it, in the order the lesson gives them.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you meet an example of it, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
