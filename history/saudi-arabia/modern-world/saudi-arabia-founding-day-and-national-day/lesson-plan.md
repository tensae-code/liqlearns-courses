# Lesson plan — Saudi Arabia's Founding Day and National Day

**Course:** History — Saudi Arabia · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Saudi Arabia's Founding Day and National Day
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Saudi Arabia keeps two founding dates: Founding Day, when Imam Muhammad bin Saud founded the Saudi state in 1727 (1139هـ), and National Day, when King Abdulaziz unified the country in 1932 (1351هـ) — with Flag Day marking when Abdulaziz approved the flag's form.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps into Saudi Arabia's Past — it stands in the book's own order in this course (recommended, never forced).

- Mixes the two days up → founding came first: Imam Muhammad bin Saud founded the state in 1727 (1139هـ). Unification came in 1932 (1351هـ) under King Abdulaziz. One began the state; the other made it one kingdom.
- Go back to the one thing: Saudi Arabia keeps two founding dates: Founding Day, when Imam Muhammad bin Saud founded the Saudi state in 1727 (1139هـ), and National Day, when King Abdulaziz unified the country in 1932 (1351هـ) — with Flag Day marking when Abdulaziz approved the flag's form.
