# History — Saudi Arabia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Saudi Arabia's
own — built from Saudi Arabia's Social Studies book for
Grade 4 (student book, Arabic, unabridged full-year edition).
Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not
grades**: the titles below sit inside Level 1, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grade the
book comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: method first (what
history is, sources, cause and effect, timelines), then the
nation's days and its unification story, then the deep past
of the peninsula, its holy cities and its antiquities, and
last the prophets' stories on the child's own map. That
ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints
point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — first steps into Saudi Arabia's past** (Social
Studies, Grade 4, student book):

- `first-steps/` — Level 1, first title in path order (with
  the heritage lesson closing the level's walk, in the
  placements file's order). What History Is (history as the
  study of past events, and the historian's six questions);
  From Evidence to a Written History (primary sources —
  antiquities, witnesses, books of the time, photographs,
  letters and documents — against secondary sources, and the
  duty to refuse false news); Cause and Effect in History
  (every event has causes that led to it and results that
  followed); Dating the Past (the timeline built from the
  unification 1351هـ, the death of King Abdulaziz 1373هـ, and
  King Salman's accession 1436هـ); and Heritage: What It Is
  and Why It Matters (the Rajajil columns at Sakaka, al-Hijr
  at al-Ula, al-Ukhdud at Najran, Juwatha Mosque at al-Ahsa,
  and the National Museum in Riyadh).
- `modern-world/` — Level 1, second title. Saudi Arabia's
  Unification under Abdulaziz (the "Brave Man" story: a
  secret letter carried past watching enemies so the unified
  country could act as one); and Saudi Arabia's Founding Day
  and National Day (Imam Muhammad bin Saud founds the state
  in 1727, 1139هـ; King Abdulaziz unifies the country in
  1932, 1351هـ; and Flag Day).
- `ancient-world/` — Level 1, third title. The Arabian
  Peninsula, Cradle of Civilisations ('Ad, Thamud, Madyan,
  al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans and Kinda; the bridge
  between Egypt and Iraq; the birthplace of Arabic; history
  in rock inscriptions); The Arabian Peninsula before Islam
  (Tasm and Jadis, tribal wars, courage, generosity and
  protecting the neighbour, Makkah's caravans and Khadija
  bint Khuwaylid); The Rise and Expansion of Islam (Makkah
  and the Kaaba, Madinah of the Prophet's migration, Mosque
  and tomb, and Masjid al-Qiblatain); and Faiths That Shaped
  States (Adam and Noah, and the Messengers of Strong
  Resolve — Ibrahim, Musa and Isa — with Madyan near Tabuk
  and Hajar's search between Safa and Marwa).
