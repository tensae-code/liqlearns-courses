# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Civilización egipcia — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Egipto — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Pharaoh Menes unifies the Nile peoples about 3100 BCE; the river's flood calendar of inundation, sowing and harvest orders Egyptian life under a god-king.
b) The Nile strip lives by its floods; Theban princes reunify the country, expel the Hyksos and conquer to Syria and Nubia under Thutmose III and Ramses II before Assyrians and Cambyses end the empire.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
