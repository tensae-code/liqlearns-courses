# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Civilización egipcia", Pharaoh Menes unifies the Nile peoples about 3100 BCE; the river's flood calendar of inundation, sowing and harvest orders Egyptian life under a god-king (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Egipto", the Nile strip lives by its floods; Theban princes reunify the country, expel the Hyksos and conquer to Syria and Nubia under Thutmose III and Ramses II before Assyrians and Cambyses end the empire (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
