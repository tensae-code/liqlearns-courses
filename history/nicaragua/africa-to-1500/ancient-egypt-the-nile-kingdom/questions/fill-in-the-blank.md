# Ancient egypt the nile kingdom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Civilización egipcia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pharaoh Menes unifies the Nile peoples about 3100 BCE; the river's flood calendar of inundation, sowing and harvest orders Egyptian life under a god-king.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Egipto" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Nile strip lives by its floods; Theban princes reunify the country, expel the Hyksos and conquer to Syria and Nubia under Thutmose III and Ramses II before Assyrians and Cambyses end the empire.
