# From stone tools to farming — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "El Neolítico y la revolución agrícola", people notice a buried seed sprouts; farming and herding make villages sedentary, populations grow and work specialises (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La Edad de los Metales", copper, then harder bronze, then iron give farming and war stronger tools, just as the first civilisations rise (Sub lesson 4)
