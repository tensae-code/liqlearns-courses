# From stone tools to farming — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Edad de Piedra: Paleolítico y Neolítico" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Hunter-gatherer nomads in bands of twenty to fifty paint cave walls and worship fertility; farming then turns predators into producers, villages appear, surpluses are bartered and population grows.

**2.** Tell the story of "Edad de los Metales: cobre, bronce y hierro" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Soft copper gives way to hard bronze, and the Hittites guard the secret of iron — a metal that abounds everywhere — until it remakes tools and war; megaliths like Stonehenge rise in the same age.
