# From stone tools to farming — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La comunidad primitiva" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Nomad gatherers, hunters and fishers share tools and divide the catch equally with no classes and no surplus; Neolithic farming settles them, the surplus is born, barter and merchants follow.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Formas de vida durante el Paleolítico" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Nomad hunter-gatherers master fire and stone tools as Australopithecus gives way through Homo habilis and erectus to Neanderthals and Homo sapiens.
