# Nicaragua literacy crusade 1980 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Efemérides: anniversary of the Gran Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 1980 literacy crusade "Heroes and Martyrs for the Liberation of Nicaragua" teaches the country to read, closing officially 23 August.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Logros sociales: alfabetización y salud" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Adult education reaches 140,000 newly literate people a year, Río San Juan is declared free of illiteracy in 1987 winning the Krupskaya medal, and the school milk programme feeds 200,000 children.
