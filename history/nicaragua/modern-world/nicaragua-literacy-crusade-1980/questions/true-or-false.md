# Nicaragua literacy crusade 1980 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Logros de la Revolución: la Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización", over 100,000 mostly young literacy volunteers cut illiteracy from 50.3 percent to 12.9 percent in five months of 1980, teaching also in the Caribbean coast's own languages (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Efemérides: anniversary of the Gran Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización", the 1980 literacy crusade "Heroes and Martyrs for the Liberation of Nicaragua" teaches the country to read, closing officially 23 August (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
