# Nicaragua literacy crusade 1980 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Logros sociales: alfabetización y salud" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Adult education reaches 140,000 newly literate people a year, Río San Juan is declared free of illiteracy in 1987 winning the Krupskaya medal, and the school milk programme feeds 200,000 children.

**2.** Tell the story of "Logros de la Revolución: la Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Over 100,000 mostly young literacy volunteers cut illiteracy from 50.3 percent to 12.9 percent in five months of 1980, teaching also in the Caribbean coast's own languages.
