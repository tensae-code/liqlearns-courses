# Video script — Nicaragua literacy crusade 1980

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Nicaragua literacy crusade 1980.

The 1980 literacy crusade "Heroes and Martyrs for the Liberation of Nicaragua" teaches the country to read, closing officially 23 August.

Adult education reaches 140,000 newly literate people a year, Río San Juan is declared free of illiteracy in 1987 winning the Krupskaya medal, and the school milk programme feeds 200,000 children.

Over 100,000 mostly young literacy volunteers cut illiteracy from 50.3 percent to 12.9 percent in five months of 1980, teaching also in the Caribbean coast's own languages.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
