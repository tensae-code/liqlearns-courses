# The english revolution — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La Revolución Inglesa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A republic is proclaimed but Cromwell rules as military dictator for nine years, imposing puritanism and the 1651 Navigation Act against Dutch trade and subjecting Catholic Ireland to cruel domination.
