# The second world war the pacific and the results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Segunda Guerra Mundial: consecuencias" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fifty to sixty million die, the Holocaust and the atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki mark the war, Europe declines and the US and USSR emerge as the two superpowers.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Segunda Guerra Mundial y sus efectos en América" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The war's end is taught through its American settlement — a new world order to map, and the question of what the Somoza dictatorship gained from the war.
