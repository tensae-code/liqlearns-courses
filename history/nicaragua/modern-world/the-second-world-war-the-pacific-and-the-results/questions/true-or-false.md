# The second world war the pacific and the results — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Segunda Guerra Mundial: consecuencias", fifty to sixty million die, the Holocaust and the atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki mark the war, Europe declines and the US and USSR emerge as the two superpowers (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La Segunda Guerra Mundial y sus efectos en América", the war's end is taught through its American settlement — a new world order to map, and the question of what the Somoza dictatorship gained from the war (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
