# The second world war the pacific and the results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Segunda Guerra Mundial: consecuencias" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Fifty to sixty million die, the Holocaust and the atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki mark the war, Europe declines and the US and USSR emerge as the two superpowers.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Segunda Guerra Mundial y sus efectos en América" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The war's end is taught through its American settlement — a new world order to map, and the question of what the Somoza dictatorship gained from the war.
