# Video script — The second world war the pacific and the results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The second world war the pacific and the results.

Fifty to sixty million die, the Holocaust and the atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki mark the war, Europe declines and the US and USSR emerge as the two superpowers.

The war's end is taught through its American settlement — a new world order to map, and the question of what the Somoza dictatorship gained from the war.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
