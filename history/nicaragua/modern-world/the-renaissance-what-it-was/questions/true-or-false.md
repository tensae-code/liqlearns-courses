# The renaissance what it was — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "El Renacimiento", in Florence, Milan, Rome and Venice the classical past is dug out of Rome's own ruins and reborn — though the change touches the upper classes while most lives stay medieval (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "El Renacimiento", in 15th-century Italy the human being moves to the centre of art and thought, and Greek and Roman culture is reborn across Europe (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
