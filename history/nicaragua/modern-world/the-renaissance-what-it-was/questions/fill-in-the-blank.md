# The renaissance what it was — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "El Renacimiento" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In 15th-century Italy the human being moves to the centre of art and thought, and Greek and Roman culture is reborn across Europe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Edad Moderna" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The modern age is dated from Constantinople's fall in 1453 to the French Revolution of 1789; kings recover power from weakened feudal lords and the modern states — France, England, Spain — are born.
