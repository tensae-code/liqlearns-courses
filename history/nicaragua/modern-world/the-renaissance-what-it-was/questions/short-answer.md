# The renaissance what it was — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La Edad Moderna" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The modern age is dated from Constantinople's fall in 1453 to the French Revolution of 1789; kings recover power from weakened feudal lords and the modern states — France, England, Spain — are born.

**2.** Tell the story of "El Renacimiento" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** In Florence, Milan, Rome and Venice the classical past is dug out of Rome's own ruins and reborn — though the change touches the upper classes while most lives stay medieval.
