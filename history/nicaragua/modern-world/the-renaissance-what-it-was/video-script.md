# Video script — The renaissance what it was

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The renaissance what it was.

In 15th-century Italy the human being moves to the centre of art and thought, and Greek and Roman culture is reborn across Europe.

The modern age is dated from Constantinople's fall in 1453 to the French Revolution of 1789; kings recover power from weakened feudal lords and the modern states — France, England, Spain — are born.

In Florence, Milan, Rome and Venice the classical past is dug out of Rome's own ruins and reborn — though the change touches the upper classes while most lives stay medieval.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
