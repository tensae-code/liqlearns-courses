# Central america conquest and colonisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Conquista y colonización de Centroamérica"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Spanish captains conquer the isthmus province by province and colonisation replaces indigenous rule.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Invasión colonial española en Centroamérica"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The invasion brings extermination, new diseases — syphilis, measles, typhoid — and forced labour to the original peoples.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
