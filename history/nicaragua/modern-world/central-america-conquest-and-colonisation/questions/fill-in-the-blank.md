# Central america conquest and colonisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Primeras ciudades fundadas por los españoles en Centroamérica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** León, Granada and the other first Spanish cities are planted as instruments of rule over conquered peoples.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Conquista de Centroamérica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Scattered peoples without political unity, who nevertheless fight bravely, force the Spaniards into repeated expeditions across the isthmus.
