# Central america conquest and colonisation — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Primeras ciudades fundadas por los españoles en Centroamérica", León, Granada and the other first Spanish cities are planted as instruments of rule over conquered peoples (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Conquista de Centroamérica", scattered peoples without political unity, who nevertheless fight bravely, force the Spaniards into repeated expeditions across the isthmus (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
