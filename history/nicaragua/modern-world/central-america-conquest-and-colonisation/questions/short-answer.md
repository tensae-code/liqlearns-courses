# Central america conquest and colonisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Conquista y colonización de Centroamérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Spanish captains conquer the isthmus province by province and colonisation replaces indigenous rule.

**2.** Tell the story of "Invasión colonial española en Centroamérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The invasion brings extermination, new diseases — syphilis, measles, typhoid — and forced labour to the original peoples.
