# Central america conquest and colonisation

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Central america conquest and colonisation" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conquista y colonización de Centroamérica

Spanish captains conquer the isthmus province by province and colonisation replaces indigenous rule.

## Sub lesson 2 — Invasión colonial española en Centroamérica

The invasion brings extermination, new diseases — syphilis, measles, typhoid — and forced labour to the original peoples.

## Sub lesson 3 — Primeras ciudades fundadas por los españoles en Centroamérica

León, Granada and the other first Spanish cities are planted as instruments of rule over conquered peoples.

## Sub lesson 4 — Conquista de Centroamérica

Scattered peoples without political unity, who nevertheless fight bravely, force the Spaniards into repeated expeditions across the isthmus.
