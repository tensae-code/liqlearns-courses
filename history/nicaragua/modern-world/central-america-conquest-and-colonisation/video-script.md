# Video script — Central america conquest and colonisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Central america conquest and colonisation.

Spanish captains conquer the isthmus province by province and colonisation replaces indigenous rule.

The invasion brings extermination, new diseases — syphilis, measles, typhoid — and forced labour to the original peoples.

León, Granada and the other first Spanish cities are planted as instruments of rule over conquered peoples.

Scattered peoples without political unity, who nevertheless fight bravely, force the Spaniards into repeated expeditions across the isthmus.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
