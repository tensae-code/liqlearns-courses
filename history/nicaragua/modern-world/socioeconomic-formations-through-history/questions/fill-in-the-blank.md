# Socioeconomic formations through history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Formaciones socioeconómicas" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** To understand the present and project the future, society's whole evolution is divided into five formations — primitive community, slavery, feudalism, capitalism and socialism — each defined by who owns the means of production.
