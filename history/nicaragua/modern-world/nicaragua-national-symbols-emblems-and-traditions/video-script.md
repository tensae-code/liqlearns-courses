# Video script — Nicaragua national symbols emblems and traditions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Nicaragua national symbols emblems and traditions.

The homeland is introduced as a mother; land of lakes Cocibolca and Xolotlán and volcanoes, whose people are solidary, hardworking and peace-loving.

Blue-white-blue flag and triangle shield are decoded piece by piece — rainbow is peace, Phrygian cap is liberty, five volcanoes are the five Central American countries united.

Three living emblems are chosen from Nicaragua's own nature and each gets its story and use, the madroño flowering in December for La Purísima altars.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
