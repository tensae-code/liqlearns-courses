# Nicaragua national symbols emblems and traditions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Vivencias y nociones de mi Patria Nicaragua" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The homeland is introduced as a mother; land of lakes Cocibolca and Xolotlán and volcanoes, whose people are solidary, hardworking and peace-loving.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Símbolos Patrios y su significado (flag, shield, anthem)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Blue-white-blue flag and triangle shield are decoded piece by piece — rainbow is peace, Phrygian cap is liberty, five volcanoes are the five Central American countries united.
