# Nicaragua national symbols emblems and traditions — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Emblemas Nacionales (sacuanjoche flower, madroño tree, guardabarranco bird)", three living emblems are chosen from Nicaragua's own nature and each gets its story and use, the madroño flowering in December for La Purísima altars (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Vivencias y nociones de mi Patria Nicaragua", the homeland is introduced as a mother; land of lakes Cocibolca and Xolotlán and volcanoes, whose people are solidary, hardworking and peace-loving (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
