# Nicaragua national symbols emblems and traditions — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Emblemas Nacionales (sacuanjoche flower, madroño tree, guardabarranco bird) — a) (Sub lesson 3)
**2.** Vivencias y nociones de mi Patria Nicaragua — b) (Sub lesson 1)

Meanings:
a) Three living emblems are chosen from Nicaragua's own nature and each gets its story and use, the madroño flowering in December for La Purísima altars.
b) The homeland is introduced as a mother; land of lakes Cocibolca and Xolotlán and volcanoes, whose people are solidary, hardworking and peace-loving.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
