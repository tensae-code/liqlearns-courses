# Nicaragua national symbols emblems and traditions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Vivencias y nociones de mi Patria Nicaragua"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The homeland is introduced as a mother; land of lakes Cocibolca and Xolotlán and volcanoes, whose people are solidary, hardworking and peace-loving.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
