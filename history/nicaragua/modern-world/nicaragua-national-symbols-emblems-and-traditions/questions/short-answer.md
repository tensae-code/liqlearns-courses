# Nicaragua national symbols emblems and traditions — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Símbolos Patrios y su significado (flag, shield, anthem)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Blue-white-blue flag and triangle shield are decoded piece by piece — rainbow is peace, Phrygian cap is liberty, five volcanoes are the five Central American countries united.

**2.** Tell the story of "Emblemas Nacionales (sacuanjoche flower, madroño tree, guardabarranco bird)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Three living emblems are chosen from Nicaragua's own nature and each gets its story and use, the madroño flowering in December for La Purísima altars.
