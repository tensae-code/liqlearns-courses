# Nicaragua history in periods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Periodización de la Historia de Nicaragua" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The national past is divided into pre-Columbian, colonial (1502-1821), state-formation (1821-1893) and modern (1893-1979) periods, each summed up by its defining change.
