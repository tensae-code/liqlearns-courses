# The reformation the catholic response — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Contrarreforma" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Trent answers Luther with the Inquisition revived, an Index of forbidden books and new missionary orders; Ignatius of Loyola's Jesuits carry the Counter-Reformation to Paraguay, India and China.
