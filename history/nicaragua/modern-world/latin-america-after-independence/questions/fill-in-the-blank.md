# Latin america after independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "América Latina después de la Independencia: características generales" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The wars militarise society and ruin the economies; caudillismo rules the century while liberals and conservatives fight over the wreckage.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Conflictos territoriales" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Undefined colonial borders turn republic against republic; Paraguay's war ends in 1870 with its population cut from about 1.5 million to 221,000, and Chile, Bolivia and Peru fight over the Pacific coast.
