# Latin america after independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Conflictos territoriales" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Undefined colonial borders turn republic against republic; Paraguay's war ends in 1870 with its population cut from about 1.5 million to 221,000, and Chile, Bolivia and Peru fight over the Pacific coast.

**2.** Tell the story of "La situación de los esclavos y los indígenas: la abolición" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Independence does not free the enslaved — plantation and mine labour continues, and only Enlightenment ideas and the 1789 Declaration slowly build abolition's case; full liberation is achieved only in Haiti.
