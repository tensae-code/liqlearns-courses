# Latin america after independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "La situación de los esclavos y los indígenas: la abolición", independence does not free the enslaved — plantation and mine labour continues, and only Enlightenment ideas and the 1789 Declaration slowly build abolition's case; full liberation is achieved only in Haiti (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "América Latina después de la Independencia: características generales", the wars militarise society and ruin the economies; caudillismo rules the century while liberals and conservatives fight over the wreckage (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
