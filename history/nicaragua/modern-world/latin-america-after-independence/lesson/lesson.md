# Latin america after independence

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Latin america after independence" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — América Latina después de la Independencia: características generales

The wars militarise society and ruin the economies; caudillismo rules the century while liberals and conservatives fight over the wreckage.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conflictos territoriales

Undefined colonial borders turn republic against republic; Paraguay's war ends in 1870 with its population cut from about 1.5 million to 221,000, and Chile, Bolivia and Peru fight over the Pacific coast.

## Sub lesson 3 — La situación de los esclavos y los indígenas: la abolición

Independence does not free the enslaved — plantation and mine labour continues, and only Enlightenment ideas and the 1789 Declaration slowly build abolition's case; full liberation is achieved only in Haiti.
