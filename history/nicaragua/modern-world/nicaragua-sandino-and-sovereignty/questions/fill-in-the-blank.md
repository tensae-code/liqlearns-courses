# Nicaragua sandino and sovereignty — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Segunda intervención norteamericana y la gesta de Sandino" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Sandino, working in Mexico, hears of the invasion, returns, and on 26 Oct 1926 rises at the San Albino mine rather than lay down arms.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Fundación del Ejército Defensor de la Soberanía Nacional; ventajas guerrilleras; asesinato de Sandino" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Sandino's small army beats the marines by refusing long battles, vanishing on paths only locals know; after the marines leave, Sandino is murdered in 1934.
