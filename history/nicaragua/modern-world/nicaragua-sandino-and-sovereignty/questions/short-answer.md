# Nicaragua sandino and sovereignty — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Personajes históricos: Augusto Nicolás Calderón Sandino" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Sandino, General of Free Men and Women, is retold as the patriot who would not accept foreign occupation.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Gesta de Benjamín Zeledón" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** When Mena is captured Zeledón takes command of the 1912 resistance, holds out at La Barranca and El Coyotepe in Masaya with only courage and love of country, and is captured and killed after an unequal fight.
