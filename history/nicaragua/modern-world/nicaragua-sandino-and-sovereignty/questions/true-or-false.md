# Nicaragua sandino and sovereignty — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Héroes de la Patria: Augusto Nicolás Calderón Sandino", Sandino, born 18 May in Niquinohomo, becomes "General of Free Men and Women"; the book says you cannot speak of Nicaragua or homeland without him (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Héroes de la Patria: Blanca Arauz", Blanca Arauz stands beside Sandino in the defence of national sovereignty, the telegraphist wife who carried the general's messages in wartime (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
