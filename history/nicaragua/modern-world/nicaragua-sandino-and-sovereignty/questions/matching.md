# Nicaragua sandino and sovereignty — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Lucha del General Augusto C. Sandino — a) (Sub lesson 9)
**2.** Asesinato de Sandino — b) (Sub lesson 10)

Meanings:
a) The boy who saw Zeledón's corpse carted past in 1912 refuses the 1927 pact, marries Blanca Aráuz and raises the Army in Defence of National Sovereignty from 29 men and 40 rifles at El Chipote.
b) The marines, unable to beat the EDSN, build a native Guardia; after peace talks with Sacasa, Sandino is seized and murdered on 21 February 1934, and over 300 Sandinista peasants are massacred at Wiwilí.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
