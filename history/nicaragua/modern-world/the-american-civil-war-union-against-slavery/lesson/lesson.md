# The american civil war union against slavery

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The american civil war union against slavery" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

A slaveholding plantation South and an industrial abolitionist North collide under Lincoln; after Bull Run, Antietam and Gettysburg, Lee surrenders at Appomattox and slavery is abolished, though the Ku Klux Klan rises from the resentment.
