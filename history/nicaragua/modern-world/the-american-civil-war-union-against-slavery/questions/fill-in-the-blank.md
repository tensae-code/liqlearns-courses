# The american civil war union against slavery — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Guerra de Secesión (1861-1865)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** A slaveholding plantation South and an industrial abolitionist North collide under Lincoln; after Bull Run, Antietam and Gettysburg, Lee surrenders at Appomattox and slavery is abolished, though the Ku Klux Klan rises from the resentment.
