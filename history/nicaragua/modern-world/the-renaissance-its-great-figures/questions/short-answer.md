# The renaissance its great figures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Humanismo y los grandes del Renacimiento" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raphael, Dante and Shakespeare lead the revival; Erasmus mocks ignorance and Thomas More imagines an ideal society.

**2.** Tell the story of "El Humanismo" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The humanists preach free thought against dogma and nature as the source of science; Erasmus of Rotterdam, a priest, mocks the Church's dignitaries and superstitions in the Praise of Folly.
