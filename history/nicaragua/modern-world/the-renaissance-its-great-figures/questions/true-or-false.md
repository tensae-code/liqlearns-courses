# The renaissance its great figures — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Humanismo y los grandes del Renacimiento", Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raphael, Dante and Shakespeare lead the revival; Erasmus mocks ignorance and Thomas More imagines an ideal society (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "El Humanismo", the humanists preach free thought against dogma and nature as the source of science; Erasmus of Rotterdam, a priest, mocks the Church's dignitaries and superstitions in the Praise of Folly (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
