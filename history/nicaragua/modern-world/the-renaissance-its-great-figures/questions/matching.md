# The renaissance its great figures — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Humanismo y los grandes del Renacimiento — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** El Humanismo — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raphael, Dante and Shakespeare lead the revival; Erasmus mocks ignorance and Thomas More imagines an ideal society.
b) The humanists preach free thought against dogma and nature as the source of science; Erasmus of Rotterdam, a priest, mocks the Church's dignitaries and superstitions in the Praise of Folly.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
