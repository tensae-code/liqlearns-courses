# European colonial domination in the americas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Colonización inglesa: causas y características" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** England's thirteen colonies grow by private initiative and Crown together, a heterogeneous European population under royal governors and elected assemblies.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "El Colonialismo y los Nuevos Estados Europeos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Spain and Portugal absorb indigenous populations into mixed settlements while Britain and France displace them; Britain's strategic colonies from Gibraltar to Hong Kong make it mistress of the sea routes, with India as the prize.
