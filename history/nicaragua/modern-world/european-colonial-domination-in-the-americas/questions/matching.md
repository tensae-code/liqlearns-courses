# European colonial domination in the americas — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Dominación colonial española, portuguesa, inglesa y francesa en América — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Consecuencias de la dominación colonial europea — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Spain and Portugal divide the new continent first, Britain and France follow; each empire rules differently — viceroyalties, sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans, family farms in the Thirteen Colonies.
b) The bill is counted in four columns — economic dependence, lost sovereignty, an indigenous population crashed by epidemics, and mestizaje under an imposed culture and religion.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
