# European colonial domination in the americas — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La Dominación Colonial Europea: colonización española" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Three centuries of rule through viceroys, encomiendas and tribute; the Church evangelises by liquidating indigenous belief while some friars defend the indigenous against the encomenderos' abuses.

**2.** Tell the story of "Colonización portuguesa en América" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Brazil-wood dye and then sugar pull Portugal into Brazil; Souza founds San Vicente in 1532 and fights off French corsairs for the coast.
