# European colonial domination in the americas — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Conquista de América y la Resistencia Indígena: las Antillas y México", conquest is defined by massacre, cruelty and greed; after the Antilles are exhausted, Cortés sails from Cuba in 1519 with 700 men after Aztec gold (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Conquista española en América del Sur", Pizarro exploits the Inca civil war between Huáscar and Atahualpa; German bankers' agents conquer Venezuela brutally on Spain's debts, and Tordesillas hands Brazil to Portugal (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
