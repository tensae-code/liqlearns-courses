# Video script — European colonial domination in the americas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: European colonial domination in the americas.

Spain and Portugal divide the new continent first, Britain and France follow; each empire rules differently — viceroyalties, sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans, family farms in the Thirteen Colonies.

The bill is counted in four columns — economic dependence, lost sovereignty, an indigenous population crashed by epidemics, and mestizaje under an imposed culture and religion.

Conquest is defined by massacre, cruelty and greed; after the Antilles are exhausted, Cortés sails from Cuba in 1519 with 700 men after Aztec gold.

Pizarro exploits the Inca civil war between Huáscar and Atahualpa; German bankers' agents conquer Venezuela brutally on Spain's debts, and Tordesillas hands Brazil to Portugal.

Three centuries of rule through viceroys, encomiendas and tribute; the Church evangelises by liquidating indigenous belief while some friars defend the indigenous against the encomenderos' abuses.

Brazil-wood dye and then sugar pull Portugal into Brazil; Souza founds San Vicente in 1532 and fights off French corsairs for the coast.

England's thirteen colonies grow by private initiative and Crown together, a heterogeneous European population under royal governors and elected assemblies.

Spain and Portugal absorb indigenous populations into mixed settlements while Britain and France displace them; Britain's strategic colonies from Gibraltar to Hong Kong make it mistress of the sea routes, with India as the prize.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
