# Video script — Indigenous resistance in the americas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Indigenous resistance in the americas.

Against taxes, forced labour and abuse, resistance runs from daily refusal to armed rising; Túpac Amaru, Cuauhtémoc and Nicaragua's own Diriangén lead it, and Las Casas denounces the abuses to the Crown.

Enriquillo and Hatuey in the Antilles, Urracá in Panama, Tecum Umán among the Maya and Tenochtitlan's three-month siege symbolise a resistance that never accepted conquest.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
