# Indigenous resistance in the americas — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Resistencia indígena en América — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** La Resistencia Indígena — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Against taxes, forced labour and abuse, resistance runs from daily refusal to armed rising; Túpac Amaru, Cuauhtémoc and Nicaragua's own Diriangén lead it, and Las Casas denounces the abuses to the Crown.
b) Enriquillo and Hatuey in the Antilles, Urracá in Panama, Tecum Umán among the Maya and Tenochtitlan's three-month siege symbolise a resistance that never accepted conquest.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
