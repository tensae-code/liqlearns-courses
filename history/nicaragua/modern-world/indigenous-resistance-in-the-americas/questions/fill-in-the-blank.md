# Indigenous resistance in the americas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Resistencia indígena en América" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Against taxes, forced labour and abuse, resistance runs from daily refusal to armed rising; Túpac Amaru, Cuauhtémoc and Nicaragua's own Diriangén lead it, and Las Casas denounces the abuses to the Crown.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Resistencia Indígena" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Enriquillo and Hatuey in the Antilles, Urracá in Panama, Tecum Umán among the Maya and Tenochtitlan's three-month siege symbolise a resistance that never accepted conquest.
