# Indigenous resistance in the americas — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Resistencia indígena en América", against taxes, forced labour and abuse, resistance runs from daily refusal to armed rising; Túpac Amaru, Cuauhtémoc and Nicaragua's own Diriangén lead it, and Las Casas denounces the abuses to the Crown (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La Resistencia Indígena", Enriquillo and Hatuey in the Antilles, Urracá in Panama, Tecum Umán among the Maya and Tenochtitlan's three-month siege symbolise a resistance that never accepted conquest (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
