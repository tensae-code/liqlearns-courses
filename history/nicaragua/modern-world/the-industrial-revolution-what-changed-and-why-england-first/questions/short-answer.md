# The industrial revolution what changed and why england first — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Revolución Industrial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** In mid-18th-century Britain enclosures, new crops and Watt's steam engine move production from workshops to factories and create the working class.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Revolución Industrial: los nuevos inventos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Watt's 1776 steam engine multiplies one worker's output many times over; textile machines, blast furnaces and Stephenson's locomotive move the world from handwork to factories.
