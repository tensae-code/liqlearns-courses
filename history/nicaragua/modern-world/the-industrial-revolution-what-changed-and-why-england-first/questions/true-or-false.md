# The industrial revolution what changed and why england first — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Impacto de la Revolución Industrial en América", industrial commerce displaces agriculture as the leading activity, railways and the telegraph remake communications, and the state steps back from the economy (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La Revolución Industrial", cold climate, wool, coal, iron and population growth mechanise England first; children work fourteen-to-sixteen-hour days in inhuman factory housing as industrial capitalism replaces the commercial kind (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
