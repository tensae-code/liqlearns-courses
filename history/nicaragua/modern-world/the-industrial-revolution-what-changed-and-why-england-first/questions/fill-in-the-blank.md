# The industrial revolution what changed and why england first — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Impacto de la Revolución Industrial en América" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Industrial commerce displaces agriculture as the leading activity, railways and the telegraph remake communications, and the state steps back from the economy.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Revolución Industrial" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Cold climate, wool, coal, iron and population growth mechanise England first; children work fourteen-to-sixteen-hour days in inhuman factory housing as industrial capitalism replaces the commercial kind.
