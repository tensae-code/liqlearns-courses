## Sub lesson 1 — Revolución Industrial

In mid-18th-century Britain enclosures, new crops and Watt's steam engine move production from workshops to factories and create the working class.

## Sub lesson 2 — La Revolución Industrial: los nuevos inventos

Watt's 1776 steam engine multiplies one worker's output many times over; textile machines, blast furnaces and Stephenson's locomotive move the world from handwork to factories.

## Sub lesson 3 — Impacto de la Revolución Industrial en América

Industrial commerce displaces agriculture as the leading activity, railways and the telegraph remake communications, and the state steps back from the economy.

## Sub lesson 4 — La Revolución Industrial

Cold climate, wool, coal, iron and population growth mechanise England first; children work fourteen-to-sixteen-hour days in inhuman factory housing as industrial capitalism replaces the commercial kind.
