## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Revolución Industrial** — In mid-18th-century Britain enclosures, new crops and Watt's steam engine move production from workshops to factories and create the working class.
2. **§2 La Revolución Industrial: los nuevos inventos** — Watt's 1776 steam engine multiplies one worker's output many times over; textile machines, blast furnaces and Stephenson's locomotive move the world from handwork to factories.
3. **§3 Impacto de la Revolución Industrial en América** — Industrial commerce displaces agriculture as the leading activity, railways and the telegraph remake communications, and the state steps back from the economy.
4. **§4 La Revolución Industrial** — Cold climate, wool, coal, iron and population growth mechanise England first; children work fourteen-to-sixteen-hour days in inhuman factory housing as industrial capitalism replaces the commercial kind.
