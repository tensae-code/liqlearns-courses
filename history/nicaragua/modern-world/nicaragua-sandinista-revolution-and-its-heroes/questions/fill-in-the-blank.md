# Nicaragua sandinista revolution and its heroes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Efemérides: anniversary of Comandante Carlos Fonseca Amador's passing" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** The year closes by returning to Fonseca's mausoleum and death anniversary, the founder remembered at the moment he fell.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Aniversario de la Revolución Popular Sandinista" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** The Revolution's anniversary is celebrated as the department's living historical feast, linking 1856's defence of sovereignty to 1979.
