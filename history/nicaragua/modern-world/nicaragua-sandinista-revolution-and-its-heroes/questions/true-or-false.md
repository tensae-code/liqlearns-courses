# Nicaragua sandinista revolution and its heroes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Héroes de la Patria: Carlos Fonseca Amador", Fonseca founds the Frente Sandinista and is named Father of the Sandinista Popular Revolution, a life shown as proof ordinary people can change the country (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Héroes de la Patria: Julio Buitrago Urroz", Buitrago, a hardworking boy who loved work from childhood, becomes Father of the Urban Resistance, with a house-museum keeping his story (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
