# Nicaragua sandinista revolution and its heroes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Héroes de la Patria: Arlen Siu" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Four years before the Revolution triumphs, young Arlen Siu gives her life; a song, "El cenzontle pregunta por Arlen Siu", asks after the girl who never came back.

**2.** Tell the story of "Héroes de la Patria: Bertha Calderón" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Calderón, a reader inspired by Fidel's books, is killed a month before the Revolution's triumph and is remembered as a heroine of that final push.
