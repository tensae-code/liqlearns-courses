# Alba and its social missions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Alianza Bolivariana para las Américas (ALBA)" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** ALBA answers poverty — called the most powerful weapon of mass destruction — with literacy and post-literacy programmes, Misión Milagro eye care and communicational sovereignty through TeleSur and Radio del Sur.
