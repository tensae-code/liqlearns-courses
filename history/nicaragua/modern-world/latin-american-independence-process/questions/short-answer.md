# Latin american independence process — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Mujeres en la independencia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Manuela Sáenz, Leona Vicario, Policarpa Salavarrieta and Juana Azurduy are pictured as heroines of the independence wars, not bystanders.

**2.** Tell the story of "Antecedentes de la Independencia: el panorama internacional" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Voltaire, Rousseau and Montesquieu arm the criollos with ideas just as Napoleon invades Spain in 1808 and the captive king leaves the colonies to govern themselves.
