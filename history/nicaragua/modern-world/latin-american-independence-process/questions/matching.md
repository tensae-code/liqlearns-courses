# Latin american independence process — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** El proceso independentista: las Juntas de Gobierno — a) (Sub lesson 9)
**2.** Independencia de América del Sur — b) (Sub lesson 10)

Meanings:
a) Criollos first govern in the captive king's name — Chuquisaca, La Paz and Quito in 1809 are shot down, but Caracas, Buenos Aires, Bogotá and Santiago hold in 1810.
