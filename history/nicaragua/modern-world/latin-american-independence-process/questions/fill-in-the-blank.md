# Latin american independence process — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Revolución Haitiana" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** On an island disputed by three empires, Haiti's enslaved people make their rebellion itself the independence; Toussaint Louverture proclaims the abolition of slavery.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Procesos independentistas hispanoamericanos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Bourbon reforms raise taxes and submission, Napoleon's invasion precipitates the break, and the US and French examples show the colonies the way.
