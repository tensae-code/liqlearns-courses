# Latin american independence process — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Las campañas de San Martín y Bolívar; el caso de Brasil", Hidalgo's Grito de Dolores opens Mexico's road, Central America proclaims independence 15 Sept 1821, San Martín and Bolívar free the south between 1817 and 1825, and Brazil parts from Portugal when Dom Pedro refuses to sail home (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Resultados de la independencia latinoamericana", tribute ends and republics are born, but Gran Colombia and the Central American federation fragment, caudillos and civil wars follow, and economic dependence on Europe survives (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
