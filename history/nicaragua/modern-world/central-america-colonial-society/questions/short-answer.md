# Central america colonial society — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Características sociales y culturales de la vida colonial española" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Colonial life sorts society by birth and mixes cultures under Spanish dominance — church, caste and town.

**2.** Tell the story of "Consecuencias de la dominación europea; mestizaje y diferenciación social" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** European domination's consequences are counted, and mestizaje creates a mixed society still divided by rank.
