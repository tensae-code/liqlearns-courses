# Central america colonial society — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Características sociales y culturales de la vida colonial española" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Colonial life sorts society by birth and mixes cultures under Spanish dominance — church, caste and town.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Consecuencias de la dominación europea; mestizaje y diferenciación social" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** European domination's consequences are counted, and mestizaje creates a mixed society still divided by rank.
