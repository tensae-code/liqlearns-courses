# Central america colonial society — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Características sociales y culturales de la vida colonial española", colonial life sorts society by birth and mixes cultures under Spanish dominance — church, caste and town (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Consecuencias de la dominación europea; mestizaje y diferenciación social", European domination's consequences are counted, and mestizaje creates a mixed society still divided by rank (Sub lesson 2)
