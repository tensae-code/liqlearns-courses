# Central america colonial society

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Central america colonial society" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Características sociales y culturales de la vida colonial española

Colonial life sorts society by birth and mixes cultures under Spanish dominance — church, caste and town.

## Sub lesson 2 — Consecuencias de la dominación europea; mestizaje y diferenciación social

European domination's consequences are counted, and mestizaje creates a mixed society still divided by rank.
