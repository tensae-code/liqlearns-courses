# Video script — Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan.

Columbus, backed by Spain's Catholic Monarchs, sails from Palos 3 Aug 1492 and reaches Guanahaní 12 Oct; only on his fourth voyage (1502) does he sight Nicaragua's Caribbean coast in a storm that nearly sinks him.

Columbus sells his westward project, sails four times, and his last voyage brings him to Nicaragua's coast.

Columbus's fourth voyage (1502) coasts Central America, the first European contact that opens the conquest century.

Storm-beaten on his fourth voyage, Columbus rounds Cabo Gracias a Dios on 12 September 1502 and coasts Nicaragua's Caribbean shore, losing a boat at the Río Grande.

Hunting the doubtful strait to the Spice Islands, Columbus coasts Nicaragua's Caribbean shore on 12 September 1502 and names Cabo Gracias a Dios.

Columbus sails from Palos on 3 August 1492 with ninety frightened men who fear a flat earth, and at dawn on 12 October the people of Guanahaní meet the strangers.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
