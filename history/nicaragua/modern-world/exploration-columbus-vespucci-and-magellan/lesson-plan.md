## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Descubrimiento de Nicaragua** — Columbus, backed by Spain's Catholic Monarchs, sails from Palos 3 Aug 1492 and reaches Guanahaní 12 Oct; only on his fourth voyage (1502) does he sight Nicaragua's Caribbean coast in a storm that nearly sinks him.
2. **§2 El proyecto de Cristóbal Colón; los viajes de Colón y su relación con Nicaragua** — Columbus sells his westward project, sails four times, and his last voyage brings him to Nicaragua's coast.
3. **§3 Cuarto viaje de Colón y su relación con Centroamérica** — Columbus's fourth voyage (1502) coasts Central America, the first European contact that opens the conquest century.
4. **§4 Descubrimiento de Nicaragua** — Storm-beaten on his fourth voyage, Columbus rounds Cabo Gracias a Dios on 12 September 1502 and coasts Nicaragua's Caribbean shore, losing a boat at the Río Grande.
5. **§5 La llegada de los europeos a Nicaragua: el cuarto viaje de Colón** — Hunting the doubtful strait to the Spice Islands, Columbus coasts Nicaragua's Caribbean shore on 12 September 1502 and names Cabo Gracias a Dios.
6. **§6 El Descubrimiento de América y encuentro con las sociedades aborígenes** — Columbus sails from Palos on 3 August 1492 with ninety frightened men who fear a flat earth, and at dawn on 12 October the people of Guanahaní meet the strangers.
