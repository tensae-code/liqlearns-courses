# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Descubrimiento de Nicaragua" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Columbus, backed by Spain's Catholic Monarchs, sails from Palos 3 Aug 1492 and reaches Guanahaní 12 Oct; only on his fourth voyage (1502) does he sight Nicaragua's Caribbean coast in a storm that nearly sinks him.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "El proyecto de Cristóbal Colón; los viajes de Colón y su relación con Nicaragua" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Columbus sells his westward project, sails four times, and his last voyage brings him to Nicaragua's coast.
