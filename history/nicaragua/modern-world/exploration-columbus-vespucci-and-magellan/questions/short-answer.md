# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La llegada de los europeos a Nicaragua: el cuarto viaje de Colón" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Hunting the doubtful strait to the Spice Islands, Columbus coasts Nicaragua's Caribbean shore on 12 September 1502 and names Cabo Gracias a Dios.

**2.** Tell the story of "El Descubrimiento de América y encuentro con las sociedades aborígenes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Columbus sails from Palos on 3 August 1492 with ninety frightened men who fear a flat earth, and at dawn on 12 October the people of Guanahaní meet the strangers.
