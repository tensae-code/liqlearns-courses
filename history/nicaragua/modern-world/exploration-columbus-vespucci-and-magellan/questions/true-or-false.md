# Exploration columbus vespucci and magellan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Cuarto viaje de Colón y su relación con Centroamérica", Columbus's fourth voyage (1502) coasts Central America, the first European contact that opens the conquest century (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Descubrimiento de Nicaragua", storm-beaten on his fourth voyage, Columbus rounds Cabo Gracias a Dios on 12 September 1502 and coasts Nicaragua's Caribbean shore, losing a boat at the Río Grande (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
