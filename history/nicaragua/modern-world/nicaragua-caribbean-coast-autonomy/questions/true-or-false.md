# Nicaragua caribbean coast autonomy — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Títulos reales, personería jurídica y Ley 445", royal titles granted from 1591, legal personality laws of 1914-18 and the 2003 Law 445 on communal property still defend indigenous land and self-government today (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Ocupación inglesa en la Costa Caribe", pirates, loggers and planters remake the Caribbean coast; in the 18th century England allies with the Miskitu and consolidates hegemony, leaving Afro-Caribbean culture such as the Palo de Mayo (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
