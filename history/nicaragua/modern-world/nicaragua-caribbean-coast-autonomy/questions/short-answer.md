# Nicaragua caribbean coast autonomy — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Evolución de la Región Atlántica: protectorado y enclave" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Britain's protectorate over the Miskitu monarchy keeps the new state out of its own Caribbean from 1821 to 1860; the Zeledón-Wyke Treaty recognises Nicaraguan sovereignty on paper while a foreign timber and banana enclave grows at Bluefields.

**2.** Tell the story of "La reincorporación de la Mosquitia (1894)" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Zelaya sends General Cabezas, who lowers the Mosquitia flag at Bluefields on 12 February 1894; a Miskitu convention legitimises reincorporation and the 1905 Harrison-Altamirano Treaty ends British claims.
