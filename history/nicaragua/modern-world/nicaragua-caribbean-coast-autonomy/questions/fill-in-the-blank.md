# Nicaragua caribbean coast autonomy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Costa Caribe: de disputa imperial a región" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Disputed by Spain, England and the US for its canal position since 1633, the old Mosquito Coast is at last treated as part of Nicaragua's own story.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Ley de Autonomía de 1987" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Literacy reaches the coast in Spanish, English and Miskitu; the 1987 Autonomy Law and Article 181 recognise indigenous communal land, languages and self-government for the first time.
