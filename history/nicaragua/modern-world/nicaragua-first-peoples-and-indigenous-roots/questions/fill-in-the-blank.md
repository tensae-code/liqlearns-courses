# Nicaragua first peoples and indigenous roots — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Organización política y social de los primeros pobladores" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Cacique-led indigenous societies organise power and community life before any Spanish town exists.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Origen y forma de vida de los primeros pobladores de Nicaragua" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** The first peoples arrive and make lives across the territory that becomes Nicaragua, the root story every later chapter grows from.
