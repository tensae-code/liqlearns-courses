# Nicaragua first peoples and indigenous roots — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Influencia del medio geográfico en el poblamiento del departamento" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Geography decides where department settlements grow, the same land-first logic scaled up from municipality to department.

**2.** Tell the story of "Actividades económicas de los primeros pobladores de mi departamento" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** The first peoples farm, fish and trade to live; their work is the department's first economy.
