# Cuba before and in revolution

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Cuba before and in revolution" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

Cuba's 1902 republic is born amended into total US dependence, sugar latifundios pass to US companies, and workers and students build the unity that makes revolution thinkable; after 1959 Washington's dirty war brings 299 counter-revolutionary bands and Playa Girón.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Cuba before and in revolution as Nicaragua's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's story in your own words.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the story happens in real life. Note it.
