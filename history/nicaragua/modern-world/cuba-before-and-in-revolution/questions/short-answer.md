# Cuba before and in revolution — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Situación socio-económica de Cuba antes de 1959 y la Revolución Cubana" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Cuba's 1902 republic is born amended into total US dependence, sugar latifundios pass to US companies, and workers and students build the unity that makes revolution thinkable; after 1959 Washington's dirty war brings 299 counter-revolutionary bands and Playa Girón.
