# Socialism as a socioeconomic formation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "El Socialismo" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Born in Russia at the start of the 20th century and spread after the Second World War, socialism makes the means of production society's property under collective planning — and is replanned for the 21st century against poverty, hunger and exploitation.
