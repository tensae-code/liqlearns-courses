# Socialism as a socioeconomic formation

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Socialism as a socioeconomic formation" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

Born in Russia at the start of the 20th century and spread after the Second World War, socialism makes the means of production society's property under collective planning — and is replanned for the 21st century against poverty, hunger and exploitation.
