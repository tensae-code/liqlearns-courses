# Video script — National liberation movements in latin america

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: National liberation movements in latin america.

Workers, students and intellectuals answer repression with liberation movements in the 1960s-80s, while South America's debt crisis instead pushes dictatorships back toward elected governments.

El Salvador's FMLN (1980), Honduras's Cinchoneros and Nicaragua's FSLN (1961) fight for land, wages and an end to military rule; the Sandinista triumph inspires the region.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
