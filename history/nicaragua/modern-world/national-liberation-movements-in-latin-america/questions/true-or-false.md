# National liberation movements in latin america — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Movimientos de liberación nacional en América Latina", workers, students and intellectuals answer repression with liberation movements in the 1960s-80s, while South America's debt crisis instead pushes dictatorships back toward elected governments (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Movimientos centroamericanos: FMLN, Cinchoneros y FSLN", El Salvador's FMLN (1980), Honduras's Cinchoneros and Nicaragua's FSLN (1961) fight for land, wages and an end to military rule; the Sandinista triumph inspires the region (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
