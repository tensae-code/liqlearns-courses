# National liberation movements in latin america — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Movimientos de liberación nacional en América Latina" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Workers, students and intellectuals answer repression with liberation movements in the 1960s-80s, while South America's debt crisis instead pushes dictatorships back toward elected governments.

**2.** Tell the story of "Movimientos centroamericanos: FMLN, Cinchoneros y FSLN" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** El Salvador's FMLN (1980), Honduras's Cinchoneros and Nicaragua's FSLN (1961) fight for land, wages and an end to military rule; the Sandinista triumph inspires the region.
