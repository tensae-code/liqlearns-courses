# Nicaragua peace elections and after 1990 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La lucha armada y los acuerdos de paz" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** US-financed Contra war and Reagan's 1985 embargo besiege the Revolution; pressure aims to force negotiation, and peace talks follow.

**2.** Tell the story of "Elecciones de 1990" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Under the peace accords, the US-backed UNO coalition of Violeta Barrios de Chamorro wins the 25 February 1990 elections with 54.74 percent to the FSLN's 40.82.
