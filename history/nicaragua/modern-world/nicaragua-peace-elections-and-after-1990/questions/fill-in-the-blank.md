# Nicaragua peace elections and after 1990 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Transformaciones del gobierno de la UNO" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Chamorro abolishes patriotic military service, demobilises both armies and burns the weapons, while agrarian-reform projects are dismantled and rearmed ex-combatants reappear.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Los gobiernos neoliberales 1996-2006" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Alemán (1997) and Bolaños (2002) continue structural adjustment under IMF conditions while health and education are neglected, until debt relief under the HIPC initiative is negotiated.
