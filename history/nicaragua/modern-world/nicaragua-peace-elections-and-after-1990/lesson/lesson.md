# Nicaragua peace elections and after 1990

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Nicaragua peace elections and after 1990" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pacificación en Nicaragua: Esquipulas I y II

Central America's presidents meet at Esquipulas and on 6-7 Aug 1987 sign procedures for a firm and lasting peace, choosing coexistence over war.

## Sub lesson 2 — Elecciones de febrero de 1990 y Nicaragua desde 1990 hasta la actualidad

The UNO wins the February 1990 elections and power changes hands by ballot, opening the Nicaragua of today.

## Sub lesson 3 — Gobiernos liberales 1996-2000 y 2001-2006

Alemán and Bolaños govern on free-market policies; the book charges corruption, privatised electricity and telephones, and rolled-back free health and education.

## Sub lesson 4 — Gobierno de Reconciliación y Unidad Nacional y sus programas sociales

The GRUN returns with social programmes presented as restoring what the liberal governments removed.

## Sub lesson 5 — La lucha armada y los acuerdos de paz

US-financed Contra war and Reagan's 1985 embargo besiege the Revolution; pressure aims to force negotiation, and peace talks follow.

## Sub lesson 6 — Elecciones de 1990

Under the peace accords, the US-backed UNO coalition of Violeta Barrios de Chamorro wins the 25 February 1990 elections with 54.74 percent to the FSLN's 40.82.

## Sub lesson 7 — Transformaciones del gobierno de la UNO

Chamorro abolishes patriotic military service, demobilises both armies and burns the weapons, while agrarian-reform projects are dismantled and rearmed ex-combatants reappear.

## Sub lesson 8 — Los gobiernos neoliberales 1996-2006

Alemán (1997) and Bolaños (2002) continue structural adjustment under IMF conditions while health and education are neglected, until debt relief under the HIPC initiative is negotiated.

## Sub lesson 9 — El Gobierno de Reconciliación y Unidad Nacional desde 2007

The returning GRUN runs its social programmes through women — Bono Productivo, Usura Cero, Hambre Cero, Plan Techo — under a "Christian, socialist, solidary" banner.

## Sub lesson 10 — La lucha armada y los acuerdos de paz

From 1981 the US funds the Contra army and imposes a trade embargo; the presidents' peace process answers, and Ortega's 1984 election with 67 percent is recognised abroad except in Washington.

## Sub lesson 11 — El Gobierno de la UNO y los gobiernos neoliberales

The UNO's Violeta Chamorro wins 55 percent in 1990 on war-weariness; demobilisation, privatisation and structural adjustment follow, Alemán wins in 1996, and unemployment and emigration to Costa Rica and the US deepen.

## Sub lesson 12 — El Gobierno de Reconciliación y Unidad Nacional desde 2007

The returning GRUN builds a "Christian, socialist, solidary" model of direct citizen power, running its programmes through women — Bono Productivo, Usura Cero, Plan Techo.

## Sub lesson 13 — Los nuevos problemas y el modelo de poder ciudadano

The book closes the century by turning to the GRUN's Christian, Socialist and Solidary citizen-power model, women's advance into political power after the 1920 US suffrage amendment, and the region's new problems of irregular migration and child poverty.
