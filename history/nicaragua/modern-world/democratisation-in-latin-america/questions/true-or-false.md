# Democratisation in latin america — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Democratización en América Latina", after 1985 elected governments return across the region, peace coming through negotiated accords — and the Contadora group of Mexico, Colombia, Venezuela and Panama seeking a Central American settlement (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Democratización en América del Sur: Argentina y Chile", Argentina's junta leaves thousands dead and disappeared before elections return in 1983; in Chile the 1988 plebiscite votes Pinochet out and free elections follow in 1989 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
