# Democratisation in latin america — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Democratización en América Latina" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After 1985 elected governments return across the region, peace coming through negotiated accords — and the Contadora group of Mexico, Colombia, Venezuela and Panama seeking a Central American settlement.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Democratización en América del Sur: Argentina y Chile" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Argentina's junta leaves thousands dead and disappeared before elections return in 1983; in Chile the 1988 plebiscite votes Pinochet out and free elections follow in 1989.
