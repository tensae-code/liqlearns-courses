# Video script — Democratisation in latin america

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Democratisation in latin america.

After 1985 elected governments return across the region, peace coming through negotiated accords — and the Contadora group of Mexico, Colombia, Venezuela and Panama seeking a Central American settlement.

Argentina's junta leaves thousands dead and disappeared before elections return in 1983; in Chile the 1988 plebiscite votes Pinochet out and free elections follow in 1989.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
