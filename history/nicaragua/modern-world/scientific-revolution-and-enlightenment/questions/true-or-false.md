# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Transformaciones de los siglos XVII y XIX: el Despotismo Ilustrado", a rationalist current grows from the Renaissance to attack absolute government, mercantilism and the Church's power, preparing the Enlightenment (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La Ilustración y sus principales filósofos", Locke denies divine right and founds government on agreement; Voltaire is jailed and exiled for attacking privilege, and Rousseau declares man good by nature and sovereignty the people's will (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
