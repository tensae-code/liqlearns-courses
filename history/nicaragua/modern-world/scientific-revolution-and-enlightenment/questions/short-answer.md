# Scientific revolution and enlightenment — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Transformaciones de los siglos XVII y XIX: el Despotismo Ilustrado" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A rationalist current grows from the Renaissance to attack absolute government, mercantilism and the Church's power, preparing the Enlightenment.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Ilustración y sus principales filósofos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Locke denies divine right and founds government on agreement; Voltaire is jailed and exiled for attacking privilege, and Rousseau declares man good by nature and sovereignty the people's will.
