# Foreign interests in central america — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Pretensiones extranjeras en Nicaragua" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** California gold makes a canal urgent; the Monroe Doctrine warns Europe off while the US and Britain sign the Clayton-Bulwer Treaty of 1850 over Nicaragua's head, ignoring its sovereignty.

**2.** Tell the story of "Intereses ingleses y norteamericanos en Centroamérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Britain and the United States circle the isthmus for trade routes and a canal, treating its republics as a corridor.
