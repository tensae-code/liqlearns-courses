# Foreign interests in central america — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Intervenciones militares de Estados Unidos en América Latina, siglos XIX y XX" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** US military interventions repeat across Latin America for two centuries, a pattern each republic meets in turn.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tratado Clayton-Bulwer" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** In 1850 the US and Britain sign over Central America's head, agreeing neither will exclusively control an isthmian canal.
