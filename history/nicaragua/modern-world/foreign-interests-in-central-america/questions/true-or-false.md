# Foreign interests in central america — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Tratado Clayton-Bulwer", in 1850 the US and Britain sign over Central America's head, agreeing neither will exclusively control an isthmian canal (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Pretensiones extranjeras en Nicaragua", coveted since colonial times for its lake-and-river canal route, Nicaragua becomes urgent after California gold in 1848, with Tehuantepec, Nicaragua and Panama competing as transit routes (Sub lesson 4)
