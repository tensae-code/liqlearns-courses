# Globalisation and the european union — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Impacto de la globalización y la Unión Europea" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** The USSR's 1991 break-up ends the Cold War and neoliberal globalisation follows, widening the rich-poor gap; Maastricht turns the European Community into a Union with a single currency.
