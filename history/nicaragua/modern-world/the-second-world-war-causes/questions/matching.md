# The second world war causes — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Segunda Guerra Mundial: causas — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** La Segunda Guerra Mundial — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Versailles's resentments and the Great Depression weaken democracies and feed the alliances that drag the world into a second, crueller war.
b) Versailles's humiliations feed German revanchism; fascist expansionism meets democratic weakness, and Hitler's demand for Danzig triggers the invasion of Poland.
