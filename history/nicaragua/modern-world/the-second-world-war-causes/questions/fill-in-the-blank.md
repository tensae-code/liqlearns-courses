# The second world war causes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Segunda Guerra Mundial: causas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Versailles's resentments and the Great Depression weaken democracies and feed the alliances that drag the world into a second, crueller war.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Segunda Guerra Mundial" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Versailles's humiliations feed German revanchism; fascist expansionism meets democratic weakness, and Hitler's demand for Danzig triggers the invasion of Poland.
