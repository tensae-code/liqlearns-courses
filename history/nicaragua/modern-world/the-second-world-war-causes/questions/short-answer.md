# The second world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Segunda Guerra Mundial: causas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Versailles's resentments and the Great Depression weaken democracies and feed the alliances that drag the world into a second, crueller war.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Segunda Guerra Mundial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Versailles's humiliations feed German revanchism; fascist expansionism meets democratic weakness, and Hitler's demand for Danzig triggers the invasion of Poland.
