# Video script — Alliance for progress in central america

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Alliance for progress in central america.

The Punta del Este charter folds dictatorial Nicaragua into a US counter-revolutionary strategy; the Alliance brings Common Market integration, TIAR and CONDECA armies against the new FSLN.

The Central American Common Market is presented as a facade of development inside the Alliance for Progress, a US strategy to stop the Cuban example, complete with CONDECA armies trained by marines.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
