# Alliance for progress in central america — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Papel de la política de Alianza para el Progreso — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Sistema de Integración Centroamericana — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) The Punta del Este charter folds dictatorial Nicaragua into a US counter-revolutionary strategy; the Alliance brings Common Market integration, TIAR and CONDECA armies against the new FSLN.
b) The Central American Common Market is presented as a facade of development inside the Alliance for Progress, a US strategy to stop the Cuban example, complete with CONDECA armies trained by marines.
