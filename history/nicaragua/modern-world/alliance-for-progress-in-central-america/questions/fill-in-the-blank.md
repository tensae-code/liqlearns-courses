# Alliance for progress in central america — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Papel de la política de Alianza para el Progreso" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Punta del Este charter folds dictatorial Nicaragua into a US counter-revolutionary strategy; the Alliance brings Common Market integration, TIAR and CONDECA armies against the new FSLN.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Sistema de Integración Centroamericana" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Central American Common Market is presented as a facade of development inside the Alliance for Progress, a US strategy to stop the Cuban example, complete with CONDECA armies trained by marines.
