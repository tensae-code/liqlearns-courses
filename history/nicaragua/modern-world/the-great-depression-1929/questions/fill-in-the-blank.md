# The great depression 1929 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Crisis de 1929 y sus consecuencias en América" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Latin America learns to produce what it imported, some states suspend foreign debt, nationalist movements rise against US imperialism, and states emerge stronger — as populist governments or as military dictatorships.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Crisis General del Capitalismo de 1929" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** America's instalment-buying prosperity collapses; savers queue at banks, US capital is withdrawn from Germany and Austria, and the Great Depression ruins factories and jobs worldwide until Roosevelt proposes a new capitalism.
