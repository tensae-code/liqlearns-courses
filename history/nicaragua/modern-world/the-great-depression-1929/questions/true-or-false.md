# The great depression 1929 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Crisis de 1929 y sus consecuencias en América", Latin America learns to produce what it imported, some states suspend foreign debt, nationalist movements rise against US imperialism, and states emerge stronger — as populist governments or as military dictatorships (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Crisis General del Capitalismo de 1929", America's instalment-buying prosperity collapses; savers queue at banks, US capital is withdrawn from Germany and Austria, and the Great Depression ruins factories and jobs worldwide until Roosevelt proposes a new capitalism (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
