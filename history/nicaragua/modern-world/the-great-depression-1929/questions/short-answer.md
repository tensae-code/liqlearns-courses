# The great depression 1929 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La crisis económica de 1929 en Nicaragua" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Depression exposes the export model's weakness — state revenues collapse and real GDP per capita falls to Central America's lowest in 1936-38.

**2.** Tell the story of "Repercusiones de la crisis económica de 1929 en Nicaragua" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The world crisis finds Nicaragua wholly dependent on outside factors and unprepared rulers; the local economy is paralysed.
