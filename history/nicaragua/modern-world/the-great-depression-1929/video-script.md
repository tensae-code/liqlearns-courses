# Video script — The great depression 1929

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The great depression 1929.

The Depression exposes the export model's weakness — state revenues collapse and real GDP per capita falls to Central America's lowest in 1936-38.

The world crisis finds Nicaragua wholly dependent on outside factors and unprepared rulers; the local economy is paralysed.

Latin America learns to produce what it imported, some states suspend foreign debt, nationalist movements rise against US imperialism, and states emerge stronger — as populist governments or as military dictatorships.

America's instalment-buying prosperity collapses; savers queue at banks, US capital is withdrawn from Germany and Austria, and the Great Depression ruins factories and jobs worldwide until Roosevelt proposes a new capitalism.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
