# Video script — The first world war causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The first world war causes.

Territorial claims in the Balkans and Alsace-Lorraine, Anglo-German economic rivalry and colonial clashes harden Europe into the Triple Entente and Triple Alliance.

The Balkan peoples fight to escape two empires while Russia and Austria-Hungary collide over the straits; Britain is displaced by Germany, the United States and Japan in the imperialist rivalry.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
