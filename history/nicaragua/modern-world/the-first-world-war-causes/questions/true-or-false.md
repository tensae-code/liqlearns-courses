# The first world war causes — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Primera Guerra Mundial: causas", territorial claims in the Balkans and Alsace-Lorraine, Anglo-German economic rivalry and colonial clashes harden Europe into the Triple Entente and Triple Alliance (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Primera Guerra Mundial", the Balkan peoples fight to escape two empires while Russia and Austria-Hungary collide over the straits; Britain is displaced by Germany, the United States and Japan in the imperialist rivalry (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
