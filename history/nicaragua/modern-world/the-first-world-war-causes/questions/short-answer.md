# The first world war causes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Primera Guerra Mundial: causas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Territorial claims in the Balkans and Alsace-Lorraine, Anglo-German economic rivalry and colonial clashes harden Europe into the Triple Entente and Triple Alliance.

**2.** Tell the story of "Primera Guerra Mundial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Balkan peoples fight to escape two empires while Russia and Austria-Hungary collide over the straits; Britain is displaced by Germany, the United States and Japan in the imperialist rivalry.
