# Scientific and technological advances — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Adelantos científicos y tecnológicos, siglos XVIII-XX" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Bacon's method, the dynamo, Morse's telegraph, Bell's telephone and Marconi's wireless remake life; the 20th century adds the atomic arms race, the space race, the internet — and the greenhouse effect and green parties answering it.
