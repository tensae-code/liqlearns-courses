# Scientific and technological advances

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Scientific and technological advances" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

Bacon's method, the dynamo, Morse's telegraph, Bell's telephone and Marconi's wireless remake life; the 20th century adds the atomic arms race, the space race, the internet — and the greenhouse effect and green parties answering it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Scientific and technological advances as Nicaragua's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
