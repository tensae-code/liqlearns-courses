# Central america liberal reform and modernisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Reforma Liberal en Centroamérica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Liberal reformers across the isthmus attack church and communal land to remake their countries for export capitalism.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La modernización económica de Centroamérica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Coffee, railways and ports modernise the isthmus's economies — and bind them to foreign markets.
