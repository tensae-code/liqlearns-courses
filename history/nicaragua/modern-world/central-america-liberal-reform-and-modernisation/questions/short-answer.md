# Central america liberal reform and modernisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La Reforma Liberal en Centroamérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Liberal reformers across the isthmus attack church and communal land to remake their countries for export capitalism.

**2.** Tell the story of "La modernización económica de Centroamérica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Coffee, railways and ports modernise the isthmus's economies — and bind them to foreign markets.
