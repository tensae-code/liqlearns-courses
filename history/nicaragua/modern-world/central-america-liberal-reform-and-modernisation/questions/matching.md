# Central america liberal reform and modernisation — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** La Reforma Liberal en Centroamérica — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** La modernización económica de Centroamérica — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Liberal reformers across the isthmus attack church and communal land to remake their countries for export capitalism.
b) Coffee, railways and ports modernise the isthmus's economies — and bind them to foreign markets.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
