# Central america liberal reform and modernisation — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "La Reforma Liberal en Centroamérica", liberal reformers across the isthmus attack church and communal land to remake their countries for export capitalism (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La modernización económica de Centroamérica", coffee, railways and ports modernise the isthmus's economies — and bind them to foreign markets (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
