# Central america independence and federation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Centroamérica ante la anexión a México" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The new provinces are annexed to Mexico and must fight again, this time to be free of an American empire.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Federación Centroamericana" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** The five provinces try to stay one country in a federation, the isthmus's great failed union.
