# Central america independence and federation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Independencia de México (Nueva España) y Centroamérica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Hidalgo's Grito is crushed, but when the Cádiz Constitution threatens criollo interests, Iturbide and Guerrero join in the Plan de Iguala of 24 February 1821.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Independencia de Centroamérica: causas internas y externas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Internal grievances and external revolutions converge; independence is the end of a long process, not a single day.
