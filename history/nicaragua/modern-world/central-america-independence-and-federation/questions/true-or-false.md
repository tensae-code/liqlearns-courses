# Central america independence and federation — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Proclamación de la Independencia de Centroamérica", on 15 September 1821 the Act is signed in Guatemala and the isthmus leaves Spain (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Próceres de la Independencia de Centroamérica", the próceres earn the title by ending colonial rule — among them priest Tomás Ruiz of Chinandega, tortured and held nearly seven years in irons for the Belén conspiracy (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
