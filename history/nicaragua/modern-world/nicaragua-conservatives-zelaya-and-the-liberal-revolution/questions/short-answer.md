# Nicaragua conservatives zelaya and the liberal revolution — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Los Treinta Años conservadores" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** After the National War an oligarchic peace holds to 1893 — property qualifications for voting and office, income from customs and monopolies, no direct taxes.

**2.** Tell the story of "Cambios económicos: el café y la modernización" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** From 1858 recovery makes Nicaragua Central America's most stable economy; coffee ties it to the world market, expropriation creates an agricultural proletariat, and the first bank opens in 1888.
