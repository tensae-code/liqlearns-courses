# Nicaragua conservatives zelaya and the liberal revolution — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** El café y la modernización conservadora — a) (Sub lesson 9)
**2.** Características del gobierno de Zelaya — b) (Sub lesson 10)

Meanings:
a) Coffee spreads from the Sierras de Managua to Matagalpa and Jinotega and becomes the country's main source of foreign earnings; telegraph lines from 1876 and lake steamers stitch the republic together.
b) Zelaya's liberals expropriate church and communal land for coffee, build the first professional army, supreme court and police — and Zelaya, re-elected by a loyal assembly, never calls elections in sixteen years.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
