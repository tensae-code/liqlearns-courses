# Nicaragua conservatives zelaya and the liberal revolution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Causas de la caída de Zelaya" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Internal revolts and war costs meet external US hostility; the execution of US mercenaries Cannon and Groce on 16 November 1909 gives Washington its pretext, and Zelaya resigns on 16 December.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Cambios en la sociedad nicaragüense 1857-1893: el gobierno conservador" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** A small circle of intermarried families rules oligarchically for thirty years on customs and monopoly income, with no direct taxes.
