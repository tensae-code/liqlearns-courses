# Absolutism and mercantilism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Absolutismo y Mercantilismo" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Felipe II consolidates absolutism on American gold and silver, and Louis XIV makes divine right its maximum expression, while mercantilist protection guards the colonial empires' wealth.
