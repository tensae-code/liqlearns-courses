# Nicaragua colonial rule and society — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Organización político-administrativa de los pueblos indígenas bajo la colonia", indigenous towns are reorganised under colonial rule, their own authorities fitted into Spain's system (Sub lesson 3)
