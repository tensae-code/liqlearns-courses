# Nicaragua colonial rule and society — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Características sociales de la colonia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Colony sorts people into ranked groups — Spaniards, criollos, mestizos, indigenous and enslaved — and rank decides a life.

**2.** Tell the story of "La iglesia en la vida colonial; misioneros, Leyes de Indias y Contreras" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Missionaries evangelise the indigenous; when the New Laws try to protect them, governor Contreras rebels against the limits on his power.
