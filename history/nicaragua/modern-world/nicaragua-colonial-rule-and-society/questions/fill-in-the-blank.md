# Nicaragua colonial rule and society — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Los ingleses y los misquitos en la región del Caribe" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** English pirates, raiding slow Spanish galleons, ally with the Miskitu — expert turtle-fishers and sailors — so Spain never truly holds the Caribbean coast.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Fundación de ciudades y reducciones a pueblos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Granada and León are founded as conquest proceeds; survivors are congregated into 198 reducciones — supervised new towns that, paradoxically, preserve communal land and indigenous cabildos.
