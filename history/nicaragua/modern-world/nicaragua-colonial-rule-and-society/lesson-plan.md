# Lesson plan — Nicaragua colonial rule and society

**Course:** History — Nicaragua · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Nicaragua colonial rule and society
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Nicaragua whole in one sweep: conquest and colony, independence and the Federation, Walker and San Jacinto, the conservative years and Zelaya, US interventions, Sandino, the Somoza dictatorship, Revolution, peace and the governments after 1990 (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Fundación de las primeras ciudades** — León and Granada are founded as the first Spanish cities, planting colonial power in indigenous land.
2. **§2 Dominación colonial: características político-administrativas de las colonias españolas** — Spain rules through a colonial administration built to command the provinces from above.
3. **§3 Organización político-administrativa de los pueblos indígenas bajo la colonia** — Indigenous towns are reorganised under colonial rule, their own authorities fitted into Spain's system.
4. **§4 Características económicas coloniales: el indígena y las tierras que cultivaba** — The colonial economy rests on indigenous labour and on taking and working the land the indigenous farmed.
5. **§5 Características sociales de la colonia** — Colony sorts people into ranked groups — Spaniards, criollos, mestizos, indigenous and enslaved — and rank decides a life.
6. **§6 La iglesia en la vida colonial; misioneros, Leyes de Indias y Contreras** — Missionaries evangelise the indigenous; when the New Laws try to protect them, governor Contreras rebels against the limits on his power.
7. **§7 Los ingleses y los misquitos en la región del Caribe** — English pirates, raiding slow Spanish galleons, ally with the Miskitu — expert turtle-fishers and sailors — so Spain never truly holds the Caribbean coast.
8. **§8 Fundación de ciudades y reducciones a pueblos** — Granada and León are founded as conquest proceeds; survivors are congregated into 198 reducciones — supervised new towns that, paradoxically, preserve communal land and indigenous cabildos.
9. **§9 Características de la colonia española en Nicaragua** — Cacao tribute collapses as the indigenous population falls, gold mining and pitch enrich colonisers, society hardens into peninsulares, criollos and dominated indigenous, mestizo and mulatto classes under León's governor.
10. **§10 Fundación de las primeras ciudades en Nicaragua** — Hernández de Córdoba founds León and Granada in 1524 to fix royal authority on conquered land, each city with its assigned purpose.
11. **§11 Características económicas de la Colonia: encomienda, añil y ganado** — The New Laws dress forced labour as encomienda and repartimiento; indigo haciendas worked by indigenous labour and cattle estates in Matagalpa, Estelí and Chontales carry the colonial economy.
12. **§12 Características sociales de la población colonial** — Colonial society is ranked strictly by origin and skin colour — peninsulares on top, then criollos, with the dominated below.
13. **§13 Aspecto político-administrativo de la Colonia** — Nicaragua is governed as a province of the Kingdom of Guatemala, divided into five partidos that later become the departments, each town under its cabildo.
