# Video script — Central america peace and democratisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Central america peace and democratisation.

The presidents' summits and the Esquipulas II accords end the isthmus's wars by negotiation and open democratisation.

At Esquipulas in 1987 the five presidents adopt Óscar Arias's plan for firm and lasting peace; Guatemala and El Salvador then negotiate their own wars to an end by 1992.

The Contadora group mediates from 1983 without result; Óscar Arias's 1987 presidential conference opens the talks that end the Contra war.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
