# Central america peace and democratisation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Esquipulas II y los acuerdos de paz centroamericanos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** At Esquipulas in 1987 the five presidents adopt Óscar Arias's plan for firm and lasting peace; Guatemala and El Salvador then negotiate their own wars to an end by 1992.

**2.** Tell the story of "Contadora y Esquipulas: el camino a la paz" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The Contadora group mediates from 1983 without result; Óscar Arias's 1987 presidential conference opens the talks that end the Contra war.
