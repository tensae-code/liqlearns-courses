# Central america peace and democratisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Pacificación y democratización en Centroamérica: cumbres presidenciales y Esquipulas II" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The presidents' summits and the Esquipulas II accords end the isthmus's wars by negotiation and open democratisation.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Esquipulas II y los acuerdos de paz centroamericanos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** At Esquipulas in 1987 the five presidents adopt Óscar Arias's plan for firm and lasting peace; Guatemala and El Salvador then negotiate their own wars to an end by 1992.
