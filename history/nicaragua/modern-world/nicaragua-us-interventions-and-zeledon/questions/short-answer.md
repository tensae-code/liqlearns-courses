# Nicaragua us interventions and zeledon — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Intervenciones norteamericanas 1909-1933" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Taft's Big Stick continues the Monroe Doctrine; US control is tabulated sector by sector — coffee, energy, railways and banking at 100 percent.

**2.** Tell the story of "Los Pactos Dawson" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** US recognition is bought with pacts that script Nicaragua's politics — a constituent assembly, Estrada president, Díaz vice-president, Zelayistas excluded.
