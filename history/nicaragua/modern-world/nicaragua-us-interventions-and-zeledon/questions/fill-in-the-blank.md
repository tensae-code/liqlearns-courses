# Nicaragua us interventions and zeledon — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Tratado Bryan-Chamorro (1914)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Nicaragua grants the US perpetual exclusive rights to an interoceanic canal by the San Juan river route, signed in Washington on 5 August 1914.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Pacto del Espino Negro (1927)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Under a black-thorn tree at Tipitapa on 4 May 1927, Stimson makes Moncada lay down his nearly victorious constitutionalist army — one general refuses, and his refusal becomes Sandino's war.
