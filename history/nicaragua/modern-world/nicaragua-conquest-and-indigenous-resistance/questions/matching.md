# Nicaragua conquest and indigenous resistance — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Resistencia indígena contra el dominio español en Nicaragua — a) (Sub lesson 9)
**2.** 12 de Octubre: Día de la resistencia indígena — b) (Sub lesson 1)

Meanings:
a) Diriangén's and Nicaragua's attacks are the first organised combats against a foreign invader in Nicaragua's history; in twenty-seven years of exceptional cruelty under Pedrarias and Contreras, a population estimated at a million falls to 10,000.
b) Spain claims the "discovered" lands and sends conquest; Gil González Dávila is welcomed by Cacique Nicaragua in 1523 and baptisms follow, but further north Diriangén asks for time to think — and prepares resistance instead.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
