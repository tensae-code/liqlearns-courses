# Nicaragua conquest and indigenous resistance — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Proceso de conquista de Nicaragua" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Conquest comes from the Pacific, not the Caribbean — Gil González reaches the Mar Dulce, then Pedrarias's captain Hernández de Córdoba founds Granada and León in 1524 and the two conquerors fight each other for the land.

**2.** Tell the story of "Resistencia indígena como consecuencia de la conquista" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Colonisation and resistance begin simultaneously in 1523; Wheelock's "indigenous roots of the anti-colonial struggle" frames resistance as a tradition that never ends.
