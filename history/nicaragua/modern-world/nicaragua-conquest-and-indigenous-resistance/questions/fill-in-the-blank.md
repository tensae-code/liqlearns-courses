# Nicaragua conquest and indigenous resistance — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Proceso de conquista en Nicaragua y resistencia indígena" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Gil González reaches Cacique Nicarao in 1523 and their famous dialogue astonishes the Spaniards; days later, on 17 April, Diriangén's men attack — the first armed defence of the land — but Spanish arms and indigenous division decide the outcome.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Consecuencias de la Conquista" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** The indigenous population collapses catastrophically; Las Casas preaches against the abuses in León and Granada and Bishop Valdivieso is murdered by the Contreras brothers for defending the indigenous, while the New Laws of 1542 arrive too late.
