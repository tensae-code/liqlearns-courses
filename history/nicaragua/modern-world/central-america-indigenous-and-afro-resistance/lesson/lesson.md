# Central america indigenous and afro resistance

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Central america indigenous and afro resistance" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

Cruelly subjugated, indigenous and Afro-descendant people never stop resisting — armed struggle, uprisings and refusal across the 16th-18th centuries.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Central america indigenous and afro resistance as Nicaragua's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
