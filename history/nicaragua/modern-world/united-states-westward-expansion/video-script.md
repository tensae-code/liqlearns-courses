# Video script — United states westward expansion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: United states westward expansion.

Louisiana bought, Florida and Texas annexed, Mexico beaten in 1846-48 and gold pulling settlers west, the transcontinental railroad binds the conquered territories into the Union.

The Monroe Doctrine warns Europe off while the US takes the West, exterminating its indigenous peoples; in 1898 it seizes Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines and Guam from Spain.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
