# United states westward expansion

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "United states westward expansion" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — La expansión de Estados Unidos hacia el Oeste

Louisiana bought, Florida and Texas annexed, Mexico beaten in 1846-48 and gold pulling settlers west, the transcontinental railroad binds the conquered territories into the Union.

## Sub lesson 2 — El expansionismo norteamericano

The Monroe Doctrine warns Europe off while the US takes the West, exterminating its indigenous peoples; in 1898 it seizes Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines and Guam from Spain.
