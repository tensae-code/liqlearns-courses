# United states westward expansion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "La expansión de Estados Unidos hacia el Oeste"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Louisiana bought, Florida and Texas annexed, Mexico beaten in 1846-48 and gold pulling settlers west, the transcontinental railroad binds the conquered territories into the Union.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
