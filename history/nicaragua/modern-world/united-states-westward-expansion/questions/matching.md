# United states westward expansion — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** La expansión de Estados Unidos hacia el Oeste — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** El expansionismo norteamericano — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Louisiana bought, Florida and Texas annexed, Mexico beaten in 1846-48 and gold pulling settlers west, the transcontinental railroad binds the conquered territories into the Union.
b) The Monroe Doctrine warns Europe off while the US takes the West, exterminating its indigenous peoples; in 1898 it seizes Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines and Guam from Spain.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
