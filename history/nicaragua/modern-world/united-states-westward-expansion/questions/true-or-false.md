# United states westward expansion — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "La expansión de Estados Unidos hacia el Oeste", Louisiana bought, Florida and Texas annexed, Mexico beaten in 1846-48 and gold pulling settlers west, the transcontinental railroad binds the conquered territories into the Union (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "El expansionismo norteamericano", the Monroe Doctrine warns Europe off while the US takes the West, exterminating its indigenous peoples; in 1898 it seizes Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines and Guam from Spain (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
