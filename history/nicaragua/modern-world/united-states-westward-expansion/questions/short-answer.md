# United states westward expansion — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La expansión de Estados Unidos hacia el Oeste" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Louisiana bought, Florida and Texas annexed, Mexico beaten in 1846-48 and gold pulling settlers west, the transcontinental railroad binds the conquered territories into the Union.

**2.** Tell the story of "El expansionismo norteamericano" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Monroe Doctrine warns Europe off while the US takes the West, exterminating its indigenous peoples; in 1898 it seizes Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines and Guam from Spain.
