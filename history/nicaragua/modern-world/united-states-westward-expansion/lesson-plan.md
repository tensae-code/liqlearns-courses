# Lesson plan — United states westward expansion

**Course:** History — Nicaragua · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** United states westward expansion
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The world sweep: prehistory and the river civilisations, Greece and Rome, the Renaissance, colonisation of America, the great revolutions, Latin American independence, the United States' story, both world wars, and Latin America's dictatorships and return to democracy (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 La expansión de Estados Unidos hacia el Oeste** — Louisiana bought, Florida and Texas annexed, Mexico beaten in 1846-48 and gold pulling settlers west, the transcontinental railroad binds the conquered territories into the Union.
2. **§2 El expansionismo norteamericano** — The Monroe Doctrine warns Europe off while the US takes the West, exterminating its indigenous peoples; in 1898 it seizes Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Philippines and Guam from Spain.
