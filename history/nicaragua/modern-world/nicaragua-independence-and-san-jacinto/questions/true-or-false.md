# Nicaragua independence and san jacinto — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Efemérides: anniversary of General José Dolores Estrada's death", pupils visit/read the inscription honouring "the illustrious General José Dolores Estrada", closing the San Jacinto story begun at his birth anniversary (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Celebraciones de hechos históricos de mi departamento (unit introduction)", historical events are kept alive by celebrating them; the unit frames discovery, resistance, autonomy, independence and heroes as one patriotic calendar (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
