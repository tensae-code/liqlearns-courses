# Nicaragua independence and san jacinto — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Las fiestas patrias: Independencia de Centroamérica y Nicaragua" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** The September patriotic festivals retell how Central America became independent and Nicaragua kept that freedom.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Batalla de San Jacinto" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Liberal-conservative civil wars invite William Walker's filibusters; at the Acta de Matagalpa (20 Apr 1856) Nicaraguans unite across parties, and at dawn 14 Sept 1856 some 120 patriots under Estrada defeat 300 filibusters at Hacienda San Jacinto, Andrés Castro killing one with a stone.
