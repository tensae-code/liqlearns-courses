# Nicaragua independence and san jacinto — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Personajes históricos: Gesta de Enmanuel Mongalo" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Teacher Enmanuel Mongalo runs with a burning torch under enemy fire to set the filibusters' position alight, a schoolmaster turned hero of the Walker war.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Antecedentes de la Guerra Nacional: agresión inglesa y disputa EE.UU.–Inglaterra por Nicaragua" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Britain seizes Caribbean positions and the US and Britain rival over a canal route through Nicaragua, making the small country a prize.
