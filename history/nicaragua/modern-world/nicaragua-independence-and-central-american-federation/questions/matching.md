# Nicaragua independence and central american federation — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** La independencia llega a Nicaragua: Acta y Acta de los Nublados — a) (Sub lesson 9)
**2.** Anexión a México — b) (Sub lesson 10)

Meanings:
a) León's conservative Diputación answers Guatemala's 15 September Act with its own cloudy counter-act; Granada rejects it, and the León-Granada elite rivalry ignites decades of civil war.
b) Iturbide's Plan de Iguala — Catholic exclusivity, independence as constitutional monarchy, union of castes — wins the monarchists and pulls Central America into the Mexican empire.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
