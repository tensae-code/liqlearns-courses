# Nicaragua independence and central american federation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Nicaragua ante la anexión a México" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Freedom is immediately tested — the provinces are annexed to Iturbide's Mexico and Nicaraguan towns split over accepting it.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Federación Centroamericana y Francisco Morazán" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** The five provinces federate in 1823; Morazán, elected 1830, fights to hold the union together against its breakers.
