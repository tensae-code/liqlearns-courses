# Nicaragua independence and central american federation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "El proceso independentista: antecedentes" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Three hundred years of absolutist monarchy concentrate all power in Spain, and the colony's own grievances prepare the break.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Movimientos preindependentistas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Hidalgo's 1810 rising and Morelos carry Mexico's revolt; in Nicaragua over 8,000 people with machetes and sticks surround the governor's house in León on 13 December 1811 and form their own junta, Granada following on 22 December.
