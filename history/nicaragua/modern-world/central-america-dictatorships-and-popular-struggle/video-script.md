# Video script — Central america dictatorships and popular struggle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Central america dictatorships and popular struggle.

After the 1930s world crisis, caudillismo hardens into military dictatorships backed by oligarchies, and popular struggle answers them.

Unions, peasant and student organisations — and women inside them — carry the long fight against the dictatorships.

Integration's stability leaves Guatemala's indigenous majority outside every programme and Nicaragua under a pseudo-democracy whose real power is the Guardia Nacional.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
