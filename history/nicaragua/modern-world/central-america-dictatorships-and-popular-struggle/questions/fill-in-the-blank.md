# Central america dictatorships and popular struggle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Las dictaduras militares y la lucha popular en Centroamérica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After the 1930s world crisis, caudillismo hardens into military dictatorships backed by oligarchies, and popular struggle answers them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "El papel de las organizaciones populares y de la mujer en la lucha centroamericana" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Unions, peasant and student organisations — and women inside them — carry the long fight against the dictatorships.
