# Central america dictatorships and popular struggle — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "El papel de las organizaciones populares y de la mujer en la lucha centroamericana" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Unions, peasant and student organisations — and women inside them — carry the long fight against the dictatorships.

**2.** Tell the story of "Cambios políticos y la lucha por la democracia" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Integration's stability leaves Guatemala's indigenous majority outside every programme and Nicaragua under a pseudo-democracy whose real power is the Guardia Nacional.
