# Mexican revolution — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La Revolución Mexicana" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Thirty years of Porfirio Díaz's re-elections explode; Madero opens the revolt, Villa and Orozco fight in the north and Zapata makes his bloody campaign against the local oligarchs in Morelos.
