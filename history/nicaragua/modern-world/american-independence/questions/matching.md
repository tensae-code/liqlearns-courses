# American independence — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Independencia de las 13 colonias en Estados Unidos — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Independencia de las Trece Colonias — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Taxed and distant from Britain, the Thirteen Colonies break away and found a republic whose Declaration circulates in Spanish through South America.
b) The Stamp Act drives nine colonies to a rights declaration in New York; Washington's militias win at Lexington, independence is declared on 4 July 1776 and recognised at Versailles in 1783.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
