# American independence — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Independencia de las 13 colonias en Estados Unidos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Taxed and distant from Britain, the Thirteen Colonies break away and found a republic whose Declaration circulates in Spanish through South America.

**2.** Tell the story of "Independencia de las Trece Colonias" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Stamp Act drives nine colonies to a rights declaration in New York; Washington's militias win at Lexington, independence is declared on 4 July 1776 and recognised at Versailles in 1783.
