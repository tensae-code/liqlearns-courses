# American independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Independencia de las 13 colonias en Estados Unidos"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Taxed and distant from Britain, the Thirteen Colonies break away and found a republic whose Declaration circulates in Spanish through South America.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Independencia de las Trece Colonias"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The Stamp Act drives nine colonies to a rights declaration in New York; Washington's militias win at Lexington, independence is declared on 4 July 1776 and recognised at Versailles in 1783.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
