# American independence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Independencia de las 13 colonias en Estados Unidos", taxed and distant from Britain, the Thirteen Colonies break away and found a republic whose Declaration circulates in Spanish through South America (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Independencia de las Trece Colonias", the Stamp Act drives nine colonies to a rights declaration in New York; Washington's militias win at Lexington, independence is declared on 4 July 1776 and recognised at Versailles in 1783 (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
