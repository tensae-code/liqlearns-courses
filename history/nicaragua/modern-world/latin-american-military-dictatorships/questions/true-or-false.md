# Latin american military dictatorships — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Regímenes políticos en América Latina después de la Segunda Guerra Mundial", after 1945 the United States swaps open military intervention for economic, political and military dominance over the region's regimes (Sub lesson 3)
