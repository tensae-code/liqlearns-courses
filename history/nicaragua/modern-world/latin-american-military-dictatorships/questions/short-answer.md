# Latin american military dictatorships — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Dictaduras: Trujillo, Batista, Pinochet y Bordaberry" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trujillo rules the Dominican Republic to 1961, Batista ties Cuba to the US, Pinochet's 1973 coup bombs La Moneda and opens Chile's violent dictatorship, and Bordaberry dissolves Uruguay's parliament.

**2.** Tell the story of "Regímenes políticos en América Latina después de la Segunda Guerra Mundial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** After 1945 the United States swaps open military intervention for economic, political and military dominance over the region's regimes.
