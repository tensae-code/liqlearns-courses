# Latin american military dictatorships — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Dictaduras militares en América Latina" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Born of the 1929 crisis and Cold War confrontation, dictatorships concentrate power with US support and secret police — Ubico in Guatemala, Hernández Martínez in El Salvador, Somoza García in Nicaragua from 1937.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Dictaduras: Trujillo, Batista, Pinochet y Bordaberry" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Trujillo rules the Dominican Republic to 1961, Batista ties Cuba to the US, Pinochet's 1973 coup bombs La Moneda and opens Chile's violent dictatorship, and Bordaberry dissolves Uruguay's parliament.
