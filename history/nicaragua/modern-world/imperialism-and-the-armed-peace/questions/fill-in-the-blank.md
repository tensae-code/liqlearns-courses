# Imperialism and the armed peace — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Europa en el siglo XX: imperialismo y la Paz Armada" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Capitalism reaches its imperialist phase in a race for unexploited territories; nationalism, war propaganda and an arms race harden Europe into two rival blocs in an armed peace.
