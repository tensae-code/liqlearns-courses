# The first world war versailles and the results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Primera Guerra Mundial: consecuencias" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Four dynasties fall, women enter the workforce in place of the soldiers, economies sink into war debt and inflation, and a revanchist nationalism is left behind.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Primera Guerra Mundial y sus consecuencias en América" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Imperial rivalry mobilises 60 million European soldiers and kills ten million; empires fall, the map is redrawn by self-determination unevenly applied, the League of Nations is born and the United States comes out favoured.
