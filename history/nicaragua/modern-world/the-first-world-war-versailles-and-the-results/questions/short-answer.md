# The first world war versailles and the results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Primera Guerra Mundial y sus consecuencias en América" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Imperial rivalry mobilises 60 million European soldiers and kills ten million; empires fall, the map is redrawn by self-determination unevenly applied, the League of Nations is born and the United States comes out favoured.

**2.** Tell the story of "Consecuencias de la Primera Guerra Mundial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Austria is declared responsible and split from Hungary; Europe loses its hegemony to an undamaged United States, which becomes creditor to the Allies.
