# Video script — The first world war versailles and the results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The first world war versailles and the results.

Four dynasties fall, women enter the workforce in place of the soldiers, economies sink into war debt and inflation, and a revanchist nationalism is left behind.

Imperial rivalry mobilises 60 million European soldiers and kills ten million; empires fall, the map is redrawn by self-determination unevenly applied, the League of Nations is born and the United States comes out favoured.

Austria is declared responsible and split from Hungary; Europe loses its hegemony to an undamaged United States, which becomes creditor to the Allies.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
