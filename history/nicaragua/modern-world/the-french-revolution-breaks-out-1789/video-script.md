# Video script — The french revolution breaks out 1789

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The french revolution breaks out 1789.

Bread prices, royal waste and Enlightenment ideas force the Estates General in 1789; the Third Estate's revolution makes the people sovereign and opens the contemporary age.

On 14 July 1789 Paris storms the Bastille, the state prison and symbol of absolute royal power, and France's revolution becomes the example other peoples follow (placed by the book inside its post-independence unit).

Turgot's attempt to tax the nobility fails, prices rise and the king masses troops; on 14 July 1789 Paris storms the Bastille, and Louis XVI's flight and forced oath of 1791 seal the old regime's fall.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
