# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "La Revolución Francesa", Turgot's attempt to tax the nobility fails, prices rise and the king masses troops; on 14 July 1789 Paris storms the Bastille, and Louis XVI's flight and forced oath of 1791 seal the old regime's fall (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Revolución Francesa", bread prices, royal waste and Enlightenment ideas force the Estates General in 1789; the Third Estate's revolution makes the people sovereign and opens the contemporary age (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
