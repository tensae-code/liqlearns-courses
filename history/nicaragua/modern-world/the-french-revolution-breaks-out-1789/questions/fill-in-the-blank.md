# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Revolución Francesa" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bread prices, royal waste and Enlightenment ideas force the Estates General in 1789; the Third Estate's revolution makes the people sovereign and opens the contemporary age.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Revolución Francesa" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** On 14 July 1789 Paris storms the Bastille, the state prison and symbol of absolute royal power, and France's revolution becomes the example other peoples follow (placed by the book inside its post-independence unit).
