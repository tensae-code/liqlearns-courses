# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Revolución Francesa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** On 14 July 1789 Paris storms the Bastille, the state prison and symbol of absolute royal power, and France's revolution becomes the example other peoples follow (placed by the book inside its post-independence unit).

**2.** Tell the story of "La Revolución Francesa" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Turgot's attempt to tax the nobility fails, prices rise and the king masses troops; on 14 July 1789 Paris storms the Bastille, and Louis XVI's flight and forced oath of 1791 seal the old regime's fall.
