# The french revolution breaks out 1789 — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** La Revolución Francesa — a) (Sub lesson 3)
**2.** Revolución Francesa — b) (Sub lesson 1)

Meanings:
a) Turgot's attempt to tax the nobility fails, prices rise and the king masses troops; on 14 July 1789 Paris storms the Bastille, and Louis XVI's flight and forced oath of 1791 seal the old regime's fall.
b) Bread prices, royal waste and Enlightenment ideas force the Estates General in 1789; the Third Estate's revolution makes the people sovereign and opens the contemporary age.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
