**Course:** History — Nicaragua · **Title:** Modern world · **Lesson:** Exploration causes and results
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Nicaragua whole in one sweep: conquest and colony, independence and the Federation, Walker and San Jacinto, the conservative years and Zelaya, US interventions, Sandino, the Somoza dictatorship, Revolution, peace and the governments after 1990 (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Antecedentes del descubrimiento de América** — Europe's search for trade routes sets the scene; America is reached because of what Europe wanted from Asia.
2. **§2 Exploraciones de ultramar** — Blocked spice routes, a round Earth, portolan charts, compass and astrolabe push Europeans overseas; world trade shifts from the Mediterranean to the Atlantic.
3. **§3 Situación de la sociedad europea en el siglo XV** — Feudalism decays into commercial capitalism; an expanding trade needs new customers, and the fall of Constantinople in 1453 closes the spice routes.
4. **§4 Las grandes expediciones geográficas** — Profit, Christian expansion against Islam and the blocked road to the Indies combine into the great enterprise of discovery.
5. **§5 Los grandes descubrimientos geográficos** — When the Turks take Constantinople in 1453 and close the Genoese route, Portugal's search for an alternative road to the spices launches the age of discoveries.
