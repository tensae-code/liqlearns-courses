# Exploration causes and results

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Exploration causes and results" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Antecedentes del descubrimiento de América

Europe's search for trade routes sets the scene; America is reached because of what Europe wanted from Asia.

## Sub lesson 2 — Exploraciones de ultramar

Blocked spice routes, a round Earth, portolan charts, compass and astrolabe push Europeans overseas; world trade shifts from the Mediterranean to the Atlantic.

## Sub lesson 3 — Situación de la sociedad europea en el siglo XV

Feudalism decays into commercial capitalism; an expanding trade needs new customers, and the fall of Constantinople in 1453 closes the spice routes.

## Sub lesson 4 — Las grandes expediciones geográficas

Profit, Christian expansion against Islam and the blocked road to the Indies combine into the great enterprise of discovery.

## Sub lesson 5 — Los grandes descubrimientos geográficos

When the Turks take Constantinople in 1453 and close the Genoese route, Portugal's search for an alternative road to the spices launches the age of discoveries.
