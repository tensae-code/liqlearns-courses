# Exploration causes and results — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Los grandes descubrimientos geográficos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** When the Turks take Constantinople in 1453 and close the Genoese route, Portugal's search for an alternative road to the spices launches the age of discoveries.

**2.** Tell the story of "Antecedentes del descubrimiento de América" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Europe's search for trade routes sets the scene; America is reached because of what Europe wanted from Asia.
