# Exploration causes and results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Antecedentes del descubrimiento de América"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Europe's search for trade routes sets the scene; America is reached because of what Europe wanted from Asia.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Exploraciones de ultramar"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Blocked spice routes, a round Earth, portolan charts, compass and astrolabe push Europeans overseas; world trade shifts from the Mediterranean to the Atlantic.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
