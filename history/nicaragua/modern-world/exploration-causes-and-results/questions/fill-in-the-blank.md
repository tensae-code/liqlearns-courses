# Exploration causes and results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Exploraciones de ultramar" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Blocked spice routes, a round Earth, portolan charts, compass and astrolabe push Europeans overseas; world trade shifts from the Mediterranean to the Atlantic.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Situación de la sociedad europea en el siglo XV" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Feudalism decays into commercial capitalism; an expanding trade needs new customers, and the fall of Constantinople in 1453 closes the spice routes.
