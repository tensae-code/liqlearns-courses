# Nicaragua somoza dictatorship — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "La acción heroica de Rigoberto López Pérez", at a party in León's Casa del Obrero on 21 Sept 1956, Rigoberto López Pérez shoots Somoza García; the book calls it the beginning of the end, though the sons inherit power and repress harder (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La sociedad nicaragüense entre 1936-1979", growth booms on cotton and the 1960 Central American Common Market, yet cotton expansion strips thousands of peasants of land and culture is kept under the dynasty's thumb (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
