# Nicaragua somoza dictatorship — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La sociedad nicaragüense entre 1936 y 1979" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Population doubles and half the country moves to the cities by 1979, migration from countryside to town remaking Nicaraguan society under the dynasty.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Características de la dictadura somocista: bases del poder" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** The departing marines leave West Point-trained Somoza García commanding the Guardia Nacional — the first step to owning the country.
