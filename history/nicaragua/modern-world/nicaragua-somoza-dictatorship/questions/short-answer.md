# Nicaragua somoza dictatorship — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ascenso y consolidación de la dinastía somocista" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Sacasa resigns into exile on 6 June 1936; Somoza García takes the presidency in January 1937 ruling by his formula of silver for friends and lead for enemies, buying the Guardia's loyalty.

**2.** Tell the story of "La fortuna somocista y la modernización económica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** By 1979 the family owns a fifth of Nicaragua's cultivable land and a fortune of 500-600 million dollars built on forced sales, commissions and monopolies.
