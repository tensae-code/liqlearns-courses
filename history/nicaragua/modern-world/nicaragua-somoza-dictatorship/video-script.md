# Video script — Nicaragua somoza dictatorship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Nicaragua somoza dictatorship.

The US-created Guardia Nacional becomes the Somoza family's personal army; Somoza García coups Sacasa in 1936, takes the presidency 1 Jan 1937 and enriches himself by repression.

Under the dynasty's second half the country is surveyed whole — wealth for the family, hardship and protest for the people.

At a party in León's Casa del Obrero on 21 Sept 1956, Rigoberto López Pérez shoots Somoza García; the book calls it the beginning of the end, though the sons inherit power and repress harder.

Growth booms on cotton and the 1960 Central American Common Market, yet cotton expansion strips thousands of peasants of land and culture is kept under the dynasty's thumb.

Sacasa resigns into exile on 6 June 1936; Somoza García takes the presidency in January 1937 ruling by his formula of silver for friends and lead for enemies, buying the Guardia's loyalty.

By 1979 the family owns a fifth of Nicaragua's cultivable land and a fortune of 500-600 million dollars built on forced sales, commissions and monopolies.

Population doubles and half the country moves to the cities by 1979, migration from countryside to town remaking Nicaraguan society under the dynasty.

The departing marines leave West Point-trained Somoza García commanding the Guardia Nacional — the first step to owning the country.

Somoza has Sandino murdered in 1934, takes the presidency in 1937 and starts a forty-year dynasty; the 1950 Pact of the Generals buys conservative collaboration.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
