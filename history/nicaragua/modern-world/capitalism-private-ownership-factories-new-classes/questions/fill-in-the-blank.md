# Capitalism private ownership factories new classes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "El Capitalismo" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Private owners keep the profit and capital rules over labour; a free-competition phase runs from the 16th to the late 19th century before Lenin's imperialist phase of finance capital and exported capital takes over to the age of globalisation.
