Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "India" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Aryan horsemen with iron conquer the north and their Vedas sing the conquest; Brahmin priests then freeze society into five castes down to the Dalits, while the Mahabharata and Ramayana epics carry the civilisation's memory.
