Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Civilizaciones de América: Aztecas, Mayas e Incas"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Three empires are compared side by side — Tenochtitlan, the Maya city-states and Cuzco — alike in stone building and many gods, different in language, rule and writing.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
