# Aztec maya and inca compared — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Civilizaciones de América: Aztecas, Mayas e Incas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Three empires are compared side by side — Tenochtitlan, the Maya city-states and Cuzco — alike in stone building and many gods, different in language, rule and writing.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Legado de las civilizaciones Mayas, Aztecas e Incas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Maya calendars and glyphs, Aztec codices and Tenochtitlan itself, Inca Machu Picchu and Quechua survive as the three civilisations' living legacy.
