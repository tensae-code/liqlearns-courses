# Aztec maya and inca compared — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Legado de las civilizaciones Mayas, Aztecas e Incas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Maya calendars and glyphs, Aztec codices and Tenochtitlan itself, Inca Machu Picchu and Quechua survive as the three civilisations' living legacy.

**2.** Tell the story of "Civilizaciones indígenas de América" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Only three peoples reach the highest, postclassic level — Maya, Aztec and Inca — and the unit studies them in turn as America's great civilisations.
