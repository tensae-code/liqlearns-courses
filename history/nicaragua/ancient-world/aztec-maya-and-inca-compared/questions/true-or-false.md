# Aztec maya and inca compared — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Civilizaciones indígenas de América", only three peoples reach the highest, postclassic level — Maya, Aztec and Inca — and the unit studies them in turn as America's great civilisations (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Civilizaciones de América: Aztecas, Mayas e Incas", three empires are compared side by side — Tenochtitlan, the Maya city-states and Cuzco — alike in stone building and many gods, different in language, rule and writing (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
