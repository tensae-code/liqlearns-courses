# Video script — Aztec maya and inca compared

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Aztec maya and inca compared.

Three empires are compared side by side — Tenochtitlan, the Maya city-states and Cuzco — alike in stone building and many gods, different in language, rule and writing.

Maya calendars and glyphs, Aztec codices and Tenochtitlan itself, Inca Machu Picchu and Quechua survive as the three civilisations' living legacy.

Only three peoples reach the highest, postclassic level — Maya, Aztec and Inca — and the unit studies them in turn as America's great civilisations.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
