# The maya — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Economía, política y sociedad de los mayas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Maya farming and trade feed a ranked society ruled from ceremonial cities.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Legado cultural de los mayas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Maya writing, astronomy, calendar and building survive the cities' fall as a living legacy for Central America.
