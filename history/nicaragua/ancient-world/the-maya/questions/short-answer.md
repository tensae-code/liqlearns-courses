# The maya — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Logros científicos de los mayas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Priest-astronomers who keep the writing also keep the calendars, predict eclipses and read the rainy and dry seasons for the farmers.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Cultura Maya: características geográficas, políticas, sociales y culturales" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Maya build city-states across the isthmus's north, ordering politics, society and culture around kings, priests and maize.
