# The maya — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Legado cultural de los mayas", Maya writing, astronomy, calendar and building survive the cities' fall as a living legacy for Central America (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La Civilización Maya", peaceful classic Maya abandon their cities about 900 for reasons still unknown, then return as warriors in Yucatán's confederated Chichén-Itzá, Uxmal and Mayapán (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
