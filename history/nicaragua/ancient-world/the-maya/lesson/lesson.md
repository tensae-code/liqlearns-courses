# The maya

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The maya" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — La Cultura Maya: características geográficas, políticas, sociales y culturales

The Maya build city-states across the isthmus's north, ordering politics, society and culture around kings, priests and maize.

## Sub lesson 2 — Economía, política y sociedad de los mayas

Maya farming and trade feed a ranked society ruled from ceremonial cities.

## Sub lesson 3 — Legado cultural de los mayas

Maya writing, astronomy, calendar and building survive the cities' fall as a living legacy for Central America.

## Sub lesson 4 — La Civilización Maya

Peaceful classic Maya abandon their cities about 900 for reasons still unknown, then return as warriors in Yucatán's confederated Chichén-Itzá, Uxmal and Mayapán.

## Sub lesson 5 — Logros científicos de los mayas

Priest-astronomers who keep the writing also keep the calendars, predict eclipses and read the rainy and dry seasons for the farmers.
