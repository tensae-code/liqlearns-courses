# Ancient civilisations greece crete — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "La Grecia Helénica", mountain valleys split Greece into sovereign polis city-states that colonise the coasts; Alexander then defeats Darius III at Granicus, Issus and Gaugamela and builds the age's largest empire in four years (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Civilización de Grecia", Greece is named the cradle of democracy, philosophy, literature and the Olympic Games, its thinkers asking for the first time why the world is as it is (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
