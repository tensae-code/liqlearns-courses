# Ancient civilisations greece crete — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Civilización de Grecia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Greece is named the cradle of democracy, philosophy, literature and the Olympic Games, its thinkers asking for the first time why the world is as it is.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Civilización Griega: Creta y Micenas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Palace-centred Crete under legendary King Minos trades bronze and cloth across three continents; warlike Mycenae of the Trojan War age follows, then the Dorian invasion.
