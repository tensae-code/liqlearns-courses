# Ancient civilisations greece crete — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Civilización Griega: Creta y Micenas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Palace-centred Crete under legendary King Minos trades bronze and cloth across three continents; warlike Mycenae of the Trojan War age follows, then the Dorian invasion.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Grecia Helénica" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Mountain valleys split Greece into sovereign polis city-states that colonise the coasts; Alexander then defeats Darius III at Granicus, Issus and Gaugamela and builds the age's largest empire in four years.
