## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Civilización de Grecia** — Greece is named the cradle of democracy, philosophy, literature and the Olympic Games, its thinkers asking for the first time why the world is as it is.
2. **§2 Civilización Griega: Creta y Micenas** — Palace-centred Crete under legendary King Minos trades bronze and cloth across three continents; warlike Mycenae of the Trojan War age follows, then the Dorian invasion.
3. **§3 La Grecia Helénica** — Mountain valleys split Greece into sovereign polis city-states that colonise the coasts; Alexander then defeats Darius III at Granicus, Issus and Gaugamela and builds the age's largest empire in four years.
