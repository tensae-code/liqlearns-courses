## Sub lesson 1 — Civilización de Grecia

Greece is named the cradle of democracy, philosophy, literature and the Olympic Games, its thinkers asking for the first time why the world is as it is.

## Sub lesson 2 — Civilización Griega: Creta y Micenas

Palace-centred Crete under legendary King Minos trades bronze and cloth across three continents; warlike Mycenae of the Trojan War age follows, then the Dorian invasion.

## Sub lesson 3 — La Grecia Helénica

Mountain valleys split Greece into sovereign polis city-states that colonise the coasts; Alexander then defeats Darius III at Granicus, Issus and Gaugamela and builds the age's largest empire in four years.
