# Video script — Ancient civilisations greece crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Ancient civilisations greece crete.

Greece is named the cradle of democracy, philosophy, literature and the Olympic Games, its thinkers asking for the first time why the world is as it is.

Palace-centred Crete under legendary King Minos trades bronze and cloth across three continents; warlike Mycenae of the Trojan War age follows, then the Dorian invasion.

Mountain valleys split Greece into sovereign polis city-states that colonise the coasts; Alexander then defeats Darius III at Granicus, Issus and Gaugamela and builds the age's largest empire in four years.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
