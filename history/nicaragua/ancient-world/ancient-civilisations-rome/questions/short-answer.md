# Ancient civilisations rome — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Civilización romana" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Romulus's city passes through monarchy, republic and empire, and leaves Latin, Roman law, aqueducts and the spread of Christianity as its legacy.

**2.** Tell the story of "Civilización Romana: periodos históricos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Romulus kills Remus and founds the city; monarchy gives way to a republic balancing consuls, Senate and assemblies, and Servius Tullius's wealth-based classes arm the plebs' long fight for rights.
