# Ancient civilisations rome — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Conquista del Mediterráneo", Rome takes Italy by divide-and-rule, makes the Mediterranean a Roman lake — Mare Nostrum — and grows rich on enslaved prisoners while the Gracchi brothers' land laws die with them (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "La decadencia del imperio romano y su legado cultural", Sulla's perpetual dictatorship, the First Triumvirate and Spartacus's slave revolt precede Octavian's empire; Diocletian's tetrarchy fails and Constantine ends Rome's centrality, leaving law, language and letters behind (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
