# Ancient civilisations rome — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Conquista del Mediterráneo" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Rome takes Italy by divide-and-rule, makes the Mediterranean a Roman lake — Mare Nostrum — and grows rich on enslaved prisoners while the Gracchi brothers' land laws die with them.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "La decadencia del imperio romano y su legado cultural" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Sulla's perpetual dictatorship, the First Triumvirate and Spartacus's slave revolt precede Octavian's empire; Diocletian's tetrarchy fails and Constantine ends Rome's centrality, leaving law, language and letters behind.
