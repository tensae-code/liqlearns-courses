# Video script — Ancient civilisations rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Ancient civilisations rome.

Romulus's city passes through monarchy, republic and empire, and leaves Latin, Roman law, aqueducts and the spread of Christianity as its legacy.

Romulus kills Remus and founds the city; monarchy gives way to a republic balancing consuls, Senate and assemblies, and Servius Tullius's wealth-based classes arm the plebs' long fight for rights.

Rome takes Italy by divide-and-rule, makes the Mediterranean a Roman lake — Mare Nostrum — and grows rich on enslaved prisoners while the Gracchi brothers' land laws die with them.

Sulla's perpetual dictatorship, the First Triumvirate and Spartacus's slave revolt precede Octavian's empire; Diocletian's tetrarchy fails and Constantine ends Rome's centrality, leaving law, language and letters behind.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
