# Ancient civilisations china — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Civilización china — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** China — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Dynasties from Xia to Qin rule the Yellow and Yangtze valleys; mandarins, silk, porcelain, paper, gunpowder and the compass mark China's gifts.
b) Shang and warring Chou lordships are unified by Qin Shi Huang-ti, the First Emperor; mandarins transmit Confucius's doctrine and silk caravans cross hostile deserts to Europe.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
