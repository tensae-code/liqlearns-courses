# Ancient civilisations china — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Civilización china", dynasties from Xia to Qin rule the Yellow and Yangtze valleys; mandarins, silk, porcelain, paper, gunpowder and the compass mark China's gifts (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "China", Shang and warring Chou lordships are unified by Qin Shi Huang-ti, the First Emperor; mandarins transmit Confucius's doctrine and silk caravans cross hostile deserts to Europe (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
