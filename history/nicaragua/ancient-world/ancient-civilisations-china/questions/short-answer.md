# Ancient civilisations china — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Civilización china" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Dynasties from Xia to Qin rule the Yellow and Yangtze valleys; mandarins, silk, porcelain, paper, gunpowder and the compass mark China's gifts.

**2.** Tell the story of "China" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Shang and warring Chou lordships are unified by Qin Shi Huang-ti, the First Emperor; mandarins transmit Confucius's doctrine and silk caravans cross hostile deserts to Europe.
