## Sub lesson 1 — Civilización china

Dynasties from Xia to Qin rule the Yellow and Yangtze valleys; mandarins, silk, porcelain, paper, gunpowder and the compass mark China's gifts.

## Sub lesson 2 — China

Shang and warring Chou lordships are unified by Qin Shi Huang-ti, the First Emperor; mandarins transmit Confucius's doctrine and silk caravans cross hostile deserts to Europe.
