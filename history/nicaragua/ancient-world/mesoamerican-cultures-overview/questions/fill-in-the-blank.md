# Mesoamerican cultures overview — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Culturas Mesoamericanas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mesoamerica is framed as one shared cultural world of farming, cities, calendars and gods before its two great cases are studied.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Nuestro tronco común: el Área Mesoamericana" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mexico and most of Central America are framed as one shared cultural world — stepped temple platforms, ball games, glyph writing, astronomy and a precise calendar — before Europeans arrive.
