# Mesoamerican cultures overview — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Culturas Mesoamericanas"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Mesoamerica is framed as one shared cultural world of farming, cities, calendars and gods before its two great cases are studied.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
