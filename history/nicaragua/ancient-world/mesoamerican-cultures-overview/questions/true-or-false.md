# Mesoamerican cultures overview — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Culturas Mesoamericanas", Mesoamerica is framed as one shared cultural world of farming, cities, calendars and gods before its two great cases are studied (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Nuestro tronco común: el Área Mesoamericana", Mexico and most of Central America are framed as one shared cultural world — stepped temple platforms, ball games, glyph writing, astronomy and a precise calendar — before Europeans arrive (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
