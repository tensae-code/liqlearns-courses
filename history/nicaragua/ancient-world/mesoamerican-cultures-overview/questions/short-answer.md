# Mesoamerican cultures overview — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Culturas Mesoamericanas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Mesoamerica is framed as one shared cultural world of farming, cities, calendars and gods before its two great cases are studied.

**2.** Tell the story of "Nuestro tronco común: el Área Mesoamericana" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mexico and most of Central America are framed as one shared cultural world — stepped temple platforms, ball games, glyph writing, astronomy and a precise calendar — before Europeans arrive.
