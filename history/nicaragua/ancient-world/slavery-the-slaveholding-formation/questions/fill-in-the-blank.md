# Slavery the slaveholding formation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "El Esclavismo" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** Metal tools let people produce more than they need, and the surplus opens the way to owning the producer himself — the slave can be sold, tortured or killed, yet the age still invents writing, irrigation, coinage and the pyramids.
