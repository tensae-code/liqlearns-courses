# The inca and the andes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "La Civilización Inca" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** A totalitarian Inca state runs two million square kilometres of Andes through curacas and family-group chiefs; terraced andenes, storehouses and the quipu knot-records hold the empire together without writing.
