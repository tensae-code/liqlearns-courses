# The peopling of the americas

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The peopling of the americas" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Teorías sobre el origen del poblamiento de América y Centroamérica

Humans are not native to America; Hrdlička's Bering theory has Siberian hunters crossing Beringia in the last glaciation, 14,000-13,000 BCE, while rival routes cross the Pacific.

## Sub lesson 2 — Primeros pobladores de América: teorías del poblamiento

Four rival theories compete — Hrdlička's single Asian crossing at Bering, Rivet's Pacific voyages, Ameghino's Argentine origin, and an Australian route via Antarctica.

## Sub lesson 3 — Formas de vida de los primeros pobladores de América

Nomad hunters following herds polish stone points; farming and animal domestication then settle them and raise the first urban centres.

## Sub lesson 4 — Testimonio de la presencia del ser humano: teorías sobre el poblamiento de América

Hrdlička's Asian hunters cross a land bridge some 50,000 years ago in his single-origin theory, while Rivet argues for additional Pacific crossings.

## Sub lesson 5 — Teorías sobre el origen del ser humano americano

Hrdlička's Asian hunters crossing Bering are the most accepted account, but a multiple-origin hypothesis adds Polynesians, Melanesians and Australians sailing or walking to South America.

## Sub lesson 6 — Forma de vida y organización de los primeros pobladores de América

Paleoindio mammoth hunters polish stone points for 33,000 years of story; when the ice ends about 7,000 years ago, farming settles the peoples and pottery, weaving and gold work follow.
