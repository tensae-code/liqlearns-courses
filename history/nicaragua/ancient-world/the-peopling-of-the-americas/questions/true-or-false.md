# The peopling of the americas — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Formas de vida de los primeros pobladores de América", nomad hunters following herds polish stone points; farming and animal domestication then settle them and raise the first urban centres (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Testimonio de la presencia del ser humano: teorías sobre el poblamiento de América", Hrdlička's Asian hunters cross a land bridge some 50,000 years ago in his single-origin theory, while Rivet argues for additional Pacific crossings (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
