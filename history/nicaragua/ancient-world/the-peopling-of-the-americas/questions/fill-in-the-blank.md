# The peopling of the americas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Teorías sobre el origen del poblamiento de América y Centroamérica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Humans are not native to America; Hrdlička's Bering theory has Siberian hunters crossing Beringia in the last glaciation, 14,000-13,000 BCE, while rival routes cross the Pacific.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Primeros pobladores de América: teorías del poblamiento" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Four rival theories compete — Hrdlička's single Asian crossing at Bering, Rivet's Pacific voyages, Ameghino's Argentine origin, and an Australian route via Antarctica.
