# The peopling of the americas — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Teorías sobre el origen del ser humano americano" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Hrdlička's Asian hunters crossing Bering are the most accepted account, but a multiple-origin hypothesis adds Polynesians, Melanesians and Australians sailing or walking to South America.

**2.** Tell the story of "Forma de vida y organización de los primeros pobladores de América" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Paleoindio mammoth hunters polish stone points for 33,000 years of story; when the ice ends about 7,000 years ago, farming settles the peoples and pottery, weaving and gold work follow.
