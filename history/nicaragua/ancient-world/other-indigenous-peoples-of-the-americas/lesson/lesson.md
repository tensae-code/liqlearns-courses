# Other indigenous peoples of the americas

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Other indigenous peoples of the americas" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

Inuit seal-hunters, politically advanced Iroquois whose women choose leaders, and Chile's Araucanians who resist Spain fill out the continent; today 50 million indigenous people, a tenth of Latin America, still live marginalised.
