# Other indigenous peoples of the americas — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Otras culturas indígenas de América y la población indígena en la actualidad" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**Answer:** Inuit seal-hunters, politically advanced Iroquois whose women choose leaders, and Chile's Araucanians who resist Spain fill out the continent; today 50 million indigenous people, a tenth of Latin America, still live marginalised.
