# The crusades — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Las Cruzadas" turns on this: __________.
**Answer:** At Byzantium's plea, Urban II calls Christendom in 1095 to recover the Holy Places; eight crusades over two centuries fail to hold Jerusalem but shake the feudal order that launched them.
