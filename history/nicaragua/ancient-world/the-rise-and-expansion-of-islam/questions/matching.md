# The rise and expansion of islam — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Civilización Árabe: Mahoma y el Islam — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Las conquistas árabes — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Orphaned Muhammad, a shepherd and trader married to the wealthy Khadija, receives the archangel Gabriel's call at forty and preaches one God, almsgiving, fasting and the pilgrimage to Mecca.
b) Arab conquest frees the Near East and North Africa from Greek, Roman and Byzantine colonialism and builds a new unified civilisation, while Visigothic Spain disappears and Byzantium shrinks.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
