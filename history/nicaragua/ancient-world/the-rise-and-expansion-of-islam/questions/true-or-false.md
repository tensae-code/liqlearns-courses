# The rise and expansion of islam — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Civilización Árabe: Mahoma y el Islam", orphaned Muhammad, a shepherd and trader married to the wealthy Khadija, receives the archangel Gabriel's call at forty and preaches one God, almsgiving, fasting and the pilgrimage to Mecca (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Las conquistas árabes", Arab conquest frees the Near East and North Africa from Greek, Roman and Byzantine colonialism and builds a new unified civilisation, while Visigothic Spain disappears and Byzantium shrinks (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
