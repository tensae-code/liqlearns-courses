# The rise and expansion of islam — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Civilización Árabe: Mahoma y el Islam" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Orphaned Muhammad, a shepherd and trader married to the wealthy Khadija, receives the archangel Gabriel's call at forty and preaches one God, almsgiving, fasting and the pilgrimage to Mecca.

**2.** Tell the story of "Las conquistas árabes" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Arab conquest frees the Near East and North Africa from Greek, Roman and Byzantine colonialism and builds a new unified civilisation, while Visigothic Spain disappears and Byzantium shrinks.
