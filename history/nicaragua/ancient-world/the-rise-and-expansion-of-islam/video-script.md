# Video script — The rise and expansion of islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The rise and expansion of islam.

Orphaned Muhammad, a shepherd and trader married to the wealthy Khadija, receives the archangel Gabriel's call at forty and preaches one God, almsgiving, fasting and the pilgrimage to Mecca.

Arab conquest frees the Near East and North Africa from Greek, Roman and Byzantine colonialism and builds a new unified civilisation, while Visigothic Spain disappears and Byzantium shrinks.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
