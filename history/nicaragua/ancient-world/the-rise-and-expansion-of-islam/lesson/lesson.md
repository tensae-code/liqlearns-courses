# The rise and expansion of islam

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "The rise and expansion of islam" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Civilización Árabe: Mahoma y el Islam

Orphaned Muhammad, a shepherd and trader married to the wealthy Khadija, receives the archangel Gabriel's call at forty and preaches one God, almsgiving, fasting and the pilgrimage to Mecca.

## Sub lesson 2 — Las conquistas árabes

Arab conquest frees the Near East and North Africa from Greek, Roman and Byzantine colonialism and builds a new unified civilisation, while Visigothic Spain disappears and Byzantium shrinks.
