# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Civilización mesopotámica", between the Tigris and Euphrates, kings, priests and scribes build a ranked society that invents cuneiform writing and Hammurabi's law code (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Mesopotamia", between the Tigris and Euphrates, Sumerians, Akkadians and Assyrians alternate dominion; deciphered cuneiform reveals the first contracts, law codes and cheque-like documents, and a seven-day week still in use (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
