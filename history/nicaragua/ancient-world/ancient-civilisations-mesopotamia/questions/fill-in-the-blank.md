# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Civilización mesopotámica" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Between the Tigris and Euphrates, kings, priests and scribes build a ranked society that invents cuneiform writing and Hammurabi's law code.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Mesopotamia" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Between the Tigris and Euphrates, Sumerians, Akkadians and Assyrians alternate dominion; deciphered cuneiform reveals the first contracts, law codes and cheque-like documents, and a seven-day week still in use.
