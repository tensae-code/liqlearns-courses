# Ancient civilisations mesopotamia — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Civilización mesopotámica — a) (Sub lesson 1)
**2.** Mesopotamia — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Between the Tigris and Euphrates, kings, priests and scribes build a ranked society that invents cuneiform writing and Hammurabi's law code.
b) Between the Tigris and Euphrates, Sumerians, Akkadians and Assyrians alternate dominion; deciphered cuneiform reveals the first contracts, law codes and cheque-like documents, and a seven-day week still in use.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
