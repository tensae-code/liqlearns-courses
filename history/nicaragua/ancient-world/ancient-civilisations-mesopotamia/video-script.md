# Video script — Ancient civilisations mesopotamia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Ancient civilisations mesopotamia.

Between the Tigris and Euphrates, kings, priests and scribes build a ranked society that invents cuneiform writing and Hammurabi's law code.

Between the Tigris and Euphrates, Sumerians, Akkadians and Assyrians alternate dominion; deciphered cuneiform reveals the first contracts, law codes and cheque-like documents, and a seven-day week still in use.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
