# River valley civilisations overview — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Civilizaciones agrícolas: los pueblos de los grandes ríos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Some 6,000 years ago peoples settle the floodplains of great rivers whose silt feeds big harvests, and cities grow around irrigation and trade.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Primeras civilizaciones agrícolas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The History half turns to the first farming civilisations, each studied under the same five headings — geography, politics, society, economy, culture.
