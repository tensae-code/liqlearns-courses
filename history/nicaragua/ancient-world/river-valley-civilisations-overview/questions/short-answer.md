# River valley civilisations overview — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Civilizaciones agrícolas: los pueblos de los grandes ríos" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Some 6,000 years ago peoples settle the floodplains of great rivers whose silt feeds big harvests, and cities grow around irrigation and trade.

**2.** Tell the story of "Primeras civilizaciones agrícolas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The History half turns to the first farming civilisations, each studied under the same five headings — geography, politics, society, economy, culture.
