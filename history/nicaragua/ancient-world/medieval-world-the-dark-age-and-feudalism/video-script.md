# Video script — Medieval world the dark age and feudalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Medieval world the dark age and feudalism.

The medieval world is framed as the ten centuries from the Western Empire's fall in 476 to Constantinople's in 1453; barbarian kingdoms fragment Roman unity into the map of medieval Europe.

Weak kings, invasions and land-as-wealth build feudalism — three fixed estates of fighters, prayers and workers bound by vassalage; the Church, the one institution left standing, fights kings over investitures and excommunicates Henry IV.

The fief is land held for military service; the serf pays his lord in kind, in labour days on the lord's fields with his own tools, or in money, while the self-sufficient fief economy barely develops.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
