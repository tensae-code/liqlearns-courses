# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Sistema Feudal" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Weak kings, invasions and land-as-wealth build feudalism — three fixed estates of fighters, prayers and workers bound by vassalage; the Church, the one institution left standing, fights kings over investitures and excommunicates Henry IV.

**2.** Tell the story of "El Feudalismo" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The fief is land held for military service; the serf pays his lord in kind, in labour days on the lord's fields with his own tools, or in money, while the self-sufficient fief economy barely develops.
