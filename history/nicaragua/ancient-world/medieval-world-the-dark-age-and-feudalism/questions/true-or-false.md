# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "El Feudalismo", the fief is land held for military service; the serf pays his lord in kind, in labour days on the lord's fields with his own tools, or in money, while the self-sufficient fief economy barely develops (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Las invasiones de los Bárbaros", the medieval world is framed as the ten centuries from the Western Empire's fall in 476 to Constantinople's in 1453; barbarian kingdoms fragment Roman unity into the map of medieval Europe (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
