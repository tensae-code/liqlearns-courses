# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** El Feudalismo — a) (Sub lesson 3)
**2.** Las invasiones de los Bárbaros — b) (Sub lesson 1)

Meanings:
a) The fief is land held for military service; the serf pays his lord in kind, in labour days on the lord's fields with his own tools, or in money, while the self-sufficient fief economy barely develops.
b) The medieval world is framed as the ten centuries from the Western Empire's fall in 476 to Constantinople's in 1453; barbarian kingdoms fragment Roman unity into the map of medieval Europe.
