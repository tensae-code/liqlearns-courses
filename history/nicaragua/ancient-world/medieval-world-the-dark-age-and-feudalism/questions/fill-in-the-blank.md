# Medieval world the dark age and feudalism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Las invasiones de los Bárbaros" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The medieval world is framed as the ten centuries from the Western Empire's fall in 476 to Constantinople's in 1453; barbarian kingdoms fragment Roman unity into the map of medieval Europe.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Sistema Feudal" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Weak kings, invasions and land-as-wealth build feudalism — three fixed estates of fighters, prayers and workers bound by vassalage; the Church, the one institution left standing, fights kings over investitures and excommunicates Henry IV.
