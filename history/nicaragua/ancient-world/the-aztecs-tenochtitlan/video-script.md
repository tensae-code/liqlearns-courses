# Video script — The aztecs tenochtitlan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: The aztecs tenochtitlan.

The Aztecs organise an empire from Tenochtitlan, a lake city turned capital of tribute and war.

An emperor, tribute provinces and chinampa farming power the Aztec state until its world ends at contact.

The Gran Tlatoani rules through a Great Council that can depose him; chinampa-fed Tenochtitlan feeds 25 million people, and war captives feed the sacrifices instead.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
