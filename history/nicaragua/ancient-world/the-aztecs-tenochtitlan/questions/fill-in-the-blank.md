# The aztecs tenochtitlan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Civilización Azteca: organización" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Aztecs organise an empire from Tenochtitlan, a lake city turned capital of tribute and war.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Sistema de gobierno y economía de los Aztecas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An emperor, tribute provinces and chinampa farming power the Aztec state until its world ends at contact.
