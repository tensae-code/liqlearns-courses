# The aztecs tenochtitlan — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "La Civilización Azteca", the Gran Tlatoani rules through a Great Council that can depose him; chinampa-fed Tenochtitlan feeds 25 million people, and war captives feed the sacrifices instead (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Civilización Azteca: organización", the Aztecs organise an empire from Tenochtitlan, a lake city turned capital of tribute and war (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
