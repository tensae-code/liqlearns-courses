# The aztecs tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Sistema de gobierno y economía de los Aztecas" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An emperor, tribute provinces and chinampa farming power the Aztec state until its world ends at contact.

**2.** Tell the story of "La Civilización Azteca" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The Gran Tlatoani rules through a Great Council that can depose him; chinampa-fed Tenochtitlan feeds 25 million people, and war captives feed the sacrifices instead.
