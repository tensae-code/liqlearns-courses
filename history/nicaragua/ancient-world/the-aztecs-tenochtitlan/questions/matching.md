# The aztecs tenochtitlan — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** La Civilización Azteca — a) (Sub lesson 3)
**2.** Civilización Azteca: organización — b) (Sub lesson 1)

Meanings:
a) The Gran Tlatoani rules through a Great Council that can depose him; chinampa-fed Tenochtitlan feeds 25 million people, and war captives feed the sacrifices instead.
b) The Aztecs organise an empire from Tenochtitlan, a lake city turned capital of tribute and war.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
