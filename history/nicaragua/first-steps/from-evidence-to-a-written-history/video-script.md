# Video script — From evidence to a written history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: From evidence to a written history.

The evidence is walked over and looked at — the Acahualinca footprints in Managua, Chontales idols, petroglyphs and shell mounds at Monkey Point and El Ostional.

Shell middens at Monkey Point dated 7,600 years old, mammoth bones with stone tools at El Bosque, and human footprints preserved at Acahualinca are walked over as the physical proof of the first Nicaraguans.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
