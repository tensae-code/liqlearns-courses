# From evidence to a written history — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Testimonios de los primeros pobladores", the evidence is walked over and looked at — the Acahualinca footprints in Managua, Chontales idols, petroglyphs and shell mounds at Monkey Point and El Ostional (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Testimonios en Nicaragua: Monkey Point, El Bosque y las huellas de Acahualinca", shell middens at Monkey Point dated 7,600 years old, mammoth bones with stone tools at El Bosque, and human footprints preserved at Acahualinca are walked over as the physical proof of the first Nicaraguans (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
