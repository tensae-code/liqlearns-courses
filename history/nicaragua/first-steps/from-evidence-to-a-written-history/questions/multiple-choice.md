# From evidence to a written history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Testimonios de los primeros pobladores"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The evidence is walked over and looked at — the Acahualinca footprints in Managua, Chontales idols, petroglyphs and shell mounds at Monkey Point and El Ostional.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Testimonios en Nicaragua: Monkey Point, El Bosque y las huellas de Acahualinca"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Shell middens at Monkey Point dated 7,600 years old, mammoth bones with stone tools at El Bosque, and human footprints preserved at Acahualinca are walked over as the physical proof of the first Nicaraguans.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
