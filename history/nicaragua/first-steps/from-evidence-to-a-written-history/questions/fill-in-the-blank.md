# From evidence to a written history — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Testimonios de los primeros pobladores" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The evidence is walked over and looked at — the Acahualinca footprints in Managua, Chontales idols, petroglyphs and shell mounds at Monkey Point and El Ostional.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Testimonios en Nicaragua: Monkey Point, El Bosque y las huellas de Acahualinca" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Shell middens at Monkey Point dated 7,600 years old, mammoth bones with stone tools at El Bosque, and human footprints preserved at Acahualinca are walked over as the physical proof of the first Nicaraguans.
