# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Testimonios de los primeros pobladores" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The evidence is walked over and looked at — the Acahualinca footprints in Managua, Chontales idols, petroglyphs and shell mounds at Monkey Point and El Ostional.

**2.** Tell the story of "Testimonios en Nicaragua: Monkey Point, El Bosque y las huellas de Acahualinca" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Shell middens at Monkey Point dated 7,600 years old, mammoth bones with stone tools at El Bosque, and human footprints preserved at Acahualinca are walked over as the physical proof of the first Nicaraguans.
