# From evidence to a written history

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "From evidence to a written history" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Testimonios de los primeros pobladores

The evidence is walked over and looked at — the Acahualinca footprints in Managua, Chontales idols, petroglyphs and shell mounds at Monkey Point and El Ostional.

## Sub lesson 2 — Testimonios en Nicaragua: Monkey Point, El Bosque y las huellas de Acahualinca

Shell middens at Monkey Point dated 7,600 years old, mammoth bones with stone tools at El Bosque, and human footprints preserved at Acahualinca are walked over as the physical proof of the first Nicaraguans.
