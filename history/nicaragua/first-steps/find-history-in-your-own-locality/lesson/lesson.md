# Find history in your own locality

## Do it first

Before you read: say what you already know about "Find history in your own locality" — a name, a place, a date, anything. Write it down. This lesson tells the story properly; at the end, check what you said against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Personajes destacados de su municipio

Each child finds the outstanding people of their own municipality and tells who they were and what they did there.

## Sub lesson 2 — Paseo para conocer hechos, monumentos y lugares históricos de su municipio

A class walk visits the municipality's own historical events, monuments and places, including the Sandino monument on Loma de Tiscapa and the San Jacinto hero Andrés Castro's monument.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hechos y personas relevantes del pasado de mi municipio

Each municipality has its own relevant past events and people; pupils research and retell theirs.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the story of Find history in your own locality as Nicaragua's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. Retell the story — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's story in your own words.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the story happens in real life. Note it.
