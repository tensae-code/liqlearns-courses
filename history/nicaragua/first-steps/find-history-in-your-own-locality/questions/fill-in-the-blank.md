# Find history in your own locality — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Personajes destacados de su municipio" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Each child finds the outstanding people of their own municipality and tells who they were and what they did there.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Paseo para conocer hechos, monumentos y lugares históricos de su municipio" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A class walk visits the municipality's own historical events, monuments and places, including the Sandino monument on Loma de Tiscapa and the San Jacinto hero Andrés Castro's monument.
