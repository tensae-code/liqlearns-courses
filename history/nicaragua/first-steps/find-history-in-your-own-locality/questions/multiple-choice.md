# Find history in your own locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Personajes destacados de su municipio"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Each child finds the outstanding people of their own municipality and tells who they were and what they did there.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Paseo para conocer hechos, monumentos y lugares históricos de su municipio"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) A class walk visits the municipality's own historical events, monuments and places, including the Sandino monument on Loma de Tiscapa and the San Jacinto hero Andrés Castro's monument.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
