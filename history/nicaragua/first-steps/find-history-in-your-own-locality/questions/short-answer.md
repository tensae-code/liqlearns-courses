# Find history in your own locality — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Paseo para conocer hechos, monumentos y lugares históricos de su municipio" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A class walk visits the municipality's own historical events, monuments and places, including the Sandino monument on Loma de Tiscapa and the San Jacinto hero Andrés Castro's monument.

**2.** Tell the story of "Hechos y personas relevantes del pasado de mi municipio" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Each municipality has its own relevant past events and people; pupils research and retell theirs.
