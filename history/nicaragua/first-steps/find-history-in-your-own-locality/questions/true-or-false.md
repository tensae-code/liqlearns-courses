# Find history in your own locality — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Hechos y personas relevantes del pasado de mi municipio", each municipality has its own relevant past events and people; pupils research and retell theirs (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Personajes destacados de su municipio", each child finds the outstanding people of their own municipality and tells who they were and what they did there (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
