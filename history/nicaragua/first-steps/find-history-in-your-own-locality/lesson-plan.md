# Lesson plan — Find history in your own locality

**Course:** History — Nicaragua · **Title:** First steps · **Lesson:** Find history in your own locality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my homeland Nicaragua, its symbols and heroes, and the first peoples of my municipality and department (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Find history in your own locality as Nicaragua's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my homeland Nicaragua, its symbols and heroes, and the first peoples of my municipality and department — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Personajes destacados de su municipio** — Each child finds the outstanding people of their own municipality and tells who they were and what they did there.
2. **§2 Paseo para conocer hechos, monumentos y lugares históricos de su municipio** — A class walk visits the municipality's own historical events, monuments and places, including the Sandino monument on Loma de Tiscapa and the San Jacinto hero Andrés Castro's monument.
3. **§3 Hechos y personas relevantes del pasado de mi municipio** — Each municipality has its own relevant past events and people; pupils research and retell theirs.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
