# Video script — Find history in your own locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: Find history in your own locality.

Each child finds the outstanding people of their own municipality and tells who they were and what they did there.

A class walk visits the municipality's own historical events, monuments and places, including the Sandino monument on Loma de Tiscapa and the San Jacinto hero Andrés Castro's monument.

Each municipality has its own relevant past events and people; pupils research and retell theirs.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
