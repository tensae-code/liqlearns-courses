## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Introducción al estudio de la Historia** — History is introduced as a school subject in its own right, the story of collective human experience past and present.
2. **§2 Objeto de estudio e importancia de la Historia** — What History studies and why it matters are argued explicitly — a people that knows its past understands its present.
3. **§3 Prehistoria e Historia: la división del pasado** — The past is split at the invention of writing; before it, archaeologists rebuild prehistory from remains, after it, written history begins.
4. **§4 Las primeras sociedades humanas** — History studies humanity's evolution; before writing there is only prehistory, recoverable solely from paleontological and archaeological remains.
