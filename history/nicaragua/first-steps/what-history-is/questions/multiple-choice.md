# What history is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Introducción al estudio de la Historia"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) History is introduced as a school subject in its own right, the story of collective human experience past and present.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Objeto de estudio e importancia de la Historia"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) What History studies and why it matters are argued explicitly — a people that knows its past understands its present.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
