# What history is — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** True or false: In "Prehistoria e Historia: la división del pasado", the past is split at the invention of writing; before it, archaeologists rebuild prehistory from remains, after it, written history begins (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.

**2.** True or false: In "Las primeras sociedades humanas", History studies humanity's evolution; before writing there is only prehistory, recoverable solely from paleontological and archaeological remains (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: True** — That is the story as this lesson teaches it.
