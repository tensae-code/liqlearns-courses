# What history is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Prehistoria e Historia: la división del pasado" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The past is split at the invention of writing; before it, archaeologists rebuild prehistory from remains, after it, written history begins.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Las primeras sociedades humanas" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** History studies humanity's evolution; before writing there is only prehistory, recoverable solely from paleontological and archaeological remains.
