# Video script — What history is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story — an animated map: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson tells one story: What history is.

History is introduced as a school subject in its own right, the story of collective human experience past and present.

What History studies and why it matters are argued explicitly — a people that knows its past understands its present.

The past is split at the invention of writing; before it, archaeologists rebuild prehistory from remains, after it, written history begins.

History studies humanity's evolution; before writing there is only prehistory, recoverable solely from paleontological and archaeological remains.

That is the turn of this story — remember who was in it, where it happened, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
