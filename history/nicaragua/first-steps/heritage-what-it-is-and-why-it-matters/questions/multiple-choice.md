# Heritage what it is and why it matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in "Tradiciones representativas de su comunidad (fiestas patronales, trajes y bailes)"? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Each community's patron-saint festival, costumes and dances are presented as inherited celebrations the children must recognise as their own.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens in "Costumbres y tradiciones de mi municipio"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Customs handed down in the municipality are collected as living heritage the children themselves practise.  b) a story with no people and no place  c) a list of dates to copy without a story  d) nothing changes and nothing comes of it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
