# Heritage what it is and why it matters — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** Tell the story of "Legado cultural de los primeros pobladores" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** What the first peoples left — language, food, craft, belief — is named a cultural legacy still present in Nicaragua today.

**2.** Tell the story of "Legado cultural de la época colonial" — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** The colonial centuries also leave a mixed cultural legacy — language, religion, towns and customs that Nicaraguans still live.
