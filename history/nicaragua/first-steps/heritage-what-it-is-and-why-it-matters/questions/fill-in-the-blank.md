# Heritage what it is and why it matters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Legado cultural de los primeros grupos humanos" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 7)
**Answer:** Prehistory's legacy is counted in four columns — farming surpluses, social ranks, the first cities and states, and cave art, ritual dance and burial customs.

**2.** Fill in the blank: The story of "Legado cultural de la Colonia (española e inglesa)" turns on this: __________. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** Two colonial legacies are compared — Spanish Catholic imposition resisted and kept alive in secret on the Pacific side, English-backed Miskitu self-rule on the Caribbean side.
