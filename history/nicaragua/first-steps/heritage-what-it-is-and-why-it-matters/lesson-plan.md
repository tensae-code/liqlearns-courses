# Lesson plan — Heritage what it is and why it matters

**Course:** History — Nicaragua · **Title:** First steps · **Lesson:** Heritage what it is and why it matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my homeland Nicaragua, its symbols and heroes, and the first peoples of my municipality and department (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the story of Heritage what it is and why it matters as Nicaragua's own books tell it — who was in it, where it happened, what turned, and what came of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First stories at home: my homeland Nicaragua, its symbols and heroes, and the first peoples of my municipality and department — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§1 Tradiciones representativas de su comunidad (fiestas patronales, trajes y bailes)** — Each community's patron-saint festival, costumes and dances are presented as inherited celebrations the children must recognise as their own.
2. **§2 Costumbres y tradiciones de mi municipio** — Customs handed down in the municipality are collected as living heritage the children themselves practise.
3. **§3 Arte, cultura y tradición de los primeros pobladores** — Indigenous art and tradition survive as the department's cultural legacy, not a vanished past.
4. **§4 Celebraciones tradicionales: costumbres, tradiciones y creencias de mi departamento** — Traditional celebrations carry the department's beliefs and customs forward each year.
5. **§5 Legado cultural de los primeros pobladores** — What the first peoples left — language, food, craft, belief — is named a cultural legacy still present in Nicaragua today.
6. **§6 Legado cultural de la época colonial** — The colonial centuries also leave a mixed cultural legacy — language, religion, towns and customs that Nicaraguans still live.
7. **§7 Legado cultural de los primeros grupos humanos** — Prehistory's legacy is counted in four columns — farming surpluses, social ranks, the first cities and states, and cave art, ritual dance and burial customs.
8. **§8 Legado cultural de la Colonia (española e inglesa)** — Two colonial legacies are compared — Spanish Catholic imposition resisted and kept alive in secret on the Pacific side, English-backed Miskitu self-rule on the Caribbean side.
9. **§9 Legado cultural de la Edad Media** — The medieval legacy is counted in Romanesque round arches and Gothic pointed ones, Dante's journey through hell, purgatory and heaven, and the chivalric code of courtesy and protecting the weak.
