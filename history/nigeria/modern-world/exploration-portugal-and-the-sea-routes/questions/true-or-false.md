# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Stanley's letter home from Buganda helped bring the missionaries to the kingdom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Speke, Grant and Stanley reached Buganda as traders of the Zanzibar caravans.
**Answer: False** — They were explorers hunting the source of the Nile — the first Europeans in Buganda.
