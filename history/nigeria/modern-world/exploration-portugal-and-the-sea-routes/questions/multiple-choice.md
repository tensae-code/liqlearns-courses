# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) The first Europeans in Buganda — Speke, Grant and Stanley — came hunting the source of the Nile; Mutesa welcomed them because he wanted European guns against Egypt, and Stanley's letter home brought the missionaries.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) The first Europeans in Buganda — Speke, Grant and Stanley — came hunting the source of the Nile; Mutesa welcomed them because he wanted European guns against Egypt, and Stanley's letter home brought the missionaries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why did Mutesa welcome the European explorers?
a) He wanted to convert to Christianity at once  b) He wanted European guns against Egypt  c) He wanted to sell his kingdom's cloves  d) He wanted maps of the Cape Colony
**Answer: b) He wanted European guns against Egypt** — That is what this lesson teaches.
