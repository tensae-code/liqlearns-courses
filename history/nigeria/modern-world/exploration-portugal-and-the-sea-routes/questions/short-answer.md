# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What were Speke, Grant and Stanley hunting?
**Answer:** The source of the Nile — Europe's great geographical prize, which drew them to the lake kingdoms.

**2.** What did Stanley's letter home set in motion?
**Answer:** It praised Buganda and called for Christian teachers, and the missionaries answered — arriving to a court their rival teachings would soon divide.
