# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the comparative method of this course?
**Answer:** Hold two states side by side, ask the same questions of each, and learn from where their answers match and where they differ.

**2.** Who built this series and framed its method?
**Answer:** The curriculum's own authors — the series grew from the NERDC workshops at Lagos in 1985 and Kano in 1986, as the placements file records the preface framing it.
