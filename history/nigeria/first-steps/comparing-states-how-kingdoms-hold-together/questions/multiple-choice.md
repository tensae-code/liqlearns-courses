# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Nigeria's history course teaches the past by holding two states side by side — Dahomey against Buganda, Egypt against Zanzibar — so the learner can see how each state answered the same century in its own way.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Nigeria's history course teaches the past by holding two states side by side — Dahomey against Buganda, Egypt against Zanzibar — so the learner can see how each state answered the same century in its own way.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which pair does the book's preface itself set side by side?
a) Nigeria against Britain, and France against Germany  b) Dahomey against Buganda, and Egypt against Zanzibar  c) Rome against Greece, and China against India  d) Lagos against Kano, and Accra against Abidjan
**Answer: b) Dahomey against Buganda, and Egypt against Zanzibar** — That is what this lesson teaches.
