# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Nigeria's history course teaches the past by holding two states side by side — Dahomey against Buganda, Egypt against Zanzibar — so the learner can see how each state answered the same century in its own way. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
