# Southern Africa's First Peoples: San, Khoikhoi and Bantu — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name Southern Africa's three early ways of life in the book's order.
**Answer:** San hunter-gatherers, Khoikhoi herders, and Bantu-speaking farming chiefdoms.

**2.** Why does this lesson come before Shaka and the Mfecane?
**Answer:** Because every later drama plays out on land already peopled and claimed by the San, the Khoikhoi and the Bantu chiefdoms — the first peoples are the foundation of the region's story.
