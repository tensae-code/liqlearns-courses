# Southern Africa's First Peoples: San, Khoikhoi and Bantu — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The San are described as click-speaking hunter-gatherers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Bantu farming chiefdoms were the first people ever to live in Southern Africa.
**Answer: False** — The San and Khoikhoi held the land for centuries before the Bantu farming chiefdoms spread across it.
