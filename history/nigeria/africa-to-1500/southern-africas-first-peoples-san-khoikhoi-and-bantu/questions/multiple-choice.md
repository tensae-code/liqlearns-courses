# Southern Africa's First Peoples: San, Khoikhoi and Bantu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Southern Africa's first peoples were the click-speaking San hunter-gatherers and the Khoikhoi herders, who held the land for centuries before Bantu farming chiefdoms spread across it.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Southern Africa's first peoples were the click-speaking San hunter-gatherers and the Khoikhoi herders, who held the land for centuries before Bantu farming chiefdoms spread across it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What made the Khoikhoi different from the San in the book's survey?
a) The Khoikhoi were farmers with iron hoes; the San were herders  b) The Khoikhoi were herders of cattle and sheep; the San were hunter-gatherers  c) The Khoikhoi lived in stone cities; the San lived at sea  d) There was no difference; the names mean the same people
**Answer: b) The Khoikhoi were herders of cattle and sheep; the San were hunter-gatherers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
