# Hands-on — Ethiopia: Kingdoms and Faiths Before 1800

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Most of this course's lands were partitioned. One was not. Before meeting the emperors who reunited it, walk its deep background: mountains and lowlands, an ancient Christian kingdom, a second faith arriving, and a capital of stone castles. This is the survey the book gives before Theodore — learn the land before its unifier.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Before Theodore, Ethiopia already carried a long history — highland and lowland peoples, Aksum's ancient Christianity, Islam's arrival, and the emperors of Gondar — the background to a country that was never colonised. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
