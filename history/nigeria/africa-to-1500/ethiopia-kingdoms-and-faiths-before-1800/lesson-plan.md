# Lesson plan — Ethiopia: Kingdoms and Faiths Before 1800

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ethiopia: Kingdoms and Faiths Before 1800
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Buganda and Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before Theodore, Ethiopia already carried a long history — highland and lowland peoples, Aksum's ancient Christianity, Islam's arrival, and the emperors of Gondar — the background to a country that was never colonised.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Buganda and Ethiopia — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
