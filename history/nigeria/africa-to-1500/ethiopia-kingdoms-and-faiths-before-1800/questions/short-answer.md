# Ethiopia: Kingdoms and Faiths Before 1800 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What are the two worlds of Ethiopia's stage?
**Answer:** The highlands — cool, mountainous, plough-farming — and the lowlands around them, home to pastoral peoples.

**2.** Name two inheritances of Ethiopia before 1800.
**Answer:** Aksum's ancient Christianity (with its church, manuscripts and stone churches) and the Gondar emperors' stone-caste capital — alongside Islam's arrival in the region.
