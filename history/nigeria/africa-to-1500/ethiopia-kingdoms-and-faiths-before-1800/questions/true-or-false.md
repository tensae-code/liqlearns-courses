# Ethiopia: Kingdoms and Faiths Before 1800 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Islam arrived in the region and Muslim communities lived alongside the Christian highland kingdom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ethiopia was partitioned in the scramble like the sultanate of Zanzibar.
**Answer: False** — Ethiopia was never colonised — it met the scramble with a state and story already centuries deep.
