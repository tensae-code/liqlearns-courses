# Ethiopia: Kingdoms and Faiths Before 1800 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Before Theodore, Ethiopia already carried a long history — highland and lowland peoples, Aksum's ancient Christianity, Islam's arrival, and the emperors of Gondar — the background to a country that was never colonised. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
