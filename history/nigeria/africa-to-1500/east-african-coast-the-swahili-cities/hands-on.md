# Hands-on — The East African Coast: The Swahili Cities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at a map of Africa's east coast: a long shore facing Arabia and Persia across one ocean. Ask: what happens to the people of a shore when sailors from across that ocean settle, marry in, and stay for centuries? This lesson answers with a whole new people.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On the East African coast, Bantu islanders and mainlanders intermarried with Arab and Persian settlers, and a new coastal people — the Waswahili — and their city-states were born. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
