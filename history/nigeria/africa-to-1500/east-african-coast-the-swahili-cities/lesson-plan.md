# Lesson plan — The East African Coast: The Swahili Cities

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** The East African Coast: The Swahili Cities
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Method and the East African Coast (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On the East African coast, Bantu islanders and mainlanders intermarried with Arab and Persian settlers, and a new coastal people — the Waswahili — and their city-states were born.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Method and the East African Coast — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: On the East African coast, Bantu islanders and mainlanders intermarried with Arab and Persian settlers, and a new coastal people — the Waswahili — and their city-states were born.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a market town the learner knows where two peoples married and mixed until their children were a people of their own.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.

## The run of the lesson (in order)

2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Swahili were Arabs who conquered the coast → the Waswahili were a new people born on the coast itself, from Bantu islanders intermarrying with Arab and Persian settlers over centuries.
- Go back to the one thing: On the East African coast, Bantu islanders and mainlanders intermarried with Arab and Persian settlers, and a new coastal people — the Waswahili — and their city-states were born. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
