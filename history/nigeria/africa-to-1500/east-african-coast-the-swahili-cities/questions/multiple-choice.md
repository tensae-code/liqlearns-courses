# The East African Coast: The Swahili Cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) On the East African coast, Bantu islanders and mainlanders intermarried with Arab and Persian settlers, and a new coastal people — the Waswahili — and their city-states were born.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) On the East African coast, Bantu islanders and mainlanders intermarried with Arab and Persian settlers, and a new coastal people — the Waswahili — and their city-states were born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who were the Shirazi in the Swahili towns?
a) Portuguese soldiers who garrisoned the coast  b) Families who claimed descent from Persian settlers of Shiraz and held pride of place  c) Omani sultans who ruled from Zanzibar  d) Bantu farmers who refused all sea trade
**Answer: b) Families who claimed descent from Persian settlers of Shiraz and held pride of place** — That is what this lesson teaches.
