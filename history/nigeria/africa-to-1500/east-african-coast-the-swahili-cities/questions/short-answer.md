# The East African Coast: The Swahili Cities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How were the Waswahili born?
**Answer:** Bantu islanders and coastal people intermarried with Arab and Persian settlers over centuries, and their descendants formed a new coastal people, the Waswahili.

**2.** Name two islands or places of the coast named in this part of the book.
**Answer:** Zanzibar and Pemba (the coastlands facing the Indian Ocean).
