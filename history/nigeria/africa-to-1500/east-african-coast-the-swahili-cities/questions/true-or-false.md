# The East African Coast: The Swahili Cities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Waswahili were a new coastal people born of intermarriage between Bantu islanders and Arab and Persian settlers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Swahili city-states were ruled as one empire by a single king.
**Answer: False** — Each city-state ruled itself; there was no single king over the coast.
