# Video script — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Protestant and Catholic missions arrived in Buganda a week apart, each told Mutesa the other was not a proper Christian, and the Kabaka's court split into rival religious parties.

Answering Stanley's call, the missions came to Buganda almost together: the Protestant Church Missionary Society and the French Catholic missionaries arrived within a week of each other. Buganda now had three faiths at court — Islam, brought by the Arab traders, and two rival Christianities.

Each mission warned Mutesa against the other: each told the Kabaka that the other party was not a proper Christian. The court did what courts do with rival teachings — it took sides. Converts gathered into religious parties, Protestant, Catholic and Muslim, and loyalty to a faith began to rival loyalty to clan and king.

A divided court is a weakened kingdom. The parties Mutesa had tried to balance became factions with foreign patrons and foreign guns behind them — the fuel of the civil wars that open in the next lesson, when a new Kabaka tried to master parties no king had created and no king could now dismiss.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Nigeria.
