# Hands-on — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two teachers arrive in the same town in the same week, each saying the other's teaching is false. The town must choose — and choosing divides it. That is what happened to a whole kingdom's court. Ask, as you read: who gained when the court split?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Protestant and Catholic missions arrived in Buganda a week apart, each told Mutesa the other was not a proper Christian, and the Kabaka's court split into rival religious parties. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
