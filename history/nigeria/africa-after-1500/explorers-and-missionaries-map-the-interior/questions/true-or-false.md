# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The court's converts gathered into rival religious parties — Protestant, Catholic and Muslim.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Mutesa succeeded in keeping all the new faiths out of his court.
**Answer: False** — The faiths entered the court and divided it; Mutesa tried to balance parties he could not dismiss.
