# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did each mission tell Mutesa about the other?
**Answer:** That the other was not a proper Christian — each warned the Kabaka against its rival.

**2.** Why did the split court weaken Buganda?
**Answer:** Loyalty to a religious party began to rival loyalty to clan and king, and the factions had foreign patrons and guns behind them — fuel for civil war.
