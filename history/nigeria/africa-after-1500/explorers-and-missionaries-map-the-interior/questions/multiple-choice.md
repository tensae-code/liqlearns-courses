# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Protestant and Catholic missions arrived in Buganda a week apart, each told Mutesa the other was not a proper Christian, and the Kabaka's court split into rival religious parties.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Protestant and Catholic missions arrived in Buganda a week apart, each told Mutesa the other was not a proper Christian, and the Kabaka's court split into rival religious parties.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How far apart did the Protestant and Catholic missions arrive in Buganda?
a) Exactly ten years apart  b) Within a week of each other  c) On the same ship, as one mission  d) A generation apart, Catholics first
**Answer: b) Within a week of each other** — That is what this lesson teaches.
