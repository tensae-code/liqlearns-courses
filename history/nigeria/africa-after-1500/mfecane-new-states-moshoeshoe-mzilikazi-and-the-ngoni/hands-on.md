# Hands-on — The Mfecane: New States — Moshoeshoe, Mzilikazi and the Ngoni

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A stone dropped in a pond does not only move the water it touches. Shaka's wars scattered peoples hundreds of kilometres — and the scattered did not vanish: they built. Four new states rise in this lesson from the scattering. Trace each one's road, and notice what the emptied land behind them invited.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Mfecane — the scattering set off by Shaka's wars — drove peoples who built new kingdoms far away: Moshoeshoe's Lesotho, Mzilikazi's Ndebele, Soshangane's Gaza and the Ngoni — while emptied land invited the Boers in. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
