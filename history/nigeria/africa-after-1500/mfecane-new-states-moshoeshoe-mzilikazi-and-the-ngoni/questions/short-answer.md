# The Mfecane: New States — Moshoeshoe, Mzilikazi and the Ngoni — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four new states the lesson builds from the scattering.
**Answer:** Moshoeshoe's Lesotho, Mzilikazi's Ndebele, Soshangane's Gaza, and the Ngoni (who migrated far north).

**2.** What did the emptied land invite?
**Answer:** The Boers: trekking farmers read the war-thinned land as vacant and moved in — the Trek of the next lessons.
