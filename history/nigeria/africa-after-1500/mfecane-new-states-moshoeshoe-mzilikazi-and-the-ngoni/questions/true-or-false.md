# The Mfecane: New States — Moshoeshoe, Mzilikazi and the Ngoni — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mzilikazi broke from Shaka and founded the Ndebele kingdom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The land emptied by the Mfecane was truly uninhabited and unclaimed.
**Answer: False** — It was thinned by war and flight — its peoples scattered, not erased — yet the Boers read it as vacant and moved in.
