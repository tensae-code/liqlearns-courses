# The Mfecane: New States — Moshoeshoe, Mzilikazi and the Ngoni — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) The Mfecane — the scattering set off by Shaka's wars — drove peoples who built new kingdoms far away: Moshoeshoe's Lesotho, Mzilikazi's Ndebele, Soshangane's Gaza and the Ngoni — while emptied land invited the Boers in.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) The Mfecane — the scattering set off by Shaka's wars — drove peoples who built new kingdoms far away: Moshoeshoe's Lesotho, Mzilikazi's Ndebele, Soshangane's Gaza and the Ngoni — while emptied land invited the Boers in.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which kingdom did Moshoeshoe build?
a) Gaza, in the east  b) Lesotho, gathered on a mountain stronghold  c) The Ndebele kingdom in the north  d) The Zulu kingdom itself
**Answer: b) Lesotho, gathered on a mountain stronghold** — That is what this lesson teaches.
