# Lesson plan — White Settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** White Settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Southern Africa: Revolution, Trek and Union (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From 1835 some 6,000 Voortrekkers sold up and wagonned north out of British rule to found free Boer states — fighting Dingane's Zulu and Mzilikazi's Ndebele on the way, and building Natal, the Orange Free State and the Transvaal on a racial order that ranked every other race below Europeans.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Southern Africa: Revolution, Trek and Union — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Great Trek: meaning, causes and course** — Some 6,000 Voortrekkers sell up and wagon north in parties from 1835, leaving British rule for good to found free Boer states in Natal and across the Vaal.
2. **Trek wars with the Zulu and Ndebele and the Boer republics, 1836–1858** — The trekkers fight Dingane and Mzilikazi, found Natal, the Orange Free State and the Transvaal, and write a racial order that ranks every other race below Europeans.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: From 1835 some 6,000 Voortrekkers sold up and wagonned north out of British rule to found free Boer states — fighting Dingane's Zulu and Mzilikazi's Ndebele on the way, and building Natal, the Orange Free State and the Transvaal on a racial order that ranked every other race below Europeans.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a group the learner knows that left a place to be free of its rules — and the question of what rules they then made for others.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the Trek founded one united Boer nation at once → it founded separate republics in sequence — Natal, then the Orange Free State and the Transvaal to 1858 — after wars with Dingane and Mzilikazi, and on a written racial order.
- Go back to the one thing: From 1835 some 6,000 Voortrekkers sold up and wagonned north out of British rule to found free Boer states — fighting Dingane's Zulu and Mzilikazi's Ndebele on the way, and building Natal, the Orange Free State and the Transvaal on a racial order that ranked every other race below Europeans. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
