# White Settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Boer republics wrote a racial order ranking every other race below Europeans. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The trekkers numbered some 60,000 in the first movement from 1835. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The book's figure is some 6,000 Voortrekkers in the first movement.
