# White Settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
a) From 1835 some 6,000 Voortrekkers sold up and wagonned north out of British rule to found free Boer states — fighting Dingane's Zulu and Mzilikazi's Ndebele on the way, and building Natal, the Orange Free State and the Transvaal on a racial order that ranked every other race below Europeans.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) From 1835 some 6,000 Voortrekkers sold up and wagonned north out of British rule to found free Boer states — fighting Dingane's Zulu and Mzilikazi's Ndebele on the way, and building Natal, the Orange Free State and the Transvaal on a racial order that ranked every other race below Europeans.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which peoples did the trekkers fight in the wars of 1836–1858? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Moshoeshoe's Sotho and the Swahili towns  b) Dingane's Zulu and Mzilikazi's Ndebele  c) Buganda and the Omani Arabs  d) The San and Khoikhoi only
**Answer: b) Dingane's Zulu and Mzilikazi's Ndebele** — That is what this lesson teaches.
