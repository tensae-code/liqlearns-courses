# White Settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did the Voortrekkers leave, and what did they mean to found? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** They sold up and left British rule at the Cape for good, to found free Boer states — in Natal and across the Vaal — under their own law, language and labour order.

**2.** Name the three Boer republics of this lesson. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Natal, the Orange Free State and the Transvaal.
