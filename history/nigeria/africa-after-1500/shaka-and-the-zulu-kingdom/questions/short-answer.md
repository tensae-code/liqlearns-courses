# Shaka and the Zulu Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name Shaka's three military reforms.
**Answer:** The short stabbing spear for close fighting, bare feet hardened for speed and distance, and age regiments loyal to the king as the army's skeleton.

**2.** How were conquered chiefdoms treated in Shaka's kingdom?
**Answer:** They were absorbed into the Zulu kingdom — their young men drafted into its regiments and their identities re-forged as Zulu.
