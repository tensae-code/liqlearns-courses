# Shaka and the Zulu Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Shaka — the mocked illegitimate boy who became Dingiswayo's commander — seized the small Zulu chiefdom and remade war itself: the short stabbing spear, bare feet, and age regiments.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Shaka — the mocked illegitimate boy who became Dingiswayo's commander — seized the small Zulu chiefdom and remade war itself: the short stabbing spear, bare feet, and age regiments.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What weapon change defined Shaka's warfare?
a) Musket volleys replaced spears entirely  b) The short stabbing spear replaced the long throwing spear — war fought chest to chest  c) Cannon were mounted on ox-wagons  d) Poisoned arrows replaced hand-to-hand fighting
**Answer: b) The short stabbing spear replaced the long throwing spear — war fought chest to chest** — That is what this lesson teaches.
