# Shaka and the Zulu Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Shaka's regiments were forbidden to marry until the king released them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Shaka was the legitimate heir, welcomed to the Zulu throne as a boy.
**Answer: False** — He was the mocked illegitimate son, exiled, who rose as Dingiswayo's commander and seized the chiefdom.
