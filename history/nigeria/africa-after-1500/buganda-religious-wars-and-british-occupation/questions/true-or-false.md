# Buganda: Religious Wars and British Occupation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The religious parties fought three civil wars between 1888 and 1890. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Lugard commanded a regiment of the regular British army sent by Parliament. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — He commanded the army of the IBEAC — a chartered company, not the regular army.
