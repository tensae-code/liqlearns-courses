# Buganda: Religious Wars and British Occupation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Kabaka Mwanga's struggle with Buganda's religious parties broke into three civil wars in 1888–1890; Lugard's company army settled who ruled, and Baganda agents then became the agents of British occupation across Uganda.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Kabaka Mwanga's struggle with Buganda's religious parties broke into three civil wars in 1888–1890; Lugard's company army settled who ruled, and Baganda agents then became the agents of British occupation across Uganda.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** For how long did Apolo Kaggwa back the British connection? (Sub lesson 2)
a) For 3 years  b) For 37 years  c) For 12 years  d) For one rainy season
**Answer: b) For 37 years** — That is what this lesson teaches.
