# Buganda: Religious Wars and British Occupation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who was Bishop Hannington and what happened to him? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** An Anglican bishop approaching Buganda who was killed on Mwanga's orders — a warning to the foreign faiths that became a famous martyrdom.

**2.** How did Buganda rise above its neighbours under the protectorate? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Its chiefs backed Britain and its agents, like Kakungulu, conquered and administered the east in Britain's name — so Buganda was privileged above the peoples its agents occupied.
