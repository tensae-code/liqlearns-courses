# Hands-on — Buganda: Religious Wars and British Occupation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A young king inherits a court split into armed camps of faith. He strikes at all of them — and unites them against himself. Three civil wars follow in two years, and a foreign company's captain with a small army decides the outcome. Then comes the strangest turn: the kingdom that was fought over becomes the empire's own agent over its neighbours.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Kabaka Mwanga's struggle with Buganda's religious parties broke into three civil wars in 1888–1890; Lugard's company army settled who ruled, and Baganda agents then became the agents of British occupation across Uganda. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
