# Ethiopia: Yohannes IV Against Egypt and Italy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Yohannes IV won Menelik's submission by accord rather than war, crushed Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura, watched Italy take Eritrea with British blessing, and died in 1889 fighting the Mahdists.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Yohannes IV won Menelik's submission by accord rather than war, crushed Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura, watched Italy take Eritrea with British blessing, and died in 1889 fighting the Mahdists.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** At which battles did Yohannes crush Egypt's invasions?
a) Maqdala and Gondar  b) Gundet and Gura  c) Fashoda and Khartoum  d) Isandlwana and Ulundi
**Answer: b) Gundet and Gura** — That is what this lesson teaches.
