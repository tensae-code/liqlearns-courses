# Ethiopia: Yohannes IV Against Egypt and Italy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Italy took Eritrea with British blessing during Yohannes's reign.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Yohannes died in bed in 1889 after a long illness.
**Answer: False** — He died fighting the Mahdists in 1889.
