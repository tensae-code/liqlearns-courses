# Ethiopia: Yohannes IV Against Egypt and Italy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Yohannes secure Menelik's submission?
**Answer:** By accord — an agreement rather than a war — holding the empire together without a civil war.

**2.** Name the three external pressures of Yohannes's reign.
**Answer:** Egypt's invasions under Khedive Ismail (beaten at Gundet and Gura), Italy's seizure of Eritrea with British blessing, and the Mahdists, against whom he died in 1889.
