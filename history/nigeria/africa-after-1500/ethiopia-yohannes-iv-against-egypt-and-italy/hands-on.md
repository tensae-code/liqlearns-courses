# Hands-on — Ethiopia: Yohannes IV Against Egypt and Italy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
One emperor, three directions of danger: a rival king inside his own country, an invading empire from the north, a new European power landing on his coast with another empire's blessing — and a fourth enemy waiting at the end. This is statecraft under siege. Watch how Yohannes chooses, each time, which fight to fight and which to settle.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Yohannes IV won Menelik's submission by accord rather than war, crushed Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura, watched Italy take Eritrea with British blessing, and died in 1889 fighting the Mahdists. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
