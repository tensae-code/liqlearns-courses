# Ethiopia: Yohannes IV Against Egypt and Italy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Yohannes IV won Menelik's submission by accord rather than war, crushed Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura, watched Italy take Eritrea with British blessing, and died in 1889 fighting the Mahdists. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
