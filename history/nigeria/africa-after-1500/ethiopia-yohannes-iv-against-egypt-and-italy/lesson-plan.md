# Lesson plan — Ethiopia: Yohannes IV Against Egypt and Italy

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Ethiopia: Yohannes IV Against Egypt and Italy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Buganda and Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Yohannes IV won Menelik's submission by accord rather than war, crushed Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura, watched Italy take Eritrea with British blessing, and died in 1889 fighting the Mahdists.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Buganda and Ethiopia — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Yohannes IV won Menelik's submission by accord rather than war, crushed Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura, watched Italy take Eritrea with British blessing, and died in 1889 fighting the Mahdists.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a leader the learner knows who settled one rivalry by agreement precisely because two worse dangers were coming.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Yohannes won Shewa by conquering Menelik in war → he won Menelik's submission by accord, saving the empire a civil war; his wars were against Egypt at Gundet and Gura, and against the Mahdists who killed him in 1889.
- Go back to the one thing: Yohannes IV won Menelik's submission by accord rather than war, crushed Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura, watched Italy take Eritrea with British blessing, and died in 1889 fighting the Mahdists. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
