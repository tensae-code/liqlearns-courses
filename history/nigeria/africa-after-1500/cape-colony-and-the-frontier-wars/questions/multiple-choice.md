# The Cape Colony and the Frontier Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The Cape began as Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station and grew into a settler colony whose trekboers fought a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa; Britain then took the Cape, landed 5,000 settlers in 1820, and reformed law, language and slavery itself by 1835.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) The Cape began as Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station and grew into a settler colony whose trekboers fought a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa; Britain then took the Cape, landed 5,000 settlers in 1820, and reformed law, language and slavery itself by 1835.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How many British settlers landed at the Cape in 1820? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Some 500  b) Some 5,000  c) Some 50,000  d) None — the 1820 landing is a myth in the book
**Answer: b) Some 5,000** — That is what this lesson teaches.
