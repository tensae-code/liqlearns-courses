# The Cape Colony and the Frontier Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What was Van Riebeeck's 1652 commission? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A refreshment station — gardens and a fort to supply Dutch East India Company ships on the India run with fresh food and water.

**2.** Name three British reforms to 1835 and how the Boers read them. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Anglicised law and administration, English pressed over Dutch, and the emancipation of the slaves — read by many Boers as an attack on their whole way of life.
