# The Cape Colony and the Frontier Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The trekboers' expansion inland brought a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa over land and cattle. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Britain's reforms left slavery at the Cape untouched. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The reforms to 1835 included the emancipation of the slaves — the sharpest blow, as the Boers read it.
