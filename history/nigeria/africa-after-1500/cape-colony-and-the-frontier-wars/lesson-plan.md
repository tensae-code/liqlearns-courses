# Lesson plan — The Cape Colony and the Frontier Wars

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** The Cape Colony and the Frontier Wars
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Southern Africa: Revolution, Trek and Union (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Cape began as Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station and grew into a settler colony whose trekboers fought a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa; Britain then took the Cape, landed 5,000 settlers in 1820, and reformed law, language and slavery itself by 1835.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Southern Africa: Revolution, Trek and Union — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Dutch settlement at the Cape and the frontier wars** — Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station grows into a settler colony whose trekboers fight a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa over land and cattle.
2. **British occupation of the Cape, 1820 settlers and the reforms to 1835** — Britain takes the Cape twice, lands 5,000 settlers in 1820, anglicises law and language and emancipates the slaves — reforms the Boers read as an attack on their whole way of life.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
