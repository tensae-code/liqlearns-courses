# South Africa: Diamonds, Gold and the Road to Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Kimberley diamonds and Witwatersrand gold transformed South Africa — pulling in Gujarati traders and Gandhi himself — until two Boer wars ended with the Boers surrendering independence in 1902 and South Africa uniting under Britain.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Kimberley diamonds and Witwatersrand gold transformed South Africa — pulling in Gujarati traders and Gandhi himself — until two Boer wars ended with the Boers surrendering independence in 1902 and South Africa uniting under Britain.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where was the great goldfield found?
a) At Kimberley, with the diamonds  b) On the Witwatersrand — the Rand  c) In Natal's sugar fields  d) On Zanzibar's clove islands
**Answer: b) On the Witwatersrand — the Rand** — That is what this lesson teaches.
