# South Africa: Diamonds, Gold and the Road to Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did diamonds and gold lead toward war?
**Answer:** Kimberley diamonds and Witwatersrand gold made the Boer republics — above all the Transvaal — a prize of world value; Britain moved to take the whole country, and two Anglo-Boer wars followed.

**2.** Who were the Gujarati traders in this lesson, and who came with their story?
**Answer:** Indian traders from Gujarat drawn by the mineral economy, who faced discriminatory laws — and among the Indians in South Africa was the young lawyer Gandhi, whose campaigns against those laws began there.
