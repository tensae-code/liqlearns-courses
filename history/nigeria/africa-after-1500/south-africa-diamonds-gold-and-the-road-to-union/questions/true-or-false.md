# South Africa: Diamonds, Gold and the Road to Union — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gandhi's campaigns against discriminatory laws began in South Africa, where he had come as a young lawyer.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Boers kept their independence after the second Anglo-Boer war.
**Answer: False** — They surrendered independence in 1902, and South Africa united under Britain.
