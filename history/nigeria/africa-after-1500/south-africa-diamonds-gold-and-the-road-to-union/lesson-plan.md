# Lesson plan — South Africa: Diamonds, Gold and the Road to Union

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** South Africa: Diamonds, Gold and the Road to Union
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Southern Africa: Revolution, Trek and Union (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kimberley diamonds and Witwatersrand gold transformed South Africa — pulling in Gujarati traders and Gandhi himself — until two Boer wars ended with the Boers surrendering independence in 1902 and South Africa uniting under Britain.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Southern Africa: Revolution, Trek and Union — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Kimberley diamonds and Witwatersrand gold transformed South Africa — pulling in Gujarati traders and Gandhi himself — until two Boer wars ended with the Boers surrendering independence in 1902 and South Africa uniting under Britain.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a discovery the learner knows — oil, a mine, a new road — that changed a quiet place's politics within a generation.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
