# Hands-on — Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your family's land divided at a table in another country, by men who never asked you. That is the partition this lesson tells — not of a map in the abstract, but of one sultan's own realm, cut between two empires while he watched.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Omani sultanate itself was partitioned: Germany seized the mainland coast after a revolt against its company, Britain took Zanzibar as a protectorate in 1890 — and the sultan was not even consulted. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
