# Lesson plan — Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Method and the East African Coast (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Omani sultanate itself was partitioned: Germany seized the mainland coast after a revolt against its company, Britain took Zanzibar as a protectorate in 1890 — and the sultan was not even consulted.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Method and the East African Coast — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Omani sultanate itself was partitioned: Germany seized the mainland coast after a revolt against its company, Britain took Zanzibar as a protectorate in 1890 — and the sultan was not even consulted.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a family or community the learner knows whose land or business was divided by decisions taken elsewhere, without them at the table.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a picture of a map of the East African coast being divided — the mainland coast shaded for Germany, the island of Zanzibar shaded for Britain's 1890 protectorate, and the sultan's palace unshaded and small between them — the student points to the mainland, the island and the palace and says what happened to each.
   - ADD GIF: a short GIF of a short GIF of the sultanate's map splitting — mainland and island coloured in two different empires' colours one after the other — the student watches and names which power took which part, and in what year the island followed.

5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
