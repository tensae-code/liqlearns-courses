# Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How was the sultanate partitioned?
**Answer:** Germany took the mainland coast after the revolt against its company, and Britain took Zanzibar as a protectorate in 1890.

**2.** What does the sultan's exclusion from the decision show?
**Answer:** That the age of partition no longer treated African rulers as parties to be consulted — their realms were divided by empires without them.
