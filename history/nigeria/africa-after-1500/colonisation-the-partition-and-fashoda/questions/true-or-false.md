# Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Germany seized the mainland coast after a revolt against its company's rule.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The sultan was consulted and agreed to the partition of his realm.
**Answer: False** — He was not even consulted — the partition was done over his head.
