# Colonisation: The Partition and Fashoda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) The Omani sultanate itself was partitioned: Germany seized the mainland coast after a revolt against its company, Britain took Zanzibar as a protectorate in 1890 — and the sultan was not even consulted.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) The Omani sultanate itself was partitioned: Germany seized the mainland coast after a revolt against its company, Britain took Zanzibar as a protectorate in 1890 — and the sultan was not even consulted.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened to Zanzibar in 1890?
a) Germany annexed it to its mainland colony  b) Britain declared it a protectorate  c) The sultan sold it to Oman  d) It became fully independent of all empires
**Answer: b) Britain declared it a protectorate** — That is what this lesson teaches.
