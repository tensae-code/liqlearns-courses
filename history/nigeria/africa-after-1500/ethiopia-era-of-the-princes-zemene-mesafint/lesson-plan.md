# Lesson plan — Ethiopia: The Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint)

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Ethiopia: The Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint)
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Buganda and Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the Era of the Princes — the Zemene Mesafint — Ethiopia's emperors became puppets while regional lords ruled the country in pieces, and the people waited for a prophesied Theodore to reunite them.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Buganda and Ethiopia — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
