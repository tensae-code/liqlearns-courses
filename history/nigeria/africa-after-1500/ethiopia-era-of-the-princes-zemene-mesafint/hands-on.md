# Hands-on — Ethiopia: The Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A throne with a king on it, and no power in his hands. Around him, lords ruling their own provinces like kings themselves, making and unmaking emperors. When a country lives like that for generations, it starts to dream of one man who will end it. Ethiopia dreamed of a name: Theodore.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the Era of the Princes — the Zemene Mesafint — Ethiopia's emperors became puppets while regional lords ruled the country in pieces, and the people waited for a prophesied Theodore to reunite them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
