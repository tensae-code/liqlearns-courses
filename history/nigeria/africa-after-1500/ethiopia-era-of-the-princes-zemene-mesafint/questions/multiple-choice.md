# Ethiopia: The Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) In the Era of the Princes — the Zemene Mesafint — Ethiopia's emperors became puppets while regional lords ruled the country in pieces, and the people waited for a prophesied Theodore to reunite them.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) In the Era of the Princes — the Zemene Mesafint — Ethiopia's emperors became puppets while regional lords ruled the country in pieces, and the people waited for a prophesied Theodore to reunite them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What were the emperors during the Zemene Mesafint?
a) Absolute rulers of a united empire  b) Puppets, raised and deposed by the regional lords  c) Priests ruling from Aksum  d) Governors appointed by Egypt
**Answer: b) Puppets, raised and deposed by the regional lords** — That is what this lesson teaches.
