# Ethiopia: The Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint) — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Regional lords ruled Ethiopia in pieces during the Era of the Princes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The prophecy said a queen named Mary would reunite Ethiopia.
**Answer: False** — The prophecy named Theodore as the king who would reunite the country.
