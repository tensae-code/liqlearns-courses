# Ethiopia: The Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does Zemene Mesafint mean in this lesson?
**Answer:** The Era of the Princes — the age when emperors became puppets and regional lords ruled Ethiopia in pieces, to 1855.

**2.** What hope did the divided land hold?
**Answer:** A prophecy that a king named Theodore would come, reunite Ethiopia and restore the empire's strength.
