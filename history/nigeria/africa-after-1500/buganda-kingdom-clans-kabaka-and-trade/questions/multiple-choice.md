# The Buganda Kingdom: Clans, Kabaka and Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Buganda, the strongest state on Lake Victoria, was built of some forty totemic clans under one Kabaka — and in 1845 Arab traders from Zanzibar reached it with guns and cloth, and Islam followed them.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Buganda, the strongest state on Lake Victoria, was built of some forty totemic clans under one Kabaka — and in 1845 Arab traders from Zanzibar reached it with guns and cloth, and Islam followed them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** By what year had Arab traders from Zanzibar reached Buganda? (Sub lesson 2)
a) By 1652, bringing horses and wine  b) By 1845, bringing guns and cloth  c) By 1890, bringing the protectorate  d) By 1800, bringing cloves and ivory only
**Answer: b) By 1845, bringing guns and cloth** — That is what this lesson teaches.
