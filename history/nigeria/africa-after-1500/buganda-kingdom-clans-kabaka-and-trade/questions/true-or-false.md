# The Buganda Kingdom: Clans, Kabaka and Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** No one in Buganda could marry inside their own clan or their mother's clan — except the Kabaka. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Islam reached Buganda only after Europeans arrived. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Islam followed the Arab traders who reached Buganda by 1845, long before Europeans came.
