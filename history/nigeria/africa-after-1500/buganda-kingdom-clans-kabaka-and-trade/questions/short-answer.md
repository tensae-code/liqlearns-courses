# The Buganda Kingdom: Clans, Kabaka and Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Buganda's clan marriage law hold the kingdom together? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Marriage had to reach outside one's own clan and one's mother's clan, weaving clan to clan across the kingdom — while the Kabaka's own marriages bound the clans to the throne.

**2.** What did the Zanzibari Arabs bring to Buganda? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Guns and cloth — and, following the traders, Islam, which began changing the Kabaka's court from the inside.
