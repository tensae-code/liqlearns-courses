# The Northern Nguni Revolution Before Shaka — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Age regiments mustered young men by age-set, loyal to the confederation above their chiefdoms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Shaka served as a herdboy and never saw the Mthethwa system.
**Answer: False** — Shaka served as Dingiswayo's commander and learned the new warfare inside the Mthethwa confederation.
