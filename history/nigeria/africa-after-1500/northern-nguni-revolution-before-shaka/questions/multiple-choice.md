# The Northern Nguni Revolution Before Shaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Before Shaka, the northern Nguni were already in revolution: Dingiswayo of the Mthethwa and Zwide of the Ndwandwe built confederations and age-regiment armies — and Zwide killed Dingiswayo in 1817, setting the stage for Shaka.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Before Shaka, the northern Nguni were already in revolution: Dingiswayo of the Mthethwa and Zwide of the Ndwandwe built confederations and age-regiment armies — and Zwide killed Dingiswayo in 1817, setting the stage for Shaka.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who killed Dingiswayo in 1817?
a) Shaka of the Zulu  b) Zwide of the Ndwandwe  c) Moshoeshoe of the Sotho  d) Van Riebeeck of the Cape
**Answer: b) Zwide of the Ndwandwe** — That is what this lesson teaches.
