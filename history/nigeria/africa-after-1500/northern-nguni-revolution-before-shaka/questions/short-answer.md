# The Northern Nguni Revolution Before Shaka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What was new about the northern Nguni revolution?
**Answer:** Chiefdoms combined into confederations, and armies were reorganised as age regiments loyal above the single chiefdom — a new scale of power before Shaka.

**2.** How did the Dingiswayo–Zwide contest end, and why does it matter?
**Answer:** Zwide killed Dingiswayo in 1817; the Mthethwa lost its builder, and Dingiswayo's young commander Shaka inherited the stage.
