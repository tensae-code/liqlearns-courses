# Ethiopia: Tewodros II the Unifier — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Kassa — an outlawed shifta whose widowed mother hawked medicine in Gondar — fought his way to the throne as Tewodros II, ended the Era of the Princes, reformed church and army, and died at Maqdala in 1868 facing a British expedition.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Kassa — an outlawed shifta whose widowed mother hawked medicine in Gondar — fought his way to the throne as Tewodros II, ended the Era of the Princes, reformed church and army, and died at Maqdala in 1868 facing a British expedition.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did Tewodros die in 1868?
a) At Gondar, poisoned by the lords  b) At Maqdala, his mountain fortress, facing a British expedition  c) At Aksum, in peaceful old age  d) At Zanzibar, in exile
**Answer: b) At Maqdala, his mountain fortress, facing a British expedition** — That is what this lesson teaches.
