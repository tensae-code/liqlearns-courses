# Ethiopia: Tewodros II the Unifier — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tewodros reformed both the church and the army as part of reunifying Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Tewodros was crowned in 1855 under his birth name Kassa and kept it as emperor.
**Answer: False** — He took the prophesied name Theodore — Tewodros II — on becoming emperor.
