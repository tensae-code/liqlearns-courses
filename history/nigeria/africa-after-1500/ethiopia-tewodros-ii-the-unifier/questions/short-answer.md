# Ethiopia: Tewodros II the Unifier — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Kassa reach the throne?
**Answer:** From outlaw shifta he fought battle by battle, defeated the regional lords, ended the Era of the Princes and was crowned in 1855 as Tewodros II.

**2.** Name two of Tewodros's reforms.
**Answer:** He broke the regional lords' independence to reunite the country, reordered the church's lands and privileges, and rebuilt the army as a national force with modern weapons and pay (any two).
