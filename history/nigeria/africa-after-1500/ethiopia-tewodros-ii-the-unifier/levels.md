# Ethiopia: Tewodros II the Unifier — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Kassa — an outlawed shifta whose widowed mother hawked medicine in Gondar — fought his way to the throne as Tewodros II, ended the Era of the Princes, reformed church and army, and died at Maqdala in 1868 facing a British expedition. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
