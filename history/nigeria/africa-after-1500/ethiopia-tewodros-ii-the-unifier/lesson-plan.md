# Lesson plan — Ethiopia: Tewodros II the Unifier

**Course:** History — Nigeria · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Ethiopia: Tewodros II the Unifier
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Buganda and Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kassa — an outlawed shifta whose widowed mother hawked medicine in Gondar — fought his way to the throne as Tewodros II, ended the Era of the Princes, reformed church and army, and died at Maqdala in 1868 facing a British expedition.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Buganda and Ethiopia — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
