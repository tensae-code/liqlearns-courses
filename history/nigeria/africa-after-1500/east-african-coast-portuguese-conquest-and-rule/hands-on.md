# Hands-on — The East African Coast: Portuguese Conquest and Rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A city-state can defy a trader. It cannot so easily defy a fleet with cannon that has come to stay. The Swahili towns ruled themselves and quarrelled with each other. Now ask what happens when an armed empire treats that whole divided coast as one prize.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Portuguese came to the East African coast hunting ports for the India route, demanded tribute, plundered Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba, and held the coast as one province — until Oman drove them out. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
