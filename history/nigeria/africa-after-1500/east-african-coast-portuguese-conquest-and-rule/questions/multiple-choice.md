# The East African Coast: Portuguese Conquest and Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) The Portuguese came to the East African coast hunting ports for the India route, demanded tribute, plundered Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba, and held the coast as one province — until Oman drove them out.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) The Portuguese came to the East African coast hunting ports for the India route, demanded tribute, plundered Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba, and held the coast as one province — until Oman drove them out.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which towns does the book name as plundered by the Portuguese?
a) Kilwa, Sofala and Lamu only  b) Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba  c) Dahomey, Buganda and Egypt  d) Cape Town, Natal and the Transvaal
**Answer: b) Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba** — That is what this lesson teaches.
