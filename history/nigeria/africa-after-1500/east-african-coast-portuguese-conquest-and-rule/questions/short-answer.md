# The East African Coast: Portuguese Conquest and Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did the Portuguese want the East African coast?
**Answer:** As ports on the sea road to India — places to control the route, demand tribute and hold the coast as one province.

**2.** Who ended Portuguese rule on the coast?
**Answer:** Oman, which grew strong, crossed the ocean and drove the Portuguese out.
