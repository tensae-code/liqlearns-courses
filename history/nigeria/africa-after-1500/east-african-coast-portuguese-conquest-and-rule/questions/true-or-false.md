# The East African Coast: Portuguese Conquest and Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Portuguese held the divided Swahili coast together as one province of their empire.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Portuguese were driven from the coast by the Swahili city-states acting alone.
**Answer: False** — It was Oman — a rising Arabian sea power — that drove them out.
