# The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain pressed treaty after treaty on the sultans to end the East African slave trade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** All the European powers enforced the anti-slavery treaties with equal energy.
**Answer: False** — Other powers looked away; enforcement was uneven and driven by Britain.
