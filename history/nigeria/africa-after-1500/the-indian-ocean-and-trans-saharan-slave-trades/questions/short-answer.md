# The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did the treaties try to do?
**Answer:** End the East African slave trade in the Omani empire, step by step — limiting and then banning the trade the plantation economy depended on.

**2.** Why was ending the trade so hard for Zanzibar's economy?
**Answer:** Its clove plantations were worked by enslaved labour and its caravans carried captives — the trade was a load-bearing part of the system.
