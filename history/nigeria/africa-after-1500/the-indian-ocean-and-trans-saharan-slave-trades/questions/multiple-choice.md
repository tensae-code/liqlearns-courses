# The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Britain pressed treaty after treaty on the Omani sultans to end the East African slave trade — the trade the plantation economy depended on — while other powers looked away.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Britain pressed treaty after treaty on the Omani sultans to end the East African slave trade — the trade the plantation economy depended on — while other powers looked away.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why did the sultans hesitate over the anti-slavery treaties?
a) They had never heard of the slave trade  b) The plantation economy depended on the slave trade the treaties attacked  c) They wanted Portugal to return to the coast  d) They were waiting for Buganda's permission
**Answer: b) The plantation economy depended on the slave trade the treaties attacked** — That is what this lesson teaches.
