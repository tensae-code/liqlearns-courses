# Hands-on — East Africa and the Omani Turn

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A ruler packs up his whole court and moves it across an ocean — 3,200 kilometres of water — to an island off another continent. Madness, or the smartest move of his life? Hold the question. This lesson follows the sultan who did it, and the island market he built.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Sayyid Said of Oman moved his capital across 3,200 km of ocean to Zanzibar and rebuilt Omani power on African soil, turning Zanzibar into the nerve centre where ivory, guns and cloves changed hands. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
