# East Africa and the Omani Turn — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was Zanzibar a strategic capital for Said? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** It stood at the door of the East African interior and its caravan trade — ivory, guns and cloves all changed hands there, under his control and taxation.

**2.** What three goods name Zanzibar's market in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ivory, guns and cloves.
