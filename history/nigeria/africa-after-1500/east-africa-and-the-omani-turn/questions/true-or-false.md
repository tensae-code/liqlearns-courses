# East Africa and the Omani Turn — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sayyid Said moved his capital from Oman to Zanzibar, across about 3,200 km of ocean. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Foreigners were free to travel and trade anywhere in Said's interior. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Said banned foreigners from the interior; Arabs and Swahili were made the middlemen.
