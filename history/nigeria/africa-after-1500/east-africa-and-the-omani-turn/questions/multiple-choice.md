# East Africa and the Omani Turn — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Sayyid Said of Oman moved his capital across 3,200 km of ocean to Zanzibar and rebuilt Omani power on African soil, turning Zanzibar into the nerve centre where ivory, guns and cloves changed hands.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Sayyid Said of Oman moved his capital across 3,200 km of ocean to Zanzibar and rebuilt Omani power on African soil, turning Zanzibar into the nerve centre where ivory, guns and cloves changed hands.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Whom did Said make the middlemen of the interior trade? (Sub lesson 2)
a) European companies licensed to trade inland  b) Arabs and Swahili, after banning foreigners from the interior  c) The Portuguese garrisons of the old forts  d) Buganda's royal traders from Lake Victoria
**Answer: b) Arabs and Swahili, after banning foreigners from the interior** — That is what this lesson teaches.
