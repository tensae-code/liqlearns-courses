# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) Arab sailors kept the secret of the monsoon winds for centuries and monopolised the dhow trade of the Indian Ocean — ivory, gold and tortoise shell — until others learned the winds.  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary learners  c) that learners need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Nigeria's book has no way of its own
**Answer: a) Arab sailors kept the secret of the monsoon winds for centuries and monopolised the dhow trade of the Indian Ocean — ivory, gold and tortoise shell — until others learned the winds.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What goods does the book name in the dhow trade from the coast?
a) Cloves, coffee and cotton  b) Ivory, gold and tortoise shell  c) Iron, horses and salt  d) Silk, tea and porcelain only
**Answer: b) Ivory, gold and tortoise shell** — That is what this lesson teaches.
