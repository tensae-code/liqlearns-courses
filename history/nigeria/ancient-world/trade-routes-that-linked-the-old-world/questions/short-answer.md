# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why was the monsoon secret worth a monopoly?
**Answer:** Whoever knew when the winds blew each way could run the Indian Ocean trade on schedule; keeping that knowledge to themselves let Arab sailors monopolise the dhow trade.

**2.** What broke the old monopoly in the end?
**Answer:** Others learned the winds — and new sailors, above all the Portuguese, found their own way to the coast.
