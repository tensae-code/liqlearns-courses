# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The monsoon winds reverse with the seasons, so a captain who knows the calendar can sail out and home on schedule.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Arab sailors published the monsoon secret freely as soon as they learned it.
**Answer: False** — They kept the secret for centuries — guarding it was their monopoly.
