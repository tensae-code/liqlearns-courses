# History — Colombia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Colombia's own — built from Colombia's Ministerio de Educación Nacional books (Spanish: Escuela Nueva, Postprimaria Rural, Secundaria Activa). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': first steps in the child's own past, the deep past and Colombia's ancient peoples, colony, independence and Colombia's peoples, the ancient and medieval worlds widened, revolutions, constitutions and the nineteenth century, and the twentieth century of world war, conflict and reform. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
