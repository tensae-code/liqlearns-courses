# Ancient Civilisations: China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Tema 19: Stratified societies”?
a) Mesopotamia's stratification is fixed by law, China is a society directed by sages where Confucian family values guarantee harmony, slaves stand last on the scale, and the Inca hatunruna work the land  b) García Márquez's proclamation opens the century; Panama's strategic value drives the relationship, interoceanic traffic through the Canal opens in 1914 and is officially inaugurated in 1920  c) The child orders past, present and future on a "tren del tiempo" and sees their own body and character change as time passes  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Mesopotamia's stratification is fixed by law, China is a society directed by sages where Confucian family values guarantee harmony, slaves stand last on the scale, and the Inca hatunruna work the land** — That is what this lesson teaches.
