Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Módulo 1 / Guía 1: Who were they and where did they live?”?
a) The Romans give way to medieval castles; Constantine becomes emperor in 324, backs Christianity and moves the Empire's capital to Constantinople  b) Cerro Rico at Potosí is discovered, gold and silver become wealth itself, and the colonial system revives the traffic in enslaved Africans to work America  c) Diversity predates the Europeans; Colombia's surviving Indigenous groups are listed by territory — Emberá, the peoples of Vaupés and more — and the Guambianos are reconstructing their own history, memory and legacies, long forgotten and ignored  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Romans give way to medieval castles; Constantine becomes emperor in 324, backs Christianity and moves the Empire's capital to Constantinople** — That is what this lesson teaches.
