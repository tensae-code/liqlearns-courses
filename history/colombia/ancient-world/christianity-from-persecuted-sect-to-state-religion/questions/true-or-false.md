# Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Romans give way to medieval castles; Constantine becomes emperor in 324, backs Christianity and moves the Empire's capital to Constantinople
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 14: Christianity and Islam” is mainly about this: Cirilo cannot believe a man will tell him everything he always wanted to know about his ancestors; in the Baudó the newborn's ombligo ritual binds the child to its land
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Places across the Empire turn Christian and faith becomes resistance to oppression, while Muhammad preaches that the rich must share, Islam rests on five pillars, and its culture grows from the peoples it rules
