# Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Módulo 1 / Guía 1: Who were they and where did they live?”?
**Answer:** The Romans give way to medieval castles; Constantine becomes emperor in 324, backs Christianity and moves the Empire's capital to Constantinople Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 14: Christianity and Islam” end?
**Answer:** Places across the Empire turn Christian and faith becomes resistance to oppression, while Muhammad preaches that the rich must share, Islam rests on five pillars, and its culture grows from the peoples it rules Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
