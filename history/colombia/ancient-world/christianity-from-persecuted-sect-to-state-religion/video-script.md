# Video script — Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The Romans give way to medieval castles; Constantine becomes emperor in 324, backs Christianity and moves the Empire's capital to Constantinople Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
Places across the Empire turn Christian and faith becomes resistance to oppression, while Muhammad preaches that the rich must share, Islam rests on five pillars, and its culture grows from the peoples it rules Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Colombia.
