# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Temas 12–15: America, Europe, Asia, Africa”?
**Answer:** America is native land and mixing of cultures, Europe a land of seasons and exchanges of knowledge, Asia the land of the Silk Road, and Africa the land of the Black Athena Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 21: Commerce and cultural contact” end?
**Answer:** Trade carries goods and cultures together, so contact between peoples remakes both Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
