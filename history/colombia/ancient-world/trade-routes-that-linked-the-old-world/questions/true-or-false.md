# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) America is native land and mixing of cultures, Europe a land of seasons and exchanges of knowledge, Asia the land of the Silk Road, and Africa the land of the Black Athena
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 21: Commerce and cultural contact” is mainly about this: Every community has a history, and groups pick one cultural expression, research it in books and by asking adults, and present it
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Trade carries goods and cultures together, so contact between peoples remakes both
