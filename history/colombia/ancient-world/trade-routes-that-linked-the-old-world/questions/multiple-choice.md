# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Temas 12–15: America, Europe, Asia, Africa”?
a) America is native land and mixing of cultures, Europe a land of seasons and exchanges of knowledge, Asia the land of the Silk Road, and Africa the land of the Black Athena  b) Machines enter textile production in the first phase, the bourgeois grows immensely richer, and the smoke-spitting train is blamed for the age's diseases and plagues  c) In Kogi mythology the elder brothers hold the centre of humanity, the Tairona build artificial terraces to farm, and the Wayúu hold La Guajira at South America's northern tip  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) America is native land and mixing of cultures, Europe a land of seasons and exchanges of knowledge, Asia the land of the Silk Road, and Africa the land of the Black Athena** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Tema 21: Commerce and cultural contact”?
a) Trade carries goods and cultures together, so contact between peoples remakes both  b) Populist governments claim to represent the people's interests — Paz Estenssoro reaches power in Bolivia after coups — dictatorships spread, and the United Fruit Company controls production and refuses the workers' demands  c) Homes move from adobe and mud to brick and then to "intelligent" houses, so learners walk through time by walking through houses  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Trade carries goods and cultures together, so contact between peoples remakes both** — That is what this lesson teaches.
