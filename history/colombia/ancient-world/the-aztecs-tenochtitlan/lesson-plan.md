## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tema 18: Political structures in Antiquity** — Power in ancient Mesopotamia turns custom into divine belief; at its splendour the Aztec empire rules, the Inca organises his empire, and the Muisca order their own polity
2. **Unidad 4 / Tema 21: Empires in America before the Europeans arrived** — Only from about 700 do the great Aztec, Inca and Maya empires rise; the Mexica impose themselves group by group, the Maya remain ancient and mysterious, the Inca myth mirrors the Aztec one, and Tairona society is divided in its own way

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
