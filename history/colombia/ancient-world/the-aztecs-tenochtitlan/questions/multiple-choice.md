# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Tema 18: Political structures in Antiquity”?
a) Power in ancient Mesopotamia turns custom into divine belief; at its splendour the Aztec empire rules, the Inca organises his empire, and the Muisca order their own polity  b) Slavery is universal and ancient — in Antiquity and the Middle Ages the enslaved work fields and mines — before it becomes an Atlantic system  c) The war's antecedents are assembled before its development is followed  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Power in ancient Mesopotamia turns custom into divine belief; at its splendour the Aztec empire rules, the Inca organises his empire, and the Muisca order their own polity** — That is what this lesson teaches.
