# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Power in ancient Mesopotamia turns custom into divine belief; at its splendour the Aztec empire rules, the Inca organises his empire, and the Muisca order their own polity
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unidad 4 / Tema 21: Empires in America before the Europeans arrived” is mainly about this: After the Catholic reconquest of Spain from the Muslims drains the Crown, Columbus sails, makes four voyages still believing he is in Asia, and meets numerous Indigenous peoples
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Only from about 700 do the great Aztec, Inca and Maya empires rise; the Mexica impose themselves group by group, the Maya remain ancient and mysterious, the Inca myth mirrors the Aztec one, and Tairona society is divided in its own way
