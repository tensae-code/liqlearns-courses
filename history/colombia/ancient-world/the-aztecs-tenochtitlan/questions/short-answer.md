# The Aztecs: Tenochtitlan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Tema 18: Political structures in Antiquity”?
**Answer:** Power in ancient Mesopotamia turns custom into divine belief; at its splendour the Aztec empire rules, the Inca organises his empire, and the Muisca order their own polity Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unidad 4 / Tema 21: Empires in America before the Europeans arrived” end?
**Answer:** Only from about 700 do the great Aztec, Inca and Maya empires rise; the Mexica impose themselves group by group, the Maya remain ancient and mysterious, the Inca myth mirrors the Aztec one, and Tairona society is divided in its own way Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
