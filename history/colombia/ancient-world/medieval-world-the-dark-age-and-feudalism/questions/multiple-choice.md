**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 6: Social life in the societies of the 5th–15th centuries”?
a) Feudal Europe stratifies into clergy, nobility and peasants, and women's work holds the system up  b) Recently independent Latin America faces neocolonisation while the European powers colonise Africa — Italy taking part of the east coast — with forced labour, political domination and exploitation, and a violence against Africans that no wealth satisfies  c) A chronology table walks Colombia's student movement event by event, each with its motives and strategies  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
