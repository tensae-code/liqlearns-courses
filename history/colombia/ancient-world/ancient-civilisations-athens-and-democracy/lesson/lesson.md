# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

In Athens only free adult native men are citizens; Rome falls in the 5th century, the world lives nearly a thousand years under other powers, and a new idea of democracy and of the State must be built Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
