## Sub lesson 1 — Empresa Conquistadora, Colombia panel

The Tayrona and Muisca built great villages and complex cultures; Muisca descendants still live on the Cundiboyacense high plain and Tayrona descendants in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Módulo 2 / Guía 6: Goldwork of the millenary peoples

America's ancient peoples work metals into sacred art, thanking Mother Earth for the materials, a craft Colombia's own ancient peoples carried to mastery Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

## Sub lesson 3 — Tema 16: The Taironas and their agricultural techniques

The turn in the middle is the heart of it: before the turn things stand one way, after it they stand another, and the people in the story must live in the new way.
