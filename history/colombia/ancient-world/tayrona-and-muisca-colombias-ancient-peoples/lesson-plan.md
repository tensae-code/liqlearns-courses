## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Empresa Conquistadora, Colombia panel** — The Tayrona and Muisca built great villages and complex cultures; Muisca descendants still live on the Cundiboyacense high plain and Tayrona descendants in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta
2. **Módulo 2 / Guía 6: Goldwork of the millenary peoples** — America's ancient peoples work metals into sacred art, thanking Mother Earth for the materials, a craft Colombia's own ancient peoples carried to mastery
3. **Tema 16: The Taironas and their agricultural techniques** — The Tairona culture of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta and the Caribbean plain farms its steep land with its own techniques
4. **Guía 8: How other cultures related to the environment** — In Kogi mythology the elder brothers hold the centre of humanity, the Tairona build artificial terraces to farm, and the Wayúu hold La Guajira at South America's northern tip
