# Tayrona and Muisca: Colombia's Ancient Peoples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Tayrona and Muisca built great villages and complex cultures; Muisca descendants still live on the Cundiboyacense high plain and Tayrona descendants in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Módulo 2 / Guía 6: Goldwork of the millenary peoples” is mainly about this: The welfare state provides health and education, the USSR builds an Eastern Bloc of occupied and satellite states, decolonisation in Asia and Africa follows the exhaustion of the metropolis–colony relation, and after the Cold War the Balkans become Europe's main war
**Answer: False** — It is about this: America's ancient peoples work metals into sacred art, thanking Mother Earth for the materials, a craft Colombia's own ancient peoples carried to mastery
