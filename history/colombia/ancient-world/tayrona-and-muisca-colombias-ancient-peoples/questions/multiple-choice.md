# Tayrona and Muisca: Colombia's Ancient Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Empresa Conquistadora, Colombia panel”?
a) The Tayrona and Muisca built great villages and complex cultures; Muisca descendants still live on the Cundiboyacense high plain and Tayrona descendants in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta  b) Christianity and Islam both expand by conquest to convert distant peoples to creeds held universal and true, and Catholicism is then carried to America by missionary priests, some of whom confront the conquerors  c) Late-medieval trade revives, cities organise themselves, and their prosperous merchants enjoy a culture and education above their governors'  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Tayrona and Muisca built great villages and complex cultures; Muisca descendants still live on the Cundiboyacense high plain and Tayrona descendants in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta** — That is what this lesson teaches.
