# The Maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Empresa Conquistadora, board panels and questions 1–17”?
a) The Maya and Aztecs built enormous empires that were disappearing with the Spanish arrival, leaving only direct Maya descendants; the game's questions then run the whole conquest — why Africans were captured, who backed Columbus, what the Taíno called San Salvador, and which treaty split the world  b) The Tairona culture of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta and the Caribbean plain farms its steep land with its own techniques  c) Cerro Rico at Potosí is discovered, gold and silver become wealth itself, and the colonial system revives the traffic in enslaved Africans to work America  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The Maya and Aztecs built enormous empires that were disappearing with the Spanish arrival, leaving only direct Maya descendants; the game's questions then run the whole conquest — why Africans were captured, who backed Columbus, what the Taíno called San Salvador, and which treaty split the world** — That is what this lesson teaches.
