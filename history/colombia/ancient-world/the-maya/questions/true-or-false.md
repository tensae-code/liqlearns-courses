# The Maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Maya and Aztecs built enormous empires that were disappearing with the Spanish arrival, leaving only direct Maya descendants; the game's questions then run the whole conquest — why Africans were captured, who backed Columbus, what the Taíno called San Salvador, and which treaty split the world
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Módulo 1 / Guía 2: The Maya of yesterday and today” is mainly about this: The thirteen colonies' contradictions become a war of independence and, mid-war on 4 July, a constitution; in Latin America the Bourbons' arrival in the mid-1700s breeds the discontent that ends in independence, Gainza's Central America included
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Maya communities still speak their languages and live by family customs inherited from the ancestors who built a writing system and a calendar
