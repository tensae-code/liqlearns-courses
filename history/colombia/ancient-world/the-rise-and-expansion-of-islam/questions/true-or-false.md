# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Christianity and Islam both expand by conquest to convert distant peoples to creeds held universal and true, and Catholicism is then carried to America by missionary priests, some of whom confront the conquerors
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 5: Technological knowledge of yesterday and today” is mainly about this: Children compare dress, food, music and festivals across Colombia's regions and then record their own region's customs
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Islamic world advances science and technology, navigation improves, and cartographic progress underpins the explorations that begin in the mid-1400s
