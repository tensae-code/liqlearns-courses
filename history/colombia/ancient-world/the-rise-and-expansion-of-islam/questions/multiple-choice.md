**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Módulo 2 / Guía 4: Religious traditions in the societies of the 5th–15th centuries”?
a) Christianity and Islam both expand by conquest to convert distant peoples to creeds held universal and true, and Catholicism is then carried to America by missionary priests, some of whom confront the conquerors  b) Rafael Núñez's project of change is debated from his own discourse, the liberal Constitution of 1863 written at Rionegro is read as one of Colombians' many attempts, and learners tabulate Colombia's constitutions  c) The law of 15 June 1853 separates Church and State, the Liberal party splits, a constitution consolidates nine sovereign states, Núñez presides from 8 April 1880 for four periods, the Concordat of 1887 follows, and the Constitution of 1886 is reformed down to 1958 and 2005  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
