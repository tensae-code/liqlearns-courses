# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Colombia · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Ancient and Medieval Worlds Widen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Christianity and Islam both expand by conquest to convert distant peoples to creeds held universal and true, and Catholicism is then carried to America by missionary priests, some of whom confront the conquerors
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Ancient and Medieval Worlds Widen (recommended, never forced).
