# Medieval Art, Music and Indo-Christian Art — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Gregorian chant such as Puer natus est survives as an early carol, Islamic art decorates without images, and Indigenous artists of the 1500s create what is named Indo-Christian art — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
