# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Tema 22: Sacred and urban architecture”?
a) Kramer describes a typical Mesopotamian city, Rome founds cities across its world, and Babylon raises the Hanging Gardens on the Euphrates  b) Indigenous peoples, Black/Afro-Colombian, Raizal and Palenquero communities, and the Rrom (Gitano) people are presented one by one, and Colombia is then named a mestizo people born of their meeting  c) In fifty years the Spanish conquer by contract and dispute; three routes — Caribbean coast, south-west and centre — push inland, and Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada leaves Santa Marta with 600 foot soldiers and over 100 horsemen  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Kramer describes a typical Mesopotamian city, Rome founds cities across its world, and Babylon raises the Hanging Gardens on the Euphrates** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Rome is not built in a day — seven kings, then consuls; the Punic Wars break Carthage, Hannibal finds asylum in Syria, Caesar is murdered, the Second Triumvirate follows, and Augustus reforms the state and gives Roman law** — That is what this lesson teaches.
