# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Kramer describes a typical Mesopotamian city, Rome founds cities across its world, and Babylon raises the Hanging Gardens on the Euphrates
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 13: Origins and consolidation of the Roman Empire” is mainly about this: Gregorian chant such as Puer natus est survives as an early carol, Islamic art decorates without images, and Indigenous artists of the 1500s create what is named Indo-Christian art
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Rome is not built in a day — seven kings, then consuls; the Punic Wars break Carthage, Hannibal finds asylum in Syria, Caesar is murdered, the Second Triumvirate follows, and Augustus reforms the state and gives Roman law
