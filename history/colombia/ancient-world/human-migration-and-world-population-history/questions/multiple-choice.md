# Human Migration and World Population History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Módulo 4 / Guía 12: Historical panorama of world population”?
a) Ten thousand years ago small bands spread over the Earth; the Inuit come from northern Asia, African peoples migrate across their continent, and today over ten million Mexican-born people live in the United States  b) Mesopotamia's stratification is fixed by law, China is a society directed by sages where Confucian family values guarantee harmony, slaves stand last on the scale, and the Inca hatunruna work the land  c) Monarchy comes from the Greek for one ruler; imperial China runs on dynasties back to the Xia of the 21st century BC, and the Carolingian monarchy reunifies provinces with Church and Pope behind it  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Ten thousand years ago small bands spread over the Earth; the Inuit come from northern Asia, African peoples migrate across their continent, and today over ten million Mexican-born people live in the United States** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Tema 9: Settlement routes from Africa to America”?
a) From Africa the first humans spread and finally cross into America, peopling a new continent  b) Only from about 700 do the great Aztec, Inca and Maya empires rise; the Mexica impose themselves group by group, the Maya remain ancient and mysterious, the Inca myth mirrors the Aztec one, and Tairona society is divided in its own way  c) France holds northern North America and Antillean islands and Holland is present too; the mita obliges a quota of Indigenous workers, colonial society ranks its castes with the enslaved at the bottom — a ranking North America's states already argue over — and colonised communities rapidly learn the imposed language while keeping their own  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) From Africa the first humans spread and finally cross into America, peopling a new continent** — That is what this lesson teaches.
