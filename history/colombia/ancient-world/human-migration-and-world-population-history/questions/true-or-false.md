# Human Migration and World Population History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ten thousand years ago small bands spread over the Earth; the Inuit come from northern Asia, African peoples migrate across their continent, and today over ten million Mexican-born people live in the United States
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 9: Settlement routes from Africa to America” is mainly about this: Machine-breakers give way to unions demanding better conditions and wages instead of destruction, until Thiers demands the National Guard's surrender and sends soldiers against Paris
**Answer: False** — It is about this: From Africa the first humans spread and finally cross into America, peopling a new continent
