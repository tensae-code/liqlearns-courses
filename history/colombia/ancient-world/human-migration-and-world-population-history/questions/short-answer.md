# Human Migration and World Population History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Módulo 4 / Guía 12: Historical panorama of world population”?
**Answer:** Ten thousand years ago small bands spread over the Earth; the Inuit come from northern Asia, African peoples migrate across their continent, and today over ten million Mexican-born people live in the United States Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 9: Settlement routes from Africa to America” end?
**Answer:** From Africa the first humans spread and finally cross into America, peopling a new continent Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
