# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Módulo 3 / Guía 8: Cartography in Babylon and Greece”?
**Answer:** The oldest maps are some 5,000 years old, and Babylon and Greece turn territory into drawn knowledge Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 16: Agricultural techniques in the cultures of Antiquity” end?
**Answer:** 5,000 years ago hydraulic societies rise; between the Tigris and Euphrates, where it hardly rains, irrigation makes the alluvial plain fertile, and in America chinampas float gardens on swamps Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
