# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The oldest maps are some 5,000 years old, and Babylon and Greece turn territory into drawn knowledge
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 16: Agricultural techniques in the cultures of Antiquity” is mainly about this: Free men depend on lords in feudal relations, farm technique improves hard lives, the Crusades run from the 11th to the 14th century, the Catholic Church expands, scholasticism teaches, and burgos rise
**Answer: False** — It is about this: 5,000 years ago hydraulic societies rise; between the Tigris and Euphrates, where it hardly rains, irrigation makes the alluvial plain fertile, and in America chinampas float gardens on swamps
