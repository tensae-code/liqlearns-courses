# Myths, Customs and Traditions as Past — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Myth comes from Greek mythos, a tale of gods and extraordinary beings; customs bind a community and traditions are handed down, and children album their community's traditions and research others across Colombia — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
