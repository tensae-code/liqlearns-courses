# 1989: The Berlin Wall and the USSR's Dissolution — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The old USSR was a great set of diverse peoples — Orthodox Russia, Ukraine and Moldova among them — and its unravelling, Berlin Wall and all, remakes the world from 1989 — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
