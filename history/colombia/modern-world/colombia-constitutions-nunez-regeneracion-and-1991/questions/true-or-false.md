# Colombia's Constitutions: Núñez, Regeneración and 1991 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rafael Núñez's project of change is debated from his own discourse, the liberal Constitution of 1863 written at Rionegro is read as one of Colombians' many attempts, and learners tabulate Colombia's constitutions
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guías 15–16: The Constitution of 1991 / The Constitutions of 1886 and 1991” is mainly about this: Portugal sails too, and the two crowns split the world with an imaginary north–south line at Tordesillas
**Answer: False** — It is about this: A timeline runs from 1843, when Pedro Alcántara Herrán is elected mid-civil-war, to 1990, when university students' initiative makes the National Constituent Assembly possible; the 1991 charter ends the Church–State settlement of 1886, replaces the state of siege with graduated emergencies, and is reformed 26 times in 17 years
