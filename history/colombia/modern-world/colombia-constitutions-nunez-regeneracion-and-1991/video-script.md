# Video script — Colombia's Constitutions: Núñez, Regeneración and 1991

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Rafael Núñez's project of change is debated from his own discourse, the liberal Constitution of 1863 written at Rionegro is read as one of Colombians' many attempts, and learners tabulate Colombia's constitutions Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
