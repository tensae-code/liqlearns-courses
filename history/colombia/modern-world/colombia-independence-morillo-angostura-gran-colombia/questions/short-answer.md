# Colombia's Independence: Morillo, Angostura and Gran Colombia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 2: The birth of a nation”?
**Answer:** The early acts still name Fernando VII while demanding criollo power; Pablo Morillo besieges Cartagena from 18 August 1815 in the Spanish reconquest; on 17 December 1819 the Congress of Angostura proclaims the Republic of Colombia, with Santander vice-president of Cundinamarca, Bolívar's union dream fails at the Congress of Panama, and bolivarianos and santanderistas split Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Módulo 2 / Guía 4: Why did we become independent?” end?
**Answer:** The causes of New Granada's independence are built from sources — Captain Antonio Baraya's company guards the council houses as the movement opens, and a merchant lover of liberty asks whether Europe will let an old serpent devour the fairest part of the globe Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
