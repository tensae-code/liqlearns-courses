# Colombia's Independence: Morillo, Angostura and Gran Colombia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The early acts still name Fernando VII while demanding criollo power; Pablo Morillo besieges Cartagena from 18 August 1815 in the Spanish reconquest; on 17 December 1819 the Congress of Angostura proclaims the Republic of Colombia, with Santander vice-president of Cundinamarca, Bolívar's union dream fails at the Congress of Panama, and bolivarianos and santanderistas split
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Módulo 2 / Guía 4: Why did we become independent?” is mainly about this: The child becomes a reporter, interviews an adult about the community's history and lays it out as a frieze of its games and past
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The causes of New Granada's independence are built from sources — Captain Antonio Baraya's company guards the council houses as the movement opens, and a merchant lover of liberty asks whether Europe will let an old serpent devour the fairest part of the globe
