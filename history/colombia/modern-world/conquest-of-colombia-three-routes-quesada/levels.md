# Conquest of Colombia: Three Routes and Quesada — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In fifty years the Spanish conquer by contract and dispute; three routes — Caribbean coast, south-west and centre — push inland, and Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada leaves Santa Marta with 600 foot soldiers and over 100 horsemen — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
