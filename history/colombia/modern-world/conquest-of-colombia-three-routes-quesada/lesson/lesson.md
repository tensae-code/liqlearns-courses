# Conquest of Colombia: Three Routes and Quesada

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Conquest of Colombia: Three Routes and Quesada** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

In fifty years the Spanish conquer by contract and dispute; three routes — Caribbean coast, south-west and centre — push inland, and Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada leaves Santa Marta with 600 foot soldiers and over 100 horsemen Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
