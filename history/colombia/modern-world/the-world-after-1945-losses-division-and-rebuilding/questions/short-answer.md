# The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 4: Welfare state, Eastern Bloc, decolonisation and the Balkans”?
**Answer:** The welfare state provides health and education, the USSR builds an Eastern Bloc of occupied and satellite states, decolonisation in Asia and Africa follows the exhaustion of the metropolis–colony relation, and after the Cold War the Balkans become Europe's main war Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 20: Decolonisation and international conflicts in the Cold War frame” end?
**Answer:** Vietnam begins as a kind of national revolution, a ruler is overthrown mid-conflict, and the United States sinks into the war Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
