# The World after 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The welfare state provides health and education, the USSR builds an Eastern Bloc of occupied and satellite states, decolonisation in Asia and Africa follows the exhaustion of the metropolis–colony relation, and after the Cold War the Balkans become Europe's main war
