**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 4: Welfare state, Eastern Bloc, decolonisation and the Balkans”?
a) The welfare state provides health and education, the USSR builds an Eastern Bloc of occupied and satellite states, decolonisation in Asia and Africa follows the exhaustion of the metropolis–colony relation, and after the Cold War the Balkans become Europe's main war  b) Learners invent a newspaper for a time before newspapers, then dissect four conquest chronicles to name each author's interest, and judge a manuscript account of Mexico's conquest  c) Original peoples raise great constructions, farm with their own tools and techniques, and the Inca keep accounts on the knotted cords of the quipu  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The welfare state provides health and education, the USSR builds an Eastern Bloc of occupied and satellite states, decolonisation in Asia and Africa follows the exhaustion of the metropolis–colony relation, and after the Cold War the Balkans become Europe's main war** — That is what this lesson teaches.
