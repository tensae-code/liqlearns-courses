# The Great Depression, 1929 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Temas 11–12: The economic crisis of 29 in the United States / World crisis and structural change in Colombia”?
a) The crash crosses the world and forces Colombia's own structural change  b) After four decades of conservative government the liberals rule; the Constitutional Reform of 1936 under López provokes immediate controversy, and Eduardo Santos is elected in 1937  c) The child becomes a reporter, interviews an adult about the community's history and lays it out as a frieze of its games and past  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The crash crosses the world and forces Colombia's own structural change** — That is what this lesson teaches.
