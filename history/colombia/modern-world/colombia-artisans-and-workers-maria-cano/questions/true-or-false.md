# Colombia's Artisans and Workers: María Cano — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Late-1800s artisans form Democratic Societies against free trade; river, tramway, oil and banana workers follow, their early strikes are violently repressed, and María Cano of Medellín — a woman and no worker herself — commits her reading-formed ideas to the workers' struggle
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 14: Workers unite to defend their rights” is mainly about this: Three revolutions frame independence — the Rights of Man and of the Citizen are proclaimed, and the industrial revolution's inventions roll from the 1700s into the 1900s
**Answer: False** — It is about this: After a disaster no union groupings arise in Colombia until after the First World War, and politicians' incursion into the unions then blocks their free development
