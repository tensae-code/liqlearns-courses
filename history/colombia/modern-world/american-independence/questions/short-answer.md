# American Independence — short answer

**1.** What happens in “Tema 13: Independence processes in North America and Latin America”?
**Answer:** The thirteen colonies' contradictions become a war of independence and, mid-war on 4 July, a constitution; in Latin America the Bourbons' arrival in the mid-1700s breeds the discontent that ends in independence, Gainza's Central America included Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
