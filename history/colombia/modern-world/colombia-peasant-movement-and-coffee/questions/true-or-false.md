# Colombia's Peasant Movement and Coffee — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Peasant families work the coffee harvests together, organise, and are driven off the land; rural statistics and the question of whether their organisations still exist close the guide
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Módulo 4 / Guía 10: Migrations and conflicts of our land” is mainly about this: Stone struck on stone makes the first tools; in Colombia nomad fishers are followed, thousands of years later, by potters and gardeners on the Atlantic coast, known only from archaeological finds
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Colonos travel to baldío lands, peasants answer the agrarian conflict with land invasions and the first peasant leagues, and López Pumarejo's thought is set against Laureano Gómez's discourse
