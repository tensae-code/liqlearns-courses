# Colombia's Peasant Movement and Coffee — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 3: The Colombian peasant movement”?
**Answer:** Peasant families work the coffee harvests together, organise, and are driven off the land; rural statistics and the question of whether their organisations still exist close the guide Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Módulo 4 / Guía 10: Migrations and conflicts of our land” end?
**Answer:** Colonos travel to baldío lands, peasants answer the agrarian conflict with land invasions and the first peasant leagues, and López Pumarejo's thought is set against Laureano Gómez's discourse Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
