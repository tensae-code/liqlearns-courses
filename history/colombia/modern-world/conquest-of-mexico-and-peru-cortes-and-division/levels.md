# Conquest of Mexico and Peru: Cortés and Division — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Requerimiento is read at midnight, far from the town, in Spanish; Cortés exploits Totonac discontent for alliances, besieged invaders flee without food or water, and the Inca, divided, fall — bravery and Indigenous division decide the conquests — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
