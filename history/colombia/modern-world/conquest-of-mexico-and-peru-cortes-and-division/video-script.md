# Video script — Conquest of Mexico and Peru: Cortés and Division

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The Requerimiento is read at midnight, far from the town, in Spanish; Cortés exploits Totonac discontent for alliances, besieged invaders flee without food or water, and the Inca, divided, fall — bravery and Indigenous division decide the conquests Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Colombia.
