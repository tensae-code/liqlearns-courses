# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Guía 3: The Spanish arrived in America… / 12 October 1492”?
a) After the Catholic reconquest of Spain from the Muslims drains the Crown, Columbus sails, makes four voyages still believing he is in Asia, and meets numerous Indigenous peoples  b) In the last decades of the 1800s and the first of the 1900s the great powers divide the world, and imperialism is distinguished from the older colonialism  c) The old USSR was a great set of diverse peoples — Orthodox Russia, Ukraine and Moldova among them — and its unravelling, Berlin Wall and all, remakes the world from 1989  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) After the Catholic reconquest of Spain from the Muslims drains the Crown, Columbus sails, makes four voyages still believing he is in Asia, and meets numerous Indigenous peoples** — That is what this lesson teaches.
