# Exploration: Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Guía 3: The Spanish arrived in America… / 12 October 1492”?
**Answer:** After the Catholic reconquest of Spain from the Muslims drains the Crown, Columbus sails, makes four voyages still believing he is in Asia, and meets numerous Indigenous peoples Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
