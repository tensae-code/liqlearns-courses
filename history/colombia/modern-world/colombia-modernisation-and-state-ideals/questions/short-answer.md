# Colombia's Modernisation and State Ideals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 6: The impact of modernisation in Colombia”?
**Answer:** By the late 1920s Colombia enters modernisation with most people barely participating in its cultural, social and economic processes, and rival ideals of the State collide Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Temas 6–8: Expansion of the agricultural frontier, the awakening of the economy in the twenties, rural malaise” end?
**Answer:** The farm frontier expands and industry starts late, the 1920s economy awakens, and rural discontent builds over the agrarian structure Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
