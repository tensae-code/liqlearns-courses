# Colombia's Modernisation and State Ideals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) By the late 1920s Colombia enters modernisation with most people barely participating in its cultural, social and economic processes, and rival ideals of the State collide
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Temas 6–8: Expansion of the agricultural frontier, the awakening of the economy in the twenties, rural malaise” is mainly about this: Colombia is mestizo with African roots that persist and change; Afro-Colombian children narrate their own lives — Antonio Mow, raizal of San Andrés, and Juana Pabla Matamba, palenquera — the 1991 Constitution recognises Afro-Colombian rights, and Manuel Zapata Olivella heads the roll of the distinguished
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The farm frontier expands and industry starts late, the 1920s economy awakens, and rural discontent builds over the agrarian structure
