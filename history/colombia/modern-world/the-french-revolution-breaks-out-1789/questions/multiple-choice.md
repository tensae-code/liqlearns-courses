**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 2: The impact of the French Revolution in Colombia”?
a) Revolution is first drawn, then read in fragments; the democratic state is set against the monarchic, and Lefebvre's claim that idealism was a real force in the Revolution is put to learners  b) Colonos travel to baldío lands, peasants answer the agrarian conflict with land invasions and the first peasant leagues, and López Pumarejo's thought is set against Laureano Gómez's discourse  c) Washerwomen work outside Bogotá in 1830, naturalists catalogue over 20,000 plant and 7,000 animal species, modernismo arrives in literature, market days end in the chicherías, and women — 10% of the country then, near 40% of the workforce now — measure the century's change  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Revolution is first drawn, then read in fragments; the democratic state is set against the monarchic, and Lefebvre's claim that idealism was a real force in the Revolution is put to learners** — That is what this lesson teaches.
