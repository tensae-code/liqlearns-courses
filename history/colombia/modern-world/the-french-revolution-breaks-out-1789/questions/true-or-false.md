# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Revolution is first drawn, then read in fragments; the democratic state is set against the monarchic, and Lefebvre's claim that idealism was a real force in the Revolution is put to learners
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Unidad 3 / Tema 12: The bourgeois revolutions” is mainly about this: Families are compared then and now and each child builds an árbol genealógico, turning kinship into a first family history
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Old French society's conflicts boil until on 14 July the Paris crowd storms the Bastille and frees its prisoners
