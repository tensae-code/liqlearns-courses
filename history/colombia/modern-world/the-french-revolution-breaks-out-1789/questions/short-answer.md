# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 2: The impact of the French Revolution in Colombia”?
**Answer:** Revolution is first drawn, then read in fragments; the democratic state is set against the monarchic, and Lefebvre's claim that idealism was a real force in the Revolution is put to learners Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Unidad 3 / Tema 12: The bourgeois revolutions” end?
**Answer:** Old French society's conflicts boil until on 14 July the Paris crowd storms the Bastille and frees its prisoners Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
