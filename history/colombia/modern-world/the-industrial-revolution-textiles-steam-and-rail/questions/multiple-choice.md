# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Guía 3: The Industrial Revolution”?
a) English industrialisation is preceded by 200 years of change; artisans petition that machines will ruin their livelihoods and a few rich businessmen will monopolise every trade  b) Monroe's discourse warns Europe off; on 13 May 1846 Congress declares war on Mexico and takes Arizona, California, Nevada and New Mexico; and the Maine's explosion gives the excuse for war on Spain  c) A law-by-law table runs the agrarian reforms — Ley 200 meant to give land to those who work it — down to articles 64, 65 and 311 of the Political Constitution, and learners compare what was proposed with what was executed  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) English industrialisation is preceded by 200 years of change; artisans petition that machines will ruin their livelihoods and a few rich businessmen will monopolise every trade** — That is what this lesson teaches.
