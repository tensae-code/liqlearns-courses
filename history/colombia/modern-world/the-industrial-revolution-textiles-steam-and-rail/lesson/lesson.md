# The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Industrial Revolution: Textiles, Steam and Rail** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

English industrialisation is preceded by 200 years of change; artisans petition that machines will ruin their livelihoods and a few rich businessmen will monopolise every trade Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
