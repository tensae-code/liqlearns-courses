# The Second World War: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 4: The interwar period and the Second World War (1939–1945)”?
a) The happy twenties give way to a global military conflict, and a survivor's testimony insists the signs were meant to prove Jews, Gypsies and Slavs were cattle, refuse, filth  b) Every group makes its own culture — knowledge, values, foods and traditions that give it identity — and each child must teach theirs to others  c) The Tayrona and Muisca built great villages and complex cultures; Muisca descendants still live on the Cundiboyacense high plain and Tayrona descendants in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The happy twenties give way to a global military conflict, and a survivor's testimony insists the signs were meant to prove Jews, Gypsies and Slavs were cattle, refuse, filth** — That is what this lesson teaches.
