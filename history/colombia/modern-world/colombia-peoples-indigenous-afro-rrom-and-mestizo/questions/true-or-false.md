# Colombia's Peoples: Indigenous, Afro-Colombian, Rrom and Mestizo — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Indigenous peoples, Black/Afro-Colombian, Raizal and Palenquero communities, and the Rrom (Gitano) people are presented one by one, and Colombia is then named a mestizo people born of their meeting
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 7: Black, Afro-Colombian, Raizal and Palenquero communities” is mainly about this: The socialist-inspired Workers' Party splits into Bolsheviks and their rivals, opening the century of war
