# The Renaissance: What It Was — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 18: Political expressions and citizen organisation”?
**Answer:** Growing farm production and population revive cities as distribution centres, and the Renaissance period is characterised by the growth of cities and people Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 19: The Renaissance and the era of discoveries” end?
**Answer:** Born in Italy, Renaissance thought glorifies this life — Leonardo da Vinci hunts the universe's secrets — Venetian merchants fund it, translated texts return, and royal backing renews nautical science and cartography Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
