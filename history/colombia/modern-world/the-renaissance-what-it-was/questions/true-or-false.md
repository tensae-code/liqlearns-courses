# The Renaissance: What It Was — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Growing farm production and population revive cities as distribution centres, and the Renaissance period is characterised by the growth of cities and people
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 19: The Renaissance and the era of discoveries” is mainly about this: Egypt's pyramids need stone blocks hauled to place and its medicine knows the body's organs; ancient India's gifts and Indigenous America's — Maya learning and Inca organisation — complete the inheritance
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Born in Italy, Renaissance thought glorifies this life — Leonardo da Vinci hunts the universe's secrets — Venetian merchants fund it, translated texts return, and royal backing renews nautical science and cartography
