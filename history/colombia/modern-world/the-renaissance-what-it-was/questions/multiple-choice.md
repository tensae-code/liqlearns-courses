# The Renaissance: What It Was — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 18: Political expressions and citizen organisation”?
a) Growing farm production and population revive cities as distribution centres, and the Renaissance period is characterised by the growth of cities and people  b) Players are asked how families, barrios and jobs have changed, whether Black communities endured over 300 years of slavery, and what the free enslaved people who fled to palenques in colonial times were called  c) Learners invent a newspaper for a time before newspapers, then dissect four conquest chronicles to name each author's interest, and judge a manuscript account of Mexico's conquest  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Growing farm production and population revive cities as distribution centres, and the Renaissance period is characterised by the growth of cities and people** — That is what this lesson teaches.
