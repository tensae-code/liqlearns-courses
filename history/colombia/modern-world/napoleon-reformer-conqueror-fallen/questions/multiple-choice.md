# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Tema 12: From revolution to Napoleon and restoration”?
a) Military chiefs draft a constitution and win its vote, but the Bourbon monarchy restores itself in 1814 and Napoleon declines, defeated  b) A grandmother arrives in Viotá, south-west of Bogotá, around 1918 from a small vereda, and learners must build interview questions and put them to two working women of their own region  c) 5,000 years ago hydraulic societies rise; between the Tigris and Euphrates, where it hardly rains, irrigation makes the alluvial plain fertile, and in America chinampas float gardens on swamps  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Military chiefs draft a constitution and win its vote, but the Bourbon monarchy restores itself in 1814 and Napoleon declines, defeated** — That is what this lesson teaches.
