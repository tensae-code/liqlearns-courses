# Video script — Colombia's Internal Conflict: Guerrillas, Paramilitaries and Peace Talks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

The army attacks Marquetalia, the "independent republic"; the FARC expand on a war economy, paramilitary units spread in the late 1980s and grow from guerrilla ally-enemy into a power of their own, and the M-19's successful negotiation — demobilisation, reduced sentences, a political party — is the model peace case, with the AUC talks and their law beside it Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
Forced displacement is named as the conflict's rural face, and learners must propose definitions and solutions of their own Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
The violence wave does not stop; violence-generating agents are catalogued as the government opens peace offensives and the economy transforms Hold the place in your mind as the story moves. The turn in the middle is the heart of it: before the turn things stand one way, after it they stand another, and the people in the story must live in the new way.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Colombia.
