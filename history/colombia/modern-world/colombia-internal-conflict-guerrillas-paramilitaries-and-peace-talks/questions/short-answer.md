# Colombia's Internal Conflict: Guerrillas, Paramilitaries and Peace Talks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Módulo 5 / Guías 13–14: The internal conflict and the peace dialogues”?
**Answer:** The army attacks Marquetalia, the "independent republic"; the FARC expand on a war economy, paramilitary units spread in the late 1980s and grow from guerrilla ally-enemy into a power of their own, and the M-19's successful negotiation — demobilisation, reduced sentences, a political party — is the model peace case, with the AUC talks and their law beside it Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Guía 6: Why is there violence, unemployment and poverty in a resource-rich Colombia?” end?
**Answer:** Forced displacement is named as the conflict's rural face, and learners must propose definitions and solutions of their own Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
