# Colombia's Internal Conflict: Guerrillas, Paramilitaries and Peace Talks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The army attacks Marquetalia, the "independent republic"; the FARC expand on a war economy, paramilitary units spread in the late 1980s and grow from guerrilla ally-enemy into a power of their own, and the M-19's successful negotiation — demobilisation, reduced sentences, a political party — is the model peace case, with the AUC talks and their law beside it
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 6: Why is there violence, unemployment and poverty in a resource-rich Colombia?” is mainly about this: Europe's geographic expansion over America revives the slave system to exploit its resources; at home the feudal lord's derecho de pernada abuses his serfs, Spain lives the Reconquista — 1492 is not only Columbus's year — and Caribe peoples resist in the Sierra Nevada and along the Magdalena
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Forced displacement is named as the conflict's rural face, and learners must propose definitions and solutions of their own
