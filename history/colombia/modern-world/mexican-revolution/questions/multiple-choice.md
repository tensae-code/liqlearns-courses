**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Tema 3: The Mexican Revolution”?
a) Mexico rises in the Americas' first great social revolution of the century, studied in the same breath as Russia's  b) Washerwomen work outside Bogotá in 1830, naturalists catalogue over 20,000 plant and 7,000 animal species, modernismo arrives in literature, market days end in the chicherías, and women — 10% of the country then, near 40% of the workforce now — measure the century's change  c) Coffee production jumps 62% by 1933, the Federación de Cafeteros organises a fund and becomes decisive for industrial development itself  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
