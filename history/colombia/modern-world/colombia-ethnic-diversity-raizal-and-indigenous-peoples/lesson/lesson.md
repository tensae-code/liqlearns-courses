# Colombia's Ethnic Diversity: Raizal and Indigenous Peoples

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Colombia's Ethnic Diversity: Raizal and Indigenous Peoples** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Colombia's diversity is the result of the whole preceding history; the Raizal people of the Caribbean islands descend from Indigenous, Spanish, French, English, Dutch and African mixing, and learners research one Indigenous people's history and culture Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
