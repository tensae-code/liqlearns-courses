# Colombia's Ethnic Diversity: Raizal and Indigenous Peoples — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Colombia's diversity is the result of the whole preceding history; the Raizal people of the Caribbean islands descend from Indigenous, Spanish, French, English, Dutch and African mixing, and learners research one Indigenous people's history and culture — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
