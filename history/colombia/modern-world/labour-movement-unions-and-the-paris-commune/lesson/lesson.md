# Labour Movement: Unions and the Paris Commune

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Labour Movement: Unions and the Paris Commune** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Machine-breakers give way to unions demanding better conditions and wages instead of destruction, until Thiers demands the National Guard's surrender and sends soldiers against Paris Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
