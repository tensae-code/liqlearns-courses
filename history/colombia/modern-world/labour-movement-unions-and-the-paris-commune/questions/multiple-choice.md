**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Tema 15: The labour movement”?
a) Machine-breakers give way to unions demanding better conditions and wages instead of destruction, until Thiers demands the National Guard's surrender and sends soldiers against Paris  b) The viceregal state's inclusion and repression are mostly accepted, until protest breaks out — Túpac Amaru's rebellion is the worked example, drawn and compared by learners  c) Enlightenment ideas of legislative power belonging to the people alone are read in primary fragments and traced into Colombia's own constitution  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
