# The Industrial Revolution: Its Two Stages

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **The Industrial Revolution: Its Two Stages** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Machines enter textile production in the first phase, the bourgeois grows immensely richer, and the smoke-spitting train is blamed for the age's diseases and plagues Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
