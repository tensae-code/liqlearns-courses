# Video script — Colombia: Frente Nacional and FARC Origins

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Two central agreements set the rules — liberals and conservatives alternate and share power, political culture fails to democratise, and in 1964, with the 14 June operation, the FARC are born Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
Fear of a US invasion drives the missile crisis; the Cuban revolution's influence breeds new armed groups in Colombia, and the Frente Nacional's own problems — corruption among the gravest — are named Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Colombia.
