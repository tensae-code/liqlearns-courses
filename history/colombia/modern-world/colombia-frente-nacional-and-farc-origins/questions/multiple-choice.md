# Colombia: Frente Nacional and FARC Origins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 12: The Frente Nacional”?
a) Two central agreements set the rules — liberals and conservatives alternate and share power, political culture fails to democratise, and in 1964, with the 14 June operation, the FARC are born  b) Clocks evolve until Galileo Galilei times a pendulum's swing, and children then order important events on a línea de tiempo and draw the line of their own life  c) Colombia's diversity is the result of the whole preceding history; the Raizal people of the Caribbean islands descend from Indigenous, Spanish, French, English, Dutch and African mixing, and learners research one Indigenous people's history and culture  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Two central agreements set the rules — liberals and conservatives alternate and share power, political culture fails to democratise, and in 1964, with the 14 June operation, the FARC are born** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Fear of a US invasion drives the missile crisis; the Cuban revolution's influence breeds new armed groups in Colombia, and the Frente Nacional's own problems — corruption among the gravest — are named** — That is what this lesson teaches.
