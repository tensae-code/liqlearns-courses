# Latin America: Populism and Dictatorships

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Latin America: Populism and Dictatorships** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Populist governments claim to represent the people's interests — Paz Estenssoro reaches power in Bolivia after coups — dictatorships spread, and the United Fruit Company controls production and refuses the workers' demands Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
