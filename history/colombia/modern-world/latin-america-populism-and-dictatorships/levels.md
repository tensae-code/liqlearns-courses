# Latin America: Populism and Dictatorships — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Populist governments claim to represent the people's interests — Paz Estenssoro reaches power in Bolivia after coups — dictatorships spread, and the United Fruit Company controls production and refuses the workers' demands — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
