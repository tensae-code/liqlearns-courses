# The US and Latin America: Monroe to the Maine — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Tema 18: Relations of the United States with Latin America”?
**Answer:** Monroe's discourse warns Europe off; on 13 May 1846 Congress declares war on Mexico and takes Arizona, California, Nevada and New Mexico; and the Maine's explosion gives the excuse for war on Spain Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 9: The United States and its interventionist role in Latin America and the Caribbean” end?
**Answer:** The Platt Amendment's articles let Washington bind Cuba's foreign agreements, the template of early-century intervention Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
