# The US and Latin America: Monroe to the Maine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Monroe's discourse warns Europe off; on 13 May 1846 Congress declares war on Mexico and takes Arizona, California, Nevada and New Mexico; and the Maine's explosion gives the excuse for war on Spain
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 9: The United States and its interventionist role in Latin America and the Caribbean” is mainly about this: Myth comes from Greek mythos, a tale of gods and extraordinary beings; customs bind a community and traditions are handed down, and children album their community's traditions and research others across Colombia
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The Platt Amendment's articles let Washington bind Cuba's foreign agreements, the template of early-century intervention
