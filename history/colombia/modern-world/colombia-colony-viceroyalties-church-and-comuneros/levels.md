# Colombia's Colony: Viceroyalties, Church and Comuneros — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 1: What was the Colony?
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: After the conquest Spain rules through viceroyalties; pirates such as Morgan and Drake raid the coasts, the Church judges other faiths, and at Zipaquirá Archbishop Caballero y Góngora promises the Comuneros their demands — then the promises are broken and the movement dissolves — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guía 12: Economy and colonial society in New Granada
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The colony organises labour and land through the mita turns, the encomienda grants and the resguardo towns, while the Church collects tithes and names parish priests — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Guía 14: Colony — government and administration
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Carlos V defines an administration for the Indies in 1524, officials face the Juicio de Residencia, the viceroyalty traces to the Capitulaciones de Santa Fe, and Reales Audiencias and the Cabildo — Spain's medieval town council adapted — govern America — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unidad 2 / Temas 8–11: Political, economic, social and cultural aspects of colonial America
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: France holds northern North America and Antillean islands and Holland is present too; the mita obliges a quota of Indigenous workers, colonial society ranks its castes with the enslaved at the bottom — a ranking North America's states already argue over — and colonised communities rapidly learn the imposed language while keeping their own — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
