## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guía 1: What was the Colony?** — After the conquest Spain rules through viceroyalties; pirates such as Morgan and Drake raid the coasts, the Church judges other faiths, and at Zipaquirá Archbishop Caballero y Góngora promises the Comuneros their demands — then the promises are broken and the movement dissolves
2. **Guía 12: Economy and colonial society in New Granada** — The colony organises labour and land through the mita turns, the encomienda grants and the resguardo towns, while the Church collects tithes and names parish priests
3. **Guía 14: Colony — government and administration** — Carlos V defines an administration for the Indies in 1524, officials face the Juicio de Residencia, the viceroyalty traces to the Capitulaciones de Santa Fe, and Reales Audiencias and the Cabildo — Spain's medieval town council adapted — govern America
4. **Unidad 2 / Temas 8–11: Political, economic, social and cultural aspects of colonial America** — France holds northern North America and Antillean islands and Holland is present too; the mita obliges a quota of Indigenous workers, colonial society ranks its castes with the enslaved at the bottom — a ranking North America's states already argue over — and colonised communities rapidly learn the imposed language while keeping their own
