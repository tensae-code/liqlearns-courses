# Colombia's Colony: Viceroyalties, Church and Comuneros — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 1: What was the Colony?”?
**Answer:** After the conquest Spain rules through viceroyalties; pirates such as Morgan and Drake raid the coasts, the Church judges other faiths, and at Zipaquirá Archbishop Caballero y Góngora promises the Comuneros their demands — then the promises are broken and the movement dissolves Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Guía 12: Economy and colonial society in New Granada” end?
**Answer:** The colony organises labour and land through the mita turns, the encomienda grants and the resguardo towns, while the Church collects tithes and names parish priests Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
