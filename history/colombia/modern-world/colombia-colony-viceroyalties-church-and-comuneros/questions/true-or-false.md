# Colombia's Colony: Viceroyalties, Church and Comuneros — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) After the conquest Spain rules through viceroyalties; pirates such as Morgan and Drake raid the coasts, the Church judges other faiths, and at Zipaquirá Archbishop Caballero y Góngora promises the Comuneros their demands — then the promises are broken and the movement dissolves
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 12: Economy and colonial society in New Granada” is mainly about this: Mexico rises in the Americas' first great social revolution of the century, studied in the same breath as Russia's
**Answer: False** — It is about this: The colony organises labour and land through the mita turns, the encomienda grants and the resguardo towns, while the Church collects tithes and names parish priests
