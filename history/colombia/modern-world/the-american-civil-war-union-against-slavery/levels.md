# The American Civil War: Union against Slavery — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The United States consolidates through a four-year civil war of bloody battles, its president's stated object the Union — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
