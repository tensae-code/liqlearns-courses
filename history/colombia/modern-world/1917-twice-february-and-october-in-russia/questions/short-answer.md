# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Módulo 2 / Guía 4: A century in the middle of war”?
**Answer:** The socialist-inspired Workers' Party splits into Bolsheviks and their rivals, opening the century of war Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 3: Russian Revolution and Mexican Revolution” end?
**Answer:** Russia's 1917 turns twice in one year, and Mexico's revolution is set beside it as the Americas' own social revolution Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
