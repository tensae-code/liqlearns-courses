# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The socialist-inspired Workers' Party splits into Bolsheviks and their rivals, opening the century of war
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 3: Russian Revolution and Mexican Revolution” is mainly about this: America is native land and mixing of cultures, Europe a land of seasons and exchanges of knowledge, Asia the land of the Silk Road, and Africa the land of the Black Athena
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Russia's 1917 turns twice in one year, and Mexico's revolution is set beside it as the Americas' own social revolution
