# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Módulo 2 / Guía 4: A century in the middle of war”?
a) The socialist-inspired Workers' Party splits into Bolsheviks and their rivals, opening the century of war  b) Children interview an older relative or neighbour with a set guide, then share and compare what the elders remember  c) Investigators find an old message in unknown symbols, answer culture questions to build the key, convert symbol by symbol, and only then can read what the past left them  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The socialist-inspired Workers' Party splits into Bolsheviks and their rivals, opening the century of war** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Russia's 1917 turns twice in one year, and Mexico's revolution is set beside it as the Americas' own social revolution  b) After the conquest Spain rules through viceroyalties; pirates such as Morgan and Drake raid the coasts, the Church judges other faiths, and at Zipaquirá Archbishop Caballero y Góngora promises the Comuneros their demands — then the promises are broken and the movement dissolves  c) Among divine kingships and food-gatherer bands, Justinian stands as the law-giving emperor whose political thought ordered an empire  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Russia's 1917 turns twice in one year, and Mexico's revolution is set beside it as the Americas' own social revolution** — That is what this lesson teaches.
