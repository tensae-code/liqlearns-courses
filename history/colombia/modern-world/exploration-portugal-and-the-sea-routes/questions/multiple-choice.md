# Exploration: Portugal and the Sea Routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Guía 4: Why is our continent called America? / The art of cartography”?
a) Vespucci, not Columbus, gives the continent its name; Balboa's Darién crossing, Ptolemy's world-without-America map and the advances that made the expeditions possible complete the picture  b) Homes move from adobe and mud to brick and then to "intelligent" houses, so learners walk through time by walking through houses  c) The continent's first groups live as nomads, maize becomes the storable grain that anchors villages, and Indigenous adaptation to each landscape is the lesson's praise  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Vespucci, not Columbus, gives the continent its name; Balboa's Darién crossing, Ptolemy's world-without-America map and the advances that made the expeditions possible complete the picture** — That is what this lesson teaches.
