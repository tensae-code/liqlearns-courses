# Colombia: 19th-Century Daily Life, Science and Women — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Washerwomen work outside Bogotá in 1830, naturalists catalogue over 20,000 plant and 7,000 animal species, modernismo arrives in literature, market days end in the chicherías, and women — 10% of the country then, near 40% of the workforce now — measure the century's change — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
