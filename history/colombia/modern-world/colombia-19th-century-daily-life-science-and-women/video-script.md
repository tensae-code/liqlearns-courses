# Video script — Colombia: 19th-Century Daily Life, Science and Women

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

Washerwomen work outside Bogotá in 1830, naturalists catalogue over 20,000 plant and 7,000 animal species, modernismo arrives in literature, market days end in the chicherías, and women — 10% of the country then, near 40% of the workforce now — measure the century's change Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Colombia.
