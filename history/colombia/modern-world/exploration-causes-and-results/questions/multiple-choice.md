# Exploration: Causes and Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 3C–D: New worlds and hidden worlds / Imagine you are a Taíno Indigenous girl”?
a) Learners write letters home as one of Columbus's sailors, then retell the arrival as a Taíno girl watching the foreigner's impact  b) Confrontation reaches the cities as the army represses harder, Gaitán's killers are demanded, by 1948 violence covers the country, and a massive exodus empties the countryside — learners interview their own elders about it  c) Indigenous peoples, Black/Afro-Colombian, Raizal and Palenquero communities, and the Rrom (Gitano) people are presented one by one, and Colombia is then named a mestizo people born of their meeting  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Learners write letters home as one of Columbus's sailors, then retell the arrival as a Taíno girl watching the foreigner's impact** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Portugal sails too, and the two crowns split the world with an imaginary north–south line at Tordesillas** — That is what this lesson teaches.
