# Exploration: Causes and Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Learners write letters home as one of Columbus's sailors, then retell the arrival as a Taíno girl watching the foreigner's impact
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 5: The division of the world between Spanish and Portuguese / Treaty of Tordesillas” is mainly about this: Original peoples raise great constructions, farm with their own tools and techniques, and the Inca keep accounts on the knotted cords of the quipu
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Portugal sails too, and the two crowns split the world with an imaginary north–south line at Tordesillas
