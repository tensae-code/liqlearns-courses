# Exploration: Causes and Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 3C–D: New worlds and hidden worlds / Imagine you are a Taíno Indigenous girl”?
**Answer:** Learners write letters home as one of Columbus's sailors, then retell the arrival as a Taíno girl watching the foreigner's impact Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Guía 5: The division of the world between Spanish and Portuguese / Treaty of Tordesillas” end?
**Answer:** Portugal sails too, and the two crowns split the world with an imaginary north–south line at Tordesillas Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
