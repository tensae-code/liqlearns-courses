# Hands-on — Colombia: La Violencia, the Bogotazo and Gaitán

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
On a blank page, draw a simple map of where this story happens — Colombia: La Violencia, the Bogotazo and Gaitán — and mark the place where the story turns. Write one line under the map saying what changed there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Violence first erupts as conservative Mariano Ospina Pérez takes power; Jorge Eliécer Gaitán's many faces and the Bogotazo of 9 April, during the IX Pan-American Conference, are read through industrialists' and conservatives' rival versions Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
