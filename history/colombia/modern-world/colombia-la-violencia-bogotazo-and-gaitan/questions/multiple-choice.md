# Colombia: La Violencia, the Bogotazo and Gaitán — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 11: Causes and effects of La Violencia”?
a) Violence first erupts as conservative Mariano Ospina Pérez takes power; Jorge Eliécer Gaitán's many faces and the Bogotazo of 9 April, during the IX Pan-American Conference, are read through industrialists' and conservatives' rival versions  b) The child orders past, present and future on a "tren del tiempo" and sees their own body and character change as time passes  c) Children ask neighbours and relatives who have lived elsewhere how things change, treating change over time and place as observable fact  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Violence first erupts as conservative Mariano Ospina Pérez takes power; Jorge Eliécer Gaitán's many faces and the Bogotazo of 9 April, during the IX Pan-American Conference, are read through industrialists' and conservatives' rival versions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
