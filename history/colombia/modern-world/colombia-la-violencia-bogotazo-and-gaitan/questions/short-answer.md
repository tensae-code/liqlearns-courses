# Colombia: La Violencia, the Bogotazo and Gaitán — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 11: Causes and effects of La Violencia”?
**Answer:** Violence first erupts as conservative Mariano Ospina Pérez takes power; Jorge Eliécer Gaitán's many faces and the Bogotazo of 9 April, during the IX Pan-American Conference, are read through industrialists' and conservatives' rival versions Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Tema 18: The violence of the mid-20th century” end?
**Answer:** Confrontation reaches the cities as the army represses harder, Gaitán's killers are demanded, by 1948 violence covers the country, and a massive exodus empties the countryside — learners interview their own elders about it Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
