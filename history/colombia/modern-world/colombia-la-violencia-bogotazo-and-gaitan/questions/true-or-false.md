# Colombia: La Violencia, the Bogotazo and Gaitán — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Violence first erupts as conservative Mariano Ospina Pérez takes power; Jorge Eliécer Gaitán's many faces and the Bogotazo of 9 April, during the IX Pan-American Conference, are read through industrialists' and conservatives' rival versions
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Tema 18: The violence of the mid-20th century” is mainly about this: From Africa the first humans spread and finally cross into America, peopling a new continent
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Confrontation reaches the cities as the army represses harder, Gaitán's killers are demanded, by 1948 violence covers the country, and a massive exodus empties the countryside — learners interview their own elders about it
