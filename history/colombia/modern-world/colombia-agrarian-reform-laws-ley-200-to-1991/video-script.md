# Video script — Colombia's Agrarian Reform Laws: Ley 200 to 1991

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.

A law-by-law table runs the agrarian reforms — Ley 200 meant to give land to those who work it — down to articles 64, 65 and 311 of the Political Constitution, and learners compare what was proposed with what was executed Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Colombia.
