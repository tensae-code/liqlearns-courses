# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Alejo and Mariana ask who they are and how their families differ, so the past starts with the child's own life
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 3: Families before and now / My family tree” is mainly about this: 5,000 years ago hydraulic societies rise; between the Tigris and Euphrates, where it hardly rains, irrigation makes the alluvial plain fertile, and in America chinampas float gardens on swamps
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Families are compared then and now and each child builds an árbol genealógico, turning kinship into a first family history
