# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 1–2: Who am I? / What is the family?”?
a) Alejo and Mariana ask who they are and how their families differ, so the past starts with the child's own life  b) Each barrio is said to have its own history and profile, and Noíl's Arhuaco community at Nabusímake shows an Indigenous town's own way of life  c) In eastern Africa millions of years ago the first ancestors learn to hunt, knap stone tools, wander and finally speak, beginning humanity's long road  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Alejo and Mariana ask who they are and how their families differ, so the past starts with the child's own life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 3: Families before and now / My family tree”?
a) Families are compared then and now and each child builds an árbol genealógico, turning kinship into a first family history  b) Three revolutions frame independence — the Rights of Man and of the Citizen are proclaimed, and the industrial revolution's inventions roll from the 1700s into the 1900s  c) European intervention in the trade in human beings, at over 50 million people, is named without precedent as the background to today's conflicts over rights  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Families are compared then and now and each child builds an árbol genealógico, turning kinship into a first family history** — That is what this lesson teaches.
