# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **What History Is** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 1–2: Who am I? / What is the family?

Alejo and Mariana ask who they are and how their families differ, so the past starts with the child's own life Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guía 3: Families before and now / My family tree

Families are compared then and now and each child builds an árbol genealógico, turning kinship into a first family history Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
