# Historiography: The History of History Writing — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A conqueror who founded cities, a king advised by the Council of the Indies, an artisan of Bogotá's Democratic Society, a coffee colonist of Viejo Caldas, an ANUC peasant organiser, a workers' lawyer and Morillo himself each speak in first person, and the learner must know the history well enough to name the speaker — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
