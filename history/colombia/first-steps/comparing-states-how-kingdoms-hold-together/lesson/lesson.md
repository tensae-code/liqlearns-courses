## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 3 / Tema 12: Characteristics of empires

The first empires grow from demography and nature's riches, and Felipe II of Spain (1527–1598) is the modern example held up for comparison Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tema 26: Differences and similarities in America and Europe

The two worlds' societies are set side by side to close the unit, comparing how each organised power and life Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
