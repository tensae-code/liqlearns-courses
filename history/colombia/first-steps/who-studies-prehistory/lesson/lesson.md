# Who Studies Prehistory

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Who Studies Prehistory** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Humanity's history is the longest and most complex story of all, and the Aztec sun-and-moon gods Tecuciztécatl and Nanahuatzin, the Inca universe and the Muisca and Kogi stars show each people's own account of beginnings Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
