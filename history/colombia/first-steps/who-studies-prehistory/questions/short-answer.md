# Who Studies Prehistory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Unidad 2 / Tema 7–8: Origin and evolution of the human being”?
**Answer:** Humanity's history is the longest and most complex story of all, and the Aztec sun-and-moon gods Tecuciztécatl and Nanahuatzin, the Inca universe and the Muisca and Kogi stars show each people's own account of beginnings Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
