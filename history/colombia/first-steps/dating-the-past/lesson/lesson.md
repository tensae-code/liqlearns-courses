# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Dating the Past** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 1: You change with time / the time train

The child orders past, present and future on a "tren del tiempo" and sees their own body and character change as time passes Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guía 10: Changes in my community through time / Housing through time

Homes move from adobe and mud to brick and then to "intelligent" houses, so learners walk through time by walking through houses Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.

## Sub lesson 3 — Guía 10: Jobs and dress through time

The first groups fished, hunted and gathered, grandparents sewed and carried by hand, and new inventions create new professions; Roman dress (the toga) is the one world-history glimpse Hold the place in your mind as the story moves. The turn in the middle is the heart of it: before the turn things stand one way, after it they stand another, and the people in the story must live in the new way.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unidad 1 / Guía 1: How is time measured?
