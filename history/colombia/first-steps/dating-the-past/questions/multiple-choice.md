# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 1: You change with time / the time train”?
a) The child orders past, present and future on a "tren del tiempo" and sees their own body and character change as time passes  b) Enslaved Africans bring rites, songs, dances, gods and deep knowledge; the Laws of the Indies answer denunciations of abuse, the free flee and fortify palenques, and oral tradition, trees and drums carry the legacy — filed here as the Colombia telling of the Atlantic trade (see also Book 10 Guía 6 note below)  c) America's ancient peoples work metals into sacred art, thanking Mother Earth for the materials, a craft Colombia's own ancient peoples carried to mastery  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) The child orders past, present and future on a "tren del tiempo" and sees their own body and character change as time passes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 10: Changes in my community through time / Housing through time”?
a) Homes move from adobe and mud to brick and then to "intelligent" houses, so learners walk through time by walking through houses  b) Monarchies triumph over the Greek polis, and at Philip of Macedon's death his son Alexander builds an empire that also transforms Greece  c) Carlos V defines an administration for the Indies in 1524, officials face the Juicio de Residencia, the viceroyalty traces to the Capitulaciones de Santa Fe, and Reales Audiencias and the Cabildo — Spain's medieval town council adapted — govern America  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Homes move from adobe and mud to brick and then to "intelligent" houses, so learners walk through time by walking through houses** — That is what this lesson teaches.
