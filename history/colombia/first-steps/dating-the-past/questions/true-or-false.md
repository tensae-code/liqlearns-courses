# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The child orders past, present and future on a "tren del tiempo" and sees their own body and character change as time passes
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 10: Changes in my community through time / Housing through time” is mainly about this: Rome is not built in a day — seven kings, then consuls; the Punic Wars break Carthage, Hannibal finds asylum in Syria, Caesar is murdered, the Second Triumvirate follows, and Augustus reforms the state and gives Roman law
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Homes move from adobe and mud to brick and then to "intelligent" houses, so learners walk through time by walking through houses
