# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 1: You change with time / the time train”?
**Answer:** The child orders past, present and future on a "tren del tiempo" and sees their own body and character change as time passes Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Guía 10: Changes in my community through time / Housing through time” end?
**Answer:** Homes move from adobe and mud to brick and then to "intelligent" houses, so learners walk through time by walking through houses Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
