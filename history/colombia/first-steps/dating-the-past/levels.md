# Dating the Past — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 1: You change with time / the time train
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The child orders past, present and future on a "tren del tiempo" and sees their own body and character change as time passes — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guía 10: Changes in my community through time / Housing through time
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Homes move from adobe and mud to brick and then to "intelligent" houses, so learners walk through time by walking through houses — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Guía 10: Jobs and dress through time
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The first groups fished, hunted and gathered, grandparents sewed and carried by hand, and new inventions create new professions; Roman dress (the toga) is the one world-history glimpse — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Carrera Equina review questions 4–16
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Players are asked how families, barrios and jobs have changed, whether Black communities endured over 300 years of slavery, and what the free enslaved people who fled to palenques in colonial times were called — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Unidad 1 / Guía 1: How is time measured?
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Humans invent the notion of time to measure duration, then hours, weeks and months from the Sun's cycles, so measuring comes before dating — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Guía 2: The history of the clock / Juanito's timelines
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Clocks evolve until Galileo Galilei times a pendulum's swing, and children then order important events on a línea de tiempo and draw the line of their own life — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — Guía 6D: How do things change?
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Children ask neighbours and relatives who have lived elsewhere how things change, treating change over time and place as observable fact — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 8 — El monopolio del Tiempo y el Espacio, squares 4 and 31
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Players pick the oldest of three clocks and then order María's life on her timeline — first pregnant, house bought, or cows bought — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 9 — Tema 11: Historical periods and techniques of adaptation
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Prehistory is humanity's remotest age; with new techniques humans pass from a predatory to a productive economy and settled societies appear — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
