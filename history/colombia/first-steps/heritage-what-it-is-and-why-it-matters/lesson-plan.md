## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guía 10: Monuments tell stories** — Monuments are introduced as objects that narrate the past, and children research a chosen monument's history and design one for their own barrio
2. **Guía 6: Expressions of culture / Teach your culture** — Every group makes its own culture — knowledge, values, foods and traditions that give it identity — and each child must teach theirs to others
3. **Guía 2: Cultural legacies that endure** — Myths interpreted the world in dualities of good and evil, and the African heritage is traced forward into the Colombian context

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
