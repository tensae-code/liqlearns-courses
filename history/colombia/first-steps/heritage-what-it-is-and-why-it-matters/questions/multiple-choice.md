# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 10: Monuments tell stories”?
a) Monuments are introduced as objects that narrate the past, and children research a chosen monument's history and design one for their own barrio  b) America is native land and mixing of cultures, Europe a land of seasons and exchanges of knowledge, Asia the land of the Silk Road, and Africa the land of the Black Athena  c) Feudal Europe stratifies into clergy, nobility and peasants, and women's work holds the system up  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Monuments are introduced as objects that narrate the past, and children research a chosen monument's history and design one for their own barrio** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Every group makes its own culture — knowledge, values, foods and traditions that give it identity — and each child must teach theirs to others** — That is what this lesson teaches.
