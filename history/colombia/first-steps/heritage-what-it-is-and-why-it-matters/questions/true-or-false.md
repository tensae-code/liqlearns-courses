# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Monuments are introduced as objects that narrate the past, and children research a chosen monument's history and design one for their own barrio
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 6: Expressions of culture / Teach your culture” is mainly about this: Old French society's conflicts boil until on 14 July the Paris crowd storms the Bastille and frees its prisoners
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Every group makes its own culture — knowledge, values, foods and traditions that give it identity — and each child must teach theirs to others
