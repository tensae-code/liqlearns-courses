# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 10: Monuments tell stories”?
**Answer:** Monuments are introduced as objects that narrate the past, and children research a chosen monument's history and design one for their own barrio Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Guía 6: Expressions of culture / Teach your culture” end?
**Answer:** Every group makes its own culture — knowledge, values, foods and traditions that give it identity — and each child must teach theirs to others Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
