# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Children compare dress, food, music and festivals across Colombia's regions and then record their own region's customs
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Guía 5: Does your barrio have a history? / Noíl in Nabusímake” is mainly about this: Gregorian chant such as Puer natus est survives as an early carol, Islamic art decorates without images, and Indigenous artists of the 1500s create what is named Indo-Christian art
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Each barrio is said to have its own history and profile, and Noíl's Arhuaco community at Nabusímake shows an Indigenous town's own way of life
