# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 3: How are the people of my region?”?
a) Children compare dress, food, music and festivals across Colombia's regions and then record their own region's customs  b) Myth comes from Greek mythos, a tale of gods and extraordinary beings; customs bind a community and traditions are handed down, and children album their community's traditions and research others across Colombia  c) After the conquest Spain rules through viceroyalties; pirates such as Morgan and Drake raid the coasts, the Church judges other faiths, and at Zipaquirá Archbishop Caballero y Góngora promises the Comuneros their demands — then the promises are broken and the movement dissolves  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Children compare dress, food, music and festivals across Colombia's regions and then record their own region's customs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
