# Lesson plan — Find History in Your Own Locality

**Course:** History — Colombia · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Find History in Your Own Locality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: Your Own Past, Time and Place (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: Your Own Past, Time and Place (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guía 3: How are the people of my region?** — Children compare dress, food, music and festivals across Colombia's regions and then record their own region's customs
2. **Guía 5: Does your barrio have a history? / Noíl in Nabusímake** — Each barrio is said to have its own history and profile, and Noíl's Arhuaco community at Nabusímake shows an Indigenous town's own way of life
3. **Guía 5: The story of the road at La Portada** — Delegates Matilde and Helena negotiate with the municipality, which supplies machinery while the community works, and the vereda gets its road
4. **Guía 10D: After the history of my municipality / Reporter of my history** — The child becomes a reporter, interviews an adult about the community's history and lays it out as a frieze of its games and past
5. **Guía 11D: The history and culture of your community** — Every community has a history, and groups pick one cultural expression, research it in books and by asking adults, and present it
6. **Guía 2C: Questions about the place where you live** — With parents, grandparents or neighbours, children answer questions about their home place's past, using family memory as the source

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
