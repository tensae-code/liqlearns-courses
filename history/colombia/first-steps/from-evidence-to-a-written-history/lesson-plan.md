## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guía 4: Interview an elder** — Children interview an older relative or neighbour with a set guide, then share and compare what the elders remember
2. **Descifrando el Mensaje Secreto** — Investigators find an old message in unknown symbols, answer culture questions to build the key, convert symbol by symbol, and only then can read what the past left them
3. **Guía 5C–D: Historical-fiction game / Analysis of historical text fragments** — Learners invent a newspaper for a time before newspapers, then dissect four conquest chronicles to name each author's interest, and judge a manuscript account of Mexico's conquest
4. **Guía 2C: Interpret documents** — Learners compare two documents and set Santander's "laws will give you liberty" line of thought against Bolívar's sayings, judging independence from its leaders' own words
5. **Guía 4D: The history of the grandmothers** — A grandmother arrives in Viotá, south-west of Bogotá, around 1918 from a small vereda, and learners must build interview questions and put them to two working women of their own region
6. **Guía 1: Guidelines for reading and analysing sources (PAF)** — Before any history, learners get a method — identify the author, themes, dates and place of every source, and file a card for each

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
