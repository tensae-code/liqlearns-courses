# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the heart of the story in “Guía 4: Interview an elder”?
a) Children interview an older relative or neighbour with a set guide, then share and compare what the elders remember  b) A conqueror who founded cities, a king advised by the Council of the Indies, an artisan of Bogotá's Democratic Society, a coffee colonist of Viejo Caldas, an ANUC peasant organiser, a workers' lawyer and Morillo himself each speak in first person, and the learner must know the history well enough to name the speaker  c) Mesopotamia's stratification is fixed by law, China is a society directed by sages where Confucian family values guarantee harmony, slaves stand last on the scale, and the Inca hatunruna work the land  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Children interview an older relative or neighbour with a set guide, then share and compare what the elders remember** — That is what this lesson teaches.
