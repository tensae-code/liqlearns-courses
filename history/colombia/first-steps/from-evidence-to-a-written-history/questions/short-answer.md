# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What happens in “Guía 4: Interview an elder”?
**Answer:** Children interview an older relative or neighbour with a set guide, then share and compare what the elders remember Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the story in “Descifrando el Mensaje Secreto” end?
**Answer:** Investigators find an old message in unknown symbols, answer culture questions to build the key, convert symbol by symbol, and only then can read what the past left them Picture the place first, then the people in it, then the turn. Nothing in this part is decoration: each step leads to the next, and the end of the part is different from its beginning because of what happens in between.
