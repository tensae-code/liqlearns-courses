# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Children interview an older relative or neighbour with a set guide, then share and compare what the elders remember
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Descifrando el Mensaje Secreto” is mainly about this: Enlightenment ideas of legislative power belonging to the people alone are read in primary fragments and traced into Colombia's own constitution
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Investigators find an old message in unknown symbols, answer culture questions to build the key, convert symbol by symbol, and only then can read what the past left them
