# From Evidence to a Written History — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 4: Interview an elder
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Children interview an older relative or neighbour with a set guide, then share and compare what the elders remember — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Descifrando el Mensaje Secreto
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Investigators find an old message in unknown symbols, answer culture questions to build the key, convert symbol by symbol, and only then can read what the past left them — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Guía 5C–D: Historical-fiction game / Analysis of historical text fragments
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Learners invent a newspaper for a time before newspapers, then dissect four conquest chronicles to name each author's interest, and judge a manuscript account of Mexico's conquest — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Guía 2C: Interpret documents
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Learners compare two documents and set Santander's "laws will give you liberty" line of thought against Bolívar's sayings, judging independence from its leaders' own words — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Guía 4D: The history of the grandmothers
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A grandmother arrives in Viotá, south-west of Bogotá, around 1918 from a small vereda, and learners must build interview questions and put them to two working women of their own region — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Guía 1: Guidelines for reading and analysing sources (PAF)
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Before any history, learners get a method — identify the author, themes, dates and place of every source, and file a card for each — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
