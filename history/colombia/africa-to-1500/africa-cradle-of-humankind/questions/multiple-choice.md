# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Guía 1: The ancestors of the human being”?
a) In eastern Africa millions of years ago the first ancestors learn to hunt, knap stone tools, wander and finally speak, beginning humanity's long road  b) The two worlds' societies are set side by side to close the unit, comparing how each organised power and life  c) Machines enter textile production in the first phase, the bourgeois grows immensely richer, and the smoke-spitting train is blamed for the age's diseases and plagues  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) In eastern Africa millions of years ago the first ancestors learn to hunt, knap stone tools, wander and finally speak, beginning humanity's long road** — That is what this lesson teaches.
