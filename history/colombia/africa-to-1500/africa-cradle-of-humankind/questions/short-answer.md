# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Guía 1: The ancestors of the human being”?
**Answer:** In eastern Africa millions of years ago the first ancestors learn to hunt, knap stone tools, wander and finally speak, beginning humanity's long road Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
