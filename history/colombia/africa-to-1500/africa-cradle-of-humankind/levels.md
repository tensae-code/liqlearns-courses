# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — In eastern Africa millions of years ago the first ancestors learn to hunt, knap stone tools, wander and finally speak, beginning humanity's long road — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
