# From Stone Tools to Farming — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Stone struck on stone makes the first tools; in Colombia nomad fishers are followed, thousands of years later, by potters and gardeners on the Atlantic coast, known only from archaeological finds — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
