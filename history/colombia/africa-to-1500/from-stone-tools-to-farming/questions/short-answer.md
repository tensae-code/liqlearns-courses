# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What happens in “Guía 1–2: First tools / The first nomads in Colombia”?
**Answer:** Stone struck on stone makes the first tools; in Colombia nomad fishers are followed, thousands of years later, by potters and gardeners on the Atlantic coast, known only from archaeological finds Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
