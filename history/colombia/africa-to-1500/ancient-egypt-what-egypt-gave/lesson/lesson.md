# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

## Do it first

Before you read, write one line: what do you think the story of **Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave** will be about? Then draw a rough map from memory of where you think it happens. Keep both — you will check them at the end.

Egypt's pyramids need stone blocks hauled to place and its medicine knows the body's organs; ancient India's gifts and Indigenous America's — Maya learning and Inca organisation — complete the inheritance Follow the people in it, the place they stand in, and the moment the story turns. What changes at that turn is what the people who come after must live with — that is why this part is told, and told in this order.
