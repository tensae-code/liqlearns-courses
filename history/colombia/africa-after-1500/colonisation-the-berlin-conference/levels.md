# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Recently independent Latin America faces neocolonisation while the European powers colonise Africa — Italy taking part of the east coast — with forced labour, political domination and exploitation, and a violence against Africans that no wealth satisfies — in their own words, give one real example from the lesson, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
