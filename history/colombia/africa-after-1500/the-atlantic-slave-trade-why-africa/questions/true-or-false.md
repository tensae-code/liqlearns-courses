# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Why Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Enslaved Africans bring rites, songs, dances, gods and deep knowledge; the Laws of the Indies answer denunciations of abuse, the free flee and fortify palenques, and oral tradition, trees and drums carry the legacy — filed here as the Colombia telling of the Atlantic trade (see also Book 10 Guía 6 note below)
**Answer: True**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The part “Módulo 6 / Guía 16: Conflicts in the societies of the 5th–15th centuries” is mainly about this: Places across the Empire turn Christian and faith becomes resistance to oppression, while Muhammad preaches that the rich must share, Islam rests on five pillars, and its culture grows from the peoples it rules
**Answer: False** — It is about this: Europe's geographic expansion over America revives the slave system to exploit its resources; at home the feudal lord's derecho de pernada abuses his serfs, Spain lives the Reconquista — 1492 is not only Columbus's year — and Caribe peoples resist in the Sierra Nevada and along the Magdalena
