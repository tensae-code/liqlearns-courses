# Slavery before the Atlantic Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the heart of the story in “Guía 5: Slavery in the history of humanity”?
a) Slavery is universal and ancient — in Antiquity and the Middle Ages the enslaved work fields and mines — before it becomes an Atlantic system  b) 94 Indigenous peoples live in Colombia, many bilingual or trilingual, educated first by elders' counsel, protected in resguardos; Páez leader and senator Jesús Enrique Piñacué and the Wayúu of La Guajira carry the telling  c) Power in ancient Mesopotamia turns custom into divine belief; at its splendour the Aztec empire rules, the Inca organises his empire, and the Muisca order their own polity  d) A story that is not taught in this lesson
**Answer: a) Slavery is universal and ancient — in Antiquity and the Middle Ages the enslaved work fields and mines — before it becomes an Atlantic system** — That is what this lesson teaches.
