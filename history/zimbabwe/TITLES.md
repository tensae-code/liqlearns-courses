# History — Zimbabwe course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Zimbabwe's
own — built from Zimbabwe's nine PlusOne Social Science
teacher guides (English, Secondary Book Press, Harare, 2025),
from ECD A to Grade 7. Inside the course the organisation is
by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside the
levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grade the book comes from lives only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books' spiral: the child's own
story first (family, locality, heritage, nation), then the
deep past, then colonisation and the two Chimurengas, then
independent Zimbabwe. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
