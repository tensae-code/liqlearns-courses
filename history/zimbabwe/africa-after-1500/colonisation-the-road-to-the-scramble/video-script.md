# Video script — Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. In Zimbabwe the road to colonisation ran through paper: leaders were tricked into signing agreements, fertile land was lost, and displacement and broken customs followed — the pen did what the spear had not.

First: The pen does what the spear has not — leaders are tricked into signing agreements, fertile land is lost, and displacement and broken customs follow.

Remember it this way: in Zimbabwe the road to colonisation ran through paper: leaders were tricked into signing agreements, fertile land was lost, and displacement and broken customs followed — the pen did what the spear had not.
