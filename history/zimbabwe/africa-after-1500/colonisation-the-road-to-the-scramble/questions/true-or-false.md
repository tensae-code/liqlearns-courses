# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The pen does what the spear has not — leaders are tricked into signing agreements, fertile land is lost, and displacement and broken customs follow.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: in Zimbabwe the road to colonisation ran through paper: leaders were tricked into signing agreements, fertile land was lost, and displacement and broken customs followed — the pen did what the spear had not.**
