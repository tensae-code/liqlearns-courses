# Colonisation: The Road to the Scramble

## Do it first

Picture a leader putting his mark on a paper he cannot read, in a language he does not speak — and a people's fertile land passing with that mark. This lesson tells how the pen opened the road the spear had kept shut.

## The story

The pen does what the spear has not — leaders are tricked into signing agreements, fertile land is lost, and displacement and broken customs follow.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: in Zimbabwe the road to colonisation ran through paper: leaders were tricked into signing agreements, fertile land was lost, and displacement and broken customs followed — the pen did what the spear had not.

Give one real example from the story, and name the lesson's key words: agreements, land dispossession, displacement, broken customs, colonisation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
