# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilising Mission — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain's shopping list is named plainly — cheap labour, raw materials, land — and Rhodes's Cape-to-Cairo dream stretches over the whole map.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Britain's reasons for colonising Zimbabwe are named plainly in Zimbabwe's own books: cheap labour, raw materials and land — with Rhodes's Cape-to-Cairo dream stretched over the whole map.**
