# Why Europe Colonised: Motives and the Civilising Mission

## Do it first

Ask the plain question this lesson's title asks: why did they come? Zimbabwe's books answer it as a shopping list. Read the list before you judge the story built on top of it.

## The story

Britain's shopping list is named plainly — cheap labour, raw materials, land — and Rhodes's Cape-to-Cairo dream stretches over the whole map.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: Britain's reasons for colonising Zimbabwe are named plainly in Zimbabwe's own books: cheap labour, raw materials and land — with Rhodes's Cape-to-Cairo dream stretched over the whole map.
