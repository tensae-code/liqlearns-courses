# Zimbabwe: Ubuntu, Unhu, Vumunhu

## Do it first

Think of one thing you can only do because other people exist — being greeted, being taught, being fed, being forgiven. This lesson names the value underneath all of it: a person is a person through other people.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 3, Unit 12 Attributes of Ubuntu/Unhu/Vumunhu

The heritage topics close by naming the value that carries them — a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders become the rules the community lives by.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic 3, Unit 9 Attributes of unhu/ubuntu/vumunhu

Unhu is unpacked into its attributes, so the value stops being a word and becomes the checklist of how a good person treats others.
