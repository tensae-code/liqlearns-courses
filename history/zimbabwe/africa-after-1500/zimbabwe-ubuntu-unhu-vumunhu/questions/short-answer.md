# Zimbabwe: Ubuntu, Unhu, Vumunhu — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ubuntu — Unhu, Vumunhu — is the value that carries Zimbabwe's heritage: a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders are the rules the community lives by.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Topic 3, Unit 9 Attributes of unhu/ubuntu/vumunhu"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Unhu is unpacked into its attributes, so the value stops being a word and becomes the checklist of how a good person treats others.
