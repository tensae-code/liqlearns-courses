# Zimbabwe: Ubuntu, Unhu, Vumunhu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The heritage topics close by naming the value that carries them — a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders become the rules the community lives by.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) The heritage topics close by naming the value that carries them — a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders become the rules the community lives by.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) Unhu is unpacked into its attributes, so the value stops being a word and becomes the checklist of how a good person treats others.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) Unhu is unpacked into its attributes, so the value stops being a word and becomes the checklist of how a good person treats others.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
