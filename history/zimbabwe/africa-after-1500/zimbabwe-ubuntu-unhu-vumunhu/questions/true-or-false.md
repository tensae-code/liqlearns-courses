# Zimbabwe: Ubuntu, Unhu, Vumunhu — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The heritage topics close by naming the value that carries them — a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders become the rules the community lives by. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Ubuntu — Unhu, Vumunhu — is the value that carries Zimbabwe's heritage: a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders are the rules the community lives by.**
