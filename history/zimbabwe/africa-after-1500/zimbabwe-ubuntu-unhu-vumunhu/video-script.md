# Video script — Zimbabwe: Ubuntu, Unhu, Vumunhu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Ubuntu — Unhu, Vumunhu — is the value that carries Zimbabwe's heritage: a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders are the rules the community lives by.

Part 1 — Topic 3, Unit 12 Attributes of Ubuntu/Unhu/Vumunhu. The heritage topics close by naming the value that carries them — a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders become the rules the community lives by.

Part 2 — Topic 3, Unit 9 Attributes of unhu/ubuntu/vumunhu. Unhu is unpacked into its attributes, so the value stops being a word and becomes the checklist of how a good person treats others.

Remember it this way: Ubuntu — Unhu, Vumunhu — is the value that carries Zimbabwe's heritage: a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders are the rules the community lives by.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
