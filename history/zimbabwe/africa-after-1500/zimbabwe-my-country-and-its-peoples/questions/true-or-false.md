# Zimbabwe: My Country and Its Peoples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zimbabwe is named as home with Harare its capital and 10 provinces, and its peoples are introduced one by one — Ndau dancing muchongoyo at Chipinge, Tonga fishing at Binga, Chewa dancing Gule Wamkulu at Hwange. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Zimbabwe is one country made of many peoples: Harare is its capital, it has 10 provinces, and each of its peoples — Ndau, Tonga, Chewa, Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndebele, Venda and others — is known by where it lives, the tongue it speaks and the customs it keeps.**
