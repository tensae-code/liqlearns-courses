# Zimbabwe: My Country and Its Peoples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Zimbabwe is one country made of many peoples: Harare is its capital, it has 10 provinces, and each of its peoples — Ndau, Tonga, Chewa, Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndebele, Venda and others — is known by where it lives, the tongue it speaks and the customs it keeps.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Topic 2, Unit 7 Ethnic groups and languages"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The peoples lesson returns with languages attached, so each group is known by where it lives and the tongue it speaks.
