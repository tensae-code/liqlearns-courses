# Zimbabwe: My Country and Its Peoples — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 2, Unit 6 My country and ethnic groups
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Zimbabwe is named as home with Harare its capital and 10 provinces, and its peoples are introduced one by one — Ndau dancing muchongoyo at Chipinge, Tonga fishing at Binga, Chewa dancing Gule Wamkulu at Hwange. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
