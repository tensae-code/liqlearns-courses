# Video script — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The first Europeans came to Zimbabwe in three kinds — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each kind changed the country differently.
