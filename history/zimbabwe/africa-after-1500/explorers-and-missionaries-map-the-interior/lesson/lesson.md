# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

## Do it first

Three strangers walk into the same country. One carries a book and a school, one a rifle aimed at elephants, one a pan for gold. This lesson sorts them — because what each one wanted decided what each one changed.

## The story

Three kinds of stranger arrive — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each changes the country differently.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: the first Europeans came to Zimbabwe in three kinds — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each kind changed the country differently.
