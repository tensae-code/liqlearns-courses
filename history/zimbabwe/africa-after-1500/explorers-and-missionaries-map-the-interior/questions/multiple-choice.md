# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story?
a) Three kinds of stranger arrive — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each changes the country differently.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) Three kinds of stranger arrive — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each changes the country differently.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) The first Europeans came to Zimbabwe in three kinds — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each kind changed the country differently.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) The first Europeans came to Zimbabwe in three kinds — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each kind changed the country differently.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
