# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three kinds of stranger arrive — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each changes the country differently.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: the first Europeans came to Zimbabwe in three kinds — missionaries like Robert Moffat with schools and Christianity, hunters like Frederick Courtney Selous after ivory, and prospectors after gold — and each kind changed the country differently.**
