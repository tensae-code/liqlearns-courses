# Zimbabwe: How Settlements Grew

## Do it first

Look at the place you live and ask how it grew: which came first, the village, the road, the town? This lesson reads settlements as history — every town a deposit of the story before it.

## The story

Where people live is itself a history — settlements grow from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything before.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: where people live is itself a history: Zimbabwe's settlements grew from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything that came before.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: where people live is itself a history: Zimbabwe's settlements grew from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything that came before. Give one real example from the story, and name the lesson's key words: settlements, village, town, city, development of settlements. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
