# Zimbabwe: How Settlements Grew — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story?
a) Where people live is itself a history — settlements grow from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything before.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) Where people live is itself a history — settlements grow from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything before.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) Where people live is itself a history: Zimbabwe's settlements grew from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything that came before.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) Where people live is itself a history: Zimbabwe's settlements grew from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything that came before.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
