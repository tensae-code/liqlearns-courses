# Zimbabwe: How Settlements Grew — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Where people live is itself a history — settlements grow from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything before.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: where people live is itself a history: Zimbabwe's settlements grew from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything that came before.**
