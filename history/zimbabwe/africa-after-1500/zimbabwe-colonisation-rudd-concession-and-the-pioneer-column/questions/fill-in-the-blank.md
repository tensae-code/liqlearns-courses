# Zimbabwe: Colonisation — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Lobengula was misled into the ____ Concession — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals.
**Answer:** Rudd

**2.** In ____ the Pioneer Column marched in and raised the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury.
**Answer:** 1890
