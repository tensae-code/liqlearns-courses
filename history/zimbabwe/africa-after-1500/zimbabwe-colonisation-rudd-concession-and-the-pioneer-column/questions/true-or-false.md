# Zimbabwe: Colonisation — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Lobengula signs friendship with Moffat in 1888, is misled into the Rudd Concession — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals — and in 1890 the Pioneer Column marches in and raises the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Zimbabwe was colonised by a trick and a march: Lobengula, who had signed friendship with Moffat in 1888, was misled into the Rudd Concession — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals — and in 1890 the Pioneer Column marched in and raised the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury.**
