# Zimbabwe: Colonisation — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe was colonised by a trick and a march: Lobengula, who had signed friendship with Moffat in 1888, was misled into the Rudd Concession — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals — and in 1890 the Pioneer Column marched in and raised the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury.
