# Lesson plan — Zimbabwe: Colonisation — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column

**Course:** History — Zimbabwe · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Zimbabwe: Colonisation — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Strangers Arrive, Colonisation and the Two Chimurengas (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Zimbabwe was colonised by a trick and a march: Lobengula, who had signed friendship with Moffat in 1888, was misled into the Rudd Concession — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals — and in 1890 the Pioneer Column marched in and raised the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Strangers Arrive, Colonisation and the Two Chimurengas — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Zimbabwe's books themselves work, teaching the past as stories of named people and named places.
