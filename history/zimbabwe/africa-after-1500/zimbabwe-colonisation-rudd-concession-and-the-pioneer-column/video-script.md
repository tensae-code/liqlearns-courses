# Video script — Zimbabwe: Colonisation — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Zimbabwe was colonised by a trick and a march: Lobengula, who had signed friendship with Moffat in 1888, was misled into the Rudd Concession — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals — and in 1890 the Pioneer Column marched in and raised the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury.
