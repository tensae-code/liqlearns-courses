# Zimbabwe: Colonisation — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column

## Do it first

A king signs friendship with a missionary he trusts. Two years later men acting on a paper he was misled into signing march into his country and raise a foreign flag. This lesson tells the trick and the march, step by step.

## The story

Lobengula signs friendship with Moffat in 1888, is misled into the Rudd Concession — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals — and in 1890 the Pioneer Column marches in and raises the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: Zimbabwe was colonised by a trick and a march: Lobengula, who had signed friendship with Moffat in 1888, was misled into the Rudd Concession — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals — and in 1890 the Pioneer Column marched in and raised the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury.
