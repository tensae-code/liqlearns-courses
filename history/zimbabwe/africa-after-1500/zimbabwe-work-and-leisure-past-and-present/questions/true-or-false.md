# Zimbabwe: Work and Leisure, Past and Present — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The same needs, two worlds — the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren to show what changed and what did not.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: the same needs, two worlds: the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren show what changed in Zimbabwe — and what did not.**
