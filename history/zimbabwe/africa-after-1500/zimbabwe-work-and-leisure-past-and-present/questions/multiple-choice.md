# Zimbabwe: Work and Leisure, Past and Present — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story?
a) The same needs, two worlds — the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren to show what changed and what did not.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) The same needs, two worlds — the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren to show what changed and what did not.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) The same needs, two worlds: the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren show what changed in Zimbabwe — and what did not.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) The same needs, two worlds: the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren show what changed in Zimbabwe — and what did not.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
