# Zimbabwe: Work and Leisure, Past and Present

## Do it first

Ask a grandparent what work looked like at your age, and what play looked like. Then look at your own day. This lesson sets the two worlds side by side — and the gaps and the matches are both the history.

## The story

The same needs, two worlds — the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren to show what changed and what did not.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: the same needs, two worlds: the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren show what changed in Zimbabwe — and what did not.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the same needs, two worlds: the work and play of grandparents set against the work and play of their grandchildren show what changed in Zimbabwe — and what did not. Give one real example from the story, and name the lesson's key words: work and leisure, past and present, grandparents, grandchildren, change. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
