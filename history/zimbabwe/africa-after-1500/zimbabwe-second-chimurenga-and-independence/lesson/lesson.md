# Zimbabwe: The Second Chimurenga and Independence

## Do it first

Count thirteen years: from the first shots at Chinhoyi in 1966 to a table at Lancaster House in 1979, and a flag rising in 1980. This lesson tells the second rising — and what independence actually means when it arrives.

## Sub lesson 1 — Second Chimurenga/Umvukela

ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, open fire at Chinhoyi in 1966; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling end at Lancaster House in 1979 — and independence comes on 18 April 1980.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic 2, Unit 4 Independence and self-governance

1980 is re-read as a definition — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choose their leaders, control their resources and keep their own identity.
