# Video script — Zimbabwe: The Second Chimurenga and Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Second Chimurenga opened at Chinhoyi in 1966, when ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, took up the gun; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling ended at Lancaster House in 1979, and independence came on 18 April 1980 — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choosing their leaders, controlling their resources and keeping their own identity.

Part 1 — Second Chimurenga/Umvukela. ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, open fire at Chinhoyi in 1966; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling end at Lancaster House in 1979 — and independence comes on 18 April 1980.

Part 2 — Topic 2, Unit 4 Independence and self-governance. 1980 is re-read as a definition — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choose their leaders, control their resources and keep their own identity.

Remember it this way: the Second Chimurenga opened at Chinhoyi in 1966, when ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, took up the gun; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling ended at Lancaster House in 1979, and independence came on 18 April 1980 — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choosing their leaders, controlling their resources and keeping their own identity.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
