# Zimbabwe: The Second Chimurenga and Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, open fire at Chinhoyi in 1966; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling end at Lancaster House in 1979 — and independence comes on 18 April 1980. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: the Second Chimurenga opened at Chinhoyi in 1966, when ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, took up the gun; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling ended at Lancaster House in 1979, and independence came on 18 April 1980 — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choosing their leaders, controlling their resources and keeping their own identity.**
