# Zimbabwe: The Second Chimurenga and Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Second Chimurenga opened at Chinhoyi in 1966, when ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, took up the gun; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling ended at Lancaster House in 1979, and independence came on 18 April 1980 — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choosing their leaders, controlling their resources and keeping their own identity.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Topic 2, Unit 4 Independence and self-governance"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1980 is re-read as a definition — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choose their leaders, control their resources and keep their own identity.
