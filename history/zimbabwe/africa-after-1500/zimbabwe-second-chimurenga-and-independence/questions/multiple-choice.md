# Zimbabwe: The Second Chimurenga and Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, open fire at Chinhoyi in 1966; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling end at Lancaster House in 1979 — and independence comes on 18 April 1980.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) ZANU and ZAPU's armies, ZANLA and ZIPRA, open fire at Chinhoyi in 1966; thirteen years of war over land, taxes and unequal schooling end at Lancaster House in 1979 — and independence comes on 18 April 1980.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) 1980 is re-read as a definition — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choose their leaders, control their resources and keep their own identity.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) 1980 is re-read as a definition — a people who had no say under colonial rule now choose their leaders, control their resources and keep their own identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
