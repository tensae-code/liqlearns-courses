# Zimbabwe: Heroes from Nehanda to the Liberation War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Heroes shrink from the national acre to the district, then grow back through three faces — Mbuya Nehanda, the brave woman who fought for freedom, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara — and children draw them proud and brave for the classroom wall. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Zimbabwe's heroes run in one line from the district's own heroes up to the nation: Mbuya Nehanda, the spirit medium who mobilised the First Chimurenga and was executed for it with Sekuru Kaguvi, and Joshua Nkomo and Josiah Tongogara of the liberation war — heroes children draw proud and brave for the classroom wall.**
