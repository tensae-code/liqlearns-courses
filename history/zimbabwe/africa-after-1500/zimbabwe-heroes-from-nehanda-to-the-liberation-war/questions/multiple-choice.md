# Zimbabwe: Heroes from Nehanda to the Liberation War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Heroes shrink from the national acre to the district, then grow back through three faces — Mbuya Nehanda, the brave woman who fought for freedom, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara — and children draw them proud and brave for the classroom wall.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) Heroes shrink from the national acre to the district, then grow back through three faces — Mbuya Nehanda, the brave woman who fought for freedom, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara — and children draw them proud and brave for the classroom wall.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: b) The war is mobilised by the spirits — Mbuya Nehanda, Sekuru Kaguvi and Mukwati predict battles, protect fighters and urge the people on — and Nehanda and Kaguvi are executed for it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
