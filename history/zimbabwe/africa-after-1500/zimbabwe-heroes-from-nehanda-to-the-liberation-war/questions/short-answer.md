# Zimbabwe: Heroes from Nehanda to the Liberation War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's heroes run in one line from the district's own heroes up to the nation: Mbuya Nehanda, the spirit medium who mobilised the First Chimurenga and was executed for it with Sekuru Kaguvi, and Joshua Nkomo and Josiah Tongogara of the liberation war — heroes children draw proud and brave for the classroom wall.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Spirit mediums and heroes of the First Chimurenga"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The war is mobilised by the spirits — Mbuya Nehanda, Sekuru Kaguvi and Mukwati predict battles, protect fighters and urge the people on — and Nehanda and Kaguvi are executed for it.
