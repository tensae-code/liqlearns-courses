# Zimbabwe: Heroes from Nehanda to the Liberation War

## Do it first

Picture a classroom wall filling with faces: a district hero first, then a woman medium who sent a people to war, then the commanders of a liberation struggle. This lesson draws that line of heroes, from Nehanda to the liberation war.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 2, Unit 8 Local heroes and heroines

Heroes shrink from the national acre to the district, then grow back through three faces — Mbuya Nehanda, the brave woman who fought for freedom, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara — and children draw them proud and brave for the classroom wall.

## Sub lesson 2 — Spirit mediums and heroes of the First Chimurenga

The war is mobilised by the spirits — Mbuya Nehanda, Sekuru Kaguvi and Mukwati predict battles, protect fighters and urge the people on — and Nehanda and Kaguvi are executed for it.
