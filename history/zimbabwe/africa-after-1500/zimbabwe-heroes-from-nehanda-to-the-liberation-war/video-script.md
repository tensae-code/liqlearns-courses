# Video script — Zimbabwe: Heroes from Nehanda to the Liberation War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Zimbabwe's heroes run in one line from the district's own heroes up to the nation: Mbuya Nehanda, the spirit medium who mobilised the First Chimurenga and was executed for it with Sekuru Kaguvi, and Joshua Nkomo and Josiah Tongogara of the liberation war — heroes children draw proud and brave for the classroom wall.

Part 1 — Topic 2, Unit 8 Local heroes and heroines. Heroes shrink from the national acre to the district, then grow back through three faces — Mbuya Nehanda, the brave woman who fought for freedom, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara — and children draw them proud and brave for the classroom wall.

Part 2 — Spirit mediums and heroes of the First Chimurenga. The war is mobilised by the spirits — Mbuya Nehanda, Sekuru Kaguvi and Mukwati predict battles, protect fighters and urge the people on — and Nehanda and Kaguvi are executed for it.

Remember it this way: Zimbabwe's heroes run in one line from the district's own heroes up to the nation: Mbuya Nehanda, the spirit medium who mobilised the First Chimurenga and was executed for it with Sekuru Kaguvi, and Joshua Nkomo and Josiah Tongogara of the liberation war — heroes children draw proud and brave for the classroom wall.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
