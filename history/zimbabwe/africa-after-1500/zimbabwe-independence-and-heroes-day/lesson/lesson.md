# Zimbabwe: Independence and Heroes Day

## Do it first

Name one day your whole country stops for. Now ask: what story does that day retell? This lesson teaches Zimbabwe's calendar — the days on which the country tells its own story back to itself, every year.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 2, Unit 6 National events and celebrations

On 18 April the country marks the 1980 break from colonial rule, and on Heroes Day it names those who died for that freedom — Tongogara, Chitepo, Nkomo, Sally Mugabe, Ruth Chinamano — and lays them at the Heroes Acre.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic 2, Unit 6 National events / celebrations

The national calendar returns at Grade 3 with the district added — the country's milestones are now also the town's own days of remembrance.

## Sub lesson 3 — The five holidays

The year is re-founded in holidays — Independence Day, Heroes Day, Defence Forces Day, Unity Day (22 December 1987) and Workers Day — each one a chapter of the national story retold annually.
