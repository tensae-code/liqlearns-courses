# Zimbabwe: Independence and Heroes Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's year is founded on its story: Independence Day on 18 April marks the 1980 break from colonial rule, Heroes Day names those who died for that freedom and lays them at the National Heroes Acre, and the year's other holidays — Defence Forces Day, Unity Day on 22 December and Workers Day — each retell a chapter of the national story.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Topic 2, Unit 6 National events / celebrations"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The national calendar returns at Grade 3 with the district added — the country's milestones are now also the town's own days of remembrance.
