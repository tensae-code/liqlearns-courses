# Zimbabwe: Independence and Heroes Day — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** On 18 April the country marks the 1980 break from colonial rule, and on Heroes Day it names those who died for that freedom — Tongogara, Chitepo, Nkomo, Sally Mugabe, Ruth Chinamano — and lays them at the Heroes Acre. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Zimbabwe's year is founded on its story: Independence Day on 18 April marks the 1980 break from colonial rule, Heroes Day names those who died for that freedom and lays them at the National Heroes Acre, and the year's other holidays — Defence Forces Day, Unity Day on 22 December and Workers Day — each retell a chapter of the national story.**
