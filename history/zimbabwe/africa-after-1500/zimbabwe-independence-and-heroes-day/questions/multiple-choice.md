# Zimbabwe: Independence and Heroes Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) On 18 April the country marks the 1980 break from colonial rule, and on Heroes Day it names those who died for that freedom — Tongogara, Chitepo, Nkomo, Sally Mugabe, Ruth Chinamano — and lays them at the Heroes Acre.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) On 18 April the country marks the 1980 break from colonial rule, and on Heroes Day it names those who died for that freedom — Tongogara, Chitepo, Nkomo, Sally Mugabe, Ruth Chinamano — and lays them at the Heroes Acre.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) The national calendar returns at Grade 3 with the district added — the country's milestones are now also the town's own days of remembrance.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) The national calendar returns at Grade 3 with the district added — the country's milestones are now also the town's own days of remembrance.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
