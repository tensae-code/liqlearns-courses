# Zimbabwe: The First Chimurenga, 1896–97 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** In 1896–97 the Shona and Ndebele, old rivals, rose together against the British South Africa Company — driven by land and cattle taken, labour forced, taxes imposed, and drought and cattle disease on top — and were beaten by superior guns.
