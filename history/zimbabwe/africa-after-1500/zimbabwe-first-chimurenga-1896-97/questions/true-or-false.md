# Zimbabwe: The First Chimurenga, 1896–97 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Land and cattle taken, labour forced, taxes imposed, drought and cattle disease on top — Shona and Ndebele, old rivals, rise together in 1896–97 against the British South Africa Company, and are beaten by superior guns.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: in 1896–97 the Shona and Ndebele, old rivals, rose together against the British South Africa Company — driven by land and cattle taken, labour forced, taxes imposed, and drought and cattle disease on top — and were beaten by superior guns.**
