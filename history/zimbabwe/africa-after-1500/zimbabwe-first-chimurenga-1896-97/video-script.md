# Video script — Zimbabwe: The First Chimurenga, 1896–97

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. In 1896–97 the Shona and Ndebele, old rivals, rose together against the British South Africa Company — driven by land and cattle taken, labour forced, taxes imposed, and drought and cattle disease on top — and were beaten by superior guns.
