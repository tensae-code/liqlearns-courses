# Zimbabwe: The First Chimurenga, 1896–97

## Do it first

Two peoples who had fought each other put down their old quarrel and rise together. Ask why, and the answer is a pile of grievances: land, cattle, labour, taxes — and hunger on top. This lesson tells the first rising.

## The story

Land and cattle taken, labour forced, taxes imposed, drought and cattle disease on top — Shona and Ndebele, old rivals, rise together in 1896–97 against the British South Africa Company, and are beaten by superior guns.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: in 1896–97 the Shona and Ndebele, old rivals, rose together against the British South Africa Company — driven by land and cattle taken, labour forced, taxes imposed, and drought and cattle disease on top — and were beaten by superior guns.
