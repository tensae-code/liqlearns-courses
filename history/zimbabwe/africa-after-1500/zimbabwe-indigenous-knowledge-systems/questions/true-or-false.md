# Zimbabwe: Indigenous Knowledge Systems — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The knowledge that kept communities alive — healing, farming, weather and craft learned without books — is named as a system in its own right, worthy of the classroom. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Indigenous Knowledge Systems are a system in their own right: the knowledge that kept communities alive — healing, farming, weather and craft learned without books, and food kept by drying, smoking and storing — judged on its own merits beside today's methods.**
