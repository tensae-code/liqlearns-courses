# Zimbabwe: Indigenous Knowledge Systems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Indigenous Knowledge Systems are a system in their own right: the knowledge that kept communities alive — healing, farming, weather and craft learned without books, and food kept by drying, smoking and storing — judged on its own merits beside today's methods.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Topic 3, Unit 10 Indigenous and contemporary ways of preserving food"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The old food-keeping knowledge — drying, smoking, storing — is set beside today's methods and judged on its own merits, IKS as living technology.
