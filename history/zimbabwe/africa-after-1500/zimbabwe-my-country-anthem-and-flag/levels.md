# Zimbabwe: My Country, Anthem and Flag — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — My country, National anthem, National flag
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Children learn their country is Zimbabwe, stand at attention for its special song, and decode the flag — green for the land, gold for minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic 2, Units 7–9 National flag, National school pledge, National anthem
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The flag's five colours are sung as a rhyme — red for the blood of those who fought for freedom, white for peace — and the morning pledge turns that story into a daily promise of hard work, honesty and unity. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — National anthem, National flag, National colours
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The anthem and flag return, and the colours step out of the flag to be learned on their own, so the freedom story in red and the peace in white become the child's first national vocabulary. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Topic 2, Units 3–5 National anthem, National symbols, National school pledge
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Anthem, symbols and pledge are gathered as one kit of belonging, recited and sung until the nation's story is carried in the child's own voice. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Topic 2, Unit 7 National symbols
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The symbols are revisited as a set to be explained, not just recognised — each one carries a piece of the country's story. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Topic 2, Unit 10 Importance of the national school pledge
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The pledge is now studied for why it matters — a daily spoken promise that binds the schoolchild to the country's story. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 7 — Topic 2, Unit 10 National symbols and celebrations
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The settlement after the war is symbolic — flag, Zimbabwe bird and coat of arms on money and documents, so the state children fought for is seen every day. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
