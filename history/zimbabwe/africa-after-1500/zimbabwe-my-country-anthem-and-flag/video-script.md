# Video script — Zimbabwe: My Country, Anthem and Flag

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Zimbabwe's anthem, flag, national colours, school pledge and symbols are a kit of belonging: the flag's colours carry the country's story — green for the land, gold for its minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people, white for peace — and the pledge turns that story into a daily promise.

Part 1 — My country, National anthem, National flag. Children learn their country is Zimbabwe, stand at attention for its special song, and decode the flag — green for the land, gold for minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people.

Part 2 — Topic 2, Units 7–9 National flag, National school pledge, National anthem. The flag's five colours are sung as a rhyme — red for the blood of those who fought for freedom, white for peace — and the morning pledge turns that story into a daily promise of hard work, honesty and unity.

Part 3 — National anthem, National flag, National colours. The anthem and flag return, and the colours step out of the flag to be learned on their own, so the freedom story in red and the peace in white become the child's first national vocabulary.

Part 4 — Topic 2, Units 3–5 National anthem, National symbols, National school pledge. Anthem, symbols and pledge are gathered as one kit of belonging, recited and sung until the nation's story is carried in the child's own voice.

Part 5 — Topic 2, Unit 7 National symbols. The symbols are revisited as a set to be explained, not just recognised — each one carries a piece of the country's story.

Part 6 — Topic 2, Unit 10 Importance of the national school pledge. The pledge is now studied for why it matters — a daily spoken promise that binds the schoolchild to the country's story.

Part 7 — Topic 2, Unit 10 National symbols and celebrations. The settlement after the war is symbolic — flag, Zimbabwe bird and coat of arms on money and documents, so the state children fought for is seen every day.

Remember it this way: Zimbabwe's anthem, flag, national colours, school pledge and symbols are a kit of belonging: the flag's colours carry the country's story — green for the land, gold for its minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people, white for peace — and the pledge turns that story into a daily promise.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
