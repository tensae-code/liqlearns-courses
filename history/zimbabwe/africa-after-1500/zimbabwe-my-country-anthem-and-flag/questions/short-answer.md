# Zimbabwe: My Country, Anthem and Flag — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's anthem, flag, national colours, school pledge and symbols are a kit of belonging: the flag's colours carry the country's story — green for the land, gold for its minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people, white for peace — and the pledge turns that story into a daily promise.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Topic 2, Units 7–9 National flag, National school pledge, National anthem"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The flag's five colours are sung as a rhyme — red for the blood of those who fought for freedom, white for peace — and the morning pledge turns that story into a daily promise of hard work, honesty and unity.
