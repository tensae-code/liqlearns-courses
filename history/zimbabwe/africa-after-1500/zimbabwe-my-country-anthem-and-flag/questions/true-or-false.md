# Zimbabwe: My Country, Anthem and Flag — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Children learn their country is Zimbabwe, stand at attention for its special song, and decode the flag — green for the land, gold for minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Zimbabwe's anthem, flag, national colours, school pledge and symbols are a kit of belonging: the flag's colours carry the country's story — green for the land, gold for its minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people, white for peace — and the pledge turns that story into a daily promise.**
