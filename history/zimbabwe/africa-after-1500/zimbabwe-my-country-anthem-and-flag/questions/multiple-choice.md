# Zimbabwe: My Country, Anthem and Flag — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Children learn their country is Zimbabwe, stand at attention for its special song, and decode the flag — green for the land, gold for minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) Children learn their country is Zimbabwe, stand at attention for its special song, and decode the flag — green for the land, gold for minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) The flag's five colours are sung as a rhyme — red for the blood of those who fought for freedom, white for peace — and the morning pledge turns that story into a daily promise of hard work, honesty and unity.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) The flag's five colours are sung as a rhyme — red for the blood of those who fought for freedom, white for peace — and the morning pledge turns that story into a daily promise of hard work, honesty and unity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
