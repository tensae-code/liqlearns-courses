# Zimbabwe: My Country, Anthem and Flag

## Do it first

Stand at attention, as if the anthem were playing. Now picture the flag: green, gold, red, black, white. Each colour is a sentence of the country's story. This lesson teaches you to read them.

## Sub lesson 1 — My country, National anthem, National flag

Children learn their country is Zimbabwe, stand at attention for its special song, and decode the flag — green for the land, gold for minerals, red for the blood heroes lost to make the country free, black for its people.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic 2, Units 7–9 National flag, National school pledge, National anthem

The flag's five colours are sung as a rhyme — red for the blood of those who fought for freedom, white for peace — and the morning pledge turns that story into a daily promise of hard work, honesty and unity.

## Sub lesson 3 — National anthem, National flag, National colours

The anthem and flag return, and the colours step out of the flag to be learned on their own, so the freedom story in red and the peace in white become the child's first national vocabulary.

## Sub lesson 4 — Topic 2, Units 3–5 National anthem, National symbols, National school pledge

Anthem, symbols and pledge are gathered as one kit of belonging, recited and sung until the nation's story is carried in the child's own voice.

## Sub lesson 5 — Topic 2, Unit 7 National symbols

The symbols are revisited as a set to be explained, not just recognised — each one carries a piece of the country's story.

## Sub lesson 6 — Topic 2, Unit 10 Importance of the national school pledge

The pledge is now studied for why it matters — a daily spoken promise that binds the schoolchild to the country's story.

## Sub lesson 7 — Topic 2, Unit 10 National symbols and celebrations

The settlement after the war is symbolic — flag, Zimbabwe bird and coat of arms on money and documents, so the state children fought for is seen every day.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example from the story, and name the lesson's key words: national anthem, national flag, national colours, school pledge, national symbols, Zimbabwe bird, coat of arms. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
