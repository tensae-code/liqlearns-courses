# Lesson plan — Zimbabwe: Land Reform and the Return of the Land

**Course:** History — Zimbabwe · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Zimbabwe: Land Reform and the Return of the Land
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Independent Zimbabwe: Land, Towns and Living Heritage (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the land question outlived the war: land stayed in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller, Britain's funding promises failed, and in 2000 fast-track reform under Robert Mugabe redistributed the land to black Zimbabweans — taught in Zimbabwe's books as empowerment, food production and cultural restoration.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Independent Zimbabwe: Land, Towns and Living Heritage — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
