# Zimbabwe: Land Reform and the Return of the Land

## Do it first

A war is fought over land. The war ends — but the land has not moved. This lesson follows what happened next: a slow market scheme, broken funding promises, and then, in 2000, a fast-track reform that moved the land at last.

## The story

The war's central grievance outlives the war — land stays in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller, Britain's funding promises fail, and in 2000 fast-track reform under Robert Mugabe redistributes the land to black Zimbabweans.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: the land question outlived the war: land stayed in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller, Britain's funding promises failed, and in 2000 fast-track reform under Robert Mugabe redistributed the land to black Zimbabweans — taught in Zimbabwe's books as empowerment, food production and cultural restoration.
