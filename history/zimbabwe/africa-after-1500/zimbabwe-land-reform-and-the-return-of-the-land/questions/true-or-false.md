# Zimbabwe: Land Reform and the Return of the Land — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war's central grievance outlives the war — land stays in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller, Britain's funding promises fail, and in 2000 fast-track reform under Robert Mugabe redistributes the land to black Zimbabweans.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: the land question outlived the war: land stayed in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller, Britain's funding promises failed, and in 2000 fast-track reform under Robert Mugabe redistributed the land to black Zimbabweans — taught in Zimbabwe's books as empowerment, food production and cultural restoration.**
