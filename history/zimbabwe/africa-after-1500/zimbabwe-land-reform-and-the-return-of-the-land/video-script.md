# Video script — Zimbabwe: Land Reform and the Return of the Land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The land question outlived the war: land stayed in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller, Britain's funding promises failed, and in 2000 fast-track reform under Robert Mugabe redistributed the land to black Zimbabweans — taught in Zimbabwe's books as empowerment, food production and cultural restoration.
