# Video script — Zimbabwe: Culture, Language and Respect for Elders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Language is how culture is handed down: swapping a blunt demand for polite words to an elder is culture being passed on, one sentence at a time.

First: A child learns to swap a blunt demand for polite words to an elder, and language itself becomes the way respect — and culture — is handed down.

Remember it this way: language is how culture is handed down: swapping a blunt demand for polite words to an elder is culture being passed on, one sentence at a time.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
