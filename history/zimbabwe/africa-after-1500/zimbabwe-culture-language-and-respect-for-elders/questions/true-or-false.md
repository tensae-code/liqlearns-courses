# Zimbabwe: Culture, Language and Respect for Elders — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A child learns to swap a blunt demand for polite words to an elder, and language itself becomes the way respect — and culture — is handed down.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: language is how culture is handed down: swapping a blunt demand for polite words to an elder is culture being passed on, one sentence at a time.**
