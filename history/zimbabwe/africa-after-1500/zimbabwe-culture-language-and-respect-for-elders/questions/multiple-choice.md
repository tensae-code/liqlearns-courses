# Zimbabwe: Culture, Language and Respect for Elders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story?
a) A child learns to swap a blunt demand for polite words to an elder, and language itself becomes the way respect — and culture — is handed down.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) A child learns to swap a blunt demand for polite words to an elder, and language itself becomes the way respect — and culture — is handed down.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) Language is how culture is handed down: swapping a blunt demand for polite words to an elder is culture being passed on, one sentence at a time.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) Language is how culture is handed down: swapping a blunt demand for polite words to an elder is culture being passed on, one sentence at a time.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
