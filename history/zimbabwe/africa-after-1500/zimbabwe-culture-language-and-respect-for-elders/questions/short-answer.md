# Zimbabwe: Culture, Language and Respect for Elders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Language is how culture is handed down: swapping a blunt demand for polite words to an elder is culture being passed on, one sentence at a time.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Culture in relation to language"?
**Answer:** A child learns to swap a blunt demand for polite words to an elder, and language itself becomes the way respect — and culture — is handed down.
