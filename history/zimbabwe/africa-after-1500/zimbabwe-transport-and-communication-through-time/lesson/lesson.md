# Zimbabwe: Transport and Communication Through Time

## Do it first

A message once travelled at a runner's pace, and goods on a carrier's back. Now both fly. This lesson travels the road between — footpaths and runners first, then rail, road and air.

## The story

From footpaths and runners to rail, road and air — how Zimbabwe learned to move goods and messages, told as a story of change over time.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: Zimbabwe learned to move goods and messages by a long road of change: from footpaths and runners to rail, road and air.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Zimbabwe learned to move goods and messages by a long road of change: from footpaths and runners to rail, road and air. Give one real example from the story, and name the lesson's key words: transport, communication, footpaths, runners, rail, road, air, development. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
