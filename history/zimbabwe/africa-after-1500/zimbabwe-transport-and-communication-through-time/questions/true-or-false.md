# Zimbabwe: Transport and Communication Through Time — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From footpaths and runners to rail, road and air — how Zimbabwe learned to move goods and messages, told as a story of change over time.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Zimbabwe learned to move goods and messages by a long road of change: from footpaths and runners to rail, road and air.**
