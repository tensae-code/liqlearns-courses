# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A small child learns to say her own name, age and totem, names mother, father, siblings and the guardian who keeps her safe, and finds her story starts at home.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) A small child learns to say her own name, age and totem, names mother, father, siblings and the guardian who keeps her safe, and finds her story starts at home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) The child's world widens in rings — family first, then friends, then school — and each ring has people with roles and shared rules that hold it together.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) The child's world widens in rings — family first, then friends, then school — and each ring has people with roles and shared rules that hold it together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
