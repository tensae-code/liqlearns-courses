# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A small child learns to say her own name, age and totem, names mother, father, siblings and the guardian who keeps her safe, and finds her story starts at home. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: history begins at home: a child's own name, family and locality are the first history there is, and the family tree, the local heritage site and the named helpers of the district are the tools that turn that home story into history.**
