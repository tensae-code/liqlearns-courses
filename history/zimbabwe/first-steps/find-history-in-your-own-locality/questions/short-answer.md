# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** History begins at home: a child's own name, family and locality are the first history there is, and the family tree, the local heritage site and the named helpers of the district are the tools that turn that home story into history.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Myself and my family, my friends, my school, roles of family members, family norms and values"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The child's world widens in rings — family first, then friends, then school — and each ring has people with roles and shared rules that hold it together.
