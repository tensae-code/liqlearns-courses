# Find History in Your Own Locality — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Myself, my family, siblings and guardians
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A small child learns to say her own name, age and totem, names mother, father, siblings and the guardian who keeps her safe, and finds her story starts at home. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myself and my family, my friends, my school, roles of family members, family norms and values
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The child's world widens in rings — family first, then friends, then school — and each ring has people with roles and shared rules that hold it together. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Myself, my family, my extended family, my friends, my school, my teacher
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The family story grows a ring — grandparents, aunts and uncles join parents and siblings — and the child learns the wider kin who carry the family's memory. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Relatives, family norms and values; Relationship between the family and the community
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The family is set inside the community that surrounds it, and the child learns the two hold each other up through shared norms and values. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 5 — Topic 1, Units 1–2 Types of families; Family tree
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A child draws her family as a tree and discovers the drawing is a tool — it shows relationships, finds her origins, and captures stories and traditions before they are lost. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 6 — Research your local heritage site
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The class project sends each child to ask parents and teachers for the heritage site nearest home, find why it matters, and present it — history begins with the place you can walk to. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The family gathers and its gatherings become the place where the child hears who the family has been and what it hopes to become. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
