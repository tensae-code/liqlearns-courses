# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Before the modern courthouse, the dare — the indigenous court — held the community together, hearing disputes and settling them under its own rules.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
