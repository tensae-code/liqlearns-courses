# Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Before the modern courthouse, the dare — the indigenous court — held the community together, hearing disputes and settling them under its own rules. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: kingdoms hold together — and fall — in ways that can be compared: kings advised by chiefs and spirit mediums, classes of royals and commoners, the indigenous court (the dare) hearing disputes, and declines that come by drought of resources or by outside interference.**
