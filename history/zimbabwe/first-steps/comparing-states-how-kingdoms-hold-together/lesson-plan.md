# Lesson plan — Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together

**Course:** History — Zimbabwe · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Comparing States: How Kingdoms Hold Together
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Zimbabwe Long Ago: Early People and the Pre-colonial States (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** kingdoms hold together — and fall — in ways that can be compared: kings advised by chiefs and spirit mediums, classes of royals and commoners, the indigenous court (the dare) hearing disputes, and declines that come by drought of resources or by outside interference.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Zimbabwe Long Ago: Early People and the Pre-colonial States — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Topic 2, Unit 9 The indigenous court system** — Before the modern courthouse, the dare — the indigenous court — held the community together, hearing disputes and settling them under its own rules.
2. **Comparing the states** — The class lays the kingdoms side by side — kings advised by chiefs and spirit mediums like Chaminuka and Nehanda, classes of royals and commoners, and declines by drought of resources or by Portuguese interference.
3. **Topic 2, Unit 5 Systems of governance** — Ways of ruling are compared — the indigenous systems Zimbabwe had and the systems that came later — so learners can ask what holds a state together.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
