# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Children handle baskets, clay pots and wooden plates and learn that special things we can touch and keep carry the family's past into the present. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: heritage is what we get from the past that matters today, in two kinds — tangible heritage, the things we can touch and keep, and intangible heritage, the things we do and say — and both kinds survive only when each generation practises, guards and deliberately preserves them.**
