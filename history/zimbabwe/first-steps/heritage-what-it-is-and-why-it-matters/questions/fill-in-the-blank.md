# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Heritage you can touch and keep — baskets, clay pots, monuments — is called ____ heritage. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** tangible

**2.** Stories, songs, dances and respectful greetings are ____ heritage: heritage you cannot touch but can do and say. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** intangible
