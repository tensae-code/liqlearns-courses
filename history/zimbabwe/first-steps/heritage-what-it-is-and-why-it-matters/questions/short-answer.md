# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Heritage is what we get from the past that matters today, in two kinds — tangible heritage, the things we can touch and keep, and intangible heritage, the things we do and say — and both kinds survive only when each generation practises, guards and deliberately preserves them.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Intangible heritage"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Children practise kneeling and clapping hands to greet an elder and learn that stories, songs and respectful greetings are heritage you cannot touch but can do and say.
