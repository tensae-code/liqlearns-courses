# Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial States — Mutapa, Rozvi and Ndebele — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nyatsimba Mutota founds Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese; Changamire Dombo's Rozvi hold power by the spear; Mzilikazi builds the Ndebele state in ranks — three answers to how a kingdom is made.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: before the colony, Zimbabwe's land held kingdoms made three different ways: Nyatsimba Mutota founded Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese, Changamire Dombo's Rozvi held power by the spear, and Mzilikazi built the Ndebele state in ranks.**
