# Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial States — Mutapa, Rozvi and Ndebele — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Before the colony, Zimbabwe's land held kingdoms made three different ways: Nyatsimba Mutota founded Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese, Changamire Dombo's Rozvi held power by the spear, and Mzilikazi built the Ndebele state in ranks.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Mutapa, Rozvi, Ndebele"?
**Answer:** Nyatsimba Mutota founds Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese; Changamire Dombo's Rozvi hold power by the spear; Mzilikazi builds the Ndebele state in ranks — three answers to how a kingdom is made.
