# Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial States — Mutapa, Rozvi and Ndebele — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Nyatsimba ____ founded Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese.
**Answer:** Mutota

**2.** ____ built the Ndebele state in ranks, after Changamire Dombo's Rozvi held power by the spear.
**Answer:** Mzilikazi
