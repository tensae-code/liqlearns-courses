# Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial States — Mutapa, Rozvi and Ndebele

## Do it first

Three founders, three answers to the same question — how is a kingdom made? One builds on gold, one on the spear, one on ranks of regiments. This lesson lines the three up side by side.

## The story

Nyatsimba Mutota founds Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese; Changamire Dombo's Rozvi hold power by the spear; Mzilikazi builds the Ndebele state in ranks — three answers to how a kingdom is made.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: before the colony, Zimbabwe's land held kingdoms made three different ways: Nyatsimba Mutota founded Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese, Changamire Dombo's Rozvi held power by the spear, and Mzilikazi built the Ndebele state in ranks.
