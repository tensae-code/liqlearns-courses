# Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial States — Mutapa, Rozvi and Ndebele — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: before the colony, Zimbabwe's land held kingdoms made three different ways: Nyatsimba Mutota founded Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese, Changamire Dombo's Rozvi held power by the spear, and Mzilikazi built the Ndebele state in ranks — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
