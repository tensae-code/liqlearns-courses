# Video script — Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial States — Mutapa, Rozvi and Ndebele

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. Before the colony, Zimbabwe's land held kingdoms made three different ways: Nyatsimba Mutota founded Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese, Changamire Dombo's Rozvi held power by the spear, and Mzilikazi built the Ndebele state in ranks.
