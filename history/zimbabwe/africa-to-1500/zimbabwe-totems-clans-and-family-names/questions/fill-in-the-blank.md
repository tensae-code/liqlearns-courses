# Zimbabwe: Totems, Clans and Family Names — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A family's ____ and clan name tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where the family comes from.
**Answer:** totem

**2.** The totem a child first meets as a small child becomes, in this lesson, a ____ that organises a whole people.
**Answer:** system
