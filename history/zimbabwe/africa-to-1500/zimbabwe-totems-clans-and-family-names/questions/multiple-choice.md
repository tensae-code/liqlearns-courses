# Zimbabwe: Totems, Clans and Family Names — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story?
a) The totem a child was teased for not knowing at ECD becomes a system — clan names that tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) The totem a child was teased for not knowing at ECD becomes a system — clan names that tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) The totem system is a clan system carried in names: a family's totem and clan name tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) The totem system is a clan system carried in names: a family's totem and clan name tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
