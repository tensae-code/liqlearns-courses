# Zimbabwe: Totems, Clans and Family Names — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The totem a child was teased for not knowing at ECD becomes a system — clan names that tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: the totem system is a clan system carried in names: a family's totem and clan name tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.**
