# Zimbabwe: Totems, Clans and Family Names — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The totem system is a clan system carried in names: a family's totem and clan name tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Topic 1, Units 3–4 Family names and totems; Purpose of the totem system"?
