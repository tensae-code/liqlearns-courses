# Video script — Zimbabwe: Totems, Clans and Family Names

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The totem system is a clan system carried in names: a family's totem and clan name tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.

First: The totem a child was teased for not knowing at ECD becomes a system — clan names that tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.

Remember it this way: the totem system is a clan system carried in names: a family's totem and clan name tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
