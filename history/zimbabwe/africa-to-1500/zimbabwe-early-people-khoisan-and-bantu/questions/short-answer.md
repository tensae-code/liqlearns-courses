# Zimbabwe: Early People — Khoisan and Bantu — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** About 2,000 years ago Bantu newcomers walked into the Khoisan's land carrying iron-working and farming, settled in permanent villages with cattle, and became the peoples Zimbabwe is made of — Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others.
