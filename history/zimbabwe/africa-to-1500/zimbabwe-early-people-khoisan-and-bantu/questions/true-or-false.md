# Zimbabwe: Early People — Khoisan and Bantu — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** About 2,000 years ago Bantu newcomers walk into the Khoisan's land carrying iron-working and farming, settle in permanent villages with cattle, and become the Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: about 2,000 years ago Bantu newcomers walked into the Khoisan's land carrying iron-working and farming, settled in permanent villages with cattle, and became the peoples Zimbabwe is made of — Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others.**
