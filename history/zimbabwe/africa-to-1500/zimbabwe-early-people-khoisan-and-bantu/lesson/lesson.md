# Zimbabwe: Early People — Khoisan and Bantu

## Do it first

Picture two toolkits meeting: stone on one side, iron on the other; a moving camp on one side, a settled village with cattle on the other. About 2,000 years ago that meeting happened in Zimbabwe. This lesson tells it.

## The story

About 2,000 years ago Bantu newcomers walk into the Khoisan's land carrying iron-working and farming, settle in permanent villages with cattle, and become the Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: about 2,000 years ago Bantu newcomers walked into the Khoisan's land carrying iron-working and farming, settled in permanent villages with cattle, and became the peoples Zimbabwe is made of — Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others.
