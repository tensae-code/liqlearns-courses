# Lesson plan — Zimbabwe: Early People — Khoisan and Bantu

**Course:** History — Zimbabwe · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Zimbabwe: Early People — Khoisan and Bantu
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Zimbabwe Long Ago: Early People and the Pre-colonial States (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** about 2,000 years ago Bantu newcomers walked into the Khoisan's land carrying iron-working and farming, settled in permanent villages with cattle, and became the peoples Zimbabwe is made of — Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Zimbabwe Long Ago: Early People and the Pre-colonial States — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Zimbabwe's books themselves work, teaching the past as stories of named people and named places.
