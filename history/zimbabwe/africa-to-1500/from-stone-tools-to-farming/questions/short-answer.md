# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's first people were the Khoisan: hunter-gatherers who hunted with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, moved with food and water, wore animal skins, spoke in clicks, and painted their lives on rock.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Khoisan"?
**Answer:** The first Zimbabweans hunt with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, move with food and water, wear animal skins, speak in clicks, and paint their lives on rock.
