# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first Zimbabweans hunt with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, move with food and water, wear animal skins, speak in clicks, and paint their lives on rock.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Zimbabwe's first people were the Khoisan: hunter-gatherers who hunted with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, moved with food and water, wore animal skins, spoke in clicks, and painted their lives on rock.**
