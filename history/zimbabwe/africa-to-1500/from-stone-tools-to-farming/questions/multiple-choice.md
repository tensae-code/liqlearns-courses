# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story?
a) The first Zimbabweans hunt with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, move with food and water, wear animal skins, speak in clicks, and paint their lives on rock.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) The first Zimbabweans hunt with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, move with food and water, wear animal skins, speak in clicks, and paint their lives on rock.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) Zimbabwe's first people were the Khoisan: hunter-gatherers who hunted with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, moved with food and water, wore animal skins, spoke in clicks, and painted their lives on rock.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) Zimbabwe's first people were the Khoisan: hunter-gatherers who hunted with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, moved with food and water, wore animal skins, spoke in clicks, and painted their lives on rock.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
