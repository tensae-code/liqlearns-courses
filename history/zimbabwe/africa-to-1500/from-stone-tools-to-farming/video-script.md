# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Zimbabwe's first people were the Khoisan: hunter-gatherers who hunted with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, moved with food and water, wore animal skins, spoke in clicks, and painted their lives on rock.

First: The first Zimbabweans hunt with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, move with food and water, wear animal skins, speak in clicks, and paint their lives on rock.

Remember it this way: Zimbabwe's first people were the Khoisan: hunter-gatherers who hunted with bows, poisoned arrows and stone tools, moved with food and water, wore animal skins, spoke in clicks, and painted their lives on rock.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
