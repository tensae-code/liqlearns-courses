# Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial Family Life

## Do it first

Picture a family before any colony existed — no foreign school, no foreign court, its own rules for honouring a mother and a father. This lesson restores that family as a world that worked.

## The story

Before the colony, motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles, and the lesson restores the pre-colonial family as a world that worked by its own values.

That is the whole of this short lesson, and it is enough: before the colony, the Zimbabwean family was a world that worked by its own values: motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles at its centre.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: before the colony, the Zimbabwean family was a world that worked by its own values: motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles at its centre. Give one real example from the story, and name the lesson's key words: pre-colonial family, motherhood, fatherhood, cultural values. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
