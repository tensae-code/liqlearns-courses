# Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial Family Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story?
a) Before the colony, motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles, and the lesson restores the pre-colonial family as a world that worked by its own values.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) Before the colony, motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles, and the lesson restores the pre-colonial family as a world that worked by its own values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach?
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) Before the colony, the Zimbabwean family was a world that worked by its own values: motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles at its centre.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) Before the colony, the Zimbabwean family was a world that worked by its own values: motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles at its centre.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
