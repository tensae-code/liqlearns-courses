# Zimbabwe: Pre-colonial Family Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Before the colony, motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles, and the lesson restores the pre-colonial family as a world that worked by its own values.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: before the colony, the Zimbabwean family was a world that worked by its own values: motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles at its centre.**
