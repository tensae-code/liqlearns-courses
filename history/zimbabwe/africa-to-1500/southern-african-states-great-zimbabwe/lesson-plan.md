# Lesson plan — Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe

**Course:** History — Zimbabwe · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Zimbabwe Long Ago: Early People and the Pre-colonial States (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Great Zimbabwe is the stone city at the heart of Zimbabwe's story: capital of an 11th–15th-century kingdom that traded gold and ivory as far as Asia, built the Great Enclosure without cement, and gave the modern country its name and its bird — standing with Khami Ruins and Matobo Hills as the heritage children must learn to guard.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Zimbabwe Long Ago: Early People and the Pre-colonial States — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Great Zimbabwe** — The great stone ruins stand in the lesson as Zimbabwe's own man-made monument, named alongside Victoria Falls and Hwange as the inheritance children must learn to guard.
2. **Topic 6, Unit 22 Shelter and heritage sites** — Shelter grows up into stone — children draw and build models of Great Zimbabwe's walls and towers, Khami Ruins and Matobo Hills, and write the story of a visit where a guide tells how the ancient Shona lived there.
3. **Topic 2, Unit 6 Contribution of the early people to the Zimbabwean heritage** — The early people's gifts are still standing — Great Zimbabwe, Khami and Chesvingo in stone — and still spoken, in the Bantu word munhu, person, that Shona keeps today.
4. **Great Zimbabwe** — The stone city is capital of an 11th–15th-century kingdom that traded gold and ivory as far as Asia, built the Great Enclosure without cement, and gave the modern country its name and its bird.
5. **Topic 6, Unit 23 Shelter and heritage sites** — The heritage-sites unit returns at Grade 5 under Shelter, tying the stone walls children model to the houses people still build.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
