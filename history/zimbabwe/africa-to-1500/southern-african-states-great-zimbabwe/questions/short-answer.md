# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Great Zimbabwe is the stone city at the heart of Zimbabwe's story: capital of an 11th–15th-century kingdom that traded gold and ivory as far as Asia, built the Great Enclosure without cement, and gave the modern country its name and its bird — standing with Khami Ruins and Matobo Hills as the heritage children must learn to guard.

**2.** What happens in the part called "Topic 6, Unit 22 Shelter and heritage sites"? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Shelter grows up into stone — children draw and build models of Great Zimbabwe's walls and towers, Khami Ruins and Matobo Hills, and write the story of a visit where a guide tells how the ancient Shona lived there.
