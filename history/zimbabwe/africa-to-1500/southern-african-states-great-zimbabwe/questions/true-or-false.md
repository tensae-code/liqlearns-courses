# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The great stone ruins stand in the lesson as Zimbabwe's own man-made monument, named alongside Victoria Falls and Hwange as the inheritance children must learn to guard. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that nothing in its story ever changed, and that no part of it is handed down or remembered. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite: Great Zimbabwe is the stone city at the heart of Zimbabwe's story: capital of an 11th–15th-century kingdom that traded gold and ivory as far as Asia, built the Great Enclosure without cement, and gave the modern country its name and its bird — standing with Khami Ruins and Matobo Hills as the heritage children must learn to guard.**
