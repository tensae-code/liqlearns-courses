# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells this lesson's story? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The great stone ruins stand in the lesson as Zimbabwe's own man-made monument, named alongside Victoria Falls and Hwange as the inheritance children must learn to guard.  b) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) only foreign rulers have a past, and Zimbabwe's own people have none
**Answer: a) The great stone ruins stand in the lesson as Zimbabwe's own man-made monument, named alongside Victoria Falls and Hwange as the inheritance children must learn to guard.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What else does this lesson teach? (Sub lesson 2)
a) every age in Zimbabwe lived in exactly the same way, with nothing handed down  b) Shelter grows up into stone — children draw and build models of Great Zimbabwe's walls and towers, Khami Ruins and Matobo Hills, and write the story of a visit where a guide tells how the ancient Shona lived there.  c) nothing in Zimbabwe's story ever changed, and no one in it ever acted  d) the past can be known without any story, evidence or memory at all
**Answer: b) Shelter grows up into stone — children draw and build models of Great Zimbabwe's walls and towers, Khami Ruins and Matobo Hills, and write the story of a visit where a guide tells how the ancient Shona lived there.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
