# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why is Africa called humanity's cradle in this lesson?
a) because the first human bones are found in Africa  b) because the first books were written there  c) because the biggest cities stand there  d) because the Nile flows there
**Answer: a) because the first human bones are found in Africa** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the first human communities live on at the start?
a) wild fruit and roots gathered from nature  b) farmed grain and kept cattle  c) bread bought in markets  d) fish traded from the coast
**Answer: a) wild fruit and roots gathered from nature** — That is what this lesson teaches.
