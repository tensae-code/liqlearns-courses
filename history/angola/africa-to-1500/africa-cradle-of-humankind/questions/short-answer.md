# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does the book call the long making of humans from the hominids?
**Answer:** Humanisation — the slow evolution of the hominids toward Homo sapiens.

**2.** How did the first human communities feed themselves?
**Answer:** On what nature gave them directly — wild fruit and roots they gathered where they grew.
