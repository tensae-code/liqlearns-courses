# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The hominids evolved, step by step, toward Homo sapiens.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The oldest human bones were first found on another continent, and only later in Africa.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: the first human bones are found in Africa.
