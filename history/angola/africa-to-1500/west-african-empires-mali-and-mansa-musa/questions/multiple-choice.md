# West African Empires: Mali and Mansa Musa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which empire succeeded Ghana as the trading empire of the western Sudan?
a) Mali  b) Egypt  c) Kongo  d) Rome
**Answer: a) Mali** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who ruled Mali in its golden age?
a) Mansa Musa  b) the king of Ghana  c) a Portuguese captain  d) the Mwene Mutapa
**Answer: a) Mansa Musa** — That is what this lesson teaches.
