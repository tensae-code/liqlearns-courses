# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why could a farming kingdom on the Nile grow rich and organised?
**Answer:** The river's watered banks gave rich farming land in the middle of desert; farming surplus let the kingdom feed itself, build and organise.

**2.** Where exactly did Egypt sit?
**Answer:** On the banks of the Nile in the north-east of Africa, in the green band of watered land between the deserts.
