# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ancient Egypt was a farming kingdom that grew rich and organised on the Nile's banks.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ancient Egypt stood on another continent and only traded with Africa.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Egypt is an African civilisation, born on African ground.
