# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at a map of north-east Africa: desert, desert everywhere — and one green line running through it, a great river. Ask yourself: if you were a farmer thousands of years ago, where on that map would you settle? The answer is where this lesson's kingdom rose.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: On the banks of the Nile a farming kingdom grows rich and organised — Ancient Egypt, the first great civilisation this course meets, studied by where it sat and how its people lived. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
