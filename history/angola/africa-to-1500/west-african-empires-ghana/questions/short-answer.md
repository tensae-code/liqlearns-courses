# West African Empires: Ghana — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Which two goods crossed the Sahara in Ghana's trade, and from which directions?
**Answer:** Salt from the desert going south, and gold from the southern lands going north.

**2.** Why did Ghana not need to own the gold mines to grow rich?
**Answer:** It stood at the meeting place of the trade and taxed every load that crossed its ground — position, held and guarded, was enough.
