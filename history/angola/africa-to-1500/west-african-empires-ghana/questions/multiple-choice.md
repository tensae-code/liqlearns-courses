# West African Empires: Ghana — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What trade made Ghana powerful?
a) the gold-and-salt trade across the Sahara  b) the slave trade to America  c) the ivory trade to Asia  d) the cloth trade down the Nile
**Answer: a) the gold-and-salt trade across the Sahara** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did Ghana's king grow rich from the trade?
a) he taxed every load that crossed Ghana's ground  b) he owned all the gold mines himself  c) he sold his own salt only  d) he closed the desert routes
**Answer: a) he taxed every load that crossed Ghana's ground** — That is what this lesson teaches.
