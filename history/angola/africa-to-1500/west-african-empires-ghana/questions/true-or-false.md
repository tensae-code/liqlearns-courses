# West African Empires: Ghana — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ghana was the first of the great West African empires this course meets.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ghana's power came only from soldiers and not at all from trade.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Ghana's power was built on trade, and on taxing it.
