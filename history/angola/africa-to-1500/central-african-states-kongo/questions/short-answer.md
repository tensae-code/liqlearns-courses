# Central African States: Kongo — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does this course place Kongo beside Ghana and Mali?
**Answer:** To show that Africans built wide, organised states of their own — statehood in Africa did not arrive with the Europeans.

**2.** What made Kongo a real state and not a small chiefdom?
**Answer:** It had a king at its head, provinces under its rule, and an ordered government over a broad territory.
