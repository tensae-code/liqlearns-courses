# Central African States: Kongo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did the Kingdom of Kongo stand?
a) south of the forest, on Angola's own doorstep  b) on the banks of the Nile  c) across the Sahara in the desert  d) on an island in the Indian Ocean
**Answer: a) south of the forest, on Angola's own doorstep** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What kind of state was Kongo before the Europeans arrived?
a) a wide, organised state ruled by its own kings  b) a small village with no ruler  c) a European colony  d) a camp of wandering hunters
**Answer: a) a wide, organised state ruled by its own kings** — That is what this lesson teaches.
