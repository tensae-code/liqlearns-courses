# Central African States: Kongo — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kongo was the pre-colonial power standing closest to Angola itself.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Africans had no organised states of their own before Europeans arrived.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Kongo was a wide, organised state long before the Europeans reached its coasts.
