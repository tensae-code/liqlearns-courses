# Hands-on — Central African States: Kongo

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Every lesson so far has been far away — Egypt's river, the Sahara's caravans. Now come home. Ask: before any European ship arrived, who ruled the lands just north of Angola itself? A wide, organised kingdom stood there — and this lesson is its story.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: South of the forest, the Kingdom of Kongo rules a wide, organised state — the pre-colonial power standing on Angola's own doorstep. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
