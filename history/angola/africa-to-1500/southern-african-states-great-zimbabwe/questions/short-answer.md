# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does Mwene Mutapa's pattern compare with Ghana's?
**Answer:** Both powers rest on gold and trade: Ghana taxed loads crossing the desert, while Mwene Mutapa held the gold lands themselves and the road to the Indian Ocean coast.

**2.** Name the four powers this theme has now placed on the map, and where each stood.
**Answer:** Egypt on the Nile; Ghana and Mali on the Sahara routes of the western Sudan; Kongo south of the forest; Mwene Mutapa in the southern gold country.
