# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Mwene Mutapa state control?
a) the gold country and the trade toward the Indian Ocean coast  b) the Sahara salt pans  c) the Nile's farming band  d) the forest kingdoms of the north
**Answer: a) the gold country and the trade toward the Indian Ocean coast** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Toward which coast did Mwene Mutapa's trade run?
a) the Indian Ocean coast  b) the Atlantic coast  c) the Mediterranean coast  d) the Red Sea coast
**Answer: a) the Indian Ocean coast** — That is what this lesson teaches.
