# Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mwene Mutapa's power rested on controlling gold country and its trade, like Ghana's and Mali's power rested on trade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Mwene Mutapa was a state of the far north, on the Sahara's edge.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it stood in the south, facing the Indian Ocean.
