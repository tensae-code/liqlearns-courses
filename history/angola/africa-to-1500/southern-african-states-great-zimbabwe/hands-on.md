# Hands-on — Southern African States: Great Zimbabwe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow the gold one more time — not north across the desert now, but east, toward a different sea. In the southern gold country a state holds the mines and the routes to the coast. Ask: what does control of gold country give a state that faces the Indian Ocean?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In the south, the Mwene Mutapa state controls the gold country and the trade toward the Indian Ocean coast — the southern power of this course's great-kingdoms theme (filed under the Great Zimbabwe title). Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
