# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At the start, the first people lived in complete dependence on nature.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Hunting and fishing were things nature laid ready for people, needing no skill or working together.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: they take skill, tools, cooperation and courage.
