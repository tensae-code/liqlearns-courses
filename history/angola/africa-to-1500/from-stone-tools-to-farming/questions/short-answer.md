# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three ways of making a living this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Hunting, fishing and gathering — done together, as a community's work.

**2.** What did dependence on nature mean for the first people?
**Answer:** They ate only what the land happened to give — fruit and roots as they came — and moved on when a place gave no more.
