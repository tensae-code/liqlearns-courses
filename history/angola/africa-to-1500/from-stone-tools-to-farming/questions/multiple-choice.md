# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the turn this lesson teaches?
a) early humans begin to hunt, fish and gather together instead of merely taking what nature gives  b) the first cities are built  c) the first kings take power  d) people begin to write books
**Answer: a) early humans begin to hunt, fish and gather together instead of merely taking what nature gives** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is hunting and fishing a step beyond dependence on nature?
a) they take skill, tools and cooperation — people act on the world instead of only taking what the season offers  b) animals are easier to find than fruit  c) rivers never dry up  d) nature gives more to hunters
**Answer: a) they take skill, tools and cooperation — people act on the world instead of only taking what the season offers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
