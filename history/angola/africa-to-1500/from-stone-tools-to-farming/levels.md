# From Stone Tools to Farming — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Early humans take the first step beyond dependence on nature: instead of merely taking what nature gives, they begin to hunt, fish and gather together — the first step toward making a living. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
