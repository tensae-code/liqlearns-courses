# Lesson plan — From Stone Tools to Farming

**Course:** History — Angola · **Title:** Africa To 1500 · **Lesson:** From Stone Tools to Farming
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: What History Is and the First People (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Early humans take the first step beyond dependence on nature: instead of merely taking what nature gives, they begin to hunt, fish and gather together — the first step toward making a living.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: What History Is and the First People — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
