# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did the European powers meet to divide Africa on paper?
a) in Berlin, at the Berlin Conference  b) in Luanda  c) in Cairo  d) in Lisbon's harbour only
**Answer: a) in Berlin, at the Berlin Conference** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Africans do when the armies came to occupy the territories?
a) they resisted the takeover  b) they had all left the continent  c) they drew the lines themselves  d) they invited the armies in everywhere
**Answer: a) they resisted the takeover** — That is what this lesson teaches.
