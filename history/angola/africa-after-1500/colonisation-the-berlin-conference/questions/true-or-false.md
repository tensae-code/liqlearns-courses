# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A claim to African territory counted only when a power actually occupied the ground — effective occupation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** African rulers sat at the Berlin table and agreed the division of their own continent.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: the powers divided Africa among themselves, with no African ruler at the table.
