# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What came before the Berlin Conference, and what came after it?
**Answer:** Before: explorers mapped the interior. After: armies occupied the territories to make the paper claims real.

**2.** Why does the book name African resistance inside this theme?
**Answer:** Because the occupation was fought, not simply received — colonisation was done to resisting peoples, at the point of arms.
