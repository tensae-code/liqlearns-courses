# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the book name Africa's ruling classes inside the slave-trade theme?
**Answer:** Because some African rulers and traders fed the trade — capturing, selling and supplying captives — and the lesson names plainly what the book names.

**2.** What was the cost of the trade to African societies themselves?
**Answer:** They bled people: the young and strong were taken, communities were weakened, and wars and raids were fed by the demand.
