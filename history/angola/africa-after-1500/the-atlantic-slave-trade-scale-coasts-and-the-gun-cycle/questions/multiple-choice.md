# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what numbers were captives shipped across the Atlantic?
a) in vast numbers, generation after generation  b) a few hundred in total  c) only volunteers who chose to go  d) only soldiers captured in one war
**Answer: a) in vast numbers, generation after generation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What part did some of Africa's ruling classes play, as the book files it?
a) some rulers and traders fed the trade, supplying captives to the ships  b) they all united to stop the ships at once  c) they never heard of the trade  d) they bought the ships and freed everyone
**Answer: a) some rulers and traders fed the trade, supplying captives to the ships** — That is what this lesson teaches.
