# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The societies that supplied captives grew stronger as their people were shipped away.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: their societies bled people and were weakened.

**2.** The slave trade had European buyers and ships, and also African suppliers.
**Answer: True**
