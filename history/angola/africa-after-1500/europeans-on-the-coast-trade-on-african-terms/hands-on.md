# Hands-on — Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand on Angola's coast five hundred years ago and watch the horizon: a sail, then another — ships from a far continent, feeling their way down a coast they do not know. What are they looking for? And what will their coming open? This lesson stands at that shore.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Portuguese and other European ships work down Africa's coasts seeking sea routes and trade — opening the era that turns into the slave trade. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
