# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The European ships opened the era that turned into the slave trade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Europeans found Africa empty of kingdoms when their ships arrived.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: they came to organised states like Kongo, trading on their own ground.
