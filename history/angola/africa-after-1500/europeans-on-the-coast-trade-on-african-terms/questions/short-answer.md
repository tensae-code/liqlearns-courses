# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Africa first meet the European maritime expansion?
**Answer:** At the shore: the ships worked down the coasts, and the Europeans began as visitors dependent on the African powers whose lands they had reached.

**2.** What does this theme open, according to the book's filing?
**Answer:** The era that turns into the slave trade — the sea routes soon carried a traffic in human beings across the Atlantic.
