# Europeans on the Coast: Trade on African Terms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What were the Portuguese and other European ships seeking as they worked down Africa's coasts?
a) sea routes and trade  b) new farmland for their own peasants  c) a place to exile their prisoners  d) the source of the Nile only
**Answer: a) sea routes and trade** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which Europeans led this maritime expansion down Africa's coasts?
a) the Portuguese  b) the Romans  c) the Egyptians  d) the Malians
**Answer: a) the Portuguese** — That is what this lesson teaches.
