# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the "Year of Africa" in this lesson?
a) 1960 — the year state after new state was born and the colonial map broke  b) the year the Berlin Conference met  c) the year the first ships arrived  d) the year the slave trade began
**Answer: a) 1960 — the year state after new state was born and the colonial map broke** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which system was the last to fall?
a) South Africa's apartheid  b) the Kingdom of Kongo  c) the Mali Empire  d) the Berlin Conference
**Answer: a) South Africa's apartheid** — That is what this lesson teaches.
