# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** National liberation movements freed the colonies where independence was refused.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The colonial map drawn at Berlin still stands unchanged today.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: the Year of Africa, 1960, broke the colonial map as new states were born.
