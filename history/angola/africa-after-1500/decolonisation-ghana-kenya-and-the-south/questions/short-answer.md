# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What rose across Africa to drive the struggle for the independences?
**Answer:** Nationalism — the determination of peoples to rule themselves — organised into movements demanding and fighting for the end of colonial rule.

**2.** How does this lesson close the whole course's arc?
**Answer:** Africa — humanity's cradle, its kingdoms taxed and traded, its people shipped and colonised — stands at last in its own states again, with apartheid the last system to fall.
