# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Nationalism rises and new states are born: the first countries win independence, the "Year of Africa" (1960) breaks the colonial map, liberation movements free the remaining colonies — and South Africa's apartheid is the last system to fall. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
