# The Atlantic Slave Trade: How It Ended — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the book study abolition by causes AND consequences, not causes alone?
**Answer:** Because an ending is also a beginning: what followed from abolition — weakened societies and a new European advance — shaped everything after it.

**2.** What new thing did abolition open the door to?
**Answer:** A new European advance — Europe coming no longer only for captives but, soon, for the land itself.
