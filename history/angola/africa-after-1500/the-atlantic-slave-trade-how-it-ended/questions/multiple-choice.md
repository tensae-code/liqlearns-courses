# The Atlantic Slave Trade: How It Ended — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened to the Atlantic slave trade in this lesson?
a) it was abolished  b) it grew larger than ever  c) it moved to the Indian Ocean only  d) it was renamed and continued unchanged
**Answer: a) it was abolished** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did abolition leave behind in African societies, as the book files it?
a) societies weakened — and a door opened to a new European advance  b) societies richer than before the trade  c) a united African empire  d) the Europeans gone from Africa for good
**Answer: a) societies weakened — and a door opened to a new European advance** — That is what this lesson teaches.
