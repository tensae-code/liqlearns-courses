# Hands-on — Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The armies have taken the ground. Now the offices open: clerks, tax lists, labour recruiters. Ask the question this lesson answers: once a colony is held, how is it run — and who does the work, and who takes the profit?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Inside the colonies, administrations tax, conscript and exploit African labour — the colonial system implanted to make the colonies pay for their rulers. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
