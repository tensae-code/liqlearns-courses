# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What are the three working parts of the colonial system as the book files them?
**Answer:** Administration, control, and the exploitation of African labour.

**2.** What does exploitation mean in this lesson?
**Answer:** African work and African wealth flowing upward to the colonial power — the colonies made to pay for their rulers, under an administration built for control.
