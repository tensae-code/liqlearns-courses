# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was implanted behind the occupation, once the ground was held?
a) the colonial system — administration, control and exploitation of African labour  b) a parliament of the colonised  c) free trade with no taxes  d) the old kingdoms restored
**Answer: a) the colonial system — administration, control and exploitation of African labour** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did colonial administrations get African labour?
a) they taxed and conscripted — labour was extracted by compulsion  b) they paid the highest wages in the world  c) they asked for volunteers only  d) they brought all workers from Europe
**Answer: a) they taxed and conscripted — labour was extracted by compulsion** — That is what this lesson teaches.
