# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The colonies were made to pay for their rulers through African work and wealth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Colonial taxation left Africans free to live without working for the rulers.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: a tax payable in the rulers' money drove people to work for the rulers to earn it.
