# Video script — Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Inside the colonies, administrations tax, conscript and exploit African labour — the colonial system implanted to make the colonies pay for their rulers.

Occupation was only the door. Behind it, the Europeans **implanted the colonial system**: an **administration** set over the territory, ruling its people from above, built for **control** — and for gain. The book files this theme by its working parts: administration, control, and the **exploitation of African labour**.

The system's hands reached into ordinary life. Colonial administrations **taxed** Africans — and a tax that must be paid in the rulers' money drives people to work for the rulers to earn it. They **conscripted** labour — taking workers by compulsion for the roads, the farms, the mines and the ports the colony wanted built. Labour was not freely hired at a fair wage; it was extracted, the way the land itself had been taken.

That is the meaning of **exploitation** here: the colonies were made to **pay for their rulers** — African work and African wealth flowing upward to the colonial power, under an administration whose purpose was control first and the people's good not at all.

A system like this carries its own ending inside it: peoples taxed, conscripted and exploited do not forget who they were before it. The final lesson of the course tells how they rose — the struggle for the independences.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school, in Angola.
