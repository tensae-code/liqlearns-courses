# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two of the sciences that help History, and say what one of them does.
**Answer:** Any two of: geography (where things happened), anthropology (human beings and their ways of life), linguistics (languages and their old words), chronology (events in their right order in time), archaeology (digging up what people left in the ground).

**2.** Why is History called an investigation and not just a story?
**Answer:** Because the historian gathers evidence and tests it before telling what happened — the word itself comes from the Greek "histor", investigation.
