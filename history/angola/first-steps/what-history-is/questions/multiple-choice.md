# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the Greek word "histor", behind the word History, mean?
a) investigation — a searching-out  b) a list of kings  c) a very old story  d) tomorrow's news
**Answer: a) investigation — a searching-out** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which science helps History by digging up the things people left in the ground?
a) archaeology  b) chronology  c) linguistics  d) geography
**Answer: a) archaeology** — That is what this lesson teaches.
