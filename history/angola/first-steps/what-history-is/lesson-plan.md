# Lesson plan — What History Is

**Course:** History — Angola · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What History Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps: What History Is and the First People (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History — its name comes from the Greek word "histor", meaning investigation — is the science that studies human life through time, and it does not work alone: geography, anthropology, linguistics, chronology and archaeology help it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps: What History Is and the First People — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: History — its name comes from the Greek word "histor", meaning investigation — is the science that studies human life through time, and it does not work alone: geography, anthropology, linguistics, chronology and archaeology help it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town in Angola — or, for this book's lessons, from the Angola they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Angola and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
