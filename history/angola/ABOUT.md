# About the History — Angola course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Grade 6 História student book. The one exception is the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Angola inside the History folder
(one course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what
they prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a
recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school
grade the book comes from appears only in each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`, as provenance. History is taught as
stories: who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

The course as built has **three levels**, following the
Grade 6 book's own themes as filed:

1. **Level 1 — First Steps: What History Is and the First
   People.** What History is (the science of human life
   through time, and its helper sciences); Africa as
   humanity's cradle; and the first step beyond dependence
   on nature — hunting, fishing and gathering together.
2. **Level 2 — Ancient Africa and Its Great Kingdoms.**
   Egypt on the Nile; Ghana and Mali on the Sahara
   gold-and-salt routes; Kongo on Angola's own doorstep;
   Mwene Mutapa in the southern gold country.
3. **Level 3 — Africa After 1500: Slave Trade, Colonisation
   and Freedom.** The European maritime expansion; the
   Atlantic slave trade and its abolition; colonisation —
   Berlin, occupation and the implanted colonial system;
   and the struggle for the independences, closing with
   apartheid as the last system to fall.

All fourteen lessons stand on a **student book** and count
in the acceptance reckoning below.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the
book's own shape, walked in order):

- *One rising arc:* "Start with the question itself: what

  Egypt on the Nile, Ghana and Mali grown rich on gold and
