# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the past teach us to say instead of 'I and my'?
a) 'we and our'  b) 'me and mine'  c) 'you and yours'  d) nothing at all
**Answer: a) 'we and our'** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who does Mahesh write his letter to?
a) his friend Gyani  b) the king  c) his teacher only  d) a stranger
**Answer: a) his friend Gyani** — That is what this lesson teaches.
