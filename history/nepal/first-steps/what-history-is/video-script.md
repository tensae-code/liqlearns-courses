# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is the story of the past, and its first lesson is a way of living: think 'we and our' before 'I and my', remember the brave people before you, and serve your country.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Mahesh writes a letter to his friend Gyani, and the letter carries the whole point of looking backward.

Remember it this way: History is the story of the past, and its first lesson is a way of living: think 'we and our' before 'I and my', remember the brave people before you, and serve your country.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
