# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did the three pupil groups build medieval Nepal's story? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Each group researched one kingdom set in the library, wrote it large on chart paper, and peer-taught the others.

**2.** Name two oral sources of history. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Hero ballads, folk tales and proverbs (any two).
