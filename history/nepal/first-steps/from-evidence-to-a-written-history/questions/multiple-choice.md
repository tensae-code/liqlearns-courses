# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is one of the eight historical sources hunted in the community? (Sub lesson 1)
a) stone inscriptions  b) motor cars  c) telephones  d) plastic toys
**Answer: a) stone inscriptions** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What are vamshavali? (Sub lesson 4)
a) genealogies — written family and royal lines  b) coins  c) folk songs  d) temples
**Answer: a) genealogies — written family and royal lines** — That is what this lesson teaches.
