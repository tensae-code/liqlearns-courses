# From Evidence to a Written History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under the 2013 BS Act, a rest house over 100 years old can count as a protected historical object. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Photographs and posters cannot be used as evidence for history. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer: False** — the guide sources the modern story from photographs, pictures, posters, video clips and documents.
