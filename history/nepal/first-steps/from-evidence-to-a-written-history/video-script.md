# Video script — From Evidence to a Written History

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
We know the past from evidence — inscriptions, coins, old documents, stories, pictures and objects — and a written history is built by hunting that evidence, sorting it and keeping it safe.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. History is a record of past events we did not see — so how can we know it? By its objects. What counts as a historical object? The law itself answers: under the Ancient Monuments Preservation Act 2013 BS, manuscripts, genealogies, inscriptions, copperplates, coins, statues, temples, chaityas and rest houses more than a hundred years old are protected things. Three groups, three kingdom sets, one classroom wall. Nepal's past is sorted into its evidence. The modern story is sourced from things you can see.

Remember it this way: We know the past from evidence — inscriptions, coins, old documents, stories, pictures and objects — and a written history is built by hunting that evidence, sorting it and keeping it safe.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
