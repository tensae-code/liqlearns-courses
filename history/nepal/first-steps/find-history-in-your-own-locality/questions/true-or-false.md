# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Chhewang's grandparents in Humla had a road, a school and a health post. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — they had none of these; goods came dear by plane and mule.

**2.** Ahmed Hassan gave his own land for the school. (Sub lesson 10)
**Answer: True**
