# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Tenzing trade his old life for at Trishuli Bazaar? (Sub lesson 3)
a) market food and foreign-style clothes  b) a fort and a tunnel  c) a school and a library  d) coins and copperplates
**Answer: a) market food and foreign-style clothes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How old were Dankot's ruined fort and durbar? (Sub lesson 5)
a) over 1,000 years old  b) ten years old  c) one hundred years old  d) built last year
**Answer: a) over 1,000 years old** — That is what this lesson teaches.
