# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two neighbours whose work changed in Banjaria, Bara, and say what changed. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer:** Janaki's father stopped cutting forest trees and became an electrician; Mohan repairs cycles; Rajia moved from river fishing to pond fish-farming (any two).

**2.** What does Bibek Khadgi trace as his identity record? (Sub lesson 9)
**Answer:** His Kshatriya clan lineage of the Atri gotra, spreading from Doti across Nepal and into Assam, Darjeeling and Sikkim.
