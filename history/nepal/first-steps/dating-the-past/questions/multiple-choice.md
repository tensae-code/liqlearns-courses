# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Rita Chaudhary tell her class? (Sub lesson 1)
a) her birth day, date, month and year  b) only her name  c) only the year she was born  d) her friend's birthday
**Answer: a) her birth day, date, month and year** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Into what three periods is Nepal's history divided? (Sub lesson 3)
a) ancient, medieval and modern  b) old, older and oldest  c) kings, queens and princes  d) before, during and after school
**Answer: a) ancient, medieval and modern** — That is what this lesson teaches.
