# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ramesh was born at Kalaiya in Bara on 14 Magh 2054 BS. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** A timeline puts events in any order you like. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: False** — a timeline keeps events in date order, oldest to newest.
