# Video script — Dating the Past

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History starts with a date: your own birthday, written with its day, month and year, is the first dated record of the past — and timelines and periods are how all later dates are kept in order.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Meet Rita Chaudhary of Dang. Radha wants to know about her friend Ramesh, so she asks his mother the historian's questions: when was he born, and where? The answer comes back full and dated — Wednesday 14 Magh 2054 BS, at Kalaiya in Bara. Before the stories, the map of time itself. For the modern unit, the guide's method is the timeline itself.

Remember it this way: History starts with a date: your own birthday, written with its day, month and year, is the first dated record of the past — and timelines and periods are how all later dates are kept in order.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
