# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Old temples, manuscripts and copper plates belong to one family only. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — they are the nation's property, belonging to everyone.

**2.** Kaladhar was honoured for winning battles. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — he was honoured for service — the road, the irrigation canal and the rest places he built.
