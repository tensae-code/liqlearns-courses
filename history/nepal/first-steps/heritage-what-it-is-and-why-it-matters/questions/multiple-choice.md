# Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a national symbol of Nepal? (Sub lesson 1)
a) the danfe bird  b) the eagle  c) the lion  d) the lotus flag
**Answer: a) the danfe bird** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which place is named as World Heritage? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Lumbini  b) Dankot fort  c) Sinja valley  d) Kapilavastu hall
**Answer: a) Lumbini** — That is what this lesson teaches.
