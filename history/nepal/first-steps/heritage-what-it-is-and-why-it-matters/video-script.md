# Video script — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Heritage is what the people before us made and left — symbols, temples, old objects and ancestors' good work — and it belongs to everyone, so everyone must protect it.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Some things belong to every Nepali at once. Temples, chaityas, durbars, manuscripts and copper plates: the things our ancestors made are not theirs any more — they are the nation's property, held by all of us. In Lamidanda, Khotang, Sombahadur remembers his grandfather Kaladhar — not for battles, but for service.

Remember it this way: Heritage is what the people before us made and left — symbols, temples, old objects and ancestors' good work — and it belongs to everyone, so everyone must protect it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
