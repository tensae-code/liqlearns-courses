# About the History — Nepal course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — Nepal's own
Social Studies books and teacher guides. The one exception
is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Nepal inside the History folder (one
course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what
they prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a
recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school
grade each book comes from appears only here and in each
lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance. History is
taught as stories: who, where, what turned, and what came
of it. The course has **32 lessons in 4 levels**, stocking
**84 placements**:

1. **Level 1 — First Steps in History** (5 lessons): the
   child's own past — dating, family and locality, heritage,
   what history is, and its evidence.
2. **Level 2 — Ancient Nepal and the Old World** (10
   lessons): the name and the lake, the first dynasties,
   Mithila, the Shakyas, the Licchavis, the luminaries, and
   Greece, Rome and the Indus beside them.
3. **Level 3 — Medieval Nepal** (3 lessons): Sinja and the
   Khas kingdom, the Malla kingdoms, and the Baise, Chaubise
   and Sen kingdoms.
4. **Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World** (14
   lessons): unification to the federal republic, with the
   Renaissance, the voyages and the two World Wars.
