# The Panchayat Era and the 1990 People's Movement

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — The Panchayat Era and the 1990 People's Movement. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

In 2017 BS Mahendra strikes: parties are banned, and the partyless Panchayat system rules for thirty years. The lesson follows the long resistance — the referendum of 2036 BS, movements raised and repression answering them — all the way to the 2046 BS People's Movement, which restores multiparty democracy. Thirty years is a long lesson in how a people keeps a banned idea alive.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: King Mahendra's 2017 BS coup banned parties and set up the partyless Panchayat for thirty years — until the 2046 BS People's Movement won multiparty democracy back. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
