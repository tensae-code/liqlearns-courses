# Nepal's Poets, Print and Everest Luminaries

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Nepal's Poets, Print and Everest Luminaries. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

The last gallery is made of words, print and a mountain. Bhanubhakta retold the Ramayana in easy Nepali and became the pioneer poet; Motiram Bhatta published him and brought the printing press that spread his words. Falgunanda reformed his Kirat people's customs and ended animal sacrifice among them. And Pasang Lhamu Sherpa climbed where no Nepali woman had stood — the summit of Everest, in 2050 BS — and died on the mountain. A poem, a press, a reform and a summit: modern Nepal honouring its own.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Nepal's modern lights worked with words, presses and mountains: Bhanubhakta gave the Ramayana in easy Nepali, Motiram printed him and brought the press, Falgunanda reformed his people's customs, and Pasang Lhamu Sherpa was the first Nepali woman on Everest. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
