# Nepal's Poets, Print and Everest Luminaries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did Falgunanda do among the Kirat?
**Answer:** He reformed customs and ended animal sacrifice among them.

**2.** Why is Bhanubhakta called the pioneer poet?
**Answer:** He retold the Ramayana in easy Nepali that everyone could read.
