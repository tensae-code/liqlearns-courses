# Nepal's Poets, Print and Everest Luminaries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Motiram Bhatta published Bhanubhakta and brought the printing press.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pasang Lhamu climbed Everest and returned safely to write a book.
**Answer: False** — she reached the summit in 2050 BS and died on the mountain.
