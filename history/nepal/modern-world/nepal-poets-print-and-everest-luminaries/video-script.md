# Video script — Nepal's Poets, Print and Everest Luminaries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Nepal's modern lights worked with words, presses and mountains: Bhanubhakta gave the Ramayana in easy Nepali, Motiram printed him and brought the press, Falgunanda reformed his people's customs, and Pasang Lhamu Sherpa was the first Nepali woman on Everest.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. The last gallery is made of words, print and a mountain.

Remember it this way: Nepal's modern lights worked with words, presses and mountains: Bhanubhakta gave the Ramayana in easy Nepali, Motiram printed him and brought the press, Falgunanda reformed his people's customs, and Pasang Lhamu Sherpa was the first Nepali woman on Everest.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
