# Video script — The Second World War, 1939 to 1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Second World War rose out of the First War's settlement and the rise of Hitler, Mussolini and the Axis — and Nepal has its own place in it, in the Gurkha soldiers who fought with the Allies on far fronts.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. The second war grows out of the first war's settlement.

Remember it this way: The Second World War rose out of the First War's settlement and the rise of Hitler, Mussolini and the Axis — and Nepal has its own place in it, in the Gurkha soldiers who fought with the Allies on far fronts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
