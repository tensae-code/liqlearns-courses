# Lesson plan — The Second World War, 1939 to 1945

**Course:** History — Nepal · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Second World War, 1939 to 1945
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Second World War rose out of the First War's settlement and the rise of Hitler, Mussolini and the Axis — and Nepal has its own place in it, in the Gurkha soldiers who fought with the Allies on far fronts.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (recommended, never forced).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Nepal's books themselves work, telling the past as stories and tasks.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: The Second World War rose out of the First War's settlement and the rise of Hitler, Mussolini and the Axis — and Nepal has its own place in it, in the Gurkha soldiers who fought with the Allies on far fronts. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
