# The Second World War, 1939 to 1945

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — The Second World War, 1939 to 1945. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

The second war grows out of the first war's settlement. Hitler, Mussolini and the Axis rise; the lesson follows causes and course to results across the world. And Nepal stands inside this world story, not outside it: Nepali Gurkha soldiers fought with the Allies on far fronts. A small country's soldiers marched through a world war — that is Nepal's place in the lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Second World War rose out of the First War's settlement and the rise of Hitler, Mussolini and the Axis — and Nepal has its own place in it, in the Gurkha soldiers who fought with the Allies on far fronts. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: state the lesson's one thing aloud, in your own words.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the people, the place, the turn, and what came of it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
