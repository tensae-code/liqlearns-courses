# The Second World War, 1939 to 1945 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who fought with the Allies from Nepal?
a) Gurkha soldiers  b) the Malla kings  c) the Shakya assembly  d) Rana palace guards only
**Answer: a) Gurkha soldiers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** The Axis rose under which leaders?
a) Hitler and Mussolini  b) Janak and Mithi  c) Romulus and Homer  d) Dante and Erasmus
