# The Second World War, 1939 to 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Second World War grew out of the First War's settlement.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Nepal had no part in the Second World War story.
**Answer: False** — Nepali Gurkha soldiers fought with the Allies on far fronts.
