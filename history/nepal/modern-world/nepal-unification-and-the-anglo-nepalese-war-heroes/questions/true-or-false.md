# Nepal's Unification and the Anglo-Nepalese War Heroes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Women and children fought beside Bal Bhadra Kunwar at Nalapani. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Prithvi Narayan Shah advised taking bribes to keep the kingdom rich. (Sub lesson 6)
**Answer: False** — his Divya Upadesh teaches: take no bribes and give none.
