# Nepal's Unification and the Anglo-Nepalese War Heroes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did Rajendra Laxmi and Bahadur Shah each do for unification? (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Rajendra Laxmi ruled as regent and pushed the armies west; after her death Bahadur Shah returned as regent and carried unification to the Mahakali and beyond.

**2.** Tell the taking of Kathmandu in the campaign story. (Sub lesson 8)
**Answer:** After Nuwakot, Kirtipur and Makwanpur and a blockade, Kathmandu was seized during Indrajatra while Jayaprakash Malla feasted; Lalitpur and Bhaktapur followed.
