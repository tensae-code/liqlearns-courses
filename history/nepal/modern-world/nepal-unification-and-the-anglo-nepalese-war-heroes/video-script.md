# Video script — Nepal's Unification and the Anglo-Nepalese War Heroes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Prithvi Narayan Shah and the people with him welded the scattered kingdoms into one Nepal — and when the British war came, commanders like Balbhadra, Amar Singh and Bhakti Thapa defended it so bravely that even defeat brought honour.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. When the British attacked, Nepal's defenders became its first national heroes. In a dream, the unifier explains himself. A class research report tells unification as the people's work, not the king's alone. Prithvi Narayan died, and the work passed to two regents who quarrelled. Trade ambition turned to war. He was born at Gorkha on 27 Poush 1779 BS and crowned at twenty, with a vow: weld the scattered states into one Nepal. The gallery continues into the later ages. The unification campaign is told fort by fort. The war's heroes are honoured one by one. The modern age opens in 1825 BS with Prithvi Narayan Shah.

Remember it this way: Prithvi Narayan Shah and the people with him welded the scattered kingdoms into one Nepal — and when the British war came, commanders like Balbhadra, Amar Singh and Bhakti Thapa defended it so bravely that even defeat brought honour.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
