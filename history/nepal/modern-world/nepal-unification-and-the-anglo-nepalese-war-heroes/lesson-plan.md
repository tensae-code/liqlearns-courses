# Lesson plan — Nepal's Unification and the Anglo-Nepalese War Heroes

**Course:** History — Nepal · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Nepal's Unification and the Anglo-Nepalese War Heroes
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Prithvi Narayan Shah and the people with him welded the scattered kingdoms into one Nepal — and when the British war came, commanders like Balbhadra, Amar Singh and Bhakti Thapa defended it so bravely that even defeat brought honour.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Our Heroes and Heroines** — When the British attacked, Nepal's defenders became its first national heroes.
2. **Our National Heroes — Prithvi Narayan, Balabhadra and Amar Singh** — In a dream, the unifier explains himself.
3. **The Unification of Nepal — People's Participation** — A class research report tells unification as the people's work, not the king's alone.
4. **Rajendra Laxmi and Bahadur Shah in the Unification** — Prithvi Narayan died, and the work passed to two regents who quarrelled.
5. **The Nepal–British War** — Trade ambition turned to war.
6. **National Luminary Prithvi Narayan Shah** — He was born at Gorkha on 27 Poush 1779 BS and crowned at twenty, with a vow: weld the scattered states into one Nepal.
7. **Our National Luminaries (later figures)** — The gallery continues into the later ages.
8. **The Unification of Nepal — Causes and Effects** — The unification campaign is told fort by fort.
9. **The Contribution of the Heroes in the Nepal–British War** — The war's heroes are honoured one by one.
10. **The Modern Period** — The modern age opens in 1825 BS with Prithvi Narayan Shah.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: Prithvi Narayan Shah and the people with him welded the scattered kingdoms into one Nepal — and when the British war came, commanders like Balbhadra, Amar Singh and Bhakti Thapa defended it so bravely that even defeat brought honour. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
