# Lesson plan — The Renaissance: What It Was

**Course:** History — Nepal · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Renaissance: What It Was
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Renaissance was Europe's great rebirth of art, literature and science: Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael painted and built, and thinkers from Dante and Erasmus to Copernicus and Galileo changed how people saw the world.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (recommended, never forced).

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The Renaissance was Europe's great rebirth of art, literature and science: Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael painted and built, and thinkers from Dante and Erasmus to Copernicus and Galileo changed how people saw the world.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: The Renaissance was Europe's great rebirth of art, literature and science: Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael painted and built, and thinkers from Dante and Erasmus to Copernicus and Galileo changed how people saw the world. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
