# Video script — The Renaissance: What It Was

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Renaissance was Europe's great rebirth of art, literature and science: Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael painted and built, and thinkers from Dante and Erasmus to Copernicus and Galileo changed how people saw the world.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Pictures open the lesson: Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raphael, Dante, Erasmus.

Remember it this way: The Renaissance was Europe's great rebirth of art, literature and science: Leonardo, Michelangelo and Raphael painted and built, and thinkers from Dante and Erasmus to Copernicus and Galileo changed how people saw the world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
