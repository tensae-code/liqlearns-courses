# Lesson plan — Rana Rule and Its Reforms

**Course:** History — Nepal · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Rana Rule and Its Reforms
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** For 104 years the Rana prime ministers ruled as a family: Jung Bahadur seized power and made the post hereditary — a rule of few rights but a few real reforms, ended at last by education, martyrs, parties and revolution.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Rana Rule — Political, Social and Economic Condition** — In 1903 BS Jung Bahadur seized power and did something new: he made the prime ministership hereditary in his family, and Rana rule lasted 104 years.
2. **The Rise of Rana Rule and the Causes of Its End; Rana Rule's Economic and Social Effects** — Jang Bahadur seizes power in the Kot massacre and founds a hereditary prime-ministership that lasts 104 years.
3. **State Power after Unification** — After the unifier, power moves through Shah kings and strong ministers.
4. **The Modern Period — Economy, Law, Culture and Art Skill** — The modern centuries are surveyed whole to close the course.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: For 104 years the Rana prime ministers ruled as a family: Jung Bahadur seized power and made the post hereditary — a rule of few rights but a few real reforms, ended at last by education, martyrs, parties and revolution. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
