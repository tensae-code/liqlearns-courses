# Hands-on — Rana Rule and Its Reforms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud, in your own words: For 104 years the Rana prime ministers ruled as a family: Jung Bahadur seized power and made the post hereditary — a rule of few rights but a few real reforms, ended at last by education, martyrs, parties and revolution. Hold it in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the story's place, its people and the moment everything turned, and label each part. Say each part aloud as you draw it: who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see the lesson's teaching at work, note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
