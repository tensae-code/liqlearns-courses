# Video script — Rana Rule and Its Reforms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
For 104 years the Rana prime ministers ruled as a family: Jung Bahadur seized power and made the post hereditary — a rule of few rights but a few real reforms, ended at last by education, martyrs, parties and revolution.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. In 1903 BS Jung Bahadur seized power and did something new: he made the prime ministership hereditary in his family, and Rana rule lasted 104 years. Jang Bahadur seizes power in the Kot massacre and founds a hereditary prime-ministership that lasts 104 years. After the unifier, power moves through Shah kings and strong ministers. The modern centuries are surveyed whole to close the course.

Remember it this way: For 104 years the Rana prime ministers ruled as a family: Jung Bahadur seized power and made the post hereditary — a rule of few rights but a few real reforms, ended at last by education, martyrs, parties and revolution.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
