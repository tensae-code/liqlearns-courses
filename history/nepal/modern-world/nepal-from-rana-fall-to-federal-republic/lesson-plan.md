# Lesson plan — Nepal: From Rana Fall to Federal Republic

**Course:** History — Nepal · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Nepal: From Rana Fall to Federal Republic
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From 2007 BS Nepal walked one long road — Rana rule falls, democracy is born and lost and won again, a republic is declared in 2065 BS, and the 2072 BS constitution makes a federal republic — and daily life changed at every step.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Modern Nepal and the Modern World (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Political Events from 2007 BS to the Present** — Now the whole modern story is laid out as one dated chain.
2. **Social and Economic Achievements, 2007 BS to the Present** — Read the same timeline again, this time for daily life.
3. **The Democratic Republic and Its Achievements** — What did the democratic republic actually bring? Pupils chart the gains of the Loktantrik Ganatantra and hunt down its economic and social reform achievements, one by one.
4. **The Revolution of 2007 BS and the Delhi Compromise** — After 104 years of Rana rule, the Nepali Congress launches an armed revolution in 2006 BS.
5. **A Decade of the Democratic System (2007–2017 BS)** — The decade after the revolution is democracy's first try.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: From 2007 BS Nepal walked one long road — Rana rule falls, democracy is born and lost and won again, a republic is declared in 2065 BS, and the 2072 BS constitution makes a federal republic — and daily life changed at every step. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
