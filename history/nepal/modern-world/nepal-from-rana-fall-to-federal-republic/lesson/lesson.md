# Nepal: From Rana Fall to Federal Republic

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Nepal: From Rana Fall to Federal Republic. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Political Events from 2007 BS to the Present

Now the whole modern story is laid out as one dated chain. 2007 BS: the Ranas fall and democracy is born. 2017 BS: Mahendra's coup and the one-party Panchayat. 2046 BS: the people's movement. From 2052 BS: the Maoist insurgency. Then Constituent Assembly elections, the republic declared on 15 Jestha 2065 BS with Dr Ram Baran Yadav as first President, the constitution of 2072 BS, and the local elections of 2074 BS. A timeline is an argument: each date pulls the next one after it.
## Sub lesson 2 — Social and Economic Achievements, 2007 BS to the Present

Read the same timeline again, this time for daily life. From 2013 BS come the Nepal Rastra Bank and planned development; schools multiply and Tribhuvan University rises; roads spread, and radio, telephone, television and the internet arrive one by one. After 2046 BS, private banks and industry grow. Political change is not only flags and assemblies — it is what reaches the kitchen, the school and the road.
## Sub lesson 3 — The Democratic Republic and Its Achievements

What did the democratic republic actually bring? Pupils chart the gains of the Loktantrik Ganatantra and hunt down its economic and social reform achievements, one by one. Then the weighing: monarchy in one column, republic in the other, compared honestly. A republic is not praised by chanting its name; it is judged by what it delivers to ordinary life.
## Sub lesson 4 — The Revolution of 2007 BS and the Delhi Compromise

After 104 years of Rana rule, the Nepali Congress launches an armed revolution in 2006 BS. The four martyrs of 1997 BS are remembered as its forerunners. King Tribhuvan shelters in the Indian embassy and flies to Delhi — a king in flight from his own prime ministers. The Delhi Compromise of Falgun 2007 BS ends it: Rana rule is over, and a constituent assembly is promised. A century of family rule ends in a compromise signed in another country's capital.
## Sub lesson 5 — A Decade of the Democratic System (2007–2017 BS)

The decade after the revolution is democracy's first try. Parties form, the first elections come in 2015 BS, and B. P. Koirala leads an elected government. Then King Mahendra dismisses it, and the decade ends. The lesson weighs it honestly: what did democracy achieve in ten years — and why did it fall? A system can be right and still be fragile.
