# Video script — Nepal: From Rana Fall to Federal Republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From 2007 BS Nepal walked one long road — Rana rule falls, democracy is born and lost and won again, a republic is declared in 2065 BS, and the 2072 BS constitution makes a federal republic — and daily life changed at every step.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Now the whole modern story is laid out as one dated chain. Read the same timeline again, this time for daily life. What did the democratic republic actually bring? Pupils chart the gains of the Loktantrik Ganatantra and hunt down its economic and social reform achievements, one by one. After 104 years of Rana rule, the Nepali Congress launches an armed revolution in 2006 BS. The decade after the revolution is democracy's first try.

Remember it this way: From 2007 BS Nepal walked one long road — Rana rule falls, democracy is born and lost and won again, a republic is declared in 2065 BS, and the 2072 BS constitution makes a federal republic — and daily life changed at every step.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
