Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When was the republic declared? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 15 Jestha 2065 BS  b) 7 Falgun 2007 BS  c) 2036 BS  d) 2074 BS

**2.** What ended Rana rule in Falgun 2007 BS? (Sub lesson 4)
