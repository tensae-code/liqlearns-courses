# Video script — The First World War, 1914 to 1918

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Secret alliances, fierce nationalism, militarism and imperial rivalry pulled Europe into war in July 1914; four years of fighting ended with the Treaty of Versailles in 1919, a huge cost and a new map.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Europe pulls itself into war.

Remember it this way: Secret alliances, fierce nationalism, militarism and imperial rivalry pulled Europe into war in July 1914; four years of fighting ended with the Treaty of Versailles in 1919, a huge cost and a new map.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
