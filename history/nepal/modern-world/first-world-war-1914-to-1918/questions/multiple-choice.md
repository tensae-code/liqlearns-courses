# The First World War, 1914 to 1918 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what month and year did the war begin?
a) July 1914  b) July 1814  c) Falgun 2007 BS  d) September 1939
**Answer: a) July 1914** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What treaty ended the war in 1919?
a) the Treaty of Versailles  b) the Treaty of Sugauli  c) the Delhi Compromise  d) the Peace Accord
**Answer: a) the Treaty of Versailles** — That is what this lesson teaches.
