# Nepal's Democratic Leaders: Pushpa Lal and B. P. Koirala

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Nepal's Democratic Leaders: Pushpa Lal and B. P. Koirala. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

Two lives, two parties, one long struggle. Pushpa Lal Shrestha, expelled and exiled, founds the Nepal Communist Party in 2006 BS and organises against Rana and then Panchayat rule until his death in New Delhi in 2035 BS. B. P. Koirala, jailed by the Ranas, becomes Nepal's first elected prime minister in 2016 BS; King Mahendra jails him again; and in 2033 BS he returns from exile preaching national reconciliation. Opponents of each other, they were partners in one thing: neither would accept a Nepal without the people's voice.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Pushpa Lal founded the Communist Party from exile and organised all his life; B. P. Koirala became Nepal's first elected prime minister, was jailed by a king, and came home preaching national reconciliation. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
