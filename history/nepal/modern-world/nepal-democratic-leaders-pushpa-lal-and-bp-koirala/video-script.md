# Video script — Nepal's Democratic Leaders: Pushpa Lal and B. P. Koirala

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Pushpa Lal founded the Communist Party from exile and organised all his life; B. P. Koirala became Nepal's first elected prime minister, was jailed by a king, and came home preaching national reconciliation.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Two lives, two parties, one long struggle.

Remember it this way: Pushpa Lal founded the Communist Party from exile and organised all his life; B. P. Koirala became Nepal's first elected prime minister, was jailed by a king, and came home preaching national reconciliation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
