# Nepal's Democratic Leaders: Pushpa Lal and B. P. Koirala — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** B. P. Koirala returned from exile in 2033 BS preaching national reconciliation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pushpa Lal lived quietly and never organised against Rana rule.
**Answer: False** — he organised against Rana and Panchayat rule all his life, from exile.
