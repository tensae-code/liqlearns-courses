# Video script — Nepal's First Prime Minister and the Road to Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bhimsen Thapa, Nepal's first Prime Minister, held the country against British power; Ram Shah made Gorkha a centre of justice; and King Tribhuvan at last sided with the people so Rana rule could end.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Three more lives carry Nepal toward the modern age.

Remember it this way: Bhimsen Thapa, Nepal's first Prime Minister, held the country against British power; Ram Shah made Gorkha a centre of justice; and King Tribhuvan at last sided with the people so Rana rule could end.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
