# Nepal's First Prime Minister and the Road to Democracy

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Nepal's First Prime Minister and the Road to Democracy. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

Three more lives carry Nepal toward the modern age. Bhimsen Thapa, the first Prime Minister, dreamed of driving the British from Asia — and after defeat signed the Sugauli Treaty to save what could be saved. Ram Shah of Gorkha, long before, made his kingdom famous for justice, with standard measures every trader could trust. And King Tribhuvan, in the end, sided with his people — and on 7 Falgun 2007 BS the Rana regime ended. A strong minister, a just king, and a king who chose the people.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Bhimsen Thapa, Nepal's first Prime Minister, held the country against British power; Ram Shah made Gorkha a centre of justice; and King Tribhuvan at last sided with the people so Rana rule could end. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
