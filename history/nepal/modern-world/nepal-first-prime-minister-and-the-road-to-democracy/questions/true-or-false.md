# Nepal's First Prime Minister and the Road to Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ram Shah made Gorkha a centre of justice with standard measures.
**Answer: True**

**2.** King Tribhuvan opposed the people and saved Rana rule.
**Answer: False** — he sided with the people, and Rana rule ended.
