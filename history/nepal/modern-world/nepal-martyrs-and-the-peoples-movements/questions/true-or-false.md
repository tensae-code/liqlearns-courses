# Nepal's Martyrs and the People's Movements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Shahid Gate in Kathmandu honours the four martyrs. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** No martyrs fell after Rana rule ended. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — martyrs fell again under the Panchayat and in the Second People's Movement.
