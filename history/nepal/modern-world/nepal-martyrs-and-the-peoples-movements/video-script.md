# Video script — Nepal's Martyrs and the People's Movements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Again and again Nepalis gave their lives for the people's rights — the four martyrs under the Ranas, martyrs under the Panchayat, and those who fell in the Second People's Movement that won Loktantra.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. For 104 years the Rana family ruled Nepal, and opposing them could cost your life. On Magh 16, at a martyrs' programme, Shanti lays a garland for Sagar Singh, who was martyred in Lalitpur in 2046 BS. The martyrs are taught with pictures, charts and documentary film, so their faces are known and not only their names.

Remember it this way: Again and again Nepalis gave their lives for the people's rights — the four martyrs under the Ranas, martyrs under the Panchayat, and those who fell in the Second People's Movement that won Loktantra.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
