# People's Movement II, Civil War and the Republic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Comprehensive Peace Accord ended the Maoist war. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The republic was declared before the constituent assembly elections. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — the chain runs: assembly elections, then the declaration of the federal democratic republic.
