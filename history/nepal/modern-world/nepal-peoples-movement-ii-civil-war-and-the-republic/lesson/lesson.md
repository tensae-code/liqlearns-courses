# People's Movement II, Civil War and the Republic

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — People's Movement II, Civil War and the Republic. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Major Political Events from 2046 BS to 2063 BS

Multiparty democracy returns — and then the harder years begin. In 2052 BS the Maoist armed conflict opens. The palace massacre and the royal takeover follow. Then the country answers: the Second People's Movement of 2062/63 BS fills the streets, and the Comprehensive Peace Accord ends the war. The road to a republic was not a straight line; it was fought, lost and marched.
## Sub lesson 2 — The Political Event-Chain from 2063 BS to 2079 BS

The chain closes. Constituent assembly elections are held; the federal democratic republic is declared; the long constitution-writing drags and argues; and in 2072 BS the constitution stands. From monarchy to federal republic in one lesson-chain — the longest single walk in Nepal's modern story, ending at the present day.
