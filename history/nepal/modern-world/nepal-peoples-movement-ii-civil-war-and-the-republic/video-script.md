# Video script — People's Movement II, Civil War and the Republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Maoist war from 2052 BS, the palace massacre and the royal takeover drove Nepal to the Second People's Movement of 2062/63 BS; peace followed, and the road opened to a federal republic and the 2072 BS constitution.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Multiparty democracy returns — and then the harder years begin. The chain closes.

Remember it this way: The Maoist war from 2052 BS, the palace massacre and the royal takeover drove Nepal to the Second People's Movement of 2062/63 BS; peace followed, and the road opened to a federal republic and the 2072 BS constitution.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
