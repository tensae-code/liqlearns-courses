# The Anglo-Nepalese War and the Treaty of Sugauli — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Border quarrels over Butwal and Sheoraj helped push Nepal into war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Nepal was conquered and ruled by the Company after the war.
**Answer: False** — Nepal lost land but kept its independence.
