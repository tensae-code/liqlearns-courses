# Video script — The Anglo-Nepalese War and the Treaty of Sugauli

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Border quarrels pushed Nepal into war with the East India Company in 1814; the Treaty of Sugauli ended it in 1816 — Nepal lost a third of its land, but kept its independence.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Why did Nepal fight the East India Company in 1814? Border quarrels over Butwal and Sheoraj, and the Nawab's pressure, pushed the two powers into war.

Remember it this way: Border quarrels pushed Nepal into war with the East India Company in 1814; the Treaty of Sugauli ended it in 1816 — Nepal lost a third of its land, but kept its independence.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
