# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who is pictured as Rome's founder?
a) Romulus  b) Remus's teacher  c) Julius's son  d) a Greek sailor

**2.** Which of these is a Roman legacy?
a) law  b) pagoda art  c) Maithili painting  d) the gana assembly
