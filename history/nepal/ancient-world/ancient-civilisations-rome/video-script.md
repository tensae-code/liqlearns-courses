# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rome grew from Romulus's city into a great empire, and its law, building and roads shaped the world that came after it.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Rome begins with a pictured founder: Romulus, and the city that carries his name.

Remember it this way: Rome grew from Romulus's city into a great empire, and its law, building and roads shaped the world that came after it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
