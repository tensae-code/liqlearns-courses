# Medieval Nepal: Baise, Chaubise and Sen Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rudra Sen made Palpa a state in 1550 BS. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The small kingdoms grew stronger forever and never weakened. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — they rose, competed and weakened — the divided map unification later gathered.
