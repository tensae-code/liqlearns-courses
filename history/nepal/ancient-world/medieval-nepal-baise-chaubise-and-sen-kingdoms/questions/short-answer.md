# Medieval Nepal: Baise, Chaubise and Sen Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How do pupil groups learn this lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Groups research the Baise, Chaubise and Sen kingdoms in the school library and present their finds.

**2.** Into what did Mukunda Sen divide his kingdom? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** He split it among his sons, with branches such as Makwanpur, Chaudandi and Vijaypur.
