# Medieval Nepal: Baise, Chaubise and Sen Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did the Baise states stand? (Sub lesson 1)
a) west of the Kali Gandaki  b) in the Kathmandu Valley  c) on the Terai only  d) east of Vijaypur
**Answer: a) west of the Kali Gandaki** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which small kingdoms rose beyond the valley in the medieval period? (Sub lesson 2)
a) the Baise, Chaubise and Sen kingdoms  b) only the Gopals  c) only the Licchavis  d) the Rana courts
**Answer: a) the Baise, Chaubise and Sen kingdoms** — That is what this lesson teaches.
