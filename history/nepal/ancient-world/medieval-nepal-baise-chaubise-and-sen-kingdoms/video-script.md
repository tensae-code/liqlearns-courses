# Video script — Medieval Nepal: Baise, Chaubise and Sen Kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Beyond the valley, medieval Nepal was a map of small kingdoms — the Baise in the west, the Chaubise in the Gandak hills, and the Sen kingdoms from Palpa to the east — each rising as bigger kingdoms weakened.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Class groups take this lesson as researchers: to the school library, and back with presentations. Beyond the valley, the small kingdoms rise and fall.

Remember it this way: Beyond the valley, medieval Nepal was a map of small kingdoms — the Baise in the west, the Chaubise in the Gandak hills, and the Sen kingdoms from Palpa to the east — each rising as bigger kingdoms weakened.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
