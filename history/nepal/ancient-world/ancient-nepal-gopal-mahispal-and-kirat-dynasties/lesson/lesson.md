## Sub lesson 1 — Ancient Nepal

The valley was a lake once; when its water drained, the first rulers came. The Gopals, cow-herders from Mathura, ruled some 500 years, from Bhumi Gupta to Yaksha Gupta. The buffalo-herding Mahispals followed for about 150 years. Then came the Kirats, who ruled over a thousand years, from Yalambar at Gokarna, until the Lichchhavis defeated them. Three herding ages, one after another — that is ancient Nepal's first royal story.
## Sub lesson 2 — Ancient Nepal — Economic and Social Condition

Ancient Nepal is set in a table against today. First the dynasties — Mahispal, Gopal and Kirat, their first kings and their last. Then the world they lived in: farming and cattle, iron and copper work, temples raised and ancestors worshipped. Column by column, the class sets that world beside present-day economic life. The point is not that the past was poorer; it is that work, worship and rule have all changed their shapes.
