# Video script — Ancient Nepal: Gopal, Mahispal and Kirat Dynasties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ancient Nepal was ruled in turn by cow-herding Gopals, buffalo-herding Mahispals and then the Kirat kings for over a thousand years — a farming, cattle-keeping world the Licchavis finally defeated.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. The valley was a lake once; when its water drained, the first rulers came. Ancient Nepal is set in a table against today.

Remember it this way: Ancient Nepal was ruled in turn by cow-herding Gopals, buffalo-herding Mahispals and then the Kirat kings for over a thousand years — a farming, cattle-keeping world the Licchavis finally defeated.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
