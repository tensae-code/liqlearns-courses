# Ancient Nepal: Gopal, Mahispal and Kirat Dynasties — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Mahispals were buffalo-herders. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ancient society had no metal work at all. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — it worked iron and copper.
