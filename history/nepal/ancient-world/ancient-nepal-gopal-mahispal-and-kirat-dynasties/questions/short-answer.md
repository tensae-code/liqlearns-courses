# Ancient Nepal: Gopal, Mahispal and Kirat Dynasties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the first and last Gopal kings as the lesson gives them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Bhumi Gupta, the first, and Yaksha Gupta, the last.

**2.** Name two things ancient people did for a living. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Farming, cattle-keeping, iron and copper work (any two).
