# Video script — The Licchavi Age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Licchavis ruled the valley for centuries and left their story carved in stone: Manadeva's victories, Amshuvarma's clever balance between Tibet and India, temples, coins and fine art.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. For centuries the Licchavis rule the valley, and they leave their record in the most durable form there is: inscriptions.

Remember it this way: The Licchavis ruled the valley for centuries and left their story carved in stone: Manadeva's victories, Amshuvarma's clever balance between Tibet and India, temples, coins and fine art.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
