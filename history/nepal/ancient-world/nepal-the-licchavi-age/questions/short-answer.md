# The Licchavi Age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two things the Licchavi age is remembered for.
**Answer:** Manadeva's victories, Amshuvarma's statecraft, temples, coinage, and its art and administration (any two).

**2.** What palace did Amshuvarma raise?
**Answer:** The Kailashkut palace.
