# The Licchavi Age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How do we know the Licchavi story?
a) from their inscriptions carved in stone  b) from television  c) from the Ranas  d) from British letters only
**Answer: a) from their inscriptions carved in stone** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which Licchavi figure balanced Tibet and India?
a) Amshuvarma  b) Jang Bahadur  c) Yaksha Malla  d) Mithi
**Answer: a) Amshuvarma** — That is what this lesson teaches.
