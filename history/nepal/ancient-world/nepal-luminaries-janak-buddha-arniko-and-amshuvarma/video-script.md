# Video script — Nepal's Luminaries: Janak, Buddha, Arniko and Amshuvarma

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Nepal remembers its ancient great ones as lights: Janak the wise king, Buddha the teacher of truth and non-violence born at Lumbini, Arniko who carried pagoda art to China, Sankhadhar who freed the poor from debt, and Amshuvarma the reforming ruler.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Nepal's oldest gallery of great ones opens in Mithila: King Janak, who made his kingdom flourish, and his daughter Sita, who married Ram. He was born at Lumbini on Vaishakh Purnima in 563 BCE, son of Suddhodana and Mayadevi — a prince. In 1245 CE a boy sculptor named Balbahu is born in Nepal — the boy the world will call Arniko. The gallery of ancient and medieval luminaries opens in Mithila with King Janak and his daughter Sita.

Remember it this way: Nepal remembers its ancient great ones as lights: Janak the wise king, Buddha the teacher of truth and non-violence born at Lumbini, Arniko who carried pagoda art to China, Sankhadhar who freed the poor from debt, and Amshuvarma the reforming ruler.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
