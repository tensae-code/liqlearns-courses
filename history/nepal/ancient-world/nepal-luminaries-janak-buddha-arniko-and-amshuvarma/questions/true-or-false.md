# Nepal's Luminaries: Janak, Buddha, Arniko and Amshuvarma — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Buddha was born at Lumbini in 563 BCE. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Arniko was a king of Gorkha. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: False** — he was an artist of Patan who led craftsmen to Tibet and China.
