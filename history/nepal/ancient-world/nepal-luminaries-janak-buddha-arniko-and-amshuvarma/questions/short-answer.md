# Nepal's Luminaries: Janak, Buddha, Arniko and Amshuvarma — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What four sights turned the prince toward enlightenment? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** An old man, a sick man, a corpse and an ascetic.

**2.** Why is Buddha called the Light of Asia? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Because his teaching of truth and non-violence, born at Lumbini, spread across Asia and the world.
