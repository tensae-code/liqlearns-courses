# The Shakya Clan and Kapilavastu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What kind of rule did the Shakyas have?
a) a gana — rule by assembly  b) one hereditary emperor  c) Rana rule  d) British rule
**Answer: a) a gana — rule by assembly** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who was born from the Shakya clan?
a) Siddhartha Gautam, the Buddha  b) Arniko  c) Romulus  d) Homer
**Answer: a) Siddhartha Gautam, the Buddha** — That is what this lesson teaches.
