# Video script — The Shakya Clan and Kapilavastu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Shakya clan of Kapilavastu ruled itself through an assembly — a republican gana on the Terai plain — and from this clan Siddhartha Gautam, the Buddha, was born.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. On the Terai plain stands Kapilavastu, home of the Shakya clan — the clan of Siddhartha Gautam, the Buddha.

Remember it this way: The Shakya clan of Kapilavastu ruled itself through an assembly — a republican gana on the Terai plain — and from this clan Siddhartha Gautam, the Buddha, was born.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
