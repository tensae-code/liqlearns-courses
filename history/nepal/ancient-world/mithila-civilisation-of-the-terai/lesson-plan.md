# Lesson plan — Mithila Civilisation of the Terai

**Course:** History — Nepal · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Mithila Civilisation of the Terai
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Ancient Nepal and the Old World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On the Terai plain, Mithila grew around Janakpur: King Janak's capital, the Maithili language, folk art painted from local things, and festivals that still carry the old story.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Ancient Nepal and the Old World (recommended, never forced).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Nepal's books themselves work, telling the past as stories and tasks.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to its turn: who was there, where it happened, and what changed. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: On the Terai plain, Mithila grew around Janakpur: King Janak's capital, the Maithili language, folk art painted from local things, and festivals that still carry the old story. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
