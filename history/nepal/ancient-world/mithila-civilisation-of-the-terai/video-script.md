# Video script — Mithila Civilisation of the Terai

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
On the Terai plain, Mithila grew around Janakpur: King Janak's capital, the Maithili language, folk art painted from local things, and festivals that still carry the old story.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Down on the Terai plain stands one of South Asia's oldest civilisations.

Remember it this way: On the Terai plain, Mithila grew around Janakpur: King Janak's capital, the Maithili language, folk art painted from local things, and festivals that still carry the old story.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
