# Video script — Sinja Civilisation and the Khas Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
At Sinja in Jumla the Khas kings built a great hill kingdom — and from its Khas speech the Nepali language itself grew, with its first inscription standing at Dullu.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Pupils meet this civilisation through a documentary film: Sinja, in Jumla, capital of the Khas kingdom founded by Nagaraj, come from Tibet's Ngari.

Remember it this way: At Sinja in Jumla the Khas kings built a great hill kingdom — and from its Khas speech the Nepali language itself grew, with its first inscription standing at Dullu.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
