# Sinja Civilisation and the Khas Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Khas kingdom was founded by Nagaraj from Tibet's Ngari.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Sinja is still one united kingdom today.
**Answer: False** — it broke apart around 1450 BS into the Baise and Chaubise states.
