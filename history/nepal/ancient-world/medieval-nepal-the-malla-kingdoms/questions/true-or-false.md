# Medieval Nepal: The Malla Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Aridev Malla took the name 'Malla' from a wrestling bout. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The valley kingdoms never traded with Tibet. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — they grew rich trading with Tibet.
