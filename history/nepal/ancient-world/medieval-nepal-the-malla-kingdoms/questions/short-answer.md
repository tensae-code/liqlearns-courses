# Medieval Nepal: The Malla Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two features of medieval valley society. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Caste and occupation order, sati, child marriage, festivals like Indra Jatra, pandits and monastery schools, silver coinage, Tibet trade (any two).

**2.** Name two parts of medieval life the final survey reconstructs. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Land and trade, caste-ordered society, festivals, and pagoda art and craft (any two).
