# Medieval Nepal: The Malla Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From what year does the medieval period run, as the books divide it? (Sub lesson 1)
a) 936 BS, the start of Nepal Sambat  b) 1825 BS  c) 2007 BS  d) 569 BS
**Answer: a) 936 BS, the start of Nepal Sambat** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Into which three kingdoms did Yaksha Malla's sons divide the valley? (Sub lesson 3)
a) Kantipur, Patan and Bhaktapur  b) Baise, Chaubise and Sen  c) Gorkha, Nuwakot and Makwanpur  d) Jumla, Doti and Palpa
**Answer: a) Kantipur, Patan and Bhaktapur** — That is what this lesson teaches.
