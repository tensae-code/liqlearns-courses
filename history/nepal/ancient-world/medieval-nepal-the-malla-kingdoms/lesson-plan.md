# Lesson plan — Medieval Nepal: The Malla Kingdoms

**Course:** History — Nepal · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Medieval Nepal: The Malla Kingdoms
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Medieval Nepal (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** For some 900 medieval years the Malla kings ruled the valley — after Yaksha Malla's death his sons split it into Kantipur, Patan and Bhaktapur, rival kingdoms rich in art, coins, festivals and trade with Tibet.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Medieval Nepal (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Medieval Nepal** — Nepal's own books divide its past into three ages: ancient — Gopal, Mahispal, Kirat, Lichchhavi; medieval, from 936 BS, the start of Nepal Sambat, for some 900 years; and modern, after 1825 BS.
2. **Medieval Nepal — Political, Social and Economic Condition** — The three valley kingdoms lived as rivals who sometimes allied and sometimes invaded, calling in Sen or Gorkha armies when it suited them.
3. **The Medieval Political Condition** — After the Licchavis come the Mallas.
4. **The Medieval Economic, Social and Cultural Condition** — Medieval life is reconstructed whole, not only its kings.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

- Go back to the one thing: For some 900 medieval years the Malla kings ruled the valley — after Yaksha Malla's death his sons split it into Kantipur, Patan and Bhaktapur, rival kingdoms rich in art, coins, festivals and trade with Tibet.
