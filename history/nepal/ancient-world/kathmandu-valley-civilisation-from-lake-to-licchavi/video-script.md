# Video script — Kathmandu Valley Civilisation: From Lake to Licchavi

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Kathmandu Valley's civilisation begins with its lake: the lotus story and the draining of the water, then herding dynasties in turn, until the valley becomes a settled, farming, worshipping land.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. The valley's civilisation starts under water.

Remember it this way: The Kathmandu Valley's civilisation begins with its lake: the lotus story and the draining of the water, then herding dynasties in turn, until the valley becomes a settled, farming, worshipping land.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
