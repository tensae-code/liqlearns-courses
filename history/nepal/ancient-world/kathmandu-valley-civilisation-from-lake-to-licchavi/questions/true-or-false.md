# Kathmandu Valley Civilisation: From Lake to Licchavi — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Gopal, Mahispal and Kirat dynasties followed one another after the draining.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The valley was always dry land with cities on it.
**Answer: False** — it began as a great lake.
