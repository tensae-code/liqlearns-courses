# Ancient Civilisations: India — the Indus Valley — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which two cities does the lesson study?
a) Mohenjo-daro and Harappa  b) Kathmandu and Patan  c) Rome and Athens  d) Gorkha and Nuwakot
**Answer: a) Mohenjo-daro and Harappa** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** The Indus civilisation is the earliest civilisation of which region?
a) South Asia  b) Europe  c) the Arctic  d) East Africa
**Answer: a) South Asia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
