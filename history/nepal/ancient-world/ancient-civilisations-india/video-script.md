# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: India — the Indus Valley

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Indus Valley cities — Mohenjo-daro and Harappa — were among the world's earliest cities: planned streets and great buildings raised by a people of South Asia thousands of years ago.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. On the map of Asia, find the Indus.

Remember it this way: The Indus Valley cities — Mohenjo-daro and Harappa — were among the world's earliest cities: planned streets and great buildings raised by a people of South Asia thousands of years ago.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
