# Ancient Civilisations: India — the Indus Valley

## Do it first

Before you read anything, do this: say out loud what you already think this lesson is about, from its name alone — Ancient Civilisations: India — the Indus Valley. Name one person, one place, or one thing you expect to meet in it. Keep your guess in mind; the story will test it.

On the map of Asia, find the Indus. Here stand Mohenjo-daro and Harappa, pictured in brick and ruin — the earliest civilisation of South Asia. The class studies how these cities lived and charts what they left behind. Long before the valley kingdoms of Nepal, people in this same region were already raising planned cities; the Indus is the deep root of the region's story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Indus Valley cities — Mohenjo-daro and Harappa — were among the world's earliest cities: planned streets and great buildings raised by a people of South Asia thousands of years ago. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood where the same kind of thing happens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
