# Ancient Civilisations: Greece — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who is pictured as a teller of histories from Greece?
a) Herodotus  b) Romulus  c) Leonardo  d) Dante
**Answer: a) Herodotus** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did pupils use to locate Greece?
a) an atlas and wall maps  b) a telescope  c) a printing press  d) a timeline only
**Answer: a) an atlas and wall maps** — That is what this lesson teaches.
