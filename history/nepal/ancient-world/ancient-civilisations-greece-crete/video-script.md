# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ancient Greece gave the later world city life, art and big thinking — a civilisation Nepali pupils meet on the map of Europe, with Homer's poems and Herodotus, the teller of histories.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Sail west across the old maps to Greece.

Remember it this way: Ancient Greece gave the later world city life, art and big thinking — a civilisation Nepali pupils meet on the map of Europe, with Homer's poems and Herodotus, the teller of histories.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
