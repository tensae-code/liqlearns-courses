# Video script — Nepal: The Name and the Kathmandu Valley Lake

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Long ago the Kathmandu Valley was a great lake; the story tells how its water drained away so people could settle — and the country's name, Nepal, carries rival stories that must be weighed against each other.

Watch the map and the pictures as the story moves. Why is the country called Nepal? The stories compete, and no single one wins easily. The teacher opens the valley's oldest story as a performance: long ago, the Kathmandu Valley was one great lake. The oldest lesson returns with harder questions. The final survey begins before history itself.

Remember it this way: Long ago the Kathmandu Valley was a great lake; the story tells how its water drained away so people could settle — and the country's name, Nepal, carries rival stories that must be weighed against each other.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
