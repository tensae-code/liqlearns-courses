# Nepal: The Name and the Kathmandu Valley Lake — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who is said to have drained the valley lake by cutting the Chobhar gorge? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Manjushree  b) King Janak  c) Bise Nagarchi  d) Yalambar
**Answer: a) Manjushree** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** About when is the word 'Nepal' first written? (Sub lesson 4)
a) about 500–600 CE  b) about 2000 BCE  c) in 2072 BS  d) it was never written
**Answer: a) about 500–600 CE** — That is what this lesson teaches.
