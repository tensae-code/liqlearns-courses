# Nepal: The Name and the Kathmandu Valley Lake — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Manjushree cut the gorge at ____ and drained the lake. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Chobhar

**2.** Thirteen crore years ago a sea called ____ covered the land. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer:** Tethys
