# Nepal: The Name and the Kathmandu Valley Lake — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two rival bases pupils weigh for the name 'Nepal'. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Religious, historical and linguistic bases (any two); or the sage Ne, Kiranti, Tibetan, Newari and Lepcha explanations.

**2.** What are pupils asked to hunt about their own settlement? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The naming basis of their own settlement — why their place carries its name.
