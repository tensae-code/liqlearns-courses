# Lesson plan — Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Heritage: What It Is and Why It Matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before any period is studied, the course asks what art itself is — and where old art lives now: the museum, the house where a people's memory is kept.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks heritage means only old buildings → heritage is everything a people keeps from its past — pictures, statues, objects and the collections that guard them; the museum is where that keeping happens.
- Go back to the one thing: Before any period is studied, the course asks what art itself is — and where old art lives now: the museum, the house where a people's memory is kept. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
