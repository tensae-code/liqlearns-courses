# Lesson plan — Symbolism and Munch: Art of Dreams and Anxiety

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Symbolism and Munch: Art of Dreams and Anxiety
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The century ends dreaming — Pre-Raphaelites and symbolists paint myths, dreams and death rather than the visible world, Rodin remakes sculpture from the inside, and Munch's Scream turns modern anxiety into an icon before expressionism has a name.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (recommended, never forced).
