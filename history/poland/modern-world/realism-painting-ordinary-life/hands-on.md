# Hands-on — Realism: Painting Ordinary Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: The realists turn their backs on heroes and gods and paint stone-breakers, funerals in provincial towns and peasants at work — a scandal at the salons, and a claim that ordinary life is worthy of the biggest canvases. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson — f stone-breakers hammering rocks beside a road, painted large and plain, a village funeral passing behind — and under your drawing write the one thing in your own words. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
