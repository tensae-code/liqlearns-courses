# Video script — Impressionism: Paris Paints Modern Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Manet scandalises Paris, then Monet and his friends take painting outdoors to catch light itself — railways, boulevards, regattas and haystacks at every hour; the academy's rejects build their own exhibitions and invent modern art's marketplace.

Modern art begins with a scandal and a walk outdoors. Manet scandalises Paris first — his flat, bold pictures defying the salon. Then Monet and his friends take painting outside altogether, easels in the fields and streets, to catch light itself before it changes: railways in steam, boulevards in sun, regattas glittering, haystacks painted at every hour of the day to show light remaking the same thing endlessly.

Rejected by the academy's salons, they build their own exhibitions — and invent modern art's marketplace in the process: independent shows, dealers, collectors, a public that buys the new. Painting's subject becomes modern life itself, seen in a shimmer of broken colour. The rebels of this lesson found the world every later movement inherits.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
