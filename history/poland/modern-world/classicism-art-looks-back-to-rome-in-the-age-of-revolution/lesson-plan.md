# Lesson plan — Classicism: Art Looks Back to Rome in the Age of Revolution

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Classicism: Art Looks Back to Rome in the Age of Revolution
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Disgusted with Rococo frivolity, Europe digs up Pompeii and dresses revolution and empire alike in Roman virtue — temples for banks and parliaments, David's oath scenes for republics, and Napoleon's empire style for a new Caesar.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (recommended, never forced).
