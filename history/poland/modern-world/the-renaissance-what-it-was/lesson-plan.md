# Lesson plan — The Renaissance: What It Was

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Renaissance: What It Was
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Rich Italian city-states, proud of Rome's ruins under their feet, decide the Middle Ages were a long sleep — scholars dig out ancient books, bankers and popes pay for beauty, and art wakes up measuring the world with new eyes.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (recommended, never forced).
