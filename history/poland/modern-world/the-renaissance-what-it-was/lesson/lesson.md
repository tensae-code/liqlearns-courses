# The Renaissance: What It Was

## Do it first

Think of a story about the past that you already know — from home, from school, from your street. Say out loud who was in it, where it happened, and what changed by its end. Keep that shape in mind: this lesson's story is told the same way — people, place, the turn, and what came of it.

## The story

The Renaissance begins with a decision. In Italy's rich city-states — Florence, Venice, Rome itself — men stand among ancient ruins and decide that the Middle Ages between them and Rome were a long sleep. Rome's remains lie under their feet; Rome's books can be dug out and read again. The age calls itself a rebirth: antiquity, born again.

Money makes the waking possible. Bankers and popes pay for beauty — palaces, churches, paintings, statues — and scholars dig out the ancient authors while artists learn to measure the world with new eyes: perspective, anatomy, proportion studied from nature and from the ancients together. Art wakes up, and it does not go back to sleep.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Rebirth needs two things: old books dug out, and rich patrons willing to pay. Italy had both.
