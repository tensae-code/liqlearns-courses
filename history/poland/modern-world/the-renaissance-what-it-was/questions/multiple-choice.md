# The Renaissance: What It Was — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where does the Renaissance begin?
a) in Italy's rich city-states  b) in Poland's villages  c) in Spain's monasteries only  d) in northern sea ports
**Answer: a) in Italy's rich city-states** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the age call the Middle Ages?
a) a long sleep  b) a golden age  c) a second Rome  d) an age of giants
**Answer: a) a long sleep** — That is what this lesson teaches.
