# Baroque Rome: Art of the Counter-Reformation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who made marble fly?
a) Bernini  b) Borromini  c) Titian  d) Dürer
**Answer: a) Bernini** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did illusionist painters do to ceilings?
a) broke the roof open onto heaven  b) covered them in gold leaf only  c) left them plain white  d) hung lamps from them
**Answer: a) broke the roof open onto heaven** — That is what this lesson teaches.
