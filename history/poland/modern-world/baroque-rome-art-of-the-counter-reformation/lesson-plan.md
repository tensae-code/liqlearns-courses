## If the learner is stuck
- Confuses the two rivals → Bernini embraces crowds and makes marble fly; Borromini bends walls like waves. Caravaggio is the painter of tavern light and shadow.
- Go back to the one thing: Bernini makes marble fly and St Peter's Square embrace the crowds; Borromini bends walls like waves; Caravaggio drags saints into tavern light and shadow; and ceiling painters break the roof open onto heaven. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
