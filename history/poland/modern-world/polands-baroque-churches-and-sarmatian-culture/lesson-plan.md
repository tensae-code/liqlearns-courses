# Lesson plan — Poland's Baroque: Churches and Sarmatian Culture

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Poland's Baroque: Churches and Sarmatian Culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Commonwealth covers itself in Baroque churches built as fortresses of faith through wars with Sweden and the Ottomans, while the szlachta's Sarmatian culture — coffin portraits, oriental dress, country manors — gives Polish Baroque a face found nowhere else.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (recommended, never forced).
