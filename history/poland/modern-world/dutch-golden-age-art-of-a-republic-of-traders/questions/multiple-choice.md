# Dutch Golden Age: Art of a Republic of Traders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Dutch buyers hang on their own walls?
a) small pictures of homes, ships, landscapes and faces  b) huge altarpieces  c) royal portraits only  d) tapestries of battles
**Answer: a) small pictures of homes, ships, landscapes and faces** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who painted big and sensuous for church and court in Flanders?
a) Rubens  b) Rembrandt  c) El Greco  d) Bernini
**Answer: a) Rubens** — That is what this lesson teaches.
