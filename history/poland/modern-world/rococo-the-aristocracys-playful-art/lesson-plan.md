# Lesson plan — Rococo: The Aristocracy's Playful Art

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Rococo: The Aristocracy's Playful Art
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After the Sun King's death the aristocracy trades glory for pleasure — rooms shrink to salons, curves turn to shells and scrolls, and painting fills with silk, flirtation and garden parties on the eve of a revolution nobody at the party expects.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (recommended, never forced).
