# Poland's Last King and Classicist Warsaw — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Stanisław August make Warsaw?
a) a classicist capital of theatres, palaces and Łazienki park  b) a fortress city  c) a sea port  d) an industrial town
**Answer: a) a classicist capital of theatres, palaces and Łazienki park** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What closed in while Warsaw flowered?
a) the partitions  b) the Gothic age  c) the Dutch trade  d) the pilgrims' roads
**Answer: a) the partitions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
