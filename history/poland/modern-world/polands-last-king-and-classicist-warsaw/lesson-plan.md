# Lesson plan — Poland's Last King and Classicist Warsaw

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Poland's Last King and Classicist Warsaw
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Stanisław August, Poland's last king, makes Warsaw a classicist capital of theatres, palaces and Łazienki park even as the partitions close in — a court culture flowering in the shadow of the state's destruction.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (recommended, never forced).

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Poland's books themselves work, telling each period's story through the art it left.
