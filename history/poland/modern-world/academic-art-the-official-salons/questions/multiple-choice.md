# Academic Art: The Official Salons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who defined what governments called good art?
a) the academies and their official salons  b) the newspapers  c) the merchants  d) the engineers
**Answer: a) the academies and their official salons** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the young rebels define themselves against?
a) the academic establishment  b) the Gothic style  c) the Dutch market  d) the Roman ruins
**Answer: a) the academic establishment** — That is what this lesson teaches.
