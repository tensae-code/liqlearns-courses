# Hands-on — Academic Art: The Official Salons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: Against every revolt stands the academy — official salons, state prizes and polished mythological and historical canvases define what governments call good art, the establishment the young rebels of the century define themselves against. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson — f a grand salon wall hung floor to ceiling with polished mythological canvases in gold frames, judges below — and under your drawing write the one thing in your own words. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
