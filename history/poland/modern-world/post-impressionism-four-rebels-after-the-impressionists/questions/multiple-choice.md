# Post-Impressionism: Four Rebels After the Impressionists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who rebuilt the world in solid geometry?
a) Cézanne  b) Gauguin  c) Toulouse-Lautrec  d) Monet
**Answer: a) Cézanne** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened to Van Gogh's pictures in his lifetime?
a) he sold almost nothing  b) he sold thousands  c) kings collected them all  d) the salon crowned them
**Answer: a) he sold almost nothing** — That is what this lesson teaches.
