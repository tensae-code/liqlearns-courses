# Lesson plan — Post-Impressionism: Four Rebels After the Impressionists

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Post-Impressionism: Four Rebels After the Impressionists
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Four heirs break four different ways — Cézanne rebuilds the world in solid geometry, Gauguin flees to Brittany and Tahiti for the primitive, Van Gogh burns feeling into swirling colour and sells almost nothing, and Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the cabaret poster.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (recommended, never forced).
