# Video script — Post-Impressionism: Four Rebels After the Impressionists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Four heirs break four different ways — Cézanne rebuilds the world in solid geometry, Gauguin flees to Brittany and Tahiti for the primitive, Van Gogh burns feeling into swirling colour and sells almost nothing, and Toulouse-Lautrec takes art to the cabaret poster.

Impressionism's heirs inherit a shimmering world — and each one breaks it a different way. Cézanne rebuilds: beneath the shimmer he finds solid geometry, cylinders and cones, painting as architecture. Gauguin flees: to Brittany, then to Tahiti, hunting the primitive, the unspoiled world Europe had paved over.

Van Gogh burns: feeling itself becomes swirling colour, cypresses and stars writhing with emotion — and he sells almost nothing in his life. Toulouse-Lautrec descends cheerfully into Paris nightlife and takes art to the cabaret poster, flat and unforgettable on every wall. Four rebels, four roads — and all of modern art travels down one or another of them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
