# Video script — Art Nouveau and Secession: A Total Style for 1900

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Around 1900 the young secede from the academies — whiplash lines and plant forms swallow architecture, furniture, posters and jewellery whole from Barcelona to Vienna, and Klimt covers Vienna's new art in gold — a last attempt at one total style before modernism breaks apart.

Around 1900, the young walk out. In Vienna they call it the Secession — artists seceding, breaking away from the academies — and across Europe the same break wears one style: Art Nouveau. Its line is the whiplash, a curve like a plant stem or a wave, and it swallows everything whole: architecture, furniture, posters, jewellery, whole rooms designed as single works of art, from Barcelona to Vienna.

In Vienna, Klimt covers the new art in gold — portraits and allegories shimmering in patterned gold leaf, Byzantium's lesson learned again in a modern capital. It is a last attempt at one total style for the whole of life — made just before modernism breaks art apart into movements that agree on nothing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
