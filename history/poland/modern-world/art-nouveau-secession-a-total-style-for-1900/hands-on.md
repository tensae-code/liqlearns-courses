# Hands-on — Art Nouveau and Secession: A Total Style for 1900

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: Around 1900 the young secede from the academies — whiplash lines and plant forms swallow architecture, furniture, posters and jewellery whole from Barcelona to Vienna, and Klimt covers Vienna's new art in gold — a last attempt at one total style before modernism breaks apart. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson — f a room where chair, lamp, poster and doorway all curve in the same whiplash plant lines, a gold portrait on the wall — and under your drawing write the one thing in your own words. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
