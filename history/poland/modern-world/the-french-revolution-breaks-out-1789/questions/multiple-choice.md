# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what year did America declare independence?
a) 1776  b) 1789  c) 1815  d) 1689
**Answer: a) 1776** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who crowned himself out of the revolutionary storm?
a) Napoleon  b) Louis XIV  c) Charlemagne  d) Justinian
**Answer: a) Napoleon** — That is what this lesson teaches.
