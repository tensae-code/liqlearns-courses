# Mannerism: Italy's Restless Art After the Renaissance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did the Mannerists abandon balance?
a) they felt perfection was already achieved and could only be escaped  b) they had forgotten how to draw  c) their patrons ordered mistakes  d) paint ran short
**Answer: a) they felt perfection was already achieved and could only be escaped** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened to Rome in these years?
a) it was sacked, as Italy was invaded  b) it became a republic of artists  c) it was rebuilt in blue brick  d) it sank into the sea
**Answer: a) it was sacked, as Italy was invaded** — That is what this lesson teaches.
