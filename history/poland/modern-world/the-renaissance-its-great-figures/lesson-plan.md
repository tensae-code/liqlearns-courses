# Lesson plan — The Renaissance: Its Great Figures

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Renaissance: Its Great Figures
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The great named masters arrive — Michelangelo carving and painting like a force of nature, Leonardo turning painting into a science of light and mind, Raphael balancing it all, and Titian making Venice glow in colour.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The great named masters arrive — Michelangelo carving and painting like a force of nature, Leonardo turning painting into a science of light and mind, Raphael balancing it all, and Titian making Venice glow in colour.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Poland's books themselves work, telling each period's story through the art it left.
