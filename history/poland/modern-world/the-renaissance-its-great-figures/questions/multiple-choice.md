# The Renaissance: Its Great Figures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which master made Venice glow in colour?
a) Titian  b) Raphael  c) Michelangelo  d) Leonardo
**Answer: a) Titian** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Michelangelo work like?
a) a force of nature  b) a careful accountant  c) a quiet copyist  d) a travelling merchant
**Answer: a) a force of nature** — That is what this lesson teaches.
