# Poland's Painters Under Partition: Matejko and History Painting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did Matejko's paintings matter so much to Poles?
a) they taught Polish history when schools could not  b) they were the smallest in Europe  c) they showed foreign lands only  d) they won foreign prizes only
**Answer: a) they taught Polish history when schools could not** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What two things do his canvases show of the old Commonwealth?
a) its triumphs and its sins  b) only its kings  c) only its churches  d) only its landscapes
**Answer: a) its triumphs and its sins** — That is what this lesson teaches.
