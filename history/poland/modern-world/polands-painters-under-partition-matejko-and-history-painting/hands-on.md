# Hands-on — Poland's Painters Under Partition: Matejko and History Painting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: A partitioned nation paints its memory to survive — Matejko's enormous canvases of Grunwald and the old Commonwealth's triumphs and sins hang in Kraków as a substitute parliament of the imagination, teaching Poles their history when schools could not. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson — f an enormous history canvas of a medieval battle filling a Kraków gallery wall, a crowd of Poles standing before it — and under your drawing write the one thing in your own words. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
