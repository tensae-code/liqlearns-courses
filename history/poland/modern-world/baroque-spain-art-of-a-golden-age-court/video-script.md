# Video script — Baroque Spain: Art of a Golden-Age Court

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Spain's Habsburg court, rich on American silver and strict in faith, fills its palaces and monasteries with grave, intense painting — an empire's pride and melancholy in one frame.

Spain's Baroque wears a graver face than Rome's. The Habsburg court is rich on American silver — the treasure of a world empire — and strict in its Catholic faith, and the painting it fills its palaces and monasteries with matches both: grave, intense, unflinching, saints and kings rendered with a solemn weight found nowhere else in the age.

Pride and melancholy share the frame. This is an empire at its height and already conscious of decline, and its art holds the two together — splendour without frivolity, faith without softness. The Spanish golden age paints as its court lives: rich, strict and shadowed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
