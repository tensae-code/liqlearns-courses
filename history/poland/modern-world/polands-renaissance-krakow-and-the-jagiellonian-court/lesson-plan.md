# Lesson plan — Poland's Renaissance: Kraków and the Jagiellonian Court

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Poland's Renaissance: Kraków and the Jagiellonian Court
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Jagiellonian court makes Kraków a northern Florence — Italian architects rebuild Wawel with an arcaded courtyard, the Sigismund Chapel rises as the period's jewel, and Polish nobles' manors and town halls take up the new style — Poland's own Renaissance golden age.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Rebirth and Splendour: Renaissance, Baroque and Rococo (recommended, never forced).
