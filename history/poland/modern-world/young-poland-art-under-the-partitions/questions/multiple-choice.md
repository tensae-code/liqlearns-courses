# Young Poland: Art Under the Partitions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are Young Poland's two capitals of the spirit?
a) Kraków and Zakopane  b) Warsaw and Gdańsk  c) Poznań and Lwów  d) Vienna and Prague
**Answer: a) Kraków and Zakopane** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What fuses in Wyspiański's work?
a) stained glass and drama  b) sculpture and engineering  c) poster and photography  d) music and architecture
**Answer: a) stained glass and drama** — That is what this lesson teaches.
