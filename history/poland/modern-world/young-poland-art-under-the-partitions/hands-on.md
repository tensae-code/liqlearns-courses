# Hands-on — Young Poland: Art Under the Partitions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: Young Poland makes Kraków and Zakopane a capital of the spirit — highland cottages become a national style, stained glass and drama fuse in Wyspiański's visions, and a generation paints folklore and myth as the programme of a country that exists in culture before it exists on the map. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson — f a carved timber Zakopane cottage with a steep roof under mountain peaks, a glowing stained-glass window beside it — and under your drawing write the one thing in your own words. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
