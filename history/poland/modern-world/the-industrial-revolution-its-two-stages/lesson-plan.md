# Lesson plan — The Industrial Revolution: Its Two Stages

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Industrial Revolution: Its Two Stages
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Steam, iron and the factory remake the century's cities faster than any style can follow — engineers raise bridges, stations and exhibition halls of iron and glass while architects dress banks and parliaments in borrowed Gothic and Renaissance clothes.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Revolution, Industry and the Nation in Art: Classicism to Young Poland (recommended, never forced).
