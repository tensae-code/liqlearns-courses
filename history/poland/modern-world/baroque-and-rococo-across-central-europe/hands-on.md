# Hands-on — Baroque and Rococo Across Central Europe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: The styles travel east and north — pilgrimage churches and palaces rise across the German lands, Austria and Bohemia, each court and monastery translating Rome and Versailles into its own accent. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson — f a swirling pilgrimage church in the hills of Bohemia, its towers curving against the sky — and under your drawing write the one thing in your own words. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
