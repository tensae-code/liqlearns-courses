# History — Poland course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Poland's own —
built from the three volumes of Poland's *Ars longa*
art-history textbooks for its art schools (student books,
Polish). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels,
not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in
path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The
grade each book comes from lives only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the series': every world period is
told as a story through its art, and every period's story
comes home to Poland — prehistoric art on Polish lands,
Romanesque Poland, Kraków's Renaissance, Sarmatian Baroque,
Matejko's partitioned nation, Young Poland. That ladder IS
the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder).
