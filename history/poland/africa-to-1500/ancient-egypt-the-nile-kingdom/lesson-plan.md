# Lesson plan — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Nile kingdom builds for eternity — pyramids and temples for gods and god-kings, statues carved to a strict canon so a pharaoh's face outlives him by three thousand years, and tomb paintings that pack a whole life for the journey after death.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (recommended, never forced).
