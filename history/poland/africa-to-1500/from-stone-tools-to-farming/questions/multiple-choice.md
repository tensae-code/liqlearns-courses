# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the first artists paint on cave walls?
a) the animals they chased  b) their kings  c) their houses  d) maps of rivers
**Answer: a) the animals they chased** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What changed people's art most in this lesson?
a) farming settling people down  b) the invention of paper  c) the coming of kings  d) sea travel
**Answer: a) farming settling people down** — That is what this lesson teaches.
