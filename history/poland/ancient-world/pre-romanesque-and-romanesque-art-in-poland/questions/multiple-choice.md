# Pre-Romanesque and Romanesque Art in Poland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what styles did the young Polish state first build?
a) pre-Romanesque and Romanesque, the same styles as the West  b) Gothic only  c) only in wood  d) in Babylonian blue brick
**Answer: a) pre-Romanesque and Romanesque, the same styles as the West** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What showed Poland carving in Europe's manner?
a) its cathedral doors with Bible scenes  b) its gold masks  c) its cave paintings  d) its aqueducts
**Answer: a) its cathedral doors with Bible scenes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
