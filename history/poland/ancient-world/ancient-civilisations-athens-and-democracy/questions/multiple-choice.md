# Ancient Civilisations: Athens and Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Athens invent?
a) democracy  b) the ziggurat  c) the pyramid  d) glazed brick
**Answer: a) democracy** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What gave every Greek temple and statue its subject?
a) the Greeks' beliefs in the Olympian family  b) the kings' orders  c) foreign models  d) the sea trade
**Answer: a) the Greeks' beliefs in the Olympian family** — That is what this lesson teaches.
