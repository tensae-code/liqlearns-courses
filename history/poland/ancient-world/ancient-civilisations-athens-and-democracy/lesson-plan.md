## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Greece was one united kingdom → it was many free city-states, often quarrelling; Athens and Sparta are the lesson's two opposite examples, joined only by their shared gods.
- Go back to the one thing: Free city-states quarrel under the same gods — Athens invents democracy, Sparta trains for war, and the Greeks' beliefs in the Olympian family give every temple and statue its subject. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
