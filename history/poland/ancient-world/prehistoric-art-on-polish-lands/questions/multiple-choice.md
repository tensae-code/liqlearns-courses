# Prehistoric Art on Polish Lands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson show happening on Polish lands?
a) the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns as in the world story  b) the building of cathedrals  c) the coming of kings  d) the first factories
**Answer: a) the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns as in the world story** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which home evidence does the lesson name?
a) caves, amber-working and early settlements  b) castles and town halls  c) ships and harbours  d) railways and stations
**Answer: a) caves, amber-working and early settlements** — That is what this lesson teaches.
