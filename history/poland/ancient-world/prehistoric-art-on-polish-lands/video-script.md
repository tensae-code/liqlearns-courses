# Video script — Prehistoric Art on Polish Lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The world story comes home: the caves, amber-working and early settlements of the Polish lands show the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns happening in the learners' own ground.

Every world period in this series ends by coming home, and prehistory comes home first. The same turns the last lesson followed across the world — hunting peoples, then farming settlements — happened here too, in the ground the learners themselves stand on.

The Polish lands hold their own evidence: caves that sheltered the earliest people, amber worked by early hands — the golden resin of the Baltic shore, shaped and traded — and the remains of early settlements, where farming life took root. The lesson's point is simple and large: prehistory is not only a story about faraway places. It is also the story of this soil.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
