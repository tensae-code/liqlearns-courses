# Lesson plan — Prehistoric Art on Polish Lands

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Prehistoric Art on Polish Lands
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The world story comes home: the caves, amber-working and early settlements of the Polish lands show the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns happening in the learners' own ground.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The world story comes home: the caves, amber-working and early settlements of the Polish lands show the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns happening in the learners' own ground.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Poland's books themselves work, telling each period's story through the art it left.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks prehistory happened only in famous faraway caves → the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns happened on Polish lands too; the caves, amber-working and settlements there are the proof.
- Go back to the one thing: The world story comes home: the caves, amber-working and early settlements of the Polish lands show the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns happening in the learners' own ground. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
