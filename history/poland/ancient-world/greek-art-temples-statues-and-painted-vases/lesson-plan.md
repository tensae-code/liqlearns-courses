# Lesson plan — Greek Art: Temples, Statues and Painted Vases

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Greek Art: Temples, Statues and Painted Vases
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Greeks hunt for perfect proportion — temples tuned like music, statues that step out of stiffness into living balance, and black-and-red vases that carry the myths and the games into every Greek home.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (recommended, never forced).

- Thinks Greek temples were sized by guesswork → they were tuned like music, every part measured in proportion; the statues' move from stiffness to balance is the same search in stone.
