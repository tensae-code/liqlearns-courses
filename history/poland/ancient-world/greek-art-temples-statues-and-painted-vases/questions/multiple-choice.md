# Greek Art: Temples, Statues and Painted Vases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Greek art hunt for above all?
a) perfect proportion  b) the biggest size  c) the brightest colour  d) the oldest look
**Answer: a) perfect proportion** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did Greek statues change over time?
a) they stepped out of stiffness into living balance  b) they grew smaller  c) they were painted blue  d) they were buried with gold masks
**Answer: a) they stepped out of stiffness into living balance** — That is what this lesson teaches.
