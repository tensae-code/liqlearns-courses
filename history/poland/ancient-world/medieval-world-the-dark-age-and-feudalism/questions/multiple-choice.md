# The Medieval World: The Dark Age and Feudalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who kept learning alive after Rome fell?
a) the monks in their monasteries  b) the merchants  c) the sailors  d) the gladiators
**Answer: a) the monks in their monasteries** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who followed Charlemagne in renewing the empire?
a) the Ottonians  b) the Minoans  c) the Assyrians  d) the Etruscans
**Answer: a) the Ottonians** — That is what this lesson teaches.
