# Lesson plan — Gothic Art: Cathedrals and Towns

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Gothic Art: Cathedrals and Towns
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Faith and Stone: The Medieval World (CURRICULUM.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Gothic is just taller Romanesque → the turn is structural and human at once: pointed arches and glass walls, proud towns building beside bishops, and Giotto's weighted, grieving people opening the next age.
- Go back to the one thing: The cathedrals race each other into the sky on pointed arches and coloured glass; towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches; and at the period's end Giotto starts painting people with weight and grief again, Bosch fills his Garden with warning — the Middle Ages handing over to something new. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
