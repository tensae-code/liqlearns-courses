# Romanesque Art: Churches of Stone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When does Europe cover itself in stone churches?
a) around the year 1000  b) around the year 100  c) in the age of farming  d) after the factories
**Answer: a) around the year 1000** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who paid for the Romanesque building wave?
a) pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries  b) foreign empires  c) sea traders only  d) the Roman army
**Answer: a) pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries** — That is what this lesson teaches.
