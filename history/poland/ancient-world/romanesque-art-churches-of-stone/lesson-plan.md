# Lesson plan — Romanesque Art: Churches of Stone

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Romanesque Art: Churches of Stone
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Faith and Stone: The Medieval World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Around the year 1000 Europe covers itself in stone churches — thick walls, round arches and carved portals preaching the Bible to people who cannot read, built by pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries from France to Poland.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Faith and Stone: The Medieval World (recommended, never forced).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Poland's books themselves work, telling each period's story through the art it left.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the carved portals were only ornament → they preached: with most people unable to read, the sculpture over the doors taught the Bible to everyone who entered.
- Go back to the one thing: Around the year 1000 Europe covers itself in stone churches — thick walls, round arches and carved portals preaching the Bible to people who cannot read, built by pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries from France to Poland. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
