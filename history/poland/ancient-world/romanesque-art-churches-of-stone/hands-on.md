# Hands-on — Romanesque Art: Churches of Stone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: Around the year 1000 Europe covers itself in stone churches — thick walls, round arches and carved portals preaching the Bible to people who cannot read, built by pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries from France to Poland. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson — f a Romanesque church with thick walls and round arches, pilgrims walking a road toward its carved doorway — and under your drawing write the one thing in your own words. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
