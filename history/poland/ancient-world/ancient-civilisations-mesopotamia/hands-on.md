# Hands-on — Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud: Between two rivers, the Sumerians raise the first cities and ziggurats, the Assyrians carve palace walls with kings hunting and conquering, and Babylon rebuilds in glazed blue brick — three peoples, one river plain, each leaving its power written in art. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the scene at the heart of this lesson — f a stepped ziggurat rising over a flat river plain, with a blue glazed gate in front of it — and under your drawing write the one thing in your own words. Say each part aloud as you draw it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Note each time you see it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
