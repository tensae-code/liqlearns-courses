# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where does this civilisation rise?
a) between two rivers, on one river plain  b) on a sea island  c) in a mountain valley  d) in a desert oasis
**Answer: a) between two rivers, on one river plain** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did the Assyrians carve on their palace walls?
a) their kings hunting and conquering  b) farmers planting crops  c) sailors on ships  d) children at school
**Answer: a) their kings hunting and conquering** — That is what this lesson teaches.
