# The Etruscans: Rome's Teachers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who ruled Italy before Rome?
a) the Etruscans  b) the Egyptians  c) the Minoans  d) the Assyrians
**Answer: a) the Etruscans** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What fills the walls of Etruscan tombs?
a) feasting and dancing  b) sea battles  c) farming scenes only  d) maps of Italy
**Answer: a) feasting and dancing** — That is what this lesson teaches.
