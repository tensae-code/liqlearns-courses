# Lesson plan — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On Crete the Minoans fill a palace at Knossos with bull-leapers and sea creatures, careless of walls because their ships rule the sea; on the mainland the Mycenaeans build fortress walls of giant stones and are buried with gold masks — the two childhoods of Greece.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps, Prehistory and the Ancient World (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the two peoples up → Crete is the island: Minoans, Knossos, bull-leapers, no walls because ships rule the sea. The mainland is Mycenae: fortress walls of giant stones, gold burial masks.
- Go back to the one thing: On Crete the Minoans fill a palace at Knossos with bull-leapers and sea creatures, careless of walls because their ships rule the sea; on the mainland the Mycenaeans build fortress walls of giant stones and are buried with gold masks — the two childhoods of Greece. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
