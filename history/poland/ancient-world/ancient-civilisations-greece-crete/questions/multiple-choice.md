# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did Knossos need no fortress wall?
a) because Minoan ships ruled the sea  b) because the island had no enemies at all  c) because stone was forbidden  d) because the palace was hidden underground
**Answer: a) because Minoan ships ruled the sea** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What were Mycenaean fortress walls built of?
a) giant stones  b) glazed blue brick  c) wood and clay  d) white marble columns
**Answer: a) giant stones** — That is what this lesson teaches.
