# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Rome do with Greek beauty?
a) put it to work for an empire  b) hid it in tombs  c) painted it blue  d) left it in Greece
**Answer: a) put it to work for an empire** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does a triumphal arch turn a victory into?
a) street theatre  b) a temple service  c) a marketplace  d) a school lesson
**Answer: a) street theatre** — That is what this lesson teaches.
