# Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why did early Christians paint in symbols?
a) so only believers could read them, while the faith was hunted  b) because they had no colours  c) because walls were small  d) because Rome ordered pictures banned everywhere
**Answer: a) so only believers could read them, while the faith was hunted** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What turned the hunted sect into the state church?
a) an emperor converted  b) a great battle at sea  c) a new temple in Athens  d) the fall of Babylon
**Answer: a) an emperor converted** — That is what this lesson teaches.
