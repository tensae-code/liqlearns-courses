# Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

## Do it first

Think of a story about the past that you already know — from home, from school, from your street. Say out loud who was in it, where it happened, and what changed by its end. Keep that shape in mind: this lesson's story is told the same way — people, place, the turn, and what came of it.

## The story

The first Christian art is hidden art. The faith is hunted — its believers persecuted — so they meet and bury their dead underground, in the catacombs, and paint there in symbols only believers can read: signs that speak to the faithful and say nothing to the hunter. A whole art of secrecy grows in the dark.

Then comes one of history's great turns: an emperor converts. Constantine takes the faith, the hunted become the state church, and everything changes place. The symbols come up out of the catacombs into the light, and the faith builds openly — great basilicas for worship, and mosaics shining on their walls. The same signs, first whispered underground, now proclaimed in gold above ground.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: One faith, two weathers: painted whispers underground, golden mosaics above.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A hunted faith paints in secret in the catacombs, in symbols only believers can read; then an emperor converts, the hunted become the state church, and the symbols come up into the light as basilicas and mosaics. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: catacombs, basilica. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: retell the story aloud — people, place, the turn, what came of it.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the story as a row of scenes, with the turn marked.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
