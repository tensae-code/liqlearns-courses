# Lesson plan — Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

**Course:** History — Poland · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Faith and Stone: The Medieval World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A hunted faith paints in secret in the catacombs, in symbols only believers can read; then an emperor converts, the hunted become the state church, and the symbols come up into the light as basilicas and mosaics.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Faith and Stone: The Medieval World (recommended, never forced).

- Go back to the one thing: A hunted faith paints in secret in the catacombs, in symbols only believers can read; then an emperor converts, the hunted become the state church, and the symbols come up into the light as basilicas and mosaics.
