# Video script — Christianity: From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A hunted faith paints in secret in the catacombs, in symbols only believers can read; then an emperor converts, the hunted become the state church, and the symbols come up into the light as basilicas and mosaics.

The first Christian art is hidden art. The faith is hunted — its believers persecuted — so they meet and bury their dead underground, in the catacombs, and paint there in symbols only believers can read: signs that speak to the faithful and say nothing to the hunter. A whole art of secrecy grows in the dark.

Then comes one of history's great turns: an emperor converts. Constantine takes the faith, the hunted become the state church, and everything changes place. The symbols come up out of the catacombs into the light, and the faith builds openly — great basilicas for worship, and mosaics shining on their walls. The same signs, first whispered underground, now proclaimed in gold above ground.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
