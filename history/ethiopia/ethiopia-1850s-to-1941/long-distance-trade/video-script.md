# Video script — The Long-Distance Trade — Bonga, Amole and Maria Theresa Thalers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The peoples and polities of southern, northern and eastern Ethiopia and the Horn were connected by two major trade routes starting from Bonga, capital of Kafa; Muslim traders known as Afkala and Jabarti (Jeberti) played the pivotal role; salt bars (amole) and Maria Theresa thalers served as the medium of exchange; and trade itself was the most important catalyst in relations between peoples and polities — routes, major market centres, trade items and people's interaction are the lesson's four contents.
