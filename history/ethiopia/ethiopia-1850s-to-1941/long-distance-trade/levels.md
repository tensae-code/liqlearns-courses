# The Long-Distance Trade — Bonga, Amole and Maria Theresa Thalers — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The peoples and polities of southern, northern and eastern Ethiopia and the Horn were connected by two major trade routes starting from Bonga, capital of Kafa; Muslim traders known as Afkala and Jabarti (Jeberti) played the pivotal role; salt bars (amole) and Maria Theresa thalers served as the medium of exchange; and trade itself was the most important catalyst in relations between peoples and polities — routes, major market centres, trade items and people's interaction are the lesson's four contents. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
