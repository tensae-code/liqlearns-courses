# Repulsing Three Aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ethiopia's survival was tested in sequence: the British military expedition under Sir Robert Napier, arising from Tewodros's modernisation drive and his detention of envoys, which ended with Tewodros's death; Egypt's attempt to dominate the Nile basin and the country, repulsed decisively at Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876); the Hewett Treaty and its results; Italian encroachment in the north, repulsed through the 1880s; and the Mahdist invasion, which consumed Emperor Yohannes IV's life in 1889 — unity in defence of sovereignty is the lesson's theme.
