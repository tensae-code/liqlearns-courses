# Repulsing Three Aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Ethiopia's survival was tested in sequence: the British military expedition under Sir Robert Napier, arising from Tewodros's modernisation drive and his detention of envoys, which ended with Tewodros's death; Egypt's attempt to dominate the Nile basin and the country, repulsed decisively at Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876); the Hewett Treaty and its results; Italian encroachment in the north, repulsed through the 1880s; and the Mahdist invasion, which consumed Emperor Yohannes IV's life in 1889 — unity in defence of sovereignty is the lesson's theme.

**2.** What does the part "External relations, challenges and threats" teach?
**Answer:** Egypt's invasions (defeated at Gundet and Gura, 1875–76), the Mahdist clash, and Italy's advance from Massawa through the Treaty of Wuchale's fatal translation dispute to war.
