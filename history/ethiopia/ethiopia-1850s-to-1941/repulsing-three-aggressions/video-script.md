# Video script — Repulsing Three Aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ethiopia's survival was tested in sequence: the British military expedition under Sir Robert Napier, arising from Tewodros's modernisation drive and his detention of envoys, which ended with Tewodros's death; Egypt's attempt to dominate the Nile basin and the country, repulsed decisively at Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876); the Hewett Treaty and its results; Italian encroachment in the north, repulsed through the 1880s; and the Mahdist invasion, which consumed Emperor Yohannes IV's life in 1889 — unity in defence of sovereignty is the lesson's theme.
