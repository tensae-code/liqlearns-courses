# Video script — Kumsa Moreda and Leqa Nekemte — Incorporation by Submission

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Unit 9's Activity 9.2 sends pupils to research Kumsa Moreda, and the guide's answer supplies the content: a hereditary ruler of the Leqa Nekemte Oromo state in present-day Wollega, whose state submitted peacefully to Menelik's forces, its rulers keeping power after baptism with traditional titles such as Dejazmach — a peaceful-incorporation case that extends the student book's Unit 3 expansion lesson and appears in the student book nowhere in this form.
