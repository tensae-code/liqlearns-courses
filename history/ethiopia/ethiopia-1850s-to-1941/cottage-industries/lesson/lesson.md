## Cottage industries — and the artisans' low status

Ethiopia's cottage industries are immemorial: metalworking, pottery, tannery, carpentry, masonry, weaving, jewellery and basket-making, with blacksmiths manufacturing a wide variety of agricultural implements. The lesson's social point is sharp: the general public disdained and marginalised artisans, and consequently locally produced implements and household furniture showed little improvement or sophistication — a technology held back by stigma.
