# 1935 — Fascist Italy Returns

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: 1935 — Fascist Italy Returns. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Fascist Italy's aggression, 1935–36

Despite Adwa, Italy never gave up its desire for Ethiopia: under Mussolini, Fascist Italy invaded in the Second Italo-Ethiopian War — its causes, and the major fronts of the invasion (the book maps the Italian advances), against an Ethiopia whose emperor's plea the League of Nations met with deaf ears (the lesson's own brainstorming question).
