# Video script — Menilek II — the Present Shape of Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Menilek II is credited with unprecedented territorial expansion and the creation of the present shape of Ethiopia — through forceful subjugation, peaceful submission and boundary-delimitation treaties combined — together with the centralisation and modernisation attempts (administrative and religious reforms, the founding order of the modern state) that close the 1855–1913 making-of-the-state lesson.
