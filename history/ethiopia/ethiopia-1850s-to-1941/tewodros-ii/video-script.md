# Video script — Tewodros II — Ending the Era of Warlords

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The making of modern Ethiopia ran on twin processes: unification of the northern and north-central regions and principalities, and territorial expansion into the southern half.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The making of modern Ethiopia ran on twin processes: unification of the northern and north-central regions and principalities, and territorial expansion into the southern half.

Tewodros II reunifies by force and falls at Maqdala (1868); Yohannes IV holds the north against Egypt, Mahdists and Italy and falls at Metemma (1889); Menelik II succeeds and builds the modern central state.

The guide's activity feedback gives the reform programme the student book summarises: Tewodros reformed administration, religion and the military, the military reform being the most important and least ambivalent — in organisation, discipline and armament.

Remember it this way: The making of modern Ethiopia ran on twin processes: unification of the northern and north-central regions and principalities, and territorial expansion into the southern half.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
