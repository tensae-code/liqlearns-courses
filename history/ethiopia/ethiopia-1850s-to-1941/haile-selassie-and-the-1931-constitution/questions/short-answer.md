# Haile Selassie Consolidates — the 1931 Constitution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The struggle's resolution: the diarchy and then the consolidation by which Haile Selassie, crowned emperor in 1930, built absolute power — regional rulers losing their autonomy in the 1930s, and the 1931 constitution (whose features pupils must state, and which Unit 6 will contrast with 1955's) giving the centralised monarchy its legal frame.
