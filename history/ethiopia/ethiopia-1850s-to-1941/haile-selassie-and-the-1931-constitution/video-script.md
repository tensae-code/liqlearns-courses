# Video script — Haile Selassie Consolidates — the 1931 Constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The struggle's resolution: the diarchy and then the consolidation by which Haile Selassie, crowned emperor in 1930, built absolute power — regional rulers losing their autonomy in the 1930s, and the 1931 constitution (whose features pupils must state, and which Unit 6 will contrast with 1955's) giving the centralised monarchy its legal frame.
