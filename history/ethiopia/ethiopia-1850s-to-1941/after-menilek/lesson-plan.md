# Lesson plan — After Menilek — Succession and the Coming Invasion

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia, Mid-19th Century to 1941 · **Lesson:** After Menilek — Succession and the Coming Invasion
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 11 — Ethiopia, Mid-19th Century to 1941 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From 1906 the ruling elites' power struggle opens: the measures Menilek took to avert a struggle among his successors, and then the reign and reforms of Lij Iyasu, his designated heir — the first act of the 1906–1935 succession drama.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 11 — Ethiopia, Mid-19th Century to 1941 (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
