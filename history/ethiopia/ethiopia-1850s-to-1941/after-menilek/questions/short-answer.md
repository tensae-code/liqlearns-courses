# After Menilek — Succession and the Coming Invasion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** From 1906 the ruling elites' power struggle opens: the measures Menilek took to avert a struggle among his successors, and then the reign and reforms of Lij Iyasu, his designated heir — the first act of the 1906–1935 succession drama.

**2.** What does the part "Power struggle among the ruling elites, 1906–1935" teach?
**Answer:** Menelik's illness opens succession struggles: Iyasu V's contested reign and deposition (1916), Zewditu's empress-ship with Ras Teferi (Haile Selassie) as regent, his coronation as emperor (1930), and the road to the 1935 Italian invasion that closes the unit's 1941 frame.
