# After Menilek — Succession and the Coming Invasion

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: After Menilek — Succession and the Coming Invasion. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The succession struggle begins — Menilek's precautions and Lij Iyasu

From 1906 the ruling elites' power struggle opens: the measures Menilek took to avert a struggle among his successors, and then the reign and reforms of Lij Iyasu, his designated heir — the first act of the 1906–1935 succession drama.

## Power struggle among the ruling elites, 1906–1935

Menelik's illness opens succession struggles: Iyasu V's contested reign and deposition (1916), Zewditu's empress-ship with Ras Teferi (Haile Selassie) as regent, his coronation as emperor (1930), and the road to the 1935 Italian invasion that closes the unit's 1941 frame.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
