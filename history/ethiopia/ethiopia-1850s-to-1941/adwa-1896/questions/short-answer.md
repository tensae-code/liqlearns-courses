# Adwa 1896 — the Victory That Echoed Worldwide — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Italy's colonising attempt turned on the Treaty of Wuchale, whose Article XVII differed between its Amharic and Italian versions (pupils must elaborate the difference) — the disputed clause Italy read as a protectorate.

**2.** What does the part "The Victory of Adwa" teach?
**Answer:** On 1 March 1896 Ethiopia destroys the Italian invasion at Adwa: mobilisation of the whole empire, Ras Makonnen, Alula and Taytu's roles, and Adwa's world echo as a symbol of anti-colonial resistance, sealed by the Treaty of Addis Ababa.
