# Adwa 1896 — the Victory That Echoed Worldwide

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Adwa 1896 — the Victory That Echoed Worldwide. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Wuchale and Adwa

Italy's colonising attempt turned on the Treaty of Wuchale, whose Article XVII differed between its Amharic and Italian versions (pupils must elaborate the difference) — the disputed clause Italy read as a protectorate. The attempt was settled at the Battle of Adwa (1896): the first decisive victory of Black people over a powerful white European power, a source of pride and motivation for freedom fighters worldwide, which brought down Premier Francesco Crispi's government in Italy.

## The Victory of Adwa

On 1 March 1896 Ethiopia destroys the Italian invasion at Adwa: mobilisation of the whole empire, Ras Makonnen, Alula and Taytu's roles, and Adwa's world echo as a symbol of anti-colonial resistance, sealed by the Treaty of Addis Ababa.
