## Yohannes IV — decentralisation and religious settlement

After Tewodros's fall, three contenders — Wagshum Gobeze of Lasta (whose reign was too short and uneventful), Kassa Mircha of Tigray and Menilek of Shewa — came to the throne in turn. Yohannes IV (Kassa Mircha) is renowned for attempting a decentralised system of administration and for terminating the religious controversy within the Ethiopian Orthodox Church — a unifier who ruled by accommodating regional power rather than crushing it.
