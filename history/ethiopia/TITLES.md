# History — Ethiopia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Ethiopia's own —
built from the Ministry of Education's standalone History
student textbooks for Grades 9–12, with their teacher guides
as labelled evidence. Inside the course the organisation is
by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their
levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grade each book comes from lives only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the historian's craft and
human origins first, then the alternating world–Ethiopia
sweep — ancient world, Ethiopia and the Horn, the medieval
world, Africa, the slave-trade centuries, Ethiopia's own
states and systems century by century, revolution — and then
the modern sweep told the same way, closing where the books
close: on Ethiopia's indigenous knowledge and heritages.
That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced —
a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints
point down this ladder).
