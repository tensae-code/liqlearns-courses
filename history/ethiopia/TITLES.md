# History — Ethiopia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Ethiopia's own —
built from Ethiopia's Social Studies student books for Grades
7–8, its History student books for Grades 9–12 and its
Economics student books for Grades 9–12, with teacher guides
as labelled evidence. Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their
levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grade each book comes from lives only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the ancient land first
(Punt, D'aamat, Aksum), then the medieval kingdoms and
sultanates side by side, then the making of modern Ethiopia —
unification, the invasions beaten back, Adwa — then the hard
twentieth century whole (occupation, liberation, empire,
revolution, federation), and last the economy the same books
teach: development measured, three systems in a century, and
the plans, strategies and enterprise since 1991. That ladder
IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder).
