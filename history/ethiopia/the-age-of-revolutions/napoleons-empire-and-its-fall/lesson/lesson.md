## The Napoleonic era — empire and downfall

Between 1805 and 1808 Napoleon built a large European empire — Belgium, Spain, Holland, Switzerland, Italy, the Rhine German states, Poland and France, with Russia, Prussia, Denmark and Norway as allies — spreading democratic ideals even as he ruled, and awakening national resistance among the conquered. Britain resisted throughout: Nelson destroyed the allied fleets at Trafalgar (1805); Napoleon's Continental System blockade (1806–1810) failed to force Britain to submit; Russia gave him his strongest resistance. Defeat ended in exile to St Helena in the South Atlantic, where he died in 1821; his final defeat was at Waterloo (review).
