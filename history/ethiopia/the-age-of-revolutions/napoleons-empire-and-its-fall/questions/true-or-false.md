# Napoleon's Empire — and Why It Fell — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Between 1805 and 1808 Napoleon built a large European empire — Belgium, Spain, Holland, Switzerland, Italy, the Rhine German states, Poland and France, with Russia, Prussia, Denmark and Norway as allies — spreading democratic ideals even as he ruled, and awakening national resistance among the conquered.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: America's War of Independence — Thirteen Colonies Become the USA.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Between 1805 and 1808 Napoleon built a large European empire — Belgium, Spain, Holland, Switzerland, Italy, the Rhine German states, Poland and France, with Russia, Prussia, Denmark and Norway as allies — spreading democratic ideals even as he ruled, and awakening national resistance among the conquered.
