# Napoleon's Empire — and Why It Fell — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Between 1805 and 1808 Napoleon built a large European empire — Belgium, Spain, Holland, Switzerland, Italy, the Rhine German states, Poland and France, with Russia, Prussia, Denmark and Norway as allies — spreading democratic ideals even as he ruled, and awakening national resistance among the conquered.

**2.** What does the part "Napoleon verdict as content" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's key characterises the Napoleonic period's double effect — France a major power during it, weakened after, while Austria-Hungary and other European powers' positions shifted — the analytical close of the course, matching the student book's peace coda placement.
