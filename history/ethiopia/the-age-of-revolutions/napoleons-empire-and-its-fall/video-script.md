# Video script — Napoleon's Empire — and Why It Fell

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Between 1805 and 1808 Napoleon built a large European empire — Belgium, Spain, Holland, Switzerland, Italy, the Rhine German states, Poland and France, with Russia, Prussia, Denmark and Norway as allies — spreading democratic ideals even as he ruled, and awakening national resistance among the conquered.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Between 1805 and 1808 Napoleon built a large European empire — Belgium, Spain, Holland, Switzerland, Italy, the Rhine German states, Poland and France, with Russia, Prussia, Denmark and Norway as allies — spreading democratic ideals even as he ruled, and awakening national resistance among the conquered.

The guide's key characterises the Napoleonic period's double effect — France a major power during it, weakened after, while Austria-Hungary and other European powers' positions shifted — the analytical close of the course, matching the student book's peace coda placement.

Remember it this way: Between 1805 and 1808 Napoleon built a large European empire — Belgium, Spain, Holland, Switzerland, Italy, the Rhine German states, Poland and France, with Russia, Prussia, Denmark and Norway as allies — spreading democratic ideals even as he ruled, and awakening national resistance among the conquered.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
