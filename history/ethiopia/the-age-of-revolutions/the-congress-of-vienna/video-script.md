# Video script — Vienna 1815 — Redrawing Europe, Ignoring Nations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After Napoleon's final defeat, virtually every European state sent delegates to Vienna (arriving from September 1814) to build a long-term peace: Austria's Metternich hosted, with Tsar Alexander I for Russia, Hardenberg for Prussia, Castlereagh (then Wellington) for Britain and Talleyrand for restored Bourbon France.
