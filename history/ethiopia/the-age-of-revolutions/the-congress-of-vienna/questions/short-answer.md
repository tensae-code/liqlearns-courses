# Vienna 1815 — Redrawing Europe, Ignoring Nations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** After Napoleon's final defeat, virtually every European state sent delegates to Vienna (arriving from September 1814) to build a long-term peace: Austria's Metternich hosted, with Tsar Alexander I for Russia, Hardenberg for Prussia, Castlereagh (then Wellington) for Britain and Talleyrand for restored Bourbon France.

**2.** What does the part "Peace coda" teach?
**Answer:** The book's final text closes Unit 9 not on Napoleon but on a stated moral: war is destructive in all its forms and peace is the precondition of industrialisation and democratisation — the curriculum's own closing value, preserved verbatim in intent as a summary placement.
