# Lesson plan — France Before 1789 — Three Estates and a Breaking Order

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** The Age of Revolutions, 1750s–1815 · **Lesson:** France Before 1789 — Three Estates and a Breaking Order
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 9 — The Age of Revolutions, 1750s–1815 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pre-revolutionary France was a feudal, oppressed society divided into three Estates: the First (clergy) and Second (nobility) owned vast land, held every privilege and paid no taxes; the Third — peasants and bourgeoisie together, the largest section — owned little land, held no rights and paid heavy taxes.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 9 — The Age of Revolutions, 1750s–1815 (recommended, never forced).
