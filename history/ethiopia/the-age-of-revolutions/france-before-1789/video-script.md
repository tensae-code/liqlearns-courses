# Video script — France Before 1789 — Three Estates and a Breaking Order

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Pre-revolutionary France was a feudal, oppressed society divided into three Estates: the First (clergy) and Second (nobility) owned vast land, held every privilege and paid no taxes; the Third — peasants and bourgeoisie together, the largest section — owned little land, held no rights and paid heavy taxes.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
