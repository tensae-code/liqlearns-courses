# France Before 1789 — Three Estates and a Breaking Order — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pre-revolutionary France was a feudal, oppressed society divided into three Estates: the First (clergy) and Second (nobility) owned vast land, held every privilege and paid no taxes; the Third — peasants and bourgeoisie together, the largest section — owned little land, held no rights and paid heavy taxes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: 1789–1799 — Rights of Man, Republic, Terror.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Pre-revolutionary France was a feudal, oppressed society divided into three Estates: the First (clergy) and Second (nobility) owned vast land, held every privilege and paid no taxes; the Third — peasants and bourgeoisie together, the largest section — owned little land, held no rights and paid heavy taxes.
