# France Before 1789 — Three Estates and a Breaking Order — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Pre-revolutionary France was a feudal, oppressed society divided into three Estates: the First (clergy) and Second (nobility) owned vast land, held every privilege and paid no taxes; the Third — peasants and bourgeoisie together, the largest section — owned little land, held no rights and paid heavy taxes.

**2.** What does the part "The French Revolution — causes and outbreak" teach?
**Answer:** France's old regime — estates, royal debt, bread prices — collapses in 1789: Estates-General, Tennis Court Oath, Bastille, Declaration of the Rights of Man.
