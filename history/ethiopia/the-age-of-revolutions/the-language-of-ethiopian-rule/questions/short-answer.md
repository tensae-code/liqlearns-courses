# The Language of Ethiopian Rule — a Terms Reference — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The book closes with a glossary of the Ethiopian titles and institutions its text depends on — Abbaa, Abbaa Gadaa, Abbaa Muudaa, Abbaa Duulaa, Balabbat, Balambaras, Butta ceremony, Chaffe, Dejjazmach, Etege, Fitawurari, Gabbar, Gadaa, Geber, Gult, Guddifacha, Grazmach, Jeberti, Janissaries, Kawo, Luwa, Manor, Moggasa, Odaa, Negadras, Nigus, Qaalluu, Qegnazmach, Ras, Rist, Tato, Sera, Woma — a reference lesson in itself: the learner who masters these terms holds the keys to Units 3, 7 and 8.
