# The Language of Ethiopian Rule — a Terms Reference

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Language of Ethiopian Rule — a Terms Reference. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Glossary — Ethiopia's own vocabulary of rule

The book closes with a glossary of the Ethiopian titles and institutions its text depends on — Abbaa, Abbaa Gadaa, Abbaa Muudaa, Abbaa Duulaa, Balabbat, Balambaras, Butta ceremony, Chaffe, Dejjazmach, Etege, Fitawurari, Gabbar, Gadaa, Geber, Gult, Guddifacha, Grazmach, Jeberti, Janissaries, Kawo, Luwa, Manor, Moggasa, Odaa, Negadras, Nigus, Qaalluu, Qegnazmach, Ras, Rist, Tato, Sera, Woma — a reference lesson in itself: the learner who masters these terms holds the keys to Units 3, 7 and 8.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
