# Video script — America's War of Independence — Thirteen Colonies Become the USA

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The war of 1775–1783, by which thirteen British colonies won independence: the colonies' congresses organised non-consumption of British imports, met at Philadelphia (10 May 1775) to create the Army of the United Colonies under George Washington, and in 1776 declared independence in the document drafted by Thomas Jefferson; the federalists and anti-federalists finally joined to ratify the constitution on 25 June (1788).
