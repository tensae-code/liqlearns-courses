# America's War of Independence — Thirteen Colonies Become the USA

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: America's War of Independence — Thirteen Colonies Become the USA. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The American War of Independence (Book 1 telling)

The war of 1775–1783, by which thirteen British colonies won independence: the colonies' congresses organised non-consumption of British imports, met at Philadelphia (10 May 1775) to create the Army of the United Colonies under George Washington, and in 1776 declared independence in the document drafted by Thomas Jefferson; the federalists and anti-federalists finally joined to ratify the constitution on 25 June (1788). The war liberated the American people, created the USA, and highly influenced France and Latin America — though it did not abolish slavery (review).

## The American War of Independence (Book 3 telling)

Britain's thirteen colonies revolt over taxation and representation, declare independence (1776, Jefferson's Declaration), and win a new republic with French help.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
