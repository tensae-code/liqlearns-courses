# America's War of Independence — Thirteen Colonies Become the USA — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The war of 1775–1783, by which thirteen British colonies won independence: the colonies' congresses organised non-consumption of British imports, met at Philadelphia (10 May 1775) to create the Army of the United Colonies under George Washington, and in 1776 declared independence in the document drafted by Thomas Jefferson; the federalists and anti-federalists finally joined to ratify the constitution on 25 June (1788).

**2.** What does the part "The American War of Independence (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** Britain's thirteen colonies revolt over taxation and representation, declare independence (1776, Jefferson's Declaration), and win a new republic with French help.
