The Industrial Revolution (begun in late-1700s Britain, spreading slowly worldwide) created industrial capitalism: politically and economically it matured into laissez-faire, liberalism, competition and a free-market economy, with Britain by the mid-19th century "the world's workshop".

Political, economic and social effects systematised: urbanisation, population growth, a middle class and a working class, trade unions, socialism's rise, and the push to imperialism for markets and raw materials.

Remember it this way: The Industrial Revolution (begun in late-1700s Britain, spreading slowly worldwide) created industrial capitalism: politically and economically it matured into laissez-faire, liberalism, competition and a free-market economy, with Britain by the mid-19th century "the world's workshop".
