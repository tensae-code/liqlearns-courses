# Industrial Capitalism — Workshop of the World, Two New Classes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Industrial Revolution (begun in late-1700s Britain, spreading slowly worldwide) created industrial capitalism: politically and economically it matured into laissez-faire, liberalism, competition and a free-market economy, with Britain by the mid-19th century "the world's workshop".

**2.** What does the part "Effects of the Industrial Revolution" teach?
**Answer:** Political, economic and social effects systematised: urbanisation, population growth, a middle class and a working class, trade unions, socialism's rise, and the push to imperialism for markets and raw materials.
