# The Industrial Revolution — Steam, Factories, New Classes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Industrial Revolution means machines replacing hand labour: a shift from farming and handicrafts to factory manufacturing, beginning in Great Britain in the 1780s and spreading over decades.

**2.** What does the part "The Industrial Revolution — what changed" teach?
**Answer:** Beginning in Britain: steam, factories, coal and iron, new transport — why Britain first and what the factory system did to work.
