# Lesson plan — 1789–1799 — Rights of Man, Republic, Terror

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** The Age of Revolutions, 1750s–1815 · **Lesson:** 1789–1799 — Rights of Man, Republic, Terror
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 9 — The Age of Revolutions, 1750s–1815 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In sequence: the National Assembly formed; in August 1789 it declared the Rights of Man and of the Citizen; the 1791 constitution established a limited monarchy with voting restricted to tax-paying "active citizens" (the rest "passive"); the revolution radicalised into the Reign of Terror under the Jacobins and Robespierre, the guillotine executing counter-revolutionaries, Girondins and Danton's followers, alongside Jacobin reforms — until the revolutionary government itself was overthrown in 1799.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 9 — The Age of Revolutions, 1750s–1815 (recommended, never forced).
