# 1789–1799 — Rights of Man, Republic, Terror — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** In sequence: the National Assembly formed; in August 1789 it declared the Rights of Man and of the Citizen; the 1791 constitution established a limited monarchy with voting restricted to tax-paying "active citizens" (the rest "passive"); the revolution radicalised into the Reign of Terror under the Jacobins and Robespierre, the guillotine executing counter-revolutionaries, Girondins and Danton's followers, alongside Jacobin reforms — until the revolutionary government itself was overthrown in 1799.

**2.** What does the part "The French Revolution — republic, Terror and reaction" teach?
**Answer:** The monarchy falls, the republic fights Europe, the Committee of Public Safety runs the Reign of Terror (1793–94), and reaction follows Thermidor.
