# 1789–1799 — Rights of Man, Republic, Terror — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In sequence: the National Assembly formed; in August 1789 it declared the Rights of Man and of the Citizen; the 1791 constitution established a limited monarchy with voting restricted to tax-paying "active citizens" (the rest "passive"); the revolution radicalised into the Reign of Terror under the Jacobins and Robespierre, the guillotine executing counter-revolutionaries, Girondins and Danton's followers, alongside Jacobin reforms — until the revolutionary government itself was overthrown in 1799.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Napoleon — the Revolution's Heir and Its Emperor.
