# 1789–1799 — Rights of Man, Republic, Terror — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: In sequence: the National Assembly formed; in August 1789 it declared the Rights of Man and of the Citizen; the 1791 constitution established a limited monarchy with voting restricted to tax-paying "active citizens" (the rest "passive"); the revolution radicalised into the Reign of Terror under the Jacobins and Robespierre, the guillotine executing counter-revolutionaries, Girondins and Danton's followers, alongside Jacobin reforms — until the revolutionary government itself was overthrown in 1799. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
