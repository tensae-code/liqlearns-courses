# Napoleon — the Revolution's Heir and Its Emperor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Napoleon Bonaparte (born 1769 on Corsica; an army officer from his twenties, "the son of the revolution", famous from 1795 as the revolution's defender) seized power in 1799 and ruled France as a military dictatorship that nevertheless kept the Revolution's fruits, above all civil equality, and did great constructive work — without political democracy.

**2.** What does the part "Content the guide adds — Napoleon's road to power, and the French Revolution's effects list" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's Napoleon overview adds the career steps and domestic programme the student book compresses: victory over Austria in Italy in 1796 made his name; the short-lived conquest of Egypt 1798–1801 followed; as ruler he made employment and promotion in government depend on ability, and expanded education, opening state secondary schools and universities.
