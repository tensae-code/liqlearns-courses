# Napoleon — the Revolution's Heir and Its Emperor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Napoleon Bonaparte (born 1769 on Corsica; an army officer from his twenties, "the son of the revolution", famous from 1795 as the revolution's defender) seized power in 1799 and ruled France as a military dictatorship that nevertheless kept the Revolution's fruits, above all civil equality, and did great constructive work — without political democracy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Napoleon's Empire — and Why It Fell.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Napoleon Bonaparte (born 1769 on Corsica; an army officer from his twenties, "the son of the revolution", famous from 1795 as the revolution's defender) seized power in 1799 and ruled France as a military dictatorship that nevertheless kept the Revolution's fruits, above all civil equality, and did great constructive work — without political democracy.
