# Is History a Science?

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Is History a Science?. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## History among the sciences

History is compared with the natural sciences (no controlled experiments, value judgements present) and placed among the social sciences; it still counts as a science because claims must be proved from reliable sources by a shared method, with Ranke and the German Historical School named as founders of modern academic history.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
