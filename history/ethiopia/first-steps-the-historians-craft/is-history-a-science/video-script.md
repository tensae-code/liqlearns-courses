# Video script — Is History a Science?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is compared with the natural sciences (no controlled experiments, value judgements present) and placed among the social sciences; it still counts as a science because claims must be proved from reliable sources by a shared method, with Ranke and the German Historical School named as founders of modern academic history.
