# Is History a Science? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) History is compared with the natural sciences (no controlled experiments, value judgements present) and placed among the social sciences; it still counts as a science because claims must be proved from reliable sources by a shared method, with Ranke and the German Historical School named as founders of modern academic history.  b) Interpreting the Past — From Evidence to a Written History  c) Byzantium — the Eastern Empire That Lasted a Thousand Years  d) none of these
**Answer: a) History is compared with the natural sciences (no controlled experiments, value judgements present) and placed among the social sciences; it still counts as a science because claims must be proved from reliable sources by a shared method, with Ranke and the German Historical School named as founders of modern academic history.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?

**Answer: a) History among the sciences** — That is this lesson's first part.
