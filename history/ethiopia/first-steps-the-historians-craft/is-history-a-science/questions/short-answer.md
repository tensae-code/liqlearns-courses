# Is History a Science? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** History is compared with the natural sciences (no controlled experiments, value judgements present) and placed among the social sciences; it still counts as a science because claims must be proved from reliable sources by a shared method, with Ranke and the German Historical School named as founders of modern academic history.
