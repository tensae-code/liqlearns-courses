## Prehistory and history — where the line falls

History, from the Greek Istoria (inquiry), is the study of humans in the past; it is said to begin about 5,500 years ago when writing began. Everything before writing is prehistory, recovered not from documents but by archaeologists and anthropologists from material clues; history proper deals with the past from writing and recording onward, and centrally with how humans interacted with each other and with the natural environment to produce food, shelter and clothing.
