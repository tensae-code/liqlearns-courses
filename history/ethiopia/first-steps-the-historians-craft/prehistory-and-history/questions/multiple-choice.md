# Prehistory and History — the Writing Line — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) History, from the Greek Istoria (inquiry), is the study of humans in the past; it is said to begin about 5,500 years ago when writing began.  b) Fact or Opinion? Reading the Past Critically  c) The Dark Age — Rome Falls, Europe Goes Rural  d) none of these
**Answer: a) History, from the Greek Istoria (inquiry), is the study of humans in the past; it is said to begin about 5,500 years ago when writing began.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Prehistory and history — where the line falls  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Prehistory and history — where the line falls** — That is this lesson's first part.
