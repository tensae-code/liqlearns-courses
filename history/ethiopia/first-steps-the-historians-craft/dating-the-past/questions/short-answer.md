# Dating the Past — Calendars, Timelines and Periods — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Dating fixes when events happened; time is chunked into decade, century and millennium; chronology orders events by calendar.

**2.** What does the part "Chronology spine" teach?
**Answer:** The book's single timeline runs human origins → ancient civilisations → Ethiopia to the 13th century → medieval/early-modern world → Africa to 1500 → Africa and the outside world → Ethiopia 1270–1855 (units 7–8 overlapping the world chronology) → revolutions 1789–1815; pupils are expected to hold Ethiopian and world time side by side.
