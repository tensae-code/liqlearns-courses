# Human Evolution — Hominids and Ethiopia's Fossils — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** By the evolutionist account all living humans are Homo sapiens, descended within the genus Homo of the hominid family, whose members are known from fossils and are marked by bipedalism — walking upright on two feet.

**2.** What does the part "Stages of human evolution" teach?
**Answer:** The hominid sequence is taught in order — Australopithecus (Lucy/Dinknesh named for Ethiopia), Homo habilis, Homo erectus, Homo sapiens — with Ethiopia presented as a major centre of human evolution to be located on a world map.
