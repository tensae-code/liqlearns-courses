# Video script — Human Evolution — Hominids and Ethiopia's Fossils

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
By the evolutionist account all living humans are Homo sapiens, descended within the genus Homo of the hominid family, whose members are known from fossils and are marked by bipedalism — walking upright on two feet.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

By the evolutionist account all living humans are Homo sapiens, descended within the genus Homo of the hominid family, whose members are known from fossils and are marked by bipedalism — walking upright on two feet.

The hominid sequence is taught in order — Australopithecus (Lucy/Dinknesh named for Ethiopia), Homo habilis, Homo erectus, Homo sapiens — with Ethiopia presented as a major centre of human evolution to be located on a world map.

Remember it this way: By the evolutionist account all living humans are Homo sapiens, descended within the genus Homo of the hominid family, whose members are known from fossils and are marked by bipedalism — walking upright on two feet.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
