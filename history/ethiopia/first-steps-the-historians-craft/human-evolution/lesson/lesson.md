## The evolution of human beings — hominids and Ethiopia's fossils

By the evolutionist account all living humans are Homo sapiens, descended within the genus Homo of the hominid family, whose members are known from fossils and are marked by bipedalism — walking upright on two feet. Ethiopia's finds anchor the story: the earliest hominid found in Ethiopia, from Aramis in the Middle Awash (Afar), dated about 4.4 million years ago and described by Tim White's team, followed by Australopithecus afarensis, with Donald Johanson's 1974 find presented as the oldest complete skeleton then known.

## Stages of human evolution

The hominid sequence is taught in order — Australopithecus (Lucy/Dinknesh named for Ethiopia), Homo habilis, Homo erectus, Homo sapiens — with Ethiopia presented as a major centre of human evolution to be located on a world map.
