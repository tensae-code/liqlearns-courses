# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History is defined as the organised, critical study of the human past from evidence — the word traced to Greek *istoria*, inquiry — covering pre-history before writing (c.4000 BC) as well as the written past, and dealing in change and continuity and cause and effect.
