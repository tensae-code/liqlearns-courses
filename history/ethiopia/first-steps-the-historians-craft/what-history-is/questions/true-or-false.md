# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History is defined as the organised, critical study of the human past from evidence — the word traced to Greek *istoria*, inquiry — covering pre-history before writing (c.4000 BC) as well as the written past, and dealing in change and continuity and cause and effect.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Prehistory and History — the Writing Line.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: History is defined as the organised, critical study of the human past from evidence — the word traced to Greek *istoria*, inquiry — covering pre-history before writing (c.4000 BC) as well as the written past, and dealing in change and continuity and cause and effect.
