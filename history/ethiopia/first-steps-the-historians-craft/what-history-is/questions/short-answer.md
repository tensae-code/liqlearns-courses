# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** History is defined as the organised, critical study of the human past from evidence — the word traced to Greek *istoria*, inquiry — covering pre-history before writing (c.4000 BC) as well as the written past, and dealing in change and continuity and cause and effect.

**2.** What does the part "What history is" teach?
