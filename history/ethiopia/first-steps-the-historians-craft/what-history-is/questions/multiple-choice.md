Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) History is defined as the organised, critical study of the human past from evidence — the word traced to Greek *istoria*, inquiry — covering pre-history before writing (c.4000 BC) as well as the written past, and dealing in change and continuity and cause and effect.  b) Prehistory and History — the Writing Line  c) The Kingdom of Damot  d) none of these
