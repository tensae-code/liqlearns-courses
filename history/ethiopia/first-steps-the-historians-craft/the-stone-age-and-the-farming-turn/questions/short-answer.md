# The Stone Age and the Farming Turn — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Tool-making distinguishes humans; the earliest tools were stone.

**2.** What does the part "Early human culture — tools, fire and the Neolithic Revolution" teach?
**Answer:** Stone-tool ages (Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic), the control of fire, and the Neolithic Revolution — farming and animal domestication — as the turn from food gathering to food production.
