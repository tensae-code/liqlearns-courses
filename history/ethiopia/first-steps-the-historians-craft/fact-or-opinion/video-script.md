# Video script — Fact or Opinion? Reading the Past Critically

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History as a body of knowledge is an account of the past built on facts and evidence, not a novel or an opinion: a fact can be proved with supporting information, an opinion is a writer's unprovable viewpoint, and telling them apart by critical thinking is presented as one of the core skills history teaches.
