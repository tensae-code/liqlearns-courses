# Fact or Opinion? Reading the Past Critically — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** History as a body of knowledge is an account of the past built on facts and evidence, not a novel or an opinion: a fact can be proved with supporting information, an opinion is a writer's unprovable viewpoint, and telling them apart by critical thinking is presented as one of the core skills history teaches.
