# Fact or Opinion? Reading the Past Critically — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) History as a body of knowledge is an account of the past built on facts and evidence, not a novel or an opinion: a fact can be proved with supporting information, an opinion is a writer's unprovable viewpoint, and telling them apart by critical thinking is presented as one of the core skills history teaches.  b) Is History a Science?  c) Two Romes — the Fallen West and the Byzantine East  d) none of these
**Answer: a) History as a body of knowledge is an account of the past built on facts and evidence, not a novel or an opinion: a fact can be proved with supporting information, an opinion is a writer's unprovable viewpoint, and telling them apart by critical thinking is presented as one of the core skills history teaches.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Fact, opinion and history as inquiry  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Fact, opinion and history as inquiry** — That is this lesson's first part.
