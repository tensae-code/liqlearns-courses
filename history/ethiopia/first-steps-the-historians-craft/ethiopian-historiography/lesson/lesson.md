## Ethiopian historiography

Ethiopia's own writing tradition is taught as content: royal chronicles from the 14th century recording emperors' deeds almost uninterruptedly to the early 20th century, plus hagiographies (saints' lives) as a second source tradition — their strengths and their court-centred bias.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
