# Video script — Ethiopian Historiography — Chronicles and Hagiographies as Sources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ethiopia's own writing tradition is taught as content: royal chronicles from the 14th century recording emperors' deeds almost uninterruptedly to the early 20th century, plus hagiographies (saints' lives) as a second source tradition — their strengths and their court-centred bias.
