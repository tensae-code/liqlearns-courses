# Ethiopian Historiography — Chronicles and Hagiographies as Sources — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Ethiopia's own writing tradition is taught as content: royal chronicles from the 14th century recording emperors' deeds almost uninterruptedly to the early 20th century, plus hagiographies (saints' lives) as a second source tradition — their strengths and their court-centred bias.
