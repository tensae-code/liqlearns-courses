Pass mark: Historiography studies how knowledge of the past is attained and transmitted: the oldest written history is placed in China (records before 1000 BC); Herodotus is named father of history and Thucydides its first critical writer; history became an academic discipline in the later 19th century, with Leopold von Ranke as father of modern historiography; Ethiopia had its own indigenous chronicle tradition, changed in the early 20th century, and Ethiopian academic history dates from the 1960s. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
