# Historiography — How History-Writing Grew — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Historiography studies how knowledge of the past is attained and transmitted: the oldest written history is placed in China (records before 1000 BC); Herodotus is named father of history and Thucydides its first critical writer; history became an academic discipline in the later 19th century, with Leopold von Ranke as father of modern historiography; Ethiopia had its own indigenous chronicle tradition, changed in the early 20th century, and Ethiopian academic history dates from the 1960s.

**2.** What does the part "Historiography marking position" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's keys fix the expected answers on the discipline's founders and terms (istoria, the father of history, change and continuity, abuse of history) matching student placement 2–4 — no divergence.
