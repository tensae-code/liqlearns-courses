# Video script — Historiography — How History-Writing Grew

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Historiography studies how knowledge of the past is attained and transmitted: the oldest written history is placed in China (records before 1000 BC); Herodotus is named father of history and Thucydides its first critical writer; history became an academic discipline in the later 19th century, with Leopold von Ranke as father of modern historiography; Ethiopia had its own indigenous chronicle tradition, changed in the early 20th century, and Ethiopian academic history dates from the 1960s.
