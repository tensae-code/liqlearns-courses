# Creation and Evolution — Two Accounts of Human Origin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The creationist view holds that humans and all living things were created by God or a supernatural being; the evolutionist (scientific) view, formulated by Charles Darwin (1809–1882), holds that living things including humans arose by gradual succession and variation from earlier forms.  b) Africa, Cradle of Humankind  c) The Reformation — Luther and the Splitting of the Church  d) none of these
**Answer: a) The creationist view holds that humans and all living things were created by God or a supernatural being; the evolutionist (scientific) view, formulated by Charles Darwin (1809–1882), holds that living things including humans arose by gradual succession and variation from earlier forms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Two theories of human origin  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Two theories of human origin** — That is this lesson's first part.
