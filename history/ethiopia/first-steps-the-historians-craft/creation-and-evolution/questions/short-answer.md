# Creation and Evolution — Two Accounts of Human Origin — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The creationist view holds that humans and all living things were created by God or a supernatural being; the evolutionist (scientific) view, formulated by Charles Darwin (1809–1882), holds that living things including humans arose by gradual succession and variation from earlier forms.

**2.** What does the part "Evolution marking position" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's Unit 1 keys handle the creation-versus-evolution question by instructing the teacher to have pupils decide between the Biblical notion and the scientific view on the evidence as presented — the official handling of a sensitive point, recorded as teacher evidence.
