# Video script — Creation and Evolution — Two Accounts of Human Origin

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The creationist view holds that humans and all living things were created by God or a supernatural being; the evolutionist (scientific) view, formulated by Charles Darwin (1809–1882), holds that living things including humans arose by gradual succession and variation from earlier forms.

Creation accounts and the scientific theory of evolution are set side by side as the two families of explanation for human origins, with Darwin and natural selection introduced.

The guide's Unit 1 keys handle the creation-versus-evolution question by instructing the teacher to have pupils decide between the Biblical notion and the scientific view on the evidence as presented — the official handling of a sensitive point, recorded as teacher evidence.
