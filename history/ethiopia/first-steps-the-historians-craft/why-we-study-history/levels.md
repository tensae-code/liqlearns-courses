# Why We Study History — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: History is taught as knowing the past in order to understand the present and foresee future developments; it builds national and international understanding, democratic principles and nationalism, trains research skills (collecting and analysing information to reach conclusions), gives a sense of identity, and forms conscious citizens who take part in shaping their future. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
