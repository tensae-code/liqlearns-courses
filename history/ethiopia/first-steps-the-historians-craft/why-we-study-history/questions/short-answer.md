# Why We Study History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** History is taught as knowing the past in order to understand the present and foresee future developments; it builds national and international understanding, democratic principles and nationalism, trains research skills (collecting and analysing information to reach conclusions), gives a sense of identity, and forms conscious citizens who take part in shaping their future.
