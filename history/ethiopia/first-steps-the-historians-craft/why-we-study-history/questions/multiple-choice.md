**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) History is taught as knowing the past in order to understand the present and foresee future developments; it builds national and international understanding, democratic principles and nationalism, trains research skills (collecting and analysing information to reach conclusions), gives a sense of identity, and forms conscious citizens who take part in shaping their future.  b) Historiography — How History-Writing Grew  c) The Rise and Expansion of Islam  d) none of these
