# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Africa is presented as the centre of the human-origins evidence: the oldest australopithecine, from northern Chad (found 2001, 7–6 million years old); Ardipithecus ramidus fossils aged 4.4 million years from Afar (1994); and Australopithecus afarensis — Lucy, Dinkinesh — found at Hadar in Afar in 1974, about 3.18 million years old.
