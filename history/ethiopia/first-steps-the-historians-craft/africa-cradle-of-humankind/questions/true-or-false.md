# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Africa is presented as the centre of the human-origins evidence: the oldest australopithecine, from northern Chad (found 2001, 7–6 million years old); Ardipithecus ramidus fossils aged 4.4 million years from Afar (1994); and Australopithecus afarensis — Lucy, Dinkinesh — found at Hadar in Afar in 1974, about 3.18 million years old.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: The Stone Age and the Farming Turn.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Africa is presented as the centre of the human-origins evidence: the oldest australopithecine, from northern Chad (found 2001, 7–6 million years old); Ardipithecus ramidus fossils aged 4.4 million years from Afar (1994); and Australopithecus afarensis — Lucy, Dinkinesh — found at Hadar in Afar in 1974, about 3.18 million years old.
