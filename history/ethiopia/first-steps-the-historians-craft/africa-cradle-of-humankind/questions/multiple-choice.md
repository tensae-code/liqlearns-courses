# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Africa is presented as the centre of the human-origins evidence: the oldest australopithecine, from northern Chad (found 2001, 7–6 million years old); Ardipithecus ramidus fossils aged 4.4 million years from Afar (1994); and Australopithecus afarensis — Lucy, Dinkinesh — found at Hadar in Afar in 1974, about 3.18 million years old.  b) The Stone Age and the Farming Turn  c) Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Africa is presented as the centre of the human-origins evidence: the oldest australopithecine, from northern Chad (found 2001, 7–6 million years old); Ardipithecus ramidus fossils aged 4.4 million years from Afar (1994); and Australopithecus afarensis — Lucy, Dinkinesh — found at Hadar in Afar in 1974, about 3.18 million years old.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Africa and human evolution — the continent of first finds  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Africa and human evolution — the continent of first finds** — That is this lesson's first part.
