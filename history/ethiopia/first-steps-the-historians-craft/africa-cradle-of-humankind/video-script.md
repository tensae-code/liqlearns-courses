# Video script — Africa, Cradle of Humankind

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Africa is presented as the centre of the human-origins evidence: the oldest australopithecine, from northern Chad (found 2001, 7–6 million years old); Ardipithecus ramidus fossils aged 4.4 million years from Afar (1994); and Australopithecus afarensis — Lucy, Dinkinesh — found at Hadar in Afar in 1974, about 3.18 million years old.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
