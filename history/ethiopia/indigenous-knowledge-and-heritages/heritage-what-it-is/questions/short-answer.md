# Heritage — What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Heritage is what can be passed from generation to generation, conserved or inherited, with historic or cultural value: it can be a physical object, property, building or place that is owned and passed on — and just as truly a practice: language, culture, popular song, literature and dress help us comprehend who we are as much as artefacts and buildings.

**2.** What does the part "Local-heritage identification task (guide-only task design)" teach?
**Answer:** Activity 9.3's guided group task — identify tangible and intangible heritage sites in the pupils' own town, zone or region, with the teacher helping the identification — is a ready-made local-fieldwork lesson the student book only implies.
