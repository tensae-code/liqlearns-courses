# Heritage — What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Heritage is what can be passed from generation to generation, conserved or inherited, with historic or cultural value: it can be a physical object, property, building or place that is owned and passed on — and just as truly a practice: language, culture, popular song, literature and dress help us comprehend who we are as much as artefacts and buildings.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Three Kinds of Heritage — Nature, Practice, Thing.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Heritage is what can be passed from generation to generation, conserved or inherited, with historic or cultural value: it can be a physical object, property, building or place that is owned and passed on — and just as truly a practice: language, culture, popular song, literature and dress help us comprehend who we are as much as artefacts and buildings.
