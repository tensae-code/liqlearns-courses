## Heritage — meaning and values

Heritage is what can be passed from generation to generation, conserved or inherited, with historic or cultural value: it can be a physical object, property, building or place that is owned and passed on — and just as truly a practice: language, culture, popular song, literature and dress help us comprehend who we are as much as artefacts and buildings. "Heritage values" are the meanings and values individuals and groups bestow on heritage; the unit summary adds that heritage is part of the social glue binding communities and expressing identity — everything people want to save, material culture and nature together.
