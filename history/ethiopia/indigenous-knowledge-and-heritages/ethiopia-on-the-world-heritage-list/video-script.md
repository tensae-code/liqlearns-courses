# Video script — Ethiopia on the World Heritage List

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The registered list is walked site by site: the Stelae of Aksum (inscribed 1980; symbol of the Aksumite kingdom's wealth and importance); the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela (one of the first sites ever listed, 1978); the Lower Valley of the Awash (1980; palaeo-anthropological research 300 km north-east of Addis Ababa in Afar); the Lower Valley of the Omo (1980; human and animal fossils and stone tools fundamental to studying human evolution); Tiya (36 monuments including 32 carved stelae, 90 km south of Addis Ababa) — with the remaining registered sites (the review adds Harar Jugol and Fiche-Chambalala to the pupil's map of them).
