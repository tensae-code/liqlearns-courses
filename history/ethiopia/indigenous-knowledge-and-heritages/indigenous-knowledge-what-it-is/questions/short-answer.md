# Indigenous Knowledge — the Knowledge That Keeps a Community Alive — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Indigenous knowledge is the knowledge that helps a society judge which activities are compatible with its way of life; the knowledge local people use to make a living in a particular environment — "local knowledge" — passed down from generation to generation through oral tradition and practice.

**2.** What does the part "Indigenous knowledge — definition and characteristic features" teach?
**Answer:** Indigenous knowledge is defined as communities' own tested knowledge of nature, farming, medicine, weather and social order, characterised as local, oral, experience-based and holistic — with Ethiopian examples (gadaa governance, traditional medicine, terracing, calendar) treated as knowledge, not folklore.
