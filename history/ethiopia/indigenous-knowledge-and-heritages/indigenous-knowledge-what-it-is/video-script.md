Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Indigenous knowledge is the knowledge that helps a society judge which activities are compatible with its way of life; the knowledge local people use to make a living in a particular environment — "local knowledge" — passed down from generation to generation through oral tradition and practice.

Indigenous knowledge is defined as communities' own tested knowledge of nature, farming, medicine, weather and social order, characterised as local, oral, experience-based and holistic — with Ethiopian examples (gadaa governance, traditional medicine, terracing, calendar) treated as knowledge, not folklore.

Remember it this way: Indigenous knowledge is the knowledge that helps a society judge which activities are compatible with its way of life; the knowledge local people use to make a living in a particular environment — "local knowledge" — passed down from generation to generation through oral tradition and practice.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
