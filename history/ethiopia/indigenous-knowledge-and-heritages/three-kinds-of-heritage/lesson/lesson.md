# Three Kinds of Heritage — Nature, Practice, Thing

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Three Kinds of Heritage — Nature, Practice, Thing. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Types of heritage — natural, intangible, tangible

Three kinds are defined in UNESCO's frame: natural heritage (natural features, geological and physiographical formations, habitats of threatened species, and natural sites of outstanding scientific, conservation or beauty value); intangible heritage (practices, representations, expressions, knowledge and skills, with their instruments and artefacts — in the domains of oral traditions and expressions including language, performing arts, social practices, rituals and festive events, knowledge and practices concerning nature and the universe, and indigenous handicrafts); and tangible heritage (physical artefacts produced, maintained and transmitted across generations — artistic creations, buildings, historic places, monuments).
