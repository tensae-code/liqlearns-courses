# Three Kinds of Heritage — Nature, Practice, Thing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Three kinds are defined in UNESCO's frame: natural heritage (natural features, geological and physiographical formations, habitats of threatened species, and natural sites of outstanding scientific, conservation or beauty value); intangible heritage (practices, representations, expressions, knowledge and skills, with their instruments and artefacts — in the domains of oral traditions and expressions including language, performing arts, social practices, rituals and festive events, knowledge and practices concerning nature and the universe, and indigenous handicrafts); and tangible heritage (physical artefacts produced, maintained and transmitted across generations — artistic creations, buildings, historic places, monuments).
