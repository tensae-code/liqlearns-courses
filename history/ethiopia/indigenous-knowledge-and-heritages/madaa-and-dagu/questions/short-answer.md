# Mada'a and Dagu — Afar Law and the Human News Network — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Afar, a predominantly pastoral people of the north-east, resolve conflicts by Mada'a: an unwritten indigenous law, transmitted orally, administered by elders and clan leaders chosen from different clans who do not represent the disputing members, its decisions resting on forgiveness, respect for elders and compensation by transfer of resources.
