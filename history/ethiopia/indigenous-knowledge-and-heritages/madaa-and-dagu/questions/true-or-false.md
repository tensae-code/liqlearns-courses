# Mada'a and Dagu — Afar Law and the Human News Network — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Afar, a predominantly pastoral people of the north-east, resolve conflicts by Mada'a: an unwritten indigenous law, transmitted orally, administered by elders and clan leaders chosen from different clans who do not represent the disputing members, its decisions resting on forgiveness, respect for elders and compensation by transfer of resources.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo Ways of Making Peace.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The Afar, a predominantly pastoral people of the north-east, resolve conflicts by Mada'a: an unwritten indigenous law, transmitted orally, administered by elders and clan leaders chosen from different clans who do not represent the disputing members, its decisions resting on forgiveness, respect for elders and compensation by transfer of resources.
