## Afar systems — Mada'a law and the Dagu news network

The Afar, a predominantly pastoral people of the north-east, resolve conflicts by Mada'a: an unwritten indigenous law, transmitted orally, administered by elders and clan leaders chosen from different clans who do not represent the disputing members, its decisions resting on forgiveness, respect for elders and compensation by transfer of resources. Their second system is Dagu, an age-old information-exchange network — an indigenous communication system in which news of what people have seen or heard passes person to person and serves all members of society virtually equally regardless of status. The book figures the Dagu in action.
