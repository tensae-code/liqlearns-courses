# Video script — Mada'a and Dagu — Afar Law and the Human News Network

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Afar, a predominantly pastoral people of the north-east, resolve conflicts by Mada'a: an unwritten indigenous law, transmitted orally, administered by elders and clan leaders chosen from different clans who do not represent the disputing members, its decisions resting on forgiveness, respect for elders and compensation by transfer of resources.
