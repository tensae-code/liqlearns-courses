# Shimgelina — the Elders Step Between

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Shimgelina — the Elders Step Between. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Shimgelina — elder mediation

Shimgelina (Shimegilena), the cultural peace-building process figured in the book and associated in the pair work with the Amhara people, is set beside the Oromo systems for comparison: respected elders mediating conflict toward reconciliation — pupils must compare their own locality's method with it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
