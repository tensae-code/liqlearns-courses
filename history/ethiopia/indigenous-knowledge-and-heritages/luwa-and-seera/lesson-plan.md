# Lesson plan — Luwa and Seera — Governing by Age Grades and by Code

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Indigenous Knowledge and Heritages of Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Luwa and Seera — Governing by Age Grades and by Code
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 15 — Indigenous Knowledge and Heritages of Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Sidama Luwa system is an age-related institution with ritual, cultural and political roles: five rotating age grades (Darara, Fullassa, Hirobora, Wawassa, Mogissa), each rotating every eight years, whose twin objectives are recruiting and training able-bodied men for the nation's defence and developing potential elders (Cimeeyye) to replace the current ones — all males progress cyclically from youthful service to senior redistribution of wealth and knowledge, learning by attending the councils where elders decide.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 15 — Indigenous Knowledge and Heritages of Ethiopia (recommended, never forced).
