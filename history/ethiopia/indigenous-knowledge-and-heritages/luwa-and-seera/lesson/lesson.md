# Luwa and Seera — Governing by Age Grades and by Code

## Do it first

## Sidama Luwa and the Seera of Kambata and Hadiya

The Sidama Luwa system is an age-related institution with ritual, cultural and political roles: five rotating age grades (Darara, Fullassa, Hirobora, Wawassa, Mogissa), each rotating every eight years, whose twin objectives are recruiting and training able-bodied men for the nation's defence and developing potential elders (Cimeeyye) to replace the current ones — all males progress cyclically from youthful service to senior redistribution of wealth and knowledge, learning by attending the councils where elders decide. In Kambata and Hadiya, Seera is the code of behaviour governing interactions of people, clans and territorial groups — including love, marriage and family, peer groups, work and enjoyment parties, games, hunting bands, and how farmers treat the environment, youth treat elders and women and men relate — aiming at peace, correction and reintegration, administered by village elders regarded as wise and patient.
