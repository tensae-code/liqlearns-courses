# Luwa and Seera — Governing by Age Grades and by Code — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Sidama Luwa system is an age-related institution with ritual, cultural and political roles: five rotating age grades (Darara, Fullassa, Hirobora, Wawassa, Mogissa), each rotating every eight years, whose twin objectives are recruiting and training able-bodied men for the nation's defence and developing potential elders (Cimeeyye) to replace the current ones — all males progress cyclically from youthful service to senior redistribution of wealth and knowledge, learning by attending the councils where elders decide.
