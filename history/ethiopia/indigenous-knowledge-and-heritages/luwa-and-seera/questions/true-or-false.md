# Luwa and Seera — Governing by Age Grades and by Code — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Sidama Luwa system is an age-related institution with ritual, cultural and political roles: five rotating age grades (Darara, Fullassa, Hirobora, Wawassa, Mogissa), each rotating every eight years, whose twin objectives are recruiting and training able-bodied men for the nation's defence and developing potential elders (Cimeeyye) to replace the current ones — all males progress cyclically from youthful service to senior redistribution of wealth and knowledge, learning by attending the councils where elders decide.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Heritage — What It Is and Why It Matters.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The Sidama Luwa system is an age-related institution with ritual, cultural and political roles: five rotating age grades (Darara, Fullassa, Hirobora, Wawassa, Mogissa), each rotating every eight years, whose twin objectives are recruiting and training able-bodied men for the nation's defence and developing potential elders (Cimeeyye) to replace the current ones — all males progress cyclically from youthful service to senior redistribution of wealth and knowledge, learning by attending the councils where elders decide.
