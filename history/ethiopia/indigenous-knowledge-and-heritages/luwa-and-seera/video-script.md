# Video script — Luwa and Seera — Governing by Age Grades and by Code

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Sidama Luwa system is an age-related institution with ritual, cultural and political roles: five rotating age grades (Darara, Fullassa, Hirobora, Wawassa, Mogissa), each rotating every eight years, whose twin objectives are recruiting and training able-bodied men for the nation's defence and developing potential elders (Cimeeyye) to replace the current ones — all males progress cyclically from youthful service to senior redistribution of wealth and knowledge, learning by attending the councils where elders decide.
