# The Modern World in Ethiopian Terms — a Glossary Reference

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Modern World in Ethiopian Terms — a Glossary Reference. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Glossary — the vocabulary of the modern world and of Ethiopia

The book closes with a glossary running from capitalism's and the Cold War's terms (Zionism and Zollverein are its final entries) to Ethiopia's federal and indigenous vocabulary — the reference lesson for the whole course, pairing Unit 1's Zollverein with Unit 9's indigenous terms in one alphabetical tool.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
