# Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo Ways of Making Peace

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo Ways of Making Peace. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Oromo systems — Jaarsummaa, Sinqe and Gumaa

The Oromo systems surveyed: Jaarsummaa, elder-mediated reconciliation (the book figures the Jaarsuummaa process); the Sinqe institution, centred on the ritual stick of an Oromo woman (figured), through which women's collective voice intervenes against injustice; and Gumaa, the blood-compensation settlement that closes a killing's feud.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
