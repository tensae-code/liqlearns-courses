# Video script — Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo Ways of Making Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Oromo systems surveyed: Jaarsummaa, elder-mediated reconciliation (the book figures the Jaarsuummaa process); the Sinqe institution, centred on the ritual stick of an Oromo woman (figured), through which women's collective voice intervenes against injustice; and Gumaa, the blood-compensation settlement that closes a killing's feud.
