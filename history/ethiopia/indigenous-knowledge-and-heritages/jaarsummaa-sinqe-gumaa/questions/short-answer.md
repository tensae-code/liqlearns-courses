# Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo Ways of Making Peace — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Oromo systems surveyed: Jaarsummaa, elder-mediated reconciliation (the book figures the Jaarsuummaa process); the Sinqe institution, centred on the ritual stick of an Oromo woman (figured), through which women's collective voice intervenes against injustice; and Gumaa, the blood-compensation settlement that closes a killing's feud.
