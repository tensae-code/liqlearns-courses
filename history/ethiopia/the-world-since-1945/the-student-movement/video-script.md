# Video script — The Student Movement — the Empire's Children Turn Critics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The universities' students became the regime's most articulate opposition — the lesson is built around a video of Tilahun Gizaw's speech, which pupils watch and use to describe the movement's nature: the students who named the land question and carried the revolution's vocabulary into the streets.
