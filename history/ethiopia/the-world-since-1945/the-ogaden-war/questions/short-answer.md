# The Ogaden War — 1977 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Ogaden had been contested since Somalia's independence in 1960: Somalia was committed to uniting all five Somali-speaking territories under its flag's five-pointed star (South and North Somalia, French Somaliland/Djibouti, the Ogaden, Kenya's Northern Frontier District); a border war in January 1964 ended, after OAU peacemaking, with no change of boundaries — and in 1977 the two countries entered serious war again, with foreign powers' influence on both sides.
