# The Ogaden War — 1977

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Ogaden War — 1977. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Ethio-Somalia war of 1977

The Ogaden had been contested since Somalia's independence in 1960: Somalia was committed to uniting all five Somali-speaking territories under its flag's five-pointed star (South and North Somalia, French Somaliland/Djibouti, the Ogaden, Kenya's Northern Frontier District); a border war in January 1964 ended, after OAU peacemaking, with no change of boundaries — and in 1977 the two countries entered serious war again, with foreign powers' influence on both sides.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
