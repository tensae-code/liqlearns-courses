# Video script — The Ogaden War — 1977

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Ogaden had been contested since Somalia's independence in 1960: Somalia was committed to uniting all five Somali-speaking territories under its flag's five-pointed star (South and North Somalia, French Somaliland/Djibouti, the Ogaden, Kenya's Northern Frontier District); a border war in January 1964 ended, after OAU peacemaking, with no change of boundaries — and in 1977 the two countries entered serious war again, with foreign powers' influence on both sides.
