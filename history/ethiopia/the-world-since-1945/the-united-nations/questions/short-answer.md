# The United Nations — the League's Successor, Built to Last — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Established in 1945 (193 member states at the book's writing) on the Charter's goals and ideals, succeeding the League of Nations (the text dates the League 1926–1946 here; its own Unit 4 says founded 1919 — see Verification): its formation conferences, mission, principles, main organs (pupils design the organ chart) and achievements are the lesson's contents.

**2.** What does the part "The United Nations Organization" teach?
**Answer:** The UN's founding (1945), its organs and purposes, and Ethiopia's place as a founding member.
