# The Non-Aligned Movement — Bandung to Belgrade — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: NAM's concept originated at the Asia-Africa Bandung Conference (Bandung, Indonesia, 18–24 April 1955), where 29 heads of state of the first post-colonial generation urged a UN economic development fund and condemned apartheid, racial discrimination and colonialism; the first NAM conference followed at Belgrade in September 1961, led by Tito of Yugoslavia, Nasser of Egypt, Nehru of India, Nkrumah of Ghana and Sukarno of Indonesia, with its purpose later enumerated in the Havana Declaration of 1979 — the national independence, sovereignty, territorial integrity and security of the non-aligned. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
