## The Non-Aligned Movement

NAM's concept originated at the Asia-Africa Bandung Conference (Bandung, Indonesia, 18–24 April 1955), where 29 heads of state of the first post-colonial generation urged a UN economic development fund and condemned apartheid, racial discrimination and colonialism; the first NAM conference followed at Belgrade in September 1961, led by Tito of Yugoslavia, Nasser of Egypt, Nehru of India, Nkrumah of Ghana and Sukarno of Indonesia, with its purpose later enumerated in the Havana Declaration of 1979 — the national independence, sovereignty, territorial integrity and security of the non-aligned.
