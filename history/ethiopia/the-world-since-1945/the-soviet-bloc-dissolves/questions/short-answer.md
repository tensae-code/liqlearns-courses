# 1989–1991 — the Soviet Bloc Dissolves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Mikhail Gorbachev, Soviet leader from 1985, introduced glasnost (openness — transparency, freer speech and press) and perestroika (restructuring — some private ownership, a hybrid communist-capitalist economy); the reforms encouraged people to demand more rights, protests spread, and in 1989 anti-communist movements swept the Eastern Bloc.

**2.** What does the part "Dissolution of the communist bloc" teach?
**Answer:** Gorbachev's reforms, 1989's revolutions, the USSR's dissolution (1991) and the unipolar aftermath — the hinge into the post-1991 Ethiopian unit.
