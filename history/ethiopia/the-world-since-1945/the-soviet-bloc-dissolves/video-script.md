# Video script — 1989–1991 — the Soviet Bloc Dissolves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Mikhail Gorbachev, Soviet leader from 1985, introduced glasnost (openness — transparency, freer speech and press) and perestroika (restructuring — some private ownership, a hybrid communist-capitalist economy); the reforms encouraged people to demand more rights, protests spread, and in 1989 anti-communist movements swept the Eastern Bloc.
