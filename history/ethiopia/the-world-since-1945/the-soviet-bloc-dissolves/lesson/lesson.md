## The collapse of the Soviet Union

Mikhail Gorbachev, Soviet leader from 1985, introduced glasnost (openness — transparency, freer speech and press) and perestroika (restructuring — some private ownership, a hybrid communist-capitalist economy); the reforms encouraged people to demand more rights, protests spread, and in 1989 anti-communist movements swept the Eastern Bloc. The USSR could no longer match US military spending amid Eastern Bloc economic problems; the Cold War ended in the 1990s with the USSR's disintegration into over a dozen independent nations.

## Dissolution of the communist bloc

Gorbachev's reforms, 1989's revolutions, the USSR's dissolution (1991) and the unipolar aftermath — the hinge into the post-1991 Ethiopian unit.
