# Society and Economy Under the Late Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The 6.1.5 survey: trade, industry, the financial sector, education, transport and communication, and health in the post-liberation decades — the development ledger against which the regime's promises, and its opposition, are measured.

**2.** What does the part "Socio-economic conditions and social organisations" teach?
**Answer:** Land tenure and the peasantry, slow modernisation, education's growth, and the new social organisations — students, teachers, workers — that become the regime's challengers.
