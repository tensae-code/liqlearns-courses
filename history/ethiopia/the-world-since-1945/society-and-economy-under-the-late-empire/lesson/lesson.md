# Society and Economy Under the Late Empire

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Society and Economy Under the Late Empire. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The imperial economy, sector by sector

The 6.1.5 survey: trade, industry, the financial sector, education, transport and communication, and health in the post-liberation decades — the development ledger against which the regime's promises, and its opposition, are measured.

## Socio-economic conditions and social organisations

Land tenure and the peasantry, slow modernisation, education's growth, and the new social organisations — students, teachers, workers — that become the regime's challengers.
