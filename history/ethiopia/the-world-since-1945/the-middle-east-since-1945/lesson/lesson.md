# The Middle East Since 1945 — the Arab-Israeli Conflict

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Middle East Since 1945 — the Arab-Israeli Conflict. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Arab-Israeli conflict

The Middle East's defining post-1945 conflict: rooted in Zionism — defined in the book's Glossary as the movement that sought to resettle Jews in Palestine — and the creation of Israel, it ran through successive Arab-Israeli wars into a durable unresolved question of land, statehood and refugees, drawing the superpowers' rivalry into the region.

## The Middle East

Israel's creation (1948), the Arab–Israeli wars, Suez (1956), oil's new power and the 1979 Iranian revolution.
