# The Middle East Since 1945 — the Arab-Israeli Conflict — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Middle East's defining post-1945 conflict: rooted in Zionism — defined in the book's Glossary as the movement that sought to resettle Jews in Palestine — and the creation of Israel, it ran through successive Arab-Israeli wars into a durable unresolved question of land, statehood and refugees, drawing the superpowers' rivalry into the region.

**2.** What does the part "The Middle East" teach?
**Answer:** Israel's creation (1948), the Arab–Israeli wars, Suez (1956), oil's new power and the 1979 Iranian revolution.
