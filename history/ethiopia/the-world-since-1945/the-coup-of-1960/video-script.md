# Video script — December 1960 — the Coup That Lasted Two Days

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
On 14 December 1960 the coup makers — Brigadier-General Mengistu Neway and Germame Neway, whose photograph from Bahru Zewde's History of Modern Ethiopia the book prints — proclaimed a new government: the Emperor's son Asfawosen as salaried constitutional monarch, Ras Imru Haile Selassie as prime minister, General Mulugeta Buli as chief of staff.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
