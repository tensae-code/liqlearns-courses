# December 1960 — the Coup That Lasted Two Days — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** On 14 December 1960 the coup makers — Brigadier-General Mengistu Neway and Germame Neway, whose photograph from Bahru Zewde's History of Modern Ethiopia the book prints — proclaimed a new government: the Emperor's son Asfawosen as salaried constitutional monarch, Ras Imru Haile Selassie as prime minister, General Mulugeta Buli as chief of staff.

**2.** What does the part "Consolidation of autocracy and oppositions to the monarchy" teach?
**Answer:** Power centralises in the emperor; opposition grows — the 1960 coup attempt, the student movement, Eritrean insurgency, peasant revolts (Bale, Gojjam) — through the famine that discredits the throne.
