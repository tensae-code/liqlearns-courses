# Liberated but Influenced — Restoration, Britain and the American Turn — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Haile Selassie returned to Addis Ababa on 5 May 1941, yet despite official recognition of his sovereignty Ethiopia remained under British influence — the treaties of 1942 and 1944 signed with Great Britain are to be evaluated for their essence — before American influence succeeded the British as the restored monarchy's main external anchor.

**2.** What does the part "Restoration of imperial rule and external influences" teach?
**Answer:** Haile Selassie returns (1941); the Anglo-Ethiopian agreements, Eritrea's federation (1952) then annexation (1962), and American alliance shape the restored empire.
