# Lesson plan — Liberated but Influenced — Restoration, Britain and the American Turn

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** The World Since 1945 and Ethiopia, 1941–1991 · **Lesson:** Liberated but Influenced — Restoration, Britain and the American Turn
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 13 — The World Since 1945 and Ethiopia, 1941–1991 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Haile Selassie returned to Addis Ababa on 5 May 1941, yet despite official recognition of his sovereignty Ethiopia remained under British influence — the treaties of 1942 and 1944 signed with Great Britain are to be evaluated for their essence — before American influence succeeded the British as the restored monarchy's main external anchor.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 13 — The World Since 1945 and Ethiopia, 1941–1991 (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
