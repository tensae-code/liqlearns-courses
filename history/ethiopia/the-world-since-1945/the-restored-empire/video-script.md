# Video script — Liberated but Influenced — Restoration, Britain and the American Turn

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Haile Selassie returned to Addis Ababa on 5 May 1941, yet despite official recognition of his sovereignty Ethiopia remained under British influence — the treaties of 1942 and 1944 signed with Great Britain are to be evaluated for their essence — before American influence succeeded the British as the restored monarchy's main external anchor.
