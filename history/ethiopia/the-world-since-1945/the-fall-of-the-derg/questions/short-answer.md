# 1991 — How the Derg Fell — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Derg faced armed opposition from the EPLF, TPLF, OLF and others while civil war impoverished the country; when change came in the Soviet Union, Ethiopia lost its vital eastern-bloc military support and aid.

**2.** What does the part "Decline and downfall of the Derg" teach?
**Answer:** War in Eritrea and Tigray, economic failure and the Soviet bloc's collapse strip the regime's supports; the EPRDF advances and Mengistu flees in May 1991.
