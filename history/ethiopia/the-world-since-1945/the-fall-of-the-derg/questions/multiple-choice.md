# 1991 — How the Derg Fell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The Derg faced armed opposition from the EPLF, TPLF, OLF and others while civil war impoverished the country; when change came in the Soviet Union, Ethiopia lost its vital eastern-bloc military support and aid.  b) North Africa Frees Itself — Egypt to Algeria's Hard Road  c) Dating the Past — Calendars, Timelines and Periods  d) none of these

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?

**Answer: a) Foreign influences and the fall of the Derg** — That is this lesson's first part.
