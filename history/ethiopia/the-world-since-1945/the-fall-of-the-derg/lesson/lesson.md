# 1991 — How the Derg Fell

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: 1991 — How the Derg Fell. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Foreign influences and the fall of the Derg

The Derg faced armed opposition from the EPLF, TPLF, OLF and others while civil war impoverished the country; when change came in the Soviet Union, Ethiopia lost its vital eastern-bloc military support and aid. The EPRDF front — TPLF, EPDM, OPDO and EDORM — controlled Addis Ababa on 28 May 1991, ending the Derg regime and opening Unit 8. — Printed p. 167 (unit summary; the fall is Unit 6's closing content per its introduction).

## Decline and downfall of the Derg

War in Eritrea and Tigray, economic failure and the Soviet bloc's collapse strip the regime's supports; the EPRDF advances and Mengistu flees in May 1991.
