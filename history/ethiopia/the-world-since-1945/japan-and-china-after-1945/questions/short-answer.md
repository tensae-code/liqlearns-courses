# Japan Rises Again; China Turns Communist — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Japan, before WWII Asia's most industrialised nation, lost its factories and Asian possessions yet stands as the first Asian case of post-war recovery under the new order.

**2.** What does the part "Asia during the Cold War" teach?
**Answer:** Communist victory in China (1949), the Korean War, Vietnam's wars, and decolonising Asia's place between the blocs.
