# Japan Rises Again; China Turns Communist

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Japan Rises Again; China Turns Communist. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Cold War Asia I — Japan and China

Japan, before WWII Asia's most industrialised nation, lost its factories and Asian possessions yet stands as the first Asian case of post-war recovery under the new order. In China the communists under Mao Zedong defeated Chiang Kai-shek's nationalists (the exercise corrects the claim that Chiang founded the Communist Party) — Asia's largest state joining the communist side and remaking the Cold War's balance.

## Asia during the Cold War

Communist victory in China (1949), the Korean War, Vietnam's wars, and decolonising Asia's place between the blocs.
