# Korea Divided, Vietnam at War — the Cold War Turns Hot in Asia

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Korea Divided, Vietnam at War — the Cold War Turns Hot in Asia. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Cold War Asia II — Korea and Vietnam

Korea was divided between Kim Il-sung's communist North and Syngman Rhee's South (the matching exercise fixes both, and Nixon's "Vietnamization"). Vietnam's wars run in two acts in the book's telling: the "dirty war" of French–Vietnamese confrontation 1946–54, then American involvement in the Second Indo-China War (policies of successive US presidents, 1957–1973, are the set question), with Ho Chi Minh and the Viet Minh as the independence movement's face.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
