# Video script — The Derg Builds Socialism — Reforms and Their Costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The new government adopted Marxism-Leninism as its official ideology and set out to build a socialist state: its socio-economic and political reforms (the land reform at their head), together with its record on human and democratic rights, the 1984/85 famine that followed drought and crop failure, and the resettlement and villagization programmes — judged in the summary as ineffective and poorly coordinated — are the lesson's ledger.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The Derg's programme: land reform (1975), nationalisations, villagisation and resettlement, the Workers' Party and the 1987 PDRE constitution — taught with their costs, including the Red Terror the book names directly.

The guide's Derg overview supplies the specifics the student book's reform lesson leaves general: in December 1974 the Development through Co-operation Campaign (Edget Behebret) was launched under the philosophy of Ethiopia Tikdem, carrying a literacy programme and rural development; in March 1975 the Land Reform Proclamation nationalised all rural land and brought feudal relations in rural Ethiopia to an end; the lesson's competencies add the Red and White Terror and the essence of the 1987 Constitution as required study.
