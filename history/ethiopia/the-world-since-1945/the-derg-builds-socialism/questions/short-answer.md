# The Derg Builds Socialism — Reforms and Their Costs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The new government adopted Marxism-Leninism as its official ideology and set out to build a socialist state: its socio-economic and political reforms (the land reform at their head), together with its record on human and democratic rights, the 1984/85 famine that followed drought and crop failure, and the resettlement and villagization programmes — judged in the summary as ineffective and poorly coordinated — are the lesson's ledger.

**2.** What does the part "Content the guide adds — the Derg's programme named and dated, and who started the 1977 war" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's Derg overview supplies the specifics the student book's reform lesson leaves general: in December 1974 the Development through Co-operation Campaign (Edget Behebret) was launched under the philosophy of Ethiopia Tikdem, carrying a literacy programme and rural development; in March 1975 the Land Reform Proclamation nationalised all rural land and brought feudal relations in rural Ethiopia to an end; the lesson's competencies add the Red and White Terror and the essence of the 1987 Constitution as required study.
