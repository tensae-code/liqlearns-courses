# Video script — Peasants Against the Empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The earliest opposition was rural: the Woyane revolt in Tigray (1943), where semi-pastoralists, highland peasants and some local feudal lords formed a united front under Bilata Haile Mariam Reda against the returned regime; the Bale revolt (General Jagema Kello was later assigned as Bale's governor); and the Gojjam revolts, triggered finally in 1968 by the 1967 agricultural income tax — peasants refused the tax assessors entry, petitioned, then defied the state, and the government answered with militia and armed police in Mota, Qolla Dega Damot and Bichena districts.
