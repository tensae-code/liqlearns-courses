# Lesson plan — Peasants Against the Empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** The World Since 1945 and Ethiopia, 1941–1991 · **Lesson:** Peasants Against the Empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 13 — The World Since 1945 and Ethiopia, 1941–1991 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The earliest opposition was rural: the Woyane revolt in Tigray (1943), where semi-pastoralists, highland peasants and some local feudal lords formed a united front under Bilata Haile Mariam Reda against the returned regime; the Bale revolt (General Jagema Kello was later assigned as Bale's governor); and the Gojjam revolts, triggered finally in 1968 by the 1967 agricultural income tax — peasants refused the tax assessors entry, petitioned, then defied the state, and the government answered with militia and armed police in Mota, Qolla Dega Damot and Bichena districts.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 13 — The World Since 1945 and Ethiopia, 1941–1991 (recommended, never forced).
