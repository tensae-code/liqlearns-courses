# Peasants Against the Empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The earliest opposition was rural: the Woyane revolt in Tigray (1943), where semi-pastoralists, highland peasants and some local feudal lords formed a united front under Bilata Haile Mariam Reda against the returned regime; the Bale revolt (General Jagema Kello was later assigned as Bale's governor); and the Gojjam revolts, triggered finally in 1968 by the 1967 agricultural income tax — peasants refused the tax assessors entry, petitioned, then defied the state, and the government answered with militia and armed police in Mota, Qolla Dega Damot and Bichena districts. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
