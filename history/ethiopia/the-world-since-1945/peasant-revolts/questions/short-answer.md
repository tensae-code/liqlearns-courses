# Peasants Against the Empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The earliest opposition was rural: the Woyane revolt in Tigray (1943), where semi-pastoralists, highland peasants and some local feudal lords formed a united front under Bilata Haile Mariam Reda against the returned regime; the Bale revolt (General Jagema Kello was later assigned as Bale's governor); and the Gojjam revolts, triggered finally in 1968 by the 1967 agricultural income tax — peasants refused the tax assessors entry, petitioned, then defied the state, and the government answered with militia and armed police in Mota, Qolla Dega Damot and Bichena districts.
