# Video script — Eritrea — a Federation Undone, a Thirty-Year Struggle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Eritrea came under British control in 1941; its fate became an international question, settled by the UN General Assembly's 1950 recommendation as federation with Ethiopia in 1952: Eritrea governing its domestic affairs through its own legislative, executive and judicial powers, while defence, foreign affairs, currency and finance, interstate commerce and communications went to the Ethiopian government.
