## The question of Eritrea — federation, annexation, armed struggle, referendum

Eritrea came under British control in 1941; its fate became an international question, settled by the UN General Assembly's 1950 recommendation as federation with Ethiopia in 1952: Eritrea governing its domestic affairs through its own legislative, executive and judicial powers, while defence, foreign affairs, currency and finance, interstate commerce and communications went to the Ethiopian government. Measures contrary to the Federal Act then thoroughly undermined Eritrean autonomy and created the discontent from which the armed struggle grew — the question the section carries up to referendum.
