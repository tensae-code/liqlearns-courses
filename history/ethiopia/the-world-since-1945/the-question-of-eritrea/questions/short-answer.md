# Eritrea — a Federation Undone, a Thirty-Year Struggle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Eritrea came under British control in 1941; its fate became an international question, settled by the UN General Assembly's 1950 recommendation as federation with Ethiopia in 1952: Eritrea governing its domestic affairs through its own legislative, executive and judicial powers, while defence, foreign affairs, currency and finance, interstate commerce and communications went to the Ethiopian government.
