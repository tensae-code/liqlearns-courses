# The Cold War — Blocs, Containment, Arms Race — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** WWII produced two superpowers, the USA and the USSR, whose wartime partnership turned to confrontation by 1945–47 as Stalin consolidated control of Central and Eastern Europe (Tehran, Yalta, Potsdam mark the souring): a "cold" war of no direct combat, fought by military build-up, influence expansion and ideological delegitimation — capitalist free markets and multiparty politics against a centralised one-party communist system.

**2.** What does the part "Cold War realities" teach?
**Answer:** Ideological bipolarity in practice: NATO and the Warsaw Pact, the arms and space races, Korea as hot war, and the non-aligned response.
