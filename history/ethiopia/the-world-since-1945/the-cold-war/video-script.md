# Video script — The Cold War — Blocs, Containment, Arms Race

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
WWII produced two superpowers, the USA and the USSR, whose wartime partnership turned to confrontation by 1945–47 as Stalin consolidated control of Central and Eastern Europe (Tehran, Yalta, Potsdam mark the souring): a "cold" war of no direct combat, fought by military build-up, influence expansion and ideological delegitimation — capitalist free markets and multiparty politics against a centralised one-party communist system.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
