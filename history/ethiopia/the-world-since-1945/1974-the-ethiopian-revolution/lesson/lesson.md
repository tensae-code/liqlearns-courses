# 1974 — Revolution, Deposition, the Derg

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: 1974 — Revolution, Deposition, the Derg. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Ethiopian revolution and the fall of the monarchy

The last two decades of Haile Selassie's reign saw opposition peak in February 1974 amid worsening social, economic and political conditions and protests from every section of society; the spontaneous movement enabled the organised military to assume power — the Emperor was deposed and the Derg emerged, ending the monarchy.

## The 1974 Revolution

Famine, oil prices, mutinies and strikes cascade into revolution: the Derg takes power, the emperor is deposed (September 1974) and the monarchy abolished.
