# 1974 — Revolution, Deposition, the Derg — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The last two decades of Haile Selassie's reign saw opposition peak in February 1974 amid worsening social, economic and political conditions and protests from every section of society; the spontaneous movement enabled the organised military to assume power — the Emperor was deposed and the Derg emerged, ending the monarchy.

**2.** What does the part "The 1974 Revolution" teach?
**Answer:** Famine, oil prices, mutinies and strikes cascade into revolution: the Derg takes power, the emperor is deposed (September 1974) and the monarchy abolished.
