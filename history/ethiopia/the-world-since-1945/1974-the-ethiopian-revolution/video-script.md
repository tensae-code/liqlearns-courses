# Video script — 1974 — Revolution, Deposition, the Derg

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

The last two decades of Haile Selassie's reign saw opposition peak in February 1974 amid worsening social, economic and political conditions and protests from every section of society; the spontaneous movement enabled the organised military to assume power — the Emperor was deposed and the Derg emerged, ending the monarchy.
