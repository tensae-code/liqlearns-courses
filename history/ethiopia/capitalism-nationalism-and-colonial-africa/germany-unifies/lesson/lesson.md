# Germany Unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Germany Unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The unification of Germany

Germany unified under Prussia against its rival Austria: Bismarck's "Blood and Iron" policy, prepared economically by the Zollverein customs union, fought Prussia's three wars (the exercise fixes Sadowa among them) to exclude Austria and then defeat France; by the Treaty of Frankfurt (10 May 1871) France ceded coal- and iron-rich Alsace and Lorraine and paid huge indemnities, and in 1871 the German Empire was proclaimed at the Palace of Versailles with King William of Prussia as Kaiser — unification staged, pointedly, on French soil.

## Unification of Germany

Germany unifies under Prussia through Bismarck's "blood and iron" wars (Denmark, Austria, France) to the empire proclaimed at Versailles in 1871.
