# Germany Unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871 — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Germany unified under Prussia against its rival Austria: Bismarck's "Blood and Iron" policy, prepared economically by the Zollverein customs union, fought Prussia's three wars (the exercise fixes Sadowa among them) to exclude Austria and then defeat France; by the Treaty of Frankfurt (10 May 1871) France ceded coal- and iron-rich Alsace and Lorraine and paid huge indemnities, and in 1871 the German Empire was proclaimed at the Palace of Versailles with King William of Prussia as Kaiser — unification staged, pointedly, on French soil. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
