# Germany Unifies — Bismarck, Blood and Iron, Versailles 1871 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Germany unified under Prussia against its rival Austria: Bismarck's "Blood and Iron" policy, prepared economically by the Zollverein customs union, fought Prussia's three wars (the exercise fixes Sadowa among them) to exclude Austria and then defeat France; by the Treaty of Frankfurt (10 May 1871) France ceded coal- and iron-rich Alsace and Lorraine and paid huge indemnities, and in 1871 the German Empire was proclaimed at the Palace of Versailles with King William of Prussia as Kaiser — unification staged, pointedly, on French soil.

**2.** What does the part "Unification of Germany" teach?
**Answer:** Germany unifies under Prussia through Bismarck's "blood and iron" wars (Denmark, Austria, France) to the empire proclaimed at Versailles in 1871.
