# Lesson plan — Italy Unifies — Cavour, Garibaldi, and Unification from Above and Below

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 · **Lesson:** Italy Unifies — Cavour, Garibaldi, and Unification from Above and Below
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 10 — Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Italy, broken into many smaller states sharing language, culture, history and religion, unified in the second half of the 19th century by different patriotic approaches working together; Cavour is named the architect of the united Italy for his central involvement in the unification process, the Kingdom of Italy was proclaimed in 1861, and the new government inherited the challenge of tension between the industrialising north and the agrarian south (the exercises correct the reversed claim) and a weakened papacy.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 10 — Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 (recommended, never forced).
