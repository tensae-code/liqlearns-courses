# Italy Unifies — Cavour, Garibaldi, and Unification from Above and Below — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Italy, broken into many smaller states sharing language, culture, history and religion, unified in the second half of the 19th century by different patriotic approaches working together; Cavour is named the architect of the united Italy for his central involvement in the unification process, the Kingdom of Italy was proclaimed in 1861, and the new government inherited the challenge of tension between the industrialising north and the agrarian south (the exercises correct the reversed claim) and a weakened papacy.

**2.** What does the part "Italy-from-above-and-below formulation" teach?
**Answer:** The guide instructs that Italian unification be taught as the joint product of Piedmont-Sardinia's government measures "from above" and popular revolt "from below", and German unification taught by comparison with Italy — the analytical frame for student placements 5–6.
