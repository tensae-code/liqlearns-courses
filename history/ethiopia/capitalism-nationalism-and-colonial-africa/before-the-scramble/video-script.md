# Video script — Before the Scramble — Africa Trading, Mostly Unruled

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Africa's commercial transition was driven by industrialising Western Europe's rising demand for industrial inputs: African exports broadened to vegetable and palm oils, groundnuts, gum, ivory, gold, hides and skins, rubber, cotton, copper and cash crops (cocoa, coffee, tea, tobacco).
