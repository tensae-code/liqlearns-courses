# West Africa Resists — Samori Ture, Ahmadu Seku, Asante — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** African responses ranged from armed resistance to nonviolent forms — indigenous press, trade unionism, organised religion, associations, literature and art, mass action.

**2.** What does the part "Resistance thread" teach?
**Answer:** African resistance (§2.2), Adwa (§3.5), anti-fascist patriot resistance implied in the 1935–41 close of Unit 3, and liberation movements in Unit 7 form one deliberate thread of African agency across the grade.
