# Video script — Indirect Rule and Settler Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The contrasting systems: British indirect rule, governing through existing African chiefs and institutions; and settler rule, where white settler minorities held land and power directly — the system whose geography (pupils sketch the colonial map and locate the white settlement areas) explains where later liberation would be hardest.
