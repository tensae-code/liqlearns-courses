# Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Three systems of ruling the partitioned continent: company rule, where chartered companies governed as the first administration; and the French-style direct rule with its assimilation policy, ruling through European officials and aiming to make colonised people into French citizens in culture.

**2.** What does the part "Colonial administration and the colonial states" teach?
**Answer:** The systems are compared: British indirect rule, French direct rule/assimilation and association, Portuguese and Belgian variants — and the colonial state's extractive economy of cash crops, taxes and forced labour.
