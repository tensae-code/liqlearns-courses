# Video script — Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Three systems of ruling the partitioned continent: company rule, where chartered companies governed as the first administration; and the French-style direct rule with its assimilation policy, ruling through European officials and aiming to make colonised people into French citizens in culture.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
