# Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation

## Colonial policies I — company rule, direct rule and assimilation

Three systems of ruling the partitioned continent: company rule, where chartered companies governed as the first administration; and the French-style direct rule with its assimilation policy, ruling through European officials and aiming to make colonised people into French citizens in culture. Pupils must compare British and French policies directly.

## Colonial administration and the colonial states

The systems are compared: British indirect rule, French direct rule/assimilation and association, Portuguese and Belgian variants — and the colonial state's extractive economy of cash crops, taxes and forced labour.
