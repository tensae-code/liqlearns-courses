## Nationalism and the "Eastern Question"

The Eastern Question was the 19th- and early-20th-century diplomatic problem created by three forces together: rising nationalist feeling among the Balkan peoples, the declining Ottoman (Turkish) Empire, and the divergent interests of the Great Powers in the Near East. Economic and social change, international rivalry and unsolved problems combined into a succession of Balkan crises with serious consequences for all Europe.

## The Eastern Question and the Balkan Wars

The Ottoman Empire's decline becomes the "Eastern Question": great-power rivalry over its remains, Balkan nationalisms, and the Balkan Wars of 1912–13 as the fuse toward 1914.
