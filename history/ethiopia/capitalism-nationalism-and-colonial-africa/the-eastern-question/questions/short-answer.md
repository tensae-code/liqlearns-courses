# The Eastern Question — Nationalism in the Balkans, Empires in Collision — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Eastern Question was the 19th- and early-20th-century diplomatic problem created by three forces together: rising nationalist feeling among the Balkan peoples, the declining Ottoman (Turkish) Empire, and the divergent interests of the Great Powers in the Near East.

**2.** What does the part "Eastern Question framing (guide's formulation)" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's presentation defines the Eastern Question explicitly as the Balkan peoples' liberation struggle against Ottoman Turkish rule plus the great-power politics accompanying it, and notes it remained unsolved even after World War I — a sharper formulation than the student text's, used as the teaching definition.
