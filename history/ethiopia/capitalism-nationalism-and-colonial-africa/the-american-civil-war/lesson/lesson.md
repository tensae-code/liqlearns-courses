# The American Civil War — Union Against Slavery's States

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The American Civil War — Union Against Slavery's States. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The American Civil War (Book 2 telling)

From 1861 to 1865 the United States fought the Confederate States of America — eleven southern states that seceded in 1860–61 after Lincoln's election — over the long-standing disagreement about slavery. Lincoln and the North opposed secession; the war ended with the Confederacy's defeat and the abolition of slavery in the USA. Lincoln's portrait is the lesson's figure.

## The American Civil War (Book 4 telling)

Slavery divides the United States; the Civil War (1861–65) under Lincoln destroys slavery (Emancipation, 13th Amendment) and preserves the Union at enormous cost.

## Civil War weight in the key *(teacher-guide evidence)*

The guide's Unit 1 overview fixes the war's scale (1861–65, 620,000 casualties) and that slavery outweighed all other divisive issues — the marking emphasis for student placement 7.
