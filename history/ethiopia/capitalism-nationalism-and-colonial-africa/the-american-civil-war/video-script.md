# Video script — The American Civil War — Union Against Slavery's States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From 1861 to 1865 the United States fought the Confederate States of America — eleven southern states that seceded in 1860–61 after Lincoln's election — over the long-standing disagreement about slavery.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

From 1861 to 1865 the United States fought the Confederate States of America — eleven southern states that seceded in 1860–61 after Lincoln's election — over the long-standing disagreement about slavery.

Slavery divides the United States; the Civil War (1861–65) under Lincoln destroys slavery (Emancipation, 13th Amendment) and preserves the Union at enormous cost.

The guide's Unit 1 overview fixes the war's scale (1861–65, 620,000 casualties) and that slavery outweighed all other divisive issues — the marking emphasis for student placement 7.

Remember it this way: From 1861 to 1865 the United States fought the Confederate States of America — eleven southern states that seceded in 1860–61 after Lincoln's election — over the long-standing disagreement about slavery.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
