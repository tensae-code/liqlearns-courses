# The American Civil War — Union Against Slavery's States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** From 1861 to 1865 the United States fought the Confederate States of America — eleven southern states that seceded in 1860–61 after Lincoln's election — over the long-standing disagreement about slavery.

**2.** What does the part "Civil War weight in the key" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's Unit 1 overview fixes the war's scale (1861–65, 620,000 casualties) and that slavery outweighed all other divisive issues — the marking emphasis for student placement 7.
