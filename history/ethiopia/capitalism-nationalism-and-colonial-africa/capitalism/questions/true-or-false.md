# Capitalism — Features and Its Marxian Critique — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Capitalism, dominant in the West since feudalism's collapse, rests on private ownership of most means of production; the Industrial Revolution (begun in 18th-century England, then Belgium, France and the German states) transformed it — coal and steam replaced wind and water, the factory system replaced the domestic system of home workshops, and new socio-economic classes, above all industrial capitalists and factory workers, emerged from the tremendous economic transformation of Western Europe and the USA in the 19th century.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: The Industrial Revolution and the Road to Imperialism.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Capitalism, dominant in the West since feudalism's collapse, rests on private ownership of most means of production; the Industrial Revolution (begun in 18th-century England, then Belgium, France and the German states) transformed it — coal and steam replaced wind and water, the factory system replaced the domestic system of home workshops, and new socio-economic classes, above all industrial capitalists and factory workers, emerged from the tremendous economic transformation of Western Europe and the USA in the 19th century.
