# Capitalism — Features and Its Marxian Critique — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Capitalism, dominant in the West since feudalism's collapse, rests on private ownership of most means of production; the Industrial Revolution (begun in 18th-century England, then Belgium, France and the German states) transformed it — coal and steam replaced wind and water, the factory system replaced the domestic system of home workshops, and new socio-economic classes, above all industrial capitalists and factory workers, emerged from the tremendous economic transformation of Western Europe and the USA in the 19th century.

**2.** What does the part "Theory-before-empire design" teach?
**Answer:** Capitalism and Marxian economics are taught first so colonisation, the Russian Revolution and the Cold War are analysed with named concepts (imperialism, laissez-faire, Social Darwinism) rather than narrated bare.
