# Lesson plan — Capitalism — Features and Its Marxian Critique

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 · **Lesson:** Capitalism — Features and Its Marxian Critique
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 10 — Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Capitalism, dominant in the West since feudalism's collapse, rests on private ownership of most means of production; the Industrial Revolution (begun in 18th-century England, then Belgium, France and the German states) transformed it — coal and steam replaced wind and water, the factory system replaced the domestic system of home workshops, and new socio-economic classes, above all industrial capitalists and factory workers, emerged from the tremendous economic transformation of Western Europe and the USA in the 19th century.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 10 — Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 (recommended, never forced).
