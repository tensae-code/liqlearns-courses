# Video script — Why Europe Colonised — Motives and the Civilising-Mission Claim

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The lesson weighs the motives — economic (raw materials and markets, assessed as the driving factors), strategic and ideological — and sets pupils to assess the role of missionaries on the eve of colonisation; the colonisers' self-justification, the "civilizing mission", is stated plainly as an asserted structure of ideas in which European practices were civilised and universal and non-European societies uncivilised and inferior — with Kipling's "Take up the White Man's Burden" printed in the book as the period document of that ideology.
