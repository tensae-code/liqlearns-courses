# Why Europe Colonised — Motives and the Civilising-Mission Claim — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The lesson weighs the motives — economic (raw materials and markets, assessed as the driving factors), strategic and ideological — and sets pupils to assess the role of missionaries on the eve of colonisation; the colonisers' self-justification, the "civilizing mission", is stated plainly as an asserted structure of ideas in which European practices were civilised and universal and non-European societies uncivilised and inferior — with Kipling's "Take up the White Man's Burden" printed in the book as the period document of that ideology.
