# Lesson plan — Why Europe Colonised — Motives and the Civilising-Mission Claim

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 · **Lesson:** Why Europe Colonised — Motives and the Civilising-Mission Claim
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 10 — Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The lesson weighs the motives — economic (raw materials and markets, assessed as the driving factors), strategic and ideological — and sets pupils to assess the role of missionaries on the eve of colonisation; the colonisers' self-justification, the "civilizing mission", is stated plainly as an asserted structure of ideas in which European practices were civilised and universal and non-European societies uncivilised and inferior — with Kipling's "Take up the White Man's Burden" printed in the book as the period document of that ideology.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 10 — Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 (recommended, never forced).
