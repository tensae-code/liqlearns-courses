# The Industrial Revolution and the Road to Imperialism

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Industrial Revolution and the Road to Imperialism. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.
