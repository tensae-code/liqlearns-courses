# What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — Political, Economic, Social — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** In three registers: political (artificial states and boundaries, destroyed indigenous authorities), economic (policies that extracted rather than developed — Africa weakened economically, its resources and labour serving the coloniser), and social/cultural (indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed, Africa weakened culturally).
