# What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — Political, Economic, Social — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: In three registers: political (artificial states and boundaries, destroyed indigenous authorities), economic (policies that extracted rather than developed — Africa weakened economically, its resources and labour serving the coloniser), and social/cultural (indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed, Africa weakened culturally). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
