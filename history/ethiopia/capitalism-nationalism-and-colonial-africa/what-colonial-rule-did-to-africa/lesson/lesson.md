# What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — Political, Economic, Social

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — Political, Economic, Social. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The impacts of colonial rule on Africa

In three registers: political (artificial states and boundaries, destroyed indigenous authorities), economic (policies that extracted rather than developed — Africa weakened economically, its resources and labour serving the coloniser), and social/cultural (indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed, Africa weakened culturally). The summary's verdict is blunt: colonial economic policies destroyed rather than helped the colonies.
