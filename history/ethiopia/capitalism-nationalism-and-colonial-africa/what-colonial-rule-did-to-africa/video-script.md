# Video script — What Colonial Rule Did to Africa — Political, Economic, Social

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In three registers: political (artificial states and boundaries, destroyed indigenous authorities), economic (policies that extracted rather than developed — Africa weakened economically, its resources and labour serving the coloniser), and social/cultural (indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed, Africa weakened culturally).
