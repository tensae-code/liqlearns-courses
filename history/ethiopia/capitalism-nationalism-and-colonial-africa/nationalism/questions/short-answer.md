# Nationalism — the Feeling That Remade Maps — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Nationalist feeling was prepared by earlier movements: the Renaissance (national languages and literatures breeding national self-consciousness), the Protestant Reformation (breaking Europe's religious unity and papal supremacy), and the expansion of commerce and colonies (wealth building strong states).

**2.** What does the part "Nationalism" teach?
**Answer:** Nationalism is defined and its 19th-century growth factors traced — language, culture, liberal ideas, opposition to foreign rule — as the force that remakes Europe's map.
