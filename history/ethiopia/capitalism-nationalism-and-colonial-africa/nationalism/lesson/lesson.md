## Features of nationalism and the making of nation states

Nationalist feeling was prepared by earlier movements: the Renaissance (national languages and literatures breeding national self-consciousness), the Protestant Reformation (breaking Europe's religious unity and papal supremacy), and the expansion of commerce and colonies (wealth building strong states). After Napoleon came "reaction": at Vienna in 1815 Britain, Russia, Prussia and Austria drew a settlement meant to reverse the Napoleonic changes, against which nationalism — shared language, culture, history and religion — pressed for nation states.

## Nationalism

Nationalism is defined and its 19th-century growth factors traced — language, culture, liberal ideas, opposition to foreign rule — as the force that remakes Europe's map.
