# Video script — Maji-Maji — and Resistance in the South and North

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
East Africa's exemplar is the Maji-Maji uprising in German Tanganyika (1905–1907), the most significant African challenge to German colonial rule: against high taxation and forced labour (compulsory cotton-growing and road-building), the movement led by the prophet Kinjikitle Ngwale united peoples across the south — Germany held four African colonies (Tanganyika, Togo, Cameroon, Namibia).

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
