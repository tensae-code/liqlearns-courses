## Early resistance in East, South and North Africa

East Africa's exemplar is the Maji-Maji uprising in German Tanganyika (1905–1907), the most significant African challenge to German colonial rule: against high taxation and forced labour (compulsory cotton-growing and road-building), the movement led by the prophet Kinjikitle Ngwale united peoples across the south — Germany held four African colonies (Tanganyika, Togo, Cameroon, Namibia). The unit's southern and northern resistance sections complete the continental survey on the same pattern: primary resistance first, then the organised movements of the colonial period proper.
