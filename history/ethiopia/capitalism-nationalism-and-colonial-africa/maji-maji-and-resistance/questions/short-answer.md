# Maji-Maji — and Resistance in the South and North — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** East Africa's exemplar is the Maji-Maji uprising in German Tanganyika (1905–1907), the most significant African challenge to German colonial rule: against high taxation and forced labour (compulsory cotton-growing and road-building), the movement led by the prophet Kinjikitle Ngwale united peoples across the south — Germany held four African colonies (Tanganyika, Togo, Cameroon, Namibia).
