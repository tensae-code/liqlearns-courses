# Maji-Maji — and Resistance in the South and North — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: East Africa's exemplar is the Maji-Maji uprising in German Tanganyika (1905–1907), the most significant African challenge to German colonial rule: against high taxation and forced labour (compulsory cotton-growing and road-building), the movement led by the prophet Kinjikitle Ngwale united peoples across the south — Germany held four African colonies (Tanganyika, Togo, Cameroon, Namibia). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
