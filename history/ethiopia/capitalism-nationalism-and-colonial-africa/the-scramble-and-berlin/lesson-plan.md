# Lesson plan — The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a Continent Partitioned on Paper

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 · **Lesson:** The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a Continent Partitioned on Paper
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 10 — Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Between 1884 and 1914 European nations took control of the continent in the Scramble, partitioning the largely unexplored interior into protectorates, colonies and "free-trade areas"; the Berlin Conference of 1884–1885 is the diplomatic machine of the partition, called to regulate colonial rivalries before they became wars among Europeans.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 10 — Capitalism, Nationalism and Colonial Africa, 1815–1914 (recommended, never forced).
