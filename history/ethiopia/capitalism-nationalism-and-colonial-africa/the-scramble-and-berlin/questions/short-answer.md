# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a Continent Partitioned on Paper — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Between 1884 and 1914 European nations took control of the continent in the Scramble, partitioning the largely unexplored interior into protectorates, colonies and "free-trade areas"; the Berlin Conference of 1884–1885 is the diplomatic machine of the partition, called to regulate colonial rivalries before they became wars among Europeans.

**2.** What does the part "Content the guide adds — Berlin's mechanics: who called it, who came, who was absent" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's Scramble overview adds the conference's working facts: the Berlin Conference (1884–85) was also known as the "Congo Conference"; Portugal proposed it; fourteen European countries met in Berlin regarding the division of Africa; although the conference was about Africa, Africans were not invited nor involved; and of the fourteen, France, Germany, Great Britain and Portugal were the major players.
