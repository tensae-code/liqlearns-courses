# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a Continent Partitioned on Paper — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Between 1884 and 1914 European nations took control of the continent in the Scramble, partitioning the largely unexplored interior into protectorates, colonies and "free-trade areas"; the Berlin Conference of 1884–1885 is the diplomatic machine of the partition, called to regulate colonial rivalries before they became wars among Europeans.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: From Legitimate Trade to the Scramble for Africa.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Between 1884 and 1914 European nations took control of the continent in the Scramble, partitioning the largely unexplored interior into protectorates, colonies and "free-trade areas"; the Berlin Conference of 1884–1885 is the diplomatic machine of the partition, called to regulate colonial rivalries before they became wars among Europeans.
