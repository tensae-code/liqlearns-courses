# The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade and the Triangular Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Atlantic trade arose amid three West African circumstances: the fragmentation of the large states (Ghana, Mali, Songhai), the region's established role as a source of enslaved people for the Sahara traffic, and coastal states' willingness to exchange slaves for arms.

**2.** What does the part "Content the guide adds — why Henry's voyages turned to slaves, and why Africans traded" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's activity answers supply two causal claims the student book does not make: (1) Prince Henry's sponsored voyages were criticised for their rising expense and had to pay for themselves; his hopes of West African gold materialised only as some gold dust, but the slave trade proved profitable and soon became the basis of the sponsored voyages; (2) Africans who engaged in slave trading did so because they received guns and goods from Europeans, making it lucrative for both sides.
