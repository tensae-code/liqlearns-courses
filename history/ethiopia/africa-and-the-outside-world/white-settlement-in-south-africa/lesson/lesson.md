# White Settlement in South Africa — 1652 and the Great Trek

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: White Settlement in South Africa — 1652 and the Great Trek. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## White settlement in South Africa (Book 1 telling)

In 1652 the Dutch trading company planted a tiny permanent settlement on South Africa's southern shore to regulate trade with the Khoisan and to supply fresh fruit and vegetables and a hospital for sick sailors on passing ships — a supply station that paved the way for permanent white settlement in South Africa, whose settlers' motives the lesson examines.

## White settlement in South Africa (Book 3 telling)

Dutch then British settlement at the Cape, the Great Trek, Boer republics, and conflict with African states over land.
