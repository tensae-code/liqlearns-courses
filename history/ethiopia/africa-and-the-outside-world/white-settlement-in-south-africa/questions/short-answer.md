# White Settlement in South Africa — 1652 and the Great Trek — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** In 1652 the Dutch trading company planted a tiny permanent settlement on South Africa's southern shore to regulate trade with the Khoisan and to supply fresh fruit and vegetables and a hospital for sick sailors on passing ships — a supply station that paved the way for permanent white settlement in South Africa, whose settlers' motives the lesson examines.

**2.** What does the part "White settlement in South Africa (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** Dutch then British settlement at the Cape, the Great Trek, Boer republics, and conflict with African states over land.
