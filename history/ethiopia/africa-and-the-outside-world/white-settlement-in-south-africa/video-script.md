# Video script — White Settlement in South Africa — 1652 and the Great Trek

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In 1652 the Dutch trading company planted a tiny permanent settlement on South Africa's southern shore to regulate trade with the Khoisan and to supply fresh fruit and vegetables and a hospital for sick sailors on passing ships — a supply station that paved the way for permanent white settlement in South Africa, whose settlers' motives the lesson examines.
