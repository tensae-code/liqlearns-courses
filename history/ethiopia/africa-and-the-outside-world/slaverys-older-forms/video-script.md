# Video script — Slavery's Older Forms — Within Africa and Before the Atlantic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Slave traffic was not new: it had a long history in Africa, Europe and Asia at a fairly steady, small scale — the early market was Southwest Asia, where enslaved Africans were used mainly as domestic servants; captives from Nubia went down the Nile to Egypt in pharaonic times, others crossed the Sahara in Roman times or went from north-east Africa to the Persian Gulf and Indian states.
