## Slavery before the Atlantic trade

Slave traffic was not new: it had a long history in Africa, Europe and Asia at a fairly steady, small scale — the early market was Southwest Asia, where enslaved Africans were used mainly as domestic servants; captives from Nubia went down the Nile to Egypt in pharaonic times, others crossed the Sahara in Roman times or went from north-east Africa to the Persian Gulf and Indian states. All of it is set against the scale of what followed.

## Slavery inside Africa

Slavery's forms within African societies before and beside the export trades — domestic, pawnship and military slavery distinguished.
