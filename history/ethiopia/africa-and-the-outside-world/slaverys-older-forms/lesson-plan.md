# Lesson plan — Slavery's Older Forms — Within Africa and Before the Atlantic

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Africa and the Outside World, 1500–1880s · **Lesson:** Slavery's Older Forms — Within Africa and Before the Atlantic
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Africa and the Outside World, 1500–1880s (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Slave traffic was not new: it had a long history in Africa, Europe and Asia at a fairly steady, small scale — the early market was Southwest Asia, where enslaved Africans were used mainly as domestic servants; captives from Nubia went down the Nile to Egypt in pharaonic times, others crossed the Sahara in Roman times or went from north-east Africa to the Persian Gulf and Indian states.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Africa and the Outside World, 1500–1880s (recommended, never forced).
