# Slavery's Older Forms — Within Africa and Before the Atlantic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Slave traffic was not new: it had a long history in Africa, Europe and Asia at a fairly steady, small scale — the early market was Southwest Asia, where enslaved Africans were used mainly as domestic servants; captives from Nubia went down the Nile to Egypt in pharaonic times, others crossed the Sahara in Roman times or went from north-east Africa to the Persian Gulf and Indian states.

**2.** What does the part "Slavery inside Africa" teach?
**Answer:** Slavery's forms within African societies before and beside the export trades — domestic, pawnship and military slavery distinguished.
