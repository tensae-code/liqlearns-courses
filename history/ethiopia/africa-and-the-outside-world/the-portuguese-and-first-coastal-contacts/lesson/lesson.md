# The Portuguese and the First Coastal Contacts

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Portuguese and the First Coastal Contacts. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Contact with the outside world — the Portuguese first

Portugal established the early coastal contacts with West Africa: its distant objective was India, its immediate goal to bypass Muslim North Africa for direct access to West African gold — the wealth from which would fund further exploration.

## Early contacts along the western and central African coasts

Portuguese then other European coastal trade: gold, ivory and pepper first, fortified trading posts, and African rulers controlling the terms of contact.
