# The Portuguese and the First Coastal Contacts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Portugal established the early coastal contacts with West Africa: its distant objective was India, its immediate goal to bypass Muslim North Africa for direct access to West African gold — the wealth from which would fund further exploration.

**2.** What does the part "Early contacts along the western and central African coasts" teach?
**Answer:** Portuguese then other European coastal trade: gold, ivory and pepper first, fortified trading posts, and African rulers controlling the terms of contact.
