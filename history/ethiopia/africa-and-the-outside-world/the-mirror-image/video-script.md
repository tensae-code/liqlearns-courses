# Video script — The Mirror Image — How Europe Pictured Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

Two interpretive additions: (1) inter-continental contact worked by exchange of ideas and technology — "for example, North Africa became Arabized"; (2) the guide's answer on early European contact states that Europeans created an image of Africa as the perverse opposite of Europe — its mirror image — so that Europe's own superiority would appear self-evident by contrast, and Europe's idea of itself was predicated on that image.
