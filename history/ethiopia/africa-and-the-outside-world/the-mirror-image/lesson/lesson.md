# The Mirror Image — How Europe Pictured Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Mirror Image — How Europe Pictured Africa. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Content the guide adds — Arabization, and Europe's mirror-image of Africa *(teacher-guide evidence)*

Two interpretive additions: (1) inter-continental contact worked by exchange of ideas and technology — "for example, North Africa became Arabized"; (2) the guide's answer on early European contact states that Europeans created an image of Africa as the perverse opposite of Europe — its mirror image — so that Europe's own superiority would appear self-evident by contrast, and Europe's idea of itself was predicated on that image.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
