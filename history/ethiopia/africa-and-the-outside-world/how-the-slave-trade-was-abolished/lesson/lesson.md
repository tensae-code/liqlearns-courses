# How the Slave Trade Was Abolished

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: How the Slave Trade Was Abolished. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Abolition of the slave trade

Abolition unfolds country by country — Denmark, Britain (1807), the United States — driven by abolitionist movements, enslaved people's resistance and changing economics, with enforcement by naval patrols.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches, in your own words: Abolition unfolds country by country — Denmark, Britain (1807), the United States — driven by abolitionist movements, enslaved people's resistance and changing economics, with enforcement by naval patrols. Give one real example from the story. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
