# Explorers and Missionaries — Mapping and Converting the Interior — true or false

**1.** Europeans remained deeply ignorant of Africa's interior despite centuries of coastal trade; abolition of the slave trade revived their interest, and between the 1780s and 1870s explorers travelled inland — presented by historians as moved by scientific enquiry, adventure or fame, while their financiers were moved by future trading profit; much of Africa was mapped in the process.
