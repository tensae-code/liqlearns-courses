# Explorers and Missionaries — Mapping and Converting the Interior — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Europeans remained deeply ignorant of Africa's interior despite centuries of coastal trade; abolition of the slave trade revived their interest, and between the 1780s and 1870s explorers travelled inland — presented by historians as moved by scientific enquiry, adventure or fame, while their financiers were moved by future trading profit; much of Africa was mapped in the process.

**2.** What does the part "European explorers and missionaries, 1770–1870" teach?
**Answer:** Explorer and missionary penetration of the interior (Livingstone named among them) maps Africa for Europe and prepares the ground for partition.
