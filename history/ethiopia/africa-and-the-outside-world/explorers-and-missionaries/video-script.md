# Video script — Explorers and Missionaries — Mapping and Converting the Interior

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Europeans remained deeply ignorant of Africa's interior despite centuries of coastal trade; abolition of the slave trade revived their interest, and between the 1780s and 1870s explorers travelled inland — presented by historians as moved by scientific enquiry, adventure or fame, while their financiers were moved by future trading profit; much of Africa was mapped in the process.
