# Legitimate Commerce — Palm Oil and Produce After the Slave Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** As Europeans gradually outlawed the slave trade, West Africa became the field for "legitimate commerce" — legal trade in commodities replacing trade in people, the period's pivot from slave exports to goods exports.

**2.** What does the part "The Legitimate Trade" teach?
**Answer:** After abolition, "legitimate" commerce in palm oil, groundnuts and other produce replaces human cargoes on the coasts — with new pressures of its own.
