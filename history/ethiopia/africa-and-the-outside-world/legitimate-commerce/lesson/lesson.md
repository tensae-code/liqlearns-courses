# Legitimate Commerce — Palm Oil and Produce After the Slave Trade

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Legitimate Commerce — Palm Oil and Produce After the Slave Trade. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The "legitimate" trade

As Europeans gradually outlawed the slave trade, West Africa became the field for "legitimate commerce" — legal trade in commodities replacing trade in people, the period's pivot from slave exports to goods exports.

## The Legitimate Trade

After abolition, "legitimate" commerce in palm oil, groundnuts and other produce replaces human cargoes on the coasts — with new pressures of its own.
