This lesson is about one thing.
As Europeans gradually outlawed the slave trade, West Africa became the field for "legitimate commerce" — legal trade in commodities replacing trade in people, the period's pivot from slave exports to goods exports.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

As Europeans gradually outlawed the slave trade, West Africa became the field for "legitimate commerce" — legal trade in commodities replacing trade in people, the period's pivot from slave exports to goods exports.

After abolition, "legitimate" commerce in palm oil, groundnuts and other produce replaces human cargoes on the coasts — with new pressures of its own.
