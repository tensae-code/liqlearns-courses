# Video script — The Great Depression — 1929 and the World It Broke

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Great Depression, the worst downturn in the industrialised world's history, began with the New York Wall Street crash of October 1929: personal income, tax revenue, profits and prices dropped, international trade plunged by more than 50%, US unemployment rose to 25% (33% in some countries), construction halted, and farm communities suffered as crop prices fell about 60% — by 1933 failing companies were laying off workers in steepening spirals of falling output and employment.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
