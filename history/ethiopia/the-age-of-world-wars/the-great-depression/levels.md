# The Great Depression — 1929 and the World It Broke — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The Great Depression, the worst downturn in the industrialised world's history, began with the New York Wall Street crash of October 1929: personal income, tax revenue, profits and prices dropped, international trade plunged by more than 50%, US unemployment rose to 25% (33% in some countries), construction halted, and farm communities suffered as crop prices fell about 60% — by 1933 failing companies were laying off workers in steepening spirals of falling output and employment. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
