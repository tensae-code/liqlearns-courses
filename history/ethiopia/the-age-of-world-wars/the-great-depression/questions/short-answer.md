# The Great Depression — 1929 and the World It Broke — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Great Depression, the worst downturn in the industrialised world's history, began with the New York Wall Street crash of October 1929: personal income, tax revenue, profits and prices dropped, international trade plunged by more than 50%, US unemployment rose to 25% (33% in some countries), construction halted, and farm communities suffered as crop prices fell about 60% — by 1933 failing companies were laying off workers in steepening spirals of falling output and employment.

**2.** What does the part "Interwar period — capitalist crisis, Fascism and Nazism" teach?
**Answer:** The 1929 crash and Depression shake capitalism; Mussolini's Fascism in Italy and Hitler's Nazism in Germany rise on the wreckage, with militarist Japan alongside.
