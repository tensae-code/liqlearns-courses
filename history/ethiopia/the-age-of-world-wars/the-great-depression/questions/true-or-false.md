# The Great Depression — 1929 and the World It Broke — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Great Depression, the worst downturn in the industrialised world's history, began with the New York Wall Street crash of October 1929: personal income, tax revenue, profits and prices dropped, international trade plunged by more than 50%, US unemployment rose to 25% (33% in some countries), construction halted, and farm communities suffered as crop prices fell about 60% — by 1933 failing companies were laying off workers in steepening spirals of falling output and employment.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Fascism and Nazism — Two Dictatorships Compared.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The Great Depression, the worst downturn in the industrialised world's history, began with the New York Wall Street crash of October 1929: personal income, tax revenue, profits and prices dropped, international trade plunged by more than 50%, US unemployment rose to 25% (33% in some countries), construction halted, and farm communities suffered as crop prices fell about 60% — by 1933 failing companies were laying off workers in steepening spirals of falling output and employment.
