# Lesson plan — The Great Depression — 1929 and the World It Broke

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** The Age of World Wars, 1914–1945 · **Lesson:** The Great Depression — 1929 and the World It Broke
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 12 — The Age of World Wars, 1914–1945 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Great Depression, the worst downturn in the industrialised world's history, began with the New York Wall Street crash of October 1929: personal income, tax revenue, profits and prices dropped, international trade plunged by more than 50%, US unemployment rose to 25% (33% in some countries), construction halted, and farm communities suffered as crop prices fell about 60% — by 1933 failing companies were laying off workers in steepening spirals of falling output and employment.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 12 — The Age of World Wars, 1914–1945 (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. - Loses the story in the names and dates → go back to the one turn: who wanted what, what changed, and what came of it. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: The Great Depression, the worst downturn in the industrialised world's history, began with the New York Wall Street crash of October 1929: personal income, tax revenue, profits and prices dropped, international trade plunged by more than 50%, US unemployment rose to 25% (33% in some countries), construction halted, and farm communities suffered as crop prices fell about 60% — by 1933 failing companies were laying off workers in steepening spirals of falling output and employment. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
