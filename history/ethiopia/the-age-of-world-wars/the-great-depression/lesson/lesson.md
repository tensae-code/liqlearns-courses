# The Great Depression — 1929 and the World It Broke

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Great Depression — 1929 and the World It Broke. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The worldwide economic crisis

The Great Depression, the worst downturn in the industrialised world's history, began with the New York Wall Street crash of October 1929: personal income, tax revenue, profits and prices dropped, international trade plunged by more than 50%, US unemployment rose to 25% (33% in some countries), construction halted, and farm communities suffered as crop prices fell about 60% — by 1933 failing companies were laying off workers in steepening spirals of falling output and employment.

## Interwar period — capitalist crisis, Fascism and Nazism

The 1929 crash and Depression shake capitalism; Mussolini's Fascism in Italy and Hitler's Nazism in Germany rise on the wreckage, with militarist Japan alongside.
