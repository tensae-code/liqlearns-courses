# The Second World War — a War Made by the Peace of 1919 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Fought between the Allied Powers (France, Britain, the USA from December 1941, the USSR) and the Axis (Germany, Italy from June 1940, Japan), with roots in the two preceding decades: the fascist ascensions, and Nazi Germany's complaint that Versailles was a dictated peace.

**2.** What does the part "World War II" teach?
**Answer:** Causes, the main theatres (Europe, North Africa and the Horn — including Ethiopia's liberation in 1941 — the Pacific), and the Allied victory of 1945.
