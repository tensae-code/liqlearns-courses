# The Second World War — a War Made by the Peace of 1919

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Second World War — a War Made by the Peace of 1919. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Second World War, 1939–45

Fought between the Allied Powers (France, Britain, the USA from December 1941, the USSR) and the Axis (Germany, Italy from June 1940, Japan), with roots in the two preceding decades: the fascist ascensions, and Nazi Germany's complaint that Versailles was a dictated peace. Its effects close the unit: the global power balance shifted from Western Europe to the United States and the Soviet Union — the doorway to Unit 5.

## World War II

Causes, the main theatres (Europe, North Africa and the Horn — including Ethiopia's liberation in 1941 — the Pacific), and the Allied victory of 1945.
