# 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Against the Czars' autocracy, discontented sectors of Russian society organised in secret: liberals wanting parliamentary government, Social Revolutionaries urging peasants to seize landlords' land, and the most radical group, the Marxists following Karl Marx.

**2.** What does the part "The Russian Revolution of 1917" teach?
**Answer:** February and October 1917: the Tsar falls, the Bolsheviks under Lenin seize power, and the first socialist state begins — capitalism's Lesson-1 theory turned into history.
