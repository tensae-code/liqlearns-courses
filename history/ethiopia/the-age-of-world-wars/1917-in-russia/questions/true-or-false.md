# 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Against the Czars' autocracy, discontented sectors of Russian society organised in secret: liberals wanting parliamentary government, Social Revolutionaries urging peasants to seize landlords' land, and the most radical group, the Marxists following Karl Marx.
