# Video script — 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Against the Czars' autocracy, discontented sectors of Russian society organised in secret: liberals wanting parliamentary government, Social Revolutionaries urging peasants to seize landlords' land, and the most radical group, the Marxists following Karl Marx.
