# 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The October 1917 Russian Revolution

Against the Czars' autocracy, discontented sectors of Russian society organised in secret: liberals wanting parliamentary government, Social Revolutionaries urging peasants to seize landlords' land, and the most radical group, the Marxists following Karl Marx. The lesson distinguishes the February Revolution from the October Revolution and weighs October's effects — the world's first communist state arising out of the war.

## The Russian Revolution of 1917

February and October 1917: the Tsar falls, the Bolsheviks under Lenin seize power, and the first socialist state begins — capitalism's Lesson-1 theory turned into history.
