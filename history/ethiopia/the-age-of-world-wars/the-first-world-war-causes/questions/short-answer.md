# The First World War — the Alliance Trap and the Spark at Sarajevo — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Great War (1914–18) involved most of Europe with Russia, the United States, the Middle East and other regions.

**2.** What does the part "World War I and its settlement" teach?
**Answer:** Causes (alliances, imperial rivalry, Sarajevo), the trench war, and the Versailles settlement with the League of Nations — whose failure over Ethiopia in 1935 the pupils are positioned to judge from Unit 3.
