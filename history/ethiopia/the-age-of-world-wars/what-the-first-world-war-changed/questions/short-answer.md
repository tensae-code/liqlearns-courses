# What the First World War Changed — Empires Down, America and Communism Up — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The war ran to the Central Powers' defeat, and its consequences remade the world: empires destroyed, several new nation-states established, independence movements promoted in the European colonies, the United States driven to become a world power, and the conditions created for communism's rise in Russia and Hitler's in Germany; Versailles made Germany responsible for the outbreak and imposed loss of foreign territories, a reduced army and navy, and reparations.
