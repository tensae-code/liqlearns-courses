# What the First World War Changed — Empires Down, America and Communism Up — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The war ran to the Central Powers' defeat, and its consequences remade the world: empires destroyed, several new nation-states established, independence movements promoted in the European colonies, the United States driven to become a world power, and the conditions created for communism's rise in Russia and Hitler's in Germany; Versailles made Germany responsible for the outbreak and imposed loss of foreign territories, a reduced army and navy, and reparations.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: 1917 Twice — February and October in Russia.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The war ran to the Central Powers' defeat, and its consequences remade the world: empires destroyed, several new nation-states established, independence movements promoted in the European colonies, the United States driven to become a world power, and the conditions created for communism's rise in Russia and Hitler's in Germany; Versailles made Germany responsible for the outbreak and imposed loss of foreign territories, a reduced army and navy, and reparations.
