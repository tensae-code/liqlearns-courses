# Video script — What the First World War Changed — Empires Down, America and Communism Up

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The war ran to the Central Powers' defeat, and its consequences remade the world: empires destroyed, several new nation-states established, independence movements promoted in the European colonies, the United States driven to become a world power, and the conditions created for communism's rise in Russia and Hitler's in Germany; Versailles made Germany responsible for the outbreak and imposed loss of foreign territories, a reduced army and navy, and reparations.
