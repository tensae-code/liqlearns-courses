# The League of Nations — Collective Security's First Try

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The League of Nations — Collective Security's First Try. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The League of Nations

Founded in 1919, the League sought to maintain peace through collective security and negotiated diplomacy and to improve global welfare — and ultimately failed its principal goal: unable to stop Axis aggression in the 1930s (Ethiopia's unanswered plea in Unit 3 is the book's own case), it did not prevent the next world war.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
