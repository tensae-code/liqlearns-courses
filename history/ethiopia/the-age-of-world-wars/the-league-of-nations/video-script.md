# Video script — The League of Nations — Collective Security's First Try

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Founded in 1919, the League sought to maintain peace through collective security and negotiated diplomacy and to improve global welfare — and ultimately failed its principal goal: unable to stop Axis aggression in the 1930s (Ethiopia's unanswered plea in Unit 3 is the book's own case), it did not prevent the next world war.
