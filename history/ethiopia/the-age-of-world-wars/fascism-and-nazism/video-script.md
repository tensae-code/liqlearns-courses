Ideologically similar inter-war movements, and the main causes of WWII: Mussolini formed his Fascist party in 1919 and a Fascist state stood in Italy from 1922, built on a poor peasantry's and workers' disappointed wartime hopes; Hitler's National Socialist German Workers' Party took power in 1933 on resentment of Versailles.
