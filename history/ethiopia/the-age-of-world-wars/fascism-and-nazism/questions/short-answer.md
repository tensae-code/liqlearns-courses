# Fascism and Nazism — Two Dictatorships Compared — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Ideologically similar inter-war movements, and the main causes of WWII: Mussolini formed his Fascist party in 1919 and a Fascist state stood in Italy from 1922, built on a poor peasantry's and workers' disappointed wartime hopes; Hitler's National Socialist German Workers' Party took power in 1933 on resentment of Versailles.
