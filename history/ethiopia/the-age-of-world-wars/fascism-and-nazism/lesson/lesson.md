# Fascism and Nazism — Two Dictatorships Compared

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Fascism and Nazism — Two Dictatorships Compared. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Fascism in Italy and Nazism in Germany

Ideologically similar inter-war movements, and the main causes of WWII: Mussolini formed his Fascist party in 1919 and a Fascist state stood in Italy from 1922, built on a poor peasantry's and workers' disappointed wartime hopes; Hitler's National Socialist German Workers' Party took power in 1933 on resentment of Versailles. The lesson requires the two to be compared and contrasted and their rises separately explained; the book's figure charts the causes of both rises together.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
