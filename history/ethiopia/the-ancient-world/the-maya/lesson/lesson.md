# The Maya

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Maya. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Maya (Book 1 telling)

First of the unit's three Latin American civilisations, treated in its own subsection before the Inca and Aztecs.

## The Maya (Book 3 telling)

In Mesoamerica the Maya build city-states with pyramids, a calendar and a full writing system in the rainforest.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches, in your own words: First of the unit's three Latin American civilisations, treated in its own subsection before the Inca and Aztecs. Give one real example from the story. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Retell this lesson's story aloud — people, place, the turn, what came of it.
2. Draw the story as a row of scenes, one scene per part of the lesson, with the turn marked.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and note one place where its teaching connects to your own locality.
