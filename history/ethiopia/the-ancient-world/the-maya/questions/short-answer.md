# The Maya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** First of the unit's three Latin American civilisations, treated in its own subsection before the Inca and Aztecs.

**2.** What does the part "The Maya (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** In Mesoamerica the Maya build city-states with pyramids, a calendar and a full writing system in the rainforest.
