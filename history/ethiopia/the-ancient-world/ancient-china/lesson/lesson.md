## China — Shang, Zhou, Confucius and the Wall

Chinese civilisation began in the Yellow River (Hwang Ho) valley in the third millennium BC. The first dynasty, Shang (c.1700–1122 BC), produced bronze vessels, war chariots and a writing system; the Zhou dynasty (from c.1122 BC to 256 BC) saw literary classics and philosophy emerge, above all Confucius (551–479 BC), whose Confucianism teaches a well-ordered society of ranked duties. The ancient Chinese also built the Great Wall.
