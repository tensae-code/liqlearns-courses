# Ancient China — Dynasties, Bronze and Confucius — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Chinese civilisation began in the Yellow River (Hwang Ho) valley in the third millennium BC.

**2.** What does the part "Ancient China" teach?
**Answer:** Shang and Zhou China: the Mandate of Heaven, oracle-bone writing becoming characters, and Confucian thought ordering society.
