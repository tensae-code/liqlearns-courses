# Mesopotamia — Sumer, City-States and First Laws — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Between the Tigris and Euphrates (modern Iraq, eastern Syria, southern Turkey) arose the earliest river-valley civilisation: not one unified kingdom but warring city-states, unlike Egypt.

**2.** What does the part "Mesopotamia" teach?
**Answer:** In the Tigris–Euphrates valley: Sumerian cities, cuneiform, Hammurabi's law code, and successive Babylonian and Assyrian empires.
