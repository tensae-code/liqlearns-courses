# Ancient Egypt — Pharaohs, Kingdoms and the Nile — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** A Nile civilisation from the early 4th millennium BC, divided into Pre-dynastic and Old, Middle and New Kingdoms, ruled by pharaohs — themselves held to be gods in a polytheist religion, with belief in life after death — supported by civilian officials; its enduring achievements are placed in building technology (the pyramid age) and writing, using papyrus.

**2.** What does the part "Ancient Egypt (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** Egypt grows on the Nile: pharaonic government, religion, writing (hieroglyphs), pyramids and the calendar, and its relations southward.
