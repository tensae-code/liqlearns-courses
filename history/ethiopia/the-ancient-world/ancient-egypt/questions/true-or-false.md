# Ancient Egypt — Pharaohs, Kingdoms and the Nile — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A Nile civilisation from the early 4th millennium BC, divided into Pre-dynastic and Old, Middle and New Kingdoms, ruled by pharaohs — themselves held to be gods in a polytheist religion, with belief in life after death — supported by civilian officials; its enduring achievements are placed in building technology (the pyramid age) and writing, using papyrus.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Nubia, Kush and Meroe.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: A Nile civilisation from the early 4th millennium BC, divided into Pre-dynastic and Old, Middle and New Kingdoms, ruled by pharaohs — themselves held to be gods in a polytheist religion, with belief in life after death — supported by civilian officials; its enduring achievements are placed in building technology (the pyramid age) and writing, using papyrus.
