# Ancient Egypt — Pharaohs, Kingdoms and the Nile

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Ancient Egypt — Pharaohs, Kingdoms and the Nile. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Ancient Egypt (Book 1 telling)

A Nile civilisation from the early 4th millennium BC, divided into Pre-dynastic and Old, Middle and New Kingdoms, ruled by pharaohs — themselves held to be gods in a polytheist religion, with belief in life after death — supported by civilian officials; its enduring achievements are placed in building technology (the pyramid age) and writing, using papyrus.

## Ancient Egypt (Book 3 telling)

Egypt grows on the Nile: pharaonic government, religion, writing (hieroglyphs), pyramids and the calendar, and its relations southward.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
