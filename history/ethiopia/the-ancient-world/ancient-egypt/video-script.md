# Video script — Ancient Egypt — Pharaohs, Kingdoms and the Nile

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A Nile civilisation from the early 4th millennium BC, divided into Pre-dynastic and Old, Middle and New Kingdoms, ruled by pharaohs — themselves held to be gods in a polytheist religion, with belief in life after death — supported by civilian officials; its enduring achievements are placed in building technology (the pyramid age) and writing, using papyrus.
