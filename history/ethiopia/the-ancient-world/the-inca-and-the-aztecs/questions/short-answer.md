# The Inca and the Aztecs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The unit's remaining pre-Columbian civilisations, each in its own subsection: the Inca of the Andes and the Aztecs of Mexico, completing the American side of the ancient-world survey.

**2.** What does the part "The Aztecs" teach?
**Answer:** The Aztecs build Tenochtitlan on a lake, rule an empire by tribute, and practise a religion centred on sacrifice.
