# The Inca and the Aztecs

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Inca and the Aztecs. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Inca and the Aztecs

The unit's remaining pre-Columbian civilisations, each in its own subsection: the Inca of the Andes and the Aztecs of Mexico, completing the American side of the ancient-world survey.

## The Aztecs

The Aztecs build Tenochtitlan on a lake, rule an empire by tribute, and practise a religion centred on sacrifice.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
