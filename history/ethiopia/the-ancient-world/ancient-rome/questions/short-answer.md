# Ancient Rome — Law, Roads and Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Rome began c.753 BC on hills above the Tiber and passed from kingdom through Republic (from 509 BC) to Empire.

**2.** What does the part "Civilizations in Europe — Rome" teach?
**Answer:** Rome from republic to empire: law, legions, roads and engineering, citizenship, and the empire's division and western fall by c.476 AD.
