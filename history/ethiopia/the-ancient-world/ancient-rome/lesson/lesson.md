# Ancient Rome — Law, Roads and Empire

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Ancient Rome — Law, Roads and Empire. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Ancient Rome

Rome began c.753 BC on hills above the Tiber and passed from kingdom through Republic (from 509 BC) to Empire. Its legacy: Roman law as foundation of most Western European and Latin American legal systems; engineering — roads, bridges, aqueducts; public baths and amphitheatres, the Colosseum pictured; provincials' loyalty, and eventually citizenship for all free males.

## Civilizations in Europe — Rome

Rome from republic to empire: law, legions, roads and engineering, citizenship, and the empire's division and western fall by c.476 AD.
