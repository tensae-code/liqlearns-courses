# Nubia, Kush and Meroe

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Nubia, Kush and Meroe. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Nubia

Egypt's southern neighbour and rival-partner: a kingdom of powerful kings and queens whose economy rested on agriculture, pastoralism and trade, which influenced and was influenced by Egypt politically and culturally, and which was famous besides its building for its iron industry.

## Kush–Meroe

South of Egypt, Kush — Kerma, Napata and Meroe in turn — rules Egypt as the 25th Dynasty, then retreats south to iron-working Meroe with its own script and pyramids. — Printed pp. 28–28 (within §2.1).
