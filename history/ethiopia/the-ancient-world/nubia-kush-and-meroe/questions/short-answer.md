# Nubia, Kush and Meroe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Egypt's southern neighbour and rival-partner: a kingdom of powerful kings and queens whose economy rested on agriculture, pastoralism and trade, which influenced and was influenced by Egypt politically and culturally, and which was famous besides its building for its iron industry.

**2.** What does the part "Kush–Meroe" teach?
**Answer:** South of Egypt, Kush — Kerma, Napata and Meroe in turn — rules Egypt as the 25th Dynasty, then retreats south to iron-working Meroe with its own script and pyramids.
