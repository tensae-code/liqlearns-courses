# Nubia, Kush and Meroe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Egypt's southern neighbour and rival-partner: a kingdom of powerful kings and queens whose economy rested on agriculture, pastoralism and trade, which influenced and was influenced by Egypt politically and culturally, and which was famous besides its building for its iron industry.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Mesopotamia — Sumer, City-States and First Laws.
