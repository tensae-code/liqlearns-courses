# Ancient India — Indus Cities, Hinduism and Buddhism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first Indian civilisation began in the Indus Valley (c.2500 BC, at its height c.1500 BC) with its own counting, measuring, weighing and writing systems, irrigation ditches and canals, trade reaching Mesopotamia, and artisans working copper, bronze and gold.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Ancient China — Dynasties, Bronze and Confucius.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The first Indian civilisation began in the Indus Valley (c.2500 BC, at its height c.1500 BC) with its own counting, measuring, weighing and writing systems, irrigation ditches and canals, trade reaching Mesopotamia, and artisans working copper, bronze and gold.
