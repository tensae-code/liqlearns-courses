## Ancient Greece

Greece, emerging about 2,500 years ago, is called the birthplace of Western civilisation: culturally united Hellenes (common language, religion, culture) who never united politically, living in poleis — city-states of a town plus its villages and farmland, Athens and Sparta the best known. Classical and Hellenistic achievements listed: architecture (the Parthenon), the invention of drama, marble and bronze sculpture, history-writing (Herodotus on the Persian Wars, Thucydides' unbiased account of the Peloponnesian War), and medicine.
