# Ancient Greece — City-States and a Classical Legacy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Greece, emerging about 2,500 years ago, is called the birthplace of Western civilisation: culturally united Hellenes (common language, religion, culture) who never united politically, living in poleis — city-states of a town plus its villages and farmland, Athens and Sparta the best known.

**2.** What does the part "Civilizations in Europe — Greece" teach?
**Answer:** Greek city-states, Athens and Sparta contrasted, democracy, philosophy, and Alexander's Hellenistic spread.
