# Video script — Christianity — From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Christianity began in 1st-century Palestine on the teaching of Jesus, born at Bethlehem in Roman Judea; Rome first saw Christians as a threat and persecuted them (from Nero, AD 54–68).

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Christianity began in 1st-century Palestine on the teaching of Jesus, born at Bethlehem in Roman Judea; Rome first saw Christians as a threat and persecuted them (from Nero, AD 54–68).

Christianity begins in Roman Judea, spreads through the empire despite persecution, is legalised under Constantine and becomes the state religion — the bridge into the medieval units.

Remember it this way: Christianity began in 1st-century Palestine on the teaching of Jesus, born at Bethlehem in Roman Judea; Rome first saw Christians as a threat and persecuted them (from Nero, AD 54–68).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
