# Christianity — From Persecuted Sect to State Religion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Christianity began in 1st-century Palestine on the teaching of Jesus, born at Bethlehem in Roman Judea; Rome first saw Christians as a threat and persecuted them (from Nero, AD 54–68).

**2.** What does the part "The rise and spread of Christianity (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** Christianity begins in Roman Judea, spreads through the empire despite persecution, is legalised under Constantine and becomes the state religion — the bridge into the medieval units.
