# Lesson plan — Christianity — From Persecuted Sect to State Religion

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Christianity — From Persecuted Sect to State Religion
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Christianity began in 1st-century Palestine on the teaching of Jesus, born at Bethlehem in Roman Judea; Rome first saw Christians as a threat and persecuted them (from Nero, AD 54–68).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Ancient World (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Christianity began in 1st-century Palestine on the teaching of Jesus, born at Bethlehem in Roman Judea; Rome first saw Christians as a threat and persecuted them (from Nero, AD 54–68).
2. Give one real example of it from the story the lesson tells.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as Ethiopia's books themselves work, telling the past as stories to be asked about, placed and retold.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE — a picture of a map of Christianity's spread from Palestine across the Roman world → the student points to the people and the place and says who is who and where it happens.
   - ADD VIDEO — a video telling a story-style script of Constantine's conversion and the Edict of Milan → the student listens and answers along.
   - ADD IMAGE — a picture of a map of Christianity's spread to c.500 AD → the student points to the people and the place and says who is who and where it happens.
   - ADD GIF — a short GIF of a spread map shading outward century by century — yes → the student watches and says what changed.
   - ADD VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student listens and answers along.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's one thing and gives a real example (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the story in the names and dates → go back to the one turn: who wanted what, what changed, and what came of it. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: Christianity began in 1st-century Palestine on the teaching of Jesus, born at Bethlehem in Roman Judea; Rome first saw Christians as a threat and persecuted them (from Nero, AD 54–68). Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
