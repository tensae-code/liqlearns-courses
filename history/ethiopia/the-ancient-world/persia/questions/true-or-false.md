# Persia — Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From the land of modern Iran and Afghanistan, speakers of an "Aryan" language built the Persian Empire — founded by Cyrus the Great c.550 BC, the largest empire of the ancient world, stretching from the Balkans to the Indus Valley and into North Africa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Ancient India — Indus Cities, Hinduism and Buddhism.
