# Persia — Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Persia — Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Persia

From the land of modern Iran and Afghanistan, speakers of an "Aryan" language built the Persian Empire — founded by Cyrus the Great c.550 BC, the largest empire of the ancient world, stretching from the Balkans to the Indus Valley and into North Africa. Its contributions are placed in government, law and religion: many new roads, the world's first postal service, and Zoroastrianism, founded by the prophet Zoroaster.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
