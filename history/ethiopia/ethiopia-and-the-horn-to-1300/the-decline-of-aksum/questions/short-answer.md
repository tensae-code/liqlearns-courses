# The Decline of Aksum — Losing the Sea, Retreating South — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** From the late 6th century Aksum lost its Arabian territories and Red Sea position (governors and soldiers expelled c.580–590), internal disturbances compounded the loss, Beja pressure from the north could not be checked, and the state retreated south — to Kubar in southern Tigray and by the late 9th century into Agaw country — setting up the Zagwe succession.
