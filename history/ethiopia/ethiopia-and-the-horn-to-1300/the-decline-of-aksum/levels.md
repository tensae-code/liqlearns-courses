# The Decline of Aksum — Losing the Sea, Retreating South — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: From the late 6th century Aksum lost its Arabian territories and Red Sea position (governors and soldiers expelled c.580–590), internal disturbances compounded the loss, Beja pressure from the north could not be checked, and the state retreated south — to Kubar in southern Tigray and by the late 9th century into Agaw country — setting up the Zagwe succession. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
