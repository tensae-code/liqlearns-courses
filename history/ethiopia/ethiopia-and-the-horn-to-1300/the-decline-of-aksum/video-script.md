# Video script — The Decline of Aksum — Losing the Sea, Retreating South

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From the late 6th century Aksum lost its Arabian territories and Red Sea position (governors and soldiers expelled c.580–590), internal disturbances compounded the loss, Beja pressure from the north could not be checked, and the state retreated south — to Kubar in southern Tigray and by the late 9th century into Agaw country — setting up the Zagwe succession.
