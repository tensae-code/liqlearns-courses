# The Peopling of Ethiopia — Push, Pull, Fission and Fusion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Ethiopia's ethnocultural groups formed by a long process of fission and fusion: people moved under push factors (shortage of resources, conflict) and pull factors (available resources, better living), in small groups and larger movements, producing the present settlement pattern — traditions of origin are weighed as evidence.

**2.** What does the part "Settlement of the peoples of the region" teach?
**Answer:** The peopling and settlement patterns of the Ethiopian region are summarised from linguistic, oral and archaeological evidence.
