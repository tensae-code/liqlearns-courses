# Video script — The Peopling of Ethiopia — Push, Pull, Fission and Fusion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ethiopia's ethnocultural groups formed by a long process of fission and fusion: people moved under push factors (shortage of resources, conflict) and pull factors (available resources, better living), in small groups and larger movements, producing the present settlement pattern — traditions of origin are weighed as evidence.
