# The Zagwe and the Churches of Lalibela — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Around 1150 AD Mera Tekle Haymanot founded the Zagwe dynasty from the local Agaw ruling class of Lasta, with a new capital at Adefa (Roha).

**2.** What does the part "The Zagwe Dynasty" teach?
**Answer:** The Zagwe — Agaw kings ruling from Lasta after Aksum — remembered above all for the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela under King Lalibela.
