## The Zagwe dynasty

Around 1150 AD Mera Tekle Haymanot founded the Zagwe dynasty from the local Agaw ruling class of Lasta, with a new capital at Adefa (Roha). The book stresses this was a shift of power — from the Semitic-speaking Aksumite rulers to Cushitic-speaking Agaw — not a new kingdom. Its economy was agricultural with trade income, the Zeila port now the main outlet; its rulers are the dynasty of the rock-hewn church age that follows in Ethiopian memory.

## The Zagwe Dynasty

The Zagwe — Agaw kings ruling from Lasta after Aksum — remembered above all for the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela under King Lalibela.
