# How the Great Religions Reached Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Indigenous beliefs, the coming of Judaism, Christianity's arrival at Aksum in the 4th century (Frumentius, Ezana, the Nine Saints) and Islam's arrival in the 7th century (the first hijra to Aksum) are taught in sequence.

**2.** What does the part "Religions as historical actors" teach?
**Answer:** Christianity, Islam and Judaism are each introduced where they historically arrive (Units 2, 3, 4) and then tracked as state-shaping forces in Ethiopia and Africa — a recurring analytical thread, not a separate religion course.
