# How the Great Religions Reached Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Indigenous beliefs, the coming of Judaism, Christianity's arrival at Aksum in the 4th century (Frumentius, Ezana, the Nine Saints) and Islam's arrival in the 7th century (the first hijra to Aksum) are taught in sequence.  b) The Peopling of Ethiopia — Push, Pull, Fission and Fusion  c) Shaka and the Zulu Kingdom  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Indigenous beliefs, the coming of Judaism, Christianity's arrival at Aksum in the 4th century (Frumentius, Ezana, the Nine Saints) and Islam's arrival in the 7th century (the first hijra to Aksum) are taught in sequence.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
