# How the Great Religions Reached Ethiopia

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: How the Great Religions Reached Ethiopia. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Major religions of Ethiopia — indigenous, Judaism, Christianity, Islam

Indigenous beliefs, the coming of Judaism, Christianity's arrival at Aksum in the 4th century (Frumentius, Ezana, the Nine Saints) and Islam's arrival in the 7th century (the first hijra to Aksum) are taught in sequence.

## Religions as historical actors

Christianity, Islam and Judaism are each introduced where they historically arrive (Units 2, 3, 4) and then tracked as state-shaping forces in Ethiopia and Africa — a recurring analytical thread, not a separate religion course. — Units 2–8.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
