# The Sultanate of Shewa — First State on the Zeila Road — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** As northern trade declined, Zeila on the Gulf of Aden became the chief port for south-eastern Ethiopian trade and Islam's main gateway into the Horn; Muslim states formed along the Zeila road linking port to rich interior, and viable Muslim communities and states stood along its branches from the 8th–9th centuries — of these, the Sultanate of Shewa (first under the Makhzumite dynasty, succeeded in 1285 by Ifat's Walasma dynasty per the unit introduction) was the earliest.

**2.** What does the part "The Sultanate of Shewa (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** The Muslim Sultanate of Shewa (from the 9th–10th century) as the earliest known Muslim state in the region, its Makhzumite dynasty and its absorption by Ifat.
