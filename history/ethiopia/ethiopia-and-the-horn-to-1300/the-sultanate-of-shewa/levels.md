# The Sultanate of Shewa — First State on the Zeila Road — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: As northern trade declined, Zeila on the Gulf of Aden became the chief port for south-eastern Ethiopian trade and Islam's main gateway into the Horn; Muslim states formed along the Zeila road linking port to rich interior, and viable Muslim communities and states stood along its branches from the 8th–9th centuries — of these, the Sultanate of Shewa (first under the Makhzumite dynasty, succeeded in 1285 by Ifat's Walasma dynasty per the unit introduction) was the earliest. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
