## Christianity in Ethiopia

Christianity was introduced in the early 4th century AD: Frumentius (Freminatos) was consecrated by Patriarch Athanasius of Alexandria as Abuna Salama, first bishop of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, and Alexandria supplied Ethiopia's church head until 1959, when Abune Baselios became the first Ethiopian Patriarch. Christianity expanded further under the Zagwe (1150–1270) with growth in art, literature, architecture and music. Later layers are also placed: the Jesuit Catholic attempt of the 16th–17th centuries (Susenyos's 1622 conversion and its failure) and Protestantism's arrival mainly in the early 19th century, spreading widely in the 20th through missions.
