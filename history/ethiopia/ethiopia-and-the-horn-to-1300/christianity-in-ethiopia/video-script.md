# Video script — Christianity in Ethiopia — Frumentius to an Ethiopian Patriarch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Christianity was introduced in the early 4th century AD: Frumentius (Freminatos) was consecrated by Patriarch Athanasius of Alexandria as Abuna Salama, first bishop of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, and Alexandria supplied Ethiopia's church head until 1959, when Abune Baselios became the first Ethiopian Patriarch.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
