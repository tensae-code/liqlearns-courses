# Christianity in Ethiopia — Frumentius to an Ethiopian Patriarch — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Christianity was introduced in the early 4th century AD: Frumentius (Freminatos) was consecrated by Patriarch Athanasius of Alexandria as Abuna Salama, first bishop of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, and Alexandria supplied Ethiopia's church head until 1959, when Abune Baselios became the first Ethiopian Patriarch.
