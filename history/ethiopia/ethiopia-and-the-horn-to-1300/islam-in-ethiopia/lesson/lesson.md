## Islam in Ethiopia

Islam, founded by the Prophet Mohammed c.610 AD, reached Ethiopia peacefully and first as refuge: persecuted early Muslims were received by the Aksumite king (the Al-Nejashi mosque pictured), and the Prophet is said to have exempted Ethiopia from jihad in return. After Adulis declined and Arab control of the Red Sea grew, Islam entered the interior along trade routes from the port of Zeila, forming Muslim sultanates — Shewa the earliest — and spreading to Harar, Shewa, Wollo and the south-central regions.
