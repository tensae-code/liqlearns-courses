# The Kingdom of Damot — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Damot, south of the Abbay, a wealthy pagan kingdom of gold and slaves in trade with the highlands, later absorbed into the Christian kingdom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: The Dark Age — Rome Falls, Europe Goes Rural.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Damot, south of the Abbay, a wealthy pagan kingdom of gold and slaves in trade with the highlands, later absorbed into the Christian kingdom.
