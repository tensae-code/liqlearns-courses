# Aksum — Stelae, Adulis and a Red Sea Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Aksum emerged in the 1st century AD with its nucleus and political-religious centre at the city of Aksum, adorned with temples and monolithic stelae.

**2.** This lesson teaches: The Decline of Aksum — Losing the Sea, Retreating South.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Aksum emerged in the 1st century AD with its nucleus and political-religious centre at the city of Aksum, adorned with temples and monolithic stelae.
