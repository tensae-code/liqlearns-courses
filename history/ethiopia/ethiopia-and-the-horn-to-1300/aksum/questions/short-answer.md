# Aksum — Stelae, Adulis and a Red Sea Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Aksum emerged in the 1st century AD with its nucleus and political-religious centre at the city of Aksum, adorned with temples and monolithic stelae.

**2.** What does the part "The Aksumite Kingdom" teach?
**Answer:** Aksum's rise: Red Sea trade through Adulis, Ezana's conversion, coinage, Ge'ez script, stelae and obelisks, Kaleb's Himyar expedition, and the kingdom's long decline as trade routes shifted.
