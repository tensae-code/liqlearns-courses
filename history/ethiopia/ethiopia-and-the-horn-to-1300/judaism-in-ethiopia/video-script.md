# Video script — Judaism in Ethiopia — the Bete-Israel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Judaism is established in the Old Testament teachings and resembles Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity in several practices; in Ethiopia it is followed by the Bete-Israel ("house of Israel"), who trace descent from Israelite tribes, formerly lived in north-west Ethiopia, preserved their tradition for generations under pressure from Christian neighbours, and largely left for Israel in the flights of the 1980s–1990s.
