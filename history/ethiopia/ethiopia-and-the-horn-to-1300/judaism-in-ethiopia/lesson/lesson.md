## Judaism and the Bete-Israel

Judaism is established in the Old Testament teachings and resembles Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity in several practices; in Ethiopia it is followed by the Bete-Israel ("house of Israel"), who trace descent from Israelite tribes, formerly lived in north-west Ethiopia, preserved their tradition for generations under pressure from Christian neighbours, and largely left for Israel in the flights of the 1980s–1990s. How and when Judaism reached Ethiopia is noted as still debated.

## The Bete-Israel (Ethiopian Jews)
