# Judaism in Ethiopia — the Bete-Israel — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Judaism is established in the Old Testament teachings and resembles Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity in several practices; in Ethiopia it is followed by the Bete-Israel ("house of Israel"), who trace descent from Israelite tribes, formerly lived in north-west Ethiopia, preserved their tradition for generations under pressure from Christian neighbours, and largely left for Israel in the flights of the 1980s–1990s.

**2.** What does the part "The Bete-Israel (Ethiopian Jews)" teach?
**Answer:** The Bete-Israel: their origins as debated by historians, their crafts, landholding and relations with the Christian kingdom to the 13th century.
