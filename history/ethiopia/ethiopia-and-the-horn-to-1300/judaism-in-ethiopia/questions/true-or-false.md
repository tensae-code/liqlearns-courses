# Judaism in Ethiopia — the Bete-Israel — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Judaism is established in the Old Testament teachings and resembles Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity in several practices; in Ethiopia it is followed by the Bete-Israel ("house of Israel"), who trace descent from Israelite tribes, formerly lived in north-west Ethiopia, preserved their tradition for generations under pressure from Christian neighbours, and largely left for Israel in the flights of the 1980s–1990s.
**Answer: True**
