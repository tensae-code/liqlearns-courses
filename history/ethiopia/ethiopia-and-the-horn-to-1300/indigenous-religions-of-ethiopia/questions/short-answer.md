# Indigenous Religions of Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The oldest layer of Ethiopian religion: beliefs native to a given culture and land, in which sedentary farming communities developed priests who prayed for rain, thanksgiving and safety from natural hazards and were paid tribute for their services, with special reverence for ancestors and sacrifice to spirits — the foundation on which later religions landed.
