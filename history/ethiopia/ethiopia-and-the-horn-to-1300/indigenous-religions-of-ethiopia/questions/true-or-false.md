# Indigenous Religions of Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The oldest layer of Ethiopian religion: beliefs native to a given culture and land, in which sedentary farming communities developed priests who prayed for rain, thanksgiving and safety from natural hazards and were paid tribute for their services, with special reverence for ancestors and sacrifice to spirits — the foundation on which later religions landed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Judaism in Ethiopia — the Bete-Israel.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The oldest layer of Ethiopian religion: beliefs native to a given culture and land, in which sedentary farming communities developed priests who prayed for rain, thanksgiving and safety from natural hazards and were paid tribute for their services, with special reverence for ancestors and sacrifice to spirits — the foundation on which later religions landed.
