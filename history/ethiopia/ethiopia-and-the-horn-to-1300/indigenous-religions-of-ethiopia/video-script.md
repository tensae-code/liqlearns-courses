# Video script — Indigenous Religions of Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The oldest layer of Ethiopian religion: beliefs native to a given culture and land, in which sedentary farming communities developed priests who prayed for rain, thanksgiving and safety from natural hazards and were paid tribute for their services, with special reverence for ancestors and sacrifice to spirits — the foundation on which later religions landed.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
