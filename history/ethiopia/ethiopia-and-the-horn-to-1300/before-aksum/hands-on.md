# Hands-on — Before Aksum — Punt, Damat and the Early States

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the lesson's one thing out loud, in your own words: Aksum was not the first centralised polity of the region: principalities rose and fell before it — Punt, Hawlti-Melazo, Addi-Seglemeni, Metera, Kaskase and Da'amat are named — in a geographical setting tied to the Red Sea and the northern highlands, interacting with the world beyond. Hold it in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: draw the story as a row of scenes, one scene per part of the lesson, with the people, the place and the turn in each scene. Say each scene aloud as you draw it: who is there, what happens, what changes.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see its teaching at work — a story of the past being told, asked about, placed in time or acted on — note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
