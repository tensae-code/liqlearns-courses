# Video script — Before Aksum — Punt, Damat and the Early States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Aksum was not the first centralised polity of the region: principalities rose and fell before it — Punt, Hawlti-Melazo, Addi-Seglemeni, Metera, Kaskase and Da'amat are named — in a geographical setting tied to the Red Sea and the northern highlands, interacting with the world beyond.

Before Aksum: Damat (Da'amat) and other Red Sea–linked polities of the northern highlands, with Sabaean contacts, temples and inscriptions such as Yeha.

The guide's answer to the pre-Aksumite activity gives a concrete find the student book only names: Addi-Seglemeni lies 10 km south-west of Aksum, where a stone slab was fetched and the oldest Ethiopian monumental inscription discovered; and it states that these sites were initiated by the Habashat and Agazi tribes who migrated into Ethiopia from Saudi Arabia (southern Arabia).
