# Before Aksum — Punt, Damat and the Early States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Aksum was not the first centralised polity of the region: principalities rose and fell before it — Punt, Hawlti-Melazo, Addi-Seglemeni, Metera, Kaskase and Da'amat are named — in a geographical setting tied to the Red Sea and the northern highlands, interacting with the world beyond.

**2.** What does the part "Content the guide adds — Addi-Seglemeni and the Habashat/Agazi migration" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's answer to the pre-Aksumite activity gives a concrete find the student book only names: Addi-Seglemeni lies 10 km south-west of Aksum, where a stone slab was fetched and the oldest Ethiopian monumental inscription discovered; and it states that these sites were initiated by the Habashat and Agazi tribes who migrated into Ethiopia from Saudi Arabia (southern Arabia).
