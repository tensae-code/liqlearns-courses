# Ethiopia's Language Families — Cushitic, Semitic, Omotic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Ethiopia and the Horn hold over 80 languages: those of Ethiopia belong to the Afro-Asiatic and Nilo-Saharan super-families.

**2.** What does the part "Language families of Ethiopia and the Horn" teach?
**Answer:** The region's peoples are sorted by language family — Semitic, Cushitic, Omotic and Nilo-Saharan — and mapped, as the key to who lives where in all later Ethiopian units.
