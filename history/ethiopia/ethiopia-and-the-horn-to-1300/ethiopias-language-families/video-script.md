# Video script — Ethiopia's Language Families — Cushitic, Semitic, Omotic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ethiopia and the Horn hold over 80 languages: those of Ethiopia belong to the Afro-Asiatic and Nilo-Saharan super-families.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The region's peoples are sorted by language family — Semitic, Cushitic, Omotic and Nilo-Saharan — and mapped, as the key to who lives where in all later Ethiopian units.
