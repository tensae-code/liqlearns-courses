## Languages of Ethiopia and the Horn

Ethiopia and the Horn hold over 80 languages: those of Ethiopia belong to the Afro-Asiatic and Nilo-Saharan super-families. Within Afro-Asiatic, Ethiopian languages fall into Cushitic (largest by area and speakers; North, Central and East Cushitic), Semitic (northern — Ge'ez, the church language, Tigre, Tigrinya; southern — Amharic, Argobba, extinct Gafat, Gurage, Silt'e, Harari, Zay) and Omotic (centred on the Omo basin, with Shinasha far north in Benishangul-Gumuz). Some languages are extinct or endangered as speaker numbers fall — Gafat is the worked example. Unity is stressed beyond the diversity.

## Language families of Ethiopia and the Horn

The region's peoples are sorted by language family — Semitic, Cushitic, Omotic and Nilo-Saharan — and mapped, as the key to who lives where in all later Ethiopian units.
