# Lesson plan — East Africa — Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda, Kenya's Hard Road

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Africa Since 1960 and Ethiopia Since 1991 · **Lesson:** East Africa — Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda, Kenya's Hard Road
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 14 — Africa Since 1960 and Ethiopia Since 1991 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Uganda attained independence in 1962 as a single entity under Prime Minister Milton Obote, through a coalition of the royalist Kabaka Yekka movement and Obote's Uganda People's Congress; Tanganyika won independence in 1961 under Julius Nyerere's TANU, Zanzibar in 1963, and the two federated in 1964 as Tanzania; Kenya's road was not smooth — its peasants had been dispossessed, and its struggle was the region's hardest; the former German colonies of Rwanda and Burundi, handed to other powers as League mandates, closed the section (Rwanda a republic at independence in 1962, Burundi a kingdom until November 1966).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 14 — Africa Since 1960 and Ethiopia Since 1991 (recommended, never forced).
