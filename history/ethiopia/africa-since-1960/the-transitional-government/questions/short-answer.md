# 1991 — the Charter and the Transitional Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** After the civil war, the EPRDF — the ethno-nationalist coalition dominated by the TPLF, with EPDM, OPDO and (from 1992) SEPDM — toppled the military junta; the US-sponsored London Conference of May 1991 (the government under Tesfaye Dinka, the EPLF under Isaias Afwerki among the parties) preceded the transition.

**2.** What does the part "The Transitional Government of Ethiopia" teach?
**Answer:** After 1991: the transitional charter, the EPRDF-led transition, Eritrea's independence (1993), and the reordering of the state on ethnic-federal lines.
