# 1991 — the Charter and the Transitional Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the civil war, the EPRDF — the ethno-nationalist coalition dominated by the TPLF, with EPDM, OPDO and (from 1992) SEPDM — toppled the military junta; the US-sponsored London Conference of May 1991 (the government under Tesfaye Dinka, the EPLF under Isaias Afwerki among the parties) preceded the transition.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: The 1995 Constitution and the FDRE — Unity in Diversity, and Its Open Questions.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: After the civil war, the EPRDF — the ethno-nationalist coalition dominated by the TPLF, with EPDM, OPDO and (from 1992) SEPDM — toppled the military junta; the US-sponsored London Conference of May 1991 (the government under Tesfaye Dinka, the EPLF under Isaias Afwerki among the parties) preceded the transition.
