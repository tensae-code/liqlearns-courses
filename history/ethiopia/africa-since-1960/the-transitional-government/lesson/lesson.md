# 1991 — the Charter and the Transitional Government

## The Transitional Government and the 1991 Charter

After the civil war, the EPRDF — the ethno-nationalist coalition dominated by the TPLF, with EPDM, OPDO and (from 1992) SEPDM — toppled the military junta; the US-sponsored London Conference of May 1991 (the government under Tesfaye Dinka, the EPLF under Isaias Afwerki among the parties) preceded the transition. The TGE's 87-member Council of Representatives drafted the National Charter that functioned as a transitional constitution, establishing 14 "self-governing regions" of "nations, nationalities and peoples" and laying the foundation for the federal form of government. — Printed pp. 194–197 (and summary, printed p.207).

## The Transitional Government of Ethiopia

After 1991: the transitional charter, the EPRDF-led transition, Eritrea's independence (1993), and the reordering of the state on ethnic-federal lines.
