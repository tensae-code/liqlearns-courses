# Video script — 1991 — the Charter and the Transitional Government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After the civil war, the EPRDF — the ethno-nationalist coalition dominated by the TPLF, with EPDM, OPDO and (from 1992) SEPDM — toppled the military junta; the US-sponsored London Conference of May 1991 (the government under Tesfaye Dinka, the EPLF under Isaias Afwerki among the parties) preceded the transition.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
