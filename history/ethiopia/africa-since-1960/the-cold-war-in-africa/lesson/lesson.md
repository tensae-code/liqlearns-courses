# The Cold War Fought in Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Cold War Fought in Africa. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Cold War and Africa

The superpowers compete across Africa — Congo, Angola, and the Horn's flip of alliances (Somalia and Ethiopia exchanging patrons in 1977–78) as the type case.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
