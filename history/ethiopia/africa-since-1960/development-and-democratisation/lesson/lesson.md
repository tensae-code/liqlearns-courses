# Development and Democratisation — ADLI and After

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Development and Democratisation — ADLI and After. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Development issues and democratisation challenges

Ethiopia remains one of the world's poorest countries despite its history and resources. In 1992 the TGE adopted a free-market economic model — a clear departure from the previous regime's socialism — and the Agricultural Development-Led Industrialization (ADLI, printed "ALDI") strategy: raise agricultural productivity to generate a surplus that stimulates demand and founds industrialisation of the predominantly agrarian economy. The democratisation process's challenges close the unit's political arc.

## Socio-economic issues

Post-1991 growth and its discontents: poverty, land, unemployment, inflation and development policy as open questions for the pupils' own generation.
