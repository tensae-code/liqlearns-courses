# Apartheid — the System South Africa Built

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Apartheid — the System South Africa Built. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Apartheid in South Africa

South Africa's system of institutionalised racial rule is taught as its own lesson inside the liberation unit — the internal regime the region's struggles, and the OAU's liberation mission, were ultimately aimed at.
