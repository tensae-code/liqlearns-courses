# After the Flag — Independent Africa's Economies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Political independence did not bring economic independence: Africa grew substantially from 1965 to 1974, then trended downward, GDP barely keeping pace with population growth; international debt and drought were two principal adverse factors of the 1980s–90s (the 1980s the "lost decade"), with only modest recovery in the later 1990s.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: The Cold War Fought in Africa.
