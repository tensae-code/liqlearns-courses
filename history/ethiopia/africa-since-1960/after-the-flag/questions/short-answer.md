# After the Flag — Independent Africa's Economies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Political independence did not bring economic independence: Africa grew substantially from 1965 to 1974, then trended downward, GDP barely keeping pace with population growth; international debt and drought were two principal adverse factors of the 1980s–90s (the 1980s the "lost decade"), with only modest recovery in the later 1990s.

**2.** What does the part "Economy and society in independent Africa" teach?
**Answer:** Inherited colonial economies, cash-crop dependence, debt, urbanisation and education's expansion — the development problem stated plainly.
