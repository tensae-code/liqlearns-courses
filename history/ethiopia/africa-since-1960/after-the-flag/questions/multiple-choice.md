# After the Flag — Independent Africa's Economies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Political independence did not bring economic independence: Africa grew substantially from 1965 to 1974, then trended downward, GDP barely keeping pace with population growth; international debt and drought were two principal adverse factors of the 1980s–90s (the 1980s the "lost decade"), with only modest recovery in the later 1990s.  b) The Cold War Fought in Africa  c) Nubia, Kush and Meroe  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Political independence did not bring economic independence: Africa grew substantially from 1965 to 1974, then trended downward, GDP barely keeping pace with population growth; international debt and drought were two principal adverse factors of the 1980s–90s (the 1980s the "lost decade"), with only modest recovery in the later 1990s.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
