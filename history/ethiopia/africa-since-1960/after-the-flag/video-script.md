# Video script — After the Flag — Independent Africa's Economies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Political independence did not bring economic independence: Africa grew substantially from 1965 to 1974, then trended downward, GDP barely keeping pace with population growth; international debt and drought were two principal adverse factors of the 1980s–90s (the 1980s the "lost decade"), with only modest recovery in the later 1990s.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
