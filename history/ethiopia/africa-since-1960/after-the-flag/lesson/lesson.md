# After the Flag — Independent Africa's Economies

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: After the Flag — Independent Africa's Economies. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The struggle for economic independence

Political independence did not bring economic independence: Africa grew substantially from 1965 to 1974, then trended downward, GDP barely keeping pace with population growth; international debt and drought were two principal adverse factors of the 1980s–90s (the 1980s the "lost decade"), with only modest recovery in the later 1990s. The counter-move taught here is regional economic integration — the regional economic organisations African leaders formed to integrate their economies.

## Economy and society in independent Africa

Inherited colonial economies, cash-crop dependence, debt, urbanisation and education's expansion — the development problem stated plainly.
