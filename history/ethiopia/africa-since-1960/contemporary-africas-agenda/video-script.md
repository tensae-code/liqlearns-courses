# Video script — Contemporary Africa's Agenda — the Colonial Hangover and After

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The newly independent states, products of colonialism, kept stronger ties with their former colonial masters — the "colonial hangover", France's neo-colonial grip on its former sub-Saharan colonies the named case — and face contested boundaries (peoples of one ethnic group shared between states), ethnicity and ethnic conflicts, corruption, nepotism and client-patron relations, poverty, unemployment, population pressure, and environmental and climatic problems.
