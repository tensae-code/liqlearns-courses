# Pan-Africanism — From Manchester 1945 to the OAU and the AU — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Pan-Africanism — the idea that peoples of African descent have common interests and should be unified — originated in the New World in the 18th–19th centuries within the Black liberation struggle; at the Fifth Pan-African Congress (Manchester, October 1945) African delegates dominated for the first time and the focus became the liberation of colonised Africa.

**2.** What does the part "Pan-Africanism — from the OAU to the African Union" teach?
**Answer:** Pan-Africanism's idea-history culminates institutionally: the OAU founded in Addis Ababa (1963), its liberation and unity record, and its transformation into the African Union (2002).
