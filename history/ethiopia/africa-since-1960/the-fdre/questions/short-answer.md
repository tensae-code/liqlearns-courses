# The 1995 Constitution and the FDRE — Unity in Diversity, and Its Open Questions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The 1995 constitution established the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia: nine regional states (and two city administrations in the summary's count) in a federation many commentators call "ethnic federalism" for its ethno-linguistic basis of state formation.

**2.** What does the part "Ethiopia after the 1995 Constitution" teach?
**Answer:** The FDRE constitution: federalism, nations' and nationalities' rights including secession's Article 39, elections and the party landscape since 1995.
