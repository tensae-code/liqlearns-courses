# The OAU Judged — Liberator, but Not Peacekeeper — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The verdict is double: Pan-Africanism and the OAU played an irreplaceable role in liberating Africa from European domination and white minority rule in particular; yet the OAU failed to bring peace, prosperity, security and stability — for this reason and others it was dissolved at the beginning of the 21st century and replaced by the African Union.
