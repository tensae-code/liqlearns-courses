# Video script — The OAU Judged — Liberator, but Not Peacekeeper

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The verdict is double: Pan-Africanism and the OAU played an irreplaceable role in liberating Africa from European domination and white minority rule in particular; yet the OAU failed to bring peace, prosperity, security and stability — for this reason and others it was dissolved at the beginning of the 21st century and replaced by the African Union.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
