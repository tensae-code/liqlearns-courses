## Hydro-politics of the Nile (Abay) basin

Ethiopia sources two of the Nile's three major tributaries — the Abay (Blue Nile) and Tekkeze (Atbara) — and contributes about 86% of the Nile's waters, yet from time immemorial the most extensive use has been Egypt's. The 1929 and 1959 agreements shared all the Nile's water between the two lower riparians, Egypt and Sudan, ignoring the other riparians' natural and legal rights; in recent years the upper riparians, Ethiopia in particular, have asserted their development ambitions and pushed for a new agreement enshrining equitable rights and harmonious use.
