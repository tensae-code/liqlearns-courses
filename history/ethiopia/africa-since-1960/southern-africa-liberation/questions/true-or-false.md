# Southern Africa — Liberation at Gunpoint, 1960s–1980 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Southern Africa was the settler belt — British Southern Rhodesia, Northern Rhodesia, Nyasaland, Bechuanaland and Basutoland, South Africa itself (holding South West Africa/Namibia under mandate), and Portugal's Angola and Mozambique — and its 1960s–70s dominant theme was armed struggle: in Zimbabwe, ZANU under Robert Mugabe and ZAPU under Joshua Nkomo fought a long, bitter war that ended white minority rule, bringing majority rule in April 1980 with Mugabe as first prime minister and Rhodesia renamed Zimbabwe; in Namibia, SWAPO organised the long war against South African rule; the Portuguese colonies' independence was delayed until the mid-1970s.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Apartheid — the System South Africa Built.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Southern Africa was the settler belt — British Southern Rhodesia, Northern Rhodesia, Nyasaland, Bechuanaland and Basutoland, South Africa itself (holding South West Africa/Namibia under mandate), and Portugal's Angola and Mozambique — and its 1960s–70s dominant theme was armed struggle: in Zimbabwe, ZANU under Robert Mugabe and ZAPU under Joshua Nkomo fought a long, bitter war that ended white minority rule, bringing majority rule in April 1980 with Mugabe as first prime minister and Rhodesia renamed Zimbabwe; in Namibia, SWAPO organised the long war against South African rule; the Portuguese colonies' independence was delayed until the mid-1970s.
