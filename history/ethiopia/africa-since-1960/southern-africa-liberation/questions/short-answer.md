# Southern Africa — Liberation at Gunpoint, 1960s–1980 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Southern Africa was the settler belt — British Southern Rhodesia, Northern Rhodesia, Nyasaland, Bechuanaland and Basutoland, South Africa itself (holding South West Africa/Namibia under mandate), and Portugal's Angola and Mozambique — and its 1960s–70s dominant theme was armed struggle: in Zimbabwe, ZANU under Robert Mugabe and ZAPU under Joshua Nkomo fought a long, bitter war that ended white minority rule, bringing majority rule in April 1980 with Mugabe as first prime minister and Rhodesia renamed Zimbabwe; in Namibia, SWAPO organised the long war against South African rule; the Portuguese colonies' independence was delayed until the mid-1970s.
