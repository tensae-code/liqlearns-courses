# Video script — Southern Africa — Liberation at Gunpoint, 1960s–1980

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Southern Africa was the settler belt — British Southern Rhodesia, Northern Rhodesia, Nyasaland, Bechuanaland and Basutoland, South Africa itself (holding South West Africa/Namibia under mandate), and Portugal's Angola and Mozambique — and its 1960s–70s dominant theme was armed struggle: in Zimbabwe, ZANU under Robert Mugabe and ZAPU under Joshua Nkomo fought a long, bitter war that ended white minority rule, bringing majority rule in April 1980 with Mugabe as first prime minister and Rhodesia renamed Zimbabwe; in Namibia, SWAPO organised the long war against South African rule; the Portuguese colonies' independence was delayed until the mid-1970s.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
