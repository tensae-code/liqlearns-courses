# Southern Africa — Liberation at Gunpoint, 1960s–1980 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Southern Africa was the settler belt — British Southern Rhodesia, Northern Rhodesia, Nyasaland, Bechuanaland and Basutoland, South Africa itself (holding South West Africa/Namibia under mandate), and Portugal's Angola and Mozambique — and its 1960s–70s dominant theme was armed struggle: in Zimbabwe, ZANU under Robert Mugabe and ZAPU under Joshua Nkomo fought a long, bitter war that ended white minority rule, bringing majority rule in April 1980 with Mugabe as first prime minister and Rhodesia renamed Zimbabwe; in Namibia, SWAPO organised the long war against South African rule; the Portuguese colonies' independence was delayed until the mid-1970s. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
