# Video script — North Africa Frees Itself — Egypt to Algeria's Hard Road

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The first breakthroughs came in the north: Egypt, a British protectorate from 1882, where Saad Zaghlul's nationalist delegation's demand to end the protectorate was backed by demonstrations and strikes across the country; Morocco, independent by negotiated settlement on 2 March 1956, its French and Spanish zones reunited; Tunisia, independent in 1956 under Habib Bourguiba's Neo-Destour Party; and Algeria, where a large settler population, organised in the "Secret Armed Organization" to block independence, forced the FLN under Ahmed Ben Bella into a bitter guerrilla war from 1954 to 1962, ended by agreement and referendum.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
