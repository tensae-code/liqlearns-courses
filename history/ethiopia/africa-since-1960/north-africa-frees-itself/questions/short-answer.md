# North Africa Frees Itself — Egypt to Algeria's Hard Road — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The first breakthroughs came in the north: Egypt, a British protectorate from 1882, where Saad Zaghlul's nationalist delegation's demand to end the protectorate was backed by demonstrations and strikes across the country; Morocco, independent by negotiated settlement on 2 March 1956, its French and Spanish zones reunited; Tunisia, independent in 1956 under Habib Bourguiba's Neo-Destour Party; and Algeria, where a large settler population, organised in the "Secret Armed Organization" to block independence, forced the FLN under Ahmed Ben Bella into a bitter guerrilla war from 1954 to 1962, ended by agreement and referendum.

**2.** What does the part "The road to independence and the rise of independent states" teach?
**Answer:** The independence wave crests (1960, the Year of Africa), Ghana and Nkrumah leading; colonies become states across the continent within two decades.
