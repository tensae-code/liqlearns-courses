# Moggasa and Guddifacha — Belonging by Adoption — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Oromo assimilated non-Oromo people by two adoption mechanisms: Guddifacha, a family's adoption of a child, who then enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's; and Moggasa, the adoption of individuals or whole communities into an Oromo clan, whose members then shared everything equally, gaining protection and material benefit.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Egalitarian Systems of Governance — Luwa, Ballee and Other Traditions.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The Oromo assimilated non-Oromo people by two adoption mechanisms: Guddifacha, a family's adoption of a child, who then enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's; and Moggasa, the adoption of individuals or whole communities into an Oromo clan, whose members then shared everything equally, gaining protection and material benefit.
