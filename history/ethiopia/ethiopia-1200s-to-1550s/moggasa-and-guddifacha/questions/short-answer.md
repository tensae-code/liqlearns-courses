# Moggasa and Guddifacha — Belonging by Adoption — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Oromo assimilated non-Oromo people by two adoption mechanisms: Guddifacha, a family's adoption of a child, who then enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's; and Moggasa, the adoption of individuals or whole communities into an Oromo clan, whose members then shared everything equally, gaining protection and material benefit.

**2.** What does the part "Moggasa and Guddifacha — adoption as integration" teach?
**Answer:** The Oromo assimilated non-Oromo people by two adoption mechanisms: Guddifacha, a family's adoption of a child, who then enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's; and Moggasa, the adoption of individuals or whole communities into an Oromo clan, whose members then shared everything equally, gaining protection and material benefit.
