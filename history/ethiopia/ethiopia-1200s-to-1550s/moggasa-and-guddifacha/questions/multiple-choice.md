# Moggasa and Guddifacha — Belonging by Adoption — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The Oromo assimilated non-Oromo people by two adoption mechanisms: Guddifacha, a family's adoption of a child, who then enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's; and Moggasa, the adoption of individuals or whole communities into an Oromo clan, whose members then shared everything equally, gaining protection and material benefit.  b) Egalitarian Systems of Governance — Luwa, Ballee and Other Traditions  c) Company Rule, Direct Rule, Assimilation  d) none of these
**Answer: a) The Oromo assimilated non-Oromo people by two adoption mechanisms: Guddifacha, a family's adoption of a child, who then enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's; and Moggasa, the adoption of individuals or whole communities into an Oromo clan, whose members then shared everything equally, gaining protection and material benefit.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Moggasa and Guddifacha — adoption as integration  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Moggasa and Guddifacha — adoption as integration** — That is this lesson's first part.
