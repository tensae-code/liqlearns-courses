# Moggasa and Guddifacha — Belonging by Adoption — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The Oromo assimilated non-Oromo people by two adoption mechanisms: Guddifacha, a family's adoption of a child, who then enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's; and Moggasa, the adoption of individuals or whole communities into an Oromo clan, whose members then shared everything equally, gaining protection and material benefit. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
