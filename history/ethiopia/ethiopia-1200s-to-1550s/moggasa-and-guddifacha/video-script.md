# Video script — Moggasa and Guddifacha — Belonging by Adoption

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Oromo assimilated non-Oromo people by two adoption mechanisms: Guddifacha, a family's adoption of a child, who then enjoyed rights equal to a biological child's; and Moggasa, the adoption of individuals or whole communities into an Oromo clan, whose members then shared everything equally, gaining protection and material benefit.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
