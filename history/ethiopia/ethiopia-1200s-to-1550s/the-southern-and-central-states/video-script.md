# Video script — The Southern and Central States — Damot, Kafa and Their Neighbours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
South and centre held states and peoples of indigenous religion: Damot (probably the earliest, south of the Blue Nile, its ruler Motalami converted by Abune Tekle Haymanot in the late 13th century), Kafa, Ennarya, Bizamo, Walaita, Yem, Kambata, the Gurage chiefdoms and the Agaw kingdom of Gojjam — each examined for its political and socio-economic order as the Christian kingdom pressed to bring them under its rule.
