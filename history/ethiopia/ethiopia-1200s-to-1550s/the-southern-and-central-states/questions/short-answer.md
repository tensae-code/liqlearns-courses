# The Southern and Central States — Damot, Kafa and Their Neighbours — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** South and centre held states and peoples of indigenous religion: Damot (probably the earliest, south of the Blue Nile, its ruler Motalami converted by Abune Tekle Haymanot in the late 13th century), Kafa, Ennarya, Bizamo, Walaita, Yem, Kambata, the Gurage chiefdoms and the Agaw kingdom of Gojjam — each examined for its political and socio-economic order as the Christian kingdom pressed to bring them under its rule.

**2.** What does the part "Southern and central states — Gojjam, Bizamo, Wajj, Ganz, Ennarya, the Gonga" teach?
**Answer:** A survey of the western and southern polities bordering the highland kingdom — Gojjam, the small states of Bizamo, Wajj and Ganz, wealthy Ennarya, and the Gonga peoples — their economies and their dealings with the kingdom.
