# Amda Seyon and the Kingdom's Expansion

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Amda Seyon and the Kingdom's Expansion. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Consolidation and expansion under the restored dynasty

Amda Seyon and his successors expand the kingdom south and east by war, marriage and church planting, fixing its medieval shape.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
