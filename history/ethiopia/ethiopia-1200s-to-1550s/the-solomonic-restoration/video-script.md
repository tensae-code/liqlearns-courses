# Video script — 1270 — Yekuno-Amlak and the Solomonic Restoration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In 1270 Yekuno-Amlak, a local ruler in Amhara claiming descent from the Aksumite kings, took power and founded the dynasty that called itself "Solomonic" — the quotation marks are the book's own, because the claim rests on the legend of the Queen of Sheba's union with King Solomon, elaborated in the 14th-century Kibre Negest ("Glory of Kings"), which makes Menelik I, their son, the first Solomonic king.
