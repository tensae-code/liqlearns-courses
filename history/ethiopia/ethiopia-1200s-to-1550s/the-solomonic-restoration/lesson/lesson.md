## The "Solomonic" dynasty — restoration and its legitimising legend

In 1270 Yekuno-Amlak, a local ruler in Amhara claiming descent from the Aksumite kings, took power and founded the dynasty that called itself "Solomonic" — the quotation marks are the book's own, because the claim rests on the legend of the Queen of Sheba's union with King Solomon, elaborated in the 14th-century Kibre Negest ("Glory of Kings"), which makes Menelik I, their son, the first Solomonic king. The legend legitimised successive monarchs' power all the way to Haile Selassie I (1974) and was used to brand the Zagwe as illegitimate.

## Restoration of the "Solomonic" dynasty

In 1270 Yekuno Amlak restores the claimed Solomonic line, legitimised by the *Kebra Nagast* tradition linking the dynasty to Solomon and Sheba.
