# 1270 — Yekuno-Amlak and the Solomonic Restoration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** In 1270 Yekuno-Amlak, a local ruler in Amhara claiming descent from the Aksumite kings, took power and founded the dynasty that called itself "Solomonic" — the quotation marks are the book's own, because the claim rests on the legend of the Queen of Sheba's union with King Solomon, elaborated in the 14th-century Kibre Negest ("Glory of Kings"), which makes Menelik I, their son, the first Solomonic king.

**2.** What does the part "Restoration of the "Solomonic" dynasty" teach?
**Answer:** In 1270 Yekuno Amlak restores the claimed Solomonic line, legitimised by the *Kebra Nagast* tradition linking the dynasty to Solomon and Sheba.
