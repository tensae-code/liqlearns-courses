# 1270 — Yekuno-Amlak and the Solomonic Restoration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1270 Yekuno-Amlak, a local ruler in Amhara claiming descent from the Aksumite kings, took power and founded the dynasty that called itself "Solomonic" — the quotation marks are the book's own, because the claim rests on the legend of the Queen of Sheba's union with King Solomon, elaborated in the 14th-century Kibre Negest ("Glory of Kings"), which makes Menelik I, their son, the first Solomonic king.
