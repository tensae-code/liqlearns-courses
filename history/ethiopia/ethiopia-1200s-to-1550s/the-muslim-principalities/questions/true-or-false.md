# The Muslim Principalities and Sultanates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Once Islam reached Ethiopia and the Horn, a chain of Muslim sultanates formed along the long-distance trade routes, above all the Zeila road: Ifat, Fatigar, Dawaro, Dara, Bali, Hadya, Arababini, Sharka and Adal are named — states whose existence, like the Christian kingdom's, turned on controlling trade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Caravans to Zeila — the Eastern Trade.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Once Islam reached Ethiopia and the Horn, a chain of Muslim sultanates formed along the long-distance trade routes, above all the Zeila road: Ifat, Fatigar, Dawaro, Dara, Bali, Hadya, Arababini, Sharka and Adal are named — states whose existence, like the Christian kingdom's, turned on controlling trade.
