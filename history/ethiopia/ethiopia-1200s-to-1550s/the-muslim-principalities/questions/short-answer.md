# The Muslim Principalities and Sultanates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Once Islam reached Ethiopia and the Horn, a chain of Muslim sultanates formed along the long-distance trade routes, above all the Zeila road: Ifat, Fatigar, Dawaro, Dara, Bali, Hadya, Arababini, Sharka and Adal are named — states whose existence, like the Christian kingdom's, turned on controlling trade.

**2.** What does the part "Expansion of Islam and the Muslim sultanates" teach?
**Answer:** Ifat, Adal and sister sultanates grow on the eastern trade routes; their rise is tied to commerce in slaves, ivory and gold through Zeila.
