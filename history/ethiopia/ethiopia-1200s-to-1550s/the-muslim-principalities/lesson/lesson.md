# The Muslim Principalities and Sultanates

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Muslim Principalities and Sultanates. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Muslim principalities

Once Islam reached Ethiopia and the Horn, a chain of Muslim sultanates formed along the long-distance trade routes, above all the Zeila road: Ifat, Fatigar, Dawaro, Dara, Bali, Hadya, Arababini, Sharka and Adal are named — states whose existence, like the Christian kingdom's, turned on controlling trade. — Printed p. 123 (and unit introduction, printed p.119).

## Expansion of Islam and the Muslim sultanates

Ifat, Adal and sister sultanates grow on the eastern trade routes; their rise is tied to commerce in slaves, ivory and gold through Zeila.
