# Video script — The Muslim Principalities and Sultanates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Once Islam reached Ethiopia and the Horn, a chain of Muslim sultanates formed along the long-distance trade routes, above all the Zeila road: Ifat, Fatigar, Dawaro, Dara, Bali, Hadya, Arababini, Sharka and Adal are named — states whose existence, like the Christian kingdom's, turned on controlling trade.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
