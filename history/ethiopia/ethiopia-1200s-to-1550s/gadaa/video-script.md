# Video script — Gadaa — Government by Age Grades and Eight-Year Succession

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Gadaa is the Oromo indigenous socio-political system of self-governance: society is organised in age grades with power transferring every eight years (the Butta ceremony) at the assembly (chaffe) under leaders such as the Abbaa Gadaa, with named offices — Abbaa Bokku (father of the sceptre), Abbaa Sa'a, Abbaa Sera (father of law), Abbaa Alanga, Abbaa Duulaa (war leader), Abbaa Chaffe (head of assembly).
