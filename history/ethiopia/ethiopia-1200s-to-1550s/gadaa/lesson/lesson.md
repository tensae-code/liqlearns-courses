## The Gadaa system of the Oromo

Gadaa is the Oromo indigenous socio-political system of self-governance: society is organised in age grades with power transferring every eight years (the Butta ceremony) at the assembly (chaffe) under leaders such as the Abbaa Gadaa, with named offices — Abbaa Bokku (father of the sceptre), Abbaa Sa'a, Abbaa Sera (father of law), Abbaa Alanga, Abbaa Duulaa (war leader), Abbaa Chaffe (head of assembly). The book credits it with checks and balances through regular eight-year succession, division of power among executive, legislative and judicial functions, balanced opposition among five parties, power-sharing, laws standing above all, accountability, confession and impeachment (buqqisu), and a testing period between election and investiture.
