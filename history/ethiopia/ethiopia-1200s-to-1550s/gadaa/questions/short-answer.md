# Gadaa — Government by Age Grades and Eight-Year Succession — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Gadaa is the Oromo indigenous socio-political system of self-governance: society is organised in age grades with power transferring every eight years (the Butta ceremony) at the assembly (chaffe) under leaders such as the Abbaa Gadaa, with named offices — Abbaa Bokku (father of the sceptre), Abbaa Sa'a, Abbaa Sera (father of law), Abbaa Alanga, Abbaa Duulaa (war leader), Abbaa Chaffe (head of assembly).

**2.** What does the part "The Gadaa system of the Oromo" teach?
**Answer:** Gadaa is the Oromo indigenous socio-political system of self-governance: society is organised in age grades with power transferring every eight years (the Butta ceremony) at the assembly (chaffe) under leaders such as the Abbaa Gadaa, with named offices — Abbaa Bokku (father of the sceptre), Abbaa Sa'a, Abbaa Sera (father of law), Abbaa Alanga, Abbaa Duulaa (war leader), Abbaa Chaffe (head of assembly).
