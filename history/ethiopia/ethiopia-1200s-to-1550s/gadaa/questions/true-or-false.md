# Gadaa — Government by Age Grades and Eight-Year Succession — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gadaa is the Oromo indigenous socio-political system of self-governance: society is organised in age grades with power transferring every eight years (the Butta ceremony) at the assembly (chaffe) under leaders such as the Abbaa Gadaa, with named offices — Abbaa Bokku (father of the sceptre), Abbaa Sa'a, Abbaa Sera (father of law), Abbaa Alanga, Abbaa Duulaa (war leader), Abbaa Chaffe (head of assembly).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Moggasa and Guddifacha — Belonging by Adoption.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Gadaa is the Oromo indigenous socio-political system of self-governance: society is organised in age grades with power transferring every eight years (the Butta ceremony) at the assembly (chaffe) under leaders such as the Abbaa Gadaa, with named offices — Abbaa Bokku (father of the sceptre), Abbaa Sa'a, Abbaa Sera (father of law), Abbaa Alanga, Abbaa Duulaa (war leader), Abbaa Chaffe (head of assembly).
