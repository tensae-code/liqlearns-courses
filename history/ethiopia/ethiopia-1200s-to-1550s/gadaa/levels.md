# Gadaa — Government by Age Grades and Eight-Year Succession — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Gadaa is the Oromo indigenous socio-political system of self-governance: society is organised in age grades with power transferring every eight years (the Butta ceremony) at the assembly (chaffe) under leaders such as the Abbaa Gadaa, with named offices — Abbaa Bokku (father of the sceptre), Abbaa Sa'a, Abbaa Sera (father of law), Abbaa Alanga, Abbaa Duulaa (war leader), Abbaa Chaffe (head of assembly). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
