# The Oromo Expansion — a Map Redrawn, Peoples Intermingled — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The period's defining movement: expanding Oromo destroyed the old states of Damot, Bizamo and Ennarya; Damot and Gafat peoples were assimilated or pushed north into the Christian community; the Christian kingdom, weakened territorially and financially, shifted its centre from Shewa to Lake Tana and Gondar; Adal shrank to walled Harar.

**2.** What does the part "The Oromo population movement" teach?
**Answer:** From the mid-16th century the Oromo expand northward in waves organised by the *gadaa* generation system, transforming the demography and politics of the whole region — the book's major movement story.
