# The Oromo Expansion — a Map Redrawn, Peoples Intermingled — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The period's defining movement: expanding Oromo destroyed the old states of Damot, Bizamo and Ennarya; Damot and Gafat peoples were assimilated or pushed north into the Christian community; the Christian kingdom, weakened territorially and financially, shifted its centre from Shewa to Lake Tana and Gondar; Adal shrank to walled Harar.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Gadaa — Government by Age Grades and Eight-Year Succession.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The period's defining movement: expanding Oromo destroyed the old states of Damot, Bizamo and Ennarya; Damot and Gafat peoples were assimilated or pushed north into the Christian community; the Christian kingdom, weakened territorially and financially, shifted its centre from Shewa to Lake Tana and Gondar; Adal shrank to walled Harar.
