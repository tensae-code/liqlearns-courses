# Video script — The Oromo Expansion — a Map Redrawn, Peoples Intermingled

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
The period's defining movement: expanding Oromo destroyed the old states of Damot, Bizamo and Ennarya; Damot and Gafat peoples were assimilated or pushed north into the Christian community; the Christian kingdom, weakened territorially and financially, shifted its centre from Shewa to Lake Tana and Gondar; Adal shrank to walled Harar.
