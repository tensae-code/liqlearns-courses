# Video script — Egalitarian Systems of Governance — Luwa, Ballee and Other Traditions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Other peoples' traditional systems close the unit as comparisons to Gadaa: the Sidama Luwa age-grade system rotating every eight years under the Mote and the woma-songo council; the Gedeo ballee system; Konso's decentralised organisation; Burji's elective administration — evidence, in the book's presentation, that self-governance traditions were widespread, not unique to one people.
