## Egalitarian systems of governance

Other peoples' traditional systems close the unit as comparisons to Gadaa: the Sidama Luwa age-grade system rotating every eight years under the Mote and the woma-songo council; the Gedeo ballee system; Konso's decentralised organisation; Burji's elective administration — evidence, in the book's presentation, that self-governance traditions were widespread, not unique to one people. — Printed pp. 143–150 (surveyed through the unit summary and review).
