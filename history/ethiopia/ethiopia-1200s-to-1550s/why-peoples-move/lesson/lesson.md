# Why Peoples Move — Push and Pull in the Horn

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Why Peoples Move — Push and Pull in the Horn. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Population movements — causes

Movement has always shaped the Horn: people move under push factors (resource shortage, military conflict, drought, demographic pressure) and pull factors (resources and a better living environment), at varied scales and in different directions — the analytical frame the next lesson applies to the period's great movement.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
