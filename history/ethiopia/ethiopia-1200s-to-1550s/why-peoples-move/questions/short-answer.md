# Why Peoples Move — Push and Pull in the Horn — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Movement has always shaped the Horn: people move under push factors (resource shortage, military conflict, drought, demographic pressure) and pull factors (resources and a better living environment), at varied scales and in different directions — the analytical frame the next lesson applies to the period's great movement.
