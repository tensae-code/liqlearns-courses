# Video script — Why Peoples Move — Push and Pull in the Horn

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Movement has always shaped the Horn: people move under push factors (resource shortage, military conflict, drought, demographic pressure) and pull factors (resources and a better living environment), at varied scales and in different directions — the analytical frame the next lesson applies to the period's great movement.
