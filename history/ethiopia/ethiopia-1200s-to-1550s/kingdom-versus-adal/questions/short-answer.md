# Kingdom Versus Adal — the Wars of Imam Ahmad, 1529–1559 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The underlying conflict was monopoly of long-distance trade (great wealth, and the link between Ethiopia's peoples) plus territorial expansion: Ifat, defeated in 1332, lost control of the Zeila route to Amda Seyon, and its successors fought to recover independence and the route.

**2.** What does the part "Christian kingdom versus Adal — to the wars of the 1520s–40s" teach?
**Answer:** Rivalry over trade and territory hardens into the wars of Imam Ahmad ibn Ibrahim al-Ghazi (Gragn), whose campaigns of 1529–43 shatter the highland kingdom before Portuguese musketeers help reverse them — told as outline: invasion, near-collapse, and the turning at Wayna Daga (1543).
