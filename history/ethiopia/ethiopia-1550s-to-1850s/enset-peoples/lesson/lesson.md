## The enset-culture peoples — Gurage and Siltie

Southern and south-western Ethiopia, among the country's most densely populated areas, shared the "enset-culture complex": markets served as communication across ethnic borders. The Gurage chiefdoms, west of Lake Ziway (printed "Lake Dambal"), are first mentioned in 14th-century written sources, with oral traditions of descent from Christian settlers from Gura in Eritrea. The Siltie organised through Sera: descent reckoned also through the mother line, a general assembly (YeMule Siltie Melcho) and a judiciary (the Raga system); their economy was mixed agriculture — maize, wheat and spices in the lowlands, enset in the highlands.
