# Harar — Walled City of Trade and Islamic Learning — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** After Ifat's defeat in 1332, members of its ruling family retreated to Harar and established the Sultanate of Adal there c.1380; Harar became a centre of Islamic culture and political power controlling the trade routes from the Gulf of Aden and the Indian Ocean coast, and from the late 16th to the 19th century the walled town was a major trade centre between coast and interior highlands and a seat of Islamic learning since the 14th century; its later Emirate was founded by Ali ibn Dawd.

**2.** What does the part "Harar" teach?
**Answer:** The walled city of Harar under its emirs: a commercial and Islamic scholarly city linking highlands, lowlands and the Gulf of Aden trade.
