# Harar — Walled City of Trade and Islamic Learning — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Ifat's defeat in 1332, members of its ruling family retreated to Harar and established the Sultanate of Adal there c.1380; Harar became a centre of Islamic culture and political power controlling the trade routes from the Gulf of Aden and the Indian Ocean coast, and from the late 16th to the 19th century the walled town was a major trade centre between coast and interior highlands and a seat of Islamic learning since the 14th century; its later Emirate was founded by Ali ibn Dawd.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: The Afar Sultanates and Somali Society.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: After Ifat's defeat in 1332, members of its ruling family retreated to Harar and established the Sultanate of Adal there c.1380; Harar became a centre of Islamic culture and political power controlling the trade routes from the Gulf of Aden and the Indian Ocean coast, and from the late 16th to the 19th century the walled town was a major trade centre between coast and interior highlands and a seat of Islamic learning since the 14th century; its later Emirate was founded by Ali ibn Dawd.
