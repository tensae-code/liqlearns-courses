# Video script — Harar — Walled City of Trade and Islamic Learning

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After Ifat's defeat in 1332, members of its ruling family retreated to Harar and established the Sultanate of Adal there c.1380; Harar became a centre of Islamic culture and political power controlling the trade routes from the Gulf of Aden and the Indian Ocean coast, and from the late 16th to the 19th century the walled town was a major trade centre between coast and interior highlands and a seat of Islamic learning since the 14th century; its later Emirate was founded by Ali ibn Dawd.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.
