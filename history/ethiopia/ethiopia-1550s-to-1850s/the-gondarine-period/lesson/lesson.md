## The Gondarine period

Gondar, founded by Fasiladas c.1636, ended the era of ruling from temporary camps: strategically placed on the long-distance trade route linking the south-west with Massawa and Mettema, it stayed the political centre for two centuries. The first three kings' Gondar saw permanent palaces and churches (the Fasilides castle pictured), growing trade, cash payment for services and other urban elements, and a luxurious court — before decline set in from the early 18th century amid political disorder and church doctrinal controversy. Susenyos's declaration of Catholicism as the official religion (review) belongs to this period's opening crisis.

## The Gondarine Period, 1636–1769

Gondar's century: castle-building emperors, court culture, church debates, and slow weakening as regional lords grow.
