# Sera and Woma — the Cushitic States' Own Governments — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** From the mid-16th century Highland East Cushitic peoples — Hadiya, Kambata, Sidama, Tambaro, Kabena, Halaba, Gedeo, Konso, Burji — formed states relatively free of outside domination.

**2.** What does the part "Southern-systems content confirmation" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's Unit 8 content lists name the indigenous systems directly (Gedeo's and Konso's indigenous governments; Gurage chiefdoms with Yajoka Qicha and Gordanna Sera) — confirming and slightly extending student placements 74–76: the Gurage systems Yajoka Qicha and Gordanna Sera are named only in the guide.
