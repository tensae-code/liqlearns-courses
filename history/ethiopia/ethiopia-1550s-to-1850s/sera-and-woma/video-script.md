# Video script — Sera and Woma — the Cushitic States' Own Governments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
From the mid-16th century Highland East Cushitic peoples — Hadiya, Kambata, Sidama, Tambaro, Kabena, Halaba, Gedeo, Konso, Burji — formed states relatively free of outside domination.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

From the mid-16th century Highland East Cushitic peoples — Hadiya, Kambata, Sidama, Tambaro, Kabena, Halaba, Gedeo, Konso, Burji — formed states relatively free of outside domination.

Sidama-area states under the *sera-woma* order, then short studies of the Gedeo, the terraced Konso with their walled towns, and the Gurage chiefdoms.

The guide's Unit 8 content lists name the indigenous systems directly (Gedeo's and Konso's indigenous governments; Gurage chiefdoms with Yajoka Qicha and Gordanna Sera) — confirming and slightly extending student placements 74–76: the Gurage systems Yajoka Qicha and Gordanna Sera are named only in the guide.

Remember it this way: From the mid-16th century Highland East Cushitic peoples — Hadiya, Kambata, Sidama, Tambaro, Kabena, Halaba, Gedeo, Konso, Burji — formed states relatively free of outside domination.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
