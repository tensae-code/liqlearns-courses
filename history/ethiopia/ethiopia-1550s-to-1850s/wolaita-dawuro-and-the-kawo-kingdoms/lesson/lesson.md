## Southern states II — Wolaita, Dawuro and the Omotic kingdoms

Wolaita (Malla), linked to Damot since the 13th century, kept its Motolami-founded ruling line until the Tigre-Mala replaced it in the late 15th century, and its line lasted into the late 19th; its king's title is Kawo (review). Dawuro was established in the 16th century by the immigrant Kawuka clan, which by c.1700 had built a dynasty. The wider Omotic pattern: a hereditary king titled khati, with subordinate official titles varying by place (Basketo's Dana, Guda, Bitena, Gudena, Chemach; Male's Goda, Kagotoka, Katoydka).

## Omotic states under the kawo/kati system

Wolaita and its neighbours under *kawo* kings, their wars of expansion and resistance to highland pressure.
