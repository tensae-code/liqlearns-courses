# Wolaita, Dawuro and the Kawo Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Wolaita (Malla), linked to Damot since the 13th century, kept its Motolami-founded ruling line until the Tigre-Mala replaced it in the late 15th century, and its line lasted into the late 19th; its king's title is Kawo (review).

**2.** What does the part "Omotic states under the kawo/kati system" teach?
**Answer:** Wolaita and its neighbours under *kawo* kings, their wars of expansion and resistance to highland pressure.
