# Wolaita, Dawuro and the Kawo Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Wolaita (Malla), linked to Damot since the 13th century, kept its Motolami-founded ruling line until the Tigre-Mala replaced it in the late 15th century, and its line lasted into the late 19th; its king's title is Kawo (review).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Sera and Woma — the Cushitic States' Own Governments.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Wolaita (Malla), linked to Damot since the 13th century, kept its Motolami-founded ruling line until the Tigre-Mala replaced it in the late 15th century, and its line lasted into the late 19th; its king's title is Kawo (review).
