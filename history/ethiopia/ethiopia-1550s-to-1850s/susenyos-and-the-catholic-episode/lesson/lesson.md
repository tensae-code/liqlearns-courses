# Susenyos and the Catholic Episode

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Susenyos and the Catholic Episode. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Attempts at consolidation — Susenyos and the Catholic episode

Emperor Susenyos tries to recentralise the kingdom and, converted by Jesuit missionaries, imposes Catholicism — provoking rebellion that forces his abdication (1632).

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
