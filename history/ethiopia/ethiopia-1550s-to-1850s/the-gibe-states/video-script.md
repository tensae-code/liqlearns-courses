# Video script — The Gibe States — From Gadaa to Kingdoms of Coffee and Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In Gibe and Wallaga the expanding Oromo gradually abandoned Gadaa and formed monarchies in the early 19th century: Limmu-Ennarya; Jimma, the strongest, popular under Abba Jifar I (r.1830–1855), resting on Islam's ideological support and commercial prosperity from coffee and the slave trade; Gomma, the earliest Gibe state to accept Islam, under rulers such as Oncho Jilcha; and Gera, known from c.1835 under the warrior-administrator Tullu Gunji, in conflict with Gomma.
