This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: In Gibe and Wallaga the expanding Oromo gradually abandoned Gadaa and formed monarchies in the early 19th century: Limmu-Ennarya; Jimma, the strongest, popular under Abba Jifar I (r.1830–1855), resting on Islam's ideological support and commercial prosperity from coffee and the slave trade; Gomma, the earliest Gibe state to accept Islam, under rulers such as Oncho Jilcha; and Gera, known from c.1835 under the warrior-administrator Tullu Gunji, in conflict with Gomma. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
