# The Gibe States — From Gadaa to Kingdoms of Coffee and Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Gibe and Wallaga the expanding Oromo gradually abandoned Gadaa and formed monarchies in the early 19th century: Limmu-Ennarya; Jimma, the strongest, popular under Abba Jifar I (r.1830–1855), resting on Islam's ideological support and commercial prosperity from coffee and the slave trade; Gomma, the earliest Gibe state to accept Islam, under rulers such as Oncho Jilcha; and Gera, known from c.1835 under the warrior-administrator Tullu Gunji, in conflict with Gomma.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Gumuz, Shinasha and the Gold Sheikhdoms of Benishangul.
