## Eastern states — the Somali clans and Guurti

Somali society combined pastoralists with settled farming and trading communities, organised in clans whose indigenous governing structure was the Guurti, a council of elders deciding criminal and civil cases — a highly democratic process in which all adult males had equal access and participation, working at sub-clan, clan and inter-clan levels.
