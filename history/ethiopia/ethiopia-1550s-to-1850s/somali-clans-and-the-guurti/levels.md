# Somali Clans and the Guurti Council — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Somali society combined pastoralists with settled farming and trading communities, organised in clans whose indigenous governing structure was the Guurti, a council of elders deciding criminal and civil cases — a highly democratic process in which all adult males had equal access and participation, working at sub-clan, clan and inter-clan levels. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
