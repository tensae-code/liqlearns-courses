# Somali Clans and the Guurti Council — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Somali society combined pastoralists with settled farming and trading communities, organised in clans whose indigenous governing structure was the Guurti, a council of elders deciding criminal and civil cases — a highly democratic process in which all adult males had equal access and participation, working at sub-clan, clan and inter-clan levels.
