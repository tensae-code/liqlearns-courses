# The Yejju — the Dynasty Behind the Throne

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Yejju — the Dynasty Behind the Throne. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Yejju dynasty

In 1786 Ali established the Yejju dynasty (the Warra Sheh), which governed northern Ethiopia until the mid-19th century in the name of Gondar's puppet emperors, centred at Debre Tabor. It was the leading power of the Zemene-Mesafint at its strongest under Ras Gugsa (1803–1825), resisted in the north by Tigray's Ras Walda Sellase of Enderta (r.1790–1816) and Dejjazmach Sabagadis (r.1822–1831), whom Gugsa tried to neutralise.
