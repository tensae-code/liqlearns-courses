# Shewa — the Kingdom That Grew Apart — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Around the end of the 17th or beginning of the 18th century the Shewan kingdom grew in the Manz region under its founder, the local chief Negasi: Shewa had been the Christian state's earlier political centre before pressure from Adal and the Oromo expansion shifted the capital to Lake Tana; Gondarine kings such as Iyassu I (r.1682–1706) sent expeditions to control it, but Shewa gradually detached itself — the kingdom that would matter most in the century after this book's period.

**2.** What does the part "The Kingdom of Shewa (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** While the north fragments, Shewa grows under its own dynasty — Sahle Selassie expanding and enriching the kingdom that will later reunify Ethiopia.
