# The Afar Sultanates and Somali Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Afar inhabit the "Afar Triangle" of Ethiopia, Eritrea and Djibouti — land presented both as a cradle of humankind and archaeologically rich, with abundant resources and strategic weight.

**2.** What does the part "Afar sultanates and the Somali people" teach?
**Answer:** The Afar sultanates (Aussa, Bidu, Gobaad, Tajura, Rahayito) controlling the salt and caravan corridors, and Somali clan society and its coastal trade.
