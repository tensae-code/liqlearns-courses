## Do it first

## Eastern states — the Afar sultanates

The Afar inhabit the "Afar Triangle" of Ethiopia, Eritrea and Djibouti — land presented both as a cradle of humankind and archaeologically rich, with abundant resources and strategic weight. Predominantly Muslim pastoralists organised in a confederation of sultanates — Aussa, Bidu, Tadjoura and Rahaito among them — each of several villages with its own culture, linked in the 14th century in Christian sources to the Adal Sultanate.

## Afar sultanates and the Somali people

The Afar sultanates (Aussa, Bidu, Gobaad, Tajura, Rahayito) controlling the salt and caravan corridors, and Somali clan society and its coastal trade. — Printed pp. 218–218 (within §8.1.3).
