# Video script — Gumuz, Shinasha and the Gold Sheikhdoms of Benishangul

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Along the narrow lowland of the Ethio-Sudanese border lived Nilo-Saharan-speaking peoples in a gold-producing region known since Punt and associated with "silent commerce".

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Along the narrow lowland of the Ethio-Sudanese border lived Nilo-Saharan-speaking peoples in a gold-producing region known since Punt and associated with "silent commerce".

The western borderlands: the sheikhdoms of Benishangul-Gumuz tied to Sudanese trade, and the local chiefs of the Gambella peoples.

Remember it this way: Along the narrow lowland of the Ethio-Sudanese border lived Nilo-Saharan-speaking peoples in a gold-producing region known since Punt and associated with "silent commerce".

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
