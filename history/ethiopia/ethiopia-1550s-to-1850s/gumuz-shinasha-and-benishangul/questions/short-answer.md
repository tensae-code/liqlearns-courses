# Gumuz, Shinasha and the Gold Sheikhdoms of Benishangul — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Along the narrow lowland of the Ethio-Sudanese border lived Nilo-Saharan-speaking peoples in a gold-producing region known since Punt and associated with "silent commerce".

**2.** What does the part "Benishangul-Gumuz sheikhdoms and the Gambella chiefs" teach?
**Answer:** The western borderlands: the sheikhdoms of Benishangul-Gumuz tied to Sudanese trade, and the local chiefs of the Gambella peoples.
