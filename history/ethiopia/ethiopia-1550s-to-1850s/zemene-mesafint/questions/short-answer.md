# Zemene-Mesafint — the Era of Warlords — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** From the assassination of Iyoas to 1855, real power lay with regional warlords behind puppet emperors at Gondar.

**2.** What does the part "The Zemene Mesafint, 1769–1855" teach?
**Answer:** The Era of the Princes: emperors become puppets at Gondar while warlords — Ras Mikael Sehul, the Yejju lords and others — rule the regions and fight for supremacy.
