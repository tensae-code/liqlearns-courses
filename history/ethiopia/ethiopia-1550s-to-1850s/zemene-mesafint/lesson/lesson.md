# Zemene-Mesafint — the Era of Warlords

## The Zemene-Mesafint — the Era of Warlords

From the assassination of Iyoas to 1855, real power lay with regional warlords behind puppet emperors at Gondar. The period's politics were made in faction: the Wollo party of Itege Wubit against the Qara party of her mother-in-law Itege Mentewab, regent for Iyoas (r.1755–1769), who gave the Wallo Oromo a power base; Ras Mikael Sehul, the kingmaker, was defeated by a coalition of Gojjam, Amhara, Lasta and Wollo lords at Sarba-Kussa in 1771 and retired to rule Tigray until 1780. Continuous war damaged trade: merchants faced robbery and heavy taxes at each warlord's gate (Shewa, the review notes, stood apart from these wars).

## The Zemene Mesafint, 1769–1855

The Era of the Princes: emperors become puppets at Gondar while warlords — Ras Mikael Sehul, the Yejju lords and others — rule the regions and fight for supremacy.
