# Lesson plan — Zemene-Mesafint — the Era of Warlords

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia, Mid-16th to Mid-19th Century · **Lesson:** Zemene-Mesafint — the Era of Warlords
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 8 — Ethiopia, Mid-16th to Mid-19th Century (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From the assassination of Iyoas to 1855, real power lay with regional warlords behind puppet emperors at Gondar.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 8 — Ethiopia, Mid-16th to Mid-19th Century (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: From the assassination of Iyoas to 1855, real power lay with regional warlords behind puppet emperors at Gondar.
2. Give one real example of it from the story the lesson tells.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as Ethiopia's books themselves work, telling the past as stories to be asked about, placed and retold.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE — a picture of a map of Ethiopia splintered among rival lords' territories → the student points to the people and the place and says who is who and where it happens.
   - ADD VIDEO — a video telling a story-style script — Mentewab, the empress who ruled through her grandson → the student listens and answers along.
   - ADD IMAGE — a picture of a map of the warlord regions c.1800 → the student points to the people and the place and says who is who and where it happens.
   - ADD VIDEO — a video telling a story script on a puppet emperor and the ras who ruled him → the student listens and answers along.
   - ADD VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student listens and answers along.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's one thing and gives a real example (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the story in the names and dates → go back to the one turn: who wanted what, what changed, and what came of it. Name those three aloud, in order.
- Go back to the one thing: From the assassination of Iyoas to 1855, real power lay with regional warlords behind puppet emperors at Gondar. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
