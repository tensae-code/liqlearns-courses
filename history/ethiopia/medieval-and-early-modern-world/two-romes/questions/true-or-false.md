# Two Romes — the Fallen West and the Byzantine East — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The West falls to Germanic kingdoms while the Eastern Roman (Byzantine) Empire survives a thousand years from Constantinople — Justinian, Orthodox Christianity, and its role shielding and trading with two worlds.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Byzantium — the Eastern Empire That Lasted a Thousand Years.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The West falls to Germanic kingdoms while the Eastern Roman (Byzantine) Empire survives a thousand years from Constantinople — Justinian, Orthodox Christianity, and its role shielding and trading with two worlds.
