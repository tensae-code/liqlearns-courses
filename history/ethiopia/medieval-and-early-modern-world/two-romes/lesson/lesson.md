# Two Romes — the Fallen West and the Byzantine East

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Two Romes — the Fallen West and the Byzantine East. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Fall of the Western Roman Empire; the Byzantine Empire

The West falls to Germanic kingdoms while the Eastern Roman (Byzantine) Empire survives a thousand years from Constantinople — Justinian, Orthodox Christianity, and its role shielding and trading with two worlds.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example from the story. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
