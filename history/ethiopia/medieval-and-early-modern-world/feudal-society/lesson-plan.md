# Lesson plan — Feudal Society — Lords, Knights, Church and Manor

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** The Medieval and Early Modern World, 500–1789 · **Lesson:** Feudal Society — Lords, Knights, Church and Manor
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — The Medieval and Early Modern World, 500–1789 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Out of the Dark Age came feudal society: the Catholic Church and feudal lords controlled land and people and therefore power; the economy was agricultural with low but rising productivity; society was ordered in estates, with knights as its mounted soldiers — the manor village is the unit's working picture (the Glossary defines "manor").
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — The Medieval and Early Modern World, 500–1789 (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
