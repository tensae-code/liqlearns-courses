## Feudal society

Out of the Dark Age came feudal society: the Catholic Church and feudal lords controlled land and people and therefore power; the economy was agricultural with low but rising productivity; society was ordered in estates, with knights as its mounted soldiers — the manor village is the unit's working picture (the Glossary defines "manor").

## Main features of the Middle Ages — feudalism and the Church

Feudal society (kings, lords, vassals, serfs), manorial economy, the Catholic Church's power, monasticism, the Crusades, and the late-medieval crises and towns' revival.
