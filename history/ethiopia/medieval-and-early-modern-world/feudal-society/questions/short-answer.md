# Feudal Society — Lords, Knights, Church and Manor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Out of the Dark Age came feudal society: the Catholic Church and feudal lords controlled land and people and therefore power; the economy was agricultural with low but rising productivity; society was ordered in estates, with knights as its mounted soldiers — the manor village is the unit's working picture (the Glossary defines "manor").

**2.** What does the part "Main features of the Middle Ages — feudalism and the Church" teach?
**Answer:** Feudal society (kings, lords, vassals, serfs), manorial economy, the Catholic Church's power, monasticism, the Crusades, and the late-medieval crises and towns' revival.
