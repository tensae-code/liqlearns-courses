# The Renaissance — Rebirth of Learning and Humanism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Renaissance (14th–17th centuries) was the "rebirth" of learning: it began in urban Italy — an age of recovery from 14th-century disasters, with a new emphasis on individual ability — and spread to England, France, Germany, Spain and Holland.

**2.** What does the part "The Renaissance (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** Italy's rebirth of classical learning: humanism, art (Leonardo, Michelangelo), the printing press, and the movement's spread north.
