# The Ottoman Empire — From Sultanate to Superpower

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Ottoman Empire — From Sultanate to Superpower. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The expansion of the Ottoman Empire

The Ottoman Sultanate continued the Islamic civilisation and became a superpower of its age: its expansion across the eastern Mediterranean and into Europe, capped by the conquest of Constantinople in 1453 (renamed Istanbul), made it Europe's great counterpart power for the rest of the period.

## The Ottoman Turkish Empire

Ottoman growth from Anatolia to the conquest of Constantinople (1453) and an empire spanning three continents, its sultan-caliphs, army and administration.
