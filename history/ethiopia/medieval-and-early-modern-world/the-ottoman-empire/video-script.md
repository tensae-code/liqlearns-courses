# Video script — The Ottoman Empire — From Sultanate to Superpower

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
The Ottoman Sultanate continued the Islamic civilisation and became a superpower of its age: its expansion across the eastern Mediterranean and into Europe, capped by the conquest of Constantinople in 1453 (renamed Istanbul), made it Europe's great counterpart power for the rest of the period.
