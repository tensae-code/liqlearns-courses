# The Ottoman Empire — From Sultanate to Superpower — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Ottoman Sultanate continued the Islamic civilisation and became a superpower of its age: its expansion across the eastern Mediterranean and into Europe, capped by the conquest of Constantinople in 1453 (renamed Istanbul), made it Europe's great counterpart power for the rest of the period.

**2.** What does the part "The Ottoman Turkish Empire" teach?
**Answer:** Ottoman growth from Anatolia to the conquest of Constantinople (1453) and an empire spanning three continents, its sultan-caliphs, army and administration.
