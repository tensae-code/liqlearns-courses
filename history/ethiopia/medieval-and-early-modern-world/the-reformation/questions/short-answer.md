# The Reformation — Luther and the Splitting of the Church — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Martin Luther's religious reforms broke Western Christianity: Protestantism emerged from the revolt against Roman Catholic practice, with national forms — in Britain the Protestants' church became the Anglican church, Calvin's followers taking forms such as the Presbyterian — and the settlement after Europe's religious wars (the Peace of Westphalia) is set as a discussion of settling disputes by peaceful means.

**2.** What does the part "The Reformation (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** Luther's 1517 protest over indulgences splits Western Christianity; Protestant churches, Catholic Counter-Reformation, and religious wars follow.
