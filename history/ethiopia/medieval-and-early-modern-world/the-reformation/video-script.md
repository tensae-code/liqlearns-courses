# Video script — The Reformation — Luther and the Splitting of the Church

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Martin Luther's religious reforms broke Western Christianity: Protestantism emerged from the revolt against Roman Catholic practice, with national forms — in Britain the Protestants' church became the Anglican church, Calvin's followers taking forms such as the Presbyterian — and the settlement after Europe's religious wars (the Peace of Westphalia) is set as a discussion of settling disputes by peaceful means.
