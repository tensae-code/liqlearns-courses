# Explorations and Discoveries — Why Europe Took to the Sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** In the 15th–16th centuries Europeans sailed to bypass the Ottoman blockade of the old routes to the Far East, encouraged by interest in long-distance trade and new inventions such as the compass, and sponsored by rulers seeking profit and access; the voyages' economic effect was huge profits from local trade networks, faster globalisation, and — with subsequent colonial conquest — faster development of capitalism.

**2.** What does the part "The Age of Exploration and the inception of globalization" teach?
**Answer:** Portuguese and Spanish voyages (Dias, da Gama, Columbus, Magellan), the Columbian exchange, and the first truly global trade web — told with its costs to the peoples reached.
