# Explorations and Discoveries — Why Europe Took to the Sea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the 15th–16th centuries Europeans sailed to bypass the Ottoman blockade of the old routes to the Far East, encouraged by interest in long-distance trade and new inventions such as the compass, and sponsored by rulers seeking profit and access; the voyages' economic effect was huge profits from local trade networks, faster globalisation, and — with subsequent colonial conquest — faster development of capitalism.
