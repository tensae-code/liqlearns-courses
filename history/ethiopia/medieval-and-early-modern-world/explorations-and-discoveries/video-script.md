# Video script — Explorations and Discoveries — Why Europe Took to the Sea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the 15th–16th centuries Europeans sailed to bypass the Ottoman blockade of the old routes to the Far East, encouraged by interest in long-distance trade and new inventions such as the compass, and sponsored by rulers seeking profit and access; the voyages' economic effect was huge profits from local trade networks, faster globalisation, and — with subsequent colonial conquest — faster development of capitalism.
