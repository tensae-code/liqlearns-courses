# Video script — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Islam arose in the Arabian Peninsula with the Prophet Mohammed's teaching at Mecca c.610 AD — "submission to the will of Allah"; opposed by the Quraysh, in 622 AD the Prophet and his followers made the Hijra to Medina, year one of the Islamic calendar.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

Islam arose in the Arabian Peninsula with the Prophet Mohammed's teaching at Mecca c.610 AD — "submission to the will of Allah"; opposed by the Quraysh, in 622 AD the Prophet and his followers made the Hijra to Medina, year one of the Islamic calendar.

Islam's birth in Arabia under Muhammad, the caliphates, and its rapid expansion across the Middle East, North Africa and Spain, with Islamic civilisation's scholarship and trade.

The guide's activity answer names the mechanisms the student book leaves general: Islam spread through military conquest, trade, pilgrimage and missionaries — the four-channel summary the build should carry into the rise-of-Islam lesson.

Remember it this way: Islam arose in the Arabian Peninsula with the Prophet Mohammed's teaching at Mecca c.610 AD — "submission to the will of Allah"; opposed by the Quraysh, in 622 AD the Prophet and his followers made the Hijra to Medina, year one of the Islamic calendar.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
