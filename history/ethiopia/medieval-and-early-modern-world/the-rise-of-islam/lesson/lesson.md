# The Rise and Expansion of Islam

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Rise and Expansion of Islam. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The rise and expansion of Islam (Book 1 telling)

Islam arose in the Arabian Peninsula with the Prophet Mohammed's teaching at Mecca c.610 AD — "submission to the will of Allah"; opposed by the Quraysh, in 622 AD the Prophet and his followers made the Hijra to Medina, year one of the Islamic calendar. After his death the Arabs expanded to build a great empire and civilisation under the Umayyad and then Abbasid caliphs. The book pictures the Kaaba at Mecca during the Hajj.

## The rise and expansion of Islam (Book 3 telling)

Islam's birth in Arabia under Muhammad, the caliphates, and its rapid expansion across the Middle East, North Africa and Spain, with Islamic civilisation's scholarship and trade.

## Content the guide adds — Islam's four channels of spread *(teacher-guide evidence)*

The guide's activity answer names the mechanisms the student book leaves general: Islam spread through military conquest, trade, pilgrimage and missionaries — the four-channel summary the build should carry into the rise-of-Islam lesson.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
