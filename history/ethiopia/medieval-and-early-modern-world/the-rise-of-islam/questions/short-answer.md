# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Islam arose in the Arabian Peninsula with the Prophet Mohammed's teaching at Mecca c.610 AD — "submission to the will of Allah"; opposed by the Quraysh, in 622 AD the Prophet and his followers made the Hijra to Medina, year one of the Islamic calendar.

**2.** What does the part "Content the guide adds — Islam's four channels of spread" teach?
**Answer:** The guide's activity answer names the mechanisms the student book leaves general: Islam spread through military conquest, trade, pilgrimage and missionaries — the four-channel summary the build should carry into the rise-of-Islam lesson.
