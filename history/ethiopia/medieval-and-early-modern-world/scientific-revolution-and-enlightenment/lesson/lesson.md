# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment

Copernicus to Newton rebuilds the picture of nature by observation and experiment; Enlightenment thinkers (Locke, Montesquieu, Rousseau, Voltaire) apply reason to government and society.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
