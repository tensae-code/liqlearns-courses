# Video script — Byzantium — the Eastern Empire That Lasted a Thousand Years

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The East Roman Empire continued after the West fell: an Orthodox imperial autocracy holding part of Europe and western Asia from Constantinople, until it fell to the Ottoman Turks in 1453.

Look at the map. Watch where the story happens, and who is in it.

The East Roman Empire continued after the West fell: an Orthodox imperial autocracy holding part of Europe and western Asia from Constantinople, until it fell to the Ottoman Turks in 1453.

Remember it this way: The East Roman Empire continued after the West fell: an Orthodox imperial autocracy holding part of Europe and western Asia from Constantinople, until it fell to the Ottoman Turks in 1453.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
