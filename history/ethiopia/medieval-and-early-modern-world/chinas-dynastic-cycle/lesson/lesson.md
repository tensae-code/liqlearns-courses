# China's Dynastic Cycle and the Mandate of Heaven

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: China's Dynastic Cycle and the Mandate of Heaven. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The dynastic cycle in China (Book 1 telling)

Imperial China is presented as an autocracy administered by highly educated Confucian scholar-officials, whose understanding of their own history ran on two ideas: the dynastic cycle (dynasties rise, decay and are replaced) and the Mandate of Heaven (rule lasts while heaven's mandate is kept).

## The dynastic cycle in China (Book 3 telling)

China's dynastic cycle — founding vigour, prosperity, decay, rebellion, replacement — through Tang, Song and Ming achievements in technology, trade and bureaucracy.
