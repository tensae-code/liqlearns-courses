# China's Dynastic Cycle and the Mandate of Heaven — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Imperial China is presented as an autocracy administered by highly educated Confucian scholar-officials, whose understanding of their own history ran on two ideas: the dynastic cycle (dynasties rise, decay and are replaced) and the Mandate of Heaven (rule lasts while heaven's mandate is kept).

**2.** What does the part "The dynastic cycle in China (Book 3 telling)" teach?
**Answer:** China's dynastic cycle — founding vigour, prosperity, decay, rebellion, replacement — through Tang, Song and Ming achievements in technology, trade and bureaucracy.
