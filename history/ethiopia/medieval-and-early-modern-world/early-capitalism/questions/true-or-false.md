# Early Capitalism — Merchants, Towns and Long-Distance Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In early modern Europe most people lived in extreme poverty while towns became centres of production and exchange: Crusades-era trade revival restored old Roman cities, merchants learned to buy raw materials, have workers manufacture, and sell finished goods at the best price; after the Discoveries, long-distance international trade brought big profits — against transport difficulties, insecurity from war and robbery, and heavy tolls.
