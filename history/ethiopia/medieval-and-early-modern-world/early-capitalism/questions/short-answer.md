# Early Capitalism — Merchants, Towns and Long-Distance Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** In early modern Europe most people lived in extreme poverty while towns became centres of production and exchange: Crusades-era trade revival restored old Roman cities, merchants learned to buy raw materials, have workers manufacture, and sell finished goods at the best price; after the Discoveries, long-distance international trade brought big profits — against transport difficulties, insecurity from war and robbery, and heavy tolls.

**2.** What does the part "Early capitalism — towns and long-distance trade" teach?
**Answer:** From 1500: revived towns, merchant capital, banking, joint trade routes and the price revolution begin capitalism in Europe.
