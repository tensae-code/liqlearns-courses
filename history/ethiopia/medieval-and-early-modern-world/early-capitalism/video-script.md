# Video script — Early Capitalism — Merchants, Towns and Long-Distance Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
In early modern Europe most people lived in extreme poverty while towns became centres of production and exchange: Crusades-era trade revival restored old Roman cities, merchants learned to buy raw materials, have workers manufacture, and sell finished goods at the best price; after the Discoveries, long-distance international trade brought big profits — against transport difficulties, insecurity from war and robbery, and heavy tolls.
