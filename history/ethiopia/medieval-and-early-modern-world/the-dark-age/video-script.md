# Video script — The Dark Age — Rome Falls, Europe Goes Rural

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Western Europe 500–1000 AD is presented as a decline of civilisation: the Western Roman Empire was destroyed by internal division and decay and by external invasions — Anglo-Saxons and Franks from the Germanic peoples, Huns and Magyars from Asia.
