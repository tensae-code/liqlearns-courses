# The Dark Age — Rome Falls, Europe Goes Rural

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Dark Age — Rome Falls, Europe Goes Rural. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The Dark Age

Western Europe 500–1000 AD is presented as a decline of civilisation: the Western Roman Empire was destroyed by internal division and decay and by external invasions — Anglo-Saxons and Franks from the Germanic peoples, Huns and Magyars from Asia. Societies became rural, cities shrank, Greco-Roman civilisation faded, feudalism emerged as the new order, bishops and monks kept the records, knights practised chivalry, and war and blood feud were common — until stability began returning c.1000.
