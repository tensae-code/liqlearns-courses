# The Trans-Saharan Trade — Camels, Salt and Gold

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: The Trans-Saharan Trade — Camels, Salt and Gold. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Trans-Saharan trade

Peoples north and south of the Sahara had irregular contact long before the regular caravan trade, which became organised after the introduction of the camel and ran regularly from the 4th century as an expansion of older intra- and inter-regional trade; it built robust economic and political systems on both sides of the desert and, with Islam's spread, strengthened North–tropical Africa relations.

## Trade as the connecting engine

Red Sea, trans-Saharan, Indian Ocean, caravan and Atlantic trades are the mechanism linking nearly every unit; trade-route mapping is the book's most repeated skill. — Units 2–8.
