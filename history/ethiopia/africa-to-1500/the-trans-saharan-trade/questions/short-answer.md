# The Trans-Saharan Trade — Camels, Salt and Gold — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Peoples north and south of the Sahara had irregular contact long before the regular caravan trade, which became organised after the introduction of the camel and ran regularly from the 4th century as an expansion of older intra- and inter-regional trade; it built robust economic and political systems on both sides of the desert and, with Islam's spread, strengthened North–tropical Africa relations.

**2.** What does the part "Trade as the connecting engine" teach?
**Answer:** Red Sea, trans-Saharan, Indian Ocean, caravan and Atlantic trades are the mechanism linking nearly every unit; trade-route mapping is the book's most repeated skill.
