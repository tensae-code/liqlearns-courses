# Islam in West Africa — Faith Travelling with Trade

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Islam in West Africa — Faith Travelling with Trade. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## The spread of Islam and its impact in West Africa

Islam promoted and widened the Trans-Saharan caravan trade between West Africa and the Mediterranean, enriched West African and Muslim traders alike, drew North African Muslims to settle in West African commercial centres, and linked diverse peoples through shared culture and cooperation — the religious precondition for the West African empires that follow.
