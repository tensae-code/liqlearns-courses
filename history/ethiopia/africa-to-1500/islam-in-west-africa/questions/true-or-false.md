# Islam in West Africa — Faith Travelling with Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Islam promoted and widened the Trans-Saharan caravan trade between West Africa and the Mediterranean, enriched West African and Muslim traders alike, drew North African Muslims to settle in West African commercial centres, and linked diverse peoples through shared culture and cooperation — the religious precondition for the West African empires that follow.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Ghana — the First Trans-Saharan Trading Empire.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Islam promoted and widened the Trans-Saharan caravan trade between West Africa and the Mediterranean, enriched West African and Muslim traders alike, drew North African Muslims to settle in West African commercial centres, and linked diverse peoples through shared culture and cooperation — the religious precondition for the West African empires that follow.
