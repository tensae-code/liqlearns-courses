# Islam in West Africa — Faith Travelling with Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Islam promoted and widened the Trans-Saharan caravan trade between West Africa and the Mediterranean, enriched West African and Muslim traders alike, drew North African Muslims to settle in West African commercial centres, and linked diverse peoples through shared culture and cooperation — the religious precondition for the West African empires that follow.
