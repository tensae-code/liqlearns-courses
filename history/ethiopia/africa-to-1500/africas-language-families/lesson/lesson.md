## Languages and peoples of Africa

Over 1,000 interrelated languages are spoken across the continent, classified by shared vocabulary into four super-families: Congo-Kordofanian (Niger-Congo), Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan and Khoisan — with the caveat that not all languages now spoken in Africa are native to it (Arabic, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese are named).

## African languages — classification and distribution

Africa's languages are classified (Niger-Congo, Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan, Khoisan) and mapped as the frame for the continent's states.
