# Africa's Language Families — depth

This lesson's parts are distinct and sequential, so they stand as its sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Over 1,000 interrelated languages are spoken across the continent, classified by shared vocabulary into four super-families: Congo-Kordofanian (Niger-Congo), Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan and Khoisan — with the caveat that not all languages now spoken in Africa are native to it (Arabic, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese are named). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
