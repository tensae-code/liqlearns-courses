# Africa's Language Families — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Over 1,000 interrelated languages are spoken across the continent, classified by shared vocabulary into four super-families: Congo-Kordofanian (Niger-Congo), Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan and Khoisan — with the caveat that not all languages now spoken in Africa are native to it (Arabic, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese are named).

**2.** What does the part "African languages — classification and distribution" teach?
**Answer:** Africa's languages are classified (Niger-Congo, Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan, Khoisan) and mapped as the frame for the continent's states.
