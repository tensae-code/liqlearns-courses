# Africa's Language Families — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Over 1,000 interrelated languages are spoken across the continent, classified by shared vocabulary into four super-families: Congo-Kordofanian (Niger-Congo), Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan and Khoisan — with the caveat that not all languages now spoken in Africa are native to it (Arabic, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese are named).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: North Africa — Fatimids, Mamluk Egypt and the Funj.
