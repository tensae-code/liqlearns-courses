# Songhai — the Last of the Three Empires

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Songhai — the Last of the Three Empires. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Songhai

The third of the medieval empires became powerful by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade, trading with the Berbers, its kings accepting Islam early in the 11th century, and prospering further as gold came up from the forest region — until a Moroccan army defeated it at the Battle of Tondibi in 1591 and the empire ended.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
