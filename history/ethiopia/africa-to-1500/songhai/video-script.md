# Video script — Songhai — the Last of the Three Empires

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The third of the medieval empires became powerful by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade, trading with the Berbers, its kings accepting Islam early in the 11th century, and prospering further as gold came up from the forest region — until a Moroccan army defeated it at the Battle of Tondibi in 1591 and the empire ended.
