# Songhai — the Last of the Three Empires — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The third of the medieval empires became powerful by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade, trading with the Berbers, its kings accepting Islam early in the 11th century, and prospering further as gold came up from the forest region — until a Moroccan army defeated it at the Battle of Tondibi in 1591 and the empire ended.
