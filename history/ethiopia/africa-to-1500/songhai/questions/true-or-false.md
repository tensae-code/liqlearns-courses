# Songhai — the Last of the Three Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The third of the medieval empires became powerful by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade, trading with the Berbers, its kings accepting Islam early in the 11th century, and prospering further as gold came up from the forest region — until a Moroccan army defeated it at the Battle of Tondibi in 1591 and the empire ended.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Ghana, Mali and Songhai — Empires of Gold.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The third of the medieval empires became powerful by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade, trading with the Berbers, its kings accepting Islam early in the 11th century, and prospering further as gold came up from the forest region — until a Moroccan army defeated it at the Battle of Tondibi in 1591 and the empire ended.
