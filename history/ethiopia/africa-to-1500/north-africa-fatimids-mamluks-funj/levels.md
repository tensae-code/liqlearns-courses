# North Africa — Fatimids, Mamluk Egypt and the Funj — depth

Pass mark: After the Arab conquest (Egypt 642 AD, then the whole coast to Morocco and into Spain by century's end) and an 11th-century revival of Islam with further Arab immigration, North Africa's great states of the period were Fatimid then Mamluk Egypt — the Turkish-origin Mamluks ruling 1250–1517, with Ethio-Egyptian relations full of conflict — and the loosely united Funj sultanate, which fought Christian Ethiopia over trade routes and lasted until Egypt conquered it in the 1820s. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
