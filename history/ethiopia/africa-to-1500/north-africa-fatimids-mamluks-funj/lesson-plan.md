# Lesson plan — North Africa — Fatimids, Mamluk Egypt and the Funj

**Course:** History — Ethiopia · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** North Africa — Fatimids, Mamluk Egypt and the Funj
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Africa to 1500 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After the Arab conquest (Egypt 642 AD, then the whole coast to Morocco and into Spain by century's end) and an 11th-century revival of Islam with further Arab immigration, North Africa's great states of the period were Fatimid then Mamluk Egypt — the Turkish-origin Mamluks ruling 1250–1517, with Ethio-Egyptian relations full of conflict — and the loosely united Funj sultanate, which fought Christian Ethiopia over trade routes and lasted until Egypt conquered it in the 1820s.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — Africa to 1500 (recommended, never forced).
