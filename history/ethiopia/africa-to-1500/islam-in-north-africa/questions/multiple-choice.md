# Islam in North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Islam crosses North Africa by conquest and then by trade and scholars, reshaping the Maghreb and linking it to West African gold routes.  b) Islam in West Africa — Faith Travelling with Trade  c) The Oromo Expansion — a Map Redrawn, Peoples Intermingled  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Islam crosses North Africa by conquest and then by trade and scholars, reshaping the Maghreb and linking it to West African gold routes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Spread of Islam to North Africa  b) a topic from another course  c) today's weather  d) none of these
**Answer: a) Spread of Islam to North Africa** — That is this lesson's first part.
