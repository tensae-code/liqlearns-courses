# Islam in North Africa — true or false

**2.** This lesson teaches: Islam in West Africa — Faith Travelling with Trade.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Islam crosses North Africa by conquest and then by trade and scholars, reshaping the Maghreb and linking it to West African gold routes.
