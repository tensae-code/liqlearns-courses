## Kanem-Bornu and the Hausa states

Kanem-Bornu, a grassland empire under a Muslim dynasty, grew on trade: its army kept the routes safe and taxed traders, and it declined as trade shifted from inland routes to the Atlantic coast. The Hausa city-states of Nigeria (emerged 1000–1200 AD — Kano, Katsina, Zazzau/Zaria, Gobir, Kebbi, Rano, Zamfara) were built by peoples who were both pastoralists and farmers.
