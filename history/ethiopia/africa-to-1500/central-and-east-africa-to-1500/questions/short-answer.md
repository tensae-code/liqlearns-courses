# Central and East Africa — Bantu Movements, Kongo and the Swahili Coast — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** From the 1st millennium BC Bantu-speaking farming peoples moved slowly — as small communities, not invading hordes — from the Niger region into the Congo basin and East Africa.

**2.** What does the part "Central and Eastern Africa" teach?
**Answer:** The Swahili city-states of the east coast, the Luba/Lunda worlds of the centre, and inland states trading ivory and gold to the Indian Ocean.
