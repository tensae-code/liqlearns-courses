## Equatorial, Central and Eastern Africa

From the 1st millennium BC Bantu-speaking farming peoples moved slowly — as small communities, not invading hordes — from the Niger region into the Congo basin and East Africa. The Kingdom of Kongo was founded by Bantu people on the lower Zaire (Congo) River in the late 14th century, and the Swahili coast is set as the discussion case for cultural diffusion — an African trading culture blended by contact across the Indian Ocean.

## Central and Eastern Africa

The Swahili city-states of the east coast, the Luba/Lunda worlds of the centre, and inland states trading ivory and gold to the Indian Ocean.
