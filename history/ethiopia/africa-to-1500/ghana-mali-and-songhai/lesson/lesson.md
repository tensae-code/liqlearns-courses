# Ghana, Mali and Songhai — Empires of Gold

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Ghana, Mali and Songhai — Empires of Gold. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## States in West Africa — Ghana, Mali, Songhai

In sequence: Ghana's gold trade, Mali under Mansa Musa with Timbuktu's scholarship, and Songhai under Sonni Ali and Askia Muhammad — the Sudanic empires and trans-Saharan trade.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches, in your own words: In sequence: Ghana's gold trade, Mali under Mansa Musa with Timbuktu's scholarship, and Songhai under Sonni Ali and Askia Muhammad — the Sudanic empires and trans-Saharan trade. Give one real example from the story. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
