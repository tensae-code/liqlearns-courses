# Africa Connected — Trade and Faith Between Regions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Before 1500 Africans were connected within the continent and beyond it: trade was the most important link, and the introduction of Christianity and Islam shaped Africa's relations with the outside world — the framing for the trade lesson and the contacts lesson that close the unit.

**2.** What does the part "Relationships and exchanges among Africa's regions" teach?
**Answer:** The unit closes by connecting the regions: trans-Saharan, Nile valley, Red Sea and Indian Ocean exchanges moving gold, salt, ivory, faiths and ideas across the continent.
