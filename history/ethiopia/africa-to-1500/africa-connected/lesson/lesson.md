# Africa Connected — Trade and Faith Between Regions

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Africa Connected — Trade and Faith Between Regions. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Africa's intra- and inter-continental relations

Before 1500 Africans were connected within the continent and beyond it: trade was the most important link, and the introduction of Christianity and Islam shaped Africa's relations with the outside world — the framing for the trade lesson and the contacts lesson that close the unit.

## Relationships and exchanges among Africa's regions

The unit closes by connecting the regions: trans-Saharan, Nile valley, Red Sea and Indian Ocean exchanges moving gold, salt, ivory, faiths and ideas across the continent.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
