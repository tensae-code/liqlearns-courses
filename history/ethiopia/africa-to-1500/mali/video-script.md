# Video script — Mali — Mansa Musa and Timbuktu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Ghana's successor empire: Islam spread widely along the Niger with its language, laws and customs; its greatest king, Mansa Musa (1312–1337), made his famous pilgrimage to Mecca, and Timbuktu became one of the most important centres of Islamic learning and culture.
