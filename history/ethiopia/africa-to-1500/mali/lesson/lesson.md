# Mali — Mansa Musa and Timbuktu

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Mali — Mansa Musa and Timbuktu. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Mali

Ghana's successor empire: Islam spread widely along the Niger with its language, laws and customs; its greatest king, Mansa Musa (1312–1337), made his famous pilgrimage to Mecca, and Timbuktu became one of the most important centres of Islamic learning and culture. The book pictures a cartoon of Mansa Musa.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
