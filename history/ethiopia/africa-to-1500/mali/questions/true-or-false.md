# Mali — Mansa Musa and Timbuktu — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ghana's successor empire: Islam spread widely along the Niger with its language, laws and customs; its greatest king, Mansa Musa (1312–1337), made his famous pilgrimage to Mecca, and Timbuktu became one of the most important centres of Islamic learning and culture.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Songhai — the Last of the Three Empires.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: Ghana's successor empire: Islam spread widely along the Niger with its language, laws and customs; its greatest king, Mansa Musa (1312–1337), made his famous pilgrimage to Mecca, and Timbuktu became one of the most important centres of Islamic learning and culture.
