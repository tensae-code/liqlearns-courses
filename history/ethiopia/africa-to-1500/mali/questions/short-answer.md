# Mali — Mansa Musa and Timbuktu — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** Ghana's successor empire: Islam spread widely along the Niger with its language, laws and customs; its greatest king, Mansa Musa (1312–1337), made his famous pilgrimage to Mecca, and Timbuktu became one of the most important centres of Islamic learning and culture.
