## Southern Africa — Great Zimbabwe, Mwene Mutapa, the Khoi-Khoi

Southern states formed more slowly from independent villages consolidating from the 11th century. The Shona built Great Zimbabwe (from c.1000 AD; great stone walls and tower in the 13th–14th centuries), heart of a trading empire based on cattle, crops and gold traded to the Indian Ocean coast, 11th–15th centuries. After 1450 the Rozwi clan kings founded the Mwene Mutapa empire, later conquered by the Portuguese in the late 16th century. The Khoi-Khoi shifted c.1000 AD from hunting to sheep and cattle herding and expanded from c.1300.
