# Video script — Ghana — the First Trans-Saharan Trading Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Soninke-founded empire (4th century AD, at its height c.1000 AD, in modern Mauritania, Mali and Senegal) grew great by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade and taxing it; the book names the trade — with Islam's rise a contributing factor — as the main source of its greatness.
