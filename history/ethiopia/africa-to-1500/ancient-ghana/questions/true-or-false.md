# Ghana — the First Trans-Saharan Trading Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Soninke-founded empire (4th century AD, at its height c.1000 AD, in modern Mauritania, Mali and Senegal) grew great by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade and taxing it; the book names the trade — with Islam's rise a contributing factor — as the main source of its greatness.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches: Mali — Mansa Musa and Timbuktu.
**Answer: False** — this lesson teaches: The Soninke-founded empire (4th century AD, at its height c.1000 AD, in modern Mauritania, Mali and Senegal) grew great by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade and taxing it; the book names the trade — with Islam's rise a contributing factor — as the main source of its greatness.
