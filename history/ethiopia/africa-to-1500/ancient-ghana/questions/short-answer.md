# Ghana — the First Trans-Saharan Trading Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is this lesson's one thing?
**Answer:** The Soninke-founded empire (4th century AD, at its height c.1000 AD, in modern Mauritania, Mali and Senegal) grew great by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade and taxing it; the book names the trade — with Islam's rise a contributing factor — as the main source of its greatness.
