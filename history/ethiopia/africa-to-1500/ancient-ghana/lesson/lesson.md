# Ghana — the First Trans-Saharan Trading Empire

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: Ghana — the First Trans-Saharan Trading Empire. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Ancient Ghana

The Soninke-founded empire (4th century AD, at its height c.1000 AD, in modern Mauritania, Mali and Senegal) grew great by controlling the Trans-Saharan trade and taxing it; the book names the trade — with Islam's rise a contributing factor — as the main source of its greatness.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
