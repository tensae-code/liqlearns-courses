# First European Contacts — the Hinge to 1500

## Do it first

Before you read, say out loud what you already know about this: First European Contacts — the Hinge to 1500. Who was involved? Where did it happen? What changed? Keep those three questions — who, where, what turned — in mind as you read: this lesson is told as a story, in that shape.

## Early contacts with the outside world

Africa's external relations to 1500 were led by trade and the two incoming religions; by the unit's end the new actors are Europeans — Portugal and Spain determined to dominate — and most African kingdoms and empires end in the 16th century as the Atlantic slave trade begins: the hinge into Unit 6.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: Read the story once for the turn — who wanted what, what changed, what came of it. Then read it again for the names and dates; they stick once the turn is clear.
