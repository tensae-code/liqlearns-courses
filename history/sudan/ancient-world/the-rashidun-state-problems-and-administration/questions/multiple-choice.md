# The Rashidun State Problems and Administration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Problems That Faced the State under the Rashidun”?
a) Empire is not only conquered; it has to be filed, paid for and governed  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Empire is not only conquered; it has to be filed, paid for and governed** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Problems That Faced the State under the Rashidun”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Empire is not only conquered; it has to be filed, paid for and governed  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Empire is not only conquered; it has to be filed, paid for and governed** — That is what this lesson teaches.
