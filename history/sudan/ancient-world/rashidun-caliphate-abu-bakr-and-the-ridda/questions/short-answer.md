# Rashidun Caliphate Abu Bakr and the Ridda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Abu Bakr's caliphate”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the merchant of Quraysh, first of the caliphs, is chosen to lead and holds the young state together through its first crisis

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Islamic State under the Four Caliphs (Abu Bakr part)”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the Prophet is dead, Arabia is in revolt, and one elderly merchant refuses to let the state die with him
