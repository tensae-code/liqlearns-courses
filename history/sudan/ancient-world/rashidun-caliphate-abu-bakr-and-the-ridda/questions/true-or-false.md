# Rashidun Caliphate Abu Bakr and the Ridda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The merchant of Quraysh, first of the caliphs, is chosen to lead and holds the young state together through its first crisis. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Islamic State under the Four Caliphs (Abu Bakr part)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Prophet is dead, Arabia is in revolt, and one elderly merchant refuses to let the state die with him.
