# Rashidun Caliphate Abu Bakr and the Ridda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Abu Bakr's caliphate”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The merchant of Quraysh, first of the caliphs, is chosen to lead and holds the young state together through…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The merchant of Quraysh, first of the caliphs, is chosen to lead and holds the young state together through…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Islamic State under the Four Caliphs (Abu Bakr part)”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The Prophet is dead, Arabia is in revolt, and one elderly merchant refuses to let the state die with him  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The Prophet is dead, Arabia is in revolt, and one elderly merchant refuses to let the state die with him** — That is what this lesson teaches.
