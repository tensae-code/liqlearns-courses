# North Arabian Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Kingdoms of the North”?
a) Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Kingdoms of the North”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all** — That is what this lesson teaches.
