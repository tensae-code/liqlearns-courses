# North Arabian Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Kingdoms of the North” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all.
