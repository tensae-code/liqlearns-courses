# North Arabian Kingdoms

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Kingdoms of the North. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Kingdoms of the North

Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: North Arabian Kingdoms. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Arab kingdoms grow fat in the space between two empires, and Petra's rock palaces outlive them all.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson's story echoes in real life. Note it.
