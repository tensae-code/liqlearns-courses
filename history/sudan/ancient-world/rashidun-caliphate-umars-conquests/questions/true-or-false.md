# Rashidun Caliphate Umars Conquests — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Umar, famed for courage, firmness and justice, leads the state as it grows beyond Arabia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Umar part” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Under the sternest of the caliphs the armies take Syria, Iraq, Persia and Egypt.
