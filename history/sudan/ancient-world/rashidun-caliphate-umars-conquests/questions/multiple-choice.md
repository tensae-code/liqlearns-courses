# Rashidun Caliphate Umars Conquests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Umar's caliphate”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Umar, famed for courage, firmness and justice, leads the state as it grows beyond Arabia  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Umar, famed for courage, firmness and justice, leads the state as it grows beyond Arabia** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Umar part”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Under the sternest of the caliphs the armies take Syria, Iraq, Persia and Egypt  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Under the sternest of the caliphs the armies take Syria, Iraq, Persia and Egypt** — That is what this lesson teaches.
