# Rashidun Caliphate Umars Conquests

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Umar's caliphate; Umar part. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Umar's caliphate

Umar, famed for courage, firmness and justice, leads the state as it grows beyond Arabia.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Umar part

Under the sternest of the caliphs the armies take Syria, Iraq, Persia and Egypt.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
