# Umayyad Conquests West Al Andalus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A coastline won fortress by fortress ends in a crossing that puts Islam in Europe for eight centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Islamic Conquests under the Umayyads — the West” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A coastline won fortress by fortress ends in a crossing that puts Islam in Europe for eight centuries.
