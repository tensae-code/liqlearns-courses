# Roman Law Roads Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Roman Civilisation (a)”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Rome takes Greek culture and builds it to imperial scale  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Rome takes Greek culture and builds it to imperial scale** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Roman Civilisation (b)”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Law, roads and citizenship hold together what the legions won  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Law, roads and citizenship hold together what the legions won** — That is what this lesson teaches.
