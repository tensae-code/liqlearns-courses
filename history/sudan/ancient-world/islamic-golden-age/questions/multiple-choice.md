# Islamic Golden Age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Effect of Islamic Civilisation on Europe”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Knowledge travels uphill — from the Muslim world into a Europe that had forgotten its own learning  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Knowledge travels uphill — from the Muslim world into a Europe that had forgotten its own learning** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Islamic State under the Abbasids”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Revolution makes a new capital, and the new capital makes an age of learning  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Revolution makes a new capital, and the new capital makes an age of learning** — That is what this lesson teaches.
