# Islamic Golden Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Knowledge travels uphill — from the Muslim world into a Europe that had forgotten its own learning. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Islamic State under the Abbasids” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Revolution makes a new capital, and the new capital makes an age of learning.
