# Umayyad Conquests East — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Islamic Conquests under the Umayyads — the East”?
a) In one reign Islam's frontier runs from the Atlantic to the road to China  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In one reign Islam's frontier runs from the Atlantic to the road to China** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Islamic Conquests under the Umayyads — the East”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In one reign Islam's frontier runs from the Atlantic to the road to China  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In one reign Islam's frontier runs from the Atlantic to the road to China** — That is what this lesson teaches.
