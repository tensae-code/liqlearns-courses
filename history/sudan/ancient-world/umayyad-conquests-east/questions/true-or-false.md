# Umayyad Conquests East — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In one reign Islam's frontier runs from the Atlantic to the road to China.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Islamic Conquests under the Umayyads — the East” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In one reign Islam's frontier runs from the Atlantic to the road to China.
