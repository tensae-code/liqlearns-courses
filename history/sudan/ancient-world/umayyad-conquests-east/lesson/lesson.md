# Umayyad Conquests East

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Islamic Conquests under the Umayyads — the East. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Islamic Conquests under the Umayyads — the East

In one reign Islam's frontier runs from the Atlantic to the road to China.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In one reign Islam's frontier runs from the Atlantic to the road to China. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Umayyad Conquests East. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
