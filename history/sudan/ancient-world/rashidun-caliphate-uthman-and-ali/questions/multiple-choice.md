# Rashidun Caliphate Uthman and Ali — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Uthman's caliphate”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Uthman gathers the Quran into one book so its reading is one, and builds a fleet to guard the state's coasts  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Uthman gathers the Quran into one book so its reading is one, and builds a fleet to guard the state's coasts** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Ali's caliphate”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Ali, the Prophet's cousin raised in his house and the first boy to believe, closes the Rashidun age as the…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Ali, the Prophet's cousin raised in his house and the first boy to believe, closes the Rashidun age as the…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
