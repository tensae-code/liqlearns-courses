# Rashidun Caliphate Uthman and Ali — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Uthman's caliphate”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Uthman gathers the Quran into one book so its reading is one, and builds a fleet to guard the state's coasts

**2.** Who is in the story of “Ali's caliphate”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ali, the Prophet's cousin raised in his house and the first boy to believe, closes the Rashidun age as the state's map reaches its widest
