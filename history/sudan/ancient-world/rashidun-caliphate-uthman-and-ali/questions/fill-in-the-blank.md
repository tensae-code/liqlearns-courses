# Rashidun Caliphate Uthman and Ali — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ gathers the Quran into one book so its reading is one, and builds a fleet to guard the state's coasts (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Uthman

**2.** ____, the Prophet's cousin raised in his house and the first boy to believe, closes the Rashidun age as the state's map reaches its widest (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Ali
