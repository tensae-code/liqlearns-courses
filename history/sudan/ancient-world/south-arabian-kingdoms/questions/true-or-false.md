# South Arabian Kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Where the rains fall, Arabia builds kingdoms with councils, dams and caravan tolls.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Kingdoms of the South” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Where the rains fall, Arabia builds kingdoms with councils, dams and caravan tolls.
