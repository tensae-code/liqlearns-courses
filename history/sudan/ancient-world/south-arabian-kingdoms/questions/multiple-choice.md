# South Arabian Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Kingdoms of the South”?
a) Where the rains fall, Arabia builds kingdoms with councils, dams and caravan tolls  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Where the rains fall, Arabia builds kingdoms with councils, dams and caravan tolls** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Kingdoms of the South”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Where the rains fall, Arabia builds kingdoms with councils, dams and caravan tolls  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Where the rains fall, Arabia builds kingdoms with councils, dams and caravan tolls** — That is what this lesson teaches.
