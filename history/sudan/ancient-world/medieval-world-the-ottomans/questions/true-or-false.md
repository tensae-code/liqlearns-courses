# Medieval World the Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The state Sudan's own conquerors served begins as a handful of horsemen on Byzantium's edge. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Expansion into Iran and the Arab Lands” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In two years the Ottomans inherit the whole Arab east, Sudan's northern horizon included.
