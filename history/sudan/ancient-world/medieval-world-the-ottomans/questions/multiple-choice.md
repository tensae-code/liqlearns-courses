# Medieval World the Ottomans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Rise of the Ottoman State, and The Ottoman State under Muhammad II the Conqueror”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The state Sudan's own conquerors served begins as a handful of horsemen on Byzantium's edge  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The state Sudan's own conquerors served begins as a handful of horsemen on Byzantium's edge** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Expansion into Iran and the Arab Lands”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In two years the Ottomans inherit the whole Arab east, Sudan's northern horizon included  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In two years the Ottomans inherit the whole Arab east, Sudan's northern horizon included** — That is what this lesson teaches.
