## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Rise of the Ottoman State, and The Ottoman State under Muhammad II the Conqueror** — The state Sudan's own conquerors served begins as a handful of horsemen on Byzantium's edge
2. **Expansion into Iran and the Arab Lands** — In two years the Ottomans inherit the whole Arab east, Sudan's northern horizon included
3. **Systems of Government in the Ottoman State** — The machine that governed three continents, explained part by part
4. **The Decline and Collapse of the Ottoman Caliphate State** — The sick man of Europe sickens for two centuries, then dies in the Great War

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Sudan, Africa and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
