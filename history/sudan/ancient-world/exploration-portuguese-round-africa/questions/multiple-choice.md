# Exploration Portuguese Round Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Geographical Discoveries”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The sea routes found in these decades are the same ones that will bring colonialism to Sudan's own coasts  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The sea routes found in these decades are the same ones that will bring colonialism to Sudan's own coasts** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The European Geographical Discoveries Movement”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In one generation Europe's ships join every ocean, and no coast — Sudan's included — is outside the story any…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In one generation Europe's ships join every ocean, and no coast — Sudan's included — is outside the story any…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
