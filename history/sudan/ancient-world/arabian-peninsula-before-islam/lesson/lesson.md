# Arabian Peninsula Before Islam

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The State of Arab Society before Islam; Arabia before Islam. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The State of Arab Society before Islam

A society of poets, markets and clan loyalties waits for the message that will unite it.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Arabia before Islam

A land with no king and no capital, where a man's tribe is his state, his army and his law.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
