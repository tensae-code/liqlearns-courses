# Arabian Peninsula Before Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A society of poets, markets and clan loyalties waits for the message that will unite it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Arabia before Islam” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A land with no king and no capital, where a man's tribe is his state, his army and his law.
