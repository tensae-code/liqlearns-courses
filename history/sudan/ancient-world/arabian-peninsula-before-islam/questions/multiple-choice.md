# Arabian Peninsula Before Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The State of Arab Society before Islam”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A society of poets, markets and clan loyalties waits for the message that will unite it  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A society of poets, markets and clan loyalties waits for the message that will unite it** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Arabia before Islam”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A land with no king and no capital, where a man's tribe is his state, his army and his law  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A land with no king and no capital, where a man's tribe is his state, his army and his law** — That is what this lesson teaches.
