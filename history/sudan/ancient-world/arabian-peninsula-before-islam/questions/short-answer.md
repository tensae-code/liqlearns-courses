# Arabian Peninsula Before Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The State of Arab Society before Islam”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a society of poets, markets and clan loyalties waits for the message that will unite it

**2.** Who is in the story of “Arabia before Islam”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a land with no king and no capital, where a man's tribe is his state, his army and his law
