# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Rightly-Guided Caliphs”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** when the Prophet dies his closest companions — the first to believe and the defenders of the call — take up the rule one after another

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Call and the Islamic State in the Time of the Prophet”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a preacher becomes a state-builder, and a city of refuge becomes a capital
