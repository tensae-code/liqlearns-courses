# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** When the Prophet dies his closest companions — the first to believe and the defenders of the call — take up the rule one after another. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Call and the Islamic State in the Time of the Prophet” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A preacher becomes a state-builder, and a city of refuge becomes a capital.
