# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Rightly-Guided Caliphs”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) When the Prophet dies his closest companions — the first to believe and the defenders of the call — take up…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) When the Prophet dies his closest companions — the first to believe and the defenders of the call — take up…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Call and the Islamic State in the Time of the Prophet”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A preacher becomes a state-builder, and a city of refuge becomes a capital  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A preacher becomes a state-builder, and a city of refuge becomes a capital** — That is what this lesson teaches.
