# Lesson plan — The Rise and Expansion of Islam

**Course:** History — Sudan · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Rise and Expansion of Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Arabia, the Islamic Caliphates and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** When the Prophet dies his closest companions — the first to believe and the defenders of the call — take up the rule one after another.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Arabia, the Islamic Caliphates and the Ancient World — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Rightly-Guided Caliphs** — When the Prophet dies his closest companions — the first to believe and the defenders of the call — take up the rule one after another
2. **The Call and the Islamic State in the Time of the Prophet** — A preacher becomes a state-builder, and a city of refuge becomes a capital
3. **The Islamic State in the Time of the Rightly Guided Caliphs** — Four caliphs in thirty years turn a city-state into an empire
4. **The Spread of Islam in North Africa** — Within a generation the whole southern shore of the Mediterranean is Muslim
5. **The Islamic State in the Time of the Prophet** — In ten years a persecuted preacher becomes the head of a state
6. **and 9, The Islamic State under the Rightly Guided Caliphs** — The state outlives its founder and doubles in size with every caliph
7. **North Africa part** — The northern coast is won early, and becomes the bridge to Spain and to the desert routes

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: When the Prophet dies his closest companions — the first to believe and the defenders of the call — take up the rule one after another.
2. Give one real example of it from the story as taught — a person, a place and the turn in their fate.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, and a simple map of Sudan, Africa and the world you can draw yourself. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the books themselves work.
