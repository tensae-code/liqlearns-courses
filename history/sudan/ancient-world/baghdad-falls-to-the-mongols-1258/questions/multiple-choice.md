# Baghdad Falls to the Mongols 1258 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Weakness and Fall of the Abbasid State”?
a) An empire weakened from inside for centuries is finished in forty days by horsemen from the steppe  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) An empire weakened from inside for centuries is finished in forty days by horsemen from the steppe** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Weakness and Fall of the Abbasid State”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) An empire weakened from inside for centuries is finished in forty days by horsemen from the steppe  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) An empire weakened from inside for centuries is finished in forty days by horsemen from the steppe** — That is what this lesson teaches.
