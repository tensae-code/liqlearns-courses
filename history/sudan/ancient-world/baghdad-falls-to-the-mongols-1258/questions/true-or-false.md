# Baghdad Falls to the Mongols 1258 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An empire weakened from inside for centuries is finished in forty days by horsemen from the steppe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Weakness and Fall of the Abbasid State” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: An empire weakened from inside for centuries is finished in forty days by horsemen from the steppe.
