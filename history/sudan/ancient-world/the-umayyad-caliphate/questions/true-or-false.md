# The Umayyad Caliphate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** One dynasty rules an empire stretching from Spain to India. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Fall of the Umayyad State” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The empire grew faster than its rulers' justice, and the dynasty pays for it.
