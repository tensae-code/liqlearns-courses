# The Umayyad Caliphate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Umayyad State and The Conquests in the Umayyad Era”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) One dynasty rules an empire stretching from Spain to India  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) One dynasty rules an empire stretching from Spain to India** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Fall of the Umayyad State”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The empire grew faster than its rulers' justice, and the dynasty pays for it  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The empire grew faster than its rulers' justice, and the dynasty pays for it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
