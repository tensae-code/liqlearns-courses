# The Renaissance Rebirth of Learning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The European Renaissance”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Europe wakes, and the textbook closes the circle that began in its dark ages  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Europe wakes, and the textbook closes the circle that began in its dark ages** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The European Renaissance”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Learning is reborn and the modern world begins  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Learning is reborn and the modern world begins** — That is what this lesson teaches.
