# Europe in the Dark Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Europe in the Ages of Darkness”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) While Sudan's and Islam's cities flourish, the Sudanese textbook shows Europe in its dark centuries  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) While Sudan's and Islam's cities flourish, the Sudanese textbook shows Europe in its dark centuries** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Middle Ages in Europe”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The secondary course re-opens Europe where the primary course left it, in its middle centuries  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The secondary course re-opens Europe where the primary course left it, in its middle centuries** — That is what this lesson teaches.
