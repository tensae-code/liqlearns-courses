# Europe in the Dark Ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** While Sudan's and Islam's cities flourish, the Sudanese textbook shows Europe in its dark centuries. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Middle Ages in Europe” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The secondary course re-opens Europe where the primary course left it, in its middle centuries.
