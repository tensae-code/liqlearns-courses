# Greek Democracy Philosophy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Factors Behind the Greek and Roman Civilisations, and Greek Civilisation (a)”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Poor soil and a broken coastline push the Greeks to the sea, to colonies and to ideas  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Poor soil and a broken coastline push the Greeks to the sea, to colonies and to ideas** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Greek Civilisation (b)”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The city-state experiments with every way of ruling men, including rule by the citizens themselves  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The city-state experiments with every way of ruling men, including rule by the citizens themselves** — That is what this lesson teaches.
