# Greek Democracy Philosophy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Poor soil and a broken coastline push the Greeks to the sea, to colonies and to ideas. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Greek Civilisation (b)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The city-state experiments with every way of ruling men, including rule by the citizens themselves.
