# Greek Democracy Philosophy

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Factors Behind the Greek and Roman Civilisations, and Greek Civilisation (a); Greek Civilisation (b). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Factors Behind the Greek and Roman Civilisations, and Greek Civilisation (a)

Poor soil and a broken coastline push the Greeks to the sea, to colonies and to ideas.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greek Civilisation (b)

The city-state experiments with every way of ruling men, including rule by the citizens themselves.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
