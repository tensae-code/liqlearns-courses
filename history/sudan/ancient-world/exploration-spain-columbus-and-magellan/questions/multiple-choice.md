# Exploration Spain Columbus and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The European Geographical Discoveries — Spain and the Rest”?
a) In thirty years the map of the world doubles, and every later empire sails on these charts  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In thirty years the map of the world doubles, and every later empire sails on these charts** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The European Geographical Discoveries — Spain and the Rest”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In thirty years the map of the world doubles, and every later empire sails on these charts  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In thirty years the map of the world doubles, and every later empire sails on these charts** — That is what this lesson teaches.
