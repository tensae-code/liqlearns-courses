# Exploration Spain Columbus and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In thirty years the map of the world doubles, and every later empire sails on these charts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The European Geographical Discoveries — Spain and the Rest” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In thirty years the map of the world doubles, and every later empire sails on these charts.
