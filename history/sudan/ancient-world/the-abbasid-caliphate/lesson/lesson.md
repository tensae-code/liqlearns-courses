# The Abbasid Caliphate

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Rise of the Abbasid State (132–656هـ / 750–1258م); The Conquests of the Abbasid State. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Rise of the Abbasid State (132–656هـ / 750–1258م)

A revolution organised in whispers for thirty years takes an empire in three.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Conquests of the Abbasid State

Two empires bleed each other for two centuries along one frontier, and neither can finish the other.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
