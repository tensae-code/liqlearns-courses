# The Abbasid Caliphate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A revolution organised in whispers for thirty years takes an empire in three. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Conquests of the Abbasid State” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Two empires bleed each other for two centuries along one frontier, and neither can finish the other.
