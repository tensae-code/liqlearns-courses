# The Abbasid Caliphate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Rise of the Abbasid State (132–656هـ / 750–1258م)”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a revolution organised in whispers for thirty years takes an empire in three

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Conquests of the Abbasid State”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** two empires bleed each other for two centuries along one frontier, and neither can finish the other
