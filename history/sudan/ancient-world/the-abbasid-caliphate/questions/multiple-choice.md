# The Abbasid Caliphate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Rise of the Abbasid State (132–656هـ / 750–1258م)”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A revolution organised in whispers for thirty years takes an empire in three  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A revolution organised in whispers for thirty years takes an empire in three** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Conquests of the Abbasid State”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Two empires bleed each other for two centuries along one frontier, and neither can finish the other  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Two empires bleed each other for two centuries along one frontier, and neither can finish the other** — That is what this lesson teaches.
