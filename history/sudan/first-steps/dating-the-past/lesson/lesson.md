# Dating the Past

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The calendar; The Calendar. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The calendar

A calendar is how we know time — when were you born? — and the Muslims chose the Prophet's Hijra as the year their own history starts from.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Calendar

Two calendars hang side by side in Sudanese life, and both are clocks for reading the past.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
