# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A calendar is how we know time — when were you born? — and the Muslims chose the Prophet's Hijra as the year their own history starts from. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Calendar” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Two calendars hang side by side in Sudanese life, and both are clocks for reading the past.
