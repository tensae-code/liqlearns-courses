# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The calendar”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A calendar is how we know time — when were you born? — and the Muslims chose the Prophet's Hijra as the year…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A calendar is how we know time — when were you born? — and the Muslims chose the Prophet's Hijra as the year…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Calendar”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Two calendars hang side by side in Sudanese life, and both are clocks for reading the past  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Two calendars hang side by side in Sudanese life, and both are clocks for reading the past** — That is what this lesson teaches.
