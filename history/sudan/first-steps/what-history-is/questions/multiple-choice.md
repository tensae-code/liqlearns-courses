# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Our Homeland Sudan”?
a) Before any dates, the child learns that history is a story real people lived, and that his own land has worn…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Before any dates, the child learns that history is a story real people lived, and that his own land has worn…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Our Homeland Sudan”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Before any dates, the child learns that history is a story real people lived, and that his own land has worn…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Before any dates, the child learns that history is a story real people lived, and that his own land has worn…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
