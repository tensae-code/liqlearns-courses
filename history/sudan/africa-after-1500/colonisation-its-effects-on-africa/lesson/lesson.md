# Colonisation its Effects on Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: آثار الاستعمار الأوروبي على القارة الأفريقية. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

Cultures distorted and languages imposed, raw materials drained while African industry is kept backward, racism and tribal division sown, borders redrawn to fight over after independence.
