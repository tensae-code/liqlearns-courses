# Colonisation its Effects on Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “آثار الاستعمار الأوروبي على القارة الأفريقية”?
a) Cultures distorted and languages imposed, raw materials drained while African industry is kept backward,…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Cultures distorted and languages imposed, raw materials drained while African industry is kept backward,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “آثار الاستعمار الأوروبي على القارة الأفريقية”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Cultures distorted and languages imposed, raw materials drained while African industry is kept backward,…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Cultures distorted and languages imposed, raw materials drained while African industry is kept backward,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
