# Sudan Turco Egyptian Conquest and Resistance

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: and 6, The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan and Its Resistance; The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan; Turco-Egyptian Rule in Sudan (3). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — and 6, The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan and Its Resistance

An empire marches a thousand miles up the Nile in one season, and spends sixty years being resisted.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan

A divided land of sultanates meets a modern army, and in one campaigning season Sudan has a foreign master.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — Turco-Egyptian Rule in Sudan (3)

Sixty years of refusal, in arms, in flight and in mutiny, until refusal becomes a revolution.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
