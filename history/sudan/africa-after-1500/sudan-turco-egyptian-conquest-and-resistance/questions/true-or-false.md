# Sudan Turco Egyptian Conquest and Resistance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An empire marches a thousand miles up the Nile in one season, and spends sixty years being resisted. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A divided land of sultanates meets a modern army, and in one campaigning season Sudan has a foreign master.
