# Sudan Turco Egyptian Conquest and Resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “and 6, The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan and Its Resistance”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) An empire marches a thousand miles up the Nile in one season, and spends sixty years being resisted  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) An empire marches a thousand miles up the Nile in one season, and spends sixty years being resisted** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A divided land of sultanates meets a modern army, and in one campaigning season Sudan has a foreign master  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A divided land of sultanates meets a modern army, and in one campaigning season Sudan has a foreign master** — That is what this lesson teaches.
