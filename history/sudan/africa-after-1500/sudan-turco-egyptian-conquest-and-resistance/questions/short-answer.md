# Sudan Turco Egyptian Conquest and Resistance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “and 6, The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan and Its Resistance”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** an empire marches a thousand miles up the Nile in one season, and spends sixty years being resisted

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a divided land of sultanates meets a modern army, and in one campaigning season Sudan has a foreign master
