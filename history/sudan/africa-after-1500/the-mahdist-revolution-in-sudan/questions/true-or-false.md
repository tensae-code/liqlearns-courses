# The Mahdist Revolution in Sudan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A holy man with no army declares the empire's rule finished, and Sudan believes him. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Liberation of El Obeid (1882) and the Hicks Pasha Expedition (November 1883)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Ten thousand government soldiers march into the forest of Shaykan and do not march out.
