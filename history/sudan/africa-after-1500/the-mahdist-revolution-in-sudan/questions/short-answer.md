# The Mahdist Revolution in Sudan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Mahdist Revolution 1881–1885, Its Causes and the Reasons for Its Success”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a holy man with no army declares the empire's rule finished, and Sudan believes him

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Liberation of El Obeid (1882) and the Hicks Pasha Expedition (November 1883)”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** ten thousand government soldiers march into the forest of Shaykan and do not march out
