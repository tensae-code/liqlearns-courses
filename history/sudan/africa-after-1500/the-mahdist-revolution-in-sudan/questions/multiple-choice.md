# The Mahdist Revolution in Sudan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Mahdist Revolution 1881–1885, Its Causes and the Reasons for Its Success”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A holy man with no army declares the empire's rule finished, and Sudan believes him  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A holy man with no army declares the empire's rule finished, and Sudan believes him** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Liberation of El Obeid (1882) and the Hicks Pasha Expedition (November 1883)”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Ten thousand government soldiers march into the forest of Shaykan and do not march out  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Ten thousand government soldiers march into the forest of Shaykan and do not march out** — That is what this lesson teaches.
