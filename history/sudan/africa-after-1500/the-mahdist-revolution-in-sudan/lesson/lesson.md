# The Mahdist Revolution in Sudan

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Mahdist Revolution 1881–1885, Its Causes and the Reasons for Its Success; The Liberation of El Obeid (1882) and the Hicks Pasha Expedition (November 1883); The Mahdist Revolution in Eastern Sudan, and The Liberation of Khartoum. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.
