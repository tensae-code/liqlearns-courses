# Hands-on — Sudan Under Turco Egyptian Rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a line across a page — a road or a timeline. Mark the parts of this story on it in order: Administration under Muhammad Ali Pasha; Sudan under Abbas and Muhammad Said; The Era of; Education in the Turco-Egyptian Era; Unit 1, Review Lesson, Sudan under Turco-Egyptian Rule 1821–1885; Rule and Administration in the Turco-Egyptian Era (Muhammad Ali's era, 1821–1848); Sudan under Muhammad Ali's Successors Abbas and Said (1848–1863); The Era of Khedive Ismail in Sudan (1863–1879); Turco-Egyptian Rule in Sudan (1) (1821–1885); Turco-Egyptian Rule in Sudan (2). Under each mark, write who is there and what turns. This lesson's story is: Occupation doubles as state-building;.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Occupation doubles as state-building;. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson's story echoing in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of your town. Each time you see an echo (or its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
