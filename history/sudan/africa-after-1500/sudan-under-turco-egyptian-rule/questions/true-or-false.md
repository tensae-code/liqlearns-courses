# Sudan Under Turco Egyptian Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Occupation doubles as state-building; the Sudan that exists today is drawn on the map in these sixty years. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Education in the Turco-Egyptian Era” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The occupier's schools are also the seedbed of the men who will end the occupation.
