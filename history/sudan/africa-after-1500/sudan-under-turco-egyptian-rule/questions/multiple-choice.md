# Sudan Under Turco Egyptian Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Administration under Muhammad Ali Pasha; Sudan under Abbas and Muhammad Said; The Era of”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Occupation doubles as state-building; the Sudan that exists today is drawn on the map in these sixty years  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Occupation doubles as state-building; the Sudan that exists today is drawn on the map in these sixty years** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Education in the Turco-Egyptian Era”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The occupier's schools are also the seedbed of the men who will end the occupation  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The occupier's schools are also the seedbed of the men who will end the occupation** — That is what this lesson teaches.
