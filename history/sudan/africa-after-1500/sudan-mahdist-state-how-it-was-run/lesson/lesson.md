# Sudan Mahdist State How it Was Run

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: نظم الحكم والإدارة في الدولة المهدية. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## نظم الحكم والإدارة في الدولة المهدية

The Mahdi rules through four khalifas modelled on the Prophet's, with Abdullahi al-Taishi first among them; the Bayt al-Mal treasury, Sharia courts, provincial amirs and a banner-divided army hold the new state together.
