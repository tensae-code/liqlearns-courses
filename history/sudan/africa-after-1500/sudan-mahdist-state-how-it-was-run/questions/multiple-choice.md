# Sudan Mahdist State How it Was Run — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “نظم الحكم والإدارة في الدولة المهدية”?
a) The Mahdi rules through four khalifas modelled on the Prophet's, with Abdullahi al-Taishi first among them;…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Mahdi rules through four khalifas modelled on the Prophet's, with Abdullahi al-Taishi first among them;…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “نظم الحكم والإدارة في الدولة المهدية”?
