# Darfur the Fur and Keira Sultanates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (4)”?
a) The west's great sultanate runs on customary law, granted land and the pilgrim road  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The west's great sultanate runs on customary law, granted land and the pilgrim road** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The west's great sultanate runs on customary law, granted land and the pilgrim road  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
