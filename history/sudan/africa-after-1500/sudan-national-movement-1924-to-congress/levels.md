# Sudan National Movement 1924 to Congress — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Sudanese Union (1920) works in secret leaflets, Ali Abd al-Latif's White Flag League leads the 1924 revolution — demonstrations in Khartoum, the cadets' stand and — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
