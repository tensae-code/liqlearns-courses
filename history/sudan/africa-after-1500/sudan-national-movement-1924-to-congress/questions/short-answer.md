# Sudan National Movement 1924 to Congress — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “الحركة الوطنية السودانية الحديثة 1918–1953”.
**Answer:** the Sudanese Union (1920) works in secret leaflets, Ali Abd al-Latif's White Flag League leads the 1924 revolution — demonstrations in Khartoum, the cadets' stand and Abd al-Fadil al-Maz at the Blue Nile bridge — and the Graduates' Congress of 1938 turns protest into a national programme, demanding self-determination in 1942
