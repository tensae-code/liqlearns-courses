# Sudan Under the Anglo Egyptian Condominium — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Condominium Agreement”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Neither colony nor independence: a legal fiction governs Sudan for half a century  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Neither colony nor independence: a legal fiction governs Sudan for half a century** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Development of Constitutional Institutions in the Condominium Period”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The Sudanese are given chairs in the room, then demand the room  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The Sudanese are given chairs in the room, then demand the room** — That is what this lesson teaches.
