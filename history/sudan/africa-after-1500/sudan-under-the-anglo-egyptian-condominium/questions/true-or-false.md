# Sudan Under the Anglo Egyptian Condominium — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Neither colony nor independence: a legal fiction governs Sudan for half a century. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Development of Constitutional Institutions in the Condominium Period” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The Sudanese are given chairs in the room, then demand the room.
