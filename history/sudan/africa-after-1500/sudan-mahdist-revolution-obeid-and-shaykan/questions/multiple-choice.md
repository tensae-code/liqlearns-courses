# Sudan Mahdist Revolution Obeid and Shaykan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “المواجهات العسكرية (2)”?
a) El Obeid is starved into surrender in July 1883 after a costly Friday assault teaches the Mahdi the siege;…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) El Obeid is starved into surrender in July 1883 after a costly Friday assault teaches the Mahdi the siege;…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
