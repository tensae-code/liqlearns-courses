# Sudan Mahdist Revolution Obeid and Shaykan

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: المواجهات العسكرية (2). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## المواجهات العسكرية (2)

El Obeid is starved into surrender in July 1883 after a costly Friday assault teaches the Mahdi the siege; then Hicks Pasha's great Egyptian army is annihilated in the Shaykan forest, November 1883.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: El Obeid is starved into surrender in July 1883 after a costly Friday assault teaches the Mahdi the siege;. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Sudan Mahdist Revolution Obeid and Shaykan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
