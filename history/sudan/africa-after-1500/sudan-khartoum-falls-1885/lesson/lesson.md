# Sudan Khartoum Falls 1885

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: المواجهات العسكرية (3) — تحرير الخرطوم. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## المواجهات العسكرية (3) — تحرير الخرطوم

With Kordofan, Darfur and the east won, Egypt votes to evacuate and sends Gordon; the siege closes every road, and at dawn on 26 January 1885 the Ansar storm Khartoum and Gordon dies at his palace — Wolseley's relief column arrives two days late.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: With Kordofan, Darfur and the east won, Egypt votes to evacuate and sends Gordon;. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Sudan Khartoum Falls 1885. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
