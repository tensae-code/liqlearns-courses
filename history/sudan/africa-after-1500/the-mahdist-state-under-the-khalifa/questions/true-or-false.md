# The Mahdist State Under the Khalifa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A revolution must become a state, and the Khalifa's iron rule is the price. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Mahdist State's Foreign Policy, and Its System of Government and Administration” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Omdurman behaves like a capital: it taxes, judges, negotiates and makes war.
