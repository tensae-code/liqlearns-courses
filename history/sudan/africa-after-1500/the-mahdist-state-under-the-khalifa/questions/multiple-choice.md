# The Mahdist State Under the Khalifa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Intellectual Foundations of the Mahdiyya, and The Era of the Khalifa Abdullahi 1885–1898”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A revolution must become a state, and the Khalifa's iron rule is the price  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A revolution must become a state, and the Khalifa's iron rule is the price** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Mahdist State's Foreign Policy, and Its System of Government and Administration”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Omdurman behaves like a capital: it taxes, judges, negotiates and makes war  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Omdurman behaves like a capital: it taxes, judges, negotiates and makes war** — That is what this lesson teaches.
