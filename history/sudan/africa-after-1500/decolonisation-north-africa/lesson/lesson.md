# Decolonisation North Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Algeria part; Algeria part; The Independence Movement in the Arab Maghreb. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Algeria part

From Abd al-Qadir's resistance and the 1871 Muqrani revolt, through the Star of North Africa and Ben Badis' scholars, the Sétif massacre of 1945 teaches Algeria that only arms will do; the FLN rises on 1 November 1954 and wins independence on 5 July 1962 at the price of a million martyrs.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Algeria part

Thirty years of parties and petitions, one massacre, then seven years of war — and Algeria is free.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Independence Movement in the Arab Maghreb

Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia press France and Italy toward the exits.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
