# Decolonisation North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From Abd al-Qadir's resistance and the 1871 Muqrani revolt, through the Star of North Africa and Ben Badis' scholars, the Sétif massacre of 1945 teaches Algeria that only arms will do; the FLN rises on 1 November 1954 and wins independence on 5 July 1962 at the price of a million martyrs. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Algeria part” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Thirty years of parties and petitions, one massacre, then seven years of war — and Algeria is free.
