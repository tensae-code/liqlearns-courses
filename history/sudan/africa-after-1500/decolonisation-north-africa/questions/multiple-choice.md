# Decolonisation North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Algeria part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) From Abd al-Qadir's resistance and the 1871 Muqrani revolt, through the Star of North Africa and Ben Badis'…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) From Abd al-Qadir's resistance and the 1871 Muqrani revolt, through the Star of North Africa and Ben Badis'…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Algeria part”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Thirty years of parties and petitions, one massacre, then seven years of war — and Algeria is free  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Thirty years of parties and petitions, one massacre, then seven years of war — and Algeria is free** — That is what this lesson teaches.
