# Decolonisation North Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Algeria part”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from Abd al-Qadir's resistance and the 1871 Muqrani revolt, through the Star of North Africa and Ben Badis' scholars, the Sétif massacre of 1945 teaches Algeria that only arms will do; the FLN rises on 1 November 1954 and wins independence on 5 July 1962 at the price of a million martyrs

**2.** Who is in the story of “Algeria part”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** thirty years of parties and petitions, one massacre, then seven years of war — and Algeria is free
