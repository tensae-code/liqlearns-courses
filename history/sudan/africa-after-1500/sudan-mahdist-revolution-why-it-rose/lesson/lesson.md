# Sudan Mahdist Revolution Why it Rose

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Why the Mahdist revolution rose and won. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Why the Mahdist revolution rose and won

Muhammad Ahmad, born 1843 on Labab Island and formed by the Sufi orders, proclaims the Mahdiyya from Aba Island; heavy taxes beaten out of people, conquest violence and favouritism make Sudan ready, and the revolt runs in two acts — revolution 1881–1885, state 1885–1898.
