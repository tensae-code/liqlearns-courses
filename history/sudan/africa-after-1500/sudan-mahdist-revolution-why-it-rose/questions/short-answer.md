# Sudan Mahdist Revolution Why it Rose — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Why the Mahdist revolution rose and won”.
**Answer:** Muhammad Ahmad, born 1843 on Labab Island and formed by the Sufi orders, proclaims the Mahdiyya from Aba Island; heavy taxes beaten out of people, conquest violence and favouritism make Sudan ready, and the revolt runs in two acts — revolution 1881–1885, state 1885–1898
