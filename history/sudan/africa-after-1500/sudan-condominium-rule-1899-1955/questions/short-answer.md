# Sudan Condominium Rule 1899 1955 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “نظم الحكم والإدارة في فترة الحكم الثنائي”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Britain invades for raw materials, markets and Egypt's southern border — and to avenge Gordon and bar France at Fashoda; Karari (September 1898) destroys the Mahdist state, and the invented "Condominium" of the 1899 agreement leaves Britain ruling through a Governor-General while Egyptians fill the lower posts

**2.** Who is in the story of “التطورات الاقتصادية والاجتماعية في العهد الثنائي”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** land laws make unregistered land the government's (1925), railways and Port Sudan open the country, the Sennar Dam feeds the Gezira cotton scheme, and Gordon College and the medical services remake the towns
