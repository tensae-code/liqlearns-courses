# Sudan Condominium Rule 1899 1955 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “نظم الحكم والإدارة في فترة الحكم الثنائي”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Britain invades for raw materials, markets and Egypt's southern border — and to avenge Gordon and bar France…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Britain invades for raw materials, markets and Egypt's southern border — and to avenge Gordon and bar France…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “التطورات الاقتصادية والاجتماعية في العهد الثنائي”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Land laws make unregistered land the government's (1925), railways and Port Sudan open the country, the…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Land laws make unregistered land the government's (1925), railways and Port Sudan open the country, the…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
