# Video script — Sudan Condominium Rule 1899 1955

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Britain invades for raw materials, markets and Egypt's southern border — and to avenge Gordon and bar France at Fashoda;.

نظم الحكم والإدارة في فترة الحكم الثنائي.

Britain invades for raw materials, markets and Egypt's southern border — and to avenge Gordon and bar France at Fashoda; Karari (September 1898) destroys the Mahdist state, and the invented "Condominium" of the 1899 agreement leaves Britain ruling through a Governor-General while Egyptians fill the lower posts.

التطورات الاقتصادية والاجتماعية في العهد الثنائي.

Land laws make unregistered land the government's (1925), railways and Port Sudan open the country, the Sennar Dam feeds the Gezira cotton scheme, and Gordon College and the medical services remake the towns.
