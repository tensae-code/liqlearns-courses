Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “مقاومة السودانيين للحكم الثنائي — حركات المقاومة الدينية والقبلية 1899–1918”?
a) The Khalifa dies at Umm Diwaykarat (1899), Wad Habuba rises in the Gezira (1908), the Nuba Mountains revolt…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
