# Sudan Resistance to the Condominium — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The Khalifa dies at Umm Diwaykarat (1899), Wad Habuba rises in the Gezira (1908), the Nuba Mountains revolt again and again, and Sultan Ali Dinar holds Darfur until — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
