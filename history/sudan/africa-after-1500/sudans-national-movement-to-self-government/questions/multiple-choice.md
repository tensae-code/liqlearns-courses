# Sudans National Movement to Self Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Sudanese National Movement, First and Second Phases (1899–1917; 1918–1921)”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A young officer's league carries a flag with no emblem but Sudan's name, and the movement has its first…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A young officer's league carries a flag with no emblem but Sudan's name, and the movement has its first…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The National Movement, Third, Fourth and Fifth Phases (1925–1937; 1938–1945; 1945–1953)”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Clubs become congresses, congresses become parties, and parties take the country  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Clubs become congresses, congresses become parties, and parties take the country** — That is what this lesson teaches.
