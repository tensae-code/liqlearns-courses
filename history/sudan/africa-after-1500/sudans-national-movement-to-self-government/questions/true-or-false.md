# Sudans National Movement to Self Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A young officer's league carries a flag with no emblem but Sudan's name, and the movement has its first martyr-generation. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The National Movement, Third, Fourth and Fifth Phases (1925–1937; 1938–1945; 1945–1953)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Clubs become congresses, congresses become parties, and parties take the country.
