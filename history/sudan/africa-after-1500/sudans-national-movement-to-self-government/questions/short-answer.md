# Sudans National Movement to Self Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Sudanese National Movement, First and Second Phases (1899–1917; 1918–1921)”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a young officer's league carries a flag with no emblem but Sudan's name, and the movement has its first martyr-generation

**2.** Who is in the story of “The National Movement, Third, Fourth and Fifth Phases (1925–1937; 1938–1945; 1945–1953)”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** clubs become congresses, congresses become parties, and parties take the country
