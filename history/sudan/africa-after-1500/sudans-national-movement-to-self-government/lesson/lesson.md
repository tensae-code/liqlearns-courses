# Sudans National Movement to Self Government

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Sudanese National Movement, First and Second Phases (1899–1917; 1918–1921); The National Movement, Third, Fourth and Fifth Phases (1925–1937; 1938–1945; 1945–1953). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Sudanese National Movement, First and Second Phases (1899–1917; 1918–1921)

A young officer's league carries a flag with no emblem but Sudan's name, and the movement has its first martyr-generation.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The National Movement, Third, Fourth and Fifth Phases (1925–1937; 1938–1945; 1945–1953)

Clubs become congresses, congresses become parties, and parties take the country.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
