# European Colonialism in Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “European Colonialism in Africa”?
a) The child is taught to name the thing, count its motives and recognise its instruments before he ever meets…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The child is taught to name the thing, count its motives and recognise its instruments before he ever meets…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “European Colonialism in Africa”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The child is taught to name the thing, count its motives and recognise its instruments before he ever meets…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The child is taught to name the thing, count its motives and recognise its instruments before he ever meets…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
