# Colonisation the Road to the Scramble

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: دوافع الاستعمار الأوروبي لأفريقيا; European Colonialism in the African Continent. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — دوافع الاستعمار الأوروبي لأفريقيا

Factories hungry for raw materials and markets, surplus people to export, missionaries preaching a "civilising" mission, and the chokepoints — Suez, Gibraltar, Bab el-Mandeb, the Cape — pull Europe towards Africa.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — European Colonialism in the African Continent

The continent that received Islam by caravan is now carved up by steamship and treaty.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
