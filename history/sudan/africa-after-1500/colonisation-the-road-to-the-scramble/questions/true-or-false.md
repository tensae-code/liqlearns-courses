# Colonisation the Road to the Scramble — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Factories hungry for raw materials and markets, surplus people to export, missionaries preaching a "civilising" mission, and the chokepoints — Suez, Gibraltar, Bab el-Mandeb, the Cape — pull Europe towards Africa. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “European Colonialism in the African Continent” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The continent that received Islam by caravan is now carved up by steamship and treaty.
