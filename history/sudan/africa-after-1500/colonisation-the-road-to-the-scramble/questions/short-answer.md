# Colonisation the Road to the Scramble — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “دوافع الاستعمار الأوروبي لأفريقيا”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** factories hungry for raw materials and markets, surplus people to export, missionaries preaching a "civilising" mission, and the chokepoints — Suez, Gibraltar, Bab el-Mandeb, the Cape — pull Europe towards Africa

**2.** Who is in the story of “European Colonialism in the African Continent”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the continent that received Islam by caravan is now carved up by steamship and treaty
