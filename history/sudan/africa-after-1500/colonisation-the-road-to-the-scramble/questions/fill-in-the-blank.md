# Colonisation the Road to the Scramble — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** Factories hungry for raw materials and markets, surplus people to export, missionaries preaching a "civilising" mission, and the chokepoints — ____, Gibraltar, Bab el-Mandeb, the Cape — pull Europe towards Africa (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Suez

**2.** The continent that received ____ by caravan is now carved up by steamship and treaty (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Islam
