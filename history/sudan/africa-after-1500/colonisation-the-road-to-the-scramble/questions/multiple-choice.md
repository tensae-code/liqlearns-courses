# Colonisation the Road to the Scramble — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “دوافع الاستعمار الأوروبي لأفريقيا”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Factories hungry for raw materials and markets, surplus people to export, missionaries preaching a…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Factories hungry for raw materials and markets, surplus people to export, missionaries preaching a…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “European Colonialism in the African Continent”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The continent that received Islam by caravan is now carved up by steamship and treaty  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The continent that received Islam by caravan is now carved up by steamship and treaty** — That is what this lesson teaches.
