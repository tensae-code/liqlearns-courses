# Usman Dan Fodio and the Sokoto Caliphate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “A Model of Islamic Movements and Revolutions”?
a) A teacher with a pen raises an army of believers and redraws the map of West Africa  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A teacher with a pen raises an army of believers and redraws the map of West Africa** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “A Model of Islamic Movements and Revolutions”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A teacher with a pen raises an army of believers and redraws the map of West Africa  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A teacher with a pen raises an army of believers and redraws the map of West Africa** — That is what this lesson teaches.
