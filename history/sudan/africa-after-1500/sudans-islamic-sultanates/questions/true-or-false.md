# Sudans Islamic Sultanates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two allies pull down the last Christian capital and raise a Muslim sultanate on the Blue Nile that lasts three centuries. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (2)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In the far west, mountain farmers build a sultanate of their own that outlasts empires around it.
