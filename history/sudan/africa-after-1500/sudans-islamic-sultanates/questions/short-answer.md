# Sudans Islamic Sultanates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (1)”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** two allies pull down the last Christian capital and raise a Muslim sultanate on the Blue Nile that lasts three centuries

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (2)”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** in the far west, mountain farmers build a sultanate of their own that outlasts empires around it
