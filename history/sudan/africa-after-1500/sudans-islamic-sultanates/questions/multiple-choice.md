# Sudans Islamic Sultanates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (1)”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Two allies pull down the last Christian capital and raise a Muslim sultanate on the Blue Nile that lasts…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Two allies pull down the last Christian capital and raise a Muslim sultanate on the Blue Nile that lasts…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (2)”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In the far west, mountain farmers build a sultanate of their own that outlasts empires around it  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In the far west, mountain farmers build a sultanate of their own that outlasts empires around it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
