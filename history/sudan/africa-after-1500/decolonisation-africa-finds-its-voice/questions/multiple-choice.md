# Decolonisation Africa Finds its Voice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Two Models of National Liberation Movements (Ghana part)”?
a) The Gold Coast shows Africa the way out  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Gold Coast shows Africa the way out** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Two Models of National Liberation Movements (Ghana part)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The Gold Coast shows Africa the way out  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The Gold Coast shows Africa the way out** — That is what this lesson teaches.
