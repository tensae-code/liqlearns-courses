# Colonisation Direct and Indirect Rule

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: أنواع الاستعمار الأوروبي لأفريقيا. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## أنواع الاستعمار الأوروبي لأفريقيا

Settler colonialism erases a people's identity (France in Algeria, Britain in South Africa), economic colonialism milks a land's wealth (Sudan 1899, Egypt 1882, Libya 1911), and political colonialism rules by protectorate and mandate (the Gulf, Palestine), with language imposed through schools.
