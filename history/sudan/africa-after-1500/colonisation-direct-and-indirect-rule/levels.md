# Colonisation Direct and Indirect Rule — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Settler colonialism erases a people's identity (France in Algeria, Britain in South Africa), economic colonialism milks a land's wealth (Sudan 1899, Egypt 1882, Libya — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
