# Congo from Leopold to Lumumba

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Congo part. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Congo part

Leopold II takes the Congo as his personal estate at Berlin and bleeds it for rubber and ivory until world outcry hands it to Belgium (1908); half a century later Lumumba's MNC demands freedom, the Leopoldville riots force Brussels to the table, and the Congo stands independent on 30 June 1960 with Lumumba its premier.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Leopold II takes the Congo as his personal estate at Berlin and bleeds it for rubber and ivory until world outcry hands it to Belgium (1908);. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Congo from Leopold to Lumumba. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
