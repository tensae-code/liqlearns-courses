# Congo from Leopold to Lumumba — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Congo part”.
**Answer:** Leopold II takes the Congo as his personal estate at Berlin and bleeds it for rubber and ivory until world outcry hands it to Belgium (1908); half a century later Lumumba's MNC demands freedom, the Leopoldville riots force Brussels to the table, and the Congo stands independent on 30 June 1960 with Lumumba its premier
