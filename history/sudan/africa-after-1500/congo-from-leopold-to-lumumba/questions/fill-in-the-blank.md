# Congo from Leopold to Lumumba — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ II takes the Congo as his personal estate at Berlin and bleeds it for rubber and ivory until world outcry hands it to Belgium (1908); half a century later Lumumba's MNC demands freedom, the Leopoldville riots force Brussels to the table, and the Congo stands independent on 30 June 1960 with Lumumba its premier
