# Congo from Leopold to Lumumba — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Congo part”?
a) Leopold II takes the Congo as his personal estate at Berlin and bleeds it for rubber and ivory until world…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Leopold II takes the Congo as his personal estate at Berlin and bleeds it for rubber and ivory until world…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Congo part”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Leopold II takes the Congo as his personal estate at Berlin and bleeds it for rubber and ivory until world…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Leopold II takes the Congo as his personal estate at Berlin and bleeds it for rubber and ivory until world…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
