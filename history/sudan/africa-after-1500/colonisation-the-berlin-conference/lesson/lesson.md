# Colonisation the Berlin Conference

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: تقسيم القارة الأفريقية بين الدول الأوروبية. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## تقسيم القارة الأفريقية بين الدول الأوروبية

At Berlin (November 1884 – February 1885) fourteen states agree the rules — notify your protectorate, occupy effectively, keep the Congo and Niger free — and the map is dealt out: France the north and west, Britain the east and south, Belgium the Congo, Germany, Portugal, Spain and Italy their shares.
