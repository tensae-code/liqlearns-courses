# Colonisation the Berlin Conference — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Berlin (November 1884 – February 1885) fourteen states agree the rules — notify your protectorate, occupy effectively, keep the Congo and Niger free — and the map is dealt out: France the north and west, Britain the east and south, Belgium the Congo, Germany, Portugal, Spain and Italy their shares.
**Answer: True**
