# Colonisation the Berlin Conference — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “تقسيم القارة الأفريقية بين الدول الأوروبية”.
**Answer:** at Berlin (November 1884 – February 1885) fourteen states agree the rules — notify your protectorate, occupy effectively, keep the Congo and Niger free — and the map is dealt out: France the north and west, Britain the east and south, Belgium the Congo, Germany, Portugal, Spain and Italy their shares
