# Sudan Wins Independence 1956 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Independence of Sudan”?
a) After 135 years of foreign rule the Sudanese flag goes up over Khartoum  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) After 135 years of foreign rule the Sudanese flag goes up over Khartoum** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Independence of Sudan”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) After 135 years of foreign rule the Sudanese flag goes up over Khartoum  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) After 135 years of foreign rule the Sudanese flag goes up over Khartoum** — That is what this lesson teaches.
