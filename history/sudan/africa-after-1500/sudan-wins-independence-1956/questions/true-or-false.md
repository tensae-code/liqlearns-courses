# Sudan Wins Independence 1956 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After 135 years of foreign rule the Sudanese flag goes up over Khartoum.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Independence of Sudan” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: After 135 years of foreign rule the Sudanese flag goes up over Khartoum.
