# African Unity the Founding of the OAU

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: منظمة الوحدة الأفريقية. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## منظمة الوحدة الأفريقية

With fifteen states free by the early 1960s, thirty governments sign the charter at Addis Ababa on 25 May 1963 — Nkrumah among its loudest founders — vowing unity, sovereignty and an end to colonialism, and the organisation mediates between its members.
