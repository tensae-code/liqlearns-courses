# African Unity the Founding of the OAU — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — With fifteen states free by the early 1960s, thirty governments sign the charter at Addis Ababa on 25 May 1963 — Nkrumah among its loudest founders — vowing unity — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
