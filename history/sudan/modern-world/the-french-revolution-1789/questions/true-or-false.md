# The French Revolution 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Liberty, brotherhood, equality — the slogans are taught with the tax receipts that caused them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The French Revolution — its Causes and Results” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Liberty, brotherhood, equality — the slogans are taught with the tax receipts that caused them.
