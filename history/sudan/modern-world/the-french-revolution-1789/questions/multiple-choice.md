# The French Revolution 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The French Revolution — its Causes and Results”?
a) Liberty, brotherhood, equality — the slogans are taught with the tax receipts that caused them  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Liberty, brotherhood, equality — the slogans are taught with the tax receipts that caused them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The French Revolution — its Causes and Results”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Liberty, brotherhood, equality — the slogans are taught with the tax receipts that caused them  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Liberty, brotherhood, equality — the slogans are taught with the tax receipts that caused them** — That is what this lesson teaches.
