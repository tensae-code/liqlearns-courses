# Reform Movements in the Islamic World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Reform Movements in the Islamic World”?
a) From Arabia to Senegal, teachers and orders try to purify the faith and free the land  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) From Arabia to Senegal, teachers and orders try to purify the faith and free the land** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Reform Movements in the Islamic World”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) From Arabia to Senegal, teachers and orders try to purify the faith and free the land  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) From Arabia to Senegal, teachers and orders try to purify the faith and free the land** — That is what this lesson teaches.
