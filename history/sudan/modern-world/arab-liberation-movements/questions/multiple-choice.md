# Arab Liberation Movements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The General Features of the Arab Liberation Movements”?
a) One grammar of liberation, spoken from Casablanca to Baghdad  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) One grammar of liberation, spoken from Casablanca to Baghdad** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The General Features of the Arab Liberation Movements”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) One grammar of liberation, spoken from Casablanca to Baghdad  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) One grammar of liberation, spoken from Casablanca to Baghdad** — That is what this lesson teaches.
