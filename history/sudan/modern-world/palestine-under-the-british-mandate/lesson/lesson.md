# Palestine Under the British Mandate

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The British Mandate over Palestine, and The Palestinian Arab Revolt 1936–1939. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The British Mandate over Palestine, and The Palestinian Arab Revolt 1936–1939

The mandate's two promises collide, and Palestine answers with a six-month strike and three years of revolt.
