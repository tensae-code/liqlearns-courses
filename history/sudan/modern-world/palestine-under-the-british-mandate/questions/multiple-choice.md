# Palestine Under the British Mandate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The British Mandate over Palestine, and The Palestinian Arab Revolt 1936–1939”?
a) The mandate's two promises collide, and Palestine answers with a six-month strike and three years of revolt  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The mandate's two promises collide, and Palestine answers with a six-month strike and three years of revolt** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The British Mandate over Palestine, and The Palestinian Arab Revolt 1936–1939”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The mandate's two promises collide, and Palestine answers with a six-month strike and three years of revolt  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The mandate's two promises collide, and Palestine answers with a six-month strike and three years of revolt** — That is what this lesson teaches.
