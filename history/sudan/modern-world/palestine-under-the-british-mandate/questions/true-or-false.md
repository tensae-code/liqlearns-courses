# Palestine Under the British Mandate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The mandate's two promises collide, and Palestine answers with a six-month strike and three years of revolt.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The British Mandate over Palestine, and The Palestinian Arab Revolt 1936–1939” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The mandate's two promises collide, and Palestine answers with a six-month strike and three years of revolt.
