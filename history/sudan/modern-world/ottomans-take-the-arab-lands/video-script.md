# Video script — Ottomans Take the Arab Lands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Selim I breaks the Safavids at Chaldiran (1514), crushes the Mamluks at Marj Dabiq (1516) and takes Syria, Egypt and the Hijaz — the caliphate itself passes to him —.

امتداد الدولة العثمانية في البلاد العربية.

Selim I breaks the Safavids at Chaldiran (1514), crushes the Mamluks at Marj Dabiq (1516) and takes Syria, Egypt and the Hijaz — the caliphate itself passes to him — then Algeria (1519), Libya and Tunisia, Iraq (1534), Yemen, and in 1821 Sudan in the Sultan's name.

نظم الحكم العثماني للولايات العربية ونتائجها.

Each Arab wilaya is ruled by a wali who sends Istanbul its fixed sum and answers for order, over sanjak-beys, kashifs and a finance defterdar, with Ottoman garrisons and Hanafi judges to hold it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
