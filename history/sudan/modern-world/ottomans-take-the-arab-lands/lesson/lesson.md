# Ottomans Take the Arab Lands

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: امتداد الدولة العثمانية في البلاد العربية; نظم الحكم العثماني للولايات العربية ونتائجها. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — امتداد الدولة العثمانية في البلاد العربية

Selim I breaks the Safavids at Chaldiran (1514), crushes the Mamluks at Marj Dabiq (1516) and takes Syria, Egypt and the Hijaz — the caliphate itself passes to him — then Algeria (1519), Libya and Tunisia, Iraq (1534), Yemen, and in 1821 Sudan in the Sultan's name.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — نظم الحكم العثماني للولايات العربية ونتائجها

Each Arab wilaya is ruled by a wali who sends Istanbul its fixed sum and answers for order, over sanjak-beys, kashifs and a finance defterdar, with Ottoman garrisons and Hanafi judges to hold it.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
