# Ottomans Take the Arab Lands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Selim I breaks the Safavids at Chaldiran (1514), crushes the Mamluks at Marj Dabiq (1516) and takes Syria, Egypt and the Hijaz — the caliphate itself passes to him — then Algeria (1519), Libya and Tunisia, Iraq (1534), Yemen, and in 1821 Sudan in the Sultan's name. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “نظم الحكم العثماني للولايات العربية ونتائجها” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Each Arab wilaya is ruled by a wali who sends Istanbul its fixed sum and answers for order, over sanjak-beys,….
