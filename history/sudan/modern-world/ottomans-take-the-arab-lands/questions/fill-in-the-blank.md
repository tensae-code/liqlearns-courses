# Ottomans Take the Arab Lands — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ I breaks the Safavids at Chaldiran (1514), crushes the Mamluks at Marj Dabiq (1516) and takes Syria, Egypt and the Hijaz — the caliphate itself passes to him — then Algeria (1519), Libya and Tunisia, Iraq (1534), Yemen, and in 1821 Sudan in the Sultan's name (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Selim

**2.** Each ____ wilaya is ruled by a wali who sends Istanbul its fixed sum and answers for order, over sanjak-beys, kashifs and a finance defterdar, with Ottoman garrisons and Hanafi judges to hold it (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Arab
