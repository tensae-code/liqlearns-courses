# Ottomans Take the Arab Lands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “امتداد الدولة العثمانية في البلاد العربية”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Selim I breaks the Safavids at Chaldiran (1514), crushes the Mamluks at Marj Dabiq (1516) and takes Syria,…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Selim I breaks the Safavids at Chaldiran (1514), crushes the Mamluks at Marj Dabiq (1516) and takes Syria,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “نظم الحكم العثماني للولايات العربية ونتائجها”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Each Arab wilaya is ruled by a wali who sends Istanbul its fixed sum and answers for order, over sanjak-beys,…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Each Arab wilaya is ruled by a wali who sends Istanbul its fixed sum and answers for order, over sanjak-beys,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
