# Ottomans Take the Arab Lands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “امتداد الدولة العثمانية في البلاد العربية”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Selim I breaks the Safavids at Chaldiran (1514), crushes the Mamluks at Marj Dabiq (1516) and takes Syria, Egypt and the Hijaz — the caliphate itself passes to him — then Algeria (1519), Libya and Tunisia, Iraq (1534), Yemen, and in 1821 Sudan in the Sultan's name

**2.** Who is in the story of “نظم الحكم العثماني للولايات العربية ونتائجها”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** each Arab wilaya is ruled by a wali who sends Istanbul its fixed sum and answers for order, over sanjak-beys, kashifs and a finance defterdar, with Ottoman garrisons and Hanafi judges to hold it
