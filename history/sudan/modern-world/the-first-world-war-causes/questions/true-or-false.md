# The First World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A Serb student's bullets kill the Austrian heir at Sarajevo, and underneath lie Balkan rivalry between Austria and Russia, the Franco-German border quarrel, restless nationalities, the imperial race for markets and the armed peace of two alliance camps. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The First World War (causes part)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Alliances and empires load the gun.
