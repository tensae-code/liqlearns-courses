# The First World War Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Causes part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A Serb student's bullets kill the Austrian heir at Sarajevo, and underneath lie Balkan rivalry between…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A Serb student's bullets kill the Austrian heir at Sarajevo, and underneath lie Balkan rivalry between…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The First World War (causes part)”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Alliances and empires load the gun  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Alliances and empires load the gun** — That is what this lesson teaches.
