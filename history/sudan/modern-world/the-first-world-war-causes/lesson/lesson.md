# The First World War Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Causes part; The First World War (causes part). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Causes part

A Serb student's bullets kill the Austrian heir at Sarajevo, and underneath lie Balkan rivalry between Austria and Russia, the Franco-German border quarrel, restless nationalities, the imperial race for markets and the armed peace of two alliance camps.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The First World War (causes part)

Alliances and empires load the gun.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
