# The Second World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Germany invades Poland and the guarantees fire; behind it stand Versailles' grievances, the 1929 crash that raised Nazi, Fascist and militarist regimes, their march on Manchuria, Ethiopia, Austria and Czechoslovakia, and a League that could not stop them. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Second World War (causes part)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Versailles' grievances and the crash bring the dictators.
