# The Second World War Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Causes part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Germany invades Poland and the guarantees fire; behind it stand Versailles' grievances, the 1929 crash that…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Germany invades Poland and the guarantees fire; behind it stand Versailles' grievances, the 1929 crash that…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Second World War (causes part)”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Versailles' grievances and the crash bring the dictators  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Versailles' grievances and the crash bring the dictators** — That is what this lesson teaches.
