# Ottomans in Europe and Constantinople 1453 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Orhan organises the state — mosques, schools, coinage and the new Janissary corps — wins in the Balkans (Kosovo 1389), and in 1453 Mehmed II storms Constantinople, ends Byzantium and makes the city his capital, Istanbul.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “اتساع الإمارة العثمانية وفتوحاتها في أوروبا” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Orhan organises the state — mosques, schools, coinage and the new Janissary corps — wins in the Balkans….
