# Ottomans in Europe and Constantinople 1453 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “اتساع الإمارة العثمانية وفتوحاتها في أوروبا”.
**Answer:** Orhan organises the state — mosques, schools, coinage and the new Janissary corps — wins in the Balkans (Kosovo 1389), and in 1453 Mehmed II storms Constantinople, ends Byzantium and makes the city his capital, Istanbul
