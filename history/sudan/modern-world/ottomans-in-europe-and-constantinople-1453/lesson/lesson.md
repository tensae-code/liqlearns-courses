# Ottomans in Europe and Constantinople 1453

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: اتساع الإمارة العثمانية وفتوحاتها في أوروبا. ## اتساع الإمارة العثمانية وفتوحاتها في أوروبا

Orhan organises the state — mosques, schools, coinage and the new Janissary corps — wins in the Balkans (Kosovo 1389), and in 1453 Mehmed II storms Constantinople, ends Byzantium and makes the city his capital, Istanbul.
