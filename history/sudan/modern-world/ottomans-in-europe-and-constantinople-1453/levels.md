# Ottomans in Europe and Constantinople 1453 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Orhan organises the state — mosques, schools, coinage and the new Janissary corps — wins in the Balkans (Kosovo 1389), and in 1453 Mehmed II storms Constantinople, ends — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
