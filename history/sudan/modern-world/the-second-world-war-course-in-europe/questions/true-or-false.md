# The Second World War Course in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Axis overrun Europe and strike into Russia and Pearl Harbor by 1941; the turn comes at Midway, El Alamein and Stalingrad, Italy falls, Normandy opens the west, Hitler kills himself and Germany surrenders in May 1945. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Course part” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Europe is overrun, then liberated from two directions.
