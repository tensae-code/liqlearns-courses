# The Second World War Course in Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Course part”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** the Axis overrun Europe and strike into Russia and Pearl Harbor by 1941; the turn comes at Midway, El Alamein and Stalingrad, Italy falls, Normandy opens the west, Hitler kills himself and Germany surrenders in May 1945

**2.** Who is in the story of “Course part”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Europe is overrun, then liberated from two directions
