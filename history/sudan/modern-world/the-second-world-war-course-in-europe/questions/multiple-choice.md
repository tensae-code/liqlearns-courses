# The Second World War Course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Course part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The Axis overrun Europe and strike into Russia and Pearl Harbor by 1941; the turn comes at Midway, El Alamein…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Axis overrun Europe and strike into Russia and Pearl Harbor by 1941; the turn comes at Midway, El Alamein…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Course part”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Europe is overrun, then liberated from two directions  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Europe is overrun, then liberated from two directions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
