# Video script — The Second World War Course in Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Axis overrun Europe and strike into Russia and Pearl Harbor by 1941;.

Course part.

The Axis overrun Europe and strike into Russia and Pearl Harbor by 1941; the turn comes at Midway, El Alamein and Stalingrad, Italy falls, Normandy opens the west, Hitler kills himself and Germany surrenders in May 1945.

Course part.

Europe is overrun, then liberated from two directions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
