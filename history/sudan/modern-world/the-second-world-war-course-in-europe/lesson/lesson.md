# The Second World War Course in Europe

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Course part; Course part. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Course part

The Axis overrun Europe and strike into Russia and Pearl Harbor by 1941; the turn comes at Midway, El Alamein and Stalingrad, Italy falls, Normandy opens the west, Hitler kills himself and Germany surrenders in May 1945.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Course part

Europe is overrun, then liberated from two directions.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Axis overrun Europe and strike into Russia and Pearl Harbor by 1941;. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Second World War Course in Europe. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
