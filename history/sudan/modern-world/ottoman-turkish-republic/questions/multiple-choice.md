# Ottoman Turkish Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Abolition of the Caliphate and the Emergence of Secular Turkey”?
a) The office the whole course has followed is abolished by a parliament in Ankara  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The office the whole course has followed is abolished by a parliament in Ankara** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Abolition of the Caliphate and the Emergence of Secular Turkey”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The office the whole course has followed is abolished by a parliament in Ankara  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The office the whole course has followed is abolished by a parliament in Ankara** — That is what this lesson teaches.
