# Sudan After Independence Governments — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Thirty years, three republics, two revolutions and a merry-go-round of soldiers and parties. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Features of Socio-Economic Development in Sudan” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Dams, schemes and debts — development is the other plot of the independence story.
