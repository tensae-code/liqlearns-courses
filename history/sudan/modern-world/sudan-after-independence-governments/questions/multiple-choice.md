# Sudan After Independence Governments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Governments That Succeeded One Another in Sudan 1955–1985”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Thirty years, three republics, two revolutions and a merry-go-round of soldiers and parties  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Thirty years, three republics, two revolutions and a merry-go-round of soldiers and parties** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Features of Socio-Economic Development in Sudan”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Dams, schemes and debts — development is the other plot of the independence story  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Dams, schemes and debts — development is the other plot of the independence story** — That is what this lesson teaches.
