# Ottoman State Begins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “نشأة الدولة العثمانية”?
a) The Qayi Turkmens flee the Mongols into Seljuk Anatolia; Ertugrul wins border land for aiding the Seljuk…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Qayi Turkmens flee the Mongols into Seljuk Anatolia; Ertugrul wins border land for aiding the Seljuk…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “نشأة الدولة العثمانية”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The Qayi Turkmens flee the Mongols into Seljuk Anatolia; Ertugrul wins border land for aiding the Seljuk…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The Qayi Turkmens flee the Mongols into Seljuk Anatolia; Ertugrul wins border land for aiding the Seljuk…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
