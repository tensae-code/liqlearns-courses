# Ottoman State Begins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “نشأة الدولة العثمانية”.
**Answer:** the Qayi Turkmens flee the Mongols into Seljuk Anatolia; Ertugrul wins border land for aiding the Seljuk sultan, and his son Osman (from 1288) plants the emirate at Byzantium's weakest frontier and takes most of Bithynia before his death in 1326
