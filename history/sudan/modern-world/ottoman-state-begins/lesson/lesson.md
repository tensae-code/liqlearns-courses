# Ottoman State Begins

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: نشأة الدولة العثمانية. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## نشأة الدولة العثمانية

The Qayi Turkmens flee the Mongols into Seljuk Anatolia; Ertugrul wins border land for aiding the Seljuk sultan, and his son Osman (from 1288) plants the emirate at Byzantium's weakest frontier and takes most of Bithynia before his death in 1326.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Qayi Turkmens flee the Mongols into Seljuk Anatolia;. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Ottoman State Begins. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
