# The First World War Versailles and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Four empires — Germany, Russia, Austria-Hungary and Ottoman Turkey — fall, Europe's borders are redrawn, some 22 million have died, and the League of Nations is founded in 1920 to keep the peace. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Results part” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The peace settles old empires and plants the next war.
