# The First World War Versailles and the Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Results part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Four empires — Germany, Russia, Austria-Hungary and Ottoman Turkey — fall, Europe's borders are redrawn, some…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Four empires — Germany, Russia, Austria-Hungary and Ottoman Turkey — fall, Europe's borders are redrawn, some…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Results part”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The peace settles old empires and plants the next war  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The peace settles old empires and plants the next war** — That is what this lesson teaches.
