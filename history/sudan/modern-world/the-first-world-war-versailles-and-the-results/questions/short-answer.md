# The First World War Versailles and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Results part”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** four empires — Germany, Russia, Austria-Hungary and Ottoman Turkey — fall, Europe's borders are redrawn, some 22 million have died, and the League of Nations is founded in 1920 to keep the peace

**2.** Who is in the story of “Results part”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the peace settles old empires and plants the next war
