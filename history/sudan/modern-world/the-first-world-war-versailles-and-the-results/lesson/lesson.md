# The First World War Versailles and the Results

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Results part; Results part. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Results part

Four empires — Germany, Russia, Austria-Hungary and Ottoman Turkey — fall, Europe's borders are redrawn, some 22 million have died, and the League of Nations is founded in 1920 to keep the peace.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Results part

The peace settles old empires and plants the next war.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The First World War Versailles and the Results. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
