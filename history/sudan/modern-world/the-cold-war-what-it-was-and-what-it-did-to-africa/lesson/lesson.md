# The Cold War What it Was and What it Did to Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: التطورات العالمية بعد الحرب العالمية الثانية — الحرب الباردة. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## التطورات العالمية بعد الحرب العالمية الثانية — الحرب الباردة

Ideology, containment and the arms race freeze the superpowers into a cold war fought by aid and alliances — Berlin 1948, Korea, Hungary, Suez, Cuba's missiles, Prague, Afghanistan — until Gorbachev's peaceful coexistence, the Malta summit (1989) and Paris (1990) end it and the Soviet Union itself dissolves, leaving America alone on top.
