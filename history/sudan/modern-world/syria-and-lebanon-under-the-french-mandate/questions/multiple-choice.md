# Syria and Lebanon Under the French Mandate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Syria and Lebanon under the French Mandate”?
a) Damascus is bombarded by its "protectors", and the mandate never recovers its legitimacy  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Damascus is bombarded by its "protectors", and the mandate never recovers its legitimacy** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Syria and Lebanon under the French Mandate”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Damascus is bombarded by its "protectors", and the mandate never recovers its legitimacy  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Damascus is bombarded by its "protectors", and the mandate never recovers its legitimacy** — That is what this lesson teaches.
