# The Industrial Revolution Machines and Cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Industrial Revolution in Europe”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Steam remakes work, cities and empires  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Steam remakes work, cities and empires** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Industrial Revolution”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The machine remakes work, the city, and finally the map of other people's continents  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The machine remakes work, the city, and finally the map of other people's continents** — That is what this lesson teaches.
