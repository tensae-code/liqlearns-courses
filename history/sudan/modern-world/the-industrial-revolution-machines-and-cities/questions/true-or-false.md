# The Industrial Revolution Machines and Cities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Steam remakes work, cities and empires. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Industrial Revolution” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The machine remakes work, the city, and finally the map of other people's continents.
