# The Second World War the Pacific and the Results

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Pacific + results part; Pacific and results part. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pacific + results part

Japan fights on until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima (6 August 1945) and Nagasaki (9 August) and it surrenders on 15 August; the war leaves Germany split, the United Nations founded, the colonies stirring, and two blocs facing off — NATO against the Warsaw Pact — with the Non-Aligned Movement (1955, Sudan among its members) refusing both.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pacific and results part

The war ends in the Pacific and a new world order begins.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
