# The Second World War the Pacific and the Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Japan fights on until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima (6 August 1945) and Nagasaki (9 August) and it surrenders on 15 August; the war leaves Germany split, the United Nations founded, the colonies stirring, and two blocs facing off — NATO against the Warsaw Pact — with the Non-Aligned Movement (1955, Sudan among its members) refusing both. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Pacific and results part” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The war ends in the Pacific and a new world order begins.
