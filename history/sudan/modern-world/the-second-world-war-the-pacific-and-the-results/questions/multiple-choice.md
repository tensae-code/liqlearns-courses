# The Second World War the Pacific and the Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Pacific + results part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Japan fights on until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima (6 August 1945) and Nagasaki (9 August) and it…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Japan fights on until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima (6 August 1945) and Nagasaki (9 August) and it…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Pacific and results part”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The war ends in the Pacific and a new world order begins  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The war ends in the Pacific and a new world order begins** — That is what this lesson teaches.
