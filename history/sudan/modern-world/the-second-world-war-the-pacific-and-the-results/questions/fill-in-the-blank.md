# The Second World War the Pacific and the Results — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ fights on until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima (6 August 1945) and Nagasaki (9 August) and it surrenders on 15 August; the war leaves Germany split, the United Nations founded, the colonies stirring, and two blocs facing off — NATO against the Warsaw Pact — with the Non-Aligned Movement (1955, Sudan among its members) refusing both (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Japan

**2.** The war ends in the ____ and a new world order begins (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Pacific
