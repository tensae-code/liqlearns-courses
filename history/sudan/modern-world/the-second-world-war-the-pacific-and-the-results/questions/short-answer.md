# The Second World War the Pacific and the Results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Pacific + results part”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Japan fights on until atomic bombs fall on Hiroshima (6 August 1945) and Nagasaki (9 August) and it surrenders on 15 August; the war leaves Germany split, the United Nations founded, the colonies stirring, and two blocs facing off — NATO against the Warsaw Pact — with the Non-Aligned Movement (1955, Sudan among its members) refusing both

**2.** Who is in the story of “Pacific and results part”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the war ends in the Pacific and a new world order begins
