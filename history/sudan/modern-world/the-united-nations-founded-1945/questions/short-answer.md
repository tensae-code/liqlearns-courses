# The United Nations Founded 1945 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “هيئة الأمم المتحدة”.
**Answer:** out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination and cooperate — General Assembly, a Security Council where five great powers hold the veto, ECOSOC, trusteeship, a Secretariat and the World Court at The Hague, with FAO, UNESCO, the ILO, WHO, the IAEA and UNICEF at work beside it
