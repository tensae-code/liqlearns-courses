# The United Nations Founded 1945 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination and cooperate — General Assembly, a Security Council where five great powers hold the veto, ECOSOC, trusteeship, a Secretariat and the World Court at The Hague, with FAO, UNESCO, the ILO, WHO, the IAEA and UNICEF at work beside it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “هيئة الأمم المتحدة” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination….
