# The United Nations Founded 1945 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “هيئة الأمم المتحدة”?
a) Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “هيئة الأمم المتحدة”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
