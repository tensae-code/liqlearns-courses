# The United Nations Founded 1945

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: هيئة الأمم المتحدة. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## هيئة الأمم المتحدة

Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination and cooperate — General Assembly, a Security Council where five great powers hold the veto, ECOSOC, trusteeship, a Secretariat and the World Court at The Hague, with FAO, UNESCO, the ILO, WHO, the IAEA and UNICEF at work beside it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination and cooperate — General Assembly, a Security Council where. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The United Nations Founded 1945. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Out of the war the world builds the United Nations (1945, New York) to keep peace, honour self-determination and cooperate — General Assembly, a Security Council where.
