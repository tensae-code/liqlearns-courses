# Arab World Under European Mandates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Subjection of the Arab Lands to European Colonialism 1920–1945”?
a) Promised independence in the war, the Arabs receive French and British officials instead  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Promised independence in the war, the Arabs receive French and British officials instead** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Subjection of the Arab Lands to European Colonialism 1920–1945”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Promised independence in the war, the Arabs receive French and British officials instead  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Promised independence in the war, the Arabs receive French and British officials instead** — That is what this lesson teaches.
