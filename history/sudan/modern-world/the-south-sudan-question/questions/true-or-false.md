# The South Sudan Question — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The question the empire left behind outlives every government that tries to answer it. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “British Policy in South Sudan 1899–1955” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The empire governs one country as two, and the division outlives the empire.
