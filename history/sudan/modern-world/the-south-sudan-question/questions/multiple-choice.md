# The South Sudan Question — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Development of the South Sudan Problem 1955–1985”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The question the empire left behind outlives every government that tries to answer it  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The question the empire left behind outlives every government that tries to answer it** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “British Policy in South Sudan 1899–1955”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The empire governs one country as two, and the division outlives the empire  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The empire governs one country as two, and the division outlives the empire** — That is what this lesson teaches.
