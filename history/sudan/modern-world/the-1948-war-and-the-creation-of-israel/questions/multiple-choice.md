# The 1948 War and the Creation of Israel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The 1948 War and the Establishment of the State of Israel”?
a) The mandate ends in war, a new state is proclaimed, and three-quarters of a million Palestinians leave their…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The mandate ends in war, a new state is proclaimed, and three-quarters of a million Palestinians leave their…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The 1948 War and the Establishment of the State of Israel”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The mandate ends in war, a new state is proclaimed, and three-quarters of a million Palestinians leave their…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
