# The 1948 War and the Creation of Israel

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The 1948 War and the Establishment of the State of Israel. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The 1948 War and the Establishment of the State of Israel

The mandate ends in war, a new state is proclaimed, and three-quarters of a million Palestinians leave their homes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The mandate ends in war, a new state is proclaimed, and three-quarters of a million Palestinians leave their homes. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The 1948 War and the Creation of Israel. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
