# The 1948 War and the Creation of Israel — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The mandate ends in war, a new state is proclaimed, and three-quarters of a million Palestinians leave their homes — in their own words, give one real example from the story, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
