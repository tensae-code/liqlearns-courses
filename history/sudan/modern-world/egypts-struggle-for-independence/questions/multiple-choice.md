# Egypts Struggle for Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Egyptian Liberation Movements”?
a) The neighbour whose rule Sudan escaped now fights the same empire for its own freedom  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The neighbour whose rule Sudan escaped now fights the same empire for its own freedom** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Egyptian Liberation Movements”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The neighbour whose rule Sudan escaped now fights the same empire for its own freedom  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The neighbour whose rule Sudan escaped now fights the same empire for its own freedom** — That is what this lesson teaches.
