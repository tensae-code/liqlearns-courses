# Saudi Arabia Unification Under Abdulaziz — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Political Developments in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the Hijaz”?
a) The peninsula across the Red Sea becomes a single state in Sudan's students' grandparents' lifetime  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
