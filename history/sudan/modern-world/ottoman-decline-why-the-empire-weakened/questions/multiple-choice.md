# Ottoman Decline Why the Empire Weakened — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “تدهور الدولة العثمانية”?
a) The Janissaries marry, inherit their posts and meddle in politics; sultans weaken, governors farm the…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Janissaries marry, inherit their posts and meddle in politics; sultans weaken, governors farm the…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “تدهور الدولة العثمانية”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The Janissaries marry, inherit their posts and meddle in politics; sultans weaken, governors farm the…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The Janissaries marry, inherit their posts and meddle in politics; sultans weaken, governors farm the…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
