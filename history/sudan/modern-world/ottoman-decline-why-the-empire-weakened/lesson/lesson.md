# Ottoman Decline Why the Empire Weakened

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: تدهور الدولة العثمانية. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## تدهور الدولة العثمانية

The Janissaries marry, inherit their posts and meddle in politics; sultans weaken, governors farm the provinces to the highest bidder and squeeze the taxpayers — the great state rots from inside.
