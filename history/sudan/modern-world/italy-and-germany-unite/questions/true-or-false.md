# Italy and Germany Unite — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A peninsula of states becomes one kingdom. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “German Unity” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Prussia welds the German states into an empire that will shake the world.
