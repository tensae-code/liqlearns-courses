# Italy and Germany Unite — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Italian Unity”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** a peninsula of states becomes one kingdom

**2.** Who is in the story of “German Unity”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Prussia welds the German states into an empire that will shake the world
