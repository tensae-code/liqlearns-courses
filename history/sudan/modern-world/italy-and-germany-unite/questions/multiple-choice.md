# Italy and Germany Unite — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Italian Unity”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) A peninsula of states becomes one kingdom  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A peninsula of states becomes one kingdom** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “German Unity”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Prussia welds the German states into an empire that will shake the world  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Prussia welds the German states into an empire that will shake the world** — That is what this lesson teaches.
