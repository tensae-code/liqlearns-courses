# The First World War Course of the War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Triple Alliance holds the edge from 1914 to 1917; then revolution takes Russia out and America comes in, the balance tips and Germany surrenders. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “Course part” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Four years of trenches redraw the world's map.
