# The First World War Course of the War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Course part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The Triple Alliance holds the edge from 1914 to 1917; then revolution takes Russia out and America comes in,…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The Triple Alliance holds the edge from 1914 to 1917; then revolution takes Russia out and America comes in,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Course part”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Four years of trenches redraw the world's map  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Four years of trenches redraw the world's map** — That is what this lesson teaches.
