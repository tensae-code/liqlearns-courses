# History — Sudan course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Sudan inside the History folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path follows Sudan's own books' progression as filed — the placements file's book order is the teaching order, earliest books first — grouped into levels by the turns of Sudan's own story as its books present them: first what history is and how it is dated; then Sudan and Africa to 1500, with Sudan's own soil as the spine; then Arabia and the Islamic caliphates with the ancient world around them; then Sudan from the sultanates through foreign rule to independence, with Africa's colonisation and liberation beside it; and last the modern world — Ottomans, revolutions, world wars, the Cold War and the Arab liberation century. A lesson sits in the title folder the placements file filed it under, and its parts run in file order inside the lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — Sudan's own History and History and National Education books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — First Steps: What History Is and How We Date It** — Sudan's books open with the method itself: what history is — the story of people's life on earth in the past, made by our ancestors and continued by us — the many names this land carried before it was called Sudan, and the two calendars, Gregorian and Hijri, that hang side by side in Sudanese life as clocks for reading the past. 2 lessons, 3 placements.
2. **Level 2 — Sudan and Africa to 1500** — The long ancient story, Sudan first: Stone-Age peoples on the Nile 12,000 years ago, Kerma, Kush and Meroe, the Christian Nubian kingdoms, Darfur's stone cities; then Africa around it — Egypt, Axum, Carthage — and the two faiths arriving: Christianity, then Islam, which enters Sudan by treaty and migration, remakes the land in Arabic, and raises the Funj, Abdallab, Taqali and Fur sultanates, with the Islamic kingdoms of the Maghreb, Mali and the Swahili coast beside them. 30 lessons, 56 placements.
3. **Level 3 — Arabia, the Islamic Caliphates and the Ancient World** — Arabia before Islam — its southern and northern kingdoms and its tribal society — then the Islamic state age by age: the Prophet's state, the four Rashidun caliphs one by one and the problems and administration of their state, the Umayyads and their conquests east to China's edge and west into al-Andalus, the Abbasids and Baghdad's fall to the Mongols, and the civilisation that flowers between; around it, Europe in its dark ages learning from Islam, the Renaissance, Greece and Rome, and the Ottoman state that grows from the medieval world. 21 lessons, 55 placements.
4. **Level 4 — Sudan from the Sultanates to Independence, and Africa After 1500** — Sudan's own modern arc whole: the Islamic sultanates, the Turco-Egyptian conquest of 1821 and sixty years of rule and resistance, Gordon and the road to the Mahdiyya, the Mahdist revolution and state, the Anglo-Egyptian Condominium, the national movement, and independence in 1956 with the army years after it; beside it, Africa's colonisation — its motives, its kinds, the Berlin Conference, its effects — Usman dan Fodio's Sokoto Caliphate, the Congo from Leopold to Lumumba, decolonisation, and the founding of the OAU. 30 lessons, 51 placements.
5. **Level 5 — The Modern World: Ottomans, World Wars and the Arab Liberation Century** — The wider modern world as Sudan's books teach it: the Ottoman state from Osman to its decline, the caliphate's abolition and secular Turkey, reform movements in the Islamic world; Europe's revolutions and machines — France 1789, the Industrial Revolution, Italy and Germany uniting; the two world wars, the United Nations founded in 1945, and the Cold War; Sudan after independence — its governments and the South Sudan question; and the Arab world's liberation century — the mandates, Egypt, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Syria and Lebanon, the Maghreb, and Palestine through the 1948 war. 30 lessons, 42 placements.

**Totals: 113 lessons, 207 placements** (Level 1: 2 lessons; Level 2: 30 lessons; Level 3: 21 lessons; Level 4: 30 lessons; Level 5: 30 lessons).
