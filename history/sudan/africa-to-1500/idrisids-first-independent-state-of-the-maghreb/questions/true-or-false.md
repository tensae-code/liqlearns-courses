# Idrisids First Independent State of the Maghreb — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A refugee prince with nothing but his lineage builds the Maghreb's first state of its own.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Idrisid State in Morocco (172–375هـ / 788–985م)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A refugee prince with nothing but his lineage builds the Maghreb's first state of its own.
