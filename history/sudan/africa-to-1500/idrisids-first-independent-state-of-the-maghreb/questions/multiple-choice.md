Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Idrisid State in Morocco (172–375هـ / 788–985م)”?
a) A refugee prince with nothing but his lineage builds the Maghreb's first state of its own  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
