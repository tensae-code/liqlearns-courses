# Axum Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Axum part; The Abyssinian Civilisation of Axum. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Axum part

Yemeni migrants found Axum, which at its height (2nd–7th centuries) reaches from Nubia to the Red Sea and Somalia, trading with the world — and it is Axum's Ezana who ends Meroe.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Abyssinian Civilisation of Axum

A kingdom planted by sea-migrants rules both shores' trade and grows strong enough to end Meroe.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
