# Axum Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Axum part”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Yemeni migrants found Axum, which at its height (2nd–7th centuries) reaches from Nubia to the Red Sea and…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Yemeni migrants found Axum, which at its height (2nd–7th centuries) reaches from Nubia to the Red Sea and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Abyssinian Civilisation of Axum”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A kingdom planted by sea-migrants rules both shores' trade and grows strong enough to end Meroe  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A kingdom planted by sea-migrants rules both shores' trade and grows strong enough to end Meroe** — That is what this lesson teaches.
