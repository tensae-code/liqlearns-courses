# Axum Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ migrants found Axum, which at its height (2nd–7th centuries) reaches from Nubia to the Red Sea and Somalia, trading with the world — and it is Axum's Ezana who ends Meroe (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Yemeni

**2.** A kingdom planted by sea-migrants rules both shores' trade and grows strong enough to end ____ (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Meroe
