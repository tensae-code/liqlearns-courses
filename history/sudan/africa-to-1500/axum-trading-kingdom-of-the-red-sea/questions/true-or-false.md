# Axum Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Yemeni migrants found Axum, which at its height (2nd–7th centuries) reaches from Nubia to the Red Sea and Somalia, trading with the world — and it is Axum's Ezana who ends Meroe. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Abyssinian Civilisation of Axum” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A kingdom planted by sea-migrants rules both shores' trade and grows strong enough to end Meroe.
