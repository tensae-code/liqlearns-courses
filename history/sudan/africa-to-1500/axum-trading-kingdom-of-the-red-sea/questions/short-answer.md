# Axum Trading Kingdom of the Red Sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Axum part”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Yemeni migrants found Axum, which at its height (2nd–7th centuries) reaches from Nubia to the Red Sea and Somalia, trading with the world — and it is Axum's Ezana who ends Meroe

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Abyssinian Civilisation of Axum”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a kingdom planted by sea-migrants rules both shores' trade and grows strong enough to end Meroe
