# Islamic Civilisation in Africas Kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Features of Islamic Civilisation in the African Kingdoms”?
a) Islam in Africa is not a veneer but a working civilisation — courts, universities, cities and markets  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Islam in Africa is not a veneer but a working civilisation — courts, universities, cities and markets** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Features of Islamic Civilisation in the African Kingdoms”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Islam in Africa is not a veneer but a working civilisation — courts, universities, cities and markets  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Islam in Africa is not a veneer but a working civilisation — courts, universities, cities and markets** — That is what this lesson teaches.
