# Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Egypt part. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Egypt part

In the northern Nile valley the pharaohs raise the pyramids and Abu Simbel, and their medicine and building outlive them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In the northern Nile valley the pharaohs raise the pyramids and Abu Simbel, and their medicine and building outlive them. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson's story echoes in real life. Note it.
