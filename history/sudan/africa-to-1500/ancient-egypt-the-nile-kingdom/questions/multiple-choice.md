# Ancient Egypt the Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Egypt part”?
a) In the northern Nile valley the pharaohs raise the pyramids and Abu Simbel, and their medicine and building…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In the northern Nile valley the pharaohs raise the pyramids and Abu Simbel, and their medicine and building…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Egypt part”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) In the northern Nile valley the pharaohs raise the pyramids and Abu Simbel, and their medicine and building…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) In the northern Nile valley the pharaohs raise the pyramids and Abu Simbel, and their medicine and building…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
