# Islam Reaches West Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Islam crosses Africa less by the sword than by the caravan, the market and the teacher. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Spread of Islam in Africa” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Caravan by caravan, the map of Africa changes religion.
