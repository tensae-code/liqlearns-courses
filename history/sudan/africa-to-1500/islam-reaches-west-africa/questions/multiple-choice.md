# Islam Reaches West Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Routes of Islam's Spread in Africa, and The Factors and Means of Its Spread”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Islam crosses Africa less by the sword than by the caravan, the market and the teacher  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Islam crosses Africa less by the sword than by the caravan, the market and the teacher** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Spread of Islam in Africa”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Caravan by caravan, the map of Africa changes religion  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Caravan by caravan, the map of Africa changes religion** — That is what this lesson teaches.
