# Egyptian Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Models of the Ancient Civilisations of Africa (Egypt part)”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The northern neighbour's civilisation is taught as the first of Africa's own models  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The northern neighbour's civilisation is taught as the first of Africa's own models** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Ancient Egyptian Civilisation”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The northern Nile kingdom becomes the measure of what a civilisation is  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The northern Nile kingdom becomes the measure of what a civilisation is** — That is what this lesson teaches.
