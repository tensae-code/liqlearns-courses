# Egyptian Civilisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The northern neighbour's civilisation is taught as the first of Africa's own models. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Ancient Egyptian Civilisation” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The northern Nile kingdom becomes the measure of what a civilisation is.
