# Arab Migration Into Sudan

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Migration of the Arabs into Sudan. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Migration of the Arabs into Sudan

Sudan is Islamised by migration and treaty over centuries, not taken in a single invasion.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Sudan is Islamised by migration and treaty over centuries, not taken in a single invasion. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Arab Migration Into Sudan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
