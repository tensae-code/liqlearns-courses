# Arab Migration Into Sudan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Migration of the Arabs into Sudan”?
a) Sudan is Islamised by migration and treaty over centuries, not taken in a single invasion  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Sudan is Islamised by migration and treaty over centuries, not taken in a single invasion** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Migration of the Arabs into Sudan”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Sudan is Islamised by migration and treaty over centuries, not taken in a single invasion  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Sudan is Islamised by migration and treaty over centuries, not taken in a single invasion** — That is what this lesson teaches.
