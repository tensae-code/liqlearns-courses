# Islam in Africa South of the Sahara — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The spread of Islam in Africa”?
a) Arabs and Africans had traded since the oldest times, and along those same roads and sea routes Islam travels…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
