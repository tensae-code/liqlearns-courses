# Darfur Stone Cities of the Tunjur — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The stone-city civilisation of Darfur”?
a) In the west the Tunjur sultans raise Uri, a walled city built entirely of stone — palaces, round stone huts,…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) In the west the Tunjur sultans raise Uri, a walled city built entirely of stone — palaces, round stone huts,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The stone-city civilisation of Darfur”?

**Answer: b) In the west the Tunjur sultans raise Uri, a walled city built entirely of stone — palaces, round stone huts,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
