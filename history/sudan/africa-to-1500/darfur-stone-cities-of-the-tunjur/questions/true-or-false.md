# Darfur Stone Cities of the Tunjur — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the west the Tunjur sultans raise Uri, a walled city built entirely of stone — palaces, round stone huts, great gates, wells and paved roads.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The stone-city civilisation of Darfur” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: In the west the Tunjur sultans raise Uri, a walled city built entirely of stone — palaces, round stone huts,….
