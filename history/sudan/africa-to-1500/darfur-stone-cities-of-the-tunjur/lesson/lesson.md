# Darfur Stone Cities of the Tunjur

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The stone-city civilisation of Darfur. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The stone-city civilisation of Darfur

In the west the Tunjur sultans raise Uri, a walled city built entirely of stone — palaces, round stone huts, great gates, wells and paved roads.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In the west the Tunjur sultans raise Uri, a walled city built entirely of stone — palaces, round stone huts, great gates, wells and paved roads. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Darfur Stone Cities of the Tunjur. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
