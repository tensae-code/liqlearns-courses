# Kush and Meroe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Kush civilisation”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** from Napata the Kushite kings — Piankhy, who conquers Egypt, and his grandson Taharqa — rule from the first cataract to the sixth; then the capital moves to Meroe, city of pyramids, iron and its own Meroitic tongue, until Axum destroys it in 350 CE

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Kush Civilisation”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** the kings of Kush turn conqueror, wear Egypt's crown, and leave more pyramids than Egypt itself
