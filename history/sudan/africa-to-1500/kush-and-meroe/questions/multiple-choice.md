# Kush and Meroe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Kush civilisation”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) From Napata the Kushite kings — Piankhy, who conquers Egypt, and his grandson Taharqa — rule from the first…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) From Napata the Kushite kings — Piankhy, who conquers Egypt, and his grandson Taharqa — rule from the first…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Kush Civilisation”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The kings of Kush turn conqueror, wear Egypt's crown, and leave more pyramids than Egypt itself  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The kings of Kush turn conqueror, wear Egypt's crown, and leave more pyramids than Egypt itself** — That is what this lesson teaches.
