# Kush and Meroe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From Napata the Kushite kings — Piankhy, who conquers Egypt, and his grandson Taharqa — rule from the first cataract to the sixth; then the capital moves to Meroe, city of pyramids, iron and its own Meroitic tongue, until Axum destroys it in 350 CE. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Kush Civilisation” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: The kings of Kush turn conqueror, wear Egypt's crown, and leave more pyramids than Egypt itself.
