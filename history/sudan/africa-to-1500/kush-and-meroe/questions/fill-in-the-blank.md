# Kush and Meroe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** From ____ the Kushite kings — Piankhy, who conquers Egypt, and his grandson Taharqa — rule from the first cataract to the sixth; then the capital moves to Meroe, city of pyramids, iron and its own Meroitic tongue, until Axum destroys it in 350 CE (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Napata

**2.** The kings of ____ turn conqueror, wear Egypt's crown, and leave more pyramids than Egypt itself (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Kush
