# Fatimids from Tunisia to Cairo

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Fatimid State (297–567هـ / 909–1171م). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Fatimid State (297–567هـ / 909–1171م)

A hidden imam's missionaries build the only caliphate that ever ruled Egypt — and its mosque-university teaches still.
