# Fatimids from Tunisia to Cairo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Fatimid State (297–567هـ / 909–1171م)”?
a) A hidden imam's missionaries build the only caliphate that ever ruled Egypt — and its mosque-university…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A hidden imam's missionaries build the only caliphate that ever ruled Egypt — and its mosque-university…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Fatimid State (297–567هـ / 909–1171م)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A hidden imam's missionaries build the only caliphate that ever ruled Egypt — and its mosque-university…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A hidden imam's missionaries build the only caliphate that ever ruled Egypt — and its mosque-university…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
