# Islam and Arabic Remake Sudan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Results of the Muslim Arabs' Entry into Sudan”?
a) Faith, language and blood mix until "Sudanese" itself is a new thing made by the meeting  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Faith, language and blood mix until "Sudanese" itself is a new thing made by the meeting** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Results of the Muslim Arabs' Entry into Sudan”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Faith, language and blood mix until "Sudanese" itself is a new thing made by the meeting  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Faith, language and blood mix until "Sudanese" itself is a new thing made by the meeting** — That is what this lesson teaches.
