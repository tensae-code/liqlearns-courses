## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Kerma civilisation** — Beside today's Dongola the Nubians build an independent kingdom (2500–1500 BCE) reaching Aswan, with a palace-and-temple city of mud and burnt brick…
2. **The Kerma Civilisation** — Africa's first great kingdom south of Egypt builds palaces and temples of brick and defies the pharaohs
3. **Sudan in the Era of the Cultural Groups** — Entire nations survive as pottery in graves, and science gives them letters because their names are lost
4. **The Kerma Civilisation** — The lettered peoples give way to a named kingdom with a capital of brick
