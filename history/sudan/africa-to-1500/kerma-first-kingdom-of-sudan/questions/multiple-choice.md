# Kerma First Kingdom of Sudan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Kerma civilisation”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Beside today's Dongola the Nubians build an independent kingdom (2500–1500 BCE) reaching Aswan, with a…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Beside today's Dongola the Nubians build an independent kingdom (2500–1500 BCE) reaching Aswan, with a…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Kerma Civilisation”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Africa's first great kingdom south of Egypt builds palaces and temples of brick and defies the pharaohs  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Africa's first great kingdom south of Egypt builds palaces and temples of brick and defies the pharaohs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
