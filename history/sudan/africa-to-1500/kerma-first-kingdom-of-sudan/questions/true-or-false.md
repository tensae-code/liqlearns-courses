# Kerma First Kingdom of Sudan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Beside today's Dongola the Nubians build an independent kingdom (2500–1500 BCE) reaching Aswan, with a palace-and-temple city of mud and burnt brick and royal tombs at the Deffufa. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Kerma Civilisation” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Africa's first great kingdom south of Egypt builds palaces and temples of brick and defies the pharaohs.
