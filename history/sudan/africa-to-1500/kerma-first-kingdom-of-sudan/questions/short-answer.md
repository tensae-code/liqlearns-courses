# Kerma First Kingdom of Sudan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Kerma civilisation”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** beside today's Dongola the Nubians build an independent kingdom (2500–1500 BCE) reaching Aswan, with a palace-and-temple city of mud and burnt brick and royal tombs at the Deffufa

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Kerma Civilisation”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Africa's first great kingdom south of Egypt builds palaces and temples of brick and defies the pharaohs
