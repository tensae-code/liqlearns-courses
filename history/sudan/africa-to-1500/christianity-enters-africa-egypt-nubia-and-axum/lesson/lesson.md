# Christianity Enters Africa Egypt Nubia and Axum

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Christianity enters Africa; The Entry of Christianity into Africa. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Christianity enters Africa

St Mark brings the faith to Egypt in the first century, trade carries it to Abyssinia where King Ezana makes it his state's religion, and Nubia's kingdoms receive it too.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Entry of Christianity into Africa

A persecuted faith survives in the desert, then rides back down the Nile as the religion of kings.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.
