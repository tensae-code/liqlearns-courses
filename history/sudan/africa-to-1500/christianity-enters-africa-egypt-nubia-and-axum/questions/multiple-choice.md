# Christianity Enters Africa Egypt Nubia and Axum — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Christianity enters Africa”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) St Mark brings the faith to Egypt in the first century, trade carries it to Abyssinia where King Ezana makes…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) St Mark brings the faith to Egypt in the first century, trade carries it to Abyssinia where King Ezana makes…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Entry of Christianity into Africa”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A persecuted faith survives in the desert, then rides back down the Nile as the religion of kings  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A persecuted faith survives in the desert, then rides back down the Nile as the religion of kings** — That is what this lesson teaches.
