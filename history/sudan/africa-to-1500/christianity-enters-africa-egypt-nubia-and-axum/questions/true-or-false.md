# Christianity Enters Africa Egypt Nubia and Axum — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** St Mark brings the faith to Egypt in the first century, trade carries it to Abyssinia where King Ezana makes it his state's religion, and Nubia's kingdoms receive it too. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Entry of Christianity into Africa” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A persecuted faith survives in the desert, then rides back down the Nile as the religion of kings.
