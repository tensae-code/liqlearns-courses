# Christianity Enters Africa Egypt Nubia and Axum — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Christianity enters Africa”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** St Mark brings the faith to Egypt in the first century, trade carries it to Abyssinia where King Ezana makes it his state's religion, and Nubia's kingdoms receive it too

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Entry of Christianity into Africa”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a persecuted faith survives in the desert, then rides back down the Nile as the religion of kings
