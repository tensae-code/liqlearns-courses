# Africas Ancient Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Peoples part”?
a) Bushmen and Pygmies hunting and painting on rocks, Hottentots, Bantu farmers, and the Swahili people born…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Bushmen and Pygmies hunting and painting on rocks, Hottentots, Bantu farmers, and the Swahili people born…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “Peoples part”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Bushmen and Pygmies hunting and painting on rocks, Hottentots, Bantu farmers, and the Swahili people born…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Bushmen and Pygmies hunting and painting on rocks, Hottentots, Bantu farmers, and the Swahili people born…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
