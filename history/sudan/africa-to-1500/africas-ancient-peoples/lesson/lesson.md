# Africas Ancient Peoples

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Peoples part. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Peoples part

Bushmen and Pygmies hunting and painting on rocks, Hottentots, Bantu farmers, and the Swahili people born where Arabs met Africans on the coast.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Bushmen and Pygmies hunting and painting on rocks, Hottentots, Bantu farmers, and the Swahili people born where Arabs met Africans on the coast. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Africas Ancient Peoples. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
