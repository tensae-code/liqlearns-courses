# Islam on the Swahili Coast — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Islam on the East African Coast”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Persian and Arab sea-migrants build a Swahili Muslim civilisation of stone towns that rules the coast for five hundred years

**2.** Who is in the story of “A Model of the Islamic Kingdoms in East Africa”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** a sea-borne sultanate grows rich on gold it never mines, ruling a thousand miles of coast from one island city
