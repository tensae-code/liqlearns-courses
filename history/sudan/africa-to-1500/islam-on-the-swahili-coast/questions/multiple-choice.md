# Islam on the Swahili Coast — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Islam on the East African Coast”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Persian and Arab sea-migrants build a Swahili Muslim civilisation of stone towns that rules the coast for…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Persian and Arab sea-migrants build a Swahili Muslim civilisation of stone towns that rules the coast for…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “A Model of the Islamic Kingdoms in East Africa”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A sea-borne sultanate grows rich on gold it never mines, ruling a thousand miles of coast from one island city  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A sea-borne sultanate grows rich on gold it never mines, ruling a thousand miles of coast from one island city** — That is what this lesson teaches.
