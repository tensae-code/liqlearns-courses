# Islam on the Swahili Coast — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Persian and Arab sea-migrants build a Swahili Muslim civilisation of stone towns that rules the coast for five hundred years. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “A Model of the Islamic Kingdoms in East Africa” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A sea-borne sultanate grows rich on gold it never mines, ruling a thousand miles of coast from one island city.
