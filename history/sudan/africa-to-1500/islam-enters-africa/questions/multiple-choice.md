# Islam Enters Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Spread of Islam in Africa”?
a) A faith arrives by refuge, by caravan and by sea, and finds a continent ready to argue with it on its own…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A faith arrives by refuge, by caravan and by sea, and finds a continent ready to argue with it on its own…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Spread of Islam in Africa”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A faith arrives by refuge, by caravan and by sea, and finds a continent ready to argue with it on its own…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A faith arrives by refuge, by caravan and by sea, and finds a continent ready to argue with it on its own…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
