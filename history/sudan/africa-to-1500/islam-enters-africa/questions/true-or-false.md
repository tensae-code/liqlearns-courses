# Islam Enters Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A faith arrives by refuge, by caravan and by sea, and finds a continent ready to argue with it on its own terms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Spread of Islam in Africa” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A faith arrives by refuge, by caravan and by sea, and finds a continent ready to argue with it on its own….
