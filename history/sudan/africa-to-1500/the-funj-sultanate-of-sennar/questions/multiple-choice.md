# The Funj Sultanate of Sennar — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (1)”?
a) Three centuries of one Sudanese state, taken apart piece by piece so the child sees exactly how it worked and…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Three centuries of one Sudanese state, taken apart piece by piece so the child sees exactly how it worked and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (1)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Three centuries of one Sudanese state, taken apart piece by piece so the child sees exactly how it worked and…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Three centuries of one Sudanese state, taken apart piece by piece so the child sees exactly how it worked and…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
