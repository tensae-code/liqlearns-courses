# The Baqt Treaty Nubia and the Muslims — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Treaties between the Muslim Arabs, Nubia and the Beja”?
a) A treaty so well balanced that it holds the Nile frontier for six hundred years  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) A treaty so well balanced that it holds the Nile frontier for six hundred years** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Treaties between the Muslim Arabs, Nubia and the Beja”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) A treaty so well balanced that it holds the Nile frontier for six hundred years  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) A treaty so well balanced that it holds the Nile frontier for six hundred years** — That is what this lesson teaches.
