# The Abdallab Sheikhdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (2)”?
a) The junior partner of the Funj alliance outlives its strength and meets the invader first  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The junior partner of the Funj alliance outlives its strength and meets the invader first** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (2)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) The junior partner of the Funj alliance outlives its strength and meets the invader first  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) The junior partner of the Funj alliance outlives its strength and meets the invader first** — That is what this lesson teaches.
