# The Abdallab Sheikhdom

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (2). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (2)

The junior partner of the Funj alliance outlives its strength and meets the invader first.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The junior partner of the Funj alliance outlives its strength and meets the invader first. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Abdallab Sheikhdom. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
