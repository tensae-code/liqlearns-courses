# Christian Nubia Nobadia Makuria Alodia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Christian Nubian kingdoms”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) After Meroe falls the Nubians split into three Christian kingdoms — Nobadia at Faras, Makuria at Old Dongola,…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) After Meroe falls the Nubians split into three Christian kingdoms — Nobadia at Faras, Makuria at Old Dongola,…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Christian Nubian Kingdoms”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) For nearly a thousand years the middle Nile is a land of churches, kings and painted saints  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) For nearly a thousand years the middle Nile is a land of churches, kings and painted saints** — That is what this lesson teaches.
