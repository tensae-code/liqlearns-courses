# Christian Nubia Nobadia Makuria Alodia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “The Christian Nubian kingdoms”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** after Meroe falls the Nubians split into three Christian kingdoms — Nobadia at Faras, Makuria at Old Dongola, Alodia at Soba — building churches and living at peace with their neighbours until Islam arrives

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Christian Nubian Kingdoms”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** for nearly a thousand years the middle Nile is a land of churches, kings and painted saints
