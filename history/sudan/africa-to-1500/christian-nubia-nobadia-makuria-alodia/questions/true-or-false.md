# Christian Nubia Nobadia Makuria Alodia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After Meroe falls the Nubians split into three Christian kingdoms — Nobadia at Faras, Makuria at Old Dongola, Alodia at Soba — building churches and living at peace with their neighbours until Islam arrives. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Christian Nubian Kingdoms” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: For nearly a thousand years the middle Nile is a land of churches, kings and painted saints.
