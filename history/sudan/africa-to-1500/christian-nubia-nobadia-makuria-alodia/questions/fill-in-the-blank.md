# Christian Nubia Nobadia Makuria Alodia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** After ____ falls the Nubians split into three Christian kingdoms — Nobadia at Faras, Makuria at Old Dongola, Alodia at Soba — building churches and living at peace with their neighbours until Islam arrives (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Meroe

**2.** For nearly a thousand years the middle ____ is a land of churches, kings and painted saints (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Nile
