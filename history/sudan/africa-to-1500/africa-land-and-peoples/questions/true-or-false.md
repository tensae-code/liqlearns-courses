# Africa Land and Peoples — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The lesson starts by telling African children that Africa is not the edge of the story but its beginning. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Inhabitants of the African Continent” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: One continent, hundreds of peoples, and every one of them with a past.
