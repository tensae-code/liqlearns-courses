# Africa Land and Peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Africa and the Africans, and Africa's Location and Its Inhabitants”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The lesson starts by telling African children that Africa is not the edge of the story but its beginning  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) The lesson starts by telling African children that Africa is not the edge of the story but its beginning** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Inhabitants of the African Continent”? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) One continent, hundreds of peoples, and every one of them with a past  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) One continent, hundreds of peoples, and every one of them with a past** — That is what this lesson teaches.
