# West African Empires Mali and Mansa Musa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A gold empire so rich its king's pilgrimage bends the price of gold in Cairo.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Empire of Mali” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: A gold empire so rich its king's pilgrimage bends the price of gold in Cairo.
