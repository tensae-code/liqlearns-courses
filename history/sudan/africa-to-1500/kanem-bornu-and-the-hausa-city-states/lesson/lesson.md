# Kanem Bornu and the Hausa City States

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Hausa Kingdoms. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Hausa Kingdoms

City-states of craftsmen and merchants take a new faith from the scholars on the caravan road, slowly, over five hundred years.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: City-states of craftsmen and merchants take a new faith from the scholars on the caravan road, slowly, over five hundred years. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Kanem Bornu and the Hausa City States. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: City-states of craftsmen and merchants take a new faith from the scholars on the caravan road, slowly, over five hundred years.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson's story echoes in real life. Note it.
