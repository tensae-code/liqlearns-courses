# Kanem Bornu and the Hausa City States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Hausa Kingdoms”?
a) City-states of craftsmen and merchants take a new faith from the scholars on the caravan road, slowly, over…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) City-states of craftsmen and merchants take a new faith from the scholars on the caravan road, slowly, over…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Hausa Kingdoms”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) City-states of craftsmen and merchants take a new faith from the scholars on the caravan road, slowly, over…  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) City-states of craftsmen and merchants take a new faith from the scholars on the caravan road, slowly, over…** — That is what this lesson teaches.
