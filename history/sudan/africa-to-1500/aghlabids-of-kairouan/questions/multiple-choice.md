# Aghlabids of Kairouan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Aghlabid State (184–296هـ / 800–909م)”?
a) An Abbasid governorship quietly becomes a kingdom, and its capital becomes one of Islam's great cities  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) An Abbasid governorship quietly becomes a kingdom, and its capital becomes one of Islam's great cities** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Aghlabid State (184–296هـ / 800–909م)”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) An Abbasid governorship quietly becomes a kingdom, and its capital becomes one of Islam's great cities  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) An Abbasid governorship quietly becomes a kingdom, and its capital becomes one of Islam's great cities** — That is what this lesson teaches.
