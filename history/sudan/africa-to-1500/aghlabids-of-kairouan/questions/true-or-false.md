# Aghlabids of Kairouan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An Abbasid governorship quietly becomes a kingdom, and its capital becomes one of Islam's great cities.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Aghlabid State (184–296هـ / 800–909م)” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: An Abbasid governorship quietly becomes a kingdom, and its capital becomes one of Islam's great cities.
