# Aghlabids of Kairouan

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Aghlabid State (184–296هـ / 800–909م). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Aghlabid State (184–296هـ / 800–909م)

An Abbasid governorship quietly becomes a kingdom, and its capital becomes one of Islam's great cities.

## Say it to your companion

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Aghlabids of Kairouan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
