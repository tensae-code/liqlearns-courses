# Islam Enters Sudan Dongola and the Baqt

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Islam enters Sudan; The Islamic Conquest of the Land of Sudan and The Entry of the Arabs into Sudan; Islam in Sudan. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Islam enters Sudan

Once Egypt is opened, Arab Muslims reach Nubia; Old Dongola's church becomes a mosque as Islam settles among the Nubians by mixing and teaching.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Islamic Conquest of the Land of Sudan and The Entry of the Arabs into Sudan

Islam comes to Sudan twice — first by treaty at the frontier, then slowly, family by family, with the migrating tribes.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — Islam in Sudan

Sudan becomes Muslim not in a battle but over centuries of marriage, trade and teaching.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — this is where the lesson's story lands.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Once Egypt is opened, Arab Muslims reach Nubia;. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Islam Enters Sudan Dongola and the Baqt. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
