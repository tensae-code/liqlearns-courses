# Islam Enters Sudan Dongola and the Baqt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Islam enters Sudan”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Once Egypt is opened, Arab Muslims reach Nubia; Old Dongola's church becomes a mosque as Islam settles among…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Once Egypt is opened, Arab Muslims reach Nubia; Old Dongola's church becomes a mosque as Islam settles among…** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Islamic Conquest of the Land of Sudan and The Entry of the Arabs into Sudan”? (Sub lesson 2)
