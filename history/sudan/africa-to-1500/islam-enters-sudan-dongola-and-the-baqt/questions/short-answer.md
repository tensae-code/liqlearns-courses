# Islam Enters Sudan Dongola and the Baqt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Islam enters Sudan”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** once Egypt is opened, Arab Muslims reach Nubia; Old Dongola's church becomes a mosque as Islam settles among the Nubians by mixing and teaching

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Islamic Conquest of the Land of Sudan and The Entry of the Arabs into Sudan”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Islam comes to Sudan twice — first by treaty at the frontier, then slowly, family by family, with the migrating tribes
