# How Islam Spread in Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “The Means by which Islam Spread in Africa”?
a) Five quiet instruments do what armies did elsewhere  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
**Answer: a) Five quiet instruments do what armies did elsewhere** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these matches the story of “The Means by which Islam Spread in Africa”?
a) Nothing changes for anyone in it  b) Five quiet instruments do what armies did elsewhere  c) The people in it never act  d) It is about a place the lesson never names
**Answer: b) Five quiet instruments do what armies did elsewhere** — That is what this lesson teaches.
