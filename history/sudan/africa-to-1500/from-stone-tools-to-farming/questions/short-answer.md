# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Retell, in your own words, what happens in “Stone-Age civilisation in Sudan”. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** our ancestors lived on the Nile's banks 12,000 years ago, and the Khartoum people of 8000–3200 BCE hunted, fished and made pottery where the capital stands today

**2.** Who is in the story of “The Ancient Civilisations of Sudan”, where does it happen, and what turns? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** hunters settle on the great river, and Sudan's story starts thousands of years before any king
