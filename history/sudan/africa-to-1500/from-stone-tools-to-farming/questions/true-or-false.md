# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Our ancestors lived on the Nile's banks 12,000 years ago, and the Khartoum people of 8000–3200 BCE hunted, fished and made pottery where the capital stands today. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The part “The Ancient Civilisations of Sudan” teaches that its events changed nothing for anyone. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: Hunters settle on the great river, and Sudan's story starts thousands of years before any king.
