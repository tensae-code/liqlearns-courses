# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happens in the part called “Stone-Age civilisation in Sudan”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Our ancestors lived on the Nile's banks 12,000 years ago, and the Khartoum people of 8000–3200 BCE hunted,…  b) The story stops before anything happens  c) A different land with no connection to this lesson  d) Only a list of dates, with no people and no turn
