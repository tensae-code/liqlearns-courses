# History — Sudan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Sudan's history belongs to Sudan: this course is Sudan's own — built from Sudan's History and History and National Education student books (Arabic). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': Sudan's own land is the spine of the past — the calendar and Stone-Age Khartoum first, Kerma, Kush, Christian Nubia and the sultanates, then the conquest century, the Mahdiyya, the Condominium and independence, always measured against Arabia, Africa and the wider world the books set beside it. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
