# From Evidence to a Written History — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the lesson's one thing — History is made from evidence, and the evidence is alive: each pupil interviews a person who lived through the occupation, writes that person's experience up as a mini-biography, and groups adapt a historical event as drama — an eleven-year-old with a notebook becomes a historian, and a grandparent's memory becomes the nation's archive. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story as taught.
