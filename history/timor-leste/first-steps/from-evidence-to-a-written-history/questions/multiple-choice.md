# From Evidence to a Written History — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does each pupil do in this lesson's research?
a) memorises a printed chronology  b) interviews a person who lived through the occupation and writes the experience as a mini-biography  c) draws a map only  d) copies a poem
**Answer: b) interviews a person who lived through the occupation and writes the experience as a mini-biography** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How do groups work with a historical event in this unit?
a) they adapt it as drama  b) they hide it  c) they translate it into Portuguese  d) they vote on it
**Answer: a) they adapt it as drama** — That is what this lesson teaches.
