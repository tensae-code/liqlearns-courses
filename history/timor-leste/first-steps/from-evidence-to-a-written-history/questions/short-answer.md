# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why interview survivors of the occupation now, rather than later?
**Answer:** Because their memories are the evidence, and memory is lost when its holder dies; asked in time and written down, one person's experience becomes part of the nation's archive.

**2.** What two written and performed products does the class make from its evidence?
**Answer:** A mini-biography of the person interviewed, and a drama adaptation of a historical event, closing with presentations and reflection.
