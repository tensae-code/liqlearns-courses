# History — Timor-Leste course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Timor-Leste's
own — built from its Grade 6 Ciências Sociais (Social
Science) Teacher Guide, Period 2 (Ministério da Educação,
2016; Tetun with Portuguese parallel text). Inside the course
the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below sit inside Level 1, in path order — what the learner
proves, step by step. The grade the book comes from lives
only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: Period II of the Grade 6
story teaches 1975–2002 as lived memory, in the unit's own
lesson order — proclamation, invasion, reorganisation,
the Church and diplomacy, Santa Cruz, the clandestine front,
the pupils' own oral-history research, the referendum and
restoration, and the annex of heroes and memory. That ladder
IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — the occupation and the road back to freedom,
1975–2002** (Grade 6 Ciências Sociais Teacher Guide,
Period 2; teacher-guide evidence only):

- `modern-world/` — Level 1, main title. The proclamation of
  1975 and the Carnation Revolution that opened it; the
  Indonesian invasion and the war of 1975–1979; the
  resistance reorganised — Falintil and the 1983 ceasefire;
  the Church and the diplomatic front; the Santa Cruz
  massacre of 1991; the clandestine resistance; the
  referendum of 1999 and the Restoration of 2002; and the
  heroes and memory of the resistance.
- `first-steps/` — Level 1, the method title, in the unit's
  own order after the clandestine front. From Evidence to a
  Written History (each pupil interviews a person who lived
  through the occupation, writes the experience as a
  mini-biography, and groups adapt a historical event as
  drama).
