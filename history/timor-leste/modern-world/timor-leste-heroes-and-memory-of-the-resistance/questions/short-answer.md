# Timor-Leste: Heroes and Memory of the Resistance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four people whose biographies the annex carries.
**Answer:** Rosa 'Muki' Bonaparte, Maria Goretti Joaquim, Taur Matan Ruak and Rui Maria de Araújo.

**2.** What does this closing lesson, as a whole, teach a young republic to do?
**Answer:** To write down who its heroes were and what was done to it — biographies, chronologies, the Nobel Prize, and the CAVR truth record — so the next generation inherits names, not silence.
