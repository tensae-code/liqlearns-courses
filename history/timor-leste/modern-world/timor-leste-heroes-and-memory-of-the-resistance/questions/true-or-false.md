# Timor-Leste: Heroes and Memory of the Resistance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The annex biographies include two women of the resistance: Rosa 'Muki' Bonaparte and Maria Goretti Joaquim.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Soibada plenary assembly of 1976 dissolved the resistance and ended the people's war.
**Answer: False — the Soibada assembly organised the resistance for the prolonged people's war.**
