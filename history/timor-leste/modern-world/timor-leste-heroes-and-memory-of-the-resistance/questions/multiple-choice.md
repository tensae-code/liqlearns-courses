# Timor-Leste: Heroes and Memory of the Resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who shared the 1996 Nobel Peace Prize for Timor-Leste?
a) two generals  b) Bishop Belo and José Ramos-Horta  c) two journalists  d) the Pope and a president
**Answer: b) Bishop Belo and José Ramos-Horta** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was the CAVR?
a) a guerrilla unit  b) a student network  c) the truth and reconciliation commission whose report, CHEGA!, recorded what was done under the occupation  d) a political party
**Answer: c) the truth and reconciliation commission whose report, CHEGA!, recorded what was done under the occupation** — That is what this lesson teaches.
