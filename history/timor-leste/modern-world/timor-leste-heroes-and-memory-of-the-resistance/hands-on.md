# Hands-on — Timor-Leste: Heroes and Memory of the Resistance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Do the opening act of lesson/lesson.md again, on purpose, and say the lesson's key words aloud as you do it: Rosa Muki Bonaparte, Maria Goretti Joaquim, Taur Matan Ruak, Rui Maria de Araújo, Soibada 1976, Nobel Peace Prize 1996, CAVR, CHEGA!.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil and make this lesson's story into a strip of four drawings: the place, the people, the turn, and what came of it. Under each drawing write one sentence in your own words. Say the whole strip aloud, start to finish, without looking at the lesson.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this story's echo in real life — at home, at school, in the street: an older person remembering the occupation years, a song, a flag, a name on a wall. Note what you saw or heard, and which part of this lesson it belongs to. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
