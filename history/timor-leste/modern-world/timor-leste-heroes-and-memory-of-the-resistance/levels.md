# Timor-Leste: Heroes and Memory of the Resistance — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons (2026-11-09 ruling: sub lessons only where a lesson genuinely has distinct sequential parts). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the lesson's one thing — A young republic writes down who its heroes were and what was done to it, so the next generation inherits names, not silence: biographies of Rosa 'Muki' Bonaparte, Maria Goretti Joaquim, Taur Matan Ruak and Rui Maria de Araújo; the Soibada assembly of 1976 and the prolonged people's war; chronologies; the 1996 Nobel Peace Prize to Bishop Belo and José Ramos-Horta; and the CAVR truth commission — CHEGA! — that recorded what was done. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story as taught.
