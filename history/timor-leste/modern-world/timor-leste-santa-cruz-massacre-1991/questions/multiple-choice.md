# Timor-Leste: The Santa Cruz Massacre, 1991 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When and where did the Santa Cruz massacre happen?
a) in 1975, in the mountains  b) on 12 November 1991, at Santa Cruz cemetery in Dili  c) in 1999, at a polling station  d) in 1983, at Tasi-Tolu
**Answer: b) on 12 November 1991, at Santa Cruz cemetery in Dili** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why did Santa Cruz change the world when earlier massacres had not?
a) it was the largest battle  b) foreign journalists filmed the shooting, and the tape showed the world what the occupation was  c) the UN was meeting in Dili  d) Indonesia announced it
**Answer: b) foreign journalists filmed the shooting, and the tape showed the world what the occupation was** — That is what this lesson teaches.
