# Timor-Leste: The Santa Cruz Massacre, 1991 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The people shot at Santa Cruz were unarmed young mourners.
**Answer: True**

**2.** After the Santa Cruz film was shown, the occupation remained as hidden from the world as before.
**Answer: False — the film turned the world's eyes onto Timor; the occupation could no longer be hidden.**
