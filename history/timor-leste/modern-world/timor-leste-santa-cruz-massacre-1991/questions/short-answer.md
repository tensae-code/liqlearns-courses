# Timor-Leste: The Santa Cruz Massacre, 1991 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Which earlier massacre does the lesson recall alongside Santa Cruz?
**Answer:** The Kraras massacre.

**2.** Describe the march that ended at Santa Cruz cemetery.
**Answer:** Young mourners marched to the cemetery remembering one of their own, carrying the nation's banned flags and banners — a peaceful, unarmed procession of the young.
