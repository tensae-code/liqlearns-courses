# Timor-Leste: The Clandestine Resistance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did Falintil in the bush depend on the clandestine front?
**Answer:** Because the guerrillas needed food, medicine, news and recruits from the towns, carried in secret past the occupation.

**2.** Who did the clandestine work, in the book's telling?
**Answer:** Ordinary townspeople and very often the young — schoolchildren and students as couriers and organisers, student networks such as Renetil, and Indonesian friends in groups such as Solidamor.
