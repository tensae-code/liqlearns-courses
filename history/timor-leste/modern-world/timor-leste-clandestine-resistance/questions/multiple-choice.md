# Timor-Leste: The Clandestine Resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did clandestine couriers carry to Falintil in the bush?
a) tax forms  b) letters, food and medicine  c) newspapers from abroad only  d) nothing — the bush supplied itself
**Answer: b) letters, food and medicine** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was Renetil?
a) an Indonesian army unit  b) a student resistance network  c) a church choir  d) a political party in Lisbon
**Answer: b) a student resistance network** — That is what this lesson teaches.
