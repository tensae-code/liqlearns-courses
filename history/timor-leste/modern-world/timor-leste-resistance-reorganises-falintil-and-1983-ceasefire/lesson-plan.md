# Lesson plan — Timor-Leste: The Resistance Reorganises — Falintil and the 1983 Ceasefire

**Course:** History — Timor-Leste · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Timor-Leste: The Resistance Reorganises — Falintil and the 1983 Ceasefire
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Occupation and the Road Back to Freedom, 1975–2002 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Beaten as a conventional army, the resistance reorganises: Falintil fights a guerrilla war, the struggle works on three fronts together, and in 1983 a ceasefire — Kontaktu Dame, the peace contact — pauses the fighting until its collapse sets off uprisings.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the book itself works: pupils sing the resistance songs, act the events as drama, read photographs and poems, build a class timeline, and interview ordinary people who survived the occupation.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks Falintil was a new, separate army → Falintil is the reorganised resistance itself, fighting the guerrilla way after positional war failed.
- Thinks the 1983 ceasefire ended the war → it collapsed, and uprisings followed its collapse; the occupation and the resistance both continued.
- Go back to the one thing: Beaten as a conventional army, the resistance reorganises: Falintil fights a guerrilla war, the struggle works on three fronts together, and in 1983 a ceasefire — Kontaktu Dame, the peace contact — pauses the fighting until its collapse sets off uprisings. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
