# Timor-Leste: The Resistance Reorganises — Falintil and the 1983 Ceasefire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the 1983 ceasefire collapsed, uprisings followed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The three fronts fought separately, with no connection between them.
**Answer: False — the armed, clandestine and diplomatic fronts worked together as one struggle.**
