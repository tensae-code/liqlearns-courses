# Timor-Leste: The Resistance Reorganises — Falintil and the 1983 Ceasefire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did the resistance have to reorganise?
**Answer:** Because positional war — holding ground as a conventional army — could be surrounded and destroyed by Indonesia's campaigns; as guerrillas it could survive and keep fighting.

**2.** Name the three fronts of the reorganised resistance.
**Answer:** The armed front (Falintil in the bush), the clandestine front (the secret networks in the towns), and the diplomatic front (the cause carried abroad).
