# Timor-Leste: The Resistance Reorganises — Falintil and the 1983 Ceasefire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did the resistance fight after it reorganised?
a) as a navy  b) it stopped fighting  c) as Falintil guerrillas, with the struggle organised on three fronts  d) as a fixed army in the capital
**Answer: c) as Falintil guerrillas, with the struggle organised on three fronts** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was Kontaktu Dame?
a) the 1983 ceasefire — the peace contact — between the resistance and Indonesia  b) a song  c) a massacre  d) a referendum
**Answer: a) the 1983 ceasefire — the peace contact — between the resistance and Indonesia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
