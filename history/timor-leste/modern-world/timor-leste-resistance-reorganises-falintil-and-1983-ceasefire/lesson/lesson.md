# Timor-Leste: The Resistance Reorganises — Falintil and the 1983 Ceasefire

## Do it first

Say these words out loud, slowly, and think about each one as you say it: Falintil, guerrilla war, three fronts, 1983 ceasefire, Kontaktu Dame, uprisings. Now say, in one sentence of your own, what you think this lesson's story will be about. Hold that sentence in mind — the story below will test it.

## The story

An army that holds ground can be surrounded and destroyed — the campaigns of the first war years proved that. So the resistance did the hardest thing an army can do: it changed its whole way of fighting while the war was still going on. It reorganised itself.

The reorganised resistance fought as Falintil guerrillas — small, mobile fighters in the mountains, striking and vanishing, impossible to encircle the way a fixed army can be. And the struggle was organised on three fronts together: the armed front in the bush, the clandestine front in the towns (a later lesson tells its story in full), and the diplomatic front abroad (the next lesson but one). No front fought alone; together they kept a whole nation in the field.

In 1983 came a strange pause: a ceasefire, remembered as Kontaktu Dame — the peace contact — between the resistance and Indonesia. For a moment the guns quietened. But the ceasefire collapsed, and its collapse was followed by uprisings — the people rising again rather than accepting the occupation as settled. Remember the turn: reorganise or die — guerrillas, three fronts, a ceasefire used and lost, and uprisings after it.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: tell this story aloud to someone today, in your own words, without the page in front of you. A story you can retell is a story you own.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Beaten as a conventional army, the resistance reorganises: Falintil fights a guerrilla war, the struggle works on three fronts together, and in 1983 a ceasefire — Kontaktu Dame, the peace contact — pauses the fighting until its collapse sets off uprisings. Give one real example from the story — a small guerrilla group moving at night through mountain forest, carrying a message from a town network — the three fronts working as one — and name the lesson's key words: Falintil, guerrilla war, three fronts, 1983 ceasefire, Kontaktu Dame, uprisings. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
