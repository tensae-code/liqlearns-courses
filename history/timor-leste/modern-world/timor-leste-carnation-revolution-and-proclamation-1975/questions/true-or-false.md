# Timor-Leste: The Carnation Revolution and the Proclamation of 1975 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Carnation Revolution in Portugal opened the way to decolonisation for Timor-Leste.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Timor-Leste's first proclaimed republic lasted for many peaceful years before any invasion.
**Answer: False — the proclamation of 28 November 1975 was followed nine days later by Indonesia's invasion in December 1975.**
