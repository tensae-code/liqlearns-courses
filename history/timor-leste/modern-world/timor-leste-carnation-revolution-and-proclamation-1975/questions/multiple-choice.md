# Timor-Leste: The Carnation Revolution and the Proclamation of 1975 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the signal for Portugal's Carnation Revolution?
a) a church bell  b) Zeca Afonso's song Grândola Vila Morena played as the agreed signal  c) a flag raised in Dili  d) a letter from the United Nations
**Answer: b) Zeca Afonso's song Grândola Vila Morena played as the agreed signal** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happened in Dili on 28 November 1975?
a) Indonesia invaded  b) a referendum was held  c) Fretilin proclaimed the Democratic Republic of Timor-Leste, with Francisco Xavier do Amaral as president  d) the Pope said mass
**Answer: c) Fretilin proclaimed the Democratic Republic of Timor-Leste, with Francisco Xavier do Amaral as president** — That is what this lesson teaches.
