# Timor-Leste: The Carnation Revolution and the Proclamation of 1975 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What anthem was sung for the first time at the 1975 proclamation, and who became president?
**Answer:** The anthem Pátria was sung for the first time, and Francisco Xavier do Amaral became the first president of the proclaimed Democratic Republic of Timor-Leste.

**2.** Why did a revolution in Lisbon matter to people in Dili?
**Answer:** Because Portugal ruled Timor as a colony; when the dictatorship fell in the Carnation Revolution, Portugal began decolonisation, opening the way for Timor-Leste to proclaim its own republic.
