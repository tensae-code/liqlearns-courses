# Lesson plan — Timor-Leste: The Indonesian Invasion and the War, 1975–1979

**Course:** History — Timor-Leste · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Timor-Leste: The Indonesian Invasion and the War, 1975–1979
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Occupation and the Road Back to Freedom, 1975–2002 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In December 1975 Indonesia — Timor-Leste's giant neighbour — invades the nine-day-old republic; Fretilin organises the war, first holding ground in positional war, and Indonesia's encirclement-and-annihilation campaigns devastate the population — a devastation the book reads through photographs and Xanana Gusmão's poem Esperanças Rasgadas, Torn Hopes.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the book itself works: pupils sing the resistance songs, act the events as drama, read photographs and poems, build a class timeline, and interview ordinary people who survived the occupation.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the war fell only on soldiers → the book's own emphasis is the population: the encirclement-and-annihilation campaigns devastated ordinary people, which is why photographs and a poem carry the lesson.
- Thinks the resistance began as guerrillas → first it was positional war, an army holding ground; the guerrilla reorganisation comes in the next lesson, after the positional war was beaten.
- Go back to the one thing: In December 1975 Indonesia — Timor-Leste's giant neighbour — invades the nine-day-old republic; Fretilin organises the war, first holding ground in positional war, and Indonesia's encirclement-and-annihilation campaigns devastate the population — a devastation the book reads through photographs and Xanana Gusmão's poem Esperanças Rasgadas, Torn Hopes. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
