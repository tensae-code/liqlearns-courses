# Timor-Leste: The Indonesian Invasion and the War, 1975–1979 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fretilin's war began as positional war — an army holding ground.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The invasion and campaigns harmed only Fretilin soldiers and left the population untouched.
**Answer: False — the campaigns devastated the population; the book reads that devastation through photographs and Gusmão's poem.**
