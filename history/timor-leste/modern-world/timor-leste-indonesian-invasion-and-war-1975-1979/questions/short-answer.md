# Timor-Leste: The Indonesian Invasion and the War, 1975–1979 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Whose poem does the book read to teach the devastation, and what is its English name?
**Answer:** Xanana Gusmão's poem Esperanças Rasgadas — Torn Hopes.

**2.** How did Fretilin first organise the war after the invasion?
**Answer:** As positional war — holding ground and defending positions, an army facing an army, before the later guerrilla reorganisation.
