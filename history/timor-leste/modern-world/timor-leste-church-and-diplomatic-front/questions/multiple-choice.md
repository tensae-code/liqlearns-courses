# Timor-Leste: The Church and the Diplomatic Front — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Pope's mass at Tasi-Tolu become?
a) a market day  b) a demonstration — a vast gathering that showed the nation to the world  c) a military parade  d) a ceasefire signing
**Answer: b) a demonstration — a vast gathering that showed the nation to the world** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where did Timor-Leste's diplomats carry the cause?
a) only to Dili  b) to the United Nations and the world  c) to Indonesia's army headquarters  d) nowhere — there were no diplomats
**Answer: b) to the United Nations and the world** — That is what this lesson teaches.
