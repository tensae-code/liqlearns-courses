# Timor-Leste: The Church and the Diplomatic Front — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did the Church help the resistance without carrying guns?
**Answer:** It sheltered the people — its parishes and bishop stood between ordinary people and the occupation — and the Pope's mass at Tasi-Tolu gathered the nation in one visible place.

**2.** What single claim did the diplomats repeat at the UN and around the world?
**Answer:** That the Timorese people had never freely chosen the occupation, and their claim to decide their own future was still alive.
