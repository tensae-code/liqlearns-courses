# Timor-Leste: The Church and the Diplomatic Front — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under the occupation, the Catholic Church sheltered the people of Timor-Leste.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The diplomatic front argued that the Timorese had already been freely asked and had chosen the occupation.
**Answer: False — it argued the opposite: the people had never been freely asked, and their right to choose still stood.**
