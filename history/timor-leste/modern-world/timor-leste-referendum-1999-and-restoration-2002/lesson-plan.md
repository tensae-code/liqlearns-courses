# Lesson plan — Timor-Leste: The Referendum of 1999 and the Restoration of 2002

**Course:** History — Timor-Leste · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Timor-Leste: The Referendum of 1999 and the Restoration of 2002
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Occupation and the Road Back to Freedom, 1975–2002 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After 24 years the people are finally asked: political change in Indonesia in 1998 and the founding of the CNRT open the way to the referendum of 30 August 1999, where the people choose independence by a landslide — Indonesia answers with scorched-earth destruction as it leaves — and on 20 May 2002 independence is restored and the flag rises again.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: After 24 years the people are finally asked: political change in Indonesia in 1998 and the founding of the CNRT open the way to the referendum of 30 August 1999, where the people choose independence by a landslide — Indonesia answers with scorched-earth destruction as it leaves — and on 20 May 2002 independence is restored and the flag rises again.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself, as the book itself works: pupils sing the resistance songs, act the events as drama, read photographs and poems, build a class timeline, and interview ordinary people who survived the occupation.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the referendum was close → the book's word is a landslide: the people chose independence overwhelmingly.
- Thinks independence came on referendum day → the vote was 30 August 1999; after the scorched-earth destruction, independence was formally restored on 20 May 2002.
- Go back to the one thing: After 24 years the people are finally asked: political change in Indonesia in 1998 and the founding of the CNRT open the way to the referendum of 30 August 1999, where the people choose independence by a landslide — Indonesia answers with scorched-earth destruction as it leaves — and on 20 May 2002 independence is restored and the flag rises again. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
