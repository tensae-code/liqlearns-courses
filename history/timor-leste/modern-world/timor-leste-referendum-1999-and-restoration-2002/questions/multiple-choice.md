# Timor-Leste: The Referendum of 1999 and the Restoration of 2002 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened on 30 August 1999?
a) the invasion  b) the referendum, in which the people chose independence by a landslide  c) the Santa Cruz massacre  d) the 1983 ceasefire
**Answer: b) the referendum, in which the people chose independence by a landslide** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the Restoration of Independence, and when was it?
a) a church feast in 1991  b) the formal restoration of independence on 20 May 2002, when the flag rose again over a free Timor-Leste  c) a vote in Lisbon  d) the founding of Renetil
**Answer: b) the formal restoration of independence on 20 May 2002, when the flag rose again over a free Timor-Leste** — That is what this lesson teaches.
