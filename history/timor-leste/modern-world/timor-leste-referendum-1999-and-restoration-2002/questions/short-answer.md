# Timor-Leste: The Referendum of 1999 and the Restoration of 2002 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What opened the way to the referendum, in the years just before 1999?
**Answer:** Political change in Indonesia in 1998, and the founding of the CNRT to unite the Timorese resistance for the political road.

**2.** Through what classroom tool does the book work the 1984–1999 arc?
**Answer:** A class timeline, 1984–1999, on which pupils place each turn of the road to the referendum.
