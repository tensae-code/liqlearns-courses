# Timor-Leste: The Referendum of 1999 and the Restoration of 2002 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** As Indonesia left after the referendum, scorched-earth destruction burned and wrecked much of the country.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The CNRT was founded to divide the resistance into rival parties before the vote.
**Answer: False — the CNRT was founded to unite the resistance for the political road to the referendum.**
