# Timor-Leste: The Referendum of 1999 and the Restoration of 2002

## Do it first

Say these words out loud, slowly, and think about each one as you say it: CNRT, referendum 30 August 1999, scorched earth, Restoration of Independence, 20 May 2002. Now say, in one sentence of your own, what you think this lesson's story will be about. Hold that sentence in mind — the story below will test it.

## The story

For twenty-four years the diplomats' claim had been the same: the Timorese had never been freely asked. In 1998, political change came to Indonesia itself, and the door the resistance had pushed for decades finally opened. The Timorese prepared as one people: the CNRT was founded to unite the resistance for the political road ahead.

On 30 August 1999 came the referendum — the free asking at last. The people voted, and chose independence by a landslide. But the answer was met with fire: as Indonesia left, its forces and militias carried out scorched-earth destruction, burning and wrecking the country the people had just voted to make their own. The book works this whole arc through a class timeline, 1984–1999, so pupils place each turn on the long road themselves.

The story does not end in the ashes. The destruction could not un-vote the vote. On 20 May 2002, independence was formally restored — the Restoration of Independence — and the flag proclaimed in 1975 rose again over a free Timor-Leste, this time recognised by the world. Remember the arc whole: asked at last, chosen by a landslide, burned for it — and restored.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: tell this story aloud to someone today, in your own words, without the page in front of you. A story you can retell is a story you own.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: After 24 years the people are finally asked: political change in Indonesia in 1998 and the founding of the CNRT open the way to the referendum of 30 August 1999, where the people choose independence by a landslide — Indonesia answers with scorched-earth destruction as it leaves — and on 20 May 2002 independence is restored and the flag rises again. Give one real example from the story — a long queue of voters before dawn on 30 August 1999, people who had waited 24 years to be asked, standing patiently to mark one paper — and name the lesson's key words: CNRT, referendum 30 August 1999, scorched earth, Restoration of Independence, 20 May 2002. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
