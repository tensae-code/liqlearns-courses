# History — Uganda course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Uganda's own — built from Uganda's Social Studies and History and Political Education books (English). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the district's past from the elders first, then the national march — peoples, kingdoms, foreigners, protectorate, independence — then East Africa whole, then the same story with causes, then outside pressures and the replies to them, and last Southern and West Africa as the shared continental past. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
