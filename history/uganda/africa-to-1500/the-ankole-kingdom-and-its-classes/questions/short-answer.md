# The Ankole Kingdom and Its Classes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Term 2: Ankole Kingdom”?
**Answer:** Ruhinda, grandson of Wamala, founds Ankole; its king is the Omugabe, and its people stand in two classes — Bairu cultivators below Bahima pastoralists.
