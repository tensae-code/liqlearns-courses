# The Ankole Kingdom and Its Classes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 2: Ankole Kingdom”?
a) Ruhinda, grandson of Wamala, founds Ankole; its king is the Omugabe, and its people stand in two classes — Bairu cultivators below Bahima pastoralists.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2: Ankole Kingdom” is right?
a) Ruhinda, grandson of Wamala, founds Ankole; its king is the Omugabe, and its people stand in two classes — Bairu cultivators below Bahima pastoralists.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
