# The Ankole Kingdom and Its Classes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ruhinda, grandson of Wamala, founds Ankole; its king is the Omugabe, and its people stand in two classes — Bairu cultivators below Bahima pastoralists.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2: Ankole Kingdom” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Ruhinda, grandson of Wamala, founds Ankole; its king is the Omugabe, and its people stand in two classes — Bairu cultivators below Bahima pastoralists.
