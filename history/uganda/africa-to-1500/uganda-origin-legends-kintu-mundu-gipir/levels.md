# Uganda Origin Legends: Kintu, Mundu and Gipir — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Elders' stories tell where each people began — Kintu and Nambi in Buganda, Mundu and Sera in Bugisu, Gipir and Labong in Acholi — and the legends carry each tribe's culture. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
