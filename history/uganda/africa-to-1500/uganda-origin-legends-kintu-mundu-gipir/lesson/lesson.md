# Uganda Origin Legends: Kintu, Mundu and Gipir

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic: People in Our District; Lesson: Legends. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic: People in Our District; Lesson: Legends

Elders' stories tell where each people began — Kintu and Nambi in Buganda, Mundu and Sera in Bugisu, Gipir and Labong in Acholi — and the legends carry each tribe's culture.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
