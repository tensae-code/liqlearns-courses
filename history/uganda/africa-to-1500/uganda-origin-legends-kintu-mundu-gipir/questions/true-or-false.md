# Uganda Origin Legends: Kintu, Mundu and Gipir — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Elders' stories tell where each people began — Kintu and Nambi in Buganda, Mundu and Sera in Bugisu, Gipir and Labong in Acholi — and the legends carry each tribe's culture.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic: People in Our District; Lesson: Legends” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Elders' stories tell where each people began — Kintu and Nambi in Buganda, Mundu and Sera in Bugisu, Gipir and Labong in Acholi — and the legends carry each tribe's culture.
