# Uganda Origin Legends: Kintu, Mundu and Gipir — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic: People in Our District; Lesson: Legends”?
a) Elders' stories tell where each people began — Kintu and Nambi in Buganda, Mundu and Sera in Bugisu, Gipir and Labong in Acholi — and the legends carry each tribe's culture.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: People in Our District; Lesson: Legends” is right?
a) Elders' stories tell where each people began — Kintu and Nambi in Buganda, Mundu and Sera in Bugisu, Gipir and Labong in Acholi — and the legends carry each tribe's culture.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
