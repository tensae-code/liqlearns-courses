# How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Peoples organise in trade communities by their traditional work — cultivation, pastoralism, fishing — barter first, then the Arabs bring cowrie shells from the Maldives and trade turns monetary. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: Long-distance trade and the Zenj empire” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Caravans move together against wild animals and bandits; Baganda, Nyamwezi, Kamba and Yao trade ivory, hoes, salt and honey by barter until cowrie shells arrive.
