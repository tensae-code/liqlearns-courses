# Archaeology and the Leakeys at Olduvai — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History before writing is dug, not read — Dr Louis Leakey comes with his wife Mary and finds the oldest skull of man at Olduvai Gorge in 1959; museums keep the artefacts safe. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2, Topic: The East African Community; Lesson: The Cradle of Human Race in East Africa” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: History's sources are named — oral tradition, linguistics, anthropology, archaeology — fossils are dug by excavators at named sites, and East Africa is taught as mankind's cradle.
