# The Bantu Migrations into Uganda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bantu peoples settle the lake region and the mountain slopes — Bakonzo on Rwenzori, Bafumbira and Bakiga on Mufumbiro, Bagisu on Elgon. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “SST Term 2; Lesson 2: The origin of different Ethnic groups in Uganda” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Each group is fixed by work and place — Bantu farmers in the interlacustrine region, Luo cattle-keepers and fishermen, Plain Nilotes in the semi-arid north, Highland Nilotes on Elgon's slopes.
