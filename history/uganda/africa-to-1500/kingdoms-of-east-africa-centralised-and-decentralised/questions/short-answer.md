# Kingdoms of East Africa: Centralised and Decentralised — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Term 2: Kingdoms and chiefdoms”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Peoples ruled by kings live under centralised monarchy, those under chiefs under a decentralised system; every kingdom has one hereditary king, special regalia, classes, and royal tombs for its dead kings.
