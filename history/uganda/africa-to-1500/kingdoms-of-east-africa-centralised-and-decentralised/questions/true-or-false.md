# Kingdoms of East Africa: Centralised and Decentralised — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Peoples ruled by kings live under centralised monarchy, those under chiefs under a decentralised system; every kingdom has one hereditary king, special regalia, classes, and royal tombs for its dead kings. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Chapter 5: State Formation in East Africa” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The decentralised peoples — Maasai, Chagga, Kikuyu, Nyamwezi, Acholi — move youths through age grades by initiation at about 18, while the Nyamwezi ntemi rules through gunguli leaders who collect tribute, farm his fields and raise his armies.
