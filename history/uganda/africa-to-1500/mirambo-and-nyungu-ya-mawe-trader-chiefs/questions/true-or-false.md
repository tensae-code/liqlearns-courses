# Mirambo and Nyungu ya Mawe, Trader-Chiefs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Two Tanzanian strongmen build states on the trade routes — Mirambo's power leaks away to local chiefs after him, and Nyungu ya Mawe, "pot of stones", rules from Kiwele. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2: The Nyamwezi chiefdoms” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The Ntemi are supreme rulers who lead prayers, make sacrifices and keep the royal drum; Chief Mirambo is named among their famous leaders.
