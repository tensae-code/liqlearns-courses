# Mirambo and Nyungu ya Mawe, Trader-Chiefs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 1: Rise of Nyungu ya Mawe”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Two Tanzanian strongmen build states on the trade routes — Mirambo's power leaks away to local chiefs after him, and Nyungu ya Mawe, "pot of stones", rules from Kiwele.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2: The Nyamwezi chiefdoms” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The Ntemi are supreme rulers who lead prayers, make sacrifices and keep the royal drum; Chief Mirambo is named among their famous leaders.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
