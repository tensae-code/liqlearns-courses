# Lesson plan — Mirambo and Nyungu ya Mawe, Trader-Chiefs

**Course:** History — Uganda · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Mirambo and Nyungu ya Mawe, Trader-Chiefs
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — East Africa Whole: Migrations, Kingdoms, Trade and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Two Tanzanian strongmen build states on the trade routes — Mirambo's power leaks away to local chiefs after him, and Nyungu ya Mawe, "pot of stones", rules from Kiwele.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — East Africa Whole: Migrations, Kingdoms, Trade and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Term 1: Rise of Nyungu ya Mawe** — Two Tanzanian strongmen build states on the trade routes — Mirambo's power leaks away to local chiefs after him, and Nyungu ya Mawe, "pot of stones", rules from Kiwele.
2. **Term 2: The Nyamwezi chiefdoms** — The Ntemi are supreme rulers who lead prayers, make sacrifices and keep the royal drum; Chief Mirambo is named among their famous leaders.
3. **Term 1, Topic: External Contacts and Pressures 1800–1880; Lesson 3: The rise of Military States** — Strong leadership and new military tactics raise military states in East Africa, the Nyamwezi foremost in the lesson's tasks.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
