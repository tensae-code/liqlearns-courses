# Video script — Mirambo and Nyungu ya Mawe, Trader-Chiefs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Two Tanzanian strongmen build states on the trade routes — Mirambo's power leaks away to local chiefs after him, and Nyungu ya Mawe, "pot of stones", rules from Kiwele.

On the map, follow this part: Term 1: Rise of Nyungu ya Mawe. Two Tanzanian strongmen build states on the trade routes — Mirambo's power leaks away to local chiefs after him, and Nyungu ya Mawe, "pot of stones", rules from Kiwele.

On the map, follow this part: Term 2: The Nyamwezi chiefdoms. The Ntemi are supreme rulers who lead prayers, make sacrifices and keep the royal drum; Chief Mirambo is named among their famous leaders.

On the map, follow this part: Term 1, Topic: External Contacts and Pressures 1800–1880; Lesson 3: The rise of Military States. Strong leadership and new military tactics raise military states in East Africa, the Nyamwezi foremost in the lesson's tasks.
