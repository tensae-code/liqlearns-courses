# The Wanga Kingdom of Western Kenya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Around Lake Victoria in western Kenya stands the only kingdom Kenya had, named after its founder, Wanga. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Chapter 4: Traditional institutions” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: When the British arrive in Kenya in 1883 they find one organised centralised state — the Wanga Kingdom of Mumia in Kakamega — and Kenya, unlike Uganda, barely recognises cultural leaders to this day.
