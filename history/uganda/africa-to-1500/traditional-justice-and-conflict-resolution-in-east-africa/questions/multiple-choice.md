# Traditional Justice and Conflict Resolution in East Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Chapter 4: Justice in traditional culture”?
a) Two feuding clans cut a sheep and a goat in half and exchange the halves, then drink the bitter herb Oput together to "wash away bitterness" — justice as a shared drink, not a verdict.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own

**2.** Which statement about “Chapter 4: Justice in traditional culture” is right?
a) Two feuding clans cut a sheep and a goat in half and exchange the halves, then drink the bitter herb Oput together to "wash away bitterness" — justice as a shared drink, not a verdict.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
