# Lesson plan — Origin Stories: Creation and Evolution in East Africa

**Course:** History — Uganda · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Origin Stories: Creation and Evolution in East Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — East Africa with Causes: State Formation, Religions, Trade and Colonisation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Three origin stories sit side by side for judgement — the culture's own traditions, Adam fashioned from dust in Eden, and Darwin's evolution — and the learner must weigh their advantages and disadvantages in a table.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — East Africa with Causes: State Formation, Religions, Trade and Colonisation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 2: Traditional views, Adam and Eve, and Darwin** — Three origin stories sit side by side for judgement — the culture's own traditions, Adam fashioned from dust in Eden, and Darwin's evolution — and the learner must weigh their advantages and disadvantages in a table.
2. **Chapter 2: The Origin of Man (guide)** — The guide adds the scientists' universe — Newton's theory and the Big Bang — beside Adam and Eve, then names Darwin's the most reliable of the scientific theories and sets a classroom debate: creation versus evolution.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
