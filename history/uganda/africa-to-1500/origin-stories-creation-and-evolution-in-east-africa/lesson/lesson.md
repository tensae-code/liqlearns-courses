# Origin Stories: Creation and Evolution in East Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Chapter 2: Traditional views, Adam and Eve, and Darwin; Chapter 2: The Origin of Man (guide). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 2: Traditional views, Adam and Eve, and Darwin

Three origin stories sit side by side for judgement — the culture's own traditions, Adam fashioned from dust in Eden, and Darwin's evolution — and the learner must weigh their advantages and disadvantages in a table.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 2: The Origin of Man (guide)

The guide adds the scientists' universe — Newton's theory and the Big Bang — beside Adam and Eve, then names Darwin's the most reliable of the scientific theories and sets a classroom debate: creation versus evolution.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.
