# Video script — Origin Stories: Creation and Evolution in East Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Three origin stories sit side by side for judgement — the culture's own traditions, Adam fashioned from dust in Eden, and Darwin's evolution — and the learner must weigh their advantages and disadvantages in a table.

On the map, follow this part: Chapter 2: Traditional views, Adam and Eve, and Darwin. On the map, follow this part: Chapter 2: The Origin of Man (guide). The guide adds the scientists' universe — Newton's theory and the Big Bang — beside Adam and Eve, then names Darwin's the most reliable of the scientific theories and sets a classroom debate: creation versus evolution.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
