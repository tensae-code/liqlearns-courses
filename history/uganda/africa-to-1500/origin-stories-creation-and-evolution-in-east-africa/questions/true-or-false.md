# Origin Stories: Creation and Evolution in East Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three origin stories sit side by side for judgement — the culture's own traditions, Adam fashioned from dust in Eden, and Darwin's evolution — and the learner must weigh their advantages and disadvantages in a table. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Chapter 2: The Origin of Man (guide)” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The guide adds the scientists' universe — Newton's theory and the Big Bang — beside Adam and Eve, then names Darwin's the most reliable of the scientific theories and sets a classroom debate: creation versus evolution.
