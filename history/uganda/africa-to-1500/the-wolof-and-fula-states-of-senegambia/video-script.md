# Video script — The Wolof and Fula States of Senegambia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars.
