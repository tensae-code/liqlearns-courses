# The Wolof and Fula States of Senegambia — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
