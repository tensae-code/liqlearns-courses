# The Wolof and Fula States of Senegambia

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Wolof Empire. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Wolof Empire

A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
