# The Wolof and Fula States of Senegambia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Wolof Empire”?
a) A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Wolof Empire” is right?
a) A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
