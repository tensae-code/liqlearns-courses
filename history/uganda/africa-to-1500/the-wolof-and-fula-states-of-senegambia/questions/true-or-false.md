# The Wolof and Fula States of Senegambia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Wolof Empire” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars.
