# The Wolof and Fula States of Senegambia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Wolof Empire”?
**Answer:** A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars.
