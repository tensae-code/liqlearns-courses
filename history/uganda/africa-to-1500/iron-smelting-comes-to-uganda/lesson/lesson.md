# Iron Smelting Comes to Uganda

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 6: Iron smelting; Term 1: The Iron Age. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 6: Iron smelting

Iron smelting begins with the Cushites at Meroe and is brought to Uganda by the Bachwezi; the blacksmith's pangas and hoes remake farming and war.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Term 1: The Iron Age

Iron smelting, begun (the book says) by the Cushites at Meroe, is brought into East Africa by the Bachwezi, and the blacksmith becomes the maker and repairer of the new tools.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.
