# Iron Smelting Comes to Uganda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 6: Iron smelting”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Iron smelting begins with the Cushites at Meroe and is brought to Uganda by the Bachwezi; the blacksmith's pangas and hoes remake farming and war.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 1: The Iron Age” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Iron smelting, begun (the book says) by the Cushites at Meroe, is brought into East Africa by the Bachwezi, and the blacksmith becomes the maker and repairer of the new tools.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
