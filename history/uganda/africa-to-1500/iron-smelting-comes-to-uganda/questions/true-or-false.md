# Iron Smelting Comes to Uganda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Iron smelting begins with the Cushites at Meroe and is brought to Uganda by the Bachwezi; the blacksmith's pangas and hoes remake farming and war. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: The Iron Age” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Iron smelting, begun (the book says) by the Cushites at Meroe, is brought into East Africa by the Bachwezi, and the blacksmith becomes the maker and repairer of the new tools.
