# Video script — The Luo Migrations and the Biito Dynasty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Luo speakers migrate from Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan to the lakes, bringing millet, sorghum and sweet potatoes, founding the Luo-Biito dynasty — and displacing those they find.

On the map, follow this part: Topic 6: The River-Lake Nilotes. Luo speakers migrate from Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan to the lakes, bringing millet, sorghum and sweet potatoes, founding the Luo-Biito dynasty — and displacing those they find.

On the map, follow this part: Term 1: The River-Lake Nilotes. The Luo speakers leave Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan and settle by the lakes and rivers that name them.

On the map, follow this part: Chapter 3: The Luo. The River-Lake Nilotes come from Bahr el Gazel in Southern Sudan, pastoralists whose family fills the map — Dinka, Alur, Luo, Shilluk, Bor, Anuak, Acholi.
