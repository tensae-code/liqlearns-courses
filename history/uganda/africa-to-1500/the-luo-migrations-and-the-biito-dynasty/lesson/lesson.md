# The Luo Migrations and the Biito Dynasty

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 6: The River-Lake Nilotes; Term 1: The River-Lake Nilotes; Chapter 3: The Luo. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 6: The River-Lake Nilotes

Luo speakers migrate from Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan to the lakes, bringing millet, sorghum and sweet potatoes, founding the Luo-Biito dynasty — and displacing those they find.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Term 1: The River-Lake Nilotes

The Luo speakers leave Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan and settle by the lakes and rivers that name them.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 3: The Luo

The River-Lake Nilotes come from Bahr el Gazel in Southern Sudan, pastoralists whose family fills the map — Dinka, Alur, Luo, Shilluk, Bor, Anuak, Acholi.
