# The Luo Migrations and the Biito Dynasty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 6: The River-Lake Nilotes”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Luo speakers migrate from Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan to the lakes, bringing millet, sorghum and sweet potatoes, founding the Luo-Biito dynasty — and displacing those they find.
