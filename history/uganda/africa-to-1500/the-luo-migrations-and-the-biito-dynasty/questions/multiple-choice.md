# The Luo Migrations and the Biito Dynasty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 6: The River-Lake Nilotes”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Luo speakers migrate from Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan to the lakes, bringing millet, sorghum and sweet potatoes, founding the Luo-Biito dynasty — and displacing those they find.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 1: The River-Lake Nilotes” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The Luo speakers leave Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan and settle by the lakes and rivers that name them.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
