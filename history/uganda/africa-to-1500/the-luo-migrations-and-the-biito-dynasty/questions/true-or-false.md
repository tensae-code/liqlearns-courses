# The Luo Migrations and the Biito Dynasty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Luo speakers migrate from Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan to the lakes, bringing millet, sorghum and sweet potatoes, founding the Luo-Biito dynasty — and displacing those they find. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: The River-Lake Nilotes” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The Luo speakers leave Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan and settle by the lakes and rivers that name them.
