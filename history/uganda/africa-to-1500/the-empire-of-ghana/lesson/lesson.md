# The Empire of Ghana

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic: Empires of Western Sudan; Lesson 2: The Empire of Ghana. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic: Empires of Western Sudan; Lesson 2: The Empire of Ghana

Founded around 500 AD between the Niger and Senegal rivers — water and a defensive barrier in one — Ghana grows as Islam spreads through the trade.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
