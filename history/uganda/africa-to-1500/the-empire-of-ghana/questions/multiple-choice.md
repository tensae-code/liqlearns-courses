# The Empire of Ghana — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan; Lesson 2: The Empire of Ghana”?
a) Founded around 500 AD between the Niger and Senegal rivers — water and a defensive barrier in one — Ghana grows as Islam spreads through the trade.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan; Lesson 2: The Empire of Ghana” is right?
a) Founded around 500 AD between the Niger and Senegal rivers — water and a defensive barrier in one — Ghana grows as Islam spreads through the trade.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
