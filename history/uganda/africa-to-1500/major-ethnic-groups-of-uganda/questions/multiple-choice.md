# Major Ethnic Groups of Uganda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “SST Term 2, Topic: The People of the Pre-Colonial Period in Uganda; Lesson 1: Ethnic groups in Uganda”?
a) The sketch map's arrows show where each people entered — Bantu the largest, Nilotes in three branches, Sudanic peoples in West Nile.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
