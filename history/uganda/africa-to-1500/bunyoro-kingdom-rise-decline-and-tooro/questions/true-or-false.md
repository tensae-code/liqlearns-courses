# Bunyoro Kingdom: Rise, Decline and Tooro — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bunyoro, once the region's great power, declines because it is too big for one ruler, torn by internal conflict and pressed by rising neighbours — Tooro and Buganda — before the colonialists arrive. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: Bunyoro kingdom” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Bunyoro grows under Omukama Kamurasi, and his son Kabalega inherits a kingdom whose districts the book maps onto today's Hoima, Masindi and Buliisa.
