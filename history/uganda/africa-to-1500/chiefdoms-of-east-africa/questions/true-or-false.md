# Chiefdoms of East Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Beside the kingdoms stand the chiefdoms — Iteso, Kikuyu, Basoga, Acholi, Kamba, Masai, Chagga — each ruled by a chief, and below them the clan, headed by a clan leader who keeps the records.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: Chiefdoms in East Africa” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Beside the kingdoms stand the chiefdoms — Iteso, Kikuyu, Basoga, Acholi, Kamba, Masai, Chagga — each ruled by a chief, and below them the clan, headed by a clan leader who keeps the records.
