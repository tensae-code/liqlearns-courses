# Chiefdoms of East Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 1: Chiefdoms in East Africa”?
a) Beside the kingdoms stand the chiefdoms — Iteso, Kikuyu, Basoga, Acholi, Kamba, Masai, Chagga — each ruled by a chief, and below them the clan, headed by a clan leader who keeps the records.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 1: Chiefdoms in East Africa” is right?
a) Beside the kingdoms stand the chiefdoms — Iteso, Kikuyu, Basoga, Acholi, Kamba, Masai, Chagga — each ruled by a chief, and below them the clan, headed by a clan leader who keeps the records.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
