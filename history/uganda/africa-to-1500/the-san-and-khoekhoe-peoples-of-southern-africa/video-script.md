# Video script — The San and Khoekhoe Peoples of Southern Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The San may never have migrated from anywhere — remnants of the late Stone Age itself; they worship the creator Kaggen through stars, sun, moon and ancestors, dancing the movements of the animals they hunt.

On the map, follow this part: Term 2, History of Southern Africa since 1000 AD: The San. The San may never have migrated from anywhere — remnants of the late Stone Age itself; they worship the creator Kaggen through stars, sun, moon and ancestors, dancing the movements of the animals they hunt.

On the map, follow this part: Term 2: The Khoi Khoi. The herders who follow the San rear fat-tailed sheep and long-horned cattle; a groom lives with the bride's family until the first child, and the bride leaves with animals that remain hers.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
