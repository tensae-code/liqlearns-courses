# The San and Khoekhoe Peoples of Southern Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 2, History of Southern Africa since 1000 AD: The San; Term 2: The Khoi Khoi. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Term 2, History of Southern Africa since 1000 AD: The San

The San may never have migrated from anywhere — remnants of the late Stone Age itself; they worship the creator Kaggen through stars, sun, moon and ancestors, dancing the movements of the animals they hunt.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Term 2: The Khoi Khoi

The herders who follow the San rear fat-tailed sheep and long-horned cattle; a groom lives with the bride's family until the first child, and the bride leaves with animals that remain hers.
