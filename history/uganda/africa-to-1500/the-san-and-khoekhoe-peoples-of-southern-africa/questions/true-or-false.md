# The San and Khoekhoe Peoples of Southern Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The San may never have migrated from anywhere — remnants of the late Stone Age itself; they worship the creator Kaggen through stars, sun, moon and ancestors, dancing the movements of the animals they hunt. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2: The Khoi Khoi” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The herders who follow the San rear fat-tailed sheep and long-horned cattle; a groom lives with the bride's family until the first child, and the bride leaves with animals that remain hers.
