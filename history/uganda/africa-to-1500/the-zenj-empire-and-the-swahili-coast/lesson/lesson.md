# The Zenj Empire and the Swahili Coast

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 1: The Zenj empire; Chapter 7 (guide): Rise of the coastal states. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Term 1: The Zenj empire

"Zenj" means black — the land of the black people, founded on the coast by the Arab Hassan Bin Ali, an empire of trade more than of rule.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 7 (guide): Rise of the coastal states

The coastal states rise on recruitive profits, monsoon winds and overseas trade with the Middle East, India, Asia and Europe, with Cushitic middlemen linking coast to interior.
