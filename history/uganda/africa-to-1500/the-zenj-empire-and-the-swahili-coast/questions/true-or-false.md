# The Zenj Empire and the Swahili Coast — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** "Zenj" means black — the land of the black people, founded on the coast by the Arab Hassan Bin Ali, an empire of trade more than of rule. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Chapter 7 (guide): Rise of the coastal states” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The coastal states rise on recruitive profits, monsoon winds and overseas trade with the Middle East, India, Asia and Europe, with Cushitic middlemen linking coast to interior.
