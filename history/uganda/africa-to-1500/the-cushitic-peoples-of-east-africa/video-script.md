# Video script — The Cushitic Peoples of East Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing.
Drought and pasture shortage drive the Cushitic herders — Somali, Boran, Galla, Rendille — into East Africa.

On the map, follow this part: Term 1: The Cushites. Drought and pasture shortage drive the Cushitic herders — Somali, Boran, Galla, Rendille — into East Africa.
