# The Cushitic Peoples of East Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 1: The Cushites. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Term 1: The Cushites

Drought and pasture shortage drive the Cushitic herders — Somali, Boran, Galla, Rendille — into East Africa.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Drought and pasture shortage drive the Cushitic herders — Somali, Boran, Galla, Rendille — into East Africa. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Cushitic Peoples of East Africa. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
