# Mansa Musa and the Empire of Mali — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The reign of Mansa Musa (1307–1337)”?
a) Mansa Kankan Musa extends Mali north and east with a strong army, controls the gold trade, and draws Islamic scholars to an empire he works to purify in Islam.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The reign of Mansa Musa (1307–1337)” is right?
a) Mansa Kankan Musa extends Mali north and east with a strong army, controls the gold trade, and draws Islamic scholars to an empire he works to purify in Islam.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
