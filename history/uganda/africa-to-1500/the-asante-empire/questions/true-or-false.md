# The Asante Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Strong leaders raise Asante around Kumasi; the Asantehene rules a metropolitan core of 40 miles while provincial chiefs keep their thrones under him — and the empire falls as the strong leaders die and conquered states break away.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Asante Empire” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Strong leaders raise Asante around Kumasi; the Asantehene rules a metropolitan core of 40 miles while provincial chiefs keep their thrones under him — and the empire falls as the strong leaders die and conquered states break away.
