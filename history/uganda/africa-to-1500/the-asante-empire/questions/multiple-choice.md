# The Asante Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Asante Empire”?
a) Strong leaders raise Asante around Kumasi; the Asantehene rules a metropolitan core of 40 miles while provincial chiefs keep their thrones under him — and the empire falls as the strong leaders die and conquered states break away.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Asante Empire” is right?
a) Strong leaders raise Asante around Kumasi; the Asantehene rules a metropolitan core of 40 miles while provincial chiefs keep their thrones under him — and the empire falls as the strong leaders die and conquered states break away.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
