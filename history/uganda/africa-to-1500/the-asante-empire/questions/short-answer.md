# The Asante Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Asante Empire”?
**Answer:** Strong leaders raise Asante around Kumasi; the Asantehene rules a metropolitan core of 40 miles while provincial chiefs keep their thrones under him — and the empire falls as the strong leaders die and conquered states break away.
