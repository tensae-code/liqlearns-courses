# The Asante Empire

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Asante Empire. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The Asante Empire

Strong leaders raise Asante around Kumasi; the Asantehene rules a metropolitan core of 40 miles while provincial chiefs keep their thrones under him — and the empire falls as the strong leaders die and conquered states break away.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
