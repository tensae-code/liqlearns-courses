# The Bachwezi and the Chwezi Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 6: Political Organisation — the Chwezi empire”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 1: Political organisation of ethnic groups” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The Bachwezi's gifts are listed like a king's inventory — salt mining at Lake Katwe, iron smelting, long-horned cattle, coffee, bark cloth.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
