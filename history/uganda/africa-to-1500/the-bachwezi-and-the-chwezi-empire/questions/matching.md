# The Bachwezi and the Chwezi Empire — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Topic 6: Political Organisation — the Chwezi empire — a) (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Term 1: Political organisation of ethnic groups — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) The Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold.
