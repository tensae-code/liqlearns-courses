# The Bachwezi and the Chwezi Empire — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: Political organisation of ethnic groups” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The Bachwezi's gifts are listed like a king's inventory — salt mining at Lake Katwe, iron smelting, long-horned cattle, coffee, bark cloth.
