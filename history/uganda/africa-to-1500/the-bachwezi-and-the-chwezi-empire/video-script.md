# Video script — The Bachwezi and the Chwezi Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold.

On the map, follow this part: Topic 6: Political Organisation — the Chwezi empire. The Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold.

On the map, follow this part: Term 1: Political organisation of ethnic groups. The Bachwezi's gifts are listed like a king's inventory — salt mining at Lake Katwe, iron smelting, long-horned cattle, coffee, bark cloth.

On the map, follow this part: Term 2: Economic contributions of the Bachwezi. Coffee, iron working, pottery and the salt mines of Lake Katwe — the Bachwezi are remembered as teachers of crafts.

On the map, follow this part: Chapter 5: Bachwezi Dynasty. The Batembuzi, gods come from heaven, found Bunyoro; the Chwezi who follow prize long-horned cattle, grow coffee, beans and millet, make pottery, and play omweso in grass-thatched huts smeared with cow dung.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
