# The Bachwezi and the Chwezi Empire

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 6: Political Organisation — the Chwezi empire; Term 1: Political organisation of ethnic groups; Term 2: Economic contributions of the Bachwezi; Chapter 5: Bachwezi Dynasty. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 6: Political Organisation — the Chwezi empire

The Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Term 1: Political organisation of ethnic groups

The Bachwezi's gifts are listed like a king's inventory — salt mining at Lake Katwe, iron smelting, long-horned cattle, coffee, bark cloth.

## Sub lesson 3 — Term 2: Economic contributions of the Bachwezi

Coffee, iron working, pottery and the salt mines of Lake Katwe — the Bachwezi are remembered as teachers of crafts.

The Batembuzi, gods come from heaven, found Bunyoro; the Chwezi who follow prize long-horned cattle, grow coffee, beans and millet, make pottery, and play omweso in grass-thatched huts smeared with cow dung.
