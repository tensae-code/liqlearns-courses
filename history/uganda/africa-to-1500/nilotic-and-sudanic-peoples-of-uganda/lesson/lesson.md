# Nilotic and Sudanic Peoples of Uganda

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 6: Highland and Plain Nilotes; the Sudanic peoples. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic 6: Highland and Plain Nilotes; the Sudanic peoples

Drought, attacks, epidemics and land hunger push the Nilotes south; the Sudanic Lugbara, Madi, Okebu and Lendu flee civil wars into Uganda's north-west.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
