# Nilotic and Sudanic Peoples of Uganda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Drought, attacks, epidemics and land hunger push the Nilotes south; the Sudanic Lugbara, Madi, Okebu and Lendu flee civil wars into Uganda's north-west.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic 6: Highland and Plain Nilotes; the Sudanic peoples” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Drought, attacks, epidemics and land hunger push the Nilotes south; the Sudanic Lugbara, Madi, Okebu and Lendu flee civil wars into Uganda's north-west.
