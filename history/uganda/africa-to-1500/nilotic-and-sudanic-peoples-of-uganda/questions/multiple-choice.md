# Nilotic and Sudanic Peoples of Uganda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 6: Highland and Plain Nilotes; the Sudanic peoples”?
a) Drought, attacks, epidemics and land hunger push the Nilotes south; the Sudanic Lugbara, Madi, Okebu and Lendu flee civil wars into Uganda's north-west.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 6: Highland and Plain Nilotes; the Sudanic peoples” is right?
a) Drought, attacks, epidemics and land hunger push the Nilotes south; the Sudanic Lugbara, Madi, Okebu and Lendu flee civil wars into Uganda's north-west.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
