# Video script — Nilotic and Sudanic Peoples of Uganda

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Drought, attacks, epidemics and land hunger push the Nilotes south; the Sudanic Lugbara, Madi, Okebu and Lendu flee civil wars into Uganda's north-west.

On the map, follow this part: Topic 6: Highland and Plain Nilotes; the Sudanic peoples. Drought, attacks, epidemics and land hunger push the Nilotes south; the Sudanic Lugbara, Madi, Okebu and Lendu flee civil wars into Uganda's north-west.
