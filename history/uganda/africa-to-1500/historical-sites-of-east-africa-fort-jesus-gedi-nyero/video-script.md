# Video script — Historical Sites of East Africa: Fort Jesus, Gedi and Nyero

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Fort Jesus guards Mombasa, Olduvai's remains reach back 1.9 million years, Gedi flourishes on the Kenyan coast from the 13th to the 17th century, and the Nyero rock paintings mark Uganda's own deep past.

On the map, follow this part: Chapter 1: Historical sites in East Africa. Fort Jesus guards Mombasa, Olduvai's remains reach back 1.9 million years, Gedi flourishes on the Kenyan coast from the 13th to the 17th century, and the Nyero rock paintings mark Uganda's own deep past.
