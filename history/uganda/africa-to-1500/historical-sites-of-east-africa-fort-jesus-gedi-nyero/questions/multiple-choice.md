# Historical Sites of East Africa: Fort Jesus, Gedi and Nyero — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Chapter 1: Historical sites in East Africa”?
a) Fort Jesus guards Mombasa, Olduvai's remains reach back 1.9 million years, Gedi flourishes on the Kenyan coast from the 13th to the 17th century, and the Nyero rock paintings mark Uganda's own deep past.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Chapter 1: Historical sites in East Africa” is right?
a) Fort Jesus guards Mombasa, Olduvai's remains reach back 1.9 million years, Gedi flourishes on the Kenyan coast from the 13th to the 17th century, and the Nyero rock paintings mark Uganda's own deep past.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
