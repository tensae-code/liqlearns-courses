## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Topic 6: Buganda kingdom** — The Kabaka holds absolute power under crown, stool, spear and drum; Buganda grows stronger through the 1700s, and the legend of Kintu, first Muganda, teaches the kingdom's morals — later kabakas run from infant Daudi Chwa of the 1900 Agreement to Muteesa II, who refused an East African federation.
2. **Term 1: Growth of Buganda kingdom** — Strong leaders, unity, a standing army and a strategic shore on Lake Victoria make 18th-century Buganda expand.
3. **Term 2: Buganda Kingdom, how it was founded** — The Baganda divide into 52 clans named for animal, plant and bird totems, and believe in ancestral gods — Musoke, Dungu, Kibuuka, Mukasa.
4. **Chapter 5: Buganda Kingdom** — Kyabagu, Junju and Suna unite a small kingdom and restructure it — bataka clan chiefs guard the land, collect taxes, conscript the army and present page boys to the Kabaka, while the Balubaale spirits are consulted through mediums.
