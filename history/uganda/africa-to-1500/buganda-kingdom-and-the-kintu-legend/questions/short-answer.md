# Buganda Kingdom and the Kintu Legend — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 6: Buganda kingdom”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Kabaka holds absolute power under crown, stool, spear and drum; Buganda grows stronger through the 1700s, and the legend of Kintu, first Muganda, teaches the kingdom's morals — later kabakas run from infant Daudi Chwa of the 1900 Agreement to Muteesa II, who refused an East African federation.
