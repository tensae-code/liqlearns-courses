# Buganda Kingdom and the Kintu Legend — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Kabaka holds absolute power under crown, stool, spear and drum; Buganda grows stronger through the 1700s, and the legend of Kintu, first Muganda, teaches the kingdom's morals — later kabakas run from infant Daudi Chwa of the 1900 Agreement to Muteesa II, who refused an East African federation. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: Growth of Buganda kingdom” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Strong leaders, unity, a standing army and a strategic shore on Lake Victoria make 18th-century Buganda expand.
