# Cultural Leaders in Pre-Colonial Uganda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “SST Term 2: Roles of cultural leaders”?
a) Before the state, cultural leaders settle disputes, protect the community, unite the people and keep the ceremonies of birth, marriage and circumcision.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “SST Term 2: Roles of cultural leaders” is right?
a) Before the state, cultural leaders settle disputes, protect the community, unite the people and keep the ceremonies of birth, marriage and circumcision.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
