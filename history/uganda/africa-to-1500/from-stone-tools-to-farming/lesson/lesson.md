# From Stone Tools to Farming

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 6: The People of Pre-Colonial Uganda — early man; Term 1: The Stone Age; Chapter 2: The Origin of Man; Chapter 2 (guide): Stages of man's development. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 6: The People of Pre-Colonial Uganda — early man

Middle Stone Age man lives in caves, roasts meat, hunts with traps, sleeps under animal skins and tames the first animal, the dog.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Term 1: The Stone Age

Stone-age man hunts, gathers fruit, fishes and collects wild honey; his tools name his age, and the Leakeys' 1959 skull at Olduvai Gorge makes East Africa the classroom's cradle of mankind.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chapter 2: The Origin of Man

Dogs are the first animals kept at home, for hunting and cave protection; then come advanced tools and pottery — the Stone Age told as a series of firsts.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.
