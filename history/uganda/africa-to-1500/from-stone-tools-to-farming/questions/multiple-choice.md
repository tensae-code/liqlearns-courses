# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 6: The People of Pre-Colonial Uganda — early man”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Middle Stone Age man lives in caves, roasts meat, hunts with traps, sleeps under animal skins and tames the first animal, the dog.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 1: The Stone Age” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Stone-age man hunts, gathers fruit, fishes and collects wild honey; his tools name his age, and the Leakeys' 1959 skull at Olduvai Gorge makes East Africa the classroom's cradle of mankind.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
