# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Middle Stone Age man lives in caves, roasts meat, hunts with traps, sleeps under animal skins and tames the first animal, the dog. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: The Stone Age” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Stone-age man hunts, gathers fruit, fishes and collects wild honey; his tools name his age, and the Leakeys' 1959 skull at Olduvai Gorge makes East Africa the classroom's cradle of mankind.
