# Video script — From Stone Tools to Farming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Middle Stone Age man lives in caves, roasts meat, hunts with traps, sleeps under animal skins and tames the first animal, the dog.

On the map, follow this part: Topic 6: The People of Pre-Colonial Uganda — early man. Middle Stone Age man lives in caves, roasts meat, hunts with traps, sleeps under animal skins and tames the first animal, the dog.

On the map, follow this part: Term 1: The Stone Age. Stone-age man hunts, gathers fruit, fishes and collects wild honey; his tools name his age, and the Leakeys' 1959 skull at Olduvai Gorge makes East Africa the classroom's cradle of mankind.

On the map, follow this part: Chapter 2: The Origin of Man. Dogs are the first animals kept at home, for hunting and cave protection; then come advanced tools and pottery — the Stone Age told as a series of firsts.

On the map, follow this part: Chapter 2 (guide): Stages of man's development. Fire is invented, then domesticated plants lead to agriculture; the model answers send learners to Olduvai Gorge and to the elders outside school.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
