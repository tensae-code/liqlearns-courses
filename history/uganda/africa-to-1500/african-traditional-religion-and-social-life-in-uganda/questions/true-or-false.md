# African Traditional Religion and Social Life in Uganda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Before the foreign religions, each society names God its own way — Katonda for the Baganda, Rugaba for the Banyoro, Ruhanga for the Banyankole, Lokusuban for the Iteso — and clan ceremonies, taboos and traditional education hold the people together. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: Culture and religion” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: One God, many names on the classroom table — Katonda for Buganda, Ruhanga for Ankole, Were for Bugishu, Ngai for the Kikuyu.
