# African Traditional Religion and Social Life in Uganda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 6: Social Organisation”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Before the foreign religions, each society names God its own way — Katonda for the Baganda, Rugaba for the Banyoro, Ruhanga for the Banyankole, Lokusuban for the Iteso — and clan ceremonies, taboos and traditional education hold the people together.
