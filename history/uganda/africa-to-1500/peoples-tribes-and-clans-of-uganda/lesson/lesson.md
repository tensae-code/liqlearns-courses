# Peoples, Tribes and Clans of Uganda

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic: People in Our District; Lessons: Legends / Factors that Determine Settlement Patterns. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic: People in Our District; Lessons: Legends / Factors that Determine Settlement Patterns

A tribe shares one origin and language, a clan one forefather; drought, the search for water and for jobs drive migration, and fertile soil, rain, food and trade decide where people finally settle.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
