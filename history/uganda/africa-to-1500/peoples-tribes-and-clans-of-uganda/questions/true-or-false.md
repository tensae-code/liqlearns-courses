# Peoples, Tribes and Clans of Uganda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A tribe shares one origin and language, a clan one forefather; drought, the search for water and for jobs drive migration, and fertile soil, rain, food and trade decide where people finally settle.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic: People in Our District; Lessons: Legends / Factors that Determine Settlement Patterns” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: A tribe shares one origin and language, a clan one forefather; drought, the search for water and for jobs drive migration, and fertile soil, rain, food and trade decide where people finally settle.
