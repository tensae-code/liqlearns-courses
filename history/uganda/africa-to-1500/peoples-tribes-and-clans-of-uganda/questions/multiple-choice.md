# Peoples, Tribes and Clans of Uganda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic: People in Our District; Lessons: Legends / Factors that Determine Settlement Patterns”?
a) A tribe shares one origin and language, a clan one forefather; drought, the search for water and for jobs drive migration, and fertile soil, rain, food and trade decide where people finally settle.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: People in Our District; Lessons: Legends / Factors that Determine Settlement Patterns” is right?
a) A tribe shares one origin and language, a clan one forefather; drought, the search for water and for jobs drive migration, and fertile soil, rain, food and trade decide where people finally settle.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
