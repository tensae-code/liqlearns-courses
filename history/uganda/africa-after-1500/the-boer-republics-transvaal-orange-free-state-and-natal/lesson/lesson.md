# The Boer Republics: Transvaal, Orange Free State and Natal

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 3, Topic: The Boer Republics. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Term 3, Topic: The Boer Republics

Dingane falls to his half-brother Mpande, backed by Pretorius; the Sand River Convention (1852) frees the Transvaal, Bloemfontein (1854) the Orange Free State — but Natal, annexed by Britain in 1843, is the republic that does not last.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
