# The Boer Republics: Transvaal, Orange Free State and Natal — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Dingane falls to his half-brother Mpande, backed by Pretorius; the Sand River Convention (1852) frees the Transvaal, Bloemfontein (1854) the Orange Free State — but Natal, annexed by Britain in 1843, is the republic that does not last.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 3, Topic: The Boer Republics” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Dingane falls to his half-brother Mpande, backed by Pretorius; the Sand River Convention (1852) frees the Transvaal, Bloemfontein (1854) the Orange Free State — but Natal, annexed by Britain in 1843, is the republic that does not last.
