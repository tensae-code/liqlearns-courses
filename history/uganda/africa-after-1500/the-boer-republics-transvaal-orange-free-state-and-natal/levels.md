# The Boer Republics: Transvaal, Orange Free State and Natal — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Dingane falls to his half-brother Mpande, backed by Pretorius; the Sand River Convention (1852) frees the Transvaal, Bloemfontein (1854) the Orange Free State — but Natal, annexed by Britain in 1843, is the republic that does not last. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
