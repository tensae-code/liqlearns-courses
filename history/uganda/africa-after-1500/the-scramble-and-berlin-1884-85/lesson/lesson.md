## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 7: The Imperial British East Africa Company and the Scramble

In 1888 Britain charters the IBEA Company to administer its 1886 treaty share; Kabaka Mwanga begs the Company's Jackson for help in 1889, and Emin Pasha's Equatoria falls to Kitchener's Anglo-Egyptian force in 1898.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic 4: The scramble, partition and the Heligoland treaty

In 1890 Britain trades the Heligoland islands to Germany for a free hand in Uganda — an island in Europe swapped for a kingdom in Africa.
