# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1888 Britain charters the IBEA Company to administer its 1886 treaty share; Kabaka Mwanga begs the Company's Jackson for help in 1889, and Emin Pasha's Equatoria falls to Kitchener's Anglo-Egyptian force in 1898. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic 4: The scramble, partition and the Heligoland treaty” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: In 1890 Britain trades the Heligoland islands to Germany for a free hand in Uganda — an island in Europe swapped for a kingdom in Africa.
