# Indians and the Uganda Railway

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 7: Contributions of the Indians; Term 1: The Indians in East Africa; Chapter 8: Asians in East Africa; Term 2: The Uganda Railway. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 7: Contributions of the Indians

Allidina Visram opens Kampala's first shop, Indian coolies build the Kenya–Uganda railway, rupees become currency and Banyan moneylenders finance the new economy.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 4 — Term 2: The Uganda Railway

Britain wants Uganda as the Nile's source and for its cotton, richer than Kenya's; the line planned from 1892 kills those who refuse it passage — the Nandi leader Koitale among them — and grows towns on European and Indian businesses.
