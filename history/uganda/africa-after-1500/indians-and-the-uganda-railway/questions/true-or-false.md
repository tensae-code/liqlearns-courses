# Indians and the Uganda Railway — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Allidina Visram opens Kampala's first shop, Indian coolies build the Kenya–Uganda railway, rupees become currency and Banyan moneylenders finance the new economy. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: The Indians in East Africa” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The testing exercises ask why Indian coolies were brought to Uganda and which Indians became the money lenders — labour first, then the dukas.
