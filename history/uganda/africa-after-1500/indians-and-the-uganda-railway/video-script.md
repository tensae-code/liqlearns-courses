# Video script — Indians and the Uganda Railway

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.

On the map, follow this part: Chapter 8: Asians in East Africa. Besides the Europeans, the Asians take an interest in East Africa — the chapter plants them inside the colonisation story, not after it.

On the map, follow this part: Term 2: The Uganda Railway. Britain wants Uganda as the Nile's source and for its cotton, richer than Kenya's; the line planned from 1892 kills those who refuse it passage — the Nandi leader Koitale among them — and grows towns on European and Indian businesses.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
