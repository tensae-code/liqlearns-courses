**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Beginnings at Home: Legends, Peoples and Kingdoms of Uganda — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Topic 7: Contributions of the Indians** — Allidina Visram opens Kampala's first shop, Indian coolies build the Kenya–Uganda railway, rupees become currency and Banyan moneylenders finance the new economy.
2. **Term 1: The Indians in East Africa** — The testing exercises ask why Indian coolies were brought to Uganda and which Indians became the money lenders — labour first, then the dukas.
3. **Chapter 8: Asians in East Africa** — Besides the Europeans, the Asians take an interest in East Africa — the chapter plants them inside the colonisation story, not after it.
4. **Term 2: The Uganda Railway** — Britain wants Uganda as the Nile's source and for its cotton, richer than Kenya's; the line planned from 1892 kills those who refuse it passage — the Nandi leader Koitale among them — and grows towns on European and Indian businesses.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
