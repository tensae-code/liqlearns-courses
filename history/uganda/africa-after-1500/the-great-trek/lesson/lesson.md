# The Great Trek

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 3, Topic: The Great Trek. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Term 3, Topic: The Great Trek

From about 1835 the trekkers pack overnight and move under Trigardt, Potgieter, Retief, Maritz and Pretorius — fleeing English law, African interpreters in court, and the 1811 circuit laws that treated all people alike; at Vegkop 430 Matabele and 2 Boers die.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: From about 1835 the trekkers pack overnight and move under Trigardt, Potgieter, Retief, Maritz and Pretorius — fleeing English law, African interpreters in court, and the 1811 circuit laws that treated all people alike; at Vegkop 430 Matabele and 2 Boers die. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Great Trek. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
