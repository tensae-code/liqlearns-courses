# The Great Trek — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From about 1835 the trekkers pack overnight and move under Trigardt, Potgieter, Retief, Maritz and Pretorius — fleeing English law, African interpreters in court, and the 1811 circuit laws that treated all people alike; at Vegkop 430 Matabele and 2 Boers die.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 3, Topic: The Great Trek” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: From about 1835 the trekkers pack overnight and move under Trigardt, Potgieter, Retief, Maritz and Pretorius — fleeing English law, African interpreters in court, and the 1811 circuit laws that treated all people alike; at Vegkop 430 Matabele and 2 Boers die.
