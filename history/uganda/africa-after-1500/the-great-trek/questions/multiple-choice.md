# The Great Trek — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 3, Topic: The Great Trek”?
a) From about 1835 the trekkers pack overnight and move under Trigardt, Potgieter, Retief, Maritz and Pretorius — fleeing English law, African interpreters in court, and the 1811 circuit laws that treated all people alike; at Vegkop 430 Matabele and 2 Boers die.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 3, Topic: The Great Trek” is right?
a) From about 1835 the trekkers pack overnight and move under Trigardt, Potgieter, Retief, Maritz and Pretorius — fleeing English law, African interpreters in court, and the 1811 circuit laws that treated all people alike; at Vegkop 430 Matabele and 2 Boers die.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
