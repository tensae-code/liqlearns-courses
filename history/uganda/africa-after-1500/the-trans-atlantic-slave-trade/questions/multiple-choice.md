# The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Senior Four Term 1, Topic 1: The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade (Triangular Trade)”?
a) The trade begins in 1441 with the Portuguese on the West African coast; as the trans-Saharan trade declines, its traders move over in numbers, and chiefs and kings bring the captives down to the European buyers.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Senior Four Term 1, Topic 1: The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade (Triangular Trade)” is right?
a) The trade begins in 1441 with the Portuguese on the West African coast; as the trans-Saharan trade declines, its traders move over in numbers, and chiefs and kings bring the captives down to the European buyers.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
