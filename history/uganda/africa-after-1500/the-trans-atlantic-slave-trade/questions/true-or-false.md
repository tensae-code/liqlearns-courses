# The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The trade begins in 1441 with the Portuguese on the West African coast; as the trans-Saharan trade declines, its traders move over in numbers, and chiefs and kings bring the captives down to the European buyers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Senior Four Term 1, Topic 1: The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade (Triangular Trade)” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The trade begins in 1441 with the Portuguese on the West African coast; as the trans-Saharan trade declines, its traders move over in numbers, and chiefs and kings bring the captives down to the European buyers.
