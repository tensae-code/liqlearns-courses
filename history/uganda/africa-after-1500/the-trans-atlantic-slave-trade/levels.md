# The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The trade begins in 1441 with the Portuguese on the West African coast; as the trans-Saharan trade declines, its traders move over in numbers, and chiefs and kings bring the captives down to the European buyers. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
