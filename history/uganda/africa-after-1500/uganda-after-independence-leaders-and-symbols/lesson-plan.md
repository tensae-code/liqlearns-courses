## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Topic 10: Uganda as an Independent Nation** — The presidents file past — Obote twice, Amin, Lule's 68 days — Amin brings Muteesa's body home to Kasubi in 1972; the expelled Asians are allowed back, UPE and USE are introduced, and the flag, anthem and crested-crane coat of arms teach the new nation's meanings.
2. **SST Term 3, Topic: Uganda as an Independent Nation** — The flag's red is brotherhood, the coat of arms — designed by Cecil Todd — joins drum, spear and shield to coffee and cotton, and the child draws the crested crane and asks why it was chosen.
3. **Topic 4: Post-independence Uganda** — Muteesa II, Kabaka from 1939, dies in 1969; Amin expels the Asians in 1972 and rules by decree until exiles of the UNLF and Tanzania overthrow him in 1979; the leaders' list runs to Museveni, 1986 to date.
