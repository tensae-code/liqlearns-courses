# Uganda After Independence: Leaders and Symbols — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The presidents file past — Obote twice, Amin, Lule's 68 days — Amin brings Muteesa's body home to Kasubi in 1972; the expelled Asians are allowed back, UPE and USE are introduced, and the flag, anthem and crested-crane coat of arms teach the new nation's meanings. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “SST Term 3, Topic: Uganda as an Independent Nation” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The flag's red is brotherhood, the coat of arms — designed by Cecil Todd — joins drum, spear and shield to coffee and cotton, and the child draws the crested crane and asks why it was chosen.
