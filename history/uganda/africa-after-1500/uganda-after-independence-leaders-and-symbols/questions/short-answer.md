# Uganda After Independence: Leaders and Symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 10: Uganda as an Independent Nation”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The presidents file past — Obote twice, Amin, Lule's 68 days — Amin brings Muteesa's body home to Kasubi in 1972; the expelled Asians are allowed back, UPE and USE are introduced, and the flag, anthem and crested-crane coat of arms teach the new nation's meanings.
