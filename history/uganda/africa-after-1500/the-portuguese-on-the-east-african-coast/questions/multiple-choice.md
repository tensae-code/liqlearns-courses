# The Portuguese on the East African Coast — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 4: The Portuguese explorers and conquest”?
a) The Portuguese are the first Europeans here, hunting the sea route to India — Cabral fails at Sofala in 1500, da Gama captures the Zenj headquarters in 1502, and two centuries of coastal rule begin.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 4: The Portuguese explorers and conquest” is right?
a) The Portuguese are the first Europeans here, hunting the sea route to India — Cabral fails at Sofala in 1500, da Gama captures the Zenj headquarters in 1502, and two centuries of coastal rule begin.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
