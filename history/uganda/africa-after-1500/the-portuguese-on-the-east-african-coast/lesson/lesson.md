# The Portuguese on the East African Coast

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 4: The Portuguese explorers and conquest. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic 4: The Portuguese explorers and conquest

The Portuguese are the first Europeans here, hunting the sea route to India — Cabral fails at Sofala in 1500, da Gama captures the Zenj headquarters in 1502, and two centuries of coastal rule begin.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
