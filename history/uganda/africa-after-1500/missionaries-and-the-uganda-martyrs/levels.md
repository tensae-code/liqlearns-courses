# Missionaries and the Uganda Martyrs — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — CMS Protestants come first, Verona Fathers reach the north in 1910, Apollo Kivebulaya becomes "apostle to the pygmies" — and at Namugongo on 3 June 1886 Kizito and his fellow pages are burned, the Uganda Martyrs whose shrine still marks the walk to death. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
