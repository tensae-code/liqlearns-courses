# Missionaries and the Uganda Martyrs

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 7: Christian Missionaries in Uganda. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic 7: Christian Missionaries in Uganda

CMS Protestants come first, Verona Fathers reach the north in 1910, Apollo Kivebulaya becomes "apostle to the pygmies" — and at Namugongo on 3 June 1886 Kizito and his fellow pages are burned, the Uganda Martyrs whose shrine still marks the walk to death.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
