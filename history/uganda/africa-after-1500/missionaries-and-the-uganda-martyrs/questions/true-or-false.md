# Missionaries and the Uganda Martyrs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** CMS Protestants come first, Verona Fathers reach the north in 1910, Apollo Kivebulaya becomes "apostle to the pygmies" — and at Namugongo on 3 June 1886 Kizito and his fellow pages are burned, the Uganda Martyrs whose shrine still marks the walk to death.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic 7: Christian Missionaries in Uganda” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: CMS Protestants come first, Verona Fathers reach the north in 1910, Apollo Kivebulaya becomes "apostle to the pygmies" — and at Namugongo on 3 June 1886 Kizito and his fellow pages are burned, the Uganda Martyrs whose shrine still marks the walk to death.
