# Missionaries and the Uganda Martyrs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 7: Christian Missionaries in Uganda”?
**Answer:** CMS Protestants come first, Verona Fathers reach the north in 1910, Apollo Kivebulaya becomes "apostle to the pygmies" — and at Namugongo on 3 June 1886 Kizito and his fellow pages are burned, the Uganda Martyrs whose shrine still marks the walk to death.
