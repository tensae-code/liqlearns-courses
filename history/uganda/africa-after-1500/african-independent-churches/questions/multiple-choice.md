# African Independent Churches — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 3: Independent churches”?
a) Followers who see the gap between the missionaries' preaching and practice break away — Isaiah Shembe's Nazareth Baptist Church in KwaZulu-Natal leads the lesson's examples.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 3: Independent churches” is right?
a) Followers who see the gap between the missionaries' preaching and practice break away — Isaiah Shembe's Nazareth Baptist Church in KwaZulu-Natal leads the lesson's examples.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
