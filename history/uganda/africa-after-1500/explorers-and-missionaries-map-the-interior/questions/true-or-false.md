# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Speke leaves the sick Grant at King Rumanika's Karagwe and reaches Kabaka Muteesa I's palace; Stanley comes three times, first hunting the missing Livingstone for two newspapers — and the explorers rename Nalubaale "Lake Victoria" and open the way to missionaries. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “SST Term 2: Foreign groups and the explorers' journeys” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The child draws the explorers' routes across the sketch map of Uganda and names the foreign groups who followed them in.
