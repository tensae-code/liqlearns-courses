## Sub lesson 3 — Topic 4: European exploration in East Africa

Speke and Burton come first into the interior; Grant falls sick at Karagwe under King Rumanika's care while Speke pushes on into Buganda; Bellefonds meets Stanley at the Kabaka's palace in April 1875 and backs his call for missionaries.

The explorers exaggerate what they saw, the traders sign treaties the home governments later use to occupy East Africa, and the Africans who welcomed them "with open hands" did not know what the acts would lead to.
