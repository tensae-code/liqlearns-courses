# The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Slaves are got by raiding villages, buying war captives from chiefs and seizing the lonely; Livingstone campaigns against the trade in Parliament's hearing, Seyyid Said helps the British fight the traders at sea, and Sharp and Clarkson organise abolition. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 1: Slave trade in northern Uganda” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Sir Samuel Baker builds Fort Patiko to shield Acholi from the Khartoumer slave traders, yet the trade resists abolition because kings and chiefs grow rich on it.
