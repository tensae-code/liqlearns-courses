Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 7: The Slave Trade and its Abolition”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Slaves are got by raiding villages, buying war captives from chiefs and seizing the lonely; Livingstone campaigns against the trade in Parliament's hearing, Seyyid Said helps the British fight the traders at sea, and Sharp and Clarkson organise abolition.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
