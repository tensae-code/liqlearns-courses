# The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 7: The Slave Trade and its Abolition
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Slaves are got by raiding villages, buying war captives from chiefs and seizing the lonely; Livingstone campaigns against the trade in Parliament's hearing, Seyyid Said helps the British fight the traders at sea, and Sharp and Clarkson organise abolition. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sir Samuel Baker builds Fort Patiko to shield Acholi from the Khartoumer slave traders, yet the trade resists abolition because kings and chiefs grow rich on it. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Domestic slavery was already in the family culture when Arab and Persian traders made it a commerce; slaves carry the ivory to the coast and are sold themselves; the Masai fight the traders off, but families are broken and whole districts depopulated. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.
