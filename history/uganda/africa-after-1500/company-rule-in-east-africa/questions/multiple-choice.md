# Company Rule in East Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 4: Trading companies”?
a) IBEACO and the German East Africa Company rule first, but too few administrators and missionary opposition end company rule.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 4: Trading companies” is right?
a) IBEACO and the German East Africa Company rule first, but too few administrators and missionary opposition end company rule.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
