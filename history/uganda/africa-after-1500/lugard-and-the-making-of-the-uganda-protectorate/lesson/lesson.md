# Lugard and the Making of the Uganda Protectorate

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 4: British rule in Uganda. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic 4: British rule in Uganda

Uganda is ruled 1888–1894 by a company, IBEACO, personified by Captain Frederick Lugard (in Uganda Dec 1890–May 1892); commissioners follow, Coryndon forms LEGCO in 1921 and Archer opens Makerere.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
