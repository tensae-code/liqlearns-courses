# Lugard and the Making of the Uganda Protectorate — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Uganda is ruled 1888–1894 by a company, IBEACO, personified by Captain Frederick Lugard (in Uganda Dec 1890–May 1892); commissioners follow, Coryndon forms LEGCO in 1921 and Archer opens Makerere. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
