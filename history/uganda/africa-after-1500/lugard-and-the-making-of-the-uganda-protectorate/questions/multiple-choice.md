# Lugard and the Making of the Uganda Protectorate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 4: British rule in Uganda”?
a) Uganda is ruled 1888–1894 by a company, IBEACO, personified by Captain Frederick Lugard (in Uganda Dec 1890–May 1892); commissioners follow, Coryndon forms LEGCO in 1921 and Archer opens Makerere.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 4: British rule in Uganda” is right?
a) Uganda is ruled 1888–1894 by a company, IBEACO, personified by Captain Frederick Lugard (in Uganda Dec 1890–May 1892); commissioners follow, Coryndon forms LEGCO in 1921 and Archer opens Makerere.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
