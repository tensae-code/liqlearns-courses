Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Uganda is ruled 1888–1894 by a company, IBEACO, personified by Captain Frederick Lugard (in Uganda Dec 1890–May 1892); commissioners follow, Coryndon forms LEGCO in 1921 and Archer opens Makerere.
