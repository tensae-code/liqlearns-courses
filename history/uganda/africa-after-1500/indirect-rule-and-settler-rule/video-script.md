On the map, follow this part: Topic 4: Indirect rule. The book weighs indirect rule fairly — Africans learn new administration and keep their kingdoms and cultures; the colonialists get cheap, quiet rule.

On the map, follow this part: Senior Four Term 1: Indirect rule in West Africa. Britain rules cheaply through chiefs content with gifts of outdated guns and second-hand cloth, leaving the Emirs their tax powers so the system is welcomed.
