Match each part with its story.

**1.** Topic 8: Colonial Administration — Indirect Rule — a) (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** SST Term 2: Indirect and direct rule — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:

b) Indirect rule works through the kingdoms of Buganda, Tooro and Ankole without disturbing local leaders; where there was resistance — Kigezi, Bugisu, West Nile, Bunyoro — the British rule directly, through Baganda agents.
