# Indirect Rule and Settler Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Captain Lugard introduces indirect rule — local leaders supervise cash crops and collect taxes for the British; natives learn leadership and modern law, and lose their independence. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “SST Term 2: Indirect and direct rule” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Indirect rule works through the kingdoms of Buganda, Tooro and Ankole without disturbing local leaders; where there was resistance — Kigezi, Bugisu, West Nile, Bunyoro — the British rule directly, through Baganda agents.
