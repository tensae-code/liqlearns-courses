# Indirect Rule and Settler Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 8: Colonial Administration — Indirect Rule”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Captain Lugard introduces indirect rule — local leaders supervise cash crops and collect taxes for the British; natives learn leadership and modern law, and lose their independence.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “SST Term 2: Indirect and direct rule” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Indirect rule works through the kingdoms of Buganda, Tooro and Ankole without disturbing local leaders; where there was resistance — Kigezi, Bugisu, West Nile, Bunyoro — the British rule directly, through Baganda agents.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
