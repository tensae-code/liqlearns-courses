## Do it first

Hatred of German rule and heavy taxation unite Matumbi, Bena, Ngoni, Pogoro, Zaramo and Luguru in joint attacks from 30 August; German scorched earth destroys farms and livestock — and the survivors' lesson becomes TANU, the party that finally wins independence.
