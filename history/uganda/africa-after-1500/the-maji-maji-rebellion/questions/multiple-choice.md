# The Maji-Maji Rebellion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 4: The Maji-Maji rebellion”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Tanganyika's fiercest rising, 1905–1907, unites peoples against German rule.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2; Lesson 13: The Maji-Maji Rebellion, Tanganyika” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Hatred of German rule and heavy taxation unite Matumbi, Bena, Ngoni, Pogoro, Zaramo and Luguru in joint attacks from 30 August; German scorched earth destroys farms and livestock — and the survivors' lesson becomes TANU, the party that finally wins independence.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
