# The Maji-Maji Rebellion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tanganyika's fiercest rising, 1905–1907, unites peoples against German rule. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2; Lesson 13: The Maji-Maji Rebellion, Tanganyika” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Hatred of German rule and heavy taxation unite Matumbi, Bena, Ngoni, Pogoro, Zaramo and Luguru in joint attacks from 30 August; German scorched earth destroys farms and livestock — and the survivors' lesson becomes TANU, the party that finally wins independence.
