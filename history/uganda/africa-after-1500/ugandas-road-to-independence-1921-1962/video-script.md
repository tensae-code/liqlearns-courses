# Video script — Uganda's Road to Independence, 1921–1962

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
LEGCO is formed in 1921 under Governor Coryndon at European businessmen's prompting; parties follow — DP in 1954, UPU in 1959 — the world wars shake the empire, the 1961 London conference promises elections and a referendum on Bunyoro's lost counties, and self-government comes in 1962.
