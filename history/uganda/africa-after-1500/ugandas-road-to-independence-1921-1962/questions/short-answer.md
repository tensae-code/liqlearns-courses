# Uganda's Road to Independence, 1921–1962 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 9: LEGCO, Parties and Independence”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** LEGCO is formed in 1921 under Governor Coryndon at European businessmen's prompting; parties follow — DP in 1954, UPU in 1959 — the world wars shake the empire, the 1961 London conference promises elections and a referendum on Bunyoro's lost counties, and self-government comes in 1962.
