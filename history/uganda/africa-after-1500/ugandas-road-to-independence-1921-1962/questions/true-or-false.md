# Uganda's Road to Independence, 1921–1962 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** LEGCO is formed in 1921 under Governor Coryndon at European businessmen's prompting; parties follow — DP in 1954, UPU in 1959 — the world wars shake the empire, the 1961 London conference promises elections and a referendum on Bunyoro's lost counties, and self-government comes in 1962. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “SST Term 2: Formation of the Legislative Council” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: LEGCO is formed, the first Africans join it in 1945, and the council becomes a school of the independence struggle.
