# Uganda's Road to Independence, 1921–1962 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 9: LEGCO, Parties and Independence”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) LEGCO is formed in 1921 under Governor Coryndon at European businessmen's prompting; parties follow — DP in 1954, UPU in 1959 — the world wars shake the empire, the 1961 London conference promises elections and a referendum on Bunyoro's lost counties, and self-government comes in 1962.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
