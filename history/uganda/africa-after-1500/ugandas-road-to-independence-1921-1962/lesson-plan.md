## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Topic 9: LEGCO, Parties and Independence** — LEGCO is formed in 1921 under Governor Coryndon at European businessmen's prompting; parties follow — DP in 1954, UPU in 1959 — the world wars shake the empire, the 1961 London conference promises elections and a referendum on Bunyoro's lost counties, and self-government comes in 1962.
2. **SST Term 2: Formation of the Legislative Council** — LEGCO is formed, the first Africans join it in 1945, and the council becomes a school of the independence struggle.
3. **SST Term 2, Lesson 3: Groups and individuals that led the struggle for national independence** — Four names carry the struggle — Ignatius Kangave Musaazi, Benedicto Kiwanuka, Dr Apollo Milton Obote and Sir Edward Muteesa II.
4. **Topic 4: Uganda's administrators and LEGCO** — The Namirembe Agreement is signed for Buganda by Michael Kintu; Governor Crawford organises Uganda's first general elections and completes the parliament building.
5. **Topic 4: Uganda's road and Ignatius Musaazi** — Uganda wins independence by peaceful struggle where Kenya fought; Musaazi founds the African Farmers' Union in 1947 and the Uganda National Congress, the first national party, in March 1952.
