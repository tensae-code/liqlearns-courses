# Early Attempts at East African Federation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Term 2: Attempts at East African unification”?
**Answer:** Lugard tries to merge Kenya and Uganda in 1896, Johnston in 1900, Eliot in 1905 — all fail; the Baganda, fearing for their privileged position under the 1900 Agreement, oppose under Muteesa, and Tanganyika's governor opposes too.
