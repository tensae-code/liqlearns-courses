# Early Attempts at East African Federation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 2: Attempts at East African unification”?
a) Lugard tries to merge Kenya and Uganda in 1896, Johnston in 1900, Eliot in 1905 — all fail; the Baganda, fearing for their privileged position under the 1900 Agreement, oppose under Muteesa, and Tanganyika's governor opposes too.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2: Attempts at East African unification” is right?
a) Lugard tries to merge Kenya and Uganda in 1896, Johnston in 1900, Eliot in 1905 — all fail; the Baganda, fearing for their privileged position under the 1900 Agreement, oppose under Muteesa, and Tanganyika's governor opposes too.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
