# Early Attempts at East African Federation — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Lugard tries to merge Kenya and Uganda in 1896, Johnston in 1900, Eliot in 1905 — all fail; the Baganda, fearing for their privileged position under the 1900 Agreement, oppose under Muteesa, and Tanganyika's governor opposes too. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
