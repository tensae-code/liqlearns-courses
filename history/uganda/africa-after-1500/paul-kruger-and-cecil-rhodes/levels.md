# Paul Kruger and Cecil Rhodes — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Kruger, president of the Transvaal for four terms, fights Africans and then the British in 1880–81 and 1899–1900 before fleeing — a life lived, the book says, in love and defence of his people. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
