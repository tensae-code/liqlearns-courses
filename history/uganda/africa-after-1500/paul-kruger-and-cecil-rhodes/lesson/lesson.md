# Paul Kruger and Cecil Rhodes

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 3; Lesson 4: Key Personalities. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Term 3; Lesson 4: Key Personalities

Kruger, president of the Transvaal for four terms, fights Africans and then the British in 1880–81 and 1899–1900 before fleeing — a life lived, the book says, in love and defence of his people.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Paul Kruger and Cecil Rhodes. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
