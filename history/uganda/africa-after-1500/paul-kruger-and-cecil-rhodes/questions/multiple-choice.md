# Paul Kruger and Cecil Rhodes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 3; Lesson 4: Key Personalities”?
a) Kruger, president of the Transvaal for four terms, fights Africans and then the British in 1880–81 and 1899–1900 before fleeing — a life lived, the book says, in love and defence of his people.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 3; Lesson 4: Key Personalities” is right?
a) Kruger, president of the Transvaal for four terms, fights Africans and then the British in 1880–81 and 1899–1900 before fleeing — a life lived, the book says, in love and defence of his people.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
