# German Rule in Tanganyika and the British Mandate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** German rule is hated for cruelty and forced plantation labour; after the war the British expel German settlers, replace Jumbes and Akidas with traditional chiefs, and rename the country Tanganyika. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2; Lesson 15: Colonial Administration in Tanganyika (Direct Rule)” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The Germans rule directly — by force, an 1891 railway to the coast, and missionaries whose "beautiful" preaching softens hearts — because, having tasted Tanganyikan resistance, they dare not rule indirectly.
