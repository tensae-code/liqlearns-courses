# German Rule in Tanganyika and the British Mandate

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 4: Tanganyika changes hands; Term 2; Lesson 15: Colonial Administration in Tanganyika (Direct Rule). As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 4: Tanganyika changes hands

German rule is hated for cruelty and forced plantation labour; after the war the British expel German settlers, replace Jumbes and Akidas with traditional chiefs, and rename the country Tanganyika.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Term 2; Lesson 15: Colonial Administration in Tanganyika (Direct Rule)

The Germans rule directly — by force, an 1891 railway to the coast, and missionaries whose "beautiful" preaching softens hearts — because, having tasted Tanganyikan resistance, they dare not rule indirectly.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.
