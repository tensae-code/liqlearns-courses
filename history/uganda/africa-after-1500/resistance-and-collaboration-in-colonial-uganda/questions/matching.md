# Resistance and Collaboration in Colonial Uganda — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Topic 9: The Road to Independence — Resistance and Collaborators — a) (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Topic 4: Semei Kakungulu — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Kabaka Mwanga, Omukama Kabalega and Chief Awich resist while Ankole's premier Nuwa Mbaguta extends British rule and builds roads and schools; in 1907 the Banyoro rise in the Nyangire ("I have refused") rebellion against Baganda chiefs ruling them for the British.
