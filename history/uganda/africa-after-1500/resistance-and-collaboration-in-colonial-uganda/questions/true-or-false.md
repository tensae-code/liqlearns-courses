# Resistance and Collaboration in Colonial Uganda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kabaka Mwanga, Omukama Kabalega and Chief Awich resist while Ankole's premier Nuwa Mbaguta extends British rule and builds roads and schools; in 1907 the Banyoro rise in the Nyangire ("I have refused") rebellion against Baganda chiefs ruling them for the British. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic 4: Semei Kakungulu” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Kakungulu plants Baganda agents as chiefs across the east and north, helps Colonel Colville capture Mwanga and Kabalega in Lango in 1899, and tries to push British rule into Karamoja.
