# Resistance and Collaboration in Colonial Uganda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 9: The Road to Independence — Resistance and Collaborators”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Kabaka Mwanga, Omukama Kabalega and Chief Awich resist while Ankole's premier Nuwa Mbaguta extends British rule and builds roads and schools; in 1907 the Banyoro rise in the Nyangire ("I have refused") rebellion against Baganda chiefs ruling them for the British.
