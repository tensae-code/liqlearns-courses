# Resistance and Collaboration in Colonial Uganda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 9: The Road to Independence — Resistance and Collaborators”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Kabaka Mwanga, Omukama Kabalega and Chief Awich resist while Ankole's premier Nuwa Mbaguta extends British rule and builds roads and schools; in 1907 the Banyoro rise in the Nyangire ("I have refused") rebellion against Baganda chiefs ruling them for the British.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 4: Semei Kakungulu” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Kakungulu plants Baganda agents as chiefs across the east and north, helps Colonel Colville capture Mwanga and Kabalega in Lango in 1899, and tries to push British rule into Karamoja.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
