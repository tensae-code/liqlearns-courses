# Resistance and Collaboration in Colonial Uganda — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 9: The Road to Independence — Resistance and Collaborators
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Kabaka Mwanga, Omukama Kabalega and Chief Awich resist while Ankole's premier Nuwa Mbaguta extends British rule and builds roads and schools; in 1907 the Banyoro rise in the Nyangire ("I have refused") rebellion against Baganda chiefs ruling them for the British. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic 4: Semei Kakungulu
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Kakungulu plants Baganda agents as chiefs across the east and north, helps Colonel Colville capture Mwanga and Kabalega in Lango in 1899, and tries to push British rule into Karamoja. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Topic 4: Reactions to colonial rule in East Africa
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The class sorts the names into two columns — collaborators Kaggwa, Kakungulu, Mbaguta, Mumia; resisters Mwanga, Kabalega, Chief Awich. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — Topic: The Road to Independence
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Africans react to foreign rule in two directions, and the newly schooled generation finds the natural resources suddenly hard to exploit without factories. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

## Sub lesson 5 — Chapter 9, Topic: Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Collaborators hope for guns and rewards; resisters rise over land, imposed culture and treaties they were cheated in translation — and lose, the book says, to weak economies, internal wars, missionary teaching, and betrayal like the Jumbe Magoya's. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

## Sub lesson 6 — Chapter 9: Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Muteesa I collaborates for firearms against his western enemies, Nabongo Mumia and Laibon Lenana calculate likewise, while Mwanga resists at once; Kakungulu then extends British rule over the rest of Uganda for them. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

## Sub lesson 7 — Chapter 9: Results of the response
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Resistance ends in destroyed villages and food stores, white brutality in Bunyoro and Hehe land, and a lost confidence in the traditional leaders and medicine men who "falsely led the masses". — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

## Sub lesson 8 — Chapter 9 (guide): Why neighbours chose differently
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Buganda collaborates because Bunyoro has resisted; Toro collaborates because Ankole has; and Kakungulu exports the whole Kiganda system of administration into eastern Uganda. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.
