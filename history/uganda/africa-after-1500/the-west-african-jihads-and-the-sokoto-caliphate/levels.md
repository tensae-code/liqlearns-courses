# The West African Jihads and the Sokoto Caliphate — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Uthman dan Fodio, Fulani preacher and scholar, leads the first jihad in Hausa land against unfair courts and impure practice; his successor Mohammed Bello builds the Sokoto Caliphate's Quranic schools and mosques. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
