# The West African Jihads and the Sokoto Caliphate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Senior Four Term 1: The 19th-century jihads”?
a) Uthman dan Fodio, Fulani preacher and scholar, leads the first jihad in Hausa land against unfair courts and impure practice; his successor Mohammed Bello builds the Sokoto Caliphate's Quranic schools and mosques.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Senior Four Term 1: The 19th-century jihads” is right?
a) Uthman dan Fodio, Fulani preacher and scholar, leads the first jihad in Hausa land against unfair courts and impure practice; his successor Mohammed Bello builds the Sokoto Caliphate's Quranic schools and mosques.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
