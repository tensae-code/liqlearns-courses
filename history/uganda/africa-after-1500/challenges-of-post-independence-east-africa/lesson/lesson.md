# Challenges of Post-Independence East Africa

Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 4: Challenges facing post-independence East African countries; Topic: The Road to Independence. ## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 4: Challenges facing post-independence East African countries

The new states inherit illiteracy, poor transport, civil wars, coups and human-rights violations — the independence generation's unfinished work.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic: The Road to Independence

After independence the region's economies stall — little to export, low income, poverty and poor living standards are the inheritance the lessons name.
