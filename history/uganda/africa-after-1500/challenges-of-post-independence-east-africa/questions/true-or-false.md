# Challenges of Post-Independence East Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The new states inherit illiteracy, poor transport, civil wars, coups and human-rights violations — the independence generation's unfinished work. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic: The Road to Independence” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: After independence the region's economies stall — little to export, low income, poverty and poor living standards are the inheritance the lessons name.
