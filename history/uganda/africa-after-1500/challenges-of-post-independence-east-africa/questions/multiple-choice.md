# Challenges of Post-Independence East Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 4: Challenges facing post-independence East African countries”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The new states inherit illiteracy, poor transport, civil wars, coups and human-rights violations — the independence generation's unfinished work.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: The Road to Independence” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) After independence the region's economies stall — little to export, low income, poverty and poor living standards are the inheritance the lessons name.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
