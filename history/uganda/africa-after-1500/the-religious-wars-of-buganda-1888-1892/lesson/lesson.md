# The Religious Wars of Buganda, 1888–1892

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Chapter 6: Results of foreign religions. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Chapter 6: Results of foreign religions

Converts divide into Wafranza (Catholics) and Wangereza (Protestants) and fight; Lugard silences the wars in 1893 and the Protestants triumph in Uganda's politics "up to now"; Namugongo's 3rd June is remembered yearly.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
