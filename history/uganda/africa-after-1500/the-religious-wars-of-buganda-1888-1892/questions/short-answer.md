# The Religious Wars of Buganda, 1888–1892 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Chapter 6: Results of foreign religions”?
**Answer:** Converts divide into Wafranza (Catholics) and Wangereza (Protestants) and fight; Lugard silences the wars in 1893 and the Protestants triumph in Uganda's politics "up to now"; Namugongo's 3rd June is remembered yearly.
