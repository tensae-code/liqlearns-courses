# The Religious Wars of Buganda, 1888–1892 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Chapter 6: Results of foreign religions”?
a) Converts divide into Wafranza (Catholics) and Wangereza (Protestants) and fight; Lugard silences the wars in 1893 and the Protestants triumph in Uganda's politics "up to now"; Namugongo's 3rd June is remembered yearly.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Chapter 6: Results of foreign religions” is right?
a) Converts divide into Wafranza (Catholics) and Wangereza (Protestants) and fight; Lugard silences the wars in 1893 and the Protestants triumph in Uganda's politics "up to now"; Namugongo's 3rd June is remembered yearly.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
