# The Religious Wars of Buganda, 1888–1892 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Converts divide into Wafranza (Catholics) and Wangereza (Protestants) and fight; Lugard silences the wars in 1893 and the Protestants triumph in Uganda's politics "up to now"; Namugongo's 3rd June is remembered yearly.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Chapter 6: Results of foreign religions” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
