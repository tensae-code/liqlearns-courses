# The Nandi Resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 2: The Nandi Resistance”?
a) The Nandi, who have beaten all their neighbours including the Maasai, are inspired by their Orkoiyot, a symbol of unity with supernatural powers — and pay for resistance in depopulation.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2: The Nandi Resistance” is right?
a) The Nandi, who have beaten all their neighbours including the Maasai, are inspired by their Orkoiyot, a symbol of unity with supernatural powers — and pay for resistance in depopulation.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
