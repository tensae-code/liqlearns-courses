# The Nandi Resistance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Nandi, who have beaten all their neighbours including the Maasai, are inspired by their Orkoiyot, a symbol of unity with supernatural powers — and pay for resistance in depopulation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2: The Nandi Resistance” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
