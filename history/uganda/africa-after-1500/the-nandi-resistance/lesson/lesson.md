# The Nandi Resistance

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 2: The Nandi Resistance. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Term 2: The Nandi Resistance

The Nandi, who have beaten all their neighbours including the Maasai, are inspired by their Orkoiyot, a symbol of unity with supernatural powers — and pay for resistance in depopulation.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Nandi, who have beaten all their neighbours including the Maasai, are inspired by their Orkoiyot, a symbol of unity with supernatural powers — and pay for resistance in depopulation. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Nandi Resistance. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
