# Moshesh and the Sotho Nation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Moshesh, born 1786, learns statecraft from the elder Mohlomi, holds his mountain kingdom against Zulu, Ndebele and Boer, then defeats the British in battle and wins the peace with a diplomat's letter to Cathcart.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2; Lesson 8: The Sotho Nation” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Moshesh, born 1786, learns statecraft from the elder Mohlomi, holds his mountain kingdom against Zulu, Ndebele and Boer, then defeats the British in battle and wins the peace with a diplomat's letter to Cathcart.
