# Moshesh and the Sotho Nation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 2; Lesson 8: The Sotho Nation”?
a) Moshesh, born 1786, learns statecraft from the elder Mohlomi, holds his mountain kingdom against Zulu, Ndebele and Boer, then defeats the British in battle and wins the peace with a diplomat's letter to Cathcart.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2; Lesson 8: The Sotho Nation” is right?
a) Moshesh, born 1786, learns statecraft from the elder Mohlomi, holds his mountain kingdom against Zulu, Ndebele and Boer, then defeats the British in battle and wins the peace with a diplomat's letter to Cathcart.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
