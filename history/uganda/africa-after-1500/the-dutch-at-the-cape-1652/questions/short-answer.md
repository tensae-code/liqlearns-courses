# The Dutch at the Cape, 1652 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Term 2: Dutch settlement at the Cape”?
**Answer:** The Dutch plant themselves at the Cape in 1652 for fresh water and food for sailors bound for the Far East; the company that rules them turns harsh, disorganised and corrupt, its courts judging in its own favour.
