# The Dutch at the Cape, 1652 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 2: Dutch settlement at the Cape”?
a) The Dutch plant themselves at the Cape in 1652 for fresh water and food for sailors bound for the Far East; the company that rules them turns harsh, disorganised and corrupt, its courts judging in its own favour.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2: Dutch settlement at the Cape” is right?
a) The Dutch plant themselves at the Cape in 1652 for fresh water and food for sailors bound for the Far East; the company that rules them turns harsh, disorganised and corrupt, its courts judging in its own favour.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
