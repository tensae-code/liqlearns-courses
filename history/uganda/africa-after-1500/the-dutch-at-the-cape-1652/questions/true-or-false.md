# The Dutch at the Cape, 1652 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Dutch plant themselves at the Cape in 1652 for fresh water and food for sailors bound for the Far East; the company that rules them turns harsh, disorganised and corrupt, its courts judging in its own favour.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2: Dutch settlement at the Cape” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The Dutch plant themselves at the Cape in 1652 for fresh water and food for sailors bound for the Far East; the company that rules them turns harsh, disorganised and corrupt, its courts judging in its own favour.
