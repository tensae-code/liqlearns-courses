# The 1900 Buganda Agreement and Uganda's Boundaries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 8: How Uganda Became a Nation”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
