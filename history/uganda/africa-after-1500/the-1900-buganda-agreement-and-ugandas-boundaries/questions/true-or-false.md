# The 1900 Buganda Agreement and Uganda's Boundaries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “SST Term 2: How Uganda became a nation” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Before the treaties, the British form the Imperial British East Africa Company; then agreements are signed with the kings of Buganda, Ankole, Bunyoro and Toro, one kingdom at a time.
