# The 1900 Buganda Agreement and Uganda's Boundaries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 8: How Uganda Became a Nation”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910.
