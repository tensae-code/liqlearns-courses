# The 1900 Buganda Agreement and Uganda's Boundaries — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Topic 8: How Uganda Became a Nation — a) (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** SST Term 2: How Uganda became a nation — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) The 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910.
b) Before the treaties, the British form the Imperial British East Africa Company; then agreements are signed with the kings of Buganda, Ankole, Bunyoro and Toro, one kingdom at a time.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
