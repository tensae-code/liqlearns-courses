# The 1900 Buganda Agreement and Uganda's Boundaries — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 8: How Uganda Became a Nation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.
