The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

Johnston signs with the three regents of the child Kabaka Daudi Chwa II — Kagwa, Mugwanya, Kisingiri; Buganda expects British guns against Bunyoro and Egypt, and the Kabaka keeps his throne as "His Highness" — under close British supervision.

On the map, follow this part: Term 2; Lesson 10: The Buganda Agreement. The agreement exists in two versions, English and Luganda — and where they conflict, the English one rules; it is also signed to settle Buganda's boundary quarrel with Bunyoro after Kabalega's rebellion.
