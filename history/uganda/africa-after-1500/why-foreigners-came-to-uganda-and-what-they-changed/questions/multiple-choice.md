# Why Foreigners Came to Uganda and What They Changed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “SST Term 2: Reasons why foreigners came; their influence and contributions”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Pupils weigh the foreigners' positive and negative contributions themselves — new farming methods and tools against overused resources, undermined culture and new divisions.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: The Road to Independence in East Africa; Lesson: Positive and negative contributions of explorers, missionaries and colonialists” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The foreigners' ledger is balanced at home — new products and schools against over-exploited minerals and diseases like smallpox — and the elders are the witnesses.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
