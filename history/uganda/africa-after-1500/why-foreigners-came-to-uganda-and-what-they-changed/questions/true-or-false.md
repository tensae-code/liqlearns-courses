# Why Foreigners Came to Uganda and What They Changed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils weigh the foreigners' positive and negative contributions themselves — new farming methods and tools against overused resources, undermined culture and new divisions. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic: The Road to Independence in East Africa; Lesson: Positive and negative contributions of explorers, missionaries and colonialists” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The foreigners' ledger is balanced at home — new products and schools against over-exploited minerals and diseases like smallpox — and the elders are the witnesses.
