# Abolition and the Founding of Sierra Leone and Liberia — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Britain declares the trade illegal first; Sierra Leone and Liberia are founded as homes for freed slaves from England, Jamaica and America, whose Creole settlers publish the Sierra Leone Weekly News and raise Bishop Ajayi Crowther. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
