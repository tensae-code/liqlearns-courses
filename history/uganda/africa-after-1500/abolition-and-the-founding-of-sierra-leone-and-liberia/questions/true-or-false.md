# Abolition and the Founding of Sierra Leone and Liberia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain declares the trade illegal first; Sierra Leone and Liberia are founded as homes for freed slaves from England, Jamaica and America, whose Creole settlers publish the Sierra Leone Weekly News and raise Bishop Ajayi Crowther.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Senior Four Term 1: Abolition and the freed slaves” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Britain declares the trade illegal first; Sierra Leone and Liberia are founded as homes for freed slaves from England, Jamaica and America, whose Creole settlers publish the Sierra Leone Weekly News and raise Bishop Ajayi Crowther.
