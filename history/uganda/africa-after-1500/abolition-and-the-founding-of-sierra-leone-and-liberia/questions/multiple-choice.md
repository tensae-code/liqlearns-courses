# Abolition and the Founding of Sierra Leone and Liberia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Senior Four Term 1: Abolition and the freed slaves”?
a) Britain declares the trade illegal first; Sierra Leone and Liberia are founded as homes for freed slaves from England, Jamaica and America, whose Creole settlers publish the Sierra Leone Weekly News and raise Bishop Ajayi Crowther.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Senior Four Term 1: Abolition and the freed slaves” is right?
a) Britain declares the trade illegal first; Sierra Leone and Liberia are founded as homes for freed slaves from England, Jamaica and America, whose Creole settlers publish the Sierra Leone Weekly News and raise Bishop Ajayi Crowther.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
