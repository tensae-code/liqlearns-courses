# Abolition and the Founding of Sierra Leone and Liberia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Senior Four Term 1: Abolition and the freed slaves”?
**Answer:** Britain declares the trade illegal first; Sierra Leone and Liberia are founded as homes for freed slaves from England, Jamaica and America, whose Creole settlers publish the Sierra Leone Weekly News and raise Bishop Ajayi Crowther.
