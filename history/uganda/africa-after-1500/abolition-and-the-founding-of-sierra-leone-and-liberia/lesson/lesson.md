# Abolition and the Founding of Sierra Leone and Liberia

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Senior Four Term 1: Abolition and the freed slaves. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Senior Four Term 1: Abolition and the freed slaves

Britain declares the trade illegal first; Sierra Leone and Liberia are founded as homes for freed slaves from England, Jamaica and America, whose Creole settlers publish the Sierra Leone Weekly News and raise Bishop Ajayi Crowther.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
