# Kwame Nkrumah and Ghana's Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Senior Four Term 1: The role of Dr Kwame Nkrumah. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Senior Four Term 1: The role of Dr Kwame Nkrumah

Nkrumah, a goldsmith's son of the Nzima born in 1909, forms the Convention People's Party in 1949, mobilises Ghana as one people, and wins independence in 1957.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
