# Kwame Nkrumah and Ghana's Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Senior Four Term 1: The role of Dr Kwame Nkrumah”?
a) Nkrumah, a goldsmith's son of the Nzima born in 1909, forms the Convention People's Party in 1949, mobilises Ghana as one people, and wins independence in 1957.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Senior Four Term 1: The role of Dr Kwame Nkrumah” is right?
a) Nkrumah, a goldsmith's son of the Nzima born in 1909, forms the Convention People's Party in 1949, mobilises Ghana as one people, and wins independence in 1957.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
