# Kwame Nkrumah and Ghana's Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nkrumah, a goldsmith's son of the Nzima born in 1909, forms the Convention People's Party in 1949, mobilises Ghana as one people, and wins independence in 1957.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Senior Four Term 1: The role of Dr Kwame Nkrumah” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
