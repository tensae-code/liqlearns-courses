# The Egyptian Penetration of Northern Uganda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 1: Egyptian penetration”?
a) Egypt pushes south for the Nile, the climate and the fertile soils; Emin Pasha and Sir Samuel Baker are the imperialists the learner must research.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 1: Egyptian penetration” is right?
a) Egypt pushes south for the Nile, the climate and the fertile soils; Emin Pasha and Sir Samuel Baker are the imperialists the learner must research.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
