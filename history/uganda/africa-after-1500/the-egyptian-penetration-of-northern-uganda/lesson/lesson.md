# The Egyptian Penetration of Northern Uganda

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 1: Egyptian penetration. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Term 1: Egyptian penetration

Egypt pushes south for the Nile, the climate and the fertile soils; Emin Pasha and Sir Samuel Baker are the imperialists the learner must research.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
