# Kenya's Road to Independence and Mau Mau — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kenya's LEGCO of 1907 serves the white settlers until Eliud Mathu becomes its first African member in 1944; the Mau-Mau rise to liberate the land, end the colour bar and the Kipande system, and force independence.
**Answer: True**
