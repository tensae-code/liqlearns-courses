# Kenya's Road to Independence and Mau Mau — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 4: Kenya's LEGCO and the Mau-Mau”?
a) Kenya's LEGCO of 1907 serves the white settlers until Eliud Mathu becomes its first African member in 1944; the Mau-Mau rise to liberate the land, end the colour bar and the Kipande system, and force independence.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 4: Kenya's LEGCO and the Mau-Mau” is right?
a) Kenya's LEGCO of 1907 serves the white settlers until Eliud Mathu becomes its first African member in 1944; the Mau-Mau rise to liberate the land, end the colour bar and the Kipande system, and force independence.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
