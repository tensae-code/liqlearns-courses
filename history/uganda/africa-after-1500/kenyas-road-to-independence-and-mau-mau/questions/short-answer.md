# Kenya's Road to Independence and Mau Mau — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Topic 4: Kenya's LEGCO and the Mau-Mau”?
**Answer:** Kenya's LEGCO of 1907 serves the white settlers until Eliud Mathu becomes its first African member in 1944; the Mau-Mau rise to liberate the land, end the colour bar and the Kipande system, and force independence.
