# Arab Traders in Uganda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Arab traders come for ivory, gold, ostrich feathers and slaves, and leave Islam, new goods and the cowrie currency behind.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic 7: Foreign Influence — Early Foreign (Arab) Traders” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Arab traders come for ivory, gold, ostrich feathers and slaves, and leave Islam, new goods and the cowrie currency behind.
