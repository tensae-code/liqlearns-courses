# The Mfecane — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mfecane means "crushing" — Dingiswayo borrows European lessons from the Cape to remake the Mthethwa, population pressure does the rest, and the open country is left virtually deserted.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Term 2, Topic: The Mfecane” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Mfecane means "crushing" — Dingiswayo borrows European lessons from the Cape to remake the Mthethwa, population pressure does the rest, and the open country is left virtually deserted.
