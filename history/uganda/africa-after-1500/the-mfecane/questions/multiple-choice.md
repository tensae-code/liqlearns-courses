# The Mfecane — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 2, Topic: The Mfecane”?
a) Mfecane means "crushing" — Dingiswayo borrows European lessons from the Cape to remake the Mthethwa, population pressure does the rest, and the open country is left virtually deserted.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2, Topic: The Mfecane” is right?
a) Mfecane means "crushing" — Dingiswayo borrows European lessons from the Cape to remake the Mthethwa, population pressure does the rest, and the open country is left virtually deserted.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
