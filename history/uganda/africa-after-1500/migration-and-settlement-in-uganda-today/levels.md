# Migration and Settlement in Uganda Today — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Today's migrant moves village-to-town, town-to-village and every way between for jobs, land, water and safety — the old migration story in modern dress. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
