# Migration and Settlement in Uganda Today — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 6: Migration of People Today; Settlement Patterns”?
a) Today's migrant moves village-to-town, town-to-village and every way between for jobs, land, water and safety — the old migration story in modern dress.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 6: Migration of People Today; Settlement Patterns” is right?
a) Today's migrant moves village-to-town, town-to-village and every way between for jobs, land, water and safety — the old migration story in modern dress.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
