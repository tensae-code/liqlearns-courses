# Uganda Becomes a British Protectorate, 1894 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Portal goes home, recommends the protectorate and dies of typhoid at 36; on 18 June 1894 Uganda is declared a British Protectorate, and governors from Jackson of the First World War to Crawford of the first general elections administer it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic 7: Uganda under British Colonial Rule” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Portal goes home, recommends the protectorate and dies of typhoid at 36; on 18 June 1894 Uganda is declared a British Protectorate, and governors from Jackson of the First World War to Crawford of the first general elections administer it.
