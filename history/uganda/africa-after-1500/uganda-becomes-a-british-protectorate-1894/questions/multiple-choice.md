# Uganda Becomes a British Protectorate, 1894 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 7: Uganda under British Colonial Rule”?
a) Portal goes home, recommends the protectorate and dies of typhoid at 36; on 18 June 1894 Uganda is declared a British Protectorate, and governors from Jackson of the First World War to Crawford of the first general elections administer it.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 7: Uganda under British Colonial Rule” is right?
a) Portal goes home, recommends the protectorate and dies of typhoid at 36; on 18 June 1894 Uganda is declared a British Protectorate, and governors from Jackson of the First World War to Crawford of the first general elections administer it.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
