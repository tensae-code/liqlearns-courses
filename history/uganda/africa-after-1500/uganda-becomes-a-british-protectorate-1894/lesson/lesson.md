# Uganda Becomes a British Protectorate, 1894

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 7: Uganda under British Colonial Rule. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic 7: Uganda under British Colonial Rule

Portal goes home, recommends the protectorate and dies of typhoid at 36; on 18 June 1894 Uganda is declared a British Protectorate, and governors from Jackson of the First World War to Crawford of the first general elections administer it.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
