# The Hehe Rebellion and Chief Mkwawa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic 4: The Hehe Rebellion. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic 4: The Hehe Rebellion

Chief Mkwawa's Wahehe defy the Germans from 1891; cornered, Mkwawa hangs himself, and the Germans cut off his head and take it to Germany.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Chief Mkwawa's Wahehe defy the Germans from 1891; cornered, Mkwawa hangs himself, and the Germans cut off his head and take it to Germany. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Hehe Rebellion and Chief Mkwawa. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
