# The Hehe Rebellion and Chief Mkwawa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Chief Mkwawa's Wahehe defy the Germans from 1891; cornered, Mkwawa hangs himself, and the Germans cut off his head and take it to Germany.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic 4: The Hehe Rebellion” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Chief Mkwawa's Wahehe defy the Germans from 1891; cornered, Mkwawa hangs himself, and the Germans cut off his head and take it to Germany.
