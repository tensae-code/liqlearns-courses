# The Hehe Rebellion and Chief Mkwawa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic 4: The Hehe Rebellion”?
a) Chief Mkwawa's Wahehe defy the Germans from 1891; cornered, Mkwawa hangs himself, and the Germans cut off his head and take it to Germany.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 4: The Hehe Rebellion” is right?
a) Chief Mkwawa's Wahehe defy the Germans from 1891; cornered, Mkwawa hangs himself, and the Germans cut off his head and take it to Germany.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
