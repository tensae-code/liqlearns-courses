# British Rule at the Cape and Boer Grievances — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Britain loosens the old restrictions, the Somerset proclamation of 1823 cuts Africans' working hours and lets Christian slaves attend church — and every reform is counted by the Boers in lost man-hours. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
