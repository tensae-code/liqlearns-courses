# British Rule at the Cape and Boer Grievances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 2: The British at the Cape”?
a) Britain loosens the old restrictions, the Somerset proclamation of 1823 cuts Africans' working hours and lets Christian slaves attend church — and every reform is counted by the Boers in lost man-hours.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 2: The British at the Cape” is right?
a) Britain loosens the old restrictions, the Somerset proclamation of 1823 cuts Africans' working hours and lets Christian slaves attend church — and every reform is counted by the Boers in lost man-hours.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
