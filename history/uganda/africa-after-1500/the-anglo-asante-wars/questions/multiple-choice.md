# The Anglo-Asante Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The British–Asante wars”?
a) The Dutch hand Elmina to the British, who stop paying the Asante rent and block their coastal trade; the wars that follow cost Britain so much it sends a committee to investigate.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The British–Asante wars” is right?
a) The Dutch hand Elmina to the British, who stop paying the Asante rent and block their coastal trade; the wars that follow cost Britain so much it sends a committee to investigate.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
