# The Anglo-Asante Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Dutch hand Elmina to the British, who stop paying the Asante rent and block their coastal trade; the wars that follow cost Britain so much it sends a committee to investigate.
**Answer: True**
