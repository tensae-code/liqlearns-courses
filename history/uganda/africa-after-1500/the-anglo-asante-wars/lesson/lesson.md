# The Anglo-Asante Wars

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The British–Asante wars. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Topic: Empires of Western Sudan: The British–Asante wars

The Dutch hand Elmina to the British, who stop paying the Asante rent and block their coastal trade; the wars that follow cost Britain so much it sends a committee to investigate.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Anglo-Asante Wars. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
