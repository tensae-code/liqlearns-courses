# Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: SST Term 2: Effects of the colonial administrative system; Topic 4: Effects of colonial rule on East Africa's economy; Topic 9: Economic, Social and Political Developments during the Colonial Period. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — SST Term 2: Effects of the colonial administrative system

Taxation is introduced, resources are overused, language, religion and indigenous education are undermined, and divisions are created among Uganda's people.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Topic 4: Effects of colonial rule on East Africa's economy

Roads, industries and new currency on one side; heavy taxes, collapsed local industries and over-exploited resources on the other.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — Topic 9: Economic, Social and Political Developments during the Colonial Period

Cotton seed is distributed in 1904 and ginneries rise; the Mill Hill Fathers build Uganda's first school, Namilyango College, in 1902, to teach chiefs' sons.
