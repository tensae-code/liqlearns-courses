# Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “SST Term 2: Effects of the colonial administrative system”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Taxation is introduced, resources are overused, language, religion and indigenous education are undermined, and divisions are created among Uganda's people.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Topic 4: Effects of colonial rule on East Africa's economy” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Roads, industries and new currency on one side; heavy taxes, collapsed local industries and over-exploited resources on the other.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
