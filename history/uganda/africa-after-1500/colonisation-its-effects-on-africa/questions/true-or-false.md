# Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Taxation is introduced, resources are overused, language, religion and indigenous education are undermined, and divisions are created among Uganda's people. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** The story of “Topic 4: Effects of colonial rule on East Africa's economy” teaches the opposite of what this lesson says. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Roads, industries and new currency on one side; heavy taxes, collapsed local industries and over-exploited resources on the other.
