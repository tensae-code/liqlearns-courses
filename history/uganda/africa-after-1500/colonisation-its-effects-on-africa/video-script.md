# Video script — Colonisation: Its Effects on Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Taxation is introduced, resources are overused, language, religion and indigenous education are undermined, and divisions are created among Uganda's people.

On the map, follow this part: SST Term 2: Effects of the colonial administrative system. Taxation is introduced, resources are overused, language, religion and indigenous education are undermined, and divisions are created among Uganda's people.

On the map, follow this part: Topic 4: Effects of colonial rule on East Africa's economy. Roads, industries and new currency on one side; heavy taxes, collapsed local industries and over-exploited resources on the other.

On the map, follow this part: Topic 9: Economic, Social and Political Developments during the Colonial Period. Cotton seed is distributed in 1904 and ginneries rise; the Mill Hill Fathers build Uganda's first school, Namilyango College, in 1902, to teach chiefs' sons.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
