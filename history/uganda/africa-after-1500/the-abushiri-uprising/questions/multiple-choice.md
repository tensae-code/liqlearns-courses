# The Abushiri Uprising — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Chapter 9: Coastal resistance”?
a) The coastal tribes resist the abolition of the slave trade itself — it fetched them profits — and rise in the Abushiri uprising.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Chapter 9: Coastal resistance” is right?
a) The coastal tribes resist the abolition of the slave trade itself — it fetched them profits — and rise in the Abushiri uprising.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
