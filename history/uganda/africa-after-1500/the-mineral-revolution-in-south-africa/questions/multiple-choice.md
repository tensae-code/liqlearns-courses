# The Mineral Revolution in South Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Term 3, Topic: The Mineral Revolution”?
a) Diamonds at Kimberley in 1867, two years before Suez, reverse the interior's fortunes — Africans had been doing better than the white interior communities; now Kimberley and Johannesburg rise, slums with them, and the newcomers are named Uitlanders.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Term 3, Topic: The Mineral Revolution” is right?
a) Diamonds at Kimberley in 1867, two years before Suez, reverse the interior's fortunes — Africans had been doing better than the white interior communities; now Kimberley and Johannesburg rise, slums with them, and the newcomers are named Uitlanders.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
