# The Mineral Revolution in South Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 3, Topic: The Mineral Revolution. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Term 3, Topic: The Mineral Revolution

Diamonds at Kimberley in 1867, two years before Suez, reverse the interior's fortunes — Africans had been doing better than the white interior communities; now Kimberley and Johannesburg rise, slums with them, and the newcomers are named Uitlanders.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
