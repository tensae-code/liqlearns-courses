# Shaka and the Zulu Nation

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Term 2: The rise of the Zulu nation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Term 2: The rise of the Zulu nation

Shaka's most significant chiefs are military indunas with no blood tie to the king — loyalty by merit, discipline by design — and the king himself an "unequalled dictator".

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it.
