# The Rise of African Nationalism — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The ex-servicemen of the Second World War come home having seen that "even the white man cries and can die"; Ethiopia's defeat of Italy in 1935–41 inspires the continent, and the OAU backs the last liberations. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
