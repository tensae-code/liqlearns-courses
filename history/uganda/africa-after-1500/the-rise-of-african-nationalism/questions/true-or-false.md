# The Rise of African Nationalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The ex-servicemen of the Second World War come home having seen that "even the white man cries and can die"; Ethiopia's defeat of Italy in 1935–41 inspires the continent, and the OAU backs the last liberations.
