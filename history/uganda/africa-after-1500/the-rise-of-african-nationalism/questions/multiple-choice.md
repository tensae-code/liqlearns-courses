# The Rise of African Nationalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Senior Four Term 1, Topic: The Rise of Nationalism in West Africa”?
a) The ex-servicemen of the Second World War come home having seen that "even the white man cries and can die"; Ethiopia's defeat of Italy in 1935–41 inspires the continent, and the OAU backs the last liberations.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Ugandan way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Senior Four Term 1, Topic: The Rise of Nationalism in West Africa” is right?
a) The ex-servicemen of the Second World War come home having seen that "even the white man cries and can die"; Ethiopia's defeat of Italy in 1935–41 inspires the continent, and the OAU backs the last liberations.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Uganda's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
