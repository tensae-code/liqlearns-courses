# The East African Community: Rise, Collapse and Revival — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — The High Commission of 1946 becomes EACSO, then the Community of Amin, Kenyatta and Nyerere's portraits collapses — trade declines, movement is restricted, enmity grows — and is revived for common taxes, peace and unity. — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
