# History — Iraq course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Iraq's own — built from Iraq's Social Studies and History student books (Arabic). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside their levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the child's own yesterday first, then Mesopotamia in the land the child lives in, then the Islamic cities and Iraq province by province, then the ancient world whole, Arabia and the Prophet, modern Iraq's founding, Arab-Islamic civilisation as a system, Europe and America, and last Iraq and the Arab world in colonialism and liberation. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
