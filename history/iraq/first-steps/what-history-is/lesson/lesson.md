# What History Is

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The benefits of studying history; history is the time that has passed us; Ch.1 The rise of ancient civilisations: why study them; Ch.1 The concept of civilisation, urban life and culture. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — The benefits of studying history; history is the time that has passed us

Pupils learn that knowing why they passed last year teaches them how to succeed this year, and that others' mistakes are lessons not to repeat.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.
