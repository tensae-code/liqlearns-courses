# Video script — What History Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Pupils learn that knowing why they passed last year teaches them how to succeed this year, and that others' mistakes are lessons not to repeat.

On the map, follow this part: The benefits of studying history; history is the time that has passed us. Pupils learn that knowing why they passed last year teaches them how to succeed this year, and that others' mistakes are lessons not to repeat.

On the map, follow this part: Ch.1 The rise of ancient civilisations: why study them. The pupil is told civilisation history recovers humanity's heritage and lets the present learn from past experiments before the story even begins.

On the map, follow this part: Ch.1 The concept of civilisation, urban life and culture. Before any story, the pupil must separate civilisation from mere city life and culture, the frame for everything that follows.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
