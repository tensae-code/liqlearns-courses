# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The benefits of studying history; history is the time that has passed us”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Pupils learn that knowing why they passed last year teaches them how to succeed this year, and that others' mistakes are lessons not to repeat.

**2.** What came of “Ch.1 The concept of civilisation, urban life and culture”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Before any story, the pupil must separate civilisation from mere city life and culture, the frame for everything that follows.
