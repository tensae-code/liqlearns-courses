# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The benefits of studying history; history is the time that has passed us”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Pupils learn that knowing why they passed last year teaches them how to succeed this year, and that others' mistakes are lessons not to repeat.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Ch.1 The rise of ancient civilisations: why study them” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The pupil is told civilisation history recovers humanity's heritage and lets the present learn from past experiments before the story even begins.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
