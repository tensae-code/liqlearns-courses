# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pupils learn that knowing why they passed last year teaches them how to succeed this year, and that others' mistakes are lessons not to repeat. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.1 The concept of civilisation, urban life and culture” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Before any story, the pupil must separate civilisation from mere city life and culture, the frame for everything that follows.
