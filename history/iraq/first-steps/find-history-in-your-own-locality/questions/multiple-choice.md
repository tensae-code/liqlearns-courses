# Find History in Your Own Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Activity: collect pictures of historical remains and name them”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The pupil becomes the historian, gathering photographs of antiquities and labelling each one in a personal notebook.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Historical sketch: Duhok governorate” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Mountain Duhok's old towns and citadels are set before the pupil as the landmarks of home to be found and recorded.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
