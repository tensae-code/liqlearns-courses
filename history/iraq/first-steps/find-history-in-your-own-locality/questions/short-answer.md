# Find History in Your Own Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Activity: collect pictures of historical remains and name them”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The pupil becomes the historian, gathering photographs of antiquities and labelling each one in a personal notebook.

**2.** What came of “Historical sketch: Duhok governorate”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Mountain Duhok's old towns and citadels are set before the pupil as the landmarks of home to be found and recorded.
