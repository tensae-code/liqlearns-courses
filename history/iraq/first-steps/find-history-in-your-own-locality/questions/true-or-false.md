# Find History in Your Own Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The pupil becomes the historian, gathering photographs of antiquities and labelling each one in a personal notebook. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Historical sketch: Duhok governorate” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Mountain Duhok's old towns and citadels are set before the pupil as the landmarks of home to be found and recorded.
