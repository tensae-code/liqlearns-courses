# Find History in Your Own Locality

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Activity: collect pictures of historical remains and name them; Historical sketch: Duhok governorate. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Activity: collect pictures of historical remains and name them

The pupil becomes the historian, gathering photographs of antiquities and labelling each one in a personal notebook.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Historical sketch: Duhok governorate

Mountain Duhok's old towns and citadels are set before the pupil as the landmarks of home to be found and recorded.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the pupil becomes the historian, gathering photographs of antiquities and labelling each one in a personal notebook. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Find History in Your Own Locality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the pupil becomes the historian, gathering photographs of antiquities and labelling each one in a personal notebook.
