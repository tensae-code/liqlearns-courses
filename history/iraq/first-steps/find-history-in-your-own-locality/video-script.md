# Video script — Find History in Your Own Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The pupil becomes the historian, gathering photographs of antiquities and labelling each one in a personal notebook.

On the map, follow this part: Activity: collect pictures of historical remains and name them. The pupil becomes the historian, gathering photographs of antiquities and labelling each one in a personal notebook.

On the map, follow this part: Historical sketch: Duhok governorate. Mountain Duhok's old towns and citadels are set before the pupil as the landmarks of home to be found and recorded.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
