# Dating the Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Historical concepts: yesterday, last week, last month, last year; Gregorian (solar) and Hijri (lunar) months”?
a) A boy is asked what he did yesterday, and the class counts back through his week, month and year to learn how the past is measured.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Historical concepts: yesterday, last week, last month, last year; Gregorian (solar) and Hijri (lunar) months” is right?
a) A boy is asked what he did yesterday, and the class counts back through his week, month and year to learn how the past is measured.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
