# Dating the Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Historical concepts: yesterday, last week, last month, last year; Gregorian (solar) and Hijri (lunar) months”?
**Answer:** A boy is asked what he did yesterday, and the class counts back through his week, month and year to learn how the past is measured.
