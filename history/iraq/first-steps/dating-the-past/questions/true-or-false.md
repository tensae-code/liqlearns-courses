# Dating the Past — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A boy is asked what he did yesterday, and the class counts back through his week, month and year to learn how the past is measured.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Historical concepts: yesterday, last week, last month, last year; Gregorian (solar) and Hijri (lunar) months” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: A boy is asked what he did yesterday, and the class counts back through his week, month and year to learn how the past is measured.
