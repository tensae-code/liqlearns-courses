# Video script — Dating the Past

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A boy is asked what he did yesterday, and the class counts back through his week, month and year to learn how the past is measured.

On the map, follow this part: Historical concepts: yesterday, last week, last month, last year; Gregorian (solar) and Hijri (lunar) months. A boy is asked what he did yesterday, and the class counts back through his week, month and year to learn how the past is measured.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
