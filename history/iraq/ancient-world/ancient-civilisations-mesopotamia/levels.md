# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Iraq's old cities: Ur and Hatra
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Ur rises in the south in the 3rd millennium BC, remembered as Abraham's birthplace, its ziggurat still standing; Hatra, city of the sun south-west of Mosul, keeps its temples and statues. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In the south, Sumer invents cuneiform writing, raises ziggurats above the flood plain and writes laws to protect people's rights. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example from the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Babylonian civilisation and Hammurabi
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files.
