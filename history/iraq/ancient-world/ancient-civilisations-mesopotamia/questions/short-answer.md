# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Iraq's old cities: Ur and Hatra”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ur rises in the south in the 3rd millennium BC, remembered as Abraham's birthplace, its ziggurat still standing; Hatra, city of the sun south-west of Mosul, keeps its temples and statues.

**2.** What came of “Ch.1 The civilisations before: Mesopotamia, Egypt, the Levant and others”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 24)
**Answer:** A survey runs from Mesopotamia and Egypt through the Levant to the Arabs before Islam, setting the stage the new civilisation inherits.
