# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ur rises in the south in the 3rd millennium BC, remembered as Abraham's birthplace, its ziggurat still standing; Hatra, city of the sun south-west of Mosul, keeps its temples and statues. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.1 The civilisations before: Mesopotamia, Egypt, the Levant and others” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 24)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: A survey runs from Mesopotamia and Egypt through the Levant to the Arabs before Islam, setting the stage the new civilisation inherits.
