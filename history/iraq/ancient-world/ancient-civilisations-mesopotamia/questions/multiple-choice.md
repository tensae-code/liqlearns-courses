# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Iraq's old cities: Ur and Hatra”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Ur rises in the south in the 3rd millennium BC, remembered as Abraham's birthplace, its ziggurat still standing; Hatra, city of the sun south-west of Mosul, keeps its temples and statues.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Iraq, land of civilisations: the Sumerians” is right? (Sub lesson 2)
a) In the south, Sumer invents cuneiform writing, raises ziggurats above the flood plain and writes laws to protect people's rights.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
