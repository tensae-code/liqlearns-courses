# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Iraq's old cities: Ur and Hatra — a) (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Iraq, land of civilisations: the Sumerians — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Ur rises in the south in the 3rd millennium BC, remembered as Abraham's birthplace, its ziggurat still standing; Hatra, city of the sun south-west of Mosul, keeps its temples and statues.
b) In the south, Sumer invents cuneiform writing, raises ziggurats above the flood plain and writes laws to protect people's rights.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
