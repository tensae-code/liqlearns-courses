# Video script — Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Iranian tribes enter the plateau in the early 1st millennium bc.

On the map, follow this part: Ch.5 Neighbours: Persia. Iranian tribes enter the plateau in the early 1st millennium BC; Achaemenid empire and Sassanian society follow, with Zoroastrian and older cults, noble and priestly classes, Aramaic across the empire, and Ctesiphon's great arch.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
