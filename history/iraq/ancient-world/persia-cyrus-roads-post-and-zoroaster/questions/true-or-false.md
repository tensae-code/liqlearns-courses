# Persia: Cyrus, Roads, Post and Zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Iranian tribes enter the plateau in the early 1st millennium BC; Achaemenid empire and Sassanian society follow, with Zoroastrian and older cults, noble and priestly classes, Aramaic across the empire, and Ctesiphon's great arch.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.5 Neighbours: Persia” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Iranian tribes enter the plateau in the early 1st millennium BC; Achaemenid empire and Sassanian society follow, with Zoroastrian and older cults, noble and priestly classes, Aramaic across the empire, and Ctesiphon's great arch.
