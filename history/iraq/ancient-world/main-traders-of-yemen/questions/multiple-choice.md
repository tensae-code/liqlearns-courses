# Ma'in, Traders of Yemen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Ma'in, the traders' kingdom”?
a) Among Yemen's oldest kingdoms (c.1300–630 BC), Ma'in in al-Jawf monopolises the north–south incense road and learns writing to keep its trade accounts.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Ma'in, the traders' kingdom” is right?
a) Among Yemen's oldest kingdoms (c.1300–630 BC), Ma'in in al-Jawf monopolises the north–south incense road and learns writing to keep its trade accounts.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
