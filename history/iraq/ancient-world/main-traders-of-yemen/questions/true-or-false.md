# Ma'in, Traders of Yemen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Among Yemen's oldest kingdoms (c.1300–630 BC), Ma'in in al-Jawf monopolises the north–south incense road and learns writing to keep its trade accounts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ma'in, the traders' kingdom” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Among Yemen's oldest kingdoms (c.1300–630 BC), Ma'in in al-Jawf monopolises the north–south incense road and learns writing to keep its trade accounts.
