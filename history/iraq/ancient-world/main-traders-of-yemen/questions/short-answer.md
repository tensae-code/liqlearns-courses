# Ma'in, Traders of Yemen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Ma'in, the traders' kingdom”?
**Answer:** Among Yemen's oldest kingdoms (c.1300–630 BC), Ma'in in al-Jawf monopolises the north–south incense road and learns writing to keep its trade accounts.
