# Video script — Baghdad: House of Wisdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Al-mansur's palace library grows under harun al-rashid and al-ma'mun into the house of wisdom.

On the map, follow this part: Bayt al-Hikma and the houses of knowledge. Al-Mansur's palace library grows under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun into the House of Wisdom, where Christian Yuhanna ibn Masawayh and Persian al-Fadl ibn Nawbakht lead teams translating the world's books into Arabic.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
