# Baghdad: House of Wisdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Bayt al-Hikma and the houses of knowledge”?
**Answer:** Al-Mansur's palace library grows under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun into the House of Wisdom, where Christian Yuhanna ibn Masawayh and Persian al-Fadl ibn Nawbakht lead teams translating the world's books into Arabic.
