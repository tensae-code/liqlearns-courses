# Baghdad: House of Wisdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Al-Mansur's palace library grows under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun into the House of Wisdom, where Christian Yuhanna ibn Masawayh and Persian al-Fadl ibn Nawbakht lead teams translating the world's books into Arabic.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Bayt al-Hikma and the houses of knowledge” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Al-Mansur's palace library grows under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun into the House of Wisdom, where Christian Yuhanna ibn Masawayh and Persian al-Fadl ibn Nawbakht lead teams translating the world's books into Arabic.
