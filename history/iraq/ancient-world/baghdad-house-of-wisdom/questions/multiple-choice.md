# Baghdad: House of Wisdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Bayt al-Hikma and the houses of knowledge”?
a) Al-Mansur's palace library grows under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun into the House of Wisdom, where Christian Yuhanna ibn Masawayh and Persian al-Fadl ibn Nawbakht lead teams translating the world's books into Arabic.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Bayt al-Hikma and the houses of knowledge” is right?
a) Al-Mansur's palace library grows under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun into the House of Wisdom, where Christian Yuhanna ibn Masawayh and Persian al-Fadl ibn Nawbakht lead teams translating the world's books into Arabic.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
