# Video script — The Ghassanids

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Azd tribes flee yemen after the marib dam breaks and, as byzantium's arab allies against persia and the desert raiders, build the white palace, al-mushatta and al-qastal.

On the map, follow this part: The Ghassanids. Azd tribes flee Yemen after the Marib dam breaks and, as Byzantium's Arab allies against Persia and the desert raiders, build the White Palace, al-Mushatta and al-Qastal.
