# The Ghassanids — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Azd tribes flee Yemen after the Marib dam breaks and, as Byzantium's Arab allies against Persia and the desert raiders, build the White Palace, al-Mushatta and al-Qastal.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The Ghassanids” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Azd tribes flee Yemen after the Marib dam breaks and, as Byzantium's Arab allies against Persia and the desert raiders, build the White Palace, al-Mushatta and al-Qastal.
