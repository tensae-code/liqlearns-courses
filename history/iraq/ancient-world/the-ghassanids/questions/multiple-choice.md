# The Ghassanids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The Ghassanids”?
a) Azd tribes flee Yemen after the Marib dam breaks and, as Byzantium's Arab allies against Persia and the desert raiders, build the White Palace, al-Mushatta and al-Qastal.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The Ghassanids” is right?
a) Azd tribes flee Yemen after the Marib dam breaks and, as Byzantium's Arab allies against Persia and the desert raiders, build the White Palace, al-Mushatta and al-Qastal.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
