# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Achaeans and dorians in a mountainous, island-strewn land turn seaward toward egypt, the levant and mesopotamia.

On the map, follow this part: Greece: land, people and city-states. Achaeans and Dorians in a mountainous, island-strewn land turn seaward toward Egypt, the Levant and Mesopotamia.

On the map, follow this part: Greek achievements: philosophy and astronomy. From the 6th century BC the Greeks found philosophy — "love of wisdom" — and prove the earth a sphere turning on its axis.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
