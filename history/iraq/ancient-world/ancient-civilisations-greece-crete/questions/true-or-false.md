# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Achaeans and Dorians in a mountainous, island-strewn land turn seaward toward Egypt, the Levant and Mesopotamia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Greek achievements: philosophy and astronomy” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: From the 6th century BC the Greeks found philosophy — "love of wisdom" — and prove the earth a sphere turning on its axis.
