# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Greece: land, people and city-states”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Achaeans and Dorians in a mountainous, island-strewn land turn seaward toward Egypt, the Levant and Mesopotamia.

**2.** What came of “Greek achievements: philosophy and astronomy”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From the 6th century BC the Greeks found philosophy — "love of wisdom" — and prove the earth a sphere turning on its axis.
