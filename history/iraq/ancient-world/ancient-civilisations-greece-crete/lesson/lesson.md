# Ancient Civilisations: Greece and Crete

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Greece: land, people and city-states; Greek achievements: philosophy and astronomy. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Greece: land, people and city-states

Achaeans and Dorians in a mountainous, island-strewn land turn seaward toward Egypt, the Levant and Mesopotamia.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greek achievements: philosophy and astronomy

From the 6th century BC the Greeks found philosophy — "love of wisdom" — and prove the earth a sphere turning on its axis.
