# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Clay tablets and the Sumerian lyre show a people who turned wedge-shaped marks into records, music and law. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Mesopotamia's sciences: writing, medicine, astronomy, time” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Cuneiform spreads with the Akkadian language; doctors diagnose and prescribe; astronomers chart planets, split the day into twelve parts and the week into seven days.
