# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia's Gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Sumer's gifts: writing, ziggurats, laws and the lyre”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Clay tablets and the Sumerian lyre show a people who turned wedge-shaped marks into records, music and law.

**2.** What came of “Mesopotamia's sciences: writing, medicine, astronomy, time”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 5)
**Answer:** Cuneiform spreads with the Akkadian language; doctors diagnose and prescribe; astronomers chart planets, split the day into twelve parts and the week into seven days.
