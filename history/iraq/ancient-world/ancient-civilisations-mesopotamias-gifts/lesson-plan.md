## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Sumer's gifts: writing, ziggurats, laws and the lyre** — Clay tablets and the Sumerian lyre show a people who turned wedge-shaped marks into records, music and law.
2. **Masterpieces of our old civilisation: Sumer, Akkad, Babylon, Assyria** — The Sumerian lyre and treasures, an Akkadian king's head, Babylon's tower and Assyria's winged bull parade past as proof in pictures.
3. **The Iraqis were the first to invent writing and recording** — Temples need accounts, so Iraqis press clay tablets with a triangular reed pen and invent cuneiform, then roll cylinder seals that print their owner's name and picture.
4. **The marks of civilisation: writing invented** — Pictorial writing turns daily acts into records, and with writing human history proper begins.
5. **Mesopotamia's sciences: writing, medicine, astronomy, time** — Cuneiform spreads with the Akkadian language; doctors diagnose and prescribe; astronomers chart planets, split the day into twelve parts and the week into seven days.
