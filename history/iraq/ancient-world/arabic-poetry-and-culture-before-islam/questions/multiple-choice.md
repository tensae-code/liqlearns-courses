# Arabic Poetry and Culture Before Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Arabia's cultural life: poetry, prose and building”?
a) Eloquence is the Arabs' art — prose genres and poetry flourish while sculptors and builders raise forts, monasteries and temples.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own

a) Eloquence is the Arabs' art — prose genres and poetry flourish while sculptors and builders raise forts, monasteries and temples.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
