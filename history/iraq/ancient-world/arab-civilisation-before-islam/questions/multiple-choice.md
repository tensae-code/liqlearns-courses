# Arab Civilisation Before Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Ch.2 The Arabs' civilisation before Islam”?
a) The pre-Islamic Arabs are given a civilisation of their own — states, trade, poetry and faiths — not an empty age of ignorance.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Ch.2 The Arabs' civilisation before Islam” is right?
a) The pre-Islamic Arabs are given a civilisation of their own — states, trade, poetry and faiths — not an empty age of ignorance.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
