# Saba and the Marib Dam

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Saba and the Marib dam. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Saba and the Marib dam

Saba grows rich on incense, gold and the India–Abyssinia sea trade, rules first through priest-kings (mukarribs) then kings at Marib, and dams the floods to save its farms.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: saba grows rich on incense, gold and the India–Abyssinia sea trade, rules first through priest-kings (mukarribs) then kings at Marib, and dams the floods to save its farms. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Saba and the Marib Dam. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
