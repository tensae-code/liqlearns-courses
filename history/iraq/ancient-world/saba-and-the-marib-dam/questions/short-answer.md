# Saba and the Marib Dam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Saba and the Marib dam”?
**Answer:** Saba grows rich on incense, gold and the India–Abyssinia sea trade, rules first through priest-kings (mukarribs) then kings at Marib, and dams the floods to save its farms.
