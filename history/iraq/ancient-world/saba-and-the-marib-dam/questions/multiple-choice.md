# Saba and the Marib Dam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Saba and the Marib dam”?
a) Saba grows rich on incense, gold and the India–Abyssinia sea trade, rules first through priest-kings (mukarribs) then kings at Marib, and dams the floods to save its farms.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Saba and the Marib dam” is right?
a) Saba grows rich on incense, gold and the India–Abyssinia sea trade, rules first through priest-kings (mukarribs) then kings at Marib, and dams the floods to save its farms.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
