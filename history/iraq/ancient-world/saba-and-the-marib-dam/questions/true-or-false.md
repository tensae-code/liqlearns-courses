# Saba and the Marib Dam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Saba grows rich on incense, gold and the India–Abyssinia sea trade, rules first through priest-kings (mukarribs) then kings at Marib, and dams the floods to save its farms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Saba and the Marib dam” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Saba grows rich on incense, gold and the India–Abyssinia sea trade, rules first through priest-kings (mukarribs) then kings at Marib, and dams the floods to save its farms.
