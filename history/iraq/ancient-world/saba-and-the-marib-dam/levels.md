# Saba and the Marib Dam — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — saba grows rich on incense, gold and the India–Abyssinia sea trade, rules first through priest-kings (mukarribs) then kings at Marib, and dams the floods to save its farms — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
