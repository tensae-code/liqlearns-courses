# Arabia Before Islam: Tribe and Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Ch.3 Arabia before Islam: tribe, allies and women's place”?
a) The tribe is a state in miniature with its own land and sovereignty; women hold widely differing places from town to desert, and allies join the tribe without its blood.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Ch.3 Arabia before Islam: tribe, allies and women's place” is right?
a) The tribe is a state in miniature with its own land and sovereignty; women hold widely differing places from town to desert, and allies join the tribe without its blood.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
