# The Judiciary in the Islamic State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Ch.4 The judiciary”?
a) The qadi's court, the chief judge and the mazalim jurisdiction turn justice into an institution separate from the ruler's whim.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Ch.4 The judiciary” is right?
a) The qadi's court, the chief judge and the mazalim jurisdiction turn justice into an institution separate from the ruler's whim.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
