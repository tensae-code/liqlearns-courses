# Baghdad's Great Schools: Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Iraq, home of knowledge: the Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya schools. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Iraq, home of knowledge: the Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya schools

The Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.
