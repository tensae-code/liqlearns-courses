# Video script — Baghdad's Great Schools: Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.

On the map, follow this part: Iraq, home of knowledge: the Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya schools. The Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
