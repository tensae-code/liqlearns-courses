# Baghdad's Great Schools: Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Iraq, home of knowledge: the Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya schools”?
**Answer:** The Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university.
