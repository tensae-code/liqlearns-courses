# Baghdad's Great Schools: Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Iraq, home of knowledge: the Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya schools”?
a) The Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Iraq, home of knowledge: the Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya schools” is right?
a) The Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
