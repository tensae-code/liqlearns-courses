# Baghdad's Great Schools: Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Iraq, home of knowledge: the Nizamiyya and Mustansiriyya schools” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university.
