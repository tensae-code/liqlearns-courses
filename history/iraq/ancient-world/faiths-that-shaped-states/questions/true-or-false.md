# Faiths That Shaped States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every Sumerian city raises its own temple on a flood-safe ziggurat; temples own farms and schools, Babylon alone holds 153 temples, and the Mandaeans still baptise in the southern rivers. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The temple at the centre of the old city” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The temple is at once shrine, treasury and economic centre, binding ritual to the people's daily bread.
