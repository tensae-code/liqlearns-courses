# Faiths That Shaped States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Our land, cradle of religions and temples”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Every Sumerian city raises its own temple on a flood-safe ziggurat; temples own farms and schools, Babylon alone holds 153 temples, and the Mandaeans still baptise in the southern rivers.

**2.** What came of “The temple at the centre of the old city”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The temple is at once shrine, treasury and economic centre, binding ritual to the people's daily bread.
