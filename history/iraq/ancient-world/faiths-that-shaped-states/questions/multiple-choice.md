**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Our land, cradle of religions and temples”? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Every Sumerian city raises its own temple on a flood-safe ziggurat; temples own farms and schools, Babylon alone holds 153 temples, and the Mandaeans still baptise in the southern rivers.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
