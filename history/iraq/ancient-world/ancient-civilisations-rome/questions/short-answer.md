# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Rome: republic to empire”?
**Answer:** Italian tribes found Rome, build a city-state republic that subdues Italy, then political failure and civil strife carry the state from republic to empire.
