# The Kingdom of Kinda — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 4th-century-BC central Arabia, Kinda's alliances stitch centre, south and north into a bond wider than the tribe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The kingdom of Kinda” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: In 4th-century-BC central Arabia, Kinda's alliances stitch centre, south and north into a bond wider than the tribe.
