# Rome Divides: Roman Law and Society — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — rome's gift is law, from the Twelve Tables of 450 BC onward, ruling a society of nobles, knights, traders and landless workers — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
