# Scholarship in the Arab-Islamic Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Ch.7 Intellectual life: religious, linguistic and human sciences”?
a) Qur'an and hadith sciences, grammar and history-writing grow into disciplines with their own masters and methods.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
