# Iraqi Scholars of the Islamic Age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Iraqi scholars: grammar, chemistry, history and light”?
**Answer:** Abu al-Aswad invents Arabic grammar in Basra; Jabir ibn Hayyan founds chemistry on experiment; the Mandaean al-Sabi writes on the astrolabe; al-Masudi travels and writes Meadows of Gold; Basra-born Ibn al-Haytham explains the eye and the rainbow; Shuhda of Baghdad teaches hadith to men and women.
