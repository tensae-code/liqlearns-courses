# Iraqi Scholars of the Islamic Age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Iraqi scholars: grammar, chemistry, history and light”?
a) Abu al-Aswad invents Arabic grammar in Basra; Jabir ibn Hayyan founds chemistry on experiment; the Mandaean al-Sabi writes on the astrolabe; al-Masudi travels and writes Meadows of Gold; Basra-born Ibn al-Haytham explains the eye and the rainbow; Shuhda of Baghdad teaches hadith to men and women.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Iraqi scholars: grammar, chemistry, history and light” is right?
a) Abu al-Aswad invents Arabic grammar in Basra; Jabir ibn Hayyan founds chemistry on experiment; the Mandaean al-Sabi writes on the astrolabe; al-Masudi travels and writes Meadows of Gold; Basra-born Ibn al-Haytham explains the eye and the rainbow; Shuhda of Baghdad teaches hadith to men and women.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
