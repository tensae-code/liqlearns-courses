# Iraqi Scholars of the Islamic Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Abu al-Aswad invents Arabic grammar in Basra; Jabir ibn Hayyan founds chemistry on experiment; the Mandaean al-Sabi writes on the astrolabe; al-Masudi travels and writes Meadows of Gold; Basra-born Ibn al-Haytham explains the eye and the rainbow; Shuhda of Baghdad teaches hadith to men and women.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Iraqi scholars: grammar, chemistry, history and light” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Abu al-Aswad invents Arabic grammar in Basra; Jabir ibn Hayyan founds chemistry on experiment; the Mandaean al-Sabi writes on the astrolabe; al-Masudi travels and writes Meadows of Gold; Basra-born Ibn al-Haytham explains the eye and the rainbow; Shuhda of Baghdad teaches hadith to men and women.
