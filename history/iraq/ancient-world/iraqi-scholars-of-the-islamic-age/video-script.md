# Video script — Iraqi Scholars of the Islamic Age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Abu al-aswad invents arabic grammar in basra; jabir ibn hayyan founds chemistry on experiment; the mandaean al-sabi writes on the astrolabe; al-masudi travels and writes meadows of gold.

On the map, follow this part: Iraqi scholars: grammar, chemistry, history and light. Abu al-Aswad invents Arabic grammar in Basra; Jabir ibn Hayyan founds chemistry on experiment; the Mandaean al-Sabi writes on the astrolabe; al-Masudi travels and writes Meadows of Gold; Basra-born Ibn al-Haytham explains the eye and the rainbow; Shuhda of Baghdad teaches hadith to men and women.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
