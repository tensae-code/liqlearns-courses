# The Decline of Arab-Islamic Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Ch.8 Influence on the West, then decline”?
a) The book ends by asking why its own civilisation fell behind the world's progress, weighing internal decay against external blows.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Ch.8 Influence on the West, then decline” is right?
a) The book ends by asking why its own civilisation fell behind the world's progress, weighing internal decay against external blows.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
