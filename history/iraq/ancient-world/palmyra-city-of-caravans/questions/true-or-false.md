# Palmyra, City of Caravans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A spring in the Syrian desert, halfway between Euphrates and Damascus, becomes the caravan city whose merchants organise, guard and provision the desert convoys.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Palmyra (Tadmor)” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: A spring in the Syrian desert, halfway between Euphrates and Damascus, becomes the caravan city whose merchants organise, guard and provision the desert convoys.
