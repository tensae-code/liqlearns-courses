# Palmyra, City of Caravans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Palmyra (Tadmor)”?
a) A spring in the Syrian desert, halfway between Euphrates and Damascus, becomes the caravan city whose merchants organise, guard and provision the desert convoys.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Palmyra (Tadmor)” is right?
a) A spring in the Syrian desert, halfway between Euphrates and Damascus, becomes the caravan city whose merchants organise, guard and provision the desert convoys.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
