# Palmyra, City of Caravans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Palmyra (Tadmor)”?
**Answer:** A spring in the Syrian desert, halfway between Euphrates and Damascus, becomes the caravan city whose merchants organise, guard and provision the desert convoys.

**2.** What came of “Palmyra (Tadmor)”, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** A spring in the Syrian desert, halfway between Euphrates and Damascus, becomes the caravan city whose merchants organise, guard and provision the desert convoys.
