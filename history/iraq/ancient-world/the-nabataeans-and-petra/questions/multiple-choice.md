# The Nabataeans and Petra — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Ch.4 Arabia's centres: the Nabataeans”?
a) Arab Nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 BC) at Petra where the Iraq–Yemen–Syria–Egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as Rhodes, Delos and Rome, and carve theatre, monastery and the Daughter's Palace.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Arab Nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 BC) at Petra where the Iraq–Yemen–Syria–Egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as Rhodes, Delos and Rome, and carve theatre, monastery and the Daughter's Palace.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
