# The Nabataeans and Petra — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Ch.4 Arabia's centres: the Nabataeans”?
**Answer:** Arab Nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 BC) at Petra where the Iraq–Yemen–Syria–Egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as Rhodes, Delos and Rome, and carve theatre, monastery and the Daughter's Palace.
