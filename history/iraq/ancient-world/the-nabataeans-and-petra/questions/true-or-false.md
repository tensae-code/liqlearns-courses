# The Nabataeans and Petra — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Arab Nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 BC) at Petra where the Iraq–Yemen–Syria–Egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as Rhodes, Delos and Rome, and carve theatre, monastery and the Daughter's Palace.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.4 Arabia's centres: the Nabataeans” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Arab Nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 BC) at Petra where the Iraq–Yemen–Syria–Egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as Rhodes, Delos and Rome, and carve theatre, monastery and the Daughter's Palace.
