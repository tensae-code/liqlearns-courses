# Video script — The Nabataeans and Petra

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Arab nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 bc) at petra where the iraq–yemen–syria–egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as rhodes, delos and rome, and carve theatre.

On the map, follow this part: Ch.4 Arabia's centres: the Nabataeans. Arab Nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 BC) at Petra where the Iraq–Yemen–Syria–Egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as Rhodes, Delos and Rome, and carve theatre, monastery and the Daughter's Palace.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
