# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Utba ibn Ghazwan founds Basra in 14 AH as an administrative and military base for the eastern conquests; it becomes Iraq's only seaport at the head of the Gulf. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.6 Cities beyond the amsar” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 30)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Older cities that Islam inherited and newer foundations alike are folded into one urban civilisation.
