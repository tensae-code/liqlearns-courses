# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Islamic cities: Basra founded — a) (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Islamic cities: Kufa becomes a capital — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Utba ibn Ghazwan founds Basra in 14 AH as an administrative and military base for the eastern conquests; it becomes Iraq's only seaport at the head of the Gulf.
b) Old Kufa beside al-Hira becomes the seat of the fourth caliph Ali ibn Abi Talib in 36 AH and stays a major religious centre.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
