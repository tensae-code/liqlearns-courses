# The Rise and Expansion of Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Islamic cities: Basra founded”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Utba ibn Ghazwan founds Basra in 14 AH as an administrative and military base for the eastern conquests; it becomes Iraq's only seaport at the head of the Gulf.

**2.** What came of “Ch.6 Cities beyond the amsar”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 30)
**Answer:** Older cities that Islam inherited and newer foundations alike are folded into one urban civilisation.
