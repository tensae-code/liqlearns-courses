# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Arabia's economy: herding, trade and the great markets”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Meccan caravans carry Chinese silk east–west beyond the famous two journeys a year, and Arabs gather to trade and recite at Ukaz and Dhu al-Majaz by Arafat.

**2.** What came of “Ch.8 How the civilisation travelled: trade and translation routes to Europe”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Europe–Levant trade, al-Andalus and Sicily carry Arab-Islamic learning westward; even the Crusaders' two centuries (490–690 AH) become a channel.
