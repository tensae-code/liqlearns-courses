# Trade Routes That Linked the Old World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Meccan caravans carry Chinese silk east–west beyond the famous two journeys a year, and Arabs gather to trade and recite at Ukaz and Dhu al-Majaz by Arafat. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.8 How the civilisation travelled: trade and translation routes to Europe” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Europe–Levant trade, al-Andalus and Sicily carry Arab-Islamic learning westward; even the Crusaders' two centuries (490–690 AH) become a channel.
