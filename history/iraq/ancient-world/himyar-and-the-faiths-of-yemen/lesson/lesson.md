# Himyar and the Faiths of Yemen

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Himyar. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Himyar

Himyar's Yemen receives Judaism and then Christianity by missionaries from al-Hira and Syria, while King Karib's inscriptions already call his god Lord of Heaven and Earth.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Himyar and the Faiths of Yemen. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
