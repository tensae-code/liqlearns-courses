# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Ch.3 The Nile civilisation: Egypt united and the Old Kingdom”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** About 3150 BC Egypt is united; the pyramid age of dynasties 4–6 raises the stepped and great pyramids that made Egypt famous.

**2.** What came of “The New Kingdom empire and the long decline”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Three dynasties hold an empire for five centuries; from the 21st dynasty weakness and decay open Egypt to foreign interference.
