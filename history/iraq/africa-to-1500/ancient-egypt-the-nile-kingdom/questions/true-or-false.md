# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** About 3150 BC Egypt is united; the pyramid age of dynasties 4–6 raises the stepped and great pyramids that made Egypt famous. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The New Kingdom empire and the long decline” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Three dynasties hold an empire for five centuries; from the 21st dynasty weakness and decay open Egypt to foreign interference.
