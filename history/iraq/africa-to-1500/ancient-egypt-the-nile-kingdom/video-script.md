# Video script — Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

On the map, follow this part: Ch.3 The Nile civilisation: Egypt united and the Old Kingdom. About 3150 BC Egypt is united; the pyramid age of dynasties 4–6 raises the stepped and great pyramids that made Egypt famous.

On the map, follow this part: Middle Kingdom troubles and the Hyksos. The land splinters into provinces, and the Hyksos ride in from the Levant to rule for some two centuries, bringing new weapons.

On the map, follow this part: The New Kingdom empire and the long decline. Three dynasties hold an empire for five centuries; from the 21st dynasty weakness and decay open Egypt to foreign interference.
