# Ancient Egypt: The Nile Kingdom

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Ch.3 The Nile civilisation: Egypt united and the Old Kingdom; Middle Kingdom troubles and the Hyksos; The New Kingdom empire and the long decline. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch.3 The Nile civilisation: Egypt united and the Old Kingdom

About 3150 BC Egypt is united; the pyramid age of dynasties 4–6 raises the stepped and great pyramids that made Egypt famous.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Middle Kingdom troubles and the Hyksos

The land splinters into provinces, and the Hyksos ride in from the Levant to rule for some two centuries, bringing new weapons.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 3 — The New Kingdom empire and the long decline

Three dynasties hold an empire for five centuries; from the 21st dynasty weakness and decay open Egypt to foreign interference.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.
