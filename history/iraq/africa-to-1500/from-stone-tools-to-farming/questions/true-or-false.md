# From Stone Tools to Farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Humans move from caves and gathered food through fire and stone tools to metals, each age named for what their hands had learned.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Prehistory: the Stone Ages to the Chalcolithic” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Humans move from caves and gathered food through fire and stone tools to metals, each age named for what their hands had learned.
