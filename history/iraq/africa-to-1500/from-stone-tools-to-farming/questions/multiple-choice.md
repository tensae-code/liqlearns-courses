# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Prehistory: the Stone Ages to the Chalcolithic”?
a) Humans move from caves and gathered food through fire and stone tools to metals, each age named for what their hands had learned.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Prehistory: the Stone Ages to the Chalcolithic” is right?
a) Humans move from caves and gathered food through fire and stone tools to metals, each age named for what their hands had learned.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
