# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Nile society: temples, classes and women's standing”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A steeply classed society crowns its temples with stores and estates, yet Egyptian women hold a standing higher than Greek women's.

**2.** What came of “Egypt's civilisational achievements”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From the primeval ocean in their creation thought to their sciences, Egypt's gifts are summed as a civilisation's legacy.
