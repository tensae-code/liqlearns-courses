# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A steeply classed society crowns its temples with stores and estates, yet Egyptian women hold a standing higher than Greek women's. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Egypt's civilisational achievements” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: From the primeval ocean in their creation thought to their sciences, Egypt's gifts are summed as a civilisation's legacy.
