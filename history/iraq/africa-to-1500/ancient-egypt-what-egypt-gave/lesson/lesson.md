# Ancient Egypt: What Egypt Gave

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Nile society: temples, classes and women's standing; Egypt's civilisational achievements. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Nile society: temples, classes and women's standing

A steeply classed society crowns its temples with stores and estates, yet Egyptian women hold a standing higher than Greek women's.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Egypt's civilisational achievements

From the primeval ocean in their creation thought to their sciences, Egypt's gifts are summed as a civilisation's legacy.
