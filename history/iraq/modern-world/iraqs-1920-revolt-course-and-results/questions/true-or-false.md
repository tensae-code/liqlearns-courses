# Iraq's 1920 Revolt: Course and Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From 30 June 1920 the rebels take Abu Sukhayr, Shamiyya, Diwaniyah, Karbala and Najaf, fight the fierce battle of Rumaytha under Sheikh Shaalan Abu al-Jun, and rise in Kurdistan too; the revolt forces Britain to found an Iraqi state. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The 1920 Revolt and its settlement” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The revolt that forced a state is given its full course inside the war chapter.
