# Lebanon's National Pact and Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Lebanon's independence”?
a) President Bishara al-Khoury and Riad al-Solh seal the National Pact, amending the constitution to affirm a fully independent Lebanon.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Lebanon's independence” is right?
a) President Bishara al-Khoury and Riad al-Solh seal the National Pact, amending the constitution to affirm a fully independent Lebanon.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
