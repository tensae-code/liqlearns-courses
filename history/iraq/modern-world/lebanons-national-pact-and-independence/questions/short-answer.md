# Lebanon's National Pact and Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Lebanon's independence”?
**Answer:** President Bishara al-Khoury and Riad al-Solh seal the National Pact, amending the constitution to affirm a fully independent Lebanon.

**2.** What came of “Lebanon's independence”, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** President Bishara al-Khoury and Riad al-Solh seal the National Pact, amending the constitution to affirm a fully independent Lebanon.
