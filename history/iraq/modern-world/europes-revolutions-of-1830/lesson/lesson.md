# Europe's Revolutions of 1830

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Ch.3 The revolutions of 1830. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Ch.3 The revolutions of 1830

Charles X's reaction sparks Paris; Belgium seizes the moment, wins independence at the London conference, and reformers press in Switzerland and England.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.
