# Founding the Iraqi State: Faisal Becomes King — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Founding the modern Iraqi state”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Cairo Conference and London talks choose Amir Faisal ibn al-Husayn; a provisional government prepares a constitutional king for Iraq.

**2.** What came of “Ch.4 Iraq 1921–1939: Faisal crowned”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Amir Faisal ibn al-Husayn is installed as Iraq's constitutional king and the monarchy's first two decades begin.
