# Founding the Iraqi State: Faisal Becomes King — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Cairo Conference and London talks choose Amir Faisal ibn al-Husayn; a provisional government prepares a constitutional king for Iraq. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.4 Iraq 1921–1939: Faisal crowned” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Amir Faisal ibn al-Husayn is installed as Iraq's constitutional king and the monarchy's first two decades begin.
