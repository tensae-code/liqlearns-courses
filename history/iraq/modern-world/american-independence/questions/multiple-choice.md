# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Ch.2 American independence”?
a) Stamp, molasses and tea taxes enrage the colonies; the Declaration asserts that all men are created equal, and Britain loses America.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Ch.2 American independence” is right?
a) Stamp, molasses and tea taxes enrage the colonies; the Declaration asserts that all men are created equal, and Britain loses America.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
