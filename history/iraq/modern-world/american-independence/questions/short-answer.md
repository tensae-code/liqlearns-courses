# American Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Ch.2 American independence”?
**Answer:** Stamp, molasses and tea taxes enrage the colonies; the Declaration asserts that all men are created equal, and Britain loses America.

**2.** What came of “Ch.2 American independence”, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** Stamp, molasses and tea taxes enrage the colonies; the Declaration asserts that all men are created equal, and Britain loses America.
