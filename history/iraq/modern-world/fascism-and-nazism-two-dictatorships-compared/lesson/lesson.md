# Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Fascism in Italy; Nazism in Germany. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fascism in Italy

Italy, cheated of its war aims, turns to Mussolini, who signs a non-aggression pact with the Soviets in 1933 yet collides with France over Africa.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nazism in Germany

Hitler, chancellor in 1933, makes his party the only legal one and preaches the "Aryan" master race from Mein Kampf.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.
