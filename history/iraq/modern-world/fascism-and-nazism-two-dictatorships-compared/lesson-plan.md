**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — Europe and America, 1789–1945 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Fascism in Italy** — Italy, cheated of its war aims, turns to Mussolini, who signs a non-aggression pact with the Soviets in 1933 yet collides with France over Africa.
2. **Nazism in Germany** — Hitler, chancellor in 1933, makes his party the only legal one and preaches the "Aryan" master race from Mein Kampf.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
