# Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Fascism in Italy”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Italy, cheated of its war aims, turns to Mussolini, who signs a non-aggression pact with the Soviets in 1933 yet collides with France over Africa.

**2.** What came of “Nazism in Germany”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Hitler, chancellor in 1933, makes his party the only legal one and preaches the "Aryan" master race from Mein Kampf.
