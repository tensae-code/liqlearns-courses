# Fascism and Nazism: Two Dictatorships Compared — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Italy, cheated of its war aims, turns to Mussolini, who signs a non-aggression pact with the Soviets in 1933 yet collides with France over Africa. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Nazism in Germany” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Hitler, chancellor in 1933, makes his party the only legal one and preaches the "Aryan" master race from Mein Kampf.
