# Iraq Independent, 1932 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Britain agrees to end the mandate under the 1930 treaty; parliament ratifies it 69 of 88, and Iraq enters the League of Nations a sovereign state. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Independence within the monarchy's first decades” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Treaty, constitution and League membership carry Iraq from mandate to formal independence inside this chapter's span.
