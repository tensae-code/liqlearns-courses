# Iraq Independent, 1932 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The mandate ends: Iraq independent”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Britain agrees to end the mandate under the 1930 treaty; parliament ratifies it 69 of 88, and Iraq enters the League of Nations a sovereign state.

**2.** What came of “Independence within the monarchy's first decades”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Treaty, constitution and League membership carry Iraq from mandate to formal independence inside this chapter's span.
