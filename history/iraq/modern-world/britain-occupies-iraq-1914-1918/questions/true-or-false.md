# Britain Occupies Iraq, 1914–1918 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Indian forces land below Abadan in 1914, are besieged at Kut, then take Baghdad and finally Mosul as the Mudros armistice is announced on 31 October 1918. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.3 Iraq during and after the First World War” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: British interests, invasion, occupation and the mandate settlement are retold at full length.
