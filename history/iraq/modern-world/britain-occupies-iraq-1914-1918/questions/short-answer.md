# Britain Occupies Iraq, 1914–1918 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The British occupation in the First World War”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Indian forces land below Abadan in 1914, are besieged at Kut, then take Baghdad and finally Mosul as the Mudros armistice is announced on 31 October 1918.

**2.** What came of “Ch.3 Iraq during and after the First World War”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** British interests, invasion, occupation and the mandate settlement are retold at full length.
