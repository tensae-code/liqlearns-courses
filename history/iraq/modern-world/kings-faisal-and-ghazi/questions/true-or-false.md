# Kings Faisal and Ghazi — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Faisal I dies and his son Ghazi takes the throne, then Ghazi's own sudden death is announced — two successions shake the young kingdom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Faisal dies; Ghazi reigns and dies” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Faisal I dies and his son Ghazi takes the throne, then Ghazi's own sudden death is announced — two successions shake the young kingdom.
