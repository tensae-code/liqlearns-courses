# Kings Faisal and Ghazi — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Faisal dies; Ghazi reigns and dies”?
**Answer:** Faisal I dies and his son Ghazi takes the throne, then Ghazi's own sudden death is announced — two successions shake the young kingdom.
