# Kings Faisal and Ghazi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Faisal dies; Ghazi reigns and dies”?
a) Faisal I dies and his son Ghazi takes the throne, then Ghazi's own sudden death is announced — two successions shake the young kingdom.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Faisal dies; Ghazi reigns and dies” is right?
a) Faisal I dies and his son Ghazi takes the throne, then Ghazi's own sudden death is announced — two successions shake the young kingdom.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
