# Tunisia and Morocco Become Protectorates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France imposes the Bardo protectorate on a resisting Tunisia; Morocco, the one Arab land never Ottoman, is split between French and Spanish zones, Ceuta, Melilla and Ifni to Spain.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Tunisia and Morocco” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
