# Tunisia and Morocco Become Protectorates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Tunisia and Morocco”?
**Answer:** France imposes the Bardo protectorate on a resisting Tunisia; Morocco, the one Arab land never Ottoman, is split between French and Spanish zones, Ceuta, Melilla and Ifni to Spain.
