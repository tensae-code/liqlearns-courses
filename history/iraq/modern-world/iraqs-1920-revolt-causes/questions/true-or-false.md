# Iraq's 1920 Revolt: Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A ruined economy, clerics of Najaf, Karbala, Kadhimiyya and Samarra awakening national feeling, and Britain's arrests and exiles of national figures light the fuse.
