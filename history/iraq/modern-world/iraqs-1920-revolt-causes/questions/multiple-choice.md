# Iraq's 1920 Revolt: Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The 1920 Revolt: its causes”?
a) A ruined economy, clerics of Najaf, Karbala, Kadhimiyya and Samarra awakening national feeling, and Britain's arrests and exiles of national figures light the fuse.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The 1920 Revolt: its causes” is right?
a) A ruined economy, clerics of Najaf, Karbala, Kadhimiyya and Samarra awakening national feeling, and Britain's arrests and exiles of national figures light the fuse.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
