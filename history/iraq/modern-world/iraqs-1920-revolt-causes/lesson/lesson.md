# Iraq's 1920 Revolt: Causes

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The 1920 Revolt: its causes. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The 1920 Revolt: its causes

A ruined economy, clerics of Najaf, Karbala, Kadhimiyya and Samarra awakening national feeling, and Britain's arrests and exiles of national figures light the fuse.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a ruined economy, clerics of Najaf, Karbala, Kadhimiyya and Samarra awakening national feeling, and Britain's arrests and exiles of national figures light the fuse. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Iraq's 1920 Revolt: Causes. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
