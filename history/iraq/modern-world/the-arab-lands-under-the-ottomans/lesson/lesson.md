# The Arab Lands Under the Ottomans

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Ch.2 The Arab lands in the Ottoman age 1516–1918. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Ch.2 The Arab lands in the Ottoman age 1516–1918

Selim's victory at Ridaniyya brings Egypt and Syria under the sultan; the Barbarossa brothers hold North Africa's coast for the empire.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.
