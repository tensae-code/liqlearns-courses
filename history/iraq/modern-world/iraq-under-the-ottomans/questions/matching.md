# Iraq Under the Ottomans — matching

Match each part with its story.

**1.** Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age: the last centuries — a) (Sub lesson 1)

**2.** Baghdad rises against its governors — b) (Sub lesson 2)

Meanings:
a) Suleiman the Magnificent takes Baghdad in 1534; four centuries of Ottoman rule settle into stagnation, stirred late by Tanzimat reforms, steam navigation, the telegraph and foreign archaeology missions.
b) The city's intellectuals and quarters join repeated uprisings, among them the 1832 rising led by the mufti Abd al-Ghani Al Jamil against Wali Ali Rida.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
