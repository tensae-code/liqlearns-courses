# Iraq Under the Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Suleiman the Magnificent takes Baghdad in 1534; four centuries of Ottoman rule settle into stagnation, stirred late by Tanzimat reforms, steam navigation, the telegraph and foreign archaeology missions. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Political, social and economic life under four centuries of Ottoman rule is examined in depth, the full version of the Grade 9 sketch.
