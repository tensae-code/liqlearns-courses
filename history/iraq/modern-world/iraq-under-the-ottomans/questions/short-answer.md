# Iraq Under the Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age: the last centuries”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Suleiman the Magnificent takes Baghdad in 1534; four centuries of Ottoman rule settle into stagnation, stirred late by Tanzimat reforms, steam navigation, the telegraph and foreign archaeology missions.

**2.** What came of “Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 3)
