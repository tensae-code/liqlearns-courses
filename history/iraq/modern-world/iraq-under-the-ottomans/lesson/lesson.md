# Iraq Under the Ottomans

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age: the last centuries; Baghdad rises against its governors; Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age: the last centuries

Suleiman the Magnificent takes Baghdad in 1534; four centuries of Ottoman rule settle into stagnation, stirred late by Tanzimat reforms, steam navigation, the telegraph and foreign archaeology missions.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this part, where it happens, and what changes because of it — the next part builds on exactly this.

## Sub lesson 2 — Baghdad rises against its governors

The city's intellectuals and quarters join repeated uprisings, among them the 1832 rising led by the mufti Abd al-Ghani Al Jamil against Wali Ali Rida.
