# Video script — Iraq Under the Ottomans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Suleiman the magnificent takes baghdad in 1534.

On the map, follow this part: Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age: the last centuries. Suleiman the Magnificent takes Baghdad in 1534; four centuries of Ottoman rule settle into stagnation, stirred late by Tanzimat reforms, steam navigation, the telegraph and foreign archaeology missions.

On the map, follow this part: Baghdad rises against its governors. The city's intellectuals and quarters join repeated uprisings, among them the 1832 rising led by the mufti Abd al-Ghani Al Jamil against Wali Ali Rida.

On the map, follow this part: Ch.1 Iraq in the Ottoman age. Political, social and economic life under four centuries of Ottoman rule is examined in depth, the full version of the Grade 9 sketch.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
