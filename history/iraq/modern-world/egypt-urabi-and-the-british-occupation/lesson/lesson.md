# Egypt: Urabi and the British Occupation

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Ch.6 European colonialism: Egypt. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Ch.6 European colonialism: Egypt

Khedive Ismail yields to a national ministry and constitutional promise; Ahmed Urabi leads the army's stand — and Britain occupies Egypt, disbands its army and fines its people.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
