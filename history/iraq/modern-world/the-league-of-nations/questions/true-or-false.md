# The League of Nations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Paris peace births a world organisation of Assembly and Council to keep the peace — each member one vote.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.6 Between the wars: the League of Nations” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The Paris peace births a world organisation of Assembly and Council to keep the peace — each member one vote.
