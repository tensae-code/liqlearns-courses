# Europe's Revolutions of 1848 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The revolutions of 1848”?
a) France makes Napoleon III; Austria-Hungary's peoples rise until the Russian tsar's army crushes them in 1849; Prussia's king dodges the constitution his people demand.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own

**2.** Which statement about “The revolutions of 1848” is right?
a) France makes Napoleon III; Austria-Hungary's peoples rise until the Russian tsar's army crushes them in 1849; Prussia's king dodges the constitution his people demand.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
