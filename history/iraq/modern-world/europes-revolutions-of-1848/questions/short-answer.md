# Europe's Revolutions of 1848 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The revolutions of 1848”?
**Answer:** France makes Napoleon III; Austria-Hungary's peoples rise until the Russian tsar's army crushes them in 1849; Prussia's king dodges the constitution his people demand.

**2.** What came of “The revolutions of 1848”, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** France makes Napoleon III; Austria-Hungary's peoples rise until the Russian tsar's army crushes them in 1849; Prussia's king dodges the constitution his people demand.
