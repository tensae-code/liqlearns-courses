# Europe's Revolutions of 1848 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France makes Napoleon III; Austria-Hungary's peoples rise until the Russian tsar's army crushes them in 1849; Prussia's king dodges the constitution his people demand.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The revolutions of 1848” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: France makes Napoleon III; Austria-Hungary's peoples rise until the Russian tsar's army crushes them in 1849; Prussia's king dodges the constitution his people demand.
