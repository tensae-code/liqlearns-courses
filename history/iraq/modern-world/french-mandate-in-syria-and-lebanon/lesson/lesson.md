# French Mandate in Syria and Lebanon

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Syria and Lebanon under France. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Syria and Lebanon under France

Faisal enters Damascus with the Arab armies, then Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demands acceptance of the French mandate within four days.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: faisal enters Damascus with the Arab armies, then Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demands acceptance of the French mandate within four days. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: French Mandate in Syria and Lebanon. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
