# French Mandate in Syria and Lebanon — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Syria and Lebanon under France”?
**Answer:** Faisal enters Damascus with the Arab armies, then Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demands acceptance of the French mandate within four days.
