# French Mandate in Syria and Lebanon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Syria and Lebanon under France”?
a) Faisal enters Damascus with the Arab armies, then Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demands acceptance of the French mandate within four days.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Syria and Lebanon under France” is right?
a) Faisal enters Damascus with the Arab armies, then Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demands acceptance of the French mandate within four days.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
