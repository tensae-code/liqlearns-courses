# Video script — Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The general of toulon and italy, who campaigned in egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds france's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the russian campaign of 1812.

On the map, follow this part: Napoleon: reformer at home, conqueror abroad. The general of Toulon and Italy, who campaigned in Egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds France's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the Russian campaign of 1812.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
