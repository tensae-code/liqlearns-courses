# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The general of Toulon and Italy, who campaigned in Egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds France's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the Russian campaign of 1812.
**Answer: True**
