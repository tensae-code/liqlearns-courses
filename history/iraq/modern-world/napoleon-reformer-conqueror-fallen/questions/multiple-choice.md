# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Napoleon: reformer at home, conqueror abroad”?
a) The general of Toulon and Italy, who campaigned in Egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds France's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the Russian campaign of 1812.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Napoleon: reformer at home, conqueror abroad” is right?
a) The general of Toulon and Italy, who campaigned in Egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds France's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the Russian campaign of 1812.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
