# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Napoleon: reformer at home, conqueror abroad”?
**Answer:** The general of Toulon and Italy, who campaigned in Egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds France's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the Russian campaign of 1812.
