# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Napoleon: reformer at home, conqueror abroad. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Napoleon: reformer at home, conqueror abroad

The general of Toulon and Italy, who campaigned in Egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds France's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the Russian campaign of 1812.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the general of Toulon and Italy, who campaigned in Egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds France's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the Russian campaign of 1812. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
