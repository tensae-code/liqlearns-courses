# Napoleon: Reformer, Conqueror, Fallen — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the general of Toulon and Italy, who campaigned in Egypt in 1798, seizes power in 1799, rebuilds France's administration, schools and university — then breaks on the Russian campaign of 1812 — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
