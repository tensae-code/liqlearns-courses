# Iraq's First Constitution and the British Treaty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The Constituent Assembly and the Basic Law”?
**Answer:** The Assembly elected to write the constitution wrestles the Anglo-Iraqi treaty to ratification by the British deadline of 10 June 1924, then enacts the Basic Law.
