# Iraq's First Constitution and the British Treaty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Assembly elected to write the constitution wrestles the Anglo-Iraqi treaty to ratification by the British deadline of 10 June 1924, then enacts the Basic Law.
