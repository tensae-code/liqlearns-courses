# Iraq's First Constitution and the British Treaty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The Constituent Assembly and the Basic Law”?
a) The Assembly elected to write the constitution wrestles the Anglo-Iraqi treaty to ratification by the British deadline of 10 June 1924, then enacts the Basic Law.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The Constituent Assembly and the Basic Law” is right?
a) The Assembly elected to write the constitution wrestles the Anglo-Iraqi treaty to ratification by the British deadline of 10 June 1924, then enacts the Basic Law.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
