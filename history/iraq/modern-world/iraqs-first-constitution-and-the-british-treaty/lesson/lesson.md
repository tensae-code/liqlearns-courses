# Iraq's First Constitution and the British Treaty

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Constituent Assembly and the Basic Law. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Constituent Assembly and the Basic Law

The Assembly elected to write the constitution wrestles the Anglo-Iraqi treaty to ratification by the British deadline of 10 June 1924, then enacts the Basic Law.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.
