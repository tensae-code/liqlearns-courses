# Britain in the Gulf and South Arabia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Britain in the Gulf and South Arabia”?
a) Missions survey the southern coasts and map them while the Wahhabi movement reshapes Arabia's interior.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Britain in the Gulf and South Arabia” is right?
a) Missions survey the southern coasts and map them while the Wahhabi movement reshapes Arabia's interior.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
