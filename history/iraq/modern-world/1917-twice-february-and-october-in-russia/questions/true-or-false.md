# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** War topples the Romanovs (1613–1917); a provisional government in March 1917 gives way to the Soviet state, recognised first by Germany, Italy and Britain.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The Soviet Union emerges a world power” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
