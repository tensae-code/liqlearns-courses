# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The Soviet Union emerges a world power”?
**Answer:** War topples the Romanovs (1613–1917); a provisional government in March 1917 gives way to the Soviet state, recognised first by Germany, Italy and Britain.

**2.** What came of “The Soviet Union emerges a world power”, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** War topples the Romanovs (1613–1917); a provisional government in March 1917 gives way to the Soviet state, recognised first by Germany, Italy and Britain.
