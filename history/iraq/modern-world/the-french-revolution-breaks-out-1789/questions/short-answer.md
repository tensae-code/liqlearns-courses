# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The revolution breaks out”?
**Answer:** A bankrupt king summons the Estates-General; the Third Estate makes itself a National Assembly, Paris storms the Bastille, and the Declaration of Rights follows.

**2.** What came of “The revolution breaks out”, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** A bankrupt king summons the Estates-General; the Third Estate makes itself a National Assembly, Paris storms the Bastille, and the Declaration of Rights follows.
