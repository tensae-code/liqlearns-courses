# The French Revolution Breaks Out, 1789 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A bankrupt king summons the Estates-General; the Third Estate makes itself a National Assembly, Paris storms the Bastille, and the Declaration of Rights follows.
**Answer: True**
