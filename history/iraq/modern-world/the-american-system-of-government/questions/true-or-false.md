# The American System of Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The founders build a federal republic of Congress, courts and parties, its powers listed one by one for the Iraqi pupil.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The American political system” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The founders build a federal republic of Congress, courts and parties, its powers listed one by one for the Iraqi pupil.
