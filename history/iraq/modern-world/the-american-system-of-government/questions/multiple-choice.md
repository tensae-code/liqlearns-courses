# The American System of Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The American political system”?
a) The founders build a federal republic of Congress, courts and parties, its powers listed one by one for the Iraqi pupil.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own

**2.** Which statement about “The American political system” is right?

**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
