# Capitalism: Private Ownership, Factories, New Classes

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Industrial capitalism and the colonial drive. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Industrial capitalism and the colonial drive

Factory profits hunt new fields at home and in the colonies; population and industry together push Europe to seize cheap raw materials and captive markets.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.
