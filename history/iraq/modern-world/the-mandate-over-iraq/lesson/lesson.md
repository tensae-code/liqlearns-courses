# The Mandate Over Iraq

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Occupation becomes mandate. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Occupation becomes mandate

The post-war settlement puts the peoples detached from the Ottoman state under mandate, and Iraqis begin meeting — in homes and mosques, Najaf among them — to demand freedom.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the post-war settlement puts the peoples detached from the Ottoman state under mandate, and Iraqis begin meeting — in homes and mosques, Najaf among them — to demand freedom. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Mandate Over Iraq. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
