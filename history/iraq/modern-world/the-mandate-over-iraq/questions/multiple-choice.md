# The Mandate Over Iraq — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Occupation becomes mandate”?
a) The post-war settlement puts the peoples detached from the Ottoman state under mandate, and Iraqis begin meeting — in homes and mosques, Najaf among them — to demand freedom.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Occupation becomes mandate” is right?
a) The post-war settlement puts the peoples detached from the Ottoman state under mandate, and Iraqis begin meeting — in homes and mosques, Najaf among them — to demand freedom.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
