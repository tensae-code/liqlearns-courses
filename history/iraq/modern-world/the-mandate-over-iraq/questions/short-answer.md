# The Mandate Over Iraq — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Occupation becomes mandate”?
**Answer:** The post-war settlement puts the peoples detached from the Ottoman state under mandate, and Iraqis begin meeting — in homes and mosques, Najaf among them — to demand freedom.
