# The Mandate Over Iraq — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The post-war settlement puts the peoples detached from the Ottoman state under mandate, and Iraqis begin meeting — in homes and mosques, Najaf among them — to demand freedom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Occupation becomes mandate” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The post-war settlement puts the peoples detached from the Ottoman state under mandate, and Iraqis begin meeting — in homes and mosques, Najaf among them — to demand freedom.
