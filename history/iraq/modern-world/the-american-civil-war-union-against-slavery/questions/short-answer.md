# The American Civil War: Union Against Slavery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The American Civil War 1861–1865”?
**Answer:** Industrial North against plantation South; twenty-three states fight eleven for four years from April 1861, and Lincoln is remembered as the emancipator.

**2.** What came of “The American Civil War 1861–1865”, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** Industrial North against plantation South; twenty-three states fight eleven for four years from April 1861, and Lincoln is remembered as the emancipator.
