# The American Civil War: Union Against Slavery — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Industrial North against plantation South; twenty-three states fight eleven for four years from April 1861, and Lincoln is remembered as the emancipator.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The American Civil War 1861–1865” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Industrial North against plantation South; twenty-three states fight eleven for four years from April 1861, and Lincoln is remembered as the emancipator.
