# The American Civil War: Union Against Slavery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The American Civil War 1861–1865”?
a) Industrial North against plantation South; twenty-three states fight eleven for four years from April 1861, and Lincoln is remembered as the emancipator.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The American Civil War 1861–1865” is right?
a) Industrial North against plantation South; twenty-three states fight eleven for four years from April 1861, and Lincoln is remembered as the emancipator.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
