# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The world after 1945”?
a) The book ends on the divided, rebuilding world the pupils' own century inherits.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The world after 1945” is right?
a) The book ends on the divided, rebuilding world the pupils' own century inherits.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
