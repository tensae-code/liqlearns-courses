# The World After 1945: Losses, Division and Rebuilding

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The world after 1945. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The world after 1945

The book ends on the divided, rebuilding world the pupils' own century inherits.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.
