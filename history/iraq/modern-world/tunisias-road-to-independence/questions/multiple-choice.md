# Tunisia's Road to Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Tunisia's liberation”?
a) From the 1911–12 clashes and arrests, through Bourguiba's party backed by the ruling family, arrests breed armed resistance until France negotiates independence.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Tunisia's liberation” is right?
a) From the 1911–12 clashes and arrests, through Bourguiba's party backed by the ruling family, arrests breed armed resistance until France negotiates independence.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
