# Tunisia's Road to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Tunisia's liberation”?
**Answer:** From the 1911–12 clashes and arrests, through Bourguiba's party backed by the ruling family, arrests breed armed resistance until France negotiates independence.
