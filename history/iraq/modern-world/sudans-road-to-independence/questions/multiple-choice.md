# Sudan's Road to Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Sudan's liberation”?
a) Sudanese reject imposed schemes, rise in tribal revolts after the war under Egypt's influence, and refuse a half-Sudanese legislature as less than independence.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Sudan's liberation” is right?
a) Sudanese reject imposed schemes, rise in tribal revolts after the war under Egypt's influence, and refuse a half-Sudanese legislature as less than independence.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
