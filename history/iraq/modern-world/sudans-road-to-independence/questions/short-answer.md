# Sudan's Road to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Sudan's liberation”?
**Answer:** Sudanese reject imposed schemes, rise in tribal revolts after the war under Egypt's influence, and refuse a half-Sudanese legislature as less than independence.
