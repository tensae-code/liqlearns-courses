# The Congress of Vienna, 1815 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The Congress of Vienna 1815”?
a) Europe's victors redraw the map, restore the old order, abolish the slave trade on paper — and ignore the national feeling growing underneath.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The Congress of Vienna 1815” is right?
a) Europe's victors redraw the map, restore the old order, abolish the slave trade on paper — and ignore the national feeling growing underneath.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
