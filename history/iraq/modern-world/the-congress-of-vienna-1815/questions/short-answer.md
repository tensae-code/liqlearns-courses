# The Congress of Vienna, 1815 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The Congress of Vienna 1815”?
**Answer:** Europe's victors redraw the map, restore the old order, abolish the slave trade on paper — and ignore the national feeling growing underneath.
