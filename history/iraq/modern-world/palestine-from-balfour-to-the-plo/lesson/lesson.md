# Palestine: From Balfour to the PLO

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Palestine. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Palestine

Palestinians reject the Balfour promise and the mandate in demonstrations and strikes; al-Qassam's men open the 1936 revolt from Jaffa's general strike; America throws its weight behind Zionism, the question goes to the UN, and the PLO is founded in 1964 to lead the armed factions.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: palestinians reject the Balfour promise and the mandate in demonstrations and strikes; al-Qassam's men open the 1936 revolt from Jaffa's general strike. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Palestine: From Balfour to the PLO. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
