# Palestine: From Balfour to the PLO — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Palestinians reject the Balfour promise and the mandate in demonstrations and strikes; al-Qassam's men open the 1936 revolt from Jaffa's general strike; America throws its weight behind Zionism, the question goes to the UN, and the PLO is founded in 1964 to lead the armed factions.
