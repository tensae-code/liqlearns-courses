# Palestine: From Balfour to the PLO — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Palestine”?
a) Palestinians reject the Balfour promise and the mandate in demonstrations and strikes; al-Qassam's men open the 1936 revolt from Jaffa's general strike; America throws its weight behind Zionism, the question goes to the UN, and the PLO is founded in 1964 to lead the armed factions.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Palestine” is right?
a) Palestinians reject the Balfour promise and the mandate in demonstrations and strikes; al-Qassam's men open the 1936 revolt from Jaffa's general strike; America throws its weight behind Zionism, the question goes to the UN, and the PLO is founded in 1964 to lead the armed factions.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
