# Midhat Pasha and Ottoman Reforms in Iraq

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Midhat Pasha's reforms in Baghdad. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Midhat Pasha's reforms in Baghdad

The reforming governor founds the government press and Iraq's first newspaper, tackles tribal land settlement — enriching some sheikhs into great landlords — and builds factories and funds.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.
