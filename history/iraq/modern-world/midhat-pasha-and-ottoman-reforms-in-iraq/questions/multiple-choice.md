# Midhat Pasha and Ottoman Reforms in Iraq — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Midhat Pasha's reforms in Baghdad”?
a) The reforming governor founds the government press and Iraq's first newspaper, tackles tribal land settlement — enriching some sheikhs into great landlords — and builds factories and funds.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
