# The Second World War: Course in Europe

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: The Second World War in Europe 1939–1945. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## The Second World War in Europe 1939–1945

The European war runs its course from invasion to the Axis collapse.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the European war runs its course from invasion to the Axis collapse. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: The Second World War: Course in Europe. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
