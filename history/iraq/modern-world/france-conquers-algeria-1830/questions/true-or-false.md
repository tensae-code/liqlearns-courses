# France Conquers Algeria, 1830 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France declares war in 1827, blockades the ports for three years and takes Algiers on 5 July 1830.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Algeria conquered” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: France declares war in 1827, blockades the ports for three years and takes Algiers on 5 July 1830.
