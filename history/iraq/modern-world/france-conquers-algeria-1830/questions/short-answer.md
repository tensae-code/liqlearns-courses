# France Conquers Algeria, 1830 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Algeria conquered”?
**Answer:** France declares war in 1827, blockades the ports for three years and takes Algiers on 5 July 1830.

**2.** What came of “Algeria conquered”, as the lesson tells it?
**Answer:** France declares war in 1827, blockades the ports for three years and takes Algiers on 5 July 1830.
