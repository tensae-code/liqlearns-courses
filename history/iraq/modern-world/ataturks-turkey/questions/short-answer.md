# Atatürk's Turkey — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Turkey remade”?
**Answer:** The Turkish republic rewrites its 1924 constitution by 1939 in work, education, belief and personal freedom — the pupil's nearest example of forced modernisation.
