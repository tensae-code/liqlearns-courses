# Republic and Reign of Terror — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Republic, Convention and the road to the Directory”?
**Answer:** Monarchy is abolished and the republic proclaimed after Valmy; constitutions multiply until the five-man Directory fails to give France calm.
