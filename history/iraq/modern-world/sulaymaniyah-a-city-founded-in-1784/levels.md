# Sulaymaniyah, a City Founded in 1784 — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — unlike the ancient cities around it, Sulaymaniyah is a planned new city of the late Ottoman centuries that grew into Kurdistan's cultural capital — in their own words, give one real example from the story as taught, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
