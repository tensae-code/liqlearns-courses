# Iraq and the Second World War: 1941 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Iraq and the Second World War: 1941”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** With France fallen, Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's National Defence government forms after the Regent flees to Basra; Britain lands troops at Basra and from Jordan and wins the May 1941 war.

**2.** What came of “Ch.5 Iraq from the Second World War to 1958: the 1941 uprising”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The National Defence Government, the May 1941 rising and Britain's second occupation open the final Iraq chapter.
