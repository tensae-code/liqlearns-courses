# Iraq and the Second World War: 1941 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** With France fallen, Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's National Defence government forms after the Regent flees to Basra; Britain lands troops at Basra and from Jordan and wins the May 1941 war. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Ch.5 Iraq from the Second World War to 1958: the 1941 uprising” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: The National Defence Government, the May 1941 rising and Britain's second occupation open the final Iraq chapter.
