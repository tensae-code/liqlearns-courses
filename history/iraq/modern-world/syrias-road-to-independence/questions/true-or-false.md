# Syria's Road to Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** France takes Damascus after Maysalun in 1920; only when France itself falls in June 1940 does Syria's road to independence open.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Syria's liberation” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: France takes Damascus after Maysalun in 1920; only when France itself falls in June 1940 does Syria's road to independence open.
