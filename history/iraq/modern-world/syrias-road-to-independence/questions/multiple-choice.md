# Syria's Road to Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Syria's liberation”?
a) France takes Damascus after Maysalun in 1920; only when France itself falls in June 1940 does Syria's road to independence open.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Syria's liberation” is right?
a) France takes Damascus after Maysalun in 1920; only when France itself falls in June 1940 does Syria's road to independence open.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
