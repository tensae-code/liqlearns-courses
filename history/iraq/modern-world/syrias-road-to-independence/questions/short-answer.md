# Syria's Road to Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Syria's liberation”?
**Answer:** France takes Damascus after Maysalun in 1920; only when France itself falls in June 1940 does Syria's road to independence open.
