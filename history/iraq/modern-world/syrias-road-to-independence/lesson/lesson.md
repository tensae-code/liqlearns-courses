# Syria's Road to Independence

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Syria's liberation. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Syria's liberation

France takes Damascus after Maysalun in 1920; only when France itself falls in June 1940 does Syria's road to independence open.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: france takes Damascus after Maysalun in 1920; only when France itself falls in June 1940 does Syria's road to independence open. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Syria's Road to Independence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
