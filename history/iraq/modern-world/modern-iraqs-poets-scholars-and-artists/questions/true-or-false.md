# Modern Iraq's Poets, Scholars and Artists — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Al-Jawahiri sings to the Tigris from exile; al-Sayyab, Nazik al-Malaika, Sargon Boulus and the Kurdish poet Goran voice their homeland; Mandaean physicist Abdul Jabbar Abdullah becomes Baghdad University's first president after 1958; sculptor Jawad Saleem raises the Freedom Monument in Tahrir Square.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Modern Iraq's poets, scholars and artists” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Al-Jawahiri sings to the Tigris from exile; al-Sayyab, Nazik al-Malaika, Sargon Boulus and the Kurdish poet Goran voice their homeland; Mandaean physicist Abdul Jabbar Abdullah becomes Baghdad University's first president after 1958; sculptor Jawad Saleem raises the Freedom Monument in Tahrir Square.
