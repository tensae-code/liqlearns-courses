# Modern Iraq's Poets, Scholars and Artists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Modern Iraq's poets, scholars and artists”?
a) Al-Jawahiri sings to the Tigris from exile; al-Sayyab, Nazik al-Malaika, Sargon Boulus and the Kurdish poet Goran voice their homeland; Mandaean physicist Abdul Jabbar Abdullah becomes Baghdad University's first president after 1958; sculptor Jawad Saleem raises the Freedom Monument in Tahrir Square.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Modern Iraq's poets, scholars and artists” is right?
a) Al-Jawahiri sings to the Tigris from exile; al-Sayyab, Nazik al-Malaika, Sargon Boulus and the Kurdish poet Goran voice their homeland; Mandaean physicist Abdul Jabbar Abdullah becomes Baghdad University's first president after 1958; sculptor Jawad Saleem raises the Freedom Monument in Tahrir Square.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
