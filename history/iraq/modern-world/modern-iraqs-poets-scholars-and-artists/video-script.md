# Video script — Modern Iraq's Poets, Scholars and Artists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — an animated map, plain words: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Al-jawahiri sings to the tigris from exile; al-sayyab, nazik al-malaika.

On the map, follow this part: Modern Iraq's poets, scholars and artists. Al-Jawahiri sings to the Tigris from exile; al-Sayyab, Nazik al-Malaika, Sargon Boulus and the Kurdish poet Goran voice their homeland; Mandaean physicist Abdul Jabbar Abdullah becomes Baghdad University's first president after 1958; sculptor Jawad Saleem raises the Freedom Monument in Tahrir Square.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. Then go and do the lesson.
