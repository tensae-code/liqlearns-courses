# Sudan: The Mahdiyya and Condominium

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Sudan: the Mahdi and the dual rule. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sudan: the Mahdi and the dual rule

Muhammad Ahmad the Mahdi rises in 1881; after his state falls, Sudan is ruled jointly by Britain and Egypt under a British governor-general.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: muhammad Ahmad the Mahdi rises in 1881; after his state falls, Sudan is ruled jointly by Britain and Egypt under a British governor-general. Retell the story in order — who, where, what turned, what came of it — and name the lesson's key words: Sudan: The Mahdiyya and Condominium. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
