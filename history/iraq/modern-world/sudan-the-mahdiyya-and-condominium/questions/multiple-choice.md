# Sudan: The Mahdiyya and Condominium — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Sudan: the Mahdi and the dual rule”?
a) Muhammad Ahmad the Mahdi rises in 1881; after his state falls, Sudan is ruled jointly by Britain and Egypt under a British governor-general.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Sudan: the Mahdi and the dual rule” is right?
a) Muhammad Ahmad the Mahdi rises in 1881; after his state falls, Sudan is ruled jointly by Britain and Egypt under a British governor-general.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
