# Sudan: The Mahdiyya and Condominium — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Muhammad Ahmad the Mahdi rises in 1881; after his state falls, Sudan is ruled jointly by Britain and Egypt under a British governor-general.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Sudan: the Mahdi and the dual rule” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Muhammad Ahmad the Mahdi rises in 1881; after his state falls, Sudan is ruled jointly by Britain and Egypt under a British governor-general.
