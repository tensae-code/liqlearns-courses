# Iraq Joins the United Nations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “Iraq helps found the United Nations”?
a) Iraq plays a leading part in drafting the UN Charter — 111 articles in nineteen chapters for keeping the peace — closing the book's modern story.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own

a) Iraq plays a leading part in drafting the UN Charter — 111 articles in nineteen chapters for keeping the peace — closing the book's modern story.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no learner ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Iraq's books
