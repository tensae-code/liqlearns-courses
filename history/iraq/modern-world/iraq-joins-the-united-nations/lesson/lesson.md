# Iraq Joins the United Nations

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Iraq helps found the United Nations. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Iraq helps found the United Nations

Iraq plays a leading part in drafting the UN Charter — 111 articles in nineteen chapters for keeping the peace — closing the book's modern story.

Hold the turn in mind: who is in this story, where it happens, and what changes because of it. That turn is the whole lesson.
