# The Great Depression, 1929 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “The world economic crisis of 1929”?
**Answer:** Wall Street crashes on 24 October 1929 as revived Europe stops buying; the crisis circles the globe.
