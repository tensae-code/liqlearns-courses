# The Great Depression, 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Wall Street crashes on 24 October 1929 as revived Europe stops buying; the crisis circles the globe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “The world economic crisis of 1929” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Wall Street crashes on 24 October 1929 as revived Europe stops buying; the crisis circles the globe.
