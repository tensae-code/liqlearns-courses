Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these tells the story of “The world economic crisis of 1929”?
a) Wall Street crashes on 24 October 1929 as revived Europe stops buying; the crisis circles the globe.  b) that this part never happened and the lesson invents it  c) that it concerns only officials and never ordinary people  d) that it is the same story in every country, with no Iraqi way of its own
