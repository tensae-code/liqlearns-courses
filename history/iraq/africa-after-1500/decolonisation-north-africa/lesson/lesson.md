# Decolonisation: North Africa

## Do it first

Before you read, draw a line across a page. At the left end write "before", at the right end "after". Under it, write the names of the parts of this story in the order you expect to meet them: Ch.7 Arab liberation: Egypt's road; Libya's resistance and independence; Algeria's liberation movement. As you read, check your order against the story's own order — history is learned by following what turned, and when.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch.7 Arab liberation: Egypt's road

Crowds raise the slogans — end the protectorate, independence, evacuation — until Britain sends a high commissioner to face a nation in revolt.
