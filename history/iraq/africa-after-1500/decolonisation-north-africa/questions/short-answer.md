# Decolonisation: North Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what happens in “Ch.7 Arab liberation: Egypt's road”? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Crowds raise the slogans — end the protectorate, independence, evacuation — until Britain sends a high commissioner to face a nation in revolt.

**2.** What came of “Algeria's liberation movement”, as the lesson tells it? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** Algerian workers in France found the Star of North Africa in 1926; post-war parties demand separation from France, and the road runs to the 1954 revolution.
