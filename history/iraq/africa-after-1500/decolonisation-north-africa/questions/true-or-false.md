# Decolonisation: North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Crowds raise the slogans — end the protectorate, independence, evacuation — until Britain sends a high commissioner to face a nation in revolt. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that “Algeria's liberation movement” is a story from another country that Iraq's books never mention. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches: Algerian workers in France found the Star of North Africa in 1926; post-war parties demand separation from France, and the road runs to the 1954 revolution.
