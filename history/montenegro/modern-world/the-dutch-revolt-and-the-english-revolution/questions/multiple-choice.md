# The Dutch Revolt And The English Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.4 Revolutions in the Netherlands and England" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The old estates order breaks, and political change comes first, then change in every sphere of life
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The old estates order breaks, and political change comes first, then change in every sphere of life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.4 Revolutions in the Netherlands and England"?
a) The old estates order breaks, and political change comes first, then change in every sphere of life  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
