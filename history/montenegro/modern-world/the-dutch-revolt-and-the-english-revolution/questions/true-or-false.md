# The Dutch Revolt And The English Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The old estates order breaks, and political change comes first, then change in every sphere of life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.4 Revolutions in the Netherlands and England" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
