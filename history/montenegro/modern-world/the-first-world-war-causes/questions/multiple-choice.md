# The First World War Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.1 The First World War" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The occasion for the war was the assassination of the Austro-Hungarian heir Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 by members of Young Bosnia
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The occasion for the war was the assassination of the Austro-Hungarian heir Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 by members of Young Bosnia** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.1 The First World War"?
a) The occasion for the war was the assassination of the Austro-Hungarian heir Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 by members of Young Bosnia  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) The occasion for the war was the assassination of the Austro-Hungarian heir Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 by members of Young Bosnia** — That is what this lesson teaches.
