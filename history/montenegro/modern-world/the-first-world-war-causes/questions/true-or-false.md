# The First World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The occasion for the war was the assassination of the Austro-Hungarian heir Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo on 28 June 1914 by members of Young Bosnia.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.1 The First World War" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
