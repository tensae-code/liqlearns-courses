# Montenegro Becomes a Constitutional Monarchy and Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A constitutional monarchy: by 1905 only Russia and Montenegro in Europe have no constitution; when the tsar is forced to grant one in October 1905, Montenegro is left the only European country whose power does not rest on a constitution, and Nikola grants one.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.4.3 Montenegro" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
