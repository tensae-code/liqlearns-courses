Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** A constitutional monarchy: by ____ only Russia and Montenegro in Europe have no constitution; when the tsar is forced to grant one in October 1905, Montenegro is left the only European country whose power does not rest on a constitution, and Nikola grants one.
