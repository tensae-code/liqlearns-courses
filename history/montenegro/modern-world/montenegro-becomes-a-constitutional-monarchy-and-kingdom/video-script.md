# Video script — Montenegro Becomes a Constitutional Monarchy and Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. A constitutional monarchy: by 1905 only Russia and Montenegro in Europe have no constitution; when the tsar is forced to grant one in October 1905, Montenegro is left the only European country whose power does not rest on a constitution, and Nikola grants one.

Now the part called "Ch.4.3 Montenegro". A constitutional monarchy: by 1905 only Russia and Montenegro in Europe have no constitution; when the tsar is forced to grant one in October 1905, Montenegro is left the only European country whose power does not rest on a constitution, and Nikola grants one. In the story's own turn: the last absolute ruler in Europe gives his people a constitution because he refuses to be last.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
