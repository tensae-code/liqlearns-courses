1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
