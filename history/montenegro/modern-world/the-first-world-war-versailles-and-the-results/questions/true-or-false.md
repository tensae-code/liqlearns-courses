# The First World War Versailles And The Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.3 The consequences of the First World War" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
