# The First World War Versailles And The Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.3 The consequences of the First World War" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.3 The consequences of the First World War"?
a) Some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons** — That is what this lesson teaches.
