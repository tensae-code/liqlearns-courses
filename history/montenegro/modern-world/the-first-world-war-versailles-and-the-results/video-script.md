# Video script — The First World War Versailles And The Results

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. Some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons.

Now the part called "Ch.1.3 The consequences of the First World War". Some 66 million men mobilised, the whole economy subordinated to war production, and terrible losses from mass destructive weapons. In the story's own turn: four years of total war leave a ruined, indebted, redrawn Europe.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
