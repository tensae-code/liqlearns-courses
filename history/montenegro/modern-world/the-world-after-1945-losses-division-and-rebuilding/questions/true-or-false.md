# The World After 1945 Losses Division And Rebuilding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At Potsdam in 1945 the Allies confirm Germany's division into four zones (USSR, USA, Britain, France), and Berlin, though inside the Soviet zone, is divided into four sectors.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.3 The world after the Second World War" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
