# Montenegro 1851–1878: Principality and Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.4.1 Montenegro 1851–1876" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire. In the story's own turn: a secular prince replaces the prince-bishops, and a battlefield victory makes the powers draw Montenegro's borders.

**2.** What came of "Ch.4.2 Montenegro 1876–1905" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** From the Podgorica incident (the killing of the Ottoman official Juso Mučin Krnić and the revenge killing of some twenty innocent Montenegrins) through the 1876–78 war and international recognition to Prince Nikola's long rule. In the story's own turn: a murder in a border town lights the war that finally makes Montenegro an internationally recognised state.
