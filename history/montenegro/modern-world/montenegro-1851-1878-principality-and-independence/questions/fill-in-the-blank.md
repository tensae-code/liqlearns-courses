# Montenegro 1851–1878: Principality and Independence — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Danilo

**2.** From the Podgorica incident (the killing of the Ottoman official Juso Mučin Krnić and the revenge killing of some twenty innocent Montenegrins) through the ____–78 war and international recognition to Prince Nikola's long rule. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1876
