# Montenegro 1851–1878: Principality and Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.4.2 Montenegro 1876–1905" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
