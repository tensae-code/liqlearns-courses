# Video script — Montenegro 1851–1878: Principality and Independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

Now the part called "Ch.4.1 Montenegro 1851–1876". Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire. In the story's own turn: a secular prince replaces the prince-bishops, and a battlefield victory makes the powers draw Montenegro's borders.

Now the part called "Ch.4.2 Montenegro 1876–1905". From the Podgorica incident (the killing of the Ottoman official Juso Mučin Krnić and the revenge killing of some twenty innocent Montenegrins) through the 1876–78 war and international recognition to Prince Nikola's long rule. In the story's own turn: a murder in a border town lights the war that finally makes Montenegro an internationally recognised state.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
