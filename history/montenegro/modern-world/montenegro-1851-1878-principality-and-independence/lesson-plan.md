# Lesson plan — Montenegro 1851–1878: Principality and Independence

**Course:** History — Montenegro · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Montenegro 1851–1878: Principality and Independence
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Discoveries, Revolutions and Montenegro Builds a State (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire; From the Podgorica incident (the killing of the Ottoman official Juso Mučin Krnić and the revenge killing of some twenty innocent Montenegrins) through the 1876–78 war and international recognition to Prince Nikola's long rule.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Discoveries, Revolutions and Montenegro Builds a State — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire; From the Podgorica incident (the killing of the Ottoman official Juso Mučin Krnić and the revenge killing of some twenty innocent Montenegrins) through the 1876–78 war and international recognition to Prince Nikola's long rule. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
