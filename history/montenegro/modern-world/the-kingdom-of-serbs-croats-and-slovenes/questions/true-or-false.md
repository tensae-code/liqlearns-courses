# The Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Serbian government works after the war to unite all Serbs in one great state, counting on Montenegro's annexation and parts of Austro-Hungarian territory, its size depending on the Entente's stance.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.4 The Kingdom of SHS" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
