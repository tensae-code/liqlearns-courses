# The Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.2.4 The Kingdom of SHS" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The Serbian government works after the war to unite all Serbs in one great state, counting on Montenegro's annexation and parts of Austro-Hungarian territory, its size depending on the Entente's stance
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
