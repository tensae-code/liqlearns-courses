# Video script — The Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Serbian government works after the war to unite all Serbs in one great state, counting on Montenegro's annexation and parts of Austro-Hungarian territory, its size depending on the Entente's stance.

Now the part called "Ch.2.4 The Kingdom of SHS". The Serbian government works after the war to unite all Serbs in one great state, counting on Montenegro's annexation and parts of Austro-Hungarian territory, its size depending on the Entente's stance. In the story's own turn: a new South Slav kingdom is assembled from a victorious Serbia and the ruins of two empires.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
