# American Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.3.1" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The American War of Independence (1775–1783) ends with the creation of the United States
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The American War of Independence (1775–1783) ends with the creation of the United States** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.3.1"?
a) The American War of Independence (1775–1783) ends with the creation of the United States  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) The American War of Independence (1775–1783) ends with the creation of the United States** — That is what this lesson teaches.
