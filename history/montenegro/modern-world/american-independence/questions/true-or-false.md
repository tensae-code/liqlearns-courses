# American Independence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The American War of Independence (1775–1783) ends with the creation of the United States.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
