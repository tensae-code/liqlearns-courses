# Napoleon Reformer Conqueror Fallen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Napoleon's wars, the Congress of Vienna 1814/1815, and revolution in Europe in 1848.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.1" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
