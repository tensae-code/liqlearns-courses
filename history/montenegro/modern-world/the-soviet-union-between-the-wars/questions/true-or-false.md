# The Soviet Union Between The Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Land to the peasants without compensation, factories and banks nationalised.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.5 The world of socialism: the Bolshevik government introduces war communism during the civil war of 1918–1920" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
