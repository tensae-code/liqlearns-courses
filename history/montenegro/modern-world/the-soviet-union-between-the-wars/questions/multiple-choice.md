# The Soviet Union Between The Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.5 The world of socialism: the Bolshevik government introduces war communism during the civil war of 1918–1920" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Land to the peasants without compensation, factories and banks nationalised
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Land to the peasants without compensation, factories and banks nationalised** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.5 The world of socialism: the Bolshevik government introduces war communism during the civil war of 1918–1920"?
a) Land to the peasants without compensation, factories and banks nationalised  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Land to the peasants without compensation, factories and banks nationalised** — That is what this lesson teaches.
