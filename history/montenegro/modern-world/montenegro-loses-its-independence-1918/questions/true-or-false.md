# Montenegro Loses Its Independence 1918 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The loss of state independence: before the war the idea of unification with Serbia is widespread in Montenegro, but Cetinje and Belgrade never agree on how; King Nikola's state disappears into the unification.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.3 Montenegro 1918–1921" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
