# Exploration Causes And Results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** New sea routes above all to India, then all the oceans, and lands and continents unknown until then.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.1 The great geographical discoveries and the rise of Europe" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
