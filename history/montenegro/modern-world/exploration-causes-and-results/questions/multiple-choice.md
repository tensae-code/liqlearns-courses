# Exploration Causes And Results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.1 The great geographical discoveries and the rise of Europe" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) New sea routes above all to India, then all the oceans, and lands and continents unknown until then
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) New sea routes above all to India, then all the oceans, and lands and continents unknown until then** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.1 The great geographical discoveries and the rise of Europe"?
a) New sea routes above all to India, then all the oceans, and lands and continents unknown until then  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) New sea routes above all to India, then all the oceans, and lands and continents unknown until then** — That is what this lesson teaches.
