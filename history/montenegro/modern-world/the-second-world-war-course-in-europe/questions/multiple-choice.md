# The Second World War Course In Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.3.2 War operations 1942–1945: to mid-1942 the Axis advances everywhere" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Japan takes most of south-east Asia, most of Europe is in Tripartite hands, German submarines torpedo Atlantic shipping — then the tide turns
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Japan takes most of south-east Asia, most of Europe is in Tripartite hands, German submarines torpedo Atlantic shipping — then the tide turns** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.3.2 War operations 1942–1945: to mid-1942 the Axis advances everywhere"?
a) Japan takes most of south-east Asia, most of Europe is in Tripartite hands, German submarines torpedo Atlantic shipping — then the tide turns  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Japan takes most of south-east Asia, most of Europe is in Tripartite hands, German submarines torpedo Atlantic shipping — then the tide turns** — That is what this lesson teaches.
