# The Second World War Course In Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Japan takes most of south-east Asia, most of Europe is in Tripartite hands, German submarines torpedo Atlantic shipping — then the tide turns.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.2 War operations 1942–1945: to mid-1942 the Axis advances everywhere" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
