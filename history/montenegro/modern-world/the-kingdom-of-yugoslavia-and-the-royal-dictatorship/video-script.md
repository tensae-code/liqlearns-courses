# Video script — The Kingdom Of Yugoslavia And The Royal Dictatorship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. And royal dictatorship follows.

Now the part called "Ch.2.5 The Kingdom of Yugoslavia 1929–1941: of all the nationally dissatisfied Yugoslav peoples the Croats resist most; the conflict culminates in parliament when a Radical deputy mortally wounds Croatian Peasant Party representatives after mutual insults". And royal dictatorship follows. In the story's own turn: shots in the parliament kill Yugoslav democracy, and the king rules by decree.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
