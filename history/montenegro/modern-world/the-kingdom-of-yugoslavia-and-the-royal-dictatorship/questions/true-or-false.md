# The Kingdom Of Yugoslavia And The Royal Dictatorship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** And royal dictatorship follows.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.5 The Kingdom of Yugoslavia 1929–1941: of all the nationally dissatisfied Yugoslav peoples the Croats resist most; the conflict culminates in parliament when a Radical deputy mortally wounds Croatian Peasant Party representatives after mutual insults" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
