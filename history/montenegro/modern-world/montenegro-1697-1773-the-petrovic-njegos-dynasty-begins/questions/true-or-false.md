# Montenegro 1697–1773: The Petrović Njegoš Dynasty Begins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** With Metropolitan Danilo's election in 1697 the Cetinje metropolitanate becomes the leading political institution of Montenegrin society and Danilo founds the fourth Montenegrin dynasty, Petrović Njegoš; ties with Russia are established, the guvernadur's office created, and the impostor-tsar Šćepan Mali rules. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.1 (continued)" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
