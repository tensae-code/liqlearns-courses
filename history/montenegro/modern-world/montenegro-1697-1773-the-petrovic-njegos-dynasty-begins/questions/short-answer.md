# Montenegro 1697–1773: The Petrović Njegoš Dynasty Begins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.2.1 Montenegro 1697–1773" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** With Metropolitan Danilo's election in 1697 the Cetinje metropolitanate becomes the leading political institution of Montenegrin society and Danilo founds the fourth Montenegrin dynasty, Petrović Njegoš; ties with Russia are established, the guvernadur's office created, and the impostor-tsar Šćepan Mali rules. In the story's own turn: a bishop becomes a hereditary prince, a tiny mountain theocracy finds a Russian patron, and a false tsar fools everyone.

**2.** What came of "Ch.2.1 (continued)" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The process of accepting Islam in the borderlands, and the myth of the "istraga poturica" (the investigation/extermination of converts) examined as a myth. In the story's own turn: the book itself teaches pupils to test a famous national legend against the evidence.
