# Montenegro 1697–1773: The Petrović Njegoš Dynasty Begins — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** With Metropolitan Danilo's election in ____ the Cetinje metropolitanate becomes the leading political institution of Montenegrin society and Danilo founds the fourth Montenegrin dynasty, Petrović Njegoš; ties with Russia are established, the guvernadur's office created, and the impostor-tsar Šćepan Mali rules. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** 1697

**2.** The process of accepting ____ in the borderlands, and the myth of the "istraga poturica" (the investigation/extermination of converts) examined as a myth. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Islam
