# Video script — Montenegro 1697–1773: The Petrović Njegoš Dynasty Begins

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. With Metropolitan Danilo's election in 1697 the Cetinje metropolitanate becomes the leading political institution of Montenegrin society and Danilo founds the fourth Montenegrin dynasty, Petrović Njegoš; ties with Russia are established, the guvernadur's office created, and the impostor-tsar Šćepan Mali rules; The process of accepting Islam in the borderlands, and the myth of the "istraga poturica" (the investigation/extermination of converts) examined as a myth.

Now the part called "Ch.2.1 Montenegro 1697–1773". With Metropolitan Danilo's election in 1697 the Cetinje metropolitanate becomes the leading political institution of Montenegrin society and Danilo founds the fourth Montenegrin dynasty, Petrović Njegoš; ties with Russia are established, the guvernadur's office created, and the impostor-tsar Šćepan Mali rules. In the story's own turn: a bishop becomes a hereditary prince, a tiny mountain theocracy finds a Russian patron, and a false tsar fools everyone.

Now the part called "Ch.2.1 (continued)". The process of accepting Islam in the borderlands, and the myth of the "istraga poturica" (the investigation/extermination of converts) examined as a myth. In the story's own turn: the book itself teaches pupils to test a famous national legend against the evidence.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
