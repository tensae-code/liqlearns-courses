# Montenegro under Ottoman Rule: The 1500s–1600s — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the fall under Ottoman rule, the harach and the other obligations of the raya are introduced; "Montenegro" in this period means the land seated at Cetinje.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.4.5 Montenegro in the 16th and 17th centuries" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
