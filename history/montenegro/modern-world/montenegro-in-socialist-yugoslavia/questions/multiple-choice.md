# Montenegro In Socialist Yugoslavia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.5.3 Montenegro in the SFRJ" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The rebuilding of war-destroyed Montenegro begins immediately after liberation and lasts to the spring of 1947, driven by voluntary labour
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The rebuilding of war-destroyed Montenegro begins immediately after liberation and lasts to the spring of 1947, driven by voluntary labour** — That is what this lesson teaches.
