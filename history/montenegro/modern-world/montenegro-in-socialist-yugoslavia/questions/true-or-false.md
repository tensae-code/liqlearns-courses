# Montenegro In Socialist Yugoslavia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The rebuilding of war-destroyed Montenegro begins immediately after liberation and lasts to the spring of 1947, driven by voluntary labour.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.5.3 Montenegro in the SFRJ" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
