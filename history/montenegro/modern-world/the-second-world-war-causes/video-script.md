# Video script — The Second World War Causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Germany attacks Poland; Britain and France declare war on 3 September but give Poland no effective help, and Poland is defeated within a month, its government fleeing to London.

Now the part called "Ch.3.1 The beginning of the Second World War". Germany attacks Poland; Britain and France declare war on 3 September but give Poland no effective help, and Poland is defeated within a month, its government fleeing to London. In the story's own turn: the democracies declare war and watch their ally fall.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
