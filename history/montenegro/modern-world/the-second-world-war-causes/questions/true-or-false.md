# The Second World War Causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Germany attacks Poland; Britain and France declare war on 3 September but give Poland no effective help, and Poland is defeated within a month, its government fleeing to London.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.1 The beginning of the Second World War" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
