# The Second World War Causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.3.1 The beginning of the Second World War" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Germany attacks Poland; Britain and France declare war on 3 September but give Poland no effective help, and Poland is defeated within a month, its government fleeing to London
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Germany attacks Poland; Britain and France declare war on 3 September but give Poland no effective help, and Poland is defeated within a month, its government fleeing to London** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.3.1 The beginning of the Second World War"?
a) Germany attacks Poland; Britain and France declare war on 3 September but give Poland no effective help, and Poland is defeated within a month, its government fleeing to London  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Germany attacks Poland; Britain and France declare war on 3 September but give Poland no effective help, and Poland is defeated within a month, its government fleeing to London** — That is what this lesson teaches.
