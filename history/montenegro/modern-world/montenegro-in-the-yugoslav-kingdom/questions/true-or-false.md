# Montenegro In The Yugoslav Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** From 1922 to 1929 Montenegro is the core of the Zeta oblast, and from 1929 to 1941 of the Zeta banovina.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.6 Montenegro in the Yugoslav kingdom: the kingdom's territorial order changes twice" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
