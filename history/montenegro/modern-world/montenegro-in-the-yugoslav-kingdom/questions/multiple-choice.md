# Montenegro In The Yugoslav Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.2.6 Montenegro in the Yugoslav kingdom: the kingdom's territorial order changes twice" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) From 1922 to 1929 Montenegro is the core of the Zeta oblast, and from 1929 to 1941 of the Zeta banovina
