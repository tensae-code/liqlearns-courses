## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Montenegro's books themselves work, telling the past as stories of people, places and turns.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Answered by the July uprising; In December 1943 the Italian partisan division "Garibaldi" is formed in Pljevlja — while German units rush in from Albania and Herzegovina. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
