# Video script — Montenegro in the Second World War: Occupation and Uprising

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Answered by the July uprising; In December 1943 the Italian partisan division "Garibaldi" is formed in Pljevlja — while German units rush in from Albania and Herzegovina.

Now the part called "Ch.4.2 Montenegro in the war to Italy's capitulation: in 1941 the Italians occupy Montenegro, annex Boka Kotorska, attach Ulcinj, Tuzi, Plav, Gusinje and Rožaje to Albania, and plan a nominally independent client state from the rest". Answered by the July uprising. In the story's own turn: an occupier tries to resurrect Montenegro as a puppet, and Montenegro rises instead.

Now the part called "Ch.4.4 Montenegro after Italy's capitulation: Italian divisions join the Yugoslav People's Liberation Army". In December 1943 the Italian partisan division "Garibaldi" is formed in Pljevlja — while German units rush in from Albania and Herzegovina. In the story's own turn: yesterday's occupiers become comrades-in-arms in Montenegro's mountains.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
