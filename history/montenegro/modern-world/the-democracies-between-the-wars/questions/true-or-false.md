# The Democracies Between The Wars — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Three separated powers, parliament holding legislative power — is the political ideal of most people (France, Britain, the USA as models).
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.4 The world of bourgeois democracy: in most European countries in the 1920s parliamentary democracy" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
