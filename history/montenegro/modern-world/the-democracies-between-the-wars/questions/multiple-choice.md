**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.4 The world of bourgeois democracy: in most European countries in the 1920s parliamentary democracy" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Three separated powers, parliament holding legislative power — is the political ideal of most people (France, Britain, the USA as models)
