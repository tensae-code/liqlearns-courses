# Serbia In The First World War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Austria-Hungary, accusing Serbia of complicity in the assassination, sends an ultimatum to a Serbia exhausted by the Balkan Wars.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.2 Serbia in the First World War" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
