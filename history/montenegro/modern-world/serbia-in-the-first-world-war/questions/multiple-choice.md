# Serbia In The First World War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.2.2 Serbia in the First World War" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Austria-Hungary, accusing Serbia of complicity in the assassination, sends an ultimatum to a Serbia exhausted by the Balkan Wars
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Austria-Hungary, accusing Serbia of complicity in the assassination, sends an ultimatum to a Serbia exhausted by the Balkan Wars** — That is what this lesson teaches.
