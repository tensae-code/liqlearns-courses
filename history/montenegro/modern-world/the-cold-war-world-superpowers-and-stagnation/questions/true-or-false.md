# The Cold War World Superpowers And Stagnation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A state that could send a man into space and destroy life on earth could not give its citizens the living standard of any leading Western country.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.4 The contemporary world: the Soviet paradox" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
