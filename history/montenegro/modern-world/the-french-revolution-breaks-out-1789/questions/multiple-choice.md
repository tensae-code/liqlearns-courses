# The French Revolution Breaks Out 1789 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.3.1" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The French bourgeois revolution, begun in 1789, the second of the era's two great revolutions
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The French bourgeois revolution, begun in 1789, the second of the era's two great revolutions** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.3.1"?
a) The French bourgeois revolution, begun in 1789, the second of the era's two great revolutions  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) The French bourgeois revolution, begun in 1789, the second of the era's two great revolutions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
