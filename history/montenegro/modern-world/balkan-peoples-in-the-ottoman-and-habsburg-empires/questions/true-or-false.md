# Balkan Peoples In The Ottoman And Habsburg Empires — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The great powers' military and political interests interlace in the Balkans; the sultan's reforms improve the position of the Christian population by privileges, and resistance to the reforms follows.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.2 Balkan societies in the Ottoman and Habsburg empires" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
