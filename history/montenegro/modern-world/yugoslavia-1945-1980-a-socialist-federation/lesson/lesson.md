# Yugoslavia 1945–1980: A Socialist Federation

## Do it first

Take a pencil and a page. Write the lesson's title at the top: "Yugoslavia 1945–1980: A Socialist Federation". Under it, leave one empty box for each part of the story, in order: Ch.5.1 Yugoslavia 1945–1961; Ch.5.2 Yugoslavia 1961–1980. As you read each part, fill its box with three words: who, where, and what turned. By the end of the lesson your page is the whole story in your own hand — and this lesson is about exactly what fills those boxes.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch.5.1 Yugoslavia 1945–1961

AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow. The turn of the story, as the book tells it: the Partisans' wartime parliament becomes a socialist federation of six republics.

Hold the turn in this part: who was there, where it happened, what changed, and what came of it — that turn is what makes it history, not just a list of names.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch.5.2 Yugoslavia 1961–1980

With Western economic aid and credits Yugoslavia invests heavily in industry, transport and energy, shifting from heavy industry toward light industry and living standards. The turn of the story, as the book tells it: a socialist state borrows from the West to build a consumer society of its own.

Notice what the story is really about underneath the names: people meeting, moving, ruling, believing or building — and the change their meeting made.

> Tip — from us, not from the books: hold any story in four questions — who, where, what turned, and what came of it. A story you can question is a story you can retell.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: fill the boxes again from memory, then check them against the lesson.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: draw the part that struck you most, and label it with who, where and what turned.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where this story's pattern shows itself. Note it.
