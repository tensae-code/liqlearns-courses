# Yugoslavia 1945–1980: A Socialist Federation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.5.2 Yugoslavia 1961–1980" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
