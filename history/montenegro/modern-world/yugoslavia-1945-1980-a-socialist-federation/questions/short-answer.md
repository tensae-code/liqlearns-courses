# Yugoslavia 1945–1980: A Socialist Federation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.5.1 Yugoslavia 1945–1961" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow. In the story's own turn: the Partisans' wartime parliament becomes a socialist federation of six republics.

**2.** What came of "Ch.5.2 Yugoslavia 1961–1980" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** With Western economic aid and credits Yugoslavia invests heavily in industry, transport and energy, shifting from heavy industry toward light industry and living standards. In the story's own turn: a socialist state borrows from the West to build a consumer society of its own.
