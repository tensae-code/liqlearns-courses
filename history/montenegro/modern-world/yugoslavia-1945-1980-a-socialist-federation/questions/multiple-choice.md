# Yugoslavia 1945–1980: A Socialist Federation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.5.1 Yugoslavia 1945–1961" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.5.2 Yugoslavia 1961–1980"? (Sub lesson 2)

**Answer: a) With Western economic aid and credits Yugoslavia invests heavily in industry, transport and energy, shifting from heavy industry toward light industry and living standards** — That is what this lesson teaches.
