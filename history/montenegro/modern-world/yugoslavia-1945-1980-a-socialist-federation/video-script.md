# Video script — Yugoslavia 1945–1980: A Socialist Federation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow; With Western economic aid and credits Yugoslavia invests heavily in industry, transport and energy, shifting from heavy industry toward light industry and living standards.

Now the part called "Ch.5.1 Yugoslavia 1945–1961". AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow. In the story's own turn: the Partisans' wartime parliament becomes a socialist federation of six republics.

Now the part called "Ch.5.2 Yugoslavia 1961–1980". With Western economic aid and credits Yugoslavia invests heavily in industry, transport and energy, shifting from heavy industry toward light industry and living standards. In the story's own turn: a socialist state borrows from the West to build a consumer society of its own.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
