# Scientific Revolution And Enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After 1648 Europe develops economy, state and culture; the Baroque style is born in Italy and spreads, and new scientific and enlightened ideas follow.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.5 Artistic and Enlightenment thought in early-modern Europe" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
