# Everyday Life In Interwar Yugoslavia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In 1921 about 79% of its working population farms small holdings with animal-drawn ploughs.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.7 The economic, social and cultural life of Yugoslavia 1918–1941: an agrarian country" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
