# Everyday Life In Interwar Yugoslavia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.2.7 The economic, social and cultural life of Yugoslavia 1918–1941: an agrarian country" tell?

b) In 1921 about 79% of its working population farms small holdings with animal-drawn ploughs
