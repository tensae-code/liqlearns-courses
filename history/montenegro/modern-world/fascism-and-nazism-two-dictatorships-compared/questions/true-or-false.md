# Fascism And Nazism Two Dictatorships Compared — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In crisis-hit Italy (inflation, unemployment, strikes) Mussolini, a former teacher and journalist, founds the Fasci di Combattimento in 1919, and the fascists fight the workers with factory owners and police.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.6 The world of fascism and Nazism" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
