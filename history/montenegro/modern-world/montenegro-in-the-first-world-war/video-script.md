# Video script — Montenegro In The First World War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Then the war swallows it.

Now the part called "Ch.2.1 Montenegro in the First World War: Montenegro faces consolidating its rule in the newly liberated lands, a great international loan for its finances, and plans to regulate Lake Skadar's level". Then the war swallows it. In the story's own turn: a small kingdom at its most hopeful moment is crushed between Austria-Hungary and its Serbian ally's ambitions.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
