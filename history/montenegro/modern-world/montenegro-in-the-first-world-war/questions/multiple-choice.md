# Montenegro In The First World War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.2.1 Montenegro in the First World War: Montenegro faces consolidating its rule in the newly liberated lands, a great international loan for its finances, and plans to regulate Lake Skadar's level" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Then the war swallows it
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Then the war swallows it** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Then the war swallows it  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
