# Exploration Portugal And The Sea Routes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** European sailors who knew only the Mediterranean and a corner of the Atlantic put out onto the oceans in Portuguese and Spanish service, seeking the sea route to India.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.5 Conditions in Europe in the second half of the 15th century" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
