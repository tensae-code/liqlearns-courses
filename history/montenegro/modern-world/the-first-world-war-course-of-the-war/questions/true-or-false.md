# The First World War Course Of The War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The course of the war to 1917, the main fronts, the entry of the USA into the war, and the war's end.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.1 (continued)" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
