# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Russia's ruling circles entered the war to block Austro-German supremacy and to take Constantinople from the Ottomans; war-weariness turns into the February and October revolutions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.2 The Revolution in Russia" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
