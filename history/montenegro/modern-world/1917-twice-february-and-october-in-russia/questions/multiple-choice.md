# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.2 The Revolution in Russia" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Russia's ruling circles entered the war to block Austro-German supremacy and to take Constantinople from the Ottomans; war-weariness turns into the February and October revolutions
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Russia's ruling circles entered the war to block Austro-German supremacy and to take Constantinople from the Ottomans; war-weariness turns into the February and October revolutions** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.2 The Revolution in Russia"?
a) Russia's ruling circles entered the war to block Austro-German supremacy and to take Constantinople from the Ottomans; war-weariness turns into the February and October revolutions  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Russia's ruling circles entered the war to block Austro-German supremacy and to take Constantinople from the Ottomans; war-weariness turns into the February and October revolutions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
