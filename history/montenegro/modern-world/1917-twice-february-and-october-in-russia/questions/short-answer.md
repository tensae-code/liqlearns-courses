# 1917 Twice: February and October in Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.1.2 The Revolution in Russia" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Russia's ruling circles entered the war to block Austro-German supremacy and to take Constantinople from the Ottomans; war-weariness turns into the February and October revolutions. In the story's own turn: an empire that went to war for the Straits collapses into revolution at home.
