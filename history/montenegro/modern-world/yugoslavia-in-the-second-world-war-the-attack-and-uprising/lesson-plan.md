## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Through the war's first 18 months Yugoslavia tries neutrality and compromise on the Croatian question; the March days of 1941 (the 25th and 27th) end the balancing, and the Axis attacks; As in the great offensive against the free territory of the Užice Republic in western Serbia at the end of 1941; The occupiers stir religious and national hatred among Yugoslav peoples and arm collaborators against the People's Liberation Movement, so the anti-fascist struggle also carries elements of civil war. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
