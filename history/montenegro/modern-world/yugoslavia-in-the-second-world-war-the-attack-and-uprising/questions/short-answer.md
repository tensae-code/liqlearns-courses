# Yugoslavia in the Second World War: The Attack and Uprising — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.4.1 The fascist attack on Yugoslavia" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Through the war's first 18 months Yugoslavia tries neutrality and compromise on the Croatian question; the March days of 1941 (the 25th and 27th) end the balancing, and the Axis attacks. In the story's own turn: a coup against joining the Axis brings the Luftwaffe over Belgrade within days.

**2.** What came of "Ch.4.3 The liberation war and revolution in Yugoslavia 1941–1945: the occupier's method is to encircle the main Partisan forces with superior strength and destroy them" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** As in the great offensive against the free territory of the Užice Republic in western Serbia at the end of 1941. In the story's own turn: a guerrilla army survives seven offensives and turns a liberation war into a revolution.
