# Yugoslavia in the Second World War: The Attack and Uprising — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Through the war's first 18 months Yugoslavia tries neutrality and compromise on the Croatian question; the March days of 1941 (the 25th and 27th) end the balancing, and the Axis attacks. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.4.3 The liberation war and revolution in Yugoslavia 1941–1945: the occupier's method is to encircle the main Partisan forces with superior strength and destroy them" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
