# Video script — Yugoslavia in the Second World War: The Attack and Uprising

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

Now the part called "Ch.4.1 The fascist attack on Yugoslavia". Through the war's first 18 months Yugoslavia tries neutrality and compromise on the Croatian question; the March days of 1941 (the 25th and 27th) end the balancing, and the Axis attacks. In the story's own turn: a coup against joining the Axis brings the Luftwaffe over Belgrade within days.

Now the part called "Ch.4.3 The liberation war and revolution in Yugoslavia 1941–1945: the occupier's method is to encircle the main Partisan forces with superior strength and destroy them". As in the great offensive against the free territory of the Užice Republic in western Serbia at the end of 1941. In the story's own turn: a guerrilla army survives seven offensives and turns a liberation war into a revolution.

Now the part called "Ch.4.5 The characteristics of the war in Yugoslavia 1941–1945". The occupiers stir religious and national hatred among Yugoslav peoples and arm collaborators against the People's Liberation Movement, so the anti-fascist struggle also carries elements of civil war. In the story's own turn: Yugoslavia's war is three wars at once.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
