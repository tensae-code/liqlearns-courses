# History — Montenegro course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Montenegro inside the History folder, organised by
**levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved
the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school
grade the books come from appears only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation.

**How the levels derive:** Montenegro's own books are the ladder.
The placements file's book order is the teaching order —
earliest books first: the Grade 6 book is a world-history year
(history itself, prehistory, the Ancient East, Greece, Rome)
that lands, in its final lesson, on home ground — the Illyrian
tribes and Roman ruins of Montenegro itself; the Grade 7 book
alternates Europe and home in strict rhythm — migration,
feudalism, Byzantium, the Church and Islam, then the Slavs
arrive and the book stays with Duklja/Zeta (Vojislavljevići,
Nemanjići, Balšići, Crnojevići) as Europe's late-medieval story
frames it; the Grade 8 book alternates two world chapters and
two home chapters — Europe of discoveries and revolutions, then
Montenegro 1697–1851 building a theocratic state, the world to
1914, then Montenegro 1851–1914 racing to catch it; and the
Grade 9 book teaches the twentieth century as a double story
throughout — every world chapter immediately followed by its
Montenegrin/Yugoslav chapter — ending where Montenegro's own
story ends: the breakup of Yugoslavia and the renewal of
Montenegrin independence. The four levels below group that
progression the way Montenegro's own story divides. The ladder
is a recommended path, never a forced gate.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — Montenegro's
Istorija (History) student books for Grades 6–9 — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient World** — the historian's first tools first — what history is, how its evidence speaks, and how its time is cut and counted — then the deep past in the books' own order: stone and metal, the river states of the Ancient East and Egypt, Greece from Crete to Alexander, Rome from a Latin town to a fallen empire, closing on home ground with the Illyrian tribes and Roman ruins of Montenegro itself. 19 lessons, in titles: `first-steps/` (3 lessons), `ancient-world/` (15 lessons), `africa-to-1500/` (1 lessons). 23 placements.
2. **Level 2 — The Medieval World and Medieval Montenegro** — Europe and home in strict rhythm, as the Grade 7 book alternates them: migration and feudalism, Byzantium, the Church and Islam, then the Slavs arrive and the book stays with Duklja and Zeta — Vojislavljevići, Nemanjići, Balšići, Crnojevići — as late-medieval Europe, the Ottoman advance and Montenegro's first Ottoman centuries frame it. 18 lessons, in titles: `ancient-world/` (16 lessons), `modern-world/` (2 lessons). 22 placements.
3. **Level 3 — Discoveries, Revolutions and Montenegro Builds a State** — two world chapters and two home chapters alternating, as the Grade 8 book runs them: discoveries, Renaissance, Reformation and the first revolutions, then Montenegro 1697–1851 building a theocratic state under the Petrović Njegoš prince-bishops; the industrial and revolutionary world to 1914, then Montenegro 1851–1914 racing to catch it — principality, wars, constitution and kingdom. 17 lessons, in title: `modern-world/` (17 lessons). 19 placements.
4. **Level 4 — The Twentieth Century and Independence Regained** — the double story the Grade 9 book tells throughout — every world chapter (the wars, the revolutions, the -isms, the Cold War) immediately followed by its Montenegrin and Yugoslav chapter — ending where Montenegro's own story ends: the breakup of Yugoslavia and the renewal of Montenegrin independence. 23 lessons, in title: `modern-world/` (23 lessons). 27 placements.

**Totals: 77 lessons, 91 placements** (Level 1: 19 lessons, 23 placements; Level 2: 18 lessons, 22 placements; Level 3: 17 lessons, 19 placements; Level 4: 23 lessons, 27 placements — all from four student books).
