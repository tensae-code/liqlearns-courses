# Dating The Past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.I §3: Why and how the past is divided into periods" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Clear economic, state and cultural breaks mark the periods of human development
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Clear economic, state and cultural breaks mark the periods of human development** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.I §4: How time is reckoned in history"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The calendar and dating events exactly  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) The calendar and dating events exactly** — That is what this lesson teaches.
