# Dating The Past — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.I §3: Why and how the past is divided into periods" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Clear economic, state and cultural breaks mark the periods of human development. In the story's own turn: historians cut the endless rope of time into named lengths so it can be held.

**2.** What came of "Ch.I §4: How time is reckoned in history" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The calendar and dating events exactly. In the story's own turn: once people can write, they need a date for everything, and the calendar becomes history's ruler.
