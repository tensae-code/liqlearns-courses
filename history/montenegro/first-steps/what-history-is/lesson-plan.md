# Lesson plan — What History Is

**Course:** History — Montenegro · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What History Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History as the science that studies the past of human society, and why it matters to learn it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient World — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: history as the science that studies the past of human society, and why it matters to learn it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school — one place where this lesson's story touches their own life, and how.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as Montenegro's books themselves work, telling the past as stories of people, places and turns.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: History as the science that studies the past of human society, and why it matters to learn it. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
