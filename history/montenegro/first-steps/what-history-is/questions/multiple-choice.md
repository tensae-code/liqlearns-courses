# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.I Introduction to History §1: What is history?" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) History as the science that studies the past of human society, and why it matters to learn it
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) History as the science that studies the past of human society, and why it matters to learn it** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.I Introduction to History §1: What is history?"?
a) History as the science that studies the past of human society, and why it matters to learn it  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) History as the science that studies the past of human society, and why it matters to learn it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
