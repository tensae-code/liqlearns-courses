# What History Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** History as the science that studies the past of human society, and why it matters to learn it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.I Introduction to History §1: What is history?" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
