# The Rise And Expansion Of Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.5 The Arabs and Islam" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) At the start of the 7th century a state forms on the Arabian peninsula among Bedouin tribal society that will reshape the Mediterranean and Europe
