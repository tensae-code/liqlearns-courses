# The Rise And Expansion Of Islam — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At the start of the 7th century a state forms on the Arabian peninsula among Bedouin tribal society that will reshape the Mediterranean and Europe.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.5 The Arabs and Islam" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
