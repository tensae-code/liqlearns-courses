# The Black Death And Late Medieval Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the great epidemic of 1347–1349 roughly a third of Europe's population dies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.2 Society in the developed and late Middle Ages: plague epidemics transform Europe" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
