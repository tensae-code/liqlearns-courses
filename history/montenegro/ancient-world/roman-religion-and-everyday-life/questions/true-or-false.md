# Roman Religion And Everyday Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Household gods fed before meals, Roman religion under strong Greek influence from the 4th century BCE; farming of vines, olives and grain around the Mediterranean.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.V §4–5: What the ancient Romans believed, and the economy of ancient Rome" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
