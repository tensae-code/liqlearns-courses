# Illyrians and Roman Rule in Montenegro — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Illyrian tribes (Ardiaei, Labeatae, Docleatae, Pirusti) on the land of today's Montenegro before and under Rome's conquest.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.V §7: The territory of Montenegro in the Roman state" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
