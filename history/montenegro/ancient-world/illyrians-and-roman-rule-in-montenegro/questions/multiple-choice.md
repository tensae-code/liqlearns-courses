# Illyrians and Roman Rule in Montenegro — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.V §7: The territory of Montenegro in the Roman state" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The Illyrian tribes (Ardiaei, Labeatae, Docleatae, Pirusti) on the land of today's Montenegro before and under Rome's conquest
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The Illyrian tribes (Ardiaei, Labeatae, Docleatae, Pirusti) on the land of today's Montenegro before and under Rome's conquest** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.V §7: The territory of Montenegro in the Roman state"?
a) The Illyrian tribes (Ardiaei, Labeatae, Docleatae, Pirusti) on the land of today's Montenegro before and under Rome's conquest  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) The Illyrian tribes (Ardiaei, Labeatae, Docleatae, Pirusti) on the land of today's Montenegro before and under Rome's conquest** — That is what this lesson teaches.
