# Duklja: The First Montenegrin State — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** The first ____ state, the state of the Dukljan Slavs, arises on land once held by Illyrian tribes and then by Roman provinces. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Montenegrin

**2.** Stefan Vojislav's line holds Zeta; Prince Mihailo (____–1081) inherits, and the 1054 division of the Christian Church into Orthodox and Catholic shapes his politics between Byzantium and Rome. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** 1046
