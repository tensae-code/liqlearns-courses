# Duklja: The First Montenegrin State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.2.1 Duklja (Zeta)" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The first Montenegrin state, the state of the Dukljan Slavs, arises on land once held by Illyrian tribes and then by Roman provinces
