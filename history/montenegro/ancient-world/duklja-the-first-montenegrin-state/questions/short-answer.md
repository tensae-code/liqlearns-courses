# Duklja: The First Montenegrin State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.2.1 Duklja (Zeta)" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The first Montenegrin state, the state of the Dukljan Slavs, arises on land once held by Illyrian tribes and then by Roman provinces. In the story's own turn: on Rome's old frontier the Slavs of Duklja build the first state Montenegrins claim as their own.

**2.** What came of "Ch.2.2 Zeta in the age of the Vojislavljevići" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Stefan Vojislav's line holds Zeta; Prince Mihailo (1046–1081) inherits, and the 1054 division of the Christian Church into Orthodox and Catholic shapes his politics between Byzantium and Rome. In the story's own turn: a small coastal kingdom plays two empires and two churches against each other to stay a kingdom.
