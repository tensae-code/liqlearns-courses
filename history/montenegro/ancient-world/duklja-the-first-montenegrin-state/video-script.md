# Video script — Duklja: The First Montenegrin State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The first Montenegrin state, the state of the Dukljan Slavs, arises on land once held by Illyrian tribes and then by Roman provinces; Stefan Vojislav's line holds Zeta; Prince Mihailo (1046–1081) inherits, and the 1054 division of the Christian Church into Orthodox and Catholic shapes his politics between Byzantium and Rome.

Now the part called "Ch.2.1 Duklja (Zeta)". The first Montenegrin state, the state of the Dukljan Slavs, arises on land once held by Illyrian tribes and then by Roman provinces. In the story's own turn: on Rome's old frontier the Slavs of Duklja build the first state Montenegrins claim as their own.

Now the part called "Ch.2.2 Zeta in the age of the Vojislavljevići". Stefan Vojislav's line holds Zeta; Prince Mihailo (1046–1081) inherits, and the 1054 division of the Christian Church into Orthodox and Catholic shapes his politics between Byzantium and Rome. In the story's own turn: a small coastal kingdom plays two empires and two churches against each other to stay a kingdom.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
