# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Greece — Crete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Aegean culture created on Crete and at Mycenae in the 3rd millennium BCE spreads to neighbouring islands and coasts.

Now the part called "Ch.IV Ancient Greece §1: Cretan-Mycenaean culture". The Aegean culture created on Crete and at Mycenae in the 3rd millennium BCE spreads to neighbouring islands and coasts. In the story's own turn: a sea-going palace culture flowers on Crete and is carried to mainland Mycenae.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
