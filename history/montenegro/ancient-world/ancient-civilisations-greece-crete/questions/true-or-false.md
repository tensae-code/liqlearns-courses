# Ancient Civilisations: Greece — Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Aegean culture created on Crete and at Mycenae in the 3rd millennium BCE spreads to neighbouring islands and coasts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.IV Ancient Greece §1: Cretan-Mycenaean culture" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
