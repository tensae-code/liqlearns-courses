# Ancient Civilisations: Greece — Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.IV Ancient Greece §1: Cretan-Mycenaean culture" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The Aegean culture created on Crete and at Mycenae in the 3rd millennium BCE spreads to neighbouring islands and coasts
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The Aegean culture created on Crete and at Mycenae in the 3rd millennium BCE spreads to neighbouring islands and coasts** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.IV Ancient Greece §1: Cretan-Mycenaean culture"?
a) The Aegean culture created on Crete and at Mycenae in the 3rd millennium BCE spreads to neighbouring islands and coasts  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) The Aegean culture created on Crete and at Mycenae in the 3rd millennium BCE spreads to neighbouring islands and coasts** — That is what this lesson teaches.
