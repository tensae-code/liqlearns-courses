# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.V §3: The age of the Empire, and the fall of the Roman Empire" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Slave and provincial revolts force a professional army; emperors follow, and the empire eventually collapses
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Slave and provincial revolts force a professional army; emperors follow, and the empire eventually collapses** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.V §3: The age of the Empire, and the fall of the Roman Empire"?
a) Slave and provincial revolts force a professional army; emperors follow, and the empire eventually collapses  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Slave and provincial revolts force a professional army; emperors follow, and the empire eventually collapses** — That is what this lesson teaches.
