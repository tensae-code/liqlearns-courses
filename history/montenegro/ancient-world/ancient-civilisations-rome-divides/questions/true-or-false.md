# Ancient Civilisations: Rome Divides — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Slave and provincial revolts force a professional army; emperors follow, and the empire eventually collapses.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.V §3: The age of the Empire, and the fall of the Roman Empire" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
