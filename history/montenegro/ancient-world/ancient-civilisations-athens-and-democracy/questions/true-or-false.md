# Athens and Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The golden age of Athens: after victory Athens leads a naval league, taxes its allies and governs itself by its citizens' assembly under Pericles.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.IV §4: Athenian democracy" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
