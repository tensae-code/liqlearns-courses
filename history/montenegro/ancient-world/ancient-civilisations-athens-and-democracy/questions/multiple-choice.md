# Athens and Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.IV §4: Athenian democracy" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The golden age of Athens: after victory Athens leads a naval league, taxes its allies and governs itself by its citizens' assembly under Pericles

**Answer: b) The golden age of Athens: after victory Athens leads a naval league, taxes its allies and governs itself by its citizens' assembly under Pericles** — That is what this lesson teaches.
