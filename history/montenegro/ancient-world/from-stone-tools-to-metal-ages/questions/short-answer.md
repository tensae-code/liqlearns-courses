# From Stone Tools to Metal Ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.II Prehistory §1: How people lived in the Stone Age" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Palaeolithic gatherers and hunters in hordes with chipped stone, fire and speech; Neolithic polished stone, farming, stock-breeding, pile-dwellings, pottery, clan, matriarchy and patriarchy. In the story's own turn: for the longest stretch of the human story people live by gathering and hunting, then in a blink they farm, build and settle.

**2.** What came of "Ch.II §2: How people lived in the Metal Age" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Copper found at the end of the Neolithic, then bronze and iron tools and weapons, and the crafts. In the story's own turn: a softer, shinier stone that melts changes what hands can make and what armies can take.
