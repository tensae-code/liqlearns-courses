# From Stone Tools to Metal Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.II Prehistory §1: How people lived in the Stone Age" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Palaeolithic gatherers and hunters in hordes with chipped stone, fire and speech; Neolithic polished stone, farming, stock-breeding, pile-dwellings, pottery, clan, matriarchy and patriarchy
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Palaeolithic gatherers and hunters in hordes with chipped stone, fire and speech; Neolithic polished stone, farming, stock-breeding, pile-dwellings, pottery, clan, matriarchy and patriarchy** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.II §2: How people lived in the Metal Age"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Copper found at the end of the Neolithic, then bronze and iron tools and weapons, and the crafts  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Copper found at the end of the Neolithic, then bronze and iron tools and weapons, and the crafts** — That is what this lesson teaches.
