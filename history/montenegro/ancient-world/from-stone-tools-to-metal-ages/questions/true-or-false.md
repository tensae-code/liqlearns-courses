# From Stone Tools to Metal Ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Palaeolithic gatherers and hunters in hordes with chipped stone, fire and speech; Neolithic polished stone, farming, stock-breeding, pile-dwellings, pottery, clan, matriarchy and patriarchy. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.II §2: How people lived in the Metal Age" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
