# From Stone Tools to Metal Ages — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** ____ gatherers and hunters in hordes with chipped stone, fire and speech; Neolithic polished stone, farming, stock-breeding, pile-dwellings, pottery, clan, matriarchy and patriarchy. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Palaeolithic

**2.** Copper found at the end of the ____, then bronze and iron tools and weapons, and the crafts. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Neolithic
