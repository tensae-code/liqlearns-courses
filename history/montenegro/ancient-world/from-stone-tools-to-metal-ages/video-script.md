# Video script — From Stone Tools to Metal Ages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Palaeolithic gatherers and hunters in hordes with chipped stone, fire and speech; Neolithic polished stone, farming, stock-breeding, pile-dwellings, pottery, clan, matriarchy and patriarchy; Copper found at the end of the Neolithic, then bronze and iron tools and weapons, and the crafts.

Now the part called "Ch.II Prehistory §1: How people lived in the Stone Age". Palaeolithic gatherers and hunters in hordes with chipped stone, fire and speech; Neolithic polished stone, farming, stock-breeding, pile-dwellings, pottery, clan, matriarchy and patriarchy. In the story's own turn: for the longest stretch of the human story people live by gathering and hunting, then in a blink they farm, build and settle.

Now the part called "Ch.II §2: How people lived in the Metal Age". Copper found at the end of the Neolithic, then bronze and iron tools and weapons, and the crafts. In the story's own turn: a softer, shinier stone that melts changes what hands can make and what armies can take.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
