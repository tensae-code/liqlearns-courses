# The Balkan States Before The Ottoman Conquest — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Mrnjavčević brothers hold much of Macedonia — and their resistance breaks on the river Maritsa (1371).
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.4.3 Serbia, Bosnia and Croatia on the eve of the Ottoman conquests: Serbia fragments among feudal lords" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
