# The Great Migration And The Fall Of The West — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.1 The Great Migration of Peoples" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Germanic tribes in contact with Rome develop farming, crafts, private property and inequality, and pour across the empire's borders
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Germanic tribes in contact with Rome develop farming, crafts, private property and inequality, and pour across the empire's borders** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.1 The Great Migration of Peoples"?
a) Germanic tribes in contact with Rome develop farming, crafts, private property and inequality, and pour across the empire's borders  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Germanic tribes in contact with Rome develop farming, crafts, private property and inequality, and pour across the empire's borders** — That is what this lesson teaches.
