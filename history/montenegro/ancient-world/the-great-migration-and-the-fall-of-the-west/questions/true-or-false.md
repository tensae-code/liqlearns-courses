# The Great Migration And The Fall Of The West — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Germanic tribes in contact with Rome develop farming, crafts, private property and inequality, and pour across the empire's borders.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.1 The Great Migration of Peoples" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
