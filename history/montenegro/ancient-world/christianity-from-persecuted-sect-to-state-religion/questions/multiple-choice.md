# Christianity From Persecuted Sect To State Religion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.4 The Christian Church and its organisation" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) By the end of the 4th century Christianity passes from persecuted religion to the empire's only valid faith, and builds its church organisation
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) By the end of the 4th century Christianity passes from persecuted religion to the empire's only valid faith, and builds its church organisation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.4 The Christian Church and its organisation"?
a) By the end of the 4th century Christianity passes from persecuted religion to the empire's only valid faith, and builds its church organisation  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) By the end of the 4th century Christianity passes from persecuted religion to the empire's only valid faith, and builds its church organisation** — That is what this lesson teaches.
