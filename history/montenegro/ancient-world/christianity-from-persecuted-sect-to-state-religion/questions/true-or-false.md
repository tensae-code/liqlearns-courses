# Christianity From Persecuted Sect To State Religion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** By the end of the 4th century Christianity passes from persecuted religion to the empire's only valid faith, and builds its church organisation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.4 The Christian Church and its organisation" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
