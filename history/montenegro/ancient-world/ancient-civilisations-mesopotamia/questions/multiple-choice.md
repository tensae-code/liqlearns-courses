# Ancient Civilisations: Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.III The Ancient East §1: Where and how the states of the Ancient East arose" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) On the great rivers; Egypt, the Mesopotamian states (Sumerian, Akkadian, Assyrian, Babylonian), Phoenicia, Israel, Persia, China, India
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) On the great rivers; Egypt, the Mesopotamian states (Sumerian, Akkadian, Assyrian, Babylonian), Phoenicia, Israel, Persia, China, India** — That is what this lesson teaches.
