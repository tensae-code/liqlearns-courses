# Zeta under the Crnojevići and the Ottoman Advance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.4.2 Zeta (Montenegro) in the age of the Crnojevići" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast. In the story's own turn: the Crnojevići rule a shrinking mountain state while Venice eats its coast and the Ottomans close in.
