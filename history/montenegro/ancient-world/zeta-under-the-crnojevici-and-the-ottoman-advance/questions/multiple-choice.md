# Zeta under the Crnojevići and the Ottoman Advance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.4.2 Zeta (Montenegro) in the age of the Crnojevići" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast

d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast** — That is what this lesson teaches.
