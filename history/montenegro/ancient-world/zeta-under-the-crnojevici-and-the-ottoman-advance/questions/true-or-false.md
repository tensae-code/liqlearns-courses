# Zeta under the Crnojevići and the Ottoman Advance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.4.2 Zeta (Montenegro) in the age of the Crnojevići" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
