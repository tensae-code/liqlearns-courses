# Video script — Zeta under the Crnojevići and the Ottoman Advance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast.

Now the part called "Ch.4.2 Zeta (Montenegro) in the age of the Crnojevići". Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast. In the story's own turn: the Crnojevići rule a shrinking mountain state while Venice eats its coast and the Ottomans close in.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
