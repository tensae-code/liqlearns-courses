# Video script — Christianisation of the South Slavs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language.

Now the part called "Ch.2.3 The states of the South Slavs". Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language. In the story's own turn: the Slavs get the gospel in words they understand, and letters of their own to write it in.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
