# Christianisation of the South Slavs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.2.3 The states of the South Slavs" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.2.3 The states of the South Slavs"?
a) Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language** — That is what this lesson teaches.
