# Christianisation of the South Slavs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.2.3 The states of the South Slavs" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language. In the story's own turn: the Slavs get the gospel in words they understand, and letters of their own to write it in.
