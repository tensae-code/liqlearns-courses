# Christianisation of the South Slavs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.3 The states of the South Slavs" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
