# Video script — Medieval World The Dark Age And Feudalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. After the fall of the Western Roman Empire, constant wars destroy cities and the economy; the feudal order and the Frankish state arise; Population grows, forests are cleared and marshes drained, the heavy plough spreads, and medieval towns of a few hundred or thousand people rise under kings, bishops and great lords.

Now the part called "Ch.1.2 Feudal society". After the fall of the Western Roman Empire, constant wars destroy cities and the economy; the feudal order and the Frankish state arise. In the story's own turn: in a world without safety, land is traded for protection and Europe becomes a pyramid of lords and peasants.

Now the part called "Ch.3.1 The development of feudalism in Western Europe from the 12th century". In the story's own turn: Europe recovers.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.
