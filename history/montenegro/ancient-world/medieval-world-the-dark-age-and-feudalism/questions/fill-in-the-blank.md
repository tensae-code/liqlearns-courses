# Medieval World The Dark Age And Feudalism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word.

**1.** After the fall of the ____ Empire, constant wars destroy cities and the economy; the feudal order and the Frankish state arise. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Western Roman

**2.** Population grows, forests are cleared and marshes drained, the heavy plough spreads, and medieval towns of a few hundred or thousand people rise under kings, bishops and great lords — the land at the heart of this lesson is ____. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Montenegro
