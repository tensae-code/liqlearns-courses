# Medieval World The Dark Age And Feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** After the fall of the Western Roman Empire, constant wars destroy cities and the economy; the feudal order and the Frankish state arise. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.3.1 The development of feudalism in Western Europe from the 12th century" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
