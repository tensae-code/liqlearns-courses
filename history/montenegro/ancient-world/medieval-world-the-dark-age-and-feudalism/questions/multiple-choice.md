# Medieval World The Dark Age And Feudalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.2 Feudal society" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) After the fall of the Western Roman Empire, constant wars destroy cities and the economy; the feudal order and the Frankish state arise
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
