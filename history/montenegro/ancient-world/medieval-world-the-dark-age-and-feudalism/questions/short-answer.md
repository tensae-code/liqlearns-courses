# Medieval World The Dark Age And Feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.1.2 Feudal society" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** After the fall of the Western Roman Empire, constant wars destroy cities and the economy; the feudal order and the Frankish state arise. In the story's own turn: in a world without safety, land is traded for protection and Europe becomes a pyramid of lords and peasants.

**2.** What came of "Ch.3.1 The development of feudalism in Western Europe from the 12th century" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Population grows, forests are cleared and marshes drained, the heavy plough spreads, and medieval towns of a few hundred or thousand people rise under kings, bishops and great lords. In the story's own turn: Europe recovers.
