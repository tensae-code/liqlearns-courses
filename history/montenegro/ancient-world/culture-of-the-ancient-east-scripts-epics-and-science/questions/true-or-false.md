# Culture of the Ancient East: Scripts, Epics and Science — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first picture scripts simplified over time, Sumerian cuneiform, hieroglyphs, the Phoenician alphabet, the Epic of Gilgamesh, the Book of the Dead, the Bible, the Vedas, mathematics, medicine, astronomy.
