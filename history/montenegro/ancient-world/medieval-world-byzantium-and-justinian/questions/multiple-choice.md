# Medieval World Byzantium And Justinian — multiple choice

**1.** What does the part "Ch.1.3 Byzantium to the 13th century" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The Eastern Roman Empire lives on after the West falls, attempts to restore the Roman Empire, and spreads its economic, religious and cultural influence

d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The Eastern Roman Empire lives on after the West falls, attempts to restore the Roman Empire, and spreads its economic, religious and cultural influence** — That is what this lesson teaches.
