# Medieval World Byzantium And Justinian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.1.3 Byzantium to the 13th century" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Eastern Roman Empire lives on after the West falls, attempts to restore the Roman Empire, and spreads its economic, religious and cultural influence. In the story's own turn: Rome dies in the west and lives a thousand more years in Greek Constantinople.

**2.** What came of "Ch.3.4 Byzantium from the 13th century" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The Crusaders come, Venice turns trade rivalry into conquest, and a Latin Empire is set up on Byzantine land. In the story's own turn: the empire that survived a thousand years is carved up by its fellow Christians.
