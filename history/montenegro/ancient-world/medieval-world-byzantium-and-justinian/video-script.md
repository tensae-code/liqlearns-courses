# Video script — Medieval World Byzantium And Justinian

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Eastern Roman Empire lives on after the West falls, attempts to restore the Roman Empire, and spreads its economic, religious and cultural influence; The Crusaders come, Venice turns trade rivalry into conquest, and a Latin Empire is set up on Byzantine land.

Now the part called "Ch.1.3 Byzantium to the 13th century". The Eastern Roman Empire lives on after the West falls, attempts to restore the Roman Empire, and spreads its economic, religious and cultural influence. In the story's own turn: Rome dies in the west and lives a thousand more years in Greek Constantinople.

Now the part called "Ch.3.4 Byzantium from the 13th century". The Crusaders come, Venice turns trade rivalry into conquest, and a Latin Empire is set up on Byzantine land. In the story's own turn: the empire that survived a thousand years is carved up by its fellow Christians.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
