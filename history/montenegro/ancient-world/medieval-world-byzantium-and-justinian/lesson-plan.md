# Lesson plan — Medieval World Byzantium And Justinian

**Course:** History — Montenegro · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Medieval World Byzantium And Justinian
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Medieval World and Medieval Montenegro (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Eastern Roman Empire lives on after the West falls, attempts to restore the Roman Empire, and spreads its economic, religious and cultural influence; The Crusaders come, Venice turns trade rivalry into conquest, and a Latin Empire is set up on Byzantine land.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — The Medieval World and Medieval Montenegro — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. ## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: The Eastern Roman Empire lives on after the West falls, attempts to restore the Roman Empire, and spreads its economic, religious and cultural influence; The Crusaders come, Venice turns trade rivalry into conquest, and a Latin Empire is set up on Byzantine land. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
