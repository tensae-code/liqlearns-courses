# Medieval World The Ottomans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Ottoman (Turkish) Empire: under Sultan Murad I (1359–1389) the Ottomans take Thrace, move their capital to Edirne, and press unceasingly on Byzantium, Bulgaria and Serbia. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.1.3 The Ottoman Empire as a military and political power in south-eastern Europe in the 16th and 17th centuries" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
