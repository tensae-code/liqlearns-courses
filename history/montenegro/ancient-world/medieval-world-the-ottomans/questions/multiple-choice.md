Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.4 (introduction)" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The Ottoman (Turkish) Empire: under Sultan Murad I (1359–1389) the Ottomans take Thrace, move their capital to Edirne, and press unceasingly on Byzantium, Bulgaria and Serbia
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The Ottoman (Turkish) Empire: under Sultan Murad I (1359–1389) the Ottomans take Thrace, move their capital to Edirne, and press unceasingly on Byzantium, Bulgaria and Serbia** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.1.3 The Ottoman Empire as a military and political power in south-eastern Europe in the 16th and 17th centuries"? (Sub lesson 2)
