# Medieval World The Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.4 (introduction)" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The Ottoman (Turkish) Empire: under Sultan Murad I (1359–1389) the Ottomans take Thrace, move their capital to Edirne, and press unceasingly on Byzantium, Bulgaria and Serbia. In the story's own turn: a frontier beylik becomes the power at Europe's gate, and the Balkan states meet it unprepared.

**2.** What came of "Ch.1.3 The Ottoman Empire as a military and political power in south-eastern Europe in the 16th and 17th centuries" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Under Suleiman I the empire reaches its greatest territorial, military and political height. In the story's own turn: at its peak the sultan's empire is one of the two superpowers of the European world.
