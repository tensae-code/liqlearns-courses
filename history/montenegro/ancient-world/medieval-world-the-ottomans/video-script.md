# Video script — Medieval World The Ottomans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. The Ottoman (Turkish) Empire: under Sultan Murad I (1359–1389) the Ottomans take Thrace, move their capital to Edirne, and press unceasingly on Byzantium, Bulgaria and Serbia; Under Suleiman I the empire reaches its greatest territorial, military and political height.

Now the part called "Ch.4 (introduction)". The Ottoman (Turkish) Empire: under Sultan Murad I (1359–1389) the Ottomans take Thrace, move their capital to Edirne, and press unceasingly on Byzantium, Bulgaria and Serbia. In the story's own turn: a frontier beylik becomes the power at Europe's gate, and the Balkan states meet it unprepared.

Now the part called "Ch.1.3 The Ottoman Empire as a military and political power in south-eastern Europe in the 16th and 17th centuries". Under Suleiman I the empire reaches its greatest territorial, military and political height. In the story's own turn: at its peak the sultan's empire is one of the two superpowers of the European world.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
