# Macedonia And Alexander The Great — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.IV §6: The Macedonian state" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) In Greece's general weakness after the Peloponnesian War, Macedonia strengthens in the north under Philip II, with Alexander's conquests and their consequences

**Answer: b) In Greece's general weakness after the Peloponnesian War, Macedonia strengthens in the north under Philip II, with Alexander's conquests and their consequences** — That is what this lesson teaches.
