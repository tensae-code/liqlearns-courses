# Macedonia And Alexander The Great — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Greece's general weakness after the Peloponnesian War, Macedonia strengthens in the north under Philip II, with Alexander's conquests and their consequences.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.IV §6: The Macedonian state" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
