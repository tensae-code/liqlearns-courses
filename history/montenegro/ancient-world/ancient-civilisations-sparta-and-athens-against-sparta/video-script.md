# Video script — Sparta and Athens against Sparta

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Sparta, Athens, and Greek colonisation around the Mediterranean; Sparta exploits the discontent of Athens' allies and the two leading poleis ruin Greece between them.

Now the part called "Ch.IV §2: The Greek poleis". Sparta, Athens, and Greek colonisation around the Mediterranean. In the story's own turn: one people splits into hundreds of rival city-states and plants them on every shore.

Now the part called "Ch.IV §5: The Peloponnesian War". Sparta exploits the discontent of Athens' allies and the two leading poleis ruin Greece between them. In the story's own turn: the Greeks who beat Persia together spend a generation beating each other.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
