# Sparta and Athens against Sparta — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.IV §2: The Greek poleis" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Sparta, Athens, and Greek colonisation around the Mediterranean
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Sparta, Athens, and Greek colonisation around the Mediterranean** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.IV §5: The Peloponnesian War"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Sparta exploits the discontent of Athens' allies and the two leading poleis ruin Greece between them  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Sparta exploits the discontent of Athens' allies and the two leading poleis ruin Greece between them** — That is what this lesson teaches.
