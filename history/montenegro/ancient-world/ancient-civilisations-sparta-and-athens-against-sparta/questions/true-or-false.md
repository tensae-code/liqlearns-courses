# Sparta and Athens against Sparta — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sparta, Athens, and Greek colonisation around the Mediterranean. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.IV §5: The Peloponnesian War" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
