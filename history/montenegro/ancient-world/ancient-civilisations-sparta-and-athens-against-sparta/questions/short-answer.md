# Sparta and Athens against Sparta — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.IV §2: The Greek poleis" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sparta, Athens, and Greek colonisation around the Mediterranean. In the story's own turn: one people splits into hundreds of rival city-states and plants them on every shore.

**2.** What came of "Ch.IV §5: The Peloponnesian War" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Sparta exploits the discontent of Athens' allies and the two leading poleis ruin Greece between them. In the story's own turn: the Greeks who beat Persia together spend a generation beating each other.
