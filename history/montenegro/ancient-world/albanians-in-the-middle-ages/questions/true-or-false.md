# Albanians In The Middle Ages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Zenebishi, Thopia, Muzaka and Arianiti families.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.4.4 The Albanians in the Middle Ages: after Dušan's death the old governors' territories separate, and new magnates rise from tribal elders" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
