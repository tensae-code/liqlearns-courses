# Albanians In The Middle Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.4.4 The Albanians in the Middle Ages: after Dušan's death the old governors' territories separate, and new magnates rise from tribal elders" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) The Zenebishi, Thopia, Muzaka and Arianiti families
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) The Zenebishi, Thopia, Muzaka and Arianiti families** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.4.4 The Albanians in the Middle Ages: after Dušan's death the old governors' territories separate, and new magnates rise from tribal elders"?
a) The Zenebishi, Thopia, Muzaka and Arianiti families  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) The Zenebishi, Thopia, Muzaka and Arianiti families** — That is what this lesson teaches.
