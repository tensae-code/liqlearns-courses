Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.IV §8: Greek and Hellenistic culture" — who, where, and what turned.
**Answer:** Socrates, Plato and Aristotle turn philosophy to man himself; then Greek culture fuses with Eastern cultures into planned Hellenistic cities with libraries and water systems. In the story's own turn: Athens asks what a human is, and Alexander's heirs build cities to spread the answer.
