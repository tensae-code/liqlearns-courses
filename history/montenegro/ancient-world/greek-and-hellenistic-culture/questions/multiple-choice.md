# Greek And Hellenistic Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.IV §8: Greek and Hellenistic culture" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Socrates, Plato and Aristotle turn philosophy to man himself; then Greek culture fuses with Eastern cultures into planned Hellenistic cities with libraries and water systems
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Socrates, Plato and Aristotle turn philosophy to man himself; then Greek culture fuses with Eastern cultures into planned Hellenistic cities with libraries and water systems** — That is what this lesson teaches.
