# Greek And Hellenistic Culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Socrates, Plato and Aristotle turn philosophy to man himself; then Greek culture fuses with Eastern cultures into planned Hellenistic cities with libraries and water systems.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.IV §8: Greek and Hellenistic culture" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
