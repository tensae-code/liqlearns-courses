**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Socrates, Plato and Aristotle turn philosophy to man himself; then Greek culture fuses with Eastern cultures into planned Hellenistic cities with libraries and water systems.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and the Ancient World — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the thread of the story → go back to the part names in order and say each part's turn in one breath: who, where, what turned.
- Mixes up the people or the places → draw the story as a line of pictures, one per part, and label each with its place and its people.
- Go back to the one thing: Socrates, Plato and Aristotle turn philosophy to man himself; then Greek culture fuses with Eastern cultures into planned Hellenistic cities with libraries and water systems. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
