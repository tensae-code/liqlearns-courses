# Greek Religion Myths And The Olympic Games — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.IV §7: The beliefs of the Greeks, myths and games" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Politically divided Greeks are one people through language, gods, customs, sanctuaries, festivals and the Olympic games
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Politically divided Greeks are one people through language, gods, customs, sanctuaries, festivals and the Olympic games** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.IV §7: The beliefs of the Greeks, myths and games"?
a) Politically divided Greeks are one people through language, gods, customs, sanctuaries, festivals and the Olympic games  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Politically divided Greeks are one people through language, gods, customs, sanctuaries, festivals and the Olympic games** — That is what this lesson teaches.
