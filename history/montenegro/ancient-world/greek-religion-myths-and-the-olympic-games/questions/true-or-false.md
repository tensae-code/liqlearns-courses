# Greek Religion Myths And The Olympic Games — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Politically divided Greeks are one people through language, gods, customs, sanctuaries, festivals and the Olympic games.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.IV §7: The beliefs of the Greeks, myths and games" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
