# Zeta in the Nemanjić State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Stefan Nemanja gives Zeta with Trebinje to his eldest son Vukan, who bears a king's title; Zeta keeps a special position inside the Serbian state.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2.4 Zeta in the Nemanjić state" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
