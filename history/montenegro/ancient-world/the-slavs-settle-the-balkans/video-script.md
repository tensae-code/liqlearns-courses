# Video script — The Slavs Settle the Balkans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. South Slavs settle around the middle and lower Danube; Avars arrive in Pannonia in the later 6th century and Slavs and Avars together raid Byzantine provinces to Thessalonica.

Now the part called "Ch.2 The arrival of the ancestors of the Montenegrins and the South Slavs in the Balkans". South Slavs settle around the middle and lower Danube; Avars arrive in Pannonia in the later 6th century and Slavs and Avars together raid Byzantine provinces to Thessalonica. In the story's own turn: the child's ancestors walk into the Balkans behind the Avar wars and stay.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
