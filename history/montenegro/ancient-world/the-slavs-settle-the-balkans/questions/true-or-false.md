# The Slavs Settle the Balkans — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** South Slavs settle around the middle and lower Danube; Avars arrive in Pannonia in the later 6th century and Slavs and Avars together raid Byzantine provinces to Thessalonica.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.2 The arrival of the ancestors of the Montenegrins and the South Slavs in the Balkans" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
