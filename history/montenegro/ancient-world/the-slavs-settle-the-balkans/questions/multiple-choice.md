# The Slavs Settle the Balkans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.2 The arrival of the ancestors of the Montenegrins and the South Slavs in the Balkans" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) South Slavs settle around the middle and lower Danube; Avars arrive in Pannonia in the later 6th century and Slavs and Avars together raid Byzantine provinces to Thessalonica
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) South Slavs settle around the middle and lower Danube; Avars arrive in Pannonia in the later 6th century and Slavs and Avars together raid Byzantine provinces to Thessalonica** — That is what this lesson teaches.
