# Zeta under the Balšići — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Lords of Bar and Budva — make themselves independent around 1360 and fight both the Ottoman Empire and Venice.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.4.1 Zeta in the age of the Balšići: after Tsar Stefan Dušan's death weakens Serbia's centre, the Balšići" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned.
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
