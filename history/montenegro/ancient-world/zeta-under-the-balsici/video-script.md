# Video script — Zeta under the Balšići

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Lords of Bar and Budva — make themselves independent around 1360 and fight both the Ottoman Empire and Venice.

Now the part called "Ch.4.1 Zeta in the age of the Balšići: after Tsar Stefan Dušan's death weakens Serbia's centre, the Balšići". Lords of Bar and Budva — make themselves independent around 1360 and fight both the Ottoman Empire and Venice. In the story's own turn: as an empire cracks, a coastal family carves out a lordship between sultan and republic.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
