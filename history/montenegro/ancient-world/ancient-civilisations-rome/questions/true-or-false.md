# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Italic peoples, Etruscans and Greek colonies on the Apennine peninsula; a Latin city grows into a state. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this lesson, "Ch.V §2: State and social order in ancient Rome" is presented as a made-up tale with no real people, no real places and nothing that turned. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False — the lesson tells it as Montenegro's real story, with its people, its places and its turn, as the books teach it.**
