# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.V Ancient Rome §1: The origin and development of the state" tell? (Sub lesson 1)
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Italic peoples, Etruscans and Greek colonies on the Apennine peninsula; a Latin city grows into a state
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Italic peoples, Etruscans and Greek colonies on the Apennine peninsula; a Latin city grows into a state** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.V §2: State and social order in ancient Rome"? (Sub lesson 2)
a) First a kingdom (king as commander, judge and priest, advised by the Senate), then the republic of patricians and plebeians  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) First a kingdom (king as commander, judge and priest, advised by the Senate), then the republic of patricians and plebeians** — That is what this lesson teaches.
