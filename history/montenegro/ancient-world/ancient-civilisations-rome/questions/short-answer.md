# Ancient Civilisations: Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the story of "Ch.V Ancient Rome §1: The origin and development of the state" — who, where, and what turned. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Italic peoples, Etruscans and Greek colonies on the Apennine peninsula; a Latin city grows into a state. In the story's own turn: one Latin town among many peoples of Italy begins the climb to an empire.

**2.** What came of "Ch.V §2: State and social order in ancient Rome" — what does the lesson say followed from it? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** First a kingdom (king as commander, judge and priest, advised by the Senate), then the republic of patricians and plebeians. In the story's own turn: Rome throws out its kings and invents a republic run by an aristocracy it slowly has to share.
