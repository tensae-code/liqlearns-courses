# Video script — Ancient Civilisations: Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

---

This lesson is about one thing. Italic peoples, Etruscans and Greek colonies on the Apennine peninsula; a Latin city grows into a state; First a kingdom (king as commander, judge and priest, advised by the Senate), then the republic of patricians and plebeians.

Now the part called "Ch.V Ancient Rome §1: The origin and development of the state". Italic peoples, Etruscans and Greek colonies on the Apennine peninsula; a Latin city grows into a state. In the story's own turn: one Latin town among many peoples of Italy begins the climb to an empire.

Now the part called "Ch.V §2: State and social order in ancient Rome". First a kingdom (king as commander, judge and priest, advised by the Senate), then the republic of patricians and plebeians. In the story's own turn: Rome throws out its kings and invents a republic run by an aristocracy it slowly has to share.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
