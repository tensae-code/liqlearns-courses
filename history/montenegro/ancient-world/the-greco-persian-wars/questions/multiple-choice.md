# The Greco Persian Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the part "Ch.IV §3: The Greco-Persian Wars" tell?
a) it is a story with no people and no places in it
b) Persia at its peak, from the Indus to Asia Minor, steps through conquered Thrace into Europe against the tiny poleis
c) nothing changed, and nobody moved or decided anything
d) the past cannot be known, so the book leaves it blank
**Answer: b) Persia at its peak, from the Indus to Asia Minor, steps through conquered Thrace into Europe against the tiny poleis** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which sentence matches this lesson's story of "Ch.IV §3: The Greco-Persian Wars"?
a) Persia at its peak, from the Indus to Asia Minor, steps through conquered Thrace into Europe against the tiny poleis  b) the opposite happened, and the lesson says so plainly  c) this part is not in the lesson at all  d) nobody in this part did anything that mattered
**Answer: a) Persia at its peak, from the Indus to Asia Minor, steps through conquered Thrace into Europe against the tiny poleis** — That is what this lesson teaches.
