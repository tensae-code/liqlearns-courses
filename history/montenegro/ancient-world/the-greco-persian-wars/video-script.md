# Video script — The Greco Persian Wars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. The story is told the animated-map way: who, where, what turned.

This lesson is about one thing. Persia at its peak, from the Indus to Asia Minor, steps through conquered Thrace into Europe against the tiny poleis.

Now the part called "Ch.IV §3: The Greco-Persian Wars". Persia at its peak, from the Indus to Asia Minor, steps through conquered Thrace into Europe against the tiny poleis. In the story's own turn: the largest empire on earth meets the smallest states in Greece, and the small states win.

Remember it this way: follow the parts in order, and at each one ask — who, where, what turned, and what came of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
