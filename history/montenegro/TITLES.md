# History — Montenegro course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Montenegro's own —
built from Montenegro's Istorija (History) student books for
Grades 6–9 of primary school (student books, Montenegrin).
Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**:
the titles below sit inside the levels, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grade each book comes from
lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': a world-history year that
lands on home ground (antiquity ending in Doclea), a medieval
year alternating Europe and Duklja/Zeta, a year of discoveries
and revolutions alternating with Montenegro building its state,
and a twentieth century told as a double story — world chapter,
then Montenegrin chapter — to independence regained. That
ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner
can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder).
