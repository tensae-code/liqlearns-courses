# From Stone Tools to Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the lesson teach tools and way of life together?
**Answer:** Because the tools shaped the life: what the hands could make decided how people got food, sheltered and worked together.

**2.** Give one job a stone tool made possible.
**Answer:** Cutting, scraping or digging — any job the lesson names, done better with a shaped edge than with bare hands.
