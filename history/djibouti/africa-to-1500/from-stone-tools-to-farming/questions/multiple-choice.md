# From Stone Tools to Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the first African societies shape from stone?
a) coins  b) tools for cutting, scraping and digging  c) glass windows  d) books
**Answer: b) tools for cutting, scraping and digging** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What was built around the tools?
a) nothing  b) a way of life — how people ate, sheltered and worked together  c) a single king's palace only  d) walls against all strangers
**Answer: b) a way of life — how people ate, sheltered and worked together** — That is what this lesson teaches.
