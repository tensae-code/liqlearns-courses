# Daily Life in Ancient Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What changes when homes gather into a town? (Sub lesson 1)
a) nothing at all  b) how people live together — neighbours, shared streets and markets, rules for a crowd  c) people stop needing families  d) food is no longer cooked
**Answer: b) how people live together — neighbours, shared streets and markets, rules for a crowd** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What decided how ancient societies thrived, in the food part? (Sub lesson 3)
a) the colour of their clothes  b) what they grew, hunted and cooked  c) the names of their kings  d) how far away the sea was
**Answer: b) what they grew, hunted and cooked** — That is what this lesson teaches.
