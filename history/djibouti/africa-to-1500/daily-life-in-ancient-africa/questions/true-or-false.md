# Daily Life in Ancient Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The family is the first community every child belongs to and learns from. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Dress and ornaments told nothing about who a person was. (Sub lesson 4)
**Answer: False** — Dress, ornaments and art carried each civilisation's identity on the body — readable at a glance.
