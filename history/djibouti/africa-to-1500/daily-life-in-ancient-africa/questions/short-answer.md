# Daily Life in Ancient Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is the family called the first community? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Because a child belongs to it first and learns from it first — language, work, manners and the people's stories — long before any school.

**2.** How does food shape a whole society? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The crops, animals and cooking a land supports set the size of towns, the health of children and the reach of trade — how the society thrives.
