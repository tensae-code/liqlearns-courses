# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What evidence shows where humankind began?
**Answer:** The first fossils — bones and traces preserved in the earth — found in Africa.

**2.** Why does the book put this lesson near the front of the course?
**Answer:** Because it is the beginning of the whole story: civilisations and everything after them grow from humankind's African start.
