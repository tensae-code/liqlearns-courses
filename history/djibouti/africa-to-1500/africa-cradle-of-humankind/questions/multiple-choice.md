# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where do the first fossils show humankind beginning?
a) in Europe  b) in Africa  c) on an island that sank  d) in America
**Answer: b) in Africa** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did people do from that African beginning?
a) stayed in one valley forever  b) spread out across the continent and in time beyond it  c) vanished and began again elsewhere  d) waited for ships to carry them
**Answer: b) spread out across the continent and in time beyond it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
