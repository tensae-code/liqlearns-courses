# Africa, Cradle of Humankind — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The human story begins in Africa, according to the first fossils.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Fossils are useless traces that tell us nothing about early humans.
**Answer: False** — The first fossils are the evidence that shows humankind beginning and evolving in Africa.
