# Lesson plan — Africa, Cradle of Humankind

**Course:** History — Djibouti · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Africa, Cradle of Humankind
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — First Steps and Africa's Beginnings (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The first fossils show humankind beginning in Africa: the human story starts on this continent and spreads out from it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — First Steps and Africa's Beginnings — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The first fossils show humankind beginning in Africa: the human story starts on this continent and spreads out from it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a family that can name the village where its oldest known ancestor lived — the place every later branch of the family spread out from.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
