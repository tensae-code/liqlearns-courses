# How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How can a desert be a road? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Caravans learned its wells and seasons and crossed it regularly, carrying goods, news and ideas — so the crossing tied the lands on either side together.

**2.** Why is a circuit stronger than a single trading journey? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Because it repeats and is expected: towns grow where it passes, and far-apart people live inside the same web of exchange.
