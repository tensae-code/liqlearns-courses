# How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What double name does the book give the Sahara? (Sub lesson 1)
a) wall and prison  b) barrier and home of exchanges  c) sea and forest  d) beginning and end
**Answer: b) barrier and home of exchanges** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did trade circuits do for the great empires? (Sub lesson 2)
a) divided them into isolated villages  b) stitched them together into one working world  c) ended all farming  d) made towns unnecessary
**Answer: b) stitched them together into one working world** — That is what this lesson teaches.
