# How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The great rivers grew civilisations on their banks. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** A trade circuit is a single journey that never repeats. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — A circuit repeats — routes looping town to town — which is what makes it stronger than a single journey.
