# Kingdoms, Empires and Other African States — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four kinds of life a state had to organise. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Political, administrative, social and economic life.

**2.** Why does the lesson say the states 'built twice'? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Once in power — the organised state — and once in culture: the learning and art that survived after the rulers and empires fell.
