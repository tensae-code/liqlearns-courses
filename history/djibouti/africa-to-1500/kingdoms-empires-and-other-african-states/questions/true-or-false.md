# Kingdoms, Empires and Other African States — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A state is only its ruler; nothing keeps working when the ruler sleeps. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — A state is its organisation — laws, officials, taxes, judged disputes — which keeps working beyond the ruler.

**2.** Courts and cities produced learning and art. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True**
