# Kingdoms, Empires and Other African States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did African peoples build, in this lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
a) only villages with no organisation  b) kingdoms, empires and other states, created and organised  c) ships for other continents only  d) nothing before Europeans came
**Answer: b) kingdoms, empires and other states, created and organised** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What outlives the rulers of a kingdom or empire? (Sub lesson 2)
a) nothing at all  b) the learning and art its courts and cities produced  c) only the ruler's name  d) the taxes
**Answer: b) the learning and art its courts and cities produced** — That is what this lesson teaches.
