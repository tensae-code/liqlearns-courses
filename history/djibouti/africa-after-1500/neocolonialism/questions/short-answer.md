# Neocolonialism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two practices of neocolonialism.
**Answer:** Any two the lesson names: control through money lent and owed, trade terms set elsewhere, foreign companies and influence steering a state's choices.

**2.** Why is naming neocolonialism called a first defence?
**Answer:** Because control that is seen can be resisted; control that hides behind a changed flag narrows independence without being noticed.
