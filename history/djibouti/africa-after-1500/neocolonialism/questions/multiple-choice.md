# Neocolonialism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is neocolonialism?
a) a new kind of colony with governors  b) foreign control that stays after independence, through money, trade and influence  c) a festival after independence  d) the building of new capitals
**Answer: b) foreign control that stays after independence, through money, trade and influence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does neocolonial control work?
a) through governors and garrisons  b) without governors — through loans, trade terms, companies and influence  c) it does not work at all  d) through anthems
**Answer: b) without governors — through loans, trade terms, companies and influence** — That is what this lesson teaches.
