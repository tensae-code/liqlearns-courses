# Neocolonialism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Independence in law can be narrowed in fact by neocolonial control.
**Answer: True**

**2.** When the flag changed, all foreign control ended everywhere at once.
**Answer: False** — The flag changed but foreign control found new ways to stay — that is neocolonialism.
