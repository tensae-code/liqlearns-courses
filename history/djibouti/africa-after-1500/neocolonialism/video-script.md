# Video script — Neocolonialism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Neocolonialism is control without the flag: after independence, foreign control found new ways to stay — through money, trade and influence rather than governors.

The lesson begins with a definition. **Neocolonialism** is the continuation of foreign control after formal independence: the flag changes, the anthem is sung — and yet the decisive levers of the country's life remain in outside hands.

Its **practices** work without governors: control through money lent and owed, through trade terms set elsewhere, through companies and influence that steer a young state's choices. Its **consequences** are the lesson's warning — independence that is real in law can be narrowed in fact, and a people must learn to see control that no longer wears a uniform. Naming it is the first defence against it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
