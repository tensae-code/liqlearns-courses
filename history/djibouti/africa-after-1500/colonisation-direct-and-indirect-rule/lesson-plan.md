# Lesson plan — Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

**Course:** History — Djibouti · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The colonial empires ruled in different systems — direct rule, indirect rule, and apartheid — but every system, however it was arranged, put the coloniser on top.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The colonial empires ruled in different systems — direct rule, indirect rule, and apartheid — but every system, however it was arranged, put the coloniser on top.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: two managers running shops in different styles — one deciding everything, one working through supervisors — while the ownership, and the profit, stay in the same hands.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.
