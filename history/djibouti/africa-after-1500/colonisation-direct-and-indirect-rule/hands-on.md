# Hands-on — Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two head teachers run two schools differently: one manages every classroom herself; the other rules through the class prefects. The styles differ. Who is in charge in both schools? Hold that question. This lesson compares the colonial systems — and gives the same answer for all of them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The colonial empires ruled in different systems — direct rule, indirect rule, and apartheid — but every system, however it was arranged, put the coloniser on top. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
