# Video script — Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The colonial empires ruled in different systems — direct rule, indirect rule, and apartheid — but every system, however it was arranged, put the coloniser on top.

Once the land was taken, each empire had to **rule** it, and the empires chose different **systems**. Under **direct rule**, the coloniser's own officials governed. Under **indirect rule**, the coloniser governed through local rulers kept under its command. South Africa's system hardened into **apartheid** — rule by forced separation of peoples.

The lesson's judgement is plain, and it is the point of the comparison: **each empire rules differently, but every system puts the coloniser on top**. The differences matter — they shaped daily life differently in different colonies — but no arrangement of colonial rule made the colonised the masters of their own country. That is what 'colonial system' means, in all its forms.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
