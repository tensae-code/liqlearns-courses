# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which systems of colonial rule does the lesson name?
a) direct rule, indirect rule and apartheid  b) democracy, federation and monarchy  c) councils, courts and markets  d) none — all empires ruled identically
**Answer: a) direct rule, indirect rule and apartheid** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did all the colonial systems have in common?
a) they were all equally gentle  b) every one put the coloniser on top  c) they all ended quickly  d) they all used local languages only
**Answer: b) every one put the coloniser on top** — That is what this lesson teaches.
