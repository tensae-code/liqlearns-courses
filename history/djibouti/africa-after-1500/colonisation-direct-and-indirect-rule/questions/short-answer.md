# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How do direct and indirect rule differ?
**Answer:** Direct rule governs through the coloniser's own officials; indirect rule governs through local rulers kept under the coloniser's command.

**2.** Why compare the systems if the verdict is the same for all?
**Answer:** Because their differences shaped daily life differently — but the comparison shows the shared core: in every system the coloniser stood on top.
