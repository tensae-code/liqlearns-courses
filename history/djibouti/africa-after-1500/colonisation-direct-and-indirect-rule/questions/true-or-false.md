# Colonisation: Direct and Indirect Rule — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under indirect rule, the coloniser governed through local rulers under its command.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Indirect rule meant the colonised ruled themselves freely.
**Answer: False** — Local rulers governed under the coloniser's command; every system put the coloniser on top.
