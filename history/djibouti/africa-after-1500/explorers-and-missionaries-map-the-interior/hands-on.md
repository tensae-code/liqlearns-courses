# Hands-on — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A stranger knocks at a town's gate. He says he has come to see the land, to teach, to trade. The town lets him in. Years later, soldiers arrive who have read his maps and his letters. Was the first knock innocent? This lesson tells how Europe's presence began — and what followed behind it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Europe's presence in Africa came in stages: travellers, missionaries, explorers and traders arrived first — and settlement and conquest followed behind them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
