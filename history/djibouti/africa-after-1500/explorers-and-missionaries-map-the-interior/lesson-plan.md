# Lesson plan — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

**Course:** History — Djibouti · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Europe's presence in Africa came in stages: travellers, missionaries, explorers and traders arrived first — and settlement and conquest followed behind them.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Europe's presence in Africa came in stages: travellers, missionaries, explorers and traders arrived first — and settlement and conquest followed behind them.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a visitor welcomed to survey a family's land for a school, whose drawings are later used by others to claim the land — the welcome and the use made of it are two different things.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.
