# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two forms of the early European presence.
**Answer:** Any two: voyages/travellers, missions, exploration, commerce/trade.

**2.** How did the first arrivals prepare the way for what followed?
**Answer:** Their maps, reports and trading posts became the knowledge and footholds from which settlement and conquest were organised.
