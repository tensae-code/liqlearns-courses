# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The maps and reports of explorers and missionaries later served the taking of the land.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Colonisation began with armies, before any traveller or missionary came.
**Answer: False** — Travellers, missionaries, explorers and traders arrived first; settlement and conquest followed behind them.
