# Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who arrived first in Europe's presence in Africa?
a) armies of occupation  b) travellers, missionaries, explorers and traders  c) tax collectors  d) railway builders
**Answer: b) travellers, missionaries, explorers and traders** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What followed behind the first arrivals?
a) nothing  b) settlement, and then conquest  c) immediate independence  d) the end of all trade
**Answer: b) settlement, and then conquest** — That is what this lesson teaches.
