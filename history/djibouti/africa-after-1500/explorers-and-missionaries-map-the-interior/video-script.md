# Video script — Explorers and Missionaries Map the Interior

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Europe's presence in Africa came in stages: travellers, missionaries, explorers and traders arrived first — and settlement and conquest followed behind them.

Europe's presence in Africa took **forms**, in an order the lesson insists on. First came the **travellers, missionaries, explorers and traders**: voyages of discovery, missions carrying a new faith, exploration of the interior, and commerce along the coasts and rivers. These were the faces Africa first knew.

But the lesson refuses to stop at the first faces. Behind the travellers and missions came **settlement** — and behind settlement, conquest. The maps the explorers drew, the reports the missionaries sent and the trading posts the merchants planted became the knowledge and the footholds from which the taking of the land was organised. First the knock at the gate; then, in the next lessons, the seizure of the house.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
