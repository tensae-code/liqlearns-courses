1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The guide argues that the continent cannot unite while half its people are held back: the question of gender — the full place of women — belongs to Africa's unity and its future.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a school that finally opens its science prizes to every pupil and discovers talent it had been wasting — the whole school rises when the held-back half is freed.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
