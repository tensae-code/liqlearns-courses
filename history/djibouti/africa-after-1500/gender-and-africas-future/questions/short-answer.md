# Gender and Africa's Future — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In which fields does the lesson name women's full place?
**Answer:** Education, work, public life and the decisions of their countries.

**2.** Why does the book place this lesson inside the unity unit?
**Answer:** Because unity is the unit's theme, and the guide argues gender belongs inside it: no unity is whole while half the people are held back.
