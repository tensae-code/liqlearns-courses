# Gender and Africa's Future — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the guide argue about gender and unity?
a) they are unrelated  b) the continent cannot unite while half its people are held back  c) unity concerns borders only  d) women's place is a foreign topic
**Answer: b) the continent cannot unite while half its people are held back** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What fraction of Africa's people are women, in the lesson's terms?
a) a small minority  b) half  c) almost none  d) the lesson does not say
**Answer: b) half** — That is what this lesson teaches.
