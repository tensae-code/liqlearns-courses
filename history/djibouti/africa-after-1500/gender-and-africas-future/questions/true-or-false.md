# Gender and Africa's Future — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The full place of women is a condition of African unity, not a side topic.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A unity built by only half the people would still be a whole unity.
**Answer: False** — The guide argues it would be half a unity — the continent rises whole, or it does not rise.
