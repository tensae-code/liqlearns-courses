# Video script — Gender and Africa's Future

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The guide argues that the continent cannot unite while half its people are held back: the question of gender — the full place of women — belongs to Africa's unity and its future.

This lesson joins two things that are often kept apart: **the question of gender** and **the unity of the continent**. The guide's argument is direct: a continent **cannot unite while half its people are held back** — and half of Africa's people are its women.

So the full place of women — in education, in work, in public life and in the decisions of their countries — is not a side topic added to the unity project; it is a condition of it. A unity built by only half the people would be half a unity. The book sets the question before its young readers as part of Africa's future itself: the continent rises whole, or it does not rise.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
