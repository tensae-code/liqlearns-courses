# Hands-on — African Resistance to Colonisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A stranger arrives with a paper and says: sign, and your land is ours. Some sign under pressure. What happens next in town after town across a continent? This lesson answers: people fought — from the very first treaties onward. The conquest was never a walk into empty, silent land.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: African peoples fought the colonial conquest from the first treaties onward — resistance was not an afterthought to colonisation; it began with the conquest itself. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
