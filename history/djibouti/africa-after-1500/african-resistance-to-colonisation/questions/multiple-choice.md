# African Resistance to Colonisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When did African resistance to colonisation begin?
a) only after independence  b) from the first treaties onward  c) fifty years after the conquest  d) it never began
**Answer: b) from the first treaties onward** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What were the first treaties, in the conquest's story?
a) trade receipts only  b) papers pressed on rulers as the opening move of taking the land  c) peace songs  d) schoolbooks
**Answer: b) papers pressed on rulers as the opening move of taking the land** — That is what this lesson teaches.
