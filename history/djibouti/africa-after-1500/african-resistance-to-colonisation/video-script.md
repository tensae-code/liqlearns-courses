# Video script — African Resistance to Colonisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
African peoples fought the colonial conquest from the first treaties onward — resistance was not an afterthought to colonisation; it began with the conquest itself.

The conquest of Africa was **fought**, not simply received. From the **first treaties** onward — the papers pressed on rulers as the opening move of taking — African peoples organised **resistance movements** against the conquest: refusing, fighting, and contesting the seizure of their lands and their rule.

Place this lesson beside the Berlin lesson and the story corrects itself. The powers wrote their rules in Europe, but on African ground every advance met people defending their own country. Resistance did not wait for the colonial systems to settle in; it began with the conquest itself, and its memory feeds directly into the liberation movements of the next lesson.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
