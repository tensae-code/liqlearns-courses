# Liberation Movements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did resistance grow into?
a) trading companies  b) organised liberation movements  c) colonial armies  d) mission schools
**Answer: b) organised liberation movements** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is a liberation movement's declared aim?
a) lower taxes  b) independence — the end of colonial rule  c) a new treaty  d) better governors
**Answer: b) independence — the end of colonial rule** — That is what this lesson teaches.
