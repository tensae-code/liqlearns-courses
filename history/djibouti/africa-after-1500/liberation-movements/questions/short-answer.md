# Liberation Movements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does a liberation movement differ from the first resistance?
**Answer:** The first resistance refused particular seizures; the movements organised whole peoples behind one aim — ending colonial rule altogether.

**2.** Why does this lesson stand at the end of its level?
**Answer:** Because it is the bridge: organised movements aiming at independence lead directly to the independences of the next level.
