# Hands-on — The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The Atlantic is not the only ocean in this story, and ships are not the only carriers. Picture two older roads out of Africa: a caravan line crossing the desert northward, and sailing boats leaving the east coast. Who is walking in those lines, against their will? This lesson follows those two trades.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: For centuries, caravans across the Sahara and dhows across the Indian Ocean carried enslaved Africans out of the continent — the trans-Saharan and Indian Ocean slave trades, their causes and their consequences. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
