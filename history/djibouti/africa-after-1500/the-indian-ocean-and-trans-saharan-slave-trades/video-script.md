# Video script — The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
For centuries, caravans across the Sahara and dhows across the Indian Ocean carried enslaved Africans out of the continent — the trans-Saharan and Indian Ocean slave trades, their causes and their consequences.

Two great trades carried enslaved Africans away long before and long beside the Atlantic one. The **trans-Saharan trade** moved captives north on **caravans** across the desert; the **Indian Ocean trade** moved them east on **dhows** — the sailing boats of that ocean — from Africa's eastern coast. Both ran for **centuries**.

The lesson weighs their **causes and consequences** together: demand in the lands beyond desert and ocean pulled the trades onward, supply was fed by war and raiding within Africa, and the consequences fell on the continent — people drained away, communities living under the threat of capture, and routes of ordinary trade shadowed by the trade in human beings.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
