# The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which two slave trades does this lesson teach?
a) the Atlantic and the Pacific  b) the trans-Saharan and the Indian Ocean trades  c) the river and the lake trades  d) none — there was only one trade
**Answer: b) the trans-Saharan and the Indian Ocean trades** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How were enslaved people carried in these two trades?
a) only by train  b) by caravan across the desert and by dhow across the ocean  c) they were never moved  d) by aeroplane
**Answer: b) by caravan across the desert and by dhow across the ocean** — That is what this lesson teaches.
