# The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What pulled these trades onward, and what fed them?
**Answer:** Demand in the lands beyond desert and ocean pulled them; war and raiding within Africa fed the supply of captives.

**2.** Name one consequence of these trades for Africa.
**Answer:** People drained away from the continent, or communities living under the threat of capture — the human cost the lesson names.
