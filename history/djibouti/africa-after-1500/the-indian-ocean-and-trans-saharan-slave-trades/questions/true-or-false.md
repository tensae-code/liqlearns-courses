# The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The trans-Saharan and Indian Ocean slave trades ran for centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2.** These trades had no consequences inside Africa.
**Answer: False** — People were drained away and communities lived under the threat of capture — consequences the lesson weighs.
