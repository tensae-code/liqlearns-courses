# Lesson plan — The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades

**Course:** History — Djibouti · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** The Indian Ocean and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** For centuries, caravans across the Sahara and dhows across the Indian Ocean carried enslaved Africans out of the continent — the trans-Saharan and Indian Ocean slave trades, their causes and their consequences.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: For centuries, caravans across the Sahara and dhows across the Indian Ocean carried enslaved Africans out of the continent — the trans-Saharan and Indian Ocean slave trades, their causes and their consequences.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: two roads leaving one town in opposite directions, each carrying people away who will never return — the town is changed by both roads, not only the famous one.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.
