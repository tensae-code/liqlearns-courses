# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the European powers do at the Berlin Conference?
a) freed the colonies  b) wrote the rules for taking and occupying African land  c) asked African rulers for permission  d) divided Europe among themselves
**Answer: b) wrote the rules for taking and occupying African land** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who was missing from the Berlin table?
a) the European powers  b) Africans — no African sat at the table where Africa's rules were written  c) the map-makers  d) the translators
**Answer: b) Africans — no African sat at the table where Africa's rules were written** — That is what this lesson teaches.
