# Colonisation: The Berlin Conference — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did rival European powers want common rules for Africa?
**Answer:** So their rival claims would be made, recognised and held by agreed terms — organising the taking instead of fighting each other over it.

**2.** Why does the lesson stress where the conference was held?
**Answer:** Because Africa's fate was ruled on in Europe, without Africans present — the paper rules then governed the occupation on the ground.
