# Hands-on — Colonisation: The Berlin Conference

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine the owners of your town's land being decided in a meeting in a far capital — a meeting to which nobody from your town was invited — where the deciders draw up rules for who may take which street. That is the shape of this lesson. The town is a continent. The meeting is Berlin.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: European powers met in Berlin and wrote the rules for taking African land — a conference about Africa, held in Europe, that set the terms of the occupation to come. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
