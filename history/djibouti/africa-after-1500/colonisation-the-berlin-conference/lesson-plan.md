# Lesson plan — Colonisation: The Berlin Conference

**Course:** History — Djibouti · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Colonisation: The Berlin Conference
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** European powers met in Berlin and wrote the rules for taking African land — a conference about Africa, held in Europe, that set the terms of the occupation to come.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: European powers met in Berlin and wrote the rules for taking African land — a conference about Africa, held in Europe, that set the terms of the occupation to come.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a school timetable written by people who never asked the pupils — the rules are real and will be enforced, which is exactly why it matters who wrote them.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
