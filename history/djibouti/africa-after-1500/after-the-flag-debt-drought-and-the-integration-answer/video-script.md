# Video script — After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Independent Africa faces new challenges — environment, population, health, debt and globalisation — new dominations and new problems that its own children must solve.

Independence was a beginning, not an ending. This lesson lists the **new challenges** the book sets before the continent's children: the **environment** under pressure, **population** growing and moving, **health** to be secured for all, **debt** owed to outside lenders, and **globalisation** binding Africa's fortunes to a world economy it does not command.

The book's framing is sober and bracing: independent Africa faces **new dominations and new problems** — and they are problems **its children must solve**. No empire will solve them; that is what independence means. The lesson hands the desk of files to the learner's own generation, deliberately.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
