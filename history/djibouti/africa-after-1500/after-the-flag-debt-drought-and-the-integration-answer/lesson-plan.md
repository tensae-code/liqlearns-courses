## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Independent Africa faces new challenges — environment, population, health, debt and globalisation — new dominations and new problems that its own children must solve.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a family that finally gets its own house and, with the keys, its first repair bills, debts and decisions — freedom and the problems of freedom arriving together.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
