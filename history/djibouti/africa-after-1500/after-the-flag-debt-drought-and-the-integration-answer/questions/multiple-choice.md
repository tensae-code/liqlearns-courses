# After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which challenges does the book name for independent Africa?
a) none — independence solved everything  b) environment, population, health, debt and globalisation  c) only the weather  d) only foreign armies
**Answer: b) environment, population, health, debt and globalisation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who must solve these new problems, in the book's framing?
a) the old empires  b) Africa's own children — no one else will  c) nobody  d) the United Nations alone
**Answer: b) Africa's own children — no one else will** — That is what this lesson teaches.
