# After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three of the new challenges the book lists.
**Answer:** Any three of: environment, population, health, debt, globalisation.

**2.** Why does the book hand these problems to the learner's generation?
**Answer:** Because that is what independence means: no empire will solve them, so Africa's own children must.
