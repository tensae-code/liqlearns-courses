# Hands-on — After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The celebration ends. The flag is up. Now the new state opens its account books, looks at its rain gauges, its clinics, its growing towns — and finds a desk full of hard problems no anthem can solve. This lesson names those problems honestly, as the inheritance of independence.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Independent Africa faces new challenges — environment, population, health, debt and globalisation — new dominations and new problems that its own children must solve. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
