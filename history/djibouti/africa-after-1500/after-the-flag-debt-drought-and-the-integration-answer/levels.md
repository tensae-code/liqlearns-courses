# After the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Independent Africa faces new challenges — environment, population, health, debt and globalisation — new dominations and new problems that its own children must solve. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
