# Video script — Resistance to Slavery and the Slave Trades

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The enslaved resisted at every step — resistance to slavery and the slave trades runs through the whole story — and the lesson names slavery what it is: a crime against humanity.

This lesson restores the other half of the story. The enslaved **resisted at every step**: resisting capture, escaping on the march and from the coasts, rising against the trades that hunted them. Resistance was not rare or late — it runs from the first moment of the first capture to the last of the trades.

And the book gives the whole system its true name: **slavery, a crime against humanity**. That naming matters. It says the wrong was not only in cruel masters or hard journeys; the thing itself — human beings held, sold and worked as property — was a crime, and those who fought it, at whatever step and at whatever cost, stood on the side of humanity itself.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
