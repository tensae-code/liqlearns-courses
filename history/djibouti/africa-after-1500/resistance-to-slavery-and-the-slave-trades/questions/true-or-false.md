# Resistance to Slavery and the Slave Trades — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The enslaved resisted at every step of their captivity.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Calling slavery a crime against humanity is about cruel masters only, not the system itself.
**Answer: False** — The naming condemns the thing itself — human beings held and sold as property — not only individual cruelty.
