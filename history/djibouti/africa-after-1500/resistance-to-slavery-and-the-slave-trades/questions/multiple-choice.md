# Resistance to Slavery and the Slave Trades — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When did resistance to slavery happen?
a) only at the very end  b) at every step — capture, march, coast and beyond  c) never — people went quietly  d) only after independence
**Answer: b) at every step — capture, march, coast and beyond** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the book name slavery?
a) a normal business  b) a crime against humanity  c) a favour to the enslaved  d) a small mistake
**Answer: b) a crime against humanity** — That is what this lesson teaches.
