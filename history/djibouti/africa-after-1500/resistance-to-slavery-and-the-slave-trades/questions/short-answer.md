# Resistance to Slavery and the Slave Trades — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two steps at which the enslaved resisted.
**Answer:** Any two the lesson names: resisting capture, escaping on the march, escaping from the coasts, rising against the trades.

**2.** Why does it matter to name slavery a crime against humanity?
**Answer:** Because it judges the system itself, not only its cruelties — and it places those who resisted on the side of humanity.
