# Lesson plan — Resistance to Slavery and the Slave Trades

**Course:** History — Djibouti · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Resistance to Slavery and the Slave Trades
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The enslaved resisted at every step — resistance to slavery and the slave trades runs through the whole story — and the lesson names slavery what it is: a crime against humanity.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Slavery, Colonisation and Resistance — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The enslaved resisted at every step — resistance to slavery and the slave trades runs through the whole story — and the lesson names slavery what it is: a crime against humanity.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a person who refuses, escapes, fights back or holds onto their language and name under a system built to erase them — resistance takes many forms, all of them human.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.
