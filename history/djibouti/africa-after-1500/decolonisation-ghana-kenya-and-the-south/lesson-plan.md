# Lesson plan — Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South

**Course:** History — Djibouti · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Independence, Unity and Africa's Future (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Colony by colony, Africa won its flags back — the liberation of the continent came by negotiation in some lands and by hard struggle in others, until the colonial map was undone.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Independence, Unity and Africa's Future — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Colony by colony, Africa won its flags back — the liberation of the continent came by negotiation in some lands and by hard struggle in others, until the colonial map was undone.
2. Give one real example of it from their own life: a row of houses on one street regaining their own keys one by one — some by agreement with the landlord, some only after a long fight — until the whole street holds its own keys.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the story itself and the learner's own life, as the book itself works.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks independence came all at once, one way → colony by colony, by negotiation in some lands and by violent struggle in others.
- Go back to the one thing: Colony by colony, Africa won its flags back — the liberation of the continent came by negotiation in some lands and by hard struggle in others, until the colonial map was undone. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
