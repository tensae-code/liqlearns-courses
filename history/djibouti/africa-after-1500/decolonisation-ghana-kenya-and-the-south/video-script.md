# Video script — Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Colony by colony, Africa won its flags back — the liberation of the continent came by negotiation in some lands and by hard struggle in others, until the colonial map was undone.

This is the lesson the whole level turns on: **the liberation of the continent**. Colony by colony, the colonial map was undone and **Africa won its flags back** — new states raising their own colours where the empires' colours had flown.

The roads differed, and the book names both: **peaceful independences**, won by negotiation, pressure and politics; and **violent independences**, won through hard struggle where the coloniser would not let go. Ghana's road, Kenya's road and the long road of the south are among the stories this process holds. Different roads, one destination — a continent of states governing themselves, facing the new questions of the lessons that follow.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
