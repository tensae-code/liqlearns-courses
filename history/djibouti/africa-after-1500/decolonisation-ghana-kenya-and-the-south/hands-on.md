# Hands-on — Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture the colonial map of Africa — every land coloured with an empire's colour. Now picture the colours changing, land by land, year by year, to flags of their own. Some changes will be signed at tables. Others will be fought for. This lesson tells the great unmaking: decolonisation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Colony by colony, Africa won its flags back — the liberation of the continent came by negotiation in some lands and by hard struggle in others, until the colonial map was undone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
