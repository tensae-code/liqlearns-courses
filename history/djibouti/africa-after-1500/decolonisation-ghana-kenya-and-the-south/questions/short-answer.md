# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why did some colonies have to fight for independence?
**Answer:** Because some colonisers would not let go by negotiation; where the table failed, independence came by struggle.

**2.** What is decolonisation, in one sentence?
**Answer:** The process by which, colony by colony, Africa ended colonial rule and raised its own flags — by negotiation or by struggle.
