# Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did Africa win its flags back?
a) all on one day  b) colony by colony  c) by asking Europe once  d) they were never won back
**Answer: b) colony by colony** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What two roads to independence does the book name?
a) by sea and by air  b) peaceful, by negotiation — and violent, by struggle  c) by letter and by song  d) fast and slow
**Answer: b) peaceful, by negotiation — and violent, by struggle** — That is what this lesson teaches.
