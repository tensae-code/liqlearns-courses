# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What idea stands behind the founding of the OAU? (Sub lesson 1)
a) isolationism  b) pan-Africanism — African peoples share one fate and should stand together  c) colonialism renewed  d) each state alone forever
**Answer: b) pan-Africanism — African peoples share one fate and should stand together** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What keeps pulling the unity project back? (Sub lesson 2)
a) nothing  b) borders, rivalries and outside interests  c) the weather  d) too many schools
**Answer: b) borders, rivalries and outside interests** — That is what this lesson teaches.
