# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The OAU was in time renewed as the African Union. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Once the OAU was founded, African unity was complete and unchallenged. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Borders, rivalries and outside interests keep pulling the unity project back.
