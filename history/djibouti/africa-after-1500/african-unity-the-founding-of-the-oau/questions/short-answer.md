# African Unity: The Founding of the OAU — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** At what two heights is unity built in this lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Continent-wide, in the OAU/African Union — and regionally, in the integrations of neighbouring states.

**2.** Name the three obstacles to unity the book names. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Borders, rivalries, and outside interests.
