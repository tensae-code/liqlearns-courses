# Video script — African Unity: The Founding of the OAU

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
The new states tried to stand together: pan-Africanism inspired the founding of the OAU and later the African Union, with regional integrations beside them — while borders, rivalries and outside interests kept pulling the unity project back.

Part: Pan-Africanism, the OAU, the African Union and Regional Integrations.

Behind the founding stands an idea older than the states themselves: **pan-Africanism** — the conviction that African peoples share one fate and should stand together. When independence came, the new states tried to give that idea a house: the **OAU**, the Organisation of African Unity, founded to unite the continent's states — and in time renewed as the **African Union**.

Beside the continent-wide body grew **regional integrations** — nearer groupings of neighbouring states binding their economies and policies together. Unity, in this lesson, is built at two heights at once: the continent in one organisation, and the regions in theirs.

Part: The Obstacles to African Unity.

The book is honest about the drag on the project. **Borders** — inherited lines that divide peoples and inflame disputes — **rivalries** between states and leaders, and **outside interests** that profit from a divided continent all **keep pulling the unity project back**.

So the lesson ends in tension, and the tension is the teaching: unity was founded, truly — organisations exist, summits meet, regions integrate — yet the obstacles are also real, and every generation of Africans inherits both the founding and the fight against what undoes it.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
