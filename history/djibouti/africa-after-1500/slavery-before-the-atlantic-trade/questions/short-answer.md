# Slavery Before the Atlantic Trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two forms of unfreedom the lesson names.
**Answer:** Any two of: internal slavery, captivity, dependence, servitude.

**2.** Why must this lesson come before the lessons on the ocean trades?
**Answer:** Because the trades grew on ground where captivity already existed; knowing the older forms shows what the trades changed — their scale and nature.
