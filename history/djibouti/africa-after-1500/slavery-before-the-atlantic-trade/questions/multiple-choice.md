# Slavery Before the Atlantic Trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What existed inside Africa before the Atlantic slave trade?
a) no unfreedom of any kind  b) forms of internal slavery, captivity, dependence and servitude  c) only free farmers  d) the Atlantic trade itself
**Answer: b) forms of internal slavery, captivity, dependence and servitude** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does the lesson keep the forms distinct?
a) to make the lesson longer  b) because a captive, a dependent and a servant stood in different conditions with different rules  c) they are all exactly the same  d) distinctions do not matter in history
**Answer: b) because a captive, a dependent and a servant stood in different conditions with different rules** — That is what this lesson teaches.
