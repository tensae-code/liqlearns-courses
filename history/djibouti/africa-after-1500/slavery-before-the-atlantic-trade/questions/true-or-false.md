# Slavery Before the Atlantic Trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Captivity and dependence existed inside Africa before the Atlantic trade.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Atlantic trade invented captivity in Africa from nothing.
**Answer: False** — Forms of captivity and dependence already existed; the ocean trades grew on that ground and changed its scale and nature.
