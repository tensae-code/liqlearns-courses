# Video script — Slavery Before the Atlantic Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. History is told as a story here — animated-map style: who, where, what turned, and what came of it, in plain words.

---

This lesson tells one story.
Before the Atlantic ships, captivity and dependence already existed inside Africa in several forms — internal slavery, captivity, dependence and servitude were part of the continent's older story.

This lesson stands **before the ships**. Long before the Atlantic trade, African societies already knew **forms of unfreedom**: **internal slavery**, **captivity** — prisoners taken in war — and wider conditions of **dependence and servitude**, where a person's labour and movement belonged, in different degrees, to another.

The forms were not one thing, and the lesson keeps them distinct: a captive's fate, a dependent's place in a household, a servant's obligations — each had its own rules and its own harshness. Naming them honestly matters for everything after: when the ocean trades arrive in the next lessons, they will grow on ground where captivity already existed — and they will change its scale and nature beyond recognition.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
