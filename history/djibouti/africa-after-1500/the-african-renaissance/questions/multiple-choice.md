# The African Renaissance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the African Renaissance?
a) a European art period  b) a call for Africa to renew itself from its own heritage and confidence  c) a new colonial plan  d) a trade fair
**Answer: b) a call for Africa to renew itself from its own heritage and confidence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What are the Renaissance's foundations, in the book's telling?
a) foreign aid alone  b) Africa's own heritage and confidence  c) forgetting the past entirely  d) copying other continents in everything
**Answer: b) Africa's own heritage and confidence** — That is what this lesson teaches.
