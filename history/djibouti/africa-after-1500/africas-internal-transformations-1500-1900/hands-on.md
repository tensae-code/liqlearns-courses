# Hands-on — Africa's Internal Transformations, 1500–1900

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A river changes its course. Do you explain it by the sea it flows to — or by the land, the rain and the river's own waters? Between 1500 and 1900, African societies changed greatly. This lesson asks where the force of that change came from — and the book's answer may surprise you.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Between 1500 and 1900 Africa's societies kept changing from their own internal dynamics — the great socio-political formations of those centuries rose from Africa's own forces, not from European triggers. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
