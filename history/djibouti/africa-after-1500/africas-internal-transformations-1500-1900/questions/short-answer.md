# Africa's Internal Transformations, 1500–1900 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is a 'great socio-political formation' in this lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A state or society on a large scale that rose and transformed the region around it during these centuries.

**2.** What does 'endogenous causes' mean? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Causes from inside — Africa's own peoples' decisions, rivalries, ideas, trade and ambitions, not outside triggers.
