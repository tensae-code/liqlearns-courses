# Africa's Internal Transformations, 1500–1900 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The continent of 1900 was much changed from the continent of 1500. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True**

**2.** Europeans triggered the internal transformations of 1500–1900. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The guide insists Europeans did not trigger these changes; Africa's own forces did.
