# Africa's Internal Transformations, 1500–1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What happened to African societies between 1500 and 1900? (Sub lesson 1)
a) they froze unchanged  b) they kept changing — great new socio-political formations rose  c) they vanished  d) they waited for Europeans to organise them
**Answer: b) they kept changing — great new socio-political formations rose** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the guide insist about the causes of these changes? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Europeans triggered them all  b) the causes were endogenous — Africa's own forces drove them  c) there were no causes  d) the weather did it alone
**Answer: b) the causes were endogenous — Africa's own forces drove them** — That is what this lesson teaches.
