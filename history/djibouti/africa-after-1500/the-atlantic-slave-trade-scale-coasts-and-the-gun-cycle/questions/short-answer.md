# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Where did the demand that caused the Atlantic trade lie?
**Answer:** In the Americas — the demand for labour there pulled the trade across the ocean.

**2.** Give one consequence of the trade inside Africa.
**Answer:** The loss of millions, above all the young and strong, and societies bent around raiding, wars and fear — the consequences the lesson names.
