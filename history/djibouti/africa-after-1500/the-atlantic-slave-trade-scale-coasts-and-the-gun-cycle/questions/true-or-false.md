# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Americas were built in large part on the stolen labour of enslaved Africans.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Africa lost mainly its oldest people to the Atlantic trade.
**Answer: False** — It lost above all the young and strong — the loss that bent whole societies.
