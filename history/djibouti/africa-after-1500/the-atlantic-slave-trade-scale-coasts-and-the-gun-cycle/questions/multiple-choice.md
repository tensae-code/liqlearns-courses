# The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many people did the Atlantic slave trade tear from Africa?
a) a few hundred  b) millions, over centuries  c) only soldiers  d) none — people went willingly
**Answer: b) millions, over centuries** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which two continents did the trade reshape?
a) Asia and Europe  b) Africa and the Americas  c) Africa and Australia  d) Europe and the Americas only
**Answer: b) Africa and the Americas** — That is what this lesson teaches.
