# Hands-on — The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and the Gun Cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Count slowly to ten. Now imagine that counting, person by person, not to ten but to millions — each number a person taken from a home, a family, a language. That is the scale of this lesson's story. Hold the weight of the number. This lesson tells what the Atlantic trade did to two continents.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Atlantic slave trade tore millions of people from Africa and reshaped two continents — Africa that lost them, and the Americas that were built on their stolen labour. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
