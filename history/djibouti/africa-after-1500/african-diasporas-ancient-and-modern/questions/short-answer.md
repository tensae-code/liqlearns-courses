# African Diasporas, Ancient and Modern — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three ages of diaspora the book names.
**Answer:** Ancient, modern and contemporary diasporas.

**2.** How does the book hold both truths about the diasporas at once?
**Answer:** It honours their contributions and their ties to home, while naming the brain drain — the loss of the trained and talented — as a real cost to the continent.
