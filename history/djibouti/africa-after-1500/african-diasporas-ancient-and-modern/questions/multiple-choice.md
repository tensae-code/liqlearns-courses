# African Diasporas, Ancient and Modern — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What formed the earliest diasporas, above all?
a) tourism  b) the slave trades, which scattered Africans by force  c) student exchanges  d) sports leagues
**Answer: b) the slave trades, which scattered Africans by force** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the 'brain drain'?
a) a medical condition  b) the departure of the trained and talented — a loss the continent counts  c) a river in Africa  d) a kind of examination
**Answer: b) the departure of the trained and talented — a loss the continent counts** — That is what this lesson teaches.
