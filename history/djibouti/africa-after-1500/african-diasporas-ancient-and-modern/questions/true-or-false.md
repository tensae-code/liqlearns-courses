# African Diasporas, Ancient and Modern — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Africans in the diasporas stay tied to home, sending back money, ideas and loyalty.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The diasporas contribute nothing to the modern world.
**Answer: False** — Scattered Africans keep building the modern world — its labour, arts, sciences and struggles.
