# African Diasporas, Ancient and Modern — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Africans scattered across the world — by slavery, by migration, by exile — form diasporas ancient, modern and contemporary: they keep building the modern world, stay tied to home, and their departure by brain drain is a loss Africa counts. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
