# From Evidence to a Written History — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two kinds of source a class might hunt for.
**Answer:** Traces such as objects people made and used, written pages, photographs, and the spoken memories of elders — any two.

**2.** Why is questioning sources especially important for Africa's history?
**Answer:** Because much of the continent's past was carried in memory and objects rather than books, so careful questioning is what turns that evidence into a written past.
