---

This lesson tells one story.
History is the ordered story of past events: children learn what history is, and learn to place events in order on a timeline.

Every course of stories needs a first lesson, and this book begins by naming its subject. **History is the ordered story of past events** — not a heap of old things, but events told in the order they happened, so that one event can explain the next.

The tool of that order is the **timeline**: a line along which events are placed, earlier ones to one side, later ones to the other. A child who can place an event on the line — before this, after that — has taken the first step of the historian: turning the past from a blur into a story that can be followed, checked and retold.

Now you. Say the story back, in your own words. Then go and do the lesson's Do It Yourself — today, at home, at school.
