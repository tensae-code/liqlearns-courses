# What History Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is history?
**Answer:** History is the ordered story of past events — the past told in its order, so one event can explain the next.

**2.** Why does the order of events matter?
**Answer:** Because what came before helps explain what came after; out of order, the story stops making sense.
