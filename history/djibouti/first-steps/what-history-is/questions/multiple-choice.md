# What History Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is history, as this lesson teaches?
a) a heap of old objects with no order  b) the ordered story of past events  c) a list of kings' names only  d) stories invented for fun
**Answer: b) the ordered story of past events** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does a timeline do?
a) it hides the past  b) it places events in their order so the story can be followed  c) it makes events happen  d) it replaces all other sources
**Answer: b) it places events in their order so the story can be followed** — That is what this lesson teaches.
