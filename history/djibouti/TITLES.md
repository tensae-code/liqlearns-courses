# History — Djibouti course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

History belongs to the country: this course is Djibouti's
own — built from the one book filed under Djibouti, the
UNESCO General History of Africa (HGA) pedagogical-guide
prototype for ages 10–12 (French, teacher guide), whose
placements are pan-African: Africa's own story, told whole.
Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not
grades**: the titles below sit inside Levels 1–4, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grade
band the guide comes from lives only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the guide's: 9 learning units (UA)
feeding one base competence — arguing for continental
integration from Africa's past. The craft and the
beginnings first, then ancient Africa and its kingdoms,
then slavery, colonisation and resistance, and last
independence, unity and the future the guide hands to its
children. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — first steps and Africa's beginnings** (UA1),
in `first-steps/` and `africa-to-1500/`:

- `first-steps/` — What History Is (the ordered story of
  past events, told on a timeline) · From Evidence to a
  Written History (sources hunted and questioned, and
  turned into Africa's written past).
- `africa-to-1500/` — Africa, Cradle of Humankind (the
  first fossils show the human story beginning in Africa)
  · From Stone Tools to Farming (the first societies'
  stone tools and the way of life built around them).

**Level 2 — ancient Africa, its kingdoms and empires**
(UA2–UA3), in `africa-to-1500/`:

- Daily Life in Ancient Africa (four sub lessons: homes
  and towns · the family · food · dress, ornaments and
  art) · How Africa's Regions Traded with Each Other (two
  sub lessons: the Sahara, barrier and road, and the great
  rivers · the trade circuits of the great empires) ·
  Kingdoms, Empires and Other African States (two sub
  lessons: building and organising the states · learning,
  art and culture that outlived the rulers).

**Level 3 — slavery, colonisation and resistance**
(UA4–UA6), in `africa-after-1500/`:

- Slavery Before the Atlantic Trade (captivity, dependence
  and servitude inside the continent) · The Indian Ocean
  and Trans-Saharan Slave Trades (caravans and dhows, for
  centuries) · The Atlantic Slave Trade: Scale, Coasts and
  the Gun Cycle (millions torn away; two continents
  reshaped) · Resistance to Slavery and the Slave Trades
  (resistance at every step; slavery named a crime against
  humanity) · Africa's Internal Transformations, 1500–1900
  (two sub lessons: the great formations · Africa's own
  forces — the endogenous causes) · Explorers and
  Missionaries Map the Interior (travellers, missions and
  traders first; settlement and conquest behind them) ·
  Colonisation: The Berlin Conference (the rules for
  taking African land, written in Europe) · Colonisation:
  Direct and Indirect Rule (direct, indirect, apartheid —
  every system with the coloniser on top) · African
  Resistance to Colonisation (from the first treaties
  onward) · Liberation Movements (resistance organised,
  aiming at independence).

**Level 4 — independence, unity and Africa's future**
(UA7–UA9), in `africa-after-1500/`:

- Decolonisation: Ghana, Kenya and the South (colony by
  colony, by negotiation or by struggle) · Neocolonialism
  (the flag changes; control finds new ways to stay) ·
  African Unity: The Founding of the OAU (two sub lessons:
  pan-Africanism, the OAU, the African Union and regional
  integrations · the obstacles to African unity) · After
  the Flag: Debt, Drought and the Integration Answer (the
  new challenges: environment, population, health, debt,
  globalisation) · The African Renaissance (renewal from
  Africa's own heritage and confidence) · Gender and
  Africa's Future (no unity while half the people are held
  back) · African Diasporas, Ancient and Modern (scattered
  Africans building the modern world, tied to home; the
  brain drain counted).
