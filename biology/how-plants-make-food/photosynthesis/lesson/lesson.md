## Do it first
Go outside and find a green leaf. Hold it up to the sun. Feel how warm the
sun is on your hand. That warmth is energy — and the leaf is catching it too.

## Then the explanation
- A plant cannot walk to find food, so it makes its own food.
- The leaf takes in sunlight, air, and water.
- Inside the leaf, the sunlight's energy turns the air and water into food
  (sugar) for the plant.
- While it does this, the plant lets out oxygen — the air we breathe.

## Remember it like this
Leaf + sunlight + water + air → food for the plant + oxygen for us.
