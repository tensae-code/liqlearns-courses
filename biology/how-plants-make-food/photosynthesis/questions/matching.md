# Photosynthesis — matching

Matching questions only: join the two sides that go together.
Answers are at the bottom.

## Level 1 — the basics
Join each thing on the left to what it does on the right.

1. Sunlight      a) The plant drinks it from the ground
2. Water         b) It gives the energy to make food
3. Air           c) The plant lets this out — we breathe it
4. Oxygen        d) The leaf takes this in to make food

## Answers
1-b, 2-a, 3-d, 4-c
