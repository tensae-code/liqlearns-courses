# Photosynthesis — fill in the blank

Fill questions only: put the missing word in the space.
Grouped by level. Answers are at the bottom.

## Level 1 — the basics
1. A plant makes its own ______.
2. The leaf catches ______ from the sun.
3. The plant takes in water and ______ to make its food.
4. While it makes food, the plant lets out ______ — the air we breathe.

## Level 2 — a step deeper
1. A plant cannot ______ to find food, so it makes its own.
2. Inside the leaf, the sunlight's ______ turns the air and water into food.

## Answers
Level 1: 1-food, 2-light (sunlight), 3-air, 4-oxygen
Level 2: 1-walk, 2-energy
