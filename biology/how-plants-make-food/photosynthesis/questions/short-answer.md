# Photosynthesis — short answer

Short-answer questions only: answer in a word or a short sentence.
Grouped by level.

## Level 1 — the basics
1. What does a plant make for itself?
2. What three things does a leaf need to make food?
3. What gas does the plant let out?

## Level 2 — a step deeper
1. Why can't a plant just go and find food like an animal?
2. Where inside the plant does the food-making happen?
3. What would happen to a plant kept in a dark room? Why?

## Level 3 — think it through
1. Why do most leaves look flat and wide? Connect it to catching sunlight.
2. If the air had no carbon dioxide, what would change for the plant?
