# Photosynthesis — multiple choice

Choice questions only: pick the right answer. Grouped by level.
Answers are at the bottom — the teacher side holds them in the real app.

## Level 1 — the basics
1. What does a plant make for itself?
   a) Shoes  b) Food  c) Cars  d) Books
2. What does a leaf catch from the sun?
   a) Rain  b) Wind  c) Light  d) Snow
3. What gas does the plant let out while it makes food?
   a) Oxygen  b) Smoke  c) Dust  d) Steam

## Level 2 — a step deeper
1. Why does a plant make its own food?
   a) It cannot walk to find food  b) It likes cooking  c) It has no leaves  d) It sleeps all day
2. A plant kept in a dark room will…
   a) Grow faster  b) Make more food  c) Grow weak, because it has no light  d) Turn into a tree

## Answers
Level 1: 1-b, 2-c, 3-a
Level 2: 1-a, 2-c
