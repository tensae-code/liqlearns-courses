# Photosynthesis — true or false

True-or-false questions only. Grouped by level.
Answers are at the bottom.

## Level 1 — the basics
1. A plant can walk to find food. (True / False)
2. A leaf needs sunlight to make food. (True / False)
3. A plant lets out oxygen while it makes food. (True / False)

## Level 2 — a step deeper
1. A plant can make food in a dark room with no light at all. (True / False)
2. The food a plant makes is a kind of sugar. (True / False)

## Answers
Level 1: 1-False, 2-True, 3-True
Level 2: 1-False, 2-True
