# Photosynthesis — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: the dark cupboard plant
You need: two small plants (or bean seeds in two cups), a cupboard.
1. Put one plant by the window. Put the other in a dark cupboard.
2. Give both the same water every day.
3. After one week, look at both. Which one grew? Which one looks weak?
4. Say why, in your own words: what was the dark plant missing?

## Project 2: bubbles from a leaf
You need: a clear glass of water, a fresh green leaf, a sunny spot.
1. Put the leaf in the water, in the sun.
2. Wait and watch the leaf closely.
3. Tiny bubbles appear on the leaf — that is the oxygen the plant lets out
   while it makes its food.
