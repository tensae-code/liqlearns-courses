# Photosynthesis — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget. This file can
be handed to a video-making AI as-is.

---

[Picture: the sun in the sky]

This is the sun.
The sun gives light.
Light is energy. Energy makes things work.

[Picture: a green plant under the sun]

This is a plant.
The plant cannot walk. It cannot go and buy food.
So the plant makes its own food.

[Picture: the leaf catching the light]

The leaf catches the light.
The leaf drinks water from the ground.
The leaf breathes in air.

[Picture: inside the leaf, light + water + air becoming food]

Inside the leaf, the light cooks the water and the air together.
They become food. Sweet food. Food for the plant.

[Picture: bubbles of air leaving the leaf]

And look — the leaf gives something back.
It gives out clean air.
The air we breathe.

[Picture: a child taking a deep breath under a tree]

So every breath you take…
a plant helped make it.
