# Hands-on — Nitrates — gunpowder, saltpetre and exploding fertiliser

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Heat table (paper only)
Make the family's heat table: one row per nitrate of the lesson (sodium, potassium by family, silver, ammonium), columns for the products and the warning if any. The ammonium row must carry the book's warning word for word in your own hand.

## Ring at the junction (paper only)
Draw the brown-ring tube in section: the two liquid layers, the acid's path down the wall, and the ring at the junction. Label each layer and write, beside the ring, the product's printed formula «FeSO₄·NO» and the activity number that owns the test.
