# Nitrates — gunpowder, saltpetre and exploding fertiliser — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Chile saltpetre is…
a) Sodium nitrate  b) Potassium nitrate  c) Silver nitrate  d) Ammonium nitrate
**Answer: a) Sodium nitrate**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) "Gunpowder salt" (ملح البارود) is…
a) Sodium nitrate  b) Potassium nitrate  c) Silver nitrate  d) Calcium nitrate
**Answer: b) Potassium nitrate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A property of ALL nitrates in the lesson is that they…
a) Are insoluble  b) Explode on heating gently  c) Dissolve in water  d) Are coloured
**Answer: c) Dissolve in water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Heated, sodium nitrate gives…
a) The metal and NO₂  b) Nitrous oxide and water  c) Nothing — it is unchanged  d) Nitrite and oxygen
**Answer: d) Nitrite and oxygen**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Strongly heated ammonium nitrate…
a) Can detonate — warm it gently, with great care  b) Melts quietly  c) Turns to ammonia  d) Sublimes
**Answer: a) Can detonate — warm it gently, with great care**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The brown ring test's brown product is written…
a) Fe(OH)₃  b) «FeSO₄·NO»  c) AgCl  d) «FeSO₄·NO₂»
**Answer: b) «FeSO₄·NO»**
