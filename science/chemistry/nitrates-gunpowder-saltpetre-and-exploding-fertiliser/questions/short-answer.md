# Nitrates — gunpowder, saltpetre and exploding fertiliser — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the three heat equations of the lesson.
**Answer: 2NaNO₃ → 2NaNO₂ + O₂; 2AgNO₃ → 2Ag + 2NO₂ + O₂; NH₄NO₃ → N₂O + 2H₂O (as printed, nitrous oxide and water).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Describe the brown ring test's bench order exactly.
**Answer: Sodium nitrate solution with fresh iron(II) sulfate solution in the tube; concentrated sulfuric acid run slowly down the wall so it forms its own layer; the brown «FeSO₄·NO» ring appears at the junction.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the book's ammonium nitrate warning in its own terms.
**Answer: Strongly heated NH₄NO₃ can detonate; ammonium nitrate must be warmed gently and with great care.**
