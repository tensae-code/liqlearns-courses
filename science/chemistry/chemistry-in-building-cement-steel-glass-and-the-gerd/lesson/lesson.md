## Chemistry in building

Chemistry supplies glass, steel and cement for safer houses, multi-storey buildings, durable dams and bridges; the worked example is the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam in Benishangul-Gumuz — 6,450 MW on the Blue Nile, about 30 km from the Sudan border, set to be Africa's largest hydropower project.
