# Systematisation — review of Grades 10 and 11 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Cuba's Grade 11 systematisation unit lasts…
a) 2 hours  b) 20 hours  c) 200 hours  d) one lesson
**Answer: b) 20 hours**

**2.** The unit's content is…
a) a new topic  b) structured revision of Grades 10 and 11  c) laboratory safety  d) organic chemistry only
**Answer: b) structured revision of Grades 10 and 11**

**3.** The periodic-law part of the frame uses the modern table with…
a) 8 columns  b) 12 columns  c) 18 columns  d) 32 columns
**Answer: c) 18 columns**

**4.** In the guide's systematisation, table position is predicted from…
a) atomic mass alone  b) electron distribution  c) density  d) colour
**Answer: b) electron distribution**

**5.** Reactions are classified in the unit by…
a) colour change  b) oxidation-number change  c) smell  d) state only
**Answer: b) oxidation-number change**

**6.** This lesson's sources are…
a) two student textbooks  b) a programme and a methodological guide — teacher evidence  c) a workbook  d) a syllabus only
**Answer: b) a programme and a methodological guide — teacher evidence**
