# Systematisation — review of Grades 10 and 11 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is a systematisation unit, in Cuba's Grade 11 pattern?
**Answer: A closing unit — 20 hours — whose content is the structured revision of two years of chemistry, re-organising everything taught into one system instead of introducing anything new.**

**2.** Name the frame the unit systematises.
**Answer: Periodic law and the modern 18-column table, the main inorganic substance classes and their genetic relation, reaction classification, energy and rate, equilibrium constants and shifts, and quantitative problems on samples, reactions and solutions.**

**3.** Which equilibrium systems does the guide carry the constants across?
**Answer: Homogeneous and heterogeneous systems, and molecular and ionic systems.**

**4.** Why does this lesson count in no country's acceptance?
**Answer: Both its sources are teacher evidence — the Grade 11 programme and the Grade 11 methodological guide — not student textbooks, so the lesson stands on teacher evidence only, counted nowhere.**
