Today: **Systematisation — review of Grades 10 and 11** — Cuba's way of finishing: systematise.
Cuba's Grade 11 course does not end with a new topic. It ends with twenty hours that re-organise two whole years — the periodic law and its table, the classes of substances, the kinds of reaction, energy, rate and equilibrium — into one system that holds together. Nothing new; everything connected.
