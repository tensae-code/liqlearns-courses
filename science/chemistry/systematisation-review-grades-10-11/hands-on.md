# Hands-on — Systematisation — review of Grades 10 and 11

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the two-year wall chart
On one large sheet, draw the frame Cuba's Unit 4 uses: periodic law and the 18-column table at the top; under it the main inorganic substance classes with arrows for their genetic relation; beside them reaction classification (by energy, and by oxidation-number change), energy and rate, and equilibrium constants and shifts. Place five things you learned in Grades 10–11 onto the chart, each in its box, and draw the arrow that joins each pair.

## Predict from position
Pick one element. From its electron distribution alone, predict its table position, then check against the chart — the guide's own systematisation exercise. Say which periodic property you used and whether the prediction held.
