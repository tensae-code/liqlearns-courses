# Lewis acids and bases — electron pairs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Define a Lewis acid.
a) Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms  b) An electron-pair acceptor  c) The monomer units in the chain  d) The atmosphere
**Answer: b) An electron-pair acceptor**

**2. ** Define a Lewis base.
a) An electron-pair donor  b) To become more stable  c) Removing electrons from atoms  d) Electron pairs repel and spread apart
**Answer: a) An electron-pair donor**

**3. ** Give a Lewis acid with no protons.
a) Hydrogen bonding between molecules  b) Alkoxyalkane  c) The carbonyl group, C=O  d) BF₃ (or a metal ion)
**Answer: d) BF₃ (or a metal ion)**

**4. ** What does the base's pair move into?
a) Near neutral  b) Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)  c) An empty orbital of the acid  d) Strong acid + strong base salt
**Answer: c) An empty orbital of the acid**
