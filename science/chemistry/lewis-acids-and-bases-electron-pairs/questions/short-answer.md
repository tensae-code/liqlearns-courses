# Lewis acids and bases — electron pairs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Define a Lewis acid.
**Answer: An electron-pair acceptor**

**2. ** Define a Lewis base.
**Answer: An electron-pair donor**

**3. ** Give a Lewis acid with no protons.
**Answer: BF₃ (or a metal ion)**

**4. ** What does the base's pair move into?
**Answer: An empty orbital of the acid**
