BF₃ has no proton to give, yet it behaves as an acid toward ammonia.

Lewis: an acid is an electron-pair acceptor; a base is an electron-pair donor.

The definition widens the family beyond protons — BF₃ accepts a pair into boron's empty orbital, and metal ions act as Lewis acids.

Every Brønsted–Lowry base is a Lewis base (it donates the pair that holds the proton), but Lewis acids include many species with no proton at all.

Now you. Say it back: Define a Lewis acid.
Answer: An electron-pair acceptor.
