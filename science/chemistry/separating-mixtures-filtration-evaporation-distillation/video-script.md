Today: **Separating mixtures — filtration, evaporation and distillation** — Morocco's way: one chapter, the whole family of methods, and the pure body at the end.

Sand in water? Let it rest and pour — that is décantation. Still cloudy? Pour it through a filter: the water goes through, the sand stays. Two methods, one idea — the solid is big enough to stop.
Salt in water is different: no filter can catch what has dissolved. So change the water's state instead. Evaporate it, and the salt stays behind. Or distil: boil, catch the vapour, cool it back to liquid — now you have the water back too, pure.
And that is where the chapter is heading: every constituent you get back on its own is a pure body, one body with its own definite properties. A mixture is bodies together; separation is getting the pure bodies back, one method per kind of mixture.
