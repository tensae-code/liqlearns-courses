# Separating mixtures — filtration, evaporation and distillation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The solid left on the filter after filtration is the _____.
**Answer: residue**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Letting a mixture rest so the solid settles, then pouring the liquid off, is _____.
**Answer: décantation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Boiling a mixture and condensing its vapour back to a liquid collected apart is _____.
**Answer: distillation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A constituent recovered on its own, with definite properties of its own, is a pure _____.
**Answer: body**
