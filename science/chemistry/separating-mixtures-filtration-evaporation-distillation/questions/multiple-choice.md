# Separating mixtures — filtration, evaporation and distillation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In filtration, the liquid that passes through the filter is called the…
a) Residue  b) Filtrate  c) Sediment  d) Vapour
**Answer: b) Filtrate**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Décantation separates a mixture by…
a) Boiling it  b) Letting the solid settle at rest, then pouring the liquid off gently  c) Passing it through paper  d) Dissolving the solid
**Answer: b) Letting the solid settle at rest, then pouring the liquid off gently**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Salt dissolved in water cannot be recovered by filtration because…
a) Salt is too heavy  b) The dissolved body has spread through the liquid as particles too small for the filter to hold back  c) Filters dissolve in salt water  d) Salt water is a pure body
**Answer: b) The dissolved body has spread through the liquid as particles too small for the filter to hold back**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Distillation recovers the liquid, and not only the solid, because…
a) It filters twice  b) The vapour is condensed back to liquid and collected apart  c) The mixture is left at rest longer  d) The boiler is made of glass
**Answer: b) The vapour is condensed back to liquid and collected apart**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Evaporation of salt water leaves behind…
a) The water  b) Nothing  c) The salt, while the water is lost as vapour  d) A new mixture
**Answer: c) The salt, while the water is lost as vapour**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A pure body (corps pur) is…
a) Any mixture that looks clear  b) One body only, with definite properties of its own, the same in every sample  c) A body that has been filtered  d) A liquid with nothing dissolved in it yet
**Answer: b) One body only, with definite properties of its own, the same in every sample**
