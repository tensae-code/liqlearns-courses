# Purification of organic compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Crystallisation purifies a solid because the compound…
a) Sublimes on heating  b) Is freely soluble in a hot solvent and sparingly soluble in the same solvent cold, so it separates as crystals on cooling while soluble impurities stay in the mother liquor  c) Reacts with the solvent  d) Is insoluble in every solvent
**Answer: b) Is freely soluble in a hot solvent and sparingly soluble in the same solvent cold, so it separates as crystals on cooling while soluble impurities stay in the mother liquor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Camphor mixed with sand is purified best by…
a) Steam distillation  b) Fractional distillation  c) Sublimation — camphor passes straight to vapour and condenses pure, leaving the sand  d) Extraction
**Answer: c) Sublimation — camphor passes straight to vapour and condenses pure, leaving the sand**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A liquid that decomposes at its ordinary boiling point is distilled…
a) With steam  b) Under reduced pressure, so it boils before it can decompose  c) Fractionally  d) Not at all — it cannot be purified
**Answer: b) Under reduced pressure, so it boils before it can decompose**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Steam distillation requires the compound to be…
a) Soluble in water  b) Steam-volatile and insoluble in water  c) A solid  d) Coloured
**Answer: b) Steam-volatile and insoluble in water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In thin-layer chromatography, a component's Rf value is…
a) Its boiling point  b) The ratio of the distance it travels to the distance the solvent front travels  c) Its mass  d) The size of its spot
**Answer: b) The ratio of the distance it travels to the distance the solvent front travels**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A chromatogram of a sample shows two spots. The verdict is…
a) The sample is pure  b) The plate was too long  c) The sample is impure — a pure compound shows one spot  d) The solvent was wrong
**Answer: c) The sample is impure — a pure compound shows one spot**
