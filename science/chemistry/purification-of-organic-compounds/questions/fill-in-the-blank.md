# Purification of organic compounds — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solid that passes directly from solid to vapour on heating is purified by _____.
**Answer: sublimation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Glycerol, which decomposes at its ordinary boiling point, is purified by distillation under reduced _____.
**Answer: pressure**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In differential extraction the compound moves into the solvent in which it is more _____.
**Answer: soluble**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Paper chromatography is the school form of _____ chromatography.
**Answer: partition**
