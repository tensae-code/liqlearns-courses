# Arrhenius acids and bases — and where the idea stops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does an Arrhenius acid give in water?
a) Spontaneous as written  b) Turns raw materials into valuable products  c) Fewer gas moles on the product side  d) H⁺ ions
**Answer: d) H⁺ ions**

**2. ** What does an Arrhenius base give in water?
a) The electron  b) Photons knock electrons off a metal  c) OH⁻ ions  d) atomos
**Answer: c) OH⁻ ions**

**3. ** Name a base Arrhenius cannot explain.
a) The partial pressures  b) Ammonia, NH₃  c) ½(bonding − antibonding electrons)  d) Particles are far apart
**Answer: b) Ammonia, NH₃**

**4. ** In water, H⁺ really exists as…
a) H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)  b) A carboxylic acid  c) In fruits and flowers  d) The acid's salt plus the alcohol
**Answer: a) H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)**
