# Arrhenius acids and bases — and where the idea stops — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does an Arrhenius acid give in water?
**Answer: H⁺ ions**

**2. ** What does an Arrhenius base give in water?
**Answer: OH⁻ ions**

**3. ** Name a base Arrhenius cannot explain.
**Answer: Ammonia, NH₃**

**4. ** In water, H⁺ really exists as…
**Answer: H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)**
