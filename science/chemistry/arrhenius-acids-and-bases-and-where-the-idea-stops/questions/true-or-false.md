# Arrhenius acids and bases — and where the idea stops — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What does an Arrhenius acid give in water — H⁺ ions
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What does an Arrhenius base give in water — Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH
**Answer: False**

**3. ** Name a base Arrhenius cannot explain. — Ammonia, NH₃
**Answer: True**

**4. ** In water, H⁺ really exists as… — Harvests remove it from the soil
**Answer: False**
