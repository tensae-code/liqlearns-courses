# Arrhenius acids and bases — and where the idea stops — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What does an Arrhenius acid give in water? — d
2. What does an Arrhenius base give in water? — a
3. Name a base Arrhenius cannot explain. — b
4. In water, H⁺ really exists as… — c

**Answer key:** 1–d: H⁺ ions · 2–a: OH⁻ ions · 3–b: Ammonia, NH₃ · 4–c: H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)
