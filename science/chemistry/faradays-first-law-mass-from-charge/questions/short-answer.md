# Faraday's first law — mass from charge — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** State Faraday's first law.
**Answer: Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q)**

**2. ** Write Q in terms of I and t.
**Answer: Q = I × t**

**3. ** What is one faraday?
**Answer: The charge of one mole of electrons (≈96,500 C)**

**4. ** What unit measures charge?
**Answer: The coulomb**
