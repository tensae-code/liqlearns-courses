# Faraday's first law — mass from charge — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. State Faraday's first law. — a
2. Write Q in terms of I and t. — b
3. What is one faraday? — c
4. What unit measures charge? — d

**Answer key:** 1–a: Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q) · 2–b: Q = I × t · 3–c: The charge of one mole of electrons (≈96,500 C) · 4–d: The coulomb
