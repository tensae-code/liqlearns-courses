# Faraday's first law — mass from charge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** State Faraday's first law.
a) Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q)  b) ½(bonding − antibonding electrons)  c) Particles are far apart  d) The partial pressures
**Answer: a) Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q)**

**2. ** Write Q in terms of I and t.
a) A carboxylic acid  b) In fruits and flowers  c) The acid's salt plus the alcohol  d) Q = I × t
**Answer: d) Q = I × t**

**3. ** What is one faraday?
a) 0.00 V  b) Negative (spontaneous)  c) The charge of one mole of electrons (≈96,500 C)  d) Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses
**Answer: c) The charge of one mole of electrons (≈96,500 C)**

**4. ** What unit measures charge?
a) Three  b) The coulomb  c) The rise in average temperature  d) Redesign, reuse or efficiency in the plant
**Answer: b) The coulomb**
