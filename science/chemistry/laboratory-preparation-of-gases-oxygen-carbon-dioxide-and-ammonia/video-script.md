Today: **Laboratory preparation of gases** — Dominica's CSEC gas preparations: properties first, apparatus second.

Three gases, three designs, one rule: read the properties and build accordingly. Oxygen fizzes out of hydrogen peroxide on a black catalyst and collects calmly over water. Carbon dioxide comes from rock and acid — hydrochloric, never sulfuric, whose insoluble sulfate would seal the rock and stop the show. Ammonia, alkaline and thirsty for water, may be dried only on calcium oxide and must be caught rising, by downward displacement, its fullness announced when damp red litmus turns blue at the mouth.
