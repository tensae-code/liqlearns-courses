# Laboratory preparation of gases — oxygen, carbon dioxide and ammonia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A preparation is designed from the gas's…
a) Colour and smell  b) Solubility, reactivity with drying agents, and density  c) Price  d) Name
**Answer: b) Solubility, reactivity with drying agents, and density**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Laboratory oxygen is made from hydrogen peroxide with a catalyst of…
a) Calcium oxide  b) Manganese(IV) oxide  c) Calcium carbonate  d) Sulfuric acid
**Answer: b) Manganese(IV) oxide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Sulfuric acid must not be used to make carbon dioxide because…
a) It is too weak  b) Its calcium sulfate is insoluble and coats the carbonate, stopping the reaction  c) It produces oxygen instead  d) It is colourless
**Answer: b) Its calcium sulfate is insoluble and coats the carbonate, stopping the reaction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Dry ice is solid carbon dioxide at…
a) 0 °C  b) −10 °C  c) −78.5 °C  d) −100 °C
**Answer: c) −78.5 °C**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Ammonia may be dried only with…
a) Concentrated sulfuric acid  b) Calcium oxide  c) Water  d) Hydrochloric acid
**Answer: b) Calcium oxide**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A vessel is known to be full of ammonia when moist red litmus at its mouth…
a) Turns blue  b) Bleaches  c) Stays red  d) Catches fire
**Answer: a) Turns blue**
