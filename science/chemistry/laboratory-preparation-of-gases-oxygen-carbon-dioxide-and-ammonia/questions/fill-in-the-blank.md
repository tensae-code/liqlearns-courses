# Laboratory preparation of gases — oxygen, carbon dioxide and ammonia — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A substance that speeds a reaction and is unchanged at the end is a _____.
**Answer: catalyst**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The acid used with calcium carbonate to make carbon dioxide is _____ acid.
**Answer: hydrochloric**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Ammonia is collected by _____ displacement of air, because it is less dense than air.
**Answer: downward**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Ammonia is made by heating calcium hydroxide with _____ chloride.
**Answer: ammonium**
