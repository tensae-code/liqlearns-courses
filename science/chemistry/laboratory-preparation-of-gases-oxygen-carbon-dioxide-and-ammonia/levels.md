# Laboratory preparation of gases — oxygen, carbon dioxide and ammonia — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Designing a preparation from the gas's properties — oxygen first, Carbon dioxide — and the acid you must not use, Ammonia — the gas that must stay dry), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, re-deriving each preparation from the gas's property table (solubility, density, reactivity) before checking the book's design, and naming the test that proves each vessel full or each gas present.
