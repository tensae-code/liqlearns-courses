# Lesson plan — Metalloids

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Metalloids
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the metalloids — the elements with both metal and non-metal character, shown depending on the conditions — as Afghanistan's Grade 11 textbook teaches them in Ch.11 through boron (fibres in plastics stronger than steel and lighter than aluminium, for aircraft and spacecraft bodies; borazon pictured) and silicon (semiconductor; window glass from melting silicon with CaO; SiO₂ with a structure like diamond)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. No other lesson is needed first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Neither metal nor non-metal — both, by conditions** — The metalloids are the border elements: both metal and non-metal character in one element, the side shown depending on the conditions (Ch.11, printed p. 224).
2. **Boron — fibres stronger than steel, lighter than aluminium** — Boron fibres set in plastics make a material stronger than steel and lighter than aluminium, used in aircraft and spacecraft bodies; borazon is pictured.
3. **Silicon — the semiconductor, glass and the diamond-like oxide** — Silicon the semiconductor; window glass from melting silicon with CaO; SiO₂ structured like diamond.
