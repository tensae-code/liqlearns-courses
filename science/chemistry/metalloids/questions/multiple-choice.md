# Metalloids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A metalloid is an element that…
a) Is always a metal  b) Has both metal and non-metal character, shown depending on the conditions  c) Is always a gas  d) Never reacts
**Answer: b) Has both metal and non-metal character, shown depending on the conditions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Boron fibres set in plastics make a material that is…
a) Heavier than steel and weaker than aluminium  b) Stronger than steel and lighter than aluminium  c) A semiconductor  d) Used only for window glass
**Answer: b) Stronger than steel and lighter than aluminium**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The book names boron-fibre material at work in…
a) Window glass  b) Aircraft and spacecraft bodies  c) Fertilisers  d) Pacemaker batteries
**Answer: b) Aircraft and spacecraft bodies**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Ordinary window glass, in the book, comes from melting silicon with…
a) NaCl  b) CaO  c) Zn  d) H₂SO₄
**Answer: b) CaO**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The structure of SiO₂ is like the structure of…
a) Graphite  b) A salt lattice  c) Diamond  d) Ice
**Answer: c) Diamond**
