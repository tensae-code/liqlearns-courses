# Metalloids — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does "depending on the conditions" mean in the book's definition of a metalloid?
**Answer:** The same element can show metal-like behaviour under some conditions and non-metal-like behaviour under others — it does not belong wholly to either side.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Give silicon's three appearances in the chapter and what it does in each.
**Answer:** As a semiconductor it carries electronics; melted with CaO it gives ordinary window glass; its oxide SiO₂ has a structure like diamond.
