# Video script — Describing a gas — P, V, T, n

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four numbers completely describe a sample of gas.

A gas is described by pressure P, volume V, temperature T and amount n (moles).

Pressure is the force of particle collisions on the container walls, measured with a barometer (air pressure) or manometer (enclosed gas).

Units convert between atmospheres, mmHg, torr and pascals — conversions are drilled before the gas laws use them.

Now you. Say it back: Name the four gas variables.
Answer: Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n).
