# Describing a gas — P, V, T, n — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name the four gas variables.
a) Electron pairs repel and spread apart  b) Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)  c) To become more stable  d) Removing electrons from atoms
**Answer: b) Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)**

**2. ** What measures atmospheric pressure?
a) A barometer  b) Hydrogen bonding between molecules  c) Alkoxyalkane  d) The carbonyl group, C=O
**Answer: a) A barometer**

**3. ** What measures an enclosed gas's pressure?
a) Strong acid + strong base salt  b) Near neutral  c) Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)  d) A manometer
**Answer: d) A manometer**

**4. ** What causes gas pressure?
a) A small molecule that joins into chains  b) The biosphere  c) Particle collisions with the walls  d) Care/treatment — it pollutes
**Answer: c) Particle collisions with the walls**
