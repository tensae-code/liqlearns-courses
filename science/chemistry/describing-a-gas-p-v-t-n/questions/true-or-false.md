# Describing a gas — P, V, T, n — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Name the four gas variables. — Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What measures atmospheric pressure — Particles are locked in place
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What measures an enclosed gas's pressure — A manometer
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What causes gas pressure — Tenfold
**Answer: False**
