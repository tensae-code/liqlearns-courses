# Describing a gas — P, V, T, n — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name the four gas variables.
**Answer: Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)**

**2. ** What measures atmospheric pressure?
**Answer: A barometer**

**3. ** What measures an enclosed gas's pressure?
**Answer: A manometer**

**4. ** What causes gas pressure?
**Answer: Particle collisions with the walls**
