Today: **Carbon dioxide — properties, preparation and tests** — Greece's way, from its own book.

Carbon dioxide: no colour, no smell — but heavier than air, so it sinks, pools, and pours downward like an invisible liquid.

It puts flames out. That is its danger in a cellar and its job in an extinguisher.

Make it from marble chips and dilute acid — fizz, no heating — and collect it downward, the way its density tells you to.

Prove it with limewater: clear turns milky. That white cloud is carbon dioxide's signature.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
