# Carbon dioxide — properties, preparation and tests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon dioxide compared with air is…
a) Lighter  b) The same density  c) Denser  d) Weightless
**Answer: c) Denser**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A flame lowered into carbon dioxide…
a) Burns brighter  b) Goes out — the gas supports neither combustion nor respiration  c) Turns blue  d) Explodes
**Answer: b) Goes out — the gas supports neither combustion nor respiration**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The laboratory preparation uses marble chips with…
a) Water  b) Dilute hydrochloric acid  c) Sodium hydroxide  d) Limewater
**Answer: b) Dilute hydrochloric acid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Carbon dioxide is collected by downward delivery because it is…
a) Hot  b) Soluble in acid  c) Denser than air  d) Colourless
**Answer: c) Denser than air**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The test that proves carbon dioxide is…
a) It pops at a splint  b) It relights a glowing splint  c) It turns limewater milky  d) It bleaches litmus
**Answer: c) It turns limewater milky**
