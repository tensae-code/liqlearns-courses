# Carbon dioxide — properties, preparation and tests — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can carbon dioxide be poured from one vessel down into another, and why does that make it dangerous in wells?
**Answer: Because it is denser than air it flows downward and pools in low places; a well or cellar can fill with it invisibly, and it supports neither combustion nor respiration, so it suffocates.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Balance the preparation in words: what are the two products besides carbon dioxide?
**Answer: Calcium chloride and water: CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give two uses of carbon dioxide and name the property each one spends.
**Answer: Fire extinguishers — it smothers flames, supporting no combustion; fizzy drinks — it dissolves in water under pressure (dry ice, subliming cold and clean, is also accepted).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) State the natural balance the lesson closes on, and what upsets it.
**Answer: Photosynthesis draws carbon dioxide out of the air while respiration and burning return it; when the returns — above all burning — outgrow the drawing-down, the excess builds up and drives the greenhouse effect.**
