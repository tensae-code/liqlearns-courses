## Sub lessons
1. **The properties of carbon dioxide** — Carbon dioxide is a colourless, odourless gas, denser than air — it can be poured downward from one vessel to another — slightly soluble in water (the fizz of fizzy drinks), and it supports neither combustion nor ordinary respiration, which is why it smothers flames.
2. **Preparing it in the laboratory** — The standard laboratory preparation is marble chips (calcium carbonate) with dilute hydrochloric acid: CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂, the gas collected by downward delivery (upward displacement of air) because it is denser than air and slightly soluble — Greece's Grade 9 laboratory guide runs this preparation as its 9th exercise.
3. **The limewater test — and the gas at work** — Carbon dioxide's signature test is limewater: the gas turns clear limewater milky. Its uses spend its properties — fire extinguishers, fizzy drinks, dry ice — and in nature photosynthesis draws it down while respiration and burning return it, the balance that links the gas to the greenhouse effect.
