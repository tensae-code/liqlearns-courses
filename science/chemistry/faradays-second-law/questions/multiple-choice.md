# Faraday's second law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** State Faraday's second law.
a) The minimum energy for reaction on collision  b) Temperature  c) Toward products  d) Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses
**Answer: d) Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses**

**2. ** What charge do series cells share?
a) Open versus sealed storage (rancidity)  b) An empty orbital of the acid  c) The same quantity of electricity  d) They become single bonds
**Answer: c) The same quantity of electricity**

**3. ** Equivalent mass is molar mass ÷ …
a) Clay  b) The ion's charge number  c) Nitric acid, HNO₃  d) Care — dose, protection, water safety
**Answer: b) The ion's charge number**

**4. ** What are equivalent masses also called?
a) Chemical equivalents  b) The isotopes, weighted by abundance  c) An emission spectrum  d) Position and momentum
**Answer: a) Chemical equivalents**
