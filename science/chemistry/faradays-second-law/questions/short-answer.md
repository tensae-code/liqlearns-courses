# Faraday's second law — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** State Faraday's second law.
**Answer: Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses**

**2. ** What charge do series cells share?
**Answer: The same quantity of electricity**

**3. ** Equivalent mass is molar mass ÷ …
**Answer: The ion's charge number**

**4. ** What are equivalent masses also called?
**Answer: Chemical equivalents**
