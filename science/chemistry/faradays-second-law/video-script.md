# Video script — Faraday's second law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pass the same charge through two cells in series.

Faraday's second law: when the same quantity of electricity passes through different electrolytes, the masses deposited are proportional to the substances' equivalent masses (chemical equivalents).

Cells in series receive the same charge, so the law can be read directly off them.

Equivalent mass = molar mass ÷ the ion's charge number (the electrons per ion).

Now you. Say it back: State Faraday's second law.
Answer: Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses.
