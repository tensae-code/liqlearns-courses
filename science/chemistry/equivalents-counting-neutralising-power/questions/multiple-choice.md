# Equivalents — counting neutralising power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What do equivalents count?
a) Oxidation and reduction half-reactions  b) The anode  c) Aluminium  d) Neutralising power
**Answer: d) Neutralising power**

**2. ** Equivalent mass of an acid is molar mass ÷ …
a) Heavy metals (from industry/mining)  b) Fertilised fields (agriculture)  c) The number of replaceable H⁺  d) Photosynthesis
**Answer: c) The number of replaceable H⁺**

**3. ** Equal equivalents of acid and base do what?
a) Metal properties  b) Exactly neutralise each other  c) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ (variable)  d) Lone pairs
**Answer: b) Exactly neutralise each other**

**4. ** Why does H₂SO₄ count double per mole?
a) It gives two replaceable H⁺  b) Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics)  c) Mid-chain, between two carbons  d) Acetic acid (ethanoic acid)
**Answer: a) It gives two replaceable H⁺**
