# Equivalents — counting neutralising power — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What do equivalents count?
**Answer: Neutralising power**

**2. ** Equivalent mass of an acid is molar mass ÷ …
**Answer: The number of replaceable H⁺**

**3. ** Equal equivalents of acid and base do what?
**Answer: Exactly neutralise each other**

**4. ** Why does H₂SO₄ count double per mole?
**Answer: It gives two replaceable H⁺**
