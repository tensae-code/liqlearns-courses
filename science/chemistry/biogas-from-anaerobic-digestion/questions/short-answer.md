# Biogas — from anaerobic digestion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must the digester be sealed against air?
**Answer: The bacteria that do this digestion work without oxygen; open to air, different organisms and full breakdown to carbon dioxide would take over and no methane-rich gas would collect.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A farmer burns raw biogas in the kitchen but cannot sell it to the grid. What step is missing, and what does it remove?
**Answer: Upgrading — removing the carbon dioxide (and impurities) so the remaining biomethane meets the grid's standard.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In what two ways does one load of farm waste pay the farmer?
**Answer: Once as biogas (fuel for cooking, heating or generating, upgradable to biomethane), and again as digestate fertiliser returned to the fields.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three waste streams the book feeds the digester.
**Answer: Manure, crop residues and food waste.**
