# Biogas — from anaerobic digestion — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The sealed tank in which the waste breaks down is the anaerobic _____.
**Answer: digester**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The burnable alkane that makes up about 60% of biogas is _____.
**Answer: methane**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Cleaned-up biogas fed into the gas grid is called _____.
**Answer: biomethane**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The digested leftover returned to the fields is the _____.
**Answer: digestate**
