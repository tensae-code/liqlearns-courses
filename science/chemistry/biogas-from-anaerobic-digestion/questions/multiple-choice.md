# Biogas — from anaerobic digestion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Anaerobic means the digester is…
a) Heated strongly  b) Sealed without air  c) Open to the wind  d) Filled with oxygen
**Answer: b) Sealed without air**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The breakdown of the waste is done by…
a) Burning  b) Rusting  c) Bacteria  d) Sunlight
**Answer: c) Bacteria**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Biogas as collected is about…
a) 100% methane  b) 60% methane, 40% carbon dioxide  c) 40% methane, 60% carbon dioxide  d) 60% oxygen, 40% methane
**Answer: b) 60% methane, 40% carbon dioxide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Upgrading biogas produces…
a) Petrol  b) Biomethane for the grid or vehicle fuel  c) Coal gas  d) Hydrogen
**Answer: b) Biomethane for the grid or vehicle fuel**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The digestate left in the tank is used as…
a) Animal feed  b) Building sand  c) Fertiliser  d) Fuel for the digester fire
**Answer: c) Fertiliser**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Biogas is renewable because…
a) It is made from waste that regrows each season, in a cycle  b) The tank never empties  c) Methane cannot burn out  d) It needs no bacteria
**Answer: a) It is made from waste that regrows each season, in a cycle**
