# Biogas — from anaerobic digestion — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The anaerobic digester — sealed against air, The gas itself — 60% methane, and upgrading it, Digestate — the leftover that feeds the fields), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, tracing one load of farm waste through digester, upgrade and field in order, and setting biogas against natural gas point by point (source, mixture, renewability).
