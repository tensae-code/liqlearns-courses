Today: **Biogas** — Dominica's CSEC renewable alkane: waste in, methane out.

Seal manure, crop residues and food waste away from air, and bacteria digest them into biogas — about sixty percent methane, forty percent carbon dioxide. Burn it on the farm as it comes, or clean it up into biomethane for the gas grid and for vehicles. And when the gas is drawn off, what remains is not waste either: the digestate goes back to the fields as fertiliser. One tank, two harvests — fuel and soil — from a pile that was a problem.
