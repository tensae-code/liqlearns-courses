# Hands-on — Biogas — from anaerobic digestion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The waste audit (field exercise)
Walk your home or school kitchen and yard and list every waste stream that could feed a digester (manure, crop residues, food waste and their local equivalents). For each, write one line: what it is, roughly how much appears in a week, and what currently happens to it. End with the total a digester would have to hold.

## 60/40 by volume (kitchen demonstration — home-safe materials only)
Fill a 1-litre bottle with water. Mark the level that is 60% of it with a band, and pour water up to the band into a measuring jug: that volume stands for the methane in a litre of biogas. Pour the rest: the carbon dioxide's share. In two lines, say why the first share is the fuel and what upgrading does to the second.

## The loop drawn (paper exercise)
Draw the farm loop as a circle of labelled arrows: crops → food and residues → animals and manure → digester → biogas to the kitchen and digestate to the field → crops. Under the drawing, write where a fossil fuel's line would break the circle.
