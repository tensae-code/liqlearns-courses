# First transition series — elements and their uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Titanium's compound in sunscreen is…
a) TiO₂  b) V₂O₅  c) KMnO₄  d) ZnS
**Answer: a) TiO₂**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Cobalt-60 is used for…
a) Food preservation and tumour treatment  b) Galvanising iron  c) Dental implants  d) Car springs
**Answer: a) Food preservation and tumour treatment**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Fehling's solution is associated with which element's chemistry?
a) Copper  b) Zinc  c) Scandium  d) Vanadium
**Answer: a) Copper**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Nickel works in NiCd batteries and as a catalyst for…
a) Hydrogenation  b) Electrolysis of water  c) Photosynthesis  d) Nitration
**Answer: a) Hydrogenation**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Zinc protects iron by…
a) Galvanising  b) Alloying it into brass only  c) Painting it  d) Magnetising it
**Answer: a) Galvanising**
