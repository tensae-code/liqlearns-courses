# First transition series — elements and their uses — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give one use each for scandium, chromium and zinc, as Egypt teaches them.
**Answer:** Scandium — MiG jets and stadium lamps; chromium — plating (and dichromate as oxidant); zinc — galvanising iron and ZnS X-ray screens.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the tour give each element its own use-and-compound set?
**Answer:** The ten elements' work differs element by element — jets, antiseptics, batteries, screens — so the series is honestly learned as ten fitted sets, not one general claim about 'transition metals'.
