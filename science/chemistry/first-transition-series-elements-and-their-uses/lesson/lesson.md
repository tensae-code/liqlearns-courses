# First transition series — elements and their uses

## Do it first
Before you read: ten metals in a row of the table build jets, colour sunscreens, preserve food and plate car bumpers. Say out loud: could you give even three of them a job? Then read on: Egypt's Grade 12 book tours the first transition series element by element — scandium to zinc — with the crust share and the working compounds of each.

## Sub lesson 1 — Scandium to chromium — light metals, hard jobs

The tour opens with crust percentages, then one use-and-compound set per element. **Scandium** strengthens **MiG jets and stadium lamps**; **titanium** builds **aircraft and dental implants**, its **TiO₂** whitening sunscreen. **Vanadium** hardens **car-spring steel** and its **V₂O₅** catalyses industry; **chromium** gives **plating** its shine and, as **dichromate**, its oxidising bite.

## Sub lesson 2 — Manganese to nickel — rails, medicine, batteries

**Ferromanganese** steels the rails, and **KMnO₄** disinfects as an antiseptic. **Cobalt-60** preserves food and treats tumours with its gamma rays. **Nickel** stores charge in **NiCd batteries** and catalyses **hydrogenation** of oils — the series working in the rail yard, the clinic and the kitchen at once.

## Sub lesson 3 — Copper and zinc — the closers

**Copper** appears as **copper sulphate** and in **Fehling's solution**, the sugar test. **Zinc** closes the tour **galvanising** iron against rust and glowing as **ZnS on X-ray screens**. Ten elements, ten fitted sets of work — the chapter's argument that the first series is learned element by element, not as a blur.

## The key ideas

- **Sc** — MiG jets, stadium lamps; **Ti** — aircraft, dental implants, TiO₂ sunscreen; **V** — car-spring steel, V₂O₅ catalyst; **Cr** — plating, dichromate oxidant.
- **Mn** — ferromanganese rail steel, KMnO₄ antiseptic; **Co-60** — food preservation, tumour treatment; **Ni** — NiCd batteries, hydrogenation.
- **Cu** — copper sulphate, Fehling's solution; **Zn** — galvanising, ZnS X-ray screens.
