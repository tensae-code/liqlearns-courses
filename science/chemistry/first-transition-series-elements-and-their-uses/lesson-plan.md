# Lesson plan — First transition series — elements and their uses

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** First transition series — elements and their uses
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** First transition series — elements and their uses in Egypt's Grade 12 student book (Chapter One, printed pp. 5–7): Egypt's element-by-element tour of Sc→Zn: crust percentages, then one use-and-compound set each (scandium in MiG jets and stadium lamps, titanium in aircraft and dental implants with TiO₂ sunscreen, vanadium in car-spring steel and V₂O₅ catalyst, chromium plating and dichromate oxidant, ferromanganese rail steel and KMnO₄ antiseptic, cobalt-60 in food preservation and tumour treatment, nickel in NiCd batteries and hydrogenation, copper sulphate and Fehling's solution, zinc galvanising and ZnS X-ray screens).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. No other lesson is needed first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Scandium to chromium — light metals, hard jobs** — The tour opens with crust percentages, then one use-and-compound set per element. **Scandium** strengthens **MiG jets and stadium lamps**; **titanium** builds **aircraft and dental implants**, its **TiO₂** whitening sunscreen. **Vanadium** hardens **car-spring steel** and its **V₂O₅** catalyses industry; **chromium** gives **plating** its shine and, as **dichromate**, its oxidising bite.
2. **Manganese to nickel — rails, medicine, batteries** — **Ferromanganese** steels the rails, and **KMnO₄** disinfects as an antiseptic. **Cobalt-60** preserves food and treats tumours with its gamma rays. **Nickel** stores charge in **NiCd batteries** and catalyses **hydrogenation** of oils — the series working in the rail yard, the clinic and the kitchen at once.
3. **Copper and zinc — the closers** — **Copper** appears as **copper sulphate** and in **Fehling's solution**, the sugar test. **Zinc** closes the tour **galvanising** iron against rust and glowing as **ZnS on X-ray screens**. Ten elements, ten fitted sets of work — the chapter's argument that the first series is learned element by element, not as a blur.
