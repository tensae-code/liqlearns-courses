# Hands-on — First transition series — elements and their uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ten-element strip
Write Sc Ti V Cr Mn Fe Co Ni Cu Zn along a strip. Under each, write its one headline use from the lesson. Cover the uses and recall them along the strip, then cover the symbols and recall by use.

## Compound match
Write the compounds on slips — TiO₂, V₂O₅, KMnO₄, ZnS — and match each to its element and its job, saying each pairing aloud.
