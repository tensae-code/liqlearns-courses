# Ionisation energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) Ionisation energy is the energy an isolated gaseous atom in its ground state must absorb to discharge an…
b) Molecular mass = sum of atomic masses in a molecule (worked targets include SO₂ and caffeine);
c) A mole is the amount containing as many entities as there are atoms in exactly 12 g of carbon-12:…
d) the reducing agent is the substance oxidised — the agent is named for what it does to the other.
**Answer: a) Ionisation energy is the energy an isolated gaseous atom in its ground state must absorb to discharge an…**

**2. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) I–II (contents) and unit openings throughout.
b) Successive ionisations cost ever more (the ion is increasingly positive).
c) decimal places are distinguished from significant figures in reporting measured and calculated results.
d) isomers differ in physical properties (branched ones boil lower).
**Answer: b) Successive ionisations cost ever more (the ion is increasingly positive).**

**3. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
