## Covalent bonding

Where the electronegativity difference is too small for transfer, atoms share electron pairs: one shared pair = single bond, two pairs = double bond (oxygen molecule), three pairs = triple bond (nitrogen molecule); formation is walked through atom by atom for the first molecules, and pupils model single/double/triple molecules.
