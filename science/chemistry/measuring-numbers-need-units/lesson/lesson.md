## Measuring

Measurement is comparing a quantity with a fixed standard unit; the unit opens with pupils listing traditional ways of measuring mass, length, time and volume in their own marketplaces (goods exchanged by estimation, pictured in a traditional market) before any SI is introduced. A measured quantity is meaningless without its unit — the Addis Ababa–Hawassa road is 275 kilometres, not "275" — and common devices are named for each job: metre stick (length), burette, pipette, graduated cylinder and volumetric flask (volume), balance (mass), thermometer (temperature);

macroscopic properties are measured directly, microscopic ones indirectly.
