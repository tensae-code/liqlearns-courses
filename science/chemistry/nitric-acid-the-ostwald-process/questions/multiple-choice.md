# Nitric acid — the Ostwald process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What feedstock starts the Ostwald process?
a) Ammonia  b) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ (variable)  c) Lone pairs  d) Metal properties
**Answer: a) Ammonia**

**2. ** What is the first oxidation product?
a) Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics)  b) Mid-chain, between two carbons  c) Acetic acid (ethanoic acid)  d) Nitrogen monoxide, NO
**Answer: d) Nitrogen monoxide, NO**

**3. ** NO₂ absorbed in water gives…
a) Takes electrons (is itself reduced)  b) Yes — it is one of the three factors  c) Nitric acid, HNO₃  d) A pH range of about two units
**Answer: c) Nitric acid, HNO₃**

**4. ** What is recycled in absorption?
a) Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms  b) NO  c) The monomer units in the chain  d) The atmosphere
**Answer: b) NO**
