# Nitric acid — the Ostwald process — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What feedstock starts the Ostwald process?
**Answer: Ammonia**

**2. ** What is the first oxidation product?
**Answer: Nitrogen monoxide, NO**

**3. ** NO₂ absorbed in water gives…
**Answer: Nitric acid, HNO₃**

**4. ** What is recycled in absorption?
**Answer: NO**
