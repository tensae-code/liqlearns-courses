# Video script — Nitric acid — the Ostwald process

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yesterday's product is today's feedstock: ammonia is burned in air, in stages, to become an acid.

The Ostwald process oxidises ammonia in stages over a catalyst: first to nitrogen monoxide (NO), then NO is oxidised to nitrogen dioxide (NO₂).

NO₂ is absorbed in water to give nitric acid, HNO₃ (with NO recycled).

Nitric acid serves fertilisers, explosives and chemical manufacture as presented.

Now you. Say it back: What feedstock starts the Ostwald process?
Answer: Ammonia.
