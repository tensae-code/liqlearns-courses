# Lesson plan — Bohr's model and hydrogen energy levels

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Bohr's model and hydrogen energy levels
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Atomic Structure and Periodic Properties of the Elements (Grade 11 Chemistry).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bohr's model puts the electron in fixed allowed orbits — energy levels labelled n = 1, 2, 3 … up to ∞.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 11 Chemistry lessons before it in Unit 1 — Atomic Structure and Periodic Properties of the Elements — and on the learner's earlier chemistry. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
1. **Fixed levels — no falling in** — Bohr's model puts the electron in fixed allowed orbits — energy levels labelled n = 1, 2, 3 … up to ∞.
2. **Jumps emit and absorb photons** — An electron in an allowed level does not radiate energy away, so the atom is stable.
3. **Hydrogen's spectrum from the levels** — When an electron jumps from a higher level to a lower one, it emits a photon whose energy equals the energy difference.
4. **What the model hands on** — Absorbing a photon of exactly that energy lifts the electron to a higher level.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier Grade 11 Chemistry lessons, especially the lessons of Unit 1 before this one. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer, in their own words: what does n label in Bohr's model?
2. Answer, in their own words: what is emitted when an electron falls to a lower level?
3. Answer, in their own words: what equals the emitted photon's energy?
4. Answer, in their own words: why doesn't Bohr's electron spiral into the nucleus?
