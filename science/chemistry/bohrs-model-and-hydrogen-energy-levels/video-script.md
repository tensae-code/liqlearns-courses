# Video script — Bohr's model and hydrogen energy levels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

If electrons circled the nucleus like planets, physics says they would spiral in and the atom would collapse.

Bohr's model puts the electron in fixed allowed orbits — energy levels labelled n = 1, 2, 3 … up to ∞.

An electron in an allowed level does not radiate energy away, so the atom is stable.

When an electron jumps from a higher level to a lower one, it emits a photon whose energy equals the energy difference.

Absorbing a photon of exactly that energy lifts the electron to a higher level.

Now you. Say it back: What does n label in Bohr's model?
Answer: The allowed energy levels (orbits).
