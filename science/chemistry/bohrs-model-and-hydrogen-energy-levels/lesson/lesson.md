# Bohr's model and hydrogen energy levels

## Do it first
If electrons circled the nucleus like planets, physics says they would spiral in and the atom would collapse. Atoms do not collapse. Before reading, guess what rule Bohr imposed to stop the fall.

[Picture 21616 — Hydrogen levels n=1 to ∞, with jumps.]

## Sub lesson 1 — Fixed levels — no falling in
Bohr's model puts the electron in fixed allowed orbits — energy levels labelled n = 1, 2, 3 … up to ∞.

[GIF 21617 — electron jumping levels, photon leaving]

## Sub lesson 2 — Jumps emit and absorb photons
An electron in an allowed level does not radiate energy away, so the atom is stable.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hydrogen's spectrum from the levels
When an electron jumps from a higher level to a lower one, it emits a photon whose energy equals the energy difference.

## Sub lesson 4 — What the model hands on
Absorbing a photon of exactly that energy lifts the electron to a higher level. The model gives hydrogen's spectrum through energy calculations.
