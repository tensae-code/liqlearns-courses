# Bohr's model and hydrogen energy levels — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does n label in Bohr's model?
**Answer: The allowed energy levels (orbits)**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** What is emitted when an electron falls to a lower level?
**Answer: A photon**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** What equals the emitted photon's energy?
**Answer: The difference between the two levels**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Why doesn't Bohr's electron spiral into the nucleus?
**Answer: Allowed levels do not radiate energy**
