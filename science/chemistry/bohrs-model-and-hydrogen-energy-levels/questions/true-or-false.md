# Bohr's model and hydrogen energy levels — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does n label in Bohr's model — The allowed energy levels (orbits)
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** What is emitted when an electron falls to a lower level — The electrochemical (reactivity) series
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** What equals the emitted photon's energy — The difference between the two levels
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Why doesn't Bohr's electron spiral into the nucleus — Gamma rays
**Answer: False**
