# Bohr's model and hydrogen energy levels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does n label in Bohr's model?
a) They are rearranged, never created or destroyed  b) The allowed energy levels (orbits)  c) Solid wastes and agrochemicals  d) Prevention of waste
**Answer: b) The allowed energy levels (orbits)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is emitted when an electron falls to a lower level?
a) A photon  b) H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair  c) Layers slide over the flowing electron sea  d) Molecular crystals
**Answer: a) A photon**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** What equals the emitted photon's energy?
a) –al  b) Two  c) No C=C bonds  d) The difference between the two levels
**Answer: d) The difference between the two levels**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) ** Why doesn't Bohr's electron spiral into the nucleus?
a) The anode  b) Aluminium  c) Allowed levels do not radiate energy  d) Oxidation and reduction half-reactions
**Answer: c) Allowed levels do not radiate energy**
