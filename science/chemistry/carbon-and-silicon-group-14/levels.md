# Carbon and silicon — the group-14 pair — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (One group, two elements — four bonds apiece, Carbon — one element, several substances, Silicon — the stone element), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, reading a substance's properties off its drawn architecture, and matching each silicon use to the semiconductor property.
