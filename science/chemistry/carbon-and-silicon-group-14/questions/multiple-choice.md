# Carbon and silicon — the group-14 pair — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon and silicon are grouped together because both…
a) Are metals  b) Carry four electrons in the outer shell and are tetravalent  c) Are gases  d) Have the same mass
**Answer: b) Carry four electrons in the outer shell and are tetravalent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The group pattern shows in compounds as the shared formulas…
a) CO and SiO  b) CO₂ and SiO₂  c) C₂O and Si₂O  d) CH₂ and SiH₂
**Answer: b) CO₂ and SiO₂**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Diamond is the hardest natural substance and does not conduct because…
a) It is made of metal atoms  b) Every atom is locked by four strong bonds in a rigid network with no free electrons  c) It contains silicon  d) It is a liquid frozen hard
**Answer: b) Every atom is locked by four strong bonds in a rigid network with no free electrons**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Graphite marks paper and conducts electricity because…
a) It is wet  b) Its atoms sit in weakly held sliding layers, with a free electron moving along each layer  c) It is an allotrope of silicon  d) It burns easily
**Answer: b) Its atoms sit in weakly held sliding layers, with a free electron moving along each layer**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Silicon in nature is…
a) Found free in rivers  b) The crust's second most abundant element, but never found free  c) A gas in the air  d) Rarer than gold
**Answer: b) The crust's second most abundant element, but never found free**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The property of silicon the electronics industry stands on is that it is…
a) A superconductor  b) An insulator  c) A semiconductor  d) Magnetic
**Answer: c) A semiconductor**
