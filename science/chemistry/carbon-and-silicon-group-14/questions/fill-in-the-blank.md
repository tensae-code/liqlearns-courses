# Carbon and silicon — the group-14 pair — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon and silicon both have _____ electrons in their outer shell.
**Answer: four**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) One element existing in several different simple substances is called _____.
**Answer: allotropy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The pencil's marking core is the allotrope called _____.
**Answer: graphite**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Silicon's crustal abundance is second only to _____.
**Answer: oxygen**
