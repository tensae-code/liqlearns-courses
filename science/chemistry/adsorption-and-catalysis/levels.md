# Adsorption and catalysis — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Adsorption — gathering at the surface, Catalysis — promoters, poisons, and the two phases, Shape-selective catalysis — and the living catalysts), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, sorting new catalysis descriptions by phase and by the promoter/poison/selectivity tests, and reading a Freundlich plot's two constants from its straight line.
