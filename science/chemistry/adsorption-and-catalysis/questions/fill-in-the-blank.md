# Adsorption and catalysis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The substance gathered on a surface is the _____.
**Answer: adsorbate**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Adsorption held by van der Waals forces is called _____.
**Answer: physisorption**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A substance that destroys a catalyst's activity is a _____.
**Answer: poison**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The zeolite that turns alcohols into petrol is _____-5.
**Answer: ZSM**
