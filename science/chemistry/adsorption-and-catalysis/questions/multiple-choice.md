# Adsorption and catalysis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Adsorption differs from absorption in that the adsorbate…
a) Soaks through the whole bulk  b) Gathers at the surface only  c) Dissolves the solid  d) Reacts irreversibly in every case
**Answer: b) Gathers at the surface only**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Chemisorption is marked by…
a) Van der Waals forces and many layers  b) Chemical bonds, one layer, high specificity and irreversibility  c) Falling off when pressure rises  d) Occurring only in liquids
**Answer: b) Chemical bonds, one layer, high specificity and irreversibility**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Freundlich isotherm x/m = k·p^(1/n), x/m is…
a) The pressure at saturation  b) The mass of adsorbate adsorbed per gram of adsorbent  c) The temperature  d) The catalyst's activity
**Answer: b) The mass of adsorbate adsorbed per gram of adsorbent**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Molybdenum added to the iron of the Haber process is a…
a) Poison  b) Product  c) Promoter — it raises the catalyst's activity without being a catalyst itself  d) Reactant
**Answer: c) Promoter — it raises the catalyst's activity without being a catalyst itself**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The Contact process oxidises SO₂ over…
a) Nickel  b) V₂O₅ — a heterogeneous catalyst  c) Manganese dioxide solution  d) ZSM-5
**Answer: b) V₂O₅ — a heterogeneous catalyst**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) ZSM-5 converts alcohols into petrol-range hydrocarbons by…
a) Homogeneous catalysis  b) Shape-selective catalysis — its pores admit and release only molecules of the right size  c) Enzyme catalysis  d) Poisoning
**Answer: b) Shape-selective catalysis — its pores admit and release only molecules of the right size**
