# Brønsted–Lowry — protons handed over — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Define a Brønsted–Lowry acid.
a) Firing in a kiln  b) Mechanical or chemical  c) A proton donor  d) Ammonia
**Answer: c) A proton donor**

**2. ** How do conjugate pairs differ?
a) Boxes and arrows  b) By exactly one proton  c) A photon  d) A probability description
**Answer: b) By exactly one proton**

**3. ** Why is ammonia basic in this theory?
a) It accepts a proton from water  b) The minimum energy for reaction on collision  c) Temperature  d) Toward products
**Answer: a) It accepts a proton from water**

**4. ** What is an amphiprotic substance?
a) They become single bonds  b) Open versus sealed storage (rancidity)  c) An empty orbital of the acid  d) One that can donate or accept a proton
**Answer: d) One that can donate or accept a proton**
