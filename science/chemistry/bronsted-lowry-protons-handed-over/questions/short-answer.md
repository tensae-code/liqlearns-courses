# Brønsted–Lowry — protons handed over — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Define a Brønsted–Lowry acid.
**Answer: A proton donor**

**2. ** How do conjugate pairs differ?
**Answer: By exactly one proton**

**3. ** Why is ammonia basic in this theory?
**Answer: It accepts a proton from water**

**4. ** What is an amphiprotic substance?
**Answer: One that can donate or accept a proton**
