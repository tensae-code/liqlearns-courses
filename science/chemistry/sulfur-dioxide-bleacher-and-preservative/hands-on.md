# Hands-on — Sulfur dioxide — bleacher and preservative

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

(Paper work only — sulfur dioxide is a harmful gas and is never made or breathed in this lesson.)

## Tool or hazard — the sorting table
Draw a two-column table, TOOL and HAZARD. File each case: a bleached straw hat; a sulfur candle burned in a sealed grain store; smoke from a coal fire drifting over a town; dried fruit kept with SO₂; factory chimneys near a school; a coloured fabric that regained its colour in the air. For each one, write the sentence from the lesson that decides its column.

## The returning colour — a timeline
Sketch the life of one bleached material as a three-frame strip: coloured → bleached white → colour returning in air. Under each frame write what is happening and which substance is responsible. The third frame is the proof frame: say out loud what it proves about SO₂ bleaching that the second frame alone could not.
