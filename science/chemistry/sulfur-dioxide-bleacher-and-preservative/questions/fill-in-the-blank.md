# Sulfur dioxide — bleacher and preservative — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The gas formed when sulfur burns, used as a bleacher, is sulfur _____.
**Answer: dioxide**

**2.** Most materials bleached by SO₂ regain their _____ on exposure to air.
**Answer: colours**

**3.** Burning sulfur inside a space _____ it.
**Answer: sterilises**

**4.** SO₂ poured into the air from coal burning and industry is a serious _____ hazard.
**Answer: health**
