# Sulfur dioxide — bleacher and preservative — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Sulfur dioxide has the formula…
a) SO  b) SO₂  c) SO₃  d) S₂O
**Answer: b) SO₂**

**2.** Materials bleached by SO₂, left in the air, usually…
a) stay white forever  b) regain their colours  c) catch fire  d) turn to sulfur
**Answer: b) regain their colours**

**3.** Burning sulfur inside a closed space…
a) colours it  b) sterilises it  c) cools it  d) feeds it
**Answer: b) sterilises it**

**4.** Sulfur dioxide is also used to…
a) preserve foods  b) make glass  c) raise bread  d) soften water
**Answer: a) preserve foods**

**5.** Sulfur ignites spontaneously at about…
a) 40 °C  b) 100 °C  c) 400 °C  d) 1000 °C
**Answer: c) 400 °C**

**6.** SO₂ in town air from coal burning and industry is…
a) a preservative  b) a steriliser  c) a serious health hazard  d) harmless
**Answer: c) a serious health hazard**
