Today: **Sulfur dioxide — bleacher and preservative** — Iraq's way: one gas, three faces.
Burn sulfur and you get sulfur dioxide, SO₂. It bleaches — but leave the bleached cloth in the air and the colour comes back: air undoes its work. Burn sulfur inside a closed room and the gas sterilises the room; it preserves food the same way, by being hostile to what rots it. But pour it into the open air from coal and factories and it turns hazard — a gas that harms health. Tool in its place, danger out of it.
