# Hands-on — Catalysts and light — in rate and equilibrium

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-hills sketch
Sketch the energy profile of a reversible reaction with and without a catalyst: both hills lowered equally for the catalysed path. Mark the equilibrium mixture's composition on the sketch and write why it did not change.

## Factor cards
Make cards for the chapter's rate factors as this lesson leaves them — catalyst (both ways equally), enzyme, converter, light (two examples). Sort them into 'changes position' and 'changes only speed' — all of them land in one pile; say why.
