# Catalysts and light — in rate and equilibrium — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** A catalyst speeds both directions ______, so the equilibrium position does not move.
**Answer:** equally

**2.** ______ are the biological catalysts named in the chapter.
**Answer:** Enzymes

**3.** On photographic film, light turns Ag⁺ into metallic ______.
**Answer:** silver (Ag)
