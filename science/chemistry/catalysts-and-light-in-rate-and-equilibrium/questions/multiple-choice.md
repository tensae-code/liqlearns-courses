# Catalysts and light — in rate and equilibrium — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Adding a catalyst to an equilibrium mixture…
a) Reaches equilibrium faster without moving its position  b) Shifts equilibrium toward products  c) Shifts equilibrium toward reactants  d) Stops the back reaction
**Answer: a) Reaches equilibrium faster without moving its position**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why doesn't the position move?
a) The catalyst speeds the forward and back reactions equally  b) The catalyst is used up  c) Equilibrium ignores all factors  d) The catalyst raises the temperature
**Answer: a) The catalyst speeds the forward and back reactions equally**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Enzymes are…
a) Biological catalysts  b) A kind of light  c) Inorganic salts  d) Equilibrium constants
**Answer: a) Biological catalysts**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In photographic film, light drives…
a) Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag  b) Ag → Ag⁺ + e⁻  c) AgBr dissolving in water  d) Silver evaporating
**Answer: a) Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Which is Egypt's example of light as a rate factor in living things?
a) Photosynthesis  b) Respiration at night  c) Digestion  d) Fermentation
**Answer: a) Photosynthesis**
