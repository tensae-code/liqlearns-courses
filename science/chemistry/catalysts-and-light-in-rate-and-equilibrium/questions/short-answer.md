# Catalysts and light — in rate and equilibrium — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A classmate says a catalyst must increase an equilibrium's yield. Correct them in two sentences.
**Answer:** A catalyst lowers the activation energy of both the forward and the back reaction, so it speeds both equally. The mixture reaches the same equilibrium position — the same yield — only sooner.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the book's two light-driven examples and what light does in each.
**Answer:** Photosynthesis — light drives the plant's food-making chemistry; silver bromide film — light reduces Ag⁺ to metallic silver (Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag), forming the photograph's image.
