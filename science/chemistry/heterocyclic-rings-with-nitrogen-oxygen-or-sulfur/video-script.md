Today: **Heterocyclic rings — with nitrogen, oxygen or sulfur** — Iraq's way: rings with a guest atom.
A ring is heterocyclic when one of its members is not carbon. Pyridine: a six-membered nitrogen ring, part of nicotine. Pyrrole: a five-membered nitrogen ring, a building unit of haemoglobin and chlorophyll. Furan carries oxygen, thiophene sulfur — and both are resonance hybrids. Penicillin and many antibiotics are built on rings like these.
