# Heterocyclic rings — with nitrogen, oxygen or sulfur — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Rings holding atoms besides carbon are called _____ compounds.
**Answer: heterocyclic**

**2.** The six-membered nitrogen ring found in nicotine is _____.
**Answer: pyridine**

**3.** The five-membered nitrogen ring that builds haemoglobin and chlorophyll is _____.
**Answer: pyrrole**

**4.** The five-membered sulfur ring named beside furan is _____.
**Answer: thiophene**
