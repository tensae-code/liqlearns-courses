# Heterocyclic rings — with nitrogen, oxygen or sulfur — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A heterocyclic ring is a ring that holds…
a) only carbon  b) atoms besides carbon as ring members  c) a metal centre  d) no bonds
**Answer: b) atoms besides carbon as ring members**

**2.** Pyridine is a six-membered ring whose guest atom is…
a) oxygen  b) sulfur  c) nitrogen  d) chlorine
**Answer: c) nitrogen**

**3.** Pyridine is part of…
a) haemoglobin  b) nicotine  c) chlorophyll only  d) penicillin only
**Answer: b) nicotine**

**4.** Pyrrole, a building unit of haemoglobin and chlorophyll, is a…
a) six-membered oxygen ring  b) five-membered nitrogen ring  c) five-membered sulfur ring  d) chain, not a ring
**Answer: b) five-membered nitrogen ring**

**5.** Furan's guest atom is…
a) nitrogen  b) sulfur  c) oxygen  d) phosphorus
**Answer: c) oxygen**

**6.** Furan and thiophene are both…
a) open chains  b) resonance hybrids  c) salts  d) metals
**Answer: b) resonance hybrids**
