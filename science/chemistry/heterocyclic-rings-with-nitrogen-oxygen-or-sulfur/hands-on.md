# Hands-on — Heterocyclic rings — with nitrogen, oxygen or sulfur

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ring census — carbon or guest?
Draw the four rings of the lesson as hexagons and pentagons of circles: pyridine, pyrrole, furan, thiophene. Colour every carbon one colour and every guest atom another, and label each ring with its size and guest. Then answer out loud for each: could this ring be mistaken for benzene — and what single look settles it?

## Where the rings work — the match cards
Write four job cards: nicotine; haemoglobin and chlorophyll; penicillin and the antibiotics; a resonance hybrid boiling at 31 °C. Match each card to its ring (pyridine; pyrrole; heterocyclic systems in general; furan) and say the matching fact from the lesson for each pair.
