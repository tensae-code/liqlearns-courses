# Cleaner production — Unit 5 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does cleaner production prevent?
a) Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance  b) Waste, at the source  c) Inward pull on surface particles  d) The rate constant
**Answer: b) Waste, at the source**

**2. ** What is end-of-pipe treatment?
a) Treating pollution after it is made  b) Yes — it reaches equilibrium  c) The melting point  d) With a group Start-up Activity
**Answer: a) Treating pollution after it is made**

**3. ** Name a cleaner-production method.
a) Manufacturing processes, incl. Ethiopia's  b) Ammonia  c) Pests  d) Redesign, reuse or efficiency in the plant
**Answer: d) Redesign, reuse or efficiency in the plant**

**4. ** What closes Grade 12 chemistry?
a) The number of neutrons  b) A continuous spectrum  c) This unit's summary, key terms and review  d) Negative
**Answer: c) This unit's summary, key terms and review**
