# Cleaner production — Unit 5 review

## Do it first
A factory can scrub its chimney — or stop making the smoke. Before the review, say which of those is cleaner production, and why the other is called 'end-of-pipe'.

[Picture 21786 — A factory before/after cleaner production.]

Cleaner production prevents waste at the source: redesigning processes, reusing materials and improving efficiency inside the plant.

It is distinguished from end-of-pipe treatment, which deals with pollution only after it is made.

Unit 5 — and Grade 12 chemistry — closes with summary, key terms and review.
