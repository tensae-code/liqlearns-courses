# Video script — Cleaner production — Unit 5 review

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A factory can scrub its chimney — or stop making the smoke.

Cleaner production prevents waste at the source: redesigning processes, reusing materials and improving efficiency inside the plant.

It is distinguished from end-of-pipe treatment, which deals with pollution only after it is made.

Unit 5 — and Grade 12 chemistry — closes with summary, key terms and review.

Now you. Say it back: What does cleaner production prevent?
Answer: Waste, at the source.
