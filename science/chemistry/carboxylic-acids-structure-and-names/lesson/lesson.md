# Carboxylic acids — structure and names

## Do it first
Vinegar's bite is one small molecule with a group that is half carbonyl, half hydroxyl. Before reading, draw that group from its name: carboxyl.

[Picture 21694 — Ethanoic acid with its carboxyl group.]

The carboxyl group –COOH joins a carbonyl C=O and a hydroxyl –OH on one carbon.

IUPAC names end in –oic acid, counting the carboxyl carbon as carbon 1.

Common names of the first members — formic, acetic and their kin — are given and used in the section.
