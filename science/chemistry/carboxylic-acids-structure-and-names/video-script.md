# Video script — Carboxylic acids — structure and names

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vinegar's bite is one small molecule with a group that is half carbonyl, half hydroxyl.

The carboxyl group –COOH joins a carbonyl C=O and a hydroxyl –OH on one carbon.

IUPAC names end in –oic acid, counting the carboxyl carbon as carbon 1.

Common names of the first members — formic, acetic and their kin — are given and used in the section.

Now you. Say it back: Write the carboxyl group.
Answer: –COOH.
