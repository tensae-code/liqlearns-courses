# Carboxylic acids — structure and names — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Write the carboxyl group.
**Answer: –COOH**

**2. ** What ending do acid names take?
**Answer: –oic acid**

**3. ** What two parts form the carboxyl group?
**Answer: Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH**

**4. ** Give vinegar's acid its common name.
**Answer: Acetic acid (ethanoic acid)**
