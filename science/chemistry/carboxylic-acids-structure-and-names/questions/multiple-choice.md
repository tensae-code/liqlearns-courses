# Carboxylic acids — structure and names — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Write the carboxyl group.
a) The longest chain carrying –OH  b) –COOH  c) How far the equilibrium lies toward products  d) Unreacted gases
**Answer: b) –COOH**

**2. ** What ending do acid names take?
a) –oic acid  b) H⁺ ions  c) 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴  d) Henderson–Hasselbalch
**Answer: a) –oic acid**

**3. ** What two parts form the carboxyl group?
a) Ammonia  b) Firing in a kiln  c) Mechanical or chemical  d) Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH
**Answer: d) Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH**

**4. ** Give vinegar's acid its common name.
a) A probability description  b) Boxes and arrows  c) Acetic acid (ethanoic acid)  d) A photon
**Answer: c) Acetic acid (ethanoic acid)**
