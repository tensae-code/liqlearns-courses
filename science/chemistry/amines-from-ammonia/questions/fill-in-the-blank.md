# Amines — from ammonia — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Amines are built from _____.
**Answer: ammonia**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An amine with three alkyl groups on nitrogen is _____.
**Answer: tertiary**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The book's worked case is _____amine, with three methyl groups.
**Answer: trimethyl**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The central atom of the amine family is _____.
**Answer: nitrogen**
