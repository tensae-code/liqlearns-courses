# Amines — from ammonia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Amines are built from…
a) Methane  b) Ammonia  c) Water  d) An alcohol
**Answer: b) Ammonia**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A secondary amine's nitrogen carries…
a) One alkyl group  b) Two alkyl groups  c) Three alkyl groups  d) No alkyl groups
**Answer: b) Two alkyl groups**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Trimethylamine is…
a) Primary  b) Secondary  c) Tertiary  d) Not an amine
**Answer: c) Tertiary**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The atom at the centre of every amine is…
a) Carbon  b) Oxygen  c) Nitrogen  d) Sulfur
**Answer: c) Nitrogen**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Amine names are read off…
a) The longest chain only  b) The substituents on nitrogen  c) The boiling point  d) The acid parent
**Answer: b) The substituents on nitrogen**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Replacing ammonia's hydrogens one, two, three at a time gives…
a) Three unrelated families  b) Primary, secondary, tertiary amines  c) Amides  d) Nitriles
**Answer: b) Primary, secondary, tertiary amines**
