# Amines — from ammonia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How is an amine built, in the book's construction?
**Answer: From ammonia, by replacing one, two or three of its hydrogens with alkyl groups.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What decides an amine's class?
**Answer: How many alkyl groups its nitrogen carries: one — primary, two — secondary, three — tertiary.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the book's worked amine and justify its class.
**Answer: Trimethylamine — three methyl substituents on nitrogen, so tertiary.**
