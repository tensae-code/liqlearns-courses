# Hands-on — Amines — from ammonia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Climb the ladder (paper only)
Draw ammonia, then its three replacements in a row, circling the group swapped at each step. Under each, write its class and its name pattern, ending with trimethylamine as the book works it.

## Sort the nitrogens (paper only)
You are given five nitrogen structures in words (one, two, three, one, two alkyl groups). Sort them into the three classes in a table, and for the three-group one, write its full name from its substituents.
