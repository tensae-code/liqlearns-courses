## Drawing organic molecules; functional groups

Organic structures are represented at several levels (full structural formulas showing every bond, condensed formulas, and line representations), and compounds are classified by their functional group — the part of the molecule that determines its chemical and physical properties. Hydrocarbons (carbon + hydrogen only) are the parent family: saturated (single bonds only) vs unsaturated (double/triple bonds present).
