## Aluminium

Aluminium, the most abundant metal in the crust, is extracted by electrolysis of molten aluminium oxide mixed with cryolite (which lowers the working temperature) — the Hall (Hall–Héroult) process. It resists corrosion because a thin oxide film seals its surface, and its light alloys serve aircraft and similar construction.
