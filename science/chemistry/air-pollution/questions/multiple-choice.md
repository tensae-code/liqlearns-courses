# Air pollution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which gases cause acid rain?
a) Weeds  b) Silica — the glass former  c) Molasses  d) Sulfur and nitrogen oxides (SO₂/NOₓ)
**Answer: d) Sulfur and nitrogen oxides (SO₂/NOₓ)**

**2. ** Name a vehicle pollutant.
a) Electron diffraction  b) A dumbbell (two lobes)  c) Carbon monoxide (or particulates/hydrocarbons)  d) Only certain sharp lines
**Answer: c) Carbon monoxide (or particulates/hydrocarbons)**

**3. ** What hangs over polluted cities?
a) Initial, Change, Equilibrium  b) Smog  c) Change in amount/concentration per unit time  d) No — it is dynamic, both ways continue
**Answer: b) Smog**

**4. ** Air pollution harms what first?
a) Lungs and health  b) Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly  c) PV = nRT  d) It accepts a proton from water
**Answer: a) Lungs and health**
