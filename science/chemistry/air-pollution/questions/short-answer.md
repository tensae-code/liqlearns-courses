# Air pollution — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which gases cause acid rain?
**Answer: Sulfur and nitrogen oxides (SO₂/NOₓ)**

**2. ** Name a vehicle pollutant.
**Answer: Carbon monoxide (or particulates/hydrocarbons)**

**3. ** What hangs over polluted cities?
**Answer: Smog**

**4. ** Air pollution harms what first?
**Answer: Lungs and health**
