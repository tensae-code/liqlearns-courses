# Video script — Air pollution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

City air carries invisible cargo from exhausts, factories and fires.

Major air pollutants include carbon monoxide, sulfur and nitrogen oxides, particulates and unburned hydrocarbons, from vehicles, industry and burning as presented.

Their effects: acid rain from SO₂/NOₓ dissolving in rain, smog over cities, and direct harm to lungs and health.

The lesson ties each pollutant to its sources and effects.

Now you. Say it back: Which gases cause acid rain?
Answer: Sulfur and nitrogen oxides (SO₂/NOₓ).
