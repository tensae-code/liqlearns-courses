# Why the periodic table works — Unit 1 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does classification make study?
a) Anode to cathode  b) Electrochemical  c) Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets  d) Systematic
**Answer: d) Systematic**

**2. ** How were gaps in the early table used?
a) Subatomic particles  b) Beryllium  c) To predict new elements' properties  d) Hazardous substances
**Answer: c) To predict new elements' properties**

**3. ** What does an element's position let you do?
a) Kelvin temperature  b) Predict its properties  c) It dents instead of shattering  d) Particles in motion
**Answer: b) Predict its properties**

**4. ** What closes Unit 1?
a) The summary, key terms and review exercise  b) It falls  c) Ethanoic (acetic) acid  d) Pleasant (fruity)
**Answer: a) The summary, key terms and review exercise**
