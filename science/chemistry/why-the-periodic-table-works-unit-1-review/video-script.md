# Video script — Why the periodic table works — Unit 1 review

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before the review, test yourself: from its place alone, what could you predict about an element you have never handled — its size, its greed for electrons, whether it is a metal? If you can answer, the table has done its work.

Classification makes study systematic: learn one group member well and you know the family's pattern.

Properties of undiscovered or unmeasured elements can be predicted from their position — gaps in the early table were filled this way.

Unit 1's chain runs: atom → subatomic particles → quantum numbers → configurations → the table → its trends.

Now you. Say it back: What does classification make study?
Answer: Systematic.
