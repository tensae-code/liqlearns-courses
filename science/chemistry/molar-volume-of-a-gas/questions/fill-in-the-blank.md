# Molar volume of a gas — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) One mole of any gas at fixed temperature and pressure occupies the same _____.
**Answer: volume**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At 0 °C and 1 atm, Vm = _____ L/mol.
**Answer: 22.4**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The amount of a gas is n = V / _____.
**Answer: Vm**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The shared-volume law is the law of Avogadro–_____.
**Answer: Ampère**
