# Molar volume of a gas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the Avogadro–Ampère law as the 5AS book states it.
**Answer: At the same temperature and pressure, one mole of any gas occupies the same volume (the molar volume).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do the books always name the conditions beside Vm?
**Answer: The molar volume is only fixed at stated conditions — 22.4 L/mol at 0 °C and 1 atm; at other conditions the value differs (24 L/mol in the 5AS worked example).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the three counts of amount n that the Lettres book links.
**Answer: n = N/NA from particles, n = m/M from mass, and n = V/Vm from gas volume.**
