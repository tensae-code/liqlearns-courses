# Molar volume of a gas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At the same temperature and pressure, one mole of any gas occupies…
a) Different volumes by mass  b) The same volume  c) 22.4 L always, at any conditions  d) A volume set by its formula
**Answer: b) The same volume**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The molar volume at 0 °C and 1 atm, as the Lettres manuals give it, is…
a) 24 L/mol  b) 1 L/mol  c) 22.4 L/mol  d) 6.02×10²³ L/mol
**Answer: c) 22.4 L/mol**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The amount in 11.2 L of gas at 0 °C and 1 atm is…
a) 2 mol  b) 0.5 mol  c) 1 mol  d) 22.4 mol
**Answer: b) 0.5 mol**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The law behind the shared volume is named for…
a) Lavoisier only  b) Boyle only  c) Dalton  d) Avogadro–Ampère
**Answer: d) Avogadro–Ampère**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The science-track book's butane-bottle example works with Vm =…
a) 24 L/mol  b) 22.4 L/mol  c) 1 L/mol  d) 44.8 L/mol
**Answer: a) 24 L/mol**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) n = V/Vm gives the amount from…
a) A mass  b) A particle count  c) A gas volume  d) A concentration
**Answer: c) A gas volume**
