# Lesson plan — Molar volume of a gas

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Molar volume of a gas
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** at the same temperature and pressure one mole of any gas occupies the same volume — the molar volume Vm: 22.4 L/mol at 0 °C and 1 atm in the Lettres manuals, 24 L/mol in the worked butane-bottle example of the science-track Chimie 5AS — and the amount of a gas is read from its volume as n = V/Vm (Mauritania, Grades 11 — *Chimie 5ème AS*, Ch. 1, printed p. 12; *Sciences Physiques 5AS — Lettres Modernes*, Part IV Ch. 2, printed pp. 101–109; *Sciences Physiques 5AS — Lettres Originelles* (Arabic), Ch. 2, printed pp. 51–55)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. The mole — counting by weighing is recommended first, never forced.
