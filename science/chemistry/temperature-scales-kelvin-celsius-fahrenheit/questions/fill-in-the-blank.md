# Temperature scales — kelvin, Celsius, Fahrenheit — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** heat is energy that flows spontaneously from hotter to colder, never the _____.
**Answer: reverse**

**2. ** The Celsius and Kelvin degree are the same size, offset by 273.15 (K = °C + 273.15), so a _____ *change* is numerically equal on both scales;
**Answer: temperature**

**3. ** the Fahrenheit degree is smaller (180 °F spans the same interval as 100 °C between water's freezing and _____ points) and its zero is shifted, giving the two conversion formulas with the −32 adjustment.
**Answer: boiling**

**4. ** Worked example _____ 100 °F to about 38 °C.
**Answer: converts**
