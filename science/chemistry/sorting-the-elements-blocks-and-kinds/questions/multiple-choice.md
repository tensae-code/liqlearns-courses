# Sorting the elements — blocks and kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Where are the non-metals found?
a) Henderson–Hasselbalch  b) On the right of the table  c) H⁺ ions  d) 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴
**Answer: b) On the right of the table**

**2. ** What sits on the metal/non-metal border?
a) Metalloids  b) Ammonia  c) Firing in a kiln  d) Mechanical or chemical
**Answer: a) Metalloids**

**3. ** Name the four blocks.
a) A photon  b) A probability description  c) Boxes and arrows  d) s, p, d and f
**Answer: d) s, p, d and f**

**4. ** What decides an element's block?
a) Temperature  b) Toward products  c) Which subshell is filling  d) The minimum energy for reaction on collision
**Answer: c) Which subshell is filling**
