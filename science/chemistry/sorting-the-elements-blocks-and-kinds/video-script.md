# Video script — Sorting the elements — blocks and kinds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The table mixes shiny bendable metals, gases that form no compounds easily, and odd borderline cases.

Elements sort into metals (left and centre), non-metals (right) and metalloids along the border between them.

Representative elements, transition elements and inner-transition elements are the table's great families.

By which subshell is filling, the table divides into s, p, d and f blocks — the block follows straight from the electron configuration.

Now you. Say it back: Where are the non-metals found?
Answer: On the right of the table.
