# Hydrides — ionic, covalent and metallic — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A compound of hydrogen with another element is a _____.
**Answer: hydride**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In an ionic hydride, hydrogen carries the charge _____ (sign and number).
**Answer: −1 (H⁻)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ammonia and methane are _____ hydrides.
**Answer: covalent**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Hydrogen held in the gaps of a metal lattice makes a _____ hydride.
**Answer: metallic**
