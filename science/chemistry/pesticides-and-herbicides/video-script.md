# Video script — Pesticides and herbicides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A sprayed field yields more — and the spray does not read labels about which insects are welcome.

Pesticides kill pests; herbicides kill weeds — classes of farm chemicals grouped by their target and chemistry, as presented.

They raise agricultural yields by protecting crops.

The section frames their hazards and care: correct dose, protective handling and respect for water and non-target life.

Now you. Say it back: What do herbicides kill?
Answer: Weeds.
