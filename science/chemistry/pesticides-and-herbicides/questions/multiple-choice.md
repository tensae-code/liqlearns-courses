# Pesticides and herbicides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What do herbicides kill?
a) Carboxylic acids  b) Weeds  c) R–O–R  d) Strong acids (HBr/HI)
**Answer: b) Weeds**

**2. ** What do pesticides kill?
a) Pests  b) Comparing Ka and Kb  c) Above 7  d) Anions are oxidised
**Answer: a) Pests**

**3. ** Why are they used?
a) Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet  b) Rubber, cellulose or protein  c) The pollution lessons  d) To protect crops and raise yields
**Answer: d) To protect crops and raise yields**

**4. ** What must travel with their use?
a) It rises from 1 to 8  b) Its ions are locked in place  c) Care — dose, protection, water safety  d) To predict new elements' properties
**Answer: c) Care — dose, protection, water safety**
