# Pesticides and herbicides — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What do herbicides kill?
**Answer: Weeds**

**2. ** What do pesticides kill?
**Answer: Pests**

**3. ** Why are they used?
**Answer: To protect crops and raise yields**

**4. ** What must travel with their use?
**Answer: Care — dose, protection, water safety**
