# Sodium hydroxide — chlor-alkali — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name the three chlor-alkali products.
a) Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry  b) H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)  c) A weak acid's ionisation strength  d) Sodium hydroxide, chlorine, hydrogen
**Answer: d) Sodium hydroxide, chlorine, hydrogen**

**2. ** What cell type do modern plants use?
a) Sodium hydroxide, chlorine, hydrogen  b) Clinker  c) Membrane cells  d) Harvests remove it from the soil
**Answer: c) Membrane cells**

**3. ** What feedstock is electrolysed?
a) +½ or −½  b) Brine  c) Gamma rays  d) One-electron species only
**Answer: b) Brine**

**4. ** Name a NaOH use.
a) Soap or paper making  b) It breaks particle arrangements  c) ⇌ (double arrow)  d) Pure solids and liquids
**Answer: a) Soap or paper making**
