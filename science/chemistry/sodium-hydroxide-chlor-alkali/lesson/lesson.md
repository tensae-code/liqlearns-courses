# Sodium hydroxide — chlor-alkali

## Do it first
Brine electrolysis from Grade 12's Unit 2 returns as an industry. Before reading, recite its three products and pick the one this lesson is named for.

[Picture 21758 — A membrane-cell diagram.]

Chlor-alkali manufacture electrolyses brine to give sodium hydroxide, chlorine and hydrogen — the three co-products.

Modern plants use membrane cells to keep the products apart and pure.

Sodium hydroxide serves soap, paper and chemical industries.

## Say it to your companion
Explain Sodium hydroxide — chlor-alkali to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.
