Unit 1 Redox Reactions: oxidation and reduction first as gain or loss of oxygen (oxidation also as loss of hydrogen), then redox as simultaneous oxidation and reduction — the unit opener already uses the electron picture for rusting (iron oxidised to Fe(III) ions by losing electrons, oxygen reduced by gaining them). Unit 3 Energy from Chemicals: chemical energy to heat with exothermic/endothermic changes, making and breaking bonds, exothermic reactions giving out heat, endothermic reactions, dissolving and energy changes, heat of neutralisation investigated experimentally, and saving energy. Unit 4 Rate of Reaction and Dynamic Equilibrium: fast and slow reactions as rate of chemical change, measuring rate by visible changes, factors affecting the speed of chemical reaction (with an experiment to investigate them), catalysts and enzymes. Unit 5 Acids and Bases: acids' properties and reactions, "acidity needs water", strong and weak acids as fully or partially ionised, indicators showing acidity or alkalinity, the pH scale as the strength of an acid or alkali, changing pH, bases' properties and reactions, neutralisation as the reaction of hydrogen ions and hydroxide ions, and types of oxides — basic, acidic, neutral or amphoteric. Unit 6 Salts: salts as the replacement of acids' hydrogen, solubility of salts (which dissolve), preparing soluble salts, and preparing insoluble salts by ionic precipitation (AgCl, PbCl₂, and the sulfates CaSO₄/BaSO₄/PbSO₄ appear in the solubility work).
