# Video script — Sodium hydroxide — chlor-alkali

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Brine electrolysis from Grade 12's Unit 2 returns as an industry.

Chlor-alkali manufacture electrolyses brine to give sodium hydroxide, chlorine and hydrogen — the three co-products.

Modern plants use membrane cells to keep the products apart and pure.

Sodium hydroxide serves soap, paper and chemical industries.

Now you. Say it back: Name the three chlor-alkali products.
Answer: Sodium hydroxide, chlorine, hydrogen.
