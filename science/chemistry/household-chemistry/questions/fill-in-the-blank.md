# Household chemistry — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The household chemical that makes water able to carry grease away is _____.
**Answer: soap**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A manufactured cleaning agent designed for a set washing job is a _____.
**Answer: detergent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The household chemical that attacks colour itself and kills germs is a _____.
**Answer: bleach**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Household cleaners must never be _____ together.
**Answer: mixed**
