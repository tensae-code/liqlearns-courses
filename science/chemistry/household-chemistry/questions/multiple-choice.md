# Household chemistry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Soap's cleaning job is to…
a) Colour the water  b) Make water able to carry grease away  c) Soften cloth fibres permanently  d) Kill every germ instantly
**Answer: b) Make water able to carry grease away**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fiji's Year 12 book teaches household chemistry as…
a) A named closing strand, Strand 5.3  b) A footnote in the glossary  c) Part of the periodic table  d) An exam trick
**Answer: a) A named closing strand, Strand 5.3**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A detergent, in lesson terms, is…
a) Any coloured liquid  b) A manufactured cleaning agent designed for a set washing job  c) Melted soap  d) Pure water with bubbles
**Answer: b) A manufactured cleaning agent designed for a set washing job**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Detergents keep working where soap struggles, for example in…
a) The dark  b) Hard water, where soap loses power and leaves scum  c) Boiling oil  d) Frozen water
**Answer: b) Hard water, where soap loses power and leaves scum**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A bleach's special job is to…
a) Add colour  b) Attack colour itself, whitening and killing germs  c) Make water softer  d) Polish wood
**Answer: b) Attack colour itself, whitening and killing germs**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The household safety rule for cleaners is…
a) Mix two for double strength  b) Use bleach with everything  c) Use one at a time as the label directs, and never mix them  d) Labels are decoration
**Answer: c) Use one at a time as the label directs, and never mix them**
