Today: **Household chemistry** — the strand Fiji's Year 12 book closes with.

Under the sink is a chemistry set. Soap makes water carry grease away. Detergents are manufactured cleaners that keep working where soap gives up. Bleaches attack colour itself and kill germs. Each bottle is a chemical made for one job — so read the label, use one at a time, and never mix them.
