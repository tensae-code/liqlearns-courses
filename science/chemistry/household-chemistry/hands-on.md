# Hands-on — Household chemistry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The label hunt (observation only — home, no chemicals touched)
With an adult, list the cleaning products in your home **without opening or mixing any of them**. For each, copy from its label: its kind (soap, detergent, bleach or other cleaner), the job it is sold for, and one safety direction printed on it. Sort your list into Fiji's four household kinds.

## Soap versus detergent (home materials — washing-up, with an adult)
Wash two equally greasy plates or cups, one with soap and one with a washing detergent, in the same amount of warm water. Note what each does to the grease and what the water looks like after. Write the difference in lesson terms: both are cleaners; say what you observed about how each one worked.

## One product, one job (paper exercise)
Take six cleaning jobs around a home — hands, dishes, laundry, white cloth, stove top, bathroom surfaces. For each, write which of Fiji's four kinds you would reach for and why, and finish with the safety rule in your own words: one at a time, as the label directs, never mixed.
