# Hands-on — Nuclear binding energy and mass defect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work the shortcut
Multiply 0.0305 × 931 on paper and confirm Egypt's 28.3955 MeV. Then divide by helium's 4 nucleons to get its binding energy per nucleon, and say what that number is for.

## Two nuclei compared
Write the three steps — defect, ×931, ÷ nucleons — as a recipe card. Under it, note that Egypt works the recipe twice: helium, then oxygen-16. Say why a recipe, not a single worked answer, is the point.
