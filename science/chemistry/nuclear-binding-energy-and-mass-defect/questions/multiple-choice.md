# Nuclear binding energy and mass defect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The mass defect of a nucleus is…
a) Its mass shortfall against its separate nucleons added together  b) Extra mass gained from electrons  c) The mass of its neutrons only  d) A weighing error
**Answer: a) Its mass shortfall against its separate nucleons added together**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) One atomic mass unit of defect corresponds to…
a) 931 MeV  b) 28.4 MeV  c) 1 joule  d) 0.0305 MeV
**Answer: a) 931 MeV**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Helium's mass defect, as Egypt works it, is…
a) 0.0305 u  b) 0.5 u  c) 4 u  d) 931 u
**Answer: a) 0.0305 u**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Helium's binding energy comes out at…
a) 28.3955 MeV  b) 931 MeV  c) 3.72 MeV  d) 0.0305 MeV
**Answer: a) 28.3955 MeV**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Nuclear stability is compared by…
a) Binding energy per nucleon  b) Total mass alone  c) Atomic number alone  d) The number of electrons
**Answer: a) Binding energy per nucleon**
