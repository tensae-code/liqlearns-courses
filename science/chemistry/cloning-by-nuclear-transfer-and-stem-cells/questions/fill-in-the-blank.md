# Cloning by nuclear transfer — and stem cells — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Reproductive cloning by _____ (Dolly) is set against therapeutic cloning, with its ethics and law.
**Answer: nuclear transfer**

**2.** Stem cells are classified by potency: embryonic, adult and induced _____.
**Answer: pluripotent**

**3.** _____ and gene expression are the genetic potential from which cloning starts.
**Answer: Differentiation**
