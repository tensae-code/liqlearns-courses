# Cloning by nuclear transfer — and stem cells

## Do it first
Before reading, say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is: cloning by nuclear transfer — and stem cells. Then read on — this is Italy's way of teaching it, as the Italy placements describe the book's own treatment.

## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo F, Capitolo 15, §§18–20 — Italy's book (Grade 13)

Cloning from genetic potential: differentiation and gene expression, reproductive cloning by nuclear transfer (Dolly) against therapeutic cloning with its ethics and law, stem cells classified by potency (embryonic, adult, induced pluripotent) and their therapeutic uses.

## The key ideas

- Reproductive cloning by nuclear transfer (Dolly) is set against therapeutic cloning, with its ethics and law.
- Stem cells are classified by potency: embryonic, adult and induced pluripotent.
- Differentiation and gene expression are the genetic potential from which cloning starts.
