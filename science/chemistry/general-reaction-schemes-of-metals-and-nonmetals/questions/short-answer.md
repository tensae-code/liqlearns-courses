# General reaction schemes of metals and nonmetals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the metal scheme's five arrows from memory.
**Answer: + O₂ → basic oxide; + nonmetal → salt; + H₂O → hydroxide + H₂; + acid → salt + H₂; + base → salt + H₂ (amphoteric metals only).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Write the nonmetal scheme's five arrows.
**Answer: + H₂ → hydrogen compound; + O₂ → oxide; + metal → salt; + H₂O → acid; + base → salt + H₂O.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What do the KBr/KI displacements prove?
**Answer: Chlorine is the stronger oxidiser — it takes the halide ions' place, freeing bromine (yellow) and iodine (brown); oxidising power falls down the group.**
