# General reaction schemes of metals and nonmetals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On the metal scheme, metal + acid gives…
a) Salt + water  b) Salt + hydrogen  c) Hydroxide + hydrogen  d) Oxide + hydrogen
**Answer: b) Salt + hydrogen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Metal + water gives, on the scheme…
a) Salt + H₂  b) Hydroxide + H₂  c) Acid + H₂  d) Oxide only
**Answer: b) Hydroxide + H₂**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) On the nonmetal scheme, nonmetal + base gives…
a) Salt + H₂  b) Salt + water  c) Hydrogen compound  d) Oxide
**Answer: b) Salt + water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Sulfur's characteristic oxidation states in the lesson are…
a) −2, +4, +6  b) −3, +3, +5  c) 0 only  d) +1, +2
**Answer: a) −2, +4, +6**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Chlorine water in KBr solution produces…
a) Nothing  b) Yellow bromine, displaced  c) Brown iodine  d) A precipitate of KCl
**Answer: b) Yellow bromine, displaced**
