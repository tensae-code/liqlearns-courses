# Hands-on — General reaction schemes of metals and nonmetals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Scheme-chart wall
Draw Bulgaria's two scheme-charts large: METAL in the centre with its five arrows, NONMETAL mirrored beside it. Under each arrow, pin one real reaction from earlier lessons (with its equation) that obeys the arrow — the chart is only believed when every arrow carries an example.

## Predict-then-check
For sodium, aluminium and sulfur, predict every scheme reaction on paper first. Then check each prediction against the earlier lessons on those elements and mark PREDICTED RIGHT / NEEDS THE EXCEPTION — noting the amphoteric arrows and the metals too unreactive for the water arrow.
