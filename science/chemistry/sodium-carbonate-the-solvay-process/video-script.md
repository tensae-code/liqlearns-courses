# Video script — Sodium carbonate — the Solvay process

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Salt and limestone in, soda ash out — and the ammonia used walks in a circle, borrowed and returned.

The Solvay (ammonia-soda) process makes sodium carbonate from brine (sodium chloride) and limestone (calcium carbonate).

Ammonia is used in the process and recovered — it circulates rather than being consumed.

Sodium carbonate (soda ash) serves glass-making, detergents and chemical manufacture.

Now you. Say it back: Name the Solvay raw materials.
Answer: Brine and limestone.
