# Sodium carbonate — the Solvay process — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name the Solvay raw materials.
**Answer: Brine and limestone**

**2. ** What chemical circulates, recovered?
**Answer: Ammonia**

**3. ** Sodium carbonate is also called…
**Answer: Soda ash**

**4. ** Name a soda ash use.
**Answer: Glass-making or detergents**
