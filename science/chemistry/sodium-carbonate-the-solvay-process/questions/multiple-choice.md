# Sodium carbonate — the Solvay process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name the Solvay raw materials.
a) Brine and limestone  b) Its C=C double bond(s)  c) Ethyl ethanoate  d) Flavours, perfumes or solvents
**Answer: a) Brine and limestone**

**2. ** What chemical circulates, recovered?
a) Purifies a metal at the cathode  b) A cell potential depending on [H₃O⁺]  c) It regrows or refills  d) Ammonia
**Answer: d) Ammonia**

**3. ** Sodium carbonate is also called…
a) Indivisible — cannot be cut  b) Alpha, beta and gamma  c) Soda ash  d) CO₂
**Answer: c) Soda ash**

**4. ** Name a soda ash use.
a) Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)  b) Glass-making or detergents  c) Positive kernels in a lattice  d) The bonding MO
**Answer: b) Glass-making or detergents**
