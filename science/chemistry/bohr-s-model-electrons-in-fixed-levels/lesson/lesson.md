## Bohr's model

Bohr (1913), Rutherford's student, repaired the collapse problem: electrons orbit only at specific allowed distances — energy levels/shells designated K, L, M, N — without radiating, and jump between levels absorbing or releasing energy (quantum leaps).4.3).
