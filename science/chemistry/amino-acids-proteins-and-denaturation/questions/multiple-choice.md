# Amino acids, proteins and denaturation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An amino acid is…
a) A chain of sugars  b) A molecule with an amino group (–NH₂) and a carboxyl group (–COOH) on one central carbon, differing from the next amino acid only in its R group  c) A fat with nitrogen added  d) One link of a protein with no groups of its own
**Answer: b) A molecule with an amino group (–NH₂) and a carboxyl group (–COOH) on one central carbon, differing from the next amino acid only in its R group**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What makes one amino acid different from another?
a) Its amino group  b) Its carboxyl group  c) Its side chain, the R group  d) Its central carbon
**Answer: c) Its side chain, the R group**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Amino acids join into a protein by…
a) Peptide links, losing water at each join  b) Joining R group to R group  c) Melting together with heat  d) Sharing electrons pair by pair
**Answer: a) Peptide links, losing water at each join**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What sets how a protein chain folds?
a) The temperature of the room  b) The sequence — the exact order of amino acids along the chain  c) The size of the dish it is in  d) The metal at its centre
**Answer: b) The sequence — the exact order of amino acids along the chain**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Denaturation is…
a) The chain splitting back into amino acids  b) The fold undoing — weak holding bonds broken by heat, acid or alkali — while the peptide chain stays whole  c) A protein joining to another protein  d) The R groups being replaced
**Answer: b) The fold undoing — weak holding bonds broken by heat, acid or alkali — while the peptide chain stays whole**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The white of an egg turning white and firm in a hot pan is…
a) Hydrolysis  b) A new protein being built  c) Denaturation seen at home  d) Fermentation
**Answer: c) Denaturation seen at home**
