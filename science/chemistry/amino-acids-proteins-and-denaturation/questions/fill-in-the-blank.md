# Amino acids, proteins and denaturation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The part of an amino acid that differs from one amino acid to the next is the side chain, called the _____ group.
**Answer: R**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Amino acids are joined into a protein by _____ links, losing water at each join.
**Answer: peptide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The blood protein Ecuador sets beside chlorophyll is _____.
**Answer: haemoglobin**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The undoing of a protein's fold by heat or acid, without breaking its chain, is called _____.
**Answer: denaturation**
