Today: **Amino acids, proteins and denaturation** — Ecuador's way: the chemistry of the foods and the body in the student's own home.

Every amino acid is built on one plan: one carbon in the middle, an amino group on it, an acid group on it — and one side chain, the R group, that is different every time. Change the side chain, change the unit. Twenty units, one plan.
Join the units link by link, water leaving at every join, and the long chain is a protein. The order of the units folds the chain, and the fold does the work — haemoglobin carrying oxygen, built like chlorophyll catching light.
Heat the protein, or meet it with acid, and the fold comes undone while the chain stays whole. That is denaturation — the egg white turning white and firm in the pan, never to run again. And because the body accepts these chains, medicine builds repairs from them.
