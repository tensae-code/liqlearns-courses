# Oxygen — its properties and tests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about oxygen — its properties and tests?
a) Oxygen is a colourless, odourless gas, a little denser than air, only slightly soluble in water, neutral to litmus — and it is recognised by one sure test: it relights a glowing splint.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Oxygen is a colourless, odourless gas, a little denser than air, only slightly soluble in water, neutral to litmus — and it is recognised by one sure test: it relights a glowing splint.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about what oxygen is like?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Oxygen is a colourless gas with no smell and no taste.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Oxygen is a colourless gas with no smell and no taste.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about denser than air — and what that decides?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Oxygen is slightly denser (heavier) than air.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Oxygen is slightly denser (heavier) than air.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about the glowing-splint test?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Oxygen supports combustion far more strongly than air does.
**Answer: d) Oxygen supports combustion far more strongly than air does.**
