# Oxygen — its properties and tests — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Oxygen is a colourless gas with no smell and no taste.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Denser than air — and what that decides is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** Oxygen keeps people and animals alive in breathing, makes fires and fuels burn, rusts iron, and is given pure to patients and divers and welders.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
