# Hands-on — Oxygen — its properties and tests

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
With a teacher's help and care: light a wooden splint, let the flame die to a glow, and push it into a jar of freshly prepared oxygen — watch it burst back into flame. Write down exactly what you saw; that observation is the test for oxygen.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of oxygen — its properties and tests — oxygen is a colourless, odourless gas, a little denser than air, only slightly soluble in water, neutral to litmus — and it is recognised by one sure test: it relights a glowing splint. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
