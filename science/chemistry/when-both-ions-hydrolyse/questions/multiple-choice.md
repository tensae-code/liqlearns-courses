# When both ions hydrolyse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What decides the pH when both ions hydrolyse?
a) With a group Start-up Activity  b) Comparing Ka and Kb  c) Yes — it reaches equilibrium  d) The melting point
**Answer: b) Comparing Ka and Kb**

**2. ** If Ka > Kb, the solution is…
a) Acidic  b) Manufacturing processes, incl. Ethiopia's  c) Ammonia  d) Pests
**Answer: a) Acidic**

**3. ** If Kb > Ka, the solution is…
a) Negative  b) The number of neutrons  c) A continuous spectrum  d) Basic
**Answer: d) Basic**

**4. ** If Ka ≈ Kb, the solution is…
a) At the surface, at any temperature  b) The slope of the tangent  c) Near neutral  d) Particles are locked in place
**Answer: c) Near neutral**
