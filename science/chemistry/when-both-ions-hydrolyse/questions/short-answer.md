# When both ions hydrolyse — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What decides the pH when both ions hydrolyse?
**Answer: Comparing Ka and Kb**

**2. ** If Ka > Kb, the solution is…
**Answer: Acidic**

**3. ** If Kb > Ka, the solution is…
**Answer: Basic**

**4. ** If Ka ≈ Kb, the solution is…
**Answer: Near neutral**
