# Carbonic acid and carbonates — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon dioxide dissolved in water forms _____ acid.
**Answer: carbonic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Washing soda is sodium _____.
**Answer: carbonate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Baking soda is sodium _____.
**Answer: hydrogencarbonate**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The fizzing of a carbonate with acid is called _____.
**Answer: effervescence**
