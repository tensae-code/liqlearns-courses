# Carbonic acid and carbonates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbonic acid has the formula…
a) H₂CO₃  b) HCO₃  c) CH₄  d) H₂SO₄
**Answer: a) H₂CO₃**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbonic acid is described as weak and dibasic because…
a) It is strong and has one hydrogen  b) Only a little of the dissolved CO₂ becomes acid, and the acid can give up two hydrogen ions  c) It never reacts  d) It is a solid acid
**Answer: b) Only a little of the dissolved CO₂ becomes acid, and the acid can give up two hydrogen ions**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The hydrogencarbonate ion is…
a) CO₃²⁻  b) HCO₃⁻  c) OH⁻  d) CO₂
**Answer: b) HCO₃⁻**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Baking soda is…
a) Sodium carbonate  b) Calcium carbonate  c) Sodium hydrogencarbonate  d) Sodium hydroxide
**Answer: c) Sodium hydrogencarbonate**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A carbonate with a dilute acid gives…
a) Hydrogen  b) A salt, water and carbon dioxide, with fizzing  c) Oxygen only  d) No reaction ever
**Answer: b) A salt, water and carbon dioxide, with fizzing**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Limestone caves are carved because…
a) Acid rain contains sulfuric acid only  b) Rainwater bearing CO₂ — carbonic acid — dissolves calcium carbonate as the soluble hydrogencarbonate  c) Rivers freeze the rock  d) Carbonates dissolve in pure water quickly
**Answer: b) Rainwater bearing CO₂ — carbonic acid — dissolves calcium carbonate as the soluble hydrogencarbonate**
