# Hands-on — Carbonic acid and carbonates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The fizz test
With a teacher, add dilute acid drop by drop to three powders — a known carbonate, a known non-carbonate, and an unknown — and test any gas with limewater. Record the full reading for each: fizz or none, limewater milky or clear, and your verdict.

## The cave in a jar
Bubble carbon dioxide (from a carbonate and acid) through limewater until it clears again — the carbonate has dissolved as hydrogencarbonate. Then warm the clear solution gently and watch the cloudiness return. Write the story of the jar in the lesson's own equations.
