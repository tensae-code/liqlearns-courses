# Acid–base neutralisation and equivalence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define the equivalence point in the book's terms.
**Answer: The point in a dosing where the acid and base have been mixed in exactly the reaction equation's proportions — neither in excess — and the mixture is neutral.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Write the equivalence relation and name each symbol.
**Answer: Ca·Va = Cb·Vb — Ca, Va the acid's concentration and volume; Cb, Vb the base's.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is all of a dosing organised around one volume?
**Answer: Because only at that volume do the amounts match the equation exactly, so only there does the measured volume convert, through Ca·Va = Cb·Vb, into the unknown concentration.**
