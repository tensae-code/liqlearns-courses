# Acid–base neutralisation and equivalence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Neutralisation is complete, in the book's sense, at…
a) The first drop of base  b) The equivalence point  c) Double the base volume  d) Boiling
**Answer: b) The equivalence point**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At equivalence, Ca·Va equals…
a) Cb·Vb  b) Ca·Vb  c) Va·Vb  d) 1
**Answer: a) Cb·Vb**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) If Ca = 0.10 mol/L, Va = 10 mL and Cb = 0.20 mol/L, then Vb at equivalence is…
a) 20 mL  b) 5 mL  c) 10 mL  d) 40 mL
**Answer: b) 5 mL**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Past equivalence, the mixture holds an excess of…
a) Acid  b) Base (the added partner)  c) Salt only  d) Water only
**Answer: b) Base (the added partner)**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The mixture at the equivalence point is…
a) Strongly acidic  b) Strongly basic  c) Neutral  d) Colourless always
**Answer: c) Neutral**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The equivalence relation serves to find…
a) The molar mass  b) An unknown concentration or volume  c) The pH paper colour  d) The gas volume
**Answer: b) An unknown concentration or volume**
