# Acid–base neutralisation and equivalence — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Acid dosed against base undergoes _____.
**Answer: neutralisation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At the _____ point, acid and base match the equation's proportions exactly.
**Answer: equivalence**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Ca·Va = Cb·_____.
**Answer: Vb**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Past equivalence the added partner is in _____.
**Answer: excess**
