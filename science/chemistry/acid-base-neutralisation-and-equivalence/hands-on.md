# Hands-on — Acid–base neutralisation and equivalence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dose to the point (teacher-led bench)
With a teacher present and the safety kit on, dose a dilute acid against a dilute base drop by drop with the class's indicator, stopping at the first lasting colour change. Record the volume and name it, in the book's term, before any calculation is done.

## Before you pour (paper only)
A bench plan asks for the volume of 0.15 mol/L NaOH that will neutralise 20.0 mL of 0.10 mol/L HCl. Compute it on paper first (13.3 mL), then write what the mixture holds if 20.0 mL of base were poured instead — and why that is not equivalence.
