## Effective nuclear charge and shielding

Before the four trends, the explanatory tool is built: outer electrons feel not the full nuclear charge but an effective one, reduced by shielding — inner electrons partially cancelling the nucleus's pull. More shielding (or a farther outer shell) means a more weakly held outer electron; this one idea is then used to explain radius, ionisation energy and the rest.
