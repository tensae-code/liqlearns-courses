Today: **Alkynes — ethyne from carbide**.

Alkynes, CₙH₂ₙ₋₂, carry a C≡C triple bond.

The laboratory preparation of the first member, ethyne (acetylene), is by reacting calcium carbide with water;

further preparation by alkylation of sodium acetylide is given.

Reactions parallel the alkenes' additions, in two steps: full hydrogenation to the alkane, or partial hydrogenation over Lindlar's catalyst stopping at the alkene;

hydrohalogenation follows Markovnikov's rule.
