# The electron-sea model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the 'sea' in a metal?
a) Delocalised electrons  b) BF₃ (or a metal ion)  c) Buffer solutions  d) The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺
**Answer: a) Delocalised electrons**

**2. ** To which atom do sea electrons belong?
a) The Solvay process (Unit 3)  b) Broken-down fibre from wood  c) Distillation  d) No single atom
**Answer: d) No single atom**

**3. ** What sits in the sea?
a) The Aufbau principle  b) The energy needed to remove an electron  c) Positive kernels in a lattice  d) The particle's mass
**Answer: c) Positive kernels in a lattice**

**4. ** What does the model explain next?
a) The –OH carbon holds three other carbons  b) Metal properties  c) They converge to one level  d) The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance
**Answer: b) Metal properties**
