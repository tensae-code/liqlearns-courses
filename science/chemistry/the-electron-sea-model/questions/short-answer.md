# The electron-sea model — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is the 'sea' in a metal?
**Answer: Delocalised electrons**

**2. ** To which atom do sea electrons belong?
**Answer: No single atom**

**3. ** What sits in the sea?
**Answer: Positive kernels in a lattice**

**4. ** What does the model explain next?
**Answer: Metal properties**
