# Video script — The electron-sea model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One page, one picture — the model that explains metals.

The electron-sea model pictures a metal as positive kernels arranged in a lattice, immersed in a 'sea' of delocalised electrons.

The electrons belong to no single atom — they are free to move through the whole structure.

This one picture is the explanation the next lesson spends on conductivity, bending and lustre.

Now you. Say it back: What is the 'sea' in a metal?
Answer: Delocalised electrons.
