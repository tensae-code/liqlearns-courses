# Halogens — group 17 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The halogens sit in which group of the periodic table?
a) Group 1  b) Group 2  c) Group 17  d) Group 18
**Answer: c) Group 17**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How many outer electrons does every halogen atom carry?
a) 1  b) 2  c) 7  d) 8
**Answer: c) 7**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Which halogen is a red-brown liquid at room temperature?
a) Fluorine  b) Chlorine  c) Bromine  d) Iodine
**Answer: c) Bromine**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Reactivity in the halogen family…
a) Increases down the group  b) Falls down the group — fluorine is the most reactive  c) Is the same for all members  d) Is lowest at the top
**Answer: b) Falls down the group — fluorine is the most reactive**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Chlorine is passed into a bromide solution. What happens?
a) Nothing  b) Bromine is freed — the stronger halogen displaces the weaker  c) Chlorine is displaced  d) The chlorine turns to iodine
**Answer: b) Bromine is freed — the stronger halogen displaces the weaker**
