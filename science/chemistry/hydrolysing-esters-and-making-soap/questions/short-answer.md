# Hydrolysing esters — and making soap — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does hydrolysis split an ester into?
**Answer: Its acid and its alcohol**

**2. ** What is saponification?
**Answer: Making soap by alkaline hydrolysis of fats**

**3. ** Alkaline hydrolysis of an ester gives…
**Answer: The acid's salt plus the alcohol**

**4. ** Acid hydrolysis is the reverse of…
**Answer: Esterification**
