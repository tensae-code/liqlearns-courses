# Hydrolysing esters — and making soap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does hydrolysis split an ester into?
a) Its acid and its alcohol  b) The isotopes, weighted by abundance  c) An emission spectrum  d) Position and momentum
**Answer: a) Its acid and its alcohol**

**2. ** What is saponification?
a) Inward pull on surface particles  b) The rate constant  c) Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance  d) Making soap by alkaline hydrolysis of fats
**Answer: d) Making soap by alkaline hydrolysis of fats**

**3. ** Alkaline hydrolysis of an ester gives…
a) The melting point  b) With a group Start-up Activity  c) The acid's salt plus the alcohol  d) Yes — it reaches equilibrium
**Answer: c) The acid's salt plus the alcohol**

**4. ** Acid hydrolysis is the reverse of…
a) Pests  b) Esterification  c) Manufacturing processes, incl. Ethiopia's  d) Ammonia
**Answer: b) Esterification**
