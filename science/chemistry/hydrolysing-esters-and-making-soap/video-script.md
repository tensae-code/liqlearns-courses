# Video script — Hydrolysing esters — and making soap

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What an acid and alcohol joined, water can split.

Hydrolysis splits an ester back into its carboxylic acid and alcohol — acid-catalysed hydrolysis is the reverse of esterification.

Alkaline hydrolysis gives the salt of the acid plus the alcohol — it is not reversible the same way.

Hydrolysing a fat (a big ester) with alkali gives glycerol and soap: this is saponification.

Now you. Say it back: What does hydrolysis split an ester into?
Answer: Its acid and its alcohol.
