# Addition vs condensation polymerisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 3) ** What is lost in condensation polymerisation — A small molecule (usually water)
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** What bond opens in addition polymerisation — Solid → liquid → gas
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Name an addition polymer. — Polyethene
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) ** Name a condensation polymer. — BF₃ (or a metal ion)
**Answer: False**
