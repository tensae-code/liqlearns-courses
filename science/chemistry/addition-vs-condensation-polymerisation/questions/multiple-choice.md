# Addition vs condensation polymerisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is lost in condensation polymerisation?
a) A small molecule (usually water)  b) A (symmetrical) ether  c) Carbon 1  d) Carbon dioxide, CO₂
**Answer: a) A small molecule (usually water)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What bond opens in addition polymerisation?
a) Exactly neutralise each other  b) So electrons lost equal electrons gained  c) Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q)  d) The C=C double bond
**Answer: d) The C=C double bond**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** Name an addition polymer.
a) Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil  b) Pesticide and fertiliser residues  c) Polyethene  d) PET or nylon
**Answer: c) Polyethene**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name a condensation polymer.
a) Temporary flickering dipoles  b) PET or nylon  c) A negative ion (anion)  d) Strong lattice attractions in all directions
**Answer: b) PET or nylon**
