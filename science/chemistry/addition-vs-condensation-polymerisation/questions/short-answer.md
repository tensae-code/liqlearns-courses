# Addition vs condensation polymerisation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is lost in condensation polymerisation?
**Answer: A small molecule (usually water)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What bond opens in addition polymerisation?
**Answer: The C=C double bond**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** Name an addition polymer.
**Answer: Polyethene**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Name a condensation polymer.
**Answer: PET or nylon**
