# Video script — Addition vs condensation polymerisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two ways to build a chain: click the beads together whole, or snap them together discarding a small piece each time.

Addition polymerisation: monomers with C=C bonds open their double bonds and add on, one after another — nothing is lost.

Condensation polymerisation: monomers join with the loss of a small molecule, usually water, at each link.

The products' uses follow their structures — the section works named polymers one by one.

Now you. Say it back: What is lost in condensation polymerisation?
Answer: A small molecule (usually water).
