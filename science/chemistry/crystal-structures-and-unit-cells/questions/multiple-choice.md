# Crystal structures and unit cells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a close-packed layer stacking, each sphere of the next layer…
a) Sits directly on top of one sphere below  b) Rests in a hollow of the layer below  c) Floats between layers  d) Is smaller than the layer below
**Answer: b) Rests in a hollow of the layer below**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A unit cell is…
a) One whole crystal  b) The smallest repeating box whose contents rebuild the whole crystal  c) A single atom  d) The box a crystal is stored in
**Answer: b) The smallest repeating box whose contents rebuild the whole crystal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A corner atom counts 1/8 to one cell because…
a) Only 1/8 of it is real  b) Eight cells meet at that corner and share it  c) Corners are cut off  d) The cell is one-eighth full
**Answer: b) Eight cells meet at that corner and share it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A body-centred cubic (bcc) cell owns…
a) 1 atom  b) 2 atoms  c) 4 atoms  d) 9 atoms
**Answer: b) 2 atoms**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The bcc count is built as…
a) 8 × 1/8 = 1, plus the centre atom = 2  b) 8 corners + 1 centre = 9  c) 6 × 1/2 = 3, plus 1 = 4  d) 8 × 1/2 = 4
**Answer: a) 8 × 1/8 = 1, plus the centre atom = 2**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A face-centred cubic (fcc) cell owns…
a) 1 atom  b) 2 atoms  c) 3 atoms  d) 4 atoms
**Answer: d) 4 atoms**
