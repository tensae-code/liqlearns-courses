# Crystal structures and unit cells — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In close packing, each sphere rests in a _____ of the layer below.
**Answer: hollow**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The smallest repeating box of a crystal is called the _____ cell.
**Answer: unit**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A corner atom is shared between _____ cells, so it counts 1/8 to each.
**Answer: eight**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A body-centred cubic cell owns _____ atoms in total.
**Answer: two**
