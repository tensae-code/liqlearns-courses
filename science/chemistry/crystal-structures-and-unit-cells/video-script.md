Today: **Crystal structures and unit cells** — Sri Lanka's way: a crystal is a packing of spheres, and one small box tells you the whole story.

Stack oranges in a crate: each layer rests in the hollows of the layer below. A crystal is that packing, repeated. The smallest repeating box is the unit cell.
Now count fairly. A corner atom is shared by eight cells, so it counts one-eighth. Eight corners make one whole atom — and a body-centred cell adds its centre atom: two atoms per cell. Corners only: one. Faces as well: four.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
