# Hands-on — Open, closed and isolated chemical systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Sort the systems
Make nine cards: open pan of boiling water; sealed flask warming in the sun; thermos of hot tea; burning methane; zinc in acid in a beaker; sealed apparatus electrolysing water; a plant in a sealed glass jar under light; an open cup of ice melting; a pressure cooker with its valve shut. Sort them OPEN / CLOSED / (nearly) ISOLATED and defend each sort by naming what crosses the border.

## Find the boundary
Pick one reaction from another lesson. Draw its system boundary as a box, label the surroundings, and draw two arrows: what matter crosses, what energy crosses. The arrows — not the box — decide its class.
