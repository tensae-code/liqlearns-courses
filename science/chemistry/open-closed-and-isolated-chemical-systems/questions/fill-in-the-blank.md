# Open, closed and isolated chemical systems — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) An open system exchanges both matter and _____.
**Answer: energy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Systems that exchange neither matter nor energy are called _____.
**Answer: isolated**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A system with more than one phase is called _____.
**Answer: heterogeneous**
