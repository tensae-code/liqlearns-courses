# Open, closed and isolated chemical systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A heterogeneous system is one with…
a) No surroundings  b) More than one phase, with phase boundaries  c) Only gases  d) No energy exchange
**Answer: b) More than one phase, with phase boundaries**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A closed system exchanges with its surroundings…
a) Matter and energy  b) Matter only  c) Energy only  d) Neither
**Answer: c) Energy only**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Bulgaria's example of a closed system is…
a) Methane burning  b) Water being electrolysed in a sealed apparatus  c) Zinc in acid  d) A kettle boiling open
**Answer: b) Water being electrolysed in a sealed apparatus**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Isolated systems…
a) Are common in laboratories  b) Exchange energy only  c) Do not exist in reality  d) Exchange matter only
**Answer: c) Do not exist in reality**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Zinc dissolving in acid is classed as…
a) Closed and homogeneous  b) Open and heterogeneous  c) Isolated  d) Closed and heterogeneous
**Answer: b) Open and heterogeneous**
