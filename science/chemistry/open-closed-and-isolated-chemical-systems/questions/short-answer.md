# Open, closed and isolated chemical systems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What separates a system from its surroundings in this framing?
**Answer: The border between the chosen substances and everything else — the border decides whether matter, energy, both or neither can pass.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the three system kinds by what they exchange.
**Answer: Open exchanges matter and energy; closed exchanges energy only; isolated exchanges neither.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Classify a candle burning under a sealed glass dome that warms the room.
**Answer: Closed and (while wax, air and flame products mix in phases) heterogeneous in its parts — no matter escapes the dome, but heat energy crosses the glass.**
