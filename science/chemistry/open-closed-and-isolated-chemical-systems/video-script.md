# Video script — Open, closed and isolated chemical systems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Open, closed and isolated chemical systems** — Bulgaria's framing. Every reaction is a system against its surroundings. Open: matter and energy both cross — methane burning. Closed: energy only — water electrolysed in a sealed apparatus. Isolated: neither crosses — and none exist in reality. One phase, homogeneous; more phases, heterogeneous — zinc in acid.
