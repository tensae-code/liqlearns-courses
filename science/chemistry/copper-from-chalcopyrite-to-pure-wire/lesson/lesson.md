## Copper

Copper is extracted by roasting its ore chalcopyrite (CuFeS₂); the roasting product is impure blister copper, which is refined further by electrolysis to pure copper. Its properties — malleable, ductile, an excellent conductor of heat and electricity — fix its uses: electric wires and alloys.
