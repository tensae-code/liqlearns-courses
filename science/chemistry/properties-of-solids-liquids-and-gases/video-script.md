# Video script — Properties of solids, liquids and gases

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A stone keeps its volume anywhere; water takes its container's shape but keeps its volume; air takes both.

Solids keep shape and volume: particles locked in place, strongly attracted.

Liquids keep volume but take the container's shape: particles close but able to slide past each other, so liquids flow and diffuse slowly.

Gases take both shape and volume of the container: particles far apart, weakly attracted, diffusing fast and easily compressed.

Now you. Say it back: Which state keeps volume but not shape?
Answer: Liquid.
