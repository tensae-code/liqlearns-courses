# Properties of solids, liquids and gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which state keeps volume but not shape?
a) A probability description  b) Boxes and arrows  c) Liquid  d) A photon
**Answer: c) Liquid**

**2. ** Why can gases be compressed?
a) Toward products  b) Particles are far apart  c) The minimum energy for reaction on collision  d) Temperature
**Answer: b) Particles are far apart**

**3. ** Which state diffuses fastest?
a) Gas  b) They become single bonds  c) Open versus sealed storage (rancidity)  d) An empty orbital of the acid
**Answer: a) Gas**

**4. ** Why does a solid keep its shape?
a) Nitric acid, HNO₃  b) Care — dose, protection, water safety  c) Clay  d) Particles are locked in place
**Answer: d) Particles are locked in place**
