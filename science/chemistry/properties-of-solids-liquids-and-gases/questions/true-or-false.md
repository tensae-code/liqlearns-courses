**1. ** Which state keeps volume but not shape — Liquid
**Answer: True**

**2. ** Why can gases be compressed — Exactly neutralise each other
**Answer: False**

**3. ** Which state diffuses fastest — Gas
**Answer: True**

**4. ** Why does a solid keep its shape — Negative
