# Properties of solids, liquids and gases — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which state keeps volume but not shape?
**Answer: Liquid**

**2. ** Why can gases be compressed?
**Answer: Particles are far apart**

**3. ** Which state diffuses fastest?
**Answer: Gas**

**4. ** Why does a solid keep its shape?
**Answer: Particles are locked in place**
