# Video script — Cement — clinker to concrete

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A rotating kiln longer than a football pitch roasts rock into grey nodules.

Cement starts from limestone and clay, crushed and blended as a raw mix.

The mix is burned in a rotating kiln to form clinker — the high-temperature stages drive off CO₂ and form the cement minerals.

Clinker is ground with a little gypsum to give cement powder; mixed with water it sets — the hydration that binds concrete.

Now you. Say it back: Name cement's two raw materials.
Answer: Limestone and clay.
