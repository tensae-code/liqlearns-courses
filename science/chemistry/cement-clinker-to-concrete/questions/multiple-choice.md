# Cement — clinker to concrete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name cement's two raw materials.
a) Limestone and clay  b) Nitric acid, HNO₃  c) Care — dose, protection, water safety  d) Clay
**Answer: a) Limestone and clay**

**2. ** What does the kiln produce?
a) The isotopes, weighted by abundance  b) An emission spectrum  c) Position and momentum  d) Clinker
**Answer: d) Clinker**

**3. ** What is ground in with clinker?
a) The rate constant  b) Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance  c) Gypsum  d) Inward pull on surface particles
**Answer: c) Gypsum**

**4. ** What makes cement set?
a) With a group Start-up Activity  b) Hydration with water  c) Yes — it reaches equilibrium  d) The melting point
**Answer: b) Hydration with water**
