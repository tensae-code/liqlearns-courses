# Cement — clinker to concrete — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name cement's two raw materials.
**Answer: Limestone and clay**

**2. ** What does the kiln produce?
**Answer: Clinker**

**3. ** What is ground in with clinker?
**Answer: Gypsum**

**4. ** What makes cement set?
**Answer: Hydration with water**
