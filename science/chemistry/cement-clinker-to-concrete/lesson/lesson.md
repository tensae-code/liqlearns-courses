# Cement — clinker to concrete

## Do it first
A rotating kiln longer than a football pitch roasts rock into grey nodules. Before reading, name the two starting rocks and the roasted product.

[Picture 21762 — A rotating kiln cross-section.]

Cement starts from limestone and clay, crushed and blended as a raw mix.

The mix is burned in a rotating kiln to form clinker — the high-temperature stages drive off CO₂ and form the cement minerals.

Clinker is ground with a little gypsum to give cement powder; mixed with water it sets — the hydration that binds concrete. Ethiopian plants anchor the lesson.
