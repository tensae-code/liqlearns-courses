# Corrosion — iron as its own cell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What two things does rusting need?
a) Chemical equivalents  b) ΔG° = −nFE°  c) Oxygen and water  d) The electrochemical (reactivity) series
**Answer: c) Oxygen and water**

**2. ** At anodic regions, iron is…
a) Plato and Aristotle  b) Oxidised (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻)  c) Lungs and health  d) CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone
**Answer: b) Oxidised (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻)**

**3. ** What is rust, chemically?
a) Hydrated iron(III) oxide, Fe₂O₃·xH₂O  b) Whether the Lewis structure is sound  c) Delocalised electrons  d) No stable molecule forms
**Answer: a) Hydrated iron(III) oxide, Fe₂O₃·xH₂O**

**4. ** Rusting is what kind of process?
a) They do not react with most solutes  b) –COOH  c) Alcohol → aldehyde → acid  d) Electrochemical
**Answer: d) Electrochemical**
