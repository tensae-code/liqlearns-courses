# Corrosion — iron as its own cell — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What two things does rusting need?
**Answer: Oxygen and water**

**2. ** At anodic regions, iron is…
**Answer: Oxidised (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻)**

**3. ** What is rust, chemically?
**Answer: Hydrated iron(III) oxide, Fe₂O₃·xH₂O**

**4. ** Rusting is what kind of process?
**Answer: Electrochemical**
