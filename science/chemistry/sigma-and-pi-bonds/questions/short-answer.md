# Sigma and pi bonds — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can a pi bond never be the only bond between two atoms?
**Answer:** A pi bond's sideways overlap needs the atoms held at bonding distance by a sigma bond first; the sigma frame comes first and the pi bond forms alongside it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A double bond is stronger than a single bond, yet reacts more easily. Explain both halves.
**Answer:** The double bond holds two bonds (sigma + pi) so it is stronger overall, but its pi bond is weak and exposed, so reactions break the pi part while the sigma bond survives.
