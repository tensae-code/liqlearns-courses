# Sigma and pi bonds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A sigma bond is formed by…
a) Head-on overlap of orbitals  b) Sideways overlap of p orbitals  c) Ions transferring electrons  d) Hydrogen bonding
**Answer: a) Head-on overlap of orbitals**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A pi bond is formed by…
a) Sideways overlap of pure p orbitals  b) Head-on overlap  c) Metallic bonding  d) A coordinate bond only
**Answer: a) Sideways overlap of pure p orbitals**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A double bond consists of…
a) One sigma and one pi bond  b) Two sigma bonds  c) Two pi bonds  d) One pi bond only
**Answer: a) One sigma and one pi bond**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Which partner is weaker and breaks first in reactions?
a) The pi bond  b) The sigma bond  c) Both break together always  d) Neither can break
**Answer: a) The pi bond**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A triple bond contains…
a) One sigma and two pi bonds  b) Three sigma bonds  c) Three pi bonds  d) Two sigma and one pi
**Answer: a) One sigma and two pi bonds**
