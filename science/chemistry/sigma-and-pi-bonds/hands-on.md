# Hands-on — Sigma and pi bonds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hands as orbitals
Hold your two fists end to end, knuckles touching — that is head-on overlap, a sigma bond. Now hold your palms parallel, fingers pointing the same way, overlapping side by side above an imaginary line — that is the sideways overlap of a pi bond. Say which grip feels weaker, and why the book agrees.

## Count the bonds
Write the bond orders for the bonds in H₂, O₂ and N₂ (single, double, triple). Under each, write its sigma/pi count: 1σ; 1σ+1π; 1σ+2π. Say the counting rule aloud once.
