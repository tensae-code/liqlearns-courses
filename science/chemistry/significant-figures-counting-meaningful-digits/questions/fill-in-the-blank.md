# Significant figures — counting meaningful digits — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** Five rules are taught with examples: _____ digits always count;
**Answer: nonzero**

**2. ** zeros between _____ digits count;
**Answer: nonzero**

**3. ** trailing zeros after a decimal point in _____ above 1 count (with the sub-1 cases spelled out);
**Answer: numbers**

**4. ** and trailing zeros in a whole number without a decimal point are ambiguous — _____ notation removes the ambiguity (400 as 4×10², 4.0×10² or 4.00×10²).
**Answer: scientific**
