## Significant figures

Significant figures are the meaningful digits of a measurement, its last digit being the uncertain one. Five rules are taught with examples: nonzero digits always count; zeros between nonzero digits count;

leading zeros never count; trailing zeros after a decimal point in numbers above 1 count (with the sub-1 cases spelled out); and trailing zeros in a whole number without a decimal point are ambiguous — scientific notation removes the ambiguity (400 as 4×10², 4.0×10² or 4.00×10²).
