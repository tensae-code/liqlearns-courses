Today: **Significant figures — counting meaningful digits**.

Significant figures are the meaningful digits of a measurement, its last digit being the uncertain one.

Five rules are taught with examples: nonzero digits always count;

zeros between nonzero digits count;

trailing zeros after a decimal point in numbers above 1 count (with the sub-1 cases spelled out);

and trailing zeros in a whole number without a decimal point are ambiguous — scientific notation removes the ambiguity (400 as 4×10², 4.0×10² or 4.00×10²).

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
