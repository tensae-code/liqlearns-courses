Today: **Nitric oxide — the little molecule that messages the body** — China's way, from the book's own treatment. A full background dossier on NO: Science magazine's 1992 "Molecule of the Year"; the bonding puzzle (15 valence electrons; quantum-chemical energy levels match N₂'s pattern, not O₂'s; a three-electron π bond), bond length 105 pm and bond energy 678 kJ/mol tabulated against N₂ (111 pm, 942) and O₂ (121 pm, 494), paramagnetic as a one-unpaired-electron radical; then its second life — the endothelium-derived relaxing factor that dilates blood vessels, synthesised from L-arginine, with a biological half-life of only 3–5 seconds, acting as a neurotransmitter that needs no receptor or storage vesicle.
