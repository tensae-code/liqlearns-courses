# Lesson plan — Nitric oxide — the little molecule that messages the body

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Nitric oxide — the little molecule that messages the body
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A full background dossier on NO: Science magazine's 1992 "Molecule of the Year"; the bonding puzzle (15 valence electrons; quantum-chemical energy levels match N₂'s pattern, not O₂'s; a three-electron π bond), bond length 105 pm and bond energy 678 kJ/mol tabulated against N₂ (111 pm, 942) and O₂ (121 pm, 494), paramagnetic as a one-unpaired-electron radical; then its second life — the endothelium-derived relaxing factor that dilates blood vessels, synthesised from L-arginine, with a biological half-life of only 3–5 seconds, acting as a neurotransmitter that needs no receptor or storage vesicle.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.
