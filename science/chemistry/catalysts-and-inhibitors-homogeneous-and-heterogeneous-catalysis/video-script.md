Today: **Catalysts and inhibitors** — Bulgaria's three theatres. A catalyst changes the rate and survives unchanged; an inhibitor slows. In solution it works through intermediates and is regenerated. On a solid surface, adsorption holds and weakens the reactants, and the activation energy falls. In the body, enzymes do the same work — fast, cool, and specific.
