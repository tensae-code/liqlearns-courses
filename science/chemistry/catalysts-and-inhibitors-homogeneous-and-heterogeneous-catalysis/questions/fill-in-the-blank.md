# Catalysts and inhibitors — homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A catalyst that slows a reaction is called an _____.
**Answer: inhibitor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Reactants held and weakened on a catalyst's surface are held by _____.
**Answer: adsorption**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The body's catalysts are called _____.
**Answer: enzymes**
