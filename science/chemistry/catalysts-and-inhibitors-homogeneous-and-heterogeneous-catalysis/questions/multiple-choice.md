# Catalysts and inhibitors — homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A catalyst at the end of a reaction is…
a) Used up  b) Chemically unchanged  c) Turned into product  d) Always a gas
**Answer: b) Chemically unchanged**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A substance that slows a reaction is an…
a) Enzyme  b) Inhibitor  c) Adsorbent  d) Intermediate
**Answer: b) Inhibitor**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Heterogeneous catalysis works by…
a) Dissolving the catalyst  b) Adsorption — reactants held and weakened on the surface  c) Raising the temperature  d) Removing the products
**Answer: b) Adsorption — reactants held and weakened on the surface**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Adsorption lowers the reaction's…
a) Mass  b) Activation energy  c) Yield of catalyst  d) Volume
**Answer: b) Activation energy**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Enzymes are marked by speed, low working temperature and great…
a) Acidity  b) Specificity  c) Hardness  d) Solubility
**Answer: b) Specificity**
