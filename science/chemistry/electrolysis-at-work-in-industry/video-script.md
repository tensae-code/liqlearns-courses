# Video script — Electrolysis at work in industry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A cheap steel key comes out of a bath wearing a skin of chromium.

Electroplating coats an object (made the cathode) with a thin layer of a chosen metal — for protection and appearance.

Electrolysis refines metals: impure copper is purified at the cathode, as the case studies showed.

Aluminium is extracted by electrolysis of its molten ore, and chlor-alkali production electrolyses brine — industry runs on this unit's cells.

Now you. Say it back: In plating, the object is which electrode?
Answer: The cathode.
