# Electrolysis at work in industry — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** In plating, the object is which electrode?
**Answer: The cathode**

**2. ** What does electrolytic refining do?
**Answer: Purifies a metal at the cathode**

**3. ** Which metal is extracted by electrolysis of molten ore?
**Answer: Aluminium**

**4. ** Name a chlor-alkali product.
**Answer: Chlorine, hydrogen or sodium hydroxide**
