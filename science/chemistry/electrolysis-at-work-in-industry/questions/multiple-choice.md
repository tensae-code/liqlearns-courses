# Electrolysis at work in industry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** In plating, the object is which electrode?
a) Alkoxyalkane  b) The carbonyl group, C=O  c) The cathode  d) Hydrogen bonding between molecules
**Answer: c) The cathode**

**2. ** What does electrolytic refining do?
a) Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)  b) Purifies a metal at the cathode  c) Strong acid + strong base salt  d) Near neutral
**Answer: b) Purifies a metal at the cathode**

**3. ** Which metal is extracted by electrolysis of molten ore?
a) Aluminium  b) Care/treatment — it pollutes  c) A small molecule that joins into chains  d) The biosphere
**Answer: a) Aluminium**

**4. ** Name a chlor-alkali product.
a) Properties repeat periodically with atomic number  b) Eight electrons — an octet  c) Lattice formation  d) Chlorine, hydrogen or sodium hydroxide
**Answer: d) Chlorine, hydrogen or sodium hydroxide**
