# Electrolysis at work in industry — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. In plating, the object is which electrode? — c
2. What does electrolytic refining do? — d
3. Which metal is extracted by electrolysis of molten ore? — a
4. Name a chlor-alkali product. — b

**Answer key:** 1–c: The cathode · 2–d: Purifies a metal at the cathode · 3–a: Aluminium · 4–b: Chlorine, hydrogen or sodium hydroxide
