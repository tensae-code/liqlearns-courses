# Ostwald's dilution law and weak-electrolyte calculations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ostwald's dilution law, as Egypt derives it, is…
a) Ka = α²C  b) Ka = αC²  c) α = Ka × C  d) [H⁺] = Ca·Ka
**Answer: a) Ka = α²C**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) On diluting a weak acid, its degree of ionisation…
a) Rises  b) Falls  c) Stays the same  d) Falls to zero
**Answer: a) Rises**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) For a weak acid, [H₃O⁺] is calculated as…
a) √(Ca·Ka)  b) Ca·Ka  c) Ca ÷ Ka  d) √(Ca ÷ Ka)
**Answer: a) √(Ca·Ka)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) α stands for…
a) The degree of ionisation  b) The acid's concentration  c) The temperature  d) The equilibrium constant
**Answer: a) The degree of ionisation**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The weak base worked in the chapter is…
a) Ammonia  b) Sodium hydroxide  c) Calcium hydroxide  d) Potassium hydroxide
**Answer: a) Ammonia**
