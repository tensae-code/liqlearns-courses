# Hands-on — Ostwald's dilution law and weak-electrolyte calculations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Root drill
Take a weak acid with Ca = 0.1 mol/L and Ka = 1.8×10⁻⁵. Compute Ca·Ka, take the square root, and write [H₃O⁺] with its working shown — the chapter's method on fresh numbers.

## Paradox in a table
For the same Ka, compute α = √(Ka/C) at C = 1, 0.01 and 0.0001. Watch α grow as C falls, and write the one sentence that explains the table.
