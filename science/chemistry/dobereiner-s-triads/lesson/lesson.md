## Döbereiner's triads

Döbereiner (1829) grouped similar elements in threes — Li/Na/K, Ca/Sr/Ba, Cl/Br/I, S/Se/Te — finding the middle element's atomic weight is (about) the arithmetic mean of the other two, shown numerically for each triad. The scheme failed to generalise: only a few triads exist and new elements did not fit.
