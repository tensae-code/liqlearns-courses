# Domestic water treatment — and softening — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The town works — flocculate, filter, chlorinate, Treatment at home — filters, chlorine, boiling, The five softenings — taking the hardness out), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, walking a litre of river water through the town works and a litre of seawater through reverse osmosis step by step, and matching each of the five softenings to the hardness kind and setting it serves.
