# Domestic water treatment — and softening — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first step of large-scale treatment is…
a) Chlorination  b) Flocculation with alum and sedimentation  c) Distillation  d) Ion exchange
**Answer: b) Flocculation with alum and sedimentation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Town filtration beds are layers of…
a) Gravel, sand and charcoal  b) Zeolite and brine  c) Alum and lime  d) Fibre and carbon only
**Answer: a) Gravel, sand and charcoal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Chlorination at the town works uses chlorine at about…
a) 5%  b) 500 ppm  c) 5 ppm (or monochloramine)  d) Pure chlorine gas undiluted
**Answer: c) 5 ppm (or monochloramine)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A home fibre filter's job is to…
a) Disinfect the water  b) Strain out suspended solids  c) Soften the water  d) Add minerals
**Answer: b) Strain out suspended solids**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Washing soda softens water by…
a) Boiling it  b) Precipitating CaCO₃ from both kinds of hardness  c) Exchanging ions on a resin  d) Filtering it
**Answer: b) Precipitating CaCO₃ from both kinds of hardness**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The desalination workhorse for sea and brackish water is…
a) The solar still alone  b) Washing soda  c) Reverse osmosis  d) Alum flocculation
**Answer: c) Reverse osmosis**
