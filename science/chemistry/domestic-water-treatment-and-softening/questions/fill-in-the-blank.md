# Domestic water treatment — and softening — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The chemical that gathers fine suspended matter into sinking clumps is _____.
**Answer: alum**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A _____ filter adsorbs tastes and odours at home scale, as the town's charcoal layer did.
**Answer: carbon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A spent ion-exchange resin is brought back to life by regeneration with _____.
**Answer: brine**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Boiling cures only _____ hardness, and leaves limescale behind.
**Answer: temporary**
