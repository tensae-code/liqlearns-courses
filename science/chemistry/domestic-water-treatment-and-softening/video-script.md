Today: **Domestic water treatment and softening** — Dominica's CSEC water works: from river to tap, and hard to soft.

A town makes river water drinkable in three ordered acts: alum gathers the grit into sinking clumps, beds of gravel, sand and charcoal strain and sweeten what remains, and chlorine — five parts per million — kills what no filter can catch. At home the same ideas shrink to fibre and carbon filters and the boiling pot. And when the water is hard, five softenings stand ready, from the kettle and the washing soda to the zeolite resin that brine brings back to life — and out at the coast, reverse osmosis, pressing the sea itself into drinking water.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
