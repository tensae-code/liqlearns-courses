# Element symbols and chemical formulas — first steps — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Helium travelling alone is written _____.
**Answer: He**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The small low number that counts atoms is the _____.
**Answer: subscript**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Gold's symbol Au comes from the Latin _____.
**Answer: Aurum**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Mercury's symbol Hg comes from _____.
**Answer: Hydrargyrum**
