# Element symbols and chemical formulas — first steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The subscript in O₂ counts…
a) Molecules  b) Oxygen atoms in one molecule  c) Charges  d) Pages
**Answer: b) Oxygen atoms in one molecule**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Ozone is written…
a) O  b) O₂  c) O₃  d) 3O
**Answer: c) O₃**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Sulfur's molecule in the lesson is written…
a) S  b) S₂  c) S₆  d) S₈
**Answer: d) S₈**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The symbol Fe comes from the Latin…
a) Ferrum  b) Kalium  c) Natrium  d) Plumbum
**Answer: a) Ferrum**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Sodium's symbol Na comes from…
a) Natrium  b) Aurum  c) Hydrargyrum  d) Kalium
**Answer: a) Natrium**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A compound's formula is read…
a) As one word  b) Atom-by-atom, each symbol with its subscript  c) Backwards  d) Only by its first letter
**Answer: b) Atom-by-atom, each symbol with its subscript**
