# Hands-on — Element symbols and chemical formulas — first steps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Symbol cards (paper exercise)
Make cards for the six Latin-root symbols in the lesson: symbol on the front, Latin root and element name on the back. Shuffle and test yourself until all six are instant, then add He, O, Cl and S from the subscript set.

## Formula reading drill (paper exercise)
Write O₂, O₃, Cl₂ and S₈ in a row. Under each, write in words what one molecule holds. Then do the same for one compound formula a teacher gives you, reading it atom-by-atom exactly as the lesson does.
