Today: **Element symbols and chemical formulas — first steps** — chemistry's written language, the Syrian Grade 7 way.

A small low number counts atoms: O₂, O₃, Cl₂, S₈. Old elements keep Latin symbols — Fe for iron, from Ferrum; Au for gold, from Aurum. And a compound's formula is read atom-by-atom, until the molecule's whole make-up is spoken.

Symbols first — every later formula stands on this step.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
