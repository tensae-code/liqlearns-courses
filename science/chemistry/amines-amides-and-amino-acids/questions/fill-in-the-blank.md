# Amines, amides and amino acids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Amines are built from _____, NH₃, by replacing its hydrogens with carbon groups.
**Answer: ammonia**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The fishy smell of decaying fish is largely the amine _____.
**Answer: trimethylamine**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The diamide CO(NH₂)₂, used as a nitrogen fertiliser, is called _____.
**Answer: urea**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The simplest amino acid, aminoethanoic acid, is commonly called _____.
**Answer: glycine**
