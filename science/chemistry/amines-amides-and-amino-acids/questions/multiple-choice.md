# Amines, amides and amino acids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A primary amine is…
a) Ammonia with all three hydrogens replaced  b) Ammonia with one hydrogen replaced by a carbon group  c) A carbonyl joined to nitrogen  d) An amino acid with the acid group removed
**Answer: b) Ammonia with one hydrogen replaced by a carbon group**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Amines behave as bases because their nitrogen…
a) Has lost its lone pair  b) Keeps a lone pair of electrons that can accept a proton  c) Contains a C=O group  d) Is joined to three carbons
**Answer: b) Keeps a lone pair of electrons that can accept a proton**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The amide group is…
a) –NH₂  b) –COOH  c) –CONH₂  d) –OH
**Answer: c) –CONH₂**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ethanamide is named from, and prepared from, which acid?
a) Methanoic acid  b) Ethanoic acid, with ammonia, losing water  c) Propanoic acid  d) Ethanoic acid, with an alcohol, losing water
**Answer: b) Ethanoic acid, with ammonia, losing water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An amino acid is a molecule that carries…
a) Two amine groups  b) Two carboxylic-acid groups  c) One –NH₂ group and one –COOH group  d) One amide group only
**Answer: c) One –NH₂ group and one –COOH group**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) When amino acids join into a protein, they…
a) Gain a water molecule at each join  b) Lose a water molecule at each join, forming an amide (peptide) link  c) Lose ammonia at each join  d) Join through their carbon skeletons only
**Answer: b) Lose a water molecule at each join, forming an amide (peptide) link**
