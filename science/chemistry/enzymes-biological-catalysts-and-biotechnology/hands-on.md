# Hands-on — Enzymes — biological catalysts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Enzyme roll-call (paper only)
Six reactions are listed: maltose to glucose; sucrose to glucose and fructose; glucose to ethanol and carbon dioxide in brewing; starch toward maltose in digestion (name the class at work); urea hydrolysed by the enzyme specific to it; protein cut in the stomach. Assign each its NCERT enzyme from the lesson.

## Optimum detective (paper only)
A brewer's fermentation stalls in two different weeks: in week one the vat was kept at 45 °C; in week two a cleaning rinse left the vat slightly acidic. For each week, name the fault in enzyme terms — which condition left its optimum, and what happened to the zymase's shape in the worse case.

## Kitchen catalysis watch (kitchen materials only)
With an adult, set a cut apple's two halves side by side: leave one plain, and brush the other with lemon juice. Record both after thirty minutes. Browning is enzyme-driven (the apple's own oxidases at work in air); the acid shifts the surface pH away from their optimum. Say which of sub lesson 2's two working conditions the lemon juice attacks.
