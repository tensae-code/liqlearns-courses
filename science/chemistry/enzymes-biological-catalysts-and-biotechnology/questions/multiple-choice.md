# Enzymes — biological catalysts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An enzyme is…
a) A living cell  b) A globular protein that catalyses a biochemical reaction  c) A carbohydrate store  d) A mineral cofactor
**Answer: b) A globular protein that catalyses a biochemical reaction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The substrate is held during the reaction at the enzyme's…
a) Surface generally  b) Active site — a shaped hollow formed by the protein's folding  c) Carboxyl end  d) Coenzyme store
**Answer: b) Active site — a shaped hollow formed by the protein's folding**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The first step of NCERT's mechanism is…
a) The products leave  b) The substrate settles into the active site, forming an enzyme–substrate complex  c) The enzyme is used up  d) The pH changes
**Answer: b) The substrate settles into the active site, forming an enzyme–substrate complex**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Most human enzymes work best near…
a) 273 K and pH 2  b) 310 K and pH 7  c) 373 K and pH 7  d) Any temperature, once made
**Answer: b) 310 K and pH 7**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Boiling an enzyme solution ends its catalysis because…
a) The substrate evaporates  b) The protein denatures — the folded shape that forms the active site collapses  c) Water stops dissolving it  d) The enzyme boils away
**Answer: b) The protein denatures — the folded shape that forms the active site collapses**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Zymase is the enzyme complex that…
a) Splits sucrose  b) Hydrolyses urea  c) Ferments glucose to ethanol and carbon dioxide  d) Cuts proteins in the stomach
**Answer: c) Ferments glucose to ethanol and carbon dioxide**
