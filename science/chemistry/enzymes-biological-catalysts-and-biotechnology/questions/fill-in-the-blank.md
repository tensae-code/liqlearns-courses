# Enzymes — biological catalysts — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Enzymes are globular _____.
**Answer: proteins**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The substrate first forms an enzyme–_____ complex at the active site.
**Answer: substrate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Activity is greatest at the _____ temperature and pH.
**Answer: optimum**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The enzyme that ferments glucose in yeast is _____.
**Answer: zymase**
