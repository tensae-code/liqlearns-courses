# Ceramics — from clay to fired ware — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is the starting material?
**Answer: Clay**

**2. ** Why dry before firing?
**Answer: To remove water (avoid cracking/steam)**

**3. ** What hardens ware permanently?
**Answer: Firing in a kiln**

**4. ** What can finish the surface?
**Answer: Glaze**
