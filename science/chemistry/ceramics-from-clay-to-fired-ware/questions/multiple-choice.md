# Ceramics — from clay to fired ware — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the starting material?
a) Negative (spontaneous)  b) Clay  c) Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses  d) 0.00 V
**Answer: b) Clay**

**2. ** Why dry before firing?
a) To remove water (avoid cracking/steam)  b) The rise in average temperature  c) Redesign, reuse or efficiency in the plant  d) Three
**Answer: a) To remove water (avoid cracking/steam)**

**3. ** What hardens ware permanently?
a) Positive kernels  b) Its overlap concentrates density between nuclei  c) Gas  d) Firing in a kiln
**Answer: d) Firing in a kiln**

**4. ** What can finish the surface?
a) An ester and water  b) Dilute  c) Glaze  d) A silver mirror
**Answer: c) Glaze**
