# Video script — Ceramics — from clay to fired ware

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wet clay slumps; fired clay rings and holds water for years.

Ceramics begin with clay preparation — cleaning, kneading and mixing to a workable body.

The clay is shaped (thrown, moulded or pressed), then dried to remove water before firing.

Firing in a kiln at high temperature hardens the ware permanently; glazing may finish it.

Now you. Say it back: What is the starting material?
Answer: Clay.
