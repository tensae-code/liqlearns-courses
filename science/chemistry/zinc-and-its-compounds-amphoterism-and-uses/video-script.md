Today: **Zinc and its compounds** — Bulgaria's materials portrait. Zinc gives up electrons gladly, to Zn²⁺ — then seals itself under a carbonate skin, which is why galvanised iron survives the rain. Its oxide and hydroxide are amphoteric; its chloride dissolves cellulose into parchment paper; and your own body keeps two grams of it.
