# Zinc and its compounds — amphoterism and uses

## Do it first
A roof of galvanised iron outlives its owner in the rain, unrusted. The grey skin doing that work is zinc. Before reading, say out loud: why would a reactive metal protect better the more it reacts at its surface? Bulgaria's Grade 10 materials lesson answers with zinc's own chemistry.

## Sub lesson 1 — Zinc the metal

Zinc (**Z 30, electron arrangement 2 8 18 2, density 7.133**) was known in alloys since antiquity but isolated only in the **mid-18th century**. A **good reducer forming Zn²⁺**, it **skins over in air with a basic carbonate layer that blocks oxygen** — the reason galvanised roofing protects iron. Like aluminium it reacts with **acids and with alkali bases** (the hydrogen proven by the burning-splint pop), and **Zn + HCl is the laboratory's standard hydrogen preparation**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The compounds at work

**ZnO is white and amphoteric** — zinc white paint, pharmaceuticals, sunscreens and skin creams; **Zn(OH)₂ is likewise amphoteric** and decomposes to ZnO on heating. Concentrated **ZnCl₂ solutions dissolve starch, cellulose and silk** — the textile industry's trick and the making of **parchment paper**, whose dissolved surface seals the sheet translucent and waterproof, softened with glycerol. **ZnSO₄ impregnates timber against rot**; **ZnS with BaSO₄ makes the white paint lithopone**. The body holds **about 2 g of Zn²⁺** with a **~15 mg daily need** — deficiency stunts growth, excess poisons ('foundry fever') — and a sidebar warns that cadmium, zinc's group-mate, displaces it from enzymes and poisons up the food chain.

## The key ideas

- Zinc: Z 30, good reducer to Zn²⁺; its basic-carbonate skin blocks oxygen — galvanising. Zn + HCl is the standard laboratory hydrogen preparation.
- ZnO and Zn(OH)₂ are amphoteric. ZnCl₂ dissolves cellulose (parchment paper); ZnSO₄ preserves timber; ZnS + BaSO₄ = lithopone.
- The body holds ~2 g Zn²⁺, needing ~15 mg/day; cadmium is the warning in the margin.

## Do It Yourself
1. Why does galvanised iron not rust where the zinc skin is whole? (Zinc's surface seals itself with a basic carbonate layer that blocks oxygen, and the zinc coat stands between air, water and the iron.)
2. Zinc oxide is shaken with dilute acid, and separately with concentrated alkali; both dissolve it. What property is shown? (Amphoterism — ZnO reacts with both acids and bases.)
