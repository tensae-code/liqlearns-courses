# Zinc and its compounds — amphoterism and uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is zinc classed with aluminium in the lesson's reactions?
**Answer: Both react with acids and with alkali bases — zinc's hydrogen with alkali is proven by the burning-splint pop, as aluminium's is.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is parchment paper made, per the lesson?
**Answer: Concentrated ZnCl₂ solution dissolves the paper's surface cellulose; that dissolved layer seals the sheet translucent and waterproof, softened with glycerol.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) State zinc's place in the body, with both edges.
**Answer: About 2 g of Zn²⁺ in the body and ~15 mg needed daily: deficiency stunts growth, excess poisons — 'foundry fever'.**
