# Zinc and its compounds — amphoterism and uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Zinc's atomic number is…
a) 12  b) 30  c) 26  d) 48
**Answer: b) 30**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Zinc protects itself in air by…
a) Evaporating  b) A basic carbonate skin that blocks oxygen  c) Turning into ZnS  d) Melting
**Answer: b) A basic carbonate skin that blocks oxygen**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The laboratory's standard hydrogen preparation in the lesson is…
a) Zn + HCl  b) Electrolysis only  c) Mg + water  d) Cu + HCl
**Answer: a) Zn + HCl**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Zn(OH)₂ on heating decomposes to…
a) Zn and H₂O  b) ZnO  c) ZnCl₂  d) ZnS
**Answer: b) ZnO**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Lithopone, the white paint, is…
a) ZnO alone  b) ZnS with BaSO₄  c) ZnCl₂ solution  d) Pure ZnSO₄
**Answer: b) ZnS with BaSO₄**
