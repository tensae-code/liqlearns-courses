# Hands-on — Zinc and its compounds — amphoterism and uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Protection map
Draw a galvanised roof sheet in cross-section: iron core, zinc coat, carbonate skin. Label each layer with its job from the lesson — and add the scratch case: what the zinc does when the coat is cut (link forward to the protection lesson).

## Compound-at-work cards
Make five cards — ZnO, Zn(OH)₂, ZnCl₂, ZnSO₄, ZnS+BaSO₄ — each with the compound's property on the front and its work on the back (paint and creams; heating to ZnO; parchment paper; timber against rot; lithopone). Shuffle and test: property → work, work → property.
