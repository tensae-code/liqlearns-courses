# Hands-on — How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 7 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a lesson the Syrian way (paper exercise)
Open any Chemistry lesson in a Syrian-style course. Before reading the body, copy out its objectives and key words. After finishing, tick each objective you can now do and underline each key word you can explain without looking. Anything unticked is your revision list.

## Try and conclude (observation record)
Take one safe everyday change — ice melting, sugar dissolving in tea. Write two lines headed “I try” (what you did and saw) and one line headed “I conclude” (what it shows). Keep the two apart, exactly as the book does.
