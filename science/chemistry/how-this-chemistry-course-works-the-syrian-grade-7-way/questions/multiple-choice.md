# How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 7 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where does Syria's Grade 7 book state its curriculum design?
a) In the index  b) In its preface, printed p. 3  c) Only in the teacher's copy  d) On the back cover
**Answer: b) In its preface, printed p. 3**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The preface describes the curriculum as…
a) Pure memorisation  b) Research-and-experiment, tied to daily life and the local environment  c) Examination drilling only  d) Reading without practical work
**Answer: b) Research-and-experiment, tied to daily life and the local environment**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Every Chemistry lesson in the book opens with…
a) A test  b) Printed objectives and key words  c) A story  d) The answers
**Answer: b) Printed objectives and key words**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Objectives tell the learner…
a) The page numbers  b) What they will be able to do by the end  c) The teacher's name  d) Last year's marks
**Answer: b) What they will be able to do by the end**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In an “I try and I conclude” experiment, the learner first…
a) Reads the conclusion  b) Copies the answer  c) Tries the experiment and watches what happens  d) Closes the book
**Answer: c) Tries the experiment and watches what happens**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The conclusion of an experiment is stated in…
a) The glossary  b) An explicit “I conclude” box  c) The next book  d) A footnote only
**Answer: b) An explicit “I conclude” box**
