# How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 7 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What kind of curriculum does the Grade 7 preface describe?
**Answer: A research-and-experiment curriculum, tied to daily life and the local environment — chemistry learned by investigating and experimenting, on examples drawn from the learner's own world.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What two things open every Chemistry lesson, and what does each one do?
**Answer: Printed objectives — what the learner will be able to do by the end — and key words — the lesson's own vocabulary, named before the teaching starts.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does an “I try and I conclude” experiment run, in order?
**Answer: The learner tries the bench experiment first and watches what happens; only then is the finding stated, in an explicit “I conclude” box that can be checked against what the trying showed.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) What completes the lesson's shape after the experiments?
**Answer: Worked applications, enrichment boxes, unit questions and a closing hands-on project — the idea is applied, stretched, tested and used.**
