# Hands-on — Transition elements — the B-groups survey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tour map (paper only)
Draw the B block as the book tours it: IB to VIIIB in order, VIIIB drawn as its three horizontal rows. Under each group write its one personality line from the lesson. Star the two groups whose personalities are mechanisms, not adjectives (VIB's skin; IB's variability), and write the mechanism under the star.

## Echo table (paper only)
From the guide's bridge [teacher evidence], copy three A/B echo pairs (e.g. ClO₄⁻ against MnO₄⁻; NaCl against CuCl and AgCl) and write one sentence on what an 'echo' means here: same-numbered groups, similar formulas — the B group behaving like a heavier shadow of the A.
