# Transition elements — the B-groups survey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The coinage metals of group IB show…
a) Variable valency — +1, +2, +3  b) Fixed +1 only  c) Only −1  d) No compounds
**Answer: a) Variable valency — +1, +2, +3**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Zinc, cadmium and mercury (IIB) hold which oxidation state?
a) +1 only  b) +2 only (mercury also Hg₂²⁺)  c) +3 only  d) 0 only
**Answer: b) +2 only (mercury also Hg₂²⁺)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The most electropositive transition group in the tour is…
a) IB  b) VIIB  c) IIIB  d) VIIIB
**Answer: c) IIIB**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Chromium's corrosion resistance comes from…
a) Its softness  b) Its colour  c) Dissolving in acids  d) An oxide skin sealing the surface
**Answer: d) An oxide skin sealing the surface**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Manganese's group is VIIB, with oxidation states…
a) From +2 to +7  b) Only +2  c) From −1 to +1  d) Only +7
**Answer: a) From +2 to +7**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) By the guide's test, an element is transitional if its d or f sub-shell is… [teacher evidence]
a) Completely filled always  b) Partially filled, in the free state or in a compound  c) Empty  d) In the p block
**Answer: b) Partially filled, in the free state or in a compound**
