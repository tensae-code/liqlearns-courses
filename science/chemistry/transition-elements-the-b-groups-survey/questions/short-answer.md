# Transition elements — the B-groups survey — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Walk VIIIB: name its three horizontal families and the platinum metals' two uses in the tour.
**Answer: Iron–cobalt–nickel; ruthenium–rhodium–palladium; osmium–iridium–platinum. The platinum metals serve jewellery and laboratory ware.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What two configuration exceptions does the first-series table show, and which rows are tabled separately?
**Answer: Chromium and copper carry 4s¹ instead of 4s². The lanthanides (4f) and actinides (5f) — the inner-transition series — are tabled separately.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) List the guide's four characteristic transition traits. [teacher evidence]
**Answer: Several oxidation states from one element; coloured compounds (most transition compounds are coloured); paramagnetism from unpaired d/f electrons; a strong tendency to form complex ions.**
