Today: **The mole — counting by weighing**.

A mole is the amount containing as many entities as there are atoms in exactly 12 g of carbon-12: 6.022×10²³, Avogadro's number.

One mole of any two substances contains equal numbers of particles (but different masses).

Conversions run in all directions: moles ↔ mass (via molar mass), moles ↔ particles, with worked examples (moles in 176 g of CO₂ is a review target).

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
