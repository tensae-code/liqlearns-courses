# The mole — counting by weighing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about the mole — counting by weighing? Use the lesson's own example.
**Answer: Conversions run in all directions: moles ↔ mass (via molar mass), moles ↔ particles, with worked examples (moles in 176 g of CO₂ is a review target).**

**2. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about the mole — counting by weighing? Use the lesson's own example.
**Answer: A mole is the amount containing as many entities as there are atoms in exactly 12 g of carbon-12: 6.022×10²³, Avogadro's number.**

**3. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about the mole — counting by weighing? Use the lesson's own example.
