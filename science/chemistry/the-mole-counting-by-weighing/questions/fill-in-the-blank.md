# The mole — counting by weighing — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** One mole of any two substances contains equal _____ of particles (but different masses).
**Answer: numbers**

**2. ** Conversions run in all directions: moles ↔ mass (via molar mass), moles ↔ _____, with worked examples (moles in 176 g of CO₂ is a review target).
**Answer: particles**

**3. ** A mole is the amount containing as many entities as there are atoms in _____ 12 g of carbon-12: 6.022×10²³, Avogadro's number.
**Answer: exactly**

**4. ** One mole of any two substances contains equal _____ of particles (but different masses).
**Answer: numbers**
