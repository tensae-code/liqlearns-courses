# Coordinate covalent bonds — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** once formed it is indistinguishable from an _____ covalent bond.
**Answer: ordinary**

**2. ** Worked formations include carbon monoxide and the hydronium ion, plus _____ chloride existing as the covalent dimer Al₂Cl₆;
**Answer: aluminium**

**3. ** the review set adds the ammonium ion, whose fourth N–H bond is _____ (as is the third O–H bond in hydronium).
**Answer: coordinate**

**4. ** A covalent bond in which both shared electrons come from one atom: the donor _____ a lone pair to an electron-poor partner;
**Answer: supplies**
