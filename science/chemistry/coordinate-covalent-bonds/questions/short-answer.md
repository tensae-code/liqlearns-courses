# Coordinate covalent bonds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about coordinate covalent bonds? Use the lesson's own example.
**Answer: Worked formations include carbon monoxide and the hydronium ion, plus aluminium chloride existing as the covalent dimer Al₂Cl₆;**

**2. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about coordinate covalent bonds? Use the lesson's own example.
**Answer: the review set adds the ammonium ion, whose fourth N–H bond is coordinate (as is the third O–H bond in hydronium).**

**3. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about coordinate covalent bonds? Use the lesson's own example.
**Answer: A covalent bond in which both shared electrons come from one atom: the donor supplies a lone pair to an electron-poor partner;**
