Today: **Coordinate covalent bonds**.

A covalent bond in which both shared electrons come from one atom: the donor supplies a lone pair to an electron-poor partner;

once formed it is indistinguishable from an ordinary covalent bond.

Worked formations include carbon monoxide and the hydronium ion, plus aluminium chloride existing as the covalent dimer Al₂Cl₆;

the review set adds the ammonium ion, whose fourth N–H bond is coordinate (as is the third O–H bond in hydronium).
