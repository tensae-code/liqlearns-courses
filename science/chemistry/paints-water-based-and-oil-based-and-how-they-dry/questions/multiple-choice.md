# Paints — water-based and oil-based, and how they dry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Paints are divided in the lesson by their…
a) Solvent — water-based against oil-based  b) Colour  c) Price  d) Country
**Answer: a) Solvent — water-based against oil-based**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A water-based paint dries by…
a) Oxygen cross-linking  b) Evaporation of its water  c) Burning  d) Freezing
**Answer: b) Evaporation of its water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The classic solvent of oil paints is…
a) Water  b) Alcohol  c) Turpentine  d) Acetone
**Answer: c) Turpentine**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Oil paint's chemical drying stitches its chains with atoms of…
a) Nitrogen  b) Carbon dioxide  c) Hydrogen  d) Oxygen from the air
**Answer: d) Oxygen from the air**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Lead pigments were banned in many countries from…
a) 1977, after child poisonings and deaths  b) 1900, for cost  c) 2000, for colour  d) 1950, by painters' unions
**Answer: a) 1977, after child poisonings and deaths**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Barium sulfate in a paint serves as an…
a) Pigment  b) Extender  c) Solvent  d) Binder
**Answer: b) Extender**
