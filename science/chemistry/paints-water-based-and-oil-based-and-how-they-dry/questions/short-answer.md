# Paints — water-based and oil-based, and how they dry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three polymer families of the water-based paints.
**Answer: Styrene–butadiene emulsions, polyvinyl acetate, and acrylics (polymerised from acrylonitrile) — with strong adhesion and rust resistance.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Contrast the two dryings in two sentences.
**Answer: A water paint dries physically: its water evaporates and the film sticks. An oil paint dries in two stages — the solvent evaporates, then the oil dries chemically as oxygen stitches its unsaturated chains into a hard cross-linked film.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give the white pigment, the banned pigment family with its compounds, and the two extenders.
**Answer: White: titanium dioxide. Banned: the lead compounds PbCO₃, Pb(OH)₂, PbSO₄. Extenders: barium sulfate and barium carbonate.**
