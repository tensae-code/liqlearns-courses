# Paints — water-based and oil-based, and how they dry — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Water-based paints are polymer _____ in water.
**Answer: emulsions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Oil paints' classic solvent is _____.
**Answer: turpentine**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An oil paint's film hardens as _____ stitches its chains together.
**Answer: oxygen**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Titanium _____ gives the modern white pigment.
**Answer: dioxide**
