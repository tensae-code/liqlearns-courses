# Hands-on — Paints — water-based and oil-based, and how they dry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two doors (paper only)
Write the two doors' diaries in parallel columns, hour by hour in words: what leaves the water paint, what leaves then joins the oil paint, and when each film can be touched. End each diary with the kind of change its drying was — physical or chemical — and the evidence.

## Label audit (paper only)
Draft the honest label for a modern water-based emulsion paint from the lesson: binder family, white pigment, extender, drying mechanism, and the one historical pigment family that must NOT be in it — with the year and reason of its ban.
