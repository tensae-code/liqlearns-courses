# Protein structure — four levels and insulin's two chains — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing China's book teaches in this lesson — point 1 of the key ideas.
**Answer: Protein architecture in levels: primary sequence; hydrogen-bonded coils/sheets (secondary); folding (tertiary); multi-chain assemblies (quaternary) — with insulin as the fully-known case (A chain 21 + B chain 30 amino acids, joined by disulfide bridges; first protein ever sequenced); proteins stay amphoteric, hydrolyse stepwise to amino acids (digestion), denature and salt out (linked back to Bk 5), and enzymes are introduced as protein catalysts of extreme specificity and mildness.**
