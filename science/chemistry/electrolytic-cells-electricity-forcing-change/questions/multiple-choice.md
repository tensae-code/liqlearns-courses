# Electrolytic cells — electricity forcing change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which electrode do cations move to?
a) In fixed whole-number ratios  b) A tiny, dense, positive centre exists  c) The proton number Z  d) The cathode
**Answer: d) The cathode**

**2. ** What sign is the electrolytic cathode?
a) A manometer  b) Resistance to flow  c) Negative  d) Diamond
**Answer: c) Negative**

**3. ** What happens at the anode?
a) Flavours, perfumes or solvents  b) Anions are oxidised  c) Its C=C double bond(s)  d) Ethyl ethanoate
**Answer: b) Anions are oxidised**

**4. ** What drives an electrolytic cell?
a) An external power source  b) Purifies a metal at the cathode  c) A cell potential depending on [H₃O⁺]  d) It regrows or refills
**Answer: a) An external power source**
