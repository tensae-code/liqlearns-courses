# Electrolytic cells — electricity forcing change — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which electrode do cations move to?
**Answer: The cathode**

**2. ** What sign is the electrolytic cathode?
**Answer: Negative**

**3. ** What happens at the anode?
**Answer: Anions are oxidised**

**4. ** What drives an electrolytic cell?
**Answer: An external power source**
