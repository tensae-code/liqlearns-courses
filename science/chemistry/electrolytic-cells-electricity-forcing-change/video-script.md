# Video script — Electrolytic cells — electricity forcing change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some decompositions will never happen alone — pass a current and they are forced.

Electrolysis is the decomposition of an electrolyte by an electric current, in an electrolytic cell driven by an external power source.

The cathode is the negative electrode: cations (positive ions) move to it and are reduced there.

Ion movement through the melt or solution and electron flow through the wires complete the circuit.

Now you. Say it back: Which electrode do cations move to?
Answer: The cathode.
