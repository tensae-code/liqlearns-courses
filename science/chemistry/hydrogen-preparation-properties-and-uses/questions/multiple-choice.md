# Hydrogen — preparation, properties and uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hydrogen on Earth is found…
a) Free, as a gas, everywhere  b) Almost always combined — in water, acids and living matter  c) Only in the Sun  d) As a solid metal
**Answer: b) Almost always combined — in water, acids and living matter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Hydrogen gas is…
a) Heavier than air  b) Coloured green  c) The least dense substance known, about fourteen times lighter than air  d) Odourless but dense
**Answer: c) The least dense substance known, about fourteen times lighter than air**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The standard laboratory preparation of hydrogen is…
a) Heating water  b) Zinc with dilute hydrochloric acid  c) Burning methane  d) Electrolysis of brine only
**Answer: b) Zinc with dilute hydrochloric acid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The test that proves a gas is hydrogen is…
a) It turns limewater milky  b) A glowing splint relights  c) A lighted splint gives a sharp pop  d) It smells of almonds
**Answer: c) A lighted splint gives a sharp pop**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) When hydrogen burns in oxygen, the only product is…
a) Carbon dioxide  b) Water  c) Hydrogen peroxide  d) Steam and soot
**Answer: b) Water**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O, hydrogen is acting as…
a) An acid  b) A catalyst  c) A reducing agent  d) An oxidising agent
**Answer: c) A reducing agent**
