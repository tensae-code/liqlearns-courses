# Hydrogen — preparation, properties and uses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The lightest of all elements, first in the periodic system, is _____.
**Answer: hydrogen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Zinc and dilute hydrochloric acid give zinc chloride and _____.
**Answer: hydrogen (H₂)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The sharp sound hydrogen makes at a lighted splint is called the _____ test.
**Answer: pop**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Hydrogen passed over heated copper(II) oxide _____ the oxide to copper metal.
**Answer: reduces**
