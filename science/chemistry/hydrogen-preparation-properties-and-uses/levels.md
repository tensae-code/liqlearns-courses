# Hydrogen — preparation, properties and uses — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The lightest element — everywhere and nowhere free, Preparing hydrogen — and proving it is hydrogen, Properties and uses — the reducing gas that makes water), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, writing the preparation and reduction equations from memory, and matching each use of hydrogen to the property it rides on.
