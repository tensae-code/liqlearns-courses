Today: **Hydrogen — preparation, properties and uses** — Belarus's way: a full section for the first element — prepared, proved, and put to work.

Start with the paradox — the commonest element in the universe, and you have never met it free. On Earth hydrogen hides: in water, in every acid, in living things. Stars are made of it; we have to dig it out of compounds.
The laboratory digs it out with a metal and a dilute acid — zinc and hydrochloric acid — and proves the gas by its own test: a lighted splint, and a sharp pop. No other common gas answers that way.
And the free gas earns its keep: it burns to water and nothing else, it strips oxygen out of hot metal oxides, and it lifts balloons, feeds the ammonia factories and hardens oils. One element, one section, whole.
