# Nature's cycles — water, carbon, nitrogen

## Do it first
The nitrogen in your protein was once in the air, then in a root nodule, then in soil. Before reading, trace one atom's loop for one of the great cycles.

[Picture 21777 — The nitrogen cycle over farmland.]

The water cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection move water through the spheres, with no chemistry lost.

The carbon cycle: photosynthesis takes CO₂ into living matter; respiration, decay and combustion return it.

The nitrogen cycle: nitrogen-fixing bacteria convert N₂ into usable compounds; plants build proteins; decay and denitrifying bacteria return N₂ to the air. The oxygen cycle runs coupled to carbon's. Each step's chemistry is the section's content.
