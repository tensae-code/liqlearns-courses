# Nature's cycles — water, carbon, nitrogen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What do nitrogen-fixing bacteria do?
a) Yes — it is one of the three factors  b) Convert N₂ into usable compounds  c) A pH range of about two units  d) Takes electrons (is itself reduced)
**Answer: b) Convert N₂ into usable compounds**

**2. ** What takes CO₂ into living matter?
a) Photosynthesis  b) The monomer units in the chain  c) The atmosphere  d) Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms
**Answer: a) Photosynthesis**

**3. ** What returns CO₂ to the air?
a) To become more stable  b) Removing electrons from atoms  c) Electron pairs repel and spread apart  d) Respiration, decay and combustion
**Answer: d) Respiration, decay and combustion**

**4. ** Name the water cycle's stages.
a) Alkoxyalkane  b) The carbonyl group, C=O  c) Evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection  d) Hydrogen bonding between molecules
**Answer: c) Evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection**
