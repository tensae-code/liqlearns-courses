# Nature's cycles — water, carbon, nitrogen — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What do nitrogen-fixing bacteria do?
**Answer: Convert N₂ into usable compounds**

**2. ** What takes CO₂ into living matter?
**Answer: Photosynthesis**

**3. ** What returns CO₂ to the air?
**Answer: Respiration, decay and combustion**

**4. ** Name the water cycle's stages.
**Answer: Evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection**
