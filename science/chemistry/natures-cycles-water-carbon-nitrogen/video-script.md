# Video script — Nature's cycles — water, carbon, nitrogen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The nitrogen in your protein was once in the air, then in a root nodule, then in soil.

The water cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection move water through the spheres, with no chemistry lost.

The carbon cycle: photosynthesis takes CO₂ into living matter; respiration, decay and combustion return it.

The nitrogen cycle: nitrogen-fixing bacteria convert N₂ into usable compounds; plants build proteins; decay and denitrifying bacteria return N₂ to the air.

Now you. Say it back: What do nitrogen-fixing bacteria do?
Answer: Convert N₂ into usable compounds.
