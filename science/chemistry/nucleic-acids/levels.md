# Nucleic acids — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Building blocks — bases, sugars, nucleotides, The double helix — pairing rules and the two strands, What nucleic acids do — storing, copying, directing), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, building nucleotides from their three parts for both nucleic acids, writing complementary strands by the pairing rules, and separating the jobs of mRNA, rRNA and tRNA.
