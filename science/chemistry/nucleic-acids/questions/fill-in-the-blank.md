# Nucleic acids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Adenine and guanine are the _____ bases.
**Answer: purine**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Nucleotides are joined into a chain by _____ linkages.
**Answer: phosphodiester**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The DNA structure was proposed by Watson and _____.
**Answer: Crick**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) DNA copying itself before cell division is called _____.
**Answer: replication**
