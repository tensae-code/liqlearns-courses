# Nucleic acids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A nucleotide consists of…
a) Base + sugar only  b) A nitrogen base, a pentose sugar and a phosphate group  c) Three phosphates  d) An amino acid and a base
**Answer: b) A nitrogen base, a pentose sugar and a phosphate group**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The sugar of DNA is…
a) β-D-ribose  b) Glucose  c) β-D-2-deoxyribose  d) Fructose
**Answer: c) β-D-2-deoxyribose**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Uracil is found…
a) In DNA only  b) In RNA only — it takes thymine's place there  c) In both  d) In neither
**Answer: b) In RNA only — it takes thymine's place there**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the double helix, guanine pairs with cytosine through…
a) One hydrogen bond  b) Two hydrogen bonds  c) Three hydrogen bonds  d) A phosphodiester link
**Answer: c) Three hydrogen bonds**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The two strands of DNA are described as complementary because…
a) They are identical  b) Each strand's sequence completely determines the other's by the pairing rules  c) They run in the same direction  d) They contain the same sugar as RNA
**Answer: b) Each strand's sequence completely determines the other's by the pairing rules**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The RNA that fetches amino acids to the growing protein is…
a) mRNA  b) rRNA  c) tRNA  d) DNA
**Answer: c) tRNA**
