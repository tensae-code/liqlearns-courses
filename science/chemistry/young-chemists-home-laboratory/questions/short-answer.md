# The young chemist's home laboratory — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What makes the home laboratory a programme rather than a pile of tricks?
**Answer:** It is a named, planned set of safe home experiments with a safety frame, given its own place in the teacher guide — the experiments are chosen, not improvised.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the foam show in the hydrogen peroxide experiment?
**Answer:** The foam catches the gas given off as the hydrogen peroxide decomposes, making the gas of the decomposition reaction visible.
