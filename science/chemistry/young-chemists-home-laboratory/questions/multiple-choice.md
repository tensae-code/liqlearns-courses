# The young chemist's home laboratory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The young chemist's home laboratory is…
a) A shop that sells chemicals  b) A named programme of safe home experiments in the Grade 8 teacher guide  c) A television programme  d) The school laboratory's store room
**Answer: b) A named programme of safe home experiments in the Grade 8 teacher guide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's source is…
a) The Grade 8 student textbook  b) The Grade 8 teacher guide (teacher evidence)  c) The Grade 9 student textbook  d) The Grade 7 student textbook
**Answer: b) The Grade 8 teacher guide (teacher evidence)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The foam experiment of the programme is…
a) Burning hydrogen  b) The decomposition of hydrogen peroxide, its gas caught in foam  c) Making soap from fat  d) Freezing oxygen
**Answer: b) The decomposition of hydrogen peroxide, its gas caught in foam**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The programme's reagent shelf is…
a) Bought laboratory chemicals  b) Starch and other kitchen reagents — what a home already holds  c) Anything found in a garage  d) Medicines from the cabinet
**Answer: b) Starch and other kitchen reagents — what a home already holds**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The home laboratory runs…
a) By improvising freely  b) Inside the programme's safety frame, with named experiments  c) Only during examinations  d) Without any adult knowledge
**Answer: b) Inside the programme's safety frame, with named experiments**
