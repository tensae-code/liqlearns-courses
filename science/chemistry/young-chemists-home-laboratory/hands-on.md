# Hands-on — The young chemist's home laboratory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the shelf
List the kitchen substances the programme's strand names (starch first) and one more your teacher approves. For each, say one safe observation you could make with it at home — looking only, no mixing of cleaners or chemicals, ever.

## Watch, do not touch
With a grown-up present and doing any lighting, observe a natural-gas burner flame from a safe distance: its colour, its shape, where it is hottest by its look. Record three observations; that record is the experiment.
