# Composite materials — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A composite material is made by _____ two or more different materials.
**Answer: combining**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In reinforced concrete, the bars that carry the pulling force are made of _____.
**Answer: steel**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Fibre optics guide _____ along hair-thin glass fibres.
**Answer: light**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Photochromic glass _____ in bright light and clears in shade.
**Answer: darkens**
