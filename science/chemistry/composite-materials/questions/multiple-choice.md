# Composite materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A composite material is…
a) A single pure element  b) Two or more different materials combined so the combination does a job neither part does as well alone  c) Any melted metal  d) A material that dissolves in water
**Answer: b) Two or more different materials combined so the combination does a job neither part does as well alone**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a composite, the parts…
a) React away into one new substance  b) Melt into each other completely  c) Keep their own identities and strengths inside the combination  d) Disappear
**Answer: c) Keep their own identities and strengths inside the combination**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Reinforced concrete works because…
a) Concrete alone is strong under pulling  b) Steel bars inside the concrete carry the pulling (tension) while the concrete carries the squeezing (compression)  c) The steel dissolves into the concrete  d) It is painted grey
**Answer: b) Steel bars inside the concrete carry the pulling (tension) while the concrete carries the squeezing (compression)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Fibre optics carry signals by…
a) Electric current in copper  b) Sound travelling through glass  c) Light guided along hair-thin glass fibres  d) Heat moving along the cable
**Answer: c) Light guided along hair-thin glass fibres**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Photochromic glass is special because it…
a) Never breaks  b) Darkens in bright light and clears again in shade  c) Is the cheapest glass  d) Conducts electricity
**Answer: b) Darkens in bright light and clears again in shade**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) As Malaysia presents them in this family, superconductors carry electric current…
a) With extra resistance  b) Only in daylight  c) Without resistance, wasting no energy as heat on the way  d) More slowly than any wire
**Answer: c) Without resistance, wasting no energy as heat on the way**
