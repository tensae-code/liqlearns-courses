Today: **Composite materials** — the closing idea of Malaysia's manufactured-substances chapter.

A composite is a partnership. Concrete is strong when squeezed and weak when pulled; steel is strong when pulled — lock steel bars inside concrete and the pair carries bridges. Draw glass into hair-thin fibres and it stops being a window and starts carrying light and signals. Mix light-sensitive material into glass and the lens shades itself in the sun. Combine, and each part does the job it is best at.

One material rarely does everything. A composite is what you build when the job needs two strengths at once.
