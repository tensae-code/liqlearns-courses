# Titrations and their curves — Unit 1 review — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the equivalence point?
**Answer: Where equivalent amounts have exactly reacted**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Strong base into weak acid: equivalence pH is…
**Answer: Above 7**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** Which pairing has no sharp jump?
**Answer: Weak acid–weak base**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** What instrument delivers the titrant?
**Answer: A burette**
