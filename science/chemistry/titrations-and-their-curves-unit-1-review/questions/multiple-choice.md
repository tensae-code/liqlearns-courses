# Titrations and their curves — Unit 1 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is the equivalence point?
a) By-products  b) Vanadium(V) oxide  c) Where equivalent amounts have exactly reacted  d) Electricity and water
**Answer: c) Where equivalent amounts have exactly reacted**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Strong base into weak acid: equivalence pH is…
a) E = hν  b) Above 7  c) The atoms themselves change  d) Fields do not bend them — they carry no charge
**Answer: b) Above 7**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** Which pairing has no sharp jump?
a) Weak acid–weak base  b) Solid → liquid → gas  c) None  d) Crystalline
**Answer: a) Weak acid–weak base**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** What instrument delivers the titrant?
a) Soaps  b) Only slightly soluble  c) Three  d) A burette
**Answer: d) A burette**
