# Soil — composition, properties and pH — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The dark organic matter in soil, from dead plants and animals, is called _____.
**Answer: humus**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The soil property set by particle size — how fast water runs through — is its _____.
**Answer: drainage**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Soil pH measures the _____ of the water held in the soil.
**Answer: acidity**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Soil shaken in water settles into _____ that reveal its composition.
**Answer: layers**
