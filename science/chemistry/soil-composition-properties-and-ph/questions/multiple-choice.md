# Soil — composition, properties and pH — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Soil is best described as…
a) A single pure substance  b) A mixture of mineral particles, humus, water, air and living things  c) Only broken rock  d) Only dead plants
**Answer: b) A mixture of mineral particles, humus, water, air and living things**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Shaking soil in water and letting it settle shows…
a) That soil dissolves completely  b) Layers — coarse grains settling first, finer particles above, the lightest matter last  c) That soil is one substance  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: b) Layers — coarse grains settling first, finer particles above, the lightest matter last**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A sandy soil drains quickly because…
a) It has no particles  b) Its coarse grains leave wide spaces that water runs through  c) It is always dry  d) Clay repels water
**Answer: b) Its coarse grains leave wide spaces that water runs through**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The dark organic share of soil, humus, matters because…
a) It makes soil heavier only  b) It is the store of nutrients plants draw on, and darkens the soil  c) It stops all drainage  d) It is the soil's air
**Answer: b) It is the store of nutrients plants draw on, and darkens the soil**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Soil pH is measured by…
a) Guessing from the soil's colour  b) Testing a soil-and-water mixture with an indicator or a pH meter  c) Weighing the soil  d) Burning the soil
**Answer: b) Testing a soil-and-water mixture with an indicator or a pH meter**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A farmer wants the soil's pH number because…
a) It steers which nutrients in the soil plants can actually take up  b) It tells the soil's weight  c) It replaces watering  d) It names the soil
**Answer: a) It steers which nutrients in the soil plants can actually take up**
