# Radioactivity and Rutherford's nucleus

## Do it first
Fire thousands of tiny heavy bullets at a sheet of gold so thin it is almost nothing. You expect nearly all to pass straight through. A few bounce straight back. What must be inside the sheet?

[Picture 29256 — Most pass through — a few meet the nucleus.]

Becquerel and others found that some atoms give off radiation on their own — radioactivity — in three kinds: alpha, beta and gamma.

Rutherford fired alpha particles at gold foil. Most passed straight through, so an atom is mostly empty space.

A few alpha particles bounced back sharply. Something tiny, dense and positive must sit at the atom's centre.

That centre is the nucleus: nearly all the atom's mass in a very small volume.
