# Video script — Radioactivity and Rutherford's nucleus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fire thousands of tiny heavy bullets at a sheet of gold so thin it is almost nothing.

Becquerel and others found that some atoms give off radiation on their own — radioactivity — in three kinds: alpha, beta and gamma.

Rutherford fired alpha particles at gold foil.

A few alpha particles bounced back sharply.

That centre is the nucleus: nearly all the atom's mass in a very small volume.

Now you. Say it back: Name the three kinds of radiation.
Answer: Alpha, beta and gamma.
