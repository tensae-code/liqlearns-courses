# Radioactivity and Rutherford's nucleus — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name the three kinds of radiation.
**Answer: Alpha, beta and gamma**

**2. ** What did most alpha particles do in the gold-foil experiment?
**Answer: Passed straight through**

**3. ** What does a sharp bounce-back tell us?
**Answer: A tiny, dense, positive centre exists**

**4. ** What is the atom's dense centre called?
**Answer: The nucleus**
