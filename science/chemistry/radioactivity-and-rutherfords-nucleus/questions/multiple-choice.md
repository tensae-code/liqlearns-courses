# Radioactivity and Rutherford's nucleus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name the three kinds of radiation.
a) Alpha, beta and gamma  b) R–O–R  c) Strong acids (HBr/HI)  d) Carboxylic acids
**Answer: a) Alpha, beta and gamma**

**2. ** What did most alpha particles do in the gold-foil experiment?
a) Comparing Ka and Kb  b) Above 7  c) Anions are oxidised  d) Passed straight through
**Answer: d) Passed straight through**

**3. ** What does a sharp bounce-back tell us?
a) Rubber, cellulose or protein  b) The pollution lessons  c) A tiny, dense, positive centre exists  d) Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet
**Answer: c) A tiny, dense, positive centre exists**

**4. ** What is the atom's dense centre called?
a) Its ions are locked in place  b) The nucleus  c) To predict new elements' properties  d) It rises from 1 to 8
**Answer: b) The nucleus**
