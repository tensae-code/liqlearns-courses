# Radioactivity and Rutherford's nucleus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Name the three kinds of radiation. — Alpha, beta and gamma
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What did most alpha particles do in the gold-foil experiment — Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What does a sharp bounce-back tell us — A tiny, dense, positive centre exists
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What is the atom's dense centre called — PET or nylon
**Answer: False**
