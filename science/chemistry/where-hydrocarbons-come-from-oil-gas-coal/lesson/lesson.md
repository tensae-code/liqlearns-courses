## Where hydrocarbons come from

The three great sources are compared: petroleum (crude oil — the main source, its hydrocarbons chiefly alkanes/cycloalkanes), natural gas (mainly methane, with biogas as its renewable twin), and coal. The section's questions fix the judgements: which fraction of petroleum is in highest demand (the petrol/gasoline range), and why a country's natural gas matters economically.
