# Lesson plan — Group 15 elements in technology

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Group 15 elements in technology
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Group 15 elements in technology in Egypt's Grade 11 student book (Chapter Two, STS section, printed pp. 52–55): Group 5A at work, one spread per element: liquid nitrogen in cryotherapy and storing biological samples; phosphorus into blue LEDs and high-refractive-index optical glass with anti-reflection lens coatings; gallium arsenide (arsenic) in phone solar panels and lasers; antimony(III) oxide as a flame retardant; bismuth in low-melting alloys for fire sprinklers and in lead-free solder.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. No other lesson is needed first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Nitrogen and phosphorus — cold and light** — **Liquid nitrogen** works by its cold: **cryotherapy** in medicine and **storing biological samples** that must not change. **Phosphorus** works in light and glass: into **blue LEDs**, and into **high-refractive-index optical glass** whose lenses carry **anti-reflection coatings** — the group's nonmetals at the two ends of the thermometer and the lens.
2. **Arsenic and antimony — panels, lasers and fire safety** — **Arsenic**, as **gallium arsenide**, serves in **phone solar panels and lasers** — a semiconductor job no silicon-free lesson should skip. **Antimony(III) oxide** is a **flame retardant**: the heavy pnictogen's compound slows burning in the materials around us.
3. **Bismuth — the gentle heavyweight** — **Bismuth** closes the spread in **low-melting alloys for fire sprinklers** — the alloy melts early in a fire and lets the water go — and in **lead-free solder**, doing lead's old job without lead's poison. Down the group the character has turned metallic, and the uses turn with it.
