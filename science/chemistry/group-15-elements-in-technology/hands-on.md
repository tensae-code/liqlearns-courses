# Hands-on — Group 15 elements in technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One spread each
Make a five-row table: element, technology, the property that fits it (cold, light-bending, semiconducting, fire-slowing, low-melting). Fill it from the lesson, then cover the technology column and recall each from the element's name.

## Down the group
Order the five elements down the group and say, for each step, whether the use you wrote is more nonmetal-like or metal-like. Compare with the lesson's closing point.
