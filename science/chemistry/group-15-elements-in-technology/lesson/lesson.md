# Group 15 elements in technology

## Do it first
Before you read: one group of the table freezes warts off skin, lights phone screens, sprinkles water on fires and solders pipes — without any of its members being a metal you'd build with. Say out loud: which group could that be? Then read on: Egypt's STS section walks group 5A (group 15), one element, one technology at a time.

## Sub lesson 1 — Nitrogen and phosphorus — cold and light

**Liquid nitrogen** works by its cold: **cryotherapy** in medicine and **storing biological samples** that must not change. **Phosphorus** works in light and glass: into **blue LEDs**, and into **high-refractive-index optical glass** whose lenses carry **anti-reflection coatings** — the group's nonmetals at the two ends of the thermometer and the lens.

## Sub lesson 2 — Arsenic and antimony — panels, lasers and fire safety

**Arsenic**, as **gallium arsenide**, serves in **phone solar panels and lasers** — a semiconductor job no silicon-free lesson should skip. **Antimony(III) oxide** is a **flame retardant**: the heavy pnictogen's compound slows burning in the materials around us.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bismuth — the gentle heavyweight

**Bismuth** closes the spread in **low-melting alloys for fire sprinklers** — the alloy melts early in a fire and lets the water go — and in **lead-free solder**, doing lead's old job without lead's poison. Down the group the character has turned metallic, and the uses turn with it.

## The key ideas

- **Liquid nitrogen**: cryotherapy and storing biological samples. **Phosphorus**: blue LEDs and high-index optical glass with anti-reflection coatings.
- **Gallium arsenide**: phone solar panels and lasers. **Antimony(III) oxide**: flame retardant.
- **Bismuth**: low-melting alloys for fire sprinklers, and lead-free solder.
