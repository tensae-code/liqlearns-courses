# Group 15 elements in technology — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Walk the group: one technology per element, in Egypt's order.
**Answer:** Nitrogen — liquid nitrogen for cryotherapy and storing biological samples; phosphorus — blue LEDs and high-index optical glass with anti-reflection coatings; arsenic — gallium arsenide in phone solar panels and lasers; antimony — antimony(III) oxide as a flame retardant; bismuth — low-melting alloys for fire sprinklers and lead-free solder.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is bismuth chosen for fire-sprinkler alloys?
**Answer:** Its alloys melt at low temperatures, so the sprinkler's plug melts early in a fire and releases the water — the melting point is the working property.
