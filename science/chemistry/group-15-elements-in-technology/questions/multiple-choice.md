# Group 15 elements in technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Liquid nitrogen is used in…
a) Cryotherapy and storing biological samples  b) Flame retardants  c) Lead-free solder  d) Street lamps
**Answer: a) Cryotherapy and storing biological samples**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Phosphorus goes into…
a) Blue LEDs and high-refractive-index optical glass  b) Fire sprinklers  c) Breathing apparatus  d) Atomic clocks
**Answer: a) Blue LEDs and high-refractive-index optical glass**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Gallium arsenide serves in…
a) Phone solar panels and lasers  b) Fertilisers  c) Optical glass only  d) Solder
**Answer: a) Phone solar panels and lasers**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Antimony(III) oxide is used as a…
a) Flame retardant  b) Coolant  c) Catalyst  d) Fuel
**Answer: a) Flame retardant**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Bismuth's low-melting alloys are used for…
a) Fire sprinklers  b) LEDs  c) Lasers  d) Cryotherapy
**Answer: a) Fire sprinklers**
