# Degree of dissociation α — strong, weak and medium electrolytes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) α is defined as…
a) All particles over dissociated particles  b) Dissociated particles over all dissolved particles, a/A  c) The lamp's brightness in volts  d) The solution's mass over its volume
**Answer: b) Dissociated particles over all dissolved particles, a/A**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Strong electrolytes have α…
a) Below 3%  b) Between 3% and 30%  c) Above 30%  d) Exactly 100% always
**Answer: c) Above 30%**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which set is Bulgaria's medium class?
a) HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃  b) Acetic acid, phenol, HCN  c) Formic, sulfurous and phosphoric acids  d) All inorganic salts
**Answer: c) Formic, sulfurous and phosphoric acids**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Weak electrolytes in solution sit in…
a) No dissociation at all  b) A molecules ⇌ ions equilibrium  c) Irreversible dissociation  d) Precipitation
**Answer: b) A molecules ⇌ ions equilibrium**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) The lamp test compared the acids at…
a) 1 mol/L  b) 0.1 mol/L  c) 10 mol/L  d) Saturation
**Answer: b) 0.1 mol/L**
