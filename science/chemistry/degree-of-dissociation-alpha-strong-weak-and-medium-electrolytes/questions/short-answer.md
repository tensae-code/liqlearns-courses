# Degree of dissociation α — strong, weak and medium electrolytes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What did the lamp test prove, and what quantity replaced the lamp?
**Answer: At equal concentration the acids carry current very differently, so their dissociation differs; the degree of dissociation α = a/A replaced the lamp's verdict with a number.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) File H₂SO₄, acetic acid and phosphoric acid into Bulgaria's bands.
**Answer: H₂SO₄ strong (>30%); acetic acid weak (<3%); phosphoric acid medium (3–30%).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is a weak electrolyte's dissociation written with ⇌?
**Answer: Because it is an equilibrium — molecules and ions coexist, with only a small fraction dissociated at any moment.**
