Today: **Degree of dissociation α** — Bulgaria's three bands. Same strength, two lamps: hydrochloric acid blazes, acetic acid glows dim — because the split into ions differs. α counts it: dissociated over dissolved. Above 30%, strong. Below 3%, weak. And Bulgaria keeps a middle band — 3 to 30% — for formic, sulfurous and phosphoric acids.
