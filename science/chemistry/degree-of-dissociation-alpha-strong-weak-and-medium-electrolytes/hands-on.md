# Hands-on — Degree of dissociation α — strong, weak and medium electrolytes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Band filing
File twelve substances into Bulgaria's three bands from the lesson's lists and rules (NaCl, KOH, acetic acid, HNO₃, phenol, formic acid, Ba(OH)₂, HCN, sulfurous acid, K₂SO₄, H₂S, phosphoric acid), writing each one's band and the α range that puts it there.

## α arithmetic
Compute α for: 900 of 1000 particles dissociated; 20 of 1000; 150 of 1000. File each result into its band and name one lesson electrolyte that lives in that band.
