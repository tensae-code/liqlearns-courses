# Hands-on — Carbocations and carbanions — charged carbon

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two structures, one formula — the C₂H₆O build
With paper atoms (or bottle caps labelled C, H, O), build C₂H₆O two different ways: once as ethyl alcohol (the oxygen between a carbon and a hydrogen, –OH) and once as dimethyl ether (the oxygen between two carbons). Under each build, write its structural formula and circle what differs: the oxygen's partners. Say out loud why no molecular formula could ever separate your two builds.

## Charge cards — cation or anion?
Make three cards for one carbon centre: no charge, positive charge, negative charge. For the two charged cards write the species' name (carbocation/carbonium ion; carbanion) and one phrase for its electron state (electron-poor; electron-rich). Shuffle and name each card against the clock.
