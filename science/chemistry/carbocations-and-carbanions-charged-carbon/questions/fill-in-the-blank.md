# Carbocations and carbanions — charged carbon — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Carbon carrying a positive charge is the _____ ion, or carbocation.
**Answer: carbonium**

**2.** Carbon carrying a negative charge is a _____.
**Answer: carbanion**

**3.** The formula that shows each atom's partners, bond types and valence is the _____ formula.
**Answer: structural (displayed)**

**4.** C₂H₆O is both ethyl alcohol and dimethyl _____.
**Answer: ether**
