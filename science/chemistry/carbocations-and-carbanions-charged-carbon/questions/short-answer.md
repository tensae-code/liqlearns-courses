# Carbocations and carbanions — charged carbon — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the two charged carbon species and the charge on each.
**Answer: The carbocation (carbonium ion) carries a positive charge; the carbanion carries a negative charge — the two opposite charge states of carbon.**

**2.** What does a structural (displayed) formula show that a molecular formula cannot?
**Answer: It shows each atom's partners, the bond types and the valence — the arrangement of the atoms — while the molecular formula gives only which atoms and how many.**

**3.** Use C₂H₆O to prove that a molecular formula can name two substances.
**Answer: C₂H₆O is the molecular formula of both ethyl alcohol and dimethyl ether — two different substances with different structures — so the formula alone cannot say which substance is meant.**

**4.** Why must the structural formula be drawn before charged carbon species can be discussed honestly?
**Answer: Because the species differ in arrangement and charge, not in atom counts: only a drawing that shows partners, bond types and valence can show where the charge sits and how the carbon is joined.**
