# Carbocations and carbanions — charged carbon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A carbocation is carbon carrying a…
a) negative charge  b) positive charge  c) double bond  d) ring
**Answer: b) positive charge**

**2.** The carbonium ion is another name for the…
a) carbanion  b) carbocation  c) carbonate ion  d) carbonyl group
**Answer: b) carbocation**

**3.** A carbanion is carbon carrying a…
a) positive charge  b) negative charge  c) triple bond  d) lone ring
**Answer: b) negative charge**

**4.** The structural (displayed) formula shows each atom's partners, bond types and…
a) mass  b) valence  c) colour  d) melting point
**Answer: b) valence**

**5.** C₂H₆O is the molecular formula of…
a) ethyl alcohol only  b) dimethyl ether only  c) both ethyl alcohol and dimethyl ether  d) neither
**Answer: c) both ethyl alcohol and dimethyl ether**

**6.** A molecular formula alone cannot show…
a) the atom counts  b) the element kinds  c) the arrangement — partners and bond types  d) the total charge of a neutral molecule
**Answer: c) the arrangement — partners and bond types**
