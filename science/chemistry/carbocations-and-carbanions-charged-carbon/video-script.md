Today: **Carbocations and carbanions — charged carbon** — Iraq's way: carbon can carry a charge.
Carbon with a positive charge is the carbonium ion — the carbocation. Carbon with a negative charge is the carbanion. Two charged carbons, opposite signs. And to draw such things honestly you need the structural formula: it shows who is joined to whom, with what bond — because a plain molecular formula cannot: C₂H₆O is both ethyl alcohol and dimethyl ether until the structure is drawn.
