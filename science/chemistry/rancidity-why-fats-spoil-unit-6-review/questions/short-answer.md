# Rancidity — why fats spoil — Unit 6 review — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is rancidity?
**Answer: The spoiling of fats and oils**

**2. ** What attacks double bonds in oxidative rancidity?
**Answer: Oxygen**

**3. ** How is rancidity slowed?
**Answer: Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry**

**4. ** What does hydrolytic rancidity split?
**Answer: The fat's ester links (by water)**
