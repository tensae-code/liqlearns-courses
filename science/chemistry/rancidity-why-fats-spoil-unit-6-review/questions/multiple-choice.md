# Rancidity — why fats spoil — Unit 6 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is rancidity?
a) Plants (seeds)  b) Margarine (solid fat)  c) The spoiling of fats and oils  d) No hydrogen bonding between esters
**Answer: c) The spoiling of fats and oils**

**2. ** What attacks double bonds in oxidative rancidity?
a) NO  b) Oxygen  c) Oxygen and water  d) Ethiopia's own resource base
**Answer: b) Oxygen**

**3. ** How is rancidity slowed?
a) Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry  b) In fixed whole-number ratios  c) A tiny, dense, positive centre exists  d) The proton number Z
**Answer: a) Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry**

**4. ** What does hydrolytic rancidity split?
a) Diamond  b) A manometer  c) Resistance to flow  d) The fat's ester links (by water)
**Answer: d) The fat's ester links (by water)**
