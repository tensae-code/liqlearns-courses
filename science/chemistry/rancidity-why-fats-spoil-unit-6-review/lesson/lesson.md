# Rancidity — why fats spoil — Unit 6 review

## Do it first
Open a packet of chips, leave it a week, and smell. The book's own activity starts exactly there. Before the review, say what the air and the water in food have been doing to the fat.

[Picture 21710 — Open versus sealed packets after storage.]

Rancidity is the spoiling of fats and oils, giving off-smells and tastes.

Hydrolytic rancidity: water splits fat ester links, freeing fatty acids. Oxidative rancidity: oxygen attacks the double bonds.

Prevention is storage: seal out air, keep cool, dark and dry — the stored-chips activity compares open and sealed packets. Unit 6 closes with its summary, key terms and review.
