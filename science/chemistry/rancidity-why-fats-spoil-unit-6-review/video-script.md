# Video script — Rancidity — why fats spoil — Unit 6 review

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open a packet of chips, leave it a week, and smell.

Rancidity is the spoiling of fats and oils, giving off-smells and tastes.

Hydrolytic rancidity: water splits fat ester links, freeing fatty acids.

Prevention is storage: seal out air, keep cool, dark and dry — the stored-chips activity compares open and sealed packets.

Now you. Say it back: What is rancidity?
Answer: The spoiling of fats and oils.
