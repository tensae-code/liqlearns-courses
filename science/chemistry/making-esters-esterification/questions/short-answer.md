# Making esters — esterification — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What catalyst does esterification use?
**Answer: An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)**

**2. ** Esterification gives ester plus what?
**Answer: Water**

**3. ** Is esterification reversible?
**Answer: Yes — it reaches equilibrium**

**4. ** Name an ester use.
**Answer: Flavours, perfumes or solvents**
