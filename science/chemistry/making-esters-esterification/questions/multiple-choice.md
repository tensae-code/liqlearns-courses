# Making esters — esterification — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What catalyst does esterification use?
a) Properties repeat periodically with atomic number  b) Eight electrons — an octet  c) Lattice formation  d) An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)
**Answer: d) An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)**

**2. ** Esterification gives ester plus what?
a) Hydrogen bonds  b) An –OH group  c) Water  d) Le Chatelier's principle
**Answer: c) Water**

**3. ** Is esterification reversible?
a) Acidic  b) Yes — it reaches equilibrium  c) Tenfold  d) 7 — neutral
**Answer: b) Yes — it reaches equilibrium**

**4. ** Name an ester use.
a) Flavours, perfumes or solvents  b) Limestone and clay  c) Chrome and vegetable tanning  d) The tail (water-hating end)
**Answer: a) Flavours, perfumes or solvents**
