# Making esters — esterification — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What catalyst does esterification use — An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)
**Answer: True**

**2. ** Esterification gives ester plus what — Purifies a metal at the cathode
**Answer: False**

**3. ** Is esterification reversible — Yes — it reaches equilibrium
**Answer: True**

**4. ** Name an ester use. — The uncertainty principle
**Answer: False**
