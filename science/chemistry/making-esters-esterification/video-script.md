Warm an acid with an alcohol and a catalyst, and the mixture reaches a balance — never all ester.

Fischer esterification: a carboxylic acid reacts with an alcohol, with an acid catalyst (concentrated sulfuric acid as presented), to give an ester and water.

The reaction is reversible — it reaches equilibrium (⇌), which is why yields are managed, not total.

Esters so made serve as flavours, perfumes and solvents.

Now you. Say it back: What catalyst does esterification use?
Answer: An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid).
