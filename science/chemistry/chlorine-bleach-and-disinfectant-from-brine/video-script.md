Today: **Chlorine — bleach and disinfectant from brine**.

Chlorine is manufactured by the electrolysis of brine.

It is a powerful oxidising agent, reacts with heated metals to form chlorides, and chlorine/chlorine water serve as bleach for coloured materials and as disinfectant.
