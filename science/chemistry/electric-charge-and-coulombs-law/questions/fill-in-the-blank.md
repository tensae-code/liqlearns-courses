# Electric charge and Coulomb's law — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Like charges repel and opposite charges attract; _____, F = k·Q1·Q2/d², makes the force directly proportional to the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance, with k ≈ 9.0·10⁹ N·m²/C² in vacuum.
**Answer: Coulomb's law**

**2.** Crookes's cathode rays, Thomson's 1897 charge/mass measurement and Millikan's 1910 _____ (−1.602·10⁻¹⁹ C) together reveal the electron.
**Answer: elementary charge**

**3.** Rubbing creates nothing — charge is _____: one body loses exactly the electrons the other gains.
**Answer: conserved**
