Today: **Uncertainty — no measurement is exact**.

Every measurement carries uncertainty, in two kinds: systematic (consistently too large or small — reaction time, parallax, miscalibrated balance;

removable once known) and random (unpredictable variation from the estimated last digit;

Absolute uncertainty and percent uncertainty are both computed (23.25 g ± 0.05 g = ±0.2%).
