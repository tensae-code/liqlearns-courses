## Uncertainty

Every measurement carries uncertainty, in two kinds: systematic (consistently too large or small — reaction time, parallax, miscalibrated balance; removable once known) and random (unpredictable variation from the estimated last digit; reducible, never eliminable).

Results are reported as value ± uncertainty — (6.0 ± 0.1) mL from a finer cylinder — and the uncertainty of a mean is estimated three ways (half the range, furthest reading from the mean, instrument resolution, including the digital-instrument case). Absolute uncertainty and percent uncertainty are both computed (23.25 g ± 0.05 g = ±0.2%).
