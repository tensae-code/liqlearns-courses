# Ions and the ECG heart signal — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** The signals an ECG records are made by ______ moving across heart-muscle membranes.
**Answer:** ions

**2.** Too much or too little of the key ions shows up as a heart-______ disturbance.
**Answer:** rhythm

**3.** An ECG reads the heart's activity from electrodes placed on the ______.
**Answer:** skin
