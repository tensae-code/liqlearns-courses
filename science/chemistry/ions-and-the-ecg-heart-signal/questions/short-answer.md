# Ions and the ECG heart signal — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in the book's terms, how the heart makes an electrical signal.
**Answer:** Sodium, potassium and calcium ions move across the membranes of heart-muscle cells; moving charge is current, and the coordinated movement across the heart's cells adds up to the electrical signal.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can an ion imbalance be spotted on an ECG?
**Answer:** The ECG trace is drawn by ion movements, so when the blood's ion balance slips, the heart muscle fires differently and the rhythm disturbance appears in the trace.
