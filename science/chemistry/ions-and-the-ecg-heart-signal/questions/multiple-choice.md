# Ions and the ECG heart signal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The heart's electrical signals are made by…
a) Ions moving across heart-muscle membranes  b) Bones rubbing together  c) Air moving in the lungs  d) Blood changing colour
**Answer: a) Ions moving across heart-muscle membranes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which ions does Egypt's book name for the heart signal?
a) Na⁺, K⁺ and Ca²⁺  b) Fe²⁺, Cu²⁺ and Zn²⁺  c) Cl⁻, Br⁻ and I⁻  d) H⁺ and OH⁻ only
**Answer: a) Na⁺, K⁺ and Ca²⁺**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An ECG records the signal from…
a) Electrodes on the skin  b) A probe inside the heart  c) The patient's voice  d) A blood sample
**Answer: a) Electrodes on the skin**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Ion imbalance in the blood shows up as…
a) A heart-rhythm disturbance  b) Stronger bones  c) Better eyesight  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) A heart-rhythm disturbance**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The ECG trace's waves correspond to…
a) The heart muscle's ion movements, beat by beat  b) The patient's breathing only  c) Room temperature  d) The machine warming up
**Answer: a) The heart muscle's ion movements, beat by beat**
