# Hands-on — Ions and the ECG heart signal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace a beat
Draw a wavy line like an ECG trace across a page. Mark one repeating beat. Under it, write the three ions Egypt's book names — Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺ — and one sentence saying what those ions are doing to make the line.

## Balance and rhythm
Write two short lists: what the heart's ion movements do when the balance is right (a regular trace, a steady rhythm), and what the book says happens when the ion balance slips (rhythm disturbance). Say both lists aloud to a companion.
