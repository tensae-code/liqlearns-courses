## Oxides

Oxides are binary compounds of oxygen with another element, classified by behaviour: acidic oxides (oxides of nonmetals; those dissolving in water are acid anhydrides), basic oxides (oxides of metals; water-soluble ones are basic anhydrides), amphoteric oxides (showing both acidic and basic behaviour), neutral oxides (neither), and peroxides (containing the –O–O– link, oxygen in oxidation state −1).
