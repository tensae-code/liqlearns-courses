Today: **Nanotechnology in industry** — chemistry at its smallest working scale.

A nanometre is a billionth of a metre. Grind a substance down to pieces that size and almost every atom ends up on a surface — ready to react, to stick, to carry, to catalyse. The substance has not changed; its size has, and that is enough. So industry puts nano to work everywhere at once: smaller electronics, medicines and sunscreens carried by nanoparticles, coatings a few atoms thick, catalysts and water treatment powered by all that surface.

Nanotechnology is not a new substance. It is a new size — and at that size, familiar chemistry learns new tricks.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
