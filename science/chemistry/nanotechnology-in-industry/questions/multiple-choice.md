# Nanotechnology in industry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) One nanometre is…
a) A thousandth of a metre  b) A billionth of a metre (10⁻⁹ m)  c) A millionth of a metre  d) The width of a hair
**Answer: b) A billionth of a metre (10⁻⁹ m)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A nanoparticle is…
a) A new element  b) A particle only a few nanometres across — a cluster of atoms or molecules  c) Any dust grain  d) A kind of cell
**Answer: b) A particle only a few nanometres across — a cluster of atoms or molecules**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Dividing a substance into nanoparticles changes its behaviour chiefly because…
a) Its atoms change into other atoms  b) Its surface area compared with its volume becomes enormous, so far more of it is at a surface, ready to react  c) It becomes heavier  d) It stops being a chemical
**Answer: b) Its surface area compared with its volume becomes enormous, so far more of it is at a surface, ready to react**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Nanotechnology is best described as…
a) A new substance  b) A new reaction  c) A new size regime for chemistry — the same substances, working at nanometre scale  d) A kind of polymer
**Answer: c) A new size regime for chemistry — the same substances, working at nanometre scale**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Malaysia places nanotechnology in its Form 5 book as…
a) A history section  b) An application section of the consumer and industrial chemistry chapter  c) A table of elements  d) A laboratory safety rule
**Answer: b) An application section of the consumer and industrial chemistry chapter**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Which list names industries where Malaysia's section applies nanotechnology?
a) Farming only  b) Electronics, medicine and cosmetics, coatings and materials, catalysis and water treatment  c) Mining only  d) Shipbuilding only
**Answer: b) Electronics, medicine and cosmetics, coatings and materials, catalysis and water treatment**
