# Nanotechnology in industry — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) One nanometre is a _____ of a metre.
**Answer: billionth**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) At the nanoscale, a substance's _____ area compared with its volume becomes enormous.
**Answer: surface**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Malaysia's nanotechnology section is titled an _____ of nanotechnology in industry.
**Answer: application**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Nanoparticles are only a few _____ across.
**Answer: nanometres**
