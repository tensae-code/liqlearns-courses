# Artificial transmutation and projectiles — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** The ______ is the preferred projectile because it is uncharged.
**Answer:** neutron

**2.** Bombarding a target nucleus to change it is called artificial ______.
**Answer:** transmutation

**3.** In the worked example, aluminium-27 becomes ______-24.
**Answer:** sodium
