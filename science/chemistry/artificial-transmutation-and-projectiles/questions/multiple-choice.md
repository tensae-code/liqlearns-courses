# Artificial transmutation and projectiles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Artificial transmutation works by…
a) Bombarding a target nucleus with a projectile  b) Heating an element strongly  c) Dissolving one element in another  d) Waiting for natural decay
**Answer: a) Bombarding a target nucleus with a projectile**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The preferred projectile is the neutron because…
a) It is uncharged, so the nucleus cannot repel it  b) It is the heaviest projectile  c) It carries a +2 charge  d) It is a photon
**Answer: a) It is uncharged, so the nucleus cannot repel it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Which of these is NOT one of Egypt's four projectiles?
a) Gamma photon  b) Alpha particle  c) Proton  d) Deuteron
**Answer: a) Gamma photon**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In ²⁷Al + n → ²⁴Na + α, the product sodium isotope is…
a) Sodium-24  b) Sodium-23  c) Sodium-27  d) Sodium-28
**Answer: a) Sodium-24**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A deuteron is…
a) A hydrogen-2 nucleus used as a projectile  b) A helium nucleus  c) A fast electron  d) Two neutrons
**Answer: a) A hydrogen-2 nucleus used as a projectile**
