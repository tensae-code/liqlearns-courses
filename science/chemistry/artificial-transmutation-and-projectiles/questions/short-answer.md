# Artificial transmutation and projectiles — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Verify the worked transmutation by A and Z.
**Answer:** A: 27 + 1 = 28 on the left; 24 + 4 = 28 on the right. Z: 13 + 0 = 13 on the left; 11 + 2 = 13 on the right. Both balances hold.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do charged projectiles need high speeds to work at all?
**Answer:** The target nucleus is positive and repels a charged projectile; only a fast projectile can push through that repulsion to reach the nucleus — the uncharged neutron faces no such barrier.
