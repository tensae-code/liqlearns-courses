# Lesson plan — Artificial transmutation and projectiles

## Sub lessons
1. **Induced radioactivity — decay on demand** — **Artificial (induced) radioactivity** is made, not found: a stable **target nucleus is bombarded with a projectile**, absorbs it or is struck by it, and emerges as a **new, often radioactive, nucleus**. The change is a transmutation — one element becomes another — and it obeys the same A/Z bookkeeping as natural decay.
2. **The four projectiles — and the favourite** — Egypt lists the missiles: **alpha particles, protons, neutrons and deuterons**. Three of the four carry charge, and a charged projectile is repelled by the target's positive nucleus before it can strike. The **neutron is preferred, being uncharged** — nothing pushes it away, so it reaches the nucleus even at modest speeds.
3. **The worked strike — aluminium to sodium** — The book's worked example: **aluminium-27 + neutron → sodium-24 + alpha**. Check it by the bookkeeping: A, 27 + 1 = 24 + 4; Z, 13 + 0 = 11 + 2. Both balances hold — a light, stable metal has been transmuted into a radioactive isotope of another element, made to order.
