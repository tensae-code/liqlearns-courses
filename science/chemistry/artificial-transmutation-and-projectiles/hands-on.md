# Hands-on — Artificial transmutation and projectiles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bookkeeping drill
Write the worked equation large. Under each side, add the A values and the Z values and write the two sums. Say the two checks aloud: A balances, Z balances.

## Choose your projectile
For a target with a large positive nucleus, rank the four projectiles by how easily each reaches the nucleus, and write one line of reason for the neutron's first place.
