# Priority of organic functional groups — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The highest-priority group takes the name's _____.
**Answer: suffix**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Groups that do not win are named as _____.
**Answer: prefixes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Highest in the priority order stand carboxylic _____.
**Answer: acids**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Lowest in the priority order stand alkyl _____.
**Answer: halides**
