# Priority of organic functional groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The priority order is needed when a molecule carries…
a) No functional group  b) Exactly one functional group  c) More than one functional group  d) Only carbon atoms
**Answer: c) More than one functional group**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Under the rule, the highest-priority group present takes the…
a) Prefix  b) Suffix — the family ending of the name  c) A number only  d) Nothing; it is dropped
**Answer: b) Suffix — the family ending of the name**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Highest in Fiji's priority order stand…
a) Alkyl halides  b) Carboxylic acids  c) Alkanes  d) Ethers
**Answer: b) Carboxylic acids**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Lowest in the order stand…
a) Carboxylic acids  b) Esters  c) Alkyl halides — named as a prefix when other groups are present  d) Amides
**Answer: c) Alkyl halides — named as a prefix when other groups are present**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Fiji's summary table gives, for every family, its…
a) Colour and smell  b) General formula and ending  c) Price per gram  d) Discoverer's name
**Answer: b) General formula and ending**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the naming method, after ranking the groups you…
a) Name them alphabetically  b) Give the suffix to the highest and prefixes to the rest  c) Drop all but one group  d) Start from the longest group
**Answer: b) Give the suffix to the highest and prefixes to the rest**
