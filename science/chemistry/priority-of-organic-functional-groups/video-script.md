Today: **Priority of organic functional groups** — the league table of names, in Fiji's Year 13 book.

A molecule can carry several functional groups, but a name has only one ending. So the groups stand in a fixed order: carboxylic acids highest, alkyl halides lowest. The highest group takes the suffix; every other group steps forward as a prefix. List the groups, rank them, name by the order — and a crowded molecule names as surely as a simple one.
