# Hands-on — Paramagnetism and diamagnetism — from unpaired electrons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the boxes
Draw five-box diagrams for Zn (d¹⁰), Ni²⁺ (d⁸) and Fe²⁺ (d⁶), filling singly before pairing. Count and label the unpaired in each, then write the magnetic order under the three diagrams.

## Predict a stranger
Take Mn²⁺ (3d⁵). Draw its boxes, count the unpaired (five), and predict where it would stand in the Fe²⁺/Ni²⁺/Zn order — then say the rule you used.
