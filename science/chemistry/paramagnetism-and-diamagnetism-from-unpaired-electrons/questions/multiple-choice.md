# Paramagnetism and diamagnetism — from unpaired electrons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A substance is paramagnetic when it has…
a) Unpaired electrons  b) All electrons paired  c) A full d sublevel  d) No d electrons at all
**Answer: a) Unpaired electrons**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The magnetic moment of a paramagnetic substance grows with…
a) The number of unpaired electrons  b) Its mass  c) Its melting point  d) The number of paired electrons
**Answer: a) The number of unpaired electrons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Fe²⁺ (3d⁶) has how many unpaired electrons?
a) Four  b) Two  c) Six  d) None
**Answer: a) Four**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A zinc compound (3d¹⁰) is…
a) Diamagnetic  b) Strongly paramagnetic  c) Ferromagnetic  d) Non-magnetic in every sense
**Answer: a) Diamagnetic**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Ni²⁺ (3d⁸) has how many unpaired electrons?
a) Two  b) Eight  c) Four  d) None
**Answer: a) Two**
