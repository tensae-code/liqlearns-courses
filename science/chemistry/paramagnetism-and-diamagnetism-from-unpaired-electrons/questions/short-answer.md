# Paramagnetism and diamagnetism — from unpaired electrons — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Order Zn, Ni²⁺ and Fe²⁺ compounds by magnetic pull, with reasons.
**Answer:** Fe²⁺ strongest (3d⁶, four unpaired), then Ni²⁺ (3d⁸, two unpaired), then zinc compounds (3d¹⁰, none unpaired — diamagnetic, no pull).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why must the box diagram come before the prediction?
**Answer:** The magnetic behaviour is fixed by the unpaired count, and the count is only honestly read from the box diagram filled by Hund's rule — pairing guesses made without it go wrong, as Fe²⁺ shows.
