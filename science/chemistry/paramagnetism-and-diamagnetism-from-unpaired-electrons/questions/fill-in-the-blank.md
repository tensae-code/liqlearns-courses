# Paramagnetism and diamagnetism — from unpaired electrons — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Substances with unpaired electrons are called ______.
**Answer:** paramagnetic

**2.** The magnetic ______ grows with the unpaired count.
**Answer:** moment

**3.** Zinc's d sublevel is ______, so its compounds are diamagnetic.
**Answer:** full (d¹⁰)
