# Video script — Water pollution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A pond fed by farm runoff turns green, thick and lifeless.

Water pollutants include fertiliser runoff (nitrates and phosphates), phosphate detergents, untreated sewage, pesticides and heavy metals — the guide's answer key lists the same classes.

Nutrient enrichment (eutrophication) feeds algal blooms; when the algae decay, decomposers consume the water's oxygen and aquatic life suffocates.

Heavy metals and pesticides poison directly and can accumulate through food chains.

Now you. Say it back: What does eutrophication mean?
Answer: Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms.
