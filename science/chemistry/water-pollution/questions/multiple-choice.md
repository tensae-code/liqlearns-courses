# Water pollution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does eutrophication mean?
a) Rubber, cellulose or protein  b) The pollution lessons  c) Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms  d) Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet
**Answer: c) Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms**

**2. ** What runs out when algae decay?
a) Its ions are locked in place  b) The water's dissolved oxygen  c) To predict new elements' properties  d) It rises from 1 to 8
**Answer: b) The water's dissolved oxygen**

**3. ** Name a heavy-metal pollutant source class.
a) Heavy metals (from industry/mining)  b) The –OH position  c) An alkene  d) Williamson
**Answer: a) Heavy metals (from industry/mining)**

**4. ** Name the pollutant in detergents.
a) The conjugate base  b) Acidic  c) It gives two replaceable H⁺  d) Phosphates
**Answer: d) Phosphates**
