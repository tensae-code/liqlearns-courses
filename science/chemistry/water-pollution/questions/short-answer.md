# Water pollution — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does eutrophication mean?
**Answer: Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms**

**2. ** What runs out when algae decay?
**Answer: The water's dissolved oxygen**

**3. ** Name a heavy-metal pollutant source class.
**Answer: Heavy metals (from industry/mining)**

**4. ** Name the pollutant in detergents.
**Answer: Phosphates**
