## Metallic bonding

Metal atoms release their valence electrons into a shared, freely moving "sea" surrounding a lattice of positive metal ions (pictured); the attraction between the ion lattice and the electron sea is the metallic bond, and pupils model it. Bond strength depends on the number of valence electrons contributed, the charge/size factors named in the section's exercise set.
