Today: **Metallic bonding — the electron sea**.

Metal atoms release their valence electrons into a shared, freely moving "sea" surrounding a lattice of positive metal ions (pictured);

the attraction between the ion lattice and the electron sea is the metallic bond, and pupils model it.

Bond strength depends on the number of valence electrons contributed, the charge/size factors named in the section's exercise set.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
