# Recognising substances by their properties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A substance in this lesson is identified by…
a) Its label alone  b) Its observed properties  c) Its price  d) The colour of its bottle
**Answer: b) Its observed properties**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The identification work in §1.4 is done as…
a) A dictation  b) Group experimental work  c) A drawing exercise  d) Silent reading
**Answer: b) Group experimental work**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A property shared by two substances…
a) Identifies both at once  b) Cannot separate them — the differing property is the clue  c) Must be ignored in all tests  d) Proves they are the same substance
**Answer: b) Cannot separate them — the differing property is the clue**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Litmus in this lesson is used as…
a) A fuel  b) An indicator whose behaviour is an observed property  c) A cleaning agent  d) A preservative
**Answer: b) An indicator whose behaviour is an observed property**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The order the lesson teaches is…
a) Name first, properties after  b) Observe properties first, then the name follows  c) Properties are never recorded  d) Only the teacher observes
**Answer: b) Observe properties first, then the name follows**
