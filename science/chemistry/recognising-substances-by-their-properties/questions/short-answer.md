# Recognising substances by their properties — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must identification start from observed properties rather than from a guessed name?
**Answer:** Because the name has to follow the evidence: the observed properties are what single out one substance from another, so guessing first puts the answer before the proof.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Two white powders look identical. What does the group look for, and why?
**Answer:** A property where they differ — a shared property cannot separate them, so only a differing observed property (for example their behaviour in water or with litmus) can identify each one.
