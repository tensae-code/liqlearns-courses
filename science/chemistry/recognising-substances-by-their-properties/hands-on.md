# Hands-on — Recognising substances by their properties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Test and record
Take three safe kitchen substances your teacher or a grown-up approves (for example salt, sugar and bicarbonate of soda). For each, record three observed properties — look, feel, and what happens in a spoon of water. Cover the names and identify each one from your records alone.

## The differing property
From your records, find for each pair of substances the one property where they differ. Say out loud why that property, and not a shared one, is the clue that separates them.
