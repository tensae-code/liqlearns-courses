# When the octet rule breaks — ionic cases — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Is the octet rule a law?
**Answer: No — a guide that can break**

**2. ** What charges can iron form?
**Answer: Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ (variable)**

**3. ** Why can transition metals vary their charge?
**Answer: d electrons can also be lost**

**4. ** Where do we learn actual ionic charges?
**Answer: From the compounds themselves**
