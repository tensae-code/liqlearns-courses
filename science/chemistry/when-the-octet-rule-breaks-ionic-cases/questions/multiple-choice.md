# When the octet rule breaks — ionic cases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Is the octet rule a law?
a) A positive ion (cation)  b) d electrons can also be lost  c) No — a guide that can break  d) It shrinks
**Answer: c) No — a guide that can break**

**2. ** What charges can iron form?
a) Weakly polar  b) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ (variable)  c) One with three –OH groups  d) Yeast (its enzymes)
**Answer: b) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ (variable)**

**3. ** Why can transition metals vary their charge?
a) d electrons can also be lost  b) Suppresses it  c) OH⁻ — so the solution is alkaline  d) Its range must fall in the titration's pH jump
**Answer: a) d electrons can also be lost**

**4. ** Where do we learn actual ionic charges?
a) Centrifuging (spinning)  b) Slows microbial growth  c) PET  d) From the compounds themselves
**Answer: d) From the compounds themselves**
