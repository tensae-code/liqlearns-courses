# Biochemistry — an introduction — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (What biochemistry is, The big molecules of life, Chemistry that keeps life going), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, sorting named substances into the four families, and tracing one mouthful of food through breakdown to energy.
