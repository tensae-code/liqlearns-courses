# Biochemistry — an introduction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Biochemistry is…
a) The study of rocks  b) The chemistry of living things  c) The study of the weather  d) A branch of astronomy
**Answer: b) The chemistry of living things**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In Cambodia's Grade 10 book, the Biochemistry section stands…
a) At the start of the atom chapter  b) Inside the periodic-table chapter  c) Closing the organic chemistry chapter and the whole Grade 10 course  d) In an appendix of tables
**Answer: c) Closing the organic chemistry chapter and the whole Grade 10 course**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The four big families of living matter are…
a) Metals, nonmetals, salts and gases  b) Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids and nucleic acids  c) Acids, bases, salts and oxides  d) Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes and benzene
**Answer: b) Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids and nucleic acids**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The nucleic acids' chief job is…
a) Releasing energy  b) Storing the instructions for which proteins to build  c) Making membranes  d) Carrying oxygen
**Answer: b) Storing the instructions for which proteins to build**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An enzyme is…
a) A vitamin  b) A mineral salt  c) A protein catalyst the body makes, speeding one particular reaction step  d) A kind of sugar
**Answer: c) A protein catalyst the body makes, speeding one particular reaction step**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Inside cells, reactions run…
a) At random  b) In ordered chains — breaking down for energy and building up for growth and repair  c) Only when food is eaten  d) Only in plants
**Answer: b) In ordered chains — breaking down for energy and building up for growth and repair**
