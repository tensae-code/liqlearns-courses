# Biochemistry — an introduction — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The chemistry of living things is called _____.
**Answer: biochemistry**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The family that stores the cell's instructions — DNA's family — is the _____ acids.
**Answer: nucleic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Proteins are built by joining smaller units called _____ acids.
**Answer: amino**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A protein catalyst that speeds one step of a cell's reaction chain is an _____.
**Answer: enzyme**
