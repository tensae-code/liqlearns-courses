Today: **Biochemistry — an introduction** — Cambodia's way: the Grade 10 course ends by walking its chemistry into a living body.

All year the chemistry has been atoms, bonds and carbon compounds in flasks. Now look at your own hand. It is made of those same compounds — and inside every cell of it, reactions are running right now, in ordered chains, breaking food down for energy and building the body up. The study of those substances and those reactions has a name: biochemistry.
The living body builds itself from four big families. Carbohydrates are the quick fuels. Proteins are the builders and the workers. Fats are the long-term store. And the nucleic acids hold the instructions that tell the cell what to build.
And none of it would run fast enough without enzymes — protein catalysts the body makes for itself, each one speeding one step of one chain. That is biochemistry: chemistry, alive.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
