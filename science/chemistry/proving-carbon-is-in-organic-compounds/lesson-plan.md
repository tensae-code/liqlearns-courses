# Lesson plan — Proving carbon is in organic compounds

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Proving carbon is in organic compounds
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the founding proof of organic chemistry the Syrian way: bread burned and sugar heated both end as black carbon, so carbon is the element common to all organic substances — and organic chemistry is the branch that studies carbon's compounds (Syria, Grade 9 — *الفيزياء والكيمياء للصف التاسع الأساسي (Physics & Chemistry, Grade 9 Basic)*, U5 L1, printed p. 148)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

## Sub lessons
1. **Two heatings, one black end — bread and sugar** — The book heats two unlike organic substances: **a piece of bread burned** and **a spoonful of sugar heated**. Different foods, different tastes, different make-up in every way but one — **both end as black carbon**.
2. **The element common to all organic substances** — From the two heatings the book draws its conclusion (printed p. 149): **the element common to all organic substances is carbon**. Bread has it, sugar has it — and the claim reaches to every organic substance: no carbon, not organic.
3. **Organic chemistry — the branch that studies carbon's compounds** — The lesson closes by naming the branch: **organic chemistry is the branch that studies carbon's compounds**. The name is historical — these substances first came from living things — but the definition the book teaches is the carbon rule proved by the heatings.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the black end is 'burnt food', not an element → the black residue is carbon itself — what stays when heat drives everything else out of the organic substance.
- Thinks 'organic' means 'from a living thing' as the definition → that is the name's history; the book's taught definition is the carbon rule — organic chemistry studies carbon's compounds.
- Expects one heating to be enough as proof → two UNLIKE substances (bread, sugar) ending the same way is what carries the claim to all organic substances.
