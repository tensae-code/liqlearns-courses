# Hands-on — Proving carbon is in organic compounds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Charring watch (teacher demonstration / kitchen observation only)
Watch bread toast darken toward black, or sugar heated by a teacher in the school laboratory. Record the colour stages and the point where blackening — carbon appearing — is unmistakable. No home heating of powders; observe what cooking already does.

## Carbon hunt (paper exercise)
List ten substances from home and street. Mark each: organic (carbon's compound, by the lesson's rule) or not, with one line of reason each. Keep a 'not sure' column — the rule, not a guess, decides.
