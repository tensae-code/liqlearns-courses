# Proving carbon is in organic compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Syria heats which two substances to open organic chemistry?
a) Salt and sand  b) Bread and sugar  c) Iron and sulfur  d) Water and oil
**Answer: b) Bread and sugar**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Both heatings end as…
a) Ash only  b) Black carbon  c) Steam  d) A clear liquid
**Answer: b) Black carbon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The element common to all organic substances is…
a) Oxygen  b) Hydrogen  c) Carbon  d) Nitrogen
**Answer: c) Carbon**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The conclusion is drawn on printed p.…
a) 148  b) 149  c) 151  d) 156
**Answer: b) 149**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Organic chemistry is the branch that studies…
a) Metals  b) Carbon's compounds  c) Gases only  d) Acids only
**Answer: b) Carbon's compounds**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The blackening of overdone toast is…
a) A different fact  b) This same proof happening by accident  c) A chemical impossibility  d) Proof of oxygen
**Answer: b) This same proof happening by accident**
