# Proving carbon is in organic compounds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe the book's proof, step by step.
**Answer: A piece of bread is burned and a spoonful of sugar is heated; two unlike organic substances both end as black carbon — so carbon is shown to stand inside both, and by the pair, inside organic substances generally.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What exactly is concluded on printed p. 149?
**Answer: That the element common to all organic substances is carbon, and that organic chemistry is the branch that studies carbon's compounds.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why do two substances make a stronger proof than one?
**Answer: Bread and sugar differ in almost every way; that both leave the same black carbon shows the common element is not a quirk of one substance but the shared mark of the organic family.**
