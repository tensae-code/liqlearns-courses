# Chromium and copper — configuration exceptions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The filling rules predict chromium's configuration as…
a) [Ar] 4s² 3d⁴  b) [Ar] 4s¹ 3d⁵  c) [Ar] 4s² 3d⁶  d) [Ar] 3d⁶
**Answer: a) [Ar] 4s² 3d⁴**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In chromium's real configuration, the electron that moves goes…
a) From 3d to 4s  b) From 4s to 3d  c) From 4p to 3d  d) Nowhere — nothing moves
**Answer: b) From 4s to 3d**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) After the move, chromium's d sublevel is…
a) Empty  b) Half-full, with 5 electrons  c) Completely full, with 10 electrons  d) Holding 4 electrons
**Answer: b) Half-full, with 5 electrons**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Copper's real configuration is…
a) [Ar] 4s² 3d⁹  b) [Ar] 4s¹ 3d¹⁰  c) [Ar] 4s² 3d¹⁰  d) [Ar] 3d¹¹
**Answer: b) [Ar] 4s¹ 3d¹⁰**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Chromium and copper are taught in Kuwait's book as…
a) Proof the filling rules are wrong  b) Named exceptions to the filling rules  c) Atoms with no d sublevel  d) Noble gases
**Answer: b) Named exceptions to the filling rules**
