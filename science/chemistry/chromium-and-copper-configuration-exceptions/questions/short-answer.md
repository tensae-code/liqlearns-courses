# Chromium and copper — configuration exceptions — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) "What happened in chromium?" Answer in the book's own terms.
**Answer:** One electron moves from 4s to 3d, so instead of the predicted 4s² 3d⁴ the atom is 4s¹ 3d⁵ — the d sublevel becomes half-full, with one electron in each of its five orbitals.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How is copper's exception the same move as chromium's, and how does its landing differ?
**Answer:** Copper also moves one electron from 4s to 3d (4s¹, not 4s²), but its d sublevel ends completely full — 3d¹⁰ — where chromium's ends half-full at 3d⁵.
