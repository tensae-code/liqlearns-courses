# Hands-on — Chromium and copper — configuration exceptions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the move
Draw chromium's predicted arrangement as two boxes — 4s with two electrons, 3d with four — then draw an arrow moving one electron from 4s into the empty fifth 3d orbital. Label the result 4s¹ 3d⁵ and write, under it, the book's reason: the d sublevel is now half-full.

## Say the copper twin
Say in one sentence how copper's exception is the same move with a different landing: one electron moves from 4s to 3d, and the d sublevel ends completely full at ten.
