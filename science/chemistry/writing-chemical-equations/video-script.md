Today: **Writing chemical equations**.

Step 1: write the word equation (hydrogen + nitrogen → ammonia;

"+" reads "reacts with", the arrow reads "to produce/form/give/yield").

Step 2: replace words with symbols and formulas.

Equations also carry physical-state symbols and quantities;

the difference between a reaction (the event) and an equation (its shorthand) is made explicit, as is the law behind balancing — conservation of mass.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
