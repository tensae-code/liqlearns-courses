## Writing chemical equations

Step 1: write the word equation (hydrogen + nitrogen → ammonia; "+" reads "reacts with", the arrow reads "to produce/form/give/yield"). Step 2: replace words with symbols and formulas.

Step 3: balance. Equations also carry physical-state symbols and quantities; the difference between a reaction (the event) and an equation (its shorthand) is made explicit, as is the law behind balancing — conservation of mass.
