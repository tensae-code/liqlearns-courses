## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo F, Capitolo 15, §§13–17 — Italy's book (Grade 13)

Genomics as a discipline: the omic sciences mapped (genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, epigenomics), comparative genomics and evolutionary distance, the Human Genome Project's revelations (few genes, much repetitive and regulatory DNA), shotgun sequencing, and bioinformatics over the genetic databases.

## The key ideas

- The omic sciences are mapped as one frame: genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, epigenomics.
- The Human Genome Project's revelations: few genes, and much repetitive and regulatory DNA.
- Shotgun sequencing and bioinformatics over the genetic databases are the discipline's working tools.
