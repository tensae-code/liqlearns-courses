Today: **Fire chemistry — flash point and prevention** — Vietnam's way, from its Grade 10 book.

Flash point: the lowest temperature at which a fuel's vapour will flash with an ignition source near. Auto-ignition temperature: where it catches with no source at all. Flame temperature: how hot its flame burns.

A low flash point makes a fuel dangerous at ordinary temperatures — ignitable vapour is already present.

Prevention is the science applied: keep fuels cool, sealed and ventilated; shut out sparks and hot surfaces (auto-ignition needs no spark).

A fire dies by cooling, smothering or fuel removal — one break for each thing the chain needs.
