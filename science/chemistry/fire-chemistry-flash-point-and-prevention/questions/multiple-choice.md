# Fire chemistry — flash point and prevention — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The flash point of a fuel is…
a) The temperature of its flame  b) The lowest temperature at which its vapour will flash with an ignition source near  c) The temperature at which it catches by itself  d) The temperature at which it boils
**Answer: b) The lowest temperature at which its vapour will flash with an ignition source near**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Auto-ignition temperature is where a fuel…
a) Freezes  b) Catches fire with no spark or flame at all  c) Stops burning  d) Evaporates completely
**Answer: b) Catches fire with no spark or flame at all**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A fuel with a flash point of −40 °C, on a 30 °C day, …
a) Is safe — no vapour can form  b) Has ignitable vapour over it already — it must be kept sealed and away from ignition sources  c) Cannot burn  d) Burns without oxygen
**Answer: b) Has ignitable vapour over it already — it must be kept sealed and away from ignition sources**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Water puts out a wood fire mainly by…
a) Removing the fuel  b) Cooling the fuel below its ignition temperature  c) Adding oxygen  d) Raising the flash point
**Answer: c) Adding oxygen**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A lid on a burning pan kills the fire by…
a) Cooling  b) Smothering — cutting off oxygen  c) Removing fuel  d) Diluting the vapour
**Answer: b) Smothering — cutting off oxygen**
