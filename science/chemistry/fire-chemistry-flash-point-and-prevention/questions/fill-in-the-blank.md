# Fire chemistry — flash point and prevention — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Below its _____ point, too little vapour rises from a fuel to catch.
**Answer: flash**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Prevention keeps fuels cool, sealed and _____, so vapour never gathers.
**Answer: ventilated**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The _____ temperature is how hot a fuel's flame itself burns.
**Answer: flame**
