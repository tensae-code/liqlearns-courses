# Video script — Electrolysis case studies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Brine in, three useful products out.

Electrolysis of brine (concentrated sodium chloride solution): hydrogen at the cathode, chlorine at the anode, and sodium hydroxide left in solution — three products, one cell.

Copper sulfate with inert electrodes: copper at the cathode, oxygen at the anode, and the blue colour fades as copper leaves.

With a copper anode instead, the anode itself dissolves and replaces the copper deposited — the basis of refining, run as a case here.

Now you. Say it back: Name brine electrolysis's three products.
Answer: Hydrogen, chlorine, sodium hydroxide.
