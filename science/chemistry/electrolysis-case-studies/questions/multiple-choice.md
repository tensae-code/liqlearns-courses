# Electrolysis case studies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name brine electrolysis's three products.
a) Temporary flickering dipoles  b) Hydrogen, chlorine, sodium hydroxide  c) A negative ion (anion)  d) Strong lattice attractions in all directions
**Answer: b) Hydrogen, chlorine, sodium hydroxide**

**2. ** At which electrode does chlorine form?
a) The anode  b) Fermentation of sugars  c) The Williamson synthesis  d) –one
**Answer: a) The anode**

**3. ** With inert electrodes on CuSO₄, the anode gas is…
a) Nothing — it spectates  b) Neutralising power  c) H₂O  d) Oxygen
**Answer: d) Oxygen**

**4. ** What happens with a copper anode in CuSO₄?
a) The C=C double bond  b) Respiration, decay and combustion  c) The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper  d) Molasses
**Answer: c) The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper**
