# Electrolysis case studies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Name brine electrolysis's three products. — Hydrogen, chlorine, sodium hydroxide
**Answer: True**

**2. ** At which electrode does chlorine form — Three, along x, y and z
**Answer: False**

**3. ** With inert electrodes on CuSO₄, the anode gas is… — Oxygen
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What happens with a copper anode in CuSO₄ — A silver mirror
**Answer: False**
