# Electrolysis case studies — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name brine electrolysis's three products.
**Answer: Hydrogen, chlorine, sodium hydroxide**

**2. ** At which electrode does chlorine form?
**Answer: The anode**

**3. ** With inert electrodes on CuSO₄, the anode gas is…
**Answer: Oxygen**

**4. ** What happens with a copper anode in CuSO₄?
**Answer: The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper**
