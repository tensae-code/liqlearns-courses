# Video script — Lattice energy — why ionic solids hold together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Forming ions from atoms costs energy — yet ionic solids form eagerly and hold together fiercely.

Making ions costs steps of energy: ionisation energy to remove electrons, repaid in part by electron affinity.

The great payback is lattice energy — the energy released when gaseous ions settle into the crystal lattice.

Born–Haber reasoning adds the steps in a cycle: if the lattice release outweighs the costs, the ionic solid forms and is stable.

Now you. Say it back: What is lattice energy?
Answer: Energy released when ions form the crystal lattice.
