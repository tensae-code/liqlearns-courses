# Lattice energy — why ionic solids hold together — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is lattice energy?
**Answer: Energy released when ions form the crystal lattice**

**2. ** Which step pays back the cost of making ions?
**Answer: Lattice formation**

**3. ** What cycle adds the energy steps?
**Answer: The Born–Haber cycle**

**4. ** What does ionisation energy cost?
**Answer: Removing electrons from atoms**
