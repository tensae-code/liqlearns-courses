# Lattice energy — why ionic solids hold together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is lattice energy?
a) Gamma rays  b) One-electron species only  c) +½ or −½  d) Energy released when ions form the crystal lattice
**Answer: d) Energy released when ions form the crystal lattice**

**2. ** Which step pays back the cost of making ions?
a) ⇌ (double arrow)  b) Pure solids and liquids  c) Lattice formation  d) It breaks particle arrangements
**Answer: c) Lattice formation**

**3. ** What cycle adds the energy steps?
a) OH⁻ ions  b) The Born–Haber cycle  c) No — the same ester family  d) The spoiling of fats and oils
**Answer: b) The Born–Haber cycle**

**4. ** What does ionisation energy cost?
a) Removing electrons from atoms  b) Inputs (raw materials), process, products  c) Phosphorus  d) Soda ash
**Answer: a) Removing electrons from atoms**
