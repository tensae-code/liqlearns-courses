# Lattice energy — why ionic solids hold together

## Do it first
Forming ions from atoms costs energy — yet ionic solids form eagerly and hold together fiercely. Some step must pay the energy back with interest. Before reading, guess which step pays.

[Picture 21634 — Steps up — then the lattice pays it back.]

Making ions costs steps of energy: ionisation energy to remove electrons, repaid in part by electron affinity.

The great payback is lattice energy — the energy released when gaseous ions settle into the crystal lattice.

Born–Haber reasoning adds the steps in a cycle: if the lattice release outweighs the costs, the ionic solid forms and is stable.
