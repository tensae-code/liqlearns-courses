Today: **Materials — metals, ceramics, polymers and composites** — Bulgaria's materials science. A material is a substance with a purpose; its structure runs from atoms to grains to the whole panel, and its properties fall in six groups. Metals stretch, ceramics harden and shatter, polymers lighten — and where no family suffices, composites are engineered: fibreglass, light as plastic, strong as glass.
