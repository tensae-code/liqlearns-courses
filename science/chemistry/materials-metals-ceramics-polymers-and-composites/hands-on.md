# Hands-on — Materials — metals, ceramics, polymers and composites

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Family sorting bench
Sort twelve objects (steel spoon, brass tap, porcelain cup, cement block, glass pane, plastic crate, rubber tyre, fibreglass boat model, silicon chip, wooden ruler as the outsider, aluminium foil, ceramic tile) into METAL / CERAMIC / POLYMER / COMPOSITE / OUTSIDE, defending each sort with two table properties.

## Property-group audit
Take one object and audit it through all six property groups — mechanical, electric, thermal, magnetic, optical, chemical — one line each, naming the family trait behind each line. The audit is Bulgaria's definition of knowing a material.
