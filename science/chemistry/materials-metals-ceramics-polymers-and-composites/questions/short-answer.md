# Materials — metals, ceramics, polymers and composites — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three structure levels with one example each from the lesson.
**Answer: Atomic structure; microstructure (a polycrystalline grain pattern); macrostructure (a sandwich composite panel).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Run the family table for density and melting in words.
**Answer: Metals: high density, mid-to-high melting. Ceramics: melting far above metals. Polymers: light, the low-melting, elastic family.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What problem do composites exist to solve?
**Answer: No single family holds every needed property at once — a composite combines families (fibreglass: polymer lightness with glass strength) to engineer the missing combination.**
