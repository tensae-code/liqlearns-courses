# Materials — metals, ceramics, polymers and composites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A material is defined as a substance or mixture with…
a) A crystal lattice  b) A definite purpose  c) Metallic bonding  d) A high melting point
**Answer: b) A definite purpose**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The six property groups are counted by…
a) Colour  b) The acting influence — mechanical, electric, thermal, magnetic, optical, chemical  c) Price  d) Origin
**Answer: b) The acting influence — mechanical, electric, thermal, magnetic, optical, chemical**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Compared with metals, ceramics are…
a) Softer and more plastic  b) Harder, far more brittle, mostly insulators  c) Better conductors  d) Lower-melting
**Answer: b) Harder, far more brittle, mostly insulators**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Fibreglass is a composite of…
a) Two metals  b) A polymer matrix and glass fibres  c) Glass and ceramic only  d) Rubber and sulfur
**Answer: b) A polymer matrix and glass fibres**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Optical fibres beat copper cable partly by…
a) Conducting electricity better  b) Total internal reflection — immune to electric and magnetic fields  c) Being heavier  d) Melting lower
**Answer: b) Total internal reflection — immune to electric and magnetic fields**
