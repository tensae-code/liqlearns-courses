# Water purification — filtering and chlorination — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Passing water through gravel, sand and cloth to remove suspended matter is called _____.
**Answer: filtering**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Adding a measured amount of chlorine to kill germs in water is called _____.
**Answer: chlorinating**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The invisible, dangerous share of dirty water is its _____.
**Answer: germs**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Water is left to _____ first, so the heaviest matter sinks before filtering.
**Answer: settle**
