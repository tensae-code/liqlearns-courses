# Water purification — filtering and chlorination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The dangerous share of dirty drinking water is…
a) The suspended soil  b) The germs — invisible, and able to spread disease even in clear-looking water  c) The colour  d) The sand
**Answer: b) The germs — invisible, and able to spread disease even in clear-looking water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Purification is always at least two jobs because…
a) Water is heavy  b) Suspended matter must be taken out and germs must be killed — no single step does both  c) Filters are expensive  d) Chlorine changes the colour
**Answer: b) Suspended matter must be taken out and germs must be killed — no single step does both**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In filtering, the solid held back by the filter is called the…
a) Filtrate  b) Residue  c) Chlorine  d) Solution
**Answer: b) Residue**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Filtering cannot make water fully safe because it does not stop…
a) Sand  b) Dissolved matter, and reliably not germs — both pass through with the water  c) Water itself  d) Light
**Answer: b) Dissolved matter, and reliably not germs — both pass through with the water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Chlorinating the filtered water…
a) Removes the sand  b) Kills the germs the filter let through, and a trace guards the water afterwards  c) Makes the water salty  d) Boils the water
**Answer: b) Kills the germs the filter let through, and a trace guards the water afterwards**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The purification chain, in working order, is…
a) Chlorinate → settle → filter  b) Filter → chlorinate → settle  c) Settle → filter → chlorinate  d) Settle → chlorinate → drink at once
**Answer: c) Settle → filter → chlorinate**
