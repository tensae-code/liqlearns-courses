---

Today: **Water purification** — Mongolia's way: the stated goal of its Grade 9 water section.

Water can fool you twice. It can look muddy and be mostly harmless grit — or look crystal clear and carry germs that make a family ill. So purification is two jobs, not one.
First, take out what is suspended. Let the water settle — the heavy dirt sinks. Then filter it: gravel, sand, cloth. The grit stays behind; the water runs clear. But clear is not safe — the germs slipped through with the water.
So, second job: chlorinate. A small, measured dose of chlorine kills the germs, and a trace of it stands guard in the water afterwards.
Settle, filter, chlorinate — and test, never trust a look. That is how drinking water is made fit.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
