# Water purification — filtering and chlorination — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Why water must be purified — the water there is, Filtering — taking out what is suspended, Chlorinating — and recognising water), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, sorting a list of water troubles to the step that answers each, and arguing why the chain's order — settle, filter, chlorinate — cannot be shuffled without losing something.
