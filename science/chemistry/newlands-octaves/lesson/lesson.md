## Newlands' octaves

Newlands arranged the 56 known elements by increasing atomic weight and found every eighth element repeats the first's properties — named for the musical octave, and introduced through an activity on repetition in Ethiopian cultural music. Limitations: undiscovered noble gases had no place, and the repetition breaks down beyond calcium.
