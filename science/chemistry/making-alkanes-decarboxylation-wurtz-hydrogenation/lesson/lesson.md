## Making alkanes

Laboratory/industrial routes: decarboxylation — heating sodium acetate with soda lime (NaOH/CaO) gives methane (the named lab synthesis); the Wurtz reaction — alkyl halides with sodium couple into longer alkanes; hydrogenation of unsaturated hydrocarbons over nickel;

and fractional distillation of petroleum as the bulk source; methane also forms by decay of animal/plant matter (biogas link).
