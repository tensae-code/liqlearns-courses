# Kinds of alcohols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is a trihydric alcohol?
a) –one  b) One with three –OH groups  c) Fermentation of sugars  d) The Williamson synthesis
**Answer: b) One with three –OH groups**

**2. ** What makes an alcohol tertiary?
a) The –OH carbon holds three other carbons  b) Nothing — it spectates  c) Neutralising power  d) H₂O
**Answer: a) The –OH carbon holds three other carbons**

**3. ** How many –OH groups has a monohydric alcohol?
a) Molasses  b) The C=C double bond  c) Respiration, decay and combustion  d) One
**Answer: d) One**

**4. ** What carbon is judged for primary/secondary/tertiary?
a) A positive ion (cation)  b) d electrons can also be lost  c) The carbon carrying the –OH  d) It shrinks
**Answer: c) The carbon carrying the –OH**
