# Kinds of alcohols — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is a trihydric alcohol?
**Answer: One with three –OH groups**

**2. ** What makes an alcohol tertiary?
**Answer: The –OH carbon holds three other carbons**

**3. ** How many –OH groups has a monohydric alcohol?
**Answer: One**

**4. ** What carbon is judged for primary/secondary/tertiary?
**Answer: The carbon carrying the –OH**
