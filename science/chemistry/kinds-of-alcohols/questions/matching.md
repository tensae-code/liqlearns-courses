# Kinds of alcohols — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What is a trihydric alcohol? — b
2. What makes an alcohol tertiary? — c
3. How many –OH groups has a monohydric alcohol? — d
4. What carbon is judged for primary/secondary/tertiary? — a

**Answer key:** 1–b: One with three –OH groups · 2–c: The –OH carbon holds three other carbons · 3–d: One · 4–a: The carbon carrying the –OH
