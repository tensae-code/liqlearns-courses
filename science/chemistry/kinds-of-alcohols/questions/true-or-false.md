# Kinds of alcohols — true or false

**1. ** What is a trihydric alcohol — One with three –OH groups

**Answer: False**

**3. ** How many –OH groups has a monohydric alcohol — One
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What carbon is judged for primary/secondary/tertiary — A photon
