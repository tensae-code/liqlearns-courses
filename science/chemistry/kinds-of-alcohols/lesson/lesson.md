# Kinds of alcohols

## Do it first
Glycerol carries three –OH groups; ethanol one. One alcohol's –OH sits on a carbon holding one other carbon; another's on a carbon holding three. Before reading, say the two ways alcohols are sorted here.

[Picture 21683 — Primary, secondary, tertiary skeletons.]

By –OH count: monohydric (one), dihydric (two) and trihydric (three) alcohols.

By the carbon carrying the –OH: primary (that carbon holds one other carbon), secondary (two) and tertiary (three).

The two sortings are independent — an alcohol takes one label from each.
