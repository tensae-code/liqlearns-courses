# Video script — Kinds of alcohols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Glycerol carries three –OH groups; ethanol one.

By –OH count: monohydric (one), dihydric (two) and trihydric (three) alcohols.

By the carbon carrying the –OH: primary (that carbon holds one other carbon), secondary (two) and tertiary (three).

The two sortings are independent — an alcohol takes one label from each.

Now you. Say it back: What is a trihydric alcohol?
Answer: One with three –OH groups.
