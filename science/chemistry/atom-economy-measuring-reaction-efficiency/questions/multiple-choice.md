# Atom economy — measuring reaction efficiency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Atom economy measures…
a) How fast a reaction runs  b) The share of reactant atoms ending in the wanted product  c) The purity of the product  d) The temperature of reaction
**Answer: b) The share of reactant atoms ending in the wanted product**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Atom economy is calculated from…
a) A titration  b) The balanced equation's mole masses  c) The colour change  d) The reaction time
**Answer: b) The balanced equation's mole masses**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An addition reaction's atom economy is…
a) 0%  b) 50%  c) 100%  d) Always the highest cost
**Answer: c) 100%**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Percentage yield differs from atom economy because yield…
a) Is known before the experiment  b) Is measured after the fact against the equation's promise  c) Ignores by-products entirely  d) Uses no masses
**Answer: b) Is measured after the fact against the equation's promise**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A by-product in the equation does what to atom economy?
a) Raises it  b) Lowers it by the mass discarded  c) Leaves it unchanged  d) Doubles it
**Answer: b) Lowers it by the mass discarded**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) Atom economy is used to…
a) Choose between rival routes on paper first  b) Weigh a precipitate  c) Set the temperature  d) Name the products
**Answer: a) Choose between rival routes on paper first**
