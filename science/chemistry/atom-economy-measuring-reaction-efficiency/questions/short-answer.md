# Atom economy — measuring reaction efficiency — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define atom economy and give its formula.
**Answer: The percentage of the reactants' mass that the balanced equation can turn into the wanted product: (mass of wanted product ÷ total mass of all products) × 100.**

**2.** Why does the book place atom economy inside stoichiometry?
**Answer: Because it is a calculation on the equation itself — mole masses and ratios — run before experiment, the same arithmetic family as limiting reactants and yield, one step earlier in the judging.**

**3.** Two routes make the same product; one is an addition, one discards a by-product. Which does atom economy pick, and what can it not tell you?
**Answer: The addition (100%). It cannot tell you the yield, rate or conditions — those must still be measured; atom economy judges only the equation's built-in waste.**
