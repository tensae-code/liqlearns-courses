# Hands-on — Atom economy — measuring reaction efficiency

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Score two routes (paper only)
Route A: reactants of total mass 100 (equation masses) give 80 of wanted product and 20 of by-product. Route B is an addition giving 100 of product from 100 of reactants. Compute both atom economies and write which route you would trial first, with the reason in one sentence.

## Equation audit (paper only)
Take any substitution equation you know. Circle the atoms that end in the wanted product and box the atoms discarded. Under it, write what change of reaction type would raise its atom economy, and why.
