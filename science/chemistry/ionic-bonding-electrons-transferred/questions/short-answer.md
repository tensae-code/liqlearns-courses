# Ionic bonding — electrons transferred — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** In ionic bonding, what moves between atoms?
**Answer: Electrons are transferred**

**2. ** What does the metal atom become?
**Answer: A positive ion (cation)**

**3. ** What holds the ions together?
**Answer: Electrostatic attraction**

**4. ** What does the non-metal become?
**Answer: A negative ion (anion)**
