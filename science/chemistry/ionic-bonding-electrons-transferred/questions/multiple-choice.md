# Ionic bonding — electrons transferred — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** In ionic bonding, what moves between atoms?
a) The pollution lessons  b) Electrons are transferred  c) Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet  d) Rubber, cellulose or protein
**Answer: b) Electrons are transferred**

**2. ** What does the metal atom become?
a) A positive ion (cation)  b) To predict new elements' properties  c) It rises from 1 to 8  d) Its ions are locked in place
**Answer: a) A positive ion (cation)**

**3. ** What holds the ions together?
a) The –OH position  b) An alkene  c) Williamson  d) Electrostatic attraction
**Answer: d) Electrostatic attraction**

**4. ** What does the non-metal become?
a) Acidic  b) It gives two replaceable H⁺  c) A negative ion (anion)  d) The conjugate base
**Answer: c) A negative ion (anion)**
