# Line spectra — fingerprints of elements — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does a line spectrum show?
**Answer: Only certain sharp lines**

**2. ** What kind of spectrum is a rainbow?
**Answer: A continuous spectrum**

**3. ** Why are line spectra called fingerprints?
**Answer: Each element's pattern is unique**

**4. ** When a gas gives out light, what spectrum is it?
**Answer: An emission spectrum**
