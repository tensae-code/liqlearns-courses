# Line spectra — fingerprints of elements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What does a line spectrum show — Only certain sharp lines
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What kind of spectrum is a rainbow — Singly occupy each equal orbital first
**Answer: False**

**3. ** Why are line spectra called fingerprints — Each element's pattern is unique
**Answer: True**

**4. ** When a gas gives out light, what spectrum is it — Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH
**Answer: False**
