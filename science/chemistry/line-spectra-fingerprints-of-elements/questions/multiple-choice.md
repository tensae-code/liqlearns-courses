# Line spectra — fingerprints of elements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does a line spectrum show?
a) A small molecule that joins into chains  b) The biosphere  c) Only certain sharp lines  d) Care/treatment — it pollutes
**Answer: c) Only certain sharp lines**

**2. ** What kind of spectrum is a rainbow?
a) Lattice formation  b) A continuous spectrum  c) Properties repeat periodically with atomic number  d) Eight electrons — an octet
**Answer: b) A continuous spectrum**

**3. ** Why are line spectra called fingerprints?
a) Each element's pattern is unique  b) Le Chatelier's principle  c) Hydrogen bonds  d) An –OH group
**Answer: a) Each element's pattern is unique**

**4. ** When a gas gives out light, what spectrum is it?
a) Tenfold  b) 7 — neutral  c) Acidic  d) An emission spectrum
**Answer: d) An emission spectrum**
