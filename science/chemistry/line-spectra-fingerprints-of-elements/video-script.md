# Video script — Line spectra — fingerprints of elements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pass sunlight through a prism and you get a full rainbow band.

A continuous spectrum shows every colour blending into the next, like a rainbow.

A line spectrum shows only certain sharp lines — only certain frequencies are present.

Heated or discharged gases emit line spectra (emission); gases can also absorb their own lines (absorption).

Each element's line pattern is unique — a fingerprint by which the element can be identified.

Now you. Say it back: What does a line spectrum show?
Answer: Only certain sharp lines.
