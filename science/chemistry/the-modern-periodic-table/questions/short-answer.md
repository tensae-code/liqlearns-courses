# The modern periodic table — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** By what are elements arranged in the modern table?
**Answer: Increasing atomic number**

**2. ** What are the table's rows called?
**Answer: Periods**

**3. ** What are its columns called?
**Answer: Groups**

**4. ** What does the periodic law state?
**Answer: Properties repeat periodically with atomic number**
