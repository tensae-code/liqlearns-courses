# The modern periodic table — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** By what are elements arranged in the modern table?
a) An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)  b) Kinked tails cannot pack closely  c) Increasing atomic number  d) Flavourings and perfumes
**Answer: c) Increasing atomic number**

**2. ** What are the table's rows called?
a) Fewer gas moles on the product side  b) Periods  c) Spontaneous as written  d) Turns raw materials into valuable products
**Answer: b) Periods**

**3. ** What are its columns called?
a) Groups  b) atomos  c) The electron  d) Photons knock electrons off a metal
**Answer: a) Groups**

**4. ** What does the periodic law state?
a) ½(bonding − antibonding electrons)  b) Particles are far apart  c) The partial pressures  d) Properties repeat periodically with atomic number
**Answer: d) Properties repeat periodically with atomic number**
