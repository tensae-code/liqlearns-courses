## Oxidising and reducing agents

The oxidising agent is the substance reduced (it takes electrons / its oxidation number falls); the reducing agent is the substance oxidised — the agent is named for what it does to the other. Analysis routine: assign oxidation numbers, find what rose (oxidised, the reducing agent) and what fell (reduced, the oxidising agent);

reactions with no oxidation-number change are non-redox (neutralisation and double displacement are the named examples); metals are typically strong reducing agents, nonmetals strong oxidising agents.
