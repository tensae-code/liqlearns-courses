# Oxidising and reducing agents — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** the reducing agent is the _____ oxidised — the agent is named for what it does to the other.
**Answer: substance**

**2. ** Analysis routine: assign oxidation numbers, find what rose (_____, the reducing agent) and what fell (reduced, the oxidising agent);
**Answer: oxidised**

**3. ** reactions with no oxidation-number change are non-redox (_____ and double displacement are the named examples);
**Answer: neutralisation**

**4. ** metals are typically strong reducing agents, _____ strong oxidising agents.
**Answer: nonmetals**
