# Phenol — properties, preparation and uses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Phenol's formula, –OH on a benzene ring, is written C₆H₅_____.
**Answer: OH**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The white precipitate phenol gives with bromine water is 2,4,6-_____phenol.
**Answer: tribromo**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) With iron(III) chloride solution, phenol gives a _____ colour.
**Answer: violet**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The industrial route to phenol from benzene is named after its intermediate, _____.
**Answer: cumene**
