# Phenol — properties, preparation and uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Phenol differs from the alcohols because its –OH group is…
a) Missing  b) Joined directly to a benzene ring  c) Joined to two carbons  d) Replaced by –COOH
**Answer: b) Joined directly to a benzene ring**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Phenol's old name, carbolic acid, records that phenol is…
a) A carboxylic acid  b) A strong acid  c) Weakly acidic  d) Not acidic at all
**Answer: c) Weakly acidic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Phenol reacts with sodium hydroxide solution, but unlike ethanoic acid it does NOT…
a) Dissolve in water at all  b) Fizz with carbonates — it is weaker than carbonic acid  c) React with sodium  d) Smell
**Answer: b) Fizz with carbonates — it is weaker than carbonic acid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Phenol's recognition test with bromine water gives…
a) No reaction  b) A blue solution  c) Immediate decolourisation and a white precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromophenol  d) A silver mirror
**Answer: c) Immediate decolourisation and a white precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromophenol**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The modern industrial preparation of phenol is…
a) From coal tar only  b) The cumene process from benzene, with acetone as co-product  c) Fermentation  d) From seawater
**Answer: b) The cumene process from benzene, with acetone as co-product**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Bakelite is…
a) A natural resin  b) A phenol–formaldehyde resin — among the first synthetic plastics  c) A kind of glass  d) A phenol dye
**Answer: b) A phenol–formaldehyde resin — among the first synthetic plastics**
