Today: **Phenol — properties, preparation and uses** — Belarus's way: a full phenol strand closing the alcohols part — the relative that behaves differently.

Start with the puzzle — the same –OH group as ethanol, yet this substance reacts with sodium hydroxide and cleaned the old hospitals as carbolic acid. The difference is the seat: in phenol the –OH sits directly on a benzene ring, and the ring makes it weakly acidic.
Its tests tell the story in order. Sodium — yes. Sodium hydroxide — yes, where ethanol refuses. Carbonates — no fizz; it is weaker even than carbonic acid. And bromine water falls white at once with tribromophenol, no catalyst — the ring itself is wide awake.
Industry wins it from benzene through cumene, gaining acetone on the way, and builds Bakelite, antiseptics and dyes from it. Two sections, one molecule, whole.
