Today: **Chemical reactions — reactants become products**.

A physical change forms no new substance (evaporating, powdering sugar, melting ice);

a chemical change forms one or more new substances with new composition (milk curdling, photosynthesis, eggs rotting).

Stoichiometry is named at the outset as the quantitative study of reactants and products.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
