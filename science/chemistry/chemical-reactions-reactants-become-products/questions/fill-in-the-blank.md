# Chemical reactions — reactants become products — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** a chemical change forms one or more new substances with new composition (milk _____, photosynthesis, eggs rotting).
**Answer: curdling**

**2. ** Stoichiometry is named at the outset as the quantitative study of _____ and products.
**Answer: reactants**

**3. ** A physical change forms no new substance (_____, powdering sugar, melting ice);
**Answer: evaporating**

**4. ** a chemical change forms one or more new substances with new composition (milk _____, photosynthesis, eggs rotting).
**Answer: curdling**
