## Chemical reactions

A physical change forms no new substance (evaporating, powdering sugar, melting ice); a chemical change forms one or more new substances with new composition (milk curdling, photosynthesis, eggs rotting). A chemical reaction converts reactants into products with completely different characteristics (sulphur + oxygen → sulphur dioxide, a pungent colourless gas), and its tell-tale signs are listed: new substances, heat and/or light produced, colour change, temperature change.

Stoichiometry is named at the outset as the quantitative study of reactants and products.
