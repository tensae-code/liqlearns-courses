# Valence bond theory and hybridisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What overlap makes a sigma bond?
a) Ethiopia's own resource base  b) NO  c) End-to-end (axial) overlap  d) Oxygen and water
**Answer: c) End-to-end (axial) overlap**

**2. ** Which hybridisation has methane's carbon?
a) The proton number Z  b) sp³  c) In fixed whole-number ratios  d) A tiny, dense, positive centre exists
**Answer: b) sp³**

**3. ** Why is a sigma bond stronger than pi?
a) Its overlap concentrates density between nuclei  b) Diamond  c) A manometer  d) Resistance to flow
**Answer: a) Its overlap concentrates density between nuclei**

**4. ** What is promotion?
a) Its C=C double bond(s)  b) Ethyl ethanoate  c) Flavours, perfumes or solvents  d) Lifting an electron so orbitals stand ready
**Answer: d) Lifting an electron so orbitals stand ready**
