# Valence bond theory and hybridisation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What overlap makes a sigma bond?
**Answer: End-to-end (axial) overlap**

**2. ** Which hybridisation has methane's carbon?
**Answer: sp³**

**3. ** Why is a sigma bond stronger than pi?
**Answer: Its overlap concentrates density between nuclei**

**4. ** What is promotion?
**Answer: Lifting an electron so orbitals stand ready**
