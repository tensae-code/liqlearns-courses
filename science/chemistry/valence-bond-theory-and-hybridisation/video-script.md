Carbon's ground state has only two unpaired electrons, yet carbon forms four equal bonds in methane.

Valence bond theory: a covalent bond forms where atomic orbitals overlap, the shared pair sitting in the overlap.

A sigma (σ) bond is end-to-end (axial) overlap; a pi (π) bond is sideways overlap.

Hybridisation mixes an atom's orbitals before bonding: sp³ hybrids (tetrahedral, as in methane), sp² (trigonal planar, as in ethene) and sp (linear, as in ethyne).

AlCl₃ is sp² while AlCl₄⁻ differs — hybridisation follows the actual bonded arrangement (teacher guide evidence).

Now you. Say it back: What overlap makes a sigma bond?
Answer: End-to-end (axial) overlap.
