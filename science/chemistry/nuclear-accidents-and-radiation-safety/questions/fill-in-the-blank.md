# Nuclear accidents and radiation safety — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The radioactive substance inside the Goiânia radiotherapy capsule was caesium-_____.
**Answer: 137**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Goiânia accident happened in the year _____.
**Answer: 1987**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The people who handled the capsule did not know the _____.
**Answer: danger**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A finished radioactive source is disposed of as radioactive _____ under control.
**Answer: waste**
