# Video script — Alcohol properties — hydrogen bonding again

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethanol mixes with water in all proportions and boils far above a similar-sized alkane.

Alcohols hydrogen-bond through their –OH groups — to each other and to water.

Hydrogen bonding raises boiling points well above comparable alkanes.

Short-chain alcohols mix fully with water; as the hydrocarbon chain lengthens, solubility falls — the chain behaves like an alkane.

Now you. Say it back: What bonds alcohols to water?
Answer: Hydrogen bonds.
