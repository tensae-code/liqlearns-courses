# Alcohol properties — hydrogen bonding again

## Do it first
Ethanol mixes with water in all proportions and boils far above a similar-sized alkane. Before reading, name the force from Unit 2 that explains both facts.

[Picture 21685 — Ethanol hydrogen-bonded in water.]

Alcohols hydrogen-bond through their –OH groups — to each other and to water.

Hydrogen bonding raises boiling points well above comparable alkanes.

Short-chain alcohols mix fully with water; as the hydrocarbon chain lengthens, solubility falls — the chain behaves like an alkane.
