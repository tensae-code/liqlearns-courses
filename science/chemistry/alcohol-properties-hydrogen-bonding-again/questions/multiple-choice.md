# Alcohol properties — hydrogen bonding again — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What bonds alcohols to water?
a) They become single bonds  b) Open versus sealed storage (rancidity)  c) An empty orbital of the acid  d) Hydrogen bonds
**Answer: d) Hydrogen bonds**

**2. ** What happens to solubility as the chain grows?
a) Care — dose, protection, water safety  b) Clay  c) It falls  d) Nitric acid, HNO₃
**Answer: c) It falls**

**3. ** Why do alcohols boil high?
a) Position and momentum  b) Hydrogen bonding between molecules  c) The isotopes, weighted by abundance  d) An emission spectrum
**Answer: b) Hydrogen bonding between molecules**

**4. ** Which alcohols mix fully with water?
a) The short-chain ones  b) Inward pull on surface particles  c) The rate constant  d) Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance
**Answer: a) The short-chain ones**
