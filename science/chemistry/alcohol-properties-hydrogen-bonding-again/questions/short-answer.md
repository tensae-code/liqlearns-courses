# Alcohol properties — hydrogen bonding again — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What bonds alcohols to water?
**Answer: Hydrogen bonds**

**2. ** What happens to solubility as the chain grows?
**Answer: It falls**

**3. ** Why do alcohols boil high?
**Answer: Hydrogen bonding between molecules**

**4. ** Which alcohols mix fully with water?
**Answer: The short-chain ones**
