# Ethers — structure and names — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Write the ether general form.
**Answer: R–O–R**

**2. ** What does an ether lack that an alcohol has?
**Answer: An –OH group**

**3. ** How do common ether names end?
**Answer: With the word 'ether'**

**4. ** What is the IUPAC style for ethers?
**Answer: Alkoxyalkane**
