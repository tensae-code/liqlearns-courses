# Ethers — structure and names — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Write the ether general form.
a) R–O–R  b) Iron  c) It would form a corrosive mist  d) Soap or paper making
**Answer: a) R–O–R**

**2. ** What does an ether lack that an alcohol has?
a) In the nucleus  b) The light's frequency  c) Multi-electron spectra  d) An –OH group
**Answer: d) An –OH group**

**3. ** How do common ether names end?
a) Glass  b) Yes, to reactants  c) With the word 'ether'  d) At constant T, P × V is constant
**Answer: c) With the word 'ether'**

**4. ** What is the IUPAC style for ethers?
a) The fat's ester links (by water)  b) Alkoxyalkane  c) Making soap by alkaline hydrolysis of fats  d) Three — one per ester link
**Answer: b) Alkoxyalkane**
