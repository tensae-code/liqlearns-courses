# Ethers — structure and names

## Do it first
Two carbon groups, one oxygen bridge. Before reading, say why an ether is not an alcohol even though both carry C, H and O.

[Picture 21689 — An ether with its oxygen bridge.]

An ether has the general form R–O–R: an oxygen bridging two carbon groups, with no –OH.

IUPAC names call the smaller group an alkoxy substituent (alkoxyalkane names).

Common names name both groups followed by 'ether' (as in diethyl ether).
