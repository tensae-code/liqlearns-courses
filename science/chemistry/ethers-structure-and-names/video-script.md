# Video script — Ethers — structure and names

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two carbon groups, one oxygen bridge.

An ether has the general form R–O–R: an oxygen bridging two carbon groups, with no –OH.

IUPAC names call the smaller group an alkoxy substituent (alkoxyalkane names).

Common names name both groups followed by 'ether' (as in diethyl ether).

Now you. Say it back: Write the ether general form.
Answer: R–O–R.
