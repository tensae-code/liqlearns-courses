## Galvanic cells

Galvanic cells convert chemical energy into electrical energy through a spontaneous redox reaction split into two half-cells: the Daniell cell (zinc strip in its solution, copper in its, joined by wire and salt bridge) is dissected: oxidation at the zinc anode (negative in a galvanic cell), reduction at the copper cathode (positive), the two half-reactions, and their sum as the cell reaction. Oxidation always at the anode, reduction at the cathode, in every cell type.
