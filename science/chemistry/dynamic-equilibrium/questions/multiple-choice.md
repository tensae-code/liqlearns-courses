# Dynamic equilibrium — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What becomes equal at equilibrium?
a) The forward and reverse rates  b) Limestone and clay  c) Chrome and vegetable tanning  d) The tail (water-hating end)
**Answer: a) The forward and reverse rates**

**2. ** Does reaction stop at equilibrium?
a) The main energy level (and orbital size)  b) Periods  c) Predict its properties  d) No — it is dynamic, both ways continue
**Answer: d) No — it is dynamic, both ways continue**

**3. ** What stays constant at equilibrium?
a) Unreacted gases  b) The longest chain carrying –OH  c) Macroscopic properties (concentrations and so on)  d) How far the equilibrium lies toward products
**Answer: c) Macroscopic properties (concentrations and so on)**

**4. ** What do rate curves do as equilibrium nears?
a) Henderson–Hasselbalch  b) They converge to one level  c) H⁺ ions  d) 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴
**Answer: b) They converge to one level**
