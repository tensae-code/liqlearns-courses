# Dynamic equilibrium — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What becomes equal at equilibrium? — a
2. Does reaction stop at equilibrium? — b
3. What stays constant at equilibrium? — c
4. What do rate curves do as equilibrium nears? — d

**Answer key:** 1–a: The forward and reverse rates · 2–b: No — it is dynamic, both ways continue · 3–c: Macroscopic properties (concentrations and so on) · 4–d: They converge to one level
