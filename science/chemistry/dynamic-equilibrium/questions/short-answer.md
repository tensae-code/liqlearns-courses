# Dynamic equilibrium — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What becomes equal at equilibrium?
**Answer: The forward and reverse rates**

**2. ** Does reaction stop at equilibrium?
**Answer: No — it is dynamic, both ways continue**

**3. ** What stays constant at equilibrium?
**Answer: Macroscopic properties (concentrations and so on)**

**4. ** What do rate curves do as equilibrium nears?
**Answer: They converge to one level**
