# Dynamic equilibrium — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What becomes equal at equilibrium — The forward and reverse rates
**Answer: True**

**2. ** Does reaction stop at equilibrium — H⁺ ions
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What stays constant at equilibrium — Macroscopic properties (concentrations and so on)
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What do rate curves do as equilibrium nears — Photosynthesis
**Answer: False**
