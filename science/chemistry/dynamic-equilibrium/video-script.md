# Video script — Dynamic equilibrium

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

At equilibrium the mixture looks frozen — nothing changes.

Equilibrium is reached when the forward and reverse rates become equal.

From then on, macroscopic properties (concentrations, colour, pressure) stay constant — not because reaction stopped, but because the two rates balance.

The balance is dynamic: both reactions continue at equal rates.

Now you. Say it back: What becomes equal at equilibrium?
Answer: The forward and reverse rates.
