# Dynamic equilibrium

## Do it first
At equilibrium the mixture looks frozen — nothing changes. Yet both reactions are still running. Before reading, say what exactly is equal, if concentrations need not be.

[Picture 21673 — Rate curves meeting and running level.]

Equilibrium is reached when the forward and reverse rates become equal.

From then on, macroscopic properties (concentrations, colour, pressure) stay constant — not because reaction stopped, but because the two rates balance.

The balance is dynamic: both reactions continue at equal rates. Rate–time graphs show the two rates converging to one level line; concentration–time graphs level off.
