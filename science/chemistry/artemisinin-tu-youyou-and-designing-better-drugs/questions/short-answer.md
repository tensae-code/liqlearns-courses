# Artemisinin — Tu Youyou — and designing better drugs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing China's book teaches in this lesson — point 1 of the key ideas.
**Answer: Drug design from molecular structure and dose: in the 1970s Chinese scientists, inspired by traditional medical texts, isolated artemisinin from sweet wormwood, then modified its structure into a family of antimalarials that saved millions (Tu Youyou's 2015 Nobel Prize photograph); sustained-release designs (polymer-coated aspirin) show chemistry improving a drug's delivery.**
