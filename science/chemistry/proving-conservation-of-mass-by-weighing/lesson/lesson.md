## Sub lesson 1 — The sealed flask — silver nitrate and hydrochloric acid

Two printed experiments verify the law by weighing: (1) silver nitrate solution tipped from a slanted test tube into dilute hydrochloric acid inside a stoppered conical flask — white silver chloride precipitates, and the sealed flask weighs the same before and after; (2) barium nitrate and sodium sulphate solutions mixed, the barium sulphate precipitate filtered, dried and weighed, and the sodium nitrate recovered from the filtrate by evaporation, so all product masses are accounted. A calculation applies the law (potassium carbonate mass from KOH + CO₂ − water).
