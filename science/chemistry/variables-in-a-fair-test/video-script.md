Today: **Variables in a fair test** — Libya's way: change one thing, hold the rest, and the result can be trusted.
Every investigation has three kinds of variable. The independent variable is the one thing you change. The dependent variable is what you measure. The controlled variables are everything else, held fixed. Libya's practical course opens with an unknown solid dissolving in water: guess which variables could matter, test ONE, time the dissolving, and graph the result. Change two things at once and the experiment can no longer say which one did it.
