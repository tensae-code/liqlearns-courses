# Variables in a fair test — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The variable you choose to change is the _____ variable.
**Answer: independent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Variables held fixed so they cannot spoil the comparison are _____ variables.
**Answer: controlled**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An intelligent guess about which variables could matter, made before testing, is a _____.
**Answer: hypothesis**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The dissolving investigation measures the _____ taken for complete dissolution.
**Answer: time**
