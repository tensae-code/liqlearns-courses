## Sub lesson 1 — From a solid — weigh, dissolve, make to the mark

Practical preparation from a solid runs in four steps: calculate the mass you need, weigh it, dissolve it, and make the solution up to the mark in a volumetric flask — 250.0 mL of 0.100 M NaOH is the worked style. The safety order acid-into-water, carried over from Grade 9, holds throughout.

## Sub lesson 2 — By dilution — from a concentrated stock

The second direction is dilution: you start from a concentrated stock solution and add solvent until the concentration falls to the one you want. The amounts are linked by the C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ logic — concentration times volume before dilution equals concentration times volume after it — so you calculate the stock volume to measure, transfer it, and make up to the mark.
