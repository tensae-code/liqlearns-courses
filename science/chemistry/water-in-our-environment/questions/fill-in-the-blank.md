# Water in our environment — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The great store of Earth's water is the seas and _____.
**Answer: oceans**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Agriculture and livestock take about _____ percent of fresh water.
**Answer: 70**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A watercourse that runs after rain and is dry much of the year is called a _____ (oued).
**Answer: wadi**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The investigation method the Djibouti chapters run is the démarche d'_____.
**Answer: investigation**
