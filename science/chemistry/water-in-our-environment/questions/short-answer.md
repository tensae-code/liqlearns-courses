# Water in our environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is most of Earth's water not directly usable?
**Answer: Most of it is sea water — salt water that cannot be drunk or used on crops as it is. The fresh water people need is only a small part of the whole, held in rivers, lakes, groundwater and ice.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one fresh food and one dry food, and say which holds more water and how you can tell.
**Answer: A fresh fruit or vegetable holds much water — it is juicy when cut and loses weight and shrivels as it dries. A dry food such as grain holds very little. (Any correct pair with the evidence described earns the mark.)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Where can the biggest saving of fresh water be made, and why?
**Answer: In agriculture and livestock, because the guide gives them about 70% of fresh water use — the biggest share, so small savings there outweigh larger savings in small uses.**
