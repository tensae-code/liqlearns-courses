# Water in our environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Most of Earth's water is…
a) Fresh water in rivers  b) Salt water in the seas and oceans  c) Water in foods  d) Groundwater only
**Answer: b) Salt water in the seas and oceans**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Fresh water people can use is found in…
a) Only the sea  b) Rivers, lakes, groundwater and ice  c) Fizzy drinks  d) Clouds only
**Answer: b) Rivers, lakes, groundwater and ice**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) About what share of fresh water do agriculture and livestock take, in the guide?
a) About 7%  b) About 30%  c) About 70%  d) About 100%
**Answer: c) About 70%**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The human body, as the chapter teaches, is…
a) Almost all water  b) More than half water  c) One tenth water  d) Dry inside
**Answer: b) More than half water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Djibouti's chapters open with…
a) A test  b) A local situation-problème, then an investigation  c) A definition to copy  d) A song
**Answer: b) A local situation-problème, then an investigation**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The oued d'Ambouli in this chapter is…
a) A fizzy drink  b) A local watercourse (wadi) used as the opening situation  c) A laboratory chemical  d) A sea port
**Answer: b) A local watercourse (wadi) used as the opening situation**
