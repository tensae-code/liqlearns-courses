Today: **Water in our environment**.

Water is everywhere — and yet the water we can drink is a small share of it all. The seas hold most of Earth's water, and sea water is too salty to use. The fresh water we need sits in rivers, in lakes, under the ground and in ice. Your own body is more than half water, and your food carries water too. And who uses most fresh water? Farms and animals — about seventy in every hundred parts. So looking after water starts there. That is Djibouti's first chemistry lesson: know where your water is, and do not waste the share you can use.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
