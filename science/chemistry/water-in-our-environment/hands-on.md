# Hands-on — Water in our environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reservoir survey
Draw a simple map or diagram of where water is found around your home or school: taps, tanks, wells, bottles, any watercourse. For each place, write whether the water is usable as it is, usable after treatment, or not usable — and why. Compare your survey with a partner's: did you count the same reservoirs?

## Water in foods — look, do not taste chemicals
Take three fresh foods and one dry food (from the kitchen only — never from a laboratory). Describe the evidence each gives of its water: juice when cut, weight and feel, how it changes as it dries over a day on a clean plate. Record which holds most and which holds least, in the guide's order.
