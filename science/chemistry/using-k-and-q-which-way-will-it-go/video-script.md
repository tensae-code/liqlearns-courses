# Video script — Using K and Q — which way will it go?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mix the chemicals in any amounts you like — the mixture is not at equilibrium.

The reaction quotient Q is the equilibrium expression filled with the concentrations right now, not at equilibrium.

If Q < K, the reaction moves forward to make more products.

Equilibrium concentrations are found with the ICE method: tabulate Initial, Change and Equilibrium amounts, then solve with Kc.

Now you. Say it back: What is Q?
Answer: The mass-action ratio at current concentrations.
