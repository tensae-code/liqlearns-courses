# Using K and Q — which way will it go? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is Q?
a) The energy needed to remove an electron  b) The mass-action ratio at current concentrations  c) The particle's mass  d) The Aufbau principle
**Answer: b) The mass-action ratio at current concentrations**

**2. ** If Q < K, which way does reaction go?
a) Forward, making products  b) They converge to one level  c) The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance  d) The –OH carbon holds three other carbons
**Answer: a) Forward, making products**

**3. ** What does ICE stand for?
a) An exercise of the same kind  b) An electron-pair acceptor  c) Le Chatelier's principle  d) Initial, Change, Equilibrium
**Answer: d) Initial, Change, Equilibrium**

**4. ** When Q = K, what is true?
a) Silica — the glass former  b) Molasses  c) The system is at equilibrium  d) Weeds
**Answer: c) The system is at equilibrium**
