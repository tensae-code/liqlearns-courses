# Hands-on — Ionic lattice structures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a NaCl corner
With two colours of beads, bottle caps or drawn dots, build a 3×3×3 alternating block (in layers on paper if you have no beads): no two neighbours the same colour. Pick the centre ion and count its opposite-kind neighbours in your model — the ones touching it directly.

## Count like the book
Say the two counts from the lesson — NaCl type 6, CsCl type 8 — and point at where the 6 neighbours of a centre ion stand: above, below, and four around.

## The formula question
Hold one pair of beads and ask a companion: is this an NaCl molecule? Have them answer from the lesson — the crystal is the lattice; the formula is only the ratio.
