Today: **Ionic lattice structures** — Laos's way: no molecules at all — one endless alternating lattice.

Positive, negative, positive, negative, in three dimensions: that is an ionic crystal. In the NaCl type each ion touches six of the other kind; in the CsCl type, eight — the ions' sizes decide. And with no molecules, only the empirical formula is written.
