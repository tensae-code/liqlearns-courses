# Ionic lattice structures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The structure of an ionic compound is…
a) Separate molecules  b) A regular three-dimensional lattice of alternating ions  c) Chains of atoms  d) Layers of molecules
**Answer: b) A regular three-dimensional lattice of alternating ions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) For an ionic compound we write…
a) Its molecular formula  b) Only its empirical formula — the simplest ratio  c) No formula at all  d) The lattice diagram as its formula
**Answer: b) Only its empirical formula — the simplest ratio**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the NaCl (rock-salt) type, each ion touches…
a) 4 of the other kind  b) 6 of the other kind  c) 8 of the other kind  d) 12 of the other kind
**Answer: b) 6 of the other kind**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The CsCl type is the structure of…
a) MgO  b) KCl  c) CsBr  d) NaF
**Answer: c) CsBr**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) What decides whether a compound takes the 6-neighbour or the 8-neighbour type?
a) Its colour  b) The ions' sizes  c) Its melting point  d) The solvent
**Answer: b) The ions' sizes**
