# Ionic lattice structures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Describe the NaCl type and name three compounds the book lists in its family.
**Answer:** Each ion touches 6 ions of the other kind in a regular alternating lattice (the rock-salt structure). Book's family includes NaCl, NaF, NaBr, KCl, MgO, CaO.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the CsCl type differ from the NaCl type?
**Answer:** In the CsCl type each ion touches 8 of the other kind instead of 6; the book lists CsCl, CsBr, CsI and NH₄Cl.
