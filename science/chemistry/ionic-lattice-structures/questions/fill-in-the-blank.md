# Ionic lattice structures — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** An ionic compound has no separate molecules, so only its ______ formula is written.
**Answer:** empirical

**2.** In the NaCl type each ion touches ______ ions of the other kind.
**Answer:** 6 (six)

**3.** In the CsCl type each ion touches ______ ions of the other kind.
**Answer:** 8 (eight)
