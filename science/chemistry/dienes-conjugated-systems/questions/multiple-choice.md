# Dienes — conjugated systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A diene is a hydrocarbon carrying…
a) One triple bond  b) Two C=C double bonds in one molecule  c) A benzene ring  d) Two –OH groups
**Answer: b) Two C=C double bonds in one molecule**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) CH₂=CH–CH₂–CH=CH₂ is an example of…
a) A cumulated diene  b) A conjugated diene  c) An isolated diene  d) An alkyne
**Answer: c) An isolated diene**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a conjugated diene, the π electrons…
a) Vanish  b) Belong to one carbon only  c) Spread (delocalise) over all four carbons of the system  d) Move into the nucleus
**Answer: c) Spread (delocalise) over all four carbons of the system**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The evidence that a conjugated diene reacts as one unit, not two separate alkenes, is…
a) Its colour  b) 1,4-addition — a reagent adding across the ends of the whole system  c) Its smell  d) Its boiling point
**Answer: b) 1,4-addition — a reagent adding across the ends of the whole system**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Natural rubber is the polymer of…
a) Ethene  b) Isoprene — 2-methylbuta-1,3-diene  c) Benzene  d) Ethyne
**Answer: b) Isoprene — 2-methylbuta-1,3-diene**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Vulcanisation improves rubber by…
a) Melting it  b) Cross-linking its chains with sulfur  c) Adding water  d) Removing its double bonds entirely
**Answer: b) Cross-linking its chains with sulfur**
