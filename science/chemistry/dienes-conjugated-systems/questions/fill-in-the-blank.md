# Dienes — conjugated systems — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A hydrocarbon with two double bonds in one molecule is called a _____.
**Answer: diene**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Double–single–double in alternation is the _____ arrangement.
**Answer: conjugated**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The spreading of π electrons over the whole conjugated system is called _____.
**Answer: delocalisation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Buta-1,3-diene joins into rubber by addition _____.
**Answer: polymerisation**
