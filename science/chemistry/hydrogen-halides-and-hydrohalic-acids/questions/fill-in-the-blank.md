# Hydrogen halides and hydrohalic acids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The hydrogen halide of bromine is hydrogen _____.
**Answer: bromide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In water, HBr becomes _____ acid.
**Answer: hydrobromic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The strongest of the four hydrohalic acids is _____ acid.
**Answer: hydriodic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) HF is singled out in the family because of its _____ bonding.
**Answer: hydrogen**
