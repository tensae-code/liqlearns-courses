# Hydrogen halides and hydrohalic acids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The hydrogen halides are…
a) HF, HCl, HBr, HI  b) HCl, NaCl, KCl, CaCl₂  c) HF, H₂O, NH₃, CH₄  d) HClO, HBrO, HIO, HFO
**Answer: a) HF, HCl, HBr, HI**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) All four hydrogen halides share which behaviour?
a) They are coloured gases  b) They fume in moist air and dissolve eagerly in water  c) They are stored in plastic  d) They are liquids at room temperature
**Answer: b) They fume in moist air and dissolve eagerly in water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Hydrochloric acid is…
a) The gas HCl itself  b) Hydrogen chloride dissolved in water  c) A mixture of hydrogen and chlorine  d) Chlorine dissolved in water
**Answer: b) Hydrogen chloride dissolved in water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The acid strength of the hydrohalic acids…
a) Is the same for all four  b) Falls down the family, HF strongest  c) Grows down the family, HI strongest and HF weakest  d) Is decided by their colour
**Answer: c) Grows down the family, HI strongest and HF weakest**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) HF boils far higher than its family place promises because…
a) It is the heaviest  b) Its molecules are held together by hydrogen bonds that must be broken  c) It contains two hydrogens  d) It is an acid
**Answer: b) Its molecules are held together by hydrogen bonds that must be broken**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Hydrofluoric acid is stored in plastic because it…
a) Is a gas  b) Is too strong for glass stoppers  c) Attacks glass  d) Evaporates through glass
**Answer: c) Attacks glass**
