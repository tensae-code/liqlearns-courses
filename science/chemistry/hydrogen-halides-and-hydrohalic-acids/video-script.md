Today: **Hydrogen halides and hydrohalic acids** — Sri Lanka's way: four brothers compared side by side, and the smallest one breaks every rule.

Hydrogen joins each halogen one to one: HF, HCl, HBr, HI. Four fuming gases that dissolve eagerly in water — and in water they become the hydrohalic acids. The acids grow stronger down the family: hydriodic acid is the strongest of the four.
But the first brother is the odd one. HF's molecules hold each other with hydrogen bonds, so it boils too high, acts weaker as an acid — and eats glass, which no other acid here can do.
