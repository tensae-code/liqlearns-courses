# Hands-on — Alkenes and alkynes — the unsaturated families

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Family sort (paper exercise)
Write six molecule descriptions on cards: single bonds only C₅H₁₂; one double bond C₃H₆; one triple bond C₂H₂; -ene name; -yne name; C₄H₈. Sort them under three headings — alkanes, alkenes, alkynes — checking each card against bond, formula and name together.

## Formula ladder (paper exercise)
For n = 2, 3, 4, write the alkane, alkene and alkyne formulas in a 3×3 grid from the general formulas. Ring the pattern down each column: what does each step — single to double to triple — subtract?
