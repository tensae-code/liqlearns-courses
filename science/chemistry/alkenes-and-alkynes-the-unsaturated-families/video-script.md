Today: **Alkenes and alkynes — the unsaturated families**.

One double bond: the alkenes, CₙH₂ₙ, names in -ene — ethene for plastics, fibres and ripening fruit. One triple bond: the alkynes, CₙH₂ₙ₋₂, names in -yne — ethyne burning at 1255 kilojoules a mole in the welder's flame.

The bond kind names the family.
