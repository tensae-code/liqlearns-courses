# Alkenes and alkynes — the unsaturated families — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Alkenes are also called _____.
**Answer: olefins**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Alkynes are also called _____.
**Answer: acetylenes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Alkyne names end in _____.
**Answer: -yne**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The oxy-acetylene flame is a _____ flame.
**Answer: welding**
