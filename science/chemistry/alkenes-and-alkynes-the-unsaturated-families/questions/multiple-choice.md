# Alkenes and alkynes — the unsaturated families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The alkenes carry…
a) No double bonds  b) One double bond  c) One triple bond  d) Only single bonds
**Answer: b) One double bond**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The alkenes' general formula is…
a) CₙH₂ₙ₊₂  b) CₙH₂ₙ  c) CₙH₂ₙ₋₂  d) CₙHₙ
**Answer: b) CₙH₂ₙ**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Alkene names end in…
a) -ane  b) -ene  c) -yne  d) -ol
**Answer: b) -ene**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Ethene is used for…
a) Welding only  b) Plastics, polyester fibres and ripening fruit  c) Fuel for lamps only  d) Making soap
**Answer: b) Plastics, polyester fibres and ripening fruit**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The alkynes' general formula is…
a) CₙH₂ₙ₊₂  b) CₙH₂ₙ  c) CₙH₂ₙ₋₂  d) C₂H₂ only
**Answer: c) CₙH₂ₙ₋₂**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Ethyne's complete combustion releases, per mole…
a) 1255 kJ  b) 125.5 kJ  c) 22.4 kJ  d) 6.022 kJ
**Answer: a) 1255 kJ**
