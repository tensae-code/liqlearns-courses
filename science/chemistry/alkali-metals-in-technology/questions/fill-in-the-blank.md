# Alkali metals in technology — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** ______ in atomic clocks keeps GPS satellite timing.
**Answer:** Caesium

**2.** Sodium vapour lamps and a liquid-sodium ______ both use sodium's properties.
**Answer:** coolant

**3.** Potassium ______ goes into closed breathing apparatus.
**Answer:** superoxide
