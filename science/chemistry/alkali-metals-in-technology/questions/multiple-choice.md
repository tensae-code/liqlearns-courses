# Alkali metals in technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Lithium's named uses in Egypt's STS section are…
a) Phone/EV batteries and light alloys  b) Street lamps and reactor coolant  c) Fertilisers and breathing apparatus  d) Atomic clocks
**Answer: a) Phone/EV batteries and light alloys**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Falcon 9 example belongs to which element's light alloys?
a) Lithium  b) Sodium  c) Rubidium  d) Caesium
**Answer: a) Lithium**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Sodium serves in a nuclear reactor as…
a) A liquid coolant  b) The fuel  c) The shielding  d) The control rods
**Answer: a) A liquid coolant**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Potassium superoxide is used in…
a) Closed breathing apparatus  b) Street lamps  c) GPS satellites  d) Scratch-resistant glass
**Answer: a) Closed breathing apparatus**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Caesium atomic clocks keep the timing for…
a) GPS satellites  b) Street lamps  c) EV batteries  d) Night-vision goggles
**Answer: a) GPS satellites**
