# Alkali metals in technology — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give one use for each alkali metal, as Egypt teaches them.
**Answer:** Lithium — phone/EV batteries and light alloys (Falcon 9 tanks); sodium — vapour street lamps and liquid coolant in the sodium reactor; potassium — fertilisers and potassium superoxide in closed breathing apparatus; rubidium — rubidium bromide in scratch-resistant glass and night-vision kit; caesium — atomic clocks keeping GPS timing.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the same family supply both a battery metal and a clock metal?
**Answer:** Each member's own properties fit a different job — lithium's lightness and electrochemistry suit batteries and alloys, caesium's perfectly regular atomic vibrations suit timekeeping; the STS section teaches one fitted use per element.
