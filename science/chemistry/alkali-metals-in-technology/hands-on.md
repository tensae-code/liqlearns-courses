# Hands-on — Alkali metals in technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the metal to the job
Write the five elements on slips and the five jobs on others — batteries/alloys, lamps/coolant, fertilisers/breathing, glass/night vision, clocks/GPS. Match them, then check against the lesson and say each pairing aloud.

## One use, one property
For two elements, write the property that fits the use: lithium's lightness for the Falcon 9 tanks; caesium's steady vibrations for the clock. Say why the property and the job belong together.
