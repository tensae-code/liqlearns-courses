## Protons, neutrons and electrons compared

Electrons, protons and neutrons are tabulated by location, charge and mass: electron — outside the nucleus, charge −1, mass 5.4858×10⁻⁴ amu (almost nothing of the atom's mass); proton — in the nucleus, +1, 1.0073 amu; neutron — in the nucleus, no charge, 1.0087 amu, slightly heavier than the proton;

protons plus neutrons make virtually all the mass.
