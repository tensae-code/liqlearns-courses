# Protons, neutrons and electrons compared — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** proton — in the _____, +1, 1.0073 amu;
**Answer: nucleus**

**2. ** neutron — in the nucleus, no charge, 1.0087 amu, _____ heavier than the proton;
**Answer: slightly**

**3. ** protons plus _____ make virtually all the mass.
**Answer: neutrons**

**4. ** Electrons, protons and neutrons are tabulated by _____, charge and mass: electron — outside the nucleus, charge −1, mass 5.4858×10⁻⁴ amu (almost nothing of the atom's mass);
**Answer: location**
