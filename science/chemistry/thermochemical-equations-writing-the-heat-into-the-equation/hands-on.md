# Hands-on — Thermochemical equations — writing the heat into the equation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The key ideas, on paper
On one strip of paper, write the lesson's key ideas in order, one box per idea, in your own words. Then cover the strip and retell the whole lesson from the boxes. Uncover and check against lesson/lesson.md.

## Teach it back
Teach the hardest key idea in this lesson to someone else, out loud, once — using only the lesson's own examples. Note the question they ask you, and answer it from the lesson.
