## What changes solubility

Temperature usually raises the solubility of solids and liquids in water but lowers that of gases; pressure barely affects solids/liquids but raises gas solubility — Henry's law: a gas's solubility is directly proportional to its partial pressure above the solution (soda water is the standing example). Intermolecular attraction strength underlies all of it.
