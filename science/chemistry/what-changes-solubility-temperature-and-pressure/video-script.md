Today: **What changes solubility — temperature and pressure**.

Temperature usually raises the solubility of solids and liquids in water but lowers that of gases;

pressure barely affects solids/liquids but raises gas solubility — Henry's law: a gas's solubility is directly proportional to its partial pressure above the solution…

Intermolecular attraction strength underlies all of it.
