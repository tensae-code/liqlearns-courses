Today: **Kevlar and high-performance polymers** — the fibre Iran pictures inside its clothing chapter.

Kevlar is a polymer — chains of monomers — spun into a fibre and built to perform. Its chains stand straight, side by side, holding hands with hydrogen bonds, so when something pulls, every chain pulls together. That is how soft woven cloth can stop a bullet, how a tyre gets its strength, and how a cable stays light and does not snap.
Ordinary fibres are chosen to be comfortable. Kevlar is chosen for the jobs where failing is not allowed.
