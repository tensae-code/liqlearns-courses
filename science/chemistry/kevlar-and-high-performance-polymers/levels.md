# Kevlar and high-performance polymers — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (A polymer made to perform, Why the fibre is so strong, Kevlar at work — the applications Iran pictures), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, sorting fibres into ordinary and high-performance by the job each is chosen for, and tracing one pictured application back to the chain-packing that makes it possible.
