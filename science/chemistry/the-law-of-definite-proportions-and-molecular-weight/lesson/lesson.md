## The law of definite proportions

Proust's law: the masses of elements in a compound always occur in the same proportion, wherever the compound comes from. To work with it the book first builds two tools: molecular weight as the sum of the atomic masses in a molecule (worked on the ammonium ion: 14 + 4×1 = 18), and the mole defined through carbon-12, with moles = given mass ÷ molecular mass (full solution-concentration use promised for Grade 10).
