# Industrial ethanol

## Do it first
Molasses — sugar-making's leftover — becomes fuel, solvent and disinfectant. Before reading, say the two great steps, one living and one physical.

[Picture 21768 — Fermenters and a distillation column.]

Industrial ethanol is made by fermenting a sugar feedstock — molasses in the Ethiopian context — with yeast, giving dilute ethanol and CO₂.

Distillation then concentrates the ethanol, separating it by its boiling point from the fermented liquid.

Grades and uses (fuel, solvent, medical, beverages as presented) depend on the purity the distillation reaches.
