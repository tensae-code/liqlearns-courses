# Industrial ethanol — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What feedstock is fermented in Ethiopia?
**Answer: Molasses**

**2. ** What concentrates the ethanol?
**Answer: Distillation**

**3. ** What gas does fermentation give off?
**Answer: Carbon dioxide**

**4. ** Name an industrial ethanol use.
**Answer: Fuel, solvent or disinfectant**
