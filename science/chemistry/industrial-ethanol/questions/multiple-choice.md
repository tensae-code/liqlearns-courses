# Industrial ethanol — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What feedstock is fermented in Ethiopia?
a) Whether the Lewis structure is sound  b) Delocalised electrons  c) No stable molecule forms  d) Molasses
**Answer: d) Molasses**

**2. ** What concentrates the ethanol?
a) –COOH  b) Alcohol → aldehyde → acid  c) Distillation  d) They do not react with most solutes
**Answer: c) Distillation**

**3. ** What gas does fermentation give off?
a) The same quantity of electricity  b) Carbon dioxide  c) A burette  d) Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature
**Answer: b) Carbon dioxide**

**4. ** Name an industrial ethanol use.
a) Fuel, solvent or disinfectant  b) The hydrosphere  c) Carbon monoxide (or particulates/hydrocarbons)  d) No — natural, but strengthened by humans
**Answer: a) Fuel, solvent or disinfectant**
