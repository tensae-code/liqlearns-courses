# Video script — Industrial ethanol

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Molasses — sugar-making's leftover — becomes fuel, solvent and disinfectant.

Industrial ethanol is made by fermenting a sugar feedstock — molasses in the Ethiopian context — with yeast, giving dilute ethanol and CO₂.

Distillation then concentrates the ethanol, separating it by its boiling point from the fermented liquid.

Grades and uses (fuel, solvent, medical, beverages as presented) depend on the purity the distillation reaches.

Now you. Say it back: What feedstock is fermented in Ethiopia?
Answer: Molasses.
