Today: **Atoms, elements and chemical symbols** — Mauritania's way: a pure substance has one kind of constituent and cannot be split into simpler substances; the atom is the fundamental constituent — the smallest separable constituent of matter; atoms of one kind make an element, and each element carries a symbol written with one capital letter, or a capital followed by a small letter — that is the whole lesson in one breath — the sub lessons now walk it slowly, book in hand.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
