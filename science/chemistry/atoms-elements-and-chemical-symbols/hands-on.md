# Hands-on — Atoms, elements and chemical symbols

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the table (paper only)
Reproduce the collège table of eight elements — H, O, C, N, Na, Fe, Cu, Au — with each element's name, and mark which symbols follow the one-capital rule and which the capital-plus-small rule.

## One kind or two? (kitchen-safe sorting)
List six household substances by name only (no handling): for each, say from the lesson's definitions whether it is presented as one kind of constituent or several — and what further fact you would need to be sure. Describe only; the lesson is a sorting of ideas.
