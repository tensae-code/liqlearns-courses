# Atoms, elements and chemical symbols — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The fundamental constituent of matter in these books is the _____.
**Answer: atom**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Atoms of one kind make an _____.
**Answer: element**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Iron's symbol, from the collège table, is _____.
**Answer: Fe**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A symbol is one capital letter, or a capital followed by a _____ letter.
**Answer: small**
