# Atoms, elements and chemical symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The fundamental constituent of a pure substance is…
a) The molecule only  b) The atom  c) The ion  d) The mixture
**Answer: b) The atom**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An element is made of atoms of…
a) Many kinds  b) One kind  c) No kind  d) Two kinds
**Answer: b) One kind**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Which symbol is written correctly for sodium?
a) NA  b) na  c) Na  d) So
**Answer: c) Na**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The 3AS book calls the atom the smallest _____ constituent of matter.
a) Coloured  b) Separable  c) Heavy  d) Liquid
**Answer: b) Separable**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A pure substance cannot be…
a) Weighed  b) Split into simpler substances  c) Named  d) Seen
**Answer: b) Split into simpler substances**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The symbol Au stands for…
a) Silver  b) Aluminium  c) Gold  d) Argon
**Answer: c) Gold**
