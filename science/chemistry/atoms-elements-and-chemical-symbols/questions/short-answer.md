# Atoms, elements and chemical symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define an element from its atoms.
**Answer: An element is made of atoms of one kind — every atom in it is the same kind.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the symbol writing rule.
**Answer: One capital letter, or a capital letter followed by a small letter.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is a pure substance, in the 2AS book's terms?
**Answer: A substance with one kind of constituent, which cannot be decomposed into simpler substances.**
