# Video script — Naming alcohols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A branched alcohol needs a name that rebuilds it exactly.

IUPAC names of alcohols end in –ol, on the longest chain carrying the –OH.

Number the chain so the –OH gets the lowest possible number, and cite that number in the name.

Common names (like 'ethyl alcohol') survive alongside the systematic ones; branched examples are worked in the section.

Now you. Say it back: What ending do alcohol names take?
Answer: –ol.
