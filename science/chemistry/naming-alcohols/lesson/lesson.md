# Naming alcohols

## Do it first
A branched alcohol needs a name that rebuilds it exactly. Before reading, say what the –ol ending promises, and what the numbers in a name are for.

[Picture 21684 — A branched alcohol named step by step.]

IUPAC names of alcohols end in –ol, on the longest chain carrying the –OH.

Number the chain so the –OH gets the lowest possible number, and cite that number in the name.

Common names (like 'ethyl alcohol') survive alongside the systematic ones; branched examples are worked in the section.

## Say it to your companion
