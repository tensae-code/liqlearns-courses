# Naming alcohols — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What ending do alcohol names take?
**Answer: –ol**

**2. ** What gets the lowest number?
**Answer: The –OH position**

**3. ** On which chain is the name built?
**Answer: The longest chain carrying –OH**

**4. ** Give a common alcohol name.
**Answer: Ethyl alcohol (ethanol)**
