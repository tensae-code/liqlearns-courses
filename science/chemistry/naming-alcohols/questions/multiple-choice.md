# Naming alcohols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What ending do alcohol names take?
a) –ol  b) A carboxylic acid  c) In fruits and flowers  d) The acid's salt plus the alcohol
**Answer: a) –ol**

**2. ** What gets the lowest number?
a) Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses  b) 0.00 V  c) Negative (spontaneous)  d) The –OH position
**Answer: d) The –OH position**

**3. ** On which chain is the name built?
a) Redesign, reuse or efficiency in the plant  b) Three  c) The longest chain carrying –OH  d) The rise in average temperature
**Answer: c) The longest chain carrying –OH**

**4. ** Give a common alcohol name.
a) Gas  b) Ethyl alcohol (ethanol)  c) Positive kernels  d) Its overlap concentrates density between nuclei
**Answer: b) Ethyl alcohol (ethanol)**
