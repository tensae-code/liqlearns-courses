# Measuring and expressing reaction rate — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rate defined and measured
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The rate of a reaction is the change in amount or concentration of a reactant or product per unit time — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Average and instantaneous rate
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Rate can be followed by gas volume collected, mass loss, or colour change, as the reaction allows — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rate graphs and tangents
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The average rate covers a time interval; the instantaneous rate is the slope of the tangent to the concentration–time curve at one moment — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Rate law, order and the constant k
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Rate expressions summarise measurement: the rate law links rate to concentrations raised to powers (orders), with k the rate constant — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.
