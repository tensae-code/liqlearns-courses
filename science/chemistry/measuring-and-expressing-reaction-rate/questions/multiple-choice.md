# Measuring and expressing reaction rate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Define reaction rate.
a) Suppresses it  b) OH⁻ — so the solution is alkaline  c) Its range must fall in the titration's pH jump  d) Change in amount/concentration per unit time
**Answer: d) Change in amount/concentration per unit time**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What gives the instantaneous rate on a graph?
a) Slows microbial growth  b) PET  c) The slope of the tangent  d) Centrifuging (spinning)
**Answer: c) The slope of the tangent**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** Name one way to follow a rate.
a) The noble gases  b) Gas volume, mass loss, or colour change  c) Three, along x, y and z  d) s, p, d and f
**Answer: b) Gas volume, mass loss, or colour change**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) ** What is k in the rate law?
a) The rate constant  b) Forward, making products  c) The carbonyl group, C=O  d) The short-chain ones
**Answer: a) The rate constant**
