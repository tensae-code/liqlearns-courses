# Measuring and expressing reaction rate — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** Define reaction rate.
**Answer: Change in amount/concentration per unit time**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** What gives the instantaneous rate on a graph?
**Answer: The slope of the tangent**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** Name one way to follow a rate.
**Answer: Gas volume, mass loss, or colour change**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is k in the rate law?
**Answer: The rate constant**
