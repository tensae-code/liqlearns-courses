# Measuring and expressing reaction rate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** Define reaction rate. — Change in amount/concentration per unit time
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** What gives the instantaneous rate on a graph — Harvests remove it from the soil
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** Name one way to follow a rate. — Gas volume, mass loss, or colour change
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is k in the rate law — A negative ion (anion)
**Answer: False**
