# Video script — Measuring and expressing reaction rate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Marble chips fizz in acid — wildly at first, then slower, then not at all.

The rate of a reaction is the change in amount or concentration of a reactant or product per unit time.

Rate can be followed by gas volume collected, mass loss, or colour change, as the reaction allows.

The average rate covers a time interval; the instantaneous rate is the slope of the tangent to the concentration–time curve at one moment.

Rate expressions summarise measurement: the rate law links rate to concentrations raised to powers (orders), with k the rate constant.

Now you. Say it back: Define reaction rate.
Answer: Change in amount/concentration per unit time.
