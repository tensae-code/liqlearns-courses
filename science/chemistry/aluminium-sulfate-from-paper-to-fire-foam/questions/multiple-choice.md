# Aluminium sulfate — from paper to fire foam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Aluminium sulfate is made by reacting Al₂O₃ with…
a) Sulfuric acid  b) Hydrochloric acid  c) Sodium hydroxide  d) Water alone
**Answer: a) Sulfuric acid**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The crystals of aluminium sulfate carry how many waters?
a) 2  b) 18  c) 7  d) 10
**Answer: b) 18**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) At red heat, aluminium sulfate breaks down to alumina and…
a) CO₂ and H₂O  b) SO₃ only  c) SO₂ and O₂  d) Aluminium metal
**Answer: c) SO₂ and O₂**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Its solution is acidic because of…
a) Evaporation  b) Its colour  c) Added acid  d) Hydrolysis
**Answer: d) Hydrolysis**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In a foam extinguisher, aluminium sulfate works with…
a) Sodium bicarbonate  b) Sodium chloride  c) Lime  d) Sand
**Answer: a) Sodium bicarbonate**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Foam extinguishers are the answer to which fires in the lesson?
a) Electrical fires only  b) Oil fires  c) Metal fires  d) Paper fires only
**Answer: b) Oil fires**
