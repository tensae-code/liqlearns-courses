# Aluminium sulfate — from paper to fire foam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the preparation equation of aluminium sulfate.
**Answer: Al₂O₃ + 3H₂SO₄ → Al₂(SO₄)₃ + 3H₂O.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the lesson's four industrial uses of the salt besides fire foam.
**Answer: Paper making, leather tanning, dye fixing and water purification.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State what happens to the salt's waters and then to the salt itself as heating grows stronger.
**Answer: The 18 waters of crystallisation are lost on heating; at red heat the salt decomposes to alumina, SO₂ and O₂.**
