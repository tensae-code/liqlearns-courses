# Hands-on — Aluminium sulfate — from paper to fire foam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four trades, one salt (paper only)
Write the salt's four trades — paper, leather, dyeing, water — in a column. Beside each, write in one line which property of the salt (its acidity by hydrolysis, or its aluminium) the trade spends, as the lesson presents it. Mark any trade where the lesson gives the use without the reason — honest gaps stay visible.

## Foam plan (paper only)
An oil fire must not be fought with water. Write the extinguisher's story in four steps from the lesson: the two chemicals, the gas they make, the foam it builds, and why the blanket kills the fire. End with the reason water fails the same fire.
