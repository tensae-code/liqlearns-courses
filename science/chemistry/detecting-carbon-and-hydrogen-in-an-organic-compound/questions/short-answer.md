# Detecting carbon and hydrogen in an organic compound — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give both tests with their observations and the element each proves.
**Answer:** Water test: anhydrous copper sulphate turns from white to blue — proves hydrogen (as water from the burning). Limewater test: lime water turns milky with the gas — proves carbon (as carbon dioxide).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why must the compound's own oxygen not confuse the result?
**Answer:** The tests detect the products, not their oxygen source: water still proves hydrogen and CO₂ still proves carbon, wherever the oxygen came from — the copper(II) oxide simply guarantees the burning is complete.
