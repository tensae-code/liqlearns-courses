# Detecting carbon and hydrogen in an organic compound — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The substance is heated with…
a) Copper(II) oxide  b) Lime water  c) Anhydrous copper sulphate  d) Sodium hydroxide
**Answer: a) Copper(II) oxide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Water from the compound is detected when anhydrous copper sulphate turns…
a) White to blue  b) Blue to white  c) Milky  d) Black
**Answer: a) White to blue**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Carbon dioxide is detected by lime water turning…
a) Milky  b) Blue  c) Clear  d) Yellow
**Answer: a) Milky**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The blue colour change proves the compound contained…
a) Hydrogen  b) Carbon  c) Oxygen  d) Nitrogen
**Answer: a) Hydrogen**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The copper(II) oxide's job in the practical is…
a) To supply oxygen for complete burning  b) To colour the flame  c) To absorb the water  d) To cool the tube
**Answer: a) To supply oxygen for complete burning**
