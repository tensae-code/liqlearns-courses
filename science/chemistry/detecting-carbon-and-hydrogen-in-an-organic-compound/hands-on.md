# Hands-on — Detecting carbon and hydrogen in an organic compound

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Flow the gases
Draw the practical as a flow: compound + CuO, heated → vapour to the copper sulphate tube → gas on to the lime water. Label each station with the colour change watched for and the element it proves.

## Verdict drill
Write three observation cards — 'sulphate stayed white', 'sulphate turned blue, lime water clear', 'both changes seen'. Under each, write the verdict the practical allows: no hydrogen shown; hydrogen shown, carbon not shown; both proven.
