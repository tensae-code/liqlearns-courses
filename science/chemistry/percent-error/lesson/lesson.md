## Percent error

The guide's worked review answers introduce percent error as a named, formula-driven quantity — percent error = |true value − experimental value| ÷ |true value| × 100 — and work it twice on density results (0.798 vs 0.802 g/mL → 0.5%; 0.864 vs 0.837 g/mL → 3.1%). The student textbook teaches relative error/deviation inside its density practical but does not state this formula in its main text.
