## SI prefixes

Prefixes scale units by powers of ten; the full table runs tera (10¹²) through giga, mega, kilo, hecto, deca, then deci, centi, milli, micro, nano to pico (10⁻¹²), each with symbol, decimal value and a worked example (1 km = 10³ m, 1 µm = 10⁻⁶ m…), with everyday symbols such as kV, kW and km.
