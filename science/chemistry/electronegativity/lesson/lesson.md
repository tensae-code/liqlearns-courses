## Electronegativity

Electronegativity — an atom's pull on shared electrons in a bond — is the fourth major trend, rising across a period and falling down a group with fluorine at the top; the unit's trend family is completed by metallic character moving the opposite way. These values are the bridge into Unit 5: electronegativity differences will decide bond type and polarity.
