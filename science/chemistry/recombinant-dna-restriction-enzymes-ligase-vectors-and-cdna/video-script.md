Today: **Recombinant DNA — restriction enzymes, ligase, vectors and cDNA** — Italy's way, from the book's own treatment. Restriction enzymes cut DNA — blunt ends against sticky ends — and DNA ligase reforms the phosphodiester bond. Cloning vectors (viral, BAC, YAC) are chosen by the job they must do. Cloning from mRNA runs through cDNA, and nucleotide probes find genes by hybridisation.
