## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo F, Capitolo 15, §§1–5 — Italy's book (Grade 13)

Genetic engineering's cut-and-paste: restriction enzymes (blunt against sticky ends), DNA ligase reforming the phosphodiester bond, cloning vectors (viral, BAC, YAC) chosen by job, cloning from mRNA through cDNA, and nucleotide probes finding genes by hybridisation.

## The key ideas

- Restriction enzymes cut DNA — blunt ends against sticky ends — and DNA ligase reforms the phosphodiester bond.
- Cloning vectors (viral, BAC, YAC) are chosen by the job they must do.
- Cloning from mRNA runs through cDNA, and nucleotide probes find genes by hybridisation.
