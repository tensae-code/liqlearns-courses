# Recombinant DNA — restriction enzymes, ligase, vectors and cDNA — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** _____ cut DNA — blunt ends against sticky ends — and DNA ligase reforms the phosphodiester bond.
**Answer: Restriction enzymes**

**2.** Cloning _____ (viral, BAC, YAC) are chosen by the job they must do.
**Answer: vectors**

**3.** Cloning from mRNA runs through _____, and nucleotide probes find genes by hybridisation.
**Answer: cDNA**
