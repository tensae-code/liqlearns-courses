# Recombinant DNA — restriction enzymes, ligase, vectors and cDNA — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about Restriction enzymes.
**Answer: Restriction enzymes cut DNA — blunt ends against sticky ends — and DNA ligase reforms the phosphodiester bond.**

**2.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about vectors.
**Answer: Cloning vectors (viral, BAC, YAC) are chosen by the job they must do.**

**3.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about cDNA.
**Answer: Cloning from mRNA runs through cDNA, and nucleotide probes find genes by hybridisation.**
