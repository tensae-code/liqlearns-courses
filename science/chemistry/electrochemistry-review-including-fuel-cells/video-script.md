# Video script — Electrochemistry review — including fuel cells

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hydrogen and oxygen, kept apart by a membrane, make electricity and pure water — no flame.

Unit 2 consolidates: electrolytic cells consume electricity to force change; galvanic cells produce it from spontaneous change; potentials rank the push; Faraday's laws do the bookkeeping.

The review's fuel-cell questions run a hydrogen–oxygen cell through ΔG° and E° — a galvanic cell fed continuously with fuel.

This lesson is the unit's summary, checklist and review exercise, worked.

Now you. Say it back: A fuel cell is what kind of cell?
Answer: Galvanic (it produces electricity).
