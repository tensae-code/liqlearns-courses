# Electrochemistry review — including fuel cells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** A fuel cell is what kind of cell?
a) Soda ash  b) Galvanic (it produces electricity)  c) Inputs (raw materials), process, products  d) Phosphorus
**Answer: b) Galvanic (it produces electricity)**

**2. ** What are a fuel cell's products (H₂/O₂)?
a) Electricity and water  b) The neutron  c) c = λν  d) The difference between the two levels
**Answer: a) Electricity and water**

**3. ** For a working fuel cell, ΔG° is…
a) Elastically — no energy lost  b) It stays constant during the change  c) It lowers the activation energy path  d) Negative (spontaneous)
**Answer: d) Negative (spontaneous)**

**4. ** Which cells consume electricity?
a) Plants (seeds)  b) Margarine (solid fat)  c) Electrolytic cells  d) No hydrogen bonding between esters
**Answer: c) Electrolytic cells**
