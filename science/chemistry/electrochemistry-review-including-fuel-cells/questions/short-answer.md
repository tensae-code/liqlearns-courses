# Electrochemistry review — including fuel cells — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** A fuel cell is what kind of cell?
**Answer: Galvanic (it produces electricity)**

**2. ** What are a fuel cell's products (H₂/O₂)?
**Answer: Electricity and water**

**3. ** For a working fuel cell, ΔG° is…
**Answer: Negative (spontaneous)**

**4. ** Which cells consume electricity?
**Answer: Electrolytic cells**
