# Electrochemistry review — including fuel cells — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** A fuel cell is what kind of cell — Galvanic (it produces electricity)
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What are a fuel cell's products (H₂/O₂) — Brine
**Answer: False**

**3. ** For a working fuel cell, ΔG° is… — Negative (spontaneous)
**Answer: True**

**4. ** Which cells consume electricity — Molten or in solution — ions move
**Answer: False**
