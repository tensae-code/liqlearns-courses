# Hands-on — Waxes and phospholipids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build both from parts
Draw the two builds side by side as part-diagrams: a wax as fatty acid + long-chain monohydric alcohol, and a phospholipid as glycerol + two fatty acids + phosphoric acid. Label beeswax's two parts — myricyl alcohol and palmitic acid — on the wax drawing.

## Heads to the water
Sketch a row of phospholipid molecules at a water surface: circles for the polar heads touching the water, paired lines for the non-polar tails pointing away. Say in one sentence why they line up that way.
