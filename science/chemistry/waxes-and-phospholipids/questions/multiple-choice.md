# Waxes and phospholipids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A wax is an ester of a long-chain fatty acid with…
a) Glycerol  b) A long-chain monohydric alcohol  c) Phosphoric acid  d) A short-chain acid
**Answer: b) A long-chain monohydric alcohol**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Beeswax is built from…
a) Myricyl alcohol and palmitic acid  b) Glycerol and oleic acid  c) Ethanol and ethanoic acid  d) Phosphoric acid and glycerol
**Answer: a) Myricyl alcohol and palmitic acid**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In nature, waxes coat plant leaves and animal fur…
a) To feed them  b) Against water  c) To colour them  d) To warm them directly
**Answer: b) Against water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A phospholipid is a glycerol ester carrying…
a) Three fatty acids  b) Two fatty acids and a phosphoric acid  c) One fatty acid and two phosphoric acids  d) No fatty acids
**Answer: b) Two fatty acids and a phosphoric acid**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Phospholipids form…
a) Beeswax  b) The membranes of living cells  c) Soap  d) Solid fats
**Answer: b) The membranes of living cells**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A phospholipid's head is…
a) Non-polar and water-fearing  b) Polar and water-loving  c) Made of two fatty acids  d) The glycerol itself
**Answer: b) Polar and water-loving**
