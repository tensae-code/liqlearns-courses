# Waxes and phospholipids — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define a wax and give the book's example with its two building parts.
**Answer:** A wax is an ester of a long-chain fatty acid with a long-chain monohydric alcohol; beeswax is built from myricyl alcohol and palmitic acid.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How do a phospholipid's two ends explain its place in a cell membrane?
**Answer:** Its head is polar and water-loving while its tails are non-polar and water-fearing, so in water the molecules line up heads-to-water and tails-hidden — the sheet that forms the cell's membrane.
