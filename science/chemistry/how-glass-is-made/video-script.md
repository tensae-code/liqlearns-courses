# Video script — How glass is made

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sand, soda and stone go into a furnace and come out transparent.

Glass is made by melting sand (silica), soda ash (sodium carbonate) and limestone (calcium carbonate) together at high temperature.

The melt is formed — blown, pressed or floated — into bottles, sheets and ware, then cooled carefully (annealing).

Kinds of glass (soda-lime, borosilicate and others as presented) vary the recipe; the lesson sits in Ethiopian industry context.

Now you. Say it back: Name glass's three raw materials.
Answer: Sand, soda ash, limestone.
