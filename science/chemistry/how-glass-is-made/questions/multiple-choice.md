# How glass is made — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name glass's three raw materials.
a) Neutralising power  b) H₂O  c) Sand, soda ash, limestone  d) Nothing — it spectates
**Answer: c) Sand, soda ash, limestone**

**2. ** What is sand's chemical role?
a) Respiration, decay and combustion  b) Silica — the glass former  c) Molasses  d) The C=C double bond
**Answer: b) Silica — the glass former**

**3. ** What is careful cooling called?
a) Annealing  b) It shrinks  c) A positive ion (cation)  d) d electrons can also be lost
**Answer: a) Annealing**

**4. ** Where does the soda ash come from?
a) One with three –OH groups  b) Yeast (its enzymes)  c) Weakly polar  d) The Solvay process (Unit 3)
**Answer: d) The Solvay process (Unit 3)**
