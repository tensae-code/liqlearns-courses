# How glass is made — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name glass's three raw materials.
**Answer: Sand, soda ash, limestone**

**2. ** What is sand's chemical role?
**Answer: Silica — the glass former**

**3. ** What is careful cooling called?
**Answer: Annealing**

**4. ** Where does the soda ash come from?
**Answer: The Solvay process (Unit 3)**
