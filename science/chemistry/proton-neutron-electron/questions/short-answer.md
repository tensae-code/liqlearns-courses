# Proton, neutron, electron — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is the atomic number Z?
**Answer: The number of protons**

**2. ** Where are protons and neutrons found?
**Answer: In the nucleus**

**3. ** Which subatomic particle is by far the lightest?
**Answer: The electron**

**4. ** What charge does the neutron carry?
**Answer: No charge — it is neutral**
