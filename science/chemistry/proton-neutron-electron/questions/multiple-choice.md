# Proton, neutron, electron — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the atomic number Z?
a) H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)  b) A weak acid's ionisation strength  c) The number of protons  d) Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry
**Answer: c) The number of protons**

**2. ** Where are protons and neutrons found?
a) Clinker  b) In the nucleus  c) Harvests remove it from the soil  d) Sodium hydroxide, chlorine, hydrogen
**Answer: b) In the nucleus**

**3. ** Which subatomic particle is by far the lightest?
a) The electron  b) Gamma rays  c) One-electron species only  d) +½ or −½
**Answer: a) The electron**

**4. ** What charge does the neutron carry?
a) It breaks particle arrangements  b) ⇌ (double arrow)  c) Pure solids and liquids  d) No charge — it is neutral
**Answer: d) No charge — it is neutral**
