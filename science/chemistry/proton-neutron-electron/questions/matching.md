# Proton, neutron, electron — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What is the atomic number Z? — c
2. Where are protons and neutrons found? — d
3. Which subatomic particle is by far the lightest? — a
4. What charge does the neutron carry? — b

**Answer key:** 1–c: The number of protons · 2–d: In the nucleus · 3–a: The electron · 4–b: No charge — it is neutral
