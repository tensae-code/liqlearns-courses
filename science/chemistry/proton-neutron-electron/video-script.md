# Video script — Proton, neutron, electron

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three particles build every atom.

The proton carries a positive charge, sits in the nucleus, and has relative mass about 1.

The neutron carries no charge, sits in the nucleus, and has relative mass about 1.

The electron carries a negative charge, moves around the nucleus, and is very light — its mass is almost negligible beside the proton's.

The atomic number Z is simply the number of protons, and it fixes which element the atom is.

Now you. Say it back: What is the atomic number Z?
Answer: The number of protons.
