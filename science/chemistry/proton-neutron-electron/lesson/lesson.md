# Proton, neutron, electron

## Do it first
Three particles build every atom. Before you read, sort them from what you already know: which one is negative, which one positive, which one neutral — and which one is by far the lightest?

[Picture 29259 — Three particles — charge, mass, place.]

The proton carries a positive charge, sits in the nucleus, and has relative mass about 1.

The neutron carries no charge, sits in the nucleus, and has relative mass about 1.

The electron carries a negative charge, moves around the nucleus, and is very light — its mass is almost negligible beside the proton's.

The atomic number Z is simply the number of protons, and it fixes which element the atom is.

## Say it to your companion
Explain Proton, neutron, electron to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.
