# Hands-on — Alkanes and the homologous series

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort and say
Collect or draw six everyday examples that belong to this lesson — alkanes and the homologous series — and sort them into two groups of your own choosing. Defend each placing in one sentence.

## Teach it with objects
Using stones, sticks, bottle tops or drawings, build the lesson's main example on a table and teach it to a partner, pointing at each part as you name it.
