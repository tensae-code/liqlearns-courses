## Alkanes and the homologous series

A homologous series is a family whose members differ by successive –CH₂– units, share a general formula and similar chemistry, and show graded physical properties. The alkanes are the saturated family, general formula CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, named by a carbon-count prefix + "-ane" (methane onward), each member's formula generated from the general formula.
