# Hands-on — Radicals and valency

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build formulas from valency cards
On cards write the elements Na, Ca, Al and the radicals OH, NO₃, CO₃, SO₄ with each one's valency. Lay out pairs and write the compound each pair forms, using the balance rule — brackets wherever a radical is taken twice or more. Say each formula aloud as you build it.

## Radical spotting
Write the formulas CaCO₃, NH₄NO₃, Na₂SO₄, Al(OH)₃, (NH₄)₃PO₄. Underline each radical wherever it appears and ring its charge. Count how many different radicals you found.
