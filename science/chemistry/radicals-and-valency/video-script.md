Today: **Radicals and valency** — South Sudan's way: the charged groups, and the number that joins them.
A single atom can carry a charge. But atoms also travel in teams. A radical is a group of atoms carrying one charge, acting as one ion: hydroxide OH⁻, carbonate CO₃²⁻, ammonium NH₄⁺. Each radical also has a valency — its combining power, equal to its charge. Compounds form when the valencies balance: calcium, valency 2, takes two hydroxides, valency 1 — Ca(OH)₂, the radical in brackets because the whole team is taken twice.
