# Radicals and valency — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A group of atoms carrying one charge and behaving as a single ion is called a _____.
**Answer: radical**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The ammonium radical is written NH₄ with charge _____.
**Answer: +1 (one positive)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Valency is the _____ power of an element or radical.
**Answer: combining**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) When two whole radicals join a compound, the radical is written inside _____.
**Answer: brackets**
