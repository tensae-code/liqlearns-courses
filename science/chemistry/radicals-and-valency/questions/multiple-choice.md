# Radicals and valency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A radical is…
a) One atom with a charge  b) A group of atoms carrying one charge, behaving as a single unit  c) An uncharged molecule  d) The centre of an atom
**Answer: b) A group of atoms carrying one charge, behaving as a single unit**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The carbonate radical is written…
a) CO₃²⁻  b) CO²⁻  c) C₂O₃⁻  d) CO₃⁺
**Answer: a) CO₃²⁻**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Which radical carries a charge of +1?
a) Sulfate, SO₄²⁻  b) Phosphate, PO₄³⁻  c) Ammonium, NH₄⁺  d) Carbonate, CO₃²⁻
**Answer: c) Ammonium, NH₄⁺**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Valency means…
a) The mass of an atom  b) The combining power of an element or radical  c) The number of atoms in a molecule  d) The charge of a nucleus
**Answer: b) The combining power of an element or radical**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The valency of the sulfate radical is…
a) 1  b) 3  c) 4  d) 2
**Answer: d) 2**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Calcium (valency 2) joins hydroxide (valency 1) as…
a) CaOH  b) Ca(OH)₂  c) Ca₂OH  d) Ca(OH)
**Answer: b) Ca(OH)₂**
