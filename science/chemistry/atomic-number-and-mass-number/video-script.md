Today: **Atomic number and mass number**.

Atomic number Z = number of protons (the identity of the element);

mass number A = protons + neutrons (nucleons);

The nuclear symbol writes A as superscript and Z as subscript at the left of the element symbol;

worked examples find p/e/n counts for given atoms and write full symbols for hydrogen and helium isotopes.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
