## Atomic number and mass number

Atomic number Z = number of protons (the identity of the element); mass number A = protons + neutrons (nucleons); neutrons = A − Z.

The nuclear symbol writes A as superscript and Z as subscript at the left of the element symbol; worked examples find p/e/n counts for given atoms and write full symbols for hydrogen and helium isotopes.
