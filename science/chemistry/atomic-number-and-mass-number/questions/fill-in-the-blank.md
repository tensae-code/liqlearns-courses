# Atomic number and mass number — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** mass number A = protons + _____ (nucleons);
**Answer: neutrons**

**2. ** The nuclear symbol writes A as superscript and Z as _____ at the left of the element symbol;
**Answer: subscript**

**3. ** worked examples find p/e/n counts for given atoms and write full symbols for _____ and helium isotopes.
**Answer: hydrogen**

**4. ** Atomic number Z = number of protons (the _____ of the element);
**Answer: identity**
