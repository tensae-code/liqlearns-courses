# Hands-on — Vitamins and hormones

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Deficiency detective (paper only)
Five case cards: bleeding gums and slow-healing wounds on a sailor's diet without fresh food; a child whose legs bow as they grow; an adult who cannot see in dim light; inflamed nerves and weakness on polished rice alone; a baby whose blood clots too slowly. Name each vitamin and the diet change that answers it, using NCERT's table as taught in the lesson.

## Label audit at home (kitchen materials only)
With an adult, read the nutrition labels of three packaged foods or a multivitamin pack in the home, if present. List every vitamin named, and sort your list into the two solubility families. Say, from sub lesson 1, which family's members the body cannot store — and what that means for how regularly they must be eaten.

## Messenger match (paper only)
Four scenarios: blood glucose climbs after a meal; blood glucose falls during a long fast; a cold, slow, tired patient with a low metabolic rate is investigated; a sudden fright sends the heart racing. For each, name the hormone (and its gland) that answers, and the direction of its action.
