# Vitamins and hormones — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Vitamins — the diet's small necessities, in two families, The deficiency table — what each vitamin guards, Hormones — messengers in the blood), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, reading deficiency cases off NCERT's vitamin table by solubility family, and matching hormone scenarios — blood glucose, metabolic rate, emergency — to gland and messenger.
