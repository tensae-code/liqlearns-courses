# Vitamins and hormones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A vitamin is defined as…
a) Any organic food  b) An organic compound the diet must supply in small amounts, whose absence causes a deficiency disease  c) A mineral  d) An energy store
**Answer: b) An organic compound the diet must supply in small amounts, whose absence causes a deficiency disease**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The fat-soluble vitamins are…
a) B and C  b) A, D, E and K — stored in liver and fatty tissues  c) Only D  d) None; all vitamins are water-soluble
**Answer: b) A, D, E and K — stored in liver and fatty tissues**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Scurvy — bleeding gums, wounds that will not heal — is the deficiency of…
a) Vitamin A  b) Vitamin D  c) Vitamin C, ascorbic acid  d) Vitamin K
**Answer: c) Vitamin C, ascorbic acid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Rickets in children is the deficiency of…
a) Vitamin B₁  b) Vitamin D  c) Vitamin B₁₂  d) Vitamin E
**Answer: b) Vitamin D**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Insulin, from the pancreas, acts to…
a) Raise blood glucose  b) Lower blood glucose — its deficiency is diabetes mellitus  c) Set the metabolic rate  d) Raise blood pressure
**Answer: b) Lower blood glucose — its deficiency is diabetes mellitus**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The emergency hormone, readied in seconds, is…
a) Thyroxine  b) Glucagon  c) Adrenaline, from the adrenal medulla  d) Insulin
**Answer: c) Adrenaline, from the adrenal medulla**
