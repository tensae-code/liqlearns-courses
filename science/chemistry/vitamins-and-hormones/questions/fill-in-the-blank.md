# Vitamins and hormones — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Vitamins B and C are _____-soluble.
**Answer: water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Vitamin C's chemical name is _____ acid.
**Answer: ascorbic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Lack of vitamin B₁ causes _____-beri.
**Answer: beri**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The hormone that lowers blood glucose is _____.
**Answer: insulin**
