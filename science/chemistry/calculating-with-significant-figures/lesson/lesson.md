## Calculating with significant figures

Addition/subtraction answers keep no more decimal places than the least-precise input; multiplication/division answers keep no more significant figures than the input with fewest; exact numbers (definitions, counts) count as infinitely significant.

Rounding is defined digit by digit (drop below 5, round up at 5 or more), with worked sums in both families.
