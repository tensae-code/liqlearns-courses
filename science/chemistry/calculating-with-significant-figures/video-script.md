# Video script — Calculating with significant figures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Calculating with significant figures**.

Addition/subtraction answers keep no more decimal places than the least-precise input;

multiplication/division answers keep no more significant figures than the input with fewest;

exact numbers (definitions, counts) count as infinitely significant.

Rounding is defined digit by digit (drop below 5, round up at 5 or more), with worked sums in both families.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
