## Average atomic mass

Isotope masses are expressed relative to carbon-12 (the atomic mass unit, also called the dalton). Because elements occur as isotope mixtures, chemists use the average (weighted) atomic mass: sum over isotopes of (% abundance × isotope mass) ÷ 100 — worked on boron (20% of one isotope…) and set as exercises.
