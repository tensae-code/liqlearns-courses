# Average atomic mass — the weighted mean — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** Because elements occur as isotope mixtures, chemists use the average (_____) atomic mass: sum over isotopes of (% abundance × isotope mass) ÷ 100 — worked on boron (20% of one isotope…) and set as exercises.
**Answer: weighted**

**2. ** Isotope masses are _____ relative to carbon-12 (the atomic mass unit, also called the dalton).
**Answer: expressed**
