# Hands-on — Classification of inorganic compounds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the scheme
On paper, draw the cover scheme from memory: metal, nonmetal, basic oxide, acidic oxide, base, acid, salt — with arrows where one leads to another. Check it against the lesson and fix any missing arrow.

## Place the compound
Take three inorganic compounds from earlier lessons. For each, say its class and one thing the class predicts about its behaviour.
