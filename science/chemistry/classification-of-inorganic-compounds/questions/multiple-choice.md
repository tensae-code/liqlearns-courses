# Classification of inorganic compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The four main classes of inorganic compounds are…
a) Metals, nonmetals, gases, liquids  b) Oxides, bases, acids, salts  c) Atoms, molecules, ions, mixtures  d) Solids, solutions, alloys, ores
**Answer: b) Oxides, bases, acids, salts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Armenia's Grade 8 book prints the classification scheme…
a) Only in an appendix  b) On its own cover  c) Nowhere — it is never drawn  d) In the Grade 7 book only
**Answer: b) On its own cover**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) On the scheme, the metal road runs…
a) Metal → acidic oxide → acid  b) Metal → basic oxide → base  c) Metal → salt directly, always  d) Metal → nonmetal → acid
**Answer: b) Metal → basic oxide → base**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The two roads of the scheme meet in…
a) The oxides  b) The bases  c) The salts  d) The metals
**Answer: c) The salts**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The scheme is taught…
a) After all four classes are finished  b) First, as one map, before each class is studied in turn  c) Only for examinations  d) In the teacher guide only
**Answer: b) First, as one map, before each class is studied in turn**
