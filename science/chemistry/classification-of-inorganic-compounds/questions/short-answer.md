# Classification of inorganic compounds — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four main classes of inorganic compounds.
**Answer:** Oxides, bases, acids and salts.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Draw the cover scheme in words: what are its two roads and where do they meet?
**Answer:** The metal road runs metal → basic oxide → base; the nonmetal road runs nonmetal → acidic oxide → acid; the two roads meet in the salts.
