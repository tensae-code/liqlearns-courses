# The rate law — and reaction mechanism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The orders in a rate law are found from…
a) The balanced equation's coefficients  b) Initial-rates experiments  c) The periodic table  d) The products
**Answer: b) Initial-rates experiments**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Doubling [A] quadruples the rate. The order in A is…
a) Zero  b) One  c) Two  d) Four
**Answer: c) Two**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The overall order of rate = k[A][B]² is…
a) 2  b) 3  c) 1  d) 6
**Answer: b) 3**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Raising the temperature speeds a reaction, in the rate law's terms, because…
a) The orders grow  b) k grows  c) Concentrations grow  d) The equation changes
**Answer: b) k grows**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The step of a mechanism that sets the overall rate is the…
a) First step  b) Fastest step  c) Rate-determining (slowest) step  d) Last step
**Answer: c) Rate-determining (slowest) step**
