Today: **The rate law — and reaction mechanism** — Greece's way, from its own book.

The rate law says how the rate answers to the concentrations: rate = k times each concentration raised to its order.

Orders come from experiment — change one concentration at a time and watch the starting rate. Never read them off the equation.

The constant k carries the conditions, temperature above all: warm it, and k itself grows.

Behind the law stands a mechanism — steps, intermediates, and one slowest step that sets the pace and must predict the law.
