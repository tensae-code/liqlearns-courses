Today: **Analytical spectroscopy — IR, NMR, UV and mass** — South Sudan's way: four machines, four questions.
Infrared asks: which bonds? Every organic compound absorbs infrared, and each bond vibration takes its own wavelength — so the spectrum names the functional groups. NMR asks: how are the hydrogens arranged? It counts the different hydrogen environments. Ultraviolet asks about conjugated systems, the alternating double bonds. And mass spectroscopy weighs the molecule itself and reads its fragments. Four testimonies — and together they name the structure.
