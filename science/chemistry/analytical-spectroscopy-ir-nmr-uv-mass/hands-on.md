# Hands-on — Analytical spectroscopy — IR, NMR, UV and mass

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four questions drill
On four cards write the four techniques. On eight more write questions — "Which groups are present?", "How many hydrogen environments?", "What does the molecule weigh?", "Is there a conjugated system?", and four more of your own from the lesson. Deal the questions and match each to its technique, saying why.

## Read a verdict
Your teacher describes an unknown: IR shows C=O and a broad O–H; the mass spectrum's heaviest line is at 60. Write the fullest verdict the evidence allows — family, mass — and name the technique each part of the verdict came from.
