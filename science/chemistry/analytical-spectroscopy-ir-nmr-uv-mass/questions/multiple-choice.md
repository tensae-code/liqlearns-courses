# Analytical spectroscopy — IR, NMR, UV and mass — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In infrared spectroscopy, what absorbs at a characteristic wavelength?
a) Each element  b) Each kind of bond vibration  c) Each colour  d) Each hydrogen only
**Answer: b) Each kind of bond vibration**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An IR spectrum is used chiefly to identify…
a) The molecular mass  b) The functional groups present  c) The melting point  d) The hydrogen environments
**Answer: b) The functional groups present**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) NMR is described in the book as…
a) A weighing technique  b) A structure-determining technique  c) A separation method  d) A titration
**Answer: b) A structure-determining technique**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The NMR spectrum counts…
a) The carbons only  b) The different hydrogen environments and their sizes  c) The electrons  d) The fragments
**Answer: b) The different hydrogen environments and their sizes**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) UV spectroscopy speaks most strongly for molecules with…
a) Single bonds only  b) Conjugated systems — alternating double bonds  c) No bonds  d) Ionic lattices
**Answer: b) Conjugated systems — alternating double bonds**

**6.** (Sub lesson 4) The heaviest line of a mass spectrum gives…
a) The Rf value  b) The molecular mass  c) The boiling point  d) The pH
**Answer: b) The molecular mass**
