# Analytical spectroscopy — IR, NMR, UV and mass — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can IR distinguish a carboxylic acid from an ester?
**Answer: Both show the C=O absorption, but the acid also shows the broad O–H absorption of its –OH group; the ester has the C=O without that O–H band.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What two facts does an NMR spectrum give about a molecule's hydrogens?
**Answer: How many different hydrogen environments the molecule has, and — from the sizes of the signals — how many hydrogens sit in each environment.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 4) What are the two testimonies of a mass spectrum?
**Answer: The heaviest ion gives the molecular mass; the pattern of lighter fragment ions — the pieces the molecule breaks into — fingerprints its structure.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Which of the four techniques is the slimmest witness, and why?
**Answer: Ultra-violet spectroscopy: it confirms a conjugated framework and follows concentrations, but does not map the whole structure the way NMR and the mass pattern do.**
