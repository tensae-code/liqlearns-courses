# Analytical spectroscopy — IR, NMR, UV and mass — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In IR, each bond _____ absorbs at its own characteristic wavelength.
**Answer: vibration**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) NMR counts the different _____ environments in a molecule.
**Answer: hydrogen**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) UV absorption is strongest for _____ systems of alternating double bonds.
**Answer: conjugated**

**4.** (Sub lesson 4) The heaviest line in a mass spectrum gives the _____ mass.
**Answer: molecular**
