## Burning wood

Where the student book asks what happens to wood when it burns, the guide supplies the full teacher answer: the wood's structure is transformed into carbon dioxide and water vapour, and the residual ash consists of calcium carbonate, potash, phosphates and trace metals; the change releases heat energy to the surroundings, and defining "structure", "transformation" and "energy" is to be done through this example.
