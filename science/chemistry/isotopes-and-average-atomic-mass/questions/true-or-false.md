# Isotopes and average atomic mass — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What differs between isotopes of one element — The number of neutrons
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What is the mass number — How far the equilibrium lies toward products
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What does the table's atomic mass average over — The isotopes, weighted by abundance
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What stays the same in all isotopes of an element — Oxidation and reduction half-reactions
**Answer: False**
