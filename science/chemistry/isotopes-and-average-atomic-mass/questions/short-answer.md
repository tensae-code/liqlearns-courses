# Isotopes and average atomic mass — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What differs between isotopes of one element?
**Answer: The number of neutrons**

**2. ** What is the mass number?
**Answer: Protons + neutrons**

**3. ** What does the table's atomic mass average over?
**Answer: The isotopes, weighted by abundance**

**4. ** What stays the same in all isotopes of an element?
**Answer: The proton number Z**
