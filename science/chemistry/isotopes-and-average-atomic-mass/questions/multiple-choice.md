# Isotopes and average atomic mass — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What differs between isotopes of one element?
a) H₂O  b) The number of neutrons  c) Nothing — it spectates  d) Neutralising power
**Answer: b) The number of neutrons**

**2. ** What is the mass number?
a) Protons + neutrons  b) Molasses  c) The C=C double bond  d) Respiration, decay and combustion
**Answer: a) Protons + neutrons**

**3. ** What does the table's atomic mass average over?
a) It shrinks  b) A positive ion (cation)  c) d electrons can also be lost  d) The isotopes, weighted by abundance
**Answer: d) The isotopes, weighted by abundance**

**4. ** What stays the same in all isotopes of an element?
a) Yeast (its enzymes)  b) Weakly polar  c) The proton number Z  d) One with three –OH groups
**Answer: c) The proton number Z**
