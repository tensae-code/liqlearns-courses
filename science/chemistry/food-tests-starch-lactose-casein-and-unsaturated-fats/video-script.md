# Video script — Food tests — starch, lactose, casein and unsaturated fats

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Food tests** — Bulgaria's laboratory on margarine and milk. Iodine betrays starch. Permanganate, decolourised, betrays unsaturated fat. Dilute acetic acid throws milk's casein down; copper hydroxide proves its lactose; biuret confirms the protein. Four reagents, four invisible things made visible.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
