# Hands-on — Food tests — starch, lactose, casein and unsaturated fats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Test-plan table
Before any bench work, build the lesson's plan as a table: FOOD / FRACTION SOUGHT / REAGENT / POSITIVE RESULT — four rows (margarine–starch; margarine–unsaturated residues; milk–casein; milk–lactose) plus the biuret confirmation row. The table is filled from the lesson alone.

## Fraction map
Draw milk as a flow map: milk → (add dilute acetic acid) → casein curds out + clear solution → (Cu(OH)₂) → lactose proven. Under each arrow write what the step separates and what it proves. Paper only — the real bench version belongs to the school laboratory with a teacher present.
