# Food tests — starch, lactose, casein and unsaturated fats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Starch in margarine is tested with…
a) Biuret reagent  b) Iodine in the aqueous layer  c) FeCl₃  d) Litmus
**Answer: b) Iodine in the aqueous layer**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Unsaturated fatty-acid residues are shown by…
a) KMnO₄ decolourisation  b) A silver mirror  c) Blue litmus reddening  d) Precipitation with AgNO₃
**Answer: a) KMnO₄ decolourisation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Casein is precipitated from milk by…
a) Ethanol  b) Dilute acetic acid  c) Concentrated NaOH  d) Iodine
**Answer: b) Dilute acetic acid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Lactose in the clear solution is proven with…
a) Cu(OH)₂  b) BaCl₂  c) FeCl₃  d) KMnO₄
**Answer: a) Cu(OH)₂**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Proteins are confirmed by the…
a) Iodine test  b) Biuret reaction  c) Flame test  d) Fizz test
**Answer: b) Biuret reaction**
