# Food tests — starch, lactose, casein and unsaturated fats — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does KMnO₄ decolourisation prove about margarine's fat residues?
**Answer: That they are unsaturated — carbon–carbon double bonds are present, which decolourise the permanganate.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) List milk's fractions as the laboratory separates them.
**Answer: Casein (the protein fraction, precipitated by dilute acetic acid), then lactose in the clear solution (proven with Cu(OH)₂), with protein confirmed by biuret.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Milk curdles with ethanol and with CuSO₄. What is happening to the protein?
**Answer: Its structure is being destroyed — the protein denatures and comes out of solution as curds, by two different agents.**
