# Food tests — starch, lactose, casein and unsaturated fats — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Iodine turns _____ blue-violet.
**Answer: starch**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Milk's protein fraction, precipitated by dilute acetic acid, is _____.
**Answer: casein**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Proteins are confirmed by the _____ reaction.
**Answer: biuret**
