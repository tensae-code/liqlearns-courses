# Aluminium — a metal that fights acids and alkalis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the equations for aluminium with (a) dilute HCl and (b) NaOH solution.
**Answer: (a) 2Al + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂. (b) 2Al + 2NaOH + 2H₂O → 2NaAlO₂ + 3H₂↑.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can aluminium tankers carry nitric acid?
**Answer: Nitric acid forms a protective oxide coat on aluminium that seals the surface, so the acid does not attack the metal — the tanker is safe precisely because of the coat.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the compounds aluminium forms with sulfur, nitrogen and carbon.
**Answer: Al₂S₃ (with sulfur), AlN (with nitrogen) and Al₄C₃ (with carbon); with the halogens, AlCl₃ and AlF₃.**
