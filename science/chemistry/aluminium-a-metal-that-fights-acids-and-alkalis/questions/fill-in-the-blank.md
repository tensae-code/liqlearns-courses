# Aluminium — a metal that fights acids and alkalis — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Aluminium in nitric acid is protected by its _____ coat.
**Answer: oxide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) With NaOH, aluminium forms soluble _____ and hydrogen.
**Answer: aluminates**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A metal that reacts with both acids and alkalis is called _____.
**Answer: amphoteric**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Aluminium with dilute hydrochloric acid releases the gas _____.
**Answer: hydrogen**
