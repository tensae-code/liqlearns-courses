# Aluminium — a metal that fights acids and alkalis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Aluminium with dilute hydrochloric acid reacts…
a) Fast, giving AlCl₃ and hydrogen  b) Not at all  c) Slowly, giving SO₂  d) Only when heated for hours
**Answer: a) Fast, giving AlCl₃ and hydrogen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Aluminium does not react with nitric acid because…
a) Nitric acid is weak  b) A protective oxide coat seals the metal  c) Aluminium is a noble metal  d) The acid is too dilute
**Answer: b) A protective oxide coat seals the metal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Hot concentrated sulfuric acid on aluminium gives off…
a) H₂  b) O₂  c) SO₂  d) Cl₂
**Answer: c) SO₂**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) With NaOH solution, aluminium gives…
a) A precipitate and oxygen  b) Nothing — alkalis never attack metals  c) Sodium metal  d) A soluble aluminate and hydrogen
**Answer: d) A soluble aluminate and hydrogen**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Aluminium's behaviour toward both acids and alkalis is called…
a) Amphoteric  b) Inert  c) Catalytic  d) Radioactive
**Answer: a) Amphoteric**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Workbook Activity 1 studies aluminium powder by…
a) Dissolving it in water  b) Tipping it onto a non-luminous Bunsen flame  c) Freezing it  d) Weighing it in air
**Answer: b) Tipping it onto a non-luminous Bunsen flame**
