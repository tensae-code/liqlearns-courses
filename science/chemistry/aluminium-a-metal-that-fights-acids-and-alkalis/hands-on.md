# Hands-on — Aluminium — a metal that fights acids and alkalis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reaction map (paper only)
Draw aluminium at the centre of a page and its partners around it — oxygen, dilute HCl, concentrated HCl, hot concentrated H₂SO₄, HNO₃, NaOH, sulfur, nitrogen, carbon, chlorine. Draw an arrow for each reaction with its products, and a barred arrow for nitric acid with the reason written on the bar. The map is the lesson.

## Name that gas (paper only)
The workbook's Activity 2 ends with a question: aluminium powder heated in NaOH solution gives off a gas — name it and write the equation. Write the workbook answer in full, then add the test you would use to prove the gas is what you named, from the course's gas lessons.
