# Lesson plan — Calcium compounds — lime and limestone

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Calcium compounds — lime and limestone
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the calcium-compounds family taught as one industrial lesson — limestone (calcium carbonate, CaCO₃), its thermal decomposition to quicklime (calcium oxide, CaO), slaking to slaked lime (calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂, and limewater), and the family's uses in farming, building and industry; Cambodia's Grade 11 book teaches it as §3 of its inorganic industrial compounds chapter (Ch.5 §3, printed p. 160), after ammonia and sulfuric acid

## Sub lessons
1. **Limestone — calcium carbonate** — Limestone is calcium carbonate, CaCO₃, a rock built from the shells and skeletons of ancient sea creatures, and the starting material of the whole family. It fizzes with dilute acid, giving carbon dioxide — the limewater test of this course.
2. **Quicklime and slaked lime** — Heat limestone strongly and it breaks down — thermal decomposition — into quicklime, calcium oxide CaO, driving off carbon dioxide: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂. Add water to quicklime and it slakes, with much heat given out, into slaked lime, calcium hydroxide Ca(OH)₂: CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂. Slaked lime dissolved sparingly in water is limewater.
3. **What the lime family is for** — Cambodia teaches calcium compounds inside its inorganic industrial compounds chapter, between sulfuric acid and the close of the chapter: quicklime and slaked lime neutralise acidic soils, slaked lime makes mortar and whitewash and treats water, limestone feeds cement making and the blast furnace, and the cycle limestone → quicklime → slaked lime → back to carbonate in mortar is the family's working circle.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Mixes quicklime up with slaked lime → follow the water: quicklime CaO is limestone with the CO₂ driven off; slaked lime Ca(OH)₂ is quicklime with water taken in.
