# Calcium compounds — lime and limestone — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The chemical name of limestone is calcium _____.
**Answer: carbonate**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The change CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂, driven by strong heat, is a thermal _____.
**Answer: decomposition**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂, is commonly called _____ lime.
**Answer: slaked**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The clear solution of slaked lime in water, used to test for carbon dioxide, is called _____.
**Answer: limewater**
