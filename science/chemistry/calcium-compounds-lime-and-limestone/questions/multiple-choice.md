# Calcium compounds — lime and limestone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Limestone is…
a) Calcium oxide  b) Calcium hydroxide  c) Calcium carbonate, CaCO₃  d) Calcium sulfate
**Answer: c) Calcium carbonate, CaCO₃**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Limestone's signature test is that it…
a) Burns with a blue flame  b) Fizzes with dilute acid, giving carbon dioxide  c) Dissolves quietly in water  d) Turns litmus blue
**Answer: b) Fizzes with dilute acid, giving carbon dioxide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Heating limestone strongly gives…
a) Slaked lime and water  b) Quicklime and carbon dioxide  c) Cement directly  d) Calcium metal
**Answer: b) Quicklime and carbon dioxide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Quicklime is…
a) CaCO₃  b) Ca(OH)₂  c) CaO — calcium oxide  d) CaCl₂
**Answer: c) CaO — calcium oxide**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Slaking quicklime means…
a) Heating it again  b) Adding water, giving out heat, to form slaked lime Ca(OH)₂  c) Dissolving it in acid  d) Grinding it finer
**Answer: b) Adding water, giving out heat, to form slaked lime Ca(OH)₂**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Mortar hardens because slaked lime slowly…
a) Dries out  b) Melts  c) Takes carbon dioxide back from the air and becomes calcium carbonate again  d) Reacts with sand to form glass
**Answer: c) Takes carbon dioxide back from the air and becomes calcium carbonate again**
