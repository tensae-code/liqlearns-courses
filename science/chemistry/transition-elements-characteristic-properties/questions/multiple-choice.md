# Transition elements — characteristic properties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which set names characteristic properties of the transition elements?
a) Fixed oxidation state and colourless ions  b) Variable oxidation states, coloured compounds, catalysis and complex formation  c) Low density and low melting point  d) Forming only ionic compounds
**Answer: b) Variable oxidation states, coloured compounds, catalysis and complex formation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The deep-blue [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺ is the lesson's example of…
a) An alloy  b) A complex ion — a central metal ion holding ligands  c) An oxidation state  d) A catalyst
**Answer: b) A complex ion — a central metal ion holding ligands**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The single structural fact behind the family's properties is…
a) A full outer shell  b) A partly filled (n−1)d subshell, with (n−1)d and ns electrons close in energy  c) Seven valence electrons  d) A very large atom
**Answer: b) A partly filled (n−1)d subshell, with (n−1)d and ns electrons close in energy**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Paramagnetism in transition metals comes from…
a) Paired electrons  b) Unpaired d electrons  c) Their density  d) Their colour
**Answer: b) Unpaired d electrons**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Down group 11, copper → silver → gold, reactivity…
a) Increases  b) Decreases — gold is found native  c) Stays identical  d) Vanishes at silver
**Answer: b) Decreases — gold is found native**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Gold dissolves in…
a) Nitric acid alone  b) Aqua regia  c) Hot water  d) Sodium hydroxide
**Answer: b) Aqua regia**
