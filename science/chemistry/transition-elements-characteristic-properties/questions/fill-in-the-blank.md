# Transition elements — characteristic properties — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Iron's +2 and +3 compounds show that transition elements have _____ oxidation states.
**Answer: variable**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The family's properties flow from the partly filled (n−1)_____ subshell.
**Answer: d**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Copper, silver and gold are the _____ metals of group 11.
**Answer: coinage**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Gold resists single acids and dissolves only in aqua _____.
**Answer: regia**
