# Hands-on — Transition elements — characteristic properties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Signature sort (paper only)
Six observations are listed: a blue solution; a metal recovered unchanged after speeding a reaction; an element with compounds at +2 and +6; a deep-blue colour appearing when ammonia is added to a copper solution; a metal drawn weakly to a magnet; a native metal found free in a river gravel. Match each to the characteristic property it shows, from sub lesson 1.

## One subshell, six readings (paper only)
Draw the partly filled d subshell as the centre of a page and write the six characteristic properties around it; on each spoke write the one phrase from sub lesson 2 that connects that property to the subshell.

## Group-11 gradient (paper only)
Make a three-row table for copper, silver and gold: reaction with air, acid that dissolves it, and one use. Fill it from sub lesson 3 and write one sentence on what the table shows about the group.
