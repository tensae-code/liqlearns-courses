# Video script — The kinetic theory of matter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ice holds its shape, water flows, steam vanishes into air — the same substance.

The kinetic theory: all matter is made of particles in motion.

In a solid the particles vibrate about fixed positions; in a liquid they move about while staying close; in a gas they move fast and far apart.

Particle motion and energy rise in the order solid → liquid → gas.

Now you. Say it back: What does the kinetic theory say all matter is?
Answer: Particles in motion.
