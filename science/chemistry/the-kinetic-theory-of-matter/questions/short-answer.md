# The kinetic theory of matter — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does the kinetic theory say all matter is?
**Answer: Particles in motion**

**2. ** How do solid particles move?
**Answer: They vibrate about fixed positions**

**3. ** In what order does particle energy rise?
**Answer: Solid → liquid → gas**

**4. ** In which state are particles far apart?
**Answer: Gas**
