# The kinetic theory of matter — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What does the kinetic theory say all matter is — Particles in motion
**Answer: True**

**2. ** How do solid particles move — The uncertainty principle
**Answer: False**

**3. ** In what order does particle energy rise — Solid → liquid → gas
**Answer: True**

**4. ** In which state are particles far apart — A (symmetrical) ether
**Answer: False**
