# The kinetic theory of matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does the kinetic theory say all matter is?
a) atomos  b) The electron  c) Photons knock electrons off a metal  d) Particles in motion
**Answer: d) Particles in motion**

**2. ** How do solid particles move?
a) Particles are far apart  b) The partial pressures  c) They vibrate about fixed positions  d) ½(bonding − antibonding electrons)
**Answer: c) They vibrate about fixed positions**

**3. ** In what order does particle energy rise?
a) The acid's salt plus the alcohol  b) Solid → liquid → gas  c) A carboxylic acid  d) In fruits and flowers
**Answer: b) Solid → liquid → gas**

**4. ** In which state are particles far apart?
a) Gas  b) Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses  c) 0.00 V  d) Negative (spontaneous)
**Answer: a) Gas**
