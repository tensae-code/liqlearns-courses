# The kinetic theory of matter

## Do it first
Ice holds its shape, water flows, steam vanishes into air — the same substance. Before reading, say what its particles must be doing differently in the three states.

[Picture 21654 — Three states, three levels of motion.]

The kinetic theory: all matter is made of particles in motion.

In a solid the particles vibrate about fixed positions; in a liquid they move about while staying close; in a gas they move fast and far apart.

Particle motion and energy rise in the order solid → liquid → gas. This is the master idea of the whole unit.
