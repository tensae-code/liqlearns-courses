# Hands-on — Polymerisation in the classroom — glue, borax and interfacial films

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design the record table (paper only)
Draw the activity's record table before any cup is poured: columns for cup, borax dose (mL), stretch length (cm), and the cross-linking verdict. The table is the activity's memory — the guide's lesson is read off it, so its columns must be the variables the guide names.

## Classify the processes (paper only)
Five process cards: bulk, solution, suspension, emulsion, interfacial. For the interfacial card, write the rope trick's name and where its polymer forms (at the meeting of two phases). For the classroom cup, write which family member it is *not* — the cups cross-link a ready polymer rather than polymerising monomers — and why the guide still files it here.
