# Polymerisation in the classroom — glue, borax and interfacial films — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give Activity 15's quantities in full.
**Answer: Paper glue 60 mL mixed with 60 mL water, poured into four paper cups; borax solution of 5 g in 100 mL water added in increasing doses — 5 mL, 10 mL, 20 mL — and each cup's contents drawn out and stretched [teacher evidence].**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the guide's classification of the ways polymerisation is run.
**Answer: Bulk polymerisation, solution polymerisation, suspension and emulsion polymerisation — with interfacial polymerisation named as the same family of process [teacher evidence].**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain the stretch ladder in the guide's own causal chain.
**Answer: Borax cross-links the glue polymer; more borax → more cross-linking → greater molecular weight → stronger polymer → greater stretch before it gives [teacher evidence].**
