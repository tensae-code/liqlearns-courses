# Polymerisation in the classroom — glue, borax and interfacial films — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The glue mixture of Activity 15 is…
a) 60 mL paper glue with 60 mL water  b) Glue alone  c) Borax in glue  d) Water alone
**Answer: a) 60 mL paper glue with 60 mL water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The borax solution's strength is…
a) 50 g in 100 mL  b) 5 g in 100 mL water  c) 5 g in a litre  d) Saturated
**Answer: b) 5 g in 100 mL water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) As the borax dose rises, the product's stretch…
a) Shrinks  b) Stays fixed  c) Grows  d) Turns to liquid
**Answer: c) Grows**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Borax changes the glue by…
a) Dissolving them  b) Colouring them  c) Breaking them apart  d) Cross-linking its polymer chains
**Answer: d) Cross-linking its polymer chains**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) More cross-linking gives, by the guide's rule,…
a) Greater molecular weight and a stronger polymer  b) A weaker polymer  c) A shorter chain  d) No change
**Answer: a) Greater molecular weight and a stronger polymer**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The nylon rope trick is the guide's example of…
a) Bulk polymerisation  b) Interfacial polymerisation  c) Emulsion polymerisation  d) Suspension only
**Answer: b) Interfacial polymerisation**
