## Discovering the neutron

Chadwick (1932) bombarded beryllium with α-particles and found radiation of material particles un-deflected by electric fields — neutral particles, the neutrons Rutherford had predicted; his model completes the picture: a small positive nucleus of protons and neutrons with electrons revolving around it.
