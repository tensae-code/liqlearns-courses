# Video script — Paper and pulp

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wood becomes a white sheet you can write on.

Pulping breaks wood (or other fibre) down to a fibre pulp — mechanically or chemically, as presented.

The pulp is washed, bleached and beaten, then spread as a watery web on rollers/screens.

Pressing and drying the web gives the paper sheet — Ethiopian production is presented in the section.

Now you. Say it back: What is pulp?
Answer: Broken-down fibre from wood.
