# Paper and pulp — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is pulp?
**Answer: Broken-down fibre from wood**

**2. ** What is the pulp spread as?
**Answer: A watery web (on screens/rollers)**

**3. ** What finishes the sheet?
**Answer: Pressing and drying**

**4. ** Name a pulping route.
**Answer: Mechanical or chemical**
