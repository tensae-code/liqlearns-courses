# Paper and pulp — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is pulp?
a) Prevention of waste  b) They are rearranged, never created or destroyed  c) Broken-down fibre from wood  d) Solid wastes and agrochemicals
**Answer: c) Broken-down fibre from wood**

**2. ** What is the pulp spread as?
a) Molecular crystals  b) A watery web (on screens/rollers)  c) H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair  d) Layers slide over the flowing electron sea
**Answer: b) A watery web (on screens/rollers)**

**3. ** What finishes the sheet?
a) Pressing and drying  b) –al  c) Two  d) No C=C bonds
**Answer: a) Pressing and drying**

**4. ** Name a pulping route.
a) Oxidation and reduction half-reactions  b) The anode  c) Aluminium  d) Mechanical or chemical
**Answer: d) Mechanical or chemical**
