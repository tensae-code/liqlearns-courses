# Paper and pulp — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What is pulp — Broken-down fibre from wood
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What is the pulp spread as — The electrochemical (reactivity) series
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What finishes the sheet — Pressing and drying
**Answer: True**

**4. ** Name a pulping route. — Gamma rays
**Answer: False**
