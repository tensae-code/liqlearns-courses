# Paper and pulp — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What is pulp? — c
2. What is the pulp spread as? — d
3. What finishes the sheet? — a
4. Name a pulping route. — b

**Answer key:** 1–c: Broken-down fibre from wood · 2–d: A watery web (on screens/rollers) · 3–a: Pressing and drying · 4–b: Mechanical or chemical
