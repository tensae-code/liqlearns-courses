Today: **Primary, secondary, tertiary and quaternary carbons** — Iraq's way: classify a carbon by its company.
Count how many other carbons a carbon touches. One — primary. Two — secondary. Three — tertiary. Four — quaternary, with no hydrogen left at all. And the hydrogens take the name of the carbon that holds them. One count, four classes — and you can read any chain at sight.
