# Primary, secondary, tertiary and quaternary carbons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A carbon's class is decided by counting its…
a) hydrogens  b) carbon neighbours  c) oxygen neighbours  d) total bonds of every kind
**Answer: b) carbon neighbours**

**2.** A carbon joined to two other carbons is…
a) primary  b) secondary  c) tertiary  d) quaternary
**Answer: b) secondary**

**3.** A carbon joined to four other carbons is…
a) primary  b) secondary  c) tertiary  d) quaternary
**Answer: d) quaternary**

**4.** A quaternary carbon holds how many hydrogens?
a) one  b) two  c) none  d) four
**Answer: c) none**

**5.** Hydrogens on a secondary carbon are called…
a) primary hydrogens  b) secondary hydrogens  c) quaternary hydrogens  d) free hydrogens
**Answer: b) secondary hydrogens**

**6.** A carbon joined to three other carbons is…
a) primary  b) secondary  c) tertiary  d) quaternary
**Answer: c) tertiary**
