# Primary, secondary, tertiary and quaternary carbons — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A carbon joined to one other carbon is a _____ carbon.
**Answer: primary**

**2.** A carbon joined to three other carbons is a _____ carbon.
**Answer: tertiary**

**3.** A carbon joined to four other carbons is a _____ carbon.
**Answer: quaternary**

**4.** Hydrogens take the class of the _____ that holds them.
**Answer: carbon**
