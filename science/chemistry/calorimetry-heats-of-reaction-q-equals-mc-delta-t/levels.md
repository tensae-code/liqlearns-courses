# Calorimetry — heats of reaction, q = mcΔT — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The simple calorimeter and q = mcΔT — putting a number on the warmth, Heat of neutralisation — one reaction underneath them all, Heat of solution — ammonium chloride and the cold cup), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, working fresh q = mcΔT problems forwards (temperature → heat → heat per mole) and backwards (a wanted temperature change → the amount to react), and stating the three calorimeter assumptions unprompted.
