Today: **Calorimetry** — Dominica's CSEC energy calculations: warmth, numbered.

A foam cup, a thermometer, and one equation — q equals m times c times delta-T — turn a warm cup into a measurement. Mix any strong acid with any strong alkali and the answer is always the same, about minus 56.3 kilojoules per mole, because underneath every pair the same single reaction runs: hydroxide plus hydrogen ion makes water. Dissolve ammonium chloride instead and the cup cools by a calculable amount — its dissolving breaks more than its solvation repays, and the water pays the difference. Heat stops being a feeling and becomes a number.
