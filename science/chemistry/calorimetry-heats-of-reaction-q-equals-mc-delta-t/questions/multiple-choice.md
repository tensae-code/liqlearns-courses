# Calorimetry — heats of reaction, q = mcΔT — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The heat taken up or given out in the calorimeter is calculated as…
a) q = m ÷ c × ΔT  b) q = m × c × ΔT  c) q = ΔT ÷ (m × c)  d) q = m × ΔT ÷ c
**Answer: b) q = m × c × ΔT**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In q = mcΔT for a neutralisation, m is the mass of…
a) The acid alone  b) The alkali alone  c) The whole mixture  d) The cup
**Answer: c) The whole mixture**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The heat of neutralisation of strong acids by strong alkalis is about…
a) −25.6 kJ mol⁻¹  b) +56.3 kJ mol⁻¹  c) −56.3 kJ mol⁻¹  d) A different value for every pair
**Answer: c) −56.3 kJ mol⁻¹**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) That heat is the same for every strong pair because underneath, the reaction is always…
a) H₂ + O₂ → H₂O  b) OH⁻ + H⁺ → H₂O  c) Na⁺ + Cl⁻ → NaCl  d) Acid + alkali → salt
**Answer: b) OH⁻ + H⁺ → H₂O**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The heat of solution of ammonium chloride is +25.6 kJ mol⁻¹; the plus sign means dissolving is…
a) Exothermic  b) Endothermic  c) Neutral  d) Explosive
**Answer: b) Endothermic**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Ammonium chloride cools its water because…
a) The salt is stored cold  b) Bond breaking costs more energy than solvation releases  c) The cup loses heat  d) Water evaporates
**Answer: b) Bond breaking costs more energy than solvation releases**
