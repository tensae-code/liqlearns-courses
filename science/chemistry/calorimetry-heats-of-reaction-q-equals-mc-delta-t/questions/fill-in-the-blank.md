# Calorimetry — heats of reaction, q = mcΔT — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The insulated vessel in which heats of reaction are measured is a _____.
**Answer: calorimeter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In q = mcΔT, the symbol c is the _____ heat capacity.
**Answer: specific**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Reading the peak temperature where two best-fit lines cross, in a titration done by thermometer, is _____ titration.
**Answer: thermometric**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Water molecules gathering round freed ions, releasing energy, is called _____.
**Answer: solvation**
