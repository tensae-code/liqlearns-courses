# Electrolysis of molten salts — and what electrolysis is for — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** half-cell reactions _____ into the cell reaction.
**Answer: combine**

**2. ** The named industrial uses close the unit: production and purification/refining of metals, and _____ — electrolysis is not a way of producing electricity (a review trap).
**Answer: electroplating**

**3. ** In a molten binary electrolyte the only ions present discharge at the electrodes: _____ are reduced to metal at the cathode, anions oxidised at the anode (worked on a molten salt);
**Answer: cations**
