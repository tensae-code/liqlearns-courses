## Electrolysis of molten salts

In a molten binary electrolyte the only ions present discharge at the electrodes: cations are reduced to metal at the cathode, anions oxidised at the anode (worked on a molten salt); half-cell reactions combine into the cell reaction. The named industrial uses close the unit: production and purification/refining of metals, and electroplating — electrolysis is not a way of producing electricity (a review trap).
