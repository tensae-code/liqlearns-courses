# Dalton's atomic theory — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** atoms of an element are _____ in mass;
**Answer: identical**

**2. ** atoms combine in whole-number ratios to form _____;
**Answer: compounds**

**3. ** in reactions atoms are rearranged, never _____, destroyed or split.
**Answer: created**

**4. ** The theory explains all three laws of _____ combination.
**Answer: chemical**
