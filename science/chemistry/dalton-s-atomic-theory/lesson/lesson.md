## Dalton's atomic theory

Dalton (1808), the British schoolteacher who revived atomos on a scientific basis: each element consists of one kind of indivisible atom; atoms of an element are identical in mass; atoms combine in whole-number ratios to form compounds;

in reactions atoms are rearranged, never created, destroyed or split. The theory explains all three laws of chemical combination.
