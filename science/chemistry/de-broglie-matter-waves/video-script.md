The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Light, a wave, behaves as particles — photons.

De Broglie proposed that every moving particle has a wavelength, given by λ = h/mv (h is Planck's constant, m mass, v speed).

Heavy, slow things have wavelengths far too small to notice; light particles like electrons have measurable ones.

Electron diffraction — electrons bending and interfering like waves — is the evidence.

Wave–particle duality is now a two-way street: waves act as particles, particles act as waves.

Now you. Say it back: Write de Broglie's relation.
Answer: λ = h/mv.
