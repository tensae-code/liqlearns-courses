# De Broglie — matter waves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Write de Broglie's relation.
a) Singly occupy each equal orbital first  b) Effective nuclear charge and shielding  c) Valence (outer-shell) electrons  d) λ = h/mv
**Answer: d) λ = h/mv**

**2. ** What experiment evidences electron waves?
a) The carbon carrying the –OH  b) Carbon dioxide and water  c) Electron diffraction  d) The side with fewer gas moles
**Answer: c) Electron diffraction**

**3. ** Why don't we notice a thrown ball's wavelength?
a) The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺  b) It is far too small — the ball is heavy  c) BF₃ (or a metal ion)  d) Buffer solutions
**Answer: b) It is far too small — the ball is heavy**

**4. ** In λ = h/mv, what is m?
a) The particle's mass  b) The Solvay process (Unit 3)  c) Broken-down fibre from wood  d) Distillation
**Answer: a) The particle's mass**
