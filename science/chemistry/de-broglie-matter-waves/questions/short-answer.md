# De Broglie — matter waves — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Write de Broglie's relation.
**Answer: λ = h/mv**

**2. ** What experiment evidences electron waves?
**Answer: Electron diffraction**

**3. ** Why don't we notice a thrown ball's wavelength?
**Answer: It is far too small — the ball is heavy**

**4. ** In λ = h/mv, what is m?
**Answer: The particle's mass**
