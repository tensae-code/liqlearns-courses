# Alpha, beta and gamma radiation compared — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Complete the comparison from memory: what each radiation is, its charge, and its bending in a field.
**Answer:** Alpha — helium nucleus, +2, bent slightly; beta — fast electron, −1, bent strongly; gamma — photon, uncharged, unaffected.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does a field bend alpha and beta in opposite directions?
**Answer:** Their charges are opposite (+2 against −1), and a field pushes opposite charges opposite ways; the amounts differ because alpha is far heavier.
