# Alpha, beta and gamma radiation compared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Alpha radiation is…
a) A helium nucleus, charge +2  b) A fast electron, charge −1  c) A photon, uncharged  d) A neutron stream
**Answer: a) A helium nucleus, charge +2**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In a magnetic field, beta radiation bends…
a) Strongly  b) Slightly  c) Not at all  d) Only when heated
**Answer: a) Strongly**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Gamma radiation is…
a) A photon — uncharged and unaffected by fields  b) A helium nucleus  c) A fast electron  d) A proton stream
**Answer: a) A photon — uncharged and unaffected by fields**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Alpha bends only slightly mainly because…
a) It is heavy, with a double charge  b) It is uncharged  c) It travels slowest in vacuum  d) It is a photon
**Answer: a) It is heavy, with a double charge**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The radiations come from…
a) Radioactive isotopes  b) Stable isotopes  c) All isotopes equally  d) Only synthetic elements
**Answer: a) Radioactive isotopes**
