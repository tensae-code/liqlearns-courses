# Hands-on — Alpha, beta and gamma radiation compared

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the table
Draw Egypt's three-column table from memory — is / charge / bend in a field — for alpha, beta and gamma. Check it against the lesson, fix any cell you missed, and say the gamma row aloud last: photon, uncharged, unaffected.

## Sort the bends
Write three cards: slight bend, strong bend, no bend. Pin each to its radiation and say the reason — mass and charge — for each card's place.
