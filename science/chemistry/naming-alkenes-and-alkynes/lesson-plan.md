# Lesson plan — Naming alkenes and alkynes

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Naming alkenes and alkynes
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** naming the unsaturated chains: the longest chain carrying the multiple bond, numbered from the end nearest it, with the bond's position index placed in the name — alkene names in -ène/-ene, alkyne names in -yne, from the science-track 6AS rule to the Lettres books' lowest-locant naming (Mauritania, Grade 12 — *Chimie 6ème AS*, Ch. 3, printed p. 36; *Sciences Physiques 6AS — Lettres Modernes*, Ch. 1, printed pp. 46–58; *Sciences Physiques 6AS — Lettres Originelles* (Arabic), Ch. 1, printed pp. 39–45)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Naming alkanes — the IUPAC rules is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
