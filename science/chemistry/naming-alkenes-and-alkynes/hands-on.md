# Hands-on — Naming alkenes and alkynes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Address the bond (paper only)
Draw but-1-ene and but-2-ene as developed formulas. Under each, point to the index in its name and the bond it addresses; then write one sentence on why the index cannot be dropped from either name.

## Name them (paper only)
Three skeletal descriptions are given: a 4-carbon chain, double bond at carbon 1; a 4-carbon chain, triple bond at carbon 1; a 5-carbon chain with a methyl branch and triple bond at carbon 1. Name each by the books' rules (but-1-ene; but-1-yne; 4-methylpent-1-yne as the LO book works it).
