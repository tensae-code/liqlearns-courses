# Naming alkenes and alkynes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the parent-chain rule for unsaturated naming.
**Answer: Choose the longest chain carrying the multiple bond as the parent chain.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is the numbering direction chosen, and where does the index go?
**Answer: Number from the end nearest the multiple bond so it takes the lowest locant, and place that position index in the name.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give the two suffixes and their bonds.
**Answer: -ene for the double bond (alkenes); -yne for the triple bond (alkynes).**
