# Naming alkenes and alkynes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The parent chain must carry the _____ bond.
**Answer: multiple**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Numbering gives the bond the _____ locant.
**Answer: lowest**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A triple bond takes the suffix -_____.
**Answer: yne**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In but-2-ene, the double bond starts at carbon _____.
**Answer: 2**
