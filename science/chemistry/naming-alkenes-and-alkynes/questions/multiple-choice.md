# Naming alkenes and alkynes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The parent chain of an alkene is the longest chain…
a) Of all  b) Carrying the double bond  c) Without branches  d) Of carbons only
**Answer: b) Carrying the double bond**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The chain is numbered from…
a) The middle  b) Either end — it does not matter  c) The end nearest the multiple bond  d) The heaviest end
**Answer: c) The end nearest the multiple bond**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The suffix for a triple bond is…
a) -ane  b) -ene  c) -yne  d) -ol
**Answer: c) -yne**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) But-1-ene and but-2-ene differ in…
a) Chain length  b) The double bond's position  c) Formula  d) Family
**Answer: b) The double bond's position**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The position index in an unsaturated name gives…
a) The molar mass  b) Where the multiple bond sits  c) The number of hydrogens  d) The state
**Answer: b) Where the multiple bond sits**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Alkynes follow the general formula…
a) CnH2n+2  b) CnH2n  c) CnH2n−2  d) CnHn
**Answer: c) CnH2n−2**
