# Dalton's law of partial pressures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define partial pressure in the book's own terms.
**Answer:** The partial pressure of one component of a mixture is the pressure that component would exert if it occupied the mixture's whole volume on its own, at the same temperature.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State Dalton's law and the book's route to it.
**Answer:** The total pressure of a gas mixture equals the sum of its components' partial pressures; Kuwait's book derives the law from the ideal gas law, each component's amount fixing its partial pressure at the shared volume and temperature.
