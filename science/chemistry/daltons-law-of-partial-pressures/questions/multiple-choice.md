# Dalton's law of partial pressures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The partial pressure of a gas in a mixture is the pressure it would exert…
a) In a vacuum at any temperature  b) Occupying the mixture's volume alone, at the same temperature  c) If the other gases were heated  d) Per litre of the liquid it dissolves in
**Answer: b) Occupying the mixture's volume alone, at the same temperature**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Dalton's law says the total pressure of a gas mixture is…
a) The largest partial pressure  b) The sum of the components' partial pressures  c) The product of the partial pressures  d) Always 100 kPa
**Answer: b) The sum of the components' partial pressures**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Kuwait's book derives Dalton's law from…
a) Boyle's law alone  b) The ideal gas law  c) Charles' law alone  d) The periodic table
**Answer: b) The ideal gas law**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The book's worked mixture, air, is made of…
a) Oxygen only  b) Oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide and traces of other gases  c) Hydrogen and helium  d) Carbon dioxide only
**Answer: b) Oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide and traces of other gases**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A mixture's partial pressures, added together, give…
a) More than the total pressure  b) Less than the total pressure  c) Exactly the total pressure  d) The temperature of the mixture
**Answer: c) Exactly the total pressure**
