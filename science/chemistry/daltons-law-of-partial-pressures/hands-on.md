# Hands-on — Dalton's law of partial pressures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Share out a pressure
Take a total pressure of 100 kPa for a made-up two-gas mixture in one vessel. Give the first gas a partial pressure of 30 kPa, work out the second gas's partial pressure from Dalton's law, and say in one sentence what that 70 kPa means — the pressure that gas would exert alone in the same vessel at the same temperature.

## Name air's shares
List air's components as Kuwait's book names them — oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide and traces — and say what Dalton's law claims about their partial pressures and the one air pressure.
