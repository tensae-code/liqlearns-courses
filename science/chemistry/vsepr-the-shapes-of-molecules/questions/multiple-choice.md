# VSEPR — the shapes of molecules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does VSEPR stand on?
a) Le Chatelier's principle  b) Hydrogen bonds  c) An –OH group  d) Electron pairs repel and spread apart
**Answer: d) Electron pairs repel and spread apart**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is the tetrahedral bond angle?
a) 7 — neutral  b) Acidic  c) 109.5°  d) Tenfold
**Answer: c) 109.5°**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** Which repels more, lone or bonding pairs?
a) The tail (water-hating end)  b) Lone pairs  c) Limestone and clay  d) Chrome and vegetable tanning
**Answer: b) Lone pairs**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) ** What shape is water?
a) Bent  b) The main energy level (and orbital size)  c) Periods  d) Predict its properties
**Answer: a) Bent**
