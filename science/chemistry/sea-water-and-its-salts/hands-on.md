# Hands-on — Sea water and its salts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Taste is evidence — look, do not taste unknown water
If you are at a sea or a salt lake with a grown-up, look at the shore line: the crust left where water has dried is the sea giving its salts back. Record where the crust forms and say, from the lesson, what it is.

## Water taken away
Put a spoon of salty water (table salt in clean water, made with a grown-up) on a plate in a sunny place. Look again when the water has gone and name what remains — and say what that proves about salts in solution.
