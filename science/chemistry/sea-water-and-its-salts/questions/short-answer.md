# Sea water and its salts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In what state are the sea's salts while they are in the water?
**Answer:** Dissolved — held in solution, invisible in the water, which is why they must be obtained by taking the water away.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is the sea called a salt source in this lesson?
**Answer:** Because salts are obtained from its water: the water holds salts in solution and gives them back when the water is removed.
