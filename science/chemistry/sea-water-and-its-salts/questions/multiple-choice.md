# Sea water and its salts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The guide's named lesson is…
a) Rivers and their fish  b) The sea and its salts  c) Rain and snow  d) Wells and springs
**Answer: b) The sea and its salts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's source is…
a) The Grade 9 student textbook  b) The Grade 9 teacher guide, contents (teacher evidence)  c) The Grade 8 teacher guide  d) The Grade 7 student textbook
**Answer: b) The Grade 9 teacher guide, contents (teacher evidence)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Sea water holds its salts…
a) As floating grains  b) In solution — dissolved and invisible  c) Only at the bottom  d) Only near rivers
**Answer: b) In solution — dissolved and invisible**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The sea counts in this lesson as…
a) A salt source — salts are obtained from it  b) Pure water  c) A substance with no chemistry  d) A mixture that can never be separated
**Answer: a) A salt source — salts are obtained from it**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The sea lesson stands in the guide beside the teaching of…
a) Electrolysis  b) Water hardness and its removal  c) The periodic table  d) Organic compounds
**Answer: b) Water hardness and its removal**
