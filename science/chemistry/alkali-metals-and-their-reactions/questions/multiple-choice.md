# Alkali metals and their reactions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The three grades of oxide the alkali metals form with oxygen are…
a) Normal oxides, peroxides and superoxides  b) Acidic, basic and neutral oxides  c) Monoxide, dioxide and trioxide only  d) Oxides, hydroxides and hydrides
**Answer: a) Normal oxides, peroxides and superoxides**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) With hydrogen, the alkali metals form…
a) Ionic hydrides  b) Covalent hydrides  c) Acids  d) Peroxides
**Answer: a) Ionic hydrides**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Lithium reacting directly with nitrogen gives…
a) Li₃N  b) LiN₃  c) NH₃  d) LiNO₃
**Answer: a) Li₃N**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A sodium fire must not be put out with water because…
a) Water reacts with sodium, releasing hydrogen and feeding the fire  b) Water makes sodium melt  c) Sodium floats away  d) Water turns sodium into an acid
**Answer: a) Water reacts with sodium, releasing hydrogen and feeding the fire**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) On strong heating, alkali-metal carbonates and nitrates…
a) Decompose  b) Sublime unchanged  c) Turn into the metal  d) Absorb CO₂
**Answer: a) Decompose**
