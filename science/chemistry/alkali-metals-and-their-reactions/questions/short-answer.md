# Alkali metals and their reactions — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the alkali metals' reaction partners in Egypt's order, as far as you can.
**Answer:** Oxygen (normal oxides, peroxides, superoxides), hydrogen (ionic hydrides), halogens, other nonmetals (e.g. nitrogen to Li₃N), water, and acids — closing with the thermal decomposition of their carbonates and nitrates.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is Li₃N a surprising product?
**Answer:** Nitrogen gas is famously unreactive, yet lithium combines with it directly to the ionic nitride Li₃N — a measure of how eager lithium is to give up its outer electron.
