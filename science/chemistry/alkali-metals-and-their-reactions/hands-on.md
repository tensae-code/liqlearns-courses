# Hands-on — Alkali metals and their reactions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the partners
Write the family's reaction partners on slips — oxygen, hydrogen, halogens, nitrogen, water, acids, heat (for the salts). Beside each, write the product kind Egypt names. Shuffle the slips and re-sort them into the book's order from memory.

## The fire rule
Write the bench rule in your own words: what a sodium fire is, why water makes it worse (hydrogen released, reaction fed), and what the observation teaches about the whole family's reactivity.
