# Waste management — the hierarchy and e-waste — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the five ranked activities in order.
**Answer: 1 prevent, 2 reduce, 3 reuse, 4 recycle, 5 landfill.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What three cargoes make e-waste special?
**Answer: Valuable metals (gold, copper), harmful mercury, and NF₃ — the LCD-screen gas with a greenhouse effect 17,000 times CO₂'s.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Read Bulgaria's landfill figure against the EU's as a hierarchy verdict.
**Answer: Bulgaria landfills 318 kg per person against the EU's 162 — nearly double — meaning far more of its waste reaches the last resort instead of being prevented, reused or recycled above it.**
