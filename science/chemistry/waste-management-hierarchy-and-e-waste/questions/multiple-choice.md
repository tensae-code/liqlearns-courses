# Waste management — the hierarchy and e-waste — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The hierarchy's first activity is…
a) Recycle  b) Landfill  c) Prevent  d) Reuse
**Answer: c) Prevent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Landfill stands at rank…
a) 1  b) 3  c) 5 — the last resort  d) 2
**Answer: c) 5 — the last resort**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) E-waste is the…
a) Smallest stream  b) Fastest-growing stream  c) Only recycled stream  d) Harmless stream
**Answer: b) Fastest-growing stream**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) NF₃ from LCD screens has a greenhouse effect, against CO₂, of…
a) 17 times  b) 170 times  c) 17,000 times  d) Equal
**Answer: c) 17,000 times**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Landfilled waste per person, Bulgaria vs EU, is…
a) 162 vs 318 kg  b) 318 vs 162 kg  c) Equal  d) 103 vs 132 kg
**Answer: b) 318 vs 162 kg**
