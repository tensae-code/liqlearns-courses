# Hands-on — Waste management — the hierarchy and e-waste

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Hierarchy staircase
Draw the five steps as a staircase, prevention at the top. Place ten household waste stories (a repaired chair, a refilled bottle, a composted peel, a buried battery, a recycled can, a bought-less week, a burnt tyre as the offender, a collected phone, a reused jar, a landfilled bag) on their steps and count which step your household uses most.

## Two-number audit
Write Bulgaria's and the EU's three numbers (generated, recycled, landfilled) as paired bars on paper. Underneath, compute the landfilled share of generated for each (318/460; 162/492) and state the hierarchy verdict in one sentence.
