## One side pays for both

The guide introduces the dative bond through an Ethiopian dinner analogy absent from the student book: person A has 200 birr, spends 40 birr sharing three doro wet with friends and has 160 birr left; the friend has nothing, so the only way they eat tibs together is for A to pay for both — one side contributing everything to the shared thing, exactly as one atom donates both electrons. Its worked coordinate formations go beyond the student text: NH₃→BF₃, NH₃ + H⁺, the Al₂Cl₆ dimer, and carbon monoxide explained electron by electron (oxygen donating a pair after the double bond leaves carbon two electrons short).
