Today: **Atomic radius — size trends**.

Atomic radius is half the distance between nuclei of two neighbouring atoms (in a metal network, or in a diatomic molecule such as iodine).

Trends: radius increases down a group (new shells outweigh rising nuclear charge — Li to Cs) and decreases across a period (same shell, stronger pull).

Worked ordering: N < P < Si, using group and period rules separately;

radius data are plotted against atomic number to show the saw-tooth periodicity.
