# Atomic radius — size trends — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** Trends: radius increases down a group (new shells outweigh rising _____ charge — Li to Cs) and decreases across a period (same shell, stronger pull).
**Answer: nuclear**

**2. ** Worked ordering: N < P < Si, using group and period rules _____;
**Answer: separately**

**3. ** radius data are plotted against atomic number to show the saw-tooth _____.
**Answer: periodicity**

**4. ** Atomic radius is half the distance between nuclei of two neighbouring atoms (in a metal _____, or in a diatomic molecule such as iodine).
**Answer: network**
