# Video script — Dalton revised — the modern atomic theory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dalton said atoms can never be split and that all atoms of one element have exactly the same mass.

Modern science keeps Dalton's frame but corrects three postulates.

Atoms are divisible: they are built from smaller subatomic particles.

Atoms of the same element can differ in mass — these are isotopes.

Nuclear change is different from chemical rearrangement: in nuclear change the atoms themselves change.

Now you. Say it back: Into what can atoms be divided, in the modern theory?
Answer: Subatomic particles.
