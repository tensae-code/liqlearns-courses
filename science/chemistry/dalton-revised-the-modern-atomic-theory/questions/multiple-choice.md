# Dalton revised — the modern atomic theory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Into what can atoms be divided, in the modern theory?
a) None  b) Crystalline  c) Subatomic particles  d) Solid → liquid → gas
**Answer: c) Subatomic particles**

**2. ** What are atoms of the same element with different masses called?
a) Three  b) Isotopes  c) Soaps  d) Only slightly soluble
**Answer: b) Isotopes**

**3. ** How does nuclear change differ from a chemical reaction?
a) The atoms themselves change  b) Anode to cathode  c) Electrochemical  d) Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets
**Answer: a) The atoms themselves change**

**4. ** How many of Dalton's postulates needed modern correction here?
a) Hazardous substances  b) Subatomic particles  c) Beryllium  d) Three
**Answer: d) Three**
