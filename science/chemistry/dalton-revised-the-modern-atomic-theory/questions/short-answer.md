# Dalton revised — the modern atomic theory — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Into what can atoms be divided, in the modern theory?
**Answer: Subatomic particles**

**2. ** What are atoms of the same element with different masses called?
**Answer: Isotopes**

**3. ** How does nuclear change differ from a chemical reaction?
**Answer: The atoms themselves change**

**4. ** How many of Dalton's postulates needed modern correction here?
**Answer: Three**
