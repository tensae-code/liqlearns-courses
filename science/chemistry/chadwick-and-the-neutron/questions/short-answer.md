# Chadwick and the neutron — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What did Chadwick discover?
**Answer: The neutron**

**2. ** What target did Chadwick bombard?
**Answer: Beryllium**

**3. ** How does the neutron's mass compare with the proton's?
**Answer: About the same**

**4. ** Why are neutrons hard to detect?
**Answer: Fields do not bend them — they carry no charge**
