# Chadwick and the neutron — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What did Chadwick discover?
a) Its C=C double bond(s)  b) Ethyl ethanoate  c) Flavours, perfumes or solvents  d) The neutron
**Answer: d) The neutron**

**2. ** What target did Chadwick bombard?
a) A cell potential depending on [H₃O⁺]  b) It regrows or refills  c) Beryllium  d) Purifies a metal at the cathode
**Answer: c) Beryllium**

**3. ** How does the neutron's mass compare with the proton's?
a) Alpha, beta and gamma  b) About the same  c) CO₂  d) Indivisible — cannot be cut
**Answer: b) About the same**

**4. ** Why are neutrons hard to detect?
a) Fields do not bend them — they carry no charge  b) Positive kernels in a lattice  c) The bonding MO  d) Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)
**Answer: a) Fields do not bend them — they carry no charge**
