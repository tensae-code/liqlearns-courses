# Video script — Chadwick and the neutron

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The nucleus holds protons, but protons alone cannot explain the nucleus's mass — there is mass there carrying no charge.

Chadwick bombarded beryllium and produced a radiation made of neutral particles.

Each neutral particle has about the same mass as a proton but no charge.

The neutron completes the nuclear picture: a nucleus holds protons and neutrons.

A neutral particle is hard to detect because electric and magnetic fields do not bend it.

Now you. Say it back: What did Chadwick discover?
Answer: The neutron.
