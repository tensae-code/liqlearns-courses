# Chadwick and the neutron

## Do it first
The nucleus holds protons, but protons alone cannot explain the nucleus's mass — there is mass there carrying no charge. What kind of particle would explain it, and how would you hunt for a particle you cannot see and cannot bend?

[Picture 29258 — Beryllium + alpha → neutrons out.]

Chadwick bombarded beryllium and produced a radiation made of neutral particles.

Each neutral particle has about the same mass as a proton but no charge. It is the neutron.

The neutron completes the nuclear picture: a nucleus holds protons and neutrons.

A neutral particle is hard to detect because electric and magnetic fields do not bend it.
