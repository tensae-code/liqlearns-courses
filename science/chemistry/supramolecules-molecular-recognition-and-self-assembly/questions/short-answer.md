# Supramolecules — molecular recognition and self-assembly — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing China's book teaches in this lesson — point 1 of the key ideas.
**Answer: Supramolecules: two or more molecules associated by intermolecular forces into an organised aggregate — molecular recognition (a host shaped for one guest, crown ethers sized to specific metal ions) and self-assembly as the working principles behind enzyme action and much of modern materials chemistry.**
