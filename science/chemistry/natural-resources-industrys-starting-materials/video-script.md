# Video script — Natural resources — industry's starting materials

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some raw materials regrow; some, once dug, are gone.

Renewable resources regrow or refill (crops, timber, water); non-renewable ones are finite (minerals, fossil fuels).

Ethiopia's resource base — agricultural produce, minerals, water power and more — is the starting stock of its industries.

Industry planning begins from which resources a country actually holds.

Now you. Say it back: What makes a resource renewable?
Answer: It regrows or refills.
