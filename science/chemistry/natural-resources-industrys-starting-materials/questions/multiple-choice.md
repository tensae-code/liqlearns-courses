# Natural resources — industry's starting materials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What makes a resource renewable?
a) Source, movement, changes, effects  b) Into water and food chains  c) Waste, at the source  d) It regrows or refills
**Answer: d) It regrows or refills**

**2. ** Give a non-renewable example.
a) London dispersion  b) End-to-end (axial) overlap  c) Minerals or fossil fuels  d) Molten or in solution — ions move
**Answer: c) Minerals or fossil fuels**

**3. ** What does Ethiopian industry start from?
a) Carbon dioxide, CO₂  b) Ethiopia's own resource base  c) A (symmetrical) ether  d) Carbon 1
**Answer: b) Ethiopia's own resource base**

**4. ** Give a renewable example.
a) Crops, timber or water  b) Exactly neutralise each other  c) So electrons lost equal electrons gained  d) Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q)
**Answer: a) Crops, timber or water**
