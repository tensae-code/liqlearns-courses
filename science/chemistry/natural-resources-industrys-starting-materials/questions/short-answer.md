# Natural resources — industry's starting materials — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What makes a resource renewable?
**Answer: It regrows or refills**

**2. ** Give a non-renewable example.
**Answer: Minerals or fossil fuels**

**3. ** What does Ethiopian industry start from?
**Answer: Ethiopia's own resource base**

**4. ** Give a renewable example.
**Answer: Crops, timber or water**
