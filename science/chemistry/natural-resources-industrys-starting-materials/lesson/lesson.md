# Natural resources — industry's starting materials

## Do it first
Some raw materials regrow; some, once dug, are gone. Before reading, sort Ethiopia's industry feedstocks under those two heads.

[Picture 21749 — Ethiopian resources mapped to industries.]

Renewable resources regrow or refill (crops, timber, water); non-renewable ones are finite (minerals, fossil fuels).

Ethiopia's resource base — agricultural produce, minerals, water power and more — is the starting stock of its industries.

Industry planning begins from which resources a country actually holds.

## Say it to your companion
Explain Natural resources — industry's starting materials to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.

## Do It Yourself
1. Write the lesson's key ideas in your own words, in a list, without opening the lesson.
2. Work one example of your own for each key idea — your own numbers, your own substances, the lesson's own rules.
3. Check your examples against the lesson. Fix anything that disagrees, and say what rule it broke.
4. Teach the hardest idea in the lesson to someone else, out loud, once.
