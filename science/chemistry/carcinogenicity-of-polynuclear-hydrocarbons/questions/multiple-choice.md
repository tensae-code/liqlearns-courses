# Carcinogenicity of polynuclear hydrocarbons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A polynuclear hydrocarbon is…
a) A long straight chain  b) Two or more benzene rings fused together in one flat molecule  c) A hydrocarbon with one triple bond  d) A ring of six carbons only
**Answer: b) Two or more benzene rings fused together in one flat molecule**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these does NCERT name in §13.6?
a) Methane  b) Ethyne  c) Benzo(a)pyrene  d) Ethanol
**Answer: c) Benzo(a)pyrene**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Polynuclear hydrocarbons are formed when organic matter…
a) Is distilled  b) Burns completely  c) Burns incompletely — as with tobacco, coal and petroleum  d) Is crystallised
**Answer: c) Burns incompletely — as with tobacco, coal and petroleum**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The body's main entry routes for these compounds are…
a) Through the skin only  b) Breathed in with smoke and swallowed with smoked and burnt food  c) By injection  d) They cannot enter the body
**Answer: b) Breathed in with smoke and swallowed with smoked and burnt food**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The cancer mechanism NCERT describes is…
a) The hydrocarbon burns the lungs directly  b) The body's biochemical reactions on the hydrocarbon produce products that damage DNA  c) The rings block blood vessels  d) The smoke starves cells of oxygen
**Answer: b) The body's biochemical reactions on the hydrocarbon produce products that damage DNA**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A substance that causes cancer in this way is called…
a) Catalytic  b) Aromatic  c) Carcinogenic  d) Homologous
**Answer: c) Carcinogenic**
