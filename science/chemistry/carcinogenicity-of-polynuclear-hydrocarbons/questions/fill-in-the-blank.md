# Carcinogenicity of polynuclear hydrocarbons — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Polynuclear hydrocarbons are built of benzene rings _____ together.
**Answer: fused**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) They are formed on the _____ combustion of tobacco, coal and petroleum.
**Answer: incomplete**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In the body their reaction products damage _____.
**Answer: DNA**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The best-known member, found in tobacco smoke and coal tar, is benzo(a)_____.
**Answer: pyrene**
