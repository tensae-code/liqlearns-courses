# The thermite reaction — welding with aluminium — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Thermite is a mixture of aluminium powder and…
a) Iron(III) oxide  b) Copper oxide  c) Sand  d) Sodium chloride
**Answer: a) Iron(III) oxide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Thermite is lit with…
a) A match  b) A magnesium ribbon  c) A spark plug  d) Sunlight
**Answer: b) A magnesium ribbon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The thermite reaction reaches about…
a) 350 °C  b) 1000 °C  c) 3500 °C  d) 660 °C
**Answer: c) 3500 °C**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The thermite equation's iron product is…
a) Iron(III) oxide  b) Iron powder, cold  c) Steel wire  d) Molten iron, born liquid
**Answer: d) Molten iron, born liquid**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Aluminium wins manganese from MnO₂ because aluminium…
a) Binds oxygen more strongly  b) Melts lower  c) Is cheaper  d) Dissolves the oxide
**Answer: a) Binds oxygen more strongly**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In 3MnO₂ + 4Al → 3Mn + 2Al₂O₃, the reducing agent is…
a) MnO₂  b) Aluminium  c) Manganese  d) Al₂O₃
**Answer: b) Aluminium**
