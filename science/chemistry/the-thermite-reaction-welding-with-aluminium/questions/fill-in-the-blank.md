# The thermite reaction — welding with aluminium — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Thermite is aluminium powder mixed with iron(III) _____.
**Answer: oxide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The mixture is lit with a _____ ribbon.
**Answer: magnesium**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The reaction reaches about _____ °C.
**Answer: 3500**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Thermite's great work is welding _____ joints.
**Answer: rail**
