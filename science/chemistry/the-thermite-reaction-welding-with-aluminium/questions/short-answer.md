# The thermite reaction — welding with aluminium — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the thermite equation and state what each side's key substance does.
**Answer: Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → 2Fe + Al₂O₃. The aluminium takes the oxide's oxygen (oxidised, the reducing agent); the iron(III) oxide loses it (reduced), leaving molten iron.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the book's other example of aluminium as a reducing agent, as an equation.
**Answer: 3MnO₂ + 4Al → 3Mn + 2Al₂O₃ — aluminium wins manganese from its oxide.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the weld form exactly at the joint?
**Answer: The iron is produced molten at about 3500 °C inside the crucible set over the rail joint; it runs into the joint and freezes there, joining the rails as one piece.**
