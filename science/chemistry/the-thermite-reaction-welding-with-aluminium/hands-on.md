# Hands-on — The thermite reaction — welding with aluminium

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Oxygen tug-of-war (paper only)
Draw the thermite reaction as a tug-of-war over oxygen: Fe₂O₃ holding oxygen on one side, aluminium pulling on the other. Under the drawing, write the equation and label which side wins and what the prize's heat does. Then do the same, smaller, for the manganese example.

## Plan the weld (paper only)
List everything the rail gang must carry to a broken rail in open country, from the lesson alone: the mixture, its container, its lighter, and the one number that says the weld will work. Beside each item, write its job.
