# Diazonium salts — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Aniline with NaNO₂/HCl in the cold undergoes _____.
**Answer: diazotisation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In every replacement reaction the diazo group leaves as _____ gas.
**Answer: nitrogen**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The copper-catalysed replacement of the diazo group by Cl, Br or CN is the _____ reaction.
**Answer: Sandmeyer**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Coupling joins the rings through an –_____=N– link.
**Answer: N**
