# Diazonium salts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Diazotisation of aniline requires…
a) NaNO₂/HCl at 273–278 K  b) Refluxing with concentrated acid  c) CuCN/KCN  d) Light and chlorine
**Answer: a) NaNO₂/HCl at 273–278 K**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Alkyl diazonium salts cannot be used like aryl ones because…
a) Alkyl amines do not react with nitrous acid  b) They decompose at once — no ring resonance steadies them — losing N₂ to give the alcohol  c) They are too stable  d) They are coloured
**Answer: b) They decompose at once — no ring resonance steadies them — losing N₂ to give the alcohol**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Sandmeyer preparation of chlorobenzene uses…
a) KI, warmed  b) CuCl in HCl  c) HBF₄ then heat  d) H₃PO₂
**Answer: b) CuCl in HCl**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Warming benzenediazonium chloride in water gives…
a) Benzene  b) Chlorobenzene  c) Phenol, with nitrogen evolved  d) Aniline back
**Answer: c) Phenol, with nitrogen evolved**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Fluorobenzene is made from the diazonium salt by…
a) The Balz–Schiemann route — precipitating the fluoroborate and heating the dry salt  b) Sandmeyer with CuF  c) Coupling  d) Reduction
**Answer: a) The Balz–Schiemann route — precipitating the fluoroborate and heating the dry salt**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) p-Hydroxyazobenzene forms when benzenediazonium chloride couples with…
a) Aniline in acid  b) Phenol in mildly alkaline solution — an orange azo dye  c) Benzene  d) CuCN
**Answer: b) Phenol in mildly alkaline solution — an orange azo dye**
