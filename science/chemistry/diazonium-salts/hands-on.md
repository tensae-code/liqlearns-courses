# Hands-on — Diazonium salts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Route planner (paper only)
Five targets are named: chlorobenzene, bromobenzene, benzonitrile, iodobenzene, phenol — all from aniline. For each, write the diazotisation step once, then the replacement step with its reagent and conditions from the lesson, and mark which routes are Sandmeyer reactions.

## Dye detective at home (kitchen materials only)
With an adult, find three coloured fabrics or printed items at home whose colour is strong and even (a dyed scarf, a printed t-shirt, a coloured paper). Azo dyes are the largest class of synthetic dyes. Record the items and their colours. Say, from sub lesson 3, what structural feature — the –N=N– link between two rings — you would expect the dye molecules of at least the synthetic-coloured items to share.

## Cold rule (paper only)
A student prepares benzenediazonium chloride at room temperature on a warm day and leaves the flask while fetching phenol for a coupling. Write the two things that go wrong, naming the gas evolved and the product the salt becomes in water.
