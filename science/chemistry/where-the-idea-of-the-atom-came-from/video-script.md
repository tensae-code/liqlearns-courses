# Video script — Where the idea of the atom came from

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Take a piece of chalk or a lump of clay.

Long ago, Greek thinkers argued about matter.

Democritus argued the opposite: if you keep dividing, you reach a piece that cannot be divided at all.

The whole of atomic science starts from that question: is there a smallest piece of matter, and can we ever see it?.

Now you. Say it back: What did Democritus call the indivisible piece of matter?
Answer: atomos.
