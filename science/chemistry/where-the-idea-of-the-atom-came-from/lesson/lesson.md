# Where the idea of the atom came from

## Do it first
Take a piece of chalk or a lump of clay. Break it in half. Break one half again. Keep going in your mind — could you break it forever, or would you reach a piece too small to break?

[Picture 29250 — Keep dividing — until one piece cannot be divided.]

Long ago, Greek thinkers argued about matter. Plato and Aristotle held that matter is continuous — it can be divided again and again without end.

Democritus argued the opposite: if you keep dividing, you reach a piece that cannot be divided at all. He called it atomos — the indivisible.

The whole of atomic science starts from that question: is there a smallest piece of matter, and can we ever see it?
