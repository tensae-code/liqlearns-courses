# Where the idea of the atom came from — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What did Democritus call the indivisible piece of matter?
a) atomos  b) The main energy level (and orbital size)  c) Periods  d) Predict its properties
**Answer: a) atomos**

**2. ** Who held that matter is continuous and endlessly divisible?
a) How far the equilibrium lies toward products  b) Unreacted gases  c) The longest chain carrying –OH  d) Plato and Aristotle
**Answer: d) Plato and Aristotle**

**3. ** What question opens atomic science?
a) 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴  b) Henderson–Hasselbalch  c) Whether there is a smallest piece of matter  d) H⁺ ions
**Answer: c) Whether there is a smallest piece of matter**

**4. ** What does the word atomos mean?
a) Mechanical or chemical  b) Indivisible — cannot be cut  c) Ammonia  d) Firing in a kiln
**Answer: b) Indivisible — cannot be cut**
