# Where the idea of the atom came from — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What did Democritus call the indivisible piece of matter?
**Answer: atomos**

**2. ** Who held that matter is continuous and endlessly divisible?
**Answer: Plato and Aristotle**

**3. ** What question opens atomic science?
**Answer: Whether there is a smallest piece of matter**

**4. ** What does the word atomos mean?
**Answer: Indivisible — cannot be cut**
