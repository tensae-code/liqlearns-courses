# Superacids — stronger than pure sulfuric acid — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An acid stronger than pure sulfuric acid is called a _____.
**Answer: superacid**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The scale used to measure acidity beyond pure sulfuric acid is the Hammett acidity function, written _____.
**Answer: H₀**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The superacid mixture of fluorosulfuric acid and antimony pentafluoride is called _____ acid.
**Answer: magic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In industry, superacids are used as powerful _____ — substances that speed reactions without being used up.
**Answer: catalysts**
