# Standard potentials — ranking the push — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is the reference electrode?
a) The standard hydrogen electrode (SHE)  b) Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly  c) PV = nRT  d) It accepts a proton from water
**Answer: a) The standard hydrogen electrode (SHE)**

**2. ** What E° is the SHE assigned?
a) Iron  b) It would form a corrosive mist  c) Soap or paper making  d) 0.00 V
**Answer: d) 0.00 V**

**3. ** Write the EMF relation.
a) The light's frequency  b) Multi-electron spectra  c) EMF = E°cathode − E°anode  d) In the nucleus
**Answer: c) EMF = E°cathode − E°anode**

**4. ** Positive E°cell means the reaction is…
a) Yes, to reactants  b) Spontaneous as written  c) At constant T, P × V is constant  d) Glass
**Answer: b) Spontaneous as written**
