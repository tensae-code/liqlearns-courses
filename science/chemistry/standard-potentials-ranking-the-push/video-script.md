# Video script — Standard potentials — ranking the push

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every half-reaction has a measurable 'push'.

The standard hydrogen electrode (SHE) is the reference electrode, assigned E° = 0.

Standard electrode potentials E° tabulate each half-reaction as a reduction; more positive means a stronger pull to be reduced.

Cell EMF = E°cathode − E°anode.

Now you. Say it back: What is the reference electrode?
Answer: The standard hydrogen electrode (SHE).
