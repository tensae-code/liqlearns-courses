# Bond parameters — energy, length and angle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing China's book teaches in this lesson — point 1 of the key ideas.
**Answer: A bond measured three ways: bond energy (kJ/mol to break 1 mol of the bond into gas atoms; average values, e.g. C—H, tabulated — and used to estimate reaction heats), bond length (nuclear distance; C—C 154 pm > C=C 133 pm > C≡C 120 pm as energy rises), and bond angle (proving bonds are directional; values from X-ray diffraction).**
