# Lesson plan — Bond parameters — energy, length and angle

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Bond parameters — energy, length and angle
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A bond measured three ways: bond energy (kJ/mol to break 1 mol of the bond into gas atoms; average values, e.g. C—H, tabulated — and used to estimate reaction heats), bond length (nuclear distance; C—C 154 pm > C=C 133 pm > C≡C 120 pm as energy rises), and bond angle (proving bonds are directional; values from X-ray diffraction).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
