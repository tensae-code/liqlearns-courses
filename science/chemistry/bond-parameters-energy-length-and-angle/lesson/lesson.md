# Bond parameters — energy, length and angle

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 2 §1 — China's book (China, Senior High (Selective Compulsory 2 — Structure and Properties of Matter))

A bond measured three ways: bond energy (kJ/mol to break 1 mol of the bond into gas atoms; average values, e.g. C—H, tabulated — and used to estimate reaction heats), bond length (nuclear distance; C—C 154 pm > C=C 133 pm > C≡C 120 pm as energy rises), and bond angle (proving bonds are directional; values from X-ray diffraction).
