## Oxidation numbers

Oxidation (state) numbers are assigned by a rule set (free elements zero; monatomic ions equal to their charge; oxygen usually −2, hydrogen usually +1, with their standard exceptions;

the sum over a neutral compound zero, over an ion equal to its charge), and pupils practise on species up to permanganate (Mn in KMnO₄ is a review target). Oxidation is then defined two ways that must agree: loss of electrons, or increase in oxidation number; reduction is gain of electrons, or decrease in oxidation number.
