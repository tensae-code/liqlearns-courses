Today: **Oxidation numbers**.

Oxidation (state) numbers are assigned by a rule set (free elements zero;

monatomic ions equal to their charge;

oxygen usually −2, hydrogen usually +1, with their standard exceptions;

the sum over a neutral compound zero, over an ion equal to its charge), and pupils practise on species up to permanganate (Mn in KMnO₄ is a review target).

Oxidation is then defined two ways that must agree: loss of electrons, or increase in oxidation number;

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
