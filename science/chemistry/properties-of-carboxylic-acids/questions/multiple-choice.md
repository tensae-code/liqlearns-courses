# Properties of carboxylic acids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is a dimer?
a) Two acid molecules paired by hydrogen bonds  b) Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics)  c) Mid-chain, between two carbons  d) Acetic acid (ethanoic acid)
**Answer: a) Two acid molecules paired by hydrogen bonds**

**2. ** How many hydrogen bonds hold a dimer?
a) A pH range of about two units  b) Takes electrons (is itself reduced)  c) Yes — it is one of the three factors  d) Two
**Answer: d) Two**

**3. ** Why are acid boiling points high?
a) The atmosphere  b) Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms  c) Dimer hydrogen bonding  d) The monomer units in the chain
**Answer: c) Dimer hydrogen bonding**

**4. ** How does solubility change with chain length?
a) Electron pairs repel and spread apart  b) It falls  c) To become more stable  d) Removing electrons from atoms
**Answer: b) It falls**
