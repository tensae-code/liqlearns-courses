# Properties of carboxylic acids

## Do it first
Ethanoic acid boils higher than even alcohols of its size, and the first acids smell sharp and taste sour. Before reading, picture two acid molecules holding each other with both hands — what is that pair called?

[Picture 21695 — Two acid molecules paired as a dimer.]

Carboxylic acids hydrogen-bond in pairs — dimers — two molecules held by two hydrogen bonds, which raises boiling points high.

Short-chain acids mix with water; solubility falls as the chain grows.

The sour tastes and sharp odours of the small acids (vinegar) are the family's everyday face.
