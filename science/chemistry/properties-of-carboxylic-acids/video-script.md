# Video script — Properties of carboxylic acids

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethanoic acid boils higher than even alcohols of its size, and the first acids smell sharp and taste sour.

Carboxylic acids hydrogen-bond in pairs — dimers — two molecules held by two hydrogen bonds, which raises boiling points high.

Short-chain acids mix with water; solubility falls as the chain grows.

The sour tastes and sharp odours of the small acids (vinegar) are the family's everyday face.

Now you. Say it back: What is a dimer?
Answer: Two acid molecules paired by hydrogen bonds.
