# Thermochemistry in daily life — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An _____ change gives heat out to its surroundings.
**Answer: exothermic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A cold pack runs an _____ change, absorbing heat from what it touches.
**Answer: endothermic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A self-heating can needs no stove because the _____ reaction is carried inside the can.
**Answer: exothermic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Cooking fuel is chosen for its steady exothermic _____.
**Answer: combustion**
