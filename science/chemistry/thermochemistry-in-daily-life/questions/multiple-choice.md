# Thermochemistry in daily life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An endothermic change…
a) Gives heat out to its surroundings  b) Takes heat in from its surroundings, which cool down  c) Always involves burning  d) Cannot happen in a bag
**Answer: b) Takes heat in from its surroundings, which cool down**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Malaysia closes its heat chapter by…
a) Repeating the definitions  b) Applying exothermic and endothermic reactions to daily life  c) Testing the chapter with an Achievement Test only  d) Moving to polymers
**Answer: b) Applying exothermic and endothermic reactions to daily life**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A cold pack turns cold because…
a) Cold was stored inside it in a factory  b) Its change is endothermic — it absorbs heat from what it touches  c) It contains ice that never melts  d) Air escapes from it
**Answer: b) Its change is endothermic — it absorbs heat from what it touches**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A hot pack warms your hands because its change is…
a) Endothermic  b) Neutral  c) Exothermic — it gives heat out while it runs  d) Frozen
**Answer: c) Exothermic — it gives heat out while it runs**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A self-heating can heats its food by…
a) A battery inside the lid  b) An exothermic reaction kept apart from the food until the can is activated  c) Sunlight through the metal  d) Friction from shaking
**Answer: b) An exothermic reaction kept apart from the food until the can is activated**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Cooking fuel is chosen because its combustion is…
a) Endothermic  b) Exothermic — giving heat out steadily as it burns  c) Without any heat change  d) Too weak to measure
**Answer: b) Exothermic — giving heat out steadily as it burns**
