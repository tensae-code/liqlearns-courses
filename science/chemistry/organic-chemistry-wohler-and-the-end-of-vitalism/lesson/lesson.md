## Organic chemistry

The vitalism theory held that living things possess a special life force needed to make organic compounds, so they could never be made artificially; Wöhler's 1828 laboratory synthesis of urea (a constituent of urine) from inorganic material disproved it. Organic chemistry is then defined as the study of carbon compounds, natural and synthetic.
