# Phosphorus — white and red — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** Its two common allotropes are contrasted: white _____ — highly reactive, stored under water;
**Answer: phosphorus**

**2. ** red phosphorus — _____ stable.
**Answer: relatively**

**3. ** It reacts with oxygen and with _____.
**Answer: chlorine**

**4. ** Phosphorus is manufactured by heating rock _____ with coke and silica in an electric furnace.
**Answer: phosphate**
