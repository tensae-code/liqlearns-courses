## Phosphorus

Phosphorus is manufactured by heating rock phosphate with coke and silica in an electric furnace. Its two common allotropes are contrasted: white phosphorus — highly reactive, stored under water; red phosphorus — relatively stable.

It reacts with oxygen and with chlorine.3.2).
