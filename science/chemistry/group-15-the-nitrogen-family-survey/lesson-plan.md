# Lesson plan — Group 15 — the nitrogen family survey

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Group 15 — the nitrogen family survey
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The fifth main group (VA) surveyed as Yemen's Grade 11 book surveys it: N, P, As, Sb, Bi with configurations ns2np3, existing as molecules N2, P4, As4 and Sb4 with bismuth a crystalline solid metal, several members showing two allotropic forms while nitrogen has none, their hydrides running NH3 and PH3 (bismuth's falling apart to the element), and their oxides in +3 and +5 forms whose acidity fades from nitrogen down to bismuth
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Representative elements and family names is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
