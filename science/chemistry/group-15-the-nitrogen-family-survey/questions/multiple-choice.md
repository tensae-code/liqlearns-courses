# Group 15 — the nitrogen family survey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Group 15's configuration pattern is…
a) ns²np³  b) ns²np¹  c) ns²np⁴  d) ns¹
**Answer: a) ns²np³**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Phosphorus exists as the molecule…
a) P₂  b) P₄  c) P  d) P₈
**Answer: b) P₄**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The family's crystalline solid metal is…
a) Nitrogen  b) Phosphorus  c) Bismuth  d) Arsenic
**Answer: c) Bismuth**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The member with NO allotropic form is…
a) Phosphorus  b) Antimony  c) Arsenic  d) Nitrogen
**Answer: d) Nitrogen**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The hydride that falls apart to its element is…
a) Bismuth's  b) Ammonia  c) Phosphine  d) Arsenic's
**Answer: a) Bismuth's**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Down the family, the oxides' acidity…
a) Grows  b) Fades  c) Turns to alkalinity at the head  d) Stays equal
**Answer: b) Fades**
