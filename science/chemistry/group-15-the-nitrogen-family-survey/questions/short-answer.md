# Group 15 — the nitrogen family survey — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) List the members with the molecules four of them form.
**Answer: N (N₂), P (P₄), As (As₄), Sb (Sb₄); Bi is a crystalline solid metal, not a grouped molecule.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the family's two compound series and their trends.
**Answer: Hydrides: NH₃ and PH₃, with bismuth's too unstable to keep. Oxides: +3 and +5 forms across the family, acidity fading from nitrogen down to bismuth.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does allotropy appear in this family, and who is the exception?
**Answer: Several members show two allotropic forms — one lighter and transparent, growing more stable down the group. Nitrogen is the exception: it has no allotropic form.**
