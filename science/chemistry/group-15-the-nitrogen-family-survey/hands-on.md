# Hands-on — Group 15 — the nitrogen family survey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Family portrait (paper only)
Draw the family's portrait as a staircase of five steps, N at the top, Bi at the bottom. On each step write: its existence (molecule or metal), one hydride or oxide fact, and whether its character leans nonmetal or metal. The staircase must show the slide the survey teaches.

## Two series table (paper only)
Make the family's two-series table: rows for hydrides and oxides, columns for the members. Fill every cell the lesson fills, and mark with a dash the cell the lesson says cannot exist (bismuth's hydride) — with its reason written under the table.
