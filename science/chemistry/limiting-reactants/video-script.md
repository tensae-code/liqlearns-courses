Today: **Limiting reactants**.

When reactants are mixed in non-ratio amounts, the one used up first — the limiting reactant — caps the product;

the others are in excess and partly left over.

Identification routine (convert both to product amounts;

the smaller wins) and full worked problems: 25 g NH₃ with 4 mol O₂ → limiting reactant, mass of NO and mass of H₂O formed, are the review's model questions.
