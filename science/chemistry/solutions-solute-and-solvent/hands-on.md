# Hands-on — Solutions — solute and solvent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch it vanish (kitchen-safe)
Stir a spoon of sugar into a glass of water until no grain can be seen. Hold the glass to the light and write: is the mixture homogeneous by the lesson's test? Name the solute and solvent, and the word for this kind of solution.

## Find the fence (kitchen-safe, teacher or family present)
Keep adding sugar spoon by spoon to half a glass of water, stirring fully each time, and count the spoons until grains remain undissolved after long stirring. Record the count and name the state of the solution at that point, in the book's term.
