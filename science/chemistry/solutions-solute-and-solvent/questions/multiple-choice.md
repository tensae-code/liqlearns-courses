# Solutions — solute and solvent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solution is a _____ mixture made by dissolving.
a) Heterogeneous  b) Homogeneous  c) Solid  d) Temporary
**Answer: b) Homogeneous**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The substance that dissolves is the…
a) Solvent  b) Solute  c) Residue  d) Filtrat
**Answer: b) Solute**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An aqueous solution is one whose solvent is…
a) Alcohol  b) Oil  c) Water  d) Air
**Answer: c) Water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Liquid-in-liquid dissolving is bounded by…
a) Solubility  b) Miscibility  c) Boiling  d) Colour
**Answer: b) Miscibility**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Past the solubility limit, a solution is…
a) Aqueous  b) Dilute  c) Saturated  d) Impossible
**Answer: c) Saturated**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In a true solution, every part is…
a) Different  b) Like every other part  c) Settled  d) Coloured
**Answer: b) Like every other part**
