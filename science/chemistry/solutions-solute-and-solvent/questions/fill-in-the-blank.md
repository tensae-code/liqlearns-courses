# Solutions — solute and solvent — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solution is a _____ mixture.
**Answer: homogeneous**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The substance dissolved is the _____.
**Answer: solute**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) When water is the solvent, the solution is _____.
**Answer: aqueous**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Solid-in-liquid dissolving stops at the solute's _____.
**Answer: solubility**
