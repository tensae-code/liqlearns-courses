# Solutions — solute and solvent — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define a solution with its two roles named.
**Answer: A homogeneous mixture made by dissolving one or more solutes (what dissolves) in a solvent (what it dissolves into).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What makes a solution aqueous?
**Answer: Its solvent is water.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the two fences on dissolving and their pairs.
**Answer: Miscibility bounds liquid dissolving in liquid; solubility bounds solid dissolving in liquid — past the solubility limit the solution is saturated.**
