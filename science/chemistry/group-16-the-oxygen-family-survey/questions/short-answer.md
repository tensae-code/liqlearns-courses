# Group 16 — the oxygen family survey — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) List the family in order with its configuration.
**Answer: O, S, Se, Te, Po and element 116 — ns²np⁴.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the survey's four reaction partners with one example each.
**Answer: Metals (2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO or Mg + S → MgS); oxygen itself (SeO₂, SO₃); halogens (SCl₂); hydrogen (H₂O, H₂S).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Match each use to its element: sulfuric acid and vulcanisation; photocells; rust-proof coatings.
**Answer: Sulfur; selenium; tellurium.**
