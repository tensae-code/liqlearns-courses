# Group 16 — the oxygen family survey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Group 16's configuration pattern is…
a) ns²np⁴  b) ns²np³  c) ns²np⁶  d) ns²np¹
**Answer: a) ns²np⁴**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Hydrogen bonding among the family's hydrides exists in…
a) H₂S only  b) H₂O only  c) All of them equally  d) None of them
**Answer: b) H₂O only**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Oxygen's anomaly begins with its high…
a) Melting point  b) Atomic mass  c) Electronegativity  d) Colour
**Answer: c) Electronegativity**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Sulfur's uses in the survey do NOT include…
a) Sulfuric acid  b) Rubber vulcanisation  c) Dyes  d) Making nitric acid
**Answer: d) Making nitric acid**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Selenium's working property is that its conductivity…
a) Rises with light  b) Falls with light  c) Vanishes when warm  d) Becomes infinite in the dark
**Answer: a) Rises with light**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Polonium was named by Marie Curie for…
a) Her laboratory  b) Her homeland, Poland  c) Her husband  d) The city of Paris
**Answer: b) Her homeland, Poland**
