# Hands-on — Group 16 — the oxygen family survey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Anomaly card (paper only)
Make the family's anomaly card: two boxes — 'oxygen can, the rest cannot' (ionic compounds with some metals) and 'H₂O has, the rest have not' (hydrogen bonding). Under each, write the property of oxygen that the book blames or credits. The card leads the survey, as the book leads with it.

## Uses index (paper only)
Index the family's work: for S, Se and Te, write the element, its working property in the lesson's words, and its uses. Finish with polonium's line — no use, but a name and its story in one sentence.
