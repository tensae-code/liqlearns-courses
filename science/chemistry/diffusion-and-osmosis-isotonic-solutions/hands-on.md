# Hands-on — Diffusion and osmosis — isotonic solutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Cell diary
Draw the red blood cell three times — in isotonic, hypotonic and hypertonic solution — with arrows showing the net water movement in each. Label each drawing with Bulgaria's outcome word: unchanged, bursts, shrivels.

## Two movements audit
For each scene, write DIFFUSION or OSMOSIS and name what moves: perfume crossing a room; a layered coloured solution mixing overnight; dried raisins swelling in tea; roots taking up soil water; sugar spreading through stirred tea.
