Today: **Diffusion and osmosis — isotonic solutions** — Bulgaria's two movements. Diffusion: particles spread from crowded to empty until all is uniform. Osmosis: a membrane lets only the solvent through, so water climbs toward the concentrated side — and the climb measures osmotic pressure. Match blood at 0.9% saline and cells rest; weaker, they burst; stronger, they shrivel.
