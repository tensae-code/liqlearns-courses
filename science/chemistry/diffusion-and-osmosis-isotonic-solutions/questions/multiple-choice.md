# Diffusion and osmosis — isotonic solutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Diffusion moves particles…
a) From low to high concentration  b) From high to low concentration until uniform  c) Only through membranes  d) Only when heated
**Answer: b) From high to low concentration until uniform**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A semipermeable membrane passes…
a) All particles  b) Only the solute  c) Only the solvent  d) Only gases
**Answer: c) Only the solvent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Osmotic pressure depends on temperature and…
a) The solute's colour  b) Particle count  c) The solute's name  d) The container's shape
**Answer: b) Particle count**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Saline isotonic with blood is…
a) 0.09%  b) 0.9%  c) 9%  d) 5%
**Answer: b) 0.9%**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In a hypertonic solution a red blood cell…
a) Bursts  b) Swells  c) Shrivels  d) Divides
**Answer: c) Shrivels**
