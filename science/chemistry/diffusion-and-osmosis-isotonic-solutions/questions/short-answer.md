# Diffusion and osmosis — isotonic solutions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What stops the osmotic rise, and what does its height measure?
**Answer: The column's own pressure stops the net flow; its height measures the osmotic pressure Π of the concentrated solution.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Define isotonic, hypotonic and hypertonic by the cell test.
**Answer: Isotonic matches blood's osmotic pressure (cells unchanged); hypotonic is weaker (cells swell and burst); hypertonic is stronger (cells shrivel).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the plant example of osmosis from the lesson.
**Answer: Roots draw soil water by osmosis — solvent crossing membranes toward the more concentrated cell fluids.**
