# Diffusion and osmosis — isotonic solutions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Solvent crossing a semipermeable membrane toward the concentrated side is called _____.
**Answer: osmosis**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The saline strength isotonic with blood is _____ percent.
**Answer: 0.9**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a _____ solution red blood cells swell and burst.
**Answer: hypotonic**
