# Lesson plan — Diffusion and osmosis — isotonic solutions

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Diffusion and osmosis — isotonic solutions

## Sub lessons
1. **Diffusion, and osmosis through a membrane** — **Diffusion** is particle movement **from higher to lower concentration until uniform** — Bulgaria's layered coloured solution left to mix; it runs **faster warm and stirred**. **Osmosis** is diffusion with a gatekeeper: a **semipermeable membrane passes only the solvent**, so the net flow runs **toward the concentrated side** and raises its level. The height of the stopping column measures the **osmotic pressure Π**, which depends on **temperature and particle count — not on the solute's nature**.
2. **Isotonic solutions and the body** — A solution matching the body's own osmotic pressure is **isotonic**: **0.9% saline is isotonic with blood**. In a **hypotonic** (weaker) solution red blood cells **swell and burst**; in a **hypertonic** (stronger) one they **shrivel** — Bulgaria draws the cells one by one. The same osmosis swells dried fruits in water and lets plant roots draw soil water.
