# Organic compounds and carbon's four bonds — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon shares _____ bonds to complete its octet.
**Answer: four**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The models built are ethane, ethene and _____.
**Answer: ethyne**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Ethene's carbon–carbon bond is a _____ bond.
**Answer: double**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon's outer electrons are drawn in a _____ picture.
**Answer: Lewis**
