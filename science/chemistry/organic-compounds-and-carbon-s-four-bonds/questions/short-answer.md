# Organic compounds and carbon's four bonds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is carbon halfway-house sharing its natural move?
**Answer: Carbon has four outer electrons — exactly half of a full outer level of eight — and sits small in the second main level, so it shares readily and completes its octet by sharing four bonds.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the three carbon–carbon bond kinds with their model molecules.
**Answer: Single — one shared pair (ethane); double — two shared pairs (ethene); triple — three shared pairs (ethyne), built and compared as ball-and-stick models and concluded on printed p. 151.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What does carbon's four-bond rule make possible?
**Answer: Backbone chains: carbon bonded to carbon to carbon, each holding four bonds — the skeleton every organic family in the unit is built on.**
