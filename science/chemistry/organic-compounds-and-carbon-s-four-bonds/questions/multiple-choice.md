# Organic compounds and carbon's four bonds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon's outer electrons number…
a) 2  b) 4  c) 6  d) 8
**Answer: b) 4**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Carbon sits in which main level?
a) First  b) Second  c) Third  d) Seventh
**Answer: b) Second**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Carbon completes its octet by…
a) Losing four electrons  b) Gaining four electrons  c) Sharing four bonds  d) Splitting its nucleus
**Answer: c) Sharing four bonds**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Ethane's carbon–carbon bond is…
a) Single  b) Double  c) Triple  d) Ionic
**Answer: a) Single**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Ethyne's carbon–carbon bond is…
a) Single  b) Double  c) Triple  d) Metallic
**Answer: c) Triple**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A double bond holds how many shared pairs?
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3  d) 4
**Answer: b) 2**
