Today: **Organic compounds and carbon's four bonds**.

Four outer electrons, halfway to eight — so carbon shares, four bonds at once, and completes its octet. Join two carbons with one pair, two pairs, three pairs and you have ethane, ethene, ethyne: the single, double and triple bonds whole families are sorted by.

Four bonds is carbon's whole building licence.
