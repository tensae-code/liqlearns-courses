# Hands-on — Organic compounds and carbon's four bonds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the three models (hands-on — balls and sticks, or drawn)
Build or draw ball-and-stick models of ethane, ethene and ethyne as the book does. Between the carbons use one stick, two sticks, three sticks. Write under each: molecule name, bond kind, pairs shared.

## Four bonds audit (paper exercise)
Take methane, ethane and ethene. For every carbon in each, count its bonds and check the total is four. Any carbon short of four is a drawing error — find and fix it.
