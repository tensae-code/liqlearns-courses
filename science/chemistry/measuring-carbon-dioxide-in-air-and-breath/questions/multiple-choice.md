# Measuring carbon dioxide in air and breath — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The absorbent in the flask is…
a) 6M sodium hydroxide, sealed under mineral oil  b) Limewater  c) Distilled water  d) Sulfuric acid
**Answer: a) 6M sodium hydroxide, sealed under mineral oil**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) As CO₂ is absorbed, tube B's liquid level…
a) Falls  b) Rises, until it stops  c) Boils  d) Changes colour
**Answer: b) Rises, until it stops**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The flask is shaken held by the neck so that…
a) It does not break  b) The oil mixes in  c) The hand's heat does not falsify the reading  d) The teacher can see it
**Answer: c) The hand's heat does not falsify the reading**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Atmospheric pressure is taken in the calculation as…
a) 100 mmHg  b) 1000 mmHg  c) 76 mmHg  d) 760 mmHg
**Answer: d) 760 mmHg**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Air's CO₂ percentage comes out at about…
a) 0.03%  b) 3%  c) 0.3%  d) 21%
**Answer: a) 0.03%**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In Activity 14, breath is blown through tube A…
a) Once, hard  b) Six to eight times, with the cheek  c) Through a pump  d) After running
**Answer: b) Six to eight times, with the cheek**
