# Measuring carbon dioxide in air and breath — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the percentage formula and the absorption reaction as printed.
**Answer: %CO₂ = (mm Hg of CO₂ ÷ atmospheric mm Hg) × 100, with atmospheric pressure 760 mmHg at sea level. Absorption: 2NaOH + CO₂ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O [teacher evidence].**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the two handling rules of the measurement and the reason for each.
**Answer: Seal the alkali under mineral oil until measuring — so it absorbs none of the room's CO₂ early. Hold the flask by the neck while shaking — so hand heat does not change the pressure and falsify the level [teacher evidence].**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does Activity 14 differ from Activity 13, and what does the class do with the pair?
**Answer: Activity 14 measures exhaled breath (six to eight cheek-blown breaths through tube A) where 13 measures air; the class then compares breath against air [teacher evidence].**
