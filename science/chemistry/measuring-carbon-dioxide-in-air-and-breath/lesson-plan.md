# Lesson plan — Measuring carbon dioxide in air and breath

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Measuring carbon dioxide in air and breath
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Carbon dioxide measured volumetrically with a drawn two-tube manometer apparatus, written only in Yemen's Grade 12 teacher guide [teacher evidence]: air drawn into a conical flask over a small beaker of 6M sodium hydroxide sealed under mineral oil, the flask shaken by hand — held by the neck so the hand's heat does not falsify the reading — until the liquid level in tube B stops rising, and the CO2 percentage computed from partial pressure against 760 mmHg, matching the known ~0.03% (2NaOH + CO2 -> Na2CO3 + H2O); Activity 14 repeats the measurement on exhaled breath, blown through tube A six to eight times with the cheek, and the class compares breath against air
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Carbon dioxide — properties, preparation and tests is recommended first, never forced.
