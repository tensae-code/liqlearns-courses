# Hands-on — Measuring carbon dioxide in air and breath

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Apparatus drawing (paper only)
Draw the guide's apparatus in section: conical flask, the small beaker of alkali under its oil layer inside, tubes A and B with their liquid levels. Mark tube B twice — before and after absorption — and write the one sentence that explains the level's rise. Beside the flask, draw the hand holding it by the neck, with its reason captioned.

## The two readings (paper only)
Write the comparison table the class ends with: rows for air and breath, columns for the level's rise, the computed percentage, and the source of the CO₂. Air's row carries the guide's ≈ 0.03%; breath's row is written as the comparison the activity demands — higher, and by the body's own production.
