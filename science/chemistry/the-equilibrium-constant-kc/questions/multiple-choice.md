# The equilibrium constant Kc — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does K's size tell us?
a) Fields do not bend them — they carry no charge  b) E = hν  c) How far the equilibrium lies toward products  d) The atoms themselves change
**Answer: c) How far the equilibrium lies toward products**

**2. ** What is left out of Kc expressions?
a) Crystalline  b) Pure solids and liquids  c) Solid → liquid → gas  d) None
**Answer: b) Pure solids and liquids**

**3. ** What law gives the equilibrium expression?
a) The law of mass action  b) Soaps  c) Only slightly soluble  d) Three
**Answer: a) The law of mass action**

**4. ** What does Kp use instead of concentration?
a) Anode to cathode  b) Electrochemical  c) Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets  d) Partial pressures
**Answer: d) Partial pressures**
