# Video script — The equilibrium constant Kc

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every equilibrium mixture of the same reaction at the same temperature gives the same number when you combine its concentrations one special way.

The law of mass action gives the equilibrium expression: products' concentrations over reactants', each raised to its equation coefficient.

Its value is the equilibrium constant Kc (Kp does the same with partial pressures for gases).

Homogeneous equilibria have all species in one phase; heterogeneous equilibria span phases — pure solids and liquids are left out of the expression.

A large K means the equilibrium lies far toward products; a small K, toward reactants.

Now you. Say it back: What does K's size tell us?
Answer: How far the equilibrium lies toward products.
