# Video script — Tanning — hide to leather

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A raw hide rots in days; leather lasts decades.

Tannery work prepares hides: soaking, liming and removing hair and flesh.

Tanning stabilises the hide's protein — with chromium salts (chrome tanning) or plant extracts (vegetable tanning), as presented — so it cannot rot.

Finishing (dyeing, oiling, drying) completes the leather; the section notes the effluent the process produces and its care.

Now you. Say it back: What does tanning do to a hide?
Answer: Stabilises its protein so it cannot rot.
