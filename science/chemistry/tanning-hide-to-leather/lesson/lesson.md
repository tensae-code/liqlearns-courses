# Tanning — hide to leather

## Do it first
A raw hide rots in days; leather lasts decades. Before reading, say what tanning must change inside the hide — and why tanneries must watch their wastewater.

[Picture 21766 — Hides in tanning drums.]

Tannery work prepares hides: soaking, liming and removing hair and flesh.

Tanning stabilises the hide's protein — with chromium salts (chrome tanning) or plant extracts (vegetable tanning), as presented — so it cannot rot.

Finishing (dyeing, oiling, drying) completes the leather; the section notes the effluent the process produces and its care.
