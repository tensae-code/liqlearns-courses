# Tanning — hide to leather — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does tanning do to a hide?
**Answer: Stabilises its protein so it cannot rot**

**2. ** Name the two tanning kinds.
**Answer: Chrome and vegetable tanning**

**3. ** What is removed in preparation?
**Answer: Hair and flesh**

**4. ** What must tannery effluent get?
**Answer: Care/treatment — it pollutes**
