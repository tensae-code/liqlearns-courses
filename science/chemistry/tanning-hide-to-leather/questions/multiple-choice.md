# Tanning — hide to leather — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does tanning do to a hide?
a) Multi-electron spectra  b) Stabilises its protein so it cannot rot  c) In the nucleus  d) The light's frequency
**Answer: b) Stabilises its protein so it cannot rot**

**2. ** Name the two tanning kinds.
a) Chrome and vegetable tanning  b) At constant T, P × V is constant  c) Glass  d) Yes, to reactants
**Answer: a) Chrome and vegetable tanning**

**3. ** What is removed in preparation?
a) Making soap by alkaline hydrolysis of fats  b) Three — one per ester link  c) The fat's ester links (by water)  d) Hair and flesh
**Answer: d) Hair and flesh**

**4. ** What must tannery effluent get?
a) By-products  b) Vanadium(V) oxide  c) Care/treatment — it pollutes  d) Electricity and water
**Answer: c) Care/treatment — it pollutes**
