## Isotopes

Isotopes are atoms of the same element (same Z) differing in neutrons and mass number. Hydrogen's three are named individually — protium (no neutron), deuterium (one), tritium (two, "heavy hydrogen") — while other elements' isotopes are named by mass number (carbon-12, carbon-13, lithium-6, lithium-7 worked). Many isotopes have unstable radioactive nuclei (radioisotopes, e.g.

carbon-14) that decay releasing destructive particles, hence protective suits for work with them. Isotopes are also the evidence that forced Dalton's "all atoms of an element identical" postulate to be revised — nature supplies each element as a constant mixture of its isotopes.
