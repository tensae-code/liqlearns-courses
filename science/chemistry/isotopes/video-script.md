Today: **Isotopes**.

Isotopes are atoms of the same element (same Z) differing in neutrons and mass number.

Many isotopes have unstable radioactive nuclei (radioisotopes, e.g.

carbon-14) that decay releasing destructive particles, hence protective suits for work with them.
