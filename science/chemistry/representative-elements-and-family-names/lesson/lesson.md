## Representative elements and family names

The representative (main-group) elements, Groups IA–VIIA, have incompletely filled s or p subshells of the highest level; noble gases have filled p subshells (helium 1s²). Traditional family names are listed: Group I alkali metals, II alkaline earth metals, III boron family, IV carbon family, V nitrogen family, VI oxygen family, VII halogens, VIII noble gases.

Same group = same outer configuration (alkali metals ns¹, alkaline earths ns², halogens ns²np⁵) = similar chemical behaviour — with the caution that Groups IIIA–VIA still vary widely inside (carbon nonmetal, silicon/germanium metalloids, tin/lead metals). Valence electrons are defined here as the outer electrons involved in bonding.
