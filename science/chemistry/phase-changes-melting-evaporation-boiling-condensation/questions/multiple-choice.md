# Phase changes — melting, evaporation, boiling and condensation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When a solid melts, its particles…
a) Break into smaller particles  b) Stay the same particles but break free of their fixed positions and move past one another  c) Turn into a new substance  d) Stop moving
**Answer: b) Stay the same particles but break free of their fixed positions and move past one another**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A liquid freezes at…
a) A lower temperature than it melts  b) Any temperature  c) The same temperature at which the solid melts  d) Room temperature only
**Answer: c) The same temperature at which the solid melts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Evaporation happens…
a) Only at the boiling point  b) Throughout the liquid  c) At the surface, at any temperature, as the fastest particles escape  d) Only in sunlight
**Answer: c) At the surface, at any temperature, as the fastest particles escape**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Drops of water on the cold underside of a lid above steam are…
a) Boiling  b) Condensation — gas particles losing energy on a colder surface and gathering back into liquid  c) Melting  d) Freezing
**Answer: b) Condensation — gas particles losing energy on a colder surface and gathering back into liquid**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) While a liquid is boiling, its temperature…
a) Keeps rising steadily  b) Falls  c) Stays at the boiling point while the change completes  d) Rises then falls
**Answer: c) Stays at the boiling point while the change completes**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In every phase change, the particles themselves…
a) Change into new particles  b) Do not change — only their arrangement, spacing and motion change  c) Disappear  d) Double in number
**Answer: b) Do not change — only their arrangement, spacing and motion change**
