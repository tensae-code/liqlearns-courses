# Phase changes — melting, evaporation, boiling and condensation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solid turning into a liquid at its melting point is called _____.
**Answer: melting**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Gas turning back into a liquid on a colder surface is called _____.
**Answer: condensation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The fixed temperature at which a liquid turns to gas throughout its whole body is its boiling _____.
**Answer: point**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A phase change is a _____ change, because no new substance is made.
**Answer: physical**
