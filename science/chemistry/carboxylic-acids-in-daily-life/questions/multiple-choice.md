# Carboxylic acids in daily life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What acid is in vinegar?
a) Ethanoic (acetic) acid  b) Purifies a metal at the cathode  c) A cell potential depending on [H₃O⁺]  d) It regrows or refills
**Answer: a) Ethanoic (acetic) acid**

**2. ** What food role do acids play?
a) CO₂  b) Indivisible — cannot be cut  c) Alpha, beta and gamma  d) Preserving (preservatives)
**Answer: d) Preserving (preservatives)**

**3. ** What are long-chain acid salts?
a) The bonding MO  b) Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)  c) Soaps  d) Positive kernels in a lattice
**Answer: c) Soaps**

**4. ** Vinegar is strong or dilute acid?
a) The alcohol (alkyl part)  b) Dilute  c) Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH  d) Hydrolysis (as presented)
**Answer: b) Dilute**
