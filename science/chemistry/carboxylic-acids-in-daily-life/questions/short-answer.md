# Carboxylic acids in daily life — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What acid is in vinegar?
**Answer: Ethanoic (acetic) acid**

**2. ** What food role do acids play?
**Answer: Preserving (preservatives)**

**3. ** What are long-chain acid salts?
**Answer: Soaps**

**4. ** Vinegar is strong or dilute acid?
**Answer: Dilute**
