# Video script — Carboxylic acids in daily life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your kitchen already stocks this family: the vinegar bottle and the soap dish.

Vinegar is a dilute solution of ethanoic (acetic) acid, used in food and preserving.

Carboxylic acids and their salts serve as preservatives.

Long-chain acid salts are soaps — the family's working end, developed in the ester lessons.

Now you. Say it back: What acid is in vinegar?
Answer: Ethanoic (acetic) acid.
