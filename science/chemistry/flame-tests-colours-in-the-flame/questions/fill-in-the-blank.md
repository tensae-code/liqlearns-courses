# Flame tests — colours in the flame — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The sample is carried into the flame on a clean _____ loop.
**Answer: wire**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Each metal gives the flame its own _____.
**Answer: colour**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Observation first, then _____: the colour is seen, the metal is named.
**Answer: inference**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The flame test belongs to the _____ half of qualitative analysis.
**Answer: cation**
