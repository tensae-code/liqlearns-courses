# Flame tests — colours in the flame — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a flame test, the sample is held…
a) Beside the flame  b) In a hot flame, on a clean wire loop  c) In cold water  d) Under a microscope
**Answer: b) In a hot flame, on a clean wire loop**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The wire must be clean because…
a) A dirty wire is dangerous  b) Leftover sample writes another metal's colour over the true one  c) Clean wires are cheaper  d) The flame is hotter on clean wire
**Answer: b) Leftover sample writes another metal's colour over the true one**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the flame test, the metal is identified by…
a) The smell of the sample  b) The colour it gives the flame  c) The mass of the sample  d) The sound it makes
**Answer: b) The colour it gives the flame**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The colour seen is the observation; the metal's name is…
a) Another observation  b) The inference drawn from the observation  c) A guess that needs no evidence  d) The controlled variable
**Answer: b) The inference drawn from the observation**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In Libya's Grade 12 practical workbook, the flame test is…
a) The only experiment in the book  b) Experiment 10, inside a run of ion-identification experiments  c) A front-matter diagram only  d) Part of the exercises workbook
**Answer: b) Experiment 10, inside a run of ion-identification experiments**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The tests around the flame test in the scheme…
a) Replace it entirely  b) Confirm what the colour claims — the cation half of qualitative analysis  c) Are only for anions  d) Are never used with metals
**Answer: b) Confirm what the colour claims — the cation half of qualitative analysis**
