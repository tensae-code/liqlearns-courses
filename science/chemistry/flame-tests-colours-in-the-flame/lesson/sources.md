Structural summary (from the placements file): Unlike the other workbooks this one teaches a whole skill the student book does not: QUALITATIVE ANALYSIS. Front matter: a list of scientific vs common names of the chemicals and ions used, laboratory safety rules, the periodic table, Tables 1–3, a qualitative-analysis introduction whose first rule is that instructions must not be followed blindly — the student must know what each reagent is doing (solvent, precipitant, acid, alkali, oxidising agent or reducing agent) — then three flowcharts (identifying gases by colour/odour/tests: colourless O₂/H₂/H₂O/CO₂, violet I₂, yellow-green Cl₂, brown NO₂/Br₂; rapid anion identification: dilute HCl effervescence, BaCl₂/HCl for sulfate, the Br⁻/Cl⁻/I⁻/NO₃⁻/SO₄²⁻ set; rapid cation identification with ammonia solution: reddish-brown precipitate insoluble in excess = Fe³⁺, green/blue/white precipitates, Na⁺/K⁺ by elimination), and a worked model of recording observations and inferences in a three-column table. Then the experiments: (1) tests to identify alkanes and alkenes (iodine crystal solubility among them); (2) esters — preparing flavours and fragrances by heating an alcohol with an organic acid, students naming the ester from each alcohol–acid pair (methanol/ethanol/1-propanol/pentan-1-ol × butanoic/ethanoic acids); (3) determining the properties of an unknown organic compound; (4) studying a sodium salt NaXO₃; (5) identifying the oxidation states of a metal; (6-1, 6-2) identifying metal ions; (7) identifying the metal in three salts; (8) determining the amount of ZnO added to HCl — a quantitative experiment; (9) identifying ions in solution; (10) the flame test for metal ions; (11) analysis of a sodium salt and its solution; (12) identifying a transition metal; (13-1) the relative displacement strength of the halogens in the periodic table; (13-2) investigating the effect of hot and cold water on transition elements; (14) designing experiments to show the reactivity order of metals. Experiments are dated write-up sheets with observation tables throughout. Placements at liqlearns-courses-work/expansion-chemistry-2026-11-12/libya-placements.md.
