# Video script — Flame tests — colours in the flame

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Flame tests — colours in the flame** — Libya's way: the metal writes its name in colour.
Dip a clean wire loop into the sample and hold it in a hot flame. The metal in the sample colours the flame — and each metal's colour is its own. See the colour, name the metal: observation first, inference after. In Libya's Grade 12 practical book this is Experiment 10, one test inside the whole qualitative-analysis scheme, with the wet tests for ions all around it to confirm what the colour claims.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
