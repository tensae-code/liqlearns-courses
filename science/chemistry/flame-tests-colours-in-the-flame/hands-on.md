# Hands-on — Flame tests — colours in the flame

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Observation or inference?
Write each statement on a card and sort the cards into OBSERVATION and INFERENCE: (a) the flame turned the colour recorded for the sample; (b) the sample contains the metal whose colour that is; (c) the wire was used before and not cleaned; (d) the colour was muddy and mixed. Check your sorting against sub lesson 2 — and say what (c) does to (a) and (b).

## The scheme around the test
From sub lesson 3, list the identification jobs the workbook sets around Experiment 10 (the metal in three salts; ions in solution; a transition metal identified) and write, for each, whether the flame test alone could finish the job or a wet test must confirm it.
