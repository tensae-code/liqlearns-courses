# Video script — Collision theory — what speeds a reaction up — Unit 4 review

Powdered chalk vanishes in acid in seconds; the same mass as one lump takes minutes.

Collision theory: reactions happen when particles collide with enough energy (at least the activation energy) and in the right orientation — an effective collision.

Higher concentration (or pressure for gases) puts more particles in the space, so collisions come more often.

Greater surface area exposes more particles to collision.

Unit 4 closes: every factor works by raising the rate of effective collisions.

Now you. Say it back: What makes a collision effective?
Answer: Enough energy and right orientation.
