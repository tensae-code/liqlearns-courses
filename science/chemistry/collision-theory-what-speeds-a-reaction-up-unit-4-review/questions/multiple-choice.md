# Collision theory — what speeds a reaction up — Unit 4 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What makes a collision effective?
a) The charge of one mole of electrons (≈96,500 C)  b) The anode  c) Enough energy and right orientation  d) Negative
**Answer: c) Enough energy and right orientation**

**2. ** What is activation energy?
a) Waste, at the source  b) The minimum energy for reaction on collision  c) Source, movement, changes, effects  d) Into water and food chains
**Answer: b) The minimum energy for reaction on collision**

**3. ** How does a catalyst work?
a) It lowers the activation energy path  b) Molten or in solution — ions move  c) London dispersion  d) End-to-end (axial) overlap
**Answer: a) It lowers the activation energy path**

**4. ** Why does surface area matter?
a) A (symmetrical) ether  b) Carbon 1  c) Carbon dioxide, CO₂  d) More particles are exposed to collisions
**Answer: d) More particles are exposed to collisions**
