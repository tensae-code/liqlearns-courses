# Nutrients — proteins, carbohydrates, fats and vitamins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing China's book teaches in this lesson — point 1 of the key ideas.
**Answer: The six nutrient classes (protein, carbohydrate, oil/fat, vitamin, mineral salts, water); proteins built from amino acids, digested and rebuilt, about 18 kJ/g; haemoglobin's Fe²⁺ carrying oxygen and carbon monoxide binding 200–300 times more strongly — the chemistry of coal-gas poisoning; a data card on enzymes as protein catalysts, and cards on smoking and on formaldehyde denaturing protein.**

**2.** State one thing China's book teaches in this lesson — point 2 of the key ideas.
**Answer: Carbohydrates: starch (C₆H₁₀O₅)ₙ hydrolysed by enzymes to glucose, glucose oxidised in the body (C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O, about 16 kJ/g, supplying 60–70% of energy), sucrose as the table sugar, and a cellulose card; oils and fats at about 39 kJ/g as the body's reserve fuel; vitamins and the diseases of lacking them (night blindness, scurvy); the Chinese Residents' Balanced Diet Pagoda as the national guide to daily amounts (printed p. 95).**
