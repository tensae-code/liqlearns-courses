# Lesson plan — Nutrients — proteins, carbohydrates, fats and vitamins

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Nutrients — proteins, carbohydrates, fats and vitamins
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The six nutrient classes (protein, carbohydrate, oil/fat, vitamin, mineral salts, water); proteins built from amino acids, digested and rebuilt, about 18 kJ/g; haemoglobin's Fe²⁺ carrying oxygen and carbon monoxide binding 200–300 times more strongly — the chemistry of coal-gas poisoning; a data card on enzymes as protein catalysts, and cards on smoking and on formaldehyde denaturing protein.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State in their own words: The six nutrient classes (protein, carbohydrate, oil/fat, vitamin, mineral salts, water); proteins built from amino acids, digested and rebuilt, about 18 kJ/g; haemoglobin's Fe²⁺ carrying oxygen and carbon monoxide binding 200–300 times more strongly — the chemistry of coal-gas poisoning; a data card on enzymes as protein catalysts, and cards on smoking and on formaldehyde denaturing protein.
2. State in their own words: Carbohydrates: starch (C₆H₁₀O₅)ₙ hydrolysed by enzymes to glucose, glucose oxidised in the body (C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O, about 16 kJ/g, supplying 60–70% of energy), sucrose as the table sugar, and a cellulose card; oils and fats at about 39 kJ/g as the body's reserve fuel; vitamins and the diseases of lacking them (night blindness, scurvy); the Chinese Residents' Balanced Diet Pagoda as the national guide to daily amounts (printed p. 95).
