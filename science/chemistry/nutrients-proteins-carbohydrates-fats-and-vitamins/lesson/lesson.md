# Nutrients — proteins, carbohydrates, fats and vitamins

## Do it first
Before reading, say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is: nutrients proteins carbohydrates fats and vitamins. Then read on — this is China's way of teaching it, as the China placements describe the book's own treatment.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 12 Topic 1 人类重要的营养物质 — China's book (China, Grade 9)

The six nutrient classes (protein, carbohydrate, oil/fat, vitamin, mineral salts, water); proteins built from amino acids, digested and rebuilt, about 18 kJ/g; haemoglobin's Fe²⁺ carrying oxygen and carbon monoxide binding 200–300 times more strongly — the chemistry of coal-gas poisoning; a data card on enzymes as protein catalysts, and cards on smoking and on formaldehyde denaturing protein.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 12 Topic 1 — China's book (China, Grade 9)

Carbohydrates: starch (C₆H₁₀O₅)ₙ hydrolysed by enzymes to glucose, glucose oxidised in the body (C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O, about 16 kJ/g, supplying 60–70% of energy), sucrose as the table sugar, and a cellulose card; oils and fats at about 39 kJ/g as the body's reserve fuel; vitamins and the diseases of lacking them (night blindness, scurvy); the Chinese Residents' Balanced Diet Pagoda as the national guide to daily amounts (printed p. 95).
