# Stereoisomerism — enantiomers and diastereomers — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) The rotation of plane-polarised light by an enantiomer is called optical _____.
**Answer: activity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Same connections, different space arrangement: _____isomers.
**Answer: stereo**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Stereoisomers that are not mirror images are called _____.
**Answer: diastereomers**
