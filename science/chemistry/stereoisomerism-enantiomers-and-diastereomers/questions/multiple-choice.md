# Stereoisomerism — enantiomers and diastereomers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Stereoisomers differ from constitutional isomers in that they have…
a) Different formulas  b) The same connections but a different arrangement in space  c) Different elements  d) No carbon
**Answer: b) The same connections but a different arrangement in space**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The doorway to enantiomerism is a carbon carrying…
a) A double bond  b) Four identical groups  c) Four different groups  d) A charge
**Answer: c) Four different groups**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Enantiomers are…
a) Superposable mirror images  b) Non-superposable mirror images  c) Not mirror images at all  d) The same substance twice
**Answer: b) Non-superposable mirror images**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An equal mixture of two enantiomers (racemic) is…
a) Twice as active  b) Optically inactive — the rotations cancel  c) A diastereomer  d) A constitutional isomer
**Answer: b) Optically inactive — the rotations cancel**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Diastereomers can be separated by ordinary methods because they…
a) Are mirror images  b) Differ in ordinary physical properties  c) Have different formulas  d) Are gases
**Answer: b) Differ in ordinary physical properties**
