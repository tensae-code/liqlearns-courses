## Ethiopia's chemical industries

An industry is defined, chemical industries are sorted into product families (industrial inorganic chemicals; plastics and synthetics; drugs;

soaps and toilet goods; paints; industrial organic chemicals;

agricultural chemicals; miscellaneous), and chemical products are classed as basic chemicals, intermediates for further manufacture, and finished products. The core content is a 19-row table of Ethiopian enterprises with city and product — among them Ziway Caustic Soda (sodium hydroxide), Abijata Soda Ash (trona), Adola Magnesium Oxide, Adami Tulu Pesticide Processing, Repi and other soap/detergent makers, Nefas Silk and Kadisco paints, cosmetics makers — plus named cement (Mugher, Derba, Dangote…), sugar (Wonji, Finchaa…), paper, pharmaceutical and tyre producers, and the government's industrial parks.

A project work sends the class on an industrial visit to a local plant to observe raw materials, processes and finished products and report back.
