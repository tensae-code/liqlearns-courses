# Video script — Ethiopia's chemical industries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Ethiopia's chemical industries**.

An industry is defined, chemical industries are sorted into product families (industrial inorganic chemicals;

miscellaneous), and chemical products are classed as basic chemicals, intermediates for further manufacture, and finished products.

A project work sends the class on an industrial visit to a local plant to observe raw materials, processes and finished products and report back.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
