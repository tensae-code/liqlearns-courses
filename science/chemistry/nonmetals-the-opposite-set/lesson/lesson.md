## Nonmetals

Nonmetals show the opposite property set to metals (poor conductors, brittle or gaseous, acidic oxides, oxidising behaviour), with the section surveying common nonmetals (nitrogen, oxygen, phosphorus, chlorine among them) and their everyday uses before each is taken in turn.3.1).
