# Video script — Polymers — chains of monomers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One small molecule is a bead; ten thousand linked are a rope.

A monomer is a small molecule that can join with others like itself; a polymer is the very long chain built from many monomers.

The degree of polymerisation counts the monomer units in the chain.

Everyday polymers surround us — polyethene bags, PET bottles, nylon, natural rubber — the cover's PET polymerisation scheme is the emblem.

Now you. Say it back: What is a monomer?
Answer: A small molecule that joins into chains.
