# Polymers — chains of monomers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is a monomer?
a) The short-chain ones  b) A small molecule that joins into chains  c) Forward, making products  d) The carbonyl group, C=O
**Answer: b) A small molecule that joins into chains**

**2. ** What is a polymer?
a) A long chain of many monomers  b) By exactly one proton  c) In steps, each with its own Ka  d) No excess H₃O⁺ or OH⁻ is made
**Answer: a) A long chain of many monomers**

**3. ** What does degree of polymerisation count?
a) Brine  b) Hydration with water  c) Water  d) The monomer units in the chain
**Answer: d) The monomer units in the chain**

**4. ** Name the cover's polymer.
a) The orbital's orientation  b) On the right of the table  c) PET  d) The uncertainty principle
**Answer: c) PET**
