# Polymers — chains of monomers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is a monomer?
**Answer: A small molecule that joins into chains**

**2. ** What is a polymer?
**Answer: A long chain of many monomers**

**3. ** What does degree of polymerisation count?
**Answer: The monomer units in the chain**

**4. ** Name the cover's polymer.
**Answer: PET**
