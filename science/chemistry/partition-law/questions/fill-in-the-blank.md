# The partition law — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The constant ratio of concentrations between two immiscible solvents is the partition _____.
**Answer: coefficient**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The partition law holds at a fixed _____.
**Answer: temperature**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Recovering a solute by shaking it into a preferred immiscible solvent is solvent _____.
**Answer: extraction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Partition measurements can be used to find the formula of a _____ ion.
**Answer: complex**
