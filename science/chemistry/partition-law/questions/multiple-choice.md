# The partition law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The partition law states that, at a fixed temperature, a solute between two immiscible solvents keeps constant its…
a) Total mass  b) Ratio of concentrations in the two layers  c) Concentration in each layer separately  d) Colour
**Answer: b) Ratio of concentrations in the two layers**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) If K = C(A)/C(B) = 5 and the concentration in B is 0.01 mol/L, the concentration in A is…
a) 0.002 mol/L  b) 0.05 mol/L  c) 0.5 mol/L  d) 5 mol/L
**Answer: b) 0.05 mol/L**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The partition law in its simple form requires the solute to be…
a) Concentrated and hot  b) In the same molecular state in both solvents, in a dilute solution  c) Fully reacted with one solvent  d) A solid
**Answer: b) In the same molecular state in both solvents, in a dilute solution**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Several extractions with small portions of solvent remove more solute than one extraction with the whole volume because…
a) Small portions mix better  b) Each shaking removes the fixed share the coefficient allows, so repeated portions keep taking shares of what remains  c) The coefficient changes with portion size  d) One large portion cannot touch the solute
**Answer: b) Each shaking removes the fixed share the coefficient allows, so repeated portions keep taking shares of what remains**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the distribution method for a complex ion, the formula is found by…
a) Weighing the complex directly  b) Measuring how the partition shifts as ligand is added, which reveals the proportions in which metal and ligand combine  c) Heating the complex until it breaks  d) Filtering the complex out
**Answer: b) Measuring how the partition shifts as ligand is added, which reveals the proportions in which metal and ligand combine**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Solvent extraction separates the layers after shaking mainly with…
a) A filter paper  b) A separatory funnel  c) A centrifuge only  d) A magnet
**Answer: b) A separatory funnel**
