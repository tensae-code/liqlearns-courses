# Amines, amides and nitriles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The group that defines an amine is…
a) –COOH  b) –NH₂  c) –C≡N  d) –OH
**Answer: b) –NH₂**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Brazil introduces amines through the neurotransmitters because…
a) They are made of pure nitrogen  b) They are the only amines in the body  c) They share the amine group, each carrying the same nitrogen signature  d) They contain no carbon
**Answer: c) They share the amine group, each carrying the same nitrogen signature**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A nerve impulse runs on concentration differences of…
a) Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺  b) H⁺ and OH⁻  c) Fe²⁺ and Zn²⁺  d) Na⁺ and K⁺, which must be restored after each impulse
**Answer: d) Na⁺ and K⁺, which must be restored after each impulse**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ceramides, named on cosmetics labels, belong to the family of…
a) Amides — they are the skin barrier's own sealing lipids  b) Nitriles  c) Esters  d) Alkanes
**Answer: a) Amides — they are the skin barrier's own sealing lipids**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A nitrile carries the group…
a) –NH₂  b) –CONH₂  c) –C≡N, a carbon triple-bonded to nitrogen  d) –CHO
**Answer: c) –C≡N, a carbon triple-bonded to nitrogen**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Hydrolysing a nitrile takes it…
a) Straight to an amine  b) Through the amide, on its way to the carboxylic acid  c) To an alkane  d) Nowhere — nitriles never react
**Answer: b) Through the amide, on its way to the carboxylic acid**
