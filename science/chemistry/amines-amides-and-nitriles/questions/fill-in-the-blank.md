# Amines, amides and nitriles — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The chemical messengers between nerve cells are called _____.
**Answer: neurotransmitters**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The amides named on cosmetics labels, the skin barrier's own lipids, are the _____.
**Answer: ceramides**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In a nitrile, carbon is joined to nitrogen by a _____ bond.
**Answer: triple**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Brazil's book closes on UV-B radiation and skin _____.
**Answer: cancer**
