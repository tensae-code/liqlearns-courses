Today: **Amines, amides and nitriles** — Brazil's closing chapter: the nitrogen functions, the chemistry life cannot do without.

Three families, one element: nitrogen. The amines carry –NH₂ — and that is the group your nerve messengers, the neurotransmitters, all share. The amides join nitrogen to a carbonyl, –CONH₂ — and that is the ceramide on the cream jar, your skin's own seal. The nitriles end in a triple bond, –C≡N.
And the families are joined: add water to a nitrile and you pass through the amide to the acid; reduce it and you arrive at the amine. One nitrogen, three shapes — in your nerves, on your skin, under the sun.
