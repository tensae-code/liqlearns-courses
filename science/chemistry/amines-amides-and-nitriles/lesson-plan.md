# Lesson plan — Amines, amides and nitriles

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Amines, amides and nitriles
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the nitrogen functions as one connected family — amines (the –NH₂ group, shared by the neurotransmitters, while the nerve impulse itself runs on Na⁺/K⁺ concentration differences that must be restored), amides (the –CONH₂ group, met on the cosmetics label as ceramides, the skin barrier's own amides), and nitriles (the –C≡N group, nitrogen at a triple bond) — joined by reactions that run between them (nitrile hydrolysis through the amide to the acid; nitrile reduction to the amine); Brazil teaches them as the closing chapter of its whole public textbook, and closes the book on UV-B and skin cancer
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Amines, amides and amino acids is recommended first, never forced.

## Sub lessons
1. **Amines — the group the messengers share** — The –NH₂ group from ammonia, its lone pair making amines bases; Brazil's entry: the neurotransmitters share this group, and the impulse they serve runs on Na⁺/K⁺ differences restored after each pass.
2. **Amides — the carbonyl joined to nitrogen, on the cosmetics label** — The –CONH₂ group; ceramides, the skin barrier's own amides, named on cream jars — the amide link as the body's sealing and joining chemistry.
3. **Nitriles — nitrogen at the triple bond** — The –C≡N group; hydrolysis through the amide to the acid and reduction to the amine join the three families into one; the book closes on UV-B and skin cancer.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the one element: all three families are carbon chemistry carrying nitrogen; only the group changes — –NH₂, –CONH₂, –C≡N — and the group sets the family.
- Mixes amides up with Amines, amides and amino acids → that lesson sorts amines and amides beside amino acids as derivatives of the alkanoic acids; this lesson follows Brazil's closing trio — amines, amides, nitriles — the nitrogen functions, met in nerves and cosmetics.
- Thinks the nitrile's nitrogen carries hydrogens like the amine's → the nitrile nitrogen carries none: it sits at the end of a triple bond, –C≡N, which is why hydrolysis and reduction can walk it into the other two families.
