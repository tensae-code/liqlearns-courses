# Amines, amides and nitriles — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Amines — the group the messengers share, Amides — the carbonyl joined to nitrogen, on the cosmetics label, Nitriles — nitrogen at the triple bond), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, sorting new structures into the three families by their groups alone, and tracing one nitrogen atom through nitrile → amide → acid and nitrile → amine, naming the reaction at each step.
