# How the electron was discovered — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Toward which plate does a cathode ray bend?
a) The –OH carbon holds three other carbons  b) A positive plate  c) They converge to one level  d) The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance
**Answer: b) A positive plate**

**2. ** What particle did Thomson identify from cathode rays?
a) The electron  b) An exercise of the same kind  c) An electron-pair acceptor  d) Le Chatelier's principle
**Answer: a) The electron**

**3. ** What did Millikan's oil-drop experiment measure?
a) Weeds  b) Silica — the glass former  c) Molasses  d) The charge of one electron
**Answer: d) The charge of one electron**

**4. ** What charge does the electron carry?
a) Electron diffraction  b) A dumbbell (two lobes)  c) Negative  d) Only certain sharp lines
**Answer: c) Negative**
