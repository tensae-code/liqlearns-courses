# How the electron was discovered — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Toward which plate does a cathode ray bend — A positive plate
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What particle did Thomson identify from cathode rays — CO₂
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What did Millikan's oil-drop experiment measure — The charge of one electron
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What charge does the electron carry — Irreversible
**Answer: False**
