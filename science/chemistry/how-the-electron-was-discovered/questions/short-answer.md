# How the electron was discovered — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Toward which plate does a cathode ray bend?
**Answer: A positive plate**

**2. ** What particle did Thomson identify from cathode rays?
**Answer: The electron**

**3. ** What did Millikan's oil-drop experiment measure?
**Answer: The charge of one electron**

**4. ** What charge does the electron carry?
**Answer: Negative**
