# How the electron was discovered

## Do it first
Imagine a sealed glass tube with almost all the air pumped out, wired to a high voltage. A glowing ray crosses the tube. Is the ray made of something? How could you test it?

[Picture 29254 — The ray bends to the positive plate — it is negative.]

In a cathode-ray tube, a ray travels from the negative cathode across the tube.

The ray bends toward a positive plate, so the ray carries negative charge.

Thomson measured the ray's charge-to-mass behaviour and concluded the ray is made of one universal particle — the electron — found in every atom.

Millikan's oil-drop experiment measured the charge on a single electron.
