# Video script — How the electron was discovered

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Imagine a sealed glass tube with almost all the air pumped out, wired to a high voltage.

In a cathode-ray tube, a ray travels from the negative cathode across the tube.

The ray bends toward a positive plate, so the ray carries negative charge.

Thomson measured the ray's charge-to-mass behaviour and concluded the ray is made of one universal particle — the electron — found in every atom.

Millikan's oil-drop experiment measured the charge on a single electron.

Now you. Say it back: Toward which plate does a cathode ray bend?
Answer: A positive plate.
