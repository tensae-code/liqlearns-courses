# Using precipitation — separating ions, cleaning wastewater, dissolving scale — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** _____ still holds SO₄²⁻, Mg²⁺ and Ca²⁺ after filtering: learners plan and then perform the precipitation sequence (excess BaCl₂, then NaOH, then Na₂CO₃ — which also removes the added Ba²⁺), filter, neutralise with HCl and evaporate, debating reagent order and why each excess must itself be removed.
**Answer: Crude salt**

**2.** _____ a tool: add a reagent to precipitate Fe³⁺ from industrial waste by pH control, or Na₂S to drop Cu²⁺/Hg²⁺ as sulfides (chemical-precipitation wastewater flow diagram); precipitation dissolved again by removing an ion (CaCO₃ scale in acid — and boiler-scale chemistry), with the design rule: choose the reagent that leaves the least residual ion and adds nothing worse.
**Answer: Precipitation as**
