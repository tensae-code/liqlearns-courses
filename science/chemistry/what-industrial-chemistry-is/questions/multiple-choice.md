# What industrial chemistry is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does industrial chemistry do?
a) Turns raw materials into valuable products  b) Centrifuging (spinning)  c) Slows microbial growth  d) PET
**Answer: a) Turns raw materials into valuable products**

**2. ** What changes from lab to plant?
a) Three, along x, y and z  b) s, p, d and f  c) The noble gases  d) Scale — yield, cost and by-products matter
**Answer: d) Scale — yield, cost and by-products matter**

**3. ** What does this unit tour?
a) The carbonyl group, C=O  b) The short-chain ones  c) Manufacturing processes, incl. Ethiopia's  d) Forward, making products
**Answer: c) Manufacturing processes, incl. Ethiopia's**
