# What industrial chemistry is

## Do it first
A mine holds rock; a market wants metal, fertiliser, soap. Before reading, say in one sentence what stands between the rock and the market.

[Picture 21748 — Raw material in, products out.]

Industrial chemistry turns raw materials into valuable products at scale.

It links the laboratory reaction to the plant: yields, conditions, costs and by-products all matter at scale.

This unit tours the processes — first the great chemical manufactures, then Ethiopia's own industries.
