# Video script — What industrial chemistry is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A mine holds rock; a market wants metal, fertiliser, soap.

Industrial chemistry turns raw materials into valuable products at scale.

It links the laboratory reaction to the plant: yields, conditions, costs and by-products all matter at scale.

This unit tours the processes — first the great chemical manufactures, then Ethiopia's own industries.

Now you. Say it back: What does industrial chemistry do?
Answer: Turns raw materials into valuable products.
