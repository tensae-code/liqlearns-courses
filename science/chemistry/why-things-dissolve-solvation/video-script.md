Today: **Why things dissolve — solvation**.

Dissolving is solvation: solvent particles surround and separate the solute's particles (hydration when the solvent is water).

Whether it happens is governed by inter-particle attractions: if solute–solvent attractions match solvent–solvent and solute–solute ones, the solution is ideal and…

stronger cross-attractions make dissolving exothermic (ΔHsoln < 0);

weaker ones make it endothermic (ΔHsoln > 0).

"Like dissolves like" is the working prediction rule.
