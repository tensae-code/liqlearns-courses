# Why things dissolve — solvation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** Whether it happens is governed by inter-particle attractions: if solute–_____ attractions match solvent–solvent and solute–solute ones, the solution is ideal and ΔHsoln = 0;
**Answer: solvent**

**2. ** stronger cross-attractions make _____ exothermic (ΔHsoln < 0);
**Answer: dissolving**

**3. ** weaker ones make it _____ (ΔHsoln > 0).
**Answer: endothermic**

**4. ** "Like dissolves like" is the _____ prediction rule.
**Answer: working**
