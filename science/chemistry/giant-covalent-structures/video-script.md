Today: **Giant covalent structures** — Libya's way: one crystal, one enormous molecule.
Some covalent substances are not made of small molecules at all. In a giant covalent structure every atom is bonded to its neighbours, and the bonding runs through the whole piece — diamond, silica in sand, graphite-style carbon networks. There are no molecules to pull apart, so these substances are very hard and melt only at very high temperatures: to melt one, you must break the strong bonds themselves.
