# Giant covalent structures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a giant covalent structure…
a) Atoms sit as separate small molecules  b) Every atom is covalently bonded into one continuous network  c) Ions are held by electrostatic forces only  d) There are no bonds at all
**Answer: b) Every atom is covalently bonded into one continuous network**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The book's other word for this kind of structure is…
a) Macromolecule  b) Mixture  c) Alloy  d) Electrolyte
**Answer: a) Macromolecule**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a giant covalent substance?
a) Ice  b) Silica (sand)  c) Carbon dioxide gas  d) Glucose
**Answer: b) Silica (sand)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In diamond, each carbon atom is bonded to…
a) One other atom  b) Two others in a chain  c) Four others in a 3-D network  d) No other atoms
**Answer: c) Four others in a 3-D network**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Giant covalent substances have very high melting points because melting must…
a) Only loosen weak forces between molecules  b) Break strong covalent bonds throughout the structure  c) Remove electrons from every atom  d) Dissolve the crystal
**Answer: b) Break strong covalent bonds throughout the structure**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Simple molecular substances melt at low temperatures because…
a) Their covalent bonds are weak  b) Only the weak forces between molecules give way  c) They contain no bonds  d) They are always liquids
**Answer: b) Only the weak forces between molecules give way**
