# Giant covalent structures — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A giant covalent crystal is effectively one enormous _____.
**Answer: molecule**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sand is the giant covalent substance _____, silicon dioxide.
**Answer: silica**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Giant covalent substances are very _____ and melt only at very high temperatures.
**Answer: hard**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Melting a giant covalent substance means breaking _____ bonds throughout the network.
**Answer: covalent**
