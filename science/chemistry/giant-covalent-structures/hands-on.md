# Hands-on — Giant covalent structures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the structures
Write eight substances on cards — sodium chloride, diamond, water, silica (sand), glucose, graphite, magnesium oxide, carbon dioxide. Sort them into the three classes of the unit: ionic lattice, simple molecules, giant covalent. Check your sorting against sub lessons 1 and 2.

## Predict from the structure
For diamond, ice and silica, write the prediction each structure makes before you check: hard or soft? high or low melting point? Then write the one-sentence reason each prediction rests on, using sub lesson 3.
