# Water hardness — temporary and permanent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Hard water is hard because it carries dissolved…
a) Sodium chloride  b) Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ ions  c) Carbon dioxide bubbles  d) Soap
**Answer: b) Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ ions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Soap is wasted in hard water because the hardness ions…
a) Dissolve the soap  b) Precipitate it as scum before it can lather  c) Boil it away  d) Colour it grey harmlessly
**Answer: b) Precipitate it as scum before it can lather**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Temporary hardness is caused by calcium and magnesium…
a) Sulfates  b) Chlorides  c) Hydrogencarbonates  d) Nitrates
**Answer: c) Hydrogencarbonates**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The reaction CaCO₃ + H₂O + CO₂ → Ca(HCO₃)₂ is also the reaction that…
a) Makes soap  b) Carves limestone caves  c) Softens water  d) Forms scum
**Answer: b) Carves limestone caves**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Permanent hardness is caused by calcium and magnesium…
a) Hydrogencarbonates  b) Carbonates  c) Sulfates  d) Hydroxides
**Answer: c) Sulfates**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Boiling a permanently hard sample leaves it…
a) Soft  b) Still hard  c) Distilled  d) Sterile and soft
**Answer: b) Still hard**
