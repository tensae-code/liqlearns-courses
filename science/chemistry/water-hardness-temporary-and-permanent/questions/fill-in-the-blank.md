# Water hardness — temporary and permanent — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The grey precipitate soap forms in hard water is called _____.
**Answer: scum**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Boiling temporary-hard water deposits the hardness as a white _____ in the kettle.
**Answer: fur (scale / limescale)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The gas dissolved in rainwater that lets it dissolve limestone is carbon _____.
**Answer: dioxide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Rain carrying dissolved ions out of soil is called _____.
**Answer: leaching**
