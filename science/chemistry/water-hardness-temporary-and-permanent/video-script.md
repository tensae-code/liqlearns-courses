Today: **Water hardness** — Dominica's CSEC hardness unit: two kinds, one kettle test.

Clear water can still be hard — loaded with calcium and magnesium ions that seize your soap and drop it as scum before a single bubble stands. If the ions arrived as hydrogencarbonates, dissolved out of limestone by rainy carbon dioxide — the same reaction that carves the caves — the hardness is temporary, and boiling drives it out as kettle fur. If they arrived as sulfates, the hardness is permanent, and no kettle will cure it. Two kinds, told apart by one partner: the anion.
