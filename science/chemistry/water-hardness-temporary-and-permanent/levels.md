# Water hardness — temporary and permanent — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Hard water — the ions that waste soap, Temporary hardness — the limestone transaction, Permanent hardness — the sulfates stay), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, writing the cave equation from memory and reading it in both directions (cave carved / hardness formed), and sorting fresh water samples by anion into temporary, permanent or soft with the boiling verdict attached.
