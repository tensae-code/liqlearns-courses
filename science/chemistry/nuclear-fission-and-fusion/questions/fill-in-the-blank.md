# Nuclear fission and fusion — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Uranium-_____ dates the age of the Earth.
**Answer: 235**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The warning sign for radiation is the _____ sign.
**Answer: trefoil**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The stated harm of the radiations is _____ damage.
**Answer: tissue**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Controlled fission generates _____ in a reactor core.
**Answer: electricity**
