# Nuclear fission and fusion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How do the two isotopes divide the dating work?
**Answer: Carbon-14, decaying at a pace suited to thousands of years, dates once-living remains; uranium-235, far slower, reaches back to date the age of the Earth itself.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What single fact stands behind the sun, the bomb and the reactor?
**Answer: Mass converts to energy, and a small mass yields an enormous energy — released by the sun, uncontrolled in the nuclear bomb, and governed as controlled fission in a reactor core to generate electricity.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the book fence nuclear work?
**Answer: With measurement and warning: the Geiger counter detects radiation that cannot be seen or felt, and the radiation trefoil sign warns that nuclear radiation is present (printed p. 178) — alongside the stated weighing of medicine's benefits against tissue damage.**
