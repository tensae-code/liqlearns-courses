# Nuclear fission and fusion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon-14 is used to date…
a) The Earth's age  b) Once-living remains  c) Rocks only  d) The sun
**Answer: b) Once-living remains**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The age of the Earth is dated with…
a) Carbon-14  b) Uranium-235  c) Hydrogen-3  d) Oxygen-16
**Answer: b) Uranium-235**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Einstein's point, as the book states it, is that…
a) Mass disappears for nothing  b) A small mass yields an enormous energy  c) Energy cannot change form  d) Fission needs no control
**Answer: b) A small mass yields an enormous energy**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Controlled fission in a reactor core generates…
a) Medicine  b) Electricity  c) Fertiliser  d) Plastic
**Answer: b) Electricity**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Nuclear medicine uses radioactivity to…
a) Date remains  b) Diagnose, trace and treat cancerous tumours  c) Weld metals  d) Ripen fruit
**Answer: b) Diagnose, trace and treat cancerous tumours**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The instrument that detects radiation is the…
a) Voltameter  b) Geiger counter  c) Bunsen burner  d) Balance
**Answer: b) Geiger counter**
