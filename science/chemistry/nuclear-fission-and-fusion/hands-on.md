# Hands-on — Nuclear fission and fusion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two clocks (paper exercise)
Write two dating jobs — a wooden tool from an old settlement, a rock from the Earth's early crust. Assign each its isotope from the lesson and say in one line why the other isotope would fail the job (pace too fast, or too slow).

## Tool or harm ledger (paper exercise)
Make the book's own balance sheet: three columns — the work, what it gives, what it risks — for reactor electricity and for nuclear medicine, using only facts from the lesson. Finish with the two fences: the counter and the sign.
