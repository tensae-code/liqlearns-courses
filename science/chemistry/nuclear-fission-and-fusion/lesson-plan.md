# Lesson plan — Nuclear fission and fusion

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Nuclear fission and fusion
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** nuclear energy at work the Syrian way: radioactive isotopes dating the past, mass converting to energy in the sun and the bomb, controlled fission in a reactor core generating electricity, and nuclear medicine — held against the stated harm of tissue damage (Syria, Grade 9 — *الفيزياء والكيمياء للصف التاسع الأساسي (Physics & Chemistry, Grade 9 Basic)*, U6 L1, printed p. 177)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

## Sub lessons
1. **Radioactive isotopes as clocks — dating the past** — The lesson first sets radioactive isotopes to work as **clocks**: **carbon-14 dates once-living remains** — its steady decay measures the time since the living thing died — and **uranium-235 dates the age of the Earth** itself, its far slower decay reaching back to the planet's beginning.
2. **Mass into energy — the sun, the bomb, the reactor** — Behind the works stands Einstein's point as the book states it: **mass converts to energy, and a small mass yields an enormous energy**. The book's two great examples are the **sun**, pouring out energy from mass, and the **nuclear bomb**, releasing it uncontrolled.
3. **Nuclear medicine — and the harms, the counter and the sign** — The third work is healing: **nuclear medicine diagnoses, traces and treats cancerous tumours** — radioactive tracers show where the body's chemistry is going wrong, and aimed radiation attacks the tumour. The book sets this **against the stated harms: tissue damage** from the same radiations.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks dating measures the isotope's age directly → the isotope decays at a fixed pace; counting what REMAINS of it in the remains reads the time since death — or since the Earth's rocks formed.
- Treats reactor and bomb as different physics → both are mass converting to energy uncontrolled vs controlled: governed fission makes electricity; the difference is control.
- Hears only danger or only benefit → the book holds both together: medicine that diagnoses and treats, against the stated harm of tissue damage — the weighing is part of the lesson.
