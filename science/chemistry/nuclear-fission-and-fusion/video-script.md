# Video script — Nuclear fission and fusion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Nuclear fission and fusion** — the nucleus at work.

Carbon-14 dates the once-living; uranium-235 dates the Earth. A small mass yields enormous energy — in the sun, in the bomb, and governed in a reactor core making electricity. And in the hospital, the same radiations diagnose, trace and treat tumours — weighed honestly against the tissue they can harm.

Power is not the question; control and purpose are.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
