# Nuclear fission and fusion

## Do it first
Before you read: the same nuclear change that can destroy tissue can also find a tumour and treat it. Say out loud: what decides whether a powerful thing is a tool or a weapon? Then read on: Syria's Grade 9 book puts nuclear energy's works side by side, on printed p. 177.

## Sub lesson 1 — Radioactive isotopes as clocks — dating the past

The lesson first sets radioactive isotopes to work as **clocks**: **carbon-14 dates once-living remains** — its steady decay measures the time since the living thing died — and **uranium-235 dates the age of the Earth** itself, its far slower decay reaching back to the planet's beginning.

One principle, two reaches: the isotope's decay runs at its own fixed pace, so counting what is left reads the time that has passed — thousands of years for carbon-14, billions for uranium-235.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mass into energy — the sun, the bomb, the reactor

Behind the works stands Einstein's point as the book states it: **mass converts to energy, and a small mass yields an enormous energy**. The book's two great examples are the **sun**, pouring out energy from mass, and the **nuclear bomb**, releasing it uncontrolled.

Against the bomb the book sets the controlled form: **controlled fission in a reactor core generates electricity** — the nucleus split at a governed pace, its energy taken off as heat and turned to electric power. Fission governed is a power station; fission ungoverned is the bomb. The difference is control, not the physics.

## Sub lesson 3 — Nuclear medicine — and the harms, the counter and the sign

The third work is healing: **nuclear medicine diagnoses, traces and treats cancerous tumours** — radioactive tracers show where the body's chemistry is going wrong, and aimed radiation attacks the tumour. The book sets this **against the stated harms: tissue damage** from the same radiations.

The tools of respect follow (printed p. 178): the **Geiger counter**, which clicks out radiation you cannot see or feel, and the **radiation trefoil sign**, the warning mark that says nuclear radiation is present. Work with the nucleus is fenced by measurement and warning.

## The key ideas

- **Carbon-14 dates once-living remains; uranium-235 dates the age of the Earth** (U6 L1, printed p. 177).
- **Mass converts to energy — a small mass yields an enormous energy**: the sun and the nuclear bomb; **controlled fission in a reactor core generates electricity**.
- **Nuclear medicine diagnoses, traces and treats tumours**, against the harm of **tissue damage**; the **Geiger counter** and **trefoil sign** follow (printed p. 178).
