Today: **Colligative properties** — Bolivia's closing solutions unit: four shifts, one count.

Stir particles into water and four numbers move together: the vapour pressure falls, the boiling point climbs, the freezing point drops, and osmosis switches on. What sets the size of every shift? Not what the particles are — how many there are. Raoult priced the vapour: the solvent keeps only its own share of the pressure. And one count per kilogram — the molality — drags freezing down by Kf × m and pushes boiling up by Kb × m. That is why salt melts road ice: the count commands the temperature.
