# Colligative properties — Raoult's law and the freezing and boiling shifts — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Properties that depend on the number of dissolved particles and not their kind are called _____ properties.
**Answer: colligative**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Under Raoult's law, the vapour-pressure lowering equals the solute's mole fraction times the pure solvent's _____ pressure.
**Answer: vapour**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Dissolving a substance always pushes the solvent's freezing point _____ and its boiling point up.
**Answer: down**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The shift formulas use molality: moles of solute per _____ of solvent.
**Answer: kilogram**
