# Colligative properties — Raoult's law and the freezing and boiling shifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A colligative property depends on…
a) The kind of solute  b) The colour of the solute  c) The number of dissolved particles, not their kind  d) The volume of the beaker
**Answer: c) The number of dissolved particles, not their kind**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Doubling the dissolved particles…
a) Halves every shift  b) Doubles every colligative shift  c) Changes only the colour  d) Stops the shifts
**Answer: b) Doubles every colligative shift**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Raoult's law states the solution's vapour pressure as…
a) p = x(solute) × P°  b) p = P° + x(solvent)  c) p = x(solvent) × P°  d) p = P° ÷ x(solvent)
**Answer: c) p = x(solvent) × P°**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) With x(solvent) = 0.90 and P° = 23.8 mmHg, the lowering ΔP is…
a) 23.8 mmHg  b) 21.4 mmHg  c) 2.4 mmHg — the solute's share, 0.10 × 23.8  d) 0.90 mmHg
**Answer: c) 2.4 mmHg — the solute's share, 0.10 × 23.8**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A 0.50 mol/kg non-electrolyte solution in water freezes at…
a) 0 °C  b) −0.93 °C — ΔTf = 1.86 × 0.50  c) −1.86 °C  d) +0.93 °C
**Answer: b) −0.93 °C — ΔTf = 1.86 × 0.50**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The concentration unit in ΔTf = Kf × m and ΔTb = Kb × m is…
a) Molarity, mol per litre of solution  b) Percent by mass  c) Molality, mol per kilogram of solvent  d) Mole fraction
**Answer: c) Molality, mol per kilogram of solvent**
