# Hands-on — Catalysis and enzymes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the catalyst (paper only)
Three reaction stories are written out: in one a pinch of solid is recovered unchanged, in one a gas is consumed into the product, in one a lamp drives the change. For each, apply the chapter's three marks and rule catalyst or not — naming the light case with the book's own verdict.

## Phase sorting (paper only)
List four catalysis cases — a dissolved catalyst in a solution reaction, a solid under gases, an enzyme in a cell, a solid in a liquid. Sort them homogeneous/heterogeneous in a two-column table, one line of justification each.
