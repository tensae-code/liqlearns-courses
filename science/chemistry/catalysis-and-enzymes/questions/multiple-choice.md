# Catalysis and enzymes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A catalyst appears in the bilan equation…
a) As a reactant  b) As a product  c) Never  d) Twice
**Answer: c) Never**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A catalyst is used in…
a) Equal amount to the reactants  b) Tiny quantity  c) Double amount  d) No quantity at all — it is absent
**Answer: b) Tiny quantity**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Enzymes are…
a) Mineral catalysts  b) Biological protein catalysts  c) Lights  d) Acids always
**Answer: b) Biological protein catalysts**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Heterogeneous catalysis means the catalyst is…
a) In the same phase as the mixture  b) In a different phase from the mixture  c) A gas always  d) Dissolved
**Answer: b) In a different phase from the mixture**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Light, in the book's ruling, is…
a) The best catalyst  b) A catalyst when strong  c) Not a catalyst  d) An enzyme
**Answer: c) Not a catalyst**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A catalyst speeds…
a) Formation only  b) Disappearance only  c) Both formation and disappearance  d) Neither
**Answer: c) Both formation and disappearance**
