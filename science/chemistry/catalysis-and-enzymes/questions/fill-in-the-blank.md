# Catalysis and enzymes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A _____ speeds a reaction while appearing in no bilan equation.
**Answer: catalyst**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Catalysis with the catalyst in another phase is called _____.
**Answer: heterogeneous**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Enzymes are biological _____ catalysts.
**Answer: protein**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) _____ is explicitly ruled out as a catalyst in the 7AS book.
**Answer: Light**
