# Catalysis and enzymes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the catalyst's three marks from the chapter.
**Answer: It speeds the reaction (formation and disappearance), it appears in no bilan equation, and it acts in tiny quantity.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Distinguish homogeneous from heterogeneous catalysis.
**Answer: Homogeneous: catalyst and reacting mixture share one phase. Heterogeneous: the catalyst is in a different phase from the mixture.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What are enzymes, and what is light not?
**Answer: Enzymes are biological protein catalysts. Light is not a catalyst — the book rules it out explicitly.**
