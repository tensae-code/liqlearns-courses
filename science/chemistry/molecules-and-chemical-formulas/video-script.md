Today: **Molecules and chemical formulas**.

H₂O. Tiny writing — but it tells you a whole molecule. The letters are the elements: H for hydrogen, O for oxygen. The little numbers count the atoms: H₂ means two hydrogens; no number means just one. So water is two hydrogen atoms joined with one oxygen. CO₂: one carbon, two oxygens. CH₄: one carbon, four hydrogens. And now sort them: a formula using one kind of atom only, like H₂, is a simple body. A formula using several kinds, like water, is a compound body. Count the symbols, and you can read any formula chemistry writes.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
