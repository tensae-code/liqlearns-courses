# Hands-on — Molecules and chemical formulas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Model counting
Build or draw the guide's three molecules as joined spheres (paper circles do): H₂O, CO₂, CH₄. Colour or label each sphere with its element symbol, then under each model write the formula and the atom count you read off your own model. Swap with a partner: their count from your model must match your formula, or one of you recounts aloud, sphere by sphere.

## Formula reading drill
Write five formulas on cards — include H₂ and N₂ among them — and for each card a partner must say: the elements present, the atoms of each, and simple or compound. The reader may only use the rule: symbol, subscript, count 1 when there is none.
