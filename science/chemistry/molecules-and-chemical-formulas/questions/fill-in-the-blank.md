# Molecules and chemical formulas — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The small number written low after a symbol in a formula is a _____.
**Answer: subscript**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) H₂O holds _____ hydrogen atoms.
**Answer: two**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A body built of one kind of atom only, like N₂, is a _____ body.
**Answer: simple**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) CH₄ holds one carbon atom and _____ hydrogen atoms.
**Answer: four**
