# Molecules and chemical formulas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Read CH₄ aloud as a counting sentence, and say whether methane is a simple or a compound body, with the reason.
**Answer: One carbon atom and four hydrogen atoms. Methane is a compound body, because its molecules hold two different kinds of atom — carbon and hydrogen.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A pupil reads CO₂ as 'two carbons and one oxygen.' Correct the reading and state the rule the pupil reversed.
**Answer: CO₂ is one carbon atom and two oxygen atoms. The subscript belongs to the symbol just before it: C has no subscript, so 1; O carries the 2.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Sort H₂, N₂, H₂O and CO₂ into simple and compound, using the symbol-count rule.
**Answer: Simple: H₂ and N₂ (one different symbol each). Compound: H₂O and CO₂ (two different symbols each).**
