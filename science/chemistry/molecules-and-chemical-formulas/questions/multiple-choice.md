# Molecules and chemical formulas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In H₂O, the molecule holds…
a) 1 hydrogen and 2 oxygen  b) 2 hydrogen and 1 oxygen  c) 2 water atoms  d) 1 hydrogen and 1 oxygen
**Answer: b) 2 hydrogen and 1 oxygen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) CO₂ counted atom by atom is…
a) 2 carbon and 1 oxygen  b) 1 carbon and 2 oxygen  c) 1 carbon dioxide atom  d) 2 oxygen only
**Answer: b) 1 carbon and 2 oxygen**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) CH₄ is made of…
a) 4 carbon and 1 hydrogen  b) 1 carbon and 4 hydrogen  c) 1 methane atom  d) 4 hydrogen only
**Answer: b) 1 carbon and 4 hydrogen**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A subscript missing after a symbol means…
a) Zero atoms  b) One atom  c) Two atoms  d) Ignore the element
**Answer: b) One atom**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Which is a simple body?
a) H₂O  b) CO₂  c) CH₄  d) H₂
**Answer: d) H₂**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A body is a compound body when its molecules hold…
a) One kind of atom  b) Several kinds of atom  c) No atoms  d) Only hydrogen
**Answer: b) Several kinds of atom**
