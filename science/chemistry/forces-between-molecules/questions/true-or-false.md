# Forces between molecules — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** When does hydrogen bonding occur — H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What causes London dispersion forces — Source, movement, changes, effects
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What do intermolecular forces explain — Boiling points and solubility
**Answer: True**

**4. ** Which force acts in all molecules — Inward pull on surface particles
