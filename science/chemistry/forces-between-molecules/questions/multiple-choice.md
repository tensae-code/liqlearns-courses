# Forces between molecules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** When does hydrogen bonding occur?
a) Two  b) No C=C bonds  c) H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair  d) –al
**Answer: c) H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair**

**2. ** What causes London dispersion forces?
a) Aluminium  b) Temporary flickering dipoles  c) Oxidation and reduction half-reactions  d) The anode
**Answer: b) Temporary flickering dipoles**

**3. ** What do intermolecular forces explain?
a) Boiling points and solubility  b) Photosynthesis  c) Heavy metals (from industry/mining)  d) Fertilised fields (agriculture)
**Answer: a) Boiling points and solubility**

**4. ** Which force acts in all molecules?
a) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ (variable)  b) Lone pairs  c) Metal properties  d) London dispersion
**Answer: d) London dispersion**
