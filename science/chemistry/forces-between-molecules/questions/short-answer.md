# Forces between molecules — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** When does hydrogen bonding occur?
**Answer: H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair**

**2. ** What causes London dispersion forces?
**Answer: Temporary flickering dipoles**

**3. ** What do intermolecular forces explain?
**Answer: Boiling points and solubility**

**4. ** Which force acts in all molecules?
**Answer: London dispersion**
