# Video script — Nitrogen fertilisers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soil tires; nitrogen leaves with every harvest.

Nitrogen fertilisers return nitrogen to soil: ammonium salts (like ammonium sulfate), urea, and DAP (di-ammonium phosphate), which delivers phosphorus too.

The unit outcomes also name aluminium sulphate among the products treated in this industrial survey.

Each fertiliser's nitrogen form and content decide its use and dose.

Now you. Say it back: Name a nitrogen fertiliser.
Answer: Urea, an ammonium salt, or DAP.
