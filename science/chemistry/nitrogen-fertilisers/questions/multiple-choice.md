# Nitrogen fertilisers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name a nitrogen fertiliser.
a) Solid → liquid → gas  b) None  c) Crystalline  d) Urea, an ammonium salt, or DAP
**Answer: d) Urea, an ammonium salt, or DAP**

**2. ** What else does DAP deliver?
a) Only slightly soluble  b) Three  c) Phosphorus  d) Soaps
**Answer: c) Phosphorus**

**3. ** What does DAP stand for?
a) Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets  b) Di-ammonium phosphate  c) Anode to cathode  d) Electrochemical
**Answer: b) Di-ammonium phosphate**

**4. ** Why do fields need nitrogen returned?
a) Harvests remove it from the soil  b) Hazardous substances  c) Subatomic particles  d) Beryllium
**Answer: a) Harvests remove it from the soil**
