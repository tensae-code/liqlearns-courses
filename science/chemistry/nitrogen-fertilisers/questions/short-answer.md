# Nitrogen fertilisers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name a nitrogen fertiliser.
**Answer: Urea, an ammonium salt, or DAP**

**2. ** What else does DAP deliver?
**Answer: Phosphorus**

**3. ** What does DAP stand for?
**Answer: Di-ammonium phosphate**

**4. ** Why do fields need nitrogen returned?
**Answer: Harvests remove it from the soil**
