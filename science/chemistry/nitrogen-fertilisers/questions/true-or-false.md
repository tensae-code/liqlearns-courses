# Nitrogen fertilisers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Name a nitrogen fertiliser. — Urea, an ammonium salt, or DAP
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What else does DAP deliver — The –OH position
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What does DAP stand for — Di-ammonium phosphate
**Answer: True**

**4. ** Why do fields need nitrogen returned — Purifies a metal at the cathode
**Answer: False**
