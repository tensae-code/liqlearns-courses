# Nitrogen fertilisers — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. Name a nitrogen fertiliser. — d
2. What else does DAP deliver? — a
3. What does DAP stand for? — b
4. Why do fields need nitrogen returned? — c

**Answer key:** 1–d: Urea, an ammonium salt, or DAP · 2–a: Phosphorus · 3–b: Di-ammonium phosphate · 4–c: Harvests remove it from the soil
