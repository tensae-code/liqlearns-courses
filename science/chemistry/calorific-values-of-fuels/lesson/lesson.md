# Calorific values of fuels — and food energy

## Do it first
Two fuels can fill the same tank and take you very different distances — and leave very different air behind. Before reading, say out loud: what two questions should be asked of any fuel before choosing it? Bulgaria's Grade 10 exercise lesson asks exactly those: how much energy, and at what cost.

## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing a fuel — Bulgaria's mix

Bulgaria's 2022 energy-carrier pie is the lesson's home data: **gas fuels 48%, biofuels 16%, imported coal 22%**, among others. Coal carries ash and harmful flue gases; liquid fuels come from petroleum fractions, with **methanol, ethanol and biodiesel classed as renewable**; **natural gas is the preferred fuel**; **biogas** is made from organic waste by oxygen-free bacteria, its residue fertilising the soil; and **hydrogen** is named the fuel of the future, giving in fuel cells **only water and energy**. Fuels are compared by **emissions per 1000 GJ** across petroleum, natural gas and coal.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ethanol loop and food caloricity

Ethanol is drawn as a **closed loop**: photosynthesis → glucose → fermentation → ethanol → combustion → CO₂ — the CO₂ the burning returns is the CO₂ the growing took, which makes ethanol an ecologically clean renewable fuel. The same energy bookkeeping turns on food: **carbohydrates 4, fats 9, proteins 4 kcal/g** — and the lesson computes a full recipe's caloricity from a composition table, gram by gram.

## The key ideas

- Judge a fuel twice: energy carried, and emissions cost (compared per 1000 GJ).
- Bulgaria 2022 (as printed): gas fuels 48%, imported coal 22%, biofuels 16%. Natural gas preferred; hydrogen gives only water and energy.
- Food energy: carbohydrates 4, fats 9, proteins 4 kcal/g. Ethanol's loop is closed: photosynthesis to combustion and back to CO₂.

## Do It Yourself
1. A food portion holds 10 g carbohydrate, 5 g fat and 8 g protein. Compute its energy. (10×4 + 5×9 + 8×4 = 40 + 45 + 32 = 117 kcal.)
2. Why is ethanol called ecologically clean in Bulgaria's drawing? (Its cycle is closed: the CO₂ released in combustion is the CO₂ taken up by photosynthesis when the crop grew.)
