# Hands-on — Calorific values of fuels — and food energy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Recipe caloricity
Take a real recipe or food label with grams of carbohydrate, fat and protein. Compute its total energy at 4/9/4 kcal/g, showing each term. Then halve the fat on paper and recompute — say how much of the drop came from fat's 9 kcal/g.

## Fuel judgement table
Build a two-question table for five fuels (coal, petrol, natural gas, ethanol, hydrogen): energy source or fraction, renewable or not, what its burning puts into the air. Finish with Bulgaria's verdicts: natural gas preferred, hydrogen the future.
