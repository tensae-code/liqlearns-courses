Today: **Calorific values of fuels — and food energy** — Bulgaria's exercise. Ask every fuel two questions: how much energy, and what does its burning cost the air? Bulgaria's own mix: gas 48%, coal 22%, biofuels 16%. Ethanol loops closed — field to tank to air and back to field. And food is fuel too: 4, 9, 4 — carbohydrate, fat, protein, kilocalories per gram.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
