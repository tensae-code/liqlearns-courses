# Calorific values of fuels — and food energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In Bulgaria's 2022 pie as printed, the largest share was…
a) Imported coal  b) Biofuels  c) Gas fuels  d) Hydrogen
**Answer: c) Gas fuels**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The fuel Bulgaria's book names as preferred is…
a) Coal  b) Natural gas  c) Petrol  d) Wood
**Answer: b) Natural gas**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Hydrogen in a fuel cell gives…
a) CO₂ and water  b) Only water and energy  c) Ash and energy  d) Methane
**Answer: b) Only water and energy**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Fats carry, per gram…
a) 4 kcal  b) 9 kcal  c) 2 kcal  d) 7 kcal
**Answer: b) 9 kcal**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The ethanol loop closes because combustion returns…
a) Glucose  b) Oxygen  c) The CO₂ that photosynthesis took up  d) Water only
**Answer: c) The CO₂ that photosynthesis took up**
