# Calorific values of fuels — and food energy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the two questions Bulgaria's lesson asks of a fuel.
**Answer: How much energy it carries (its calorific value), and what emissions and residues it costs — compared per 1000 GJ.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Compute the energy in 20 g of pure fat.
**Answer: 20 × 9 = 180 kcal.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Where does biogas come from, and what is left over?
**Answer: From organic waste broken down by bacteria without oxygen; the residue fertilises soil.**
