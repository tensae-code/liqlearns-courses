# Hands-on — Diluting a solution — C₁V₁ = C₂V₂

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The trebling taste (kitchen materials — sugar and water only)
Dissolve one spoon of sugar in a small glass of water and taste a drop. Add water until the volume is about three times the first, stir, and taste again. Record: taste weaker or stronger? Sugar added or removed during the diluting? Connect both answers to C₁V₁ = C₂V₂.

## Dilution chain (paper exercise)
Start from C₁V₁ with C₁ = 1 mol/L, V₁ = 100 mL. Dilute to 200 mL, then to 400 mL, finding C₂ at each step with the law. Write the pattern: each doubling of volume does what to the concentration?
