# Diluting a solution — C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In Syria's discovery, the sugar solution is…
a) Doubled in volume  b) Trebled in volume and tastes weaker  c) Evaporated  d) Frozen
**Answer: b) Trebled in volume and tastes weaker**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) When a solution is diluted, the amount of dissolved solute…
a) Falls to zero  b) Is unchanged  c) Doubles  d) Turns to gas
**Answer: b) Is unchanged**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The dilution law is…
a) C₁ + V₁ = C₂ + V₂  b) C₁V₁ = C₂V₂  c) C₁/V₁ = C₂V₂  d) C = n × V
**Answer: b) C₁V₁ = C₂V₂**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Trebling the volume makes the concentration…
a) Three times larger  b) A third as large  c) Unchanged  d) Zero
**Answer: b) A third as large**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The book works the law on a solution of…
a) Sugar only  b) Sodium hydroxide  c) Salt only  d) Vinegar
**Answer: b) Sodium hydroxide**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The safety rule for acids is…
a) Water into acid, quickly  b) Acid into water, always  c) Either way is safe  d) Never dilute acids
**Answer: b) Acid into water, always**
