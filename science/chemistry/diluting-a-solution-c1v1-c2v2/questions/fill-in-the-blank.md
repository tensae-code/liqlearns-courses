# Diluting a solution — C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dilution adds solvent while the _____ is conserved.
**Answer: solute**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The dilution law reads C₁V₁ = _____ .
**Answer: C₂V₂**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Trebled volume gives a taste that is _____.
**Answer: weaker**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Acid is always added to _____, never the reverse.
**Answer: water**
