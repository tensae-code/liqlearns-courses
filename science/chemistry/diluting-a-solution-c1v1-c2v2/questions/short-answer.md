# Diluting a solution — C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the tasting discovery prove solute is conserved?
**Answer: The trebled sugar solution tastes weaker yet no sugar was taken out — the same dissolved sugar is spread through three times the volume, so the amount stands while the concentration falls.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A solution of concentration 0.5 mol/L and volume 100 mL is diluted to 500 mL. Find the new concentration.
**Answer: C₂ = C₁V₁/V₂ = 0.5 × 100/500 = 0.1 mol/L — the solute amount (0.05 mol) is the same before and after.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the acid safety rule and its reason.
**Answer: Acid is always added to water, never the reverse (printed p. 100): water added to concentrated acid can boil violently at the first drop and spit acid, while acid added to a body of water spreads its heat safely.**
