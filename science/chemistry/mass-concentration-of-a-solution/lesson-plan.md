# Lesson plan — Mass concentration of a solution

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Mass concentration of a solution
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** mass concentration Cm = m/V in grams per litre, as the Djibouti Grade 8 guide computes it from real drink labels in Ch. 6 Activité 1 — Coca-Cola at 118 g/L against orange juice at 26 g/L, to answer a real question: which is the sweetest drink? ## Sub lessons
1. **The measure — mass in each litre** — **Mass concentration** answers one question about a solution: **how much dissolved substance (solute) is there in each litre of solution?** It is a mass divided by a volume: the mass of solute dissolved, spread over the volume of solution it is dissolved in.
2. **The guide's activity — reading drink labels** — Ch.
3. **Answering with the number — the sweetest drink** — With both concentrations in g/L, the activity's question answers itself: **the sweetest drink is the one with the highest mass concentration of sugar — Coca-Cola, at 118 g/L**, carrying more than four times the sugar per litre of the orange juice at 26 g/L.
