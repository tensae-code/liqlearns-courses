Today: **Mass concentration of a solution**.

Which drink is sweetest? Do not trust your tongue — measure it. Mass concentration tells you how many grams of sugar sit in each litre of drink. The formula is Cm equals m over V: mass in grams, volume in litres, answer in grams per litre. Djibouti's book reads the labels: Coca-Cola works out at 118 grams per litre. Orange juice: 26 grams per litre. Same measure, fair fight — the cola carries more than four times the sugar in every litre. That is mass concentration: one division, and the number answers the question.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
