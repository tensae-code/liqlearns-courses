# Hands-on — Mass concentration of a solution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Label work
Take two real drink labels (photograph or copy the nutrition lines). For each: extract the sugar mass and the volume it refers to, convert the volume to litres, and compute Cm = m/V in g/L. Rank the drinks by your computed concentrations and write the one-sentence verdict, the way the guide's activity does.

## Same sugar, different volumes
On paper, a fixed 59 g of sugar is dissolved to make (a) 0.5 L, (b) 1 L, (c) 2 L of drink. Compute Cm for each, and describe in words what happens to the sweetness measure as the same sugar is spread over more solution — then check your three answers against the formula.
