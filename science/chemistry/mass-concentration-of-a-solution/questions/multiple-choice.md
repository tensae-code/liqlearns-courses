# Mass concentration of a solution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Mass concentration is calculated as…
a) Cm = V / m  b) Cm = m / V  c) Cm = m × V  d) Cm = m − V
**Answer: b) Cm = m / V**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The unit of mass concentration in this lesson is…
a) mol/L  b) g/L  c) %  d) kg only
**Answer: b) g/L**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In the guide's activity, Coca-Cola's sugar concentration is…
a) 26 g/L  b) 118 g/L  c) 11.8 g/L  d) 260 g/L
**Answer: b) 118 g/L**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The orange juice in the same activity measures…
a) 118 g/L  b) 52 g/L  c) 26 g/L  d) 12 g/L
**Answer: c) 26 g/L**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) 59 g of sugar in 0.5 L of drink gives Cm =…
a) 29.5 g/L  b) 59 g/L  c) 118 g/L  d) 0.5 g/L
**Answer: c) 118 g/L**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) 'The sweetest drink', in this activity, means the drink with…
a) The biggest bottle  b) The highest mass concentration of sugar  c) The darkest colour  d) The most fizz
**Answer: b) The highest mass concentration of sugar**
