# Mass concentration of a solution — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Mass concentration is written Cm = m / _____.
**Answer: V**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) 118 g/L means 118 _____ of solute in every litre of solution.
**Answer: grams**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In the guide's activity the sweetest drink is _____.
**Answer: Coca-Cola**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Before dividing, the volume from a label must be converted to _____.
**Answer: litres**
