# Particle accelerators — the Large Hadron Collider — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Large Hadron Collider is a particle _____.
**Answer: accelerator**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In a collision, the _____ that fly out are the evidence.
**Answer: fragments**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Rutherford probed the atom with _____ particles fired at gold foil.
**Answer: alpha**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Each new probe in the unit reached _____ than the one before.
**Answer: deeper**
