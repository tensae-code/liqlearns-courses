# Particle accelerators — the Large Hadron Collider — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why does the book call the collider the modern instrument for probing the atom?
**Answer: Because it continues the unit's own method — aiming particles at matter and reading the evidence — at speeds and depths no discharge tube or gold foil could reach.**

**2.** Compare the collider's evidence with Rutherford's.
**Answer: Rutherford read alpha particles scattered by a nucleus; the collider reads the fragments produced when particles collide and break open. In both, what comes out is the evidence for what was inside.**

**3.** What does the enrichment add to the atom story?
**Answer: That the story does not end at proton, neutron and electron: probing inside the atom continues, with the Large Hadron Collider as its current frontier.**
