# Particle accelerators — the Large Hadron Collider — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Large Hadron Collider is presented in the book as…
a) A new kind of discharge tube  b) The modern instrument for probing inside the atom  c) A microscope for atoms  d) A model of the nucleus
**Answer: b) The modern instrument for probing inside the atom**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A particle accelerator works by…
a) Heating gases until they glow  b) Dissolving atoms in acid  c) Driving particles to enormous speeds and colliding them  d) Splitting light into spectra
**Answer: c) Driving particles to enormous speeds and colliding them**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Rutherford's gold-foil experiment is the collider's ancestor because both…
a) Use gold  b) Read what comes out of particles striking matter as evidence of what is inside  c) Measure pH  d) Produce electricity
**Answer: b) Read what comes out of particles striking matter as evidence of what is inside**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In a high-speed collision, the evidence is…
a) A photograph of the atom  b) The colour of the gas  c) The fragments that fly out  d) The temperature of the room
**Answer: c) The fragments that fly out**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The enrichment page sits in the unit on…
a) Acids and bases  b) Atomic structure  c) The reactivity series  d) Electrolysis
**Answer: b) Atomic structure**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Each new probe in Unit 1 reached…
a) Less than the one before  b) The same depth  c) Deeper than the one before  d) Only the surface
**Answer: c) Deeper than the one before**
