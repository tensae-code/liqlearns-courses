# Hands-on — Particle accelerators — the Large Hadron Collider

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The probe ladder (paper only)
List Unit 1's probes in order — discharge tube, cathode rays, gold foil, the collider — and beside each write what it revealed and what smaller inside it left unprobed. Finish the ladder with one sentence on what all four share.

## Read the wreckage (paper only)
A collision sends out three fragments that no one put in. In two sentences, argue from the lesson why those fragments are evidence about the inside of the particles that collided — using Rutherford's scattered alphas as your comparison.
