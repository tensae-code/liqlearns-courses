# The greenhouse effect and global warming

## Do it first
Sunlight enters; heat tries to leave; certain gases hold the door half shut. Before reading, separate two phrases people confuse: warming, and climate change.

[Picture 21782 — Infrared rays trapped by greenhouse gases.]

The greenhouse effect: greenhouse gases let sunlight in but absorb outgoing infrared radiation, warming the Earth — a natural effect that human-added gases are strengthening.

Global warming is the resulting rise in average temperature.

Climate change is the wider set of changes — rainfall, seasons, extremes — that follow from the warming. The lesson distinguishes the three terms carefully.
