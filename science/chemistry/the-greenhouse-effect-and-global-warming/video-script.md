# Video script — The greenhouse effect and global warming

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sunlight enters; heat tries to leave; certain gases hold the door half shut.

The greenhouse effect: greenhouse gases let sunlight in but absorb outgoing infrared radiation, warming the Earth — a natural effect that human-added gases are strengthening.

Global warming is the resulting rise in average temperature.

Climate change is the wider set of changes — rainfall, seasons, extremes — that follow from the warming.

Now you. Say it back: What do greenhouse gases absorb?
Answer: Outgoing infrared radiation.
