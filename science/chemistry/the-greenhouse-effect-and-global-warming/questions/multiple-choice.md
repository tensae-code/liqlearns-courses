# The greenhouse effect and global warming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What do greenhouse gases absorb?
a) Outgoing infrared radiation  b) Gamma rays  c) One-electron species only  d) +½ or −½
**Answer: a) Outgoing infrared radiation**

**2. ** What is global warming?
a) It breaks particle arrangements  b) ⇌ (double arrow)  c) Pure solids and liquids  d) The rise in average temperature
**Answer: d) The rise in average temperature**

**3. ** Is the greenhouse effect itself unnatural?
a) The spoiling of fats and oils  b) OH⁻ ions  c) No — natural, but strengthened by humans  d) No — the same ester family
**Answer: c) No — natural, but strengthened by humans**

**4. ** Climate change is broader than warming how?
a) Soda ash  b) It includes rainfall, seasons and extremes  c) Inputs (raw materials), process, products  d) Phosphorus
**Answer: b) It includes rainfall, seasons and extremes**
