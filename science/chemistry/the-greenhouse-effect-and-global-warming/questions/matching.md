# The greenhouse effect and global warming — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What do greenhouse gases absorb? — a
2. What is global warming? — b
3. Is the greenhouse effect itself unnatural? — c
4. Climate change is broader than warming how? — d

**Answer key:** 1–a: Outgoing infrared radiation · 2–b: The rise in average temperature · 3–c: No — natural, but strengthened by humans · 4–d: It includes rainfall, seasons and extremes
