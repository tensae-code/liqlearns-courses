# The greenhouse effect and global warming — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What do greenhouse gases absorb?
**Answer: Outgoing infrared radiation**

**2. ** What is global warming?
**Answer: The rise in average temperature**

**3. ** Is the greenhouse effect itself unnatural?
**Answer: No — natural, but strengthened by humans**

**4. ** Climate change is broader than warming how?
**Answer: It includes rainfall, seasons and extremes**
