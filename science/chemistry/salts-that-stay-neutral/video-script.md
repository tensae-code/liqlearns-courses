# Video script — Salts that stay neutral

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dissolve table salt and the water stays pH 7.

A salt of a strong acid and a strong base (like NaCl) gives a neutral solution.

Neither ion hydrolyses: the cation of a strong base and the anion of a strong acid have no effective reaction with water.

No H₃O⁺ or OH⁻ excess is produced, so pH stays 7.

Now you. Say it back: What pH does NaCl solution have?
Answer: 7 — neutral.
