# Salts that stay neutral — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What pH does NaCl solution have?
a) 7 — neutral  b) It breaks particle arrangements  c) ⇌ (double arrow)  d) Pure solids and liquids
**Answer: a) 7 — neutral**

**2. ** Do its ions hydrolyse?
a) No — the same ester family  b) The spoiling of fats and oils  c) OH⁻ ions  d) No — neither ion hydrolyses
**Answer: d) No — neither ion hydrolyses**

**3. ** What kind of salt stays neutral?
a) Phosphorus  b) Soda ash  c) Strong acid + strong base salt  d) Inputs (raw materials), process, products
**Answer: c) Strong acid + strong base salt**

**4. ** Why no pH change?
a) The difference between the two levels  b) No excess H₃O⁺ or OH⁻ is made  c) The neutron  d) c = λν
**Answer: b) No excess H₃O⁺ or OH⁻ is made**
