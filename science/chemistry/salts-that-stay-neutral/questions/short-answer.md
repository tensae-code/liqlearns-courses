# Salts that stay neutral — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What pH does NaCl solution have?
**Answer: 7 — neutral**

**2. ** Do its ions hydrolyse?
**Answer: No — neither ion hydrolyses**

**3. ** What kind of salt stays neutral?
**Answer: Strong acid + strong base salt**

**4. ** Why no pH change?
**Answer: No excess H₃O⁺ or OH⁻ is made**
