## Testing for ions

The unit closes by testing for named species with specific reagents (review matching set): iron(II) ions, iodide ions (silver nitrate), sulphate ions (acidified barium nitrate), nitrate ions (iron(II) sulphate and sulphuric acid — the brown-ring family; and heating with aluminium), and carbon dioxide (limewater in the section work). Each test is a reagent → observable result pair.
