# Plastics — additives, recycling codes and plastic waste — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Fillers can make up, of a plastic, as much as…
a) 5%  b) 50–60%  c) 90%  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) 50–60%**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Plasticisers are added to…
a) Make PVC rigid  b) Turn rigid PVC into artificial leather  c) Colour the polymer  d) Remove stabilisers
**Answer: b) Turn rigid PVC into artificial leather**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Code 05 stands for…
a) PET  b) PVC  c) PP — polypropylene  d) PS
**Answer: c) PP — polypropylene**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Thermoset waste is…
a) Melted and remoulded  b) Shredded mechanically into fillers  c) Burned in the open  d) Dissolved
**Answer: b) Shredded mechanically into fillers**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) The first synthetic polymer, 1907, was…
a) Nylon  b) Bakelite  c) PET  d) PVC
**Answer: b) Bakelite**
