# Lesson plan — Plastics — additives, recycling codes and plastic waste

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Plastics — additives, recycling codes and plastic waste
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A plastic is a formulated material — polymer plus fillers, plasticisers and stabilisers — and its afterlife is sorted by the codes 01–07 (Bulgaria, Grade 10 — §56, printed pp. 142–144)

## Sub lessons
1. **The recipe — what a plastic is made of** — Plastics are **formulated materials** — over **350 million tonnes a year**. Bulgaria's composition wheel: the **polymer** plus **fillers up to 50–60%** (wood flour, paper, kaolin, chalk), **plasticisers** that turn rigid PVC into artificial leather, **stabilisers** without which PVC cannot serve, accelerators and colourants. The virtues: **density 0.8–1.4, insulation, water and chemical resistance, cheap casting and pressing**. The weaknesses: **heat, hardness, cold brittleness, flammability, ageing, toxic additives**. **Bakelite (1907)** stands as the first synthetic polymer; a property table files PE, PVC, polystyrene, PTFE, PET and polypropylene.
2. **The waste reckoning — codes 01–07** — Plastic waste pollutes **cities, oceans and rivers** — Bulgaria shows a drowned bag. The exits: **recycling, repeated use, reduced use, biodegradable substitutes**. Recycling runs on **separate collection by the international codes: 01 PET, 02 PE-HD, 03 PVC, 04 PE-LD, 05 PP, 06 PS, 07 other**. And **thermoset products cannot be recycled at all** — their waste is shredded mechanically into fillers for other materials.
