# Hands-on — Plastics — additives, recycling codes and plastic waste

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Code hunt
Collect (on paper, by listing) ten plastic items from home. For each: find or recall its recycling code, name the polymer from the lesson's code list, and sort the ten into RECYCLABLE BY CODE / THERMOSET — FILLER ONLY. Count the 07s and say what 'other' costs the recycler.

## Recipe card
Write the recipe card of one plastic object: polymer, probable fillers, plasticiser or stabiliser where the lesson names them (PVC is the worked case), one virtue the recipe buys and one weakness it keeps.
