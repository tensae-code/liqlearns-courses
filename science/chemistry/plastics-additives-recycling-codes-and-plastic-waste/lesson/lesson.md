# Plastics — additives, recycling codes and plastic waste

## Do it first
The bottle in your hand is not one substance — it is a recipe. Before reading, say out loud: what besides the polymer itself might be inside a plastic, and why? Bulgaria's Grade 10 plastics lesson opens the recipe, weighs the family's virtues against its weaknesses, and follows the bottle after the bin.

## Sub lesson 1 — The recipe — what a plastic is made of

Plastics are **formulated materials** — over **350 million tonnes a year**. Bulgaria's composition wheel: the **polymer** plus **fillers up to 50–60%** (wood flour, paper, kaolin, chalk), **plasticisers** that turn rigid PVC into artificial leather, **stabilisers** without which PVC cannot serve, accelerators and colourants. The virtues: **density 0.8–1.4, insulation, water and chemical resistance, cheap casting and pressing**. The weaknesses: **heat, hardness, cold brittleness, flammability, ageing, toxic additives**. **Bakelite (1907)** stands as the first synthetic polymer; a property table files PE, PVC, polystyrene, PTFE, PET and polypropylene.

## Sub lesson 2 — The waste reckoning — codes 01–07

Plastic waste pollutes **cities, oceans and rivers** — Bulgaria shows a drowned bag. The exits: **recycling, repeated use, reduced use, biodegradable substitutes**. Recycling runs on **separate collection by the international codes: 01 PET, 02 PE-HD, 03 PVC, 04 PE-LD, 05 PP, 06 PS, 07 other**. And **thermoset products cannot be recycled at all** — their waste is shredded mechanically into fillers for other materials.

## The key ideas

- A plastic = polymer + formulation: fillers to 50–60%, plasticisers (rigid PVC → artificial leather), stabilisers, colourants.
- Virtues: light (0.8–1.4), insulating, water/chemical resistant, cheap to shape. Weaknesses: heat, cold brittleness, flammability, ageing, toxic additives.
- Recycling sorts by codes 01 PET, 02 PE-HD, 03 PVC, 04 PE-LD, 05 PP, 06 PS, 07 other. Thermosets cannot be recycled — shredded as filler.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion: the recipe — what a plastic is made of; the waste reckoning — codes 01–07 — in that order, in your own words, with the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you one question about it, and answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself
1. A drinks bottle is stamped 01 and a carrier bag 04. Name both polymers. (01 is PET; 04 is PE-LD (low-density polyethylene).)
2. Why can a Bakelite handle never be melted down for a new product? (Bakelite is a thermoset — once set it never softens again; its waste can only be shredded into filler.)
