# Groups and periods — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** each period but the first starts with an alkali metal and ends with a noble gas;
**Answer: Groups**

**2. ** period number = number of _____ shells).
**Answer: occupied**

**3. ** Groups are the vertical columns whose _____ share outer-electron patterns.
**Answer: members**

**4. ** An activity has pupils rebuild the table's logic themselves: sort the first 18 _____ by same-outer-configuration (columns appear) and by same shell count (rows appear).
**Answer: elements**
