## The five branches of chemistry

Modern chemistry is divided into five main disciplines, each defined by what it studies: physical chemistry (properties, reaction rates, energy transfers, molecular structure), organic chemistry (carbon compounds — over twenty million known, the basis of living matter), inorganic chemistry (non-carbon substances, rocks and minerals, energy/information materials), analytical chemistry (separating, identifying and quantifying what is in a sample) and biochemistry (chemical processes in living things). The scope of chemistry is then widened: explaining the natural world, careers, informed citizens — and a warning that chemistry also produces dangerous substances that harm people and the environment.
