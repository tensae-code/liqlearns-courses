# The liquid state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Where does evaporation happen?
a) An alkene  b) Williamson  c) At the surface, at any temperature  d) The –OH position
**Answer: c) At the surface, at any temperature**

**2. ** Which particles escape in evaporation?
a) It gives two replaceable H⁺  b) The fastest surface particles  c) The conjugate base  d) Acidic
**Answer: b) The fastest surface particles**

**3. ** What causes surface tension?
a) Inward pull on surface particles  b) Pressing and drying  c) Fuel, solvent or disinfectant  d) Thermoplastic
**Answer: a) Inward pull on surface particles**

**4. ** What is viscosity?
a) Singly occupy each equal orbital first  b) Effective nuclear charge and shielding  c) Valence (outer-shell) electrons  d) Resistance to flow
**Answer: d) Resistance to flow**
