# The liquid state — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Where does evaporation happen?
**Answer: At the surface, at any temperature**

**2. ** Which particles escape in evaporation?
**Answer: The fastest surface particles**

**3. ** What causes surface tension?
**Answer: Inward pull on surface particles**

**4. ** What is viscosity?
**Answer: Resistance to flow**
