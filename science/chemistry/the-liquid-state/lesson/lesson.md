# The liquid state

## Do it first
Water's surface can hold up a careful needle, and a hot day dries a puddle without boiling it. Before reading, explain both from the particles at the liquid's surface.

[Picture 21662 — Surface molecules held by inward forces.]

In a liquid, particles are close and attract each other, yet can move past one another.

Evaporation: the fastest surface particles escape into the air — it happens at any temperature, at the surface only.

Surface tension: surface particles are pulled inward by those below, tightening the surface like a skin. Viscosity is the liquid's resistance to flow.
