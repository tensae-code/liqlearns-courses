# Video script — The liquid state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Water's surface can hold up a careful needle, and a hot day dries a puddle without boiling it.

In a liquid, particles are close and attract each other, yet can move past one another.

Evaporation: the fastest surface particles escape into the air — it happens at any temperature, at the surface only.

Surface tension: surface particles are pulled inward by those below, tightening the surface like a skin.

Now you. Say it back: Where does evaporation happen?
Answer: At the surface, at any temperature.
