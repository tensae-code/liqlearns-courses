# Suspensions, solutions and colloids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** homogeneous mixtures are _____ down to atoms/ions/molecules.
**Answer: uniform**

**2. ** Suspensions carry fine solid _____ in liquid/gas and can be filtered.
**Answer: particles**

**3. ** A true solution is the _____ end of the scale: solvent (major) + solute (minor).
**Answer: homogeneous**

**4. ** Heterogeneous mixtures have _____ distinct parts;
**Answer: physically**
