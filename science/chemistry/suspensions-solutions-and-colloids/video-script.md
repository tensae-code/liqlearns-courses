Today: **Suspensions, solutions and colloids**.

Heterogeneous mixtures have physically distinct parts;

homogeneous mixtures are uniform down to atoms/ions/molecules.

Suspensions carry fine solid particles in liquid/gas and can be filtered.

A true solution is the homogeneous end of the scale: solvent (major) + solute (minor).
