## Suspensions, solutions and colloids

Heterogeneous mixtures have physically distinct parts; homogeneous mixtures are uniform down to atoms/ions/molecules. Suspensions carry fine solid particles in liquid/gas and can be filtered.

Colloids disperse insoluble particles uniformly at an in-between size: they scatter a light beam (the Tyndall effect — the practical test for a colloid), come as gel, emulsion, sol, foam and aerosol, can be made to separate by coagulation, and include association colloids built of micelles — the basis of soap/detergent cleansing action and of cloud seeding for artificial rain (both named applications). A true solution is the homogeneous end of the scale: solvent (major) + solute (minor).
