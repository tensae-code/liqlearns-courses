Today: **Water of crystallisation** — South Sudan's way: the water hidden inside the crystal, weighed out by heat.
Some crystals are built with water inside them — a fixed number of water molecules in every formula unit, written after a dot: CuSO₄·5H₂O. Heat the crystals and the water leaves: blue turns white, and the balance reads lighter. Heat to constant mass, and the mass lost is the water itself. Divide by the mass you started with and you have the percentage water of crystallisation — a number the balance, not the book, has the last word on.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
