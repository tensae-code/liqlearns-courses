# Water of crystallisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Water of crystallisation is…
a) Water wetting the outside of a crystal  b) Water built into the crystal in a fixed proportion  c) Water the salt dissolves in  d) Steam above a salt
**Answer: b) Water built into the crystal in a fixed proportion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) CuSO₄·5H₂O contains, per formula unit,…
a) Five grams of water  b) Five water molecules built into the crystal  c) Fifty waters  d) No water — the dot is decoration
**Answer: b) Five water molecules built into the crystal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The salt left after the water of crystallisation is driven off is called…
a) Hydrated  b) Anhydrous  c) Deliquescent  d) Neutral
**Answer: b) Anhydrous**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) "Heat to constant mass" means…
a) Heat once, quickly  b) Heat, cool and weigh repeatedly until the mass stops changing  c) Heat until the salt melts  d) Weigh without heating
**Answer: b) Heat, cool and weigh repeatedly until the mass stops changing**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the experiment, the water of crystallisation is found as…
a) The mass of the empty crucible  b) The mass gained on heating  c) The mass lost on heating to constant mass  d) The volume of steam
**Answer: c) The mass lost on heating to constant mass**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Percentage water of crystallisation is…
a) Mass of water ÷ mass of anhydrous salt × 100  b) Mass of water ÷ mass of hydrated salt × 100  c) Mass of salt × 100  d) A fixed 50% for all hydrates
**Answer: b) Mass of water ÷ mass of hydrated salt × 100**
