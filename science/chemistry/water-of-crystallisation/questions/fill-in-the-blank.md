# Water of crystallisation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A salt with water built into its crystal is called a _____ salt.
**Answer: hydrated**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In dot notation the water is written after a _____.
**Answer: dot**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The mass lost on heating to constant mass is the _____ of crystallisation.
**Answer: water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Percentage water = mass of water ÷ mass of _____ salt × 100.
**Answer: hydrated**
