# Hands-on — Water of crystallisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Blue to white and back
With a teacher: heat a few blue copper sulfate crystals in a dry tube until they turn white, noting any droplets on the cool wall. Let the tube cool, add two drops of water, and watch. Write the full story — hydrate, water off, anhydrous, water back — in the lesson's terms.

## Find the percentage
With a teacher: weigh a crucible and hydrated salt, heat to constant mass with weighings between heatings, and record every mass. Calculate the percentage water of crystallisation and compare it with the theory value for the salt your teacher names.
