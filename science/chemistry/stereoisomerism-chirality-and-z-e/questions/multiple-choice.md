# Stereoisomerism — chirality and Z/E — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Stereoisomers share their formula and…
a) Nothing else  b) Their connections  c) Their melting points always  d) Their names
**Answer: b) Their connections**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Chiral molecules are mirror images that…
a) Always fit exactly  b) Cannot be superimposed  c) Have different formulas  d) Are gases
**Answer: b) Cannot be superimposed**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Z/E naming replaces…
a) IUPAC  b) cis/trans for RCH=CHR′ alkenes  c) The suffix -ene  d) Numbering
**Answer: b) cis/trans for RCH=CHR′ alkenes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In the Z form, the priority groups lie…
a) Opposite  b) On the same side  c) On different carbons only  d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) On the same side**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Configuration isomerism differs from constitution isomerism in…
a) Formula  b) Connections  c) Arrangement in space  d) Mass
**Answer: c) Arrangement in space**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A left hand and a right glove fail to fit as a picture of…
a) Position isomerism  b) Chirality  c) Chain isomerism  d) Function isomerism
**Answer: b) Chirality**
