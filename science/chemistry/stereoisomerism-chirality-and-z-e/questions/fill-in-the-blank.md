# Stereoisomerism — chirality and Z/E — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Mirror molecules that cannot be superimposed show _____.
**Answer: chirality**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Same connections, different arrangement in space: _____ isomerism.
**Answer: configuration**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) For RCH=CHR′ alkenes, Z/E replaces _____/trans.
**Answer: cis**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In the _____ form the priority groups lie on the same side.
**Answer: Z**
