# Stereoisomerism — chirality and Z/E — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define chirality in the book's terms.
**Answer: Mirror-image molecules that cannot be superimposed — the molecule and its mirror are distinct stereoisomers, as left and right hands are distinct.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) When does Z/E naming apply, and what does it replace?
**Answer: For alkenes RCH=CHR′, naming the double bond's arrangement; it replaces the cis/trans naming.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Place configuration isomerism in the book's survey.
**Answer: After constitution isomerism (chain, position, function — differences of connection), configuration isomerism is the difference of arrangement in space with connections unchanged.**
