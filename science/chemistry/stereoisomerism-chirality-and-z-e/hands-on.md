# Hands-on — Stereoisomerism — chirality and Z/E

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mirror test (paper and hands)
Hold your left hand to a mirror: the image is a right hand. Try, in words and sketches, every rotation you can think of to lay the image onto your right hand palm-to-palm. Write the verdict in the lesson's terms, and name the property it demonstrates.

## Same side or opposite? (paper only)
Three RCH=CHR′ double bonds are drawn with their priority groups marked. For each, rule Z or E with the reason in one line — same side or opposite — and state what naming system the book says this replaces.
