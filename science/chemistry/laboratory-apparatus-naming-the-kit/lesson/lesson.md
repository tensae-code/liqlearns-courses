# Laboratory apparatus — naming the kit

## Do it first
Before you read: look at the picture for this lesson — the full labelled apparatus wall chart. Say out loud what you see, what you think is happening, and one question it raises. Do not try any chemical step yourself: in this course, laboratory work is observed and described first, and the safety rules come before any practical work.

## Laboratory apparatus

The standard kit is pictured and named in one large figure — beaker (mixing/stirring/heating; its markings are not precise), Erlenmeyer and Florence flasks, graduated cylinder and graduated/volumetric pipets, buret, funnel, evaporating dish, crucible and cover, test tubes with rack, holder and brush, Bunsen burner, ring stand and support, wire gauze, clay triangle, crucible tongs, utility clamp, spatula/scoopula, watch glass, wash bottle, medicine dropper — with the beaker described in prose as the model entry, and an activity asking which pieces are measuring instruments.
