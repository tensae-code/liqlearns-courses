Today: **Discovering the proton**.

Electrical neutrality predicted positive particles;

Goldstein (1886) used a perforated cathode and found rays travelling opposite to the cathode rays (canal rays);

Rutherford showed these particles are identical to the hydrogen ion — the proton, a fundamental positive particle in every nucleus.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
