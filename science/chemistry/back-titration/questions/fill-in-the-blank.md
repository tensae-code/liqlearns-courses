# Back titration — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Back titration serves substances that cannot be titrated _____.
**Answer: directly**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The first reagent is added in measured _____.
**Answer: excess**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The second titration measures the _____ reagent.
**Answer: leftover**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Reacted = added − _____.
**Answer: leftover**
