# Back titration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Back titration is needed when a substance…
a) Is a liquid  b) Cannot be titrated directly — insoluble, slow, or without a sharp end point  c) Is brightly coloured  d) Is an acid
**Answer: b) Cannot be titrated directly — insoluble, slow, or without a sharp end point**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The first step of a back titration is to add…
a) Exactly enough reagent  b) A drop of indicator only  c) A known, measured excess of a standard reagent  d) Pure water
**Answer: c) A known, measured excess of a standard reagent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The second titration measures…
a) The substance itself  b) The leftover reagent that did not react  c) The indicator  d) The solvent
**Answer: b) The leftover reagent that did not react**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The amount that reacted with the substance is…
a) Added + leftover  b) Added − leftover  c) Leftover alone  d) Added × leftover
**Answer: b) Added − leftover**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) 0.0500 mol of acid was added; 0.0200 mol was left over. The acid that reacted with the sample is…
a) 0.0700 mol  b) 0.0200 mol  c) 0.0300 mol  d) 0.0500 mol
**Answer: c) 0.0300 mol**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) A solid lump of limestone is best measured by…
a) Direct titration from a burette  b) Back titration — dissolve it in excess standard acid, then titrate the leftover  c) Weighing only, never titration  d) Indicator colour alone
**Answer: b) Back titration — dissolve it in excess standard acid, then titrate the leftover**
