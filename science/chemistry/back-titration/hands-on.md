# Hands-on — Back titration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The limestone measurement
With a teacher: weigh a small piece of limestone, drop it into a measured excess of standard acid, and when the fizzing stops, titrate the leftover acid with standard alkali. Run the three steps and report the moles of acid the rock consumed.

## Design the pair
For two substances your teacher names — one soluble and fast, one a solid — write which titration each needs and the full plan for the one needing the back route: reagent, excess, second titration, arithmetic.
