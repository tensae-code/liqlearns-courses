Today: **Back titration** — South Sudan's way: measure the leftover, and you have measured the substance.
Some substances cannot be titrated directly — a solid rock will not run from a burette, and a slow reaction shows no sharp end point. So turn the measurement around. Add a known excess of a standard reagent — more than enough — and let the substance finish reacting with it. Then titrate what is left over. Added minus leftover is what the substance consumed, and that number is the measurement.
