# Thermodynamics — spontaneity, entropy and free energy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A spontaneous process is one that…
a) Happens very quickly  b) Runs in one direction by itself under the given conditions  c) Releases heat  d) Needs a catalyst
**Answer: b) Runs in one direction by itself under the given conditions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which statement about spontaneous processes is true?
a) They are always fast  b) They are always exothermic  c) Spontaneous says nothing about speed — rusting is spontaneous and slow  d) Their reverses are also spontaneous under the same conditions
**Answer: c) Spontaneous says nothing about speed — rusting is spontaneous and slow**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Entropy measures…
a) The total heat in a body  b) How widely matter and energy are dispersed — the number of arrangements available  c) The mass of a system  d) The temperature of the surroundings
**Answer: b) How widely matter and energy are dispersed — the number of arrangements available**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The second law of thermodynamics says that in every spontaneous process…
a) The system's entropy increases  b) The entropy of the universe increases  c) Enthalpy decreases  d) Temperature rises
**Answer: b) The entropy of the universe increases**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Gibbs free energy is given by…
a) ΔG = ΔH + TΔS  b) ΔG = ΔS − TΔH  c) ΔG = ΔH − TΔS  d) ΔG = ΔH × ΔS
**Answer: c) ΔG = ΔH − TΔS**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A process with ΔH > 0 and ΔS > 0 is spontaneous…
a) At every temperature  b) At no temperature  c) Only at low temperature  d) Only when T is high enough for TΔS to outweigh ΔH
**Answer: d) Only when T is high enough for TΔS to outweigh ΔH**
