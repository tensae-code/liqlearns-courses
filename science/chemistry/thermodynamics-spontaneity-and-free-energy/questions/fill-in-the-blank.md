# Thermodynamics — spontaneity, entropy and free energy — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The reverse of a spontaneous process, under the same conditions, is _____.
**Answer: nonspontaneous**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Entropy measures the _____ of matter and energy.
**Answer: dispersal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The arrangements counted by entropy are called _____.
**Answer: microstates**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) When ΔG = 0, the system is at _____.
**Answer: equilibrium**
