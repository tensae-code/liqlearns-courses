# Isotopes and the mass spectrometer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the machine, its maker and year, and its refinement.
**Answer: The mass spectrometer — Aston's, 1919 (its helium beam photograph is reproduced); Bainbridge's refinement followed in 1933.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) List the isotope sets the lesson defines.
**Answer: Hydrogen's: ¹H, ²H, ³H. Uranium's: ²³⁴U, ²³⁵U, ²³⁸U — atoms of one element with different masses.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give three rows of the chemical/nuclear contrast in your own words.
**Answer: Any three: nuclear reactions change nuclei, chemical ones do not; nuclear deals in protons/neutrons, chemical in electrons; nuclear energies are vastly larger; nuclear equations balance mass number and atomic number; nuclear rates are unaffected by temperature, pressure and catalysts.**
