# Isotopes and the mass spectrometer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In nuclear equations, the two numbers that must balance are…
a) Mass number and atomic number  b) Mass and volume  c) Charge and temperature  d) Electrons and protons only
**Answer: a) Mass number and atomic number**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Aston's mass spectrometer (1919) did what to atoms?
a) Fused them  b) Separated them by mass  c) Made them radioactive  d) Counted their electrons
**Answer: b) Separated them by mass**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Oxygen's fractional atomic mass as printed is…
a) 16.0000 exactly  b) 15.5  c) 15.9994  d) 17.008
**Answer: c) 15.9994**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Neon's weighted-mean relative atomic mass is worked as…
a) 20.000  b) 19.8  c) 21.0  d) 20.197
**Answer: d) 20.197**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) An artificial radioisotope in the lesson is made by…
a) Bombarding ²⁷Al with a slow neutron  b) Heating aluminium  c) Dissolving aluminium in acid  d) Cooling aluminium
**Answer: a) Bombarding ²⁷Al with a slow neutron**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Nuclear reaction rates, by the contrast table, are…
a) Strongly raised by catalysts  b) Unaffected by temperature, pressure and catalysts  c) Set by concentration  d) Stopped by cooling
**Answer: b) Unaffected by temperature, pressure and catalysts**
