# Inorganic nomenclature — hydrides, hydroxides and acids — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Metallic hydrides and the derivatives of ammonia, Hydroxides — the –OH family and its names, The polyhydrated oxoacids — meta, pyro and ortho), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, naming new formulas from the three families by their construction alone, and rebuilding a formula from a name and a name from a formula, both ways, until the road runs clean in each direction.
