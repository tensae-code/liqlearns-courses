# Inorganic nomenclature — hydrides, hydroxides and acids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A binary compound of hydrogen with a metal, hydrogen standing as H⁻, is a metallic _____.
**Answer: hydride**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The group –OH travels as one ion with a single negative charge: the _____ ion.
**Answer: hydroxide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Of the three phosphoric acids, the one with the least water in its making is the _____ acid.
**Answer: meta (metafosfórico)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The pyro acid carries _____ phosphorus atoms in one molecule, because it forms from two ortho molecules with one water gone.
**Answer: two**
