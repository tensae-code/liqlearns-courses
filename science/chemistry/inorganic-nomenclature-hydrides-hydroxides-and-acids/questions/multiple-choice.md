# Inorganic nomenclature — hydrides, hydroxides and acids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a metallic hydride, hydrogen stands as…
a) H⁺  b) H⁻, the hydride ion — which is why the metal's charge fixes how many stand beside it  c) Neutral H atoms  d) OH⁻
**Answer: b) H⁻, the hydride ion — which is why the metal's charge fixes how many stand beside it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) CaH₂ is named…
a) Hidruro de calcio  b) Dihidruro de calcio — the prefix carries the count of two hydrides  c) Hidróxido de calcio  d) Ácido cálcico
**Answer: b) Dihidruro de calcio — the prefix carries the count of two hydrides**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The hydroxide group is…
a) –OH travelling as one ion, OH⁻  b) Separate O and H ions  c) H₂O  d) –COOH
**Answer: a) –OH travelling as one ion, OH⁻**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Calcium hydroxide is written Ca(OH)₂, not CaOH₂, because…
a) Calcium is heavy  b) The OH group travels as one ion, so two of them stand in brackets  c) Oxygen needs two hydrogens  d) Names always use brackets
**Answer: b) The OH group travels as one ion, so two of them stand in brackets**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Fe(OH)₃ is hidróxido de hierro (III) because…
a) It holds three OH groups  b) Iron's charge varies, and the name states this compound's charge as (III) so the formula can be rebuilt from the name  c) It was the third hydroxide discovered  d) Iron is element 3
**Answer: b) Iron's charge varies, and the name states this compound's charge as (III) so the formula can be rebuilt from the name**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) P₂O₅ drinking the most water gives…
a) Ácido metafosfórico, HPO₃  b) Ácido pirofosfórico, H₄P₂O₇  c) Ácido ortofosfórico, H₃PO₄ — three waters, the fullest-hydrated form  d) No acid at all
**Answer: c) Ácido ortofosfórico, H₃PO₄ — three waters, the fullest-hydrated form**
