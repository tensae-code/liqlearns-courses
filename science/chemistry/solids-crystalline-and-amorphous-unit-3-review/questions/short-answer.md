# Solids — crystalline and amorphous — Unit 3 review — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which solid kind has regular particle order?
**Answer: Crystalline**

**2. ** How does an amorphous solid soften?
**Answer: Over a range of temperatures**

**3. ** Give an amorphous example.
**Answer: Glass**

**4. ** How does a crystalline solid melt?
**Answer: At one sharp temperature**
