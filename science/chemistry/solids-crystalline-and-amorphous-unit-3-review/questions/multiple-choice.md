# Solids — crystalline and amorphous — Unit 3 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which solid kind has regular particle order?
a) Crystalline  b) No — the same ester family  c) The spoiling of fats and oils  d) OH⁻ ions
**Answer: a) Crystalline**

**2. ** How does an amorphous solid soften?
a) Inputs (raw materials), process, products  b) Phosphorus  c) Soda ash  d) Over a range of temperatures
**Answer: d) Over a range of temperatures**

**3. ** Give an amorphous example.
a) c = λν  b) The difference between the two levels  c) Glass  d) The neutron
**Answer: c) Glass**

**4. ** How does a crystalline solid melt?
a) It lowers the activation energy path  b) At one sharp temperature  c) Elastically — no energy lost  d) It stays constant during the change
**Answer: b) At one sharp temperature**
