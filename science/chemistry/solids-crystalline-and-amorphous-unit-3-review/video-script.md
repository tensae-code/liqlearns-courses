# Video script — Solids — crystalline and amorphous — Unit 3 review

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Quartz melts at one sharp temperature; glass softens over a range and was once poured in cathedral windows.

Crystalline solids have particles in a regular, repeating order and melt at a sharp temperature.

Amorphous solids (like glass) have disordered particles and soften over a range of temperatures.

Melting and freezing are the particle picture in reverse: order loosening, order locking.

Now you. Say it back: Which solid kind has regular particle order?
Answer: Crystalline.
