# Solids — crystalline and amorphous — Unit 3 review

## Do it first
Quartz melts at one sharp temperature; glass softens over a range and was once poured in cathedral windows. Before the review, say what particle arrangement makes the difference.

[Picture 21666 — Ordered crystal beside disordered glass.]

Crystalline solids have particles in a regular, repeating order and melt at a sharp temperature.

Amorphous solids (like glass) have disordered particles and soften over a range of temperatures.

Melting and freezing are the particle picture in reverse: order loosening, order locking. Unit 3 closes with its summary and review.
