## The key ideas

- Matter has more states than the familiar three: plasma — a hot, ionised gas of charged particles — and the exotic states reached at the extremes, among them the Bose–Einstein condensate, where particles cooled almost to absolute zero merge into a single quantum state; between solid and liquid stand the intermediate states, the liquid crystals, which flow like liquids while keeping some of a crystal's order.
