# States beyond solid, liquid and gas — plasma and BEC — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 9 book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Plasma, intermediate and exotic states (Bose–Einstein condensate, liquid crystals) introduced as distinct states of matter (Pakistan (Punjab), Grade 9 — Chemistry 9, SNC 2023, Chapter 1, printed p. 1).**

**2.** How does Pakistan (Punjab)'s Grade 11 book treat liquid crystals?
**Answer: Liquid crystals described with daily-life uses and differentiated from pure liquids and crystalline solids (Pakistan (Punjab), Grade 11 — Chemistry XI, PECTAA, Ch. 5, printed p. 91).**
