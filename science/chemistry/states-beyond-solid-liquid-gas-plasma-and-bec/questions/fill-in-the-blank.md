# States beyond solid, liquid and gas — plasma and BEC — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A _____ — a hot, ionised gas of charged particles — is a state of matter beyond solid, liquid and gas.
**Answer: plasma**

**2.** Cooled almost to absolute zero, particles can merge into a single quantum state called a Bose–Einstein _____.
**Answer: condensate**
