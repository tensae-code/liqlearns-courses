## Acids

Arrhenius acids release hydrogen ions (protons) in aqueous solution; strong acids ionise almost completely, weak acids only slightly. General properties are taught as a behaviour list: sour behaviour toward indicators (litmus, phenolphthalein, methyl orange/red, universal indicator — plus natural indicators: red cabbage extract, coloured flower petals, turmeric), reactions with active metals (hydrogen evolved), with carbonates, and with bases in neutralisation.

Common laboratory acids and methods of preparation of acids are covered in the section.3).
