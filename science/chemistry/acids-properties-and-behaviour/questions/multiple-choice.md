# Acids — properties and behaviour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) Five rules are taught with examples: nonzero digits always count;
b) These values are the bridge into Unit 5: electronegativity differences will decide bond type and…
c) Whole-molecule polarity then depends on geometry: ethane is nonpolar overall;
d) Arrhenius acids release hydrogen ions (protons) in aqueous solution;
**Answer: d) Arrhenius acids release hydrogen ions (protons) in aqueous solution;**

**2. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) Sulphur occurs as the element in underground deposits and is extracted by the Frasch process…
b) strong acids ionise almost completely, weak acids only slightly.
c) Electrons, protons and neutrons are tabulated by location, charge and mass: electron — outside the…
d) I–II (contents) and unit openings throughout.
**Answer: b) strong acids ionise almost completely, weak acids only slightly.**

**3. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) atoms of an element are identical in mass;
b) Temperature measures hotness/coldness;
c) Common laboratory acids and methods of preparation of acids are covered in the section.
d) Significant figures are the meaningful digits of a measurement, its last digit being the uncertain one.
**Answer: c) Common laboratory acids and methods of preparation of acids are covered in the section.**

**4. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) Arrhenius acids release hydrogen ions (protons) in aqueous solution;
b) Mendeleev's law: properties are a periodic function of atomic mass.
c) Concentration states amount of solute per amount of solution;
d) once formed it is indistinguishable from an ordinary covalent bond.
**Answer: a) Arrhenius acids release hydrogen ions (protons) in aqueous solution;**

**5. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) Early classifications were built on atomic mass because atoms' inner structure was not yet known.
b) Electrical neutrality predicted positive particles;
c) Atomic number Z = number of protons (the identity of the element);
d) strong acids ionise almost completely, weak acids only slightly.
**Answer: d) strong acids ionise almost completely, weak acids only slightly.**
