# How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 8 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every Grade 8 Chemistry lesson opens with…
a) Unit questions  b) Printed objectives and key words  c) The project  d) The answers
**Answer: b) Printed objectives and key words**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The bench experiments are headed…
a) “Read and copy”  b) “I try and I conclude”  c) “Watch only”  d) “Memorise”
**Answer: b) “I try and I conclude”**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Worked applications do what?
a) Repeat the objectives  b) Carry the concluded idea into solved examples  c) Replace the experiments  d) Close the book
**Answer: b) Carry the concluded idea into solved examples**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Each lesson's own check is its…
a) Enrichment box  b) Self-test  c) Title page  d) Contents list
**Answer: b) Self-test**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The Chemistry unit's closing project in this book investigates…
a) Rainfall  b) Iron rust  c) Moon phases  d) Plant growth
**Answer: b) Iron rust**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Enrichment boxes…
a) State the conclusion  b) Set the objectives  c) Stretch the idea sideways without breaking the lesson's line  d) Mark the self-test
**Answer: c) Stretch the idea sideways without breaking the lesson's line**
