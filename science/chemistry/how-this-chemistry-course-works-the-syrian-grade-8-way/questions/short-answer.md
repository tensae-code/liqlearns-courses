# How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 8 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) List the parts of the Syrian Grade 8 lesson shape in order.
**Answer: Printed objectives and key words; “I try and I conclude” bench experiments with an explicit conclusion box; worked applications; enrichment boxes; and a self-test per lesson — with unit questions and a closing project ending the unit.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How do the self-test and the unit questions differ?
**Answer: The self-test checks one lesson as the learner leaves it; the unit questions gather and check the whole unit together.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the closing project for, beyond the questions?
**Answer: It makes the learner use the unit's chemistry on a real question — here, whether water alone or air alone rusts iron — and report the findings.**
