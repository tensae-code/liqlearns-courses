# Hands-on — How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 8 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map one lesson (paper exercise)
Take any lesson from a Syrian-style book and list its parts in order — objectives, key words, try-and-conclude experiments, conclusion boxes, applications, enrichment, self-test. Mark any part your own book leaves out.

## Write a self-test (paper exercise)
For a lesson you have finished, write three self-test questions — one on a key word, one on a conclusion box, one on a worked application — then answer them a day later without looking.
