# Properties of ionic compounds — true or false (Grade 11 addition)

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Why are melting points of ionic compounds high — Strong lattice attractions in all directions
**Answer: True**

**2.** Why are ionic crystals brittle — Molten or in solution — ions move
**Answer: False**

**3.** When do ionic compounds conduct — Molten or in solution — ions move
**Answer: True**

**4.** Why doesn't solid salt conduct — Strong lattice attractions in all directions
**Answer: False**
