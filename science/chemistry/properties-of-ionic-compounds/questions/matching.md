# Properties of ionic compounds — matching (Grade 11 addition)

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. Why are melting points of ionic compounds high? — d
2. Why are ionic crystals brittle? — a
3. When do ionic compounds conduct? — b
4. Why doesn't solid salt conduct? — c
