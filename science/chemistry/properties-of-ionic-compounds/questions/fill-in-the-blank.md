# Properties of ionic compounds — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** high melting/_____ points (much energy to break the lattice);
**Answer: boiling**

**2. ** brittle — a strike shifts layers, like charges meet and repel, and the _____ shatters (pictured before/after);
**Answer: crystal**

**3. ** soluble in polar solvents ("like dissolves like"), _____ insoluble in nonpolar ones;
**Answer: generally**

**4. ** non-conducting as solids but conducting when molten or _____, when ions are free to move to the electrodes (pictured).
**Answer: dissolved**
