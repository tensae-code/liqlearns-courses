# Properties of ionic compounds — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: say what this lesson teaches — properties of ionic compounds — explain its main example, and answer its questions from the lesson text.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to work, new examples from the learner's own surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.


## Grade 11 addition depth
The Grade 11 part passes when the learner states its key ideas in their own words: Ionic compounds have high melting points: the lattice attractions are strong in every direction and take much energy to break; They are brittle: a blow shifts the lattice so like charges align, and repulsion splits the crystal along the plane; Solid ionic compounds do not conduct — the ions are locked.
