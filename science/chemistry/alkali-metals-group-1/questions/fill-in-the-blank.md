# Alkali metals — group 1 — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Group 1 metals form ions with a _____ charge of one.
**Answer: positive (+1)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The solution an alkali metal makes in water is an _____.
**Answer: alkali**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) _____ batteries power phones and electric cars.
**Answer: Lithium**
