# Alkali metals — group 1 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every alkali metal atom carries how many outer electrons?
a) 1  b) 2  c) 7  d) 8
**Answer: a) 1**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Alkali metals are stored under oil because they…
a) Evaporate quickly  b) React with air and water at once, being eager electron-losers  c) Are too valuable to see  d) Melt in daylight
**Answer: b) React with air and water at once, being eager electron-losers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Potassium in water differs from lithium in water because…
a) Potassium does not react  b) Potassium's hydrogen catches fire — reactivity increases down the group  c) Lithium explodes and potassium fizzes  d) Potassium sinks
**Answer: b) Potassium's hydrogen catches fire — reactivity increases down the group**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Alkali metals are obtained from their compounds by…
a) Carbon reduction  b) Heating alone  c) Electrolysis  d) Filtration
**Answer: c) Electrolysis**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The K of NPK fertilisers is which element's symbol?
a) Calcium  b) Potassium  c) Phosphorus  d) Sodium
**Answer: b) Potassium**
