# Alkali metals — group 1 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Write what is seen and name the products when sodium meets water.
**Answer: The sodium melts into a ball that darts on the surface, fizzing; the products are sodium hydroxide solution (an alkali) and hydrogen gas.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does every alkali-metal compound contain the metal as a +1 ion?
**Answer: Each atom holds exactly one outer electron and loses exactly that one in reactions, so the ion formed always carries a single positive charge.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does reactivity increase down group 1?
**Answer: Down the group the outer electron sits in a shell farther from the nucleus and is held more weakly, so it is lost more easily and reactions grow fiercer.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name two sodium compounds in daily life and a use for each.
**Answer: Any two of: sodium chloride (common salt — food), sodium hydroxide (soap/paper), sodium carbonate (glass/washing), sodium hydrogencarbonate (baking).**
