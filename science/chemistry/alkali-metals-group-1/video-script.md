Today: **Alkali metals — group 1** — Vietnam's way, from its Grade 12 book.

Group 1 — Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs — holds one outer electron, lost eagerly to form +1 ions; the metals are soft, light, and stored under oil.

With water they give an alkaline hydroxide + hydrogen; the reaction grows from lithium's fizz to potassium's flame and beyond — reactivity increases down the group.

The family is won by electrolysis of its compounds; no ordinary reducing agent frees it.

Sodium compounds (salt, caustic soda, washing soda, baking soda), potassium fertilisers and lithium batteries are the group's working faces.
