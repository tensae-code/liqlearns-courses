Today: **Avogadro's principle — and why water is H2O, not HO** — Italy's way, from the book's own treatment. Equal volumes of different gases, at the same temperature and pressure, contain the same number of molecules — this is Avogadro's principle. Hydrogen and oxygen are diatomic molecules, so water is H2O, not the HO Dalton supposed. Gay-Lussac's law of combining volumes: gases reacting at the same temperature and pressure combine in volume ratios of small whole numbers — hydrogen and oxygen react 2:1 to make water.
