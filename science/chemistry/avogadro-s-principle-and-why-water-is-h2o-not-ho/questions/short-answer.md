# Avogadro's principle — and why water is H2O, not HO — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about Avogadro's principle.
**Answer: Equal volumes of different gases, at the same temperature and pressure, contain the same number of molecules — this is Avogadro's principle.**

**2.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about diatomic.
**Answer: Hydrogen and oxygen are diatomic molecules, so water is H2O, not the HO Dalton supposed.**

**3.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about combining volumes.
**Answer: Gay-Lussac's law of combining volumes: gases reacting at the same temperature and pressure combine in volume ratios of small whole numbers — hydrogen and oxygen react 2:1 to make water.**
