# Avogadro's principle — and why water is H2O, not HO — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Equal volumes of different gases, at the same temperature and pressure, contain the same number of molecules — this is _____.
**Answer: Avogadro's principle**

**2.** Hydrogen and oxygen are _____ molecules, so water is H2O, not the HO Dalton supposed.
**Answer: diatomic**

**3.** Gay-Lussac's law of _____: gases reacting at the same temperature and pressure combine in volume ratios of small whole numbers — hydrogen and oxygen react 2:1 to make water.
**Answer: combining volumes**
