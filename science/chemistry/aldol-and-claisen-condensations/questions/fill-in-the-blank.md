# Aldol and Claisen condensations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The _____ builds carbon–carbon bonds, and the guide derives it from the α-hydrogen's acidity.
**Answer: aldol condensation**

**2.** The _____ stands in the deepening list beside transesterification and the mechanism of nucleophilic addition to the carbonyl carbon.
**Answer: Claisen condensation**

**3.** _____ formation from carbonyl compounds and ammonia derivatives belongs to the same condensation family in the guide's map.
**Answer: Imine**
