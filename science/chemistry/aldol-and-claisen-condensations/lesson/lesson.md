# Aldol and Claisen condensations

## Do it first
Before reading, say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is: aldol and claisen condensations. Then read on — this is Italy's way of teaching it, as the Italy placements describe the book's own treatment.

## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo C, Capitolo 8, §3 and Per approfondire — Italy's book (Grade 13)

Carbon–carbon bond formation by condensation: the aldol condensation derived from the α-hydrogen's acidity, imine formation from carbonyl + ammonia derivatives, and in the deepening list the Claisen condensation, transesterification and the mechanism of nucleophilic addition to the carbonyl carbon.

## The key ideas

- The aldol condensation builds carbon–carbon bonds, and the guide derives it from the α-hydrogen's acidity.
- The Claisen condensation stands in the deepening list beside transesterification and the mechanism of nucleophilic addition to the carbonyl carbon.
- Imine formation from carbonyl compounds and ammonia derivatives belongs to the same condensation family in the guide's map.
