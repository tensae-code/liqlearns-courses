# Shapes of s, p and d orbitals — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What shape is an s orbital?
**Answer: A sphere**

**2. ** How many p orbitals are there, and how are they set?
**Answer: Three, along x, y and z**

**3. ** What shape is a p orbital?
**Answer: A dumbbell (two lobes)**

**4. ** What does an orbital shape represent?
**Answer: A probability region**
