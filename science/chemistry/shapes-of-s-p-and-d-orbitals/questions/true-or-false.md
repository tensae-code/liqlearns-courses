# Shapes of s, p and d orbitals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What shape is an s orbital — A sphere
**Answer: True**

**2. ** How many p orbitals are there, and how are they set — A pH range of about two units
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What shape is a p orbital — A dumbbell (two lobes)
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What does an orbital shape represent — The atoms themselves change
**Answer: False**
