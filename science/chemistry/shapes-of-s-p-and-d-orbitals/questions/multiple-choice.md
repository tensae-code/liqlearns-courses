# Shapes of s, p and d orbitals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What shape is an s orbital?
a) A sphere  b) Forward, making products  c) The carbonyl group, C=O  d) The short-chain ones
**Answer: a) A sphere**

**2. ** How many p orbitals are there, and how are they set?
a) By exactly one proton  b) In steps, each with its own Ka  c) No excess H₃O⁺ or OH⁻ is made  d) Three, along x, y and z
**Answer: d) Three, along x, y and z**

**3. ** What shape is a p orbital?
a) Hydration with water  b) Water  c) A dumbbell (two lobes)  d) Brine
**Answer: c) A dumbbell (two lobes)**

**4. ** What does an orbital shape represent?
a) On the right of the table  b) A probability region  c) The uncertainty principle  d) The orbital's orientation
**Answer: b) A probability region**
