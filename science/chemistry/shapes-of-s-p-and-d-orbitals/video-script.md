# Video script — Shapes of s, p and d orbitals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An orbital is not a path — it is a region where the electron is likely to be found.

An s orbital is a sphere around the nucleus.

A p orbital is a dumbbell with two lobes, and there are three of them, pointing along the x, y and z axes.

d orbitals have more complex shapes, and f orbitals more complex still.

Each shape is a probability region — the electron is found inside it most of the time, not on a drawn track.

Now you. Say it back: What shape is an s orbital?
Answer: A sphere.
