# Air-bag chemistry — sodium azide — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) The air-bag solid is sodium _____.
**Answer: azide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The gas that fills the bag is _____.
**Answer: nitrogen**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) 2 mol of NaN₃ give _____ mol of N₂.
**Answer: 3**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The bag is sized by a _____ calculation.
**Answer: stoichiometry**
