# Air-bag chemistry — sodium azide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The gas that fills an air bag comes from…
a) A compressed-gas tank  b) The car's exhaust  c) A chemical reaction in the crash instant  d) The tyres
**Answer: c) A chemical reaction in the crash instant**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The air-bag reaction uses…
a) Sodium chloride  b) Sodium azide  c) Sodium hydroxide  d) Sodium carbonate
**Answer: b) Sodium azide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) 2NaN₃ → 2Na + 3N₂: 2 mol of azide give…
a) 2 mol of nitrogen  b) 1 mol of nitrogen  c) 3 mol of nitrogen  d) 6 mol of nitrogen
**Answer: c) 3 mol of nitrogen**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The bag's fixed volume decides first…
a) The colour of the bag  b) The moles of nitrogen needed  c) The mass of the car  d) The speed of the crash
**Answer: b) The moles of nitrogen needed**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Too little sodium azide loaded means…
a) A faster bag  b) An under-filled bag  c) A heavier bag  d) Nothing changes
**Answer: b) An under-filled bag**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) The balanced equation in this lesson works as…
a) A decoration  b) A measuring instrument for the charge  c) A safety warning  d) A list of products only
**Answer: b) A measuring instrument for the charge**
