# Air-bag chemistry — sodium azide — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why can the air bag not simply carry its gas ready-made?
**Answer: The gas must arrive in a fraction of a second in a known volume from something small and shelf-stable; a stored charge of sodium azide that decomposes on signal stores the gas in compact solid form and releases it by reaction.**

**2.** Read the azide equation as a measuring instrument.
**Answer: 2NaN₃ → 2Na + 3N₂ fixes the ratio: from the moles of nitrogen the bag needs, multiply by 2/3 to get the moles of azide, then by its molar mass for the charge to load.**

**3.** What two failures does exact stoichiometry prevent?
**Answer: Under-filling (too little azide, too little nitrogen) and over-filling beyond the bag's design (too much).**
