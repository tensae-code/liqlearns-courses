# Hands-on — Air-bag chemistry — sodium azide

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Size a bag (paper only)
A demonstration bag needs 3.0 mol of N₂. Using the ratio 2 NaN₃ : 3 N₂, calculate the moles of sodium azide to load, then its mass (Mr of NaN₃ = 65). Show the two steps — ratio first, mass second — as the lesson orders them.

## Design brief (paper only)
Write the air bag's design brief in four lines from the lesson: the time limit, the fixed volume, the shelf demand on the stored chemical, and the one calculation that sizes it. Under it, name the failure on each side of the exact charge.
