# Hess's law and heats of formation and combustion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A heat of formation is the heat of forming…
a) 1 g of a compound  b) 1 mol of a compound from its stable elements  c) Any amount of an element  d) A compound from another compound
**Answer: b) 1 mol of a compound from its stable elements**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At the standard conditions Bulgaria uses, the temperature is…
a) 273 K  b) 298 K  c) 373 K  d) 100 K
**Answer: b) 298 K**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Hess's law says a reaction's heat effect depends only on…
a) The catalyst  b) The path taken  c) The initial and final states  d) The speed
**Answer: c) The initial and final states**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) From 393.5 = Q₂ + 282.5, the formation heat of CO is…
a) 676 kJ/mol  b) 282.5 kJ/mol  c) 111 kJ/mol  d) 393.5 kJ/mol
**Answer: c) 111 kJ/mol**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A reaction's heat effect equals formation heats of products minus those of reactants, each…
a) Divided by its moles  b) Weighted by its moles  c) Squared  d) Ignored
**Answer: b) Weighted by its moles**
