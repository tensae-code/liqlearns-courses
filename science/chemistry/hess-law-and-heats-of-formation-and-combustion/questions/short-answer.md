# Hess's law and heats of formation and combustion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define heat of combustion as Bulgaria's book does.
**Answer: The heat released when a substance burns completely to the highest oxides.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State Hess's law and the law it stands on.
**Answer: The heat effect depends only on the initial and final states, not the path — a consequence of the conservation of energy.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A compound decomposes with the same heat magnitude as its formation. Why?
**Answer: Formation and decomposition swap the same initial and final states, so by Hess's law (and energy conservation) the heats are equal in size, opposite in direction.**
