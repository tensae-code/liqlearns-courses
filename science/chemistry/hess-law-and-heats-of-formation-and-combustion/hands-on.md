# Hands-on — Hess's law and heats of formation and combustion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## The two-path ledger
On paper, draw graphite's two routes to CO₂ as a ledger: direct (393.5) against via-CO (Q₂ + 282.5). Solve for the missing step, then check by finding a second pair of routes in this course (a fuel burned directly vs its formation-then-burning) and balancing its ledger the same way.

## Formation-heat sums
Using Bulgaria's table values named in the lesson (NH₃ +46, Al₂O₃ +1670 kJ/mol in the released-positive convention), write the corollary sum for one reaction of your choosing from another lesson, term by term, products first, reactants second, each weighted by its moles.
