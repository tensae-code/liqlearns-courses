Today: **Hess's law and heats of formation and combustion** — Bulgaria's thermochemistry. Formation heat: one mole built from the stable elements — the elements themselves count zero. Combustion heat: burning complete, to the highest oxides. And Hess's law: the heat cares only where you start and where you finish — graphite reaches CO₂ by either road for the same 393.5. So hard steps, like CO's 111, are found by subtraction.
