# Types of organic reactions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Swapping one atom or group in a molecule for another is called _____.
**Answer: substitution**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A reaction in which the carbon skeleton reorders into an isomer, with nothing added or lost, is a _____.
**Answer: rearrangement (isomerisation)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A bond breaking unevenly, both electrons going to one fragment, is _____ fission.
**Answer: heterolytic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Ethene reacting with bromine is electrophilic _____.
**Answer: addition**
