# Types of organic reactions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ethene + Br₂ → dibromoethane is an example of…
a) Substitution  b) Addition  c) Elimination  d) Rearrangement
**Answer: b) Addition**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Ethanol → ethene + H₂O is an example of…
a) Elimination — a small molecule removed, a double bond formed  b) Addition  c) Substitution  d) Polymerisation only
**Answer: a) Elimination — a small molecule removed, a double bond formed**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Homolytic fission of a bond gives…
a) Two ions  b) Two radicals — each fragment keeping one electron of the pair  c) One radical and one ion  d) A double bond
**Answer: b) Two radicals — each fragment keeping one electron of the pair**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A nucleophile is…
a) An electron-pair acceptor  b) A particle with an unpaired electron  c) An electron-pair donor that seeks electron-poor centres  d) A neutral solvent
**Answer: c) An electron-pair donor that seeks electron-poor centres**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The chlorination of methane in sunlight is fully classified as…
a) Electrophilic addition  b) Free-radical substitution  c) Nucleophilic substitution  d) Elimination
**Answer: b) Free-radical substitution**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Belarus places this classification…
a) In the first chapter  b) In the closing generalisation part (Part 10), re-sorting the whole organic year  c) In an appendix of answers  d) In the Grade 10 book
**Answer: b) In the closing generalisation part (Part 10), re-sorting the whole organic year**
