Today: **Types of organic reactions** — Belarus's way: the organic year closed by sorting its own reactions — two axes, every reaction filed.

Start with a shelf of dozens of reactions from the whole organic year. Belarus ends the book by sorting them. First axis — what happens to the molecule: addition, substitution, elimination, rearrangement, and oxidation against reduction. Five families; everything files somewhere.
Second axis — how the bond breaks on the way. Evenly, and the fragments are radicals that run chain reactions. Unevenly, and the fragments are ions — from which come the nucleophiles that donate electron pairs and the electrophiles that accept them.
So every reaction earns two names: free-radical substitution for methane and chlorine, electrophilic addition for ethene and bromine. Hold both axes, and the shelf of chapters becomes one subject with a system.
