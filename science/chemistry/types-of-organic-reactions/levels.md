# Types of organic reactions — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Sorting by what happens to the molecule, Sorting by how the bond breaks, Using the system — every reaction has two names), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, filing new reactions on both axes, and explaining how conditions switch one reactant pair between substitution and elimination.
