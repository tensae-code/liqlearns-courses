# Hands-on — Types of organic reactions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The great re-sort (paper only)
Write these eight reactions on cards: ethene + H₂; methane + Cl₂ (sunlight); ethanol → ethene; chloroethane + OH⁻ → ethanol; propene + HBr; ethanoic acid from ethanol (oxidation); bromoethane → ethene (hot alkali); a straight-chain alkane re-knitting branched. File each card into the five by-result families, saying the bookkeeping mark out loud for each filing. Note the two cards whose family changes with conditions and say what chooses between them.

## Two names for every reaction
Take the three reactions worked in sub lesson 3 — methane chlorination, ethene bromination, bromoethane with hydroxide — and write each one's full two-axis name on a card: result family first, mechanism family second. Shuffle the cards, then re-derive each name from the equation alone, checking against the lesson.

## Break the bond both ways
Draw a Cl–Cl bond and a C–Br bond on paper. For each, draw the homolytic breaking (one arrow each way, radicals marked) and the heterolytic breaking (both electrons to one fragment, ions marked). Under each drawing, name one real reaction from the year that runs on that pattern.
