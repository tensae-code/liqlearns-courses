# Making carboxylic acids — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Oxidising an aldehyde gives…
**Answer: A carboxylic acid**

**2. ** What is the oxidation ladder?
**Answer: Alcohol → aldehyde → acid**

**3. ** Which alcohol type oxidises to an acid?
**Answer: A primary alcohol**

**4. ** Name another route to acids.
**Answer: Hydrolysis (as presented)**
