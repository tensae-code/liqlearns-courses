# Making carboxylic acids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Oxidising an aldehyde gives…
a) An electron-pair acceptor  b) Le Chatelier's principle  c) A carboxylic acid  d) An exercise of the same kind
**Answer: c) A carboxylic acid**

**2. ** What is the oxidation ladder?
a) Molasses  b) Alcohol → aldehyde → acid  c) Weeds  d) Silica — the glass former
**Answer: b) Alcohol → aldehyde → acid**

**3. ** Which alcohol type oxidises to an acid?
a) A primary alcohol  b) Only certain sharp lines  c) Electron diffraction  d) A dumbbell (two lobes)
**Answer: a) A primary alcohol**

**4. ** Name another route to acids.
a) Change in amount/concentration per unit time  b) No — it is dynamic, both ways continue  c) Initial, Change, Equilibrium  d) Hydrolysis (as presented)
**Answer: d) Hydrolysis (as presented)**
