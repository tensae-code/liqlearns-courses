# Video script — Making carboxylic acids

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The oxidation ladder from the alcohol lesson had one more rung.

Oxidation of a primary alcohol, carried past the aldehyde, gives the carboxylic acid; oxidation of an aldehyde gives the same acid directly.

Hydrolysis routes (of nitriles/esters as presented) also yield acids.

The ladder alcohol → aldehyde → acid is the section's organising picture.

Now you. Say it back: Oxidising an aldehyde gives…
Answer: A carboxylic acid.
