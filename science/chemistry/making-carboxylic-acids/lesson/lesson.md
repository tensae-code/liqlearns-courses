# Making carboxylic acids

## Do it first
The oxidation ladder from the alcohol lesson had one more rung. Before reading, climb it: primary alcohol → ? → acid.

[Picture 21698 — The oxidation ladder, three rungs.]

Oxidation of a primary alcohol, carried past the aldehyde, gives the carboxylic acid; oxidation of an aldehyde gives the same acid directly.

Hydrolysis routes (of nitriles/esters as presented) also yield acids.

The ladder alcohol → aldehyde → acid is the section's organising picture.

## Say it to your companion
Explain Making carboxylic acids to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.

## Do It Yourself
1. Write the lesson's key ideas in your own words, in a list, without opening the lesson.
2. Work one example of your own for each key idea — your own numbers, your own substances, the lesson's own rules.
3. Check your examples against the lesson. Fix anything that disagrees, and say what rule it broke.
4. Teach the hardest idea in the lesson to someone else, out loud, once.
