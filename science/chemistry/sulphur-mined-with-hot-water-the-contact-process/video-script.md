Today: **Sulphur — mined with hot water; the Contact Process**.

Sulphur occurs as the element in underground deposits and is extracted by the Frasch process (superheated water melts it in place and air lifts it out).

It burns/reacts with oxygen to sulphur dioxide and sulphur trioxide — the feedstock chain for manufacturing sulphuric acid by the Contact Process.
