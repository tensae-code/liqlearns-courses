# Metals and the reactivity series — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** The reactivity series orders metals and predicts behaviour (_____ which liberate hydrogen from dilute hydrochloric acid — copper does not — and which occur free in nature — gold does);
**Answer: including**

**2. ** extraction method follows reactivity: the most active metals can be won from their compounds only by _____, since no chemical reducing agent is stronger.
**Answer: electrolysis**

**3. ** Active metals occur only as compounds (_____/ores).
**Answer: minerals**
