## Metals and the reactivity series

About 80% of elements are metals, sharing the physical set (ductile, malleable, sonorous, lustrous, conducting heat and electricity) and the chemical set (reducing agents, forming basic oxides), while specific properties vary widely. Active metals occur only as compounds (minerals/ores). The reactivity series orders metals and predicts behaviour (including which liberate hydrogen from dilute hydrochloric acid — copper does not — and which occur free in nature — gold does);

extraction method follows reactivity: the most active metals can be won from their compounds only by electrolysis, since no chemical reducing agent is stronger.1–5.2.1).
