# Systems, surroundings and specific heat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Fix the vocabulary: system, surroundings, boundary — one line each.
**Answer: System: the part under study (the flask's contents). Surroundings: everything outside it that can exchange heat with it. Boundary: the surface dividing them, across which heat may or may not pass.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Distinguish adiabatic from isothermal, and heat from temperature.
**Answer: Adiabatic: no heat crosses the boundary (temperature may change). Isothermal: temperature stays constant (heat may cross). Heat is energy in flow; temperature is the reading that heat flow changes.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Work the copper block: state the data and the result as the book gives them.
**Answer: 95.49 g of copper absorbs 849 J as it warms from 25 °C to 48 °C; dividing heat by mass × rise gives the specific heat 0.387 J/g·°C.**
