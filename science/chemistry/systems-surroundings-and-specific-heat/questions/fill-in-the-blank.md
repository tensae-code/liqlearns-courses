# Systems, surroundings and specific heat — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Energy is never made or destroyed — it obeys the _____ law.
**Answer: conservation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) No heat crosses the boundary in an _____ change.
**Answer: adiabatic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Specific heat prices the warming of one _____ by one degree.
**Answer: gram**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A gram of iron warms about _____ times faster than a gram of water.
**Answer: nine**
