# Systems, surroundings and specific heat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Chemical energy, in the book's definition, is…
a) Potential energy stored in a substance's structure  b) Heat already released  c) The temperature of a substance  d) Electricity in a battery
**Answer: a) Potential energy stored in a substance's structure**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An adiabatic change is one where…
a) Temperature stays constant  b) No heat crosses the boundary  c) No work is done  d) The system is open
**Answer: b) No heat crosses the boundary**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Isothermal means…
a) No heat crosses  b) Constant pressure only  c) Constant temperature  d) No boundary
**Answer: c) Constant temperature**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Specific heat is the heat needed to raise…
a) One mole by ten degrees  b) Any mass to boiling  c) One litre by one degree  d) One gram of a substance by one degree
**Answer: d) One gram of a substance by one degree**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The worked copper block's specific heat comes out at…
a) 0.387 J/g·°C  b) 4.18 J/g·°C  c) 0.449 J/g·°C  d) 849 J/g·°C
**Answer: a) 0.387 J/g·°C**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Water's specific heat, the table's highest common value, is…
a) 0.449 J/g·°C  b) 4.18 J/g·°C  c) 1.00 J/g·°C  d) 2.06 J/g·°C
**Answer: b) 4.18 J/g·°C**
