# Hands-on — Systems, surroundings and specific heat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the parts (paper only)
Draw the book's flask: mixture inside, flask, bench, air. Draw the boundary as a dashed line and label system, surroundings, boundary. Then write two captions: one for an adiabatic version of the flask (what changes in the drawing?) and one for an isothermal run (what must the surroundings do?).

## The nine-times table (paper only)
From the lesson's two numbers — water 4.18, iron 0.449 J/g·°C — calculate how much heat raises 10 g of each by 5 °C, and write the pair of answers as the table's lesson in one sentence: same heat, ninefold difference in warmth.
