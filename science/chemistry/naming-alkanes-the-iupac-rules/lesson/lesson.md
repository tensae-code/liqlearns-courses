## Naming alkanes

The naming algorithm is taught as rules: choose the longest continuous carbon chain as parent; name branches as alkyl substituents; number the chain to give substituents the lowest numbers;

list substituents alphabetically with di-/tri- multipliers (2-methylheptane; 4-ethyl-2,2-dimethylhexane; 3,3-diethyl-2,5-dimethylnonane are the review's workout set) — including why plausible-looking names are wrong (wrong parent chain, wrong numbering direction, misplaced multipliers).
