## Testing oxides with indicators

Printed experiments make the classification visible: burning sulphur and magnesium/calcium, dissolving the products in water and testing with universal indicator and blue/red litmus to show one solution acidic and the other basic; and aluminium oxide treated with both hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide, its indicator behaviour proving it amphoteric. Indicators are defined here as the testing tools (universal indicator, litmus) whose colour changes signal acid/base character.
