Today: **Biodegradable and non-biodegradable polymers** — the closing sorting of Fiji's polymer strand.

Some polymers the living world can eat: micro-organisms and their enzymes break down starch, cellulose and polyesters, and the material goes back into the cycles. Others persist — polythene, nylon, PVC, polypropene, Teflon — and stay where they fall, harming land and marine life, giving off toxic emissions when they weather and burn, and costing more to recycle than they easily repay.

A polymer's ending is decided by its chemistry, on the day it is made.
