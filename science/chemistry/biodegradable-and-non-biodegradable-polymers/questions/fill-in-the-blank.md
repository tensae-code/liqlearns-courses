# Biodegradable and non-biodegradable polymers — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Biodegradable polymers are broken down by micro-organisms and their _____.
**Answer: enzymes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Two natural biodegradable polymers Fiji names are starch and _____.
**Answer: cellulose**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A polymer the living world cannot break down is called _____.
**Answer: non-biodegradable**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Burning persisting plastics gives off _____ emissions.
**Answer: toxic**
