# Biodegradable and non-biodegradable polymers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A biodegradable polymer is one that…
a) Melts in sunlight  b) Micro-organisms and enzymes can break down  c) Is always natural  d) Cannot be made into objects
**Answer: b) Micro-organisms and enzymes can break down**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is in Fiji's biodegradable family?
a) Polythene  b) PVC  c) Starch  d) Teflon
**Answer: c) Starch**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A non-biodegradable polymer…
a) Breaks down in a season  b) Persists — the living world cannot break it down  c) Is always a liquid  d) Is made by plants
**Answer: b) Persists — the living world cannot break it down**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which set is Fiji's non-biodegradable family?
a) Starch, cellulose, polyesters  b) Polythene, nylon, PVC, polypropene, Teflon  c) Wool, silk, starch  d) PVC, starch, nylon
**Answer: b) Polythene, nylon, PVC, polypropene, Teflon**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) One problem of the persisting polymers the book names is…
a) They are too cheap to make  b) Harm to land and marine life from accumulated pollution  c) They melt too easily  d) They cannot be coloured
**Answer: b) Harm to land and marine life from accumulated pollution**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Burning persisting plastics as disposal is a problem because…
a) It is slow  b) It gives off toxic emissions  c) It makes them biodegradable  d) It costs nothing
**Answer: b) It gives off toxic emissions**
