# Video script — Reactions of alcohols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethanol burns, fizzes gently on sodium, and can lose water two different ways.

Alcohols burn (combustion) to carbon dioxide and water.

With sodium they give a sodium alkoxide and hydrogen gas — a gentler version of sodium with water.

Dehydration removes water: one molecule gives an alkene; two molecules can give an ether.

Now you. Say it back: What does ethanol burn to?
Answer: Carbon dioxide and water.
