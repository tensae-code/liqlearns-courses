# Reactions of alcohols — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What does ethanol burn to? — b
2. What gas comes off with sodium? — c
3. Dehydrating one alcohol molecule gives… — d
4. A secondary alcohol oxidises to… — a

**Answer key:** 1–b: Carbon dioxide and water · 2–c: Hydrogen · 3–d: An alkene · 4–a: A ketone
