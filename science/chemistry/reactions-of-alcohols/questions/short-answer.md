# Reactions of alcohols — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does ethanol burn to?
**Answer: Carbon dioxide and water**

**2. ** What gas comes off with sodium?
**Answer: Hydrogen**

**3. ** Dehydrating one alcohol molecule gives…
**Answer: An alkene**

**4. ** A secondary alcohol oxidises to…
**Answer: A ketone**
