# Reactions of alcohols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does ethanol burn to?
a) Oxidised (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻)  b) Carbon dioxide and water  c) The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper  d) Zinc
**Answer: b) Carbon dioxide and water**

**2. ** What gas comes off with sodium?
a) Hydrogen  b) Solid wastes and agrochemicals  c) Prevention of waste  d) They are rearranged, never created or destroyed
**Answer: a) Hydrogen**

**3. ** Dehydrating one alcohol molecule gives…
a) H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair  b) Layers slide over the flowing electron sea  c) Molecular crystals  d) An alkene
**Answer: d) An alkene**

**4. ** A secondary alcohol oxidises to…
a) Two  b) No C=C bonds  c) A ketone  d) –al
**Answer: c) A ketone**
