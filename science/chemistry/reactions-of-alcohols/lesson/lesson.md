# Reactions of alcohols

## Do it first
Ethanol burns, fizzes gently on sodium, and can lose water two different ways. Before reading, map its fates: burn it, dehydrate it, oxidise it — what family does each road reach?

[Picture 21688 — A reaction map centred on ethanol.]

Alcohols burn (combustion) to carbon dioxide and water.

With sodium they give a sodium alkoxide and hydrogen gas — a gentler version of sodium with water.

Dehydration removes water: one molecule gives an alkene; two molecules can give an ether. Oxidation climbs the ladder — primary alcohols to aldehydes then carboxylic acids, secondary alcohols to ketones. Alcohols plus carboxylic acids give esters (esterification).
