# Percentage yield — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** actual yield is what an experiment really gives (_____ less);
**Answer: usually**

**2. ** percentage yield = actual ÷ theoretical × 100, and higher _____ yield means a more efficient reaction.
**Answer: percentage**

**3. ** Worked family: N₂O₅ decomposition with 40 g _____ material giving 4.5 g O₂.
**Answer: starting**

**4. ** Theoretical yield is the calculated _____ from the limiting reactant;
**Answer: maximum**
