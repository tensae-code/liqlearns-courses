Today: **Percentage yield**.

Theoretical yield is the calculated maximum from the limiting reactant;

actual yield is what an experiment really gives (usually less);

percentage yield = actual ÷ theoretical × 100, and higher percentage yield means a more efficient reaction.

Worked family: N₂O₅ decomposition with 40 g starting material giving 4.5 g O₂.
