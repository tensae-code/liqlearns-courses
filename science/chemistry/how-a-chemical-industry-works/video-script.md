# Video script — How a chemical industry works

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A plant is sited neither beside the mine nor the market by accident.

A manufacturing industry is characterised by its raw materials, its process (the chemistry run continuously), and its products and by-products.

Siting weighs raw-material supply, energy, water, transport and markets.

Inputs, process and products in general terms are the frame every later process lesson fills in.

Now you. Say it back: What three things characterise an industry?
Answer: Inputs (raw materials), process, products.
