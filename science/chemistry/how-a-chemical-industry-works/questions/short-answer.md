# How a chemical industry works — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What three things characterise an industry?
**Answer: Inputs (raw materials), process, products**

**2. ** Name a siting factor.
**Answer: Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets**

**3. ** What runs continuously in a plant?
**Answer: The process (chemistry)**

**4. ** What besides products leaves?
**Answer: By-products**
