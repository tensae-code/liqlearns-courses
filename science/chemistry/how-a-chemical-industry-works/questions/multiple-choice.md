# How a chemical industry works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What three things characterise an industry?
a) The number of neutrons  b) A continuous spectrum  c) Inputs (raw materials), process, products  d) Negative
**Answer: c) Inputs (raw materials), process, products**

**2. ** Name a siting factor.
a) The slope of the tangent  b) Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets  c) Particles are locked in place  d) At the surface, at any temperature
**Answer: b) Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets**

**3. ** What runs continuously in a plant?
a) The process (chemistry)  b) Flavourings and perfumes  c) An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)  d) Kinked tails cannot pack closely
**Answer: a) The process (chemistry)**

**4. ** What besides products leaves?
a) Spontaneous as written  b) Turns raw materials into valuable products  c) Fewer gas moles on the product side  d) By-products
**Answer: d) By-products**
