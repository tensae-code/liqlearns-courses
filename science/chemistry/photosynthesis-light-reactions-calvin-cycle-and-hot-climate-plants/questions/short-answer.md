# Photosynthesis — light reactions, Calvin cycle and hot-climate plants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about Calvin cycle.
**Answer: The light reactions — two photosystems, water splitting, ATP, NADPH and O₂ by chemiosmotic photophosphorylation on the thylakoid membrane — feed the Calvin cycle, with Rubisco at its centre.**

**2.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about Photorespiration.
**Answer: Photorespiration is set against the Calvin cycle's energy balance.**

**3.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about CAM.
**Answer: C3, C4 and CAM are the adaptations to hot climates; photosynthesis and respiration are set out as opposite reactions.**
