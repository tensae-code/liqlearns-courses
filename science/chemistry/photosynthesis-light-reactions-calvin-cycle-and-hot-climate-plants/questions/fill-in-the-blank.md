# Photosynthesis — light reactions, Calvin cycle and hot-climate plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The light reactions — two photosystems, water splitting, ATP, NADPH and O₂ by chemiosmotic photophosphorylation on the thylakoid membrane — feed the _____, with Rubisco at its centre.
**Answer: Calvin cycle**

**2.** _____ is set against the Calvin cycle's energy balance.
**Answer: Photorespiration**

**3.** C3, C4 and _____ are the adaptations to hot climates; photosynthesis and respiration are set out as opposite reactions.
**Answer: CAM**
