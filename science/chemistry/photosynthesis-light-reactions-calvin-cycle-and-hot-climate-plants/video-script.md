Today: **Photosynthesis — light reactions, Calvin cycle and hot-climate plants** — Italy's way, from the book's own treatment. The light reactions — two photosystems, water splitting, ATP, NADPH and O₂ by chemiosmotic photophosphorylation on the thylakoid membrane — feed the Calvin cycle, with Rubisco at its centre. Photorespiration is set against the Calvin cycle's energy balance. C3, C4 and CAM are the adaptations to hot climates; photosynthesis and respiration are set out as opposite reactions.
