# Natural rubber and synthetic rubber — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Latex is…
a) The sap that makes wood hard  b) The milky liquid from the rubber tree — rubber particles in water  c) A synthetic polymer  d) Melted tyre rubber
**Answer: b) The milky liquid from the rubber tree — rubber particles in water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Latex becomes solid natural rubber by…
a) Freezing  b) Coagulation — its rubber particles clumping together, helped by acid  c) Burning  d) Painting
**Answer: b) Coagulation — its rubber particles clumping together, helped by acid**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Vulcanisation is…
a) Heating rubber with sulfur so sulfur cross-links form between the chains  b) Cooling rubber until it cracks  c) Stretching rubber until it snaps  d) Dissolving rubber in oil
**Answer: a) Heating rubber with sulfur so sulfur cross-links form between the chains**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Vulcanised rubber, compared with raw rubber, is…
a) Softer and stickier  b) Harder, stronger, more elastic and more resistant to heat  c) Unable to stretch at all  d) No longer a polymer
**Answer: b) Harder, stronger, more elastic and more resistant to heat**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Synthetic rubbers are…
a) Rubber collected from wild trees  b) Factory-made polymers from petroleum-based monomers, copying and extending natural rubber  c) Rubber mixed with sand  d) Natural rubber painted a different colour
**Answer: b) Factory-made polymers from petroleum-based monomers, copying and extending natural rubber**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Malaysia teaches rubber inside its polymer chapter because…
a) Rubber is a metal  b) Rubber is a polymer — and a historic Malaysian industry, taught as chemistry  c) The chapter needed filling  d) Trees are plants, not chemistry
**Answer: b) Rubber is a polymer — and a historic Malaysian industry, taught as chemistry**
