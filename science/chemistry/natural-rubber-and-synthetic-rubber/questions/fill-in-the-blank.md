# Natural rubber and synthetic rubber — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The milky liquid from the rubber tree is called _____.
**Answer: latex**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Vulcanisation heats rubber with _____.
**Answer: sulfur**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Sulfur forms _____ between the rubber chains, locking them together.
**Answer: cross-links**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Synthetic rubbers are built in factories from petroleum-based _____.
**Answer: monomers**
