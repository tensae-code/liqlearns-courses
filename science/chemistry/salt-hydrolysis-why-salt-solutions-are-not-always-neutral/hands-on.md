# Hands-on — Salt hydrolysis — why salt solutions are not always neutral

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Parentage predictions (paper only)
For each of the workbook's five salts, write its two parents (acid and base, strong or weak), predict its solution's pH verdict from the scheme, and write the hydrolysis equation if an ion acts. Your table is the prediction Activity 14's meter will judge.

## The meter's court (paper only)
Write Activity 14 as a court report: the scheme is the law, the five solutions are the cases, the pH meter is the witness. For each case give the verdict you expect and the equation that argues it — then one sentence on what a disagreeing meter reading would mean for the scheme.
