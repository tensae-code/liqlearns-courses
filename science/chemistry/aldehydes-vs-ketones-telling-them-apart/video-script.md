# Video script — Aldehydes vs ketones — telling them apart

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two clear liquids, both C=O.

Both families are polar (higher boiling than alkanes) but cannot hydrogen-bond to each other, and the smaller members dissolve in water.

Aldehydes oxidise easily — on to carboxylic acids — while ketones resist oxidation.

Tollens' reagent gives a silver mirror with aldehydes only; Fehling-type tests likewise answer aldehydes (as presented).

Now you. Say it back: Which oxidises easily, aldehyde or ketone?
Answer: The aldehyde.
