# Aldehydes vs ketones — telling them apart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which oxidises easily, aldehyde or ketone?
a) At the surface, at any temperature  b) The slope of the tangent  c) The aldehyde  d) Particles are locked in place
**Answer: c) The aldehyde**

**2. ** What does Tollens' reagent make with an aldehyde?
a) Kinked tails cannot pack closely  b) A silver mirror  c) Flavourings and perfumes  d) An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)
**Answer: b) A silver mirror**

**3. ** Aldehydes oxidise on to what?
a) Carboxylic acids  b) Spontaneous as written  c) Turns raw materials into valuable products  d) Fewer gas moles on the product side
**Answer: a) Carboxylic acids**

**4. ** What do Fehling-type tests answer?
a) atomos  b) The electron  c) Photons knock electrons off a metal  d) Aldehydes, not ketones
**Answer: d) Aldehydes, not ketones**
