# Aldehydes vs ketones — telling them apart — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Which oxidises easily, aldehyde or ketone — The aldehyde
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What does Tollens' reagent make with an aldehyde — It dents instead of shattering
**Answer: False**

**3. ** Aldehydes oxidise on to what — Carboxylic acids
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What do Fehling-type tests answer — An exercise of the same kind
**Answer: False**
