# Aldehydes vs ketones — telling them apart — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which oxidises easily, aldehyde or ketone?
**Answer: The aldehyde**

**2. ** What does Tollens' reagent make with an aldehyde?
**Answer: A silver mirror**

**3. ** Aldehydes oxidise on to what?
**Answer: Carboxylic acids**

**4. ** What do Fehling-type tests answer?
**Answer: Aldehydes, not ketones**
