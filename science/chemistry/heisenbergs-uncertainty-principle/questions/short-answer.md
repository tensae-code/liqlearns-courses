# Heisenberg's uncertainty principle — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What two quantities cannot both be known exactly?
**Answer: Position and momentum**

**2. ** Is the uncertainty a fault of instruments?
**Answer: No — it is built into nature**

**3. ** What replaces the sharp orbit?
**Answer: A probability description**

**4. ** Whose principle is this?
**Answer: Heisenberg's**
