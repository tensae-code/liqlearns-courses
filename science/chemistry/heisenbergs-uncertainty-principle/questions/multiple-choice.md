# Heisenberg's uncertainty principle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What two quantities cannot both be known exactly?
a) Delocalised electrons  b) No stable molecule forms  c) Position and momentum  d) Whether the Lewis structure is sound
**Answer: c) Position and momentum**

**2. ** Is the uncertainty a fault of instruments?
a) Alcohol → aldehyde → acid  b) No — it is built into nature  c) They do not react with most solutes  d) –COOH
**Answer: b) No — it is built into nature**

**3. ** What replaces the sharp orbit?
a) A probability description  b) A burette  c) Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature  d) The same quantity of electricity
**Answer: a) A probability description**

**4. ** Whose principle is this?
a) The hydrosphere  b) Carbon monoxide (or particulates/hydrocarbons)  c) No — natural, but strengthened by humans  d) Heisenberg's
**Answer: d) Heisenberg's**
