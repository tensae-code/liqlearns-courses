# Hands-on — Half-reaction time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the halfway time (paper only)
An advancement curve is given as a table: x = 0, 0.2, 0.35, 0.45, 0.52, 0.56, 0.58, 0.59, 0.60 mol at t = 0–8 min, flattening at xf = 0.60 mol. Plot it, mark xf/2 = 0.30 mol, and read t½ off your graph (about 2 min — between the 0.2 and 0.35 readings, nearer 2).

## Race the curves (paper only)
Two reactions reach the same xf; one's t½ is 5 min, the other's 45 min. Sketch both advancement curves on one pair of axes, label each t½, and write the one sentence the comparison proves.
