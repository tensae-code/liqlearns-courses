# Half-reaction time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) At the half-reaction time, the advancement x equals…
a) xf  b) xf/2  c) 2xf  d) 0
**Answer: b) xf/2**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) t½ is read from…
a) A table of constants  b) The advancement–time curve, graphically  c) The molar mass  d) The colour
**Answer: b) The advancement–time curve, graphically**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A small t½ means the reaction is…
a) Slow  b) Fast  c) Total  d) Reversible
**Answer: b) Fast**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The disappearance curve of a reactant gives t½ at…
a) A different time from the advancement curve  b) The same halfway moment  c) The end only  d) Never
**Answer: b) The same halfway moment**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The symbol for half-reaction time is…
a) xmax  b) τ  c) t½  d) Vm
**Answer: c) t½**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) xf in the definition is…
a) The initial advancement  b) The final advancement  c) The maximal volume  d) The rate
**Answer: b) The final advancement**
