# Half-reaction time — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define the half-reaction time.
**Answer: t½ is the time at which the advancement reaches half its final value: x = xf/2.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Describe the graphical reading of t½.
**Answer: On the advancement–time curve, halve the final advancement xf, run from xf/2 across to the curve and down to the time axis — the time read there is t½.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What does comparing two t½ values tell you?
**Answer: Which reaction is faster: the smaller t½ reached its halfway advancement sooner.**
