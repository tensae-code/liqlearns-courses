## Scientific notation

Very large and very small numbers are written as a coefficient times a power of ten (a tiny mass as 3.27×10⁻²²), letting the writer show exactly which digits are significant and making such numbers workable; decimal places are distinguished from significant figures in reporting measured and calculated results.
