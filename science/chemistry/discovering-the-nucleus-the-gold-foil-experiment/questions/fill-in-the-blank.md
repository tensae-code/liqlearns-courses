# Discovering the nucleus — the gold-foil experiment — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** Conclusion: mass and positive charge concentrate in a tiny _____ (about 10⁻¹³ cm across) with electrons around it — the planetary/nuclear model.
**Answer: nucleus**

**2. ** Its two named drawbacks: a circling electron should radiate energy and spiral into the _____ (the atom should collapse), and it cannot explain line-spectra patterns.
**Answer: nucleus**

**3. ** Rutherford predicted a neutral nuclear _____ to explain why proton repulsion does not blow the nucleus apart.
**Answer: particle**

**4. ** Rutherford, Geiger and Marsden fired α-particles at thin gold foil (1909/1911): most passed straight, a small _____ deflected at large angles, a few rebounded — impossible on the plum-pudding model.
**Answer: fraction**
