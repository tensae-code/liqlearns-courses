# Electromagnetic radiation and the spectrum

## Do it first
Sunlight warms you, radio waves carry music, and X-rays see through flesh. Before reading, guess what single thing all three are, travelling at the same speed.

[Picture 29261 — c = λν — the whole spectrum, radio to gamma.]

Electromagnetic radiation travels as waves, described by wavelength λ (the length of one wave), frequency ν (waves per second) and amplitude (wave height).

All of it travels at the speed of light, c, and the three are locked together: c = λν.

Long wavelength means low frequency; short wavelength means high frequency.

The spectrum runs from radio waves (long λ) through visible light to gamma rays (short λ).
