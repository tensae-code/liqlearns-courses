# Electromagnetic radiation and the spectrum — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does λ stand for?
a) Wavelength  b) Same charge → masses ∝ equivalent masses  c) 0.00 V  d) Negative (spontaneous)
**Answer: a) Wavelength**

**2. ** Write the relation between c, λ and ν.
a) The rise in average temperature  b) Redesign, reuse or efficiency in the plant  c) Three  d) c = λν
**Answer: d) c = λν**

**3. ** If wavelength grows, what happens to frequency?
a) Its overlap concentrates density between nuclei  b) Gas  c) It falls  d) Positive kernels
**Answer: c) It falls**

**4. ** Which end of the spectrum has the shortest waves?
a) Dilute  b) Gamma rays  c) A silver mirror  d) An ester and water
**Answer: b) Gamma rays**
