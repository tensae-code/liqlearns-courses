# Electromagnetic radiation and the spectrum — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does λ stand for?
**Answer: Wavelength**

**2. ** Write the relation between c, λ and ν.
**Answer: c = λν**

**3. ** If wavelength grows, what happens to frequency?
**Answer: It falls**

**4. ** Which end of the spectrum has the shortest waves?
**Answer: Gamma rays**
