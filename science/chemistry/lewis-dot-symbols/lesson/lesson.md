# Lewis dot symbols

## Do it first
You need quick bookkeeping for outer electrons before any bonding work. Before reading, how would you write carbon — four outer electrons — as a one-glance symbol?

[Picture 21633 — Dot symbols across one period.]

A Lewis electron-dot symbol is the element's symbol with dots around it for its valence (outer-shell) electrons.

Dots are placed one per side first, then paired — the same discipline as Hund's rule in boxes.

Dot symbols for a full period show the count rising 1 to 8 across the main groups, and every bonding lesson after this one uses them.

## Say it to your companion
Explain Lewis dot symbols to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.
