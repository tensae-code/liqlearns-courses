# Lewis dot symbols — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What do the dots in a Lewis symbol show?
**Answer: Valence (outer-shell) electrons**

**2. ** How are dots placed first?
**Answer: One per side, then paired**

**3. ** How does the dot count run across a period?
**Answer: It rises from 1 to 8**

**4. ** What is at the centre of a Lewis symbol?
**Answer: The element's symbol**
