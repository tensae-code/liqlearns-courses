# Lewis dot symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What do the dots in a Lewis symbol show?
a) Valence (outer-shell) electrons  b) CO₂  c) Indivisible — cannot be cut  d) Alpha, beta and gamma
**Answer: a) Valence (outer-shell) electrons**

**2. ** How are dots placed first?
a) Positive kernels in a lattice  b) The bonding MO  c) Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)  d) One per side, then paired
**Answer: d) One per side, then paired**

**3. ** How does the dot count run across a period?
a) Hydrolysis (as presented)  b) The alcohol (alkyl part)  c) It rises from 1 to 8  d) Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH
**Answer: c) It rises from 1 to 8**

**4. ** What is at the centre of a Lewis symbol?
a) ΔG° = −nFE°  b) The element's symbol  c) The electrochemical (reactivity) series  d) Chemical equivalents
**Answer: b) The element's symbol**
