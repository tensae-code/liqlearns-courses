# Lewis dot symbols — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What do the dots in a Lewis symbol show — Valence (outer-shell) electrons
**Answer: True**

**2. ** How are dots placed first — Hydrogen bonding between molecules
**Answer: False**

**3. ** How does the dot count run across a period — It rises from 1 to 8
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What is at the centre of a Lewis symbol — Anode to cathode
**Answer: False**
