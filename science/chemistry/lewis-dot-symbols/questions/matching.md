# Lewis dot symbols — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What do the dots in a Lewis symbol show? — a
2. How are dots placed first? — b
3. How does the dot count run across a period? — c
4. What is at the centre of a Lewis symbol? — d

**Answer key:** 1–a: Valence (outer-shell) electrons · 2–b: One per side, then paired · 3–c: It rises from 1 to 8 · 4–d: The element's symbol
