# Soap and detergents — Unit 3 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What reaction makes soap?
a) It stays constant during the change  b) It lowers the activation energy path  c) Saponification  d) Elastically — no energy lost
**Answer: c) Saponification**

**2. ** What is a micelle?
a) Margarine (solid fat)  b) Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet  c) No hydrogen bonding between esters  d) Plants (seeds)
**Answer: b) Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet**

**3. ** Which end of soap grips grease?
a) The tail (water-hating end)  b) Oxygen and water  c) Ethiopia's own resource base  d) NO
**Answer: a) The tail (water-hating end)**

**4. ** Why do detergents beat soap in hard water?
a) In fixed whole-number ratios  b) A tiny, dense, positive centre exists  c) The proton number Z  d) No scum forms; they keep working
**Answer: d) No scum forms; they keep working**
