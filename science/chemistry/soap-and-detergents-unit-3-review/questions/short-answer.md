# Soap and detergents — Unit 3 review — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What reaction makes soap?
**Answer: Saponification**

**2. ** What is a micelle?
**Answer: Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet**

**3. ** Which end of soap grips grease?
**Answer: The tail (water-hating end)**

**4. ** Why do detergents beat soap in hard water?
**Answer: No scum forms; they keep working**
