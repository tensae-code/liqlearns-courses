# Video script — Soap and detergents — Unit 3 review

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A soap molecule has a head that loves water and a tail that loves grease — it is a double agent.

Soap is made by saponification — hydrolysing fats/oils with alkali (Grade 11's reaction, industrialised here, with local manufacture presented).

Soap cleans because its molecules form micelles: tails inward gripping grease, heads outward in the water, so grease lifts away in the rinse.

Detergents are synthetic cleaners that keep working in hard water where soap forms scum.

Now you. Say it back: What reaction makes soap?
Answer: Saponification.
