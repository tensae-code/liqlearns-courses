# What makes an element a transition metal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An element is a transition metal, on Egypt's definition, when it has…
a) An incomplete d (or f) sublevel in its atom or in one of its ions  b) A complete d sublevel in all its ions  c) Ten d electrons in its atom  d) A filled 4s sublevel
**Answer: a) An incomplete d (or f) sublevel in its atom or in one of its ions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Zinc is excluded because…
a) It is d¹⁰ in the atom and in its +2 ion alike  b) It forms no ions  c) It is not in the d block  d) It has no d electrons
**Answer: a) It is d¹⁰ in the atom and in its +2 ion alike**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Copper counts as a transition metal because…
a) Its ions are d⁹ — incomplete  b) Its atom is d⁹  c) It is used for coins  d) It has a 4s² configuration
**Answer: a) Its ions are d⁹ — incomplete**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Chromium's actual configuration promotes one electron to reach…
a) A half-filled 3d⁵ sublevel  b) A filled 4s sublevel  c) An empty 3d sublevel  d) A d¹⁰ sublevel
**Answer: a) A half-filled 3d⁵ sublevel**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The coinage metals are…
a) Cu, Ag and Au  b) Zn, Cd and Hg  c) Fe, Co and Ni  d) Cr, Mn and Fe
**Answer: a) Cu, Ag and Au**
