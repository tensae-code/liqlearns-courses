# Hands-on — What makes an element a transition metal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Definition court
Write six element cards — Cu, Zn, Ag, Cd, Au, Hg. For each, write its d count in the atom and in its common ion, then stamp QUALIFIES or DOES NOT with the clause you used. Check against the lesson.

## Explain the anomaly
Write chromium's expected configuration (4s² 3d⁴) and its actual one (4s¹ 3d⁵) side by side. Circle what the move buys — a half-filled d — and say the reason aloud in one sentence.
