# Video script — Properties of ethers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Diethyl ether boils far below ethanol though their masses are near.

Ethers are weakly polar but cannot hydrogen-bond to each other (no –OH), so their boiling points are low, near those of alkanes of similar mass.

They dissolve many organic substances, which is why they serve as solvents; diethyl ether's history as an anaesthetic is noted.

Their volatility and flammability demand care.

Now you. Say it back: Why are ether boiling points low?
Answer: No hydrogen bonding between ether molecules.
