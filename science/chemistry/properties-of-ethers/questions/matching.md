# Properties of ethers — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. Why are ether boiling points low? — d
2. What are ethers used as? — a
3. Are ethers polar? — b
4. What hazard do ethers carry? — c

**Answer key:** 1–d: No hydrogen bonding between ether molecules · 2–a: Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics) · 3–b: Weakly polar · 4–c: Volatility and flammability
