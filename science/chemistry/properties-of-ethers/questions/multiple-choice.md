# Properties of ethers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Why are ether boiling points low?
a) Pressing and drying  b) Fuel, solvent or disinfectant  c) Thermoplastic  d) No hydrogen bonding between ether molecules
**Answer: d) No hydrogen bonding between ether molecules**

**2. ** What are ethers used as?
a) Effective nuclear charge and shielding  b) Valence (outer-shell) electrons  c) Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics)  d) Singly occupy each equal orbital first
**Answer: c) Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics)**

**3. ** Are ethers polar?
a) Carbon dioxide and water  b) Weakly polar  c) The side with fewer gas moles  d) The carbon carrying the –OH
**Answer: b) Weakly polar**

**4. ** What hazard do ethers carry?
a) Volatility and flammability  b) BF₃ (or a metal ion)  c) Buffer solutions  d) The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺
**Answer: a) Volatility and flammability**
