# Properties of ethers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Why are ether boiling points low?
**Answer: No hydrogen bonding between ether molecules**

**2. ** What are ethers used as?
**Answer: Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics)**

**3. ** Are ethers polar?
**Answer: Weakly polar**

**4. ** What hazard do ethers carry?
**Answer: Volatility and flammability**
