# Video script — Why oils are liquid and fats are solid

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Same family, same backbone — yet one pours and one stands.

Saturated tails are straight, pack closely, and attract strongly — so fats rich in them are solid, with higher melting points.

Unsaturated tails are kinked, cannot pack closely, and attract weakly — so oils rich in them are liquid.

The section also discusses the energy content of fats and oils and oxidation in their chemistry, as presented.

Now you. Say it back: Why are fats solid?
Answer: Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly.
