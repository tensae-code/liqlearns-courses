# Why oils are liquid and fats are solid — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Why are fats solid?
**Answer: Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly**

**2. ** Why are oils liquid?
**Answer: Kinked tails cannot pack closely**

**3. ** Which tails are straight?
**Answer: Saturated tails**

**4. ** What does tight packing raise?
**Answer: The melting point**
