# Why oils are liquid and fats are solid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Why are fats solid?
a) Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly  b) The side with fewer gas moles  c) The carbon carrying the –OH  d) Carbon dioxide and water
**Answer: a) Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly**

**2. ** Why are oils liquid?
a) BF₃ (or a metal ion)  b) Buffer solutions  c) The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺  d) Kinked tails cannot pack closely
**Answer: d) Kinked tails cannot pack closely**

**3. ** Which tails are straight?
a) Broken-down fibre from wood  b) Distillation  c) Saturated tails  d) The Solvay process (Unit 3)
**Answer: c) Saturated tails**

**4. ** What does tight packing raise?
a) The energy needed to remove an electron  b) The melting point  c) The particle's mass  d) The Aufbau principle
**Answer: b) The melting point**
