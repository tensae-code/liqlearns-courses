# Why oils are liquid and fats are solid

## Do it first
Same family, same backbone — yet one pours and one stands. Before reading, answer with packing: which tails pack tightly, and what does tight packing do to a melting point?

[Picture 21707 — Packed tails (solid) beside kinked (liquid).]

Saturated tails are straight, pack closely, and attract strongly — so fats rich in them are solid, with higher melting points.

Unsaturated tails are kinked, cannot pack closely, and attract weakly — so oils rich in them are liquid.

The section also discusses the energy content of fats and oils and oxidation in their chemistry, as presented.
