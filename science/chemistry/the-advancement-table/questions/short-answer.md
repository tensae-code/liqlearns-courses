# The advancement table — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does the advancement x measure?
**Answer: How much reaction has happened — the single quantity from which every species' amount at that moment is computed through the equation's coefficients.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the table describe the final state of a total reaction?
**Answer: Set x = xmax (fixed by the limiting reactant): the limiting reactant is at zero, the other amounts follow from the rows — everything left and everything made is read off.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What does τ < 1 tell you?
**Answer: The reaction stopped short of its maximal advancement — xf < xmax — as reversible reactions do; it was not total.**
