# Hands-on — The advancement table

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rule the table (paper only)
For the combustion of 2 mol of methane with 3 mol of oxygen (CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O), draw the full advancement table: initial amounts, amounts at x, xmax, and the final state. Name the limiting reactant from the table, not by guessing.

## Total or not? (paper only)
A reaction's table gives xmax = 0.40 mol. In three runs the measured xf values are 0.40, 0.31 and 0.12 mol. Compute τ for each and label each run total or stopped-short, in the 7AS book's terms.
