# Hands-on — Nuclear binding energy and the stability band

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the map (paper only)
Draw both of the lesson's maps side by side: the binding-energy-per-nucleon curve (label the iron peak, the 28–138 summit range, uranium-235 on the slope, and hydrogen at the foot) and the stability band (n against p, the band bending after 20, the line past 83 marked 'none stable'). Arrows on the curve show fission and fusion both climbing.

## Judge three nuclei (paper only)
Judge, from the band alone: (a) a nucleus with Z = 10, n = 10; (b) Z = 40, n = 40; (c) Z = 90, n = 140. For each write 'in the band / short of neutrons / past the end' with the rule that decides it.
