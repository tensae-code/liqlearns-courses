# Nuclear binding energy and the stability band — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the three conservation rules for nuclear equations.
**Answer: Mass numbers balance on both sides; atomic numbers balance on both sides; and mass and energy together are conserved (mass can become energy and back, by Einstein's relation).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which mass range does the book call the most stable, and what two reactions climb toward it from opposite sides?
**Answer: Masses 28–138, around the peak near iron. Fission climbs from the heavy side; fusion climbs from the light side.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Describe the stability band's shape in three clauses.
**Answer: Up to atomic number 20, stable nuclei hold n = p; beyond 20 the band bends to n > p; beyond 83 no nucleus is stable.**
