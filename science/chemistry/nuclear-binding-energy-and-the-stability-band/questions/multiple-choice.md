# Nuclear binding energy and the stability band — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Helium-4's mass defect as printed is…
a) 0.030379 u  b) 0.403 u  c) 4.00260 u  d) 0.0030379 u
**Answer: a) 0.030379 u**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Helium's binding energy per nucleon is…
a) 7.6 MeV  b) 7.75 MeV  c) 931 MeV  d) 4.54 MeV
**Answer: b) 7.75 MeV**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The binding-energy-per-nucleon curve peaks near…
a) Uranium  b) Hydrogen  c) Iron  d) Lead
**Answer: c) Iron**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Uranium-235 gains stability by fission because it sits…
a) At the summit  b) Below all light nuclei  c) At zero binding  d) On the curve's downhill side, at 7.6 MeV per nucleon
**Answer: d) On the curve's downhill side, at 7.6 MeV per nucleon**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the stability band, beyond Z = 20 the stable nuclei have…
a) More neutrons than protons  b) Equal numbers always  c) More protons than neutrons  d) No neutrons
**Answer: a) More neutrons than protons**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The magic numbers of the lesson are…
a) 1, 3, 5, 7, 9  b) 2, 8, 20, 50, 82, 126  c) 2, 4, 6, 8, 10  d) 18, 36, 54, 86
**Answer: b) 2, 8, 20, 50, 82, 126**
