# Common salt — extraction and uses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rock salt from _____ in al-Hudaydah is already high-purity.
**Answer: al-Salif**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sea salt is won by solar _____ of sea water.
**Answer: evaporation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Solid salt is an _____ crystal of Na⁺ and Cl⁻.
**Answer: ionic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Salt is the feedstock for sodium hydroxide, soda and _____ acid.
**Answer: hydrochloric**
