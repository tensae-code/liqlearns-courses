# Common salt — extraction and uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Yemen's high-purity rock salt is named from…
a) Al-Salif in al-Hudaydah, and Shabwa and Marib  b) Aden only  c) Sana'a and Taiz  d) Imported mines
**Answer: a) Al-Salif in al-Hudaydah, and Shabwa and Marib**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Rock salt needs only…
a) Electrolysis  b) Crushing and packing  c) Dissolving and filtering  d) Roasting
**Answer: b) Crushing and packing**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Sea salt made by solar evaporation carries which impurities?
a) NaOH and soda  b) Sand only  c) CaCl₂ and MgCl₂  d) CaCO₃ and gypsum
**Answer: c) CaCl₂ and MgCl₂**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Sodium chloride melts at…
a) 100 °C  b) 318 °C  c) 1535 °C  d) 804 °C
**Answer: d) 804 °C**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Pure sodium chloride, left in damp air,…
a) Does not absorb moisture  b) Melts into brine at once  c) Turns yellow  d) Absorbs moisture strongly
**Answer: a) Does not absorb moisture**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Salt is the feedstock for all of these EXCEPT…
a) Sodium hydroxide  b) Oxygen  c) Soda  d) Hydrochloric acid
**Answer: b) Oxygen**
