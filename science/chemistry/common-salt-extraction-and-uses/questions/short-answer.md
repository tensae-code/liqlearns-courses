# Common salt — extraction and uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give Yemen's two salt sources and the treatment each needs.
**Answer: Rock salt (al-Salif, Shabwa, Marib): mined, crushed and packed — already high-purity. Sea salt (solar evaporation, e.g. at Aden): sea water evaporated by the sun, leaving salt with CaCl₂ and MgCl₂ impurities.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is a freezing mixture made with salt and ice colder than ice alone — what is salt's job there? (Answer from the use as the book names it, in one or two sentences.)
**Answer: Salt is the book's named partner in freezing mixtures: mixed with ice it drives the mixture's temperature below 0 °C, which is why the mixture, not plain ice, is used where colder-than-ice cold is needed.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State salt's structure and the two physical constants the book gives for it.
**Answer: An ionic crystal of Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions; it melts at 804 °C and boils at 1413 °C.**
