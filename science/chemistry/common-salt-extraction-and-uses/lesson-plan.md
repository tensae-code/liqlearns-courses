# Lesson plan — Common salt — extraction and uses

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Common salt — extraction and uses
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sodium chloride placed in Yemen first, as the Grade 10 book places it: high-purity rock salt from al-Salif in al-Hudaydah and the deposits of Shabwa and Marib needing only crushing and packing, solar evaporation of sea water at places such as Aden yielding salt carrying CaCl2 and MgCl2 impurities, and the salt itself — an ionic crystal melting at 804 °C — at work in food, preservation, freezing mixtures and as feedstock for NaOH, soda and hydrochloric acid
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Sea water and its salts is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
