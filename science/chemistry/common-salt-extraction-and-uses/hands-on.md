# Hands-on — Common salt — extraction and uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Salt map of Yemen (paper only)
Draw a rough map of Yemen and mark the three salt places the lesson names — al-Salif on the Red Sea coast, Shabwa, Marib, and Aden for sea salt. Beside each, write its kind (rock or sea) and the treatment it needs before sale.

## Evaporate like Aden (teacher-led, kitchen materials only)
With a teacher: dissolve two spoonfuls of table salt in a cup of warm water, pour a little into a shallow dish, and leave it in the sun or a warm place until dry. Compare the crystals that return with the salt you started from, and write what the experiment shows about solar evaporation — and what it cannot show about the impurities in real sea salt.
