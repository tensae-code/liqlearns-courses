## Chemistry, the central science

Science learns about the universe by observing, testing and modelling; its branches overlap, and chemistry sits at the centre because everything studied is matter. The named overlap fields are taught one by one: biochemistry (chemistry–biology, e.g.

isolating active compounds from medicinal plants), geochemistry (chemistry–geology: abundance and distribution of compounds and isotopes in the earth), physical chemistry and chemical physics (chemistry–physics, matter and energy at atomic scale) and medicinal chemistry (chemistry–medicine: designing drugs).
