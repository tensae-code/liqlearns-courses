# Proteins of the future — food footprints as chemistry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about 16,000 L.
**Answer: Producing 1 kg of beef takes 16,000 L of water against 2,000 L for 1 kg of soy, while soy delivers 0.34 kg of protein per kg against beef's 0.17 kg.**

**2.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about insect.
**Answer: More than 1900 insect species are considered edible worldwide, and farmed insects emit fewer greenhouse gases and less ammonia than other farm animals.**

**3.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about overshoot.
**Answer: Humanity has been in ecological overshoot since the 1970s — today consuming the resources of 1.7 planets a year, on the Global Footprint Network data the book prints.**
