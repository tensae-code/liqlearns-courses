# Proteins of the future — food footprints as chemistry — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Producing 1 kg of beef takes _____ of water against 2,000 L for 1 kg of soy, while soy delivers 0.34 kg of protein per kg against beef's 0.17 kg.
**Answer: 16,000 L**

**2.** More than 1900 _____ species are considered edible worldwide, and farmed insects emit fewer greenhouse gases and less ammonia than other farm animals.
**Answer: insect**

**3.** Humanity has been in ecological _____ since the 1970s — today consuming the resources of 1.7 planets a year, on the Global Footprint Network data the book prints.
**Answer: overshoot**
