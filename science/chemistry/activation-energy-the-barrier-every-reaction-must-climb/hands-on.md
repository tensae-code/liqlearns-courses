# Hands-on — Activation energy — the barrier every reaction must climb

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the hill (paper only)
Draw the energy hill for H₂ + I₂ → 2HI from the lesson: reactants on the left shelf, products in the right valley, the activated complex [H···H···I···I] drawn at the pass, and the activation energy marked as the climb from shelf to pass. Then draw the rock's journey beside it with the book's three stations labelled.

## Count the crowd (paper only)
Sketch the distribution curve twice — T₁ and T₂ — shading the activated part black on the first and red on the second, as the book does. Under each sketch write the fraction in words ('few', 'many') and the one sentence that explains why the second reaction runs faster.
