# Activation energy — the barrier every reaction must climb — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Only molecules with at least the _____ energy can react on collision.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In the complex [H···H···I···I], the old bonds are half-_____.
