# Activation energy — the barrier every reaction must climb — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On the distribution curve at T₁, the molecules that can react are…
a) The black-shaded few past the activation-energy line  b) All of them  c) None of them  d) Only the average ones
**Answer: a) The black-shaded few past the activation-energy line**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Heating to T₂ chiefly increases the rate by…
a) Making collisions gentler  b) Multiplying the fraction of molecules with enough energy  c) Removing the barrier entirely  d) Changing the products
**Answer: b) Multiplying the fraction of molecules with enough energy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The activated complex for H₂ + I₂ is drawn as…
a) [H₂I₂] as a stable molecule  b) Four separate atoms  c) [H···H···I···I]  d) H₂ + I₂ side by side unchanged
**Answer: c) [H···H···I···I]**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In the activated complex, the old bonds are…
a) Fully broken long before  b) Unchanged  c) Doubled  d) Half-broken, and the new bonds half-formed
**Answer: d) Half-broken, and the new bonds half-formed**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the rock picture, the activation energy is…
a) The push that gets the rock over the pass  b) The valley's depth  c) The rock's weight  d) The mountainside's height above the sea
**Answer: a) The push that gets the rock over the pass**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A collision between molecules below the activation energy…
a) Always reacts slowly  b) Does not react, however well aimed  c) Reacts if repeated  d) Reacts only in light
**Answer: b) Does not react, however well aimed**
