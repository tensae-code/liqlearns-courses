# Activation energy — the barrier every reaction must climb — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain the two shadings of the book's curve and what changes between them.
**Answer: At T₁ only the black-shaded molecules — a small fraction — have at least the activation energy. At T₂ the distribution shifts and the red-shaded crowd joins them: the activated fraction is multiplied, and with it the rate.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is an activated complex, in the lesson's terms?
**Answer: The fleeting arrangement at the top of the energy barrier, where the reactants' old bonds are half-broken and the products' new bonds half-formed — for H₂ + I₂ drawn [H···H···I···I].**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Retell the rock picture and name its three stations with their meanings.
**Answer: A rock on a mountainside (أ — the reactants) must be pushed over the pass (ب — the activated complex; the push is the activation energy) before it can roll down to the valley (ج — the products) and stay there.**
