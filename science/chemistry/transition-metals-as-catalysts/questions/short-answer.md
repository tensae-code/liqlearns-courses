# Transition metals as catalysts — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Walk the mechanism in order, from bare surface to finished reaction.
**Answer:** Reactant molecules adsorb onto the metal surface, bonded by its 4s/3d electrons; held there they are concentrated and their bonds weakened; with activation energy lowered they react; the products leave the surface, freeing it to grip the next molecules — the catalyst unchanged.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the catalyst survive its own reaction?
**Answer:** Its part is a temporary surface grip, not a chemical consumption: it bonds reactants, lets them react, and releases products, ending each cycle as it began.
