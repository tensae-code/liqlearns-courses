# Transition metals as catalysts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Egypt explains transition-metal catalysis by…
a) Surface adsorption of reactants onto the metal  b) The metal dissolving in the reactants  c) The metal being consumed slowly  d) Magnetism pulling molecules together
**Answer: a) Surface adsorption of reactants onto the metal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Adsorbed reactant molecules are…
a) Concentrated on the surface with their bonds weakened  b) Cooled to freezing  c) Turned into the metal  d) Given extra electrons permanently
**Answer: a) Concentrated on the surface with their bonds weakened**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A catalyst lowers the reaction's…
a) Activation energy  b) Final energy of the products  c) Temperature requirement to zero  d) Atom count
**Answer: a) Activation energy**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) V₂O₅ catalyses which process?
a) The contact process  b) Haber–Bosch  c) Oil hydrogenation  d) Hydrogen peroxide decomposition
**Answer: a) The contact process**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) MnO₂ is the book's catalyst for decomposing…
a) Hydrogen peroxide  b) Water  c) Ammonia  d) Sulphuric acid
**Answer: a) Hydrogen peroxide**
