# Transition metals as catalysts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Reactants are held on the catalyst's ______ by adsorption.
**Answer:** surface

**2.** Adsorption weakens the reactants' ______ and lowers the activation energy.
**Answer:** bonds

**3.** The catalyst for the contact process is ______.
**Answer:** V₂O₅
