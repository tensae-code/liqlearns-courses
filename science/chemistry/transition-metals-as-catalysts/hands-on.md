# Hands-on — Transition metals as catalysts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Profile sketch
Draw an energy-profile diagram with two curves from the same start to the same end: a high hill labelled uncatalysed, a lower hill labelled catalysed. Mark the activation energy on each and say why the products' level did not move.

## Case cards
Make four cards — Ni, Fe, V₂O₅, MnO₂ — and write each one's reaction on the back. Test yourself both ways: catalyst to reaction, reaction to catalyst.
