# Noble gases — group 18 — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Group 18's outer-shell configuration is ns²np⁶, called a completed _____.
**Answer: octet**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The noble gas of discharge-tube signs is _____.
**Answer: neon**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The chemist who made the first noble-gas compound in 1962 was _____.
**Answer: Bartlett**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Xenon and fluorine in a 1:20 ratio under pressure give Xe_____₆.
**Answer: F**
