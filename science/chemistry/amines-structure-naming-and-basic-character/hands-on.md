# Hands-on — Amines — structure, naming and basic character

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name and sort (paper only)
You are given three structures: CH₃NH₂, CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂, C₆H₅NH₂. Name each by the book's route (the last by its working name), then order the three by expected boiling point against a matching alkane and alcohol, using the table's rule.

## Salt formation (paper only)
Butylamine meets hydrochloric acid. Write what the nitrogen's lone pair does, name the kind of substance formed, and state in one sentence which family behaviour of ammonia the amine has kept.
