# Amines — structure, naming and basic character — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Give the amine family's structure rule.
**Answer: Nitrogen bonded to carbon: the –NH₂ amino group, or that group with one, two or three of its hydrogens replaced by carbon groups.**

**2.** Read the boiling-point table the book's way.
**Answer: Amines boil above similar alkanes and below similar alcohols — N–H hydrogen bonding holds them more than an alkane's plain surface forces, less than an alcohol's stronger O–H hydrogen bonds.**

**3.** Connect aniline to the family's importance.
**Answer: Aniline, C₆H₅NH₂, is the dye-manufacture amine — the industrial member through which the book introduces the family and its uses.**
