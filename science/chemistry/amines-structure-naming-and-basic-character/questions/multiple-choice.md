# Amines — structure, naming and basic character — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The amine family's working entry example is…
a) Ethanol  b) Aniline, of dye manufacture  c) Benzene itself  d) Methanoic acid
**Answer: b) Aniline, of dye manufacture**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Aniline's formula is…
a) C₆H₅NH₂  b) CH₃NH₂ only  c) C₂H₅OH  d) CH₃COOH
**Answer: a) C₆H₅NH₂**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂NH₂ is named…
a) Pentylamine  b) Butylamine  c) Propylamine  d) Ethylamine
**Answer: b) Butylamine**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Amine boiling points, against similar alkanes and alcohols, are…
a) Lowest of the three  b) Between the alkanes and the alcohols  c) Highest of the three  d) Identical to the alkanes
**Answer: b) Between the alkanes and the alcohols**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Amines are bases because the nitrogen's lone pair…
a) Donates electrons to metals  b) Accepts a proton  c) Forms hydrogen bonds only  d) Breaks C–C bonds
**Answer: b) Accepts a proton**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) The group that marks an amine is…
a) –OH  b) –COOH  c) –NH₂ and its substituted forms  d) –CHO
**Answer: c) –NH₂ and its substituted forms**
