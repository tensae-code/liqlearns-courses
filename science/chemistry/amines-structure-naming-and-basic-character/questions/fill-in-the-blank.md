# Amines — structure, naming and basic character — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The dye-manufacture amine is _____.
**Answer: aniline**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂NH₂ is named _____.
**Answer: butylamine**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Amines are _____ — they accept a proton on nitrogen.
**Answer: bases**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The amine group is written –NH₂ and its _____ forms.
**Answer: substituted**
