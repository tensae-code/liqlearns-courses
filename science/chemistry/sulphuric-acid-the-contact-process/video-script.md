# Video script — Sulphuric acid — the Contact process

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

More sulfuric acid is made than any other chemical on Earth.

The Contact process: sulfur (or sulfide ore) is burned to SO₂; SO₂ is oxidised over a vanadium(V) oxide catalyst to SO₃ — the contact step.

SO₃ is absorbed into concentrated acid to form oleum, not into water directly (which would make a corrosive mist); oleum is then diluted to sulfuric acid.

Uses run from fertilisers to petroleum refining and chemical manufacture.

Now you. Say it back: What catalyst oxidises SO₂?
Answer: Vanadium(V) oxide.
