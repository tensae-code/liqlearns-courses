# Sulphuric acid — the Contact process — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What catalyst oxidises SO₂?
**Answer: Vanadium(V) oxide**

**2. ** SO₃ is absorbed into what?
**Answer: Concentrated acid, forming oleum**

**3. ** Why not absorb SO₃ in water?
**Answer: It would form a corrosive mist**

**4. ** The middle product SO₃ comes from…
**Answer: Oxidising SO₂**
