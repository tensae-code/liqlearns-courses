# Sulphuric acid — the Contact process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What catalyst oxidises SO₂?
a) The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance  b) The –OH carbon holds three other carbons  c) Vanadium(V) oxide  d) They converge to one level
**Answer: c) Vanadium(V) oxide**

**2. ** SO₃ is absorbed into what?
a) Le Chatelier's principle  b) Concentrated acid, forming oleum  c) An exercise of the same kind  d) An electron-pair acceptor
**Answer: b) Concentrated acid, forming oleum**

**3. ** Why not absorb SO₃ in water?
a) It would form a corrosive mist  b) Weeds  c) Silica — the glass former  d) Molasses
**Answer: a) It would form a corrosive mist**

**4. ** The middle product SO₃ comes from…
a) Only certain sharp lines  b) Electron diffraction  c) A dumbbell (two lobes)  d) Oxidising SO₂
**Answer: d) Oxidising SO₂**
