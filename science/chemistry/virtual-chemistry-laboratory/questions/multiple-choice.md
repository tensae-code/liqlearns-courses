# Virtual chemistry laboratory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A virtual chemistry experiment is…
a) A film of someone else's experiment  b) An experiment's procedure run in a computer simulation, with the learner choosing reagents and conditions  c) A textbook chapter  d) A video game with no chemistry
**Answer: b) An experiment's procedure run in a computer simulation, with the learner choosing reagents and conditions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which experiment is the strongest case for the virtual bench?
a) Titrating an acid  b) A reaction with an explosive gas mixture too dangerous for the school bench  c) Measuring a mass  d) Filtering a precipitate
**Answer: b) A reaction with an explosive gas mixture too dangerous for the school bench**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In a virtual experiment, sound method means…
a) Changing every condition at once  b) Changing one condition at a time and recording exactly what happens  c) Clicking until it looks right  d) Skipping repeats
**Answer: b) Changing one condition at a time and recording exactly what happens**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A simulation's result is best described as…
a) A measurement  b) A prediction from a model, to be checked against reality  c) Proof that needs no bench  d) Always wrong
**Answer: b) A prediction from a model, to be checked against reality**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) What can the virtual bench not teach?
a) How outcomes answer conditions  b) Hand skill, real hazard judgement and the feel of apparatus  c) How to record results  d) How to repeat a run
**Answer: b) Hand skill, real hazard judgement and the feel of apparatus**
