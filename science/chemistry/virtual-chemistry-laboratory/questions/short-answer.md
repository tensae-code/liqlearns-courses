# Virtual chemistry laboratory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give two advantages of the virtual laboratory that no real bench can match.
**Answer: Any two: dangerous experiments become safe to run; expensive ones become cheap; very slow or very fast changes can be compressed or stretched; the same run can be repeated with one condition changed, many times.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does a virtual experiment still demand one-variable discipline?
**Answer: Because its results answer the conditions the learner sets; if two conditions change together, no conclusion about either is possible — the same logic as the real bench.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A classmate says the simulation proves the real experiment will look exactly the same. Correct them.
**Answer: The simulation shows what its model was built to show — smells, textures and real-apparatus behaviour may differ; the virtual result is a prediction to check, not a replacement for the bench.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does Vietnam's book place the virtual practical inside a practicals topic instead of replacing practicals with it?
**Answer: Because the two benches teach different halves: the virtual bench widens reach and repetition, while the real bench teaches hands, care with real chemicals and surprise — the course needs both.**
