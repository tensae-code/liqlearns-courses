Today: **Virtual chemistry laboratory** — Vietnam's way, from its Grade 10 book.

A virtual experiment is a real experiment's procedure run in a computer simulation: choose, set, run, observe, record.

Its reach is the point: experiments too dangerous, costly, slow or fast for the school bench become runnable — and repeatable with one condition changed at a time.

Discipline carries over unchanged: one variable at a time, exact recording, repeats before conclusions.

A simulation is a model — its results are predictions; the real bench still teaches hands, hazards and surprise.
