# Indicators — choosing by colour range — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is an indicator, chemically?
**Answer: A weak acid/base with two coloured forms**

**2. ** Over what does an indicator change?
**Answer: A pH range of about two units**

**3. ** How is an indicator chosen?
**Answer: Its range must fall in the titration's pH jump**

**4. ** What is the colour-change moment called?
**Answer: The end point**
