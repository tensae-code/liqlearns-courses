# Indicators — choosing by colour range — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is an indicator, chemically?
a) A weak acid/base with two coloured forms  b) Tenfold  c) 7 — neutral  d) Acidic
**Answer: a) A weak acid/base with two coloured forms**

**2. ** Over what does an indicator change?
a) Limestone and clay  b) Chrome and vegetable tanning  c) The tail (water-hating end)  d) A pH range of about two units
**Answer: d) A pH range of about two units**

**3. ** How is an indicator chosen?
a) Periods  b) Predict its properties  c) Its range must fall in the titration's pH jump  d) The main energy level (and orbital size)
**Answer: c) Its range must fall in the titration's pH jump**

**4. ** What is the colour-change moment called?
a) The longest chain carrying –OH  b) The end point  c) How far the equilibrium lies toward products  d) Unreacted gases
**Answer: b) The end point**
