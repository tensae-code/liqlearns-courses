# Video script — Preserving food

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every preservation method is an attack on spoilage microbes' living conditions.

Drying removes water microbes need; salting draws water out likewise.

Heating kills microbes (and canning seals them out); refrigeration slows their growth.

Chemical preservation (acids, sulfites and kin as presented) makes food hostile to microbes.

Now you. Say it back: What does drying remove?
Answer: Water.
