# Preserving food — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does drying remove?
**Answer: Water**

**2. ** What does heating do to microbes?
**Answer: Kills them**

**3. ** What does refrigeration do?
**Answer: Slows microbial growth**

**4. ** What does salting do?
**Answer: Draws water out of the food**
