# Preserving food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does drying remove?
a) Water  b) Singly occupy each equal orbital first  c) Effective nuclear charge and shielding  d) Valence (outer-shell) electrons
**Answer: a) Water**

**2. ** What does heating do to microbes?
a) The side with fewer gas moles  b) The carbon carrying the –OH  c) Carbon dioxide and water  d) Kills them
**Answer: d) Kills them**

**3. ** What does refrigeration do?
a) Buffer solutions  b) The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺  c) Slows microbial growth  d) BF₃ (or a metal ion)
**Answer: c) Slows microbial growth**

**4. ** What does salting do?
a) Distillation  b) Draws water out of the food  c) The Solvay process (Unit 3)  d) Broken-down fibre from wood
**Answer: b) Draws water out of the food**
