# Oxygen functional groups — the map

## Do it first
Five groups containing oxygen organise a whole unit of organic chemistry. Before reading, write what you expect each to look like if you know alcohols end in –OH.

[Picture 21682 — Five oxygen groups on one chart.]

The hydroxyl group –OH characterises alcohols.

The ether group has oxygen bridging two carbons, R–O–R.

The carbonyl group C=O sits at a chain end in aldehydes and mid-chain in ketones; the carboxyl group –COOH defines carboxylic acids, and the ester group joins an acid part to an alcohol part. This map previews the unit family by family.
