# Video script — Oxygen functional groups — the map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five groups containing oxygen organise a whole unit of organic chemistry.

The hydroxyl group –OH characterises alcohols.

The ether group has oxygen bridging two carbons, R–O–R.

The carbonyl group C=O sits at a chain end in aldehydes and mid-chain in ketones; the carboxyl group –COOH defines carboxylic acids, and the ester group joins an acid part to an alcohol part.

Now you. Say it back: Which group defines alcohols?
Answer: –OH (hydroxyl).
