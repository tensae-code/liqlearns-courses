# Oxygen functional groups — the map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which group defines alcohols?
a) Partial pressures  b) It falls  c) –OH (hydroxyl)  d) Irreversible
**Answer: c) –OH (hydroxyl)**

**2. ** What does the ether group look like?
a) A weak acid's ionisation strength  b) R–O–R — oxygen bridging carbons  c) Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry  d) H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)
**Answer: b) R–O–R — oxygen bridging carbons**

**3. ** What group do aldehydes and ketones share?
a) The carbonyl group, C=O  b) Harvests remove it from the soil  c) Sodium hydroxide, chlorine, hydrogen  d) Clinker
**Answer: a) The carbonyl group, C=O**

**4. ** What group defines carboxylic acids?
a) Gamma rays  b) One-electron species only  c) +½ or −½  d) –COOH (carboxyl)
**Answer: d) –COOH (carboxyl)**
