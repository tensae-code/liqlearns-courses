# Oxygen functional groups — the map — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which group defines alcohols?
**Answer: –OH (hydroxyl)**

**2. ** What does the ether group look like?
**Answer: R–O–R — oxygen bridging carbons**

**3. ** What group do aldehydes and ketones share?
**Answer: The carbonyl group, C=O**

**4. ** What group defines carboxylic acids?
**Answer: –COOH (carboxyl)**
