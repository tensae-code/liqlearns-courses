# Buffers — holding pH steady — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What two ingredients make a buffer?
a) Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)  b) A weak acid and its salt (conjugate base)  c) Positive kernels in a lattice  d) The bonding MO
**Answer: b) A weak acid and its salt (conjugate base)**

**2. ** What mops up added H⁺ in a buffer?
a) The conjugate base  b) Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH  c) Hydrolysis (as presented)  d) The alcohol (alkyl part)
**Answer: a) The conjugate base**

**3. ** What relation calculates buffer pH?
a) The electrochemical (reactivity) series  b) Chemical equivalents  c) ΔG° = −nFE°  d) Henderson–Hasselbalch
**Answer: d) Henderson–Hasselbalch**

**4. ** Name a natural buffer.
a) CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone  b) Plato and Aristotle  c) Blood  d) Lungs and health
**Answer: c) Blood**
