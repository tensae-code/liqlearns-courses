# Buffers — holding pH steady — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What two ingredients make a buffer?
**Answer: A weak acid and its salt (conjugate base)**

**2. ** What mops up added H⁺ in a buffer?
**Answer: The conjugate base**

**3. ** What relation calculates buffer pH?
**Answer: Henderson–Hasselbalch**

**4. ** Name a natural buffer.
**Answer: Blood**
