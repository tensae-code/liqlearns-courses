# Buffers — holding pH steady — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What two ingredients make a buffer — A weak acid and its salt (conjugate base)
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What mops up added H⁺ in a buffer — The atoms themselves change
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What relation calculates buffer pH — Henderson–Hasselbalch
**Answer: True**

**4. ** Name a natural buffer. — The side with fewer gas moles
**Answer: False**
