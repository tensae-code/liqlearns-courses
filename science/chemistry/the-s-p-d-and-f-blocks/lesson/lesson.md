## The s, p, d and f blocks

Elements are classified by the subshell receiving the last electron — s, p, d and f blocks (with the f-block's general valence configuration given) — and, in the section's wider classification, into metals, nonmetals and metalloids and transition/inner-transition sets; an exercise has pupils find, for N, Na, Si, Fe, Zn, Kr and Ce, the valence electrons and the block of the last electron.
