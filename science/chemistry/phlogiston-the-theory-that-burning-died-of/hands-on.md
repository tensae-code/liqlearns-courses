# Hands-on — Phlogiston — the theory that burning died of

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Weigh the argument (paper only)
A candle burns down and 'loses' mass; a steel nail rusts and gains mass. For each, write (a) what phlogiston theory would say, (b) what the oxygen account says, and (c) which account the balance supports and why. No burning needed — argue from the lesson's weighings.

## Theory obituary (paper only)
Write phlogiston's obituary in four lines: when it was born (1701, Stahl), what it explained well (burning, calxes, smelting with charcoal), what killed it (Lavoisier's weighings), and what replaced it (combination with oxygen).
