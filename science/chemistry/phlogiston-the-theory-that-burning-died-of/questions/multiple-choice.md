# Phlogiston — the theory that burning died of — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In Stahl's theory, burning a substance means it…
a) Releases phlogiston  b) Gains oxygen  c) Loses electrons  d) Breaks into atoms
**Answer: a) Releases phlogiston**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A metal's calx, in phlogiston language, is…
a) The metal plus oxygen  b) The metal minus phlogiston  c) The pure metal  d) The metal dissolved in acid
**Answer: b) The metal minus phlogiston**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Charcoal restores a calx to metal, in the theory, because charcoal…
a) Is rich in oxygen  b) Melts the calx  c) Is rich in phlogiston and gives it back  d) Removes carbon dioxide
**Answer: c) Is rich in phlogiston and gives it back**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Lavoisier's killing evidence was that sulfur and phosphorus, on burning,…
a) Lose weight  b) Keep the same weight  c) Turn to gas completely  d) Gain weight
**Answer: d) Gain weight**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Saving phlogiston after Lavoisier would require it to have…
a) Negative weight  b) No colour  c) A smell  d) Positive charge
**Answer: a) Negative weight**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The phlogiston story belongs to which stage of Yemen's history unit?
a) The Greek stage  b) The fourth historical stage, from 1701 onwards  c) The Dalton stage  d) The modern stage
**Answer: b) The fourth historical stage, from 1701 onwards**
