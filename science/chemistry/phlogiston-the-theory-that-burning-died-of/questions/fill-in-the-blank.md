# Phlogiston — the theory that burning died of — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Burning, in Stahl's theory, releases a substance called _____.
**Answer: phlogiston**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A metal that has lost its phlogiston is called its _____.
**Answer: calx**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) _____ is rich in phlogiston and restores the metal from its calx.
**Answer: Charcoal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Lavoisier showed sulfur and phosphorus _____ weight when they burn.
**Answer: gain**
