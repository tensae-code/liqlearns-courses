# Phlogiston — the theory that burning died of — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write the phlogiston account of a metal burning, as an equation in words.
**Answer: Metal → calx + phlogiston (released): the calx is what remains when the metal's phlogiston has left it.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is 'charcoal gives phlogiston back' not just a story — what real process did it describe?
**Answer: Smelting: heating a metal's calx (oxide) with charcoal really does restore the metal, and the theory named charcoal's part in it — the description worked until weighed carefully.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State Lavoisier's counter-evidence and the absurdity it forces.
**Answer: Sulfur and phosphorus gain weight when they burn. A released substance cannot add weight, so phlogiston would need negative weight — an absurdity that ends the theory.**
