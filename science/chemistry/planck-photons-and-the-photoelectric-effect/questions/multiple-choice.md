# Planck, photons and the photoelectric effect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Write the energy of one photon.
a) Nitric acid, HNO₃  b) Care — dose, protection, water safety  c) Clay  d) E = hν
**Answer: d) E = hν**

**2. ** What do we call one packet of light energy?
a) An emission spectrum  b) Position and momentum  c) A photon  d) The isotopes, weighted by abundance
**Answer: c) A photon**

**3. ** What decides whether photoelectrons are ejected?
a) Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance  b) The light's frequency  c) Inward pull on surface particles  d) The rate constant
**Answer: b) The light's frequency**

**4. ** What happens in the photoelectric effect?
a) Photons knock electrons off a metal  b) Yes — it reaches equilibrium  c) The melting point  d) With a group Start-up Activity
**Answer: a) Photons knock electrons off a metal**
