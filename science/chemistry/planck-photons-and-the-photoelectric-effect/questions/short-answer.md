# Planck, photons and the photoelectric effect — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Write the energy of one photon.
**Answer: E = hν**

**2. ** What do we call one packet of light energy?
**Answer: A photon**

**3. ** What decides whether photoelectrons are ejected?
**Answer: The light's frequency**

**4. ** What happens in the photoelectric effect?
**Answer: Photons knock electrons off a metal**
