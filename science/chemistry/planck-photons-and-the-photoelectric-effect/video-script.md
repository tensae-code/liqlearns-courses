# Video script — Planck, photons and the photoelectric effect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Shine a dim violet light on a certain metal and electrons fly off at once.

Planck proposed that energy comes in small packets called quanta.

Einstein applied this to light: light travels as photons, each photon carrying energy E = hν.

In the photoelectric effect, photons knock electrons out of a metal surface.

Only frequency decides whether electrons are ejected and how much energy they carry — brighter light of too-low frequency ejects none.

Now you. Say it back: Write the energy of one photon.
Answer: E = hν.
