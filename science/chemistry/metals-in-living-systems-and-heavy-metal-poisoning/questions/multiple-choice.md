# Metals in living systems — and heavy-metal poisoning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The metal at the centre of chlorophyll is…
a) Iron  b) Magnesium  c) Zinc  d) Calcium
**Answer: b) Magnesium**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Haemoglobin carries oxygen around its…
a) Sodium  b) Potassium  c) Iron  d) Lead
**Answer: c) Iron**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A macro-mineral is one needed at…
a) Any amount  b) Over 100 mg a day  c) Under 100 mg a day  d) Exactly 100 mg a day
**Answer: b) Over 100 mg a day**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Minamata disease was poisoning by…
a) Lead  b) Cadmium  c) Arsenic  d) Mercury
**Answer: d) Mercury**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Which set is the book's heavy-metal set?
a) Iron, zinc, calcium, sodium  b) Lead, mercury, cadmium, arsenic  c) Magnesium, potassium, zinc, iron  d) Copper, tin, aluminium, gold
**Answer: b) Lead, mercury, cadmium, arsenic**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The fish the book names as carrying the heaviest heavy-metal loads are…
a) Sardine and sprat  b) Shark, marlin, tuna and swordfish  c) Tilapia and catfish  d) Herring and mackerel only
**Answer: b) Shark, marlin, tuna and swordfish**
