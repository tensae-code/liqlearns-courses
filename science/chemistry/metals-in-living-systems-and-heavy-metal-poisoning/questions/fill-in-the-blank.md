# Metals in living systems — and heavy-metal poisoning — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The blood protein that carries oxygen around iron is _____.
**Answer: haemoglobin**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The hatter driven 'mad' by his trade was poisoned by compounds of _____.
**Answer: mercury**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Heavy metals concentrate as they climb because they _____ in the body instead of being excreted.
**Answer: persist (build up / accumulate)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A spent car battery is a _____-acid battery, and belongs in collection and recycling, not ordinary refuse.
**Answer: lead**
