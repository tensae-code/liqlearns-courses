Today: **Metals in living systems** — Dominica's CSEC living-systems metals: essentials, then poisons.

A leaf is green around magnesium; your blood is red around iron. Life is built on metal ions — calcium, zinc, sodium, potassium — sorted only by the dose, macro above a hundred milligrams a day, micro below it. Then come the metals with no job at all: lead, mercury, cadmium, arsenic. They persist, they climb food chains into shark and swordfish, they wrote Minamata into history — and they sit in our own batteries and bulbs, waiting on a decision about disposal.
