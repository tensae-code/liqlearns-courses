# Metals in living systems — and heavy-metal poisoning — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The metals life runs on — magnesium, iron and the rest, The heavy metals — lead, mercury, cadmium and arsenic, Build-up through food chains — and the disposal problem), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, matching each essential metal to its named job and each heavy metal to its sources and effects, and tracing mercury from a factory drain to a swordfish steak link by link.
