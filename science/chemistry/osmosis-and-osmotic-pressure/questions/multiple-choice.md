# Osmosis and osmotic pressure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A semi-permeable membrane passes…
a) Solute only  b) Solvent only  c) Both freely  d) Neither
**Answer: b) Solvent only**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In osmosis the net flow runs…
a) Toward the pure solvent  b) Toward the solution side  c) In circles  d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) Toward the solution side**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The side whose level rises is…
a) The solvent side  b) The solution side  c) Neither side  d) Both equally
**Answer: b) The solution side**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Osmotic pressure is the pressure that…
a) Pushes solute through  b) Stops the net flow of osmosis  c) Boils the solution  d) Freezes the solvent
**Answer: b) Stops the net flow of osmosis**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Solvent crosses both ways, yet the flow is net one way because…
a) The membrane is one-way  b) More solvent particles arrive from the solvent side than return  c) Solute pulls them  d) Gravity acts
**Answer: b) More solvent particles arrive from the solvent side than return**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The lesson's enrichment carries the membrane idea into…
a) Nanotube membranes  b) Distillation  c) Electrolysis  d) Titration
**Answer: a) Nanotube membranes**
