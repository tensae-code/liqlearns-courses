# Osmosis and osmotic pressure — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A semi-permeable membrane holds back the _____ particles.
**Answer: solute**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Osmosis is the crossing of _____ particles through the membrane.
**Answer: solvent**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The pressure that stops the flow is the _____ pressure.
**Answer: osmotic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The enrichment names _____ membranes as the modern form.
**Answer: nanotube**
