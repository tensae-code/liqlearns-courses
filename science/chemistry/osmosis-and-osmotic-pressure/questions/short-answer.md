# Osmosis and osmotic pressure — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define osmosis in the book's particle picture.
**Answer: Solvent particles cross a semi-permeable membrane toward the solution side — more arriving than returning — so the solvent net-flows to dilute the solution while the solute stays held back.**

**2.** Why can the solute not even things out itself?
**Answer: The membrane's openings are too small for the dissolved particles; only the solvent can cross, so all levelling must be done by solvent movement.**

**3.** Where does osmosis sit among the solutions unit's properties?
**Answer: It is the colligative property that appears across a membrane — alongside vapour-pressure lowering, boiling elevation and freezing depression in the properties of solutions.**
