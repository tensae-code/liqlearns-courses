# Hands-on — Osmosis and osmotic pressure

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Particle drawing (paper only)
Draw the membrane with pure water on the left and a solution on the right: solvent dots small, solute dots large and held back. Draw arrows both ways with different thicknesses, mark which level rises, and caption it with the net-flow reason in one sentence.

## Predict the levels (paper only)
Two set-ups: (A) water against a weak solution, (B) water against a strong solution, same membrane. Sketch the final levels for both and write which builds the greater osmotic pressure, with the particle reason.
