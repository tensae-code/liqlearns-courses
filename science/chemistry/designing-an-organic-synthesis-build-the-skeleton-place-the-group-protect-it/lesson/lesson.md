## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 3 §5 — China's book (China, Senior High (Selective Compulsory 3 — Foundations of Organic Chemistry))

Organic synthesis as design: Wöhler's urea opens it; the two tasks are building the carbon skeleton (grow, shorten, ring-form) and introducing functional groups; routes judged by raw-material cost, step yield, atom economy, mild/green operations; a hydroxyl group that would not survive a step is protected as an ether and unmasked afterwards — worked route-design exercises (e.g. to ethyl ethanoate and aspirin-type targets) require learners to compare forward synthesis from a given feedstock.
