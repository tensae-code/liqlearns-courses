Today: **Designing an organic synthesis — build the skeleton, place the group, protect it** — China's way, from the book's own treatment. Organic synthesis as design: Wöhler's urea opens it; the two tasks are building the carbon skeleton (grow, shorten, ring-form) and introducing functional groups; routes judged by raw-material cost, step yield, atom economy, mild/green operations; a hydroxyl group that would not survive a step is protected as an ether and unmasked afterwards — worked route-design exercises (e.g. to ethyl ethanoate and aspirin-type targets) require learners to compare forward synthesis from a given feedstock.
