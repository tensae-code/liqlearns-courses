# Why metals conduct and bend — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Why do metals conduct — Mobile sea electrons carry charge
**Answer: True**

**2. ** Why can metals bend without breaking — The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What gives metals their lustre — Mobile electrons at the surface
**Answer: True**

**4. ** How does a metal differ from an ionic crystal when struck — Only certain sharp lines
**Answer: False**
