# Why metals conduct and bend — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Why do metals conduct?
a) A burette  b) Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature  c) The same quantity of electricity  d) Mobile sea electrons carry charge
**Answer: d) Mobile sea electrons carry charge**

**2. ** Why can metals bend without breaking?
a) Carbon monoxide (or particulates/hydrocarbons)  b) No — natural, but strengthened by humans  c) Layers slide over the flowing electron sea  d) The hydrosphere
**Answer: c) Layers slide over the flowing electron sea**

**3. ** What gives metals their lustre?
a) Attraction of kernels to the electron pool  b) Mobile electrons at the surface  c) Energy released when ions form the crystal lattice  d) They share two and three pairs
**Answer: b) Mobile electrons at the surface**

**4. ** How does a metal differ from an ionic crystal when struck?
a) It dents instead of shattering  b) R–O–R  c) Strong acids (HBr/HI)  d) Carboxylic acids
**Answer: a) It dents instead of shattering**
