# Why metals conduct and bend — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Why do metals conduct?
**Answer: Mobile sea electrons carry charge**

**2. ** Why can metals bend without breaking?
**Answer: Layers slide over the flowing electron sea**

**3. ** What gives metals their lustre?
**Answer: Mobile electrons at the surface**

**4. ** How does a metal differ from an ionic crystal when struck?
**Answer: It dents instead of shattering**
