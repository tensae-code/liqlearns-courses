# Video script — Why metals conduct and bend

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Strike an ionic crystal and it shatters; strike a metal sheet and it dents.

Metals conduct electricity and heat because the sea electrons are mobile and carry charge and energy through the lattice.

Metals are malleable and ductile because layers of kernels can slide over the sea without breaking the bond — the sea flows with them, unlike the ionic lattice.

Lustre comes from the mobile electrons interacting with light at the surface; melting behaviour follows the strength of the kernel–sea attraction.

Now you. Say it back: Why do metals conduct?
Answer: Mobile sea electrons carry charge.
