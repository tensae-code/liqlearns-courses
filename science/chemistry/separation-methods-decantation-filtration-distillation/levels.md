# Separation methods — decantation, filtration, distillation and the rest — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Decantation and filtration — the insoluble solid comes out, Magnetic separation and sublimation — one special property does the work, Distillation, crystallisation and chromatography — for what is dissolved or mixed), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, matching each of the seven procedures to a mixture, and planning a full multi-step separation of a three-component mixture.
