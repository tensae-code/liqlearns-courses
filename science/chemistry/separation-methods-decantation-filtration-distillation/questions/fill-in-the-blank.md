# Separation methods — decantation, filtration, distillation and the rest — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The solid that stays on the filter paper is called the _____.
**Answer: residue**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A magnet separates iron filings from sand because iron is _____ and sand is not.
**Answer: magnetic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) When a dissolved solid comes out of a solution as the solvent leaves, forming regular shapes, the process is _____.
**Answer: crystallisation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In distillation, the vapour is turned back into liquid by _____.
**Answer: condensation (condensing)**
