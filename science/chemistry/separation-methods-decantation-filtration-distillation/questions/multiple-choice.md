# Separation methods — decantation, filtration, distillation and the rest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Decantation separates a mixture by…
a) Boiling it  b) Letting the solid settle, then pouring the liquid off gently  c) Passing a magnet through it  d) Freezing it
**Answer: b) Letting the solid settle, then pouring the liquid off gently**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In filtration, the liquid that passes through the filter is called the…
a) Residue  b) Filtrate  c) Crystal  d) Vapour
**Answer: b) Filtrate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Iron filings can be taken out of sand by magnetic separation because…
a) Iron is heavier than sand  b) Iron dissolves in sand  c) Iron is magnetic and sand is not  d) Iron sublimes
**Answer: c) Iron is magnetic and sand is not**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Sublimation is the change where a solid…
a) Melts, then boils  b) Turns straight into vapour without melting  c) Dissolves in water  d) Turns into crystals from a solution
**Answer: b) Turns straight into vapour without melting**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Distillation recovers a liquid from a solution by…
a) Filtering it  b) Boiling it and condensing the vapour back to liquid  c) Shaking it  d) Passing it through paper
**Answer: b) Boiling it and condensing the vapour back to liquid**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Chromatography separates the components of ink because…
a) They boil at different temperatures  b) They are different sizes of grain  c) Each travels through the paper at its own speed  d) One of them is magnetic
**Answer: c) Each travels through the paper at its own speed**
