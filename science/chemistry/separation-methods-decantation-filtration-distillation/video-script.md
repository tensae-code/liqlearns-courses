Today: **Separation methods** — North Macedonia's way: seven procedures, one strand, for taking a mixture apart.

A mixture is bodies put together, each still itself — so each can be got back. If a solid will not dissolve, let the mixture rest and pour the liquid off the settled solid: that is decantation. Then pour what is left through a filter: the liquid passes, the solid stays. That is filtration.
Some mixtures part by one special property. A magnet pulls iron filings clean out of sand. A solid that sublimes turns straight into vapour when warmed, and lands pure again on a cold surface.
And what is dissolved? Boil it and catch the vapour as liquid again — distillation. Or let the liquid leave slowly and watch the solid come back as crystals. Or let colours climb a strip of paper, each at its own speed, until the mixture shows itself as bands — chromatography. Seven methods; the mixture in front of you chooses which one.
