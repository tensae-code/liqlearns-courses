# Separating mixtures — filtration and settling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe filtration of water + sand, naming what is recovered in each place.
**Answer: Pour the mixture through filter paper in a funnel. The water passes through as the filtrate and collects below; the sand is held back on the paper as the residue. Both parts of the mixture are recovered, unchanged.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is décantation less complete than filtration?
**Answer: The liquid must be poured off the settled solid gently, and the last part cannot be poured without disturbing the sediment — some liquid stays with the solid, and a little solid may pour over. A filter holds the solid back completely.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the chapter link the classroom methods to Djibouti itself?
**Answer: It carries filtration and settling up to the city's wastewater-treatment station, whose stages — screening, settling and further separation — are the same separations at city size, cleaning used water before it returns to the environment.**

**Answer: The Djibouti Grade 6 teacher guide only (Ch. 3, Activités 3–4, printed p. 35) — teacher-guide evidence, counted nowhere; no Djibouti pupil book is held.**
