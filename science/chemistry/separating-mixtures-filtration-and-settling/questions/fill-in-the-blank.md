# Separating mixtures — filtration and settling — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pouring a mixture through a filter is called _____.
**Answer: filtration**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Letting a mixture rest so the solid settles is called _____.
**Answer: décantation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The solid left on the filter paper is the _____.
**Answer: residue**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The station that cleans a city's used water is a wastewater-_____ station.
**Answer: treatment**
