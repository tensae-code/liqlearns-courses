# Hands-on — Separating mixtures — filtration and settling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two methods, one mixture — plan and compare
On paper, plan both separations for the same mixture (water + clean sand): list the equipment, the steps in order, what is recovered where, and one weakness of each method. If a supervised class practical is available with kitchen materials only, carry both out and check your plan against what actually happened — which method left the clearer water, and why?

## Station in stages
Draw the wastewater-treatment station as a flow of stages — dirty water in, clean water out — and label each stage with the separation it performs (screening, settling, further separation). Under the drawing, write which classroom method each stage grows from.
