Today: **Separating mixtures — filtration and settling**.

Sand in water — how do you get them apart? Two ways, says Djibouti's book. First: filtration. Pour the mixture through a filter. The water runs through; the sand stays on the paper. Second: décantation. Let the mixture rest. The sand sinks and settles. Then pour the water off, gently, and leave the sand behind. And these are not only classroom tricks: Djibouti's wastewater station cleans the city's used water with the same ideas, grown large — settling tanks and separating stages. Small beaker, big station — the same science.
