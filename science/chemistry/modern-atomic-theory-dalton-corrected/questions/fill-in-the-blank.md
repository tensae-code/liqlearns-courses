# Modern atomic theory — Dalton corrected — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** atoms of one element are not all _____ in mass (isotopes);
**Answer: identical**

**2. ** atoms can be changed in _____ processes;
**Answer: nuclear**

**3. ** and combining ratios need not always be simple in _____ compounds.
**Answer: complex**

**4. ** The modern theory keeps Dalton's core — reactions as _____ of atoms.
**Answer: rearrangement**
