## Modern atomic theory

Dalton's postulates are revised against later evidence: atoms are divisible into subatomic particles; atoms of one element are not all identical in mass (isotopes); atoms can be changed in nuclear processes;

and combining ratios need not always be simple in complex compounds. The modern theory keeps Dalton's core — reactions as rearrangement of atoms.
