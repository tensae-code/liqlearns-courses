# Aldehydes and ketones — the carbonyl group — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What group do aldehydes and ketones share?
**Answer: The carbonyl group, C=O**

**2. ** Where is the aldehyde carbonyl?
**Answer: At the chain end, carrying H**

**3. ** Where is the ketone carbonyl?
**Answer: Mid-chain, between two carbons**

**4. ** Write the aldehyde group.
**Answer: –CHO**
