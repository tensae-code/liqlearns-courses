# Aldehydes and ketones — the carbonyl group — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What group do aldehydes and ketones share?
a) The carbonyl group, C=O  b) Three, along x, y and z  c) s, p, d and f  d) The noble gases
**Answer: a) The carbonyl group, C=O**

**2. ** Where is the aldehyde carbonyl?
a) Forward, making products  b) The carbonyl group, C=O  c) The short-chain ones  d) At the chain end, carrying H
**Answer: d) At the chain end, carrying H**

**3. ** Where is the ketone carbonyl?
a) In steps, each with its own Ka  b) No excess H₃O⁺ or OH⁻ is made  c) Mid-chain, between two carbons  d) By exactly one proton
**Answer: c) Mid-chain, between two carbons**

**4. ** Write the aldehyde group.
a) Water  b) –CHO  c) Brine  d) Hydration with water
**Answer: b) –CHO**
