# Aldehydes and ketones — the carbonyl group — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What group do aldehydes and ketones share — The carbonyl group, C=O
**Answer: True**

**2. ** Where is the aldehyde carbonyl — The isotopes, weighted by abundance
**Answer: False**

**3. ** Where is the ketone carbonyl — Mid-chain, between two carbons
**Answer: True**

**4. ** Write the aldehyde group. — Hydrogen bonding between molecules
**Answer: False**
