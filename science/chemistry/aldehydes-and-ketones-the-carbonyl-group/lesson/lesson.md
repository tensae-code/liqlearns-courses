# Aldehydes and ketones — the carbonyl group

## Do it first
Both families carry C=O. One puts it at the very end of the chain; the other hides it in the middle. Before reading, say which is which — and why the end one always carries a hydrogen.

[Picture 21691 — Ethanal and propanone, carbonyls marked.]

Aldehydes and ketones share the carbonyl group, C=O.

In an aldehyde the carbonyl carbon is at the chain end and carries a hydrogen: –CHO.

In a ketone the carbonyl carbon sits mid-chain, bonded to two carbons. Ethanal and propanone are the section's model pair.
