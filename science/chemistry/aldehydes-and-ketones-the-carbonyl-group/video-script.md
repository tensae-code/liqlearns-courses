# Video script — Aldehydes and ketones — the carbonyl group

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Both families carry C=O.

Aldehydes and ketones share the carbonyl group, C=O.

In an aldehyde the carbonyl carbon is at the chain end and carries a hydrogen: –CHO.

In a ketone the carbonyl carbon sits mid-chain, bonded to two carbons.

Now you. Say it back: What group do aldehydes and ketones share?
Answer: The carbonyl group, C=O.
