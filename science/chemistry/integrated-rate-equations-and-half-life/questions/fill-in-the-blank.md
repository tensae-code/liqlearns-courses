# Integrated rate equations and half-life — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A zero-order concentration falls by equal _____ in equal times.
**Answer: amounts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) For first order, k = (2.303/t) _____ ([R]₀/[R]).
**Answer: log**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The first-order half-life is t½ = _____ / k.
**Answer: 0.693**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Cane-sugar inversion in excess water is _____ first order.
**Answer: pseudo**
