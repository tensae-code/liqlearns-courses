# Integrated rate equations and half-life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) For a zero-order reaction, [R] against time is…
a) A curve falling by fractions  b) A straight line — [R] = [R]₀ − kt  c) A horizontal line  d) A straight line rising
**Answer: b) A straight line — [R] = [R]₀ − kt**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The half-life of a zero-order reaction is…
a) 0.693/k  b) [R]₀/2k — it grows with the starting concentration  c) Always 1/k  d) Independent of everything
**Answer: b) [R]₀/2k — it grows with the starting concentration**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The units of a first-order rate constant are…
a) mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹  b) L mol⁻¹ s⁻¹  c) s⁻¹ — time⁻¹ only  d) Dimensionless
**Answer: c) s⁻¹ — time⁻¹ only**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In equal time steps a first-order concentration falls by equal…
a) Amounts  b) Fractions — the same proportion each step  c) Logarithms  d) Nothing — it is constant
**Answer: b) Fractions — the same proportion each step**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The half-life of a first-order reaction, t½ = 0.693/k, is special because it…
a) Depends on [R]₀  b) Is independent of the starting concentration  c) Equals the rate  d) Applies only to gases
**Answer: b) Is independent of the starting concentration**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The hydrolysis of ethyl acetate in a large excess of water is pseudo first order because…
a) Water is a product  b) Water's concentration stays effectively constant and folds into the rate constant  c) The reaction is zero order  d) Ethyl acetate is a catalyst
**Answer: b) Water's concentration stays effectively constant and folds into the rate constant**
