# Video script — The common ion effect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethanoic acid barely ionises.

The common ion effect: adding an ion that a weak electrolyte already produces suppresses its ionisation.

Le Chatelier explains it: the added common ion pushes the ionisation equilibrium back toward the un-ionised form.

It is the working principle underneath buffers, which are built next.

Now you. Say it back: What does a common ion do to ionisation?
Answer: Suppresses it.
