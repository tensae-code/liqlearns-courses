# The common ion effect — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does a common ion do to ionisation?
**Answer: Suppresses it**

**2. ** Which principle explains it?
**Answer: Le Chatelier's principle**

**3. ** Where does the equilibrium shift?
**Answer: Back toward the un-ionised form**

**4. ** What is built on this effect?
**Answer: Buffer solutions**
