# The common ion effect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does a common ion do to ionisation?
a) It rises from 1 to 8  b) Its ions are locked in place  c) Suppresses it  d) To predict new elements' properties
**Answer: c) Suppresses it**

**2. ** Which principle explains it?
a) Williamson  b) Le Chatelier's principle  c) The –OH position  d) An alkene
**Answer: b) Le Chatelier's principle**

**3. ** Where does the equilibrium shift?
a) Back toward the un-ionised form  b) The conjugate base  c) Acidic  d) It gives two replaceable H⁺
**Answer: a) Back toward the un-ionised form**

**4. ** What is built on this effect?
a) Pressing and drying  b) Fuel, solvent or disinfectant  c) Thermoplastic  d) Buffer solutions
**Answer: d) Buffer solutions**
