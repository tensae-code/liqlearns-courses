# Le Chatelier's principle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** State Le Chatelier's principle.
a) The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance  b) Energy released when ions form the crystal lattice  c) They share two and three pairs  d) Attraction of kernels to the electron pool
**Answer: a) The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance**

**2. ** What does a catalyst do to equilibrium position?
a) R–O–R  b) Strong acids (HBr/HI)  c) Carboxylic acids  d) Nothing — it shifts nothing
**Answer: d) Nothing — it shifts nothing**

**3. ** Raising pressure shifts toward which side?
a) Above 7  b) Anions are oxidised  c) The side with fewer gas moles  d) Comparing Ka and Kb
**Answer: c) The side with fewer gas moles**

**4. ** Adding reactant shifts the equilibrium…
a) The pollution lessons  b) Toward products  c) Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet  d) Rubber, cellulose or protein
**Answer: b) Toward products**
