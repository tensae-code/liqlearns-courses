# Le Chatelier's principle — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** State Le Chatelier's principle.
**Answer: The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance**

**2. ** What does a catalyst do to equilibrium position?
**Answer: Nothing — it shifts nothing**

**3. ** Raising pressure shifts toward which side?
**Answer: The side with fewer gas moles**

**4. ** Adding reactant shifts the equilibrium…
**Answer: Toward products**
