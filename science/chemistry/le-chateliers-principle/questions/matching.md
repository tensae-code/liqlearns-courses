# Le Chatelier's principle — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. State Le Chatelier's principle. — a
2. What does a catalyst do to equilibrium position? — b
3. Raising pressure shifts toward which side? — c
4. Adding reactant shifts the equilibrium… — d

**Answer key:** 1–a: The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance · 2–b: Nothing — it shifts nothing · 3–c: The side with fewer gas moles · 4–d: Toward products
