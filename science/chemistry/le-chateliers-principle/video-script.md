# Video script — Le Chatelier's principle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Squeeze an equilibrium — add reactant, crush the volume, heat it — and it shifts so as to fight your change.

Le Chatelier's principle: when a system at equilibrium is disturbed, it shifts in the direction that partly counteracts the change.

Adding a reactant shifts the equilibrium toward products; removing one shifts it back.

A catalyst does NOT shift the position of equilibrium — it speeds both directions equally, so equilibrium is reached sooner at the same position.

Now you. Say it back: State Le Chatelier's principle.
Answer: The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance.
