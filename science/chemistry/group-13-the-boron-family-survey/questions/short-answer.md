# Group 13 — the boron family survey — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) List the family in order and give the two table columns the book's survey is read from besides configuration.
**Answer: B, Al, Ga, In, Tl. Besides the ns²np¹ configurations: any two of first ionisation energies, radii in pm, melting points, electronegativities.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the family's oxide contrast in one sentence.
**Answer: Boron's oxide is acidic while alumina (aluminium's oxide) is amphoteric — the family's character turns from acidic at the head toward basic down the group.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the book open the family with gallium's photograph?
**Answer: A metal melting in a hand makes the data table's melting-point row visible and startling — the survey's method is table first, character read off the numbers.**
