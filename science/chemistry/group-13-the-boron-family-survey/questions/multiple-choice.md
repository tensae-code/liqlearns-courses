# Group 13 — the boron family survey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Group 13's outer configuration pattern is…
a) ns²np¹  b) ns¹  c) ns²  d) ns²np⁵
**Answer: a) ns²np¹**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The non-metal of the family is…
a) Aluminium  b) Boron — a metalloid  c) Gallium  d) Thallium
**Answer: b) Boron — a metalloid**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Group 13 hydroxides, unlike those of IA and IIA,…
a) Are strong alkalis  b) Are gases  c) Will not dissolve in water  d) Dissolve violently
**Answer: c) Will not dissolve in water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Boron's oxide is…, while alumina is…
a) Basic; acidic  b) Neutral; basic  c) Amphoteric; acidic  d) Acidic; amphoteric
**Answer: d) Acidic; amphoteric**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Down group 13, basic character…
a) Grows steadily  b) Falls steadily  c) Stays identical  d) Alternates
**Answer: a) Grows steadily**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The metal photographed melting in a hand is…
a) Sodium  b) Gallium  c) Mercury  d) Tin
**Answer: b) Gallium**
