# Hands-on — Group 13 — the boron family survey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the table (paper only)
From the lesson, write the family's five names in order with their configurations. Under each, predict from its position: metal or metalloid, oxide acidic or basic or amphoteric. Then check your predictions against the lesson's character statements and correct the row you got wrong.

## A metal that melts in the hand (paper only)
Gallium melts below body temperature but is a true metal. Write three sentences: what its melting point must be (above/below 37 °C and why), why it is still a solid in a cool room, and what its place in the family table tells you about its neighbours' melting points — using only the lesson's survey idea, table first.
