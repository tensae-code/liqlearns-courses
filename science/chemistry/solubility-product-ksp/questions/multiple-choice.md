# The solubility product — Ksp — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ksp is the product of…
a) The masses of the ions  b) The ion concentrations, each raised to its coefficient  c) The volumes mixed  d) The temperatures
**Answer: b) The ion concentrations, each raised to its coefficient**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a saturated solution of a sparingly soluble salt, the solid and its ions are…
a) All dissolved  b) In dynamic equilibrium  c) Finished reacting  d) Separated forever
**Answer: b) In dynamic equilibrium**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) On mixing, if Q > Ksp, then…
a) Nothing happens  b) The salt precipitates until Q falls to Ksp  c) The salt all dissolves  d) Q becomes negative
**Answer: b) The salt precipitates until Q falls to Ksp**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) For a 1 : 1 salt, the molar solubility s is…
a) s = Ksp  b) s = Ksp²  c) s = √Ksp  d) s = 1/Ksp
**Answer: c) s = √Ksp**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Adding a common ion to a sparingly soluble salt's solution…
a) Raises its solubility  b) Lowers its solubility  c) Destroys Ksp  d) Heats the solution
**Answer: b) Lowers its solubility**
