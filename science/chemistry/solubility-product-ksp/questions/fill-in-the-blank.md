# The solubility product — Ksp — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The constant for a dissolving sparingly soluble salt is written K_____.
**Answer: sp**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The product of ion concentrations actually present, before equilibrium is reached, is written _____.
**Answer: Q**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) For an AB₂ salt, Ksp = 4s_____.
**Answer: ³**
