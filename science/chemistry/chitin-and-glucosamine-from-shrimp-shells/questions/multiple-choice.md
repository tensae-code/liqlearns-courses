# Chitin and glucosamine — from shrimp shells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The polymer that stiffens shrimp shells is…
a) Cellulose  b) Starch  c) Chitin  d) Protein
**Answer: c) Chitin**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Chitin's chain is built of units of which kind?
a) Glucose only  b) Glucosamine-like units — an amino sugar  c) Amino acids  d) Fatty acids
**Answer: b) Glucosamine-like units — an amino sugar**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The preparation frees glucosamine from chitin by…
a) Burning  b) Acid hydrolysis — splitting the chain's links with water  c) Freezing  d) Filtration alone
**Answer: b) Acid hydrolysis — splitting the chain's links with water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The product is isolated as glucosamine hydrochloride because…
a) It is a gas  b) In the acid the glucosamine takes its stable, soluble salt form  c) It evaporates  d) It is coloured
**Answer: b) In the acid the glucosamine takes its stable, soluble salt form**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Glucosamine matters to the body as a building unit of…
a) Bone mineral  b) Cartilage — joint tissue  c) Haemoglobin  d) Cellulose fibre
**Answer: b) Cartilage — joint tissue**
