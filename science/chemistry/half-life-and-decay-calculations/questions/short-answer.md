# Half-life and decay calculations — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A sample is watched for 4 half-lives. What fraction remains, step by step?
**Answer:** ½ after the first, ¼ after the second, ⅛ after the third and 1⁄16 after the fourth — each step halves what the previous step left.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the decay curve never quite reach zero?
**Answer:** Each half-life removes only half of what remains at its start; there is always a half of a half left, so the curve approaches zero without arriving — the arithmetic of repeated halving.
