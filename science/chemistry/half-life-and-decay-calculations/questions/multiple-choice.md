# Half-life and decay calculations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The half-life of an isotope is the time taken for…
a) Half of what remains to decay  b) All of it to decay  c) Half of the original atoms to form  d) The sample to halve in size physically
**Answer: a) Half of what remains to decay**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The number of decay steps is calculated as…
a) D = total time ÷ half-life  b) D = half-life ÷ total time  c) D = total time × half-life  d) D = mass ÷ half-life
**Answer: a) D = total time ÷ half-life**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) After 3 half-lives, the fraction remaining is…
a) ⅛  b) ⅓  c) ½  d) ¼
**Answer: a) ⅛**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Uranium-238's half-life, in Egypt's table, is…
a) 4.51×10⁹ years  b) 56 hours  c) Fractions of a millisecond  d) 4.51 years
**Answer: a) 4.51×10⁹ years**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A half-life can be changed by…
a) Nothing in the laboratory — it is fixed by the nucleus  b) Heating the sample  c) Dissolving the sample  d) Applying pressure
**Answer: a) Nothing in the laboratory — it is fixed by the nucleus**
