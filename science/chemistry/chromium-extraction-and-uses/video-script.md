Today: **Chromium — extraction and uses** — South Sudan's way: the metal that armours steel.
Chromium comes from chromite, FeCr₂O₄. The road out runs through the chromates to chromium(III) oxide, and the oxide is reduced by aluminium — a fiercer oxygen-grabber than carbon. Most chromium never travels alone: as ferrochrome it goes straight into steel. There it seals the surface with an invisible oxide skin — and that is why stainless steel shines and does not rust, and why a whisper-thin coat of chrome keeps a tap bright for years.
