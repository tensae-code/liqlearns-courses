# Chromium — extraction and uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name chromium's ore and its formula.
**Answer: Chromite, FeCr₂O₄ — iron chromium oxide, the one great ore of chromium.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Outline the extraction road from chromite to chromium.
**Answer: Roast chromite with sodium carbonate in air to sodium chromate; convert to sodium dichromate and then to chromium(III) oxide; reduce the oxide with aluminium: Cr₂O₃ + 2Al → 2Cr + Al₂O₃. Much chromium is instead made as ferrochrome for steel.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is aluminium, not carbon, the reducing agent here?
**Answer: Chromium grips oxygen too strongly for the plain carbon reduction used for zinc and iron at this level; aluminium has the greater grip on oxygen and tears it away — the thermite-style reduction.**
