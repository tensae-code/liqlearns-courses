# Chromium — extraction and uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The ore of chromium is…
a) Zinc blende  b) Chromite, FeCr₂O₄  c) Bauxite  d) Haematite
**Answer: b) Chromite, FeCr₂O₄**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Chromium resists corrosion because…
a) It never meets air  b) Its surface seals itself with a thin, tough oxide layer  c) It is a noble gas  d) It is painted at the mine
**Answer: b) Its surface seals itself with a thin, tough oxide layer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Chromium(III) oxide is reduced to chromium by…
a) Carbon, like zinc oxide  b) Aluminium, in a thermite-style reduction  c) Water  d) Copper
**Answer: b) Aluminium, in a thermite-style reduction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ferrochrome is…
a) A chromium ore  b) An iron-chromium alloy fed directly to steel-making  c) A chromium pigment  d) Pure chromium
**Answer: b) An iron-chromium alloy fed directly to steel-making**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Stainless steel owes its rust-proofing to…
a) Its paint  b) Its chromium content and the surface oxide layer  c) Being kept dry  d) Its carbon content alone
**Answer: b) Its chromium content and the surface oxide layer**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Chrome plating gives an object…
a) A soft black coat  b) A thin, bright, hard, corrosion-resistant coat  c) A layer of paint  d) Magnetic power
**Answer: b) A thin, bright, hard, corrosion-resistant coat**
