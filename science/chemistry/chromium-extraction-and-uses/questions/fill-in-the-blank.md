# Chromium — extraction and uses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Chromium's ore is called _____.
**Answer: chromite**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The oxide reduced in the final step is chromium(III) _____.
**Answer: oxide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Iron and chromium reduced together make _____.
**Answer: ferrochrome**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Steel that resists rust through its chromium is called _____ steel.
**Answer: stainless**
