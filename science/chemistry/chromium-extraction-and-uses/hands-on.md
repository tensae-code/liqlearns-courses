# Hands-on — Chromium — extraction and uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The six-metals wall chart
Complete the extraction unit's wall chart for all six metals — sodium, aluminium, iron, zinc, chromium, copper: ore, formula, extraction method, one chief use each, taken from each metal's lesson. Read the chart top to bottom and say the unit's rule in one sentence.

## Shine hunt
Find five chromium users around you — a sink, cutlery, a tap, a kettle, trims — and for each write whether the chromium is there as stainless steel or as plating, and what it is protecting.
