# Water and life — the properties of water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Greece's Grade 8 book opens Unit 2 — "from water to the atom" — with which substance?
a) Salt  b) Water  c) Iron  d) Air
**Answer: b) Water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Most of the Earth's surface is covered by…
a) Forest  b) Desert  c) Water  d) Ice
**Answer: c) Water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ice floats on water because…
a) Ice is warmer  b) Ice is less dense than liquid water  c) Water pushes it up with air bubbles  d) Ice is a different substance
**Answer: b) Ice is less dense than liquid water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In the water cycle, the step where vapour becomes cloud droplets is…
a) Evaporation  b) Precipitation  c) Collection  d) Condensation
**Answer: d) Condensation**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The water cycle is driven by…
a) The Moon  b) The Sun  c) The wind alone  d) Volcanoes
**Answer: b) The Sun**
