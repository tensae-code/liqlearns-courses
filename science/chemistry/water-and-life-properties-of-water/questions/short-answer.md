# Water and life — the properties of water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does a lake freeze from the top down rather than the bottom up?
**Answer: Because ice is less dense than liquid water, the first ice forms at the surface and floats there; the floating layer then insulates the water below, which stays liquid.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one reason water's being liquid across life's temperatures matters to living things.
**Answer: Living chemistry runs in liquid water — blood, sap and cell fluids must flow; if water were a gas or a solid at ordinary temperatures, none of that transport could happen.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the four stages of the water cycle in order, starting from the sea.
**Answer: Evaporation from the sea, condensation into clouds, precipitation as rain or snow, and collection back into seas, lakes and groundwater.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does Greece's book put water before mixtures and solutions?
**Answer: Because water is the substance those chapters are built on: rain makes mixtures, the sea is a solution, and water as solvent is the next section's subject — water is the entry point to the whole journey.**
