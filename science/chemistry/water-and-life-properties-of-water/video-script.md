Today: **Water and life — the properties of water** — Greece's way, from its own book.

Greece starts its chemistry journey with water — the substance that covers most of the Earth and fills every living thing, your own body included.

Water is unusual: it stays liquid across life's temperatures, takes a long heating to boil, and dissolves more things than almost any other liquid.

And strangest of all, water expands as it freezes — ice floats, lakes freeze from the top, and life waits out the winter underneath.

The Sun drives the water round its cycle: evaporation, condensation, rain, collection — the same water, forever.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
