# Hands-on — Radioisotopes at work — and the americium smoke detector

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chain diagram
Draw the detector chain in four boxes — alpha ionises air → ions carry current → smoke stops current → alarm sounds — and say it through once without looking.

## Match isotope to job
Write the isotopes on slips (Am-241, Tc-99, Tl-201, Co-60, P tracer, O-18, C-14) and the jobs on others. Match all seven, then check against the lesson's key ideas.
