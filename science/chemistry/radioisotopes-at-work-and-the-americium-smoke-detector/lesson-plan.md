# Lesson plan — Radioisotopes at work — and the americium smoke detector

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Radioisotopes at work — and the americium smoke detector
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Radioisotopes at work — and the americium smoke detector in Egypt's Grade 11 student book (Chapter Three, STS section, printed pp. 69, 73–74): How a smoke detector works (americium-241 alpha ionises the air; smoke stops the current and triggers the alarm) and the isotope uses list: technetium-99 and thallium-201 in medical imaging, cobalt-60 gamma in food irradiation and wheat breeding, phosphorus tracers followed with a Geiger counter, oxygen-18 proving photosynthesis oxygen comes from water, and carbon-14 dating applied to a pharaonic mummy.

## Sub lessons
1. **The smoke detector — americium at work** — Inside the detector, **americium-241** emits **alpha particles that ionise the air** in a small chamber; the ions carry a tiny steady **current** between two plates. When **smoke enters, it stops the current** — the ion flow is broken — and the drop **triggers the alarm**. The radioactivity is not a hazard bolted on; it is the sensing element itself.
2. **Medicine and food — imaging and gamma** — Egypt's uses list runs the working isotopes: **technetium-99 and thallium-201 in medical imaging**, lighting up organs for the camera; **cobalt-60's gamma rays in food irradiation** — killing the microbes that spoil food — and in **wheat breeding**, where irradiation raises new varieties.
3. **Tracers and dating — following atoms** — A **phosphorus tracer** in a plant is followed with a **Geiger counter** to watch the element travel. **Oxygen-18** settled a famous question: labelled water proved **photosynthesis's oxygen comes from the water**, not the CO₂. And **carbon-14 dating** closes the list at home — applied to a **pharaonic mummy**, reading the age of ancient Egypt from its own remains.
