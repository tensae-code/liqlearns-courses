# Radioisotopes at work — and the americium smoke detector — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a smoke detector, americium-241's alpha particles…
a) Ionise the air so it carries a current  b) Burn the smoke  c) Produce light  d) Heat the room
**Answer: a) Ionise the air so it carries a current**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The alarm triggers when…
a) Smoke stops the ion current  b) The americium runs out  c) The battery is removed  d) Air stops moving
**Answer: a) Smoke stops the ion current**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Technetium-99 and thallium-201 are used for…
a) Medical imaging  b) Food irradiation  c) Smoke detection  d) Dating mummies
**Answer: a) Medical imaging**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Oxygen-18 labelling proved that photosynthesis's oxygen comes from…
a) Water  b) Carbon dioxide  c) The soil  d) Sunlight itself
**Answer: a) Water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Carbon-14 dating was applied, in Egypt's example, to…
a) A pharaonic mummy  b) A wheat field  c) A smoke detector  d) A hospital scan
**Answer: a) A pharaonic mummy**
