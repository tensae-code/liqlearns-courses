# Radioisotopes at work — and the americium smoke detector — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain the smoke detector's full chain, cause to alarm.
**Answer:** Americium-241's alpha particles ionise the chamber's air; the ions carry a small current between plates; smoke particles interrupt that current, and the detector reads the drop and sounds the alarm.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the isotope behind each job: imaging the heart, irradiating food, following phosphorus in a plant.
**Answer:** Technetium-99 (and thallium-201) for medical imaging; cobalt-60 for food irradiation; a radioactive phosphorus tracer, followed with a Geiger counter, for the plant.
