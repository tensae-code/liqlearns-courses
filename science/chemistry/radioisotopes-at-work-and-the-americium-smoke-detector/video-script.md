Today: **Radioisotopes at work — and the americium smoke detector** — Egypt's way: How a smoke detector works (americium-241 alpha ionises the air; smoke stops the current and triggers the alarm) and the isotope uses list: technetium-99 and thallium-201 in medical imaging, cobalt-60 gamma in food irradiation and wheat breeding, phosphorus tracers followed with a Geiger counter, oxygen-18 proving photosynthesis oxygen comes from water, and carbon-14 dating applied to a pharaonic mummy.
