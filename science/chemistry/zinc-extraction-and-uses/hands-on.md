# Hands-on — Zinc — extraction and uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reactivity decides
Lay the reactivity series beside the six metals of the extraction unit — sodium, aluminium, zinc, iron, copper, chromium — and for each write its extraction method from its lesson. Say the rule that joins the list: the more reactive the metal, the harder the extraction.

## Spot the zinc
Walk a room or a street and list everything made of or coated with zinc — galvanised buckets and gates, brass handles, battery cases. For each, write the property of zinc the object is using.
