# Zinc — extraction and uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name zinc's two ores with their formulas.
**Answer: Zinc blende (sphalerite), zinc sulfide ZnS — the chief ore; and calamine, zinc carbonate ZnCO₃.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the two extraction steps with their equations.
**Answer: Roast the blende in air: 2ZnS + 3O₂ → 2ZnO + 2SO₂. Then reduce the oxide with carbon: ZnO + C → Zn + CO — the zinc leaving as vapour and condensing.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does a scratch in a galvanised coating not lead to rusting, when a scratch in paint does?
**Answer: Zinc is more reactive than iron, so at a scratch the zinc corrodes preferentially — sacrificially — and the iron is protected; paint only covers, and once broken it protects nothing.**
