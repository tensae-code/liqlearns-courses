# Zinc — extraction and uses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Zinc blende is zinc _____, ZnS.
**Answer: sulfide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Heating the sulfide ore strongly in air is called _____.
**Answer: roasting**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) At furnace temperature zinc comes off as a _____ and is condensed.
**Answer: vapour**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Steel coated with zinc is called _____ steel.
**Answer: galvanised**
