# Zinc — extraction and uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The chief ore of zinc is…
a) Bauxite  b) Zinc blende, ZnS  c) Haematite  d) Chromite
**Answer: b) Zinc blende, ZnS**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Calamine, the second zinc ore, is…
a) ZnO  b) ZnSO₄  c) ZnCO₃  d) ZnCl₂
**Answer: c) ZnCO₃**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Roasting zinc blende in air produces…
a) Zinc and sulfur  b) Zinc oxide and sulfur dioxide  c) Zinc sulfide and oxygen  d) Zinc carbonate
**Answer: b) Zinc oxide and sulfur dioxide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Zinc oxide is reduced to zinc by…
a) Electrolysis of the molten oxide only  b) Carbon, the zinc coming off as vapour and condensing  c) Hydrogen from water  d) Heating alone
**Answer: b) Carbon, the zinc coming off as vapour and condensing**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Carbon reduction works for zinc because zinc is…
a) Above carbon in the reactivity series  b) Below carbon in the reactivity series  c) A non-metal  d) Unreactive like gold
**Answer: b) Below carbon in the reactivity series**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Galvanised steel resists rust because…
a) Zinc paint hides the steel  b) Zinc is more reactive and corrodes first, protecting the iron sacrificially  c) Zinc is unreactive  d) The coating is plastic
**Answer: b) Zinc is more reactive and corrodes first, protecting the iron sacrificially**
