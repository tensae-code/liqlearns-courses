Today: **Zinc — extraction and uses** — South Sudan's way: the metal that guards iron.
Zinc comes from zinc blende, ZnS. First the ore is roasted in air, turning the sulfide into zinc oxide and sulfur dioxide. Then carbon reduces the oxide — and because zinc boils low, the metal leaves the furnace as a vapour and condenses as liquid zinc. Its great job is protection: steel coated in zinc is galvanised, and the zinc rusts first so the iron does not. Zinc also joins copper to make brass, and serves in every dry-cell battery.
