# Hands-on — Nuclear fusion in the sun and on Earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two arrows
Draw fission as one big circle splitting into two smaller ones, and fusion as two small circles joining into one bigger one. Label each with Egypt's example reaction and say which way the mass defect pays out in each.

## Sort the table
Write six cards — outer electrons, nucleons, mass conserved, mass→energy, ordinary conditions, extreme conditions. Sort them into two columns, Chemical and Nuclear, then check against the lesson.
