# Nuclear fusion in the sun and on Earth — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Deuterium + tritium fuse to helium-4 plus a ______.
**Answer:** neutron

**2.** The sun fuses ______ into helium.
**Answer:** hydrogen

**3.** Chemical reactions conserve ______; nuclear reactions convert it to energy.
**Answer:** mass
