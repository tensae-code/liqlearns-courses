# Nuclear fusion in the sun and on Earth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Nuclear fusion is…
a) Light nuclei joining into a heavier nucleus  b) A heavy nucleus splitting  c) A neutron decaying  d) Electrons being shared
**Answer: a) Light nuclei joining into a heavier nucleus**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The reactor fusion reaction Egypt teaches is…
a) Deuterium + tritium → helium-4 + neutron  b) U-235 splitting into two nuclei  c) Carbon + oxygen → carbon dioxide  d) Hydrogen + oxygen → water
**Answer: a) Deuterium + tritium → helium-4 + neutron**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The sun's fusion builds which element out of hydrogen?
a) Helium  b) Carbon  c) Uranium  d) Iron
**Answer: a) Helium**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In chemical reactions, the particles rearranged are…
a) Outer electrons  b) Nucleons  c) Protons only  d) Whole nuclei
**Answer: a) Outer electrons**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Compared with chemical reactions, nuclear reactions release…
a) Vastly larger energies  b) Slightly smaller energies  c) Exactly the same energies  d) No energy
**Answer: a) Vastly larger energies**
