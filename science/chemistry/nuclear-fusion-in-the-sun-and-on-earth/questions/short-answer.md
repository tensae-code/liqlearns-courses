# Nuclear fusion in the sun and on Earth — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the fusion–fission contrast in one sentence each.
**Answer:** Fission splits a heavy nucleus into lighter pieces; fusion joins light nuclei into a heavier one. Both release the energy of their mass defect.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give two rows of Egypt's closing table from memory.
**Answer:** Any two: nucleons rearranged (nuclear) against outer electrons (chemical); mass converted to energy against mass conserved; extreme conditions against ordinary conditions; vastly larger energies against modest ones.
