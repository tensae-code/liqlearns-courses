# Lesson plan — Nuclear fusion in the sun and on Earth

## Sub lessons
1. **Fusion — joining the light nuclei** — **Nuclear fusion** is the reverse of fission: **light nuclei join into a heavier nucleus**, and the mass defect of the joining is released as energy. In reactors the worked reaction is **deuterium + tritium → helium-4 + neutron** — two hydrogen isotopes fusing into helium, with a neutron carrying off part of the energy.
2. **The sun's own steps** — The sun fuses hydrogen by its own steps, which Egypt writes as equations: **helium-3 pairs** meeting, and **a proton + deuterium** joining — step by step building helium out of hydrogen in the sun's core. The sunlight on your skin is the mass defect of those joinings, travelled across space.
3. **Nuclear vs chemical — the closing table** — The chapter closes with a contrast table. **Chemical reactions** shuffle **outer electrons**, conserve mass, and run at ordinary conditions with modest energies. **Nuclear reactions** rearrange **nucleons**, convert mass to energy by E = mc², demand **extreme conditions**, and release **vastly larger energies**. Two chemistries of change, kept apart by what actually moves.
