# Chemical analysis — qualitative and quantitative — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can quantitative work not sensibly come first?
**Answer:** A measurement needs a known target and a method fitted to it; until qualitative analysis has identified what is present, there is nothing definite to measure or to choose a method for.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two radicals a qualitative salt analysis must identify, with their other names.
**Answer:** The acidic radical — the anion — and the basic radical — the cation. Together they name the salt.
