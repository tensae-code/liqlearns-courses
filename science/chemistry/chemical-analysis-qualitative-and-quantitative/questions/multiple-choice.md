# Chemical analysis — qualitative and quantitative — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Qualitative analysis answers which question?
a) What is in the sample  b) How much is present  c) How fast it reacts  d) What colour the laboratory is
**Answer: a) What is in the sample**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The fixed order of analysis is…
a) Qualitative first, then quantitative  b) Quantitative first, then qualitative  c) Either order equally  d) Only quantitative matters
**Answer: a) Qualitative first, then quantitative**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The acidic radical of a salt is its…
a) Anion  b) Cation  c) Metal atom  d) Solvent
**Answer: a) Anion**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is one of Egypt's fields for analysis?
a) Medicine  b) Astrology  c) Cartography  d) Calligraphy
**Answer: a) Medicine**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A blood-sugar measurement, once glucose is identified, is…
a) Quantitative analysis  b) Qualitative analysis  c) Neither  d) A separation
**Answer: a) Quantitative analysis**
