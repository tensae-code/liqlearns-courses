# Hands-on — Chemical analysis — qualitative and quantitative

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two questions drill
Take three samples — a blood test, a soil report, an air-pollution reading. For each, write the qualitative question and the quantitative question it answers, in that order.

## Radical hunt
Write five salt formulas (e.g. NaCl, CuSO₄, CaCO₃, NH₄NO₃, FeCl₃). Split each into its cation and anion and label which is the basic and which the acidic radical.
