# Video script — Reactions of ethers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethers sit quietly through most reactions — that is why they make good solvents.

Ethers are relatively unreactive — inert to most reagents under ordinary conditions.

Strong acids (concentrated HBr/HI as presented) cleave the C–O bond.

Left exposed to air and light, ethers can form explosive peroxides — a storage risk the section flags.

Now you. Say it back: Are ethers reactive or inert?
Answer: Relatively inert.
