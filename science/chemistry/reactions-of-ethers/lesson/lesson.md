# Reactions of ethers

## Do it first
Ethers sit quietly through most reactions — that is why they make good solvents. Before reading, say what can still break them, and what danger old ether bottles hide.

Ethers are relatively unreactive — inert to most reagents under ordinary conditions.

Strong acids (concentrated HBr/HI as presented) cleave the C–O bond.

Left exposed to air and light, ethers can form explosive peroxides — a storage risk the section flags.

## Say it to your companion
Explain Reactions of ethers to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.
