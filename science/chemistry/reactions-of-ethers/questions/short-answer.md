# Reactions of ethers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Are ethers reactive or inert?
**Answer: Relatively inert**

**2. ** What cleaves the ether bond?
**Answer: Strong acids (HBr/HI)**

**3. ** What dangerous compounds form in old ethers?
**Answer: Peroxides**

**4. ** Why are ethers good solvents?
**Answer: They do not react with most solutes**
