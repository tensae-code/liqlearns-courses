# Reactions of ethers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Are ethers reactive or inert?
a) The difference between the two levels  b) Relatively inert  c) The neutron  d) c = λν
**Answer: b) Relatively inert**

**2. ** What cleaves the ether bond?
a) Strong acids (HBr/HI)  b) Elastically — no energy lost  c) It stays constant during the change  d) It lowers the activation energy path
**Answer: a) Strong acids (HBr/HI)**

**3. ** What dangerous compounds form in old ethers?
a) No hydrogen bonding between esters  b) Plants (seeds)  c) Margarine (solid fat)  d) Peroxides
**Answer: d) Peroxides**

**4. ** Why are ethers good solvents?
a) Ethiopia's own resource base  b) NO  c) They do not react with most solutes  d) Oxygen and water
**Answer: c) They do not react with most solutes**
