Today: **The Earth's crust and common minerals** — Sri Lanka's way: the crust read from its own abundance table.

The ground under your feet is mostly two elements: oxygen and silicon — about three-quarters of the whole crust between them. Then aluminium, iron, calcium, magnesium, and everything else in traces.
Two great families of minerals furnish that crust. The carbonates, built on the carbonate group — limestone's calcite, and dolomite carrying calcium and magnesium together. And the silicates, built on silicon and oxygen — quartz, feldspar, mica, and the gem beryl.
