# The Earth's crust and common minerals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The two elements that together make up about three-quarters of the Earth's crust are…
a) Iron and aluminium  b) Oxygen and silicon  c) Calcium and magnesium  d) Carbon and oxygen
**Answer: b) Oxygen and silicon**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A crustal abundance table (such as Table 6‑6) tells you…
a) The age of the crust  b) How much of the crust each element makes up  c) Where each mine is  d) The price of each mineral
**Answer: b) How much of the crust each element makes up**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Carbonate minerals are built on the group…
a) SiO₃  b) SO₄²⁻  c) CO₃²⁻  d) NO₃⁻
**Answer: c) CO₃²⁻**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Dolomite, CaCO₃·MgCO₃, is one mineral carrying…
a) Sodium and potassium  b) Calcium and magnesium together  c) Iron and aluminium  d) Silicon and oxygen only
**Answer: b) Calcium and magnesium together**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Silicates are the crust's largest mineral family because…
a) They are the hardest minerals  b) They are built from the crust's two most abundant elements, silicon and oxygen  c) They are all gemstones  d) They dissolve in water
**Answer: b) They are built from the crust's two most abundant elements, silicon and oxygen**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Beryl, Be₃Al₂(SiO₃)₆, belongs to which family?
a) Carbonates  b) Oxides only  c) Sulphides  d) Silicates
**Answer: d) Silicates**
