# The Earth's crust and common minerals — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The most abundant element in the Earth's crust is _____.
**Answer: oxygen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The mineral of limestone and chalk, CaCO₃, is called _____.
**Answer: calcite**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Dolomite carries calcium and _____ together in one formula.
**Answer: magnesium**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The simplest silicate, the hard glassy mineral of sand, is _____.
**Answer: quartz**
