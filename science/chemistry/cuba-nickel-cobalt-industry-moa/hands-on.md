# Hands-on — Cuba's nickel and cobalt industry — Moa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ore to export — the Moa chain
On one long strip of paper, build the chain in four boxes: lateritic ore in the ground → mining at Moa, Holguín → industrial production of nickel and cobalt compounds → nickel as Cuba's leading mining export. Under each box write one fact from the lesson. Then cover the strip and retell the chain from memory.

## Two metals, one industry — sorting cards
Make cards for nickel and cobalt and for the three other holders the book names (Canada, Russia, New Caledonia). Sort: which cards are metals Moa produces compounds of, and which are fellow nickel-holding places? Say the rule you sorted by out loud.
