# Cuba's nickel and cobalt industry — Moa — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Cuba's nickel sits in _____ ores.
**Answer: lateritic**

**2.** The Ni and Co industry stands at _____, in Holguín.
**Answer: Moa**

**3.** _____ is the leading export of Cuban mining.
**Answer: Nickel**

**4.** Moa produces compounds of nickel and _____.
**Answer: cobalt**
