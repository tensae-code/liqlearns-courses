# Cuba's nickel and cobalt industry — Moa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Where and from what ores does Cuba produce its nickel and cobalt compounds?
**Answer: At Moa, in Holguín, from lateritic ores — the mining-metallurgical industry there produces nickel and cobalt compounds industrially.**

**2.** Which four nickel holders does the Grade 11 book name?
**Answer: Canada, Cuba, Russia and New Caledonia.**

**3.** Why is Moa the metals chapter's worked example in Cuba?
**Answer: Because it is Cuba's own industry: nickel is the leading export of Cuban mining, so obtaining metals is taught through the home production, as a research-work topic in the programme.**

**4.** What is the difference between the programme's placement and the student book's placement of Moa?
**Answer: The programme (teacher evidence) sets Moa's Ni/Co production as a research-work topic inside the obtaining-metals line; the student book gives the production its own section, §5.5.**
