# Cuba's nickel and cobalt industry — Moa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Cuba's nickel is held in…
a) lateritic ores  b) sea water  c) coal seams  d) limestone
**Answer: a) lateritic ores**

**2.** The Ni and Co compounds are produced industrially at…
a) Havana  b) Moa, Holguín  c) Santiago  d) Matanzas
**Answer: b) Moa, Holguín**

**3.** Which pair of metals does the Moa industry produce compounds of?
a) iron and steel  b) copper and zinc  c) nickel and cobalt  d) lead and tin
**Answer: c) nickel and cobalt**

**4.** The book names Cuba among the world's main holders of…
a) gold  b) nickel  c) diamonds  d) uranium
**Answer: b) nickel**

**5.** Nickel is, in Cuban mining…
a) a minor by-product  b) not exported  c) the leading export  d) imported
**Answer: c) the leading export**

**6.** In the Grade 11 programme, the Moa production is set as…
a) an exam question  b) a research-work topic in the metals unit  c) a footnote  d) a song
**Answer: b) a research-work topic in the metals unit**
