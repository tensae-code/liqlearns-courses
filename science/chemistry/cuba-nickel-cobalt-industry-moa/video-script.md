Today: **Cuba's nickel and cobalt industry — Moa** — Cuba's way: metals are learned from Cuba's own mines.
In the ground at Moa, in Holguín, lie lateritic ores — and Cuba stands among the world's main nickel holders, with Canada, Russia and New Caledonia. There the industry produces compounds of two metals together: nickel and cobalt. And nickel is the leading export of Cuban mining — the chemistry of getting metals, told from the country itself.
