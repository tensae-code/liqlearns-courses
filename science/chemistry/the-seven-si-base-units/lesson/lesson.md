## The seven SI base units

The International System (1960) rests on seven base units, given in a table with quantity, name and symbol: length–metre–m, mass–kilogram–kg, time–second–s, electric current–ampere–A, temperature–kelvin–K, amount of substance–mole–mol, luminous intensity–candela–cd.
