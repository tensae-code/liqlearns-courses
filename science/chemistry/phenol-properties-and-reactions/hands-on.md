# Hands-on — Phenol — properties and reactions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Three hydroxyls compared
Build Bulgaria's Grade 10 comparison as a table: ethanol / glycerol / phenol against the tests — litmus or pH, sodium, NaOH, Cu(OH)₂ freshly precipitated, FeCl₃. Fill every cell from the two lessons (this one and the alcohols lessons) and circle the row where phenol breaks the family pattern.

## Test trail
Write phenol's identification trail as a two-step card: suspected phenol → FeCl₃ → violet confirms; → bromine water → white 2,4,6-tribromophenol confirms again. Paper only — phenol is a poison and is never handled outside the school laboratory with a teacher present.
