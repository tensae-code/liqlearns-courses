# Phenol — properties and reactions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Phenol's aqueous solution has pH…
a) Above 7  b) Exactly 7 always  c) Below 7 — it is a weak acid  d) 14
**Answer: c) Below 7 — it is a weak acid**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Phenol is made from benzene via…
a) Direct hydration  b) Chlorobenzene + NaOH  c) Fermentation  d) Electrolysis
**Answer: b) Chlorobenzene + NaOH**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Bromine water with phenol gives…
a) No reaction  b) White 2,4,6-tribromophenol  c) A silver mirror  d) Blue solution
**Answer: b) White 2,4,6-tribromophenol**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Phenol is proved by FeCl₃ giving…
a) Violet  b) Brick-red  c) White precipitate  d) Green flame
**Answer: a) Violet**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Bakelite, phenol's plastic, cannot be recycled because it…
a) Dissolves in water  b) Never softens again once set  c) Is a metal  d) Evaporates
**Answer: b) Never softens again once set**
