# Phenol — properties and reactions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write phenol's dissociation and name its salt with sodium.
**Answer: C₆H₅OH ⇌ C₆H₅O⁻ + H⁺; with sodium (or NaOH) it gives sodium phenolate.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the Grade 10 comparison prove the ring's influence on –OH?
**Answer: Phenol's solution stands below pH 7 and phenol reacts with sodium and sodium hydroxide where ethanol does not — the ring has changed the hydroxyl group's character.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name phenol's two faces in human use.
**Answer: A bactericide once standard as a 5% disinfectant — and itself a protoplasmic poison and a pollutant biology cannot clean.**
