# Phenol — properties and reactions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Phenol's old medical name is _____ acid.
**Answer: carbolic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Nitration of phenol gives _____ acid, an explosive.
**Answer: picric**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The first plastic, from phenol and formaldehyde, is _____.
**Answer: Bakelite**
