# Naming covalent compounds — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three kinds of element pairs the book says form covalent compounds, with its example for the first.
**Answer:** Non-metal with non-metal (e.g. H₂O, CO₂); metalloid with non-metal (e.g. BF₃); a few metals such as Be and Sn with non-metals.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Give the full Greek prefix run from 1 to 6 as the book uses it.
**Answer:** Mono (1), di (2), tri (3), tetra (4), penta (5), hexa (6).
