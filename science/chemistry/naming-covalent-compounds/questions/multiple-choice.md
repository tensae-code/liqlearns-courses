# Naming covalent compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which pair forms a covalent compound by the book's sorting?
a) A metal with a metal  b) A non-metal with a non-metal, e.g. CO₂  c) A metal with water  d) Two noble gases
**Answer: b) A non-metal with a non-metal, e.g. CO₂**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In PCl₃, the counts the name must announce are…
a) 1 phosphorus, 3 chlorine  b) 3 phosphorus, 1 chlorine  c) 1 phosphorus, 1 chlorine  d) 5 phosphorus, 3 chlorine
**Answer: a) 1 phosphorus, 3 chlorine**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) CO is named…
a) Carbon dioxide  b) Carbon monoxide  c) Carbon oxide  d) Monocarbon oxide
**Answer: b) Carbon monoxide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The prefix for four atoms is…
a) Tri  b) Penta  c) Tetra  d) Hexa
**Answer: c) Tetra**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) P₄O₁₀ is named…
a) Phosphorus oxide  b) Tetraphosphorus decaoxide  c) Pentaphosphorus dioxide  d) Phosphorus decaoxide
**Answer: b) Tetraphosphorus decaoxide**
