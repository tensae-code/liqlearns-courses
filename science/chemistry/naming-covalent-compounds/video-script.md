Today: **Naming covalent compounds** — Laos's way: count the atoms, and let Greek prefixes announce the count.

Covalent compounds join non-metals (and their near neighbours). Their names keep the first element's name, give the second an -ide ending, and count with mono, di, tri, tetra, penta, hexa. CO₂ is carbon dioxide; CO is carbon monoxide; P₄O₁₀ is tetraphosphorus decaoxide.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
