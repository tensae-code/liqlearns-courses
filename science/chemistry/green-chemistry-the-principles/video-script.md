# Video script — Green chemistry — the principles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Chemistry can design its own conscience.

Green chemistry designs products and processes that reduce or eliminate hazardous substances.

atom economy — design reactions so most reactant atoms end in the product (the guide works this definition with examples: mass of desired product over total mass of products, as a percentage); safer solvents (the guide's dry-cleaning case uses liquid CO₂ in place of toxic organic solvents);.

energy efficiency, use of catalysis, and designing products to degrade safely after use.

Now you. Say it back: What is atom economy?
Answer: Mass of desired product ÷ total mass of products (as %).
