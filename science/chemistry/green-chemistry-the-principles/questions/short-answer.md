# Green chemistry — the principles — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is atom economy?
**Answer: Mass of desired product ÷ total mass of products (as %)**

**2. ** Name the first green principle.
**Answer: Prevention of waste**

**3. ** Give a safer-solvent example.
**Answer: Liquid CO₂ replacing toxic solvents**

**4. ** What does green chemistry design out?
**Answer: Hazardous substances**
