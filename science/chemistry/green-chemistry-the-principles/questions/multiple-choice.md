# Green chemistry — the principles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is atom economy?
a) Its overlap concentrates density between nuclei  b) Gas  c) Mass of desired product ÷ total mass of products (as %)  d) Positive kernels
**Answer: c) Mass of desired product ÷ total mass of products (as %)**

**2. ** Name the first green principle.
a) Dilute  b) Prevention of waste  c) A silver mirror  d) An ester and water
**Answer: b) Prevention of waste**

**3. ** Give a safer-solvent example.
a) Liquid CO₂ replacing toxic solvents  b) Negative  c) The charge of one mole of electrons (≈96,500 C)  d) The anode
**Answer: a) Liquid CO₂ replacing toxic solvents**

**4. ** What does green chemistry design out?
a) Source, movement, changes, effects  b) Into water and food chains  c) Waste, at the source  d) Hazardous substances
**Answer: d) Hazardous substances**
