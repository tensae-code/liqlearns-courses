## Electron configuration

Electrons occupy main energy levels (shells) K, L, M, N around the nucleus, each shell holding a limited number of electrons, filling from the innermost out; the ground-state arrangement of an atom's electrons over its shells is its electronic configuration, written as shell numbers (sodium 2,8,1) and drawn as diagrammatic atoms with electrons on rings. Pupils practise writing configurations and drawing atoms for the first elements.
