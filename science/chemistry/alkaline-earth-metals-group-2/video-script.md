Today: **Alkaline earth metals — group 2** — Vietnam's way, from its Grade 12 book.

Group 2 — Be, Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba — holds two outer electrons, lost to form +2 ions; the family is harder and steadier than group 1.

Reactivity increases down the group: magnesium needs steam to show it, calcium reacts steadily with cold water.

Calcium hydroxide is limewater; calcium carbonate is limestone — the family's chemistry is written across buildings, bones and shells.

Flame colours mark the members: calcium brick-red, strontium crimson, barium apple-green.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
