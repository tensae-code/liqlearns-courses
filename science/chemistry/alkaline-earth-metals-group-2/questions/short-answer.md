# Alkaline earth metals — group 2 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is group 2 described as steadier than group 1?
**Answer: Its atoms must lose two outer electrons, held more tightly than group 1's single loose electron, so the metals are harder, denser and less reactive.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the reactivity pattern of group 2 with water and its reason.
**Answer: Reactivity increases down the group: magnesium barely reacts with cold water while calcium reacts steadily — the outer electrons sit farther from the nucleus lower down and are lost more easily.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What turns limewater milky, and what is the white solid?
**Answer: Carbon dioxide; the white solid is calcium carbonate, formed from the calcium hydroxide of the limewater.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name two places calcium's chemistry appears in the body or the landscape.
**Answer: Any two: bones and teeth, shells, limestone/marble hills and rocks (cement and building stone).**
