# Alkaline earth metals — group 2 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Every alkaline-earth metal atom carries how many outer electrons?
a) 1  b) 2  c) 7  d) 8
**Answer: b) 2**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Group 2 ions carry which charge?
a) +1  b) +2  c) −1  d) −2
**Answer: b) +2**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which group 2 metal reacts steadily with cold water?
a) Beryllium  b) Magnesium  c) Calcium  d) None of them
**Answer: c) Calcium**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Limewater is a solution of…
a) Calcium carbonate  b) Calcium hydroxide  c) Sodium hydroxide  d) Magnesium chloride
**Answer: b) Calcium hydroxide**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Barium's flame colour is…
a) Brick-red  b) Crimson  c) Apple-green  d) Lilac
**Answer: c) Apple-green**
