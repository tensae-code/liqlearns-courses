# Hands-on — Alkaline earth metals — group 2

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Retell and test (paper only)
Close the lesson. Write the lesson's one thing in one sentence, list its sub lessons from memory (Two outer electrons — the family character; Reactions — steadier, and stronger down the group; The calcium strand — lime, bone and shell), and under each write the example the lesson itself used. Then open the lesson and check every line against it.

## Work the lesson's own questions (paper only)
Take the three Do It Yourself tasks at the end of lesson/lesson.md. Do each on paper, in full sentences, without looking back at the sub lessons; then check each answer against the lesson text and correct in a different colour.

## Teach it onward (no materials)
Teach this lesson to someone at home in five minutes, using only examples from the lesson. Note the one question they asked that you could not answer from the lesson — that is the part to re-read.
