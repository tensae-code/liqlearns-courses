# Acid rain — formation and effects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The acid-rain precursor the strand studies first is…
a) Carbon dioxide  b) Sulfur(IV) oxide, SO₂  c) Nitrogen  d) Ozone

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The strand sits in Armenia's Grade 9 Theme 1, which is…
a) Chemical reactions only  b) Chemistry and the environment  c) Organic chemistry  d) The periodic table
**Answer: b) Chemistry and the environment**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The research lesson (printed p. 28) studies acid rain's effect on…
a) Plastic toys  b) Stone monuments and structures  c) River fish only  d) Glass windows
**Answer: b) Stone monuments and structures**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Armenia's own evidence for the damage is…
a) Import statistics  b) Its stone heritage standing in the open air  c) Laboratory glassware  d) Newspaper reports
**Answer: b) Its stone heritage standing in the open air**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) §1.10 closes the strand with…
a) Balancing equations  b) Acid rains and environmental problems  c) The mole  d) Electrolysis
**Answer: b) Acid rains and environmental problems**
