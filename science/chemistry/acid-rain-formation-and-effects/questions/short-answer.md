# Acid rain — formation and effects — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a precursor, using SO₂ as the example?
**Answer:** A precursor is the substance from which something else forms — SO₂ is the gas in the air from which acid rain forms, so the strand studies it first.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does Armenia investigate acid rain on stone monuments rather than only in a laboratory?
**Answer:** Because Armenia's carved stone heritage stands in the open air and shows the damage directly — the monuments and structures are the evidence the effect is read from.
