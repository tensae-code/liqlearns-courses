# Hands-on — Acid rain — formation and effects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the stone
Find an old stone wall, step or monument near you (looking only). Record where the stone's surface is rough, pitted or crumbling and where it is sheltered and smooth. Say how the difference fits the lesson's research strand.

## Trace the strand
On paper, draw the strand's three steps — SO₂ precursor, acid rain, stone damage — with one line under each saying what is studied there. Say the whole chain aloud from your drawing.
