# Viruses — structure, lytic and lysogenic cycles, retroviruses and prions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Viruses are obligate parasites built of capsid and envelope, with DNA or RNA genomes; phages run the _____ cycle against the lysogenic cycle.
**Answer: lytic**

**2.** Retroviruses use _____ — the exception to the central dogma — and HIV's integration into the host genome explains its latency.
**Answer: reverse transcriptase**

**3.** _____ and viroids are smaller pathogens still, and the mobile elements (plasmids, transposons) are presented as viruses' plausible ancestors.
**Answer: Prions**
