Today: **Viruses — structure, lytic and lysogenic cycles, retroviruses and prions** — Italy's way, from the book's own treatment. Viruses are obligate parasites built of capsid and envelope, with DNA or RNA genomes; phages run the lytic cycle against the lysogenic cycle. Retroviruses use reverse transcriptase — the exception to the central dogma — and HIV's integration into the host genome explains its latency. Prions and viroids are smaller pathogens still, and the mobile elements (plasmids, transposons) are presented as viruses' plausible ancestors.
