## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo F, Capitolo 14, §§1–8 — Italy's book (Grade 13)

Viruses as obligate parasites: capsid and envelope, DNA and RNA genomes; the infection cycle, phage lytic against lysogenic; RNA viruses and their pandemic history; retroviruses and reverse transcriptase as the exception to the central dogma (HIV's cycle rebuilt, integration explaining its latency); emerging viruses, DNA viruses (papillomavirus), prions and viroids as smaller pathogens still, and the mobile elements (plasmids, transposons) as viruses' plausible ancestors.

## The key ideas

- Viruses are obligate parasites built of capsid and envelope, with DNA or RNA genomes; phages run the lytic cycle against the lysogenic cycle.
- Retroviruses use reverse transcriptase — the exception to the central dogma — and HIV's integration into the host genome explains its latency.
- Prions and viroids are smaller pathogens still, and the mobile elements (plasmids, transposons) are presented as viruses' plausible ancestors.
