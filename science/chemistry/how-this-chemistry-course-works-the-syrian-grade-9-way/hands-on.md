# Hands-on — How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 9 way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design hunt (paper exercise)
Take one lesson from each Grade 9 Chemistry unit — solutions, hydrocarbons, radioactivity. For each, list where its objectives, key words, conclusion box, safety or enrichment box and self-test stand. Confirm the design is the same in all three.

## Safety first (paper exercise)
From a lesson with a safety box, copy the safety instruction in your own words and write beside it the accident it prevents. Then write the order rule this course teaches: safety read before, never after.
