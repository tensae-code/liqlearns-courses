# How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 9 way — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three Chemistry units and the span they occupy.
**Answer: Unit 4 Inorganic chemistry, Unit 5 Organic chemistry and Unit 6 Nuclear chemistry — occupying printed pp. 95–180 of the combined volume.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) List the shared design's lesson parts.
**Answer: Objectives and key words opening the lesson; “I try and I conclude” experiments with an explicit conclusion box; worked applications; enrichment and safety boxes; a self-test per lesson — with unit questions and a closing chemistry project ending the course.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does one shared design help the learner?
**Answer: The shape learned in the first unit costs no more learning in the later units — all the learner's effort goes on the new chemistry itself, not on finding how each lesson is built.**
