# How this chemistry course works — the Syrian Grade 9 way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Grade 9 book's three Chemistry units are…
a) Atomic, bonding, mole  b) Inorganic, organic and nuclear chemistry  c) Acids, metals, gases  d) Physics, chemistry, biology
**Answer: b) Inorganic, organic and nuclear chemistry**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The Chemistry units open at printed p.…
a) 54  b) 95  c) 134  d) 180
**Answer: b) 95**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Every lesson opens with…
a) Unit questions  b) Objectives and key words  c) The project  d) A safety warning only
**Answer: b) Objectives and key words**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The boxes with new weight at Grade 9 are the…
a) Enrichment boxes  b) Safety boxes  c) Title boxes  d) Answer boxes
**Answer: b) Safety boxes**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Each lesson's own check is its…
a) Self-test  b) Index entry  c) Contents line  d) Preface
**Answer: a) Self-test**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The closing chemistry project of this book is…
a) Iron rust  b) Oil refining  c) A thermal flask  d) A sundial
**Answer: b) Oil refining**
