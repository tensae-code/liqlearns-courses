# Molar volume of gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) One mole of any gas at the standard conditions occupies…
a) 1 L  b) 22.4 L  c) 44.8 L  d) 100 L
**Answer: b) 22.4 L**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The two standard conditions are…
a) 25 °C and 1 atm  b) 0 °C and 1 atm  c) 100 °C and 2 atm  d) 0 °C and 10 atm
**Answer: b) 0 °C and 1 atm**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Syria reads the fact off one-mole cylinders of…
a) Hydrogen, chlorine, argon  b) Helium, oxygen and nitrogen  c) Steam only  d) Carbon dioxide only
**Answer: b) Helium, oxygen and nitrogen**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The volume of 2 mol of a gas at the standard conditions is…
a) 22.4 L  b) 11.2 L  c) 44.8 L  d) 2 L
**Answer: c) 44.8 L**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) 44.8 L of hydrogen at the standard conditions is…
a) 1 mol  b) 2 mol  c) 4 mol  d) 0.5 mol
**Answer: b) 2 mol**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The forward rule is…
a) V = n ÷ 22.4  b) V = n × 22.4 L  c) V = m × 22.4  d) n = V × 22.4
**Answer: b) V = n × 22.4 L**
