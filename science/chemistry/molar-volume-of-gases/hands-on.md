# Hands-on — Molar volume of gases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mole bridge (paper exercise)
Complete the chain three ways on paper: 1 mol → volume; 44.8 L → amount; amount 0.5 mol → volume. Write beside each step the rule you used (× 22.4 or ÷ 22.4) and the conditions you are assuming.

## Room for a mole (measurement exercise)
22.4 L is about the volume of a large bucket-and-a-half. Measure a container at home or school (length × width × height in cm, divided by 1000 gives litres) and say how close it comes to holding one mole of gas at the standard conditions.
