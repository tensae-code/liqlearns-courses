Today: **Molar volume of gases** — the same room for every mole.

Helium, oxygen, nitrogen — one mole of each, three cylinders, one volume: 22.4 litres at 0 °C and 1 atmosphere. Forward, V = n × 22.4. Backwards, 44.8 litres of hydrogen is 2 moles.

The mole is the bridge; 22.4 litres is its gas crossing.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
