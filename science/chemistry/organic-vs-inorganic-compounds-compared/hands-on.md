# Hands-on — Organic vs inorganic compounds — compared

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rebuild the table
Draw two columns, Organic and Inorganic, and five rows — solubility, melting point, flammability, conductivity, reaction rate. Fill them from the lesson, then add the remaining rows from the key ideas and check.

## Bench prophecy
Take three new substances — sugar, sand, vegetable oil. Predict each one's bench from the table's pattern, row by row, before you look anything up.
