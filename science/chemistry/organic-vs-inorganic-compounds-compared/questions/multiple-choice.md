# Organic vs inorganic compounds — compared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which substance belongs on Egypt's inorganic bench?
a) Copper sulphate  b) Paraffin  c) Acetone  d) Glycerol
**Answer: a) Copper sulphate**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which property is one of the table's eleven rows?
a) Isomerism  b) Magnetism  c) Radioactivity  d) Hardness of water
**Answer: a) Isomerism**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Organic compounds' typical bond type, in the table, is…
a) Covalent  b) Ionic in every case  c) Metallic  d) Hydrogen only
**Answer: a) Covalent**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Compared with inorganic salts, organic compounds generally react…
a) More slowly  b) Instantly in all cases  c) Only when electrolysed  d) Never
**Answer: a) More slowly**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The organic bench's liquids include…
a) Ethanol and acetone  b) Salt solution only  c) Copper sulphate  d) Water
**Answer: a) Ethanol and acetone**
