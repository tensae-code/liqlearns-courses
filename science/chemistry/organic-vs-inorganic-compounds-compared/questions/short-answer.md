# Organic vs inorganic compounds — compared — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name five rows of the eleven-row table.
**Answer:** Any five of: structure, solubility, melting/boiling points, odour, flammability, bond type, conductivity, reaction rate, polymerisation, isomerism (the table runs to eleven rows in Egypt's drilling).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the chapter begin with a bench, not a definition?
**Answer:** The contrast is first observed — the two benches are smelled, mixed, warmed and burned — and the table generalises what the bench showed, so every row is an observation before it is a claim.
