## Bases

Arrhenius bases release hydroxide (OH⁻) ions in aqueous solution; strong bases ionise almost completely, weak bases slightly. Their general properties mirror the acids': characteristic indicator colours, a soapy feel, reaction with acids in neutralisation, and behaviour toward ammonium salts and metal ions in the qualitative work that follows in the unit.

Common bases and their preparation/uses are covered alongside.4).
