# The integrated metabolism of carbohydrates, fats and proteins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about urea cycle.
**Answer: The pathways interlock on one map: glycogen metabolism and glycogenesis, gluconeogenesis, the pentose-phosphate pathway, β-oxidation against lipogenesis, and amino-acid catabolism by transamination into the urea cycle.**

**2.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about liver.
**Answer: The liver is the hub organ of the integrated system, with adipose tissue, muscle, brain and kidney in their roles.**

**3.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about insulin.
**Answer: Hormonal regulation — insulin, glucagon, adrenaline, cortisol — controls the integrated metabolism.**
