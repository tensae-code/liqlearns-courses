# The integrated metabolism of carbohydrates, fats and proteins — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The pathways interlock on one map: glycogen metabolism and glycogenesis, gluconeogenesis, the pentose-phosphate pathway, β-oxidation against lipogenesis, and amino-acid catabolism by transamination into the _____.
**Answer: urea cycle**

**2.** The _____ is the hub organ of the integrated system, with adipose tissue, muscle, brain and kidney in their roles.
**Answer: liver**

**3.** Hormonal regulation — _____, glucagon, adrenaline, cortisol — controls the integrated metabolism.
**Answer: insulin**
