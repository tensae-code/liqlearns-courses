Today: **The integrated metabolism of carbohydrates, fats and proteins** — Italy's way, from the book's own treatment. The pathways interlock on one map: glycogen metabolism and glycogenesis, gluconeogenesis, the pentose-phosphate pathway, β-oxidation against lipogenesis, and amino-acid catabolism by transamination into the urea cycle. The liver is the hub organ of the integrated system, with adipose tissue, muscle, brain and kidney in their roles. Hormonal regulation — insulin, glucagon, adrenaline, cortisol — controls the integrated metabolism.
