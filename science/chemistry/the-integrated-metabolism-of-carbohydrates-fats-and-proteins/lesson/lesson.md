# The integrated metabolism of carbohydrates, fats and proteins

## Do it first
Before reading, say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is: the integrated metabolism of carbohydrates, fats and proteins. Then read on — this is Italy's way of teaching it, as the Italy placements describe the book's own treatment.

## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo E, Capitolo 12, §§18–23 — Italy's book (Grade 13)

The pathways interlocked: ATP from carbohydrates, lipids and proteins on one map; glycogen metabolism and glycogenesis, gluconeogenesis, the pentose-phosphate pathway; β-oxidation against lipogenesis; amino-acid catabolism by transamination into the urea cycle (ammonium eliminated as urea); organ-level integration (liver as the hub, adipose, muscle, brain, kidney); hormonal regulation — insulin, glucagon, adrenaline, cortisol.

## The key ideas

- The pathways interlock on one map: glycogen metabolism and glycogenesis, gluconeogenesis, the pentose-phosphate pathway, β-oxidation against lipogenesis, and amino-acid catabolism by transamination into the urea cycle.
- The liver is the hub organ of the integrated system, with adipose tissue, muscle, brain and kidney in their roles.
- Hormonal regulation — insulin, glucagon, adrenaline, cortisol — controls the integrated metabolism.
