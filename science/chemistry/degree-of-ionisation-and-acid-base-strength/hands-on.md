# Hands-on — Degree of ionisation — and acid–base strength

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fair-test design (paper only)
Design the zinc comparison on paper: two acids, the variables you hold equal (concentration, zinc surface, temperature, volume), the one you change, and the observation you record. Write the sentence your results would prove.

## Fraction sketches (paper only)
Draw 20 acid molecules in water twice: once for a strong acid (all split into ions), once for a weak one (only 3 of 20 split). Under each, write its degree of ionisation as a fraction and one behaviour the drawing predicts.
