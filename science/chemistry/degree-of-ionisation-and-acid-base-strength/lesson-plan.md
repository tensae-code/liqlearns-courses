# Lesson plan — Degree of ionisation — and acid–base strength

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Degree of ionisation — and acid–base strength
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** strength defined by degree of ionisation in water: strong acids and bases ionise fully, weak ones only partly — and equal-concentration acids are compared by their reaction rate with zinc (Jordan, Grade 9 Term 1 — Unit 2, Lesson 1, printed pp. 52–54)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Acids — properties and behaviour is recommended first, never forced.
