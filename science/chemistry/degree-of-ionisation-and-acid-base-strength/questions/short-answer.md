# Degree of ionisation — and acid–base strength — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define acid strength the book's way.
**Answer: By degree of ionisation in water: a strong acid ionises (almost) completely, a weak acid only partly.**

**2.** How does the zinc experiment make an invisible fraction visible?
**Answer: Equal-concentration acids are reacted with zinc; the reaction rate follows the hydrogen ions actually present, so the faster reaction exposes the larger degree of ionisation.**

**3.** Why can a weak acid at high concentration still behave gently?
**Answer: Because only a small fraction of its molecules are ionised at any moment — the free hydrogen ions, which do the reacting, are few however much acid was dissolved.**
