# Degree of ionisation — and acid–base strength — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Strength is defined by the degree of _____.
**Answer: ionisation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A weak acid ionises only _____.
**Answer: partly**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Equal-concentration acids are compared by reaction rate with _____ metal.
**Answer: zinc**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A strong acid ionises almost _____ in water.
**Answer: completely**
