# Degree of ionisation — and acid–base strength — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Degree of ionisation is…
a) The acid's concentration  b) The fraction of dissolved molecules that have split into ions  c) The pH of the solution  d) The mass of acid dissolved
**Answer: b) The fraction of dissolved molecules that have split into ions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A strong acid in water…
a) Ionises only slightly  b) Does not ionise  c) Ionises (almost) completely  d) Ionises only when heated
**Answer: c) Ionises (almost) completely**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A weak base is weak because it…
a) Is dilute  b) Ionises only partly in water  c) Contains no hydroxide at all  d) Reacts with zinc
**Answer: b) Ionises only partly in water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In the book's comparison, the acids dropped on zinc have…
a) Different concentrations  b) Equal concentrations  c) Equal volumes of zinc only  d) Different temperatures
**Answer: b) Equal concentrations**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The strong acid reacts with zinc faster because…
a) It is hotter  b) Its hydrogen ions are all present at once  c) Zinc dissolves in it whole  d) It has more molecules per bottle
**Answer: b) Its hydrogen ions are all present at once**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) Strength and concentration are…
a) The same idea  b) Opposites  c) Different ideas — ionising against amount dissolved  d) Both read off the pH scale
**Answer: c) Different ideas — ionising against amount dissolved**
