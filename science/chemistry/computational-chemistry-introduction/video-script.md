Today: **Computational chemistry — an introduction** — Vietnam's way, from its Grade 10 book.

Structural parameters — bond lengths, bond angles, shape — are computed by finding the arrangement of lowest energy.

Energy parameters compare stabilities and estimate reaction energies before any bench work.

The computation is a model: its numbers are claims, trusted when they agree with measurement.

Computation screens the unmade and the un-isolable — new drugs, catalysts and materials — before synthesis.
