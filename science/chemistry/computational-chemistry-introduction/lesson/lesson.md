## Sub lesson 1 — Computing a molecule's structure

The last practical of Vietnam's advanced Grade 10 book (Bài 11) turns the computer from simulator to calculator: **computing structural and energy parameters** of molecules. A molecule is given to the program as atoms and connections; the program works out the arrangement the molecule settles into — its **bond lengths, bond angles and shape** — the structural parameters.

This is the VSEPR lesson made numerical: where VSEPR predicts a shape from electron-pair repulsion, the computation prices every candidate shape and keeps the steadiest — the geometry of **lowest energy**. The output is a structure you can rotate, measure and compare with the shape the rules predicted.

## Sub lesson 2 — Computing a molecule's energy

The same calculation returns **energy parameters**: how much energy the molecule holds in that arrangement, and how the energy changes when the molecule bends, stretches or reacts. Comparing energies compares stabilities — the lower-energy arrangement is the one nature keeps — and reaction energies can be estimated before any bench work.

Bài 11 is an introduction, and its honesty matters: the numbers come from a **model** of the molecule (simplified atoms, simplified forces). The computed structure is a claim to test against measured bond lengths and energies — close agreement is what earns the model its trust.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why chemists compute

Computation reaches where measurement cannot go alone: molecules too unstable to isolate, reactions too fast to watch, and designs not yet made — a new drug, catalyst or material can be screened on screen before anyone synthesises it.

Vietnam places this practical beside the virtual laboratory at the end of Grade 10's advanced book: the computer as the chemist's third instrument, after the bench and the balance — one that must always answer to both.

## The key ideas

- **Structural parameters** — bond lengths, bond angles, shape — are computed by finding the arrangement of **lowest energy**.
- **Energy parameters** compare stabilities and estimate reaction energies before any bench work.
- The computation is a **model**: its numbers are claims, trusted when they agree with measurement.
- Computation screens the unmade and the un-isolable — new drugs, catalysts and materials — before synthesis.
