# Computational chemistry — an introduction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Structural parameters of a molecule include…
a) Its price and colour  b) Bond lengths, bond angles and shape  c) Its discoverer  d) Its melting point only
**Answer: b) Bond lengths, bond angles and shape**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A computation keeps the candidate shape with…
a) The most atoms  b) The lowest energy  c) The longest bonds  d) The highest energy
**Answer: b) The lowest energy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Energy parameters from a computation let a chemist…
a) Weigh the molecule  b) Compare stabilities and estimate reaction energies before bench work  c) Photograph the molecule  d) Change its formula
**Answer: b) Compare stabilities and estimate reaction energies before bench work**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Computed numbers deserve trust when…
a) They look complicated  b) They agree with measured values  c) They are very long  d) The computer is new
**Answer: b) They agree with measured values**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Which job is computation's own?
a) Washing glassware  b) Screening a not-yet-made drug candidate before synthesis  c) Holding a flask  d) Reading a thermometer
**Answer: b) Screening a not-yet-made drug candidate before synthesis**
