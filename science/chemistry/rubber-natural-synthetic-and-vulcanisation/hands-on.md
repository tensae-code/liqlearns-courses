# Hands-on — Rubber — natural, synthetic and vulcanisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Chain drawings
Draw the isoprene chain three times: coiled and separate (raw), stretched (elastic moment), and sulfur-cross-linked (vulcanised). Under each drawing write the behaviour it explains — sticky/brittle, stretch-and-return, strength and wear.

## Tyre ledger
Build the tyre-waste ledger from the lesson: several hundred million tyres a year in; the exits — crumb (name its three uses), regeneration, burning — and the 10% rule for new tyres. Mark which exit keeps the material a material, and which spends it.
