Today: **Rubber — natural, synthetic and vulcanisation** — Bulgaria's story. Latex weeps from the tree, a third of it rubber: coiled isoprene chains, sticky in heat, brittle in cold. Goodyear's sulfur stitches the coils into a network — and rubber becomes the tyre. Then come buna and buna-S, and a mountain of worn tyres to answer for.
