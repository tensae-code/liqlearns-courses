# Rubber — natural, synthetic and vulcanisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe the chain picture before and after vulcanisation.
**Answer: Before: separate, strongly coiled linear chains that slide (sticky when warm) or lock (brittle when cold). After: sulfur cross-links join the chains into one network — stronger, harder-wearing, elastic for longer.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Compare buna with natural rubber, per the lesson.
**Answer: Buna resists water and gases better, but is weaker in elasticity and wear.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name three uses of worn-tyre crumb or regenerated rubber.
**Answer: Crumb for hydro-insulation, asphalt-rubber road surfaces and playground mats; regenerated rubber with restored elasticity; and burning for oil products and technical carbon (any three as printed).**
