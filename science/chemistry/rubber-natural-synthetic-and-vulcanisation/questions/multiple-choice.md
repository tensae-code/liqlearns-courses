# Rubber — natural, synthetic and vulcanisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Natural rubber is a polymer of…
a) Ethene  b) Isoprene — 2-methyl-1,3-butadiene  c) Styrene only  d) Glucose
**Answer: b) Isoprene — 2-methyl-1,3-butadiene**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Latex as tapped holds rubber at…
a) 5%  b) 35–40%  c) 90%  d) 100%
**Answer: b) 35–40%**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Vulcanisation heats rubber with…
a) Oxygen  b) A little sulfur  c) Hydrochloric acid  d) Copper
**Answer: b) A little sulfur**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Buna is…
a) Natural latex  b) Butadiene rubber  c) A vulcanising agent  d) Tyre crumb
**Answer: b) Butadiene rubber**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Crumb rubber may enter new tyres up to…
a) 50%  b) 10%  c) 1%  d) 90%
**Answer: b) 10%**
