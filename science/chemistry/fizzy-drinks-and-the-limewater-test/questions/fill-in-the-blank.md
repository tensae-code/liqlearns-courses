# Fizzy drinks and the limewater test — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The gas dissolved in a fizzy drink is carbon _____.
**Answer: dioxide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The test liquid that turns cloudy with CO₂ is _____.
**Answer: limewater**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Collecting a gas by pushing water out of an inverted vessel is collection by _____ of water.
**Answer: displacement**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A degassed bottle weighs _____ than it did with its gas in it.
**Answer: less**
