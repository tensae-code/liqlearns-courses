# Fizzy drinks and the limewater test — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the full identification of the fizzy-drink gas: the test, the observation, and the conclusion.
**Answer: Bubble the collected gas through limewater. The limewater turns cloudy (milky). That cloudiness is the recognition test for carbon dioxide, so the gas dissolved in the drink is CO₂.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the chapter prove a gas has mass?
**Answer: By weighing: the sealed (or freshly opened, full) bottle is weighed, the gas is allowed to escape completely, and the bottle is weighed again. It weighs less, and the lost mass is the mass of the escaped gas.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why must temperature be controlled when classes compare how much gas different drinks hold?
**Answer: Because temperature changes how much gas escapes — a cold drink holds more of its gas and a warm drink gives more up. Without the same temperature, the comparison would measure temperature, not the drinks.**
