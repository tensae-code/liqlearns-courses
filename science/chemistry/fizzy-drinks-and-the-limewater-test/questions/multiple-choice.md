# Fizzy drinks and the limewater test — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A fizzy drink is a mixture of…
a) Two liquids  b) A liquid and a dissolved gas  c) A solid and a liquid  d) Two gases
**Answer: b) A liquid and a dissolved gas**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The gas is collected by…
a) Filtration  b) Displacement of water  c) Décantation  d) Heating
**Answer: b) Displacement of water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Limewater identifies carbon dioxide because it…
a) Turns blue  b) Turns cloudy (milky)  c) Fizzes  d) Changes smell
**Answer: b) Turns cloudy (milky)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The bottle weighing less after degassing proves…
a) Gas has no mass  b) The escaped gas has mass  c) The drink evaporated completely  d) The balance is broken
**Answer: b) The escaped gas has mass**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A cold fizzy drink, compared with a warm one…
a) Holds more of its gas  b) Has no gas  c) Fizzes more on opening  d) Tastes of limewater
**Answer: a) Holds more of its gas**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The limewater used in the test is prepared by…
a) Décantation only  b) Filtration  c) Degassing  d) Weighing
**Answer: b) Filtration**
