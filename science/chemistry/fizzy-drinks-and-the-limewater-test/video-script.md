Today: **Fizzy drinks and the limewater test**.

What is hiding in a fizzy drink? A gas, dissolved in the liquid while the bottle is sealed. Open it, and out it rushes. Catch it over water — it pushes the water down and fills the jar. Now test it with limewater: the limewater turns cloudy. That cloudy test names the gas — carbon dioxide. And weigh the bottle before and after the gas escapes: the bottle is lighter. The missing weight is the gas's own mass — a gas is real matter. Keep the drink cold, and more of its gas stays in. That is the whole investigation, Djibouti's way.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
