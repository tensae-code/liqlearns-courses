# Hands-on — Fizzy drinks and the limewater test

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The degassing weighing — plan it exactly
On paper, write the weighing investigation as a numbered protocol: what is weighed first, how the gas is let out completely, what is weighed second, and what result proves a gas has mass. Add the fair-test line: how temperature is kept the same for both weighings, and why the guide's temperature result makes that necessary.

## Collection over water — drawing check
Draw the collection-by-displacement apparatus: basin of water, inverted full vessel, delivery tube from the drink. Label where the gas gathers and where the water is pushed down, and write one sentence on why the collected volume can be read from the vessel.
