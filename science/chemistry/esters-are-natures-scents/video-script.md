# Video script — Esters are nature's scents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bananas, oranges and flowers smell the way they do because of one family of compounds.

Esters are the fragrant compounds responsible for many fruit and flower scents.

They occur naturally in fruits and flowers and are also made for flavourings and perfumes.

An ester forms from a carboxylic acid and an alcohol — the next lessons take up its name, properties and making.

Now you. Say it back: What family gives fruits their scents?
Answer: Esters.
