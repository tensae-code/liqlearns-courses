# Esters are nature's scents

## Do it first
Bananas, oranges and flowers smell the way they do because of one family of compounds. Before reading, guess the family — it is built from an acid and an alcohol.

[Picture 21701 — Fruits labelled with their ester scents.]

Esters are the fragrant compounds responsible for many fruit and flower scents.

They occur naturally in fruits and flowers and are also made for flavourings and perfumes.

An ester forms from a carboxylic acid and an alcohol — the next lessons take up its name, properties and making.

## Say it to your companion
Explain Esters are nature's scents to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.
