# Esters are nature's scents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What family gives fruits their scents?
a) Harvests remove it from the soil  b) Sodium hydroxide, chlorine, hydrogen  c) Clinker  d) Esters
**Answer: d) Esters**

**2. ** Where do esters occur naturally?
a) One-electron species only  b) +½ or −½  c) In fruits and flowers  d) Gamma rays
**Answer: c) In fruits and flowers**

**3. ** What two families make an ester?
a) Pure solids and liquids  b) A carboxylic acid and an alcohol  c) It breaks particle arrangements  d) ⇌ (double arrow)
**Answer: b) A carboxylic acid and an alcohol**

**4. ** What are made esters used in?
a) Flavourings and perfumes  b) No — the same ester family  c) The spoiling of fats and oils  d) OH⁻ ions
**Answer: a) Flavourings and perfumes**
