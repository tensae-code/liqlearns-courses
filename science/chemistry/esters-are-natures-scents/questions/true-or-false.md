# Esters are nature's scents — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What family gives fruits their scents — Esters
**Answer: True**

**2. ** Where do esters occur naturally — Le Chatelier's principle
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What two families make an ester — A carboxylic acid and an alcohol
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What are made esters used in — The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper
**Answer: False**
