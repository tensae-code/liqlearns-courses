# Esters are nature's scents — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What family gives fruits their scents?
**Answer: Esters**

**2. ** Where do esters occur naturally?
**Answer: In fruits and flowers**

**3. ** What two families make an ester?
**Answer: A carboxylic acid and an alcohol**

**4. ** What are made esters used in?
**Answer: Flavourings and perfumes**
