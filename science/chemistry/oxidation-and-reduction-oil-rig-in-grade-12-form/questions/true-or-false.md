# Oxidation and reduction — OIL RIG in Grade 12 form — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Expand OIL RIG. — Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What happens to oxidation number in oxidation — Anode to cathode
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What does an oxidising agent do — Takes electrons (is itself reduced)
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What does a reducing agent do — The particle's mass
**Answer: False**
