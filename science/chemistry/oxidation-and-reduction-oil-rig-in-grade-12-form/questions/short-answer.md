# Oxidation and reduction — OIL RIG in Grade 12 form — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Expand OIL RIG.
**Answer: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)**

**2. ** What happens to oxidation number in oxidation?
**Answer: It rises**

**3. ** What does an oxidising agent do?
**Answer: Takes electrons (is itself reduced)**

**4. ** What does a reducing agent do?
**Answer: Gives electrons (is itself oxidised)**
