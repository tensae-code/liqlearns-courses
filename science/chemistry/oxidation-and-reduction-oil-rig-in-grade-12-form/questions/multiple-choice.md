# Oxidation and reduction — OIL RIG in Grade 12 form — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Expand OIL RIG.
a) Distillation  b) Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)  c) The Solvay process (Unit 3)  d) Broken-down fibre from wood
**Answer: b) Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)**

**2. ** What happens to oxidation number in oxidation?
a) It rises  b) The particle's mass  c) The Aufbau principle  d) The energy needed to remove an electron
**Answer: a) It rises**

**3. ** What does an oxidising agent do?
a) They converge to one level  b) The system shifts to partly counteract a disturbance  c) The –OH carbon holds three other carbons  d) Takes electrons (is itself reduced)
**Answer: d) Takes electrons (is itself reduced)**

**4. ** What does a reducing agent do?
a) An electron-pair acceptor  b) Le Chatelier's principle  c) Gives electrons (is itself oxidised)  d) An exercise of the same kind
**Answer: c) Gives electrons (is itself oxidised)**
