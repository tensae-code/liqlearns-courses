# Video script — Oxidation and reduction — OIL RIG in Grade 12 form

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A magnesium ribbon burns blinding white in oxygen.

Oxidation is loss of electrons; reduction is gain of electrons — OIL RIG (Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain).

In oxygen terms: oxidation gains oxygen or loses hydrogen; reduction loses oxygen or gains hydrogen.

In oxidation-number terms: oxidation raises the oxidation number; reduction lowers it.

Now you. Say it back: Expand OIL RIG.
Answer: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons).
