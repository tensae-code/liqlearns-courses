# Oxidation and reduction — OIL RIG in Grade 12 form

## Do it first
A magnesium ribbon burns blinding white in oxygen. Electrons move in that flash. Before reading, state the Grade 12 definitions three ways — electrons, oxygen, oxidation number.

[Picture 21730 — Electrons leaving one species, entering another.]

Oxidation is loss of electrons; reduction is gain of electrons — OIL RIG (Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain).

In oxygen terms: oxidation gains oxygen or loses hydrogen; reduction loses oxygen or gains hydrogen.

In oxidation-number terms: oxidation raises the oxidation number; reduction lowers it. The oxidising agent takes electrons (and is itself reduced); the reducing agent gives them (and is itself oxidised).

## Say it to your companion
Explain Oxidation and reduction — OIL RIG in Grade 12 form to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.
