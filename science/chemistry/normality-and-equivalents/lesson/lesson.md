## Normality and equivalents

Normality counts equivalents per litre (molarity × the number of reactive units per formula unit, e.g. ionisable H⁺), the unit acid–base work uses; dilution problems in normality (preparing a 0.500 N solution from a 1.25 N stock style) are set.
