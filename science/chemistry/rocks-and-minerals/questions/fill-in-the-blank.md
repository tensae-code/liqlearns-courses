# Rocks and minerals — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Quartz, always SiO₂ wherever it is found, is an example of a _____.
**Answer: mineral**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How a mineral's surface takes the light — metallic, glassy or dull — is its _____.
**Answer: lustre**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Rocks that cool and solidify from molten material are called _____ rocks.
**Answer: igneous**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Rocks remade by heat and pressure without melting are called _____ rocks.
**Answer: metamorphic**
