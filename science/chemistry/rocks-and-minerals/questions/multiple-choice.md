# Rocks and minerals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A mineral is best defined as…
a) Any hard lump found in the ground  b) A naturally occurring solid with a definite chemical composition and an ordered internal structure  c) A mass of several substances gathered together  d) Anything that can be mined
**Answer: b) A naturally occurring solid with a definite chemical composition and an ordered internal structure**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The colour of a mineral's powder, drawn across an unglazed tile, is called its…
a) Lustre  b) Cleavage  c) Streak  d) Texture
**Answer: c) Streak**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Granite, visibly made of quartz, feldspar and mica grains locked together, is a rock rather than a mineral because…
a) It is too big to be a mineral  b) A rock is a natural mass of one or more minerals, while a mineral is one definite substance  c) It is found on the surface  d) It contains no crystals
**Answer: b) A rock is a natural mass of one or more minerals, while a mineral is one definite substance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A rock is identified by…
a) Its weight alone  b) The minerals it contains, the size and arrangement of its grains, and its texture  c) Its price  d) The place it was bought
**Answer: b) The minerals it contains, the size and arrangement of its grains, and its texture**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) An igneous rock forms when…
a) Layers of sediment are cemented  b) Molten rock material cools and solidifies  c) An old rock is heated and squeezed without melting  d) Minerals dissolve in water
**Answer: b) Molten rock material cools and solidifies**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A metamorphic rock is…
a) Cooled lava  b) Deposited shells and sand  c) An older rock remade by heat and pressure without melting  d) A single mineral crystal
**Answer: c) An older rock remade by heat and pressure without melting**
