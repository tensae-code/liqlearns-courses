## Miscible and immiscible liquids

Liquid pairs are described as miscible (mix in all proportions) or immiscible — the words soluble/insoluble are reserved for other phase pairs — and the outcome is again read off polarity ("like dissolves like"; why a nonpolar liquid such as octane refuses water is a review question).
