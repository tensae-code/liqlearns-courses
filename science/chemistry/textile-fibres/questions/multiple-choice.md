# Textile fibres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A fibre is…
a) A finished cloth  b) A long thin thread that can be spun and woven  c) A dye  d) A weaving machine
**Answer: b) A long thin thread that can be spun and woven**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which is a natural fibre?
a) Nylon  b) Polyester  c) Cotton  d) Rayon's chemical feedstock
**Answer: c) Cotton**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Synthetic fibres are…
a) Grown on plants  b) Shorn from animals  c) Manufactured polymers drawn into threads  d) Mined from rock
**Answer: c) Manufactured polymers drawn into threads**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In Laos's book, the Fibres chapter (Ch. 31) stands between…
a) Alcohols and Phenol  b) Plastics (Ch. 30) and Rubber (Ch. 32)  c) Petroleum and Plastics  d) Lipids and Proteins
**Answer: b) Plastics (Ch. 30) and Rubber (Ch. 32)**
