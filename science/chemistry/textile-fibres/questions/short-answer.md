# Textile fibres — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two families of fibres and one example of each, with its source.
**Answer:** Natural fibres, grown — e.g. cotton from plants, wool/silk from animals. Synthetic fibres, manufactured — e.g. nylon or polyester, polymers made by the chemical industry and drawn into threads.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why are fibres taught inside a part on fuels, chemical products and pollution?
**Answer:** Because fibres — above all the synthetic family — are chemical products: polymers supplied by the same petrochemical industry as plastics and rubber, the chapters fibres stands between.
