# Hands-on — Textile fibres

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the labels
Gather five items of clothing or cloth and read their labels (or judge by feel where no label exists). List each item and its fibre as the label states it.

## Sort by the lesson's families
Divide your list into natural and synthetic, and say the source of each fibre you named — plant, animal, or factory polymer.

## The chapter-neighbours test
Write the three chapter titles in the book's order — Plastics, Fibres, Rubber — and say in one sentence what kind of material all three chapters are about.
