## Sub lessons
1. **Fibres — the threads everything woven starts from** — Every cloth begins as fibres — long, thin threads that can be spun into yarn and woven or knitted into fabric.
2. **Natural fibres — from plants and animals** — Some fibres are grown, not made: **cotton** and other plant fibres from plants, **wool** and **silk** from animals.
3. **Synthetic fibres — made by chemistry** — The other family is manufactured: **synthetic fibres** such as nylon and polyester, built by the chemical industry from petrochemical starting materials — polymers drawn out into threads.
