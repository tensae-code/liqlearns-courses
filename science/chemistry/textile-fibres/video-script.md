Today: **Textile fibres** — Laos's way: fibres as chemical products, taught between plastics and rubber.

Cloth starts as fibres — threads to spin and weave. Some are grown: cotton from plants, wool and silk from animals. Others are made: nylon and polyester, polymers drawn into threads by the chemical industry, with their properties chosen in the making.
