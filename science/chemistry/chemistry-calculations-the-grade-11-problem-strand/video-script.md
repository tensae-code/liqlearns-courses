# Video script — Chemistry calculations — the Grade 11 problem strand

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four kinds of problem run through Grade 11 like a spine.

The method, for every calculation in the course: write what is Given, state what is Wanted, choose the Formula, then substitute with Units and check the answer's size.

Photon and atomic-mass problems: E = hν and the abundance-weighted average (Units 1).

Gas problems: the laws gathered in PV = nRT (Unit 3).

Now you. Say it back: What are the method's four steps?
Answer: Given → Wanted → Formula → Units.
