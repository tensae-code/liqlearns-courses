# Chemistry calculations — the Grade 11 problem strand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What are the method's four steps?
a) No excess H₃O⁺ or OH⁻ is made  b) Given → Wanted → Formula → Units  c) By exactly one proton  d) In steps, each with its own Ka
**Answer: b) Given → Wanted → Formula → Units**

**2. ** Which equation gathers the gas laws?
a) PV = nRT  b) Brine  c) Hydration with water  d) Water
**Answer: a) PV = nRT**

**3. ** What tool solves equilibrium concentrations?
a) The uncertainty principle  b) The orbital's orientation  c) On the right of the table  d) The ICE table
**Answer: d) The ICE table**

**4. ** What follows each worked example in the book?
a) Partial pressures  b) –OH (hydroxyl)  c) An exercise of the same kind  d) Irreversible
**Answer: c) An exercise of the same kind**
