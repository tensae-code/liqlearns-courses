# Where Bohr's model fails — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** For which species does Bohr's model work?
a) One-electron species only  b) In the nucleus  c) The light's frequency  d) Multi-electron spectra
**Answer: a) One-electron species only**

**2. ** What can Bohr's model not explain in spectra?
a) At constant T, P × V is constant  b) Glass  c) Yes, to reactants  d) Fine structure
**Answer: d) Fine structure**

**3. ** Which principle do fixed orbits conflict with?
a) Three — one per ester link  b) The fat's ester links (by water)  c) The uncertainty principle  d) Making soap by alkaline hydrolysis of fats
**Answer: c) The uncertainty principle**

**4. ** What does the model fail on, with helium?
a) Vanadium(V) oxide  b) Multi-electron spectra  c) Electricity and water  d) By-products
**Answer: b) Multi-electron spectra**
