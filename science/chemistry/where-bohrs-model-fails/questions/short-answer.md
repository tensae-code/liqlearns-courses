# Where Bohr's model fails — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** For which species does Bohr's model work?
**Answer: One-electron species only**

**2. ** What can Bohr's model not explain in spectra?
**Answer: Fine structure**

**3. ** Which principle do fixed orbits conflict with?
**Answer: The uncertainty principle**

**4. ** What does the model fail on, with helium?
**Answer: Multi-electron spectra**
