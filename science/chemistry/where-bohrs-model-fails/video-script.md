# Video script — Where Bohr's model fails

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bohr's model predicts hydrogen's lines beautifully.

Bohr's model works only for one-electron species — hydrogen and ions with a single electron.

It fails to explain the spectra of multi-electron atoms.

It cannot account for fine structure — lines that split into close pairs under high resolution.

Its fixed orbits conflict with the uncertainty principle.

Now you. Say it back: For which species does Bohr's model work?
Answer: One-electron species only.
