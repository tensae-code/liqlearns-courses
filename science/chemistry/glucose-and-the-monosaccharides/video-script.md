Today: **Glucose and the monosaccharides** — Bulgaria's deduction. Heat it: C, H, O — C₆H₁₂O₆. Litmus stays neutral: no acid group. Copper hydroxide dissolves dark blue: five alcohol groups. Silver mirror: an aldehyde group. Glucose is a polyhydroxyaldehyde — the cellular fuel — and fructose beside it is the ketose of honey.
