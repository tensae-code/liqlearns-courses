# Glucose and the monosaccharides — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Glucose carries _____ hydroxyl groups.
**Answer: five**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A sugar with an aldehyde group is called an _____.
**Answer: aldose**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The monosaccharide of fruits and honey, a ketose, is _____.
**Answer: fructose**
