# Glucose and the monosaccharides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Glucose's molecular formula is…
a) C₆H₁₀O₅  b) C₆H₁₂O₆  c) C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁  d) C₂H₆O
**Answer: b) C₆H₁₂O₆**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The silver mirror in the deduction proves glucose holds…
a) A carboxyl group  b) An aldehyde group  c) A ketone group  d) No oxygen groups
**Answer: b) An aldehyde group**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Glucose is classed as an…
a) Aldose — a polyhydroxyaldehyde  b) Ketose  c) Carboxylic acid  d) Ester
**Answer: a) Aldose — a polyhydroxyaldehyde**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Fructose differs from glucose as a…
a) Larger molecule  b) Ketose — ketone group at C2  c) Salt  d) Polysaccharide
**Answer: b) Ketose — ketone group at C2**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Blood normally holds glucose at…
a) 8–12 mg per 100 cm³  b) 80–120 mg per 100 cm³  c) 800 mg per 100 cm³  d) Zero
**Answer: b) 80–120 mg per 100 cm³**
