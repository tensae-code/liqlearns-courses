# Glucose and the monosaccharides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Run Bulgaria's deduction in order, test and conclusion.
**Answer: Heating → C, H, O, C₆H₁₂O₆. Neutral litmus → no –COOH. Cu(OH)₂ dark-blue → polyhydric alcohol, five –OH. Silver mirror → aldehyde group. Conclusion: a polyhydroxyaldehyde (aldose), open-chain in equilibrium with its cyclic form.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What two reactions does glucose do as an aldehyde?
**Answer: Hydrogenation to sorbitol, and oxidation to gluconic acid.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Write the cellular-fuel equation.
**Answer: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy.**
