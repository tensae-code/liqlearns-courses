# Hands-on — Glucose and the monosaccharides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Deduction flowchart
Draw Bulgaria's deduction as a flowchart with four test boxes (heating; litmus; Cu(OH)₂; ammoniacal Ag₂O), each arrowed to the conclusion it forces. Under the chart, write the verdict sentence in full: glucose is a polyhydroxyaldehyde.

## Open chain, closed ring
Build glucose twice on paper: the open chain with the aldehyde carbon numbered 1, and the cyclic form with its C1–C5 oxygen bridge. Mark the five –OH groups in both drawings and the aldehyde group in the open form.
