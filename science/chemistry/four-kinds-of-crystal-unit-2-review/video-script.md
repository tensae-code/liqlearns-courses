Diamond is among the hardest things known; candle wax melts in the sun; both are carbon-based solids.

Ionic crystals: ions in a lattice, held by electrostatic attraction — hard, brittle, conduct when molten.

Molecular crystals: molecules held by intermolecular forces — soft, low-melting.

Covalent-network crystals: atoms covalently bonded through the whole solid, as in diamond — extremely hard and high-melting.

Metallic crystals: kernels in an electron sea — conducting and malleable.

Now you. Say it back: What particles fill a covalent-network crystal?
Answer: Atoms bonded through the whole solid.
