# Four kinds of crystal — Unit 2 review

## Do it first
Diamond is among the hardest things known; candle wax melts in the sun; both are carbon-based solids. Before the review, say what four kinds of crystal the unit has built, and which kind each is.

[Picture 21653 — Four crystals side by side.]

Ionic crystals: ions in a lattice, held by electrostatic attraction — hard, brittle, conduct when molten.

Molecular crystals: molecules held by intermolecular forces — soft, low-melting.

Covalent-network crystals: atoms covalently bonded through the whole solid, as in diamond — extremely hard and high-melting.

Metallic crystals: kernels in an electron sea — conducting and malleable. Unit 2 closes by comparing the four by particles, forces and properties.
