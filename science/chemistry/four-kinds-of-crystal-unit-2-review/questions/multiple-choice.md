# Four kinds of crystal — Unit 2 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What particles fill a covalent-network crystal?
a) Atoms bonded through the whole solid  b) PET or nylon  c) Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil  d) Pesticide and fertiliser residues
**Answer: a) Atoms bonded through the whole solid**

**2. ** Give an example of a network crystal.
a) A negative ion (anion)  b) Strong lattice attractions in all directions  c) Temporary flickering dipoles  d) Diamond
**Answer: d) Diamond**

**3. ** Which crystal kind is soft and low-melting?
a) The Williamson synthesis  b) –one  c) Molecular crystals  d) Fermentation of sugars
**Answer: c) Molecular crystals**

**4. ** What holds a metallic crystal together?
a) H₂O  b) Kernels in an electron sea  c) Nothing — it spectates  d) Neutralising power
**Answer: b) Kernels in an electron sea**
