# Four kinds of crystal — Unit 2 review — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What particles fill a covalent-network crystal?
**Answer: Atoms bonded through the whole solid**

**2. ** Give an example of a network crystal.
**Answer: Diamond**

**3. ** Which crystal kind is soft and low-melting?
**Answer: Molecular crystals**

**4. ** What holds a metallic crystal together?
**Answer: Kernels in an electron sea**
