# Naming esters — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What is an ester's first name-part from? — c
2. What is the second part from? — d
3. Name ethanol + ethanoic acid's ester. — a
4. Where do you split an ester to name it? — b

**Answer key:** 1–c: The alcohol (alkyl part) · 2–d: The acid (alkanoate part) · 3–a: Ethyl ethanoate · 4–b: At the ester oxygen
