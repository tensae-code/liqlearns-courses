# Naming esters — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is an ester's first name-part from?
**Answer: The alcohol (alkyl part)**

**2. ** What is the second part from?
**Answer: The acid (alkanoate part)**

**3. ** Name ethanol + ethanoic acid's ester.
**Answer: Ethyl ethanoate**

**4. ** Where do you split an ester to name it?
**Answer: At the ester oxygen**
