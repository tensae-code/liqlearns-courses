# Naming esters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is an ester's first name-part from?
a) The C=C double bond  b) Respiration, decay and combustion  c) The alcohol (alkyl part)  d) Molasses
**Answer: c) The alcohol (alkyl part)**

**2. ** What is the second part from?
a) d electrons can also be lost  b) The acid (alkanoate part)  c) It shrinks  d) A positive ion (cation)
**Answer: b) The acid (alkanoate part)**

**3. ** Name ethanol + ethanoic acid's ester.
a) Ethyl ethanoate  b) One with three –OH groups  c) Yeast (its enzymes)  d) Weakly polar
**Answer: a) Ethyl ethanoate**

**4. ** Where do you split an ester to name it?
a) Suppresses it  b) OH⁻ — so the solution is alkaline  c) Its range must fall in the titration's pH jump  d) At the ester oxygen
**Answer: d) At the ester oxygen**
