# Video script — Naming esters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An ester's name has two words, like a person's first name and family name — one from each parent.

An ester's name has two parts: the alkyl part from the alcohol, then the alkanoate part from the acid.

Ethyl ethanoate, for example, is ethanol's ethyl joined to ethanoic acid's ethanoate.

To name an ester, split it at the ester oxygen: name the alcohol side first, the acid side second.

Now you. Say it back: What is an ester's first name-part from?
Answer: The alcohol (alkyl part).
