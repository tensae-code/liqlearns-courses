# Sodium peroxide — breathing oxygen from a solid — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing China's book teaches in this lesson — point 1 of the key ideas.
**Answer: Na₂O₂ compared with Na₂O: with water it gives NaOH + O₂ (glowing splint, warmth), with CO₂ it gives Na₂CO₃ + O₂ — the chemistry behind oxygen supplies in breathing masks and submarines.**
