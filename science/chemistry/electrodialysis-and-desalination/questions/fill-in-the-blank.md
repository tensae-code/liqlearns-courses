# Electrodialysis and desalination — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Separating salt from seawater through ion-exchange membranes is called ______.
**Answer:** electrodialysis

**2.** In electrodialysis, Na⁺ moves through ______ membranes toward the negative electrode.
**Answer:** cation (positive-ion)

**3.** The migration of Na⁺ and Cl⁻ in electrodialysis is pictured in the book's Fig. ______.
**Answer:** 24.4
