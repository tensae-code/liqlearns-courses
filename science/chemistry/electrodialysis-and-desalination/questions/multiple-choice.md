# Electrodialysis and desalination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dissolved salt in seawater is present as…
a) Sand-like grains  b) Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions spread through the water  c) A separate salty layer  d) Gas bubbles
**Answer: b) Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions spread through the water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An ion-exchange membrane…
a) Lets every ion through  b) Lets only one kind of ion through  c) Lets water through but no ions  d) Melts the salt
**Answer: b) Lets only one kind of ion through**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In electrodialysis the Na⁺ ions migrate…
a) Toward the positive electrode  b) Toward the negative electrode, through cation membranes  c) Nowhere — they stay dissolved evenly  d) Into the air
**Answer: b) Toward the negative electrode, through cation membranes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The water left behind in the freshened channels is…
a) Saltier  b) Desalinated — its salt removed  c) Turned to steam  d) Made acidic
**Answer: b) Desalinated — its salt removed**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) Laos's book places electrodialysis inside…
a) The food-chemistry part  b) Ch. 24, Advances in electrochemistry  c) The cement chapter  d) The kinetics part
**Answer: b) Ch. 24, Advances in electrochemistry**
