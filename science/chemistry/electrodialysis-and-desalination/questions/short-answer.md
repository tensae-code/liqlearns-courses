# Electrodialysis and desalination — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) What is an ion-exchange membrane, and what are the two kinds?
**Answer:** A sheet that lets only one kind of ion pass: cation membranes pass positive ions and block negative ones; anion membranes pass negative ions and block positive ones.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Describe, step by step, what happens to Na⁺ and Cl⁻ when the current is switched on.
**Answer:** Na⁺ migrates toward the negative electrode through the cation membranes; Cl⁻ migrates toward the positive electrode through the anion membranes; the ions collect in alternate channels and the water in the other channels is left fresher (desalinated).

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can filtration not remove dissolved salt from seawater?
**Answer:** Because the salt is dissolved as ions spread evenly through the water — there are no solid grains for a filter to catch.
