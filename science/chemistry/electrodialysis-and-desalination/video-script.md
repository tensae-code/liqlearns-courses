Today: **Electrodialysis and desalination** — Laos's way: salt pulled out of seawater by electricity, through gates that pass one kind of ion at a time.

Seawater's salt is dissolved — Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions everywhere, too spread out to filter. So the ions are made to walk out: membranes that pass only positive ions, membranes that pass only negative ions, and a current to pull them. Sodium goes one way, chloride the other, and the water between is left fresh.
