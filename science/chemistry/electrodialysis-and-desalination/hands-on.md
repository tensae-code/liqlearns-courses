# Hands-on — Electrodialysis and desalination

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the stack
On paper, draw five channels in a row, separated by membranes you label alternately C (cation) and A (anion), with a − electrode at the left end and a + electrode at the right. Fill every channel with dots labelled Na⁺ and Cl⁻.

## Run the current, on paper
Now redraw the stack after the current has run: move each Na⁺ one channel toward the − side through C membranes only, and each Cl⁻ one channel toward the + side through A membranes only. Count the ions left in the middle channel and say out loud what has happened to its water.

## Say why the gates matter
Cover the membrane labels and explain to a companion, from your drawing alone, why both kinds of membrane are needed — what would go wrong if every membrane passed both ions.
