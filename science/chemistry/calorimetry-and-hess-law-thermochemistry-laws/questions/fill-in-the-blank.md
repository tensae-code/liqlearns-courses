# Calorimetry and Hess's law — the laws of thermochemistry — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The insulated vessel used to measure reaction heat is a _____.
**Answer: calorimeter**

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) The law that the route does not matter is _____ law.
**Answer: Hess's**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Reversing a reaction reverses the _____ of its ΔH.
**Answer: sign**
