# Calorimetry and Hess's law — the laws of thermochemistry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In calorimetry, the heat moved is calculated as…
a) q = m / cΔT  b) q = mcΔT  c) q = ΔT / mc  d) q = m + c + ΔT
**Answer: b) q = mcΔT**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) To turn a measured q into a molar enthalpy change you divide by…
a) The mass  b) The temperature  c) The moles reacted  d) 100
**Answer: c) The moles reacted**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The Lavoisier–Laplace law says reversing a reaction…
a) Doubles its ΔH  b) Reverses the sign of its ΔH  c) Leaves ΔH at zero  d) Makes it endothermic always
**Answer: b) Reverses the sign of its ΔH**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) If every amount in a thermochemical equation is doubled, its ΔH…
a) Is unchanged  b) Halves  c) Doubles  d) Changes sign
**Answer: c) Doubles**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Hess's law states that a reaction's total enthalpy change…
a) Depends on the route  b) Is the same by every route  c) Is always negative  d) Cannot be calculated
**Answer: b) Is the same by every route**
