Today: **Calorimetry and Hess's law — the laws of thermochemistry** — Greece's way, from its own book.

Trap a reaction's heat in water, measure the temperature change, and q = mcΔT turns warmth into a number.

Reverse a reaction and its heat flips sign. Double the amounts and the heat doubles. That is the bookkeeping.

Hess's law says the route does not matter — only the start and the finish.

So the reaction you cannot measure, you reach by a cycle: add known steps, cancel everything else, and the enthalpies add with them.
