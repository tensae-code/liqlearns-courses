# Video script — Where fats and oils come from

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Butter, niter kibbeh, sunflower oil, nug (niger seed) oil — before reading, sort the fats and oils you know by where they come from: animal or plant?.

Fats come mainly from animal sources and are solid at room temperature.

Oils come mainly from plants — seeds such as nug, sunflower and sesame in the book's local activities — and are liquid at room temperature.

Both are the same chemical family: esters of glycerol with fatty acids, built in the next lesson.

Now you. Say it back: Fats come mainly from…
Answer: Animals.
