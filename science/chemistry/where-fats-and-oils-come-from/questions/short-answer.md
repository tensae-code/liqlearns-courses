# Where fats and oils come from — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Fats come mainly from…
**Answer: Animals**

**2. ** Oils come mainly from…
**Answer: Plants (seeds)**

**3. ** Name a local oilseed in the activities.
**Answer: Nug (niger seed), sunflower or sesame**

**4. ** Are fats and oils different families?
**Answer: No — the same ester family**
