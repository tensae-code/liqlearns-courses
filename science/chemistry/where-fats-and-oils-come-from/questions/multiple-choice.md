# Where fats and oils come from — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Fats come mainly from…
a) Layers slide over the flowing electron sea  b) Molecular crystals  c) Animals  d) H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair
**Answer: c) Animals**

**2. ** Oils come mainly from…
a) No C=C bonds  b) Plants (seeds)  c) –al  d) Two
**Answer: b) Plants (seeds)**

**3. ** Name a local oilseed in the activities.
a) Nug (niger seed), sunflower or sesame  b) Oxidation and reduction half-reactions  c) The anode  d) Aluminium
**Answer: a) Nug (niger seed), sunflower or sesame**

**4. ** Are fats and oils different families?
a) Photosynthesis  b) Heavy metals (from industry/mining)  c) Fertilised fields (agriculture)  d) No — the same ester family
**Answer: d) No — the same ester family**
