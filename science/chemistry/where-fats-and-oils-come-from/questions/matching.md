# Where fats and oils come from — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. Fats come mainly from… — c
2. Oils come mainly from… — d
3. Name a local oilseed in the activities. — a
4. Are fats and oils different families? — b

**Answer key:** 1–c: Animals · 2–d: Plants (seeds) · 3–a: Nug (niger seed), sunflower or sesame · 4–b: No — the same ester family
