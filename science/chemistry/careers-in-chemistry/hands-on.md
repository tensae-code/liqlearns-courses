# Hands-on — Careers in chemistry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's own exercise — eight careers (home materials — paper, old magazines or printed pictures)
Do Fiji's exercise as printed: collect or draw pictures of **eight chemistry-related careers** and paste each one on its own page or card. Under each picture write the career's name and one line saying what chemistry the person uses. The agricultural scientist and the biochemist from the book must be two of your eight.

## Career to workplace (paper exercise)
For each of your eight careers, write the place the person mainly works — field, laboratory, factory, hospital, office, outdoors — and the one thing they hand over at the end of a working day: a test result, a product, advice, a measurement. Say what joins them all: every one of them is paid to know what substances do.

## The career I would test first (writing)
Pick the one career of your eight that you would most like to try. Write five lines: what the person does, what chemistry from this course it uses, and what you would ask them if you could follow them for one day.
