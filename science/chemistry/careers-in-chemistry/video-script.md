# Video script — Careers in chemistry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Careers in chemistry** — the lesson Fiji's Year 11 book teaches on its second page.

Chemistry is not only a subject; it is people's work. Fiji's book names the agricultural scientist, testing soil and advising farmers, and the biochemist, studying the chemistry inside living things — and then it sets you a job: find pictures of eight chemistry-related careers and match each one to the work it does.

Open a chemistry book and you are also looking at a list of possible lives.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
