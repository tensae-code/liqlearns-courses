# Careers in chemistry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where does Fiji's Year 11 book teach chemistry careers?
a) In an appendix at the back  b) Inside Strand 1.1 Introduction to Chemistry, as content  c) Only on a classroom poster  d) In the glossary only
**Answer: b) Inside Strand 1.1 Introduction to Chemistry, as content**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The strand's exercise asks learners to…
a) Memorise eight formulas  b) Write eight equations  c) Paste pictures of eight chemistry-related careers  d) Visit eight factories
**Answer: c) Paste pictures of eight chemistry-related careers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An agricultural scientist's chemistry work centres on…
a) Stars and planets  b) Soil, fertilisers and crops  c) Typing and filing  d) Car engines only
**Answer: b) Soil, fertilisers and crops**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The agricultural scientist hands farmers mainly…
a) Songs  b) Test results and advice on what the soil needs  c) New land  d) Free tools
**Answer: b) Test results and advice on what the soil needs**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A biochemist studies…
a) Only rocks  b) The chemistry of living things  c) Only the weather  d) Ancient history
**Answer: b) The chemistry of living things**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The book's named pair — agricultural scientist and biochemist — together show that chemistry careers…
a) Are all the same job  b) Happen only in one laboratory  c) Form a family of different jobs in different places  d) Require no chemistry
**Answer: c) Form a family of different jobs in different places**
