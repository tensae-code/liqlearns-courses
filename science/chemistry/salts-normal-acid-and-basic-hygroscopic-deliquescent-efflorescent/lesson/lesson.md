## Sub lesson 1 — Normal, acid and basic salts

A salt is an ionic compound whose cation comes from a base and anion from an acid. Classes: normal salts (all ionisable H of the acid replaced by metal/ammonium), acid salts (only partly replaced — ionisable H remains), basic salts (contain ionisable hydroxide).
