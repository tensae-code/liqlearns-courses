# Covalent bonding and Lewis structures

## Do it first
Two hydrogen atoms approach. Neither can steal the other's electron — they are equals. Before reading, say what arrangement could give both a full shell at once.

[Picture 21638 — One pair, two pairs, three pairs shared.]

## Sub lesson 1 — Sharing a pair
In a covalent bond, atoms share a pair of electrons; both atoms count the shared pair toward their shells.

[GIF 21639 — two atoms' electrons pairing between them]

## Sub lesson 2 — Single, double, triple — and coordinate bonds
Single, double and triple bonds share one, two and three pairs. More shared pairs means a shorter, stronger bond.

## Sub lesson 3 — Drawing Lewis structures
A coordinate (dative) bond is a shared pair where both electrons come from one atom.

## Sub lesson 4 — Formal charge — checking the drawing
Lewis structures draw the sharing: count valence electrons, connect atoms, complete octets, and check with formal charge — the charge an atom would carry if sharing were perfectly equal.
