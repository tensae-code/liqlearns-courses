# Video script — Covalent bonding and Lewis structures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two hydrogen atoms approach.

In a covalent bond, atoms share a pair of electrons; both atoms count the shared pair toward their shells.

Single, double and triple bonds share one, two and three pairs.

A coordinate (dative) bond is a shared pair where both electrons come from one atom.

Lewis structures draw the sharing: count valence electrons, connect atoms, complete octets, and check with formal charge — the charge an atom would carry if sharing were perfectly equal.

Now you. Say it back: What is shared in a covalent bond?
Answer: A pair of electrons.
