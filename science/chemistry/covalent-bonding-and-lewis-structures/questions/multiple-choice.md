# Covalent bonding and Lewis structures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is shared in a covalent bond?
a) A pair of electrons  b) Inward pull on surface particles  c) The rate constant  d) Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance
**Answer: a) A pair of electrons**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** How do double and triple bonds differ?
a) Yes — it reaches equilibrium  b) The melting point  c) With a group Start-up Activity  d) They share two and three pairs
**Answer: d) They share two and three pairs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** What is a coordinate bond?
a) Ammonia  b) Pests  c) A pair donated by one atom alone  d) Manufacturing processes, incl. Ethiopia's
**Answer: c) A pair donated by one atom alone**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) ** What does formal charge check?
a) A continuous spectrum  b) Whether the Lewis structure is sound  c) Negative  d) The number of neutrons
**Answer: b) Whether the Lewis structure is sound**
