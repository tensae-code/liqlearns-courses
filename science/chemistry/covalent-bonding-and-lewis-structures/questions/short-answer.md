# Covalent bonding and Lewis structures — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is shared in a covalent bond?
**Answer: A pair of electrons**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** How do double and triple bonds differ?
**Answer: They share two and three pairs**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** What is a coordinate bond?
**Answer: A pair donated by one atom alone**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does formal charge check?
**Answer: Whether the Lewis structure is sound**
