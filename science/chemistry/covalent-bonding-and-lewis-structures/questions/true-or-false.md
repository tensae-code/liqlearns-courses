# Covalent bonding and Lewis structures — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is shared in a covalent bond — A pair of electrons
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** How do double and triple bonds differ — Oxygen and water
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** What is a coordinate bond — A pair donated by one atom alone
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** What does formal charge check — Three, along x, y and z
**Answer: False**
