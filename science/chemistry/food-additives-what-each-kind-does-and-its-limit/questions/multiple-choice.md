# Food additives — what each kind does and its limit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A food additive is…
a) The main ingredient of a food  b) A substance added to food on purpose to do a job — colour, keeping, texture, taste  c) Anything found in food by accident  d) A vitamin the body makes itself
**Answer: b) A substance added to food on purpose to do a job — colour, keeping, texture, taste**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On a label, an additive is named by…
a) Its full chemical name, always  b) A picture of the factory  c) Its E-number — one number for one named substance  d) The shop's own code
**Answer: c) Its E-number — one number for one named substance**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In Moldova's book, the kinds are sorted first by…
a) Price  b) The job each kind does in the food  c) Alphabetical order of names  d) The country they come from
**Answer: b) The job each kind does in the food**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Colourings are the additives whose job is…
a) Keeping food from spoiling  b) Sweetening  c) The food's colour  d) Making food heavier
**Answer: c) The food's colour**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) As the book presents them, sulfur dioxide and the sulfites are classed among the additives that are…
a) Toxic  b) Harmless at any amount  c) Vitamins  d) Colourings
**Answer: a) Toxic**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The book's closing advice is to…
a) Eat only packaged food  b) Avoid the long-term consumption of synthetically additivated products  c) Never read labels  d) Add more additives at home
**Answer: b) Avoid the long-term consumption of synthetically additivated products**
