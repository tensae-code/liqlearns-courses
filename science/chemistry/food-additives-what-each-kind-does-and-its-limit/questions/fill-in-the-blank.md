# Food additives — what each kind does and its limit — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On a food label, each additive is named by its _____-number.
**Answer: E**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The additives whose job is the food's colour are called _____.
**Answer: colourings**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In the book's sorting, sulfur _____ and the sulfites are classed among the toxic additives.
**Answer: dioxide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Sodium benzoate carries the E-number _____.
**Answer: E211**
