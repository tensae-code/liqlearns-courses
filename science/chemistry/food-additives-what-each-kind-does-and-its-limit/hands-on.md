# Hands-on — Food additives — what each kind does and its limit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read three real labels
Find three packaged foods — at home or in a shop — and copy out every E-number on each label, with the food's name beside it. Count how many coded additives each food carries, and which food carries the most.

## Sort your codes by job
For each E-number you found, write the job its kind does in that food where the lesson lets you say it — colour, keeping, or another working kind from sub lesson 2. Where the lesson does not name the code, write "kind not named in the lesson" — do not guess.

## Match the named ones to the book's sorting
On cards, write: sulfur dioxide, sulfites, phosphates, citric acid (E330), sodium benzoate (E211). Turn the cards over and, from memory, sort them into the book's two harm classes as sub lesson 3 presents them — classed toxic, and classed carcinogenic. Check your sorting against sub lesson 3, then say the book's closing advice out loud, in its own words.
