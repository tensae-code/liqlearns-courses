# Green chemistry and the circular economy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) List green chemistry's working principles as Bulgaria prints them.
**Answer: Prevent waste rather than clean it up; design less hazardous syntheses; save energy (avoid high temperatures and pressures); use catalysts for lower energy and safer reagents; prefer renewable/recyclable raw materials; restrict or render safe auxiliary substances such as solvents.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the ozone-thinning entries in the air census.
**Answer: N₂O, NO and the freons.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the soil census's three families.
**Answer: Over-applied nitrogen/phosphorus salts; herbicides, fungicides and insecticides; and toxic arsenic, antimony, selenium, lead, cadmium and mercury from industry and transport.**
