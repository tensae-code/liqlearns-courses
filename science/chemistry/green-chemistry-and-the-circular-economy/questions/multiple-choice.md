# Green chemistry and the circular economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A pollutant is a substance foreign to the environment or…
a) Always a metal  b) Present above permitted levels  c) Always a gas  d) Useful in industry
**Answer: b) Present above permitted levels**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The circular economy's three bends are repair, reuse and…
a) Burning  b) Recycling back into production  c) Landfill  d) Export
**Answer: b) Recycling back into production**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Green chemistry's first principle is…
a) Clean up waste faster  b) Prevent waste rather than clean it up  c) Use more solvent  d) Raise temperatures
**Answer: b) Prevent waste rather than clean it up**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The greenhouse pair in the census is…
a) SO₂ and CO  b) CO₂ and CH₄  c) NO and NO₂  d) Freons and N₂O
**Answer: b) CO₂ and CH₄**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In water, excess nutrients cause harm by…
a) Raising dissolved oxygen  b) Feeding microorganisms until dissolved oxygen falls  c) Freezing the surface  d) Removing oil
**Answer: b) Feeding microorganisms until dissolved oxygen falls**
