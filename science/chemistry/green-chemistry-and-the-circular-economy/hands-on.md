# Hands-on — Green chemistry and the circular economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Bend the line
Draw one product's linear road (dig → make → use → dump) and mark every point where Bulgaria's circle can bend it: repair, reuse in another form, recycle into production. Under each bend write what it saves — raw material, energy, or both.

## Census filing
File fifteen pollutant cards into AIR / WATER / SOIL from the lesson's census (aerosols, SO₂, freons, nitrates, oil film, detergents, discarded polymers, herbicides, lead, CO, CH₄, phosphates, mercury, N₂O, salinised groundwater) and name each card's harm in the lesson's own terms.
