# What equilibrium needs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name the three conditions for equilibrium.
**Answer: Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance**

**2. ** Why must the system be closed?
**Answer: So nothing enters or leaves**

**3. ** What must stay constant?
**Answer: Temperature**

**4. ** What kind of balance is equilibrium?
**Answer: Dynamic**
