# What equilibrium needs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name the three conditions for equilibrium.
a) Photosynthesis  b) Heavy metals (from industry/mining)  c) Fertilised fields (agriculture)  d) Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance
**Answer: d) Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance**

**2. ** Why must the system be closed?
a) Lone pairs  b) Metal properties  c) So nothing enters or leaves  d) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ (variable)
**Answer: c) So nothing enters or leaves**

**3. ** What must stay constant?
a) Acetic acid (ethanoic acid)  b) Temperature  c) Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics)  d) Mid-chain, between two carbons
**Answer: b) Temperature**

**4. ** What kind of balance is equilibrium?
a) Dynamic  b) A pH range of about two units  c) Takes electrons (is itself reduced)  d) Yes — it is one of the three factors
**Answer: a) Dynamic**
