# Video script — What equilibrium needs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tip an equilibrium mixture into an open dish, or heat it mid-experiment — the balance you were studying is gone.

Equilibrium needs a closed system — nothing enters or leaves.

It needs constant temperature.

And it is the state of dynamic balance itself — forward and reverse rates equal.

Now you. Say it back: Name the three conditions for equilibrium.
Answer: Closed system, constant temperature, dynamic balance.
