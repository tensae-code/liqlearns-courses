# What equilibrium needs

## Do it first
Tip an equilibrium mixture into an open dish, or heat it mid-experiment — the balance you were studying is gone. Before reading, list the three conditions the balance demands.

[Picture 21675 — A sealed flask with its three conditions.]

Equilibrium needs a closed system — nothing enters or leaves.

It needs constant temperature.

And it is the state of dynamic balance itself — forward and reverse rates equal. The three conditions together are the stated requirements.

## Say it to your companion
