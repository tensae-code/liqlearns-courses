## Coal

Heating coal out of contact with air (destructive distillation/coking) splits it into coke (nearly pure carbon — blast-furnace fuel, and the source of water gas, H₂ + CO, and producer gas, N₂ + CO), coal gas, and coal tar, from which aromatic hydrocarbons and many other substances are isolated by fractional distillation.5).

Coal itself is essentially an impure form of carbon which also contains hydrocarbons, given off as volatiles when it is heated in the absence of air; what remains after heating is a purer form of carbon — coke.
