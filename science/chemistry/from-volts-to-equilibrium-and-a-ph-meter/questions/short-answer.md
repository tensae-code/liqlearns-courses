# From volts to equilibrium — and a pH meter — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Write the ΔG°–E° relation.
**Answer: ΔG° = −nFE°**

**2. ** What does a large E° imply for K?
**Answer: A very large equilibrium constant**

**3. ** What does a pH meter actually measure?
**Answer: A cell potential depending on [H₃O⁺]**

**4. ** What does the Nernst relation adjust for?
**Answer: Non-standard concentrations**
