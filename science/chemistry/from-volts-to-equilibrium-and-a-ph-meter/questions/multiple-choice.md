# From volts to equilibrium — and a pH meter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Write the ΔG°–E° relation.
a) The conjugate base  b) Acidic  c) It gives two replaceable H⁺  d) ΔG° = −nFE°
**Answer: d) ΔG° = −nFE°**

**2. ** What does a large E° imply for K?
a) Fuel, solvent or disinfectant  b) Thermoplastic  c) A very large equilibrium constant  d) Pressing and drying
**Answer: c) A very large equilibrium constant**

**3. ** What does a pH meter actually measure?
a) Valence (outer-shell) electrons  b) A cell potential depending on [H₃O⁺]  c) Singly occupy each equal orbital first  d) Effective nuclear charge and shielding
**Answer: b) A cell potential depending on [H₃O⁺]**

**4. ** What does the Nernst relation adjust for?
a) Non-standard concentrations  b) The side with fewer gas moles  c) The carbon carrying the –OH  d) Carbon dioxide and water
**Answer: a) Non-standard concentrations**
