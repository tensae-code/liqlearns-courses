# Video script — From volts to equilibrium — and a pH meter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A cell's voltage hides three other quantities inside it.

Electrical work links to free energy: ΔG° = −nFE°, where n is moles of electrons and F the Faraday constant.

E° also fixes the equilibrium constant of the cell reaction — a large E° means a reaction that goes essentially to completion.

Away from standard concentrations the Nernst relation adjusts the potential; a pH meter is a small galvanic cell whose potential depends on [H₃O⁺], which is how it measures pH.

Now you. Say it back: Write the ΔG°–E° relation.
Answer: ΔG° = −nFE°.
