# From volts to equilibrium — and a pH meter

## Do it first
A cell's voltage hides three other quantities inside it. Before reading, guess the chain: volts to energy, energy to equilibrium — and a glass probe that reads pH by voltage.

[Picture 21744 — A pH probe drawn as a small cell.]

Electrical work links to free energy: ΔG° = −nFE°, where n is moles of electrons and F the Faraday constant. Negative ΔG° matches positive E° — a spontaneous cell.

E° also fixes the equilibrium constant of the cell reaction — a large E° means a reaction that goes essentially to completion.

Away from standard concentrations the Nernst relation adjusts the potential; a pH meter is a small galvanic cell whose potential depends on [H₃O⁺], which is how it measures pH.
