# Hands-on — Nuclear fission — chain reaction and uranium enrichment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chain sketch
Draw one U-235 nucleus splitting into two fragments with three neutrons flying out; draw each neutron striking a new U-235. Count the second generation's fissions and say why the reaction sustains itself.

## The enrichment ladder
Draw a bar for natural uranium (0.7%), reactor fuel (3–5%) and weapons grade (>90%). Label each bar with its use, and write the El Dabaa plant's name under the reactor bar.
