# Nuclear fission — chain reaction and uranium enrichment — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must reactor uranium be enriched at all?
**Answer:** Natural uranium is only 0.7% U-235 — too little of the fissile isotope for a chain reaction to sustain itself in a reactor; enrichment raises the share to 3–5%.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Distinguish reactor fuel from weapon material by the numbers.
**Answer:** Reactor fuel runs at 3–5% U-235; weapons exceed 90% U-235. Same isotope, different enrichment — the share decides the use.
