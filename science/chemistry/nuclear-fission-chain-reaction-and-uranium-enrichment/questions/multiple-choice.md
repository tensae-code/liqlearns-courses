# Nuclear fission — chain reaction and uranium enrichment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Fission of uranium-235 is started by…
a) A slow neutron  b) A fast electron  c) A gamma photon only  d) Strong heating
**Answer: a) A slow neutron**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A chain reaction is self-sustaining because…
a) Each fission's neutrons can split further nuclei  b) The fuel heats itself chemically  c) U-238 splits readily  d) The neutrons multiply in water
**Answer: a) Each fission's neutrons can split further nuclei**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Natural uranium contains U-235 at about…
a) 0.7%  b) 3–5%  c) 50%  d) 99.3%
**Answer: a) 0.7%**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Power-reactor fuel is enriched to…
a) 3–5% U-235  b) 0.7% U-235  c) Over 90% U-235  d) 100% U-238
**Answer: a) 3–5% U-235**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) One gram of U-235 is equated, in Egypt's book, to burning…
a) 3 tons of coal  b) 3 kg of coal  c) 300 tons of coal  d) 1 g of coal
**Answer: a) 3 tons of coal**
