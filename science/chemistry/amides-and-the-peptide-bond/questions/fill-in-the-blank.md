# Amides and the peptide bond — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An amide's carbonyl carbon carries a _____ atom.
**Answer: nitrogen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Amide names end in -_____.
**Answer: amide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The amide link in proteins is the _____ bond.
**Answer: peptide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Amides are classed primary, secondary and _____.
**Answer: tertiary**
