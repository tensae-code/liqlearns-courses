# Amides and the peptide bond — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define an amide by its structure.
**Answer: A carboxylic-acid derivative whose carbonyl carbon carries a nitrogen atom — the C(=O)–N arrangement.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the three preparation doors the chapter names.
**Answer: Amine + carboxylic acid; amine + acyl chloride; amine + anhydride (with a second amine molecule to trap HCl where it forms).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the peptide bond?
**Answer: The amide link joining amino acids — repeated along the chain, it builds a protein.**
