# Amides and the peptide bond — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In an amide, the carbonyl carbon carries…
a) A chlorine  b) A nitrogen atom  c) A second oxygen only  d) A metal
**Answer: b) A nitrogen atom**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Amide names are made by swapping the acid's -oïque for…
a) -ol  b) -al  c) -amide  d) -oate
**Answer: c) -amide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The peptide bond links…
a) Sugars  b) Amino acids into proteins  c) Fatty acids  d) Nucleotides
**Answer: b) Amino acids into proteins**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Amides are derivatives of…
a) Alcohols  b) Alkanes  c) Carboxylic acids  d) Amines alone
**Answer: c) Carboxylic acids**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Primary, secondary and tertiary amides are sorted by…
a) Chain length  b) The nitrogen's substitution  c) Boiling point  d) Colour
**Answer: b) The nitrogen's substitution**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Preparing an amide from an acyl chloride and an amine needs two amine molecules when…
a) The product is tertiary  b) HCl must be trapped  c) Water is absent  d) It is heated
**Answer: b) HCl must be trapped**
