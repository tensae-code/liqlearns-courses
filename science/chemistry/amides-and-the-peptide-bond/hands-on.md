# Hands-on — Amides and the peptide bond

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sew the chain (paper models)
Draw three amino-acid boxes in a row. Between each pair, draw the amide link C(=O)–N and circle it; label each circled link with its protein name. Then count: a chain of 10 amino acids holds how many peptide bonds? (9.)

## Name the family (paper only)
Write the acid-name ending swap three times from the lesson (-oïque → -amide), then sort three given amide structures into primary, secondary and tertiary by their nitrogen's substitution, saying the rule aloud each time.
