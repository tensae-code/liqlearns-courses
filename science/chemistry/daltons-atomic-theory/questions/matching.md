# Dalton's atomic theory — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. According to Dalton, what is every element made of? — d
2. How do atoms join to form compounds? — a
3. What happens to atoms in a chemical reaction, in Dalton's theory? — b
4. How do atoms of different elements compare in Dalton's theory? — c

**Answer key:** 1–d: Atoms · 2–a: In fixed whole-number ratios · 3–b: They are rearranged, never created or destroyed · 4–c: They are different from each other
