# Dalton's atomic theory — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** According to Dalton, what is every element made of?
**Answer: Atoms**

**2. ** How do atoms join to form compounds?
**Answer: In fixed whole-number ratios**

**3. ** What happens to atoms in a chemical reaction, in Dalton's theory?
**Answer: They are rearranged, never created or destroyed**

**4. ** How do atoms of different elements compare in Dalton's theory?
**Answer: They are different from each other**
