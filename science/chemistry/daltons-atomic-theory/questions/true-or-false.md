# Dalton's atomic theory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** According to Dalton, what is every element made of — Atoms
**Answer: True**

**2. ** How do atoms join to form compounds — Iron
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What happens to atoms in a chemical reaction, in Dalton's theory — They are rearranged, never created or destroyed
**Answer: True**

**4. ** How do atoms of different elements compare in Dalton's theory — To predict new elements' properties
**Answer: False**
