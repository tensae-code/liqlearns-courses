# Dalton's atomic theory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** According to Dalton, what is every element made of?
a) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺ (variable)  b) Lone pairs  c) Metal properties  d) Atoms
**Answer: d) Atoms**

**2. ** How do atoms join to form compounds?
a) Mid-chain, between two carbons  b) Acetic acid (ethanoic acid)  c) In fixed whole-number ratios  d) Solvents (and, historically, anaesthetics)
**Answer: c) In fixed whole-number ratios**

**3. ** What happens to atoms in a chemical reaction, in Dalton's theory?
a) Yes — it is one of the three factors  b) They are rearranged, never created or destroyed  c) A pH range of about two units  d) Takes electrons (is itself reduced)
**Answer: b) They are rearranged, never created or destroyed**

**4. ** How do atoms of different elements compare in Dalton's theory?
a) They are different from each other  b) The monomer units in the chain  c) The atmosphere  d) Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms
**Answer: a) They are different from each other**
