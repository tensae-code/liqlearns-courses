# Video script — Dalton's atomic theory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Weigh out two samples of the same element in your mind — say two lumps of pure carbon.

Dalton's early-1800s theory says: every element is made of tiny particles called atoms.

All atoms of one element are alike; atoms of different elements are different.

Compounds form when atoms join in fixed whole-number ratios — one to one, one to two, and so on.

In a chemical reaction atoms are rearranged, never created or destroyed.

Now you. Say it back: According to Dalton, what is every element made of?
Answer: Atoms.
