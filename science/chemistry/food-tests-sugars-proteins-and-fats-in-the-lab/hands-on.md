# Hands-on — Food tests — sugars, proteins and fats in the lab

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Predict the bench (paper only)
Four unlabelled tubes hold glucose, fructose, maltose and sucrose solutions. Write the Benedict's result you predict for each after five minutes in the water bath, with the structural reason under each — the guide's Activities 8 and 10 done on paper. (No bench work: these tests are the guide's, to be read and reasoned here.)

## Read the scale (paper only)
A clinic reports four urine Benedict's results: clear, green tinge, yellow-orange, red-brown. Write each patient's scale reading from Activity 9, and the per cent band it belongs to. End with the guide's note of what a day's urine normally carries.
