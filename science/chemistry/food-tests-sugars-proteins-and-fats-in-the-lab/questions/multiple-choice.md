# Food tests — sugars, proteins and fats in the lab — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Glucose throws the red precipitate quickly because it carries a free…
a) Aldehyde group  b) Ketone group  c) Carboxyl group  d) Amino group
**Answer: a) Aldehyde group**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sucrose does not reduce Benedict's solution because…
a) It is insoluble  b) Both its reactive groups are consumed in the bond between its units  c) It is not a sugar  d) It is too sweet
**Answer: b) Both its reactive groups are consumed in the bond between its units**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A urine reading of 0.1–0.5% sugar is scaled as…
a) Healthy  b) Moderate diabetes  c) Traces  d) Severe diabetes
**Answer: c) Traces**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The protein test turning orange with alkali after nitric acid is the…
a) Brown ring  b) Biuret colour alone  c) Charring test  d) Xanthoproteic reaction
**Answer: d) Xanthoproteic reaction**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the fat hydrolysis activity, the soap is found…
a) Floating on saturated brine  b) Dissolved invisibly  c) At the tube's bottom as oil  d) As a gas
**Answer: a) Floating on saturated brine**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Concentrated sulfuric acid chars sugar black; the black is…
a) Sulfur  b) Carbon  c) Ash from the air  d) Burnt glass
**Answer: b) Carbon**
