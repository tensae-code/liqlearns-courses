# Food tests — sugars, proteins and fats in the lab — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe Activity 8's test and its two results with their structural reasons.
**Answer: Glucose and fructose are warmed with Benedict's or Fehling's solution in a water bath for five minutes. Glucose — an aldehyde carrier — throws a red precipitate quickly; fructose — a ketone carrier — gives it slowly [teacher evidence].**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the urine scale's four readings in order.
**Answer: No reaction — healthy; greenish tinge/precipitate at 0.1–0.5% — traces; yellow-orange at 1% — moderate diabetes; red-brown at 2% — severe [teacher evidence].**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State Activity 12's three class detections, one per food family.
**Answer: Carbohydrate: phenolphthalein pink with sugar, and charring to black carbon with concentrated sulfuric acid. Protein: nitric acid coagulum turning orange with alkali (xanthoproteic). Fat: cholesterol with concentrated sulfuric acid giving the red cholesterylene [teacher evidence].**
