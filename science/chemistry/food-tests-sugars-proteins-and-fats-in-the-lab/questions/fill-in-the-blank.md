# Food tests — sugars, proteins and fats in the lab — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Maltose reduces Benedict's because one _____ group stays free.
**Answer: aldehyde**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Urine sugar at 2% reads _____ diabetes on the guide's scale.
**Answer: severe**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Egg white + nitric acid, then alkali, turns _____ — the xanthoproteic reaction.
**Answer: orange**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The soap from hydrolysed fat floats on saturated _____.
**Answer: brine**
