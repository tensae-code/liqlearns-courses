# Hands-on — Ionic radius — cations and anions compared

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shrink and swell sketches (paper only)
Draw one atom twice: as its cation (outer shell removed, cloud pulled in) and a second atom as its anion (extra electron added, cloud swollen). Label which is smaller, which larger, and the electron change that caused each.

## Chart the charges (paper only)
Copy the book's pattern as a strip: groups in order, charges 1+, 2+, 3+, then 2−, − under the right groups. Under the strip, write the Cl/Cl⁻ worked pair in one line: same nucleus, one electron more, larger ion.
