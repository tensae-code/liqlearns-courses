# Ionic radius — cations and anions compared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A cation is formed by…
a) Gaining electrons  b) Losing electrons  c) Gaining protons  d) Splitting the nucleus
**Answer: b) Losing electrons**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Compared with its parent atom, a cation is…
a) Larger  b) The same size  c) Smaller  d) Twice the mass
**Answer: c) Smaller**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An anion is larger than its atom because…
a) Its nucleus grew  b) The same nuclear charge now holds more electrons, which crowd the outer shell  c) It lost a shell  d) It gained protons
**Answer: b) The same nuclear charge now holds more electrons, which crowd the outer shell**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Cl and Cl⁻ differ by…
a) One proton  b) One neutron  c) One electron  d) One shell of protons
**Answer: c) One electron**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The book's charted anion charges are…
a) 1+, 2+, 3+  b) 2− and −  c) Only 3−  d) 0
**Answer: b) 2− and −**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) Losing the outer shell entirely happens for…
a) Many metal cations  b) All anions  c) Chlorine anions  d) Noble gases only
**Answer: a) Many metal cations**
