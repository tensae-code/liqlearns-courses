# Ionic radius — cations and anions compared — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State the ionic-radius rule both ways.
**Answer: Cations are smaller than their parent atoms (electrons lost, nuclear pull tightened over fewer electrons); anions are larger (electrons gained, crowding the outer shell under the same pull).**

**2.** Work the book's pair: why is Cl⁻ larger than Cl?
**Answer: Both have 17 protons; Cl⁻ has one electron more. The unchanged nuclear charge holds an extra electron, the outer shell crowds, and the ion measures larger.**

**3.** What charge pattern does the book chart across the groups?
**Answer: Cations at 1+, 2+ and 3+ on the metal side; anions at 2− and − on the non-metal side.**
