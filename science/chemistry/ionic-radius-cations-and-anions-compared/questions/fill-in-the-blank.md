# Ionic radius — cations and anions compared — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A cation is _____ than the atom it came from.
**Answer: smaller**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An anion is formed when an atom _____ electrons.
**Answer: gains**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Cl⁻ has one _____ more than Cl.
**Answer: electron**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The same _____ charge holds more electrons in an anion.
**Answer: nuclear**
