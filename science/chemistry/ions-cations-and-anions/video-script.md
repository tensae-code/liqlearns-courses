Today: **Ions — cations and anions** — Kazakhstan's way, from the books' own treatment. An atom becomes an ion when its electrons stop balancing its protons: an atom that loses electrons becomes a positive ion — a cation — and one that gains electrons becomes a negative ion — an anion; the charge is q = p − e⁻, and an ion's properties differ from the atom it came from.
