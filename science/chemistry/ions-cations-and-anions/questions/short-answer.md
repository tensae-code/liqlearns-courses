# Ions — cations and anions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Kazakhstan's Grade 8 book (Grade 8, bilingual ed. Kaliev) teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Atoms become cations when protons outnumber electrons and anions in the reverse case; charge q = p − e⁻. (Kazakhstan, Grade 8 — Grade 8, bilingual ed. Kaliev, Ch. 1.2 Formation of ions, PDF p. 20).**

**2.** How does Kazakhstan's Grade 8 book (Grade 8, Express Publishing) teach this lesson's thing?
**Answer: An atom that loses electrons becomes a positive ion, one that gains becomes negative (same proposed slug as Book 17). (Kazakhstan, Grade 8 — Grade 8, Express Publishing, Module 1, printed p. 16).**
