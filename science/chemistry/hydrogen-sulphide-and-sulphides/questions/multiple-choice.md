# Hydrogen sulphide and sulphides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) H₂S as a gas is called…
a) Hydrosulphuric acid  b) Hydrogen sulphide  c) Sulphuric acid  d) Sodium sulphide
**Answer: b) Hydrogen sulphide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Hydrogen sulphide is the compound of…
a) Hydrogen and oxygen  b) Sulphur and oxygen  c) Hydrogen and sulphur  d) Hydrogen and chlorine
**Answer: c) Hydrogen and sulphur**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) H₂S dissolved in water is called…
a) Hydrogen sulphide  b) Sulphuric acid  c) Hydrosulphuric acid  d) A sulphide
**Answer: c) Hydrosulphuric acid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The gas-name / acid-name pair in this lesson repeats the same module's pair…
a) Carbon dioxide / carbonic acid only  b) Hydrogen chloride / hydrochloric acid  c) Ammonia / ammonium hydroxide  d) Sulphur dioxide / sulphurous acid only
**Answer: b) Hydrogen chloride / hydrochloric acid**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The sulphide ion is…
a) S²⁺  b) SO₄²⁻  c) HS⁻  d) S²⁻
**Answer: d) S²⁻**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Sodium sulphide has the formula…
a) NaS  b) Na₂S  c) NaS₂  d) Na₂SO₄
**Answer: b) Na₂S**
