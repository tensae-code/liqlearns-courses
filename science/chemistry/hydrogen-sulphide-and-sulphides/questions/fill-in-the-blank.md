# Hydrogen sulphide and sulphides — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The gas H₂S is called hydrogen _____.
**Answer: sulphide**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Dissolved in water, H₂S forms _____ acid.
**Answer: hydrosulphuric**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The salts of hydrosulphuric acid are called _____.
**Answer: sulphides**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The sulphide ion carries the formula _____.
**Answer: S²⁻**
