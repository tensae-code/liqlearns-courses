# Hydrogen sulphide and sulphides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is hydrogen sulphide, and in what state is it met under that name?
**Answer: Hydrogen sulphide is the compound of hydrogen and sulphur, H₂S, met as a gas — the sulphide name belongs to the gas.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does H₂S have two names in this lesson?
**Answer: Because the name follows the state: the gas is hydrogen sulphide, and the same substance dissolved in water forms an acid solution, hydrosulphuric acid — one substance, two states, two names.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How is a sulphide built from hydrosulphuric acid?
**Answer: A metal takes the place of the acid's hydrogen; what remains of the acid is the sulphide ion, S²⁻ — H₂S with both hydrogens given up — so the salt is a metal joined to S²⁻, its formula written by valency exchange.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Write the formula of calcium sulphide and say how the charges balance.
**Answer: CaS — one Ca²⁺ balances one S²⁻ exactly, two positive charges against two negative.**
