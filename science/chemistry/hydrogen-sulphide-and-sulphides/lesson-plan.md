# Lesson plan — Hydrogen sulphide and sulphides

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Hydrogen sulphide and sulphides
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** H₂S in its two states with two names — the gas hydrogen sulphide and its aqueous solution hydrosulphuric acid — and the sulphides, sulphur's main salts (Mozambique, PESD I Módulo 4 — Lição 9, printed p. 59)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Hydrogen halides and hydrohalic acids is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Hydrogen sulphide — the gas** — **Hydrogen sulphide, H₂S**, is the compound of hydrogen and sulphur met as a **gas**, in sulphur's own lesson sequence after sulphur, its oxides and sulphuric acid.
2. **Hydrosulphuric acid — the gas in water** — Dissolved in water, the same H₂S is an acid solution with its own name: **hydrosulphuric acid** — the gas keeps the sulphide name, the solution takes the acid name, exactly as with hydrogen chloride and hydrochloric acid in the same module.
3. **Sulphides — sulphur's main salts** — Hydrosulphuric acid's salts are the **sulphides**, built on the **sulphide ion, S²⁻** — a metal in hydrogen's place, formulas written by valency exchange (Na₂S, CaS).

## Before this lesson

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Mixes the two names up → ask "gas or solution?" first: the gas is hydrogen sulphide, the solution is hydrosulphuric acid.
