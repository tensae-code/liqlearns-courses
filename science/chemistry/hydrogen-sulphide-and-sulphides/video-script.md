Today: **Hydrogen sulphide and sulphides** — Mozambique's way: one substance, two names, and its salts.
Hydrogen and sulphur join into a gas, H₂S — hydrogen sulphide. Put that gas into water and it becomes an acid, and the acid has its own name: hydrosulphuric acid. Gas — one name. Solution — another name. And the acid's salts? They are the sulphides, built on the sulphide ion, S²⁻: sodium sulphide, calcium sulphide — sulphur's main salts.
