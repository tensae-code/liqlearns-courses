# Radioactive decay — emissions and nuclear equations — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Check Egypt's alpha example by the bookkeeping.
**Answer:** ²³⁸U → ²³⁴Th + ⁴He: mass numbers 238 = 234 + 4; atomic numbers 92 = 90 + 2. Both balances hold, so the equation is a possible decay.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How do beta-minus and positron emission differ in their effect on Z?
**Answer:** Beta-minus raises Z by 1 (a neutron becomes a proton); positron emission lowers Z by 1 (a proton becomes a neutron). Both leave A unchanged.
