# Radioactive decay — emissions and nuclear equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a nuclear equation, what must balance?
a) Both mass number A and atomic number Z  b) Only the mass number  c) Only the charge of the electrons  d) The number of neutrons only
**Answer: a) Both mass number A and atomic number Z**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Alpha emission changes A and Z by…
a) A −4, Z −2  b) A unchanged, Z +1  c) A −1, Z −1  d) A +4, Z +2
**Answer: a) A −4, Z −2**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In beta-minus (negatron) emission, inside the nucleus a…
a) Neutron decays into a proton, emitting the electron  b) Proton decays into a neutron  c) Helium nucleus is emitted  d) Photon carries off a proton
**Answer: a) Neutron decays into a proton, emitting the electron**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) ²²Na → ²²Ne is an example of…
a) Positron emission  b) Alpha emission  c) Gamma-only emission  d) Fission
**Answer: a) Positron emission**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Gamma emission changes…
a) Neither A nor Z — it carries energy only  b) A by −4  c) Z by +1  d) Z by −1
**Answer: a) Neither A nor Z — it carries energy only**
