# Radioactive decay — emissions and nuclear equations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Alpha emission lowers the mass number by ______.
**Answer:** 4

**2.** In beta-minus emission a ______ inside the nucleus decays into a proton.
**Answer:** neutron

**3.** Gamma emission accompanies other decays, carrying away ______ only.
**Answer:** energy
