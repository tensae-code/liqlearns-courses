discovered by Becquerel in 1896 from a uranium salt blackening a photographic plate, the radiation sorts in an electric field into α rays (helium nuclei, charge 2+, mass 4 — ⁴₂He), β rays (fast electrons, β− or ⁰₋₁e) and γ rays (uncharged electromagnetic radiation of frequency and energy above X-rays), with penetration to match (α stopped by paper, β by aluminium, γ only by a thick lead screen, Figura 2.11);
