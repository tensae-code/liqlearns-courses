# Hands-on — Radioactive decay — emissions and nuclear equations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Balance checker
Write the four Egypt equations — U→Th, C→N, Na→Ne, and Co with its gamma — and under each, check the A sum and the Z sum on both sides. Tick both balances before moving on.

## Predict the product
Cover the product in ¹⁴C → ¹⁴N. From the rule (beta-minus: A same, Z +1), work out the product's A and Z, name the element from a periodic table, then uncover and check.
