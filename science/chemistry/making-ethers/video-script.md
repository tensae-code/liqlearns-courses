Two routes build the oxygen bridge.

The Williamson synthesis: a sodium alkoxide reacts with a haloalkane to give the ether — the general-purpose route, able to make unsymmetrical ethers.

Dehydration of two alcohol molecules (acid conditions, as presented) gives symmetrical ethers.

The two routes together cover the preparations the section presents.

Now you. Say it back: Name the general ether synthesis.
Answer: The Williamson synthesis.
