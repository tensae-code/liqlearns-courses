# Making ethers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name the general ether synthesis.
a) CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone  b) Plato and Aristotle  c) The Williamson synthesis  d) Lungs and health
**Answer: c) The Williamson synthesis**

**2. ** What reacts in the Williamson route?
a) No stable molecule forms  b) A sodium alkoxide with a haloalkane  c) Whether the Lewis structure is sound  d) Delocalised electrons
**Answer: b) A sodium alkoxide with a haloalkane**

**3. ** Dehydrating two alcohols gives…
a) A (symmetrical) ether  b) They do not react with most solutes  c) –COOH  d) Alcohol → aldehyde → acid
**Answer: a) A (symmetrical) ether**

**4. ** Which route makes unsymmetrical ethers?
a) A burette  b) Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature  c) The same quantity of electricity  d) Williamson
**Answer: d) Williamson**
