# Making ethers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name the general ether synthesis.
**Answer: The Williamson synthesis**

**2. ** What reacts in the Williamson route?
**Answer: A sodium alkoxide with a haloalkane**

**3. ** Dehydrating two alcohols gives…
**Answer: A (symmetrical) ether**

**4. ** Which route makes unsymmetrical ethers?
**Answer: Williamson**
