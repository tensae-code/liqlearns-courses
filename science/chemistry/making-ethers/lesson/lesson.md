# Making ethers

## Do it first
Two routes build the oxygen bridge. Before reading, guess one: what dehydration from the alcohol lesson might join two alcohol molecules into one ether?

## Say it to your companion
Explain Making ethers to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.

## Do It Yourself
1. Write the lesson's key ideas in your own words, in a list, without opening the lesson.
2. Work one example of your own for each key idea — your own numbers, your own substances, the lesson's own rules.
3. Check your examples against the lesson. Fix anything that disagrees, and say what rule it broke.
4. Teach the hardest idea in the lesson to someone else, out loud, once.
