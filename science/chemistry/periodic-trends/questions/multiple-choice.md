# Periodic trends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Across a period, what happens to atomic radius?
a) It shrinks  b) A pH range of about two units  c) Takes electrons (is itself reduced)  d) Yes — it is one of the three factors
**Answer: a) It shrinks**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What is ionisation energy?
a) The monomer units in the chain  b) The atmosphere  c) Nutrient enrichment causing algal blooms  d) The energy needed to remove an electron
**Answer: d) The energy needed to remove an electron**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** Which element has the greatest electronegativity?
a) Removing electrons from atoms  b) Electron pairs repel and spread apart  c) Fluorine  d) To become more stable
**Answer: c) Fluorine**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) ** What two ideas explain the trends?
a) The carbonyl group, C=O  b) Effective nuclear charge and shielding  c) Hydrogen bonding between molecules  d) Alkoxyalkane
**Answer: b) Effective nuclear charge and shielding**
