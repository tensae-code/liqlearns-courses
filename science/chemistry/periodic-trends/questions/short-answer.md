# Periodic trends — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** Across a period, what happens to atomic radius?
**Answer: It shrinks**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** What is ionisation energy?
**Answer: The energy needed to remove an electron**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** Which element has the greatest electronegativity?
**Answer: Fluorine**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** What two ideas explain the trends?
**Answer: Effective nuclear charge and shielding**
