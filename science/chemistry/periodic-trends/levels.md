# Periodic trends — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Radius — across and down
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Atomic radius shrinks across a period (greater nuclear charge pulls the same shells tighter) and grows down a group (new shells are added) — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ionisation energy, affinity, electronegativity
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Ionisation energy — the energy to remove an electron — generally rises across a period and falls down a group — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Metallic character and the two explanations
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Electron affinity and electronegativity (an atom's pull on shared electrons, greatest at fluorine) generally rise across a period; metallic character does the opposite — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Comparing elements from their places
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Every trend is explained by the same two ideas: effective nuclear charge and shielding by inner electrons — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.
