## Iron and steel

Iron, second most abundant metal, is extracted in the blast furnace charged with iron ore, coke and limestone under a hot air blast; limestone removes silica impurities as slag; the direct product is impure pig (cast) iron.

Wrought iron is the purest form, obtained by removing most impurities from pig iron. Steel — iron alloyed with definite carbon and metals such as chromium, nickel, tungsten, vanadium, manganese — is made from pig iron by the Bessemer converter, the open-hearth furnace and the basic oxygen process, the converter's job being to remove impurity elements by oxidation.
