Today: **Iron and steel — the blast furnace**.

Iron, second most abundant metal, is extracted in the blast furnace charged with iron ore, coke and limestone under a hot air blast;

limestone removes silica impurities as slag;

the direct product is impure pig (cast) iron.

Wrought iron is the purest form, obtained by removing most impurities from pig iron.
