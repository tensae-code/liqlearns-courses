# Metals in nature — and extraction methods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Metals found free (native) in nature are always…
a) The most reactive  b) The least reactive — gold and platinum among them  c) The lightest  d) The most common
**Answer: b) The least reactive — gold and platinum among them**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An ore is…
a) Any rock  b) A rock carrying enough of a metal compound to be worth working  c) Pure metal  d) A furnace
**Answer: b) A rock carrying enough of a metal compound to be worth working**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Iron is freed from its ore mainly by…
a) Electrolysis  b) Reduction by carbon/carbon monoxide in the blast furnace  c) Heating alone  d) Dissolving in water
**Answer: b) Reduction by carbon/carbon monoxide in the blast furnace**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Sodium and aluminium need electrolysis because…
a) They are rare  b) Their compounds are too stable for any chemical reducing agent to break economically  c) They melt too low  d) They are gases
**Answer: b) Their compounds are too stable for any chemical reducing agent to break economically**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) What assigns each metal its extraction method?
a) Its colour  b) Its price  c) Its place in the reactivity series  d) Its discoverer
**Answer: c) Its place in the reactivity series**
