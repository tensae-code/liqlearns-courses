## Mendeleev's periodic table

Mendeleev's law: properties are a periodic function of atomic mass. His table's arrangement, group names as he left them (alkali metals, alkaline earths, pnicogens, chalcogens, halogens, noble gases) and period lengths are described; merits — properties became systematic and studyable, and gaps in the table guided the discovery of new elements;

demerits — some pairs sit in the wrong mass order (Ar/K, Te/I, Co/Ni), which atomic mass alone cannot fix.2).
