# Mendeleev's periodic table — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** His table's arrangement, group names as he left them (alkali metals, alkaline earths, pnicogens, _____, halogens, noble gases) and period lengths are described;
**Answer: chalcogens**

**2. ** merits — properties became systematic and _____, and gaps in the table guided the discovery of new elements;
**Answer: studyable**

**3. ** _____ — some pairs sit in the wrong mass order (Ar/K, Te/I, Co/Ni), which atomic mass alone cannot fix.
**Answer: demerits**

**4. ** Mendeleev's law: properties are a _____ function of atomic mass.
**Answer: periodic**
