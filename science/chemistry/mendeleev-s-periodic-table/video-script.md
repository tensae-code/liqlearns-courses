# Video script — Mendeleev's periodic table

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Mendeleev's periodic table**.

Mendeleev's law: properties are a periodic function of atomic mass.

His table's arrangement, group names as he left them (alkali metals, alkaline earths, pnicogens, chalcogens, halogens, noble gases) and period lengths are described;

merits — properties became systematic and studyable, and gaps in the table guided the discovery of new elements;

demerits — some pairs sit in the wrong mass order (Ar/K, Te/I, Co/Ni), which atomic mass alone cannot fix.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
