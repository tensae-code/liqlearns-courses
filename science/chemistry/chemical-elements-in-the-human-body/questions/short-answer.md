# Chemical elements in the human body — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Kazakhstan's Grade 7 book (Grade 7, NIS (Kazakh)) teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Elements of life: body composition by mass (O 65%, C 18.5%, H 9.5%, N 3.2%), and macro-, micro- and ultra-microelements (Ca, F, I, Na, K, Fe) with their jobs and deficiency signs. (Kazakhstan, Grade 7 — Grade 7, NIS (Kazakh), Ch. 5.1, printed p. 44).**

**2.** How does Kazakhstan's Grade 7 book (Grade 7, Ospanova (Uzbek)) teach this lesson's thing?
**Answer: Elements in the human body: a distribution figure (O, C, H, N, Ca, P…), macroelements (>0.005%) versus microelements, and a table of Ca, P, K, Cl, S, Na giving food sources, jobs in the body and deficiency signs; proteins, fats and carbohydrates as C/H/O(/N) compounds with calorie shares. (Kazakhstan, Grade 7 — Grade 7, Ospanova (Uzbek), Ch. VIII, §18, printed pp. 100–104).**
