# Lesson plan — Chemical elements in the human body

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Chemical elements in the human body
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The chemical elements a person is made of: the macroelements that make up almost all body mass (oxygen 65%, carbon 18.5%, hydrogen 9.5%, nitrogen about 3%, then calcium, phosphorus, potassium and the rest) and the microelements needed in traces — what each group does in the body, how elements are detected in food and in the organism, and what a balanced diet must supply.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
