# Alkyl halides — naming, properties and uses — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 3) Give the family's physical properties as Kuwait lists them.
**Answer:** Alkyl halides are nearly insoluble in water; their boiling points stand above the alkanes' and rise with the halogen's mass and with chain length; the bromine and iodine compounds are denser than water.

**2.** (Sub lesson 4) Name the three families an alkyl halide can be prepared into by substitution, and the reagent for each.
**Answer:** Alcohols (with aqueous bases), ethers (with alkoxides) and amines (with sodium amide) — in each, another group takes the halogen's place.
