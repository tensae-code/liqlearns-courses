# Alkyl halides — naming, properties and uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Vinyl chloride is used to make…
a) PVC pipes  b) Glass  c) Soap  d) Fertiliser
**Answer: a) PVC pipes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) An aryl (phenyl) halide carries its halogen…
a) On a carbon chain's end  b) On a benzene ring  c) On an oxygen atom  d) Nowhere — it has no halogen
**Answer: b) On a benzene ring**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In IUPAC naming, identical halogens are counted with…
a) Roman numerals  b) Di-, tri-, tetra- prefixes  c) Greek letters only  d) Their atomic masses
**Answer: b) Di-, tri-, tetra- prefixes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A halide whose halogen-bearing carbon is attached to two other carbons is…
a) Primary  b) Secondary  c) Tertiary  d) Aryl
**Answer: b) Secondary**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Alkanes are halogenated directly…
a) In the dark when cold  b) Under UV light  c) Under water  d) With a base
**Answer: b) Under UV light**

**6.** (Sub lesson 4) An alkyl halide with an alkoxide gives…
a) An alcohol  b) An ether  c) An amine  d) An ester only
**Answer: b) An ether**
