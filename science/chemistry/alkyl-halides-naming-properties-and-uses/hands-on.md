# Hands-on — Alkyl halides — naming, properties and uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort three halides
Draw three short alkyl halides of your own — the halogen on an end carbon, on a middle carbon attached to two carbons, and on a carbon attached to three. Label each primary, secondary or tertiary, saying what decided you.

## Match the preparation
Write the three substitution preparations from the lesson as a matching list — aqueous base, alkoxide, sodium amide on one side; alcohol, ether, amine on the other — then cover one side and recall it.
