## Ionic bonding

An ionic bond forms between a metal and a nonmetal with a large electronegativity difference: the metal transfers valence electrons to the nonmetal, both reach octets, and the resulting cation and anion attract electrostatically. Worked formations: sodium + chlorine → Na⁺ and Cl⁻ (NaCl); calcium giving one electron to each of two chlorines (CaCl₂).

A cation/anion table ties charges to groups (Group IVA–VIA generally do not form simple ionic bonds).2.1).
