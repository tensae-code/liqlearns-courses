# Hands-on — Grignard reagents — and nucleophilic addition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the delivery (paper only)
Sketch ethanal's C=O with δ+ and δ− marked. Draw the ethyl group of CH₃CH₂MgCl arriving at the carbon, the π electrons moving onto oxygen, and the –OMgCl product. Label nucleophile and the new bond.

## Grow a skeleton (paper only)
A synthesis needs a three-carbon alcohol from methanal. Choose the Grignard reagent whose R group supplies the missing carbons, write the addition in the lesson's form, and state the carbon count before and after.
