# Grignard reagents — and nucleophilic addition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Describe the nucleophilic addition step by step.
**Answer: The Grignard's carbon group, carrying the bond's electrons, attacks the δ+ carbonyl carbon; the C=O π electrons move onto the δ− oxygen; the product is an alkoxide ending in –OMgX, with the new C–C bond formed.**

**2.** Why is the Grignard carbon nucleophilic?
**Answer: Its bond to magnesium places the shared electrons toward the carbon, so the R group behaves as an electron-pair giver — a carbon nucleophile that can be delivered onto an electron-poor carbon.**

**3.** What has the worked reaction achieved for synthesis?
**Answer: Growth: ethanal's two-carbon skeleton gains the delivered ethyl group — a new C–C bond — giving, after work-up, a four-carbon alcohol; carbon chains are built to order.**
