# Grignard reagents — and nucleophilic addition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A Grignard reagent is written…
a) RMgX  b) R–OH  c) R–X alone  d) MgCO₃
**Answer: a) RMgX**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The book's worked reagent is…
a) Methyl magnesium bromide  b) Ethyl magnesium chloride  c) Sodium ethoxide  d) Ethyl ethanoate
**Answer: b) Ethyl magnesium chloride**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the carbonyl, the oxygen carries…
a) δ+  b) δ−  c) A full positive charge  d) No charge separation
**Answer: b) δ−**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The first product's oxygen ends as…
a) –OH at once  b) –OMgX  c) =O unchanged  d) O²⁻ free
**Answer: b) –OMgX**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The nucleophile in the reaction is…
a) The carbonyl carbon  b) The Grignard's carbon group  c) The halogen  d) Water
**Answer: b) The Grignard's carbon group**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The worked case reacts the reagent with…
a) Ethane  b) Ethanol  c) Ethanal  d) Ethanoic acid
**Answer: c) Ethanal**
