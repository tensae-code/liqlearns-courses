# Lesson plan — Gypsum and plaster — setting with water

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Gypsum and plaster — setting with water
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Calcium sulfate as gypsum, CaSO4·2H2O, met through Yemen's Grade 10 book's own bench moment: powder the plaster of Yemen's traditional qamariya window-screen craftsmen, add water, and it sets quickly into a hard mass giving out heat — the paste that fills ceiling voids and casts statues, setting before it is even placed if you dawdle
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Calcium compounds — lime and limestone is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
