# Gypsum and plaster — setting with water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gypsum's formula is…
a) CaSO₄·2H₂O  b) CaSO₄  c) CaCO₃  d) Ca(OH)₂
**Answer: a) CaSO₄·2H₂O**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) When plaster powder meets water, it…
a) Dissolves and stays liquid  b) Sets quickly into a hard mass, giving out heat  c) Fizzes CO₂  d) Melts
**Answer: b) Sets quickly into a hard mass, giving out heat**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The water in gypsum's formula is…
a) Only on the surface  b) Absent  c) Built into the crystal  d) What makes it dissolve
**Answer: c) Built into the crystal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The craftsmen of the lesson make…
a) Glass bottles  b) Cement blocks  c) Metal tools  d) Qamariya — carved window screens
**Answer: d) Qamariya — carved window screens**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Set plaster is used in the lesson for all EXCEPT…
a) Making sodium  b) Filling ceiling voids  c) Casting statues  d) Window-screen craft
**Answer: a) Making sodium**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The danger of plaster's speed is that it can set…
a) Only after a week  b) Before it is even placed  c) In the bag without water  d) Never, without a kiln
**Answer: b) Before it is even placed**
