# Gypsum and plaster — setting with water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, from the formula, why plaster hardens when water is added.
**Answer: Gypsum is CaSO₄·2H₂O — the crystal includes water. Plaster is the same salt with that water driven off; adding water lets the crystal rebuild itself with its water restored, and the rebuilt crystal is the hard mass — the setting also gives out heat.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Describe the book's bench test exactly as it is set.
**Answer: Take a little of the plaster used by Yemen's traditional qamariya craftsmen, powder it, add water — and it sets quickly into a hard mass, giving out heat.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give the working rule the plaster's speed imposes, with its reason.
**Answer: Everything must be ready before the water is added, because the setting is fast: dawdle, and the paste sets before it is placed.**
