# Gypsum and plaster — setting with water — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gypsum's formula is CaSO₄·_____.
**Answer: 2H₂O**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Adding water to plaster makes it set into a hard mass, giving out _____.
**Answer: heat**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Yemen's traditional _____ makers work in this plaster.
**Answer: qamariya (window-screen)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Set plaster fills house-ceiling _____ and casts statues.
**Answer: voids**
