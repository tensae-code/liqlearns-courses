# Hands-on — Gypsum and plaster — setting with water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ready before water (paper only)
A plasterer must fill a ceiling void that will take ten minutes to prepare, with a paste that sets in five. Write the work plan the lesson's warning demands: what is prepared first, when the water goes in, and what is never done after mixing. The plan is the hands-on — plaster rewards the prepared.

## Water in the crystal (paper only)
Gypsum, CaSO₄·2H₂O, is one of this course's water-of-crystallisation cases. Write one paragraph connecting this lesson to the water-of-crystallisation lesson: what the '·2H₂O' means, what heating takes away, and what setting gives back.
