# Climate agreements and environmental law

## Do it first
In 1992 the world agreed the climate was its problem; turning agreement into duty took three more conferences and a place in national law. Before reading, say out loud: what can a treaty do that a speech cannot? Bulgaria's Grade 10 book walks the agreement line, then brings it home to Article 55.

## Sub lesson 1 — The agreement line

**Rio 1992** — the UN Framework Convention: the developed industrial countries carry the **greatest responsibility**. **Kyoto 1997** — the first **binding targets** and the **carbon-credit quota system (one credit = one tonne of CO₂)**, in force from **2005**. **Paris 2015** — hold the global rise **under 2 °C**, every country filing **its own action plan**. **Katowice 2018** — under the motto **'From black to green'**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The law at home — and the closing project

At home, the **Bulgarian constitution's Article 55** gives citizens the **right to a healthy environment and the duty to protect it**; the **Environmental Protection Act** sets the framework; national data show industry using only **26.7% of produced energy but 83.62% of consumed water**, with greenhouse emissions **down about 41% over 1990–2015**. The closing project lesson turns law into conduct: **waste archaeology** (accumulations 200,000 years old; Athens' first municipal dump in 400 BC; Rome's collection service in 200 BC), four household situations audited, an **ecological code** to complete and actually apply (know the laws, buy environment-safe goods, collect waste separately, hand hazardous waste in apart), a traffic-light poster for younger pupils, research into Bulgaria's geothermal waters, and a structured debate — 'The environment in which I live and I'.

## The key ideas

- Rio 1992: framework, developed countries' greatest responsibility. Kyoto 1997: first binding targets, carbon credits (1 credit = 1 t CO₂), in force 2005.
- Paris 2015: hold the rise under 2 °C, national action plans. Katowice 2018: 'From black to green'.
- Home law: Constitution Article 55 — the right to a healthy environment and the duty to protect it; the Environmental Protection Act is the framework.

## Say it to your companion
