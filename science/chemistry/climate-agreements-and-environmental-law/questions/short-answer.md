# Climate agreements and environmental law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Walk the agreement line with each stop's addition.
**Answer: Rio 1992 — framework convention, developed countries most responsible. Kyoto 1997 — first binding targets and carbon credits, in force 2005. Paris 2015 — under 2 °C, national plans. Katowice 2018 — 'From black to green'.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which act is Bulgaria's environmental framework law?
**Answer: The Environmental Protection Act, standing under the constitution's Article 55.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two items of the closing project's ecological code.
**Answer: Any two as printed: know the laws; buy environment-safe goods; collect waste separately; hand hazardous waste (batteries, mercury lamps, thermometers) in apart.**
