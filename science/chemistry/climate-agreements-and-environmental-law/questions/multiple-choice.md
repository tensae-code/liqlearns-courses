# Climate agreements and environmental law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Rio 1992 produced…
a) Binding targets  b) The UN Framework Convention  c) Carbon credits  d) The 2 °C limit
**Answer: b) The UN Framework Convention**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) One carbon credit equals…
a) One tonne of CO₂  b) One factory  c) One degree  d) One year
**Answer: a) One tonne of CO₂**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Kyoto's targets entered into force in…
a) 1997  b) 2005  c) 2015  d) 1992
**Answer: b) 2005**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Paris 2015 set the global rise to be held under…
a) 1 °C  b) 2 °C  c) 3 °C  d) 5 °C
**Answer: b) 2 °C**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Article 55 of Bulgaria's constitution gives citizens a right and a…
a) Tax  b) Duty to protect the environment  c) Subsidy  d) Vote
**Answer: b) Duty to protect the environment**
