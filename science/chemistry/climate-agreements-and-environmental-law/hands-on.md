# Hands-on — Climate agreements and environmental law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Agreement timeline
Draw the timeline 1992 → 1997 → 2005 → 2015 → 2018 with each conference's one-line addition pinned under its year. The line must show the ratchet: framework, binding targets, force, universal plans, implementation motto.

## Code completion
Complete Bulgaria's ecological code for your own household in the lesson's four terms (know the laws; environment-safe goods; separate collection; hazardous waste apart), then audit one week against it and record the traffic-light verdict the project asks for: green, amber or red, with the evidence.
