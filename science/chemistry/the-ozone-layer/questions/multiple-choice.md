# The ozone layer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ozone is…
a) Oxygen with two atoms in the molecule  b) Oxygen with three atoms in the molecule, O₃  c) A compound of oxygen and chlorine  d) Carbon dioxide under another name
**Answer: b) Oxygen with three atoms in the molecule, O₃**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The ozone layer sits in…
a) The air we breathe at ground level  b) The stratosphere, high above the ground  c) The oceans  d) The soil
**Answer: b) The stratosphere, high above the ground**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The ozone layer protects the surface chiefly by absorbing…
a) Visible light  b) Heat from fires  c) Ultraviolet radiation, above all UV-B  d) X-rays from hospitals
**Answer: c) Ultraviolet radiation, above all UV-B**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The gases chiefly blamed for thinning the layer are…
a) CFCs, which carry chlorine to the stratosphere  b) Oxygen and nitrogen  c) Water vapour  d) Smoke from cooking fires
**Answer: a) CFCs, which carry chlorine to the stratosphere**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) One chlorine atom can destroy many ozone molecules because…
a) It grows bigger each time  b) It is set free again after each destruction — it works catalytically  c) It turns into thousands of atoms  d) Ozone cannot resist it at all
**Answer: b) It is set free again after each destruction — it works catalytically**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The world agreement to phase out the thinning gases is…
a) The Paris climate meeting  b) The Montreal Protocol  c) The Geneva Convention  d) The Kyoto Protocol
**Answer: b) The Montreal Protocol**
