# The ozone layer — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ozone's formula is _____.
**Answer: O₃**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The harmful part of ultraviolet radiation the layer chiefly absorbs is called _____.
**Answer: UV-B**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The deep spring thinning of the layer over Antarctica is called the ozone _____.
**Answer: hole**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) UV-B damage that clouds the eye's lens is called _____.
**Answer: cataracts**
