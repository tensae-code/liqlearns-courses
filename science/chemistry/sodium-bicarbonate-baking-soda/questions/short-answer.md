# Sodium bicarbonate — baking soda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give both preparations of sodium bicarbonate named in the lesson.
**Answer: Laboratory: pass CO₂ into cold caustic soda solution. Industrial: it is the intermediate precipitated in the Solvay (ammonia-soda) process on the way to sodium carbonate.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the decomposition equation and the temperature condition.
**Answer: Above 100 °C: 2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + CO₂ + H₂O.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the lesson's four uses and the single property behind three of them.
**Answer: Baking powder, fire extinguishers, leather tanning, medicine against stomach acidity. Behind the first, second and fourth stands the same property: the powder releases CO₂ — to lift dough, to smother flame, and as it neutralises acid.**
