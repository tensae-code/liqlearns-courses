# Sodium bicarbonate — baking soda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the laboratory, sodium bicarbonate is made by passing CO₂ into…
a) Cold caustic soda solution  b) Hot limewater  c) Cold brine  d) Dilute sulfuric acid
**Answer: a) Cold caustic soda solution**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Industrially, sodium bicarbonate is known as the intermediate of the…
a) Downs cell  b) Solvay process  c) Haber process  d) Contact process
**Answer: b) Solvay process**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Heated above 100 °C, 2NaHCO₃ gives…
a) 2NaOH + 2CO₂  b) NaCl + CO₂ + H₂O  c) Na₂CO₃ + CO₂ + H₂O  d) Na₂O + 2CO₂ + H₂
**Answer: c) Na₂CO₃ + CO₂ + H₂O**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) With an acid, sodium bicarbonate…
a) Turns blue  b) Releases hydrogen  c) Does nothing  d) Fizzes, releasing CO₂
**Answer: d) Fizzes, releasing CO₂**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Baking powder raises dough because the bicarbonate releases…
a) Carbon dioxide bubbles  b) Steam only  c) Oxygen  d) Hydrogen
**Answer: a) Carbon dioxide bubbles**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Which use does NOT belong to sodium bicarbonate in the lesson?
a) Fire extinguishers  b) Making glass  c) Leather tanning  d) Medicine against stomach acidity
**Answer: b) Making glass**
