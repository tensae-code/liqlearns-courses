# Sodium bicarbonate — baking soda — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sodium bicarbonate is prepared in the laboratory by passing CO₂ into cold _____ soda solution.
**Answer: caustic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Industrially the bicarbonate is the _____ intermediate.
**Answer: Solvay**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Above _____ °C the bicarbonate breaks down to the carbonate, CO₂ and water.
**Answer: 100**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In baking powder, released _____ raises the dough.
**Answer: carbon dioxide**
