# Hands-on — Sodium bicarbonate — baking soda

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The fizz test (kitchen materials only, with a teacher)
Put half a spoon of baking soda in a glass, add a spoon of vinegar, and watch: the fizz is the lesson's acid reaction. Write the gas's name, and one sentence each on how the same fizz raises dough and settles stomach acid. Repeat with plain water instead of vinegar and record the difference.

## Label the powder (paper only)
Design the label for a box of baking soda from the lesson alone: its two preparations, its decomposition temperature, and its four uses — each use with the one-line reason the lesson gives. Anything you cannot source from the lesson stays off the label.
