# Allotropy — forms of an element — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Allotropy is…
a) One element with atoms of different masses  b) One element existing in more than one form  c) Two elements joined in a compound  d) A substance dissolving in water
**Answer: b) One element existing in more than one form**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's source is…
a) The Grade 7 student textbook  b) The Grade 7 teacher guide, Theme 2.8 extension (teacher evidence)  c) The Grade 8 student textbook  d) The Grade 9 teacher guide
**Answer: b) The Grade 7 teacher guide, Theme 2.8 extension (teacher evidence)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The guide deepens allotropy by sorting substances into…
a) Acids and bases  b) Molecular and non-molecular structure  c) Metals and nonmetals  d) Soluble and insoluble
**Answer: b) Molecular and non-molecular structure**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Ozone is named in the guide as…
a) A compound of oxygen and hydrogen  b) An example among the forms of the one element oxygen  c) A non-molecular metal  d) An isotope of oxygen
**Answer: b) An example among the forms of the one element oxygen**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Two allotropes of one element differ in…
a) The element they are made of  b) The arrangement of the same atoms — and so their properties  c) Nothing at all  d) Their atomic number
**Answer: b) The arrangement of the same atoms — and so their properties**
