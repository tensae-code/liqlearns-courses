# Hands-on — Allotropy — forms of an element

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Same atoms, two drawings
Draw one element's atoms twice: once grouped into separate small molecules, once joined in one continuing structure. Label the drawings molecular and non-molecular, and say which sorting the guide's extension uses.

## Say the ozone point
Say in one sentence how ozone proves allotropy: it and the oxygen of the air are built from the one element, yet they are different substances.
