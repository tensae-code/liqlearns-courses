# Hazards of polymers and plastics — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The unreacted small molecules left trapped in a finished plastic are called residual _____.
**Answer: monomer**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A _____ is mixed into a polymer to make it soft, but is not bonded to the chains.
**Answer: plasticizer**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Discarded plastic harms animals by ingestion and by _____.
**Answer: entanglement**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Plastic waste should never be _____ — it should be collected, buried or recycled.
**Answer: burned**
