# Hazards of polymers and plastics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bhutan's §6.8 says the main hazard of a polymer is…
a) The long polymer chain itself  b) The small molecules that travel with it — residual monomer, released molecules and additives  c) Its colour  d) Its weight
**Answer: b) The small molecules that travel with it — residual monomer, released molecules and additives**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Residual monomer is dangerous where its polymer is not, because the monomer is…
a) Larger  b) Small, mobile and often toxic — able to migrate out of the material  c) Coloured  d) A gas at all temperatures
**Answer: b) Small, mobile and often toxic — able to migrate out of the material**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A plasticizer makes a polymer soft, but it is a hazard because it is…
a) Bonded too strongly  b) Mixed in but not chemically bonded, so it slowly leaches out  c) Made of metal  d) Radioactive
**Answer: b) Mixed in but not chemically bonded, so it slowly leaches out**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) As a plasticized plastic ages and loses plasticizer, the plastic itself becomes…
a) Softer  b) Stiffer  c) Heavier  d) Transparent
**Answer: b) Stiffer**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The two ways discarded plastic harms animals, as the chapter names them, are…
a) Noise and light  b) Eating it as false food and entanglement  c) Cold and heat  d) Smell and taste
**Answer: b) Eating it as false food and entanglement**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The chapter's answer to plastic waste is…
a) Burn it quickly  b) Never burn it — collect, bury or recycle it, and keep it out of fields and water  c) Throw it in rivers  d) Bury it in grazing ground
**Answer: b) Never burn it — collect, bury or recycle it, and keep it out of fields and water**
