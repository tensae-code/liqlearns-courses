Today: **Hazards of polymers and plastics** — Bhutan's way: the polymer chains themselves are large and unreactive.

The hazard is not the chain — it is the small molecules travelling with it: the monomer that never reacted, the plasticizer that was never bonded, the fumes waiting for a fire.. That is the whole lesson in one breath — the sub lessons now walk it slowly, book in hand.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
