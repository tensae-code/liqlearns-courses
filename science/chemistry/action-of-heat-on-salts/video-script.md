Today: **Action of heat on salts** — South Sudan's way: heat it, and the pattern names the salt.
Heat splits many salts apart. Carbonates mostly give the metal oxide and carbon dioxide — zinc carbonate leaves zinc oxide, yellow while hot, white when cold. Nitrates split by reactivity: the most reactive metals' nitrates give a nitrite and oxygen; the middle metals give the oxide, brown nitrogen dioxide and oxygen; silver nitrate goes all the way to the metal. You read the result by its gases — limewater for carbon dioxide, a glowing splint for oxygen, brown fumes you can see — and by the residue left behind.
