# Hands-on — Action of heat on salts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pattern sorting on cards
Write six salts on cards — zinc carbonate, calcium carbonate, sodium carbonate, lead nitrate, potassium nitrate, silver nitrate. Sort them by what strong heating does: decomposes to oxide + CO₂; resists; gives nitrite + O₂; gives oxide + NO₂ + O₂; gives the metal. Check your sorting against sub lesson 2.

## Read the tube
For each of three imagined heatings, write the full reading: (a) a white salt, gas turns limewater milky, residue yellow hot and white cold; (b) a salt crackling with brown fumes; (c) a salt unchanged by the Bunsen flame. Name the family each reading points to, using sub lesson 3.
