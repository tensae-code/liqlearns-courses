# Action of heat on salts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Heating a salt strongly so that it splits into simpler substances is…
a) Melting  b) Thermal decomposition  c) Dissolving  d) Neutralisation
**Answer: b) Thermal decomposition**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Most metal carbonates decompose on strong heating to give…
a) The metal and oxygen  b) The metal oxide and carbon dioxide  c) The metal hydroxide and carbon  d) Nothing — carbonates never change
**Answer: b) The metal oxide and carbon dioxide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Zinc oxide is… while hot and… when cold.
a) White; yellow  b) Yellow; white  c) Brown; black  d) Blue; green
**Answer: b) Yellow; white**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The nitrates of the middle metals (like lead nitrate) give off…
a) Only steam  b) Brown nitrogen dioxide and oxygen, leaving the metal oxide  c) Hydrogen and nitrogen  d) Carbon dioxide only
**Answer: b) Brown nitrogen dioxide and oxygen, leaving the metal oxide**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Carbon dioxide is confirmed by…
a) A glowing splint relighting  b) Limewater turning milky  c) A squeaky pop  d) Blue litmus turning red
**Answer: b) Limewater turning milky**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Brown fumes on heating a salt point to…
a) Carbon dioxide  b) Oxygen  c) Nitrogen dioxide from a nitrate  d) Water vapour
**Answer: c) Nitrogen dioxide from a nitrate**
