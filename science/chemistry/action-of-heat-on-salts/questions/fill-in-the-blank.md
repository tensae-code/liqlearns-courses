# Action of heat on salts — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The splitting of a compound by heat alone is called thermal _____.
**Answer: decomposition**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Most metal carbonates decompose to the metal _____ and carbon dioxide.
**Answer: oxide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Lead nitrate crackles as it decomposes — the crackling is called _____.
**Answer: decrepitation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Oxygen is confirmed when it relights a _____ splint.
**Answer: glowing**
