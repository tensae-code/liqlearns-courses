# Reactions of carboxylic acids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Acid + carbonate gives what gas?
a) Soaps  b) Only slightly soluble  c) Three  d) Carbon dioxide, CO₂
**Answer: d) Carbon dioxide, CO₂**

**2. ** Acid + metal gives what gas?
a) Electrochemical  b) Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets  c) Hydrogen  d) Anode to cathode
**Answer: c) Hydrogen**

**3. ** Acid + alcohol gives…
a) Beryllium  b) An ester and water  c) Hazardous substances  d) Subatomic particles
**Answer: b) An ester and water**

**4. ** What salts do acids form with bases?
a) Salts (by neutralisation)  b) It dents instead of shattering  c) Particles in motion  d) Kelvin temperature
**Answer: a) Salts (by neutralisation)**
