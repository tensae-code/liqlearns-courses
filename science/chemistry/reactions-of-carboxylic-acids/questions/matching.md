# Reactions of carboxylic acids — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. Acid + carbonate gives what gas? — d
2. Acid + metal gives what gas? — a
3. Acid + alcohol gives… — b
4. What salts do acids form with bases? — c

**Answer key:** 1–d: Carbon dioxide, CO₂ · 2–a: Hydrogen · 3–b: An ester and water · 4–c: Salts (by neutralisation)
