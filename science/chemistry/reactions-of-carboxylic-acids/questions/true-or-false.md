# Reactions of carboxylic acids — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Acid + carbonate gives what gas — Carbon dioxide, CO₂
**Answer: True**

**2. ** Acid + metal gives what gas — In fixed whole-number ratios
**Answer: False**

**3. ** Acid + alcohol gives… — An ester and water
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What salts do acids form with bases — Forward, making products
**Answer: False**
