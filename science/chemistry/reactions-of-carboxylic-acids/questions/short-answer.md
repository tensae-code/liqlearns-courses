# Reactions of carboxylic acids — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Acid + carbonate gives what gas?
**Answer: Carbon dioxide, CO₂**

**2. ** Acid + metal gives what gas?
**Answer: Hydrogen**

**3. ** Acid + alcohol gives…
**Answer: An ester and water**

**4. ** What salts do acids form with bases?
**Answer: Salts (by neutralisation)**
