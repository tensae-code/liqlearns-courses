# Video script — Reactions of carboxylic acids

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Drop acid on a carbonate and it fizzes.

Carboxylic acids are weak acids: they form salts with bases and alkalis (neutralisation).

With metals they give a salt and hydrogen; with carbonates, a salt, water and carbon dioxide — the fizzing test.

With alcohols they give esters and water (esterification); reduction takes them back toward alcohols, as presented.

Now you. Say it back: Acid + carbonate gives what gas?
Answer: Carbon dioxide, CO₂.
