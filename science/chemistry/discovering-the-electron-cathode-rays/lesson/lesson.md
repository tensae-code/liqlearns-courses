## Discovering the electron

In the Crookes/cathode-ray tube, Thomson's experiment series is walked through: cathode rays travel straight (shadow of an object), have particle nature (they turn a paddle wheel), and are negatively charged (bent by electric and magnetic fields); their listed properties include affecting a ZnS screen, ionising gases, penetrating matter and producing X-rays on metals. Thomson measured the charge-to-mass ratio and proposed the plum-pudding model (1904) — electrons embedded in a positive mass;

Millikan's oil-drop experiment (1909) fixed the electron's charge and mass.4.2).
