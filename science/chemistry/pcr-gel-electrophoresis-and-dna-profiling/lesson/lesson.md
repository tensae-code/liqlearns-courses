# PCR, gel electrophoresis and DNA profiling

## Do it first
Before reading, say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is: pcr, gel electrophoresis and dna profiling. Then read on — this is Italy's way of teaching it, as the Italy placements describe the book's own treatment.

## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo F, Capitolo 15, §§6–9 — Italy's book (Grade 13)

The analysis toolkit: PCR's three temperature steps and exponential yield computed; gel electrophoresis sizing fragments against a marker; DNA profiling — fingerprints, genetic markers, repetitive DNA and STRs — and its applications (forensics, archaeology, environment, food, diagnostics).

## The key ideas

- PCR's three temperature steps produce an exponential yield, and the guide computes it.
- Gel electrophoresis sizes DNA fragments against a marker.
- DNA profiling uses fingerprints, genetic markers, repetitive DNA and STRs — in forensics, archaeology, environment, food and diagnostics.
