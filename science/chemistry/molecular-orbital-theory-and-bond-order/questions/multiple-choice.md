# Molecular orbital theory and bond order — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** State the bond-order formula.
a) Water  b) ½(bonding − antibonding electrons)  c) Brine  d) Hydration with water
**Answer: b) ½(bonding − antibonding electrons)**

**2. ** What does zero bond order mean?
a) No stable molecule forms  b) The uncertainty principle  c) The orbital's orientation  d) On the right of the table
**Answer: a) No stable molecule forms**

**3. ** What does O₂'s MO diagram explain?
a) Irreversible  b) Partial pressures  c) –OH (hydroxyl)  d) Its paramagnetism (unpaired electrons)
**Answer: d) Its paramagnetism (unpaired electrons)**

**4. ** Which MO is lower in energy?
a) H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)  b) A weak acid's ionisation strength  c) The bonding MO  d) Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry
**Answer: c) The bonding MO**
