# Molecular orbital theory and bond order — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** State the bond-order formula.
**Answer: ½(bonding − antibonding electrons)**

**2. ** What does zero bond order mean?
**Answer: No stable molecule forms**

**3. ** What does O₂'s MO diagram explain?
**Answer: Its paramagnetism (unpaired electrons)**

**4. ** Which MO is lower in energy?
**Answer: The bonding MO**
