# Equilibrium at work in industry — Unit 5 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Why is industrial temperature a compromise?
a) Diamond  b) A manometer  c) Resistance to flow  d) Rate needs heat; yield needs cool
**Answer: d) Rate needs heat; yield needs cool**

**2. ** What does recycling recover?
a) Ethyl ethanoate  b) Flavours, perfumes or solvents  c) Unreacted gases  d) Its C=C double bond(s)
**Answer: c) Unreacted gases**

**3. ** What speeds the approach to equilibrium?
a) It regrows or refills  b) A catalyst  c) Purifies a metal at the cathode  d) A cell potential depending on [H₃O⁺]
**Answer: b) A catalyst**

**4. ** Which principle guides the choices?
a) Le Chatelier's principle  b) CO₂  c) Indivisible — cannot be cut  d) Alpha, beta and gamma
**Answer: a) Le Chatelier's principle**
