# Hands-on — The Born–Haber cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lay the loop
Write the six energies of the NaCl cycle on six cards (formation route on one, the five steps on the others), with + for energy taken in and − for energy released, as the book's sum uses them. Arrange them in a loop on the table.

## Walk it and sum it
Walk a finger around the loop, adding aloud: 109 + 121 + 494 − 347 − 787 + 410. You must land on zero. If you do not, find the sign you misread — the loop is the check.

## Hide one card
Turn the lattice-energy card face down and find its value from the other five, out loud. This is the cycle's real job — do it twice.
