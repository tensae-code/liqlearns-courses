# The Born–Haber cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Born–Haber cycle works because…
a) Energies around a closed loop must sum to zero  b) Sodium is a metal  c) Lattice energy is always the largest step  d) Chlorine is a gas
**Answer: a) Energies around a closed loop must sum to zero**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In the book's NaCl cycle, the ionisation energy of Na(g) is…
a) 109 kJ/mol  b) 121 kJ/mol  c) 494 kJ/mol  d) 787 kJ/mol
**Answer: c) 494 kJ/mol**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The step that releases the lattice energy is…
a) Sublimation of Na  b) The gaseous ions coming together as NaCl(s)  c) Dissociation of Cl₂  d) Ionisation of Na(g)
**Answer: b) The gaseous ions coming together as NaCl(s)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The book's cycle sum 109 + 121 + 494 − 347 − 787 + 410 equals…
a) 410  b) 787  c) 0  d) −410
**Answer: c) 0**
