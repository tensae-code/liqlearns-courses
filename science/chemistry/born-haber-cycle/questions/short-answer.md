# The Born–Haber cycle — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) List the five steps of the NaCl cycle with the book's energy for each.
**Answer:** Sublimation of Na 109 kJ/mol; half the Cl₂ dissociation 121 kJ/mol; ionisation of Na(g) 494 kJ/mol; electron affinity of Cl 347 kJ/mol released; lattice formation 787 kJ/mol released.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the Born–Haber cycle mainly used to find, and how?
**Answer:** The lattice energy (or another unmeasurable step): because the energies around the closed cycle sum to zero, the unknown step equals what the known steps leave unbalanced.
