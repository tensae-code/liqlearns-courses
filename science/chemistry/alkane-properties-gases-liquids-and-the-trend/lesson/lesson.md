## Alkane properties

Across the series, melting/boiling points and density rise with chain length (and branching lowers boiling point); the first members are gases at room temperature, middle members liquids (which alkanes are liquid is a review judgement: C₁₀H₂₂ yes, C₃H₈ and C₄H₁₀ no), and alkanes are insoluble in water — properties read off intermolecular forces growing with size.
