# Greenhouse gases — which and how much — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. Name the five greenhouse gases treated. — d
2. Which gas comes from swamps and cattle? — a
3. What is the largest human-driven contributor? — b
4. N₂O comes notably from… — c

**Answer key:** 1–d: CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone · 2–a: Methane, CH₄ · 3–b: CO₂ · 4–c: Fertilised fields (agriculture)
