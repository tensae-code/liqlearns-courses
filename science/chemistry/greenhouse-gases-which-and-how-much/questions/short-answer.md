# Greenhouse gases — which and how much — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name the five greenhouse gases treated.
**Answer: CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone**

**2. ** Which gas comes from swamps and cattle?
**Answer: Methane, CH₄**

**3. ** What is the largest human-driven contributor?
**Answer: CO₂**

**4. ** N₂O comes notably from…
**Answer: Fertilised fields (agriculture)**
