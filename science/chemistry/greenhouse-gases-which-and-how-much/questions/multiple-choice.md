# Greenhouse gases — which and how much — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name the five greenhouse gases treated.
a) It shrinks  b) A positive ion (cation)  c) d electrons can also be lost  d) CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone
**Answer: d) CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone**

**2. ** Which gas comes from swamps and cattle?
a) Yeast (its enzymes)  b) Weakly polar  c) Methane, CH₄  d) One with three –OH groups
**Answer: c) Methane, CH₄**

**3. ** What is the largest human-driven contributor?
a) Its range must fall in the titration's pH jump  b) CO₂  c) Suppresses it  d) OH⁻ — so the solution is alkaline
**Answer: b) CO₂**

**4. ** N₂O comes notably from…
a) Fertilised fields (agriculture)  b) Centrifuging (spinning)  c) Slows microbial growth  d) PET
**Answer: a) Fertilised fields (agriculture)**
