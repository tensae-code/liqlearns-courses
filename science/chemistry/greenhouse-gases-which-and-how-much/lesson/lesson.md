# Greenhouse gases — which and how much

## Do it first
Five gases share the blame unequally. Before reading, rank your suspects: the famous one, the one from swamps and cattle, the one from fertilised fields.

[Picture 21784 — The main greenhouse gases with sources.]

The main greenhouse gases treated are carbon dioxide (CO₂), water vapour (H₂O), methane (CH₄), nitrous oxide (N₂O) and ozone.

Each is discussed by its sources and its warming effect — the unit review fixes an order of importance among them.

CO₂ from burning fossil fuels and deforestation is the largest human-driven contributor as presented.

## Say it to your companion
Explain Greenhouse gases — which and how much to your companion in your own words, using the lesson's own examples. Then let your companion ask you the questions from the end of the lesson until you can answer each one without looking.
