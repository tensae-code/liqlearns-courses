# Video script — Greenhouse gases — which and how much

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five gases share the blame unequally.

The main greenhouse gases treated are carbon dioxide (CO₂), water vapour (H₂O), methane (CH₄), nitrous oxide (N₂O) and ozone.

Each is discussed by its sources and its warming effect — the unit review fixes an order of importance among them.

CO₂ from burning fossil fuels and deforestation is the largest human-driven contributor as presented.

Now you. Say it back: Name the five greenhouse gases treated.
Answer: CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone.
