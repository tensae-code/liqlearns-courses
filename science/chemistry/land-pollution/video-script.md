# Video script — Land pollution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soil is the slowest sphere to forgive.

Land pollution is the degradation of soil by solid wastes dumped on it.

Agrochemical residues — pesticides and excess fertilisers — accumulate in soil as presented.

Degraded soil loses fertility and can pass pollutants into water and food chains.

Now you. Say it back: What degrades soil in land pollution?
Answer: Solid wastes and agrochemicals.
