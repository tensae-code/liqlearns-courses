# Land pollution — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What degrades soil in land pollution?
**Answer: Solid wastes and agrochemicals**

**2. ** What accumulates from farming?
**Answer: Pesticide and fertiliser residues**

**3. ** What does degraded soil lose?
**Answer: Fertility**

**4. ** Where can soil pollutants pass?
**Answer: Into water and food chains**
