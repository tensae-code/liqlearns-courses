# Land pollution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What degrades soil in land pollution?
a) Alpha, beta and gamma  b) Solid wastes and agrochemicals  c) CO₂  d) Indivisible — cannot be cut
**Answer: b) Solid wastes and agrochemicals**

**2. ** What accumulates from farming?
a) Pesticide and fertiliser residues  b) Positive kernels in a lattice  c) The bonding MO  d) Pressure, volume, temperature, amount (n)
**Answer: a) Pesticide and fertiliser residues**

**3. ** What does degraded soil lose?
a) Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH  b) Hydrolysis (as presented)  c) The alcohol (alkyl part)  d) Fertility
**Answer: d) Fertility**

**4. ** Where can soil pollutants pass?
a) Chemical equivalents  b) ΔG° = −nFE°  c) Into water and food chains  d) The electrochemical (reactivity) series
**Answer: c) Into water and food chains**
