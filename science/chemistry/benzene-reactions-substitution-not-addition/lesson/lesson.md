## Benzene reactions

Physically a colourless liquid of characteristic smell, insoluble in water. Chemically it prefers substitution over addition (preserving the ring): halogenation, nitration and related ring substitutions replace one hydrogen at a time, while addition happens only under forcing conditions; it burns with a smoky flame (high carbon content).
