# Solar cells and photovoltaics — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Light in, electricity out — the photovoltaic idea, Inside the cell — the junction that separates charge, From one cell to a working technology), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, sorting energy technologies into burning / spinning / photovoltaic by how each makes electricity, and tracing one photon's work from sunlight to a current in a wire.
