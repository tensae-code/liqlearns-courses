The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Solar cells and photovoltaics** — the advanced technology Iran pictures in its materials chapter: electricity straight out of sunlight.

Sunlight falls on a thin silicon wafer. Inside, the light frees electrons. The join between the wafer's two layers pushes the freed electrons one way and the gaps they leave the other way — so one face of the cell fills with electrons and the other face waits for them. Join a wire, and they flow: a current, made by light alone.
No fire. No spinning. Just light in, electricity out — while the sun shines. For the night, the cell charges a battery.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
