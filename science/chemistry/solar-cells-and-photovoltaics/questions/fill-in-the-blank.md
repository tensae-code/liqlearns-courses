# Solar cells and photovoltaics — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solar cell turns sunlight directly into _____.
**Answer: electricity**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sunlight arrives as packets of energy called _____.
**Answer: photons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The join between the cell's two layers, which separates the charges, is the _____.
**Answer: junction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Cells are joined together into _____ sized to the job.
**Answer: panels**
