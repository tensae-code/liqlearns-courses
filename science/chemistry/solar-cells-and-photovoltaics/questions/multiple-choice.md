# Solar cells and photovoltaics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A solar cell produces electricity…
a) By burning sunlight  b) Directly from sunlight, inside the solid material itself  c) By spinning in the wind  d) From stored chemicals only
**Answer: b) Directly from sunlight, inside the solid material itself**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The conversion of light into electric voltage inside a material is called…
a) The photovoltaic effect  b) Electrolysis  c) Combustion  d) Distillation
**Answer: a) The photovoltaic effect**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Most solar cells are made of the semiconductor…
a) Copper  b) Silicon  c) Iron  d) Carbon as graphite only
**Answer: b) Silicon**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Inside the cell, the junction between the two layers…
a) Stores the sunlight as heat  b) Melts the wafer  c) Drives light-freed electrons one way and the gaps they leave the other way, separating the charges  d) Reflects all the light away
**Answer: c) Drives light-freed electrons one way and the gaps they leave the other way, separating the charges**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A working solar system for night-time light needs, besides the panel…
a) A fuel tank  b) Storage — a battery the panel charges while the sun shines  c) A steam turbine  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: b) Storage — a battery the panel charges while the sun shines**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Iran pictures and questions the solar cell inside its Grade 12 chapter on…
a) Food  b) Gases  c) Materials — art, beauty and durability  d) Acids
**Answer: c) Materials — art, beauty and durability**
