# Haloalkanes — nucleophilic substitution — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The reactive bond of a haloalkane is the polar C–_____ bond.
**Answer: X**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) S_N2 attack inverts the centre 'like an umbrella' — the word is _____.
**Answer: inversion**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) S_N1 passes through a flat intermediate called a _____.
**Answer: carbocation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Mirror-image forms of a chiral molecule are called _____.
**Answer: enantiomers**
