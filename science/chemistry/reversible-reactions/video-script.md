# Video script — Reversible reactions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Water evaporates from an open dish and is gone.

Some reactions go to completion — irreversible for practical purposes.

Reversible reactions can run in both directions: the products can react back to reactants, shown with the ⇌ arrow.

In a closed bottle, evaporation and condensation run together — the doorway example to equilibrium.

Now you. Say it back: What arrow marks a reversible reaction?
Answer: ⇌ (double arrow).
