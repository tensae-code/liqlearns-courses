# Reversible reactions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What arrow marks a reversible reaction?
a) Pests  b) ⇌ (double arrow)  c) Manufacturing processes, incl. Ethiopia's  d) Ammonia
**Answer: b) ⇌ (double arrow)**

**2. ** In a sealed bottle, what balances evaporation?
a) Condensation  b) Negative  c) The number of neutrons  d) A continuous spectrum
**Answer: a) Condensation**

**3. ** What do we call reactions that go to completion?
a) Particles are locked in place  b) At the surface, at any temperature  c) The slope of the tangent  d) Irreversible
**Answer: d) Irreversible**

**4. ** Can products of a reversible reaction react back?
a) An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)  b) Kinked tails cannot pack closely  c) Yes, to reactants  d) Flavourings and perfumes
**Answer: c) Yes, to reactants**
