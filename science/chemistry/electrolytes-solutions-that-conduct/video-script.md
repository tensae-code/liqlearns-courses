Today: **Electrolytes — solutions that conduct**.

An ionic solute dissolves into separate ions;

the solution conducts electricity and is an electrolyte — strong if dissociation is complete, weak if only partial.

A molecular solute dissolves as whole molecules: a nonelectrolyte whose solution does not conduct.
