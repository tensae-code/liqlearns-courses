# Electrolytes — solutions that conduct — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about electrolytes — solutions that conduct? Use the lesson's own example.
**Answer: A molecular solute dissolves as whole molecules: a nonelectrolyte whose solution does not conduct.**

**2. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about electrolytes — solutions that conduct? Use the lesson's own example.
**Answer: An ionic solute dissolves into separate ions;**

**3. ** In your own words: what does this lesson teach about electrolytes — solutions that conduct? Use the lesson's own example.
**Answer: the solution conducts electricity and is an electrolyte — strong if dissociation is complete, weak if only partial.**
