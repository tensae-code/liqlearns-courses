# Electrolytes — solutions that conduct — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** the solution conducts electricity and is an _____ — strong if dissociation is complete, weak if only partial.
**Answer: electrolyte**

**2. ** A molecular solute dissolves as whole molecules: a _____ whose solution does not conduct.
**Answer: nonelectrolyte**

**3. ** An ionic solute dissolves into _____ ions;
**Answer: separate**

**4. ** the solution conducts electricity and is an _____ — strong if dissociation is complete, weak if only partial.
**Answer: electrolyte**
