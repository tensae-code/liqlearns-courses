Today: **Cycloalkanes — alkanes in rings**.

Ring alkanes (alicyclic hydrocarbons) close the chain into a ring, losing two hydrogens to the general formula CₙH₂ₙ;

they are named with the prefix cyclo- and behave largely like alkanes.
