# Octet-rule exceptions — expanded and incomplete octets — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the two directions in which molecules break the octet rule.
**Answer: Incomplete octets — fewer than eight electrons around the central atom — and expanded octets — more than eight.**

**2.** How does the book prove an exception instead of asserting it?
**Answer: By Lewis structures: the bonding and non-bonding pairs around the central atom are drawn and counted, so the short or over-full count is verified electron by electron.**

**3.** Why does the rule survive its exceptions?
**Answer: It still describes the small, common molecules it was built from; the exceptions mark its limits — larger central atoms, and a few stable short counts — rather than overturning the pattern.**
