# Octet-rule exceptions — expanded and incomplete octets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The octet rule asks for how many outer electrons around an atom?
a) Two  b) Six  c) Eight  d) Eighteen
**Answer: c) Eight**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An incomplete octet has…
a) Exactly eight electrons  b) Fewer than eight around the central atom  c) More than eight  d) No bonds
**Answer: b) Fewer than eight around the central atom**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) An expanded octet has…
a) Fewer than eight  b) Eight exactly  c) More than eight around the central atom  d) Only lone pairs
**Answer: c) More than eight around the central atom**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Expanded octets are found where the central atom is…
a) Hydrogen  b) A larger atom below the second period  c) Always carbon  d) Always a metal
**Answer: b) A larger atom below the second period**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) The book treats the exceptions by…
a) Hiding them  b) Tabulating their Lewis structures beside the obeying molecules  c) Calling them errors  d) Skipping Lewis structures
**Answer: b) Tabulating their Lewis structures beside the obeying molecules**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) After this lesson, the octet rule stands as…
a) A law with no failures  b) The pattern of the small common molecules, with named limits  c) Useless  d) A rule for metals only
**Answer: b) The pattern of the small common molecules, with named limits**
