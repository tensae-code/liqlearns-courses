# Octet-rule exceptions — expanded and incomplete octets — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The octet rule asks for _____ outer electrons.
**Answer: eight**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Fewer than eight is an _____ octet.
**Answer: incomplete**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) More than eight is an _____ octet.
**Answer: expanded**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Lewis _____ let you count the electrons pair by pair.
**Answer: structures**
