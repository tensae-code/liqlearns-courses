# Hands-on — Octet-rule exceptions — expanded and incomplete octets

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the centre (paper only)
Draw (or copy) two Lewis structures — one obeying molecule and one from the exceptions table. Count the electrons around the central atom in each, pair by pair, and write the verdict under each: octet obeyed, incomplete, or expanded.

## Sort the set (paper only)
You are given six central-atom electron counts: 8, 6, 8, 10, 4, 12. Sort them into three labelled piles — obeys, incomplete, expanded — and write the one-sentence rule you used for each pile.
