# Properties of esters

## Do it first
Esters smell sweet but boil lower than their parent acids. Before reading, say which bond the acid parent had that the ester has lost.

Esters cannot hydrogen-bond to each other (no –OH or –COOH free), so their boiling points are lower than the parent acids' and alcohols'.

Many are volatile liquids with pleasant odours.

They dissolve in organic solvents and only slightly in water (small esters somewhat more).
