# Video script — Properties of esters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Esters smell sweet but boil lower than their parent acids.

Esters cannot hydrogen-bond to each other (no –OH or –COOH free), so their boiling points are lower than the parent acids' and alcohols'.

Many are volatile liquids with pleasant odours.

They dissolve in organic solvents and only slightly in water (small esters somewhat more).

Now you. Say it back: Why do esters boil lower than acids?
Answer: No hydrogen bonding between esters.
