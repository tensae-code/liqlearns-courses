# Properties of esters — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Why do esters boil lower than acids?
**Answer: No hydrogen bonding between esters**

**2. ** What are ester odours like?
**Answer: Pleasant (fruity)**

**3. ** Are esters volatile?
**Answer: Many are volatile liquids**

**4. ** How do esters take to water?
**Answer: Only slightly soluble**
