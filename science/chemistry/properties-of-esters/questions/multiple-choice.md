# Properties of esters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Why do esters boil lower than acids?
a) Three  b) No hydrogen bonding between esters  c) The rise in average temperature  d) Redesign, reuse or efficiency in the plant
**Answer: b) No hydrogen bonding between esters**

**2. ** What are ester odours like?
a) Pleasant (fruity)  b) Positive kernels  c) Its overlap concentrates density between nuclei  d) Gas
**Answer: a) Pleasant (fruity)**

**3. ** Are esters volatile?
a) A silver mirror  b) An ester and water  c) Dilute  d) Many are volatile liquids
**Answer: d) Many are volatile liquids**

**4. ** How do esters take to water?
a) The charge of one mole of electrons (≈96,500 C)  b) The anode  c) Only slightly soluble  d) Negative
**Answer: c) Only slightly soluble**
