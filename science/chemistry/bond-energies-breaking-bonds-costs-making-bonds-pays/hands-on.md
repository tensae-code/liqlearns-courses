# Hands-on — Bond energies — breaking bonds costs, making bonds pays

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Balance-sheet practice (paper only)
A reaction breaks bonds worth 1,240 kJ and makes bonds worth 1,515 kJ. Write the two columns, compute ΔH, and state exothermic or endothermic with the sign explained in one sentence.

## Trend reading (paper only)
Methane, ethane, propane burn in turn. Without numbers, rank their combustion energies and write the bond-account reason — which column grows, and why it grows faster than the other.
