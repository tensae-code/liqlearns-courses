# Bond energies — breaking bonds costs, making bonds pays — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Breaking bonds _____ energy; making bonds gives it out.
**Answer: costs**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Bond energy is quoted per _____ of bonds.
**Answer: mole**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) ΔH ≈ energies broken _____ energies made.
**Answer: minus**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Alkane combustion energy rises with molar _____.
**Answer: mass**
