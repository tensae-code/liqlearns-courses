# Bond energies — breaking bonds costs, making bonds pays — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Bond energy is the energy needed to break…
a) One bond in a solid  b) One mole of bonds between two gas-state atoms  c) One mole of any substance  d) One electron from an atom
**Answer: b) One mole of bonds between two gas-state atoms**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Forming a bond…
a) Costs the bond energy  b) Gives out the bond energy  c) Costs twice the bond energy  d) Does nothing energetically
**Answer: b) Gives out the bond energy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) ΔH from bond energies is…
a) Made minus broken  b) Broken minus made  c) Broken plus made  d) Made divided by broken
**Answer: b) Broken minus made**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) If breaking costs more than making pays back, the reaction is…
a) Exothermic  b) Endothermic  c) Neutral  d) Impossible
**Answer: b) Endothermic**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Along the alkanes, combustion energy…
a) Falls with molar mass  b) Stays equal  c) Rises with molar mass  d) Depends only on state
**Answer: c) Rises with molar mass**

**6.** (Sub lesson 1) A flame's heat is, in this lesson's account…
a) Created by the spark  b) The difference between the breaking and making columns  c) Stored in the match  d) Taken from the air's bonds only
**Answer: b) The difference between the breaking and making columns**
