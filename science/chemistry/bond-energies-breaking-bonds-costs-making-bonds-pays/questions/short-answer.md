# Bond energies — breaking bonds costs, making bonds pays — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Keep the accounts for a reaction in words.
**Answer: Add up the bond energies of every bond broken in the reactants (the cost), add up the bond energies of every bond formed in the products (the payback); ΔH is cost minus payback.**

**2.** Why must bonds break before a reaction can pay out heat?
**Answer: The products' bonds — whose forming releases the energy — can only form once the reactants' molecules are broken open; breaking is the payment that buys the rearrangement.**

**3.** Use the balance sheet to explain the alkane trend.
**Answer: Each larger alkane burns to more carbon dioxide and water, forming more product bonds; the payback grows with molar mass, so combustion energy rises along the series.**
