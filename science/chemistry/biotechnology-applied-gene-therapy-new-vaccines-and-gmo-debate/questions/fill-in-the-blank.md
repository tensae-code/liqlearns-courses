# Biotechnology applied — gene therapy, new vaccines and the GMO debate — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Recombinant organisms produce proteins and drugs, and mammalian-cell _____ runs by electroporation, viral vectors and microinjection.

**2.** The new _____ generations — recombinant, viral-vector, RNA — are set against traditional vaccines.

**3.** _____, from ADA-SCID and HIV to tumours, carries an eugenics warning in the guide, and GM plants and animals are debated on scientific sources.
