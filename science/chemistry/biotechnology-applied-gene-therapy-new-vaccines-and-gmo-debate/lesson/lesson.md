Biotechnology applied: recombinant organisms producing proteins and drugs, mammalian-cell transfection (electroporation, viral vectors, microinjection), traditional vaccines against the new generations (recombinant, viral-vector, RNA), gene therapy from ADA-SCID and HIV to tumours with its eugenics warning, and GM plants and animals with the risk debate assessed on scientific sources.

- Gene therapy, from ADA-SCID and HIV to tumours, carries an eugenics warning in the guide, and GM plants and animals are debated on scientific sources.
