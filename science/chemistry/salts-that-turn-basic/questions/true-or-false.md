# Salts that turn basic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** Which ion hydrolyses in sodium ethanoate — The ethanoate (anion)
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What is left behind in the water — Whether the Lewis structure is sound
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What does Kh link to — The parent acid's Ka (via Kw)
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What does the strong base's cation do — No — the same ester family
**Answer: False**
