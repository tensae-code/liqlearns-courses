# Salts that turn basic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which ion hydrolyses in sodium ethanoate?
a) One with three –OH groups  b) Yeast (its enzymes)  c) Weakly polar  d) The ethanoate (anion)
**Answer: d) The ethanoate (anion)**

**2. ** What is left behind in the water?
a) Its range must fall in the titration's pH jump  b) Gives electrons (is itself oxidised)  c) OH⁻ — so the solution is alkaline  d) Suppresses it
**Answer: c) OH⁻ — so the solution is alkaline**

**3. ** What does Kh link to?
a) PET  b) The parent acid's Ka (via Kw)  c) Centrifuging (spinning)  d) Slows microbial growth
**Answer: b) The parent acid's Ka (via Kw)**

**4. ** What does the strong base's cation do?
a) Nothing — it spectates  b) Three, along x, y and z  c) s, p, d and f  d) The noble gases
**Answer: a) Nothing — it spectates**
