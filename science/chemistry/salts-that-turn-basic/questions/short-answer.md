# Salts that turn basic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which ion hydrolyses in sodium ethanoate?
**Answer: The ethanoate (anion)**

**2. ** What is left behind in the water?
**Answer: OH⁻ — so the solution is alkaline**

**3. ** What does Kh link to?
**Answer: The parent acid's Ka (via Kw)**

**4. ** What does the strong base's cation do?
**Answer: Nothing — it spectates**
