# Video script — Salts that turn basic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sodium ethanoate comes from a strong base and a weak acid.

In a salt of a weak acid and a strong base, the anion hydrolyses: it takes a proton from water, leaving OH⁻ behind.

The solution is therefore alkaline.

The cation of the strong base spectates — it does not hydrolyse.

Now you. Say it back: Which ion hydrolyses in sodium ethanoate?
Answer: The ethanoate (anion).
