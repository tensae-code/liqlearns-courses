# Hands-on — Valence — the combining power of an element

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count from models (paper exercise)
Draw ball-and-stick sketches of water, ammonia and methane. On each central atom, ring the bonds it forms and write its valency beside it. Check your three numbers against the lesson: 2, 3, 4.

## Two countings sorted (paper exercise)
List six elements from the lesson in two columns — counted by shared pairs, counted by electrons transferred — and write each one's valency and the counting you used.
