Today: **Valence — the combining power of an element**.

Count what one atom does. In water oxygen shares two pairs; in ammonia nitrogen shares three; in methane carbon shares four. Where nothing is shared, count the electrons lost or gained instead. That count is the valency — and the valency table built from it is what writes formulas.

One power, two countings.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
