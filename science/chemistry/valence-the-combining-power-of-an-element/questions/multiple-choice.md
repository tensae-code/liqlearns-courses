# Valence — the combining power of an element — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Valency is an element's…
a) Mass  b) Combining power  c) Colour  d) Melting point
**Answer: b) Combining power**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In water, oxygen's valency — counted in shared pairs — is…
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3  d) 4
**Answer: b) 2**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In methane, carbon forms how many shared pairs?
a) 1  b) 2  c) 3  d) 4
**Answer: d) 4**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In an ionic compound, valency equals the number of…
a) Atoms present  b) Electrons lost or gained  c) Letters in the name  d) Crystals formed
**Answer: b) Electrons lost or gained**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Sodium loses one electron, so its ionic valency is…
a) 0  b) 1  c) 2  d) 3
**Answer: b) 1**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The element valency table is used next for…
a) Naming colours  b) Writing compound formulas by electrical neutrality  c) Weighing gases  d) Testing acids
**Answer: b) Writing compound formulas by electrical neutrality**
