# Valence — the combining power of an element — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Nitrogen's valency in ammonia, counted in shared pairs, is _____.
**Answer: 3**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Valency is an element's _____ power.
**Answer: combining**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Magnesium loses two electrons, so its ionic valency is _____.
**Answer: 2**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Syria reads valency off models of water, ammonia and _____.
**Answer: methane**
