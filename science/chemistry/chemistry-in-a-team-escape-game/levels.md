# Chemistry in a team escape game — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner describes the game's set-up and two phases and why no team can finish alone, names the six elements most common in the human body with their symbols, reads the six atomic numbers from a periodic table and explains how they become the lock code, and states honestly what kind of book the game comes from — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has three sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling the game's phases without notes, finding atomic numbers for elements beyond the six, and designing a boxed puzzle whose lock checks one true chemistry fact.
