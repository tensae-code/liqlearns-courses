# Chemistry in a team escape game — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A table escape game in Finnish is called a _____.
**Answer: pöytäpakopeli**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In phase 2, the teams combine their _____ pieces and solve the central final puzzle together.
**Answer: jigsaw**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The element with symbol Ca, one of the six most common in the human body, is _____.
**Answer: calcium**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The number of protons of an element, read from its cell in the periodic table, is its _____ number.
**Answer: atomic**
