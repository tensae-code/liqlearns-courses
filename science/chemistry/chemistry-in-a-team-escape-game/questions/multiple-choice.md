# Chemistry in a team escape game — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In phase 1 of the JUMISSA! game, each team…
a) Solves three boxed puzzles at its own table and earns jigsaw pieces  b) Solves the central final puzzle  c) Watches the game masters play  d) Memorises the periodic table
**Answer: a) Solves three boxed puzzles at its own table and earns jigsaw pieces**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The game's stated purpose is…
a) Testing chemistry  b) Group bonding — a new class becoming a team  c) Winning a prize  d) Finishing fastest
**Answer: b) Group bonding — a new class becoming a team**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The six elements most common in the human body are…
a) Iron, zinc, copper, iodine, sulfur and sodium  b) Gold, silver, lead, tin, mercury and aluminium  c) Oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, calcium and phosphorus  d) Helium, neon, argon, krypton, xenon and radon
**Answer: c) Oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, calcium and phosphorus**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the chemistry case, the six elements are marked…
a) In red ink  b) With a UV pen, visible under ultraviolet light  c) On the yellow card  d) On the box lid
**Answer: b) With a UV pen, visible under ultraviolet light**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The lock code is formed by…
a) Reading the elements' atomic numbers from the periodic table and substituting them into the formula on the yellow card  b) Counting the letters in each element's name  c) Guessing until the lock opens  d) Asking the game masters
**Answer: a) Reading the elements' atomic numbers from the periodic table and substituting them into the formula on the yellow card**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The atomic numbers of oxygen, carbon and hydrogen are…
a) 16, 12 and 2  b) 8, 6 and 1  c) 7, 20 and 15  d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: b) 8, 6 and 1**
