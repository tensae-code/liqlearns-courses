**Book 1:** *Jumissa pakopeli ryhmäytymiseen (JUMISSA! table escape game)* (student activity / game instructions) — Finland. Title as printed: "JUMISSA! -Pöytäpakopeli" (authors Ulla Huttu ja Paula Turunen). Grade: Grade 7 (record says grades 7–9 target group on the cover sheet). Kind as verified: student activity / game instructions — NOT a chemistry textbook. A linear table-top escape game for group bonding (ryhmäytyminen), ~60 min, min. 8 players, four colour teams. Repo path: grade-07/gov/finland/chemistry/student/finland-g07-aoe1557-jumissa-pakopelin-ohje.pdf (TSV blob URL and books.json record are the same book — duplicate record, read once, cited once). Structural summary: Game-master instructions: room set-up with four team tables plus a central final puzzle, two-phase play (teams solve three boxed puzzles each in phase 1, combine jigsaw pieces in phase 2), colour-coded task cards, lock codes given throughout for the game masters. Most puzzles are general (Caesar cipher, flags, matchsticks, health alphabet, football World Cup text, arithmetic). The single chemistry-bearing puzzle: a case holds an article about elements plus a periodic table; six elements most common in the human body (O, C, H, N, Ca, P) are marked with a UV pen and their atomic numbers, read from the periodic table and substituted into a formula on a yellow card, form a lock code.
