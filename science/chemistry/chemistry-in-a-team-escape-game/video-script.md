# Video script — Chemistry in a team escape game

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Chemistry in a team escape game** — Finland's way: a whole class, four colour teams, and one lock that only chemistry opens.

The game is an escape room on tables. Each team solves three boxed puzzles at its own table and earns pieces of a jigsaw. Then all the teams join their pieces and solve the last puzzle together. Nobody finishes alone — the game is played so a new class becomes one team.
Inside one case there is an article about elements and a periodic table. Marked in invisible UV ink are the six elements your body is mostly made of: oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, calcium and phosphorus. Read their atomic numbers in the table — 8, 6, 1, 7, 20, 15 — put them into the formula on the yellow card, and the answer is the code that opens the lock.
One game, one chemistry key: the periodic table used as a tool, the way chemists really use it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
