## Workout: draw it, name it, fix the wrong name

The unit's workout set is itself a teaching item: draw structures from names and name from structures across alkanes and alkenes (including halogenoalkenes such as 3-bromo-2-chlorohex-1-ene), name all five C₆H₁₄ isomers, complete the unit's signature reaction equations (hydrogenation, decarboxylation, Wurtz coupling, dehydration, hydration, carbide hydrolysis family), and diagnose five incorrect names — the naming rules of placements 67 and 76 applied as error-spotting.

## Say it to your companion
Explain workout: draw it, name it, fix the wrong name to your companion in your own words. Use the lesson's own example, say why it matters, and let your companion ask you one question you must answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself
1. Write the one thing this lesson teaches — workout: draw it, name it, fix the wrong name — in one sentence of your own, without looking back. Then check it against the lesson.
2. Find one example of this lesson's idea in your home, your market or your school compound, and describe it in two sentences. If the lesson is laboratory work, describe only — do not handle chemicals.
3. Draw the lesson's picture from memory, label its parts, and compare your drawing with the picture in the lesson.
