# Mixtures — homogeneous and heterogeneous — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A mixture that shows one phase is called _____.
**Answer: homogeneous**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A mixture that shows two phases is called _____.
**Answer: heterogeneous**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Sugar and salt _____ in water; tea and sand do not.
**Answer: dissolve**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The guide sorts mixtures first by what is _____.
**Answer: seen**
