# Mixtures — homogeneous and heterogeneous — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A homogeneous mixture shows…
a) Two phases  b) One phase — it looks the same everywhere  c) No phases  d) A gas phase only
**Answer: b) One phase — it looks the same everywhere**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Water + sand is…
a) Homogeneous  b) Heterogeneous — two phases  c) A compound  d) Pure water
**Answer: b) Heterogeneous — two phases**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Water + sugar is homogeneous because the sugar…
a) Sinks  b) Dissolves in the water  c) Changes colour  d) Floats
**Answer: b) Dissolves in the water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Which pair are both heterogeneous in the guide?
a) Water+sugar and water+salt  b) Water+tea and water+sand  c) Water+salt and water+tea  d) Sugar and salt
**Answer: b) Water+tea and water+sand**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A 'phase' in this chapter means…
a) A step in an experiment  b) A part of a mixture that looks uniform through itself  c) A kind of chemical  d) A week of the course
**Answer: b) A part of a mixture that looks uniform through itself**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Tea does not dissolve in water, so water+tea is…
a) Homogeneous  b) Heterogeneous  c) Distilled  d) A solution of tea
**Answer: b) Heterogeneous**
