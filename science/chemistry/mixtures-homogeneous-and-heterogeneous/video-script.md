Today: **Mixtures — homogeneous and heterogeneous**.

Mix sugar into water: it vanishes — one clear liquid, the same everywhere. Mix sand into water: the sand stays sand — you can see it. Djibouti's book sorts mixtures by that look. One phase, one uniform look: homogeneous — like water with sugar, or water with salt. Two phases, two things your eye can tell apart: heterogeneous — like water with tea, or water with sand. And the reason underneath? Sugar and salt dissolve in water. Tea and sand do not. See first: one phase or two. Then explain: dissolved — or not.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
