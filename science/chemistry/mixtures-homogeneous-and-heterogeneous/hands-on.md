# Hands-on — Mixtures — homogeneous and heterogeneous

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The four guide mixtures, described
Work through the guide's four mixtures on paper (or, in a supervised class practical, with kitchen materials only: water, sugar, salt, tea, clean sand). For each one, record: what is seen before stirring, what is seen after stirring and resting, how many phases, and the sorting word. The observation column must be filled before the sorting column — that order is the lesson.

## Phase hunt
Find three mixtures in daily life (a drink, a soup, muddy water, a sauce). For each, count the phases the eye can see and write the sorting word, with the evidence in one sentence each.
