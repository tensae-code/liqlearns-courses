# Hands-on — Position isomers — where the group sits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Move the group
Draw one short carbon chain twice. Put the same functional group on the first carbon in one drawing and on the second carbon in the other. Under each drawing write the same molecular formula, and label the pair: position isomers.

## Say the definition
Say Kuwait's definition in one sentence — what changes, and what stays the same — without looking back at the lesson.
