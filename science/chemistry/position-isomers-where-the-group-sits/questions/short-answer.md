# Position isomers — where the group sits — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Give Kuwait's definition of position isomers in one sentence.
**Answer:** Position isomers are compounds with the same molecular formula that arise when the functional group's place on the main carbon chain changes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Two compounds share a formula and a main chain, and their names differ only in the functional group's position number. What are they to each other, and why?
**Answer:** They are position isomers: everything — formula, chain, group — is the same except the carbon the group sits on, and that different place makes them different compounds.
