# The Downs cell — making sodium from its salt — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Downs cell electrolyses _____ sodium chloride.
**Answer: molten**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Chlorine forms at the _____ anode.
**Answer: graphite**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) At the cathode, sodium ions are _____ to sodium metal.
**Answer: reduced**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Liquid sodium _____ up into its own collecting chamber.
**Answer: floats**
