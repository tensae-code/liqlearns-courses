# The Downs cell — making sodium from its salt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Downs cell electrolyses sodium chloride in which state?
a) Molten  b) In cold water  c) In hot water  d) As a solid block
**Answer: a) Molten**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The cathode of the Downs cell is…
a) A graphite rod  b) An iron ring  c) A copper plate  d) The cell wall of lead
**Answer: b) An iron ring**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) At the anode, chloride ions…
a) Are reduced to chlorine  b) Form sodium chloride  c) Are oxidised to chlorine gas  d) Turn into sodium
**Answer: c) Are oxidised to chlorine gas**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The cathode half-equation is…
a) Na → Na⁺ + e⁻  b) 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻  c) Na⁺ + Cl⁻ → NaCl  d) Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na
**Answer: d) Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Sodium is collected separately because it…
a) Floats on the melt into its own chamber  b) Sinks to the cell floor  c) Dissolves in the chlorine  d) Turns to gas
**Answer: a) Floats on the melt into its own chamber**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The diaphragm between the electrodes is made of…
a) Graphite  b) Iron gauze  c) Glass  d) Plastic mesh
**Answer: b) Iron gauze**
