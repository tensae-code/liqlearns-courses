# The Downs cell — making sodium from its salt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give both half-equations of the Downs cell and name the process at each electrode.
**Answer: Anode: 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻ — oxidation. Cathode: Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na — reduction.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is a chemical reducing agent not used to make sodium?
**Answer: Sodium is too reactive: no ordinary chemical reducer is strong enough to free it from its compounds cheaply — electrolysis, the strongest method, is required.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What would go wrong without the diaphragm and the collecting chamber?
**Answer: The sodium and chlorine would meet and recombine violently (back to sodium chloride), and the sodium would also attack air and moisture — the products must be kept apart from birth.**
