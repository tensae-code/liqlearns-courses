# Denatured alcohol and illicit liquor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Denatured alcohol is…
a) Ethanol with the water removed  b) Ethanol made undrinkable on purpose — methanol added, with foul taste and violet dye  c) Alcohol made from wood  d) Any illicit liquor
**Answer: b) Ethanol made undrinkable on purpose — methanol added, with foul taste and violet dye**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Denaturing exists so that industrial spirit can be…
a) Made stronger  b) Sold duty-free, free of drink tax, without being drunk  c) Exported  d) Stored longer
**Answer: b) Sold duty-free, free of drink tax, without being drunk**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Spurious liquor kills and blinds because it is stretched with…
a) Water  b) Methanol, which the body turns into formic acid  c) Sugar  d) Ethanol
**Answer: b) Methanol, which the body turns into formic acid**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The first identification of suspect liquor, as the chapter teaches it, is…
a) Its taste  b) Its origin — unlabelled, cheap and of unknown source  c) Its colour  d) Its bottle shape
**Answer: b) Its origin — unlabelled, cheap and of unknown source**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The chapter reckons ethanol's impacts in which four columns?
a) Price, taste, strength, colour  b) Environment, economy, society and health  c) Home, school, work, travel  d) Past, present, future, myth
**Answer: b) Environment, economy, society and health**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Activity 9.7, the chapter's closing case study by Dr. Chencho Dorji, is titled…
a) "The Chemistry of Brewing"  b) "The Myth behind Alcohol Happiness"  c) "Spirit of Bhutan"  d) "Denaturing at Work"
**Answer: b) "The Myth behind Alcohol Happiness"**
