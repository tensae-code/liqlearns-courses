# Denatured alcohol and illicit liquor — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Denatured alcohol is commonly sold as _____ spirit.
**Answer: methylated**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The violet _____ in methylated spirit is its visible warning that it is not a drink.
**Answer: dye**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The body turns methanol into _____ acid, which attacks the optic nerve.
**Answer: formic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The closing case study calls alcohol's promised happiness a _____.
**Answer: myth**
