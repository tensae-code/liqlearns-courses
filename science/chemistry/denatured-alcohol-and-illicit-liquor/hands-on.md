# Hands-on — Denatured alcohol and illicit liquor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Label detective (paper only)
Design the warning label methylated spirit should carry, from sub lesson 1: it must say what the liquid is, what has been added, the dye's meaning, and the one-line reason it must never be drunk. Compare your label against the lesson — every fact on it must come from the lesson.

## Origin check (paper only)
Three bottles are described: a sealed, labelled bottle from a licensed shop; an unlabelled bottle sold cheaply at a roadside; a violet-tinted bottle in a school laboratory. For each, write its likely identity from the lesson and the safe handling the chapter teaches.

## Four columns (paper only)
Draw the chapter's four columns — environment, economy, society, health — and place these six items: distillery waste; tax revenue; a road accident after drinking; liver disease; a distillery worker's wages; a family's broken budget. Defend each placement in one phrase.
