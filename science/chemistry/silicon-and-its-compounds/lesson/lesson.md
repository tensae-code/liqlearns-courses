# Silicon and its compounds

## Do it first
Before reading, say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is: silicon and its compounds. Then read on — this is Kazakhstan's way of teaching it, as the Kazakhstan placements describe the books' own treatment.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 10 §10.1–10.2 Silicon; physical properties and uses of silicon — Kazakhstan's Grade 9 book (Grade 9, NIS trial)

Diamond-like lattice, semiconductor behaviour, n- and p-type doping with group-13/15 atoms; solar cells and microchips. (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, NIS trial, Ch. 10 §10.1–10.2 Silicon; physical properties and uses of silicon, printed p. 154).

## Sub lesson 7 — Ch. 8 §39 Silicon and its compounds — Kazakhstan's Grade 9 book (Grade 9, Usmanova et al.)

## Sub lesson 8 — Module 8 Silicon and its compounds — Kazakhstan's Grade 9 book (Grade 9, Express Publishing)

Silicon, silicon dioxide and silicon carbide as covalent macromolecular (diamond-like) structures in one table; silicon won from silica and coke; ferrosilicon and aluminium–silicon alloys; silicones; silica in glass, cement, bricks and precision casting (same proposed slug as Books 21/27/28/31). (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, Express Publishing, Module 8 Silicon and its compounds, printed p. 108).

## Sub lesson 9 — Module 8: silicon as a semiconductor — Kazakhstan's Grade 9 book (Grade 9, Express Publishing)

n-type doping with phosphorus, p-type with boron, positive holes, the p–n junction and depletion layer, forward and reverse bias in a diode, and transistors into silicon chips. (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, Express Publishing, Module 8: silicon as a semiconductor, printed p. 112).

## The key ideas

- Silicon — the group-14 element with a diamond-like lattice and semiconductor behaviour, tuned by n-type and p-type doping into diodes and chips — and its compounds: silicon dioxide, silicic acid and the silicates, and the silicate industry (glass, cement, ceramics) built on them, including Kazakhstan's own silicate industry.
- Diamond-like lattice, semiconductor behaviour, n- and p-type doping with group-13/15 atoms; solar cells and microchips. (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, NIS trial, Ch. 10 §10.1–10.2 Silicon; physical properties and uses of silicon, printed p. 154).
