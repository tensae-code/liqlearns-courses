# Lesson plan — Silicon and its compounds

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Silicon and its compounds
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Silicon — the group-14 element with a diamond-like lattice and semiconductor behaviour, tuned by n-type and p-type doping into diodes and chips — and its compounds: silicon dioxide, silicic acid and the silicates, and the silicate industry (glass, cement, ceramics) built on them, including Kazakhstan's own silicate industry.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
