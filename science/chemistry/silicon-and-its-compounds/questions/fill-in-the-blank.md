# Silicon and its compounds — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Silicon's semiconductor behaviour is tuned by _____: n-type with a group-15 element, p-type with a group-13 element.
**Answer: doping**

**2.** Silica, silicic acid and the _____ are silicon's working compounds, the base of glass and cement.
**Answer: silicates**
