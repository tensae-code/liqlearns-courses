# Hands-on — Carbon monoxide — the silent killer and furnace gas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Safety poster (paper only)
Design the poster the lesson implies for a cold-region home: the brazier, the closed room, the invisible gas, and the three defences (airflow, never sleeping with the brazier burning in a closed room, knowing the gas has no smell). Every claim on the poster must come from the lesson.

## One gas, two jobs (paper only)
Write two short paragraphs with the same opening line — 'Carbon monoxide…' — one ending at the closed bedroom, one at the blast furnace. Under each, write the equation that belongs to it and the property of the gas that decides the ending.
