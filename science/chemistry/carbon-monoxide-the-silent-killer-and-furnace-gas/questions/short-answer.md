# Carbon monoxide — the silent killer and furnace gas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write both of the lesson's equations, one for CO's birth and one for its furnace work.
**Answer: Birth: 2C + O₂ → 2CO (limited oxygen). Furnace: Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain the haemoglobin hijack in two sentences.
**Answer: Carbon monoxide occupies haemoglobin in place of oxygen and holds the seat. The blood then cannot carry oxygen to the tissues, which starve while the victim goes on breathing.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is ¹⁴C's use as the lesson closes?
**Answer: Natural carbon's trace of radioactive ¹⁴C works as a clock: its remaining radioactivity dates fossils and old trees.**
