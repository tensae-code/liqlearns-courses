# Carbon monoxide — the silent killer and furnace gas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon monoxide forms when carbon burns in…
a) Limited oxygen  b) Pure oxygen  c) Water  d) Nitrogen
**Answer: a) Limited oxygen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) 2C + O₂ → 2CO needs which condition?
a) A catalyst  b) Too little oxygen for complete burning  c) High pressure  d) Sunlight
**Answer: b) Too little oxygen for complete burning**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Carbon monoxide can be detected by a person nearby by…
a) Its blue colour  b) Its rotten smell  c) Neither sight nor smell — it is colourless and odourless  d) Its sweet taste
**Answer: c) Neither sight nor smell — it is colourless and odourless**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) It kills by taking oxygen's place on…
a) Insulin  b) Chlorophyll  c) Enzymes of the stomach  d) Haemoglobin
**Answer: d) Haemoglobin**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The book's named scene of its deaths is…
a) Charcoal burned for warmth in closed rooms in winter  b) Swimming pools  c) Laboratories  d) Mountain tops
**Answer: a) Charcoal burned for warmth in closed rooms in winter**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the blast furnace, CO serves as…
a) The fuel gas only  b) The reducing gas  c) The flux  d) The coolant
**Answer: b) The reducing gas**
