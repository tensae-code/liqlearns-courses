# Carbon monoxide — the silent killer and furnace gas — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon monoxide is colourless and _____.
**Answer: odourless**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) It rides the _____ in place of oxygen.
**Answer: haemoglobin**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In the blast furnace, CO is the _____ gas.
**Answer: reducing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Radioactive carbon-_____ dates fossils and old trees.
**Answer: 14**
