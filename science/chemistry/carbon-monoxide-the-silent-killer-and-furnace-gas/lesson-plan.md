# Lesson plan — Carbon monoxide — the silent killer and furnace gas

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Carbon monoxide — the silent killer and furnace gas
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Carbon monoxide as Yemen's Grade 11 book teaches it: formed when carbon burns in limited oxygen (2C + O2 -> 2CO), colourless and odourless, riding the haemoglobin in place of oxygen and so killing — the text names the yearly winter deaths when families burn charcoal for warmth in closed rooms in cold regions — and industrially the reducing gas of the blast furnace (Fe2O3 + 3CO -> 2Fe + 3CO2), closing with radioactive 14C in natural carbon, used to date fossils and old trees
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. Complete and incomplete combustion is recommended first, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
