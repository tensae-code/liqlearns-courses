## Periodicity

The unit opens with an Ethiopian calendar (Nehassie 2013 E.C.) whose missing entries can be filled from the repeating pattern of weeks — the concrete model for periodicity: properties repeating at regular intervals let you predict the unknown from its position. Early classifications were built on atomic mass because atoms' inner structure was not yet known.
