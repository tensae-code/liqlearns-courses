# Video script — Complete and incomplete combustion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Complete and incomplete combustion** — one flame, two burnings.

Open the air hole: oxygen plentiful, flame blue, carbon dioxide and water — complete. Close it: oxygen short, flame yellow and sooty, a dish blackened — and carbon monoxide, the silent blood poison, formed — incomplete.

The oxygen supply decides which combustion you get.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
