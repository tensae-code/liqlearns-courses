# Hands-on — Complete and incomplete combustion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Flame watching (teacher demonstration only — no home flames)
In the school laboratory only, with a teacher: watch a Bunsen flame with the air hole open, then closed. Record flame colour, and hold a cold porcelain dish briefly in each flame as the book does. Write which run blackened the dish and connect it to the lesson's two kinds.

## Combustion in the home (observation only)
List three burning things at home or school — a gas ring, a candle, a stove. For each, write how air reaches the flame and one sign you could observe of complete or incomplete burning (soot marks, flame colour). Do not adjust any appliance; observe and record only.
