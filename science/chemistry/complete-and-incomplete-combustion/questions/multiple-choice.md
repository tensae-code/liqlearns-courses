# Complete and incomplete combustion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Combustion is reaction with…
a) Nitrogen  b) Oxygen at a suitable temperature  c) Water  d) Carbon dioxide
**Answer: b) Oxygen at a suitable temperature**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In Syria's test, the one thing changed is…
a) The fuel  b) The burner  c) The air hole — open vs closed  d) The room
**Answer: c) The air hole — open vs closed**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Complete combustion gives a flame that is…
a) Yellow and sooty  b) Blue  c) Green  d) Invisible
**Answer: b) Blue**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The products of complete combustion are…
a) Soot and carbon monoxide  b) Carbon dioxide and water  c) Hydrogen and oxygen  d) Ash only
**Answer: b) Carbon dioxide and water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A porcelain dish held in the incomplete flame…
a) Stays clean  b) Turns blue  c) Is blackened by soot  d) Melts
**Answer: c) Is blackened by soot**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Carbon monoxide is dangerous because it…
a) Smells strongly  b) Silently poisons the blood  c) Colours the flame  d) Feeds the fire
**Answer: b) Silently poisons the blood**
