# Complete and incomplete combustion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe Syria's Bunsen test and what it isolates.
**Answer: One Bunsen flame is run twice with a single change — the air hole open, then closed — so the oxygen supply is the only difference; whatever differs between the two flames is the oxygen's doing.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Contrast the two combustions: oxygen, flame, products.
**Answer: Complete: plentiful oxygen, blue flame, carbon dioxide + water. Incomplete: oxygen short, yellow sooty flame blackening a porcelain dish, and carbon monoxide formed.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What does the enrichment box (printed p. 27) add?
**Answer: The warning that carbon monoxide silently poisons the blood — colourless and odourless, it is taken up where oxygen should ride, which is why incomplete combustion is dangerous, not merely dirty.**
