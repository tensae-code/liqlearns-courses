# Complete and incomplete combustion — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Combustion is reaction with _____ at a suitable temperature.
**Answer: oxygen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) With plentiful oxygen the Bunsen flame burns _____.
**Answer: blue**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Incomplete combustion blackens a porcelain dish with _____.
**Answer: soot**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The gas that silently poisons the blood is carbon _____.
**Answer: monoxide**
