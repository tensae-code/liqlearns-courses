# Preferential discharge — which ion wins

## Do it first
At one electrode, three different ions queue to be discharged. Only one goes first. Before reading, list what you think the judges are.

[Picture 21734 — Competing ions, the winner marked.]

When several ions could discharge at an electrode, the one actually discharged is chosen by preferential discharge.

The judges: the ion's position in the electrochemical (reactivity) series — the easier to discharge goes first;

the concentration of the ion — a much more concentrated ion can win over position; and the nature of the electrode itself. The three factors together decide every case in the section.
