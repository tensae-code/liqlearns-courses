# Video script — Preferential discharge — which ion wins

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

At one electrode, three different ions queue to be discharged.

When several ions could discharge at an electrode, the one actually discharged is chosen by preferential discharge.

The judges: the ion's position in the electrochemical (reactivity) series — the easier to discharge goes first;.

the concentration of the ion — a much more concentrated ion can win over position; and the nature of the electrode itself.

Now you. Say it back: Name the three discharge judges.
Answer: Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature.
