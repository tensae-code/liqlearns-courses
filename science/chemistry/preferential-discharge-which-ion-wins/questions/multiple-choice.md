# Preferential discharge — which ion wins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Name the three discharge judges.
a) The orbital's orientation  b) On the right of the table  c) Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature  d) The uncertainty principle
**Answer: c) Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature**

**2. ** What can overturn position order?
a) –OH (hydroxyl)  b) Much higher concentration  c) Irreversible  d) Partial pressures
**Answer: b) Much higher concentration**

**3. ** What is the series called?
a) The electrochemical (reactivity) series  b) Seal out air; keep cool, dark, dry  c) H₃O⁺ (on a water molecule)  d) A weak acid's ionisation strength
**Answer: a) The electrochemical (reactivity) series**

**4. ** Does electrode material matter?
a) Harvests remove it from the soil  b) Sodium hydroxide, chlorine, hydrogen  c) Clinker  d) Yes — it is one of the three factors
**Answer: d) Yes — it is one of the three factors**
