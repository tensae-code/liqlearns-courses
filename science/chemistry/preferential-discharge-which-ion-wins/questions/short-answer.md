# Preferential discharge — which ion wins — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Name the three discharge judges.
**Answer: Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature**

**2. ** What can overturn position order?
**Answer: Much higher concentration**

**3. ** What is the series called?
**Answer: The electrochemical (reactivity) series**

**4. ** Does electrode material matter?
**Answer: Yes — it is one of the three factors**
