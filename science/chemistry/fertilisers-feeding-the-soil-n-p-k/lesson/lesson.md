## Fertilisers

Plants need mineral nutrients in two tiers — macronutrients (needed in large amounts) and micronutrients (traces). Fertilisers, natural or synthetic, restore soil fertility and raise crop-producing potential; synthetic fertilisers are classified by the nutrient they supply — nitrogen fertilisers, phosphorus fertilisers and potassium fertilisers (the N-P-K families).

Pesticides are the companion agricultural chemicals, used to kill the pests that cut yields — the Unit 1 Grade 9 industry content (Adami Tulu) now met from the user's side.5).
