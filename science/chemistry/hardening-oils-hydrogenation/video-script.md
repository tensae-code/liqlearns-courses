# Video script — Hardening oils — hydrogenation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Margarine starts as oil.

Hardening adds hydrogen across the C=C double bonds of an oil, with a nickel-type catalyst as presented, converting unsaturated tails toward saturated ones.

The straighter tails pack better, so the oil becomes a solid fat — margarine is made this way.

Partial hydrogenation hardens only some double bonds, giving in-between textures.

Now you. Say it back: What gas hardens oils?
Answer: Hydrogen, H₂.
