# Hardening oils — hydrogenation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What gas hardens oils?
**Answer: Hydrogen, H₂**

**2. ** What happens to C=C bonds in hardening?
**Answer: They become single bonds**

**3. ** What product is made by hardening?
**Answer: Margarine (solid fat)**

**4. ** What does partial hydrogenation do?
**Answer: Hardens only some double bonds**
