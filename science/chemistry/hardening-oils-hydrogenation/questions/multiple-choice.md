# Hardening oils — hydrogenation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What gas hardens oils?
a) They do not react with most solutes  b) –COOH  c) Alcohol → aldehyde → acid  d) Hydrogen, H₂
**Answer: d) Hydrogen, H₂**

**2. ** What happens to C=C bonds in hardening?
a) Position in the series, concentration, electrode nature  b) The same quantity of electricity  c) They become single bonds  d) A burette
**Answer: c) They become single bonds**

**3. ** What product is made by hardening?
a) No — natural, but strengthened by humans  b) Margarine (solid fat)  c) The hydrosphere  d) Carbon monoxide (or particulates/hydrocarbons)
**Answer: b) Margarine (solid fat)**

**4. ** What does partial hydrogenation do?
a) Hardens only some double bonds  b) Energy released when ions form the crystal lattice  c) They share two and three pairs  d) Attraction of kernels to the electron pool
**Answer: a) Hardens only some double bonds**
