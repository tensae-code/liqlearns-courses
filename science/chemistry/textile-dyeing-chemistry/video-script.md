Today: **Textile dyeing chemistry** — Laos's way: dyeing taught as an industry, where colour must be bound into cloth, not just soaked onto it.

A dye is colour that attaches to fibres and stays — through washing and wear. The dyer controls the dye, the fibre, the bath and the fixing, and the same dye takes differently on different cloth. That control is the chemistry — and in Laos's book it earns dyeing its own industry chapter.
