# Textile dyeing chemistry — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is dyeing classed as chemistry rather than only as craft?
**Answer:** Because it is the controlled binding of a coloured substance (the dye) to a material (the fibre) — the result depends on the dye, the fibre kind, the bath conditions and fixing, all chemical controls.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Name four industries whose chapters stand with dyeing in Part VII of Laos's book.
**Answer:** Any four of: mineral industry, cement, ceramics, glass, perfume, scientific-fertiliser industry.
