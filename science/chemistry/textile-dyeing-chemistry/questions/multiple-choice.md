# Textile dyeing chemistry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dyeing differs from simply soaking cloth in coloured water because…
a) The water is hotter  b) The dye must attach to the fibres and stay there  c) The cloth is thicker  d) The colour is darker
**Answer: b) The dye must attach to the fibres and stay there**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The colour of a dyeing is controlled by…
a) Only the dye  b) Dye, fibre kind, bath, time, temperature and fixing  c) Only the temperature  d) The shape of the cloth
**Answer: b) Dye, fibre kind, bath, time, temperature and fixing**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In Laos's Grade 12 book, dyeing is taught as…
a) A food chapter  b) An industry chapter (Ch. 32) in the Chemical industry part  c) A kinetics chapter  d) A hobby note
**Answer: b) An industry chapter (Ch. 32) in the Chemical industry part**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A dye is…
a) Any coloured powder  b) A coloured substance that binds to fibres so the colour stays  c) A kind of fibre  d) A washing chemical
**Answer: b) A coloured substance that binds to fibres so the colour stays**
