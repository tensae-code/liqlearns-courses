# Hands-on — Textile dyeing chemistry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Soak and rinse (kitchen-safe)
With a teacher or at home with plain food colouring only: soak two different cloth scraps (e.g. cotton and a synthetic) in the same coloured water for the same time. Rinse both the same way.

## Compare
Lay the scraps side by side. Which took more colour? Which lost more on rinsing? Say, from the lesson, what decided it: the dye is the same — so the difference is the fibre and how the dye binds to it.

## Record like a dyer
Write the dyer's four controls from the lesson (dye, fibre, bath/time/temperature, fixing) and mark which ones you held the same in your test and which one you changed.
