# Carboxylic acid derivatives — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Derivatives are made by replacing the _____ of the carboxyl group.
**Answer: –OH**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The amide named as the book's food example is _____.
**Answer: nicotinamide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Removing _____ from two acid molecules forms an anhydride.
**Answer: water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Acyl chlorides are named as –oyl _____.
**Answer: chlorides**
