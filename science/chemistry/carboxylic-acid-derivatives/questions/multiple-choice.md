# Carboxylic acid derivatives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A carboxylic acid derivative is made by…
a) Adding a carbon ring  b) Replacing the –OH of the carboxyl group  c) Removing all the oxygen  d) Heating any alcohol
**Answer: b) Replacing the –OH of the carboxyl group**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Amides are made, as Fiji's book gives it, by…
a) Freezing the acid  b) Heating the ammonium salt of the carboxylic acid  c) Adding PCl₅ to an alcohol  d) Distilling a hydrocarbon
**Answer: b) Heating the ammonium salt of the carboxylic acid**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An acid anhydride is formed by…
a) Removing water from two carboxylic acid molecules  b) Adding water to an acid  c) Burning an acid  d) Mixing two metals
**Answer: a) Removing water from two carboxylic acid molecules**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ethanoic anhydride is named in the book as used for…
a) Soap  b) Aspirin  c) Glass  d) Fertiliser
**Answer: b) Aspirin**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Acyl chlorides are named as…
a) –amide names  b) –oyl chlorides  c) –ane names  d) –ate salts
**Answer: b) –oyl chlorides**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) An acyl chloride is prepared from the acid with…
a) Water or steam only  b) PCl₅ or PCl₃, then separated by fractional distillation  c) Ammonia and heat  d) Sodium hydroxide
**Answer: b) PCl₅ or PCl₃, then separated by fractional distillation**
