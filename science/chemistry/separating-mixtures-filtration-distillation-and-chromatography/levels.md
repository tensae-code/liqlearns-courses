# Separating mixtures — filtration, distillation and chromatography — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Choosing the method — and filtration, Evaporation, crystallisation and distillation — recovering what dissolved, The separating funnel and paper chromatography — liquids that will not mix, colours that will not stay), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, matching fresh mixtures to methods with the deciding property named each time, and drawing the distillation apparatus from memory with every part labelled.
