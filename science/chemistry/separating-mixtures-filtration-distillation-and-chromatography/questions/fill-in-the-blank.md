# Separating mixtures — filtration, distillation and chromatography — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The solid left on the filter paper is called the _____.
**Answer: residue**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In simple distillation the vapour is cooled back to liquid inside a Liebig _____.
**Answer: condenser**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Rum and the fractions of petroleum are separated by _____ distillation.
**Answer: fractional**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In a separating funnel the _____ layer, being denser, runs out first.
**Answer: lower**
