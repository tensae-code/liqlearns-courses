# Separating mixtures — filtration, distillation and chromatography — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The rule that chooses a separation method is…
a) Always filter first  b) Choose by the physical property in which the components differ  c) Heat every mixture  d) Use the newest apparatus
**Answer: b) Choose by the physical property in which the components differ**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In filtration, the liquid that passes through the paper is the…
a) Residue  b) Filtrate  c) Distillate  d) Crystal
**Answer: b) Filtrate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Crystallisation is preferred over evaporation when…
a) The solvent is wanted  b) The solid would be damaged by heating it dry  c) The mixture is coloured  d) The solid is insoluble
**Answer: b) The solid would be damaged by heating it dry**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Fractional distillation separates two liquids because they have different…
a) Colours  b) Densities  c) Boiling points  d) Smells
**Answer: c) Boiling points**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A separating funnel works for oil and water because the two liquids are…
a) Miscible  b) Immiscible  c) Both gases  d) Both solids
**Answer: b) Immiscible**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Paper chromatography can serve as forensic evidence because…
a) Ink is expensive  b) Each ink spreads into a spot pattern that acts as a fingerprint  c) Paper is white  d) Solvents climb quickly
**Answer: b) Each ink spreads into a spot pattern that acts as a fingerprint**
