Today: **Separating mixtures** — Dominica's CSEC separation strand: one rule picks every method.

A mixture comes apart only where its components differ. Too big to pass the paper? Filtration — residue on top, filtrate below. Dissolved? Evaporate it dry, or grow crystals slowly. Want the liquid itself? Distil it — once through a Liebig condenser for a solution, up a fractionating column when two liquids must part, as petroleum and rum part. Two liquids that will not mix meet in the separating funnel. And the tiniest mixtures climb a paper in chromatography — far enough that a court can read a pen's fingerprint in its spots.
