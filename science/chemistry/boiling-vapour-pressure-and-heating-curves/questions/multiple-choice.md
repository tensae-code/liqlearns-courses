# Boiling, vapour pressure and heating curves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** When does a liquid boil?
a) The alcohol (alkyl part)  b) When vapour pressure equals outside pressure  c) Carbonyl C=O and hydroxyl –OH  d) Hydrolysis (as presented)
**Answer: b) When vapour pressure equals outside pressure**

**2. ** What happens to temperature on a plateau?
a) It stays constant during the change  b) The electrochemical (reactivity) series  c) Chemical equivalents  d) ΔG° = −nFE°
**Answer: a) It stays constant during the change**

**3. ** What is the normal boiling point measured at?
a) Lungs and health  b) CO₂, H₂O vapour, CH₄, N₂O, ozone  c) Plato and Aristotle  d) 1 atm (atmospheric pressure)
**Answer: d) 1 atm (atmospheric pressure)**

**4. ** What does heat do during a plateau?
a) Delocalised electrons  b) No stable molecule forms  c) It breaks particle arrangements  d) Whether the Lewis structure is sound
**Answer: c) It breaks particle arrangements**
