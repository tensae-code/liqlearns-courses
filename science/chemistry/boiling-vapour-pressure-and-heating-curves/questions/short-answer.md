# Boiling, vapour pressure and heating curves — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** When does a liquid boil?
**Answer: When vapour pressure equals outside pressure**

**2. ** What happens to temperature on a plateau?
**Answer: It stays constant during the change**

**3. ** What is the normal boiling point measured at?
**Answer: 1 atm (atmospheric pressure)**

**4. ** What does heat do during a plateau?
**Answer: It breaks particle arrangements**
