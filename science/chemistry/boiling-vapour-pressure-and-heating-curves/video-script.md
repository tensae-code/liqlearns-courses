# Video script — Boiling, vapour pressure and heating curves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Heat ice steadily and its temperature climbs — then stops dead at melting, though heat still flows in.

Vapour pressure is the pressure of a liquid's vapour above it; it rises with temperature.

The molar heat of vaporisation is the energy to turn one mole of liquid into gas; the molar heat of fusion does the same for melting.

On a heating curve, sloping parts show temperature rising; the flat plateaus are phase changes, where added heat breaks particle arrangements instead of raising temperature.

Now you. Say it back: When does a liquid boil?
Answer: When vapour pressure equals outside pressure.
