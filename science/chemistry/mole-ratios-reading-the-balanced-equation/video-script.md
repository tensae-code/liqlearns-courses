Today: **Mole ratios — reading the balanced equation**.

The coefficients of a balanced equation are mole ratios between any two substances in the reaction;

every stoichiometric problem starts by writing and balancing the equation and reading the needed ratio off it (mole–mole problems).

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
