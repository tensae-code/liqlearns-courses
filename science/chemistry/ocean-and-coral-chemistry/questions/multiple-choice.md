# Ocean and coral chemistry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In seawater, dissolved carbon dioxide is in equilibrium with…
a) Carbonic acid, hydrogen carbonate ions and carbonate ions  b) Calcium carbonate only  c) Sodium chloride  d) Oxygen and hydrogen
**Answer: a) Carbonic acid, hydrogen carbonate ions and carbonate ions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When seawater becomes more acidic, its carbonate ions…
a) Increase  b) Stay the same  c) Pick up hydrogen ions and become hydrogen carbonate, so carbonate grows scarce  d) Turn into calcium ions
**Answer: c) Pick up hydrogen ions and become hydrogen carbonate, so carbonate grows scarce**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A coral polyp builds its skeleton out of…
a) Sand grains glued together  b) Calcium carbonate, from calcium ions and carbonate ions taken from seawater  c) Sodium carbonate from the Solvay process  d) Its own dead cells only
**Answer: b) Calcium carbonate, from calcium ions and carbonate ions taken from seawater**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The building equation for a coral skeleton is…
a) Ca²⁺ + CO₃²⁻ → CaCO₃  b) CO₂ + H₂O → H₂CO₃  c) NaCl → Na⁺ + Cl⁻  d) CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂
**Answer: a) Ca²⁺ + CO₃²⁻ → CaCO₃**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Ocean acidification happens when…
a) The ocean warms  b) The ocean absorbs extra carbon dioxide, forming more carbonic acid and lowering the pH  c) Coral polyps die  d) Rain falls on the reef
**Answer: b) The ocean absorbs extra carbon dioxide, forming more carbonic acid and lowering the pH**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In more acidic seawater, existing coral skeleton can…
a) Grow faster  b) Turn into sand at once  c) Dissolve back into the water  d) Become metal
**Answer: c) Dissolve back into the water**
