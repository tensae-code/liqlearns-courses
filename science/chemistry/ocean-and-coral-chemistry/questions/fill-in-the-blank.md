# Ocean and coral chemistry — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon dioxide dissolved in seawater reacts with water to form carbonic _____.
**Answer: acid**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A coral skeleton is made of calcium _____.
**Answer: carbonate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The small animal that builds the coral skeleton is called a _____.
**Answer: polyp**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Seawater growing more acidic as it absorbs carbon dioxide is called ocean _____.
**Answer: acidification**
