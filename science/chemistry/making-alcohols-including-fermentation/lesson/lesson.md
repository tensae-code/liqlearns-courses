# Making alcohols — including fermentation

## Do it first
Tella and tej bubble as they brew — a gas is being made alongside the alcohol. Before reading, say which gas, and what living thing does the work.

[Picture 21686 — A fermentation vessel bubbling CO₂.]

Alkenes add water (hydration) to give alcohols, under the section's conditions.

Fermentation: sugars are broken down by yeast enzymes to ethanol and carbon dioxide — the route of traditional Ethiopian brews in the book's activities.

Alcohols also come from reduction of carbonyl compounds and hydrolysis routes, as presented.
