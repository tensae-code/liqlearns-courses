# Making alcohols — including fermentation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What gas does fermentation release?
**Answer: Carbon dioxide, CO₂**

**2. ** What breaks sugar down in fermentation?
**Answer: Yeast (its enzymes)**

**3. ** What does alkene hydration add?
**Answer: Water, across the double bond**

**4. ** Name the traditional-brewing route.
**Answer: Fermentation of sugars**
