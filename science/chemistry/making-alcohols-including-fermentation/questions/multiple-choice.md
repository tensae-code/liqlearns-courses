# Making alcohols — including fermentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What gas does fermentation release?
a) Near neutral  b) Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)  c) Carbon dioxide, CO₂  d) Strong acid + strong base salt
**Answer: c) Carbon dioxide, CO₂**

**2. ** What breaks sugar down in fermentation?
a) The biosphere  b) Yeast (its enzymes)  c) Care/treatment — it pollutes  d) A small molecule that joins into chains
**Answer: b) Yeast (its enzymes)**

**3. ** What does alkene hydration add?
a) Water, across the double bond  b) Properties repeat periodically with atomic number  c) Eight electrons — an octet  d) Lattice formation
**Answer: a) Water, across the double bond**

**4. ** Name the traditional-brewing route.
a) Le Chatelier's principle  b) Hydrogen bonds  c) An –OH group  d) Fermentation of sugars
**Answer: d) Fermentation of sugars**
