# Lesson plan — Writing electron configurations

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Writing electron configurations
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Atomic Structure and Periodic Properties of the Elements (Grade 11 Chemistry).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Aufbau principle: electrons fill the lowest-energy orbitals first, in the filling order the diagram gives.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 11 Chemistry lessons before it in Unit 1 — Atomic Structure and Periodic Properties of the Elements — and on the learner's earlier chemistry. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
1. **Aufbau — lowest first** — The Aufbau principle: electrons fill the lowest-energy orbitals first, in the filling order the diagram gives.
2. **Pauli — two per orbital, opposite spins** — The Pauli exclusion principle: one orbital holds at most two electrons, and they must have opposite spins.
3. **Hund — singly before pairing** — Hund's rule: in a set of equal-energy orbitals, electrons occupy each singly first, with spins parallel, before pairing up.
4. **Configurations, diagrams and the exceptions** — With the three rules you can write the ground-state configuration and the orbital (box) diagram of any atom or ion — including the Cr/Cu-type exceptions, where a half-filled or fully filled subshell wins extra stability.

1. Answer, in their own words: which rule says fill lowest energy first?
2. Answer, in their own words: what does Pauli's principle limit?
3. Answer, in their own words: what does Hund's rule require before pairing?
4. Answer, in their own words: what is an orbital diagram drawn with?
