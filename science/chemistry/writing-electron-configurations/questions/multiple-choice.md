# Writing electron configurations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Which rule says fill lowest energy first?
a) A (symmetrical) ether  b) Carbon 1  c) Carbon dioxide, CO₂  d) The Aufbau principle
**Answer: d) The Aufbau principle**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** What does Pauli's principle limit?
a) So electrons lost equal electrons gained  b) Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q)  c) Two electrons per orbital, opposite spins  d) Exactly neutralise each other
**Answer: c) Two electrons per orbital, opposite spins**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** What does Hund's rule require before pairing?
a) Pesticide and fertiliser residues  b) Singly occupy each equal orbital first  c) PET or nylon  d) Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil
**Answer: b) Singly occupy each equal orbital first**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) ** What is an orbital diagram drawn with?
a) Boxes and arrows  b) A negative ion (anion)  c) Strong lattice attractions in all directions  d) Temporary flickering dipoles
**Answer: a) Boxes and arrows**
