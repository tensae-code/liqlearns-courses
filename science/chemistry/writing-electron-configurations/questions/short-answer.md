# Writing electron configurations — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** Which rule says fill lowest energy first?
**Answer: The Aufbau principle**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** What does Pauli's principle limit?
**Answer: Two electrons per orbital, opposite spins**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** What does Hund's rule require before pairing?
**Answer: Singly occupy each equal orbital first**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** What is an orbital diagram drawn with?
**Answer: Boxes and arrows**
