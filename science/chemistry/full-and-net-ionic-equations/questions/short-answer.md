# Full and net ionic equations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Write BaCl₂ + Na₂SO₄ in net ionic form and name the spectators.
**Answer: Ba²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ → BaSO₄↓; Na⁺ and Cl⁻ are the spectators — present in the full ionic equation, cancelled in the net.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does neutralisation run to completion?
**Answer: Because H⁺ and OH⁻ bind durably into water, a very weak electrolyte — the product the completion rule requires.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What three checks does the exercise lesson run on a proposed ion-exchange reaction?
**Answer: Its dissociation equations must be right, the solubility table must allow the pairing, and a precipitate, gas or weak electrolyte must form for it to complete.**
