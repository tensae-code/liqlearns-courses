# Full and net ionic equations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The shortest writing, keeping only interacting ions, is the _____ ionic equation.
**Answer: net**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The ions that cancel between full and net equations are the _____ ions.
**Answer: spectator**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Ion-exchange completes when a precipitate, a gas or a _____ electrolyte forms.
**Answer: weak**
