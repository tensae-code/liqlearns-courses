# Full and net ionic equations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An ion-exchange reaction runs to completion only when there forms…
a) A strong electrolyte  b) A precipitate, a gas or a weak electrolyte  c) A coloured solution  d) Heat only
**Answer: b) A precipitate, a gas or a weak electrolyte**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The net ionic equation keeps…
a) Every ion in the beaker  b) Only the ions that actually interact  c) Only the cations  d) Only the products
**Answer: b) Only the ions that actually interact**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Every neutralisation, netted, is…
a) H⁺ + OH⁻ ⇌ H₂O  b) Na⁺ + Cl⁻ → NaCl  c) H₂ + O₂ → H₂O  d) An unchanged solution
**Answer: a) H⁺ + OH⁻ ⇌ H₂O**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The most sensitive reagent for traces of Pb²⁺, per the lesson, precipitates…
a) PbCl₂  b) PbSO₄  c) PbS, already at 5.0×10⁻¹⁶ mol/L  d) Nothing
**Answer: c) PbS, already at 5.0×10⁻¹⁶ mol/L**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) White AgCl and black Ag₂S appear in the lesson as…
a) Gases  b) Precipitation tests  c) Indicators  d) Catalysts
**Answer: b) Precipitation tests**
