# Hands-on — Full and net ionic equations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Cancel the spectators
Write BaCl₂ + Na₂SO₄ and H₂SO₄ + NaOH in full ionic form on paper. Cross out every spectator with one line, then copy what survives as the net equation. Say for each survivor why it stayed: it precipitated, or it bound into a weak electrolyte.

## Solubility-table judgements
From a solubility table, judge five proposed mixings (from other lessons' salts and acids): for each, write COMPLETES or COEXISTS, the product that decides it, and the net ionic equation if it completes.
