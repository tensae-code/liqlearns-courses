## Nitrogen

Nitrogen is about 80% of air by volume and is manufactured by fractional distillation of liquefied air. It is relatively inert at ordinary temperatures; heated, it combines with Group IA/IIA metals to form nitrides and with oxygen to form the oxides NO and NO₂.
