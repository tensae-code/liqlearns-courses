# Molecular mass, formula mass, molar mass — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** formula mass = the same sum for a _____ unit of an ionic compound (Ca₃(PO₄)₂ = 310 is a review target);
**Answer: formula**

**2. ** either, expressed in grams, is the molar mass — the mass of one mole of the _____.
**Answer: substance**

**3. ** Percentage composition of a compound's _____ belongs to this same calculation family.
**Answer: elements**

**4. ** Molecular mass = sum of atomic masses in a molecule (worked _____ include SO₂ and caffeine);
**Answer: targets**
