## Molecular mass, formula mass, molar mass

Molecular mass = sum of atomic masses in a molecule (worked targets include SO₂ and caffeine); formula mass = the same sum for a formula unit of an ionic compound (Ca₃(PO₄)₂ = 310 is a review target); either, expressed in grams, is the molar mass — the mass of one mole of the substance.

Percentage composition of a compound's elements belongs to this same calculation family.5.1).
