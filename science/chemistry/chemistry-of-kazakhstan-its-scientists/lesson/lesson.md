# Chemistry of Kazakhstan — its scientists

## Do it first
Before reading, say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is: chemistry of kazakhstan — its scientists. Then read on — this is Kazakhstan's way of teaching it, as the Kazakhstan placements describe the books' own treatment.

## Sub lesson 1 — Front matter "Chemistry of Kazakhstan in Faces" — Kazakhstan's Grade 9 book (Grade 9, Usmanova et al.)

The book opens with the country's own chemists and what each founded: Usanovich's theory of acids and bases (1938), Birimzhanov's theory of salt formation, Kozlovsky's amalgam metallurgy, Sokolsky's catalysis school, Songina's electrochemistry of rare metals, Azerbayev's acetylene chemistry, Rafikov's polymerisation principles, Nadirov's work on West Kazakhstan oil fractions. (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, Usmanova et al., Front matter "Chemistry of Kazakhstan in Faces", printed p. 4).
