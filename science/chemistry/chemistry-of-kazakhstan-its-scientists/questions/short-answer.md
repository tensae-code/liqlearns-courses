# Chemistry of Kazakhstan — its scientists — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Kazakhstan's Grade 9 book (Grade 9, Usmanova et al.) teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: The book opens with the country's own chemists and what each founded: Usanovich's theory of acids and bases (1938), Birimzhanov's theory of salt formation, Kozlovsky's amalgam metallurgy, Sokolsky's catalysis school, Songina's electrochemistry of rare metals, Azerbayev's acetylene chemistry, Rafikov's polymerisation principles, Nadirov's work on West Kazakhstan oil fractions. (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, Usmanova et al., Front matter "Chemistry of Kazakhstan in Faces", printed p. 4).**
