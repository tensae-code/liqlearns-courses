Today: **Chemistry of Kazakhstan — its scientists** — Kazakhstan's way, from the books' own treatment. Kazakhstan's own chemists and what each founded, as the Usmanova Grade 9 book's opening gallery 'Chemistry of Kazakhstan in Faces' presents them: Usanovich's theory of acids and bases (1938), Birimzhanov's theory of salt formation, Kozlovsky's amalgam metallurgy, Sokolsky's catalysis school, Songina's electrochemistry of rare metals, Azerbayev's acetylene chemistry, Rafikov's polymerisation principles and Nadirov's work on West Kazakhstan oil fractions.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
