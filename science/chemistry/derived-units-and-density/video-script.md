Today: **Derived units and density**.

Derived units are built by substituting base units into defining equations (area m², speed m/s, force = newton, pressure = pascal, energy = joule, in a table).

the laboratory works in litres and millilitres (1 L = 1 dm³, 1 mL = 1 cm³).
