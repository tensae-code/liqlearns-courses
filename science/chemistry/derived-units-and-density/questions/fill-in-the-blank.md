# Derived units and density — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** the laboratory works in litres and _____ (1 L = 1 dm³, 1 mL = 1 cm³).
**Answer: millilitres**

**2. ** Density is mass per unit volume, d = m/V, a characteristic property: a worked example has Chaltu measure an _____ solvent (30.5 g, 35.1 mL, d ≈ 0.869 g/mL) and identify it as toluene from a density table;
**Answer: unknown**

**3. ** density also exposes _____ gold, since likely admixtures are less dense.
**Answer: adulterated**

**4. ** Derived units are built by substituting base units into _____ equations (area m², speed m/s, force = newton, pressure = pascal, energy = joule, in a table).
**Answer: defining**
