# Salts that turn acidic

## Do it first
Ammonium chloride — strong acid, weak base parentage — makes water acidic. Before reading, run the mirror argument of the last lesson: which ion acts this time?

[Picture 21724 — An ammonium ion handing a proton to water.]

In a salt of a strong acid and a weak base, the cation hydrolyses: NH₄⁺ hands a proton to water, forming H₃O⁺.

The solution is therefore acidic, and its pH is calculated from the hydrolysis as presented.

The anion of the strong acid spectates.
