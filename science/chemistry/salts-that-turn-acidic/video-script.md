# Video script — Salts that turn acidic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ammonium chloride — strong acid, weak base parentage — makes water acidic.

In a salt of a strong acid and a weak base, the cation hydrolyses: NH₄⁺ hands a proton to water, forming H₃O⁺.

The solution is therefore acidic, and its pH is calculated from the hydrolysis as presented.

The anion of the strong acid spectates.

Now you. Say it back: Which ion hydrolyses in NH₄Cl?
Answer: The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺.
