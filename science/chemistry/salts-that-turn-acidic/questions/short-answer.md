# Salts that turn acidic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which ion hydrolyses in NH₄Cl?
**Answer: The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺**

**2. ** What does NH₄⁺ give to water?
**Answer: A proton — forming H₃O⁺**

**3. ** The resulting solution is…
**Answer: Acidic**

**4. ** Which ion spectates?
**Answer: The strong acid's anion**
