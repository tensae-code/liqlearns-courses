# Salts that turn acidic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which ion hydrolyses in NH₄Cl?
a) An ester and water  b) Dilute  c) The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺  d) A silver mirror
**Answer: c) The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺**

**2. ** What does NH₄⁺ give to water?
a) The anode  b) A proton — forming H₃O⁺  c) Negative  d) The charge of one mole of electrons (≈96,500 C)
**Answer: b) A proton — forming H₃O⁺**

**3. ** The resulting solution is…
a) Acidic  b) Source, movement, changes, effects  c) Into water and food chains  d) Waste, at the source
**Answer: a) Acidic**

**4. ** Which ion spectates?
a) Molten or in solution — ions move  b) London dispersion  c) End-to-end (axial) overlap  d) The strong acid's anion
**Answer: d) The strong acid's anion**
