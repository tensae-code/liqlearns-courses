# Salts that turn acidic — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. Which ion hydrolyses in NH₄Cl? — c
2. What does NH₄⁺ give to water? — d
3. The resulting solution is… — a
4. Which ion spectates? — b

**Answer key:** 1–c: The ammonium cation, NH₄⁺ · 2–d: A proton — forming H₃O⁺ · 3–a: Acidic · 4–b: The strong acid's anion
