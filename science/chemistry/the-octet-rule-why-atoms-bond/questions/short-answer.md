# The octet rule — why atoms bond — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What do atoms tend to achieve in their outer shell?
**Answer: Eight electrons — an octet**

**2. ** Name the three bond types.
**Answer: Ionic, covalent and metallic**

**3. ** Why do atoms bond?
**Answer: To become more stable**

**4. ** Which gases are the stability model?
**Answer: The noble gases**
