# The octet rule — why atoms bond — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What do atoms tend to achieve in their outer shell?
a) Silica — the glass former  b) Molasses  c) Eight electrons — an octet  d) Weeds
**Answer: c) Eight electrons — an octet**

**2. ** Name the three bond types.
a) A dumbbell (two lobes)  b) Ionic, covalent and metallic  c) Only certain sharp lines  d) Electron diffraction
**Answer: b) Ionic, covalent and metallic**

**3. ** Why do atoms bond?
a) To become more stable  b) Change in amount/concentration per unit time  c) No — it is dynamic, both ways continue  d) Initial, Change, Equilibrium
**Answer: a) To become more stable**

**4. ** Which gases are the stability model?
a) Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly  b) PV = nRT  c) It accepts a proton from water  d) The noble gases
**Answer: d) The noble gases**
