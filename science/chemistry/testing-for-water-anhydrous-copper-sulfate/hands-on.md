# Hands-on — Testing for water — anhydrous copper sulfate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the test on paper
You are given four result cards: (1) powder stays white on a dry powder sample, (2) powder turns blue on a fresh food, (3) powder stays white with acetone, (4) powder turns blue with a clear liquid. For each card, write the conclusion the test allows — 'contains water' or 'no water shown' — and for card 4 write the one extra sentence the test does NOT allow (it does not prove the liquid is pure water).

## Design the fair version
Plan, on paper only, how a class would test three foods fairly with this test: same amount of powder, same waiting time, one known-water sample and one acetone sample as controls. Say what each control is for. Do not carry the practical out with laboratory chemicals outside a supervised laboratory.
