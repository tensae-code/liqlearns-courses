# Testing for water — anhydrous copper sulfate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Anhydrous copper sulfate is…
a) Blue crystals  b) A white powder  c) A clear liquid  d) A blue liquid
**Answer: b) A white powder**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The powder shows water is present when it…
a) Dissolves silently  b) Turns blue  c) Turns red  d) Fizzes
**Answer: b) Turns blue**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Acetone in the guide's exercises…
a) Turns the powder blue  b) Leaves the powder white — it contains no water  c) Turns the powder green  d) Is never tested
**Answer: b) Leaves the powder white — it contains no water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The test can prove…
a) Exactly how much water a food holds  b) Whether water is present  c) That a liquid is pure water  d) That a liquid is safe to drink
**Answer: b) Whether water is present**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A fresh food turns the powder blue because…
a) It is coloured  b) It contains water  c) It is sweet  d) It is solid
**Answer: b) It contains water**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the Djibouti guide this test appears in…
a) Ch. 2 exercises (printed p. 27)  b) The Physics part  c) Ch. 4 only  d) The sommaire
**Answer: a) Ch. 2 exercises (printed p. 27)**
