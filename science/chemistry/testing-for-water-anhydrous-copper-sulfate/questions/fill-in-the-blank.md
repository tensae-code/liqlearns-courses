# Testing for water — anhydrous copper sulfate — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Anhydrous copper sulfate is a _____ powder before the test.
**Answer: white**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) When water is present, the powder turns _____.
**Answer: blue**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The liquid used as the negative case in the exercises is _____.
**Answer: acetone**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) This kind of test, which shows whether a substance is present, is a _____ test.
**Answer: recognition**
