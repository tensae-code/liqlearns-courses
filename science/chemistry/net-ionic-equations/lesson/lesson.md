## Net ionic equations

Reactions between dissolved ionic compounds are written three ways: the full (molecular) equation, the complete ionic equation (all dissolved strong electrolytes split into ions), and the net ionic equation after cancelling spectator ions — the form that shows the actual change, typically a precipitate, water or a gas forming (aided by the solubility rules).7).
