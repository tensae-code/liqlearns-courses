# Transition metals and complex ions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The transition metals are…
a) The Group 1 and 2 metals  b) The d-block metals, whose ions keep a partly filled d subshell  c) The noble gases  d) The halogens
**Answer: b) The d-block metals, whose ions keep a partly filled d subshell**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which pair shows variable oxidation states?
a) Na⁺ and Na²⁺  b) Ca²⁺ and Ca³⁺  c) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺  d) Al³⁺ and Al⁴⁺
**Answer: c) Fe²⁺ and Fe³⁺**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A ligand bonds to a central metal by…
a) Giving the metal a proton  b) Donating a lone pair into a metal orbital — a coordinate bond  c) Sharing one electron each, always  d) Transferring an electron completely
**Answer: b) Donating a lone pair into a metal orbital — a coordinate bond**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺, the coordination number is…
a) 2  b) 3  c) 6  d) 4
**Answer: d) 4**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A complex is coloured because…
a) The metal is painted by its ligands  b) Electrons absorb light matching the d-orbital splitting gap, and the colour seen is the complement of the colour absorbed  c) It reflects all colours equally  d) Its ions vibrate
**Answer: b) Electrons absorb light matching the d-orbital splitting gap, and the colour seen is the complement of the colour absorbed**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A complex with unpaired d electrons is…
a) Diamagnetic  b) Colourless  c) Paramagnetic — drawn into a magnetic field  d) Always low-spin
**Answer: c) Paramagnetic — drawn into a magnetic field**
