# Transition metals and complex ions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The transition metals occupy the _____ block of the periodic table.
**Answer: d**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A molecule or ion that donates a lone pair to a central metal is called a _____.
**Answer: ligand**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The number of donor atoms bound to the centre is the _____ number.
**Answer: coordination**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The splitting of the d orbitals by ligands is called _____-field splitting.
**Answer: crystal**
