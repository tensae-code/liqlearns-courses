# Transition metals and complex ions — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (What makes a transition metal — the partly filled d subshell, Coordination compounds — ligands around a centre, Colour and magnetism — split d orbitals and unpaired electrons), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, reading new complex formulas part by part, and predicting colour and spin for new ligand sets.
