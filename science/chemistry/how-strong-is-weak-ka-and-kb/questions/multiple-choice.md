# How strong is 'weak'? Ka and Kb — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does Ka measure?
a) Only certain sharp lines  b) Electron diffraction  c) A dumbbell (two lobes)  d) A weak acid's ionisation strength
**Answer: d) A weak acid's ionisation strength**

**2. ** Smaller pKa means…
a) No — it is dynamic, both ways continue  b) Initial, Change, Equilibrium  c) A stronger weak acid  d) Change in amount/concentration per unit time
**Answer: c) A stronger weak acid**

**3. ** How do polyprotic acids ionise?
a) It accepts a proton from water  b) In steps, each with its own Ka  c) Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly  d) PV = nRT
**Answer: b) In steps, each with its own Ka**

**4. ** What does percentage ionisation tell?
a) The fraction that has split  b) Iron  c) It would form a corrosive mist  d) Soap or paper making
**Answer: a) The fraction that has split**
