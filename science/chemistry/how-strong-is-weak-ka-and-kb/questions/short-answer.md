# How strong is 'weak'? Ka and Kb — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does Ka measure?
**Answer: A weak acid's ionisation strength**

**2. ** Smaller pKa means…
**Answer: A stronger weak acid**

**3. ** How do polyprotic acids ionise?
**Answer: In steps, each with its own Ka**

**4. ** What does percentage ionisation tell?
**Answer: The fraction that has split**
