# Video script — How strong is 'weak'? Ka and Kb

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hydrochloric acid splits completely in water; ethanoic acid splits about one molecule in a hundred.

Strong acids and bases ionise completely; weak ones ionise only partly, reaching an equilibrium.

Ka measures a weak acid's ionisation; Kb a weak base's.

Percentage ionisation tells what fraction has split.

Now you. Say it back: What does Ka measure?
Answer: A weak acid's ionisation strength.
