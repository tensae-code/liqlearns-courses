# Galvanic cells — chemistry making electricity — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** In the Daniell cell, which metal is the anode?
**Answer: Zinc**

**2. ** What does the salt bridge do?
**Answer: Completes the circuit; keeps solutions neutral**

**3. ** Electrons flow in the wire from…
**Answer: Anode to cathode**

**4. ** Which electrode is negative in a galvanic cell?
**Answer: The anode**
