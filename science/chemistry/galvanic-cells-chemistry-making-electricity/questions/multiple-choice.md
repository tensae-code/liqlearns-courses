# Galvanic cells — chemistry making electricity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** In the Daniell cell, which metal is the anode?
a) Pleasant (fruity)  b) Zinc  c) It falls  d) Ethanoic (acetic) acid
**Answer: b) Zinc**

**2. ** What does the salt bridge do?
a) Completes the circuit; keeps solutions neutral  b) The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper  c) Zinc  d) Oxidised (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻)
**Answer: a) Completes the circuit; keeps solutions neutral**

**3. ** Electrons flow in the wire from…
a) Solid wastes and agrochemicals  b) Prevention of waste  c) They are rearranged, never created or destroyed  d) Anode to cathode
**Answer: d) Anode to cathode**

**4. ** Which electrode is negative in a galvanic cell?
a) Layers slide over the flowing electron sea  b) Molecular crystals  c) The anode  d) H on N, O or F attracted to a lone pair
**Answer: c) The anode**
