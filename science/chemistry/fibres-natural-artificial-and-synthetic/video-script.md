# Video script — Fibres — natural, artificial and synthetic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Fibres — natural, artificial and synthetic** — Bulgaria's three families. Nature's: cellulose from boll and stem, protein from sheep and silkworm. The artificials: cellulose rebuilt as viscose and acetate. The synthetics: PET, nylon, polyacrylonitrile. And the flame is the forensic witness — paper smell for cotton, horn for wool.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
