# Hands-on — Fibres — natural, artificial and synthetic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Identification table drill
Copy Bulgaria's identification table as a drill grid with the fibre names covered. For each row (cotton/flax, wool/silk, viscose, acetate, polyamide, polyester, PAN), fill burning, smell/residue and the three reagent behaviours from the lesson, then uncover and correct. Paper drill — the real burning tests belong to the school laboratory with a teacher present.

## Label audit
Audit five clothing labels at home: file each fibre named into NATURAL / ARTIFICIAL / SYNTHETIC, note blends, and predict each garment's burning verdict and moisture behaviour from its family before checking against the lesson.
