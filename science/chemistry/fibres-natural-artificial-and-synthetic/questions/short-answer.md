# Fibres — natural, artificial and synthetic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Distinguish artificial from synthetic fibres by their starting material.
**Answer: Artificials are rebuilt from a natural polymer — cellulose (viscose, acetate silk). Synthetics are polymerised from small chemical building blocks (PET, nylon-6,6, PAN).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the burning verdicts for cotton and wool in full.
**Answer: Cotton: burns fast, smells of burnt paper (cellulose). Wool: burns slowly, smells of horn, leaves a crushable black bead (protein).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name three destinations for recycled textile waste from the lesson.
**Answer: Insulation boards, filter media, coarse ceramics and activated carbon (any three).**
