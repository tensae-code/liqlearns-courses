# Fibres — natural, artificial and synthetic — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Silk and wool are _____ fibres — their polymer is protein.
**Answer: animal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The cheapest artificial fibre, shown at Paris in 1889, is _____.
**Answer: viscose**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Wool burning leaves a crushable black _____.
**Answer: bead**
