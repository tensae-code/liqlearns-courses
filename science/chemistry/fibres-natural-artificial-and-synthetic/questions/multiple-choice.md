# Fibres — natural, artificial and synthetic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Flax and hemp take their fibre from the plant's…
a) Seed  b) Stem  c) Root  d) Flower
**Answer: b) Stem**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Viscose is made by working cellulose with…
a) NaOH and CS₂  b) H₂SO₄ and HNO₃  c) ZnCl₂  d) Formaldehyde
**Answer: a) NaOH and CS₂**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Nylon-6,6 is a…
a) Natural protein  b) Polyamide synthetic  c) Artificial cellulose ester  d) Mineral fibre
**Answer: b) Polyamide synthetic**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Cotton burning smells of…
a) Horn  b) Burnt paper  c) Vinegar  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Burnt paper**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Wool's weakness shown in the laboratory is to…
a) Acids  b) Alkali — dissolving detergent solutions  c) Cold water  d) Light
**Answer: b) Alkali — dissolving detergent solutions**
