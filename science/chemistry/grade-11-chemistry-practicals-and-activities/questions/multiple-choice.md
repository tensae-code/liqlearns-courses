# Grade 11 chemistry practicals and activities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** How do units open?
a) With a group Start-up Activity  b) Exactly neutralise each other  c) So electrons lost equal electrons gained  d) Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q)
**Answer: a) With a group Start-up Activity**

**2. ** Name a practical rate measurement.
a) PET or nylon  b) Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil  c) Pesticide and fertiliser residues  d) Collecting gas over time
**Answer: d) Collecting gas over time**

**3. ** What does the chips activity compare?
a) Strong lattice attractions in all directions  b) Temporary flickering dipoles  c) Open versus sealed storage (rancidity)  d) A negative ion (anion)
**Answer: c) Open versus sealed storage (rancidity)**

**4. ** Where does practical chemistry belong?
a) –one  b) In a supervised laboratory, following the book  c) Fermentation of sugars  d) The Williamson synthesis
**Answer: b) In a supervised laboratory, following the book**
