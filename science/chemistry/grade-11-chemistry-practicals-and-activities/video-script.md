# Video script — Grade 11 chemistry practicals and activities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every unit of Grade 11 chemistry carries hands-on work.

Unit openings begin with a Start-up Activity in groups — burning wood and rusting iron open the atomic unit's discussion.

Rate is measured practically: collecting gas, timing mass loss and watching colour change (Unit 4's experiments).

The rancidity activity stores potato chips open and sealed and compares smells (Unit 6).

Now you. Say it back: How do units open?
Answer: With a group Start-up Activity.
