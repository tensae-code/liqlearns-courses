Today: **Democritus — atoms and the void**.

Leucippus and his pupil Democritus (c.

460–370 BC) proposed atomism, in five points: all matter is atoms too small to see and indivisible (atomos = uncuttable);

a void of empty space lies between atoms so they can move;

atoms are homogeneous, with no internal structure;

atoms differ in size, shape and weight, and change is their rearranging — the "solid sphere" model.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
