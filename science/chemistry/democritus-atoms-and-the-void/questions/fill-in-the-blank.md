# Democritus — atoms and the void — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** 460–370 BC) proposed atomism, in five points: all matter is atoms too small to see and _____ (atomos = uncuttable);
**Answer: indivisible**

**2. ** a void of empty space lies between atoms so they can move;
**Answer: Democritus**

**3. ** atoms are homogeneous, with no _____ structure;
**Answer: internal**

**4. ** atoms differ in size, shape and weight, and change is their _____ — the "solid sphere" model.
**Answer: rearranging**
