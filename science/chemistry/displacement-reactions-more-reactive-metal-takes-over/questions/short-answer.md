# Displacement reactions — the more reactive metal takes over — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe what is seen when a zinc electrode is dipped in copper(II) sulphate solution, and say why it happens.
**Answer:** The blue colour fades and copper deposits on/around the zinc while zinc passes into solution as Zn²⁺: zinc is the more reactive metal, so it takes copper's place in the compound and copper is displaced.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) What do fluorine's and iodine's displacement powers together show about the halogen family?
**Answer:** They fix the family's reactivity order end to end: fluorine at the top displaces every other halogen from its compounds, and iodine at the bottom displaces none — displacement power falls down the family.
