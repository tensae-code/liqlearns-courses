# Displacement reactions — the more reactive metal takes over — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When zinc is dipped in copper(II) sulphate solution, what happens?
a) Nothing at all  b) Zinc takes copper's place in the compound — zinc dissolves as Zn²⁺ and copper deposits  c) Copper takes zinc's place in the compound  d) The solution turns zinc-coloured
**Answer: b) Zinc takes copper's place in the compound — zinc dissolves as Zn²⁺ and copper deposits**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A displacement reaction is one in which…
a) A compound breaks into its elements on heating  b) A more reactive element pushes a less reactive one out of its compound  c) Two solutions mix and nothing changes  d) A gas is given off by an acid
**Answer: b) A more reactive element pushes a less reactive one out of its compound**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A metal's position in the electrochemical series is read as…
a) Its melting point  b) Its reactivity — the higher metal displaces the ones below it  c) Its colour  d) Its density
**Answer: b) Its reactivity — the higher metal displaces the ones below it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Which halogen can displace all the other halogens from solutions of their compounds?
a) Chlorine  b) Bromine  c) Iodine  d) Fluorine
**Answer: d) Fluorine**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Iodine can displace…
a) All the halogens  b) Only fluorine  c) None of the halogens  d) Only chlorine
**Answer: c) None of the halogens**
