# Chromatography — paper chromatography and Rf values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do the components of a mixture travel different distances on the paper?
**Answer: Each component splits its time between sticking to the stationary paper and dissolving in the moving solvent; the more it prefers the solvent, the further it is carried, so the components separate by travel.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is the starting line drawn in pencil, not ink?
**Answer: Ink would itself run up the paper and contaminate the chromatogram; pencil graphite does not dissolve in the solvent or travel.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does an Rf value identify a substance?
**Answer: For a given substance in a given solvent at a given temperature, Rf is constant. Running a known substance on the same paper and matching Rf values identifies the unknown spot.**
