# Chromatography — paper chromatography and Rf values — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The moving solvent in chromatography is the _____ phase.
**Answer: mobile**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The paper is the _____ phase.
**Answer: stationary**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Rf stands for _____ factor.
**Answer: retention**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) An Rf value is always a fraction below _____.
**Answer: 1 (one)**
