# Chromatography — paper chromatography and Rf values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In chromatography, the mixture is separated because…
a) Its components boil at different temperatures  b) Its components travel at different rates between a stationary phase and a moving mobile phase  c) Its components have different colours only  d) A magnet pulls one component out
**Answer: b) Its components travel at different rates between a stationary phase and a moving mobile phase**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In paper chromatography the stationary phase is…
a) The solvent  b) The paper  c) The ink  d) The tank
**Answer: b) The paper**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The starting spot must be placed…
a) Below the solvent level  b) Above the solvent level, on a pencil starting line  c) At the top of the paper  d) In the solvent itself
**Answer: b) Above the solvent level, on a pencil starting line**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The separated pattern on the finished paper is called a…
a) Filtrate  b) Chromatogram  c) Distillate  d) Residue
**Answer: b) Chromatogram**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Rf is calculated as…
a) Distance of the front ÷ distance of the spot  b) Distance moved by the spot ÷ distance moved by the solvent front  c) Mass ÷ volume  d) Spot distance × front distance
**Answer: b) Distance moved by the spot ÷ distance moved by the solvent front**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A spot travels 3.0 cm and the front 6.0 cm. The Rf is…
a) 2.0  b) 0.5  c) 3.0  d) 0.25
**Answer: b) 0.5**
