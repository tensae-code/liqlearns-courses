# Hands-on — Chromatography — paper chromatography and Rf values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Open an ink
Put a small spot of black ink on a pencil line near the foot of a strip of paper, stand the strip in a little water with the spot above the water level, and wait. Mark the solvent front when you stop, measure every spot's distance, and calculate each Rf.

## Two pens, one fingerprint
Run two different pens' inks side by side on one paper, with a third lane for a mixture of both. Compare the spot patterns and Rf values lane by lane, and write which pen — if either — matches each spot in the mixture lane.
