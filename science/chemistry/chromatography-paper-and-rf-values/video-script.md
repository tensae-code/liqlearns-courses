Today: **Chromatography — paper chromatography and Rf values** — South Sudan's way: separation by travel, identity by number.
One drop of ink is a mixture. Stand its paper in solvent and the solvent climbs, carrying each part of the ink at its own speed — the parts that love the solvent travel far, the parts that cling to the paper lag. The pattern is a chromatogram. Divide each spot's distance by the solvent front's distance and you have its Rf value — a constant, a fingerprint. Match the number, and you have named the substance.
