Today: **Bacterial gene transfer — transformation, transduction, conjugation and plasmids** — Italy's way, from the book's own treatment. Bacteria gain genetic variability by mutation at short generation times and by horizontal transfer between cells. The three transfer routes are transformation, generalised and specialised transduction, and conjugation. Plasmids carry new traits between bacteria — including antibiotic resistance.
