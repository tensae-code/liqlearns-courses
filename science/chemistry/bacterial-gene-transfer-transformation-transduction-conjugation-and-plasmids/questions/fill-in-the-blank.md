# Bacterial gene transfer — transformation, transduction, conjugation and plasmids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Bacteria gain genetic variability by mutation at short generation times and by _____ between cells.
**Answer: horizontal transfer**

**2.** The three transfer routes are transformation, generalised and specialised transduction, and _____.
**Answer: conjugation**

**3.** _____ carry new traits between bacteria — including antibiotic resistance.
**Answer: Plasmids**
