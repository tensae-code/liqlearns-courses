# Bacterial gene transfer — transformation, transduction, conjugation and plasmids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about horizontal transfer.
**Answer: Bacteria gain genetic variability by mutation at short generation times and by horizontal transfer between cells.**

**2.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about conjugation.
**Answer: The three transfer routes are transformation, generalised and specialised transduction, and conjugation.**

**3.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about Plasmids.
**Answer: Plasmids carry new traits between bacteria — including antibiotic resistance.**
