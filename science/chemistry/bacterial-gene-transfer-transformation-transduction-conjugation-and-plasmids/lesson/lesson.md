## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo F, Capitolo 14, §§9–11 — Italy's book (Grade 13)

Bacterial genetic variability mechanised: mutation at short generation times, and horizontal transfer by transformation, generalised and specialised transduction, and conjugation; plasmids' structure and role — new traits and antibiotic resistance passed between bacteria.

## The key ideas

- Bacteria gain genetic variability by mutation at short generation times and by horizontal transfer between cells.
- The three transfer routes are transformation, generalised and specialised transduction, and conjugation.
- Plasmids carry new traits between bacteria — including antibiotic resistance.
