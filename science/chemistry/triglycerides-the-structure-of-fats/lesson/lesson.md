# Triglycerides — the structure of fats

## Do it first
One glycerol, three fatty acids, three ester links. Before reading, say how many water molecules the building of one fat molecule releases.

[Picture 21706 — A triglyceride with three tails.]

A triglyceride is glycerol esterified with three fatty acids — three ester groups in one molecule.

The three tails may be saturated (straight) or unsaturated (kinked), mixed in one molecule.

Building it releases three water molecules, one per ester link — esterification three times over.
