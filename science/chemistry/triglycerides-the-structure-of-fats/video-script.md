# Video script — Triglycerides — the structure of fats

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One glycerol, three fatty acids, three ester links.

A triglyceride is glycerol esterified with three fatty acids — three ester groups in one molecule.

The three tails may be saturated (straight) or unsaturated (kinked), mixed in one molecule.

Building it releases three water molecules, one per ester link — esterification three times over.

Now you. Say it back: How many fatty acids in a triglyceride?
Answer: Three.
