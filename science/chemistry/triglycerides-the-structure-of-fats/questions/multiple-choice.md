# Triglycerides — the structure of fats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** How many fatty acids in a triglyceride?
a) Yes, to reactants  b) Three  c) At constant T, P × V is constant  d) Glass
**Answer: b) Three**

**2. ** What is the backbone molecule?
a) Glycerol  b) Making soap by alkaline hydrolysis of fats  c) Three — one per ester link  d) The fat's ester links (by water)
**Answer: a) Glycerol**

**3. ** How many waters are released building it?
a) Electricity and water  b) By-products  c) Vanadium(V) oxide  d) Three — one per ester link
**Answer: d) Three — one per ester link**

**4. ** Can its tails be mixed?
a) Fields do not bend them — they carry no charge  b) E = hν  c) Yes — saturated and unsaturated  d) The atoms themselves change
**Answer: c) Yes — saturated and unsaturated**
