# Triglycerides — the structure of fats — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** How many fatty acids in a triglyceride?
**Answer: Three**

**2. ** What is the backbone molecule?
**Answer: Glycerol**

**3. ** How many waters are released building it?
**Answer: Three — one per ester link**

**4. ** Can its tails be mixed?
**Answer: Yes — saturated and unsaturated**
