Today: **Classifying substances — the inorganic and organic family trees** — Bulgaria's map of everything. Two trees. The inorganic tree: simple substances — metals and nonmetals — and compounds: hydrogen compounds, oxides, hydroxides, acids and salts. The organic tree: hydrocarbons; the oxygen families — alcohols and phenols, aldehydes and ketones, esters, carboxylic acids; and the nitrogen families — amines and amino acids. Name the tree, then the branch, then the family.
