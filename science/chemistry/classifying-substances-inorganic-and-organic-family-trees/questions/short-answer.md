# Classifying substances — the inorganic and organic family trees — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the two great divisions of inorganic substances.
**Answer: Simple substances (metals and nonmetals) and compounds (hydrogen compounds, oxides, hydroxides, acids and salts).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do alcohols and phenols share a branch?
**Answer: Both carry the hydroxyl group, –OH — the oxygen-containing branch is sorted by the working group, and the hydroxyl group is theirs.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A substance contains carbon, hydrogen and nitrogen and is an amine. File it fully.
**Answer: Organic tree, nitrogen-containing branch — the amines.**
