# Classifying substances — the inorganic and organic family trees — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Compounds of the inorganic tree include oxides, hydroxides, acids and _____.
**Answer: salts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes and arenes are the _____.
**Answer: hydrocarbons**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Amines belong to the _____-containing branch of the organic tree.
**Answer: nitrogen**
