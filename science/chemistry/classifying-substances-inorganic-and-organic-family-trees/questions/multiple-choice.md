# Classifying substances — the inorganic and organic family trees — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Table salt, NaCl, belongs on…
a) The organic tree, as a hydrocarbon  b) The inorganic tree, among the salts  c) The organic tree, among the esters  d) Neither tree
**Answer: b) The inorganic tree, among the salts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The five compound families of the inorganic tree are hydrogen compounds, oxides, hydroxides, acids and…
a) Esters  b) Arenes  c) Salts  d) Amines
**Answer: c) Salts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes and arenes are together the…
a) Nitrogen-containing compounds  b) Hydrocarbons  c) Carbonyl compounds  d) Hydroxides
**Answer: b) Hydrocarbons**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Amines and amino acids sit on the organic tree's…
a) Hydrocarbon branch  b) Oxygen-containing branch  c) Nitrogen-containing branch  d) Salt branch
**Answer: c) Nitrogen-containing branch**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Aldehydes and ketones are grouped as…
a) Hydroxyl compounds  b) Carbonyl compounds  c) Hydrocarbons  d) Esters
**Answer: b) Carbonyl compounds**
