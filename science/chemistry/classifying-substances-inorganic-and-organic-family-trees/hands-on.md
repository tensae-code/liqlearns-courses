# Hands-on — Classifying substances — the inorganic and organic family trees

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Draw the two trees
On one large sheet, draw Bulgaria's two trees from memory of the lesson: trunk, first branches, families. Then file twelve substance cards (NaCl, CH₄, H₂SO₄, C₂H₅OH, CaO, NH₂CH₂COOH, C₆H₆, NaOH, CO₂, CH₃CHO, KCl, C₃H₆) onto the branches, saying the full path out loud for each.

## Kitchen filing
List ten substances from a kitchen or bathroom. For each, decide its tree and branch and write the reason in one phrase (for example: vinegar — organic tree, carboxylic acids, because it carries –COOH).
