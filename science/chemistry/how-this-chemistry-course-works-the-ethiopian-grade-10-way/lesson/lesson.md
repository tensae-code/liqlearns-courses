## How this Chemistry course works

The Grade 10 course runs: Unit 1 Chemical Reactions and Stoichiometry, Unit 2 Solutions, Unit 3 Important Inorganic Compounds, Unit 4 Energy Changes and Electrochemistry, Unit 5 Metals and Nonmetals, Unit 6 Hydrocarbons and their Natural Sources. The Grade 9 rhythm continues — Unit Outcomes, a Start-up Activity from daily life (fruit ripening and injera fermenting from teff flour open Unit 1; a farmer's fertiliser dose, a nurse's medicine dose and a chef's ingredient amounts open its quantity strand), group Activities, worked Examples, Experiments, Unit Summary in bold-term prose, and Review Exercises in tiers (true/false, missing-word, multiple choice, problem-solving/workout).

The centre of gravity shifts to quantitative chemistry: the mole, five concentration units, stoichiometric problem families and percent yield are drilled as named problem types, and the back half is applied — acids/bases/salts tied to fertilisers and ion tests, metals tied to named industrial processes (Hall, blast furnace, Contact, Frasch), hydrocarbons tied to petroleum refining and octane numbers. Ethiopian anchors persist: injera/teff, local fuels ("local beer" appears on the cover collage), Ethiopian fertiliser and industry contexts.
