## Millikan's oil-drop experiment

The student book names Millikan's experiment and its result only; the guide narrates it for teaching in three steps — (i) balancing an oil drop between gravity and the electric field, (ii) calculating the drop's velocity, (iii) calculating the charge — from which the electron's charge (and then its mass) was obtained.
