# Balancing redox equations — true or false (Grade 12 addition)

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** What are the two halves of a redox split — Oxidation and reduction half-reactions
**Answer: True**

**2.** In acid, what balances oxygen — H⁺
**Answer: False**

**3.** In acid, what balances hydrogen — H⁺
**Answer: True**

**4.** Why multiply the half-reactions — Oxidation and reduction half-reactions
**Answer: False**
