# Balancing redox equations — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: say what this lesson teaches — balancing redox equations — explain its main example, and answer its questions from the lesson text.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to work, new examples from the learner's own surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.


## Grade 12 addition depth
The Grade 12 part passes when the learner states its key ideas in their own words: Split the reaction into two half-reactions: one for oxidation (electrons on the right), one for reduction (electrons on the left); In acidic medium: balance atoms other than O and H, balance O with H₂O, H with H⁺, then charge with electrons; Multiply the half-reactions so electrons lost equal electrons gained, add them, and cancel what appears on both sides.
