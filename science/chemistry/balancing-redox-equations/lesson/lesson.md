## Balancing redox equations

A stepwise method: assign oxidation numbers, draw the change bridges, make total increase equal total decrease (the electrons lost equal those gained), insert those coefficients, then finish balancing the remaining atoms by inspection.4.4).

## Grade 12 addition — Balancing redox equations (Chemistry Band B)

This lesson also carries the Grade 12 teaching of this topic, added by the Chemistry Band B build (Ethiopia Wave 4). Work it after the part above.

[Picture 21731 — Two half-equations being combined.]

Split the reaction into two half-reactions: one for oxidation (electrons on the right), one for reduction (electrons on the left).

In acidic medium: balance atoms other than O and H, balance O with H₂O, H with H⁺, then charge with electrons. In basic medium, the same steps use OH⁻/H₂O handling as presented.

Multiply the half-reactions so electrons lost equal electrons gained, add them, and cancel what appears on both sides. Worked examples run the method step by step.
