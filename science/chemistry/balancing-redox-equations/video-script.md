## Grade 12 addition
Split the reaction into two half-reactions: one for oxidation (electrons on the right), one for reduction (electrons on the left).

In acidic medium: balance atoms other than O and H, balance O with H₂O, H with H⁺, then charge with electrons.

Multiply the half-reactions so electrons lost equal electrons gained, add them, and cancel what appears on both sides.
