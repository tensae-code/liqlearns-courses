# The Arab and Muslim founders of chemistry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Yemen's book calls Jabir ibn Hayyan the true founder of chemistry chiefly because of…
a) His experimental method and his classification of substances  b) His royal title  c) His invention of printing  d) His work on astronomy alone
**Answer: a) His experimental method and his classification of substances**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In Jabir's classification, sulfur and mercury belong among the…
a) Stones  b) Volatile "spirits"  c) Boraxes  d) Vitriols
**Answer: b) Volatile "spirits"**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Jabir's first statement was of…
a) Atomic weights  b) The periodic law  c) Fixed proportions — substances combine in definite proportions  d) Radioactivity
**Answer: c) Fixed proportions — substances combine in definite proportions**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Sulfuric acid's old name in Jabir's world was…
a) Gold water  b) Caustic soda  c) Aqua regia  d) Oil of vitriol
**Answer: d) Oil of vitriol**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The scholar of the Canon in the honour roll is…
a) Ibn Sina  b) Al-Razi  c) Al-Jildaki  d) Al-Zahrawi
**Answer: a) Ibn Sina**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The book traces the word chemistry to an Arabic verb meaning…
a) To mix  b) To hide / conceal  c) To burn  d) To weigh
**Answer: b) To hide / conceal**
