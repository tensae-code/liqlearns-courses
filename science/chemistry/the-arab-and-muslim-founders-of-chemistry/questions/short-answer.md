# The Arab and Muslim founders of chemistry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name Jabir's six substance classes.
**Answer: Metals; volatile spirits (such as sulfur and mercury); stones; vitriols; boraxes; and salts.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What was wrongly credited to English scholars ten centuries later?
**Answer: The first statement of fixed proportions — Jabir had stated it long before, as the book notes quoting Berthelot.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give two scholars from the roll after Jabir and one thing each is remembered for.
**Answer: Any two, e.g. al-Razi (~220 works), Ibn Sina (the Canon), al-Jildaki (fixed proportions), al-Hamdani (a book on gold and silver), al-Zahrawi (a rule of chemical combination).**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does a classification count as founding work for a science?
**Answer: It turns a heap of separate materials into a system that can be compared, searched and tested — later workers can find a substance's family and predict its behaviour from its class.**
