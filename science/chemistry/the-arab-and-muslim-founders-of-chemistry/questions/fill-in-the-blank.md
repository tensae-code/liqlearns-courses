# The Arab and Muslim founders of chemistry — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Jabir ibn Hayyan is called the true _____ of chemistry.
**Answer: founder**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sulfur and mercury are classed by Jabir among the volatile _____.
**Answer: spirits**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Jabir stated that substances combine in fixed _____.
**Answer: proportions**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Sulfuric acid was called the oil of _____.
**Answer: vitriol**
