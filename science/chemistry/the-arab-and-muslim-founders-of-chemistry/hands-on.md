# Hands-on — The Arab and Muslim founders of chemistry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Founder cards (paper only)
Make a card for each scholar in the lesson — Jabir, al-Razi, Ibn Sina, al-Jildaki, al-Hamdani, al-Zahrawi. On each card write the one contribution the lesson names, and sort the cards so Jabir's method card stands first. Say aloud why the book puts him first.

## Classify like Jabir (paper only)
List ten substances from your kitchen and workshop (salt, sulfur if you have seen it, iron, vinegar, ash, oil, chalk, copper wire, sand, soap). Sort them under Jabir's six classes as best they fit, and mark the ones his classes cannot hold — the lesson's point is what a classification can and cannot do.
