# Iron and its three oxides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Iron's two oxidation states of importance are…
a) +2 and +3  b) +1 and +2  c) +3 and +6  d) 0 and +2 only
**Answer: a) +2 and +3**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Dilute acid on iron gives…
a) Fe(II) salts  b) Fe(III) salts only  c) No reaction  d) Iron oxide
**Answer: a) Fe(II) salts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Concentrated nitric acid passivates iron, meaning…
a) A protective film stops the reaction  b) The iron dissolves violently  c) The iron turns to Fe₃O₄  d) The acid is neutralised
**Answer: a) A protective film stops the reaction**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Fe₃O₄ is proven a mixed oxide because hot concentrated acid gives…
a) Both Fe(II) and Fe(III) salts  b) Only Fe(II) salts  c) Only Fe(III) salts  d) No salts at all
**Answer: a) Both Fe(II) and Fe(III) salts**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Which is iron(III) oxide?
a) Fe₂O₃  b) FeO  c) Fe₃O₄  d) Fe(OH)₃
**Answer: a) Fe₂O₃**
