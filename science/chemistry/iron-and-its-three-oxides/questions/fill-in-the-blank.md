# Iron and its three oxides — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** Concentrated nitric acid ______ iron, sealing it under a protective film.
**Answer:** passivates

**2.** Fe₃O₄ is a ______ oxide, holding +2 and +3 iron together.
**Answer:** mixed

**3.** Dilute acids dissolve iron to ______ salts.
**Answer:** Fe(II) / iron(II)
