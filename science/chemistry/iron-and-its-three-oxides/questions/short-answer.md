# Iron and its three oxides — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the three oxides with the iron state in each.
**Answer:** FeO — iron(II); Fe₂O₃ — iron(III); Fe₃O₄ — mixed, holding iron(II) and iron(III) together (FeO·Fe₂O₃ in effect).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A student says Fe₃O₄ must contain iron in a +8/3 state. Answer with Egypt's proof.
**Answer:** The fraction is an average, not a state: hot concentrated acid draws both Fe(II) and Fe(III) salts out of Fe₃O₄, proving the powder holds the two real states side by side — a mixed oxide.
