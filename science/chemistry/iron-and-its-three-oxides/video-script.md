Today: **Iron and its three oxides** — Egypt's way: Iron's own chemistry: +2/+3 states only of importance, reactions with air, steam, chlorine, sulphur and acids (dilute acids give Fe(II) salts; concentrated nitric acid passivates), then FeO, Fe₂O₃ and Fe₃O₄ one by one — preparation (oxalate decomposition, hydroxide ignition, partial reduction), and Fe₃O₄ proven a mixed oxide because hot concentrated acid gives both Fe(II) and Fe(III) salts.
