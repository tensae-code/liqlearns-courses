# Hands-on — Iron and its three oxides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Oxide cards
Make three cards — FeO, Fe₂O₃, Fe₃O₄. On each back write the iron state(s) and one preparation route or test from the lesson. Shuffle and drill: formula to states, states to formula.

## The proof retold
Write the Fe₃O₄ proof as a three-line argument: claim (mixed oxide), test (hot concentrated acid), evidence (both Fe(II) and Fe(III) salts appear). Say it aloud as one breath.
