# Testing purity by melting and boiling point — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A pure solid melts…
a) At any temperature it likes  b) At one sharp, fixed temperature  c) Only in a vacuum  d) Over a wide range of temperatures
**Answer: b) At one sharp, fixed temperature**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A pure liquid boils…
a) At one fixed temperature  b) At a different temperature every time  c) Only when stirred  d) Below room temperature
**Answer: a) At one fixed temperature**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An impurity in a solid…
a) Raises its melting point and sharpens it  b) Lowers its melting point and spreads the melting over a range  c) Has no effect on melting  d) Makes it evaporate
**Answer: b) Lowers its melting point and spreads the melting over a range**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An impurity in a liquid…
a) Lowers its boiling point  b) Raises its boiling point  c) Makes it freeze  d) Turns it into a gas at once
**Answer: b) Raises its boiling point**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The chromatography check that agrees with a purity verdict is…
a) Many spots  b) A single spot  c) No spot at all  d) A spot that never moves
**Answer: b) A single spot**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In Libya's practical book, the melting/boiling purity test is run on…
a) Gold and silver  b) Naphthalene, and water or ethanol  c) Salt and sand only  d) Crude oil
**Answer: b) Naphthalene, and water or ethanol**
