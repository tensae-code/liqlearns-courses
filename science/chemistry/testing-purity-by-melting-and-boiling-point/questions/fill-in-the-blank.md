# Testing purity by melting and boiling point — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A pure substance melts at a sharp, _____ temperature.
**Answer: fixed**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Impurities _____ the melting point of a solid.
**Answer: lower**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An impurity makes a liquid's boiling point _____.
**Answer: rise (go up)**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The practical test compares the measured point with the _____ value of the pure substance.
**Answer: book (known pure)**
