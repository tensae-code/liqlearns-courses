# Hands-on — Testing purity by melting and boiling point

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the thermometer cards
Write five samples on cards: (a) melts sharply at 80 °C, book value 80 °C; (b) melts from 74 °C to 79 °C, book value 80 °C; (c) boils at exactly 100 °C; (d) boils at 104 °C, book value 100 °C; (e) one spot only on its chromatogram AND a sharp melting point. Give each card its verdict — pure or impure — with the reason, using sub lessons 1 and 2.

## The slow-heating rule
Write out why the sample must be heated slowly in this test: what would fast heating hide, and what would it make you wrongly conclude? Check your answer against sub lesson 3.
