Today: **Testing purity by melting and boiling point** — Libya's way: the sharp point is the verdict.
A pure solid melts at one sharp temperature. A pure liquid boils at one fixed temperature. Add an impurity and the sharpness goes: the melting point drops and spreads over a range, and the boiling point climbs. So the test is simple — heat the sample slowly, watch the thermometer, and compare. Naphthalene, water and ethanol are the samples Libya's practical book runs it on. Sharp point at the book value: pure. Smeared point, or the wrong value: a mixture.
