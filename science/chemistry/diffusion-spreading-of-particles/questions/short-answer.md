# Diffusion — the spreading of particles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does diffusion stop looking like movement once the spread is even, even though the particles still move?
**Answer: The particles keep wandering, but crowded and scarce places no longer exist — as many wander one way as the other, so the spread stays even.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What makes the ink-in-water proof honest evidence for particle motion?
**Answer: Nothing stirs or shakes the glass, yet the colour spreads through the whole liquid — the only mover left is the particles' own motion.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does a smell cross a room so much faster than ink crosses a glass of water?
**Answer: Gas particles are far apart, bump less and move faster and farther; liquid particles are close, bump constantly and wander slowly.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) State the relation between molecular mass and the rate of diffusion.
**Answer: At the same temperature, particles of smaller molecular mass diffuse faster and particles of larger molecular mass diffuse more slowly — the qualitative Graham-type relation Mongolia's Grade 9 section shows.**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) How can diffusion act as a kind of balance for particles too small to weigh?
**Answer: Because the spreading rate is set by molecular mass, comparing how fast two gases diffuse compares their particles' masses without ever putting a particle on a balance.**
