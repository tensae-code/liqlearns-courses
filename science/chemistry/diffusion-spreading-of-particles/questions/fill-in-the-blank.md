# Diffusion — the spreading of particles — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The spreading of particles from crowded to scarce, with no stirring, is called _____.
**Answer: diffusion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A smell crossing a room is the proof of diffusion in a _____.
**Answer: gas**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The rate of diffusion depends on the particles' molecular _____.
**Answer: mass**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) At the same temperature, _____ particles diffuse faster than heavier ones.
**Answer: lighter**
