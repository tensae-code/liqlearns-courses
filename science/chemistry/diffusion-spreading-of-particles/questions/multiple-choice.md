# Diffusion — the spreading of particles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Diffusion is…
a) Stirring a mixture until it is even  b) Particles spreading from where they are crowded to where they are scarce, by their own motion, until evenly spread  c) A chemical reaction between two substances  d) Particles settling to the bottom
**Answer: b) Particles spreading from where they are crowded to where they are scarce, by their own motion, until evenly spread**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Diffusion needs no stirring because…
a) Water does the stirring  b) It is driven by the particles' own constant motion  c) Gravity pulls the particles evenly  d) Air currents always help
**Answer: b) It is driven by the particles' own constant motion**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The proof of diffusion in a liquid is…
a) Ink dropped in still water colours the whole glass with no stirring  b) Ink sinks and stays at the bottom  c) Water evaporates  d) The glass warms up
**Answer: a) Ink dropped in still water colours the whole glass with no stirring**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Compared with a gas, diffusion in a liquid is…
a) Impossible  b) Faster  c) Slower, because the particles are closer and wander more slowly  d) The same speed
**Answer: c) Slower, because the particles are closer and wander more slowly**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) At the same temperature, the particles that diffuse fastest are…
a) The heaviest  b) The lightest — the rate depends on molecular mass  c) The ones with the strongest smell  d) The largest in size only
**Answer: b) The lightest — the rate depends on molecular mass**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Two gases at the same temperature are released together. The one detected first at the far end is the one…
a) With the heavier particles  b) With the lighter particles  c) With more particles  d) That was released louder
**Answer: b) With the lighter particles**
