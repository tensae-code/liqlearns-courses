# Hands-on — Diffusion — the spreading of particles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The unstirred glass (observation only — water and food colouring)
Fill a tall glass with still water and let it settle completely. Let one drop of food colouring slide down the inside wall to the bottom — do not stir, do not move the glass. Sketch the colour's spread every five minutes for half an hour. Say what moved, what did no moving at all (you, the spoon), and what that proves about the particles.

## Warm against cold (observation only — water and food colouring)
Two identical glasses, one of cold water, one of warm (teacher handles the warm water). One drop of colouring in each, at the same moment, neither stirred. Time how long each takes to colour evenly, and explain the difference from the particles' speed — then say what sub lesson 3 would predict if the two glasses held two different gases instead.

## The smell map (observation only)
With a teacher, a scented item is opened at one marked point in the classroom and left there. Classmates in different seats raise a hand the moment they first detect it, and the times are noted with the seats. Plot the order on a sketch of the room: say what the pattern shows about diffusion in a gas, and name one thing in the room (a draught, a fan) that could disturb the honest reading.
