Today: **Electrolytic cells — forcing a reaction with electricity**.

An electrolytic cell converts electrical energy into chemical energy: a DC source forces a non-spontaneous redox reaction.

The galvanic/electrolytic contrast is tabulated: same anode-oxidation rule, opposite electrode signs and opposite energy direction.

Electrolysis is defined as using electricity to decompose an electrolyte / run a redox reaction to produce useful substances.
