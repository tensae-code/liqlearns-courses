## Electrolytic cells

An electrolytic cell converts electrical energy into chemical energy: a DC source forces a non-spontaneous redox reaction. Its polarity is set by the external source — the anode is the electrode joined to the positive terminal (anions arrive, oxidation happens, electrons leave the cell), the cathode joins the negative terminal (cations arrive, reduction happens, electrons enter). The galvanic/electrolytic contrast is tabulated: same anode-oxidation rule, opposite electrode signs and opposite energy direction.

Electrolysis is defined as using electricity to decompose an electrolyte / run a redox reaction to produce useful substances.4).
