# Lesson plan — Electrolytic cells — forcing a reaction with electricity

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** Electrolytic cells — forcing a reaction with electricity
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — Unit 4 — Energy Changes and Electrochemistry (Grade 10 band, CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** An electrolytic cell converts electrical energy into chemical energy: a DC source forces a non-spontaneous redox reaction.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier Chemistry lessons of the Grade 10 band — matter, measurement and the atom come before bonding, reactions and carbon; a learner can still land here directly and follow it.
