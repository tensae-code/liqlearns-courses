# Electrolytic cells — forcing a reaction with electricity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** The galvanic/electrolytic contrast is tabulated: same anode-_____ rule, opposite electrode signs and opposite energy direction.
**Answer: oxidation**

**2. ** Electrolysis is defined as using electricity to decompose an _____ / run a redox reaction to produce useful substances.
**Answer: electrolyte**

**3. ** An electrolytic cell converts electrical energy into _____ energy: a DC source forces a non-spontaneous redox reaction.
**Answer: chemical**

**4. ** The galvanic/electrolytic contrast is tabulated: same anode-_____ rule, opposite electrode signs and opposite energy direction.
**Answer: oxidation**
