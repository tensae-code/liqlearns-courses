# Pure substances — simple and compound — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define the two kinds of pure substance.
**Answer: Simple: built from atoms of one kind. Compound: built from several kinds of atom joined.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is O₂ simple although its molecule holds two atoms?
**Answer: Both atoms are of one kind — the sorting counts kinds of atom, not numbers of atoms.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is a molecule, and what does its formula show?
**Answer: A molecule is an association of atoms; its formula shows the nature and the number of its atoms.**
