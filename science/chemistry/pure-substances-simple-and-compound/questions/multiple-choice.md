# Pure substances — simple and compound — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A pure simple substance is built from…
a) Several kinds of atom  b) Atoms of one kind  c) Ions only  d) Mixtures
**Answer: b) Atoms of one kind**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a pure compound substance?
a) O₂  b) H₂  c) H₂O  d) N₂
**Answer: c) H₂O**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The sorting counts…
a) Atoms  b) Kinds of atom  c) Masses  d) Volumes
**Answer: b) Kinds of atom**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A molecule is…
a) A single atom always  b) An association of atoms  c) A mixture  d) A symbol
**Answer: b) An association of atoms**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The formula of a molecule shows the nature and _____ of its atoms.
a) Colour  b) Number  c) Price  d) Speed
**Answer: b) Number**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the 3AS book's sorting, glucose is classed with the…
a) Simple molecules  b) Complex (compound) molecules  c) Elements  d) Mixtures
**Answer: b) Complex (compound) molecules**
