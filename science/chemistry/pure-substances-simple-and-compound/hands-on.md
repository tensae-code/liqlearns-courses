# Hands-on — Pure substances — simple and compound

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the bench (paper only)
Write the 2AS list — H₂, O₂, H₂O, CO₂, C₄H₁₀, NaCl — as cards. Sort them into two piles by the lesson's rule, then write the rule in one sentence under each pile, naming what was counted.

## Read and sort (paper only)
Five formulas are given (N₂, Fe₂O₃, CH₄, Cl₂, glucose's formula as printed in the 3AS chapter). For each: name the kinds of atom it shows, count them, and sort the substance simple or compound from the formula alone.
