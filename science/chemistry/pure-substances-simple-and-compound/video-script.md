Today: **Pure substances — simple and compound** — Mauritania's way: pure substances sorted by their building — a corps pur simple built from atoms of one kind (H₂, O₂), a corps pur composé from several kinds (H₂O, CO₂, C₄H₁₀, NaCl) — and the chemical formula as the writing that shows the nature and number of a molecule's atoms. That is the whole lesson in one breath — the sub lessons now walk it slowly, book in hand.
