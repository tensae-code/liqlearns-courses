# Saturated and supersaturated solutions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** unsaturated holds less (and dilute vs concentrated are _____ comparisons, not saturation claims);
**Answer: relative**

**2. ** supersaturated holds more than equilibrium allows, prepared by cooling a saturated _____ carefully, and is unstable — a seed crystal triggers crystallisation.
**Answer: solution**

**3. ** Solubility is defined as the _____ amount per given amount of solvent.
**Answer: maximum**

**4. ** A saturated solution holds the maximum solute at that _____, in dynamic equilibrium with undissolved solute;
**Answer: temperature**
