Today: **Saturated and supersaturated solutions**.

A saturated solution holds the maximum solute at that temperature, in dynamic equilibrium with undissolved solute;

unsaturated holds less (and dilute vs concentrated are relative comparisons, not saturation claims);

supersaturated holds more than equilibrium allows, prepared by cooling a saturated solution carefully, and is unstable — a seed crystal triggers crystallisation.

Solubility is defined as the maximum amount per given amount of solvent.
