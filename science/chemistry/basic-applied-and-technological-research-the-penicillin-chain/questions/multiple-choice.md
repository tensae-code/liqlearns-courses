# Basic, applied and technological research — the penicillin chain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The three kinds of research in Yemen's book are…
a) Basic, applied and technological  b) Pure, mixed and industrial  c) Old, new and future  d) School, university and factory
**Answer: a) Basic, applied and technological**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In the penicillin chain, basic research did the…
a) Mass production  b) Discovery of penicillin and its structure  c) Factory design  d) Advertising
**Answer: b) Discovery of penicillin and its structure**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Applied research turned penicillin into…
a) A mould garden  b) A new element  c) An antibiotic industry  d) A textbook
**Answer: c) An antibiotic industry**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is the book's named negative application?
a) Optical fibres  b) Fertilisers  c) Local drug manufacture  d) The atomic bomb
**Answer: d) The atomic bomb**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Optical fibres are named in the lesson as a…
a) Positive application of research  b) Negative application  c) Kind of research  d) Feedback rule
**Answer: a) Positive application of research**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The lesson's closing picture of science and society is…
a) A one-way line  b) A loop — society feeds back on science  c) A ladder with science on top  d) Two separate worlds
**Answer: b) A loop — society feeds back on science**
