# Basic, applied and technological research — the penicillin chain — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define each of the three kinds of research in one clause.
**Answer: Basic: finding out how the world is. Applied: aiming a finding at a use. Technological: turning the use into repeatable production at scale.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Walk the penicillin chain, naming which kind of research does each step.
**Answer: Basic — penicillin discovered and its structure worked out. Applied — an antibiotic industry built on it. Technological — mass production of the medicine.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Give two positive applications the book names and say which part of the loop they sit in.
**Answer: Any two of local drug manufacture, pesticides, fertilisers, optical fibres — they are applications: research's products reaching society, which then feeds back on the science.**
