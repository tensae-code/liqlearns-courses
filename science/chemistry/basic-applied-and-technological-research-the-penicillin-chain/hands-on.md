# Hands-on — Basic, applied and technological research — the penicillin chain

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the work (paper only)
Write six cards: 'measure a new substance's structure', 'design a factory line for a known drug', 'test whether a plant extract kills germs, to know', 'turn a lab dye into cheap cloth dye', 'build the machines that spin optical fibre', 'ask whether a pesticide should be allowed'. Sort the cards under basic / applied / technological / society's feedback, and defend each sort aloud.

## Your own chain (paper only)
Pick one thing in your home that research produced (a medicine, a fertiliser, a phone cable). Write its chain in three steps, one per kind of research, inventing nothing you cannot support: what had to be discovered, what use it was aimed at, and how it came to be made in quantity.
