# Hands-on — Molecular and structural formulas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the set
Draw structural formulas for C₂H₄ and C₂H₂, counting the bonds at each carbon aloud until both carbons reach four. Then draw CH₃OH and check every atom's valency.

## Find the second C₂H₄Cl₂
Draw C₂H₄Cl₂ with both chlorines on one carbon; then draw it with one chlorine on each carbon. Write one sentence on why the molecular formula could not tell your two drawings apart.
