# Molecular and structural formulas — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is the molecular formula not enough for organic chemistry?
**Answer:** Different substances can share one molecular formula while their atoms are arranged differently — C₂H₄Cl₂ is the book's case — and properties follow the arrangement, so chemistry needs the structural formula that shows it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State the four valencies and what the number counts.
**Answer:** Carbon 4, oxygen 2, nitrogen 3, hydrogen 1 — each number is the count of covalent bonds that atom forms in its compounds.
