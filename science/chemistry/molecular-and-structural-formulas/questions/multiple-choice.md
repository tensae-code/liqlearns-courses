# Molecular and structural formulas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A molecular formula tells you…
a) How many atoms of each element, but not their arrangement  b) The full arrangement of the atoms  c) Only the arrangement  d) The compound's melting point
**Answer: a) How many atoms of each element, but not their arrangement**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Carbon's valency, as the count of its covalent bonds, is…
a) 4  b) 2  c) 3  d) 1
**Answer: a) 4**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Nitrogen is…
a) Trivalent  b) Monovalent  c) Divalent  d) Tetravalent
**Answer: a) Trivalent**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In C₂H₂, the bond between the carbons is…
a) A triple bond  b) A single bond  c) A double bond  d) An ionic bond
**Answer: a) A triple bond**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A structural formula is checked by…
a) Every atom carrying exactly its valency of bonds  b) Counting letters in the name  c) The formula's length  d) Its colour
**Answer: a) Every atom carrying exactly its valency of bonds**
