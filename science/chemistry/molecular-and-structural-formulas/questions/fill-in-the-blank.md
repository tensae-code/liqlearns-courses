# Molecular and structural formulas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank from the lesson.

**1.** A ______ formula draws the arrangement of the atoms.
**Answer:** structural

**2.** Oxygen is ______valent — it forms two covalent bonds.
**Answer:** di

**3.** In CH₃OH, the oxygen carries ______ bonds, to carbon and to hydrogen.
**Answer:** two
