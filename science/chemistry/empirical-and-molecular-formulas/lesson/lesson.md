## Empirical and molecular formulas

The empirical formula gives the smallest whole-number ratio of atoms; the molecular formula gives the exact atom counts and is a whole-number multiple of the empirical one. Routine: from percentage composition to empirical formula (85.62% C / 14.38% H worked family), then scale by the measured molecular mass to the molecular formula (28.03 → C₂H₄;

56.06 → C₄H₈; the borane BH₃/28 amu case).
