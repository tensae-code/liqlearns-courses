A plastic bowl softens in hot water and can be remelted; a pan handle never will.

By origin: natural polymers (rubber, cellulose, proteins) versus synthetic ones (plastics, nylon).

By structure: linear chains, branched chains and cross-linked networks — cross-linking makes rigid, infusible materials.

By response to heat: thermoplastics soften on heating and can be remoulded; thermosetting polymers set permanently and cannot.

Now you. Say it back: Which polymer kind can be remoulded?
Answer: Thermoplastic.
