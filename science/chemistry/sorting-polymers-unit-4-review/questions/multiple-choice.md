# Sorting polymers — Unit 4 review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which polymer kind can be remoulded?
a) Flavourings and perfumes  b) An acid catalyst (conc. sulfuric acid)  c) Kinked tails cannot pack closely  d) Thermoplastic
**Answer: d) Thermoplastic**

**2. ** What structure makes a polymer rigid?
a) Turns raw materials into valuable products  b) Fewer gas moles on the product side  c) Cross-linked  d) Spontaneous as written
**Answer: c) Cross-linked**

**3. ** Give a natural polymer.
a) Photons knock electrons off a metal  b) Rubber, cellulose or protein  c) atomos  d) The electron
**Answer: b) Rubber, cellulose or protein**

**4. ** Thermosetting polymers, once set…
a) Cannot be remoulded  b) ½(bonding − antibonding electrons)  c) Particles are far apart  d) The partial pressures
**Answer: a) Cannot be remoulded**
