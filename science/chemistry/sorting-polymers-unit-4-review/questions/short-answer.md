# Sorting polymers — Unit 4 review — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Which polymer kind can be remoulded?
**Answer: Thermoplastic**

**2. ** What structure makes a polymer rigid?
**Answer: Cross-linked**

**3. ** Give a natural polymer.
**Answer: Rubber, cellulose or protein**

**4. ** Thermosetting polymers, once set…
**Answer: Cannot be remoulded**
