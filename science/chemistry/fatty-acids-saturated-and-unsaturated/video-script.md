# Video script — Fatty acids — saturated and unsaturated

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Butter stands firm; oil pours.

Fatty acids are long-chain carboxylic acids, the building blocks of fats and oils.

Saturated fatty acids have no C=C bonds — their chains are straight and pack tightly.

Unsaturated fatty acids have one (mono-) or more (poly-) C=C bonds, which kink the chain and prevent tight packing.

Now you. Say it back: What are fatty acids?
Answer: Long-chain carboxylic acids.
