# Fatty acids — saturated and unsaturated — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What are fatty acids?
**Answer: Long-chain carboxylic acids**

**2. ** What kinks an unsaturated chain?
**Answer: Its C=C double bond(s)**

**3. ** Saturated means the chain has…
**Answer: No C=C bonds**

**4. ** What do fatty acids build?
**Answer: Fats and oils**
