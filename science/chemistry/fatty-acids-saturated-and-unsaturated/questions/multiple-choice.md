# Fatty acids — saturated and unsaturated — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What are fatty acids?
a) Anions are oxidised  b) Long-chain carboxylic acids  c) Comparing Ka and Kb  d) Above 7
**Answer: b) Long-chain carboxylic acids**

**2. ** What kinks an unsaturated chain?
a) Its C=C double bond(s)  b) Soap molecules surrounding a grease droplet  c) Rubber, cellulose or protein  d) The pollution lessons
**Answer: a) Its C=C double bond(s)**

**3. ** Saturated means the chain has…
a) To predict new elements' properties  b) It rises from 1 to 8  c) Its ions are locked in place  d) No C=C bonds
**Answer: d) No C=C bonds**

**4. ** What do fatty acids build?
a) An alkene  b) Williamson  c) Fats and oils  d) The –OH position
**Answer: c) Fats and oils**
