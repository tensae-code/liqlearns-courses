# Epoxides — and why their ring opens — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about Epoxides.
**Answer: Epoxides (epossidi) are a class of their own among the ethers: their ring opens with acids, halogen acids and water, where ordinary ethers are inert.**

**2.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about hybridisation.
**Answer: Symmetric against asymmetric ethers, and oxygen's hybridisation and the ring's geometry, explain the reactivity contrast.**

**3.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about teacher evidence only.
**Answer: This lesson stands on the Campbell teacher guide's programme tables as teacher evidence only — no Italian student book in this wave carries the topic.**
