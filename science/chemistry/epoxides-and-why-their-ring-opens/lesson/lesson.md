## The key ideas

- Epoxides (epossidi) are a class of their own among the ethers: their ring opens with acids, halogen acids and water, where ordinary ethers are inert.
- Symmetric against asymmetric ethers, and oxygen's hybridisation and the ring's geometry, explain the reactivity contrast.
