# Video script — How sugar is made

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cane arrives by the truckload, crystals leave in bags.

Sugar cane is crushed between rollers to extract the juice.

The juice is treated and clarified, then evaporated to a syrup.

Crystallisation grows sugar crystals in the syrup; spinning (centrifuging) separates crystals from molasses, and refining whitens the sugar.

Now you. Say it back: What is crushed first?
Answer: Sugar cane (for juice).
