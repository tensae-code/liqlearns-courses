# How sugar is made — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What is crushed first?
**Answer: Sugar cane (for juice)**

**2. ** What separates crystals from molasses?
**Answer: Centrifuging (spinning)**

**3. ** What is the thick syrup residue called?
**Answer: Molasses**

**4. ** What stage grows the crystals?
**Answer: Crystallisation**
