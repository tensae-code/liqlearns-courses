# How sugar is made — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What is crushed first?
a) Care/treatment — it pollutes  b) A small molecule that joins into chains  c) The biosphere  d) Sugar cane (for juice)
**Answer: d) Sugar cane (for juice)**

**2. ** What separates crystals from molasses?
a) Eight electrons — an octet  b) Lattice formation  c) Centrifuging (spinning)  d) Properties repeat periodically with atomic number
**Answer: c) Centrifuging (spinning)**

**3. ** What is the thick syrup residue called?
a) An –OH group  b) Molasses  c) Le Chatelier's principle  d) Hydrogen bonds
**Answer: b) Molasses**

**4. ** What stage grows the crystals?
a) Crystallisation  b) Tenfold  c) 7 — neutral  d) Acidic
**Answer: a) Crystallisation**
