# Alkyl halides — substitution and elimination — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (What an alkyl halide is, Substitution — the halogen is swapped out, Elimination — HX leaves and a double bond forms), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, working the lesson's examples with changed halogens or chains, and telling the two reaction routes apart in new cases.
