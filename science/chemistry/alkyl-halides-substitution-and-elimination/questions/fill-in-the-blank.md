# Alkyl halides — substitution and elimination — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An alkyl halide is also called a _____.
**Answer: halogenoalkane**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In a substitution, another group takes the _____ place on the same carbon.
**Answer: halogen's**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In an elimination, the halogen and a neighbouring hydrogen leave together as _____.
**Answer: HX**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Elimination forms a carbon–carbon _____ bond, and the product is an alkene.
**Answer: double**
