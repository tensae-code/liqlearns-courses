# Alkyl halides — substitution and elimination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An alkyl halide has the general form…
a) R–OH  b) R–X, an alkyl group carrying a halogen  c) R–COOH  d) R–O–R
**Answer: b) R–X, an alkyl group carrying a halogen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Chloroethane is made from ethane by…
a) Adding a chlorine atom and keeping every hydrogen  b) Replacing one hydrogen with a chlorine atom  c) Removing two hydrogens  d) Joining two ethane molecules
**Answer: b) Replacing one hydrogen with a chlorine atom**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) When chloroethane reacts with aqueous alkali, the product is…
a) Ethene  b) Ethanoic acid  c) Ethanol — the chlorine is swapped for –OH  d) Ethane
**Answer: c) Ethanol — the chlorine is swapped for –OH**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A reaction is a substitution when…
a) HX is removed and a double bond forms  b) The molecule burns in oxygen  c) Another group takes the halogen's place on the same carbon  d) Two molecules join into a chain
**Answer: c) Another group takes the halogen's place on the same carbon**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) In the elimination of HBr from bromoethane, what leaves the molecule?
a) Br₂  b) HBr — the bromine and a hydrogen from the neighbouring carbon  c) Only the bromine  d) Only a hydrogen
**Answer: b) HBr — the bromine and a hydrogen from the neighbouring carbon**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The product of an elimination from an alkyl halide is…
a) An alcohol  b) An ester  c) An alkane  d) An alkene, with a C=C double bond
**Answer: d) An alkene, with a C=C double bond**
