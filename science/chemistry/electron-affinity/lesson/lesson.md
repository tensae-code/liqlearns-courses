## Electron affinity

Electron affinity is the energy change when a neutral gaseous atom gains an electron to form a negative ion. First electron affinities are negative (energy released, e.g. chlorine −349 kJ/mol);

second electron affinities are positive (energy must be forced in against the negative ion). Exothermic vs endothermic sign conventions are taught alongside, with a dropped-book energy analogy. Affinities are used mainly for Groups VI–VII;

metals have low electron affinity because their nuclei hold valence electrons weakly and they prefer to lose electrons and form cations.4.3).
