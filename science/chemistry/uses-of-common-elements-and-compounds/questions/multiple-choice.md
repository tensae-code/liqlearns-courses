# Uses of common elements and compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about uses of common elements and compounds?
a) The elements and compounds around us earn their place by what they do — oxygen for breathing and burning, iron for building, water and salts for life — and each has a market value because people need what it does.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The elements and compounds around us earn their place by what they do — oxygen for breathing and burning, iron for building, water and salts for life — and each has a market value because people need what it does.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about elements at work?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Common elements do daily jobs: oxygen is breathed and supports burning; iron and aluminium are built into tools, roofs and vehicles; copper carries electricity in wires; carbon in its forms fuels fires and writes as graphite.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Common elements do daily jobs: oxygen is breathed and supports burning; iron and aluminium are built into tools, roofs and vehicles; copper carries electricity in wires; carbon in its forms fuels fires and writes as graphite.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about compounds at work?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Compounds join elements into new materials with new uses: water for drinking, cooking and growing; common salt for food and preserving; limestone and cement for building; soaps and fertilisers are made compounds.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Compounds join elements into new materials with new uses: water for drinking, cooking and growing; common salt for food and preserving; limestone and cement for building; soaps and fertilisers are made compounds.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about use gives value?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Because elements and compounds do needed work, they are bought and sold: metals, fuels, salts and fertilisers all carry a market value that rises and falls with how much people need them and how hard they are to get.
**Answer: d) Because elements and compounds do needed work, they are bought and sold: metals, fuels, salts and fertilisers all carry a market value that rises and falls with how much people need them and how hard they are to get.**
