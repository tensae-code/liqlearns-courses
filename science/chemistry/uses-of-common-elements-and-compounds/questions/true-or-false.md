# Uses of common elements and compounds — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Common elements do daily jobs: oxygen is breathed and supports burning; iron and aluminium are built into tools, roofs and vehicles; copper carries electricity in wires; carbon in its forms fuels fires and writes as graphite.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Compounds at work is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** Because elements and compounds do needed work, they are bought and sold: metals, fuels, salts and fertilisers all carry a market value that rises and falls with how much people need them and how hard they are to get.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
