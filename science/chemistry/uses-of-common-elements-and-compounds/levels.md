# Uses of common elements and compounds — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the elements and compounds around us earn their place by what they do — oxygen for breathing and burning, iron for building, water and salts for life — and each has a market value because people need what it does.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
