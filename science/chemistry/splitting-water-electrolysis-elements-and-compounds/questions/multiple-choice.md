# Splitting water — electrolysis, elements and compounds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Electrolysis of water gives hydrogen and oxygen in the volume ratio…
a) 1 : 1  b) 1 : 2  c) 2 : 1  d) 3 : 1
**Answer: c) 2 : 1**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The gas that relights a glowing splint is…
a) Hydrogen  b) Oxygen  c) Carbon dioxide  d) Nitrogen
**Answer: b) Oxygen**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Water is a compound because…
a) It is wet  b) It can be split chemically into simpler substances  c) It is found in nature  d) It is a liquid
**Answer: b) It can be split chemically into simpler substances**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An element is a substance that…
a) Is always a gas  b) Shines  c) Cannot be broken down by chemical means  d) Dissolves in water
**Answer: c) Cannot be broken down by chemical means**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A pure substance is recognised by…
a) Its colour  b) Its fixed physical constants  c) Its price  d) Its container
**Answer: b) Its fixed physical constants**
