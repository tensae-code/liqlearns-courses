Today: **The law of conservation of mass**.

A scientific law describes or predicts phenomena from repeated observation without explaining why;

a theory is the well-tested explanation — the distinction frames the unit's three laws.

Lavoisier's 1789 law of conservation of mass: in a closed system matter is neither created nor destroyed;

total mass of reactants equals total mass of products, element by element, because atoms are not changed into other elements in ordinary reactions.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
