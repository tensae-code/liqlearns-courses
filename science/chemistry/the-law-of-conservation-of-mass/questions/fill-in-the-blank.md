# The law of conservation of mass — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** a theory is the well-tested explanation — the _____ frames the unit's three laws.
**Answer: distinction**

**2. ** Lavoisier's 1789 law of conservation of mass: in a closed system matter is _____ created nor destroyed;
**Answer: neither**

**3. ** total mass of reactants equals total mass of products, element by element, because atoms are not _____ into other elements in ordinary reactions.
**Answer: changed**

**4. ** A scientific law describes or predicts phenomena from _____ observation without explaining why;
**Answer: repeated**
