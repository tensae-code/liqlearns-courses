# Cellular respiration — glycolysis, Krebs cycle and oxidative phosphorylation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Respiration is staged redox: glycolysis, pyruvate decarboxylation and the _____, then the electron-transport chain and chemiosmosis, with the ATP balance per glucose computed.
**Answer: Krebs cycle**

**2.** The proton gradient drives _____ in oxidative phosphorylation.
**Answer: ATP synthase**

**3.** Lactic and alcoholic _____ are the anaerobic fallback, their lower yield justified against respiration.
**Answer: fermentation**
