# Cellular respiration — glycolysis, Krebs cycle and oxidative phosphorylation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about Krebs cycle.
**Answer: Respiration is staged redox: glycolysis, pyruvate decarboxylation and the Krebs cycle, then the electron-transport chain and chemiosmosis, with the ATP balance per glucose computed.**

**2.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about ATP synthase.
**Answer: The proton gradient drives ATP synthase in oxidative phosphorylation.**

**3.** State one thing Italy's book teaches in this lesson about fermentation.
**Answer: Lactic and alcoholic fermentation are the anaerobic fallback, their lower yield justified against respiration.**
