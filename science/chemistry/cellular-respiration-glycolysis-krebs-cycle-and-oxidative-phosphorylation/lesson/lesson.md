## Sub lesson 1 — Modulo E, Capitolo 12, §§10–17 — Italy's book (Grade 13)

Respiration as staged redox: glycolysis (its endergonic and exergonic phases, substrate-level phosphorylation, pyruvate's fates), pyruvate decarboxylation and the Krebs cycle with its ATP/NADH/FADH₂ yield computed per glucose, the electron-transport chain and chemiosmosis (the proton gradient driving ATP synthase), the full ATP balance per glucose, lactic and alcoholic fermentation as the anaerobic fallback with its lower yield justified, and the substances that block respiration (toxins, uncouplers).

## The key ideas

- Respiration is staged redox: glycolysis, pyruvate decarboxylation and the Krebs cycle, then the electron-transport chain and chemiosmosis, with the ATP balance per glucose computed.
- The proton gradient drives ATP synthase in oxidative phosphorylation.
- Lactic and alcoholic fermentation are the anaerobic fallback, their lower yield justified against respiration.
