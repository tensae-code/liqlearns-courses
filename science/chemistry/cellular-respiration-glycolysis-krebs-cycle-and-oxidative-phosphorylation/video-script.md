Today: **Cellular respiration — glycolysis, Krebs cycle and oxidative phosphorylation** — Italy's way, from the book's own treatment. Respiration is staged redox: glycolysis, pyruvate decarboxylation and the Krebs cycle, then the electron-transport chain and chemiosmosis, with the ATP balance per glucose computed. The proton gradient drives ATP synthase in oxidative phosphorylation. Lactic and alcoholic fermentation are the anaerobic fallback, their lower yield justified against respiration.
