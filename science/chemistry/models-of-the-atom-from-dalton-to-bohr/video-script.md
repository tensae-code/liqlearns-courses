Today: **Models of the atom — from Dalton to Bohr** — Sri Lanka's way: the whole of chemistry opened by a chapter about models.

Nobody can see an atom. So science draws a picture of it — a model — and tests the picture. A good model explains what we know and predicts what we will find next.
Dalton's picture: a solid ball. Then the electron was found, and Thomson's picture put electrons inside a positive sphere. Then Rutherford's foil experiment showed the mass sits in a tiny centre — the nucleus. And Bohr repaired the last fault: electrons live in fixed levels and do not fall in.
Four pictures. Each one replaced the last only when the evidence forced it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
