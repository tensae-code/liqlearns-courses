# Models of the atom — from Dalton to Bohr — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A scientific model is…
a) A photograph of the real thing  b) A working picture of a thing that cannot be inspected directly, judged by what it explains and predicts  c) A guess that never changes  d) The final truth about a thing
**Answer: b) A working picture of a thing that cannot be inspected directly, judged by what it explains and predicts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The two tests of a model are…
a) It is old and famous  b) It is drawn neatly and labelled  c) It explains what is known and predicts what will be found next  d) It agrees with the teacher's picture
**Answer: c) It explains what is known and predicts what will be found next**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Dalton's model pictured the atom as…
a) A tiny nucleus with electrons around it  b) A solid, indivisible ball  c) Electrons in fixed levels  d) A wave
**Answer: b) A solid, indivisible ball**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Thomson's model changed Dalton's because…
a) The electron had been found — the atom has parts  b) Atoms were seen to collapse  c) The gold-foil experiment bounced particles back  d) Hydrogen's spectrum was measured
**Answer: a) The electron had been found — the atom has parts**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Rutherford's gold-foil experiment showed that…
a) Atoms are solid balls  b) Electrons sit in fixed levels  c) Nearly all the mass is in a tiny central nucleus and the atom is mostly empty space  d) Atoms join in whole-number ratios
**Answer: c) Nearly all the mass is in a tiny central nucleus and the atom is mostly empty space**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Bohr's repair to Rutherford's model was…
a) Removing the nucleus  b) Electrons occupying fixed allowed levels and not radiating while they stay in them  c) Making the atom solid again  d) Putting electrons inside a positive sphere
**Answer: b) Electrons occupying fixed allowed levels and not radiating while they stay in them**
