# Models of the atom — from Dalton to Bohr — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A model is judged by what it explains and what it _____.
**Answer: predicts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Dalton pictured the atom as a solid, _____ ball.
**Answer: indivisible**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In Rutherford's model, nearly all the atom's mass is concentrated in a tiny central _____.
**Answer: nucleus**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) In Bohr's model, electrons occupy fixed allowed _____ and do not radiate while they stay in them.
**Answer: levels**
