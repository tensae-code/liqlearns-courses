# Hands-on — The genetic connection between organic classes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw both chains
On paper draw two chains: the Grade 8 inorganic chain (metal → basic oxide → base → salt) and the guide's organic link (hydrocarbons → alcohols). Say aloud what the two chains have in common — that is the genetic idea.

## Where from, what next
For the alcohols, answer the chain's two questions from the lesson: where do they come from on the road, and what does standing on a road mean for how the class is learned?
