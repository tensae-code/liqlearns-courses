# The genetic connection between organic classes — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does it mean that hydrocarbons and alcohols are genetically linked?
**Answer:** They are joined by a real transformation: the alcohols can be obtained from the hydrocarbons, so the classes form one connected system rather than separate islands.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) How is the organic genetic link parallel to the inorganic one?
**Answer:** In Grade 8 the inorganic classes were joined in chains, each reached from the one before; the guide does the same for the organic classes, teaching the connection itself as content.
