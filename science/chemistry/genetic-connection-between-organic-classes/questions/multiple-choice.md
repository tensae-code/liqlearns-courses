# The genetic connection between organic classes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The genetic connection in this lesson joins…
a) Atoms in a molecule  b) Classes of compounds by the transformations between them  c) Countries on a map  d) Lessons in a timetable
**Answer: b) Classes of compounds by the transformations between them**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson's source is…
a) The Grade 9 student textbook  b) The Grade 9 teacher guide, contents (teacher evidence)  c) The Grade 8 student textbook  d) The Grade 7 teacher guide
**Answer: b) The Grade 9 teacher guide, contents (teacher evidence)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The guide's named organic link runs…
a) Alcohols → hydrocarbons only  b) Hydrocarbons → alcohols  c) Acids → salts  d) Metals → alcohols
**Answer: b) Hydrocarbons → alcohols**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The organic chain is parallel to…
a) The Grade 7 laboratory rules  b) The inorganic chains of Grade 8  c) The periodic table  d) The gas laws
**Answer: b) The inorganic chains of Grade 8**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A genetic link between classes is made of…
a) Similar names  b) Real transformations — one class obtained from another  c) Similar colours  d) Alphabetical order
**Answer: b) Real transformations — one class obtained from another**
