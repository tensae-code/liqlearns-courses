# Lesson plan — The genetic connection between organic classes

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Chemistry · **Lesson:** The genetic connection between organic classes
**Place in the curriculum:** Chemistry — appended to Unit 17 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the genetic connection between organic classes — the genetic link between hydrocarbons and alcohols — carried into organic chemistry in Armenia's Grade 9 teacher guide (printed p. 124, contents), parallel to the inorganic chains of Grade 8 (teacher evidence only)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. No other lesson is needed first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The genetic idea, carried over** — In Grade 8 the classes of **inorganic** compounds were joined in **genetic chains** — each class reached from the one before.
2. **Hydrocarbons → alcohols — the named link** — The guide names the link it teaches: **the genetic link between hydrocarbons and alcohols**.
3. **One family from another** — A genetic link means the classes form **one connected system**: to know a class is also to know where it comes from and what it can become.
