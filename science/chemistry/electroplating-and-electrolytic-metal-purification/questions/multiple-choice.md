# Electroplating and electrolytic metal purification — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In electroplating, the object to be coated is made the…
a) Cathode  b) Anode  c) Electrolyte  d) Salt bridge
**Answer: a) Cathode**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) For the silver-plated jug, the electrolyte is…
a) Silver nitrate solution  b) Copper sulphate solution  c) Silver metal  d) Distilled water
**Answer: a) Silver nitrate solution**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In copper purification, the pure copper collects…
a) On the cathode, at 99.95% purity  b) In the anode mud  c) In the solution  d) On the anode
**Answer: a) On the cathode, at 99.95% purity**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Silver and gold impurities end up…
a) As anode mud, and are recovered  b) Dissolved in the solution  c) Plated onto the cathode  d) Lost as gases
**Answer: a) As anode mud, and are recovered**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Iron and zinc impurities during copper purification…
a) Stay in solution  b) Plate out first  c) Form the anode mud  d) Evaporate
**Answer: a) Stay in solution**
