# Electroplating and electrolytic metal purification — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must the plating anode be the same metal as the coating?
**Answer:** As plating removes the metal's ions from the solution onto the object, the anode dissolves and replaces exactly those ions, holding the bath's concentration steady — a silver anode for a silver bath, dissolving as fast as the jug plates.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Account for every part of a lump of impure copper after purification.
**Answer:** The copper itself plates pure (99.95%) onto the cathode; the more reactive impurities, iron and zinc, dissolve and stay in solution; the less reactive silver and gold never dissolve — they fall as anode mud and are recovered.
