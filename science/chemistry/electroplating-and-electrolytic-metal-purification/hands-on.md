# Hands-on — Electroplating and electrolytic metal purification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two cells compared
Draw the two cells side by side — plating jug and copper purification. Label cathode, anode and electrolyte in each, and write one line under each saying which electrode the valuable thing happens at, and why they differ.

## Impurity map
Draw an impure copper anode with three arrows: copper to the cathode, iron/zinc into the solution, silver/gold down into the mud. Say the rule that sorts them — reactivity — in one sentence.
