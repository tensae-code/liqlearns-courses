Today: **The Greek four elements**.

Greek thought begins with Empedocles' claim that all materials are made of four elements — earth, air, water and fire;

Plato coined the word "element" for them and Aristotle added distinguishing properties (hot/cold, wet/dry) that let one element turn into another, as clay becomes a pot…

The four-element scheme held for over two thousand years.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
