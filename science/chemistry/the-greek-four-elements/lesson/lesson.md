## The Greek four elements

Greek thought begins with Empedocles' claim that all materials are made of four elements — earth, air, water and fire; Plato coined the word "element" for them and Aristotle added distinguishing properties (hot/cold, wet/dry) that let one element turn into another, as clay becomes a pot in the oven or air cools into rain. The four-element scheme held for over two thousand years.
