# The Greek four elements — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** Plato coined the word "element" for them and Aristotle added distinguishing _____ (hot/cold, wet/dry) that let one element turn into another, as clay becomes a pot in the oven or air cools into rain.
**Answer: properties**

**2. ** The four-element scheme held for over two _____ years.
**Answer: thousand**

**3. ** Greek thought begins with Empedocles' claim that all _____ are made of four elements — earth, air, water and fire;
**Answer: materials**

**4. ** Plato coined the word "element" for them and Aristotle added distinguishing _____ (hot/cold, wet/dry) that let one element turn into another, as clay becomes a pot in the oven or air cools into rain.
**Answer: properties**
