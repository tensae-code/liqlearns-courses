Today: **The genetic connection between the classes of inorganic substances** — Belarus's way: the classes taught as one connected system, not four boxes.

Start with the four classes — oxides, acids, bases and salts. Belarus refuses to leave them as four lists. Each class can be turned into the next, and the tie that links them is called the genetic connection, because each substance in a chain is born from the one before it.
Walk the two great chains. A metal: calcium burns to its basic oxide, the oxide meets water to give the base, the base is neutralised to a salt. A nonmetal: sulfur burns to its acidic oxide, the oxide meets water to give the acid, the acid is neutralised to a salt. The chains meet at the salt.
And the proof it is one system: zinc oxide reacts with an acid like a base and with an alkali like an acid. Amphoteric substances walk both chains at once.
