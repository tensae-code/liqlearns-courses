# The genetic connection between the classes of inorganic substances — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The transformations linking the classes of inorganic substances into one system are called the _____ connection.
**Answer: genetic**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Calcium's chain runs Ca → CaO → Ca(OH)₂ → _____.
**Answer: CaCl₂ (a salt — calcium chloride in the lesson's chain)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An acid and a base reacting together to give a salt and water is called _____.
**Answer: neutralisation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) An oxide that reacts with both acids and alkalis is called _____.
**Answer: amphoteric**
