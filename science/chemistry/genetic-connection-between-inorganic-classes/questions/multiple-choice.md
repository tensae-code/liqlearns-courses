# The genetic connection between the classes of inorganic substances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The genetic connection between the classes of inorganic substances is…
a) A family tree of the elements  b) The system of transformations by which oxides, acids, bases and salts change into one another in chains  c) The order of the periodic table  d) A bond between two atoms
**Answer: b) The system of transformations by which oxides, acids, bases and salts change into one another in chains**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a transformation chain, what travels unchanged through every stop?
a) The class of the substance  b) The element at the chain's heart  c) The state of matter  d) The colour
**Answer: b) The element at the chain's heart**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The chain of a typical metal runs…
a) Metal → acidic oxide → acid → salt  b) Metal → salt → base → oxide  c) Metal → basic oxide → base → salt  d) Metal → base → acidic oxide → salt
**Answer: c) Metal → basic oxide → base → salt**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The metal and nonmetal chains meet at…
a) The oxide  b) The water  c) The salt, where acid and base neutralise each other  d) They never meet
**Answer: c) The salt, where acid and base neutralise each other**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Zinc oxide dissolving in both an acid and an alkali proves that…
a) Zinc is not a metal  b) ZnO is amphoteric — the crossing showing the two chains are one system  c) Alkalis are acids  d) Oxides are salts
**Answer: b) ZnO is amphoteric — the crossing showing the two chains are one system**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The arrow from a basic oxide to its base (CaO → Ca(OH)₂) is carried by reaction with…
a) An acid  b) Oxygen  c) Water  d) A salt
**Answer: c) Water**
