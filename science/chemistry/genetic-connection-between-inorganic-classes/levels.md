# The genetic connection between the classes of inorganic substances — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (One family, not four boxes, The two great chains — metal and nonmetal, Walking a chain — and the amphoteric crossing), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, laying named substances onto the two chains, and building a full chain for a new element with the equation on every arrow.
