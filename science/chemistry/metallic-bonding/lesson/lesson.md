# Metallic bonding

## Do it first
A metal is not molecules, and not ions in a salt lattice — yet it holds together strongly. Before reading, say what the atoms might contribute to a shared pool to glue themselves.

[Picture 21644 — Kernels held in a pool of electrons.]

In metallic bonding, metal atoms pool their valence electrons.

The atoms become positive kernels (ions) sitting in fixed positions.

The bond is the attraction between the positive kernels and the mobile pooled electrons around them.
