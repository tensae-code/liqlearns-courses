# Video script — Metallic bonding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A metal is not molecules, and not ions in a salt lattice — yet it holds together strongly.

In metallic bonding, metal atoms pool their valence electrons.

The atoms become positive kernels (ions) sitting in fixed positions.

The bond is the attraction between the positive kernels and the mobile pooled electrons around them.

Now you. Say it back: What do metal atoms pool?
Answer: Their valence electrons.
