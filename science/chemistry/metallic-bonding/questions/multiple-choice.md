# Metallic bonding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What do metal atoms pool?
a) The fat's ester links (by water)  b) Their valence electrons  c) Making soap by alkaline hydrolysis of fats  d) Three — one per ester link
**Answer: b) Their valence electrons**

**2. ** What are the metal atom cores called?
a) Positive kernels  b) Electricity and water  c) By-products  d) Vanadium(V) oxide
**Answer: a) Positive kernels**

**3. ** What is the metallic bond?
a) The atoms themselves change  b) Fields do not bend them — they carry no charge  c) E = hν  d) Attraction of kernels to the electron pool
**Answer: d) Attraction of kernels to the electron pool**

**4. ** Where do the pooled electrons sit?
a) None  b) Crystalline  c) Mobile, around the kernels  d) Solid → liquid → gas
**Answer: c) Mobile, around the kernels**
