# Metallic bonding — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What do metal atoms pool?
**Answer: Their valence electrons**

**2. ** What are the metal atom cores called?
**Answer: Positive kernels**

**3. ** What is the metallic bond?
**Answer: Attraction of kernels to the electron pool**

**4. ** Where do the pooled electrons sit?
**Answer: Mobile, around the kernels**
