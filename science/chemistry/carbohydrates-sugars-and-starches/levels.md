# Carbohydrates — sugars and starches — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The single sugars, Sugars joined — doubles, chains and stores, Carbohydrates in living beings — use, benefits and harms), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, sorting named carbohydrates by unit count, and tracing a mouthful of starch from the plate to glucose in the blood.
