# Carbohydrates — sugars and starches — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The single sugars that cannot be split into smaller sugars are called _____.
**Answer: monosaccharides**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The sugar of milk, a disaccharide, is called _____.
**Answer: lactose**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The animal's quick energy store, a glucose-chain polysaccharide, is called _____.
**Answer: glycogen**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Sugar feeding the mouth's bacteria is the chemistry behind tooth _____.
**Answer: decay**
