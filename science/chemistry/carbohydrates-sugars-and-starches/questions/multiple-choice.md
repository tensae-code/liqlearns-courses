# Carbohydrates — sugars and starches — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A carbohydrate is a compound of…
a) Carbon and hydrogen only  b) Carbon, hydrogen and oxygen  c) Carbon, nitrogen and oxygen  d) Carbon and oxygen only
**Answer: b) Carbon, hydrogen and oxygen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Glucose, fructose and galactose are all…
a) Disaccharides  b) Polysaccharides  c) Monosaccharides sharing the formula C₆H₁₂O₆  d) Not sugars at all
**Answer: c) Monosaccharides sharing the formula C₆H₁₂O₆**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In the D/L sorting of sugars, nearly all sugars used by living things are…
a) L sugars  b) D sugars  c) Half D and half L  d) Neither D nor L
**Answer: b) D sugars**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Ordinary table sugar, sucrose, is…
a) A monosaccharide  b) A disaccharide — glucose joined to fructose  c) A polysaccharide  d) A protein
**Answer: b) A disaccharide — glucose joined to fructose**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Starch, glycogen and cellulose are alike in being…
a) Single sugars  b) Double sugars  c) Long chains of glucose units, differing in how the units are joined  d) Made of amino acids
**Answer: c) Long chains of glucose units, differing in how the units are joined**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In digestion, starch is changed to glucose by…
a) Hydrolysis — water added back across the joins  b) Burning  c) Freezing  d) Fermentation in the stomach
**Answer: a) Hydrolysis — water added back across the joins**
