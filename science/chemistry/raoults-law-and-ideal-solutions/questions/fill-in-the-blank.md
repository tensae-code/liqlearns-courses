# Raoult's law and ideal solutions — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In Raoult's law, vapour pressure is the pure value times the solvent's mole _____.
**Answer: fraction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A solution obeying Raoult's law at every concentration, with no heat of mixing, is called _____.
**Answer: ideal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) When the unlike attractions are stronger and the vapour pressure falls below the Raoult line, the deviation is _____.
**Answer: negative**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A fixed-boiling mixture whose vapour has the same composition as its liquid is an _____.
**Answer: azeotrope**
