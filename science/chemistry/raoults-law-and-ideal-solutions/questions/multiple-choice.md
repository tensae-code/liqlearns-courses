# Raoult's law and ideal solutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Raoult's law states that the vapour pressure of a solvent over a solution equals…
a) Its pure vapour pressure, unchanged  b) Its pure vapour pressure times its mole fraction in the solution  c) Its pure vapour pressure divided by its mole fraction  d) The solute's vapour pressure times its mole fraction
**Answer: b) Its pure vapour pressure times its mole fraction in the solution**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) If the solvent's mole fraction is 0.75, the vapour pressure over the solution is…
a) 0.75 of the pure solvent's vapour pressure  b) 1.25 times the pure value  c) Unchanged  d) Zero
**Answer: a) 0.75 of the pure solvent's vapour pressure**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An ideal solution is one that…
a) Never boils  b) Obeys Raoult's law at every concentration, with no heat or volume change on mixing  c) Contains no solute  d) Has zero vapour pressure
**Answer: b) Obeys Raoult's law at every concentration, with no heat or volume change on mixing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A mixture shows a positive deviation from Raoult's law when…
a) Its vapour pressure is lower than predicted and mixing gives out heat  b) Its vapour pressure is higher than predicted, the unlike attractions are weaker, and mixing takes in heat  c) It obeys the law exactly  d) Its vapour has the same composition as its liquid at every strength
**Answer: b) Its vapour pressure is higher than predicted, the unlike attractions are weaker, and mixing takes in heat**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The vapour over a boiling mixture is…
a) Poorer in the more volatile part than the liquid  b) The same as the liquid in every mixture  c) Richer in the more volatile part than the liquid it left  d) Pure solvent always
**Answer: c) Richer in the more volatile part than the liquid it left**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) An azeotrope is a mixture that…
a) Can always be separated completely by simple distillation  b) Boils with vapour of exactly the same composition as the liquid, so simple distillation cannot carry the mixture past its composition  c) Contains no volatile liquid  d) Obeys Raoult's law at every concentration
**Answer: b) Boils with vapour of exactly the same composition as the liquid, so simple distillation cannot carry the mixture past its composition**
