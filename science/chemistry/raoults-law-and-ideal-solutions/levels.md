# Raoult's law and ideal solutions — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Raoult's law — vapour pressure follows the mole fraction, Ideal solutions — and the two ways real solutions deviate, Composition diagrams and distilling deviating mixtures), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, working a Raoult's-law vapour-pressure calculation from mole fractions, and sorting named mixtures as ideal, positively deviating or negatively deviating from the direction of their deviation.
