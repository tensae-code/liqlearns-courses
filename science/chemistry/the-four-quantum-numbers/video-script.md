# Video script — The four quantum numbers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An address has a country, a town, a street, a house.

The principal quantum number n gives the main energy level and the size of the orbital.

The angular-momentum number l gives the subshell shape; it runs from 0 to n−1.

The magnetic number m_l gives the orbital's orientation; it runs from −l to +l.

The spin number m_s gives the electron's spin: +½ or −½.

Now you. Say it back: What does n specify?
Answer: The main energy level (and orbital size).
