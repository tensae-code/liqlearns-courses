# The four quantum numbers

## Do it first
An address has a country, a town, a street, a house. An electron's 'address' in an atom has four parts too. Before reading, guess what four things you would need to fix one electron's place and state.

[Picture 21621 — One electron — four numbers fix its state.]

The principal quantum number n gives the main energy level and the size of the orbital.

The angular-momentum number l gives the subshell shape; it runs from 0 to n−1.

The magnetic number m_l gives the orbital's orientation; it runs from −l to +l.

The spin number m_s gives the electron's spin: +½ or −½. Together the four numbers specify one electron's state, and counting allowed values counts the electrons each level and sublevel can hold.
