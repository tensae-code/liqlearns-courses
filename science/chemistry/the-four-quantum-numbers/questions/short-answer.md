# The four quantum numbers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does n specify?
**Answer: The main energy level (and orbital size)**

**2. ** What values can m_s take?
**Answer: +½ or −½**

**3. ** What does l specify?
**Answer: The subshell (orbital shape)**

**4. ** What does m_l specify?
**Answer: The orbital's orientation**
