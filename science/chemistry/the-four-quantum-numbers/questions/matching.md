# The four quantum numbers — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What does n specify? — b
2. What values can m_s take? — c
3. What does l specify? — d
4. What does m_l specify? — a

**Answer key:** 1–b: The main energy level (and orbital size) · 2–c: +½ or −½ · 3–d: The subshell (orbital shape) · 4–a: The orbital's orientation
