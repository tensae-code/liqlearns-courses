# The four quantum numbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does n specify?
a) It lowers the activation energy path  b) The main energy level (and orbital size)  c) Elastically — no energy lost  d) It stays constant during the change
**Answer: b) The main energy level (and orbital size)**

**2. ** What values can m_s take?
a) +½ or −½  b) No hydrogen bonding between esters  c) Plants (seeds)  d) Margarine (solid fat)
**Answer: a) +½ or −½**

**3. ** What does l specify?
a) Oxygen and water  b) Ethiopia's own resource base  c) NO  d) The subshell (orbital shape)
**Answer: d) The subshell (orbital shape)**

**4. ** What does m_l specify?
a) A tiny, dense, positive centre exists  b) The proton number Z  c) The orbital's orientation  d) In fixed whole-number ratios
**Answer: c) The orbital's orientation**
