# Video script — Chemistry in farming and food

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Chemistry in farming and food**.

Chemical fertilisers (calcium superphosphate, urea, ammonium sulphate, sodium nitrate) raise crop yields;

pesticides are sorted by target pest into fungicides, herbicides and insecticides;

chemistry also supplies better irrigation pipe.

In food, chemistry gives preservatives that keep food longer (raw-meat preservation as the local example), tests for adulterants, and improvements in availability,…

A group activity sends pupils to list the pests, weeds and the commercial and traditional pesticides and herbicides their own local farmers use.

Now you. Say it back in your own words. That is the lesson.
