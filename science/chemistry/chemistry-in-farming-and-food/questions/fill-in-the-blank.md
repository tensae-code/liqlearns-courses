# Chemistry in farming and food — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** pesticides are sorted by target pest into fungicides, _____ and insecticides;
**Answer: herbicides**

**2. ** chemistry also _____ better irrigation pipe.
**Answer: supplies**

**3. ** In food, chemistry gives preservatives that keep food longer (raw-meat preservation as the local example), tests for adulterants, and _____ in availability, appearance, nutrition and flavour.
**Answer: improvements**

**4. ** A group activity sends pupils to list the pests, weeds and the commercial and traditional _____ and herbicides their own local farmers use.
**Answer: pesticides**
