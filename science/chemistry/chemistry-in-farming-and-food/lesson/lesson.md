## Chemistry in farming and food

Chemical fertilisers (calcium superphosphate, urea, ammonium sulphate, sodium nitrate) raise crop yields; pesticides are sorted by target pest into fungicides, herbicides and insecticides; chemistry also supplies better irrigation pipe.

In food, chemistry gives preservatives that keep food longer (raw-meat preservation as the local example), tests for adulterants, and improvements in availability, appearance, nutrition and flavour. A group activity sends pupils to list the pests, weeds and the commercial and traditional pesticides and herbicides their own local farmers use.
