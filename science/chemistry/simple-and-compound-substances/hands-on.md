# Hands-on — Simple and compound substances

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the shelf
Write the headings Simple and Compound. From the lesson, sort these by their building: a substance whose formula names one element, and a substance whose formula names two. Say the test question out loud before each sort.

## Formula tells the sort
Take two formulas from earlier lessons — one naming a single element, one naming two. For each, say which sort it falls in and which part of the formula told you.
