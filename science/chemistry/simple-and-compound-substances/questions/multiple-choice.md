# Simple and compound substances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A simple substance is built from…
a) Atoms of different elements  b) Atoms of one element only  c) Molecules only  d) Ions only
**Answer: b) Atoms of one element only**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A compound substance is…
a) A mixture of elements side by side  b) Different elements joined into a new substance with its own properties  c) Any substance with many atoms  d) A substance that cannot be written as a formula
**Answer: b) Different elements joined into a new substance with its own properties**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A compound's formula names more than one element because…
a) Formulas always do  b) More than one element is really in it  c) It is a mixture  d) It was heated
**Answer: b) More than one element is really in it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The sorting test of §3.2 asks…
a) How heavy is it?  b) How many kinds of atom is it built from?  c) What colour is it?  d) Where was it found?
**Answer: b) How many kinds of atom is it built from?**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Armenia teaches this sorting right after…
a) The periodic table  b) The molecule and the chemical formula (§3.1)  c) Chemical reactions  d) The laboratory
**Answer: b) The molecule and the chemical formula (§3.1)**
