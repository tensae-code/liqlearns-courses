# Simple and compound substances — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the difference between a simple and a compound substance in one sentence each.
**Answer:** A simple substance is built from atoms of one element only; a compound substance is built from atoms of different elements joined together.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) Why is a compound not just a mixture of its elements?
**Answer:** In a compound the different atoms are joined into a new substance with its own properties; in a mixture the substances sit side by side unjoined, each keeping its own properties.
