# What environmental chemistry studies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What does environmental chemistry study?
a) Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil  b) Anode to cathode  c) Electrochemical  d) Raw materials, energy, water, transport or markets
**Answer: a) Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil**

**2. ** What three media does it cover?
a) Hazardous substances  b) Subatomic particles  c) Beryllium  d) Air, water and soil
**Answer: d) Air, water and soil**

**3. ** What questions does it ask of a pollutant?
a) Particles in motion  b) Kelvin temperature  c) Source, movement, changes, effects  d) It dents instead of shattering
**Answer: c) Source, movement, changes, effects**

**4. ** What does it underlie in this unit?
a) Pleasant (fruity)  b) The pollution lessons  c) It falls  d) Ethanoic (acetic) acid
**Answer: b) The pollution lessons**
