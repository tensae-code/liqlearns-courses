# What environmental chemistry studies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. ** What does environmental chemistry study — Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil
**Answer: True**

**2. ** What three media does it cover — Suppresses it
**Answer: False**

**3. ** What questions does it ask of a pollutant — Source, movement, changes, effects
**Answer: True**

**4. ** What does it underlie in this unit — The rise in average temperature
**Answer: False**
