# What environmental chemistry studies — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** What does environmental chemistry study?
**Answer: Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil**

**2. ** What three media does it cover?
**Answer: Air, water and soil**

**3. ** What questions does it ask of a pollutant?
**Answer: Source, movement, changes, effects**

**4. ** What does it underlie in this unit?
**Answer: The pollution lessons**
