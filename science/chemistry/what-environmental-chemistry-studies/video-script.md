# Video script — What environmental chemistry studies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A new discipline names itself in this lesson.

Environmental chemistry studies chemical processes and pollutants in air, water and soil.

It asks where pollutants come from, how they move and change, and what they do to living systems.

It supplies the science under every pollution lesson that follows in this unit.

Now you. Say it back: What does environmental chemistry study?
Answer: Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil.
