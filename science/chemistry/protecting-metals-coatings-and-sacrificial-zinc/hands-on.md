# Hands-on — Protecting metals — coatings and sacrificial zinc

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. (Paper work unless an activity says otherwise; any real bench work belongs to the school laboratory, with its safety kit and a teacher present.)

## Scratch verdicts
Draw three scratched parts — painted iron, nickel-plated iron, zinc-coated iron — each scratched to the iron. Under each, write RUSTS or RESTS and the row-based reason. The three verdicts are the lesson in miniature.

## Bolt audit
Audit five metal pairs for a wet iron structure (copper bolts, zinc bolts, nickel bolts, aluminium plates attached, tin coating scratched) using the activity row: for each, name the anode — the metal that will be eaten — and pass or fail the design.
