# Protecting metals — coatings and sacrificial zinc — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Paints and enamels protect as…
a) Sacrificial metals  b) Barrier coatings  c) Alloys  d) Catalysts
**Answer: b) Barrier coatings**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In sacrificial protection, the metal that oxidises is…
a) The iron  b) The more active attached metal  c) The paint  d) The rust
**Answer: b) The more active attached metal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Zinc protects iron at a scratch because zinc is…
a) After iron in the row  b) More active than iron, so it oxidises instead  c) Inert  d) A paint
**Answer: b) More active than iron, so it oxidises instead**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Stainless steel belongs to the family of…
a) Barrier coatings  b) Corrosion-resistant alloys  c) Sacrificial plates  d) Enamels
**Answer: b) Corrosion-resistant alloys**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Copper bolts in an iron structure are forbidden because…
a) Copper is weak  b) The pair makes iron oxidise as the cell's anode  c) Copper rusts green  d) They are expensive only
**Answer: b) The pair makes iron oxidise as the cell's anode**
