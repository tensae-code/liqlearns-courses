# Protecting metals — coatings and sacrificial zinc — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name Bulgaria's three protection families with one example each.
**Answer: Barrier coatings (chromium plating, paint); electrochemical protection (sacrificial zinc); corrosion-resistant alloys (stainless steel).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain 'the iron rests as cathode'.
**Answer: In the galvanic pair the more active metal gives up the electrons and oxidises; the iron receives the electrons — it is the cathode — and so is not consumed.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) State the nickel trap in one sentence.
**Answer: Nickel stands after iron in the activity row, so at a scratch through nickel plating it is the iron that oxidises — the plating protects only as an unbroken barrier.**
