# Gravimetric analysis — by volatilisation and precipitation — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Distinguish the two forms by what is actually weighed.
**Answer:** In volatilisation the sought part leaves and is found by the mass lost (the water from hydrated BaCl₂); in precipitation the sought part is brought down as a solid and weighed itself (barium as BaSO₄).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why heat to constant mass rather than for a fixed time?
**Answer:** Only a unchanging mass proves the driving-off is complete; a fixed time might stop early with water still inside, and the whole method stands on the weighing being exact.
