# Gravimetric analysis — by volatilisation and precipitation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Gravimetric analysis measures a constituent by…
a) Weighing it, or weighing what it becomes  b) Timing its reaction  c) Its colour intensity  d) Its volume as a gas only
**Answer: a) Weighing it, or weighing what it becomes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In the volatilisation method, heating continues until…
a) Constant mass is reached  b) The crucible melts  c) The sample changes colour  d) Ten minutes pass
**Answer: a) Constant mass is reached**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Heating hydrated barium chloride finds its…
a) Water of crystallisation  b) Melting point  c) Solubility  d) Oxidation state
**Answer: a) Water of crystallisation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) In the precipitation method, barium is weighed as…
a) BaSO₄  b) BaCl₂  c) BaO  d) Ba(OH)₂
**Answer: a) BaSO₄**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Ashless filter paper is used because…
a) It burns away completely on ignition, adding no mass  b) It is cheaper  c) It filters faster  d) It colours the precipitate
**Answer: a) It burns away completely on ignition, adding no mass**
