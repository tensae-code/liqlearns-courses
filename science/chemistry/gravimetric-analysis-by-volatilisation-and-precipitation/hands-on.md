# Hands-on — Gravimetric analysis — by volatilisation and precipitation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mass ledger
Invent a volatilisation run: hydrated salt 5.00 g; after heating 4.30 g; after reheating 4.26 g; after a third heating 4.26 g. Write why the second weighing was not enough, and what the water's mass is in the finished run.

## Flow of the precipitate
Write the precipitation method as a five-step flow — precipitate, filter on ashless paper, ignite in crucible, cool, weigh — and under it, what each step protects the final mass from.
