# Hands-on — Resonance structures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the pair
On paper, draw SO₂ twice in graphic form: first with the double bond to the left oxygen and an arrowed coordinate bond to the right oxygen; then swapped. Check each drawing: same atoms, same total bonds, only the bond kinds swapped.

## Draw the average
Now draw SO₂ a third time, giving both S–O bonds the same in-between mark (a dash and a half, or a dashed second line). Say out loud: this third drawing is the honest one — the molecule itself.

## Test a companion
Show a companion only the pair. Ask: does the molecule flip between these? Have them answer from the lesson before you confirm.
