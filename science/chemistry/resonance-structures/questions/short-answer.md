# Resonance structures — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Describe the two equivalent forms of SO₂ as the book draws them.
**Answer:** In one form one S–O bond is double and the other a coordinate bond; in the second form the two are swapped. The book draws both as dot formulas and as graphic formulas, with the coordinate bond arrowed in the graphic pair.

**2.** (Sub lesson 3) What does resonance predict about the two S–O bonds, and why?
**Answer:** They are equal — each between a single and a double bond — because the real structure is the average of the two equivalent forms, not either form alone.
