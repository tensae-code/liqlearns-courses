# Resonance structures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Resonance is needed when…
a) A molecule has no bonds  b) One formula cannot tell the whole story and two equivalent forms can  c) The molecule is ionic  d) There are two molecules
**Answer: b) One formula cannot tell the whole story and two equivalent forms can**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In the book's graphic forms of SO₂, the coordinate bond is shown…
a) Dashed  b) Arrowed  c) Doubled  d) Coloured
**Answer: b) Arrowed**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The real SO₂ molecule…
a) Flips rapidly between the two forms  b) Is the average of the two forms, with equal S–O bonds  c) Uses only the first form  d) Has no S–O bonds
**Answer: b) Is the average of the two forms, with equal S–O bonds**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The two resonance forms of SO₂ differ in…
a) Which oxygen holds the double bond and which the coordinate bond  b) The number of atoms  c) The charge  d) The elements present
**Answer: a) Which oxygen holds the double bond and which the coordinate bond**
