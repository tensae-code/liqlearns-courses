Today: **Resonance structures** — Laos's way, worked on SO₂: two correct drawings, one real molecule.

Sulfur dioxide can be drawn two equivalent ways — double bond here, coordinate bond there, then swapped, the coordinate bond arrowed. Neither drawing alone is true. The molecule is the average of the two, and its two S–O bonds are equal.
