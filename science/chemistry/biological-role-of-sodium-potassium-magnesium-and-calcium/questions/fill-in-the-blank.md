# Biological role of sodium, potassium, magnesium and calcium — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The metal ion at the centre of chlorophyll is _____.
**Answer: Mg²⁺**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Calcium is stored in bones as calcium _____.
**Answer: phosphate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The ion that triggers muscle contraction is _____.
**Answer: Ca²⁺**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Inside the body's cells the chief alkali ion is _____; outside, it is Na⁺.
**Answer: K⁺**
