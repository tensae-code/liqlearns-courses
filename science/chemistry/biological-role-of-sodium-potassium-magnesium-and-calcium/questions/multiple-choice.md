# Biological role of sodium, potassium, magnesium and calcium — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Mg²⁺ in a leaf is found…
a) In the cell walls  b) At the centre of chlorophyll, the pigment essential for photosynthesis  c) In the roots only  d) Nowhere — plants use iron there
**Answer: b) At the centre of chlorophyll, the pigment essential for photosynthesis**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Calcium is present in bones as…
a) Calcium metal  b) Phosphate — calcium phosphate, the hard mineral of the skeleton  c) Carbonate only  d) Chloride
**Answer: b) Phosphate — calcium phosphate, the hard mineral of the skeleton**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Mg²⁺ and Ca²⁺ catalyse a number of…
a) Industrial reactions  b) Enzymatic reactions — many enzymes work only with the ion in place  c) Nuclear reactions  d) Combustion reactions
**Answer: b) Enzymatic reactions — many enzymes work only with the ion in place**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Ca²⁺ ions' role in muscle, as the section teaches it, is…
a) Building the fibres  b) Triggering contraction — their flood into the muscle cell sets the fibres shortening  c) Colouring the muscle  d) Storing energy
**Answer: b) Triggering contraction — their flood into the muscle cell sets the fibres shortening**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Sodium and potassium ions divide the body's waters as…
a) Both outside cells  b) Na⁺ chiefly outside cells, K⁺ chiefly inside — their balance holds fluids steady and carries nerve impulses  c) Both inside cells  d) Neither in fluids
**Answer: b) Na⁺ chiefly outside cells, K⁺ chiefly inside — their balance holds fluids steady and carries nerve impulses**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The chapter's closing warning is that Ca²⁺ deficiency in children causes…
a) Fever  b) Malnutrition  c) Deafness  d) Anaemia
**Answer: b) Malnutrition**
