# Hands-on — Biological role of sodium, potassium, magnesium and calcium

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Staff list (paper only)
Draw the chapter's staff table: four rows (Na⁺, K⁺, Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺), and for each ion its post or posts from the lesson and the one fact that fits it for the post (pigment centre, fluid side, enzyme partner, mineral store, contraction trigger).

## Trace the green (paper only)
Write the chain from a magnesium-poor soil to a hungry village in four steps, using the lesson: soil → chlorophyll → photosynthesis → food. Mark the exact step where the Mg²⁺ itself does its work.

## Deficiency file (paper only)
Write the short case the chapter implies: a child whose diet lacks calcium. List what the body borrows, from where, and the chapter's own name for the outcome — then one sentence on why children, still building the store, are named in the warning.
