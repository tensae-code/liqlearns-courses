# Ions — atoms that gain or lose electrons — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An atom that has lost or gained electrons is called an _____.
**Answer: ion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sodium loses one electron and becomes _____.
**Answer: Na⁺**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Chlorine gains one electron and becomes _____.
**Answer: Cl⁻**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Losing electrons gives a _____ ion.
**Answer: positive**
