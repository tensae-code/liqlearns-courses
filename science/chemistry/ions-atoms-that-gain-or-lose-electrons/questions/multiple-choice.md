# Ions — atoms that gain or lose electrons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An ion is…
a) A molecule of two elements  b) An atom that has lost or gained electrons  c) An atom with a changed nucleus  d) A neutral atom
**Answer: b) An atom that has lost or gained electrons**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Sodium's atom has 11 positive charges and 11 electrons. After losing one electron it has…
a) 11 electrons  b) 10 electrons, and a +1 charge  c) 12 electrons  d) No charge
**Answer: b) 10 electrons, and a +1 charge**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Na⁺ is a…
a) Negative ion  b) Positive ion  c) Neutral atom  d) Compound body
**Answer: b) Positive ion**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Chlorine becomes Cl⁻ by…
a) Losing an electron  b) Gaining an electron  c) Losing a proton  d) Joining sodium
**Answer: b) Gaining an electron**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) When an atom forms an ion, the part that does NOT change is…
a) Its electron count  b) Its nucleus — so it stays the same element  c) Its charge  d) Its symbol's letter
**Answer: b) Its nucleus — so it stays the same element**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Gaining electrons makes an ion…
a) Positive  b) Negative  c) Neutral  d) Twice as heavy
**Answer: b) Negative**
