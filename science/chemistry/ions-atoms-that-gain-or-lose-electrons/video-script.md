Today: **Ions — atoms that gain or lose electrons**.

An atom is balanced: sodium has eleven positive charges and eleven electrons — no charge at all. But electrons can move. Sodium loses one electron. Now it has eleven positives and only ten electrons — one positive charge left over. That particle is Na⁺, a positive ion. Chlorine runs the other way: it gains an electron, the negatives win by one, and it becomes Cl⁻, a negative ion. Lose electrons — positive ion. Gain electrons — negative ion. And the nucleus never changes, so the element stays itself. That is an ion.
