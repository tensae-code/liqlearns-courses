# Video script — Water ionises itself — pH and pOH

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Pure water is neutral, yet it conducts a whisper of electricity — ions exist in it, made by water itself.

Water self-ionises: H₂O + H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + OH⁻.

pH = −log[H₃O⁺] and pOH = −log[OH⁻]; the scales run 0–14 in ordinary work, with pH + pOH = 14 at 25 °C.

Neutral water has pH 7; acids sit below 7, bases above.

Answer: Kw = [H₃O⁺][OH⁻].
