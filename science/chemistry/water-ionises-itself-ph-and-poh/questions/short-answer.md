# Water ionises itself — pH and pOH — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Write Kw's expression.
**Answer: Kw = [H₃O⁺][OH⁻]**

**2. ** What is Kw at 25 °C?
**Answer: 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴**

**3. ** pH + pOH equals what (25 °C)?
**Answer: 14**

**4. ** One pH unit means what change in [H₃O⁺]?
**Answer: Tenfold**
