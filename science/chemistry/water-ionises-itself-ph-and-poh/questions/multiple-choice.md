# Water ionises itself — pH and pOH — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Write Kw's expression.
a) Kw = [H₃O⁺][OH⁻]  b) Hazardous substances  c) Subatomic particles  d) Beryllium
**Answer: a) Kw = [H₃O⁺][OH⁻]**

**2. ** What is Kw at 25 °C?
a) It dents instead of shattering  b) Particles in motion  c) Kelvin temperature  d) 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴
**Answer: d) 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴**

**3. ** pH + pOH equals what (25 °C)?
a) Ethanoic (acetic) acid  b) Pleasant (fruity)  c) 14  d) It falls
**Answer: c) 14**

**4. ** One pH unit means what change in [H₃O⁺]?
a) Oxidised (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻)  b) Tenfold  c) The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper  d) Zinc
**Answer: b) Tenfold**
