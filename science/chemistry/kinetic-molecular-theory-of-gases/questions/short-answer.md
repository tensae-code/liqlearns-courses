# Kinetic molecular theory of gases — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** How do ideal gas particles collide?
**Answer: Elastically — no energy lost**

**2. ** Average kinetic energy is proportional to what?
**Answer: Kelvin temperature**

**3. ** What volume do ideal particles have?
**Answer: Negligible — treated as points**

**4. ** What attractions act between ideal particles?
**Answer: None**
