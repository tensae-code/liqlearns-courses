# Kinetic molecular theory of gases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** How do ideal gas particles collide?
a) Elastically — no energy lost  b) It dents instead of shattering  c) Particles in motion  d) Kelvin temperature
**Answer: a) Elastically — no energy lost**

**2. ** Average kinetic energy is proportional to what?
a) It falls  b) Ethanoic (acetic) acid  c) Pleasant (fruity)  d) Kelvin temperature
**Answer: d) Kelvin temperature**

**3. ** What volume do ideal particles have?
a) Zinc  b) Oxidised (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻)  c) Negligible — treated as points  d) The anode dissolves, replacing deposited copper
**Answer: c) Negligible — treated as points**

**4. ** What attractions act between ideal particles?
a) They are rearranged, never created or destroyed  b) None  c) Solid wastes and agrochemicals  d) Prevention of waste
**Answer: b) None**
