# Video script — Kinetic molecular theory of gases

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The gas laws work, but why? Before reading, write the simplest possible rules for imaginary perfect gas particles that would still give real gas behaviour.

Gas particles are treated as points with negligible volume of their own.

They move randomly in straight lines and collide elastically — no energy is lost in collisions.

There are no attractions between gas particles in the ideal picture.

The average kinetic energy of the particles is proportional to the Kelvin temperature.

Now you. Say it back: How do ideal gas particles collide?
Answer: Elastically — no energy lost.
