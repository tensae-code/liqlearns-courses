# Hands-on — The named heats — solution, neutralisation, combustion, formation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Family tree of heats (paper only)
Draw the named heats as one family: solution, vaporisation, neutralisation, combustion, formation. Under each write its definition in five words or fewer, its printed number from the lesson, and its sign convention. The neutralisation branch must carry its reason, not only its number.

## Constant detective (paper only)
A student measures three strong-acid/strong-base neutralisations and gets −57.5, −57.9, −57.7 kJ/mol, then tries a weak acid and gets a different value. Write the report: why the first three agree, which reaction they all share, and what the weak acid's different value is telling her about its ionisation.
