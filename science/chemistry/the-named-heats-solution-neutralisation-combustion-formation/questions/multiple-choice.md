# The named heats — solution, neutralisation, combustion, formation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) NH₄NO₃ dissolving has ΔH = +25.7 kJ/mol, so the mixture…
a) Turns cold — the cold-pack effect  b) Warms strongly  c) Boils  d) Freezes solid at once
**Answer: a) Turns cold — the cold-pack effect**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Water's heat of vaporisation as printed is…
a) −57.7 kJ/mol  b) +44 kJ/mol  c) +25.7 kJ/mol  d) −285.9 kJ/mol
**Answer: b) +44 kJ/mol**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The heat of neutralisation for strong acid + strong base is…
a) Different for every pair  b) +57.7 kJ/mol  c) −57.7 kJ/mol, whichever pair is used  d) Zero — the ions cancel
**Answer: c) −57.7 kJ/mol, whichever pair is used**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The constancy of the neutralisation heat is explained by the single real reaction…
a) Na⁺ + Cl⁻ → NaCl  b) H₂ + O₂ → H₂O  c) Acid + base → salt  d) H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O
**Answer: d) H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The standard heat of combustion is measured in a…
a) Bomb calorimeter  b) Downs cell  c) Hofmann voltameter  d) pH meter
**Answer: a) Bomb calorimeter**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) By the stability rule, the compound with ΔH°f = −1207 kJ/mol against one with +90 is…
a) The less stable  b) The more stable  c) Equally stable  d) Not a compound
**Answer: b) The more stable**
