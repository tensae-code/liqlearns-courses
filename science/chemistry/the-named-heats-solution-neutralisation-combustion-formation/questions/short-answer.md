# The named heats — solution, neutralisation, combustion, formation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define heat of solution and give the book's pair of examples with signs.
**Answer: The heat absorbed or released when a substance dissolves (per mole as printed). NH₄NO₃: +25.7 kJ/mol, endothermic — the cold pack. NaOH: strongly exothermic on dissolving.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the four printed ΔH°c values of the lesson.
**Answer: H₂ −285.9; C (carbon) −393.5; propane −2219.2; methanol −726.6 kJ/mol.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) State the formation-heat stability rule in full.
**Answer: The more negative a compound's standard heat of formation, the more stable the compound at room temperature; compounds with positive ΔH°f tend to fall apart on their own.**
