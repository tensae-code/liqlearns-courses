# The named heats — solution, neutralisation, combustion, formation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) NH₄NO₃ dissolving is the _____ effect in a number: +25.7 kJ/mol.
**Answer: cold-pack**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Neutralisation's only real reaction is H⁺ + OH⁻ → _____.
**Answer: H₂O**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Combustion heats are measured in the _____ calorimeter.
**Answer: bomb**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The more _____ the formation heat, the more stable the compound.
**Answer: negative**
