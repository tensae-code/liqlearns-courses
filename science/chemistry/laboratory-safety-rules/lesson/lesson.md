# Laboratory safety rules

Do not try any chemical step yourself: in this course, laboratory work is observed and described first, and the safety rules come before any practical work.

## Laboratory safety rules

A full numbered rule set (21 rules) is taught as content: eye protection at all times; no unauthorised experiments or quantities; no smoking, eating or drinking;

know extinguisher and safety-shower locations; report every injury; never mouth-pipette;

never work alone; shoes, tied hair and lab apron; tidy bench and immediate spill clean-up;

boiling chips and never heating flammables over open flame; always pour acid into water; safe glass-into-stopper technique;

correct disposal and never returning reagents to the stock bottle; read the prelab before entering; never point a heated test tube at anyone;

cover cuts; and above all, think and plan ahead. Goggles, gloves and apron are explained as the protective kit.
