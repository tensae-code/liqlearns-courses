# Laboratory safety rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) Stoichiometry is named at the outset as the quantitative study of reactants and products.
b) Pupils close the section by drawing a spider diagram of chemistry's roles in their own locality.
c) an exercise has pupils find, for N, Na, Si, Fe, Zn, Kr and Ce, the valence electrons and the block of…
d) A full numbered rule set (21 rules) is taught as content: eye protection at all times;
**Answer: d) A full numbered rule set (21 rules) is taught as content: eye protection at all times;**

**2. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) no unauthorised experiments or quantities;
b) Atomic radius is half the distance between nuclei of two neighbouring atoms (in a metal network, or in a…
c) Alkynes, CₙH₂ₙ₋₂, carry a C≡C triple bond.
d) limestone removes silica impurities as slag;
**Answer: a) no unauthorised experiments or quantities;**

**3. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) this one idea is then used to explain radius, ionisation energy and the rest.
b) Three exceptions are taught by name: diamond's very high melting point (about 4027 °C), graphite…
c) know extinguisher and safety-shower locations;
d) calcium giving one electron to each of two chlorines (CaCl₂).
**Answer: c) know extinguisher and safety-shower locations;**

**4. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) tidy bench and immediate spill clean-up;
b) heat is energy that flows spontaneously from hotter to colder, never the reverse.
c) A molecular solute dissolves as whole molecules: a nonelectrolyte whose solution does not conduct.
d) Pupils practise writing configurations and drawing atoms for the first elements.
**Answer: a) tidy bench and immediate spill clean-up;**

**5. ** Which of these is true, as this lesson teaches?
a) acidity is measured practically with indicators or a pH meter.
b) macroscopic properties are measured directly, microscopic ones indirectly.
c) boiling chips and never heating flammables over open flame;
d) Lavoisier's 1789 law of conservation of mass: in a closed system matter is neither created nor destroyed;
**Answer: c) boiling chips and never heating flammables over open flame;**
