# Naming aldehydes and ketones

## Do it first
Formaldehyde and acetone are names everyone uses and no IUPAC committee chose. Before reading, say what endings the systematic names take instead.

Aldehyde IUPAC names end in –al (the carbonyl carbon is carbon 1).

Ketone IUPAC names end in –one, numbered to give the carbonyl the lowest position.

Common names persist: formaldehyde for methanal, acetone for propanone.
