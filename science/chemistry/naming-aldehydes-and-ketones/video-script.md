# Video script — Naming aldehydes and ketones

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Formaldehyde and acetone are names everyone uses and no IUPAC committee chose.

Aldehyde IUPAC names end in –al (the carbonyl carbon is carbon 1).

Ketone IUPAC names end in –one, numbered to give the carbonyl the lowest position.

Common names persist: formaldehyde for methanal, acetone for propanone.

Now you. Say it back: What ending do aldehyde names take?
Answer: –al.
