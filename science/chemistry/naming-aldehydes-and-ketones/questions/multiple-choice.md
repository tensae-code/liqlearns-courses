# Naming aldehydes and ketones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** What ending do aldehyde names take?
a) Molten or in solution — ions move  b) London dispersion  c) End-to-end (axial) overlap  d) –al
**Answer: d) –al**

**2. ** What ending do ketone names take?
a) Carbon 1  b) Carbon dioxide, CO₂  c) –one  d) A (symmetrical) ether
**Answer: c) –one**

**3. ** What is acetone's systematic name?
a) Mass deposited ∝ charge passed (m ∝ Q)  b) Propanone  c) Exactly neutralise each other  d) So electrons lost equal electrons gained
**Answer: b) Propanone**

**4. ** Which carbon is the aldehyde carbonyl counted as?
a) Carbon 1  b) PET or nylon  c) Chemical processes and pollutants in air, water, soil  d) Pesticide and fertiliser residues
**Answer: a) Carbon 1**
