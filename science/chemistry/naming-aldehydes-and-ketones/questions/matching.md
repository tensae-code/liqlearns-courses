# Naming aldehydes and ketones — matching

Match each question on the left with its answer on the right.

1. What ending do aldehyde names take? — d
2. What ending do ketone names take? — a
3. What is acetone's systematic name? — b
4. Which carbon is the aldehyde carbonyl counted as? — c

**Answer key:** 1–d: –al · 2–a: –one · 3–b: Propanone · 4–c: Carbon 1
