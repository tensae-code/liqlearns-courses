## Exothermic and endothermic

All reactions carry energy change; forms of energy interconvert, and a substance's internal energy is its potential + kinetic energy, with chemical energy stored as potential energy in bonds and intermolecular forces. Energy crosses between system and surroundings as heat and/or work.

Reactions giving heat out are exothermic; those taking heat in are endothermic; enthalpy change ΔH = H(products) − H(reactants) is negative for exothermic and positive for endothermic.
