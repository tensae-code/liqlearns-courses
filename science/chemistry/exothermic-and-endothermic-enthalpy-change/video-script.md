Today: **Exothermic and endothermic — enthalpy change**.

forms of energy interconvert, and a substance's internal energy is its potential + kinetic energy, with chemical energy stored as potential energy in bonds and…

Energy crosses between system and surroundings as heat and/or work.

Reactions giving heat out are exothermic;

those taking heat in are endothermic;

enthalpy change ΔH = H(products) − H(reactants) is negative for exothermic and positive for endothermic.
