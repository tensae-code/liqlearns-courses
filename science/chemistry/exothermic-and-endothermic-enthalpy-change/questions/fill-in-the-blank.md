# Exothermic and endothermic — enthalpy change — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1. ** Energy crosses between system and _____ as heat and/or work.
**Answer: surroundings**

**2. ** Reactions giving heat out are _____;
**Answer: exothermic**

**3. ** those taking heat in are _____;
**Answer: endothermic**

**4. ** enthalpy change ΔH = H(products) − H(reactants) is _____ for exothermic and positive for endothermic.
**Answer: negative**
