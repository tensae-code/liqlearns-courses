# Separating mixtures — decantation, filtration, distillation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Décantation separates muddy water by…
a) Boiling  b) Settling and pouring off  c) A magnet  d) Freezing
**Answer: b) Settling and pouring off**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In filtration, the solid left on the filter is the…
a) Filtrat  b) Distillate  c) Residue  d) Solution
**Answer: c) Residue**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In distillation, the part that vaporises first is…
a) The least volatile  b) The most volatile  c) The solid  d) The heaviest
**Answer: b) The most volatile**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The liquid that passes through a filter is called the…
a) Residue  b) Filtrat  c) Distillat  d) Sediment
**Answer: b) Filtrat**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The salt-marsh exercise: 40 L of seawater yield about…
a) 1 kg of salt  b) 10 kg of salt  c) No salt  d) 1 g of salt
**Answer: a) 1 kg of salt**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Distillation is the book's tool for…
a) A heterogeneous solid mixture  b) A homogeneous liquid mixture  c) A gas only  d) A pure substance
**Answer: b) A homogeneous liquid mixture**
