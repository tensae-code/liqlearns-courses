# Separating mixtures — decantation, filtration, distillation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Describe décantation in the book's terms.
**Answer: Leave the heterogeneous mixture (muddy water) at rest so the solid settles, then pour the clear liquid off the settled solid.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What decides between filtration and distillation for a liquid mixture?
**Answer: Whether anything is undissolved in it: an undissolved solid can be filtered (residue/filtrat); a homogeneous dissolved mixture must be distilled, the most volatile part vaporising first.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the distillate in the book's working case.
**Answer: Approximately pure water, condensed from the first vapour.**
