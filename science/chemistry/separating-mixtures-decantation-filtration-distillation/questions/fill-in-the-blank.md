# Separating mixtures — decantation, filtration, distillation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Settling a muddy mixture and pouring the liquid off is _____.
**Answer: décantation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The solid left on the filter is the _____.
**Answer: residue**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) In distillation the _____ volatile part vaporises first.
**Answer: most**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The liquid collected from the condenser is the _____.
**Answer: distillate**
