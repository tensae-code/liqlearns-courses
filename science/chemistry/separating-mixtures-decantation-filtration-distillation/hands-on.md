# Hands-on — Separating mixtures — decantation, filtration, distillation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Settle, pour, strain (kitchen-safe, water only)
With plain water and a spoon of soil in a jar: let it settle, decant the clear water into a second jar, then filter the remainder through a cloth. Label the three products with the book's names — decanted water, residue, filtrat — and write which step each name belongs to. Water and soil only; nothing is tasted.

## The marsh measure (paper only)
The book's exercise gives 40 L of seawater → 1 kg of salt. Work out the seawater needed for 5 kg of salt (200 L) and the salt expected from 120 L (3 kg), showing the proportion each time.
