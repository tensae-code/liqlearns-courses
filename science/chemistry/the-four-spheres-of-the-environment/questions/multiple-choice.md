# The four spheres of the environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Which sphere is all the water?
a) 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴  b) Henderson–Hasselbalch  c) The hydrosphere  d) H⁺ ions
**Answer: c) The hydrosphere**

**2. ** Which sphere is living things?
a) Mechanical or chemical  b) The biosphere  c) Ammonia  d) Firing in a kiln
**Answer: b) The biosphere**

**3. ** Which sphere is solid Earth?
a) The lithosphere  b) A photon  c) A probability description  d) Boxes and arrows
**Answer: a) The lithosphere**

**4. ** Which sphere is the gas envelope?
a) The minimum energy for reaction on collision  b) Temperature  c) Toward products  d) The atmosphere
**Answer: d) The atmosphere**
