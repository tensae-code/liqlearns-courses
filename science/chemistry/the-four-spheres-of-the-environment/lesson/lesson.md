# The four spheres of the environment

## Do it first
Air, water, rock and life are not separate stages — they trade matter constantly. Before reading, name the four spheres and one trade between any two.

[Picture 21776 — The four spheres in one diagram.]

The atmosphere is the envelope of gases around the Earth; the hydrosphere is all its water.

The lithosphere is the solid Earth — rocks and soil; the biosphere is all living things and where they live.

The four interact as chemical systems: gases dissolve in water, rocks weather into soil, life breathes the air — the environment is the exchanges.
