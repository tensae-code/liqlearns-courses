# Video script — The four spheres of the environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Air, water, rock and life are not separate stages — they trade matter constantly.

The atmosphere is the envelope of gases around the Earth; the hydrosphere is all its water.

The lithosphere is the solid Earth — rocks and soil; the biosphere is all living things and where they live.

The four interact as chemical systems: gases dissolve in water, rocks weather into soil, life breathes the air — the environment is the exchanges.

Now you. Say it back: Which sphere is all the water?
Answer: The hydrosphere.
