# The gas laws and the ideal gas equation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Boyle, Charles, Gay-Lussac, Avogadro
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Boyle's law: at constant temperature, pressure × volume is constant — squeeze the gas and its pressure rises in inverse proportion — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The combined and ideal gas laws
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The combined gas law merges the first three; the ideal gas law gathers everything into one equation: PV = nRT, with R the gas constant — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Dalton's partial pressures
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Dalton's law: the total pressure of a gas mixture is the sum of the partial pressures of its gases — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.

## Sub lesson 4 — Graham's law — diffusion and effusion
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Graham's law: lighter gases diffuse and effuse faster — rates compare inversely with the square roots of their molar masses — stated in the learner's own words and used on one fresh example.
