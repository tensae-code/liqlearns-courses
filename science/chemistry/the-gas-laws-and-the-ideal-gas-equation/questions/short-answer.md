# The gas laws and the ideal gas equation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) ** Write the ideal gas equation.
**Answer: PV = nRT**

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** State Boyle's law.
**Answer: At constant T, P × V is constant**

**3. (Sub lesson 4) ** What does Dalton's law sum?
**Answer: The partial pressures**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Which gas diffuses faster, light or heavy?
**Answer: The lighter gas (Graham's law)**
