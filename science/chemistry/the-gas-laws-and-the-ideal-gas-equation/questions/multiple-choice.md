# The gas laws and the ideal gas equation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Write the ideal gas equation.
a) Change in amount/concentration per unit time  b) No — it is dynamic, both ways continue  c) Initial, Change, Equilibrium  d) PV = nRT
**Answer: d) PV = nRT**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** State Boyle's law.
a) PV = nRT  b) It accepts a proton from water  c) At constant T, P × V is constant  d) Straight tails pack closely and attract strongly
**Answer: c) At constant T, P × V is constant**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** What does Dalton's law sum?
a) Soap or paper making  b) The partial pressures  c) Iron  d) It would form a corrosive mist
**Answer: b) The partial pressures**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) ** Which gas diffuses faster, light or heavy?
a) The lighter gas (Graham's law)  b) In the nucleus  c) The light's frequency  d) Multi-electron spectra
**Answer: a) The lighter gas (Graham's law)**
