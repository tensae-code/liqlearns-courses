The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Squeeze a sealed syringe and the push-back grows in an exact pattern.

Boyle's law: at constant temperature, pressure × volume is constant — squeeze the gas and its pressure rises in inverse proportion.

The combined gas law merges the first three; the ideal gas law gathers everything into one equation: PV = nRT, with R the gas constant.

Dalton's law: the total pressure of a gas mixture is the sum of the partial pressures of its gases.

Graham's law: lighter gases diffuse and effuse faster — rates compare inversely with the square roots of their molar masses.

Now you. Say it back: Write the ideal gas equation.
Answer: PV = nRT.
