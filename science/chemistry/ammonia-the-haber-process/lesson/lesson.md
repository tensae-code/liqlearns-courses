# Ammonia — the Haber process

## Do it first
Nitrogen from air, hydrogen from gas or water, one reversible reaction — and the same compromise you met in Grade 11, now at plant scale. Before reading, state the equation and pick your temperature dilemma in one line.

[Picture 21751 — The Haber loop with recycle arrows.]

The Haber process makes ammonia: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, a reversible, exothermic reaction.

Conditions are the Grade 11 compromise industrialised: high pressure (favours the side with fewer gas moles), a moderate temperature (rate versus yield), an iron catalyst, and recycling of unreacted N₂ and H₂.

Ammonia goes mainly to fertilisers, and onward to nitric acid and other nitrogen products.
