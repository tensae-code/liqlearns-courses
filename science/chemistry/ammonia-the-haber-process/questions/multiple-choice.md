# Ammonia — the Haber process — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Write the Haber equation.
a) Predict its properties  b) N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃  c) The main energy level (and orbital size)  d) Periods
**Answer: b) N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃**

**2. ** What catalyst is used?
a) Iron  b) How far the equilibrium lies toward products  c) Unreacted gases  d) The longest chain carrying –OH
**Answer: a) Iron**

**3. ** Why high pressure?
a) H⁺ ions  b) 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴  c) Henderson–Hasselbalch  d) Fewer gas moles on the product side
**Answer: d) Fewer gas moles on the product side**

**4. ** What is most ammonia used for?
a) Firing in a kiln  b) Mechanical or chemical  c) Fertilisers  d) Ammonia
**Answer: c) Fertilisers**
