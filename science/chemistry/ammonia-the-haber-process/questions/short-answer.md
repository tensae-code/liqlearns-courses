# Ammonia — the Haber process — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. ** Write the Haber equation.
**Answer: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃**

**2. ** What catalyst is used?
**Answer: Iron**

**3. ** Why high pressure?
**Answer: Fewer gas moles on the product side**

**4. ** What is most ammonia used for?
**Answer: Fertilisers**
