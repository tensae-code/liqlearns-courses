# Qualitative analysis of unknown substances

## Do it first
Before reading, say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is: qualitative analysis of unknown substances. Then read on — this is Kazakhstan's way of teaching it, as the Kazakhstan placements describe the books' own treatment.

Building the analysis plan and the theoretical results table before touching a reagent. (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, NIS trial, Ch. 5 §5.4 Qualitative analysis of unknown substances, printed p. 96).

## Sub lesson 3 — Module 2 — Kazakhstan's Grade 9 book (Grade 9, Express Publishing)

"Devise and carry out an experiment to identify the cations and anions of unknown substances" is a stated outcome (same proposed slug as Books 21/31). (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, Express Publishing, Module 2, printed p. 28).

## The key ideas

- Identifying an unknown substance by planning the analysis before touching a reagent: build the scheme — test for the cation and for the anion in turn — write the theoretical results table first, then run the tests and name the substance from the evidence.
- Building the analysis plan and the theoretical results table before touching a reagent. (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, NIS trial, Ch. 5 §5.4 Qualitative analysis of unknown substances, printed p. 96).
- Identifying an unknown salt from its two ions (same proposed slug as Book 21). (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, ed. Baikenov (English), Ch. 2 §2.3 Qualitative analysis of unknown salt solution, PDF p. 81).
- "Devise and carry out an experiment to identify the cations and anions of unknown substances" is a stated outcome (same proposed slug as Books 21/31). (Kazakhstan, Grade 9 — Grade 9, Express Publishing, Module 2, printed p. 28).
