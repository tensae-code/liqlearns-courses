# Radioactive isotopes at work — dating, medicine and tracers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The carbon-14 clock starts when…
a) The organism is born  b) The organism dies and stops exchanging carbon  c) The remains are dug up  d) The sample reaches a laboratory
**Answer: b) The organism dies and stops exchanging carbon**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon-14's half-life is…
a) 60,000 years  b) 5,700 years  c) 570 years  d) 11,400 years
**Answer: b) 5,700 years**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Remains with one quarter of the living carbon-14 level are about…
a) 5,700 years old  b) 2,850 years old  c) 11,400 years old  d) 60,000 years old
**Answer: c) 11,400 years old**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In radiotherapy, the radiation's job is to…
a) Picture the organ  b) Destroy diseased tissue  c) Warm the patient  d) Sterilise instruments
**Answer: b) Destroy diseased tissue**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The isotope the book names as taken up by the thyroid is…
a) Technetium-99  b) Cobalt-60  c) Iodine-131  d) Plutonium-238
**Answer: c) Iodine-131**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A pacemaker battery in the book runs on…
a) Carbon-14  b) Plutonium-238  c) Iridium-192  d) Technetium-99
**Answer: b) Plutonium-238**
