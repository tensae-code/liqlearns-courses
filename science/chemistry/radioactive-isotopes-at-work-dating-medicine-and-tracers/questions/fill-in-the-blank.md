# Radioactive isotopes at work — dating, medicine and tracers — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Carbon-14 dating reaches back to about _____ years and no further.
**Answer: 60,000**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Tiny implanted sources of iridium-192, palladium-103 and iodine-125 used in radiotherapy are called _____.
**Answer: seeds**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In tracer work the radiation is used as a _____, followed by detectors outside the body.
**Answer: signal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A plant tracer's journey is read with a _____ counter.
**Answer: Geiger**
