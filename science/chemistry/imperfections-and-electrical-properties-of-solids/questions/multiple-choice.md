# Imperfections and electrical properties of solids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In a Schottky defect…
a) An ion moves into a gap  b) Equal numbers of cations and anions are missing, and the crystal's density decreases  c) Extra metal atoms enter  d) The crystal changes colour
**Answer: b) Equal numbers of cations and anions are missing, and the crystal's density decreases**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A Frenkel defect leaves the density unchanged because…
a) The crystal melts  b) No ion leaves the crystal — one is only displaced into an interstitial site  c) The ions are equal in size  d) Electrons are added
**Answer: b) No ion leaves the crystal — one is only displaced into an interstitial site**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The yellow colour of NaCl heated in sodium vapour comes from…
a) Paint  b) F-centres — electrons trapped in anion vacancies  c) Frenkel defects  d) Sodium metal coating the surface
**Answer: b) F-centres — electrons trapped in anion vacancies**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) FeO is usually Fe₀.₉₃O–Fe₀.₉₆O rather than exactly FeO. The paying mechanism is…
a) Interstitial anions  b) Metal deficiency — some Fe²⁺ missing, other iron ions carrying the higher Fe³⁺ charge to balance  c) Sublimation  d) A Frenkel defect
**Answer: b) Metal deficiency — some Fe²⁺ missing, other iron ions carrying the higher Fe³⁺ charge to balance**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Silicon doped with phosphorus is…
a) p-type, holes moving  b) An insulator  c) n-type — the extra electrons are free to move  d) A conductor like copper
**Answer: c) n-type — the extra electrons are free to move**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A semiconductor differs from an insulator because its band gap is…
a) Larger  b) Small enough for heat or light to lift some electrons across it  c) Absent  d) Made of holes
**Answer: b) Small enough for heat or light to lift some electrons across it**
