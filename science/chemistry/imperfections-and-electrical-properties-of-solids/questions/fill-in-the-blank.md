# Imperfections and electrical properties of solids — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Equal numbers of missing cations and anions make a _____ defect.
**Answer: Schottky**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Electrons trapped in anion vacancies are called _____-centres.
**Answer: F**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Silicon doped with a group-13 element is a _____-type semiconductor.
**Answer: p**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Solids whose domains oppose and cancel are _____.
**Answer: antiferromagnetic**
