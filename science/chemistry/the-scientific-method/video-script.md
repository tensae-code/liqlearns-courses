Today: **The scientific method**.

Chemistry is an experimental, evidence-based science (the "central science" again, now as method).

One experiment never proves a hypothesis;

repeated agreement without discrepancy raises it to a theory.

The steps are first met through a sealed-bag guessing activity that maps each move onto its scientific term.
