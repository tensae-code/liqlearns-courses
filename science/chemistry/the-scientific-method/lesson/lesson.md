## The scientific method

Chemistry is an experimental, evidence-based science (the "central science" again, now as method). The method runs: (i) observation and formulation of a question, (ii) data collection and hypothesis, (iii) testing the hypothesis by experiment, (iv) analysis and conclusion — accept, reject or modify and retest. One experiment never proves a hypothesis;

repeated agreement without discrepancy raises it to a theory. The steps are first met through a sealed-bag guessing activity that maps each move onto its scientific term.
