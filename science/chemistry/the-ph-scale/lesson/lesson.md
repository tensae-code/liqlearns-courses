## The pH scale

pH = −log[H⁺] measures acidity on a scale pictured from battery acid and stomach acid through neutral water to ammonia solution, bleach and drain cleaner, with everyday substances placed on it (lemon, tomato, coffee, milk, blood, baking soda, soap). pOH = −log[OH⁻], and the water constant ties them: Kw = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 1×10⁻¹⁴ at 25 °C, so pH + pOH = 14; acidity is measured practically with indicators or a pH meter.
