# Plant reproduction by cuttings, grafting and offshoots — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the artificial ways in the book's order.
**Answer: By cuttings (pens) and grafting; by offshoots (suckers); and, in the reading, by leaves.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How do a cutting and a graft differ?
**Answer: A cutting is planted on its own to grow; a graft is joined onto another living plant so it grows there.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the activity book set side by side?
**Answer: Natural and artificial reproduction — the artificial set (cuttings, grafting, offshoots) worked by hand against the natural ways.**
