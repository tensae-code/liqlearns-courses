# Plant reproduction by cuttings, grafting and offshoots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Artificial reproduction is reproduction…
a) Plants do alone in the wild only  b) People bring about — the hand places, the plant grows  c) Machines do  d) That never works
**Answer: b) People bring about — the hand places, the plant grows**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A cutting — the book's word is a pen — is…
a) A seed  b) A piece cut from a plant and planted to grow  c) A kind of leaf  d) A root only
**Answer: b) A piece cut from a plant and planted to grow**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Grafting is…
a) Watering a plant  b) Joining a piece of one plant onto another so it grows there  c) Cutting a plant down  d) Planting a seed deeper
**Answer: b) Joining a piece of one plant onto another so it grows there**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Offshoots are also called…
a) Pens  b) Suckers  c) Seeds  d) Flowers
**Answer: b) Suckers**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The book's reading adds reproduction by…
a) Roots  b) Leaves  c) Stems only  d) Flowers
**Answer: b) Leaves**
