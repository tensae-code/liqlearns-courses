## Sub lesson 1 — Cuttings and grafting — the grower's ways

After natural reproduction, Iraq's Grade 6 book teaches **artificial reproduction**: the ways people make plants reproduce. First, **by cuttings** — the book's own word is pens: a piece cut from a plant and planted to grow — and **by grafting**, joining a piece of one plant onto another so it grows there.

Both are artificial in the book's sense: a person does the placing. The cutting is planted by a hand; the graft is joined by a hand. The plant does the growing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Offshoots — and reproduction by leaves

Second, **by offshoots — suckers**: the new growths a plant sends out beside itself, which can be taken and grown on as new plants. The book's reading adds a third road: **reproduction by leaves** — a leaf itself giving rise to a new plant.

The Grade 6 activity book works the whole artificial set by hand: **cuttings, grafting and offshoots** — natural and artificial reproduction set side by side, as the pupil's book teaches them in order.

## The key ideas

- **Artificial reproduction** is reproduction people bring about: by **cuttings (pens)**, by **grafting**, and by **offshoots (suckers)**.
- A **cutting** is a piece cut from a plant and planted; a **graft** joins a piece of one plant onto another.
- **Offshoots** are new growths beside the plant, taken and grown on; some plants even reproduce **by leaves**.
