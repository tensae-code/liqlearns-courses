# Hands-on — Plant reproduction by cuttings, grafting and offshoots

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Try a cutting
With an adult's leave, take a cutting from a plant that grows easily, plant it, and keep it watered. Record what it does week by week — the activity book's own work, at home.

## Three roads drawn
Draw the three artificial roads: a cutting planted, a graft joined, an offshoot taken. Label each road with its name — cuttings (pens), grafting, offshoots (suckers).
