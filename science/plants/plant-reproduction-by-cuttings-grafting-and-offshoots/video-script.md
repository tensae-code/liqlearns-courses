Today: **Plant reproduction by cuttings, grafting and offshoots** — Iraq's Grade 6 artificial-reproduction chapters.

Artificial reproduction is the reproduction people bring about. By cuttings — pens — a piece cut and planted. By grafting — a piece joined onto another plant. By offshoots — suckers — the growths beside the plant, taken and grown on. And the reading adds reproduction by leaves. The hand places; the plant grows.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
