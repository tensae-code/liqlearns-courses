# The flower and the fruit — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The flower — four whorls, flower types and inflorescences.
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 9 book teaches the flower's **four whorls**: the **calyx of sepals** (gamosepalous when the sepals are joined), the **stamens free or fused** (monadelphous, diadelphous and more), and the rest of the flower's plan. It sorts flowers **by ovary position — epigynous, perigynous, hypogynous** — and names the **inflorescences: spike, raceme, umbel, panicle** — the ways flowers are gathered on a plant.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The fruit and the seed — the transformed ripe ovary.
**Answer:** The same book teaches the **fruit as the transformed ripe ovary**: **dry indehiscent fruits** (achene, samara, the caryopsis of wheat, rice and maize), **dehiscent** fruits (capsule, legume, silique), **aggregate fruits** (strawberry) and **multiple fruits**. Inside is the **seed — the angiosperms' main dispersal organ** — holding the embryo, the cotyledons and the testa (seed coat).

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The flower — four whorls, flower types and inflorescences → The fruit and the seed — the transformed ripe ovary — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
