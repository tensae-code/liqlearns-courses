# The flower and the fruit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The flower — four whorls, flower types and inflorescences  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The flower — four whorls, flower types and inflorescences**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The fruit and the seed — the transformed ripe ovary  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The fruit and the seed — the transformed ripe ovary**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the flower and the fruit — the flower's four whorls, its types and inflorescences, and the fruit as the transformed ripe ovary, with the seed as the angiosperms' main dispersal organ. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Nicaragua, Grade 9 — *Ciencias Naturales, Noveno grado*, printed pp. 36–39  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
