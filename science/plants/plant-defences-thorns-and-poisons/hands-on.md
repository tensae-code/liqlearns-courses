# Hands-on — Plant defences — thorns and poisons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Defence survey
On a walk with an adult, list plants you pass and note any defence you can see — thorns, sharp edges, tough leaves. Do not touch thorny plants. Which defence is commonest near you, and is your list more like Iceland's (few thorns) or not?

## Grazer's choice
Draw a grazer facing two bushes, one thorny, one soft. Write the grazer's choice and its reason under the picture, in the grazer's own imagined words.
