# Plant defences — thorns and poisons — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Thorns are a plant defence: plants grown with thorns are unpopular with grazers.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** A plant cannot run — the thorn is how it stands its ground against being eaten.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is a thorn called a defence if the plant never moves?
**Answer:** Because it does the defender's work without moving: it makes the plant costly to eat, so grazers pass it by.
