# Life cycles of non-flowering plants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is this cycle taught separately from the flowering plants' cycle?
**Answer: Because it is a different, complete cycle — growth, maturity and new plants, with no flower stage — and the book gives it its own chapter.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the activity add to the chapter?
**Answer: The same cycle worked with the hands, stage by stage.**
