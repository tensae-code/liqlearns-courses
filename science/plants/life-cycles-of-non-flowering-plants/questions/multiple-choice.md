# Life cycles of non-flowering plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Non-flowering plants…
a) Have no life cycle  b) Live a complete cycle without flowering  c) Flower once a year  d) Are not alive
**Answer: b) Live a complete cycle without flowering**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Iraq's Grade 4 book teaches this cycle…
a) Inside the flowering chapter only  b) As a chapter of its own, before the flowering cycle  c) Only in the activity book  d) In Grade 1
**Answer: b) As a chapter of its own, before the flowering cycle**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The activity book's part is…
a) A song  b) An activity on the same life cycles, stage by stage  c) A test with no teaching  d) A map
**Answer: b) An activity on the same life cycles, stage by stage**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) A non-flowering plant makes new plants…
a) Only by flowers  b) Of its own kind, without flowering  c) Never  d) Only in water
**Answer: b) Of its own kind, without flowering**
