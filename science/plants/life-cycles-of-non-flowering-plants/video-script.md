Today: **Life cycles of non-flowering plants** — a chapter of its own in Iraq's Grade 4 book.

A plant does not need flowers to live its whole cycle. Non-flowering plants grow, mature and make new plants of their kind without ever flowering — and the book teaches their cycle separately, before the flowering plants' cycle. The activity book works it stage by stage.
