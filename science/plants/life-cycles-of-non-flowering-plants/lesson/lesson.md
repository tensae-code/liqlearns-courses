# Life cycles of non-flowering plants

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — the life cycles of plants that do not flower, as Iraq's Grade 4 pupil's book teaches them as a chapter of their own, with the activity book's matching activity. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Iraq's way in — its Ministry of Education *Science* books (the Primary Science Book Series) teach this thing at the places named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Plants that live their cycle without flowers

Iraq's Grade 4 book gives **non-flowering plants** a life-cycle chapter of their own — separate from the flowering plants' cycle that follows it. A plant does not need flowers to live its whole cycle: the non-flowering plants grow, mature and make new plants of their kind **without ever flowering**.

That separateness is the book's own structure, and it is the lesson: cycle first, kind by kind. The flowering cycle is another lesson; this one keeps to the plants whose cycle runs flower-free.

## Sub lesson 2 — The activity book's matching work

The Grade 4 activity book carries the same chapter as an **activity on the life cycles of non-flowering plants** — the cycle worked with the hands, stage by stage, as the pupil's book teaches it.

Worked in order, the activity fixes the point the chapter makes: a complete life cycle, with no flower anywhere in it.

## The key ideas

- **Non-flowering plants have life cycles of their own** — they grow, mature and make new plants without flowering.
- Iraq's Grade 4 book teaches this cycle as its own chapter, before the flowering plants' cycle.
- The activity book works the same cycle as an activity, stage by stage.
