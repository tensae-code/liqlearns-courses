# Hands-on — Life cycles of non-flowering plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stage by stage
Draw the cycle of a non-flowering plant as a circle of stages. Mark clearly: no flower stage — and write where new plants of the kind come from.

## Two cycles compared
Draw two circles side by side: non-flowering, and flowering (from the flowering-plant life-cycle lesson). Ring the stage that appears in only one of them.
