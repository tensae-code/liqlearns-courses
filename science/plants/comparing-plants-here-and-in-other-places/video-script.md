Today: **Comparing plants — here and in other places** — South Sudan's way, from its Ministry of General Education and Instruction Primary Science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

South Sudan's Primary 1 book compares the plants of the local area with plants from other parts of the world: the two sets are looked at side by side for their **similarities** — the parts and ways of growing they share, wherever they grow.

The **differences** are taught with their reason: plants grow in different areas **due to the weather conditions of the place**. A plant of a dry place and a plant of a wet place differ because their homes differ — the comparison is the lesson, and the weather is its explanation.
