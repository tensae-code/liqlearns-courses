# Comparing plants — here and in other places — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Plants here and plants far away — what is the same.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 1 book compares the plants of the local area with plants from other parts of the world: the two sets are looked at side by side for their **similarities** — the parts and ways of growing they share, wherever they grow.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: What is different — and weather is the reason.
**Answer:** The **differences** are taught with their reason: plants grow in different areas **due to the weather conditions of the place**. A plant of a dry place and a plant of a wet place differ because their homes differ — the comparison is the lesson, and the weather is its explanation.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Plants here and plants far away — what is the same → What is different — and weather is the reason — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
