# Comparing plants — here and in other places — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Plants here and plants far away — what is the same  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Plants here and plants far away — what is the same**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What is different — and weather is the reason  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What is different — and weather is the reason**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: comparing plants — here and in other places. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The books behind this lesson are…
a) South Sudan's Primary Science books (Ministry of General Education and Instruction, Republic of South Sudan)  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
