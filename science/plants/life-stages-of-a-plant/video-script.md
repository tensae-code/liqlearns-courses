The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Life stages of a plant**.

A plant does not arrive finished. Djibouti's Grade 3 guide tells its life in great stages: it begins, it grows, it reaches its full grown form and makes its own seeds. And the guide sets those stages beside the stages of a human life — your life, too, is told in stages. Two different lives, one pattern: stage by stage.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
