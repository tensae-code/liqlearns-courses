# Hands-on — Life stages of a plant

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stage watch
Pick one plant you can visit again and again — at home, at school, on your street. Draw it as it is now and label its stage. Visit it over the coming weeks and add a new drawing whenever it changes stage: new growth, flowers, seeds. Your page becomes its life in stages.

## Two lives in stages
On one page, draw two rows. Top row: a plant's great stages. Bottom row: a human life's stages — baby, child, grown. Draw arrows along each row. Under the page, write one sentence on what the comparison teaches.
