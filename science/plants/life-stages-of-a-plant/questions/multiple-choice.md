# Life stages of a plant — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant's life…
a) Arrives finished, all at once  b) Runs through great stages  c) Has no beginning  d) Never changes
**Answer: b) Runs through great stages**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At its full grown stage, a plant…
a) Stops being alive  b) Flowers and makes its own seeds  c) Turns into an animal  d) Loses its leaves for ever
**Answer: b) Flowers and makes its own seeds**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Djibouti's guide compares the plant's stages with…
a) The stages of a rock  b) The stages of a human life  c) The seasons only  d) The stages of a river
**Answer: b) The stages of a human life**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The comparison teaches that…
a) Plants and people live exactly the same life  b) Both lives are told in stages, each stage preparing the next  c) People grow from seeds  d) Plants grow old in one day
**Answer: b) Both lives are told in stages, each stage preparing the next**
