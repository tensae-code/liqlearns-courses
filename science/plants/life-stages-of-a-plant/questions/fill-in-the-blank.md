# Life stages of a plant — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant does not arrive finished — its life runs through great _____.
**Answer: stages**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) At its full grown stage, a plant flowers and makes its own _____.
**Answer: seeds**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The guide compares a plant's stages with the stages of a _____ life.
**Answer: human**
