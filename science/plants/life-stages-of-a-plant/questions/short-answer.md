# Life stages of a plant — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tell a plant's life in stages.
**Answer: It begins small and new, it grows, it reaches its full grown form and makes its own seeds, and its life completes its round.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the guide compare a plant's life with a human life?
**Answer: Both lives run through great stages — beginning small, growing, reaching the full grown form — so each life is told stage by stage, even though the two lives are different.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is different at each stage of a plant's life?
**Answer: Its size and shape and its job: beginning, growing, and full grown and seed-making are different stages of the same living thing.**
