# Lesson plan — Life stages of a plant

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Plants · **Lesson:** Life stages of a plant
**Place in the curriculum:** Plants — appended to Unit 8 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the great stages of a plant's life, from its beginning to its full growth — and the comparison Djibouti's own way sets beside it: the stages of a human life — taught in Djibouti's Grade 3 Sciences Expérimentales teacher guide as one comparison lesson
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The great stages of a plant's life** — A plant does not arrive finished. Its life runs through great stages — it begins, it grows, it reaches its full grown form and makes its own seeds, and its life completes its round. Each stage is a different size, shape and job for the same living thing.
2. **Compared with the stages of a human life** — Djibouti's guide sets the plant's stages beside our own: a human life also runs through great stages — beginning small and helpless, growing, reaching the full grown form. The comparison is the teaching: two different lives, both told in stages.
