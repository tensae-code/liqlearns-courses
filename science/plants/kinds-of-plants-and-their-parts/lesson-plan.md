# Lesson plan — Kinds of plants and their parts

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Plants · **Lesson:** Kinds of plants and their parts
**Place in the curriculum:** Plants — the seventh title on the Science ladder (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** plants come in kinds — big trees, medium shrubs, small plants — and every plant has its parts: root, stem or trunk, leaf, flower (and fruit); our crops sort into cereals, pulses, garden vegetables and fruits
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own woreda — the plants are first met outside the door, named where they grow.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Weather and Seasons — plants live by the seasons the learner can now name. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name plant kinds by size: big trees (grar, warka, eucalyptus, wanza), medium shrubs, small plants (grass, teff).
2. Name the parts of a plant: root, stem/trunk, leaf, flower — and fruit, as the guide's labelled picture adds.
3. Sort crops into the book's four groups: cereals, pulses, garden vegetables and fruits.

## What you need
A real plant to dig beside (with leave) or a potted plant; plants of three sizes to visit on a walk.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls every green thing a tree → kinds are by size and build: trees big with trunks, shrubs medium, small plants low like grass and teff.
- Forgets the root because it is hidden → dig beside one plant (with leave) or use the picture: the root is the part that starts the plant's story.
