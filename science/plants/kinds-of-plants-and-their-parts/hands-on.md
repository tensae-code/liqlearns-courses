# Hands-on — Kinds of plants and their parts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Parts portrait
Draw one plant from your street as a portrait: every part drawn and labelled. Under it, write its kind and why.

## Three sizes walk
Walk and find the three kinds in order — a big tree, a shrub, a small plant — and sketch each in your book with its height against your own body.

## Seed-to-part
Plant a bean in a clear-sided cup against the glass. Watch the root come first: the parts arrive in an order — record it.
