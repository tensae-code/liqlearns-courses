# Kinds of plants and their parts — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. Crops sort into cereals, pulses, garden vegetables and ___.**
**Answer: fruits**

**2. The stem of a tree is called its ___.**
**Answer: trunk**
**3. The male part of the flower is the ______ (ወንዴ ጽጌ).**
**Answer: stamen**

**4. Cross-pollination happens between ______ plants.**
**Answer: different**
