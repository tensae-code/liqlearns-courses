# Kinds of plants and their parts — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. A tree's stem is called its trunk.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Teff is a small plant.**
**Answer: True**
**3. Ferns have true roots, stems and leaves.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Angiosperms bear naked seed with no fruit.**
**Answer: False**

**5. Algae have true roots and leaves.**
**Answer: False**
