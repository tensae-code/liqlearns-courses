# Kinds of plants and their parts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which list is the parts of a plant?**
a) root, stem, leaf, flower  b) horn, hoof, tail  c) door, roof, wall
**Answer: a) root, stem, leaf, flower**

**2. Eucalyptus and wanza are examples of —**
a) small plants  b) big trees  c) pulses
**Answer: b) big trees**

**3. Which is a medium kind between trees and small plants?**
a) shrubs  b) cereals  c) flowers
**Answer: a) shrubs**

**4. Which crop group does maize belong to?**
a) pulses  b) cereals  c) fruits
**Answer: b) cereals**
**5. Fungi feed as parasites, symbionts or —**
a) food-makers  b) decomposers (saprophytes)  c) pollinators
**Answer: b) decomposers (saprophytes)**

**6. Mosses and liverworts are called pioneers because they —**
a) grow tallest  b) are first on bare ground, holding it against erosion  c) flower first
**Answer: b) are first on bare ground, holding it against erosion**

**7. A gymnosperm's reproductive organ is the —**
a) flower  b) cone (ቅንብብ)  c) fruit
**Answer: b) cone (ቅንብብ)**

**8. Pollination is pollen moving from —**
a) root to leaf  b) anther to stigma  c) seed to fruit
**Answer: b) anther to stigma**

**9. Maize is a —**
a) two-cotyledon seed  b) one-cotyledon seed  c) seedless plant
**Answer: b) one-cotyledon seed**

**10. Yeast is —**
a) a many-celled plant  b) a single-celled fungus  c) an alga
**Answer: b) a single-celled fungus**
