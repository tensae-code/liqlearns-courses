# Kinds of plants and their parts — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: sort local plants into the three size-kinds, name all the parts on a real plant, and place crops in the four groups
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5/6 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also separates seedless from seeded plants, names algae/fungi/bryophytes/ferns with their marks, separates gymnosperms from angiosperms, labels the flower's four parts in Amharic and English, defines pollination and its two kinds, and sorts seeds by cotyledons.
