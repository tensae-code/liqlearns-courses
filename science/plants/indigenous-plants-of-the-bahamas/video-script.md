# Video script — Indigenous plants of The Bahamas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Indigenous plants of The Bahamas** — the Bahamas' way: learn plants from the islands' own flora.

**Indigenous** means belonging to a place by nature — the plant was growing there without anybody bringing it or planting it. The Bahamas has its own indigenous **flowering plants**.
And the science is in the looking: name the flower, the leaf, the stem, the fruit, and the ground it grows in — describe the plant so exactly that someone else could walk out and find the very same plant again.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
