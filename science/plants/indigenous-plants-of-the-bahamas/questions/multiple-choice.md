# Indigenous plants of The Bahamas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Indigenous means a plant…
a) Is the most common plant in town  b) Belongs to a place by nature, growing there without people bringing or planting it  c) Is grown in a pot  d) Has the prettiest flowers
**Answer: b) Belongs to a place by nature, growing there without people bringing or planting it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant loved in every yard in town, first brought by people for gardens, is…
a) Indigenous  b) Not indigenous, however common it has become  c) Indigenous only in summer  d) Impossible to describe
**Answer: b) Not indigenous, however common it has become**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The Bahamas' indigenous flowering plants grow…
a) Only in flower pots  b) Only overseas  c) In the islands' own settings — sandy and rocky ground, wetlands and pine lands  d) Only in gardens
**Answer: c) In the islands' own settings — sandy and rocky ground, wetlands and pine lands**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Identifying a plant means…
a) Watering it  b) Naming it from what can be seen — flower, leaf, stem, fruit and place  c) Picking its flowers  d) Planting it in a yard
**Answer: b) Naming it from what can be seen — flower, leaf, stem, fruit and place**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A good description of a plant…
a) Says only that it is pretty  b) Is one word long  c) Lets someone else find the same plant and know it again  d) Never mentions where it grows
**Answer: c) Lets someone else find the same plant and know it again**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these belongs in a plant description?
a) The finder's favourite colour  b) The price of the plant  c) The flower's colour and size, the leaf's shape, and the ground it grows in  d) The name of the road only
**Answer: c) The flower's colour and size, the leaf's shape, and the ground it grows in**
