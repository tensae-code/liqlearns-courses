# Indigenous plants of The Bahamas — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant that belongs to a place by nature is _____ .
**Answer: indigenous**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The Bahamas' own indigenous plants include _____ plants, known by their flowers.
**Answer: flowering**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) To _____ a plant is to name it from what can be seen.
**Answer: identify**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A description must be exact enough for someone else to _____ the same plant again.
**Answer: find**
