# Indigenous plants of The Bahamas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Say in one sentence what makes a plant indigenous.
**Answer: It belongs to the place by nature — it grows there wild, from its own seeds, without people having brought or planted it.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is a plant growing wild not automatically indigenous?
**Answer: Because it may be an escape from someone's garden — a brought plant growing wild is still a brought plant; indigenous is about belonging by nature, not about where a stem happens to stand.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the five things identification works from.
**Answer: The flower, the leaf, the stem, the fruit and seeds, and the place where it grows.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What is wrong with describing a plant as only 'a yellow flower'?
**Answer: It fits thousands of plants; a real description adds the flower's size and arrangement, the leaf, the stem and the place, exactly enough for someone else to find the same plant.**
