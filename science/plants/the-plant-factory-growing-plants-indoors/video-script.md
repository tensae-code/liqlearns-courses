# Video script — The plant factory — growing plants indoors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **The plant factory — growing plants indoors** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Imagine a farm with no sky: the light is lamps, the rain is measured water from machines, the food is given drop by drop. That is a plant factory — every need a plant has, supplied on purpose.
Now design your own. List the needs, give each one a supplier, build it small — and let your plants judge the design.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
