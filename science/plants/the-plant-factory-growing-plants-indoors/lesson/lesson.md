# The plant factory — growing plants indoors

## Do it first
Before you read: say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is — the plant factory: plants grown indoors with everything they need — light, water, food — supplied by machines, and the learner's own factory designed and built. Then read on: this is China's way in — its 科学 (Science) textbooks teach this thing at the places named in the sources, and this lesson follows their order.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a plant factory is

A **plant factory** grows plants indoors, where nothing is left to the weather: the light comes from lamps, the water and food are measured and given by machines, and the air itself is managed. China's Grade 6 book closes its course here — entering the plant factory means meeting every need of a plant, supplied on purpose.

The factory is the plant-needs lesson turned into a building: what a plant needs to grow, the factory provides — each need named, each supply deliberate.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designing and building your own plant factory

The book's final project hands the factory to the learner: **design and build your own plant factory**. The design lists a plant's needs one by one and assigns each a supplier — light from a lamp, water on a schedule or a drip, food measured in.

Built small, the learner's factory is tested by its plants: a plant that grows well indoors, with no field and no season, proves the design supplied what the plant asked for.

## The key ideas

- A **plant factory** grows plants indoors with every need supplied by machines — light from lamps, water and food measured and given, air managed.
- The factory is the plant-needs lesson as a building: **each need named, each supply deliberate** — nothing left to weather or season.
- The learner's own factory is **proved by its plants**: growth indoors shows the design supplied what the plants needed.

## Do It Yourself
1. List everything a plant needs to grow, and beside each need write how a plant factory supplies it and how a field supplies it.
2. Design your own small plant factory on paper — a box, a lamp, a water supply — and label each part with the need it serves.
3. Teach the hardest part of this lesson to someone at home in five sentences — why a factory can grow plants with no season.
