# The plant factory — growing plants indoors — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A plant _____ grows plants indoors with their needs supplied by machines.
**Answer: factory**

**2.** In the factory, light comes from _____.
**Answer: lamps**

**3.** Each need is named and each _____ is deliberate.
**Answer: supply**

**4.** The factory is proved by its _____ growing well.
**Answer: plants**
