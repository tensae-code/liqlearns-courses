# The plant factory — growing plants indoors — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is a plant factory?
**Answer:** A place that grows plants indoors, where everything the plants need — light, water, food, managed air — is supplied by machines, on purpose, with nothing left to the weather.

**2.** How does the factory supply each of a plant's needs?
**Answer:** Light from lamps, water and food measured and given by machines, and the air itself managed — each need named and each supply deliberate.

**3.** What does the learner's own design have to do, need by need?
**Answer:** List the plant's needs one by one and assign each a supplier — lamp for light, a schedule or drip for water, measured food — before building.

**4.** How is a learner's factory tested?
**Answer:** By its plants: a plant that grows well indoors, with no field and no season, proves the design supplied what the plant asked for.
