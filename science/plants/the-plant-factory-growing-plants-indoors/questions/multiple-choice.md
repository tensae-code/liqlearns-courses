# The plant factory — growing plants indoors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant factory grows plants…
a) Indoors, with their needs supplied by machines  b) Only in open fields  c) Without any light  d) Without water
**Answer: a) Indoors, with their needs supplied by machines**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a plant factory, light comes from…
a) The moon  b) Lamps  c) Candles only  d) Nothing — plants need no light
**Answer: b) Lamps**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) What is left to the weather inside a plant factory?
a) Everything  b) Only the rain  c) Nothing — light, water, food and air are all supplied on purpose  d) Only the seasons
**Answer: c) Nothing — light, water, food and air are all supplied on purpose**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The learner's design assigns…
a) One machine to do everything unnamed  b) The weather to help  c) Nothing — plants supply themselves  d) Each of the plant's needs its own supplier
**Answer: d) Each of the plant's needs its own supplier**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A learner's plant factory is proved by…
a) Its plants growing well indoors  b) Its size alone  c) Its colour  d) Being finished quickly
**Answer: a) Its plants growing well indoors**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The plant factory is the plant-needs lesson turned into…
a) A story  b) A building  c) A song  d) An exam
**Answer: b) A building**
