# Lesson plan — The plant factory — growing plants indoors

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Plants · **Lesson:** The plant factory — growing plants indoors
**Place in the curriculum:** Plants — appended to Unit 8 (Plants) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the plant factory: plants grown indoors with everything they need — light, water, food — supplied by machines, and the learner's own factory designed and built
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **What a plant factory is** — A **plant factory** grows plants indoors, where nothing is left to the weather: the light comes from lamps, the water and food are measured and given by machines, and the air itself is managed.
2. **Designing and building your own plant factory** — The book's final project hands the factory to the learner: **design and build your own plant factory**.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: see the lesson's own opening. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain sub lesson 1 in their own words: What a plant factory is.
2. Explain sub lesson 2 in their own words: Designing and building your own plant factory.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. For any practical part: kitchen and household materials only where the hands-on says so, with adult help where the hands-on says so.
