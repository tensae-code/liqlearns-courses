# Hands-on — The plant factory — growing plants indoors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A one-plant factory
Grow one seedling indoors under a lamp with measured water, beside a matching seedling on a windowsill. Keep both for two weeks and compare: say which factory part — light or water — made any difference you see.

## Need by need
Take a healthy indoor plant and audit it like a factory inspector: light — where from? water — how much, how often? food — what kind? Write the audit as a chart with one row per need.
