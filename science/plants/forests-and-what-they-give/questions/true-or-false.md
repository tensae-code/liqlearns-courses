# Forests and what they give — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. Plants grow and reproduce.**
**Answer: True**

**2. A plant moves from place to place to find food.**
**Answer: False**
**3. Church forests preserve Ethiopia's oldest plant kinds.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Desert vegetation grows where rainfall is above 2,000 mm.**
**Answer: False**
