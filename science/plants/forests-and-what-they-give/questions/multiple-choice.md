# Forests and what they give — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A plant is a living thing that —**
a) makes its own food and stays in one place  b) walks to find food  c) eats animals
**Answer: a) makes its own food and stays in one place**

**2. Which is a gift of forests in the book's list?**
a) paper  b) rain-making machines  c) telephones
**Answer: a) paper**

**3. Forests shelter —**
a) only people  b) wild animals  c) nobody
**Answer: b) wild animals**

**4. Visitors come to forests and parks for rare animals such as —**
a) the walia and the red fox  b) cows and hens  c) house cats
**Answer: a) the walia and the red fox**
**5. Savanna grassland lies mostly at —**
a) above 3,000 m  b) 250–2,300 m  c) below sea level
**Answer: b) 250–2,300 m**

**6. Ethiopia's dense forest cover fifty years ago was —**
a) 3%  b) 11–15%  c) 30–40%
**Answer: c) 30–40%**

**7. The recovery programme is called —**
a) the Green Legacy  b) the Blue Economy  c) Masai Mara
**Answer: a) the Green Legacy**

**8. Uganda's forest cover fell from 24% to —**
a) 12.4%  b) 30%  c) 3%
**Answer: a) 12.4%**

**9. Mangrove vegetation in Amharic is —**
a) ረግረጋማ  b) ዝልትነት  c) መቃን
**Answer: a) ረግረጋማ**

**10. Which is one of the book's three forest protections?**
a) clearing faster  b) controlling forest fire  c) ending all parks
**Answer: b) controlling forest fire**
