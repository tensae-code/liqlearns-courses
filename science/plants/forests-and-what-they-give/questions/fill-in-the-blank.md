# Forests and what they give — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. Plants make their own ___.**
**Answer: food**

**2. Furniture, paper and building material come from ___ in the forest.**
**Answer: trees**
**3. Fifty years ago 30–40%; it fell to about ______%; the Green Legacy lifted it to 11–15%.**
**Answer: 3**

**4. Kenya's famous savanna is the Masai ______.**
**Answer: Mara**
