# Forests and what they give — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: give the four-mark definition of a plant and list the forest's gifts, including shelter and tourism.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5/6 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also names the four vegetation kinds by altitude, recites Ethiopia's cover story (30–40% → 3% → 11–15%, Green Legacy), states Uganda's and Tanzania's warning numbers, and names the three forest protections.
