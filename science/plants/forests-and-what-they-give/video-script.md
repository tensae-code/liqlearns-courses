# Video script — Forests and what they give

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **plant** is alive, makes its **own food**, stays in **one place**, grows and multiplies.
A **forest** gives building wood, paper, furniture — and a **home for wild animals**.
Visitors even pay to see the walia and the red fox. The forest works standing still.
