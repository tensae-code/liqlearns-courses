# Hands-on — Forests and what they give

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Forest edge inventory
At a real forest or grove edge, inventory: trees standing, birds heard, animals sheltered, shade given. Four columns, ten minutes, no touching — the forest works while you watch.

## From forest to furniture
Choose one wooden thing at home and write its life backwards: chair → plank → tree → seed. Every step was a gift on the book's list.
