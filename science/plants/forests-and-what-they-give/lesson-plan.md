# Lesson plan — Forests and what they give

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Plants · **Lesson:** Forests and what they give
**Place in the curriculum:** Plants — the plant lessons climb to the forest: many plants together, and their gifts (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** plants are living things that make their own food, stay in one place, grow and reproduce — and forests give building, paper, furniture, shelter for wild animals and tourism income
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Kinds of plants and their parts and Plants give us things and food — the single plant first; the forest is plants at nation scale.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a plant is: a living thing that makes its own food, stays in one place, grows and reproduces.
2. List what forests give: building material, paper, furniture, shelter for wild animals, tourism income.
3. Say why visitors come to forests: for rare animals — walia, red fox, rare birds.

## What you need
A church forest or grove nearby to visit or picture; failing that, the picture of one.

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines plants as 'green things' → the book's marks are stronger: living, makes its own food, stays in one place, grows and reproduces.
- Counts only wood as the forest's gift → paper, furniture, animal shelter and tourism stand beside building on the book's list.
