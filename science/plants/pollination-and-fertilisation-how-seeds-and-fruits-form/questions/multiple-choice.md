# Pollination and fertilisation — how seeds and fruits form — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Pollination is…
a) the ovary growing into fruit  b) pollen carried to the stigma  c) a seed sprouting  d) the stigma joining the ovule  
**Answer: b) pollen carried to the stigma**

**2.** Which is NOT one of the book's pollen carriers?
a) Wind  b) Insects  c) Water  d) Moonlight  
**Answer: d) Moonlight**

**3.** After fertilisation, the ovary becomes…
a) the stigma  b) the fruit  c) a leaf  d) pollen  
**Answer: b) the fruit**
