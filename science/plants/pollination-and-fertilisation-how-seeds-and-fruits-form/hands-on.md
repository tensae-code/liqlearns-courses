# Hands-on — Pollination and fertilisation — how seeds and fruits form

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow a flower
Find a flower (or a clear drawing of one) and point in order: where pollen is made, the stigma it must reach, and the ovary where the joining happens.

## The carriers table
Make a four-row table — wind, insects, water, humans — and for each, write one sentence on how that carrier could carry pollen to a stigma.
