Today: **Pollination and fertilisation — how seeds and fruits form**.

First pollination: pollen reaches the stigma, carried by wind, insects, water or humans. Then fertilisation: the pollen grain joins the ovule in the ovary. Then the ovary grows into the fruit — and the fertilised ovules become the seeds.
