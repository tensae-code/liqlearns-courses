# Plants — seedless plants: mosses, liverworts and ferns — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Liverworts and hornworts are bryophytes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ferns produce seeds in their fronds.
**Answer: False — ferns reproduce by spores; they have no seeds or flowers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Practical 5.5 compares mosses, ferns, junipers and flowering plants trait by trait.
**Answer: True**
