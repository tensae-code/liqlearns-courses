# Plants — seedless plants: mosses, liverworts and ferns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bryophytes reproduce by —
a) seeds  b) spores  c) cones
**Answer: b) spores**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mosses grow in —
a) dry deserts  b) moist places  c) the sea only
**Answer: b) moist places**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A moss has —
a) true roots  b) only root-like, stem-like and leaf-like parts  c) flowers
**Answer: b) only root-like, stem-like and leaf-like parts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A fern's underground stem is a —
a) rhizome  b) cone  c) fruit
**Answer: a) rhizome**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Fern spore patches are found —
a) on the fronds' undersides  b) in flowers  c) in cones
**Answer: a) on the fronds' undersides**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Ferns have vascular tissue called —
a) chitin and murein  b) xylem and phloem  c) muscle and nerve
**Answer: b) xylem and phloem**
