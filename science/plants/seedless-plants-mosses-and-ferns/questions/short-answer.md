# Plants — seedless plants: mosses, liverworts and ferns — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three bryophyte groups.
**Answer:** Mosses, liverworts, hornworts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give three fern features a moss does not have.
**Answer:** Any three of: true root/stem/leaves, vascular tissue (xylem/phloem), rhizome, fronds with spore patches.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Practical 5.5 do?
**Answer:** Compares collected mosses, ferns, junipers and flowering plants trait by trait.
