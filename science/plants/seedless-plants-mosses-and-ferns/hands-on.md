# Hands-on — Plants — seedless plants: mosses, liverworts and ferns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practical 5.5 — the four-way comparison
Collect small samples humanely (a moss tuft, a fern frond — take fallen fronds where you can), and table moss, fern, juniper and a flowering plant trait by trait: true parts? vascular tissue? seeds? flowers? spores? Field safety (teacher guide — evidence, item 11, guide printed pp. 84–108): some organisms may sting or bite or carry disease — never collect with bare hands, use gloves or a container, watch where you step, wash hands afterwards, and release every specimen alive and unharmed where you found it.

## Frond underside
Turn a fern frond over under a hand lens. Sketch the spore patches and the rhizome if the plant is already fallen.
