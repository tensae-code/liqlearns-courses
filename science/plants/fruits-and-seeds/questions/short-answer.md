# Fruits and seeds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** A fruit is the covering a flowering plant builds around its seeds — apple, pod, grain and nut all count.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** The fruit guards the seeds while they ripen.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Is a bean pod a fruit?
**Answer:** Yes — in the science sense a fruit is the covering built around seeds, and the pod is exactly that covering.
