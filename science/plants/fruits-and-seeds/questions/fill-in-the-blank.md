# Fruits and seeds — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Key ideas) A _____ is the covering a flowering plant builds around its seeds — apple, pod, grain and nut all count.
**Answer: fruit**

**2.** (Key ideas) A fruit is the covering a flowering plant builds around its _____ — apple, pod, grain and nut all count.
**Answer: seeds**
