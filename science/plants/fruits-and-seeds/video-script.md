# Video script — Fruits and seeds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Fruits and seeds** — Albania's way, Frutat dhe farat, straight after the flower's work.
A fruit is a covering built around seeds — apple, pod, grain, nut. It guards them while they ripen.
Inside sits the seed: a new plant packed, with food for its first growth. Packing and passenger.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
