# Hands-on — Fruits and seeds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Open and count
With an adult's knife work, open several fruits and count and draw their seeds. Make a chart: Fruit | Number of seeds | Where they sit. Read the chart back as coverings and passengers.

## Find the packed food
Soak a bean seed overnight, then split it gently with an adult's help: find the seed's two thick halves — the packed food store — and the small start of the new plant between them.
