# Video script — What a plant needs to grow

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soil to stand in. Water to drink.
Air to breathe. Sunlight to make food.
Four tins, one sowing day — and the tins teach.
A plant needs all four. Give all four, and it grows.
