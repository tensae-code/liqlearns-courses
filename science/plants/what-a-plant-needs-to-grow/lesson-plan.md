# Lesson plan — What a plant needs to grow

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Plants · **Lesson:** What a plant needs to grow
**Place in the curriculum:** Plants — after Kinds of plants and their parts (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a plant needs soil, water, air and sunlight — proved by the book's four-tin experiment: bean seeds sown the same day in an empty tin, soil only, water only, and soil + water, all given equal sun and air
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Kinds of plants and their parts, and on Sunlight keeps everything alive — the tin experiment and the two-tin light experiment are cousins, run the same fair way.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four needs: soil, water, air and sunlight.
2. Set up the four tins the book's way: empty / soil only / water only / soil + water, sown the same day, equal sun and air.
3. Observe the growth, report it, and state the conclusion: the plant needs all of soil, water, air and sunlight together.

## What you need
Four tins, bean seeds, soil, water, a sunny place — and patience measured in days.

## If the learner is stuck
- Waters all four tins 'to be fair' → fairness here means equal sun and air; the water is the very thing being tested. Each tin gets what its label says.
- Concludes after one day → seeds keep their own time; the book observes over days and reports.
- This experiment is the hands-on heart of the Plants title — run it for real, not only in words.
