# What a plant needs to grow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A plant needs which four things to grow?**
a) soil, water, air and sunlight  b) soil, stones, shade and silence  c) water, milk, soil and darkness
**Answer: a) soil, water, air and sunlight**

**2. In the experiment, all four tins must get equal —**
a) water  b) sun and air  c) soil
**Answer: b) sun and air** — sun and air are held equal; soil and water are what differ.

**3. A seed in the empty tin lacks —**
a) only soil  b) soil and water both  c) sunlight only
**Answer: b) soil and water both**

**4. Observing and ___ the growth is the experiment's last step.**
a) eating  b) reporting  c) hiding
**Answer: b) reporting**
