# Hands-on — What a plant needs to grow

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The tin diary
Photograph or draw the four tins every second day. The diary is the proof — dates and heights, no memory trusted.

## Missing-one hunt
Find a real plant that is failing. Diagnose it by the four needs: which one is it missing? Prescribe the cure.

## Fairness court
With a partner, list every way the experiment could be made unfair (different seeds, different sun, extra water). The book's fairness is the scientist's fairness.
