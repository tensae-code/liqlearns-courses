# Plant survival — special parts and dispersal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Protecting themselves, and suiting the climate.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Grade 5 book teaches how plants **protect themselves from enemies: thorns, stinging hairs, poison, thick bark and bad smells**, each catalogued with examples. Plants also **adapt to climate and season change: waxy leaves (keladi), needle leaves and thick bark in cold climates, and water-storing stems**.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Seed and fruit dispersal — travelling to new ground.
**Answer:** A plant species survives by spreading. The book teaches dispersal **by water** — fruits that float and travel on water; **by animals and humans** — hooked, sticky and eaten fruits; and **by explosive mechanism and by wind**, all compared in a summary chart. The unit's summary and questions gather the protection features and the dispersal ways together.

**Answer:** Protecting themselves, and suiting the climate → Seed and fruit dispersal — travelling to new ground — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
