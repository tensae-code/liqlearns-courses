# Hands-on — Stomata, transpiration and how leaves lose water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Time a wilting plant
Take two similar potted plants. Water one and not the other (with the owner's permission). Look at both morning and evening, note when the unwatered plant first wilts, and time it. Then water it and watch it recover. Record the whole run in a table.
