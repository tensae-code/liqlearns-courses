# Stomata, transpiration and how leaves lose water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Stomata are…
a) tiny holes in leaves  b) roots under the soil  c) flowers of the plant  d) spots of disease
**Answer: a) tiny holes in leaves**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Through the stomata the plant…
a) takes in air and loses water  b) takes in soil  c) makes seeds  d) catches insects
**Answer: a) takes in air and loses water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Transpiration is…
a) water leaving the plant through its leaves  b) water entering the roots  c) rain falling  d) dew forming
**Answer: a) water leaving the plant through its leaves**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An unwatered plant wilts because…
a) it keeps losing water through its leaves  b) its stomata close forever  c) the soil gets lighter  d) insects drink it dry
**Answer: a) it keeps losing water through its leaves**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) To see stomata, the topic prepares…
a) a slide of them for the microscope  b) a bar chart  c) a rain gauge  d) a thermometer
**Answer: a) a slide of them for the microscope**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Green plants make food using…
a) air, light and water  b) darkness and dry soil  c) salt and sand  d) wind only
**Answer: a) air, light and water**
