# Stomata, transpiration and how leaves lose water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: stomata — the leaf's tiny holes.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 7 Topic 10, "Plant leaves and water", opens the leaf: stomata are tiny holes in leaves through which plants "breathe" in air and lose water.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What are stomata, and what is transpiration?
**Answer:** Stomata are tiny holes in leaves through which the plant takes in air and loses water; transpiration is that loss of water through the leaves.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Stomata — the leaf's tiny holes → Transpiration — water leaving the leaf — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
