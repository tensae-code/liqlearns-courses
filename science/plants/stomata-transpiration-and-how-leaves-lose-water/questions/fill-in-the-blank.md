# Stomata, transpiration and how leaves lose water — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "Stomata are…" is ______.
**Answer: tiny holes in leaves**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "Through the stomata the plant…" is ______.
**Answer: takes in air and loses water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "Transpiration is…" is ______.
**Answer: water leaving the plant through its leaves**
