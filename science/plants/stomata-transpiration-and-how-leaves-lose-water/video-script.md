Today: **Stomata, transpiration and how leaves lose water** — Seychelles' way, from its Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 7).

Seychelles' Unit 7 Topic 10, "Plant leaves and water", opens the leaf: stomata are tiny holes in leaves through which plants "breathe" in air and lose water. The topic prepares a slide of stomata so the holes themselves are seen under the microscope — the leaf's surface is not solid, but pierced by openings the plant uses.

Water leaving through the stomata is transpiration. The topic times how long an unwatered plant takes to wilt: cut off from water, the plant keeps losing it through its leaves until it wilts. Green plants need air, light and water to make food — and the same leaves that take in air for food-making are the leaves that let water out.
