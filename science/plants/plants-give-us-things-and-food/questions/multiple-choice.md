# Plants give us things and food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which of these comes from plants?**
a) a stone pot  b) a paper sheet  c) an iron nail
**Answer: b) a paper sheet**

**2. Bees and butterflies feed from —**
a) roots  b) flowers  c) bark
**Answer: b) flowers**

**3. Paper is made from —**
a) stones  b) plants (wood pulp)  c) sand
**Answer: b) plants (wood pulp)**

**4. Who feeds from flowers?**
a) bees and butterflies  b) cows  c) fish
**Answer: a) bees and butterflies**
