# Hands-on — Plants give us things and food

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace-back labels
Put a leaf mark on every plant-made thing in your classroom for one day. Count them at the end — plants hold the room up.

## Bee diary
Watch one flower patch across a sunny hour. Log each visitor and minute. Bees work the flowers like farmers.

## Paper's story
Take one sheet of paper and write its life story backwards: sheet, pulp, tree, seed, soil, rain, sun.
