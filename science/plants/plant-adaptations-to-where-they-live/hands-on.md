# Hands-on — Plant adaptations — to where they live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three places, three questions
Draw three columns: desert, rain forest, swamp. In each, write the question that place asks a plant — about water, light or wet ground — before you name any plant that lives there.

## Fit a plant to its place
Cards or drawings: match each plant feature you meet in the lesson to the place it fits, and say the survival reason out loud for each match. A match without a reason does not count.
