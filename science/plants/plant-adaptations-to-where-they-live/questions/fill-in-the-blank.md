# Plant adaptations — to where they live — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An _____ is a feature that fits a plant to where it lives.
**Answer: adaptation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A desert plant's great question is how to keep _____.
**Answer: water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ conditions mean ground that is wet, with roots in water.
**Answer: Swampy**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Adaptations assist a plant's _____.
**Answer: survival**
