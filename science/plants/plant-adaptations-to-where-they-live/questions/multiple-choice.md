# Plant adaptations — to where they live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An adaptation is…
a) A plant's name  b) A feature that fits a plant to where it lives  c) A kind of seed  d) A garden tool
**Answer: b) A feature that fits a plant to where it lives**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Barbados's syllabus compares adaptations for…
a) Desert, rain forest and swampy conditions  b) Mountains only  c) The sea floor only  d) Pots on a windowsill
**Answer: a) Desert, rain forest and swampy conditions**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In a desert, the plant's great question is…
a) How to shed water  b) How to keep water  c) How to grow taller than trees  d) How to flower all year
**Answer: b) How to keep water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An adaptation matters because it…
a) Looks pretty  b) Assists the plant's survival in its place  c) Makes the plant easier to draw  d) Changes the weather
**Answer: b) Assists the plant's survival in its place**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Swampy conditions mean…
a) No water at all  b) Ground that is wet, with roots in water  c) Snow all year  d) Only sand
**Answer: b) Ground that is wet, with roots in water**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The test for an adaptation is…
a) Name the place, the feature, and how it helps survival  b) Count the leaves  c) Measure the stem only  d) Name the plant in two languages
**Answer: a) Name the place, the feature, and how it helps survival**
