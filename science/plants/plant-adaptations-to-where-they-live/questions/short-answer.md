# Plant adaptations — to where they live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which three kinds of place does Barbados's syllabus use for plant adaptations?
**Answer: Desert conditions, rain forest conditions and swampy conditions.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How does an adaptation assist survival?
**Answer: It helps the plant get what it needs and bear what its place does to it — it is the reason that kind of plant can live in that place at all.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why can the same plant body not suit every place?
**Answer: Because each place asks a different question — keeping water in the desert, reaching light and shedding water in the rain forest, living with roots in water in the swamp.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What three things must you name to show you understand an adaptation?
**Answer: The place, the feature, and how the feature helps the plant survive there.**
