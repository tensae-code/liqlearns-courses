# Plant adaptations — to where they live

## Do it first
Before you read: one plant lives where rain almost never falls. Another lives where it rains every day. A third stands with its roots in swamp water. Can the same plant body suit all three places? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Barbados's way in — its Primary Science Syllabus teaches plant adaptations place by place.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fitted to dry, wet and swampy places

An **adaptation** is a feature of a plant that fits it to the place where it lives. Barbados's syllabus sets the places side by side: **desert** conditions, where water is scarce; **rain forest** conditions, where water and shade are plentiful; and **swampy** conditions, where the ground itself is wet.

The plant of each place is built differently, because each place asks a different question: in the desert, how to keep water; in the rain forest, how to reach light and shed water; in the swamp, how to live with roots in water.

## Sub lesson 2 — How adaptations assist survival

An adaptation is not decoration. It **assists survival**: a feature that helps the plant get what it needs and bear what its place does to it is the reason that kind of plant can live there at all.

That is the syllabus's test for every example: name the place, name the feature, and say how the feature helps the plant survive there. A feature you cannot connect to survival in that place is not yet an adaptation you understand.

## The key ideas

- An **adaptation** is a feature that fits a plant to the place where it lives — desert, rain forest or swampy conditions in Barbados's syllabus.
- Each place asks a different question — keeping water, reaching light and shedding water, or living with roots in water.
- Adaptations **assist survival**: name the place, the feature, and how the feature helps — that is the test.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "What is an adaptation?" Answer: A feature of a plant that fits it to the place where it lives, helping it survive there.

## Do It Yourself
1. Pick two places from the lesson — desert and swamp, say — and write the different question each place asks of a plant.
2. Find a plant near your home and name one feature that fits it to its place. Say how that feature assists its survival.
3. Teach the hardest part of this lesson to someone at home in five sentences — how an adaptation assists survival.
