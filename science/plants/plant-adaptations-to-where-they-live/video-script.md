Today: **Plant adaptations — to where they live** — Barbados's way: every place asks its plants a different question.

An adaptation is a feature that fits a plant to its place. The desert asks: how will you keep water? The rain forest asks about light and shedding water. The swamp asks how roots can live in water. And the feature only counts as an adaptation when you can say how it assists survival.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
