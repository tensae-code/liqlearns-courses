# Hands-on — Identifying trees by their leaves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Key a leaf
Collect three fallen leaves of different shapes (do not pick from living trees). For each leaf, write two questions a key could ask about it — shape? edge? — and sort your leaves by your own questions.

## Name by leaf alone
A partner shows you a leaf with the tree hidden from view — a photo with the trunk cropped out works. Name everything you can tell from the leaf alone, then check against the whole tree.
