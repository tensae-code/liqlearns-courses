# Identifying trees by their leaves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Trees on a forest trip are named where they stand, by their leaves.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Trees on a forest trip are named where they stand, by their leaves.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?

**Answer: a) A tree key (Ég greini tré) asks about the leaf step by step, narrowing the trees down to one species.**
