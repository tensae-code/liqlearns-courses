# Identifying trees by their leaves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Trees on a forest trip are named where they stand, by their leaves.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** A tree key (Ég greini tré) asks about the leaf step by step, narrowing the trees down to one species.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What two things does the pupil hold to name a tree, Iceland's way?
**Answer:** The leaf from the tree, and the tree key — Ég greini tré — whose questions narrow the species down step by step.
