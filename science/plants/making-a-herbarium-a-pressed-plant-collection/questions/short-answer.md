# Making a herbarium — a pressed-plant collection — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about making a herbarium — a pressed-plant collection?
**Answer: The books teach this part as Making a herbarium by a five-step plan: gather in dry weather, take only a few leaves so the plant is not harmed, never pick Red Book plants, dry between clean paper, mount and label — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
