# Hands-on — Keeping local seeds and adapted varieties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Lesson 22. Lesson 22: seed kinds — traditional seed selected and saved by farmers each year; hybrid seed from crossing varieties (DICTA's improved hybrids: Dicta-660 rice, VMR sorghum, rust-resistant Ihcafé-90 and Lempira coffee, Dicta Sequía maize, Carrizalito and Deorho beans) whose seed must be bought every sowing and needs agrochemicals; and transgenic (GM) seed made in laboratories, whose consequences science cannot yet fully predict. Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
