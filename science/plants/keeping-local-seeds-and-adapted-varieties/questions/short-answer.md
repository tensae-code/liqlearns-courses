# Keeping local seeds and adapted varieties — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about lesson 22?
**Answer: Lesson 22: seed kinds — traditional seed selected and saved by farmers each year; hybrid seed from crossing varieties (DICTA's improved hybrids: Dicta-660 rice, VMR sorghum, rust-resistant Ihcafé-90 and Lempira coffee, Dicta Sequía maize, Carrizalito and Deorho beans) whose seed must be bought every sowing and needs agrochemicals; and transgenic (GM) seed made in laboratories, whose consequences science cannot yet fully predict.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about why save creole (criolla) seeds?
**Answer: Why save creole (criolla) seeds: bought or gathered once, self-sufficiency, strong resistant plants selected year by year, stronger flavour, and endless diversity of size, shape and colour — Honduras's dry-corridor trial of Brazilian heat-resistant vegetable seed in Choluteca, Valle and Comayagua opens the lesson.**

**3.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Keeping local seeds and adapted varieties.**
