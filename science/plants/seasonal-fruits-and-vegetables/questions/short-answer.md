# Seasonal fruits and vegetables — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Food has seasons.
**Answer:** Not every fruit and vegetable grows all year.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Bangladesh's fruits and vegetables by season.
**Answer:** Bangladesh's Class 3 book sorts the country's own fruits and vegetables by season: the foods of the different parts of the year are named as different sets, so a learner meets food sources as a year that turns, not as a flat list..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Why the season sort matters.
**Answer:** Sorting food by season teaches where food comes from: a food eaten in its season comes from nearby fields at their best, and the sort tells you which foods to expect, and when..

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Food has seasons → Bangladesh's fruits and vegetables by season → Why the season sort matters — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
