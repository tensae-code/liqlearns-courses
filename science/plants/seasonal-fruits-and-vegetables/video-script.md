The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Seasonal fruits and vegetables** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 3 — *Elementary Science Class 3*, printed p. 25).

Not every fruit and vegetable grows all year. Each one has its season — the part of the year when it grows well, is picked, and is sold fresh.
Bangladesh's Class 3 book sorts the country's own fruits and vegetables by season: the foods of the different parts of the year are named as different sets, so a learner meets food sources as a year that turns, not as a flat list.
Sorting food by season teaches where food comes from: a food eaten in its season comes from nearby fields at their best, and the sort tells you which foods to expect, and when.
