# Transport in plants

## Do it first
Before you read: say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is — the transportation of water and of food inside a plant. Then read on: this is Sri Lanka's way in — its Grade 8 Science Part 2 teaches transportation in plants as one unit, straight after photosynthesis: what the leaf makes and what the root takes in must both travel.

## Sub lesson 1 — Transportation of water in plants

A plant takes water in at its **roots**, and that water must reach every leaf. It travels **upward through the stem** in the plant's own conducting paths: **transportation** is this inside movement, and without it the top of a tall plant would dry while its roots stood in water.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transportation of food in plants

Food made in the leaves must travel the other way as well: **down and out to every part** — roots, stem, fruits and growing tips — that cannot make its own. Sri Lanka's book teaches the two journeys together: water up from the roots, food out from the leaves, one transport system serving the whole plant.
