The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Transport in plants** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

A plant takes water in at its roots, and that water must reach every leaf.

Food made in the leaves must travel the other way as well: down and out to every part — roots, stem, fruits and growing tips — that cannot make its own.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
