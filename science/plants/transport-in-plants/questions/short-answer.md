# Transport in plants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Transportation of water in plants.
**Answer:** A plant takes water in at its **roots**, and that water must reach every leaf. It travels **upward through the stem** in the plant's own conducting paths: **transportation** is this inside movement, and without it the top of a tall plant would dry while its roots stood in water.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Transportation of food in plants.
**Answer:** Food made in the leaves must travel the other way as well: **down and out to every part** — roots, stem, fruits and growing tips — that cannot make its own. Sri Lanka's book teaches the two journeys together: water up from the roots, food out from the leaves, one transport system serving the whole plant.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Transportation of water in plants → Transportation of food in plants — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
