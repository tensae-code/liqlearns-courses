# How plants change with the seasons — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Plants do not stay the same all year.
**Answer:** A plant does not look the same all year; as the seasons turn, the plant changes with them.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The changes through the seasons, part 1.
**Answer:** In the first part the learner watches plants through the turning year and notices the changes as they come, season by season.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The changes through the seasons, part 2.
**Answer:** In the second part the learner watches again and puts the changes in order, seeing that the pattern repeats each year.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Plants do not stay the same all year → The changes through the seasons, part 1 → The changes through the seasons, part 2 — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
