## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Plants do not stay the same all year

A plant does not look the same all year. As the seasons turn, the plant changes with them — new growth, flowers, fruit and leaves come and go in their season.

## Sub lesson 2 — The changes through the seasons, part 1

Morocco's Grade 1 guide teaches how plants change through the seasons in two parts. In the first part the learner watches plants through the turning year and notices the changes as they come, season by season.

## Sub lesson 3 — The changes through the seasons, part 2

In the second part the learner watches again and puts the changes in order: the same plant, followed through the seasons, shows a pattern — change is part of a plant's life, and the pattern repeats each year.
