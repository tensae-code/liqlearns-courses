Today: **How plants change with the seasons** — Morocco's way, from its book (Morocco, Grade 1 — *Moufid 1*, printed pp. 51–52).

A plant does not look the same all year. As the seasons turn, the plant changes with them.
Morocco's Grade 1 guide teaches this in two parts: first the learner watches how plants change through the seasons, then watches again and names the changes in order.
Watching the same plant through the year teaches that change is part of a plant's life — the plant is alive, growing and changing, not a still picture.
