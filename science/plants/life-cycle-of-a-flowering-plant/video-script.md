# Video script — Life cycle of a flowering plant

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Life cycle of a flowering plant** — Bulgaria's way: follow one bean round the whole circle.

Inside the bean: an embryo — the young plant, waiting — a coat to protect it, and stored food to feed its first growth.

Give the seed warmth, moisture and air, and it germinates. It grows, it flowers — and the flowers make new seeds, each one a new beginning.

Now you. Tell the bean's story from seed to new seeds. Say it back — in your own words.
