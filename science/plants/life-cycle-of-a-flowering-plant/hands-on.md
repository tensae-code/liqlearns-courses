# Hands-on — Life cycle of a flowering plant

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Open a bean
Soak beans overnight, then open one carefully. Find the coat, the stored food and the small embryo. Draw what you see and label the three parts.

## The three-conditions test
Set three beans on damp cotton and three on dry cotton, all in a warm place. Watch for a week: which germinate? Say which condition was missing for the others.
