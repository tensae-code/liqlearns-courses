# Life cycle of a flowering plant — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The young plant inside the seed is the _____.
**Answer: embryo**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A seed wakes and starts growing in _____.
**Answer: germination**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Germination needs warmth, _____ and air.
**Answer: moisture**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The plant's flowers make new _____, and the cycle begins again.
**Answer: seeds**
