# Life cycle of a flowering plant — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three parts of the bean seed and the job of each.
**Answer:** Embryo — the young plant itself; coat — protects it; stored food — feeds it at the start.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three conditions of germination.
**Answer:** Warmth, moisture and air.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is a plant's life called a cycle and not a line?
**Answer:** Because it closes: the plant that began from a seed ends by making new seeds, each the start of the next round.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Two seeds sit side by side; one germinates and one does not. Give a reason from the lesson.
**Answer:** One of the three conditions — warmth, moisture or air — was missing for the seed that waited.
