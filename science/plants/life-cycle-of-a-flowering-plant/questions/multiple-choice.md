# Life cycle of a flowering plant — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Inside a bean seed there is…
a) A flower  b) An embryo, a coat and stored food  c) Only water  d) A root only
**Answer: b) An embryo, a coat and stored food**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Germination needs…
a) Warmth, moisture and air  b) Darkness, cold and dryness  c) Only sunlight  d) Salt and sand
**Answer: a) Warmth, moisture and air**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The seed's embryo is fed at the start by…
a) The coat  b) Stored food in the seed  c) Rain  d) The flower
**Answer: b) Stored food in the seed**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The cycle of an annual plant closes when…
a) It is picked  b) Its flowers make new seeds  c) Winter comes  d) It is watered
**Answer: b) Its flowers make new seeds**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A seed with warmth and air but no moisture will…
a) Germinate at once  b) Wait — a condition is missing  c) Flower  d) Make seeds
**Answer: b) Wait — a condition is missing**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The order of the cycle is…
a) Flower, seed, germination  b) Seed, germination, growth, flowering, new seeds  c) Growth, seed, flower, germination  d) Seed, flower, germination, growth
**Answer: b) Seed, germination, growth, flowering, new seeds**
