# Hands-on — Growing without soil — hydroponics and vertical gardens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Alternative growing systems. alternative growing systems: hydroponics (roots in solid substrate fed by nutrient solutions in water), green roofs (organised on roofs, saving space and regulating temperature), hanging plants on walls, and vertical gardens (a green wall of diverse species) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
