# Growing without soil — hydroponics and vertical gardens — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about alternative growing systems?
**Answer: alternative growing systems: hydroponics (roots in solid substrate fed by nutrient solutions in water), green roofs (organised on roofs, saving space and regulating temperature), hanging plants on walls, and vertical gardens (a green wall of diverse species)**

**2.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Growing without soil — hydroponics and vertical gardens.**
