# The vascular system in plants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does each pipe carry, and which way?
**Answer: Xylem carries water and minerals upward from roots to leaves; phloem carries food from the leaves to all parts that need or store it.**

**2.** How does coloured water prove the xylem exists?
**Answer: The colour rises through the stem into the leaves or petals along the xylem vessels, and a cut stem shows the coloured strands.**

**3.** What problem does the vascular system solve?
**Answer: Distance: in a tall plant the roots and leaves are far apart, and the pipes join them so water and food can travel.**

**4.** Where does the phloem's load come from?
**Answer: From the leaves, where food is made by photosynthesis.**
