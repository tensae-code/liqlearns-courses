# The vascular system in plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Water rises from the roots in the _____.
**Answer: xylem**

**2.** Food travels from the leaves in the _____.
**Answer: phloem**

**3.** The plant's pipes together are its _____ system.
**Answer: vascular**

**4.** Xylem carries water and dissolved _____.
**Answer: minerals**
