# The vascular system in plants — multiple choice


**1.** The plant's transport pipes are called its…
a) Roots  b) Vascular system  c) Flowers  d) Bark
**Answer: b) Vascular system**

**2.** Xylem carries…
a) Food down from leaves  b) Water up from roots  c) Seeds to soil  d) Pollen to flowers
**Answer: b) Water up from roots**

**3.** Phloem carries…
a) Water only  b) Food made in the leaves  c) Air  d) Sand
**Answer: b) Food made in the leaves**

**4.** Coloured water rising in a stem travels in the…
a) Phloem only  b) Xylem  c) Petals  d) Roots hairs only
**Answer: b) Xylem**

**5.** A tall plant needs a vascular system because…
a) It is pretty  b) Roots and leaves are far apart and must be joined  c) It stops animals  d) It makes seeds
**Answer: b) Roots and leaves are far apart and must be joined**

**6.** Xylem flow goes…
a) Leaves to roots  b) Roots to leaves  c) Flower to flower  d) Nowhere
**Answer: b) Roots to leaves**
