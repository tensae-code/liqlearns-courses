# Hands-on — The vascular system in plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch the xylem work
Stand a pale celery stalk or a white flower in water coloured with food colouring. Leave it for some hours, then look: where has the colour travelled? Cut the stalk across and lengthwise and find the coloured strands — the xylem vessels. Draw and label them.
