# Living things live where their needs are met — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the lesson's rule about where living things live?
**Answer:** Living things live where their needs are met — each plant has its place, the forest plants in the forest and the garden plants in the garden.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why would a tulip not do well deep in the forest?
**Answer:** Because the deep forest does not meet its needs — it is a garden plant, planted and cared for where light, soil and care are given to it.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Describe Sweden's task for this idea.
**Answer:** The pupil takes the cut-out plants from the sheets and sticks each one into a two-page garden-and-forest scene, sorting every plant into the place it belongs — "Var ska växterna vara?", where should the plants be?

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two plants for the forest side and two for the garden side.
**Answer:** Forest: any two of spruce, pine, wood anemone, liverleaf, bilberry, lingonberry. Garden: any two of tulip, rose, daffodil, apple tree, pear tree, strawberry.
