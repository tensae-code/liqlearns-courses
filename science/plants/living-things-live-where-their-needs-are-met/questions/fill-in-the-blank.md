# Living things live where their needs are met — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Living things live where their _____ are met.
**Answer: needs**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The spruce belongs in the _____.
**Answer: forest**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant put where its needs are not met does not _____.
**Answer: thrive**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The pupil sticks each cut-out plant into the place it _____.
**Answer: belongs**
