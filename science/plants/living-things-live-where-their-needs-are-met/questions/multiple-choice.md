# Living things live where their needs are met — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A living thing lives where…
a) It looks prettiest  b) Its needs are met  c) There is the most space  d) Other plants tell it to
**Answer: b) Its needs are met**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these belongs in the forest in Sweden's scene?
a) Tulip  b) Rose  c) Spruce  d) Daffodil in a bed
**Answer: c) Spruce**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant put where its needs are not met…
a) Grows faster  b) Does not thrive  c) Changes into another plant  d) Needs no water
**Answer: b) Does not thrive**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In Sweden's task, the pupil proves the idea by…
a) Copying a definition  b) Sticking each cut-out plant into the place it belongs in the garden-and-forest scene  c) Naming the plants in alphabetical order  d) Colouring the scene
**Answer: b) Sticking each cut-out plant into the place it belongs in the garden-and-forest scene**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) "Var ska växterna vara?" means…
a) What are the plants called?  b) Where should the plants be?  c) How many plants are there?  d) Who planted the plants?
**Answer: b) Where should the plants be?**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A plant placed on the wrong side of the scene is wrong because…
a) It spoils the picture  b) The rule is that living things live where their needs are met, and the wrong place does not meet them  c) The glue will not hold  d) The scene has no room
**Answer: b) The rule is that living things live where their needs are met, and the wrong place does not meet them**
