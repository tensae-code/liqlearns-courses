# Video script — Living things live where their needs are met

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Living things live where their needs are met** — Sweden's way: place every plant where it belongs.

Here is a big picture: a garden on one side, a forest on the other. And here are the plants, cut out, ready to stick down. Where does the spruce go? The forest. Where does the tulip go? The garden. Where does the wood anemone go? Back to the forest floor where it grows.
Every plant has its place — the place where it finds what it needs. A living thing lives where its needs are met, and it does not thrive where they are not.
So the work is the sorting itself: pick up a plant, name its place, and say why it belongs there.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
