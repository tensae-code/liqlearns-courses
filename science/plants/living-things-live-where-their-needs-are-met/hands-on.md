# Hands-on — Living things live where their needs are met

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the two-place scene
Draw a big two-page scene of your own: a garden on one side, a wood or wild place on the other. Leave the plants out for now — the empty scene is the question: where should the plants be?

## Cut out and place
Draw and cut out six plants: spruce, apple tree, tulip, wood anemone, dandelion and rose. One by one, stick each into its place in your scene. Before each one goes down, say its place out loud and why — where its needs are met.

## The wrong-place test
Move one plant to the wrong side on purpose — the tulip into the deep forest, the spruce into the flower bed. Say what would be wrong for that plant there: what it needs that the place does not give. Then put it back where it belongs.
