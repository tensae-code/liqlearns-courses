# Classifying plants by stems and flowers

## Sub lesson 1 — Grouping by stem — tree, shrub and herb

Plants can be sorted by the kind of stem they grow on. A tree grows on one strong woody stem, a shrub grows several woody stems from low down, and an herb grows a soft green stem. Looking at the stem first gives a sort that can be seen on any plant, flowering or not.

## Sub lesson 2 — Grouping by flowers — flowering or not

The same plants can be sorted a second way: plants that bear flowers, and plants that do not. A plant's group can change when the sorting rule changes — stem in the first sort, flowers in the second.

## Sub lesson 3 — Two sorts of the same plants

Class 3 in Bangladesh teaches the two sorts side by side: first the parts of a plant are observed and drawn, then the plants are grouped — by stem type, and by having flowers or not. Grouping is the step that comes after observing.
