# Classifying plants by stems and flowers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Grouping by stem — tree, shrub and herb.
**Answer:** Plants can be sorted by the kind of stem they grow on.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Grouping by flowers — flowering or not.
**Answer:** The same plants can be sorted a second way: plants that bear flowers, and plants that do not.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Two sorts of the same plants.
**Answer:** Class 3 in Bangladesh teaches the two sorts side by side: first the parts of a plant are observed and drawn, then the plants are grouped — by stem type, and by having flowers or not.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Grouping by stem — tree, shrub and herb → Grouping by flowers — flowering or not → Two sorts of the same plants — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
