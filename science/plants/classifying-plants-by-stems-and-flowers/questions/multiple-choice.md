# Classifying plants by stems and flowers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Grouping by stem — tree, shrub and herb  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Grouping by stem — tree, shrub and herb**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Grouping by flowers — flowering or not  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Grouping by flowers — flowering or not**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Two sorts of the same plants  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Two sorts of the same plants**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: grouping plants by stem type — tree, shrub and herb compared — and by whether they have flowers or not, as Bangladesh's Class 3 book teaches it in its opening plants chapter. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 3 — *Elementary Science Class 3*, printed p. 5  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 3 — *Elementary Science Class 3*, printed p. 5**
