# Sorting fruits and vegetables — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what classifying means in this activity.
**Answer:** Classifying means grouping things by kind — fruits with fruits, vegetables with vegetables — and saying what puts each thing in its group.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the activity use fruits and vegetables children know?
**Answer:** Because the youngest learners' science starts with things they can hold, name and compare — food from home and market, where every child has something to say.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The activity begins with real fruits and vegetables the children know → the science is the sorting — classifying them into groups and saying the rule — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
