# Sorting fruits and vegetables — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Activity 2.7 of Cambodia's kindergarten core book is…
a) classifying vegetables and fruits  b) counting stars  c) wiring a circuit  d) naming minerals
**Answer: a) classifying vegetables and fruits** — The activity bank's classifying activity — sorting fruits and vegetables.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Sorting things by kind is called…
a) classifying  b) cooking  c) guessing  d) hiding
**Answer: a) classifying** — Classifying — grouping by kind — is one of the first science skills.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The activity starts with…
a) real fruits and vegetables the children know  b) pictures of planets  c) laboratory glass  d) maps
**Answer: a) real fruits and vegetables the children know** — Real things from home and market, that children can hold and name.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The book carrying this activity is…
a) the MoEYS Core Book for Kindergarten Education — Science  b) the Grade 9 book  c) a private booklet  d) the Grade 7 book
**Answer: a) the MoEYS Core Book for Kindergarten Education — Science** — The official KG core book (MoEYS, 2022) — guide evidence.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) When sorting, children say…
a) what puts each thing in its group  b) nothing  c) only the colours in English  d) the prices
**Answer: a) what puts each thing in its group** — The rule said out loud is the science of the activity.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson stands on…
a) guide evidence only — the core book is a teacher/core book  b) a Grade 9 student text  c) an exam  d) no book
**Answer: a) guide evidence only — the core book is a teacher/core book** — Filed as guide evidence, outside the acceptance count, exactly as the placements file states it.
