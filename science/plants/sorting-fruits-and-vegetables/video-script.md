# Video script — Sorting fruits and vegetables

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Sorting fruits and vegetables** — Cambodia's way, from its Kindergarten book *សៀវភៅសិក្សាគោលសម្រាប់មត្តេយ្យសិក្សា — វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ (Core Book for Kindergarten Education: Science)*.

The activity begins with real things on the table: **fruits** and **vegetables** the children know from home and market. Cambodia's kindergarten core book puts classifying them in its science activity bank (activity 2.7) — the youngest learners' science starts with the things they can hold, name and compare.

The science of the activity is the **sorting**: the children group the fruits together and the vegetables together, saying what puts each thing in its group. Sorting by kind — classifying — is one of the first science skills there is, and the core book teaches it on the food children eat, where every child already has something to say.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
