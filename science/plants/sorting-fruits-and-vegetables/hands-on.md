# Hands-on — Sorting fruits and vegetables

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The market sort
With an adult, take six fruits and vegetables at home. Sort them into two groups and say, for each one, which group it joins and why. Then mix them and let someone else sort — compare your groups.

## Draw your groups
Draw your two groups in two circles and label them. Under each drawing, write the rule you sorted by.
