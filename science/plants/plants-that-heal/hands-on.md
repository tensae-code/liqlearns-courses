# Hands-on — Plants that heal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Elder interview
Ask an elder about one listed plant: its name, its use, how it is prepared. Report exactly what they said, as their knowledge.

## Leaf rubbing school
Make leaf rubbings of three listed plants that grow near you. Label each with its name — a healer's first skill is never confusing the leaves.

## Kitchen pharmacy tour
In your own kitchen, find the listed plants that live there as food and medicine both — garlic, ginger. Say their double duty aloud.
