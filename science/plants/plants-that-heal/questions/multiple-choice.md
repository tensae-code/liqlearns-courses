# Plants that heal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The book's example of a healing plant at work: ___ eases coughs and colds.**
a) garlic  b) eucalyptus  c) teff
**Answer: a) garlic**

**2. Which of these is in the book's medicinal list?**
a) moringa (shiferaw)  b) wheat  c) banana
**Answer: a) moringa (shiferaw)**

**3. Moringa is also called —**
a) tena adam  b) shiferaw  c) eret
**Answer: b) shiferaw**

**4. Which plant eases coughs and colds in the book's example?**
a) feto  b) garlic  c) grawa
**Answer: b) garlic**
