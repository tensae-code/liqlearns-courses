# Root kinds — tap, fibrous and storage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: tap roots and fibrous roots.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 4 Topics 3–4 sort plant roots into two great kinds.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How do tap roots and fibrous roots differ, and which leaves go with each?
**Answer:** A tap root has one main root; fibrous roots are many equal thin roots. Most dicots pair wide net-veined leaves with tap roots; most monocots pair narrow parallel-veined leaves with fibrous roots.
