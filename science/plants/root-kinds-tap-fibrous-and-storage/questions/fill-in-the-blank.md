# Root kinds — tap, fibrous and storage — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "A tap root is…" is ______.
**Answer: one main root with smaller branches**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "Fibrous roots are…" is ______.
**Answer: many thin roots of about the same size**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Complete from the lesson: the answer to "Most monocots have…" is ______.
**Answer: narrow parallel-veined leaves and fibrous roots**
