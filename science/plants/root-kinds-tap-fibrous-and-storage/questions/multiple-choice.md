# Root kinds — tap, fibrous and storage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A tap root is…
a) one main root with smaller branches  b) many equal thin roots  c) no root at all  d) a root above the ground
**Answer: a) one main root with smaller branches**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fibrous roots are…
a) many thin roots of about the same size  b) one thick main root  c) roots that store only water  d) leaf veins
**Answer: a) many thin roots of about the same size**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Most monocots have…
a) narrow parallel-veined leaves and fibrous roots  b) wide net-veined leaves and tap roots  c) no roots  d) flowers without seeds
**Answer: a) narrow parallel-veined leaves and fibrous roots**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Most dicots have…
a) wide net-veined leaves and tap roots  b) parallel veins and fibrous roots  c) no leaves  d) small short banana roots
**Answer: a) wide net-veined leaves and tap roots**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Weeds must be pulled out by their roots because…
a) roots hold and support the plant, and it can regrow from them  b) roots are poisonous  c) the leaves will bite  d) the soil needs holes
**Answer: a) roots hold and support the plant, and it can regrow from them**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The banana plant's roots are described as…
a) small and short  b) one huge tap root  c) above-ground  d) net-veined
**Answer: a) small and short**
