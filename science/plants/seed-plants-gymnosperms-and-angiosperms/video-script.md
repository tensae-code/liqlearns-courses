# Video script — Plants — seed plants: gymnosperms and angiosperms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: plants are photosynthesising autotrophs with cellulose cell walls; seed plants split into gymnosperms with naked seeds, often in cones, and angiosperms whose seeds are enclosed in fruit.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
