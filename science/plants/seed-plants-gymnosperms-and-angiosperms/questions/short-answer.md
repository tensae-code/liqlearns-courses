# Plants — seed plants: gymnosperms and angiosperms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three features of the plant plan.
**Answer:** Any three of: photosynthesising autotrophs, place-bound, cellulose-walled cells, reproduce with or without seeds.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How will the field enquiry study Ethiopia's indigenous gymnosperms?
**Answer:** By their habitats, uses and protection.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two angiosperms and two gymnosperms.
**Answer:** Angiosperms: any two of teff, corn, grasses, fruit trees, roses. Gymnosperms: pine, juniper.
