# Plants — seed plants: gymnosperms and angiosperms — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gymnosperms have flowers.
**Answer: False — gymnosperms are flowerless; angiosperms flower.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Teff is an angiosperm.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Plants stay in one place.
**Answer: True**
