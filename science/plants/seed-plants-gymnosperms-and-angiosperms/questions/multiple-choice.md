# Plants — seed plants: gymnosperms and angiosperms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plants feed as —
a) consumers  b) autotrophs that photosynthesise  c) parasites only
**Answer: b) autotrophs that photosynthesise**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Plant cell walls are made of —
a) chitin  b) cellulose  c) murein
**Answer: b) cellulose**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Gymnosperm seeds are —
a) enclosed in fruit  b) naked, often in cones  c) never produced
**Answer: b) naked, often in cones**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Angiosperms are —
a) flowering plants with seeds enclosed in fruit  b) cone-bearing trees only  c) seedless plants
**Answer: a) flowering plants with seeds enclosed in fruit**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Nearly all human plant food comes from —
a) gymnosperms  b) angiosperms  c) mosses
**Answer: b) angiosperms**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Pine and juniper are —
a) angiosperms  b) gymnosperms  c) ferns
**Answer: b) gymnosperms**
