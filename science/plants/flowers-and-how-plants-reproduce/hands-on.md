# Hands-on — Flowers — and how plants reproduce

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Open a flower
With an adult, take a large flower and gently open it. Find the petals, the dust-making stamens and the pistil, and touch a fingertip to a stamen: the dust that comes off is pollen.

## Follow the pollen
Stand by a flowering plant and keep a visitor log: each insect that lands, how long it stays, and where it goes next. Read the log back as pollen's journey.
