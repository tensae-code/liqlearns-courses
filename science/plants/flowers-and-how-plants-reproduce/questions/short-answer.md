# Flowers — and how plants reproduce — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** The flower is the plant's seed-making part: petals outside, stamens making pollen, the pistil holding the future seeds.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Pollination is pollen travelling from stamen to pistil, by wind or by insects.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A bee leaves a flower dusty with pollen and flies to another flower. What job is it doing?
**Answer:** Pollination — carrying pollen from stamen to pistil, the journey reproduction depends on.
