# Flowers — and how plants reproduce — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) The flower is the plant's seed-making part: petals outside, stamens making pollen, the pistil holding the future seeds.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) The flower is the plant's seed-making part: petals outside, stamens making pollen, the pistil holding the future seeds.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Pollination is pollen travelling from stamen to pistil, by wind or by insects.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Pollination is pollen travelling from stamen to pistil, by wind or by insects.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) After pollination, seeds form — each seed the start of a new plant like its parent.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) After pollination, seeds form — each seed the start of a new plant like its parent.**
