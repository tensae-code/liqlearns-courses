Today: **Flowers — and how plants reproduce** — Albania's way, from its Grade 5 guide's situation Riprodhimi i bimëve.
The flower is the seed-maker: petals, stamens making pollen, and the pistil holding the future seeds.
Pollen travels — wind or insect — pollination happens, and seeds form. Flower, journey, seed: that is the road.
