# Parts of a seed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Inside a bean seed.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 4 book opens the seed and names its parts: **testa** (the coat), **micropyle**, **scar (hilum)**, and inside, the **embryo** — with its **plumule** and **radicle** — and the **cotyledons**, the seed's food store. The guide's word list carries the same names: dicotyledon, embryo, endosperm, hilum, micropyle, plumule, radicle.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Maize — and the one-against-two sort.
**Answer:** The maize seed is set against the bean: **monocotyledons against dicotyledons** — one cotyledon against two — with the **endosperm** as the maize seed's food store. The parts are the same story told two ways, and sorting seeds by their cotyledons is the lesson's close.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Inside a bean seed → Maize — and the one-against-two sort — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
