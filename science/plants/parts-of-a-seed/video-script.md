Today: **Parts of a seed** — South Sudan's way, from its Ministry of General Education and Instruction Primary Science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

South Sudan's Primary 4 book opens the seed and names its parts: **testa** (the coat), **micropyle**, **scar (hilum)**, and inside, the **embryo** — with its **plumule** and **radicle** — and the **cotyledons**, the seed's food store. The guide's word list carries the same names: dicotyledon, embryo, endosperm, hilum, micropyle, plumule, radicle.

The maize seed is set against the bean: **monocotyledons against dicotyledons** — one cotyledon against two — with the **endosperm** as the maize seed's food store. The parts are the same story told two ways, and sorting seeds by their cotyledons is the lesson's close.
