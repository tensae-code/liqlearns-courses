# Artificial plant growth substances in farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Artificial growth substances.
**Answer:** People have learned to **make growth substances artificially** — copies and cousins of the plant's own hormones, produced outside the plant and applied to it. Applied at the right time and dose, they steer the plant's growth the way its own substances do.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Uses in agriculture.
**Answer:** In **agriculture** the uses are practical: making cuttings root, setting and ripening fruit, and timing growth to the farmer's calendar. Sri Lanka's book places the farming uses as their own section — the laboratory idea judged in the field, where a wrong dose or a wrong day costs a crop.

**Answer:** Artificial growth substances → Uses in agriculture — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
