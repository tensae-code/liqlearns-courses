Today: **Artificial plant growth substances in farming** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

People have learned to make growth substances artificially — copies and cousins of the plant's own hormones, produced outside the plant and applied to it.

In agriculture the uses are practical: making cuttings root, setting and ripening fruit, and timing growth to the farmer's calendar.
