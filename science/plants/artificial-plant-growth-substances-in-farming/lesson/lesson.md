# Artificial plant growth substances in farming

## Do it first
Before you read: say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is — artificial growth substances and their uses in agriculture. Then read on: this is Sri Lanka's way in — its Grade 9 Science Part I follows the plant's own growth substances with the artificial ones, and asks where farming uses them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Artificial growth substances

People have learned to **make growth substances artificially** — copies and cousins of the plant's own hormones, produced outside the plant and applied to it. Applied at the right time and dose, they steer the plant's growth the way its own substances do.

## Sub lesson 2 — Uses in agriculture

In **agriculture** the uses are practical: making cuttings root, setting and ripening fruit, and timing growth to the farmer's calendar. Sri Lanka's book places the farming uses as their own section — the laboratory idea judged in the field, where a wrong dose or a wrong day costs a crop.
