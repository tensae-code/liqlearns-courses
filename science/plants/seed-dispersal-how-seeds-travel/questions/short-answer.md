# Seed dispersal — how seeds travel — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Seed dispersal is seeds travelling away from their parent — escaping the parent's shade to find open ground.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Seeds travel by shape: wings and parachutes on the wind, hooks on fur, fruit inside animals, floating on water.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A seed is shaped like a tiny parachute. How does it travel, and why does that shape help?
**Answer:** By wind — the parachute holds it in the air longer, so the wind carries it farther from its parent.
