# Video script — Seed dispersal — how seeds travel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Seed dispersal — how seeds travel** — Albania's way, with paper flyers made by the pupils' own hands.
A seed under its parent stands in shade. A seed that travels finds open ground. That travelling is dispersal.
Wings ride wind, hooks ride fur, fruit rides animals — and the paper flyer proves the wind road: shape is the ticket.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
