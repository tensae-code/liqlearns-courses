# Hands-on — Seed dispersal — how seeds travel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Paper-flyer trials
Make paper-flyer seed models in two or three shapes. Drop each from the same height three times, time or step out its flights, and keep the table: Shape | Flight 1 | Flight 2 | Flight 3. Crown the best flyer and say which real seed it copies.

## Seed hunt
Walk outside and gather fallen seeds of as many shapes as you can find. Sort them by way of travelling — wind, hooks, water, inside fruit — and defend each sorting out loud.
