# Protecting plants — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (Let's protect plants!, How to protect plants from disappearing, How to preserve the world of plants, How to protect plants, Why plants must be treated with care), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 5 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks.
