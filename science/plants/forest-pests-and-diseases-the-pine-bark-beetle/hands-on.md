# Hands-on — Forest pests and diseases — the pine bark beetle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — The pine bark beetle (gorgojo descortezador) has always existed. the pine bark beetle (gorgojo descortezador) has always existed in the pine forests, controlling pine populations like any organism in an ecosystem; the problem comes when climate conditions and forest fires boost its populations, attacking trees already weakened by fire or age; the ICF's functions: prevent, watch for, locate and combat fires, plagues and diseases (guide evidence) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
