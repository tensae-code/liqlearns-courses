Today: **How plants make new plants — flowers, pollen, seeds and life cycles** — Saudi Arabia's way, from its book (Saudi Arabia, Grade 2, Part 1 — *Science, Grade 2, Part 1*, printed pp. 38–44).

Unit 1, Ch.
1, Lesson 2 "Plants Produce New Plants": the flower forms the seeds and fruits; pollen is the powder inside the flower that helps make seeds, carried by birds, bees, wind and water; most plants grow from seeds, a seed holds food for the new plant's growth and its parts are studied on the peanut (hard shell, thin coat, the embryo, the food store — "seeds are living things"); seeds travel far by wind and water or in animals' droppings; and every plant has a life cycle like the pomegranate tree's, some lasting weeks and some (the palm) many years
