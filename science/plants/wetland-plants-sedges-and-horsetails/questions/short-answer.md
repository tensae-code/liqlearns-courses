# Wetland plants — sedges and horsetails — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Sedges are a wetland plant kind, told apart from other plant kinds — not lumped in as 'grass'.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Horsetails are a second wetland kind, sorted out from the rest in the same way.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the chapter teach telling sedges and horsetails apart at all?
**Answer:** Because wetland plants that look alike at a glance are different kinds — sorting them is knowing the wetland's living world properly.
