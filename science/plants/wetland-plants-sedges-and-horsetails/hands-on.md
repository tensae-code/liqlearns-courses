# Hands-on — Wetland plants — sedges and horsetails

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the edge
From a wetland visit with an adult, or clear pictures, sort six upright green plants into: sedge, horsetail, other kind. Say the mark you used for each sorting.

## Draw the named one
Draw vetrarkvíðastör from the lesson's description — a horsetail relative with long seed spikes — and label the spikes that name it.
