# Hands-on — Wild plants and sown plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the plants of one place
Choose one small place — a yard corner or path side. List every plant in it and sort the list: wild-growing or sown, with the reason for each.

## Seed arrival hunt
Look for a wild plant growing far from any garden. Say how its seed could have arrived there — wind, water or animal — and what it found that let it grow.
