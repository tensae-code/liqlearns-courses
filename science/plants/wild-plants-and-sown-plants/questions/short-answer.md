# Wild plants and sown plants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What makes a plant wild in this lesson — is it where it grows?
**Answer:** Not the place but the coming-to-be-there: a wild plant grows by itself, unsown, wherever its seed landed and found what it needed.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the sower do that nature does alone for wild plants?
**Answer:** The sower chooses the seed and the place, sows it, and tends the plant as it grows; for wild plants the seed travels by wind, water or animals and grows where it lands.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does Angola's Grade 2 book sort plants this way inside the plant run?
**Answer:** Because after meeting the plants of the locality and their parts, the child can sort them by origin — the wild-growing of the land and the sown of garden and field — the two sorts every planted and unplanted place shows.
