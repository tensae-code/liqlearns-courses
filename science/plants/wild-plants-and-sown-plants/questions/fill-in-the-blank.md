# Wild plants and sown plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Plants that grow by themselves are called _____ plants, espontâneas in Portuguese.
**Answer: wild**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Plants people sow and tend are _____ plants, semeadas.
**Answer: sown**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A wild plant's seed may arrive by wind, _____ or animals.
**Answer: water**
