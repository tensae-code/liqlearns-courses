# Wild plants and sown plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A wild plant (espontânea) is one that…
a) Is dangerous
b) Grows by itself — nobody sowed it
c) Grows only in water
d) Has no leaves
**Answer: b) Grows by itself — nobody sowed it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A wild plant's seed reaches its place by…
a) A shop
b) Wind, water or animals
c) A teacher
d) A lorry only
**Answer: b) Wind, water or animals**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A sown plant (semeada) is one that…
a) People chose and planted, and tend as it grows
b) Grows by itself
c) Never needs water
d) Grows only at night
**Answer: a) People chose and planted, and tend as it grows**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The sort of a plant in this lesson is read from…
a) Its colour
b) How it came to be growing there — by itself or by a sower's hand
c) Its height only
d) Its smell
**Answer: b) How it came to be growing there — by itself or by a sower's hand**
