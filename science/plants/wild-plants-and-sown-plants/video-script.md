Today: **Wild plants and sown plants** — Angola's way: sort plants by how they came to be growing there.

Wild plants — espontâneas — grow by themselves. Their seeds arrive by wind, water or animals, and grow where they land.

Sown plants — semeadas — are planted by people, who choose the seed and the place and tend them: the plants of garden and field.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
