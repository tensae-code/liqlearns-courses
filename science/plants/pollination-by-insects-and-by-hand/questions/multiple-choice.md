# Pollination — by insects and by hand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Pollination is…
a) pollen reaching the stigma  b) a flower opening  c) a seed germinating  d) a bee making honey
**Answer: a) pollen reaching the stigma**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Pollen catches on an insect because its grains are…
a) hooked  b) smooth and wet  c) very heavy  d) bright blue
**Answer: a) hooked**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Bees and moths visit flowers to…
a) collect nectar  b) lay eggs in petals  c) drink water only  d) hide from birds
**Answer: a) collect nectar**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) By hand, pollen is moved with…
a) a brush  b) a watering can  c) a spade  d) a net
**Answer: a) a brush**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Hand pollination is named for…
a) pumpkin and passion fruit flowers  b) grass and maize only  c) pine trees  d) seaweed
**Answer: a) pumpkin and passion fruit flowers**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Without pollination, that flower gives…
a) no fruit and no seed  b) two fruits  c) bigger petals  d) more leaves
**Answer: a) no fruit and no seed**
