# Hands-on — Pollination — by insects and by hand

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Brush-pollinate a flower
In a garden with a flowering pumpkin, gourd or passion fruit (with permission): touch a soft brush to the anthers of an open male flower, then brush the pollen onto the stigma of a female flower. Mark the flower with a loose thread and check it over the following days.
