# Hands-on — Symmetry in flowers and animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the middle
Draw a flower. Draw its middle line. Colour one half, then colour the other half to match it — the drawing itself shows the symmetry.

## The animal check
Draw an animal from the front — a butterfly shape serves well. Draw the middle line and check: does each side answer the other? Write where it matches and where it does not.
