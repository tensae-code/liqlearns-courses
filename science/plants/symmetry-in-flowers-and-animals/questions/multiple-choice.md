# Symmetry in flowers and animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Symmetry is…
a) Being the biggest  b) Matching across a middle — one side the mirror of the other  c) Having many colours  d) Growing fast
**Answer: b) Matching across a middle — one side the mirror of the other**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The first reading is symmetry in…
a) Rocks  b) Flowers  c) Clouds  d) Machines
**Answer: b) Flowers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The second reading is symmetry in…
a) Animal shapes  b) Rivers  c) Letters  d) Stars only
**Answer: a) Animal shapes**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) To check symmetry you…
a) Weigh the shape  b) Find the middle and check the matching halves  c) Count the legs  d) Smell it
**Answer: b) Find the middle and check the matching halves**
