# Symmetry in flowers and animals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach symmetry twice — in flowers and in animals?
**Answer: Because it is one pattern of living shapes, found in both kingdoms; reading it in each unit fixes the idea itself, not only one example.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Say where each reading sits in the Grade 5 book.
**Answer: Symmetry in flowers after the plant lessons; symmetry in animal shapes after the vertebrate and invertebrate lessons.**
