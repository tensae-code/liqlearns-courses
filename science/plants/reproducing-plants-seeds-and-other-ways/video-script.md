Today: **Reproducing plants — seeds and other ways**.

A new plant does not have to start from a seed. Djibouti's Grade 3 guide teaches the ways together: the seed way — a sown seed germinating into a new plant — and the other ways: a bulb sending up its plant, and a cutting, a piece of a living plant, growing into a whole new plant. Different beginnings, the same end: a new plant.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
