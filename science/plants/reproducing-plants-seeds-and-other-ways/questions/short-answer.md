# Reproducing plants — seeds and other ways — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the seed way of growing a new plant?
**Answer: A plant's seeds are sown and, with what a seed needs, germinate and grow into new plants of the same kind.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two ways a plant can develop without starting from a seed.
**Answer: From a bulb, and from a cutting — a piece of a living plant that grows into a whole new plant.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What do seed, bulb and cutting have in common?
**Answer: They are different beginnings that all end at the same place: a new plant.**
