# Reproducing plants — seeds and other ways — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A sown seed, cared for, grows into…
a) A stone  b) A new plant of the same kind  c) A bulb only  d) Nothing ever
**Answer: b) A new plant of the same kind**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a way to grow a new plant WITHOUT a seed?
a) From a photograph  b) From a cutting  c) From a shadow  d) From a song
**Answer: b) From a cutting**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A cutting is…
a) A piece cut from a living plant that can grow into a whole new plant  b) A kind of seed coat  c) A ripe fruit  d) A dry leaf
**Answer: a) A piece cut from a living plant that can grow into a whole new plant**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A bulb is…
a) A packed underground store that sends up a new plant  b) A flower's petal  c) A root hair  d) A seed's shell
**Answer: a) A packed underground store that sends up a new plant**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Seed, bulb and cutting are…
a) Three kinds of soil  b) Different beginnings that all end at a new plant  c) Three names for one seed  d) Ways plants die
**Answer: b) Different beginnings that all end at a new plant**
