# Reproducing plants — seeds and other ways — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A new plant can grow from a _____.
**Answer: seed**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A piece cut from a living plant and planted is called a _____.
**Answer: cutting**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An onion begins its new plant from a _____, not from a seed.
**Answer: bulb**
