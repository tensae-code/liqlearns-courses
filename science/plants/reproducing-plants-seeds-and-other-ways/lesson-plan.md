# Lesson plan — Reproducing plants — seeds and other ways

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Plants · **Lesson:** Reproducing plants — seeds and other ways
**Place in the curriculum:** Plants — appended to Unit 8 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the different ways to grow a new plant — from a seed, and without starting from a seed: from a bulb or a cutting — Djibouti's own way, in its Grade 3 Sciences Expérimentales teacher guide's Module 3, is to teach seed and non-seed ways side by side as one family of ways
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The seed way** — A new plant can grow from a seed: the seed is the plant's own packed beginning, and sown and cared for, it germinates and grows into a new plant of the same kind.
2. **Other ways — bulb and cutting** — A seed is not the only beginning. Djibouti's guide teaches the other ways in the same module: a new plant can also grow from a bulb, or from a cutting — a piece of a plant that grows into a whole new plant, without any seed starting it.
