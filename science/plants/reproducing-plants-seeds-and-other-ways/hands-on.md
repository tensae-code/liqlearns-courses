# Hands-on — Reproducing plants — seeds and other ways

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two beginnings
Set up the lesson's own comparison: one seed and one cutting, each in damp soil or a jar where you can watch it. Label each with its way — SEED or CUTTING — and check both every day for a week. Write one line a day for each: what changed?

## Hunt for beginnings
Walk around your home or school ground and find three growing plants. For each, ask: could this have started from a seed, a bulb or a cutting? Write your best answer for each, with the reason — what you can see that points to its beginning.
