# Hands-on — Seed germination and growth patterns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The daily dig
Sow a row of six bean seeds on the same day. Each day, very gently uncover one seed, look, draw what you see, and cover the next day's seed stays buried. Read your six drawings in order: coat, root, shoot, leaves — germination told by your own hand.

## The part test
Take three seedlings. Leave one whole. Cover the leaves of the second with a small piece of dark paper. Lay the third on its side. After a few days, write what each did — and what each change proves about the job of roots, stems and leaves.

## The ruler race
Two seeds, side by side, same everything. Prediction first, in writing. Then ruler measurements with dates for both, until one is clearly ahead. Finish with one sentence: my prediction was right / wrong, and the numbers that prove it.
