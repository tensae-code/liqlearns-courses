# Video script — Seed germination and growth patterns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Seed germination and growth patterns** — the Bahamas' way: sow it, watch it, measure it.

A seed is not dead — it is waiting. In **germination** it wakes: the **root** comes first, going down; then the **shoot**, going up. Each part has its job — root drinks and holds, stem carries and holds up, leaf catches the light.
And growth has a **pattern** — but you find it with a ruler, not a guess. Measure today, measure again, write the numbers. Then the Grade 4 question: two seeds — which sprouts **first**? Predict — then let the plants answer.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
