# Seed germination and growth patterns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Germination is…
a) A seed drying out for good  b) A seed beginning to grow  c) A plant making seeds  d) A leaf falling off
**Answer: b) A seed beginning to grow**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which part of the plant comes out of the seed first?
a) The flower  b) The leaf  c) The fruit  d) The root
**Answer: d) The root**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The root's function is to…
a) Catch light and make food  b) Hold the plant in the ground and take in water  c) Make seeds  d) Hold the plant up above the leaves
**Answer: b) Hold the plant in the ground and take in water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) How does this lesson say a part's function should be found?
a) By guessing from its colour  b) By asking the seed  c) By experiment — change one thing and watch what happens  d) By measuring its height only
**Answer: c) By experiment — change one thing and watch what happens**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A plant's growth pattern is read off…
a) One look at the plant  b) Ruler measurements taken again and again, with dates  c) The colour of the leaves  d) The shape of the pot
**Answer: b) Ruler measurements taken again and again, with dates**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) In the two-seed race, the right order is…
a) Measure first, never predict  b) Predict which seed germinates first, then compare growth rates by measurement  c) Predict, then refuse to check  d) Compare two plants given different water and light
**Answer: b) Predict which seed germinates first, then compare growth rates by measurement**
