# Seed germination and growth patterns — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) _____ is the seed beginning to grow.
**Answer: Germination**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In germination, the _____ comes out first, growing down.
**Answer: root**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The _____ catches light and makes the plant's food.
**Answer: leaf**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Measure a seedling's growth with a _____ , and write the number with the date.
**Answer: ruler**
