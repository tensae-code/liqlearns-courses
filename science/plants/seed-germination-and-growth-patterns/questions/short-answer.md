# Seed germination and growth patterns — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tell germination in order.
**Answer: The seed takes in water, swells and splits its coat; the root grows down first; then the shoot grows up; then the first leaves open.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the function of the leaf, and one experiment that shows it.
**Answer: The leaf catches light and makes the plant's food. Experiment: keep a leaf (or a plant's leaves) in the dark / covered — it pales and the plant grows weakly, showing the leaf's light-catching work.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Your measurements read: day 1 — 2 cm, day 3 — 3 cm, day 5 — 7 cm. When did the seedling grow fastest?
**Answer: Between day 3 and day 5 — it grew 4 cm in those two days, against 1 cm in the first two days.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why must the two seeds in the race get the same soil, water and light?
**Answer: So the comparison is fair: if everything else is the same, the difference in germination and growth rates belongs to the seeds themselves, and the prediction is truly tested.**
