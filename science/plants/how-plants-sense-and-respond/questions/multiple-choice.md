# How plants sense and respond — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When a plant's surroundings change, the change…
a) Never shows  b) Shows in the plant  c) Shows only in animals  d) Stops the plant being alive
**Answer: b) Shows in the plant**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A plant perceives its surroundings…
a) With eyes and ears like ours  b) Not at all  c) In its own way — the changes register and show in the plant  d) Only when it is moved
**Answer: c) In its own way — the changes register and show in the plant**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In Guatemala's guide, plants show environmental changes through their perception of…
a) Human voices  b) The cycles of time  c) Colours  d) Other plants' names
**Answer: b) The cycles of time**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The cycles of time include…
a) Day and night, season and season  b) Only clock time  c) Only school terms  d) Nothing a plant can meet
**Answer: a) Day and night, season and season**
