# How plants sense and respond — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How can a plant sense its surroundings without eyes or ears?
**Answer:** The plant perceives in its own way: changes in its surroundings register in it and show in its state and growth — reading the plant is reading a record of its surroundings.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State Guatemala's guide's teaching about plants and the cycles of time.
**Answer:** Plants show environmental changes through their perception of the cycles of time — the plant's life is timed by the turning of day and season, and its changes come with that turning.
