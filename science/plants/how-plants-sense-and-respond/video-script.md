The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **How plants sense and respond** — Guatemala's way: plants perceive the cycles of time.

A plant has no eyes and no ears, and it cannot walk away. But it is not cut off from its surroundings: it perceives them, and when they change, the change shows in the plant.
Guatemala's Grade 7 guide names what the plant perceives above all: the cycles of time — day and night, season and season. The plant's life is timed by those cycles, and its changes come with their turning.
So read a plant the way the guide teaches: what it shows is its answer to its surroundings.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
