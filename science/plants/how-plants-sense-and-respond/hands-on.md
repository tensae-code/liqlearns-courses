# Hands-on — How plants sense and respond

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The week's plant watch
Pick one plant and visit it at three different times of day, and on different days. Each visit, note: the light, the warmth, the wet or dry ground — and the plant's state. You are collecting what the plant shows about its surroundings.

## The cycles-of-time chart
Make a chart with two columns: The cycle turns | The plant shows. Fill it from your week's watch: each turning of day or weather on the left, what the plant showed on the right. Finish the chart with one sentence in Guatemala's guide's frame: this plant shows environmental changes through its perception of the cycles of time because…
