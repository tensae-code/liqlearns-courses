# Meadows and pastures — homes of grasses and pollinators — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Meadows and pastures — grass lands at work.
**Answer:** **Meadows** and **pastures** are the grass lands: meadows are kept as **hay meadows**, grass grown and cut for hay, and pastures are grass land where animals graze.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Medicinal plants of the meadow.
**Answer:** Among the meadow grasses grow **medicinal plants** — plants people gather for healing.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Butterflies and bees — the pollinators.
**Answer:** Over the meadow flowers fly **butterflies and bees**, and their visiting is work: they **pollinate the flowers** as they move from flower to flower.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Meadows and pastures — grass lands at work → Medicinal plants of the meadow → Butterflies and bees — the pollinators — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
