# Hands-on — Meadows and pastures — homes of grasses and pollinators

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Meadow watch (outside, ten quiet minutes)
Sit or stand at the edge of any grassy place — a meadow, pasture, verge or lawn — for ten quiet minutes. Write down: three plants you see, and every butterfly or bee visit you count. Say which visits were pollination at work.
