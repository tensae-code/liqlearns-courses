# Video script — Meadows and pastures — homes of grasses and pollinators

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Meadows and pastures — homes of grasses and pollinators** — Serbia's way, from its Grade 3 *Природа и друштво* (Ливаде и пашњаци (Meadows and pastures), printed p. 60).

**Meadows** and **pastures** are the grass lands: meadows are kept as **hay meadows**, grass grown and cut for hay, and pastures are grass land where animals graze. One grass community, two uses — cutting and grazing.

Among the meadow grasses grow **medicinal plants** — plants people gather for healing. The meadow is not grass alone: its useful plants are part of what the community gives.

Over the meadow flowers fly **butterflies and bees**, and their visiting is work: they **pollinate the flowers** as they move from flower to flower. No pollinators, no new flowers — the winged visitors hold the meadow's future.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
