Today: **Plant growth substances** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

A plant has no nerves, yet its growth is directed: plant growth substances — the plant's own hormones — are chemicals made in one part of the plant that steer growth in another.

Growth substances govern the plant's great changes: germination, stem growth, flowering, fruit ripening and leaf fall are all switched and timed by them.
