## Sub lesson 1 — Plant growth substances — the plant's own hormones

A plant has no nerves, yet its growth is directed: **plant growth substances** — the plant's own **hormones** — are chemicals made in one part of the plant that steer growth in another. They are the plant's internal messages, carried to where growth must speed up, slow down or turn.

## Sub lesson 2 — What growth substances do

Growth substances govern the plant's great changes: **germination, stem growth, flowering, fruit ripening and leaf fall** are all switched and timed by them. Sri Lanka's book teaches them by their **effects**: watch what a substance does to growth and you have read the message it carries.
