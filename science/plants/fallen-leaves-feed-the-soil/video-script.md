# Video script — Fallen leaves feed the soil; plants hold the soil

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Leaf falls. Leaf rots. Soil grows rich.
Roots grip. Leaves shield. The slope keeps its soil.
Trees shelter animals and rest people.
Clear the plants, and the rain takes the land. Keep them, and the land keeps you.
