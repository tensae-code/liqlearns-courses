# Fallen leaves feed the soil; plants hold the soil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Fallen leaves and grass, when they rot, do what to the topsoil?**
a) make it poorer  b) raise its fertility  c) turn it to stone
**Answer: b) raise its fertility**

**2. Plants stop soil erosion because —**
a) their roots grip the soil and their leaves break the rain  b) they drink all the rain  c) they are heavy
**Answer: a) their roots grip the soil and their leaves break the rain**

**3. The dark soil under old trees is rich because —**
a) stones fell there  b) generations of leaves rotted into it  c) people dug it daily
**Answer: b) generations of leaves rotted into it**

**4. Clearing plants from a slope makes erosion —**
a) stop  b) worse  c) impossible
**Answer: b) worse**
