# Hands-on — Fallen leaves feed the soil; plants hold the soil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two soils report
Compare tree soil and bare soil in writing: colour, softness, smell, what lives in each. End with the cycle sentence.

## Slope planting promise
Find one small eroding patch at school or home. Plant or protect grass on it, and note the change after the next rains.

## Shelter census
Sit under one tree for fifteen minutes and log every creature that uses it — birds, insects, lizards, people. The tree is a shelter with tenants.
