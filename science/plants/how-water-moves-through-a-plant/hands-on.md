# Hands-on — How water moves through a plant

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow the colour
Place a fresh stem with leaves in a cup of coloured water. Look again after some hours and the next day: draw where the colour has travelled and say which parts of the journey you have seen.

## Catch the leaving water
With an adult, tie a clear dry bag loosely over a leafy branch in daylight. Later, look inside the bag: the mist or drops there are water that left through the leaves. Say the whole journey that water made.
