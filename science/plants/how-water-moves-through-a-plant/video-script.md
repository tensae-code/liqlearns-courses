# Video script — How water moves through a plant

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **How water moves through a plant** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

A plant is drinking — silently. Water comes in through the roots, climbs up the stem, and leaves through the leaves into the air.
Put a stem in coloured water and watch the road light up. In, up, out — the plant is water's road from the soil to the sky.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
