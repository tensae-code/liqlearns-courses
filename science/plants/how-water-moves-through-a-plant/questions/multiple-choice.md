# How water moves through a plant — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Water enters a plant through its…
a) Roots  b) Flowers  c) Leaves only  d) Bark
**Answer: a) Roots**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) From the roots, water travels…
a) Down into the soil again  b) Up along the stem  c) Sideways into the air  d) Into the fruit only
**Answer: b) Up along the stem**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The coloured-water test shows…
a) That plants dislike water  b) That stems are hollow tubes of air  c) The water's road up the stem, marked by the colour  d) Nothing
**Answer: c) The water's road up the stem, marked by the colour**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Water leaves the plant through its…
a) Roots  b) Stem's base  c) Flowers only  d) Leaves, passing out into the air
**Answer: d) Leaves, passing out into the air**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The plant is described as a through-road because water…
a) Enters at the roots, climbs the stem and leaves at the leaves — soil to sky  b) Never moves inside it  c) Enters at the leaves only  d) Stops at the stem
**Answer: a) Enters at the roots, climbs the stem and leaves at the leaves — soil to sky**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The journey's order is…
a) Out at leaves, down the stem, in at roots  b) In through roots, up the stem, out through leaves  c) In at leaves, out at roots  d) Up the roots, out at the stem
**Answer: b) In through roots, up the stem, out through leaves**
