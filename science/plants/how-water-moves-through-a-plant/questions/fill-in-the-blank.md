# How water moves through a plant — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Water enters the plant through its _____.
**Answer: roots**

**2.** Water travels up along the _____.
**Answer: stem**

**3.** Water leaves the plant through its _____.
**Answer: leaves**

**4.** The plant is a through-road for water from soil to _____.
**Answer: sky**
