# Life on lava fields — mosses and ferns first — multiple choice

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) New lava is colonised first by mosses and ferns — hraungambri, the woolly fringe-moss, among them.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
