# Life on lava fields — mosses and ferns first — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** New lava is colonised first by mosses and ferns — hraungambri, the woolly fringe-moss, among them.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Mosses and ferns are small seedless plants that can take hold on bare lava where flowering plants cannot yet live.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why are mosses and ferns the first settlers, and not trees?
**Answer:** They are small seedless plants that can take hold on bare rock with no soil — trees need what the first settlers only later help to build.
