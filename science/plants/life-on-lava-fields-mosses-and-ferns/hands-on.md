# Hands-on — Life on lava fields — mosses and ferns first

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the capsules
Find a fern frond (or a clear picture of one). Count or estimate the spore capsules on it, Iceland's way, and write the number down with the frond's size.

## Bare to green
Draw one lava slope three times: fresh and bare, moss settling, moss and ferns together. Label who arrived first in each picture.
