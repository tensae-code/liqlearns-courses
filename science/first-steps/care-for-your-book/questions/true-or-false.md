# Care for Your Book — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A book should be kept dry and clean.**
**Answer: True** — The book-care message says to keep the book dry and clean.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) If a page tears, you should throw the book away.**
**Answer: False** — A torn page can be repaired — the book can still be read.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) The book-care rules change completely in Grade 4.**
**Answer: False** — The same message stands at the front of the Grade 4 book.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) The book-care message disappears from the older books.**
**Answer: False** — It stands at printed page ii of the Grade 5 and Grade 6 books, exactly as in the younger books.
