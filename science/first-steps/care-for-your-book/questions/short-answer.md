# Care for Your Book — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name two ways to care for your book.**
**Answer:** Cover it; keep it dry and clean; use a bookmark; never tear pages; repair torn pages (any two).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Your friend folds page corners to hold his place. What kind thing can you show him?**
**Answer:** Show him how to use a bookmark instead.
