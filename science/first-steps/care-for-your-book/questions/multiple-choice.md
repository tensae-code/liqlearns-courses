# Care for Your Book — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What should you do first with a new book?**
a) Cover it to protect it  b) Tear out the pictures  c) Leave it in the rain
**Answer: a) Cover it to protect it** — Covering the book keeps its cover from wearing out.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How do you hold your place in a book?**
a) Fold the page corner  b) Use a bookmark  c) Tear the page out
**Answer: b) Use a bookmark** — A bookmark holds your place without damaging the page.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why do we not tear pages or pictures out of a book?**
a) Pages are not needed  b) The book must stay whole so everyone can learn from it  c) Tearing is faster
**Answer: b) The book must stay whole so everyone can learn from it** — A book with torn pages cannot teach properly.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where should a book be kept?**
a) In a wet place  b) In a clean, dry place  c) On the ground outside
**Answer: b) In a clean, dry place** — Books stay good in clean, dry places.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who should care for your book?**
a) Only the teacher  b) You, the pupil who uses it  c) Nobody
**Answer: b) You, the pupil who uses it** — The book-care message is spoken to the pupil.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) The Grade 4 book's care message tells pupils to —**
a) tear out hard pages  b) cover the book and keep it dry and clean  c) write on every page
**Answer: b) cover the book and keep it dry and clean** — The message opens the Grade 4 book too, at printed page ii.

**7. (Sub lesson 3) If a page tears, the message says to —**
a) hide the book  b) tear out the page  c) repair the torn page
**Answer: c) repair the torn page** — A mended page can still be read.
