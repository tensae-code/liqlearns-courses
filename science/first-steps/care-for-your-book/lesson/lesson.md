# Care for Your Book

## Do it first

Hold your book. Look at its cover, its pages, its corners. Is it clean? Is any page torn or folded? Today you learn how to keep it like new.

## Sub lesson 1 — The book-care message

Every book in this course opens with the same message to you, the pupil: this book is yours to learn from, and it will only stay good if you care for it.

Cover the book — with plastic, or with old newspaper — so the cover does not wear out. Keep it dry and clean. Never tear out pages or pictures.

[Picture 12000 — a pupil covering a book to protect it]

## Sub lesson 2 — Using the book kindly

Use a bookmark to hold your place instead of folding a page corner. If a page tears, repair it — a mended page can still be read. Treat the book gently every day, and it will teach you all year.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: how to care for your school book so it stays clean, whole and readable all year. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Cover your own book today, with plastic or clean old newspaper.
2. Make a bookmark from a strip of paper or card.
3. Check every page of your book; gently mend any torn page you find.
