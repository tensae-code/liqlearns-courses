# Video script — Care for Your Book

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is your book.

Cover it. Keep it dry. Keep it clean.

Never tear a page. Never tear a picture.

Use a bookmark to hold your place.

If a page tears, mend it.

Care for your book — and your book will teach you all year.
