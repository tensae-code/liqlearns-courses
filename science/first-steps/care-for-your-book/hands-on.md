# Hands-on — Care for Your Book

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Covering day
With an older person's help, wrap your book's cover in plastic or newspaper, folding the edges neatly inside.

## Bookmark making
Cut a strip of card, draw a picture on it, and use it every time you stop reading.

## Hand it over carefully
Practise the last rule: wrap your book, check it page by page, and hand it to a partner with two hands, the way you would hand over something borrowed and precious. Your partner checks it and hands it back the same way.

## Check the front of a big book
Take any Grade 5 or Grade 6 book. Find printed page ii and read its message aloud to a younger pupil, the way it was once read to you.
