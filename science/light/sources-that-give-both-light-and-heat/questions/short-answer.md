# Sources that give both light and heat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Light sources and heat sources — the overlap" teach?
**Answer:** The unit's closing topic is **the relationship between sources of light and sources of heat**: the two families are not separate — some sources give light, some give heat, and some, like the sun and the flame, give **both together**.

**2.** What does the part "Sorting the sources" teach?
**Answer:** The learner's work is the sort: take the sources met in the unit — the sun, flames, lamps, fires — and place each by what it gives: light, heat, or both.
