# Sources that give both light and heat

## Do it first
Before you read: light a candle in your mind. It gives light — hold your hand near it: what else does it give? Now think of a torch bulb and of the sun. Which of your sources give light only, heat only, or both? Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — its Primary 2 unit joins light and heat in one unit, and closes by joining their sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Light sources and heat sources — the overlap

The unit's closing topic is **the relationship between sources of light and sources of heat**: the two families are not separate — some sources give light, some give heat, and some, like the sun and the flame, give **both together**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sorting the sources

The learner's work is the sort: take the sources met in the unit — the sun, flames, lamps, fires — and place each by what it gives: light, heat, or both. The sort is the proof that the relationship is understood, not just named.

## The key ideas

- **Light sources and heat sources — the overlap** — the overlap of the two families.
- **Sorting the sources** — sorting the sources.
