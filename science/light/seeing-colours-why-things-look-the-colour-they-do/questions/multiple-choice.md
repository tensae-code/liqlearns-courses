# Seeing colours — why things look the colour they do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A solid body looks the colour it…
a) lets pass  b) reflects  c) is painted inside  d) absorbs  
**Answer: b) reflects**

**2.** Black bodies…
a) reflect all colours  b) reflect none — they take all in  c) reflect black light  d) let all colours pass  
**Answer: b) reflect none — they take all in**

**3.** The spinning colour disc gathers the seven colours into…
a) black  b) a rainbow  c) white  d) grey paint  
**Answer: c) white**
