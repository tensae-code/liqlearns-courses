# Seeing colours — why things look the colour they do — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What colour does a see-through coloured body look, and why?
**Answer: The colour of the light it lets pass — the other colours are held back, so only its own colour reaches the eye.**

**2.** A white body and a black body: what does each do with the colours falling on it?
**Answer: A white body reflects all colours; a black body takes all colours in and reflects none.**
