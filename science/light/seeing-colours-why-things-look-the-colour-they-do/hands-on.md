# Hands-on — Seeing colours — why things look the colour they do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The spinning disc
Make a paper disc with the seven colours in order, spin it fast on a pencil point, and record what colour it gathers into. Compare with the splitting lesson: prism apart, disc together.

## Coloured-glass predictions
Before looking, write your prediction for a red object seen through red, green and blue coloured glass (or sweet wrappers) in turn. Then look, and mark each prediction right or wrong, with the rule that decides it.
