Today: **Seeing colours — why things look the colour they do**.

Spin the seven colours and they gather back into white. A see-through body looks the colour it lets pass; a solid body looks the colour it reflects — yellow reflects yellow, white reflects all, black reflects none.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
