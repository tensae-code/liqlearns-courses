# Hands-on — Reflection — laws, mirror images and the periscope

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Build a periscope (Activity 7.7)
Two small mirrors in a slotted cardboard tube or box, both at 45° and parallel. Test it over a wall, then draw the full ray path and label incident ray, reflected ray and normal at both mirrors.

## Image hunt (Activities 7.5–7.6)
Use a mirror and a marked object to locate where the image sits: move the object, watch the image keep the same distance behind. Then run the four-property face test and record each verdict.
