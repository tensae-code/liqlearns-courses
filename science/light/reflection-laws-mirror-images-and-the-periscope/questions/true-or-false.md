# Reflection — laws, mirror images and the periscope — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Diffuse reflection breaks the laws of reflection.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A mirror image is laterally inverted — left and right are swapped.
**Answer: True**
