# Reflection — laws, mirror images and the periscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Regular (specular) reflection happens on —
a) rough surfaces, rays scattered  b) polished surfaces, reflected rays parallel  c) any surface in the dark
**Answer: b) polished surfaces, reflected rays parallel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The normal is the line —
a) along the mirror's surface  b) standing square to the surface where the ray strikes  c) joining object and image
**Answer: b) standing square to the surface where the ray strikes** — angles are measured from the normal.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The first law of reflection states —
a) angle of incidence equals angle of reflection  b) reflected rays are always parallel  c) light is absorbed at mirrors
**Answer: a) angle of incidence equals angle of reflection**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A plane mirror's image is virtual, which means —
a) it is upside down  b) no rays actually pass through it, so no screen can catch it  c) it is smaller than the object
**Answer: b) no rays actually pass through it, so no screen can catch it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The image is as far ______ the mirror as the object is in front.
a) in front of, doubled  b) behind  c) beside
**Answer: b) behind** — equal distances, front and behind.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** AMBULANCE is written reversed on the vehicle so that —
a) it looks smart  b) a driver ahead, seeing it in a mirror, reads it correctly  c) only ambulances can read it
**Answer: b) a driver ahead, seeing it in a mirror, reads it correctly** — the mirror undoes the reversal — lateral inversion at work (teacher evidence).
