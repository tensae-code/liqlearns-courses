# Video script — Reflection — laws, mirror images and the periscope

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A full build-along / walkthrough script, as the placement names a video need for this lesson.

---

We build a periscope. Two mirrors. One tube. You will see over the wall.
Cut two slots in the tube, one near the top, one near the bottom, on opposite sides.
Push the first mirror into the top slot. Tilt it. Forty-five degrees. It must look down the tube.
Push the second mirror into the bottom slot. Tilt it the same way. The two mirrors face each other.
Hold the tube tall. Put your eye to the bottom opening. Look!
You see what is over the wall. The light bounced twice — mirror to mirror to your eye. Each bounce: angle in equals angle out.
Your turn. Build it. Draw the light's path.
