# Hands-on — Diffraction gratings — grooved surfaces splitting light by wavelength

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The CD rainbow
Darken the room. Shine a torch at a CD and catch the reflected colours on a pale wall, as the pupil practical runs it. Tilt the disc slowly and watch the colours sweep. Say aloud: the grooves are sorting the wavelengths — each colour is being sent out at its own angle.

## Read the order
Hold the disc still at an angle that shows a clear band of colours. On paper, draw the band and label the order from violet to red. Say which end is the shortest wavelength (violet) and which the longest (red) — the spectrum the grating shows is ordered purely by wavelength.

## One colour only
Shine a single-colour light (a coloured LED or a torch behind coloured film) at the disc. The broad rainbow collapses toward a narrow band. Say aloud why: there is now only one wavelength for the grooves to send out, so it reinforces only around its own angle.
