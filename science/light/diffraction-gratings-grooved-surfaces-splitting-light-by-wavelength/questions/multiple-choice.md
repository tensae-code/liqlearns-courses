# Diffraction gratings — grooved surfaces splitting light by wavelength — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A diffraction grating is…
a) A surface ruled with many fine, evenly spaced grooves  b) A triangular glass block  c) A coloured filter  d) A mirror with paint on it
**Answer: a) A surface ruled with many fine, evenly spaced grooves**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A CD works as a grating because…
a) It is painted with rainbow dye  b) Its track turns lie side by side like grooves, spaced on the scale of a wavelength of light  c) It is round  d) It contains a tiny prism
**Answer: b) Its track turns lie side by side like grooves, spaced on the scale of a wavelength of light**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) When light falls on a grating, each groove…
a) Absorbs the light  b) Changes its colour  c) Returns a copy of the wave, offset from its neighbours' copies  d) Bends it like glass
**Answer: c) Returns a copy of the wave, offset from its neighbours' copies**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The colours fan out because…
a) Each wavelength's copies reinforce at its own angle  b) The grooves are different colours  c) The torch flickers  d) The wall is painted
**Answer: a) Each wavelength's copies reinforce at its own angle**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Tilting the CD makes the colours sweep because…
a) The paint moves  b) Tilting changes the geometry, so which wavelength arrives in step toward your eye changes  c) The torch dims  d) The grooves melt
**Answer: b) Tilting changes the geometry, so which wavelength arrives in step toward your eye changes**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A prism and a grating differ in that…
a) Only the prism splits light  b) The grating is bigger  c) The prism splits light by bending it in glass; the grating splits it by interference among groove-copies  d) The grating absorbs colours
**Answer: c) The prism splits light by bending it in glass; the grating splits it by interference among groove-copies**
