# Diffraction gratings — grooved surfaces splitting light by wavelength — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A surface of many fine, evenly spaced grooves that splits light by wavelength is a diffraction _____.
**Answer: grating**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Wave copies arriving in step _____ each other; out of step, they cancel.
**Answer: reinforce**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Each wavelength is sent out strongly at its own _____.
**Answer: angle**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A prism splits light by bending; a grating splits it by _____.
**Answer: interference**
