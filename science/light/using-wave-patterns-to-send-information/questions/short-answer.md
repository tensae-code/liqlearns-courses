# Using wave patterns to send information — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Patterns can carry information — light signals and codes.
**Answer:** A wave on its own just repeats; a patterned wave — flashes long and short, in an agreed order — can stand for letters and words: light signals and codes (Grade 4, 4.3.3, printed p. 126).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Designing a solution to an information-transfer problem.
**Answer:** Utah's task is a design task: design a solution to an information-transfer problem using wave patterns — choose the wave, design the code, and make the pattern clear enough to be read at the far end.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Patterns can carry information — light signals and codes → Designing a solution to an information-transfer problem — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
