# Hands-on — Gathering and focusing light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing with your hands
On a bright day, with an adult, hold a magnifying glass (or a clear bottle of water) so sunlight gathers to a bright spot on a card — do not look at the Sun, and do not leave the spot on anything that can burn. Move the glass until the spot is smallest and brightest, and say what gathering did to the light.
