## Sub lesson 1 — Gathering light — bringing rays together

Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 textbook asks what happens when light is **gathered**: light that was spread out is brought together into a smaller place. The gathered light does not stay the same — concentrating it changes what it can do.

## Sub lesson 2 — What gathered light does

Where light is gathered, it falls **brighter** on the small place it is gathered to — the same light, packed tighter. That is the lesson's test: spread light lights a whole page weakly; gathered light lights one spot strongly.
