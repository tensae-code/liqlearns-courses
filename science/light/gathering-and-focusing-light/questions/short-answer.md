# Gathering and focusing light — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Gathering light — bringing rays together.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 textbook asks what happens when light is **gathered**: light that was spread out is brought together into a smaller place. The gathered light does not stay the same — concentrating it changes what it can do.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: What gathered light does.
**Answer:** Where light is gathered, it falls **brighter** on the small place it is gathered to — the same light, packed tighter. That is the lesson's test: spread light lights a whole page weakly; gathered light lights one spot strongly.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Gathering light — bringing rays together → What gathered light does — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
