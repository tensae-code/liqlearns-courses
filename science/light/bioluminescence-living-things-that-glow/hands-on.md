# Hands-on — Bioluminescence — living things that glow

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dark-room source hunt
In a dark room or at dusk, list every light you can see. Sort the list: human-made, natural but not alive, and — if you meet one — natural and alive. Say which family was hardest to find, and why.

## Draw the third family
Draw the light-source families as three boxes: lamps people make; the sun, moon and stars; and living light. Put one example you have seen yourself in each box you can fill.
