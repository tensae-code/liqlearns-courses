# Video script — Bioluminescence — living things that glow

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The sun makes light. A lamp makes light.
But some **living things** make light too — from their own bodies.
That is **bioluminescence**: living light.
No lamp. No sun. The light-maker is alive.
