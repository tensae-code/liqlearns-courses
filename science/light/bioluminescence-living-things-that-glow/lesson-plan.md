# Lesson plan — Bioluminescence — living things that glow

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Bioluminescence — living things that glow
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — appended to Unit 5 (Light) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** some living things make their own light — bioluminescence (المضيئات الحيوية) — a light source that is alive
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. It follows Jordan's Grade 2 book, where it is an enrichment inside the light unit, after Light and its sources.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Living light** — Jordan's enrichment: there are living things that glow — they produce light from their own bodies, so the source of the light is alive.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Light — Sources of light. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what bioluminescence means: living things making their own light.
2. Sort this source against the two known families: it is natural, but unlike the sun, moon and stars it is alive.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; a dark room or a dark evening for the hands-on.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner answers the opening question out loud before any explanation (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's sub lesson (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a dark scene lit only by a glowing living thing — the student names the source of the light.
   - ADD GIF: a 1–2 second GIF of the glow brightening and fading — the student says what changed.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson in their own words (lesson/lesson.md).
4. **Questions** — from questions/, served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the glow must come from a lamp or the sun → the lesson's one thing: the light-maker here is the living body itself.
