# Bioluminescence — living things that glow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Bioluminescence is —**
a) light reflected by the moon  b) light made by a living thing's own body  c) light from a lamp
**Answer: b) light made by a living thing's own body**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A glowing living thing belongs to which family of sources?**
a) human-made  b) natural and alive  c) it is not a source
**Answer: b) natural and alive**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a glowing living thing different from the sun?**
a) it does not glow  b) it is a lamp  c) it is alive
**Answer: c) it is alive**
