# Video script — Refraction — why the spoon looks broken

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script in the super-simple voice; the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so this is the standard short script.

---

Put the spoon in the water. Look! It looks broken.
Take it out. Not broken. The spoon is fine.
The light bent. Light bends when it crosses into water or glass. That bending is refraction.
Going into glass: the ray bends towards the line. Coming out: it bends away.
Straight down? No bend at all.
