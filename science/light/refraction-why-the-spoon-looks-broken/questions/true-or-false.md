# Refraction — why the spoon looks broken — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The spoon really bends while it is in the water.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Light slows down when it enters glass from air.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A perpendicular ray bends towards the normal.
**Answer: False**
