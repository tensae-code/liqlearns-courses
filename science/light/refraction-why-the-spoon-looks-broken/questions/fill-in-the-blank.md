# Refraction — why the spoon looks broken — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Light bending at a boundary between media is called ______.
**Answer: refraction**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Air into glass: the ray bends ______ the normal.
**Answer: towards**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Glass into air: the ray bends ______ from the normal.
**Answer: away**
