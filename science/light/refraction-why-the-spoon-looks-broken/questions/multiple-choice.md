# Refraction — why the spoon looks broken — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Refraction is the change of light's direction when it —
a) hits a mirror  b) crosses obliquely into a different transparent medium  c) is switched off
**Answer: b) crosses obliquely into a different transparent medium**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A ray arriving perpendicular to the boundary —
a) bends most of all  b) goes straight, unbent  c) turns back
**Answer: b) goes straight, unbent** — the perpendicular arrival is the exception.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The spoon in water looks broken because —
a) water softens metal  b) light from its underwater part bends at the surface, so the eye misplaces it  c) the glass magnifies it
**Answer: b) light from its underwater part bends at the surface, so the eye misplaces it**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Entering a denser medium, a ray bends —
a) away from the normal  b) towards the normal  c) backwards
**Answer: b) towards the normal** — in — towards; and it slows.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Entering a less dense medium (glass to air), a ray —
a) speeds up and bends away from the normal  b) stops  c) bends towards the normal
**Answer: a) speeds up and bends away from the normal**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Incident ray, normal and refracted ray lie in —
a) three planes  b) one plane  c) no plane
**Answer: b) one plane** — the section's law-form.
