# Lesson plan — Refraction — why the spoon looks broken

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Refraction — why the spoon looks broken
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** when light crosses obliquely into a different transparent medium its direction changes at the boundary — refraction — bending towards the normal entering a denser medium and away entering a less dense one, while a perpendicular ray goes straight; the bent spoon and the part-dipped pencil are its everyday proofs
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Reflection — laws, mirror images and the periscope: light bouncing measured; now light crossing, and bending.

## If the learner is stuck
- Says the spoon bends → lift it out: whole. The LIGHT bent at the boundary; the spoon never did.
- Swaps the bend directions → anchor on the pair: entering denser = towards the normal (and slower); leaving = away (and faster).
