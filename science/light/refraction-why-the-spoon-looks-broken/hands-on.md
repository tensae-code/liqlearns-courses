# Hands-on — Refraction — why the spoon looks broken

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Broken-spoon drawing (Activity 7.8)
Spoon and pencil in water, drawn from the side exactly as seen, then lifted out and drawn whole. The pair of drawings, with the water line marked, is the lesson's evidence.

## Block crossing
If a glass block or thick glass is available, lay it over printed text and view at a slant: the text shifts. View straight down: no shift. Record both and connect each to the bend rule.
