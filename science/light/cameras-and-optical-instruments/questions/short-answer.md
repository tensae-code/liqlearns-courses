# Cameras and optical instruments — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The camera.
**Answer:** Yemen's Grade 8 Science book closes its refraction unit with the camera: the instrument that uses lenses to catch light and hold a picture of what stands before it — taught straight after how the eye sees things, so the camera is read as the eye's built partner..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Optical instruments.
**Answer:** The same lesson widens from the camera to optical instruments as a family: the built tools that work by steering light — through lenses and mirrors — so the eye can see what it could not see alone, nearer, farther or clearer..

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The camera → Optical instruments — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
