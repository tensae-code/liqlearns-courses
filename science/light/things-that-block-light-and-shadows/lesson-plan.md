# Lesson plan — Things that block light, and shadows

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Things that block light, and shadows
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — after Things light passes through (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** wall, wooden table and book block light; blocked light is why houses are dark at night and why you cast a shadow in the sun; leafy trees give shade
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Things light passes through — the two families are taught as a pair, passer and blocker.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the blocking materials: wall, wooden table, book.
2. Say why a house is dark inside at night: its walls and roof block light, and no light is being made inside.
3. Say what a shadow is: the dark shape where your body blocks the sunlight, and why leafy trees give shade.

4. Do the book's Experiment 5 in words: an opaque body (ብርሃን ከል) 5 cm before a candle leaves a dark place behind it — and define a shadow as that lightless place (Grade 4 depth).
## What you need
Sunshine and your own body — the shadow needs nothing else; a torch and a book for the indoor proof.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a shadow is a thing the body makes → the shadow is made by the light being stopped: no light blocked, no shadow; the dark shape is the light's absence shaped like you.
- Thinks walls 'make' darkness → they block; open the door at midday and light walks in.
- This lesson's next door: Sources of light — if blockers make dark, what makes light?
