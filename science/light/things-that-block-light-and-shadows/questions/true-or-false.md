# Things that block light, and shadows — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. A house is dark inside at night partly because its walls block light.**
**Answer: True**

**2. A book held to a torch beam makes a dark patch behind it.**
**Answer: True**
**3. In the book's experiment the opaque body is placed 5 cm before the candle.**
**Answer: True**
