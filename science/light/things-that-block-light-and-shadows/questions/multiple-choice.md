# Things that block light, and shadows — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which of these blocks light?**
a) glass  b) air  c) a wooden table
**Answer: c) a wooden table**

**2. A shadow is —**
a) a dark shape where the body blocks the sunlight  b) smoke from the ground  c) a hole in the ground
**Answer: a) a dark shape where the body blocks the sunlight**

**3. Which makes the longest shadow?**
a) the midday sun  b) the low evening sun  c) no sun
**Answer: b) the low evening sun**

**4. At midday, shadows are —**
a) longest  b) shortest  c) gone forever
**Answer: b) shortest**
**5. An opaque body (ብርሃን ከል) is —**
a) a body light passes through freely  b) a body that blocks light  c) a light source
**Answer: b) a body that blocks light**

**6. In Experiment 5, a shadow is —**
a) the lightless place made where an opaque body blocks light's path  b) a kind of candle  c) the flame's reflection
**Answer: a) the lightless place made where an opaque body blocks light's path**

**7. Chapter 2 review: a shadow forms where —**
a) an opaque body blocks light's straight path  b) light bends around corners  c) the sun sets
**Answer: a) an opaque body blocks light's straight path** — The shadow item as reviewed.
