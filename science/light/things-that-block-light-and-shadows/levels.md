# Things that block light, and shadows — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: name the three blockers, explain night-dark houses and the shadow from the one rule, and track a shadow's lengthening through a day
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 4 depth (added 2026-11-12): inside the same whole lesson, practice deepens to the experiment — naming the opaque body (ብርሃን ከል), its 5 cm placing before the candle in the book's Experiment 5, and defining the shadow as the lightless place where the straight path is blocked.
