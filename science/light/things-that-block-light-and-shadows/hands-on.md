# Hands-on — Things that block light, and shadows

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shadow diary
Mark your shadow's end with a stone at three times of day. Measure with footsteps. The sun moved; you didn't — write that conclusion.

## Blocker hunt
Find five blockers at school and five at home. For each, name what is in its shadow at midday.

## Torch shadow play
In a dark room, make shadow animals with your hands in a torch beam. Every animal is blocked light shaped by your hands — say so to your audience.
