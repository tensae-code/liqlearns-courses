# Lesson plan — Light — sources, and the straight-line rule

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Light — sources, and the straight-line rule
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** bodies split into luminous (making their own light) and non-luminous (seen only by reflected light — the Moon among them), and the three-card experiment proves light travels in straight lines and does not go round corners
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Light — how seeing works: seeing needs light arriving at the eye; now where that light comes from, and the straight path it takes.

## If the learner is stuck
- Places the Moon with the luminous bodies because it shines → shining is not making; the Moon only reflects sunlight.
- Thinks the flame vanished because the card ‘blocked the view’ in a vague way → the precise claim: the straight ray through the three holes is broken; light will not bend round the displaced card.
