# Light — sources, and the straight-line rule

## Do it first

Light a candle. Push a hole through three cards, thread string through the holes and pull it tight so the cards stand in a row. Look through the last hole: there is the flame. Now nudge the middle card just a little sideways. The flame is gone — though candle, cards and your eye have barely moved. Hold that puzzle; this lesson solves it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sources of light — luminous and non-luminous

**Luminous bodies make their own light**: the Sun, fire, lamps and candles. **Non-luminous bodies make none** — a wall, a person, a tree, a book — and are seen only by light reflected from them. Sort the pictured objects into the two classes and defend every placing. The hard one is the **Moon**: it shines in the night sky, but it makes no light of its own — it is non-luminous, seen by reflected sunlight, which is why its shape changes as the Sun lights different parts of it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Light travels straight — the three-card proof

With the three holed cards aligned by the taut string, the flame is seen through all three holes. Displace one card and the flame vanishes, because **light travels in straight lines and does not go round corners**: the ray from the flame can only reach your eye along the straight line through all three holes. You see the same rule outdoors — sunbeams stream straight through gaps in tree branches and through windows, never bending around them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two classes of bodies, where the Moon belongs and why, and then walk them through the three-card proof step by step.

## Do It Yourself

1. Sort and defend: Sun, candle, Moon, mirror, tree, lamp, book, firefly lamp picture on a wall — luminous or non-luminous? Write one line of defence for the Moon and the mirror.
2. Repeat the three-card test with a torch instead of a candle. Does the rule change? Record what you did and saw.
3. Find one straight sunbeam today (window, branches, door crack). Sketch it and mark its straight edges.
