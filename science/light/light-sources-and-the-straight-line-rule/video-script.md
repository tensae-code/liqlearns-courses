# Video script — Light — sources, and the straight-line rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A full build-along / walkthrough script, as the placement names a video need for this lesson.

---

We prove light walks straight. Watch my hands.
Three cards. One hole in each card.
I push string through the holes. I pull the string tight. Now the holes are in one straight line.
The candle is here. My eye is here. I look through the holes — I see the flame!
Now I push the middle card a little to the side. Look again. No flame!
The light did not bend. Light travels in straight lines. It does not go round corners.
Your turn. Three cards, one string, one candle. Make the flame come and go.
