# Hands-on — Light — sources, and the straight-line rule

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The three-card experiment (Activity 7.3)
Exactly as the Do-it-first: holed cards, taut string, candle or torch. Each learner must make the flame appear and disappear twice, and write the rule in their own words before reading the lesson's wording.

## Beam hunting
On a sunny morning, find sunbeams through windows or branches. Hold a card with a small hole in a beam and watch the bright spot stay in line as you move the card along the beam — the beam does not follow a bent path.
