# Light — sources, and the straight-line rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A luminous body is one that —
a) reflects light well  b) makes its own light  c) is white
**Answer: b) makes its own light** — the Sun, fire, lamps and candles make their own light.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Moon is non-luminous because —
a) it is too far away  b) it makes no light; we see it by reflected sunlight  c) it only shines at night
**Answer: b) it makes no light; we see it by reflected sunlight**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A wall, a tree and a book are seen by —
a) their own light  b) reflected light  c) light stored in the daytime
**Answer: b) reflected light**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the three-card experiment the flame is seen when —
a) the cards are anywhere in a row  b) the three holes lie on one straight line  c) the string is loose
**Answer: b) the three holes lie on one straight line**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When one card is displaced, the flame vanishes because light —
a) gets tired  b) does not go round corners; it travels straight  c) changes colour
**Answer: b) does not go round corners; it travels straight**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Sunbeams through tree branches show the same rule because they —
a) curl around the leaves  b) stream in straight lines  c) fall only downwards
**Answer: b) stream in straight lines**
