# Light — sources, and the straight-line rule — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bodies that make their own light are called ______.
**Answer: luminous**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Moon is seen by ______ sunlight.
**Answer: reflected**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Light travels in ______ lines.
**Answer: straight**
