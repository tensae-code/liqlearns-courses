# Hands-on — Vision defects and correcting them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Which blur?
Hold a book at arm's length, then bring it close: which distance is easy for your eyes? Ask family members who wear glasses what blurs for them without glasses — distance or near — and match each answer to a defect and its lens from the lesson. Never test with anyone else's glasses on your own eyes for long; say why the lesson's rule (test, name, choose) matters.
