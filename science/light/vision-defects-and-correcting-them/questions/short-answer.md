# Vision defects and correcting them — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is the difference between short sight and long sight?
**Answer: In short sight the image of distant things falls in front of the retina, so far things blur; in long sight it would fall behind the retina, so near things blur.**

**2.** Which lens corrects each defect, and why?
**Answer: Concave for short sight — it spreads the rays so the image moves back; convex for long sight — it gathers rays so the image moves forward onto the retina.**

**3.** Where must the image fall for clear sight?
**Answer: Exactly on the retina.**

**4.** Why must glasses be chosen by a test?
**Answer: Because the right lens depends on the eye's own defect and strength; another person's lens puts the image in the wrong place.**
