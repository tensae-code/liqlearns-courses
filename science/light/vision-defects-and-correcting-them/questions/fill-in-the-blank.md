# Vision defects and correcting them — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Distant things blur in _____ sight.
**Answer: short**

**2.** Near things blur first in _____ sight.
**Answer: long**

**3.** Short sight is corrected by a _____ lens.
**Answer: concave**

**4.** Long sight is corrected by a _____ lens.
**Answer: convex**
