# Vision defects and correcting them — multiple choice


**1.** In short sight, distant things…
a) Are clearest  b) Blur, because the image falls in front of the retina  c) Turn red  d) Disappear
**Answer: b) Blur, because the image falls in front of the retina**

**2.** In long sight, the image would fall…
a) In front of the retina  b) Behind the retina  c) On the lens  d) Outside the eye
**Answer: b) Behind the retina**

**3.** Short sight is corrected by a…
a) Convex lens  b) Concave lens  c) Mirror  d) Prism
**Answer: b) Concave lens**

**4.** Long sight is corrected by a…
a) Concave lens  b) Convex lens  c) Coloured glass  d) Magnet
**Answer: b) Convex lens**

**5.** Clear seeing needs the image to fall exactly on the…
a) Lens  b) Retina  c) Iris  d) Cornea
**Answer: b) Retina**

**6.** Glasses are…
a) Decorations  b) Correcting lenses chosen for the eye's defect  c) Eye medicine  d) Tiny telescopes
**Answer: b) Correcting lenses chosen for the eye's defect**
