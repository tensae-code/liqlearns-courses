# Light and dark: what light is

## Do it first
Same room. Same chair, same table, same book. In one picture you can read; in the other you cannot even find the door. The only difference is light. This lesson teaches the pair: light, and its absence — darkness.

## Where there is no light, there is darkness

The Grade 1 guide's light subtopic is aimed at one competency: explaining what **darkness (ጨለማ)** is. Light and dark are taught as a **pair**: **where there is no light, there is darkness**. A room is not filled with darkness like water in a pot; the light leaves, and darkness is what remains. Put the lamp on, and the room is lit: chairs, books and faces appear. Put it off, and they are gone from sight — not gone from the room, gone from seeing. Everything the next light lessons do — what light passes through, what blocks it, where it comes from — stands on this pair.

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion the pair in one sentence, then prove it with the lamp: dark room, lamp on, lamp off. Your companion says at each step what they can suddenly see, or no longer see.

## Do It Yourself
1. With an adult's leave, darken a room at home for one minute. List three things you know are there but cannot see. Turn the light on: there they are.
2. Draw the same corner of your room twice: lit and dark. Label what disappears in the dark.
3. At night, name what gives your home light after the sun is gone — the next lessons sort those sources.
