# Hands-on — Light and dark: what light is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## On and off
With a torch in a dark place, practise the guide's demonstration: on — name five things; off — name what is left to your other senses (touch, hearing). Light is for the eyes.

## Shadow of the pair
Stand where half a room is lit and half is dark. Step across the line both ways, saying the pair aloud each time.

## Night list
Before sleep, list what your home uses against the dark. Keep the list — the sources lessons will sort it.
