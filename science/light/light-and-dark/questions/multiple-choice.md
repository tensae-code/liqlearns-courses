# Light and dark: what light is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Darkness is —**
a) a black thing that flows in  b) where there is no light  c) a kind of dust
**Answer: b) where there is no light** — the guide teaches the pair: no light, darkness.

**2. In a dark room, we cannot —**
a) breathe  b) see things  c) hear sounds
**Answer: b) see things**

**3. Which of these gives light to a room?**
a) a wall  b) a lamp  c) a book
**Answer: b) a lamp**
