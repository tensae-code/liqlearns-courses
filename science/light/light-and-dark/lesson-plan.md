**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Light and dark: what light is
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — the fifth title on the Science ladder (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** light and dark are a pair: where there is no light, there is darkness — ጨለማ
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own room at night and in the morning. This lesson rests on teacher-guide evidence only; its sources say so plainly.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what darkness is: where there is no light, there is darkness.
2. Compare the same room lit and dark, and name what can and cannot be done in each.
3. Name the switch in the guide's own demonstration: a lamp on in a dark room, then off.

## What you need
A lamp or torch and a room that can be darkened, or the picture of the room lit and dark.
