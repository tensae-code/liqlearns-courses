# Hands-on — Waves and how they carry energy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rings on still water (a basin or a quiet pond)
Fill a wide basin with water and let it settle. Drop one small stone in the middle and watch: rings spread out, the water bobs, and a floating leaf far from the splash rocks when a ring reaches it. Say out loud what travelled and what stayed.

## Draw the travelling
Draw the basin from above: the splash point, three rings, and the leaf. Under the drawing write two lists — 'what moved' and 'what stayed' — and check them against the lesson.
