# Using artificial light well — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about using artificial light well?
**Answer: 29: light for comfortable living, night life and road safety; reflectors on children's clothes and bags; the theatre lighting operator; desk lamp placed so light falls from the left (from the right for left-handers)**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about where light is needed?
**Answer: The books teach this part as festive illuminations and sky lanterns; where light must be provided — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
