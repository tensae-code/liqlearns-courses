# Hands-on — Using artificial light well

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Using artificial light well. 29: light for comfortable living, night life and road safety; reflectors on children's clothes and bags; the theatre lighting operator; desk lamp placed so light falls from the left (from the right for left-handers) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
