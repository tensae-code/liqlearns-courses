# Hands-on — Low- and high-frequency radiation around us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Slow shake, fast shake (a rope or a skipping rope)
Lay a rope on the ground and shake one end slowly: long, lazy waves travel down it — low frequency. Shake the same end fast: short, quick waves — high frequency. Count how many waves pass a mark in ten seconds each time, and say which family each shake belongs to and why.

## Sort the day's radiation
List every place radiation met you today — hearing a signal, feeling warmth, standing in sunlight. For each one, say which end of the book's range it belongs nearer to, low or high, and what decided you: the frequency, nothing else.
