# Low- and high-frequency radiation around us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Radiation sorted by frequency.
**Answer:** **Frequency** is how often a wave repeats in a second.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Low-frequency radiation — the long, gentle waves.
**Answer:** Low frequency means the wave repeats slowly, and its waves are long.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: High-frequency radiation — the short, energetic waves.
**Answer:** High frequency means the wave repeats very fast, and its waves are short — and a high-frequency wave carries more energy than a low-frequency one.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Radiation sorted by frequency → Low-frequency radiation — the long, gentle waves → High-frequency radiation — the short, energetic waves — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
