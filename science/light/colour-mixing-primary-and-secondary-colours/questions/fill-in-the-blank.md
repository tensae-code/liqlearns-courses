# Colour mixing — primary and secondary colours — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Red, yellow and blue are the _____ colours.
**Answer: primary**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Yellow + blue makes the secondary colour _____.
**Answer: green**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Adding _____ lightens a colour.
**Answer: white**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A mixed colour cannot be _____.
**Answer: unmixed**
