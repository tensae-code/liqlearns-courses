# Colour mixing — primary and secondary colours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The primary colours for mixing are…
a) Orange, green and purple  b) Red, yellow and blue  c) White, black and grey  d) Pink, brown and gold
**Answer: b) Red, yellow and blue**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Mixing red and yellow gives…
a) Green  b) Purple  c) Orange  d) Blue
**Answer: c) Orange**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A secondary colour is…
a) A colour you start with  b) A colour made by mixing two primary colours  c) The lightest colour  d) A colour that cannot be mixed
**Answer: b) A colour made by mixing two primary colours**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) To invent green, you mix…
a) Red and blue  b) Red and yellow  c) Yellow and blue  d) White and black
**Answer: c) Yellow and blue**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Adding white to a colour makes it…
a) Darker  b) Lighter  c) A primary colour  d) Impossible to see
**Answer: b) Lighter**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A mixed colour…
a) Can always be unmixed  b) Unmixes itself overnight  c) Cannot be unmixed  d) Turns back when it dries
**Answer: c) Cannot be unmixed**
