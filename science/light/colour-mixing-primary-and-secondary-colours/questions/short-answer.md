# Colour mixing — primary and secondary colours — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three primary colours and say why they are called primary.
**Answer:** Red, yellow and blue — they are the starting colours, the ones you begin with before any mixing.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three secondary colours and the two primaries that make each one.
**Answer:** Orange (red + yellow), green (yellow + blue) and purple (blue + red).

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How do you make a colour lighter, and how do you make it darker?
**Answer:** Mix in white to make it lighter; mix in black to make it darker.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why should the colour inventor mix a little at a time?
**Answer:** Because a mixed colour cannot be unmixed — mixing is a one-way road, so a mistake cannot be taken back out.
