# Video script — Colour mixing — primary and secondary colours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Colour mixing — primary and secondary colours** — Cabo Verde's way: the colour inventor, inventing colours with plasticine.

You start with three colours: red, yellow and blue. These are the primary colours — the starting colours. Now invent. Knead red and yellow together: orange. Yellow and blue: green. Blue and red: purple. You made three new colours, and no shop sold them to you.
Want a colour lighter? Mix in white. Darker? Mix in black. But remember the inventor's one rule: once colours are mixed, they cannot be unmixed. So mix a little at a time.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
