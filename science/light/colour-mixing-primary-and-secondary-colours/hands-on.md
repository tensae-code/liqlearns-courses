# Hands-on — Colour mixing — primary and secondary colours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Be the colour inventor
Take plasticine (or paint) in red, yellow and blue. Mix the three pairs, a small ball of each: red + yellow, yellow + blue, blue + red. Lay the three new colours in a row and write under each one the two primaries that made it.

## Lighter and darker
Take one secondary colour you made. Split it in two. Knead white into one half and black into the other. Put the three side by side — dark, middle, light — and say which colour you added to each end.

## The one-way test
Now try to undo one mix: take an orange ball and try, for one full minute, to get the red back out of it. Write what happened, in one sentence, and the inventor's rule it proves.
