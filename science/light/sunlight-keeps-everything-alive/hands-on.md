# Hands-on — Sunlight keeps everything alive, and what light is used for

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tin record book
Keep Experiment 2's record like a scientist: date, height, colour, for both tins, twice a week, until the difference is undeniable.

## Sun uses at home
List every job sunlight does at your home in one day — seeing, warming, drying, growing. Miss none; the guide's list is your checklist.

## Evaporation watch
Put a wet cloth in sun and one in shade. Which dries first, and where did its water go? Answer with the guide's sky-work: drawn up by the sun.
