# Sunlight keeps everything alive, and what light is used for — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Without sunlight, the book says —**
a) only plants suffer  b) nothing lives — plants fail, then the animals and people who eat them  c) only nights are dark
**Answer: b) nothing lives — plants fail, then the animals and people who eat them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In Experiment 2, the two tins must get the same —**
a) light  b) water and soil — only the light differs  c) darkness
**Answer: b) water and soil — only the light differs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a use of sunlight in the guide's list?**
a) making darkness  b) generating electricity  c) stopping plants growing
**Answer: b) generating electricity**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) The dark-kept seedling grows —**
a) taller and greener  b) pale and weak  c) not at all after one day
**Answer: b) pale and weak**
