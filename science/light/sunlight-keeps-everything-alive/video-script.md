# Video script — Sunlight keeps everything alive, and what light is used for

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Sunlight makes the plant's food. Animals eat plants. People eat both.
Two tins prove it: light grows; darkness starves.
Light for seeing, sun for drying, sun for power.
No sunlight — nothing lives. That is why light matters.
