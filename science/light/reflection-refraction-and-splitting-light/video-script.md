# Video script — Reflection, refraction and splitting light

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Light **bounces**: angle in equals angle out — that is **reflection**.
Light **bends** at a boundary — that is **refraction**. The coin appears. The pencil 'breaks'.
White light is **seven colours** mixed — **dispersion** pulls them apart.
Spin the colours fast — they merge back to **white**.
