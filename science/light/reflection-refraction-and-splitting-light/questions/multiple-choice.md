# Reflection, refraction and splitting light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The angle of incidence is measured from —**
a) the mirror's surface  b) the normal line  c) the reflected ray
**Answer: b) the normal line**

**2. Reflection's second law: angle of incidence —**
a) is double the angle of reflection  b) equals the angle of reflection  c) is unrelated
**Answer: b) equals the angle of reflection**

**3. Refraction happens because crossing a boundary changes light's —**
a) colour  b) speed, and so its direction  c) source
**Answer: b) speed, and so its direction**

**4. The coin in the cup appears when —**
a) the cup is shaken  b) water is poured in  c) the coin is polished
**Answer: b) water is poured in**

**5. White light is a mixture of —**
a) three colours  b) seven colours  c) no colours
**Answer: b) seven colours**

**6. The Newton's disc spinning fast looks —**
a) black  b) rainbow-striped  c) whitish
**Answer: c) whitish**
