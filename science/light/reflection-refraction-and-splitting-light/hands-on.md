# Hands-on — Reflection, refraction and splitting light

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experiment 7, the mirror test
With a mirror, a ray of sunlight or torchlight and a protractor, test the second law: measure angle in and angle out from the normal, three times. The law should hold every time.

## Rainbow hunt
On a sunny day with water spray (or after rain), find or make a rainbow and name it for what it is: sunlight dispersed — the prism's lesson at sky scale.
