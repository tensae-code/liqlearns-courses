# Hands-on — Sources of light: lamps people make and light from nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The family table
Build the book's table on paper, two columns, and fill it with every source you can name — at least ten. Defend the two hardest placements.

## Old-days interview
Ask the oldest person you know: 'When you were my age, what gave light at night?' Write or draw their answer and bring it to the lesson.

## Source walk
At dusk, stand outside and name each light as it comes on, family by family, until the village is fully lit.
