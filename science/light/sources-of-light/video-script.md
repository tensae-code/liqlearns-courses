# Video script — Sources of light: lamps people make and light from nature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Sun, moon, stars — **nature's** light.
Kuraz, bulb, candle, torch, fanos — **people's** light.
Every light has a source. Every source has a family.
Sort them, and you can read any night.
