# Sources of light: lamps people make and light from nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a human-made light source?**
a) the sun  b) a kuraz lamp  c) the moon
**Answer: b) a kuraz lamp**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The book sends pupils to ask parents about —**
a) electric bills  b) how people got light and fire in the old days  c) buying candles
**Answer: b) how people got light and fire in the old days**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which list is all natural sources?**
a) sun, moon, stars  b) sun, candle, bulb  c) moon, torch, fanos
**Answer: a) sun, moon, stars**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which family does a torch belong to?**
a) natural  b) human-made
**Answer: b) human-made**
