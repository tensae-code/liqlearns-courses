# Hands-on — How bright light is — lux and distance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reading-distance race (a candle substitute, a lamp, an LED torch)
Using a lamp and an LED torch (an adult handles any real candle), find how far from each source you can still read the same line of print. Record the two distances and say which source is brighter by the book's own test — and why distance is the honest comparison.

## Near and far (no materials)
Stand close to a lit lamp and read a line; then read the same line from across the room. Say, in the lesson's words, what changed and what stayed the same — the source did not change; your distance did, and brightness falls with distance.
