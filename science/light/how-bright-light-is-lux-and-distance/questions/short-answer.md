# How bright light is — lux and distance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Brightness has a measure — the lux.
**Answer:** Light can be brighter or dimmer, and North Macedonia's Grade 5 book gives that brightness a measure: the **lux**, read with a **luxmeter**.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Further from the source, dimmer.
**Answer:** The book's second step: **brightness falls as you move away from the source**.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Candle, lamp and LED — and where brightness matters.
**Answer:** The book compares sources — a **candle**, a **lamp** and an **LED** — by **reading distance**: how far away you can still read by each one's light.
