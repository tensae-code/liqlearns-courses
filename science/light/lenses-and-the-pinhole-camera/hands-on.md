# Hands-on — Lenses — and the pinhole camera

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing with your hands
Make a pinhole camera with an adult: a box, a pin hole in one end, and tracing paper across the other. Point it at a bright window (never the Sun) and describe the picture on the paper. Then hold a magnifying lens in front of the hole and say what the lens changed.
