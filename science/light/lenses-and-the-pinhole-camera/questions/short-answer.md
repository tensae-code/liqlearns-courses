# Lenses — and the pinhole camera — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The convex lens — its properties and function.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 7 guide teaches the **convex lens** first by its properties and function: a lens thick in the middle, which gathers light passing through it and can form a picture of what stands before it. The lens's job is done by its shape.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The pinhole camera — a picture with no lens.
**Answer:** Beside the lens, the guide sets the **pinhole camera**: a box with one tiny hole, which also forms a picture — with no lens at all. The two are taught together so the learner can compare them: the lens gathers light to make its picture; the pinhole lets through only a thin thread of light from each point.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Applications of convex lenses.
**Answer:** The guide closes with **applications of convex lenses** — the places lenses are put to work, in instruments that magnify and in cameras. The run is: what the lens is, the camera with no lens to compare it against, then where lenses serve.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The convex lens — its properties and function → The pinhole camera — a picture with no lens → Applications of convex lenses — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
