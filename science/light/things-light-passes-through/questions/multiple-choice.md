# Things light passes through — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which of these lets light through?**
a) a wooden door  b) glass  c) a thick book
**Answer: b) glass**

**2. In the book's test, what do we shine through the glass window?**
a) water  b) a torch  c) a candle's smoke
**Answer: b) a torch**

**3. A material light passes through is called —**
a) opaque  b) transparent  c) brittle
**Answer: b) transparent**

**4. Which of these blocks light in the test?**
a) water  b) air  c) a book
**Answer: c) a book**
