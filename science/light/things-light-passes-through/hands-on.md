# Hands-on — Things light passes through

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The home survey
Go window to window at home: what is each made of, and does light pass? Windows exist to pass light while blocking wind and rain — say that sentence about your own home.

## Water test
Fill a glass with water and shine a torch through it in a dark corner. The light arrives, bent and wobbly — but through. Water passes light.

## Air test
The torch beam crosses the room's air to reach the wall. Air is the passer we forget: name it every time the beam lands.
