# Lesson plan — Things light passes through

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Things light passes through
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — after Light and dark (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** some materials let light through — glass, water, air — and we test them the book's way, with a torch
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Light and dark — the pair is known; this lesson sorts materials by what they do to light.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Light — Light and dark: what light is. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the light-passing materials: glass, water, air.
2. Do the book's test: shine a torch through an unpainted glass window with door and windows shut, and watch the light arrive.
3. Sort materials into light-passing and light-blocking, with the guide's list (transparent against opaque).

## What you need
A torch; a glass (unpainted), a book, and a darkened corner to test in.

## If the learner is stuck
- Tests in a bright room and sees nothing → the book's test shuts door and windows first; light's travel shows against the dark.
- Calls every clear thing 'glass' → the family is behaviour, not material: water and air pass light too.
