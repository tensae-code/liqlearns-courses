# Hands-on — Materials and light — transparent, translucent, opaque

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The tray investigation (Activity 7.4)
Run the full semi-guided investigation exactly as the lesson sets it: predictions written first, the group's own fair test, results recorded, tray sorted from results. The teacher does not name the three classes until the sorting is on the table.

## Window audit
Walk the school or home and list every window, door panel and lamp cover. Sort each by class and write the job its class does there (seeing through, privacy with light, blocking light).
