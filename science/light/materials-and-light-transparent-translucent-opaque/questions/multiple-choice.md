# Materials and light — transparent, translucent, opaque — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a semi-guided investigation, the pupils themselves devise —
a) the question  b) the method of testing  c) the answer in advance
**Answer: b) the method of testing** — the question is given; the method is the pupils' own.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Predictions are written before testing so that —
a) the lesson is longer  b) disagreements between prediction and result can be seen and learned from  c) nobody has to test
**Answer: b) disagreements between prediction and result can be seen and learned from**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A fair test of the tray materials keeps the same —
a) torch and distance for every item  b) prediction for every item  c) winner for every item
**Answer: a) torch and distance for every item**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An opaque material lets —
a) all light through  b) some light through, blurred  c) no light through
**Answer: c) no light through** — a wall and wood are opaque.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Bathroom glass is translucent because it —
a) blocks all light  b) lets some light through, blurred  c) lets light through clearly
**Answer: b) lets some light through, blurred**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A transparent material can serve as a ______ for light.
a) wall  b) medium  c) shadow
**Answer: b) medium** — air and window glass carry light through clearly.
