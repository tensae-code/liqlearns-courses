# Materials and light — transparent, translucent, opaque — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Wood and stone are ______ materials: no light passes.
**Answer: opaque**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Frosted glass is ______: some light passes, blurred.
**Answer: translucent**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Air and window glass are ______ materials.
**Answer: transparent**
