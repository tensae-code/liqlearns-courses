# Light travels in straight lines — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: state the straight-line rule and prove it with the straight and bent tubes of Experiment 4.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 6 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also runs the three-card test, checks alignment with the wire, and names light's three meetings with a body (bounce back, absorbed, pass through).
