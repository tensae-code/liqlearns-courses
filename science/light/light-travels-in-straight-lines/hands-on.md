# Hands-on — Light travels in straight lines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Straight and bent
The book's Experiment 4 with a torch and two tubes at home. See it, lose it, say why — the rule from your own eyes.

## Shadow line-up
Torch, a book, a wall: line them up and the shadow lands exactly where the straight line says it must. Move the book — the shadow obeys the line.
