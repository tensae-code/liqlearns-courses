# Video script — Light travels in straight lines

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Light travels in straight lines.** Only straight. Never around a bend.
Through the straight tube: you see the candle. Through the bent tube: nothing.
Light bounces off things, straight into your eyes — that is how you see.
