# Lesson plan — Light travels in straight lines

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Light travels in straight lines
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — after the things light passes through and the things that block it: the path light itself takes (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** light travels only in straight lines — proved by the book's Experiment 4: a candle's flame is seen through a straight tube but not through a bent one
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Things light passes through and Things that block light, and shadows — this is the rule underneath both.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the rule: light travels only in straight lines.
2. Tell Experiment 4: the candle is seen through the straight tube, not through the bent tube.
3. Say light is a form of energy, and we see things when light bounces off them into our eyes.

## What you need
A candle (adult's care, or a torch in its place), a straight plastic tube and a bent one — the book's own apparatus.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks light can curve a little → the bent tube is the correction: not even a little. If it could bend, the flame would show.
- Thinks eyes send out seeing rays → light travels from the thing to the eye, bouncing off the thing; the eye receives.
