# Light travels in straight lines — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. Light will turn a corner inside a bent tube to reach your eye.**
**Answer: False**

**2. A bent tube hides the candle flame from your eye.**
**Answer: True**
**3. Shifting the middle card hides the flame.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Light travels in curves through air.**
**Answer: False**
