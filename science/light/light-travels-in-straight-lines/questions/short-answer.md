# Light travels in straight lines — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Why can the flame not be seen through the bent tube?**
**Answer: there is no straight line from the flame through the bend to the eye, and light travels only in straight lines**
**2. What does the vanishing flame in Experiment 6 prove?**
**Answer: That light travels in a straight line — when the line is broken, the light cannot reach the eye.**
