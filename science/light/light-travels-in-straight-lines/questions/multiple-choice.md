# Light travels in straight lines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Light travels —**
a) in circles  b) only in straight lines  c) any way it likes
**Answer: b) only in straight lines**

**2. In Experiment 4, the candle flame is seen through —**
a) the bent tube  b) the straight tube  c) neither tube
**Answer: b) the straight tube**

**3. Light is a form of —**
a) energy  b) water  c) stone
**Answer: a) energy**

**4. We see things when light —**
a) sleeps on them  b) bounces off them into our eyes  c) hides from them
**Answer: b) bounces off them into our eyes**
