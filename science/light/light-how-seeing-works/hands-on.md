# Hands-on — Light — how seeing works

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Judge it like the book
In pairs, deal the five evidence statements as cards. For each card the pair must say: supports Story 1, supports Story 2, or FALSE itself — and give the reason aloud. Disagreements are settled by testing, not by voting: the darkness test settles the night card.

## Lamp energy chain
Hold (do not touch) your hand near — never on — a lit lamp. Feel the warmth. Write the chain: electrical energy → heat and light. Name one other place where one energy form becomes light.
