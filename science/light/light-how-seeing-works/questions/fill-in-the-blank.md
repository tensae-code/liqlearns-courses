# Light — how seeing works — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the winning theory, light ______ off the object and enters the eyes.
**Answer: reflects (bounces)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The eye catches light and the ______ reads it as seeing.
**Answer: brain**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** With no ______, there is no sight.
**Answer: light**
