# Light — how seeing works — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Looking harder can make us see in total darkness.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Plants use light to make the food that animals and people eat.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Light is given off by hot bodies.
**Answer: True**
