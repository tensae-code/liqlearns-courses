# Light — how seeing works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The theory of seeing the evidence supports is —
a) rays leave the eyes and fetch the object  b) light reflected from the object enters the eyes  c) the object sends a copy of itself through the air
**Answer: b) light reflected from the object enters the eyes** — reflected light entering the eyes explains sunglasses, darkness and the straight-line rule.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which item on the evidence list is itself FALSE?
a) light travels in straight lines  b) sunglasses protect the eye  c) we can see in total darkness with no light at all
**Answer: c) we can see in total darkness with no light at all** — with no light at all, there is no sight — however hard we look.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sunglasses are evidence for the winning theory because they guard the eye against —
a) rays leaving the eye  b) light coming into the eye  c) the brain working too hard
**Answer: b) light coming into the eye** — protection only makes sense against something arriving.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Seeing happens through —
a) the eyes alone  b) the eyes and the brain together  c) the object alone
**Answer: b) the eyes and the brain together** — the eye catches light; the brain reads it as sight.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Light is —
a) a liquid poured out by the Sun  b) an electromagnetic wave given off by hot bodies  c) a kind of air
**Answer: b) an electromagnetic wave given off by hot bodies**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In a lamp, electrical energy becomes —
a) only sound  b) heat and light  c) only cold
**Answer: b) heat and light**
