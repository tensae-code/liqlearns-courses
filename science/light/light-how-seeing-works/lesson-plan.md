# Lesson plan — Light — how seeing works

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Light — how seeing works
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** two rival theories of seeing are judged on evidence — rays leaving the eyes to fetch the object, against light reflected from the object entering the eyes — and the second wins: light produces the sensation of sight through eyes and brain, feeds the plants we eat, and is an electromagnetic wave given off by hot bodies
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Sources of light and Light travels in straight lines at primary depth: the learner can name light sources; now the act of seeing itself is argued and settled.

## If the learner is stuck
- Chosen the eye-ray theory because ‘looking hard’ feels active → effort is in the brain and eye muscles, not in rays leaving; darkness defeats the hardest look.
- Accepted ‘we can see at night’ without checking → some listed evidence is false on purpose; judging evidence includes judging the evidence itself.
