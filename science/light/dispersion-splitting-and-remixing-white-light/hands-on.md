# Hands-on — Dispersion — splitting and remixing white light

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Newton's disc build
Card disc, seven equal painted segments in VIBGYOR order, mounted on a pencil or enzirt. Spin at three speeds and record the disc's appearance at each. A disc that will not whiten is debugged segment by segment, not discarded.

## Prism or CD fan
With a prism in sunlight (or a CD tilted in a sunbeam), catch the colour fan on white paper or a wall and mark violet and red at its two edges.
