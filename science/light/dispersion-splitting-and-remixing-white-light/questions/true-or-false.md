# Dispersion — splitting and remixing white light — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The prism adds colours that were not in the sunlight.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Red bends least of the seven colours in a prism.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Spinning Newton's disc fast mixes the colours back towards white.
**Answer: True**
