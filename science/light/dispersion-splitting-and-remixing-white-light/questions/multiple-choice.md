# Dispersion — splitting and remixing white light — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** White light split by a prism gives —
a) three colours  b) seven colours  c) one colour at a time
**Answer: b) seven colours** — VIBGYOR.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The colours fan out because —
a) the prism is painted  b) each colour refracts by a different amount  c) the light speeds up
**Answer: b) each colour refracts by a different amount**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The colour that bends most in the prism is —
a) red  b) green  c) violet
**Answer: c) violet** — violet most, red least.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Newton's disc has —
a) three segments  b) seven segments, one per colour  c) one white segment
**Answer: b) seven segments, one per colour**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Spun fast, Newton's disc looks —
a) black  b) striped forever  c) whitish
**Answer: c) whitish** — the remix proves the mixture.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A rainbow is —
a) paint in the sky  b) nature's dispersion — sunlight split by raindrops  c) a reflection of flowers
**Answer: b) nature's dispersion — sunlight split by raindrops**
