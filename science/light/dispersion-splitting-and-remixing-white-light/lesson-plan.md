# Lesson plan — Dispersion — splitting and remixing white light

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Light · **Lesson:** Dispersion — splitting and remixing white light
**Place in the curriculum:** Light — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** white light is a mixture of seven colours — violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange, red — split by a prism because each colour refracts by a different amount (violet most), seen in rainbows, soap bubbles and CDs, and remixed to whitish by spinning Newton's seven-segment disc
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Refraction — why the spoon looks broken: one ray bending; now white light bending by seven different amounts at once. It closes the Grade 8 light lessons.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the prism ADDS colours to the light → the disc remix shows the colours were in the white light all along; the prism only separates them.
- Recites the colours out of order or misses indigo → VIBGYOR, in order, violet bending most — the order IS the fan's order.
