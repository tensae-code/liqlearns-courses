# Video script — Dispersion — splitting and remixing white light

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script in the super-simple voice; the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so this is the standard short script.

---

White light goes into the prism. Seven colours come out!
Violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange, red.
Each colour bends a different amount. Violet bends most.
Now spin the disc fast. The colours mix again — the disc looks white.
The colours were inside the white light all along.
