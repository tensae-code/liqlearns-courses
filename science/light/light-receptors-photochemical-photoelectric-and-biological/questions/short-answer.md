# Light receptors — photochemical, photoelectric and biological — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the difference between a source and a receptor.
**Answer: A source gives light out; a receptor takes light in and answers it with a change — chemical, electrical or living.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the photochemical, biological and photoelectric receptors named in Mauritania's Grade 8 manual's family sorting, one of each.
**Answer: Photochemical: photographic film (light drives a chemical change). Biological: the eye/skin/leaf (a living organ answers). Photoelectric: the solar panel (light frees charge and makes current).**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does a solar calculator stop working when you cover its little window?
**Answer: Its photoelectric receptor is covered: no light falls on the cell, no charge is freed, and no current is made to run the calculator.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A leaf and a solar panel both receive sunlight. In what way are their answers different?
**Answer: The leaf is a biological receptor: it answers with a living chemical process (photosynthesis). The panel is a photoelectric receptor: it answers by freeing electrons — an electric current.**
