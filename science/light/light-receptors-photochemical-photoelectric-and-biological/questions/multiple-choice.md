# Light receptors — photochemical, photoelectric and biological — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A light receptor is…
**Answer: a) anything that takes light in and changes because of it  b) anything shiny  c) anything that makes light  d) anything transparent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these completes the chain source → journey → …?
**Answer: a) receptor  b) shadow  c) mirror  d) beam**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Photographic film is a photochemical receptor because light…
**Answer: a) drives a chemical change in it  b) passes through it  c) is reflected by it  d) heats it until it melts**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a biological receptor?
**Answer: a) the retina of the eye  b) a solar panel  c) photographic film  d) a photocell**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A solar panel is a photoelectric receptor because light falling on it…
**Answer: a) frees charge and makes a current  b) is stored as a picture  c) warms the skin  d) starts photosynthesis**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A photocell in an automatic street lamp works because its conduction…
**Answer: a) changes when light falls on it  b) never changes  c) depends on sound  d) depends on temperature only**
