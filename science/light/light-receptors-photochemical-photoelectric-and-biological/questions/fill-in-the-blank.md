# Light receptors — photochemical, photoelectric and biological — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sources give light out; _____ take light in.
**Answer: receptors**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Film darkening under light is a _____ receptor at work.
**Answer: photochemical**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The retina of the eye is a _____ receptor.
**Answer: biological**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A solar panel turns light into _____ .
**Answer: electric current (electricity)**
