# Hands-on — Light receptors — photochemical, photoelectric and biological

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

["(1) Lay a coin on photographic-style sun-print paper (or a leaf half-covered with card) in strong sun for an hour; the uncovered part changes — a photochemical receptor recording light. (2) Cover half a small solar panel (or a solar calculator's cell) and watch the output fall — a photoelectric receptor answering light. (3) Close your eyes facing a bright window, then open them: name the biological receptor and what it answers with."]
