# The pinhole camera — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) A box, a tiny hole and a screen  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The upside-down picture  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The upside-down picture**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the pinhole camera. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Eritrea's *Science for Grade Six (Science 6 Textbook)* (Ministry of Education, State of Eritrea)  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
