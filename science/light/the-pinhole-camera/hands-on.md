# Hands-on — The pinhole camera

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the camera and read its picture
With an adult's help: make a pinhole camera from a box — a tiny hole in one side, a paper screen inside the opposite side — and point it at a bright window or a candle at a safe distance. Look at the picture on the screen: name what is upside down, what is swapped left for right, and what happens to the picture when the object moves closer. No flame is handled by the learner; the adult manages any candle.
