# Waves — amplitude and wavelength — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Waves have a regular pattern — water and rope models.
**Answer:** A wave is a regular pattern that travels: ripples crossing water, a shake running down a rope (Grade 4, 4.3.1, printed p. 110).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Amplitude — how big the wave is.
**Answer:** Amplitude describes how big the wave's swing is — the height of the pattern from its resting line to its crest.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Wavelength — how long the pattern is.
**Answer:** Wavelength describes how long one full repeat of the pattern is — crest to crest.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Waves have a regular pattern — water and rope models → Amplitude — how big the wave is → Wavelength — how long the pattern is — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
