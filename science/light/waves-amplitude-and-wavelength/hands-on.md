# Hands-on — Waves — amplitude and wavelength

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rope wave measures
Make waves in a rope or a skipping rope laid on the ground. Change only the size of your shake, then only its speed, and say which measure — amplitude or wavelength — each change altered.

## Draw and label
Draw a wave pattern and label one amplitude and one wavelength on it, crest to crest.
