# Hands-on — Natural gas — a precious gas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Oil and gas side by side
Draw two columns — oil and natural gas. In each, write the finding steps from the lessons (searching, then drilling or pumping). Say out loud what is the same and what differs.

## Why precious?
Write one sentence on why the book calls natural gas precious, using only what the lesson gives: it is searched for and pumped at effort, and it serves as an energy source. Read your sentence to someone at home.
