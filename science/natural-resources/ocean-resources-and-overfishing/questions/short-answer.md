# Ocean resources and overfishing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Resources of the sea.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Additional Science Form 5 book catalogues the **resources of the sea**: the **seafood of Malaysia — fish, shellfish (molluscs, crustaceans, echinoderms) and edible seaweed** — and the **aquaculture animals: tilapia, prawns, catfish, oysters and mussels**, farmed to add to the catch.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Overfishing — the economics and the issue.
**Answer:** The book then weighs **the economics of ocean produce and its issues: what overfishing would do** — the catch is a living resource, and taking too much breaks the supply the economy stands on.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Resources of the sea → Overfishing — the economics and the issue — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
