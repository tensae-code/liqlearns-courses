## Sub lesson 1 — Resources of the sea

Malaysia's Additional Science Form 5 book catalogues the **resources of the sea**: the **seafood of Malaysia — fish, shellfish (molluscs, crustaceans, echinoderms) and edible seaweed** — and the **aquaculture animals: tilapia, prawns, catfish, oysters and mussels**, farmed to add to the catch.

## Sub lesson 2 — Overfishing — the economics and the issue

The book then weighs **the economics of ocean produce and its issues: what overfishing would do** — the catch is a living resource, and taking too much breaks the supply the economy stands on.
