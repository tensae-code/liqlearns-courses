# Video script — What counts as a natural resource

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **natural resource** is found in **nature** — on the Earth or under it.
Soil, air, water, plants, animals, minerals, sunlight.
Serving you **directly** — or **indirectly**. Both count.
