# Hands-on — What counts as a natural resource

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One-view inventory
From where you stand, without moving: inventory every natural resource in view, in a written list with its service beside it. Ten is the target.

## Resource or made thing?
Lay out five things — a stone, a wooden stool, a leaf, a metal cup, a handful of soil. Sort: resource as found, or made from resources. Defend each sort.
