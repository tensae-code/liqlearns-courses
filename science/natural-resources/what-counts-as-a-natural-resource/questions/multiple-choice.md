# What counts as a natural resource — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A natural resource is —**
a) anything people make  b) everything found in nature, on or under the Earth, that serves people  c) only gold and oil
**Answer: b) everything found in nature, on or under the Earth, that serves people**

**2. Which of these is a natural resource in the book's list?**
a) a plough  b) sunlight  c) a schoolbook
**Answer: b) sunlight**

**3. A resource that serves people indirectly —**
a) does not count  b) counts just the same  c) counts double
**Answer: b) counts just the same**

**4. Gold counts as a resource found —**
a) only on the surface  b) under the Earth  c) in the sky
**Answer: b) under the Earth**
