# Solar technology — dryers, heaters, cookers and lamps — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Photovoltaic cells of silicon joined in series; the solar lamp and street light (panel, battery, LED, controller); building a solar dryer (insulated box, sloped transparent cover, black interior, cold-air inlet low and hot-air outlet high); the solar water heater with evacuated tubes and thermosiphon circulation; and the solar cooker with its concave reflector and blackened pot.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed pp. 147–149.**
