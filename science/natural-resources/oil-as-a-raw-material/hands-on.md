# Hands-on — Oil as a raw material — more than fuel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Oil-made or not?
List six things from your home. Mark each one: 'burns oil as fuel', 'made from oil', or 'nothing to do with oil'. Use the lesson's one sure example — detergents — to check your middle column.

## Two lives in two columns
Draw two columns, 'oil burned' and 'oil built in'. Put fuels in the first and detergents in the second, then add one sentence under the second column saying what a raw material is. Check it against the lesson.
