Today: **Oil as a raw material — more than fuel** — Greece's way, from its Grade 6 book «Φυσικά» ΣΤ΄ (Greece, Grade 6).

Oil has two lives. The first is the fuel life of the earlier chapters. The second is the life of a **raw material**: oil is the starting stuff from which **products beyond fuels** are made. A raw material is not used up by burning — it is built into things, and oil serves as the building-stuff for a whole family of everyday products.
The book's feature names its example: **detergents**. The washing liquids and powders of the home are made from oil — oil not burned but built into a product that is used, not consumed as fuel. One named example carries the chapter's point: when oil runs short, it is not only fuel that runs short — the made things are at stake too.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
