# Hands-on — Plants and wild animals as resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Herbarium page
Make the full collection page: ten leaves, ten names, one use each. It is the prettiest homework in the course and the book's own.

## Guide for a day
Prepare a two-minute guide's talk for the ibex (or your area's rare animal): where it lives, why it is rare, why visitors come, how it is protected.

## Alive-value ledger
Take one wild animal of your area. Ledger its worth alive (visitors, balance of nature) against any worth taken once. Read the totals aloud.


## Rising or falling? — the book's home enquiry
Ask your parents, exactly as the book directs: are the wild animals around here more than before, or fewer — and why? Write the answer in two lines and bring it back. Family memory is data here.
