# Plants and wild animals as resources — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four uses of plants as resources.**
**Answer: food, industry (wood/paper/cloth), medicine, keeping climate steady, feeding soil, shelter (any four)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How can the decline of rare animals be saved?**
**Answer: by protecting their places and their lives (protected areas and protection)**
