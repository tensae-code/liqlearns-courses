# Plants and wild animals as resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Plants as resources keep which great thing steady?**
a) the climate  b) the market prices  c) the school timetable
**Answer: a) the climate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Wild animals earn the country income through —**
a) ploughing  b) tourism — visitors who come to see them  c) milk
**Answer: b) tourism — visitors who come to see them**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A labelled page of pasted local leaves is —**
a) a herbarium page — the book's leaf collection  b) waste paper  c) a food recipe
**Answer: a) a herbarium page — the book's leaf collection**
**4. (Sub lesson 3) In controlled hunting areas, only — are taken, so the young multiply.**
a) young animals  b) aged animals  c) female animals
**Answer: b) aged animals**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Which is one of the three kinds of protected area?**
a) national parks  b) market squares  c) school yards
**Answer: a) national parks**

**6. (Sub lesson 4) Which is a threat to wild animals in the book's list?**
a) illegal hunting for teeth, skin and meat  b) rain in its season  c) rangers at the gate
**Answer: a) illegal hunting for teeth, skin and meat**

**7. (Sub lesson 4) Habitat destruction comes from —**
a) settlement, free grazing, deforestation, town growth and farm expansion  b) storytelling  c) counting animals
**Answer: a) settlement, free grazing, deforestation, town growth and farm expansion**
