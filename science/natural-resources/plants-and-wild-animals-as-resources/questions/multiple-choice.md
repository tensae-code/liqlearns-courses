# Plants and wild animals as resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Plants as resources keep which great thing steady?**
a) the climate  b) the market prices  c) the school timetable
**Answer: a) the climate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Wild animals earn the country income through —**
a) ploughing  b) tourism — visitors who come to see them  c) milk
**Answer: b) tourism — visitors who come to see them**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A labelled page of pasted local leaves is —**
a) a herbarium page — the book's leaf collection  b) waste paper  c) a food recipe
**Answer: a) a herbarium page — the book's leaf collection**
