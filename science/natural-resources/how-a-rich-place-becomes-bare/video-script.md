# Video script — How a rich place becomes bare

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

1970: birdsong, monkeys, green.
Forty years on: gullies, silence, bare.
Loss is the wealth shrinking — driven by hunger for land, wood and grazing.
Remember the rich place. Refuse the bare one.
