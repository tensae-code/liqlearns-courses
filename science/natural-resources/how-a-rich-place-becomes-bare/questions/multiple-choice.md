# How a rich place becomes bare — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In the memoir, the place forty years later is —**
a) richer than before  b) a barren, gullied wasteland  c) a new town
**Answer: b) a barren, gullied wasteland**

**2. Resource loss means —**
a) resources moving house  b) the natural content shrinking or vanishing  c) resources getting cheaper
**Answer: b) the natural content shrinking or vanishing**

**3. The memoir's author first knew the place in —**
a) 1970 E.C.  b) last year  c) 2000 E.C.
**Answer: a) 1970 E.C.**

**4. Resource loss is the natural content —**
a) growing  b) shrinking or vanishing  c) moving to towns
**Answer: b) shrinking or vanishing**
