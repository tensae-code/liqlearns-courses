# Hands-on — How a rich place becomes bare

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Memory walk
Walk one place with an elder — physically or in talk — and collect its two pictures. Young scientists record elders before the record is lost.

## Gully study
Find one gully. Measure its story as far as you can: what runs in it in rain, what it has eaten (soil, path, field edge). Gullies are loss with an address.

## Forty-years letter
Write a letter from yourself in forty years, returning to your favourite green place. What do you beg the present to do? That begging is next lesson's content.
