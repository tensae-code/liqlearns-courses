Today: **Storing energy — fuels, batteries and food** — Seychelles' way, from its Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 11).

Seychelles' Unit 11 Topic 4, "Storing energy", looks at where Seychelles keeps chemical energy ready for use: petrol, gas, kerosene and charcoal are stored fuels — energy held in a tank, a bottle or a sack until burning lets it out as heat, light and movement. A battery stores energy in chemical form too, and releases it as electrical energy when the circuit is made.

Food is on the same list: the energy in food is stored chemical energy, released by the body that eats it. Storing is what makes energy usable at the time it is needed — fuel in the store, charge in the battery, food in the house — and every store runs out unless it is filled again.
