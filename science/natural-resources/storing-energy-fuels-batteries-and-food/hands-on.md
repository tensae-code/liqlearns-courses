# Hands-on — Storing energy — fuels, batteries and food

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the energy stores
List everything in your home that stores energy (fuels, batteries, foods, candles, gas bottles). Sort the list into: stores that burn, stores that give electricity, and stores that are eaten. Say for each one what form the stored energy is, and what it is released as.
