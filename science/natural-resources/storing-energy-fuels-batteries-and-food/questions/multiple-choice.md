# Storing energy — fuels, batteries and food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Petrol, gas, kerosene and charcoal store…
a) chemical energy  b) sound energy only  c) moonlight  d) magnetism
**Answer: a) chemical energy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A battery releases its stored energy as…
a) electrical energy  b) food  c) charcoal  d) rainfall
**Answer: a) electrical energy**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Food stores…
a) chemical energy for the body  b) electricity for lamps  c) petrol  d) gas in a bottle
**Answer: a) chemical energy for the body**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Stored energy is useful because it can be…
a) released at the time it is needed  b) never used  c) kept without ever running out  d) seen with the eyes
**Answer: a) released at the time it is needed**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Burning a fuel lets its stored energy out as…
a) heat, light and movement  b) cold and darkness  c) water and soil  d) silence
**Answer: a) heat, light and movement**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Every energy store…
a) runs out unless it is filled again  b) fills itself  c) lasts forever  d) works without being used
**Answer: a) runs out unless it is filled again**
