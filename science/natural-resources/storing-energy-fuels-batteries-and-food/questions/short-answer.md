# Storing energy — fuels, batteries and food — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: energy stored in fuels and batteries.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 11 Topic 4, "Storing energy", looks at where Seychelles keeps chemical energy ready for use: petrol, gas, kerosene and charcoal are stored fuels — energy held in a tank, a bottle or a sack until burning lets it out as heat, light and movement.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name four stored fuels this lesson names for Seychelles, and what kind of energy food stores.
**Answer:** Petrol, gas, kerosene and charcoal; food stores chemical energy, released by the body that eats it.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Energy stored in fuels and batteries → Food — the body's stored energy — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
