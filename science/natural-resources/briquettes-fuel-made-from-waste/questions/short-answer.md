# Briquettes — fuel made from waste — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Briquettes: carbonised briquettes (material charred in low oxygen, powdered, bound with clay, rice-flour starch, molasses or taro starch, then moulded) against non-carbonised ones (the home dung cake; high-pressure piston, screw and roller presses); Khumaltar's 1986 rice-husk briquettes and Nepal Rastra Bank's briquettes made from worn-out banknotes as the country's own cases.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed pp. 150–151.**
