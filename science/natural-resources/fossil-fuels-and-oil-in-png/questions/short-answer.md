# Fossil fuels — and oil in Papua New Guinea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What a fossil fuel is.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 10 guide opens its Minerals and Fossil Fuel topic with the definition: a **fossil fuel** is a fuel formed from the remains of living things long ago, dug or pumped from the ground and burned for energy — oil is the topic's worked example.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Oil production in Papua New Guinea.
**Answer:** The guide then turns to the country itself: **oil production in PNG** — Papua New Guinea produces oil from its own ground. The country's own resource is the lesson's case: a fossil fuel not in a faraway example, but in PNG's own land.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Minerals of Papua New Guinea.
**Answer:** The same topic names the **minerals of PNG and their properties** — the country's own mineral wealth, taught by what each mineral is like. Fossil fuels and minerals together are Papua New Guinea's underground resources, and the guide teaches them as the country's own.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What a fossil fuel is → Oil production in Papua New Guinea → Minerals of Papua New Guinea — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
