# Using the sea — and its resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The sea as a natural resource  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The sea as a natural resource**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Papua New Guinea's own coastal way  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Papua New Guinea's own coastal way**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the use of the sea — Papua New Guinea's own coastal way of teaching natural resources. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The books behind this lesson are…
a) Papua New Guinea's science teacher guides (Department of Education, Papua New Guinea) — guide evidence  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A student book only
