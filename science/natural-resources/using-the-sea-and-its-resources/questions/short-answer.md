# Using the sea — and its resources — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The sea as a natural resource.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 3 guide teaches natural resources through the country's own coast: **use of the sea** is one of its named lessons, beside uses of trees and uses of soil. For an island country, the sea is not scenery — it is a resource people live from.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Papua New Guinea's own coastal way.
**Answer:** The guide's way is the lesson's way: resources are taught from the life around the learner — the sea first among them for coastal Papua New Guinea. What the sea gives, and how people use it, is the country's own teaching of what a natural resource is.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The sea as a natural resource → Papua New Guinea's own coastal way — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
