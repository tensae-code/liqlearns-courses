Today: **Using the sea — and its resources** — Papua New Guinea's way, from its Department of Education science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 3 guide teaches natural resources through the country's own coast: **use of the sea** is one of its named lessons, beside uses of trees and uses of soil. For an island country, the sea is not scenery — it is a resource people live from.

The guide's way is the lesson's way: resources are taught from the life around the learner — the sea first among them for coastal Papua New Guinea. What the sea gives, and how people use it, is the country's own teaching of what a natural resource is.
