**Course:** Science · **Title:** Natural Resources · **Lesson:** Bioenergy — biomass, biogas and biofuels

## Sub lessons
1. **Bioenergy defined** — bioenergy defined: energy produced from organic matter (biomass — everything living beings are made of, their products and their wastes), used by direct combustion or by transformation into solid, liquid and gaseous biofuels.
2. **Solid biofuels** — solid biofuels: firewood (cut wood not used as timber — the main solid biofuel in Honduras), agro-industrial residues (stalks, leaves, seeds and fruit husks left after harvest) and wood chips, made into compacted pellets and briquettes that substitute for firewood.
3. **Biodiesel** — biodiesel: made from oil plants (sunflower, soy, African palm, peanut, mustard) and from used cooking oils and fats; it is degradable, low in toxicity and produces fewer polluting gases, and powers cars and electric generators.
