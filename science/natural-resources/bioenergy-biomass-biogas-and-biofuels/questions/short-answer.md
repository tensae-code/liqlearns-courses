# Bioenergy — biomass, biogas and biofuels — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about bioenergy defined?
**Answer: bioenergy defined: energy produced from organic matter (biomass — everything living beings are made of, their products and their wastes), used by direct combustion or by transformation into solid, liquid and gaseous biofuels.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about solid biofuels?
**Answer: solid biofuels: firewood (cut wood not used as timber — the main solid biofuel in Honduras), agro-industrial residues (stalks, leaves, seeds and fruit husks left after harvest) and wood chips, made into compacted pellets and briquettes that substitute for firewood.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about biodiesel?
**Answer: biodiesel: made from oil plants (sunflower, soy, African palm, peanut, mustard) and from used cooking oils and fats; it is degradable, low in toxicity and produces fewer polluting gases, and powers cars and electric generators.**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Bioenergy — biomass, biogas and biofuels.**
