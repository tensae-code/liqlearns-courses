# Video script — Minerals from the ground

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Stone, gravel, sand, salt, iron — **dug and found**.
Ring, wire, cross, coins — **made from the ground**.
Coal, oil, gold — **the country's income**.
Used once, gone forever. Spend minerals with thought.

Limestone, granite, opal, copper — the list grows at Grade 4. And every mineral goes **to work**: jewellery, machines, factories, energy, export. Ask the people who dig and shape them.
