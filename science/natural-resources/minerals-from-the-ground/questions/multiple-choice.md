# Minerals from the ground — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Minerals are things —**
a) grown in gardens  b) dug from the ground or found on it  c) made in factories from air
**Answer: b) dug from the ground or found on it**

**2. Non-renewable means —**
a) never useful  b) used once and gone — it does not grow back  c) too heavy to move
**Answer: b) used once and gone — it does not grow back**

**3. Which mineral is liquid in the Grade 3 list?**
a) stone  b) fuel/oil  c) gravel
**Answer: b) fuel/oil**

**4. Minerals are —**
a) renewable  b) non-renewable  c) alive
**Answer: b) non-renewable**
**5. Which mineral joins the list at Grade 4?**
a) opal  b) plastic  c) rubber
**Answer: a) opal**

**6. One use of minerals in the Grade 4 book is —**
a) export income  b) making rain  c) growing crops directly
**Answer: a) export income**
