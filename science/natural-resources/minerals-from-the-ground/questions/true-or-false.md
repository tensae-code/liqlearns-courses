# Minerals from the ground — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. A silver cross and coins are products made from minerals.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Gold is a mineral that earns the country income.**
**Answer: True**
**3. Opal is found in South Gondar and Wollo.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Gold is Ethiopia's second export earner after tantalum.**
**Answer: False**
