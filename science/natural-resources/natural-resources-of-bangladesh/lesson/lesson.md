## Sub lesson 2 — Ore minerals and energy minerals

The chapter sorts Bangladesh's mineral resources into two kinds: ore minerals, mined for what can be made from them, and energy minerals, mined for the energy they release. The sort is the country's own inventory of what lies under its ground.

## Sub lesson 3 — Resources in their place

Taught last, after resources and their kinds in general, the home inventory gives the general lesson a local address: these are the resources whose use and care the learner's own country must decide.
