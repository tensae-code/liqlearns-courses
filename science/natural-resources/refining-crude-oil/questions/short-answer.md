# Refining crude oil — what a refinery does — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: A barrel with a history — crude oil before processing.
**Answer:** Oil from the ground is **crude oil** — raw, unprocessed, not yet usable as it comes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Processing crude oil — the refinery at work.
**Answer:** The changing happens in a **refinery**: processing crude oil is the refinery's work — the raw oil goes in, and usable products come out.
