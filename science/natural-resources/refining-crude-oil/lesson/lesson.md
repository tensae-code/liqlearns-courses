# Refining crude oil — what a refinery does

## Do it first
The oil that comes out of the ground cannot go straight into a car or a heater. Something must be done to it first, in a special factory. What is that place called, and what does it do? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: Greece's Grade 6 book follows one barrel through that processing.

## Sub lesson 1 — A barrel with a history — crude oil before processing

Oil from the ground is **crude oil** — raw, unprocessed, not yet usable as it comes. The book's feature follows **a barrel with a history**: one barrel of this crude, with a past in the ground, on its way to being changed. Crude is the starting material; nothing in the home burns crude as it comes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Processing crude oil — the refinery at work

The changing happens in a **refinery**: processing crude oil is the refinery's work — the raw oil goes in, and usable products come out. That is why the journey needs this chapter of its own: between the drilling of the last lesson and the using of the next ones stands the processing, and Greece teaches it as a step in its own right.
