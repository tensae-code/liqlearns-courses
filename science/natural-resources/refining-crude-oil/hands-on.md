# Hands-on — Refining crude oil — what a refinery does

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Before and after the refinery
Draw two boxes — 'crude oil, as it comes from the ground' and 'after the refinery' — and an arrow between them labelled with the refinery's work: processing. Say out loud why the arrow cannot be skipped.

## Retell the barrel's history
Tell the barrel's story in four sentences: where it began, what it was when it came up, where it was processed, and what it became usable for. Check each sentence against the lesson.
