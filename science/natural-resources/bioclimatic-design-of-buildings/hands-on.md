# Hands-on — Bioclimatic design of buildings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your home's design
Stand in your home and list: where does the Sun enter, and when? Where does water come in and go out? For each, write whether the house was designed with it or against it. Check your list against the lesson.

## Draw the meeting
Draw one house with the Sun on one side and water on the other, and arrows showing each one welcomed into the design. Title your drawing with the book's words: technology meets the Sun and the water.
