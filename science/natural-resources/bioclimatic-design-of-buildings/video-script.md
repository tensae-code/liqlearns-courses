The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Bioclimatic design of buildings** — Greece's way, from its Grade 6 book «Φυσικά» ΣΤ΄ (Greece, Grade 6).

**Bioclimatic design** designs a building around its climate — and the first partner is the **Sun**. A house designed around the Sun takes the Sun's light and warmth into its plan, instead of shutting them out and paying fuel to replace them. The design comes first; the saving follows from it.
The second partner is **water**: the same design reckons with water in the house's plan. The book's spread names the meeting plainly — **technology meets the Sun and the water**: building know-how joined to the two great givens of the place. A bioclimatic house is that meeting, built.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
