# Solar energy — from the sun — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) The sun as a source  b) Using the sun's energy  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Solar energy
**Answer: d) Solar energy**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about the sun's energy, named on its own?
a) Solar energy  b) The sun as a source  c) Using the sun's energy
**Answer: a) Solar energy**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about a source that arrives daily?
a) Solar energy  b) The sun as a source  c) Using the sun's energy
**Answer: b) The sun as a source**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about the sun's energy put to work?
a) Solar energy  b) The sun as a source  c) Using the sun's energy
**Answer: c) Using the sun's energy**
