# Solar energy — from the sun — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Solar energy.
**Answer: the sun's energy, named on its own**

**2.** This part of the lesson is about _____: The sun as a source.
**Answer: a source that arrives daily**

**3.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Using the sun's energy.
**Answer: the sun's energy put to work**
