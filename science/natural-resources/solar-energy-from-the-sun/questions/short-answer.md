# Solar energy — from the sun — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Solar energy" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 5 guide teaches **solar energy** as a named unit inside its energy run: the sun's energy, named and studied on its own before the fuels unit that follows.

**2.** What does the part "The sun as a source" teach?
**Answer:** Solar energy is a source that arrives daily and does not finish: the guide places the unit between its careers unit and renewable and non-renewable fuels, so the sun is met first as a source among sources.

**3.** What does the part "Using the sun's energy" teach?
**Answer:** Energy named and placed among the sources can be used on purpose.
