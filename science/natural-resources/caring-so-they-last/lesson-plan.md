# Lesson plan — Caring so they last: looking after natural resources

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Natural Resources · **Lesson:** Caring so they last: looking after natural resources
**Place in the curriculum:** Natural Resources — the answer lesson, with the tree-nursery project (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the harms of loss — soil washed away, fertility and yields falling, wild animals fleeing and vanishing, climate disturbed — and the care that answers: using nature without destroying it: planting trees, building terraces, protecting wild animals, stopping forest clearing; with the school tree-nursery project
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on How a rich place becomes bare — the loss described; this lesson is the book's reply, and it ends in a project, not a paragraph.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the harms: soil washed away, fertility and yields falling, wild animals fleeing and vanishing, climate disturbed.
2. State the care principle: using nature without destroying it.
3. Name the care acts: planting trees, building terraces, protecting wild animals, stopping forest clearing — and ask farmers about erosion causes, the book's field enquiry.
4. Run the nursery project: prepare a seedling bed at school, raise native tree seedlings, water and tend them, plant them around the school and on bare land.

5. Count the Grade 4 causes of depletion — population's farm-land expansion, wood demand, forest clearing and burning, degradation, pollution, climate change, drought and flood, desert spread, species loss, poverty, erosion — and the protection methods that answer them.
6. Name the traditional conservation methods — rotation, intercropping, dung and compost, fallow, terracing, contour ploughing, stopping free grazing, fenced no-cut areas — and prepare a care plan for one local resource, the book's project.
## What you need
Seedling bed space at school or home, native tree seed or seedlings, water, a slope that needs a terrace's example to look at.

## If the learner is stuck
- Plants trees and walks away → the project verbs are raise, water, tend, plant out. A dead seedling is a paragraph, not a programme.
- Recites cares but cannot match them to harms → pair them as the book does: each care answers a named harm.
- This lesson closes the Grades 1–3 natural-resources band; the environment title gathers the same duty at home and school scale.
