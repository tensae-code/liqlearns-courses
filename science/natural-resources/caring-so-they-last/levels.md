# Caring so they last: looking after natural resources — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: pair each harm with its care act and run the nursery project through to planting out
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 4 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also counts the depletion causes, answers with the book's protection methods (many seedlings for every tree cut), and names the traditional methods (rotation, intercropping, dung/compost, fallow, terracing, contour ploughing, no free grazing, fenced no-cut areas), ending in a care plan for one local resource.
