# Caring so they last: looking after natural resources — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. Care means using nature without ___ it.**
**Answer: destroying**

**2. The nursery raises ___ tree seedlings.**
**Answer: native**
**3. Ploughing sloping land ___ the slope is a traditional protection method.**
**Answer: across**
**4. Agenda ______ carries the 17 goals.**
**Answer: 2030**

**5. Goal 6 is clean ______ and sanitation for all.**
**Answer: water**
