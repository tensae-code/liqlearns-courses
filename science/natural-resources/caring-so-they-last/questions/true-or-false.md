# Caring so they last: looking after natural resources — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. Wild animals fleeing and vanishing is one of the harms of resource loss.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Stopping forest clearing is a care act.**
**Answer: True**
**3. Resting farm land (fallow) is one of the traditional conservation methods.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Free grazing is one of the traditional ways of protecting soil.**
**Answer: False**
