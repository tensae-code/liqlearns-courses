# Caring so they last: looking after natural resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is one of the book's care acts?**
a) clearing forests  b) building terraces  c) burning grassland
**Answer: b) building terraces**

**2. The nursery project raises seedlings of —**
a) any imported trees  b) native trees  c) plastic trees
**Answer: b) native trees**

**3. Which harms follow resource loss?**
a) yields rising  b) fertility and yields falling  c) more wild animals
**Answer: b) fertility and yields falling**

**4. Planting trees answers which harms?**
a) washed soil and disturbed climate  b) none  c) only darkness
**Answer: a) washed soil and disturbed climate**
