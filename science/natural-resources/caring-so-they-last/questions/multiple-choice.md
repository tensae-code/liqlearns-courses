# Caring so they last: looking after natural resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is one of the book's care acts?**
a) clearing forests  b) building terraces  c) burning grassland
**Answer: b) building terraces**

**2. The nursery project raises seedlings of —**
a) any imported trees  b) native trees  c) plastic trees
**Answer: b) native trees**

**3. Which harms follow resource loss?**
a) yields rising  b) fertility and yields falling  c) more wild animals
**Answer: b) fertility and yields falling**

**4. Planting trees answers which harms?**
a) washed soil and disturbed climate  b) none  c) only darkness
**Answer: a) washed soil and disturbed climate**
**5. One cause of resource depletion in the book is —**
a) farm-land expansion for a growing population  b) too much terracing  c) planting seedlings
**Answer: a) farm-land expansion for a growing population**

**6. The book's replanting rule is —**
a) one seedling for every tree cut  b) many seedlings for every tree cut  c) no replanting needed
**Answer: b) many seedlings for every tree cut**

**7. Intercropping means —**
a) sowing crops together  b) burning fields  c) grazing everywhere
**Answer: a) sowing crops together**
**8. Sustainable development must not compromise —**
a) today's needs  b) future generations' ability to meet their needs  c) exports
**Answer: b) future generations' ability to meet their needs**

**9. Agenda 2030 was signed in —**
a) 2015  b) 2000  c) 2030
**Answer: a) 2015**

**10. How many goals does it carry?**
a) 7  b) 17  c) 30
**Answer: b) 17**

**11. Which is one of the book's three pupil projects?**
a) building a road  b) sorting dumped rubbish by kind  c) catching wildlife
**Answer: b) sorting dumped rubbish by kind**

**12. Substitution (teacher guide — evidence) means —**
a) replacing non-renewable resources with renewable ones  b) using more fuel  c) stopping all farming
**Answer: a) replacing non-renewable resources with renewable ones**
