# Video script — Caring so they last: looking after natural resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soil washes. Yields fall. Animals flee. Climate stirs.
Plant trees. Build terraces. Protect the wild. Stop the clearing.
Raise seedlings in the school bed. Plant them on the bare land.
Use nature. Never destroy it. That is care.

Why does a rich land run down? More mouths, more wood, cleared forests, tired soil. The answer is old and new together: **terraces, seedlings — many for every tree cut** — and the elders' ways: **rotation, fallow, dung, contour lines**.
