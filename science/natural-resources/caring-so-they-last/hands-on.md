# Hands-on — Caring so they last: looking after natural resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The nursery itself
The project IS the hands-on: bed, seed, water, tend, plant out. Keep a survival count a month after planting — survival is the result, not planting.

## Terrace lines
On a bare slope (with leave), lay out one contour line with stones or sticks. You have drawn a terrace's first sentence.

## Protection promise
Choose one wild creature or one tree near school and become its named protector for the term. Log threats and defences.
