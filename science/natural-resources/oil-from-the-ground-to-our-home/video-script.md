# Video script — Oil — from the ground to our home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Oil — from the ground to our home** — Greece's way, from its Grade 6 book «Φυσικά» ΣΤ΄ (Greece, Grade 6).

The journey starts with a **search**: oil is looked for underground, where it lies hidden. When it is found, it is **drilled** for — brought up from the underground where it was kept. Searching first, drilling second: the book's order is the journey's order, and its feature name for the prize is **"black gold"** — gold, because of what it is worth; black, because of what comes up.
Drilled oil is still far from any home. The journey's last leg is **transporting** it — moving the oil from where it comes out of the ground to the places that will use it. Ground to home is the chapter's whole arc: searched for, drilled, transported — and only then used.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
