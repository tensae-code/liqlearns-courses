# Oil — from the ground to our home

## Do it first
Oil heats homes and moves cars — but nobody picks it up off the ground. It begins buried deep under the earth or the sea. What has to happen, step by step, before it reaches a home? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: Greece's Grade 6 book walks that whole journey in one chapter.

## Sub lesson 1 — Searching for oil — and drilling for it

The journey starts with a **search**: oil is looked for underground, where it lies hidden. When it is found, it is **drilled** for — brought up from the underground where it was kept. Searching first, drilling second: the book's order is the journey's order, and its feature name for the prize is **"black gold"** — gold, because of what it is worth; black, because of what comes up.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transporting oil — from the ground to our home

Drilled oil is still far from any home. The journey's last leg is **transporting** it — moving the oil from where it comes out of the ground to the places that will use it. Ground to home is the chapter's whole arc: searched for, drilled, transported — and only then used.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "What are the three steps of oil's journey in Greece's chapter, in order?" Answer: Searching for it underground, drilling for it and bringing it up, then transporting it to our home — the book's feature name for oil is "black gold".
