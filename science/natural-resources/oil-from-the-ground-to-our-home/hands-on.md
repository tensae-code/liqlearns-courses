# Hands-on — Oil — from the ground to our home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the journey
Draw oil's journey in three boxes in the book's order — searching, drilling, transporting — with one line under each box saying what happens there. Check the order against the lesson.

## Black gold — why the name?
Write two lines: why the book's feature calls oil "gold", and why it calls it "black". Then say both lines out loud to someone at home.
