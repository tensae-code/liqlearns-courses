# Hands-on — Checking a product's whole life cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: science, technology and society: environment-friendly tools and the product life-cycle analysis — six stages (getting raw materials, manufacture, transport to shops and homes, use and reuse, recycling, and final disposal by burial or burning) by which a product's effect on the environment is judged; hand-made products pass the test easily, being of natural materials that decompose Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
