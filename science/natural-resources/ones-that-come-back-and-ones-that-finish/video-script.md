# Video script — Ones that come back and ones that finish

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some resources **come back**: water, air, plants — **renewable**.
Some are **finished** once used: petrol, iron, gold, coal — **non-renewable**.
Guard the ones that return. Spend the others with thought.
