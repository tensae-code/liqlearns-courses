# Hands-on — Ones that come back and ones that finish

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-family table
Copy the book's starting table — soil, water, minerals, walia, trees — and sort it with the rule, writing your reason beside each. Disagreements are the lesson: argue them.

## Home audit
Walk your home and yard listing resources in use. Two columns: COMES BACK / FINISHES. Count the columns — most homes are surprised.
