# Ones that come back and ones that finish — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: state both rules with the book's examples and sort a mixed list by them.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also sorts the Grade 5 lists (trees/animals/plants/air against fuel, coal and the seven named minerals) and argues the water question with the care condition.
