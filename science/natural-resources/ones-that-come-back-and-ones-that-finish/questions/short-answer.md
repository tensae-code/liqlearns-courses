# Ones that come back and ones that finish — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. What is the difference between the two families of resources?**
**Answer: renewable ones come back after use and are not exhausted; non-renewable ones are finished once used**
**2. Is water renewable? Give the lesson's answer.**
**Answer: Yes — its cycle renews it — but it lasts only if used with care, like every renewable resource.**
