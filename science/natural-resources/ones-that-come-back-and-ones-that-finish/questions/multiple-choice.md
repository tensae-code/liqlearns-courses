# Ones that come back and ones that finish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Renewable resources —**
a) are finished once used  b) come back after use and are not exhausted  c) are only minerals
**Answer: b) come back after use and are not exhausted**

**2. Which is renewable in the book's list?**
a) petrol  b) water  c) coal
**Answer: b) water**

**3. Which is non-renewable in the book's list?**
a) air  b) plants  c) gold
**Answer: c) gold**

**4. Diesel is —**
a) renewable  b) non-renewable  c) not a resource
**Answer: b) non-renewable**
**5. Which is non-renewable in the book's list?**
a) trees  b) air  c) gold
**Answer: c) gold**

**6. Renewable resources last long if —**
a) never touched  b) used with care  c) burned quickly
**Answer: b) used with care**

**7. How many minerals does the book's non-renewable list name?**
a) seven — gold, copper, diamond, iron, brass, salt, stone  b) two  c) none
**Answer: a) seven — gold, copper, diamond, iron, brass, salt, stone**

**8. (Sub lesson 1) Chapter 3 review: a resource that comes back after use is called —**
a) non-renewable  b) renewable  c) waste
**Answer: b) renewable** — Renewable, as reviewed.
