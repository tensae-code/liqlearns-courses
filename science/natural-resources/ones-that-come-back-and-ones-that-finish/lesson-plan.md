# Lesson plan — Ones that come back and ones that finish

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Natural Resources · **Lesson:** Ones that come back and ones that finish
**Place in the curriculum:** Natural Resources — the great sorting of resources: renewable and non-renewable (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** resources sort into two families: renewable ones come back after use and are not exhausted (water, air, plants), and non-renewable ones are finished once used (petrol, diesel, iron, gold, copper, coal)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What counts as a natural resource — the whole family defined; now it is sorted in two.

## If the learner is stuck
- Sorts by solid/liquid instead of coming back/finishing → the families are about return, not state: water (liquid) returns; coal (solid) finishes; air (gas) returns.
- Thinks renewable means we cannot harm it → polluted water and felled forests stop 'coming back' in any useful way; the care lessons guard the return.
