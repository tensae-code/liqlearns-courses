# Energy sources around us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Energy is —**
a) a kind of food  b) the ability to do a work  c) only electricity
**Answer: b) the ability to do a work**

**2. Which is one of Ethiopia's energy sources?**
a) coal from the moon  b) wind  c) none of these
**Answer: b) wind**

**3. Ashegoda and Adama are —**
a) hydro dams  b) wind farms  c) biogas plants
**Answer: b) wind farms**

**4. The GERD is a —**
a) wind farm  b) solar panel  c) hydro dam
**Answer: c) hydro dam**

**5. Energy is used for —**
a) light, industry and farming  b) only light  c) sleeping
**Answer: a) light, industry and farming**
