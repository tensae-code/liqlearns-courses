# Hands-on — Energy sources around us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The waterwheel
Build a small waterwheel from sticks and spoons and hold it under a poured stream (or a tap, with an adult). Energy you can watch working — the guide's river-and-mill picture, made.

## Asset map
Pin the named assets on the class map: GERD, Gilgel Gibe, Tana Beles, Fincha, Tekeze; Ashegoda and Adama wind. The map is Ethiopia's energy skeleton.
