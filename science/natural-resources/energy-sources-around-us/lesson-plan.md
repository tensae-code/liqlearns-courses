# Lesson plan — Energy sources around us

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Natural Resources · **Lesson:** Energy sources around us
**Place in the curriculum:** Natural Resources — energy, a resource the country is still building (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** energy is the ability to do a work; Ethiopia's power comes from water, sun, wind, fuel and biogas — hydro dams (GERD, Gilgel Gibe I and II, Tana Beles, Fincha, Tekeze), wind farms (Ashegoda and Adama) and solar panels — used for light, industry and farming, and the Grade 5/6 guides sort energy sources renewable against non-renewable (teacher guide — evidence)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ones that come back and ones that finish — energy sources sort by that same family line.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Natural Resources — Ones that come back and ones that finish. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define energy as the guides print it: the ability to do a work (አንድን ሥራ የመሥራት አቅም).
2. Name Ethiopia's energy sources — water, sun, wind, fuel, biogas — and its named assets: the hydro dams and the two wind farms.
3. Say what the energy is used for: light, industry and farming.

## What you need
A torch (dry-cell energy), a picture of a dam; the class's own list of everything at home that eats energy.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks energy is only electricity → food giving strength and a river turning a mill are the guides' own energy pictures; electricity is one form energy takes.
- Names the GERD a wind farm → sort the assets by their source: GERD is water (hydro); Ashegoda and Adama are wind.
