# Fuels we use — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Types of fuel" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 1 guide teaches **types of fuel**, and the Grade 2 guide teaches types **and importance of fuels**: a fuel is a thing burned to give energy, and the fuels people use can be named and sorted.

**2.** What does the part "Why fuels matter" teach?
**Answer:** The Grade 2 unit's second half is the **importance** of fuels: cooking, warmth and work all hang on fuel, which is why the topic is taught to the youngest grades.

**3.** What does the part "Fuels, used with care" teach?
**Answer:** Grades 3 and 4 return to **fuels** as their own unit inside the energy run, after forms of energy: knowing the fuel and its importance grows into using each fuel with care.
