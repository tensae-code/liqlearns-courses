# Fuels we use — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Why fuels matter  b) Fuels, used with care  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Types of fuel
**Answer: d) Types of fuel**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about fuels named and sorted?
a) Types of fuel  b) Why fuels matter  c) Fuels, used with care
**Answer: a) Types of fuel**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about fuel behind cooking, warmth and work?
a) Types of fuel  b) Why fuels matter  c) Fuels, used with care
**Answer: b) Why fuels matter**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about each fuel used with care?
a) Types of fuel  b) Why fuels matter  c) Fuels, used with care
**Answer: c) Fuels, used with care**
