# Fuels we use

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — fuels we use — the types of fuel and why they matter, as Zimbabwe's Grade 1, 2, 3 and 4 guides teach the unit. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Types of fuel

Zimbabwe's Grade 1 guide teaches **types of fuel**, and the Grade 2 guide teaches types **and importance of fuels**: a fuel is a thing burned to give energy, and the fuels people use can be named and sorted.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why fuels matter

The Grade 2 unit's second half is the **importance** of fuels: cooking, warmth and work all hang on fuel, which is why the topic is taught to the youngest grades.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fuels, used with care

Grades 3 and 4 return to **fuels** as their own unit inside the energy run, after forms of energy: knowing the fuel and its importance grows into using each fuel with care.

## The key ideas

- **Types of fuel** — fuels named and sorted.
- **Why fuels matter** — fuel behind cooking, warmth and work.
- **Fuels, used with care** — each fuel used with care.
