# Hands-on — Water, soil and air around us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Water's web
Draw the book's concept-web: WATER in the centre, its uses radiating — then add a red ring: the pollutions that threaten every spoke.

## Topsoil depth test
Push a marked stick into topsoil in a garden and in a yard. Compare the fertile depth. The garden's richness is a depth you can measure.

## Tree planting for air
Plant or protect one tree and register it under air's protection list: this tree is an air cleaner on duty.
