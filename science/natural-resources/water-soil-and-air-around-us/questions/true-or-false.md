# Water, soil and air around us — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) All living things breathe air.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Soil is found in black, red and brown colours.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Water is used for transport and power as well as drinking and cooking.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Air pollution is a harm the book says is happening now.**
**Answer: True**
