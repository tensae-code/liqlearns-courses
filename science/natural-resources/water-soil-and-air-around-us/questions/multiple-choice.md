# Water, soil and air around us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a natural resource?**
a) a plastic chair  b) soil  c) a school book
**Answer: b) soil** — soil is given by nature, not made by people.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Soil, in the book's definition, is —**
a) any dust  b) the fertile top layer where roots reach  c) deep rock
**Answer: b) the fertile top layer where roots reach**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The book's chain runs: without soil, no plants — and without plants, no —**
a) rain  b) animals  c) stones
**Answer: b) animals**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Which is a source of water in the book's list?**
a) a factory  b) a spring  c) a latrine
**Answer: b) a spring**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Which of these pollutes water?**
a) dung left near a source  b) a clean cover  c) boiling
**Answer: a) dung left near a source**

**6. (Sub lesson 4) Green plants use which part of the air to make food?**
a) carbon dioxide  b) smoke  c) dust
**Answer: a) carbon dioxide**

**7. (Sub lesson 4) Which of these protects the air?**
a) burning waste in the street  b) planting trees  c) cutting trees
**Answer: b) planting trees**
