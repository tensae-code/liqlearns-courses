# How fossil fuels formed

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — the formation of fossil fuel over long ages, as Iraq's Grade 4 pupil's book teaches it, with the activity book's work on fossil fuel sources and forms. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Iraq's way in — its Ministry of Education *Science* books (the Primary Science Book Series) teach this thing at the places named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Formed over long ages

Iraq's Grade 4 book teaches **fossil fuel** by its making: its formation took **long ages**. The fuel was not made in a day or a year — it formed slowly, over ages of the Earth, long before anyone dug for it.

That slowness is the fact the whole subject stands on, and the book teaches it next to the fuels that finish: what took ages to form cannot be remade on any human timescale — formed once, over long ages, and used once.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sources and forms — the activity book's work

The Grade 4 activity book works the same ground from the other side: **fossil fuel sources and forms** — where fossil fuels are got from, and the forms they come in.

Chapter and activity together give the fuel its whole frame: formed over long ages below, got from its sources, and met in its forms above.

## The key ideas

- **Fossil fuel formed over long ages** — not in a day or a year.
- What took ages to form cannot be remade on a human timescale.
- The activity book adds fossil fuels' **sources and forms**.
