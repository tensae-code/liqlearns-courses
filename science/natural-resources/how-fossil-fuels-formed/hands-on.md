# Hands-on — How fossil fuels formed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The long-ages line
Draw a line for time: a day, a year, a lifetime, and then — far beyond the page — the long ages of formation. Mark where fossil fuel's making sits.

## Sources and forms
Make a two-column table: sources of fossil fuel, forms of fossil fuel. Fill each column from what you know of the fuels lessons, and check it against the lesson.
