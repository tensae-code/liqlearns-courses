# How fossil fuels formed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Fossil fuel formed…
a) In a day  b) In a year  c) Over long ages  d) In a factory
**Answer: c) Over long ages**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Because formation took ages, fossil fuel…
a) Can be remade quickly  b) Cannot be remade on a human timescale  c) Is not used  d) Is alive
**Answer: b) Cannot be remade on a human timescale**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The activity book's work is fossil fuels'…
a) Prices only  b) Sources and forms  c) Songs  d) Maps only
**Answer: b) Sources and forms**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The chapter teaches fossil fuel by…
a) Its making — its formation  b) Its colour  c) Its smell  d) Its name in English
**Answer: a) Its making — its formation**
