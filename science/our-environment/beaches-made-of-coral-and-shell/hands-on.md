# Hands-on — Beaches — made of coral and shell

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a handful of sand
If you can reach a beach or a sand sample, spread a spoonful on paper and sort it with your fingers and eyes: shell pieces, coral pieces, dark grains, white grains. Say what your sort tells you about where that sand came from. If no beach is near, do the sorting from the lesson's two sand kinds in a drawing, labelled by source.

## Beach food chain in three
Draw the beach chain from the lesson in three boxes: sand animals → crabs → seabirds (and the birds that also feed on the sand animals directly). Under it, draw one salt-tolerant shore plant and label the salt it must tolerate.
