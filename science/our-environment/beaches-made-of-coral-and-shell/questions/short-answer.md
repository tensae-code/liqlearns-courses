# Beaches — made of coral and shell — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How can the colour of a beach tell you what it is made of?
**Answer: White sand on these islands is broken coral and shell — reef remains; dark sand on a high island is volcanic, from the island's own rock. The colour points to the source.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is a beach called a habitat in this lesson?
**Answer: Because living things make their home and find their food there: tiny sand animals live in the sand and feed crabs and seabirds, and salt-tolerant shore plants grow along the sheltered shore.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Where do salt-tolerant shore plants grow, as the readers teach it?
**Answer: On the sheltered, lagoon-facing sandy shore of coral fragments, where they can live with the sea's salt and spray.**
