# Beaches — made of coral and shell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Low-island beach sand is made of…
a) Ground mountain rock only  b) Broken coral and shells  c) Mud from rivers  d) Fallen leaves
**Answer: b) Broken coral and shells**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A lagoon-facing beach is made of…
a) Coral fragments  b) Volcanic ash only  c) Ice  d) Clay
**Answer: a) Coral fragments**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Dark beach sand on a high island comes from…
a) Burnt wood  b) Volcanic rock  c) Black shells  d) Deep-sea mud
**Answer: b) Volcanic rock**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The tiny sand animals are food for…
a) Taro plants  b) Crabs and seabirds  c) Mangrove trees  d) Clouds
**Answer: b) Crabs and seabirds**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The plants of the sheltered lagoon-facing shore must be able to tolerate…
a) Snow  b) Salt  c) Darkness  d) Fresh river water
**Answer: b) Salt**
