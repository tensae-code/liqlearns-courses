# Beaches — made of coral and shell — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Beach sand on low islands is broken _____ and shells.
**Answer: coral**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Dark sand on high islands is _____ sand.
**Answer: volcanic**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Tiny sand animals feed crabs and _____.
**Answer: seabirds**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Shore plants on the lagoon-facing beach must tolerate _____.
**Answer: salt**
