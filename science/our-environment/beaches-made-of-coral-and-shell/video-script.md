Today: **Beaches — made of coral and shell** — Micronesia's way: read the sand, and you read the island.

On a low Pacific island, the beach sand is broken coral and shells — reef animals' hard remains, ground down to grains. On a high island the sand can be dark, from volcanic rock, next to the white coral sand. And the beach is alive: tiny animals in the sand feed crabs and seabirds, and tough plants that can bear salt grow along the sheltered lagoon shore.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
