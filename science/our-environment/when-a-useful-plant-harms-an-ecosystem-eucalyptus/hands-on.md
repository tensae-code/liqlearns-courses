# Hands-on — When a useful plant harms an ecosystem — eucalyptus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: science, technology and society: the eucalyptus — a fast-growing Australian tree planted worldwide for timber, paper pulp and insect oils, yet accused of drinking deep groundwater, poisoning the soil against native plants and spreading forest fires with its flammable oils; Saudi afforestation research on it continues at the Prince Sultan centre Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
