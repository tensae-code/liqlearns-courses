# Hands-on — Adaptation — how living things fit where they live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fit or fail
Make a two-column table: environment — living thing that fits it. Fill it with pairs you know: water–fish, tree–bird, dry ground–its own animal, and three more from your own place. Under the table, write the lesson's question and its answer: why are the same animals not found everywhere?

## One change, two answers
Choose one change of conditions — rain after dry weeks, or the cold season arriving. Write two short paragraphs: how one animal meets the change, and how one plant meets it. Read both aloud: the animal moves and adjusts its doings; the plant answers from where it stands.
