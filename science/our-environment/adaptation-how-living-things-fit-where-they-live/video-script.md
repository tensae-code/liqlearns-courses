Today: **Adaptation — how living things fit where they live**.

Why are the same animals not found everywhere? Djibouti's Grade 4 guide answers with adaptation: every living thing fits its own environment. When the conditions change, animals adapt — their way of living adjusts. And plants adapt too, fitting themselves to their conditions from the spot where they grow. Fit to a place, and fit to change: that is adaptation.
