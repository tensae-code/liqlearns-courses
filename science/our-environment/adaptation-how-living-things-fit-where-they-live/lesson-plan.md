# Lesson plan — Adaptation — how living things fit where they live

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Adaptation — how living things fit where they live
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** adaptation — why the same animals are not found everywhere, how animals adapt when their environment's conditions change, and how plants adapt to the conditions of their environment — Djibouti's own way, in its Grade 4 Sciences Expérimentales teacher guide, is to teach the three as one run of lessons inside the living-environments module
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Why the same animals are not found everywhere** — Each kind of animal is found where its environment fits it. Djibouti's Grade 4 guide asks why the same animals are not found everywhere — and the answer is the lesson's ground: living things fit particular places, not every place.
2. **How animals adapt when conditions change** — Environments do not stand still. When the conditions of an animal's environment change, the animal adapts — its way of living adjusts to the new conditions — and the guide teaches that adjusting as the animal's answer to change.
3. **How plants adapt to their environment** — Plants adapt too. A plant cannot walk away from its conditions, so its adaptation is in how it lives and grows where it stands — fitting itself to the conditions of its environment as they are and as they change.
