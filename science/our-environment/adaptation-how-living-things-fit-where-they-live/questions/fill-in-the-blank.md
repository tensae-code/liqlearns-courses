# Adaptation — how living things fit where they live — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The same animals are not found _____ because each fits its own environment.
**Answer: everywhere**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) When conditions change, an animal _____ — its way of living adjusts.
**Answer: adapts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) A plant cannot walk away, so it adapts from where it _____.
**Answer: stands**
