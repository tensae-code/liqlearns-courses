# Adaptation — how living things fit where they live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is adaptation?
**Answer: The fit between a living thing and the conditions of the place it lives — why each kind is found where it fits, and not everywhere.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does an animal answer a change in its environment's conditions?
**Answer: It adapts: its way of living adjusts to the new conditions.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How is a plant's adaptation different in manner from an animal's?
**Answer: The plant cannot move to another place, so it adapts from where it stands — in how it lives and grows in its conditions.**
