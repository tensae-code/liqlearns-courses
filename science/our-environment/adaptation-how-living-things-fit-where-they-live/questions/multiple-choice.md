# Adaptation — how living things fit where they live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why are the same animals not found everywhere?
a) Because animals hide  b) Because each living thing fits its own environment  c) Because nobody has looked  d) Because animals never move
**Answer: b) Because each living thing fits its own environment**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The fit between a living thing and its environment is called…
a) Hibernation  b) Adaptation  c) Pollination  d) Evaporation
**Answer: b) Adaptation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) When an environment's conditions change, an animal…
a) Stops being alive at once  b) Adapts — its way of living adjusts to the new conditions  c) Turns into a plant  d) Never notices
**Answer: b) Adapts — its way of living adjusts to the new conditions**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A plant adapts to its environment…
a) By walking to a better place  b) By fitting how it lives and grows to its conditions, from where it stands  c) By eating animals  d) By changing its name
**Answer: b) By fitting how it lives and grows to its conditions, from where it stands**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Adaptation belongs to…
a) Animals only  b) Plants only  c) Animals and plants — every living thing fits where it lives  d) Stones and water
**Answer: c) Animals and plants — every living thing fits where it lives**
