# Hands-on — Predation and competition — animals eating and rivalling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: relations between living things in the community: predation, the commonest of them (the ladybird eating plant aphids, the sparrow hunting insects), and competition, when two kinds seek the same food, water or space Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
