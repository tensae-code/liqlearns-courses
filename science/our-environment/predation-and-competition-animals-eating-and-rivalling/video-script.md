Today: **Predation and competition — animals eating and rivalling** — Saudi Arabia's way, from its book (Saudi Arabia, First Intermediate, Part 2 — *Science, First Intermediate, Part 2*, printed p. 170).

relations between living things in the community: predation, the commonest of them (the ladybird eating plant aphids, the sparrow hunting insects), and competition, when two kinds seek the same food, water or space
competition: when a living thing takes a place in more than one chain of the web, competition follows — a struggle between living things over food, water, shelter and their other needs; plants compete too (small plants and forest flowers against tall trees for sunlight, water and nutrients), and competition happens inside one population, as sparrows compete over a plant's fruit and seeds
