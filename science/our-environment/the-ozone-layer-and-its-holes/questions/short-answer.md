# The ozone layer and its holes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The ozone covering — Earth's shield.
**Answer:** High in the atmosphere sits a covering of **ozone** that **shields Earth's surface from harmful ultraviolet rays** — the part of sunlight that harms living skin and growing plants.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Freon gases and the holes over the poles.
**Answer:** The damage came from **freon (CFC) gases** made by people: they **thin** the ozone covering, and the thinning shows most over the **North and South Poles** — the 'holes' are places where the covering has grown thin, **noticed in 1980**.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: What thinning does — and the slow recovery.
**Answer:** Where the covering thins, more ultraviolet reaches the surface: **skin diseases grow commoner, and plants and animals die**.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The ozone covering — Earth's shield → Freon gases and the holes over the poles → What thinning does — and the slow recovery — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
