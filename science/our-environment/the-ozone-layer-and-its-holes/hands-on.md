# Hands-on — The ozone layer and its holes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shield test (two sheets of paper, one with a torn thin patch)
Hold a whole sheet between a torch and your hand — the light is blocked, as ozone blocks ultraviolet. Then use a sheet thinned and torn in one patch: light leaks through the patch. Say which sheet is the healthy covering, which is the thinned one, and what the leak stands for.

## Cause and cure in one line (no materials)
Say the book's whole chain in order — freon gases released → covering thins over the poles → more ultraviolet reaches the surface → skin diseases grow and living things die → people make far fewer of the gases → the covering slowly recovers. Then say which link people could change, and did.
