# Hands-on — Primary and secondary productivity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two productivities in one garden
Watch a garden or field edge for fifteen minutes. List the producers at work (growing green plants) and every consumer you see feeding. Label each list: primary productivity, secondary productivity.

## Follow one mouthful
Pick one animal you know. Trace one mouthful of its food back down the chain to the producer that made it, and write the chain — marking where primary productivity ends and secondary begins.
