# Primary and secondary productivity — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The living matter built by green plants is _____ productivity.
**Answer: primary**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Consumers carry out _____ productivity.
**Answer: secondary**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Primary productivity sets the _____ for everything above it in the food chain.
**Answer: ceiling**
