# Primary and secondary productivity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What do the producers build, and from what?
**Answer:** Living matter — built from light, air, water and salts; that making is primary productivity.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is all secondary productivity second-hand?
**Answer:** Because consumers build only from food that producers made first — nothing in their making is new to the ecosystem; it is primary productivity passed up the chain and rebuilt.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) State the account-book picture of the pair.
**Answer:** Primary productivity is the ecosystem's income; secondary productivity is what the consumers manage to build from that income — always the smaller figure.
