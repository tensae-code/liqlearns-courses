# Primary and secondary productivity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Primary productivity is the making done by…
a) The consumers  b) The producers — green plants  c) The decomposers only  d) Rocks
**Answer: b) The producers — green plants**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Primary productivity is called primary because…
a) It is biggest in size only  b) It is first — everything else lives on it  c) It happens at night  d) Plants are oldest
**Answer: b) It is first — everything else lives on it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Secondary productivity is…
a) Plants growing  b) Consumers building their bodies from the food they eat  c) Rain falling  d) Soil forming
**Answer: b) Consumers building their bodies from the food they eat**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Secondary productivity is always smaller because…
a) Animals are lazy  b) Eating and living spend most of the producers' making  c) Plants hide their food  d) Chains are short
**Answer: b) Eating and living spend most of the producers' making**
