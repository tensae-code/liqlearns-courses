Today: **Primary and secondary productivity** — Mauritania's way: an ecosystem measured by its making.

The producers make first. Green plants build living matter from light, air, water and salts — that is primary productivity, and everything else in the ecosystem lives on it.

The consumers make second. Animals rebuild what they eat into their own bodies — secondary productivity, always smaller, because living spends most of the income. Primary is the income; secondary is what gets built from it.
