# Video script — In defence of our nature — a country project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **In defence of our nature — a country project** — Bulgaria's way: defend your nature by knowing your place.

The project: choose a protected territory near your home. Research it — its species, its rare inhabitants, and why the place matters.

That is how defence begins. A place nobody can name is a place nobody can defend. Know it first — then it can be protected.

Now you. Name the protected place nearest you, and one thing that lives there. Say it back — in your own words.
