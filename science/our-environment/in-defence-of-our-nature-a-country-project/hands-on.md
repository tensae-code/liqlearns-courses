# Hands-on — In defence of our nature — a country project

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the project in small
Choose the protected place nearest your home — a reserve, park or guarded stretch of nature. Find and write down: its name, five of its species, its rare inhabitants as far as you can learn them, and one paragraph on why it matters.

## Teach the place
Present your protected territory to someone at home in five sentences: the place, its species, its rare inhabitants, and why it matters — the project's three objects, in the project's order.
