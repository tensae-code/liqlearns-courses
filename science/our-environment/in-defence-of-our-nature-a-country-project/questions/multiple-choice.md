# In defence of our nature — a country project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The project asks each learner to research…
a) Any famous place abroad  b) A protected territory near home  c) The whole country at once  d) Only animals
**Answer: b) A protected territory near home**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The research must name the territory's…
a) Species and rare inhabitants  b) Mayor and history only  c) Size in kilometres only  d) Visitors
**Answer: a) Species and rare inhabitants**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The third object of the research is…
a) Its price  b) Why the place matters  c) Its oldest tree  d) Its roads
**Answer: b) Why the place matters**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Knowing a place is called, in this lesson,…
a) The first defence  b) A delay  c) A test  d) A holiday
**Answer: a) The first defence**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A 'rare inhabitant' is…
a) A common animal  b) An uncommon living thing the protection exists for  c) A visitor  d) A keeper
**Answer: b) An uncommon living thing the protection exists for**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The project closes Bulgaria's book as a…
a) Test  b) Project lesson  c) Story  d) Song
**Answer: b) Project lesson**
