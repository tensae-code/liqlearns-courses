# In defence of our nature — a country project — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State the project and its three research objects.
**Answer:** Research a protected territory near home: its species, its rare inhabitants, and why the place matters.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book make defence begin with research?
**Answer:** Because a defence is made of facts — which place, which species, which rare inhabitants, what is at stake — and the project gathers exactly those.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A classmate picks a famous reserve in another country. Correct the choice from the lesson.
**Answer:** The project specifies a protected territory near home — nearness is the point: the defence starts with the place the learner actually lives beside.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What can a learner who finished this project do that they could not do before?
**Answer:** Name their protected territory, name its species and rare inhabitants, and say why it matters — hold the facts a defence is made of.
