# Video script — Green technology for a sustainable environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Green technology for a sustainable environment** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 10 (Form 4) — *Sains Tingkatan 4*, printed p. 54).

Malaysia's Form 4 book teaches **Green Technology** through its **sectors — energy, waste and wastewater, agriculture and forestry, transportation, and industry** — and what each sector focuses on. In the **energy sector** it sets the socio-scientific issues openly: rising population needs pressing on the environment and natural resources.

The chapter closes by bringing **green technology into daily life** — the sectors' work landing in the choices and systems of an ordinary day — with the chapter's summative practice gathering the whole treatment.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
