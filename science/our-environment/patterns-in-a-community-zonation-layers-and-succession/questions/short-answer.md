# Patterns in a community — zonation, layers and succession — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Zonation — bands across a shore and up a mountain.
**Answer:** Zonation is the banding of organisms across a place.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Stratification — the layers of a forest.
**Answer:** A forest is also layered from top to bottom — stratification.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Succession — the lava field grows a community.
**Answer:** Succession is the pattern in time: on the Saleaula lava fields, the first plants settle in the cracks, break the rock up and make the first soil; each stage changes the conditions, until the first plants themselves die out and later kinds take their place.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Zonation — bands across a shore and up a mountain → Stratification — the layers of a forest → Succession — the lava field grows a community — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
