# Hands-on — Patterns in a community — zonation, layers and succession

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run a rope transect
Lay a rope line across a shore, a slope or a garden edge. Every metre, record what lives there and measure light or temperature; draw the profile and mark the bands.

## Read a lava field or a burnt patch
Find new ground — fresh lava, a cleared or burnt patch — and list its first plants. Predict the next stage and say what the first plants are changing to allow it.
