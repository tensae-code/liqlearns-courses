Today: **Wetlands — and why they must not be drained** — Iceland's wetland chapter, from the Líf á landi guide.
Draining wetlands shrank the waders' home: the water rail is nearly extinct in Iceland, the red-necked phalarope rare.
Restoring brings the water back — and peat bogs bind carbon dioxide, holding it in the wet ground.
