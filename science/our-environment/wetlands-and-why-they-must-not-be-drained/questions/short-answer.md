# Wetlands — and why they must not be drained — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Draining wetlands badly shrank wader habitat in Iceland — the water rail is nearly extinct there, the red-necked phalarope rare.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Restoring wetlands brings the water — and the habitat — back.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two losses that follow draining a wetland, Iceland's way.
**Answer:** The waders lose their habitat (the water rail nearly extinct, the phalarope rare), and peat ground stops binding carbon dioxide as it dries.
