# Wetlands — and why they must not be drained — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Draining wetlands badly shrank wader habitat in Iceland — the water rail is nearly extinct there, the red-necked phalarope rare.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Draining wetlands badly shrank wader habitat in Iceland — the water rail is nearly extinct there, the red-necked phalarope rare.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Restoring wetlands brings the water — and the habitat — back.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Restoring wetlands brings the water — and the habitat — back.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Peat bogs bind carbon dioxide, a second reason to keep wet ground wet.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Peat bogs bind carbon dioxide, a second reason to keep wet ground wet.**
