# Hands-on — Wetlands — and why they must not be drained

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wet or drained?
A partner describes pieces of land — a boggy breeding field, the same field drained and dry, a restored wetland with its water back. For each, say what the wading birds gain or lose.

## Count the cost
Write the wetland account in two columns: what draining gains (dry land) and what it costs (from the lesson). Read both columns aloud and say which is longer.
