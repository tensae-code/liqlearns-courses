# Hands-on — When people crowd — overpopulation and the strain on water and space

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: how people change the ecosystem, 2: overcrowding — people need places to live and work, and the more their numbers grow, the greater their need for the resources they consume, until getting space and water becomes hard; when very many people live in one limited area, overcrowding results, and it can come with the loss of some of the area's living things Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
