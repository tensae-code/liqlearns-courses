# Hands-on — How matter cycles through an ecosystem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: matter is recycled: water, nitrogen and carbon pass round the ecosystem again and again, so the gazelle's bones and muscles are built of material used before — as an aluminium can is melted and made again and again; the amount of matter on earth does not change Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
