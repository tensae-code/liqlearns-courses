# Hands-on — Mangroves — protecting coast and sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Meet a shore tree
Walk to the nearest shore, riverbank or swampy ground you know — or picture the closest one. Find a tree that stands in or beside the water. Name its parts out loud — roots, trunk, branches, leaves — and say what each part does in that wet ground.

## The dependence list
At home, write two lists from the lesson. Food and market: the things people take from mangroves, or that live in mangroves and are caught and sold. Animals: the saltwater crocodile, the mangrove crab and the night heron. Beside each animal, write one line on how it uses the mangrove.

## Draw the mangrove
Draw a mangrove tree: the water line, the mud, the roots in the mud, the trunk, and the branches and leaves above. Label every part. Then draw one animal in its place — the crab among the roots, the heron in the branches, the crocodile at the water's edge — and label it with its name.
