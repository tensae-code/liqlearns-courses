# Mangroves — protecting coast and sea — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A mangrove stands in salt water and soft _____ at the coast.
**Answer: mud**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The mangrove's _____ hold it standing in the mud.
**Answer: roots**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The saltwater _____ hunts in the mangrove's waters.
**Answer: crocodile**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Palauans' food and _____ life depends on the mangroves.
**Answer: market**
