# Mangroves — protecting coast and sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the mangrove's parts, in order from the ground up.
**Answer: Roots in the mud, then the trunk rising out of the water, then the branches and leaves in the air above.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do a mangrove's parts fit it for the coast?
**Answer: Its roots hold it in soft, salty mud where an ordinary tree would fall, while its branches and leaves stand in the air to catch the sun — one tree joined between the sea below and the air above.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two animals of the mangroves and say how each uses them.
**Answer: Any two of: the saltwater crocodile, which hunts in the mangrove's waters; the mangrove crab, which lives and feeds among the roots and mud; the night heron, which comes to the mangroves to feed.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does cutting mangroves hurt a village, not only the trees?
**Answer: Because people's food and market life depends on the mangroves and the creatures they shelter — remove the mangroves and the coast loses the food and the animals together with the trees.**
