# Mangroves — protecting coast and sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A mangrove is…
a) Any tree planted in a garden  b) A tree that stands in salt water and mud where the land meets the sea  c) A kind of fish  d) A rock at the coast
**Answer: b) A tree that stands in salt water and mud where the land meets the sea**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A mangrove's roots…
a) Hold the tree standing in soft, salty mud  b) Drink only rain water  c) Grow fruit for the market  d) Keep the tide out of the sea
**Answer: a) Hold the tree standing in soft, salty mud**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Above the trunk, a mangrove's branches and leaves…
a) Stay under the water  b) Turn into roots  c) Stand in the air, where the tree catches the sun and makes its food  d) Fall off every tide
**Answer: c) Stand in the air, where the tree catches the sun and makes its food**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Palauans depend on the mangroves for…
a) Mountain climbing  b) Food and market life  c) Snow and ice  d) Desert farming
**Answer: b) Food and market life**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these animals makes the mangroves its home ground?
a) The snow leopard  b) The desert fox  c) The mountain goat  d) The mangrove crab
**Answer: d) The mangrove crab**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The night heron…
a) Comes to the mangroves to feed  b) Builds the mangrove tree  c) Drinks the sea dry  d) Lives only in rivers
**Answer: a) Comes to the mangroves to feed**
