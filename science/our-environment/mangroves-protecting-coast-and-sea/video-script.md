Today: **Mangroves — protecting coast and sea** — Palau's way: start from the tree itself, and finish with the life that depends on it.

A mangrove is a tree that stands where the land meets the sea, in salt water and soft mud. Its parts fit that hard home: roots hold it in the mud, and trunk, branches and leaves rise above the water.
And the mangrove is not empty ground. Palauans take food from the mangroves and sell what the mangroves shelter at the market. A saltwater crocodile hunts there, a mangrove crab feeds there, and a night heron comes by night. Cut the mangrove, and the coast loses the tree, the food and the animals together.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
