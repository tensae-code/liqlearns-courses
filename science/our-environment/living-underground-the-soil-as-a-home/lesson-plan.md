# Lesson plan — Living underground — the soil as a home

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Living underground — the soil as a home
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** living underground — the soil as a home: the underground living environment, taught as a living environment of its own — Moldova's own way, in its Grade 3 Științe (Sciences) book, inside the living-environments module, between the land-and-air environment and the water environment
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The underground living environment** — A living environment can be below the ground: the soil itself is a home, and the living things of the underground live their lives in it, out of sight under the surface we walk on.
2. **One environment among the others** — Moldova's Grade 3 module teaches the living environments one by one — the land-and-air environment, then the underground, then the water environment, then our own human environments. The underground is counted as a home of its own because what lives there lives in the soil, not on the land, in the air or in the water.
