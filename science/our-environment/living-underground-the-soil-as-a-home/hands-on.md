# Hands-on — Living underground — the soil as a home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Look at the ground as a home
Choose a small patch of ground at school or at home. Look at its surface first: what lives on it? Then look at its edge — a bank, a dug corner, a place where the soil shows. What could live inside that soil, out of sight?

## Draw the underground home
Draw your patch twice: once from above, as we see it, and once as a cut through the ground, showing the soil below the surface as a home with room for living things in it. Label the surface, the soil, and the underground home.

## Sort the homes
List the living environments from the lesson — land-and-air, underground, water, and the human environments. For one living thing you know, say which home it belongs to and why the underground is or is not its home.
