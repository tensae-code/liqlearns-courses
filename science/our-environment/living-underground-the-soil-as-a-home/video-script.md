Today: **Living underground — the soil as a home**.

Look at the ground under your feet. A living environment can be down there — the soil itself is a home. Living things live their lives underground, out of sight, in the earth below the surface.
And it is one home among others. Moldova's Grade 3 book teaches the living environments one by one: the land-and-air home, the underground home, the water home, and our own human homes. Each is a home of its own, because what lives there lives in its own way.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
