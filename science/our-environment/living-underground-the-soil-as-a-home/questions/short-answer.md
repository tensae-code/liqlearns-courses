# Living underground — the soil as a home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a living environment, and what makes the underground one underground?
**Answer:** A living environment is the place where a living thing lives — its home in nature. The underground environment is the soil itself as a home: its living things live below the surface, in the earth.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the living environments in the order Moldova's Grade 3 module teaches them.
**Answer:** The land-and-air living environment, then the underground living environment, then the water living environment, then the human being's own living environments.
