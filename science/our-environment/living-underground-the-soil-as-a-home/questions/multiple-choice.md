# Living underground — the soil as a home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The underground living environment is…
a) The air above the ground  b) The soil itself, below the surface, as a home  c) A river  d) A house people build
**Answer: b) The soil itself, below the surface, as a home**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The living things of the underground live…
a) Only on the surface  b) In the earth, out of sight under the ground we walk on  c) Only in water  d) Only in the air
**Answer: b) In the earth, out of sight under the ground we walk on**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In Moldova's Grade 3 module, the underground lesson comes…
a) Before every other environment  b) After the land-and-air environment and before the water environment  c) After the human environments  d) It is not in the module
**Answer: b) After the land-and-air environment and before the water environment**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The underground counts as a home of its own because…
a) It is the biggest home  b) What lives there lives in the soil, not on the land, in the air or in the water  c) Nothing lives there  d) It is a human environment
**Answer: b) What lives there lives in the soil, not on the land, in the air or in the water**
