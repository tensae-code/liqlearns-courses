# Types of environments — land, water and built — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Land, water and built — the three sorts of surroundings.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 textbook sorts the environment around us into types: environments on **land**, environments in **water**, and **built** surroundings — the places people have made. Every living thing lives in one of these types, and the type tells you what that place gives it and what it demands of it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Papua New Guinea's own natural environments.
**Answer:** The Standards Based Grade 3 guide names Papua New Guinea's own natural environments: **rainforest, grassland and rivers**. The rainforest is land thick with trees, the grassland is open land of grasses, and the rivers are the country's great water environments — the guide teaches the types through the country's own places first.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Built environments — the surroundings people make.
**Answer:** A **built** environment is a surroundings made by people — houses, schools, gardens, roads and villages. It is still an environment: living things live in it, and it is taught in the same sorting as land and water, not as something outside nature.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Land, water and built — the three sorts of surroundings → Papua New Guinea's own natural environments → Built environments — the surroundings people make — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
