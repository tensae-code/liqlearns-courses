Today: **Types of environments — land, water and built** — Papua New Guinea's way, from its Department of Education science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

Papua New Guinea's Grade 3 textbook sorts the environment around us into types: environments on **land**, environments in **water**, and **built** surroundings — the places people have made. Every living thing lives in one of these types, and the type tells you what that place gives it and what it demands of it.

The Standards Based Grade 3 guide names Papua New Guinea's own natural environments: **rainforest, grassland and rivers**. The rainforest is land thick with trees, the grassland is open land of grasses, and the rivers are the country's great water environments — the guide teaches the types through the country's own places first.

A **built** environment is a surroundings made by people — houses, schools, gardens, roads and villages. It is still an environment: living things live in it, and it is taught in the same sorting as land and water, not as something outside nature.
