| the emerald hummingbird (colibrí esmeralda): an endemic bird of Honduras, found only in our country; like the majority of hummingbirds its colour depends on the light conditions of the environment where it develops; the emerald hummingbird presents bluish shades (matices azulados) in the neck and throat, but an emerald tone in the back, and is unmistakable by the chest and white belly (guide evidence) (Honduras — CN8 Guide) | Honduras, *CN8 Guide*, guide PDF p. 153 |
| the reading's answers: the emerald hummingbird is characterised by having blue shades in the neck and an emerald tone in the back; it inhabits in the dry forests and tropical or subtropical scrublands (matorrales), this species being common in the Valley of Agalta (Valle del Agalta), or in Santa Bárbara in the basins of the Ulúa river; it is found in danger of extinction by the destruction of its habitat and the deforestation caused by the human being; its scientific name is Amazilia luciae; it is an endemic species because it is only found in Honduras and in no other country; the principal objective of the Wildlife Refuge Emerald Hummingbird (Refugio de Vida Silvestre Colibrí Esmeralda) is to protect the Emerald Hummingbird and its habitat; other species that can be found in the refuge: endemic species of plants and orchids, as well as hundreds of migratory birds and reptiles; and the types of ecosystems found in Honduras: rainforest (selva lluviosa), mangrove forest, savannas, freshwater or salted ecosystems, among others (guide evidence) (Honduras — CN8 Guide) | Honduras, *CN8 Guide*, guide PDF p. 153 |
