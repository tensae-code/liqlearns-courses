# Hands-on — Exotic species and endemic species

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Exotic fauna defined. exotic fauna defined: species of organisms that are not native to a region; they can alter the ecosystem's balance and displace native fauna; endemic species are those proper to a determined geographic zone; a species is the set of population members able to reproduce among themselves with fertile offspring (the ocelot's genus is Leopardus) (guide evidence) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
