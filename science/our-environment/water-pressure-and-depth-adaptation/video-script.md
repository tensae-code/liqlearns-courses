Today: **Water pressure and depth adaptation** — Egypt's way, from its ministry books.

**Water pressure grows with depth.** **Pressure** in water comes from the weight of the water above. The deeper a place is, the more water stands over it, and the harder that water presses from every side. Near the surface the press is light; far down it is enormous. Egypt's Integrated Science places this inside its aquatic-environments sequence — after dissolved gases and the effect of light and solar radiation — as one of the physical conditions that divide the water world into depth zones, each with its own pressure.

**Living at different depths — adaptation to pressure.** Topic 1-9 teaches the **effect of water pressure on living organisms at different depths**: an organism's body is fitted to the pressure of the depth it lives at, and that fitting is part of its adaptation to its environment — the same adaptation theme Egypt's unit opens with, for the aquatic environment as a whole. Move an organism far from its own depth and the pressure change itself becomes the danger: depth is not only a place, it is a pressure the body is built for.
