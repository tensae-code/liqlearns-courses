# Hands-on — Water pressure and depth adaptation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel pressure grow
Punch three holes at different heights in a plastic bottle, fill it with water, and watch which jet travels farthest. Say what the lowest jet proves about pressure and depth.

## Zone a water world
Draw a deep lake or sea in bands. For each band, write its pressure (light, medium, crushing) and one way a body living there must be fitted to it.
