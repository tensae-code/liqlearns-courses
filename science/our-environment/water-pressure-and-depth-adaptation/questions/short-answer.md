# Water pressure and depth adaptation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Water pressure grows with depth.
**Answer:** **Pressure** in water comes from the weight of the water above.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Living at different depths — adaptation to pressure.
**Answer:** Topic 1-9 teaches the **effect of water pressure on living organisms at different depths**: an organism's body is fitted to the pressure of the depth it lives at, and that fitting is part of its adaptation to its environment — the same adaptation theme Egypt's unit opens with, for the aquatic environment as a whole.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Water pressure grows with depth → Living at different depths — adaptation to pressure — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
