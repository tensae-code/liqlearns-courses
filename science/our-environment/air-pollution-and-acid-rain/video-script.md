# Video script — Air pollution and acid rain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

We breathe the air — so what rides in it rides into **us**.
Tiny specks, **particulates**, can lodge deep in the **lungs**.
Two gases rise with the fumes.
They meet **water vapour**. They fall as **acid rain**.
It strips the soil, blocks the leaves, sours the lakes, **eats the buildings**.
The damage lands far. It **starts** at the fume.
