# Air pollution and acid rain — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Only factories pollute the air; fires and vehicles do not.
**Answer: False** — Fumes from factories, vehicles and fires all carry the pollution load.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Acid rain can harm a lake it falls into.
**Answer: True** — It harms lake life — fish and the small living things of the water.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Acid rain adds nutrients to soil and helps crops.
**Answer: False** — It STRIPS nutrients from soil.
