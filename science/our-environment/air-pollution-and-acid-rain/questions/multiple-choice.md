# Air pollution and acid rain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Particulates are dangerous when breathed because they —
a) are too big to breathe  b) can lodge in the lungs  c) are pure oxygen  d) clean the air
**Answer: b) can lodge in the lungs** — tiny solid specks from fumes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Air pollution means —
a) air moving fast  b) harmful matter loaded into the air  c) air at night  d) mountain air
**Answer: b) harmful matter loaded into the air**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The two gases named as acid rain's makers are —
a) oxygen and nitrogen gas  b) sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides  c) carbon dioxide and water  d) hydrogen and helium
**Answer: b) sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Acid rain forms when those gases join —
a) soil  b) water vapour in the air  c) sunlight  d) salt
**Answer: b) water vapour in the air** — and fall with rain as a weak acid.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Acid rain harms a plant by —
a) feeding its roots  b) blocking leaf stomata and degrading chlorophyll  c) making it taller  d) colouring its flowers
**Answer: b) blocking leaf stomata and degrading chlorophyll**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Acid rain's harm to buildings is that it —
a) paints them  b) slowly eats/dissolves stone and metal surfaces  c) strengthens walls  d) cools rooms
**Answer: b) slowly eats/dissolves stone and metal surfaces** — it also strips soil nutrients and harms lake life.
