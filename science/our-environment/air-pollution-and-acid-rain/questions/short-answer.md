# Air pollution and acid rain — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can polluted air harm people who never go near a factory?
**Answer:** Pollution travels in the air and is breathed wherever it reaches; particulates lodge in lungs, and pollutant gases return in acid rain far from their source.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trace the acid-rain chain in four steps.
**Answer:** Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides rise in fumes → they join water vapour in air → they fall as acid rain → damage to soil, leaves, lakes and buildings.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three of acid rain's five harms.
**Answer:** Any three of: strips soil nutrients; blocks leaf stomata; degrades chlorophyll; harms lake life; eats buildings.
