# Hands-on — Air pollution and acid rain

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Building inspection
Look at an older stone, concrete or metal surface outdoors — a wall, a roof, a monument if one is near. Note stains, pitting or crumbling. Write which part of it acid rain could have caused, and what else could have caused it — a scientist names rivals before concluding.

## Chain in drawings
Draw the acid-rain chain in five boxes with arrows: FUMES (sulphur dioxide + nitrogen oxides) → JOIN WATER VAPOUR → ACID RAIN → then three damage boxes of your choice. Present it to a companion, who must ask 'and then?' at every arrow.
