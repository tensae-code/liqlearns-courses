# Lesson plan — Air pollution and acid rain

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Air pollution and acid rain
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ecosystem and Conservation of Natural Resources, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** air pollution loads the air with harmful matter — including particulates small enough to lodge in lungs — and two pollutant gases, sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides, join water vapour in the air and fall as acid rain, which strips soil nutrients, blocks leaf stomata, degrades chlorophyll, harms lake life and eats buildings.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Pollution and its four kinds (this title), where air pollution was named among the four; this Grade 8 lesson is air alone, in depth: the lung harm and the full acid-rain chain from fume to damaged field, lake and building.

Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Polluted air — and what enters the lungs** — air's pollution load, with particulates that lodge in lungs.
2. **Acid rain — from fume to damage** — the gases join water vapour, fall as acid rain, and damage soil, leaves, lakes and buildings.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Pollution and its four kinds (Our Environment) — recommended, never forced.
## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what air pollution is, and what particulates do in lungs.
2. Trace acid rain's chain: gases, water vapour, acid rain, damage.
3. Name acid rain's five harms taught in this lesson.
## What you need
A place where fumes can be smelled safely from a distance — a road, a cooking fire — to think from; a stone or metal surface outdoors to inspect.
