# Dry rubbish in its place — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Who does the book send pupils to ask about choosing a disposal site?**
**Answer: a health worker**

**2. What two things happen when waste is not disposed of properly?**
**Answer: the home and neighbourhood become dirty, and people fall sick**
**3. Name the five ways of disposing of waste safely.**
**Answer: reuse it; feed it to small animals; rot it (composting); burn it in a pit or barrel; bury it in a waste pit**
