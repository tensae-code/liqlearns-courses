# Dry rubbish in its place — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Dry waste in the book includes —**
a) peelings, paper and plastic  b) only glass  c) smoke
**Answer: a) peelings, paper and plastic**

**2. The book's two-step road for waste is —**
a) drop it, forget it  b) container, then a proper disposal place  c) burn it anywhere
**Answer: b) container, then a proper disposal place**

**3. Onion peelings are —**
a) liquid waste  b) dry waste  c) not waste
**Answer: b) dry waste**

**4. Who is asked how a disposal site is chosen?**
a) a health worker  b) a driver  c) a shopkeeper
**Answer: a) a health worker**
**5. Proper disposal means removing waste in a way that —**
a) harms no living thing and is useful  b) is fastest  c) hides it from sight
**Answer: a) harms no living thing and is useful**

**6. The three storage kinds at the source are —**
a) usable at once, can rot, cannot rot  b) wet, dry, smelly  c) mine, yours, theirs
**Answer: a) usable at once, can rot, cannot rot**

**7. Burning waste the book's way is —**
a) on open ground anywhere  b) in a pit or barrel, not on open ground  c) never allowed in any form
**Answer: b) in a pit or barrel, not on open ground**
