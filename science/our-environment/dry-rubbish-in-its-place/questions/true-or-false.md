# Dry rubbish in its place — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. Waste left in the home and neighbourhood makes people fall sick.**
**Answer: True**

**2. Paper and plastic belong in the waste container, not on the ground.**
**Answer: True**
**3. Rotting waste — leftover food, dung, fallen leaves — becomes compost that can even be sold.**
**Answer: True**

**4. The book's safety note says to sort waste without caring about harm to yourself.**
**Answer: False**
