# Hands-on — Dry rubbish in its place

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The container audit
Does your home have a waste container where waste is actually made — kitchen and eating place? If not, make one from a reused box or tin: reuse serving disposal.

## Disposal-site sketch
Draw your area: homes, water, the disposal place. Judge the distances as a health worker would, and say what you would move.

## One-day weigh-in
Collect one day of household dry waste in the container. Estimate its bulk in baskets. That is one day; multiply by the street, by the year — the system's importance shows itself.


## The school waste-site project (the Grade 4 book's own)
Interview a health or environment expert on scientific disposal. Then, with the head teacher, build the model school waste-disposal place, run it on the weekly two-pupil rota for a month, keep the rota sheet, and report at month's end. Finish by teaching the method at home — the project is not done until it has been taught.
