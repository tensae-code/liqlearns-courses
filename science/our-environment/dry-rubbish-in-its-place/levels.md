# Dry rubbish in its place — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: name the dry wastes, walk a peel through both steps, and report your compound's real system
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 4 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also runs the three-kind sorting (usable at once / can rot / cannot rot) with its safety note, names the five safe disposal ways, and can tell the school waste-site project's whole course: interview an expert, build with the head teacher, a weekly two-pupil rota for a month, a report, and teaching the method at home.
