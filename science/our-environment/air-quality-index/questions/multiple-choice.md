# The air quality index — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The air quality index is…
a) A list of factories  b) A published grade of the air's quality on one scale  c) A kind of wind  d) A traffic rule
**Answer: b) A published grade of the air's quality on one scale**

**2.** A high air quality index warns that…
a) Rain is coming  b) The air itself has become a health matter  c) The city is quiet  d) Dust has settled
**Answer: b) The air itself has become a health matter**

**3.** Electromagnetic pollution comes from…
a) Smoke  b) Stronger fields devices create around themselves  c) Dust storms  d) River water
**Answer: b) Stronger fields devices create around themselves**

**4.** Looks alone cannot judge air quality because…
a) Air is always clean  b) Some of what is in the air cannot be seen or smelled and must be measured  c) Eyes are weak  d) The index is secret
**Answer: b) Some of what is in the air cannot be seen or smelled and must be measured**
