# The air quality index — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What does an air quality index do that looking at the sky cannot?
**Answer: It measures the pollutants in the air and publishes them as one grade everyone reads the same way; looking cannot measure invisible pollutants at all.**

**2.** Why does Iran's book put electromagnetic pollution in its air lesson?
**Answer: Because the lesson is about what affects the air around us, seen and unseen — devices' stronger fields are a pollution of the air with no smoke or smell, known only by measuring.**
