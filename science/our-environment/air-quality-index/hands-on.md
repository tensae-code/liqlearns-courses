# Hands-on — The air quality index

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the index for a week
Each morning for a week, note the published air quality index and the weather beside it. At the week's end, say whether the index followed the weather, the traffic, or neither — and what a person with asthma should do on your worst day.

## Seen and unseen
Make two lists of what can dirty the air around your home: what you can see or smell, and what you cannot. For each unseen item, write how people found out it was there.
