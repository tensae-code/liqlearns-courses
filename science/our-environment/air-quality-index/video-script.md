Today: **The air quality index** — Iran's way.

Iran's Grade 11 book puts the air quality index at the centre of its air lesson: human breathing and life are affected by air quality, so quality is graded and published as an index — a single scale that gathers the air's pollutants into a grade everyone can read, the way a thermometer gathers warmth into a number. The same lesson reaches a pollution with no smoke and no smell: devices create stronger electromagnetic fields around themselves, and the book names the result electromagnetic pollution.
