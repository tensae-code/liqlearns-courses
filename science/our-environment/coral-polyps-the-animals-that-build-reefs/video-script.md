Today: **Coral polyps — the animals that build reefs** — Palau's way: the reef is built by animals.

A reef looks like rock. It is not rock. It is built by tiny animals called coral polyps. Each polyp reaches out with little tentacles and catches microscopic animals floating past in the water — that is how a polyp eats.
Millions of polyps, joined together, build the reef. And reefs come in kinds — among them the atoll, a ring of reef in the open sea. Palau's own example is the Helen Reef atoll. Small animals, patiently building: that is what a reef is.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
