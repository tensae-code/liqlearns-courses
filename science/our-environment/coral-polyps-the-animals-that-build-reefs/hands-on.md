# Hands-on — Coral polyps — the animals that build reefs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Be the polyp
Hold one hand still like a polyp fixed to the reef, fingers up as tentacles. With the other hand, drift small scraps of paper past as microscopic floating animals. Wave the "tentacles" and catch a scrap. Say what the polyp is doing, and what the scraps stand for.

## Many builders make one reef
Take a handful of small stones or shells. One stone is one polyp's work — almost nothing. Now build the whole handful into one ring or mound together with a friend, each adding a piece at a time. Say how the reef grows the same way: many small animals, joined, over a long time.

## Draw the reef kinds
Draw two reefs: a reef along a coast, and an atoll — a ring of reef in the open sea with water inside the ring. Label the atoll with Palau's example: the Helen Reef atoll. Label each drawing with the sub lesson it comes from.
