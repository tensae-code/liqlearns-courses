# Coral polyps — the animals that build reefs — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A coral polyp is a tiny _____.
**Answer: animal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The polyp catches food with its _____.
**Answer: tentacles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Polyps joined together build the _____.
**Answer: reef**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An _____ is a ring of reef in the open sea.
**Answer: atoll**
