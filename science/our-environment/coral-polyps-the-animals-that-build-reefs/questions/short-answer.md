# Coral polyps — the animals that build reefs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How does a coral polyp get its food?
**Answer: It reaches out with its tentacles and catches microscopic animals floating past in the water — it eats by catching, as animals do.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is coral not a rock or a plant?
**Answer: Because the polyp is a living animal that catches and eats food with its tentacles; the hard reef is what the joined polyps build, not the animal itself.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does something as small as a polyp build something as big as a reef?
**Answer: Not alone — polyps joined together, in great numbers, build the reef between them, each adding its small part to the shared structure.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What is an atoll, and which one does Palau's book name?
**Answer: An atoll is a ring of reef standing in the open sea; Palau's named example is the Helen Reef atoll.**
