# Coral polyps — the animals that build reefs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A coral polyp is…
a) A kind of rock  b) A tiny animal  c) A sea plant  d) A small fish
**Answer: b) A tiny animal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A polyp catches its food with…
a) Its tentacles  b) A net it builds from sand  c) Its roots  d) Moonlight
**Answer: a) Its tentacles**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The polyp's food is…
a) Seagrass leaves  b) Sand and mud  c) Microscopic animals floating in the water  d) Sunlight alone
**Answer: c) Microscopic animals floating in the water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A reef is built by…
a) One polyp working alone  b) Polyps joined together  c) Waves piling up sand  d) Fish stacking stones
**Answer: b) Polyps joined together**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) An atoll is…
a) A single coral polyp  b) A river mouth  c) A mangrove forest  d) A ring of reef in the open sea
**Answer: d) A ring of reef in the open sea**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Palau's named atoll example is…
a) The Helen Reef atoll  b) The Great Barrier Reef  c) A freshwater lake  d) The Ngerikiil watershed
**Answer: a) The Helen Reef atoll**
