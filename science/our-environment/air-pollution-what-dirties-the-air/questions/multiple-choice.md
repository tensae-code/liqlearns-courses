# Air pollution — what dirties the air — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Air pollution means…
a) Air that is cold  b) Harmful things mixed into the air — smoke, fumes, dust and gases  c) Air that moves fast  d) Any smell at all
**Answer: b) Harmful things mixed into the air — smoke, fumes, dust and gases**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The biggest family of air pollution is…
a) Dust from leaves  b) Smoke from burning — fires, rubbish, charcoal and engines  c) Rain vapour  d) Sea spray
**Answer: b) Smoke from burning — fires, rubbish, charcoal and engines**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Exhaust fumes come from…
a) Trees  b) Vehicles, generators and factory chimneys  c) Rivers  d) Clouds
**Answer: b) Vehicles, generators and factory chimneys**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Dirty air hurts the body by…
a) Making bones grow  b) Stinging eyes and hurting lungs, worsening coughs and asthma  c) Improving breathing  d) Cooling the skin
**Answer: b) Stinging eyes and hurting lungs, worsening coughs and asthma**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The right way to deal with household rubbish, for the air's sake, is…
a) Burn it in the yard  b) Bury or bin it — never burn it in the open  c) Throw it in the road  d) Burn it at night
**Answer: b) Bury or bin it — never burn it in the open**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Trees help clean the air because…
a) They hide the smoke  b) Their leaves trap dust, and they refresh the air  c) They burn fumes  d) They stop all wind
**Answer: b) Their leaves trap dust, and they refresh the air**
