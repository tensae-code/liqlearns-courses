# Air pollution — what dirties the air — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three families of air dirt, with one example of each.
**Answer: Smoke (cooking fires, burned rubbish, charcoal), fumes (vehicle exhausts, generators, factory chimneys), and dust (dry roads and bare ground lifted by wind and traffic).**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where does the grey smear on the wiped cloth come from?
**Answer: It is dust and soot that was floating in the air and settled on the sill or leaf — a sample of the pollution everyone nearby has been breathing.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is faded, invisible smoke still pollution?
**Answer: The smoke has not disappeared — it has spread thinner into a larger volume of air, sharing its harmful specks with the whole neighbourhood.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Give two ways a family can dirty the air less, starting today.
**Answer: Any two: stop burning rubbish (bury or bin it); cook so smoke escapes away from faces and use dry, less-smoky fuel; keep engines maintained so they fume less; plant and protect trees.**
