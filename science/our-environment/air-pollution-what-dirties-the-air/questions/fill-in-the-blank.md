# Air pollution — what dirties the air — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Smoke, fumes and dust mixed into air are called air _____.
**Answer: pollution**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The sharp-smelling gases from engines are called _____.
**Answer: fumes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Air is kept clean best at its _____ — where the dirt starts.
**Answer: source**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Plant leaves trap _____, which is one way trees clean air.
**Answer: dust**
