# Air pollution — what dirties the air — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner names the three families of air pollution — smoke, fumes, dust — with a real local example of each, explains what dirty air does to lungs, eyes and plants, and gives source-level remedies (never burn rubbish, manage cooking smoke, maintain engines, plant trees), answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 2 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — doing the lesson's own experiments and sorts by hand, naming each idea aloud, and reasoning about new examples until the teaching is the learner's own.
