# Hands-on — Air pollution — what dirties the air

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-leaf test
Wipe white cloths across leaves at two places — beside a busy road and in a quiet green place. Compare the grey each cloth picks up. Say aloud: the cloth collected what the air was carrying — and what our lungs meet.

## Smoke watch
At a safe distance, watch a cooking fire's smoke: which way does it travel, how far before it fades, and who breathes it on its way? Write the smoke's journey. Say aloud: smoke does not disappear — it spreads into everybody's air.
