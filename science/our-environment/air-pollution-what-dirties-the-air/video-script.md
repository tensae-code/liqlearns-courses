# Video script — Air pollution — what dirties the air

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Air pollution**.

Look at a white cloth wiped across a windowsill: grey. That grey was floating in the air — your air. Air pollution has three families. Smoke, from fires and burning rubbish — the biggest of all. Fumes, from engines and generators. And dust, lifted from dry bare roads. Dirty air stings eyes, hurts lungs, and coats the leaves of plants. But here is the hopeful part: people make dirty air, so people can stop — never burn rubbish, let cooking smoke escape away from faces, and plant trees, the air-cleaners that never stop working.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
