# Environmental factors — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about environmental factors and their influence on ecosystems?
**Answer: Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "Environmental factors and their influence on ecosystems".**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about factors influencing the life of an ecosystem?
**Answer: The books teach this part as "Factors influencing the life of an ecosystem" (with the practical work on water conditions for seed germination) — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
