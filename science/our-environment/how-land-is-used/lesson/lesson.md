## Sub lesson 2 — Land for farming and for work

The ECD B guide continues with **agriculture**, the Grade 5 guide teaches **commercial land use**, and every primary guide in the set carries a land use unit: land is divided by the work done on it — farming, living, trading.

## Sub lesson 3 — Natural land

The Grade 6 guide's land use unit is **natural land**: land left as it is. The run completes the picture — land used by people, and land kept natural.

## The key ideas

- **Land use — where people live** — land where people live.
- **Land for farming and for work** — land divided by the work done on it.
- **Natural land** — land kept natural.
