# How land is used — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Land use — where people live" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's ECD B guide opens land use with **residential** land: the land where people live.

**2.** What does the part "Land for farming and for work" teach?
**Answer:** The ECD B guide continues with **agriculture**, the Grade 5 guide teaches **commercial land use**, and every primary guide in the set carries a land use unit: land is divided by the work done on it — farming, living, trading.

**3.** What does the part "Natural land" teach?
**Answer:** The Grade 6 guide's land use unit is **natural land**: land left as it is.
