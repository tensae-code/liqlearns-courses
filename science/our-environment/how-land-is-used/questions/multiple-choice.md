# How land is used — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Land for farming and for work  b) Natural land  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Land use — where people live
**Answer: d) Land use — where people live**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about land where people live?
a) Land use — where people live  b) Land for farming and for work  c) Natural land
**Answer: a) Land use — where people live**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about land divided by the work done on it?
a) Land use — where people live  b) Land for farming and for work  c) Natural land
**Answer: b) Land for farming and for work**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about land kept natural?
a) Land use — where people live  b) Land for farming and for work  c) Natural land
**Answer: c) Natural land**
