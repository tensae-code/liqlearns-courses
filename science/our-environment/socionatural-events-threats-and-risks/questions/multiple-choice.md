# Socionatural events — threats and risks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A socionatural event is…
a) only natural, never social  b) an event where nature and society meet, like a flood or earthquake  c) a festival  d) a kind of rain gauge  
**Answer: b) an event where nature and society meet, like a flood or earthquake**

**2.** Vulnerability means…
a) the dangerous event itself  b) how exposed and unprotected a community stands  c) the weather report  d) the damage after  
**Answer: b) how exposed and unprotected a community stands**

**3.** Risk is lowered by…
a) ignoring threats  b) building in flood paths  c) prevention — knowing threats and repairing vulnerability  d) waiting  
**Answer: c) prevention — knowing threats and repairing vulnerability**
