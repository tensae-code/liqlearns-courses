# Socionatural events — threats and risks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define threat, vulnerability and risk in one sentence each.
**Answer: Threat: the dangerous event that may come. Vulnerability: how exposed and unprotected a community stands before it. Risk: the expected harm when the threat meets that vulnerability.**

**2.** Why are floods and earthquakes called socionatural, not just natural?
**Answer: Because their harm depends on society too — where people live and build and how prepared they are — not on nature's force alone.**
