# Socionatural events — threats and risks

## Sub lesson 1 — Socionatural events

Some events are born where **nature and society meet**: **floods, landslides (slides) and earthquakes**. The book calls them **socionatural events** — natural in their force, social in where people live and build, and so in the harm they can do.

## Sub lesson 2 — Threat, vulnerability, risk

Three ideas read every such event. The **threat** is the dangerous event itself that may come. **Vulnerability** is how exposed and unprotected a community stands before it. **Risk** is the two joined: the harm that can be expected when a threat meets a vulnerable community.

## Sub lesson 3 — Prevention in the community

Risk is not fate: **prevention** lowers it. A community that knows its threats, repairs its vulnerability — where it builds, how it prepares — and practises what to do, faces the same flood or earthquake with less harm. Prevention is the lesson's closing work.
