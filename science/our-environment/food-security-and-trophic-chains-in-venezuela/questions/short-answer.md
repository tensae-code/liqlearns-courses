# Food security and trophic chains in Venezuela — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** How can a trophic chain be whole yet food security still fail?
**Answer: If the control of production and distribution shuts people out — food exists in the chain but does not reach them.**

**2.** Why does an agro-food book teach trophic chains inside food security?
**Answer: Because food is the chain made edible: security of food is security of the chains that produce it.**
