# Food security and trophic chains in Venezuela — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Food security depends first on…
a) restaurants  b) trophic chains holding  c) imports only  d) cooking  
**Answer: b) trophic chains holding**

**2.** The book's second question is who controls…
a) the weather  b) production and distribution  c) the recipes  d) the markets' names  
**Answer: b) production and distribution**

**3.** A trophic chain passes food energy from…
a) consumers to producers  b) producers to consumers  c) soil to sky  d) shops to farms  
**Answer: b) producers to consumers**
