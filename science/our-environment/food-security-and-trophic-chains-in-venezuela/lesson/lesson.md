# Food security and trophic chains in Venezuela

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's title — Food security and trophic chains in Venezuela. Say out loud anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on: this is Venezuela's way in — its Grade 7 *Alimentando con Ciencias*, an integrated book organised around the agro-food system, teaches food security as a trophic-chain question and a question of who controls production and distribution.

## Sub lesson 1 — Food security rides on trophic chains

Every meal stands at the end of a **trophic chain**: producers grow, consumers eat, and energy and matter pass along the chain. **Food security** — food reliably there for everyone — depends on those chains holding: break the chain in the field, and the table empties.

## Sub lesson 2 — Who controls production and distribution?

The book's second half is Venezuela's own integrated way: food security also depends on **who controls production and distribution** — who grows the food, who moves it, and who can reach it. A chain can be whole in nature and still fail people if its control shuts them out.
