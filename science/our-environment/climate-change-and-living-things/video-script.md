# Video script — Climate change and living things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Climate change and living things** — South Sudan's way, from its Ministry of General Education and Instruction Primary Science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

South Sudan's Primary 4 book teaches the effects of climate change on animals and plants as its own topic: plants **dry out and rot**, and the guide's answers add the other side — plants themselves **hold back climate change and raise biodiversity**.

For animals the book names **floods** and **drought forcing migration**: when drought brings lack of pasture and water, animals must move or die. The Primary 4 guide closes the topic with a class project — writing a presidential speech on climate change: the effects named, and a country's answer asked for.
