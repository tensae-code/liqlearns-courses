# Climate change and living things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What climate change does to plants.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 4 book teaches the effects of climate change on animals and plants as its own topic: plants **dry out and rot**, and the guide's answers add the other side — plants themselves **hold back climate change and raise biodiversity**.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: What climate change does to animals.
**Answer:** For animals the book names **floods** and **drought forcing migration**: when drought brings lack of pasture and water, animals must move or die. The Primary 4 guide closes the topic with a class project — writing a presidential speech on climate change: the effects named, and a country's answer asked for.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What climate change does to plants → What climate change does to animals — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
