# Video script — Energy resources — environmental degradation and its effects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

FULL SCRIPT — one felled forest, five effects, as placed (short script, teacher-evidence lesson).

[Opening — the hillside]
Here is a hillside where the forest stood. The trees are cut. Watch what follows, family by family — this is environmental degradation: the environment deteriorating, chiefly through the depletion you have already studied.

[Effect 1 — health]
The stream below runs muddy and fouled. Water like that brings illness — acute and chronic — to the families who drink it.

[Effect 2 — biodiversity]
The birds, the undergrowth, the soil life that lived in that forest are gone with it. Deforestation breaks the water cycle too — the hill dries faster now.

[Effect 3 — climate]
Forests hold the climate steady. Cut them, and the pattern shifts: rains change their season, dry spells lengthen — the same family that shrinks glaciers and breaks ice earlier, far away.

[Effect 4 — water and air]
The gully deepens every rain, carrying the hillside into the stream. Water quality falls; dust and smoke foul the air.

[Effect 5 — disasters]
Bare ground cannot hold the rain. Floods and landslides — always possible here — come more often, and strike harder.

[Close]
One felled forest. Five families of effect. Degradation is not one harm — it is the whole web of them, and it begins with taking faster than things return.
