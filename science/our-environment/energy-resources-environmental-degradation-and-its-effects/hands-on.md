# Hands-on — Energy resources — environmental degradation and its effects

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The hillside inventory
Visit or picture a degraded local place — a cut slope, a fouled stream bank, a waste-strewn common. Inventory what is worse there than it should be, and sort every item into the five families. An item that fits no family is written at the page's foot, honestly unplaced.

## One forest, five chains
Take one felled forest — real or drawn. Draw five arrows from it, one per family, and write the chain on each arrow (trees cut → stream fouled → illness; trees cut → habitats gone → species lost…). Read the five chains aloud as one story.
