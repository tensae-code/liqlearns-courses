# Energy resources — environmental degradation and its effects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Environmental degradation is —
a) a single pollution event  b) the deterioration of the environment, chiefly through resource depletion  c) the same word as depletion  d) only deforestation
**Answer: b) the deterioration of the environment, chiefly through resource depletion** — the wider worsening that follows the draining

**2.** How many effect families are named?
a) three  b) four  c) five  d) seven
**Answer: c) five** — health to disasters

**3.** Earlier blooming and shifting species ranges belong to —
a) health  b) climate change  c) water quality  d) disasters
**Answer: b) climate change** — the climate family's signs

**4.** Floods and landslides under degradation grow —
a) rarer and weaker  b) more frequent and more intense  c) fully preventable everywhere  d) unrelated
**Answer: b) more frequent and more intense** — as the environment's capacity shrinks

**5.** Biodiversity loss is driven in this lesson chiefly by —
a) zoos  b) deforestation  c) rainfall  d) recycling
**Answer: b) deforestation** — habitats gone, water cycle broken
