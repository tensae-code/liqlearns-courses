# Energy resources — environmental degradation and its effects — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** Degradation follows chiefly through resource ______.
**Answer: depletion** — its chief cause

**11.** Floods and ______ grow more frequent and intense.
**Answer: landslides** — the disaster family

**12.** Deforestation breaks the ______ cycle.
**Answer: water** — named under biodiversity loss
