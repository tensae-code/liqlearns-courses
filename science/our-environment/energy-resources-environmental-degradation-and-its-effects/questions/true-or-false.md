# Energy resources — environmental degradation and its effects — true or false

Say true or false.

**6.** Depletion and degradation are exactly the same thing.
**Answer: False** — depletion drains a resource; degradation is the wider deterioration, chiefly through it

**7.** Degradation can make water undrinkable.
**Answer: True** — the water and air quality family

**8.** Wildlife loss can affect human diets.
**Answer: True** — named in the health family

**9.** This lesson rests on the student textbook.
**Answer: False** — teacher-guide evidence only — §7.9 has no student-book counterpart
