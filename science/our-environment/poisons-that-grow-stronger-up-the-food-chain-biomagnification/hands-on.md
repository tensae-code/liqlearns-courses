# Hands-on — Poisons that grow stronger up the food chain — biomagnification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — The DDT case answered. the DDT case answered: the phenomenon is denominated biomagnification (biomagnificación); the cause of the greatest increase of the DDT concentration is the use of DDT as insecticide in the crops; and the consequences that the accumulation of this contaminant can have: poisoning (envenenamiento), intoxication, congenital malformations and death (guide evidence) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
