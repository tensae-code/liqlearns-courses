# Lesson plan — Poisons that grow stronger up the food chain — biomagnification

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Poisons that grow stronger up the food chain — biomagnification
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (Our Environment) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the DDT case answered: the phenomenon is denominated biomagnification (biomagnificación); the cause of the greatest increase of the DDT concentration is the use of DDT as insecticide in the crops; and the consequences that the accumulation of this contaminant can have: poisoning (envenenamiento), intoxication, congenital malformations and death (guide evidence)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.
