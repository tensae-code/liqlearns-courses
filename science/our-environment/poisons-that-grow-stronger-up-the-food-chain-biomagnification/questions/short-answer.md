# Poisons that grow stronger up the food chain — biomagnification — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about the DDT case answered?
**Answer: the DDT case answered: the phenomenon is denominated biomagnification (biomagnificación); the cause of the greatest increase of the DDT concentration is the use of DDT as insecticide in the crops; and the consequences that the accumulation of this contaminant can have: poisoning (envenenamiento), intoxication, congenital malformations and death (guide evidence)**

**2.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Poisons that grow stronger up the food chain — biomagnification.**
