# How ecosystems change — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A fire is a _____ change to an ecosystem.
**Answer: sudden**

**2.** A pond becoming land over years is a _____ change.
**Answer: slow**

**3.** People can change ecosystems by planting and _____.
**Answer: protecting**

**4.** Living things that cannot fit a changed home may die _____.
**Answer: out**
