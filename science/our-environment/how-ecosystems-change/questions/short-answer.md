# How ecosystems change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name one slow and one sudden ecosystem change.
**Answer: Slow: a pond silting up into dry land. Sudden: a fire, flood, drought or storm remaking a place in a day.**

**2.** How do people change ecosystems?
**Answer: By cutting forests, building, draining wetlands and polluting — and in the good direction by planting, protecting and cleaning.**

**3.** What happens to living things when their ecosystem changes?
**Answer: They fit the new conditions, move away, or die out.**

**4.** Why does the lesson ask 'slow or sudden, and what started it?'
**Answer: Because the speed and the cause tell us what the change will do and whether people can guide it.**
