# How ecosystems change — multiple choice


**1.** A pond slowly filling with silt is an example of…
a) Sudden change  b) Slow change  c) No change  d) A machine
**Answer: b) Slow change**

**2.** Which change can remake an ecosystem in a day?
a) Soil forming  b) A flood  c) Trees spreading  d) Seeds resting
**Answer: b) A flood**

**3.** When an ecosystem changes, its living things must fit in, move, or…
a) Turn to stone  b) Die out  c) Grow wings at once  d) Stop eating
**Answer: b) Die out**

**4.** Which human action changes an ecosystem for the worse?
a) Planting trees  b) Protecting a wetland  c) Draining a wetland  d) Cleaning a shore
**Answer: c) Draining a wetland**

**5.** Ecosystem change can be started by nature or by…
a) People  b) Nobody  c) Books  d) Names
**Answer: a) People**

**6.** Knowing how ecosystems change helps people to…
a) Choose changes that keep life alive  b) Stop all change for ever  c) Ignore nature  d) Build anywhere
**Answer: a) Choose changes that keep life alive**
