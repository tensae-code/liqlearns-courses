Today: **Tabu — closed seasons protecting a place** — Vanuatu's way: *Putum tabu long envaeromen* — putting a tabu on the environment.

When a garden, a reef or a piece of bush has been used and used, its plants thin out and its animals grow few and small. So the community declares the place tabu — closed. Nobody harvests, fishes or cuts there. The place rests.
And resting works: plants grow back, animals breed, and their numbers and sizes grow. When the place is strong again, the tabu is lifted and the place gives again — and when it needs rest, the tabu goes back on.
A tabu is not closing a place for ever. It is closing it in time, so it can keep giving.
