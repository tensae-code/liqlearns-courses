# Tabu — closed seasons protecting a place — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Putting a place tabu means…
a) Selling the place  b) Declaring it closed, so nobody takes from it for a time  c) Cutting everything in it  d) Forgetting where it is
**Answer: b) Declaring it closed, so nobody takes from it for a time**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) While a place is tabu, people must not…
a) Walk past it  b) Look at it  c) Harvest, fish or cut there  d) Talk about it
**Answer: c) Harvest, fish or cut there**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A tabu is put on a place that…
a) Has never been used  b) Has grown tired from taking — plants thinned, animals few or small  c) Nobody can find  d) Belongs to another country
**Answer: b) Has grown tired from taking — plants thinned, animals few or small**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Inside the closed time, the place's plants and animals…
a) Disappear  b) Grow back, breed and grow in number and size  c) Stay exactly the same  d) Move to another island
**Answer: b) Grow back, breed and grow in number and size**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) When the place has recovered, the community…
a) Lifts the tabu, and the place gives again  b) Burns the place  c) Closes it for ever  d) Sells everything at once
**Answer: a) Lifts the tabu, and the place gives again**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Vanuatu's Year 3 guide teaches the tabu as…
a) A story only  b) A science lesson — a traditional conservation practice  c) A maths game  d) A foreign custom
**Answer: b) A science lesson — a traditional conservation practice**
