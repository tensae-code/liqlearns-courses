# Tabu — closed seasons protecting a place — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does a community do when it puts a place tabu?
**Answer:** It declares the place closed for a time, and everyone agrees that nobody will harvest, fish or cut there while the tabu stands.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is a tabu put on a place?
**Answer:** Because the place has grown tired from taking — its plants have thinned and its animals or fish have grown few or small — and it needs a break to recover.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens to living things inside the closed time?
**Answer:** Plants grow back and spread, and animals and fish breed and grow, so there are more of them and they are bigger, because nobody is taking from the place.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) How is a tabu different from closing a place for ever?
**Answer:** A tabu is lifted when the place has recovered, so the place gives again — and it can be put back when the place needs rest again.
