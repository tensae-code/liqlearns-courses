# Tabu — closed seasons protecting a place — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Declaring a place closed so nobody takes from it is putting it _____.
**Answer: tabu**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A tabu is put on a place that has grown _____ from taking.
**Answer: tired**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In the closed time, plants grow back and animals _____ and grow.
**Answer: breed**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) When the place has recovered, the community _____ the tabu.
**Answer: lifts**
