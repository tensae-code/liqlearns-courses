# Hands-on — Food chains, food webs and energy pyramids

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Build a real chain
From a garden, field edge or market, write one true food chain of four links found there. Then add one crossing link — something that eats, or is eaten by, a second partner — and see your chain become a web.

## Pyramid with stones
Put 100 stones in a row for the producers' energy. Above it put 10 for the herbivores, then 1 for the next level. Say where the other 90 stones' worth of energy went at each step.
