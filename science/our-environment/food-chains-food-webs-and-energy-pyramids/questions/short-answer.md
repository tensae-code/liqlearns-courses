# Food chains, food webs and energy pyramids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write a food chain of four links starting with grass, and label producer and each consumer.
**Answer:** E.g. grass (producer) → grasshopper (herbivore) → bird (carnivore) → cat (carnivore).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are there fewer big carnivores than herbivores in a place?
**Answer:** Energy shrinks by about 90% at each level, so the top levels have little energy to share — few large consumers can be supported.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three kinds of pyramid taught in this lesson.
**Answer:** Pyramids of numbers, of biomass and of energy.
