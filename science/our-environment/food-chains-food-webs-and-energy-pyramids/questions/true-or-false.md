# Food chains, food webs and energy pyramids — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Decomposers break down dead bodies and return their materials to the soil.
**Answer: True** — That return keeps the materials cycling.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An omnivore eats only animals.
**Answer: False** — An omnivore eats BOTH plants and animals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** All the energy at one trophic level passes to the next level.
**Answer: False** — Only about 10% passes; about 90% is used, lost as heat or left over.
