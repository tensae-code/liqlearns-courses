# Food chains, food webs and energy pyramids — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A producer is —
a) an animal that eats plants  b) a green plant that makes its own food  c) a fungus that rots wood  d) a carnivore
**Answer: b) a green plant that makes its own food** — every chain starts with producers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A goat is a herbivore because it —
a) eats only plants  b) eats only animals  c) eats both  d) makes its own food
**Answer: a) eats only plants**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Many food chains crossing and joining make —
a) a pyramid  b) a food web  c) a decomposer  d) a habitat
**Answer: b) a food web**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Producers hold 10,000 units of energy. About how much reaches the herbivores? 
a) 10,000  b) 1,000  c) 100  d) 10
**Answer: b) 1,000** — the 10% rule: a tenth passes up.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The level above the herbivores would get about —
a) 1,000 units  b) 100 units  c) 10,000 units  d) 5,000 units
**Answer: b) 100 units** — a tenth of 1,000.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The pyramid of energy always narrows upward because —
a) animals are bigger at the top  b) energy is lost as heat and used for living at each level  c) plants are few  d) the sun stops shining
**Answer: b) energy is lost as heat and used for living at each level**
