# Video script — Food chains, food webs and energy pyramids

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A plant **makes** food. It is a **producer**.
An animal **eats**. It is a **consumer**.
Plant-eaters, meat-eaters, eaters of both —
and **decomposers** clean up the dead.
Chain by chain, eating links into a **web**.
Up each level, only **ten in a hundred** of the energy passes.
The pyramid narrows. That is the rule.
