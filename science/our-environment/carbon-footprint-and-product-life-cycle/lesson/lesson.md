## Sub lesson 1 — The carbon footprint and the product life cycle

Malaysia's Form 5 book teaches the **product life cycle** and the **carbon footprint: the total carbon dioxide released by a person's activities, events, organisations or products** — the footprint is counted across the whole life of a thing, not only at its use.

## Sub lesson 2 — Preserving the environment — and the carbon handprint

Against the footprint the book sets **preserving and conserving the environment: negative-emission technology and green technology across sectors**, and coins the **carbon handprint — actions that cut others' footprints**, the helping counterpart to the footprint one leaves.
