# Lesson plan — Carbon footprint and product life cycle

**Course:** Science · **Title:** our-environment · **Lesson:** Carbon footprint and product life cycle
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the carbon footprint — the total carbon dioxide released by a person's activities, events, organisations or products, counted across a product's whole life cycle — and the carbon handprint that cuts others' footprints
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The carbon footprint and the product life cycle** — Malaysia's Form 5 book teaches the **product life cycle** and the **carbon footprint: the total carbon dioxide released by a person's activities, events, organisations or products** — the footprint is counted across the whole life of a thing, not only at its use.
2. **Preserving the environment — and the carbon handprint** — Against the footprint the book sets **preserving and conserving the environment: negative-emission technology and green technology across sectors**, and coins the **carbon handprint — actions that cut others' footprints**, the helping counterpart to the footprint one leaves.

## Before this lesson
- No required first lesson. Recommended, never forced: the neighbouring lessons that touch the same ground.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain sub lesson 1 in their own words: The carbon footprint and the product life cycle.
2. Explain sub lesson 2 in their own words: Preserving the environment — and the carbon handprint.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson text alone.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Thinks a footprint counts only travel → return to sub lesson 1: it is the total CO₂ from activities, events, organisations or products, across a product's whole life cycle.
- Mixes footprint and handprint up → return to sub lesson 2: the footprint is what you release; the handprint is what your actions cut from others' release.
