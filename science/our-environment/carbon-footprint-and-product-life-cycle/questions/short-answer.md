# Carbon footprint and product life cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The carbon footprint and the product life cycle.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Form 5 book teaches the **product life cycle** and the **carbon footprint: the total carbon dioxide released by a person's activities, events, organisations or products** — the footprint is counted across the whole life of a thing, not only at its use.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Preserving the environment — and the carbon handprint.
**Answer:** Against the footprint the book sets **preserving and conserving the environment: negative-emission technology and green technology across sectors**, and coins the **carbon handprint — actions that cut others' footprints**, the helping counterpart to the footprint one leaves.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The carbon footprint and the product life cycle → Preserving the environment — and the carbon handprint — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
