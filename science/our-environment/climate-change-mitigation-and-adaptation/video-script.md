Today: **Climate change — mitigation and adaptation** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 5 — *Elementary Science Class 5*, printed p. 93).

Two strategies answer climate change. Mitigation slows the change itself: burn less fossil fuel, use more solar and wind energy, plant trees, and save energy.
Adaptation lives with the change that comes anyway: flood and cyclone shelters, coastal afforestation, salt-tolerant crops, and stronger buildings — each one fitted to a named harm.
Bangladesh's Class 5 book makes the two strategies local: the country's own hazard-vulnerability map, and a month-by-month disaster calendar, so a learner can see which harm threatens which place, and when.
