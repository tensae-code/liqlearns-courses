# Measuring biodiversity — the Simpson index — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why is counting kinds alone not enough to compare biodiversity?
**Answer: Because balance matters too: a place ruled by one kind differs from a place where many kinds are well spread, and the index reads the two together.**

**2.** State the book's worked example and its verdict.
**Answer: Region A 0.78 against region B 0.54; region A is more diverse, its number standing nearer 1.**
