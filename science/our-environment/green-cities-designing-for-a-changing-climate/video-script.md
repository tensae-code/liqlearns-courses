Today: **Green cities — designing for a changing climate** — Egypt's way, from its ministry books.

**A city under a changing climate.** Egypt's Primary 6 book reaches this project through its climate unit: weather against climate, the water cycle under heat, measuring climate change, and soil and living things adapting as environments change. A **city** gathers all of those pressures in one place — heat collects between buildings, rain runs off hard surfaces, water and soil are shared by millions. Designing for a changing climate starts from naming what is changing and what the city must still do: shelter people, move water safely, and keep life possible inside it.

**Building an environmentally friendly city — the project.** The unit project is to **build an environmentally friendly city**: learners design a city that works with its environment instead of against it. The design joins the unit's science — shade and green ground against heat, ground that lets rain soak in, water saved and soil protected — into one plan, and the plan is judged as a whole city, not as single tricks. Egypt's own way is the order: measure the change first, then design the answer.
