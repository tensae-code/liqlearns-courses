# Hands-on — Green cities — designing for a changing climate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map your town's climate pressures
Draw your street or town. Mark where heat collects, where rain water runs or floods, and where soil and plants are. Those marks are your design brief.

## Design one district
Redraw one part of your map as an environmentally friendly district for a changing climate. Label every change with the climate pressure it answers.
