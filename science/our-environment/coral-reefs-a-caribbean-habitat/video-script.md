Today: **Coral reefs — a Caribbean habitat** — Barbados's way: the reef as a living place.

A coral reef is a habitat and an ecosystem — reef organisms living together under the sea, depending on each other. Barbados's syllabus teaches why the reef matters to the Caribbean, and the hard other side: how people destroy reefs. A living place, important to the island, and in the island's people's hands.
