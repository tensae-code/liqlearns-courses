# Hands-on — Coral reefs — a Caribbean habitat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the reef community
Draw or model a reef scene: corals below, reef animals among them. Label each living thing and draw one line showing something it needs from the reef — food, shelter or a place to live.

## The reef account
Two columns on paper: what the reef gives the island, and what people do that destroys reefs. Fill both from the lesson, then read the two columns aloud as one account — importance on one side, destruction on the other.
