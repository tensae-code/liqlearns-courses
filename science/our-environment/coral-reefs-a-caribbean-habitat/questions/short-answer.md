# Coral reefs — a Caribbean habitat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What makes the reef an ecosystem, not just a place?
**Answer: Its reef organisms live together as a community, depending on each other and on the water around them.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What three things does Barbados's syllabus teach about the reef?
**Answer: The reef organisms, the reef's importance to the Caribbean, and how people destroy reefs.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why pair importance with destruction in one lesson?
**Answer: Because a habitat this important to island life can still be wrecked by the people beside it — naming the destruction is the first step of caring for it.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) How is a reef habitat like a land habitat?
**Answer: Both are living places studied the same way: who lives there, and what each living thing needs.**
