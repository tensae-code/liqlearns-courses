# Coral reefs — a Caribbean habitat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A coral reef is…
a) Only a pile of rocks  b) A habitat and ecosystem of living reef organisms  c) A kind of fishing boat  d) A beach
**Answer: b) A habitat and ecosystem of living reef organisms**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A habitat is…
a) A season  b) The place where a living thing lives and finds what it needs  c) A net  d) A type of coral
**Answer: b) The place where a living thing lives and finds what it needs**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Barbados's syllabus asks learners to study…
a) Only the sand beside the reef  b) Reef organisms, the reef's importance to the Caribbean, and how people destroy reefs  c) Only imported fish  d) Ships and harbours only
**Answer: b) Reef organisms, the reef's importance to the Caribbean, and how people destroy reefs**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The reef's importance is taught as part of…
a) Mountain life  b) Caribbean island life  c) Desert life  d) River fishing only
**Answer: b) Caribbean island life**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Naming how people destroy reefs is…
a) Unnecessary  b) The first step of caring for the reef  c) A reason to avoid the sea  d) Only for scientists
**Answer: b) The first step of caring for the reef**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) An ecosystem is…
a) One animal alone  b) A community of living things depending on each other and their surroundings  c) A single shell  d) A wave
**Answer: b) A community of living things depending on each other and their surroundings**
