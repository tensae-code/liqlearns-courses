# Hands-on — Local knowledge, local conservation

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## The interview itself
Carry out the lesson's interview: permission, the three questions (water, forest, soil), exact recording. Bring back at least one practice for each of the three resources, or the teller's clear answer that none is kept for one of them.

## The evaluation table
Build a table with four columns: PRACTICE (as told) — UNIT METHOD IT STANDS BESIDE — VERDICT (matches / extends / differs) — EVIDENCE OR REASON. Complete one row per practice heard. Read your table to the companion who interviewed someone else, and defend any 'differs' verdict.
