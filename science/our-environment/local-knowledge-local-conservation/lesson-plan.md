# Lesson plan — Local knowledge, local conservation

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Local knowledge, local conservation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ecosystem and Conservation of Natural Resources, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the unit closes by turning to the people who have conserved a place longest: pupils interview local people on how they conserve water, forest and soil, then evaluate the indigenous practices they hear against the scientific methods of the unit — neither dismissed nor copied blindly, but tested.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole Grade 8 Unit 5 set in this title and in Soil and Water — every method the unit taught (terraces to seed banks) is the measuring set this lesson carries into the field. It closes the band's Unit 5 work in Our Environment.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Asking — the interview** — interviewing local people on how they conserve water, forest and soil.
2. **Testing — indigenous practice against scientific method** — evaluating what was heard against the unit's methods: match, extend, or differ — with reasons.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Biodiversity — keeping the variety of life (Our Environment)
- Recommended first: Saving the soil — conservation methods (Soil and Water) — recommended, never forced.
## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Plan and carry out an interview on local conservation of water, forest and soil.
2. Record an indigenous practice exactly as it was told.
3. Evaluate a practice against the unit's scientific methods and say what the verdict rests on.
## What you need
An elder or experienced farmer, gardener or water-keeper to interview (with permission), a notebook, and the unit's method lists.
