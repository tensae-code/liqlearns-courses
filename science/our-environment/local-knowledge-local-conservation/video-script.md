# Video script — Local knowledge, local conservation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The oldest knowledge of a place lives in its **people**.
Ask how they keep the **water**, the **forest**, the **soil**.
**Listen.** Write it exactly as it is told.
Then hold it beside the unit's methods.
Matches? Extends? Differs? Say **what your verdict rests on**.
Respect — and testing. Both.
