# Local knowledge, local conservation — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The interview comes first; the evaluation of what was heard comes after, with notes in hand.
**Answer: True** — Collect exactly, then test — in that order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Indigenous practices should be dismissed because they were not printed in a book.
**Answer: False** — Many are the unit's own methods found by long observation; the lesson tests, it does not dismiss.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where practice and method agree, that agreement is itself a finding worth recording.
**Answer: True** — A match verdict, with its reason, belongs in the table.
