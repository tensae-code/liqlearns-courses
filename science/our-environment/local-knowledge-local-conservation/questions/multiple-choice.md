# Local knowledge, local conservation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The interview's three resources are —
a) gold, cattle, roads  b) water, forest and soil  c) schools, markets, clinics  d) rain, wind, sun
**Answer: b) water, forest and soil** — the unit closes on those three.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When recording a practice, you should —
a) improve it as you write  b) record it exactly as told, with what it is said to achieve  c) write only your opinion of it  d) skip the teller's name for it
**Answer: b) record it exactly as told, with what it is said to achieve** — evaluation comes later, in its own step.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A good interviewer during the telling —
a) argues with each practice  b) listens quietly and records — the practice may come inside a story  c) finishes the teller's sentences  d) asks only yes/no questions
**Answer: b) listens quietly and records — the practice may come inside a story**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A stone line laid level across a slope by an elder stands beside which unit method? 
a) check dams in a river  b) contour holding of soil on a slope  c) ex-situ protection  d) acid rain
**Answer: b) contour holding of soil on a slope** — indigenous practice and scientific method can be the same method.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** If a practice and a unit method DIFFER, the scientific move is to —
a) dismiss the practice at once  b) copy the practice blindly  c) say what each rests on and what observation could settle it  d) ask the teller to change it
**Answer: c) say what each rests on and what observation could settle it** — respect plus testing.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A practice that adds local knowledge the unit does not carry is said to —
a) contradict the unit  b) extend the unit's methods  c) be ex-situ  d) be pollution
**Answer: b) extend the unit's methods**
