# Local knowledge, local conservation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the three interview questions in your own words.
**Answer:** How do you conserve water here? How do you conserve the forest/trees? How do you conserve the soil? (own wording, same three resources).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the three verdicts an evaluated practice can receive.
**Answer:** Matches (a unit method), extends (adds local knowledge), differs (with the evidence each side rests on).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does this lesson come LAST in the unit?
**Answer:** Only after learning the unit's scientific methods can pupils evaluate indigenous practices against them — the methods are the measuring set.
