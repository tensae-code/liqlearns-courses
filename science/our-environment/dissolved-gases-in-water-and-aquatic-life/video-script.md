Today: **Dissolved gases in water and aquatic life** — Egypt's way, from its ministry books.

**Gases from the air dissolve in water.** Water in contact with air takes air's gases into itself: atmospheric gases **dissolve** in water, the way salt dissolves, until the water holds them invisibly. Egypt's Integrated Science builds its water unit step by step — the hydrosphere and where Earth's water is, water's chemistry and solutions, its importance for living things, and organisms' adaptations to the aquatic environment — and then names this step on its own: the gases of the atmosphere are present inside the water, not only above it.

**What dissolved gases mean for aquatic environments.** Because gases dissolve in water, an **aquatic environment** carries its air with it — and what dissolves, and how much, becomes one of the conditions every water-living organism lives under. Topic 1-7 teaches the **effect of atmospheric gases dissolving in water on aquatic environments**: the water's store of dissolved gases is part of the habitat itself, as real a part as its temperature or its light — the topics that stand on either side of this one in Egypt's unit.
