# Dissolved gases in water and aquatic life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Gases from the air dissolve in water.
**Answer:** Water in contact with air takes air's gases into itself: atmospheric gases **dissolve** in water, the way salt dissolves, until the water holds them invisibly.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: What dissolved gases mean for aquatic environments.
**Answer:** Because gases dissolve in water, an **aquatic environment** carries its air with it — and what dissolves, and how much, becomes one of the conditions every water-living organism lives under.
