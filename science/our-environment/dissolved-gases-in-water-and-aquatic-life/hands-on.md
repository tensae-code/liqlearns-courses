# Hands-on — Dissolved gases in water and aquatic life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch a gas leave water
Pour a freshly opened fizzy drink into a glass and watch the bubbles form and rise. Say where that gas was a minute before, and what its leaving shows about gases held in water.

## Map the water unit
List the conditions of an aquatic habitat from Egypt's unit order — water, dissolved gases, light, pressure — and say for each one how it reaches the organism.
