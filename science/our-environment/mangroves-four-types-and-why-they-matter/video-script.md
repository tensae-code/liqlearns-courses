Today: **Mangroves — four types, and why they matter** — the Bahamas' way: know the coast by its mangroves.

There are **four types**. At the water's edge, the **red mangrove**, standing on arching prop roots. Behind it, the **black mangrove**, its pencil roots poking up from the mud to breathe. Further in, the **white mangrove**. And on the highest, driest ground, the **buttonwood**.
Between the roots, young fish and crabs shelter — the mangrove is a nursery. And the stand guards the island itself: roots break the storm seas and hold the shore. Four types, one living wall.
