# Mangroves — four types, and why they matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How many types of mangroves does this lesson teach for The Bahamas?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four  d) Seven
**Answer: c) Four**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The red mangrove is known by…
a) Pencil roots rising from the mud  b) Arching prop roots at the water's edge  c) Button-like fruiting heads  d) Growing only on dry hills
**Answer: b) Arching prop roots at the water's edge**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The black mangrove breathes through…
a) Its flowers  b) Pencil roots that rise out of the mud  c) The buttonwood's leaves  d) Crab burrows
**Answer: b) Pencil roots that rise out of the mud**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The type on the highest, driest ground at the back of the stand is…
a) The red mangrove  b) The white mangrove  c) The buttonwood  d) The black mangrove
**Answer: c) The buttonwood**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Young fish use the mangrove roots to…
a) Shelter and grow, out of reach of bigger hunters  b) Sleep through the winter  c) Lay eggs on dry land  d) Escape from the sea for good
**Answer: a) Shelter and grow, out of reach of bigger hunters**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Mangroves matter to the islands because they…
a) Make the sea saltier  b) Guard the coast from storm seas, hold the shoreline, and nurse sea life  c) Stop all rain  d) Feed only birds
**Answer: b) Guard the coast from storm seas, hold the shoreline, and nurse sea life**
