# Mangroves — four types, and why they matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four types in shore order, water's edge to driest ground.
**Answer: Red mangrove, black mangrove, white mangrove, buttonwood.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the characteristic that names the black mangrove.
**Answer: Its pencil roots — thin spikes rising straight up out of the mud around the tree, through which it takes in air.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two organisms of the mangrove ecosystem and where each lives.
**Answer: Any two of: young fish, sheltering among the roots; crabs, feeding and burrowing in the mud; small sea organisms on and between the roots; birds, feeding at the water and resting or nesting in the branches.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Say two ways mangroves protect an island.
**Answer: Any two of: their roots break the force of storm seas before waves reach the land; their roots hold the shoreline, trapping mud and sand so the coast is built rather than washed away; their sheltered waters nurse the sea life island people live from.**
