# Mangroves — four types, and why they matter — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The _____ mangrove stands at the water's edge on arching prop roots.
**Answer: red**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The black mangrove's breathing roots are called _____ roots.
**Answer: pencil**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A mangrove stand is an _____ — a whole living community, not a row of trees.
**Answer: ecosystem**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Sheltered mangrove waters are a _____ for young sea life.
**Answer: nursery**
