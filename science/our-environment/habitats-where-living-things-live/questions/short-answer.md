# Habitats — where living things live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: A habitat — where living things live.
**Answer:** A **habitat** is a place where living things are found — the place that gives a plant or an animal what it needs. South Sudan's Primary 2 book gives the definition in its own words: a habitat is a place where plants are found, and the Primary 1 book starts the same idea by grouping plants by where they grow.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Plants grouped by where they grow.
**Answer:** Plants are sorted by their habitat: plants **in or near water** (water lily, water hyacinth, papyrus, palm trees along the river), plants of **hot and dry areas** (acacia, cactus, euphorbia, aloe vera), and plants of **cool and wet places**, whose leaves stay green all year. The same plant does not grow everywhere — it grows where its habitat fits it.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Animals grouped by where they live.
**Answer:** Animals are sorted the same way: animals that live **underground** (moles, termites, ants, squirrels) and **in the soil** (earthworms, millipedes, centipedes) — found by digging the school garden — and animals of the land and of the water. The Primary 3 guide adds the run's close: habitats for plants and animals together, and other habitats beyond the first ones named.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** A habitat — where living things live → Plants grouped by where they grow → Animals grouped by where they live — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
