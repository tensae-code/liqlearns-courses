## Sub lesson 1 — A habitat — where living things live

A **habitat** is a place where living things are found — the place that gives a plant or an animal what it needs. South Sudan's Primary 2 book gives the definition in its own words: a habitat is a place where plants are found, and the Primary 1 book starts the same idea by grouping plants by where they grow.

## Sub lesson 2 — Plants grouped by where they grow

Plants are sorted by their habitat: plants **in or near water** (water lily, water hyacinth, papyrus, palm trees along the river), plants of **hot and dry areas** (acacia, cactus, euphorbia, aloe vera), and plants of **cool and wet places**, whose leaves stay green all year. The same plant does not grow everywhere — it grows where its habitat fits it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Animals grouped by where they live

Animals are sorted the same way: animals that live **underground** (moles, termites, ants, squirrels) and **in the soil** (earthworms, millipedes, centipedes) — found by digging the school garden — and animals of the land and of the water. The Primary 3 guide adds the run's close: habitats for plants and animals together, and other habitats beyond the first ones named.
