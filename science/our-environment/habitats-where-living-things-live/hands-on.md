# Hands-on — Habitats — where living things live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's thing with your hands
Dig in the school garden (with an adult): list every living thing you find in the soil and under stones, then sort your finds and the plants around you into habitats — in or near water, hot and dry, cool and wet, underground or in the soil. Say for each one what its habitat gives it.
