# Hands-on — Biodiversity — keeping the variety of life

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Two-place count
Visit or recall two places: one varied (garden, forest edge, compound corner) and one single-kind (a crop plot, a plantation row). Spend the same short time at each and list every kind you can name. Count the lists. That count difference IS biodiversity, measured by your own eyes.

## Protection sorting
Write six cards: national park, zoo, seed bank, sanctuary, botanical garden, biosphere reserve. Sort them into IN-SITU and EX-SITU piles, then write under each pile the danger that pile answers best.
