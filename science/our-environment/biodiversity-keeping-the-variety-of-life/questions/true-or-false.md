# Biodiversity — keeping the variety of life — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's key names five families of biodiversity value, including recreation and scientific value.
**Answer: True** — Economic, ecological life support, recreation, cultural, scientific.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ex-situ protection is always better than in-situ, so parks are not needed.
**Answer: False** — In-situ protects the home itself; ex-situ is the safeguard outside it — the two answer different dangers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Habitat loss is one of the ways biodiversity is lost.
**Answer: True** — With overexploitation, exotic introduced species and pollution.
