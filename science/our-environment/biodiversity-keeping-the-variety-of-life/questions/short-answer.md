# Biodiversity — keeping the variety of life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the guide's five families of biodiversity value.
**Answer:** Economic; ecological life support; recreation; cultural; scientific.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sort into in-situ / ex-situ: zoo, biosphere reserve, botanical garden, sanctuary.
**Answer:** In-situ: biosphere reserve, sanctuary. Ex-situ: zoo, botanical garden.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is a forest edge richer in biodiversity than a wheat field, in the lesson's counting?
**Answer:** Biodiversity is counted in species: the forest edge holds many kinds living together, the wheat field is planted to essentially one kind with few others.
