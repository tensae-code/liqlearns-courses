# Biodiversity — keeping the variety of life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Biodiversity is —
a) the number of people in a place  b) the variety of life, counted in species  c) the size of the biggest animal  d) the amount of rain
**Answer: b) the variety of life, counted in species**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Most human drugs began in —
a) factories  b) living organisms  c) minerals only  d) sea salt
**Answer: a) factories** — medicine is a direct value of biodiversity.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Nutrient cycling and keeping fresh water are examples of biodiversity's —
a) direct value  b) indirect value  c) price  d) loss
**Answer: b) indirect value** — the unseen work of the variety.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the four ways biodiversity is lost? 
a) overexploitation  b) exotic introduced species  c) seed banks  d) habitat loss
**Answer: c) seed banks** — seed banks are ex-situ PROTECTION.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A national park protects biodiversity —
a) ex-situ, outside the habitat  b) in-situ, in the natural habitat  c) in a laboratory  d) not at all
**Answer: b) in-situ, in the natural habitat** — also sanctuaries and biosphere reserves.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A seed bank is ex-situ protection because it keeps seeds —
a) in their forest  b) outside the natural habitat, as a store  c) in a market  d) in a river
**Answer: b) outside the natural habitat, as a store** — with botanical gardens and zoos.
