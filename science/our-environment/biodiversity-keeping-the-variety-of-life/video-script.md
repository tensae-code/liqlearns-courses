# Video script — Biodiversity — keeping the variety of life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Biodiversity** — the variety of life, counted in **species**.
A forest holds many. A wheat field holds few.
From life come our **medicines** and our **crops**.
Life cycles our nutrients, cleans our water, **pollinates**.
It is lost by over-use, invaders, pollution, **lost homes**.
Guard the home — and keep a living copy safe outside it.
