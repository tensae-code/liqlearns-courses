# Lesson plan — Forests — what they give and what cutting them costs

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Forests — what they give and what cutting them costs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Ecosystem and Conservation of Natural Resources, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** forests give timber, fuel wood, fodder, fibre, fruit, herbal drugs and industrial raw materials, and they shelter wildlife, build and hold soil, store water, return oxygen, fix carbon dioxide and steady the climate; deforestation — driven by farm expansion, mining, settlement, roads, timber, firewood and paper — brings erosion, diversity loss, fuel scarcity and habitat destruction, answered by afforestation, alternative fuels and materials, and Ethiopia's Green Legacy planting.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Saving the soil — conservation methods (Soil and Water, Grade 8), where afforestation was one method among six, and on Biodiversity — keeping the variety of life (this title, Grade 8); the teacher guide's carbon-sink mechanism (item 16) deepens the climate line and is labelled in sources. The Biology title's conservation lessons carry forests at secondary depth — cross-referenced, not copied.

## Sub lessons
1. **What a forest gives** — the goods list and the services list; trees as carbon sinks (guide evidence).
2. **Deforestation — causes, impacts, answers** — the two lists set against each other; afforestation and Green Legacy.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Saving the soil — conservation methods (Soil and Water)
- Recommended first: Global warming — the greenhouse effect (Our Environment) — recommended, never forced.
## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List what forests give, in goods and in services.
2. List deforestation's causes and its impacts, side by side.
3. Say how a growing tree is a carbon sink, and name the answers to deforestation.
## What you need
One real tree to audit — or the picture in this lesson; knowledge of your area's planting days.
