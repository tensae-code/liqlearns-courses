# Video script — Forests — what they give and what cutting them costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A forest **gives** while it stands.
Fruit, fodder, medicine — and **shelter** for wild life.
Its roots **hold the soil**. Its leaves **clean the air**.
A growing tree is a store of **carbon**.
Cut it all — erosion comes, homes vanish, fuel runs short.
**Plant.** That is the answer with roots.
