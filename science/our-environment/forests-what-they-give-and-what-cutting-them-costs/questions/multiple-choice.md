# Forests — what they give and what cutting them costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a forest SERVICE (given while it stands)? 
a) timber  b) sheltering wildlife and holding soil  c) firewood  d) paper
**Answer: b) sheltering wildlife and holding soil** — goods are taken; services are given standing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A growing tree is a carbon sink because it —
a) releases stored carbon  b) pulls CO₂ in through its leaves and holds the carbon in its body for decades or centuries  c) makes coal at once  d) stops photosynthesis
**Answer: b) pulls CO₂ in through its leaves and holds the carbon in its body for decades or centuries** — guide evidence, printed pp. 96–99.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Forests steady the climate by —
a) blocking all rain  b) fixing carbon dioxide and returning oxygen  c) warming the air  d) stopping wind everywhere
**Answer: b) fixing carbon dioxide and returning oxygen**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a CAUSE of deforestation? 
a) afforestation  b) farm expansion and settlement  c) planting on degraded land  d) alternative fuels
**Answer: b) farm expansion and settlement** — also mining, roads, timber, firewood, paper.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An impact of deforestation is —
a) more wildlife habitat  b) fuel scarcity and erosion  c) cleaner air  d) deeper soil
**Answer: b) fuel scarcity and erosion**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Afforestation means planting trees —
a) only in existing forests  b) on vacant, degraded and roadside land  c) in rivers  d) for one day only
**Answer: b) on vacant, degraded and roadside land** — the book's named answer, with alternative fuels and materials.
