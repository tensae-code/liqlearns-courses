# Forests — what they give and what cutting them costs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A forest gives nothing until it is cut down.
**Answer: False** — Standing, it shelters wildlife, holds soil, stores water, returns oxygen, fixes CO₂ and steadies climate.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Reforestation is replanting where tree numbers have fallen.
**Answer: True** — The guide's definition.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cutting forests can make fuel scarcer for the people who cut them.
**Answer: True** — Fuel scarcity is on the impacts list.
