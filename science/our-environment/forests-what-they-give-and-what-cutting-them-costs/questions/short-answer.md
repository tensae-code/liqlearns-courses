# Forests — what they give and what cutting them costs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four goods people take from forests.
**Answer:** Any four of: timber, fuel wood, fodder, fibre, fruit, herbal drugs, industrial raw materials.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Set one cause of deforestation against the impact it most directly brings.
**Answer:** E.g. farm expansion clears habitat → habitat destruction; clearing slopes → erosion; firewood cutting beyond regrowth → fuel scarcity.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What part does the Green Legacy campaign play in this lesson?
**Answer:** It is Ethiopia's national tree-planting campaign; pupils audit their own part in it as the local, organised answer to deforestation.
