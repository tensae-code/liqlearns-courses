# Hands-on — Forests — what they give and what cutting them costs

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## One-tree audit
Choose one tree. In two columns write what it gives standing (shade, soil-holding, homes for living things, air work — and any goods taken without killing it, like fruit) and what would be lost if it were cut this week. End with one line: its worth standing, in your own words.

## Green Legacy count
Find out about the last planting season near home or school: how many seedlings were planted, where, and how many survived if anyone knows. Write your own part — planted, watered, guarded, or none yet — and one honest next step.
