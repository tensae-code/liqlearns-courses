# How people change the environment

## Sub lesson 1 — People act — the environment answers

Cambodia's Grade 9 book opens its environment chapter (Biology Ch. 5) with the widest statement first: **human actions and their effect on the environment** (Lesson 1). What people do — clearing, building, farming, burning, throwing away — does not stop at the deed; the land, water and air answer, and the answer is the environment the next lessons study.

## Sub lesson 2 — The chapter's order — effect first, pollution second

Only after this general lesson does the chapter narrow to **environmental pollution** (Lesson 2). The order is the teaching: first learn that people's actions change the environment whole, then study one great kind of that change in detail. A learner who holds Lesson 1 can place every later harm — and every care — on it.
