# Hands-on — How people change the environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Actions and answers
List five things people do around your home. Beside each, write how the land, water or air answers. Check your rows against the lesson's claim: no deed stops at the deed.

## Place the harms
Write three environmental harms you know. Above each, write the human action it answers. Say how this lesson holds them all.
