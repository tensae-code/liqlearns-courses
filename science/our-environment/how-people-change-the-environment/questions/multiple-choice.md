# How people change the environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Biology Ch. 5 of Cambodia's Grade 9 book opens with…
a) human actions and their effect on the environment  b) the planets  c) minerals  d) the Moon
**Answer: a) human actions and their effect on the environment** — Lesson 1 is the chapter's widest statement, taught first.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson that follows it narrows to…
a) environmental pollution  b) food chains  c) the skeleton  d) friction
**Answer: a) environmental pollution** — Lesson 2: pollution — one great kind of human effect, in detail.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Human deeds named in the lesson include…
a) clearing, building, farming, burning, throwing away  b) only reading  c) only sleeping  d) only singing
**Answer: a) clearing, building, farming, burning, throwing away** — Everyday actions — and the land, water and air answer them.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The environment 'answers' through…
a) land, water and air  b) books  c) marks  d) dreams
**Answer: a) land, water and air** — The answer arrives in the land, the water and the air people live from.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The chapter's teaching order is…
a) the general effect first, then pollution in detail  b) pollution only, never the general  c) names first  d) no order
**Answer: a) the general effect first, then pollution in detail** — General first, detail second — the order is the teaching.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson belongs to the book's…
a) Biology part, environment chapter  b) Physics part  c) Chemistry part  d) Earth part only
**Answer: a) Biology part, environment chapter** — Biology Ch. 5, Lesson 1 of the Grade 9 combined book.
