# How people change the environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The lesson teaches that human actions — clearing, building, farming, burning, throwing away — change the environment, and the land, water and air carry the effects.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is pollution taught after this lesson and not before?
**Answer:** Because pollution is one kind of human effect on the environment; the book teaches the general thing first so the detail has somewhere to hang.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Human actions change the environment — the land, water and air answer every deed → the chapter then narrows from that general effect to pollution in detail — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
