# Kazakhstan's Red Book — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The Red Book of the Republic of Kazakhstan, The Red Book of plants, The Red Book of Kazakhstan, Why does the Book for the protection of the animal and plant world have a red colour?), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 4 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks.
