# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan's Red Book

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan's Red Book
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (Our Environment) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The books teach this part as rare and protected species of Kazakhstan — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The Red Book of the Republic of Kazakhstan** — The books teach this part as rare and protected species of Kazakhstan — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
2. **The Red Book of plants** — The books teach this part as the Red Book's role in saving rare plants — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
3. **The Red Book of Kazakhstan** — Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "The Red Book of Kazakhstan".
4. **Why does the Book for the protection of the animal and plant world have a red colour?** — Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "Why does the Book for the protection of the animal and plant world have a red colour?".
