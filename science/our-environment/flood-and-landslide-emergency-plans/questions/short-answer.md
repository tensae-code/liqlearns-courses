# Flood and landslide emergency plans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The two dangers — floods and landslides.
**Answer:** A **flood** is water rising and spreading over land that is usually dry; a **landslide** is earth and rock sliding down a slope. Heavy rain can bring both, and a community in their path faces them as named, known dangers — not surprises.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Making the community emergency plan.
**Answer:** An **emergency plan** is made **before** the emergency: the community decides in advance what will be done, who does it, and where people go when a flood or a landslide threatens.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The two dangers — floods and landslides → Making the community emergency plan — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
