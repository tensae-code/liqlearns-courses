Today: **Flood and landslide emergency plans** — Costa Rica's way, from its Grade 7 Ciencias, Código 80002 (printed p. 124).

A **flood** is water rising and spreading over land that is usually dry; a **landslide** is earth and rock sliding down a slope. Heavy rain can bring both, and a community in their path faces them as named, known dangers — not surprises.

An **emergency plan** is made **before** the emergency: the community decides in advance what will be done, who does it, and where people go when a flood or a landslide threatens.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
