# Invasive species — lupine and mink in Iceland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** The lupine, a relative of white clover, was brought in to reclaim land — and spreading is its risk.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** The mink is an invasive predator in Iceland, at the top of its food web with no natural check above it.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What do the lupine and the mink teach together that neither teaches alone?
**Answer:** That the risk is in the bringing-in itself — plant or animal, a living thing set loose in a home with no balance ready for it can spread or hunt unchecked.
