# Invasive species — lupine and mink in Iceland

## Do it first
People bring a plant to heal bare land, and bring an animal for its fur. Years later the plant is everywhere and the animal hunts the country's own birds. When is a brought-in living thing a help, and when is it a risk? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: Iceland's guide teaches both cases together, as a warning.

## Sub lesson 1 — The lupine — brought in to reclaim land, spreading

The **lupine** — a relative of white clover — was brought in and **used to reclaim land**, Iceland's land-reclamation work (stocked in `soil-and-water/saving-the-soil-conservation-methods`). But a brought-in plant does not stay where it was put: the guide teaches the lupine **spreading** as the risk side of the same story.

## Sub lesson 2 — The mink — an invasive predator at the top of its food web

The **mink** is the animal case: a brought-in predator that escaped into Iceland's wild and sits **at the top of its food web** — an **invasive predator** with no natural check above it, hunting the country's own animals. Plant and predator together teach the lesson's one thing: brought-in living things are a risk, because the new home has no balance ready for them.

## The key ideas

- The lupine, a relative of white clover, was brought in to reclaim land — and spreading is its risk.
- The mink is an invasive predator in Iceland, at the top of its food web with no natural check above it.
- Brought-in living things are a risk: the new home has no balance ready for them.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order — The lupine — brought in to reclaim land, spreading first, then The mink — an invasive predator at the top of its food web. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "What do the lupine and the mink teach together that neither teaches alone?" Answer: That the risk is in the bringing-in itself — plant or animal, a living thing set loose in a home with no balance ready for it can spread or hunt unchecked.
