# Hands-on — Invasive species — lupine and mink in Iceland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Help or risk?
A partner names brought-in living things from the lesson and from your own area. For each, argue both columns: the help it was brought for, and the risk it became. The lesson's pair must come first.

## Top of the web
Draw the mink's food web as the lesson gives it: the mink at the top, arrows from what it hunts. Mark what is missing above the mink — and say why that missing place matters.
