# Video script — Cloud forest

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Cloud forest** — Micronesia's way: the forest that stands in the cloud.

Climb a high Pacific island to its top, and you walk into cloud. The forest there grows in thin soil, so its trees stay small — and mosses and ferns cover everything, in air that is always damp. That is the cloud forest: a mountaintop forest wrapped in its own cloud.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
