# Cloud forest — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What makes a forest a cloud forest, and not just a forest on a mountain?
**Answer: It stands in cloud — the mountaintop forest lives wrapped in the cloud layer, in always-damp air.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why are the cloud forest's trees small?
**Answer: Because its soil is thin — there is little depth of earth for big trees to root and feed in.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the small plants that cover everything in the cloud forest.
**Answer: Mosses and ferns.**
