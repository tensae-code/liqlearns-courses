# Cloud forest — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A cloud forest stands on mountaintops in _____.
**Answer: cloud**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The soil of the cloud forest is _____.
**Answer: thin**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Its trees stay _____.
**Answer: small**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ and ferns cover everything.
**Answer: Mosses**
