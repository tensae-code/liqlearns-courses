# Cloud forest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A cloud forest grows…
a) Beside the lagoon  b) On mountaintops in cloud  c) Under the sea  d) In taro pits
**Answer: b) On mountaintops in cloud**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The soil of a cloud forest is…
a) Deep and rich  b) Thin  c) Made of coral sand  d) Always dry
**Answer: b) Thin**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The trees of a cloud forest are…
a) The tallest on the island  b) Small  c) All coconut palms  d) Dead
**Answer: b) Small**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What covers everything in a cloud forest?
a) Grass  b) Sand  c) Mosses and ferns  d) Coral
**Answer: c) Mosses and ferns**
