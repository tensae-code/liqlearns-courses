# Hands-on — Cloud forest

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Thin soil, small tree
Fill two pots or tins — one with deep soil, one with a thin layer over stones. Plant the same kind of seed in each, water both the same, and watch for two weeks. Say what the thin-soil plant shows you about the cloud forest's small trees.

## Find the moss-and-fern world
Find the dampest shaded place near home — a wall base, a tree trunk's north side, a wet rock. Look closely for moss or fern growth and draw what covers the surface. Say why that small growth, and not grass, is what covers everything in the cloud forest.
