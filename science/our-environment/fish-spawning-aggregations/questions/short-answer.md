# Fish spawning aggregations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How do many reef fish reproduce, step by step?
**Answer: The females release eggs into the water and the males release sperm into the water; where an egg and a sperm meet in the water, a new fish can begin — no nest and no carrying.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What must be true of the water for the release to work?
**Answer: The eggs and the sperm must be in the same water at the same time, close enough to meet — apart or at different times, no new fish begins.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is a spawning aggregation?
**Answer: A gathering of many reef fish in one place at one time, all releasing their eggs and sperm together into the same water.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is gathering better than releasing alone?
**Answer: Alone, a fish's eggs or sperm would drift and be lost in the wide sea; in the aggregation the water fills with eggs and sperm side by side, so the meetings that make new fish happen everywhere in the crowd at once.**
