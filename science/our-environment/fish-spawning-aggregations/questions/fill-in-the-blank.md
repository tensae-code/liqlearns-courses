# Fish spawning aggregations — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The female fish releases her _____ into the water.
**Answer: eggs**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The male fish releases his _____ into the water.
**Answer: sperm**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Many fish gathering in one place to spawn is called a spawning _____.
**Answer: aggregation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the aggregation, everybody releases at the same _____ and in the same water.
**Answer: time**
