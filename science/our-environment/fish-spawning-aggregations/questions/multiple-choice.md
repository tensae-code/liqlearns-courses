# Fish spawning aggregations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Many reef fish reproduce by…
a) Laying eggs in a nest on the reef  b) Releasing eggs and sperm into the water  c) Carrying young inside the body like a dog  d) Feeding their young from a pouch
**Answer: b) Releasing eggs and sperm into the water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A new fish begins where…
a) An egg and a sperm meet in the water  b) A fish finds an empty shell  c) The moon is full  d) The tide goes out
**Answer: a) An egg and a sperm meet in the water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) For the release to work, eggs and sperm must be…
a) In different oceans  b) Released a year apart  c) In the same water at the same time  d) Hidden under the sand
**Answer: c) In the same water at the same time**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A spawning aggregation is…
a) One fish spawning alone  b) Many reef fish gathering in one place to release eggs and sperm  c) A school of young fish only  d) A market where fish are sold
**Answer: b) Many reef fish gathering in one place to release eggs and sperm**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The fish gather before releasing because…
a) Fish cannot swim alone  b) The reef is warmer in a crowd  c) Predators fear big numbers only  d) Eggs and sperm released far apart would never meet
**Answer: d) Eggs and sperm released far apart would never meet**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A spawning gathering place matters because…
a) The next generation of reef fish begins there  b) Fish sleep there  c) It is where mangroves grow  d) Sailors mark it on maps
**Answer: a) The next generation of reef fish begins there**
