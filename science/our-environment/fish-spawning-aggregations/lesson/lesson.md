# Fish spawning aggregations

## Do it first
Before you read: a bird lays eggs in a nest, and a dog carries its young inside its body. A reef fish does neither. So how does a fish make new fish — out in the open sea, with nothing to hold the eggs? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Palau's way in — its Ridge to Reef book for Grade 5 teaches fish reproduction from the gathering itself.

## Sub lesson 1 — How reef fish make new fish — eggs and sperm in the water

Many reef fish reproduce in the open water. The female fish releases her **eggs** into the water, and the male fish releases his **sperm** into the water. Where an egg and a sperm meet, a new fish can begin.

There is no nest and no carrying: the parents' bodies do their part by the release itself, and the **water** is where the eggs are fertilised and the young fish start. Everything hangs on one thing — the eggs and the sperm must be in the same water, at the same time, close enough to meet.

## Sub lesson 2 — The gathering — many fish, one place, one time

Eggs released by one fish alone, far from any other, would drift and be lost. So the fish solve it together: **many reef fish gather in one place** to release their eggs and sperm — a gathering called a **spawning aggregation**.

In the aggregation, everybody releases at the same time, in the same water. The water fills with eggs and sperm side by side, and the meetings that make new fish happen everywhere in the crowd at once. The crowd is not an accident or a party: it is the fish's way of bringing eggs and sperm together in a wide, open sea.

That is why the gathering matters beyond the day itself: a place where fish gather to spawn is a place where the next generation of reef fish begins — and what happens to that gathering reaches every fish that would have come from it.

## The key ideas

- Many reef fish reproduce by releasing **eggs and sperm into the water**, where the eggs are fertilised.
- The release only works if eggs and sperm are in the **same water at the same time**.
- A **spawning aggregation** is the gathering that makes that happen: **many fish, one place, one time**, releasing together.
- The gathering place is where the **next generation** of reef fish begins.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order — the release into the water first, then the gathering that makes it work. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "Why do the fish gather instead of releasing alone?" Answer from the meeting of eggs and sperm, not by repeating.
