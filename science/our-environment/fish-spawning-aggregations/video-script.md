Today: **Fish spawning aggregations** — Palau's way: how reef fish make new fish, together.

A fish cannot hand its eggs to its mate. So reef fish do it this way instead. The females release their eggs into the water, and the males release their sperm into the water, and the new fish begin there, in the open sea.
But eggs and sperm released far apart would never meet. So the fish gather first — many reef fish, in one place, at one time. That gathering is called a spawning aggregation. The crowd is the trick: everybody releases together, and the water fills with eggs and sperm side by side.
