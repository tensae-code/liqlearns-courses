# Ocean zones and the intertidal zone

## Sub lesson 1 — Ocean zones — depth, sunlight and temperature

Fiji's Year 10 book divides the ocean into **zones**, and reads each zone by three measures: its **depth**, the **sunlight** that reaches it, and its **temperature**. From the bright surface down, the three measures change together, and the zone is the band where they hold together.

## Sub lesson 2 — The intertidal zone — where the ocean meets the shore

One zone is not in the deep at all: the **intertidal zone**, where the ocean meets the shore. It is covered and uncovered as the water comes and goes, so the living things of this zone live in both worlds — sea and shore — in turn.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fiji's way — the shore at hand

Fiji teaches the zones as an island country: the intertidal zone is the shore its learners can walk, and the zones below it run out from that same shore by depth, sunlight and temperature (printed pp. 38–43). The ocean's ladder starts at the beach.
