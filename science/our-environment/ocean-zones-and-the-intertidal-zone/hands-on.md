# Hands-on — Ocean zones and the intertidal zone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Draw the ocean's ladder from the lesson: the shore's intertidal zone first, then the zones below it, and beside each zone write its depth, sunlight and temperature from the lesson. If you can visit a shore, stand in the intertidal zone at a low-water moment and name what lives where the sea comes and goes.
