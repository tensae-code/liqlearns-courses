# Ocean zones and the intertidal zone — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Ocean zones — depth, sunlight and temperature.
**Answer:** Fiji's Year 10 book divides the ocean into zones, and reads each zone by three measures: its depth, the sunlight that reaches it, and its temperature.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The intertidal zone — where the ocean meets the shore.
**Answer:** One zone is not in the deep at all: the intertidal zone, where the ocean meets the shore.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Fiji's way — the shore at hand.
**Answer:** Fiji teaches the zones as an island country: the intertidal zone is the shore its learners can walk, and the zones below it run out from that same shore by depth, sunlight and temperature (printed pp.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Ocean zones — depth, sunlight and temperature → The intertidal zone — where the ocean meets the shore → Fiji's way — the shore at hand — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
