# Living things tell their own story — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Life all over the world, as a drama.
**Answer:** Bangladesh's Class 6 Exercise Book retells the living world as a drama — Life All Over the World — whose characters are not human.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Why a drama?.
**Answer:** A story makes its teller choose: who eats, who hides, who travels, who waits.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The Exercise Book's own way.
**Answer:** This is the Exercise Book's way of teaching: not reading about living things, but doing something with them — here, staging their story.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Life all over the world, as a drama → Why a drama? → The Exercise Book's own way — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
