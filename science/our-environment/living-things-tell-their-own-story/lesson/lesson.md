## Sub lesson 1 — Life all over the world, as a drama

Bangladesh's Class 6 Exercise Book retells the living world as a drama — Life All Over the World — whose characters are not human. The living things themselves carry the story, each in its own place and way of life.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why a drama?

A story makes its teller choose: who eats, who hides, who travels, who waits. Told as drama, the connections between living things — the food, the homes, the seasons — become the plot, and the plot is the science.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Exercise Book's own way

This is the Exercise Book's way of teaching: not reading about living things, but doing something with them — here, staging their story. A learner who writes a living thing's part must first learn how that living thing actually lives.
