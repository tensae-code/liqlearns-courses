# Hands-on — Ecological problems of Kazakhstan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Ecological problems of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Kazakhstan's books teach this as their own lesson — "Ecological problems of the Republic of Kazakhstan". Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
