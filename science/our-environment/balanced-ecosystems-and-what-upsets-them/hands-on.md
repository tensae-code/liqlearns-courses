# Hands-on — Balanced ecosystems and what upsets them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: the balanced ecosystem: when all an ecosystem's factors work together it stays in balance, though it is always changing — a pond drying in drought drives out the frogs and insects that cannot move on, while land living things (trees, flowers, rats, earthworms) take its place Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
