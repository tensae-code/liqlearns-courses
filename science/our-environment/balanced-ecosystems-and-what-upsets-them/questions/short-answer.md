# Balanced ecosystems and what upsets them — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The balanced ecosystem.
**Answer:** the balanced ecosystem: when all an ecosystem's factors work together it stays in balance, though it is always changing — a pond drying in drought drives out the frogs and insects that cannot move on, while land living things (trees, flowers, rats, earthworms) take its place.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Changes in Ecosystems.
**Answer:** Ch. 3, Lesson 3 "Changes in Ecosystems": what changes an ecosystem — natural phenomena: volcanoes and hurricanes, and heavy rain that may wash hillsides into valleys, fill wadis and flood, while no rain at all brings drought; such changes can threaten the living things' survival, and an ecosystem needs a long time to recover its state.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The balanced ecosystem → Changes in Ecosystems — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
