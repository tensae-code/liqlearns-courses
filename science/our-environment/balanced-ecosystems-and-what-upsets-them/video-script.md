Today: **Balanced ecosystems and what upsets them** — Saudi Arabia's way, from its book (Saudi Arabia, First Intermediate, Part 2 — *Science, First Intermediate, Part 2*, printed p. 166).

the balanced ecosystem: when all an ecosystem's factors work together it stays in balance, though it is always changing — a pond drying in drought drives out the frogs and insects that cannot move on, while land living things (trees, flowers, rats, earthworms) take its place
Ch. 3, Lesson 3 "Changes in Ecosystems": what changes an ecosystem — natural phenomena: volcanoes and hurricanes, and heavy rain that may wash hillsides into valleys, fill wadis and flood, while no rain at all brings drought; such changes can threaten the living things' survival, and an ecosystem needs a long time to recover its state
