Today: **Natural hazards — and staying safe** — Papua New Guinea's way, from its Department of Education science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 10 guide opens its Natural Hazards topic with the definition and the sorting: **what natural hazards are**, their **types**, their **sources**, and their **causes and effects** — the hazard named and classified before anything is done about it.

The guide's second step is the instruments: the **equipment used to measure and monitor** natural hazards. A hazard that is watched can be warned about — measurement turns a sudden event into one people can prepare for.

The topic closes with **safety**: what to do so a natural hazard does the least harm. The guide's order is the lesson's order — know the hazard, watch the hazard, then act to stay safe.
