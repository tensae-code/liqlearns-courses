## Sub lesson 2 — Measuring and monitoring hazards

The guide's second step is the instruments: the **equipment used to measure and monitor** natural hazards. A hazard that is watched can be warned about — measurement turns a sudden event into one people can prepare for.

## Sub lesson 3 — Safety — staying safe

The topic closes with **safety**: what to do so a natural hazard does the least harm. The guide's order is the lesson's order — know the hazard, watch the hazard, then act to stay safe.
