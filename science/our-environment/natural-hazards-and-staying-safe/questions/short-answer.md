# Natural hazards — and staying safe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What natural hazards are — types, sources, causes.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 10 guide opens its Natural Hazards topic with the definition and the sorting: **what natural hazards are**, their **types**, their **sources**, and their **causes and effects** — the hazard named and classified before anything is done about it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Measuring and monitoring hazards.
**Answer:** The guide's second step is the instruments: the **equipment used to measure and monitor** natural hazards. A hazard that is watched can be warned about — measurement turns a sudden event into one people can prepare for.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Safety — staying safe.
**Answer:** The topic closes with **safety**: what to do so a natural hazard does the least harm. The guide's order is the lesson's order — know the hazard, watch the hazard, then act to stay safe.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What natural hazards are — types, sources, causes → Measuring and monitoring hazards → Safety — staying safe — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
