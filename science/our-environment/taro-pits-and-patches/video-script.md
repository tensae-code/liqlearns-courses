# Video script — Taro pits and patches

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Taro pits and patches** — Micronesia's way: a farm dug down to the water.

A low island has no rivers, so its wet ground hides underground — rain gathers there as a freshwater lens. The taro farmer digs a pit down through the sand until he reaches that wet ground, and in the pit bottom the giant swamp taro grows. And the patch is a home as well as a farm: shrimp, crabs and fish live in its water, and young dragonflies and mosquitoes begin their lives there.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
