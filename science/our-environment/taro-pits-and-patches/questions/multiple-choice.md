# Taro pits and patches — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A taro pit is…
a) A hole for storing dry sand  b) A pit dug down to wet ground where taro can grow  c) A well for drinking water only  d) A hole dug by crabs
**Answer: b) A pit dug down to wet ground where taro can grow**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On the lowest islands, the pits are dug down to…
a) The sea bed  b) Solid rock  c) The freshwater lens  d) A river
**Answer: c) The freshwater lens**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The taro that grows in the pits is…
a) Giant swamp taro  b) Breadfruit  c) Pandanus  d) Coconut
**Answer: a) Giant swamp taro**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A taro patch is also a habitat for…
a) Only the farmer's family  b) Shrimp, crabs, fish and young dragonflies and mosquitoes  c) Seabirds only  d) Coral polyps
**Answer: b) Shrimp, crabs, fish and young dragonflies and mosquitoes**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Young dragonflies and mosquitoes begin their lives…
a) In the dry sand  b) In the water of the pits  c) Inside the taro leaves  d) On the reef
**Answer: b) In the water of the pits**
