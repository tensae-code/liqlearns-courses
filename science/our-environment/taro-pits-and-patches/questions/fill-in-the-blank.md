# Taro pits and patches — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A taro pit is dug down to the _____ ground.
**Answer: wet**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) On low islands the pits reach the freshwater _____.
**Answer: lens**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The taro grown in the pits is giant _____ taro.
**Answer: swamp**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Young _____ and mosquitoes begin their lives in the pits' water.
**Answer: dragonflies**
