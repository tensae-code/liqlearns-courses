# Hands-on — Taro pits and patches

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dig to the wet layer
In a tray or patch of dry sand or soil, pour a little water deep at one spot the day before, then dig a small pit with a spoon until you reach the damp layer. Say what you had to pass through, and what the taro pit digger is reaching for in the same way.

## Who lives in the wet place?
Look at any long-wet place near home — a ditch, a pond edge, a water container garden. List every living thing you can find in or over the water. Say which of them the taro patch also shelters, from the lesson.
