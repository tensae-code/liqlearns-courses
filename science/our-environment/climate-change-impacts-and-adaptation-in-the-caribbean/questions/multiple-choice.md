# Climate change impacts and adaptation in the Caribbean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On an island, climate change first reaches island life at…
a) The moon  b) The coast, in the weather, and in food and fresh water  c) Only in books  d) Only in other countries
**Answer: b) The coast, in the weather, and in food and fresh water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A rising sea and bigger storm waves can…
a) Make beaches grow  b) Flood coastal land and wash beaches away  c) Fill wells with clean water  d) Stop all rain
**Answer: b) Flood coastal land and wash beaches away**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Hotter, drier spells strain…
a) Fresh water for drinking and farming  b) The sea's salt  c) The moon's light  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Fresh water for drinking and farming**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Preparing homes and schools for storms and floods is…
a) Minimising  b) Adaptation  c) The greenhouse effect  d) A fossil fuel
**Answer: b) Adaptation**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Burning less fossil fuel is…
a) Minimising the impact — slowing the change itself  b) Adaptation  c) A kind of flood  d) Impossible for learners
**Answer: a) Minimising the impact — slowing the change itself**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Jamaica's Grade 7 unit asks…
a) Only teachers what to do  b) How learners themselves can minimise the impact  c) Nothing about islands  d) Only about other continents
**Answer: b) How learners themselves can minimise the impact**
