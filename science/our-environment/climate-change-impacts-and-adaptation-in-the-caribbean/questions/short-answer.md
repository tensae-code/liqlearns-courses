# Climate change impacts and adaptation in the Caribbean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name two ways climate change affects island life.
**Answer:** Any two of: rising seas and storm waves flooding the coast and washing beaches away; heavier rain and floods and stronger storms damaging homes and crops; hotter, drier spells straining fresh water, food growing and fishing.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is so much island life in the path of a rising sea?
**Answer:** Because homes, roads, fishing, beaches and tourist work sit near the coast, so flood water and storm waves reach the places where people live and work first.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one example of adaptation and say what impact it answers.
**Answer:** For example, preparing homes and schools for storms and floods, protecting the coast with mangroves, or planning water use for dry spells — each answers an impact that is already coming.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Give one example of minimising the impact, and say how it differs from adaptation.
**Answer:** For example, burning less fossil fuel, protecting and planting trees, or wasting less energy and water. It differs because it slows the change itself, while adaptation prepares for the change already coming.
