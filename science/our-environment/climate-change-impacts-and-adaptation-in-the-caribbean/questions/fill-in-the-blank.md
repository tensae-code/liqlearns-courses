# Climate change impacts and adaptation in the Caribbean — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A rising sea and storm waves can _____ coastal land.
**Answer: flood**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Hotter, drier spells strain the island's fresh _____.
**Answer: water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Preparing for storms and floods is called _____.
**Answer: adaptation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Burning less _____ fuel minimises the impact by slowing the change itself.
**Answer: fossil**
