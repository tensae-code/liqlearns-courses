# Hands-on — Climate change impacts and adaptation in the Caribbean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk your coast, or your street
Walk the nearest shore, riverbank, drain or low street you know. Stand where water would go first in a flood. Name three things the water would reach — a home, a road, a shop, a farm, a well — and say which climate impact brought it there.

## The two-lists check at home
At home, run the lesson's two lists. Adapt: check one preparation — a drain cleared, water stored, loose things that a storm could lift. Minimise: do one action — switch off a light or device nobody is using, save the water from one wash for plants, or plant or water one tree or seedling. Tick each one as you do it.

## Draw the island answer
Draw an island cross-section: sea, beach, homes, farmland, hills. On the left draw one impact arriving (flood water, storm, dry cracked soil). On the right draw the island's answer — one adaptation and one minimising action. Label each drawing with the sub lesson it comes from.
