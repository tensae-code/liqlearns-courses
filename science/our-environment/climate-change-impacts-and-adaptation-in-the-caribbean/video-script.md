Today: **Climate change impacts and adaptation in the Caribbean** — Jamaica's way: start from island life, and finish with what the learner can do.

An island meets climate change where its people live: at the coast. Rising seas and storm waves flood homes and roads and wash beaches away. Heavier rain brings floods, stronger storms break homes and crops, and hotter, drier spells squeeze the fresh water that people and farms depend on.
So island people adapt: they prepare for storms and floods, they protect the coast, and they plan their water and crops for hotter days.
And they minimise the impact itself: burn less fossil fuel, protect and plant trees, and waste less energy and water. Jamaica's Grade 7 curriculum asks the learner directly — how can you minimise its impact? That question is the lesson.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
