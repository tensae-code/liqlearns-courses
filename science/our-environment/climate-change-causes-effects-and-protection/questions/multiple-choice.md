# Climate change — causes, effects and protection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What climate change is  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What climate change is**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Causes — and effects on the Earth  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Causes — and effects on the Earth**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Protections against climate change  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Protections against climate change**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: climate change — what it is, its causes, its effects on the Earth, and protections against it. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The books behind this lesson are…
