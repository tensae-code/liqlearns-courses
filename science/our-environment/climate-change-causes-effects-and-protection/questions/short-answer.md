# Climate change — causes, effects and protection — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What climate change is.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 8 guide teaches climate change as its own topic, in its own order: first **what climate change is** — a lasting change in the Earth's climate, not one hot day or one storm — and its types, the different forms the change takes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Causes — and effects on the Earth.
**Answer:** Next the guide asks for **causes**: the things that drive the change. Then **effects on the Earth**: what the change does to the land, the sea, the weather and living things — cause and effect are taught as two separate steps, so each effect can be traced back to its cause.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Protections against climate change.
**Answer:** The topic closes with **protections**: the actions that guard against climate change and its effects. The guide's order is the lesson's order — know what it is, know what causes it, know what it does, then protect.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** What climate change is → Causes — and effects on the Earth → Protections against climate change — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
