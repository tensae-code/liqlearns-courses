Today: **Climate change — causes, effects and protection** — Papua New Guinea's way, from its Department of Education science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

Papua New Guinea's Standards Based Grade 8 guide teaches climate change as its own topic, in its own order: first **what climate change is** — a lasting change in the Earth's climate, not one hot day or one storm — and its types, the different forms the change takes.

Next the guide asks for **causes**: the things that drive the change. Then **effects on the Earth**: what the change does to the land, the sea, the weather and living things — cause and effect are taught as two separate steps, so each effect can be traced back to its cause.

The topic closes with **protections**: the actions that guard against climate change and its effects. The guide's order is the lesson's order — know what it is, know what causes it, know what it does, then protect.
