Today: **Kinds of natural habitats** — Kuwait's way: the kinds of natural habitats living things live in — land homes and water homes, hot and cold, wet and dry — and what each home gives its living things.

A natural habitat is the place where a living thing lives in nature — its home-place, which gives it food, water, shelter and space. Habitats divide first into land habitats and water habitats. On land there are forests, grasslands and deserts; in water there are seas, rivers, lakes and ponds. Kuwait's Grade 2 book teaches the kinds by travelling through them: each home is named, and the living things that belong to it are named with it. Each kind of habitat gives different things. A forest gives shade and trees to live in; a desert gives heat and little water, so its plants and animals are fitted to save water; the sea gives salt water and room to swim. A living thing taken out of its habitat loses what its home gave it. That is why the kinds matter: to protect a living thing, first know which kind of home it needs.
