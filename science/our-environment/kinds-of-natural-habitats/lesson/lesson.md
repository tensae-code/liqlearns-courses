## Sub lesson 1 — Homes on land and in water

A **natural habitat** is the place where a living thing lives in nature — its home-place, which gives it food, water, shelter and space. Habitats divide first into **land habitats** and **water habitats**.

On land there are forests, grasslands and deserts; in water there are seas, rivers, lakes and ponds. Kuwait's Grade 2 book teaches the kinds by travelling through them: each home is named, and the living things that belong to it are named with it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Every home fits its living things

Each kind of habitat gives different things. A forest gives shade and trees to live in; a desert gives heat and little water, so its plants and animals are fitted to save water; the sea gives salt water and room to swim.

A living thing taken out of its habitat loses what its home gave it. That is why the kinds matter: to protect a living thing, first know which kind of home it needs.

## The key ideas

- A natural habitat is a living thing's home-place in nature.
- Habitats are land habitats (forest, grassland, desert) and water habitats (sea, river, lake, pond).
- Each habitat gives its living things food, water, shelter and space.
- A living thing out of its habitat loses what its home gave it.
