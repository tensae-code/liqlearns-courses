# Kinds of natural habitats — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A living thing's home-place in nature is its natural _____.
**Answer: habitat**

**2.** The sea is a _____ habitat.
**Answer: water**

**3.** A desert gives heat and very little _____.
**Answer: water**

**4.** A habitat gives food, water, shelter and _____.
**Answer: space**
