# Kinds of natural habitats — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is a natural habitat?
**Answer: The place in nature where a living thing lives, which gives it food, water, shelter and space.**

**2.** Name three land habitats and two water habitats.
**Answer: Land: forest, grassland, desert. Water: sea, river (also lake, pond).**

**3.** Why is a desert animal fitted differently from a forest animal?
**Answer: Because its home gives little water and great heat, so it must save water and bear heat, while the forest animal has shade and water around it.**

**4.** Why must we know a living thing's habitat to protect it?
**Answer: Because protecting the thing means protecting the home that gives it food, water, shelter and space.**
