# Kinds of natural habitats — multiple choice


**1.** A natural habitat is…
a) A cage in a zoo  b) A living thing's home-place in nature  c) A shop for animals  d) A kind of food
**Answer: b) A living thing's home-place in nature**

**2.** Which is a water habitat?
a) Desert  b) Forest  c) Sea  d) Grassland
**Answer: c) Sea**

**3.** A desert habitat gives…
a) Much rain  b) Little water and great heat  c) Ice all year  d) Deep shade
**Answer: b) Little water and great heat**

**4.** A habitat gives its living things food, water, shelter and…
a) Money  b) Space  c) Clothes  d) Books
**Answer: b) Space**

**5.** A fish taken out of its water habitat…
a) Grows legs  b) Loses what its home gave it  c) Sleeps better  d) Turns into a bird
**Answer: b) Loses what its home gave it**

**6.** Forests, grasslands and deserts are…
a) Water habitats  b) Land habitats  c) Sky habitats  d) Underground cities
**Answer: b) Land habitats**
