# Hands-on — Decomposers — rotting and recycling in nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch a rotter
On a walk with an adult, look under fallen leaves (put everything back as you found it). Note any fungi or many-legged animals you see and one dead thing they are working on.

## Before and after
Draw a dead leaf three times: fresh-fallen, half-rotted, and become soil. Label what the rotting crew did between each picture.
