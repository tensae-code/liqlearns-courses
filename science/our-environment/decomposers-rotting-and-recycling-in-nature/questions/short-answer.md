# Decomposers — rotting and recycling in nature — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Rotting is dead matter being broken down until it becomes part of the soil again — nature's recycling.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** The forest floor's rotting crew: fungi and many-legged soil animals such as millipedes.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A wood has no rotting crew. What happens to its dead leaves and branches?
**Answer:** They pile up unbroken — dead matter is not returned to the soil, and the forest's recycling stops.
