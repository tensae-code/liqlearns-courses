# Plankton — the ocean's tiniest life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where did life begin?
a) On land  b) In the sea  c) In the air  d) In rivers only
**Answer: b) In the sea**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Phytoplankton are…
a) Tiny animals with fins  b) A kind of sand  c) Tiny plants seen only with microscopes  d) Small fish
**Answer: c) Tiny plants seen only with microscopes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) How do plankton travel?
a) They drift on the currents  b) They swim against the current  c) They walk on the sea floor  d) They fly
**Answer: a) They drift on the currents**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) By photosynthesis, phytoplankton produce…
a) All the world's salt  b) Over half the world's oxygen  c) Coral skeletons  d) Rain
**Answer: b) Over half the world's oxygen**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the ocean food webs, phytoplankton are…
a) The top hunters  b) The anchor the webs rest on  c) Not eaten by anything  d) Found only on land
**Answer: b) The anchor the webs rest on**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The process by which phytoplankton make food and oxygen is…
a) Camouflage  b) Erosion  c) Photosynthesis  d) Drifting
**Answer: c) Photosynthesis**
