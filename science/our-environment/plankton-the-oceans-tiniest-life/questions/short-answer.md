# Plankton — the ocean's tiniest life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What are phytoplankton, and how big are they?
**Answer: They are tiny plants — so small they are seen only with microscopes — and they drift on the ocean currents.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do plankton end up wherever the water takes them?
**Answer: Because they drift on the currents instead of swimming against the sea; the current is their road.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How do phytoplankton help every land animal breathe?
**Answer: By photosynthesis they produce over half of the world's oxygen, which fills the air land animals breathe.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What does it mean that phytoplankton anchor the ocean food webs?
**Answer: The sea's eating chains rest on them — small creatures eat the phytoplankton, bigger creatures eat those, and the whole web stands on that anchor.**
