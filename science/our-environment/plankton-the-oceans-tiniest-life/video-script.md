Today: **Plankton — the ocean's tiniest life** — Nauru's way, in its adopted Pacific reader: the smallest life does the biggest work.

Life began in the sea. And the sea is full of life so tiny you need a microscope to see it — the plankton. Among them are the phytoplankton: tiny plants that drift on the ocean currents, carried wherever the water goes.
Small as they are, they do two giant jobs. By photosynthesis they produce over half of all the oxygen in the world. And they anchor the ocean food webs — the eating chains of the sea rest on these drifting specks. The tiniest life holds up the whole ocean.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
