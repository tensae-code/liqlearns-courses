# Nepal's answer to climate change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: The first National Communication report to the UN climate convention; the Climate Change Policy of BS 2068; the Strategic Programme for Climate Resilience in its five areas, from mountain watersheds to endangered species; the National Adaptation Programme of September 2010, running 250 programmes as nine combined programmes as printed; local adaptation plans built on upward, inclusive, responsive and flexible principles; and improved brick kilns, improved stoves, biogas, rainwater harvesting and disaster early-warning systems.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed pp. 242–244.**
