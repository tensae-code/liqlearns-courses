Today: **Nepal's answer to climate change** — Nepal's way, from its Curriculum Development Centre books.

The first National Communication report to the UN climate convention; the Climate Change Policy of BS 2068; the Strategic Programme for Climate Resilience in its five areas, from mountain watersheds to endangered species; the National Adaptation Programme of September 2010, running 250 programmes as nine combined programmes as printed; local adaptation plans built on upward, inclusive, responsive and flexible principles; and improved brick kilns, improved stoves, biogas, rainwater harvesting and disaster early-warning systems.
