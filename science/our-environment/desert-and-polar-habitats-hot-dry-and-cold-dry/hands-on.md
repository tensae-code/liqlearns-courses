# Hands-on — Desert and polar habitats — hot dry and cold dry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: Unit 2, Ch. 4, Lesson 1 "Hot and Cold Deserts": the hot desert is a dry habitat with very little rain, cold nights and sandy or rocky soil, where desert plants keep water (thick stems, spreading or deep roots, leaves that roll up by day) and its animals need little water and mostly sleep by day and come out at night; the polar region is a cold desert near the North Pole — reindeer, seals, the polar bear and the polar fox have thick white fur that warms them and hides them, and its small plants grow close to the ground in a very short summer Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
