# Nanotechnology and the environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Nanotechnology — nanomaterials and their applications.
**Answer:** **Nanotechnology** works with materials at the nanometer scale — **nanomaterials**, built and used at sizes far below what eyes can see, where materials can behave in new ways.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Nanotechnology and biosphere sustainability — the environmental dimensions.
**Answer:** Topic 3-5 joins the applications to their **environmental dimensions**: a technology that can raise energy efficiency can serve the **biosphere's sustainability**, and a new material released into air, water and soil must also be followed there — what it does in living systems is part of its design, not an afterthought.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Nanotechnology — nanomaterials and their applications → Nanotechnology and biosphere sustainability — the environmental dimensions — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
