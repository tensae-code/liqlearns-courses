# Nanotechnology and the environment

## Do it first
A material can be rebuilt at a scale far too small to see — and come out stronger, lighter, or able to carry medicine to one exact place in the body. Is that a promise for the environment, a risk to it, or both? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Egypt's Secondary 10 way in — nanotechnology applications and biosphere sustainability (Topic 3-5, printed p. 45).

## Sub lesson 1 — Nanotechnology — nanomaterials and their applications

**Nanotechnology** works with materials at the nanometer scale — **nanomaterials**, built and used at sizes far below what eyes can see, where materials can behave in new ways. Egypt's Integrated Science names its applications plainly: nanomaterials in **energy efficiency** — doing the same work with less energy — and in **drug delivery** — carrying medicine where the body needs it. The topic stands inside the book's biosphere unit, after energy flow, respiration, excretion and the nerve impulse: a human technology judged inside the living system it enters.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nanotechnology and biosphere sustainability — the environmental dimensions

Topic 3-5 joins the applications to their **environmental dimensions**: a technology that can raise energy efficiency can serve the **biosphere's sustainability**, and a new material released into air, water and soil must also be followed there — what it does in living systems is part of its design, not an afterthought. Egypt's own way is the placement itself: nanotechnology is not taught as industry alone, but inside the biosphere unit, weighed for what it gives the living world and what it asks of it.
