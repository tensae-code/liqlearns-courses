# Hands-on — Nanotechnology and the environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the applications
Make two columns — energy efficiency and drug delivery — and place each nanotechnology use you can name or invent under one. Say what makes each a nanometer-scale job.

## Follow one material
Choose one nanomaterial use and trace its path after use: where could it go in air, water or soil, and what would you want to know before calling it safe for the biosphere?
