# Hands-on — Deforestation — cutting the forests animals live in

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: how people change the ecosystem, 1: cutting the forests — people fell forest trees to build houses and make furniture and other manufactures, and cutting the trees destroys the homes and food sources of the living things that live in the forests Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
