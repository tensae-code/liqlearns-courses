# Energy resources — resource depletion and its drivers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Resource depletion means —
a) using any resource at all  b) using a resource faster than it can be replenished, until it is exhausted  c) finding a new resource  d) processing a resource
**Answer: b) using a resource faster than it can be replenished, until it is exhausted** — the rate condition is the definition

**2.** How many drivers of depletion are named?
a) three  b) five  c) seven  d) ten
**Answer: c) seven** — overpopulation to deforestation

**3.** Which of these is one of the seven drivers?
a) photosynthesis  b) mismanagement and poor practice  c) rainfall alone  d) recycling
**Answer: b) mismanagement and poor practice** — waste in the taking itself

**4.** Extraction and processing themselves cause —
a) no harm until the resource ends  b) air, land and water pollution, noise and solid waste  c) only noise  d) only benefits
**Answer: b) air, land and water pollution, noise and solid waste** — damage before exhaustion

**5.** A resource used slower than it returns is —
a) depleting  b) enduring  c) exhausted  d) renewable by definition
**Answer: b) enduring** — the rate decides
