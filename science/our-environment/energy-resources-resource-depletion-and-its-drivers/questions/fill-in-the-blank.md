# Energy resources — resource depletion and its drivers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**10.** Use faster than ______ ends in exhaustion.
**Answer: replenishment (return)** — the rate condition

**11.** Over ______ billion people — the population driver as the guide states it.
**Answer: seven** — the guide's figure

**12.** Extraction spoils air, land and ______.
**Answer: water** — three of its media
