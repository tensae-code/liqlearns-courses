# Lesson plan — Energy resources — resource depletion and its drivers

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Energy resources — resource depletion and its drivers
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — the Grade 7 teacher guide's environment strand (§7.9) filed here as teacher evidence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** resource depletion is using a resource faster than it can be replenished until it is exhausted — driven by seven named drivers — and extraction and processing themselves pollute air, land and water (teacher evidence only; this lesson stands on the teacher guide alone)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Energy — sources, and using energy wisely (Motion, Force and Energy title): the finite/renewable split is known; now the finite side's exhaustion is studied. THIS LESSON STANDS ON TEACHER-GUIDE EVIDENCE ONLY.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Energy — sources, and using energy wisely (Motion, Force and Energy). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define depletion exactly: use faster than replenishment, until exhaustion.
2. Name the seven drivers and give a local example of each.
3. Explain how extraction and processing themselves degrade the environment.

## What you need
One local resource to audit — a woodlot, a well, a quarry or a grazing common.

## The run of the lesson (in order)

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines depletion as 'using resources' → use alone is not depletion; the rate against replenishment is the whole definition.
- Lists drivers as slogans → each driver needs its mechanism: how exactly does it speed the draining?
