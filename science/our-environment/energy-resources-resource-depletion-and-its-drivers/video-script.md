# Video script — Energy resources — resource depletion and its drivers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Teacher-evidence lesson** — the teachers' book teaches what the pupils' book leaves out.
**Depletion**: using a resource **faster than it can be replaced** — until it is **gone**.
Seven drivers drain the gauge: population, consumption, industry,
climate change, pollution, mismanagement, deforestation.
