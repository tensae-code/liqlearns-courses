# Hands-on — Energy resources — resource depletion and its drivers

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Audit one resource
Choose one local resource — firewood, water, grazing land, building sand. Ask: how fast is it used? How fast does it return? Write the two rates side by side. If use outruns return, the resource is depleting — name which of the seven drivers are at work locally.

## The gauge diary
Draw the resource as a gauge. For one week, note every household use of it and every return (rain, regrowth, delivery). Re-draw the gauge at the week's end and defend the new level with your notes.
