Today: **Bangladesh's changing environment and biodiversity** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 6 — *Science Investigative Study Class 6*, printed p. 149).

Class 6 in Bangladesh closes its Investigative Study with its own country: the changing environment of Bangladesh, studied not as an example of somewhere else but as the home case.
As the environment changes — land, water and climate shifting — the living world that depends on it changes too: species lose the conditions they need, and biodiversity, the variety of life, is where the loss is counted.
The book's way is the lesson: the same inquiry tools used in every other chapter are turned, at the end, on the learner's own ground. A country's own case study is course content, carried exactly as filed.
