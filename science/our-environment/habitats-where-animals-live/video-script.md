Today: **Habitats where animals live** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 15).

Animals live where they find food and safety. Some dig burrows or live in the soil itself; others hide and rest in bushes; and birds and climbers live in trees and in the nests built in them.
Water is a home too: fish and other water animals live their whole lives in it, feeding and raising young without leaving it.
Some animals live both on land and in water, moving between the two as they feed and rest. Bangladesh's Class 4 book sorts animal homes in exactly this order: ground and cover, water, and the two together.
