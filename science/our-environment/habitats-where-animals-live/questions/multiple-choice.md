# Habitats where animals live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Homes in the ground and in cover  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Homes in the ground and in cover**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Homes in water  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Homes in water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Homes in both — land and water  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Homes in both — land and water**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: animal habitats — burrows and soil, bushes, trees and nests, water, and animals living both on land and in water — as Bangladesh's Class 4 book sorts them. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 15  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 15**
