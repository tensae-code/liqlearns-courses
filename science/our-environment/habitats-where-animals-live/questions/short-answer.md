# Habitats where animals live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Homes in the ground and in cover.
**Answer:** Animals live where they find food and safety.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Homes in water.
**Answer:** Water is a home too: fish and other water animals live their whole lives in it, feeding and raising young without leaving it..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Homes in both — land and water.
**Answer:** Some animals live both on land and in water, moving between the two as they feed and rest.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Homes in the ground and in cover → Homes in water → Homes in both — land and water — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
