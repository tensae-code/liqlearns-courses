# Hands-on — Ecosystems — populations, communities and their surroundings

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Compound survey
Walk the school compound with a notebook in two columns: BIOTIC and ABIOTIC. List at least eight things in each column. Then pick one place — a tree, a tap, a ditch — and write its population, its community and its ecosystem in three lines.

## Frames in the air
Draw four squares, one inside the next, on paper or in dust. In the smallest write HABITAT and one kind's home. In the next write POPULATION and the count of that kind. In the next write COMMUNITY and list the other kinds. In the biggest write ECOSYSTEM and add the non-living parts.
