# Ecosystems — populations, communities and their surroundings — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A population can hold many different kinds of living things.
**Answer: False** — A population is ONE kind; many kinds together are a community.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sunlight is a biotic part of an ecosystem.
**Answer: False** — Sunlight is non-living — abiotic.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An ecosystem includes both living and non-living parts.
**Answer: True** — That togetherness is the definition.
