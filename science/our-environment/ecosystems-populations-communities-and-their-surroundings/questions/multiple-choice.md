# Ecosystems — populations, communities and their surroundings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All the frogs in one pond are —
a) a habitat  b) a population  c) a community  d) an ecosystem
**Answer: b) a population** — one kind, in one place.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A community is —
a) one kind in a place  b) all the kinds living together in a place  c) the non-living surroundings  d) a single animal's home
**Answer: b) all the kinds living together in a place** — every population together.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A habitat is —
a) the place where a kind lives and finds what it needs  b) all the kinds in a place  c) the weather  d) a group of animals
**Answer: a) the place where a kind lives and finds what it needs**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An ecosystem is the community together with —
a) only other animals  b) its non-living surroundings  c) a textbook  d) the sea
**Answer: b) its non-living surroundings** — water, air, sunlight, soil and rock included.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is an abiotic part of a pond?
a) a fish  b) a pond plant  c) the water  d) a frog
**Answer: c) the water** — abiotic means non-living.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** If a pond is drained, the community changes mainly because —
a) the animals forget the way  b) an abiotic part it depends on is gone  c) the sun stops  d) populations become habitats
**Answer: b) an abiotic part it depends on is gone**
