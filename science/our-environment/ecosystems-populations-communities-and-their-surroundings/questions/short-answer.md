# Ecosystems — populations, communities and their surroundings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one example of a population in a school compound, and say which kind it counts.
**Answer:** All of one kind in the compound, e.g. all the chickens / all the eucalyptus trees — one kind, one place.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between a habitat and a population?
**Answer:** A habitat is the PLACE a kind lives; a population is the living things of that one kind in a place.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sort these into abiotic and biotic: stone, grass, air, beetle.
**Answer:** Abiotic: stone, air. Biotic: grass, beetle.
