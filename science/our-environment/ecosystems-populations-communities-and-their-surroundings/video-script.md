# Video script — Ecosystems — populations, communities and their surroundings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An **ecosystem** is a whole living place.
One kind lives in its **habitat**.
All of that one kind together are a **population**.
All the kinds together are a **community**.
Add the water, the sun, the air and the soil —
now it is an **ecosystem**.
Living parts are **biotic**. Non-living parts are **abiotic**.
Look at your pond. Name its parts.
