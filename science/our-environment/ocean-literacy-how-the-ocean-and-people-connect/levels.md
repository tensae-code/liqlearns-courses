# Ocean literacy — how the ocean and people connect — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The one ocean and what it does, The ocean's diversity, our connection, and the unexplored), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 2 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, naming the seven principles in order, and matching each principle to one thing in the learner's own life it touches.
