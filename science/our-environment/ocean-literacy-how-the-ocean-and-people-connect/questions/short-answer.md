# Ocean literacy — how the ocean and people connect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) List the first four principles in order.
**Answer: One big ocean; the ocean shapes the Earth; the ocean drives weather and climate; the ocean made the Earth habitable.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the ocean touch a person who lives far from any shore?
**Answer: Through the weather and climate it drives, the rain it sends inland, and the food and goods it carries — the inland life still runs on the ocean.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give two directions of the ocean–people interconnection.
**Answer: The ocean feeds people, carries their goods and steadies their climate; and people change the ocean by what they put into it, take from it and build on its shores.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does ocean literacy end with the unexplored principle?
**Answer: Because it is honest: the ocean is the biggest part of our planet and most of it has never been seen, so understanding the ocean includes knowing how much is still unknown.**
