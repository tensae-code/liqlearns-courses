# Ocean literacy — how the ocean and people connect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first principle of ocean literacy is…
a) There are seven separate oceans  b) There is one big ocean  c) The ocean is small beside the land  d) Oceans never move
**Answer: b) There is one big ocean**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The ocean drives…
a) Only the tides  b) Nothing on land  c) Weather and climate  d) Only fishing
**Answer: c) Weather and climate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The ocean made the Earth…
a) Habitable — fit for life  b) Flat  c) Dry  d) Cold everywhere
**Answer: a) Habitable — fit for life**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The ocean holds…
a) Very few kinds of life  b) Only fish  c) Life only at its shores  d) A great diversity of life
**Answer: d) A great diversity of life**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) "The ocean and people are interconnected" means…
a) People may visit the ocean  b) Each changes the other  c) People own the ocean  d) The ocean needs nothing
**Answer: b) Each changes the other**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The last principle says the ocean is…
a) Fully mapped  b) Empty  c) Largely unexplored  d) Closed to study
**Answer: c) Largely unexplored**
