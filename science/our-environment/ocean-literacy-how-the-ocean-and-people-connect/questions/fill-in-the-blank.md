# Ocean literacy — how the ocean and people connect — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) There is one big _____ — the named oceans are its connected parts.
**Answer: ocean**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The ocean drives weather and _____.
**Answer: climate**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The ocean and people are _____ — each changes the other.
**Answer: interconnected**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Most of the ocean is still _____ — never seen by human eyes.
**Answer: unexplored**
