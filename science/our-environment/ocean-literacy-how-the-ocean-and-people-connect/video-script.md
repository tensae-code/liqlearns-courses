# Video script — Ocean literacy — how the ocean and people connect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Ocean literacy — how the ocean and people connect** — Canada's way: seven principles, one ocean.

There is one big ocean. It shapes the Earth itself. It drives our weather and our climate. And long ago, it made this planet habitable — fit for life at all.
The ocean holds a great diversity of life, more kinds than we can count. The ocean and people are interconnected: it feeds us, carries us and steadies our climate, and what we do reaches back into it. And for all that — the ocean is still largely unexplored. Knowing these seven things is ocean literacy.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
