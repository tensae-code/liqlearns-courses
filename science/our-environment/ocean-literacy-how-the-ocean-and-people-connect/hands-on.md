# Hands-on — Ocean literacy — how the ocean and people connect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your ocean thread
At home, list everything in one day of your life that touches the ocean — food, weather, rain, things carried by ship. Beside each, write which of the seven principles it belongs to.

## The seven-principles poster
Make a poster of the seven principles, one line each, in your own words, with one drawing for the principle that touches your home most. Put it where your family can see it.

## Ask the shore
If you can visit a shore — or picture one — stand there and say the principles out loud, pointing at what each one means right there: the one water, the shaped land, the weather coming off it, the life in it.
