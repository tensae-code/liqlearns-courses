# Hands-on — Flood danger and protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your own place for flood danger
With an adult, walk a place you know near water or below a slope. Name: where flowing water could wash or flood (a road, a house, a field); one thing that prevents or reduces that danger there; and the safe ground you would go to if flooding happened. Draw the place and mark all three — danger, prevention, safe ground — on your drawing.
