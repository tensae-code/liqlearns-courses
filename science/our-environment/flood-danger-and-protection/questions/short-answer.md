# Flood danger and protection — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The dangers of flood water.
**Answer:** Laos’s Grade 2 book (Ch. 25, printed pp. 103–104) teaches the dangers of **flowing water**: it can wash away houses and flood roads. Moving water is strong — the same flow that does useful work (the water chapters teach its uses) becomes a danger when it rises and runs where people live.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Preventing floods and landslides where we live.
**Answer:** Both books teach prevention as the community’s own work. The Grade 2 book asks how to **prevent and reduce the danger** of flowing water; the Grade 3 book teaches **preventing floods and landslides where we live** — the danger is met before the flood, in the place itself, by the people who live there.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Keeping safe when flooding happens.
**Answer:** The Grade 3 book closes the chapter with **keeping safe when flooding happens**. Prevention cannot hold every flood back, so the last knowledge is conduct during the flood itself — the behaviour that keeps people safe while the water is up.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The dangers of flood water → Preventing floods and landslides where we live → Keeping safe when flooding happens — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
