# Lesson plan — Pollution and its four kinds

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Pollution and its four kinds
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — naming the damage so it can be answered (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's main environmental problems are listed together — soil erosion, land damage, deforestation, water shortage, biodiversity loss and pollutions — and pollution itself comes in four kinds: water, soil, air and noise, each with local causes to identify and solutions to propose
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Caring for our environment — care was taught resource by resource; now the damage gets its own name and kinds.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Our Environment — Caring for our environment: soil, water and air. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite Ethiopia's main environmental problems as the book lists them.
2. Name pollution's four kinds — water, soil, air, noise — and identify each kind's causes locally, as the book sets pupils to do.
3. Propose a solution for each kind, in the book's spirit: causes found locally, solutions proposed by the pupils themselves.

## What you need
A walk around the school compound (with the class) to find each kind's local evidence.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls every environmental problem pollution → the book lists six problems; pollution is one of them, with four kinds of its own.
- Thinks noise cannot pollute → the book names noise as the fourth kind: harmful sound is pollution of the air's peace, counted with the rest.
