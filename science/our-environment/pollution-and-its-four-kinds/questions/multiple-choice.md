# Pollution and its four kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. How many kinds of pollution does the book name?**
a) two  b) four  c) six
**Answer: b) four**

**2. Which is one of the four kinds?**
a) noise  b) darkness  c) distance
**Answer: a) noise**

**3. Which stands in the book's problems list?**
a) soil erosion  b) too much homework  c) cold weather
**Answer: a) soil erosion**

**4. For each kind, pupils must —**
a) ignore it  b) identify causes locally and propose solutions  c) only memorise a song
**Answer: b) identify causes locally and propose solutions**

**5. Biodiversity loss means —**
a) more animals  b) living kinds being lost  c) cleaner water
**Answer: b) living kinds being lost**
