# Hands-on — Pollution and its four kinds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The compound survey
Survey the school compound in four teams — water, soil, air, noise — each team returning with its kind's evidence and one proposed solution, reported to the class in the book's shape.

## Before and after
Choose one small polluted spot the class may clean (dry waste only, gloves or sticks, adult-led). Photograph or draw before and after; the pair of drawings is the solution made visible.
