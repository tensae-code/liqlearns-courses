# Biodiversity of Nepal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Nepal's own biodiversity: 27th in the world, 10th in Asia, 2nd in South Asia, with 3.2% of the world's plant and 1.1% of its animal variety (the country's own case)**
