# Hands-on — Natural hazards — monitoring, prediction and protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chain the protection
Pick one natural hazard. Write its protection chain in three steps — what is monitored, how the prediction and warning are made, and what the community does on warning.

## Warning message
Draft the warning message for a tsunami warning system: what it must say — hazard, places, time, action — in as few clear words as possible.
