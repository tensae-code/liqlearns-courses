# Natural hazards — monitoring, prediction and protection — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Monitoring hazards — data watched all the time.
**Answer:** Protection begins with monitoring: instruments and observers collect data on hazards continuously, so changes are caught as they start, not as they arrive (Grade 8, 8.4.5, printed p. 187).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Prediction — from data to warning.
**Answer:** The monitored data feeds prediction: from the measurements, scientists work out what is coming, where, and when — and the prediction is issued as a warning.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Protection — communities acting on the warning.
**Answer:** A warning protects only if the community acts on it: protect communities is the lesson's third step — people who know the warning signs, know where to go, and go.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Monitoring hazards — data watched all the time → Prediction — from data to warning → Protection — communities acting on the warning — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
