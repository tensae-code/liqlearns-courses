# Seagrass beds as nursery habitat — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Seagrass leaves _____, making food from sunlight.
**Answer: photosynthesise**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Seagrass beds stay in sunlit _____ water.
**Answer: shallow**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Enhalus acoroides releases its _____ onto the surface of the sea.
**Answer: pollen**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The pollen goes out at full moon and _____ moon.
**Answer: new**
