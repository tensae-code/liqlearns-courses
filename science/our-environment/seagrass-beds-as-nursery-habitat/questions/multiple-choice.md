# Seagrass beds as nursery habitat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Seagrass is…
a) A seaweed with no flowers  b) A flowering plant that lives in the sea  c) A land grass washed in by a storm  d) A kind of coral
**Answer: b) A flowering plant that lives in the sea**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Seagrass beds stay in shallow water because…
a) Their leaves photosynthesise and need sunlight  b) The deep water is too cold for roots  c) Fish push them there  d) The moon pulls them there
**Answer: a) Their leaves photosynthesise and need sunlight**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Most of Palau's seagrasses spread by…
a) Seeds carried by birds  b) Waves throwing them on the beach  c) Their roots, creeping under the sand  d) Floating leaves
**Answer: c) Their roots, creeping under the sand**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Enhalus acoroides releases its pollen…
a) Onto dry land  b) Onto the surface of the sea  c) Into the deep water  d) Onto other plants' leaves
**Answer: b) Onto the surface of the sea**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Enhalus lets its pollen go at…
a) Every midday  b) The rainy season only  c) Low tide only  d) Full moon and new moon
**Answer: d) Full moon and new moon**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) What carries the Enhalus pollen where it needs to go?
a) The water of the sea surface  b) The wind over the land  c) Night herons  d) Mangrove crabs
**Answer: a) The water of the sea surface**
