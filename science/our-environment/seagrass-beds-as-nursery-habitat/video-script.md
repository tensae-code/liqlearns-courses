Today: **Seagrass beds as nursery habitat** — Palau's way: a meadow that grows under the sea.

Seagrass looks like grass, but it is a flowering plant that lives in the sea. Its leaves make food from sunlight, just like a plant on land — so seagrass beds stay in shallow water, where the sun can reach them. Most of Palau's seagrasses spread by their roots, creeping under the sand.
And one of them, Enhalus acoroides, does something wonderful. At full moon and at new moon, it lets its pollen go onto the surface of the sea, and the water carries the pollen away. A plant that flowers by the moon, in the sea.
