# Video script — Biodiversity of Bangladesh

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Biodiversity of Bangladesh** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 7 — *Science Investigative Study Class 7*, printed p. 10).

After teaching what biodiversity is and how it is measured, Bangladesh's Class 7 book surveys the biodiversity of Bangladesh itself — the home case, where the variety of life is the learner's own country's.
The survey does not stop at one landscape: natural diversity differs across different parts of Bangladesh — river, coast, forest and hill country each carrying a different living world.
A home survey makes biodiversity countable and checkable: the learner can go and look. Bangladesh's own biodiversity, surveyed region by region, is this lesson's content.
