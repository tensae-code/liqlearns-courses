# Biodiversity of Bangladesh — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The home case, surveyed  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The home case, surveyed**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Diversity across the country's parts  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Diversity across the country's parts**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Why survey home first  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Why survey home first**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the biodiversity of Bangladesh itself, surveyed as the home case — and the natural diversity of its different parts — in Bangladesh's Class 7 book. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 7 — *Science Investigative Study Class 7*, printed p. 10  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 7 — *Science Investigative Study Class 7*, printed p. 10**
