# Biodiversity of Bangladesh — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The home case, surveyed.
**Answer:** After teaching what biodiversity is and how it is measured, Bangladesh's Class 7 book surveys the biodiversity of Bangladesh itself — the home case, where the variety of life is the learner's own country's..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Diversity across the country's parts.
**Answer:** The survey does not stop at one landscape: natural diversity differs across different parts of Bangladesh — river, coast, forest and hill country each carrying a different living world..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Why survey home first.
**Answer:** A home survey makes biodiversity countable and checkable: the learner can go and look.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The home case, surveyed → Diversity across the country's parts → Why survey home first — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
