# Estuaries — where river meets sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An estuary is…
a) A deep-sea trench  b) Where a river meets the sea and fresh and salt water mix  c) A dry riverbed  d) A kind of coral
**Answer: b) Where a river meets the sea and fresh and salt water mix**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The saltiness of an estuary changes with…
a) The season only  b) The tide and the distance from the sea  c) The wind only  d) The moon's shape only
**Answer: b) The tide and the distance from the sea**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Nearer the sea, an estuary's water is…
a) Fresher  b) Saltier  c) Always frozen  d) Dry
**Answer: b) Saltier**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The mixing of the two waters gives the estuary…
a) No food at all  b) A rich food supply  c) Only sand  d) Poison
**Answer: b) A rich food supply**
