# Estuaries — where river meets sea — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An estuary is where a _____ meets the sea.
**Answer: river**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In an estuary, fresh water and _____ water mix.
**Answer: salt**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Saltiness changes with the tide and with _____.
**Answer: distance**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The estuary holds a rich _____ supply.
**Answer: food**
