# Estuaries — where river meets sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can one spot in an estuary be fresher at one hour and saltier at another?
**Answer: Because the tide changes the mixture there — as the sea pushes in, the spot grows saltier; as the tide falls back, the river's fresh water takes a bigger share.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does the estuary's mixing provide for living things?
**Answer: A rich food supply, gathered from both the river and the sea.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) How does saltiness change with distance in an estuary?
**Answer: Nearer the sea the water is saltier; nearer the river it is fresher — the saltiness grades between the two waters.**
