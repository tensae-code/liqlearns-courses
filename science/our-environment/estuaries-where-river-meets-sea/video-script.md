The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Estuaries — where river meets sea** — Micronesia's way: the meeting place of two waters.

Where a river reaches the sea, fresh water and salt water mix — that meeting place is an estuary. Its saltiness is never fixed: the tide changes it, and the distance from the sea changes it. And the mixing gathers a rich supply of food, so the estuary feeds many living things.
