# Hands-on — Estuaries — where river meets sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mix the two waters
Fill a clear jar half with fresh water. Pour salty water (water with salt stirred in) gently down the side and watch the two waters meet and mix. Taste-testing is not needed — say, from what you see, why one jar cannot be called simply fresh or simply salt, like the estuary.

## Saltiness by distance
Draw a river running into the sea. Mark three points — near the river, at the meeting, near the sea — and label each one's saltiness in order. Then draw an arrow for the incoming tide and say what it does to the middle point's saltiness.
