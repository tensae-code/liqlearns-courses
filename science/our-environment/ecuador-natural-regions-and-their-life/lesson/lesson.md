# Ecuador's natural regions and their life

## Do it first
One country can hold several different natural worlds inside its own borders, each with its own plants and animals. Where are your country's natural regions, and which living things belong to each? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Ecuador's own lesson — its Grade 5 Ciencias Naturales locates Ecuador's natural regions and names the flora and fauna most representative of each.

## Sub lesson 1 — Locating Ecuador's natural regions

Ecuador's Grade 5 book **locates the country's natural regions**: each region is placed — where it lies in the country — so the regions together make a map of Ecuador's natural worlds. Locating comes first: a region's life can only be named once the region itself is placed.

## Sub lesson 2 — The flora and fauna most representative of each region

For each region the book names the **flora and fauna most representative of it** — the plants and animals that stand for that region, the ones that belong to it characteristically. Region by region, the representative plants and animals are what make each natural region a different living world from its neighbours.

- **Locating Ecuador's natural regions** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.
- **The flora and fauna most representative of each region** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.
