# Video script — Ecuador's natural regions and their life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Ecuador's natural regions and their life** — Ecuador's way, from its Grade 5 Ciencias Naturales (printed p. 112).

Ecuador's Grade 5 book locates the country's natural regions: each region is placed — where it lies in the country — so the regions together make a map of Ecuador's natural worlds. Locating comes first: a region's life can only be named once the region itself is placed.

For each region the book names the flora and fauna most representative of it — the plants and animals that stand for that region, the ones that belong to it characteristically. Region by region, the representative plants and animals are what make each natural region a different living world from its neighbours.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
