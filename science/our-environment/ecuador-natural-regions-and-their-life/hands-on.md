# Hands-on — Ecuador's natural regions and their life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A regions-and-life chart of Ecuador
Draw Ecuador's natural regions as the book locates them, as a chart with one space per region. In each space write the flora and fauna the book names as most representative of that region. Say, for one region, why its representative plants and animals belong there and not in the neighbouring region.
