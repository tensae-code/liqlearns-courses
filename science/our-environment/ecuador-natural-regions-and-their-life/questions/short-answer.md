# Ecuador's natural regions and their life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Locating Ecuador's natural regions.
**Answer:** Ecuador's Grade 5 book **locates the country's natural regions**: each region is placed — where it lies in the country — so the regions together make a map of Ecuador's natural worlds. Locating comes first: a region's life can only be named once the region itself is placed.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The flora and fauna most representative of each region.
**Answer:** For each region the book names the **flora and fauna most representative of it** — the plants and animals that stand for that region, the ones that belong to it characteristically. Region by region, the representative plants and animals are what make each natural region a different living world from its neighbours.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Locating Ecuador's natural regions → The flora and fauna most representative of each region — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
