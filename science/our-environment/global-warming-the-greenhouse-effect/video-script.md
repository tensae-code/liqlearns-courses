# Video script — Global warming — the greenhouse effect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A parked car in the sun grows **hot** inside.
Light gets **in**. Heat cannot get **out**.
Gases do the same for the **Earth**.
Too much gas — the world **warms**.
Seas rise. Rain fails here, floods there.
Burn less. Cut less. **Plant more.**
