# Global warming — the greenhouse effect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain the greenhouse effect in the parked car's words, in two sentences.
**Answer:** Like a parked car's windows, greenhouse gases let sunlight in but hold heat that would escape, so the inside — the Earth — warms; thickening the gas layer holds still more heat.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the two sources of human carbon dioxide and their rough shares.
**Answer:** Burning fossil fuels — about three-quarters; deforestation — most of the rest.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three effects of global warming from the lesson's list.
**Answer:** Any three of: sea-level rise, uneven rainfall, flooding, drought, food and water shortage, extinctions.
