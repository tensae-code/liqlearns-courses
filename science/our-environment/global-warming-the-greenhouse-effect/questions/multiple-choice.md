# Global warming — the greenhouse effect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the parked-car comparison, the Earth's greenhouse gases work like the car's —
a) wheels  b) windows/glass that lets light in and holds heat  c) engine  d) seats
**Answer: b) windows/glass that lets light in and holds heat**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is NOT one of the five greenhouse gases named? 
a) methane  b) nitrous oxide  c) oxygen  d) water vapour
**Answer: c) oxygen** — the five: CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide, water vapour, CFCs.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** CFCs are —
a) a kind of tree  b) chlorofluorocarbons, one of the greenhouse gases  c) a fuel  d) rain clouds
**Answer: b) chlorofluorocarbons, one of the greenhouse gases**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** About three-quarters of human carbon dioxide comes from —
a) breathing  b) burning fossil fuels  c) oceans  d) volcanoes
**Answer: b) burning fossil fuels** — most of the rest comes largely from deforestation.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a listed effect of global warming? 
a) sea-level rise  b) longer nights  c) more moons  d)  colder summers everywhere
**Answer: a) sea-level rise** — also uneven rainfall, flooding, drought, food and water shortage, extinctions.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A reduction that answers the deforestation source is —
a) burning more wood  b) planting trees and cutting fewer  c) using more coal  d) draining lakes
**Answer: b) planting trees and cutting fewer**
