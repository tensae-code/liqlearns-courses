# Global warming — the greenhouse effect — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Greenhouse gases are wholly harmful and life would be better with none at all.
**Answer: False** — A natural share keeps Earth warm enough for life; warming grows when the layer thickens beyond that share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Deforestation adds to the carbon dioxide problem.
**Answer: True** — Cut forests stop taking carbon in; burning/rotting sends it out.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Global warming can bring both flooding in some places and drought in others.
**Answer: True** — Uneven rainfall is on the effects list.
