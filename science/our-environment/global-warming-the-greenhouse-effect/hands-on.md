# Hands-on — Global warming — the greenhouse effect

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Jar in the sun
Put a thermometer or your hand's judgement to work: set a clear glass jar upside down in the sun beside an open patch of the same ground. After some minutes feel the air/soil inside and outside. Write the parked-car sentence for your jar: what let light in, what held heat, in what way is the jar like the Earth — and in what way not?

## Source hunt
List every burning thing your home and street use in a day (stove, lamp, vehicle, generator). Mark which burn fossil fuels. Beside the list, write the book's share sentence: three-quarters of human carbon dioxide comes from this family of burning — and one reduction your home could honestly make.
