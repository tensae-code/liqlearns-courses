# Hands-on — Habitats around the world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw a simple world map or a row of five boxes — desert, forest, wetland, ocean, polar. In each, draw or name one plant and one animal that live there, and write the habitat's hardest problem (no water, no light, cold…) beside them.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of habitats around the world — the world's great habitats — desert, forest, wetland, ocean and polar lands — each offer a different mix of water, temperature and food, and each is home to plants and animals fitted to exactly that mix. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
