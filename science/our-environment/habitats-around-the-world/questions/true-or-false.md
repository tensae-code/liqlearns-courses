# Habitats around the world — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A desert habitat is hot and short of water; its plants store water and its animals come out at night.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Forest and wetland is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** The ocean is the largest habitat of all: salty water without edges, lit near the surface and dark below.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
