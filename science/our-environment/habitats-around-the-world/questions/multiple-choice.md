# Habitats around the world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about habitats around the world?
a) The world's great habitats — desert, forest, wetland, ocean and polar lands — each offer a different mix of water, temperature and food, and each is home to plants and animals fitted to exactly that mix.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The world's great habitats — desert, forest, wetland, ocean and polar lands — each offer a different mix of water, temperature and food, and each is home to plants and animals fitted to exactly that mix.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about dry and cold extremes?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A desert habitat is hot and short of water; its plants store water and its animals come out at night.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A desert habitat is hot and short of water; its plants store water and its animals come out at night.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about forest and wetland?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Forests give shade, rain and layers of living space from floor to canopy, so they hold more kinds of life than almost anywhere.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Forests give shade, rain and layers of living space from floor to canopy, so they hold more kinds of life than almost anywhere.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about the ocean habitat?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) The ocean is the largest habitat of all: salty water without edges, lit near the surface and dark below.
**Answer: d) The ocean is the largest habitat of all: salty water without edges, lit near the surface and dark below.**
