# Habitats around the world — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the world's great habitats — desert, forest, wetland, ocean and polar lands — each offer a different mix of water, temperature and food, and each is home to plants and animals fitted to exactly that mix.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
