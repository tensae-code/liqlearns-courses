# Hands-on — Environmental change — causes and effects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Think of one place you know well — a field, a street, a riverside. List two changes it has seen: one natural, one made by people. For each, write one effect on the living things there.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of environmental change — causes and effects — the environment changes from natural causes — floods, droughts, storms and shifting seasons — and from human activity, and every change lands on rainfall, temperature and the habitats living things depend on. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
