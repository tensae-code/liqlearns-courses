# Environmental change — causes and effects — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the environment changes from natural causes — floods, droughts, storms and shifting seasons — and from human activity, and every change lands on rainfall, temperature and the habitats living things depend on.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
