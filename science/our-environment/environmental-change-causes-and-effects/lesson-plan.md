# Lesson plan — Environmental change — causes and effects

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Environmental change — causes and effects
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the environment changes from natural causes — floods, droughts, storms and shifting seasons — and from human activity, and every change lands on rainfall, temperature and the habitats living things depend on.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier lessons of its title, and builds the understanding of this topic at the level its source book teaches it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
