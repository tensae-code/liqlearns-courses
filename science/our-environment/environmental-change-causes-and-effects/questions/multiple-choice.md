# Environmental change — causes and effects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about environmental change — causes and effects?
a) The environment changes from natural causes — floods, droughts, storms and shifting seasons — and from human activity, and every change lands on rainfall, temperature and the habitats living things depend on.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The environment changes from natural causes — floods, droughts, storms and shifting seasons — and from human activity, and every change lands on rainfall, temperature and the habitats living things depend on.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about nature changes the environment?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Natural disasters and slow natural shifts remake a place: a flood drowns fields and homes, a drought dries ponds and crops, a storm tears trees and roofs, and the seasons themselves change what grows and what animals do.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Natural disasters and slow natural shifts remake a place: a flood drowns fields and homes, a drought dries ponds and crops, a storm tears trees and roofs, and the seasons themselves change what grows and what animals do.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about people change the environment?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Human activity changes it too: cutting forests, building towns, farming, burning fuels and throwing waste all alter the land, water and air around them.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Human activity changes it too: cutting forests, building towns, farming, burning fuels and throwing waste all alter the land, water and air around them.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about effects reach everything living?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) However it starts, environmental change shows in the same places: rainfall patterns shift, temperatures rise or fall, rivers and soils change, and habitats — the homes of plants and animals — shrink, move or vanish.
**Answer: d) However it starts, environmental change shows in the same places: rainfall patterns shift, temperatures rise or fall, rivers and soils change, and habitats — the homes of plants and animals — shrink, move or vanish.**
