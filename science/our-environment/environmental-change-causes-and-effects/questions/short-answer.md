# Environmental change — causes and effects — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: nature changes the environment.
**Answer: Natural disasters and slow natural shifts remake a place: a flood drowns fields and homes, a drought dries ponds and crops, a storm tears trees and roofs, and the seasons themselves change what grows and what animals do. These changes come whether people act or not.**

**2.** Explain: people change the environment.
**Answer: Human activity changes it too: cutting forests, building towns, farming, burning fuels and throwing waste all alter the land, water and air around them. Some human changes are useful and planned; others damage the very surroundings the changers live in.**

**3.** Explain: effects reach everything living.
**Answer: However it starts, environmental change shows in the same places: rainfall patterns shift, temperatures rise or fall, rivers and soils change, and habitats — the homes of plants and animals — shrink, move or vanish. Living things, people included, must adapt to the new conditions or suffer them.**
