# Environmental change — causes and effects — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining environmental change — causes and effects, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: The environment changes from natural causes — floods, droughts, storms and shifting seasons — and from human activity, and every change lands on rainfall, temperature and the habitats living things depend on. Natural disasters and slow natural shifts remake a place: a flood drowns fields and homes, a drought dries ponds and crops, a storm tears trees and roofs, and the seasons themselves change what grows and what animals do. These changes come whether people act or not. Human activity changes it too: cutting forests, building towns, farming, burning fuels and throwing waste all alter the land, water and air around them. Some human changes are useful and planned; others damage the very surroundings the changers live in. However it starts, environmental change shows in the same places: rainfall patterns shift, temperatures rise or fall, rivers and soils change, and habitats — the homes of plants and animals — shrink, move or vanish. Living things, people included, must adapt to the new conditions or suffer them.**
