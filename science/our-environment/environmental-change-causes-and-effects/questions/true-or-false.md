# Environmental change — causes and effects — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Natural disasters and slow natural shifts remake a place: a flood drowns fields and homes, a drought dries ponds and crops, a storm tears trees and roofs, and the seasons themselves change what grows and what animals do.
**Answer: True**

**2.** People change the environment is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** However it starts, environmental change shows in the same places: rainfall patterns shift, temperatures rise or fall, rivers and soils change, and habitats — the homes of plants and animals — shrink, move or vanish.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
