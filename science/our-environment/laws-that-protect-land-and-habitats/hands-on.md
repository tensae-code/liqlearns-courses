# Hands-on — Laws that protect land and habitats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: environmental law in the same lesson: no building begins before an environmental-impact study; if the site is the habitat of an endangered animal and building would harm it badly, permission is refused — and the National Centre for Environmental Compliance watches over such protection in the Kingdom Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
