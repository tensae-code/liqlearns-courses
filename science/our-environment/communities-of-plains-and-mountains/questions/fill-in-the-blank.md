# Communities of plains and mountains — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The plains have _____ soil and open meadows.
**Answer: fertile**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Broadleaf forest grows below the _____ in the mountains.
**Answer: conifers**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The _____ is the plains bird, camouflaged against the ground.
**Answer: partridge**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ shrubs grow highest, above the conifers.
**Answer: Alpine**
