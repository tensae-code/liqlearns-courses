# Communities of plains and mountains — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The plains community stands on…
a) Alpine shrubs  b) Fertile soil and meadows  c) Conifers  d) Rock and snow
**Answer: b) Fertile soil and meadows**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The partridge is protected by…
a) Warning colours  b) Camouflage  c) Migration  d) Hibernation
**Answer: b) Camouflage**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Which grows highest in the mountains?
a) Broadleaf forest  b) Meadows  c) Alpine shrubs  d) Water lilies
**Answer: c) Alpine shrubs**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Wild goats and eagles belong to…
a) The plains  b) The mountains  c) Fresh water  d) The sea
**Answer: b) The mountains**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Edelweiss is a plant of…
a) The plains  b) The high mountains  c) The swamp  d) The sea surface
**Answer: b) The high mountains**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The one thing changing under both communities is the land's…
a) Colour  b) Height  c) Name  d) Owner
**Answer: b) Height**
