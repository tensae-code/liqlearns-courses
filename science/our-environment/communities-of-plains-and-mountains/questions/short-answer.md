# Communities of plains and mountains — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the book's plains community.
**Answer:** Fertile soil and meadows, with small mammals and the camouflaged partridge.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the book's mountain community, in height order.
**Answer:** Broadleaf forest, then conifers, then alpine shrubs; edelweiss among the high plants, wild goats on the slopes and eagles above.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is the partridge camouflaged, in the book's placing?
**Answer:** Because the plains are open and flat — there is nowhere to hide except by matching the ground.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What decides whether a place holds the plains community or the mountain community?
**Answer:** The land's height and the conditions that go with it — soil, plants and shelter all change as the land rises.
