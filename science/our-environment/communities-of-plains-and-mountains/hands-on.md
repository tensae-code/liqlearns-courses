# Hands-on — Communities of plains and mountains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Low to high strip
Draw a long strip: flat plain at the left, rising to a high mountain at the right. Place the book's plants and animals at their heights — meadows and partridge low; broadleaf, then conifers, then alpine shrubs, edelweiss, goats and eagles high.

## Your own land
Name the land nearest your home — plain, hill or mountain. List its community as far as you know it, and say which of the book's two communities it is closer to, and why.
