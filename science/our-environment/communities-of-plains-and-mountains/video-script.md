# Video script — Communities of plains and mountains

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Communities of plains and mountains** — Bulgaria's way: read the land low, then read it high.

On the plains: fertile soil, open meadows, small mammals — and the partridge, hidden by matching the ground.

In the mountains: broadleaf forest, then conifers, then alpine shrubs; edelweiss high up, wild goats on the slopes, eagles above them all.

Now you. Start low and climb: name the community as you rise. Say it back — in your own words.
