# Hands-on — Studying populations — marking and recapture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: how populations are studied: to follow the monarch butterfly's migration, students mark each butterfly caught, release it, and count how many of the recaptured ones carry marks — the mark–release–recapture way of estimating a population without counting every individual Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
