# The Gaia hypothesis — the living Earth

Course: Science (General Science) — Unit 11: Our Environment.

## What this lesson teaches

One thing: the Gaia hypothesis — the idea that the Earth keeps itself in balance, living things and the non-living world together holding the planet's temperature, air and oceans steady, the way a body holds its own temperature — and that we are part of that system, not outside it.

This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons. It is a single idea, taught whole: the old Mother Earth visions; Lovelock and Margulis's scientific form of the idea; the hypothesis and its three postulates; Daisyworld, the model that answers the critics; "Gaia's revenge" — today's environmental crisis read through the hypothesis; and the environmental ethics the hypothesis inspires.

## Sub lessons

None — this lesson is taught whole, in one sitting.
