# The Gaia hypothesis — the living Earth — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining the Gaia hypothesis, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: The Gaia hypothesis says the Earth keeps itself in balance: living things and the non-living world together hold the planet's temperature, air and oceans steady, the way a body holds its own temperature. Lovelock and Margulis proposed it after noticing the air holds far more oxygen and far less carbon dioxide than chemistry alone would leave there — life keeps it that way. Daisyworld shows the balance needs no planner: black daisies warm a planet, white daisies cool it, and their competition holds the temperature steady as the sun brightens.**
