# The Gaia hypothesis — the living Earth — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Many peoples have seen the Earth as a living mother — Gaia to the Greeks, Pachamama in the Andes, Yemayá in Yoruba belief.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Gaia hypothesis says the planet's balance is kept by non-living things alone, and living things play no part in it.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches that living things and the non-living world regulate the planet together.**

**3.** Daisyworld shows a planet's temperature can be held steady by growth and competition alone, with no planner.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Homeostasis is a word used only for planets and never for the human body.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches homeostasis is the same self-holding seen in a body keeping its temperature, here at planetary scale.**

**5.** The book says roughly 20% of humanity commits about 80% of the aggressions against the planet.
**Answer: True**
