# The Gaia hypothesis — the living Earth — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The self-holding of a steady internal state — in a body or in the whole planet — is called _____.**
**Answer: homeostasis**

**2. In Daisyworld, _____ daisies absorb sunlight and warm the planet.**
**Answer: black**

**3. Lovelock proposed the Gaia hypothesis working with the biologist Lynn _____.**
**Answer: Margulis**

**4. The Andean name for the living Mother Earth is _____.**
**Answer: Pachamama**
