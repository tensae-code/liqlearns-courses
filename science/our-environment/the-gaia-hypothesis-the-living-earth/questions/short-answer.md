# The Gaia hypothesis — the living Earth — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the three postulates of the Gaia hypothesis.
**Answer: (1) Living things have the capacity to control their planetary environment, and they do. (2) The environment evolves by feedback — the system responds to change and holds its balance as conditions shift. (3) The system's working keeps a stable environment for the biosphere — conditions under which life can continue.**

**2.** Explain: how Daisyworld answers the critics.
**Answer: Critics such as Doolittle objected that planetary regulation would need the planet to plan or foresee, and nature does not plan. Lovelock and Watson's Daisyworld model answers with black daisies that warm the planet and white daisies that cool it: as the sun brightens, the white ones spread and the black ones shrink, so growth and competition alone — with no planner — keep the temperature nearly constant.**

**3.** Explain: the ethics the hypothesis inspires.
**Answer: If we are participants in the planet's self-regulation, not a species standing apart from the world, the human relation with Gaia must be rethought: society educates itself in planetary homeostasis, and economies and cultures are changed to attend to real needs simply instead of centring on profit and constant growth — Bookchin's social ecology is the book's example.**
