# The Gaia hypothesis — the living Earth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the Gaia hypothesis say?
a) The Earth keeps itself in balance: living things and the non-living world together hold the planet's temperature, air and oceans steady
b) The Earth's conditions never change at all
c) Only the oceans regulate the planet; the air plays no part
d) Living things have no effect on the planet's air
**Answer: a) The Earth keeps itself in balance: living things and the non-living world together hold the planet's temperature, air and oceans steady**

**2.** Who gave the Gaia idea its scientific form in the 1970s?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) James Lovelock, working with Lynn Margulis
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) James Lovelock, working with Lynn Margulis**

**3.** What was the observation about the Earth's air that started the hypothesis?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The air holds far more oxygen and far less carbon dioxide than chemistry alone would leave there — living things keep it that way
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The air holds far more oxygen and far less carbon dioxide than chemistry alone would leave there — living things keep it that way**

**4.** In Daisyworld, what happens as the sun slowly brightens?
a) White daisies spread and black daisies shrink back, so the planet's temperature stays nearly constant
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) White daisies spread and black daisies shrink back, so the planet's temperature stays nearly constant**

**5.** What does the book call "Gaia's revenge"?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) The pushback of a self-regulating planet pushed too hard — climate change, desertification, scarce and contaminated fresh water, and lost species landing on everyone
**Answer: d) The pushback of a self-regulating planet pushed too hard — climate change, desertification, scarce and contaminated fresh water, and lost species landing on everyone**
