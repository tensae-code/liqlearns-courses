# The Gaia hypothesis — the living Earth — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-idea lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the Gaia hypothesis: the Earth keeps itself in balance — living things and the non-living world together hold the planet's temperature, air and oceans steady — and we are part of that system, not outside it) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
