## Sub lesson 1 — Sunlight in the ocean

Sunlight in the ocean: how far light penetrates; primary productivity measured by chlorophyll A across days; role-play of beach zones at low and high tide (supralittoral and breaking zones) and their species.

## Sub lesson 2 — Marine biology

Marine biology: light intensity and the other physicochemical parameters rule life in the oceans; the ocean's zones and the adaptations of the organisms in each; pelagic organisms controlling buoyancy to save swimming energy; phytoplankton as primary producers through photosynthesis, grazed by zooplankton; benthic life on the bottoms; coral bleaching and human impacts — more than 80 % of the waste produced on land ends up in the sea.
