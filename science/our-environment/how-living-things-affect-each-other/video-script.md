# Video script — How living things affect each other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Living things **meet**. Every meeting can be read.
A hunter eats its prey — **predation**: plus, minus.
Two need the same food — **competition**: minus, minus.
A parasite feeds on its host — **parasitism**: plus, minus.
Both gain — **mutualism**: plus, plus.
One gains, one feels nothing — **commensalism**.
One is harmed, one feels nothing — **amensalism**.
Read the marks. The marks give the name.
