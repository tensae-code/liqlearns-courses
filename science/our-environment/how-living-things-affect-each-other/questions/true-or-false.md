# How living things affect each other — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In competition, one side gains and the other loses.
**Answer: False** — In competition BOTH sides are harmed (− / −) — they share a limited need.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In mutualism both partners gain.
**Answer: True** — + / + is the mark pair for mutualism.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A parasite usually kills its host at once, like a predator.
**Answer: False** — A parasite feeds on a living host and harms it without killing it at once.
