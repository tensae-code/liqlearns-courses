# How living things affect each other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A hawk kills and eats a chick. This is —
a) mutualism  b) competition  c) predation  d) commensalism
**Answer: c) predation** — the hawk gains, the chick loses its life.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Two bean plants crowding each other for light both grow poorly. This is —
a) competition  b) parasitism  c) mutualism  d) amensalism
**Answer: a) competition** — both lose: − / −.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Lice feeding on a person's head is parasitism because —
a) both gain  b) the lice gain and the person is harmed  c) nobody is affected  d) the person gains
**Answer: b) the lice gain and the person is harmed** — + / −, and the host is not killed at once.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bees taking nectar while pollinating flowers is —
a) parasitism  b) mutualism  c) amensalism  d) predation
**Answer: b) mutualism** — both gain: + / +.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Egrets catching insects stirred up by elephants, the elephants unaffected, is —
a) commensalism  b) competition  c) predation  d) mutualism
**Answer: a) commensalism** — + / 0.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Grass shaded out under a big tree, the tree unaffected, is —
a) mutualism  b) parasitism  c) amensalism  d) commensalism
**Answer: c) amensalism** — − / 0: one harmed, one unaffected.
