# How living things affect each other — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the +/−/0 marks for each partner in predation, and name the partners.
**Answer:** Predator +, prey −.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A bird eats insects from a cow's back and the cow is also relieved of the insects. Before answering, a classmate says commensalism. What must you check?
**Answer:** Whether the cow gains too: if the cow is helped (insects removed), it is mutualism (+/+); if truly unaffected, commensalism (+/0) — the marks decide.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one local example of parasitism and say who is parasite and who is host.
**Answer:** E.g. lice (parasite) on a human (host); or a tick on a cow.
