# Hands-on — How living things affect each other

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Pair hunt
Find or recall six pairs of living things around home or school. For each pair write the two marks (+, − or 0 for each partner) and the interaction's name. Trade lists with a companion and defend any pair you read differently.

## Marks in the air
A companion names a pair — 'tick and cow', 'bird and fruit tree', 'two goats at one trough'. Hold up fingers: thumb up for gain, thumb down for loss, flat hand for unaffected — one hand for each partner — then say the name.
