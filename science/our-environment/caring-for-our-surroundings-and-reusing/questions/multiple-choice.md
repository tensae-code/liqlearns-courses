# Caring for our surroundings, and reusing what we throw away — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is one of the guide's five care ways?**
a) burning forests  b) preventing forest fire  c) dumping waste in rivers
**Answer: b) preventing forest fire**

**2. A discarded bottle reused as a plant pot is an example of —**
a) pollution  b) reuse — a useful thing made from a thrown-away material  c) littering
**Answer: b) reuse — a useful thing made from a thrown-away material**

**3. Creating awareness in the community is —**
a) not part of care  b) one of the five care ways  c) a waste of time
**Answer: b) one of the five care ways**

**4. A cleanliness campaign is care done —**
a) alone in secret  b) in group work, together  c) only by teachers
**Answer: b) in group work, together**
