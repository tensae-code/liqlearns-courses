# Lesson plan — Caring for our surroundings, and reusing what we throw away

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Caring for our surroundings, and reusing what we throw away
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — the carer's habits and the waste craft, from the guides (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** our resources are being badly damaged, so we protect them: planting and tending trees, disposing of waste properly, preventing forest fire, cleanliness campaigns, and community awareness — and materials that can be reused or recycled are told apart, and useful things imagined from discarded materials; waste at home is disposed of carefully, and reuse assessed in group work
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Caring for our environment: soil, water and air — the manual known; this lesson adds the community habits and the reuse craft. It rests on teacher-guide evidence only; its sources say so plainly.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Our Environment — Caring for our environment: soil, water and air. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the five care ways from the Grade 2 guide: planting and tending trees, disposing of waste properly, preventing forest fire, cleanliness campaigns, creating community awareness.
2. Tell reusable and recyclable materials apart, and imagine a useful thing made from a discarded material.
3. Say the Grade 1 guide's waste rule: homes and surroundings produce leftover waste every day that must be disposed of carefully — and keep the school clean in group work, presenting how waste can be reused.

## What you need
Discarded things to reimagine: a bottle, a tin, a tyre, a sack; a compound to clean in group work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Throws everything 'away' → there is no 'away' in the guide's world: waste is disposed of carefully, and the reusable is reused.
- Plants trees but never tends → the guide's pair is planting AND tending; the tending is most of the work.
