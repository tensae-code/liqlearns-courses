# Hands-on — Caring for our surroundings, and reusing what we throw away

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reuse workshop
Hold the workshop the competency demands: a table of discarded things, each reimagined on a label — USEFUL AS: … The best three are made for real.

## Campaign day
Run the cleanliness campaign on the school compound or a shared street, group-work style: areas assigned, waste disposed of properly, results presented.

## Fire-prevention enquiry
Ask a farmer or elder: when does fire threaten the forest or fields here, and what does the community do before the season? Write the prevention list.
