# Responsible tourism and ecotourism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** ______ is a responsible journey to natural areas that protects the environment.
**Answer: Ecotourism**

**2.** The Hajj is named in the book as a symbol of ______ travel.
**Answer: religious**

**3.** Take your pack ______ and your steps ______.
**Answer: light; slow**
