# Responsible tourism and ecotourism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Tourism, as Iran's book defines it, is…
a) Any walk to school  b) Travel and activity outside one's living place, for at least one day  c) Working abroad forever  d) Watching travel films
**Answer: b) Travel and activity outside one's living place, for at least one day**

**2.** Ecotourism is…
a) The fastest travel  b) A responsible journey to natural areas that protects the environment  c) Travel with the most luggage  d) Visiting cities only
**Answer: b) A responsible journey to natural areas that protects the environment**

**3.** Tourism against sustainability principles is…
a) Still ecotourism  b) Not ecotourism  c) The best kind  d) Protected by law
**Answer: b) Not ecotourism**

**4.** Iran's protected areas and wildlife refuges exist to…
a) Stop all travel everywhere  b) Preserve natural areas and support wildlife habitats  c) Build hotels  d) Replace villages
**Answer: b) Preserve natural areas and support wildlife habitats**

**5.** The book's counsel for nature journeys is…
a) Pack heavy, step fast  b) Take your pack light and your steps slow  c) Leave litter as a sign  d) Never return
**Answer: b) Take your pack light and your steps slow**
