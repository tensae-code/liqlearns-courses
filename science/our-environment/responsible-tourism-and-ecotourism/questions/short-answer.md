# Responsible tourism and ecotourism — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the test that separates ecotourism from other tourism.
**Answer: Whether the travel protects the environment and fits its sustainability principles — if it conflicts with them, it is not ecotourism.**

**2.** Why does the book end with villages whose tourism fits their culture and customs?
**Answer: Because that is the successful pattern: visitors come, the place earns and is honoured rather than used up — tourism the place can survive and welcome again.**
