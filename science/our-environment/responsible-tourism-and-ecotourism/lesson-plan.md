# Lesson plan — Responsible tourism and ecotourism

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Responsible tourism and ecotourism
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (Our Environment) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** travelling in a way that protects the places travelled to — Iran's way, from its Grade 11 Human and Environment book's closing lesson: what tourism is and what it brings, the Hajj named as a symbol of religious travel, ecotourism defined as a responsible journey to natural areas that protects the environment, Iran's protected areas, and a successful village experience of tourism that fits the local culture and customs
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

## Sub lessons
1. **Tourism — travelling, and what it brings** — Iran's book defines tourism plainly: travel and activity done outside one's living place, for at least one day. Our country Iran has many places for touring, and tourism brings real benefits — to the traveller's own life and to the places visited. The book also reaches back: Iran has a long antiquity in tourism, and the Hajj stands in it as a symbol of religious travel.
2. **Ecotourism — travel that protects** — Against ordinary travel the book sets ecotourism: a responsible journey to natural areas that protects the environment — and it states the test sharply: any tourism in conflict with the environment's sustainability principles is not an instance of ecotourism. How visitors treat an attraction is the whole question; two approaches follow from it, one that uses a place up and one that keeps it.
3. **Protected places, and travel that fits** — Iran keeps protected areas — set aside by its Environment Protection Organisation since 1353 to preserve natural areas, with wildlife refuges among them — and the lesson closes with success: villages whose tourism fits their culture and customs, and the book's own counsel for the road — take your pack light and your steps slow.
