Today: **Responsible tourism and ecotourism** — Iran's way.

Iran's book defines tourism plainly: travel and activity done outside one's living place, for at least one day. Against ordinary travel the book sets ecotourism: a responsible journey to natural areas that protects the environment — and it states the test sharply: any tourism in conflict with the environment's sustainability principles is not an instance of ecotourism. Iran keeps protected areas — set aside by its Environment Protection Organisation since 1353 to preserve natural areas, with wildlife refuges among them — and the lesson closes with success: villages whose tourism fits their culture and customs, and the book's own counsel for the road — take your pack light and your steps slow..
