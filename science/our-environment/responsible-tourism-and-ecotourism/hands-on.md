# Hands-on — Responsible tourism and ecotourism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two approaches at one place
Choose a real attraction — a park, a shrine, a river bank. Write two visitor plans for it: one that uses it up, one that keeps it. Under each, write what the place looks like after a hundred visits.

## Pack light, step slow
Pack a bag for a day in nature twice: once with everything disposable, once light and reusable. Weigh or count both, and list what the lighter pack leaves behind — nothing.
