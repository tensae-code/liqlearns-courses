# Hands-on — Natural disasters — flood, landslide, fire — and keeping safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Walk your home ground (in fact or in mind) and find one place for each disaster: where water would flood, which slope could slide, where fire could start. For each, write the prevention that fits it.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of natural disasters — flood, landslide, fire — and keeping safe — flood, landslide and fire are natural disasters that damage homes, land and lives — and much of their damage can be prevented by how people build, plant and prepare: embankments against floods, trees holding slopes, and care with fire. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
