# Natural disasters — flood, landslide, fire — and keeping safe — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (flood, landslide and fire are natural disasters that damage homes, land and lives — and much of their damage can be prevented by how people build, plant and prepare: embankments against floods, trees holding slopes, and care with fire.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
