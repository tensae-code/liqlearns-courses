# Natural disasters — flood, landslide, fire — and keeping safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about natural disasters — flood, landslide, fire — and keeping safe?
a) Flood, landslide and fire are natural disasters that damage homes, land and lives — and much of their damage can be prevented by how people build, plant and prepare: embankments against floods, trees holding slopes, and care with fire.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Flood, landslide and fire are natural disasters that damage homes, land and lives — and much of their damage can be prevented by how people build, plant and prepare: embankments against floods, trees holding slopes, and care with fire.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about flood?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) When heavy rain swells rivers beyond their banks, water covers land, homes and crops.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) When heavy rain swells rivers beyond their banks, water covers land, homes and crops.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about landslide?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) On steep, rain-soaked slopes the soil itself slides, burying roads, fields and houses below.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) On steep, rain-soaked slopes the soil itself slides, burying roads, fields and houses below.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about fire?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Fire disasters start small — a stove, a spark, dry grass near a flame — and run when fuel and wind feed them.
**Answer: d) Fire disasters start small — a stove, a spark, dry grass near a flame — and run when fuel and wind feed them.**
