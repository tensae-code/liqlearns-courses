# Natural disasters — flood, landslide, fire — and keeping safe — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** When heavy rain swells rivers beyond their banks, water covers land, homes and crops.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Landslide is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** Fire disasters start small — a stove, a spark, dry grass near a flame — and run when fuel and wind feed them.
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
