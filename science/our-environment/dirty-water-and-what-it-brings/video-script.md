# Video script — Dirty water and what it brings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Urine, wash-water, factory flow — **liquid waste**.
Smell. Colds. Flies. Mosquitoes. Spoiled beauty. Flood poison.
Five harms from one puddle.
Drain it, and the web collapses.
