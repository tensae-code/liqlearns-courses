# Hands-on — Dirty water and what it brings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The concept-web, for real
Draw the book's web on the largest paper you have: centre LIQUID WASTE, five spokes, a small drawing on each spoke. It should teach a visitor without you speaking.

## Mosquito census
Count mosquito-breeding standing waters within fifty steps of your home. Each one found is a spoke of the web with an address.

## Beauty restored
Choose the ugliest waste corner nearby (with leave) and clean it: drained, swept, container placed. Photograph or draw before and after — spoiled beauty, unspoiled.
