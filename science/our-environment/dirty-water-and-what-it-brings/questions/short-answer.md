# Dirty water and what it brings — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Name three harms from the book's web.**
**Answer: bad smell; respiratory illness (colds, asthma); breeding places for flies and mosquitoes; spoiled beauty; flood-borne pollution of water sources (any three)**

**2. Name the liquid wastes of the book.**
**Answer: urine, wash-water, effluent from homes and factories**
