# Dirty water and what it brings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is liquid waste?**
a) banana peelings  b) wash-water  c) paper
**Answer: b) wash-water**

**2. Standing liquid waste gives breeding places to —**
a) bees and butterflies  b) flies and mosquitoes  c) fish
**Answer: b) flies and mosquitoes**

**3. Asthma is named as which kind of illness?**
a) a stomach illness  b) a respiratory illness  c) an eye illness
**Answer: b) a respiratory illness**

**4. Flood-borne pollution carries waste into —**
a) the sky  b) water sources  c) books
**Answer: b) water sources**
