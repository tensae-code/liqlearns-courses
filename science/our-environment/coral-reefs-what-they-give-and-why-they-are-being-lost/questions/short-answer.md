# Coral reefs — what they give and why they are being lost — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What the reefs give.
**Answer:** Oceans, lagoons and coral reefs stand under the Samoan way of life: fish and shellfish for food, sheltered lagoon water for travel and fishing, and the reef itself breaking the ocean's force before it reaches the shore.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Why the reefs are being lost.
**Answer:** The Global Coral Reef Monitoring Network's 2000 report found 27% of the world's reefs effectively lost.
