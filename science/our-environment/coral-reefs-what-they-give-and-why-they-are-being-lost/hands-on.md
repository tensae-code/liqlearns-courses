# Hands-on — Coral reefs — what they give and why they are being lost

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Graph the report
Draw a bar graph of reef loss from the 27% world figure against a Pacific reef in reasonably good health, and write one sentence on what the difference is credited to.

## My reef
Write the book's task in your own setting: one page on what the sea and reef near you give, and one thing that threatens them.
