Today: **Patch reefs** — Micronesia's way: islands of reef inside the lagoon.

A lagoon's floor is mostly sand and mud — but standing on it are patch reefs: solid little reef islands, growing up toward the sunlight. Their holes and edges are hiding places, where shallow-water animals shelter. A patch reef is an island of reef life in the middle of the lagoon.
