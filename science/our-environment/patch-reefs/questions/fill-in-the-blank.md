# Patch reefs — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A patch reef is a solid reef island on the _____ floor.
**Answer: lagoon**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Patch reefs grow toward the _____.
**Answer: sunlight**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Patch reefs give shallow-water animals _____ places.
**Answer: hiding**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The lagoon bottom around them is sand and _____ sediment.
**Answer: mud**
