# Patch reefs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A patch reef is…
a) A tear in a fishing net  b) A solid reef island on the lagoon floor  c) A pile of beach sand  d) A kind of cloud
**Answer: b) A solid reef island on the lagoon floor**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A patch reef grows…
a) Down into the mud  b) Toward the sunlight  c) Sideways into the open ocean only  d) Only at night
**Answer: b) Toward the sunlight**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Patch reefs give shallow-water animals…
a) Fresh water  b) Hiding places  c) Dry land  d) Taro pits
**Answer: b) Hiding places**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The bottom around a patch reef is mostly…
a) Solid reef  b) Sand and mud sediment  c) Volcanic rock  d) Grass
**Answer: b) Sand and mud sediment**
