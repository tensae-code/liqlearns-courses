# Patch reefs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Where does a patch reef stand, and what is it?
**Answer: It stands on the lagoon floor — a solid reef island inside the lagoon, separate from the reef ring at the lagoon's edge.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do shallow-water animals gather at a patch reef?
**Answer: Because its solid, holey shape offers hiding places and shelter that the open sand and mud bottom around it cannot.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Toward what does a patch reef grow, and from where?
**Answer: It grows upward toward the sunlight, building from the lagoon floor.**
