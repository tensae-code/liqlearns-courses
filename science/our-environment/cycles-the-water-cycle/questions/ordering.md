# Cycles — the water cycle — ordering

Put things in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Starting at the sea, put the loop in order: precipitation, evaporation, flow back to the sea, condensation.
**Answer: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, flow back to the sea — and then evaporation again: the loop has no starting point, so starting at the sea is a choice, not a beginning.**
