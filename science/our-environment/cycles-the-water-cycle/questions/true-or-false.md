# Cycles — the water cycle — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The droplets on the cold pan came through the pan from the ice.
**Answer: False** — they condensed out of the rising vapour from the boiling water below.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After rain falls, some of it is absorbed into the soil and some flows back to water bodies.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The water cycle begins with evaporation.
**Answer: False** — the loop has no starting point; evaporation is only one of its moves.
