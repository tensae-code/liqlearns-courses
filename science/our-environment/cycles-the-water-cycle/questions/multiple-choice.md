# Cycles — the water cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the pan-and-ice experiment, the droplets under the cold pan show —
a) evaporation  b) condensation  c) precipitation  d) absorption
**Answer: b) condensation** — vapour gathering into liquid on a cold surface.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Transpiration is water vapour given off by —
a) seas  b) clouds  c) plants  d) soil
**Answer: c) plants.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Evaporation rises from —
a) surface water — seas, lakes and rivers  b) deep underground only  c) clouds  d) ice
**Answer: a).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Precipitation in this cycle falls as —
a) vapour  b) rain  c) steam  d) groundwater
**Answer: b) rain.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Earth's water endures because —
a) new water is made every year  b) the cycle is a loop with no starting point — water is carried round, never used up  c) rain falls only on the sea  d) people use very little
**Answer: b).**
