# Cycles — the water cycle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vapour gathering into droplets is called ______.
**Answer: condensation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Water rising from a lake's surface is ______.
**Answer: evaporation.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rain is the cycle's ______ — water falling back.
**Answer: precipitation.**
