# The world's answer to climate change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: The Rio Earth Summit of 1992 and the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change, which Nepal signed on 12 June 1992 and ratified in 1994 as printed; the conferences of the parties; Agenda 21 under the motto "think globally, act locally"; the Intergovernmental Panel set up by the World Meteorological Organization and the UN Environment Programme in 1988; the Kyoto Protocol of 11 December 1997 — developed countries to cut greenhouse-gas emissions 5.2 percent below 1990 levels across 2008–2012 as printed, Nepal ratifying on 16 September 2005 as printed, and the Doha second commitment period 2013–2020; emissions trading, joint implementation and the clean development mechanism; and cutting emissions from deforestation, with compensation for poorer countries.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed pp. 244–245.**
