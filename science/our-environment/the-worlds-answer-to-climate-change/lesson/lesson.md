## Sub lesson 1 — The world's efforts

The Rio Earth Summit of 1992 and the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change, which Nepal signed on 12 June 1992 and ratified in 1994 as printed; the conferences of the parties; Agenda 21 under the motto "think globally, act locally"; the Intergovernmental Panel set up by the World Meteorological Organization and the UN Environment Programme in 1988; the Kyoto Protocol of 11 December 1997 — developed countries to cut greenhouse-gas emissions 5.2 percent below 1990 levels across 2008–2012 as printed, Nepal ratifying on 16 September 2005 as printed, and the Doha second commitment period 2013–2020; emissions trading, joint implementation and the clean development mechanism; and cutting emissions from deforestation, with compensation for poorer countries.
