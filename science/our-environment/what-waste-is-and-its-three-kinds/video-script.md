# Video script — What waste is and its three kinds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Waste** is what is **left over** after things are used.
It comes in three states: **solid** scraps, **liquid** dirty water, **polluted air**.
Every home makes all three, every day.
