# Lesson plan — What waste is and its three kinds

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** What waste is and its three kinds
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — the waste lessons at Grade 4 depth: first the definition and the three states (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** waste is what is left over after things are used — leftover food and products, human and animal waste — and it comes in three states: solid waste, liquid waste and polluted air
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Dry rubbish in its place and Dirty water and what it brings — the solid and liquid wastes were met separately; here is the whole family, named together.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what waste is: what is left over after things are used.
2. Give examples: leftover food and products, human and animal waste.
3. Name waste's three states: solid, liquid, polluted air.

## What you need
One meal's leftovers and wash-water at home — the lesson's examples are already there.

## If the learner is stuck
- Counts only solid rubbish as waste → dirty water and polluted air are waste in the book's three states; the drain and the chimney are waste-makers too.
- Thinks waste is only what factories make → the book leads with the home's own leftovers: food, products, human and animal waste.
