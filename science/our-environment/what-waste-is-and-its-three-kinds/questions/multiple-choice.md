# What waste is and its three kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Waste is —**
a) anything new  b) what is left over after things are used  c) only factory smoke
**Answer: b) what is left over after things are used**

**2. Which is one of waste's three states?**
a) solid waste  b) stone waste  c) star waste
**Answer: a) solid waste**

**3. Dirty wash-water is —**
a) solid waste  b) liquid waste  c) polluted air
**Answer: b) liquid waste**

**4. Smoke rising from a fire is waste as —**
a) polluted air  b) solid waste  c) liquid waste
**Answer: a) polluted air**

**5. Chapter 3 review: waste is —**
a) what is left over after things are used  b) only smoke  c) a kind of food
**Answer: a) what is left over after things are used** — Waste as defined and reviewed.
