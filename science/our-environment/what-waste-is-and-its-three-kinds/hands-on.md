# Hands-on — What waste is and its three kinds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The three columns day
One day, three columns on paper. Every time waste appears at home, enter it in its state column. At night, read the day's waste whole.

## Kitchen follow
Help cook one meal and list, in order, every leftover the meal creates — before, during and after eating. Order teaches where sorting could begin.
