# Protected areas and wetlands — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A place set apart under protection is a _____ area.
**Answer: protected**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Marshes and flood plains are _____ — ecosystems fitted to wet ground.
**Answer: wetlands**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A protected area guards the whole _____, not one species alone.
**Answer: habitat**
