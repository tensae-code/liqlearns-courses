# Protected areas and wetlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A protected area is…
a) A zoo  b) A place set apart under protection, guarding a habitat and its living things  c) A farm  d) A hunting ground
**Answer: b) A place set apart under protection, guarding a habitat and its living things**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What a protected area protects first is…
a) One famous animal  b) The habitat — ground, water and plant cover  c) A fence  d) A road
**Answer: b) The habitat — ground, water and plant cover**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Wetlands are taught as…
a) Wasted land  b) Ecosystems in their own right  c) Dry land  d) Sea water only
**Answer: b) Ecosystems in their own right**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Wetlands are places where…
a) Water stands or soaks the ground  b) Nothing lives  c) Sand advances  d) Trees cannot root at all
**Answer: a) Water stands or soaks the ground**
