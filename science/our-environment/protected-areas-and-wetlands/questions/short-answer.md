# Protected areas and wetlands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does a protected area protect?
**Answer:** The whole habitat of the place — its ground, water and plant cover — and every living thing that habitat carries.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why are wetlands ecosystems and not wasted land?
**Answer:** Because they hold their own living community fitted to wet ground and standing water — a working ecosystem, rich in life.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do wetlands stand inside the protection lesson?
**Answer:** They are among the places protection most concerns: rich in life, easy to drain or spoil, and impossible to remake once lost.
