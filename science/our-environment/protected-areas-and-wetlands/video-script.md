Today: **Protected areas and wetlands** — Mauritania's way: ecosystems, then the places a country guards, then the wettest ecosystems of all.

A protected area is an ecosystem given a guard: land or water set apart, where the habitat — and every living thing it carries — is kept safe.

And wetlands are ecosystems too: marshes and flood plains with their own communities fitted to wet ground. Rich in life, easy to spoil, impossible to remake — that is why protection concerns them most.
