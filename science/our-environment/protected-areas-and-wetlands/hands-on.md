# Hands-on — Protected areas and wetlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the protected places
On a map of your country, mark the protected areas you can find named. For one of them, write what it protects — the habitat first, then two living things that habitat carries.

## Wetland watch
Visit or picture a wet place — a marsh edge, a flooded plain, a pond shore. List the plants and animals you see or expect, and say for each how it is fitted to wet ground.
