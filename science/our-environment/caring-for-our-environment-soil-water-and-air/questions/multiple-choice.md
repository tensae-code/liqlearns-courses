# Caring for our environment: soil, water and air — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The book compares which two kinds of land to teach soil care?**
a) rich and poor farms  b) eroded land and protected land  c) near and far land
**Answer: b) eroded land and protected land**

**2. Water care means keeping which places unpolluted?**
a) river, spring, pond and lake  b) only wells  c) the sea
**Answer: a) river, spring, pond and lake**

**3. Terraced, planted land is the book's picture of —**
a) eroded land  b) protected land  c) unused land
**Answer: b) protected land**

**4. A gullied field shows —**
a) good care  b) care that was not done  c) a river's home
**Answer: b) care that was not done**
