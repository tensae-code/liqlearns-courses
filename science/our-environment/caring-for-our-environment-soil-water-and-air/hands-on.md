# Hands-on — Caring for our environment: soil, water and air

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The manual as posters
Make three posters, one per resource, in the book's shape: TREASURE / THREAT / CARE. Hang them where the household will read them daily.

## Slope rescue plan
Pick one eroding slope near school. Draft its rescue: cover, terraces, planting — costed in work-days, not money. Present it to a teacher.

## Air and water week
For one week, log one act per day that protected water or air at home. Small acts, truly done, beat large promises.
