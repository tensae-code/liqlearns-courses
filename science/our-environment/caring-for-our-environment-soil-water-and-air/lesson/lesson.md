# Caring for our environment: soil, water and air

## Do it first
Two fields after the same rain. One is gullied to the bone. One stands terraced and green. The difference is not luck — it is care, done resource by resource. This lesson writes the carer's manual for soil, water and air.

## Care for each resource: the manual

The Grade 2 book closes its natural-environment chapter with **care, organised by resource**. For **soil**: **stopping erosion** — its pictures set **eroded land beside protected land**, and the care methods follow from the contrast: cover the soil, terrace the slopes, plant. For **water**: keeping the **sources — river, spring, pond, lake — unpolluted**, with the care methods for each. For **air**: keeping the **air clean**.

Learn it as a manual with three pages, one per resource, each page in the same shape: the treasure, the threat, the care. Any environment problem you will ever meet files under one of these pages.

## Say it to your companion
Walk your companion through the manual: for each resource, the harm and the care. They play the inspector at your home: water source, soil cover, air — pass or fail, with the book's fix named for each fail.

## Do It Yourself
1. Erosion compare: photograph or draw eroded and protected land near you. Annotate the protection: what exactly stops the water?
2. Source guard duty: inspect your water source against the pollution list. Remove what you safely can; report what you cannot.
3. Three-resource pledge: write one care act per resource for your household, and keep it for a month, ticked daily.
