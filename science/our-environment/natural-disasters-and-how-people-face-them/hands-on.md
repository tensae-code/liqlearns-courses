# Hands-on — Natural disasters — and how people face them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A world-problem map
Draw a simple world map outline and mark one place a disaster has struck. Draw arrows in from other places, labelled with the shared facing — help, rebuilding, learning.

## Chapter neighbours
List the neighbours of disasters in Sudan's Chapter 3 — pollution, resource depletion, food security, forests, wildlife, fresh water — and say what they all share: each is a problem of man and the environment, faced together.
