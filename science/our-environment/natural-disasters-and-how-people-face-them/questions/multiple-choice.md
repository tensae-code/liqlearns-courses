# Natural disasters — and how people face them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In Sudan's Grade 12 book, disasters are taught as…
a) a local matter only  b) a world problem  c) a kind of weather  d) a mineral resource  
**Answer: b) a world problem**

**2.** Disasters sit in which chapter of the book?
a) Reading aerial photographs  b) Economic geography  c) Man and the environment  d) Map work  
**Answer: c) Man and the environment**

**3.** Who shares the work of facing a disaster?
a) Only the town struck  b) No one  c) People together — the facing is shared work  d) Only map-makers  
**Answer: c) People together — the facing is shared work**
