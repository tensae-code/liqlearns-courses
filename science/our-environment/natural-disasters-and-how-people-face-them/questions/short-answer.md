# Natural disasters — and how people face them — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is the Arabic word the book uses for disasters?
**Answer: الكوارث (al-kawārith) — disasters, taught as a world problem in Chapter 3.**

**2.** Name two neighbours of the disasters topic in the same chapter.
**Answer: Any two of: pollution of the environment, depletion of natural resources, food security, forests, wildlife, fresh water and water security, minerals and mineral fuels, sustainable development.**
