# Iceland's habitats and their living worlds

## Do it first
One small island holds lava fields, woods, wetlands, heaths, gravel plains, high mountains and seashores — and different living things in each. If you had to teach a whole country's nature, how would you sort it? Say your sorting out loud. Then read on: Iceland's guide sorts it by habitat, and this lesson teaches that sorting.

## Sub lesson 1 — A young volcanic island, very varied

Iceland is a **young volcanic island** with very varied landscapes. Its nature book, *Líf á landi* (Life on Land), does not teach its living things species by species in a list — it organises the living world **by habitat**: lava, forest, soil, grassland, wetland, heath, gravel plain, highland, human-made land and shore.

## Sub lesson 2 — Each habitat known by its characteristic organism

Each habitat is known by its **characteristic organism** — the living thing that marks it — and the guide then follows the living links outward from that organism to the others joined to it. Habitat by habitat, link by link: that is the book's whole structure, and Iceland's own way of teaching its nature. The habitats are then stocked one by one across this course (lava fields, wetlands, gravel plains and the rest).

## The key ideas

- Iceland is a young volcanic island with very varied landscapes.
- Its living world is organised by habitat: lava, forest, soil, grassland, wetland, heath, gravel plain, highland, human-made land and shore.
- Each habitat is known by its characteristic organism, with the living links followed outward from it.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order — A young volcanic island, very varied first, then Each habitat known by its characteristic organism. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "How does Líf á landi sort Iceland's living things — by species list or by habitat?" Answer: By habitat — each habitat known by its characteristic organism, then the links outward to the organisms joined to it.
