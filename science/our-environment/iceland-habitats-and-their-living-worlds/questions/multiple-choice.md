# Iceland's habitats and their living worlds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Iceland is a young volcanic island with very varied landscapes.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Iceland is a young volcanic island with very varied landscapes.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
