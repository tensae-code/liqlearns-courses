# Iceland's habitats and their living worlds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Iceland is a young volcanic island with very varied landscapes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Its living world is organised by habitat: lava, forest, soil, grassland, wetland, heath, gravel plain, highland, human-made land and shore.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does Líf á landi sort Iceland's living things — by species list or by habitat?
**Answer:** By habitat — each habitat known by its characteristic organism, then the links outward to the organisms joined to it.
