# Hands-on — Iceland's habitats and their living worlds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort by habitat
Name living things you know from your own area. Sort them by the habitat each belongs in, Iceland's way, and give each habitat its characteristic organism.

## Ten habitats
Write Iceland's ten habitats from memory, then check them against the lesson. Draw one small picture for the habitat nearest your home.
