# Video script — Iceland's habitats and their living worlds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Iceland's habitats and their living worlds** — the frame of Líf á landi, Iceland's Life on Land guide.
Iceland is a young volcanic island with very varied landscapes, and its living world is sorted by habitat — ten of them, from lava to shore.
Each habitat is known by its characteristic organism, and the links are followed outward from there.
Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
