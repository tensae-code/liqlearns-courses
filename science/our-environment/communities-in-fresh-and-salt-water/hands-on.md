# Hands-on — Communities in fresh and salt water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fresh-water watch
Visit or picture a pond, river or lake. List every living thing you can name there and check it against the book's fresh-water list — water lilies, carp, catfish, pike, herons. Add your own finds.

## Two waters table
Make a two-column table — Fresh water | Salt water. Fill each side with the book's inhabitants, then add one fact about the water itself at the head of each column.
