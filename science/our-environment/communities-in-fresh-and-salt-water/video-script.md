# Video script — Communities in fresh and salt water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Communities in fresh and salt water** — Bulgaria's way: two waters, two different worlds of life.

In fresh water: water lilies on the surface, carp, catfish and pike in the water, herons at the edge.

In salt water: algae up near the sunlight, and plankton — the sea's tiny drifting life. Same element, water — different water, different community.

Now you. Name one inhabitant of each water and say which water it belongs to. Say it back — in your own words.
