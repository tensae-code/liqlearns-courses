# Communities in fresh and salt water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the book's fresh-water inhabitants.
**Answer:** Water lilies; carp, catfish and pike; herons at the water's edge.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the book's salt-water inhabitants and where in the sea they live.
**Answer:** Algae near the sunlit surface, and plankton, the tiny drifting life.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why are the two lists different if both are water?
**Answer:** Because the water itself differs — fresh against salt — and the water decides who can live there.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) What makes these lists communities and not just lists?
**Answer:** The kinds live together in one place, linked by food relations — plant, fish and bird (or algae and plankton) in one shared water.
