# Communities in fresh and salt water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which belongs to the fresh-water community?
a) Plankton  b) Carp  c) Sea algae only  d) Coral
**Answer: b) Carp**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Herons are found…
a) In the deep sea  b) At the fresh water's edge  c) Underground  d) In the mountains only
**Answer: b) At the fresh water's edge**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Sea algae live…
a) On the dark sea floor  b) Near the sunlit surface  c) In rivers  d) In swamps only
**Answer: b) Near the sunlit surface**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Plankton is…
a) A large fish  b) Tiny drifting life of the sea  c) A water lily  d) A kind of heron
**Answer: b) Tiny drifting life of the sea**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The difference between the two communities begins with…
a) The weather  b) The water itself — fresh or salt  c) The season  d) The fishermen
**Answer: b) The water itself — fresh or salt**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Catfish and pike are…
a) Sea fish in the book's list  b) Fresh-water fish  c) Birds  d) Plants
**Answer: b) Fresh-water fish**
