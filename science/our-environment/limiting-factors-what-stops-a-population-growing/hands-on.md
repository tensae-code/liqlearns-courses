# Hands-on — Limiting factors — what stops a population growing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: limiting factors: food, space and water cap a population's growth — in the pond, a dry season shrinks the water, mosquitoes multiply as frogs and their predators thin out, and more rain restores the balance Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
