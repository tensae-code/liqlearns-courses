# The flooded forest and its problems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what the flooded forest is.
**Answer:** The flooded forest is a forest that stands in water for part of the year, whose trees and creatures live with the flood as part of their life.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do the forest's problems matter beyond the forest itself?
**Answer:** Because the forest shelters fish and feeds people and belongs to the whole waterway system — harm to it travels through the system to the life that depends on it.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The flooded forest lives with the yearly flood on Cambodia's waterway system → its environmental problems weaken the forest and the harm travels through the whole system — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
