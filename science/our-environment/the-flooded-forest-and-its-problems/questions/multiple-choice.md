# The flooded forest and its problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The flooded forest is…
a) a forest flooded for part of the year, living with the flood  b) a forest that burned  c) a garden  d) a desert wood
**Answer: a) a forest flooded for part of the year, living with the flood** — The water rises into the trees, and the forest's life follows the flood rhythm.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson is taught in the book straight after…
a) Cambodia's waterway system  b) the planets  c) electricity  d) digestion
**Answer: a) Cambodia's waterway system** — Earth Ch. 3: Lesson 1 the waterways, Lesson 2 the flooded forest.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson's second half teaches the forest's…
a) environmental problems  b) timber prices  c) history  d) songs
**Answer: a) environmental problems** — Filed exactly so: environmental problems of the flooded forest.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Harm to the forest travels…
a) through the whole system — waterways, forest and dependent life  b) nowhere  c) only upward  d) only to one tree
**Answer: a) through the whole system — waterways, forest and dependent life** — The chapter's system idea: harm moves through waterways, forest and life together.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The forest shelters…
a) fish and other creatures, and feeds people  b) nothing  c) only stones  d) only clouds
**Answer: a) fish and other creatures, and feeds people** — The flooded forest is shelter and food source — which is why its problems matter.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The book is Cambodia's Grade 7…
a) Science student book, Earth-science part  b) Physics-only book  c) Kindergarten core book  d) private exam booklet
**Answer: a) Science student book, Earth-science part** — An official MoEYS combined student book (Sala 00004320).
