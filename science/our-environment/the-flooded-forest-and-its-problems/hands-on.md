# Hands-on — The flooded forest and its problems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Flood rhythm diary
Ask an older person what the water does in the wet months near a flooded forest, lake or river they know. Write the rhythm: when it rises, what lives there then, when it falls.

## Harm travels
Draw the chain: waterway → flooded forest → the creatures and people who depend on it. Mark where one harm enters, and follow it through the chain in words.
