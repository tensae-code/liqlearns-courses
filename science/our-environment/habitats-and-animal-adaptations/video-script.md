# Video script — Habitats — where a living thing lives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Habitats — where a living thing lives** — Guatemala's way: the surroundings are a space for life.

Every living thing lives somewhere. That place is its habitat: the fish's water, the bird's tree, the plant's patch of ground.
And Guatemala's Grade 7 guide gives the idea its full name: our surroundings are a space for life. The place is not empty ground around the living thing — it is where its life happens.
So if the surroundings change, the life that can be lived there changes too.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
