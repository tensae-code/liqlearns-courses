# Habitats — where a living thing lives

## Do it first
Before you read: pick one animal or plant you know well. Where does it live? What does that place give it? Say your answers out loud. Then read on: this is Guatemala's way in — its Grade 7 guide's Learning Experience 2 is called "Our surroundings, a space for life", and it begins by asking what a habitat is.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a habitat is

A **habitat** is the place where a living thing lives.

Every living thing has its habitat: the fish's water, the bird's tree, the plant's patch of ground. The habitat is not just an address — it is the surroundings that living thing actually lives in, day by day.

## Sub lesson 2 — Our surroundings, a space for life

Guatemala's guide names the whole learning experience **"Our surroundings, a space for life"** — and that name is the second half of the lesson.

Our surroundings are a **space for life**: the place around a living thing is where its life happens. Change the surroundings and you change the life that can be lived there; that is why the guide teaches habitat inside an experience about valuing the surroundings, not as a word to memorise.

So a habitat is two things at once: the **place** a living thing lives, and the **space for its life** — the surroundings it depends on.

## The key ideas

- A **habitat** is the place where a living thing lives.
- Guatemala's guide teaches it inside Learning Experience 2, **"Our surroundings, a space for life"**: the surroundings are the space in which a living thing's life happens.
- Because the surroundings are a space for life, changing them changes the life that can be lived there.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order — what a habitat is first, then the surroundings as a space for life. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "Is a habitat just an address?" Answer with the space-for-life idea, not by repeating.

## Do It Yourself
1. Name three living things near your home and write each one's habitat in one sentence.
2. For one of them, write what would happen to it if its surroundings changed completely.
3. Teach the hardest part of this lesson to someone at home in five sentences.
