# Habitats — where a living thing lives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A habitat is…
a) A kind of animal  b) The place where a living thing lives  c) A food chain  d) A season
**Answer: b) The place where a living thing lives**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The fish's habitat is…
a) The tree  b) The soil  c) The water  d) The sky
**Answer: c) The water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Guatemala's guide calls Learning Experience 2…
a) "Our surroundings, a space for life"  b) "Machines and movement"  c) "Numbers in nature"  d) "The sky above us"
**Answer: a) "Our surroundings, a space for life"**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does changing the surroundings matter?
a) It changes nothing  b) It changes the life that can be lived there  c) It only changes the weather  d) It changes the living thing's name
**Answer: b) It changes the life that can be lived there**
