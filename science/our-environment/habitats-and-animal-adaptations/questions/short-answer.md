# Habitats — where a living thing lives — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a habitat? Give one example.
**Answer:** A habitat is the place where a living thing lives — for example, the water is the fish's habitat (the tree the bird's, the soil the plant's).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does Guatemala's guide mean by calling the surroundings "a space for life"?
**Answer:** The surroundings are not empty ground around a living thing — they are the space in which its life happens, so changing them changes the life that can be lived there.
