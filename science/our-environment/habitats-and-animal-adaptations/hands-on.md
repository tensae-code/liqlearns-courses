# Hands-on — Habitats — where a living thing lives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the habitats around you
Walk a small area near home or school. List every living thing you find and, beside each, its habitat — the exact place it lives. You are mapping habitats, not just naming animals and plants.

## One habitat, closely
Choose one habitat from your map. Draw it, label the living thing that lives there, and draw arrows from the place to the living thing for each thing the place gives it. Under the drawing, write the guide's sentence in your own words: these surroundings are a space for life because…
