# Hands-on — River life — from source to mouth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One river, two homes (paper and pencil)
Draw one long river from a high source (left) to a slow mouth (right). Above the upper part write: fast, cold — trout. Above the lower part write: slow — catfish, carp. In one sentence under it, say why the same fish do not live all the way down.
