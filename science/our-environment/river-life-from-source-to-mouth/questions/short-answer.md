# River life — from source to mouth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: One river, one changing community.
**Answer:** The **river** is taught as one **community** — but not the same community all the way down.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The upper course — trout in fast cold water.
**Answer:** High up, the river runs **fast and cold** — and that water's fish is the **trout**.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The lower course — catfish and carp in slow water.
**Answer:** Low down, the river runs **slow** — and its fish are the **catfish** and the **carp**.
