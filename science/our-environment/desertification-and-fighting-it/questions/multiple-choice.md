# Desertification — and fighting it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Desertification is…
a) A desert storm  b) Land turning to desert as its plant cover is lost and sand advances  c) Planting trees  d) A rainy season
**Answer: b) Land turning to desert as its plant cover is lost and sand advances**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) When plant cover is lost, the soil is…
a) Protected  b) Bared to wind and heat  c) Made richer at once  d) Turned to rock
**Answer: b) Bared to wind and heat**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The green belt is…
a) A sand road  b) A belt of trees planted to hold the sand back  c) A river  d) A fence of stone
**Answer: b) A belt of trees planted to hold the sand back**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the green belt, the trees' roots…
a) Drink the wells dry  b) Hold the soil in place  c) Make the sand  d) Stop the rain
**Answer: b) Hold the soil in place**
