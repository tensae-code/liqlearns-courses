# Desertification — and fighting it — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Land turning to desert is called _____.
**Answer: desertification**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The belt of trees planted against the sand is the _____ belt.
**Answer: green**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The green belt's crowns break the _____, and its roots hold the soil.
**Answer: wind**
