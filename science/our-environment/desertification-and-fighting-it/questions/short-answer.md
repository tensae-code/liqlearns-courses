# Desertification — and fighting it — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Tell how land turns to desert, step by step.
**Answer:** The plant cover is lost; without roots and shade the soil is bared to wind and heat; the sand advances, and ground that fed people and animals becomes desert.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the green belt fight desertification?
**Answer:** Planted across the sand's path, its roots hold the soil and its trunks and crowns break the wind, slowing the desert's advance at the line of trees.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does Mauritania teach this twice?
**Answer:** Grade 4 teaches the problem — how land turns to desert — and Grade 6 returns with the country's answer, the green belt: a desert-border country teaches both the loss and the planted defence.
