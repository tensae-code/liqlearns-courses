Today: **Desertification — and fighting it** — Mauritania's way: the problem, then the planted answer.

Desertification is land turning to desert. The plant cover goes, the soil lies bare, the wind takes it and the sand advances — ground that fed people becomes desert.

Mauritania plants against it: a green belt, a wall of living trees across the sand's path. Roots hold the soil; crowns break the wind. The desert slows at the line of green.
