# Hands-on — Desertification — and fighting it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wind and cover test
Make two trays of dry soil: one bare, one covered with grass or leaf litter. Blow across each from the same distance (or use a fan) and compare how much soil moves. Say which tray is the land before desertification, and which is the bared land.

## Plant a line
Plant a row of fast seedlings or cuttings along a bare edge near home or school, and keep a simple record of their growth — your own small green belt, watched over weeks.
