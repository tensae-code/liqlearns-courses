# Hands-on — Coastal ecosystems — dunes, seagrass and coral banks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk a shore in your mind
Picture the nearest coast you know, or visit one if you can. In the lesson's order, look for: sand piled by wind, plants holding the shore, shallow water where grass could grow. Write which of the three ecosystems you could actually see, and which lie hidden under the water.

## Three bands, one coast
On a long strip of paper draw the coast as three bands — land edge, shallows, offshore — and fill each band with its ecosystem: dunes and coastal plants, seagrass meadow, coral bank with a whale. Use the drawing to teach the walk to someone at home.
