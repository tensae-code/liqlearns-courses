# Lesson plan — Coastal ecosystems — dunes, seagrass and coral banks

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Coastal ecosystems — dunes, seagrass and coral banks
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (Our Environment) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the coast as a joined set of ecosystems — the dunes and their vegetation on the shore, the seagrass meadows in the shallows, and the coral banks offshore, with the Banco de la Plata as the Dominican Republic's own great example — as its Grade 5 Ciencias de la Naturaleza book teaches them, in shore-to-sea order
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Dunes and coastal vegetation** — where the sea meets the land, wind piles the sand into **dunes** — low hills of sand along the shore.
2. **Seagrass meadows — the ocean's prairies** — in the shallow water just off the shore grow **seagrass meadows** — underwater fields of grass-like plants rooted in the sandy bottom.
3. **The Banco de la Plata — corals and humpback whales** — further out lies the **Banco de la Plata** — the Silver Bank — a great offshore bank of **corals** that the book calls a paradise of corals and **humpback whales**: the whales come to its warm, sheltered waters, and the coral bank is their meeting place.

The bank completes the walk: dunes on the shore, seagrass in the shallows, and the coral bank offshore — three coastal ecosystems, each with its own life, joined in one coast.
