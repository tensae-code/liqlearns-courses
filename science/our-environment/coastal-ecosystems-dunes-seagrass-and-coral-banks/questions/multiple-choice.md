# Coastal ecosystems — dunes, seagrass and coral banks — multiple choice


**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dunes are…
a) Piles of coral  b) Wind-piled hills of sand along the shore  c) Underwater grass fields  d) Whale feeding grounds
**Answer: b) Wind-piled hills of sand along the shore**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Coastal vegetation matters on dunes because it…
a) Makes the sand  b) Holds the dunes together  c) Stops the sea  d) Feeds whales
**Answer: b) Holds the dunes together**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Seagrass meadows grow…
a) On the dunes  b) In deep ocean trenches  c) In the shallow water off the shore  d) On mountain tops
**Answer: c) In the shallow water off the shore**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The book calls seagrass meadows…
a) The ocean's prairies  b) The sea's deserts  c) Silver banks  d) Coastal forests
**Answer: a) The ocean's prairies**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The Banco de la Plata is…
a) A river bank  b) A great offshore bank of corals  c) A dune field  d) A fishing boat
**Answer: b) A great offshore bank of corals**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The Banco de la Plata is a paradise of corals and…
a) Sea turtles only  b) Humpback whales  c) Mangrove crabs  d) Night herons
**Answer: b) Humpback whales**
