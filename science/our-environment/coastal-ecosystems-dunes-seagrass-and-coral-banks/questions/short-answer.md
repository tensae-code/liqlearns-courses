# Coastal ecosystems — dunes, seagrass and coral banks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What are dunes, and what holds them together?
**Answer: Dunes are low hills of sand piled by the wind along the shore; coastal vegetation grows on them and holds them together.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Where do seagrass meadows grow, and why are they called the ocean's prairies?
**Answer: In the shallow water off the shore; like a prairie on land, the meadow is a grass field that is a whole community's home ground.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What is the Banco de la Plata?
**Answer: A great offshore coral bank off the Dominican Republic's coast — a paradise of corals and of the humpback whales that come to its waters.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Name the three coastal ecosystems in the book's shore-to-sea order.
**Answer: Dunes and coastal vegetation on the shore, seagrass meadows in the shallows, and the coral banks offshore.**
