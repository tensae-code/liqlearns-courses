# Coastal ecosystems — dunes, seagrass and coral banks — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Wind piles sand into _____ along the shore.
**Answer: dunes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Seagrass meadows are called the ocean's _____.
**Answer: prairies**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The great coral bank is the Banco de la _____.
**Answer: Plata**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Humpback _____ come to the Banco de la Plata.
**Answer: whales**
