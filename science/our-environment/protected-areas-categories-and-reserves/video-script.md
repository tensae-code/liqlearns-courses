Today: **Protected areas — categories and reserves** — Honduras's way.

the IUCN definition of a protected area: a clearly defined geographical space, recognised, dedicated and managed, by legal or other effective means, for the long-term conservation of nature with its ecosystem services and cultural values. a management plan (plan de manejo) sets a protected area's objectives and actions for protection, conservation, restoration and use, and its zoning orders the space: the core zone (zona núcleo) is absolute conservation, while the buffer zone (zona de amortiguamiento) promotes conservation with sustainable multiple use and local social, economic and cultural development. the objectives of SINAPH, the National System of Protected Areas of Honduras: conserve and integrally develop the natural and cultural resources of the protected areas, develop co-management mechanisms, promote coordination between institutions, open opportunities for sustainable use, assure conservation of biodiversity, achieve the greatest sustainable social and economic benefit, and provide instruments for proper management.
