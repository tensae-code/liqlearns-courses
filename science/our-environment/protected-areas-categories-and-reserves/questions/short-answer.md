# Protected areas — categories and reserves — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about the IUCN definition of a protected area?
**Answer: the IUCN definition of a protected area: a clearly defined geographical space, recognised, dedicated and managed, by legal or other effective means, for the long-term conservation of nature with its ecosystem services and cultural values.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about a management plan (plan de manejo) sets a protected?
**Answer: a management plan (plan de manejo) sets a protected area's objectives and actions for protection, conservation, restoration and use, and its zoning orders the space: the core zone (zona núcleo) is absolute conservation, while the buffer zone (zona de amortiguamiento) promotes conservation with sustainable multiple use and local social, economic and cultural development.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about the objectives of SINAPH, the National System of Protected Areas of Honduras?
**Answer: the objectives of SINAPH, the National System of Protected Areas of Honduras: conserve and integrally develop the natural and cultural resources of the protected areas, develop co-management mechanisms, promote coordination between institutions, open opportunities for sustainable use, assure conservation of biodiversity, achieve the greatest sustainable social and economic benefit, and provide instruments for proper management.**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Protected areas — categories and reserves.**
