# Hands-on — Protected areas — categories and reserves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — The IUCN definition of a protected area. the IUCN definition of a protected area: a clearly defined geographical space, recognised, dedicated and managed, by legal or other effective means, for the long-term conservation of nature with its ecosystem services and cultural values. Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
