# Lesson plan — Protected areas — categories and reserves

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Protected areas — categories and reserves
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — appended to Unit 11 (Our Environment) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the IUCN definition of a protected area: a clearly defined geographical space, recognised, dedicated and managed, by legal or other effective means, for the long-term conservation of nature with its ecosystem services and cultural values.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

## Sub lessons
1. **The IUCN definition of a protected area** — the IUCN definition of a protected area: a clearly defined geographical space, recognised, dedicated and managed, by legal or other effective means, for the long-term conservation of nature with its ecosystem services and cultural values.
2. **A management plan (plan de manejo) sets a protected** — a management plan (plan de manejo) sets a protected area's objectives and actions for protection, conservation, restoration and use, and its zoning orders the space: the core zone (zona núcleo) is absolute conservation, while the buffer zone (zona de amortiguamiento) promotes conservation with sustainable multiple use and local social, economic and cultural development.
3. **The objectives of SINAPH, the National System of Protected Areas of Honduras** — the objectives of SINAPH, the National System of Protected Areas of Honduras: conserve and integrally develop the natural and cultural resources of the protected areas, develop co-management mechanisms, promote coordination between institutions, open opportunities for sustainable use, assure conservation of biodiversity, achieve the greatest sustainable social and economic benefit, and provide instruments for proper management.
