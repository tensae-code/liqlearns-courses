# Video script — Introduced species and the environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Introduced species and the environment** — Fiji's way, from its book (Fiji, Year 6 — *Year 6 Elementary Science*, printed pp. 34-41).

Fiji's Year 6 book sorts living things by where they belong. Endemic species are the country's own — they live in Fiji by nature. Introduced species are non-native: people brought them in from elsewhere. The sort comes first, because the story of every introduced species starts with a bringing-in.
The book then teaches Fiji's own case: the introduced species in Fiji itself — living things that arrived from other places and stayed, living now in Fiji's environment beside the endemic species.
A brought-in species does not simply join the environment; it changes it. Fiji's book teaches how the introduced species changed Fiji's environment — the closing question of the unit (printed pp. 34–41), and the reason the endemic/introduced sort matters.
