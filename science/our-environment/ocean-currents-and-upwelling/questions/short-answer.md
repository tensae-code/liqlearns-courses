# Ocean currents and upwelling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Warm water, cold water and the thermocline.
**Answer:** The ocean is layered by temperature.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The trade winds, the currents and upwelling.
**Answer:** The southeast trade winds blow steadily over Samoa's seas, and moving air drags the surface water with it — that is how currents are driven around Samoa.
