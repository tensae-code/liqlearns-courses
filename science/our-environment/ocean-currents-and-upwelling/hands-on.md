# Hands-on — Ocean currents and upwelling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Layer the sea
Draw the sea in three bands — warm surface, thermocline, cold deep — and mark where the southeast trade winds blow and where upwelling rises.

## Ask the shore
Ask a fisher or elder where the currents run near your shore and when the water feels coldest; compare their answer with the lesson's system.
