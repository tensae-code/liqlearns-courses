# Video script — Natural and human-made communities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Natural and human-made communities** — Bulgaria's way: sort every community by who made it.

A community is all the living things in one place, linked by food. Some communities grow by themselves — the forest, the sea, the river, the swamp. Those are natural.

Others are made by people, and kept by people — the park, the fishpond, the field, the garden. Stop keeping them, and they change.

Now you. Name a community near you and say which kind it is — and why. Say it back — in your own words.
