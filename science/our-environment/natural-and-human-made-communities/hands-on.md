# Hands-on — Natural and human-made communities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort your surroundings
Name every community you can walk to from home. Sort them into two lists — natural and human-made — and write the deciding fact beside each.

## The stopped-keeping test
Pick one human-made community near you. Write what would happen to it, season by season, if people stopped keeping it for a year.
