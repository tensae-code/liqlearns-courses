# Natural and human-made communities

## Do it first
Before you read: a forest and a garden can hold many of the same kinds of living things. But one of them exists because people made it and keep it, and the other would stand if no person ever came. Which is which — and what changes when people stop keeping one of them? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Bulgaria's way in — its Grade 4 book sorts communities into the natural and the human-made.

## Sub lesson 1 — Natural communities — forest, sea, river, swamp

A **community** is all the living things living together in one place, linked by food relations. Bulgaria's Grade 4 book's Lesson 48 first names the **natural communities**: the **forest, the sea, the river and the swamp**.

These grow and hold together by themselves. Nobody planted the forest or stocked the sea; the community is the place's own, and it carries on whether people watch it or not.

## Sub lesson 2 — Human-made communities — park, fishpond, field, garden

Against them the book sets the **artificial — human-made — communities**: the **park, the fishpond, the field and the garden**.

People make these and people keep them: the park is planted, the fishpond is stocked, the field is sown, the garden is weeded. Stop the keeping and the community changes — the field goes wild, the garden runs to weeds. The test for the two kinds is one question: **did people make this community, and must people keep it?**

## The key ideas

- A **community** is all the living things in one place, linked by food relations.
- **Natural communities** grow by themselves: **forest, sea, river, swamp**.
- **Human-made communities** are made and kept by people: **park, fishpond, field, garden**.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "How do you tell a natural community from a human-made one?" Answer: Ask: did people make it, and must people keep it? A forest stands by itself; a garden stands because it is kept.
