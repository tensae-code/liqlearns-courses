# Natural and human-made communities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which list is all natural communities?
a) Park, field, garden, fishpond  b) Forest, sea, river, swamp  c) Garden, sea, park, river  d) Field, forest, fishpond, swamp
**Answer: b) Forest, sea, river, swamp**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A fishpond is a…
a) Natural community  b) Human-made community  c) Not a community  d) Kind of forest
**Answer: b) Human-made community**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Human-made communities must be…
a) Left alone  b) Kept by people  c) In water  d) Very large
**Answer: b) Kept by people**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A community is…
a) One animal  b) All the living things in one place, linked by food  c) A kind of plant  d) A season
**Answer: b) All the living things in one place, linked by food**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) If people stop keeping a field, it…
a) Stays the same  b) Changes — it goes wild  c) Becomes a sea  d) Becomes a swamp at once
**Answer: b) Changes — it goes wild**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is human-made?
a) River  b) Swamp  c) Garden  d) Sea
**Answer: c) Garden**
