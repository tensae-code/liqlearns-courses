# Natural and human-made communities — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Forest, sea, river and swamp are _____ communities.
**Answer: natural**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Park, fishpond, field and garden are _____ communities.
**Answer: human-made (artificial)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A community's living things are linked by _____ relations.
**Answer: food**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A human-made community stands because people _____ it.
**Answer: keep**
