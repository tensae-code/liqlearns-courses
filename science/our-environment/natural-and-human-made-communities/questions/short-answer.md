# Natural and human-made communities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the book's four natural and four human-made communities.
**Answer:** Natural: forest, sea, river, swamp. Human-made: park, fishpond, field, garden.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the one-question test for the two kinds?
**Answer:** Did people make this community, and must people keep it? If yes, it is human-made; if it grows and holds by itself, it is natural.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A park holds trees like a forest does. Why is it not a natural community?
**Answer:** Because people planted it and keep it — stop the keeping and it changes; the forest holds together by itself.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach the two kinds together?
**Answer:** Because the same word — community — covers both, and only the origin and the keeping tell them apart.
