# Habitat and niche — where an animal lives and what it does — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Habitat and ecological niche.
**Answer:** habitat and ecological niche: the niche is the living thing's role (the pond snail that eats algae, keeps the water clean and releases oxygen), the habitat is the place it lives (the catfish at the pond's bottom; the Arabian oryx in the Arabian desert) — and one habitat, a single apple tree, is shared by kinds that live in it in different ways.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Habitat and ecological niche — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
