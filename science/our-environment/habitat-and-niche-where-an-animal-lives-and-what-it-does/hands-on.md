# Hands-on — Habitat and niche — where an animal lives and what it does

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: habitat and ecological niche: the niche is the living thing's role (the pond snail that eats algae, keeps the water clean and releases oxygen), the habitat is the place it lives (the catfish at the pond's bottom; the Arabian oryx in the Arabian desert) — and one habitat, a single apple tree, is shared by kinds that live in it in different ways Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
