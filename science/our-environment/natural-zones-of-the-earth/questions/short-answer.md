# Natural zones of the Earth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define a natural zone.
**Answer:** A great stretch of the Earth where the living cover — climate, soil, plants and animals in a matching set — shares one character.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do the parts of a zone belong together?
**Answer:** Because they answer to the same conditions: the climate shapes the soil, and the plants and animals fit both.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What mainly sets the zones' order?
**Answer:** The change of climate across the Earth — above all heat and water.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) How does this lesson use the theme's earlier teaching on adaptation?
**Answer:** The zone is adaptation read at the scale of the whole Earth: each zone's living things are fitted to its conditions.
