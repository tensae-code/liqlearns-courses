# Natural zones of the Earth — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A great stretch of Earth with one living character is a natural _____.
**Answer: zone**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In a zone, climate, soil, plants and animals change in matching _____.
**Answer: sets**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The zones' order is set above all by heat and _____.
**Answer: water**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Each zone's living things are _____ to its conditions.
**Answer: fitted**
