# Natural zones of the Earth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A natural zone is…
a) A country's border  b) A great stretch of Earth whose living cover shares one character  c) A fenced park  d) One forest only
**Answer: b) A great stretch of Earth whose living cover shares one character**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What changes together across a zone?
a) Only place names  b) Only rivers  c) Climate, soil, plants and animals — in matching sets  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: c) Climate, soil, plants and animals — in matching sets**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Natural zones are bordered by…
a) Fences  b) Roads  c) Oceans only  d) Sets of conditions, not lines drawn by people
**Answer: d) Sets of conditions, not lines drawn by people**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The order of the zones is set by…
a) The changing climate — above all heat and water  b) Chance  c) The alphabet  d) Animals migrating
**Answer: a) The changing climate — above all heat and water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Each zone's living things are…
a) The same as every zone's  b) Fitted to its conditions  c) All able to live anywhere  d) Mostly imaginary
**Answer: b) Fitted to its conditions**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Crossing from one zone to another, you see change in…
a) Language only  b) Time of day  c) Plants, animals and the whole community  d) Nothing
**Answer: c) Plants, animals and the whole community**
