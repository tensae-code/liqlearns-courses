# Video script — Natural zones of the Earth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Natural zones of the Earth** — Armenia's way: nature in belts.

Climate changes across the Earth — and plants, animals and soil change with it, in matching sets.

A great stretch with one living character is a natural zone.

Travel far, and you cross the zones in order — set by heat and water.

Now you. Say what a natural zone is. Say it back — in your own words.
