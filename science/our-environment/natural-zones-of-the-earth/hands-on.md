# Hands-on — Natural zones of the Earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Zone postcards
Make two postcards from two imagined places — one hot and wet, one cold and dry. Draw the plants and animals that fit each. Label each postcard with its zone's conditions.

## The belt map
On an outline world map, shade the broad belts from equator to pole in different colours and write, for each belt, one plant and one animal that fit it. Your map is the natural zones in one picture.
