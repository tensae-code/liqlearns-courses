# Population ecology — counting and distribution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Counting populations, and how they are spread.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Additional Science Form 4 book teaches **population ecology**: counting populations by the **quadrat method** and the **capture-recapture method**, and describing **distribution patterns** — how the individuals of a population are spread across their ground. A population too large to count one by one is counted by sampling, and the sample is reasoned from.
