# Biomes of the world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In Guatemala's guide, a biome is…
a) A kind of animal  b) The type of vegetation of a region  c) A weather report  d) A single plant
**Answer: b) The type of vegetation of a region**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Two far-apart regions are the same biome when…
a) They are in the same country  b) Their animals are identical  c) Their vegetation type is the same  d) They share a language
**Answer: c) Their vegetation type is the same**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) To read a region by its vegetation, you ask…
a) What type of vegetation covers this region?  b) Who lives there?  c) What time is it?  d) How deep is the soil?
**Answer: a) What type of vegetation covers this region?**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Guatemala's guide teaches the biome inside Learning Experience 3, called…
a) "Our surroundings, a space for life"  b) "Our environment, a harmonious space"  c) "Healthy life"  d) "The climate factor"
**Answer: b) "Our environment, a harmonious space"**
