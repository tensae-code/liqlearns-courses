# Biomes of the world — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give Guatemala's guide's definition of a biome.
**Answer:** A biome is the type of vegetation of a region — the plant cover that characterises the place is what names the biome.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How are the biomes of the world sorted, and what changes when the vegetation changes?
**Answer:** The world's regions are sorted by the vegetation type covering each; where the vegetation changes, the biome changes, and regions with the same vegetation type share a biome wherever they are.
