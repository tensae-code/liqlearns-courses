# Video script — Biomes of the world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Biomes of the world** — Guatemala's way: a biome is the type of vegetation of a region.

Ask what covers a region — not its animals first, not its weather first, but its plants. That plant cover is the biome.
Where the vegetation type is the same, the biome is the same, even across an ocean. Where the vegetation changes, the biome changes.
So the world's regions can be sorted by what grows on them — and Guatemala's Grade 9 guide teaches that sorting inside "Our environment, a harmonious space".

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
