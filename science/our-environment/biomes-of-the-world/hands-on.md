# Hands-on — Biomes of the world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read your region by its vegetation
Go outside and record the vegetation of your region: the tallest plants, the ground cover, how thick or sparse it is. From that record alone, write your region's biome in the guide's form: the type of vegetation of this region is…

## A vegetation-only world map
Draw a simple world map and shade it using nothing but vegetation: one pattern for thick forest cover, one for open grass, one for sparse dry cover. Label each pattern with the biome it marks. Your map must work with no animal, weather or country labels at all — the vegetation alone does the sorting, as the guide's definition says.
