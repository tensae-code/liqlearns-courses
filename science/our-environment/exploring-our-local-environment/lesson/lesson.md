# Exploring our local environment

## Do it first
Before you read: say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is — exploring our local environment. Then read on: this is Seychelles' way in — the National Institute of Pedagogy's Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 13).

## Sub lesson 1 — Studying a simple habitat — the puddle

Seychelles' Unit 13 Topic 2 studies the smallest habitat there is: a puddle. Its temperature is measured as it changes over forty minutes, and its surface area is measured with a chicken-wire grid — a habitat is explored by measuring it, not only by looking at it. The puddle's size and water change as you watch: a habitat is a living, changing place.

## Sub lesson 2 — Investigating a habitat properly

Project 1, "Investigating a habitat", sets the full method for any habitat: visit it, describe it, measure its temperature and its area, name at least three plants and two animals in it, and draw a food chain of two animals and a plant from it. Description, measurement, naming and a food chain — that is a habitat investigation, and it works for a puddle, a garden or a shore.
