# Exploring our local environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The puddle's surface area is measured with…
a) a chicken-wire grid  b) a thermometer  c) a balance  d) a rain gauge
**Answer: a) a chicken-wire grid**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The puddle's temperature is followed over…
a) forty minutes  b) forty days  c) four seconds  d) a whole year
**Answer: a) forty minutes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A habitat investigation names at least…
a) three plants and two animals  b) ten animals only  c) one plant  d) no living things
**Answer: a) three plants and two animals**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Every habitat investigation ends by drawing…
a) a food chain of two animals and a plant  b) a map of the country  c) a bar chart of heights  d) a circuit
**Answer: a) a food chain of two animals and a plant**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A habitat is explored by…
a) measuring it, not only looking at it  b) looking once  c) reading about another country  d) guessing its temperature
**Answer: a) measuring it, not only looking at it**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The puddle shows a habitat is…
a) a changing place — its size and water change  b) fixed forever  c) empty of life  d) always the same temperature
**Answer: a) a changing place — its size and water change**
