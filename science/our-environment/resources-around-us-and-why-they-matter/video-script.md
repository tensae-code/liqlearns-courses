# Video script — Resources around us, and why they matter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soil from rock and rot. Water, plants, air, wild life — **natural**.
Bridges, monuments, squares — **made**.
Wild animals earn by living. A self-reliant country imports nothing it can grow.
Cut nothing without purpose. Cover the bare land. Plant the towns green.
