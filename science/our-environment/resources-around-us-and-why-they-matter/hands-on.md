# Hands-on — Resources around us, and why they matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Self-reliance table
List what your household eats and wears in a day. Mark MADE FROM OUR OWN RESOURCES where true. The marked fraction is self-reliance, measured.

## Purpose court
For one week, every plant cutting you witness gets one question: 'what is the purpose?' Log the answers — purposeful and not.

## Town trees count
Count the trees on one town street. Then count the bare spaces where the guide would plant. Present the planting case to a teacher or elder.
