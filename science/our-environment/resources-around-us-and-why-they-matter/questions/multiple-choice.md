# Resources around us, and why they matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which list is all natural resources?**
a) soil, water, plants, air, wild animals  b) bridges, monuments, squares  c) schools, dams, roads
**Answer: a) soil, water, plants, air, wild animals**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Soil is formed from —**
a) plastic and glass  b) tiny rock fragments and the decay of plants and animals  c) clouds
**Answer: b) tiny rock fragments and the decay of plants and animals**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A country self-reliant in food and clothing —**
a) must import everything  b) needs no imports  c) cannot trade
**Answer: b) needs no imports**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The guide's rule for cutting a plant is —**
a) cut freely  b) never cut any plant ever  c) do not cut without a purpose
**Answer: c) do not cut without a purpose**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Planting many trees in towns helps —**
a) dirty the air  b) clean the air  c) stop rain forever
**Answer: b) clean the air**
