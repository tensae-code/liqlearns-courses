# Lesson plan — Cycles — the carbon cycle

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Cycles — the carbon cycle
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — the eleventh title on the Science ladder (CURRICULUM.md). Unit 6 — The Earth, at General Science depth.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** carbon travels in a loop — released into the air by respiration and burning, taken in by plants for photosynthesis, passed to animals that eat the plants, returned by death and decomposition, and stored away in fossil fuels until burning releases it again.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Earth — four spheres, one system: the carbon cycle is the spheres' interdependence at work, carbon passing between atmosphere, biosphere, lithosphere and hydrosphere. It also stands on the earlier cell teaching (photosynthesis and respiration) at General Science depth.

## Sub lessons
1. **Carbon out — release** — respiration and burning put carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.
2. **Carbon in and around — uptake, eating, return and storage** — plants absorb carbon dioxide in photosynthesis; animals eat the plants; death and decomposition return the carbon; some buried carbon becomes fossil fuels, whose burning releases it again and closes the loop.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Earth — four spheres, one system (Earth and Space). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two ways carbon dioxide enters the air.
2. Say how plants take carbon in, and what the taking-in is called.
3. Trace carbon from a plant into an animal and back to the ground.
4. Say how fossil fuels fit the loop — where their carbon was, and how it comes back.

## What you need
A plant, a candle or a small fire to talk about (no need to light it), and tokens or stones to move around a drawn loop.

## If the learner is stuck
- Starts the loop at one fixed beginning → a cycle has none: start them at burning, and make them arrive back at burning by a different road.
- Forgets the fossil store → buried carbon is the loop's slow lane: it leaves the fast loop for ages, until burning brings it back at once.
- Mixes the two air moves → into the air: respiration and burning (release). Out of the air: photosynthesis (uptake). Two doors, opposite directions.
