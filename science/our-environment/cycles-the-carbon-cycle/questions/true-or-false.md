# Cycles — the carbon cycle — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Respiration and burning both put carbon dioxide into the air.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When a plant dies and decomposes, its carbon vanishes.
**Answer: False** — decomposition returns the carbon to the ground and the air; nothing vanishes, it moves.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The carbon cycle has a clear starting point and an end.
**Answer: False** — it is a loop with no starting point.
