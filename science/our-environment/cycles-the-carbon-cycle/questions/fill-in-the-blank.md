# Cycles — the carbon cycle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The gas that carries carbon into and out of the air in this cycle is carbon ______.
**Answer: dioxide.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dead plants and animals return their carbon as they ______.
**Answer: decompose (rot down).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Carbon buried deep for ages becomes ______ fuels.
**Answer: fossil.**
