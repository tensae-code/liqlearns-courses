# Cycles — the carbon cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these releases carbon dioxide into the air?
a) photosynthesis  b) respiration  c) eating  d) burial
**Answer: b) respiration** — and burning is the other release.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Burning wood releases the carbon that is in it as —
a) oxygen  b) water  c) carbon dioxide  d) soil
**Answer: c) carbon dioxide.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Plants take carbon dioxide out of the air by —
a) respiration  b) decomposition  c) photosynthesis  d) burning
**Answer: c) photosynthesis.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An animal gets its carbon by —
a) breathing it in  b) eating plants (or animals that ate plants)  c) making it in sunlight  d) digging it up
**Answer: b).**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Fossil fuels hold carbon that was —
a) never alive  b) buried from living things long ago  c) made in factories  d) rained from the sky
**Answer: b)** — buried carbon, stored deep for ages, released again by burning.
