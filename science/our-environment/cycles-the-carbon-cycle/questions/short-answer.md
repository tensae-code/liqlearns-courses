# Cycles — the carbon cycle — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Trace one carbon atom's journey from the air back to the air, through a plant and an animal.
**Answer: air → taken in by a plant in photosynthesis → eaten by an animal → released by the animal's respiration (or returned by death and decomposition, then released) → air again.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does burning fossil fuels add carbon dioxide to the air "all at once" compared with a tree rotting?
**Answer: the fossil carbon was stored away from the loop for ages; burning releases the whole store quickly, while rotting returns a single dead thing's carbon slowly.**
