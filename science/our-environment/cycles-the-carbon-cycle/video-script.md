# Video script — Cycles — the carbon cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Carbon **travels**. Round and round, in a loop.

You breathe **out** — carbon dioxide goes into the **air**.
A fire **burns** — carbon dioxide goes into the air too.

A green plant breathes it **in**. That taking-in is **photosynthesis**.
An animal eats the plant. The carbon moves on.
The animal dies. The plant dies. They **rot** — the carbon returns.

Some carbon is buried deep. It becomes **fossil fuel**.
Burn the fuel — the carbon flies back into the air.
The loop closes. The loop never stops. Now you walk it.
