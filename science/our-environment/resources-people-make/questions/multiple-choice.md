# Resources people make — and looking after them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which list is human-made resources?**
a) dam, mosque, church, school, hospital  b) river, soil, air, forest  c) rain, wind, sun
**Answer: a) dam, mosque, church, school, hospital**

**2. Which of these is a human-made resource?**
a) a river  b) a hospital  c) the air
**Answer: b) a hospital**

**3. Damaging a shared thing harms —**
a) nobody  b) everyone, including the one who did it  c) only visitors
**Answer: b) everyone, including the one who did it**
