# Lesson plan — Resources people make — and looking after them

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Our Environment · **Lesson:** Resources people make — and looking after them
**Place in the curriculum:** Our Environment — the human-made half of resources (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** things people create for use — dam, mosque, church, school, hospital — are human-made resources; as shared property they must be used and kept properly
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Resources around us — the man-made family was sorted there; this lesson, from the student book, is its keeping.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Natural Resources — the resources people make stand beside nature's in every town. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name human-made resources: dam, mosque, church, school, hospital.
2. Say the keeping rule: as shared property they must be used and kept properly.

## What you need
The shared buildings of your own area to visit in mind or in person; one act of keeping you can do this week.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats shared as ownerless ('not mine, so not my care') → the book's logic is the reverse: shared property is kept properly BECAUSE it is everyone's.
- Counts only grand buildings → the book's list includes the school itself: the nearest human-made resource is the room you learn in.
