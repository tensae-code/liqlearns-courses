# Hands-on — Resources people make — and looking after them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The biography of a building
Choose one shared building. Write its biography: built by whom, for what, used by whom, kept by whom. Interview its keeper if you can.

## Desk covenant
Adopt your own desk and chair for the term: clean weekly, repair reported, nothing carved. The smallest shared property, kept properly, is the rule practised.

## Dam or hospital visit in words
If a visit is impossible, let an elder narrate one: what the dam holds back, whom the hospital has healed. Shared things have stories; collect one.
