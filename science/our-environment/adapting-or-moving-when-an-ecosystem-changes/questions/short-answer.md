# Adapting or moving when an ecosystem changes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: What happens when the ecosystem changes.
**Answer:** what happens when the ecosystem changes: some living things are given the ability to keep living when their system changes, changing their behaviour or their homes — that response is adaptation; after a forest fire destroys the grass, deer change their food to tree bark and other plants; others cannot cope and must move to a new place with food and water (the crow on city buildings, which are not the birds' natural system); and fires themselves sometimes benefit the forest and its remaining plants.
