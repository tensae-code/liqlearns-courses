# Hands-on — Adapting or moving when an ecosystem changes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: what happens when the ecosystem changes: some living things are given the ability to keep living when their system changes, changing their behaviour or their homes — that response is adaptation; after a forest fire destroys the grass, deer change their food to tree bark and other plants; others cannot cope and must move to a new place with food and water (the crow on city buildings, which are not the birds' natural system); and fires themselves sometimes benefit the forest and its remaining plants Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
