# Endangered species of Guatemala — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How is extinction different from one individual dying?
**Answer:** An individual dying is ordinary and the kind continues; extinction is the kind itself ending for ever — and once extinct, a kind does not come back.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does Guatemala's guide teach its own country's endangered species?
**Answer:** Because endangered species are not only a world topic: every country has its own kinds at risk, and the guide makes Guatemalan learners meet their own country's list as their own content, in a complementary activity.
