# Endangered species of Guatemala — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Extinction means…
a) One individual dying  b) A kind of living thing ending for ever  c) An animal sleeping  d) A species moving country
**Answer: b) A kind of living thing ending for ever**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A species is in danger of extinction when…
a) It is very common  b) Its kind is at risk of disappearing altogether  c) It lives in a zoo  d) It is large
**Answer: b) Its kind is at risk of disappearing altogether**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Guatemala's guide teaches the endangered species of…
a) The whole world only  b) Guatemala itself — the country's own content  c) No country in particular  d) The oceans only
**Answer: b) Guatemala itself — the country's own content**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The guide sets this content as…
a) An examination  b) A complementary activity  c) A song  d) A map exercise
**Answer: b) A complementary activity**
