# Video script — Endangered species of Guatemala

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Endangered species of Guatemala** — the country's own content, from Guatemala's own guide.

One animal dying is ordinary. Extinction is different: it is the whole kind ending, for ever.
A species in danger of extinction is a kind at risk of that ending — so few left, in so few places, that without care there will soon be none.
And Guatemala's Grade 7 guide teaches this the local way: not a far-away list, but the species in danger of extinction in Guatemala itself, set as a complementary activity for Guatemalan learners.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
