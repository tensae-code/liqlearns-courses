# Hands-on — Endangered species of Guatemala

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the complementary activity
Guatemala's guide sets this as a complementary activity — so do it as one: research the species in danger of extinction in Guatemala and make the country's own list, one card per species: its name, where in Guatemala it lives, and why it is in danger.

## Many, fewer, almost none
For one species on your list, draw the three panels: many, fewer, almost none. Under the last panel, write what would be lost for ever if the kind ended — and one thing people can do before that panel is reached.
