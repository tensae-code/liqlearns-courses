# Endangered species of Guatemala

## Do it first
Before you read: can a whole kind of living thing disappear — not one animal dying, but the kind ending for ever? Has it ever happened? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Guatemala's way in — its Grade 7 guide sets the species in danger of extinction in Guatemala as the country's own content, a complementary activity alongside its environment experiences.

## Sub lesson 1 — In danger of extinction — what it means

Individuals die; that is ordinary. **Extinction** is different: it is the **kind** ending.

A species is **in danger of extinction** when its kind is at risk of disappearing altogether — when the numbers and the places it lives have shrunk so far that, without care, there will soon be none left anywhere. Once a kind is extinct, it does not come back.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's own endangered species

Guatemala's guide does not teach this as an abstract list from far away. It sets **the species in danger of extinction in Guatemala** — the country's own species, in the country's own surroundings — as a **complementary activity**: extra work the learner does alongside the main environment experiences, because this content belongs to Guatemala itself.

That is the lesson's second half: endangered species are not only a world topic. Every country has its own kinds at risk, and Guatemala's guide makes its learners meet **their own country's** list.

## The key ideas

- **Extinction** is a kind ending for ever; a species **in danger of extinction** is at risk of that ending.
- Guatemala's guide teaches **the species in danger of extinction in Guatemala** as the country's own content, set as a **complementary activity**.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order — what "in danger of extinction" means first, then Guatemala's own list as the country's own content. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "How is extinction different from one animal dying?" Answer with the kind ending, not by repeating.
