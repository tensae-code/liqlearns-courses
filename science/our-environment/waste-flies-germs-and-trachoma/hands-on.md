# Hands-on — Waste, flies, germs and trachoma

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cut the road twice
At home for one day: waste covered, food covered. You have cut the fly's road at both ends — and can say why each cut works.

## The heap inspection
With an adult, inspect where the neighbourhood's waste lies open. Count the flies. Write one sentence of report: place, flies, danger. Health begins as honest looking.
