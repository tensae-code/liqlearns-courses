# Video script — Waste, flies, germs and trachoma

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Waste left open **breeds flies**. Flies carry **germs** — from the heap to food and faces.
**Trachoma** is the eye disease on that road. Untreated, it darkens sight, step by step.
A **properly built latrine** cuts the road at its start.
