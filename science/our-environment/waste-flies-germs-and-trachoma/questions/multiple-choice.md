# Waste, flies, germs and trachoma — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Waste dumped anywhere breeds —**
a) bees  b) flies  c) frogs
**Answer: b) flies**

**2. Flies carry — from waste to people's bodies.**
a) water  b) seeds  c) germs
**Answer: c) germs**

**3. Trachoma is a disease of the —**
a) eyes  b) feet  c) teeth
**Answer: a) eyes**

**4. Untreated, trachoma leads gradually to —**
a) deafness  b) blindness  c) toothache
**Answer: b) blindness**

**5. The book's named prevention is —**
a) a properly built latrine  b) more flies  c) open dumping far away
**Answer: a) a properly built latrine**
