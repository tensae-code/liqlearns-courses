# Hands-on — Pond and lake life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw one pond, whole (paper and pencil)
Draw a pond or lake in cross-section — edge, surface and water below. Place and label: reeds at the edge, a water lily on the surface, a frog at the shallows, a fish under the water, and a water bird above. Your drawing is the community: check no station is empty.
