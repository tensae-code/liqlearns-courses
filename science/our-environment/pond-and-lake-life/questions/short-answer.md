# Pond and lake life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The standing-water community.
**Answer:** A **pond** and a **lake** hold **standing water** — water that gathers and stays — and the living things in and around it make one **community**: plants, animals and the water they share, living together in one place..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Plants of the pond and lake — reeds and water lilies.
**Answer:** The community's plants fit their places: **reeds** stand at the water's edge, and **water lilies** float their leaves on the surface.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Animals of the pond and lake — frogs, fish and water birds.
**Answer:** The community's animals fill the rest: **frogs** at the edge and in the shallows, **fish** in the water, and **water birds** on and over it.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The standing-water community → Plants of the pond and lake — reeds and water lilies → Animals of the pond and lake — frogs, fish and water birds — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
