# Pond and lake life

## Do it first
Look into still water — a pond, or the quiet edge of a lake. The surface is calm, but the place is full of life, above and below. Say one living thing you expect at the edge, and one in the water. Then read on: this is Serbia's way in — pond and lake.

## Sub lesson 1 — The standing-water community

A **pond** and a **lake** hold **standing water** — water that gathers and stays — and the living things in and around it make one **community**: plants, animals and the water they share, living together in one place.

## Sub lesson 2 — Plants of the pond and lake — reeds and water lilies

The community's plants fit their places: **reeds** stand at the water's edge, and **water lilies** float their leaves on the surface. Edge and surface — each plant holds its own station in the standing water.

## Sub lesson 3 — Animals of the pond and lake — frogs, fish and water birds

The community's animals fill the rest: **frogs** at the edge and in the shallows, **fish** in the water, and **water birds** on and over it. From reeds to birds — the pond and lake are taught as one whole community, every member in its place.

- **Plants of the pond and lake — reeds and water lilies** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "Is this member a plant of the edge, a plant of the surface, or an animal?" Answer: Reeds hold the edge; water lilies float on the surface; frogs, fish and water birds are the community's animals.
