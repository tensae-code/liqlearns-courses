# Video script — Pond and lake life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Pond and lake life** — Serbia's way, from its Grade 3 *Природа и друштво* (Бара и језеро (Pond and lake), printed p. 62).

A **pond** and a **lake** hold **standing water** — water that gathers and stays — and the living things in and around it make one **community**: plants, animals and the water they share, living together in one place.

The community's plants fit their places: **reeds** stand at the water's edge, and **water lilies** float their leaves on the surface. Edge and surface — each plant holds its own station in the standing water.

The community's animals fill the rest: **frogs** at the edge and in the shallows, **fish** in the water, and **water birds** on and over it. From reeds to birds — the pond and lake are taught as one whole community, every member in its place.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
