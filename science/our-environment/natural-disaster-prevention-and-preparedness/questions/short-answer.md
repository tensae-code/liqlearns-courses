# Natural disaster prevention and preparedness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: How a volcanic eruption happens.
**Answer:** Ecuador's Grade 5 book teaches the **volcanic eruption process in detail**: an eruption is the volcano's material breaking out at the surface. Knowing how an eruption unfolds — the process, step by step as the book details it — is the ground prevention stands on: a community prepares against the eruption it understands.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Prevention measures — before eruptions and natural disasters.
**Answer:** The book teaches **prevention measures before eruptions and natural disasters**: what is done in advance, before the eruption or the disaster, so that people are ready when it comes. Prevention belongs to the time before — measures taken beforehand, not improvised during the disaster — and it covers natural disasters generally, with the volcanic eruption as the worked case.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: An album of Ecuador's volcanoes.
**Answer:** The unit closes with an **album of Ecuador's volcanoes**: the country's own volcanoes, gathered and presented by the learners. The album is Ecuador's own way of finishing the topic — the danger studied is not abstract; it is the volcanoes of the learners' own country, named and collected.
