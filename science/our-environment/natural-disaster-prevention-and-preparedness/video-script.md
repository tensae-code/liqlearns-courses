Today: **Natural disaster prevention and preparedness** — Ecuador's way, from its Grade 5 Ciencias Naturales (printed pp. 116–121).

Ecuador's Grade 5 book teaches the volcanic eruption process in detail: an eruption is the volcano's material breaking out at the surface. Knowing how an eruption unfolds — the process, step by step as the book details it — is the ground prevention stands on: a community prepares against the eruption it understands.

The book teaches prevention measures before eruptions and natural disasters: what is done in advance, before the eruption or the disaster, so that people are ready when it comes. Prevention belongs to the time before — measures taken beforehand, not improvised during the disaster — and it covers natural disasters generally, with the volcanic eruption as the worked case.

The unit closes with an album of Ecuador's volcanoes: the country's own volcanoes, gathered and presented by the learners. The album is Ecuador's own way of finishing the topic — the danger studied is not abstract; it is the volcanoes of the learners' own country, named and collected.
