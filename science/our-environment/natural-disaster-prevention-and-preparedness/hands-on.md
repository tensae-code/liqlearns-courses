# Hands-on — Natural disaster prevention and preparedness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My volcano album page and prevention list
Make one page of a volcano album, in the book's closing way: one volcano of Ecuador, as the book presents it, named and drawn or described. Under it, write the prevention list: the measures to take before an eruption or another natural disaster, in the order a family would do them.
