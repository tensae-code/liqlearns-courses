Today: **Water environments — fresh and salt** — Iraq's way, from Grade 1.

Rivers and seas open the subject, and the measure is given at once: most water environments are salty, some are fresh — lakes are fresh. The lake is a closed-edge water environment, and Iraq's own river is the Euphrates. Grade 2 studies sea and ocean water's properties — and sets against them the fresh marshes, home to fish and birds.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
