# Water environments — fresh and salt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the properties study the Grade 2 book makes.
**Answer: The properties of sea and ocean water.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why are fresh water environments called varied?
**Answer: Because fresh is not one kind of place — marshes, lakes and rivers are all fresh, and each is its own environment.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the activity book's way of working on water environments.
**Answer: Comparing their likeness and difference.**
