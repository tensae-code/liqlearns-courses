# Water environments — fresh and salt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Most water environments are…
a) Fresh  b) Salty  c) Dry  d) Frozen
**Answer: b) Salty**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The book's fresh example in Grade 1 is…
a) The sea  b) The ocean  c) Lakes  d) The marsh only
**Answer: c) Lakes**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A lake is a water environment with…
a) No edges  b) Closed edges  c) Only salt water  d) No water
**Answer: b) Closed edges**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Iraq's own river example is…
a) The Nile  b) The Euphrates  c) The Danube  d) The Amazon
**Answer: b) The Euphrates**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The marshes are…
a) Salt water, home to no life  b) Fresh water environments, home to fish and birds  c) Made of sand  d) Underground only
**Answer: b) Fresh water environments, home to fish and birds**
