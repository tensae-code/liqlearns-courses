# Hands-on — Water environments — fresh and salt

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Likeness and difference
The activity book's own work: take two water environments — a sea and a marsh, or a river and a lake. Compare them: likeness and difference, in a two-column table.

## Fresh or salt?
List every water environment you can name. Sort the list: fresh, or salt. Check the hardest ones against the lesson.
