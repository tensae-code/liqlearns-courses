# Hands-on — Living together — mutualism, commensalism and parasitism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: symbiosis in its three kinds: mutualism, where both gain (the bird that picks insects off the zebra's skin); commensalism, where one gains and the other is unaffected (birds nesting in trees); parasitism, where one gains at the other's expense (skin insects feeding on the zebra) Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
