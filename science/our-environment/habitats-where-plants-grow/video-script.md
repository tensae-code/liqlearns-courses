Today: **Habitats where plants grow** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 13).

Plants grow where their needs are met. Some plants live in sunny places; others grow in shady, moist places where the light is filtered and the ground stays damp.
Some plants live in water itself, and others live both on land and in water, rooted where the two meet. A plant's home is read from where it is found growing, not from where a seed happened to fall.
Bangladesh's Class 4 book closes with two hard homes: saline soil, where the Sundarbans mangroves breathe through special roots called pneumatophores that rise out of the salty mud, and other trees themselves, where some plants grow on a host tree.
