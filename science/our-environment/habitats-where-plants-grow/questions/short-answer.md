# Habitats where plants grow — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Sunny and shady homes.
**Answer:** Plants grow where their needs are met.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Water plants and land-and-water plants.
**Answer:** Some plants live in water itself, and others live both on land and in water, rooted where the two meet.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Hard homes — salt soil and other trees.
**Answer:** Bangladesh's Class 4 book closes with two hard homes: saline soil, where the Sundarbans mangroves breathe through special roots called pneumatophores that rise out of the salty mud, and other trees themselves, where some plants grow on a host tree..

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Sunny and shady homes → Water plants and land-and-water plants → Hard homes — salt soil and other trees — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
