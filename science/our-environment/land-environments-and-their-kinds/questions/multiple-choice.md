# Land environments and their kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Land environments first sort into…
a) Wet and dry  b) Natural, and made by people  c) Big and small  d) Near and far
**Answer: b) Natural, and made by people**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A forest is…
a) Made by people  b) A natural land environment full of big trees  c) A water environment  d) A kind of grassland
**Answer: b) A natural land environment full of big trees**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Grasslands are…
a) Narrow streets  b) Wide lands of long grasses with grazing animals  c) Lands with no plants  d) Frozen lands
**Answer: b) Wide lands of long grasses with grazing animals**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The activity book's land work is…
a) Only reading  b) Marking environments natural or made, tick or cross  c) Counting trees only  d) Drawing one map
**Answer: b) Marking environments natural or made, tick or cross**

**5.** (Sub lesson 1) Each kind of land environment has…
a) The same properties  b) Its own properties  c) No properties  d) Only a name in Arabic
**Answer: b) Its own properties**
