# Land environments and their kinds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the two first kinds land environments sort into.
**Answer: Natural environments, and environments made by people.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the book's marks of a grassland.
**Answer: Wide lands of long grasses, with grazing animals.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the Grade 2 activity book deepen the work?
**Answer: By inquiring into the land environments' types and properties, not only their names.**
