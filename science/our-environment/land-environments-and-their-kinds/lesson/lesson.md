# Land environments and their kinds

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — the kinds of land environment — forest and grassland among them — and the sorting of land environments into natural ones and ones made by people, as Iraq's Grade 1–2 pupil's and activity books teach it. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Iraq's way in — its Ministry of Education *Science* books (the Primary Science Book Series) teach this thing at the places named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — The land environment is varied — natural, or made by people

Iraq's Grade 1 book opens the land environment as something **varied**: land environments are named from pictures and sorted into two kinds — **natural** environments, and environments **made by people**. The activity book works the same sorting with ticks and crosses: mark each pictured environment natural or made.

The Grade 2 activity book goes further in: inquiring into the land environments' **types and properties** — each kind has its own properties, not only its own name.

## Sub lesson 2 — Forest and grassland — two natural kinds

The Grade 2 book names kinds. The **forest** is a natural land environment **full of big trees**. The **grasslands — pastures** — are **wide lands of long grasses with grazing animals**.

Forest and grassland are both natural, and they are not the same kind: the trees make the one, the wide grass and its grazers make the other. Kind is decided by what fills the land.

## The key ideas

- Land environments are **varied**, and sort into **natural** ones and ones **made by people**.
- The **forest** is a natural land environment full of big trees.
- **Grasslands** are wide lands of long grasses with grazing animals.
- Each kind of land environment has its own properties.
