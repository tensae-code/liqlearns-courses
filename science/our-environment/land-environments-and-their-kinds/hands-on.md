# Hands-on — Land environments and their kinds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tick or cross
Draw six land scenes — or find six pictures. Mark each one: natural, or made by people — the activity book's own tick-and-cross work.

## Inquire into a kind
Choose one kind of land environment. Inquire into its type and properties: what fills it, what lives there, what the ground and cover are like. Write three properties.
