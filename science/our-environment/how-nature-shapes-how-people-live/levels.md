# How nature shapes how people live — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The significance of natural conditions in human life, Nature and the economic activity of people, The economic significance of natural objects, The natural conditions of the region where you live), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 4 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks.
