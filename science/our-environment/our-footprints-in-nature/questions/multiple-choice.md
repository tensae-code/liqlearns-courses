# Our footprints in nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Our footprint in nature is…
a) our shoe size  b) the human impact we leave on nature  c) a hiking trail  d) a map symbol  
**Answer: b) the human impact we leave on nature**

**2.** The book's order is…
a) reduce first, never measure  b) measure, then reduce  c) only measure  d) only celebrate  
**Answer: b) measure, then reduce**

**3.** A footprint is gathered into one picture so the impact can be…
a) hidden  b) sold  c) seen whole  d) forgotten  
**Answer: c) seen whole**
