# Video script — Cycles of matter — carbon, nitrogen, water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The air is full of **nitrogen** — but no plant can use it as it is.
First it is **changed**: into ammonia and nitrates a root can take.
Plants build protein. Animals eat. Bodies and wastes fall.
**Decomposers** break them down to ammonium.
**Denitrifying bacteria** let the nitrogen gas go — the loop is closed.
**Matter cycles.** Energy does not — it flows one way, and away.
