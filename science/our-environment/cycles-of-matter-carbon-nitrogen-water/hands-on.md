# Hands-on — Cycles of matter — carbon, nitrogen, water

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Loop with tokens
Lay a rope or draw a circle with six stops: AIR — CONVERSION — PLANT — ANIMAL — DECOMPOSERS — BACK TO AIR. Move a stone as one nitrogen atom around the loop, saying at each stop what form the nitrogen is in and who changes it.

## Cycle or flow?
Sort six cards — sunlight, heat loss, nitrogen, carbon, water, the 10% shrinking — into two piles: CYCLES BACK and FLOWS ONE WAY. Defend each placing in one sentence.
