# Cycles of matter — carbon, nitrogen, water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the nitrogen loop in order: plant protein — nitrogen gas in air — ammonium compounds — ammonia and nitrates.
**Answer:** Nitrogen gas in air → conversion to ammonia and nitrates → plant protein → (wastes/dead bodies) decomposers make ammonium compounds → denitrification back to nitrogen gas.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two kinds of bacteria the guide names for denitrification.
**Answer:** Pseudomonas and Clostridium species.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Say the lesson's contrast in one sentence, with one example of each side.
**Answer:** Matter is recycled (e.g. nitrogen/carbon/water loop round), while energy flows one way and is lost as heat.
