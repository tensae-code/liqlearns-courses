# Cycles of matter — carbon, nitrogen, water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plants cannot use nitrogen straight from the air because —
a) there is no nitrogen in air  b) it must first be converted into ammonia and nitrates  c) plants do not need nitrogen  d) nitrogen is a poison
**Answer: b) it must first be converted into ammonia and nitrates** — air is mostly nitrogen, but in an unusable form.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Decomposers in the nitrogen cycle break protein in wastes and dead bodies down into —
a) nitrogen gas  b) ammonium compounds  c) oxygen  d) protein again
**Answer: b) ammonium compounds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Denitrification is —
a) nitrate being reduced back to nitrogen gas  b) plants taking in nitrates  c) animals eating plants  d) nitrogen entering the air from fires
**Answer: a) nitrate being reduced back to nitrogen gas** — performed by Pseudomonas and Clostridium under anaerobic conditions.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these CYCLES (is recycled) in an ecosystem? 
a) energy  b) heat  c) nitrogen  d) sunlight
**Answer: c) nitrogen** — matter cycles; energy flows one way.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Energy leaves an ecosystem mainly as —
a) nitrates  b) heat  c) protein  d) rain
**Answer: b) heat** — that is why new sunlight is needed daily.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** An ecosystem under full sunlight can still starve if —
a) a matter cycle is broken, e.g. the soil's decomposers are destroyed  b) the sun is too bright  c) animals sleep  d) rain falls
**Answer: a) a matter cycle is broken, e.g. the soil's decomposers are destroyed** — no new nitrogen or carbon arrives — the cycles must keep working.
