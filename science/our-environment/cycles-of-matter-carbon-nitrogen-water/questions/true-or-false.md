# Cycles of matter — carbon, nitrogen, water — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Denitrifying bacteria work under anaerobic — no-oxygen — conditions.
**Answer: True** — Pseudomonas and Clostridium species, as the guide's notes name them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Energy is recycled in an ecosystem just like nitrogen.
**Answer: False** — Energy flows one way and dissipates as heat; only matter cycles.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The same nitrogen atoms can be used again and again.
**Answer: True** — That is what calling it a cycle means.
