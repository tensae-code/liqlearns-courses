# Video script — Extinct and endangered living things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Extinct and endangered living things** — Kuwait's way: living things that are extinct — gone for ever — and living things in danger of extinction, and why the danger can still be turned back.

A kind of living thing is extinct when every last one of it has died and none will ever live again. Extinction is for ever: no care, no money and no science can bring the kind back. Kuwait's Grade 4 book sets extinction beside its warning form: a kind that still lives but is down to few, and falling, is endangered — in danger of extinction. The two words are a before-and-after: endangered is the last chance before extinct. Kinds disappear when their habitat is destroyed, when they are hunted too much, when pollution poisons them, or when new living things brought by people out-compete or eat them. Every one of those causes has a human hand in it — which means human hands can also stop it. Endangered kinds are saved by protecting their habitat, stopping the hunting, and breeding them carefully until their numbers climb. The lesson's point is the difference one stage makes: an endangered kind can still be saved; an extinct kind cannot.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
