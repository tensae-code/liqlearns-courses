# Extinct and endangered living things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is the difference between extinct and endangered?
**Answer: Extinct kinds are gone for ever; endangered kinds still live but are few and falling, so they can still be saved.**

**2.** Name three causes of kinds disappearing.
**Answer: Habitat destruction, over-hunting and pollution (introduced living things are another).**

**3.** How are endangered kinds saved?
**Answer: By protecting their habitat, stopping the hunting and breeding them carefully until numbers climb.**

**4.** Why is 'endangered' called the last chance?
**Answer: Because at that stage saving is still possible; after extinction nothing can bring the kind back.**
