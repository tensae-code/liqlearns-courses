# Extinct and endangered living things — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A kind gone for ever is _____.
**Answer: extinct**

**2.** A kind few and falling is _____ .
**Answer: endangered**

**3.** Kinds disappear when their _____ is destroyed.
**Answer: habitat**

**4.** An endangered kind can still be _____.
**Answer: saved**
