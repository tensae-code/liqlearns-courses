# Extinct and endangered living things — multiple choice


**1.** Extinct means…
a) Sleeping for winter  b) Gone for ever — every one has died  c) Hiding from people  d) Very rare but safe
**Answer: b) Gone for ever — every one has died**

**2.** An endangered kind is…
a) Already extinct  b) Few and falling, in danger of extinction  c) Growing fast  d) Kept only in zoos
**Answer: b) Few and falling, in danger of extinction**

**3.** Which is a cause of kinds disappearing?
a) Protecting habitat  b) Over-hunting  c) Careful breeding  d) Clean water
**Answer: b) Over-hunting**

**4.** An endangered kind can still be…
a) Brought back from extinction  b) Saved  c) Nothing at all  d) Turned into another kind
**Answer: b) Saved**

**5.** Once a kind is extinct, it…
a) Returns in spring  b) Can never live again  c) Hides underground  d) Becomes endangered
**Answer: b) Can never live again**

**6.** Saving an endangered kind starts with protecting its…
a) Name  b) Habitat  c) Picture  d) Story
**Answer: b) Habitat**
