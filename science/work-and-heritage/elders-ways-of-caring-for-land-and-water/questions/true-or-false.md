# Culture — Our Elders' Ways of Caring for Land and Water — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Contour ploughing runs across the slope.**
**Answer: True** — Ploughing follows the contour, not straight downhill.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Manure feeds the soil.**
**Answer: True** — Adding manure is a listed traditional method.
