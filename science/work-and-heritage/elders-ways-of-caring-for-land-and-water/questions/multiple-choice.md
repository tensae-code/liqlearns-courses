# Culture — Our Elders' Ways of Caring for Land and Water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Terraces on a hillside —**
a) speed water up  b) hold the soil and slow the water  c) are decoration
**Answer: b) hold the soil and slow the water** — Terraces hold soil and slow runoff.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Fallowing (ማከር) means —**
a) ploughing every day  b) resting a field so it recovers  c) burning the field
**Answer: b) resting a field so it recovers** — Fallowing rests the field.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which protects a spring?**
a) Throwing waste in it  b) Keeping waste away from it  c) Trampling its banks
**Answer: b) Keeping waste away from it** — Rivers and springs are protected from waste.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A drain in a field (ቦይ) is for —**
a) guiding water  b) storing grain  c) marking games
**Answer: a) guiding water** — Field drains guide water.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Planting trees near a river —**
a) holds banks and shades the water  b) dries the river at once  c) is forbidden
**Answer: a) holds banks and shades the water** — Trees protect banks and water.
