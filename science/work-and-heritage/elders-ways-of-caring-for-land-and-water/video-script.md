# Video script — Culture — Our Elders' Ways of Caring for Land and Water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The elders knew how to hold the land.

Terraces. Drains. Ploughing across the slope.

Rest the field. Feed it with manure. Guard the spring.

Their ways still care for our land and water.
