# Culture and Climate — How the Weather Shapes Our Way of Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Explain why lowlanders wear white and highlanders dark, thick clothes.**
**Answer:** White reflects the sun's heat in hot lowlands; thick dark clothes hold warmth in cold highlands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Match two products to their zones from the table.**
**Answer:** Dega — potato; weyna dega — maize; qolla — cotton; berha — incense (any two).
