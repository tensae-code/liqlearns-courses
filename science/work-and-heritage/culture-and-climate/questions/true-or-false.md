# Culture and Climate — How the Weather Shapes Our Way of Life — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Highland clothes are thick because the highlands are cold.**
**Answer: True** — Dress follows climate.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Camels are kept in the qolla, horses in the dega.**
