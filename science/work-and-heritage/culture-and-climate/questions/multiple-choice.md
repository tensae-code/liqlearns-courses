# Culture and Climate — How the Weather Shapes Our Way of Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Lowlanders wear white mainly because —**
a) it is cheaper everywhere  b) white reflects the sun's heat in hot places  c) it is a uniform
**Answer: b) white reflects the sun's heat in hot places** — White reflects heat in the hot lowlands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In the book's table, cotton belongs to —**
a) dega  b) weyna dega  c) qolla
**Answer: c) qolla** — Qolla — cotton.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Maize in the book's zone table belongs to —**
a) berha  b) weyna dega  c) wurch
**Answer: b) weyna dega** — Weyna dega — maize.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) House building following climate means —**
a) houses answer heat, cold and rain of their place  b) all houses are identical  c) houses ignore weather
**Answer: a) houses answer heat, cold and rain of their place** — Building answers the local climate.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Incense in the table comes from —**
a) berha  b) dega  c) weyna dega
**Answer: a) berha** — Berha — incense.
