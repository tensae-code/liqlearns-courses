# Culture and Climate — How the Weather Shapes Our Way of Life

## Do it first

A camel stands in hot sand; a horse stands in cool green hills. Why does each place keep its own animal — and wear its own clothes? Think, then read.

## Sub lesson 1 — Clothes follow the climate

Why do lowlanders wear white and highlanders dark, thick clothes? Because dress answers the weather: in the hot lowlands, white reflects the sun's heat; in the cold highlands, thick dark clothes hold warmth. What looks like fashion is climate at work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Crops, animals, houses and food by zone

Everything follows: crops differ by zone (one table runs: dega — potato; weyna dega — maize; qolla — cotton; berha — incense…); animals differ (horses in the dega, camels in the qolla); house building, dress and food all take the climate's shape.
