# Hands-on — The doctor — diagnosis, treatment and prevention

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: careers linked to science: "The Doctor" — medicine is one of the most important health sciences needed by everyone, and the doctor shares in diagnosing, treating and preventing diseases and injuries; becoming a doctor who achieves high ambitions takes organising one's study time, eating healthy meals, patience, perseverance, communicating with others and bearing responsibility Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
