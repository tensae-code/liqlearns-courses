Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Toys and objects from sticks, straws and fibres  b) Objects in clay and wire  c) Objects from bottles, boxes, wood and thread  d) Toys and shapes from paper

a) Objects in clay and wire  b) Objects from bottles, boxes, wood and thread  c) Toys and shapes from paper  d) Toys and objects from sticks, straws and fibres

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about objects in clay and wire?
a) Objects in clay and wire  b) Toys and shapes from paper  c) Toys and objects from sticks, straws and fibres  d) Objects from bottles, boxes, wood and thread
**Answer: a) Objects in clay and wire**
