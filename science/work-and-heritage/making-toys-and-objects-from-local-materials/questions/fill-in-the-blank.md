# Making toys and objects from local materials — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A toy _____ is made out of paper in Primary 2.
**Answer: bird**

**2.** A toy car is made from dry _____ sticks or straws.
**Answer: sorghum**

**3.** A goat _____ is modelled out of clay.
**Answer: sculpture**

**4.** A toy airplane is made from an empty plastic _____.
**Answer: bottle**
