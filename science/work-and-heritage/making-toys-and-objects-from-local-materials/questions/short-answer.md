# Making toys and objects from local materials — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Toys and shapes from paper" teach?
**Answer:** Paper is the first making material: Rwanda's Primary 2 book makes a **toy bird out of paper**, then a **rectangle and a square out of paper**; the older years make **learning materials in paper** and shape **regular polygons and solids**.

**2.** What does the part "Toys and objects from sticks, straws and fibres" teach?
**Answer:** Sticks and straws build too: a **toy car from dry sorghum sticks or straws**, **utility objects from sticks**, and — Rwanda's own way — **utility objects with banana fibres**, the fibre of the banana plant as a making material; Primary 5 returns to toys using sorghum straws and sticks.

**3.** What does the part "Objects in clay and wire" teach?
**Answer:** Clay is modelled: a **goat sculpture** and a **bird sculpture out of clay** in Primary 2, **toys in clay and wires** in Primary 4, and toys using clay and using wires again in Primary 6.

**4.** What does the part "Objects from bottles, boxes, wood and thread" teach?
**Answer:** Nothing is wasted: a **toy airplane from an empty plastic bottle**, a **wall clock toy from a box**, **utility objects in wood**, and utility objects **in thread**.
