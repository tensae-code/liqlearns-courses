# The Venezuelan inventor of precooked corn flour

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's title — The Venezuelan inventor of precooked corn flour. Say out loud anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on: this is Venezuela's way in — its Grade 7 *Alimentando con Ciencias* closes with a complementary reading about the Venezuelan who changed the country's daily food: Luis Caballero Mejías, inventor of precooked corn flour.

## Sub lesson 1 — Luis Caballero Mejías and his invention

The complementary reading tells of **Luis Caballero Mejías**, the Venezuelan who **invented precooked corn flour** — flour already cooked before it is sold, so the daily work of preparing corn dough is transformed: add water, and the dough is ready. An invention measured at every breakfast table in the country.
