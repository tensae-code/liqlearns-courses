# The Venezuelan inventor of precooked corn flour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why is precooked corn flour an invention, not just a product?
**Answer: Because it changed the process itself: cooking moved into the flour before sale, transforming the daily work of making corn dough.**

**2.** What model does the 'periodic table of foods' borrow?
**Answer: The chemist's periodic table — foods set out in an ordered table on that model.**
