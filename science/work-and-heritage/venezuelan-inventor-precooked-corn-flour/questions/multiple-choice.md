# The Venezuelan inventor of precooked corn flour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Luis Caballero Mejías invented…
a) the water mill  b) precooked corn flour  c) the gurrufío  d) solar panels  
**Answer: b) precooked corn flour**

**2.** Precooked flour is cooked…
a) after the dough rests  b) before it is sold  c) never  d) only in factories abroad  
**Answer: b) before it is sold**

**3.** The reading's closing table orders…
a) elements  b) rivers  c) foods  d) dams  
**Answer: c) foods**
