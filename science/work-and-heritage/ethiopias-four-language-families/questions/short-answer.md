# Ethiopia's Four Language Families — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many languages and nations does the book count for Ethiopia?**
**Answer:** More than 70 languages and more than 80 nations and nationalities.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Place these in their families: Tigrinya, Afarigna, Gamogna, Nuer.**
**Answer:** Tigrinya — Semitic; Afarigna — Cushitic; Gamogna — Omotic; Nuer — Nilo-Saharan.
