# Ethiopia's Four Language Families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Ethiopia's languages fall into —**
a) two families  b) four families  c) eighty families
**Answer: b) four families** — Four families hold all 70+ languages.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The book calls Ethiopia —**
a) a museum of peoples  b) a single-language country  c) an island
**Answer: a) a museum of peoples** — A museum of peoples: 80+ nations and nationalities.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Amharic belongs to which family?**
a) Cushitic  b) Semitic  c) Omotic
**Answer: b) Semitic** — Amharic stands in the Semitic family with Ge'ez and Tigrinya.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The most widely spread family is —**
a) Omotic  b) Nilo-Saharan  c) Cushitic
**Answer: c) Cushitic** — Cushitic — Oromigna, Somaligna, Afarigna, Agewigna — spreads widest.
