# Work and Livelihood — Trade That Harms Nature; Ways of Travelling — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Selling sand and stone can also disturb nature.**
**Answer: True** — Sand, stone and gravel are named in the list.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A bajaj and an aeroplane are modern transport.**
**Answer: True** — Both are named among modern ways.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Modern transport is sorted into land, air and water transport.**
**Answer: True** — Those are the three modern kinds.
