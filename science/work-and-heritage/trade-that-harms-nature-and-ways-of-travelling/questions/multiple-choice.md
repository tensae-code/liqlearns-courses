# Work and Livelihood — Trade That Harms Nature; Ways of Travelling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The charcoal and timber trade can —**
a) plant forests  b) strip forests and erode soil  c) clean rivers
**Answer: b) strip forests and erode soil** — It strips forests and erodes soil.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) 'The ship of the desert' is —**
a) the donkey  b) the camel  c) the train
**Answer: b) the camel** — The camel is the ship of the desert.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Drying rivers can follow from —**
a) stripping forests for the timber trade  b) planting trees  c) rain
**Answer: a) stripping forests for the timber trade** — Forest stripping dries rivers, per the book.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which is traditional transport?**
a) Aeroplane  b) Donkey carrying loads  c) Train
**Answer: b) Donkey carrying loads** — Pack animals are traditional.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Transport's job in trade is to —**
a) move people and products  b) stop goods moving  c) hide goods
**Answer: a) move people and products** — Transport moves people and products.
