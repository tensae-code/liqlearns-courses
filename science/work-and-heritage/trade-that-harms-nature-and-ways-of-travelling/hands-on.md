# Hands-on — Work and Livelihood — Trade That Harms Nature; Ways of Travelling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Load and carry
Compare loads: how much can a donkey carry against a pickup? Sort load cards to the carrier that can bear them.

## Trade-harm debate
Two teams: 'this trade feeds us' vs 'this trade eats nature' — then together write the careful middle rule.

## Compare the vessels
Choose one good your family buys. Trace how it travelled to you — every vessel it rode. Then write which leg was traditional and which was modern, and which was faster.
