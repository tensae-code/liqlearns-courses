# Hands-on — Presenting Our Woreda — a Culture Exhibition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build a classroom exhibition
Each group takes one woreda or zone, makes its stall from paper, cloth scraps and drawings, and presents it in two minutes: the dress, the jewellery, the custom, the sport.

## The market beside it
Price three goods your stall's market table would sell, and write who would buy each one and why.
