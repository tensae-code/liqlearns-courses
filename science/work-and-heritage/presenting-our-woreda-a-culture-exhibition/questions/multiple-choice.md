# Presenting Our Woreda — a Culture Exhibition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The exhibition is planned by —**
a) the Region's Culture and Tourism Commission  b) a football club  c) one family
**Answer: a) the Region's Culture and Tourism Commission** — The Commission plans it, in Bahir Dar.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Beside the exhibition stalls there is —**
a) a small market  b) a harbour  c) nothing
**Answer: a) a small market** — A small market stands beside the stalls.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Gondar children bring —**
a) white honey  b) photos of Fasil Ghebbi  c) bamboo goods
**Answer: b) photos of Fasil Ghebbi** — The castles of Gondar, in photos.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) White honey comes with —**
a) Wag Himra  b) Awi  c) Dessie
**Answer: a) Wag Himra** — Wag Himra brings white honey.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Awi brings —**
a) incense  b) bamboo goods  c) posters
**Answer: b) bamboo goods** — Awi's stall carries bamboo goods.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Gojjam brings —**
a) bamboo goods  b) honey and enqutatash eskista  c) incense
**Answer: b) honey and enqutatash eskista** — Gojjam's named offerings.
