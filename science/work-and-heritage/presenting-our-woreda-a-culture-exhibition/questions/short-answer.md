# Presenting Our Woreda — a Culture Exhibition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four things each woreda presents.**
**Answer:** Its dress, its jewellery, its traditional conflict-resolution practice, and its traditional sports.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What would your own woreda bring? Name two things.**
**Answer:** The pupil's own woreda's real dress, goods or customs, named honestly.
