# Presenting Our Woreda — a Culture Exhibition — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Every woreda presents the same things at the exhibition.**
**Answer: False** — Each woreda brings its own dress, goods and customs — no two stalls alike.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Dessie brings Wollo incense.**
**Answer: True** — Incense is Dessie's named offering.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A woreda's traditional sports are part of what it presents.**
**Answer: True** — Dress, jewellery, conflict-resolution practice and traditional sports.
