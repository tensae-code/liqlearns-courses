# Culture — Good Customs We Keep — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Respecting elders is a useful traditional practice.**
**Answer: True** — Elders' respect is named among the good customs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In mediation, elders listen to both sides.**
**Answer: True** — Listening to both sides is how reconciliation works.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Caring for the elderly is a child's moral duty.**
**Answer: True** — The book states it as a duty.
