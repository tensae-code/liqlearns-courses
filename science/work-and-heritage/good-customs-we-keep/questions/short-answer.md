# Culture — Good Customs We Keep — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three useful traditional practices.**
**Answer:** Honouring dress; respecting elders; helping others; hospitality; respecting work; elder mediation; song and dance; care of elders (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What does elder mediation do?**
**Answer:** Respected elders listen to both sides of a quarrel and reconcile the people — ሽምግልና.
