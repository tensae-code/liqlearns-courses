# Culture — Good Customs We Keep — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Hospitality means —**
a) ignoring guests  b) giving a guest great honour and good hosting  c) charging guests
**Answer: b) giving a guest great honour and good hosting** — A guest is honoured and well hosted.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ሽምግልና is —**
a) a dance  b) elder mediation that reconciles people  c) a market tax
**Answer: b) elder mediation that reconciles people** — Shimglina is elder mediation/reconciliation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Traditional music can carry —**
a) peace, love and hope  b) only noise  c) fear
**Answer: a) peace, love and hope** — Music carries peace, love and hope.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which custom helps quarrelling people make peace?**
a) ሽምግልና  b) Feasting  c) Dressing up
**Answer: a) ሽምግልና** — Elder mediation reconciles.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A wedding song is sung at —**
a) a wedding  b) a ploughing only  c) an exam
**Answer: a) a wedding** — Wedding songs are named.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) Honouring traditional dress means —**
a) wearing and respecting our own dress with pride  b) hiding it  c) tearing it
**Answer: a) wearing and respecting our own dress with pride** — Traditional dress is honoured.
