# Video script — Culture — Good Customs We Keep

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A guest comes: honour them, host them well.

People quarrel: the elders listen, and peace returns — ሽምግልና.

We sing, we dance, we care for our elders.

These are the good customs we keep.
