# Hands-on — Culture — Good Customs We Keep

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Class song and dance
Rehearse a local traditional song/dance with your class and perform it — the book's own activity; keep steps modest and joyful.

## Mediation role-play
Two pupils act a small quarrel; two 'elders' mediate, listening to both sides, and reconcile them.
