# Culture — Good Customs We Keep

## Do it first

A guest arrives at your home, tired and dusty. What does your family do — in the first five minutes? That welcome has a name in this lesson. Read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Respect, dress and the honoured guest

Among our useful customs: honouring traditional dress; respecting people and elders; helping others; and hospitality — a guest is given great honour and good hosting. Respecting work belongs here too: honest work is honoured in every custom of ours.

## Sub lesson 2 — Elder mediation — ሽምግልና (shimglina)

When people quarrel, elders mediate: ሽምግልና is the traditional reconciliation in which respected elders listen to both sides and bring peace. It still holds communities together.

## Sub lesson 3 — Song, dance and care of the elderly

Traditional song and dance are kept customs: music gladdens, unites people and carries peace, love and hope — there are work songs, wedding songs and development songs. The class rehearses and performs a local song and dance (such as the Gondar traditional dance). And caring for the elderly is a child's moral duty.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: the useful traditional practices — honouring traditional dress, respecting elders, hospitality to guests, respecting work, elder mediation (ሽምግልና), traditional song and dance, and caring for the elderly. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Practise one local traditional dance step with friends or family.
2. Help an elderly neighbour this week — carry, fetch or sit and listen.
3. Write how your family hosts a guest, step by step.
