# Blacksmith tools — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "The blacksmith tools — names" teach?
**Answer:** The lesson begins with the **names** of the blacksmith tools.

**2.** What does the part "What each blacksmith tool is used for" teach?
**Answer:** Names are taught together with **uses**: what each blacksmith tool does in the shaping of metal.
