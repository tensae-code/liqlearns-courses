# Keeping chickens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce  b) Feeding chickens  c) Chicken diseases and parasites  d) Housing and caring for chickens
**Answer: d) Housing and caring for chickens**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about housing and care?
a) Feeding chickens  b) Chicken diseases and parasites  c) Housing and caring for chickens  d) Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce
**Answer: c) Housing and caring for chickens**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about breeds and reproduction?
a) Chicken diseases and parasites  b) Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce  c) Housing and caring for chickens  d) Feeding chickens
**Answer: b) Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about feeding chickens?
a) Feeding chickens  b) Housing and caring for chickens  c) Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce  d) Chicken diseases and parasites
**Answer: a) Feeding chickens**

**5.** Which part of this lesson is about diseases and parasites?
a) Housing and caring for chickens  b) Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce  c) Feeding chickens  d) Chicken diseases and parasites
**Answer: d) Chicken diseases and parasites**

**6.** Which part of this lesson is about importance and the process step by step?
a) Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce  b) Feeding chickens  c) Why keep chickens — the farming process step by step  d) Housing and caring for chickens
**Answer: c) Why keep chickens — the farming process step by step**
