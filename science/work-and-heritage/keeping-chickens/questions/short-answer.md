# Keeping chickens — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Housing and caring for chickens" teach?
**Answer:** Keeping begins with the chickens' home and their daily **care**: Rwanda's Primary 5 book opens the topic with **keeping chickens — housing and care**, because a chicken that is housed and cared for is the chicken that thrives.

**2.** What does the part "Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce" teach?
**Answer:** Next, the birds themselves: the **types of chicken breeds**, and **chicken reproduction** — how chickens multiply, so the keeper knows both which birds they keep and how the flock renews itself.

**3.** What does the part "Feeding chickens" teach?
**Answer:** A kept chicken is a fed chicken: **proper feeding of chickens** is its own topic — the keeper's daily feeding is what the eggs and the growth are made of.

**4.** What does the part "Chicken diseases and parasites" teach?
**Answer:** The keeper watches for harm: **chicken diseases and parasites** — the sicknesses and the creatures that live on the birds — are taught so the keeper can guard the flock before harm spreads.
