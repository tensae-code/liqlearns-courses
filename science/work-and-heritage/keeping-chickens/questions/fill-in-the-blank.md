# Keeping chickens — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Keeping chickens opens with housing and _____.
**Answer: care**

**2.** The _____ of chicken breeds are taught in this part.
**Answer: types**

**3.** _____ feeding is what the eggs and growth are made of.
**Answer: Proper**

**4.** The keeper guards the flock against diseases and _____.
**Answer: parasites**
