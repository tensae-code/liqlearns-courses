# Keeping chickens

## Do it first
Before you read: many homes keep chickens. What does a chicken keeper actually do, from the day the chickens arrive to the day they give eggs or meat? List the jobs. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — its Primary 5 book teaches chicken keeping whole, as the year's animal topic.

## Sub lesson 1 — Housing and caring for chickens

Keeping begins with the chickens' home and their daily **care**: Rwanda's Primary 5 book opens the topic with **keeping chickens — housing and care**, because a chicken that is housed and cared for is the chicken that thrives.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce

Next, the birds themselves: the **types of chicken breeds**, and **chicken reproduction** — how chickens multiply, so the keeper knows both which birds they keep and how the flock renews itself.

## Sub lesson 3 — Feeding chickens

A kept chicken is a fed chicken: **proper feeding of chickens** is its own topic — the keeper's daily feeding is what the eggs and the growth are made of.

## Sub lesson 4 — Chicken diseases and parasites

The keeper watches for harm: **chicken diseases and parasites** — the sicknesses and the creatures that live on the birds — are taught so the keeper can guard the flock before harm spreads.

## Sub lesson 5 — Why keep chickens — the farming process step by step

The topic closes with the **importance of chicken farming** and **the chicken farming process, step by step** — the keeping gathered into its order, from the first housing to the produce, so the learner can retell the whole farming as one run.

## The key ideas

- **Housing and caring for chickens** — housing and care.
- **Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce** — breeds and reproduction.
- **Feeding chickens** — feeding chickens.
- **Chicken diseases and parasites** — diseases and parasites.
- **Why keep chickens — the farming process step by step** — importance and the process step by step.
