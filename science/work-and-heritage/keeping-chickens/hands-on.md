# Hands-on — Keeping chickens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work through the first part by hand
Take the first sub lesson — Housing and caring for chickens — and do it with real things or a drawing: lay out, name or draw what the part teaches, and say one sentence for each item before you check it against the lesson.

## Explain the lesson's order
Write the sub lessons' names on separate cards (Housing and caring for chickens, Chicken breeds and how chickens reproduce, Feeding chickens, Chicken diseases and parasites, Why keep chickens — the farming process step by step). Mix them, then put them back in the lesson's order and explain to a partner why that order works — what each part needs from the one before it.

## Teach it at home
Teach the whole lesson to someone at home using only things you can point to around you. Where you get stuck is the part to re-read — mark it and return to it tomorrow.
