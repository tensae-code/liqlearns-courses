# Lesson plan — Our food heritage — traditional dishes of the Amhara Region

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Work and Heritage · **Lesson:** Our food heritage — traditional dishes of the Amhara Region
**Place in the curriculum:** Work and Heritage (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Amhara Region's traditional dishes and drinks, and how four of them are prepared — Abzi, Qatagna, Chechebsa and Qinche
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the food lessons; this lesson turns to the dishes our own region hands down.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The eaten list and the drinks** — injera with doro or shiro wot, tiresh, dabbo, tukusie, qwanṭa, genfo, qinche, anebabero, qocho, chibto, chechebsa; drinks atmit, beso, absh, ergo, korefe, tella, buqri, tej
2. **How four dishes are prepared (teacher guide — evidence)** — the preparation methods gathered from zone teachers' interviews — Abzi (Awi zone), Qatagna (Wag Himra), Chechebsa (Oromo zone), Qinche (most Amhara zones) — with the SNNP guide's traditional-preparation teaching as labelled evidence

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title in the Science course, in order. Recommended, never forced — a learner who lands here directly can start here.
- Nothing else is required; every term used is taught inside this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the region's traditional dishes and drinks from the book's lists.
2. Say how Abzi, Qatagna, Chechebsa and Qinche are prepared, each with its zone.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; a family cook to ask at home.

## If the learner is stuck
- The lists are long → learn them in their two families: the eaten list and the drinks list.
- The four prepared dishes blur → tie each to its zone: Abzi–Awi, Qatagna–Wag Himra, Chechebsa–Oromo zone, Qinche–most zones.
