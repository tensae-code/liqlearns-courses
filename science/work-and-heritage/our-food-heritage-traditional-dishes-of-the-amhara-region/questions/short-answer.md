# Our food heritage — traditional dishes of the Amhara Region — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name five traditional dishes from the book's list.**
**Answer:** Any five of: injera with doro or shiro wot, tiresh, dabbo, tukusie, qwanṭa, genfo, qinche, anebabero, qocho, chibto, chechebsa.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Match each prepared dish to its zone: Abzi, Qatagna, Chechebsa, Qinche.**
**Answer:** Abzi — Awi zone; Qatagna — Wag Himra; Chechebsa — Oromo zone; Qinche — most Amhara zones.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three traditional drinks.**
**Answer:** Any three of: atmit, beso, absh, ergo, korefe, tella, buqri, tej.
