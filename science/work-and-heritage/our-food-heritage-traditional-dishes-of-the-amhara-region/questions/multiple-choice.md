# Our food heritage — traditional dishes of the Amhara Region — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which is on the region's traditional eaten list?**
a) chechebsa  b) pizza  c) sushi
**Answer: a) chechebsa** — Chechebsa is on the printed list.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which is a traditional drink in the book's list?**
a) tej  b) cola  c) lemonade
**Answer: a) tej** — Tej — with atmit, beso, absh, ergo, korefe, tella, buqri.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Abzi's preparation comes from —**
a) the Awi zone  b) Wag Himra  c) the Oromia guide
**Answer: a) the Awi zone** — Abzi — Awi zone.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Qatagna's method comes from —**
a) most zones  b) Wag Himra  c) Awi
**Answer: b) Wag Himra** — Qatagna — Wag Himra.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Qinche is made —**
a) in one village only  b) across most Amhara zones  c) nowhere now
**Answer: b) across most Amhara zones** — Qinche — most Amhara zones.
