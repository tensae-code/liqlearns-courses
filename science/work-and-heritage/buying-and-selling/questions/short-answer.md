# Work and Livelihood — Buying and Selling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why is trade necessary, in one sentence?**
**Answer:** Because no one makes everything they need, goods must move by buying and selling from those who have them to those who need them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What does a farmer sell, and what does a farmer buy?**
**Answer:** Sells farm products; buys industrial goods such as oil, cloth and tools.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Name the four kinds of market in the region's trade.**
**Answer:** The crop market, the animal-products market, vegetables and fruit, and industrial products.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Why must trade in natural resources be legal?**
**Answer:** So the resource is taken by right, in measure, and stays alive for the community's future.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) Why is leaning on one product risky, and what newer sector is joining the trade?**
**Answer:** If the one product's price falls, the whole country's earnings fall with it. The newer sector is flower production.
