# Work and Livelihood — Buying and Selling — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Trade is necessary because no one makes everything they need.**
**Answer: True** — That is why trade exists.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Traders supply industrial goods to both farmers and townspeople.**
**Answer: True** — Traders stand between makers and users.
