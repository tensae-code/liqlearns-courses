# Work and Livelihood — Buying and Selling — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Trade is —**
a) growing food  b) buying and selling between people  c) making pots
**Answer: b) buying and selling between people** — Trade is buying and selling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) At market, a farmer typically —**
a) sells farm products and buys industrial goods  b) only gives things away  c) buys grain with grain only
**Answer: a) sells farm products and buys industrial goods** — Sell produce, buy goods — the two-way road.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why can't a farming family simply skip trade?**
a) They dislike towns  b) They need goods they do not make — oil, cloth, tools  c) Trade is a law only
**Answer: b) They need goods they do not make — oil, cloth, tools** — Need for others' goods makes trade necessary.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which pair shows trade's two directions?**
a) Grain to town; cloth back to farm  b) Rain up; sun down  c) Seeds in; seeds out
**Answer: a) Grain to town; cloth back to farm** — Goods flow both ways.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Selling is —**
a) giving goods for money or value  b) keeping goods  c) growing goods
**Answer: a) giving goods for money or value** — Selling exchanges goods for value.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) A farmer sells at the market —**
a) everything the family grows  b) the surplus left after family use  c) nothing
**Answer: b) the surplus left after family use** — Family use comes first; the rest is sold.

**7. (Sub lesson 3) Which is NOT one of the region's market kinds?**
a) the crop market  b) the animal-products market  c) the moon market
**Answer: c) the moon market** — The markets are crops, animal products, vegetables and fruit, and industrial products.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) Legal trade in natural resources —**
a) takes without right  b) follows the rules and keeps the resource for the future  c) is forbidden
**Answer: b) follows the rules and keeps the resource for the future** — Legal trade protects the resource it sells.

**9. (Sub lesson 4) Illegal trade in natural resources harms —**
a) no one  b) only the buyer  c) the resource and the community's future
**Answer: c) the resource and the community's future** — Taking without right eats next year's resource too.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) The region's trade pattern sends out —**
a) factory goods  b) raw farm goods and minerals  c) only flowers
**Answer: b) raw farm goods and minerals** — Raw materials go out; industrial goods come in.

**11. (Sub lesson 5) Zambia's single-product dependence is —**
a) 86% coffee  b) 94% copper  c) 20% coffee
**Answer: b) 94% copper** — Zambia leans 94% on copper; Burundi's 86% is coffee.
