# Hands-on — Work and Livelihood — Buying and Selling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Market day simulation
Class market: farmers bring (drawn) produce, traders bring (drawn) goods; everyone must end with one thing they needed and did not make.

## Who made it?
Take five home objects; for each, say who made it and how trade brought it to you.

## Walk a market in your mind
List everything sold at your nearest market. Sort your list into the four market kinds. Then pick one natural-resource good and write how it can be traded legally, so the resource lasts.

## One-product country
Play a market game: your group's whole income is one product. The teacher announces its price has halved. What happens to your group's plans? Then add a second and third product and play the announcement again.
