# Farm equipment and machines

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — farm equipment and machines — the equipment and machinery of farming, as Zimbabwe's Grade 4 guide teaches the unit. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Equipment and machinery

Zimbabwe's Grade 4 guide teaches **equipment and machinery** as a named unit inside its work topic: the farm's equipment and machines are named and studied as their own topic.

## Sub lesson 2 — Machines and the work they do

Each machine is learned with the farm work it does: equipment is not a list of shapes but a set of workers, each with its job on the farm.

## Sub lesson 3 — Equipment in its topic order

The unit stands between land use and the maintenance unit that follows it in the guide: first the land, then the equipment that works it, then caring for that equipment. That order is the guide's own way.

## The key ideas

- **Equipment and machinery** — the farm's equipment and machines, named.
- **Machines and the work they do** — each machine with its farm job.
- **Equipment in its topic order** — land, equipment, then care of it.
