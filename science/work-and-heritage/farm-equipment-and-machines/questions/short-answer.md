# Farm equipment and machines — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Equipment and machinery" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 4 guide teaches **equipment and machinery** as a named unit inside its work topic: the farm's equipment and machines are named and studied as their own topic.

**2.** What does the part "Machines and the work they do" teach?
**Answer:** Each machine is learned with the farm work it does: equipment is not a list of shapes but a set of workers, each with its job on the farm.

**3.** What does the part "Equipment in its topic order" teach?
**Answer:** The unit stands between land use and the maintenance unit that follows it in the guide: first the land, then the equipment that works it, then caring for that equipment.
