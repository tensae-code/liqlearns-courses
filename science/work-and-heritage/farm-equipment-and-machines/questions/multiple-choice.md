# Farm equipment and machines — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Machines and the work they do  b) Equipment in its topic order  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Equipment and machinery
**Answer: d) Equipment and machinery**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about the farm's equipment and machines, named?
a) Equipment and machinery  b) Machines and the work they do  c) Equipment in its topic order
**Answer: a) Equipment and machinery**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about each machine with its farm job?
a) Equipment and machinery  b) Machines and the work they do  c) Equipment in its topic order
**Answer: b) Machines and the work they do**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about land, equipment, then care of it?
a) Equipment and machinery  b) Machines and the work they do  c) Equipment in its topic order
**Answer: c) Equipment in its topic order**
