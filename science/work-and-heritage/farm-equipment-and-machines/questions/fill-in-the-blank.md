# Farm equipment and machines — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Equipment and machinery.
**Answer: the farm's equipment and machines, named**

**2.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Machines and the work they do.
**Answer: each machine with its farm job**

**3.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Equipment in its topic order.
**Answer: land, equipment, then care of it**
