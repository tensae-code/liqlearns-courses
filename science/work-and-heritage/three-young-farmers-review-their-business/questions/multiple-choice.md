# Three Young Farmers Review Their Business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The three graduates borrowed —**
a) a shop  b) 10 timad of land and money  c) a truck
**Answer: b) 10 timad of land and money** — Land and money, both borrowed.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The farm's daily milk yield was —**
a) 5 litres  b) 15 litres  c) 50 litres
**Answer: b) 15 litres** — Fifteen litres a day, with 50 quintals of barley a year.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The onions rotted because —**
a) no one liked onions  b) there was no transport to market  c) the rain was too heavy
**Answer: b) there was no transport to market** — Unsold for want of transport.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The one venture that grew was —**
a) sugarcane  b) dairy  c) barley, into packaged qolo
**Answer: c) barley, into packaged qolo** — The barley alone succeeded.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) To fix their skills gap, the three resolve to —**
a) give up farming  b) read, consult experts and practise  c) borrow more land
**Answer: b) read, consult experts and practise** — Their own three-part resolve.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) Andinet's training was in —**
a) animal rearing  b) packaged foods  c) crop production
**Answer: b) packaged foods** — Ahmed — animals; Andinet — packaged foods; Tinsae — crops.
