# Three Young Farmers Review Their Business — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Each graduate brought a different training — animals, packaged foods, crops.**
**Answer: True** — Ahmed, Andinet and Tinsae specialised differently.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) After two years, all four ventures had succeeded.**
**Answer: False** — Only the barley grew; disease, weather and missing transport defeated the rest.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Cow disease was one reason the milk fell.**
**Answer: True** — Disease cut the milk in the honest review.
