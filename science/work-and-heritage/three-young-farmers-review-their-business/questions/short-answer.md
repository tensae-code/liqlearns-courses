# Three Young Farmers Review Their Business — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How was the borrowed land divided?**
**Answer:** Between barley, fodder for dairy cows, onion and tomato, and sugarcane.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What did Solomon tell them about Chinese pupils?**
