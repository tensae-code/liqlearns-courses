# Hands-on — Three Young Farmers Review Their Business

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The review meeting
In groups, hold the three farmers' review meeting: each farmer reports one venture honestly — result and reason — and the group writes one advice line for each, as the book's seven advice questions ask.

## Skills-gap plan
Write a one-page plan the three could follow this year: what to read, which expert to consult, what to practise each month.
