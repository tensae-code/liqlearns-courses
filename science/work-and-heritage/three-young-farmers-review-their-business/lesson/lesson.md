# Three Young Farmers Review Their Business

## Do it first

Three friends graduate, borrow land and money, and farm together. Two years later they sit down and tell the truth about the harvest. What do you think they say?

## Sub lesson 1 — Borrowing, dividing, planting

The case, in summary — part one: three TVET agriculture graduates — Ahmed (animal rearing), Andinet (packaged foods) and Tinsae (crop production) — borrow 10 timad of land and money. They split the land between barley, fodder for dairy cows, onion and tomato, and sugarcane. The farm gives 15 litres of milk a day and 50 quintals of barley a year.

[Picture 12207 — the three graduates standing over their divided fields]

## Sub lesson 2 — Two years later — the honest review

Part two: after two years they review honestly. Cow disease cut the milk. The weather failed the sugarcane. The onions rotted unsold, for want of transport to market. Only the barley grew — into packaged qolo production. Their friend Solomon, in China, tells them Chinese pupils do a week of practical training every semester from grade 1 — and the three resolve to fix their skills gap by reading, consulting experts and practising.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: three graduates farmed borrowed land for two years, and their honest review — disease, weather, no transport, one success — taught them the skill they lacked. Then answer one question they ask you about it.
