# Video script — Keeping goats, sheep and pigs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Keeping goats, sheep and pigs** — Uganda's home-study way: breeds, diseases, and care.
The breeds sort two ways — exotic, brought from outside, and local, the kinds of the place. The diseases are taught with their signs and control: pneumonia, heartwater, and African swine fever in pigs.
And underneath it all is the keeper's daily care of the animals.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
