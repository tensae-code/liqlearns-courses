# Keeping goats, sheep and pigs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Breeds brought from outside are called…
a) Local breeds  b) Exotic breeds  c) Wild breeds  d) Home breeds
**Answer: b) Exotic breeds**

**2.** The kinds of the place itself are the…
a) Exotic breeds  b) Local breeds  c) Foreign breeds  d) New breeds
**Answer: b) Local breeds**

**3.** African swine fever is a disease of…
a) Goats  b) Sheep  c) Pigs  d) Rabbits
**Answer: c) Pigs**

**4.** Heartwater is named in the booklet among the diseases of…
a) Goats, sheep and pigs  b) Fish only  c) Bees only  d) Plants
**Answer: a) Goats, sheep and pigs**

**5.** A disease is taught whole in the booklet as…
a) Name only  b) Disease, signs and control  c) Price and weight  d) Colour and size
**Answer: b) Disease, signs and control**

**6.** Underneath breeds and diseases, keeping stands on…
a) Daily care of the animals  b) Selling at once  c) Luck  d) Doing nothing
**Answer: a) Daily care of the animals**
