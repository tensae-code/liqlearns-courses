# Keeping goats, sheep and pigs — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Breeds brought from outside are called _____ breeds.
**Answer: exotic**

**2.** The kinds of the place itself are _____ breeds.
**Answer: local**

**3.** African _____ fever is a disease of pigs.
**Answer: swine**

**4.** A disease is taught with its signs and its _____.
**Answer: control**
