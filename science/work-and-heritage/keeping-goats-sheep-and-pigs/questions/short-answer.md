# Keeping goats, sheep and pigs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is the difference between an exotic breed and a local breed?
**Answer:** An exotic breed was brought from outside; a local breed is one of the kinds kept in the place itself.

**2.** Name the three diseases the booklet names, and the animal African swine fever belongs to.
**Answer:** Pneumonia, heartwater and African swine fever — the last is a disease of pigs.

**3.** Why does the booklet teach a disease's signs together with its control?
**Answer:** Because control starts with seeing — a keeper who can read the signs early can act before the disease spreads.

**4.** What is the keeper's standing work, beneath breeds and diseases?
**Answer:** The daily care of the animals.
