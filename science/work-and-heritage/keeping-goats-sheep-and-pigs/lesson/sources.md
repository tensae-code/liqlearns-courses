Placement choice, on the record: this NEW LESSON was built for the Uganda placement "NEW LESSON NEEDED — keeping-goats-sheep-and-pigs" (one placement, Primary Five home-study booklet, printed pp. 40–45). No existing general-science lesson covers keeping these three animals as its own thing — `work-and-heritage/farming-crops-and-animals` teaches farming's two halves in general — so it gets its own lesson. It sits in work-and-heritage/ beside the farming lesson and the new rabbit-keeping lesson and is registered in CURRICULUM.md Unit 13 (Work and Heritage). Uganda's own way — livestock keeping taught whole in primary school (breeds, diseases with signs and control, care) as agriculture science — IS part of this lesson's content, carried exactly as the placement describes it.
