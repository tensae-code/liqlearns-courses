# Hands-on — Keeping goats, sheep and pigs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Breed sort
On cards, write goat, sheep and pig, and exotic and local. Practise the keeper's naming: for each animal, say one sentence naming it as exotic-breed or local-breed stock, and what that choice means.

## Signs-and-control drill
In pairs: one learner names a disease from the lesson (pneumonia, heartwater, African swine fever); the other must answer with the animal it belongs to and what control means — seeing the signs early and acting. Swap until all three are quick.

## A keeper's care day
Write one day of care for a small herd — goats, sheep and pigs together — hour by hour: feeding, water, shelter, and the health checks a keeper makes while doing them.
