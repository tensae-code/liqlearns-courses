Placement choice, on the record: this NEW LESSON was built for the Uganda placement "NEW LESSON NEEDED — rabbit-keeping" (one placement, Primary Four home-study booklet, printed pp. 35–38). No existing general-science lesson covers keeping rabbits as its own thing — `work-and-heritage/farming-crops-and-animals` teaches farming's two halves in general and `animals/looking-after-our-animals` teaches care of animals in general — so it gets its own lesson. It sits in work-and-heritage/ beside the farming lesson and is registered in CURRICULUM.md Unit 13 (Work and Heritage). Uganda's own way — rabbit keeping taught whole in primary school (breeds and uses, building the hutch, care, parasites and diseases) as agriculture science — IS part of this lesson's content, carried exactly as the placement describes it.
