Today: **Rabbit keeping** — Uganda's primary agriculture-science way: the animals, their house, and their care.
Rabbits come in breeds and are kept for their uses. The keeper builds them a hutch to live in.
And keeping means care every day — guarding the rabbits against parasites and diseases.
