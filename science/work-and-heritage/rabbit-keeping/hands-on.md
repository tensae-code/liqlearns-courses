# Hands-on — Rabbit keeping

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Design the hutch
On paper, design a rabbit hutch: its size, its door, where feed and water go, how it is cleaned. Present the design to a partner, who must find one weakness a rabbit would find first.

## Breeds and uses card sort
Make cards: breed names and uses from the lesson on separate cards. Mix them, then sort — match each breed card to its uses, and say one sentence for each match.

## The keeper's parasite watch
Write a watch list for a rabbit keeper: what to check on the rabbit's body, in the hutch, and in its feeding, to catch parasites and disease early. Keep it with the design from the first activity.
