# Rabbit keeping — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Rabbit keeping, as the booklet teaches it, begins with knowing the rabbits'…
a) Names only  b) Breeds and uses  c) Colours only  d) Ages in years
**Answer: b) Breeds and uses**

**2.** Rabbits are housed in a…
a) Hutch  b) Kennel  c) Stable  d) Basket in a tree
**Answer: a) Hutch**

**3.** The hutch is…
a) Bought finished only  b) Built by the keeper  c) Dug as a burrow  d) Made of water
**Answer: b) Built by the keeper**

**4.** A keeper watches rabbits for…
a) Parasites and diseases  b) Only their weight  c) Songs  d) Dreams
**Answer: a) Parasites and diseases**

**5.** Parasites are things that…
a) Feed the rabbits  b) Live on or in the rabbits and harm them  c) Build the hutch  d) Clean the hutch
**Answer: b) Live on or in the rabbits and harm them**

**6.** Keeping rabbits well means…
a) Feeding once a month  b) Daily care, a sound hutch, and guarding against parasites and diseases  c) Letting them roam the road  d) Never looking at them
**Answer: b) Daily care, a sound hutch, and guarding against parasites and diseases**
