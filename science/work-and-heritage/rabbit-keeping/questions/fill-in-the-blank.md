# Rabbit keeping — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Rabbits are kept in a house called a _____.
**Answer: hutch**

**2.** Different kinds of rabbits are called _____.
**Answer: breeds**

**3.** _____ live on or in the rabbits and harm them.
**Answer: Parasites**

**4.** Daily _____ is the keeper's main job.
**Answer: care**
