# Rabbit keeping — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What two things about rabbits does the booklet's keeper know first?
**Answer:** Their breeds and their uses — which breed the rabbits are, and what they are kept for.

**2.** What is a hutch, and who makes it?
**Answer:** The rabbits' house — built by the keeper as part of the keeping.

**3.** Name the two harms the keeper guards the rabbits against.
**Answer:** Parasites and diseases of rabbits.

**4.** Why does the booklet teach hutch-building inside a science lesson?
**Answer:** Because the rabbits' home is part of their keeping — a keeper who can build the hutch can house the animals properly from the start.
