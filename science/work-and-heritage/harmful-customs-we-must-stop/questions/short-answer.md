# Culture — Harmful Customs We Must Stop — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three harmful practices.**
**Answer:** Early marriage; feasting beyond one's means; female circumcision; uvula cutting; smoking; drunkenness (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What harm does early marriage do?**
**Answer:** It steals a child's schooling and harms her health and future.

**3. (Sub lesson 8) Set out Table 6.1: two causes and three harms of early marriage.**
**Answer:** Causes (any two): family honour, guarding virginity, seeing grandchildren, gaining wealth, high standing. Harms (any three): loneliness/separation, heavy work burden, violated reproductive-health rights, fistula, leaving school, HIV/STIs, higher mother and child death, weak decision-making power.

**4. (Sub lesson 11) Give the three-step strategy in order, and say where the field task goes.**
**Answer:** Awareness-education (teach), then prevention work, then rescue and support; the field task goes to the women-and-children's affairs branch or kebele office.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Summarise Zerfe's story in three sentences.**
**Answer:** Zerfe, born healthy, is kept out of school and married twice, is injured in childbirth by an unskilled attendant, is separated and lives in poverty; her community's support then lifts her to a better state (plot summary only).
