# Culture — Harmful Customs We Must Stop — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Uvula cutting is እንጥል መቁረጥ.**
**Answer: True** — The book gives the practice's Amharic name.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Feasting beyond one's means can ruin a family's living.**
**Answer: True** — Spending beyond means is listed for its harm.

**3. (Sub lesson 6) A teething baby's diarrhoea is caused by the gum, so the gum must be extracted.**
**Answer: False** — The class questions separate that belief from fact; teething is a natural stage.

**4. (Sub lesson 9) Religious fathers and respected persons are part of the solution, not outside it.**
**Answer: True** — Enlisting them is one of the three printed duties.

**5. (Sub lesson 4) Childbirth problems and HIV are among the printed harms of cutting girls' genitals.**
**Answer: True** — Both are printed harms.
