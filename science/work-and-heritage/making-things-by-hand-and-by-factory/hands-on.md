# Hands-on — Work and Livelihood — Making Things: By Hand and by Factory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shuttle watching
Watch weaving (live or in the lesson GIF): follow the shuttle carrying thread back and forth until cloth appears.

## Hand vs factory sorting
Sort product cards into 'by hand' and 'by factory', defending each sort with how the thing is made.

## Sort the making
List ten made things you use this week. Sort them: made by cottage industry, by modern industry, or by both together — and say what told you.

## Transform something
Take one natural thing — clay, cotton, a skin, a piece of wood. Draw its journey: cottage way and factory way, side by side, until it becomes a useful thing. Label where light or heavy industry touches the journey.
