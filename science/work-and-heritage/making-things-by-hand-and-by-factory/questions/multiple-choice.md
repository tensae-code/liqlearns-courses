# Work and Livelihood — Making Things: By Hand and by Factory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Industry means —**
a) growing crops only  b) turning products into other useful things  c) selling unchanged goods
**Answer: b) turning products into other useful things** — Industry transforms products.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A mitad is a product of —**
a) a factory only  b) cottage industry — pottery  c) mining
**Answer: b) cottage industry — pottery** — The mitad is a potter's product.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A textile factory makes —**
a) clothes, sheets and blankets  b) pots and mitads  c) sickles
**Answer: a) clothes, sheets and blankets** — Textile factories make cloth goods.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Leather work belongs to —**
a) cottage industry  b) modern industry only  c) trade
**Answer: a) cottage industry** — Leather work is cottage industry.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Sugar from a sugar factory is —**
a) a crop  b) a modern-industry product  c) a mineral
**Answer: b) a modern-industry product** — Factories make sugar.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) The blacksmith's industry changes —**
a) metal into tools  b) cotton into cloth  c) clay into pots
**Answer: a) metal into tools** — Blacksmithing works metal.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) Cottage industry (የጎጆ ኢንዱስትሪ) uses —**
a) complex machines  b) simple hand tools and traditional methods  c) only electricity
**Answer: b) simple hand tools and traditional methods** — Small scale, by hand and skill.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) Light industry uses as its raw material —**
a) farm products  b) minerals only  c) imported tools
**Answer: a) farm products** — Food, textile, sugar, paper and drinks factories work on farm produce.

**9. (Sub lesson 5) Cottage industry is —**
a) the enemy of modern industry  b) the foundation of modern industry  c) finished and gone
**Answer: b) the foundation of modern industry** — Slow and labour-heavy, cottage work is still the foundation modern industry stands on.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) Modern industry's share of Ethiopia's GDP is —**
a) over half  b) under 10%  c) about 82%
**Answer: b) under 10%** — Under 10% — recent, mostly food production, unevenly spread.

**11. (Sub lesson 6) A cement plant is which kind of industry?**
a) cottage  b) light  c) heavy
**Answer: c) heavy** — Cement is a raw material for production — heavy industry, as in Ethiopia and Kenya.

**12. (Sub lesson 6) Industry, in the book's definition, is —**
a) moving goods to market  b) transforming natural resources into useful things  c) growing crops
**Answer: b) transforming natural resources into useful things** — Cotton into clothes, minerals into tools — transformation is the mark of industry.

**13. (Sub lesson 7) Industry's share of East African economies is —**
a) under 8.9%  b) about 43%  c) over half
**Answer: a) under 8.9%** — Under 8.9% — small after wars and crises.

**14. (Sub lesson 7) A new factory helps the region because it —**
a) closes farms  b) creates jobs and stirs other sectors  c) stops trade
**Answer: b) creates jobs and stirs other sectors** — Jobs, and life stirred in farming, transport and trade around it.
