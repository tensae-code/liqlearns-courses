# Work and Livelihood — Making Things: By Hand and by Factory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Industry means —**
a) growing crops only  b) turning products into other useful things  c) selling unchanged goods
**Answer: b) turning products into other useful things** — Industry transforms products.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A mitad is a product of —**
a) a factory only  b) cottage industry — pottery  c) mining
**Answer: b) cottage industry — pottery** — The mitad is a potter's product.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A textile factory makes —**
a) clothes, sheets and blankets  b) pots and mitads  c) sickles
**Answer: a) clothes, sheets and blankets** — Textile factories make cloth goods.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Leather work belongs to —**
a) cottage industry  b) modern industry only  c) trade
**Answer: a) cottage industry** — Leather work is cottage industry.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Sugar from a sugar factory is —**
a) a crop  b) a modern-industry product  c) a mineral
**Answer: b) a modern-industry product** — Factories make sugar.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) The blacksmith's industry changes —**
a) metal into tools  b) cotton into cloth  c) clay into pots
**Answer: a) metal into tools** — Blacksmithing works metal.
