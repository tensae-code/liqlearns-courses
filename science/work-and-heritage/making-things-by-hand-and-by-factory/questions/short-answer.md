# Work and Livelihood — Making Things: By Hand and by Factory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Name two cottage industries and their products.**
**Answer:** Pottery — ensira, pots, mitad; weaving — gabi, netela, habesha kemis; blacksmithing — tools; leather work (any two with products).

**2. (Sub lesson 3) How do cottage and modern industry differ?**
**Answer:** Cottage industry works by hand and skill; modern industry works in factories at large scale.
