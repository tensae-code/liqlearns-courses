# Work and Livelihood — Making Things: By Hand and by Factory — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Clay becoming a pot is industry.**
**Answer: True** — Pottery is named cottage industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Gabi and netela are woven products.**
**Answer: True** — Weaving gives gabi, netela and habesha kemis.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Cottage and modern industry both transform products; they differ in hand vs factory.**
**Answer: True** — That contrast is the lesson.
