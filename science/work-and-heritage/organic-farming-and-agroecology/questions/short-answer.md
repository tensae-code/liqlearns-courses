# Organic farming and agroecology — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about organic crops defined?
**Answer: organic crops defined: food produced by natural methods without chemical substances, genetic manipulation or the agrochemicals that degenerate soils, letting the soil regenerate (guide evidence)**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about traditional agriculture (indigenous practices developed over generations, aimed at?
**Answer: traditional agriculture (indigenous practices developed over generations, aimed at conserving the environment and using resources sustainably) and agroecology (ecological principles applied to food production, weighing productivity, stability, sustainability and equity) (guide evidence)**

**3.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Organic farming and agroecology.**
