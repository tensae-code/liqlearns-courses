# Video script — Our Heritage — Lalibela, Gondar and the Simien Mountains

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Heritage: what the past hands down.

Lalibela's rock churches. Gondar's castles. The Simien peaks.

Songs and proverbs too — the heritage you cannot touch, only carry.

Touchable heritage. Performed heritage. Both on the world's list.

Guard it unchanged. Teach the citizens. Show it to the world.

Old keepers: churches and mosques. New keepers: museums, libraries, offices.
