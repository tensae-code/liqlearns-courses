# Hands-on — Our Heritage — Lalibela, Gondar and the Simien Mountains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Heritage hunt
Walk your surroundings with a notebook: find one natural, one tangible cultural and one intangible heritage within reach of your school. Record each with its keeper — who guards it, and how.

## Guide a visitor
Prepare a two-minute guide's talk for one heritage site: what it is, where it stands, why the world lists it, and how visitors should treat it.

## Ask a keeper
Visit or write to one institution that keeps culture — a church, mosque, museum, library or culture office. Ask: what do you keep, and how do you protect it? Report the answer to the class.
