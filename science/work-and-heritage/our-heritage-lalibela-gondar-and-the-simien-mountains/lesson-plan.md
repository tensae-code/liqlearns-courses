# Lesson plan — Our Heritage — Lalibela, Gondar and the Simien Mountains

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Work and Heritage · **Lesson:** Our Heritage — Lalibela, Gondar and the Simien Mountains
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** heritage — what passes from generation to generation — in its kinds (natural, cultural, historical; tangible and intangible), the region's three UNESCO World Heritage sites, tourism and why visitors come, and four great Ethiopians of the region.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the culture lessons — heritage is culture that has lasted, and that we now guard.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Heritage and its kinds** — heritage defined and sorted — natural/cultural/historical; tangible/intangible (Grade 4 student pp.127–130).
2. **The region's UNESCO World Heritage table** — the three UNESCO sites with their places (Grade 4 student pp.127–130).
3. **Tourism — and why visitors come** — tourism defined; its earnings, jobs and exchange; heritage as identity (Grade 4 student pp.130–131).
4. **Great thinkers and heroes of the region** — the four named figures and the research task (Grade 4 student pp.131–132).
5. **Our heritages in the teacher guides (teacher guide — evidence)** — heritage listing and sorting as the guides' stated competency (SNNP TG pp.104–106; Oromia TG pp.72–75, teacher guide — evidence).

6. **Heritage — touchable and untouchable; Ethiopia's UNESCO heritage** — heritage defined; tangible heritage (Gondar, Lalibela, Aksum, Tiya…) and intangible (Meskel Demera, Timket, Fiche Chembelala, Irecha, Ashenda…); the UNESCO table by region, and the four UNESCO intangible festivals (Grade 5).

7. **Protecting and promoting heritage** — citizens and government together: protect without changing its content, teach citizens to guard it, build protection institutions, use information technology to show the country to the world (Grade 5).

8. **The institutions that keep culture** — traditional institutions: churches and mosques; modern ones: culture and tourism offices, libraries and archives, art houses, museums (Grade 5).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- The words are new → go back to the lesson's picture and say what it shows, in home words first, then in the lesson's words.
- The parts blur together → take one part at a time and answer that part's questions before moving on; the gate is per part where sub lessons exist.
- The learner cannot do the Do It Yourself → do the first item together, then let them finish the rest alone.
