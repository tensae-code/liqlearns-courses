# Our Heritage — Lalibela, Gondar and the Simien Mountains — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Heritage is —**
a) this year's harvest  b) what passes from generation to generation  c) a kind of tax
**Answer: b) what passes from generation to generation** — The wealth of past generations, handed down.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Proverbs, tales and values are —**
a) tangible heritage  b) intangible heritage  c) natural heritage
**Answer: b) intangible heritage** — Carried inside, not touched.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which is NOT one of the region's UNESCO sites in the table?**
a) Lalibela's rock-hewn churches  b) the Simien Mountains National Park  c) the Sof Omar caves
**Answer: c) the Sof Omar caves** — The table names Lalibela, Simien and the Gondar castles.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The Gondar emperors' castles stand in —**
a) Gondar town  b) Lasta  c) Debarq
**Answer: a) Gondar town** — The castles stand in Gondar town.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Tourism earns —**
a) foreign exchange and jobs  b) nothing  c) only photographs
**Answer: a) foreign exchange and jobs** — And it speeds cultural exchange.

**6. (Sub lesson 4) Dr Haddis Alemayehu is named for —**
a) statecraft  b) art and literature, as an author  c) engineering
**Answer: b) art and literature, as an author** — The author among the four.

**7. (Sub lesson 5) የማይታዩ ቅርሶች means —**
a) natural heritages  b) intangible heritages  c) castles
**Answer: b) intangible heritages** — The guides' own term (teacher guide — evidence).
