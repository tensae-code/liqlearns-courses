# Our Heritage — Lalibela, Gondar and the Simien Mountains — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give one example each of tangible and intangible heritage.**
**Answer:** Tangible: dress, tools, architecture, alphabet and literature. Intangible: ceremonies, music (zefen, qererto, shilela), tales, proverbs, values.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) What does heritage give the new generation?**
**Answer:** Identity and unity — and a past to build on.

**3. (Sub lesson 6) Name one tangible and one intangible UNESCO heritage of Ethiopia.**
**Answer:** Tangible: e.g. Lalibela, Aksum, Gondar castles, Tiya, Konso. Intangible: Timket, Meskel, Fiche Chembelala or Irecha.

**4. (Sub lesson 7) Name two of the book's ways to protect and promote heritage.**
**Answer:** Any two of: protect without changing its content; teach citizens to guard it; build protection institutions; use information technology to show it to the world.

**5. (Sub lesson 8) Name one traditional and one modern institution that keeps culture, near where you live.**
**Answer:** Traditional: a church or a mosque. Modern: e.g. a culture and tourism office, a library, an archive, an art house or a museum (learner's own local example).
