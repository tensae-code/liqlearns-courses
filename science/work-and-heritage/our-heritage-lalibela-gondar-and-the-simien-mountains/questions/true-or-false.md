# Our Heritage — Lalibela, Gondar and the Simien Mountains — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Church forests count as natural heritage.**
**Answer: True** — Parks, lakes, animal shelters and church forests are named.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) A tourist may stay away from home for many years and still be a tourist.**
**Answer: False** — A tourist stays not more than a year.

**3. (Sub lesson 4) Tewodros II and Menelik II are named for statecraft.**
**Answer: True** — The two emperors head the four named figures.
