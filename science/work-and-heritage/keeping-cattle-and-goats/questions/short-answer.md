# Keeping cattle and goats — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "A good cowshed or goat shelter" teach?
**Answer:** Keeping starts with the animals' house: the **characteristics of a good cowshed or goat shelter** — what the shelter must be for the animals kept inside it to stay well.

**2.** What does the part "Breeds of cattle and goats" teach?
**Answer:** Then the animals themselves: the **types of cattle or goat breeds** — the keeper knows the breeds, as with every kept animal in these books, before the keeping deepens.

**3.** What does the part "Feeding cows and goats" teach?
**Answer:** **Feeding of cows and goats** is its own topic: the kept animal eats what the keeper provides, and the feeding is what the milk, the growth and the strength are made of.

**4.** What does the part "Health, sanitation and common diseases" teach?
**Answer:** The keeper guards the herd: **cattle and goat health and sanitation**, and the **common diseases of cattle and goats**, are taught together — cleanliness as the daily guard, and the diseases named so they can be known when they come.
