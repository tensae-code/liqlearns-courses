# Keeping cattle and goats — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The keeping starts with the characteristics of a good _____ or goat shelter.
**Answer: cowshed**

**2.** The keeper learns the types of cattle or goat _____.
**Answer: breeds**

**3.** _____ of cows and goats is its own topic.
**Answer: Feeding**

**4.** Health and _____ are the daily guard of the herd.
**Answer: sanitation**
