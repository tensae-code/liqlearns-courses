# Keeping cattle and goats

## Do it first
Before you read: picture a home that keeps cows, or goats, or both. Where do the animals sleep? What do they eat? What can go wrong for them? Say your answers. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — its Primary 6 book teaches cattle and goat keeping whole, as the year's animal topic.

## Sub lesson 1 — A good cowshed or goat shelter

Keeping starts with the animals' house: the **characteristics of a good cowshed or goat shelter** — what the shelter must be for the animals kept inside it to stay well.

## Sub lesson 2 — Breeds of cattle and goats

Then the animals themselves: the **types of cattle or goat breeds** — the keeper knows the breeds, as with every kept animal in these books, before the keeping deepens.

## Sub lesson 3 — Feeding cows and goats

**Feeding of cows and goats** is its own topic: the kept animal eats what the keeper provides, and the feeding is what the milk, the growth and the strength are made of.

## Sub lesson 4 — Health, sanitation and common diseases

The keeper guards the herd: **cattle and goat health and sanitation**, and the **common diseases of cattle and goats**, are taught together — cleanliness as the daily guard, and the diseases named so they can be known when they come.

## Sub lesson 5 — Why keep cattle and goats

The topic closes with the **importance of cow or goat farming** — what the keeping gives the family and the farm, the reason the whole topic is taught.

## The key ideas
