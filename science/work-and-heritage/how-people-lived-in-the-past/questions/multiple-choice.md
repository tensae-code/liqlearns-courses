# How people lived in the past — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In old times, homes were warmed and lit by…
a) Electric lamps  b) Fire  c) Solar panels  d) Gas pipes
**Answer: b) Fire**

**2.** Humans have always needed to…
a) Fly  b) Communicate with each other  c) Use telephones  d) Watch matches
**Answer: b) Communicate with each other**

**3.** News in the past travelled as fast as…
a) Light  b) A walker, a rider or a letter  c) A telephone call  d) A broadcast
**Answer: b) A walker, a rider or a letter**

**4.** Tools change because…
a) The needs disappear  b) People need the same jobs done better — faster, safer, easier  c) Old tools are ugly  d) Time passes by itself
**Answer: b) People need the same jobs done better — faster, safer, easier**
