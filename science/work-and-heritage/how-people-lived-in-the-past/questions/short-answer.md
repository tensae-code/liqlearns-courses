# How people lived in the past — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Iran's Grade 6 book begins its communication lesson with a football match at 18:30. Why?
**Answer: To make the change felt: news everyone needs at a set time once travelled only as fast as a person or a letter could carry it, while today it reaches everyone at once — the need is the same, the tool is new.**

**2.** Say one need that has never changed, and two tools that have done its job in different ages.
**Answer: Example: lighting the home — fire (then candle/oil lamp) in the past, electric light now; or sending news — a rider with a letter, then the telephone/instant message.**
