# Hands-on — How people lived in the past

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Interview the past
Ask a grandparent or an older neighbour two questions: how did your home get light at night, and how did you send a message to another town? Write their answers beside today's way of doing the same two jobs.

## One job, two ages
Choose one job — cooking, washing, travelling to school. Make a two-column table, past and now, and under it write the need that never changed.
