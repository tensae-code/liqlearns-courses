Today: **How people lived in the past** — Iran's way.

Iran's Grade 1 book opens the past with the home: in old times people used fire to warm and light their homes — no switches, no lamps wired to a grid. Iran's Grade 6 book tells the communication story: humans have always needed to communicate with each other, and suppose today at 18:30 there is a football match everyone must hear about — in the past that news travelled as fast as a walker, a rider or a letter; now it travels at once. Tools change because people need them to do more: faster news, safer light, warmer rooms.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
