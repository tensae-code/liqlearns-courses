# Our Country — the Abbay River and the Renaissance Dam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three uses of the Abbay.**
**Answer:** Drinking for people and animals; irrigation; fish farming.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the Renaissance Dam being built?**
**Answer:** To give electric light, built with all Ethiopians' money.
