# Our Country — the Abbay River and the Renaissance Dam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The Abbay's uses include —**
a) drinking, irrigation and fish farming  b) only flooding  c) nothing
**Answer: a) drinking, irrigation and fish farming** — Drinking, irrigation and fish are the named uses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The dam on the Abbay is built to give —**
a) electric light  b) only stone  c) silence
**Answer: a) electric light** — The dam is built to give electric light.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Fish farming needs —**
a) a river or water body  b) a desert  c) a road
**Answer: a) a river or water body** — Fish farming is a water use of the Abbay.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) The Abbay is also called —**
a) the Blue Nile  b) the Red Sea  c) Lake Tana
**Answer: a) the Blue Nile** — Abbay = the Blue Nile.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) The Abbay song's theme says the dammed river will —**
a) carry more soil away  b) shine its light  c) dry up
**Answer: b) shine its light** — The song: the river will shine its light.
