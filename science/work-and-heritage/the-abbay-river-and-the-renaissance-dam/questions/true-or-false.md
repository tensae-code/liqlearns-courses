# Our Country — the Abbay River and the Renaissance Dam — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Animals as well as people drink from the Abbay.**
**Answer: True** — Drinking for people and animals is named.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The book says the dam is built with all Ethiopians' money.**
**Answer: True** — That is the book's own statement.
