# Our Country — the Abbay River and the Renaissance Dam

## Do it first

One river in Ethiopia is so great that a dam on it is built by the whole country together. Do you know its name? Guess, then read.

## Sub lesson 1 — What the Abbay gives

The Abbay (Blue Nile) serves the people along it: drinking water for people and animals, irrigation water for farms, and fish farming. A great river is a working river.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Renaissance Dam — built by all Ethiopians

On the Abbay, the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam is being built with all Ethiopians' money, to give electric light. An Abbay song sings the same hope, in theme: Abbay, our great river, will stop carrying our soil away and shine its light, dammed by Ethiopian effort and money.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: the Abbay (Blue Nile) — what the great river gives: drinking water, irrigation and fish — and the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam, built with all Ethiopians' money to give electric light. Then answer one question they ask you about it.
