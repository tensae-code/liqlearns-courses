# Hands-on — The Region's Food and Dress

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the dress rail
Gather or draw six garments. Sort them twice — once by material (cotton, nylon, leather, silk), once by use (work, joy, mourning, festival, adornment) — and say which sorting was harder, and why.

## A community dish
Ask at home how one dish of your community is made, step by step, and present the steps to the class without tasting notes — the making is the culture.
