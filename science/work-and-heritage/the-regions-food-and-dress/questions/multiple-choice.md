# The Region's Food and Dress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Chechebsa belongs to —**
a) the Awi  b) the Oromo community  c) Gondar
**Answer: b) the Oromo community** — Chechebsa is the Oromo community's dish.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which food is common everywhere in the region?**
a) injera  b) abzi  c) sushi
**Answer: a) injera** — Injera, doro wot, genfo, dabbo, qolo and nifro are common everywhere.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Faith rules at the table mean —**
a) everyone eats everything  b) not every animal's meat is eaten or milk drunk by every community  c) food has no rules
**Answer: b) not every animal's meat is eaten or milk drunk by every community** — Faith shapes what is eaten and drunk.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The book pictures the traditional dress of —**
a) two places  b) five places — Awi, Gojjam, Gondar, Wollo, Shewa  c) no places
**Answer: b) five places — Awi, Gojjam, Gondar, Wollo, Shewa** — Five named regional styles.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Dress can be sorted by —**
a) material and use  b) price only  c) size only
**Answer: a) material and use** — Cotton, nylon, leather, silk — and work, joy, mourning, festival, adornment.
