# The Region's Food and Dress — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Abzi belongs to the Awi.**
**Answer: True** — Abzi is the Awi community's dish.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Clothing is not a mark of culture.**
**Answer: False** — Clothing is named as one of the marks of a culture.
