# The Region's Food and Dress — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four foods common everywhere in the region.**
**Answer:** Any four of: injera, doro wot, genfo, dabbo, qolo, nifro.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five places whose dress the book pictures.**
**Answer:** Awi, Gojjam, Gondar, Wollo and Shewa.
