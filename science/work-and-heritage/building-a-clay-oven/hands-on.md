# Hands-on — Building a clay oven

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shape a small oven
With clay or firm mud, shape a miniature oven: a floor, walls around a fire space, and an opening. Let it dry fully. You have modelled the lesson's making at hand scale — material, shaping, structure.

## Clay test
Roll two small clay balls. Leave one wet and let one dry hard. Compare: which holds its shape, which can be reshaped? Say why the oven must be the dried one before fire ever enters it.
