# Building a clay oven — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ovens are made from _____, the Mauritanian way.
**Answer: clay**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Clay is shaped when _____ and hardened before it meets fire.
**Answer: wet**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The craft chain runs: material, shaping, _____, use.
**Answer: structure**
