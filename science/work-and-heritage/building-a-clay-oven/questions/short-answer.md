# Building a clay oven — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does clay suit oven-building?
**Answer:** It is found underfoot, can be shaped by hand when wet, and once hardened it holds its shape against the fire the oven contains.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does building the oven teach beyond the oven itself?
**Answer:** The whole craft chain at once — choosing a material, shaping it, giving it structure, and putting it to use — heritage learned by making, not by description.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the parts the shaped oven body needs.
**Answer:** Walls rising around a fire space, and an opening for the fire and the bread — the structure that lets the oven hold fire and bake.
