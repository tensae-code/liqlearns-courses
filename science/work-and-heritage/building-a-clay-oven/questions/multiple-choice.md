# Building a clay oven — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The traditional oven is made from…
a) Metal sheets  b) Clay  c) Glass  d) Plastic
**Answer: b) Clay**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Clay serves as the oven's material because it…
a) Melts easily  b) Shapes when wet and hardens to stand heat  c) Is always dry  d) Cannot crack at all
**Answer: b) Shapes when wet and hardens to stand heat**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The making lesson teaches the craft chain…
a) Buying and selling  b) Material, shaping, structure, use  c) Reading and writing  d) Planting and harvesting
**Answer: b) Material, shaping, structure, use**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The oven's body is shaped with…
a) A factory mould only  b) The builders' own hands  c) A kiln first  d) Metal tools only
**Answer: b) The builders' own hands**
