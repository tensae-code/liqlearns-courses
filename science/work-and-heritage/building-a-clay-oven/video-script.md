# Video script — Building a clay oven


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Today: **Building a clay oven** — Mauritania's way: the traditional oven, made by the learners' own hands.

The material is the ground itself — clay. Wet, it shapes by hand; dried and hardened, it stands against fire. Walls rise around a fire space, an opening is left for the fire and the bread, and the oven is finished.

Grade 4 does not just hear about the traditional oven. It makes one — material, shaping, structure and use, learned in a single making.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
