# Work and Livelihood — Farming: Crops and Animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Agriculture is lived by about what share of the people?**
a) 8%  b) 82%  c) 18%
**Answer: b) 82%** — 82% of the people live by agriculture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Which list is all crops?**
a) Teff, barley, peas  b) Cattle, goats, chickens  c) Pots, cloth, sickles
**Answer: a) Teff, barley, peas** — Teff, barley and peas are crops.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Equines in the book means —**
a) cattle  b) horses, donkeys and mules  c) goats
**Answer: b) horses, donkeys and mules** — Equines: horses, donkeys, mules.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A farmer who grows wheat and keeps sheep is doing —**
a) only trade  b) agriculture — both halves  c) industry
**Answer: b) agriculture — both halves** — Crops and animals together: agriculture.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Chickpeas and beans are —**
a) pulses among the crops  b) animals  c) minerals
**Answer: a) pulses among the crops** — They are crop pulses.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) Why is agriculture called the backbone?**
a) It is hard  b) Most people live by it — the country stands on it  c) It is old
**Answer: b) Most people live by it — the country stands on it** — 82% live by it.
