# Work and Livelihood — Farming: Crops and Animals — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Economy means the daily activities by which people meet basic needs.**
**Answer: True** — That is the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Niger seed and linseed are oil crops in the book's list.**
**Answer: True** — Both are named among the crops.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Crop production and animal rearing are the two halves of agriculture.**
**Answer: True** — Farming = crops + animals.
