# Work and Livelihood — Farming: Crops and Animals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Name five crops you know from this lesson.**
**Answer:** Any five of: potato, wheat, maize, teff, barley, sorghum, finger millet, niger seed, linseed, peas, beans, chickpeas, fruits, vegetables.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two halves of agriculture.**
**Answer:** Crop production and animal rearing.

**3. (Sub lesson 4) Name three things farming gives the country.**
**Answer:** Any three of: food; raw materials for industry; foreign exchange; work for most people.

**4. (Sub lesson 5) Why does traditional farming yield little?**
**Answer:** For want of modern tools and inputs — fertiliser, improved seed, irrigation, pest and weed control and machines.

**5. (Sub lesson 6) Name four animals the region keeps and one service each gives.**
**Answer:** Any four of: chickens (eggs/meat), sheep, goats, oxen (farm power), cows (milk), camels (transport), horses, donkeys (carrying), bees (honey).
