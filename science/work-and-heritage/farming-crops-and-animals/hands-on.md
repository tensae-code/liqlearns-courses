# Hands-on — Work and Livelihood — Farming: Crops and Animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Seed sorting
Sort real seeds and grains (teff, wheat, peas, beans) into labelled cups, naming each.

## Farm halves poster
Two teams build one poster: crops team and animals team, joined under the title 'Agriculture — our backbone'.

## Make a crop-sample board
Collect small samples of local crops — a few grains or seeds of each — glue them in rows on stiff paper, label each row with its family (cereals, pulses, oilseeds), and present your board.

## Two farms, one hillside
Draw one hillside twice: farmed traditionally (shifting field, mixed crops and cattle) and farmed modernly (irrigated rows, improved seed). Label every difference, then say which harvest feeds more people — and what each farm needs to start.
