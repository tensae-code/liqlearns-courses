# Culture — How Our People Live — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Language and song are parts of culture.**
**Answer: True** — Both are named in the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The town's people all speak one language and follow one faith.**
**Answer: False** — The reading shows different languages and faiths side by side.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Culture stays exactly the same forever and never changes.**
**Answer: False** — Culture is handed down, growing and changing across generations.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) The teacher guides teach the cultures of the region as a lesson topic.**
**Answer: True** — Both guides carry it as Unit 4's first topic (teacher guide — evidence).
