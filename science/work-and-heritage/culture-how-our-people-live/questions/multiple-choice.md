# Culture — How Our People Live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Culture is —**
a) found ready-made in nature  b) a people's whole way of life, built through history  c) only food
**Answer: b) a people's whole way of life, built through history** — Culture is people's accumulated way of life.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In the reading, the town's market day is —**
a) Saturday  b) Monday  c) every night
**Answer: a) Saturday** — The Saturday market is the reading's centre.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which is NOT part of culture as the book defines it?**
a) Language  b) Song  c) A stone lying unworked in a river
**Answer: c) A stone lying unworked in a river** — Culture is made by people; an untouched stone is nature.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Farmers at the Saturday market sell —**
a) teff, wheat, honey and butter  b) only cars  c) nothing
**Answer: a) teff, wheat, honey and butter** — The reading lists farm goods.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Culture grows —**
a) in one day  b) through history, accumulated by people  c) under the ground
**Answer: b) through history, accumulated by people** — Culture accumulates through history.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) About how much of the Amhara Region's people live in the countryside?**
a) 19.5%  b) 40%  c) 80.5%
**Answer: c) 80.5%** — Most of the region lives in the countryside; 19.5% live in towns.

**7. (Sub lesson 3) The communities of the region share —**
a) a common 'tuba' culture  b) nothing  c) one language only
**Answer: a) a common 'tuba' culture** — Many communities, one shared tuba culture.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) The SNNP teacher guide adds which topic of its own?**
a) the languages spoken in the region  b) the moon's phases  c) sea fish
**Answer: a) the languages spoken in the region** — Languages are the SNNP guide's own subtopic (teacher guide — evidence).

**9. (Sub lesson 4) Different cultures in one region live —**
a) far apart, never meeting  b) side by side  c) only in books
**Answer: b) side by side** — Side by side, in one shared life.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) A farm tool is an example of —**
a) spiritual culture  b) material culture  c) no culture
**Answer: b) material culture** — Material culture is things made and used — touchable things.

**11. (Sub lesson 5) Fukera and shelela belong to —**
a) material culture  b) spiritual culture  c) foreign culture
**Answer: b) spiritual culture** — They are carried in performance and memory, not touchable — spiritual culture.
