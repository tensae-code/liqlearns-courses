# Culture — How Our People Live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Define culture in your own words.**
**Answer:** A people's whole way of life — language, religion, song, work and customs — built by people through history.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) From the reading, name two things farmers buy back at the market.**
**Answer:** Sugar, oil, shoes, clothes, corrugated iron, nails (any two).
