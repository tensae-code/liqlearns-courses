# Video script — Culture — How Our People Live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Culture is how a people lives.

Their language. Their faith. Their songs. Their work.

People build culture through history — it is not found in nature.

Amhara, Agew, Qimant, Oromo, Argobba — many peoples, one region, a shared tuba culture.

Culture: a way of life, handed down — and still growing, still changing.
