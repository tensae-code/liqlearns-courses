# Aquaculture — fish farming — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) _____ is fish farming — rearing fish on purpose in a made place.
**Answer: Aquaculture**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Aquaculture is agriculture done in _____.
**Answer: water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A fish farm is _____ first, then stocked and reared.
**Answer: created**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The syllabus asks for aquaculture's advantages and _____ together.
**Answer: disadvantages**
