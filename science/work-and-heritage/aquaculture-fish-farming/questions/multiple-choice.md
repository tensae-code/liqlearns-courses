# Aquaculture — fish farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Aquaculture is…
a) Catching wild fish only  b) Fish farming — rearing fish on purpose in a made place  c) A kind of boat  d) Freezing fish
**Answer: b) Fish farming — rearing fish on purpose in a made place**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Aquaculture is agriculture done…
a) In the air  b) In water  c) Underground  d) In a market
**Answer: b) In water**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Barbados's syllabus teaches aquaculture beside…
a) Mining  b) Poultry and cattle rearing  c) Road building  d) Weaving
**Answer: b) Poultry and cattle rearing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A fish farm must first be…
a) Sold  b) Created — built, then stocked and reared  c) Painted  d) Hidden
**Answer: b) Created — built, then stocked and reared**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The syllabus asks for aquaculture's…
a) Price only  b) Advantages and disadvantages together  c) Songs  d) History only
**Answer: b) Advantages and disadvantages together**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Farmed fish give people…
a) A supply they control, reared close and on purpose  b) No work at all  c) Only wild catches  d) Fish that need no food or care
**Answer: a) A supply they control, reared close and on purpose**
