# Aquaculture — fish farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define aquaculture.
**Answer: Aquaculture is fish farming: rearing fish on purpose, in a made place, instead of only catching wild fish.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What three things does Barbados's syllabus ask about aquaculture?
**Answer: What aquaculture is, the fish reared this way, and how fish farms are created — then its advantages and disadvantages.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How is a fish farm like the farming in the crops-and-animals lesson?
**Answer: Like a crop field or a chicken pen, it is prepared first, then stocked and reared on purpose — agriculture, done in water instead of on land.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why teach advantages and disadvantages together?
**Answer: So the learner weighs the farming choice fairly — a controlled supply on one side, a farm that must be created and kept, and can fail or harm if badly done, on the other.**
