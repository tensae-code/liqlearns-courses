# Hands-on — Aquaculture — fish farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plan a fish farm on paper
Draw a fish farm: the water place, where the fish are reared, where their food comes from. Label the parts in creation order — what is made first, what comes next.

## Weigh it like a farmer
With a friend, argue the farm both ways: one of you speaks only advantages, the other only disadvantages, then swap. Finish by saying, together, what a careful farmer would conclude.
