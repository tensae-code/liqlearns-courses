Today: **Aquaculture — fish farming** — Barbados's way: agriculture in the water.

Farmers rear chickens and cattle on land. Aquaculture rears fish — on purpose, in a made place, a farm that is created first and then stocked. Barbados's syllabus teaches it fairly: the fish reared, how the farms are made, and the advantages and disadvantages weighed together.
