# Hands-on — Simple machines in farming — plough, mattock, sickle and well bucket

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: science reading "Simple Machines in Farming": humans have relied on simple machines for thousands of years — the plough drawn by donkeys or bulls to turn the soil and prepare it for sowing; the iron hand tools, the mattock (pick) and the axe/hoe; the sickle with its steel blade and wooden handle for harvesting crops and cutting grass and tree trunks; and drawing water from the wells with the bucket and rope, by hand or with the animals' help Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
