# Selective breeding of plants and animals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How does a breeder breed OUT an unwanted quality?
**Answer: By never choosing plants or animals with that quality as parents — generation after generation it is left out of the stock's parentage, so it fades from the stock.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How were wild grasses turned into today's grain crops?
**Answer: By centuries of selective breeding: farmers saved and planted seed from the best plants each generation, choosing for yield and grain, until the chosen qualities defined the crop.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the two things a breeder chooses between, in animals and in plants.
**Answer: In animals, mates — which animals pair to parent the next generation; in plants, seed (and pollen) — which plants the next crop is grown from.**
