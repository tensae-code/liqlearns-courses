# Selective breeding of plants and animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Selective breeding is…
a) Letting plants and animals breed entirely at random  b) Improving plants and animals by choosing which ones become the parents of the next generation  c) Crossing every plant with every animal  d) A way of storing seed in the cold
**Answer: b) Improving plants and animals by choosing which ones become the parents of the next generation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In plants, the breeder's choosing works mainly through…
a) Choosing mates  b) Saved seed and pollen from the best plants  c) Cutting all the leaves  d) Watering twice a day
**Answer: b) Saved seed and pollen from the best plants**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A wanted quality grows stronger in a stock because…
a) It is written on the fence  b) Every generation's parents were chosen for it  c) The animals ask for it  d) Sunlight paints it in
**Answer: b) Every generation's parents were chosen for it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Bhutan's book teaches breeding in the same chapter as adaptation because…
a) They are the same event  b) Breeding is fitting by choosing, where adaptation is fitting by survival  c) Both happen only in winter  d) Neither involves parents
**Answer: b) Breeding is fitting by choosing, where adaptation is fitting by survival**
