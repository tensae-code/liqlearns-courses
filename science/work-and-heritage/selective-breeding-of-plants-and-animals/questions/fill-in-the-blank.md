# Selective breeding of plants and animals — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Improving plants and animals by choosing their parents is called _____ breeding.
**Answer: selective**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In plants, the choosing is done with saved _____ and pollen.
**Answer: seed**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Breeding is fitting by choosing; adaptation is fitting by _____.
**Answer: survival**
