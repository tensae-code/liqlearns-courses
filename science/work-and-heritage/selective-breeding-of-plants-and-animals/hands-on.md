# Hands-on — Selective breeding of plants and animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Seed choosing
If your home or school has seed being saved — beans, maize, chillies or any crop — spread a handful out and do the breeder's work: choose the seeds you would plant from, and say for each chosen seed what quality you picked (size, fullness, freedom from damage). Then choose the ones you would reject, and say what quality you are breeding out. The choosing, done yearly, is the whole method.

## Two herds on paper
Draw two herds of six goats each. In the first, mark the two strongest as the parents and sketch their young a little stronger than average. In the second, mark the two weakest as parents and sketch their young. Write under each drawing what happens to the herd after many generations of the same choosing — selective breeding read off your own page.
