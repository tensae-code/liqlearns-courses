# Tourism — the Smokeless Industry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Sof Omar cave belongs to which attraction group?**
a) historic  b) landform  c) parks
**Answer: b) landform** — Sof Omar is a landform attraction.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The sector is undeveloped, says the book, partly for want of —**
a) visitors' interest  b) modern hotels, markets and advertising  c) attractions
**Answer: b) modern hotels, markets and advertising** — The attractions are rich; the hotels, operators and advertising are few.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Tourism is called the smokeless —**
a) farm  b) industry  c) corridor
**Answer: b) industry** — An industry that earns without factory smoke.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Tourism's share of the region's foreign exchange is about —**
a) 7%  b) 10%  c) 17%
**Answer: c) 17%** — About 17% of foreign exchange; about 10% of total yearly income.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Lucy/Dinknesh is held by —**
a) Kenya  b) Ethiopia  c) Tanzania
**Answer: b) Ethiopia** — Ethiopia holds Lucy — fossil evidence of human origins.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Jinja's white water, at the Nile's source, is in —**
a) Uganda  b) Rwanda  c) Burundi
**Answer: a) Uganda** — Jinja is Uganda's draw, at the Nile's source.
