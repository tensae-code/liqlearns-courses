# Tourism — the Smokeless Industry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name one attraction from each of the three groups.**
**Answer:** Historic: e.g. Lalibela. Landform: e.g. Tis Abay falls. Parks: a national park or sanctuary.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name two things tourism builds besides earnings.**
**Answer:** Any two of: infrastructure (roads, health stations, electricity); friendship between the region and the world; a reason to protect the environment for the next generation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Plan a three-country tour: name one draw in each.**
**Answer:** Any three countries with a named draw from the lesson, e.g. Kenya — Maasai Mara; Tanzania — Kilimanjaro; Ethiopia — Lalibela.
