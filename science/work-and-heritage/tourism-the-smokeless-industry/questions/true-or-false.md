# Tourism — the Smokeless Industry — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Ethiopia has few tourist attractions.**
**Answer: False** — It is rich in them — the sector around them is what is undeveloped.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Tourism makes the region depend more on agriculture.**
**Answer: False** — It loosens the economy's dependence on agriculture.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) The Serengeti is in Kenya.**
**Answer: False** — The Serengeti is Tanzania's; Kenya's great park draw is the Maasai Mara.
