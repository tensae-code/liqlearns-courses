# Tourism — the Smokeless Industry

## Sub lesson 1 — Tourism in Ethiopia

Ethiopia is rich in what visitors come for — **plants and animals, historic places, landforms, cultures** — yet the Grade 5 book says plainly the sector is **undeveloped**: low development, few modern hotels and markets, limited tour operators, little modern advertising. The attractions stand in three groups: **historic** — Aksum obelisks, Gondar castles, Lalibela churches, Tana monasteries, Al-Nejashi mosque, Harar walls; **landform** — Ras Dejen, the Abbay/Tis Abay falls, Lake Tana and the Abbay's sources, Sof Omar cave, the upper Awash Rift; and **national parks and sanctuaries**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The smokeless industry

Grade 6 gives tourism its famous name: the **smokeless industry** — an industry with no chimneys. Its weight in East Africa is counted: about **17% of the region's foreign exchange** and roughly **10% of total yearly income**, creating about **7% of jobs in Tanzania, Uganda and Kenya**. And it does quieter work: it **loosens the economy's dependence on agriculture**, **spreads infrastructure** — roads, health stations, electricity — **builds friendship** between the region and the world, and gives a reason to hand a protected environment to the next generation.

## Sub lesson 3 — A tour of the attractions

The book tours the region's draws. **Kenya**, Africa's favourite tourist destination: the Maasai Mara, Maasai culture, Mount Kenya, Amboseli National Park, Lake Nakuru, Mombasa. **Burundi**: beautiful Bujumbura, Lake Tanganyika, and Kasumo, the Nile's other source. **Tanzania**: Kilimanjaro, the Serengeti, Zanzibar's ancient Stone Town. **Uganda**: the Rwenzori, its great apes, Jinja's white water at the Nile's source, Bwindi. **Rwanda**: Akagera, the Nyanza palace museum, Lake Kivu. The island draws of **Mauritius and Seychelles**. And **Ethiopia**: rich in man-made heritage and rare wild animals found nowhere else — walia, Menelik's bushbuck, the gelada — and holding **Lucy/Dinknesh**, the fossil evidence of human origins.
