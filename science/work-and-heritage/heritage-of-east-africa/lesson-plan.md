# Lesson plan — Heritage of East Africa

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Work and Heritage · **Lesson:** Heritage of East Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what heritage is at region scale — the bridge between generations — in its spiritual, movable, immovable and natural kinds, with the region's heritage cards and its four protections.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the heritage lessons for Ethiopia — the same sorting, now carried across the whole region's sites.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Heritage — the bridge between generations** — heritage is the material and spiritual things past generations made, used and handed down — a bridge joining yesterday's generation to today's; a people's identity mark and the surest historical evidence of how they lived (Grade 6).

2. **Spiritual heritage — what cannot be touched** — the first great class: songs of grief and joy (ዝማሬ, ሽለላ, ቀረርቶ, እንጉርጉሮ), the many dances, yearly festivals, tales, riddles and proverbial sayings, carried in memory and performance (Grade 6).

3. **Movable heritage — what can be carried** — metalwork of gold, silver, copper and bronze — coins, jewellery, ancient crosses; stone, clay and glass objects with inscriptions; wood, fibre, leather, bone and horn things; garments of rank; ancient writings on stone and parchment, archives and seals; fossil remains (Grade 6).

4. **Immovable heritage — and natural heritage** — immovable: dwelling caves with wall paintings, stelae and standing stones, houses of worship, palaces, ruined old cities, castles, ancient bridges, battlefields, kings' tombs; natural heritage: rivers, waterfalls, valleys, parks, mountains, great trees (Grade 6).

5. **Heritage cards — famous sites of the region** — the chapter's photo cards (plot and fact summaries): Kasubi Tombs (Uganda, 1882, four Buganda kings); Fasil Ghebbi (Gondar, 17th century, 70,000 m², 900 m wall); Timket (UNESCO intangible); Songo Mnara ruins (Tanzania, 15th–16th century trading post, five mosques, a palace, 33 houses); Lalibela (eleven rock churches, 800+ years); Kilimanjaro (5,895 m, 82% of its ice lost in a century); Mombasa's annual November festival; Fort Jesus (1593–96, Portuguese); Dire Sheikh Hussein mosque (Bale, 800+ years, pilgrimage centre); Serengeti National Park (14,760 km² of migrating herds) (Grade 6).

6. **Protecting heritage — teach, guard, register, repair** — threats: climate change, great infrastructure works, looting/theft, civil war and instability; the four protections: education, guarding, registration (ምዝገባ), repair (ጥገና) (Grade 6).

## If the learner is stuck
- The words are new → go back to the lesson's picture and say what it shows, in home words first, then in the lesson's words.
- The parts blur together → take one part at a time and answer that part's questions before moving on; the gate is per part where sub lessons exist.
- The learner cannot do the Do It Yourself → do the first item together, then let them finish the rest alone.
