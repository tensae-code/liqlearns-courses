# Heritage of East Africa — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Heritage is only about kings and palaces.**
**Answer: False** — It is everything past generations made, used and handed down — material and spiritual.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A festival can be stored in a museum like a pot.**
**Answer: False** — A festival is spiritual heritage — it exists in performance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A parchment writing cannot be heritage because it is read, not used.**
**Answer: False** — Ancient writings, archives and seals are named movable heritage.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) A castle could be moved to a museum if it were small enough to carry.**
**Answer: False** — Castles and palaces are immovable by kind — they stay where they were made.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) Fort Jesus was built in the 1800s.**
**Answer: False** — It was built by the Portuguese in 1593–96.

**6. (Sub lesson 6) Repair means rebuilding a damaged site in a modern style.**
**Answer: False** — Repair mends carefully, keeping the heritage in its own condition.
