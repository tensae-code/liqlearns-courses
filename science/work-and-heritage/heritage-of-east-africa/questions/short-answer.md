# Heritage of East Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Explain the bridge image in your own words.**
**Answer:** Past generations stand on one bank and today's on the other; the things they made, used and handed down are the bridge that joins the two.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name three kinds of spiritual heritage.**
**Answer:** Any three of: songs of grief and joy, dances, yearly festivals, tales, riddles, proverbial sayings.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Give two examples of movable heritage and say why they are movable.**
**Answer:** E.g. a coin and a parchment: both are heritage and both can be carried from place to place.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Name two immovable and two natural heritages.**
**Answer:** Immovable: e.g. stelae, a palace, a ruined city, a cave with paintings. Natural: e.g. a waterfall, a great tree, a park, a river.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) Sort three cards into their heritage classes.**
**Answer:** E.g. Fasil Ghebbi — immovable; Timket — spiritual; Serengeti — natural (movable answers should be small carried objects; the cards are mostly sites and festivals).

**6. (Sub lesson 6) Name the four protections and one threat they answer.**
**Answer:** Protections: education, guarding, registration, repair. Threat: any of climate change, infrastructure works, looting, civil war.
