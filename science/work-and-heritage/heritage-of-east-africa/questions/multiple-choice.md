# Heritage of East Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The book's image for heritage is —**
a) a wall  b) a bridge  c) a market
**Answer: b) a bridge** — A bridge joining yesterday's generation to today's.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Heritage tells one people from another — that is its —**
a) world face  b) national face  c) hidden face
**Answer: b) national face** — The national face is how one people is told from another.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A riddle passed from grandmother to child is —**
a) movable heritage  b) spiritual heritage  c) natural heritage
**Answer: b) spiritual heritage** — Tales, riddles and sayings are spiritual heritage.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Spiritual heritage is kept —**
a) in a warehouse  b) in memory and performance  c) under the ground
**Answer: b) in memory and performance** — It lives only where it is remembered and performed.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) An ancient cross of gold is —**
a) immovable heritage  b) movable heritage  c) spiritual heritage
**Answer: b) movable heritage** — It can be carried — movable heritage.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Fossil remains of ancient animals count as —**
a) movable heritage  b) no heritage  c) spiritual heritage
**Answer: a) movable heritage** — Fossils are listed among movable heritage.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) A rock-hewn church is —**
a) movable  b) immovable  c) spiritual
**Answer: b) immovable** — It stays where it was made — immovable heritage.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) A great waterfall counts as —**
a) natural heritage  b) movable heritage  c) no heritage
**Answer: a) natural heritage** — Rivers, waterfalls, valleys, parks, mountains and great trees are natural heritage.

**9. (Sub lesson 5) Kilimanjaro's height is —**
a) 5,895 m  b) 4,600 m  c) 1,700 m
**Answer: a) 5,895 m** — 5,895 m — Africa's highest, and the world's largest free-standing volcanic mountain.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) Timket, among the cards, is which heritage class?**
a) movable  b) spiritual / intangible  c) natural
**Answer: b) spiritual / intangible** — Timket is a festival — UNESCO intangible heritage.

**11. (Sub lesson 6) ምዝገባ means the protection of —**
a) repair  b) registration  c) guarding
**Answer: b) registration** — ምዝገባ is registration — recording kind, count, place and condition.

**12. (Sub lesson 6) Which is named among the worst destroyers of heritage?**
a) rain  b) war  c) schooling
**Answer: b) war** — War is named among the worst destroyers.
