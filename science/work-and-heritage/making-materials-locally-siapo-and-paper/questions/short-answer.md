# Making materials locally — siapo and paper — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Materials Samoa makes — siapo and the rest.
**Answer:** Samoa makes many of its own materials: siapo (tapa cloth) from bark, coconut cream, ice cream, bread, rope, beer and kofe powder.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Hand-made paper — and naming every change.
**Answer:** Paper can be made by hand from plant materials: the plants are pulped, the pulp is spread to form sheets, the sheets are blotted and dried.
