# Work and Livelihood — When Our Work Hurts Nature — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Lacking energy alternatives makes people depend on firewood.**
**Answer: True** — That dependence is named as a driver.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Eroded soil harms the farm itself.**
**Answer: True** — The farm loses its own soil.
