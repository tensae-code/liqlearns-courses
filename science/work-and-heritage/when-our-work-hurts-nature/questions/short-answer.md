# Work and Livelihood — When Our Work Hurts Nature — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three pressures that harm natural resources.**
**Answer:** Growing demand for farmland; firewood cutting with no alternatives; overgrazing; backward land use (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Say one way the pressure can be eased.**
**Answer:** Plant trees / graze in turns / use land wisely / find other energy (any one).
