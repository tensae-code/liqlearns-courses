# Work and Livelihood — When Our Work Hurts Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Growing population raises demand for —**
a) farmland and firewood  b) nothing  c) less land
**Answer: a) farmland and firewood** — More people need more farmland and fuel.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Overgrazing means —**
a) too few animals  b) too many animals eating a place bare  c) animals resting
**Answer: b) too many animals eating a place bare** — Overgrazing strips the grass cover.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which eases pressure on forests?**
a) Cutting more  b) Planting trees and finding other energy  c) Burning fields
**Answer: b) Planting trees and finding other energy** — Planting and alternatives ease pressure.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Bare, uncovered land in heavy rain will —**
a) grow richer  b) erode — lose its soil  c) turn to stone
**Answer: b) erode — lose its soil** — Rain strips bare soil.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) The harm in this lesson comes from —**
a) ordinary work done without care  b) wild animals only  c) the rain alone
**Answer: a) ordinary work done without care** — Work without care is the subject.
