# Hands-on — Work and Livelihood — When Our Work Hurts Nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rain on two lands
Pour water on a grass-covered soil tray and a bare one; compare the muddy runoff — overgrazing's lesson in miniature.

## Pressure chain
As a class, build the chain in cards: more people → more farmland and firewood → forest falls → soil erodes; then add the 'easing' cards that break the chain.
