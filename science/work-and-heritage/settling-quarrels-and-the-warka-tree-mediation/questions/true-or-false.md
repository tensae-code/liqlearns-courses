# Settling Quarrels — and the Warka Tree Mediation — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Most people use the court, not the traditional system.**
**Answer: False** — The traditional system is the one most people use.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Workyantifu lifted the stone off her brother's shoulder as her forgiveness.**
**Answer: True** — Taking the stone off was the sign of forgiving.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The mediation was set for Tir 21.**
**Answer: True** — The panel was named to sit on Tir 21.
