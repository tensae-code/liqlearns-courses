# Settling Quarrels — and the Warka Tree Mediation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Conflict usually comes from differences over —**
a) resources or thinking  b) the weather  c) songs
**Answer: a) resources or thinking** — Resources or thinking — the two named sources.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The traditional way of settling saves —**
a) nothing  b) time and money, and keeps kinship intact  c) only the court's time
**Answer: b) time and money, and keeps kinship intact** — Its named strengths.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Debashu and Workyantifu quarrelled over —**
a) a garden plot  b) a horse  c) a shop
**Answer: a) a garden plot** — Their parents' garden plot.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The elders ruled —**
a) all to Debashu  b) an equal split  c) sale of the plot
**Answer: b) an equal split** — The testament's equal split was upheld.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Debashu showed his apology by —**
a) leaving town  b) lifting a stone onto his shoulder  c) paying money
**Answer: b) lifting a stone onto his shoulder** — 'I have wronged you, forgive me.'

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The panel sat —**
a) in a courtroom  b) under the big warka tree between the two houses  c) at the market
**Answer: b) under the big warka tree between the two houses** — The mediation's named place.
