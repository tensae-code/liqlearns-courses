# Hands-on — Settling Quarrels — and the Warka Tree Mediation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Role-play the panel
In groups of five — two siblings, three elders — act the warka-tree mediation: the two arguments, the ruling, the stone, the forgiveness. The class names the values the panel used.

## The values list
From the case, list every value the mediation rested on — respect, tolerance, faith, shared purpose, honesty, apology — and write where each one appears in the story.
