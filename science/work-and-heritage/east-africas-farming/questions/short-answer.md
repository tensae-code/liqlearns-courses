# East Africa's Farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) State the agriculture puzzle in your own words.**
**Answer:** Most people here farm, yet living has not risen; in developed countries almost nobody farms, yet they feed themselves and aid others — the difference must lie in how farming is done.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Sort into the four activities: sorghum, tobacco, goats, lake fish.**
**Answer:** Sorghum — food crop; tobacco — cash crop; goats — livestock; lake fish — fishery.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Give one farm fact for two countries of the region.**
**Answer:** E.g. Kenya — large livestock work for milk and meat trades; Rwanda — foreign trade resting on tea leaf and coffee.
