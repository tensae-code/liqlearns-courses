# East Africa's Farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Agriculture's average share of the region's GDP is —**
a) 2%  b) 43%  c) 82%
**Answer: b) 43%** — 43% on average — about 50% in Ethiopia and its neighbours named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The book's puzzle is that developed countries farm at under 2% yet —**
a) starve  b) feed themselves and aid others  c) import all food
**Answer: b) feed themselves and aid others** — Their tiny farm share feeds them and sends aid; our huge share has not raised living.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Hides and skins work belongs to —**
a) fishery  b) livestock husbandry  c) cash crops
**Answer: b) livestock husbandry** — Hides and skins come from the livestock side.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Sea fishing is part of which activity?**
a) food crops  b) fishery  c) livestock
**Answer: b) fishery** — Fishery covers rivers, lakes, dams — and the Indian Ocean sea catch.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Africa's greatest coffee producer is —**
a) Kenya  b) Ethiopia  c) Rwanda
**Answer: b) Ethiopia** — Ethiopia — coffee first among its farm exports.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Eritrea's main activities are —**
a) tea and flowers  b) barley, sorghum, wheat and horned cattle  c) sea fishing only
**Answer: b) barley, sorghum, wheat and horned cattle** — Those cereals and cattle are Eritrea's printed picture.
