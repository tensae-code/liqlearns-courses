# East Africa's Farming — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) East Africa's farming is modern and machine-fed.**
**Answer: False** — The book prints it as backward and rain-dependent, its growth slow.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Coffee appears only as a cash crop in the table.**
**Answer: False** — The table lists coffee among food crops too — it is eaten at home and sold abroad.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Burundi's foreign trade rests on fishing.**
**Answer: False** — It rests on coffee and tea leaf.
