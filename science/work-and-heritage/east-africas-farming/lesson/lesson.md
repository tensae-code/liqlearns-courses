# East Africa's Farming

## Do it first

In your home, most of what is eaten was grown or raised by someone. In a whole region of countries, how much of all work is farming? Guess first — then meet the book's number.

## Sub lesson 1 — The biggest sector — and its puzzle

Agriculture holds the **largest place** in East Africa's economy — mixed small-scale farming of livestock, food crops, cash crops and fish — yet it averages only **43% of GDP**: about **50% in Burundi, Uganda, Ethiopia and Tanzania**, up to **46% in Rwanda**, under **30% in Eritrea, Kenya and Madagascar**. Then the book opens its puzzle as a group discussion: developed countries' agriculture is under 2%, yet they feed themselves and aid others — while East Africa's 80%-plus farming share has not raised its people's living. Why? The sector is printed as **backward and rain-dependent**, its growth slow.

## Sub lesson 2 — The four farm activities

The book's table organises the region's farming into **four activities**. **Food crops**: maize, rice, potato, banana, coffee, vegetables, wheat, sorghum and teff. **Cash crops**: tea leaf, coffee, cotton, oilseeds, coconut and tobacco. **Livestock husbandry**: horned cattle, sheep, goats, poultry, and hides and skins work. **Fishery**: fish raised in rivers, lakes and dams, and sea fishing in the Indian Ocean. Note the honest overlap the table keeps: coffee stands in both crop lists — food at home, cash abroad.

## Sub lesson 3 — What each country farms

The national pictures differ. **Rwanda**: banana, sweet potato, cassava, potato and pulses at home; its foreign trade rests on **tea leaf and coffee**. **Kenya**: tobacco, coffee, tea leaf and hides and skins, with large livestock work for milk and meat. **Burundi**: dependent on **coffee and tea leaf**, with cotton, maize and sweet potato as its backbone. **Ethiopia**: foreign trade based almost wholly on farm products — **coffee first**, as Africa's greatest coffee producer — oilseeds and pulses supporting, and **first in Africa for livestock numbers**. **Eritrea**: barley, sorghum, wheat and horned cattle.
