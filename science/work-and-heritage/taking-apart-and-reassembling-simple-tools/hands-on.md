# Hands-on — Taking apart and reassembling simple tools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Apart and back — a simple tool
With an adult's agreement, take apart one simple, safe household tool or object that is made to come apart (a pen, a hand grinder, a torch). Lay the pieces in the order they came off, name each piece's job, then reassemble in order until it works.

## Order cards
For the tool you studied, write one card per piece in dismantling order. Shuffle, then lay them in taking-apart order and in putting-back order — and say why the second order is the first one reversed.
