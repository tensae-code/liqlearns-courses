# Taking apart and reassembling simple tools — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does dismantling in order teach that looking cannot?
**Answer:** The inside of the tool: which piece held which, what each piece does, and how the pieces together made it work — so a fault can be found where it lives.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's test of success?
**Answer:** The tool reassembled in order, whole in the learner's hands and working again — the proof that the dismantling was truly understood.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) On what tools does the Grade 4 book teach this?
**Answer:** On the grinding tools — dismantling and reassembling them, in the book's experimentation field (printed p. 63).
