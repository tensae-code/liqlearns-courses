# Taking apart and reassembling simple tools — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Taking a tool apart piece by piece is _____ it.
**Answer: dismantling**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Putting the pieces back in order is _____ the tool.
**Answer: reassembling**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Dismantle to understand; reassemble to _____ it.
**Answer: prove**
