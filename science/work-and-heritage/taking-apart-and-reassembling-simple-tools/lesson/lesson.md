# Taking apart and reassembling simple tools

## Do it first
A grinding tool stops working well. You could throw it away — or take it apart, piece by piece, and learn it from the inside. What would taking it apart teach you that looking at it never will? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Mauritania's way in — its Grade 4 book teaches tools by dismantling and reassembling them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Taking a tool apart — in order

Mauritania's Grade 4 *Sciences Naturelles* book teaches **dismantling and reassembling grinding tools** (printed p. 63) in its experimentation field. Taking apart comes first: the tool is dismantled piece by piece, and the order of the taking-apart is itself the knowledge — which piece held which, what each piece does, and how the pieces together made the tool work.

A tool dismantled in order is a tool understood: nothing inside it stays a mystery, and a fault can be found where it lives instead of guessed at from outside.

## Sub lesson 2 — Putting it back together — until it works

Reassembling is the proof of the understanding. The pieces go back in their order, each in its place and facing its way, until the grinding tool stands whole and **works again**.

That is the lesson's test and its point at once: the learner has not learned the tool when it lies in pieces, but when it is whole and working in their hands — taken apart and rebuilt by the same hands. Mauritania teaches the pair as one skill: dismantle to understand, reassemble to prove it.

## The key ideas

- **Dismantling in order** teaches what each piece does and how the pieces together make the tool work.
- **Reassembling** is the proof: pieces back in order, in place, until the tool works again.
- Mauritania's Grade 4 book teaches the pair on the **grinding tools** (printed p. 63).

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "When has the learner actually learned the tool — when it is apart, or when it is back together?" Answer from the lesson: When it is back together and working: dismantling gives the understanding, but reassembling the tool until it works again is the proof that the understanding is real.
