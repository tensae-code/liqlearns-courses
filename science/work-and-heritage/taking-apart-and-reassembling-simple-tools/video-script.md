The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


Today: **Taking apart and reassembling simple tools** — Mauritania's way: learn the tool from the inside.

Take a grinding tool apart, piece by piece, in order. Now nothing inside it is a mystery: you know what each piece does and how they worked together.

Then put it back — pieces in order, each in its place — until it grinds again. Apart teaches; together proves. That is the whole skill, and Grade 4 learns it by hand.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
