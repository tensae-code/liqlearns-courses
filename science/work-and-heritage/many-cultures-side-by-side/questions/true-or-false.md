# Culture — Many Cultures Side by Side — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Different marriage ceremonies are part of diversity.**
**Answer: True** — Marriages are named among the differing customs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Neighbours of different faiths can celebrate in the same community.**
**Answer: True** — The lesson pictures faiths celebrating together.
