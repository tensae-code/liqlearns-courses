# Culture — Many Cultures Side by Side — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Cultural diversity means —**
a) one culture only  b) many cultures in one community  c) no customs at all
**Answer: b) many cultures in one community** — Many cultures side by side in one community.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Which can differ between cultures in one community?**
a) Food preparation and eating customs  b) Gravity  c) The sun's rising
**Answer: a) Food preparation and eating customs** — Food and eating customs are named differences.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A festival where neighbours of different languages celebrate together shows —**
a) diversity  b) a mistake  c) nothing
**Answer: a) diversity** — It shows cultures side by side.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which is a cultural difference?**
a) Different songs and dances  b) Different numbers of fingers  c) Different weather on one day
**Answer: a) Different songs and dances** — Songs and dances are cultural.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Diversity in a community is —**
a) something to hide  b) the normal shape of a real community  c) impossible
**Answer: b) the normal shape of a real community** — The lesson treats it as normal.
