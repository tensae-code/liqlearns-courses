# Culture — Many Cultures Side by Side — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Name four things that can differ between cultures.**
**Answer:** Languages, religions, food preparation, marriage ceremonies, songs and dances, eating customs (any four).

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give one example of diversity in your own community.**
**Answer:** Any true local example: two languages at market, two faiths' holidays, different dress.
