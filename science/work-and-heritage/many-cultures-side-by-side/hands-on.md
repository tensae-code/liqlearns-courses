# Hands-on — Culture — Many Cultures Side by Side

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Festival of neighbours
Each group presents one custom of a culture in the community — a food, a song, a dress — and the class names what it learned.

## Same and different
In pairs from different backgrounds, find two customs you share and two that differ; present all four kindly.

## One thing borrowed
Ask an elder: what is one thing our community borrowed from its neighbours in living memory? Tell its story to the class — that is diffusion with a name and a date.
