# Technology in farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Machines on the farm.
**Answer:** Agricultural technology begins with machines that do farm work: tractor, cultivator, planter, sprinkler, irrigation pump, harvester and milking machine — each machine taking over work arms once did, and with it, disease-resistant high-yield crops bred for the same fields..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Chemical help — fertilizers and pesticides.
**Answer:** Class 5 sorts agricultural technology into three kinds.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Biotechnology — breeding better plants.
**Answer:** The third kind is biotechnology: living things used to breed plants that are more nutritious, pest-resistant and productive — and, as Class 4 adds, plant breeding that can even give new flower colours..

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Machines on the farm → Chemical help — fertilizers and pesticides → Biotechnology — breeding better plants — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
