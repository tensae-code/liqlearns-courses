# Technology in farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Machines on the farm  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Machines on the farm**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Chemical help — fertilizers and pesticides  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Chemical help — fertilizers and pesticides**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Biotechnology — breeding better plants  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Biotechnology — breeding better plants**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: agricultural technology in its kinds — the farm machines, the chemical helps, and biotechnology breeding better plants — as Bangladesh's Classes 4 and 5 teach it. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 65  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 65**
