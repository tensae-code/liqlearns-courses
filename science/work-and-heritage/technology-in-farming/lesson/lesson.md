## Sub lesson 1 — Machines on the farm

Agricultural technology begins with machines that do farm work: tractor, cultivator, planter, sprinkler, irrigation pump, harvester and milking machine — each machine taking over work arms once did, and with it, disease-resistant high-yield crops bred for the same fields.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chemical help — fertilizers and pesticides

Class 5 sorts agricultural technology into three kinds. The second kind is chemical: fertilizers that feed the crop and pesticides that protect it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Biotechnology — breeding better plants

The third kind is biotechnology: living things used to breed plants that are more nutritious, pest-resistant and productive — and, as Class 4 adds, plant breeding that can even give new flower colours.
