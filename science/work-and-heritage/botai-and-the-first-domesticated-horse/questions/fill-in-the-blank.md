# Botai — and the first domesticated horse — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1.** The books teach this part as the first ______ of the horse — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
**Answer: domestication**

**2.** The books teach this part as the Botai culture and the first ______ horse — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
**Answer: domesticated**

**3.** How our ancestors tamed horses (the Botai culture) — taught as its own lesson in ______'s books; the books and pages are named in Sources.
**Answer: Kazakhstan**
