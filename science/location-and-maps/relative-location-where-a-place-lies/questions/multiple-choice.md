# Relative Location — Where a Place Lies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Relative location describes a place by —**
a) its colour  b) the known places around it  c) its age
**Answer: b) the known places around it** — Relative location compares with known places around.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) South Gondar's neighbours are described on —**
a) one side only  b) all eight sides  c) no sides
**Answer: b) all eight sides** — The worked example names neighbours in all directions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A sub-city's relative location is told by —**
a) the sub-cities and landmarks around it  b) its flag only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the sub-cities and landmarks around it** — Neighbours and landmarks give relative location at every scale.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which is a relative location?**
a) 11 degrees north  b) Beside the market  c) Very old
**Answer: b) Beside the market** — 'Beside the market' compares with a known place.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Relative location needs —**
a) known landmarks  b) a telescope  c) silent places
**Answer: a) known landmarks** — Landmarks are the anchors of relative location.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) At zone scale, a landmark can be —**
a) only a house  b) a lake like Lake Tana  c) nothing big
**Answer: b) a lake like Lake Tana** — Big natural features anchor zone descriptions.
