# Relative Location — Where a Place Lies — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) 'Beside the market, across from the clinic' is a relative location.**
**Answer: True** — It fixes the school by its neighbours.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Lake Tana can be used to describe where a zone lies.**
**Answer: True** — Natural features serve as landmarks at zone scale too.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Relative location changes if the landmarks around a place change.**
**Answer: True** — It depends on what is around the place — that is what 'relative' means.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Relative and absolute location are the same way of fixing a place.**
**Answer: False** — One uses neighbours; the other uses numbers that never change.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) Somalia lies west of Ethiopia.**
**Answer: False** — Somalia lies to the east and south-east; the west holds South Sudan and Sudan.

**6. (Sub lesson 6) Ethiopia's border with Kenya is longer than its border with Somalia.**
**Answer: False** — Kenya's is 760 km; Somalia's is 1,600 km — much longer.
