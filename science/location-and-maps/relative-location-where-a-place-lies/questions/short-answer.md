# Relative Location — Where a Place Lies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Describe your school's relative location in one sentence.**
**Answer:** A sentence naming known places around the school, e.g. 'beside the market, across from the clinic.'

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name three neighbours of South Gondar.**
**Answer:** Any three of: East Gojjam, Central Gondar, West Gojjam, Lake Tana, North Wollo, Wag Himra, South Wollo.

**3. (Sub lesson 4) What is the difference between relative and absolute location?**
**Answer:** Relative location describes a place by the places around it; absolute location gives its fixed latitude and longitude.
