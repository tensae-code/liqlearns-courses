# Hands-on — Relative Location — Where a Place Lies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find it from my words
One child describes a place in the school compound by its neighbours only; the others walk to the place described.

## Eight sides
On a big zone sketch, place neighbour name-cards on all eight sides in their true positions.

## Fix your school two ways
Write your school's location twice: once by its neighbours, once — if a map with numbers is at hand — by its latitude and longitude. Which one would still work if the market moved?
