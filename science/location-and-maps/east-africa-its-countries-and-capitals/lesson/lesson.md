# East Africa — Its Countries and Capitals

## Do it first

Name Ethiopia's neighbours from memory. Now think bigger: whole seas, whole groups of countries around Ethiopia's region. What lies north-east? What ocean lies east? Try it first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two kinds of location, at region scale

The region itself can be located both ways you know. **Relatively** — by the land bodies, water bodies and neighbours around it: the **Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden to the north-east**, the **Indian Ocean to the east**, **Sudan and DR Congo to the west**, **Botswana to the south-west**, **South Africa to the south**. A relative location can change as surroundings change. **Absolutely** — by latitude and longitude — a location never changes. East Africa's absolute frame is the one you have already met: 18° N to 26° S, 22° E to 57° E.

## Sub lesson 2 — The nineteen countries in three groups

East Africa is presented as **nineteen countries**, grouped by position. The **Great Lakes** countries: **Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, Kenya**. The **Horn**: **Ethiopia, Eritrea, Djibouti, Somalia** — bordered by Kenya to the south and Sudan to the west. The **Indian Ocean islands**: **Comoros, Mauritius, Réunion, Seychelles, Madagascar**. And four more are counted with the region: **Malawi, Zimbabwe, Zambia and Mozambique**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Capital cities of East Africa

Every country of the region has its capital, and the book tabulates them all: **Addis Ababa** (Ethiopia), **Asmara** (Eritrea), **Djibouti** (Djibouti), **Mogadishu** (Somalia), **Nairobi** (Kenya), **Kampala** (Uganda), **Dodoma** (Tanzania, with Dar es Salaam), **Kigali** (Rwanda), **Gitega** (Burundi), **Juba** (South Sudan), **Antananarivo** (Madagascar), **Port Louis** (Mauritius), **Saint-Denis** (Réunion), **Victoria** (Seychelles), **Moroni** (Comoros), **Harare** (Zimbabwe), **Lusaka** (Zambia), **Maputo** (Mozambique) and **Lilongwe** (Malawi). A capital is a country's address in one word — learn them as pairs: country, capital.

## Sub lesson 4 — East Africa's location and neighbours (teacher guide — evidence)

The Grade 6 teacher guide opens with the same region (teacher guide — evidence): East Africa placed on the map — the guide's own cover pins **Ethiopia among its neighbours**: Sudan, Eritrea, Djibouti, Somalia, Kenya, South Sudan, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Tanzania, with the **Indian Ocean and the Red Sea** at its edges. The guide's second subtopic names the region's **neighbouring countries and water bodies** — the ring of lands and seas that give East Africa its relative location.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: the region of East Africa as one place: located relatively and absolutely, grouped into its nineteen countries, with every capital named. Then answer one question they ask you about it.
