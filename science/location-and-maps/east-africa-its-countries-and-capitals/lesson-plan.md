# Lesson plan — East Africa — Its Countries and Capitals

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Location and Maps · **Lesson:** East Africa — Its Countries and Capitals
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the region of East Africa as one place: located relatively and absolutely, grouped into its nineteen countries, with every capital named.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ethiopia's location lessons — the same two ways of locating, widened one final step from country to region.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Two kinds of location, at region scale** — a place can be located relatively — by the land bodies, water bodies and neighbours around it, a location that can change as surroundings change — or absolutely, by latitude and longitude, which never changes; East Africa relatively: Red Sea and Gulf of Aden north-east, Indian Ocean east, Sudan and DR Congo west, Botswana south-west, South Africa south (Grade 6).

2. **The nineteen countries in three groups** — 19 countries grouped by position: Great Lakes (Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, Kenya); the Horn (Ethiopia, Eritrea, Djibouti, Somalia); Indian Ocean islands (Comoros, Mauritius, Réunion, Seychelles, Madagascar); plus Malawi, Zimbabwe, Zambia and Mozambique (Grade 6).

3. **Capital cities of East Africa** — the book's Table 1.1: Addis Ababa, Asmara, Djibouti, Mogadishu, Nairobi, Kampala, Dodoma (with Dar es Salaam), Kigali, Gitega, Juba, Antananarivo, Port Louis, Saint-Denis, Victoria, Moroni, Harare, Lusaka, Maputo, Lilongwe (Grade 6).

4. **East Africa's location and neighbours (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 6 guide's Unit 1: the region placed on the map with Ethiopia pinned among its neighbours, and East Africa's neighbouring countries and water bodies named (teacher guide — evidence).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title in the Science course, in order. Recommended, never forced — a learner who lands here directly can start here.
- Nothing else is required; every term used is taught inside this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and use what the part "Two kinds of location, at region scale" teaches, in their own words.
2. Say and use what the part "The nineteen countries in three groups" teaches, in their own words.
3. Say and use what the part "Capital cities of East Africa" teaches, in their own words.
4. Say and use what the part "East Africa's location and neighbours (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches, in their own words.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's own world (home, school, neighbourhood) supplies the rest.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Name Ethiopia's neighbours from memory. Now think bigger: whole seas, whole groups of countries around Ethiopia's region. What lies north-east? What ocean lies east? Try it first. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts of the lesson in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of East Africa outlined with the seas and neighbour regions named around it — the student studies it and names what it shows in the lesson's own words.
   - ADD Picture [Picture 13310]: a region chart colour-coded by the three sub-regions plus the four added countries — the student studies it and names what it shows in the lesson's own words.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the region map with a star and name at each capital — the student studies it and names what it shows in the lesson's own words.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's one thing to a companion and answers one question back (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- The words are new → go back to the lesson's picture and say what it shows, in home words first, then in the lesson's words.
- The parts blur together → take one part at a time and answer that part's questions before moving on; the gate is per part where sub lessons exist.
- The learner cannot do the Do It Yourself → do the first item together, then let them finish the rest alone.
