# East Africa — Its Countries and Capitals — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Relative location can never change.**
**Answer: False** — It can change as the surroundings change — that is its mark.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Kenya is an island country of the region.**
**Answer: False** — Kenya is counted among the Great Lakes countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Harare is the capital of Zambia.**
**Answer: False** — Harare is Zimbabwe's capital; Zambia's is Lusaka.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) The region's neighbours are only other East African countries.**
**Answer: False** — The guide names neighbouring countries and water bodies around the region as well.
