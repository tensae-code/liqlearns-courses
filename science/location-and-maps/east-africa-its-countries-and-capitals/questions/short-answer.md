# East Africa — Its Countries and Capitals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give East Africa's relative location in one sentence.**
**Answer:** East Africa lies with the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden to its north-east, the Indian Ocean to its east, Sudan and DR Congo to its west, Botswana to its south-west and South Africa to its south.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four countries counted with the region beyond the three groups.**
**Answer:** Malawi, Zimbabwe, Zambia and Mozambique.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Give the capitals of Ethiopia, Kenya and Somalia.**
**Answer:** Addis Ababa; Nairobi; Mogadishu.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Name five countries the guide places around Ethiopia on its cover map.**
**Answer:** Any five of: Sudan, Eritrea, Djibouti, Somalia, Kenya, South Sudan, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Tanzania.
