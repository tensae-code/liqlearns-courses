# East Africa — Its Countries and Capitals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which water body lies east of East Africa?**
a) the Red Sea  b) the Indian Ocean  c) the Gulf of Aden
**Answer: b) the Indian Ocean** — The Indian Ocean washes the region's eastern side.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Absolute location never changes because it rests on —**
a) neighbours  b) latitude and longitude  c) towns
**Answer: b) latitude and longitude** — Latitude and longitude are fixed; neighbours can change.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which group holds Ethiopia?**
a) Great Lakes  b) the Horn  c) the islands
**Answer: b) the Horn** — Ethiopia stands in the Horn with Eritrea, Djibouti and Somalia.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Madagascar belongs to —**
a) the Horn  b) the Great Lakes  c) the Indian Ocean islands
**Answer: c) the Indian Ocean islands** — Madagascar is one of the island countries.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) The capital of Uganda is —**
a) Nairobi  b) Kampala  c) Kigali
**Answer: b) Kampala** — Kampala is Uganda's capital; Nairobi is Kenya's, Kigali Rwanda's.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Dodoma is the capital of —**
a) Tanzania  b) Burundi  c) Mozambique
**Answer: a) Tanzania** — Dodoma, with Dar es Salaam, is Tanzania's capital entry in the table.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) On the guide's cover map, Ethiopia is shown —**
a) alone  b) pinned among its neighbours  c) without neighbours
**Answer: b) pinned among its neighbours** — The cover itself teaches the placing: Ethiopia among the named neighbours.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) The water bodies at the region's edge in the guide include —**
a) the Atlantic only  b) the Indian Ocean and the Red Sea  c) Lake Tana only
**Answer: b) the Indian Ocean and the Red Sea** — The Indian Ocean and the Red Sea border the region.
