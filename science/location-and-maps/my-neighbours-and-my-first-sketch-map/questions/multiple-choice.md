# My Neighbours and My First Sketch Map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Neighbours are —**
a) people who live far away  b) people who live in the houses near ours  c) people in another country
**Answer: b) people who live in the houses near ours** — Neighbours live in the houses near ours.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A sketch map is —**
a) a song about a place  b) a quickly drawn picture-plan of a place  c) a list of names only
**Answer: b) a quickly drawn picture-plan of a place** — A sketch map is a quickly drawn picture-plan.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do you draw first on your neighbourhood sketch map?**
a) The school far away  b) Your own home  c) A river in another land
**Answer: b) Your own home** — Start from your own home, then add neighbours, then the school.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which side words belong on a sketch map talk?**
a) Sweet and sour  b) Right, left, front, back  c) Hot and cold
**Answer: b) Right, left, front, back** — Sketch maps are talked about with side and direction words.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why draw a sketch map?**
a) To show where things are  b) To hide where things are  c) To colour only
**Answer: a) To show where things are** — A sketch map shows where things are.
