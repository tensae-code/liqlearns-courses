# My Neighbours and My First Sketch Map — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) You can say on which side of your home a neighbour lives.**
**Answer: True** — Pupils name neighbours and the side each lives on.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) On a sketch map, a neighbour's house must be drawn on the side where it really is.**
**Answer: True** — The drawing shows real positions — that is its purpose.
