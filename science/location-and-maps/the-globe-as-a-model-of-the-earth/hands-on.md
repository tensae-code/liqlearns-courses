# Hands-on — The globe as a model of the Earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — How to use a globe. The books teach this part as a globe is a model of the Earth; naming models of other objects and making a model yourself — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources. Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
