# Lesson plan — The globe as a model of the Earth

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Location and Maps · **Lesson:** The globe as a model of the Earth
**Place in the curriculum:** Location and Maps — appended to Unit 2 (Location and Maps) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The books teach this part as a globe is a model of the Earth; naming models of other objects and making a model yourself — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **How to use a globe** — The books teach this part as a globe is a model of the Earth; naming models of other objects and making a model yourself — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.
2. **Earth is a planet** — The books teach this part as the globe is a reduced model of the Earth; old pictures of a flat Earth (on a turtle) contrasted with the Sun and its circling planets; some planets, like Earth, have natural satellites — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.

## What the learner can do when it ends

2. Explain sub lesson 2 in their own words: Earth is a planet.
