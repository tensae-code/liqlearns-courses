# The globe as a model of the Earth — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about how to use a globe?
**Answer: The books teach this part as a globe is a model of the Earth; naming models of other objects and making a model yourself — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about earth is a planet?
**Answer: The books teach this part as the globe is a reduced model of the Earth; old pictures of a flat Earth (on a turtle) contrasted with the Sun and its circling planets; some planets, like Earth, have natural satellites — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
