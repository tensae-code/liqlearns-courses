# Absolute Location — A Place's Exact Position — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Absolute location is a place's —**
a) nickname  b) exact, fixed position  c) colour on a map
**Answer: b) exact, fixed position** — Absolute location is fixed and exact.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) An absolute location is stated in —**
a) longitude first, then latitude  b) latitude first, then longitude  c) names only
**Answer: b) latitude first, then longitude** — Latitude first, then longitude.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The letters N and E in an absolute location mean —**
a) names of towns  b) north and east  c) new and empty
**Answer: b) north and east** — N = north latitude, E = east longitude.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool helps read degrees off a map's edge?**
a) A ruler  b) A spoon  c) A rope only
**Answer: a) A ruler** — A ruler at the map's edges measures the position.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Relative or absolute: 'between 11°20′ and 12°30′ north' is —**
a) relative  b) absolute  c) a story
**Answer: b) absolute** — Degrees give the absolute location.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Lake Tana's latitude range is —**
a) 8°40′–14°50′ N  b) 11°20′–12°17′ N  c) 37°–37°36′ E
**Answer: b) 11°20′–12°17′ N** — Latitude is read first, from the map's side scale.

**7. (Sub lesson 3) In an absolute location, we state —**
a) longitude first, then latitude  b) latitude first, then longitude  c) only the name
**Answer: b) latitude first, then longitude** — The order is latitude, then longitude.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) The Amhara Region's longitude range is —**
a) 35°10′–40°20′ E  b) 8°40′–14°50′ E  c) 11°20′–12°17′ E
**Answer: a) 35°10′–40°20′ E** — The region runs from 35°10′ to 40°20′ east.

**9. (Sub lesson 4) The Amhara Region's latitude range is —**
a) 35°10′–40°20′ N  b) 8°40′–14°50′ N  c) 0°–90° N
**Answer: b) 8°40′–14°50′ N** — From 8°40′ north to 14°50′ north.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) Ethiopia's southern extreme, at 3° N, is —**
a) the Ogaden  b) Moyale  c) the Akobo river
**Answer: b) Moyale** — Moyale, in the south, sits at 3° N.

**11. (Sub lesson 5) Ethiopia spans how many degrees east–west?**
a) 12 degrees  b) 15 degrees  c) 48 degrees
**Answer: b) 15 degrees** — From 33° E to 48° E is a span of 15 degrees.

**12. (Sub lesson 6) One effect of Ethiopia's location the book states plainly is —**
a) it has many seaports  b) it has no seaport of its own  c) it has two time zones
**Answer: b) it has no seaport of its own** — Ethiopia is landlocked — it has no seaport of its own.

**13. (Sub lesson 6) Ethiopia's position feeds politics over which river's water?**
a) the Awash  b) the Nile  c) the Baro
**Answer: b) the Nile** — As the Nile's great source, Ethiopia's position feeds Nile-water politics downstream.

**14. (Sub lesson 7) East Africa's eastern extreme, at 57° E, is —**
a) Mauritius  b) Ras Kasar  c) the Maputo river
**Answer: a) Mauritius** — Mauritius, the island country, is the region's eastern corner.

**15. (Sub lesson 7) The region's northern corner is at —**
a) Liuwa National Park  b) Ras Kasar, Eritrea  c) Maputo
**Answer: b) Ras Kasar, Eritrea** — Ras Kasar in Eritrea marks 18° N, the north of the region.
