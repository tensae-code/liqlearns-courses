# Absolute Location — A Place's Exact Position — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Absolute location is a place's —**
a) nickname  b) exact, fixed position  c) colour on a map
**Answer: b) exact, fixed position** — Absolute location is fixed and exact.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) An absolute location is stated in —**
a) longitude first, then latitude  b) latitude first, then longitude  c) names only
**Answer: b) latitude first, then longitude** — Latitude first, then longitude.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The letters N and E in an absolute location mean —**
a) names of towns  b) north and east  c) new and empty
**Answer: b) north and east** — N = north latitude, E = east longitude.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool helps read degrees off a map's edge?**
a) A ruler  b) A spoon  c) A rope only
**Answer: a) A ruler** — A ruler at the map's edges measures the position.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Relative or absolute: 'between 11°20′ and 12°30′ north' is —**
a) relative  b) absolute  c) a story
**Answer: b) absolute** — Degrees give the absolute location.
