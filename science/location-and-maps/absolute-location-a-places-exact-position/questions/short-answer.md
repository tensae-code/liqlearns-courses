# Absolute Location — A Place's Exact Position — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Write South Gondar's absolute location.**
**Answer:** Between 11°20′–12°30′ N and 37°30′–38°40′ E.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Say the difference between relative and absolute location in one sentence.**
**Answer:** Relative location tells where a place is by what is around it; absolute location gives its exact fixed position in latitude and longitude.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Write Lake Tana's absolute location.**
**Answer:** Between 11°20′–12°17′ N latitude and 37°–37°36′45″ E longitude.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Write the Amhara Region's absolute location.**
**Answer:** Between 8°40′–14°50′ N latitude and 35°10′–40°20′ E longitude.
