# Absolute Location — A Place's Exact Position — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Absolute location does not change when a neighbouring building changes.**
**Answer: True** — It is independent of what is around the place.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) South Gondar's example uses degrees and minutes, north and east.**
**Answer: True** — 11°20′–12°30′ N and 37°30′–38°40′ E.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A whole region can be fixed by one box of latitude and longitude numbers.**
**Answer: True** — The Amhara Region is fixed exactly that way.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Every town inside the Amhara Region lies inside its coordinate box.**
**Answer: True** — The box holds the entire region.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) Ethiopia has two time zones.**
**Answer: False** — The whole country keeps one time zone, GMT+3.

**6. (Sub lesson 6) Ethiopia's location has no effect on its climate.**
**Answer: False** — The book lists climate among the things its position shapes.

**7. (Sub lesson 7) East Africa lies wholly north of the equator.**
**Answer: False** — It spans 18° N to 26° S — it crosses the equator.
