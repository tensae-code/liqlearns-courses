# Hands-on — Absolute Location — A Place's Exact Position

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ruler on the map edge
In pairs, lay a ruler along a map's degree edge and read off a marked place's position, latitude first.

## Two ways treasure hunt
Describe one classroom object two ways: relatively ('beside the window') and by a class grid square — feel the difference.

## Fix a lake, fix a region
On a gridded map of a lake or a region, read the top, bottom, left and right edge numbers and write the place's absolute location in the full form: latitude range first, then longitude.

## Frame your country
On a blank frame, mark Ethiopia's four extremes and label each with its place name. Then add the region's bigger frame around it in another colour.
