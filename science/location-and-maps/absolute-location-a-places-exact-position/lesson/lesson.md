# Absolute Location — A Place's Exact Position

## Do it first

You can say 'the school is beside the market' — but markets move. Can you give a position that never depends on what is around? That is today's idea. Read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — What absolute location is (teacher guide — evidence)

Absolute location (ፍፁማዊ መገኛ, fṣumawi megeña) is a place's exact, fixed location — it does not depend on the things around it. A market can move; a place's absolute location stays.

[Picture 12011 — a map edge with degree and minute markings and a ruler]

## Sub lesson 2 — Stating it: latitude first, then longitude

An absolute location is stated in latitude first, then longitude, in degrees and minutes, read off the map's edges with a ruler. A worked example: South Gondar lies between 11°20′–12°30′ N and 37°30′–38°40′ E. Pupils draw their own zone map and give its absolute location the same way.

## Sub lesson 3 — Worked example — Lake Tana's absolute location

Now use the grid on a real place, step by step. Lake Tana lies between 11°20′ and 12°17′ north latitude, and between 37° and 37°36′45″ east longitude. Read the lake's top and bottom edges on the latitude scale, its left and right edges on the longitude scale — and the lake is fixed, exactly, for anyone on earth.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Amhara Region's absolute location

The same fixing, widened to a whole region: the Amhara Region lies between 8°40′ and 14°50′ north latitude, and between 35°10′ and 40°20′ east longitude. One box of numbers holds the entire region — every town, farm and school inside it.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: absolute location (ፍፁማዊ መገኛ) — a place's exact position, stated in latitude then longitude, in degrees and minutes. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. On a map with degree edges, practise reading a place's latitude and longitude with a ruler.
2. Write your zone's (or woreda's) absolute location if your teacher gives you its map.
3. Explain to a friend why an absolute location never changes when a shop moves.
