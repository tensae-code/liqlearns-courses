# Lesson plan — Absolute Location — A Place's Exact Position

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Location and Maps · **Lesson:** Absolute Location — A Place's Exact Position
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** absolute location (ፍፁማዊ መገኛ) — a place's exact position, stated in latitude then longitude, in degrees and minutes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on both line families — latitude and longitude together give the exact position.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What absolute location is (teacher guide — evidence)** — absolute location (ፍፁማዊ መገኛ, fṣumawi megeña) is a place's exact, fixed location — it does not depend on the things around it.
2. **Stating it: latitude first, then longitude** — an absolute location is stated in latitude first, then longitude, in degrees and minutes, read off the map's edges with a ruler.

3. **Worked example — Lake Tana's absolute location** — Lake Tana's coordinates found step by step from the grid (Grade 4 student pp.10–12).
4. **The Amhara Region's absolute location** — the whole region fixed by one coordinate box (Grade 4 student pp.10–12).

5. **Ethiopia's absolute location** — Ethiopia lies between 3° and 15° N and 33° and 48° E; extremes at Moyale, the Tigray tip, the Ogaden and the Akobo river; spans 12° by 15°; tropics; GMT+3 (Grade 5).

6. **What Ethiopia's location causes** — effects of position: climate, culture and economy shaped; one time zone; geopolitical weight — and two negatives stated plainly: no seaport of its own, and Nile-water politics (Grade 5).

7. **East Africa's extent — the region's four corners** — East Africa spans 18° N–26° S and 22°–57° E; corners at Ras Kasar, the Maputo river, Mauritius and Liuwa National Park (Grade 6).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
