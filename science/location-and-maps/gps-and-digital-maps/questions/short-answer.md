# GPS and Digital Maps — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things GPS gives.**
**Answer:** Any three of: position, speed, time, altitude, area.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Say one job each tool does better.**
**Answer:** Google Earth: exploring the whole Earth from above. Google Maps: finding a place and its route for a real journey (wording may vary).

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Name GPS's five uses from memory.**
**Answer:** Find a location; navigate; track movement; make maps; keep precise time.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) What would you ask at a land-administration office about GPS?**
**Answer:** How GPS is used there to fix and record land positions (the chapter's reporting task; wording may vary).

**5. (Sub lesson 5) Write two questions you would ask an ICT expert about GPS.**
**Answer:** Questions about how GPS is used locally, its accuracy, or its uses in the town (learner's own, on the chapter's theme).
