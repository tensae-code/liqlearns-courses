# GPS and Digital Maps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) GPS stands for —**
a) Global Positioning System  b) General Paper Scale  c) Great Place Search
**Answer: a) Global Positioning System** — The Global Positioning System — positioning from satellites.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which is a form of GPS?**
a) a compass rose  b) a survey unit  c) a sketch map
**Answer: b) a survey unit** — Handheld, vehicle, survey and phone units are its forms.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Google Earth is mainly for —**
a) exploring the whole Earth  b) sending messages  c) printing books
**Answer: a) exploring the whole Earth** — Google Earth is the exploring tool, built on satellite images.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Both digital tools are built on —**
a) hand drawings  b) satellite images  c) old paintings
**Answer: b) satellite images** — Satellite images underlie both Google Earth and Google Maps.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Which is one of GPS's five printed uses?**
a) growing crops  b) tracking movement  c) cooking food
**Answer: b) tracking movement** — Find a location, navigate, track movement, make maps, keep precise time.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) GPS works —**
a) only in fine weather  b) in any weather, at any time  c) only at noon
**Answer: b) in any weather, at any time** — Any weather, any time — that is the system's strength.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) For a daily route with traffic and travel time, the tool is —**
a) Google Earth only  b) Google Maps  c) a globe
**Answer: b) Google Maps** — Google Maps on the phone gives routes, traffic and travel time.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) The chapter sends pupils to find which town on Google Maps?**
a) Bahir Dar  b) Mekelle  c) Hawassa
**Answer: a) Bahir Dar** — Find Bahir Dar on Google Maps — the chapter's own activity.

**9. (Sub lesson 5) Land-administration experts are interviewed about —**
a) cooking  b) location and boundaries  c) music
**Answer: b) location and boundaries** — Location and boundaries are the land office's working knowledge.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) After the interviews, pupils —**
a) keep silent  b) report back to the class  c) throw away their notes
**Answer: b) report back to the class** — Reporting back to the class is the closing step.
