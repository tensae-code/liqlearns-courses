# GPS and Digital Maps — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) GPS works only inside buildings.**
**Answer: False** — GPS fixes position from satellites — it is used outdoors by walkers, drivers and surveyors alike.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Google Maps cannot show a place from above.**
**Answer: False** — It is built on satellite images and shows places from above.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Only scientists may use GPS.**
**Answer: False** — Surveyors, explorers, pilots, captains, miners — and anyone with a phone or watch — use it.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) Google Earth is used only on paper maps.**
**Answer: False** — Google Earth is a program for exploring the whole Earth on a computer.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) Expert interviews are a distraction from learning location.**
**Answer: False** — They are the chapter's closing activity — location as a working skill.
