# Hands-on — Directions — Right, Left and the Four Directions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sunrise facing
With your class at morning time, face the sunrise together, spread your arms, and name the four directions out loud.

## Turn and tell
One child turns slowly while the others call 'front!', 'back!', 'right side!', 'left side!' — the turning child says what they face at each call.

## Alemnesh's day
Draw a small map with your home in the middle. Mark the eight directions around it. Now trace one day of your own movements — to school, to the market, home again — and label each move with its direction.

## Count the sixteen
Draw a compass rose. Mark the four main points, then build the three sub-directions between each pair, and count all sixteen aloud with a partner.
