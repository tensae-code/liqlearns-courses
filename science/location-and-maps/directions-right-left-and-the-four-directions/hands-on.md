# Hands-on — Directions — Right, Left and the Four Directions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sunrise facing
With your class at morning time, face the sunrise together, spread your arms, and name the four directions out loud.

## Turn and tell
One child turns slowly while the others call 'front!', 'back!', 'right side!', 'left side!' — the turning child says what they face at each call.
