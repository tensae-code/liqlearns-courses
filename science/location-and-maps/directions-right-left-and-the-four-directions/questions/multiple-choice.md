# Directions — Right, Left and the Four Directions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) When you face your teacher, your right hand is on —**
a) the side of your right arm  b) your back  c) your head
**Answer: a) the side of your right arm** — Right and left are found on your own body first.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the sun rise?**
a) In the west  b) In the east  c) In the north
**Answer: b) In the east** — The sun rises in the east.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) How many main directions are there?**
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four
**Answer: c) Four** — East, west, north and south — four main directions.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) If the sun is rising in front of you, you are facing —**
a) west  b) east  c) down
**Answer: b) east** — You face the sunrise in the east.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which pair are opposites?**
a) East and north  b) East and west  c) North and west
**Answer: b) East and west** — East and west are opposite directions.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) Your left hand, when you face the sunrise, points —**
a) along the sun's path, opposite your right hand  b) to the ground  c) behind you only
**Answer: a) along the sun's path, opposite your right hand** — With arms spread facing the sunrise, right and left point along the sun's path.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) The direction between north and east is —**
a) south-west  b) north-east  c) west
**Answer: b) north-east** — The sub-directions sit between the main ones.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) How many directions are there in all, with the sub-directions?**
a) four  b) six  c) eight
**Answer: c) eight** — Four main directions plus four sub-directions make eight.

**9. (Sub lesson 5) How many directions does the compass give?**
a) four  b) eight  c) sixteen
**Answer: c) sixteen** — Four main points plus twelve sub-directions built between them.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) Between north and east, the middle sub-direction is —**
a) north-east  b) east-north-east  c) north-north-east
**Answer: a) north-east** — North-east stands exactly between north and east; the other two lean toward north and toward east.

**11. (Sub lesson 6) When a compass needle stops swinging, it points —**
a) south  b) north  c) wherever you face
**Answer: b) north** — The needle settles pointing north; all other directions are counted from there.

**12. (Sub lesson 6) Why does the needle swing before it settles?**
a) it is broken  b) it is finding north  c) it is measuring distance
**Answer: b) it is finding north** — The swing is the needle finding its line; the settle is north found.
