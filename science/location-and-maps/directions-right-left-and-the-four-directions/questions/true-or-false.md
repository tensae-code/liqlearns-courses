# Directions — Right, Left and the Four Directions — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Things behind you are at your back.**
**Answer: True** — Front is what you face; back is behind you.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The sun sets in the east.**
**Answer: False** — The sun sets in the west; it rises in the east.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Standing facing the sunrise, the sunset is behind you.**
**Answer: True** — West, where the sun sets, lies opposite east.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) South-west lies between south and west.**
**Answer: True** — Each sub-direction sits between two main directions.
