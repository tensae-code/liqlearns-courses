# Lesson plan — Directions — Right, Left and the Four Directions

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Location and Maps · **Lesson:** Directions — Right, Left and the Four Directions
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the directions — right and left, front and back on your own body, and east, west, north and south found from the sun.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own body, and the sunrise and sunset they see every day.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Right and left, front and back, on your own body** — first we learn directions on our own bodies: this is my right hand, this is my left hand; this is in front of me, this is behind my back.
2. **Finding east and west from the sun (teacher guide — evidence)** — the four directions are found from the sun.
3. **The four main directions, and places beside the road** — facing the sunrise with arms spread, we name all four: east where the sun rises, west where it sets, and north and south to the sides.

4. **The eight directions** — the four sub-directions (NE, NW, SE, SW) added to the four main ones, traced through Alemnesh's day of movements from her house (Grade 4 student pp.1–3).

5. **Sixteen directions — the full compass** — direction is the line along which something moves or faces; the compass gives sixteen directions — the four main points plus twelve sub-directions built between them (Grade 5).

6. **The needle settles north** — a compass needle swings, then settles pointing north — the settling motion is what a compass does (Grade 5, GIF need).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- The words are new → go back to the lesson's picture and say what it shows, in home words first, then in the lesson's words.
- The parts blur together → take one part at a time and answer that part's questions before moving on; the gate is per part where sub lessons exist.
- The learner cannot do the Do It Yourself → do the first item together, then let them finish the rest alone.
