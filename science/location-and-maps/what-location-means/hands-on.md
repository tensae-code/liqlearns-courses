# Hands-on — What Location Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Where is it? game
One child hides a small object in the room. The others ask 'where' questions until someone says its location exactly.

## Picture places
Look at a picture of a house, a church and a mosque. For each one, say what stands near it.

## Match the map to the place
Draw your classroom as a map on paper — desk, door, windows — then stand at the door and check: does every thing on your map sit where the real thing sits?
