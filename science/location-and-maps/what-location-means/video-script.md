# Video script — What Location Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where is the book? The book is on the desk.

Where is your home? It is near the school.

Every thing is somewhere. That 'where' is its location.

In Amharic: መገኛ — megeña.

Now you: where is your bag? Say what is near it.
