# What Location Means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does location mean?**
a) The colour of a thing  b) Where a thing is found  c) The size of a thing
**Answer: b) Where a thing is found** — Location is where a thing is found.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How do we say where a place is?**
a) By its colour  b) By what is near it  c) By its price
**Answer: b) By what is near it** — We say where a place is by pointing to what is near it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Which of these can we locate at school?**
a) Only clouds  b) The school's service rooms  c) Nothing
**Answer: b) The school's service rooms** — The unit's competencies include locating the school's service rooms.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A thing's location tells us —**
a) what it is made of  b) where it is  c) how heavy it is
**Answer: b) where it is** — Location is about where, not what or how heavy.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which question asks about location?**
a) What colour is it?  b) Where is the book?  c) How old is it?
**Answer: b) Where is the book?** — 'Where' asks about location.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) To say where your home is, you can name —**
a) places near it  b) nothing at all  c) only its colour
**Answer: a) places near it** — We locate a place by what is near it.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) A map is —**
a) a song about a place  b) a tool that shows where places sit, drawn small  c) a kind of house
**Answer: b) a tool that shows where places sit, drawn small** — A map shows location on paper.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) Where a place sits is described —**
a) against other bodies and places  b) by its colour  c) by guessing
**Answer: a) against other bodies and places** — Location is always told against what is around.
