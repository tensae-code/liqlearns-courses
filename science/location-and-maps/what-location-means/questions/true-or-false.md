# What Location Means — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) We can say where things are inside our classroom.**
**Answer: True** — The lesson starts with things inside the house, compound and classroom.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Fasil Ghebbi's location is told by the town of Gondar.**
**Answer: True** — The book's own example names Gondar as where Fasil Ghebbi stands.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Our home and our school each have a location we can describe.**
**Answer: True** — We locate school, home and neighbours relative to each other.
