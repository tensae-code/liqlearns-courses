# What Location Means — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Say where your school bag is right now, using something near it.**
**Answer:** Example: my bag is under the desk / beside the door (any true answer naming a nearby thing).

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the Amharic word for location?**
**Answer:** መገኛ (megeña).

**3. (Sub lesson 4) Explain in one sentence what a map does.**
**Answer:** A map draws where places sit, small on paper, so we can see a whole place and find where things are.

**4. (Sub lesson 5) Say one thing a map can tell you that a globe shows less easily for a journey.**
**Answer:** A map can show one area large and flat with its roads, distances and directions laid out for the journey (any answer naming location, size, direction or distance for a chosen area).

**5. (Sub lesson 6) Give the guide's four marks of a map.**
**Answer:** Drawn from above on flat paper; simplified in content; reduced in size; drawn to scale.
