# Lesson plan — What Location Means

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Location and Maps · **Lesson:** What Location Means
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what location (መገኛ) means — every thing and every place is somewhere, and we say where by pointing to what is near it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own home, classroom and school compound — the places they already know.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Where things are, inside and around us** — location (መገኛ, megeña) means where a thing is found.
2. **Places we know, in pictures** — pictures of familiar places teach the word: a house, a church and a mosque — every one of them is somewhere, and we say where by pointing to what is near it.
3. **Locating the school's rooms (teacher guide — evidence)** — at school we also locate the school's own rooms and the places that serve us, and we learn to say where our school, our home and our neighbours are, each one compared with the others.

4. **What a map is** — location described against other places, and the map as the tool that shows it (Grade 4 student pp.3–5; SNNP TG p.17 and Oromia TG p.20, teacher guide — evidence).

5. **Map and globe — two ways to show the Earth** — a map is the Earth reduced and drawn flat as seen from above; a globe is the Earth's true model; maps serve travellers, pilots, captains, planners (Grade 6).

6. **What a map is (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 5 guide's definition: a map shows places from above on flat paper — simplified, reduced, drawn to scale (ልኬት); it shows location, direction, area and distance (teacher guide — evidence).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- The words are new → go back to the lesson's picture and say what it shows, in home words first, then in the lesson's words.
- The parts blur together → take one part at a time and answer that part's questions before moving on; the gate is per part where sub lessons exist.
- The learner cannot do the Do It Yourself → do the first item together, then let them finish the rest alone.
