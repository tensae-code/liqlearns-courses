# Where My Home Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) To say where your home is, you can compare it with —**
a) the school  b) the moon  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the school** — We place our home relative to the school and the woreda.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A woreda is made of —**
a) one house only  b) a group of kebeles  c) only rivers
**Answer: b) a group of kebeles** — A woreda is a group of kebeles (taught fully in Grade 2).

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence tells where a home is?**
a) My home is blue  b) My home is beside the school  c) My home is old
**Answer: b) My home is beside the school** — Only the second says where, by comparing with the school.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) If your home is on the sunrise side of the school, it lies to the —**
a) east of the school  b) west of the school  c) under the school
**Answer: a) east of the school** — The sunrise side is the east.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why do we name neighbouring kebeles?**
a) To sing about them  b) They help us say where our home is  c) No reason
**Answer: b) They help us say where our home is** — Known neighbours anchor our home's location.
