# Where My Home Is — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Your home lies on some side or direction from the school.**
**Answer: True** — Pupils say on which side/direction their home lies from the school.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Naming the kebeles near us helps us say where our home is.**
**Answer: True** — We learn the neighbouring kebeles by name.
