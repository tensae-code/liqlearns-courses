# Where My Home Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Say where your home is, compared with your school.**
**Answer:** Any true sentence, e.g. 'My home is east of the school, a short walk away.'

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name one kebele near yours.**
**Answer:** The child's own neighbouring kebele, by its real name.
