# Hands-on — Neighbours — and Measuring a Bearing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Take a bearing
With a protractor, a map and a ruler, take the bearing of three places from your school or town, following the five steps exactly. Check each other's readings in pairs.

## The border market
Visit or picture a market near a border. List what crosses in each direction, and which of the four neighbour-gifts — trade, resources, security, kinship — each crossing shows.
