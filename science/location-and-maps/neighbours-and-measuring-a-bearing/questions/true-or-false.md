# Neighbours — and Measuring a Bearing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Kinship — families on both sides of a border — is one of the gifts of neighbours.**
**Answer: True** — The benefits table names kinship.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A bearing can be read without aligning the protractor's zero with north.**
**Answer: False** — Zero must align with north before the line is read.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Distance alone, with no direction, guides a traveller exactly.**
**Answer: False** — Both distance and direction are needed.
