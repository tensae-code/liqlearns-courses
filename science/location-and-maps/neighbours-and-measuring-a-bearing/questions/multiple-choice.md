# Neighbours — and Measuring a Bearing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which is one of the things neighbours give each other?**
a) trade  b) storms  c) silence
**Answer: a) trade** — Trade heads the benefits table.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Shared rivers between neighbours are an example of —**
a) kinship  b) shared resources  c) security
**Answer: b) shared resources** — Resources used together across a border.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) To locate a neighbouring town exactly, we give its —**
a) colour and size  b) distance and direction  c) age
**Answer: b) distance and direction** — How far, and which way (teacher guide — evidence).

**4. (Sub lesson 3) A bearing is measured —**
a) in degrees from north, with a protractor  b) in kilograms  c) by guessing
**Answer: a) in degrees from north, with a protractor** — The protractor on the map gives the degrees.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Seqota lies at what bearing from Bahir Dar?**
a) 180°  b) 45°  c) 90°
**Answer: b) 45°** — Forty-five degrees — north-east.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Debre Markos lies which way from Bahir Dar?**
a) south-east  b) north-west  c) west
**Answer: a) south-east** — The second worked result of the five-step method.
