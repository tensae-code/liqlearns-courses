# Neighbours — and Measuring a Bearing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four benefits neighbours give each other.**
**Answer:** Trade, shared resources, security and kinship.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Write the five steps of measuring a bearing.**
**Answer:** Centre the protractor on the starting place; align zero with north; draw the line to the target; read the degrees where the line crosses the scale; say the bearing.
