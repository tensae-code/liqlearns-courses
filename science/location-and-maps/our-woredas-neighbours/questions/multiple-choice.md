# Our Woreda's Neighbours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A neighbouring woreda is one that —**
a) is very far away  b) borders our own woreda  c) is under the sea
**Answer: b) borders our own woreda** — Neighbours border — they touch our woreda's edge.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) To describe our woreda fully, we name neighbours on —**
a) one side only  b) every side  c) no sides
**Answer: b) every side** — Neighbours on each side complete the description.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Household neighbours and woreda neighbours are alike because both —**
a) are rivers  b) live or lie right beside us  c) are very far
**Answer: b) live or lie right beside us** — Both kinds of neighbour are right beside us.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which word names the side a neighbour lies on?**
a) East  b) Sweet  c) Loud
**Answer: a) East** — Sides are named with direction words.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) If Woreda B touches our woreda's eastern edge, B is —**
a) our neighbour to the east  b) not a neighbour  c) our capital
**Answer: a) our neighbour to the east** — A bordering woreda on the east is our eastern neighbour.
