# Our Woreda's Neighbours — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) We learn each neighbouring woreda's name and the side it lies on.**
**Answer: True** — Listing bordering woredas and their sides is the lesson's work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A woreda can have several neighbouring woredas.**
**Answer: True** — A woreda is ringed by neighbours on its different sides.
