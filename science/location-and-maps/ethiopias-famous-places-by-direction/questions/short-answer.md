# Ethiopia's Famous Places by Direction — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Place three famous places by direction from Addis Ababa.**
**Answer:** Any three placed truly, e.g. Adwa north, Gambela west, Karamara east (the learner places from the lesson's list).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the guide ask pupils to draw the places, not only name them?**
**Answer:** A drawn map proves the placing: the place must sit on its true side of the country, in its true direction from the capital.
