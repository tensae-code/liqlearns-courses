# Ethiopia's Famous Places by Direction — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Harar Jugol lies west of Addis Ababa.**
**Answer: False** — Harar Jugol is in the east; Gambela and the Baro river are the western placements.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Saying a direction aloud is the whole of the guide's map-work.**
**Answer: False** — Pupils also make their own maps to point the places out.
