# Ethiopia's Famous Places by Direction — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) From Addis Ababa, Erta Ale lies —**
a) south  b) north-east  c) west
**Answer: b) north-east** — Erta Ale is placed to the north-east of the capital.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which famous heritage site is placed with Gondar?**
a) Fasil Ghebbi  b) Tiya stelae  c) Abba Jifar palace
**Answer: a) Fasil Ghebbi** — Fasil Ghebbi is Gondar's famous place.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The guide's map-work pins which places?**
a) only the capital  b) Lalibela, Aksum, Gondar and Harar  c) only lakes
**Answer: b) Lalibela, Aksum, Gondar and Harar** — Those four famous places are the guide's named pins.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) After pinning the places, pupils —**
a) close the book  b) make their own maps pointing the places out  c) memorise a list
**Answer: b) make their own maps pointing the places out** — Making different maps to point out the famous places is the guide's second step.
