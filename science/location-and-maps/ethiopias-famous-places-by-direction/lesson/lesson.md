# Ethiopia's Famous Places by Direction

## Do it first

Stand at the centre of the room and call it Addis Ababa. Point north, east, south, west. A partner names a famous place — Aksum, Harar, Gambela. Point the way it lies from the capital. Try it first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Famous places from Addis Ababa

Every famous place can be placed from the capital. The heritage sites first: the **Aksum obelisks**, **Fasil Ghebbi** in Gondar, **Harar Jugol**, the **Tiya stelae** and the **Abba Jifar palace**. Then the natural sites, placed by direction from Addis Ababa: **Adwa to the north**, **Gambela and the Baro river to the west**, **Chebera Churchura to the south**, **Erta Ale to the north-east**, and **Karamara to the east**. Direction is the address: from the centre, each place has its side.

## Sub lesson 2 — Famous places on the map (teacher guide — evidence)

The Grade 5 teacher guide works the same ground as map-work (teacher guide — evidence): pupils pin **Lalibela, Aksum, Gondar and Harar** on the map of Ethiopia, then **make different maps of their own** to point the famous places out — a blank map of the country, filled in step by step. Placing is not finished when you can say the direction; it is finished when your own map shows it.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: placing Ethiopia's famous places by their direction from Addis Ababa — the heritage sites and the natural sites alike. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name the place. Point its way from Addis Ababa. Draw it on your own Ethiopia map.
2. Pin Aksum, Gondar, Lalibela and Harar on a blank map of Ethiopia, in their true places.
