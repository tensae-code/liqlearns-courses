# Lesson plan — Kebele and Woreda — Reading a Sketch Map

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Location and Maps · **Lesson:** Kebele and Woreda — Reading a Sketch Map
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how villages group into a kebele and kebeles into a woreda, and how to read — and draw — a woreda sketch map.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the directions and sketch-map work of the earlier location lessons.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Villages make a kebele, kebeles make a woreda** — a kebele is a group of villages together.
2. **Reading a woreda sketch map** — a sketch map of a woreda shows its kebeles and its main places.
3. **Drawing your own woreda** — now draw: your own woreda as a rough sketch map, your kebele marked inside it, and the things that border it on each side written or drawn at the edges.

4. **Drawing a sketch map — the eight steps** — the Grade 5 book's eight printed steps for a sketch map of one's own area, then a worked example judged against them: pupil Zerihun's sketch map of the Amhara Region.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
