# Hands-on — Kebele and Woreda — Reading a Sketch Map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Zoom-out drawing
Draw three boxes inside each other: a village, its kebele around it, its woreda around that. Say the names as you draw.

## Reading the real map
With the teacher, find your own kebele on a printed woreda map and point to what borders it.

## Judge a sketch map
Exchange sketch maps with a partner. Judge theirs against the eight steps: which steps did they follow, which did they miss? Write one kind, exact sentence for each miss.
