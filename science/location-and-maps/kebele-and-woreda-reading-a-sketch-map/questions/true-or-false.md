# Kebele and Woreda — Reading a Sketch Map — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A kebele is a group of villages.**
**Answer: True** — Villages → kebele → woreda, each level a group of the one below.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) We state what borders our kebele on each side.**
**Answer: True** — Pupils say what borders their kebele/woreda on each side.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) A sketch map must be a rough drawing, not a photograph.**
**Answer: True** — Pupils draw their own woreda sketch map — rough and clear is enough.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) A sketch map can be drawn well without ever looking at the real area.**
**Answer: False** — The steps begin with walking and looking, and end with checking the drawing against the ground.
