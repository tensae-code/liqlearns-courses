# Kebele and Woreda — Reading a Sketch Map — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a kebele? What is a woreda?**
**Answer:** A kebele is a group of villages; a woreda is a group of kebeles.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name one thing that borders your kebele.**
**Answer:** Any real bordering kebele, river or road, by name.

**3. (Sub lesson 4) Name any four of the eight steps of drawing a sketch map.**
**Answer:** Any four of: choose the area; walk it and look; fix north; draw the big shapes first; add roads and paths; add the main places; add names and a key; check against the ground.
