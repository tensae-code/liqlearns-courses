# Kebele and Woreda — Reading a Sketch Map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A woreda is a group of —**
a) villages only  b) kebeles  c) rivers
**Answer: b) kebeles** — Kebeles group together to make a woreda.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) On a woreda sketch map we can see —**
a) only the sky  b) its kebeles and main places  c) nothing real
**Answer: b) its kebeles and main places** — The map shows kebeles and the woreda's main places.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) The first thing to mark on your woreda sketch map is —**
a) another country  b) your own kebele  c) the sea
**Answer: b) your own kebele** — Mark your own kebele first, then what borders it.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which is the right order, small to big?**
a) Woreda, kebele, village  b) Village, kebele, woreda  c) Kebele, village, woreda
**Answer: b) Village, kebele, woreda** — Village → kebele → woreda.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What borders a kebele can be —**
a) another kebele, a river, a road  b) only clouds  c) nothing
**Answer: a) another kebele, a river, a road** — Real things border a kebele.

**6. (Sub lesson 1) Why do we read sketch maps?**
a) To know where places are and what borders them  b) To sleep  c) To hide places
**Answer: a) To know where places are and what borders them** — Maps tell us where places are.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) What is judged against the eight steps in the book's worked example?**
a) a photograph  b) Zerihun's sketch map of the Amhara Region  c) a globe
**Answer: b) Zerihun's sketch map of the Amhara Region** — Zerihun's sketch map is the worked example critiqued against the steps.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) Why fix north early when drawing a sketch map?**
a) to make it colourful  b) so every place is drawn in its true direction  c) to fill the page
**Answer: b) so every place is drawn in its true direction** — With north fixed at the top, directions on the sketch stay true.
