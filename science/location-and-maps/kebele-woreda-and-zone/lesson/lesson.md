# Kebele, Woreda and Zone

## Do it first

Village, kebele, woreda — what comes next? If many woredas join together, what are they called? Guess first, then read.

## Sub lesson 1 — The zone — one step above the woreda

A zone (ዞን, zon) is a group of neighbouring woredas, the administration level above the woreda. The whole ladder now reads: kebele → woreda → zone.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why location matters

Knowing where places are is not a game. It helps us move about, know the natural and human-made features around us, meet our basic needs, and live well with our neighbours. That is why every grade widens the map a little more.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: the next step up: a zone is a group of neighbouring woredas — and why knowing a place's location matters. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw the ladder: your village inside its kebele, inside its woreda, inside its zone, each named.
2. Name one natural and one human-made feature of your zone.
3. Ask at home: what is our zone called, and which woredas are in it?
