# Kebele, Woreda and Zone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A zone is a group of —**
a) kebeles directly  b) neighbouring woredas  c) houses
**Answer: b) neighbouring woredas** — Woredas group into a zone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Knowing location helps us —**
a) move about and know what is around us  b) forget our neighbours  c) stay lost
**Answer: a) move about and know what is around us** — Location knowledge serves travel, needs and neighbours.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which ladder is right?**
a) Zone, woreda, kebele  b) Kebele, woreda, zone  c) Woreda, zone, house
**Answer: b) Kebele, woreda, zone** — Small to big: kebele, woreda, zone.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Your zone contains —**
a) only your house  b) many woredas, each with many kebeles  c) one village
**Answer: b) many woredas, each with many kebeles** — A zone holds many woredas.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A natural feature is —**
a) a school  b) a river  c) a road
**Answer: b) a river** — Rivers are natural; schools and roads are human-made.
