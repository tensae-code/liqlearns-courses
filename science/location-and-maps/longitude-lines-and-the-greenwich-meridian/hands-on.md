# Hands-on — Longitude Lines and the Greenwich Meridian

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Orange segments
Peel an orange in segments or draw its lines: the segment edges show lines meeting at the two ends, like longitude.

## Which family?
The teacher draws a line on the globe; pupils call 'latitude!' or 'longitude!' and say why.

## Orange-segment meridians
Peel an orange in segments from top to bottom: each peel edge is a meridian. Count how the edges meet at the two ends, then find the 'zero' edge and count east and west from it.
