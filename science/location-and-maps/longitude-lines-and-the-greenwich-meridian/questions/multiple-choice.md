# Longitude Lines and the Greenwich Meridian — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Longitude lines run —**
a) east–west only  b) pole to pole  c) around the equator only
**Answer: b) pole to pole** — Longitude lines join the poles.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The 0° longitude line is called —**
a) the equator  b) the Greenwich meridian  c) the tropic
**Answer: b) the Greenwich meridian** — Longitude is measured from the Greenwich meridian, 0°.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which family of lines meets at the poles?**
a) Latitude  b) Longitude  c) Neither
**Answer: b) Longitude** — Longitude lines meet at the poles.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A place east of Greenwich has its longitude counted —**
a) east from 0°  b) south from 0°  c) from the equator
**Answer: a) east from 0°** — Longitude counts east or west from Greenwich.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Latitude lines compared with longitude lines: latitude lines —**
a) meet at poles  b) never meet and keep equal length  c) are shorter at the middle
**Answer: b) never meet and keep equal length** — Latitude lines are parallel and equal in length.
