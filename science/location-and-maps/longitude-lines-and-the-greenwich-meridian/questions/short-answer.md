# Longitude Lines and the Greenwich Meridian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Greenwich meridian?**
**Answer:** The 0° longitude line from which longitude is measured east and west.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Say one difference between latitude and longitude lines.**
**Answer:** Latitude lines run east–west and never meet; longitude lines run pole to pole and meet at the poles.
