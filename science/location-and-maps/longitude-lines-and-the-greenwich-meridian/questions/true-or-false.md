# Longitude Lines and the Greenwich Meridian — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) All longitude lines meet at the poles.**
**Answer: True** — They join at the North and South Poles.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Longitude measures distance east or west, latitude north or south.**
**Answer: True** — The two families measure the two directions.
