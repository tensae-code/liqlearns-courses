# The National Grid and Grid References — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why are the vertical lines called eastings?**
**Answer:** Because their numbers grow toward the east as you read across the grid.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A partner gives you the reference 1845. Walk through what you do.**
**Answer:** Read easting 18 along the bottom, then northing 45 up the side; the square where the two meet is the place's square.
