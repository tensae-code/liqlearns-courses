# The National Grid and Grid References — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The vertical lines of the National Grid are called —**
a) northings  b) eastings  c) meridians
**Answer: b) eastings** — Vertical lines are eastings — their numbers grow eastward.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The grid's origin sits where the equator meets —**
a) 0°  b) 34°30′ E  c) 48° E
**Answer: b) 34°30′ E** — The origin is the equator crossing 34°30′ east longitude.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In the reference 1845, the easting is —**
a) 45  b) 18  c) 1845
**Answer: b) 18** — Eastings are read first: 18, then the northing 45.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A 6-figure reference does more than a 4-figure one because it —**
a) uses bigger paper  b) pinpoints a place inside the square  c) works without a grid
**Answer: b) pinpoints a place inside the square** — Six figures split the square further and fix the exact spot.
