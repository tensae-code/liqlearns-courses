# Map Scale, Key and Symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The key of a map is also called its —**
a) መፍቻ (legend)  b) title  c) border
**Answer: a) መፍቻ (legend)** — መፍቻ — the key or legend explains the map's colours and signs.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is NOT one of the four?**
a) scale  b) north arrow  c) the mapmaker's photograph
**Answer: c) the mapmaker's photograph** — Scale, north arrow, key and year are the four.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) 700 km drawn as 10 cm gives a scale of 1 cm to —**
a) 7 km  b) 70 km  c) 700 km
**Answer: b) 70 km** — 700 ÷ 10 = 70 km for each centimetre.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A larger-scale map shows —**
a) less detail  b) more detail  c) no places
**Answer: b) more detail** — The larger the scale, the more detail the map can carry.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) On the map's colour code, green stands for —**
a) water  b) parks, forests and orchards  c) deserts
**Answer: b) parks, forests and orchards** — Green is the colour of parks, forests and orchards.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Deep blue on a map means —**
a) deep water  b) a desert  c) a main road
**Answer: a) deep water** — Blue is water; the deeper the blue, the deeper the water.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) International borders are drawn in —**
a) black  b) red  c) green
**Answer: b) red** — International borders are red; internal borders are black.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) A summer-only road and an all-season road —**
a) have the same symbol  b) have different symbols
**Answer: b) have different symbols** — The symbol table distinguishes the two road kinds.

**9. (Sub lesson 5) A map published in 2011 shows —**
a) today's world exactly  b) the world of 2011  c) no real world
**Answer: b) the world of 2011** — Its picture belongs to its own year.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) Why does the publication year matter?**
a) it changes the colours  b) things change — the map describes its own time  c) it does not matter
**Answer: b) things change — the map describes its own time** — Roads, towns and conditions change; the year fixes which world is shown.
