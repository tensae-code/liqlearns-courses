# Hands-on — Latitude Lines and the Equator

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## String on a globe
Lay string around a globe at the equator, then lay parallel strings above and below it — feel that they never meet.

## North or south?
The teacher names places on a map; pupils call 'north of the equator!' or 'south!' for each.

## Draw the whole grid
On a ball or a drawn circle-earth, draw the equator, three latitude lines, the prime meridian and three longitude lines. Label 0° twice — once for each starting line.

## Walk the parallels
Chalk a globe's parallels on the ground: equator in the middle, lines toward each 'pole'. Stand on a line, call out its latitude, and step the 111 km of one degree with big strides.
