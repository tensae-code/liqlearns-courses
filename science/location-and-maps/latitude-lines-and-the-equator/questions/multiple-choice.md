# Latitude Lines and the Equator — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Latitude lines run —**
a) pole to pole  b) east–west, parallel to the equator  c) only over Ethiopia
**Answer: b) east–west, parallel to the equator** — Latitude lines circle east–west, parallel to the equator.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The equator is the latitude line of —**
a) 90 degrees  b) 0 degrees  c) 10 degrees
**Answer: b) 0 degrees** — The equator is the 0° starting line.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A place far north of the equator has —**
a) a big latitude number  b) latitude zero  c) no latitude
**Answer: a) a big latitude number** — Distance from the equator grows the number.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which lines are parallel and never meet?**
a) Longitude lines  b) Latitude lines  c) Roads
**Answer: b) Latitude lines** — Latitude lines are parallel.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Degrees in location are written with the sign —**
a) °  b) %  c) #
**Answer: a) °** — Degrees use the ° sign.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Latitude lines run from the equator at 0° to —**
a) 90° at the poles  b) 360°  c) 10°
**Answer: a) 90° at the poles** — Latitude is measured from equator to pole.

**7. (Sub lesson 3) Longitude is measured from —**
a) the equator  b) the prime meridian  c) Lake Tana
**Answer: b) the prime meridian** — Longitude's starting line is the prime meridian.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) The equator's latitude is —**
a) 90°  b) 0°  c) 180°
**Answer: b) 0°** — The equator is the starting line, 0°.

**9. (Sub lesson 4) One degree of latitude covers about how much ground?**
a) 11 km  b) 111 km  c) 1,111 km
**Answer: b) 111 km** — Each degree spans about 111 km on the ground.

**10. (Sub lesson 5) A position is stated —**
a) longitude first  b) latitude first, then longitude  c) in kilometres
**Answer: b) latitude first, then longitude** — The fixed order is latitude first, then longitude.

**11. (Sub lesson 5) The equator crosses which of these?**
a) Ethiopia  b) Kenya  c) Tanzania
**Answer: b) Kenya** — The equator crosses Uganda, Kenya and Somalia.
