# Latitude Lines and the Equator — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) What is the equator's latitude?**
**Answer:** 0 degrees (0°).

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How do latitude lines run, and do they meet?**
**Answer:** They run east–west, parallel to the equator, and they never meet.
