# Latitude Lines and the Equator — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Latitude lines never meet each other.**
**Answer: True** — They are parallel, so they never meet.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Latitude measures how far north or south of the equator a place is.**
**Answer: True** — That is exactly what latitude shows.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) One latitude number and one longitude number together can give a place's absolute location.**
**Answer: True** — The two numbers together fix the place.

**4. (Sub lesson 4) There are 360° of latitude in all.**
**Answer: False** — There are 180°: 90° north of the equator and 90° south.

**5. (Sub lesson 5) The prime meridian passes through Ethiopia.**
**Answer: False** — It crosses Algeria, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Mali and Togo; no East African country lies on it.
