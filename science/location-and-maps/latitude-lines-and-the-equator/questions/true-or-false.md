# Latitude Lines and the Equator — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Latitude lines never meet each other.**
**Answer: True** — They are parallel, so they never meet.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Latitude measures how far north or south of the equator a place is.**
**Answer: True** — That is exactly what latitude shows.
