# Video script — Latitude Lines and the Equator

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

See the line around the earth's middle. That is the equator — zero degrees.

More lines run beside it, east and west, and they never meet.

These are latitude lines. They tell how far north or south a place is.
