# Latitude Lines and the Equator

## Do it first

Imagine lines drawn around the whole earth, like the lines around a ball. What would they be for? Look at a globe if you can, then read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lines that run east–west and never meet

Latitude lines (የኬክሮስ ዲግሪ ልኬት) are imaginary lines drawn east–west around the earth, parallel to the equator. Being parallel, they never meet each other, however far they run.

## Sub lesson 2 — The equator, the starting line

The equator is the great 0° line around the earth's middle. Latitude tells how far north or south of the equator a place is: a place's latitude is its distance from that starting line, in degrees.

## Sub lesson 3 — Latitude and longitude together — the Grade 4 grid

Grade 4 draws the whole grid. Latitude lines (ኬክሮስ, kekros) run from the equator at 0° to 90° at the poles. Longitude lines (ኬንትሮስ, kentros) run from the prime meridian, all the way around the earth — 360° of them. Together, one latitude number and one longitude number give any place's absolute location. ## Sub lesson 4 — Latitude — the parallels, the Grade 6 measure

Grade 6 measures the parallels exactly. Latitude lines are imaginary lines running east–west around the Earth. The **equator is 0°** and the **poles are 90°**, so there are **180° of latitude in all**. On the ground, each degree of latitude spans about **111 km**. And latitude divides the Earth into two halves: the **northern hemisphere** above the equator and the **southern hemisphere** below it.

[Picture 13304 — a globe with the equator and parallels drawn and the 0°–90° scale marked]

## Sub lesson 5 — Giving a position — latitude first, then longitude

A place's position is stated in a fixed order: **latitude first, then longitude**, both in degrees. Two great lines anchor the reading. The **equator crosses Uganda, Kenya and Somalia** — three countries of our own region. The **prime meridian** crosses **Algeria, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Mali and Togo** — far to the west; no East African country lies on it. So a position like a region's extent is always read the same way round: how far north or south, then how far east or west.

## Say it to your companion

Explain this lesson's one thing to your companion in your own words: latitude lines (የኬክሮስ ዲግሪ) — the imaginary lines drawn east–west around the earth, and the equator, the 0° line they are measured from. Then answer one question they ask you about it.

## Do It Yourself

1. On a ball or an orange, draw an equator line and two latitude lines around it.
2. Find Ethiopia on a globe: is it north or south of the equator?
3. Write the latitude idea in one sentence for a younger child.
4. State a position latitude-first and name the countries the equator crosses.
