# Hands-on — The Amhara Region — Where It Sits and Its Zones

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Label the region
On a blank outline of the region, write each neighbour's name on its true side, then dot three zone capitals and name them.

## Fix your own zone
Find your zone in the zones table. Say its capital, and — if a gridded map is at hand — its coordinates, in the full latitude-then-longitude form.
