# Lesson plan — The Amhara Region — Where It Sits and Its Zones

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Location and Maps · **Lesson:** The Amhara Region — Where It Sits and Its Zones
**Place in the curriculum:** the Science course's Ethiopia Environmental Science stock, primary levels (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Amhara Region fixed every way this course has learned — by its neighbours, by its coordinates, and zone by zone with capitals — plus its famous and important places (teacher guide — evidence).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the relative- and absolute-location lessons — Grade 4 widens the map from the zone to the whole region.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The region's relative location** — Amhara's six neighbours read off the region map (Grade 4 student pp.10–12).
2. **The region's zones and their capitals** — the 13 zones plus Bahir Dar city administration, each with capital and coordinates (Grade 4 student pp.12–14).
3. **Our region's location in the teacher guides (teacher guide — evidence)** — each guide fixes its own region both ways (SNNP TG pp.23–27; Oromia TG pp.18, 27–30, teacher guide — evidence).
4. **Famous and important places of our region (teacher guide — evidence)** — historical and natural heritage places, zones, special woredas and institutions (SNNP TG pp.27–36; Oromia TG pp.30–32, teacher guide — evidence).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title in the Science course, in order. Recommended, never forced — a learner who lands here directly can start here.
- Nothing else is required; every term used is taught inside this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and use what the part "The region's relative location" teaches, in their own words.
2. Say and use what the part "The region's zones and their capitals" teaches, in their own words.
3. Say and use what the part "Our region's location in the teacher guides (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches, in their own words.
4. Say and use what the part "Famous and important places of our region (teacher guide — evidence)" teaches, in their own words.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's own world (home, school, neighbourhood) supplies the rest.

## If the learner is stuck
- The words are new → go back to the lesson's picture and say what it shows, in home words first, then in the lesson's words.
- The parts blur together → take one part at a time and answer that part's questions before moving on; the gate is per part where sub lessons exist.
- The learner cannot do the Do It Yourself → do the first item together, then let them finish the rest alone.
