Six neighbours hold the Amhara Region: Eritrea north, Sudan west, Oromia south, Tigray north-east, Benishangul-Gumuz south-west, Afar east.

Thirteen zones inside it — and Bahir Dar, at eleven degrees fifty-seven north, thirty-seven degrees thirty-six east.
