# The Amhara Region — Where It Sits and Its Zones — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name Amhara's six neighbours with their sides.**
**Answer:** Eritrea — north; Sudan — west; Oromia — south and south-east; Tigray — north-east; Benishangul-Gumuz — south-west; Afar — east.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What does each row of the zones table give?**
**Answer:** A zone (or the Bahir Dar city administration), its capital, and its coordinates.
