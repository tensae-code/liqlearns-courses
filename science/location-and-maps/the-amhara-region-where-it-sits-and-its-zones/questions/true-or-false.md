# The Amhara Region — Where It Sits and Its Zones — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Eritrea lies to the north of the Amhara Region.**
**Answer: True** — Eritrea is the northern neighbour.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The Bahir Dar city administration is counted in the zones table.**
**Answer: True** — It is the fourteenth row.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Only the Amhara Region can be given an absolute location.**
**Answer: False** — Every region can be fixed both ways — the guides do it for their own regions.
