# The Amhara Region — Where It Sits and Its Zones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which neighbour lies to the WEST of the Amhara Region?**
a) Eritrea  b) Sudan  c) Afar
**Answer: b) Sudan** — Sudan borders Amhara on the west.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Afar lies to the — of Amhara.**
a) east  b) north  c) south-west
**Answer: a) east** — Afar is Amhara's eastern neighbour.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) How many rows does the zones table have?**
a) 5  b) 14 — 13 zones plus Bahir Dar city administration  c) 30
**Answer: b) 14 — 13 zones plus Bahir Dar city administration** — Thirteen zones and the city administration.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Bahir Dar's coordinates are —**
a) 11°57′ N, 37°36′ E  b) 8°40′ N, 35°10′ E  c) 0°, 0°
**Answer: a) 11°57′ N, 37°36′ E** — The table's worked example.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) A region's 'astronomical' location means its —**
a) weather  b) absolute location in latitude and longitude  c) population
**Answer: b) absolute location in latitude and longitude** — Astronomical location is the fixed, numbered location.

**6. (Sub lesson 4) Which of these belongs among a region's famous and important places?**
a) historical heritage places  b) last week's market prices  c) homework
**Answer: a) historical heritage places** — Heritage places, natural places, zones and institutions (teacher guide — evidence).
