# Making sound with code — a programmable tone generator — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) On the tone generator, the frequency number you set decides the tone's…
a) Pitch — small number, low tone; large number, high tone  b) Loudness  c) Length  d) Instrument
**Answer: a) Pitch — small number, low tone; large number, high tone**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Frequency means…
a) How loud a sound is  b) How many vibrations per second  c) How long a tone lasts  d) Which speaker plays it
**Answer: b) How many vibrations per second**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Amplitude governs…
a) Pitch  b) The tone's colour  c) Loudness — bigger amplitude, louder tone  d) The loop's speed
**Answer: c) Loudness — bigger amplitude, louder tone**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the matching task, your tone has the right pitch but is too loud. You should…
a) Lower the frequency  b) Lower the amplitude, leaving the frequency untouched  c) Restart the program  d) Add a loop
**Answer: b) Lower the amplitude, leaving the frequency untouched**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Loudness is measured and compared in…
a) Hertz  b) Metres  c) Seconds  d) Decibels
**Answer: d) Decibels**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A program loop full of set tones is…
a) A composed piece of music — each note's frequency and length chosen by the programmer  b) Random noise  c) A recording of the teacher  d) A way to save battery
**Answer: a) A composed piece of music — each note's frequency and length chosen by the programmer**
