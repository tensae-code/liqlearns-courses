# Making sound with code — a programmable tone generator — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The number of vibrations per second you set on the generator is its _____.
**Answer: frequency**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A large frequency number gives a _____ pitch.
**Answer: high**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Loudness is measured in _____.
**Answer: decibels**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Tones repeated by a program _____ become a composed piece of music.
**Answer: loop**
