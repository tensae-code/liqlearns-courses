Today: **Making sound with code** — a tone you order by number.

A tone generator takes one great input: frequency — how many vibrations per second. Type a small number, hear a low tone. Type a big number, hear a high one. Pitch is a number you set.
Loudness is the second number — how big the vibration is, read in decibels. Match a tone your teacher plays by hunting its two numbers, and you have measured a sound with your ears.
Then stack the tones in a loop and the board plays your composition. That is this sound lesson: the wave concepts learned by making the waves.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
