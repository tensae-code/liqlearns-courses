# Making sound with code — a programmable tone generator — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner sets frequencies and predicts their pitches before playing, states what amplitude governs and how loudness is read (decibels), reproduces a played tone by its numbers, and performs a short looped composition while explaining each number's job — answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has three sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — ordering tones by number alone, matching played tones under time pressure, and composing loops with rests and repeats until setting sound by number is the learner's habit, not a trick shown once.
