# Hands-on — Making sound with code — a programmable tone generator

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three numbers, three tones
Open the micro:bit tone generator (device or MakeCode simulator). Play tones at three frequencies you choose — write each number down before you play it, predict low/middle/high, then play and check your ordering. Say aloud: frequency is the pitch's number.

## The matching game
Work in pairs. One partner plays a secret tone at a chosen frequency and loudness. The other hunts: adjust frequency until the pitch matches, then loudness until the volume matches. Swap roles. Record both winning pairs of numbers — you have measured two sounds by ear.

## Compose the loop
Program a loop of at least four tones — choose each frequency and its length on purpose (a tune you know, or a pattern: low, high, high, low). Play it for your partner. Say aloud what every number in your program does.
