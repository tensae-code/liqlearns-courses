# Sound — media and speed: solid beats liquid beats gas — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A louder shout travels faster than a soft one through the same air.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound travels out in all directions from its source.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sound travels slower in a gas than in a liquid.
**Answer: True**
