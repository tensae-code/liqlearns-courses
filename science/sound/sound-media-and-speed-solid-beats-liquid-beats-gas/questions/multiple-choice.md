# Sound — media and speed: solid beats liquid beats gas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound's speed depends on —
a) its pitch  b) its loudness  c) the medium it travels through
**Answer: c) the medium it travels through** — Group 1's reason wins the argument.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Two simultaneous shouts, one higher-pitched, arrive —
a) high pitch first  b) low pitch first  c) together
**Answer: c) together**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound can travel through —
a) a vacuum only  b) air, liquids and solids — never a vacuum  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) air, liquids and solids — never a vacuum** — no matter, no vibration to carry.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sound travels fastest in solids because their particles —
a) are close-packed and hand the vibration on by collision  b) are heavier  c) are warmer
**Answer: a) are close-packed and hand the vibration on by collision**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The correct speed order is —
a) air > water > steel  b) steel > water > air  c) water > steel > air
**Answer: b) steel > water > air**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the funnel test, which ear hears a tap first tells you —
a) how loud the tap was  b) the path the sound took to reach you  c) the tap's pitch
**Answer: b) the path the sound took to reach you**
