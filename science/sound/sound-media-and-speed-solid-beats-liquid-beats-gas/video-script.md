# Video script — Sound — media and speed: solid beats liquid beats gas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script in the super-simple voice; the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so this is the standard short script.

---

Two shouts. Same moment. Which arrives first?
Not the high one. Not the loud one. Together!
Speed depends on the stuff it travels through.
Solid particles are packed close — they pass the shaking fast.
Steel beats water. Water beats air. And in empty space — no sound at all.
