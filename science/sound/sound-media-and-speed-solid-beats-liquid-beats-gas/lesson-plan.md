# Lesson plan — Sound — media and speed: solid beats liquid beats gas

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Sound · **Lesson:** Sound — media and speed: solid beats liquid beats gas
**Place in the curriculum:** Sound — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sound travels out in all directions through matter — air, liquids and solids alike — and never through a vacuum; a four-groups argument about two simultaneous shouts settles that speed depends on the medium, not on pitch or loudness, and the particle account fixes the order: close-packed solids hand the vibration on fastest, liquids slower, gases slowest
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Sound — vibration, volume and pitch: what sound is and its two qualities; now how fast it moves, and through what.

## If the learner is stuck
- Backs ‘higher pitch arrives first’ → pitch is how fast the SOURCE vibrates, not how fast the vibration travels; the medium sets travel speed.
- Cannot order the speeds → think particles: close-packed hands it on fastest (solid), scattered slowest (gas).
