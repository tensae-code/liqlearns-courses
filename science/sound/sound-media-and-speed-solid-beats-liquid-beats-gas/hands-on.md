# Hands-on — Sound — media and speed: solid beats liquid beats gas

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Funnel-and-tube listening (Activity 7.11)
One pupil listens at the tube while taps are made left, centre and right at the funnel end. Record which ear hears first each time and explain the results from travel paths.

## Through-solid race
Ear to a long table or metal rail: a tap at the far end is heard through the solid and through the air. Compare the two arrivals and state the order the lesson predicts.
