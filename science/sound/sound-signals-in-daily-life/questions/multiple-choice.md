# Sound signals in daily life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A signal is…
a) Any loud sound  b) A sound or light with an agreed meaning  c) A song  d) Speech only
**Answer: b) A sound or light with an agreed meaning**

**2.** The naqqareh drums at the shrine of Imam Reza announced…
a) Lunch  b) Sunrise and sunset  c) A football match  d) Rain
**Answer: b) Sunrise and sunset**

**3.** A signal works because…
a) It is very loud  b) Sender and receiver agreed its meaning beforehand  c) It uses words  d) Everyone has drums
**Answer: b) Sender and receiver agreed its meaning beforehand**

**4.** A ship's captain can be guided by…
a) Only written letters  b) Light and sound signals  c) Smells  d) Flags alone underwater
**Answer: b) Light and sound signals**
