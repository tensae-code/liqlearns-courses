# Sound signals in daily life — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Give two signals from daily life and the meaning each carries.
**Answer: Example: the school bell — lessons begin/end; a referee's whistle — play stops; a siren — make way, danger near.**

**2.** Why is the drumbeat at sunrise not just music?
**Answer: Because its job is to carry a message — it announces the hour to everyone in earshot; the pilgrims act on its agreed meaning.**
