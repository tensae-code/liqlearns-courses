Today: **Sound signals in daily life** — Iran's way.

Iran's Grade 2 book tells it as a historical note inside its sound lesson: at the shrine of Imam Reza, the naqqareh — great kettle drums — were sounded to announce sunrise and sunset to the pilgrims. The same book turns the class into a ship's crew: imagine you are a ship's captain, guiding and being guided by signals — a light seen, a sound heard, each with an agreed meaning.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
