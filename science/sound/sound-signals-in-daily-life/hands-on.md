# Hands-on — Sound signals in daily life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Signals round the room
In a group, agree three signals: one clap = stand, two claps = sit, a whistle (or a raised hand's flash of light) = freeze. One person sends, the rest answer. Then change one meaning without telling a newcomer — and watch what a signal is worth without the agreement.

## Captain of the ship
Blindfold one player — the ship. The captain guides them across the room with sound signals only: one tap of a spoon for "forward", two for "stop", three for "turn". Swap roles, and say which signal you would add for "danger".
