# Hands-on — Sound — vibration, volume and pitch

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Rubber-band pitch steps (Activity 7.9)
Bands over a box between two pencils; vibrating lengths 15–3 cm; prediction written before every tap; results ordered. The group's rule must be stated from its own results: shorter length, higher pitch.

## Feel the vibration
Fingertips lightly on a ringing bell, a playing radio and a humming throat. For each: where does the vibration start, and what carries it to your fingers or ears?
