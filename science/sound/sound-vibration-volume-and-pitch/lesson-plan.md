# Lesson plan — Sound — vibration, volume and pitch

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Sound · **Lesson:** Sound — vibration, volume and pitch
**Place in the curriculum:** Sound — a NEW title on the Science ladder (CURRICULUM.md). Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every sound is a vibrating object setting the surrounding medium vibrating in turn — felt at a ringing bell or a playing radio — weakening as it travels because it spends its energy on the way; and its two independent qualities are separated: volume (loudness) and pitch (fast vibration high, slow vibration low), made experimental with rubber bands stepped from 15 cm down to 3 cm
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the primary Materials lesson Sound and vibration: sound felt as buzzing there; now its two qualities are separated and measured. It opens the Sound title.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes loudness with pitch (‘louder = higher’) → they are independent: the band test changes pitch by length while tapping force changes volume.
- Thinks fading sound has slowed down → it weakens because its energy is spent on the way, not because its speed falls.
