# Video script — Sound — vibration, volume and pitch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script in the super-simple voice; the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so this is the standard short script.

---

Touch the bell. It shakes. That shaking is sound beginning.
Sound is vibration. The air vibrates too, and carries it.
Loud or soft — that is volume.
High or low — that is pitch. Fast vibration: high. Slow vibration: low.
Short band: high sound. Long band: low sound. Test it!
