# Sound — vibration, volume and pitch — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound is energy that produces hearing.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Loudness and pitch are the same quality.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The kirar sounds high against the begena's low.
**Answer: True**
