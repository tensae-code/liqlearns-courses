# Sound — vibration, volume and pitch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every sound begins with —
a) a vibrating object  b) a loudspeaker  c) silence
**Answer: a) a vibrating object** — the vibration sets the medium vibrating in turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** You can feel sound's vibration by —
a) looking at the source  b) touching a ringing bell or a playing radio  c) holding your breath
**Answer: b) touching a ringing bell or a playing radio**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound weakens as it travels because —
a) it slows down  b) it spends its energy on the way  c) air blocks it
**Answer: b) it spends its energy on the way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Volume is about —
a) high and low  b) loudness and softness  c) speed
**Answer: b) loudness and softness** — a whisper against a talking voice.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Fast vibration gives —
a) low pitch  b) high pitch  c) no pitch
**Answer: b) high pitch** — slow vibration, low pitch.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the band experiment, shortening the vibrating length —
a) lowers the pitch  b) raises the pitch  c) only makes it louder
**Answer: b) raises the pitch** — 15 cm down to 3 cm: shorter, higher.
