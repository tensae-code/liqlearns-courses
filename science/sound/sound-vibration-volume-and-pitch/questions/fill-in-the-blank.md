# Sound — vibration, volume and pitch — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every sound is a ______ object setting the medium vibrating.
**Answer: vibrating**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Slow vibration gives ______ pitch.
**Answer: low**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Shorter band length, ______ pitch.
**Answer: higher**
