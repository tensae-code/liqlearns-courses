# Musical sounds, instruments and room acoustics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What sets a musical tone's pitch?
**Answer: a) Its fundamental frequency  b) Its loudness  c) Its overtones only  d) The room**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Timbre — the colour telling a dutar from a trumpet — comes from…
**Answer: a) the strengths of the overtones  b) the fundamental alone  c) the player's hands  d) the size of the hall**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The doira belongs to the family of…
**Answer: a) membranes — a struck skin vibrates  b) strings  c) air columns  d) bells**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Shortening a vibrating string makes its pitch…
**Answer: a) higher  b) lower  c) unchanged  d) quieter only**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Reverberation is…
**Answer: a) sound persisting as reflections pile up in a room  b) an echo from mountains  c) the instrument's timbre  d) silence after music**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Which surface absorbs sound most strongly?
**Answer: a) Carpet  b) Tile  c) Glass  d) Bare plaster**
