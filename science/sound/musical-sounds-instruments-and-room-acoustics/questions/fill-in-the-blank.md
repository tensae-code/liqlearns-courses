# Musical sounds, instruments and room acoustics — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Whole-number multiples of the fundamental are its _____.
**Answer: overtones**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The tanbur and dutar are _____ instruments — strings vibrate.
**Answer: string**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In an air-column instrument, the _____ itself vibrates.
**Answer: air**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A good concert hall rings for about _____ seconds.
**Answer: two**
