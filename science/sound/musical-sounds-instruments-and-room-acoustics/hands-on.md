# Hands-on — Musical sounds, instruments and room acoustics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two rooms, one clap
Clap sharply once in the most echoing space you can find (stairwell, bathroom) and once in the softest (bedroom with curtains and bedding), timing with a phone how long the sound seems to last. List what each room's surfaces are made of. Write the acoustics rule your two claps prove, then find the middle place — a classroom — and judge it for music and for speech separately.
