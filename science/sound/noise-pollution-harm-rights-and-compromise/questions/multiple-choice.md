# Noise pollution — harm, rights and compromise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Noise pollution is sound that is —
a) any sound at all  b) loud, long-lasting, unpleasant or harmful  c) only music
**Answer: b) loud, long-lasting, unpleasant or harmful**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Most outdoor noise comes from —
a) birds and rivers  b) construction, traffic and aircraft  c) libraries
**Answer: b) construction, traffic and aircraft** — too-loud music counts too.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The real harm of noise pollution includes —
a) tired feet  b) damaged ears and permanent hearing loss  c) sunburn
**Answer: b) damaged ears and permanent hearing loss**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the role-play, the neighbour claims the right —
a) to make sound  b) not to be disturbed  c) to ban all music forever
**Answer: b) not to be disturbed**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A good end to the role-play is —
a) the louder side wins  b) a compromise both sides can state and accept  c) nobody speaks again
**Answer: b) a compromise both sides can state and accept**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The loudspeaker question is settled in the lesson by —
a) the teacher's order  b) a class debate argued from the definition  c) ignoring it
**Answer: b) a class debate argued from the definition**
