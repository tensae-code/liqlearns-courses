# Noise pollution — harm, rights and compromise — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound that is loud, long-lasting, unpleasant or harmful is ______ pollution.
**Answer: noise**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Permanent ______ loss is a harm of noise pollution.
**Answer: hearing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The role-play must be driven to a ______.
**Answer: compromise**
