# Hands-on — Noise pollution — harm, rights and compromise

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Noise inventory walk (Activity 7.14)
Walk the school grounds at break and at lesson time. Log every noise with its source, its hour and your rating against the four definition words. Propose one fix per entry.

## Role-play to compromise (Activity 7.15)
Pairs role-play noise-maker vs neighbour for three minutes, then must present a joint compromise to the class. The class tests it: can BOTH sides state it as fair? If not, it goes back for one more round.
