# Video script — Noise pollution — harm, rights and compromise

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script in the super-simple voice; the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so this is the standard short script.

---

Some sound is pollution. Loud. Long. Unpleasant. Harmful.
Traffic. Building work. Aeroplanes. Music too loud — that counts too.
Noise can damage ears. Hearing can be lost forever.
He has a right to make sound. She has a right to quiet.
So talk. Agree hours. Agree volume. That is the compromise.
