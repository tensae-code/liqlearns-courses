# Lesson plan — Noise pollution — harm, rights and compromise

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Sound · **Lesson:** Noise pollution — harm, rights and compromise
**Place in the curriculum:** Sound — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md). It closes the Sound title.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** noise pollution is sound that is loud, long-lasting, unpleasant or harmful — mostly from construction, traffic and aircraft outdoors, too-loud music included — with real harm (damaged ears, permanent hearing loss), argued out in the book's debate form: loudspeaker bans debated, and a noise-maker's right to make sound role-played against a neighbour's right not to be disturbed, driven to a compromise
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Echoes at work — echolocation and ultrasound: sound put to work; now sound out of place, and what a community does about it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines noise as ‘any loud sound’ → the definition has four tests — loud, long-lasting, unpleasant OR harmful; a brief loud celebration may pass, a moderate all-night drone may fail.
- Role-play ends with one side ‘winning’ → both rights are real; the task is a compromise both can state, not a verdict.
