# Hands-on — Echoes at work — echolocation and ultrasound

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Echo and absorption test
One clap, three targets at the same distance: bare wall, wooden door, hanging blanket. The class rates each return (clear echo / faint / none) and files each surface under reflect or absorb, from results.

## Ear protection discussion (Activity 7.13)
List the loudest workplaces learners know (mills, workshops, construction, loud music work). For each, name the protection and who should provide it; connect back to the ear relay: which station does lasting loud sound harm first?
