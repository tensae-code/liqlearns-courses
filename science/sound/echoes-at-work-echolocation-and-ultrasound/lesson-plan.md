# Lesson plan — Echoes at work — echolocation and ultrasound

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Sound · **Lesson:** Echoes at work — echolocation and ultrasound
**Place in the curriculum:** Sound — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** reflected sound at a boundary is an echo, heard separately only when it returns at least 0.1 s after the original — hard smooth surfaces reflect well, soft ones absorb — and the same reflection is put to work: bats, dolphins and ship sonar read reflections as a picture (echolocation), and ultrasound above 20 kHz images the inside of bodies and objects, in the clinic and in industry
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Hearing — the path from vibration to brain: sound received; now sound returned, and read.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks any reflected sound is heard as an echo → the return must lag at least 0.1 s to be heard separately; sooner returns merge with the original.
- Thinks ultrasound is a kind of light or X-ray → it is SOUND, above 20 kHz — too high for human ears, heard by dogs, dolphins and bats.
