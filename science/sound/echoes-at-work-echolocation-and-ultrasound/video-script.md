# Video script — Echoes at work — echolocation and ultrasound

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script in the super-simple voice; the placement names no full video need for this lesson, so this is the standard short script.

---

Shout at the cliff. Your voice comes back. That is an echo — sound bounced back.
It must come back late enough: at least a tenth of a second later.
Hard walls bounce sound. Soft cloth swallows it.
Bats listen to the bounce to ‘see’. Doctors use sound too high to hear — ultrasound — to picture a baby.
