# Echoes at work — echolocation and ultrasound — fill in the blank

Fill the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reflected sound at a boundary is an ______.
**Answer: echo**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wool and cloth ______ sound rather than reflecting it.
**Answer: absorb**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sound above 20 kHz is called ______.
**Answer: ultrasound**
