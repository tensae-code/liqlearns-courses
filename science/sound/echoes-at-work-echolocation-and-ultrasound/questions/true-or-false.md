# Echoes at work — echolocation and ultrasound — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A furnished room gives a clearer echo than a large empty room.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ultrasound is used to scan organs and in pregnancy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Workers in very loud environments need ear protection.
**Answer: True**
