# Echoes at work — echolocation and ultrasound — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An echo is —
a) a second shout  b) reflected sound at a boundary  c) sound made by walls
**Answer: b) reflected sound at a boundary**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An echo is heard separately only if it returns at least —
a) 1 minute later  b) 0.1 s after the original  c) 1 hour later
**Answer: b) 0.1 s after the original**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which surface reflects sound best?
a) wool blanket  b) cloth curtain  c) hard smooth wall
**Answer: c) hard smooth wall** — soft surfaces absorb sound.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bats and dolphins find their way by —
a) echolocation — reading reflections of their own waves  b) smell alone  c) starlight
**Answer: a) echolocation — reading reflections of their own waves** — ships do the same with sonar.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ultrasound is sound —
a) below 20 Hz  b) above 20 kHz, beyond human hearing  c) at speaking pitch
**Answer: b) above 20 kHz, beyond human hearing** — dogs, dolphins and bats can hear it.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In a medical ultrasound scan, the image is built from —
a) light photographs  b) returning reflections at boundaries inside the body  c) heat
**Answer: b) returning reflections at boundaries inside the body** — part reflects, part transmits at each boundary.
