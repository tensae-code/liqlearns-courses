# Hearing — the path from vibration to brain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The FIRST step of hearing is —
a) signals reach the brain  b) the object vibrates  c) the inner ear vibrates
**Answer: b) the object vibrates**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Electrical signals are created —
a) before the vibrations reach the ear  b) in the inner ear, after it vibrates  c) in the air
**Answer: b) in the inner ear, after it vibrates**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sound is heard — understood — in the —
a) eardrum  b) cochlea  c) brain
**Answer: c) brain** — the last step of the relay.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The outer ear's job is to —
a) make signals  b) catch air-borne sound and funnel it in  c) pump liquid
**Answer: b) catch air-borne sound and funnel it in**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The three tiny bones are in the —
a) outer ear  b) middle ear  c) inner ear
**Answer: b) middle ear** — they vibrate in turn after the eardrum.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The cochlea is —
a) a straight dry tube  b) snail-shaped and filled with liquid  c) one of the three bones
**Answer: b) snail-shaped and filled with liquid** — it turns liquid vibration onto the nerve.
