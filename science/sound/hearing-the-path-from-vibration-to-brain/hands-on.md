# Hands-on — Hearing — the path from vibration to brain

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Card relay (Activity 7.12)
Seven shuffled step-cards sequenced by each group before the anatomy is taught, then re-sequenced after Sub lesson 2; the before/after orders are both kept and the changes explained.

## Funnel ear
A paper cone held to the ear, opening turned towards a soft sound across the room, then away. Compare and connect the cone to the outer ear's funnelling job.
