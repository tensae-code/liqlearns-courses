# Lesson plan — Hearing — the path from vibration to brain

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Sound · **Lesson:** Hearing — the path from vibration to brain
**Place in the curriculum:** Sound — Unit 7, Physical Phenomena, at General Science Grade 8 depth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hearing is a relay in order — object vibrates, air vibrated, vibrations reach the ear, inner ear vibrates, electrical signals created, signals reach the brain, sound is heard — run through the ear's stations: outer ear funnelling, eardrum vibrating, three tiny middle-ear bones in turn, and the snail-shaped liquid-filled cochlea turning vibration onto a nerve for the brain
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Sound — media and speed: the vibration arrives through the medium; now the ear receives it, station by station.
