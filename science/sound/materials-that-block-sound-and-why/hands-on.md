# Hands-on — Materials that block sound — and why

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ear test (a phone or ticking clock, and household materials)
Play one steady sound. Hold different household materials — a cushion, a book, a folded cloth, a sheet of cardboard — between the sound and your ear, one at a time, at the same distance. Rank them by what your ears hear, and say which blocked most and which least.

## Fair-test check (no materials)
Say out loud what must stay the same in the ear test for it to be fair — the sound, its loudness and the distance — and what one thing changes each time. Then say why changing two things at once would spoil the answer.
