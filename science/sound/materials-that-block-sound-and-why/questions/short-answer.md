# Materials that block sound — and why — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Sound travels — until something stops it.
**Answer:** Sound travels from where it is made through the materials around it until it reaches our ears.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Materials that stop sound.
**Answer:** The book tests **materials that block sound**: put a material between the sound and the ear, and the sound that gets through is weaker — or gone.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Tested against our own ears.
**Answer:** The book's own way is the test itself: the **ears are the measuring instrument**.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Sound travels — until something stops it → Materials that stop sound → Tested against our own ears — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
