# Musical instruments as physics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The three families of instruments are named by…
a) Their country of origin  b) What vibrates — strings, air columns or membranes  c) Their size  d) Whether they are played sitting or standing
**Answer: b) What vibrates — strings, air columns or membranes**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A guitarist presses a string at its middle, halving its vibrating length. The note…
a) Halves  b) Stays the same  c) Doubles — it rises an octave  d) Becomes louder only
**Answer: c) Doubles — it rises an octave**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) To double a string's frequency by tension alone, the tension must be…
a) Doubled  b) Halved  c) Quadrupled — multiplied by four  d) Left alone
**Answer: c) Quadrupled — multiplied by four**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A pipe closed at one end sounds, against an open pipe of the same length…
a) The same note  b) An octave lower  c) An octave higher  d) No note at all
**Answer: b) An octave lower**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A trombone player pushes the slide out, lengthening the air column. The note…
a) Rises  b) Falls  c) Grows louder  d) Vanishes
**Answer: b) Falls**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) A drummer strikes the same tuned drum much harder. What changes?
a) The pitch rises  b) The pitch falls  c) The loudness grows; the pitch stays  d) The skin vibrates more slowly
**Answer: c) The loudness grows; the pitch stays**
