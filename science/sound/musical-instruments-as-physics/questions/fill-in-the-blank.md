# Musical instruments as physics — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A plucked string vibrates in a _____ wave — its middle moves most and its ends stay still.
**Answer: standing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) In a flute, the thing that vibrates is the _____ inside the tube.
**Answer: air**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Blowing harder so the column jumps to its next fitting wave is called _____ .
**Answer: overblowing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) A drum's vibrating part is a stretched skin, called a _____ .
**Answer: membrane**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Loudness is set by the vibration's _____ — how far it swings — while pitch is set by its frequency.
**Answer: amplitude**
