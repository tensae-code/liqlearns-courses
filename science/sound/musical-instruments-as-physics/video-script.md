# Video script — Musical instruments as physics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **every musical instrument is physics you can hear**.

Ask one question of any instrument — *what vibrates?* — and you know its family. In a guitar, a **string** vibrates: shorten it, tighten it, or thin it, and the note climbs. In a flute or a trumpet, the **air inside the tube** vibrates: make the tube longer and the note sinks — and a tube closed at one end sings an octave lower than the same tube open. In a drum, a stretched **skin** vibrates: tighter skin, higher note. And in every family the same second rule holds: play harder and you do not change the note — you change the *loudness*, because loudness is how *big* the vibration swings, while pitch is only how *fast* it swings.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
