# Musical instruments as physics — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner names the three instrument families by what vibrates (strings, air columns, membranes) with real examples, works the string rule f = (1/2L)√(T/μ) — halving length or quadrupling tension doubles the note, a heavier string lowers it — explains the open pipe's fundamental (wavelength 2L) against the closed pipe's (wavelength 4L, an octave lower at the same length), tunes a drum by tightness and size, and keeps pitch (frequency) and loudness (amplitude) apart on every instrument, with resonance named as the body's help, answering the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — predicting a note's change before computing it, naming each instrument's vibrator aloud, and reasoning about new instruments (a longer trumpet, a tighter drum) until the teaching is the learner's own.
