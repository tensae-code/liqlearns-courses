# How animals live together — groups, colonies and territory — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about gregarious association?
**Answer: gregarious association: herds of elephants and schools of fish, where each fish keeps a distance equal to its body length, the school fans out when threatened and has no leader; family and colonial relations (corals, jellyfish) complete the intraspecific set (guide evidence)**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about state populations?
**Answer: state populations: bees live under a queen with hundreds of drones and sterile worker females who do all the hive's work (the strongest drone fertilises the queen and is then killed by her); ants divide into workers (smallest and most numerous: defence, food-gathering, nest-enlarging, care of queen and young), soldiers (big heads and strong jaws for crushing hard food) and winged males, in underground anthills begun with earth mounds against flooding; ants benefit humans by eliminating pests and aerating the soil (guide evidence)**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about territoriality reduces competition and prevents the exhaustion of food reserves?
**Answer: territoriality reduces competition and prevents the exhaustion of food reserves; territories are demarcated with olfactory, visual or auditory signals (the rattlesnake's rattle); relations table: familiar (gorillas, humans), state (ants, bees), colonial (jellyfish), territorial (dogs, wolves) (guide evidence)**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: How animals live together — groups, colonies and territory.**
