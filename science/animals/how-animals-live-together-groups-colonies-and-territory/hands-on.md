# Hands-on — How animals live together — groups, colonies and territory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Gregarious association. gregarious association: herds of elephants and schools of fish, where each fish keeps a distance equal to its body length, the school fans out when threatened and has no leader; family and colonial relations (corals, jellyfish) complete the intraspecific set (guide evidence) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
