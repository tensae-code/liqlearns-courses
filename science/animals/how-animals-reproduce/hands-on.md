# Hands-on — How animals reproduce

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Egg or born? Sort the animals
Write down eight animals you know. Sort them into two columns: lays eggs, and born live. Check each one against the lesson's rule — birds, fish, frogs and insects lay eggs; mammals are born live.

## Parents and young
Pick one animal and draw its young beside it. Label what is the same between parent and young, and what will change as the young one grows.
