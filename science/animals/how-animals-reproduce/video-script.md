# Video script — How animals reproduce

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **How animals reproduce** — Cambodia's way, from its Grade 5 book *វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ ថ្នាក់ទី៥ (Science, Grade 5)*.

**Reproduction** is how living things make new living things of their own kind. A hen's chicks are hens, a dog's puppies are dogs — the young belong to the parents' kind. Cambodia's Grade 5 book teaches the reproduction of animals right after the reproduction of plants, so the two can be compared: plants and animals both reproduce, each in its own ways.

Animals do not all bring their young into the world the same way. Many animals — birds, fish, frogs, insects among them — lay **eggs**, and the young hatch from the eggs. Others — the mammals among them — are **born live** from the mother and are fed by her at first. Egg or birth, the end is the same: a new animal of the parents' kind, which grows and can in its turn reproduce.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
