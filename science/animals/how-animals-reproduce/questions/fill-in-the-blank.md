# How animals reproduce — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Making new living things of the same kind is called ______.
**Answer: reproduction**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Birds, fish, frogs and insects lay ______.
**Answer: eggs**

**3.** (Whole lesson) Mammals are born ______ from the mother.
**Answer: live**
