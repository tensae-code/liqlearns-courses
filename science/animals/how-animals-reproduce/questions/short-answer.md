# How animals reproduce — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what reproduction means.
**Answer:** Reproduction is how living things make new living things of their own kind.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name one animal that lays eggs and one that is born live, and say how you know each belongs in its column.
**Answer:** For example: a hen lays eggs and its chicks hatch; a dog is born live from its mother. Any correctly sorted pair from the lesson's rule earns the answer.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Reproduction is how animals make new animals of their own kind → animals reproduce in different ways — many lay eggs and the young hatch, while mammals are born live — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
