# How animals reproduce — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Reproduction is…
a) how living things make new living things of their own kind  b) how animals find food  c) how plants make seeds only  d) how animals sleep
**Answer: a) how living things make new living things of their own kind** — Reproduction makes new living things of the parents' kind — in plants and in animals.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A hen's young are…
a) chicks — hens of the next generation  b) ducks  c) frogs  d) insects
**Answer: a) chicks — hens of the next generation** — The young belong to the parents' kind.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Birds, fish, frogs and insects bring their young into the world by…
a) laying eggs  b) live birth  c) splitting in two  d) seeds
**Answer: a) laying eggs** — These animals lay eggs, and the young hatch from them.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Mammals are…
a) born live from the mother  b) hatched from eggs  c) grown from seeds  d) made from buds
**Answer: a) born live from the mother** — Mammals are born live and are fed by the mother at first.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Cambodia's Grade 5 book teaches animal reproduction straight after…
a) the reproduction of plants  b) the solar system  c) simple machines  d) the weather
**Answer: a) the reproduction of plants** — The chapter order is plants first, then animals — so the two can be compared.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The end of both ways — egg or birth — is…
a) a new animal of the parents' kind  b) a new plant  c) an egg that never hatches  d) nothing
**Answer: a) a new animal of the parents' kind** — Egg or birth, the result is a new animal that grows and can reproduce in its turn.
