# Hands-on — Camouflage and warning colours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the hider
Outside or in pictures, find an animal whose colours match its resting place. Write the animal, the place, and the colours that match.

## Two-ways chart
Draw a two-column chart — Hiding | Warning. Put the book's four examples in their columns (mantis, squirrel; wasp, salamander), then add any animals you know to each side.
