# Video script — Camouflage and warning colours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Camouflage and warning colours** — Bulgaria's way: the hiders and the advertisers in one lesson.

Some animals hide: the mantis, green in the green; the squirrel, changing its coat with the season. Their colours match their place, and hunters pass them by.

Others advertise: the wasp's stripes, the salamander's spots. Bright, striking — a warning every hunter learns. Seen, remembered, avoided.

Now you. Pick one animal from each side and say how its colour protects it. Say it back — in your own words.
