# Camouflage and warning colours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The mantis is protected by…
a) Warning stripes  b) Matching the green around it  c) Its sting  d) Migrating
**Answer: b) Matching the green around it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The wasp's stripes are…
a) Camouflage  b) Warning colours  c) A seasonal coat  d) For feeding
**Answer: b) Warning colours**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The squirrel's protective colouring is its…
a) Signal spots  b) Seasonal coat  c) Stripes  d) Shell
**Answer: b) Seasonal coat**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Warning colours protect by…
a) Hiding the animal  b) Being seen and remembered as a warning  c) Making the animal faster  d) Cooling the animal
**Answer: b) Being seen and remembered as a warning**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The salamander's signal marks are its…
a) Spots  b) Fins  c) Feathers  d) Hooves
**Answer: a) Spots**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Camouflage and warning colours are both kinds of…
a) Feeding  b) Protective colouring  c) Breathing  d) Movement
**Answer: b) Protective colouring**
