# Camouflage and warning colours — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Colouring that matches the surroundings and hides the animal is _____.
**Answer: camouflage**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The wasp advertises with warning _____.
**Answer: stripes (colours)**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The _____ changes its coat with the season.
**Answer: squirrel**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Hiding and warning are both _____ colouring.
**Answer: protective**
