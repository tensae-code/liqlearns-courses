Today: **Animal behaviour — courtship and competition** — Honduras's way.

ethology is defined as the branch of biology studying animal behaviour — the set of responses to stimuli from the surroundings; animals compete for food and for mates. pheromones: chemical substances animals release to attract a mate, perceivable only by the same species; courtship as a ritual of visual, auditory and chemical signals — the male peacock dances and shows his colours and the female selects the best performer. sexual dimorphism: males and females of a species differ in form, colour, size and reproductive organs — male birds grow showy plumage for courtship; females are larger in most insects, spiders, amphibians, reptiles and raptors, while male mammals are usually larger.
