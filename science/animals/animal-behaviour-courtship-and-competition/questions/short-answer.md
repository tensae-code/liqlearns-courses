# Animal behaviour — courtship and competition — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about ethology is defined as the branch of biology studying animal behaviour?
**Answer: ethology is defined as the branch of biology studying animal behaviour — the set of responses to stimuli from the surroundings; animals compete for food and for mates.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about pheromones?
**Answer: pheromones: chemical substances animals release to attract a mate, perceivable only by the same species; courtship as a ritual of visual, auditory and chemical signals — the male peacock dances and shows his colours and the female selects the best performer.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about sexual dimorphism?
**Answer: sexual dimorphism: males and females of a species differ in form, colour, size and reproductive organs — male birds grow showy plumage for courtship; females are larger in most insects, spiders, amphibians, reptiles and raptors, while male mammals are usually larger.**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Animal behaviour — courtship and competition.**
