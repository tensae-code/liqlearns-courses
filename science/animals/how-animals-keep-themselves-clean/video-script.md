Today: **How animals keep themselves clean** — South Sudan's way, from its Ministry of General Education and Instruction Primary Science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

South Sudan's Primary 2 book teaches how animals clean themselves, starting with the dog: dogs **roll and bite their fur**; the guide's answers give the dog's full set — **licking, nibbling, body shaking and rolling on the ground** — done to prevent germs and pests.

The chicken's way is different: chickens give themselves a **dust bath** and shake it off. Each animal has its own cleaning way, fitted to its body — fur is licked and rolled clean; feathers are dusted and shaken clean.
