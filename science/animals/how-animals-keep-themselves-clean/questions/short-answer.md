# How animals keep themselves clean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Dogs — rolling, licking and nibbling their fur.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 2 book teaches how animals clean themselves, starting with the dog: dogs **roll and bite their fur**; the guide's answers give the dog's full set — **licking, nibbling, body shaking and rolling on the ground** — done to prevent germs and pests.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Chickens — the dust bath.
**Answer:** The chicken's way is different: chickens give themselves a **dust bath** and shake it off. Each animal has its own cleaning way, fitted to its body — fur is licked and rolled clean; feathers are dusted and shaken clean.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Dogs — rolling, licking and nibbling their fur → Chickens — the dust bath — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
