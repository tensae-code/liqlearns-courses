# Hands-on — Inside an animal — body systems of a bird

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Outside to inside
First draw a hen from outside and label the external parts, as the book's outside study does. Then draw the same hen again with the inside systems showing, and say what the second drawing adds.

## Systems sorting
Write the jobs — breaks down food, takes in air, carries goods round the body, makes eggs, holds the body up — on slips. Sort the slips under the five systems of the lesson.
