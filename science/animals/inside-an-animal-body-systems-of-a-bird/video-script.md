Today: **Inside an animal — body systems of a bird** — Angola's way: outside first, then inside.

The hen's skeleton is the frame inside. Its digestive system breaks food down; its respiratory system takes in air.

Its circulatory system carries the goods of both round the body — and its reproductive system, the egg-making, continues the kind.
