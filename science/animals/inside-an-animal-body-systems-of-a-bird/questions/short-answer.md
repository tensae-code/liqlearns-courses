# Inside an animal — body systems of a bird — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the inside study begin with the skeleton?
**Answer:** Because the skeleton is the frame the rest hangs on — bones hold the bird up and give the muscles something to pull on, as the outside study began with the covering.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two systems that take things in, and what each takes in.
**Answer:** The digestive system takes in food and breaks it down; the respiratory system takes in air and gives the body oxygen.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the circulatory system serve the other systems?
**Answer:** It carries what they gather — food's goodness from digestion and oxygen from breathing — round the whole body, so every part receives both.
