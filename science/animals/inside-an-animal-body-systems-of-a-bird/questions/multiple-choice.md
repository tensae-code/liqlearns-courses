# Inside an animal — body systems of a bird — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The inside study of the hen begins with…
a) The egg
b) The skeleton — the frame of bones inside
c) The feathers
d) The beak
**Answer: b) The skeleton — the frame of bones inside**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The digestive system's job is to…
a) Take in air
b) Break food down so the body can use it
c) Make eggs
d) Carry blood
**Answer: b) Break food down so the body can use it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The respiratory system takes in…
a) Food
b) Air — giving the body the oxygen of breathing
c) Eggs
d) Feathers
**Answer: b) Air — giving the body the oxygen of breathing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The reproductive system in the hen is the system of…
a) Flying
b) Egg-making — the making of new birds
c) Digestion
d) Seeing
**Answer: b) Egg-making — the making of new birds**
