# Inside an animal — body systems of a bird — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The frame of bones inside the hen is its _____.
**Answer: skeleton**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The _____ system breaks the hen's food down.
**Answer: digestive**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) The heart and blood vessels belong to the _____ system.
**Answer: circulatory**
