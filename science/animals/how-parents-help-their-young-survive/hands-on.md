# Hands-on — How parents help their young survive

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Signal and response log
Watch young animals and their parents (in life, or on a video) for ten minutes. Log every signal you see and the response it brought, in two columns.

## Match the pairs
Write three needs — hunger, cold, danger — and for each, write the signal a young animal might give and the response its parent might make.
