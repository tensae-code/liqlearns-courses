# Video script — How parents help their young survive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **How parents help their young survive** — the United States' way, from its Utah SEEd Standards books (Grade 1).

The young speak first, in the only way they can: **offspring signals** — a baby's **crying**, a chick's **chirping** (Grade 1, 1.2.4, printed p. 93). The signal is not noise; it is the young's survival tool, announcing a need — hunger, cold, danger — to the one who can meet it.

The signal is answered: **parent responses** — **feeding** the hungry young, **comforting** the distressed young, **protecting** the threatened young. Each response meets the need the signal announced, and together the responses are how the helpless young live long enough to fend for themselves. Signal and response are one behaviour in two halves.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
