# Hands-on — Animal senses — how animals sense the world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Whose sense is it? (no materials)
Close your eyes. Have a companion name one of the book's four animals — mole, crocodile, dog, owl. Say its strongest sense from the lesson, the stimulus it answers, and one thing that sense lets it do. Then swap roles and repeat with a different animal.

## Touch like a mole (a scarf and small safe objects)
Blindfolded with a scarf, feel three small safe objects and name each by touch alone. Then say, in the lesson's words, why the mole's touch must be richer than your hand's — and what 'six times more touch receptors' means for what the mole can feel.
