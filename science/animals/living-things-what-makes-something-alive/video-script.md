# Video script — Living things — what makes something alive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: living things share a set of life characteristics together — sensitivity, reproduction, growth, respiration, nutrition, excretion, adaptation, homeostasis and metabolism — while non-living things do not.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
