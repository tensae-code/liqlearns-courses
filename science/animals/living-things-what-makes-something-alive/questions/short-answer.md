# Living things — what makes something alive — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one living and one non-living thing and give a reason for each.
**Answer:** Any living thing (e.g. sheep) with a life characteristic, and any non-living thing (e.g. stone) with its absence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List four characteristics of life.
**Answer:** Any four of: sensitivity/response, reproduction, growth and development, respiration, nutrition, excretion, adaptation, homeostasis, metabolism.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is hair a hard case to judge?
**Answer:** Hair itself does not feed, breathe or reproduce on its own — it must be judged as part of a living body, not alone.
