# Living things — what makes something alive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is living?
a) stone  b) sheep  c) table
**Answer: b) sheep** — it feeds, grows, breathes and reproduces.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is non-living?
a) mushroom  b) eucalyptus  c) soil
**Answer: c) soil** — soil shows none of the characteristics of life.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A river moves. Is the river alive?
a) yes, because it moves  b) no — movement alone does not make a thing alive
**Answer: b) no** — plants live without locomotion and rivers move without living.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Keeping the inside of the body steady is called —
a) homeostasis  b) excretion  c) nutrition
**Answer: a) homeostasis**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Getting rid of wastes is —
a) respiration  b) excretion  c) adaptation
**Answer: b) excretion**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Making more of its own kind is —
a) growth  b) sensitivity  c) reproduction
**Answer: c) reproduction**
