# Living things — what makes something alive — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A plant is alive even though it does not move from place to place.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Respiration means taking in food.
**Answer: False — respiration is breathing / releasing energy; taking in food is nutrition.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One sign alone is always enough to prove a thing is alive.
**Answer: False — judge by the characteristics together.**
