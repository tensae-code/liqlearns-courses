# Animals — Who am I? vertebrate review game

## Do it first

Here is a card: 'I lay jelly eggs in water. My young breathe with gills, my adults with lungs.' Who am I? Answer before you open the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The clue cards

Each card hides one vertebrate group behind its clues: milk + fur + lungs; feathers + hard-shelled eggs; leathery scales + lungs; jelly eggs + gills-then-lungs; gills + fins + scales. Read the clues, name the group, then check.

[Picture 32916 — five mystery cards with clue lists, answers hidden]

## Sub lesson 2 — Playing the game (doing)

Play in groups: one learner reads a card's clues one by one, the others guess the group after each clue. The fewest clues used wins the round. Every guess must name the clue that proves it.

## Say it to your companion

Explain in your own words: the five vertebrate groups can be told apart from clue cards — milk and fur, feathers and hard-shelled eggs, leathery scales, jelly eggs with gills-then-lungs, and gills with fins and scales. Give one example of your own for each part, and let your companion challenge you with one hard case.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the lesson's key facts from memory, then check them against the lesson and fix any miss.
2. Find one real example of this lesson's subject near your home or school, observe it without harming it, and record what you see.
3. Teach the one thing this lesson teaches to someone at home, using your example.
