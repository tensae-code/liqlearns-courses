# Video script — Animals — Who am I? vertebrate review game

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: the five vertebrate groups can be told apart from clue cards — milk and fur, feathers and hard-shelled eggs, leathery scales, jelly eggs with gills-then-lungs, and gills with fins and scales.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
