# Animals — Who am I? vertebrate review game — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Milk + fur + lungs — who am I?
a) bird  b) mammal  c) fish
**Answer: b) mammal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Feathers + hard-shelled eggs — who am I?
a) bird  b) reptile  c) amphibian
**Answer: a) bird**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Gills + fins + scales — who am I?
a) amphibian  b) fish  c) mammal
**Answer: b) fish**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Jelly eggs + gills-then-lungs — who am I?
a) reptile  b) bird  c) amphibian
**Answer: c) amphibian**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Leathery scales + lungs — who am I?
a) reptile  b) fish  c) mammal
**Answer: a) reptile**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the game, a guess must —
a) be fast only  b) name the clue that proves it  c) copy a partner
**Answer: b) name the clue that proves it**
