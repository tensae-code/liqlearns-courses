# Animals — Who am I? vertebrate review game — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the clue set for a bird.
**Answer:** Feathers + hard-shelled eggs (warm blood, beak, wings may support).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A card reads 'leathery scales + lungs'. Name the group and the proving clue.
**Answer:** Reptile — the leathery scales (with lungs) prove it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Make a new clue card for a mammal without using the word milk twice.
**Answer:** E.g. 'I am born alive, I have fur, I breathe with lungs, my babies drink milk.' — mammal.
