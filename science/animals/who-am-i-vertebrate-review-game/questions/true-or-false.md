# Animals — Who am I? vertebrate review game — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Only mammals feed their young on milk.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fish breathe with lungs.
**Answer: False — fish breathe with gills.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Amphibians begin with gills and later breathe with lungs.
**Answer: True**
