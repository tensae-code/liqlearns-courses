# Video script — Protists — protozoa and algae under the microscope

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson. This is a FULL script: the placement names a hay-infusion culture walkthrough with its hygiene warnings. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today we grow tiny swimmers — and we stay clean.

One. Put dry hay in a jar of pond water. Cover the jar loosely.
Two. Wait. Five days. Ten days. Other microbes grow in there too — not only the ones we want.
Three. Wash your hands. Never taste the water. Never put your fingers in your mouth.
Four. Take one drop. Put it on a slide. Look through the microscope.
Five. There! A slipper shape. It swims. Tiny hairs — cilia — ripple all over it. That is a paramecium.
Six. Wash your hands again. Wash every tool. The culture stays covered, away from food.
Clean hands. Covered jar. One drop. That is how we meet the paramecium — safely.
