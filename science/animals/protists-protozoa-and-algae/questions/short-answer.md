# Protists — protozoa and algae under the microscope — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three ways protozoa move, with an example where given.
**Answer:** Pseudopodia (amoeba), flagella, cilia (paramecium).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the hay infusion need strict hygiene?
**Answer:** Other microbes flourish in the infusion too, not only paramecium.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does Practical 5.7 study algae?
**Answer:** By observing pond-water algae under the microscope.
