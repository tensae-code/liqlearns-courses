# Protists — protozoa and algae under the microscope — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Protozoa can cause malaria, amoebiasis and giardiasis.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** You may taste the hay infusion to check it.
**Answer: False — never taste cultures; strict hygiene, wash hands.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Algae photosynthesise.
**Answer: True**
