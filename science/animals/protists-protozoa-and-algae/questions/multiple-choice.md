# Protists — protozoa and algae under the microscope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Protists are mostly —
a) multicellular with roots  b) unicellular eukaryotes  c) prokaryotes
**Answer: b) unicellular eukaryotes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An amoeba moves by —
a) cilia  b) pseudopodia  c) wings
**Answer: b) pseudopodia**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A paramecium moves by —
a) cilia  b) pseudopodia  c) flagella only
**Answer: a) cilia**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Chlamydomonas and Euglena are —
a) fungi  b) plant-like algae  c) bacteria
**Answer: b) plant-like algae**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Plant-like algae have —
a) true roots and leaves  b) no root, stem or leaf  c) seeds
**Answer: b) no root, stem or leaf**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The hay infusion culture runs for —
a) one hour  b) 5–10 days  c) one year
**Answer: b) 5–10 days** — with strict hygiene.
