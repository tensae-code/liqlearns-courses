# Hands-on — Protists — protozoa and algae under the microscope

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practical 5.6 — hay-infusion culture
With an adult: put hay in pond water, wait 5–10 days, examine a drop under the microscope. Never taste it, keep it covered, wash hands before and after. Field safety (teacher guide — evidence, item 11, guide printed pp. 84–108): some organisms may sting or bite or carry disease — never collect with bare hands, use gloves or a container, watch where you step, wash hands afterwards, and release every specimen alive and unharmed where you found it.

## Practical 5.7 — pond-water algae
Examine a drop of pond water. Sketch the green swimmers you find and note: root, stem or leaf — present or absent? Wash hands afterwards.
