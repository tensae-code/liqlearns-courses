# Comparing plants and animals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about comparing plants and animals?
**Answer: The books teach this part as 6: Venn-style comparison of plants and animals (appearance, feeding, growth, movement) and what animals need (water, air, light, heat, food); snow leopard (barys) fact box — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about plants and animals — how they are alike and different?
**Answer: The books teach this part as both breathe, feed, grow, reproduce, develop; plants are still and grow all their life, animals move and grow for a set period; a puppy's growth series from 7 days to 3 years — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
