# Animals adapting to the seasons — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about animals adapting to the seasons?
**Answer: 8: hare's coat thickening and turning white, squirrels and mice storing food, migratory birds (nightingale, swallow, chaffinch, crane) vs wintering birds with strong beaks (woodpecker, tit, magpie, sparrow), hibernation of bear, badger and hedgehog, feeding birds in winter, deer moving down from the mountains**

**2.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about how the seasons affect animals?
**Answer: The books teach this part as migration as key word; marmots hibernate six months; how forest animals prepare for winter — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources.**
