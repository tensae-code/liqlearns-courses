Today: **Animals adapting to the seasons** — Kazakhstan's way.

8: hare's coat thickening and turning white, squirrels and mice storing food, migratory birds (nightingale, swallow, chaffinch, crane) vs wintering birds with strong beaks (woodpecker, tit, magpie, sparrow), hibernation of bear, badger and hedgehog, feeding birds in winter, deer moving down from the mountains The books teach this part as migration as key word; marmots hibernate six months; how forest animals prepare for winter — worked through in the books' own presentation, at the printed pages named in Sources. moulting (linka) defined — skin, feathers, wool or fur is shed, thicker fur grows by autumn; hare grey in summer, white in winter; hibernation after heavy summer/autumn feeding; migratory birds; winter food stores (squirrel — nuts, cones, acorns, mushrooms; badger — roots and seeds; hamster — grain); the marmot's deep burrow with sleeping nest, store room and spare exits; 23 October is Snow Leopard Day, the irbis is in Kazakhstan's Red Book.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
