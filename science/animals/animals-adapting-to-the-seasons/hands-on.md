# Hands-on — Animals adapting to the seasons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Animals adapting to the seasons. 8: hare's coat thickening and turning white, squirrels and mice storing food, migratory birds (nightingale, swallow, chaffinch, crane) vs wintering birds with strong beaks (woodpecker, tit, magpie, sparrow), hibernation of bear, badger and hedgehog, feeding birds in winter, deer moving down from the mountains Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
