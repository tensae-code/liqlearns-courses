# Hands-on — How animals breathe — lungs, gills, skin and surface

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel your own breathing
Rest a hand on your chest and count your breaths in one quiet minute. Then run on the spot for a minute and count again. Write both numbers and say what changed and why your body asked for more air.

## Breathing organs table
Make a four-column table — lungs, gills, skin, whole surface. Under each, write an animal you know that breathes that way, from the lesson and your own knowledge.
