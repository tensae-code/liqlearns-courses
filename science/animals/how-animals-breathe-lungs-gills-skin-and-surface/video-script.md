Today: **How animals breathe — lungs, gills, skin and surface** — Bulgaria's way: one life process, many organs.

Breathing means taking in oxygen — and the oxygen frees the energy in food. That is what breathing is for, in every living thing.

But the organs differ: lungs here, gills there, skin in another animal, and some breathe through their whole surface. And plants breathe too.

Now you. Name an animal and say with what it breathes. Say it back — in your own words.
