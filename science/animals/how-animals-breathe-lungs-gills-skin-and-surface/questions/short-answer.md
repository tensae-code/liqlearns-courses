# How animals breathe — lungs, gills, skin and surface — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is breathing for, in the book's teaching?
**Answer:** Organisms take in oxygen to release the energy in food — breathing frees food's energy for living.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the four ways animals breathe in the lesson.
**Answer:** With lungs, with gills, through the skin, or through the whole body surface.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book add that plants breathe too?
**Answer:** Because breathing belongs to living things as such — it is a life process, not the property of one group or one organ.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A worm has no lungs and no gills. Can it breathe, and how?
**Answer:** Yes — through its skin / whole body surface, one of the ways the lesson names.
