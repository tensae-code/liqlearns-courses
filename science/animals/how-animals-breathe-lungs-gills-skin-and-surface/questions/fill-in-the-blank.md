# How animals breathe — lungs, gills, skin and surface — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Organisms take in _____ to release energy from food.
**Answer: oxygen**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fish breathe with _____.
**Answer: gills**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Some animals breathe through their _____ or their whole body surface.
**Answer: skin**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Breathing is one of the _____ processes.
**Answer: life**
