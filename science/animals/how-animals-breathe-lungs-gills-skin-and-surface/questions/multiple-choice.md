# How animals breathe — lungs, gills, skin and surface — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Breathing is taking in oxygen to…
a) Make food  b) Release energy from food  c) Grow fur  d) Sleep
**Answer: b) Release energy from food**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fish breathe with…
a) Lungs  b) Gills  c) Skin only  d) Feathers
**Answer: b) Gills**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Which list names only breathing organs from the lesson?
a) Lungs, gills, skin, whole surface  b) Wings, fins, shells, horns  c) Eyes, ears, nose, tongue  d) Roots, stems, leaves, flowers
**Answer: a) Lungs, gills, skin, whole surface**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Do plants breathe?
a) No, never  b) Yes — plants breathe too  c) Only at night, says the book  d) Only flowers do
**Answer: b) Yes — plants breathe too**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Breathing is a…
a) Kind of food  b) Life process  c) Kind of movement  d) Season
**Answer: b) Life process**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) An animal with no lungs and no gills may breathe through its…
a) Shell  b) Skin or whole body surface  c) Horns  d) Sting
**Answer: b) Skin or whole body surface**
