# Hands-on — Animal teeth — and what they eat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compare two mouths
Choose two animals you can picture clearly — one meat-eater, one plant-eater. Draw each one's teeth as best you can from what you know, and write under each drawing: this animal eats… Its teeth fit that food because…

## The teeth detective
At home or on a walk, look at any animal you can safely watch eating — a goat, a dog, a chicken, a cat. Watch which teeth (or beak) do the work, and write one sentence: the tool fits the food because… Never put fingers in an animal's mouth — watch only.
