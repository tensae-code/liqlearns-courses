# Lesson plan — Animal teeth — and what they eat

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Animals · **Lesson:** Animal teeth — and what they eat
**Place in the curriculum:** Animals — appended to Unit 9 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** whether all animals have the same teeth — they do not — and how an animal's teeth fit what it eats — Djibouti's own way, in its Grade 3 Sciences Expérimentales teacher guide, is to open with the question itself: do all animals have the same teeth?
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Do all animals have the same teeth?** — Djibouti's Grade 3 guide opens the lesson with its own question — do all animals have the same teeth? The answer the lesson works toward is no: animals' teeth differ, and the differences are not accidents.
2. **Teeth fit the food** — An animal's teeth fit what it eats: the teeth are the tools the animal works its food with, so the shape of the teeth and the animal's diet belong together — read the teeth and you can say something true about the food.
