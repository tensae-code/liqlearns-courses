# Animal teeth — and what they eat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Ask the guide's question and answer it.
**Answer: Do all animals have the same teeth? No — teeth differ from animal to animal, in shape and in what they are good for.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do teeth and diet belong in one lesson?
**Answer: Because an animal eats with its teeth: its teeth fit its food, so the teeth are a sign of the diet and the food explains the teeth.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name an animal and say what its teeth tell you.
**Answer: Any animal correctly matched: its teeth fit the food it eats — e.g. a meat-eater's teeth fit tearing meat; a plant-eater's teeth fit grinding plants.**
