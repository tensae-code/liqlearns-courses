# Animal teeth — and what they eat — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Not all animals have the same _____ — they differ from animal to animal.
**Answer: teeth**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) An animal's teeth fit what it _____.
**Answer: eats**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Teeth are the _____ the animal works its food with.
**Answer: tools**
