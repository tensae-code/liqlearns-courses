# Animal teeth — and what they eat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Do all animals have the same teeth?
a) Yes — every animal's teeth are the same  b) No — teeth differ from animal to animal  c) Only young animals have teeth  d) Animals have no teeth
**Answer: b) No — teeth differ from animal to animal**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson's first fact is found by…
a) Memorising a list  b) Sleeping on it  c) Comparing animals and looking at their teeth  d) Asking the teeth
**Answer: c) Comparing animals and looking at their teeth**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) An animal's teeth fit…
a) Its colour  b) What it eats  c) Where it sleeps  d) Its name
**Answer: b) What it eats**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Teeth are…
a) Decorations  b) The tools the animal works its food with  c) Only for smiling  d) The same in every mouth
**Answer: b) The tools the animal works its food with**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Given an animal's teeth, you can say something true about…
a) Its age in years  b) Its favourite song  c) Its food  d) Its owner's name
**Answer: c) Its food**
