Today: **Animal teeth — and what they eat**.

Djibouti's Grade 3 guide asks it straight: do all animals have the same teeth? No. Teeth differ from animal to animal — and the reason is food. An animal's teeth are the tools it eats with, so the teeth fit the food: read the teeth and you can say something true about what the animal eats.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
