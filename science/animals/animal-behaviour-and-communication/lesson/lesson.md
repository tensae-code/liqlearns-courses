# Animal behaviour and communication

## Sub lesson 1 — Signals and calls

Animals send messages to each other. Fiji's Year 4 Teacher Resource teaches animal behaviour first as **signals and calls**: the movements and sounds by which one animal tells another something — a warning, a greeting, a claim — without words.

## Sub lesson 2 — Showing — raising fins and puffing up

Some messages are shown with the body. The book's examples are an animal **raising its fins** and an animal **puffing itself up**: the body changes shape and size, and the change itself is the message — most often a protection message: I am bigger than I look; keep away.

## Sub lesson 3 — The sense of smell — protection and attracting

Not every message can be seen or heard. Animals also use their **sense of smell**: smells carry news of danger and of food, and animals use smell both **for protection** and **for attracting** — drawing others toward them. Behaviour, in Fiji's book, is the whole set: signals, calls, shows of the body, and smell.
