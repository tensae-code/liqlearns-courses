# Hands-on — Animal behaviour and communication

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Watch one animal near your home or school for a few minutes. Write down every behaviour you see — a call, a movement, a change of shape or size — and beside each one, what you think it says: protection, attracting, or something else. Compare your list with the lesson's three kinds: signals and calls, showing with the body, and smell.
