# Animal behaviour and communication — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Signals and calls  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Signals and calls**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Showing — raising fins and puffing up  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Showing — raising fins and puffing up**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The sense of smell — protection and attracting  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The sense of smell — protection and attracting**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: how animals behave and communicate — signals, calls, raising fins, puffing up, and the sense of smell, used for protection and for attracting. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
