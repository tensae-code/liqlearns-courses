# Animal behaviour and communication — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Signals and calls.
**Answer:** Animals send messages to each other.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Showing — raising fins and puffing up.
**Answer:** Some messages are shown with the body.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The sense of smell — protection and attracting.
**Answer:** Not every message can be seen or heard.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Signals and calls → Showing — raising fins and puffing up → The sense of smell — protection and attracting — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
