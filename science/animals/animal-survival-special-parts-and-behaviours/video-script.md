Today: **Animal survival — special parts and behaviours** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 5 — *Sains Tahun 5*, printed p. 50).

Malaysia's Grade 5 book opens its survival unit with **species survival (kemandirian spesies)**: animals stay alive through special body parts and behaviours. Against enemies it catalogues **sharp spines and poison (the pufferfish), shedding body parts (the lizard's tail, the octopus's arm), camouflage and warning colours**, and further special parts — **horns, shells, scales and quills** — each with its examples.

Against **extreme weather** the book teaches **bird migration, hibernation, and thick fur or blubber**; for the desert's extreme heat, only certain animals suit it — the **camel** and its features. Survival continues into the next generation: animals **protect their eggs** by hiding them, covering them, carrying them (mouth-brooding) or guarding them, and **keep their young alive** by feeding them milk, carrying them, and attacking enemies — the hen and the monkey defending their young. The unit closes with an **"imaginary animal" design task**: invent an animal and justify its special parts for survival.
