Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: animal survival — how species stay alive (kemandirian spesies) through special body parts and behaviours: against enemies, against extreme weather, and by protecting their eggs and young. That is…
