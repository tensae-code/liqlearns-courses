# Hands-on — Asexual reproduction — budding, fission and spores

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Nicaragua's book sets it: Cell reproduction without gametes — fission, budding, sporulation. Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
