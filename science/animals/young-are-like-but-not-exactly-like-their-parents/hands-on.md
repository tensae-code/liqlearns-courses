# Hands-on — Young are like, but not exactly like, their parents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Family likeness hunt
Choose a parent-and-young pair you can observe — a plant and its seedlings, an animal and its young, or photographs. List three traits the young shares with the parent and two ways it differs.

## Read the pattern
Measure one trait — leaf length, seed size — across ten young plants of one kind. Write the measurements in order and say what pattern they show.
