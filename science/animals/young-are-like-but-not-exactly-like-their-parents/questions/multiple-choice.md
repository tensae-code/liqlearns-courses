# Young are like, but not exactly like, their parents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Young resemble their parents  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Young resemble their parents**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) But not exactly like them  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) But not exactly like them**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Reading the pattern in data — inherited traits  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Reading the pattern in data — inherited traits**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: young are like but not exactly like their parents. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) *Utah SEEd Standards, Grade 1 and Grade 3 (United States)*  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) *Utah SEEd Standards, Grade 1 and Grade 3 (United States)***
