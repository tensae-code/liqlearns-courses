# Hands-on — Animals — invertebrates: insects and earthworms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practical 5.3 — insect count
Collect humanely in a container, count legs, body parts and wings under a hand lens, record — then release. Field safety (teacher guide — evidence, item 11, guide printed pp. 84–108): some organisms may sting or bite or carry disease — never collect with bare hands, use gloves or a container, watch where you step, wash hands afterwards, and release every specimen alive and unharmed where you found it.

## Practical 5.4 — earthworm study
Lift a worm gently from damp soil, find segments, head end, mouth, anus and clitellum, sketch it, and return it to its soil. Field safety (teacher guide — evidence, item 11, guide printed pp. 84–108): some organisms may sting or bite or carry disease — never collect with bare hands, use gloves or a container, watch where you step, wash hands afterwards, and release every specimen alive and unharmed where you found it.
