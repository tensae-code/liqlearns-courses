# Video script — Animals — invertebrates: insects and earthworms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: more than 95% of animals have no backbone; insects are known by six legs and three body parts, and the earthworm enriches the soil with its castings.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
