# Animals — invertebrates: insects and earthworms — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A spider passes the insect test.
**Answer: False — it has eight legs, not six.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An earthworm's body is segmented.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Specimens should be returned alive after study.
**Answer: True**
