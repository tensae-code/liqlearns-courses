# Animals — invertebrates: insects and earthworms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** More than 95% of animals are —
a) vertebrates  b) invertebrates  c) mammals
**Answer: b) invertebrates**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An insect has —
a) eight legs  b) six legs and three body parts  c) no legs
**Answer: b) six legs and three body parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is an invertebrate?
a) sheep  b) snail  c) frog
**Answer: b) snail**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The earthworm's thick band is the —
a) tympanum  b) clitellum  c) frond
**Answer: b) clitellum**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The clitellum sits —
a) at the tail tip  b) about a third of the way back  c) on the head
**Answer: b) about a third of the way back**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Earthworm castings —
a) poison the soil  b) enrich the soil  c) are stones
**Answer: b) enrich the soil**
