# Animals — invertebrates: insects and earthworms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three invertebrates.
**Answer:** Any three of: jellyfish, worms, snails, starfish, insects.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** State the insect test.
**Answer:** Six legs and three body parts (wings counted with a hand lens in the practical).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** List the earthworm parts you find in Practical 5.4.
**Answer:** Segments, sharper head end, mouth, anus, and the clitellum band a third of the way back.
