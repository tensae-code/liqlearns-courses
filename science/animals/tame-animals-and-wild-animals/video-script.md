# Video script — Tame animals and wild animals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The hen, the cat, the donkey — **ours**, under our care.
The hyena, the lion, the warthog — **free**, in forest and grassland.
Milk, clothes, transport, joy — the tame ones serve.
Draw the line by care, and every animal finds its side.
