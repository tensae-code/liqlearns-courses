# Lesson plan — Tame animals and wild animals

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Animals · **Lesson:** Tame animals and wild animals
**Place in the curriculum:** Animals — the eighth title on the Science ladder (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** domestic animals live under people's care; wild animals live in forest, grassland, caves and water — and domestic animals give people great service: food and milk, clothes, transport, entertainment
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own compound — the animals at home are the first textbook.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell domestic from wild: domestic animals live tamed under people's care (cat, hen, donkey); wild animals live free (hyena, lion, leopard, warthog) in forest, grassland, caves and water.
2. Name the services of domestic animals, from the guide: food and milk, making clothes, transport, entertainment.
3. Say when animals breed, from the guide: most breed when there is enough food and favourable weather.

4. Add the Grade 4 account of wild animals: their kinds (birds, mammals, crawling and creeping animals) and what they give us — food, scientific study, tourism income, skins and parts for ornaments.
## What you need
The animals of your own home and street to classify; a walk where wild signs might be seen or only named.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls a stray cat wild → the line is the arrangement, not the temper: domestic kinds live under people's care, even an unfriendly cat; wild kinds live free, even a quiet hyena at distance.
- Lists only food as service → the guide's list is wider: food and milk, clothes, transport, entertainment.
- The services and breeding content rests on the Grade 1 teacher guide — evidence, labelled in the sources.
