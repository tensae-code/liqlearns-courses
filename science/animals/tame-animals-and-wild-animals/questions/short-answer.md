# Tame animals and wild animals — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Name two services of domestic animals.**
**Answer: food and milk, making clothes, transport, entertainment (any two)**
**2. Name three of Ethiopia's endemic animals.**
**Answer: Any three of: gelada monkey, red (Simien) fox, walia ibex, Menelik's bushbuck, korki, mountain nyala, the wild ass.**
