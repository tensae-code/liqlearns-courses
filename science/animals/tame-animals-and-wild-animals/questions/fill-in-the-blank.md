# Tame animals and wild animals — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. Most animals breed when there is enough ___ and favourable weather.**
**Answer: food**

**2. A leopard is a ___ animal.**
**Answer: wild**
**3. The walia ___ and the red fox are pictured wild animals of the mountain park.**
**Answer: ibex**
**4. Animals found only in Ethiopia are called ______ animals.**
**Answer: endemic**

**5. The buffalo in Amharic, as the book prints it, is ______.**
**Answer: ጎሽ**
