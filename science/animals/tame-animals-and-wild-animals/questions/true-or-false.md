# Tame animals and wild animals — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. Domestic animals give services: food and milk, clothes, transport, entertainment.**
**Answer: True**

**2. A donkey is a domestic animal.**
**Answer: True**
**3. Skins and parts of wild animals are used for ornaments.**
**Answer: True**
**4. Habitat destruction is one of the two main reasons wildlife is shrinking.**
**Answer: True**

**5. The gelada is found in many countries.**
**Answer: False**
