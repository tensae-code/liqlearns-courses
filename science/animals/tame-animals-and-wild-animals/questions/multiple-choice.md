# Tame animals and wild animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A domestic animal is one that —**
a) lives in a cage at the zoo  b) lives tamed under people's care  c) is very small
**Answer: b) lives tamed under people's care**

**2. Which of these is a wild animal of the book's list?**
a) hen  b) donkey  c) hyena
**Answer: c) hyena**

**3. Where do wild animals live in the book's list?**
a) in houses  b) in forest, grassland, caves and water  c) in school
**Answer: b) in forest, grassland, caves and water**

**4. Animals breed most when —**
a) food is scarce  b) there is enough food and favourable weather  c) the weather is worst
**Answer: b) there is enough food and favourable weather**
**5. Wild animals in the book are sorted as —**
a) birds, mammals, and crawling and creeping animals  b) big and small only  c) flying only
**Answer: a) birds, mammals, and crawling and creeping animals**

**6. One thing wild animals give us in the book is —**
a) tourism income  b) homework  c) rain
**Answer: a) tourism income**
**7. How many of Ethiopia's bird species are found only in Ethiopia?**
a) 845  b) 28  c) 5
**Answer: b) 28**

**8. The walia ibex's home in Table 4.2 is —**
a) Nech Sar Park  b) the Simien Mountains National Park  c) Afar
**Answer: b) the Simien Mountains National Park**

**9. Which is NOT one of the big five?**
a) buffalo  b) giraffe  c) leopard
**Answer: b) giraffe**

**10. Elephants in Kenya are killed yearly for —**
a) milk  b) ivory  c) their tails
**Answer: b) ivory**

**11. A protection-area kind is —**
a) a controlled hunting area  b) a market  c) a factory
**Answer: a) a controlled hunting area**
