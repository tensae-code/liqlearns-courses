# Hands-on — Tame animals and wild animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Service ledger
For one domestic animal you know well, write its full service ledger for a year: everything it gives, everything it costs in care. The guide's fairness: service answers care.

## Wild homes drawing
Draw the four wild homes — forest, grassland, cave, water — with one tenant each, from the book's list or your own area's truth.

## Night listening
At night, listen. Any wild voices? Name them, place them, and say why they do not come into the compound.


## Kinds in the wild around us
From a safe distance or from family knowledge, list the wild animals of your area and sort them the Grade 4 way: birds / mammals / crawling and creeping. Beside each, write what it gives people, if the book's use-list covers it.
