# Tame animals and wild animals — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: classify the book's animals and new ones by the care test, name four services, and state the breeding-seasons rule
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 4 depth (added 2026-11-12): inside the same whole lesson, practice deepens — the learner also sorts wild animals into the book's kinds (birds, mammals, crawling and creeping animals) and states what wild animals give: food, scientific study, tourism income, skins and parts for ornaments.

Grade 5/6 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also recites the bird numbers (845/28), names the seven endemics with two homes pinned, names the big five, and states poaching and habitat destruction as the two shrinking reasons.
