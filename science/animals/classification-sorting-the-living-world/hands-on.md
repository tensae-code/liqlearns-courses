# Hands-on — Classification — sorting the living world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The compound survey
In groups, survey the school compound. Table your finds with their features, build grouping criteria, and present your groups. Field safety (teacher guide — evidence, item 11, guide printed pp. 84–108): some organisms may sting or bite or carry disease — never collect with bare hands, use gloves or a container, watch where you step, wash hands afterwards, and release every specimen alive and unharmed where you found it.

## Shelf to system
Sort ten named organisms twice: first by your own criteria, then by a partner's criteria. Say what changed and why.
