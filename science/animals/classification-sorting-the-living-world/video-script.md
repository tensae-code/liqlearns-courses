# Video script — Classification — sorting the living world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: classification groups living things by similarity and difference so life can be studied in order, newcomers identified and relationships revealed; biodiversity is the whole variety of life on Earth.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
