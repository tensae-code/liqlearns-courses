# Classification — sorting the living world — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two reasons scientists classify living things.
**Answer:** Any two of: to order study, to identify newcomers, to reveal relationships.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe how your group did the compound survey.
**Answer:** Listed organisms in the compound, recorded features such as movement, wings, legs, flowers and cones, then built grouping criteria and sorted the finds.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** State one field-safety rule for the survey and say why it matters.
**Answer:** E.g. no bare-hand collecting / use gloves or a container — some organisms may sting, bite or carry disease (teacher guide — evidence, item 11).
