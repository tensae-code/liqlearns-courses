# Classification — sorting the living world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Classification groups living things by —
a) similarity and difference  b) size only  c) where you saw them first
**Answer: a) similarity and difference**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Biodiversity is —
a) one rare species  b) Earth's whole variety of life  c) a single habitat
**Answer: b) Earth's whole variety of life**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Classification helps scientists to —
a) hide organisms  b) identify newcomers and reveal relationships  c) stop studying
**Answer: b) identify newcomers and reveal relationships**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the survey, a good feature to record is —
a) legs, wings, flowers or cones  b) the time of day only  c) your favourite colour
**Answer: a) legs, wings, flowers or cones**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Grouping criteria are —
a) rules you build from recorded features  b) guesses  c) names copied from a poster
**Answer: a) rules you build from recorded features**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Before collecting outdoors you should —
a) use bare hands  b) follow field safety: gloves or container, no bare-hand collecting  c) keep every specimen
**Answer: b) follow field safety** — teacher-guide safety guidance (item 11).
