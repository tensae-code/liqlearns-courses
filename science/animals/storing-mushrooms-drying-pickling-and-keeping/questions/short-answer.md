# Storing mushrooms — drying, pickling and keeping — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Sukuti made by blanching and sun- or oven-drying into airtight packs; the chemical drying soak (potassium bisulphite, citric acid, sugar and salt as printed) and drying at 60–62 degrees; ketchup, pickle and chutney keeping six months; and mushroom soup, pakoda, omelette and curry.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed p. 37.**
