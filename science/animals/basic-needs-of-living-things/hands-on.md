# Hands-on — Basic needs of living things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Lesson 1. Lesson 1: the basic needs of living things — water, food, air, housing, light and heat — opened Honduras's way through the story of the duck guarding her eggs and feeding her ducklings. Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
