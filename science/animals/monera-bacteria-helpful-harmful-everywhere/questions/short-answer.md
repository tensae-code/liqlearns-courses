# Monera — bacteria: helpful, harmful, everywhere — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three facts of the Monera plan.
**Answer:** Any three of: prokaryotes, no nuclear membrane, no membrane-bound organelles, murein walls, asexual fission, some flagellated, everywhere.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one harmful and one useful effect of bacteria.
**Answer:** Harmful: any of souring milk, poisoning food (decomposing matter is natural work). Useful: curdling milk.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are blue-green algae not true algae?
**Answer:** They are cyanobacteria — photosynthetic bacteria, prokaryotes of the Monera.
