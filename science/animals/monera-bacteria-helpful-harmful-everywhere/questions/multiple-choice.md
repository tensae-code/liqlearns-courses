# Monera — bacteria: helpful, harmful, everywhere — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Monera cells have —
a) a clear nucleus  b) no nuclear membrane  c) chloroplasts
**Answer: b) no nuclear membrane** — they are prokaryotes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bacteria reproduce by —
a) seeds  b) fission  c) spores in cones
**Answer: b) fission**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bacterial cell walls contain —
a) cellulose  b) chitin  c) murein
**Answer: c) murein**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a bacterial shape?
a) rods, spheres and spirals  b) fronds  c) cones
**Answer: a) rods, spheres and spirals**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Blue-green algae are really —
a) true algae  b) photosynthetic bacteria (cyanobacteria)  c) fungi
**Answer: b) photosynthetic bacteria (cyanobacteria)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A useful work of bacteria is —
a) curdling milk  b) souring food to waste it  c) poisoning food
**Answer: a) curdling milk**
