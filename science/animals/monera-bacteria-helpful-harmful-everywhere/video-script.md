# Video script — Monera — bacteria: helpful, harmful, everywhere

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: Monera are prokaryotes with no nuclear membrane and no membrane-bound organelles; bacteria sour milk and poison food yet also curdle milk usefully, and blue-green algae are photosynthetic bacteria, not true algae.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
