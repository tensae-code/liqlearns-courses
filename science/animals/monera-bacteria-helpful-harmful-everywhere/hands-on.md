# Hands-on — Monera — bacteria: helpful, harmful, everywhere

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Milk watch (adult-led)
With an adult, compare fresh milk and curdled milk by smell from a safe distance — never taste milk you suspect is spoiled. Record what changed and name the workers.

## Shape hunt in print
Find the three bacterial shapes in the lesson picture. Draw each and label it: rod, sphere, spiral.
