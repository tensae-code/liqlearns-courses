# How animals move — swim, walk, fly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which way of moving belongs to water?
a) Walking  b) Swimming  c) Flying  d) Digging  
**Answer: b) Swimming**

**2.** A bird moves through the air by…
a) swimming  b) walking  c) flying  d) crawling  
**Answer: c) flying**

**3.** Sudan's lesson teaches movement…
a) only for fish  b) animal by animal, how each moves in its place  c) only in the air  d) without naming the place  
**Answer: b) animal by animal, how each moves in its place**
