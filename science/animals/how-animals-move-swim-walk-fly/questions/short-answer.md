# How animals move — swim, walk, fly — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the three great ways of moving in this lesson and the place each belongs to.
**Answer: Swimming in water, walking on land, and flying in the air — each animal moves in its own way in the place it lives.**

**2.** How does Sudan's Grade 4 book organise this topic?
**Answer: As the first lesson of its Animals unit: movement in animals, taught animal by animal — how each animal moves in its place.**
