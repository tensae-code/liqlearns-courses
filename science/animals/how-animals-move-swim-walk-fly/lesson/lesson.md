# How animals move — swim, walk, fly

## Do it first
Think of three animals you know well. How does each one get from place to place? Say your answers out loud. Then read on: this is Sudan's way in — its Grade 4 Natural Sciences opens its Animals unit with movement in animals, how each animal moves in its place.

## Sub lesson 1 — Moving in water — swimming

Animals that live in water move by **swimming**: the body pushes against the water and the animal travels through the place it lives in. Sudan's lesson teaches movement animal by animal — starting from the animal and the place it lives in, and asking how it moves there.

## Sub lesson 2 — Moving on land — walking

Animals that live on land move by **walking** (and running) on their legs: the legs carry the body over the ground. The same lesson's question holds for every land animal — how does this animal move in its place?

## Sub lesson 3 — Moving in the air — flying

Animals with wings move by **flying**: the wings beat against the air and carry the body through it. Swimming, walking and flying are the lesson's three great ways — water, land and air, each animal fitted with its own way of moving in its place.

## The key ideas

- **Moving in water — swimming** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.
- **Moving on land — walking** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.
- **Moving in the air — flying** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "How does Sudan's lesson teach animal movement?" Answer: Animal by animal: for each animal, how it moves in the place it lives — swimming in water, walking on land, flying in the air.

## Do It Yourself
1. Name three animals — one that swims, one that walks, one that flies — and say for each how it moves in its place.
2. Act out the three ways in order — swim, walk, fly — and say which place each way belongs to.
3. Teach the hardest part of this lesson to someone at home in five sentences.
