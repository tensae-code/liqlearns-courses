Today: **How animals move — swim, walk, fly**.

Animals move — each in its own way, in its own place. In water they swim, on land they walk, in the air they fly. Sudan's Grade 4 Animals unit starts by asking, for every animal: how does this one move where it lives?

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
