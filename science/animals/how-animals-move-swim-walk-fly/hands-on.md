# Hands-on — How animals move — swim, walk, fly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Show the three ways
Make a three-column table — water, land, air. Put each animal you know in its column and write its way of moving beside it: swims, walks, flies.

## Move like the animal
Choose one animal from each column and move the way it moves, in its place. A partner names the animal and the way from your movement alone.
