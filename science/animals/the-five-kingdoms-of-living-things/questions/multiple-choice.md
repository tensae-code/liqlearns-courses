# Kingdoms — the five kingdoms of living things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many kingdoms are there in this system?
a) three  b) five  c) seven
**Answer: b) five**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a kingdom?
a) Protista  b) Insects  c) Fish
**Answer: a) Protista**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A mushroom belongs to —
a) Plantae  b) Fungi  c) Animalia
**Answer: b) Fungi**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kingdoms are separated by —
a) colour and size  b) cell structure, nutrition, body organisation and reproduction  c) country
**Answer: b) cell structure, nutrition, body organisation and reproduction**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Nutrition as a kingdom test means —
a) how the organism feeds  b) how fast it moves  c) where it sleeps
**Answer: a) how the organism feeds**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A sheep belongs to —
a) Monera  b) Animalia  c) Protista
**Answer: b) Animalia**
