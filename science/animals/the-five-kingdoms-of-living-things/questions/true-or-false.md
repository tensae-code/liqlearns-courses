# Kingdoms — the five kingdoms of living things — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Monera is one of the five kingdoms.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Size alone separates the kingdoms.
**Answer: False — cell structure, nutrition, body organisation and reproduction separate them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Plantae and Animalia are both kingdoms.
**Answer: True**
