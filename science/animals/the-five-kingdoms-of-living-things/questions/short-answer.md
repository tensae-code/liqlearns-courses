# Kingdoms — the five kingdoms of living things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name all five kingdoms.
**Answer:** Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the four tests used to separate kingdoms.
**Answer:** Cell structure, nutrition, body organisation, reproduction.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Place a bacterium and a teff plant in their kingdoms.
**Answer:** Bacterium — Monera; teff plant — Plantae.
