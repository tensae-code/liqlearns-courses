# Animal body symmetry and axes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Three kinds of symmetry — spherical, radial and bilateral  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Three kinds of symmetry — spherical, radial and bilateral**

**2.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the symmetry of an animal's body — the planes along which its two sides match: spherical, radial and bilateral. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**
