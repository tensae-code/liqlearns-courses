# Animal body symmetry and axes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Three kinds of symmetry — spherical, radial and bilateral.
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 7 Módulo Autoformativo names the invertebrate body symmetry types: **spherical** symmetry (the body matches along any plane through its centre, like a ball), **radial** symmetry (the body matches along planes through one central axis, like slices of a cake) and **bilateral** symmetry (one plane only divides the body into matching left and right halves — the symmetry of most animals, and of our own bodies). Naming an animal's symmetry is a first step in sorting the invertebrates, the animals without backbones.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Three kinds of symmetry — spherical, radial and bilateral — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
