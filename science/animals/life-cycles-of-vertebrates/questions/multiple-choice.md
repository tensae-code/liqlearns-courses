# Life cycles of vertebrates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Vertebrates are the animals…
a) Without backbones  b) With backbones  c) With wings only  d) That live only in water
**Answer: b) With backbones**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A vertebrate's life runs…
a) In a straight line that ends  b) In a cycle — young, growth, maturity, new young  c) Backwards  d) Only in winter
**Answer: b) In a cycle — young, growth, maturity, new young**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In Iraq's book, the vertebrate life-cycle chapter comes…
a) Before all animal lessons  b) After the invertebrates' life cycles  c) Inside the plants unit  d) In Grade 1
**Answer: b) After the invertebrates' life cycles**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The activity book's part is…
a) An activity on the same cycles, stage by stage  b) A spelling list  c) Nothing  d) A map of Iraq
**Answer: a) An activity on the same cycles, stage by stage**
