# Life cycles of vertebrates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the cycle's stages in order.
**Answer: Young, growth, maturity, and new young of the same kind — then the cycle begins again.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a backbone's place in this lesson?
**Answer: It is the vertebrates' mark: the animals with backbones are the group whose life cycles this chapter teaches.**
