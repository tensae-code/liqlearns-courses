# Hands-on — Life cycles of vertebrates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A vertebrate circle
Draw one vertebrate's life as a circle of stages, youngest to the return to young. Label every stage.

## Two groups, two cycles
Draw two circles: an invertebrate's cycle (from the invertebrates lessons) and a vertebrate's. Write one likeness and one difference under them.
