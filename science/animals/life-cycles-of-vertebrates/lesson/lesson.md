# Life cycles of vertebrates

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — the life cycles of vertebrate animals — the animals with backbones — as Iraq's Grade 4 pupil's book teaches them as a chapter of their own, with the activity book's matching activity. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Iraq's way in — its Ministry of Education *Science* books (the Primary Science Book Series) teach this thing at the places named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — The vertebrates' cycle, as its own chapter

After the invertebrates' life cycles, Iraq's Grade 4 book gives the **vertebrate animals** their own life-cycle chapter. Vertebrates are the animals **with backbones** — the grouping taught in the grouping-animals lessons — and their lives run in cycles: young, growth, maturity, and new young of the same kind.

The book's order is the teaching: invertebrate cycles first, vertebrate cycles as their own chapter after — each great group of animals gets its cycle studied on its own ground.

## Sub lesson 2 — The activity book's matching work

The Grade 4 activity book carries an **activity on the life cycles of vertebrate animals** — the cycle worked stage by stage, as the pupil's book teaches it.

Worked in order, the stages make the cycle visible: where the young of a vertebrate starts, how it grows, and where the cycle begins again.

## The key ideas

- **Vertebrates** are the animals with backbones, and their lives run in **cycles**.
- Iraq's Grade 4 book teaches vertebrate life cycles as their own chapter, after the invertebrates'.
- The activity book works the same cycle as an activity, stage by stage.
