# Animals — reptiles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the reptile groups.
**Answer:** Turtles and tortoises, crocodiles and alligators, lizards and snakes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three features of a reptile.
**Answer:** Any three of: dry rough scaly skin, cold-blooded, lungs, head-neck-trunk-tail, mostly four legs, creeping/burrowing land life.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain a lizard's day: rock in the morning, shade at noon.
**Answer:** As a cold-blooded animal it warms up by basking in morning sun and avoids overheating by seeking afternoon shade.
