# Animals — reptiles — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Snakes are reptiles even though they have no legs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A tortoise is an amphibian.
**Answer: False — tortoises are reptiles.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Basking is a way a cold-blooded animal controls its temperature.
**Answer: True**
