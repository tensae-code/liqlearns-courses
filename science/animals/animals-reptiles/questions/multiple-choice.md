# Animals — reptiles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reptile skin is —
a) smooth and moist  b) dry, rough and scaly  c) feathered
**Answer: b) dry, rough and scaly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Reptiles breathe with —
a) gills  b) lungs  c) their skin only
**Answer: b) lungs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Reptiles are —
a) warm-blooded  b) cold-blooded  c) always the same temperature
**Answer: b) cold-blooded**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a reptile?
a) frog  b) crocodile  c) toad
**Answer: b) crocodile**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A lizard basks in the morning to —
a) warm its body  b) lay eggs  c) shed its skin daily
**Answer: a) warm its body**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the hot afternoon a lizard —
a) basks more  b) seeks shade to cool down  c) enters water to breathe
**Answer: b) seeks shade to cool down**
