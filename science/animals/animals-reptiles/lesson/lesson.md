# Animals — reptiles

## Do it first

A lizard sits on a sunny rock in the morning and hides in shade at noon. Is it being lazy? Write what you think it is really doing.

## Sub lesson 1 — The reptile plan

Reptiles creep or burrow on land. Their skin is dry, rough and scaly. They are cold-blooded and breathe with lungs. The body runs head, neck, trunk and tail, and most have four legs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reptile kinds and temperature

The groups are turtles and tortoises, crocodiles and alligators, lizards and snakes. Because a reptile is cold-blooded, a lizard basks on a morning rock to warm up and seeks afternoon shade to cool down — temperature control by behaviour.

## Say it to your companion

Explain in your own words: reptiles are cold-blooded land animals with dry, rough, scaly skin that breathe with lungs — turtles and tortoises, crocodiles and alligators, lizards and snakes. Give one example of your own for each part, and let your companion challenge you with one hard case.
