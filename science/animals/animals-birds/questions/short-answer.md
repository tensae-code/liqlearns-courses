# Animals — birds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the bird body plan.
**Answer:** Beak, two wings, two legs, feathers; warm-blooded; toothless.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match three beaks to their foods.
**Answer:** Any three of: seed-crushing, fruit-scooping, flesh-tearing, nectar-sipping, wood-pecking, each with its beak shape.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a bird manage food without teeth?
**Answer:** The beak takes and prepares the food and the digestive system handles it.
