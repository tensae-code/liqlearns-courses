# Animals — birds — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Most birds rear their young.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every bird can fly.
**Answer: False — most fly; the ostrich does not.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A wood-pecking beak is adapted to its food.
**Answer: True**
