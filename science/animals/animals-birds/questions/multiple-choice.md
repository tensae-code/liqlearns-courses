# Animals — birds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Feathers are found on —
a) birds only  b) all flying animals  c) mammals too
**Answer: a) birds only**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Birds are —
a) cold-blooded  b) warm-blooded  c) ectothermic
**Answer: b) warm-blooded**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A bird has —
a) teeth  b) a beak and no teeth  c) gills
**Answer: b) a beak and no teeth**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A short thick beak fits —
a) sipping nectar  b) crushing seeds  c) tearing flesh
**Answer: b) crushing seeds**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A long thin beak fits —
a) sipping nectar  b) pecking wood  c) scooping fruit only
**Answer: a) sipping nectar**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The bird that does not fly, named in this lesson, is the —
a) ostrich  b) hen  c) eagle
**Answer: a) ostrich**
