# Hands-on — Animals — birds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beak watch
Watch birds feeding outdoors. Sketch each beak and write the food it is taking. Do not feed or chase them.

## Tool-beaks
Try picking up seeds, torn paper 'flesh' and water with tweezers, a spoon and a clothespin. Which tool matches which beak?
