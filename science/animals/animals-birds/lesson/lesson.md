# Animals — birds

## Do it first

You find a bird with a long thin beak and one with a short thick beak. Which one cracks seeds, and which one sips nectar? Decide before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — The bird plan

A bird has a beak, two wings, two legs and feathers — and feathers are uniquely birds'. Birds are warm-blooded. Most fly, but the ostrich does not. Most rear their young. A bird has no teeth: the beak and the digestive system handle its food.

## Sub lesson 2 — Beaks fit food

Beaks are adapted to food: seed-crushing, fruit-scooping, flesh-tearing, nectar-sipping and wood-pecking. Read a bird's beak and you can predict what it eats.

## Say it to your companion

Explain in your own words: birds are warm-blooded animals with a beak, two wings, two legs and feathers — feathers belong to birds alone — and beaks shaped to their food. Give one example of your own for each part, and let your companion challenge you with one hard case.
