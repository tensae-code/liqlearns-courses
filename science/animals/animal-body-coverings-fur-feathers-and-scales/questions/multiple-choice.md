# Animal body coverings — fur, feathers and scales — multiple choice


**1.** A mammal's body is covered in…
a) Scales  b) Fur  c) Feathers  d) A shell
**Answer: b) Fur**

**2.** A bird's body is covered in…
a) Fur  b) Scales  c) Feathers  d) Skin only
**Answer: c) Feathers**

**3.** Fish and reptiles are covered in…
a) Feathers  b) Fur  c) Wool  d) Scales
**Answer: d) Scales**

**4.** Fur helps a mammal to…
a) Fly  b) Keep warm  c) Breathe under water  d) See in the dark
**Answer: b) Keep warm**

**5.** Scales protect a fish like…
a) A blanket  b) Small armour plates  c) Wings  d) Roots
**Answer: b) Small armour plates**

**6.** A turtle carries its protection as a…
a) Feather coat  b) Shell  c) Scale fin  d) Fur hood
**Answer: b) Shell**
