# Animal body coverings — fur, feathers and scales — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Mammals are covered in _____.
**Answer: fur**

**2.** Birds are covered in _____.
**Answer: feathers**

**3.** Fish and reptiles are covered in _____.
**Answer: scales**

**4.** A covering _____ the animal's body.
**Answer: protects**
