# Animal body coverings — fur, feathers and scales — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the covering of a sheep, a bird and a fish.
**Answer: A sheep has fur (wool), a bird has feathers, and a fish has scales.**

**2.** What two jobs do feathers do for a bird?
**Answer: Feathers keep a bird warm and dry, and they let it fly.**

**3.** Why are scales compared to armour?
**Answer: Because they are hard, overlapping plates that protect the body underneath.**

**4.** How does a covering fit where an animal lives?
**Answer: It suits the home — for example short light fur in a hot desert and smooth scales in water.**
