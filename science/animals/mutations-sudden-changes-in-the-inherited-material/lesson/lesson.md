## Sub lesson 1 — A sudden change in the inherited material

Yemen's Grade 9 Science book defines a mutation as a sudden change in the inherited material — the material itself is altered, all at once, and the change can then be passed on.

## Sub lesson 2 — An extra chromosome — Patau's pattern

The book tells it through a child born with an extra chromosome 13 — Patau's pattern. In division, a chromosome pair fails to part, and the child carries 47 chromosomes instead of 46: one copying accident at division becomes a whole extra chromosome in every cell.

## Sub lesson 3 — What can trigger a mutation

The book names the agents that can trigger such changes: ultraviolet rays and X-rays, chemicals, and mustard gas. Radiation and chemicals reach the inherited material itself — that is why they can start a mutation.
