# Hands-on — Mutations — sudden changes in the inherited material

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Write the division accident step by step: a chromosome pair should part — it fails to part — one cell gets both — the child carries 47 chromosomes instead of 46. Then list the book's triggering agents and say, in one sentence, where each one reaches.
