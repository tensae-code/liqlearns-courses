# Mutations — sudden changes in the inherited material — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: A sudden change in the inherited material.
**Answer:** Yemen's Grade 9 Science book defines a mutation as a sudden change in the inherited material — the material itself is altered, all at once, and the change can then be passed on..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: An extra chromosome — Patau's pattern.
**Answer:** The book tells it through a child born with an extra chromosome 13 — Patau's pattern.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: What can trigger a mutation.
**Answer:** The book names the agents that can trigger such changes: ultraviolet rays and X-rays, chemicals, and mustard gas.

**Answer:** A sudden change in the inherited material → An extra chromosome — Patau's pattern → What can trigger a mutation — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
