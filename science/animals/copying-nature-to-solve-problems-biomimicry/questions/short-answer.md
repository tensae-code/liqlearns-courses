# Copying nature to solve problems — biomimicry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: External parts are nature's solutions.
**Answer:** Plants and animals already solve hard problems with their external parts: hooks that catch and hold, coatings that shed water, shapes that grip, dig, glide or hide (Grade 2, 2.2.4, printed p. 68).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Mimic the part to solve a human problem.
**Answer:** The design move is mimicking: copy the way the plant or animal part works, and turn it into a solution to a human problem.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** External parts are nature's solutions → Mimic the part to solve a human problem — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
