# Hands-on — Copying nature to solve problems — biomimicry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a part as a solution
Choose one external part — a burr, a webbed foot, a cactus coating, a bird's wing. Write the problem it solves and how it solves it.

## Design by mimicking
Pick a human problem — fastening, carrying water, gripping ice. Sketch a solution that mimics the part from the first activity, and label the copied working.
