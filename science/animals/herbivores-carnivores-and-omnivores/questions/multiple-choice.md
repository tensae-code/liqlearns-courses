# Herbivores, carnivores and omnivores — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Animals that eat only plants are called…
a) Herbivores  b) Carnivores  c) Omnivores  d) Predators
**Answer: a) Herbivores**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Animals that eat other animals are called…
a) Herbivores  b) Carnivores  c) Omnivores  d) Grazers
**Answer: b) Carnivores**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) An omnivore eats…
a) Only plants  b) Only other animals  c) Both plants and animals  d) Only seeds
**Answer: c) Both plants and animals**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Bulgaria's book calls plants…
a) Green factories  b) Nature's cleaners  c) Food chains  d) Green animals
**Answer: a) Green factories**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) To place an animal in its group, you ask…
a) How big is it?  b) Where does it live?  c) What does it eat?  d) How fast is it?
**Answer: c) What does it eat?**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A large animal that eats only grass and leaves is a…
a) Carnivore  b) Herbivore  c) Omnivore  d) Hunter
**Answer: b) Herbivore**
