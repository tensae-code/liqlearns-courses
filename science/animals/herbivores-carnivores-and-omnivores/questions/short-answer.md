# Herbivores, carnivores and omnivores — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three groups of animals by food and say what each group eats.
**Answer:** Herbivores eat plants; carnivores eat other animals; omnivores eat both plants and animals.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does Bulgaria's book teach these groups inside the food-chain lesson?
**Answer:** Because the group is a place in the chain: plants make the food, herbivores eat the plants, carnivores eat other animals — the menu is the animal's link in the chain.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A friend says a big animal must be a carnivore. Correct them.
**Answer:** Size does not decide the group — the food does. A big animal that eats only plants is a herbivore.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Place these by group and defend each placing: an animal that eats leaves, one that hunts other animals, one that eats fruit and insects.
**Answer:** Leaves — herbivore; hunts other animals — carnivore; fruit and insects — omnivore (both plants and animals).
