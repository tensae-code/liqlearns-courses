# Herbivores, carnivores and omnivores — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Animals that eat plants are called _____.
**Answer: herbivores**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Animals that eat both plants and animals are called _____.
**Answer: omnivores**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Every food chain starts with a _____.
**Answer: plant**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The group an animal belongs to is read off its _____.
**Answer: food (menu)**
