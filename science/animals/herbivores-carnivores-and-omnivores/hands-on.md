# Hands-on — Herbivores, carnivores and omnivores

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the animals you know
List ten animals you know. Beside each, write what it eats, then its group — herbivore, carnivore or omnivore. Check each placing by the menu alone.

## Watch one animal eat
Watch an animal feeding — a pet, a farm animal, or birds outside. Write down everything it eats in ten minutes and place it in its group from what you saw.
