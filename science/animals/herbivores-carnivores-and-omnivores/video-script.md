# Video script — Herbivores, carnivores and omnivores

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Herbivores, carnivores and omnivores** — Bulgaria's way: group every animal by its menu.

Every animal eats, but not every animal eats the same thing. Some eat only plants — those are the herbivores. Some eat other animals — those are the carnivores. And some eat both — those are the omnivores.

Bulgaria's Grade 3 book teaches the three groups where they belong: inside the food chain, which starts with plants, the green factories that make the food.

Now you. Name an animal, say what it eats, and name its group. Say it back — in your own words.
