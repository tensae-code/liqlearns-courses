Today: **Evolution and the origin of life** — Sri Lanka's way, from the books cited in the sources.

The story begins before any life: the Earth itself originated — formed as a planet — long before anything lived on it.

On that young Earth, life originated: living things arose where none had been, and the book treats the origin of life as the second step of the one process — first the planet, then the living world upon it..

From the first life, evolution is the continuing change of living organisms across generations — and Sri Lanka's book insists it has not stopped: the evolution of living organisms is taught as a continuing process, still running in every generation alive today..
