# Evolution and the origin of life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The origin of the Earth.
**Answer:** The story begins before any life: the **Earth itself originated** — formed as a planet — long before anything lived on it. Sri Lanka's book starts its evolution chapter here, with the origin of the Earth, because life's stage had to exist before life could appear on it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The origin of life.
**Answer:** On that young Earth, **life originated**: living things arose where none had been, and the book treats the origin of life as the second step of the one process — first the planet, then the living world upon it.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Evolution — a continuing process.
**Answer:** From the first life, **evolution** is the continuing change of living organisms across generations — and Sri Lanka's book insists it has not stopped: the evolution of living organisms is taught as a **continuing process**, still running in every generation alive today.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The origin of the Earth → The origin of life → Evolution — a continuing process — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
