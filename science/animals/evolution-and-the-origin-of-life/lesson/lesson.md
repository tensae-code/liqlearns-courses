# Evolution and the origin of life

## Do it first
Before you read: say out loud what you already think this lesson's one thing is — the evolutionary process — the origin of the Earth, the origin of life, and the evolution of living organisms as a continuing process. Then read on: this is Sri Lanka's way in — its Grade 9 Science Part I closes with the evolutionary process as one arc: the origin of the Earth, then the origin of life, then evolution still continuing.

## Sub lesson 1 — The origin of the Earth

The story begins before any life: the **Earth itself originated** — formed as a planet — long before anything lived on it. Sri Lanka's book starts its evolution chapter here, with the origin of the Earth, because life's stage had to exist before life could appear on it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The origin of life

On that young Earth, **life originated**: living things arose where none had been, and the book treats the origin of life as the second step of the one process — first the planet, then the living world upon it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Evolution — a continuing process

From the first life, **evolution** is the continuing change of living organisms across generations — and Sri Lanka's book insists it has not stopped: the evolution of living organisms is taught as a **continuing process**, still running in every generation alive today.
