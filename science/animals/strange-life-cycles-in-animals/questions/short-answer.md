# Strange life cycles in animals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why must the plain cycles be learned before the strange ones?
**Answer: Because strangeness is seen against the pattern: only a learner who knows the plain cycle can see where a strange one leaves it — and that it still completes.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the stages every cycle, plain or strange, must still pass.
**Answer: Young, growth, and new young of the same kind.**
