# Strange life cycles in animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A strange life cycle is one that…
a) Never completes  b) Completes itself by a surprising route  c) Has no young  d) Belongs to plants only
**Answer: b) Completes itself by a surprising route**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The reading comes…
a) Before any life cycle is taught  b) After the life-cycle chapters, as their close  c) In the maths book  d) In Grade 1
**Answer: b) After the life-cycle chapters, as their close**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) In a strange cycle, the young may be…
a) Exactly like the grown animal always  b) Unlike the grown animal  c) Made of stone  d) Invisible forever
**Answer: b) Unlike the grown animal**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Strange means…
a) Broken  b) Not a cycle  c) Surprising in its route, still a cycle  d) Impossible
**Answer: c) Surprising in its route, still a cycle**
