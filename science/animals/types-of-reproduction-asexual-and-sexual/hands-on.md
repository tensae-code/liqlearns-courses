# Hands-on — Types of reproduction — asexual and sexual

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the examples
Sort these into asexual and sexual using the one test — how many parents: a bacterium splitting; a strawberry runner; a hen's fertilised egg; a potato growing from an eye; a mango seed; yeast budding. For each, write the parent count and whether the offspring are copies or varied.
