# Types of reproduction — asexual and sexual — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is the one test that separates the two types?
**Answer: How many parents: one parent means asexual, two parents joining cells means sexual.**

**2.** Give two examples of asexual reproduction.
**Answer: A moneran splitting in two and a plant growing from a runner or cutting (yeast budding too).**

**3.** Why does sexual reproduction produce varied offspring?
**Answer: Because the offspring's hereditary material comes from two different parents and is mixed, so the young differ from each parent.**

**4.** Why does variation matter?
**Answer: It is what heredity passes on and selection acts on — without variation, a kind cannot adapt when its home changes.**
