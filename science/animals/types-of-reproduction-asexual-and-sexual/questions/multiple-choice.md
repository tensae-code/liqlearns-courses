# Types of reproduction — asexual and sexual — multiple choice


**1.** Asexual reproduction has…
a) Two parents  b) One parent  c) No offspring  d) Only seeds
**Answer: b) One parent**

**2.** Offspring of asexual reproduction are…
a) All different  b) Copies of the parent  c) Half animal half plant  d) Always seeds
**Answer: b) Copies of the parent**

**3.** Sexual reproduction joins cells from…
a) One parent  b) Two parents  c) A rock  d) The soil
**Answer: b) Two parents**

**4.** A bacterium splitting in two is…
a) Sexual reproduction  b) Asexual reproduction  c) Not reproduction  d) Fertilisation
**Answer: b) Asexual reproduction**

**5.** Offspring of sexual reproduction…
a) Are exact copies  b) Differ from both parents  c) Never resemble parents  d) Are always plants
**Answer: b) Differ from both parents**

**6.** Variation among offspring comes mainly from…
a) Asexual reproduction  b) Sexual reproduction  c) Sleeping  d) Eating
**Answer: b) Sexual reproduction**
