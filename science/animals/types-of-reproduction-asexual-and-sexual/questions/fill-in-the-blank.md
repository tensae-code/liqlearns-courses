# Types of reproduction — asexual and sexual — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** One parent, copies: _____ reproduction.
**Answer: asexual**

**2.** Two parents, varied young: _____ reproduction.
**Answer: sexual**

**3.** In plants, pollen joining the egg cell is _____.
**Answer: fertilisation**

**4.** Asexual offspring are _____ of the parent.
**Answer: copies**
