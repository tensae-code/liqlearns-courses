# Classification — scientific names and the seven ranks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who standardised the two-part naming system, and when?
**Answer:** Linnaeus, in the 1750s.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the human scientific name correctly and label its two parts.
**Answer:** Homo (genus) sapiens (species) — genus capitalised, both parts italic or underlined.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** List the seven ranks in order from widest to narrowest.
**Answer:** Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species.
