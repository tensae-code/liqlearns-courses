# Classification — scientific names and the seven ranks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A scientific name has —
a) one part  b) two parts: genus and species  c) five parts
**Answer: b) two parts: genus and species**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Homo sapiens is the scientific name of —
a) the cat  b) humans  c) the dog
**Answer: b) humans**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** By hand, a scientific name is written —
a) in capitals  b) underlined  c) in brackets
**Answer: b) underlined** — in print it is italic.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The first (widest) rank is —
a) Species  b) Genus  c) Kingdom
**Answer: c) Kingdom**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The last (narrowest) rank is —
a) Species  b) Family  c) Order
**Answer: a) Species**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The rank just above Genus is —
a) Class  b) Family  c) Phylum
**Answer: b) Family**
