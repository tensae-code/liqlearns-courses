# Video script — Classification — scientific names and the seven ranks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: every organism has one scientific name of two parts — genus and species — and sits in seven ranks from Kingdom down to Species.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
