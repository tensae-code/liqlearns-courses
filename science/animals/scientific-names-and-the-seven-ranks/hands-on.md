# Hands-on — Classification — scientific names and the seven ranks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name cards
Make cards for human, cat and two local organisms. Write the scientific name where you know it, correctly formatted, and the local common names beside it.

## Ladder in string
Lay seven narrowing hoops or chalk rings. Place organism cards in the widest ring they share, narrowing to one species.
