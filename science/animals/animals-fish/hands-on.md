# Hands-on — Animals — fish

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fin watch
Watch a fish in a tank, a river pool or a market bowl. Sketch it, label gills, fins and scales, and note which fin moves when it turns.

## Tail-beat mime
With a partner, mime the swimming strip: tail beating, fins steering. Say each part's job as you move it.
