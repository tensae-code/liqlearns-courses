# Animals — fish

## Do it first

Hold your breath and imagine living under water. What body parts would you need to breathe, move and balance there? List them before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — The fish body

A fish lives in water and breathes through its gills. Its body is covered in scales. It is cold-blooded — ectothermic — so its body temperature follows the temperature of the water around it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Moving and balancing

Fins move the fish and keep its balance in the water. Watch the tail beat side to side to push the fish forward while the other fins steer and steady it. [GIF 32917 — a fish swimming]

[GIF 32917 — a fish's fins and tail beating as it swims]

## Say it to your companion

Explain in your own words: fish are cold-blooded aquatic animals that breathe through gills, move and balance with fins, and are covered in scales. Give one example of your own for each part, and let your companion challenge you with one hard case.
