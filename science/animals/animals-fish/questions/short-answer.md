# Animals — fish — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three features of a fish.
**Answer:** Any three of: aquatic, gills, fins, scales, cold-blooded/ectothermic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two jobs do fins do?
**Answer:** Moving and balancing.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can a fish stay under water without coming up for air?
**Answer:** It breathes through its gills, taking oxygen from the water.
