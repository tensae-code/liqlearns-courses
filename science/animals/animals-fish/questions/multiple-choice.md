# Animals — fish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fish breathes through its —
a) lungs  b) gills  c) skin only
**Answer: b) gills**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A fish's body is covered in —
a) feathers  b) hair  c) scales
**Answer: c) scales**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cold-blooded (ectothermic) means —
a) the body temperature follows the water  b) the blood is always cold to touch  c) the fish lives only in cold water
**Answer: a) the body temperature follows the water**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fins help a fish to —
a) breathe  b) move and balance  c) lay eggs
**Answer: b) move and balance**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A fish lives —
a) in water  b) only on land  c) in trees
**Answer: a) in water**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The tail of a swimming fish —
a) stays still  b) beats to push it forward  c) holds air
**Answer: b) beats to push it forward**
