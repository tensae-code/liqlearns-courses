# Animals — fish — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fish are warm-blooded.
**Answer: False — fish are cold-blooded (ectothermic).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fins are only for decoration.
**Answer: False — fins move and balance the fish.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Fish breathe through gills.
**Answer: True**
