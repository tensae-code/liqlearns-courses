## Sub lessons
1. **Camouflage — hiding in plain sight** — **Camouflage** is an animal's colours and patterns matching the place where it rests, so that the animal blends in and is hard to see. The animal has not left and has not covered itself — its own body does the hiding.
2. **Why camouflage matters** — Camouflage matters because being seen is dangerous. An insect that blends into its resting place is **harder for the animals that would eat it to find** — it survives because it is overlooked.
