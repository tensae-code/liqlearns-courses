# Hands-on — Camouflage — how animals hide

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the hidden one
A friend hides drawings of insects on matching and non-matching backgrounds around the room. Time how long each takes to spot, and say what the times prove about camouflage.

## Match the resting place
Colour an insect outline to match a real leaf or piece of bark, then lay it on that place and step back. Say, from across the room, whether the camouflage works — and what gives it away if it does not.
