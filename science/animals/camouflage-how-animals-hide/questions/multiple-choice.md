# Camouflage — how animals hide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Camouflage is…
a) Running away quickly  b) Colours and patterns matching the resting place, so the animal is hard to see  c) Making a loud noise  d) Digging a hole
**Answer: b) Colours and patterns matching the resting place, so the animal is hard to see**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Barbados's syllabus teaches camouflage through…
a) Fish  b) Birds only  c) Insects  d) Farm animals
**Answer: c) Insects**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A camouflaged animal hides…
a) Underground only  b) In plain sight, on its resting place  c) Only at night in a burrow  d) Inside another animal
**Answer: b) In plain sight, on its resting place**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Camouflage matters because it…
a) Makes the animal prettier  b) Helps the animal survive — hunters overlook it  c) Makes the animal faster  d) Keeps the animal warm in winter only
**Answer: b) Helps the animal survive — hunters overlook it**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) An insect on a leaf is camouflaged when…
a) It is bright red on green  b) Its colours match the leaf  c) It sings loudly  d) It is the biggest insect there
**Answer: b) Its colours match the leaf**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Camouflage is a kind of…
a) Food  b) Survival feature  c) Nest  d) Season
**Answer: b) Survival feature**
