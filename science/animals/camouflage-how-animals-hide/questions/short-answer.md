# Camouflage — how animals hide — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define camouflage.
**Answer: Camouflage is an animal's colours and patterns matching the place where it rests, so the animal blends in and is hard to see.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does camouflage matter?
**Answer: Because being seen is dangerous: a camouflaged animal is harder for the animals that would eat it to find, so it survives by being overlooked.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Give the syllabus's kind of example.
**Answer: Insects whose colours and shapes match the leaves, stems and ground they rest on.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) How is a camouflaged animal different from one that runs away or digs?
**Answer: It neither leaves nor covers itself — its own body's colours and patterns do the hiding, in plain sight.**
