Today: **Camouflage — how animals hide** — Barbados's way: hiding in plain sight.

An insect sits on a leaf and you cannot see it. Its colours and patterns match the leaf — that matching is camouflage. The animal has not run and has not dug: its body does the hiding. And it matters, because the animal that is overlooked is the animal that survives.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
