# The life cycle of a silkworm — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The silkworm's life begins as an _____.
**Answer: egg**

**2.** A growing silkworm sheds its skin in a _____.
**Answer: moult**

**3.** The larva spins a _____ of silk around itself.
**Answer: cocoon**

**4.** Egg, larva, pupa, moth is a complete change called _____.
**Answer: metamorphosis**
