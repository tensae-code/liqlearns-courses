# The life cycle of a silkworm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The silkworm's life begins as…
a) An egg laid by the moth  b) A cocoon  c) A pupa  d) A leaf
**Answer: a) An egg laid by the moth**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The young silkworm that hatches is a…
a) Moth  b) Larva, hungry for mulberry leaves  c) Pupa  d) Spider
**Answer: b) Larva, hungry for mulberry leaves**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A growing silkworm moults because…
a) It is cold  b) It wants to fly  c) Its skin does not stretch as it grows  d) The leaves change
**Answer: c) Its skin does not stretch as it grows**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Inside its cocoon, the silkworm becomes a…
a) Egg  b) Moth at once  c) Second larva  d) Pupa
**Answer: d) Pupa**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The whole cycle in order is…
a) Egg, larva, pupa, moth  b) Egg, moth, larva, pupa  c) Larva, egg, pupa, moth  d) Pupa, egg, moth, larva
**Answer: a) Egg, larva, pupa, moth**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The moth's part in the cycle is to…
a) Spin the cocoon  b) Mate and lay the eggs that begin the cycle again  c) Eat the leaves  d) Moult
**Answer: b) Mate and lay the eggs that begin the cycle again**
