# The life cycle of a silkworm — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the silkworm's four stages in order.
**Answer:** Egg, larva, pupa, moth — the egg hatches into the larva, the larva becomes a pupa inside its cocoon, and the pupa emerges as the moth.

**2.** What is a moult, and why does the silkworm need several?
**Answer:** Shedding the old skin and growing a new, larger one; the larva's skin does not stretch, so each stage of growth needs a moult before growing can continue.

**3.** What happens inside the cocoon?
**Answer:** The larva rests as a pupa while its body remakes itself, until it is ready to emerge as a moth.

**4.** Why is the silkworm China's own example for this lesson?
**Answer:** Because it is the sericulture animal — raised in China for its silk for thousands of years — so its cycle is taught the way a silkworm keeper meets it, from the egg.
