# Hands-on — The life cycle of a silkworm

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Order the stages
On four cards, draw the egg, the larva, the pupa in its cocoon, and the moth. Shuffle them, then lay them in life order and explain each change to someone.

## Watch a moulting animal
If you can observe any growing insect safely from outside its container (never handling wild animals), note any shed skin you find and say which stage of its life the skin belongs to.
