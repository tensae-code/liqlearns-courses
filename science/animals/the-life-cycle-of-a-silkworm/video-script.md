Today: **The life cycle of a silkworm** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

An egg breaks, and a tiny silkworm crawls out, hungry for mulberry leaves. It eats and grows — and when its skin will not stretch, it moults, again and again.
Then it spins a silk cocoon and rests inside as a pupa, until a moth pushes out, lays eggs, and the whole circle begins again.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
