# Hands-on — Inheritance — genes and traits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Inherited or acquired?
List six traits in your family or class — eye colour, height, a learned language, a scar, a musical skill, hair type. Sort the list into two columns, inherited and acquired, and say what decides each column: only the inherited column can pass to the next generation.

## Run Mendel's cross on paper
Draw the cross tall × short with two forms each, T and t. Fill the four boxes for the first generation, then cross that generation with itself and count: how many of the four boxes show tall, and how many show short? Say which form is dominant and which is recessive, and what the 3-to-1 count proves.
