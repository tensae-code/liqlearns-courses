Today: **Inheritance — genes and traits** — India's way: Mendel's peas.

A child looks like its parents because traits travel in genes — the instructions carried from parent to young. But a skill you practise all your life does not travel: that is acquired, and it stops with you. Mendel crossed tall peas with short peas and the short form hid for a generation, then came back — one in four. The form that shows is dominant; the one that hides is recessive. And when inherited differences build up over many generations, kinds themselves change — that is evolution.
