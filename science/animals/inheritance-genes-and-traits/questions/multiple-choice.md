# Inheritance — genes and traits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An inherited trait is…
a) Learned during life  b) Carried in the genes from parents to young  c) Caused by exercise  d) A scar
**Answer: b) Carried in the genes from parents to young**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is an acquired trait?
a) Eye colour  b) Flower colour  c) A learned skill  d) The shape of a leaf
**Answer: c) A learned skill**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A gene shows itself by…
a) Directing the making of a protein  b) Growing during life  c) Copying a learned skill  d) Changing the weather
**Answer: a) Directing the making of a protein**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) When Mendel crossed tall peas with short peas, the first generation was…
a) All short  b) Half tall, half short  c) All tall  d) All medium
**Answer: c) All tall**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the second generation, the short form returned in about…
a) Every plant  b) One plant in four  c) One plant in a hundred  d) No plant at all
**Answer: b) One plant in four**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The form that hides when the other form is present is called…
a) Dominant  b) Acquired  c) Recessive  d) Selected
**Answer: c) Recessive**

**7.** (Sub lesson 2) In humans, a child is a son when the father gives…
a) An X chromosome  b) A protein  c) A Y chromosome  d) Two X chromosomes
**Answer: c) A Y chromosome**

**8.** (Sub lesson 3) Evolution runs on…
a) Acquired traits only  b) Inherited variation kept by selection over generations  c) Skills learned in one life  d) Scars and built muscles
**Answer: b) Inherited variation kept by selection over generations**
