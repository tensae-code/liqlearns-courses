# Inheritance — genes and traits — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Traits carried from parents to young in the genes are called _____ traits.
**Answer: inherited**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A _____ is a section of DNA carrying the instruction for one piece of the body.
**Answer: gene**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The form of a trait that shows when both forms are present is _____.
**Answer: dominant**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In Mendel's second generation, tall plants outnumbered short plants by about _____ to one.
**Answer: three (3-to-1)**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The long change of kinds, driven by inherited variation and selection, is called _____.
**Answer: evolution**
