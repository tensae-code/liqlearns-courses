# Hands-on — Looking after our animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The carer's week
Choose one animal and keep its four rules for a week, logging each day: fed, watered, shelter, health, handling. The log is your certificate.

## Fix one fail
From your inspection, choose the worst fail you are allowed to fix — a dirty water pot, an unswept corner — and fix it. Before and after drawings.

## Teach a younger carer
Show a younger child the four rules on a real animal. Their questions will find the rules you only half know.
