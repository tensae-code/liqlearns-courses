# Looking after our animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is NOT one of the four care rules?**
a) enough food and water  b) a clean shelter  c) teaching the animal to read
**Answer: c) teaching the animal to read**

**2. When an animal is sick, the book's rule is —**
a) sell it at once  b) give it medical care  c) leave it alone
**Answer: b) give it medical care**

**3. A clean shelter is —**
a) optional  b) one of the four care rules  c) only for cows
**Answer: b) one of the four care rules**

**4. Handling an animal the calm, knowing way protects —**
a) only the animal  b) only the person  c) both the person and the animal
**Answer: c) both the person and the animal**
