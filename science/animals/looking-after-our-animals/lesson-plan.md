# Lesson plan — Looking after our animals

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Animals · **Lesson:** Looking after our animals
**Place in the curriculum:** Animals — the carer's lesson (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cared-for animals get enough food and water, a clean shelter, medical care when sick, and handling that does not hurt them — and cared-for animals give more benefit
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What animals need and animals at work — the bargain tabled; this lesson is the keeping of it.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Animals — What animals need, and animals at work for people. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the four care rules: enough food and water; a clean shelter; medical care; handling animals without getting hurt (and without hurting them).
2. Say the book's promise: cared-for animals give more benefit.

## What you need
One animal you can help care for today; clean water, feed, and a broom for the shelter.

## The run of the lesson (in order)

## If the learner is stuck
- Cleans the shelter but forgets water → the rules are a set of four; the inspection fails on any one missing.
- Grabs and drags an animal → handling is a rule, not a style: calm, gentle, with the owner's way shown first.
