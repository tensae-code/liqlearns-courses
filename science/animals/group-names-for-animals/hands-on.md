# Hands-on — Group names for animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Look for groups of animals near your home or school — birds, ants, fish, hens, dogs. For each group you find, say its group name from the lesson out loud: herd, school, flock, brood, litter, colony, hive or army. Write the animal and its group name in pairs.
