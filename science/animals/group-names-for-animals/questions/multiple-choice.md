# Group names for animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Herds, schools and flocks  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Herds, schools and flocks**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Broods, litters and colonies  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Broods, litters and colonies**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Hives and armies  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Hives and armies**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the names for groups of animals — a herd of cows, a school of fish, a flock of birds, a brood of hens, a litter of cats and dogs, a colony of ants, a hive of bees, an army of caterpillars. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Fiji, Year 2 — *Elementary Science Year 2 — FALD: Learning to Know, Notes and Activities*, printed pp. 26-28  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
