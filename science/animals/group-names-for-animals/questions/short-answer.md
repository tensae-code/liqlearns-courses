# Group names for animals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Herds, schools and flocks.
**Answer:** A group of animals often has its own name.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Broods, litters and colonies.
**Answer:** The young and the small have their own names too: hens with their young are a brood of hens, cats and dogs born together are a litter of cats and dogs, and ants living and working together are a colony of ants..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Hives and armies.
**Answer:** Two more names close the book's list: bees together are a hive of bees — the name of their home and of their company together — and caterpillars on the march are an army of caterpillars.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Herds, schools and flocks → Broods, litters and colonies → Hives and armies — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
