# Group names for animals

## Do it first
Before you read: say, in one sentence, what you think this lesson's one thing is, from its title alone — **Group names for animals**. Then read on: this is Fiji's way in — its book teaches this thing at the place cited in the sources, in the order below.

## Sub lesson 1 — Herds, schools and flocks

A group of animals often has its own name. Fiji's Year 2 book teaches them inside its Family theme: cows together are a **herd of cows**, fish together are a **school of fish**, and birds together are a **flock of birds**. The name belongs to the kind of animal, not just to the number.

## Sub lesson 2 — Broods, litters and colonies

The young and the small have their own names too: hens with their young are a **brood of hens**, cats and dogs born together are a **litter of cats and dogs**, and ants living and working together are a **colony of ants**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hives and armies

Two more names close the book's list: bees together are a **hive of bees** — the name of their home and of their company together — and caterpillars on the march are an **army of caterpillars**. Eight group names in all, each one learned with its animal.
