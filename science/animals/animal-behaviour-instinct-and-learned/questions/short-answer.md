# Animal behaviour — instinct and learned — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the two kinds of behaviour, as Syria's book sorts them.
**Answer:** Innate behaviour — what an animal is born able to do — and learned behaviour — what it picks up through training.

**2.** Give three examples of innate behaviour from the book.
**Answer:** Any three of: fighting, seeking shelter, feeding, reproduction, social life, caring for the young, exploring, escaping enemies.

**3.** Give two examples of learned behaviour from the book, and say what makes them learned.
**Answer:** A parrot riding a bicycle and trained dolphins (or a cat that learns to open a door): none of these is in the animal at birth — each is picked up through training.

**4.** Why does the book teach behaviour inside its Adaptation and Behaviour unit?
**Answer:** Because behaviour is one of the ways living things fit where they live: what an animal does — innately or by learning — is part of how it survives in its environment.
