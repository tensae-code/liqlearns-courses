# Animal behaviour — instinct and learned — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Behaviour is sorted by Syria's book into…
a) Innate behaviour and learned behaviour  b) Singing and silent behaviour  c) Day behaviour and night behaviour  d) Wild behaviour and tame behaviour
**Answer: a) Innate behaviour and learned behaviour**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Innate behaviour is behaviour an animal…
a) Learns from a trainer  b) Is born able to do  c) Copies from people  d) Invents as an adult
**Answer: b) Is born able to do**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is innate behaviour?
a) A parrot riding a bicycle  b) A trained dolphin performing  c) A young animal escaping its enemies  d) A cat that has learned to open a door
**Answer: c) A young animal escaping its enemies**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Caring for the young is listed by the book as…
a) Learned behaviour  b) Innate behaviour  c) Trained behaviour  d) People's behaviour
**Answer: b) Innate behaviour**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) A cat that learns to open a door shows…
a) Innate behaviour  b) No behaviour  c) Learned behaviour picked up through experience and training  d) Instinct only
**Answer: c) Learned behaviour picked up through experience and training**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Trained dolphins perform behaviour that is…
a) Innate  b) Learned through training  c) Done only in the wild  d) Impossible for other animals
**Answer: b) Learned through training**
