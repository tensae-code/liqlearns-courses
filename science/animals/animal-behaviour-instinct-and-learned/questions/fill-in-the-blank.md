# Animal behaviour — instinct and learned — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Behaviour an animal is born with is called _____ behaviour.
**Answer: innate**

**2.** A parrot riding a bicycle is _____ behaviour.
**Answer: learned**

**3.** Escaping enemies is an example of _____ behaviour.
**Answer: innate**

**4.** Learned behaviour is picked up through _____.
**Answer: training**
