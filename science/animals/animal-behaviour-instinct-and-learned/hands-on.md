# Hands-on — Animal behaviour — instinct and learned

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch and sort behaviours
Watch animals around your home or school (from a distance, without disturbing them). Write down three things you see an animal do. For each one, decide: innate or learned? Say what tells you — could a newborn animal do it, or did this animal have to pick it up?

## The door test
Think of the book's cat that learns to open a door. Name one thing a pet or farm animal you know has learned to do, and describe the training or experience that taught it.
