Today: **Useful microorganisms in medicine, farming and industry** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 11 (Form 5) — *Sains Tingkatan 5*, printed p. 28).

Malaysia's Form 5 book maps **beneficial microorganisms across medicine, agriculture and industry**: **yeast and Lactobacillus** in food and drink, **recombinant E. coli making insulin**, **Penicillium making penicillin**, and **nitrifying bacteria in farming** — the same tiny life that can harm is put to work.

The book's own product is **eco-enzyme cleaner: a natural cleaning liquid fermented from fruit and vegetable waste**, compared with chemical detergents. **Lactobacillus serum** is given its uses: **compost, sewage treatment and livestock digestion**.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
