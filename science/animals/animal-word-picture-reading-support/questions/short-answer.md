# Animal word–picture reading support — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the Lässtöd, and how many animals does it name?
**Answer:** It is a picture–word table — a reading support — naming 24 animals, each shown as a picture with its name beside it.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How does a pupil use the table?
**Answer:** Picture first: the pupil names the animal from its picture, then meets the written word that goes with it — the picture carries the animal until the word can be read.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the material include this support?
**Answer:** So that pupils who cannot yet read can still name and work with the animals — the science work does not have to wait for reading.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) Name six animals from the table.
**Answer:** Any six of: bear, fox, moose, hare, snake, squirrel, hedgehog, cow, horse, sheep, pig, hen, rooster, bumblebee, bee, wasp, spider, ant, mosquito, swan, mallard, frog, perch, pike.
