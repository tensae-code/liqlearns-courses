# Animal word–picture reading support — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The Lässtöd is a picture–_____ table naming 24 animals.
**Answer: word**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The pupil works picture _____, word with it.
**Answer: first**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The picture carries the animal until the _____ can be read.
**Answer: word**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) The table lets pupils who cannot yet _____ still name the animals.
**Answer: read**
