# Hands-on — Animal word–picture reading support

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make your own table
Draw six animals from the 24 — say bear, fox, frog, hen, bee and pike — one in each box of a six-box grid, and write each animal's name beside its picture, copying from the lesson. This is your own Lässtöd for those six.

## Name it, then find the word
Cover the names with a strip of paper. Point at each picture and name the animal out loud. Then slide the strip away and check the word. Any animal you named wrong stays covered for one more round.

## Take the table to the portraits
Carry your table to the animal portraits (or pictures) you work with. Match each table picture to its big portrait and say the name in both places. The table and the portraits are made to work together — that is how Sweden's set is built.
