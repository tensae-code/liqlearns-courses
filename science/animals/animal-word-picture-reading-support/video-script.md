# Video script — Animal word–picture reading support

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Animal word–picture reading support** — Sweden's way: the picture carries the animal until the word can be read.

Look: a bear, in a picture, with its name right beside it. A fox. A moose. A frog. Twenty-four animals, every one with its picture and its word together.
You do not have to read the word first. You say the animal you see — then you look at its name, and the picture and the word begin to belong together.
That is the whole support: nobody has to wait until they can read before they can name the animals and join the work.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
