# Animal word–picture reading support — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's one sub lesson (The picture–word table — picture first, word with it), explains it in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 1 sub lesson — it is one short, single-skill lesson, so it stays one lesson with no sub-lesson split beyond its single part. Practice deepens inside the part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, naming table animals from the picture alone, then matching each animal to its written name, until all 24 can be named and matched.
