# Evolution — evidence and fossils — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Species change over time.
**Answer:** **Biological evolution** is species **changing over time**: a kind of living thing today is not exactly what that kind was long ago. Ecuador's Grade 9 book teaches evolution as this change over time — the starting fact the rest of the lesson's evidence must explain.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Evidence and fossils — a window on the past.
**Answer:** The change is known from its **evidence**. **Fossils** are that evidence's clearest form: they are a **window on the past**, showing living things that lived long ago, so the life of the past can be compared with the life of today. Where today's kind differs from its fossil form, the window shows the change evolution names.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Genetic variability — the beans experiment.
**Answer:** Evolution needs differences to work on, and those differences are present now: the book's **experiment with beans** shows **genetic variability** — beans of one kind are not all identical; they differ, and that differing inside one kind is the variability from which change over time can come.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Species change over time → Evidence and fossils — a window on the past → Genetic variability — the beans experiment — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
