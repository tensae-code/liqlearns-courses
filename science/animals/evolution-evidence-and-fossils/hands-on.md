# Hands-on — Evolution — evidence and fossils

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The beans variability test
Take a handful of beans of one kind, as in the book's experiment. Lay them out and compare them one by one: size, shape, colour and markings. Record how many differ from their neighbours and in what ways. The differences you can count in one handful are the lesson's genetic variability, seen with your own eyes.
