## Sub lesson 2 — DNA and RNA compared

Guía 1 evidence: DNA vs RNA compared — ribose vs deoxyribose, uracil vs thymine, single vs double chain; messenger RNA carries the copy to the cytoplasm for protein synthesis.

## Sub lesson 3 — DNA and RNA structure — bases, genes and paternity testing

DNA and RNA structure: nucleic acids found by Miescher (1869), Watson and Crick's spiral-ladder model, nitrogen bases, genes on chromosomes, and paternity testing by DNA comparison (Temas 2–3).

## Sub lesson 4 — Genotype and phenotype — crossing-over and mutations

Genotype and phenotype: the genotype stays constant through life and is transmitted to descendants; crossing-over exchanges genes between homologous chromosomes; genes altered by internal/external factors produce mutations (Tema 3).

## Sub lesson 5 — Extracting DNA from banana — how DNA structure influences beings

Guía 1: how DNA structure influences beings; extracting DNA from banana by filtering the mixture, proteins as large molecules of amino-acid chains built under DNA's instructions, and albinism as a genetic condition.
