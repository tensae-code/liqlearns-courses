# Growing mushrooms on straw — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Cultivating mushrooms the book's way: chop straw to 1–2 inches, boil 15–20 minutes, dry a day, then layer it in plastic bags with spawn between the layers, press, pierce holes, keep a week in a dark room until white mycelium covers the straw, water, and harvest from about 25 days; kannye (oyster) grown in Kathmandu from Chaitra to Kartik and gobre in the hills.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed pp. 34–36.**
