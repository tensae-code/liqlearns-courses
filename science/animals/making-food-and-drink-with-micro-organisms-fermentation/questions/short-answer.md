# Making food and drink with micro-organisms — fermentation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Yoghurt — lactic-acid bacteria at work.
**Answer:** Yoghurt is milk changed by lactic-acid bacteria, which turn the milk sugar lactose into lactic acid; the acid thickens and sours the milk.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Yeast — in beer and in bread.
**Answer:** Bread and beer are yeast's work: the yeast ferments sugars and gives off gas (and, in beer, alcohol), and the gas is what lifts the dough.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Yoghurt — lactic-acid bacteria at work → Yeast — in beer and in bread — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
