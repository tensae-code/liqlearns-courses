# Hands-on — Making food and drink with micro-organisms — fermentation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow a ferment
Watch bread dough rise or yoghurt set. Record times and temperatures, and write where the micro-organisms are working and what they are changing.

## Interview a maker
Ask a baker, brewer or yoghurt maker what happens if the mixture is too hot or too cold — match their answer to the lesson's chain.
