# Reproduction in living organisms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why is reproduction necessary for a kind to continue?
**Answer: Because every living thing dies; only by making offspring does the kind continue into the next generation.**

**2.** What words name the two generations?
**Answer: Parents — those that produce the young — and offspring — the young produced.**

**3.** How do monerans reproduce?
**Answer: By splitting — one moneran divides to make more monerans.**

**4.** Why does this lesson come before the types of reproduction?
**Answer: Because first the job must be understood — continuing the kind — and then the different ways of doing the job can be compared.**
