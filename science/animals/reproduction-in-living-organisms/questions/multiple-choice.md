# Reproduction in living organisms — multiple choice


**1.** Reproduction is…
a) Making food  b) Making new living things of the same kind  c) Growing bigger  d) Sleeping
**Answer: b) Making new living things of the same kind**

**2.** Without reproduction, every kind would…
a) Grow for ever  b) Die out  c) Turn into another kind  d) Stop eating
**Answer: b) Die out**

**3.** The young of a living thing are called its…
a) Parents  b) Offspring  c) Enemies  d) Seeds only
**Answer: b) Offspring**

**4.** Offspring generally _____ their parents.
a) Never resemble  b) Resemble  c) Eat  d) Fear
**Answer: b) Resemble**

**5.** A plant reproduces by making…
a) Stones  b) Seeds  c) Shadows  d) Rain
**Answer: b) Seeds**

**6.** Reproduction is one of the…
a) Signs of life  b) Kinds of rock  c) States of matter  d) Food groups
**Answer: a) Signs of life**
