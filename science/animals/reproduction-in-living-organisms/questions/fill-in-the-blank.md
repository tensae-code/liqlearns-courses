# Reproduction in living organisms — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Living things make more of their own kind by _____.
**Answer: reproduction**

**2.** The young are called _____.
**Answer: offspring**

**3.** Those that produce the young are the _____.
**Answer: parents**

**4.** Without reproduction, a kind would die _____.
**Answer: out**
