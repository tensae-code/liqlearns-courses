The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Reproduction in living organisms** — Kuwait's way: reproduction in living organisms — how every living thing makes more of its own kind, so life continues from generation to generation.

Reproduction is how living things make new living things of their own kind. A plant makes seeds that grow into plants like itself; animals produce young; even the smallest monerans split to make more monerans. Without reproduction, every kind would end with the death of its last member. Kuwait's Grade 6 book opens its reproduction lessons here, at the level of the whole living world, before dividing reproduction into its types in the next lesson. The order matters: first the job — continuing the kind — then the ways the job is done. The new living things are called offspring, and those that produced them are the parents. Offspring resemble their parents — that resemblance, and its small differences, is the heredity the course follows in the genes lessons. Reproduction is one of the signs of life named in the first Animals lessons; this lesson is where that sign gets its own full treatment: not eating, not growing, but the making of the next generation itself.
