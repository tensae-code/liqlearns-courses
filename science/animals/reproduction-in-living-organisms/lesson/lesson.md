## Sub lesson 1 — Life makes more life

**Reproduction** is how living things make new living things of their own kind. A plant makes seeds that grow into plants like itself; animals produce young; even the smallest monerans split to make more monerans. Without reproduction, every kind would end with the death of its last member.

Kuwait's Grade 6 book opens its reproduction lessons here, at the level of the whole living world, before dividing reproduction into its types in the next lesson. The order matters: first the job — continuing the kind — then the ways the job is done.

## Sub lesson 2 — Parents and offspring

The new living things are called **offspring**, and those that produced them are the **parents**. Offspring resemble their parents — that resemblance, and its small differences, is the heredity the course follows in the genes lessons.

Reproduction is one of the signs of life named in the first Animals lessons; this lesson is where that sign gets its own full treatment: not eating, not growing, but the making of the next generation itself.

## The key ideas

- Reproduction makes new living things of the same kind.
- Without reproduction, every kind would die out.
- Parents produce offspring, which resemble them.
- Reproduction is one of the signs of life.
