# Evolution and natural selection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Life appeared on Earth…
a) 3.8 thousand years ago  b) 3.8 million years ago  c) 3.8 billion years ago  d) 38 years ago
**Answer: c) 3.8 billion years ago**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The guide teaches life's long history as a…
a) Song  b) Timeline  c) Map  d) Recipe
**Answer: b) Timeline**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) On the timeline, human history is…
a) Most of the line  b) Half of the line  c) Only a tiny mark at the near end  d) Not on the line at all
**Answer: c) Only a tiny mark at the near end**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Evolution is…
a) One animal changing in its own lifetime  b) The slow changing of living things over very long time  c) Animals moving to a new home  d) A kind appearing in one day
**Answer: b) The slow changing of living things over very long time**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Natural selection keeps…
a) The differences that fit a living thing's place, because their owners live and leave young  b) Only the biggest animals  c) Whatever people choose  d) Nothing — all differences spread equally
**Answer: a) The differences that fit a living thing's place, because their owners live and leave young**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Over many generations, natural selection means a kind…
a) Stays exactly the same  b) Slowly becomes different from what it was  c) Disappears at once  d) Turns into people
**Answer: b) Slowly becomes different from what it was**
