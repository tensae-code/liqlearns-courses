# Evolution and natural selection — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) When did life appear on Earth, and how does the guide teach that length of time?
**Answer:** Life appeared on Earth 3.8 billion years ago; the guide teaches it as a timeline — a line of time with life's beginning far back and everything alive today at its near end.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What did that immense time produce?
**Answer:** The diversity of living things — the many different kinds, each fitted to its own place and way of living.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is natural selection?
**Answer:** Nature keeping what fits: living things whose differences fit their place live, grow and leave young behind, so those differences spread through the kind over generations.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) How do the timeline and natural selection join into one lesson?
**Answer:** The timeline gives the time — 3.8 billion years — and natural selection gives the way living things changed inside that time, into the diversity we see.
