Today: **Evolution and natural selection** — Gabon's way: begin with a timeline.

Life appeared on Earth 3.8 billion years ago. Draw that as a line of time: life begins at the far end, and everything alive today stands at the near end. Human history is only the last tiny mark on that line.
In all that time, living things changed, generation after generation. That slow changing is evolution. And the way it works is natural selection: living things differ, the ones whose differences fit their place live and leave young, and so the fitted differences spread — until, over many generations, the kind itself is different.
The timeline gives the time. Selection gives the way. Say it back to me.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
