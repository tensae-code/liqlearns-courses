# How the environment can change traits — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Traits are not inheritance alone.
**Answer:** A living thing's traits — its size, shape, colouring, condition — come from its parents, but they are not sealed there.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Explaining it from evidence.
**Answer:** The book's demand is an explanation from evidence: compare living things of a kind across different environments, observe the trait differences, and tie them to the environmental differences.
