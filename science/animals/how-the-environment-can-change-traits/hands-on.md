# Hands-on — How the environment can change traits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One kind, two environments
Grow two matching seedlings, one watered and fed, one kept short of water. Record one trait — height or leaf size — weekly, and write the explanation your evidence supports.

## Evidence table
Find two living things of one kind living in visibly different environments. Tabulate: trait, environment, and the difference the environment plausibly made.
