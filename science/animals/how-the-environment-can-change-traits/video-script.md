Today: **How the environment can change traits** — the United States' way, from its Utah SEEd Standards books (Grade 3).

A living thing's **traits** — its size, shape, colouring, condition — come from its parents, but they are not sealed there. Utah's Grade 3 standard 3.2.3 (printed p. 62) states the lesson's one thing plainly: **the environment can affect the traits of living things**. Where a thing lives — its food, water, light, space — leaves marks on the traits it shows.

The book's demand is an **explanation from evidence**: compare living things of a kind across different environments, observe the trait differences, and tie them to the environmental differences. Resemblance to parents explains part of a trait; the environment explains the rest — and which part is which is decided by the evidence, not guessed.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
