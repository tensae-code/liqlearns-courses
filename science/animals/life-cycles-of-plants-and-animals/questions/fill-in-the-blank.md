# Life cycles of plants and animals — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The four-stage cycle runs: egg, _____, pupa, adult.
**Answer: larva**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The stage in which the animal rests and changes completely is the _____.
**Answer: pupa**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The earthworm's cycle is jelly, _____, worm.
**Answer: cocoon**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The earthworm and the spider have no _____ stage.
**Answer: pupa**
