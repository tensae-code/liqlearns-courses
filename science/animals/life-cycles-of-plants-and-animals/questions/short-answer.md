# Life cycles of plants and animals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give the butterfly's life cycle in order, and say what happens at the pupa stage.
**Answer:** Egg, larva, pupa, butterfly. At the pupa stage the animal rests and changes completely, and what comes out is the adult butterfly.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name three more animals from Sweden's set of ten that travel the same four-stage road as the butterfly.
**Answer:** Any three of: bumblebee, fly, mosquito, ladybird, wasp, ant — each runs egg, larva, pupa, adult.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How is the spider's cycle different from the wasp's?
**Answer:** The wasp runs four stages — egg, larva, pupa, wasp — while the spider runs a simpler cycle — egg, cocoon, spider — with no larva and no pupa.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The grasshopper's stages are printed in the book as egg, pupa, larva, grasshopper. What does this lesson do with that printed order?
**Answer:** It carries it exactly as the book prints it — the lesson teaches the cycle as Sweden's book gives it, order and all.
