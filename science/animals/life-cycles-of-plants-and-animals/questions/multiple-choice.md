# Life cycles of plants and animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The four stages of the butterfly's cycle, in order, are…
a) Egg, pupa, larva, butterfly  b) Egg, larva, pupa, butterfly  c) Larva, egg, pupa, butterfly  d) Egg, larva, butterfly, pupa
**Answer: b) Egg, larva, pupa, butterfly**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The young stage that comes from the egg and does not look like its parent is the…
a) Pupa  b) Adult  c) Larva  d) Cocoon
**Answer: c) Larva**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these animals has a pupa stage?
a) Earthworm  b) Spider  c) Mosquito  d) None of them
**Answer: c) Mosquito**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The earthworm's cycle, as the book prints it, is…
a) Egg, larva, pupa, worm  b) Jelly, cocoon, worm  c) Egg, cocoon, pupa, worm  d) Cocoon, larva, worm
**Answer: b) Jelly, cocoon, worm**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The spider's cycle is…
a) Egg, larva, pupa, spider  b) Egg, cocoon, spider  c) Jelly, pupa, spider  d) Egg, pupa, larva, spider
**Answer: b) Egg, cocoon, spider**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The earthworm and the spider are grouped together in this lesson because both…
a) Can fly  b) Live in water  c) Have no pupa stage  d) Have six legs
**Answer: c) Have no pupa stage**
