# Hands-on — Life cycles of plants and animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stage cards
Draw the four stage cards — egg, larva, pupa, adult — on four pieces of paper. Shuffle them, then lay them in order for the butterfly. Do it again for the ladybird and the ant. Then take the pupa card away and lay the earthworm's road instead: jelly, cocoon, worm. Say out loud what changed.

## Find a stage outside
Go to a garden, a park or a pots-and-plants corner. Look — without pulling plants apart — for one stage of a real cycle: an egg cluster under a leaf, a larva (a caterpillar or grub), a pupa or cocoon, or the adult. Draw the one you found and write which animal's road it belongs to, and which stage comes next.

## The two roads chart
Make a two-column chart: Four stages | No pupa. Put the butterfly, bumblebee, fly, grasshopper, mosquito, ladybird, wasp and ant in the first column, and the earthworm and spider in the second. This is Sweden's set of ten, sorted by their roads.
