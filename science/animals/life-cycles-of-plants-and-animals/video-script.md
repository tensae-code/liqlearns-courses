# Video script — Life cycles of plants and animals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Life cycles of plants and animals** — Sweden's way: ten small creeping things, each with its own cycle in pictures.

A butterfly does not start as a small butterfly. It starts as an egg. From the egg comes a larva. The larva rests as a pupa. And out of the pupa comes the butterfly. Egg, larva, pupa, adult — the bumblebee, the fly, the mosquito, the ladybird, the wasp and the ant all pass through the same four stages.
But not every animal does. The earthworm's cycle is shorter — jelly, cocoon, worm — and the spider's is egg, cocoon, spider. No pupa at all.
So a life cycle is the road a living thing travels from its beginning to its adult self — and different animals travel different roads.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
