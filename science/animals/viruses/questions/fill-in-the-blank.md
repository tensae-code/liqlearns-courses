# Viruses — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A virus is wrapped in a _____ coat.
**Answer: protein**

**2.** A virus multiplies only inside a living _____.
**Answer: cell**

**3.** The flu is caused by a _____.
**Answer: virus**

**4.** A virus is not made of _____.
**Answer: cells**
