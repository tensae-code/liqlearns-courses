# Viruses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** How does a virus differ from a bacterium?
**Answer: A bacterium is a living cell that can feed and reproduce by itself; a virus is not a cell and can multiply only inside a living cell.**

**2.** Why can a light microscope not show a virus?
**Answer: Because a virus is far smaller than the smallest things a light microscope can resolve.**

**3.** How does a virus make copies of itself?
**Answer: It enters a living cell and uses the cell's machinery to build copies, which then burst out to enter more cells.**

**4.** Why do Kuwait's books teach viruses beside the five kingdoms?
**Answer: To set the border: viruses are made of living materials and act on living things, yet they are not themselves a living kingdom.**
