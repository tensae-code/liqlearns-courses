# Viruses — multiple choice


**1.** A virus is made of…
a) Many cells  b) One cell only  c) Hereditary material in a protein coat  d) Tiny bones
**Answer: c) Hereditary material in a protein coat**

**2.** Viruses can be seen with…
a) The naked eye  b) A light microscope  c) Neither — they are too small for a light microscope  d) A magnifying glass
**Answer: c) Neither — they are too small for a light microscope**

**3.** A virus multiplies…
a) In water  b) In soil  c) Only inside a living cell  d) In sunlight
**Answer: c) Only inside a living cell**

**4.** Which disease is caused by a virus?
a) Tooth decay  b) Flu  c) A broken bone  d) Sunburn
**Answer: b) Flu**

**5.** A virus by itself cannot…
a) Exist  b) Feed, grow or reproduce  c) Be carried by air  d) Enter a cell
**Answer: b) Feed, grow or reproduce**

**6.** Viruses are filed beside the living kingdoms but are…
a) Plants  b) Not alive in the way living things are  c) Animals  d) Fungi
**Answer: b) Not alive in the way living things are**
