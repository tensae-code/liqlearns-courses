# Hands-on — Viruses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Border of life debate
List the signs of life from the living-things lesson (feeding, growth, response, reproduction). Test a virus against each sign, one by one, and write the verdict for each. End by defending a one-sentence answer: is a virus a living thing? Use the cell rule — what it can and cannot do alone — as your evidence.
