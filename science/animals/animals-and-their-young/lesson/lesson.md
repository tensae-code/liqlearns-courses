## Sub lesson 1 — Animals and their young ones

Zimbabwe's ECD A guide teaches **animals and their young ones** as its own topic, right after domestic and wild animals: every animal the child has just met has young of its own.

## Sub lesson 2 — Matching a young animal to its parent

The topic's work is matching: the young one belongs to its parent animal, and learning the young ones extends the naming the child already does for the grown animals.

## Sub lesson 3 — Young ones grow

Young ones are not small copies that stay small — they grow into the grown animal. That growth is the topic's closing idea, taught at ECD level in the guide's own order.

## The key ideas

- **Animals and their young ones** — each animal has its young ones.
- **Matching a young animal to its parent** — the young one matches its parent.
- **Young ones grow** — young ones grow up.
