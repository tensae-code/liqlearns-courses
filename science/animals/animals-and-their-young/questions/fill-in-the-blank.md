# Animals and their young — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Animals and their young ones.
**Answer: each animal has its young ones**

**2.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Matching a young animal to its parent.
**Answer: the young one matches its parent**

**3.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Young ones grow.
**Answer: young ones grow up**
