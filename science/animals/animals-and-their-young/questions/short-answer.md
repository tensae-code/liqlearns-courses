# Animals and their young — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Animals and their young ones" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's ECD A guide teaches **animals and their young ones** as its own topic, right after domestic and wild animals: every animal the child has just met has young of its own.

**2.** What does the part "Matching a young animal to its parent" teach?
**Answer:** The topic's work is matching: the young one belongs to its parent animal, and learning the young ones extends the naming the child already does for the grown animals.

**3.** What does the part "Young ones grow" teach?
**Answer:** Young ones are not small copies that stay small — they grow into the grown animal.
