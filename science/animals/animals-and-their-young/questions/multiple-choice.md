# Animals and their young — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Matching a young animal to its parent  b) Young ones grow  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Animals and their young ones
**Answer: d) Animals and their young ones**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about each animal has its young ones?
a) Animals and their young ones  b) Matching a young animal to its parent  c) Young ones grow
**Answer: a) Animals and their young ones**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about the young one matches its parent?
a) Animals and their young ones  b) Matching a young animal to its parent  c) Young ones grow
**Answer: b) Matching a young animal to its parent**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about young ones grow up?
a) Animals and their young ones  b) Matching a young animal to its parent  c) Young ones grow
**Answer: c) Young ones grow**
