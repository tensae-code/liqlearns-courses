# Hands-on — How living things respond to their environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch a plant answer
Grow a small plant in a pot. Water only one side of the soil for a week, and keep a card screen so light comes from one side only. Draw the plant at the start and after a week: which way did the leaves turn, and where do you predict the roots grew? Say which factors the plant answered.
