# How living things respond to their environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does it mean to respond to the environment?
**Answer: To change what you do or how you grow because of something around you — light, heat, water, touch or danger.**

**2.** Give one plant response and one animal response.
**Answer: A plant turns its leaves to the light (or roots to water); an animal moves away from danger or into shade.**

**3.** How would you prove a plant responds to light?
**Answer: Grow it with light from one side only and watch the shoot bend toward the light over days.**

**4.** Why are responses useful?
**Answer: They win the living thing what it needs — light, water, safety — and so keep it alive.**
