# How living things respond to their environment — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Living things _____ to the factors around them.
**Answer: respond**

**2.** A shoot bends toward the _____.
**Answer: light**

**3.** Roots grow toward _____ in the soil.
**Answer: water**

**4.** A stone is non-living because it does not _____.
**Answer: respond**
