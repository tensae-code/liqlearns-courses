# How living things respond to their environment — multiple choice


**1.** A plant turning toward light is…
a) Sleeping  b) Responding to its environment  c) Dying  d) Making seeds
**Answer: b) Responding to its environment**

**2.** Which of these does NOT respond to the environment?
a) A tree  b) A dog  c) A stone  d) A flower
**Answer: c) A stone**

**3.** Roots grow toward…
a) Light  b) Water in the soil  c) The sky  d) Sound
**Answer: b) Water in the soil**

**4.** An animal moving into shade on a hot day is responding to…
a) Gravity  b) Heat  c) Touch  d) Darkness only
**Answer: b) Heat**

**5.** Responding to the environment is a sign of…
a) Rocks  b) Life  c) Rain  d) Machines
**Answer: b) Life**

**6.** A response helps a living thing to…
a) Stay alive  b) Turn to stone  c) Stop growing  d) Hide from water
**Answer: a) Stay alive**
