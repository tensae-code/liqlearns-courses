# Video script — How living things respond to their environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **How living things respond to their environment** — Kuwait's way: how living things respond to the different factors of the environment — light, heat, water and touch — because responding is one of the signs of life.

Living things respond to what is around them. A plant turns its leaves toward the light and sends its roots toward water. An animal moves away from danger, seeks shade in heat and water in thirst. A non-living thing does none of this: a stone does not answer the sun. Kuwait's Grade 4 book teaches responding as the living world's answer to the environment's factors — light, temperature, water, gravity and touch — each factor named with the response it calls out. A response is useful: turning to light wins a plant more food-making; moving to shade saves an animal from overheating; roots reaching water keep the plant drinking. Responses the learner will meet again inside their own body — the human body sensing and responding — begin here, in the whole living world. The book's way is to test it: cover one side of a growing plant, or water only one side of a pot, and watch which way the plant answers. The answer, seen with your own eyes, is the lesson.
