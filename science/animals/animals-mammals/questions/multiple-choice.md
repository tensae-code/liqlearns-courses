# Animals — mammals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mammal young are fed on —
a) seeds  b) milk  c) leaves
**Answer: b) milk**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mammals are born —
a) from eggs with shells  b) alive, by live birth  c) as tadpoles
**Answer: b) alive, by live birth**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Mammals breathe with —
a) gills  b) lungs  c) their skin
**Answer: b) lungs**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A dolphin must surface to —
a) breathe air  b) lay eggs  c) warm its gills
**Answer: a) breathe air**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Mammal limbs end in —
a) fins only  b) claws, nails or hooves  c) feathers
**Answer: b) claws, nails or hooves** — whales excepted.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Mammals are —
a) warm-blooded  b) cold-blooded  c) ectothermic
**Answer: a) warm-blooded**
