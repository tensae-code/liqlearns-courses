# Animals — mammals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give four features of mammals.
**Answer:** Any four of: live birth, milk-feeding, hair, four limbs ending in claws/nails/hooves, lungs, warm blood.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a dolphin rise to the surface?
**Answer:** It breathes air with lungs, so it must surface to breathe.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the whale exception to the limb rule?
**Answer:** Whales are the exception to 'four limbs ending in claws, nails or hooves'.
