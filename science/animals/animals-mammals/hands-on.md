# Hands-on — Animals — mammals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mammal trait hunt
List five local mammals. Tick live birth, milk, hair, lungs and warm blood for each — ask a family member to confirm the ones you cannot see.

## Breathing rhythm
Time your own breaths for a minute at rest. Then imagine the dolphin's surfacing rhythm: up, breathe, dive. Write why it cannot simply stay down.
