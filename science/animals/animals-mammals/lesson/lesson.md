# Animals — mammals

## Do it first

A dolphin looks like a fish and lives in the sea. Write down: fish or mammal — and what single observation would settle it?

## Sub lesson 1 — The mammal plan

Mammals give live birth and feed their young on milk. They have hair, four limbs ending in claws, nails or hooves (whales excepted), lungs, and warm blood.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mammals in the water

Whales and dolphins live in water but they are mammals, not fish: they breathe air with lungs, so they must rise to the surface to breathe. Watch the dolphin come up, breathe, and dive again. [GIF 32918 — a dolphin rising to breathe]

[GIF 32918 — a dolphin rising to breathe at the surface]

## Say it to your companion

Explain in your own words: mammals bear live young and feed them on milk, have hair, breathe with lungs and are warm-blooded — even whales and dolphins, which must surface to breathe air. Give one example of your own for each part, and let your companion challenge you with one hard case.
