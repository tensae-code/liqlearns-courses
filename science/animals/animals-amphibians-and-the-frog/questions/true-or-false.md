# Animals — amphibians and the frog — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Amphibians are ectothermic.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Male frogs croak to call mates.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** You should collect frogs with bare hands.
**Answer: False — collect humanely with a container, following field safety, and release unharmed.**
