# Animals — amphibians and the frog — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Amphibian skin is —
a) dry and scaly  b) smooth, moist and scaleless  c) feathered
**Answer: b) smooth, moist and scaleless**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A young amphibian in water breathes with —
a) lungs  b) gills  c) its feet
**Answer: b) gills**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An adult frog breathes with —
a) lungs  b) gills only  c) scales
**Answer: a) lungs**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The frog's ear disc is called the —
a) tympanum  b) clitellum  c) frond
**Answer: a) tympanum**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A frog's hind toes are —
a) webbed  b) hooved  c) clawed for digging only
**Answer: a) webbed** — for swimming.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** After studying a frog you should —
a) keep it in a jar  b) release it unharmed where you found it  c) take it home
**Answer: b) release it unharmed where you found it**
