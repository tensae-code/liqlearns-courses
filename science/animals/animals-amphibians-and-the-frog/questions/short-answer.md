# Animals — amphibians and the frog — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does breathing change as an amphibian grows?
**Answer:** Young breathe with gills in water; adults breathe with lungs on land.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List four things you observe or measure in the frog study.
**Answer:** Any four of: hind vs fore toe count (webbing), body measure, eyes, nostrils, tympanum, moist skin.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do frogs appear in one season more than another?
**Answer:** Frogs are seasonal — they appear and call (males croaking for mates) in their breeding season.
