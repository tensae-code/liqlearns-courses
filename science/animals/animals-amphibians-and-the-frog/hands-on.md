# Hands-on — Animals — amphibians and the frog

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practical 5.2 — the frog study
Collect humanely in a container, count webbed hind toes vs fore toes, measure, find eyes, nostrils and tympanum, feel the skin — then release unharmed. Field safety (teacher guide — evidence, item 11, guide printed pp. 84–108): some organisms may sting or bite or carry disease — never collect with bare hands, use gloves or a container, watch where you step, wash hands afterwards, and release every specimen alive and unharmed where you found it.

## Two-homes drawing
Draw the tadpole in water and the adult frog on land side by side. Label gills on one, lungs on the other.
