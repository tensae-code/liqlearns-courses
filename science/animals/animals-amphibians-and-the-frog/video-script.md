# Video script — Animals — amphibians and the frog

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: amphibians are ectothermic animals with smooth, moist, scaleless skin; their young breathe with gills in water and their adults breathe with lungs on land — studied by observing a frog humanely and releasing it.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
