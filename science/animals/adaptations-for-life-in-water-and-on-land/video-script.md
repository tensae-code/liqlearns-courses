# Video script — Adaptations for life in water and on land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Adaptations for life in water and on land** — Bulgaria's way: one lesson for the water home, one for the land.

Water animals are fitted for water: fins to move, shells to protect, tentacles to feel and catch, webbed feet to swim. And remember the trap — the dolphin lives in water, but it is not a fish.

Land animals are fitted for land: fittings for moving and feeding, and for the hard jobs — winter, met by sleeping through it, growing thicker fur, or migrating; and defence, with spines, shells, horns and stings.

Now you. Name one fitting, and say its job. Say it back — in your own words.
