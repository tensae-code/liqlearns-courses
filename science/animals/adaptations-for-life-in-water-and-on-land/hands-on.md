# Hands-on — Adaptations for life in water and on land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two homes, two lists
Draw a pond and a piece of land side by side. List the fittings of one water animal and one land animal you know, and write the job of each fitting beside it.

## Winter watch
Through one cold week, note what the animals around you do about winter — thicker coats, hiding away, leaving. Sort your notes under hibernate, thicker fur, migrate.
