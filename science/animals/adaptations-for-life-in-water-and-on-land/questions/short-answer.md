# Adaptations for life in water and on land — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name four fittings of water animals from Bulgaria's book and the job of each.
**Answer:** Fins for moving, shells for protection, tentacles for feeling and catching, webbed feet for swimming.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the three ways land animals meet winter in the book.
**Answer:** They hibernate, grow thicker fur, or migrate.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is 'it lives in water, so it is a fish' a mistake?
**Answer:** Because the home alone does not fix the kind — the dolphin lives in water but is not a fish; kind and fittings must be read together.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Pick one land animal and name one fitting for each job: movement, feeding, winter, defence — as far as you can.
**Answer:** Any correctly matched set, e.g. legs for movement, teeth for feeding, thicker fur for winter, horns for defence.
