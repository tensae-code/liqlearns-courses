# Adaptations for life in water and on land — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A fitting that lets a living thing live in its home is called an _____.
**Answer: adaptation**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Webbed _____ help water animals swim.
**Answer: feet**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The _____ is not a fish, though it lives in water.
**Answer: dolphin**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Animals that leave for warmer places in winter _____.
**Answer: migrate**
