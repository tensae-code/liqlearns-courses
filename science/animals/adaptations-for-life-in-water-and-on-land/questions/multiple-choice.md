# Adaptations for life in water and on land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a water adaptation?
a) Hibernation  b) Webbed feet  c) Thicker fur  d) Horns
**Answer: b) Webbed feet**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The dolphin is…
a) A fish  b) Not a fish  c) A plant  d) An insect
**Answer: b) Not a fish**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Animals that sleep through winter are…
a) Migrating  b) Hibernating  c) Moulting  d) Swimming
**Answer: b) Hibernating**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which set is for defence on land?
a) Fins and tentacles  b) Spines, shells, horns and a sting  c) Webbed feet and fins  d) Fur and feathers only
**Answer: b) Spines, shells, horns and a sting**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Growing thicker fur is an adaptation for…
a) Feeding  b) Winter  c) Swimming  d) Defence
**Answer: b) Winter**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Tentacles help a water animal to…
a) Feel and catch  b) Hibernate  c) Migrate  d) Grow fur
**Answer: a) Feel and catch**
