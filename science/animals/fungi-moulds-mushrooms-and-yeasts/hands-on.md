# Hands-on — Fungi — moulds, mushrooms and yeasts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practical 5.8 — mould garden (sealed)
With an adult: seal wet injera/bread/orange-peel pieces and soil-rubbed pieces in separate bags, watch through the bag for three days, then dispose of the sealed bags safely and wash hands. Never open or taste them. Field safety (teacher guide — evidence, item 11, guide printed pp. 84–108): some organisms may sting or bite or carry disease — never collect with bare hands, use gloves or a container, watch where you step, wash hands afterwards, and release every specimen alive and unharmed where you found it.

## Practical 5.9 — mushroom dissection
With an adult, dissect a mushroom in the field and label its parts in a sketch. Wash hands afterwards; never eat a wild mushroom. Field safety (teacher guide — evidence, item 11, guide printed pp. 84–108): some organisms may sting or bite or carry disease — never collect with bare hands, use gloves or a container, watch where you step, wash hands afterwards, and release every specimen alive and unharmed where you found it.
