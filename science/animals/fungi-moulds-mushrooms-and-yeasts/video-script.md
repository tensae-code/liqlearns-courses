# Video script — Fungi — moulds, mushrooms and yeasts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's one thing: fungi are eukaryotic, chitin-walled organisms that cannot make food — they digest dead matter and absorb it, parasitise living hosts, or live symbiotically; yeast (ersho) raises dough and ferments tella and tej.

Look at the picture. Say each part's name. Then look away — and say them again from memory.

Now you try the doing task. Slowly. Out loud. That is the lesson.
