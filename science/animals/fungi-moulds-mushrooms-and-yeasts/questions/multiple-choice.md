# Fungi — moulds, mushrooms and yeasts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fungi make food by —
a) photosynthesis  b) they cannot make food — they digest and absorb it  c) catching insects
**Answer: b) they cannot make food**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fungal cell walls contain —
a) cellulose  b) chitin  c) murein
**Answer: b) chitin**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Lichens are fungi living —
a) as parasites on feet  b) symbiotically with algae  c) in cones
**Answer: b) symbiotically with algae**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Yeast (ersho) is used to —
a) raise dough and ferment tella/tej  b) curdle milk  c) make seeds
**Answer: a) raise dough and ferment tella/tej**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In Practical 5.8 the mould bags are kept for —
a) three days, sealed  b) one hour, open  c) a year
**Answer: a) three days, sealed** — then disposed of safely, hands washed.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Athlete's foot is caused by a fungus living as a —
a) symbiont  b) parasite  c) producer
**Answer: b) parasite**
