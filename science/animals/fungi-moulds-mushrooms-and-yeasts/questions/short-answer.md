# Fungi — moulds, mushrooms and yeasts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the three ways fungi feed.
**Answer:** Digesting dead matter and absorbing it; parasitising living hosts; living symbiotically (lichens with algae).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe Practical 5.8.
**Answer:** Moulds grown on wet injera/bread/orange peel vs soil-rubbed pieces in sealed bags over three days; dispose safely, wash hands.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What local work does yeast (ersho) do?
**Answer:** It raises dough and ferments tella and tej.
