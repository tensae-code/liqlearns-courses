## Sub lesson 1 — Systematics and cladistics

Systematics and cladistics: all living things share a common origin, so their evolutionary history (phylogeny) can be traced as trees; systematics studies diversification and relationships through time.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dendrogram vs cladogram work

Dendrogram vs cladogram work: which animal is most and least similar to the turtle Staurotypus salvinii, whether two trees of the same plant species are the same tree, and completion tables for the three domains (Bacteria, Archaea, Eukarya) and the Fungi, Plantae and Animalia kingdoms.
