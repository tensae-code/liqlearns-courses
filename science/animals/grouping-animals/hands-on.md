# Hands-on — Grouping animals: by young, by movement, by food and by body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four keys, one animal
Take the cat. Write its four placements: young (milk-giver), movement (walks), food (both), body (mammal, hair). Now do the hen. Compare the two files.

## Covering collection
Draw or collect (pictures only) five coverings: hair, feather, scale, shell, soft skin. Mount them as a key that a younger child could use.

## Market of animals
In a group, each child is an animal. The class sorts the whole 'market' by one key called out — food! movement! body! — regrouping four times without error.
