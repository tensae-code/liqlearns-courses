# Video script — Grouping animals: by young, by movement, by food and by body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eggs or milk? **First key.**
Fly, crawl or walk? Land, water or both? **Second key.**
Meat, plants or both? **Third key.**
Hair, feathers, scales, shell or soft skin? **Fourth key.**
Four keys in your hand — every animal opens.
