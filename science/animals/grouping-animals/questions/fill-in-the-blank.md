# Grouping animals: by young, by movement, by food and by body — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The hen is an ___-layer.**
**Answer: egg**
**2. The insect's middle part, where the legs attach, is the ______.**
**Answer: thorax**

**3. Amphibians are ______-blooded (ደመ ቀዝቃዛ).**
**Answer: cold**
