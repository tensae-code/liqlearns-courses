# Grouping animals: by young, by movement, by food and by body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A cow belongs with the milk-givers because she —**
a) lays eggs  b) bears young and suckles them  c) lives in water
**Answer: b) bears young and suckles them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A snake moves by —**
a) flying  b) walking on legs  c) crawling on the ground
**Answer: c) crawling on the ground**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A fish is known by its —**
a) feathers  b) scales  c) soft skin
**Answer: b) scales**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In the book's food table, the lion is a —**
a) plant-eater  b) meat-eater  c) eater of both
**Answer: b) meat-eater**
