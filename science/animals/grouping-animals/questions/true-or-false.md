# Grouping animals: by young, by movement, by food and by body — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A frog lives both on land and in water.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Birds are known by feathers and wings.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A hen both walks and can flutter — movement sorts can hold two answers for one animal.**
**Answer: True**
**4. Birds are warm-blooded (ደመ ሞቃት).**
**Answer: True**

**5. Reptiles fertilise their eggs externally in water.**
**Answer: False**

**6. Warm weather speeds insect breeding.**
**Answer: True**
