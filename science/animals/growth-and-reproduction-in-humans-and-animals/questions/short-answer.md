# Growth and reproduction in humans and animals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What three changes does the Grade 1 book name for humans and animals?
**Answer: They grow, they change, and they reproduce.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How do we know whose offspring a young animal is?
**Answer: Offspring resemble their parents — the young look like the grown ones of their kind.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) What does the activity book have learners do with growth?
**Answer: Observe it and order its stages.**
