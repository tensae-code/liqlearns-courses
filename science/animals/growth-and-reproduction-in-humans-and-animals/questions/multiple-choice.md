# Growth and reproduction in humans and animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Humans and animals…
a) Never change  b) Grow, change and reproduce  c) Only sleep  d) Stay the same size
**Answer: b) Grow, change and reproduce**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Offspring…
a) Never look like their parents  b) Resemble their parents  c) Are always bigger than their parents  d) Have no kind
**Answer: b) Resemble their parents**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Rabbits reproduce by…
a) Eggs  b) Giving birth  c) Seeds  d) Cuttings
**Answer: b) Giving birth**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Birds and fish are named as reproducing by…
a) Birth only  b) Eggs  c) Budding  d) Grafting
**Answer: b) Eggs**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Chicks…
a) Hatch from eggs  b) Are born from rabbits  c) Grow on plants  d) Never grow
**Answer: a) Hatch from eggs**
