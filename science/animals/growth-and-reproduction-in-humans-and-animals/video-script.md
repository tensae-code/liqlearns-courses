Today: **Growth and reproduction in humans and animals** — Iraq's Grade 1 way.

Humans and animals grow, change and reproduce — and offspring resemble their parents. Animals reproduce two ways: some give birth, like rabbits; others by eggs, like birds and fish — and chicks hatch from eggs. The activity book adds the hands: observe growth, and order its stages.
