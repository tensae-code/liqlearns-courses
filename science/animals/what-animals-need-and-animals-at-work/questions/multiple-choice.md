# What animals need, and animals at work for people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The three things animals need are —**
a) food, shelter and water  b) toys, songs and shoes  c) books, pencils and bags
**Answer: a) food, shelter and water**

**2. Which animal is in the book's transport list?**
a) the camel  b) the frog  c) the hen
**Answer: a) the camel** — the horse, donkey, mule and camel carry.

**3. Which food gift comes from milk?**
a) eggs  b) cheese  c) leather
**Answer: b) cheese**

**4. A belt and a bag can be made from —**
a) feathers  b) leather  c) milk
**Answer: b) leather**
