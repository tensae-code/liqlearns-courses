# Lesson plan — What animals need, and animals at work for people

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Animals · **Lesson:** What animals need, and animals at work for people
**Place in the curriculum:** Animals — the services, in full (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** animals need food, shelter and water; they repay care with food (meat, milk, eggs, yoghurt, cheese, butter), transport (horse, donkey, mule, camel), clothing and goods (leather, blankets and caps from hair and wool) and labour (ploughing)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Tame and wild — the services were listed there; this lesson gives the full ledger, needs against gifts.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Animals — Tame animals and wild animals. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three needs: food, shelter and water.
2. List the food gifts: meat, milk, eggs, yoghurt, cheese, butter.
3. List the transport: horse, donkey, mule, camel; the clothing and goods: leather jacket, shoes, belt, bag, and blankets and caps from hair and wool; and labour: ploughing and draught work.

## What you need
A working animal near home to observe — a donkey with a load, an ox at a plough, a cow at milking.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists gifts but no needs → the book opens with the needs: food, shelter, water. A gift list without needs is half the bargain, and the animals pay for the missing half.
- Forgets the quiet gifts → leather goods and hair-and-wool blankets and caps are in the book's list beside the famous ones.
