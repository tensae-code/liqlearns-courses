# Video script — What animals need, and animals at work for people

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Food, shelter, water — **their needs first**.
Meat, milk, eggs — **food back**.
Donkey, camel, horse — **carrying back**.
Leather, wool, plough — **work and wear back**.
Care for them, and the gifts grow.
