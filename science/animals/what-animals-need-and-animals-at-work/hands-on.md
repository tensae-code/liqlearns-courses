# Hands-on — What animals need, and animals at work for people

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bargain poster
Make the two-sided poster for the cow: NEEDS on the left (food, shelter, water), GIFTS on the right (milk and its children, meat, leather, labour of calves grown). Hang it by the byre or the door.

## Load respect
Watch a loaded donkey pass. Judge the load as the next lesson will teach: is it within the animal's strength? Note what you saw.

## Egg economics
Follow one hen for a week on paper: feed and water in, eggs out. The smallest ledger teaches the same law as the ox's.
