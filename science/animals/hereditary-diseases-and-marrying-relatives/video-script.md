Today: **Hereditary diseases and marrying relatives** — Yemen's way, from its book (Yemen, Grade 9 — *Science, Grade 9, Part 2*, printed pp. 142, 147).

Yemen's Grade 9 Science book tells it through a neighbour's daughter who falls ill with Mediterranean anaemia because both her parents — who did not look related in their daily life — carried the same hidden gene from shared ancestors. Cousin marriage can also fix good traits; either way, a medical check before marriage is the safeguard.
The book then meets the diseases one by one: hereditary dwarfism; colour blindness, riding on the X chromosome; Down's pattern, an extra chromosome 21; and albinism, the missing pigment enzyme. Each is recognised the same way — by studying a family's pattern.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
