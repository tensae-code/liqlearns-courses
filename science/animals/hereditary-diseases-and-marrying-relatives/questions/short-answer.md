# Hereditary diseases and marrying relatives — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: When relatives marry — hidden carriers.
**Answer:** Yemen's Grade 9 Science book tells it through a neighbour's daughter who falls ill with Mediterranean anaemia because both her parents — who did not look related in their daily life — carried the same hidden gene from shared ancestors.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Hereditary diseases, one by one.
**Answer:** The book then meets the diseases one by one: hereditary dwarfism; colour blindness, riding on the X chromosome; Down's pattern, an extra chromosome 21; and albinism, the missing pigment enzyme.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** When relatives marry — hidden carriers → Hereditary diseases, one by one — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
