# Snails and slugs in the grassland

## Do it first
A snail crosses a stone, slowly, in its shell house. It cannot fly, it cannot run, the sea is all around its island. So how did its kind ever get there? Say your best idea out loud. Then read on: Iceland's grassland chapter asks exactly that about its own snail.

## Sub lesson 2 — How did it get here — and how fast does it go?

The guide asks how the brekkubobbi **may have reached Iceland — with birds, or with people**: a snail that cannot cross the sea alone must have travelled by another's help. And the chapter's activity measures the snail itself: **measure a snail's speed** — mark the start, time the crawl, and give slowness a number.

## The key ideas

- The brekkubobbi is Iceland's grassland snail, a slow shell-carrying dweller of the grassland.
- It may have reached Iceland with birds or with people — it could not cross the sea alone.
- The chapter's activity: measure a snail's speed — mark, time, and number the crawl.
