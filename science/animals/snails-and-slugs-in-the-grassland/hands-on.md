# Hands-on — Snails and slugs in the grassland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Measure a snail
Find a snail on a damp day. With an adult, mark its start line, time it for one minute without touching it, and measure how far it crawled. That distance in one minute is its speed — write it down, then let it be.

## Bird or people?
Argue both sides in turn: the brekkubobbi came with birds; the brekkubobbi came with people. Give one reason for each side, Iceland's way of asking the question.
