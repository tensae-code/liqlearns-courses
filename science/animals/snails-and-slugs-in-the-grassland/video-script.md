Today: **Snails and slugs in the grassland** — Iceland's grassland chapter, from the Líf á landi guide.
The brekkubobbi is a grassland snail on an island in the sea. How did it get there? With birds, says the guide — or with people.
And its activity measures a snail's speed: mark the start, time the crawl, number the slowness.
Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
