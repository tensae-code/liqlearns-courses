# Snails and slugs in the grassland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** The brekkubobbi is Iceland's grassland snail, a slow shell-carrying dweller of the grassland.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** It may have reached Iceland with birds or with people — it could not cross the sea alone.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two ways the guide gives for the brekkubobbi reaching Iceland.
**Answer:** With birds, or with people — the two carriers that could cross the sea the snail cannot cross alone.
