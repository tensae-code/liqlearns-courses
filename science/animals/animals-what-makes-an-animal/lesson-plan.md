# Lesson plan — Animals — what makes an animal

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Animals · **Lesson:** Animals — what makes an animal
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Living Things and their Diversity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** animals are multicellular consumers with muscle and nervous tissue whose cells have no walls and no chloroplasts; they divide into invertebrates without a backbone and vertebrates with one.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Animals and Plants primary lessons of this course (grouping animals; kinds of plants and their parts) at first depth, and it is the Grade 7 first-depth partner of the Biology title's secondary classification and kingdoms lessons — the same ground, met here first and more simply; the Biology lessons go deeper later. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
