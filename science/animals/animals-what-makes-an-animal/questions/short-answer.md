# Animals — what makes an animal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three shared features of animals.
**Answer:** Any three of: multicellular, eukaryotic, consumers, muscle tissue, nervous tissue, need oxygen, mostly sexual reproduction, move to find food, cells without walls or chloroplasts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five vertebrate groups.
**Answer:** Fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, mammals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the difference between an invertebrate and a vertebrate?
**Answer:** An invertebrate has no backbone; a vertebrate has a backbone.
