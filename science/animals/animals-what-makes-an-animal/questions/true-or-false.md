# Animals — what makes an animal — true or false

Say true or false. If false, say the right thing.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Animals have muscle and nervous tissue.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Animals make their own food in chloroplasts.
**Answer: False — animal cells have no chloroplasts; animals are consumers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An earthworm is a vertebrate.
**Answer: False — it has no backbone; it is an invertebrate.**
