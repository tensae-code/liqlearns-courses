# Animals — what makes an animal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Animals feed as —
a) producers  b) consumers  c) decomposers only
**Answer: b) consumers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Animal cells have —
a) walls and chloroplasts  b) no walls and no chloroplasts  c) cones
**Answer: b) no walls and no chloroplasts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Most animals reproduce —
a) sexually  b) by spores  c) by cones
**Answer: a) sexually**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A vertebrate is an animal —
a) with a backbone  b) with wings  c) that lives in water
**Answer: a) with a backbone**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a vertebrate group?
a) insects  b) birds  c) worms
**Answer: b) birds**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** How many vertebrate groups are named in this lesson?
a) three  b) four  c) five
**Answer: c) five** — fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, mammals.
