## Sub lesson 1 — Blood pressure — the push of the blood

The circulation moves blood around the body, and the moving blood **presses** on the vessels it runs through. That pressure — the blood pressure — is a sign of how the circulation is working, which is why it is worth measuring.

## Sub lesson 2 — Measuring blood pressure

The book teaches **blood pressure measurement** as the unit's closing skill: the pressure is measured, and its reading tells the measurer about the state of the person's circulation — a measurement that turns the whole unit's knowledge into a number that can be acted on.
