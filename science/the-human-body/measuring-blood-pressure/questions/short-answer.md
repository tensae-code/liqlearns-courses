# Measuring blood pressure — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Blood pressure — the push of the blood" teach?
**Answer:** The circulation moves blood around the body, and the moving blood **presses** on the vessels it runs through.

**2.** What does the part "Measuring blood pressure" teach?
**Answer:** The book teaches **blood pressure measurement** as the unit's closing skill: the pressure is measured, and its reading tells the measurer about the state of the person's circulation — a measurement that turns the whole unit's knowledge into a number that can be acted on.
