# Hands-on — Tissues — plant and animal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the teams
Take a leaf and a piece of cooked vegetable and look closely — with a magnifier if you have one — at how each is made of many small joined parts, not one solid block. Say out loud: the parts I can see are built of cell teams — tissues.

## Sort the tissues
On paper, draw two boxes: PLANT and ANIMAL. Write or draw every tissue the lesson names into its box, and beside the animal box add each tissue's job — signalling for nervous tissue, net-like support for reticular connective tissue.
