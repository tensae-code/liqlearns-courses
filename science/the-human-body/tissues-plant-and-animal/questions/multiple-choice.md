# Tissues — plant and animal — multiple choice


**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A tissue is…
a) One single cell  b) A group of like cells joined to do one job  c) A whole organ  d) A kind of bone
**Answer: b) A group of like cells joined to do one job**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The first great sorting of tissues is into…
a) Hard and soft  b) Big and small  c) Plant tissues and animal tissues  d) Young and old
**Answer: c) Plant tissues and animal tissues**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) An organ is built of…
a) Tissues  b) Only water  c) Single atoms  d) Seeds
**Answer: a) Tissues**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Nervous tissue's job is…
a) Holding cells in a net  b) Carrying messages — signalling  c) Making food from sunlight  d) Storing fat
**Answer: b) Carrying messages — signalling**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Reticular connective tissue is…
a) A net-like supporting tissue  b) A kind of leaf  c) The signalling tissue  d) A plant tissue
**Answer: a) A net-like supporting tissue**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Plant tissues make up…
a) Only the flower  b) The plant's parts — root, stem and leaf  c) Animal bodies  d) The soil
**Answer: b) The plant's parts — root, stem and leaf**
