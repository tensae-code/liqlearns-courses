# Tissues — plant and animal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define a tissue in one sentence.
**Answer: A tissue is a group of like cells, joined together, that do one job as a team.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) How does the book classify tissues first?
**Answer: By the living thing they belong to: plant tissues in plants and animal tissues in animals.**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two animal tissues from the book and give each one's job.
**Answer: Nervous tissue — carries messages (signalling); reticular connective tissue — a net-like support that holds other cells in place.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) How do tissues and organs fit together?
**Answer: Organs are built of tissues — many cell teams joined make the organ, as bricks make a wall.**
