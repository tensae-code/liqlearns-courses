# Tissues — plant and animal — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A group of like cells joined to do one job is a _____.
**Answer: tissue**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Tissues are sorted into plant tissues and _____ tissues.
**Answer: animal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The animal tissue that carries messages is _____ tissue.
**Answer: nervous**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) _____ connective tissue is a net-like supporting tissue.
**Answer: Reticular**
