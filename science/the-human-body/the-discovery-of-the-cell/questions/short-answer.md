# Cells — the discovery of the cell — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why could cells not be studied before the microscope?
**Answer: Every cell is microscopic — too small for the naked eye to see.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do we say Leeuwenhoek saw living cells while Hooke did not?
**Answer: Leeuwenhoek's animalcules were alive and moving; Hooke's cork boxes were dead walls only.**
