# Cells — the discovery of the cell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cell is —
a) a building block of all living things  b) a kind of microscope  c) a drop of water
**Answer: a) a building block of all living things**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A human body holds about —
a) hundreds of cells  b) thousands of cells  c) trillions of cells
**Answer: c) trillions of cells**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hooke looked at — in 1665.
a) pond water  b) cork  c) blood
**Answer: b) cork**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What Hooke saw in cork was —
a) living cells swimming  b) dead plant cell walls, like a honeycomb  c) bacteria
**Answer: b) dead plant cell walls, like a honeycomb**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Leeuwenhoek's animalcules were —
a) living single-celled creatures in pond water  b) cork boxes  c) dust
**Answer: a) living single-celled creatures in pond water**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Leeuwenhoek also saw for the first time —
a) the Moon  b) bacteria, yeast and red blood cells  c) plant roots
**Answer: b) bacteria, yeast and red blood cells**
