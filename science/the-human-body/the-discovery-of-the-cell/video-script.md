# Video script — Cells — the discovery of the cell

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Everything alive is built of **cells**. Trillions of them make you.

**Hooke** looked at cork. He saw empty boxes, like honeycomb.
Dead walls. He named them **cells**.

**Leeuwenhoek** looked at pond water. It was swimming!
Living cells — animalcules — seen alive for the first time.
