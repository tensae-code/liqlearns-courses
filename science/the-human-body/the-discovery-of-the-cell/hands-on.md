# Hands-on — Cells — the discovery of the cell

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only the page.

## Cork under the lens
If cork is available, shave the thinnest slice you can and view it with a hand lens or microscope. Sketch the boxes you see — Hooke's honeycomb — and write under it: dead walls, not living cells.

## Pond water watch
Put a drop of pond water on a white card and watch it with a lens for two quiet minutes. Note any speck that moves on its own — moving on its own is the sign of a living cell, Leeuwenhoek's sign.
