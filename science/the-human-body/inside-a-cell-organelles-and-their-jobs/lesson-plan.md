# Lesson plan — Cells — inside a cell: organelles and their jobs

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** Cells — inside a cell: organelles and their jobs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Cell as the Basis of Life, inside The Human Body (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every cell has a membrane, cytoplasm, DNA and ribosomes; inside, each organelle has a job — nucleus, mitochondria, and in plants the wall and chloroplasts — and plant and animal cells differ in exactly those plant-only parts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Cells — the discovery of the cell (what a cell is). The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Inside a cell — organelles; Plant cell or animal cell); this lesson is the first-depth Grade 7 version, built new, with the school-as-cell analogy and the local-materials cell model.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The four features every cell has** — membrane (gatekeeper), cytoplasm (jelly holding organelles), DNA (instructions) and ribosomes (protein makers).
2. **The organelles and their jobs** — nucleus, mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi body, vacuole, lysosome — through the plant/animal comparison (Table 4.2) and the school-as-cell analogy.
3. **Plant-only parts, and building a cell model** — cell wall and chloroplasts mark the plant cell; a model built from a plastic bag, cardboard, beans/stones, buttons and local materials.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cells — the discovery of the cell. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four features every cell has, with each one's job.
2. Give the job of the nucleus, mitochondria, Golgi body and vacuole.
3. Say which parts are plant-only (wall, chloroplasts) and build a labelled cell model from local materials.

## What you need
For the model: a clear plastic bag, cardboard, beans or small stones, buttons, string and labels; paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Puts chloroplasts in an animal cell → chloroplasts and the cell wall are plant-only; the picture's second colour marks exactly those two.
- Mixes membrane and wall → every cell has a membrane; a plant cell has a wall OUTSIDE its membrane as well.
