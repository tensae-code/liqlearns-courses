# Video script — Cells — inside a cell: organelles and their jobs (cell-model build-along)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This is a FULL build-along script: the learner builds while listening, pausing at each — PAUSE —. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

We are going to build a cell. You can hold this one in your hands.

You need: a clear plastic bag. Cardboard. Beans or small stones. A button. String. Labels and a pen.

**Step one.** The bag is the **membrane** — the gatekeeper skin of the cell.
Fill the bag with soft, jelly-like material. That jelly is the **cytoplasm**. — PAUSE —
Tie the bag closed. Your cell has its skin and its jelly.

**Step two.** Push the **button** into the middle. The button is the **nucleus** — the control centre, holding the instructions. — PAUSE —

**Step three.** Push in the **beans**. Each bean is a **mitochondrion** — the part that releases energy. Cells have many. Put in five or six. — PAUSE —

**Step four.** Lay the **string** in folds inside the bag. The string is the **endoplasmic reticulum** — the folded network that makes and moves materials. — PAUSE —

**Step five.** Is yours a plant cell? Then stand the bag inside a **cardboard** box edge — the cardboard is the **cell wall**, outside the membrane. Add something green for the **chloroplasts**.
An animal cell has no wall and no chloroplasts. Leave them out. — PAUSE —

**Step six.** Label every part. Bag: membrane. Jelly: cytoplasm. Button: nucleus. Beans: mitochondria. String: reticulum. Cardboard: wall.

Hold it up. You are holding a cell — every part with its job.
