# Hands-on — Cells — inside a cell: organelles and their jobs

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The school walk
Walk around your school (or picture your home compound) and list its working parts: gate, rooms, office, store, kitchen. Beside each, write the cell part with the same job. That matching is the lesson's analogy, done with your feet.

## Model swap
Exchange cell models with a partner. Without labels, name every material's part and job on your partner's model. Any part you cannot name goes back on your own study list.
