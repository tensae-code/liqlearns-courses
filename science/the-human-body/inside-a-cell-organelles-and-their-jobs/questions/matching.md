# Cells — inside a cell: organelles and their jobs — matching

Match each part with its job.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** DNA — the instructions for new cells and all functions
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Vacuole — storage space; large and central in plant cells
**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lysosome — digests worn-out parts and invaders
**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Endoplasmic reticulum — folded network that makes and moves materials

**Answer key:** 1. DNA — the instructions for new cells and all functions; 2. Vacuole — storage space; large and central in plant cells; 3. Lysosome — digests worn-out parts and invaders; 4. Endoplasmic reticulum — folded network that makes and moves materials
