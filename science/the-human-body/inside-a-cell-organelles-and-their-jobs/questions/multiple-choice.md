# Cells — inside a cell: organelles and their jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The gatekeeper of the cell, controlling what enters and leaves, is the —
a) nucleus  b) cell membrane  c) vacuole
**Answer: b) cell membrane**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The jelly that holds the organelles is the —
a) cytoplasm  b) DNA  c) cell wall
**Answer: a) cytoplasm**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ribosomes —
a) store water  b) make proteins  c) catch light
**Answer: b) make proteins**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The control centre holding the DNA is the —
a) Golgi body  b) lysosome  c) nucleus
**Answer: c) nucleus**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Mitochondria —
a) release energy from food  b) digest invaders  c) pack proteins
**Answer: a) release energy from food**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Golgi body —
a) makes the wall  b) finishes, packs and sends out proteins  c) holds cell sap
**Answer: b) finishes, packs and sends out proteins**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Which parts are plant-only?
a) nucleus and membrane  b) cell wall and chloroplasts  c) ribosomes and vacuole
**Answer: b) cell wall and chloroplasts**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In the cell model, the plastic bag stands for the —
a) membrane  b) nucleus  c) mitochondrion
**Answer: a) membrane**
