# Reproduction, puberty and staying safe — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 4)** State the key split between the two kinds of sexually transmitted infection, and what it means for staying safe.
**Answer: bacterial and parasitic infections are treatable with antibiotics and can be cured; viral infections (such as HIV) have no cure — so prevention is the protection for viral infections, and early treatment for the treatable ones.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A pupil asks: "Must I stay home from school during menstruation?" Answer plainly.
**Answer: no — menstruation is healthy and normal; with a pad or clean cloth changed regularly, school, work, sport and bathing all continue as usual.**
