# Reproduction, puberty and staying safe — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A deepening voice in a boy is a primary characteristic.
**Answer: False** — it is a secondary characteristic; primary characteristics are the reproductive organs themselves maturing.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Menstruation is a healthy sign of maturing, not a sickness.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Bacterial sexually transmitted infections can be treated with antibiotics.
**Answer: True**
