# Reproduction, puberty and staying safe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The female organs that hold and release eggs are the ______.
**Answer: ovaries**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Bleeding in each menstrual cycle lasts about ______ days.
**Answer: 3–5**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Syphilis, gonorrhea and chancroid are ______ infections, treatable with antibiotics.
**Answer: bacterial**
