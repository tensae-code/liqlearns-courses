# Reproduction, puberty and staying safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Primary characteristics are —
a) the other visible changes of puberty  b) the reproductive organs themselves maturing  c) height growth only
**Answer: b) the reproductive organs themselves maturing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** As printed, puberty comes at about —
a) girls 11–13, boys 14–16  b) girls 14–16, boys 11–13  c) both at 9
**Answer: a) girls 11–13, boys 14–16**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The testes —
a) make sperm  b) release eggs  c) line the womb
**Answer: a) make sperm**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The uterus (womb) is where —
a) sperm are made  b) a fertilised egg grows  c) eggs are stored
**Answer: b) a fertilised egg grows**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The menstrual cycle is about —
a) 7 days  b) 28 days, with 3–5 days of bleeding  c) 90 days
**Answer: b) 28 days, with 3–5 days of bleeding**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Which infection is viral and has no cure?
a) gonorrhea  b) syphilis  c) HIV/AIDS
**Answer: c) HIV/AIDS**
