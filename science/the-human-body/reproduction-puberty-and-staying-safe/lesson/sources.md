This lesson rests on the student book only; no teacher-guide material is used, so no pairing warning is needed here. Honesty note on ages: this Grade 8 book prints girls 11–13 and boys 14–16 for puberty; the earlier Adolescence and its changes lesson in this title, built from the Grade 6 book, prints about 12–14 for girls and 14–16 for boys. Both stand as their own books print them; this lesson teaches the Grade 8 book's ranges and does not silently rewrite the earlier lesson. The menstrual pattern also differs slightly by book (Grade 8: 3–5 days of bleeding; Grade 6 lesson: 4–6 days) — again, each lesson keeps its own book's figures.
