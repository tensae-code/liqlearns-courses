# Hands-on — Reproduction, puberty and staying safe

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sort the changes
On cards, write twelve changes of puberty (use Picture 34109 and the lesson). In pairs, sort them: PRIMARY pile and SECONDARY pile, then girls / boys / both. Defend any card your partner challenges.

## The 28-day circle
Draw a circle of 28 days. Mark days 1–4 as bleeding days. Count forward: when does the next cycle begin? Mark three cycles. This circle is the pattern Sub lesson 3 teaches — draw it from memory the second time.

## Clinic questions
Write the five questions a pupil might be afraid to ask about menstruation or infections, and the plain one-line answer to each, from the lesson. The writing is private; the teacher checks answers, not names.
