# Reproduction, puberty and staying safe — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as sub lessons: what the learner must show at each part, and how practice deepens inside it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Primary and secondary characteristics
Pass mark: define both kinds and sort a mixed list of changes correctly; give the printed ranges (girls about 11–13, boys about 14–16).

## Sub lesson 2 — The organs and what they do
Pass mark: name the main male and female organs and give each one's job from the function tables.

## Sub lesson 3 — Menstruation — the practical facts
Pass mark: state the ~28-day cycle and 3–5 days of bleeding, and answer the practical questions (what to use, school days, pain, bathing) plainly and correctly.

## Sub lesson 4 — Infections that pass sexually — treatable and not
Pass mark: name syphilis, gonorrhea, chancroid and HIV/AIDS, and state the split without error: bacterial and parasitic infections are treatable with antibiotics; viral infections have no cure — prevention is the protection.

The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Growing up — puberty; The male / female reproductive system; The menstrual cycle; the STI lessons); this lesson never copies or grows a Biology file.
