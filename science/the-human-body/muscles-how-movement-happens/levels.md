# Muscles — how movement happens — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as sub lessons: what the learner must show at each part, and how practice deepens inside it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three kinds of muscle
Pass mark: name skeletal, cardiac and smooth muscle and give the place each works — bones, heart, inner-organ walls.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pairs that pull — and who gives the orders
Pass mark: demonstrate a bending and straightening elbow while naming the contracting muscle each time, and sort given actions into voluntary and involuntary. Practice deepens by moving to other joints (knee, fingers) and asking the learner to predict the pair's trade before moving.

## Sub lesson 3 — When muscles fail — and the grass-pea warning
Pass mark: tell strain, sprain and spasm apart, and retell the grass-pea case with its four mitigations, stating which local belief the evidence does not support.

The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Cardiac, skeletal and smooth muscle; Muscle contraction — sliding filaments); this lesson never copies or grows a Biology file.
