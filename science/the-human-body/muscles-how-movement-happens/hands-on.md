# Hands-on — Muscles — how movement happens

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Feel the pair trade
Hand flat on the front of your upper arm: bend the elbow — the front muscle swells and hardens. Hand on the back: straighten — now the back muscle works and the front relaxes. Repeat slowly five times, naming flexor and extensor aloud at each trade.

## Voluntary or not?
With a partner calling the actions, sort them: blink when a hand claps near your face; lift a cup; feel your heartbeat after running; shiver in cold wind. Which did you order, and which ran themselves? Write the two lists.

## The safer-preparation poster
Make a poster of the grass-pea warning for a kitchen wall: the food, the toxin, the harm, and the four safer steps (metal pot, antioxidant seasonings, lemon-water soak, no unripe seeds) — drawn, not only written, so anyone can read it.
