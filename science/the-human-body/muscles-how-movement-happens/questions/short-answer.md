# Muscles — how movement happens — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** List the four safer ways to prepare grass pea.
**Answer: cook in metal pots; add antioxidant seasonings; soak the seeds in lemon water before cooking; avoid unripe seeds.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one voluntary and one involuntary muscle action, and say which kind each is.
**Answer: voluntary — lifting a cup (skeletal muscle, ordered by you); involuntary — the heartbeat (cardiac muscle, runs itself).**
