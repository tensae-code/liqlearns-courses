# Muscles — how movement happens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cardiac muscle is found —
a) in the arm  b) only in the heart  c) in the gut wall
**Answer: b) only in the heart**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Smooth muscle works in —
a) the walls of inner organs and tubes  b) the fingers only  c) the bones
**Answer: a) the walls of inner organs and tubes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Muscles are arranged in pairs at joints because —
a) one muscle is never enough  b) a muscle can only pull, never push — one pulls each way  c) pairs look better
**Answer: b) a muscle can only pull, never push — one pulls each way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When the elbow bends, the flexor ______ and the extensor ______.
a) relaxes / contracts  b) contracts / relaxes  c) both sleep
**Answer: b) contracts / relaxes**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A sprain is an injury to —
a) a ligament at a joint  b) the heart muscle  c) a bone
**Answer: a) a ligament at a joint**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Grass-pea paralysis is caused by —
a) a curse  b) a neurotoxin in the seeds, eaten heavily over a long time  c) cold weather
**Answer: b) a neurotoxin in the seeds, eaten heavily over a long time**
