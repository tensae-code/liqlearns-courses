# Muscles — how movement happens — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Skeletal muscle is the muscle fixed to the bones.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** You can order your heart to stop beating, because cardiac muscle is voluntary.
**Answer: False** — cardiac muscle is involuntary; it runs itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Soaking grass-pea seeds in lemon water is one of the safer-preparation steps.
**Answer: True**
