# Muscles — how movement happens — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The muscle of the gut wall that squeezes food along is ______ muscle.
**Answer: smooth**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The muscle pair at the elbow is the flexor and the ______.
**Answer: extensor**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A muscle contracting suddenly and painfully out of control is a ______.
**Answer: spasm**
