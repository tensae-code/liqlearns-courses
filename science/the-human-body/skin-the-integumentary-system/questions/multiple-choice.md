# Skin — the integumentary system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three layers of skin, top to bottom, are —
a) dermis, epidermis, fat  b) epidermis, dermis, subcutaneous fat  c) fat, dermis, epidermis
**Answer: b) epidermis, dermis, subcutaneous fat**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hair and nails are built of the protein —
a) pepsin  b) keratin  c) bile
**Answer: b) keratin**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of skin's five jobs?
a) making blood  b) temperature control  c) digesting food
**Answer: b) temperature control**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sweat helps the body by —
a) cooling the skin and carrying some wastes out  b) making the skin thicker  c) feeding the hair
**Answer: a) cooling the skin and carrying some wastes out**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Athlete's foot is caused by —
a) sunburn  b) a fungus growing between warm, damp toes  c) blocked pores
**Answer: b) a fungus growing between warm, damp toes**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Long, strong sun exposure over years can lead to —
a) acne  b) athlete's foot  c) skin cancer
**Answer: c) skin cancer**
