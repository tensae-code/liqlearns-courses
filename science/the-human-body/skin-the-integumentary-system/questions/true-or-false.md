# Skin — the integumentary system — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The epidermis is the deepest layer of the skin.
**Answer: False** — the epidermis is the top layer; subcutaneous fat is deepest.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Skin helps hold the body's water in.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Squeezing acne spots is the recommended care for acne.
**Answer: False** — gentle washing helps; squeezing makes acne worse.
