# Skin — the integumentary system — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Recite the care routine for inflamed skin, in order.
**Answer: wash with cool water daily; apply cool compresses; limit contact with common allergens; use wet wipes gently; avoid smoking and alcohol; keep the affected area covered and the skin moist; avoid long sun exposure — and if redness stays, visit a clinic.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the subcutaneous fat layer useful to the body?
**Answer: it cushions the body against knocks and holds its warmth in.**
