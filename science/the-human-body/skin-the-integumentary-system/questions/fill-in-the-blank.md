# Skin — the integumentary system — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The thick middle layer of skin, holding hair roots and sweat glands, is the ______.
**Answer: dermis**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The sense endings that feel touch, heat and pain sit in the ______.
**Answer: dermis**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Hair keeps warmth in two ways: as a barrier, and by trapping a layer of ______ air against the skin.
**Answer: warm**
