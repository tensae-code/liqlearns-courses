# Hands-on — Skin — the integumentary system

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Layer hunt
With a partner, look closely at the skin of a hand under the best light you have: find a hair, its root place, a nail and its pale half-moon, and the fine lines of the epidermis. Draw what you see and label the three layers on your drawing from a cutaway picture.

## The cool-compress routine
Practise the inflamed-skin routine on a partner's forearm (with leave): wash with cool water, lay on a cool compress, keep the skin moist, cover it lightly. Say each step aloud as you do it — the routine in order is the pass mark.

## If it is hard
Work by touch: feel the difference between nail (hard keratin), hair (soft keratin) and the skin of the palm and the back of the hand, and say which is which with eyes closed.
