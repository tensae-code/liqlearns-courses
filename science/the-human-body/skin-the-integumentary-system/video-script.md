# Video script — Skin — the integumentary system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your skin is not a wrapper. It is an organ — with three layers.
On top, the **epidermis**. Under it, the **dermis**. Deepest, a layer of **fat**.
Your hair and your nails are built of a protein called **keratin**.

Skin does five jobs. It **protects** you. It controls your **temperature**. It **senses** touch, heat and cold. It **sweats** — cooling you, carrying waste out. And it holds your **water** in.

Skin can be hurt: burns, athlete's foot, too much sun, acne, eczema.
Each trouble has a cause — and a care.
Care for inflamed skin gently: cool water, a cool cloth, keep it moist, keep it covered, stay out of long sun.
If redness stays — go to the clinic.

Your skin works for you every day. Work for it a little, too.
