# Skin — the integumentary system — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as sub lessons: what the learner must show at each part, and how practice deepens inside it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three layers, and keratin — hair and nails
Pass mark: name epidermis, dermis and subcutaneous fat in order, top to bottom, and say that hair and nails are built of the protein keratin.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five jobs of skin
Pass mark: list all five jobs — protection, temperature control, sensation, sweating, holding water in — and give one everyday example of each.

## Sub lesson 3 — When skin is in trouble — and caring for it
Pass mark: match burns, athlete's foot, sun damage, acne and eczema to cause and care; recite the inflamed-skin care routine in order and explain hair's two insulation ways. The routine and the hair mechanism are teacher-guide evidence and are passed as evidence, labelled as such.

The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Sense organs — the skin); this lesson never copies or grows a Biology file.
