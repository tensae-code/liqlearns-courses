# Lesson plan — Skin — the integumentary system

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** Skin — the integumentary system
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Human Body Systems and Health, the first system of the unit, inside The Human Body (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the skin is a three-layer organ — epidermis, dermis, subcutaneous fat — with hair and nails built of keratin; it does five jobs from protection to temperature control, and its troubles (burns, athlete's foot, sun damage, acne, eczema) each have a cause and a care.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The human body — systems working together (what a system is) and on Parts of the body and the five senses (skin as the sense of touch). The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Sense organs — the skin); this lesson is the Grade 8 version, built new, with the guide's skin-care routine filed as labelled teacher evidence inside it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Three layers, and keratin — hair and nails** — epidermis on top, dermis beneath, subcutaneous fat below; keratin, the building protein of hair and nails.
2. **The five jobs of skin** — protection, temperature control, sensation, sweating out waste and cooling, and holding the body's water in.
3. **When skin is in trouble — and caring for it** — burns, athlete's foot, ultraviolet light and skin cancer, acne and eczema, each with cause and care; the teacher guide's care routine for inflamed skin and how hair insulates, filed as labelled teacher evidence.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Human Body — The human body — systems working together. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the skin's three layers in order and say what keratin builds.
2. List the skin's five jobs.
3. Match the skin troubles — burns, athlete's foot, sun damage, acne, eczema — to a cause and a care for each.
4. Recite the care routine for inflamed skin and explain the two ways hair insulates (teacher-guide evidence, labelled as such).

## What you need
A mirror or a partner for looking at skin, hair and nails closely; a cool wet cloth for the care routine.
