# Lesson plan — Cells — photosynthesis and its partnership with respiration

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** Cells — photosynthesis and its partnership with respiration
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Cell as the Basis of Life, inside The Human Body (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in chloroplasts, chlorophyll traps light to turn carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen; photosynthesis and respiration interlock — each one's products are the other's raw materials.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Cells — respiration: releasing energy from food (the partner process, learned first so the exchange can close the loop) and on the chloroplast from the organelles lesson. The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Photosynthesis; The photosynthesis equation); this lesson is the first-depth Grade 7 version.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Photosynthesis — the chloroplast as food factory** — in chloroplasts, chlorophyll traps light energy to turn carbon dioxide + water into glucose + oxygen; it feeds nearly all life and supplies its oxygen.
2. **The great exchange — partnership with respiration** — respiration returns carbon dioxide and water; the two processes cycle gases between organisms and the environment in one loop.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cells — respiration: releasing energy from food. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Write the photosynthesis word equation and name the organelle and pigment.
2. Say the two things photosynthesis gives the world: food (glucose) and oxygen.
3. Explain the cycle: which gas goes leaf → animal, which returns, and what respiration gives back.

## What you need
A green leaf; paper and a pencil; sunlight or a bright window.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks plants only photosynthesise and never respire → plant cells respire too, day and night; photosynthesis runs only in the light, in chloroplasts.
- Swaps the gases → leaf gives OUT oxygen (animal breathes it in); animal gives OUT carbon dioxide (leaf takes it in). Follow the arrows.
