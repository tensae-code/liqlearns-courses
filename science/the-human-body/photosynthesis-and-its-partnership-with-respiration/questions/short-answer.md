# Cells — photosynthesis and its partnership with respiration — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is photosynthesis said to feed nearly all life?
**Answer: Almost every food chain starts with glucose made by photosynthesis; it also supplies the oxygen animals breathe.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the partnership in one sentence.
**Answer: Photosynthesis's products (glucose, oxygen) are respiration's raw materials, and respiration's products (carbon dioxide, water) are photosynthesis's raw materials.**
