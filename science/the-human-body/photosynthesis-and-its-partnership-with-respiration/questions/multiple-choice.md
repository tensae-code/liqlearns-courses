# Cells — photosynthesis and its partnership with respiration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Photosynthesis happens in the —
a) mitochondria  b) chloroplasts  c) nucleus
**Answer: b) chloroplasts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The green pigment that traps light is —
a) chlorophyll  b) haemoglobin  c) DNA
**Answer: a) chlorophyll**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Photosynthesis turns carbon dioxide + water into —
a) glucose + oxygen  b) alcohol + carbon dioxide  c) lactic acid
**Answer: a) glucose + oxygen**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Respiration returns to the plant —
a) oxygen only  b) carbon dioxide and water  c) chlorophyll
**Answer: b) carbon dioxide and water**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The gas that travels leaf → animal is —
a) carbon dioxide  b) oxygen  c) nitrogen
**Answer: b) oxygen**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Photosynthesis supplies nearly all life with —
a) food and oxygen  b) alcohol  c) cell walls
**Answer: a) food and oxygen**
