# Hands-on — Cells — photosynthesis and its partnership with respiration

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only the page.

## Leaf in the light
Put a green leafy plant (or a fresh leaf in a glass of water) in bright sunlight and watch for tiny bubbles forming on the leaf — oxygen leaving the leaf as it photosynthesises. A shaded twin leaf is the comparison: fewer or no bubbles.

## Breathe the loop
Stand beside a plant. Breathe out slowly toward it and say the loop aloud: "My carbon dioxide to you; your oxygen to me." Then write the loop as a diagram from memory and check it against the lesson's picture.
