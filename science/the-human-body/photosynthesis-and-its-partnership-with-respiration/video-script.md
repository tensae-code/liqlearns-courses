# Video script — Cells — photosynthesis and its partnership with respiration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The leaf traps light with **chlorophyll**, in the **chloroplast**.
Carbon dioxide plus water becomes **glucose plus oxygen**.
The plant makes food — and gives the world its oxygen.

Animals breathe the oxygen in, and breathe carbon dioxide back.
Respiration returns what photosynthesis used.
Leaf to animal, animal to leaf — **one great loop**.
