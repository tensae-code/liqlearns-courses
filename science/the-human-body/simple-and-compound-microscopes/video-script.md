# Video script — The microscope — simple and compound, and total magnification

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One lens. That is a **simple** microscope.
A hand lens. Reading glasses. Ten to twenty times bigger.

Two lenses together. That is a **compound** microscope.
Eyepiece times objective. Ten times forty is **four hundred times**.
Multiply — never add.
