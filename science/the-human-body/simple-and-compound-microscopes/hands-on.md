# Hands-on — The microscope — simple and compound, and total magnification

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only the page.

## Naked eye, then lens
Choose a small insect (dead, found) and a line of small book print. Sketch each one as the naked eye sees it, then as the hand lens sees it. Label the new parts the lens revealed.

## Magnification sums
Write the three objective lenses — 4×, 40×, 100× — on cards. Pair each with a 10× eyepiece card and write the product on the back. Test a partner: they must multiply, not add.
