# The microscope — simple and compound, and total magnification — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A simple microscope has —
a) two lens sets  b) one convex lens  c) a mirror only
**Answer: b) one convex lens**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A simple microscope magnifies about —
a) 2×  b) 10–20×  c) 1,000×
**Answer: b) 10–20×**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a simple microscope?
a) a hand lens  b) a telescope  c) a camera
**Answer: a) a hand lens**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Total magnification = —
a) eyepiece + objective  b) eyepiece × objective  c) objective − eyepiece
**Answer: b) eyepiece × objective**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A 10× eyepiece with a 40× objective gives —
a) 50×  b) 400×  c) 4,000×
**Answer: b) 400×**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Objectives on a compound microscope run from —
a) 4× to 100×  b) 1× to 2×  c) 200× to 300×
**Answer: a) 4× to 100×**
