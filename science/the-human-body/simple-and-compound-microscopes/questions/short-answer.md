# The microscope — simple and compound, and total magnification — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Practical 4.1's comparison, what two things do you view with the naked eye and then with a hand lens?
**Answer: An insect and book letters.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do reading glasses help a reader?
**Answer: Their single convex lenses magnify the print, like a simple microscope.**
