# The microscope — simple and compound, and total magnification — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lens you look through on a compound microscope is the ______.
**Answer: eyepiece**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 10× eyepiece × 100× objective = ______ total magnification.
**Answer: 1,000×**
