# Video script — Cells — shape, size and function

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A cell is tiny. Ten to twenty micrometres. Bacteria are tinier still.
Small is good: food and air cross a small cell fast.

And the shape fits the job.
A **disc** carries oxygen. A **spindle** contracts. A **long, branched** cell sends messages.
Shape tells you the job.
