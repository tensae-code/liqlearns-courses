# Hands-on — Cells — shape, size and function

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The marble test
Drop a marble and a large ball into water with a little food colouring in it (or picture it): the colour reaches the marble's middle far sooner. Say the cell lesson aloud: small crosses fast — that is why cells stay small.

## Shape cards
Draw the three cells on three cards, jobs on three more cards. Shuffle and match shape to job against the clock; then explain each match in one sentence.
