# Cells — shape, size and function — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A typical cell measures about —
a) 1–2 mm  b) 10–20 µm  c) 1 cm
**Answer: b) 10–20 µm**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bacteria are about — long.
a) 1–10 µm  b) 100 µm  c) 1 mm
**Answer: a) 1–10 µm**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cells stay small so that —
a) they are hard to see  b) nutrients, gases and wastes can cross them easily  c) they can hold more water
**Answer: b) nutrients, gases and wastes can cross them easily**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Biconcave disc cells that carry oxygen are —
a) nerve cells  b) muscle cells  c) red blood cells
**Answer: c) red blood cells**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Spindle-shaped cells that contract are —
a) muscle cells  b) red blood cells  c) bacteria
**Answer: a) muscle cells**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Long, branched cells transmit —
a) oxygen  b) messages  c) food
**Answer: b) messages** — nerve cells.
