# Cells — shape, size and function — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why would a very large cell starve at its centre?
**Answer: Materials cross its surface too slowly to reach the far middle in time.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the shape and job of a muscle cell.
**Answer: Spindle-shaped; it contracts (shortens) to move the body.**
