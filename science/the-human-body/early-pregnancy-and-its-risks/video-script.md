Today: **Early pregnancy — and its risks** — Angola's way: teach it right after reproduction, in time to prevent it.

Early pregnancy is pregnancy while the mother's own body is still growing — two growings loaded onto one body.

Its risks fall on her health, on the baby's start, and on her schooling. They are taught plainly so they can be avoided.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
