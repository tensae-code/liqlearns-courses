# Hands-on — Early pregnancy — and its risks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two growings timeline
Draw one timeline of a girl's growing years and mark where her body finishes growing. Mark where a baby's growing would fall if pregnancy came early, and say what the overlap means.

## Risk sorting
Write risks on slips — to the mother's health, to the baby, to schooling. Read short descriptions of early pregnancy's effects and sort each under its direction of risk.
