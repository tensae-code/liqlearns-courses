# Early pregnancy — and its risks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Early pregnancy is pregnancy that comes…
a) After schooling ends
b) While the mother's own body is still growing
c) Only in cities
d) In old age
**Answer: b) While the mother's own body is still growing**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The double load of early pregnancy is…
a) Two babies always
b) The mother's own unfinished growth and the baby's growth on one body
c) Two schools
d) Two families
**Answer: b) The mother's own unfinished growth and the baby's growth on one body**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) One risk of early pregnancy falls on…
a) Only the neighbours
b) Her schooling and the future built on it
c) The weather
d) Nothing at all
**Answer: b) Her schooling and the future built on it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The lesson teaches the risks plainly so that…
a) Learners are frightened
b) They can be avoided — early pregnancy is a risk to be prevented
c) Marks are given
d) The book is longer
**Answer: b) They can be avoided — early pregnancy is a risk to be prevented**
