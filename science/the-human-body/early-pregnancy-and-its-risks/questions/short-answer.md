# Early pregnancy — and its risks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does "early" mean in early pregnancy?
**Answer:** Early relative to the mother's own growing: the pregnancy comes while her body is still growing, before that growth is finished.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the risks to the baby in early pregnancy.
**Answer:** A still-growing body may not give the baby all it needs, so the baby's start — its growth before birth and its health at birth — is at risk.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does Angola place this lesson in Grade 6, after reproduction?
**Answer:** Because the learners have just been taught reproduction and sexual life; the next thing is the risk that follows when pregnancy comes early, taught in time to be prevented.
