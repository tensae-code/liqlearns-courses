# The social self — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The social self — the self among others.
**Answer:** The **social self** is the self as it exists among other people: the part of who we are that is formed, shown and changed in our relations with others — how we see ourselves through others' eyes, and how we present ourselves to them.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The chapter's arc — mind, brain, society.
**Answer:** Australia's own order carries the meaning: what psychology is (11.1) names the science; the brain and cognition (11.2) give the mind its organ; the social self (11.3) completes the person — a self is not finished inside one skull, but in the company it keeps.
