# Hands-on — The social self

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two columns of me (paper and pencil)
Draw two columns: 'me alone' and 'me with others'. Fill each with how you speak, act and feel in that setting. Circle one difference and write under it what formed it — whose eyes, whose company.

## Ask the company (a partner)
Ask a partner: 'When are you most yourself — alone, or with people?' Retell their answer in your own words, then say where the social self appears in it.
