# The lungs and breathing — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. Breathing takes in ___ and gives out carbon dioxide.**
**Answer: oxygen**

**2. There are ___ lungs, one on each side of the heart.**
**Answer: two**
**3. The trachea in Amharic is ______.**
**Answer: ትንቧ**

**4. Breathing in is ______ (ምጋት/ኢ-ምጋት — pick the 'in' word).**
**Answer: ምጋት**
