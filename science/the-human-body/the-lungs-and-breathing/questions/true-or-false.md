# The lungs and breathing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. The lungs are spongy and fill with air.**
**Answer: True**

**2. We breathe in carbon dioxide because the body needs it.**
**Answer: False**
**3. The nose's hairs filter dust and microbes.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Inhaled and exhaled air hold the same amount of carbon dioxide.**
**Answer: False**

**5. The lungs have muscles of their own that pump air in and out.**
**Answer: False** — the lungs have no muscle; the diaphragm and ribs do the pumping.

**6. TB (tuberculosis) is one of the diseases in the Grade 8 health-centre survey.**
**Answer: True**

**7. The COVID-19 row in this lesson is presented as current information.**
**Answer: False** — it is teacher-guide evidence dated to the guide, taught as transmission-and-prevention.
