# The lungs and breathing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. The lungs are spongy and fill with air.**
**Answer: True**

**2. We breathe in carbon dioxide because the body needs it.**
**Answer: False**
**3. The nose's hairs filter dust and microbes.**
**Answer: True**

**4. Inhaled and exhaled air hold the same amount of carbon dioxide.**
**Answer: False**
