# The lungs and breathing — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. What two gases do the lungs exchange?**
**Answer: oxygen in, carbon dioxide out**
**2. Name the air road's parts in order.**
**Answer: Nose, pharynx, trachea, bronchi, bronchioles, alveoli.**

**3. Why do the balloons in the bottle-lung model fill when the sheet is pulled down?**
**Answer: pulling the sheet down grows the space inside the bottle, so air rushes in through the tube and fills the balloons — as the diaphragm's movement draws air into the lungs.**

**4. Name four diseases from the Grade 8 respiratory survey.**
**Answer: any four of: asthma, sinusitis, influenza, COPD, bronchitis, pneumonia, TB, lung cancer.**
