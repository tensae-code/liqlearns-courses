# The lungs and breathing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The lungs are —**
a) one hard bone  b) a spongy pair  c) part of the stomach
**Answer: b) a spongy pair**

**2. When we breathe in, the lungs take in —**
a) carbon dioxide  b) oxygen  c) smoke
**Answer: b) oxygen**

**3. When we breathe out, the lungs give out —**
a) oxygen  b) water  c) carbon dioxide
**Answer: c) carbon dioxide**

**4. The lungs sit —**
a) in the legs  b) inside the chest, either side of the heart  c) inside the skull
**Answer: b) inside the chest, either side of the heart**
**5. Where are gases exchanged?**
a) the nose  b) the alveoli (ትንከረቶች)  c) the pharynx
**Answer: b) the alveoli (ትንከረቶች)**

**6. In the bottle-lung model, the rubber sheet stands for —**
a) the ribs  b) the diaphragm  c) the trachea
**Answer: b) the diaphragm**

**7. Exhaled air holds how much oxygen?**
a) 20.95%  b) 16%  c) 78%
**Answer: b) 16%**

**8. Which is one of the seven printed harms of smoking?**
a) stronger lungs  b) harm to an unborn baby  c) cleaner teeth
**Answer: b) harm to an unborn baby**
