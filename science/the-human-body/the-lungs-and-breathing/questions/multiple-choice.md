# The lungs and breathing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The lungs are —**
a) one hard bone  b) a spongy pair  c) part of the stomach
**Answer: b) a spongy pair**

**2. When we breathe in, the lungs take in —**
a) carbon dioxide  b) oxygen  c) smoke
**Answer: b) oxygen**

**3. When we breathe out, the lungs give out —**
a) oxygen  b) water  c) carbon dioxide
**Answer: c) carbon dioxide**

**4. The lungs sit —**
a) in the legs  b) inside the chest, either side of the heart  c) inside the skull
**Answer: b) inside the chest, either side of the heart**
