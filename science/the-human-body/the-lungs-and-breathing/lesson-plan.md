# Lesson plan — The lungs and breathing

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The lungs and breathing
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the inside systems, after the outside parts and senses (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the lungs are a spongy pair inside the chest that take in oxygen from the air and give out carbon dioxide
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Parts of the body and the five senses — and beside The heart: the chest's two great workers, heart and lungs, side by side.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the lungs: a spongy pair inside the chest.
2. Say what breathing takes in: oxygen.
3. Say what breathing gives out: carbon dioxide.

## What you need
Your own breath — and a hand on the ribs to feel the chest rise and fall.

## If the learner is stuck
- Swaps the gases → the rhyme is 'in with oxygen, out with carbon dioxide': the body takes what it needs and sends back what it has used.
- Thinks there is one lung → a pair, one each side of the heart — two hands on two sides of the chest make it plain.
