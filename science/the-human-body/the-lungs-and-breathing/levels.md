# The lungs and breathing — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: name the lungs as a spongy pair that takes in oxygen and gives out carbon dioxide.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also walks the breathing road in order with the Amharic part-names, works the bottle-lung model, reads Table 2.1's in/out numbers, and recites the seven harms of smoking.

Grade 8 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also walks the full air path in order to the alveoli, explains that the diaphragm and ribs do the pumping because the lungs have no muscle, builds the bottle-and-balloon lung, and names the survey diseases; the COVID-19 row is passed as teacher-guide evidence dated to the guide, never as current information. The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (The breathing system — air's path to the alveoli; COVID-19 — the spiked virus); this lesson never copies or grows a Biology file.
