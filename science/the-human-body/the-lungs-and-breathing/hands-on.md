# Hands-on — The lungs and breathing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel the fill
Hands on ribs: breathe in slowly and feel the chest swell; breathe out and feel it fall. The sponges are filling and emptying.

## Breath count
Count quiet breaths for one minute. Then run on the spot for a minute and count again — the lungs answer work just as the heart does.
