# Hands-on — The lungs and breathing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel the fill
Hands on ribs: breathe in slowly and feel the chest swell; breathe out and feel it fall. The sponges are filling and emptying.

## Breath count
Count quiet breaths for one minute. Then run on the spot for a minute and count again — the lungs answer work just as the heart does.

## Grade 8 — build the bottle-and-balloon lung
The unit project: a cut bottle, a Y-tube, two small balloons for lungs and a rubber sheet for the diaphragm. Pull the sheet down — the balloons fill; release — they empty. Present it and explain why the balloons cannot fill themselves: the lungs have no muscle; the diaphragm does the pumping.

## Grade 8 — the health-centre survey
Complete the survey table locally (with a teacher): for each named disease — asthma, sinusitis, influenza, COPD, bronchitis, pneumonia, TB, lung cancer — record whether the local health centre treats it and how it is prevented. The COVID-19 row is filled from the teacher guide's account and labelled as guide evidence, dated to the guide.
