# Teeth: two sets, four jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. How many teeth are in the milk set?**
a) 20  b) 32  c) 12
**Answer: a) 20**

**2. How many teeth are in the permanent set?**
a) 20  b) 28  c) 32
**Answer: c) 32**

**3. Which teeth bite and cut?**
a) molars  b) incisors  c) canines
**Answer: b) incisors**

**4. Which teeth pierce and tear?**
a) canines  b) premolars  c) incisors
**Answer: a) canines**

**5. Which teeth grind food small?**
a) incisors  b) canines  c) molars
**Answer: c) molars**

**6. What do premolars do?**
a) grind  b) crush  c) bite
**Answer: b) crush**
