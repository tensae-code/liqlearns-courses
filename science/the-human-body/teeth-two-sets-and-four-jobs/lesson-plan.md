# Lesson plan — Teeth: two sets, four jobs

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** Teeth: two sets, four jobs
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the digestive road begins at the mouth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a person gets two sets of teeth in a life — the milk set of 20 and the permanent set of 32 — and the four kinds of tooth each do a different job
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The digestive system — the mouth is where that tube begins, and teeth are the mouth's tools.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two sets of teeth and how many teeth each set holds: milk set 20, permanent set 32.
2. Name the four kinds of permanent tooth and each kind's job: incisors bite and cut, canines pierce and tear, premolars crush, molars grind.
3. Say how teeth are cared for: clean them morning and evening, with the local chewing stick or the factory brush.

## What you need
A mirror to look at your own teeth; a chewing stick and a toothbrush to compare, if you have them.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the two sets' numbers → milk is the smaller set for the smaller mouth: 20 first, then 32 for life.
- Thinks all teeth do the same work → the shapes differ because the jobs differ: flat front teeth cut, pointed canines tear, broad back teeth grind.
