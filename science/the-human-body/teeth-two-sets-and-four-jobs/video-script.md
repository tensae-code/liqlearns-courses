# Video script — Teeth: two sets, four jobs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two sets of teeth in one life.
First the **milk set** — twenty teeth.
Then the **permanent set** — thirty-two.
Front teeth **bite and cut**. Pointed teeth **pierce and tear**.
**Premolars crush. Molars grind.**
Clean them morning and evening — with a chewing stick, or with a brush.
