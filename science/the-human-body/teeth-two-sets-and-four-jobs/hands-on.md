# Hands-on — Teeth: two sets, four jobs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count and compare
With a partner and a mirror, count each other's teeth. Write both counts and say which set each of you is growing into.

## The two cleaning tools
Lay a chewing stick and a factory brush side by side, as the book pictures them. Clean with one in the morning and the other in the evening and say what each does well.
