# Smart prosthetic limbs — technology that helps the body

## Do it first
Your arms and legs do work for your body every day — holding, walking, running. If a limb is missing, that work still needs doing. What could stand in the missing limb's place? Jordan's Grade 2 book answers with an enrichment on **smart prosthetic limbs**.

## Sub lesson 1 — A made limb that helps the body

A **prosthetic limb** is a limb made by people to stand in for a missing limb, so the body can still do the work that limb did. A **smart** prosthetic limb adds technology to the made limb, so it can help the body move and act more like the limb it replaces.

That is why Jordan's book places this enrichment inside its human-body-and-health unit, and why its teacher's guide builds the lesson plan around **appreciating the role of science and technology**: the body lesson and the technology lesson meet in one made thing — a limb that is not grown, but designed, built and fitted to help a person.

## The key ideas

- A **prosthetic limb** is a made limb that stands in for a missing limb and helps the body do that limb's work.
- A **smart** prosthetic uses technology so the made limb works better with the body.
- Jordan's guide frames the lesson as appreciating the role of **science and technology** in helping the body.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: what a prosthetic limb is, and what the smart technology adds. Your companion may stop you once and ask one question; answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself
1. Name one job each limb of yours does, and say which job a prosthetic for that limb would have to help with first.
2. In two sentences, explain the difference between a grown limb and a prosthetic limb.
3. Teach someone at home what a smart prosthetic limb is.
