# Video script — Smart prosthetic limbs — technology that helps the body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A limb can be missing. The body's work still needs doing.
So people **make** a limb to stand in its place.
A **smart prosthetic** adds technology, so the made limb helps the body move and do.
Science and technology, helping the body.
