# Hands-on — Smart prosthetic limbs — technology that helps the body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The helper test
Try a simple job — picking up a cup, opening a door — first with your whole hand, then with one finger held still, then with a spoon held like a tool doing the hand's job. Write what each step teaches about why a made limb must be shaped for its job.

## Design a helper
Draw a made limb for one job you choose (walking, holding, running). Label: the limb it stands in for, the job it helps with, and where the smart technology sits.
