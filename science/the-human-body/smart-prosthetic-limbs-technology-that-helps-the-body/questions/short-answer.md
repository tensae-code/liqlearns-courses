# Smart prosthetic limbs — technology that helps the body — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What job does a prosthetic limb do?**
**Answer: it stands in for a missing limb and helps the body do that limb's work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a prosthetic limb smart?**
**Answer: technology that helps it work with the body**
