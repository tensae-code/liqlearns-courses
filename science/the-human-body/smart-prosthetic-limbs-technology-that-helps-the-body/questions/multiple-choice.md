# Smart prosthetic limbs — technology that helps the body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A prosthetic limb is —**
a) a limb that grows back  b) a made limb that stands in for a missing limb  c) a kind of bone
**Answer: b) a made limb that stands in for a missing limb**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the smart technology in a smart prosthetic do?**
a) helps the made limb work with the body  b) paints the limb  c) nothing
**Answer: a) helps the made limb work with the body**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Jordan's guide builds this lesson around appreciating —**
a) speed  b) the role of science and technology  c) drawing
**Answer: b) the role of science and technology**
