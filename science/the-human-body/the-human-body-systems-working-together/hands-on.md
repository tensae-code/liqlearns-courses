# Hands-on — The human body — systems working together

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The parts pile
Take a simple thing apart with leave — a pen, a torch, a bicycle bell. Lay the parts out. Say what the pile can do (nothing) and what the whole thing does. Then name the parts' jobs one by one: that is a system, in your hands.

## System spotting
On a walk or in the market, spot work being done by teams of parts: a mill, a water pump, a tej house kitchen. For each, list the parts and the one job they do together — then match it to the body system with the same kind of job.
