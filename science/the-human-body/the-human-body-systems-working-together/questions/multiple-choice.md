# The human body — systems working together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A body system is —
a) one organ working alone  b) a group of organs working together on one job  c) a pile of body parts
**Answer: b) a group of organs working together on one job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the car analogy, a pile of car parts —
a) drives slowly  b) does nothing — organised parts do the work  c) works better than a whole car
**Answer: b) does nothing — organised parts do the work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many systems does this unit walk through?
a) five  b) six  c) seven
**Answer: c) seven**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The integumentary system is —
a) skin, hair and nails  b) bones and joints  c) heart and vessels
**Answer: a) skin, hair and nails**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is each system studied with its diseases?
a) for spelling practice  b) because a failure in one system is felt in others, and care is part of knowing the system  c) diseases are not studied
**Answer: b) because a failure in one system is felt in others, and care is part of knowing the system**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Oxygen brought in by breathing reaches the muscles through —
a) the digestive system  b) the skeleton  c) the circulatory system
**Answer: c) the circulatory system**
