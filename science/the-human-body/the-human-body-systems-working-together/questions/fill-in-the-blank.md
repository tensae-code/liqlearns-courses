# The human body — systems working together — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ______ is a group of organs working together on one job.
**Answer: system**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ______ system — heart, vessels and blood — is the body's transport network.
**Answer: circulatory**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Muscles can move the body because they pull on the ______ system, the frame of bones.
**Answer: skeletal**
