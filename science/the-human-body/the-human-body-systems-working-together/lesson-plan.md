# Lesson plan — The human body — systems working together

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The human body — systems working together
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Human Body Systems and Health, the frame lesson that opens the unit, inside The Human Body (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a body system is a group of organs working together on one job — parts alone do nothing, organised systems do the work — and the body runs seven systems in this unit: integumentary (skin), muscular, skeletal, digestive, respiratory, circulatory and reproductive.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 7 cell lessons (cells → tissues → organs) and on Parts of the body and the five senses; it is the map for the six Grade 8 system lessons that follow it in this title. The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (The systems of the human body); this lesson is the Grade 8 first-depth frame, built new.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Parts alone do nothing — what a system is** — the car analogy: a pile of car parts goes nowhere; organised, the parts do the work. A system is organs working together on one job.
2. **The seven systems, one by one** — skin, muscles, skeleton, digestion, breathing, circulation, reproduction: each named with its one job.
3. **Systems together — and keeping them healthy** — no system works alone; each system in this unit is studied with its diseases and its local health care.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a system is: organs working together on one job.
2. Explain the car analogy: parts alone do nothing; organised parts do the work.
3. Name the seven systems of this unit and give each one's job in a few words.
4. Say why the unit studies each system together with its diseases and health care.

## What you need
Nothing but the lesson; a real car, bicycle or mill in the learner's own surroundings makes the analogy land.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up organ and system → an organ is one part (the heart); a system is the working team (heart + vessels + blood).
- Cannot hold seven names → learn them in the body's own order, top to toe: skin, muscles, skeleton, digestion, breathing, circulation, reproduction.
