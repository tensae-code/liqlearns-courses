# The human body — systems working together — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as sub lessons: what the learner must show at each part, and how practice deepens inside it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Parts alone do nothing — what a system is
Pass mark: define a system (organs working together on one job) and retell the car analogy in the learner's own words — parts alone do nothing, organised systems do the work.

## Sub lesson 2 — The seven systems, one by one
Pass mark: name all seven systems of the unit and give each one's job without the picture. Practice deepens by shuffling the order and asking job → system as well as system → job.

## Sub lesson 3 — Systems together — and keeping them healthy
Pass mark: give one example of two systems working together (breathing brings in the air, circulation carries it) and say why each system is studied with its diseases and care.

The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (The systems of the human body); this lesson never copies or grows a Biology file.
