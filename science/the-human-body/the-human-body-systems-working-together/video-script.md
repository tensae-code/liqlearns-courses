# Video script — The human body — systems working together (unit-intro tour of the seven systems)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This is a FULL script: the placement names a unit-intro script touring the seven systems in one pass. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at a car taken apart. Wheels here. Engine there. Seats in a pile.
Can the pile drive? No.
Parts alone do nothing. Put them together, each part doing its job — now the car goes.

Your body works the same way.
A **system** is a group of **organs working together on one job**.
This unit walks through seven systems. Here they are.

**One — the skin system.** Skin, hair and nails. It covers you and protects you.

**Two — the muscular system.** Muscles pull on bones, and you move.

**Three — the skeletal system.** Bones and joints. The frame that holds you up.

**Four — the digestive system.** It takes food apart, so the body can use it.

**Five — the respiratory system.** It brings air in, and takes waste air out.

**Six — the circulatory system.** Heart and vessels. It carries everything, everywhere.

**Seven — the reproductive system.** The system that makes new life.

Seven systems. No system works alone.
And for each one, we will also learn: what can go wrong — and how people here keep it healthy.

That is the journey of this unit. Let's begin.
