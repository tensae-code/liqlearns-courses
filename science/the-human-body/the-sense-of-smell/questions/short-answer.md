# The sense of smell — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** How does smell tell us about things we cannot see?
**Answer: Odours travel through the air to the nose, so we can know food is cooking or smoke is present before we see anything.**

**2.** Why does food taste weak when we have a cold?
**Answer: Because smell and taste work together, and a blocked nose cannot do its half of the work.**

**3.** Name two warning smells.
**Answer: Smoke and spoiled food (gas is another warning smell).**

**4.** How do we keep the nose safe?
**Answer: We keep it clean, push nothing into it, and never sniff unknown or strong chemicals closely.**
