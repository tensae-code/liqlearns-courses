# The sense of smell — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The organ of smell is the _____.
**Answer: nose**

**2.** Smell and _____ work together when we eat.
**Answer: taste**

**3.** The smell of _____ warns us a fire may be near.
**Answer: smoke**

**4.** We never push things into the _____.
**Answer: nose**
