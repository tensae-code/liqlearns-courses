# The sense of smell — multiple choice


**1.** The organ of smell is the…
a) Ear  b) Nose  c) Tongue  d) Skin
**Answer: b) Nose**

**2.** Smell helps us know things we cannot…
a) Hear  b) See  c) Touch  d) Taste
**Answer: b) See**

**3.** When the nose is blocked by a cold, food seems to…
a) Taste stronger  b) Lose its taste  c) Smell sweeter  d) Turn cold
**Answer: b) Lose its taste**

**4.** The smell of spoiled food tells us to…
a) Eat it quickly  b) Throw it away  c) Cook it longer  d) Share it
**Answer: b) Throw it away**

**5.** We must never sniff closely…
a) A flower  b) Cooking food  c) Unknown chemicals  d) An orange
**Answer: c) Unknown chemicals**

**6.** Smell works together with the sense of…
a) Hearing  b) Sight  c) Taste  d) Touch
**Answer: c) Taste**
