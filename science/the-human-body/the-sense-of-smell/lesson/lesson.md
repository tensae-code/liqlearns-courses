## Sub lesson 1 — Smelling tells us about the world

The **nose** is the organ of smell. With it we know things we cannot see: that food is cooking, that a flower is near, that smoke is in the air. Smell works together with taste — food seems to lose its taste when the nose is blocked by a cold.

Kuwait's Grade 1 book teaches smell as a way of knowing: the child names things by their smell with eyes closed, the way the book's own lesson does it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Keeping the nose safe

The nose must be treated gently. We do not push things into the nose, and we do not smell strong chemicals or unknown bottles closely — a strong smell can harm the nose and the breathing passages.

Smell is also a warning sense: the smell of smoke, of gas or of spoiled food tells us to move away or to throw the food away. Keeping the nose clean and never sniffing unknown things keeps the warning sense working.

## The key ideas

- The nose is the organ of the sense of smell.
- Smell tells us about things we cannot see, and works with taste.
- Smell warns us: smoke, gas and spoiled food.
- Never push things into the nose or sniff unknown chemicals.
