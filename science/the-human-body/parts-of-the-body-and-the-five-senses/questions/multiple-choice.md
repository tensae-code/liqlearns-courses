# Parts of the body and the five senses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which part of the body do the hands belong to?**
a) the head  b) the arm  c) the leg
**Answer: b) the arm** — the hands are at the end of the arms.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does hair do on the head?**
a) it sees  b) it covers and protects the head  c) it hears
**Answer: b) it covers and protects the head**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which sense organ tells you a stone is rough?**
a) the tongue  b) the skin  c) the nose
**Answer: b) the skin** — the skin feels rough and smooth.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) You smell injera cooking before you see it. Which sense did that?**
a) the eyes  b) the ears  c) the nose
**Answer: c) the nose**
