# Hands-on — Parts of the body and the five senses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Point and name
Go through the body from head to heel with a partner. One points, the other names, then swap. Miss nothing: hair, forehead, lips, shin, heel.

## The senses walk
Walk slowly around your home or schoolyard. Stop five times. Each time, name one thing a different sense tells you: a colour seen, a sound heard, a smell, a taste remembered, a rough or smooth touch.

## Make a person
Model a person from clay or cloth, the way the Grade 2 book does. Give your person every outside part, and point to the five sense organs when you show it.
