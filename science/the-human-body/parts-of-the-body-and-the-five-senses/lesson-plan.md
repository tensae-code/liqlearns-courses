# Lesson plan — Parts of the body and the five senses

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** Parts of the body and the five senses
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the third title on the Science ladder, after Location and Maps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the learner can name the outside parts of the body, name the five sense organs, and say what each part and each sense does
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own body — the first thing every learner can look at and touch. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Parts of the body and what they do** — the outside parts named one by one, with what each part does
2. **The five senses and what they do** — eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin — and the job of each one

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Location and Maps — saying where things are (the parts are learned by where they sit on the body). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the main outside parts of the body: head, hair, face, forehead, eye, nose, lips, neck, shoulder, arm and hand, chest, belly, waist, knee, shin, leg and heel.
2. Name the five sense organs: eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin.
3. Say what each sense does: eyes see, ears hear, nose smells, tongue tastes, skin feels.
4. Say what the other parts do: hair covers and protects, hands work, legs walk.

## What you need
Nothing but your own body. A friend or family member to point and guess with helps.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot name a part → point to it on your own body first; the name sticks when the hand has touched the place.
- Mixes up the senses → do the job with the sense shut: close the eyes and try to "see"; the sense that cannot do the job is not the one that does it.
