# Video script — Parts of the body and the five senses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is my **head**. This is my **hair**.
My **eyes** see. My **ears** hear.
My **nose** smells. My **tongue** tastes. My **skin** feels.
My **hands** work. My **legs** walk.
Five senses. Five helpers. Now you — point, and name them.
