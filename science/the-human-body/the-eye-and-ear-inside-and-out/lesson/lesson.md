## Sub lesson 1 — The eye — outside and inside

South Sudan's Primary 5 book teaches the eye in two steps: first the **external structure of the eye**, then the **internal structure** — the **retina with its light-sensitive cells sending the message to the brain**. The sense the earlier lessons use is now opened and named part by part.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ear — outside and inside

The ear is taught the same way: first the **external structure of the ear**, then the **internal structure** — the **ear drum, ossicles and the cochlea**, the parts that carry sound from the air to the message the brain hears.

## Sub lesson 3 — Our eyes and ears — and other animals'

The run closes by **comparison**: the human eye compared with the eyes of other animals, and the human ear compared with other animals' ears — the same senses, built differently in different animals, each fitted to its owner's life.
