# The eye and ear — inside and out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The eye — outside and inside  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The eye — outside and inside**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The ear — outside and inside  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The ear — outside and inside**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Our eyes and ears — and other animals'  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Our eyes and ears — and other animals'**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the eye and ear — inside and out. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**
