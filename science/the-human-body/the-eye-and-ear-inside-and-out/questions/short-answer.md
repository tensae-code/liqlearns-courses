# The eye and ear — inside and out — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The eye — outside and inside.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 5 book teaches the eye in two steps: first the **external structure of the eye**, then the **internal structure** — the **retina with its light-sensitive cells sending the message to the brain**. The sense the earlier lessons use is now opened and named part by part.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The ear — outside and inside.
**Answer:** The ear is taught the same way: first the **external structure of the ear**, then the **internal structure** — the **ear drum, ossicles and the cochlea**, the parts that carry sound from the air to the message the brain hears.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Our eyes and ears — and other animals'.
**Answer:** The run closes by **comparison**: the human eye compared with the eyes of other animals, and the human ear compared with other animals' ears — the same senses, built differently in different animals, each fitted to its owner's life.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The eye — outside and inside → The ear — outside and inside → Our eyes and ears — and other animals' — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
