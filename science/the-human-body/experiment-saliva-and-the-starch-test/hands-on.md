# Hands-on — Experiment 2.1: saliva and the starch test

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The experiment, run
Set up the two glasses with an adult, warm them to body temperature, and keep a drawn record — start, middle, end — for each glass.

## Starch hunt
Use the iodine test on potato and on two other home foods. Make a class list: foods that hold starch, foods that do not.
