# Video script — Experiment 2.1: saliva and the starch test

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bread in two glasses of water.
One glass gets **saliva** too.
Warm them like the body — thirty-seven degrees.
Watch: the saliva **breaks the bread down**.
Digestion begins **in the mouth**.
And iodine on potato tests for **starch**.
