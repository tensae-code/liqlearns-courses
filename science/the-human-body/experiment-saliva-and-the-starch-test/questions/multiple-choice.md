# Experiment 2.1: saliva and the starch test — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. In Experiment 2.1, what is added to one glass but not the other?**
a) iodine  b) saliva  c) salt
**Answer: b) saliva**

**2. To what temperature are the glasses warmed?**
a) 100 °C  b) 0 °C  c) 37 °C
**Answer: c) 37 °C** — body temperature

**3. What does the experiment show saliva doing?**
a) breaking food down  b) making teeth grow  c) cooling the mouth
**Answer: a) breaking food down**

**4. The iodine drops on potato test for —**
a) salt  b) starch  c) water
**Answer: b) starch**

**5. Digestion in the mouth is of two kinds at once —**
a) chewing (physical) and saliva's work (chemical)  b) only chewing  c) only swallowing
**Answer: a) chewing (physical) and saliva's work (chemical)**
