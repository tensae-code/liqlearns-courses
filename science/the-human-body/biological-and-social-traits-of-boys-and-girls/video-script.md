Today: **Biological and social traits of boys and girls** — Vietnam's way, from its book (Vietnam, Grade 5 — *Khoa học 5*, printed p. 85).

Vietnam's Grade 5 *Khoa học 5* sorts the characteristics of males and females into two kinds. The **biological** kind is fixed from before birth and does not change: the reproductive organs, and what follows from them — pregnancy and menstruation in females, the beard in males.

The **social** kind can change, because people and customs choose them: hairstyle, clothes, jobs and tastes. A girl's short hair or a boy's cooking changes nothing biological — those are social traits, and they differ from place to place and time to time. Sorting the two kinds apart is the lesson's one skill.
