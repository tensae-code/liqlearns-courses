# Biological and social traits of boys and girls — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Biological traits — fixed from before birth.
**Answer:** Vietnam's Grade 5 *Khoa học 5* sorts the characteristics of males and females into two kinds. The **biological** kind is fixed from before birth and does not change: the reproductive organs, and what follows from them — pregnancy and menstruation in females, the beard in males.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Social traits — the changeable kind.
**Answer:** The **social** kind can change, because people and customs choose them: hairstyle, clothes, jobs and tastes. A girl's short hair or a boy's cooking changes nothing biological — those are social traits, and they differ from place to place and time to time. Sorting the two kinds apart is the lesson's one skill.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Biological traits — fixed from before birth → Social traits — the changeable kind — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
