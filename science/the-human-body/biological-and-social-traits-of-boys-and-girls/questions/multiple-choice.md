# Biological and social traits of boys and girls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Biological traits — fixed from before birth  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Biological traits — fixed from before birth**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Social traits — the changeable kind  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Social traits — the changeable kind**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: sorting the traits of boys and girls into biological traits (fixed from before birth) and social traits (changeable). That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Vietnam, Grade 5 — *Khoa học 5*, printed p. 85  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
