# Blood — and what it carries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Blood's solid and liquid parts.
**Answer:** Papua New Guinea's Grade 6 textbook opens blood by its make-up: blood has **solid parts and a liquid part** together in one stream. It looks like one red liquid, but it is a mixture — solids carried in a liquid, each with its own job.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Carrying oxygen and carbon dioxide.
**Answer:** Blood's first carrying job is gases: it carries **oxygen** to every part of the body, and carries **carbon dioxide** back — the Grade 6 manual teaches blood by exactly this exchange, the load picked up and the load returned on the same round.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Carrying nutrients and wastes.
**Answer:** The same stream carries more: **nutrients** — the useful part of food — out to the body, and **wastes** away from it. Blood is the body's transport: gases, food and wastes all travel in it, which is why its solid and liquid parts matter together.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Blood's solid and liquid parts → Carrying oxygen and carbon dioxide → Carrying nutrients and wastes — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
