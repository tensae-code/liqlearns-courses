# Blood — and what it carries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Blood's solid and liquid parts  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Blood's solid and liquid parts**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Carrying oxygen and carbon dioxide  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Carrying oxygen and carbon dioxide**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Carrying nutrients and wastes  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Carrying nutrients and wastes**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: blood — what it is made of and what it carries around the body. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The books behind this lesson are…
a) Papua New Guinea's science books (Department of Education, Papua New Guinea)  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Papua New Guinea's science books (Department of Education, Papua New Guinea)**
