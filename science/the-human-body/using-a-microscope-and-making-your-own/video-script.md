# Video script — The microscope — using it well, and making your own (bottle-microscope build-along)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This is a FULL build-along script: the learner builds while listening, pausing at each — PAUSE —. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

We are going to build a microscope. From a bottle. And a drop of water.

You need: a clear plastic bottle. Clear plastic strips. Water. A hair, or a thin fibre. And an adult to help with cutting.

**Step one.** Cut the top off the bottle. — PAUSE —
Keep the top part. It will stand like a little tower. Put it down, wide end at the bottom.

**Step two.** Take a plastic strip. This is your slide.
Put the hair in the middle of the strip. — PAUSE —
One drop of water on the hair. The water holds it flat.

**Step three.** Slide the strip under the tower, so the hair sits in the middle. — PAUSE —

**Step four.** Now the lens. Dip a fingertip in water. Touch one round drop onto the small hole at the top of the tower.
See it? The drop sits round and clear. A round drop of water **is** a lens. — PAUSE —

**Step five.** Hold your bottle microscope up to the light. Put your eye close to the drop. Look down through it.
Move the strip a tiny bit. Move your head a tiny bit.
There — the hair is **big** now. You can see its edges.

You built a simple microscope. One lens — one drop of water.
Leeuwenhoek saw a new world with one lens, too.
