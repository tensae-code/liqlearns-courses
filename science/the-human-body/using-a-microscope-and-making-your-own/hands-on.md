# Hands-on — The microscope — using it well, and making your own

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The carry drill
Carry the microscope (or a similar-shaped stand-in) from one table to another, by arm and base, three times. A partner watches: two hands? level? slow? Only a clean pass counts.

## Two microscopes, one fibre
View the same hair or fibre through the compound microscope and through your bottle microscope. Draw both views side by side and write one sentence on what the compound microscope shows that the bottle one cannot — yet.
