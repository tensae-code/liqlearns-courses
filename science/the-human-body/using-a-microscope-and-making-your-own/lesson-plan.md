# Lesson plan — The microscope — using it well, and making your own

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The microscope — using it well, and making your own
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Cell as the Basis of Life, inside The Human Body (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** carry a microscope by arm and base together, mount a specimen under a cover slip in a drop of water, focus low power first with the coarse knob and sharpen on high power with the fine knob only — and you can build a working simple microscope from a bottle and a drop of water.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The microscope — parts and what each does (every part named). The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Using a microscope); this lesson is the first-depth Grade 7 doing version, built new.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Carrying and care rules** — carry by arm and base together; never touch lens glass; never aim the mirror at direct sun.
2. **Mounting and focusing** — mount hair or fibre in a water drop under a cover slip; focus low power first (coarse knob), then medium/high (fine knob only).
3. **Building a bottle microscope** — a working simple microscope from a cut plastic bottle, plastic-strip slides and a water-drop lens.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The microscope — parts and what each does. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Carry a microscope correctly and state the two lens-and-sun care rules.
2. Mount a hair or fibre specimen under a cover slip and focus it, low power first.
3. Build a bottle microscope with a water-drop lens and view a specimen through it.

## What you need
A compound microscope if available; a slide, cover slip, hair or fibre, water and a dropper; for the build: a clear plastic bottle, scissors (with care), clear plastic strips, water.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reaches for the coarse knob on high power → stop: on high power use the fine knob only, or the objective can strike the slide.
- No cover slip → always cover the specimen: the slip flattens it, holds the water drop, and protects the objective.
