# The microscope — using it well, and making your own — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fingers may touch the lens glass if they are clean.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** You focus on low power before moving to higher power.
**Answer: True**
