# The microscope — using it well, and making your own — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** List the mounting steps in order for a hair specimen.
**Answer: Hair on slide, one drop of water over it, cover slip lowered gently at an angle.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What do you use as slides in the bottle microscope?
**Answer: Clear plastic strips.**
