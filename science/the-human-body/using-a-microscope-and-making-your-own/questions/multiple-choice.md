# The microscope — using it well, and making your own — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Carry a microscope by —
a) the tube alone  b) the arm and the base together  c) the stage
**Answer: b) the arm and the base together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** You must never aim the mirror at —
a) a window  b) direct sun  c) a lamp
**Answer: b) direct sun** — the concentrated light can damage the eye.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A mounted specimen is covered with —
a) paper  b) a cover slip  c) a clip only
**Answer: b) a cover slip** — always use one.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** First focus is found with —
a) the fine knob on high power  b) the coarse knob on low power  c) the nosepiece
**Answer: b) the coarse knob on low power**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** On high power you use —
a) the coarse knob only  b) the fine knob only  c) both knobs together
**Answer: b) the fine knob only**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The bottle microscope's lens is —
a) bottle glass  b) a drop of water  c) a mirror
**Answer: b) a drop of water** — the round drop bulges like a convex lens.
