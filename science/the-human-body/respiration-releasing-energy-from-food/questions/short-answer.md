# Cells — respiration: releasing energy from food — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does the carbon dioxide you breathe out come from?
**Answer: It is a waste product of cellular respiration in the mitochondria.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do sprinting muscles tire quickly?
**Answer: Oxygen delivery cannot keep up, muscles ferment to lactic acid, which yields far less energy.**
