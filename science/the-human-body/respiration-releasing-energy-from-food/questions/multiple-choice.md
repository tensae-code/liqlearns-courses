# Cells — respiration: releasing energy from food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cellular respiration happens in the —
a) nucleus  b) mitochondria  c) vacuole
**Answer: b) mitochondria** — the powerhouses.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The energy carrier made by respiration is —
a) DNA  b) ATP  c) glucose
**Answer: b) ATP**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Aerobic respiration needs —
a) oxygen  b) alcohol  c) chlorophyll
**Answer: a) oxygen**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Alcoholic fermentation produces —
a) lactic acid only  b) alcohol + carbon dioxide  c) oxygen
**Answer: b) alcohol + carbon dioxide** — yeast does this.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** During hard exercise, human muscles switch to —
a) photosynthesis  b) lactic-acid fermentation  c) aerobic respiration only
**Answer: b) lactic-acid fermentation** — when oxygen runs short.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Compared with aerobic respiration, fermentation yields —
a) far more energy  b) the same energy  c) far less energy
**Answer: c) far less energy**
