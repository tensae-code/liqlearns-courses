# Video script — Cells — respiration: releasing energy from food

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Inside the **mitochondrion**, the powerhouse:
**glucose plus oxygen** becomes **energy** — plus carbon dioxide and water.
That is aerobic respiration. Big energy.

No oxygen? The cell ferments. Alcohol, or lactic acid.
Small energy — and burning, tired muscles. That is the backup plan.
