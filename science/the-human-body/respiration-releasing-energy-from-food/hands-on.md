# Hands-on — Cells — respiration: releasing energy from food

The lesson with your body, not only your eyes.

## The minute test
Run on the spot for one minute, then sit and note: breathing rate, leg feeling, how long recovery takes. Write the explanation under your notes using the lesson's words: oxygen runs short, muscles switch to lactic-acid fermentation, far less energy.

## Yeast watch
If yeast and warm sugary water are available, mix them in a bottle with a balloon over the neck and watch over an hour: the balloon fills with carbon dioxide — alcoholic fermentation at work, the same chemistry family as tella and tej.
