# The sense of taste — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The organ of taste is the _____.
**Answer: tongue**

**2.** Honey is _____ and a lemon is sour.
**Answer: sweet**

**3.** A bad taste can warn us food has _____.
**Answer: spoiled**

**4.** Taste guards the _____ of the body.
**Answer: gate**
