# The sense of taste — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the four main tastes and a food for each.
**Answer: Sweet (honey), sour (lemon), salty (salt), bitter (some medicines or leaves).**

**2.** How does taste protect the body?
**Answer: A bad or strange taste warns that food may be spoiled, so we stop eating before swallowing something harmful.**

**3.** Why does food taste different when your nose is blocked?
**Answer: Because smell does part of the work we call taste; with the nose blocked, only the tongue's part remains.**

**4.** What should you do if food tastes wrong?
**Answer: Stop eating, spit it out, and tell an adult.**
