# The sense of taste — multiple choice


**1.** The organ of taste is the…
a) Nose  b) Tongue  c) Ear  d) Skin
**Answer: b) Tongue**

**2.** Honey tastes…
a) Sour  b) Salty  c) Sweet  d) Bitter
**Answer: c) Sweet**

**3.** A lemon tastes…
a) Sweet  b) Sour  c) Salty  d) Bitter
**Answer: b) Sour**

**4.** If food tastes spoiled, we should…
a) Swallow quickly  b) Eat more  c) Stop and spit it out  d) Add sugar
**Answer: c) Stop and spit it out**

**5.** Taste works together with…
a) Hearing  b) Smell  c) Sight  d) Touch
**Answer: b) Smell**

**6.** Salt tastes…
a) Bitter  b) Sweet  c) Sour  d) Salty
**Answer: d) Salty**
