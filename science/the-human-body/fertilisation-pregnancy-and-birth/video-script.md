Today: **Fertilisation, pregnancy and birth** — Nicaragua's way, from its book (Nicaragua, Grade 6 — *Ciencias Naturales, 6to grado*, printed pp. 55–59).

Nicaragua's Grade 6 book teaches it plainly: **one sperm enters the ovum and forms the zygote, the new individual's first cell**. From that one cell, division after division builds the whole new being.

In pregnancy the **amniotic sac protects the baby**. The Grade 9 book draws the stages — **morula, blastula, gastrula** — stage by stage: the **chorion's villi enter the endometrium to form the placenta**, passing **nutrients, oxygen and antibodies** from mother to child. At **birth** the sac breaks, contractions come, and the baby is expelled.

The Grade 9 Módulo Autoformativo teaches the plants' own version: in **double fertilisation** in angiosperms, **one antherozoid (male cell) joins the oosphere to form the zygote and embryo, and the second forms the nutritive tissue** that will feed it — two joins in one event, which is why it is called double.
