# Fertilisation, pregnancy and birth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Fertilisation — one sperm enters the ovum and forms the zygote.
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 6 book teaches it plainly: **one sperm enters the ovum and forms the zygote, the new individual's first cell**. From that one cell, division after division builds the whole new being.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Pregnancy and birth — the amniotic sac, the placenta, and the way out.
**Answer:** In pregnancy the **amniotic sac protects the baby**. The Grade 9 book draws the stages — **morula, blastula, gastrula** — stage by stage: the **chorion's villi enter the endometrium to form the placenta**, passing **nutrients, oxygen and antibodies** from mother to child. At **birth** the sac breaks, contractions come, and the baby is expelled.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Double fertilisation in flowering plants — the plant parallel.
**Answer:** The Grade 9 Módulo Autoformativo teaches the plants' own version: in **double fertilisation** in angiosperms, **one antherozoid (male cell) joins the oosphere to form the zygote and embryo, and the second forms the nutritive tissue** that will feed it — two joins in one event, which is why it is called double.

**Answer:** Fertilisation — one sperm enters the ovum and forms the zygote → Pregnancy and birth — the amniotic sac, the placenta, and the way out → Double fertilisation in flowering plants — the plant parallel — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
