# Adolescence and its changes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Adolescence arrives for girls at about —**
a) 8–10 years  b) 12–14 years  c) 18–20 years
**Answer: b) 12–14 years**

**2. Adolescence arrives for boys at about —**
a) 12–14 years  b) 14–16 years  c) 10–12 years
**Answer: b) 14–16 years**

**3. The primary characteristics are —**
a) the changes of feeling  b) the reproductive organs themselves  c) the growth spurt
**Answer: b) the reproductive organs themselves**

**4. Which is a boy's change in the table?**
a) hips widening  b) the voice deepening  c) breasts developing
**Answer: b) the voice deepening**

**5. Which is a girl's change in the table?**
a) a beard growing  b) shoulders broadening  c) menstruation beginning
**Answer: c) menstruation beginning**
