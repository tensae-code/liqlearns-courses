# Hands-on — Adolescence and its changes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The table, rebuilt
On a big sheet, rebuild the book's two-column table with drawings instead of words where you can — a deepening voice as a sound wave, a growth spurt as a height line.

## Questions box
Write any question about the changes on a slip, unsigned, and put it in a class box. The teacher answers the slips plainly, in the book's words — the lesson's way of making the topic safe to ask about.
