# Video script — Adolescence and its changes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Adolescence** — the fast bridge from child to adult.
For girls, about twelve to fourteen. For boys, about fourteen to sixteen.
Bodies grow fast. Voices change. Feelings run fast.
Every change in the book's table is **normal**.
There is nothing in it to be ashamed of.
