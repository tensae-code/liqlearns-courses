# Hands-on — The heart

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Jump and feel
The book's own test: jump on the spot, then rest a hand on your chest. Feel the beat speed up with work and slow down with rest.

## Two counts
Count your beats for half a minute at rest and after jumping. Write both numbers down — the heart's answer to work, in numbers.

## Grade 8 — run the circuit (the petals game)
Outdoors: mark the heart, the lungs and the body as three stations. Carry a petal as a blood cell: heart → lungs (drop the "used" petal, pick a fresh one — oxygen loaded), lungs → heart, heart → body (deliver it), body → heart. Run both loops and name each one as you run it: pulmonary, then systemic.
