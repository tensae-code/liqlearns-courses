# The heart — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. The heart keeps working while you sleep.**
**Answer: True**

**2. The heart is a bone in the chest.**
**Answer: False**

**3. You can feel your heartbeat by resting a hand on your chest.**
**Answer: True**
**4. The heart is about fist-sized.**
**Answer: True**

**5. Platelets carry oxygen.**
**Answer: False**

**6. A vessel is named as an artery because of the direction it carries blood — away from the heart — not because of how much oxygen the blood holds.**
**Answer: True**

**7. Plasma is one of the cell kinds of blood.**
**Answer: False** — plasma is the liquid part; the cells are red cells, white cells and platelets.

**8. A stroke is one of the diseases of the circulatory system in this unit.**
**Answer: True**
