# The heart — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. How does the book tell you to feel your heart working?**
**Answer: jump, then rest a hand on your chest and feel the beat**
**2. Name the blood's three cell kinds and one job each.**
**Answer: Red cells carry oxygen and food; white cells fight disease; platelets clot the blood.**

**3. In the water-tower analogy, what stands for what?**
**Answer: the tower and its height stand for the heart holding and pushing the blood; the pipes stand for the vessels carrying it to every house — every part of the body.**

**4. Name three diseases of the circulatory system from this lesson.**
**Answer: any three of: hypertension, heart attack, stroke, heart failure.**
