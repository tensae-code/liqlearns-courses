# The heart — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. The heart pumps ___ around the body.**
**Answer: blood**

**2. The heart pumps by contracting and ___.**
**Answer: relaxing**
**3. The exchange with the body's cells is made by the ______.**
**Answer: capillaries**

**4. Blood groups are found by ______ testing.**
**Answer: serum**
