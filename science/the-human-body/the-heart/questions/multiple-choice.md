# The heart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The heart is —**
a) a bone  b) a muscle that pumps blood  c) a kind of lung
**Answer: b) a muscle that pumps blood**

**2. The heart pumps by —**
a) contracting and relaxing  b) growing and shrinking  c) opening and closing like a door
**Answer: a) contracting and relaxing**

**3. After you jump, your heartbeat is —**
a) slower  b) stopped  c) faster
**Answer: c) faster**

**4. The heart pumps —**
a) air  b) blood  c) food
**Answer: b) blood**
