# The sense of touch — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name four things touch can discover.
**Answer: Whether a thing is hot or cold, rough or smooth, hard or soft, wet or dry.**

**2.** How can you name a thing in a bag without looking?
**Answer: By feeling its shape, hardness and surface with the hands — touch gives the clues.**

**3.** Why is pain useful?
**Answer: Pain is a warning that something is harming the body, so we pull away before the harm grows.**

**4.** What three things harm the hand?
**Answer: Hot things (burns), sharp things (cuts) and strong chemicals.**
