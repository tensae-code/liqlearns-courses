# The sense of touch — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The organ of touch is the _____.
**Answer: skin**

**2.** A stone feels hard and a sponge feels _____.
**Answer: soft**

**3.** Touch warns us when a thing is too _____.
**Answer: hot**

**4.** We _____ our hands after touching dirty things.
**Answer: wash**
