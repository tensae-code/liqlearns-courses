# The sense of touch — multiple choice


**1.** We feel touch with our…
a) Ears  b) Skin and hands  c) Eyes  d) Tongue
**Answer: b) Skin and hands**

**2.** Touch can discover whether a thing is…
a) Loud or quiet  b) Hot or cold  c) Sweet or sour  d) Near or far
**Answer: b) Hot or cold**

**3.** A sponge feels…
a) Hard and rough  b) Soft  c) Sharp  d) Burning
**Answer: b) Soft**

**4.** When we touch something very hot, touch warns us to…
a) Hold it tighter  b) Pull away  c) Taste it  d) Smell it
**Answer: b) Pull away**

**5.** Which of these can harm the hand?
a) Cotton  b) A sponge  c) A sharp knife edge  d) A wooden spoon
**Answer: c) A sharp knife edge**

**6.** After touching dirty things we should…
a) Taste our fingers  b) Wash our hands  c) Close our eyes  d) Sit still
**Answer: b) Wash our hands**
