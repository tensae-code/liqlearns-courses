Today: **The sense of touch** — Kuwait's way: how we discover things by the sense of touch — hot and cold, rough and smooth, hard and soft — and what harms the hand.

The skin, and above all the hands, are the organs of touch. By touching we discover what looking cannot always tell: whether a thing is hot or cold, rough or smooth, hard or soft, wet or dry. Kuwait's Grade 1 book teaches touch by feeling with eyes closed: the child reaches into a bag and names the thing — a stone, a sponge, a spoon — by touch alone. Touch also warns us: heat, sharp edges and pain tell the hand to pull away. The hand is harmed by burns from hot things, cuts from sharp things, and strong chemicals that burn the skin. We keep the hands safe by testing heat carefully, handling sharp things with care, and washing the hands after touching dirty or unknown things — the warning sense only works if the skin stays whole and clean.
