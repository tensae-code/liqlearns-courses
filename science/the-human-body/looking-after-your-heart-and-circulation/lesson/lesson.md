# Looking after your heart and circulation

## Do it first
Your heart works every minute of your life. What can go wrong with the circulation — and what guards it? Say your answers out loud. Then read on: this is Sudan's way in — its Grade 6 Science closes its circulation lesson with keeping the circulation healthy, Sudan's own health guidance.

## Sub lesson 1 — The diseases that strike the circulation

Sudan's book names the diseases that strike the circulation: **anaemia**, **narrowed arteries**, and **raised blood pressure**. Each is a fault in the blood, the vessels or the pressure the heart works against — the circulation the heart lesson teaches (the heart, the vessels and the double circulation) is the system these diseases strike.

## Sub lesson 2 — The guard against them

The guard, as the book gives it: **varied good food**; **exercise that stirs the heart and circulation**; **cleaning wounds with water and soap so germs stay out**; and **seeing the doctor for bleeding or nosebleeds**. Looking after the circulation is daily work — food, movement, cleanliness, and the doctor when bleeding will not stop.
