# Hands-on — Looking after your heart and circulation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel exercise stir the circulation
Take your pulse resting, then after a minute of exercise your teacher directs. Record both counts and say what the rise shows: exercise stirs the heart and circulation.

## The wound-cleaning drill
Write the book's wound rule as a four-step card — clean the wound, with water, and soap, so germs stay out — and say when the card's last step is instead: see the doctor (bleeding, nosebleeds).
