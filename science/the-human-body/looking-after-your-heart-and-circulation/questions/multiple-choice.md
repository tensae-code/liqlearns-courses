# Looking after your heart and circulation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is named as a disease of the circulation?
a) Anaemia  b) A broken bone  c) Toothache  d) Sunburn  
**Answer: a) Anaemia**

**2.** Wounds are cleaned with…
a) sand  b) water and soap  c) oil  d) nothing  
**Answer: b) water and soap**

**3.** When should you see the doctor, in the book's guidance?
a) For every meal  b) For bleeding or nosebleeds  c) Only for exercise  d) Never  
**Answer: b) For bleeding or nosebleeds**
