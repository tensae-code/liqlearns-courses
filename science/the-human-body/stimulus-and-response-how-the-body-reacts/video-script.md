Today: **Stimulus and response — how the body reacts** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Something changes — light, sound, heat. Your body senses it: that change is a stimulus. Then your body answers — you pull back, turn, shade your eyes. That answer is the response.
Sense, then answer. Two halves of one event — that is how the body keeps itself safe in a changing world.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
