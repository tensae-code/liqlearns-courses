# Hands-on — Stimulus and response — how the body reacts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Catch the pair
With a companion, drop a ruler between their fingers without warning and let them catch it. Say what the stimulus was (the sight of the falling ruler) and what the response was (the catch).

## Senses at work
Close your eyes while a companion makes three different sounds or offers three safe smells. Name each stimulus and the sense that received it, then say what response your body wanted to make.
