# Stimulus and response — how the body reacts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define stimulus and response in one sentence each.
**Answer:** A stimulus is a change the body can sense; a response is the body's reaction to what it sensed.

**2.** Name one stimulus for each of three senses, with its response.
**Answer:** For example: bright light to the eyes — shading or blinking; a sudden sound to the ears — turning toward it; heat to the skin — pulling back. (Any correct sense–stimulus–response sets.)

**3.** Why does the Hebei book teach the pair before naming organs?
**Answer:** Because the event comes first: something changes and the body answers. The organs are then met as the parts that do the sensing and reacting the learner already understands.

**4.** What would be missing if the body only sensed?
**Answer:** The answer: without response the body could not protect or guide itself — sensing without reacting leaves the hot thing touched and the warning unheeded.
