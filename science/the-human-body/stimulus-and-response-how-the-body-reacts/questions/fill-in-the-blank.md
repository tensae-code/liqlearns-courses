# Stimulus and response — how the body reacts — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** A change the body can sense is a _____.
**Answer: stimulus**

**2.** The body's reaction to a stimulus is its _____.
**Answer: response**

**3.** The _____ are the body's receivers.
**Answer: senses**

**4.** Stimulus and response are two _____ of one event.
**Answer: halves**
