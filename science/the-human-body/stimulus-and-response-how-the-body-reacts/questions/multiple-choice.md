# Stimulus and response — how the body reacts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A stimulus is…
a) A change the body can sense  b) A muscle  c) A bone  d) A kind of food
**Answer: a) A change the body can sense**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The senses are the body's…
a) Movers  b) Receivers, each tuned to its own kind of change  c) Covering  d) Food stores
**Answer: b) Receivers, each tuned to its own kind of change**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A response is…
a) The change itself  b) A sense organ  c) The body's reaction to what it sensed  d) Sleep
**Answer: c) The body's reaction to what it sensed**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Pulling back from a hot thing is an example of…
a) A stimulus  b) A sense  c) Resting  d) A response to the stimulus of heat
**Answer: d) A response to the stimulus of heat**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Stimulus and response are taught together because…
a) They are two halves of one event — sense, then answer  b) They are the same word  c) The book is short  d) One is not real
**Answer: a) They are two halves of one event — sense, then answer**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A body that sensed but never reacted…
a) Would see better  b) Could not protect itself  c) Would need no senses  d) Would react twice
**Answer: b) Could not protect itself**
