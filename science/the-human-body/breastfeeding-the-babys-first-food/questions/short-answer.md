# Breastfeeding — the baby's first food — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The mother's milk first — the colostrum.
**Answer:** Yemen's Grade 9 Science book teaches the mother's milk as the baby's first food, starting with the colostrum of the first three days: a complete food that carries natural protection against germs — food and protection arriving together, from the mother..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Natural feeding against the bottle.
**Answer:** The book sets natural feeding against the bottle on four grounds: cost, cleanliness, the child's health, and the mother's closeness.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The mother's milk first — the colostrum → Natural feeding against the bottle — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
