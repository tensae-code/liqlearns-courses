Today: **Breastfeeding — the baby's first food** — Yemen's way, from its book (Yemen, Grade 9 — *Science, Grade 9, Part 2*, printed p. 130).

Yemen's Grade 9 Science book teaches the mother's milk as the baby's first food, starting with the colostrum of the first three days: a complete food that carries natural protection against germs — food and protection arriving together, from the mother.
The book sets natural feeding against the bottle on four grounds: cost, cleanliness, the child's health, and the mother's closeness. On each ground the comparison is made plainly, and natural feeding stands first.
