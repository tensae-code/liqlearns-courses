# The microscope — seeing the unseen — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A microscope ______ things too small for the naked eye.
**Answer: enlarges (makes bigger)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Hooke improved the compound microscope in the year ______.
**Answer: 1665**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Leeuwenhoek worked in the ______ (decade).
**Answer: 1670s**
