# The microscope — seeing the unseen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A microscope —
a) makes big things smaller  b) enlarges things too small for the naked eye  c) heats samples
**Answer: b) enlarges things too small for the naked eye**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A clinic worker uses a microscope to look for —
a) parasites in stool and blood samples  b) broken bones  c) fever
**Answer: a) parasites in stool and blood samples**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first compound microscope, a lens tube, was made by —
a) Hooke  b) the Janssen father and son  c) Leeuwenhoek
**Answer: b) the Janssen father and son** — in the 1590s.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Hooke improved the compound microscope in —
a) the 1590s  b) 1665  c) the 1670s
**Answer: b) 1665**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Leeuwenhoek's microscopes had —
a) many lenses  b) one single lens  c) no lens
**Answer: b) one single lens**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Leeuwenhoek's single-lens instruments —
a) saw less than the compound ones  b) out-resolved the compound ones  c) were never used
**Answer: b) out-resolved the compound ones** — they showed finer detail.
