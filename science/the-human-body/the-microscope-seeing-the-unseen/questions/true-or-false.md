# The microscope — seeing the unseen — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cells could be studied before the microscope was invented.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Janssen's lens tube was the first compound microscope.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Leeuwenhoek's instruments used many lenses in a long tube.
**Answer: False**
