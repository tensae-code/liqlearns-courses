# Hands-on — The microscope — seeing the unseen

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only the page.

## The empty-looking drop
Put a drop of pond water on a clean white surface. Look with your eyes alone, then with a hand lens if you have one. Write what changes — and what still stays unseen. That unseen part is why the microscope was needed.

## History line
On a strip of paper, mark three points: 1590s, 1665, 1670s. Write Janssen, Hooke and Leeuwenhoek under their dates and draw each one's instrument in one quick sketch.
