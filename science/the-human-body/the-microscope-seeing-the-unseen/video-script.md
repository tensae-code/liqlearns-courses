# Video script — The microscope — seeing the unseen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at a drop of water. It looks empty.
It is not empty. It is too small for your eyes.

A **microscope** makes tiny things big.
Now you can see pond life. You can see parasites in a clinic sample.
You can see **cells**.

First came a tube with lenses — **Janssen**, father and son, long ago.
Then **Hooke** made it better.
Then **Leeuwenhoek**, with one tiny lens, saw the furthest of all.
