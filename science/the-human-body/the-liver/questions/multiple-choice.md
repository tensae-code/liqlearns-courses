# The liver — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The liver is the body's —**
a) smallest organ  b) largest internal organ  c) only muscle
**Answer: b) largest internal organ**

**2. The liver sits —**
a) on the right side, under the ribs  b) in the skull  c) in the left leg
**Answer: a) on the right side, under the ribs**

**3. Bile is made by the liver and held in the —**
a) stomach  b) gall bladder  c) heart
**Answer: b) gall bladder**

**4. One job of the liver is to —**
a) filter the blood  b) take in oxygen  c) pump blood
**Answer: a) filter the blood**
