# Lesson plan — The liver

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The liver
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the inside systems, after the outside parts and senses (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the liver is the largest internal organ, sitting on the right side under the ribs — it makes bile, filters the blood and makes the proteins that clot blood
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Parts of the body and the five senses — the last of the Grade 4 book's inside organs, and the largest of them.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the liver is the largest internal organ, on the right side under the ribs.
2. Say its jobs: it makes bile (held in the gall bladder), filters the blood, and makes clotting proteins.

## What you need
Your own right side — a hand under the right ribs finds the liver's home.
