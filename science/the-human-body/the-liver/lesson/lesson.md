# The liver

## Do it first
Put your right hand under your right ribs. Behind that hand, taking up more room than any other inside organ, sits the liver — the largest internal organ you own. It never announces itself. This lesson says what it does all day in silence.

## The largest inside worker
The **liver** is the **largest internal organ** in the body. It sits on the **right side, under the ribs** — the place your hand rested.

The book names three of its jobs:

1. **It makes bile** — a juice the body uses in digestion; the bile is **held in the gall bladder** until it is needed.
2. **It filters the blood** — cleaning the blood as it passes through.
3. **It makes clotting proteins** — the materials that let blood clot and seal a cut.

Maker, filter, and sealer of cuts: the biggest inside organ earns its size.
