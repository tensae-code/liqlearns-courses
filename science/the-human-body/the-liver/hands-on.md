# Hands-on — The liver

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the inside
On a big outline of a body, place the organs you now know: brain, heart, lungs, liver, kidneys, and the food tube. The inside map is yours now.

## Filter thinking
Pour muddy water through a cloth into a cup. The cloth catches some dirt — a picture, in small, of the liver filtering blood.
