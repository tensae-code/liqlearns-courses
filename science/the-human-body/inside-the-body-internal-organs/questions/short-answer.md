# Inside the body — internal organs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Internal body parts" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 3 guide opens its whole book with **internal body parts**: the parts inside the body, named first, before the health topics that follow.

**2.** What does the part "Parts you cannot see" teach?
**Answer:** An internal organ does its work hidden.

**3.** What does the part "Knowing the inside protects it" teach?
**Answer:** The guide places internal body parts at the head of a health topic that continues with hygiene and disease: knowing what is inside the body is the start of caring for it.
