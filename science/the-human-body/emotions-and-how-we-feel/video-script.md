Today: **Emotions — and how we feel** — Angola's way: the feelings close the body's run, because a person is a body that feels as well as works.

An emotion is a feeling that rises in us — joy, sadness, anger, fear — and each has a name. Naming it is the first skill.

Emotions show in the face and body for others to read — and they change: no feeling stays at its strongest for ever.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
