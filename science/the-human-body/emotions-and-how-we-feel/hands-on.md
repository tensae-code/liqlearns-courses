# Hands-on — Emotions — and how we feel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Faces of feelings
Take turns making the face of an emotion — joy, sadness, anger, fear — while the other names it. Say what in the face gave it away.

## Emotion diary of one day
Three times in one day, stop and write or draw the emotion you feel and what brought it. At the day's end, say how the feelings changed.
