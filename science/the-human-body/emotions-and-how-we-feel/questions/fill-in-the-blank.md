# Emotions — and how we feel — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A feeling that rises in us, like joy or sadness, is an _____.
**Answer: emotion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) _____ is the emotion that rises when something threatens us.
**Answer: Fear**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Emotions _____ in the face and body, and others can read them.
