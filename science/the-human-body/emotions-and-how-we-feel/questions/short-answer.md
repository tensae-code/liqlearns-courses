# Emotions — and how we feel — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name four emotions and say when each rises.
**Answer:** Joy rises when something good comes; sadness when we lose or miss something; anger when something strikes us as wrong; fear when something threatens us.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the emotions lesson close the Grade 3 human-body run?
**Answer:** Because after the body's systems — digestive, breathing, blood, urinary, reproductive — the emotions complete the picture of a person: a body that feels as well as works.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How can you tell what another person is feeling?
**Answer:** Emotions show in the face, in the body and in what the person does next, so others can read them — as they read ours.
