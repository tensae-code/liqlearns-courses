# Emotions — and how we feel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An emotion is…
a) A bone of the body
b) A feeling that rises in us — joy, sadness, anger, fear
c) A kind of food
d) A school subject only
**Answer: b) A feeling that rises in us — joy, sadness, anger, fear**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The first skill of the emotions lesson is…
a) Hiding feelings
b) Naming each emotion
c) Running fast
d) Drawing the body
**Answer: b) Naming each emotion**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Emotions show in…
a) Only in books
b) The face, the body, and what we do next
c) The weather
d) Our shoes
**Answer: b) The face, the body, and what we do next**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) About emotions and time, the lesson says…
a) A strong feeling never changes
b) No feeling stays at its strongest for ever — emotions change
c) Feelings last one minute exactly
d) Only joy changes
**Answer: b) No feeling stays at its strongest for ever — emotions change**
