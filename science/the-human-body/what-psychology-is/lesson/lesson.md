# What psychology is

## Do it first
Australia's Grade 9 book ends with a Step-up chapter, and its first question is a whole subject in one line: what is psychology? You have studied the body all year — what would a science of the mind study? Say your answer out loud. Then read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — What is psychology? — the study of mind and behaviour

**Psychology** is the study of the mind and of behaviour — of what people think and feel, and of what they do, and how the two hang together. Australia's book opens its Step-up chapter with the question itself (11.1, 'What is psychology?'): before any part of the subject is taught, the subject is named and bounded — a science whose object is not a bone or a muscle but the person thinking and acting.

## Sub lesson 2 — A step up — from the body to the person

The chapter's title is the book's own way: a **Step-up**. The year's science climbed through the body's systems; the closing chapter steps up from the body to the person who lives in it. Psychology stands first in that chapter (11.1), the brain and cognition follow (11.2), and the social self closes it (11.3) — the mind named first, then its organ, then the person among people.

## The key ideas

- **What is psychology? — the study of mind and behaviour** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.
- **A step up — from the body to the person** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.
