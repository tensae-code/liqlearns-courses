# Hands-on — What psychology is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One day, two columns (a notebook)
For one day, note three moments in two columns: what you did, and what you were thinking or feeling just before it. At the day's end, read the pairs and say in one sentence what a psychologist would be studying in your page.

## Name the subject (no materials)
From memory, say the lesson's definition of psychology in one sentence. Then have a partner ask the book's own question — 'What is psychology?' — and answer it again without looking.
