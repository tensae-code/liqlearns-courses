# Video script — The human reproductive system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One more system of the body — the one that makes new life.
The **testes** make the **sperm cells**.
The **ovaries** make the **eggs**.
An egg is released — that is **ovulation**.
A sperm meets the egg in the **tube** — that meeting is **fertilisation**.
If there is no meeting, **menstruation** follows. The cycle turns, about every twenty-eight days.
