# The human reproductive system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The testes make —**
a) eggs  b) sperm cells  c) blood
**Answer: b) sperm cells**

**2. The ovaries make —**
a) eggs  b) sperm cells  c) milk
**Answer: a) eggs**

**3. Ovulation is —**
a) the release of an egg  b) the egg dying  c) a kind of food
**Answer: a) the release of an egg**

**4. Fertilisation happens in the —**
a) uterus  b) uterine tube  c) ovary
**Answer: b) uterine tube**

**5. The cycle repeats about every —**
a) 7 days  b) 14 days  c) 28 days
**Answer: c) 28 days**

**6. If no fertilisation occurs, what follows?**
a) menstruation  b) a zygote grows  c) ovulation stops for ever
**Answer: a) menstruation**
