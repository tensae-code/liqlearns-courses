# Lesson plan — The human reproductive system

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The human reproductive system
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the system that makes new life, taught plainly as the book teaches it (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the male testes make sperm and the female ovaries make eggs; when a sperm meets an egg in the uterine tube that meeting is fertilisation, and when no meeting happens, menstruation follows
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the inside systems already learned — digestion, heart, breathing; this is one more body system, taught with the same plain words.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the male system's basic organs: the testes, which make the sperm cells, held in the scrotum with its duct system.
2. Name the female system's basic organs: the ovaries, which make the eggs; say what ovulation is and about how often it happens.
3. Say where fertilisation happens — a sperm meets the egg in the uterine tube — and what follows either way: an embryo in the uterus, or menstruation.
