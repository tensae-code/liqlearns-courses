# Hands-on — The human reproductive system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The cycle wheel, made
Draw the 28-day cycle wheel on a big sheet, with the egg's release, the tube, the uterus and menstruation in their places. Turn the wheel and retell the month.

## Systems parade
Lay this system beside the other systems you know — digestion, circulation, breathing — one card per system, each with its organs and its job. The new card belongs in the same row.
