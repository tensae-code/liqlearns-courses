# Video script — Human growth and development stages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Human growth and development stages** — the Bahamas' way: learn the body by watching a human life grow.

A baby is an **infant** — small, growing fast, learning first to sit, then to stand, then to walk. Next comes the **young child** — walking, running, talking, learning, growing steadily. Then the **teenager** — the body grows in a sudden spurt and matures. Last, the **adult** — fully grown, able to do a grown person's full work.
Four stages, one after another: infant, young child, teenager, adult. At every stage the body changes — and what the person can do changes with it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
