# Human growth and development stages — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first stage of a human life is the _____.
**Answer: infant**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Between young child and adult comes the _____ stage.
**Answer: teenager**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The infant learns to sit, stand and then _____ .
**Answer: walk**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) An _____ is fully grown and can care for a new infant, where the circle begins again.
**Answer: adult**
