# Human growth and development stages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The four stages of a human life come in which order?
a) Infant, young child, teenager, adult  b) Adult, teenager, young child, infant  c) Teenager, infant, adult, young child  d) Young child, adult, infant, teenager
**Answer: a) Infant, young child, teenager, adult**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A teenager is…
a) A fully grown person  b) A baby in the first stage of life  c) The young person between child and adult  d) A young child who can walk
**Answer: c) The young person between child and adult**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In which stage does the body grow fastest?
a) The adult stage  b) The infant stage  c) The teenager stage  d) All stages grow at the same speed
**Answer: b) The infant stage**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The abilities of sitting, standing and then walking first arrive…
a) In the adult stage  b) Only after the teenage years  c) In the infant stage  d) In no stage — people are born walking
**Answer: c) In the infant stage**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A teenager's growth is known by…
a) Growing in a spurt, with the body maturing toward the adult form  b) Never growing at all  c) Shrinking back to a child's size  d) Learning to take first steps
**Answer: a) Growing in a spurt, with the body maturing toward the adult form**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) An adult is…
a) Still waiting for a first step  b) The fully grown person, who can do a grown person's full work and care for others  c) A child between infant and teenager  d) Known only by height, never by abilities
**Answer: b) The fully grown person, who can do a grown person's full work and care for others**
