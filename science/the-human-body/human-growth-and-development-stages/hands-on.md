# Hands-on — Human growth and development stages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The family stage line
At home, line up the people of your house by stage — infant, young child, teenager, adult — standing in a row if they are willing, or named on paper if not. Say each person's stage out loud, and one change and one ability that prove it.

## Measure the growth
Measure your own height against a wall and mark it with the date (ask permission first). If an older mark of yours is already on a wall or door frame at home, compare: how much have you grown since that mark? Write the two numbers down.

## My next stage
Write three lines: the stage you are in now, one ability you have gained in the last year, and one change you expect when you reach the next stage.
