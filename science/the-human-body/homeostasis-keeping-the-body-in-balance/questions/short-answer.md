# Homeostasis — keeping the body in balance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The inside conditions that must stay steady.
**Answer:** Eritrea's Grade 8 book closes its Human Systems unit with **homeostasis** (printed p. 34), after the digestive, respiratory, circulatory, reproductive and skeletal systems have each been taught. The word names the unit's closing idea: the body's inside is a place whose conditions — its temperature, its water, the things dissolved in its blood — must be held **steady**, inside narrow limits, while the world outside swings hot and cold, wet and dry. A system can do its own job and still fail the body if the inside it works in is allowed to drift.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Correcting a change — the body answers back.
**Answer:** Homeostasis is kept by **correction**: when a condition moves one way, the body answers with a change that pushes it back. Too hot, and the body sweats and sends more blood to the skin to lose heat; too cold, and it shivers and holds its heat in. Too much water or too little, and the kidneys and thirst adjust. The pattern is always the same three steps — a change is sensed, an answer is made, the condition returns towards its steady point — and it runs all day and all night without being thought about.

**Answer:** The inside conditions that must stay steady → Correcting a change — the body answers back — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
