# Hands-on — Homeostasis — keeping the body in balance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch one correction happen
With an adult nearby: do two minutes of brisk exercise in place, then sit and watch your own body correct itself — breathing slowing, heart settling, skin cooling as sweat dries. List, in order, what changed during the exercise and what your body did to bring each thing back. The lesson is the list: every return on it is homeostasis at work.
