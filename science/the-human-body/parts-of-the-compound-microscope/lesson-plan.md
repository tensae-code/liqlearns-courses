# Lesson plan — The microscope — parts and what each does

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The microscope — parts and what each does
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Cell as the Basis of Life, inside The Human Body (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every part of the compound microscope has a job — the viewing parts magnify, the stage holds the specimen, and the mirror, condenser and diaphragm manage the light.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The microscope — simple and compound, and total magnification (the two lens sets). The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Parts of the light microscope); this lesson is the first-depth Grade 7 version, built new, not copied.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student’s guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## If the learner is stuck
- Confuses coarse and fine knobs → coarse moves the stage a lot, for first focus; fine moves it a little, for sharpening — and only the fine knob on high power (next lesson).
- Confuses condenser and diaphragm → the condenser gathers light onto the specimen; the iris diaphragm controls how much light passes.
