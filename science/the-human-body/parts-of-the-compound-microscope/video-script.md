# Video script — The microscope — parts and what each does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Follow the light.
**Mirror** catches it. **Condenser** gathers it. **Diaphragm** lets some through.
The light passes through the specimen on the **stage**.
Up through the **objective**, up the **tube**, through the **eyepiece** — to your eye.
Every part has one job. Learn the path, and you know them all.
