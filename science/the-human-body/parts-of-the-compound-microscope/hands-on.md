# Hands-on — The microscope — parts and what each does

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Point and name
On a real microscope or a large printed diagram, a partner names a job — "the part that controls the light" — and you point to the part (diaphragm) and name it. Ten jobs, then swap.

## Build the light path
Lay seven cards on the table — mirror, condenser, diaphragm, specimen, objective, tube, eyepiece — shuffle them, then order them as the light travels. Check against the lesson.
