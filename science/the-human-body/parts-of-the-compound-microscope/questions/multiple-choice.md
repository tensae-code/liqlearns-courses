# The microscope — parts and what each does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lens you look through is the —
a) objective  b) eyepiece  c) condenser
**Answer: b) eyepiece**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Turning the revolving nosepiece —
a) focuses the image  b) swings a different objective into place  c) changes the light
**Answer: b) swings a different objective into place**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The strongest objective listed is —
a) 4×  b) 40×  c) 100× (oil)
**Answer: c) 100× (oil)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The slide rests on the —
a) stage  b) arm  c) mirror
**Answer: a) stage**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The part that reflects light up through the specimen is the —
a) diaphragm  b) mirror  c) clip
**Answer: b) mirror**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The iris diaphragm —
a) magnifies  b) holds the slide  c) controls how much light passes
**Answer: c) controls how much light passes**
