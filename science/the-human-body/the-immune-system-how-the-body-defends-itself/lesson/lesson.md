## Sub lesson 2 — How the defence works

The Exercise Book's Body as an Incredible Machine unit takes the process further: how the immune system works, as one system among the body's cooperating systems — the part whose job is guarding all the others.

## Sub lesson 3 — Antibiotics — help against bacteria

The same unit adds medicine's help: how antibiotics prevent bacterial infections. Antibiotics fight bacteria; the immune system remains the body's own defence, with medicine as its ally, not its replacement.
