# Problems of the tongue and mouth

## Do it first
Before you read: your tongue tastes, helps you speak and helps you swallow. What could go wrong with it? Name any tongue trouble you have had or heard of. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — after the tongue's functions and its hygiene, its Primary 4 book names the tongue's disorders and problems as their own topic.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tongue disorders and problems

The book teaches **tongue disorders and problems** as a named topic: the tongue, like every organ in these units, has its own troubles, and they are learned by name so they can be recognised — not feared in the dark.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why the tongue's troubles are taught with its care

The order is the teaching: functions, then hygiene, then problems — the learner who knows what the tongue does and how it is kept clean meets its disorders as the third part of one knowledge, and knows the troubles belong to the organ they already understand.

## The key ideas

- **Tongue disorders and problems** — tongue disorders.
- **Why the tongue's troubles are taught with its care** — the order — functions, hygiene, problems.
