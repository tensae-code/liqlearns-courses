# Problems of the tongue and mouth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Tongue disorders and problems" teach?
**Answer:** The book teaches **tongue disorders and problems** as a named topic: the tongue, like every organ in these units, has its own troubles, and they are learned by name so they can be recognised — not feared in the dark.

**2.** What does the part "Why the tongue's troubles are taught with its care" teach?
**Answer:** The order is the teaching: functions, then hygiene, then problems — the learner who knows what the tongue does and how it is kept clean meets its disorders as the third part of one knowledge, and knows the troubles belong to the organ they already understand.
