# The digestive system — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: name the tube's eight parts in order and say that each part does its own job on the food.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also states both digestions in the mouth, each Grade 5 job (acid and microbes, absorption into blood, the appendix's no-use, water absorption), and the three digestive troubles with their responses, appendicitis as urgent care.
