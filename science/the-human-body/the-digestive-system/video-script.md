# Video script — The digestive system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A bite goes in at the **mouth**.
Down the **pharynx**, down the **oesophagus**.
Into the **stomach** — then the **small intestine**, then the **large intestine**.
What is left waits in the **rectum**, and leaves at the **anus**.
One tube. One way. Every part does its own job.
