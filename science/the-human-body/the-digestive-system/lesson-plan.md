# Lesson plan — The digestive system

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The digestive system
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the inside systems, after the outside parts and senses (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** food travels through one long tube — mouth, pharynx, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus — and each part does its own job on the way
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Parts of the body and the five senses — the outside of the body first; now the inside road that food takes.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Human Body — Parts of the body and the five senses. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the digestive tube's parts in order: mouth, pharynx, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus.
2. Say that each part of the tube does its own job on the food as it passes.
3. Build a model of the digestive system, the book's group project.

## What you need
A bite of food to follow in the mind; paper, clay or cloth for the group model of the system.

## Grade 8 depth (added 2026-11-12)
The Grade 8 General Science book walks the same system as a story — bread and egg from mouth to egestion — adding the bolus, the epiglottis as gatekeeper, peristalsis, pepsin switched on only in place behind a mucus coat, chyme, bile, absorption through villi, water recovery, and the diseases (ulcers, constipation, dysentery, liver cirrhosis, haemorrhoids). Added slots:
- ADD IMAGE [Picture 34106]: a picture of the full tract drawn as a road with the meal's name changing at each stop (bolus → chyme → faeces) — the student names the meal at each stop.
- ADD GIF [GIF 34121]: peristalsis — a squeeze wave travelling down a tube pushing a ball ahead of it — the student squeezes a soft tube behind a marble in time with it.
- ADD AUDIO: the meal's three names read aloud (bolus, chyme, faeces) — the student says where each name belongs. (Described slot only — no audio file is made in this build.)

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the order → the tube is a story with one road: mouth first, anus last, stomach before the intestines, small before large.
- Thinks the stomach is the whole system → it is one stop on the road; the tube begins at the mouth and ends at the anus.
