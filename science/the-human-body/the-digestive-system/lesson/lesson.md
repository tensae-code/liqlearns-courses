# The digestive system

## Do it first
Swallow a bite of food. Where does it go? Most of us can only point vaguely down. Inside you there is one long tube, built for exactly that bite — and this lesson walks the bite down it, part by part, in order.

## The food tube, in order
The digestive system is the body's **food tube**, and its parts stand in one fixed order:

**mouth → pharynx → oesophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → rectum → anus**

The food enters at the **mouth**, passes the **pharynx** at the back of the throat, and travels down the **oesophagus** into the **stomach**. From the stomach it passes through the **small intestine** and then the **large intestine**; what the body cannot use is held in the **rectum** and leaves the body at the **anus**.

Each part does **its own job** on the food as it passes — the book sets the jobs out in a table, part by part. The journey is one-way: food never climbs back up the tube.

## Grade 5 — the mouth starts both digestions; stomach and intestines
The Grade 5 book walks the same tube with the jobs spelled out. **Digestion** runs through the tube helped by **digestive juices (enzymes)**. The **mouth starts both kinds at once** — teeth chew (**physical**) while **saliva works on starch (chemical)**, with the tongue pushing the food — taught through a worked example of eating bread. The **stomach's acid kills microbes** while its juices start on **protein**. The **small intestine finishes digestion and absorbs food through its wall into the blood**. The **appendix (ትርፍ አንጀት)** sits at the junction of small and large intestine with **no digestive use**. The **large intestine** makes no juices — it **absorbs water, stores faeces, and passes them out through the anus**.

## Grade 5 — keeping the digestive system healthy
Three named troubles, with causes and responses. **Constipation** — answer: drink water, eat vegetables. **Gastritis/stomach ulcer** — the stomach's own acid is held off by its **mucus shield**; when the shield fails, the acid bites the wall. **Appendicitis** — **pain to the right of the navel means urgent care**: a burst appendix can kill.

## Say it to your companion
Walk your companion down the tube: name each part as the bite reaches it, in order, without skipping — mouth, pharynx, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus.

## Do It Yourself
1. The model project, the book's own: in a group, build a model of the digestive system — tube, stomach and coiled intestines — from paper, clay or cloth, and label every part in order.
2. Finger-walk the journey on your own body: touch chin, throat, chest, stomach, belly — and name the part under your finger at each stop.
3. Order test: write the eight parts on scraps of paper, shuffle them, and lay them back in the journey's order.
