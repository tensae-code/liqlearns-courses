# Hands-on — The digestive system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the model
The book's group project: build the digestive system as a model — a long tube for the oesophagus, a bag for the stomach, coils for the intestines — and label each part in the journey's order.

## The bite's journey, drawn
Draw one long tube on a big sheet. Draw a bite at the mouth, then move a small stone — your bite — from part to part, saying each name aloud as it travels.

## Grade 8 — the hose-and-marble peristalsis model
Push a marble (or a ball of soft food) into a soft hose or a stocking. Squeeze the tube just **behind** the ball, wave after wave, and watch it travel — never squeeze in front. That travelling squeeze is peristalsis, done with your hands.

## Grade 8 — the tract on the ground
Chalk the whole digestive tract on the ground outdoors, station by station, and walk a meal through it: at each station say the meal's name there (food → bolus → chyme → faeces) and the job done.
