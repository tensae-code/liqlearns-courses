# The digestive system — ordering

Put the parts of the food tube in the order a bite of food meets them.

1. stomach
2. mouth
3. anus
4. small intestine
5. oesophagus
6. large intestine
7. pharynx
8. rectum

**Answer: mouth → pharynx → oesophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → rectum → anus**
