# The digestive system — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Name the eight parts of the food tube in order.**
**Answer: mouth, pharynx, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus**
**2. What are the two answers to constipation the book gives?**
**Answer: Drink water and eat vegetables.**

**3. Explain peristalsis using the hose-and-marble model.**
**Answer: the tube wall squeezes in a wave just behind the food, pushing it forward — as squeezing a hose behind a marble pushes the marble along the hose.**

**4. Name three diseases of the digestive system from this lesson.**
**Answer: any three of: ulcers, constipation, dysentery, liver cirrhosis, haemorrhoids.**
