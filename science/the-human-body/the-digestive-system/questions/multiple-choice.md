# The digestive system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The digestive system is —**
a) a set of separate bags  b) one long tube food travels through  c) part of the leg
**Answer: b) one long tube food travels through**

**2. Food passes from the stomach into the —**
a) pharynx  b) small intestine  c) mouth
**Answer: b) small intestine**

**3. The last part of the tube, where waste leaves the body, is the —**
a) anus  b) oesophagus  c) stomach
**Answer: a) anus**

**4. The tube between the pharynx and the stomach is the —**
a) large intestine  b) rectum  c) oesophagus
**Answer: c) oesophagus**
**5. Saliva in the mouth works on —**
a) protein  b) starch  c) fat only
**Answer: b) starch**

**6. The stomach's acid —**
a) kills microbes  b) makes teeth  c) cools the food
**Answer: a) kills microbes**

**7. Food is absorbed into the blood through the wall of the —**
a) large intestine  b) appendix  c) small intestine
**Answer: c) small intestine**

**8. Pain to the right of the navel can mean —**
a) appendicitis — urgent care  b) hunger only  c) a cold
**Answer: a) appendicitis — urgent care**

**9. Swallowed food, rolled into a soft ball, is called a —**
a) chyme  b) bolus  c) villus
**Answer: b) bolus**

**10. The flap that shuts the windpipe when you swallow is the —**
a) epiglottis  b) villus  c) mucus coat
**Answer: a) epiglottis**

**11. Food is pushed along the tube by —**
a) gravity only  b) peristalsis — a travelling wave of squeezing  c) the heartbeat
**Answer: b) peristalsis — a travelling wave of squeezing**

**12. In the small intestine, food passes into the blood through —**
a) villi  b) teeth  c) the epiglottis
**Answer: a) villi**

**13. Dysentery in this unit is caused by —**
a) Entamoeba histolytica  b) a broken bone  c) cold air
**Answer: a) Entamoeba histolytica**

**14. Chapter 2 review: the food tube's order begins —**
a) mouth, pharynx, oesophagus  b) stomach first  c) anus first
**Answer: a) mouth, pharynx, oesophagus** — Mouth, pharynx, oesophagus — the organs as reviewed.

**15. Chapter 2 review: the organ that follows the stomach in the tube is the —**
a) small intestine  b) liver  c) heart
**Answer: a) small intestine** — Small intestine follows stomach.
