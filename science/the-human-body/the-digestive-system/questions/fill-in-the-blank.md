# The digestive system — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. After the stomach, food enters the ___ intestine.**
**Answer: small**

**2. Waste is held in the ___ before it leaves the body.**
**Answer: rectum**
**3. The stomach is shielded from its own acid by a ______ shield.**
**Answer: mucus**

**4. The soup-like mixture leaving the stomach is called ______.**
**Answer: chyme**

**5. ______ joins the digestion in the small intestine and works on fats.**
**Answer: Bile**
