# The digestive system — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. Food travels through the digestive tube in one direction only.**
**Answer: True**

**2. The large intestine comes before the small intestine on the food's journey.**
**Answer: False**

**3. Each part of the digestive tube does its own job on the food.**
**Answer: True**
**4. The appendix has no digestive use.**
**Answer: True**

**5. The large intestine makes digestive juices.**
**Answer: False**

**6. The stomach protects itself with a mucus coat lining its wall.**
**Answer: True**

**7. Pepsin works everywhere in the body at all times.**
**Answer: False** — pepsin is switched on only in place, in the stomach, where it is needed.

**8. The large intestine's main work in this story is recovering water.**
**Answer: True**
