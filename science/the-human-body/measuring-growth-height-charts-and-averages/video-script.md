Today: **Measuring growth — height charts and averages** — Seychelles' way, from its Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 7).

Seychelles' Unit 7 Topic 1, "Growing up", measures growth directly: the heights of ten pupils are measured, the average height is found from the ten measures, and the results are plotted as a bar chart of height against age. Growth, which is too slow to watch, becomes visible when it is turned into numbers and a chart.

The chart is then read backwards as a prediction tool: from a chart of heights from P1 to P8, pupils estimate what heights to expect in P9. An average is the group's middle measure — it describes the group, not any one pupil — and a chart of averages by age shows the pattern of growing up while hiding each person's own ups and downs.
