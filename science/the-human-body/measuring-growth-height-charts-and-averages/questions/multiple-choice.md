# Measuring growth — height charts and averages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) An average height is found by…
a) adding the heights and dividing by how many were measured  b) taking the tallest height  c) taking the shortest height  d) guessing
**Answer: a) adding the heights and dividing by how many were measured**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson measures the heights of…
a) ten pupils  b) two pupils  c) one hundred pupils  d) no pupils
**Answer: a) ten pupils**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The bar chart plots…
a) height against age  b) age against weight  c) height against shoe size  d) weight against age
**Answer: a) height against age**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The P1–P8 chart is used to estimate…
a) P9 heights  b) P1 weights  c) exam marks  d) rainfall
**Answer: a) P9 heights**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) An average describes…
a) the group, not any one pupil  b) the tallest pupil  c) only the measurer  d) next year's weather
**Answer: a) the group, not any one pupil**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Growth becomes visible in this lesson when it is turned into…
a) numbers and a chart  b) a story  c) a song  d) a photograph only
**Answer: a) numbers and a chart**
