# Measuring growth — height charts and averages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: measuring heights and finding the average.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 7 Topic 1, "Growing up", measures growth directly: the heights of ten pupils are measured, the average height is found from the ten measures, and the results are plotted as a bar chart of height against age.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How is an average height found, and what is the chart of height against age used to estimate?
**Answer:** Add the heights and divide by the number measured; the P1–P8 chart is used to estimate P9 heights.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Measuring heights and finding the average → Reading a height-against-age chart — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
