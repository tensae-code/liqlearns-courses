# Video script — Menstruation and menstrual hygiene

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Menstruation is **natural**.
It means a healthy girl has **matured**.
It is **not a disease**. It is **not a defect**.
Four to six days, about every twenty-eight days.
A pad serves — or a clean cloth of **cotton**, washed and dried in the sun.
Nothing in it is shameful.
