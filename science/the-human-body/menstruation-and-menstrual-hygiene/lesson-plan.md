# Lesson plan — Menstruation and menstrual hygiene

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** Menstruation and menstrual hygiene
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the cycle's plain facts and care (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** menstruation is a healthy sign that a girl has matured — not a disease and not a defect — blood flows 4–6 days about every 28 days, and it is managed with a pad or a clean homemade cloth of 100% cotton
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The human reproductive system — menstruation is that system's cycle when no fertilisation happens.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the book's plain ruling: menstruation means a healthy girl has matured — it is not a disease and not a defect.
2. Say the pattern: blood flows from the uterus through the vagina for 4–6 days, about every 28 days.
3. Say how it is managed: factory-made pads, or — where price or supply is a problem — a clean homemade cloth of 100% cotton.
4. Retell Fatuma's story in summary: fear and shame giving way to relief when her aunt explains.

## What you need
A factory pad and a clean folded cotton cloth to look at, if the class can show them plainly.
