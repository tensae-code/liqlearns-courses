# Hands-on — Menstruation and menstrual hygiene

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Prepare the cloth
Fold a clean cloth of 100% cotton the way it would be used, and practise the care of it — washing it and drying it in sunlight, as a practical hygiene routine carried without embarrassment.

## Tell it before
In pairs, practise telling a younger girl the facts before her first period: what it is, how long, how often, and how it is managed — Fatuma's missing lesson, delivered kindly.
