# Menstruation and menstrual hygiene — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Menstruation, as the book states it, is —**
a) a disease  b) a healthy sign that a girl has matured  c) a defect
**Answer: b) a healthy sign that a girl has matured**

**2. Blood flows for about —**
a) 1 day  b) 4–6 days  c) 20 days
**Answer: b) 4–6 days**

**3. The cycle comes about every —**
a) 28 days  b) 7 days  c) 90 days
**Answer: a) 28 days**

**4. Where price or supply is a problem, the book's answer is —**
a) staying home  b) a clean homemade cloth of 100% cotton  c) any rag at all
**Answer: b) a clean homemade cloth of 100% cotton**

**5. In Fatuma's story, who explains menstruation to her?**
a) her brother  b) her aunt  c) a stranger
**Answer: b) her aunt**
