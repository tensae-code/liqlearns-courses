# Hormones and the endocrine system — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The endocrine system.
**Answer:** Beside the nervous system, the body runs a second messaging system: the endocrine system.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Released on demand.
**Answer:** The Exercise Book adds the living detail: hormones are released from glands on demand, when the body needs them — not poured steadily, but sent when called for..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Too much, too little.
**Answer:** And the same unit names the failure: when more or less than the needed amount is released, unwanted reactions follow.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The endocrine system → Released on demand → Too much, too little — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
