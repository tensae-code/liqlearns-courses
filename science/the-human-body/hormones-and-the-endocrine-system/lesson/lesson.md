## Sub lesson 1 — The endocrine system

Beside the nervous system, the body runs a second messaging system: the endocrine system. Class 9's Investigative Study teaches it with the important hormones of the human body — chemical messages carried to the parts they command.

## Sub lesson 2 — Released on demand

The Exercise Book adds the living detail: hormones are released from glands on demand, when the body needs them — not poured steadily, but sent when called for.

## Sub lesson 3 — Too much, too little

And the same unit names the failure: when more or less than the needed amount is released, unwanted reactions follow. A messaging system that must hit its amount is a system that can miss, in both directions.
