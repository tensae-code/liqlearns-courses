# Hands-on — The skeleton — frame, joints and fractures

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Build the skeleton in clay
The book's project: model the skeleton in clay — skull, backbone and ribs in one colour (axial), arms, legs, shoulder girdle and pelvis in another (appendicular). Present it and defend every bone's colour.

## Joint survey
Move each of these slowly: elbow, knee, fingers, shoulder, neck, jaw. For each, say what bends, what stays, and where the cushioning cartilage must be. Write the list.

## Bone-setter interview
Prepare questions for a local bone setter (with a teacher): how do you tell a break? How do you set it? What do you splint with? How long does healing take? Write the answers as a class report — their practice is local knowledge, recorded with respect.
