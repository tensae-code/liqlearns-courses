# The skeleton — frame, joints and fractures — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the main work of the axial skeleton and of the appendicular skeleton.
**Answer: the axial skeleton primarily protects the central nervous system (brain and spinal cord) and chest organs; the appendicular skeleton carries body weight, mobility and upright posture.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two questions pupils are sent to ask a local bone setter.
**Answer: any two of: how do you tell a bone is broken? How do you set it? What do you splint it with? How do you know it is healing?**
