# The skeleton — frame, joints and fractures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The axial skeleton is made of —
a) arms and legs  b) braincase, backbone and ribs  c) shoulder girdle and pelvis
**Answer: b) braincase, backbone and ribs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The adult bone count, 206, is lower than the baby's ~300 because —
a) bones are lost in childhood  b) cartilage turns to bone and separate bones fuse together  c) adults stop growing bones away
**Answer: b) cartilage turns to bone and separate bones fuse together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ligaments —
a) tie bone to bone at a joint  b) carry blood  c) make new bone
**Answer: a) tie bone to bone at a joint**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cartilage at a joint —
a) glues the joint stiff  b) cushions the bone ends so they do not grind  c) feeds the muscle
**Answer: b) cushions the bone ends so they do not grind**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** An open fracture is one where —
a) the bone bends  b) the break opens through the skin  c) only cartilage is hurt
**Answer: b) the break opens through the skin**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Rickets is a disease in which —
a) children's bones soften and bend  b) joints fill with blood  c) bones grow too long
**Answer: a) children's bones soften and bend**
