# The skeleton — frame, joints and fractures — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The appendicular skeleton's work includes carrying body weight and upright posture.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A joint is the place where two bones meet and the frame can move.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A closed fracture is more dangerous than an open one because germs can reach the bone.
**Answer: False** — the open fracture is the more dangerous, because the break opens through the skin where germs can enter.
