# The skeleton — frame, joints and fractures — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Much of a baby's skeleton is still soft ______ before it turns to bone.
**Answer: cartilage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The smooth tissue capping bone ends inside a joint is ______.
**Answer: cartilage**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In ______, bones thin and weaken with age.
**Answer: osteoporosis**
