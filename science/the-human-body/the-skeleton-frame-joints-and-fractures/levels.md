# The skeleton — frame, joints and fractures — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as sub lessons: what the learner must show at each part, and how practice deepens inside it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two skeletons in one frame
Pass mark: sort named bones into axial and appendicular and give each part's work (axial protects the central nervous system; appendicular carries weight, mobility and upright posture); explain the count falling from ~300 to 206 by cartilage replacement and fusing. The count explanation is teacher-guide evidence and is passed as evidence, labelled as such.

## Sub lesson 2 — Joints, ligaments and cartilage
Pass mark: say what a joint is, that ligaments tie bone to bone, and that cartilage cushions and protects the bone ends.

## Sub lesson 3 — Breakages, bone setters and bone diseases
Pass mark: tell open from closed fractures, name osteoporosis, osteoarthritis, rickets and Paget's disease with one line each, and frame three respectful questions for a local bone setter.

The same ground is met again at secondary depth in Biology (Axial and appendicular skeletons; Types of joints); this lesson never copies or grows a Biology file.
