# Lesson plan — The brain

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The brain
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the inside systems, after the outside parts and senses (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the brain is the body's control centre — it has three parts and sits protected inside the skull
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Parts of the body and the five senses — the senses carry news in; the brain is where the news is answered.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Human Body — Parts of the body and the five senses. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the brain is: the body's control centre.
2. Say the brain has three parts.
3. Say what protects it: the skull, the hard bone case around it.

## What you need
Nothing to hold — a hand rested on the head, feeling the hard skull, is the lesson's prop.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner taps their own head gently and feels the hard bone under the skin, and is asked what precious soft thing that bone might be guarding (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks thinking happens in the heart or stomach → every sense's news and every action's order runs through the brain; it is the one control centre.
- Forgets the protection → the hand on the head is the reminder: hard skull outside, soft brain inside.
