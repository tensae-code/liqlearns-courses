# The brain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The brain is the body's —**
a) food store  b) control centre  c) air bag
**Answer: b) control centre**

**2. The brain has —**
a) two parts  b) three parts  c) ten parts
**Answer: b) three parts**

**3. The brain is protected inside the —**
a) skull  b) stomach  c) skin only
**Answer: a) skull**
