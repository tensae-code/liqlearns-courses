# Lesson plan — Emotions and emotional intelligence

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** Emotions and emotional intelligence
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — appended to Unit 3 (The Human Body) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** self-esteem is built from the trust, affection and care a child receives, and school bullying (acoso escolar) is named as an attack on it that the community must stop.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Self-esteem is built from the trust, affection and care** — self-esteem is built from the trust, affection and care a child receives, and school bullying (acoso escolar) is named as an attack on it that the community must stop.
2. **Emotions are short physiological reactions to stimuli, while feelings** — emotions are short physiological reactions to stimuli, while feelings are their longer-lasting interpretation that shapes our mood — the book separates the two before teaching either.
3. **When an emotion fires, the brain releases hormones into** — when an emotion fires, the brain releases hormones into the blood (oxytocin, dopamine, noradrenaline, adrenaline) that prepare the body's reaction; the face alone has 42 muscles that display emotions as non-verbal communication.
