# Emotions and emotional intelligence — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about self-esteem is built from the trust, affection and care?
**Answer: self-esteem is built from the trust, affection and care a child receives, and school bullying (acoso escolar) is named as an attack on it that the community must stop.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about emotions are short physiological reactions to stimuli, while feelings?
**Answer: emotions are short physiological reactions to stimuli, while feelings are their longer-lasting interpretation that shapes our mood — the book separates the two before teaching either.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about when an emotion fires, the brain releases hormones into?
**Answer: when an emotion fires, the brain releases hormones into the blood (oxytocin, dopamine, noradrenaline, adrenaline) that prepare the body's reaction; the face alone has 42 muscles that display emotions as non-verbal communication.**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Emotions and emotional intelligence.**
