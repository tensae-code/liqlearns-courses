Today: **Emotions and emotional intelligence** — Honduras's way.

self-esteem is built from the trust, affection and care a child receives, and school bullying (acoso escolar) is named as an attack on it that the community must stop. emotions are short physiological reactions to stimuli, while feelings are their longer-lasting interpretation that shapes our mood — the book separates the two before teaching either. when an emotion fires, the brain releases hormones into the blood (oxytocin, dopamine, noradrenaline, adrenaline) that prepare the body's reaction; the face alone has 42 muscles that display emotions as non-verbal communication.
