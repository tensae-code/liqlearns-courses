The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

Go back to the lesson's first part — Self-esteem is built from the trust, affection and care. self-esteem is built from the trust, affection and care a child receives, and school bullying (acoso escolar) is named as an attack on it that the community must stop. Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
