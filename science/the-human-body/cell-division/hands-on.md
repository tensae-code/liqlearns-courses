# Hands-on — Cell division

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the doubling
Start with 1 counter for the first cell. Double the pile for each division round: 1, 2, 4, 8… After ten rounds, how many counters would there be? (Work it out on paper: 2 multiplied ten times.) Say what this arithmetic explains about how a whole body can come from one cell — and why repair never runs out of cells.
