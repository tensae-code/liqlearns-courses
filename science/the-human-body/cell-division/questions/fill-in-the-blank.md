# Cell division — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** One cell divides to become _____.
**Answer: two**

**2.** Before dividing, the cell _____ its hereditary material.
**Answer: copies**

**3.** Division fills wounds — that job is called _____.
**Answer: repair**

**4.** The Biology twin lesson is named _____.
**Answer: mitosis**
