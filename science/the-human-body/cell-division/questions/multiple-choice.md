# Cell division — multiple choice


**1.** Cell division is…
a) One cell eating another  b) One cell splitting into two after copying its material  c) Cells shrinking  d) Cells turning to stone
**Answer: b) One cell splitting into two after copying its material**

**2.** Each new cell receives…
a) No hereditary material  b) A full copy of the hereditary material  c) Half a nucleus only  d) Only water
**Answer: b) A full copy of the hereditary material**

**3.** Growth of the body happens by…
a) Cells swelling only  b) Cell division multiplying cells  c) Air entering cells  d) Bones stretching
**Answer: b) Cell division multiplying cells**

**4.** Which is a job of cell division?
a) Digestion  b) Repair of wounds  c) Seeing  d) Hearing
**Answer: b) Repair of wounds**

**5.** Skin and blood are renewed by…
a) Division of their cells  b) Sunlight  c) Sleep only  d) Food colour
**Answer: a) Division of their cells**

**6.** The secondary-depth twin of this lesson is the Biology lesson on…
a) Photosynthesis  b) Mitosis  c) Fertilisation  d) Respiration
**Answer: b) Mitosis**
