# Cell division — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What happens inside a cell before it divides?
**Answer: It copies its hereditary material, so that each of the two new cells receives a full copy.**

**2.** Name the three jobs of cell division.
**Answer: Growth, repair (including renewing worn-out cells), and making reproduction possible.**

**3.** How does a body of millions of cells come from one cell?
**Answer: By division doubling the cells again and again — 1, 2, 4, 8 — organised into tissues and organs.**

**4.** Why does this course hold cell division twice?
**Answer: This general lesson teaches it as the body's engine of growth and repair; the Biology title teaches mitosis step by step at secondary depth.**
