# Lesson plan — The kidneys

**Course:** Science · **Title:** The Human Body · **Lesson:** The kidneys
**Place in the curriculum:** The Human Body — the inside systems, after the outside parts and senses (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the kidneys — two bean-shaped organs — keep the body's water and salt in balance, remove waste, and help the body make red blood cells
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Parts of the body and the five senses — the body's cleaners, met after the tube that food travels.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the kidneys: two bean-shaped organs.
2. Say their three jobs: keep water and salt in balance, remove waste, and stimulate red blood-cell production.

## What you need
A bean to hold — the kidney's own shape, in the hand.

## If the learner is stuck
- Gives the kidneys the heart's or lungs' job → each organ has its own: heart pumps, lungs breathe, kidneys balance and clean.
- Forgets the third job → the kidneys also signal for red blood cells; the book names it, so the lesson keeps it.
