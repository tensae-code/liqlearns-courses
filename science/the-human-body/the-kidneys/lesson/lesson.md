# The kidneys

## Do it first
Hold a bean in your palm. It is small, curved, with a dent in one side. Now — your body carries two organs shaped just like this bean, and they work quietly all day keeping you in balance. They are the kidneys.

## The body's balancers
The **kidneys** are **two bean-shaped organs** inside the body. Small as they are, the book gives them three great jobs:

1. **They keep the body's water and salt in balance** — not too much, not too little.
2. **They remove waste** from the body — the cleaning work that keeps the inside fresh.
3. **They stimulate red blood-cell production** — they signal the body to make more of the red cells the blood needs.

Balance, cleaning, and a signal to build: two bean-shaped organs, working without a sound.

## Do It Yourself
1. Bean match: draw a bean, then draw the kidney beside it — same shape. Label it with the three jobs.
2. Balance thinking: drink a glass of water. The kidneys will keep the body's water level right — say what 'in balance' means in your own words.
3. Job sort: here are three jobs — pumping blood, removing waste, taking in oxygen. Which belongs to the kidneys? Defend your answer.
