# The kidneys — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The kidneys are shaped like —**
a) beans  b) stars  c) tubes
**Answer: a) beans**

**2. How many kidneys does the body have?**
a) one  b) two  c) four
**Answer: b) two**

**3. One job of the kidneys is to —**
a) pump blood  b) keep water and salt in balance  c) take in oxygen
**Answer: b) keep water and salt in balance**

**4. The kidneys also stimulate the production of —**
a) red blood cells  b) hair  c) teeth
**Answer: a) red blood cells**
