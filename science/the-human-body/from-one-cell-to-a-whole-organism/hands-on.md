# Hands-on — Cells — from one cell to a whole organism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Staircase climb
Write the five levels on five large cards and lay them as stairs on the floor. Step on each one in turn, naming the level and one example at the top of your voice. Then jump the order: a partner calls a level, you jump to its step.

## One-person shop list
List every job in a one-person shop on the left of a page, and the town worker who does that job on the right. Under it write: unicellular = left column in one cell; multicellular = right column, divided.
