# Cells — from one cell to a whole organism — ordering

Put each list in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the levels in order, smallest first: organism, tissue, cell, organ system, organ.
**Answer: cell, tissue, organ, organ system, organism**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put this animal chain in order: circulatory system, heart muscle cell, horse, heart, heart muscle tissue.
**Answer: heart muscle cell, heart muscle tissue, heart, circulatory system, horse**
