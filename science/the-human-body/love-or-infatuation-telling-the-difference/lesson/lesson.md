# Love or infatuation — telling the difference

## Do it first
Before you read: two feelings can look alike from inside. What is the difference between love and infatuation — the strong, sudden crush? Say where you would draw the line. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — its Primary 5 reproductive unit closes not with the body alone, but with this question of feelings, taught plainly to the learners meeting puberty.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two feelings that can look alike

The book names the pair: **love** and **infatuation** — two feelings a young person can confuse, taught as a topic of the science book itself, beside puberty and responsible behaviour. Naming both is the first step of the lesson.

## Sub lesson 2 — Telling the difference

The topic's work is the telling: **love or infatuation — telling the difference**. The learner is given the difference as knowledge to use — a feeling known for what it is can be answered with the safe, responsible behaviour the same unit teaches.
