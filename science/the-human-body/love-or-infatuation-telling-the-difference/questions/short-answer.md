# Love or infatuation — telling the difference — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Two feelings that can look alike" teach?
**Answer:** The book names the pair: **love** and **infatuation** — two feelings a young person can confuse, taught as a topic of the science book itself, beside puberty and responsible behaviour.

**2.** What does the part "Telling the difference" teach?
**Answer:** The topic's work is the telling: **love or infatuation — telling the difference**.
