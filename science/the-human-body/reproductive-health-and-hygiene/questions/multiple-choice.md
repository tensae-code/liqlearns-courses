# Reproductive health and hygiene — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cambodia's Grade 6 book teaches reproductive health in…
a) Lesson 9  b) Lesson 1  c) Lesson 17  d) the Earth chapter
**Answer: a) Lesson 9** — Lesson 9 — closing the book's run on reproduction.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson comes straight after lessons on…
a) the reproductive organs, puberty, and preventing pregnancy and staying safe  b) the planets  c) simple machines  d) the soil
**Answer: a) the reproductive organs, puberty, and preventing pregnancy and staying safe** — Lessons 6, 7 and 8 build to it in that order.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Hygiene means…
a) the daily care that keeps the body clean and healthy  b) a medicine  c) a kind of food  d) an exercise
**Answer: a) the daily care that keeps the body clean and healthy** — Hygiene is daily care — washing, cleanliness and clean clothes.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Pain, itching or an unusual change in the private parts is a sign to…
a) tell a trusted adult and seek care  b) hide it  c) ignore it always  d) stop washing
**Answer: a) tell a trusted adult and seek care** — Changes are signs to seek care, not things to hide.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Reproductive health is kept by…
a) care, cleanliness and staying safe  b) luck alone  c) medicine only  d) doing nothing
**Answer: a) care, cleanliness and staying safe** — Health here is the sum of small daily care and safe choices.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson stands in the book's run on…
a) the human body and reproduction  b) the Earth  c) materials  d) electricity
**Answer: a) the human body and reproduction** — It is the closing lesson of the Grade 6 reproduction sequence.
