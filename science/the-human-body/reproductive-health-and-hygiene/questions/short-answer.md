# Reproductive health and hygiene — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what reproductive health means.
**Answer:** Reproductive health is the state of a reproductive system working as it should, kept that way by care, cleanliness and staying safe.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name two daily hygiene habits from the lesson and say what each protects.
**Answer:** For example: washing and keeping the private parts clean, and wearing clean clothes — both protect the system against dirt-borne sickness and keep it healthy.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Reproductive health is a system kept healthy, closing the book's run on organs, puberty and staying safe → hygiene — daily washing, cleanliness and seeking care at any change — is how that health is kept — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
