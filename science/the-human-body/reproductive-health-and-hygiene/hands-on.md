# Hands-on — Reproductive health and hygiene

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's own order
Write the Grade 6 book's four-lesson run in order — organs, puberty, preventing pregnancy and staying safe, reproductive health — and say in one line what each lesson adds. Check against the lesson.

## Daily care list
Write the daily hygiene habits this lesson names. Beside each one, write what it protects against, in your own words.
