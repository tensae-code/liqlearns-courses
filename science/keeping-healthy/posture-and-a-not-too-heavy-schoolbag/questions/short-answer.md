# Posture — and a not-too-heavy schoolbag — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Kazakhstan's books teach about posture — and a not-too-heavy schoolbag?
**Answer: Posture defined as the body's habitual position standing, sitting and walking; a child's soft bones bend easily, carrying a bag on one shoulder curves the spine, so the class weighs each textbook and the whole bag day by day against the norm, and tests posture against a wall (nape, shoulder blades, calves, buttocks, heels touching) Posture is the body's correct formation: sit and walk upright, bac**
