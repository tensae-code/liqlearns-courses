# Hands-on — Reading food labels — nutrition information

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read three real packets (no materials beyond packaging)
Collect three empty snack packets (from home or around school, as the book's class does). For each one, find the nutrition information and write down its sugar figure and its salt figure. Put the three in order — most sugar to least, then most salt to least. Is the order the same both ways? Finish with one sentence: which packet you would choose now that you have read the labels, and which figure decided you.
