# Reading food labels — nutrition information — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The label on the packet  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The label on the packet**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Sugar and salt — the figures to watch  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Sugar and salt — the figures to watch**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Choosing from what the label says  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Choosing from what the label says**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: reading the nutrition information printed on food packaging. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Indonesia, Grade 6 — *Ilmu Pengetahuan Alam dan Sosial untuk SD/MI Kelas VI (Edisi Revisi)*, Bab 1, printed p. 19  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Indonesia, Grade 6 — *Ilmu Pengetahuan Alam dan Sosial untuk SD/MI Kelas VI (Edisi Revisi)*, Bab 1, printed p. 19**
