# Reading food labels — nutrition information — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The label on the packet.
**Answer:** Packaged food carries its story on the packet: the nutrition information — the printed panel that says what is inside the food and how much of each thing one portion holds.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Sugar and salt — the figures to watch.
**Answer:** A label holds many numbers, and the book gives its guidance on where to look first: sugar and salt.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Choosing from what the label says.
**Answer:** Reading the label is for choosing: two snacks side by side can be compared by their printed figures, and the reader picks knowing what is inside instead of guessing from the packet's front.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The label on the packet → Sugar and salt — the figures to watch → Choosing from what the label says — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
