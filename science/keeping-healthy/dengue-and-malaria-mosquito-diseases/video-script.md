# Video script — Dengue and malaria — mosquito diseases

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Dengue and malaria — mosquito diseases** — Cambodia's way, from its Grade 4 book *វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ ថ្នាក់ទី៤ (Science, Grade 4)*.

Some diseases travel from person to person through the air or through dirty hands. **Dengue fever** and **malaria** travel a different road: a **mosquito** carries them. The mosquito bites a person who has the disease, takes the sickness in with the blood, and passes it on when it bites the next person. Cambodia's Grade 4 book puts the two diseases in one lesson because the carrier is the same — stop the mosquito's road, and both diseases are stopped.

Because the carrier is the mosquito, the defence is aimed at the mosquito and its bites. Mosquitoes breed where water stands still, so the ground around the home is kept clear of standing water; and people protect themselves from bites, above all while sleeping. The lesson's point is the chain: no breeding water and no bites means no road — and a disease with no road cannot spread.
