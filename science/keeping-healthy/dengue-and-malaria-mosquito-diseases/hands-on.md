# Hands-on — Dengue and malaria — mosquito diseases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hunt the breeding water
Walk around your home or school with an adult and list every place water stands still — tins, jars, puddles, blocked drains. For each one, say what could be done so a mosquito cannot breed there. Check your list against the lesson.

## Draw the road
Draw the disease's road in three boxes: a sick person, a mosquito, a healthy person. Under the drawing, write where the road can be broken, and how.
