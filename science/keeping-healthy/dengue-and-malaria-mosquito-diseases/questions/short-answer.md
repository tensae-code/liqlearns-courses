# Dengue and malaria — mosquito diseases — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, how a mosquito carries dengue fever or malaria from one person to another.
**Answer:** The mosquito bites a person who has the disease, takes the sickness in with the blood, and passes it on when it bites the next person.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does clearing standing water help stop these diseases?
**Answer:** Mosquitoes breed where water stands still; with no breeding water there are fewer mosquitoes and the diseases' road is broken.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Dengue fever and malaria are two diseases carried by mosquitoes → they are stopped by breaking the mosquito's road — clearing breeding water and protecting people from bites — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
