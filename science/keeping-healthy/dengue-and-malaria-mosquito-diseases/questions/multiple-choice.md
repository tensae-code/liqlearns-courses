# Dengue and malaria — mosquito diseases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dengue fever and malaria are both carried by…
a) a mosquito  b) the wind  c) dirty hands only  d) cold weather
**Answer: a) a mosquito** — Both diseases travel by mosquito bite — that is why the book teaches them in one lesson.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A mosquito picks up the disease when it…
a) bites a person who has the disease  b) drinks clean water  c) rests on a wall  d) flies over a house
**Answer: a) bites a person who has the disease** — The mosquito takes the sickness in with the blood of a sick person and passes it on at the next bite.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Mosquitoes breed where…
a) water stands still  b) the ground is dry  c) the wind is strong  d) food is cooked
**Answer: a) water stands still** — Standing water is the mosquitoes' breeding place — clearing it breaks the road.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) One way to break the mosquito's road is to…
a) clear standing water around the home  b) leave jars of water open  c) sleep without any protection from bites  d) keep puddles by the door
**Answer: a) clear standing water around the home** — No breeding water and no bites means no road for the disease.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The two diseases are taught in one lesson because…
a) they share the same carrier  b) they are the same disease  c) they happen in winter  d) they are spread by food
**Answer: a) they share the same carrier** — Same carrier, same defence — the lesson's whole point.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Protecting people from bites matters most…
a) while sleeping  b) while swimming  c) at harvest time only  d) never
**Answer: a) while sleeping** — People are bitten while they rest — protection from bites, above all while sleeping, breaks the chain.
