# Dengue and malaria — mosquito diseases — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Dengue fever and malaria are carried by the ______.
**Answer: mosquito**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Mosquitoes breed where water ______ ______.
**Answer: stands still**

**3.** (Whole lesson) A disease with no ______ cannot spread.
**Answer: road**
