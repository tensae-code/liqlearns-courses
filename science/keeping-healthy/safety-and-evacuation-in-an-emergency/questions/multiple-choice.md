# Safety and evacuation in an emergency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Leaving in order  b) Practising the safe way out  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Safety and evacuation
**Answer: d) Safety and evacuation**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about leaving safely when danger comes?
a) Safety and evacuation  b) Leaving in order  c) Practising the safe way out
**Answer: a) Safety and evacuation**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about leaving in order, not in panic?
a) Safety and evacuation  b) Leaving in order  c) Practising the safe way out
**Answer: b) Leaving in order**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about the practised way out?
a) Safety and evacuation  b) Leaving in order  c) Practising the safe way out
**Answer: c) Practising the safe way out**
