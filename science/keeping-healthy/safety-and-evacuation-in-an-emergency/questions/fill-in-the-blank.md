# Safety and evacuation in an emergency — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Safety and evacuation.
**Answer: leaving safely when danger comes**

**2.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Leaving in order.
**Answer: leaving in order, not in panic**

**3.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Practising the safe way out.
**Answer: the practised way out**
