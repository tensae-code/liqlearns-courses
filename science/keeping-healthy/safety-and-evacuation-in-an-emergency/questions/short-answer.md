# Safety and evacuation in an emergency — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Safety and evacuation" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's guides teach **safety and evacuation** as a named unit at ECD A and again in Grades 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5: when danger comes, safety means leaving the place in the right way.

**2.** What does the part "Leaving in order" teach?
**Answer:** Evacuation is not running: it is leaving **in order**, the way the guides' hazard topics build it, so nobody is hurt in the leaving itself.

**3.** What does the part "Practising the safe way out" teach?
**Answer:** A way out that is only talked about is not learned.
