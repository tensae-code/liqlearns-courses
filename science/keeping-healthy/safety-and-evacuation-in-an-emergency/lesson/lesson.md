## Sub lesson 1 — Safety and evacuation

Zimbabwe's guides teach **safety and evacuation** as a named unit at ECD A and again in Grades 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5: when danger comes, safety means leaving the place in the right way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Leaving in order

Evacuation is not running: it is leaving **in order**, the way the guides' hazard topics build it, so nobody is hurt in the leaving itself.

## Sub lesson 3 — Practising the safe way out

A way out that is only talked about is not learned. The topic sits inside every year's hazards-preparedness run in Zimbabwe's guides, so the safe way out is returned to and practised year after year.

## The key ideas

- **Safety and evacuation** — leaving safely when danger comes.
- **Leaving in order** — leaving in order, not in panic.
- **Practising the safe way out** — the practised way out.
