# Traditional, modern and complementary medicine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: three kinds of medicine — traditional, modern and complementary — compared: their medicines, their strengths and weaknesses, and how to choose health products carefully. That is…
