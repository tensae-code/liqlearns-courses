# Traditional, modern and complementary medicine — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Three kinds of medicine compared.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Form 4 book compares **three kinds of medicine: traditional, modern and complementary** — their criteria side by side; **modern medicine** as the combination of health sciences (clinical medicine, research, surgery). The medicines of each approach are named: modern medicines are classified (**analgesics, antibiotics** and more) and come in forms — **pills, capsules, suspensions**. The three approaches **complement one another**; their strengths and weaknesses are compared, with an activity on natural vs man-made vitamin C.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Health products — choosing carefully.
**Answer:** For health products the book defines the **active ingredients** — the specific compounds that improve health — and teaches choosing carefully: **seek medical advice and check the product** before trusting a claim.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Three kinds of medicine compared → Health products — choosing carefully — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
