# Hands-on — Sanitation — clean water and waste

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two paths map
Draw your home twice: once following clean water in, once following waste out. Check the two drawings never cross.

## Store it safely
Practise safe storage: fill the drinking-water container, cover it, and set it in its place off the ground. Say each rule as you do it.
