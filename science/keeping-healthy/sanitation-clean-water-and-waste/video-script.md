Today: **Sanitation — clean water and waste** — Angola's way: sanitation stands just before water, because it is what makes water safe.

Basic sanitation keeps water and waste apart. Clean water is drawn, carried and stored so dirt and germs cannot get back in.

And waste — rubbish, dirty water, the latrine — is taken safely away, so it cannot return as flies, smells and sickness.
