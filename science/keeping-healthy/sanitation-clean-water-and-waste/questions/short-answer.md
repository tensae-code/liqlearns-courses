# Sanitation — clean water and waste — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the sanitation lesson come just before the water lesson in Angola's book?
**Answer:** Because sanitation is what makes water safe to use — the arrangements around water decide whether the water of the next lesson reaches people clean.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the two halves of basic sanitation.
**Answer:** Clean water kept clean — drawn, carried and stored against dirt and germs — and waste taken safely away: rubbish, dirty water and the latrine kept from water and play ground.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens when waste is left anywhere?
**Answer:** It returns — in flies, in smells and in sickness — because nothing keeps it from the water and ground people live on.
