# Sanitation — clean water and waste — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Basic sanitation is…
a) A kind of medicine
b) The arrangements that keep water and waste apart so people stay healthy
c) A school timetable
d) A type of house
**Answer: b) The arrangements that keep water and waste apart so people stay healthy**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Drinking water must be stored so that…
a) It warms up
b) Dirt and germs cannot get back into it
c) It changes colour
d) Flies can reach it
**Answer: b) Dirt and germs cannot get back into it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Waste left anywhere returns as…
a) Clean water
b) Flies, smells and sickness
c) More food
d) Shade
**Answer: b) Flies, smells and sickness**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Angola's Grade 4 book teaches basic sanitation…
a) After the history unit
b) Right before its lesson on water itself
c) In Grade 1
d) Inside the plant lessons
**Answer: b) Right before its lesson on water itself**
