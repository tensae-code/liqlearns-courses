# Hands-on — Autoimmune diseases — when the body attacks itself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Autoimmunity. autoimmunity: normally the immune system combats viruses, bacteria or any other infectious organism threatening health, but when a failure occurs the same system designed to protect the body can turn against it: when the immune system does not work adequately it cannot distinguish the body's own cells from foreign ones, and instead of fighting external antigens, the immune system's cells or the antibodies they produce can go against their own cells and tissues by error; the components involved in the offence are called autoreactive lymphocytes or autoantibodies (guide evidence) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
