# Video script — Chat, tobacco and alcohol: stimulants and their harm

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Chat, tobacco, alcohol** — stimulants, not foods.
Their harm: **mental health**, lost work, less produced, more crime, danger of illness.
The **successful** — pupil, farmer, teacher, doctor — are those who **keep away**.
