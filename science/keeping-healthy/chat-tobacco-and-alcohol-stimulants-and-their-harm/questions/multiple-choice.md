# Chat, tobacco and alcohol: stimulants and their harm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is a stimulant plant named in the book?**
a) chat  b) teff  c) maize
**Answer: a) chat**

**2. Tobacco is —**
a) the cigarette plant  b) a grain  c) a fruit tree
**Answer: a) the cigarette plant**

**3. One harm of stimulants in the book's counting is —**
a) better memory  b) lowered productivity  c) stronger teeth
**Answer: b) lowered productivity**

**4. The book's closing counsel says successful people —**
a) use stimulants daily  b) keep away from harmful drugs  c) never work
**Answer: b) keep away from harmful drugs**
