# Chat, tobacco and alcohol: stimulants and their harm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is a stimulant plant named in the book?**
a) chat  b) teff  c) maize
**Answer: a) chat**

**2. Tobacco is —**
a) the cigarette plant  b) a grain  c) a fruit tree
**Answer: a) the cigarette plant**

**3. One harm of stimulants in the book's counting is —**
a) better memory  b) lowered productivity  c) stronger teeth
**Answer: b) lowered productivity**

**4. The book's closing counsel says successful people —**
a) use stimulants daily  b) keep away from harmful drugs  c) never work
**Answer: b) keep away from harmful drugs**
**5. Addiction (የዕፅ ሱስ) is —**
a) a kind of food  b) the slavery of being ruled by a substance  c) a sport
**Answer: b) the slavery of being ruled by a substance**

**6. Which is a push factor for starting?**
a) peer pressure  b) good sleep  c) school fees paid
**Answer: a) peer pressure**

**7. Chat carries the stimulant —**
a) nicotine  b) cathinone  c) caffeine only
**Answer: b) cathinone**

**8. Cigarettes carry the stimulant —**
a) cathinone  b) nicotine  c) aspirin
**Answer: b) nicotine**

**9. In the story, Ayele and Seada's teacher decides to —**
a) only punish them  b) rescue them rather than merely punish  c) ignore them
**Answer: b) rescue them rather than merely punish**

**10. Doped athletes, when caught, —**
a) keep their medals  b) are stripped of medals and barred  c) are praised
**Answer: b) are stripped of medals and barred**
