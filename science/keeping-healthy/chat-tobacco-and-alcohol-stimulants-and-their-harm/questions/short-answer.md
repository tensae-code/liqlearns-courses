# Chat, tobacco and alcohol: stimulants and their harm — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Name the harms of stimulants as the book counts them.**
**Answer: harm mental health; waste working time; lower productivity; encourage crime; expose users to mental illness and HIV/AIDS**
**2. Name three costs from the negatives list.**
**Answer: Any three of: heavy money loss, mental disorder, sleeplessness, self-neglect, lost appetite, family break-up, a wasted life.**
