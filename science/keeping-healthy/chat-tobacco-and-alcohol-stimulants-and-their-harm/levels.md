# Chat, tobacco and alcohol: stimulants and their harm — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: name the three stimulants, count the book's five harms, and say the closing counsel.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5/6 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also defines addiction (የዕፅ ሱስ), recites the five push factors and the negative list, retells Ayele and Seada with the teacher's rescue as its point, names chat/cathinone and cigarettes/nicotine, and argues the border and doping discussions.
