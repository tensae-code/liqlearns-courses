# Hands-on — Chat, tobacco and alcohol: stimulants and their harm

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The radio programme
The book's activity, done properly: a host and two guests, five minutes, the harms counted aloud and the counsel given at the end. An audience of family counts as a broadcast.

## Time is money
List what one working hour produces on a farm or in a shop (ask a worker). Multiply by a wasted afternoon. The stimulant harm 'wasted time' becomes a number.
