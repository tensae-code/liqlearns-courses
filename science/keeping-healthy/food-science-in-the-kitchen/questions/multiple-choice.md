# Food science in the kitchen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) The kitchen as the science place  b) Science that feeds planning  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Food Science and Technology
**Answer: d) Food Science and Technology**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about food studied with science?
a) Food Science and Technology  b) The kitchen as the science place  c) Science that feeds planning
**Answer: a) Food Science and Technology**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about the kitchen as the place of the science?
a) Food Science and Technology  b) The kitchen as the science place  c) Science that feeds planning
**Answer: b) The kitchen as the science place**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about kitchen science feeding meal planning?
a) Food Science and Technology  b) The kitchen as the science place  c) Science that feeds planning
**Answer: c) Science that feeds planning**
