# Food science in the kitchen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Food Science and Technology" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 4 guide teaches **Food Science and Technology** as a named unit: food is studied with science, and the kitchen is where that science is practised daily.

**2.** What does the part "The kitchen as the science place" teach?
**Answer:** The unit sits inside the food run, between food nutrients and meal planning: the science is not in a far laboratory but in the place food is prepared, with the food itself as the material.

**3.** What does the part "Science that feeds planning" teach?
**Answer:** What the kitchen science shows feeds the next unit, meal planning and budgeting: knowing what food does as it is prepared is what makes planning meals a skilled job.
