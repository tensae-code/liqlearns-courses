# Lesson plan — Staying safe online

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Staying safe online
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** staying safe online — not sharing personal information, keeping strong passwords secret, chatting only with people you know, and knowing what counts as violating someone's personal space online — Turkey's own way, in its Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi book, under the heading Çevrim İçi Güvenliğim (my online safety)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Keeping personal information and passwords safe** — Online safety starts with what you do not share: do not share personal information online. Use strong passwords and keep them secret — a password shared is a password lost. And chat only with people you know: a friendly stranger online is still a stranger.
2. **Personal space online** — Personal space exists online as well as face to face. Violating someone's personal space online — pressing into their private space without their leave — is wrong in the same way. A safe learner respects others' personal space online and guards their own.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Thinks a friendly online stranger is safe to chat with → return to sub lesson 1: chat only with people you know.
