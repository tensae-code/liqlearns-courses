# Staying safe online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Online, you should not share…
a) A drawing  b) Personal information  c) A joke  d) A favourite colour game
**Answer: b) Personal information**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A strong password should be…
a) Told to friends  b) Written where strangers can see it  c) Kept secret  d) The same as your name, shared openly
**Answer: c) Kept secret**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Online, chat only with…
a) Anyone who is friendly  b) People you know  c) Strangers who ask nicely  d) Everyone
**Answer: b) People you know**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Personal space online…
a) Does not exist  b) Counts, just as face to face — respect others' and guard your own  c) Belongs to strangers  d) Can always be violated
**Answer: b) Counts, just as face to face — respect others' and guard your own**
