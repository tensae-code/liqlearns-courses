# Staying safe online — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name three rules of my online safety from Turkey's book.
**Answer:** Do not share personal information; use strong passwords and keep them secret; chat only with people you know.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does violating someone's personal space online mean, in the lesson's terms?
**Answer:** Pressing into someone's private space online without their leave — personal space counts online as it does face to face, so it must be respected.
