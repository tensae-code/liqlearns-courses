# Staying safe online

## Do it first
Before you read: imagine a stranger online asks where you live and what your password is. What would you share, and what would you never share? And if someone online keeps pressing into your private space after you say no — is that all right? Say your answers out loud. Then read on: this is Turkey's way in — its Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi book teaches Çevrim İçi Güvenliğim, my online safety, inside its Health and Safety learning area.

## Sub lesson 1 — Keeping personal information and passwords safe

Online safety starts with what you do not share: do not share personal information online. Use strong passwords and keep them secret — a password shared is a password lost. And chat only with people you know: a friendly stranger online is still a stranger.

## Sub lesson 2 — Personal space online

Personal space exists online as well as face to face. Violating someone's personal space online — pressing into their private space without their leave — is wrong in the same way. A safe learner respects others' personal space online and guards their own.

## The key ideas

- Do not share personal information online; use strong passwords and keep them secret.
- Chat only with people you know — a friendly stranger online is still a stranger.
- Personal space counts online too: respect others' and guard your own.
