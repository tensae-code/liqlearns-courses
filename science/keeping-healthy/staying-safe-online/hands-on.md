# Hands-on — Staying safe online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The share / never-share sort
Make two columns: Safe to share | Never share. Sort these into them: a drawing you made, your home address, your password, your favourite colour, where you go to school, a photo of your lunch. Check your sort against the lesson: personal information and passwords go under Never share.

## Password check
Invent — do not use a real one — a weak password and a strong password for a made-up character. Say what makes the strong one strong, and why even the strong one must be kept secret.

## Who do you know?
List the people it is all right to chat with online: only people you know. Write beside the list what you will do if a person you do not know starts chatting: do not answer, and tell a grown-up you trust.
