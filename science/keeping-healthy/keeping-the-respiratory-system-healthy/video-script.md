Today: **Keeping the respiratory system healthy** — Kuwait's way: the health of the respiratory system — the habits and clean air that keep the lungs and air passages working, and the harms that damage them.

The respiratory system — nose, windpipe and lungs — works every minute of life. It stays healthy with clean air, exercise that makes it work deeper, and protection from the dust and smoke that clog and scar it. Kuwait's Grade 8 book gives the system its own health lesson, after teaching its structure and how energy is released from food: first how it works, then how to keep it working. Smoking is the system's great enemy: its smoke damages the air passages and the lungs' tiny air sacs, and it harms even those who only breathe others' smoke. Dusty air, fumes and repeated chest infections harm it too, and crowding spreads the infections that attack it. The book's health rules follow from the harms: do not smoke and avoid smoky places; keep rooms aired; cover coughs so infections do not spread; exercise in clean air; and see a doctor for a cough that does not leave. A system that can never rest deserves the course's protection.
