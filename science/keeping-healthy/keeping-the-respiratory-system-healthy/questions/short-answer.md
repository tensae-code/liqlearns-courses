# Keeping the respiratory system healthy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name three things that harm the respiratory system.
**Answer: Smoking (including others' smoke), dusty or fume-filled air, and repeated chest infections.**

**2.** Why does the breathing system deserve special protection?
**Answer: Because it can never rest — it works every minute of life, so damage to it is felt with every breath.**

**3.** What are the book's health rules for this system?
**Answer: Do not smoke, avoid smoky places, air rooms, cover coughs, exercise in clean air, and see a doctor for a cough that does not leave.**

**4.** How does covering a cough protect the system?
**Answer: It stops the droplets that carry chest infections from reaching other people's air passages.**
