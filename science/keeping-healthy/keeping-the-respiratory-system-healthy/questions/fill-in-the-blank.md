# Keeping the respiratory system healthy — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The breathing system needs clean _____.
**Answer: air**

**2.** _____ is the great enemy of the lungs.
**Answer: Smoking**

**3.** We should _____ coughs so infections do not spread.
**Answer: cover**

**4.** A lasting cough should be shown to a _____.
**Answer: doctor**
