# Keeping the respiratory system healthy — multiple choice


**1.** The respiratory system stays healthy with…
a) Smoke  b) Clean air and exercise  c) Dust  d) Fumes
**Answer: b) Clean air and exercise**

**2.** The great enemy of the lungs named in the lesson is…
a) Exercise  b) Smoking  c) Fresh air  d) Sleep
**Answer: b) Smoking**

**3.** Breathing other people's smoke…
a) Is harmless  b) Harms the breathing system too  c) Strengthens lungs  d) Cleans the air
**Answer: b) Harms the breathing system too**

**4.** A cough that does not leave should be…
a) Ignored  b) Shown to a doctor  c) Hidden  d) Exercised away
**Answer: b) Shown to a doctor**

**5.** Covering coughs helps to…
a) Spread infections  b) Stop infections spreading  c) Weaken the lungs  d) Save paper
**Answer: b) Stop infections spreading**

**6.** Exercise for the breathing system is best done in…
a) Smoky rooms  b) Clean air  c) Dust  d) Traffic fumes
**Answer: b) Clean air**
