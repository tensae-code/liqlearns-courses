# Acute respiratory infections — coughs, colds and pneumonia — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about acute respiratory infections (IRA) defined?
**Answer: acute respiratory infections (IRA) defined: infections of the respiratory system caused by viruses (the most prevalent), bacteria, parasites and fungi, favoured by climate, overcrowding, poor diet, absent or inadequate breastfeeding, vitamin deficiency and misuse of antibiotics.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about how IRAs spread?
**Answer: how IRAs spread: from person to person through sneezes and saliva micro-droplets when coughing, in poorly ventilated places, and by contact with contaminated surfaces such as door handles, bus handrails and desks.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about the IRA symptom set?
**Answer: the IRA symptom set: nasal obstruction, cough, runny nose (rhinorrhoea), difficulty breathing and bone pain.**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Acute respiratory infections — coughs, colds and pneumonia.**
