| acute respiratory infections (IRA) defined: infections of the respiratory system caused by viruses (the most prevalent), bacteria, parasites and fungi, favoured by climate, overcrowding, poor diet, absent or inadequate breastfeeding, vitamin deficiency and misuse of antibiotics (Honduras — HN CN7) | Honduras, *HN CN7*, printed p. 15 |
| how IRAs spread: from person to person through sneezes and saliva micro-droplets when coughing, in poorly ventilated places, and by contact with contaminated surfaces such as door handles, bus handrails and desks (Honduras — HN CN7) | Honduras, *HN CN7*, printed p. 15 |
| the IRA symptom set: nasal obstruction, cough, runny nose (rhinorrhoea), difficulty breathing and bone pain (Honduras — HN CN7) | Honduras, *HN CN7*, printed p. 15 |
| the IRA diseases distinguished: pharyngotonsillitis (inflamed throat with fever and ulcers), bronchitis (inflamed bronchi with phlegm — acute, usually viral after a cold, versus chronic, caused by severe air pollution, tobacco and toxic gases), otitis (inflammation of the ear's cavities and ducts) and tonsillitis (infected tonsils: pain swallowing, fever with chills, ear pain) (Honduras — HN CN7) | Honduras, *HN CN7*, printed p. 16 |
| pneumonia: infection at lung level where the alveoli (the air sacs for gas exchange) fill with pus and liquid, making breathing painful and limiting oxygen absorption — per WHO, the leading cause of child mortality in the world (Honduras — HN CN7) | Honduras, *HN CN7*, printed p. 16 |
| the common cold: viral inflammation of the nasal mucosa and pharynx, very easily transmitted (nasal congestion, itchy throat, frequent sneezing, sometimes muscle pain); IRAs as a whole are a main cause of death in Honduras for under-5s and over-65s with weak immune systems (Honduras — HN CN7) | Honduras, *HN CN7*, printed p. 17 |
| IRA prevention as a picture set: wash hands with water and soap, cover nose and mouth when coughing and sneezing, eat nutritious food, avoid enclosed crowded places, keep warm when the temperature drops, and drink abundant liquids (Honduras — HN CN7) | Honduras, *HN CN7*, printed p. 17 |
