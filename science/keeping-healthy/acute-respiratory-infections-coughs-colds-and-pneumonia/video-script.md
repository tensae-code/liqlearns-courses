Today: **Acute respiratory infections — coughs, colds and pneumonia** — Honduras's way.

acute respiratory infections (IRA) defined: infections of the respiratory system caused by viruses (the most prevalent), bacteria, parasites and fungi, favoured by climate, overcrowding, poor diet, absent or inadequate breastfeeding, vitamin deficiency and misuse of antibiotics. how IRAs spread: from person to person through sneezes and saliva micro-droplets when coughing, in poorly ventilated places, and by contact with contaminated surfaces such as door handles, bus handrails and desks. the IRA symptom set: nasal obstruction, cough, runny nose (rhinorrhoea), difficulty breathing and bone pain.
