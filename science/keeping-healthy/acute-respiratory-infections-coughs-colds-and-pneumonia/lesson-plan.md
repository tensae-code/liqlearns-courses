# Lesson plan — Acute respiratory infections — coughs, colds and pneumonia

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Acute respiratory infections — coughs, colds and pneumonia
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (Keeping Healthy) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** acute respiratory infections (IRA) defined: infections of the respiratory system caused by viruses (the most prevalent), bacteria, parasites and fungi, favoured by climate, overcrowding, poor diet, absent or inadequate breastfeeding, vitamin deficiency and misuse of antibiotics.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Acute respiratory infections (IRA) defined** — acute respiratory infections (IRA) defined: infections of the respiratory system caused by viruses (the most prevalent), bacteria, parasites and fungi, favoured by climate, overcrowding, poor diet, absent or inadequate breastfeeding, vitamin deficiency and misuse of antibiotics.
2. **How IRAs spread** — how IRAs spread: from person to person through sneezes and saliva micro-droplets when coughing, in poorly ventilated places, and by contact with contaminated surfaces such as door handles, bus handrails and desks.
3. **The IRA symptom set** — the IRA symptom set: nasal obstruction, cough, runny nose (rhinorrhoea), difficulty breathing and bone pain.
