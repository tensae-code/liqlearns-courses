## Sub lesson 1 — Burns — cool with water

A burn is cooled under cold running water for at least 10 minutes. Cooling first — before creams, before coverings — is the book's rule, because the burning goes on in the skin after the flame is gone.

## Sub lesson 2 — Electric shock — never touch directly

For electric shock: switch off the power, or free the person with dry wood — never touch them directly while the current can still pass. The rescuer who grabs the person becomes the second casualty.

## Sub lesson 3 — Snakebite — still, low, and no cutting

For snakebite: keep the person still, keep the bitten part below the heart, and get help. Never suck the wound, never cut it, and never put ice on it — the book names the things not to do as plainly as the things to do.
