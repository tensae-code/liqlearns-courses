# First aid for burns, shock and snakebite — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Burns — cool with water.
**Answer:** A burn is cooled under cold running water for at least 10 minutes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Electric shock — never touch directly.
**Answer:** For electric shock: switch off the power, or free the person with dry wood — never touch them directly while the current can still pass.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Snakebite — still, low, and no cutting.
**Answer:** For snakebite: keep the person still, keep the bitten part below the heart, and get help.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Burns — cool with water → Electric shock — never touch directly → Snakebite — still, low, and no cutting — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
