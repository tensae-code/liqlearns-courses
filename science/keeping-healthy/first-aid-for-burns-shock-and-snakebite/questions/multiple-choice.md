# First aid for burns, shock and snakebite — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Burns — cool with water  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Burns — cool with water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Electric shock — never touch directly  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Electric shock — never touch directly**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Snakebite — still, low, and no cutting  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Snakebite — still, low, and no cutting**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: first aid for three emergencies — burns cooled under cold running water, electric shock answered without ever touching the person directly, and snakebite kept still and low — as Bangladesh's Class 4 book teaches them together. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 83  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 83**
