# Cooking and preparing food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Preparing food in the food run's order  b) Preparing food well and safely  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Food preparation
**Answer: d) Food preparation**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about preparing food as a taught skill?
a) Food preparation  b) Preparing food in the food run's order  c) Preparing food well and safely
**Answer: a) Food preparation**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about preparation joins raw food to the meal?
a) Food preparation  b) Preparing food in the food run's order  c) Preparing food well and safely
**Answer: b) Preparing food in the food run's order**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about preparation with hygiene beside it?
a) Food preparation  b) Preparing food in the food run's order  c) Preparing food well and safely
**Answer: c) Preparing food well and safely**
