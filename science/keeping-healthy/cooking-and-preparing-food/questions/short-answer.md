# Cooking and preparing food — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Food preparation" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 1, Grade 3 and Grade 6 guides all teach **food preparation** as a named unit inside the food run: preparing food is a skill with its own teaching, not a thing picked up by watching.

**2.** What does the part "Preparing food in the food run's order" teach?
**Answer:** The unit sits in the guides between sources of food and the food topics that follow it: first where food comes from, then preparing it.

**3.** What does the part "Preparing food well and safely" teach?
**Answer:** Preparation done well keeps the food's goodness and keeps the eater safe: the unit stands inside the guides' health topic, next to food hygiene, and is taught with that company.
