## Sub lesson 2 — Preparing food in the food run's order

The unit sits in the guides between sources of food and the food topics that follow it: first where food comes from, then preparing it. That order is the guides' own way — preparation joins the raw food to the meal.

## Sub lesson 3 — Preparing food well and safely

Preparation done well keeps the food's goodness and keeps the eater safe: the unit stands inside the guides' health topic, next to food hygiene, and is taught with that company.
