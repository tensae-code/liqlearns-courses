Today: **Keeping our senses safe** — Seychelles' way, from its Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 12).

Seychelles' Unit 12 Topic "Looking after your eyes" follows its topics on using the eyes, ears, skin and brain: the senses are how the body takes in the world, and they can be damaged by careless use. Eyes are protected from glare, strain and injury, and problems with sight are taken seriously early, while they can still be helped.

The care is practical and daily: good light for reading, rest for tired eyes, never looking straight at the sun or a bright flame, keeping sharp and dirty things away from the eyes, and seeking help when sight changes. The same care extends to the other senses the unit studied — the senses kept safe keep learning and warning the body for life.
