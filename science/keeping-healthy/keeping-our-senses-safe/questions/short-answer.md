# Keeping our senses safe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: looking after your eyes.
**Answer:** Seychelles' Unit 12 Topic "Looking after your eyes" follows its topics on using the eyes, ears, skin and brain: the senses are how the body takes in the world, and they can be damaged by careless use.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Name three daily ways of looking after your eyes from this lesson.
**Answer:** Any three of: good light for reading, resting tired eyes, never looking straight at the sun or a bright flame, keeping sharp and dirty things away from the eyes, seeking help early when sight changes.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Looking after your eyes → Care for the senses you learn through — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
