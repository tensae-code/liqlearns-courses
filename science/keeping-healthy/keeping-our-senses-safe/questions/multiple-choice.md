# Keeping our senses safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Reading should be done in…
a) good light  b) near darkness  c) sun glare  d) candle smoke
**Answer: a) good light**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) You must never look straight at…
a) the sun or a bright flame  b) a book  c) a blackboard  d) a picture
**Answer: a) the sun or a bright flame**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) When sight changes, you should…
a) seek help early  b) wait a year  c) rub the eyes hard  d) cover both eyes all day
**Answer: a) seek help early**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Sharp and dirty things should be kept…
a) away from the eyes  b) near the face  c) in reading books  d) in drinking water
**Answer: a) away from the eyes**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Tired eyes need…
a) rest  b) more glare  c) longer strain  d) smoke
**Answer: a) rest**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Sense care is…
a) a daily habit, not only for emergencies  b) only for doctors  c) only at school  d) unnecessary
**Answer: a) a daily habit, not only for emergencies**
