# Signs, lights and crossing rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does the red traffic light command?**
a) go  b) stop and wait  c) turn around
**Answer: b) stop and wait**

**2. The pedestrian light has which colours?**
a) red, yellow and green  b) red and green  c) green only
**Answer: b) red and green**

**3. The yellow traffic light means —**
a) dash across  b) get ready — do not step out carelessly  c) the light is broken
**Answer: b) get ready — do not step out carelessly**
