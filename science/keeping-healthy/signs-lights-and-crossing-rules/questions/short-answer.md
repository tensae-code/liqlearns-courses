# Signs, lights and crossing rules — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Name two rules from the book's rules table.**
**Answer: cross at the zebra; walk on the left line of the road; never cross from behind a parked vehicle; look left and right and cross; never cross holding hands in a chain (any two)**

**2. What must a walker do even when the pedestrian light is green?**
**Answer: look left and right before crossing**
