# Lesson plan — Signs, lights and crossing rules

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Signs, lights and crossing rules
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — safety at home and on the road (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the traffic signs and lights and what each colour commands, with the crossing rules gathered in one table
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Traffic accidents and the rules of the road — the learner knows where and how to cross; this lesson adds the road's own signals.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Read the printed traffic signs and say what each one means.
2. Say the traffic light: red, yellow, green — and the pedestrian light: red and green — and what each colour commands.
3. Use the rules table: cross at the zebra, walk on the left line of the road, never cross from behind a parked vehicle, look left and right and cross quickly (calmly, without dashing — the Grade 3 book's word), never cross holding hands in a chain.

## What you need
Three coloured cards — red, yellow, green — for the light game; a drawn zebra on the ground.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the pedestrian light with the traffic light → they are two lights for two users: cars obey one, people on foot obey the other; both must agree before you step out.
- Thinks green means 'run' → green means cross — after looking left and right; the Grade 3 book forbids dashing even on the zebra.
- Holds hands in a chain to cross → the book's table forbids it: a chain straggles; one adult hand for one child, at the edge, together.
