# Video script — Signs, lights and crossing rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Red — stop. Wait at the edge.
Yellow — get ready. Green — look, then cross.
Zebra under your feet. Left side as you walk.
Never from behind a parked car. Never in a chain.
Read the road, and the road keeps you safe.
