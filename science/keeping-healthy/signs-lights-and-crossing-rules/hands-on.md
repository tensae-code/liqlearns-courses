# Hands-on — Signs, lights and crossing rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Light game
One child is the light with three colour cards; the rest are walkers at a chalk zebra. Wrong move on a colour — sit out one round and say the rule you broke.

## Signs walk
Walk your street with an adult and photograph or draw every traffic sign. Under each, write its command in your own words.

## Rules poster
Build the five-rule poster with drawings. Teach it to a younger class or a younger sibling — teaching the table is the deepest way to learn it.
