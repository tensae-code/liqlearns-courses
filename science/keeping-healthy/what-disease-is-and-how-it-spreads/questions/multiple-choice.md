# What disease is, and how it spreads — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the roots of sickness, as the book teaches?**
a) germs and dirt  b) singing and dancing  c) books and pencils
**Answer: a) germs and dirt**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these carries sickness to food?**
a) a fly  b) a book  c) a stone
**Answer: a) a fly** — the fly walks on dirt, then on food.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) How do we stop the fly's sickness?**
a) leave food open  b) cover the food  c) eat quickly
**Answer: b) cover the food**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Which sickness of the eyes can cause blindness if it is not stopped?**
a) trachoma  b) a headache  c) hiccups
**Answer: a) trachoma**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Which of these must never be shared?**
a) a ball  b) a toothbrush  c) a chair
**Answer: b) a toothbrush**
