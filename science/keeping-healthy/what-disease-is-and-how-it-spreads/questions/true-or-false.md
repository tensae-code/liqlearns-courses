# What disease is, and how it spreads — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Germs are too small for our eyes to see.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Mosquitoes can carry sickness from a sick person to a well one.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Washing the face morning and evening helps stop trachoma.**
**Answer: True**
