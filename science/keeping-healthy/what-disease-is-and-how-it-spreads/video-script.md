# Video script — What disease is, and how it spreads

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A fly walks on dirt. Then it walks on food.
Germs ride. Mosquitoes ride. Dirty hands carry.
Cover the food. Wash the hands. Wash the face.
Your toothbrush is yours. Your razor is yours. Never share.
Clean stops sickness before it starts.
