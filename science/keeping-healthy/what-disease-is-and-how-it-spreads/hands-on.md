# Hands-on — What disease is, and how it spreads

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fly watch
At home or at school, watch where flies land for five minutes. Cover every food they could reach. Report what you covered.

## The latrine check
Look at the latrine you use: is it clean? Hands washed after? The books make the latrine part of stopping sickness — check it like a scientist.

## Never-share sort
Lay out ten household things. Sort them into SHARE and NEVER SHARE piles, and defend each never-share choice in the lesson's words.
