# Water safety and harbour safety equipment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** A pier or dock is a busy place right at the water's edge — a fall there is a fall into the sea.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Safety equipment is kept on piers and docks to help when someone is in the water or in danger there.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why learn the equipment before an emergency?
**Answer:** So that in the emergency itself no time is lost looking or wondering — the safe child already knows what is there and where it stands.
