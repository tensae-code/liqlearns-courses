# Water safety and harbour safety equipment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) A pier or dock is a busy place right at the water's edge — a fall there is a fall into the sea.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) A pier or dock is a busy place right at the water's edge — a fall there is a fall into the sea.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) Safety equipment is kept on piers and docks to help when someone is in the water or in danger there.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Safety equipment is kept on piers and docks to help when someone is in the water or in danger there.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Iceland's way: children get to know the harbour safety equipment itself, before any emergency.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Iceland's way: children get to know the harbour safety equipment itself, before any emergency.**
