Today: **Water safety and harbour safety equipment** — Iceland's harbour-side way, from its Grade 3 safety chapter.
A pier stands right at the water's edge. A fall there is a fall into the sea.
So safety equipment is kept on piers and docks — and the safe child knows it before any emergency.
