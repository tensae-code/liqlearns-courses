# Hands-on — Water safety and harbour safety equipment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spot the equipment
On a visit to a pier or dock with an adult — or from a picture of one — find every piece of safety equipment you can. Point to each one and say what it is for.

## Teach the pier
Draw a pier. Mark where safety equipment stands on your drawing, and teach a partner the harbour rule: know it before you need it.
