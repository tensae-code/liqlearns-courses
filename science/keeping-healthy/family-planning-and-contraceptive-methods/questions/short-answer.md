# Family planning and contraceptive methods — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about family planning defined as the mechanisms a couple uses?
**Answer: family planning defined as the mechanisms a couple uses to regulate how many children to have and the spacing between births.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about natural contraceptive methods?
**Answer: natural contraceptive methods: the rhythm/calendar method (needs a regular cycle), basal temperature (body temperature rises near the period; the fertile days are read from the temperature pattern) and the Billings mucus method (fertile mucus is wet and elastic)**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about barrier methods?
**Answer: barrier methods: the male condom, the female condom and the diaphragm placed over the cervix.**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Family planning and contraceptive methods.**
