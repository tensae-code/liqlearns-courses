# Hands-on — Family planning and contraceptive methods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Family planning defined as the mechanisms a couple uses. family planning defined as the mechanisms a couple uses to regulate how many children to have and the spacing between births. Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
