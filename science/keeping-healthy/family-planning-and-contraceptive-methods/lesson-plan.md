# Lesson plan — Family planning and contraceptive methods

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Family planning and contraceptive methods
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (Keeping Healthy) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** family planning defined as the mechanisms a couple uses to regulate how many children to have and the spacing between births.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Family planning defined as the mechanisms a couple uses** — family planning defined as the mechanisms a couple uses to regulate how many children to have and the spacing between births.
2. **Natural contraceptive methods** — natural contraceptive methods: the rhythm/calendar method (needs a regular cycle), basal temperature (body temperature rises near the period; the fertile days are read from the temperature pattern) and the Billings mucus method (fertile mucus is wet and elastic)
3. **Barrier methods** — barrier methods: the male condom, the female condom and the diaphragm placed over the cervix.

3. Explain sub lesson 3 in their own words: Barrier methods.
