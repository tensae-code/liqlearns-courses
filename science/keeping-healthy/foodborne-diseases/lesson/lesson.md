## Sub lesson 2 — How food carries disease

Food handled, stored or prepared badly can carry disease to the eater. The unit stands between the reproductive-system unit and environmental hygiene in the guide — the road from food to sickness is its teaching.

## Sub lesson 3 — Breaking the road

A road that is understood can be broken: the hygiene the same book teaches is the guard. Knowing the road and guarding it are the unit's two halves.

## The key ideas

- **Foodborne diseases** — diseases that travel in food.
- **How food carries disease** — the road from food to sickness.
- **Breaking the road** — understand the road, then guard it.
