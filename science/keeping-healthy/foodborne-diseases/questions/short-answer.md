# Foodborne diseases — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Foodborne diseases" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 6 guide teaches **foodborne diseases** as a named unit inside its health topic: some diseases travel in food, and the unit names that road.

**2.** What does the part "How food carries disease" teach?
**Answer:** Food handled, stored or prepared badly can carry disease to the eater.

**3.** What does the part "Breaking the road" teach?
**Answer:** A road that is understood can be broken: the hygiene the same book teaches is the guard.
