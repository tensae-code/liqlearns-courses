# Foodborne diseases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) How food carries disease  b) Breaking the road  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Foodborne diseases
**Answer: d) Foodborne diseases**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about diseases that travel in food?
a) Foodborne diseases  b) How food carries disease  c) Breaking the road
**Answer: a) Foodborne diseases**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about the road from food to sickness?
a) Foodborne diseases  b) How food carries disease  c) Breaking the road
**Answer: b) How food carries disease**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about understand the road, then guard it?
a) Foodborne diseases  b) How food carries disease  c) Breaking the road
**Answer: c) Breaking the road**
