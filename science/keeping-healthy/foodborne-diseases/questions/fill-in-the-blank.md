# Foodborne diseases — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Foodborne diseases.
**Answer: diseases that travel in food**

**2.** This part of the lesson is about _____: How food carries disease.
**Answer: the road from food to sickness**

**3.** This part of the lesson is about _____: Breaking the road.
**Answer: understand the road, then guard it**
