## Sub lesson 2 — Symptoms come with disease

Symptoms come with disease: a body temperature rising above 37 °C (the normal human temperature), sore throat, redness of skin and eyes, vomiting or diarrhoea, coughing, headache and muscle and joint pain, and feeling tired and needing rest; and when illness strikes: visit the doctor and follow his instructions, eat healthy food, commit to rest and not leave home except from necessity, and attend to personal cleanliness and wash the hands regularly
