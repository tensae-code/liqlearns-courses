| how do I know I am ill, and what must I do? Symptoms come with disease: a body temperature rising above 37 °C (the normal human temperature), sore throat, redness of skin and eyes, vomiting or diarrhoea, coughing, headache and muscle and joint pain, and feeling tired and needing rest; and when illness strikes: visit the doctor and follow his instructions, eat healthy food, commit to rest and not leave home except from necessity, and attend to personal cleanliness and wash the hands regularly (Saudi Arabia, Grade 4, Part 1) | Saudi Arabia, *Science, Grade 4, Part 1*, printed p. 146 |
