# Hands-on — Knowing you are ill — symptoms and what to do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: how do I know I am ill, and what must I do? Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
