# Suffocation and first aid — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Suffocation — when breathing is blocked" teach?
**Answer:** **Suffocation** is the state the unit closes with: breathing blocked or the air cut off, so the body cannot take in the air it needs.

**2.** What does the part "What to do — first aid for suffocation" teach?
**Answer:** And it is taught with its answer: **what to do** — the first aid for suffocation.
