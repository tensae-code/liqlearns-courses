# Suffocation and first aid

## Do it first
Before you read: breathing is so steady we forget it — until it is blocked. What is happening to a person who is suffocating, and what must the people near them do, fast? Say your answers. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — after the breathing system and its diseases, its Primary 6 book closes with suffocation and its first aid.

## Sub lesson 1 — Suffocation — when breathing is blocked

**Suffocation** is the state the unit closes with: breathing blocked or the air cut off, so the body cannot take in the air it needs. It is taught after the respiratory system itself, so the learner knows exactly which system is in trouble.
