# Keeping our home clean

## Do it first

Look at your own home in your mind: the floor, the cooking place, the toilet. Which parts are cleaned, with what, and how often? Start there.

## Sub lesson 1 — Keeping the toilet clean (teacher guide — evidence)

The guide's competency is plain: keep the toilet clean, using household cleaning materials — the broom, water, soap and brush of the home. A toilet kept clean is part of the home's health, not a separate chore.

## Sub lesson 2 — A clean home protects the family (teacher guide — evidence)

A family living in a clean home and clean surroundings protects itself from many diseases. The guide builds this part on the personal-hygiene teaching — washing the body and clothes — and extends the same care outward: from the body, to the rooms, to the toilet, to the compound.

## Say it to your companion

Explain, in your own words, how keeping the home and toilet clean protects the family, and name the household cleaning materials you would use.

## Do It Yourself

1. Clean one part of your home today with household materials and say what you used.
2. Make a home cleaning rota for the week: who cleans what, and when.
3. Check your home's toilet: is it clean? What one thing would make it cleaner?
