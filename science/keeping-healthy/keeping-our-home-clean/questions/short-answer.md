# Keeping our home clean — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the household cleaning materials in the lesson.**
**Answer:** Broom, water, soap and brush.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the guide teach keeping the home clean?**
**Answer:** Because a family living in a clean home and surroundings protects itself from many diseases.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) How does this lesson grow out of personal hygiene?**
**Answer:** The guide recalls keeping the body and clothes clean and extends the same care to the home and toilet.
