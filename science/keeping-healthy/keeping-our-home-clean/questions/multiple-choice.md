# Keeping our home clean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The guide's cleaning competency names —**
a) the market  b) the toilet, kept clean with household cleaning materials  c) the river
**Answer: b) the toilet, kept clean with household cleaning materials** — The toilet, with household cleaning materials.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A family in a clean home and surroundings protects itself from —**
a) many diseases  b) rain  c) homework
**Answer: a) many diseases** — Many diseases.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which set is household cleaning materials?**
a) broom, water, soap, brush  b) stones and sticks  c) books and pencils
**Answer: a) broom, water, soap, brush** — Broom, water, soap and brush.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) This home lesson extends which earlier teaching outward?**
a) map reading  b) personal hygiene — keeping the body and clothes clean  c) counting
**Answer: b) personal hygiene — keeping the body and clothes clean** — Personal hygiene, extended to the home.
