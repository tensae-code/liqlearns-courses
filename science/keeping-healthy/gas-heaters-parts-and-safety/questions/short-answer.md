# Gas heaters — parts and safety — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name the gas stove's three parts and each part's job.
**Answer:** Burners — where the gas burns and the flame is held; ignition system — lights the gas; control knobs — let the gas through and govern it to each burner.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is an appliance taken apart in the Grade 4 lesson?
**Answer:** So each part is seen, named and understood in its place — the appliance stops being a sealed mystery, and knowing the parts is the ground of handling gas safely.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) In what order do the parts work when a burner is lit?
**Answer:** Knob first — it lets the gas through; ignition next — it lights the gas; burner last — it holds the flame.
