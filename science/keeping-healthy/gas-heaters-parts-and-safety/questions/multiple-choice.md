# Gas heaters — parts and safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The gas stove part where the gas burns is the…
a) Control knob  b) Burner  c) Ignition system  d) Handle
**Answer: b) Burner**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The control knobs…
a) Light the gas  b) Let the gas through and govern it to each burner  c) Hold the flame  d) Carry the stove
**Answer: b) Let the gas through and govern it to each burner**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The ignition system…
a) Lights the gas  b) Stores the gas  c) Cools the flame  d) Weighs the stove
**Answer: a) Lights the gas**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Mauritania's Grade 4 lesson on gas heating appliances is…
a) Painting them  b) Dismantling them and naming their parts  c) Selling them  d) Lighting them in class
**Answer: b) Dismantling them and naming their parts**
