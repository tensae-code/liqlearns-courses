# Hands-on — Gas heaters — parts and safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the parts at home
With an adult, and with the stove cold and off, point to each part of the home gas stove or heater in turn — burner, ignition, control knob — and say its job aloud. Nothing is lit and nothing is taken apart.

## Part cards
On cards, write the three stove parts and three job descriptions, shuffled. Match each part to its job, then lay the matched pairs in the order they work when a burner is lit.
