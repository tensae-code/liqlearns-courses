Today: **Gas heaters — parts and safety** — Mauritania's way: know the parts, and you can command the appliance.

A gas stove has named parts: the burners, where the gas burns; the ignition system, which lights it; and the control knobs, which let the gas through and govern it. Knob, ignition, burner — that is the order of their work.

Mauritania's Grade 4 book even takes gas heating appliances apart, part by part, so nothing about them stays a mystery. An appliance you can name is an appliance you can use safely.
