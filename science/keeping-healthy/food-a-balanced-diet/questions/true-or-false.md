# Food — a balanced diet — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) One food group alone can carry everything the body needs.**
**Answer: False** — No single group carries everything; the mix is the lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) The meal-plan table is built by the pupil and reported on later.**
**Answer: True** — Built, carried out, and reported back.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Bread and tea are poisons.**
**Answer: False** — They are real food — the fault is the missing groups.
