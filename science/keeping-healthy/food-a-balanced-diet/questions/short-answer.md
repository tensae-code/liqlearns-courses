# Food — a balanced diet — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Say what a balanced diet is.**
**Answer:** Eating all the nutrient groups in the right mix.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Explain the bread-and-tea case in two sentences.**
**Answer:** A child fed only bread and tea gets a filled stomach but misses the groups that build, protect and strengthen, so the diet fails the body.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Name the groups your own meal-plan table must cover.**
**Answer:** All the nutrient groups — carbohydrates, fats and oils, protein, minerals, vitamins and water — in the right mix.
