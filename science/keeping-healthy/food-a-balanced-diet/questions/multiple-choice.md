# Food — a balanced diet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A balanced diet is —**
a) one big meal  b) eating all nutrient groups in the right mix  c) bread at every meal
**Answer: b) eating all nutrient groups in the right mix** — All groups, in the right mix.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The case-study child was fed only —**
a) bread and tea  b) rice and milk  c) injera and wot
**Answer: a) bread and tea** — Bread and tea only.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What is wrong with bread and tea alone? **
a) they are poison  b) they leave out the groups that build, protect and strengthen  c) nothing
**Answer: b) they leave out the groups that build, protect and strengthen** — The mix is missing, so the body fails.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) The pupil builds and later reports on —**
a) a market list  b) a family meal-plan table  c) a poem
**Answer: b) a family meal-plan table** — The family meal-plan table.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) A diet can fill the stomach and still —**
a) fail the body  b) be balanced  c) cost nothing
**Answer: a) fail the body** — Filling is not balancing.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Chapter 2 review: pupils report back on —**
a) the family meal plan set in §2.3  b) a poem  c) a market trip
**Answer: a) the family meal plan set in §2.3** — The meal-plan report-back is part of the review.
