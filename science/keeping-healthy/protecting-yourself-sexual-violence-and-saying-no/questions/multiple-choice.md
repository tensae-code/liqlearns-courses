# Protecting yourself: sexual violence and saying no — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Sexual violence is defined as imposing one's desire —**
a) with permission  b) by force, without consent  c) only by strangers
**Answer: b) by force, without consent**

**2. Which is one of the three kinds of gender-based violence at school?**
a) psychological  b) musical  c) agricultural
**Answer: a) psychological**

**3. Worknesh's answer to pressure was —**
a) silence  b) a firm, polite no  c) running away from school for good
**Answer: b) a firm, polite no**

**4. Which is one of the five doors to report to?**
a) the kebele administration  b) a stranger on the road  c) nobody — keep it secret
**Answer: a) the kebele administration**

**5. If an attempted rape happens, the book's advice includes —**
a) submit to threats  b) scream continuously and fight to save yourself  c) stay silent
**Answer: b) scream continuously and fight to save yourself**

**6. An effect of sexual violence the book lists is —**
a) better grades  b) anxiety and self-isolation  c) stronger ambition
**Answer: b) anxiety and self-isolation**
