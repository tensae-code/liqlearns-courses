# Hands-on — Protecting yourself: sexual violence and saying no

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Who can I tell? — the doors poster
Make the five-doors poster for the classroom wall: FAMILY — TEACHER — POLICE — WOMEN-AND-CHILDREN'S OFFICE — KEBELE. A poster a frightened pupil can read in one glance.

## The refusal, performed
Perform Worknesh's dialogue as a class read-aloud (summary lines, not the book's text): pressure, refusal, respect kept. Hearing a spoken no makes saying one possible.
