# Video script — Protecting yourself: sexual violence and saying no

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Violence is **force without consent**. It is never the victim's fault.
Three kinds: **sexual, physical, psychological**.
**Learn to say no** — firm and polite, like **Worknesh**.
Tell: **family, teacher, police, the affairs office, the kebele**.
If an attack is attempted: **do not submit, do not panic — scream, and fight to save yourself**.
