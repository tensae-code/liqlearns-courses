## Sub lesson 1 — Diabetes: too little insulin from the pancreas leaves glucose piling up in the blood

Diabetes: too little insulin from the pancreas leaves glucose piling up in the blood; the signs — passing much urine, great thirst and hunger, dizziness, blurring eyes, weight loss, tingling limbs and wounds that heal slowly — and escaping it: a balanced diet, losing weight, less stress, no smoking or alcohol, green vegetables and fruit, regular exercise and a morning walk that raises a sweat.

## Sub lesson 2 — Blood glucose measured three ways

Blood glucose measured three ways (guide evidence; lesson first proposed in the Grade 10 student book above): fasting after 8–12 hours with only water, two hours after a meal, and random; normal fasting 72–99 mg/dL and under 140 mg/dL after food, as printed; insulin shortage, obesity and an inactive life named as causes.
