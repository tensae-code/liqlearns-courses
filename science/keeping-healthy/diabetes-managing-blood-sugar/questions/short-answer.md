# Diabetes — managing blood sugar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Diabetes: too little insulin from the pancreas leaves glucose piling up in the blood; the signs — passing much urine, great thirst and hunger, dizziness, blurring eyes, weight loss, tingling limbs and wounds that heal slowly — and escaping it: a balanced diet, losing weight, less stress, no smoking or alcohol, green vegetables and fruit, regular exercise and a morning walk that raises a sweat.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed p. 184.**
