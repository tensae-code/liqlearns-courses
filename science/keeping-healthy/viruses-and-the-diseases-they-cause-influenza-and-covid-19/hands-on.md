# Hands-on — Viruses and the diseases they cause — influenza and COVID-19

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: viruses: they behave sometimes like living things and sometimes like non-living things, attack the living body and cause it disease — the novel coronavirus (COVID-19), colds and measles among them; influenza is a viral infection of the lungs and air passages whose symptoms resemble a cold's but are much stronger; COVID-19 is caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
