# Ripening fruit with chemicals and its dangers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Ethylene is the natural ripening agent in fruit and vegetables, but traders use artificial ethylene and calcium carbide to meet market demand; about 1 ppm of ethylene is enough to ripen, while in practice up to about 1,000 ppm is used, which is very harmful to health, as printed.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Shikshak Nirdeshika Vigyan Grade 10 (guide)*, printed p. 547.**
