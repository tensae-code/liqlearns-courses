# Hands-on — Public health care in our country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Care, mapped at home
List the kinds of care a sick person near you can receive, from the home outward: family care, a health worker, a clinic or hospital. Say which of these the lesson calls the country's own care, and which are the home's.

## The unit's order, retold
Retell the Grade 6 unit's order in one breath — body systems, hygiene, then public care — and say why the book puts public health care last.
