# Public health care in our country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cuba's Grade 6 book teaches that in our country…
a) Health is each person's private problem only  b) The people's health is cared for  c) Only the rich are cared for  d) Health is not taught
**Answer: b) The people's health is cared for**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) In this lesson, health care is…
a) A shared public task, for the people as a whole  b) Left to chance  c) Only for one family  d) A secret
**Answer: a) A shared public task, for the people as a whole**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Where does this teaching stand in the Grade 6 book?
a) At the very start, before the cell  b) Inside the plants chapter  c) At the end of the human-body unit, after the body systems and hygiene  d) It is not in the book
**Answer: c) At the end of the human-body unit, after the body systems and hygiene**
