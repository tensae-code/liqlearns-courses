# Public health care in our country — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Say the lesson's one teaching in your own words.
**Answer:** In Cuba, the people's health is cared for — health care is organised as a shared public task for the whole people.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach this last, after the body and hygiene?
**Answer:** Because the order is the meaning: first how the body works and how to keep it well, then who cares for the people's health beyond the home — the country itself.
