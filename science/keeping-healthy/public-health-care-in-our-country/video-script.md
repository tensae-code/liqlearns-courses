# Video script — Public health care in our country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Public health care in our country** — Cuba's way.

The book's closing claim is simple and whole: in our country, the people's health is cared for.

Cuba's book places this teaching last in the unit — after the skeleton, digestion, breathing, circulation, reproduction and adolescence, and after the hygiene lessons that close the body's own care.

Now you. What does Cuba's Grade 6 book teach, at its unit's end, about health in the country? Say it back — in your own words.
