# Moving our bodies and sleep on time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does sport do for people?**
a) makes them sleepy all day  b) makes them active, strong and healthy  c) makes them hungry only
**Answer: b) makes them active, strong and healthy**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a sport in the book's list?**
a) rope-skipping  b) watching television  c) sleeping
**Answer: a) rope-skipping**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What does staying up late with television and play spoil?**
a) nothing  b) learning and work  c) only dreams
**Answer: b) learning and work**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Gena is —**
a) a television show  b) a traditional game in the book's sports list  c) a kind of food
**Answer: b) a traditional game in the book's sports list**
