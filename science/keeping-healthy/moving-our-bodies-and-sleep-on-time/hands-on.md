# Hands-on — Moving our bodies and sleep on time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rope rhythm
Skip with a partner turning or counting. Keep the rhythm steady — that steadiness is the exercise.

## The bedtime clock
Draw a clock face showing your bedtime and your rising time. Put it by the bed for a week and keep both times.

## Games list
List every moving game played in your neighbourhood. Mark the ones that are in the book's list, and teach a friend one that is not.
