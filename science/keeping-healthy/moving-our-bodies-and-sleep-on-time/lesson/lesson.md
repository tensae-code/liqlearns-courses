# Moving our bodies and sleep on time

## Do it first
Run on the spot. Skip. Jump. Feel your heart? Feel your breath? Your body was made to move — and made to rest. This lesson teaches both halves.

## Sub lesson 1 — Exercise and sport

The Grade 2 book's sports are the ones children already know: **walking, running, football, rope-skipping, wrestling, and gena** — the traditional game. And the book states what sport does in one line: sport makes people **active, strong and healthy**. A body that moves every day works better — the heart stronger, the legs stronger, the mind readier to learn.

## Sub lesson 2 — Enough sleep, on time

The other half of a strong body is rest. The Grade 2 book's rule is short: **go to bed on time and rise on time**. Staying up late with television and play spoils the next day — learning suffers and work suffers. Sleep is not time wasted; it is the body rebuilding itself for tomorrow.

[Picture 12113 — a girl asleep in bed with a clock showing her bedtime]

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion three sports from the book, what sport does for the body, and the sleep rule. Your companion says their bedtime tonight — is it on time?

## Do It Yourself
1. Skip rope in rhythm for the count of fifty, the way the picture shows, resting when you must.
2. Play one game from the book's list this week — football, wrestling with a friend safely, or gena — and say how your body felt after.
3. Set tonight's bedtime by the clock and keep it. Note tomorrow how learning felt.
