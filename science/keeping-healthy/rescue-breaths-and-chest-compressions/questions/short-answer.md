# Rescue breaths and chest compressions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Rescuing a drowning person — without entering the water.
**Answer:** Reach from the bank: a pole, a rope or a flotation aid held or thrown out to the drowning person.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Opening the airway and rescue breaths.
**Answer:** If the person is not breathing: open the airway, then give rescue breaths — air from your own lungs into theirs, in place of the breathing that has stopped..

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: 30 chest compressions.
**Answer:** Then press the chest: 30 chest compressions, and back to rescue breaths, until breathing returns.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Rescuing a drowning person — without entering the water → Opening the airway and rescue breaths → 30 chest compressions — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
