# Rescue breaths and chest compressions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Rescuing a drowning person — without entering the water  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Rescuing a drowning person — without entering the water**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Opening the airway and rescue breaths  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Opening the airway and rescue breaths**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) 30 chest compressions  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) 30 chest compressions**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: rescuing a drowning person without entering the water, then opening the airway, rescue breaths and 30 chest compressions until breathing returns — as Bangladesh's Class 4 book teaches it. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 84  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 84**
