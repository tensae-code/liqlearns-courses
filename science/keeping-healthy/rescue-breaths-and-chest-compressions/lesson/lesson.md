## Sub lesson 1 — Rescuing a drowning person — without entering the water

Reach from the bank: a pole, a rope or a flotation aid held or thrown out to the drowning person. The rescuer does not enter the water — a panicking person can pull two people under.

## Sub lesson 2 — Opening the airway and rescue breaths

If the person is not breathing: open the airway, then give rescue breaths — air from your own lungs into theirs, in place of the breathing that has stopped.

## Sub lesson 3 — 30 chest compressions

Then press the chest: 30 chest compressions, and back to rescue breaths, until breathing returns. The count of 30 is Bangladesh's Class 4 book's own count, taught as the rhythm to keep until the person breathes.
