# Kinds of infectious disease — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Grouped by their road  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Grouped by their road**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The four groups  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The four groups**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The exception the book names  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The exception the book names**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: infectious diseases grouped by their road of spread — airborne, waterborne, by contact, and carried by animals and insects — as Bangladesh's Class 5 book groups them. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 5 — *Elementary Science Class 5*, printed p. 48  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 5 — *Elementary Science Class 5*, printed p. 48**
