# Kinds of infectious disease — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Grouped by their road.
**Answer:** Infectious diseases can be grouped by the road they travel.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The four groups.
**Answer:** Airborne: swine flu, measles, chicken pox, tuberculosis, influenza.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The exception the book names.
**Answer:** The book is careful about AIDS: it is caused by the HIV virus, but it does not spread by touching or sharing things.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Grouped by their road → The four groups → The exception the book names — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
