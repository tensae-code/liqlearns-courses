Today: **Kinds of infectious disease** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 5 — *Elementary Science Class 5*, printed p. 48).

Infectious diseases can be grouped by the road they travel. Bangladesh's Class 5 book sorts them four ways: through the air, through water, by contact, and carried by animals and insects.
Airborne: swine flu, measles, chicken pox, tuberculosis, influenza. Waterborne: diarrhoea, cholera, dysentery, typhoid. By contact: flu, Ebola, measles. Carried by animals and insects: rabies by dog bite, and malaria and dengue by mosquito.
The book is careful about AIDS: it is caused by the HIV virus, but it does not spread by touching or sharing things. Knowing a disease's road also means knowing the roads it does not take — prevention follows the right roads.
