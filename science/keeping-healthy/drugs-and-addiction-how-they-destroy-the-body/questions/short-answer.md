# Drugs and addiction — how they destroy the body — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about drugs defined (natural or laboratory-made substances entering by mouth?
**Answer: drugs defined (natural or laboratory-made substances entering by mouth, inhalation, vein or skin, acting on the central nervous system) and classified legal vs illegal: legal — alcohol (ethanol from fermentation), tobacco (Nicotiana tabacum), glue (toluene), psychopharmaceuticals, coffee (caffeine); illegal — marijuana (Cannabis sativa), opium (Papaver somniferum, source of morphine and heroin), cocaine (Erythroxylon coca), ecstasy and crack.**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about the damage of each drug tabulated organ by organ (marijuana?
**Answer: the damage of each drug tabulated organ by organ (marijuana: hallucinations, loss of time sense, damaged comprehension and memory; cocaine: raised blood pressure, damaged nose interior, lost appetite; opium: drowsiness then depression; glue: breathing difficulty, weakness, nausea)**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about the addiction mechanism?
**Answer: the addiction mechanism: psychoactive substances block or boost the brain's neurotransmitters, interfering with normal brain activity; dependence is biological and/or psychological, and the damage table is split physical (dilated pupils, lost motor coordination, deteriorated thought, death by overdose), psychological (dependence, addiction, hallucinations) and social (family problems, neglected studies, trouble with the law, rejection)**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Drugs and addiction — how they destroy the body.**
