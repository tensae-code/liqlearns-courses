# Hands-on — Drugs and addiction — how they destroy the body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Drugs defined (natural or laboratory-made substances entering by mouth. drugs defined (natural or laboratory-made substances entering by mouth, inhalation, vein or skin, acting on the central nervous system) and classified legal vs illegal: legal — alcohol (ethanol from fermentation), tobacco (Nicotiana tabacum), glue (toluene), psychopharmaceuticals, coffee (caffeine); illegal — marijuana (Cannabis sativa), opium (Papaver somniferum, source of morphine and heroin), cocaine (Erythroxylon coca), ecstasy and crack. Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
