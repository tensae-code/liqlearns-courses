# Lesson plan — Drugs and addiction — how they destroy the body

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Drugs and addiction — how they destroy the body
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (Keeping Healthy) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** drugs defined (natural or laboratory-made substances entering by mouth, inhalation, vein or skin, acting on the central nervous system) and classified legal vs illegal: legal — alcohol (ethanol from fermentation), tobacco (Nicotiana tabacum), glue (toluene), psychopharmaceuticals, coffee (caffeine); illegal — marijuana (Cannabis sativa), opium (Papaver somniferum, source of morphine and heroin), cocaine (Erythroxylon coca), ecstasy and crack.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Drugs defined (natural or laboratory-made substances entering by mouth** — drugs defined (natural or laboratory-made substances entering by mouth, inhalation, vein or skin, acting on the central nervous system) and classified legal vs illegal: legal — alcohol (ethanol from fermentation), tobacco (Nicotiana tabacum), glue (toluene), psychopharmaceuticals, coffee (caffeine); illegal — marijuana (Cannabis sativa), opium (Papaver somniferum, source of morphine and heroin), cocaine (Erythroxylon coca), ecstasy and crack.
2. **The damage of each drug tabulated organ by organ (marijuana** — the damage of each drug tabulated organ by organ (marijuana: hallucinations, loss of time sense, damaged comprehension and memory; cocaine: raised blood pressure, damaged nose interior, lost appetite; opium: drowsiness then depression; glue: breathing difficulty, weakness, nausea)
3. **The addiction mechanism** — the addiction mechanism: psychoactive substances block or boost the brain's neurotransmitters, interfering with normal brain activity; dependence is biological and/or psychological, and the damage table is split physical (dilated pupils, lost motor coordination, deteriorated thought, death by overdose), psychological (dependence, addiction, hallucinations) and social (family problems, neglected studies, trouble with the law, rejection)
