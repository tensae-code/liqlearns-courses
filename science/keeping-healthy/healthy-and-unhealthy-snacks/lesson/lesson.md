# Healthy and unhealthy snacks

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — healthy and unhealthy snacks — telling the snacks that help the body from the snacks that harm it, as Zimbabwe's ECD guides teach them side by side. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Healthy snacks

Zimbabwe's ECD A and ECD B guides both teach **healthy snacks** as a named topic: the snacks a young child eats between meals can help the body, and the guides name them as their own lesson part.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unhealthy snacks

Straight after the healthy ones, both guides teach **unhealthy snacks** — the other half of the same topic, so a child learns the pair together and can tell them apart.

## Sub lesson 3 — Choosing your snack

Naming both kinds joins into one skill: **choosing** the healthy snack when a snack is offered. The guides return to the pair at both ECD levels, so the choosing is practised twice.

## The key ideas

- **Healthy snacks** — snacks that help the body.
- **Unhealthy snacks** — snacks that harm the body.
- **Choosing your snack** — choosing the healthy snack.
