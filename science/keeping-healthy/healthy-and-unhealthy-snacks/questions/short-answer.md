# Healthy and unhealthy snacks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Healthy snacks" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's ECD A and ECD B guides both teach **healthy snacks** as a named topic: the snacks a young child eats between meals can help the body, and the guides name them as their own lesson part.

**2.** What does the part "Unhealthy snacks" teach?
**Answer:** Straight after the healthy ones, both guides teach **unhealthy snacks** — the other half of the same topic, so a child learns the pair together and can tell them apart.

**3.** What does the part "Choosing your snack" teach?
**Answer:** Naming both kinds joins into one skill: **choosing** the healthy snack when a snack is offered.
