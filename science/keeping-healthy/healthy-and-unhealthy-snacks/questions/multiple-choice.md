# Healthy and unhealthy snacks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Unhealthy snacks  b) Choosing your snack  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Healthy snacks
**Answer: d) Healthy snacks**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about snacks that help the body?
a) Healthy snacks  b) Unhealthy snacks  c) Choosing your snack
**Answer: a) Healthy snacks**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about snacks that harm the body?
a) Healthy snacks  b) Unhealthy snacks  c) Choosing your snack
**Answer: b) Unhealthy snacks**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about choosing the healthy snack?
a) Healthy snacks  b) Unhealthy snacks  c) Choosing your snack
**Answer: c) Choosing your snack**
