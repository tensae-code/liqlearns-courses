# Hands-on — Fireworks safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rules for the night
Write three fireworks safety rules for a younger child, in your own words. Read them aloud and stand by each one.

## Safe or unsafe?
A partner describes celebration scenes — watching from far away, holding a lit firework, running up to one that did not burst. Sort each scene: safe or unsafe, and say why.
