Today: **Fireworks safety** — Iceland's way, from its Grade 3 "Safe in Our Surroundings" chapter.
Fireworks burn and burst with light and noise. The same fire can burn and harm up close.
So handling fireworks safely is taught as its own aim — before the celebration night, not during it.
Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
