# Fireworks safety — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** Fireworks burn and burst with light, colour and noise — celebration and danger in the same thing.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** The fire that paints the sky can burn and harm at close range.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does a safety chapter teach fireworks at all?
**Answer:** Because fireworks are fire that bursts — children meet them at celebrations, so handling them safely is safety knowledge, taught before the night, not during it.
