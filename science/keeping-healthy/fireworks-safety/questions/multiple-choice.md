# Fireworks safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) Fireworks burn and burst with light, colour and noise — celebration and danger in the same thing.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) Fireworks burn and burst with light, colour and noise — celebration and danger in the same thing.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) The fire that paints the sky can burn and harm at close range.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) The fire that paints the sky can burn and harm at close range.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Iceland teaches handling fireworks safely as its own named aim in the safety chapter.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Iceland teaches handling fireworks safely as its own named aim in the safety chapter.**
