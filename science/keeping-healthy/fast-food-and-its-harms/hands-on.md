# Hands-on — Fast food and its harms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: science reading: "Fast Food and Its Harms" — fast meals spread worldwide because they are quick to prepare, easy to reach and cheap, and are served in large amounts, especially to children; but they hold high levels of fats, sugars and salt, with low levels of the essential nutrients and dietary fibres; eating fast food and fizzy drinks continuously makes keeping a healthy diet hard, and fast food is linked to weight gain and diabetes — so rising fast-food consumption is a great danger to public health Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
