# Fast food and its harms — short answer

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Fast Food and Its Harms.
**Answer:** science reading: "Fast Food and Its Harms" — fast meals spread worldwide because they are quick to prepare, easy to reach and cheap, and are served in large amounts, especially to children; but they hold high levels of fats, sugars and salt, with low levels of the essential nutrients and dietary fibres; eating fast food and fizzy drinks continuously makes keeping a healthy diet hard, and fast food is linked to weight gain and diabetes — so rising fast-food consumption is a great danger to public health.
