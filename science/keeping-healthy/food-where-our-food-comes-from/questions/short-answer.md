# Food — where our food comes from — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Say what food is, in the Grade 4 book's words.**
**Answer:** Anything eaten or drunk that suits health.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Sort these into source groups in words: egg, teff, milk, banana.**
**Answer:** Egg and milk come from animals; teff (grain) and banana (fruit) come from plants — placed in their source groups.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do with a food you cannot place?**
**Answer:** Bring it back as a question — do not hide a guess.
