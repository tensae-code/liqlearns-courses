# Food — where our food comes from — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Food includes drinks as well as things we eat.**
**Answer: True** — Anything eaten or drunk that suits health.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A mixed dish can be taken apart into its parts for sorting.**
**Answer: True** — Its parts have different sources.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Sorting is done by colour.**
**Answer: False** — Sorting is by source group.
