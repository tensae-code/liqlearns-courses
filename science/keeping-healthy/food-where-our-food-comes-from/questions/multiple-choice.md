# Food — where our food comes from — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In the Grade 4 book's words, food is anything eaten or drunk that —**
a) suits health  b) is expensive  c) is sweet
**Answer: a) suits health** — That is the printed definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The lesson's main doing is —**
a) cooking 33 foods  b) sorting about 33 foods into the four source groups  c) counting foods
**Answer: b) sorting about 33 foods into the four source groups** — Sorting the pictured foods by source.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) To sort a food, you first ask —**
a) its price  b) its source — where it comes from  c) its colour
**Answer: b) its source — where it comes from** — Source decides the group.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A drink can be food when it —**
a) suits health  b) is hot  c) is bought
**Answer: a) suits health** — Eaten or drunk, it must suit health.
