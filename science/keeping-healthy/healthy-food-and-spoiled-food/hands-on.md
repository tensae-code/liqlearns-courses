# Hands-on — Healthy food and spoiled food

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The drama
Stage the guide's own drama in a small group: show how healthy food and drink are prepared, stored and eaten — and one spoiled-food mistake, stopped in time.

## Kitchen enquiry
Ask the person who cooks at home: how is food preserved here? Listen for drying and salting, and look at where food is stored.

## Plate drawing
Draw your own healthy plate: grains, vegetables, egg or beans. Label why each part is there.
