# Healthy food and spoiled food — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Food overloaded with sugar, salt and fat is unhealthy food.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Food with an unusual smell may be spoiled and should not be eaten.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Healthy food has no unusual smell, taste or appearance.**
**Answer: True**
