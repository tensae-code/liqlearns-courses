# Healthy food and spoiled food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which is healthy food?**
a) spoiled food  b) fresh, uncontaminated food, low in sugar, salt and fat  c) food overloaded with sugar
**Answer: b) fresh, uncontaminated food, low in sugar, salt and fat**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Spoiled food is food spoiled by —**
a) micro-organisms and harmful things  b) washing  c) covering
**Answer: a) micro-organisms and harmful things**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which is a way the guide names for preserving food?**
a) leaving it in the sun uncovered  b) salting it  c) throwing it on the ground
**Answer: b) salting it** — refrigerating, drying and salting are the named ways.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which plate is the healthy one?**
a) grains, vegetables and egg  b) only sweets  c) fried snacks and sweets
**Answer: a) grains, vegetables and egg**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) The student's chart says to use —**
a) river water as it comes  b) treated or boiled water  c) no water
**Answer: b) treated or boiled water** — Treated or boiled water.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Raw foods that should be cooked should —**
a) be eaten raw  b) not be eaten raw  c) be left uncovered
**Answer: b) not be eaten raw** — Do not eat raw foods that should be cooked.
