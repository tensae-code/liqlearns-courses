# Video script — Healthy food and spoiled food

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fresh. Clean. Not too much sugar, salt or fat. That is healthy food.
Bad smell? Bad look? Spoiled. Do not eat it.
Keep food safe: cool it, dry it, salt it.
Spoiled food brings sickness. Fresh food builds the body.
