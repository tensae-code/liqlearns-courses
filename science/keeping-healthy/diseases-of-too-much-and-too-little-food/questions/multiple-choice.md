# Diseases of too much and too little food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Overweight and obesity — too much  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Overweight and obesity — too much**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Stunting and anaemia — too little  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Stunting and anaemia — too little**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Vietnam's own health facts  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Vietnam's own health facts**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the diseases of too much food (overweight and obesity) and of too little food (stunting and iron-deficiency anaemia) — their signs, causes and prevention. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Vietnam, Grade 4 — *Khoa học 4*, printed p. 93  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Vietnam, Grade 4 — *Khoa học 4*, printed p. 93**
