# Diseases of too much and too little food — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Overweight and obesity — too much.
**Answer:** Vietnam's Grade 4 *Khoa học 4* teaches overweight and obesity as diseases of **too much**: too much carbohydrate, fat and protein food, and too little movement. Prevention is the same pair reversed — eat in measure and move; the lesson has learners track three days of their own activity minutes.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Stunting and anaemia — too little.
**Answer:** The diseases of **too little**: stunting (undernutrition) leaves a child short and light for their age; iron-deficiency anaemia shows as pale skin, tiredness, dizziness and poor concentration. Their causes are diets short of nutrients or of iron-rich food, and stomach disease and worms; prevention is a diet that carries enough of everything, iron-rich foods among them.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Vietnam's own health facts.
**Answer:** The book closes with Vietnam's own facts (extension box): childhood stunting is a main reason Vietnamese adults do not reach their full possible height, and iron-deficiency anaemia at school age slows body growth, puberty and learning. Too much and too little are both diseases of food — the balanced middle is the healthy place.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Overweight and obesity — too much → Stunting and anaemia — too little → Vietnam's own health facts — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
