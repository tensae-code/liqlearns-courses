# Video script — Diseases of too much and too little food

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Diseases of too much and too little food** — Vietnam's way, from its book (Vietnam, Grade 4 — *Khoa học 4*, printed p. 93).

Vietnam's Grade 4 *Khoa học 4* teaches overweight and obesity as diseases of **too much**: too much carbohydrate, fat and protein food, and too little movement. Prevention is the same pair reversed — eat in measure and move; the lesson has learners track three days of their own activity minutes.

The diseases of **too little**: stunting (undernutrition) leaves a child short and light for their age; iron-deficiency anaemia shows as pale skin, tiredness, dizziness and poor concentration. Their causes are diets short of nutrients or of iron-rich food, and stomach disease and worms; prevention is a diet that carries enough of everything, iron-rich foods among them.

The book closes with Vietnam's own facts (extension box): childhood stunting is a main reason Vietnamese adults do not reach their full possible height, and iron-deficiency anaemia at school age slows body growth, puberty and learning. Too much and too little are both diseases of food — the balanced middle is the healthy place.
