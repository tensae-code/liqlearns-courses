# Hands-on — Diarrhoea, dehydration and danger signs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Diarrhoea defined (three or more loose or liquid stools. diarrhoea defined (three or more loose or liquid stools a day) in three clinical types: acute watery diarrhoea (hours to days — e.g. cholera), acute bloody diarrhoea (dysentery) and persistent diarrhoea (14 days or more) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
