# Diarrhoea, dehydration and danger signs — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** What do Honduras's books teach about diarrhoea defined (three or more loose or liquid stools?
**Answer: diarrhoea defined (three or more loose or liquid stools a day) in three clinical types: acute watery diarrhoea (hours to days — e.g. cholera), acute bloody diarrhoea (dysentery) and persistent diarrhoea (14 days or more)**

**2.** What do Honduras's books teach about the causes of acute diarrhoeal diseases?
**Answer: the causes of acute diarrhoeal diseases: water contaminated by sewage, septic tanks or latrines; infections by parasites, bacteria and viruses; inadequate nutrition; and transmission person to person, through badly cooked or insalubriously prepared food, seafood from contaminated waters, and food irrigated with contaminated water or fertilised with chemicals.**

**3.** What do Honduras's books teach about diarrhoea's toll?
**Answer: diarrhoea's toll: developing-country children under five commonly suffer at least three episodes a year, which steal growth and proper brain development and can kill — WHO (2013) figures of some 760,000 child deaths a year; basic access to drinking water, sanitation and hygiene at home is what prevents cases.**

**4.** Name this lesson's one thing.
**Answer: Diarrhoea, dehydration and danger signs.**
