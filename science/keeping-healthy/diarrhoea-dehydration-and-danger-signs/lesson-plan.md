## Sub lessons
1. **Diarrhoea defined (three or more loose or liquid stools** — diarrhoea defined (three or more loose or liquid stools a day) in three clinical types: acute watery diarrhoea (hours to days — e.g. cholera), acute bloody diarrhoea (dysentery) and persistent diarrhoea (14 days or more)
2. **The causes of acute diarrhoeal diseases** — the causes of acute diarrhoeal diseases: water contaminated by sewage, septic tanks or latrines; infections by parasites, bacteria and viruses; inadequate nutrition; and transmission person to person, through badly cooked or insalubriously prepared food, seafood from contaminated waters, and food irrigated with contaminated water or fertilised with chemicals.
3. **Diarrhoea's toll** — diarrhoea's toll: developing-country children under five commonly suffer at least three episodes a year, which steal growth and proper brain development and can kill — WHO (2013) figures of some 760,000 child deaths a year; basic access to drinking water, sanitation and hygiene at home is what prevents cases.
