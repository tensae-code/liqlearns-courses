# First aid for snake and animal bites — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: First treatment when bitten by a snake.
**Answer:** Laos’s Grade 2 book (Ch. 3, printed pp. 15–16) teaches the **first treatment when bitten by a snake** as its own named topic. **First treatment** (first aid) is the help given at once, in the first minutes, before fuller care — and a snake bite gets its own teaching because it is not the same as an ordinary cut or sting.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: First treatment when bitten by a dog or cat.
**Answer:** The same chapter teaches the **first treatment when bitten by a dog or cat** (printed pp. 15–16) as a separate named topic from the snake bite — the book keeps the two apart because the biter, the wound and the danger that follows are different.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** First treatment when bitten by a snake → First treatment when bitten by a dog or cat — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
