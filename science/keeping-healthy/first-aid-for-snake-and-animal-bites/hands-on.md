# Hands-on — First aid for snake and animal bites

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Practise the first minutes — with no real bite
In pairs, with a pretend bite (a drawn mark on the arm, never a real wound): one partner is bitten, the other says and shows the order of the first treatment for that bite — snake first, then dog or cat — while the class checks each against the lesson. Swap roles and repeat, until both treatments can be told apart and told in order.
