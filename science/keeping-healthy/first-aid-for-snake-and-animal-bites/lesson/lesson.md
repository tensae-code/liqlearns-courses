# First aid for snake and animal bites

## Do it first
Before you read: this lesson teaches one thing — first aid for snake and animal bites — the first treatment given at once when a snake bites, and when a dog or cat bites — as placed from Laos’s Grade 2 Science and Environment book, Ch. 3. What do you already know about it? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Laos's way in — its Science and Environment books teach this as its own topic, in the order below.

## Sub lesson 1 — First treatment when bitten by a snake

Laos’s Grade 2 book (Ch. 3, printed pp. 15–16) teaches the **first treatment when bitten by a snake** as its own named topic. **First treatment** (first aid) is the help given at once, in the first minutes, before fuller care — and a snake bite gets its own teaching because it is not the same as an ordinary cut or sting.

The chapter places this beside its teaching on insect bites and stings (printed p. 14, stocked in the accidents lesson): the small bite and the dangerous bite are both met first by the people who are there, so both first treatments are learned before either is needed.

## Sub lesson 2 — First treatment when bitten by a dog or cat

The same chapter teaches the **first treatment when bitten by a dog or cat** (printed pp. 15–16) as a separate named topic from the snake bite — the book keeps the two apart because the biter, the wound and the danger that follows are different.

Learning the two first treatments side by side, each under its own name, is the book’s way: when the moment comes, the helper does not improvise — they reach for the treatment that matches the bite. The Grade 2 teacher guide’s wound care for bites and stings (wash clean with soap, dry, apply antiseptic, bandage; if bitten by a dog, get away fast and call for help) is stocked as guide evidence in the accidents lesson, where the placements file put it.

## The key ideas

- **First treatment when bitten by a snake** — as taught above, in the lesson’s own order.
- **First treatment when bitten by a dog or cat** — as taught above, in the lesson’s own order.
