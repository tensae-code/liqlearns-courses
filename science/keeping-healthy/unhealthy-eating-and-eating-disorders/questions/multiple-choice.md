# Unhealthy eating and eating disorders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Eating disorders  b) Help and healthy patterns  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Unhealthy eating habits
**Answer: d) Unhealthy eating habits**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about naming the habits that harm?
a) Unhealthy eating habits  b) Eating disorders  c) Help and healthy patterns
**Answer: a) Unhealthy eating habits**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about eating disorders as a health trouble?
a) Unhealthy eating habits  b) Eating disorders  c) Help and healthy patterns
**Answer: b) Eating disorders**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about help, not blame?
a) Unhealthy eating habits  b) Eating disorders  c) Help and healthy patterns
**Answer: c) Help and healthy patterns**
