# Unhealthy eating and eating disorders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Unhealthy eating habits" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 5 guide teaches **unhealthy eating habits** as a named unit at the head of its food run: the ways of eating that work against the body, named so they can be changed.

**2.** What does the part "Eating disorders" teach?
**Answer:** The same unit teaches **eating disorders**: eating itself fallen into disorder — a health trouble of its own, not a bad habit, and taught as health, in the health topic.

**3.** What does the part "Help and healthy patterns" teach?
**Answer:** A disorder is met with help, not blame.
