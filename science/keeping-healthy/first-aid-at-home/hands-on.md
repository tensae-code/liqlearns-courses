# Hands-on — First aid at home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The order game
Write the seven gestures on seven slips of paper: stop, look, call, comfort, clean, press, rest. Shuffle them, then lay them in the lesson's order. Read the row aloud and say why the first three — stop, look, call — must come before any touching.

## Practise the gentle gestures (no real hurt)
With a companion pretending to have a small cut on the hand: talk them calm, keep them sitting still, mime washing the cut with clean water, and press a clean cloth gently on the place. Your companion judges: did help get called first? Were the gestures gentle and in order?

## The home first-aid map
Walk your home with an adult and draw its first-aid map: where the clean water is, where clean cloths are kept, and the way to the nearest adult help or health worker. Keep the map where the family can find it — learning the map now is part of first aid.
