# First aid at home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is first aid, and what are its first three gestures?
**Answer:** First aid is the first help given to a hurt or suddenly ill person before fuller help arrives; its first gestures are stop, look, and call for help.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is first aid called a bridge to real care?
**Answer:** Because it helps in the first minutes while real care — an adult, a health worker — is on its way; it does not replace that care.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the four gentle gestures that come after calling for help, and say what each does.
**Answer:** Comfort (calms the person and keeps them still and warm), clean water (washes a small cut and cools a burn), pressure (a clean cloth pressed on bleeding slows the blood), and rest (the hurt person does not go back to play or work).

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A friend cuts a finger and blood is flowing. Say, in order, what you do.
**Answer:** Stop and look; call an adult or health worker for help; comfort my friend and keep them still; wash the cut with clean water; press a clean cloth on it; let my friend rest until help comes.
