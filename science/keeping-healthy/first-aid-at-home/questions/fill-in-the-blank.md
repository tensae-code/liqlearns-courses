# First aid at home — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) First aid is the _____ help given before fuller help arrives.
**Answer: first**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The first gestures are stop, _____, and call for help.
**Answer: look**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A small cut or burn is washed and cooled with _____ water.
**Answer: clean**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A clean cloth pressed on a bleeding cut applies _____.
**Answer: pressure**
