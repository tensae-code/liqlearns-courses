# First aid at home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) First aid is…
a) The only care a hurt person needs  b) The first help given before fuller help arrives  c) Medicine bought at a shop  d) A game played after an accident
**Answer: b) The first help given before fuller help arrives**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The very first gestures of first aid are…
a) Touch, lift, run  b) Stop, look, and call for help  c) Wash, press, feed  d) Shout, cry, hide
**Answer: b) Stop, look, and call for help**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Why stop and look before touching?
a) To rest  b) To see what happened, whether the place is safe, and how hurt the person is  c) To wait for the hurt to heal itself  d) To find a cloth
**Answer: b) To see what happened, whether the place is safe, and how hurt the person is**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A small cut or burn is first treated with…
a) Dirty water  b) Soil  c) Clean water  d) Oil
**Answer: c) Clean water**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Bleeding is slowed by…
a) Pressing a clean cloth on the cut  b) Waving the hand in the air  c) Running around  d) Blowing on it
**Answer: a) Pressing a clean cloth on the cut**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) After first aid, a hurt person should…
a) Go straight back to play  b) Rest while real care comes  c) Hide the hurt  d) Do the heaviest work
**Answer: b) Rest while real care comes**
