Today: **First aid at home** — Gabon's way: learn the first gestures before you need them.

First aid is the first help, given in the first minutes after a hurt, before fuller help arrives. And its first gesture is not touching at all. Stop. Look — what happened, is the place safe, how hurt is the person? Then call for help: an adult, a neighbour, the health worker.
Only then come the gentle gestures. Comfort the person and keep them still and warm. Clean water for a small cut or a burn. A clean cloth pressed on bleeding. And rest — a hurt person does not go back to play.
Stop, look, call — then comfort, clean, press, rest. Say the order back to me.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
