# Lesson plan — First aid at home

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** First aid at home
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the first-aid gestures — the first help given at home in the first minutes after a hurt: stop, look, and call for help first, then comfort, clean water, pressure and rest — Gabon's own way, in its Grade 5 Super Efficace guide, is to give those gestures their own lesson, so the first help is learned before it is needed
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **First: stop, look, and call for help** — First aid is the first help given before fuller help arrives; its first gesture is stopping and looking — what happened, is the place safe, how hurt is the person — and then calling an adult or health worker for help.
2. **The first gestures — comfort, clean water, pressure, rest** — With help on its way: comfort the person and keep them still and warm; clean water for a small cut or a burn; a clean cloth pressed on bleeding; and rest — first aid does not send a hurt person back to play or work.

## Before this lesson

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Thinks first aid starts with touching the hurt → return to sub lesson 1: the first gestures are stop, look, and call for help — touching comes after, and gently.
- Thinks first aid replaces the health worker → return to the bridge idea: first aid is the first help, given while real care is on its way.
