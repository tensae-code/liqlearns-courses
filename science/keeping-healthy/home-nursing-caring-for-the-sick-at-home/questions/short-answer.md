# Home nursing — caring for the sick at home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Home nursing — a named health topic.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 7 book teaches **home nursing** as its own topic in its health unit: the care of a sick person **at home**, by the people of the home — taught as a skill a primary learner should know, beside stress and depression and before the unit's nutrition topics.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The home nurse's work.
**Answer:** The topic's place in the book is its teaching: most sickness is first met at home, so the home's care — watching the sick person, keeping them clean, fed and rested, and knowing when the health centre is needed — is health work, named and taught as nursing, not left to chance.
