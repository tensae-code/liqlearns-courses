# Keeping muscles healthy

## Do it first
Before you read: you keep your teeth clean and your body washed — but how do you keep your muscles healthy? And what muscle accidents could be prevented before they happen? Say your answers. Then read on: this is Rwanda's way in — its Primary 4 muscles unit closes with the muscles' hygiene and the prevention of their accidents.
