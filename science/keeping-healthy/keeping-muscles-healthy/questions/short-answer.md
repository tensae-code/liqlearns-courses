# Keeping muscles healthy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Hygiene of the muscles" teach?
**Answer:** The unit closes its knowledge with care: **hygiene of muscles** — the keeping of the muscles in health as a taught habit, in the same place where these books teach the hygiene of every system they open.

**2.** What does the part "Prevention of muscle accidents" teach?
**Answer:** And the guard before the harm: **prevention of muscle accidents** — the accidents of muscles, like cramp, are met first by preventing them.
