# Hands-on — Vaccination — building artificial immunity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: vaccination: a way of forming artificial immunity by taking a dose of the vaccine, by injection or by mouth, to protect the body from diseases (pictured: the COVID-19 vaccine; the Vision 2030 Quality of Life programme box) Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
