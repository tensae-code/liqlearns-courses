# Staying safe with technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Technology design and safety  b) Checking before you use  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Technology safety
**Answer: d) Technology safety**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about safety taught with the technology itself?
a) Technology safety  b) Technology design and safety  c) Checking before you use
**Answer: a) Technology safety**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about safety built into the design?
a) Technology safety  b) Technology design and safety  c) Checking before you use
**Answer: b) Technology design and safety**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about check first, then use?
a) Technology safety  b) Technology design and safety  c) Checking before you use
**Answer: c) Checking before you use**
