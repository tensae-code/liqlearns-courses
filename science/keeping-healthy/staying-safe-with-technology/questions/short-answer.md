# Staying safe with technology — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Technology safety" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's guides teach **technology safety** as a named unit at ECD A and B and in Grades 1 and 2: using technology is learned together with staying safe while using it, from the very first level.

**2.** What does the part "Technology design and safety" teach?
**Answer:** Grades 4, 5 and 6 name the other half: **technology design and safety** — technology should be designed so it can be used safely, and the safety is part of the design, not an afterthought.

**3.** What does the part "Checking before you use" teach?
**Answer:** The two halves join in one habit: check the technology and how you will use it **before** you use it.
