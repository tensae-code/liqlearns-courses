## Sub lesson 1 — Technology safety

Zimbabwe's guides teach **technology safety** as a named unit at ECD A and B and in Grades 1 and 2: using technology is learned together with staying safe while using it, from the very first level.

## Sub lesson 2 — Technology design and safety

Grades 4, 5 and 6 name the other half: **technology design and safety** — technology should be designed so it can be used safely, and the safety is part of the design, not an afterthought.

## Sub lesson 3 — Checking before you use

The two halves join in one habit: check the technology and how you will use it **before** you use it. That habit is the lesson's whole point, carried up the grades in Zimbabwe's own order.

## The key ideas

- **Technology safety** — safety taught with the technology itself.
- **Technology design and safety** — safety built into the design.
- **Checking before you use** — check first, then use.
