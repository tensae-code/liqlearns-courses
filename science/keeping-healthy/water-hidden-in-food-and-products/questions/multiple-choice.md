# Water hidden in food and products — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where does the book say water hides?
a) only in rivers  b) in food and products, even in the soup  c) only in the sea  d) nowhere  
**Answer: b) in food and products, even in the soup**

**2.** How do you find the hidden water in a product?
a) shake it  b) read its label's ingredients  c) freeze it  d) weigh it  
**Answer: b) read its label's ingredients**

**3.** Water in a product is one of its…
a) colours  b) prices  c) ingredients  d) names  
**Answer: c) ingredients**
