# Water hidden in food and products

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's title — Water hidden in food and products. Say out loud anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on: this is Venezuela's way in — its Grade 1 *Explorar* book teaches, in its water part, that water is hidden inside the foods and products we use every day: 'water even in the soup'.

## Sub lesson 1 — Water even in the soup

Water is not only in the tap, the river and the rain. It is **inside our food** too — even in the soup. Foods and products carry water as one of their ingredients, mixed in where we cannot always see it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading the label

The proof is on the **label**: reading a food label shows water named among the ingredients of products we eat and drink. The book's way is to look — take a product, read its ingredients, and find the water hiding there.
