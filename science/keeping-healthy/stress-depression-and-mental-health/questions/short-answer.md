# Stress, depression and mental health — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Stress — a named health topic.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 7 book teaches **stress and depression** as a named topic of its health unit — set between airborne diseases and home nursing, as health teaching in its own right: the health of the mind taught beside the health of the body.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Depression — named beside stress.
**Answer:** **Depression** is named with stress in the same topic: two states of the mind that a learner should be able to name, told apart from ordinary tiredness or a passing bad day by being taught, not left unnamed. Naming them is this lesson's one thing, exactly as the book files it.

**Answer:** Stress — a named health topic → Depression — named beside stress — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
