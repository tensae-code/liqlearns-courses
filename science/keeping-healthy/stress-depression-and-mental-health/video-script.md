Today: **Stress, depression and mental health** — South Sudan's way, from its Ministry of General Education and Instruction Primary Science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

South Sudan's Primary 7 book teaches **stress and depression** as a named topic of its health unit — set between airborne diseases and home nursing, as health teaching in its own right: the health of the mind taught beside the health of the body.

**Depression** is named with stress in the same topic: two states of the mind that a learner should be able to name, told apart from ordinary tiredness or a passing bad day by being taught, not left unnamed. Naming them is this lesson's one thing, exactly as the book files it.
