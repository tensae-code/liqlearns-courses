# Emergency supplies and responders

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — emergency supplies and responders — the supplies kept ready for an emergency and the people who respond, as Zimbabwe's guides teach them side by side. ## Sub lesson 1 — Emergency supplies

Zimbabwe's guides teach **emergency supplies** as a named unit in ECD B and in Grades 1 and 2: the things kept ready before an emergency, so help begins at once when it comes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Emergency responders and professionals

Beside the supplies, the guides teach the people: **emergency responders** in Grade 1, and **emergency professionals** in Grades 3, 4 and 5 — the trained people who come when an emergency happens.

## Sub lesson 3 — Supplies and people together

An emergency is met by both halves together: ready supplies in the hands of ready people. Zimbabwe's own way is to teach the pair inside the same hazards run, every year.

## The key ideas

- **Emergency supplies** — supplies kept ready before they are needed.
- **Emergency responders and professionals** — the trained people who respond.
- **Supplies and people together** — ready supplies, ready people.
