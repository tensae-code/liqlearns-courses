# Emergency supplies and responders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Emergency responders and professionals  b) Supplies and people together  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Emergency supplies
**Answer: d) Emergency supplies**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about supplies kept ready before they are needed?
a) Emergency supplies  b) Emergency responders and professionals  c) Supplies and people together
**Answer: a) Emergency supplies**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about the trained people who respond?
a) Emergency supplies  b) Emergency responders and professionals  c) Supplies and people together
**Answer: b) Emergency responders and professionals**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about ready supplies, ready people?
a) Emergency supplies  b) Emergency responders and professionals  c) Supplies and people together
**Answer: c) Supplies and people together**
