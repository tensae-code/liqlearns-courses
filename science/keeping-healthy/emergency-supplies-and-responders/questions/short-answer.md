# Emergency supplies and responders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Emergency supplies" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's guides teach **emergency supplies** as a named unit in ECD B and in Grades 1 and 2: the things kept ready before an emergency, so help begins at once when it comes.

**2.** What does the part "Emergency responders and professionals" teach?
**Answer:** Beside the supplies, the guides teach the people: **emergency responders** in Grade 1, and **emergency professionals** in Grades 3, 4 and 5 — the trained people who come when an emergency happens.

**3.** What does the part "Supplies and people together" teach?
**Answer:** An emergency is met by both halves together: ready supplies in the hands of ready people.
