# Video script — Immunisation and immunity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Immunisation and immunity** — Uganda's home-study way: protection built before the disease arrives.
A vaccine makes the body resistant — that resistance is immunity, and immunisation with vaccines cuts child deaths. Measles, a virus disease with sores and a rash, is prevented by an injection in the left upper arm, and the immunisation table lays out the whole childhood course.
For cholera, the home guards are boiled water and covering food — and every immunisation is written on the child health card.
