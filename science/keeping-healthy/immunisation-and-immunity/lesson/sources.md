Placement choice, on the record: this NEW LESSON was built for the Uganda placements "NEW LESSON NEEDED — immunisation-and-immunity" (four placements, Primary Five home-study booklet, printed pp. 1, 2–4, 6 and 7–8). No existing general-science lesson covers immunity and immunisation as their own thing — `keeping-healthy/what-disease-is-and-how-it-spreads` teaches what disease is and how it spreads, not how vaccines build resistance before it arrives — so it gets its own lesson. It sits in keeping-healthy/ and is registered in CURRICULUM.md Unit 14 (Keeping Healthy). Uganda's own way — opening the Primary Five home-study science course with immunisation, and closing the topic with the child health card the family keeps — IS part of this lesson's content, carried exactly as the placements describe it.
