# Hands-on — Immunisation and immunity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Resistance role-play
One learner plays the disease arriving at a house; another plays the immunised child's body, holding up a card: 'resistant — vaccine given on (date)'. Swap roles. The point to say aloud each round: protection was built before the disease came.

## Read a child health card
With permission, look at a real child health card (or draw a copy of its shape). Find where immunisations are recorded and read what is written at a visit. Say what a health worker could tell about the child from the card alone.

## Cholera guards at home
Carry out the booklet's two cholera guards once, properly: boil drinking water, and cover the food in your home. Write the two guards on a card and place it where food is kept.
