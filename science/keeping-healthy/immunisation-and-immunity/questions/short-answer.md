# Immunisation and immunity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is immunity, and how does immunisation build it?
**Answer:** Immunity is the body's resistance to a disease; immunisation builds it beforehand — a vaccine makes the body resistant so the disease cannot take hold.

**2.** Give the booklet's worked example of an immunisable disease, with its signs and its vaccine.
**Answer:** Measles — a virus disease with sores and a rash; its vaccine is given by injection in the left upper arm.

**3.** What two home practices does the booklet give against cholera?
**Answer:** Boiling drinking water and covering food.

**4.** What is the child health card for?
**Answer:** It is the record of the child's immunisations — what was given at each visit is written on it, so the child's protection can be followed.
