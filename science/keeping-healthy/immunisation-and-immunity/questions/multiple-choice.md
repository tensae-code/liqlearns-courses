# Immunisation and immunity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A vaccine protects a child by…
a) Curing any sickness at once  b) Making the body resistant before the disease arrives  c) Making food safer  d) Replacing the health card
**Answer: b) Making the body resistant before the disease arrives**

**2.** Immunisation, the booklet says, cuts…
a) School fees  b) Child deaths  c) Hair  d) Travel time
**Answer: b) Child deaths**

**3.** Measles is caused by a…
a) Worm  b) Virus  c) Mosquito  d) Fungus
**Answer: b) Virus**

**4.** The measles vaccine is given…
a) In the left upper arm, by injection  b) In the foot  c) By mouth only  d) On the health card
**Answer: a) In the left upper arm, by injection**

**5.** Cholera prevention at home, as the booklet gives it, is…
a) Boiled water and covering food  b) Open water and uncovered food  c) No water  d) Only medicine
**Answer: a) Boiled water and covering food**

**6.** What is recorded at every immunisation is written on the…
a) School report  b) Child health card  c) Exercise book  d) Wall chart
**Answer: b) Child health card**
