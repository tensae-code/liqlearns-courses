# Immunity and white blood cells — the body's defence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: How do we protect ourselves from disease? By strengthening our immunity.
**Answer:** how do we protect ourselves from disease? By strengthening our immunity — the body's ability to resist the causes of disease; natural or artificial. The immune system can recognise countless causes and so the white blood cells attack and destroy them; the skin is the body's first line of defence, and the body's secretions fight the causes too (tears, nose mucus, earwax, saliva and the digestive juice); and following healthy habits strengthens immunity and keeps our bodies sound.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Natural and artificial immunity.
**Answer:** natural and artificial immunity: natural immunity is the fast response producing antibodies that fight the disease's causes, and it lasts long — which is why you do not catch some diseases, like chickenpox, more than once; artificial immunity is formed through vaccination — it may last a short time, needing another dose of vaccine, or a long time, some of it for life.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The Human Body Systems.
**Answer:** Student References, "The Human Body Systems" opener: the immune system in depth — this system protects the body from the germs that cause diseases and can usually stop germs entering the body; when germs do find their way in, the white blood cells confront and destroy them before they cause disease; white cells are part of the blood, travel through the blood vessels, and the lymphatic vessels carry a fluid called lymph instead of blood — many white cells concentrate and live in the lymph nodes, where the body's harmful materials are disposed of; if the white cells cannot kill the germs, the germs multiply and cause disease, and in illness the immune system keeps working inside the body killing germs until the disease goes and the body returns to good health.
