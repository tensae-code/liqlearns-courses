# Hands-on — Immunity and white blood cells — the body's defence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: how do we protect ourselves from disease? By strengthening our immunity — the body's ability to resist the causes of disease; natural or artificial. The immune system can recognise countless causes and so the white blood cells attack and destroy them; the skin is the body's first line of defence, and the body's secretions fight the causes too (tears, nose mucus, earwax, saliva and the digestive juice); and following healthy habits strengthens immunity and keeps our bodies sound Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
