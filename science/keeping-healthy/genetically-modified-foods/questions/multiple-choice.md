# Genetically modified foods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Transgenic foods — genes added for resistance  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Transgenic foods — genes added for resistance**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The argument — health-risk claims and environmental impact  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The argument — health-risk claims and environmental impact**

**3.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: genetically modified (transgenic) foods — foods from plants given added genes so they resist pests, herbicides and drought — and the argument about their risks. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**4.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Nicaragua, Grade 6 — *Ciencias Naturales, 6to grado*, printed pp. 69–74  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
