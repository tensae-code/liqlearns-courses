# Genetically modified foods — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Transgenic foods — genes added for resistance.
**Answer:** Nicaragua's Grade 6 book teaches **transgenic foods**: genes are added so plants **resist pests, herbicides and drought**. The common ones it names are **maize, soya, cotton, potato, rice and tomato**.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The argument — health-risk claims and environmental impact.
**Answer:** The same book carries the other side as part of the lesson: **Greenpeace's health-risk claims**, and the **environmental impact of unnatural cross-breeding** — the new combinations do not occur in nature, and what they do to health and to the environment is argued about. The book teaches the foods and the argument together.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Transgenic foods — genes added for resistance → The argument — health-risk claims and environmental impact — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
