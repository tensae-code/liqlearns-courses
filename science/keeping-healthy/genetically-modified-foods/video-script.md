# Video script — Genetically modified foods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Genetically modified foods** — Nicaragua's way, from its book (Nicaragua, Grade 6 — *Ciencias Naturales, 6to grado*, printed pp. 69–74).

Nicaragua's Grade 6 book teaches **transgenic foods**: genes are added so plants **resist pests, herbicides and drought**. The common ones it names are **maize, soya, cotton, potato, rice and tomato**.

The same book carries the other side as part of the lesson: **Greenpeace's health-risk claims**, and the **environmental impact of unnatural cross-breeding** — the new combinations do not occur in nature, and what they do to health and to the environment is argued about. The book teaches the foods and the argument together.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
