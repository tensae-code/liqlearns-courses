# Video script — Food — how food gets spoiled

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Good food can be fouled. Flies. Rats. Dirty water. Dirty hands.

Then sickness follows — diarrhoea, typhoid, cholera.

Our friend ate unwashed fruit. The doctor asked: what did you eat? Did you wash your hands?

Wash. Cover. Keep food clean.
