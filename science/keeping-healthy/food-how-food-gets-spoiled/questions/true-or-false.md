# Food — how food gets spoiled — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Eating raw food that should be cooked can foul the body.**
**Answer: True** — It is on the printed causes list.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Typhoid can follow fouled food.**
**Answer: True** — Typhoid is one of the four named diseases.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Expired ingredients are safe if they smell fine.**
**Answer: False** — Spoiled or expired ingredients are a printed cause of fouling.
