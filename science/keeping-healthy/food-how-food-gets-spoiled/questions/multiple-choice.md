# Food — how food gets spoiled — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which creatures foul food in the book's list?**
a) flies, rats and cockroaches  b) bees and butterflies  c) fish
**Answer: a) flies, rats and cockroaches** — Flies, rats and cockroaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Dirty water, dirty utensils and dirty hands are —**
a) ways food gets fouled  b) ways food stays safe  c) cooking methods
**Answer: a) ways food gets fouled** — They are printed causes of fouling.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which is a disease that follows fouled food?**
a) cholera  b) strong bones  c) good eyesight
**Answer: a) cholera** — Cholera — with diarrhoea, amoeba and typhoid.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In the story, the boy fell ill after eating —**
a) cooked injera  b) unwashed fruit bought at the market  c) boiled water
**Answer: b) unwashed fruit bought at the market** — Unwashed market fruit, eaten with unwashed hands.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The doctor traced the cause by asking —**
a) the boy's age only  b) what he ate, whether he washed his hands, and whether the food was cooked or raw and washed  c) nothing
**Answer: b) what he ate, whether he washed his hands, and whether the food was cooked or raw and washed** — Those three questions trace the cause.
