# Food — how food gets spoiled — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List four causes of food spoiling.**
**Answer:** Any four of: flies/rats/cockroaches fouling food; dirty water; dirty utensils; spoiled or expired ingredients; dirty preparation places and hands; eating raw food.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Summarise the plot of 'Our friend and the doctor'.**
**Answer:** A boy rushes from play, eats unwashed market fruit, falls ill; the doctor's questions (what did you eat? did you wash your hands? was it cooked or raw and washed?) trace the cause.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four diseases that follow fouled food.**
**Answer:** Diarrhoea, amoeba, typhoid and cholera.
