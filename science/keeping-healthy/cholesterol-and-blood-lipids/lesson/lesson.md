# Cholesterol and blood lipids

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's subject — cholesterol and blood lipids. Then read on: this is Honduras's way in — its *Ciencias Naturales* books teach this thing at the places named in Sources, in the order below.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cholesterol is made in the liver and is used

cholesterol is made in the liver and is used to produce hormones, vitamin D and bile acids, but in excess it deposits in the arteries, obstructing blood flow — the guide calls it an enemy of the arteries and the heart (guide evidence)
