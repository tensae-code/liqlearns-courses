# Harmful eating habits — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book teach habits rather than single meals?
**Answer:** Because what a child eats again and again — the pattern — is what shapes the body; one meal alone does not.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does naming a harmful habit protect a child?
**Answer:** A habit that can be named can be judged against the healthy pattern and changed — and a changed habit changes every day that follows.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Where does this lesson stand in Mauritania's Grade 3 book?
**Answer:** Straight after the healthy eating habits lesson (printed p. 16), at printed p. 17, closing the book's run of food lessons.
