# Harmful eating habits — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A thing done again and again, until it is done without thinking, is a _____.
**Answer: habit**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The harm of a harmful eating habit is in its _____, not in one meal.
**Answer: repetition**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Harmful habits are judged against the _____ eating habits taught just before them.
**Answer: healthy**
