# Harmful eating habits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A habit harms or helps mainly because…
a) It happens once  b) It is repeated again and again  c) It is expensive  d) It is tasty
**Answer: b) It is repeated again and again**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Mauritania's Grade 3 book teaches harmful eating habits…
a) Before any food lesson  b) As their own lesson, after the healthy eating habits  c) Only for adults  d) In the animals unit
**Answer: b) As their own lesson, after the healthy eating habits**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A harmful eating habit is judged against…
a) What friends eat  b) The healthy eating pattern taught just before it  c) The price of food  d) The size of the plate
**Answer: b) The healthy eating pattern taught just before it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The protection this lesson gives is…
a) Naming the harmful habits so they can be changed  b) Never eating snacks of any kind  c) Eating only one food  d) Skipping meals
**Answer: a) Naming the harmful habits so they can be changed**
