# Hands-on — Harmful eating habits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A day's eating, sorted
Write down everything eaten and drunk yesterday, meal by meal. Sort the list into two columns — habits that help the body, habits that harm it — and say for each harmful one what made it a habit rather than a single meal.

## Swap one habit
Choose one harmful eating habit from your list. For one week, swap it for the healthy habit that answers it, and note each day whether the swap held.
