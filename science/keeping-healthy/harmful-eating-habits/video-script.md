Today: **Harmful eating habits** — Mauritania's way: the harmful side taught by name, right after the healthy side.

A habit is a thing done again and again. One meal does not shape a body; a pattern does. So Mauritania's Grade 3 book teaches the eating habits that harm the body as a lesson of their own — after grains, fruits, meats and nutrients, after the day's meals and the healthy habits.

The test of a habit is the healthy pattern beside it. Name your habits, judge each one against that pattern, and change the one that harms — a changed habit changes every day after it.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
