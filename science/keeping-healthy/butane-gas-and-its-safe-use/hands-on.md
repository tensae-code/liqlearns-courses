# Hands-on — Butane gas and its safe use

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bottle and lighter study
With an adult, look at the home's gas bottle and a lighter — without opening or lighting either. Say where the butane sits in each, in what state (liquid under pressure), and what lets it out as gas.

## Gas or charcoal?
List the cooking fuels used in your home for one week. For each meal cooked with charcoal, write what the book's preference would choose instead, and why.
