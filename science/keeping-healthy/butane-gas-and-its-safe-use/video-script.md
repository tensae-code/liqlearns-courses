Today: **Butane gas and its safe use** — Mauritania's way: know the gas by its facts, not by sight.

Butane is colourless — you cannot see it. And it liquefies easily: squeezed into a bottle, it sits as a liquid until the valve lets it out as gas. That is the gas in your cooker, and the gas in a lighter.

Mauritania's Grade 5 book ends the lesson with a choice for the environment: gas preferred over charcoal, because charcoal begins with cut wood.
