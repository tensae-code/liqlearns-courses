# Butane gas and its safe use — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Butane is a _____ gas — it cannot be seen.
**Answer: colourless**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Squeezed into a bottle, butane becomes a _____ under pressure.
**Answer: liquid**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Gas is preferred over _____ to protect the environment.
**Answer: charcoal**
