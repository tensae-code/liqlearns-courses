# Butane gas and its safe use — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Give butane's two handling facts.
**Answer:** It is colourless — a gas that cannot be seen — and it is easily liquefied, so it is stored as a liquid under pressure in bottles and lighters.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Where does butane work in the home?
**Answer:** In cookers and in lighters — the kitchen flame and the hand flame both burn it.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book prefer gas to charcoal?
**Answer:** To protect the environment: charcoal is made by burning wood, so cooking with gas spares the wood that charcoal cooking consumes.
