# Butane gas and its safe use — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Butane is…
a) A blue liquid  b) A colourless gas  c) A kind of charcoal  d) A solid fuel
**Answer: b) A colourless gas**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Butane is easily…
a) Frozen  b) Liquefied  c) Burned without air  d) Seen
**Answer: b) Liquefied**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Butane is used in…
a) Cookers and lighters  b) Radios and lamps  c) Bicycles  d) Water filters
**Answer: a) Cookers and lighters**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The book prefers gas over charcoal because…
a) Gas is cheaper everywhere  b) It protects the environment — charcoal begins with cut wood  c) Charcoal cannot cook  d) Gas needs no bottle
**Answer: b) It protects the environment — charcoal begins with cut wood**
