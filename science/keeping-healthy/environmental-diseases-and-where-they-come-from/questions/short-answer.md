# Environmental diseases and where they come from — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Environmental diseases — sickness from our surroundings.
**Answer:** An **environmental disease** is a sickness whose cause lies in the environment around us — in what surrounds the body — rather than in a germ passed from person to person.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Where they come from — the surroundings as the source.
**Answer:** The source of an environmental disease is something in the surroundings themselves — in the air breathed, the water used, the substances and conditions a person lives and works among.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Why the sorting decides the answer.
**Answer:** Telling an environmental disease from an infectious one is not naming for its own sake: the cause decides where the answer must act.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Environmental diseases — sickness from our surroundings → Where they come from — the surroundings as the source → Why the sorting decides the answer — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
