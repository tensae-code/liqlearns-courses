# Environmental diseases and where they come from

## Do it first
Australia's disease chapter first taught infectious disease — sickness that spreads. Then it turns to a different family: environmental diseases. If nobody gave it to you, where could a disease come from? Say your answer out loud. Then read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Environmental diseases — sickness from our surroundings

An **environmental disease** is a sickness whose cause lies in the environment around us — in what surrounds the body — rather than in a germ passed from person to person. Australia's book places it as its own section (8.3), after infectious disease (8.1) and other sources of infection (8.2): the chapter's order says the sorting matters — first the diseases that spread, then the diseases that come from where we live.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where they come from — the surroundings as the source

The source of an environmental disease is something in the surroundings themselves — in the air breathed, the water used, the substances and conditions a person lives and works among. Because the source sits in the environment and not in another sick person, these diseases do not travel from person to person the way infectious diseases do: they arise where the harmful condition is, and they keep arising while it stays.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why the sorting decides the answer

Telling an environmental disease from an infectious one is not naming for its own sake: the cause decides where the answer must act. A spreading disease is fought by breaking the spread; an environmental disease is fought by changing the surroundings that cause it. That is Australia's own way in this chapter — sort the disease by where it comes from, and the sorting points at what to fix.

## The key ideas

- **Environmental diseases — sickness from our surroundings** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.
- **Where they come from — the surroundings as the source** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.
- **Why the sorting decides the answer** — as taught above, in the lesson's own order.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, in the lesson's own order. Your companion may stop you once and ask: "A disease comes from the surroundings, not from another sick person. Which family is it — and how is it fought?" Answer: Environmental — and it is fought by changing the surroundings that cause it, not by breaking a spread.
