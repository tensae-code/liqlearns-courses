# Hands-on — Environmental diseases and where they come from

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Surroundings survey (school or home, on foot)
Walk your school compound or your home and list what surrounds people there: the air, the water, the substances and conditions of the place. Beside each item write whether it could be a source of sickness from the surroundings, and why.

## Sort and fix (paper and pencil)
Write down sicknesses you know by name. Sort them into two columns — spreads from person to person, or comes from the surroundings — using only the lesson's sorting question. For one environmental entry, write the change in the surroundings that answers it.
