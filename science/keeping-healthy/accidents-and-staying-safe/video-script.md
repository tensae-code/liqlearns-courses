# Video script — Accidents and staying safe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An **accident** is sudden — a burn, a cut, a flood — not a slow sickness.
Name the **hazards** around you before they name themselves: fire, flood, sharp tools.
Guard the most **vulnerable** first. Seeing early is staying safe.
