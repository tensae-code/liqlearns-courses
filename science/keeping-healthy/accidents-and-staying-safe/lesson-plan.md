# Lesson plan — Accidents and staying safe

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Accidents and staying safe
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — safety at home at Grade 4 depth: sudden accidents, and the things that can cause harm (teacher guide — evidence) (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** accidents are sudden events that can harm — the guides teach pupils to name the things around them that can cause harm (open fire and flood among them) and to understand accident and vulnerability (teacher guide — evidence)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Keeping our bodies clean and the road-safety lessons — dangers met one road at a time there; here the idea itself: accident and risk. This lesson rests on teacher-guide evidence only and says so in its sources.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Keeping Healthy — Keeping our bodies clean. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what an accident is in the guides' teaching: a sudden harmful event (teacher guide — evidence).
2. Name things around the home that can cause harm — open fire and flood are the hazards the SNNP syllabus names.
3. Say that some people and places are more vulnerable — more exposed to harm — than others.

## What you need
A careful walk at home: eyes for hazards, hands kept back.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls every sickness an accident → the guides' word is sudden: accidents arrive in a moment; sickness grows. Both are guarded, differently.
- Names hazards but not vulnerability → the same fire is a tool to the cook and a danger to the toddler; exposure is the second half of the lesson.
