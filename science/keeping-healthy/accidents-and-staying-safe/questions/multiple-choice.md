# Accidents and staying safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. An accident is —**
a) a slow sickness  b) a sudden harmful event  c) a kind of weather
**Answer: b) a sudden harmful event**

**2. Which hazards does the SNNP syllabus name for pupils to identify?**
a) fire and flood  b) snow and fog  c) wind and dew
**Answer: a) fire and flood**

**3. Vulnerability means —**
a) how exposed a person is to harm  b) how strong a person is  c) how fast a person runs
**Answer: a) how exposed a person is to harm**
