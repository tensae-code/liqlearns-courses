# Hands-on — Accidents and staying safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The hazard map
Draw your home's plan, roughly, and mark every sudden-harm thing with a star: fire, knives, water, heights. A map of hazards is the first safety tool.

## Teach the small ones
Choose one hazard and teach a younger child its rule — 'the fire is never a toy' — in words they keep. Teaching is guarding.
