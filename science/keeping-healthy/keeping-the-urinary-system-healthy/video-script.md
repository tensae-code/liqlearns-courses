Today: **Keeping the urinary system healthy** — Kuwait's way: urinary system health — drinking enough water, not holding urine, and the cleanliness that keeps the kidneys, bladder and passages free of infection and stones.

The urinary system — kidneys, ureters, bladder and urethra — filters the blood and carries waste out as urine. It stays healthy first of all with water: drinking enough lets the kidneys wash the waste out instead of letting it concentrate. Kuwait's Grade 8 book follows its excretion lessons with this health lesson, in the same pattern as its circulatory and respiratory health lessons: structure, work, then care. Holding urine for long stretches strains the bladder and lets germs multiply in it; too little water lets salts gather into painful stones; and dirt carried to the passage opening brings infections that burn and can climb toward the kidneys. The care rules answer the harms one by one: drink water through the day; empty the bladder when it calls; keep the body's openings clean, washing front to back; and see a doctor for burning, blood or pain — the system's warnings are never to be waited out.
