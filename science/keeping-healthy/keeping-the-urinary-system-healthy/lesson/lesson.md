## Sub lesson 1 — What the urinary system needs

The urinary system — kidneys, ureters, bladder and urethra — filters the blood and carries waste out as urine. It stays healthy first of all with **water**: drinking enough lets the kidneys wash the waste out instead of letting it concentrate.

Kuwait's Grade 8 book follows its excretion lessons with this health lesson, in the same pattern as its circulatory and respiratory health lessons: structure, work, then care.

## Sub lesson 2 — What harms it

Holding urine for long stretches strains the bladder and lets germs multiply in it; too little water lets salts gather into painful **stones**; and dirt carried to the passage opening brings infections that burn and can climb toward the kidneys.

The care rules answer the harms one by one: drink water through the day; empty the bladder when it calls; keep the body's openings clean, washing front to back; and see a doctor for burning, blood or pain — the system's warnings are never to be waited out.

## The key ideas

- The urinary system filters blood and removes waste as urine.
- Drinking enough water is its first health rule.
- Holding urine, too little water and dirt bring infection and stones.
- Burning, blood or pain when passing urine means: see a doctor.
