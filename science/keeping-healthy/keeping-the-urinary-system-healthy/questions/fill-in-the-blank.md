# Keeping the urinary system healthy — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The kidneys filter the _____.
**Answer: blood**

**2.** Waste leaves the body as _____.
**Answer: urine**

**3.** Too little water can form painful _____.
**Answer: stones**

**4.** Drink _____ through the day.
**Answer: water**
