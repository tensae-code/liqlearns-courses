# Keeping the urinary system healthy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the parts of the urinary system.
**Answer: Kidneys, ureters, bladder and urethra.**

**2.** Why does drinking water protect the system?
**Answer: It lets the kidneys wash waste out diluted, instead of concentrating it into infections and stones.**

**3.** Name three harms to the urinary system.
**Answer: Holding urine too long, drinking too little water, and dirt bringing infection to the passages.**

**4.** What warnings must never be waited out?
**Answer: Burning, blood or pain when passing urine — they must be shown to a doctor.**
