# Keeping the urinary system healthy — multiple choice


**1.** The urinary system's first health rule is…
a) Drink little  b) Drink enough water  c) Hold urine long  d) Avoid water
**Answer: b) Drink enough water**

**2.** Holding urine for long stretches…
a) Strengthens the bladder  b) Lets germs multiply in it  c) Cleans the kidneys  d) Makes stones dissolve
**Answer: b) Lets germs multiply in it**

**3.** Too little water can let salts gather into…
a) Muscles  b) Stones  c) Bones  d) Teeth
**Answer: b) Stones**

**4.** Burning or blood when passing urine means…
a) Wait a year  b) See a doctor  c) Drink nothing  d) Exercise harder
**Answer: b) See a doctor**

**5.** The kidneys' job is to…
a) Make urine from filtered blood waste  b) Pump blood  c) Digest food  d) Carry air
**Answer: a) Make urine from filtered blood waste**

**6.** Cleanliness protects the urinary passages from…
a) Stones only  b) Infection  c) Thirst  d) Sleep
**Answer: b) Infection**
