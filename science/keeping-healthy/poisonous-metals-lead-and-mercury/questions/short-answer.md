# Poisonous metals — lead and mercury — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Two poisonous metals of daily life: lead (car batteries, paints, petrol products, lead crystal glass, radiation shielding) damaging nearly every organ, stored in bone, and hitting children hardest (headache, stomach pain, anaemia, stunted brain development); and mercury (thermometers, barometers, tube lights) whose poisoning (hydrargia, or mercurialism) can block blood vessels, damage brain, kidneys and lungs, and cross the placenta to harm the unborn child.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science and Technology Grade 9 (Nepali, revised 2082)*, printed p. 280.**
