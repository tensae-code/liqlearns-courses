# Drugs and their effects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Cambodia's Grade 8 Biology part gives drugs…
a) their own chapter of two lessons  b) one line in the food chapter  c) no place  d) a Physics lesson
**Answer: a) their own chapter of two lessons** — Biology Ch. 6, Lessons 1–2 — a chapter of its own.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The chapter's first lesson asks…
a) what drugs are  b) how to grow food  c) what minerals are  d) how circuits work
**Answer: a) what drugs are** — Lesson 1: drugs — what they are.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The second lesson teaches the effects of drugs on…
a) the body and on life  b) plants only  c) the weather  d) machines
**Answer: a) the body and on life** — Lesson 2: effects on the body and on life, together.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The drugs chapter closes the book's run on…
a) health — after food, diet and safe food  b) the planets  c) rocks  d) electricity
**Answer: a) health — after food, diet and safe food** — Biology Ch. 5 is food; Ch. 6, drugs, closes the health run.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Drugs in this lesson are substances that…
a) act on the body beyond food's ordinary work  b) are a kind of mineral  c) make food safe  d) clean water
**Answer: a) act on the body beyond food's ordinary work** — That is the first lesson's framing — substances acting on the body, the harmful ones a named danger.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The chapter's teaching order is…
a) know what the thing is, then read its effects  b) effects first, never the thing  c) names only  d) prices first
**Answer: a) know what the thing is, then read its effects** — What they are (Lesson 1) before their effects (Lesson 2).
