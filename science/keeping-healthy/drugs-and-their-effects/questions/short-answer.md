# Drugs and their effects — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what the drugs chapter teaches.
**Answer:** The chapter teaches what drugs are, and then their effects — on the body and on a person's life around the body.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book put the effects on life beside the effects on the body?
**Answer:** Because the harm is whole: drugs damage health and also family, school, work and safety — the book reads both together.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Drugs are substances that act on the body beyond food's ordinary work, the harmful ones a named danger → their effects run through the body and through life — family, school, work and safety — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
