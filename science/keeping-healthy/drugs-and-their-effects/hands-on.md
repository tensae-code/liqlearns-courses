# Hands-on — Drugs and their effects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Body and life — two columns
Draw two columns headed Body and Life. From the lesson, write what drugs damage in each column. Say why the book teaches the two together.

## The chapter's order
Write out the Grade 8 Biology health run in order — food, diet and energy, safe food, then drugs (what they are; their effects). Say what each step adds.
