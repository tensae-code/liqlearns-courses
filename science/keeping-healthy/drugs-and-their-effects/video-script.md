# Video script — Drugs and their effects

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Drugs and their effects** — Cambodia's way, from its Grade 8 book *វិទ្យាសាស្ត្រ ថ្នាក់ទី៨ (Science, Grade 8)*.

Cambodia's Grade 8 book closes its Biology part's health run with a chapter of its own: **drugs** (Biology Ch. 6). Its first lesson asks what drugs are (Lesson 1) — the substances taken into the body that act on it beyond food's ordinary work, the harmful ones among them a danger the book names plainly for its pupils.

The second lesson follows the harm out: **the effects of drugs on the body and on life** (Lesson 2) — what they do to the body's health, and what they do to a person's life around the body: family, school, work and safety. The chapter's order is the teaching: know what the thing is first, then read its effects whole — body and life together.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
