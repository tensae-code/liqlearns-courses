# Hands-on — Keeping food safe — preserving food

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dry it yourself
Lay slices of a fruit or vegetable in the sun on a clean cloth, covered from insects. Look each day and describe the change — this is drying, the oldest preservation.

## Preserved-food hunt
Hunt the kitchen for preserved foods. For each, name the way that preserved it: dried, salted, smoked, cold, or stored covered.
