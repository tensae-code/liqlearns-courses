# Keeping food safe — preserving food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Preserving food means…
a) Eating food faster
b) Holding back spoiling so food stays safe and good for longer
c) Throwing food away
d) Cooking food twice
**Answer: b) Holding back spoiling so food stays safe and good for longer**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Food that is not preserved…
a) Lasts forever
b) Spoils — changes, smells and tastes bad, and can make us ill
c) Turns into seeds
d) Gets heavier only
**Answer: b) Spoils — changes, smells and tastes bad, and can make us ill**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which is a way of preserving food from the lesson?
a) Leaving it open to flies
b) Drying it in the sun, covered from insects
c) Keeping it warm and wet
d) Dropping it in dust
**Answer: b) Drying it in the sun, covered from insects**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The ways of preserving work by…
a) Adding dirt
b) Taking away what spoiling needs — water, warmth, dirt or insects
c) Changing the food's name
d) Painting the food
**Answer: b) Taking away what spoiling needs — water, warmth, dirt or insects**
