# Keeping food safe — preserving food — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The conservation of foods in Portuguese is A _____ dos alimentos.
**Answer: conservação**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Food left as it is will _____ — change and go bad.
**Answer: spoil**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Salting, smoking and _____ in the sun are ways of preserving food.
**Answer: drying**
