# Keeping food safe — preserving food — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why do people preserve food?
**Answer:** Because food left as it is spoils and can make us ill; preserving holds the spoiling back so food stays safe and good for longer than the day it was made or harvested.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) How does drying preserve food?
**Answer:** Drying takes water out of the food, and spoiling needs that water — with the water gone, the change that ruins food slows right down.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does Angola teach this in Grade 2 and again in Grade 5?
**Answer:** The young child first learns the conservation of foods among the food-care lessons; the older child returns to it and deepens it — choosing the way that fits the food and the place, and checking preserved food before eating.
