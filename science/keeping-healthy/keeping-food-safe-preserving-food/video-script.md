Today: **Keeping food safe — preserving food** — Angola's way: learn it with food care in Grade 2, deepen it in Grade 5.

Food left as it is spoils. Preserving food holds the spoiling back: drying, salting, smoking, keeping cold, covering and storing — each way takes away something spoiling needs.

So preserved food stays safe and good for longer — and it is still checked before eating, like all food.
