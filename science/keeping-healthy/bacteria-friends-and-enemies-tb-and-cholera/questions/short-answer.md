# Bacteria — friends and enemies: TB and cholera — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Bacteria.
**Answer:** bacteria: found in the soil, the water of rivers and seas, the air and food, and inside the human body and on the skin — single-celled microscopic living things; among them useful bacteria (those in the digestive system, and the milk bacteria) and harmful ones, like those causing sore throat and pneumonia, tuberculosis (a chronic infectious disease settling in the lung) and cholera (spread by drinking polluted water, causing severe diarrhoea and dehydration).
