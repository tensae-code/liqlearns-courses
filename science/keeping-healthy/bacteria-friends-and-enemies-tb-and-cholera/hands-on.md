# Hands-on — Bacteria — friends and enemies: TB and cholera

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: bacteria: found in the soil, the water of rivers and seas, the air and food, and inside the human body and on the skin — single-celled microscopic living things; among them useful bacteria (those in the digestive system, and the milk bacteria) and harmful ones, like those causing sore throat and pneumonia, tuberculosis (a chronic infectious disease settling in the lung) and cholera (spread by drinking polluted water, causing severe diarrhoea and dehydration) Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
