# Lesson plan — Recommended Dietary Allowance and BMI

**Course:** Science · **Title:** keeping-healthy · **Lesson:** Recommended Dietary Allowance and BMI
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) and Body Mass Index (BMI) — the two measures Malaysia's books use to say how much food a body needs and whether its mass fits its height
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **The Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA)** — Malaysia's Home Science Form 4 book teaches the **Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) table** — the recommended amounts that guide what a person should eat — as the measure standing behind a balanced diet's planning.
2. **Body Mass Index — calculating it, and reading what it says** — The same book, and Malaysia's Form 4 Science book, teach **Body Mass Index: BMI calculated from mass and height**, with a worked example. **Interpreting BMI**: overweight raises the risk of **heart attack, high blood pressure, stroke and diabetes mellitus** — and the Home Science book adds ways to overcome overweight.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Mixes RDA and BMI up → return to the sub lessons: the RDA table says what to eat; BMI is calculated from mass and height and read for what it warns of.
- Forgets the risks BMI points to → return to sub lesson 2: heart attack, high blood pressure, stroke and diabetes mellitus.
