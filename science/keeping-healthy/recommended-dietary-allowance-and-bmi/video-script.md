Today: **Recommended Dietary Allowance and BMI** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 10 (Form 4) — *Sains Rumah Tangga Tingkatan 4*, printed p. 184).

Malaysia's Home Science Form 4 book teaches the **Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) table** — the recommended amounts that guide what a person should eat — as the measure standing behind a balanced diet's planning.

The same book, and Malaysia's Form 4 Science book, teach **Body Mass Index: BMI calculated from mass and height**, with a worked example. **Interpreting BMI**: overweight raises the risk of **heart attack, high blood pressure, stroke and diabetes mellitus** — and the Home Science book adds ways to overcome overweight.
