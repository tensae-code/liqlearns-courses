# Recommended Dietary Allowance and BMI — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA).
**Answer:** Malaysia's Home Science Form 4 book teaches the **Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) table** — the recommended amounts that guide what a person should eat — as the measure standing behind a balanced diet's planning.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Body Mass Index — calculating it, and reading what it says.
**Answer:** The same book, and Malaysia's Form 4 Science book, teach **Body Mass Index: BMI calculated from mass and height**, with a worked example. **Interpreting BMI**: overweight raises the risk of **heart attack, high blood pressure, stroke and diabetes mellitus** — and the Home Science book adds ways to overcome overweight.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) → Body Mass Index — calculating it, and reading what it says — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
