# Hands-on — Fire safety and escape

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the escape before you need it
At home or at school, with an adult: choose the safe place to flee to — outside, clear of the building — then walk the escape from where you sleep and from where you learn: out of the room, out of the building, straight to the safe place. Time nothing and race nobody; the job is to know the way and the destination with your feet, before any fire.
