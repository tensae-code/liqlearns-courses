# Fire safety and escape — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: If your clothes catch fire.
**Answer:** Laos’s Grade 3 book (Ch. 3, printed pp. 13–16) opens its fire teaching with the person, not the building: **putting out burning clothes you are wearing**. When the fire is on your own clothes, the first danger is to you, and the first job is to put that fire out.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Escaping from a fire — and the safe place.
**Answer:** The chapter continues: **escaping from a fire place**, and **the safe place to flee to**. Escape is not just getting out — it is getting out to a known safe place, chosen before any fire: fleeing has a destination.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** If your clothes catch fire → Escaping from a fire — and the safe place — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
