# Ergonomics and safe lifting at work

## Sub lesson 1 — Ergonomics — work fitted to the worker

Malaysia's Home Science Form 5 book defines **ergonomics (from the Greek ergon + nomoi)**: fitting the workplace, equipment and movement to the worker, to protect safety, health and comfort — the work is shaped to the person, not the person forced to the work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Safe lifting — judge the load, then lift with posture

**Before lifting**: judge the load's weight, size, shape and distance, and the help needed. Then lift with **correct posture for shoulders, head and neck, hands, back and legs**, inside the **load-capacity limits by body height given for men and women**.
