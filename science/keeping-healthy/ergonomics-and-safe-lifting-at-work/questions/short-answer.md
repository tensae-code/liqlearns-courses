# Ergonomics and safe lifting at work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Ergonomics — work fitted to the worker.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Home Science Form 5 book defines **ergonomics (from the Greek ergon + nomoi)**: fitting the workplace, equipment and movement to the worker, to protect safety, health and comfort — the work is shaped to the person, not the person forced to the work.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Safe lifting — judge the load, then lift with posture.
**Answer:** **Before lifting**: judge the load's weight, size, shape and distance, and the help needed. Then lift with **correct posture for shoulders, head and neck, hands, back and legs**, inside the **load-capacity limits by body height given for men and women**.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Ergonomics — work fitted to the worker → Safe lifting — judge the load, then lift with posture — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
