# Emotional resilience after a disaster — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Emotional resilience" teach?
**Answer:** Every Zimbabwe guide in this set — ECD A and B and Grades 1 to 5 — closes its hazards run with **emotional resilience**: after the danger itself, the feelings remain, and they are taught as part of the topic, not left aside.

**2.** What does the part "Psycho-emotional support" teach?
**Answer:** Grades 4 and 5 name the help directly: **psycho-emotional support** — the support a person needs for their feelings after a disaster, taught as its own unit beside the resilience unit.

**3.** What does the part "Recovering — building back" teach?
**Answer:** Resilience is the building back: a person who has lived through a disaster returns to strength with support and time.
