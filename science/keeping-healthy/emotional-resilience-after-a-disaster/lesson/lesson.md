# Emotional resilience after a disaster

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — emotional resilience after a disaster — the feelings a disaster leaves, the support that helps, and recovering, as Zimbabwe's guides teach it at every level. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Emotional resilience

Every Zimbabwe guide in this set — ECD A and B and Grades 1 to 5 — closes its hazards run with **emotional resilience**: after the danger itself, the feelings remain, and they are taught as part of the topic, not left aside.

## Sub lesson 2 — Psycho-emotional support

Grades 4 and 5 name the help directly: **psycho-emotional support** — the support a person needs for their feelings after a disaster, taught as its own unit beside the resilience unit.

## Sub lesson 3 — Recovering — building back

Resilience is the building back: a person who has lived through a disaster returns to strength with support and time. Zimbabwe's own way is to teach that recovery at every level, from ECD to Grade 5.

## The key ideas

- **Emotional resilience** — feelings after a disaster are part of the topic.
- **Psycho-emotional support** — support for feelings after a disaster.
- **Recovering — building back** — recovering with support and time.
