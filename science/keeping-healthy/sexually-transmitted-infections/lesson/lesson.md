# Sexually transmitted infections

## Do it first
Some infections pass between people in one particular way — through sexual contact. Why would a book teach those infections together, as one group? Say your answer out loud. Then read on: this is Angola's way in — its Grade 6 *Ciências da Natureza* book teaches the sexually transmitted infections (As infecções sexualmente transmissíveis) whole: their notion, symptoms, prevention and treatment, just before its HIV/AIDS lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — What sexually transmitted infections are

**Sexually transmitted infections** (infecções sexualmente transmissíveis) are infections that pass from person to person **through sexual contact**. Angola's Grade 6 book gives them their own lesson and begins with the **notion** — what they are as a group: many different infections, one road of passing. Because the road is shared, what guards against one largely guards against all — which is why they are taught together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Symptoms, prevention and treatment

The book then runs the group's three practical questions. **Symptoms**: signs that an infection may be present — and the warning that signs can be absent while the infection is still there and still passing. **Prevention**: the ways of blocking the shared road, so the infections do not pass. **Treatment**: infections of this group are treated at health services — early, and by both partners — because untreated they deepen and keep passing. HIV/AIDS, the next lesson in the book, is then met as the best-known member of this group.

## The key ideas

- **Sexually transmitted infections** pass between people **through sexual contact** — many infections, one shared road.
- **Symptoms** may be present — or absent while the infection still passes; absence of signs is not safety.
- **Prevention** blocks the shared road; **treatment** is early, at health services, and for both partners.
