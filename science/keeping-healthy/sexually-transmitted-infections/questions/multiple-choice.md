# Sexually transmitted infections — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sexually transmitted infections pass between people…
a) Through water
b) Through sexual contact
c) Through air
d) Through food
**Answer: b) Through sexual contact**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) They are taught as one group because…
a) They are all the same germ
b) They share one road of passing, so prevention follows one pattern
c) They are rare
d) They have no symptoms ever
**Answer: b) They share one road of passing, so prevention follows one pattern**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) About symptoms, the lesson warns that…
a) Symptoms always appear at once
b) Signs can be absent while the infection is still there and still passing
c) Symptoms are imaginary
d) Only one symptom exists
**Answer: b) Signs can be absent while the infection is still there and still passing**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Treatment for these infections should be…
a) Delayed for years
b) Early, at health services, and for both partners
c) Done alone at home only
d) Avoided
**Answer: b) Early, at health services, and for both partners**
