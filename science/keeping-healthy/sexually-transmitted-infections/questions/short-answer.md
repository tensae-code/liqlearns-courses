# Sexually transmitted infections — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the "notion" the book begins with?
**Answer:** That sexually transmitted infections are a group: many different infections that share one road of passing — sexual contact — and are therefore taught together.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why must both partners be treated?
**Answer:** Because the infection passes between them: treating only one leaves the other carrying it, and it passes back; treated together and early, it is stopped and does not deepen.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Where does this lesson sit in Angola's Grade 6 book?
**Answer:** Just before the HIV/AIDS lesson — the group is taught whole first, then its best-known member is met in its own lesson.
