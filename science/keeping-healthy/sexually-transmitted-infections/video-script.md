Today: **Sexually transmitted infections** — Angola's way: the group whole first, then HIV/AIDS in its own lesson.

These are infections that pass through sexual contact — many infections, one shared road. That is why they are taught together.

Know the symptoms — and that signs can be absent while infection passes. Block the road by prevention, and treat early, both partners, at health services.
