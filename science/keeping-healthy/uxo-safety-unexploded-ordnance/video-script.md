Today: **UXO safety — unexploded ordnance** — Laos's way, from its Science and Environment books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

Laos’s books open with the thing itself: bombs and unexploded ordnance (UXO) left in Laos from the war years are still in the ground. The Grade 2 book teaches the **kinds of bombs**; the Grade 3 book teaches their **shapes and colours** and the **places where they still remain**.

The Grade 2 book (Ch. 2) names the causes of injury and death from UXO plainly: **digging** in ground where a bomb lies, **fires** set over buried ordnance, **dismantling** a found bomb, and **clearing grass** where one is hidden.

The Grade 2 book teaches **what to do in risky areas**; the Grade 1 teacher guide adds the clearest rule of all, as guide evidence: if you see a bomb or a bomb-like thing, **do not touch it** — the teacher instructs this clearly (guide p. 148).
