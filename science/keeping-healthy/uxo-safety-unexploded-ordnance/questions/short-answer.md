# UXO safety — unexploded ordnance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The bombs still in the ground.
**Answer:** Laos’s books open with the thing itself: bombs and unexploded ordnance (UXO) left in Laos from the war years are still in the ground. The Grade 2 book teaches the **kinds of bombs**; the Grade 3 book teaches their **shapes and colours** and the **places where they still remain**.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: How people get hurt.
**Answer:** The Grade 2 book (Ch. 2) names the causes of injury and death from UXO plainly: **digging** in ground where a bomb lies, **fires** set over buried ordnance, **dismantling** a found bomb, and **clearing grass** where one is hidden.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: What to do — in risky areas, and when you see one.
**Answer:** The Grade 2 book teaches **what to do in risky areas**; the Grade 1 teacher guide adds the clearest rule of all, as guide evidence: if you see a bomb or a bomb-like thing, **do not touch it** — the teacher instructs this clearly (guide p. 148).

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The bombs still in the ground → How people get hurt → What to do — in risky areas, and when you see one — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
