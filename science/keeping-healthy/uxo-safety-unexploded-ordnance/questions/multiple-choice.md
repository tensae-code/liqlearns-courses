# UXO safety — unexploded ordnance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The bombs still in the ground  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The bombs still in the ground**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) How people get hurt  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) How people get hurt**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What to do — in risky areas, and when you see one  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What to do — in risky areas, and when you see one**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: uxo safety — unexploded ordnance. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons’ own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons’ own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The books behind this lesson are…
a) Laos’s Science and Environment books (Ministry of Education and Sports, Lao PDR)  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Laos’s Science and Environment books (Ministry of Education and Sports, Lao PDR)**
