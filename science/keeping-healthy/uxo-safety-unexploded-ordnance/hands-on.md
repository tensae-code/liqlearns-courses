# Hands-on — UXO safety — unexploded ordnance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The safe simulation — and the sorting game
With a teacher, and only ever with safe classroom things: burst a flour- or rice-filled balloon, as Laos’s Grade 3 book does, and talk about what the burst shows about a real bomb — from a safe distance, with nothing real anywhere near. Then play the Grade 2 guide’s sorting game: a box of fruit pictures and bomb pictures — sort them, and say the rule out loud for every bomb picture: do not touch.
