# Lesson plan — Fire extinguishers — types and how to use one

**Course:** Science · **Title:** keeping-healthy · **Lesson:** Fire extinguishers — types and how to use one
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fire extinguishers — the types matched to fire classes, the fire blanket, and how to use an extinguisher (the PASS sequence), with the audit that keeps a school's extinguishers ready
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Types of extinguisher, and the fire blanket** — Malaysia's Form 4 book matches **fire extinguisher types to fire classes**, and teaches the **fire blanket — two layers of glass fibre** — for small fires and for wrapping a burning victim. The Home Science Form 5 book adds the workplace frame: high-risk rooms (kitchen, gas store), the causes of fire (faulty wiring, leaking gas, careless cooking, smoking, faulty appliances), and heat and smoke detectors.
2. **Using an extinguisher — PASS — and the audit** — The procedure: **remove the safety pin, keep the extinguisher upright, aim the nozzle** — the **PASS sequence (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep)**. Readiness is kept by work, not luck: pupils **carry out an audit on the school's fire extinguishers**, and build a simple fire extinguisher as an activity.
