# Fire extinguishers — types and how to use one — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Types of extinguisher, and the fire blanket.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Form 4 book matches **fire extinguisher types to fire classes**, and teaches the **fire blanket — two layers of glass fibre** — for small fires and for wrapping a burning victim. The Home Science Form 5 book adds the workplace frame: high-risk rooms (kitchen, gas store), the causes of fire (faulty wiring, leaking gas, careless cooking, smoking, faulty appliances), and heat and smoke detectors.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Using an extinguisher — PASS — and the audit.
**Answer:** The procedure: **remove the safety pin, keep the extinguisher upright, aim the nozzle** — the **PASS sequence (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep)**. Readiness is kept by work, not luck: pupils **carry out an audit on the school's fire extinguishers**, and build a simple fire extinguisher as an activity.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Types of extinguisher, and the fire blanket → Using an extinguisher — PASS — and the audit — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
