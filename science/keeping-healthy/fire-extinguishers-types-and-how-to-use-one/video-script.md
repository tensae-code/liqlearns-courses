Today: **Fire extinguishers — types and how to use one** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 10 (Form 4) — *Sains Tingkatan 4*, printed p. 10).

Malaysia's Form 4 book matches **fire extinguisher types to fire classes**, and teaches the **fire blanket — two layers of glass fibre** — for small fires and for wrapping a burning victim. The Home Science Form 5 book adds the workplace frame: high-risk rooms (kitchen, gas store), the causes of fire (faulty wiring, leaking gas, careless cooking, smoking, faulty appliances), and heat and smoke detectors.

The procedure: **remove the safety pin, keep the extinguisher upright, aim the nozzle** — the **PASS sequence (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep)**. Readiness is kept by work, not luck: pupils **carry out an audit on the school's fire extinguishers**, and build a simple fire extinguisher as an activity.
