Today: **Diseases of malnutrition** — Mauritania's way: the sickness that comes from food that fails the body.

Malnutrition is not one missed meal. It is food failing the body over time — too little, or missing what the body needs — and its first named sign is weight loss, the body drawing on itself.

A deficiency disease is the lack made visible: one needed thing missing from the food, continued, until the body sickens. Mauritania's Grade 6 book goes back over the younger years' deficiency diseases and deepens them — then answers them with the balanced diet and the day's rations.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
