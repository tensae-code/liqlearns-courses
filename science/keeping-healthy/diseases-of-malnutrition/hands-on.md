# Hands-on — Diseases of malnutrition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the answer plate
Draw the day's food rations as the Grade 6 book teaches them next — a balanced day's plate — and beside it write which malnutrition each part of the plate guards against.

## Spot the missing part
Take three simple daily menus. For each, name one needed thing that is missing or too small, and say what continued lack of it would do.
