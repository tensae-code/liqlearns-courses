# Diseases of malnutrition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is malnutrition a pattern and not a single event?
**Answer:** Because it grows from food failing the body over time — continued lack — not from one missed meal; its diseases develop out of that continuing failure.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What does 'revisited and deepened' mean for the Grade 6 teaching?
**Answer:** The deficiency diseases first met in the third and fourth years are taken up again, now with the full chain tied together — missing nutrient, failing body work, disease — and the balanced diet as the preventing answer.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How does the balanced diet prevent deficiency diseases?
**Answer:** By giving the body every needed thing in the day's rations, so no single needed part stays missing long enough to become disease.
