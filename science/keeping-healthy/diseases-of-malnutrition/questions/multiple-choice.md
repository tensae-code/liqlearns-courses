# Diseases of malnutrition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Malnutrition is…
a) One missed meal  b) A body whose food fails it over time — too little, or missing what it needs  c) Eating too fast  d) A disease caught from others
**Answer: b) A body whose food fails it over time — too little, or missing what it needs**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The first named sign of malnutrition in the Grade 6 run is…
a) Fever  b) Weight loss  c) A cough  d) Toothache
**Answer: b) Weight loss**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) A deficiency disease is caused by…
a) A germ in water  b) The continued lack of one needed thing in the food  c) Cold weather  d) Too much sleep
**Answer: b) The continued lack of one needed thing in the food**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The Grade 6 book answers malnutrition with…
a) Medicines only  b) The balanced diet and the day's food rations  c) Skipping meals  d) Eating one food only
**Answer: b) The balanced diet and the day's food rations**
