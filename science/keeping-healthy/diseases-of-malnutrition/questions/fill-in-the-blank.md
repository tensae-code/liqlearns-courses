# Diseases of malnutrition — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The state of a body whose food fails it over time is _____.
**Answer: malnutrition**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A sickness caused by the lack of one needed thing in the food is a _____ disease.
**Answer: deficiency**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The body not fed what it needs draws on itself and shows _____ loss.
**Answer: weight**
