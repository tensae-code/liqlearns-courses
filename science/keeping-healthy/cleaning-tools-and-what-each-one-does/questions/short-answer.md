# Cleaning tools and what each one does — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Tools for cleaning our bodies.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 2 book names the cleaning items themselves: **toothbrush and toothpaste, soap, towel, comb and handkerchief** — and which of them are **personal items never to be shared**. Each item has its own job on the body, and the lesson is learning which item does which job.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Tools for cleaning our surroundings.
**Answer:** The Primary 4 book names the tools for cleaning the environment: **broom, bucket, mop, rake, cloth, brush, slasher, hoe, panga and wheelbarrow** — and where each is used: the rake to the compound, the mop to the classroom, the wheelbarrow to the areas around the school; the slasher keeps grass short against mosquitoes and rodents.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Choosing the right tool for the job.
**Answer:** The two runs teach one rule together: a cleaning job is done with **its own tool** — the body's tools are personal and are not shared, and the surroundings' tools are matched to the place being cleaned. Naming the tool and its job, before the cleaning starts, is the lesson's one thing.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Tools for cleaning our bodies → Tools for cleaning our surroundings → Choosing the right tool for the job — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
