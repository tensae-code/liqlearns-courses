## Sub lessons
1. **Honduras's Special Law on VIH-SIDA** — Honduras's Special Law on VIH-SIDA: published in the Diario Oficial La Gaceta number 29,020 of Saturday 13 November 1999, by Decree No. 147-99, because the AIDS epidemic in the country demands implementing measures for the population to be educated and informed on the measures of prevention and control of the epidemic; its purpose is to contribute to the protection and integral promotion of people's health; the law contains 87 articles including everything respecting the rights and duties of the people who suffer this disease (guide evidence)
2. **The law's Articles 34–37** — the law's Articles 34–37: Article 34, the prevention of HIV transmission is the responsibility of the State, its institutions and the organised civil society; Article 35, the VIH/SIDA prevention actions must be realised in a coordinated form by the superior instance this Law establishes; Article 36, establishments such as hotels, motels or any other bed service must follow the prevention norms established in the respective regulation, otherwise the corresponding fine will be imposed through the municipality; Article 37, the Secretaría de Estado en el Despacho de Salud, through the National AIDS Commission (CONASIDA), will prepare a list of the medicines, vaccines, biological products, materials and equipment that have demonstrated effectiveness in the specific treatment of the HIV/AIDS infection, to manage the exoneration of the corresponding customs taxes for their importation (guide evidence)
