# Hands-on — A law to protect people with HIV and AIDS

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it like the book does
Go back to the lesson's first part — Honduras's Special Law on VIH-SIDA. Honduras's Special Law on VIH-SIDA: published in the Diario Oficial La Gaceta number 29,020 of Saturday 13 November 1999, by Decree No. 147-99, because the AIDS epidemic in the country demands implementing measures for the population to be educated and informed on the measures of prevention and control of the epidemic; its purpose is to contribute to the protection and integral promotion of people's health; the law contains 87 articles including everything respecting the rights and duties of the people who suffer this disease (guide evidence) Now do the same looking, comparing or trying with things near you, and write down what you find.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a companion the main thing this lesson teaches, using real things near you — point, move, sort or build as the lesson's subject needs — and let them ask one question. Answer from the lesson.
