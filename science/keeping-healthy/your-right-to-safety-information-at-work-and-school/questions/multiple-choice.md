# Your right to safety information at work and school — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about your right to safety information at work and school?
a) Every person at work or at school has the right to be told about the hazards around them and how to stay safe from them — safety information is a right, not a favour.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Every person at work or at school has the right to be told about the hazards around them and how to stay safe from them — safety information is a right, not a favour.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about a right to know?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) People who work or learn in a place have the right to know what dangers that place holds — which substances are hazardous, which machines can hurt, where the fire equipment and exits are — and to be taught how to protect themselves.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) People who work or learn in a place have the right to know what dangers that place holds — which substances are hazardous, which machines can hurt, where the fire equipment and exits are — and to be taught how to protect themselves.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about labels and instructions are part of the right?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Hazard signs, warning labels on containers, and safety instructions are how the right is delivered.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Hazard signs, warning labels on containers, and safety instructions are how the right is delivered.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about asking is exercising the right?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) If the information is missing, the safe and proper move is to ask — a teacher, a supervisor, a safety officer — before starting.
**Answer: d) If the information is missing, the safe and proper move is to ask — a teacher, a supervisor, a safety officer — before starting.**
