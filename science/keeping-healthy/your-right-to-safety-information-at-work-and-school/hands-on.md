# Hands-on — Your right to safety information at work and school

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Look around your classroom or home for hazard labels and safety signs. List each one and what it tells you. Then list one place where safety information is missing — an unlabelled container, an unmarked exit — and write who you would ask about it.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of your right to safety information at work and school — every person at work or at school has the right to be told about the hazards around them and how to stay safe from them — safety information is a right, not a favour. — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
