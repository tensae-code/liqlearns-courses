# Your right to safety information at work and school — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (every person at work or at school has the right to be told about the hazards around them and how to stay safe from them — safety information is a right, not a favour.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
