# Preventing infectious diseases

## Sub lesson 1 — Strengthen the body, block the roads

Prevention works on both ends: strengthen the immune system with a balanced diet, safe water and handwashing; and block the roads — ventilate rooms, and cover coughs and sneezes with a tissue or an elbow.

## Sub lesson 2 — Remove the breeding places, vaccinate

Tubs, tyres and cans holding water are where dengue and malaria mosquitoes lay their eggs — removing them removes the carriers before they fly. And vaccination teaches the body ahead of time, so the disease finds it ready.

## Sub lesson 3 — When illness comes anyway

If illness comes anyway: rest and good food while ill, and see a doctor if fever will not settle. Prevention is the first lesson; knowing when home care is not enough is part of it.
