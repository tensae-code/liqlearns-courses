# Preventing infectious diseases — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Strengthen the body, block the roads.
**Answer:** Prevention works on both ends: strengthen the immune system with a balanced diet, safe water and handwashing; and block the roads — ventilate rooms, and cover coughs and sneezes with a tissue or an elbow..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Remove the breeding places, vaccinate.
**Answer:** Tubs, tyres and cans holding water are where dengue and malaria mosquitoes lay their eggs — removing them removes the carriers before they fly.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: When illness comes anyway.
**Answer:** If illness comes anyway: rest and good food while ill, and see a doctor if fever will not settle.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Strengthen the body, block the roads → Remove the breeding places, vaccinate → When illness comes anyway — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
