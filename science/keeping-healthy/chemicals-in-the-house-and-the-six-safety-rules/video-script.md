# Video script — Chemicals in the house and the six safety rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your home is full of **chemicals** — useful, and some **poisonous**.
Children touch, then hands go to mouths. That is the child's special danger.
Six rules guard the house: buy little, read the label, handle with care, store high, dispose carefully, teach the children.
