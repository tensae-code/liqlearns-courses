# Lesson plan — Chemicals in the house and the six safety rules

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Chemicals in the house and the six safety rules
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — safety at home at Grade 4 depth: the chemicals already standing on our shelves (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** homes hold chemicals — berekina (bleach), fertilisers, and many more — some poisonous through careless handling, and children are poisoned easily because they touch chemicals and put hands to mouths; the book's six rules keep everyone safe
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Keeping our bodies clean — the home kept healthy there; here its dangers are named and ruled.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name household chemicals from the book's list: berekina (bleach), fertilisers, food salt, sugar, vinegar, flit insecticide, aspirin, DDT, cosmetics and more.
2. Say why children are in special danger: they touch chemicals and put their hands to their mouths, and poison easily.
3. Recite the six safety rules: buy only what will be used directly; buy only the amount needed; read and follow the caution label before use; handle with care; store out of children's reach and dispose of unwanted chemicals carefully; teach children safe handling.

## What you need
A look (not a touch) at the home's own chemical shelf — from a safe distance, with an adult.

## If the learner is stuck
- Sorts all fifteen as poisons → the book's table has two columns; salt, sugar and toothpaste stand on the other side. Careless handling is the hinge.
- Recites rules but stores nothing → rule five is the test at home: out of reach, and unwanted chemicals disposed of carefully.
