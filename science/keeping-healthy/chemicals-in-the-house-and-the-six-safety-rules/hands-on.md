# Hands-on — Chemicals in the house and the six safety rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The safe-distance survey
With an adult, walk the home and list where each chemical lives. Never open or handle unknown containers — the survey is done with eyes, from a safe distance.

## Rule cards
Write the six rules on six cards. Pin them (with leave) where the chemicals live — the rules at their workplace.
