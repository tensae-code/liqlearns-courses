# Chemicals in the house and the six safety rules — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Some cleaning chemicals are poisonous.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Chemicals should be stored where small children can reach them easily.**
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) One of the six rules is to teach children how to handle chemicals safely.**
**Answer: True**
