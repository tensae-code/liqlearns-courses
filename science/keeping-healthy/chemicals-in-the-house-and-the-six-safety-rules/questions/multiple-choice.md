# Chemicals in the house and the six safety rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Berekina is —**
a) a food  b) bleach, a cleaning chemical  c) a medicine for children
**Answer: b) bleach, a cleaning chemical**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Children poison easily with chemicals because —**
a) they touch chemicals and put hands to their mouths  b) they are taller  c) they read labels
**Answer: a) they touch chemicals and put hands to their mouths**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In the book's sorting, which pair does NOT harm through careless handling the way poisons do?**
a) salt and sugar  b) DDT and flit  c) berekina and crop-pest killer
**Answer: a) salt and sugar**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The first two safety rules are about —**
a) storing  b) buying only what will be used, and only the amount needed  c) teaching
**Answer: b) buying only what will be used, and only the amount needed**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Before using a chemical, you should —**
a) shake it hard  b) read and follow the caution label  c) pour it into a cup
**Answer: b) read and follow the caution label**
