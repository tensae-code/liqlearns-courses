# Planning meals and a food budget — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Meal planning" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 4 and Grade 5 guides both teach **meal planning** as a named unit: meals are planned ahead, not decided by chance at the cooking fire.

**2.** What does the part "Budgeting for food" teach?
**Answer:** The unit's own full title is meal planning **and budgeting**: the plan must fit the money there is, and the budgeting is taught as part of the same skill.

**3.** What does the part "A plan that feeds well" teach?
**Answer:** Planning and budgeting join in one test: the planned meals must still feed the family well.
