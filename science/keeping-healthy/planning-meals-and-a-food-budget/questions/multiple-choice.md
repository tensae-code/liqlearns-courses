# Planning meals and a food budget — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Budgeting for food  b) A plan that feeds well  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Meal planning
**Answer: d) Meal planning**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about meals planned ahead?
a) Meal planning  b) Budgeting for food  c) A plan that feeds well
**Answer: a) Meal planning**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about the plan fitting the money?
a) Meal planning  b) Budgeting for food  c) A plan that feeds well
**Answer: b) Budgeting for food**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about planned, budgeted, and still feeding well?
a) Meal planning  b) Budgeting for food  c) A plan that feeds well
**Answer: c) A plan that feeds well**
