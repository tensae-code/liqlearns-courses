## Sub lesson 1 — Meal planning

Zimbabwe's Grade 4 and Grade 5 guides both teach **meal planning** as a named unit: meals are planned ahead, not decided by chance at the cooking fire.

## Sub lesson 2 — Budgeting for food

The unit's own full title is meal planning **and budgeting**: the plan must fit the money there is, and the budgeting is taught as part of the same skill.

## Sub lesson 3 — A plan that feeds well

Planning and budgeting join in one test: the planned meals must still feed the family well. That test is the lesson's whole point — a cheap plan that starves is not a plan.

## The key ideas

- **Meal planning** — meals planned ahead.
- **Budgeting for food** — the plan fitting the money.
- **A plan that feeds well** — planned, budgeted, and still feeding well.
