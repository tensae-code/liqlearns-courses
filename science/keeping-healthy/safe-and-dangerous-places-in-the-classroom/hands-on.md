# Hands-on — Safe and dangerous places in the classroom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's own work
Do the work El Salvador's book sets for this lesson, as the sources describe it: spotting dangerous places/objects in the classroom (glass windows, bookcases, ceiling lamps) and safe places i. Record what you did and what it showed.

## Say it and show it
Draw or build the lesson's central thing, label its parts, and explain it to someone at home in your own words.
