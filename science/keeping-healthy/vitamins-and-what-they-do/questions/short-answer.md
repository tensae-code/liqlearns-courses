# Vitamins and what they do — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The vitamin table.
**Answer:** Bangladesh's Class 4 book teaches the vitamins in one table: A, B-complex, C, D, E and K — each vitamin with the foods that carry it, the job it does in the body, and the disease that comes when it is missing..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: What goes wrong without them.
**Answer:** The table's warning column names the sicknesses of absence: without vitamin A, night blindness; without B-complex, beriberi; without C, scurvy; without D, rickets; and goitre where iodine is missing.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Eating so no row is empty.
**Answer:** The lesson of the table is a plate that covers every row: different foods carry different vitamins, so eating different foods — not more of one food — is how the body gets them all..

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The vitamin table → What goes wrong without them → Eating so no row is empty — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
