# Vitamins and what they do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The vitamin table  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The vitamin table**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) What goes wrong without them  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) What goes wrong without them**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Eating so no row is empty  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Eating so no row is empty**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: vitamins A, B-complex, C, D, E and K in one table — their food sources, their jobs in the body, and the deficiency diseases that come when they are missing — as Bangladesh's Class 4 book teaches them. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 29  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 29**
