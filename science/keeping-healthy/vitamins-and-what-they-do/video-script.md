Today: **Vitamins and what they do** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 29).

Bangladesh's Class 4 book teaches the vitamins in one table: A, B-complex, C, D, E and K — each vitamin with the foods that carry it, the job it does in the body, and the disease that comes when it is missing.
The table's warning column names the sicknesses of absence: without vitamin A, night blindness; without B-complex, beriberi; without C, scurvy; without D, rickets; and goitre where iodine is missing. A missing vitamin or mineral shows itself as a named sickness.
The lesson of the table is a plate that covers every row: different foods carry different vitamins, so eating different foods — not more of one food — is how the body gets them all.
