## Sub lesson 1 — Free radicals — how they form, and what they do

Malaysia's Form 4 book defines **free radicals**, teaches **how they form and are found in the body**, and sets out **their effects on human health** — the damage side of oxidation, named first so the defence can be understood.

## Sub lesson 2 — Antioxidants — slowing oxidation down

**Antioxidants** are substances that **slow or stop oxidation and protect body cells from free-radical damage**; the book shows **how an antioxidant neutralises a free radical into a stable compound**. Its experiment asks which solutions slow the **oxidation (browning) of apple pieces**, the antioxidant solutions as the variable.
