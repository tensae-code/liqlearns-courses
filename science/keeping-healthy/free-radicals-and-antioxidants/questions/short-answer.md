# Free radicals and antioxidants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Free radicals — how they form, and what they do.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Form 4 book defines **free radicals**, teaches **how they form and are found in the body**, and sets out **their effects on human health** — the damage side of oxidation, named first so the defence can be understood.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Antioxidants — slowing oxidation down.
**Answer:** **Antioxidants** are substances that **slow or stop oxidation and protect body cells from free-radical damage**; the book shows **how an antioxidant neutralises a free radical into a stable compound**. Its experiment asks which solutions slow the **oxidation (browning) of apple pieces**, the antioxidant solutions as the variable.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Free radicals — how they form, and what they do → Antioxidants — slowing oxidation down — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
