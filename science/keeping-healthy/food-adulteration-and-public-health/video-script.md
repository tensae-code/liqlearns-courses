Today: **Food adulteration and public health** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 8 — *Science Exercise Book Class 8*, printed p. 149).

Food adulteration is cheating with food: food and daily necessities adulterated — mixed, thinned or faked — for profit. Bangladesh's Class 8 Exercise Book gives it a unit of its own, named with an exclamation: Food Adulteration!
The harm is not only to the purse. Adulterated food harms public health — many people, eating in trust, made ill by what should have fed them. That is why the book teaches it as a public subject, not a private warning.
As an Exercise Book unit, the lesson is worked through in sessions: learners examine, discuss and check, so that spotting adulteration becomes a practised skill, not a memorised line.
