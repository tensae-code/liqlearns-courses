# My first 1000 days — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner works through the lesson's sub lessons in order (The first 1000 days shape later health, Professor Barker's discovery — the Hertfordshire birth records, The surprise finding, and the questions still open), explains each in their own words using the lesson's own examples, and answers the lesson's questions from the lesson text.
This lesson has 3 sub lessons, proved in order: each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson; on a fail the learner reviews the questions and answers just done, then resets with another set; nothing ever relocks. Practice deepens inside each part before the learner moves on — retelling without notes, telling Barker's records story in order, and framing one question scientists still work on.
