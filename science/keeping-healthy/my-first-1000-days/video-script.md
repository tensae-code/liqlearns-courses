Today: **My first 1000 days** — the Cook Islands way: a big discovery hiding in old birth records.

Your first 1000 days — the very start of your life, your baby days, your toddler days — shape your health for the whole of your life. They are not a small start. They are the foundation.
How do we know? Professor Barker read old birth records from Hertfordshire — records of babies born long ago — and followed those people's health as grown-ups.
Inside the records was a surprise: how a life began was linked to how healthy that life stayed. Nobody expected it. And scientists are still working on the questions it opened.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
