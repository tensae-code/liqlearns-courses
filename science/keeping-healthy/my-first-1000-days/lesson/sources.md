Placement choice, on the record: this NEW LESSON was built for the Cook Islands placement "NEW LESSON NEEDED — my-first-1000-days" — cook-islands-placements.md, printed p. 60 (one placement, one lesson). No existing general-science lesson covers the first 1000 days as its own thing — the Keeping Healthy and Human Body lessons teach food, the body and disease, none of them teaches how the first 1000 days shape later health or Barker's discovery — so it gets its own lesson. It sits in keeping-healthy/ and is registered in CURRICULUM.md Unit 14 (Keeping Healthy). The discovery is taught as the book tells it — a story (Hertfordshire records, surprise finding, open questions), summarised, never transcribed. The Cook Islands book's own way — teaching a health idea through the discovery story of the scientist who found it — IS part of this lesson's content, carried exactly as the placement describes it.
