# My first 1000 days — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The first 1000 days of a life…
a) Do not matter after they pass  b) Shape later health  c) Are only about walking  d) Belong only to doctors
**Answer: b) Shape later health**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The first 1000 days run…
a) From school entry to age ten  b) Only the first week  c) From the very start of life through the baby and toddler years  d) From age five to age eight
**Answer: c) From the very start of life through the baby and toddler years**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Professor Barker's discovery came from studying…
a) Hospital bills  b) The Hertfordshire birth records  c) Cook Islands weather  d) His own diary only
**Answer: b) The Hertfordshire birth records**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The records became evidence because Barker…
a) Burned them  b) Copied them neatly  c) Followed the recorded babies into their later life and health  d) Sold them
**Answer: c) Followed the recorded babies into their later life and health**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The finding inside the records was…
a) Expected by everyone  b) A surprise — earliest days and later health were linked  c) About the weather  d) Never written down
**Answer: b) A surprise — earliest days and later health were linked**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) The questions the finding opened are…
a) All closed  b) Forbidden  c) Questions scientists still work on  d) Only for examinations
**Answer: c) Questions scientists still work on**
