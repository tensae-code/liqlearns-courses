# My first 1000 days — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does it mean to say the first 1000 days shape later health?
**Answer:** How a child grows in the first 1000 days is carried forward into adult life — the body built then is the body the adult lives in, so those days act as a foundation for later health.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What did the Hertfordshire birth records hold, and what did Barker do with them?
**Answer:** They held detailed records of babies' births and early growth; Barker studied them and followed what happened to those recorded people later in life, comparing how their lives began with their adult health.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) What was surprising about the finding?
**Answer:** That the earliest days and later health were linked at all — how a life began showed up again in the adult's health, in a way nobody had expected.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) Why does the lesson end with open questions?
**Answer:** Because the surprise opened new science: exactly what in the first 1000 days matters most, how the shaping works, and how much can still be changed later are questions scientists still work on.
