# Hands-on — Parasites — inside and outside the body

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes. Nothing in this hands-on touches a real parasite — it is drawing, sorting and habits.

## Sort by where it lives
Draw a body outline. Draw one parasite inside the outline (label it internal) and one on the outline's edge, on the skin or hair (label it external). Under each, write where it lives and how it feeds.

## The clean-habits list
Make two lists of habits: habits that keep internal parasites out (clean food and water, washed hands before eating) and habits that keep external parasites away (clean body, clean hair, clean clothes and bedding). Tick the ones you already keep, and ring one new one to start this week.

## Explain the host
In pairs: one person is the host and says what is taken from them; the other is the parasite and says where they live and what they feed on. Swap roles, once inside and once outside.
