# Parasites — inside and outside the body — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is a parasite, and what happens to its host?
**Answer:** A parasite is a living thing that lives on or in another living thing — its host — and feeds off it; the host loses food and strength and can become weak or sick.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Where do internal parasites live, and how do they get in?
**Answer:** Inside the body, in places like the gut; they get in from outside, with unclean food and water and on dirty hands.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Where do external parasites live, and what harm can they do?
**Answer:** On the outside of the body — on skin, in hair or in clothes; they bite and feed there, make the skin itch, and some can carry disease from one person or animal to another.

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) What is the one question that sorts a parasite as internal or external?
**Answer:** Where it lives — inside the body is internal; on the outside of the body is external.
