# Parasites — inside and outside the body — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A parasite is a living thing that…
a) Makes its own food  b) Lives on or in another living thing and feeds off it  c) Eats only plants  d) Lives only in water
**Answer: b) Lives on or in another living thing and feeds off it**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The living thing a parasite feeds off is called its…
a) Guest  b) Friend  c) Host  d) Hunter
**Answer: c) Host**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The feeding of a parasite is one-way because…
a) Both gain equally  b) The parasite gains food and a home; the host loses food and strength  c) The host eats the parasite  d) Neither is harmed
**Answer: b) The parasite gains food and a home; the host loses food and strength**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Internal parasites live…
a) On the skin  b) In the hair  c) Inside the body  d) In clothes
**Answer: c) Inside the body**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Worms in the gut harm the host by…
a) Taking food the body has eaten, from inside  b) Biting the skin  c) Making the skin itch  d) Living in clothes
**Answer: a) Taking food the body has eaten, from inside**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) External parasites live…
a) In the gut  b) Inside the body  c) On the skin, in the hair or in clothes  d) In rivers only
**Answer: c) On the skin, in the hair or in clothes**
