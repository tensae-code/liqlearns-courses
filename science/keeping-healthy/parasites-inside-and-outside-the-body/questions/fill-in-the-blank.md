# Parasites — inside and outside the body — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A parasite lives on or in another living thing, called its _____.
**Answer: host**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Parasites that live inside the body are called _____ parasites.
**Answer: internal**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Parasites that live on the outside of the body are called _____ parasites.
**Answer: external**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) The biting of external parasites makes the skin _____.
**Answer: itch**
