Today: **Parasites — inside and outside the body** — Afghanistan's way: parasites first, then the inside kind, then the outside kind.

A parasite is a living thing that lives on or in another living thing — its host — and feeds off it. The host loses; the parasite gains; and the host can become weak or sick.
Some parasites live inside the body — internal parasites, in places like the gut, taking the body's food from inside. Others live outside — external parasites, on the skin, the hair or the clothes, biting and feeding there, making the skin itch, and sometimes carrying disease from one person to another.
Inside or outside — that one question sorts them all.
