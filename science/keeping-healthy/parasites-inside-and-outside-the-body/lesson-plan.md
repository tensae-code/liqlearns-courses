# Lesson plan — Parasites — inside and outside the body

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Parasites — inside and outside the body
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** parasites are living things that live on or in another living thing (their host) and feed off it, harming it — they split by where they live: internal parasites inside the body, and external parasites on the outside of the body — Afghanistan's own way, in its Grade 5 Science, Health and Environment book, is to teach parasites in a run of three lessons (parasites, then internal parasites, then external parasites) straight after microbes and before disease carriers
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Parasites — living on or in a host** — A parasite lives on or in another living thing, its host, feeds off the host, and can make the host weak or sick.
2. **Internal parasites — inside the body** — Parasites that live inside the body, in places like the gut, taking the body's food from inside.
3. **External parasites — outside the body** — Parasites that live on the outside — on skin, hair or clothes — biting and feeding there, causing itching and sometimes carrying disease.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses the main idea → return to the first sub lesson and the Do-it-first questions: every later part hangs on that frame.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → compare the two titles and say what each one is about; each lesson here teaches one thing.
- Mixes internal and external up → ask only where it lives: inside the body is internal, on the outside is external.
