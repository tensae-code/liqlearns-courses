# Health and wellbeing — what it means — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Oraanga e _____ is the book's name for health and wellbeing.
**Answer: Pitoenua**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Health and wellbeing is _____ than not being sick.
**Answer: wider**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Ask different people what wellbeing means and opinions _____.
**Answer: differ**

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) To find out what wellbeing means in your place, ask the _____.
**Answer: community**
