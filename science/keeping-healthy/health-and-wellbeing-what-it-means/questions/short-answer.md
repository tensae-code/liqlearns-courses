# Health and wellbeing — what it means — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is health and wellbeing wider than "not being sick"?
**Answer:** Because a person can have no disease and still not be well — tired, badly fed, unhappy or alone; wellbeing covers the whole state of a person, body and life together.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why do opinions about what health and wellbeing means differ?
**Answer:** Each person answers from their own life, so what matters most to them — food, a strong body, family, faith, safety — shows in their answer.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What should a scientist do with differing opinions?
**Answer:** Treat them as information about what the community values, and ask the question carefully of real people before claiming to know what everyone thinks.

**4.** (Sub lesson 3) How does the book say to ask the community?
**Answer:** Prepare questions first, ask different kinds of people (not only friends), listen to the whole answer, and record what people actually said.
