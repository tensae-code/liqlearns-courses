# Health and wellbeing — what it means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The book's Cook Islands Māori name for health and wellbeing is…
a) Toku Aorangi  b) Oraanga e Pitoenua  c) Toku Pukuatu  d) Kimi Manako
**Answer: b) Oraanga e Pitoenua**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Health and wellbeing means…
a) Only not being sick  b) Only eating vegetables  c) Being well in body and in life, wider than not being sick  d) Winning at sport
**Answer: c) Being well in body and in life, wider than not being sick**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) When you ask different people what health and wellbeing means…
a) Everyone gives the same answer  b) Opinions differ  c) Only doctors may answer  d) The answers are all wrong
**Answer: b) Opinions differ**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Differing opinions about wellbeing are best treated as…
a) Mistakes to laugh at  b) A favourite to pick  c) Information about what the community values  d) A reason to stop asking
**Answer: c) Information about what the community values**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) The book's way to find out what health and wellbeing means locally is to…
a) Guess  b) Ask the community  c) Read one person's answer only  d) Skip the question
**Answer: b) Ask the community**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) When asking the community, you record…
a) What you expected people to say  b) Only your friends' answers  c) What people actually said  d) Nothing
**Answer: c) What people actually said**
