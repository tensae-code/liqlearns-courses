# Hands-on — Health and wellbeing — what it means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ask three people
Ask three people — one at home, one at school, one elder if you can — the same question: "What does health and wellbeing mean to you?" Write each answer in one sentence, exactly in their idea, not yours.

## Compare the answers
Lay the three answers side by side. Circle the words that are the same in all three, and underline the words that appear in only one. That is opinions differing, seen with your own eyes.

## Your community poster
Make a poster headed "Oraanga e Pitoenua — Health and Wellbeing in Our Community". Put the shared words in the middle and the different answers around them. Show it to one of the people you asked and ask: "Did I get your answer right?"
