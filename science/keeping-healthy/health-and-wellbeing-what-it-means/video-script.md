The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Health and wellbeing — what it means** — the Cook Islands way: begin by asking people what it means.

The Cook Islands book calls it Oraanga e Pitoenua — health and wellbeing. It is not only "I am not sick". It is being well — in your body and in your life.
But ask three people what it means, and you get three answers. One says good food. One says a strong body. One says a happy family. Opinions differ — and that is the point.
So the book does the honest thing: it asks the community. Prepare your questions, ask people, and listen.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
