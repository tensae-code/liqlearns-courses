# Fresh air and sunlight in the home

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — fresh air and sunlight in the home — ventilation and sunlight as part of a healthy home, as Zimbabwe's ECD A guide teaches them. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home

Zimbabwe's ECD A Science and Technology guide teaches **ventilation** as a health topic in its own right: a home needs fresh air moving through it, and the guide's youngest learners meet that idea at home level, before any science of gases.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sunlight in the home

Beside fresh air, the guide teaches **sunlight** in the home: rooms that let the sun in are part of the healthy home the ECD topic builds, taught together with ventilation on the same page.

## Sub lesson 3 — The healthy-air home

Fresh air and sunlight join into one habit of care: opening the home to air and light. That joining is Zimbabwe's own way in — the two are taught as one topic, not two.

## The key ideas

- **Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home** — ventilation — fresh air moving through the home.
- **Sunlight in the home** — sunlight entering the home's rooms.
- **The healthy-air home** — air and light together keep a home healthy.
