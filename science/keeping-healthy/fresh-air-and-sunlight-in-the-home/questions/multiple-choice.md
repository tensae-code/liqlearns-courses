# Fresh air and sunlight in the home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Sunlight in the home  b) The healthy-air home  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home
**Answer: d) Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about ventilation — fresh air moving through the home?
a) Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home  b) Sunlight in the home  c) The healthy-air home
**Answer: a) Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about sunlight entering the home's rooms?
a) Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home  b) Sunlight in the home  c) The healthy-air home
**Answer: b) Sunlight in the home**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about air and light together keep a home healthy?
a) Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home  b) Sunlight in the home  c) The healthy-air home
**Answer: c) The healthy-air home**
