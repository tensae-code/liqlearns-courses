# Fresh air and sunlight in the home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Ventilation — letting fresh air through the home" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's ECD A Science and Technology guide teaches **ventilation** as a health topic in its own right: a home needs fresh air moving through it, and the guide's youngest learners meet that idea at home level, before any science of gases.

**2.** What does the part "Sunlight in the home" teach?
**Answer:** Beside fresh air, the guide teaches **sunlight** in the home: rooms that let the sun in are part of the healthy home the ECD topic builds, taught together with ventilation on the same page.

**3.** What does the part "The healthy-air home" teach?
**Answer:** Fresh air and sunlight join into one habit of care: opening the home to air and light.
