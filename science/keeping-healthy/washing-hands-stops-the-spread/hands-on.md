# Hands-on — Washing hands stops the spread

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wash the route away
Wash your hands properly with water and soap: wet, soap, scrub palms, backs and between the fingers, rinse, and dry. As you scrub, say the route out loud — dirty hands carry microbes; washing removes them before hands touch mouth, food or another person.

## Explain it from drawings
Draw three pictures in a row: (1) dirty hands touching food, (2) hands being washed with soap, (3) clean hands and a plate of good food. Under each picture write one sentence. Then, pointing at your own drawings, explain how cleanliness and good food prevent disease — this is the closing evaluation of Peru's ficha, done with your own drawings.

## The two preventers chart
Make a two-column chart: Cleanliness (aseo) | Good food. Under cleanliness list the washing moments you will keep today; under good food list the good foods in your next meal. Tick each one as you do it.
