Today: **Washing hands stops the spread** — Peru's way: finish by explaining prevention from the drawings.

Microbes are too small to see, and they reach our body on dirty hands. When we wash our hands — cleanliness, aseo — that route is broken: the microbes do not travel on to our mouth, our food, or the next person we touch.
Peru's Grade 1 ficha closes with two preventers together: cleanliness and good food. Good food helps the body stay strong, and clean hands keep the microbes from arriving at all.
So look at the drawings and say it yourself: how do cleanliness and good food prevent disease?

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
