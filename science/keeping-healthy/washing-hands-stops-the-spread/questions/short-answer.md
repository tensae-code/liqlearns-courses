# Washing hands stops the spread — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) How do microbes reach our body, and what breaks that route?
**Answer:** Microbes reach the body on dirty hands; washing hands — cleanliness (aseo) — breaks that route before the hands reach the mouth, food or another person.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Why can you not check your hands are safe just by looking at them?
**Answer:** Because microbes are too small to see — hands can look clean while still carrying them, so they must be washed, not just looked at.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Name the ficha's two preventers and say what each one does.
**Answer:** Cleanliness (aseo), which keeps microbes from arriving on the hands, and good food, which helps the body stay strong — together they prevent disease.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, as if from the drawings, how cleanliness and good food prevent disease.
**Answer:** The drawing of washing shows microbes being removed from the hands so they cannot travel to the mouth or food; the drawing of good food shows a body kept strong — so disease is prevented at the route and resisted by the body.
