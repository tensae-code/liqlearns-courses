# Washing hands stops the spread — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Microbes reach our body on…
a) Clean hands  b) Dirty hands  c) Washed hands  d) Dry drawings
**Answer: b) Dirty hands**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Washing hands stops the spread because it…
a) Makes hands look nice  b) Breaks the route microbes travel on  c) Makes food taste better  d) Stops us eating
**Answer: b) Breaks the route microbes travel on**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The ficha's Spanish word for cleanliness is…
a) Gripe  b) Varicela  c) Aseo  d) Caries
**Answer: c) Aseo**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The ficha names which two preventers together?
a) Cleanliness and good food  b) Medicine and rest  c) Soap and drawings  d) Hands and feet
**Answer: a) Cleanliness and good food**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Good food prevents disease by…
a) Washing the hands  b) Helping the body stay strong  c) Hiding microbes  d) Replacing soap
**Answer: b) Helping the body stay strong**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Peru's closing evaluation asks the child to…
a) Copy a definition  b) Explain from the drawings how cleanliness and good food prevent disease  c) Name every disease  d) Draw a microbe at its true size
**Answer: b) Explain from the drawings how cleanliness and good food prevent disease**
