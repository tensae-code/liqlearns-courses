Today: **Blood pressure and keeping it in check** — Nepal's way, from its Curriculum Development Centre books.

Blood pressure: the push of blood on vessel walls; systolic pressure when the heart contracts (the book's adult range up to 120 mmHg as printed) and diastolic when it rests (about 80 mmHg), measured with a sphygmomanometer; the causes of high blood pressure — smoking, obesity, no exercise, too much salt, alcohol, age over forty, adrenal and thyroid disorders, heredity and stress — and escaping it: less fatty food, no smoking or alcohol, a balanced diet, plenty of exercise and regular checks.

Measuring blood pressure as a full protocol (guide evidence; lesson first proposed in the Grade 10 student book above): subject seated calmly, cuff at heart level, stethoscope on the brachial artery, inflate 20–30 mmHg past the point the pulse sound vanishes, then release slowly — the first sound heard is the systolic pressure and the point the sound disappears is the diastolic; normal 120/80 mmHg, high blood pressure 140/90 or more, with its symptoms, causes and prevention.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
