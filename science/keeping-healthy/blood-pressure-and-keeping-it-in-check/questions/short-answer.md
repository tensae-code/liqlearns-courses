# Blood pressure and keeping it in check — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Blood pressure: the push of blood on vessel walls; systolic pressure when the heart contracts (the book's adult range up to 120 mmHg as printed) and diastolic when it rests (about 80 mmHg), measured with a sphygmomanometer; the causes of high blood pressure — smoking, obesity, no exercise, too much salt, alcohol, age over forty, adrenal and thyroid disorders, heredity and stress — and escaping it: less fatty food, no smoking or alcohol, a balanced diet, plenty of exercise and regular checks.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed pp. 182–183.**
