# Hazards around us

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — hazards around us — what a hazard is, natural hazards, early warning signs and preparedness, as Zimbabwe's guides teach the topic at every level. Say what you already know about it out loud. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hazards

Zimbabwe's guides teach **hazards** as a named unit in ECD B and in Grades 1, 2, 3 and 4: a hazard is a danger waiting in a place, and the first skill is naming the hazards around you.

## Sub lesson 2 — Natural hazards and early warning signs

The Grade 2 unit is hazards **and early warning signs**, and the Grade 5 unit is **natural hazards**: some hazards come from nature, and many give signs before they strike — the signs are taught so they can be read.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hazards preparedness

Grade 6 files its unit under **hazards preparedness** (its contents page misprints the unit's title as the fuels unit's — the misprint is carried on the record in this lesson's sources): being ready before the hazard strikes is the topic's closing skill.

## The key ideas

- **Hazards** — naming the hazards around you.
- **Natural hazards and early warning signs** — reading the early warning signs.
- **Hazards preparedness** — being ready before it strikes.
