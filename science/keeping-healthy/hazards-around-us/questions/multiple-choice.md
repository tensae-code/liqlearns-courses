# Hazards around us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Natural hazards and early warning signs  b) Hazards preparedness  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Hazards
**Answer: d) Hazards**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about naming the hazards around you?
a) Hazards  b) Natural hazards and early warning signs  c) Hazards preparedness
**Answer: a) Hazards**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about reading the early warning signs?
a) Hazards  b) Natural hazards and early warning signs  c) Hazards preparedness
**Answer: b) Natural hazards and early warning signs**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about being ready before it strikes?
a) Hazards  b) Natural hazards and early warning signs  c) Hazards preparedness
**Answer: c) Hazards preparedness**
