# Hazards around us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Hazards" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's guides teach **hazards** as a named unit in ECD B and in Grades 1, 2, 3 and 4: a hazard is a danger waiting in a place, and the first skill is naming the hazards around you.

**2.** What does the part "Natural hazards and early warning signs" teach?
**Answer:** The Grade 2 unit is hazards **and early warning signs**, and the Grade 5 unit is **natural hazards**: some hazards come from nature, and many give signs before they strike — the signs are taught so they can be read.

**3.** What does the part "Hazards preparedness" teach?
**Answer:** Grade 6 files its unit under **hazards preparedness** (its contents page misprints the unit's title as the fuels unit's — the misprint is carried on the record in this lesson's sources): being ready before the hazard strikes is the topic's closing skill.
