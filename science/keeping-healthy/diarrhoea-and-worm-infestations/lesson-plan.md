# Lesson plan — Diarrhoea and worm infestations

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Diarrhoea and worm infestations
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — appended to Unit 14 (Keeping Healthy) (CURRICULUM.md)
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** diarrhoea diseases and worm infestations as Uganda's Primary Four home-study booklet teaches them — diarrhoea comes from contaminated water and food and is treated at home with ORS/SSS in amounts by age, and worms such as threadworm enter the body and show signs a child can learn to spot
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Diarrhoea diseases — and ORS/SSS at home** — Diarrhoea diseases have causes a child can name: **contaminated water and contaminated food**. What is swallowed carries the sickness in.
2. **Worm infestations — threadworm and other worms** — Worms live in the body as **infestations**. The booklet's named example is the **threadworm**, with other worms beside it.
