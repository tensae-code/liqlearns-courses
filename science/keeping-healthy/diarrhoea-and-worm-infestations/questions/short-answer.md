# Diarrhoea and worm infestations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Where does diarrhoea come from, and what is given at home?
**Answer:** It comes from contaminated water and contaminated food; at home the child is given ORS/SSS, in the amount for the child's age.

**2.** Why is ORS/SSS not given in one fixed amount for every child?
**Answer:** Because the booklet gives the amounts by age — a small child's amount is not an older child's amount.

**3.** Name the booklet's example worm and say how an infestation is spotted.
**Answer:** The threadworm (with other worms); an infestation is spotted by knowing how the worms enter the body and the signs they show.

**4.** What do diarrhoea and worm infestations have in common, that the booklet teaches them together?
**Answer:** Both arrive through what is swallowed — contaminated water and food carry diarrhoea in, and worms enter the body the same way.
