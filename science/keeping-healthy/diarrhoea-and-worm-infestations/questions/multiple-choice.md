# Diarrhoea and worm infestations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Diarrhoea diseases, as the booklet teaches, come from…
a) Cold weather  b) Contaminated water and food  c) Sleeping badly  d) Reading at night
**Answer: b) Contaminated water and food**

**2.** The home drink for diarrhoea is…
a) ORS/SSS  b) Tea  c) Soda  d) Milk
**Answer: a) ORS/SSS**

**3.** ORS/SSS is given in amounts that follow the child's…
a) Height  b) Weight only  c) Age  d) Shoe size
**Answer: c) Age**

**4.** The booklet's named example of a worm is the…
a) Earthworm  b) Threadworm  c) Silkworm  d) Tape measure worm
**Answer: b) Threadworm**

**5.** A worm infestation means worms…
a) Live outside the body only  b) Enter the body and live in it  c) Are always visible in food  d) Cannot harm children
**Answer: b) Enter the body and live in it**

**6.** To spot a worm infestation, the booklet teaches…
a) How they enter and the signs they show  b) Only their colour  c) Nothing — they cannot be spotted  d) Their songs
**Answer: a) How they enter and the signs they show**
