# Hands-on — Diarrhoea and worm infestations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Start with the causes hunt
Walk through your home's water and food path: where drinking water is drawn, where it is stored, how food is covered. At each step say one way contamination could enter — and the guard that stops it. That path is the path diarrhoea travels.

## The amounts-by-age chart
Draw a simple chart with three rows — a baby, a child of five, a child of ten — and write beside each: 'ORS/SSS at home — the amount for this age, from the booklet's table.' The lesson to fix in memory: the amount follows the age.

## Worm signs watch
Write the worm lesson as a watch list: how worms enter, the signs the booklet names. Keep the list where food is prepared, and check hands and nails against it for a week.
