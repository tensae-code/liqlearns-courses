Today: **Diarrhoea and worm infestations** — Uganda's home-study way: treat diarrhoea at home, and learn the worms' signs.
Diarrhoea diseases come from contaminated water and contaminated food. The home answer is ORS/SSS — the rehydration drink — given in amounts by age: the booklet gives the amount for each age.
Worms — threadworm and others — enter the body and live there as infestations. Learn how they enter and the signs they show, and an infestation can be spotted early.
