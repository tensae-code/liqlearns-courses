# Uric acid and sore joints — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Name one thing Nepal's book teaches in this lesson.
**Answer: Uric acid: the acid left when purines from our cells and food break down, normally cleared by the kidneys; when it builds up in the blood the joints ache deeply, redden and swell until walking is hard; bringing it down — drink plenty of water a little at a time, eat cherries (10 to 40 a day as printed, their anthocyanins easing the inflammation), and avoid fatty red meat, sea food and lentils.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where in Nepal's books is this lesson's first part taught?
**Answer: Nepal, *Science Grade 10*, printed pp. 184–185.**
