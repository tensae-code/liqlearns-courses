# Hands-on — Diseases that do not spread

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the chapter
Write the disease chapter's three lessons in the book's order and, under each, one line saying what it adds. Check against the lesson.

## Two kinds, two answers
List three sicknesses you have heard of. For each, write: spreads, or does not spread — and what answer the lesson says fits its kind.
