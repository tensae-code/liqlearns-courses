## Sub lesson 1 — Not caught — diseases that do not pass between people

The chapter builds in order: first what pathogens are (Lesson 1), then **infectious diseases** — the ones that spread (Lesson 2) — and then this lesson: **non-infectious diseases** (Lesson 3), the diseases a person cannot catch from another person. They arise from the body's own working, from what a person takes in, and from the life a person lives — not from a pathogen passed hand to hand.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why the difference matters

Telling the two kinds apart changes what a person does. A spreading disease is fought by breaking its road — hygiene, clean water, vaccination. A disease that does not spread is met differently: by care of the body and by the habits of a life. Cambodia's book puts the two kinds in one chapter so the difference is learned whole, in the chapter's own order.
