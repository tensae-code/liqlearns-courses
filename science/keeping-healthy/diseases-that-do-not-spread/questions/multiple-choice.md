# Diseases that do not spread — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Non-infectious diseases are diseases that…
a) do not pass from person to person  b) spread fastest  c) are carried by mosquitoes  d) come only from dirty water
**Answer: a) do not pass from person to person** — That is the lesson's definition — Biology Ch. 4, Lesson 3.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson comes straight after lessons on…
a) pathogens and infectious diseases  b) the planets  c) rocks  d) circuits
**Answer: a) pathogens and infectious diseases** — Lessons 1–2 build the contrast this lesson completes.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A non-infectious disease arises from…
a) the body's own working, what a person takes in, and the life a person lives  b) a bite  c) a sneeze  d) a shared cup
**Answer: a) the body's own working, what a person takes in, and the life a person lives** — Not from a pathogen passed hand to hand.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A spreading disease is fought by…
a) breaking its road — hygiene, clean water, vaccination  b) doing nothing  c) resting only  d) eating more
**Answer: a) breaking its road — hygiene, clean water, vaccination** — The contrast the chapter teaches: each kind has its own answer.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The book teaches the two kinds…
a) in one chapter, so the difference is learned whole  b) in different books  c) never together  d) only in Grade 4
**Answer: a) in one chapter, so the difference is learned whole** — Biology Ch. 4 holds pathogens, infectious and non-infectious diseases in one order.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The book behind this lesson is Cambodia's Grade 9…
a) Science student book, Biology part  b) Kindergarten core book  c) teacher manual  d) private booklet
**Answer: a) Science student book, Biology part** — An official MoEYS combined student book (Sala 00004309).
