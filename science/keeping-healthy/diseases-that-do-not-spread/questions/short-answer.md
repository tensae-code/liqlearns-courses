# Diseases that do not spread — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, the difference between an infectious and a non-infectious disease.
**Answer:** An infectious disease spreads from person to person, carried by pathogens; a non-infectious disease does not pass between people — it arises from the body's own working, what a person takes in, and the life they live.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the difference change what a person does?
**Answer:** Because each kind has its own answer: spreading diseases are fought by breaking their road, while diseases that do not spread are met by care of the body and the habits of a life.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Non-infectious diseases do not pass between people; they arise from the body, what it takes in and the life lived → telling them apart from infectious diseases matters because each kind is answered differently — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
