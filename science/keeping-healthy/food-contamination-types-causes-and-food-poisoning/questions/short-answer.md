# Food contamination — types, causes and food poisoning — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Three kinds of contamination — and cross-contamination.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Home Science Form 5 book defines **food contamination and cross-contamination**, and sorts contamination into three kinds: **physical** (foreign matter), **chemical** (pesticides, detergents, excess additives) and **biological** (viruses, bacteria, fungi).

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The bacteria that poison food.
**Answer:** The poisoning bacteria are tabulated by **toxin or spore, symptoms, onset time and the foods contaminated**: **Staphylococcus aureus, Clostridium botulinum, Clostridium perfringens and Vibrio cholerae** — the table is the identification tool: from the food and the timing, name the likely bacterium.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Three kinds of contamination — and cross-contamination → The bacteria that poison food — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
