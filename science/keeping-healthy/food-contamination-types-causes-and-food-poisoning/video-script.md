Today: **Food contamination — types, causes and food poisoning** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 11 (Form 5) — *Sains Rumah Tangga Tingkatan 5*, printed p. 59).

Malaysia's Home Science Form 5 book defines **food contamination and cross-contamination**, and sorts contamination into three kinds: **physical** (foreign matter), **chemical** (pesticides, detergents, excess additives) and **biological** (viruses, bacteria, fungi).

The poisoning bacteria are tabulated by **toxin or spore, symptoms, onset time and the foods contaminated**: **Staphylococcus aureus, Clostridium botulinum, Clostridium perfringens and Vibrio cholerae** — the table is the identification tool: from the food and the timing, name the likely bacterium.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
