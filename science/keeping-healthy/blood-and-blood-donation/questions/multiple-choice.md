# Blood and blood donation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In the circulation, the blood is…
a) Only a colour  b) The body's carrier, carrying substances around the body  c) Made in the lungs only  d) Not needed by the organs
**Answer: b) The body's carrier, carrying substances around the body**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) A blood donation is…
a) A healthy person giving part of their blood for someone who needs it  b) Selling blood as a job  c) A test of blood only  d) Losing blood in an accident
**Answer: a) A healthy person giving part of their blood for someone who needs it**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) After donating, the giver's body…
a) Never makes blood again  b) Turns the blood to water  c) Replaces the blood that was given  d) Stops the circulation
**Answer: c) Replaces the blood that was given**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Cuba's Grade 6 book teaches blood donation as…
a) A thing to fear  b) Only for doctors to know  c) A punishment  d) An important act — the country's own way
**Answer: d) An important act — the country's own way**
