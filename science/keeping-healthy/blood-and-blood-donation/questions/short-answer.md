# Blood and blood donation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why is losing much blood dangerous?
**Answer:** Blood is the body's carrier — it distributes the substances every part needs to stay alive.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) What happens to donated blood, and what happens to the giver?
**Answer:** The donated blood goes to a person whose life depends on receiving it; the healthy giver's body replaces what was given.
