# Blood and blood donation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Blood is the body's _____, carrying substances around it.
**Answer: carrier**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Giving part of your blood for someone who needs it is a blood _____.
**Answer: donation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The giver's body _____ the blood that was given.
**Answer: replaces**
