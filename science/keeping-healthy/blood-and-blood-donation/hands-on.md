# Hands-on — Blood and blood donation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the carrier's round
Draw the body's round in the heart lesson's words: heart → out to the body → back to the heart. Mark on your drawing where donated blood joins the round, and say in one sentence why it is needed there.

## The gift, explained at home
Explain to someone at home, in five sentences, what a blood donation is and why Cuba's book teaches its importance to Grade 6 learners. If they ask who can give, answer from the lesson: a healthy person, whose body replaces what was given.
