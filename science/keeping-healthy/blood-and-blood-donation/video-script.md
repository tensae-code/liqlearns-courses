Today: **Blood and blood donation** — Cuba's way.

The book's order is the lesson's order: first circulation — the heart distributes substances around the body through the blood — and only then donation.

A healthy person can give a part of their blood — a blood donation — and the body replaces what was given.

Now you. Why can blood be donated, and why does it matter? Say it back — in your own words.
