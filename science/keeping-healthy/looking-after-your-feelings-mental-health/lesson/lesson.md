# Looking after your feelings — mental health

## Do it first
Before you read: think about this lesson's one thing — looking after your feelings — mental health, emotional health and emotional intelligence, as Zimbabwe's Grade 4, 5 and 6 guides teach the topic. Then read on: this is Zimbabwe's way in — its PlusOne Science and Technology guides teach this topic as a unit of their own, returning to it across the grades named in this lesson's sources.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mental health

Zimbabwe's Grade 4 guide teaches **mental health** as a named unit inside its health topic: the health of the mind and feelings is health, taught beside dental health and immunisation.

## Sub lesson 2 — Emotional health

The Grade 5 guide teaches the same ground as **emotional health**: the health of a person's feelings, named as its own unit in the health run.

## Sub lesson 3 — Emotional intelligence

The Grade 6 guide completes the run with **emotional intelligence**: knowing feelings — your own and others' — and handling them well. The three units climb together: health, emotional health, then intelligence.

## The key ideas

- **Mental health** — the mind's health is health.
- **Emotional health** — the health of a person's feelings.
- **Emotional intelligence** — knowing and handling feelings well.
