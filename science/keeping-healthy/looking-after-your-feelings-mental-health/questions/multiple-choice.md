# Looking after your feelings — mental health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) Emotional health  b) Emotional intelligence  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) Mental health
**Answer: d) Mental health**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about the mind's health is health?
a) Mental health  b) Emotional health  c) Emotional intelligence
**Answer: a) Mental health**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about the health of a person's feelings?
a) Mental health  b) Emotional health  c) Emotional intelligence
**Answer: b) Emotional health**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about knowing and handling feelings well?
a) Mental health  b) Emotional health  c) Emotional intelligence
**Answer: c) Emotional intelligence**
