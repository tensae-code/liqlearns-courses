# Looking after your feelings — mental health — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "Mental health" teach?
**Answer:** Zimbabwe's Grade 4 guide teaches **mental health** as a named unit inside its health topic: the health of the mind and feelings is health, taught beside dental health and immunisation.

**2.** What does the part "Emotional health" teach?
**Answer:** The Grade 5 guide teaches the same ground as **emotional health**: the health of a person's feelings, named as its own unit in the health run.

**3.** What does the part "Emotional intelligence" teach?
**Answer:** The Grade 6 guide completes the run with **emotional intelligence**: knowing feelings — your own and others' — and handling them well.
