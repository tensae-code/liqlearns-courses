# Food — the four food groups — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four food groups and one job of each.**
**Answer:** Vegetables and fruits (protection); grains and pulses (energy, heat, building); milk and milk products (energy, strength); meat and eggs (building).

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Name two ways a family can produce food at home.**
**Answer:** Any two of: a garden, chickens, bees, a dairy cow.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Summarise the Feeders poem in one sentence.**
**Answer:** Plants' roots, stems and leaves are washed, cooked and eaten; bees make honey from flowers; animals are kept for milk and meat (summary only).
