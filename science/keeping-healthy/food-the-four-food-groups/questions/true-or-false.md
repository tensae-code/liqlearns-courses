# Food — the four food groups — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The Feeders poem is about where food comes from — plants eaten, bees making honey, animals kept for milk and meat.**
**Answer: True** — That is the poem's summarised theme.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Vegetables can be grown in a cut jerrycan, a bucket or a tin.**
**Answer: True** — That is the book's container project.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Eating from only one group is enough for the body.**
**Answer: False** — We must eat from all four groups.
