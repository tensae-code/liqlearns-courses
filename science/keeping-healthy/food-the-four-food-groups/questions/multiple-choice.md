# Food — the four food groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Food comes from —**
a) animals and plants  b) factories only  c) the market only
**Answer: a) animals and plants** — Food comes from animals and plants.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Vegetables and fruits —**
a) build the body  b) protect from disease  c) give strength only
**Answer: b) protect from disease** — They are the protection group.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Grains and pulses give —**
a) energy, heat and building  b) disease protection only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) energy, heat and building** — Energy, heat and building.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Milk and milk products give —**
a) energy and strength  b) thin bones  c) disease
**Answer: a) energy and strength** — Energy and strength.

**5. (Sub lesson 3) If we do not eat from all four groups, the book names —**
a) bleeding gums, stunted growth, thin bones  b) stronger teeth  c) faster running
**Answer: a) bleeding gums, stunted growth, thin bones** — Those are the printed harms of missing groups.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Beans can stand in for —**
a) milk  b) meat  c) fruit
**Answer: b) meat** — Beans substitute for meat.
