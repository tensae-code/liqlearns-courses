# Lesson plan — Food — the four food groups

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Food — the four food groups
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the four food groups in their concept web — vegetables and fruits, grains and pulses, milk and milk products, meat and eggs — and eating from all four
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Food — what food is; this lesson sorts that food into its four groups.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Where food comes from, and the Feeders poem** — food comes from animals and plants; the printed poem መጋቢዎች — Feeders — is named and summarised
2. **The four groups in a web** — vegetables and fruits protect from disease; grains and pulses give energy, heat and building; milk and milk products give energy and strength; meat and eggs build
3. **Eating from all four — and growing your own** — what happens if we do not eat from all four; substituting beans for meat; growing vegetables and fruit in containers

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title in the Science course, in order. Recommended, never forced — a learner who lands here directly can start here.
- Nothing else is required; every term used is taught inside this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that food comes from animals and plants, and summarise the Feeders poem's theme.
2. Name the four food groups and what each does for the body.
3. Say what happens if we do not eat from all four groups, and name a substitute (beans for meat).
4. Start growing a vegetable or fruit in a container.

## What you need
A cut jerrycan, bucket or tin, soil and seed for the container project; paper for the web.

## If the learner is stuck
- The groups blur → anchor each group to its job word: protect, energy, strength, build.
- A food seems to fit nowhere → ask where it comes from and what it does; beans sit with grains and pulses and can stand in for meat.
