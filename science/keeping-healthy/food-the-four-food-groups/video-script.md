# Video script — Food — the four food groups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Food comes from animals. Food comes from plants.

Four groups. Vegetables and fruits — they protect us.

Grains and pulses — energy. Milk — strength. Meat and eggs — they build.

Eat from all four. Grow some yourself — in a bucket, a tin, a cut jerrycan.
