# Hands-on — Using medicines properly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the directions
With an adult, read a real medicine packet's directions aloud — dose, times, warnings — without opening it for use. Proper use begins as proper reading.

## The measuring spoon
With water only, practise measuring a spoonful level, as a health worker would — never with real medicine for practice. The hand learns care.
