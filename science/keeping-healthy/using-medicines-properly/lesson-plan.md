# Lesson plan — Using medicines properly

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Using medicines properly
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — the Oromia guide's cross-cutting lesson: medicines as helps that must be used properly (teacher guide — evidence) (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** medicines help only when used properly — the Oromia Grade 4 guide's subtopic: the dose measured and directed by a health worker is the measure of proper use (teacher guide — evidence)
**This lesson's own level:** it stands beside Chat, tobacco and alcohol — the Oromia guide places proper medicine use in exactly the slot where the SNNP guide places addictive substances; the two are different lessons and are kept separate. This lesson rests on teacher-guide evidence only and says so in its sources.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Keeping Healthy — Keeping our bodies clean. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the guide's rule in its title: medicines are to be used properly (teacher guide — evidence).
2. Say a medicine's dose is measured by a health worker — not guessed, not shared, not doubled.
3. Tell this lesson apart from the stimulants lesson: that one warns against harmful substances; this one teaches care with helpful ones.

## What you need
Nothing to handle — a medicine bottle is named and pictured, never opened for this lesson.
