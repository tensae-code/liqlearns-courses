# Traffic accidents and the rules of the road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book teach road safety first among urgent issues?**
a) children meet roads every day and are hurt in traffic accidents  b) roads are new  c) cars are toys
**Answer: a) children meet roads every day and are hurt in traffic accidents**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Where should a pedestrian walk?**
a) in the middle of the road  b) on the left of the pedestrian way  c) with the vehicles
**Answer: b) on the left of the pedestrian way**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Where do we cross the road?**
a) anywhere  b) behind a parked vehicle  c) only at the zebra crossing
**Answer: c) only at the zebra crossing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) A pedestrian keeps to which side of the pedestrian way?**
a) the right  b) the left  c) the middle
**Answer: b) the left**
