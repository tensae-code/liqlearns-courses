# Traffic accidents and the rules of the road — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Traffic accidents can hurt children on ordinary walks to school.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) We should dash across the zebra as fast as we can.**
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Adults should hold children's hands when crossing.**
**Answer: True**
