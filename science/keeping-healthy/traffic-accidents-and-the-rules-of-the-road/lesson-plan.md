# Lesson plan — Traffic accidents and the rules of the road

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Traffic accidents and the rules of the road
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy — safety at home and on the road (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how children are hurt on roads, and the road rules that prevent it: keep left on the pedestrian way, cross only at the zebra, looking both ways
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the child's own walk to school — every learner already uses a road. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Location and Maps — right, left, front and back (the road rules use the body's own left and right). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how children are hurt on roads: traffic accidents, and crossing carelessly.
2. Walk on the left on the pedestrian way; vehicles keep to the drivers' way.
3. Cross only at the zebra crossing, calmly, looking right and left, checking no fast vehicle is coming.
4. Never dash into the zebra carelessly; never cross screened by a parked vehicle; adults hold children's hands to cross.

## What you need
A safe place to practise crossing: a quiet crossing, a zebra marked on the ground, or lines drawn in the schoolyard.

## If the learner is stuck
- Crosses 'quickly' by running → the book says calmly: running into the road is the dashing it forbids. Walk fast, never dash.
- Looks only one way → traffic comes from both sides; right and left, and right again, every time.
- This lesson's next door: Signs, lights and crossing rules — the road's own signals.
