# Hands-on — Traffic accidents and the rules of the road

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The crossing drill
At a schoolyard zebra (painted or chalked), drill the routine ten times: stop at the edge, right–left–right, walk calmly across. A partner watches for dashing.

## Violation hunt
Walk a short, safe stretch of roadside with an adult. Count violations of the book's rules that you see. Report them by rule, not by person.

## Hold a hand
Cross with a younger child the book's way: your hand holding theirs, at the zebra, looking together. That is the rule being taught by doing it.
