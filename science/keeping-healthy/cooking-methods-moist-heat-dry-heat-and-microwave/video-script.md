Today: **Cooking methods — moist heat, dry heat and microwave** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 11 (Form 5) — *Sains Rumah Tangga Tingkatan 5*, printed p. 110).

Malaysia's Home Science Form 5 book teaches the **moist-heat cooking methods**, each with its results, advantages and weaknesses: **blanching, poaching, boiling at 100 °C, simmering, stewing, steaming, double-boiling, braising and pot roasting** — heat carried to the food through water or steam.

**Dry-heat cooking: baking and grilling, and frying in its forms (dry, shallow, deep and pressure frying)**, each with results, advantages and weaknesses. **Microwave cooking**: microwaves penetrate food quickly — **only to about 50 mm** — and **cannot pass through metal or aluminium foil, which must never be used in a microwave oven**.
