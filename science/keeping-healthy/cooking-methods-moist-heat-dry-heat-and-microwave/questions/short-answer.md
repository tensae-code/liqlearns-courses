# Cooking methods — moist heat, dry heat and microwave — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Moist-heat cooking methods.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Home Science Form 5 book teaches the **moist-heat cooking methods**, each with its results, advantages and weaknesses: **blanching, poaching, boiling at 100 °C, simmering, stewing, steaming, double-boiling, braising and pot roasting** — heat carried to the food through water or steam.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Dry-heat cooking — and the microwave.
**Answer:** **Dry-heat cooking: baking and grilling, and frying in its forms (dry, shallow, deep and pressure frying)**, each with results, advantages and weaknesses. **Microwave cooking**: microwaves penetrate food quickly — **only to about 50 mm** — and **cannot pass through metal or aluminium foil, which must never be used in a microwave oven**.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Moist-heat cooking methods → Dry-heat cooking — and the microwave — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
