# Video script — Diseases shared between humans and animals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Diseases shared between humans and animals** — a skills investigation in Iraq's Grade 5 book.

Some diseases are not people's alone or animals' alone: the same disease can be in both. It crosses the line between them — so people's health and their animals' health are one subject, not two. And the book teaches it as an investigation: something to inquire into.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
