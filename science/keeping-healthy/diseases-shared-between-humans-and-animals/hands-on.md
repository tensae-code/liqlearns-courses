# Hands-on — Diseases shared between humans and animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One subject, not two
Draw two circles that overlap: PEOPLE and ANIMALS. In the overlap write: diseases common to both. In each outer part, write what belongs to that side alone.

## An investigator's list
List what an investigator would need to find out about one shared disease: where it is found, in whom, and how it passes. Keep to questions — the investigation comes first.
