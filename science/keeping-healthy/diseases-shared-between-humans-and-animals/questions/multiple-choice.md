# Diseases shared between humans and animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A disease common to humans and animals is one that…
a) Only people can have  b) Only animals can have  c) Both can have — the same disease in both  d) Nobody can have
**Answer: c) Both can have — the same disease in both**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) A shared disease…
a) Stays on one side  b) Crosses the line between people and animals  c) Is not a real disease  d) Is always the most dangerous
**Answer: b) Crosses the line between people and animals**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Iraq's book teaches this as…
a) A song  b) A skills investigation  c) A spelling list  d) A map exercise
**Answer: b) A skills investigation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 1) People's health and their animals' health are…
a) Two unrelated subjects  b) One subject, joined by shared diseases  c) Both unimportant  d) Only a vet's business
**Answer: b) One subject, joined by shared diseases**
