# Diseases shared between humans and animals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does a shared disease make health one subject, not two?
**Answer: Because the same disease is in both people and animals and crosses between them — what is in the animals around people concerns people's health too.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where does this investigation sit in the Grade 5 book?
**Answer: Inside its opening disease unit — after how diseases happen and their kinds and dangers.**
