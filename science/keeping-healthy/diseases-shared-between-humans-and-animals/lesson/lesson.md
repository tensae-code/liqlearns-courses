## Sub lesson 1 — One disease, two hosts

Iraq's Grade 5 book opens with disease — how diseases happen, their kinds and dangers — and inside that unit it sets an investigation: **diseases common to humans and animals**. Some diseases are not people's alone or animals' alone: the same disease can be in **both**.

That sharing is the fact the investigation works on. A disease shared between humans and animals crosses the line between them — so the health of people and the health of the animals around them are not two separate subjects, but one, and the book teaches it that way: as a **skills investigation**, something to be inquired into, not only read.

## The key ideas

- Some diseases are **common to humans and animals** — the same disease in both.
- A shared disease crosses the line between people and their animals: their health is one subject, not two.
- Iraq's book teaches it as a **skills investigation** inside its Grade 5 disease unit.
