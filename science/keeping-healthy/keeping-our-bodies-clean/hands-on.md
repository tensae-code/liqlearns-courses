# Hands-on — Keeping our bodies clean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The real wash
Wash for real, not in a picture: hands with soap or ash, face, teeth with a meswak or brush, nails checked and cut if long. Say each step aloud as you do it.

## Teach the song
Learn the cleanliness song and teach it to a younger child, pointing to each part of the body as the song names it.

## The home clean-up
Clean one small place at home the Grade 1 way: sweep it, wash what can be washed, and put cleaning things — broom, water, soap, brush — back in their place.
