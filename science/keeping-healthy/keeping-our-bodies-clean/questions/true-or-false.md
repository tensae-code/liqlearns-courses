# Keeping our bodies clean — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Cutting the nails short is part of keeping clean.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Washing the body, hair and clothes keeps lice and fleas away.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Washing face and hands every morning on rising is part of the routine.**
**Answer: True**
