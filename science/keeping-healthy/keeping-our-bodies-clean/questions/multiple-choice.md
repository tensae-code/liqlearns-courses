# Keeping our bodies clean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How should hands be washed before eating?**
a) with water only, quickly  b) with clean water and soap (or ash), scrubbing well  c) they do not need washing
**Answer: b) with clean water and soap (or ash), scrubbing well**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a meswak used for?**
a) cutting nails  b) cleaning teeth  c) combing hair
**Answer: b) cleaning teeth** — a meswak is a chewing stick whose frayed end brushes the teeth.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Where do lice like to live?**
a) in clean, washed hair  b) in unwashed hair  c) in clean water
**Answer: b) in unwashed hair**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) What can happen to other people if we do not keep clean?**
a) nothing at all  b) sickness can pass to them, and people cannot happily stay near us  c) they become cleaner
**Answer: b) sickness can pass to them, and people cannot happily stay near us**
