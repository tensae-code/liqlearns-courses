# Video script — Keeping our bodies clean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wash your **hands**. Soap. Scrub. Rinse.
Wash your **face**. Clean your **teeth**.
Cut your **nails**. Wash your **clothes**.
Clean body — no lice, no fleas, no sickness to pass on.
Every morning. Every day. Now sing it, and wash.
