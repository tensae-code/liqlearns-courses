Today: **Testing foods for sugar, fat and protein** — Seychelles' way, from its Integrated Science unit booklets (Unit 7).

Seychelles' Unit 7 Topic 6 tests foods one nutrient at a time. Sugar is tested with Benedict's reagent; fat or oil is tested by the grease-spot method — the food is rubbed on paper and the translucent spot it leaves is the sign of fat. Each test answers one question about one food: does it contain this nutrient, or not?

Protein is tested with Millon's reagent. The tests sit inside the unit's food teaching: carbohydrates and fats give energy, and proteins are for growth. Knowing which foods carry which nutrient — proved by a test, not guessed from looks — is what lets a meal be planned on knowledge.
