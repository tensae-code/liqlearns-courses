# Testing foods for sugar, fat and protein — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Sugar in a food is tested with…
a) Benedict's reagent  b) Millon's reagent  c) limewater  d) plain water
**Answer: a) Benedict's reagent**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The grease-spot method tests for…
a) fat or oil  b) protein  c) sugar  d) starch
**Answer: a) fat or oil**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) A grease spot on paper is…
a) translucent — light comes through it  b) bright red  c) invisible in every light  d) a hole in the paper
**Answer: a) translucent — light comes through it**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Protein is tested with…
a) Millon's reagent  b) Benedict's reagent  c) a grease spot  d) iodine only
**Answer: a) Millon's reagent**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Carbohydrates and fats are eaten for…
a) energy  b) growth only  c) strong bones only  d) eyesight
**Answer: a) energy**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Proteins are for…
a) growth  b) energy only  c) making food sweet  d) keeping food cold
**Answer: a) growth**
