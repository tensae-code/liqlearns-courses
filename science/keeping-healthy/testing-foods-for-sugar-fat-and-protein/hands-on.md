# Hands-on — Testing foods for sugar, fat and protein

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The grease-spot test
Rub a tiny piece of each test food (for example groundnut, coconut, bread, banana) on separate labelled squares of plain paper. Hold the dried papers to the light: a translucent grease spot marks the foods containing fat or oil. Record the results in a table. Reagent tests are teacher demonstrations only.
