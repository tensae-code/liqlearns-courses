# Hands-on — Reading expiry dates on canned food

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Date hunt
With an adult, gather the tins and packets in one cupboard. Find the printed date on each, sort them into two piles — in date and past date — and record how each maker prints the date (day/month/year, month/year).

## Shop check
Next time you are in a shop, find the date on one tinned food before it goes in the basket, and say the decision the date gives.
