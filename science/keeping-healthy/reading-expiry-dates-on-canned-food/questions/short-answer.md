# Reading expiry dates on canned food — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What does the printed date on a tin state?
**Answer:** The limit until when the maker states the food inside is safe to eat — where the food's preserved keeping ends.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why is tasting not the way to judge an old tin?
**Answer:** Because past-date food is treated as spoiled, and tasting spoiled food is itself the harm; the date gives the decision before any tasting.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Where does this lesson stand in Mauritania's Grade 4 book?
**Answer:** After preserving foods (printed p. 17) and telling spoiled food from good food (printed p. 19), at printed p. 21 — closing the book's food-keeping run.
