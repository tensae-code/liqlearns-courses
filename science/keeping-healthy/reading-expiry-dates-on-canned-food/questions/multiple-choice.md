# Reading expiry dates on canned food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The use-by date on a tin is…
a) The day the tin was bought  b) The maker's stated limit for safe eating  c) The day the shop opened  d) A decoration
**Answer: b) The maker's stated limit for safe eating**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Preserving food…
a) Makes it keep forever  b) Stretches its life, but not forever  c) Changes its date  d) Removes the need for dates
**Answer: b) Stretches its life, but not forever**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The lesson's order of actions is…
a) Open, taste, decide  b) Find the date, read the date, decide  c) Buy, store, forget  d) Smell, taste, eat
**Answer: b) Find the date, read the date, decide**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A tin past its date is treated…
a) As a treat  b) Like spoiled food — not eaten  c) As safe if it smells fine  d) As safe once cooked
**Answer: b) Like spoiled food — not eaten**
