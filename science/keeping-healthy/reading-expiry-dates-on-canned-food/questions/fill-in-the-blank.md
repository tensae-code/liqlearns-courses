# Reading expiry dates on canned food — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The date printed on tinned food is called the _____ date.
**Answer: use-by (expiry)**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) The date is read _____ the tin is opened.
**Answer: before**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Find the date, _____ the date, decide.
**Answer: read**
