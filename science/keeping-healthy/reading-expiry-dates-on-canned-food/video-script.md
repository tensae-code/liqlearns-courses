Today: **Reading expiry dates on canned food** — Mauritania's way: the tin's own numbers make the eat-or-not decision.

Preserving food makes it keep — but not forever. The maker prints the limit on the tin: the use-by date. Mauritania's Grade 4 book teaches children to read that date as a lesson of its own, after preserving food and after telling spoiled food from good.

So the order is fixed: find the date, read the date, decide — before the tin is opened, never by tasting first.
