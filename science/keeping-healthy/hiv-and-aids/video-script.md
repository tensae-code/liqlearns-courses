# Video script — HIV and AIDS: how it spreads and how it does not

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

AIDS comes from the HIV virus. It weakens the body.
It spreads by blood — needles, razors, sharp things. Never share them.
It does NOT spread by hands, by food, by the latrine, by air.
Eat together. Learn together. Be kind. That is the book's teaching.

**H-I-V**: Human — Immuno-deficiency — Virus. **AIDS**: the stage where defence has **failed**. It passes by blood, by unsterile instruments, from mother to child. It does **not** pass by handshakes, shared tables, or one glass. Know the roads — and the walls.
