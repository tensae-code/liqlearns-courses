# HIV and AIDS: how it spreads and how it does not — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: state both lists without prompting — how HIV spreads, and at least four ways it does not — and name the prevention rule
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 4 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also unpacks both names letter by letter, states the healthy-looking carrier fact, names the three printed transmission roads and preventions, and holds the complete does-not-pass list (workplaces, toilets and washrooms, sneezing and coughing, handshakes and touching, eating together, insect bites, one glass).

Grade 5/6 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also names the most-exposed groups, states the relaxing warning with its cause, sorts Table 6.2's eleven items without error, and names the region's five contributors to spread.
