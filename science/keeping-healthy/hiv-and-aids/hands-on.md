# Hands-on — HIV and AIDS: how it spreads and how it does not

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two lists on paper
Make the two-column chart — SPREADS / DOES NOT SPREAD — big and clear, from memory. Check it against the lesson. Hang it where it teaches.

## The kindness check
With a partner, act the schoolyard scene: a new child arrives and a rumour says 'do not eat with them'. Answer the rumour using only the book's list.

## Sharp-things audit
At home, count the sharp personal things — razors, needles, nail cutters. Each one: whose is it? Is it ever shared? Fix what needs fixing.


## The two lists, taught at home
Teach the two lists at home exactly as the book prints them: how HIV passes (three roads) and how it does not (the long kind list). Teaching both lists together is the community's protection — against the virus, and against fear of neighbours.
