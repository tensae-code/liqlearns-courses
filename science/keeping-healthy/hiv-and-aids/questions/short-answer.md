# HIV and AIDS: how it spreads and how it does not — short answer

Answer each question in a few words.

**1. Name three ways HIV does NOT spread.**
**Answer: touching, shaking hands, sharing a latrine, eating and drinking together, breathing (any three)**

**2. How does HIV weaken a person, in the book's words?**
**Answer: it weakens the whole body**
**3. Name the four routes Table 6.2 marks with a tick.**
**Answer: Mother to child; blood contact; sharing sharp things; sharing a toothbrush or razor.**
