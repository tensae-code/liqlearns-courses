# HIV and AIDS: how it spreads and how it does not — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. HIV spreads by breathing the same air.**
**Answer: False**

**2. HIV does not spread by sharing a latrine.**
**Answer: True**

**3. HIV can spread by a needle or razor that carries blood.**
**Answer: True**
**4. HIV can pass through untested blood products.**
**Answer: True**

**5. HIV passes by insect bites and drinking from one glass.**
**Answer: False**
