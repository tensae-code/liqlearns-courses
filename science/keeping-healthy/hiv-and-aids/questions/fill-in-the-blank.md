# HIV and AIDS: how it spreads and how it does not — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. Never share needles, razors or other ___ instruments.**
**Answer: sharp**

**2. AIDS is caused by the ___ virus.**
**Answer: HIV**
**3. AIDS is the stage when the body's ___ has failed and companion diseases begin.**
**Answer: defence**
**4. ______ is the virus; AIDS is the defence capacity destroyed.**
**Answer: HIV**

**5. Countries screen jointly at ______ posts and transport hubs.**
**Answer: border**
