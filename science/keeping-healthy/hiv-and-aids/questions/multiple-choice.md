# HIV and AIDS: how it spreads and how it does not — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. AIDS is caused by —**
a) the HIV virus  b) flies  c) dirty water
**Answer: a) the HIV virus**

**2. Which of these can spread HIV?**
a) shaking hands  b) a shared razor  c) eating together
**Answer: b) a shared razor** — blood on shared sharp instruments is the danger.

**3. A person living with HIV at your table —**
a) must eat alone  b) can eat and drink with you safely  c) must not be touched
**Answer: b) can eat and drink with you safely**
