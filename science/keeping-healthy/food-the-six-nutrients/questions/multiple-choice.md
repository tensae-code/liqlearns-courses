# Food — the six nutrients — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How many nutrients does the book name?**
a) four  b) six  c) eight
**Answer: b) six** — Six nutrients.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Protein's job is —**
a) building the body  b) making food sweet  c) cooling the body
**Answer: a) building the body** — Protein builds.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Water is about what share of the body?**
a) 10%  b) about 67% — two-thirds  c) 100%
**Answer: b) about 67% — two-thirds** — About 67%, two-thirds.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which pair completes the six with water?**
a) salt and sugar  b) minerals and vitamins  c) tea and coffee
**Answer: b) minerals and vitamins** — Minerals and vitamins.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Carbohydrates, and fats and oils, are the body's —**
a) energy carriers  b) bones  c) diseases
**Answer: a) energy carriers** — They carry energy.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Chapter 2 review (table completion): which nutrient is about 67% of the body?**
a) protein  b) water  c) fat
**Answer: b) water** — Water — about 67%, from §2.2 as reviewed.

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Chapter 2 review (true/false style): the six nutrients include minerals and vitamins.**
a) true — both are in the six  b) false  c) only vitamins
**Answer: a) true — both are in the six** — Minerals and vitamins are both among the six.
