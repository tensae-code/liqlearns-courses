# Food — the six nutrients — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Water counts as one of the six nutrients.**
**Answer: True** — Water is in the printed list of six.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Each nutrient is carried by its own foods.**
**Answer: True** — Nutrients are learned with the foods that carry them.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Water is only a tiny part of the body.**
**Answer: False** — Water is about 67% — two-thirds of the body.
