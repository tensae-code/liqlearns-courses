# Food — the six nutrients — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six nutrients.**
**Answer:** Carbohydrates, fats and oils, protein, minerals, vitamins and water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the lesson make such a point of water?**
**Answer:** Because water alone is about 67% (two-thirds) of the body, and it stands in the list as a nutrient itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name one nutrient, its job, and a food that carries it.**
**Answer:** Any correct triple, e.g. protein — builds the body — egg/meat/beans (in the lesson's terms).
