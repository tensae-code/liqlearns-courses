# Lesson plan — Food — the six nutrients

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Keeping Healthy · **Lesson:** Food — the six nutrients
**Place in the curriculum:** Keeping Healthy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the six nutrients — carbohydrates, fats and oils, protein, minerals, vitamins and water — what each does and which foods carry it; water alone is about 67% of the body
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the four food groups; this lesson opens the groups up into the six nutrients inside them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The energy and building nutrients** — carbohydrates, fats and oils, and protein — what each does and which foods carry it
2. **Minerals, vitamins and water** — minerals and vitamins — what each does; water, about 67% (two-thirds) of the body

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title in the Science course, in order. Recommended, never forced — a learner who lands here directly can start here.
- Nothing else is required; every term used is taught inside this lesson.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the six nutrients.
2. Say what each nutrient does and name a food that carries it.
3. Say that water is about 67% (two-thirds) of the body.

## What you need
Paper for the six-row table; a glass of water.
