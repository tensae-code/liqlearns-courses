# Food — what food is — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Say what food is, in your own words.**
**Answer:** Food is everything we eat and drink that carries useful things for the body and does no harm.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name yesterday's dinner and one useful thing it did for your body.**
**Answer:** Any true recall of the learner's own dinner, with one useful thing it carried (energy, building, protection — in the learner's words).

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a harmful thing not count as food here?**
**Answer:** Because the definition has two parts: it must carry useful things for the body AND do no harm.
