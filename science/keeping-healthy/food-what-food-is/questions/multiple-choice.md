# Food — what food is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Food, in this lesson's words, is —**
a) anything tasty  b) everything we eat and drink that carries useful things for the body and does no harm  c) only bread and injera
**Answer: b) everything we eat and drink that carries useful things for the body and does no harm** — That is the printed definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The lesson begins by recalling —**
a) next week's shopping  b) yesterday's breakfast, lunch and dinner  c) a restaurant menu
**Answer: b) yesterday's breakfast, lunch and dinner** — Yesterday's three meals are the starting recall.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these counts as food in this lesson's words?**
a) clean drinking water  b) a stone  c) smoke
**Answer: a) clean drinking water** — Water is drunk, carries useful things and does no harm.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) For each meal you recall, the question to ask is —**
a) was it expensive?  b) what did this food do for my body?  c) who cooked it?
**Answer: b) what did this food do for my body?** — Each food is asked what it does for the body.
