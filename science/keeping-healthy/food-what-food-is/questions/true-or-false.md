# Food — what food is — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A thing that harms the body still counts as food in this lesson's definition.**
**Answer: False** — Food must carry useful things and do no harm.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Breakfast, lunch and dinner are the three meals the lesson asks you to recall.**
**Answer: True** — Those are yesterday's three meals.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Drinks as well as things we eat can be food.**
**Answer: True** — Food is everything we eat and drink.
