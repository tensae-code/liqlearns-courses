# Food — what food is

## Do it first

Before any rule is given: name everything you ate and drank yesterday — breakfast, lunch and dinner. That list is where this lesson starts.

## Sub lesson 1 — What food is

Food is everything we eat and drink that carries useful things for the body and does no harm. Bread, injera, vegetables, milk, eggs, water — when a thing feeds the body and does not harm it, it is food.

## Sub lesson 2 — Yesterday's three meals

Think back over yesterday. What did you eat for breakfast? What for lunch? What for dinner? For each meal, ask: what did this food do for my body? A meal is not just something tasty — each food carries something useful into the body.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the lesson's definition of food in your own words, then walk them through your own yesterday — breakfast, lunch, dinner — naming one useful thing each meal carried.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write or draw yesterday's three meals in a row: breakfast, lunch, dinner.
2. Beside each meal, write one useful thing it did for your body.
3. Find one thing you ate or drank that does the body no good, and say why it does not count as food in this lesson's words.
