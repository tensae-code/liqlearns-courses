Today: **Waterborne diseases and oral saline** — Bangladesh's way, from its book (Bangladesh, Grade 4 — *Elementary Science Class 4*, printed p. 38).

Diarrhoea, cholera, dysentery, jaundice and typhoid are carried by dirty water. Bangladesh's Class 4 book names them together, with their symptoms, because they arrive by the same road: germs in water, reaching the body.
For diarrhoea, the book's own answer is oral saline made at home: a pinch of salt and a handful of molasses or sugar stirred into half a litre of safe water. It puts back the water and salts the body is losing, and it can be made in any kitchen.
Prevention stands before cure: boil, filter or purify drinking water; wash hands in the six steps the book teaches; and use clean toilets, so the germs never reach the water at all.
