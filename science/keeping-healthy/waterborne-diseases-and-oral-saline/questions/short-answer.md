# Waterborne diseases and oral saline — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The waterborne diseases.
**Answer:** Diarrhoea, cholera, dysentery, jaundice and typhoid are carried by dirty water.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Making oral saline at home.
**Answer:** For diarrhoea, the book's own answer is oral saline made at home: a pinch of salt and a handful of molasses or sugar stirred into half a litre of safe water.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Keeping the water clean.
**Answer:** Prevention stands before cure: boil, filter or purify drinking water; wash hands in the six steps the book teaches; and use clean toilets, so the germs never reach the water at all..

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The waterborne diseases → Making oral saline at home → Keeping the water clean — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
