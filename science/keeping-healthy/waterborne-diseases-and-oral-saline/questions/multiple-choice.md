# Waterborne diseases and oral saline — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The waterborne diseases  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Making oral saline at home  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Keeping the water clean  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: the waterborne diseases — diarrhoea, cholera, dysentery, jaundice and typhoid — their symptoms, making oral saline at home, and prevention — Bangladesh's own way of teaching this, in its Class 4 book. That is…

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
