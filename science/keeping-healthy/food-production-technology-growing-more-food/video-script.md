The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Food production technology — growing more food** — Malaysia's way, from its book (Malaysia, Grade 11 (Form 5) — *Sains Tingkatan 5*, printed p. 66).

Malaysia's Form 5 book teaches **raising the quality and quantity of the nation's food production** through **quality breeds, farmer education, modern technology, and optimum use of land and water** — the four levers named together.

The methods follow: **biotechnology in food production (embryo transfer, cloning, genetic engineering)**; **machinery and drones** speeding planting and harvest; and **efficient soil management — terracing, crop rotation and mixed cropping**.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
