# Food production technology — growing more food — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Raising quality and quantity.
**Answer:** Malaysia's Form 5 book teaches **raising the quality and quantity of the nation's food production** through **quality breeds, farmer education, modern technology, and optimum use of land and water** — the four levers named together.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Biotechnology, machinery and soil management.
**Answer:** The methods follow: **biotechnology in food production (embryo transfer, cloning, genetic engineering)**; **machinery and drones** speeding planting and harvest; and **efficient soil management — terracing, crop rotation and mixed cropping**.
