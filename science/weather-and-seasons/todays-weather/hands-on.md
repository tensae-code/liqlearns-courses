# Hands-on — Today's weather

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Seven mornings
Keep the morning record for a full week. On the seventh day, read the week aloud: which mornings repeated? That repetition is next lesson's door — climate.

## Three-times day
Report one whole day at its three stations — morning, midday, evening — with one weather word each and one proof (a shadow, a coat, a puddle).

## Weather board
Start a class or family weather board: date, word, yes/no table. Fill it before anything else each morning for a month.
