# Video script — Today's weather

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look up. **Sunny? Rainy? Windy? Cold?**
That is **weather** — the air, today, this hour.
Morning is not midday. Night is not day.
Write it down every morning. You are a weather watcher now.
