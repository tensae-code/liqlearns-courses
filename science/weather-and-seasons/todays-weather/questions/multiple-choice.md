# Today's weather — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Weather is —**
a) the air's condition changing within the day  b) the pattern of many years  c) a kind of cloud
**Answer: a) the air's condition changing within the day**

**2. Which set is weather words?**
a) sunny, rainy, windy  b) always, never, forever  c) big, small, round
**Answer: a) sunny, rainy, windy**

**3. Which is a weather report?**
a) 'It is windy this morning'  b) 'Our climate is wet'  c) 'Winters are cold here'
**Answer: a) 'It is windy this morning'** — weather is the air now, not the long pattern.

**4. Who reports the morning sky observation in the book's class?**
a) the pupils, to the class  b) only the teacher  c) nobody
**Answer: a) the pupils, to the class**
