# El Niño — when the Pacific warms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) State this lesson's first key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** El Niño comes every 3–7 years, when the trade winds weaken and the upwelling off South America stops.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) State this lesson's second key idea in your own words.
**Answer:** With the upwelling stopped, the eastern Pacific warms.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What breaks first in an El Niño year — the winds or the warming?
**Answer:** The winds: the trade winds weaken first, the upwelling stops, and only then does the eastern Pacific warm.
