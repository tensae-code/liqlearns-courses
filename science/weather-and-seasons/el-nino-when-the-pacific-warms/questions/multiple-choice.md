# El Niño — when the Pacific warms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Which sentence gives this lesson's first key idea?
a) El Niño comes every 3–7 years, when the trade winds weaken and the upwelling off South America stops.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) El Niño comes every 3–7 years, when the trade winds weaken and the upwelling off South America stops.**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson opens by asking the learner to…
a) Memorise the key ideas first  b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation  c) Skip to the questions  d) Copy the lesson out
**Answer: b) Answer the Do-it-first questions out loud before any explanation**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's second key idea?
a) With the upwelling stopped, the eastern Pacific warms.  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Something from a different lesson  d) None of these
**Answer: a) With the upwelling stopped, the eastern Pacific warms.**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Which sentence gives this lesson's third key idea?
a) Something from a different lesson  b) The opposite of the lesson's teaching  c) Its name means "the Christmas child" — South American fishermen notice it near Christmas.  d) None of these
**Answer: c) Its name means "the Christmas child" — South American fishermen notice it near Christmas.**
