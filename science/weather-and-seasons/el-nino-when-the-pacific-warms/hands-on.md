# Hands-on — El Niño — when the Pacific warms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pattern and break
Draw the Pacific twice: the normal pattern (winds blowing west, cold water rising in the east) and the El Niño break (weak winds, no upwelling, warm east). Label what changed.

## Count the rhythm
El Niño comes every 3–7 years. If one came this year, write the earliest and latest years the next could come. Say what makes the rhythm uneven.
