# Video script — El Niño — when the Pacific warms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **El Niño — when the Pacific warms** — Iceland's current-affairs sheet, Í dagsins önn.
Normally trade winds blow west and cold water wells up in the east. Every 3 to 7 years the winds weaken, the upwelling stops, and the east warms.
That warming is El Niño — the Christmas child, named by fishermen who notice it near Christmas.
Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
