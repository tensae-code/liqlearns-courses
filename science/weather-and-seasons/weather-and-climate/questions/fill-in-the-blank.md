# Weather and climate — fill in the blank

Fill in each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Weather changes hour by hour; ___ is the pattern that lasts.**
**Answer: climate**
**2. (Sub lesson 3) Weather is the short-term state; climate is the settled ___.**
**Answer: pattern**
**3. Rain is measured by a rain ______.**
**Answer: gauge**

**4. Climate in Amharic is የአየር ______.**
**Answer: ንብረት**
