# Weather and climate — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A farmer uses climate knowledge to decide when to sow.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Frost and dew are elements of weather and climate.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Climate is the long-term average weather of a wide area.**
**Answer: True**
**4. (Sub lesson 3) A place's climate can be hot or cold, wet or dry.**
**Answer: True**
