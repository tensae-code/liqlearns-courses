# Weather and climate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Climate is —**
a) today's rain  b) the long-repeated pattern of heat, cold, rain and wind  c) a single storm
**Answer: b) the long-repeated pattern of heat, cold, rain and wind**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) To understand a place's climate, the Grade 3 book says we need records of at least —**
a) one week  b) one year  c) thirty years
**Answer: c) thirty years**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The two key elements for study are —**
a) snow and fog  b) rain and temperature  c) dew and cloud
**Answer: b) rain and temperature**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Air is —**
a) nothing at all  b) a mixture of gases that occupies space and has weight  c) a kind of light
**Answer: b) a mixture of gases that occupies space and has weight**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which helps a farmer most for choosing sowing time?**
a) one day's weather  b) the climate pattern  c) a single cloud
**Answer: b) the climate pattern**
**6. (Sub lesson 3) In the Grade 4 book, climate is —**
a) today's state of the air  b) the long-term average of wind, temperature, rainfall and cloud over many years  c) a single rainy season
**Answer: b) the long-term average of wind, temperature, rainfall and cloud over many years**

**7. (Sub lesson 3) The book's worked example uses a station average for —**
a) Debre Berhan, over 25 years  b) one school, over one week  c) the capital, over one day
**Answer: a) Debre Berhan, over 25 years**
**8. Wind speed is measured by —**
a) a barometer  b) an anemometer  c) a rain gauge
**Answer: b) an anemometer**

**9. Air pressure is measured by —**
a) a thermometer  b) a wind vane  c) a barometer
**Answer: c) a barometer**

**10. The annual average is the 12 months —**
a) added only  b) added and divided by 12  c) multiplied
**Answer: b) added and divided by 12**

**11. Ethiopia's kiremt rain comes on winds from —**
a) Asia, dry  b) the Atlantic, moist  c) the poles
**Answer: b) the Atlantic, moist**
