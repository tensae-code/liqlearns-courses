# Weather and climate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Climate is —**
a) today's rain  b) the long-repeated pattern of heat, cold, rain and wind  c) a single storm
**Answer: b) the long-repeated pattern of heat, cold, rain and wind**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) To understand a place's climate, the Grade 3 book says we need records of at least —**
a) one week  b) one year  c) thirty years
**Answer: c) thirty years**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The two key elements for study are —**
a) snow and fog  b) rain and temperature  c) dew and cloud
**Answer: b) rain and temperature**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Air is —**
a) nothing at all  b) a mixture of gases that occupies space and has weight  c) a kind of light
**Answer: b) a mixture of gases that occupies space and has weight**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which helps a farmer most for choosing sowing time?**
a) one day's weather  b) the climate pattern  c) a single cloud
**Answer: b) the climate pattern**
