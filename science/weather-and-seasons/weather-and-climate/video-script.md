# Video script — Weather and climate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Weather: today, this hour, changing.
Climate: the pattern of thirty years.
Rain and temperature — the two to watch first.
Farmers sow by climate. You report weather. Both begin by looking up.

**Climate** is the long average — wind, temperature, rain and cloud, over **many years**. Debre Berhan's record runs **twenty-five years**. One average, and the town's climate has a name.
