# Hands-on — Weather and climate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Thirty-year question
Ask the oldest person you know: 'Has the rain changed since you were young?' Their memory is a climate record — treat it with respect and write it down.

## Element watch
For one day, note which of the eleven elements you actually meet. Tick them off the book's list; the unmet ones belong to other seasons or other places.

## Street in two seasons
Draw your street in kiremt and in bega with the guide's eye: water, mud, clothes, work, sky. Label five differences.
