# Hands-on — Weather disasters and staying safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the sky's warnings
For one week, note each day's weather and any official weather warning you hear of. Say which disaster, if any, each warning was about, and what it was warning people to do.

## A family safety list
With your family, list what your home would need ready if a weather disaster threatened — and where your family would go. Keep the list with this lesson.
