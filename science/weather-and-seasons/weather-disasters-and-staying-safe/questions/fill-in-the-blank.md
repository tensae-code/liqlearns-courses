# Weather disasters and staying safe — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** Weather that harms at its extreme is a meteorological _____.
**Answer: disaster**

**2.** Each disaster is known by what it _____.
**Answer: brings**

**3.** Staying safe begins with _____.
**Answer: knowing**

**4.** Weather _____ are taken seriously by learners who know the disasters.
**Answer: warnings**
