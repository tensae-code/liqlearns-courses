# Weather disasters and staying safe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What makes weather into a meteorological disaster?
**Answer:** Its extreme: when weather grows strong enough to harm people, homes, crops and land, it is a disaster — the same temperature, wind and rain the class measures, grown dangerous.

**2.** How is each disaster learned in the book's way?
**Answer:** By what it brings — the signs that announce it and the damage and danger that arrive with it.

**3.** Why does knowing the disasters help a learner stay safe?
**Answer:** A known disaster can be prepared for: warnings are taken seriously, families can prepare, and the learner can keep clear of the harm instead of meeting it by surprise.

**4.** Why does this lesson belong in the weather unit?
**Answer:** Because it is the same weather the unit teaches to measure and observe — met at its extreme; the measuring lessons and this one teach the same sky, watched with understanding.
