# Weather disasters and staying safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A meteorological disaster is…
a) Weather at its extreme, harming people, homes, crops and land  b) Any rainy day  c) A weather forecast  d) A season of the year
**Answer: a) Weather at its extreme, harming people, homes, crops and land**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) China's Grade 4 book places this topic…
a) In the animals unit  b) Inside the weather unit, after measuring temperature, wind and rain  c) Before any weather is taught  d) In a history book
**Answer: b) Inside the weather unit, after measuring temperature, wind and rain**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Each disaster is known by…
a) Its colour  b) Its name in another language  c) What it brings — its signs and its harms  d) The month it prefers
**Answer: c) What it brings — its signs and its harms**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Staying safe from weather disasters begins with…
a) Ignoring warnings  b) Staying outdoors to watch  c) Hoping for luck  d) Knowing them — naming each disaster and what it brings
**Answer: d) Knowing them — naming each disaster and what it brings**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) A weather warning is useful to a learner who…
a) Knows the disasters and takes the warning seriously  b) Has never heard of disasters  c) Lives where weather never changes  d) Cannot read
**Answer: a) Knows the disasters and takes the warning seriously**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) This lesson sits beside which lessons?
a) The simple machines lessons  b) The measuring and observing weather lessons — the same sky, watched with understanding  c) The digestion lessons  d) The magnets lessons
**Answer: b) The measuring and observing weather lessons — the same sky, watched with understanding**
