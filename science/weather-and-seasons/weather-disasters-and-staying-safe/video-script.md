# Video script — Weather disasters and staying safe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: **Weather disasters and staying safe** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

Weather has an extreme side: storms of wind and rain strong enough to harm homes, crops and people. Those are meteorological disasters, and each is known by what it brings.
Learn their faces and their harms — because staying safe begins with knowing. A warning only helps someone who understands what it warns about.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
