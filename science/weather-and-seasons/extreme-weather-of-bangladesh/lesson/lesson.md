# Extreme weather of Bangladesh

## Sub lesson 1 — Heat, cold, flood and drought

Bangladesh's extremes come in pairs: heat wave and cold wave; and water in both wrong amounts — flood, covering a fifth of the country in a normal rainy season, and drought, when the rain does not come.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Nor'wester and the tornado

The Nor'wester, or Kal-Baishakhi, is the summer thunderstorm of Bangladesh — sudden, violent and local. The tornado is its spinning kin, narrower and fiercer still.

## Sub lesson 3 — The cyclone — the greatest storm

The cyclone is a revolving storm 500–800 km across, born over the warm Bay of Bengal. Its worst damage is not its wind but its storm surge — the sea itself pushed onto the land. Class 5 teaches Bangladesh's extremes as the country's own weather, in its own names.
