# Extreme weather of Bangladesh — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Heat, cold, flood and drought.
**Answer:** Bangladesh's extremes come in pairs: heat wave and cold wave; and water in both wrong amounts — flood, covering a fifth of the country in a normal rainy season, and drought, when the rain does not come..

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: The Nor'wester and the tornado.
**Answer:** The Nor'wester, or Kal-Baishakhi, is the summer thunderstorm of Bangladesh — sudden, violent and local.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: The cyclone — the greatest storm.
**Answer:** The cyclone is a revolving storm 500–800 km across, born over the warm Bay of Bengal.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Heat, cold, flood and drought → The Nor'wester and the tornado → The cyclone — the greatest storm — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
