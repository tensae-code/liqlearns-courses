# Hands-on — Forecasting severe weather and early warnings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: weather safety: world institutions watch hurricanes and severe weather with radar, satellites and computers to forecast their tracks and warn people; in the Kingdom, the General Authority of Meteorology and Environmental Protection forecasts the weather of Saudi cities and marine areas and issues early warnings in emergencies Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
