# Designing solutions to weather hazards — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Weather-related hazards and their effects.
**Answer:** A weather-related hazard is dangerous weather whose effects harm people and places (Grade 3, 3.1.3, printed p. 28) — the book's worked ground is flooding and its flood barriers and similar solutions.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Design, test and optimise the solution.
**Answer:** The solution follows the design loop: design a barrier or other defence, test it against water, and optimise — improve the design from what the test showed — until the effects are reduced as far as the design can manage.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Weather-related hazards and their effects → Design, test and optimise the solution — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
