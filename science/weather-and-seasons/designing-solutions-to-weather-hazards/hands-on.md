# Hands-on — Designing solutions to weather hazards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Barrier test
Build a small flood barrier from clay, sand or card around a model house in a tray. Pour water slowly and record where it fails; improve the barrier and test again.

## Hazard and effect list
For two weather hazards, list their effects on a community and one designed solution for each, with the test that would prove it helps.
