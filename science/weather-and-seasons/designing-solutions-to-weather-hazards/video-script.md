# Video script — Designing solutions to weather hazards

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Designing solutions to weather hazards** — the United States' way, from its Utah SEEd Standards books (Grade 3).

A **weather-related hazard** is dangerous weather whose effects harm people and places (Grade 3, 3.1.3, printed p. 28) — the book's worked ground is flooding and its **flood barriers and similar** solutions. The design problem is stated exactly: the hazard will come; the **effects** are what a solution can reduce.

The solution follows the design loop: **design** a barrier or other defence, **test** it against water, and **optimise** — improve the design from what the test showed — until the effects are reduced as far as the design can manage. A first barrier that leaks is not a failure of the task; it is the task's middle. The tested, improved design is the answer.
