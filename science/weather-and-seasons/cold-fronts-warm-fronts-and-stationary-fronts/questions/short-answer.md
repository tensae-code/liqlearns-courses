# Cold fronts, warm fronts and stationary fronts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The three fronts.
**Answer:** the three fronts: the cold front forces warm air up steeply, building cumulus, heavy rain and thunderstorms with a fall in temperature; the warm front slides up gently over cold air, giving layered clouds and long steady rain, after which the temperature rises; the stationary front, where neither mass advances, can keep clouds and rain over one place for days.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The three fronts — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
