# Hands-on — Cold fronts, warm fronts and stationary fronts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: the three fronts: the cold front forces warm air up steeply, building cumulus, heavy rain and thunderstorms with a fall in temperature; the warm front slides up gently over cold air, giving layered clouds and long steady rain, after which the temperature rises; the stationary front, where neither mass advances, can keep clouds and rain over one place for days Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
