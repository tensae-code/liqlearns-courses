Today: **Cold fronts, warm fronts and stationary fronts** — Saudi Arabia's way, from its book (Saudi Arabia, First Intermediate, Part 2 — *Science, First Intermediate, Part 2*, printed p. 29).

the three fronts: the cold front forces warm air up steeply, building cumulus, heavy rain and thunderstorms with a fall in temperature; the warm front slides up gently over cold air, giving layered clouds and long steady rain, after which the temperature rises; the stationary front, where neither mass advances, can keep clouds and rain over one place for days
