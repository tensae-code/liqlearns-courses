# Hands-on — Why a weather forecast matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: why we need to know the coming weather: choosing heavy clothes in winter, the farmer's dates for sowing and harvesting, and the pilot who needs the forecast to fly his plane in safety Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
