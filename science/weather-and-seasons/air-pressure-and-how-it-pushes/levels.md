# Air pressure — and how it pushes — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (air has weight, and the air above presses down and out in every direction — that push is air pressure, and it is strong enough to hold water in an upturned glass and to crush a can emptied of air.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
