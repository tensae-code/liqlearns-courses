# Air pressure — and how it pushes — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: air presses in all directions.
**Answer: We live at the bottom of an ocean of air. Its weight presses on everything — down, up and sideways — and we do not feel it only because it presses inside us too, balancing the push. That pressing is air pressure.**

**2.** Explain: pressure at work.
**Answer: Air pressure holds a card under a full, upturned glass of water; it pushes drink up a straw when you lower the pressure in your mouth; it crushes a sealed can when the air inside is removed. Each trick is the outside air pushing where nothing pushes back.**

**3.** Explain: pressure and weather.
**Answer: Air pressure is not the same everywhere or every day: warm rising air lightens it, cool sinking air raises it, and those differences drive winds and weather changes — which is why the weather unit measures pressure alongside temperature, rain and wind.**
