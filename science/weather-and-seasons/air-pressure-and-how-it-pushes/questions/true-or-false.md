# Air pressure — and how it pushes — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** We live at the bottom of an ocean of air.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pressure at work is the opposite of what this lesson teaches.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches this as stated.**

**3.** Air pressure is not the same everywhere or every day: warm rising air lightens it, cool sinking air raises it, and those differences drive winds and weather changes — which is why the weather unit measures pressure alongside temperature, rain and wind..
**Answer: True**

**4.** This lesson's topic plays no part in science at all.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
