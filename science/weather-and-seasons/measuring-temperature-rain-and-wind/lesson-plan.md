# Lesson plan — Measuring temperature, rain and wind

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Weather and Seasons · **Lesson:** Measuring temperature, rain and wind
**Place in the curriculum:** Weather and Seasons — after Weather and climate: the elements, now measured with instruments (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** climate's elements — temperature, rainfall amount, wind and air pressure — are measured, and two instruments have names to learn: the wind vane (ዊንድቬን) shows wind direction and the rain gauge (ሬንጌጅ) measures rainfall
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Weather and climate — the elements were named there; now they are measured, Grade 4's way.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Weather and Seasons — Weather and climate. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the elements of climate: temperature, rainfall amount, wind and air pressure.
2. Say what a wind vane (ዊንድቬን) shows: the direction the wind blows from.
3. Say what a rain gauge (ሬንጌጅ) measures: the amount of rainfall.

## What you need
The pictures of the two instruments; a windy day and a rainy day to watch for.

## If the learner is stuck
- Swaps the instruments → vane = direction (it swings and points); gauge = amount (it catches and holds). The shapes teach it: an arrow points, a jar holds.
- Names weather feelings as measurements → 'heavy rain' is a feeling until the gauge reads it in millimetres.
