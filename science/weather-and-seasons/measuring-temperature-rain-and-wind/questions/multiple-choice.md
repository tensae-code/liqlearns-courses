# Measuring temperature, rain and wind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A wind vane shows —**
a) the amount of rain  b) the direction of the wind  c) the temperature
**Answer: b) the direction of the wind**

**2. A rain gauge measures —**
a) wind speed  b) air pressure  c) the amount of rainfall
**Answer: c) the amount of rainfall**

**3. Which is an element of climate?**
a) temperature  b) a market day  c) a river
**Answer: a) temperature**

**4. The Amharic-borrowed name for wind vane in the book is —**
a) ሬንጌጅ  b) ዊንድቬን  c) ጀበና
**Answer: b) ዊንድቬን**
