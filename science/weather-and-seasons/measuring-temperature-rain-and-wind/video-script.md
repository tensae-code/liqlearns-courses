# Video script — Measuring temperature, rain and wind

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Temperature, rain, wind, air pressure — the **elements** can be **measured**.
The **wind vane (ዊንድቬን)** points with the wind.
The **rain gauge (ሬንጌጅ)** catches the rain and tells how much fell.
