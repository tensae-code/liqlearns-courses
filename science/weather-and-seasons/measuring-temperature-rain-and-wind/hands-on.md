# Hands-on — Measuring temperature, rain and wind

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build later, watch now
The Science Skills lesson 'Making a wind vane and rain gauge' builds these instruments; here, first learn their faces and jobs from the picture, so the build has a target.

## School-yard survey
Walk the school yard: where would a wind vane stand where the wind reaches it freely? Where would a rain gauge sit where no roof drips into it? Instruments have addresses too.
