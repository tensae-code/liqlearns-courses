# Tornadoes and hurricanes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Severe weather, 2.
**Answer:** severe weather, 2: the tornado, a spinning funnel less than 200 m across that rarely travels 10 km or lasts 15 minutes yet destroys like a giant vacuum cleaner; and the marine hurricane, which differs in lasting weeks, travelling thousands of kilometres and spanning up to 1000 km, born in low pressure over tropical oceans and dying over land — the chapter opens with the June 2007 cyclone Gonu that struck Oman from the Arabian Sea.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
