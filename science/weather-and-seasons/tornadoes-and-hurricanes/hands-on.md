# Hands-on — Tornadoes and hurricanes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: severe weather, 2: the tornado, a spinning funnel less than 200 m across that rarely travels 10 km or lasts 15 minutes yet destroys like a giant vacuum cleaner; and the marine hurricane, which differs in lasting weeks, travelling thousands of kilometres and spanning up to 1000 km, born in low pressure over tropical oceans and dying over land — the chapter opens with the June 2007 cyclone Gonu that struck Oman from the Arabian Sea Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
