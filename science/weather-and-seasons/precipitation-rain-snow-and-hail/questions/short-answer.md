# Precipitation — rain, snow and hail — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Precipitation falls when droplets or ice crystals grow too heavy for the cloud.
**Answer:** precipitation falls when droplets or ice crystals grow too heavy for the cloud: rain, frozen rain or snow according to the air's temperature; hail grows inside towering cumulonimbus as strong updraughts carry ice crystals up again and again, adding a new ice layer each round until the stone is too heavy to stay up.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Precipitation falls when droplets or ice crystals grow too heavy for the cloud — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
