# Hands-on — Precipitation — rain, snow and hail

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: precipitation falls when droplets or ice crystals grow too heavy for the cloud: rain, frozen rain or snow according to the air's temperature; hail grows inside towering cumulonimbus as strong updraughts carry ice crystals up again and again, adding a new ice layer each round until the stone is too heavy to stay up Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
