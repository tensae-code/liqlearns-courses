# Hands-on — Hurricanes — the great spiral storms over the sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: the spiral cyclone (hurricane): a large storm with strong winds and heavy rain that forms over the oceans and moves in a circle, its winds reaching 118 km/h and more; when it moves over land its winds and rains destroy landmarks, tear up trees and cause floods (with its picture from space) Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
