# Hands-on — Day and night, rainbow and flood

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The 12-and-12 check
For three days, note sunrise and sunset by the clock or by prayer and meal times. Compare your numbers with the book's even split.

## Natural-event list
List every natural event your area knows — rain, flood, rainbow, frost, windstorm, drought. For each, prove: nature does it, people do not control it.

## Flood memory map
Draw, from family memory, where flood water reached in the worst year anyone recalls. Keep houses and the river honest on the map.
