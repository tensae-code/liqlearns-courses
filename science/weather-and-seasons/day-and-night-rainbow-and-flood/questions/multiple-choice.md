# Day and night, rainbow and flood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A full day is —**
a) 12 hours  b) 24 hours — 12 with sunlight, 12 without  c) 10 hours
**Answer: b) 24 hours — 12 with sunlight, 12 without**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A natural event is —**
a) made by people  b) happening in nature, outside human control  c) a school activity
**Answer: b) happening in nature, outside human control**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which is seen by day?**
a) the moon only  b) the sun  c) stars only
**Answer: b) the sun**
