# Video script — Day and night, rainbow and flood

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Twenty-four hours. Twelve of light, twelve of dark.
Day: sun, warmth, noise. Night: moon, cold, quiet.
Rainbow in the sky. Flood on the land.
Nature does these. We watch, we learn, we prepare.
