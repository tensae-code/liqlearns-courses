# Video script — Fog, clouds, dew and frost — water in the air

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Fog, clouds, dew and frost — water in the air** — China's way, from its 科学 (Science) textbooks.

The air is holding water you cannot see — vapour. Cool the air, and the vapour comes back as tiny drops: high up, a cloud; down where you stand, fog.
Cool a surface all night and the vapour settles on it as dew — colder still, and it settles as frost. The morning shows you how cold the night was.

Now you. Say it back — in your own words.
