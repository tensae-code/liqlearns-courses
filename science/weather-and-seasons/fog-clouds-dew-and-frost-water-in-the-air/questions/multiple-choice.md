# Fog, clouds, dew and frost — water in the air — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Fog and clouds are made of…
a) Tiny liquid droplets of cooled water vapour  b) Smoke  c) Dust alone  d) Frozen air
**Answer: a) Tiny liquid droplets of cooled water vapour**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Fog differs from a cloud in…
a) Being made of different stuff  b) Where it gathers — fog at the ground, a cloud high up  c) Never moving  d) Containing no water
**Answer: b) Where it gathers — fog at the ground, a cloud high up**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Water vapour turns to droplets when the air…
a) Warms  b) Is shaken  c) Cools  d) Is lit
**Answer: c) Cools**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Dew forms when…
a) Rain falls at night  b) The sun heats the grass  c) Wind dries the ground  d) Vapour settles as liquid drops on night-cooled surfaces
**Answer: d) Vapour settles as liquid drops on night-cooled surfaces**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Frost is…
a) Dew's frozen form, on surfaces cooled below freezing  b) A kind of cloud  c) Frozen rain only  d) Snow that melted
**Answer: a) Dew's frozen form, on surfaces cooled below freezing**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Dew and frost record…
a) The day's rainfall  b) How cold the ground became during the night  c) The wind's direction  d) The moon's phase
**Answer: b) How cold the ground became during the night**
