# Fog, clouds, dew and frost — water in the air — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** The air holds water as an invisible gas called water _____.
**Answer: vapour**

**2.** A cloud at ground level is _____.
**Answer: fog**

**3.** Vapour settled as liquid drops on cold surfaces is _____.
**Answer: dew**

**4.** Dew's frozen form is _____.
**Answer: frost**
