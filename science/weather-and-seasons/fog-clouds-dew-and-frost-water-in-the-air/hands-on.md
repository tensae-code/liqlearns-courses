# Hands-on — Fog, clouds, dew and frost — water in the air

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a cloud in a jar
With adult help, put warm water in a jar, hold a tray of ice over its mouth, and watch the cloud form inside as the vapour cools. Say where the jar's cloud sits, and what fog would be in the same jar.

## Dew watch
Before a clear night, place a dry metal tray and a dry wooden board outside. In the early morning, compare them: which carries more dew, and what does that say about which cooled more?
