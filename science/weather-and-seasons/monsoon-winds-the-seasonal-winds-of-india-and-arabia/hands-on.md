# Hands-on — Monsoon winds — the seasonal winds of India and Arabia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: social-studies link in the same lesson: the monsoon is a wind that changes direction with the season — in June–September low pressure over Asia's land draws moist ocean air that drops the heavy rains India's farming depends on, while winter's high pressure sends dry winds from land to sea; the heights of the Arabian Peninsula, Oman and Yemen are touched by the monsoon in summer Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
