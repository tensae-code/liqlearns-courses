# Hands-on — Thunderstorms — thunder, lightning and heavy rain

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: thunderstorms are one of the patterns of severe weather: a storm accompanied by thunder and lightning, heavy rain and strong winds Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
