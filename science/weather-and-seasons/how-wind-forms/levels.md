# How wind forms — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (wind is air on the move: the Sun heats the Earth unevenly, warm air rises and cool air sinks, and air flows from where it is pressed down to where it has risen — that flow is wind.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
