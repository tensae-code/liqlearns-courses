# How wind forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about how wind forms?
a) Wind is air on the move: the Sun heats the Earth unevenly, warm air rises and cool air sinks, and air flows from where it is pressed down to where it has risen — that flow is wind.
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Wind is air on the move: the Sun heats the Earth unevenly, warm air rises and cool air sinks, and air flows from where it is pressed down to where it has risen — that flow is wind.**

**2.** What does this lesson say about heating by day, cooling by night?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The Sun heats the ground and air by day; at night they cool.
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The Sun heats the ground and air by day; at night they cool.**

**3.** What does this lesson say about uneven heating makes the flow?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The Sun does not heat the Earth evenly: land warms faster than water, and sunny places warm faster than shaded ones.
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The Sun does not heat the Earth evenly: land warms faster than water, and sunny places warm faster than shaded ones.**

**4.** What does this lesson say about wind moves the weather?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) That is why coasts feel a sea breeze by day and a land breeze by night, and why winds carry clouds, rain and temperature changes from place to place.
**Answer: d) That is why coasts feel a sea breeze by day and a land breeze by night, and why winds carry clouds, rain and temperature changes from place to place.**
