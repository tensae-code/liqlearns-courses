# How wind forms — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: heating by day, cooling by night.
**Answer: The Sun heats the ground and air by day; at night they cool. Warm air is lighter and rises; cool air is heavier and sinks. This daily rising and sinking — the diurnal change — is the first mover of the air.**

**2.** Explain: uneven heating makes the flow.
**Answer: The Sun does not heat the Earth evenly: land warms faster than water, and sunny places warm faster than shaded ones. Above warm ground the air rises, leaving lighter pressure; above cool ground it sinks, pressing down. Air then flows from the high-pressure place to the low-pressure place — and moving air is wind.**

**3.** Explain: wind moves the weather.
**Answer: That is why coasts feel a sea breeze by day and a land breeze by night, and why winds carry clouds, rain and temperature changes from place to place. Follow the heating, and you can say which way the air will move.**
