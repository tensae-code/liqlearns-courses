# Effects of wind — good and bad — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach first?
a) The dangers of wind  b) Preventing the negative effects of wind  c) None of the parts in this lesson  d) The effects of wind
**Answer: d) The effects of wind**

**2.** Which part of this lesson is about the effects of wind?
a) Preventing the negative effects of wind  b) The effects of wind  c) The dangers of wind
**Answer: b) The effects of wind**

**3.** Which part of this lesson is about the dangers of wind?
a) The dangers of wind  b) The effects of wind  c) Preventing the negative effects of wind
**Answer: a) The dangers of wind**

**4.** Which part of this lesson is about preventing the negative effects?
a) The effects of wind  b) The dangers of wind  c) Preventing the negative effects of wind
**Answer: c) Preventing the negative effects of wind**
