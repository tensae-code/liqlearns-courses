# Effects of wind — good and bad — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What does the part "The effects of wind" teach?
**Answer:** Rwanda's Primary 2 book teaches the **effects of wind** as their own topic: wind is moving air, and its effects are felt everywhere — the lesson begins by naming what wind does around the learner.

**2.** What does the part "The dangers of wind" teach?
**Answer:** In Primary 4 the darker half is named plainly: the **dangers of wind** — the harm strong wind does to houses, crops, trees and people.

**3.** What does the part "Preventing the negative effects of wind" teach?
**Answer:** Both years close with protection: **measures to prevent the negative effects of wind** (Primary 2) and **prevention of the dangers of wind** (Primary 4) — the learner is not left with the danger, but with what is done against it.
