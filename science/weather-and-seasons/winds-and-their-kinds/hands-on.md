# Hands-on — Winds and their kinds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the wind for a week
Each day, record the wind: its direction (by a ribbon or a vane), and its strength (still, bending grass, bending trees). At the week's end, sort your days into kinds by how the wind blew.

## Feel the kinds
On a windy day, stand in the open, then behind a building, then behind a tree. Where is the wind strongest, and why does its kind seem to change from place to place?
