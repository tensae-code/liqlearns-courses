# Winds and their kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Wind is…
a) Only storm air  b) Air in motion  c) Air that has cooled  d) A cloud moving
**Answer: b) Air in motion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where air rests, there is…
a) A gale  b) Rain at once  c) Calm  d) Frost
**Answer: c) Calm**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Before §29, the course's weather lessons treated wind by…
a) Naming its kinds only  b) Ignoring it  c) Colour  d) Measuring its direction and strength
**Answer: d) Measuring its direction and strength**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Kinds of wind are told apart by…
a) How they blow — direction, steadiness and strength  b) Their colour  c) The month only  d) Who feels them
**Answer: a) How they blow — direction, steadiness and strength**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Naming a wind's kind is the first step to…
a) Stopping the wind  b) Reading the weather it brings  c) Making rain  d) Measuring temperature
**Answer: b) Reading the weather it brings**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) A wind's steadiness means…
a) How wet it is  b) Its temperature  c) How evenly it keeps blowing  d) Its sound only
**Answer: c) How evenly it keeps blowing**
