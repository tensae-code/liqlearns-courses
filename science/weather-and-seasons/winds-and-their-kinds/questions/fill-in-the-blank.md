# Winds and their kinds — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Wind is air in _____.
**Answer: motion**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Where air rests, there is _____.
**Answer: calm**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Winds are sorted into _____ by how they blow.
**Answer: kinds**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) A wind's direction, steadiness and _____ tell its kind.
**Answer: strength**
