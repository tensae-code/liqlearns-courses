# Winds and their kinds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Define wind in the lesson's words.
**Answer:** Wind is air in motion.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What is calm?
**Answer:** Where the air rests — no wind.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) By what three things are kinds of wind told apart?
**Answer:** Direction, steadiness and strength — the way they blow.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the kind matter for weather?
**Answer:** Naming the kind is the first step to reading the weather the wind brings.
