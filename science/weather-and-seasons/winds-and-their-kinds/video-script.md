# Video script — Winds and their kinds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Winds and their kinds** — Armenia's way.

Wind is air on the move. Where air rests, there is calm.

And winds come in kinds — told apart by their direction, their steadiness and their strength.

Name the kind, and you have taken the first step to reading the weather it brings.

Now you. Say what wind is, and name one way winds differ. Say it back — in your own words.
