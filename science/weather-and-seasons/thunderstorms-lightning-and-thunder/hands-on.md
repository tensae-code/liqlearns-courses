# Hands-on — Thunderstorms, lightning and thunder

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: severe weather, 1: thunderstorms grow in cumulonimbus, usually along cold fronts; and inside the cloud, charges separate (positive above, negative below, the ground positive) until the sudden discharge is lightning — its heat, five times the sun's surface temperature, expands the air faster than sound, and that explosion is thunder Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
