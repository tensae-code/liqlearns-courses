# Thunderstorms, lightning and thunder — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Severe weather, 1.
**Answer:** severe weather, 1: thunderstorms grow in cumulonimbus, usually along cold fronts; and inside the cloud, charges separate (positive above, negative below, the ground positive) until the sudden discharge is lightning — its heat, five times the sun's surface temperature, expands the air faster than sound, and that explosion is thunder.
