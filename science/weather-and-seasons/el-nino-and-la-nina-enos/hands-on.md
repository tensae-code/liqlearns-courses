# Hands-on — El Niño and La Niña — ENOS

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The swing diagram
Draw one Pacific Ocean twice: label the first drawing 'El Niño — warm phase' and the second 'La Niña — cold phase'. Under the pair write, in one sentence each, what changes in the ocean in that phase, and finish with the line that joins them: both are ENOS.
