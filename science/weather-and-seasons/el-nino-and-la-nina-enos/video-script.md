Today: **El Niño and La Niña — ENOS** — Costa Rica's way, from its Grade 7 Ciencias, Código 80002 (printed p. 117).

**ENOS** — the **El Niño–Southern Oscillation** — is the Pacific pattern in which the ocean and the air above it swing together between a warm phase and a cold phase, each returning in its turn.

In the **El Niño** phase the eastern Pacific **warms** beyond its normal pattern. That warming is an event with **effects**: the changed ocean changes the winds and rains above it, and weather far from the Pacific feels the shift.

In the **La Niña** phase the same eastern Pacific **cools** beyond its normal pattern — the opposite swing of the same oscillation. Its effects likewise reach into the weather: where El Niño brings one pattern of rain and dryness, La Niña brings the opposed pattern.
