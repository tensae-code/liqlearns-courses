# El Niño and La Niña — ENOS — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: ENOS — one oscillation, two phases.
**Answer:** **ENOS** — the **El Niño–Southern Oscillation** — is the Pacific pattern in which the ocean and the air above it swing together between a warm phase and a cold phase, each returning in its turn.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: El Niño — the warm phase and its effects.
**Answer:** In the **El Niño** phase the eastern Pacific **warms** beyond its normal pattern. That warming is an event with **effects**: the changed ocean changes the winds and rains above it, and weather far from the Pacific feels the shift.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: La Niña — the cold phase and its effects.
**Answer:** In the **La Niña** phase the same eastern Pacific **cools** beyond its normal pattern — the opposite swing of the same oscillation. Its effects likewise reach into the weather: where El Niño brings one pattern of rain and dryness, La Niña brings the opposed pattern.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** ENOS — one oscillation, two phases → El Niño — the warm phase and its effects → La Niña — the cold phase and its effects — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
