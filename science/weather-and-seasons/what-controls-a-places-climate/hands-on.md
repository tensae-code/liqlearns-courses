# Hands-on — What controls a place's climate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two towns, three rules
Take two real places you know — one high, one low. Write each one's climate in one line, then write which controller did most of the work.

## The wrapping test
Highland visitors wrap in thick cloth; lowland dress is light. Sort a small pile of clothes by the climate each belongs to — the controllers, felt in cloth.
