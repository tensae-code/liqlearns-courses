# Video script — What controls a place's climate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why is one place cold and another hot? Three **controllers** decide.
**Height**: climb, and it cools. **The equator**: near it, hot and wet; towards the poles, cooler.
And how far the **sea** stands from you. Three controllers — one climate.
