# What controls a place's climate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is one of the three controllers of climate?**
a) height above sea level  b) the market day  c) the colour of houses
**Answer: a) height above sea level**

**2. Places near the equator are —**
a) cold and dry  b) hot and wet  c) always snowing
**Answer: b) hot and wet**

**3. As you climb higher, the air —**
a) heats up  b) stays the same  c) cools
**Answer: c) cools**

**4. The third controller is distance from —**
a) the capital  b) seas and oceans  c) the school
**Answer: b) seas and oceans**
