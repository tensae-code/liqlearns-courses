# Lesson plan — What controls a place's climate

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Weather and Seasons · **Lesson:** What controls a place's climate
**Place in the curriculum:** Weather and Seasons — why the pattern differs: the three controllers (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three things control a place's climate — height above sea level, distance from the equator, and distance from seas and oceans: near the equator is hot and wet, and it cools towards the poles and with height
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Weather and climate and the climate-zones lesson — zones were named there; here is why they lie where they lie.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three controllers of climate: height above sea level, distance from the equator, distance from seas/oceans.
2. Say the equator rule: places near the equator are hot and wet, and it cools towards the poles.
3. Say the height rule: it cools as you climb.

## What you need
A highland memory and a lowland memory — mist on a mountain road, heat on a plain — the picture holds both.

## If the learner is stuck
- Credits the day's weather → controllers shape the long pattern, the climate; today's rain is weather, whatever the controllers say.
- Forgets there are three → height, equator-distance, sea-distance: count them on three fingers every time.
