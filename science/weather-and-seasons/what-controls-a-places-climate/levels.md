# What controls a place's climate — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: name the three controllers and state the equator rule and the height rule.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5/6 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner states the Grade 5 three controllers, then the Grade 6 five (adding nearness to water/ocean currents and forest cover), with each controller's direction of effect.
