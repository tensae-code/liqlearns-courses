# What controls a place's climate

## Do it first
One town wears mist like a scarf and its people wrap in thick cloth. Another, a day's journey away, shimmers with heat. Same country, same season. Why? Climate has controllers — three of them — and this lesson names them.

## The three controllers
The book names **three controllers** of a place's climate:

1. **Height above sea level** — the higher a place stands, the cooler it is. Climb, and the air cools: that is the **height rule**.
2. **Distance from the equator** — places **near the equator are hot and wet**, and it **cools towards the poles**. That is the **equator rule**.
3. **Distance from seas and oceans** — how far a place stands from the great waters shapes its climate too.

Hold the three together and the map makes sense: a highland town in mist and a lowland town in sun are not accidents — they are the controllers at work.

## Grade 5 — the three controllers, Ethiopia's case; Grade 6 — five controllers
The Grade 5 book names **three controllers**: **altitude** (higher = cooler), **latitude** (nearer the equator = hotter; Ethiopia's tropical position), and **distance from the sea** (farther inland = less rain, since the sea is rain's source and moist winds drop their load early). The Grade 6 book widens the list to **five**: **latitude, altitude, nearness to a water body / distance from the sea, ocean currents, and forest cover** — nearer the equator means hotter and wetter, toward the poles cold; higher means colder and rainier, low means dry and hot; nearer the sea means cooler and wetter; currents can bring heat or cold with them. The Grade 5 teacher guide's assessment (teacher guide — evidence) asks the same question in its own words: "What are Ethiopia's main climate controllers?"

## Say it to your companion
Tell your companion the three controllers, and use two of them to explain the misty highland town and the hot lowland town in the picture.

## Do It Yourself
1. Controller hunt at home: name the nearest high place and the nearest low place. Which controller explains their different air?
2. Equator thinking: Ethiopia lies near the equator — say what the equator rule predicts for its lowlands, and what the height rule does to its highlands.
3. Three-rule quiz for a friend: describe a made-up place — high, far from the equator, far from the sea — and let them predict its climate from the rules.
