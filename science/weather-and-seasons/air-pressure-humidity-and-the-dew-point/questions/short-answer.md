# Air pressure, humidity and the dew point — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Air pressure falls as we rise.
**Answer:** air pressure falls as we rise; heated air expands, thins and rises leaving low pressure, cooled air contracts and sinks making high pressure; humidity is the amount of water vapour in the air (warm air holds more), and the dew point is the temperature at which the air becomes saturated and condensation begins — with the chart showing vapour capacity roughly doubling every 10 °C.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Air pressure falls as we rise — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
