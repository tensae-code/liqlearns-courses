# Hands-on — Sandstorms — when the wind carries the desert

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: the sandstorm: a storm in which the wind carries quantities of sand and dust that form a cloud over the ground; sandstorms happen in the Kingdom at different times of the year where dry land is not covered with plants, and they harm eyes, noses and breathing, bury plants, farms and roads, and cause road accidents because sight is blocked Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
