# Sandstorms — when the wind carries the desert — short answer

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The sandstorm.
**Answer:** the sandstorm: a storm in which the wind carries quantities of sand and dust that form a cloud over the ground; sandstorms happen in the Kingdom at different times of the year where dry land is not covered with plants, and they harm eyes, noses and breathing, bury plants, farms and roads, and cause road accidents because sight is blocked.
