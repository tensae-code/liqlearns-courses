# Hands-on — Air pressure and the barometer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: air pressure is the weight of the air pressing on things, and its change changes the state of the weather; the barometer measures air pressure and the anemometer measures wind speed (both pictured and named) Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
