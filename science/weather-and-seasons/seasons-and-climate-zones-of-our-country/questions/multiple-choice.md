# Seasons and climate zones of our country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Kiremt is —**
a) the dry, hot season  b) the rainy, cold, farming season  c) a mountain
**Answer: b) the rainy, cold, farming season**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The coldest zone, with snow on the peaks, is —**
a) berha  b) qolla  c) wurch
**Answer: c) wurch**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) High places, compared with low places, get —**
a) less rain and more heat  b) more rain and less heat  c) the same everything
**Answer: b) more rain and less heat**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a control of climate in the book's list?**
a) the price of grain  b) altitude difference  c) the colour of houses
**Answer: b) altitude difference**
