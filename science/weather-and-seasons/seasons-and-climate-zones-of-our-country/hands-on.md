# Hands-on — Seasons and climate zones of our country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Zone ladder drawing
Draw the five zones as one mountain, tallest to lowest, each band labelled with its temperature and one town or peak from the book.

## Controls audit
Take one control — cloud — and watch it for a week: how do cloudy days differ in heat and rain? Report against the book's claim.

## Traveller's coat
Plan a journey from berha to wurch in one week. Write what you wear at each end and the control that makes the difference.
