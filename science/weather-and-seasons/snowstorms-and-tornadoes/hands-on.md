# Hands-on — Snowstorms and tornadoes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: the snowstorm: snow with low temperature and strong winds covers the plants, cars and roads (a snowstorm that struck the Al-Lawz mountains in the Tabuk region); the funnel tornado (tornado): rotating winds forming a long funnel on the land at 110 km/h and more, destroying most of the things in its path Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
