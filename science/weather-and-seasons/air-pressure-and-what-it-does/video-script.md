# Video script — Air pressure and what it does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: **Air pressure and what it does** — Armenia's way: first the idea, then the proof.

Air has weight. The whole depth of it presses on everything, in all directions — atmospheric pressure.

We do not feel it, because it presses inside us too. But its consequences are everywhere.

And we can prove it: experiments where nothing but the air's press can explain what happens.

Now you. Give one experiment that proves air pressure exists. Say it back — in your own words.
