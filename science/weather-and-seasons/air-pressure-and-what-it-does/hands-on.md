# Hands-on — Air pressure and what it does

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The card and the glass
Fill a glass to the brim, lay a stiff card flat over it, hold the card and turn the glass upside down over a basin, then let go of the card. What holds it? Name the press.

## The straw test
Drink through a straw, then pinch the straw's top end shut with a finger and lift: why does the liquid stay? Explain it with air pressure, not with 'sucking'.
