# Air pressure and what it does — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) What is atmospheric pressure?
**Answer:** The pressing of the air on everything, in all directions, from the weight of the whole depth of air above.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Give one consequence of air pressure named in the lesson.
**Answer:** Drinking through a straw (or: its part in the behaviour of the weather).

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) How do the §28 experiments prove pressure exists?
**Answer:** They arrange cases — water held up, a card staying fast — where no other push can explain the result, leaving the air's press as the only explanation.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the book teach the idea (§27) before the proof (§28)?
**Answer:** So the learner first knows what atmospheric pressure and its consequences are, then tests the claim with experiments.
