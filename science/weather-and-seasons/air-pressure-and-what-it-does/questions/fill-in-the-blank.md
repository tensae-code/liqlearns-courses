# Air pressure and what it does — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) The pressing of the air in all directions is _____ pressure.
**Answer: atmospheric**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Air presses because air has _____.
**Answer: weight**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Practical work 6 gives experiments _____ atmospheric pressure exists.
**Answer: proving**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the proof, when every other explanation is removed, the air's _____ is what remains.
**Answer: press**
