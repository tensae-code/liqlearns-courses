# Air pressure and what it does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Atmospheric pressure is…
a) Only the wind's push  b) The pressing of the air, in all directions  c) The weight of water  d) A kind of rain
**Answer: b) The pressing of the air, in all directions**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Air presses because…
a) The Moon pulls it  b) Heat pushes it once  c) Air has weight, and its whole depth bears down  d) It is alive
**Answer: c) Air has weight, and its whole depth bears down**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) We do not usually notice air pressure because…
a) It does not exist by day  b) It is too weak to matter  c) Only instruments feel it  d) It presses inside us too, balancing the outside
**Answer: d) It presses inside us too, balancing the outside**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Practical work 6 in Armenia's book is…
a) Experiments proving atmospheric pressure exists  b) Measuring rainfall  c) Drawing clouds  d) Making a wind vane
**Answer: a) Experiments proving atmospheric pressure exists**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) The proof pattern of those experiments is…
a) Trust the teacher's word  b) Remove every other explanation, and the air's press remains  c) Repeat until it works once  d) Measure the air's colour
**Answer: b) Remove every other explanation, and the air's press remains**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Drinking through a straw works because of…
a) Gravity alone  b) The straw's taste  c) Air pressure  d) Magnetism
**Answer: c) Air pressure**
