Today: **Humidity and measuring it** — South Sudan's way, from its Ministry of General Education and Instruction Primary Science books (see lesson/sources.md for the exact books and pages).

South Sudan's Primary 7 book teaches **humidity** as its own topic: humidity is **the water vapour in the air around us** — water carried invisibly in the air, changing with place and weather.

Humidity is measured, not guessed: the book teaches **making a hygrometer**, and the **sling psychrometer** — two thermometers, one kept wet, swung through the air — for **relative humidity**, the share of the vapour the air could hold that it is actually holding.

The unit closes with the **importance of humidity** — why the vapour in the air matters to people, plants, stored things and the weather itself — the measured number put to work.
