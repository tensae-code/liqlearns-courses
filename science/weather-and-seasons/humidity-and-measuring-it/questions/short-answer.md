# Humidity and measuring it — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Humidity — water vapour in the air.
**Answer:** South Sudan's Primary 7 book teaches **humidity** as its own topic: humidity is **the water vapour in the air around us** — water carried invisibly in the air, changing with place and weather.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Measuring humidity — the hygrometer and the psychrometer.
**Answer:** Humidity is measured, not guessed: the book teaches **making a hygrometer**, and the **sling psychrometer** — two thermometers, one kept wet, swung through the air — for **relative humidity**, the share of the vapour the air could hold that it is actually holding.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Why humidity matters.
**Answer:** The unit closes with the **importance of humidity** — why the vapour in the air matters to people, plants, stored things and the weather itself — the measured number put to work.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Humidity — water vapour in the air → Measuring humidity — the hygrometer and the psychrometer → Why humidity matters — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
