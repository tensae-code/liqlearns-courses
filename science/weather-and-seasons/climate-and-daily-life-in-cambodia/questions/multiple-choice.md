# Climate and daily life in Cambodia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This lesson is about how climate affects…
a) people's activities in Cambodia  b) the Moon's phases  c) volcanoes  d) machines
**Answer: a) people's activities in Cambodia** — Lesson 4 of the Earth chapter: climate and people's activities, in Cambodia itself.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The lesson before it teaches Cambodia's seasons from…
a) Cambodia's own weather  b) a European calendar  c) the stars  d) another country's book
**Answer: a) Cambodia's own weather** — The book reads the year's pattern from the weather its own pupils live in.

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Work follows…
a) the water and the heat  b) the Moon only  c) nothing  d) the school bell only
**Answer: a) the water and the heat** — Planting, fishing, building and travelling are timed by water and heat.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The book that teaches this is Cambodia's…
a) Grade 7 Science book, Earth-science part  b) Grade 4 book  c) Kindergarten core book  d) teacher manual Part 1
**Answer: a) Grade 7 Science book, Earth-science part** — Earth Ch. 2, Lesson 4 of the Grade 7 combined book.

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Climate in this lesson is a pattern in…
a) the sky and in the people's day  b) the sky only  c) books only  d) the sea only
**Answer: a) the sky and in the people's day** — The lesson reads the two patterns together.

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) Which activity is named as climate-shaped?
a) when fields are planted and harvested  b) how books are printed  c) how stars are named  d) how circuits are wired
**Answer: a) when fields are planted and harvested** — Farming's calendar is the lesson's first example of work following the climate.
