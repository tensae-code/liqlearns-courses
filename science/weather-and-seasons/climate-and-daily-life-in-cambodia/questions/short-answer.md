# Climate and daily life in Cambodia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words, what this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The lesson teaches how Cambodia's climate affects people's activities — work, building and travel follow the water and the heat through the year.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does Cambodia's book teach its own seasons first?
**Answer:** So that the activities lesson is read against the real pattern of the year in Cambodia, learned from Cambodia's own weather, not borrowed from another country.

**3.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Cambodia's climate is learned first from Cambodia's own weather → that climate then shapes people's activities — planting, fishing, building and travelling follow the water and the heat — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
