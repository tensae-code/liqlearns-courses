## Sub lesson 1 — Cambodia's climate — learned from its own weather first

This lesson stands on the one before it in Cambodia's Grade 7 book: **seasons and climate in Cambodia**, taught from Cambodia's own weather (Earth Ch. 2, Lesson 3). The book does not borrow another country's seasons — it reads the pattern of the year from the weather its own pupils live in, and only then asks what that pattern does.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the climate does to people's activities

Lesson 4 asks the next question: **how does the climate affect people's activities?** Work follows the water and the heat — when fields are planted and harvested, when fishing is good, how houses are built, when people travel and when they stay home. The climate is not only a pattern in the sky; in Cambodia's book it is a pattern in the people's day, and the lesson reads the two patterns together.
