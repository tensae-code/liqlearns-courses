# Hands-on — Climate and daily life in Cambodia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The year's pattern at home
For one month of the wet part of the year and one month of the dry part, list what work and travel your family does. Say which climate pattern each item follows.

## Two patterns, one page
Draw Cambodia's climate year as a circle. Around it, write the activities that belong to each part of the year. Check against the lesson: work follows the water and the heat.
