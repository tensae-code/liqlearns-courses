# Reading climate tables and averaging — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: read the three-station table — wettest and driest months, the gap, and a yearly average worked from monthly numbers.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to sort or name, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.

Grade 5 depth (added 2026-11-12): the pass mark grows — the learner also performs the five Kombolcha workings (two means, the range, wettest month, hottest/coldest months) and draws the year as a bar or line graph from the book's Table 4.1.
