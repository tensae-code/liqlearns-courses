# Hands-on — Reading climate tables and averaging

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Our school's station
The book's own project: record the school's temperature every day for a month, at the same hour, in one table. At month's end, average it — your first climate record, made by your hands.

## Bar-chart the stations
Turn the picture's chart into your own drawing: three stations, wettest and driest bars for each. Label every bar with its month.
