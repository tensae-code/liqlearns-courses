# Video script — Reading climate tables and averaging

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three towns. Twelve months. Rain and heat, written down.
Find the **wettest** month. Find the **driest**. Measure the **gap**.
Add the months, divide — that is an **average**. Now start your own school's record.
