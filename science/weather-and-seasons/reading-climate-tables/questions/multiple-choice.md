# Reading climate tables and averaging — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Table 3.1 gives temperature and rainfall for —**
a) one town, one month  b) Gondar, Bahir Dar and Kombolcha, month by month  c) every country in the world
**Answer: b) Gondar, Bahir Dar and Kombolcha, month by month**

**2. Rainfall in the table is measured in —**
a) °C  b) mm  c) hours
**Answer: b) mm**

**3. Temperature in the table is measured in —**
a) mm  b) litres  c) °C
**Answer: c) °C**

**4. To average numbers you —**
a) take the biggest one  b) add them all and divide by how many there are  c) take the first one
**Answer: b) add them all and divide by how many there are**
**5. Kombolcha's table prints monthly —**
a) wind only  b) temperature and rainfall  c) river levels
**Answer: b) temperature and rainfall**

**6. How many workings does the book set on the table (besides the graph)?**
a) five  b) two  c) none
**Answer: a) five**

**7. The temperature range is —**
a) the hottest month alone  b) biggest minus smallest  c) the mean
**Answer: b) biggest minus smallest**

**8. The year's rainfall can be drawn as —**
a) a bar or line graph  b) a pie only  c) a map only
**Answer: a) a bar or line graph**
