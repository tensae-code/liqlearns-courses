# High and low pressure centres — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Centres of pressure.
**Answer:** centres of pressure: in a high, air sinks and spreads outward turning clockwise (northern hemisphere) and the weather stays dry and settled; in a low, air rises, cools to the dew point and rains, turning anticlockwise — with the bench experiment forming a low-pressure centre with a candle under an upturned jar in a water dish.
