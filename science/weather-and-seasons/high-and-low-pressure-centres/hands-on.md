# Hands-on — High and low pressure centres

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: centres of pressure: in a high, air sinks and spreads outward turning clockwise (northern hemisphere) and the weather stays dry and settled; in a low, air rises, cools to the dew point and rains, turning anticlockwise — with the bench experiment forming a low-pressure centre with a candle under an upturned jar in a water dish Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
