# Preparing for a weather forecast — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) The forecast tells what is coming  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) The forecast tells what is coming**

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Prepare — clothing and items to carry  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Prepare — clothing and items to carry**

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) This sub lesson is mainly about…
a) Prepare for severe weather — floods, storms, extreme heat  b) A topic from a different lesson  c) Nothing in this lesson  d) Only the questions page
**Answer: a) Prepare for severe weather — floods, storms, extreme heat**

**4.** (Whole lesson) This lesson teaches one thing: preparing for a weather forecast. That is…
a) True, in the sub lessons' own order  b) False  c) Only in the video  d) Not in the book cited
**Answer: a) True, in the sub lessons' own order**

**5.** (Whole lesson) The book behind this lesson is…
a) *Utah SEEd Standards, Kindergarten (United States)*  b) A book from another country  c) No book — it is uncited  d) A teacher guide only
**Answer: a) *Utah SEEd Standards, Kindergarten (United States)***
