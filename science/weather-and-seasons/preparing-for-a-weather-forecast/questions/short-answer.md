# Preparing for a weather forecast — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: The forecast tells what is coming.
**Answer:** A forecast is a statement of the weather that is expected — rain, storm, heat, cold — before it arrives.

**2.** (Sub lesson 2) Explain, in your own words: Prepare — clothing and items to carry.
**Answer:** Preparation starts with the body and the bag: clothing chosen for the forecast weather, and items to carry that the day will need.

**3.** (Sub lesson 3) Explain, in your own words: Prepare for severe weather — floods, storms, extreme heat.
**Answer:** Some forecasts call for more than clothing: severe weather — floods, storms and extreme heat — needs preparation of its own, made before the severe weather arrives.

**4.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** The forecast tells what is coming → Prepare — clothing and items to carry → Prepare for severe weather — floods, storms, extreme heat — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
