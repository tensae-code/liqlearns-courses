# Hands-on — Preparing for a weather forecast

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Prepare from a forecast
Find today's forecast. Write three preparations it calls for — one of clothing, one item to carry, and one change of plan — and check tonight whether the forecast was right.

## Severe-weather plan
For one severe weather kind — flood, storm or extreme heat — list what your household would prepare before it arrives.
