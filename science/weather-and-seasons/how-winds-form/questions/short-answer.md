# How winds form — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Why does air over warm ground rise?
**Answer:** It is warmed, grows lighter, and rises — the first half of a wind's birth.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) What does the cooler air do while the warm air rises?
**Answer:** It moves in underneath to take the warm air's place; that moving air is the wind.

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) What is the circulation of the winds?
**Answer:** The great turning pattern in which air rises where it is warm, travels, sinks where it is cool, and returns, all around the Earth.

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Is the wind in your street part of the circulation, or separate from it?
**Answer:** Part of it — local moving air inside the planet-wide circulation.
