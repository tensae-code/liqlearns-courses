# How winds form — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Air over warmer ground grows lighter and _____.
**Answer: rises**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The cooler air moves in _____ the rising warm air.
**Answer: underneath**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) The great turning pattern of the winds around the Earth is called their _____.
**Answer: circulation**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In the circulation, air sinks where it is _____.
**Answer: cool**
