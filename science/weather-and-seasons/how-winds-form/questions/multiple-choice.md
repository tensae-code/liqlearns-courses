# How winds form — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) A wind begins where…
a) The air is perfectly still  b) Neighbouring air is heated unequally  c) Clouds push the air  d) The Moon pulls the air
**Answer: b) Neighbouring air is heated unequally**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Air over warmer ground…
a) Rises, because it has grown lighter  b) Sinks at once  c) Turns to rain  d) Stops moving
**Answer: a) Rises, because it has grown lighter**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) The cooler air in a new wind…
a) Stays where it is  b) Rises above the warm air  c) Moves in underneath the rising warm air  d) Disappears
**Answer: c) Moves in underneath the rising warm air**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) The circulation of the winds is…
a) One storm  b) A single day's breeze  c) The turning of the sea  d) The great turning pattern of air across the whole Earth
**Answer: d) The great turning pattern of air across the whole Earth**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) In the circulation, air sinks where it is…
a) Cool  b) Warm  c) Wet  d) High
**Answer: a) Cool**

**6.** (Sub lesson 2) The wind you feel on your face is…
a) Separate from the circulation  b) One small part of the planet-wide circulation  c) Made only by trees  d) Made only at the sea
**Answer: b) One small part of the planet-wide circulation**
