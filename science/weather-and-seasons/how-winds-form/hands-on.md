# Hands-on — How winds form

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feel for the two halves of a wind
On a sunny day, stand first over sun-warmed ground (a road, a bare patch), then in cool shade nearby. Hold a light ribbon or a strip of paper and watch how the air moves at the boundary. Where is the air rising, and where is it sliding in?

## Track the circulation for a week
Each day, note the wind's direction at the same hour, by a ribbon or a vane. At the week's end, read your notes as one pattern: did the winds keep coming from a side, or turn? You are reading your place inside the circulation.
