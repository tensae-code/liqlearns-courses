## Sub lesson 1 — Clouds form when rising air cools to the dew point and vapour condenses on

clouds form when rising air cools to the dew point and vapour condenses on tiny particles; they are classified by shape and height — low clouds (cumulus, which can thicken into heavy rain and thunder; layered stratus; and fog, a cloud touching the ground), middle clouds of water and ice crystals, and high feathery cirrus of ice crystals
