# Clouds — kinds by shape and height — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: Clouds form when rising air cools to the dew point and vapour condenses on.
**Answer:** clouds form when rising air cools to the dew point and vapour condenses on tiny particles; they are classified by shape and height — low clouds (cumulus, which can thicken into heavy rain and thunder; layered stratus; and fog, a cloud touching the ground), middle clouds of water and ice crystals, and high feathery cirrus of ice crystals.

**2.** (Whole lesson) Retell the whole lesson in its own order, one sentence per sub lesson.
**Answer:** Clouds form when rising air cools to the dew point and vapour condenses on — in that order, as taught in lesson/lesson.md.
