## Sub lesson 1 — How do I stay safe in severe weather?

"How do I stay safe in severe weather?": in a thunderstorm do not stand under a tree, do not use telephones, computers or other electric tools, and stay inside strong buildings; in a snowstorm stay at home where it is warm and wear heavy clothes if you must go out; when a sandstorm comes, close the doors and windows, use masks, and cover the nose with a cloth wet with water
