# Staying safe when the weather turns severe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Explain, in your own words: How do I stay safe in severe weather?.
**Answer:** "How do I stay safe in severe weather?": in a thunderstorm do not stand under a tree, do not use telephones, computers or other electric tools, and stay inside strong buildings; in a snowstorm stay at home where it is warm and wear heavy clothes if you must go out; when a sandstorm comes, close the doors and windows, use masks, and cover the nose with a cloth wet with water.
