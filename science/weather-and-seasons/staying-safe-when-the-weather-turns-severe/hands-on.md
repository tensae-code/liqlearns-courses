# Hands-on — Staying safe when the weather turns severe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's work (household materials)
Work the lesson's central example yourself, the way Saudi Arabia's book sets it: "How do I stay safe in severe weather?": in a thunderstorm do not stand under a tree, do not use telephones, computers or other electric tools, and stay inside strong buildings; in a snowstorm stay at home where it is warm and wear heavy clothes if you must go out; when a sandstorm comes, close the doors and windows, use masks, and cover the nose with a cloth wet with water Use only household materials, write down what you did and what it showed, and compare your result with the lesson.
