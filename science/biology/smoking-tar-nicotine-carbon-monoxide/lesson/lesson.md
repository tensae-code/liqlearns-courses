# Smoking — tar, nicotine and carbon monoxide

## The idea
Tar coats the gas-exchange surfaces and carries carcinogens; nicotine drives the addiction loop; carbon monoxide cuts the blood's oxygen carrying.

## The key facts
**Tar.** Tar coats the gas-exchange surfaces and carries carcinogens linked to lung cancer and chronic bronchitis.

**Nicotine.** Nicotine reaches the brain through the alveoli and blood, triggers dopamine and pleasure, then depresses that ability so more is needed — the addiction loop.

**Carbon monoxide.** Carbon monoxide cuts the blood's oxygen carrying and strains the cardiovascular system.

**Short-term effects.** Smoking has short-term effects on the heart and blood vessels.
