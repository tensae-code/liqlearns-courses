# Smoking — tar, nicotine and carbon monoxide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about tar?
a) Tar coats the gas-exchange surfaces and carries carcinogens linked to lung cancer and chronic bronchitis
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Tar coats the gas-exchange surfaces and carries carcinogens linked to lung cancer and chronic bronchitis**

**2.** What does this lesson say about nicotine?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Nicotine reaches the brain through the alveoli and blood, triggers dopamine and pleasure, then depresses that ability so more is needed — the addiction loop

d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Nicotine reaches the brain through the alveoli and blood, triggers dopamine and pleasure, then depresses that ability so more is needed — the addiction loop**

**3.** What does this lesson say about carbon monoxide?
