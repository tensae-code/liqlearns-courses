Today: Smoking — tar, nicotine and carbon monoxide.

Remember this one thing: tobacco smoke harms through tar, nicotine and carbon monoxide.

Tar coats the gas-exchange surfaces and carries carcinogens linked to lung cancer and chronic bronchitis.

Nicotine reaches the brain through the alveoli and blood, triggers dopamine and pleasure, then depresses that ability so more is needed — the addiction loop.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
