# Open and closed circulatory systems — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: animals move substances two ways: in an open circulatory system the blood leaves the vessels and bathes the tissues directly, while in a closed system it never leaves the vessels and capillaries do the exchanging — and some simple animals, like jellyfish, manage with no system at all, exchanging by diffusion. transport in animals: open vs closed circulation, heart/vessels/blood; jellyfish exchanging directly by diffusion as the no-system case**
