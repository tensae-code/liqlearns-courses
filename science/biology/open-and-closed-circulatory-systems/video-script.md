Today: Open and closed circulatory systems.

Remember this one thing: animals move substances two ways: in an open circulatory system the blood leaves the vessels and bathes the tissues directly, while in a closed system it never leaves the vessels and capillaries do the exchanging — and some simple animals, like jellyfish, manage with no system at all, exchanging by diffusion.
