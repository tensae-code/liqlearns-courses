# Open and closed circulatory systems

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Open and closed circulatory systems. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Animals move substances two ways: in an open circulatory system the blood leaves the vessels and bathes the tissues directly, while in a closed system it never leaves the vessels and capillaries do the exchanging — and some simple animals, like jellyfish, manage with no system at all, exchanging by diffusion. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from Greece's Gymnasio and Lykeio biology books, which teach it as a named topic.
