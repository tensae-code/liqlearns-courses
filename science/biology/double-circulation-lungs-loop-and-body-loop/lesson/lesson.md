# Double circulation — lungs loop and body loop

## Do it first

## The idea
Blood does not do one round trip; it does two, sharing one heart. Trace the lungs loop and the body loop, then count the beat at your wrist.

## The key facts
**Pulmonary loop.** Pulmonary circulation carries blood between heart and lungs.

**Systemic loop.** Systemic circulation carries blood between heart and the rest of the body.

**One heart.** The two loops share the heart.

**Pulse.** Pulse rate measures the heart's beat, made countable.
