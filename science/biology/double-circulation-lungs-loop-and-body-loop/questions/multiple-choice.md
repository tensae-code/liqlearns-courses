# Double circulation — lungs loop and body loop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about pulmonary loop?
a) Pulmonary circulation carries blood between heart and lungs  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Pulmonary circulation carries blood between heart and lungs**

**2.** What does this lesson say about systemic loop?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Systemic circulation carries blood between heart and the rest of the body  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Systemic circulation carries blood between heart and the rest of the body**

**3.** What does this lesson say about one heart?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) The two loops share the heart  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The two loops share the heart**

**4.** What does this lesson say about pulse?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Pulse rate measures the heart's beat, made countable
**Answer: d) Pulse rate measures the heart's beat, made countable**
