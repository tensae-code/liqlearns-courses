# Thermodynamics and entropy in ecosystems — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (an ecosystem runs on the laws of thermodynamics — energy is transformed, never created or destroyed, and at every transfer some useful energy is lost as heat while entropy rises — so energy flows one way through the trophic levels and must keep arriving, while materials cycle) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: setting this lesson beside the ecological-pyramids lesson (why the energy pyramid can never stand upside down) and beside the cycling-of-materials lesson (why materials may cycle where energy may not), and explaining all three without looking.
