# Thermodynamics and entropy in ecosystems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the first law of thermodynamics say inside an ecosystem?
a) Energy is transformed, never created or destroyed — it can always be accounted for
b) Energy appears from nothing wherever life needs it
c) It plays no part in this topic at all
d) It happens only at night and nowhere else
**Answer: a) Energy is transformed, never created or destroyed — it can always be accounted for**

**2.** What happens to useful energy at every transfer between trophic levels?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Part of it degrades into heat the living system cannot use again — entropy rises
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Part of it degrades into heat the living system cannot use again — entropy rises**

**3.** Where does Costa Rica's anthology teach the laws of thermodynamics?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) At the head of Unit 2, printed pp. 66–67 — before trophic levels, food chains and the food pyramid
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) At the head of Unit 2, printed pp. 66–67 — before trophic levels, food chains and the food pyramid**

**4.** Which of these is true of an ecosystem?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Energy flows through it one way and must keep arriving; materials cycle and are used again
**Answer: d) Energy flows through it one way and must keep arriving; materials cycle and are used again**
