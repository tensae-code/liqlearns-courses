# Thermodynamics and entropy in ecosystems — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Only a fraction of one trophic level's energy reaches the next level.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Energy cycles round an ecosystem the way carbon and nitrogen do.
**Answer: False** — energy flows one way and leaves as heat; it is materials that cycle. An ecosystem needs energy arriving constantly.

**3.** Entropy is the disorder that rises at every energy transfer.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Costa Rica's anthology teaches food chains first and never mentions thermodynamics.
**Answer: False** — Unit 2 opens with Termodinámica de los ecosistemas, Leyes de la termodinámica and Fenómeno de entropía at printed pp. 66–67, before the trophic levels and the food pyramid.
