Today: thermodynamics and entropy in ecosystems.

Remember this one thing: an ecosystem obeys the laws of physics — and Costa Rica's biology book teaches those laws outright, by name, before it teaches a single food chain.

First law: energy is never created or destroyed, only transformed. The lion's energy was the zebra's, the zebra's was the grass's, the grass's was the sunlight's.

Second law: no transformation is perfect. At every step, some useful energy escapes as heat — disorder, entropy, rises. So only a fraction of the energy climbs to the next trophic level. That is why the food pyramid has the shape it has — and Costa Rica's book puts the laws on pages 66 and 67, and the pyramid right after, on page 70.

Energy flows one way. Materials cycle. Keep those two apart and the whole unit falls into place.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
