# Affective relationships and attachment — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: what does Bolivia's "Biología de las relaciones afectivas" unit teach?
**Answer: The unit (Texto de Aprendizaje — Biología-Geografía — Segundo Año de Secundaria, printed pp. 156–158) teaches affective relationships as biology-and-health content: attachment — the bond a person builds with the people close to them — and the right to a life free of violence in relationships.**

**2.** Explain: what is Bolivia's own way in this unit?
**Answer: Bolivia gives relationships a named unit of their own inside the biology book itself — "Biología de las relaciones afectivas" — set among the body's own topics (after fertilisation and embryonic development, before nutrition and the sense organs) and taught in the Práctica → Teoría → Valoración → Producción cycle its "Vida Tierra Territorio" area texts run on, rather than leaving relationships to a separate booklet or poster.**
