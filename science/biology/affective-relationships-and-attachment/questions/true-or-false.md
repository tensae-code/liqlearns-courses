# Affective relationships and attachment — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Bolivia's book teaches affective relationships as a named unit inside the biology book.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Attachment is the bond a person builds with the people close to them.
**Answer: True**

**3.** The unit says violence is acceptable where people are close.
**Answer: False** — the unit names the right to a life free of violence in relationships.

**4.** The unit appears only on a poster, not in the book's teaching sequence.
**Answer: False** — it is a named unit, "Biología de las relaciones afectivas", in the Segundo Año book (printed pp. 156–158).
