# Affective relationships and attachment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the name of the unit Bolivia's book gives this topic?
a) "Biología de las relaciones afectivas"
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) "Biología de las relaciones afectivas"**

**2.** What is attachment, as the unit teaches it?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The bond a person builds with the people close to them
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The bond a person builds with the people close to them**

**3.** What right does the unit name?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The right to a life free of violence in relationships
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The right to a life free of violence in relationships**

**4.** Where does Bolivia's book teach this unit?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Inside the Biología-Geografía student book for Segundo Año de Secundaria, among the body's own topics
**Answer: d) Inside the Biología-Geografía student book for Segundo Año de Secundaria, among the body's own topics**
