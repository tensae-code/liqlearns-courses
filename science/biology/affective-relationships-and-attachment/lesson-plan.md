# Lesson plan — Affective relationships and attachment

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Affective relationships and attachment
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** relationships have a biology — Bolivia's Segundo Año de Secundaria Biología-Geografía book teaches a named unit, "Biología de las relaciones afectivas" (the biology of affective relationships): attachment — the bond a person builds with the people close to them — and the right to a life free of violence in relationships (Bolivia, Grade 8 — *Texto de Aprendizaje — Biología-Geografía — Segundo Año de Secundaria*, student textbook, printed pp. 156–158).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own — a learner can land here directly and follow the whole lesson. The fertilisation, implantation and pregnancy lesson is recommended first, never forced — it sits just before this unit in Bolivia's book.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: **Fertilisation, implantation and pregnancy**. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say, in their own words, the one thing this lesson teaches: Bolivia's book teaches affective relationships as biology — attachment, and the right to a life free of violence in relationships.
2. Say what attachment is: the bond a person builds with the people close to them.
3. State the right the unit names: every person has the right to a life free of violence in their relationships.
4. Explain Bolivia's way: this content is a named unit — "Biología de las relaciones afectivas" — inside the biology book itself, in the Práctica → Teoría → Valoración → Producción cycle Bolivia's area texts run on.
