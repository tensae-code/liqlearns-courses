Today: affective relationships and attachment.

Remember this one thing: relationships have a biology. Bolivia's biology book for the second year of secondary school teaches a whole unit on it — "Biología de las relaciones afectivas", the biology of affective relationships.

The unit teaches two things. The first is attachment: the bond a person builds with the people close to them — the tie that holds a relationship together. The second is a right: every person has the right to a life free of violence in their relationships. A bond is never a reason for violence; the book puts the right inside the biology unit itself.

And notice where this lives: not in a separate booklet, but in the biology book, in the same Práctica, Teoría, Valoración, Producción cycle as the hormones and the sense organs around it.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
