Today: The great land biomes.

Remember this one thing: the great land biomes are characterised by climate and their dominant plants and animals.

Land biomes include tropical rainforest, savanna grassland, desert, temperate broad-leaf forest and tundra.

Temperate broad-leaf forest has deciduous trees dropping leaves before winter, mammals hibernating, and 70 to 200 plus cm of rain.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
