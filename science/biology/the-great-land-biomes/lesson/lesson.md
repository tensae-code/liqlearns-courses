# The great land biomes

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The great land biomes. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Rainforest, savanna, desert, temperate broad-leaf forest and tundra — each with its fauna and flora.

[Picture 21077 — a world strip of the biomes in climate order]

## The key facts
**Biomes named.** Land biomes include tropical rainforest, savanna grassland, desert, temperate broad-leaf forest and tundra.

**Temperate forest.** Temperate broad-leaf forest has deciduous trees dropping leaves before winter, mammals hibernating, and 70 to 200 plus cm of rain.

**Climate.** Biomes are characterised by climate.

**Dominant life.** Biomes are characterised by their dominant plants and animals.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: the great land biomes are characterised by climate and their dominant plants and animals. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
