# The great land biomes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about biomes named?
a) Land biomes include tropical rainforest, savanna grassland, desert, temperate broad-leaf forest and tundra
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Land biomes include tropical rainforest, savanna grassland, desert, temperate broad-leaf forest and tundra**

**2.** What does this lesson say about temperate forest?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Temperate broad-leaf forest has deciduous trees dropping leaves before winter, mammals hibernating, and 70 to 200 plus cm of rain
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Temperate broad-leaf forest has deciduous trees dropping leaves before winter, mammals hibernating, and 70 to 200 plus cm of rain**

**3.** What does this lesson say about climate?

**Answer: c) Biomes are characterised by climate**

**4.** What does this lesson say about dominant life?
