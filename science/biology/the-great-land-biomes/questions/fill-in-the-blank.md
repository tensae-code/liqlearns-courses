# The great land biomes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Land biomes include tropical rainforest, _____.
**Answer: savanna grassland, desert, temperate broad-leaf forest and tundra**

**2.** Temperate broad-leaf forest has deciduous trees dropping leaves before winter, _____.
**Answer: mammals hibernating, and 70 to 200 plus cm of rain**
