# Diffusion — spreading from crowded to less crowded — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about membrane?
a) The cell membrane is selectively permeable, controlling what enters and leaves
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The cell membrane is selectively permeable, controlling what enters and leaves**

**2.** What does this lesson say about diffusion?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Diffusion is movement of molecules from high to low concentration down the gradient
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Diffusion is movement of molecules from high to low concentration down the gradient**

**3.** What does this lesson say about no energy?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all

**Answer: c) Diffusion needs no energy — it is passive transport**
