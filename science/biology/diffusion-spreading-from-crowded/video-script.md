Today: Diffusion — spreading from crowded to less crowded.

Remember this one thing: diffusion moves molecules from high to low concentration, needing no energy.

The cell membrane is selectively permeable, controlling what enters and leaves.

Diffusion is movement of molecules from high to low concentration down the gradient.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
