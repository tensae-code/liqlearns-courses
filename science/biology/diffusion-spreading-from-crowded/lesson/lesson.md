# Diffusion — spreading from crowded to less crowded

## The idea
The cell membrane is selectively permeable — it controls what enters and leaves. Diffusion is its simplest crossing.

## The key facts
**Membrane.** The cell membrane is selectively permeable, controlling what enters and leaves.

**Diffusion.** Diffusion is movement of molecules from high to low concentration down the gradient.

**No energy.** Diffusion needs no energy — it is passive transport.

**Gases.** Gases such as oxygen for respiration cross the membrane by diffusion.
