# World biomes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about climate decides the biome?
a) Temperature and rainfall together decide which biome covers a region; the plants that can live there follow, and the animals follow the plants
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Temperature and rainfall together decide which biome covers a region; the plants that can live there follow, and the animals follow the plants**

**2.** What does this lesson say about the cold biomes?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Tundra — treeless, with permanently frozen ground below the surface — and the coniferous taiga below it are the cold biomes
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Tundra — treeless, with permanently frozen ground below the surface — and the coniferous taiga below it are the cold biomes**

**3.** What does this lesson say about temperate biomes?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Temperate deciduous forest and temperate grassland fill the middle latitudes where seasons are clear and rainfall is moderate
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Temperate deciduous forest and temperate grassland fill the middle latitudes where seasons are clear and rainfall is moderate**

**4.** What does this lesson say about the dry biomes?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Deserts — hot and cold — are the biomes of too little rain, where plants space themselves out and store or save water, and animals come out at night
**Answer: d) Deserts — hot and cold — are the biomes of too little rain, where plants space themselves out and store or save water, and animals come out at night**
