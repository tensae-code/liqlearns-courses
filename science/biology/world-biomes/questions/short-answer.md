# World biomes — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: temperate biomes.
**Answer: Deciduous trees shed their leaves against the winter cold, in forests that once covered much of Europe, eastern Asia and North America. Where rain falls short of forest, grassland takes over — steppe and prairie, grazed by herds and now largely ploughed for grain.**

**2.** Explain: the dry biomes.
**Answer: Below roughly 250 mm of rain a year, vegetation thins to scattered, water-wise plants — deep roots, small or waxy leaves, water stored in stems — and the animals shelter by day. Afghanistan's own southern and western deserts are part of this biome.**
