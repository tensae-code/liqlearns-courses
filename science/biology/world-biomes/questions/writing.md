# World biomes — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining world biomes, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: A biome is a great region of the Earth sorted by its climate — its rainfall and temperature — each carrying its own characteristic plants and animals, fitted to that climate. Temperature and rainfall together decide which biome covers a region; the plants that can live there follow, and the animals follow the plants. Tundra — treeless, with permanently frozen ground below the surface — and the coniferous taiga below it are the cold biomes. Temperate deciduous forest and temperate grassland fill the middle latitudes where seasons are clear and rainfall is moderate.**
