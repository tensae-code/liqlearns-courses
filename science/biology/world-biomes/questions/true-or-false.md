# World biomes — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Temperature and rainfall together decide which biome covers a region; the plants that can live there follow, and the animals follow the plants.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A biome is decided by the country and borders it lies in..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Tundra — treeless, with permanently frozen ground below the surface — and the coniferous taiga below it are the cold biomes.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Desert plants grow densely packed together to share the rainfall..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
