# Hands-on — World biomes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Place Afghanistan on the biome strip: which biomes does the country hold — mountain, steppe, desert? Write the evidence from the plants around you for the biome nearest your home.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a world strip from pole to equator with the biomes banded in order — tundra, taiga, deciduous forest, grassland, desert, savanna, rainforest — each band with its emblem plant drawn in it — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
