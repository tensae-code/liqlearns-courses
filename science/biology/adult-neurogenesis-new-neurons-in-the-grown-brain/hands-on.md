# Hands-on — Adult neurogenesis — new neurons in the grown brain

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: new neurons are born in the adult brain — in the hippocampus and the olfactory bulb among other places — against the old dogma that brain cells only die, and running, experience and stress can raise or lower that birth rate.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: an adult brain in side view with two small bright regions marked — the hippocampus deep inside and the olfactory bulb at the front — each with tiny new neurons drawn growing there. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
