# Adult neurogenesis — new neurons in the grown brain — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: new neurons are born in the adult brain — in the hippocampus and the olfactory bulb among other places — against the old dogma that brain cells only die, and running, experience and stress can raise or lower that birth rate. adult neurogenesis against the old dogma: new neurons in the adult rat (1977) and the canary's song centre, running increasing hippocampal neurogenesis in mice, odour experience adding olfactory-bulb neurons, stress and a mother's prenatal anxiety suppressing it — and the prospect of growing neurons to repair injured brains. No existing lesson covers it.**
