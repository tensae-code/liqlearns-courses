# Adult neurogenesis — new neurons in the grown brain — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. adult neurogenesis against the old dogma: new neurons in the adult rat (1977) and the canary's song centre, running increasing hippocampal neurogenesis in mice, odour experience adding olfactory-bulb neurons, stress and a mother's prenatal anxiety suppressing it — and the prospect of growing neurons to repair injured brains. No existing lesson covers it.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修1 稳态与调节 (Senior High Biology, Selective Compulsory Book 1: Homeostasis and Regulation, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with adult neurogenesis — new neurons in the grown brain.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is adult neurogenesis — new neurons in the grown brain.
