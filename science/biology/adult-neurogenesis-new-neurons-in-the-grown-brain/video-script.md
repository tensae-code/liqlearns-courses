Today: Adult neurogenesis — new neurons in the grown brain.

Remember this one thing: new neurons are born in the adult brain — in the hippocampus and the olfactory bulb among other places — against the old dogma that brain cells only die, and running, experience and stress can raise or lower that birth rate.

adult neurogenesis against the old dogma: new neurons in the adult rat (1977) and the canary's song centre, running increasing hippocampal neurogenesis in mice, odour experience adding olfactory-bulb neurons, stress and a mother's prenatal anxiety suppressing it — and the prospect of growing neurons to repair injured brains. No existing lesson covers it.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
