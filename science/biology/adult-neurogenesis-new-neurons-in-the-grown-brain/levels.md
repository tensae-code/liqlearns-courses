# Adult neurogenesis — new neurons in the grown brain — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (new neurons are born in the adult brain — in the hippocampus and the olfactory bulb among other places — against the old dogma that brain cells only die, and running, experience and stress can raise or lower that birth rate) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
