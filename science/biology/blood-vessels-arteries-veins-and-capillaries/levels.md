# Blood vessels — arteries, veins and capillaries — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the blood travels in three kinds of vessel — arteries carrying it away from the heart in thick elastic walls, capillaries with walls one cell thick where exchange happens, and veins carrying it back with valves against the low pressure — each kind built for its part of the journey) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
