Today: Blood vessels — arteries, veins and capillaries.

Remember this one thing: the blood travels in three kinds of vessel — arteries carrying it away from the heart in thick elastic walls, capillaries with walls one cell thick where exchange happens, and veins carrying it back with valves against the low pressure — each kind built for its part of the journey.
