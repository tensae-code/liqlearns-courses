# Blood vessels — arteries, veins and capillaries — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the blood travels in three kinds of vessel — arteries carrying it away from the heart in thick elastic walls, capillaries with walls one cell thick where exchange happens, and veins carrying it back with valves against the low pressure — each kind built for its part of the journey. blood vessels as their own section**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: blood vessels as their own section**
