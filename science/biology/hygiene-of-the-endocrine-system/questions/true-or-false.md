# Hygiene of the endocrine system — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 8 book (4.9 Hygiene of the endocrine system) teaches: risk factors and gland disorders set out as rules, diet restrictions for endocrine health. (printed p. 62).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie: Manual pentru clasa a VIII-a (Grade 8)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with hygiene of the endocrine system.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is hygiene of the endocrine system.
