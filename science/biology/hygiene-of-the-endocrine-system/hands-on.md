# Hands-on — Hygiene of the endocrine system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the endocrine glands are kept healthy by hygiene: the risk factors and gland disorders are set out as rules, including diet restrictions for endocrine health.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: the endocrine glands of a human figure with a set of hygiene rule cards beside them — food, rest and avoiding risk factors. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
