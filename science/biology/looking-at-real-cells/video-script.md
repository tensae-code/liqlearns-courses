Today: Looking at real cells.

Remember this one thing: real cells are observed under the microscope and drawn as they actually appear.

Onion epidermis and cheek cells are prepared and mounted for observation.

Pupils draw and label what is actually visible rather than copying a textbook diagram.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
