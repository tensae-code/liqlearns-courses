Today: Prions, viroids and other acellular agents.

Remember this one thing: viroids and prions are smaller and simpler than viruses, and neither one is a cell.

The viroid first. It is a tiny ring — a small circular molecule of RNA — and it has no protein coat at all. Just the instruction molecule, alone.

The prion is stranger. It is only a protein, but folded the wrong way. And the wrong fold spreads: the misfolded prion converts the body's normal protein into more misfolded prions. That is the mechanism the Jordanian teacher guide teaches through mad-cow disease — and when nerve cells die, the symptoms follow.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
