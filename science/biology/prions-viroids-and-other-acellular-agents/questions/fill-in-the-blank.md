# Prions, viroids and other acellular agents — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A viroid is a small _____ RNA molecule with no protein coat.**
**Answer: circular**

**2. A prion is an infectious _____.**
**Answer: protein**

**3. A misfolded prion converts _____ protein into the misfolded form.**
**Answer: normal**

**4. In the teacher guide's account, the symptoms of mad-cow disease follow from the death of _____ cells.**
**Answer: nerve**
