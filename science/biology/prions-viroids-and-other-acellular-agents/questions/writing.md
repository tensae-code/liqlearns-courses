# Prions, viroids and other acellular agents — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how something with no cells and no genes of the usual kind — a prion — can still spread a disease.
**Model answer: A prion is a misfolded protein. When it meets the body's normal protein of the same kind, it converts that protein into the misfolded form, so the misfolding spreads from protein to protein. As the misfolded proteins accumulate and nerve cells die, the disease's symptoms appear.**
