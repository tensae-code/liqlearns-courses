# Prions, viroids and other acellular agents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is a viroid?**
a) A small bacterium  b) A small circular RNA molecule with no protein coat  c) A protein coat with no RNA  d) A kind of white blood cell
**Answer: b) A small circular RNA molecule with no protein coat**

**2. What is a prion?**
a) An infectious protein  b) A small virus with a coat  c) A circular RNA molecule  d) A single-celled fungus
**Answer: a) An infectious protein**

**3. How does a prion spread its effect, in the mad-cow discussion?**
a) It releases a poison into the blood  b) It converts normal protein into the misfolded form, protein by protein  c) It copies its RNA inside the nucleus  d) It is carried from cell to cell by white blood cells
**Answer: b) It converts normal protein into the misfolded form, protein by protein**

**4. How do viroids and prions compare with true viruses, as Jordan's student book states it?**
a) They are larger and more complex  b) They are smaller and simpler  c) They are exactly the same  d) They are cells, viruses are not
**Answer: b) They are smaller and simpler**
