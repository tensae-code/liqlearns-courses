# Prions, viroids and other acellular agents — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A viroid has a protein coat around its RNA.**
**Answer: False** — the viroid is a small circular RNA molecule with no protein coat.

**2. Viroids and prions are smaller and simpler than true viruses.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. A prion is made of RNA.**
**Answer: False** — a prion is a protein; there is no nucleic acid in it.

**4. Prusiner's name is attached to the work on prions in Jordan's teacher guide.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
