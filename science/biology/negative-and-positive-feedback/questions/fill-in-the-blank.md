# Negative and positive feedback — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** The holding-steady of the body's inside conditions is called _____.
**Answer: homeostasis**

**2.** In the temperature example, the widening of the skin's blood vessels is called _____.
**Answer: vasodilation**

**3.** Positive feedback _____ a change, making it bigger.
**Answer: amplifies**
