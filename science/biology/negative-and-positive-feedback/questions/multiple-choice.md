# Negative and positive feedback — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What does negative feedback do to a change?**
a) It reverses the change, pushing the condition back
b) It amplifies the change, making it bigger
c) It ignores the change completely
d) It happens only in plants
**Answer: a) It reverses the change, pushing the condition back**

**2. In the temperature example, what happens first after body temperature rises?**
a) Sweating stops and the vessels narrow
b) A centre in the brain detects the rise and sends out orders
c) The temperature falls at once with no answer from the body
d) The zoologists are honoured
**Answer: b) A centre in the brain detects the rise and sends out orders**

**3. How do vasodilation and sweating cool the body?**
a) The vessels close and sweat stays inside
b) Wider skin vessels lose heat at the surface, and evaporating sweat carries heat away
c) They make the brain centre switch off
d) They amplify the temperature rise
**Answer: b) Wider skin vessels lose heat at the surface, and evaporating sweat carries heat away**

**4. What does the Ethiopian zoologists part of the lesson say their life-long study serves?**
a) Only foreign universities
b) Nothing beyond their own careers
c) Both science and national development
d) Only the temperature example
**Answer: c) Both science and national development**
