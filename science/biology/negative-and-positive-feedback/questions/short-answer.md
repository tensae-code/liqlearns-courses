# Negative and positive feedback — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. Walk the temperature example round its circle: what happens from the moment body temperature rises until it falls again?**
**Answer: The brain centre detects the rise and sends out orders; the skin vessels widen (vasodilation) and sweating starts, heat leaves the body, and the temperature falls back to its steady point.**

**2. How is positive feedback different from negative feedback?**
**Answer: Negative feedback reverses a change and restores the steady state; positive feedback amplifies a change, pushing the condition further in the same direction.**

**3. Who are the Ethiopian zoologists this lesson honours, and what does their work serve?**
**Answer: They are Ethiopian university zoologists who have spent their working lives studying the country's animals; their study serves both science and national development.**
