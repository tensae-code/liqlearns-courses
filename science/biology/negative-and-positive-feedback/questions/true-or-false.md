# Negative and positive feedback — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Positive feedback reverses a change and holds the body steady.**
**Answer: False** — positive feedback amplifies a change; negative feedback reverses it.

**2. When body temperature falls, the same machinery runs in reverse: the vessels narrow and sweating stops.**
**Answer: True**

**3. Vasodilation means the blood vessels in the skin narrow.**
**Answer: False** — vasodilation means the vessels widen, so more blood flows near the surface and loses heat.

**4. The Ethiopian zoologists honoured in this lesson are university scientists who study the country's animals.**
**Answer: True**
