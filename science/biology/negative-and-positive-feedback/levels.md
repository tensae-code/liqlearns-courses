# Negative and positive feedback — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: explain homeostasis in your own words, walk the temperature example round its full circle (rise → brain centre → vasodilation and sweating → fall; falling temperature reverses the machinery), separate negative feedback (reverses) from positive feedback (amplifies), and say who the Ethiopian zoologists are and what their life-long study serves.
This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Practice deepens inside the one skill: more items to name or sort, new examples from the learner's own home and surroundings, and the harder corners of the same thing — telling apart the cases that look alike — before the learner moves on.
