# Video script — Negative and positive feedback

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your body holds its inside steady. That is homeostasis.

When your temperature rises, a centre in your brain answers.
Your skin vessels widen. You sweat.
Heat leaves your body, and your temperature falls.
The change is reversed. That is negative feedback.

When your temperature falls, the same machinery runs in reverse.
The vessels narrow. Sweating stops. You warm up again.

Positive feedback is the opposite: it makes a change bigger.

And remember the Ethiopian zoologists — a life of studying Ethiopia's animals, serving science and their country.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
