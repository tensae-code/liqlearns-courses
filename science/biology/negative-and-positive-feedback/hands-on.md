# Hands-on — Negative and positive feedback

The lesson with your hands and your eyes, not only your reading.

## Feel the loop
Run on the spot for two minutes, or step out into strong sun. Note what your body does — sweating, skin feeling warm and flushed — then sit in shade and note how it settles. Write the four steps of the temperature loop using what you felt: rise → brain centre → vasodilation and sweating → fall.

## Compare
Write the two kinds of feedback side by side: one way they are alike (both are the body answering a change) and the way they differ (one reverses the change, the other amplifies it).

## Teach it
Explain the loop to someone at home using your own hot-day example. Note the question they asked and your answer.
