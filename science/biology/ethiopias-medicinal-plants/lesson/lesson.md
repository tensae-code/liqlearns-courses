# Ethiopia's medicinal plants

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Ethiopia's medicinal plants. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Plants heal as well as feed. Ethiopia's own medicinal plants are set out in a working table — the local knowledge, organised the scientific way.

[Picture 22229 — a small herbarium-style panel of four named local medicinal plants]

## The key facts
**The table.** Medicinal plants are recorded with local name, kind, part used, how taken and condition treated.

**Fourteen and more.** The table's entries run to at least fourteen Ethiopian medicinal plants.

**Feto.** Feto — Lepidium sativum — has its seed taken orally for diarrhoea.

**Why they matter.** The lesson fixes why medicinal plants matter to local people.
