# The language areas of the cortex — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (language lives in four areas of the left cerebral cortex — W, V, H and S — each found by the deficit its damage leaves: the patient who understands but cannot speak, and the patient who speaks but cannot understand, map them before the map is shown) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
