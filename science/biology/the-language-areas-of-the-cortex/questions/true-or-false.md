# The language areas of the cortex — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. the four speech areas (W, V, H, S) derived from two lesion cases before the map is shown (the patient who understands but cannot speak; the patient who speaks but cannot understand), each area's deficit named, and the left hemisphere's dominance stated with its right-hemisphere counterpart in image thinking. No existing lesson holds the language areas.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修1 稳态与调节 (Senior High Biology, Selective Compulsory Book 1: Homeostasis and Regulation, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the language areas of the cortex.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the language areas of the cortex.
