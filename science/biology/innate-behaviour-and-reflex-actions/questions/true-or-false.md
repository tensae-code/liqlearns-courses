# Innate behaviour and reflex actions — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Behaviour is how animals interact with other organisms and the environment; innate behaviour is inborn, gene-determined and species-specific — fixed patterns such as spider web-making, bird nest-building and a chick gaping when its mother returns, the honeybee's food dance, and simple reflexes, in which the message travels only as far as the spinal cord (pulling a hand from a hot object, blinking).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
