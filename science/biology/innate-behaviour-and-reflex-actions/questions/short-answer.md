# Innate behaviour and reflex actions — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about innate behaviour and reflexes?**
**Answer: Behaviour is how animals interact with other organisms and the environment; innate behaviour is inborn, gene-determined and species-specific — fixed patterns such as spider web-making, bird nest-building and a chick gaping when its mother returns, the honeybee's food dance, and simple reflexes, in which the message travels only as far as the spinal cord (pulling a hand from a hot object, blinking).**
