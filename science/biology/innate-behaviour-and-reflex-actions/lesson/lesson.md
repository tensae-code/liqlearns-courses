# Innate behaviour and reflex actions

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — innate behaviour and reflex actions. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Behaviour is how animals interact with other organisms and the environment; innate behaviour is inborn, gene-determined and species-specific — fixed patterns such as spider web-making, bird nest-building and a chick gaping when its mother returns, the honeybee's food dance, and simple reflexes, in which the message travels only as far as the spinal cord (pulling a hand from a hot object, blinking).
