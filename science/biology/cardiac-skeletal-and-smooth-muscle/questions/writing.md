# Cardiac, skeletal and smooth muscle — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1. ** Write two or three sentences explaining three types of muscle to a younger student. Use one example from the lesson.
**Model answer: Cardiac muscle (striated, involuntary, in the heart wall, cells joined by intercalated discs), skeletal muscle (attached to bones, voluntary movement) and smooth muscle (unstriated, involuntary, in the walls of gut, bladder, uterus, vessels and airways, and raising hairs in the skin)**

**2. ** Write a short paragraph that explains three types of muscle to a friend who missed the lesson. End with one example.
**Model answer: Cardiac muscle (striated, involuntary, in the heart wall, cells joined by intercalated discs), skeletal muscle (attached to bones, voluntary movement) and smooth muscle (unstriated, involuntary, in the walls of gut, bladder, uterus, vessels and airways, and raising hairs in the skin).**

**3. ** Write two questions of your own about three types of muscle and answer each one from the lesson.
**Model answer: any two questions whose answers are stated in the lesson text above.**
