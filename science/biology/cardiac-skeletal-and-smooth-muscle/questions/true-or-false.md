# Cardiac, skeletal and smooth muscle — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Cardiac muscle (striated, involuntary, in the heart wall, cells joined by intercalated discs), skeletal muscle (attached to bones, voluntary movement) and smooth muscle (unstriated, involuntary, in the walls of gut, bladder, uterus, vessels and airways, and raising hairs in the skin).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
