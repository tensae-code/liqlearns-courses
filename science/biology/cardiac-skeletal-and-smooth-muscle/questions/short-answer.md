# Cardiac, skeletal and smooth muscle — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about three types of muscle?**
**Answer: Cardiac muscle (striated, involuntary, in the heart wall, cells joined by intercalated discs), skeletal muscle (attached to bones, voluntary movement) and smooth muscle (unstriated, involuntary, in the walls of gut, bladder, uterus, vessels and airways, and raising hairs in the skin).**
