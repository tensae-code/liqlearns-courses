# Cardiac, skeletal and smooth muscle — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. ** _____ (striated, involuntary, in the heart wall, cells joined by intercalated discs), skeletal muscle (attached to bones, voluntary movement) and smooth muscle (unstriated, involuntary, in the walls of gut, bladder, uterus, vessels and airways, and raising hairs in the skin).
**Answer: Cardiac muscle**
