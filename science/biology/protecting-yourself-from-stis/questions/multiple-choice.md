# Protecting yourself from STIs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about faithfulness?
a) Risk is reduced by faithfulness to one uninfected partner
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Risk is reduced by faithfulness to one uninfected partner**

**2.** What does this lesson say about unknown status?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Avoid sex with partners whose infection status is unknown
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Avoid sex with partners whose infection status is unknown**

**3.** What does this lesson say about condoms?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Correct condom use (male or female) acts as a barrier to bacteria and viruses
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Correct condom use (male or female) acts as a barrier to bacteria and viruses**

**4.** What does this lesson say about treatment?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Anyone suspecting infection should seek testing and treatment early, and partners should be treated too
**Answer: d) Anyone suspecting infection should seek testing and treatment early, and partners should be treated too**
