# Protecting yourself from STIs

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Protecting yourself from STIs. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Anyone suspecting infection should seek testing and treatment early — and partners should be treated too.

[Picture 21051 — a shield-style panel of protective behaviours]

## The key facts
**Faithfulness.** Risk is reduced by faithfulness to one uninfected partner.

**Unknown status.** Avoid sex with partners whose infection status is unknown.

**Condoms.** Correct condom use (male or female) acts as a barrier to bacteria and viruses.

**Treatment.** Anyone suspecting infection should seek testing and treatment early, and partners should be treated too.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: faithfulness, avoiding unknown-status partners and correct condom use greatly reduce STI risk. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
