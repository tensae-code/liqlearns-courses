# Individual development — embryonic and post-embryonic — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (every organism has an individual development — its whole life built in order from the fertilised egg: first the embryonic period, from the fertilised egg to birth or hatching, then the post-embryonic period, from birth or hatching through growth and change to the adult) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
