# Individual development — embryonic and post-embryonic — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Individual development — ontogenesis — is the whole ordered building of one organism across its life: it begins with the fertilised egg and runs until the adult, and it is a basic property of organisms, taught in the book beside reproduction itself.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Bulgaria's Pedagog 6 biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Биология и здравно образование 10. клас (Pedagog 6), printed p. 65).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with individual development — embryonic and post-embryonic.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is individual development — embryonic and post-embryonic.
