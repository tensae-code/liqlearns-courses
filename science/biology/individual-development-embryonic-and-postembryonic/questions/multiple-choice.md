# Individual development — embryonic and post-embryonic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) every organism has an individual development — its whole life built in order from the fertilised egg: first the embryonic period, from the fertilised egg to birth or hatching, then the post-embryonic period, from birth or hatching through growth and change to the adult  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) every organism has an individual development — its whole life built in order from the fertilised egg: first the embryonic period, from the fertilised egg to birth or hatching, then the post-embryonic period, from birth or hatching through growth and change to the adult**

**2. Individual development — which statement is right?**
a) Individual development — ontogenesis — is the whole ordered building of one organism across its life: it begins with the fertilised egg and runs until the adult, and it is a basic property of organisms, taught in the book beside reproduction itself.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Individual development — ontogenesis — is the whole ordered building of one organism across its life: it begins with the fertilised egg and runs until the adult, and it is a basic property of organisms, taught in the book beside reproduction itself.**

**3. The embryonic period — which statement is right?**
