# Individual development — embryonic and post-embryonic — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: every organism has an individual development — its whole life built in order from the fertilised egg: first the embryonic period, from the fertilised egg to birth or hatching, then the post-embryonic period, from birth or hatching through growth and change to the adult. The embryonic period runs from the fertilised egg to birth or hatching. Through it the egg divides and the embryo is built — the body plan laid down inside the egg, the womb or the seed, protected and fed while it forms.**
