# Video script — What makes an animal — and the animal family tree

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Animals are eukaryotic, multicellular, heterotrophic and sensitive to stimuli; they reproduce, move, respire, excrete and grow, with different body symmetries.

The guide expands the student book's characteristics section: asymmetry (no plane divides the animal into equal halves — the most primitive animals), radial and bilateral symmetry figured; and fuller accounts of multicellularity with cell specialisation, motility supported by endoskeleton, exoskeleton or hydrostatic pressure, respiration as releasing energy from food in cells, and excretion.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
