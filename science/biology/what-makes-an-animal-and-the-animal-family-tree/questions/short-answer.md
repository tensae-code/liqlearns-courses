# What makes an animal — and the animal family tree — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about characteristics of animals and the two great groups?**
**Answer: Animals are eukaryotic, multicellular, heterotrophic and sensitive to stimuli; they reproduce, move, respire, excrete and grow, with different body symmetries.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added detail?**
**Answer: The guide expands the student book's characteristics section: asymmetry (no plane divides the animal into equal halves — the most primitive animals), radial and bilateral symmetry figured; and fuller accounts of multicellularity with cell specialisation, motility supported by endoskeleton, exoskeleton or hydrostatic pressure, respiration as releasing energy from food in cells, and excretion.**
