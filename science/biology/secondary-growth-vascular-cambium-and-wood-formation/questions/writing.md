**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: woody stems thicken by secondary growth: lateral meristems — the vascular cambium ring between xylem and phloem — lay down new tissue year by year, and that growth is the formation of wood. The vascular cambium forms a ring between the xylem and the phloem; its activity produces the secondary tissues, and the formation of wood is this growth seen from inside the stem.**
