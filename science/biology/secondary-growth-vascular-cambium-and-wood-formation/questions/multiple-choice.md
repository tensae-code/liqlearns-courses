# Secondary growth — vascular cambium and wood formation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) woody stems thicken by secondary growth: lateral meristems — the vascular cambium ring between xylem and phloem — lay down new tissue year by year, and that growth is the formation of wood  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) woody stems thicken by secondary growth: lateral meristems — the vascular cambium ring between xylem and phloem — lay down new tissue year by year, and that growth is the formation of wood**

**2. Lateral meristems — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 55–58) teaches secondary growth through the lateral meristems.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 55–58) teaches secondary growth through the lateral meristems.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) The vascular cambium forms a ring between the xylem and the phloem; its activity produces the secondary tissues, and the formation of wood is this growth seen from inside the stem.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Kuwait
**Answer: b) The vascular cambium forms a ring between the xylem and the phloem; its activity produces the secondary tissues, and the formation of wood is this growth seen from inside the stem.**
