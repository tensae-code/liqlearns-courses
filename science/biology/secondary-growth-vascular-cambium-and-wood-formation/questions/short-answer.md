# Secondary growth — vascular cambium and wood formation — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: woody stems thicken by secondary growth: lateral meristems — the vascular cambium ring between xylem and phloem — lay down new tissue year by year, and that growth is the formation of wood. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 55–58) teaches secondary growth through the lateral meristems.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's Grade 11 botany unit separates the two growths cleanly: primary growth from the apical meristems first (Lesson 1-4), then secondary growth and wood formation from the cambium ring.**
