# Secondary growth — vascular cambium and wood formation — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (woody stems thicken by secondary growth: lateral meristems — the vascular cambium ring between xylem and phloem — lay down new tissue year by year, and that growth is the formation of wood) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
