Today: Secondary growth — vascular cambium and wood formation.

Remember this one thing: woody stems thicken by secondary growth: lateral meristems — the vascular cambium ring between xylem and phloem — lay down new tissue year by year, and that growth is the formation of wood.

Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 55–58) teaches secondary growth through the lateral meristems.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
