# Two kinds of cell — with and without a nucleus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about prokaryotic?
a) Prokaryotic cells (bacteria) are smaller and simpler, with no nucleus or membrane-bound organelles
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Prokaryotic cells (bacteria) are smaller and simpler, with no nucleus or membrane-bound organelles**

**2.** What does this lesson say about first cells?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Prokaryotic cells are thought to be the first cells to evolve
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Prokaryotic cells are thought to be the first cells to evolve**

**3.** What does this lesson say about eukaryotic?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Eukaryotic cells (plants, animals, fungi, protists) have a true nucleus and organelles
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Eukaryotic cells (plants, animals, fungi, protists) have a true nucleus and organelles**

**4.** What does this lesson say about comparison?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) A comparison table sets prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells side by side
**Answer: d) A comparison table sets prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells side by side**
