# Two kinds of cell — with and without a nucleus

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Two kinds of cell — with and without a nucleus. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The deepest split among cells is whether the nucleus is there at all.

[Picture 21028 — a bacterium and a plant cell at the same scale]

## The key facts
**Prokaryotic.** Prokaryotic cells (bacteria) are smaller and simpler, with no nucleus or membrane-bound organelles.

**First cells.** Prokaryotic cells are thought to be the first cells to evolve.

**Eukaryotic.** Eukaryotic cells (plants, animals, fungi, protists) have a true nucleus and organelles.

**Comparison.** A comparison table sets prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells side by side.
