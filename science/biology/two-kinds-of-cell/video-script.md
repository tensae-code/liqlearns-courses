Today: Two kinds of cell — with and without a nucleus.

Remember this one thing: prokaryotic cells have no nucleus; eukaryotic cells have a true nucleus and organelles.

Prokaryotic cells (bacteria) are smaller and simpler, with no nucleus or membrane-bound organelles.

Prokaryotic cells are thought to be the first cells to evolve.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
