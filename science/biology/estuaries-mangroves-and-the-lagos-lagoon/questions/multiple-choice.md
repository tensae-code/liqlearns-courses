# Estuaries, mangroves and the Lagos lagoon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is an estuary's ruling hardship for living things?
a) Darkness
b) Salinity varying with tide and season
c) Cold
d) No oxygen anywhere
**Answer: b) Salinity varying with tide and season**

**2.** On Lagos lagoon mangrove roots, where is the barnacle Balanus found?
a) Where salinity stays high
b) Where salinity falls lowest
c) In fresh water only
d) On the leaves
**Answer: b) Where salinity falls lowest**

**3.** The oyster on the roots is…
a) Mercierella
b) Gryphaea
c) Balanus
d) Rhizophora
**Answer: b) Gryphaea**

**4.** The red mangrove's scientific name is…
a) Mangifera indica
b) Rhizophora racemosa
c) Isoberlinia doka
d) Talinum triangulare
**Answer: b) Rhizophora racemosa**
