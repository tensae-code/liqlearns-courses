# Estuaries, mangroves and the Lagos lagoon — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** The Lagos _____ system is the book's worked estuary.
**Answer: lagoon**

**2.** The oyster zoned on the mangrove roots is _____.
**Answer: Gryphaea**

**3.** Mercierella, Gryphaea and Balanus are zoned on the roots by _____.
**Answer: salinity**
