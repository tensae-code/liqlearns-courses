# Estuaries, mangroves and the Lagos lagoon — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Mercierella lives where salinity stays high on the mangrove roots.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Estuary salinity stays constant through the year.
**Answer: False** — It varies with tide and season — that variation is the habitat's defining factor.

**3.** The Niger delta is one of Nigeria's estuarine systems in the book.
**Answer: True**
