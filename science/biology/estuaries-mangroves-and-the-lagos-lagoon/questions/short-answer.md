# Estuaries, mangroves and the Lagos lagoon — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the zonation on Lagos lagoon mangrove roots.
**Answer: Salinity decides who lives where. On the red-mangrove stilt roots, Mercierella occupies the zone where salinity stays high, the oyster Gryphaea the zone where it is lower, and the barnacle Balanus where it falls lowest — three organisms sorted along one root system by one factor.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches the estuarine habitat in Chapter 13 (printed p. 196) from its own coast: estuary types — the open mouths of the Ogun, Osun and Osse rivers, the Niger delta, the Lagos lagoon system — salinity varying with tide and season, the observed zonation on red-mangrove stilt roots, and the red mangrove's (Rhizophora racemosa) adaptations.**
