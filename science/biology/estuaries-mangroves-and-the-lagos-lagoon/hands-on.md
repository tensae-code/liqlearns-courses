# Hands-on — Estuaries, mangroves and the Lagos lagoon

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
If you can reach a lagoon, creek or mangrove edge, look at the roots and trunks at the waterline and note what is attached at each height and place. If you cannot, draw the root system from the lesson and mark the three zones — Mercierella, Gryphaea, Balanus — in salinity order, and say aloud why each sits where it sits.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — red mangroves on stilt roots at the edge of the Lagos lagoon, with the root zones labelled: Mercierella high-salinity, oyster Gryphaea lower, barnacle Balanus lowest — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
