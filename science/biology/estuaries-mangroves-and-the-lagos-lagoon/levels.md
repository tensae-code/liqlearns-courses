# Estuaries, mangroves and the Lagos lagoon — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (an estuary is where river and sea mix, and its salinity swings with tide and season sort its life into zones: on the red-mangrove stilt roots of the Lagos lagoons, Mercierella lives where salinity stays high, the oyster Gryphaea where it is lower, and the barnacle Balanus where it falls lowest) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 13, printed p. 196), without looking.
