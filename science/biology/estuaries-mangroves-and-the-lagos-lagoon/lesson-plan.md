# Lesson plan — Estuaries, mangroves and the Lagos lagoon

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Estuaries, mangroves and the Lagos lagoon
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an estuary is where river and sea mix, and its salinity swings with tide and season sort its life into zones: on the red-mangrove stilt roots of the Lagos lagoons, Mercierella lives where salinity stays high, the oyster Gryphaea where it is lower, and the barnacle Balanus where it falls lowest.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own as a single-skill lesson built from Nigeria's Senior Secondary Biology 1, Ch. 13 (printed p. 196). Related lessons are recommended first where they help, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
