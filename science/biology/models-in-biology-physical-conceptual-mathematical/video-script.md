Today: Models in biology — physical, conceptual, mathematical.

Remember this one thing: biology thinks with models, and the models come in three kinds — physical models you can hold, conceptual models that organise ideas, and mathematical models that calculate — each judged by what it can and cannot show.

the model-building practical doubles as a typology lesson: physical, conceptual and mathematical models distinguished and each judged by what it can and cannot show. No existing lesson teaches models as a method.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
