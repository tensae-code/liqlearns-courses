# Models in biology — physical, conceptual, mathematical — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: biology thinks with models, and the models come in three kinds — physical models you can hold, conceptual models that organise ideas, and mathematical models that calculate — each judged by what it can and cannot show. the model-building practical doubles as a typology lesson: physical, conceptual and mathematical models distinguished and each judged by what it can and cannot show. No existing lesson teaches models as a method.**
