# Models in biology — physical, conceptual, mathematical — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. the model-building practical doubles as a typology lesson: physical, conceptual and mathematical models distinguished and each judged by what it can and cannot show. No existing lesson teaches models as a method.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》必修1 分子与细胞 (Senior High Biology, Compulsory Book 1: Molecules and Cells, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with models in biology — physical, conceptual, mathematical.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is models in biology — physical, conceptual, mathematical.
