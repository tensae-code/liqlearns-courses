# Models in biology — physical, conceptual, mathematical

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Models in biology — physical, conceptual, mathematical. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Biology thinks with models, and the models come in three kinds — physical models you can hold, conceptual models that organise ideas, and mathematical models that calculate — each judged by what it can and cannot show. No lesson in this course taught this on its own before, so this lesson is built for it — from China's People's Education Press biology textbooks, which teach it as a named topic.
