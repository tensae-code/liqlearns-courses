# DNA replication — copying the instructions — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about dna replication?**
**Answer: DNA copies itself semi-conservatively before division: the helix unwinds and unzips, each old strand templates a new complementary strand, DNA polymerase adding nucleotides 5′ to 3′ — one leading strand continuously, the other (lagging) strand in the opposite sense — and the two new molecules re-twist; a kitchen lab extracts visible DNA from a banana with salt, soap, blending, straining and alcohol.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added banana-dna explanation?**
**Answer: The guide explains the student book's extraction lab step by step: mashing exposes more cell surface, liquid soap breaks down cell membranes to release the DNA, salt helps the DNA stay together during mashing, filtration separates the mixture, and cold alcohol precipitates the DNA so it can be lifted out.**
