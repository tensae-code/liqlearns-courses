# DNA replication — copying the instructions — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) DNA copies itself semi-conservatively before division: the helix unwinds and unzips, each old strand templates a new complementary strand, DNA polymerase adding nucleotides 5′ to 3′ — one leading strand continuously, the other (lagging) strand in the opposite sense — and the two new molecules re-twist; a kitchen lab extracts visible DNA from a banana with salt, soap, blending, straining and alcohol.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
