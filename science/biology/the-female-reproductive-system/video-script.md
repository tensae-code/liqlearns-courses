# Video script — The female reproductive system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: The female reproductive system.

Remember this one thing: the female system makes eggs in the ovaries and carries a pregnancy in the uterus.

Ovaries produce eggs and secrete oestrogen and progesterone.

Oestrogen and progesterone develop female characteristics and prepare and maintain the uterus lining.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
