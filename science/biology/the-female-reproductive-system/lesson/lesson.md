# The female reproductive system

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The female reproductive system. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Ovaries deep in the pelvic cavity produce eggs and the hormones oestrogen and progesterone.

## The key facts
**Ovaries.** Ovaries produce eggs and secrete oestrogen and progesterone.

**Hormone jobs.** Oestrogen and progesterone develop female characteristics and prepare and maintain the uterus lining.

**Oviducts.** The oviducts (Fallopian tubes) carry the egg.

**Uterus.** The uterus is where a pregnancy develops; the cervix and vagina complete the system.

## The Grade 11 depth — the system part by part
The Grade 11 book sets the system out in full. The **external structures** are the labia majora and labia minora and the clitoris. The **internal organs** are the vagina — which is also the birth canal — the uterus with its cervix, the fallopian tubes and the ovaries.

**Each organ's function, as the book tables it.** The ovaries produce eggs and secrete hormones such as oestrogen. The fallopian tubes carry the egg from ovary toward uterus. The uterus is where a fetus grows through pregnancy; its cervix is the uterus's lower opening. The vagina receives sperm and serves as the birth canal through which a baby is born.

**The system's jobs, taken together:** producing eggs, secreting hormones such as oestrogen, providing the site of fertilisation, gestating a fetus, and giving birth. Every part above serves one or more of those jobs — name the part, and its job follows.
