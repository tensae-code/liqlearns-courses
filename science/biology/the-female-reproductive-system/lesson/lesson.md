# The female reproductive system

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The female reproductive system. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Ovaries deep in the pelvic cavity produce eggs and the hormones oestrogen and progesterone.

[Picture 21045 — a labelled front-view diagram with the egg's path arrowed]

## The key facts
**Ovaries.** Ovaries produce eggs and secrete oestrogen and progesterone.

**Hormone jobs.** Oestrogen and progesterone develop female characteristics and prepare and maintain the uterus lining.

**Oviducts.** The oviducts (Fallopian tubes) carry the egg.

**Uterus.** The uterus is where a pregnancy develops; the cervix and vagina complete the system.
