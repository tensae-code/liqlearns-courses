# The female reproductive system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about ovaries?
a) Ovaries produce eggs and secrete oestrogen and progesterone
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Ovaries produce eggs and secrete oestrogen and progesterone**

**2.** What does this lesson say about hormone jobs?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Oestrogen and progesterone develop female characteristics and prepare and maintain the uterus lining
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Oestrogen and progesterone develop female characteristics and prepare and maintain the uterus lining**

**3.** What does this lesson say about oviducts?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) The oviducts (Fallopian tubes) carry the egg
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The oviducts (Fallopian tubes) carry the egg**

**4.** What does this lesson say about uterus?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) The uterus is where a pregnancy develops; the cervix and vagina complete the system
**Answer: d) The uterus is where a pregnancy develops; the cervix and vagina complete the system**
