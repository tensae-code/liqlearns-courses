# The female reproductive system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: oviducts.
**Answer: The oviducts (Fallopian tubes) carry the egg.**

**2.** Explain: uterus.
**Answer: The uterus is where a pregnancy develops; the cervix and vagina complete the system.**
**3.** Explain: the system's jobs.
**Answer: Producing eggs, secreting hormones such as oestrogen, providing the site of fertilisation, gestating a fetus, and giving birth.**
