# Eye and ear care — correction and prevention — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (The faults of sight and hearing can be corrected and guarded against: short-sight and long-sight are corrected with lenses fitted to the fault, and eyes and ears keep working when they are protected before damage is done.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
