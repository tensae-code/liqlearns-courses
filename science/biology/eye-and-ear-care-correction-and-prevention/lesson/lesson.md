# Eye and ear care — correction and prevention

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Eye and ear care — correction and prevention. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Armenia's Grade 8 "Biology. Human" book teaches the senses as analyzers — and then, in §14, turns to what happens when the analyzer is faulty: the disorders of vision and hearing, how lenses correct them, and how both senses are protected.

## The key facts
**The analyzers can fail in their parts.** Armenia's Grade 8 "Biology. Human" book teaches each sense as an analyzer — receptor, conducting pathway and brain centre. The analyzers chapter does not end at structure: it closes with §14, the prevention and correction of vision and hearing disorders — what to do when the analyzer itself is faulty.

**Short-sight.** In short-sight (myopia) distant things are blurred: the eye's lens system bends the light too strongly, or the eyeball is too long, so the image of a distant object forms in front of the retina instead of on it. Near things are seen clearly.

**Long-sight.** In long-sight (hyperopia) the fault is the other way: the image forms behind the retina, so near things are blurred and the eye strains to pull the image forward. Distant things are seen more clearly.

**Correction with lenses.** Each fault is corrected by the lens shaped to undo it: a diverging (concave) lens spreads the rays for the short-sighted eye so the image falls back onto the retina; a converging (convex) lens gathers the rays for the long-sighted eye so the image falls forward onto it. The right lens puts the image exactly where the eye can read it.

**Protecting sight and hearing.** Prevention is the other half of the section: eyes are guarded by good light for reading and work, rest from straining, and care with bright light and injury; ears are guarded against very loud sound, against objects pushed into the ear, and by treating infections early. Correction fixes a fault that exists; protection stops the fault — or a worse one — from forming.

**Armenia's way.** The section is placed as the closing section of the analyzers chapter (Ch. 3) — after the eye and the ear have been taught as analyzers, the book turns to their disorders, their correction and their prevention, as hygiene, not as an afterthought.
