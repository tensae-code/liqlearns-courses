# Eye and ear care — correction and prevention — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** In short-sight, the image of a distant object forms in front of the retina.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A converging lens is the correction for short-sight.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**

**3.** Long-sight is corrected with a converging (convex) lens.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Pushing objects into the ear protects hearing.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
