# Eye and ear care — correction and prevention — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining eye and ear care — correction and prevention, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: The faults of sight and hearing can be corrected and guarded against: short-sight and long-sight are corrected with lenses fitted to the fault, and eyes and ears keep working when they are protected before damage is done. In short-sight the image forms in front of the retina; in long-sight it forms behind it. A diverging lens corrects short-sight and a converging lens corrects long-sight, putting the image exactly on the retina.**
