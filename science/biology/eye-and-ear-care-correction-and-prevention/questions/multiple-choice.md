# Eye and ear care — correction and prevention — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about short-sight?
a) Distant things are blurred because the image forms in front of the retina
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Distant things are blurred because the image forms in front of the retina**

**2.** What does this lesson say about long-sight?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The image forms behind the retina, so near things are blurred
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The image forms behind the retina, so near things are blurred**

**3.** What does this lesson say about correction with lenses?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) A diverging lens corrects short-sight and a converging lens corrects long-sight, putting the image on the retina
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) A diverging lens corrects short-sight and a converging lens corrects long-sight, putting the image on the retina**

**4.** What does this lesson say about protecting sight and hearing?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Good light and rest protect the eyes; protection from very loud sound and injury protects the ears
**Answer: d) Good light and rest protect the eyes; protection from very loud sound and injury protects the ears**
