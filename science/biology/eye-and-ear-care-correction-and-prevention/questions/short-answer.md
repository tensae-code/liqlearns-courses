# Eye and ear care — correction and prevention — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: correction with lenses.
**Answer: Each fault is corrected by the lens shaped to undo it: a diverging (concave) lens spreads the rays for the short-sighted eye so the image falls back onto the retina; a converging (convex) lens gathers the rays for the long-sighted eye so the image falls forward onto it.**

**2.** Explain: protecting sight and hearing.
**Answer: Eyes are guarded by good light for reading and work, rest from straining, and care with bright light and injury; ears are guarded against very loud sound, against objects pushed into the ear, and by treating infections early.**
