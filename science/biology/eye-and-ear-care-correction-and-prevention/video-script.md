Today: Eye and ear care — correction and prevention.

Remember this one thing: The faults of sight and hearing can be corrected and guarded against: short-sight and long-sight are corrected with lenses fitted to the fault, and eyes and ears keep working when they are protected before damage is done.

In short-sight, distant things are blurred: the image forms in front of the retina. In long-sight the image forms behind it, and near things blur.

Each fault is corrected by the lens shaped to undo it — a diverging lens for short-sight, a converging lens for long-sight — so the image lands exactly on the retina.

And prevention guards both senses: good light and rest for the eyes, and protection from very loud sound and injury for the ears.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
