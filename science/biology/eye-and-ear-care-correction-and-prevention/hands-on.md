# Hands-on — Eye and ear care — correction and prevention

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Test the lesson on yourself: hold a book at reading distance, then look up at the farthest sign you can find — note which is clear. Then check your own habits against the lesson: your reading light, your distance from the page, and the loudest sound you stood near this week.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture in two halves — an eye with its correcting lens, showing short-sight and long-sight each bending light onto the retina, and an ear protected from loud sound, each part labelled — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
