# Video script — Invertebrates — animals without backbones

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Animals without a backbone or vertebral column: the most diverse animal group, found from deserts and deep seabeds to caves and high mountains; most lack a rigid internal skeleton, some carry a chitin exoskeleton, and they do not regulate their own body temperature — earthworms, insects, spiders, snails, sponges, jellyfish, crabs, sea stars and squid are the examples.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
