# Invertebrates — animals without backbones — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about invertebrates?**
**Answer: Animals without a backbone or vertebral column: the most diverse animal group, found from deserts and deep seabeds to caves and high mountains; most lack a rigid internal skeleton, some carry a chitin exoskeleton, and they do not regulate their own body temperature — earthworms, insects, spiders, snails, sponges, jellyfish, crabs, sea stars and squid are the examples.**
