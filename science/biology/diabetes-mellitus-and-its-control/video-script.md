Today: Diabetes mellitus and its control.

Remember this one thing: Diabetes mellitus is what happens when insulin fails: Kenya's Form 2 book teaches it inside homeostasis — insulin deficiency, its symptoms (glucose in the urine, thirst, weight loss), and its control by insulin injections, diet and exercise.

Blood sugar is held steady by insulin — and diabetes mellitus is the disease of that control failing. Without enough insulin, glucose piles up in the blood until it spills into the urine; the person is thirsty, loses weight, and the sugar runs out of them unused. Kenya's book puts the disease inside the homeostasis chapter, where it belongs, and gives the control in three parts that work together: insulin injections to replace what is missing, diet to govern what comes in, and exercise to govern what the body uses. A failed feedback loop, and the three hands that steady it.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
