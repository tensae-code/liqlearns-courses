# Diabetes mellitus and its control — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Diabetes mellitus follows a deficiency of the hormone _____.**
**Answer: insulin**

**2. Sugar lost in the urine is _____.**
**Answer: glucose**

**3. Two named symptoms are thirst and weight _____.**
**Answer: loss**

**4. Besides injections, control is by diet and _____.**
**Answer: exercise**
