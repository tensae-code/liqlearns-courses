# Diabetes mellitus and its control — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Diabetes mellitus is caused by deficiency of…**
a) adrenaline  b) insulin  c) thyroxine  d) ADH
**Answer: b) insulin**

**2. Which is a symptom of diabetes mellitus named in Kenya's book?**
a) glucose in the urine  b) blue urine  c) hair loss  d) night blindness
**Answer: a) glucose in the urine**

**3. Control of diabetes mellitus includes…**
a) insulin injections, diet and exercise  b) antibiotics  c) blood transfusion  d) complete rest only
**Answer: a) insulin injections, diet and exercise**

**4. Diabetes mellitus is taught inside which topic in Kenya's book?**
a) classification  b) homeostasis  c) gaseous exchange  d) transport in plants
**Answer: b) homeostasis — the failed control of blood sugar**
