# Diabetes mellitus and its control — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a person with untreated diabetes mellitus can eat well and still lose weight.
**Model answer: The food is eaten, but its glucose cannot be properly taken up and used without insulin, so it builds up in the blood and is lost in the urine instead. The body is starved in the middle of plenty — the sugar leaves unused, and the person's weight falls.**
