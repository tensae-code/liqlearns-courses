# Diabetes mellitus and its control — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the cause and symptoms of diabetes mellitus as Kenya's Form 2 book teaches them.
**Answer: Cause: insulin deficiency. Symptoms: glucose in the urine, thirst and weight loss (Ch. 4, printed p. 121).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching diabetes mellitus.
**Answer: Kenya places the disease inside excretion and homeostasis, as a failure of blood-sugar control: the insulin deficiency is stated, the symptoms follow from it — glucose spilling into urine, thirst, weight loss — and control is three-handed: insulin injections, diet and exercise.**
