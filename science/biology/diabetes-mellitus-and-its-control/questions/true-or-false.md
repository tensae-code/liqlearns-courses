# Diabetes mellitus and its control — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. In diabetes mellitus, glucose can appear in the urine.**
**Answer: True** — a named symptom.

**2. Weight gain is the symptom Kenya's book names.**
**Answer: False** — it names weight loss.

**3. Insulin injections replace the missing hormone.**
**Answer: True** — one of the three controls.

**4. Diet and exercise play no part in controlling diabetes mellitus.**
**Answer: False** — both are named controls.
