# Structural adaptations of animals to their environment — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: an animal's body structures are fitted to its habitat — the camel carries a water reserve for dry land, and the aquatic turtle's locomotory structures are built for swimming. Moldova's Grade 6 book (5.3 Structural adaptations of animals) teaches: body structures for different habitats, camel water reserve, locomotory-structure adaptations (aquatic turtle). (printed p. 76).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 6 book teaches animal adaptation through body structures for different habitats — the camel's water reserve and the locomotory-structure adaptations of the aquatic turtle.**
