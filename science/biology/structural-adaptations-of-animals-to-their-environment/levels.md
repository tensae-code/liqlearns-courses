# Structural adaptations of animals to their environment — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (an animal's body structures are fitted to its habitat — the camel carries a water reserve for dry land, and the aquatic turtle's locomotory structures are built for swimming) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
