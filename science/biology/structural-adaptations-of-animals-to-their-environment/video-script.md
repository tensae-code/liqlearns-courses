Today: Structural adaptations of animals to their environment.

Remember this one thing: an animal's body structures are fitted to its habitat — the camel carries a water reserve for dry land, and the aquatic turtle's locomotory structures are built for swimming.

Moldova's Grade 6 book (5.3 Structural adaptations of animals) teaches: body structures for different habitats, camel water reserve, locomotory-structure adaptations (aquatic turtle). (printed p. 76).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
