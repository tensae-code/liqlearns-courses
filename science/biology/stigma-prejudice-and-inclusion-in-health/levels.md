# Stigma, prejudice and inclusion in health — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Stigma is a mark that pushes a person out — individual stigma, as around alcoholism, mental illness or homosexuality, and tribal stigma, by race or religion; prejudice is judgement made without critical analysis; and against intolerance and exclusion the lesson sets inclusion, taught as human-rights content inside Mozambique's biology course.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by giving the two stigma forms with the module's own examples without looking — individual: alcoholism, mental illness, homosexuality; tribal: race, religion — and the definition of prejudice word for word: judgement without critical analysis.
