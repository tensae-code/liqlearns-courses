# Stigma, prejudice and inclusion in health — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why this lesson belongs in a biology module about disease.
**Model answer: The module teaches HIV and other infections, whose sufferers are often met not with care but with stigma — marks that exclude them from family, work and treatment. Prejudice, judgement without analysis, is how such marks form. Teaching inclusion inside the disease unit protects the people the biology is about.**
