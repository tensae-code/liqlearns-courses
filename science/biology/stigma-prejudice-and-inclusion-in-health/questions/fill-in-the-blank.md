# Stigma, prejudice and inclusion in health — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Judgement without critical analysis is called _____.**
**Answer: prejudice**

**2. Stigma by race or religion is called _____ stigma.**
**Answer: tribal**

**3. The opposite stance to exclusion, taught in the lesson, is _____.**
**Answer: inclusion**

**4. Stigma around alcoholism is an example of _____ stigma.**
**Answer: individual**
