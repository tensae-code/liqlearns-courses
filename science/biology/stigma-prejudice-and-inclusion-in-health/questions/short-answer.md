# Stigma, prejudice and inclusion in health — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Distinguish stigma from prejudice using the module's definitions.
**Answer: Stigma is a mark of disgrace fastened on a person (individual stigma — alcoholism, mental illness, homosexuality) or on a group (tribal stigma — race, religion) that pushes them out. Prejudice is the mental act behind such marks: judgement made without critical analysis.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD I Módulo 4 de Biologia (2023), Lesson 15 — its number skipped in the Índice, the lesson present in the body — teaches stigma (individual: alcoholism, mental illness, homosexuality; tribal: by race/religion), prejudice as judgement without critical analysis, intolerance and exclusion, and inclusion as human-rights content inside the biology course.**
