# Stigma, prejudice and inclusion in health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Prejudice, as the module defines it, is…**
a) careful judgement  b) judgement without critical analysis  c) a kind of stigma by race  d) a medical symptom
**Answer: b) judgement without critical analysis**

**2. Which of these is the module's example of individual stigma?**
a) stigma by religion  b) stigma around mental illness  c) stigma by race  d) exclusion from a market
**Answer: b) stigma around mental illness**

**3. Tribal stigma is aimed by…**
a) age and height  b) race or religion  c) occupation  d) disease symptoms
**Answer: b) race or religion**

**4. The lesson's answer to exclusion is…**
a) intolerance  b) inclusion  c) silence  d) separation
**Answer: b) inclusion**
