# Hands-on — Factors that affect human health

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: human health is shaped by three families of factors together — the environment a person lives in, their lifestyle and habits, and the heredity they are born with — the frame Greece's Grade 11 book opens its health unit with.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a person in the centre with three rings of influence around them labelled environment, lifestyle and heredity, each ring holding small examples. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
