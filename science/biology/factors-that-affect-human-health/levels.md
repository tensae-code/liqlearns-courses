# Factors that affect human health — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (human health is shaped by three families of factors together — the environment a person lives in, their lifestyle and habits, and the heredity they are born with — the frame Greece's Grade 11 book opens its health unit with) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
