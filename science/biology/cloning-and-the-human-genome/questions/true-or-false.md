# Cloning and the human genome — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Dolly was produced by normal fertilisation.**
**Answer: False** — she was cloned from a body-cell nucleus in an emptied egg.

**2. Tissue culture can multiply a chosen banana plant as exact copies.**
**Answer: True** — Kenya's cash-crop example.

**3. Sequencing means reading the genome's letters in order.**
**Answer: True** — with mapping, the human genome work.

**4. Kenya's book avoids the ethics of genetic engineering.**
**Answer: False** — eugenics and the ethical issues are named in the topic.
