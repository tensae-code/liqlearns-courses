# Cloning and the human genome — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The cloned sheep's name was _____.**
**Answer: Dolly**

**2. Pyrethrum and banana are multiplied by plant _____ culture.**
**Answer: tissue**

**3. Reading the genome's letters in order is called _____.**
**Answer: sequencing**

**4. The attempt to improve the human stock by breeding is called _____.**
**Answer: eugenics**
