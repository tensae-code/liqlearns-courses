# Cloning and the human genome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Dolly the sheep was a copy of…**
a) the egg donor  b) the nucleus donor  c) the surrogate mother  d) no animal — she was bred normally
**Answer: b) the nucleus donor**

**2. Kenya's tissue-culture examples are…**
a) maize and beans  b) pyrethrum and banana  c) tea and coffee  d) wheat and rice
**Answer: b) pyrethrum and banana**

**3. Gene mapping means…**
a) copying a gene  b) locating the genes in the genome  c) deleting genes  d) growing genes in jars
**Answer: b) locating the genes in the genome**

**4. The book treats eugenics as…**
a) a recommended policy  b) an ethical issue of genetic engineering to be faced  c) a cloning technique  d) a crop variety
**Answer: b) an ethical issue of genetic engineering to be faced**
