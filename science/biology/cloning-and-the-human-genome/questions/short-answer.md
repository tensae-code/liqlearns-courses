# Cloning and the human genome — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Describe how Dolly the sheep was made, step by step, in outline.
**Answer: A nucleus was taken from a body cell of the donor sheep and placed into an egg cell whose own nucleus had been removed; the egg developed into a lamb genetically copying the nucleus donor (Ch. 1, printed p. 48).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching cloning and the genome.
**Answer: Kenya ends its genetics chapter's applied block with the two together: cloning worked concretely — Dolly step by step, and tissue culture producing pyrethrum and banana varieties, both Kenyan cash crops — then the human genome as mapping and sequencing, with eugenics and the ethics of genetic engineering stated as part of the lesson.**
