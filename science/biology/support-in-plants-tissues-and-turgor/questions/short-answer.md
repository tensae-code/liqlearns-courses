# Support in plants — tissues and turgor — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Name the supporting tissues of a stem and what each contributes.
**Answer: Parenchyma — turgor pressure in soft stems; collenchyma — flexible strength in young stems; sclerenchyma fibres — tough rope-like strength; xylem vessels and tracheids — rigid lignified pipes (Ch. 4, printed p. 120).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching support in plants.
**Answer: Kenya opens the support chapter in the plant, tissue by tissue on a stem-section practical (grass/Tradescantia, Mexican marigold/Lantana): turgor first, then collenchyma, sclerenchyma and xylem, the herbaceous–woody contrast, twining and tendrils — and a wilting practical on named local weeds to watch turgor fail.**
