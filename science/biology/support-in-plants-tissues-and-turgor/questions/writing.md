# Support in plants — tissues and turgor — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why a herbaceous plant wilts on a hot afternoon but a woody tree beside it does not.
**Model answer: The herbaceous stem is held up mainly by water pressure in its parenchyma, and the hot afternoon spends that water faster than the roots replace it — turgor falls and the stem falls with it. The tree's support is wood, secondary xylem laid down in past seasons; it stands whether or not today's cells are turgid.**
