# Support in plants — tissues and turgor — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Water pressure inside parenchyma cells is called _____.**
**Answer: turgor**

**2. The flexible strengthening tissue with thickened corners is _____.**
**Answer: collenchyma**

**3. Rope-like strengthening fibres belong to _____.**
**Answer: sclerenchyma**

**4. A climber grips its support with twining stems and _____.**
**Answer: tendrils**
