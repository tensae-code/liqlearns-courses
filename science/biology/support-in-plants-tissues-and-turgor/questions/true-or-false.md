# Support in plants — tissues and turgor — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Turgor alone can hold a herbaceous stem upright.**
**Answer: True** — pressurised parenchyma is its main support.

**2. Sclerenchyma fibres are living, water-filled cells.**
**Answer: False** — they are tough, rope-like strengthening fibres.

**3. Xylem vessels serve both conduction and support.**
**Answer: True** — pipes that hold the plant while they feed it.

**4. Woody stems depend on turgor as their main support.**
**Answer: False** — they depend on wood; turgor serves herbaceous stems.
