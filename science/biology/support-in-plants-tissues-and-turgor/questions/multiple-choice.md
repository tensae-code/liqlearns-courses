# Support in plants — tissues and turgor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A soft stem wilts when its parenchyma cells…**
a) divide  b) lose water and turgor  c) make chlorophyll  d) become xylem
**Answer: b) lose water and turgor**

**2. Collenchyma strengthens stems by…**
a) hollow tubes  b) thickened cell corners in a flexible tissue  c) storing food  d) catching light
**Answer: b) thickened cell corners in a flexible tissue**

**3. The xylem's conducting elements named for support are…**
a) vessels and tracheids  b) sieve tubes only  c) guard cells  d) root hairs
**Answer: a) vessels and tracheids**

**4. A climbing plant gains support by…**
a) deeper roots only  b) twining stems and tendrils  c) shedding leaves  d) thicker bark
**Answer: b) twining stems and tendrils**
