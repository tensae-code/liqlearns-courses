Today: Cell signalling — the membrane as receiver.

Remember this one thing: the plasma membrane is also a receiver: it carries receptor proteins that catch message molecules from outside the cell and pass the signal inward, so the cell answers hormones and other signals without the messenger entering.

the plasma membrane as a receiver of messages
