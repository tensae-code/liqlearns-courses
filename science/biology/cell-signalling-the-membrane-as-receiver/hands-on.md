# Hands-on — Cell signalling — the membrane as receiver

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the plasma membrane is also a receiver: it carries receptor proteins that catch message molecules from outside the cell and pass the signal inward, so the cell answers hormones and other signals without the messenger entering.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a cell surface with a receptor catching a round message molecule, and a chain of small arrows lighting up inside the cell in answer. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
