# Cell signalling — the membrane as receiver — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the plasma membrane is also a receiver: it carries receptor proteins that catch message molecules from outside the cell and pass the signal inward, so the cell answers hormones and other signals without the messenger entering. the plasma membrane as a receiver of messages**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: the plasma membrane as a receiver of messages**
