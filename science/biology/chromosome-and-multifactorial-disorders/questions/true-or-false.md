# Chromosome and multifactorial disorders — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Chromosome disorders come from wrong chromosome numbers (aneuploidy — Down syndrome, trisomy 21, as the main example) or broken structure — deletion, inversion, translocation; multifactorial disorders (diabetes, cancer) arise from many genes interacting with environment and lifestyle — smoking, alcohol, diet, sleep and polluted air.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Multifactorial disorders (diabetes, cancer) arise from many genes interacting with environment and lifestyle — smoking, alcohol, diet, sleep and polluted air.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Chromosome disorders come from wrong chromosome numbers (aneuploidy — Down syndrome, trisomy 21, as the main example) or broken structure — deletion, inversion, translocation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
