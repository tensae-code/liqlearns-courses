# Chromosome and multifactorial disorders — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about chromosome disorders and multifactorial disorders?**
**Answer: Chromosome disorders come from wrong chromosome numbers (aneuploidy — Down syndrome, trisomy 21, as the main example) or broken structure — deletion, inversion, translocation; multifactorial disorders (diabetes, cancer) arise from many genes interacting with environment and lifestyle — smoking, alcohol, diet, sleep and polluted air.**
