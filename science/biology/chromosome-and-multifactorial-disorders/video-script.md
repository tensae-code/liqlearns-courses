# Video script — Chromosome and multifactorial disorders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Chromosome disorders come from wrong chromosome numbers (aneuploidy — Down syndrome, trisomy 21, as the main example) or broken structure — deletion, inversion, translocation; multifactorial disorders (diabetes, cancer) arise from many genes interacting with environment and lifestyle — smoking, alcohol, diet, sleep and polluted air.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
