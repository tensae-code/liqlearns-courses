# Meiosis — four different sex cells — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Two successive divisions of sex cells producing four non-identical haploid cells: meiosis I separates homologous chromosome pairs (reduction division — prophase I with crossing-over, metaphase I, anaphase I with chromatids staying together, telophase I), meiosis II separates sister chromatids (prophase II, metaphase II, anaphase II, telophase II plus cytokinesis); the shuffling is a source of genetic variation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
