# Meiosis — four different sex cells — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1. ** Write two or three sentences explaining meiosis to a younger student. Use one example from the lesson.
**Model answer: Two successive divisions of sex cells producing four non-identical haploid cells: meiosis I separates homologous chromosome pairs (reduction division — prophase I with crossing-over, metaphase I, anaphase I with chromatids staying together, telophase I), meiosis II separates sister chromatids (prophase II, metaphase II, anaphase II, telophase II plus cytokinesis)**
