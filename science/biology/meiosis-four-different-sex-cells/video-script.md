# Video script — Meiosis — four different sex cells

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two successive divisions of sex cells producing four non-identical haploid cells: meiosis I separates homologous chromosome pairs (reduction division — prophase I with crossing-over, metaphase I, anaphase I with chromatids staying together, telophase I), meiosis II separates sister chromatids (prophase II, metaphase II, anaphase II, telophase II plus cytokinesis); the shuffling is a source of genetic variation.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
