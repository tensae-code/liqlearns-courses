# Lesson plan — Meiosis — four different sex cells

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Meiosis — four different sex cells
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — Grades 11–12 band (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Two successive divisions of sex cells producing four non-identical haploid cells: meiosis I separates homologous chromosome pairs (reduction division — prophase I with crossing-over, metaphase I, anaphase I with chromati
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology of cells, living things and the human body, and builds the Grade 11–12 understanding of meiosis.
