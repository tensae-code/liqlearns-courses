Today: Deficiency diseases — what happens when a nutrient is missing.

Remember this one thing: each missing nutrient causes its own deficiency disease, cured or prevented by diet.

Marasmus is severe energy shortage — extreme thinness with skin hanging in folds — treated with an energy-rich balanced diet.

Kwashiorkor is protein shortage in children — flaky skin, swollen legs and abdomen, hair colour change, weakness and irritability.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
