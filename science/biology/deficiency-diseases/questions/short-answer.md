# Deficiency diseases — what happens when a nutrient is missing — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: vitamin D.
**Answer: Vitamin D is needed to absorb calcium and phosphorus for bone and teeth, and is the one vitamin the body can make in skin in sunlight.**

**2.** Explain: scurvy.
**Answer: Scurvy is caused by lack of vitamin C.**
