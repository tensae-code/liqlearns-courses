# Deficiency diseases — what happens when a nutrient is missing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about marasmus?
a) Marasmus is severe energy shortage — extreme thinness with skin hanging in folds — treated with an energy-rich balanced diet
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Marasmus is severe energy shortage — extreme thinness with skin hanging in folds — treated with an energy-rich balanced diet**

**2.** What does this lesson say about kwashiorkor?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Kwashiorkor is protein shortage in children — flaky skin, swollen legs and abdomen, hair colour change, weakness and irritability
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Kwashiorkor is protein shortage in children — flaky skin, swollen legs and abdomen, hair colour change, weakness and irritability**

**3.** What does this lesson say about vitamin D?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Vitamin D is needed to absorb calcium and phosphorus for bone and teeth, and is the one vitamin the body can make in skin in sunlight
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Vitamin D is needed to absorb calcium and phosphorus for bone and teeth, and is the one vitamin the body can make in skin in sunlight**

**4.** What does this lesson say about scurvy?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Scurvy is caused by lack of vitamin C
**Answer: d) Scurvy is caused by lack of vitamin C**
