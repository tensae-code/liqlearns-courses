Today: Bone structure and composition — ossein and minerals.

Remember this one thing: bone is a composite: a protein part (ossein) and a mineral part — and the Grade 10 practical finds out what each part gives the bone by weighing and strength-testing matched bones before and after treatment.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 113–116) opens its bone lesson with a practical: two matched chicken leg bones are weighed and tested for strength before and after treatment — opening the question of what bone is made of.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
