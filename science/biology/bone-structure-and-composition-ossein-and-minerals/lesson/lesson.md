## The key facts
**The opening question.** Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 113–116) opens its bone lesson with a practical: two matched chicken leg bones are weighed and tested for strength before and after treatment — opening the question of what bone is made of.

**Why minerals enter bone.** The Grade 11 Term 2 workbook asks the same question as its own activity (Activity 2, printed p. 19): "Why do mineral elements enter the composition of bones?" — bone composition is investigated for what the mineral part gives the bone.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait teaches bone composition by experiment, twice: in the new Grade 10 student book as a before-and-after weighing and strength practical on chicken bones, and in the Grade 11 workbook as a named investigation — the question is asked before the answer is given.
