# Hands-on — Bone structure and composition — ossein and minerals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: bone is a composite: a protein part (ossein) and a mineral part — and the Grade 10 practical finds out what each part gives the bone by weighing and strength-testing matched bones before and after treatment.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two matched chicken leg bones side by side on a balance, one labelled before treatment and one after, with a small chart for recording weight and strength. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
