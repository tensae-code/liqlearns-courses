# Bone structure and composition — ossein and minerals — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (bone is a composite: a protein part (ossein) and a mineral part — and the Grade 10 practical finds out what each part gives the bone by weighing and strength-testing matched bones before and after treatment) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
