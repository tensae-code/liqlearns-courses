# Bone structure and composition — ossein and minerals — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 113–116) opens its bone lesson with a practical: two matched chicken leg bones are weighed and tested for strength before and after treatment — opening the question of what bone is made of.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف العاشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 10, Term 1), printed pp. 113–116).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with bone structure and composition — ossein and minerals.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is bone structure and composition — ossein and minerals.
