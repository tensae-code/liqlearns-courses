# Bone structure and composition — ossein and minerals — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: bone is a composite: a protein part (ossein) and a mineral part — and the Grade 10 practical finds out what each part gives the bone by weighing and strength-testing matched bones before and after treatment. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 2-3, printed pp. 113–116) opens its bone lesson with a practical: two matched chicken leg bones are weighed and tested for strength before and after treatment — opening the question of what bone is made of.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait teaches bone composition by experiment, twice: in the new Grade 10 student book as a before-and-after weighing and strength practical on chicken bones, and in the Grade 11 workbook as a named investigation — the question is asked before the answer is given.**
