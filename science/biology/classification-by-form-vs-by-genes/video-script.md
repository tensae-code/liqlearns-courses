Today: Classification by form vs by genes.

Remember this one thing: there are two ways to classify a living thing — by its form, and by its genes.

The first way is morphological classification — التصنيف الشكلي. It groups living things by their form: the shape and structure you can see and measure.

The second way is genetic classification — التصنيف الجيني. It groups living things by their genes: the genetic material they carry inside their cells.

Palestine's Grade 10 Life Sciences book teaches modern classification in one chapter — classification, naming, the levels — and then, in §4, it sets these two ways side by side and compares them.

Same living things. Two different things to sort by: what they look like, and what they carry in their genes.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
