# Classification by form vs by genes — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: morphological classification against genetic classification.
**Answer: Morphological classification (التصنيف الشكلي) groups living things by their form — outward shape and structure that can be seen and measured. Genetic classification (التصنيف الجيني) groups them by the genetic material they carry. The two ways sort by different evidence: form is read from the outside, genes from the inside.**

**2.** Explain: Palestine's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Palestine's Grade 10 Life Sciences book teaches modern classification in one chapter — classification itself (§1), naming of living organisms (§2) and the levels of classification (§3) — and closes the chapter with §4, which sets morphological classification and genetic classification side by side and compares them (printed p. 101).**
