# Classification by form vs by genes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does morphological classification (التصنيف الشكلي) group living things by?
a) Their outward form — shape and structure that can be seen and measured
b) The place where they were found
c) The person who first described them
d) The genetic material they carry
**Answer: a) Their outward form — shape and structure that can be seen and measured**

**2.** What does genetic classification (التصنيف الجيني) group living things by?
a) Their outward form — shape and structure
b) The genetic material they carry
c) How useful they are to people
d) How large they grow
**Answer: b) The genetic material they carry**

**3.** Where does Palestine's Grade 10 book teach this comparison?
a) In the microscopy chapter, before cells
b) In the cell-division unit, after tumours
c) In §4 of its modern-classification chapter, after classification, naming and the levels
d) In the fungi chapter, after lichens
**Answer: c) In §4 of its modern-classification chapter, after classification, naming and the levels**

**4.** What is the difference between the two ways?
a) One is for plants and the other only for animals
b) One is older and the other is not taught in schools
c) They are two names for exactly the same sorting
d) Form is read from the outside of the organism; genes are read from the inside
**Answer: d) Form is read from the outside of the organism; genes are read from the inside**
