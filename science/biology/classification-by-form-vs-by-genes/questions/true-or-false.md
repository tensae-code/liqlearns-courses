# Classification by form vs by genes — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Morphological classification groups living things by their outward shape and structure.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Genetic classification groups living things by what they look like.
**Answer: False** — genetic classification groups by the genetic material the organism carries; grouping by looks is morphological classification.

**3.** Palestine's book compares the two ways in the same section, §4 of its classification chapter.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Form and genes are the same evidence, read in the same way.
**Answer: False** — form is read from the outside of the organism, genes from the inside; the two ways sort by different evidence.
