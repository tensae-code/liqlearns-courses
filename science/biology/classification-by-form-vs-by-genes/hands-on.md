# Hands-on — Classification by form vs by genes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Take a handful of mixed buttons, seeds or leaves. Sort them once by their form — shape, size and structure, the morphological way (التصنيف الشكلي) — and lay the groups out. Then say, for the same objects, what the genetic way (التصنيف الجيني) would sort by instead: not what they look like, but the genetic material inside them. Say aloud which sorting you could do with your eyes alone, and which one rests on what is carried inside.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — the two sorting tables side by side, form on one side and genes on the other — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
