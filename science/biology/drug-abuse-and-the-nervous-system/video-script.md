# Video script — Drug abuse and the nervous system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Addiction is defined as a brain-and-behaviour disease (the urge persists despite harm; earlier treatment avoids worse consequences; addiction includes alcohol and nicotine, not only illegal drugs); studies are cited that khat, alcohol and cigarettes are the substances most abused by young people in Ethiopia, including high-school and university students; heavy drinking can overwhelm the liver into alcohol poisoning, unconsciousness, coma and death, and long-term drinking into cirrhosis; khat use is linked to unprotected sex, sex-worker use and multiple partners, raising HIV risk, plus accidents, crime and joblessness.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
