# Pollination and the flowering-plant life cycle

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Pollination and the flowering-plant life cycle. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
A flowering plant's life goes round in a cycle. Pollination — pollen reaching the stigma — is the step where wind and insects do the carrying, and flowers are shaped to fit their carrier.

## The key facts
**The cycle.** The life cycle runs flower → pollination → fertilisation → seed → germination.

**Pollination.** Pollination is the transfer of pollen to the stigma.

**Two carriers.** Pollen is carried by wind and by insects.

**Fitted flowers.** Flower features fit the carrier that pollinates them.
