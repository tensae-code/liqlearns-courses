# Video script — Pollination and the flowering-plant life cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Pollination and the flowering-plant life cycle.

Remember this one thing: the flowering plant's life cycle runs flower, pollination, fertilisation, seed, germination — and pollination is pollen transfer by wind or insects

The cycle: The life cycle runs flower → pollination → fertilisation → seed → germination.

Pollination: Pollination is the transfer of pollen to the stigma.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
