# Pollination and the flowering-plant life cycle — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The life cycle runs flower → pollination → fertilisation → seed → germination.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pollination has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Pollen is carried by wind and by insects.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Fitted flowers has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
