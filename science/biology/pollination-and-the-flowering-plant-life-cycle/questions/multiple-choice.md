# Pollination and the flowering-plant life cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the cycle?
a) The life cycle runs flower → pollination → fertilisation → seed → germination  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The life cycle runs flower → pollination → fertilisation → seed → germination**

**2.** What does this lesson say about pollination?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Pollination is the transfer of pollen to the stigma  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Pollination is the transfer of pollen to the stigma**

**3.** What does this lesson say about two carriers?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Pollen is carried by wind and by insects  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Pollen is carried by wind and by insects**

**4.** What does this lesson say about fitted flowers?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Flower features fit the carrier that pollinates them
**Answer: d) Flower features fit the carrier that pollinates them**
