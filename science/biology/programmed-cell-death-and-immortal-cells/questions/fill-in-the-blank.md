# Programmed cell death and immortal cells — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Programmed cell death is also called _____.**
**Answer: apoptosis**

**2. Normal body cells, which live their span and die, are _____ cells.**
**Answer: mortal**

**3. Cells that escape the death programme and keep dividing are _____ cells.**
**Answer: immortal**

**4. In apoptosis the cell _____ itself in an orderly way.**
**Answer: dismantles**
