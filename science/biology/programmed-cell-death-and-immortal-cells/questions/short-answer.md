# Programmed cell death and immortal cells — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: programmed cell death and immortal cells, in one or two sentences.
**Answer: Programmed cell death (apoptosis) is death written into the cell's programme: at the appointed point the cell dismantles itself in an orderly way, removing cells the body no longer needs. Immortal cells are the opposite case — cells that escape that death programme and keep dividing without the normal end.**

**2.** Explain: Italy's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Italy's Grade 9 Campbell (*Biologia: concetti e collegamenti PLUS, Primo biennio*, Linx/Pearson Italia) closes its mitosis-and-meiosis unit (Unità 4) with a “Training per il futuro” (Training for the future) section on mortal and immortal cells and programmed cell death (printed p. 120).**
