# Programmed cell death and immortal cells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is apoptosis?**
a) A cell bursting at random  b) Programmed cell death — the cell dismantling itself on schedule, as part of its programme  c) A kind of cell division  d) A cell becoming immortal
**Answer: b) Programmed cell death — the cell dismantling itself on schedule, as part of its programme**

**2. What makes a cell “immortal” in this lesson's sense?**
a) It can never be seen to divide  b) It escapes the normal death programme and keeps dividing  c) It lives outside the body  d) It is the largest cell
**Answer: b) It escapes the normal death programme and keeps dividing**

**3. Why is programmed cell death useful to the body?**
a) It makes cells divide faster  b) It removes worn-out, surplus or unneeded cells in an orderly way, keeping tissues in proper shape and number  c) It stops all division  d) It turns cells into bacteria
**Answer: b) It removes worn-out, surplus or unneeded cells in an orderly way, keeping tissues in proper shape and number**

**4. Where does Italy's Grade 9 Campbell teach mortal and immortal cells?**
a) In the ecology unit  b) In the “Training for the future” close of the cell-division unit (Unità 4)  c) In the human-body volume  d) In the PLUS plant unit
**Answer: b) In the “Training for the future” close of the cell-division unit (Unità 4)**
