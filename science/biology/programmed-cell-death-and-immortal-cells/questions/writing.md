# Programmed cell death and immortal cells — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining programmed cell death to a younger student. Use the words apoptosis and immortal once each.
**Model answer: Cells do not live for ever — they are mortal, and much of their dying is planned. In apoptosis, programmed cell death, the cell takes itself apart in an orderly way when its time comes, which keeps the body's tissues the right shape and size. A cell that escapes this programme and keeps dividing without end is called an immortal cell.**
