# Lipids — glycerol and fatty acids — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: membranes.
**Answer: Lipids serve as structural components of cells, in membranes.**

**2.** Explain: stores.
**Answer: Lipids serve as energy stores for the cell and the human body as a whole.**

**3.** Explain: phospholipids (student book).
**Answer: A phospholipid has a water-loving head and water-fearing tails.**
