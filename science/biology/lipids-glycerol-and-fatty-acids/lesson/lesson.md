## The key facts
**Lipids.** Lipids are fats and oils.

**Building.** Lipids are built from glycerol and fatty acids.

**Membranes.** Lipids serve as structural components of cells, in membranes.

**Stores.** Lipids serve as energy stores for the cell and the human body as a whole.

### Student-book key facts
**Fats and oils.** Lipids include fats and oils, the body's stores.

**Phospholipids.** A phospholipid has a water-loving head and water-fearing tails.

**The bilayer.** Phospholipids self-arrange into the bilayer that makes cell membranes.

**Built from.** Lipids are built from glycerol and fatty acids.
