Today: The values of biodiversity — direct, indirect, potential.

Remember this one thing: biodiversity — held at three levels, genetic, species and ecosystem — has value in three kinds: direct value in food, fibres and medicines such as artemisinin, indirect value in carbon fixed, soil and floodwater held and climate steadied, stated to far exceed the direct, and potential value in the uses nobody knows yet.

biodiversity defined at three levels (genetic, species, ecosystem), then its value in three kinds: direct (food, fibres, and medicines — Tu Youyou's artemisinin from sweet wormwood pictured), indirect (photosynthesis fixing carbon and releasing oxygen, forests and wetlands holding soil, storing floodwater and steadying climate — stated to far exceed the direct value), and potential (the uses nobody knows yet).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
