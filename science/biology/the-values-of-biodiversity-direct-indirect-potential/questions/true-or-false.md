# The values of biodiversity — direct, indirect, potential — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. biodiversity defined at three levels (genetic, species, ecosystem), then its value in three kinds: direct (food, fibres, and medicines — Tu Youyou's artemisinin from sweet wormwood pictured), indirect (photosynthesis fixing carbon and releasing oxygen, forests and wetlands holding soil, storing floodwater and steadying climate — stated to far exceed the direct value), and potential (the uses nobody knows yet).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修2 生物与环境 (Senior High Biology, Elective Compulsory Book 2: Biology and Environment, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the values of biodiversity — direct, indirect, potential.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the values of biodiversity — direct, indirect, potential.
