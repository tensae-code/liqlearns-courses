# The values of biodiversity — direct, indirect, potential — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: biodiversity — held at three levels, genetic, species and ecosystem — has value in three kinds: direct value in food, fibres and medicines such as artemisinin, indirect value in carbon fixed, soil and floodwater held and climate steadied, stated to far exceed the direct, and potential value in the uses nobody knows yet. biodiversity defined at three levels (genetic, species, ecosystem), then its value in three kinds: direct (food, fibres, and medicines — Tu Youyou's artemisinin from sweet wormwood pictured), indirect (photosynthesis fixing carbon and releasing oxygen, forests and wetlands holding soil, storing floodwater and steadying climate — stated to far exceed the direct value), and potential (the uses nobody knows yet).**
