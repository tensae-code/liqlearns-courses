Today: Artificial propagation — cuttings, grafting and tissue culture.

Remember this one thing: farmers propagate plants artificially — from cuttings and by grafting — and the laboratory adds protoplast culture; flowering plants themselves add apomixis, seed without fertilisation.

Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Ch. 2, printed pp. 77–81) teaches artificial propagation as farmers practise it: propagating plants from cuttings and from grafts.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
