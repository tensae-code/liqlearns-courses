# Hands-on — Artificial propagation — cuttings, grafting and tissue culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: farmers propagate plants artificially — from cuttings and by grafting — and the laboratory adds protoplast culture; flowering plants themselves add apomixis, seed without fertilisation.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: three panels: a cutting with roots forming in soil, a graft union bound on a stem, and a laboratory dish of protoplast culture — each labelled with its method. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
