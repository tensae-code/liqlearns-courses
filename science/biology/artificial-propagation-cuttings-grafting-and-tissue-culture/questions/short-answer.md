# Artificial propagation — cuttings, grafting and tissue culture — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: farmers propagate plants artificially — from cuttings and by grafting — and the laboratory adds protoplast culture; flowering plants themselves add apomixis, seed without fertilisation. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Ch. 2, printed pp. 77–81) teaches artificial propagation as farmers practise it: propagating plants from cuttings and from grafts.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait teaches propagation as a farmer's and a laboratory's toolkit side by side (Grade 11, Ch. 2): cuttings and grafting first as practised methods, protoplast culture as the modern extension, apomixis as the plant's own version.**
