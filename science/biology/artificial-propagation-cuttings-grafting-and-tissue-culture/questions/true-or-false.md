# Artificial propagation — cuttings, grafting and tissue culture — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Ch. 2, printed pp. 77–81) teaches artificial propagation as farmers practise it: propagating plants from cuttings and from grafts.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف الحادي عشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 11, Term 1), printed pp. 77–81).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with artificial propagation — cuttings, grafting and tissue culture.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is artificial propagation — cuttings, grafting and tissue culture.
