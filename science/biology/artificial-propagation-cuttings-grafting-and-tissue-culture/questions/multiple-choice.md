# Artificial propagation — cuttings, grafting and tissue culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) farmers propagate plants artificially — from cuttings and by grafting — and the laboratory adds protoplast culture; flowering plants themselves add apomixis, seed without fertilisation  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) farmers propagate plants artificially — from cuttings and by grafting — and the laboratory adds protoplast culture; flowering plants themselves add apomixis, seed without fertilisation**

**2. Cuttings and grafts — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Ch. 2, printed pp. 77–81) teaches artificial propagation as farmers practise it: propagating plants from cuttings and from grafts.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 student book (Ch. 2, printed pp. 77–81) teaches artificial propagation as farmers practise it: propagating plants from cuttings and from grafts.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) The laboratory method in the same run is protoplast culture — growing plants from cells stripped of their walls.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Kuwait
**Answer: b) The laboratory method in the same run is protoplast culture — growing plants from cells stripped of their walls.**
