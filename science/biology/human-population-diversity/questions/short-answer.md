# Human population diversity — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: human populations vary — in skin colour, blood groups, body build and a thousand inherited traits — but the variation runs within one single species and mostly inside every population, so biology finds no separate human races, only one variable kind. diversity in human populations: variation within one species, no separate races in the biological sense**
