The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Human population diversity.

Remember this one thing: human populations vary — in skin colour, blood groups, body build and a thousand inherited traits — but the variation runs within one single species and mostly inside every population, so biology finds no separate human races, only one variable kind.

diversity in human populations: variation within one species, no separate races in the biological sense

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
