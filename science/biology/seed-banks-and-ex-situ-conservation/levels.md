# Seed banks and ex-situ conservation — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (seed banks conserve species outside the place they live — ex-situ conservation: seeds are dried and frozen so they keep for decades, as the Svalbard Global Seed Bank stores the world's crop seeds in Arctic cold, as insurance against their loss in the wild) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
