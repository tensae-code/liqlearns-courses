# Energy flow — and why it runs out

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Energy flow — and why it runs out. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Sunlight enters, energy climbs the chain — and leaks at every step. That leak is why no chain climbs forever.

[Picture 22269 — a shrinking-bar diagram of energy at four trophic levels]

## The key facts
**One way.** Energy flows one way through an ecosystem.

**Lost each time.** Energy is lost at every transfer between levels.

**A fraction only.** Only a fraction of each level's energy reaches the next level.

**Short chains.** Because of the losses, food chains stay short.
