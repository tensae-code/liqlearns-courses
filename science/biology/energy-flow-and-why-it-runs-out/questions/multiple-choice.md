# Energy flow — and why it runs out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about one way?
a) Energy flows one way through an ecosystem  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Energy flows one way through an ecosystem**

**2.** What does this lesson say about lost each time?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) Energy is lost at every transfer between levels  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Energy is lost at every transfer between levels**

**3.** What does this lesson say about a fraction only?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) Only a fraction of each level's energy reaches the next level  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Only a fraction of each level's energy reaches the next level**

**4.** What does this lesson say about short chains?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Because of the losses, food chains stay short
**Answer: d) Because of the losses, food chains stay short**
