# Lung volumes and capacities — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (Not all the air in the lungs moves with every breath: the air of breathing is measured in named volumes — the tidal volume of one quiet breath, the reserve volumes above and below it, and the residual volume that never leaves — and volumes added together make the lung capacities, of which vital capacity, the greatest breath you can force out after the deepest breath in, is the measure of a working lung.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by applying the lesson's idea to a new example the learner has not seen, without looking.
