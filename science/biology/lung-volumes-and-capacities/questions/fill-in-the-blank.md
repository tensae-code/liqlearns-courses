# Lung volumes and capacities — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The air of one quiet breath is the _____ volume.**
**Answer: tidal**

**2. The air that never leaves the lungs is the _____ volume.**
**Answer: residual**

**3. The greatest breath out after the deepest breath in measures the _____ capacity.**
**Answer: vital**

**4. A capacity is made by adding two or more _____ together.**
**Answer: volumes**
