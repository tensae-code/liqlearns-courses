# Lung volumes and capacities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the tidal volume?**
a) The air that never leaves the lungs  b) The air moved in or out in one quiet, normal breath  c) The greatest amount you can force out  d) All the air the lungs can hold
**Answer: b) The air moved in or out in one quiet, normal breath**

**2. What is the residual volume?**
a) Air breathed in above a quiet breath  b) Air that stays in the lungs even after the hardest breath out  c) Air in the nose only  d) Air lost in coughing
**Answer: b) Air that stays in the lungs even after the hardest breath out**

**3. Vital capacity is…**
a) the tidal volume alone  b) the residual volume alone  c) the greatest breath out after the deepest breath in — tidal volume plus both reserves  d) the air of one cough
**Answer: c) the greatest breath out after the deepest breath in — tidal volume plus both reserves**

**4. A lung capacity is…**
a) one volume by itself  b) two or more volumes added together  c) the speed of breathing  d) the number of breaths per minute
**Answer: b) two or more volumes added together**
