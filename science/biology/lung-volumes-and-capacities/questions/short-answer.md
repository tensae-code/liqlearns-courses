# Lung volumes and capacities — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the difference between a volume and a capacity.
**Answer: A volume is one measured part of the lung's air — tidal, reserve or residual. A capacity is two or more volumes added together, such as vital capacity or total lung capacity.**

**2.** Explain: Bhutan's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Bhutan's Class 9 book (Ch. 5 d, printed pp. 99–100) closes its breathing chapter by naming and defining the lung volumes and capacities — tidal volume, vital capacity among them — right after the breathing cycle and gaseous exchange.**
