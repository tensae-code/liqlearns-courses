# Lung volumes and capacities — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. A quiet breath uses all the air in the lungs.**
**Answer: False** — a quiet breath moves only the tidal volume — the reserves and residual volume stay unused.

**2. Some air remains in the lungs even after breathing out as hard as you can.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. Vital capacity includes the residual volume.**
**Answer: False** — vital capacity is the tidal volume plus the two reserves; total lung capacity adds the residual volume.

**4. Lung volumes can be named and measured.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
