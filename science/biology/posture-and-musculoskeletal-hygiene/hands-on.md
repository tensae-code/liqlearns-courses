# Hands-on — Posture and musculoskeletal hygiene

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Check your own sitting as you do this lesson: back supported, feet flat, work raised so your head is not pushed forward. Then stand, walk ten steps tall, and feel the arch of your foot — say what the lesson asks each of them to be doing.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of three spines side by side — a straight spine in correct posture, a spine curving sideways from habitually sitting crooked, and a spine stooped from a badly carried load — beside a normal foot arch and a flat foot, each labelled — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
