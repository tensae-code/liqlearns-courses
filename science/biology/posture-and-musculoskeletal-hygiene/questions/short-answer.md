# Posture and musculoskeletal hygiene — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: preventing spinal curvature.
**Answer: A growing spine bent crooked day after day can keep the bend: habitual one-sided sitting, a load always carried on the same side, and weak back muscles let the spine curve sideways or stoop. The prevention is the correction of the habit itself — even loads, straight sitting, and exercise that strengthens the muscles holding the spine.**

**2.** Explain: exercise hygiene for a growing body.
**Answer: Exercise is regular, increasing gradually, matched to a body still growing — movement strengthens the muscles that hold the skeleton straight, while sudden overloads and training through injury damage what they should build.**
