# Posture and musculoskeletal hygiene — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A growing skeleton keeps the shape it is held in.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Carrying a load always on the same side protects the spine.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**

**3.** Flat feet form when the arch of the foot flattens.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Sudden overloads are the hygienic way to exercise a growing body.
**Answer: False — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.**
