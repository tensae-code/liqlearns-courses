# Posture and musculoskeletal hygiene — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about correct posture?
a) Correct posture holds the spine in its natural curves — sitting straight, feet flat, loads carried evenly
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Correct posture holds the spine in its natural curves — sitting straight, feet flat, loads carried evenly**

**2.** What does this lesson say about preventing spinal curvature?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) A growing spine bent crooked day after day can keep the bend, so habits are corrected: even loads, straight sitting, strong back muscles
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) A growing spine bent crooked day after day can keep the bend, so habits are corrected: even loads, straight sitting, strong back muscles**

**3.** What does this lesson say about preventing flat feet?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Fitting shoes and exercise that works the foot protect the arch from flattening
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Fitting shoes and exercise that works the foot protect the arch from flattening**

**4.** What does this lesson say about exercise hygiene?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Exercise for a growing body is regular, increases gradually, and is never training through injury
**Answer: d) Exercise for a growing body is regular, increases gradually, and is never training through injury**
