Today: Posture and musculoskeletal hygiene.

Remember this one thing: A growing skeleton keeps the shape it is held in: correct posture, care that prevents spinal curvature and flat feet, and hygienic exercise keep the musculoskeletal system growing straight and strong.

Correct posture holds the spine in its natural curves — sitting straight, feet flat, loads carried evenly.

A growing spine bent crooked day after day can keep the bend, so the prevention of curvature is the correction of the habit: even loads, straight sitting, strong back muscles.

The foot's arch is protected by fitting shoes and exercise; and exercise itself is taken hygienically — regular, gradual, matched to a body still growing.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
