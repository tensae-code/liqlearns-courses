# Hands-on — Nigeria's vegetation belts — the local biomes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw a rough outline map of Nigeria and band it into the four belts from south to north. Label each belt, add the shea and locust bean trees in the savannah, and write 'Maiduguri rainfall' beside the Sahel. Say aloud, belt by belt, how the rainfall changes as you travel north.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a map of Nigeria banded into its vegetation belts from south to north — rain forest, Southern Guinea savannah, Northern Guinea savannah, Sahel — with a shea tree and a locust bean tree drawn in the savannah band — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
