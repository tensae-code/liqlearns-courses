# Nigeria's vegetation belts — the local biomes — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** The driest belt, in Borno State near Lake Chad, is the _____.
**Answer: Sahel**

**2.** The Southern and Northern _____ savannahs lie between the rain forest and the Sahel.
**Answer: Guinea**

**3.** The book works the Sahel with rainfall figures from _____.
**Answer: Maiduguri**
