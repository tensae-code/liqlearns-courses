# Nigeria's vegetation belts — the local biomes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In which belt do shea butter and locust bean trees belong, in the book?
a) Rain forest
b) Guinea savannah
c) Sahel
d) Mangrove swamp
**Answer: b) Guinea savannah**

**2.** Where is the Sahel belt in Nigeria?
a) Lagos coast
b) Borno State, near Lake Chad
c) Jos plateau only
d) The Niger delta
**Answer: b) Borno State, near Lake Chad**

**3.** Which town's rainfall figures does the book use for the Sahel?
a) Lagos
b) Ibadan
c) Maiduguri
d) Enugu
**Answer: c) Maiduguri**

**4.** What mainly decides the order of the belts from south to north?
a) Soil colour
b) Rainfall falling as you go north
c) Population
d) Roads
**Answer: b) Rainfall falling as you go north**
