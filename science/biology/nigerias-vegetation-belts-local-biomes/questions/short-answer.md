# Nigeria's vegetation belts — the local biomes — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: Nigeria's vegetation belts in order, south to north.
**Answer: Rain forest in the wet south; then the Southern Guinea savannah and the Northern Guinea savannah — grassland with trees such as shea butter, locust bean and Isoberlinia doka; then the Sahel in Borno State near Lake Chad, the driest belt, worked with Maiduguri rainfall figures. The order follows rainfall decreasing northwards.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches local biomes in Chapter 7 (printed p. 122) as the country's own vegetation belts — rain forest, the Southern and Northern Guinea savannahs with their named trees, and the Sahel in Borno State near Lake Chad — before it turns to the world biomes.**
