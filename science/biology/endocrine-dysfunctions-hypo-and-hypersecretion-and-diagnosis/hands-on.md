# Hands-on — Endocrine dysfunctions — hypo- and hypersecretion and diagnosis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: endocrine disorders are disorders of amount: under-secretion (hypo-) and over-secretion (hyper-) of a gland's hormone produce opposite diseases — and the applied case is the side effects of steroids.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two glands side by side, one drawn shrunken and pale labelled under-secretion, one enlarged labelled over-secretion, with arrows to the opposite effects of too little and too much hormone. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
