# Endocrine dysfunctions — hypo- and hypersecretion and diagnosis — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: endocrine disorders are disorders of amount: under-secretion (hypo-) and over-secretion (hyper-) of a gland's hormone produce opposite diseases — and the applied case is the side effects of steroids. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-2, printed p. 75), "Health of the Endocrine Glands", distinguishes disorders of under-secretion from disorders of over-secretion.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait closes the endocrine run with its health lesson (Grade 12, Lesson 3-2) in the Pearson frame — structure, physiology, then health — and ties the disorders to the learners' lives with the steroids applied box.**
