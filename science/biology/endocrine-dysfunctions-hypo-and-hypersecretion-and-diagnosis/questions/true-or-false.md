# Endocrine dysfunctions — hypo- and hypersecretion and diagnosis — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-2, printed p. 75), "Health of the Endocrine Glands", distinguishes disorders of under-secretion from disorders of over-secretion.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف الثاني عشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 12, Term 1), printed p. 75).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with endocrine dysfunctions — hypo- and hypersecretion and diagnosis.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is endocrine dysfunctions — hypo- and hypersecretion and diagnosis.
