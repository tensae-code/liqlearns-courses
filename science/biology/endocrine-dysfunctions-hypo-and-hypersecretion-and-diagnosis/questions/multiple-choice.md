# Endocrine dysfunctions — hypo- and hypersecretion and diagnosis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) endocrine disorders are disorders of amount: under-secretion (hypo-) and over-secretion (hyper-) of a gland's hormone produce opposite diseases — and the applied case is the side effects of steroids  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life

**2. Under and over — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-2, printed p. 75), "Health of the Endocrine Glands", distinguishes disorders of under-secretion from disorders of over-secretion.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-2, printed p. 75), "Health of the Endocrine Glands", distinguishes disorders of under-secretion from disorders of over-secretion.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
