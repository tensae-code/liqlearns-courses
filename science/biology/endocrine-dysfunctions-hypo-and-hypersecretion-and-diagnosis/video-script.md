Today: Endocrine dysfunctions — hypo- and hypersecretion and diagnosis.

Remember this one thing: endocrine disorders are disorders of amount: under-secretion (hypo-) and over-secretion (hyper-) of a gland's hormone produce opposite diseases — and the applied case is the side effects of steroids.

Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-2, printed p. 75), "Health of the Endocrine Glands", distinguishes disorders of under-secretion from disorders of over-secretion.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
