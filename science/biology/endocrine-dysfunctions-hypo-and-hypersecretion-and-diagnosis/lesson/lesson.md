disorders of under- and over-secretion distinguished, with an applied box on the side effects of steroids (same proposed slug as in the Moldova filings)

## The key facts
**Under and over.** Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 3-2, printed p. 75), "Health of the Endocrine Glands", distinguishes disorders of under-secretion from disorders of over-secretion.

**The steroid box.** The lesson's applied box treats the side effects of steroids — hormone misuse read as a hypersecretion problem the learner can meet in real life.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait closes the endocrine run with its health lesson (Grade 12, Lesson 3-2) in the Pearson frame — structure, physiology, then health — and ties the disorders to the learners' lives with the steroids applied box.
