# Monoclonal antibodies — the hybridoma trick — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining this lesson's topic to someone who has never met it.
**Model answer: A monoclonal antibody is one antibody made in bulk by one clone: fuse the single B cell that makes it to an endlessly dividing myeloma cell, select and clone the hybridoma, and it makes that one antibody for as long as it lives — the trick behind pregnancy test kits and antibody medicines. fusion by PEG, electric pulse or inactivated virus; Milstein and Köhler's 1975 solution (fuse the one-antibody B cell to an endlessly dividing myeloma cell; Nobel Prize 1984); selection and cloning of hybridomas; and the products — pregnancy test kits reading at day 8, imaging diagnostics, five of the world's ten best-selling drugs in 2015, a ¥7.2 billion Chinese market that year, and antibody–drug conjugates that carry a toxin only to the tumour.**
