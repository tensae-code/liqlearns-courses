# Monoclonal antibodies — the hybridoma trick — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. fusion by PEG, electric pulse or inactivated virus; Milstein and Köhler's 1975 solution (fuse the one-antibody B cell to an endlessly dividing myeloma cell; Nobel Prize 1984); selection and cloning of hybridomas; and the products — pregnancy test kits reading at day 8, imaging diagnostics, five of the world's ten best-selling drugs in 2015, a ¥7.2 billion Chinese market that year, and antibody–drug conjugates that carry a toxin only to the tumour.**

**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修3 生物技术与工程 (Senior High Biology, Elective Compulsory Book 3: Biotechnology and Engineering, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).
