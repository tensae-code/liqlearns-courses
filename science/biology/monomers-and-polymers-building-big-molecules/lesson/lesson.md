# Monomers and polymers — building big molecules from small

## Do it first

## The idea
Big molecules are built, not found whole. Small units join like beads on a chain — monomer by monomer — until the polymer is built.

## The key facts
**Carbon-based.** Organic molecules are carbon-based molecules.

**Monomers.** Monomers are the small units big molecules are built from.

**Polymers.** Polymers are large molecules built by joining monomers.

**Each family.** Each organic family has its own elements and its own monomer.
