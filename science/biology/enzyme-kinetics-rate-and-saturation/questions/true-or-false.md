# Enzyme kinetics — rate and saturation — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Kinetics studies reaction rates and binding: how fast product forms depends on enzyme and substrate concentrations and affinities, summarised in the Michaelis–Menten relationship between reaction rate and substrate concentration, up to the enzyme's maximum rate when its active sites are saturated.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A rate-measurement procedure the student book lacks: equal peroxide in three test tubes of liver/potato/carrot material, wait 60 seconds, and measure the height of the bubbling column in each tube to compare reaction rates; plus a group investigation of how competitive and noncompetitive inhibitors change reaction rate and the shape of its graph.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
