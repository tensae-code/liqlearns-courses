# Enzyme kinetics — rate and saturation — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about enzyme kinetics?**
**Answer: Kinetics studies reaction rates and binding: how fast product forms depends on enzyme and substrate concentrations and affinities, summarised in the Michaelis–Menten relationship between reaction rate and substrate concentration, up to the enzyme's maximum rate when its active sites are saturated.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added kinetics practice?**
**Answer: A rate-measurement procedure the student book lacks: equal peroxide in three test tubes of liver/potato/carrot material, wait 60 seconds, and measure the height of the bubbling column in each tube to compare reaction rates; plus a group investigation of how competitive and noncompetitive inhibitors change reaction rate and the shape of its graph.**
