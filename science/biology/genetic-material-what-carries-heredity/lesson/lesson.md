# Genetic material — what carries heredity

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — genetic material. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

Genetics studies how traits pass between generations through genetic material — DNA in almost all organisms, RNA in some viruses such as HIV and COVID-19; DNA is the hereditary material storing the instructions for growth, development, reproduction and survival, carried on genes along chromosomes.
