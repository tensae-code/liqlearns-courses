# Genetic material — what carries heredity — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about genetic material?**
**Answer: Genetics studies how traits pass between generations through genetic material — DNA in almost all organisms, RNA in some viruses such as HIV and COVID-19; DNA is the hereditary material storing the instructions for growth, development, reproduction and survival, carried on genes along chromosomes.**
