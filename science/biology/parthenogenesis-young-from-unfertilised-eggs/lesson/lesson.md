## The idea
A whole colony's sex balance rests on whether each egg was fertilised. This section is §4.3.5 in the book's body.

[Picture 21042 — a queen bee with two egg paths]

## The key facts
**Definition.** Parthenogenesis is development from unfertilised eggs.

**Workers.** A queen honeybee's fertilised eggs become female workers.

**Drones.** A queen honeybee's unfertilised eggs develop into males (drones).

**Balance.** A honeybee colony's sex balance rests on whether each egg was fertilised.
