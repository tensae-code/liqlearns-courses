# Video script — Parthenogenesis — young from unfertilised eggs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Parthenogenesis — young from unfertilised eggs.

Remember this one thing: in parthenogenesis, young develop from unfertilised eggs — in honeybees, unfertilised eggs become drones.

Parthenogenesis is development from unfertilised eggs.

A queen honeybee's fertilised eggs become female workers.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
