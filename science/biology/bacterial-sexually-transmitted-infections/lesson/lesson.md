# Bacterial sexually transmitted infections

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — bacterial sexually transmitted infections. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bacterial STIs

Sexually transmitted infections pass mainly by sexual contact and number over twenty; the bacterial set is worked one by one with agent, transmission, symptoms and prevention/treatment for each: bacterial vaginosis, chlamydia-type infections as presented, syphilis (Treponema; four stages tabled from painless primary chancre through rash, latency to tertiary damage of brain, heart and nerves), gonorrhoea (Neisseria; painful urination and discharge, sometimes silent) and chancroid.

## Sub lesson 2 — Added STI investigation briefs — chlamydia, HIV, pubic lice and scabies

Ready-made investigation briefs with the facts teachers should steer pupils to: chlamydia (agent Chlamydia trachomatis; painful urination and discharge; protective sex and care as prevention/treatment), HIV (a retrovirus; spread by infected body fluids, unprotected sex, shared injection equipment and blood contact), pubic lice (close contact and shared towels/bedding; shaving and hygiene; common in hot and temperate areas of Ethiopia) and a lab-based scabies investigation.
