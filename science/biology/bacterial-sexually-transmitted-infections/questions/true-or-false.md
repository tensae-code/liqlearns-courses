# Bacterial sexually transmitted infections — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Sexually transmitted infections pass mainly by sexual contact and number over twenty; the bacterial set is worked one by one with agent, transmission, symptoms and prevention/treatment for each: bacterial vaginosis, chlamydia-type infections as presented, syphilis (Treponema; four stages tabled from painless primary chancre through rash, latency to tertiary damage of brain, heart and nerves), gonorrhoea (Neisseria; painful urination and discharge, sometimes silent) and chancroid.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Ready-made investigation briefs with the facts teachers should steer pupils to: chlamydia (agent Chlamydia trachomatis; painful urination and discharge; protective sex and care as prevention/treatment), HIV (a retrovirus; spread by infected body fluids, unprotected sex, shared injection equipment and blood contact), pubic lice (close contact and shared towels/bedding; shaving and hygiene; common in hot and temperate areas of Ethiopia) and a lab-based scabies investigation.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
