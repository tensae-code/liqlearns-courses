# Bacterial sexually transmitted infections — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about bacterial stis?**
**Answer: Sexually transmitted infections pass mainly by sexual contact and number over twenty; the bacterial set is worked one by one with agent, transmission, symptoms and prevention/treatment for each: bacterial vaginosis, chlamydia-type infections as presented, syphilis (Treponema; four stages tabled from painless primary chancre through rash, latency to tertiary damage of brain, heart and nerves), gonorrhoea (Neisseria; painful urination and discharge, sometimes silent) and chancroid.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In your own words, what does this lesson teach about added sti investigation briefs?**
**Answer: Ready-made investigation briefs with the facts teachers should steer pupils to: chlamydia (agent Chlamydia trachomatis; painful urination and discharge; protective sex and care as prevention/treatment), HIV (a retrovirus; spread by infected body fluids, unprotected sex, shared injection equipment and blood contact), pubic lice (close contact and shared towels/bedding; shaving and hygiene; common in hot and temperate areas of Ethiopia) and a lab-based scabies investigation.**
