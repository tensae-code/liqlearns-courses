# Bulk transport — endocytosis and exocytosis — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (large particles cross the membrane in bulk, wrapped in membrane: endocytosis brings them in, and exocytosis sends them out — Golgi packs the material into secretory vesicles that fuse with the plasma membrane) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
