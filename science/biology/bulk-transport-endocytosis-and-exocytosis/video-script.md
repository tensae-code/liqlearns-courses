Today: Bulk transport — endocytosis and exocytosis.

Remember this one thing: large particles cross the membrane in bulk, wrapped in membrane: endocytosis brings them in, and exocytosis sends them out — Golgi packs the material into secretory vesicles that fuse with the plasma membrane.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 72–73) teaches bulk transport for large particles: endocytosis takes large particles into the cell.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
