# Hands-on — Bulk transport — endocytosis and exocytosis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: large particles cross the membrane in bulk, wrapped in membrane: endocytosis brings them in, and exocytosis sends them out — Golgi packs the material into secretory vesicles that fuse with the plasma membrane.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a cell with two arrows: a large particle being wrapped by the membrane and taken in, labelled endocytosis, and a vesicle fusing with the membrane and releasing its contents outside, labelled exocytosis. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
