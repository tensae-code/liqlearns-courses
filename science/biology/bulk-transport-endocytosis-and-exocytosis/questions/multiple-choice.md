# Bulk transport — endocytosis and exocytosis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) large particles cross the membrane in bulk, wrapped in membrane: endocytosis brings them in, and exocytosis sends them out — Golgi packs the material into secretory vesicles that fuse with the plasma membrane  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) large particles cross the membrane in bulk, wrapped in membrane: endocytosis brings them in, and exocytosis sends them out — Golgi packs the material into secretory vesicles that fuse with the plasma membrane**

**2. Endocytosis — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 72–73) teaches bulk transport for large particles: endocytosis takes large particles into the cell.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 72–73) teaches bulk transport for large particles: endocytosis takes large particles into the cell.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) The book draws exocytosis step by step: the Golgi apparatus packs material into secretory vesicles, the vesicles fuse with the plasma membrane, and the material is released outside — with neurotransmitter release as the worked example.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Kuwait
**Answer: b) The book draws exocytosis step by step: the Golgi apparatus packs material into secretory vesicles, the vesicles fuse with the plasma membrane, and the material is released outside — with neurotransmitter release as the worked example.**
