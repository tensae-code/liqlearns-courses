# Bulk transport — endocytosis and exocytosis — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: large particles cross the membrane in bulk, wrapped in membrane: endocytosis brings them in, and exocytosis sends them out — Golgi packs the material into secretory vesicles that fuse with the plasma membrane. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 72–73) teaches bulk transport for large particles: endocytosis takes large particles into the cell.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's new Grade 10 book closes its transport lesson with bulk transport drawn as a sequence (Lesson 1-4, printed pp. 72–73): the learner follows a secretory vesicle from the Golgi to the membrane, with neurotransmitter release as the example that matters later in the nervous-system lessons.**
