# Organisation of the genetic material — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (genetic material is organised very differently across life: a prokaryote keeps one circular DNA molecule loose in the cytoplasm, while a eukaryote winds its DNA with proteins into chromosomes inside a nucleus — and viruses carry theirs as DNA or RNA in a protein coat) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
