Today: Organisation of the genetic material.

Remember this one thing: genetic material is organised very differently across life: a prokaryote keeps one circular DNA molecule loose in the cytoplasm, while a eukaryote winds its DNA with proteins into chromosomes inside a nucleus — and viruses carry theirs as DNA or RNA in a protein coat.

prokaryotic single circular DNA vs complex eukaryotic organisation
