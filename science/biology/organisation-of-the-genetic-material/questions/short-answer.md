# Organisation of the genetic material — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: genetic material is organised very differently across life: a prokaryote keeps one circular DNA molecule loose in the cytoplasm, while a eukaryote winds its DNA with proteins into chromosomes inside a nucleus — and viruses carry theirs as DNA or RNA in a protein coat. prokaryotic single circular DNA vs complex eukaryotic organisation**

**2.** Explain Greece's way of teaching this.
**Answer: prokaryotic single circular DNA vs complex eukaryotic organisation**
