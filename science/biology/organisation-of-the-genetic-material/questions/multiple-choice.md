# Organisation of the genetic material — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) genetic material is organised very differently across life: a prokaryote keeps one circular DNA molecule loose in the cytoplasm, while a eukaryote winds its DNA with proteins into chromosomes inside a nucleus — and viruses carry theirs as DNA or RNA in a protein coat  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
