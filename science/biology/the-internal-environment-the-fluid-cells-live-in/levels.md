# The internal environment — the fluid cells live in — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (every cell lives in fluid: body fluid divides into the intracellular two-thirds and the extracellular third — plasma, tissue fluid and lymph — whose composition and osmotic pressure (about 770 kPa at 37 °C, over 90% of it from Na⁺ and Cl⁻) the body holds steady, a salt solution much like the seawater life came from) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
