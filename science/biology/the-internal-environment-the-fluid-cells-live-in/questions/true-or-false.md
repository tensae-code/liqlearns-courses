# The internal environment — the fluid cells live in — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. body fluid divided into intracellular (2/3) and extracellular (1/3: plasma, tissue fluid, lymph), then plasma analysed from a real composition table (sodium 137–147 mmol/L, glucose 3.9–6.1, enzymes in U/L); osmotic pressure built to a number (about 770 kPa at 37 °C, over 90% of it from Na+ and Cl−); and the remark that extracellular fluid is a salt solution much like seawater — a fossil of life's ocean origin. No existing lesson is given to the internal environment itself.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in China's People's Education Press biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (普通高中教科书《生物学》选择性必修1 稳态与调节 (Senior High Biology, Selective Compulsory Book 1: Homeostasis and Regulation, People's Education Press, 2019-standard edition)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the internal environment — the fluid cells live in.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the internal environment — the fluid cells live in.
