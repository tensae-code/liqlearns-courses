# Hands-on — Life-history strategies — r and K selection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: organisms hold their place by two broad life strategies: r-strategists make many offspring with little care, and K-strategists make few offspring with much care — the ecological strategies of a species' viability.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: two panels: a fish releasing thousands of eggs labelled r-strategy, and an elephant guarding one calf labelled K-strategy, with a scale of offspring number against care beneath. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
