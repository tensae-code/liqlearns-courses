# Video script — Effects of Climate Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

opened with a Greta Thunberg quotation and photograph; direct consequences — rising maximum temperatures, rising sea levels, higher ocean temperatures, heavier precipitation, shrinking glaciers, thawing permafrost; indirect consequences — hunger and water crises, heat-wave health risks, economic crisis, spreading pests and pathogens, biodiversity loss, ocean acidification; then the effects in turn on biodiversity, on agriculture, on forest productivity, and climate change and natural disasters — including practical flood-safety guidance (never walk, swim or drive through floodwater; move to higher ground; watch for evacuation alerts) under a "Mitigation Measure" heading.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
