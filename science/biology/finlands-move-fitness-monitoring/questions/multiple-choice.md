# Finland's Move! fitness monitoring — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is Move!?**
a) A dance programme  b) Finland's national system for monitoring school pupils' physical functional capacity  c) A brand of sports shoe  d) A school lunch menu
**Answer: b) Finland's national system for monitoring school pupils' physical functional capacity**

**2. Which three areas do the Move! batteries cover?**
a) Speed, height and weight  b) Reading, writing and arithmetic  c) Endurance, strength and mobility  d) Sight, hearing and taste
**Answer: c) Endurance, strength and mobility**

**3. Against what are Move! results read?**
a) Olympic records  b) National reference values  c) The teacher's opinion  d) Last year's weather
**Answer: b) National reference values**

**4. Why does the same measurement have to be made the same way across the country?**
a) So the results are comparable from school to school  b) So it takes longer  c) So pupils cannot practise  d) It does not matter how it is made
**Answer: a) So the results are comparable from school to school**
