# Finland's Move! fitness monitoring — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain what 'functional capacity' means in the Move! system.
**Answer: It means what a young body can actually do — how long it can keep going (endurance), how much force it can produce (strength), and how freely it can move (mobility) — measured directly by test batteries rather than guessed from appearance or weight.**

**2.** Explain Finland's way in this lesson.
**Answer: Finland monitors its school pupils' functional capacity nationally, through the Move! system (oph.fi/move), and teaches it inside the wellbeing pack as Card 14: pupils are measured with endurance, strength and mobility batteries and their results are read against national reference values, under the pack's rules — voluntary, never competitive, never centrally collected.**
