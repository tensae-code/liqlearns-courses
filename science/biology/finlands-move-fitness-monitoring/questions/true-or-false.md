# Finland's Move! fitness monitoring — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Move! is one school's local fitness idea, used nowhere else.**
**Answer: False** — Move! is Finland's national monitoring system for schools, used across the country.

**2. Mobility is one of the three Move! battery areas.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches — endurance, strength and mobility.

**3. National reference values are the gathered results of pupils across Finland.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. In the wellbeing pack, Move! results are published as a competition ranking.**
**Answer: False** — the pack's rules: measurements are voluntary, results are never compared competitively, and data is not collected centrally.
