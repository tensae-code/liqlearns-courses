# Finland's Move! fitness monitoring — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Move! monitors pupils' physical _____ capacity.**
**Answer: functional**

**2. The three batteries are endurance, strength and _____.**
**Answer: mobility**

**3. Results are read against national _____ values.**
**Answer: reference**

**4. Move! is Card _____ of Finland's wellbeing pack.**
**Answer: 14**
