# Hands-on — Finland's Move! fitness monitoring

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Try one simple functional-capacity measure of your own — for example, how long you can stand on one leg, or how many sit-to-stand movements you can do in one minute — and record it. Say which Move! battery it belongs to: endurance, strength or mobility.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the three Move! batteries from memory as three labelled panels — endurance, strength, mobility — with one test drawn in each, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
