# Video script — Finland's Move! fitness monitoring

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Finland's Move! fitness monitoring.

Remember this one thing: Finland watches over its pupils' bodies with a national system called Move! — the same measurements, made the same way, in schools across the whole country.

The measurements come in three batteries: endurance, strength and mobility — what a young body can do, tested directly.

And every result is read against national reference values, the gathered results of Finland's pupils, so one pupil's number can be understood against the whole country's picture.

In Finland's wellbeing pack, Move! is one card in a course of self-measurement — and like every card in that pack, it is voluntary and never a competition.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
