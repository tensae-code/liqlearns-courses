# Video script — Insect reproduction and metamorphosis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Insects are segmented, joint-legged arthropods with exoskeletons; the honeybee's male and female reproductive structures are figured and researched; fertilisation is usually internal, eggs are laid after mating, and development runs through metamorphosis — complete (egg, larva, pupa, adult, as in honeybees) or incomplete (egg, nymph, adult, as in grasshoppers).

The guide supplies the grasshopper's male system (paired testes, vasa deferentia, ejaculatory duct, copulatory organ, accessory glands) and female system (ovary, oviduct, spermatheca and more), and a complete/incomplete metamorphosis table: incomplete — three stages, nymphs resembling adults, wings appearing as buds, grasshoppers/cockroaches/termites; complete — four stages each looking different, most insects.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
