# Exploring inherited traits — a class survey — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: inherited traits can be counted: survey the class trait by trait — earlobe form among them — and count how many show each trait and how many do not; the class itself becomes the data. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 applications workbook (Activity 9, printed p. 36) has the class surveyed trait by trait, earlobe form among them.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's genetics activities are investigations of the learners' own class and family rather than paper-only problems: the trait survey (Activity 9) turns the classroom into the sample.**
