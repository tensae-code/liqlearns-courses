# Hands-on — Exploring inherited traits — a class survey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: inherited traits can be counted: survey the class trait by trait — earlobe form among them — and count how many show each trait and how many do not; the class itself becomes the data.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a class survey chart on a board: trait names in a column — earlobe form first — with tally marks counting how many show the trait and how many do not. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
