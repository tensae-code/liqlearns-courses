# Exploring inherited traits — a class survey

## The idea
the class surveyed trait by trait (earlobe form among them), counting how many show each trait and how many do not

[Picture — a class survey chart on a board: trait names in a column — earlobe form first — with tally marks counting how many show the trait and how many do not]

## The key facts
**The survey.** Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 1 applications workbook (Activity 9, printed p. 36) has the class surveyed trait by trait, earlobe form among them.

**Counting both ways.** For each trait the count is made both ways: how many show the trait and how many do not — the two counts together are the activity's result.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's genetics activities are investigations of the learners' own class and family rather than paper-only problems: the trait survey (Activity 9) turns the classroom into the sample.
