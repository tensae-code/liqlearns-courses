# Plant cell or animal cell? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about shared?
a) Plant and animal cells share membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria and Golgi apparatus
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Plant and animal cells share membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria and Golgi apparatus**

**2.** What does this lesson say about plant only?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Plant cells alone have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Plant cells alone have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole**

**3.** What does this lesson say about animal vacuoles?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Animal cells have only small, temporary vacuoles
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Animal cells have only small, temporary vacuoles**

**4.** What does this lesson say about shape?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Animal cells differ in shape from plant cells
**Answer: d) Animal cells differ in shape from plant cells**
