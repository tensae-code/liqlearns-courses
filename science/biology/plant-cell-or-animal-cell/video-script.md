# Video script — Plant cell or animal cell?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today: Plant cell or animal cell?.

Remember this one thing: plant cells alone have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole.

Plant and animal cells share membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria and Golgi apparatus.

Plant cells alone have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
