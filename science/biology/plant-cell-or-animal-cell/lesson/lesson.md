# Plant cell or animal cell?

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Plant cell or animal cell?. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
The two cells share much — membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria and Golgi apparatus. Three structures separate them.

[Picture 21029 — the two cells side by side with plant-only structures highlighted]

## The key facts
**Shared.** Plant and animal cells share membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, mitochondria and Golgi apparatus.

**Plant only.** Plant cells alone have a cell wall, chloroplasts and a large permanent vacuole.

**Animal vacuoles.** Animal cells have only small, temporary vacuoles.

**Shape.** Animal cells differ in shape from plant cells.
