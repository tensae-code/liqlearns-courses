# Somatic and autonomic (vegetative) nervous system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) the peripheral nervous system divides by control: the somatic system serves voluntary movement, while the autonomic (vegetative) system — sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions — runs its effectors through two-neuron chains  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
