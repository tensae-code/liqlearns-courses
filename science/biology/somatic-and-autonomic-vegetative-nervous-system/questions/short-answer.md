# Somatic and autonomic (vegetative) nervous system — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the peripheral nervous system divides by control: the somatic system serves voluntary movement, while the autonomic (vegetative) system — sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions — runs its effectors through two-neuron chains. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 4-1, printed pp. 46–49) sets the somatic nervous system against the autonomic (vegetative) nervous system.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's Grade 12 book teaches the peripheral divisions as a contrast table in the making (Lesson 4-1): somatic against autonomic, and inside the autonomic, sympathetic against parasympathetic, told apart by their neuron chains.**
