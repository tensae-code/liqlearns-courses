# Somatic and autonomic (vegetative) nervous system — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 4-1, printed pp. 46–49) sets the somatic nervous system against the autonomic (vegetative) nervous system.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف الثاني عشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 12, Term 1), printed pp. 46–49).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with somatic and autonomic (vegetative) nervous system.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is somatic and autonomic (vegetative) nervous system.
