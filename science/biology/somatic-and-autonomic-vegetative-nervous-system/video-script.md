Today: Somatic and autonomic (vegetative) nervous system.

Remember this one thing: the peripheral nervous system divides by control: the somatic system serves voluntary movement, while the autonomic (vegetative) system — sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions — runs its effectors through two-neuron chains.

Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 4-1, printed pp. 46–49) sets the somatic nervous system against the autonomic (vegetative) nervous system.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
