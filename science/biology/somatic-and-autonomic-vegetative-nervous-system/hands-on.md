# Hands-on — Somatic and autonomic (vegetative) nervous system

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the peripheral nervous system divides by control: the somatic system serves voluntary movement, while the autonomic (vegetative) system — sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions — runs its effectors through two-neuron chains.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a diagram of two pathways from the spinal cord: a single somatic neuron reaching a skeletal muscle, and an autonomic two-neuron chain reaching a gland, with the sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions drawn as two branches. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
