# Somatic and autonomic (vegetative) nervous system

## The idea
the somatic nervous system against the autonomic, whose sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions run two-neuron chains to their effectors (same proposed slug as in the Moldova filings)

## The key facts
**Somatic against autonomic.** Kuwait's Grade 12 Term 1 student book (Lesson 4-1, printed pp. 46–49) sets the somatic nervous system against the autonomic (vegetative) nervous system.

**Two-neuron chains.** The autonomic system's sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions run two-neuron chains to their effectors — the structural fact the book uses to tell the autonomic pathway from the somatic one.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's Grade 12 book teaches the peripheral divisions as a contrast table in the making (Lesson 4-1): somatic against autonomic, and inside the autonomic, sympathetic against parasympathetic, told apart by their neuron chains.
