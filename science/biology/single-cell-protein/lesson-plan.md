# Lesson plan — Single-cell protein

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Single-cell protein
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — secondary band (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Single-cell protein (SCP) is protein harvested from single-celled microorganisms — algae, yeasts, other fungi and bacteria grown in bulk on cheap feedstocks — dried and used as a protein-rich food or feed: protein grown in a tank instead of a field.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the earlier biology of living things, health and the human body, and builds the secondary band understanding of this topic.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
