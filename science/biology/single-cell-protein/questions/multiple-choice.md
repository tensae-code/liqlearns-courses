# Single-cell protein — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Single-cell protein is protein harvested from…**
a) single leaves b) single-celled microorganisms grown in bulk c) eggs d) fish
**Answer: b) single-celled microorganisms grown in bulk**

**2. Which of these is used for SCP?**
a) Spirulina, an alga b) wheat grain c) cow muscle d) tree bark
**Answer: a) Spirulina, an alga**

**3. An advantage of SCP production is that it…**
a) needs large farms b) grows in days in a tank, without farmland or seasons c) never needs feeding d) makes no waste
**Answer: b) grows in days in a tank, without farmland or seasons**

**4. Punjab teaches SCP…**
a) in its Grade 10 biotechnology chapter (Ch 17.4) b) in the cell chapter c) in the statistics chapter d) only at Grade 12
**Answer: a) in its Grade 10 biotechnology chapter (Ch 17.4)**
