# Single-cell protein — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Protein harvested from single-celled microorganisms is called _____-cell protein.**
**Answer: single**

**2. The dried cells are used as a protein-rich food or _____.**
**Answer: feed**

**3. SCP cultures can be fed on cheap materials including _____ streams.**
**Answer: waste**

**4. Algae, yeasts, fungi and _____ are the microorganisms used.**
**Answer: bacteria**
