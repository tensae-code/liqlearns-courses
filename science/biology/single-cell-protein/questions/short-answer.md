# Single-cell protein — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: what single-cell protein is and how it is produced.
**Answer: Single-cell protein is protein harvested from single-celled microorganisms — algae, yeasts, other fungi and bacteria — grown in bulk in tanks on cheap feedstocks, then separated, dried and used as a protein-rich food or feed.**

**2.** Explain: Pakistan (Punjab)'s way of teaching this.
**Answer: Punjab's Grade 10 book places SCP in Section 6, Applications of Biology, as Ch 17.4 of its biotechnology chapter (printed p. 147) — after fermentation and genetic engineering — teaching biotechnology through the products it makes.**
