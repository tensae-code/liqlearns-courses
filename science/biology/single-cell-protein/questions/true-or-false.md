# Single-cell protein — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Single-cell protein comes from the cells of microorganisms, not from a crop plant or an animal.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches

**2. SCP production takes a full growing season like a field crop.**
**Answer: False** — the microorganisms grow in days in a tank

**3. The harvested cells are dried before use as food or feed.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches

**4. SCP is taught by Punjab as a branch of pure chemistry, unrelated to biotechnology.**
**Answer: False** — it is taught inside the Grade 10 biotechnology chapter, as one of its named products
