# Hands-on — Single-cell protein

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
List three protein foods you know and where each one's protein grew — a field, an animal, a tank. Then say, for SCP, what the “crop” is and how long its growing takes compared with a field crop.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a large glass tank of green culture with bubbles rising, a harvest of dried green powder being scooped from a tray beside it, and a small heap of the powder labelled as protein food — and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
