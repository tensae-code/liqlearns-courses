# Plant tissues — meristems and definitive tissues — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (A plant is built from tissues that all begin in its meristems: primary meristems lengthen the plant, secondary meristems thicken root and stem, and the meristems produce the four definitive tissues — covering, parenchyma (storage), support and vascular (conducting) tissues.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by naming the two meristems with their directions of growth (primary — length; secondary — thickness) and listing the four definitive tissues in order without looking.
