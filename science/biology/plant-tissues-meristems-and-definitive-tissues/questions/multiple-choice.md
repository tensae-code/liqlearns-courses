# Plant tissues — meristems and definitive tissues — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Histology is the study of…**
a) organs  b) tissues  c) cells only  d) ecosystems
**Answer: b) tissues**

**2. Primary meristems make the plant…**
a) thicker  b) longer  c) greener  d) flower
**Answer: b) longer**

**3. Which of these is NOT one of the four definitive tissues?**
a) covering  b) parenchyma  c) support  d) meristem
**Answer: d) meristem**

**4. The vascular tissue's job in the plant is…**
a) storage  b) conducting  c) covering  d) photosynthesis only
**Answer: b) conducting**
