# Plant tissues — meristems and definitive tissues — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. The study of tissues is called _____.**
**Answer: histology**

**2. Meristems that lengthen the plant are called _____ meristems.**
**Answer: primary**

**3. The storage tissue of the plant is the _____.**
**Answer: parenchyma**

**4. The conducting tissue is also called _____ tissue.**
**Answer: vascular**
