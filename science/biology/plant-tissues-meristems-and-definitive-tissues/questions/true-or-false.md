# Plant tissues — meristems and definitive tissues — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Secondary meristems thicken the root and the stem.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. Definitive tissues are still dividing like meristem cells.**
**Answer: False** — definitive tissues are the finished products of the meristems.

**3. Parenchyma is the storage tissue.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**4. A plant has three definitive tissues.**
**Answer: False** — the module teaches four: covering, parenchyma, support and vascular.
