# Plant tissues — meristems and definitive tissues — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Where do all a plant's tissues come from, and what are the two kinds of source?
**Answer: All tissues come from the meristems. Primary meristems, at the tips, lengthen the plant; secondary meristems thicken the root and stem. The meristems produce the four definitive tissues.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD II plant-physiology module (Módulo 5 de Biologia), Lesson 1, opens the subject with histology — the study of tissues — defined in the book: meristems (primary for lengthening, secondary for thickening root and stem) producing the four definitive tissues: covering, parenchyma/storage, support and vascular/conducting.**
