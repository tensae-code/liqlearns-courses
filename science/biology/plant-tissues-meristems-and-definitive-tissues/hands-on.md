# Hands-on — Plant tissues — meristems and definitive tissues

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Snap a soft plant stem and look at the cut end with your eyes close: skin outside, soft flesh in the middle, tougher strands running through — covering, parenchyma and the conducting/support tissues, all made earlier at the tip. Point to the tip itself: the meristem that made them.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — a shoot tip and a root tip drawn with their small dividing meristem cells at the very ends, and arrows running back into the stem and root where the four finished tissues are labelled in a cut section — covering outside, parenchyma filling, support strands and vascular bundles — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
