# The fluid mosaic model of the cell membrane

## Do it first

## The idea
the fluid mosaic model; membrane proteins classified by job: channel, carrier, receptor, identity-marker and enzymatic proteins

[Picture — a cell membrane drawn as two rows of phospholipid heads and tails with protein shapes embedded in it — a channel, a carrier, a receptor and a marker protein each labelled with its job]

## The key facts
**The fluid mosaic model.** Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed pp. 35–36) presents the cell membrane through the fluid mosaic model: the lipid layer is fluid — its parts can move — and the proteins sit in it as a mosaic of different pieces.

**Membrane proteins by job.** The book classifies membrane proteins by the job each does: channel proteins, carrier proteins, receptor proteins, identity-marker proteins and enzymatic proteins — five named kinds, each with its own work in the membrane.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's new-curriculum Grade 10 book names the membrane model outright and teaches the membrane through its proteins' jobs — channel, carrier, receptor, identity-marker and enzymatic — rather than as a plain barrier.
