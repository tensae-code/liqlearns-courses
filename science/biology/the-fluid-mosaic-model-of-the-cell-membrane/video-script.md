Today: The fluid mosaic model of the cell membrane.

Remember this one thing: the cell membrane is a fluid mosaic: a double layer of phospholipids that can flow, with proteins scattered through it like tiles in a mosaic — and each kind of membrane protein does its own job.

Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed pp. 35–36) presents the cell membrane through the fluid mosaic model: the lipid layer is fluid — its parts can move — and the proteins sit in it as a mosaic of different pieces.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
