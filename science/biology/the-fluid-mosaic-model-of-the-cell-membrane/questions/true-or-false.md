# The fluid mosaic model of the cell membrane — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed pp. 35–36) presents the cell membrane through the fluid mosaic model: the lipid layer is fluid — its parts can move — and the proteins sit in it as a mosaic of different pieces.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كتاب الطالب — الصف العاشر — الفصل الدراسي الأول (Biology — Student Book, Grade 10, Term 1), printed pp. 35–36).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the fluid mosaic model of the cell membrane.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the fluid mosaic model of the cell membrane.
