# The fluid mosaic model of the cell membrane — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: the cell membrane is a fluid mosaic: a double layer of phospholipids that can flow, with proteins scattered through it like tiles in a mosaic — and each kind of membrane protein does its own job. Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed pp. 35–36) presents the cell membrane through the fluid mosaic model: the lipid layer is fluid — its parts can move — and the proteins sit in it as a mosaic of different pieces.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's new-curriculum Grade 10 book names the membrane model outright and teaches the membrane through its proteins' jobs — channel, carrier, receptor, identity-marker and enzymatic — rather than as a plain barrier.**
