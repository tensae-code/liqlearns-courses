# The fluid mosaic model of the cell membrane — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. What is the one thing this lesson teaches?**
a) the cell membrane is a fluid mosaic: a double layer of phospholipids that can flow, with proteins scattered through it like tiles in a mosaic — and each kind of membrane protein does its own job  b) That this topic has no rules and every case is different  c) A list of names with no process behind them  d) That the topic belongs only to scientists, not to daily life
**Answer: a) the cell membrane is a fluid mosaic: a double layer of phospholipids that can flow, with proteins scattered through it like tiles in a mosaic — and each kind of membrane protein does its own job**

**2. The fluid mosaic model — which statement is right?**
a) Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed pp. 35–36) presents the cell membrane through the fluid mosaic model: the lipid layer is fluid — its parts can move — and the proteins sit in it as a mosaic of different pieces.  b) The opposite of what the lesson teaches  c) It happens only in examinations  d) It is never observed in real life
**Answer: a) Kuwait's new Grade 10 student book (Lesson 1-2, printed pp. 35–36) presents the cell membrane through the fluid mosaic model: the lipid layer is fluid — its parts can move — and the proteins sit in it as a mosaic of different pieces.**

**3. Which statement about this lesson's topic is right?**
a) It is unrelated to the lesson's topic  b) The book classifies membrane proteins by the job each does: channel proteins, carrier proteins, receptor proteins, identity-marker proteins and enzymatic proteins — five named kinds, each with its own work in the membrane.  c) It is a myth the book corrects  d) It applies only outside Kuwait
**Answer: b) The book classifies membrane proteins by the job each does: channel proteins, carrier proteins, receptor proteins, identity-marker proteins and enzymatic proteins — five named kinds, each with its own work in the membrane.**
