# Video script — Ethiopia's Response to Climate Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethiopia as one of the world's most drought-prone countries, its vulnerability worsened by rain-fed agriculture, water, tourism and forestry dependence and poverty; the policy line from ratifying the UNFCCC (1994) through the National Adaptation Plan of Action (2007), the adaptation programme and NAMA (2010) to the Climate Resilient Green Economy strategy (2011); the Green Legacy Initiative (2019) targeting 20 billion seedlings in four years; and the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam — 5.15 gigawatts on the Abay (Blue Nile), under construction since 2011 in Benishangul-Gumuz, set to be Africa's largest hydroelectric plant, with electricity already exported to Djibouti and Sudan.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
