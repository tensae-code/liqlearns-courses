# Measuring abiotic factors in the field — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which instrument measures relative humidity?
a) Anemometer
b) Wet-and-dry-bulb hygrometer
c) Light meter
d) Aneroid barometer
**Answer: b) Wet-and-dry-bulb hygrometer**

**2.** What does an anemometer measure?
a) Light intensity
b) Air pressure
c) Wind speed
d) Temperature
**Answer: c) Wind speed**

**3.** Elevation in the field is read using…
a) a light meter
b) an aneroid barometer (air pressure)
c) gill rakers
d) a quadrat
**Answer: b) an aneroid barometer (air pressure)**

**4.** In Nigeria, day length is…
a) longest in December
b) the same all year
c) shortest in December and longest in July
d) shortest in July
**Answer: c) shortest in December and longest in July**
