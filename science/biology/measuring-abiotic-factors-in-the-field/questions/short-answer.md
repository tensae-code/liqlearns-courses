# Measuring abiotic factors in the field — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: match each abiotic factor to its instrument.
**Answer: Temperature — thermometer. Relative humidity — wet-and-dry-bulb hygrometer. Wind — vane for direction, anemometer for speed. Light intensity — light meter. Elevation — aneroid barometer, reading air pressure.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches the ecological or habitat factors in Chapter 7 (printed p. 110) instrument by instrument, then works them with Nigerian data — the Jos mean monthly temperature table from the Federal Meteorological Service, Lagos, Nigerian day length (shortest in December, longest in July), and the short-day flowering of dry-season okro.**
