# Measuring abiotic factors in the field — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A wind vane measures wind speed.
**Answer: False** — A wind vane shows direction; the anemometer measures speed.

**2.** The book's Jos temperature table comes from the Federal Meteorological Service, Lagos.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Dry-season okro is the book's example of a short-day flowering plant.
**Answer: True**
