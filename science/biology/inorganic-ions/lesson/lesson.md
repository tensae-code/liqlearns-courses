# Inorganic ions — small charged particles, many jobs

## The idea
This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only. The guide writes out why ions matter. (teacher guide — evidence)

## The key facts
**Ion.** An ion is a charged atom, positive or negative.

**Pairing.** Ions are almost always paired with an opposite charge.

**Where.** In living bodies ions occur free and dissolved in the cytoplasm or associated with complex organic substances.

**Jobs.** Ions perform a variety of functions in living bodies.

### Student-book key facts
**Macro and micro.** Ions sort into macronutrients, needed in quantity, and micronutrients, needed in traces.

**The eight.** Sodium, potassium, calcium, phosphate, chloride, iron, copper and iodide are the ions taken one by one.

**Each ion's card.** Each ion is given with its job in the body, its sources, the body's requirement, and what goes wrong in deficiency or excess.

**In the body.** In living bodies ions occur free and dissolved, doing many jobs.
