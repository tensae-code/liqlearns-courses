Today: Inorganic ions — small charged particles, many jobs.

Remember this one thing: ions are charged atoms that occur free in cytoplasm or joined to organic substances, doing many jobs.

An ion is a charged atom, positive or negative.

Ions are almost always paired with an opposite charge.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: Ions sort into macronutrients, needed in quantity, and micronutrients, needed in traces. Sodium, potassium, calcium, phosphate, chloride, iron, copper and iodide are the ions taken one by one.
