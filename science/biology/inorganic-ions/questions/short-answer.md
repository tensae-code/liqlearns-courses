# Inorganic ions — small charged particles, many jobs — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: where.
**Answer: In living bodies ions occur free and dissolved in the cytoplasm or associated with complex organic substances.**

**2.** Explain: jobs.
**Answer: Ions perform a variety of functions in living bodies.**

**3.** Explain: the eight (student book).
**Answer: Sodium, potassium, calcium, phosphate, chloride, iron, copper and iodide are the ions taken one by one.**
