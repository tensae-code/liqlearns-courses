# Gene patents — and owning a discovery — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a gene patent claims a discovered gene sequence as intellectual property, and Greece's Grade 12 bioethics chapter asks the hard question outright: can discovering a gene — finding what nature already made — fairly be owned, and what does owning it do to medicine and research) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
