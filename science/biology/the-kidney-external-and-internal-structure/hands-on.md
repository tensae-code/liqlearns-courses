# Hands-on — The kidney — external and internal structure

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: a sheep kidney cut open shows the organ's plan from outside and inside: the external form first, then the internal structures the cut reveals, each identified in place.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a sheep kidney whole beside the same kidney cut open lengthwise, the internal regions labelled as the dissection identifies them. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
