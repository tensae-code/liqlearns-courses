# The kidney — external and internal structure

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The kidney — external and internal structure. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
the kidney cut open and its external and internal structures identified

[Picture — a sheep kidney whole beside the same kidney cut open lengthwise, the internal regions labelled as the dissection identifies them]

## The key facts
**The dissection.** Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 7, printed p. 30) dissects a sheep kidney: the kidney is cut open and its external and internal structures are identified.

**Kuwait's way.** Kuwait's workbook teaches excretion through the dissecting tray (Activity 7): a real sheep kidney, cut open by the learners, its structures named on the organ itself.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words: a sheep kidney cut open shows the organ's plan from outside and inside: the external form first, then the internal structures the cut reveals, each identified in place. Use each key fact once. Then let your companion explain it back to you.
