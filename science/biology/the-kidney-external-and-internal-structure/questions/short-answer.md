# The kidney — external and internal structure — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: a sheep kidney cut open shows the organ's plan from outside and inside: the external form first, then the internal structures the cut reveals, each identified in place. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 7, printed p. 30) dissects a sheep kidney: the kidney is cut open and its external and internal structures are identified.**

**2.** Explain Kuwait's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Kuwait's workbook teaches excretion through the dissecting tray (Activity 7): a real sheep kidney, cut open by the learners, its structures named on the organ itself.**
