# The kidney — external and internal structure — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 7, printed p. 30) dissects a sheep kidney: the kidney is cut open and its external and internal structures are identified.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Kuwait's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (الأحياء — كراسة التطبيقات — الصف الحادي عشر — الفصل الدراسي الثاني (Biology — Applications Workbook, Grade 11, Term 2), printed p. 30).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the kidney — external and internal structure.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the kidney — external and internal structure.
