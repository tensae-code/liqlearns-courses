Today: The kidney — external and internal structure.

Remember this one thing: a sheep kidney cut open shows the organ's plan from outside and inside: the external form first, then the internal structures the cut reveals, each identified in place.

Kuwait's Grade 11 Term 2 applications workbook (Activity 7, printed p. 30) dissects a sheep kidney: the kidney is cut open and its external and internal structures are identified.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
