# Hands-on — Lichens — a symbiosis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find a lichen on a rock, wall or tree trunk near home or school. Decide its form — crust, leafy or tufted — and note whether lichens are plentiful there or missing, and what that may say about the air.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a slice through a lichen, labelled — fungal hyphae woven through the body with algal cells dotted among them — beside the same lichen whole on a rock in its crust form — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
