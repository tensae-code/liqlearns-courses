# Lichens — a symbiosis — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: three forms.
**Answer: Crustose lichens sit like paint on rock and bark and cannot be peeled off whole. Foliose lichens lift in leafy lobes. Fruticose lichens hang from branches or stand up like tiny shrubs — the three forms are how lichens are first sorted in the field.**

**2.** Explain: pioneers on bare rock.
**Answer: Lichens grow where almost nothing else can — bare rock, desert crust, cold mountain stone. Their acids slowly break the rock's surface, and when they die their remains mix with the dust: the first soil, in which mosses and then higher plants can root.**
