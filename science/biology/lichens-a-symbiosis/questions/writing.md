**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining lichens — a symbiosis, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: A lichen is not one living thing but two living together as one body: a fungus and an alga (or cyanobacterium) in symbiosis, each giving what the other lacks. A lichen is a symbiosis: a fungus living together with an alga or a cyanobacterium in a single body. The fungus gives shelter, water and minerals; the alga makes food by photosynthesis and shares it. Lichens grow as crusts tight on the surface (crustose), as leafy lobes (foliose), or as hanging or upright tufts (fruticose).**
