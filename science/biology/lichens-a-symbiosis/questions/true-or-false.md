# Lichens — a symbiosis — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A lichen is a symbiosis: a fungus living together with an alga or a cyanobacterium in a single body.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A lichen is a single species of fungus..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** The fungus gives shelter, water and minerals; the alga makes food by photosynthesis and shares it.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Lichens need rich soil before they can grow..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
