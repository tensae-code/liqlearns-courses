# Lichens — a symbiosis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about two partners, one body?
a) A lichen is a symbiosis: a fungus living together with an alga or a cyanobacterium in a single body
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) A lichen is a symbiosis: a fungus living together with an alga or a cyanobacterium in a single body**

**2.** What does this lesson say about what each partner gives?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) The fungus gives shelter, water and minerals; the alga makes food by photosynthesis and shares it
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The fungus gives shelter, water and minerals; the alga makes food by photosynthesis and shares it**

**3.** What does this lesson say about three forms?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Lichens grow as crusts tight on the surface (crustose), as leafy lobes (foliose), or as hanging or upright tufts (fruticose)
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Lichens grow as crusts tight on the surface (crustose), as leafy lobes (foliose), or as hanging or upright tufts (fruticose)**

**4.** What does this lesson say about pioneers on bare rock?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Lichens are pioneers: they colonise bare rock and begin the slow making of soil
**Answer: d) Lichens are pioneers: they colonise bare rock and begin the slow making of soil**
