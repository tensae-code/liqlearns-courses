# Bird reproduction — the oviduct and the egg — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Birds reproduce sexually with internal fertilisation, mostly monogamous; males have no external genital organs, females a single ovary, and sperm passes cloaca to cloaca; the ovum travels the oviduct — infundibulum, magnum, isthmus, uterus, vagina — gaining albumen and shell on the way; the finished egg's parts (yolk, chalaza, albumen, membranes, air sac, shell) are named.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Beyond the student book's egg-parts list, the guide describes the shell's protection against bacterial contamination, the two semi-permeable shell membranes forming an air cell at the large end, the egg white (albumen) in three layers — outer thin, middle thick, inner thin — valued for holding air when beaten and carrying a small share of the egg's protein, and the yolk as the nutrient store; it also restates the bird oviduct at 25–27 inches with its five sections.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
