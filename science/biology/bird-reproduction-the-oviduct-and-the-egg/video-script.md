# Video script — Bird reproduction — the oviduct and the egg

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Birds reproduce sexually with internal fertilisation, mostly monogamous; males have no external genital organs, females a single ovary, and sperm passes cloaca to cloaca; the ovum travels the oviduct — infundibulum, magnum, isthmus, uterus, vagina — gaining albumen and shell on the way; the finished egg's parts (yolk, chalaza, albumen, membranes, air sac, shell) are named.

Beyond the student book's egg-parts list, the guide describes the shell's protection against bacterial contamination, the two semi-permeable shell membranes forming an air cell at the large end, the egg white (albumen) in three layers — outer thin, middle thick, inner thin — valued for holding air when beaten and carrying a small share of the egg's protein, and the yolk as the nutrient store; it also restates the bird oviduct at 25–27 inches with its five sections.

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
