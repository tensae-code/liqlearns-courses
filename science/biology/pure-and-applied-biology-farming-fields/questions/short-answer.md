# Pure and applied biology — farming fields — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: agroforestry.
**Answer: Agroforestry deliberately incorporates trees and shrubs into crop and animal production.**

**2.** Explain: agroforestry benefit.
**Answer: Agroforestry brings environmental, economic and social benefit and has been practised for generations in Ethiopia, e.g. on smallholder farms.**

**3.** Explain: applied fields (student book).
**Answer: Applied fields put knowledge to work in farming, medicine and industry.**
