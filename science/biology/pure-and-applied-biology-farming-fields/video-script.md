The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Today: Pure and applied biology — farming fields.

Remember this one thing: horticulture grows high-value food and ornamental plants; agroforestry deliberately mixes trees into crop and animal production.

Horticulture is the science and art of growing, producing, marketing and using high-value food and ornamental plants.

Horticulture covers annuals and perennials, fruits and vegetables, indoor and landscape plants.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: Pure fields seek knowledge for its own sake. Applied fields put knowledge to work in farming, medicine and industry.
