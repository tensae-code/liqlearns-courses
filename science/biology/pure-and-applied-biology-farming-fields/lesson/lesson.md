## The key facts
**Horticulture.** Horticulture is the science and art of growing, producing, marketing and using high-value food and ornamental plants.

**Horticulture range.** Horticulture covers annuals and perennials, fruits and vegetables, indoor and landscape plants.

**Agroforestry.** Agroforestry deliberately incorporates trees and shrubs into crop and animal production.

**Agroforestry benefit.** Agroforestry brings environmental, economic and social benefit and has been practised for generations in Ethiopia, e.g. on smallholder farms.

## From the student book — the pupil-taught core
The student book sorts the sub-fields two ways at once: pure fields, which seek knowledge for its own sake, and applied fields, which put knowledge to work in farming, medicine and industry. Its tables work the sort in both directions.
