# The cell cycle — interphase prepares — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. In your own words, what does this lesson teach about the cell cycle and interphase?**
**Answer: Division is how bodies grow, wounds heal and organisms reproduce; cells pass through an ordered cycle whose interphase prepares the cell — G1 growth and building-block gathering, S phase copying the DNA, G2 further preparation — before the mitotic phase divides nucleus (karyokinesis) and cytoplasm (cytokinesis).**
