# Video script — The cell cycle — interphase prepares

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Division is how bodies grow, wounds heal and organisms reproduce; cells pass through an ordered cycle whose interphase prepares the cell — G1 growth and building-block gathering, S phase copying the DNA, G2 further preparation — before the mitotic phase divides nucleus (karyokinesis) and cytoplasm (cytokinesis).

Now say it in your own words — and find one example near your home.
