# Digestive system disorders and hygiene — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Diarrhoea, dysentery, constipation, indigestion and stomach ulcers are common disorders of the digestive canal.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Digestive diseases enter only through the air we breathe..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Cholera, typhoid and hepatitis reach the digestive system through contaminated water and food.
**Answer: True**

**4.** An ulcer is caused by swallowing a worm..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
