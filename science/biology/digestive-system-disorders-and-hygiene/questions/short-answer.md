# Digestive system disorders and hygiene — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: worms and parasites.
**Answer: Roundworms, tapeworms and their kin are swallowed as eggs or larvae in unwashed vegetables, undercooked meat or dirty water. Inside, they take food the body digested for itself, leaving weakness and poor growth, especially in children.**

**2.** Explain: hygiene of hands, water and food.
**Answer: The hygiene rule is a chain of barriers: hands washed after the latrine and before eating or cooking; water from a clean source, boiled or treated where it is doubtful; raw vegetables washed; food cooked through and covered from flies while it waits.**
