# Digestive system disorders and hygiene — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining digestive system disorders and hygiene, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: The digestive system has its own diseases — most of them carried in by dirty water, dirty hands and spoiled food — and its hygiene is the daily practice that keeps them out. Diarrhoea, dysentery, constipation, indigestion and stomach ulcers are common disorders of the digestive canal. Cholera, typhoid and hepatitis reach the digestive system through contaminated water and food. Intestinal worms enter with unwashed food, dirty water and bare feet, and live at the body's expense.**
