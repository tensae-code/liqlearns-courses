# Digestive system disorders and hygiene — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about disorders of the canal?
a) Diarrhoea, dysentery, constipation, indigestion and stomach ulcers are common disorders of the digestive canal
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Diarrhoea, dysentery, constipation, indigestion and stomach ulcers are common disorders of the digestive canal**

**2.** What does this lesson say about germ diseases ride in food and water?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Cholera, typhoid and hepatitis reach the digestive system through contaminated water and food
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Cholera, typhoid and hepatitis reach the digestive system through contaminated water and food**

**3.** What does this lesson say about worms and parasites?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Intestinal worms enter with unwashed food, dirty water and bare feet, and live at the body's expense
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Intestinal worms enter with unwashed food, dirty water and bare feet, and live at the body's expense**

**4.** What does this lesson say about hygiene of hands, water and food?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Washing hands with soap, drinking clean water, washing raw food and cooking food thoroughly keep most digestive diseases out
**Answer: d) Washing hands with soap, drinking clean water, washing raw food and cooking food thoroughly keep most digestive diseases out**
