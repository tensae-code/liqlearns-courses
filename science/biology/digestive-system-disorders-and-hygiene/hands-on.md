# Hands-on — Digestive system disorders and hygiene

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Walk the hygiene chain at home: hands, water, raw food, cooking, storage. Write down one place the chain is strong and one place it could break, and what would fix the break.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of the hygiene chain in four panels — hands washed with soap, a covered pot of clean water, vegetables being washed, and food cooking thoroughly — each panel labelled with the barrier it makes — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
