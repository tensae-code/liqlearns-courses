# Parasitic STIs and who is at risk

## Do it first
Before you read: think of one living thing near your home that shows this lesson's idea — parasitic stis and who is at risk. Hold it in your mind, then read on and check your idea against what the lesson teaches.

The parasitic set: pubic lice (crabs) in body hair, trichomoniasis (Trichomonas vaginalis), and scabies mites spread by skin contact and shared bedding; epidemiology is then defined as the study of how diseases spread and are controlled, and the risk factors for STIs in Ethiopia are listed — unprotected sex, multiple partners, a previous STI, forced sex, and alcohol and drug misuse clouding judgement.
