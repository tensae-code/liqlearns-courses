Today: The vestibular analyser and balance.

Remember this one thing: balance is sensed by the vestibular analyser, which controls static and dynamic balance and carries its signals along the vestibular nerve — the system whose disturbance appears as motion sickness.

Moldova's Grade 11 book (§11 The vestibular analyser) teaches: static and dynamic balance control, vestibular nerve pathway; case studies: otitis media, motion sickness ("rău de mișcare"). (printed p. 37).

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
