# The vestibular analyser and balance — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** State the one thing this lesson teaches and explain it.
**Answer: balance is sensed by the vestibular analyser, which controls static and dynamic balance and carries its signals along the vestibular nerve — the system whose disturbance appears as motion sickness. Moldova's Grade 11 book (§11 The vestibular analyser) teaches: static and dynamic balance control, vestibular nerve pathway; case studies: otitis media, motion sickness ("rău de mișcare"). (printed p. 37).**

**2.** Explain Moldova's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Moldova's Grade 11 book gives the vestibular analyser its own section: static and dynamic balance control, the vestibular nerve pathway, and case studies of otitis media and motion sickness (“rău de mișcare”).**
