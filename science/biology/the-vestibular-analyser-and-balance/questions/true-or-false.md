# The vestibular analyser and balance — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Moldova's Grade 11 book (§11 The vestibular analyser) teaches: static and dynamic balance control, vestibular nerve pathway; case studies: otitis media, motion sickness ("rău de mișcare"). (printed p. 37).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. This topic is taught in Moldova's Ministry of Education biology books as named content.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches (Biologie — Procese și sisteme vitale: Manual pentru clasa a XI-a (Profil real / Profil umanist) (Grade 11)).

**3. The lesson's topic has nothing to do with the vestibular analyser and balance.**
**Answer: False** — the whole lesson is the vestibular analyser and balance.
