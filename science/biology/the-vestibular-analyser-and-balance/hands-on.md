# Hands-on — The vestibular analyser and balance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: balance is sensed by the vestibular analyser, which controls static and dynamic balance and carries its signals along the vestibular nerve — the system whose disturbance appears as motion sickness.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: the inner ear with the vestibular apparatus labelled, and the vestibular nerve path running to the brain, beside a person keeping balance standing and moving. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
