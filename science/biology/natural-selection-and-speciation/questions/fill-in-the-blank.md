# Natural selection and speciation — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. ** _____ is exemplified by the Grants' Galápagos finch study: in the 1977 drought, seed plants produced fewer, larger, tougher seeds, and finches with larger beaks were selected within a single season; speciation is tabled — allopatric (geographically separated, slow, common in nature, driven by natural selection) vs sympatric (not separated, fast with autopolyploidy and slow with allopolyploidy, common in plants) — with divergent evolution and macroevolution named as diversity-builders.
**Answer: Directional selection**
