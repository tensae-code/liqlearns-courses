# Excretion — the kidney cleans the blood

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — Excretion — the kidney cleans the blood. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
Blood must be cleaned continuously. The kidneys filter it, balance it, and send the waste water on its way to the bladder.

[Picture 22264 — two kidneys with ureters, bladder and urethra labelled]

## The key facts
**Filtering.** The kidney filters the blood.

**Regulating.** The kidney regulates the concentration of solutes in the blood.

**Red cells link.** The kidney even influences red-blood-cell numbers.

**Urine's path.** Urine travels from the kidneys to the bladder, where it is stored before removal.
