# Excretion — the kidney cleans the blood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about filtering?
a) The kidney filters the blood  b) It plays no part in this topic at all  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The kidney filters the blood**

**2.** What does this lesson say about regulating?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) The kidney regulates the concentration of solutes in the blood  c) It happens only at night and nowhere else  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) The kidney regulates the concentration of solutes in the blood**

**3.** What does this lesson say about red cells link?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) The kidney even influences red-blood-cell numbers  d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) The kidney even influences red-blood-cell numbers**

**4.** What does this lesson say about urine's path?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all  b) It happens only at night and nowhere else  c) It is found only in non-living things  d) Urine travels from the kidneys to the bladder, where it is stored before removal
**Answer: d) Urine travels from the kidneys to the bladder, where it is stored before removal**
