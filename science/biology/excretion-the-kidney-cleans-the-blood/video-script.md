Today: Excretion — the kidney cleans the blood.

Remember this one thing: the kidney filters blood, regulates the concentration of solutes in it and even influences red-blood-cell numbers — urine then travels to the bladder for storage and removal

Filtering: The kidney filters the blood.

Regulating: The kidney regulates the concentration of solutes in the blood.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
