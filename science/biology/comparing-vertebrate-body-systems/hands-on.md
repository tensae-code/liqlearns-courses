# Hands-on — Comparing vertebrate body systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Draw the five hearts from memory, chamber by chamber, and under each write its group's breathing organ and temperature kind. Check against the lesson and fix what you missed.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of five hearts in a row — fish, frog, lizard, bird, mammal — each drawn with its chambers shaded so the climb from two to four can be counted at a glance — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
