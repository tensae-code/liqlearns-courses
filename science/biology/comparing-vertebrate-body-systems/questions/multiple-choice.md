# Comparing vertebrate body systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about the heart, group by group?
a) The vertebrate heart climbs in chambers: two in fish, three in amphibians and most reptiles, four in crocodiles, birds and mammals
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The vertebrate heart climbs in chambers: two in fish, three in amphibians and most reptiles, four in crocodiles, birds and mammals**

**2.** What does this lesson say about single and double circulation?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Fish run a single circulation; land vertebrates run a double circulation — one circuit to the lungs, one to the body
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Fish run a single circulation; land vertebrates run a double circulation — one circuit to the lungs, one to the body**

**3.** What does this lesson say about breathing across the groups?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Fish breathe by gills; amphibians by gills, then lungs and skin; reptiles, birds and mammals by lungs alone, birds with air sacs that make their breathing the most efficient of all
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Fish breathe by gills; amphibians by gills, then lungs and skin; reptiles, birds and mammals by lungs alone, birds with air sacs that make their breathing the most efficient of all**

**4.** What does this lesson say about body temperature?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Fish, amphibians and reptiles take the temperature of their surroundings (ectotherms); birds and mammals hold a constant warm temperature (endotherms)
**Answer: d) Fish, amphibians and reptiles take the temperature of their surroundings (ectotherms); birds and mammals hold a constant warm temperature (endotherms)**
