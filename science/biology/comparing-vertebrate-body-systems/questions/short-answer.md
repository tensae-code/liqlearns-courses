# Comparing vertebrate body systems — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: breathing across the groups.
**Answer: Water breathers extract oxygen with gills. The frog lives both lives — gills as a tadpole, lungs and a moist skin as an adult. Fully land groups depend on lungs; the bird's air-sac system passes air through the lungs in one direction, feeding flight's huge oxygen demand.**

**2.** Explain: body temperature.
**Answer: An ectotherm's activity rises and falls with the weather — the lizard basks to work. Birds and mammals burn food to hold their temperature steady, paying for freedom from the weather with a much larger appetite.**
