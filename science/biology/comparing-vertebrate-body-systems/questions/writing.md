**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining comparing vertebrate body systems, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: Across the five vertebrate groups the same systems are built to one plan and tuned differently — clearest in the heart: two chambers in fish, three in amphibians and most reptiles, four in crocodiles, birds and mammals, driving a single circulation in fish and a double one on land. The vertebrate heart climbs in chambers: two in fish, three in amphibians and most reptiles, four in crocodiles, birds and mammals. Fish run a single circulation; land vertebrates run a double circulation — one circuit to the lungs, one to the body. Fish breathe by gills; amphibians by gills, then lungs and skin; reptiles, birds and mammals by lungs alone, birds with air sacs that make their breathing the most efficient of all.**
