# Comparing vertebrate body systems — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The vertebrate heart climbs in chambers: two in fish, three in amphibians and most reptiles, four in crocodiles, birds and mammals.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A frog's adult heart has four chambers like a bird's..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Fish run a single circulation; land vertebrates run a double circulation — one circuit to the lungs, one to the body.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Reptiles and birds must return to water to lay their eggs..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
