Today: Blood groups and the Rhesus factor.

Remember this one thing: antigens A and B define the blood groups, and the Rhesus factor is a further inherited red-cell antigen.

Antigens A and B define groups A and B; AB carries both and O carries neither.

The Rhesus factor is a further inherited red-cell antigen; about 85 percent of people are Rh-positive.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.


Student-book core: The ABO groups are fixed by the antigens on the red cells and the antibodies in the plasma. The compatibility chart shows who can give blood to and receive from whom.
