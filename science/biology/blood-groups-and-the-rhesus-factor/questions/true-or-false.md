# Blood groups and the Rhesus factor — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Antigens A and B define groups A and B; AB carries both and O carries neither.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Rhesus factor has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**3.** Rh incompatibility matters when an Rh-negative mother carries an Rh-positive baby.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Donation link has nothing to do with this lesson's topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.

**5.** The Rhesus factor adds + or − to each blood group.
**Answer: True**

**6.** Erythroblastosis fetalis has nothing to do with this lesson’s topic.
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches it directly.
