# Blood groups and the Rhesus factor — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: incompatibility.
**Answer: Rh incompatibility matters when an Rh-negative mother carries an Rh-positive baby.**

**2.** Explain: donation link.
**Answer: Blood groups decide who can safely donate blood to whom.**

**3.** Explain: compatibility chart (student book).
**Answer: The compatibility chart shows who can give blood to and receive from whom.**
