# Blood groups and the Rhesus factor — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** _____ and B define groups A and B; AB carries both and O carries neither
**Answer: Antigens A**

**2.** _____ factor is a further inherited red-cell antigen; about 85 percent of people are Rh-positive
**Answer: The Rhesus**

**3.** _____ is taught against the compatibility chart, so giving is safe
**Answer: Donation**
