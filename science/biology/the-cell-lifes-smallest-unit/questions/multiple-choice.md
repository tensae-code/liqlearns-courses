# The cell — life's smallest unit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about smallest unit?
a) The cell is the smallest, basic unit of living things
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) The cell is the smallest, basic unit of living things**

**2.** What does this lesson say about unicellular?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Single-celled (unicellular) organisms such as paramecium do everything with one cell
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Single-celled (unicellular) organisms such as paramecium do everything with one cell**

**3.** What does this lesson say about visibility?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Most single-celled organisms are invisible to the naked eye, with rare exceptions such as an egg cell
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Most single-celled organisms are invisible to the naked eye, with rare exceptions such as an egg cell**

**4.** What does this lesson say about specialised cells?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Multicellular organisms are built of many specialised cells, such as nerve cells and muscle cells
**Answer: d) Multicellular organisms are built of many specialised cells, such as nerve cells and muscle cells**
