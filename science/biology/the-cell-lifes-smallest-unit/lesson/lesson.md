# The cell — life's smallest unit

## Do it first
Before you read anything: think about this lesson's title — The cell — life's smallest unit. Write down, or say aloud, anything you already know about it, and one question you want answered. Then read on and check yourself at the end.

## The idea
One cell can be a whole organism, or millions of specialised cells can build one body.

[Picture 21025 — a paramecium beside a many-celled leaf]

## The key facts
**Smallest unit.** The cell is the smallest, basic unit of living things.

**Unicellular.** Single-celled (unicellular) organisms such as paramecium do everything with one cell.

**Visibility.** Most single-celled organisms are invisible to the naked eye, with rare exceptions such as an egg cell.

**Specialised cells.** Multicellular organisms are built of many specialised cells, such as nerve cells and muscle cells.
