# Soil pollution and its control — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why soil pollution deserves its own lesson and cannot just be folded into water pollution.
**Model answer: The soil holds what falls on it: heavy metals, petroleum and solid waste stay where they land and enter farming from below, long after the air has cleared and the water run off. Its controls are different too — compost pits, pipelines and land law, not filters and flow — so the ground needs its own account.**
