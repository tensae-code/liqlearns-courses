# Soil pollution and its control — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Rain that pollutes soil from the air is _____ rain.**
**Answer: acid**

**2. Aerosols can carry _____ metals down onto soil.**
**Answer: heavy**

**3. Organic waste can be rotted down in compost _____.**
**Answer: pits**

**4. Petroleum is safer moved by _____ than by spill-prone transport.**
**Answer: pipeline**
