# Soil pollution and its control — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which is a source of soil pollution named in Kenya's book?**
a) moonlight  b) petroleum spills  c) dew  d) earthworms
**Answer: b) petroleum spills**

**2. Aerosols pollute soil with…**
a) sand  b) heavy metals  c) pollen  d) seeds
**Answer: b) heavy metals**

**3. Which control moves petroleum more safely?**
a) open trucks  b) pipeline transport  c) river barges  d) burning it off
**Answer: b) pipeline transport**

**4. Compost pits help control soil pollution by…**
a) adding inorganic fertiliser  b) taking organic waste out of the dumped load  c) making acid rain  d) burying radioactive waste
**Answer: b) taking organic waste out of the dumped load**
