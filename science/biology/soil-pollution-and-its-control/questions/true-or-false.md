# Soil pollution and its control — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Inorganic fertilisers can pollute soil.**
**Answer: True** — a named source.

**2. Acid rain falls only on water, never on soil.**
**Answer: False** — it is a named soil-pollution source.

**3. Legislation is one of Kenya's soil-pollution controls.**
**Answer: True** — law backs the programme.

**4. Radioactive emissions are handled in the same block of the book.**
**Answer: True** — with their control.
