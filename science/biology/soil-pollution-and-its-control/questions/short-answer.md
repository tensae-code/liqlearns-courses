# Soil pollution and its control — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** List the sources of soil pollution in Kenya's Form 3 book.
**Answer: Acid rain, aerosols with heavy metals, petroleum spills, inorganic fertilisers and solid waste (Ch. 2, printed p. 63).**

**2.** Explain Kenya's way of teaching soil pollution.
**Answer: Kenya gives soil its own pollution treatment inside ecology, after air and water: sources first, then controls matched to them — recycling, compost pits, incineration and biological control for the waste and farm load, pipeline transport for petroleum, legislation over all — and radioactive emissions and their control closing the block.**
