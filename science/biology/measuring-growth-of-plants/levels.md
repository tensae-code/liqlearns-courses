# Measuring growth of plants — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (plant growth is a process you can measure — elongation growth at the shoot and root tips and thickening growth of the stem — followed with marks, rulers and graphs over days and weeks) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
