# Hands-on — Measuring growth of plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: plant growth is a process you can measure — elongation growth at the shoot and root tips and thickening growth of the stem — followed with marks, rulers and graphs over days and weeks.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a seedling marked with ink bands along its stem beside a ruler, a second drawing of the same plant taller a week later, and a rising growth graph pinned beside them. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
