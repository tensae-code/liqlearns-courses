Today: Factors that affect the rate of photosynthesis.

Remember this one thing: the rate of photosynthesis is set by limiting factors — light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature — and whichever is scarcest caps the rate, so raising the others alone does nothing until the scarcest is raised.
