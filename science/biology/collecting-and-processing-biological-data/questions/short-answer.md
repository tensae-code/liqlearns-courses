# Collecting and processing biological data — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the road from raw records to a finding.
**Answer: Data are first collected by a plan — what to count or measure, in what units, how often, on how many. They are recorded honestly in tables as the work happens. Processing sorts and orders them, computes averages and ranges, and turns them into graphs, so patterns can be seen. Presentation then states the question, method, results and conclusion so others can check the work.**

**2.** Explain: North Macedonia's way of teaching this.
**Answer: North Macedonia's Biology II opens with a theme on the methods of biological research and gives collecting and processing data and scientific presentation their own lessons (Themes 1.4–1.5, printed pp. 22–25), after the scientific method, instruments and the microscope, and before ethics and safety.**
