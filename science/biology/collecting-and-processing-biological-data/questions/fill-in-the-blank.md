# Collecting and processing biological data — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. Counting and measuring by a plan made beforehand is collecting _____.**
**Answer: data**

**2. Records are kept in _____ as the work happens.**
**Answer: tables**

**3. Sorting, ordering and averaging are _____ the data.**
**Answer: processing**

**4. Stating question, method, results and conclusion for others to check is scientific _____.**
**Answer: presentation**
