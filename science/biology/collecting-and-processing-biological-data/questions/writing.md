# Collecting and processing biological data — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining why honest recording matters in biological research.
**Model answer: Every conclusion of a study is built from its records, so a record that was altered, remembered late or tidied to fit a pattern poisons everything built on it. Honest recording — every value kept, in order, as the work happens — is what lets the processing and the conclusion be trusted and checked.**
