# Transpiration and sap movement in plants — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. Transpiration happens mainly through the roots.**
**Answer: False** — the experiment shows the leaves as the site of the loss.

**2. Transpiration helps pull the raw sap up the stem.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**3. The raw sap is the food made in the leaves.**
**Answer: False** — that is the elaborated sap; the raw sap is water and mineral salts rising from the roots.

**4. Plants have adaptation mechanisms against water loss.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
