# Transpiration and sap movement in plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. About how much of the water a plant absorbs is lost by transpiration?**
a) about 1%  b) about 10%  c) about 50%  d) about 99%
**Answer: d) about 99%**

**2. In the module's experiment, the leafy branch is compared with…**
a) a flowering branch  b) a defoliated branch  c) a root cutting  d) a branch in the dark
**Answer: b) a defoliated branch**

**3. The raw sap (seiva bruta) travels…**
a) from leaves to roots  b) from roots to leaves  c) only in the bark  d) only at night
**Answer: b) from roots to leaves**

**4. The elaborated sap flows from…**
a) the roots  b) the flowers  c) the leaves  d) the stem centre
**Answer: c) the leaves**
