# Transpiration and sap movement in plants — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining how transpiration can be both the plant's biggest problem and one of its engines.
**Model answer: Transpiration throws away about 99% of the water the roots absorb, which is a constant danger of drying — the plant's biggest problem, answered by its adaptation mechanisms. But the evaporation at the leaves also pulls: it helps draw the raw sap up the stem from the roots, so the very loss that threatens the plant drives its supply line.**
