# Transpiration and sap movement in plants — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Distinguish the raw sap from the elaborated sap.
**Answer: The raw sap (seiva bruta) is water and mineral salts absorbed by the roots, rising through the centre of the stem to the leaves, driven partly by transpiration. The elaborated sap (seiva elaborada) is the food made in the leaves, flowing from the leaves to the rest of the plant.**

**2.** Explain Mozambique's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Mozambique's PESD I Módulo 1 de Biologia (2023), Lesson 9, teaches transpiration with the 99% figure at its head — the plant's biggest problem, with its adaptation mechanisms — proved by a two-branch, two-bottle, bell-jar experiment comparing leafy and defoliated branches, and joins it to the two saps: raw sap rising root to leaves through the stem centre, elaborated sap flowing from the leaves.**
