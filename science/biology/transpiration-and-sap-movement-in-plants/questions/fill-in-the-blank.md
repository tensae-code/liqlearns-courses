# Transpiration and sap movement in plants — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. A plant loses about _____ percent of absorbed water by transpiration.**
**Answer: 99**

**2. The raw sap in Portuguese is called seiva _____.**
**Answer: bruta**

**3. The food-carrying sap is the seiva _____.**
**Answer: elaborada**

**4. The experiment covers the branches with a _____ jar.**
**Answer: bell**
