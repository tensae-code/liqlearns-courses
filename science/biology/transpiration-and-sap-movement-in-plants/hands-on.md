# Hands-on — Transpiration and sap movement in plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Repeat the module's experiment: stand a leafy branch and a stripped branch in water, cover each with a clear bag or jar, and compare the misting after some hours in the light. The leafy branch clouds its cover — the leaves are where the water leaves.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory — two bottles under a glass bell jar, one holding a leafy branch and one a bare defoliated branch, the leafy one's jar clouded with condensed droplets; beside it a plant with a blue arrow rising through the stem marked raw sap and a green arrow flowing down from the leaves marked elaborated sap — label its parts, and check against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
