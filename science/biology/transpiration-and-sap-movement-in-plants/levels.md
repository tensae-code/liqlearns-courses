# Transpiration and sap movement in plants — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (A plant loses about 99% of the water it absorbs by transpiration — its biggest problem, met by adaptation mechanisms — and that same loss helps pull the raw sap (seiva bruta) up through the stem from roots to leaves, while the elaborated sap (seiva elaborada) flows out of the leaves to feed the rest of the plant.) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by holding the two saps apart without looking — raw sap (seiva bruta): roots to leaves, through the stem centre, pulled partly by transpiration; elaborated sap (seiva elaborada): from the leaves to the plant — and the experiment that proves where the loss happens.
