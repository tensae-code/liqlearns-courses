Today: Transpiration and sap movement in plants.

Remember this one thing: a plant loses about 99% of the water it absorbs by transpiration — its biggest problem, met by adaptation mechanisms — and that same loss helps pull the raw sap (seiva bruta) up through the stem from roots to leaves, while the elaborated sap (seiva elaborada) flows out of the leaves to feed the rest of the plant.

Here is a surprising number: a plant loses about ninety-nine percent of the water it takes up. That loss is transpiration, and the book calls it the plant's biggest problem. But the loss also works: it helps pull the raw sap — water and salts — up the stem to the leaves. And from the leaves flows the second sap, the elaborated sap, carrying food to the whole plant.

Now say it back, in your own words. That is the lesson.
