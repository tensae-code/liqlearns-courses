# Animalia — animals without backbones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about invertebrates?
a) Invertebrates are animals without backbones and are the great majority of animal species
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Invertebrates are animals without backbones and are the great majority of animal species**

**2.** What does this lesson say about animals eat?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Animals are multicellular eukaryotes that eat other organisms
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Animals are multicellular eukaryotes that eat other organisms**

**3.** What does this lesson say about worms and molluscs?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) Worms and molluscs (snails) are invertebrate body plans
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) Worms and molluscs (snails) are invertebrate body plans**

**4.** What does this lesson say about arthropods?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Arthropods are jointed-legged invertebrates such as insects
**Answer: d) Arthropods are jointed-legged invertebrates such as insects**
