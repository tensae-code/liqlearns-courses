# The marsh habitat — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (a marsh is the border habitat between land and water — ground that stays wet and waterlogged — and its plants and animals are adapted to living with their feet in water on dry-land's edge) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 14, printed p. 204), without looking.
