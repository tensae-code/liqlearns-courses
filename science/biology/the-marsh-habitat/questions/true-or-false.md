# The marsh habitat — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** A marsh is fully dry land.
**Answer: False** — A marsh is a border habitat — its ground stays wet and waterlogged, between open water and dry land.

**2.** Marsh animals use both the water and the land of the border.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Waterlogged soil is air-poor, which marsh roots must cope with.
**Answer: True**
