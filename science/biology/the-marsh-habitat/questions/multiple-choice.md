# The marsh habitat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A marsh is best described as…
a) deep open water
b) a border habitat of waterlogged ground between water and dry land
c) a dry grassland
d) a forest interior
**Answer: b) a border habitat of waterlogged ground between water and dry land**

**2.** The marsh's ruling condition is…
a) waterlogged soil
b) frozen ground
c) no rain
d) salt water only
**Answer: a) waterlogged soil**

**3.** Marsh plants must be adapted to…
a) living without any water
b) rooting and growing in saturated, air-poor soil
c) climbing trees
d) burying seeds deep
**Answer: b) rooting and growing in saturated, air-poor soil**

**4.** Where does the book place the marsh?
a) In the aquatic habitats chapter
b) Opening the terrestrial habitats chapter — the border habitat first
c) In the agriculture chapter
d) It does not teach the marsh
**Answer: b) Opening the terrestrial habitats chapter — the border habitat first**
