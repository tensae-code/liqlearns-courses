# The marsh habitat — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: what makes a marsh a border habitat, and what its life must cope with.
**Answer: A marsh lies between open water and dry land: its ground stays wet and waterlogged. Its plants must root and grow in saturated, air-poor soil; its animals live across the edge, using water and land together. Every adaptation in the marsh answers that one border condition.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book opens Chapter 14, terrestrial habitats, with the marsh (printed p. 204) — the terrestrial-aquatic border habitat and its plant and animal adaptations — before forest, grassland and arid lands.**
