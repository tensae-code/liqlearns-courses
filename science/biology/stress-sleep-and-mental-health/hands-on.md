# Hands-on — Stress, sleep and mental health

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: mental health is held by sleep, biological rhythms and a workable day: stress strains the nervous system, sleep restores work capacity, and the hygiene of sleep and work — regular hours, rest and routine — protects both.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a day-and-night circle drawn as a clock: work, food, rest and a sleeping figure in their places on the dial, with a stress storm cloud crossed by a calm sleep band. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
