# Stress, sleep and mental health — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (mental health is held by sleep, biological rhythms and a workable day: stress strains the nervous system, sleep restores work capacity, and the hygiene of sleep and work — regular hours, rest and routine — protects both) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
