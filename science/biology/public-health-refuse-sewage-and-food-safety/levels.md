# Public health — refuse, sewage and food safety — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (public health is a shared duty: refuse disposed of safely (dustbin, incinerator, burial), sewage disposed of safely (pit latrines, bucket toilets, the flush toilet and septic tank, whose sludge becomes fertiliser), water and food protected, and the Ministry of Health inspecting abattoirs and eating places) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by using more examples of the same facts, then the harder corners: applying the lesson to a new example from the learner's own surroundings, and explaining Nigeria's way of teaching it (Ch. 12, printed p. 179), without looking.
