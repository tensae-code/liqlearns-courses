# Lesson plan — Public health — refuse, sewage and food safety

**Course:** Science · **Title:** Biology · **Lesson:** Public health — refuse, sewage and food safety
**Place in the curriculum:** Biology — appended to Unit 16 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** public health is a shared duty: refuse disposed of safely (dustbin, incinerator, burial), sewage disposed of safely (pit latrines, bucket toilets, the flush toilet and septic tank, whose sludge becomes fertiliser), water and food protected, and the Ministry of Health inspecting abattoirs and eating places.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own as a single-skill lesson built from Nigeria's Senior Secondary Biology 1, Ch. 12 (printed p. 179). Related lessons are recommended first where they help, never forced.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
