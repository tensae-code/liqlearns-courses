# Public health — refuse, sewage and food safety — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Public health, in the book, is a duty shared by the whole community.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Sewage and refuse are handled by exactly the same methods in the book.
**Answer: False** — Refuse goes by dustbin, incinerator or burial; sewage by pit latrines, bucket toilets, and flush toilet with septic tank.

**3.** Ministry of Health inspection covers abattoirs and eating places.
**Answer: True**
