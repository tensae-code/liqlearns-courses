# Public health — refuse, sewage and food safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is NOT one of the book's refuse-disposal routes?
a) Dustbin
b) Incinerator
c) Burial
d) Leaving it in the street
**Answer: d) Leaving it in the street**

**2.** What happens to septic-tank sludge, in the book?
a) It is thrown in the river
b) It is turned to fertiliser
c) It is burned in houses
d) Nothing — it stays forever
**Answer: b) It is turned to fertiliser**

**3.** Who inspects abattoirs and eating places?
a) The school
b) The Ministry of Health
c) Market traders
d) No one
**Answer: b) The Ministry of Health**

**4.** Pit latrines and bucket toilets are methods of…
a) refuse disposal
b) sewage disposal
c) food preservation
d) water purification only
**Answer: b) sewage disposal**
