# Public health — refuse, sewage and food safety — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** Refuse can be burned in an _____.
**Answer: incinerator**

**2.** The sludge from a septic tank is turned to _____.
**Answer: fertiliser**

**3.** The _____ of Health inspects abattoirs and eating places.
**Answer: Ministry**
