# Public health — refuse, sewage and food safety — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: the book's refuse and sewage routes.
**Answer: Refuse is disposed of by dustbin, incinerator or burial. Sewage is disposed of by pit latrines, bucket toilets, and the flush toilet with a septic tank — whose sludge is turned to fertiliser. Around both stand the protection of water and food and Ministry of Health inspection of abattoirs and eating places.**

**2.** Explain: Nigeria's way of teaching this.
**Answer: Nigeria's book teaches public health in Chapter 12 (printed p. 179) as a shared community duty: refuse disposal (dustbin, incinerator, burial), sewage disposal (pit latrines, bucket toilets, flush toilet and septic tank with sludge turned to fertiliser), protection of water and food, and Ministry of Health inspection of abattoirs and eating places.**
