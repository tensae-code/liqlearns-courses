# Skin diseases and skin hygiene — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the skin has its own diseases — infectious, allergic and parasitic — and skin hygiene is their prevention: washing, individual towels and care of small wounds keep the body's cover healthy) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
