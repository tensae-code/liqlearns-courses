# Hands-on — Skin diseases and skin hygiene

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Find, point to or act out one real example of this lesson's topic in your own surroundings, and say aloud how it shows the lesson's one thing: the skin has its own diseases — infectious, allergic and parasitic — and skin hygiene is their prevention: washing, individual towels and care of small wounds keep the body's cover healthy.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's picture from memory: a skin cross-section with one healthy labelled panel and one panel showing a small infection entering through an untreated cut, beside a row of hygiene items — soap, water and a towel marked 'mine only'. Label each part and check your drawing against the lesson.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
