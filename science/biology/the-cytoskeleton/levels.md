# The cytoskeleton — depth

This file is the lesson's depth record. This lesson has no sub lessons — it is one whole, single-skill lesson.

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state the one thing this lesson teaches (the cytoskeleton is the cell's inner scaffolding of protein fibres — microtubules, microfilaments and intermediate filaments — holding its shape, moving its organelles and pulling its chromosomes apart in division) and explain each key fact in their own words. Practice deepens by matching each key fact to what it shows or does, without looking.
