# Blood and its components — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1.** Explain: white cells — the defenders.
**Answer: White cells are fewer, larger and keep their nuclei. Some patrol the blood and swallow germs whole; others make antibodies that mark germs for destruction. A rising white-cell count in illness is the sign of this defence at work.**

**2.** Explain: platelets — the clotters.
**Answer: Where a vessel is cut, platelets gather and help turn dissolved fibrinogen into fibrin threads that net the red cells into a clot. The clot is a plug and a barrier at once: blood stays in, germs stay out, while the skin repairs beneath.**
