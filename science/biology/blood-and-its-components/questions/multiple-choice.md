# Blood and its components — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson say about plasma — the carrier?
a) Plasma is the pale liquid part of blood — mostly water — carrying dissolved food, salts, hormones and waste
b) It plays no part in this topic at all
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: a) Plasma is the pale liquid part of blood — mostly water — carrying dissolved food, salts, hormones and waste**

**2.** What does this lesson say about red cells — the oxygen carriers?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) Red blood cells carry oxygen on their haemoglobin; they are small, disc-shaped and have no nucleus
c) It happens only at night and nowhere else
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: b) Red blood cells carry oxygen on their haemoglobin; they are small, disc-shaped and have no nucleus**

**3.** What does this lesson say about white cells — the defenders?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) White blood cells defend the body: they engulf germs and make antibodies
d) It is found only in non-living things
**Answer: c) White blood cells defend the body: they engulf germs and make antibodies**

**4.** What does this lesson say about platelets — the clotters?
a) It plays no part in this topic at all
b) It happens only at night and nowhere else
c) It is found only in non-living things
d) Platelets are cell fragments that start blood clotting, sealing cuts so bleeding stops and germs are kept out
**Answer: d) Platelets are cell fragments that start blood clotting, sealing cuts so bleeding stops and germs are kept out**
