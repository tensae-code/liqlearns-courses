# Blood and its components — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Plasma is the pale liquid part of blood — mostly water — carrying dissolved food, salts, hormones and waste.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Red blood cells have a large nucleus that controls the cell..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.

**3.** Red blood cells carry oxygen on their haemoglobin; they are small, disc-shaped and have no nucleus.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Platelets carry oxygen around the body..
**Answer: False** — the lesson teaches the opposite of this statement.
