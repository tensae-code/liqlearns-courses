# Blood and its components — writing

Write your answer out in full sentences.

**1.** Write two or three sentences explaining blood and its components, using the lesson's key facts.
**Model answer: Blood is a liquid tissue: red cells, white cells and platelets carried in a watery plasma — each component with its own job in transport and defence. Plasma is the pale liquid part of blood — mostly water — carrying dissolved food, salts, hormones and waste. Red blood cells carry oxygen on their haemoglobin; they are small, disc-shaped and have no nucleus. White blood cells defend the body: they engulf germs and make antibodies.**
