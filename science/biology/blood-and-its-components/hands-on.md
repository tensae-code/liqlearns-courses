# Hands-on — Blood and its components

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with real things
Press a clean fingertip gently and watch the colour leave and return — that is blood moving in tiny vessels under the skin. From memory, list blood's four components and one job of each.

## Teach it on paper
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — a picture of a drop of blood under the microscope, labelled — many small red discs, one large white cell with its nucleus, tiny platelets between them, all in pale plasma — label it, and check your drawing against the lesson. Fix anything you missed.

## Ask and answer
With a companion, ask each other the lesson's key-fact questions in turn — one asks, one answers from memory, then swap.
