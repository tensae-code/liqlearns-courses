## Sub lesson 1 — RNA structure and function

RNA is single-stranded, with ribose sugar and uracil replacing thymine; its three working types — messenger, transfer and ribosomal RNA — are all transcribed from DNA and together run protein synthesis and other cell functions; in some viruses RNA is itself the genetic material.
