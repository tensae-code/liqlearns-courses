# RNA — the working copy — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) RNA is single-stranded, with ribose sugar and uracil replacing thymine; its three working types — messenger, transfer and ribosomal RNA — are all transcribed from DNA and together run protein synthesis and other cell functions; in some viruses RNA is itself the genetic material.**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Chromosomes are defined as thread-like packages of DNA in the nucleus, each with a centromere dividing it; and a ten-row DNA-vs-RNA table consolidates the comparison (names, backbones and bases, where each is found — nucleus/mitochondria vs cytoplasm/nucleus/ribosome — self-replicating vs synthesised from DNA, and more).**
**Answer: True** — this is what the lesson teaches.
