# The immune system — born with one, acquire the other — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing words.

**1.** The immune system protects the body from outside invaders — bacteria, _____.
**Answer: viruses, fungi and toxins (chemicals produced by microbes)**

**2.** The immune system works through organs, _____.
**Answer: cells and proteins working together**

**3.** _____ — the COVID-19 vaccine named — give acquired protection
**Answer: Vaccines**
